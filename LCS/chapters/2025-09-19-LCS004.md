A day had passed since the two commanders last talked, and they were now in the Black Demon Commander So Gong's office. Unlike Do Heon's office, it was desolate, with few documents and no works of art.

Do Heon glanced down at the table. A steaming meat dish and several bottles of liquor sat on it. "Did you plant a spy among my subordinates?"

"Hm? What do you mean?"

"I'm asking if you planted a spy in my company. How else would you know to prepare drinks and food?"

So Gong chuckled softly. "You said you'd come yourself, didn't you? That you'd buy me a drink."

"How did you know I'd come at this hour?"

"You should ask yourself that. A man who's always sharp as a knife about his work hours is talking nonsense."

Do Heon smiled bitterly.

So Gong gestured to a chair. "...Sit."

The two men sat facing each other.

So Gong filled Do Heon's cup. "Welcome to hell."

"Hm?"

"I say that because I see the will to live a challenging life overflowing in your eyes."

Do Heon laughed dryly. "You're really quick-witted."

"It's not that I'm quick-witted. You're just easy to read. It's hard to find someone as honest as you in our Cult."

"Am I that bad at controlling my expression?"

"You're good at it, but only with people you're not close to."

Do Heon stared blankly at So Gong, then filled his own cup. "I guess I feel just that comfortable around you."

"I believe so."

"How naive."

"Don't try to probe my intentions. It doesn't suit you."

Do Heon finally laughed softly.

So Gong held out his cup. "Let's have a glass for the First Squad."

CLINK!

The porcelain cups clinked together with a clear sound, and the two men coolly emptied their cups. Both men looked more at ease afterward.

So Gong ate a piece of meat and said while chewing, "Controlling your expression must be tough, especially for an upright person like you."

"May I ask one thing?" Do Heon asked seriously.

"You can ask ten things, even twenty. Ah, but not twenty-one."

"......"

"Don't glare at me like that. My jokes don't always land."

Do Heon watched So Gong quietly, his gaze unusual. "Just who are you, really?"

So Gong poured a drink. "I'm the Black Demon Commander So Gong. Why ask what you already know?"

"What you said to me..." Do Heon paused for a moment before meeting So Geon's gaze and continuing, "It made me think a lot. I'm grateful and sorry, but you said something that can't be said carelessly."

"......" So Gong silently emptied his cup.

Do Heon kept his eyes on So Gong. He asked no more questions and simply waited for him to speak.

After a moment, So Gong leaned back comfortably in his chair and smiled at Do Heon. "Whew, this liquor is strong. I'm already starting to feel it. You know... I've been waiting."

"For what?"

"For you to break."

"...Break?"

"Do you remember what I said? That your field of vision is extremely narrow. That you're a pretentious man who limits yourself to justice and impartiality within your little sphere of influence."

"Yes, I remember."

"I meant it."

"I know."

"I meant it, but it was also harsh. In the current Divine Cult, how many people even try to live by the rules like you do? It was too much to insult you like that, so it's been weighing on my mind."

Do Heon shook his head. "That's not the answer I was looking for."

"And yet, you didn't send that fellow, Yoo Yisang, to the Discipline Hall."

"......"

"I know it wasn't your doing. Either the warriors did it, or your fox-like Vice Commander acted first."

"......"

"Whatever the case, you accepted that measure. The you from before wouldn't have dared to imagine such a thing."

Do Heon's eyes deepened.

So Gong closed his eyes. "There are other upright people besides you, but among those I know, you are the most upright. That's why I hoped you'd break. I hoped you'd reach a state of despair and start to doubt your own principles."

"......"

"There's nothing more ridiculous than asking someone to join you before they've realized it on their own. It's dangerous, too."

Do Heon's eyes narrowed. "Join you...? Then what you want is..."

"You asked who I am?"

"......"

"I'm just me, but I don't want to remain the Black Demon Commander."

"What do you mean?"

So Gong opened his eyes.

Do Heon was inwardly startled. So Gong's gaze burned with a powerful will, unlike anything Do Heon had seen before.

"I want to become the Black Demon Commander of a new era."

"...!"

"No, it doesn't matter if I'm a Commander or just a mere warrior. I'd be fine even as a worker with no title."

"......"

"The world ruled by the current Cult Leader isn't just flawed, it's *fundamentally wrong*."

"......"

"I believe that Jo Baekcheon, and everyone tainted by him, must disappear."

The corner of Do Heon's eye twitched faintly. So Gong's statement was truly perilous. If anyone heard it, So Gong would be dragged away for treason and killed without a word of protest.

*However...*

"Since when?" Do Heon asked.

"Hm?"

"I'm asking when you started to think like this."

So Gong smiled gently. "It's been quite a while. I don't remember exactly."

"That's a lie."

"...Is it that obvious?"

"......"

"Haha, when and why I started thinking that way isn't what's important, is it?"

"That's true." Do Heon reached for the liquor bottle to fill his cup.

So Gong suddenly leaned forward and snatched the bottle away. "I'll pour for you."

Do Heon watched the liquor slowly fill his cup, then asked, "So what do you plan to do?"

So Gong filled his own cup after Do Heon's. "We must wait for the right time."

"What time?"

"I don't know, but when the time comes, everyone will know. That this is the moment."

"......"

"Until that one moment, all we can do is endure."

"You're not alone in this, are you?"

So Gong smiled. "Do you think a timid person like me would try to do something this big alone?"

"Who are you with?"

"I'll tell you in due time."

"Why? Don't you trust me?"

"If I didn't trust you, we wouldn't be having this meeting in the first place."

"......"

"I haven't even told the others you'll be joining us yet. There's an order to things, isn't there? After I contact them, I'll arrange a meeting slowly and carefully."

Everyone knew the current Divine Cult was wrong. However, not many among them considered overthrowing it. Even Do Heon himself had never dared to think such a thing. 

If even a little of this leaked out, the entire Divine Cult would be turned upside down. That was the nature of a rebellion, after all.

So Gong said, "Anyway, thank you."

Do Heon tilted his head in confusion. "For what?"

So Gong raised his cup. "Regardless of your abilities, I really wanted you with us."

"Why is that?"

"Because I don't want to kill my friend."

"......"

"When the time comes, with your personality, wouldn't you have tried to confront us?"

"...I see."

"I don't like the tragedy of me dying by your hand, or you dying by mine."

"I know."

"......"

"I know."

Do Heon looked quietly at So Gong, then chuckled and raised his cup. "I've just been needlessly complaining to my Senior."

So Gong clinked his cup and laughed heartily. "From now on, make sure you call me Senior in private."

"Don't be ridiculous."

The two men burst into loud laughter and emptied their cups.

Do Heon felt his heart grow much lighter. His decision did not waver, but he could not shake the lingering unease in a corner of his heart. He was genuinely glad they had this drinking session.

He raised his cup. "By the way...."

So Gong quickly filled it. "Yes?"

"What did you do with the kid?"

Do Heon asked, with some surprise in his voice, "Is he obediently receiving treatment?"

"I know, right? I thought he'd struggle and demand to be killed, but he's surprisingly taking the treatment without complaint. It doesn't seem like he's enduring it because he wants to live. It looks like he's made some kind of resolution."

"...Hm." Do Heon nodded and slowly filled So Gong's cup.

So Gong asked casually, "How is that kid?"

"Hm?"

"The troublemaker from the Lee Family Trading Company."

"How could I? I saw him for the first time at the Lee Family Trading Company."

So Gong's face grew serious. "His middle qi center was strange."

A strong lower qi center made the body physically strong and sturdy, and was used for martial arts. On the other hand, the upper qi center controlled spiritual power, allowing one to see ghosts or gain extrasensory perception. If it got damaged, one went mad.

Then what about the middle qi center? The middle qi center was the root of the Five Desires and Seven Emotions. If it was properly aligned, controlling emotions became easier.

"As you know, a person's mind isn't solely influenced by the middle qi center."

"That's right. A problem in the lower qi center can affect the upper qi center, and a problem with the upper qi center can crush the middle qi center."

"It seems he's never learned inner arts, yet his lower qi center is open. Surprisingly, even his upper qi center is overdeveloped, but his middle qi center is empty."

"That's what I'm saying. I have no idea what kind of body he has."

"......"

To someone who does not know martial arts, the concept of a qi center itself would be vague. Depending on the region, some believe people were born with their qi centers open, while others believe they must be opened through diligent effort.

So Gong's statement that the 'middle qi center was empty' came from a martial arts perspective. Still, martial arts was connected to life. An empty middle qi center meant the absence of emotions a human should naturally feel.

"Did you confirm it with your inner qi?"

"Of course."

"I can't believe it."

"...?" So Gong tilted his head.
