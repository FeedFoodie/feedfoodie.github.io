Lee Hong rushed to the waiting room and froze. Four supervisors stood chatting in front of the door. Down the corridor, demonic gladiators murmured among themselves.

The stench of blood seeped through the crack in the door, overwhelming and thick.

"You bastards!" he roared.

The supervisors and demonic gladiators jumped at his voice and spun around.

"What the hell are you supervisors doing! You think this is some kind of show? Shove those bastards back into the waiting room, now!"

"S-Sorry! We're sorry!"

Two supervisors rushed toward the demonic gladiators, shouting threats. The remaining two bowed their heads frantically.

Lee Hong ground his teeth. "Damn bastards. Is this the only way you can do your jobs? Why should I even pay you a monthly salary?"

"We're sorry!"

"Shut up! You can't even maintain simple control! None of you are getting paid this month!"

Lee Hong's rage burned hotter than usual. The reason was simple. Do Heon and Hwang Museok were with him. He should've presented a flawless operation to the Inner Sect's elite leaders, but instead, he'd shown them chaos. His fury was justified.

"Move!" Lee Hong shoved the supervisors aside and stormed into the waiting room.

He flinched involuntarily.

*What is this?*

The waiting room was a sea of blood. Crimson stained not just the floor, but splattered across the walls and ceiling. An outsider would think a massacre had taken place. Three demonic gladiators lay face down in the center. Another slumped against the wall, breathing raggedly.

Both the dead and living were drenched in blood.

Lee Hong frowned. *He's going to die too.*

The man had been stabbed five times in the stomach. His right shoulder hung mangled, and his neck bore a deep gash. He'd somehow avoided severing an artery, but had already lost too much blood. Worse, part of his intestines spilled slowly from his wounds.

A supervisor in the corner asked, "Coliseum Master, what should we..."

Lee Hong raised a hand for silence and crouched before the surviving gladiator. Fear crept across the gasping man's face. Whether from the terror of death, fear of Lee Hong, or fear of someone else entirely was impossible to tell.

"Did you kill those three?" Lee Hong asked.

The demonic gladiator seemed too weak to even shake his head. He only let out ragged breaths instead of answering.

Lee Hong's face twisted with anger. "I'm asking you a question. Did you kill them?"

"Heok!"

"This bastard, when I ask a question..."

Lee Hong's eyes suddenly sharpened. He stared at the gasping gladiator's neck. What had looked like a shallow cut had actually pierced the vocal cords. The man couldn't speak. It was a miracle he was still alive with such an injury.

"Tsk." Lee Hong sighed deeply and stood. "Nothing more to see. Moronic bastards. I warned them not to kill each other, and they still caused trouble."

Normally, he would've worried about losing demonic gladiators, but right now anger came first.

"What are you all standing around for! Take the dead ones and burn them!" Lee Hong shouted.

"Coliseum Master."

"Ah, Commander Do." Lee Hong scratched his head, looking embarrassed. "This is embarrassing. You came all this way, only for me to show you such a..."

"That's not it."

"Pardon?"

Do Heon wore a peculiar expression as he pointed to one of the gladiators lying face down. "I believe there's a survivor."

"A survivor?"

The prone gladiator's arm twitched.

Lee Hong's eyes widened. "Huh! He wasn't dead?"

"His wounds look deep, but he's not dead. Seems he fainted during the fight. Looks like he hit his head on something."

"I-I see."

Do Heon personally turned the gladiator over. A subtle smile touched the corners of his eyes, tinged with cunning. "Coliseum Master."

"Yes? Please speak."

"This fellow."

Lee Hong looked down at the gladiator. An unfamiliar face. He only remembered the gladiators from Squad A. He had no idea who was in Squad B or below.

"You may leave," Lee Hong told the supervisors.

They left and closed the door behind them.

"Is he the one you were interested in, Commander?" Lee Hong asked quietly.

"Yes, he's someone I took note of a while ago. I was worried he might have died, but luckily, he survived. Both in the Demonic Coliseum and here in the waiting room."

Lee Hong immediately understood and raised an eyebrow.

*So that's it?*

The demonic beings of the Coliseum were, without exception, rough and cruel individuals from society's lowest rungs. However, some among them undoubtedly possessed outstanding talent.

It wasn't his concern, but some combat unit leaders occasionally visited the Coliseum. If they found a useful demonic being, they'd pay a bribe to secretly recruit them. It seemed the Bright Demon Commander had come to make such a request.

*However, this is unexpected.*

Countless combat unit captains and lieutenants had visited before, but not a single Commander had ever come.

This was only natural. Only meticulously selected talents could enter the Inner Court's combat units. It was a place only the cream of the crop could aspire to join.

Moreover, this was the Bright Demon Company, renowned for its strength even among the Six Companies. He never imagined that Do Heon himself would come here to recruit talent.

Lee Hong smiled inwardly. *The Bright Demon Commander... Do Heon... Not bad.*

He didn't care which gladiator was taken. The man likely wouldn't be used as a warrior anyway. He could be a strong laborer, or even warm the bed of a noble lady one needed to impress.

The important thing was that a man of Do Heon's stature was personally making a request of him.

*It's good to have him in my debt.*

A connection with even one of the Inner Court's esteemed figures would greatly help his future career.

"We'll consider this one to have died here," Lee Hong nodded.

"Thank you."

"Not at all. For someone who works tirelessly day and night for the Divine Cult, how could I not do this much for you? Hahaha!"

Do Heon smiled. However, his eyes weren't smiling. "I should offer you a token of my gratitude."

Lee Hong waved his hands dismissively. "Goodness! How can you say such a thing? Please, just take him."

"I didn't collect my winnings from Squad B. I bet a generous amount, so please take it as a windfall and put it to good use, Coliseum Master."

Lee Hong was inwardly moved. The Bright Demon Commander Do Heon was renowned for his strictness, yet here he was, showing such sincerity. Judging by his methods, this didn't seem to be his first time. For such a man to also offer money meant he showed Lee Hong considerable respect.

"That's not necessary, but since the Commander has been so considerate, I'll put the money to good use for our Cult."

"I hope that you do."

"Hahaha!" Lee Hong laughed heartily.

Do Heon turned his head and looked down at the gladiator.

Lee Cheonsang's eyes slowly opened, and their gazes met.

"Vice Commander Hwang. Let's take this fellow and go," Do Heon said with a smile.

The Demonic Coliseum was large and spacious. Its popularity matched its size, but the Inner Court executives didn't visit openly. It was their way of maintaining dignity.

The Coliseum was built with several secret entrances for such people. That was where Do Heon headed.

"Well then, I'll see you again later." Lee Hong clasped his hands in farewell. "I'll take my leave now."

"Very well."

After Lee Hong left, Do Heon said to Hwang Museok, "Put him dow..."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes suddenly opened, and he climbed down from Hwang Museok's back on his own.

Hwang Museok looked at Lee Cheonsang with a displeased expression.

Lee Cheonsang brushed the blood from his clothes. "Long time no see, sir."

Instantly, Hwang Museok's eyes reddened. A worthless demonic gladiator was acting so insolently in front of the revered Commander. "You bastard...!"

"Enough!" Do Heon stepped forward. "Yes, it's been a while since we last met."

Without a word, Lee Cheonsang tore a strip from his clothes and wrapped it around his injured forearm.

Do Heon shook his head and grabbed his wrist. "Stay still."

He tossed aside the makeshift bandage, tore a long strip from his own sleeve, and wrapped it around Lee Cheonsang's forearm.

"Your demonic art mastery is considerable. It wouldn't be a major problem for healing even if the cloth is a bit dirty, but a clean one is always better."

Lee Cheonsang silently looked at his forearm.

"It was you, wasn't it?" Do Heon asked as he tied the bandage.

"It was."

"Hoho, you surprised me. I didn't think you would die, but I did think it would be difficult to get you out after you caused such an incident."

Then, he injured himself and feigned unconsciousness. He did this so he could receive treatment and resume his activities if he was lucky enough to survive.

"I didn't know you were so meticulous."

"A peak master would've seen through it."

"Oho, you know that much now?"

"You saw through it right away."

"Hahaha!"

Hwang Museok was greatly surprised to see Do Heon laughing so heartily. He hadn't seen Do Heon laugh like that recently. Although Do Heon had become gentler than before, he never revealed his emotions so openly.

*Who in the world is this guy?*

Hwang Museok's attention naturally turned to Lee Cheonsang.

Do Heon patted Lee Cheonsang's arm. "All done. Let's leave the first aid at this and go back to get you properly treated."

Lee Cheonsang slowly rotated his arm. "I suppose I can't go back to the Demonic Coliseum."

Do Heon tilted his head. "Was there anything more for you to learn there?"

"No."

"Then there shouldn't be any problem."

"......"

"Why? Is there something else bothering you?"

"No."

Do Heon looked at Lee Cheonsang with profound eyes and tilted his head. "It seems your life there was certainly unique. You're strangely different from when I first saw you."

"Didn't you say that struggle is the best way to learn about the world?"

"Hah, did I say that?"

"You did."

"So, do you feel like you've changed a lot?"

"I don't know if I've changed."

"If you've learned enough, that in itself is a change. Your face and aura are the same, but the impression you give has certainly changed. It must mean you learned a lot."

"......"

"You probably haven't had a proper rest in half a year. Go back and rest for a few days."

Lee Cheonsang stared at Do Heon.

Do Heon tilted his head. "What is it?"

"I have something I want to ask," Lee Cheonsang said.

Do Heon nodded. *He's definitely changed.*

Lee Cheonsang had asked him questions before, but never so naturally.

"What are you curious about? Go ahead and ask."

"What do you think of the Demonic Coliseum?"

"Hmm?"

"......"

"What do I think of the Demonic Coliseum?"

"......"

Do Heon frowned slightly. "I don't know why you're suddenly asking that... What is there to think? I certainly don't have a good opinion of it. To be honest, if it weren't for you, I would never have gone near the place."

"Is that all?"

"Was there a specific answer you were hoping for?"

Lee Cheonsang's eyes deepened. "Don't you think that place deserves to be destroyed?"
