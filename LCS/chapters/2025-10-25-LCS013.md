"...?"

A familiar ceiling came into view as Lee Cheonsang opened his eyes. He slowly sat up.

"You're awake," Do Heon said.

Lee Cheonsang turned his head. Do Heon sat in a chair, looking serious. He leaned forward with his hands clasped.

His gaze dropped to Do Heon's right hand. The hand was wrapped tightly in cloth, stained with dark red blood.

"I lost," he concluded matter-of-factly.

Do Heon chuckled involuntarily. "Isn't that obvious? You can't match me after learning martial arts for only half a year."

"Is that right?"

"Yes, that's right." Do Heon sighed and leaned back in his chair. "Is your head okay?"

"It's fine."

"And your hands?"

"......" Lee Cheonsang looked down at his hands. They were bound tightly in white cloth, just like Do Heon's. A dull ache throbbed in his palms.

Confusion crossed Lee Cheonsang's face. "When...?"

"So you don't remember." Do Heon looked at Lee Cheonsang seriously.

Lee Cheonsang saw his expression and thought. *Something must have happened before I lost my memory. I'm sure I did something.*

He couldn't remember, though. He had no idea when his memory had cut off. He had just pushed forward relentlessly until his consciousness simply vanished.

"You swung your sword beyond your limits. How could your hands be okay? Your palms are a complete mess."

"......"

"That's what makes it amazing, and also what makes it dangerous."

"......"

"Displaying skill beyond your strength isn't easy. It's a rare talent, maybe one in a thousand or even ten thousand, but pulling up strength you don't have to meet a goal is a different matter entirely."

"...?"

"You used so much power your body couldn't handle it. If you were an ordinary person, every muscle in your body would've ruptured."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes gleamed. *Is that so?*

He realized it wasn't just his palms that hurt. Faint pains also rose from his abdomen, back, and thighs.

This must have happened after his consciousness faded. It was an ability he didn't know he had.

"It's a good thing you dueled me. If you'd shown that state to an enemy you couldn't handle, you wouldn't have escaped death."

"I see."

"Yes, that's right."

Lee Cheonsang watched Do Heon quietly, then closed his eyes. "It seems I owe you a favor."

Do Heon chuckled. "You sure collect favors from everywhere, don't you?"

"......"

"Besides, I wouldn't call it a favor. It's just how things are. Who knows what the future holds for a person?"

"Then I'll consider this help undone."

Do Heon's smile faded. His expression hardened. "My goodness. You're so cold right after waking up."

Lee Cheonsang looked at Do Heon with clear eyes. He could read people's expressions. Or rather, he could read and 'interpret' them. Do Heon was stiff, but he could tell the man had something difficult to say.

"Do you have something to say?"

"The Demonic Coliseum must be eliminated."

"...?"

"You said that."

"I did."

"And I said you should be the one to eliminate it."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes deepened. "Yes."

"The Cult Leader himself created the Demonic Coliseum. Eliminating a place like that without reason is a grave crime. You'd almost certainly be accused of treason."

"There's a good reason I want to do that."

"There is, but it's not a reason the Cult Leader will accept."

"I thought as much."

Do Heon's eyes widened slightly. "Do you understand?"

"I can't empathize, but I understand."

"Haha!"

It hadn't been long, but Do Heon sometimes had a thought. He thought that because Lee Cheonsang couldn't feel emotions properly, he wouldn't understand how the organization worked. He wouldn't grasp the hierarchy or the sharp power struggles.

He was half right and half wrong. Lee Cheonsang didn't understand such things by empathizing deeply like ordinary people. Instead, he skipped empathy and went straight to understanding.

Do Heon still didn't know how that was possible, but that was how Lee Cheonsang uniquely 'operated'.

In other words, Lee Cheonsang didn't learn about the world because he knew nothing. He did it because he wanted to throw himself into the knowledge he already had, to feel it and empathize.

A flicker of pity appeared in Do Heon's eyes.

*How sad. He has such overly clear standards that he hardly seems human, yet this guy who lives more strictly than anyone else goes around searching for a touch of humanity.*

Lee Cheonsang stared at him blankly. "What is it?"

"Hm? Ah, sorry. I was lost in thought for a moment." Do Heon fixed his expression. "Anyway, with your strength, eliminating the Demonic Coliseum is difficult. Even if you destroyed it right now, the Cult Leader would just kill you and build a new one."

"......"

"If you really want to achieve your goal, you have to solve the fundamental problem."

"You mean tackle the root cause."

"Yes, the root cause. People call that politics."

*Politics.* Lee Cheonsang had heard the word before, but it felt unfamiliar in this context.

"However, politics is ultimately a power struggle. It's hard to get results without power, whether it's martial arts or influence."

"Martial arts, and influence."

"That's right." Do Heon's tone grew even more serious. "Do you want to eliminate the Demonic Coliseum?"

"Like I said, it's an organization that must be eliminated."

"However, no one can eliminate it. At least, not right now."

"I see."

"Do you want to try doing what no one else can?"

"I don't want to 'try' anything. I just believe it must be done. Whether by me or someone else."

A warm smile touched Do Heon's face. Their conversations were short, yet they felt long.

Now, though, he could fully understand and accept Lee Cheonsang. Not the man who killed his own father, nor the Group Two Demonic Gladiator, but Lee Cheonsang the human being.

"Join me."

"We're already together?"

"You really know how to ruin a moment. That's not what I meant..."

"You said you owed me a favor."

"Hm?"

"And now you're repaying it. Thanks to you, I'm learning about the world."

"...?"

"We've been together from the start. As long as that favor isn't repaid, we always will be."

"...!"

"When you think you've repaid it all, just tell me. Until then, our relationship won't end."

Do Heon's jaw dropped. His expression looked like he had been hit in the head with a hammer.

Lee Cheonsang stared at him intently. "I still don't know what kind of favor it was, but I doubt it was a big one. If you think you've repaid enough, we can settle the account..."

"I can't do that." Do Heon shot to his feet. An even greater warmth filled his eyes as he looked down at Lee Cheonsang. "It was such a huge favor that I doubt I could repay it even if I died. I'll always be your debtor. At least, when it comes to you."

"If you insist on suffering, I won't stop you."

"Hahaha!" Do Heon's laughter boomed heartily.

Confusion clouded Lee Cheonsang's face again. He couldn't understand why Do Heon was laughing.

*Am I still very far from understanding people?*

Understanding people and the world still seemed like a long, distant road.

Do Heon's thoughts were different, though.

*This is the one.*

The vague pieces of understanding suddenly clicked into place, forming a complete picture.

*I simply liked this guy, who looks like a doll but is more human than anyone.*

Why did he like this kid? There were many reasons, but in truth, none of them were important.

Do Heon simply wanted to be with Lee Cheonsang. Not to have him as a subordinate. Just to live in this world together with this unique individual.

An inexplicable conviction also took root.

*If he just survives, if he just grows properly.*

An intense heat flared in Do Heon's eyes.

*This guy could become a great blessing to our Cult.*

To do anything, one needed a plan and the right talent. The eye for selecting that talent differed from person to person. Some sought physical strength, while others searched for wealth or talent.

Do Heon saw the law in Lee Cheonsang. The law of trying to understand people. The talent to become a law unto himself and change his surroundings, and by extension, the entire group.

"I'll help you properly," he declared. "I hesitated before, but I won't waver anymore. I'll give you a power that isn't easily obtained."

"I will receive it well."

Do Heon smiled. It was a very Lee Cheonsang-like answer. "Wait for a while. I'll be back."

He left the private room and returned just before 3 AM, almost two hours later, carrying two books.

"Take these." He handed the books to Lee Cheonsang.

"What are these...?"

"The first is the martial arts manual for the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes flashed. "Vajra Yaksha?"

The Vajra Yaksha was one of the Four Heavenly Kings of Buddhism. He was the heavenly king who guarded the north of Mount Sumeru, also known as King Vaiśravaṇa. He commanded the evil spirits, the Yaksha and Rakshasa, and held the highest status among the Four Heavenly Kings.

As such, he was surprised that a demonic art named after the Vajra Yaksha existed within the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, which worshipped Mara, the Great Enemy of Buddha.

Do Heon smiled. "Have you heard of the Eight Great Demonic Arts?"

"I've heard the demonic gladiators talk about them."

The Divine Cult's Eight Great Demonic Martial Arts referred to the eight highest-grade martial arts that the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult prided itself on. To be precise, they were the pinnacle of the One Hundred and Eight Demonic Path Studies and the supreme arts of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. They were divine, peerless techniques. Mastering just one could allow a person to dominate an era.

"I don't know how the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art ended up in our Cult, but it's a clear fact that the foundation of this martial art is from the Buddhist sect."

"Is this one of the Eight Great Demonic Arts?"

"Not yet."

"...?"

"If it were officially incorporated, it would probably become the Nine Great Demonic Martial Arts, but the chances of that aren't high. I don't know what kind of eccentric created this, but our Cult, the headquarters of the demonic path, and the Buddhists are incompatible."

"I know that."

"A demonic art based on Buddhist martial arts... no matter how outstanding, it would be hard to accept as a representative art of our Cult, if only for reasons of orthodoxy."

Do Heon shrugged. "That's also why I could secretly bring this martial art here. No one pays any attention to it, especially in a chaotic world like this."

Even so, it was a surprising feat. The leaders of the Divine Cult's martial units were formidable, but they were ultimately just leaders of combat units. To the true leaders holding key positions in the Inner Court, they were nothing more than well-sharpened blades.

"I also had a chance to bring one of the Eight Great Demonic Arts, but I didn't. It wouldn't have been officially received, and I could've been killed just for practicing it secretly."

"Have you learned this demonic art?"

"I haven't. It doesn't suit me."

"Then why did you bring it?"

"I thought it would surely be useful someday." Do Heon pointed at Lee Cheonsang. "Like right now."

"......"

"I don't like those bald monks myself, but I know a bit about them."

"One must know the enemy to deal with them properly."

"Haha, that's right." Do Heon's eyes deepened. "The Vajra Yaksha is King Vaiśravaṇa. It's said that King Vaiśravaṇa greatly enjoys listening to the sermons of the Buddha they worship. In other words, he's the heavenly king with open ears."

"......"

"Even when you've grown much stronger in the future, I hope you become someone who knows how to listen to people, just like the Vajra Yaksha demonic art you're about to learn."

Lee Cheonsang watched Do Heon quietly and nodded. "I understand, and what is the other book?"

"Ah, that's..." A dubious light flickered across Do Heon's face. "It's a rather dangerous item."
