Do Heon frowned when he arrived at the main gate of the Bright Demon Pavilion.

Hwang Museok stood at the center of the Bright Demon warriors, a terrifying aura of overflowing demonic qi mixed with killing intent emanating from them. The air was so tense a fight could erupt at any moment.

Facing the Bright Demon Company, the Discipline Hall members stood as stiff as stone. They knew all too well the Bright Demon Company's fearsome combat prowess.

Jeon Oh, the First Squad Leader of the Discipline Hall, seemed completely untroubled, though. He wore a calm, mocking smile.

"What's the meaning of this?" Do Heon demanded angrily.

Hwang Museok and the Bright Demon warriors looked at him.

Do Heon commanded coldly, "Stand down."

He used no unnecessary words.

The Bright Demon Company's fiery killing intent slowly subsided.

Hwang Museok took a step forward. "Commander."

"What are you doing?"

"Pardon?"

"I heard you brought a summons, so why are you creating such an unsettling atmosphere?"

Hwang Museok was flustered. "Commander. We were just..."

"Don't cause needless trouble. We're all fellow demonic practitioners."

"...I apologize."

Do Heon understood Hwang Museok's feelings, but his actions today were clearly excessive. He apologized to Jeon Oh. "It seems they're on edge from all the intense training lately. If we've been rude, I apologize."

Jeon Oh said cynically, "They say the power of the Bright Demon Company is the greatest among the Six Companies, and I see it's truly exceptional. I doubt you'd ever walk around with your spirits down, would you?"

Hwang Museok's eyes became slightly bloodshot. To be mocked so openly was too much. Even if they were from different organizations, no one shouldn't behave this way.

Do Heon remained calm, though, saying, "I'll have a word with my men. So, what brings you here?"

Even a fox borrowing a tiger's might has its limits. With Do Heon acting this way, Jeon Oh couldn't push any further.

Jeon Oh clicked his tongue and said in a rather stiff voice. "As you've heard, a summons has been issued from the higher-ups."

Do Heon tilted his head. "Are you sure it's the Discipline Hall?"

"That's correct."

"That's strange. I may have issues with other organizations, but I've never done anything to warrant being dragged away by the Discipline Hall."

"We're only following orders. The higher-ups wouldn't issue a summons without proper justification, so you, Commander, must comply."

Do Heon's eyes deepened.

Hwang Museok stepped forward. "Tell us the details."

Jeon Oh didn't even spare Hwang Museok a glance. It was a clear dismissal.

Hwang Museok's cheek twitched slightly. "Tell us the details! Isn't there a rule that you must clearly state the reason when summoning a person?"

He could say this confidently in front of all the warriors because he trusted Do Heon completely.

Jeon Oh frowned. "I'm speaking with Commander Do. The Vice Commander shouldn't interfere."

"What did you say?!" Hwang Museok's face flushed red.

No matter how great the Discipline Hall's authority, Jeon Oh was a mere squad leader. Treating the Vice Commander of an elite combat unit so condescendingly was excessively rude.

*This bastard.*

Normally, he would have unconsciously unleashed his demonic qi in a surge of anger. He only controlled his qi now because of Do Heon.

Do Heon spoke. "I don't think my Vice Commander's words are out of line. Not informing the summoned party of any details is a violation of the rules. You of all people should know that better than anyone."

Jeon Oh frowned. "You're going to have to come anyway. Is there any need to waste time?"

"Do you people throw away necessary procedures just to save time? If the Discipline Hall is swarming with people like that, who would trust its judgment?"

"Whether you trust it or not isn't our concern."

"So you'll ignore the correct procedure to the very end?"

The corner of Jeon Oh's mouth lifted slightly in a look of utter ridicule. It wasn't an expression one dared to show a Commander of the Six Companies, regardless of the Discipline Hall's authority.

WHOOSH!

Killing intent finally flowed from Hwang Museok's body. He didn't attack, but he couldn't stop the surging intent.

"......" Jeon Oh's expression stiffened slightly.

Hwang Museok's killing intent grew stronger, and Do Heon didn't restrain him. The Bright Demon warriors lined up behind them also began to emit killing intent.

Fear, madness, and killing intent are highly contagious.

Unlike a moment ago, Do Heon said nothing. He would normally have ordered the warriors to stand down immediately. If this dragged on, something serious could happen.

*Stiff-necked bastard.*

Cursing inwardly, Jeon Oh spoke with an arrogant expression. "A report came in from the outer city Demonic Coliseum. It says that you, the Bright Demon Commander, smuggled out an operational demonic gladiator without any permission."

"......" Do Heon's eyes narrowed.

Hwang Museok maintained his killing intent but was inwardly shocked. The Chairman of the Demonic Coliseum had committed numerous acts of corruption. Trading demonic gladiators for money was just a small part of his shady record. The Discipline Hall must have known this. They likely never summoned him because it offered them no particular benefit, and yet, a summons was issued for something like this?

Do Heon asked, "Did the Demonic Coliseum Master say that?"

"A report was filed, the investigation confirmed it, and the higher-ups judged there was nothing unjust in the process, so they issued the summons. It's difficult to provide further details."

Do Heon nodded. "If the higher-ups have judged it so, then it can't be helped."

Jeon Oh lifted his chin as if to say, 'See?'

Do Heon turned to Hwang Museok. "I'm going to the Discipline Hall. Make sure the warriors' training is conducted on time."

"B-But, Commander!"

"Stand down now. Nothing will happen."

At his superior's calm, reassuring tone, Hwang Museok gritted his teeth and bowed his head. "...Please take care of yourself."

The moment the Demonic Coliseum was mentioned, Lee Cheonsang came to mind. Hwang Museok tried to suppress his surging anger. The fact that they were in this situation because of a single demonic gladiator infuriated him.

*What the hell is that bastard that the Commander has to suffer this humiliation?*

Anger couldn't change reality, though. Hwang Museok gathered the warriors and withdrew.

Do Heon watched them quietly, then turned back to Jeon Oh. "I'll come out after going inside the Second Pavilion. Wait for a moment."

Jeon Oh frowned. "You must come immediately upon confirming the summons. You should know this is to prevent the potential destruction of evidence, Commander."

"Is it a crime?"

"Pardon?"

"If the crime were clear, you would've stated it was an obvious crime right here. I don't know how long I'll be at the Discipline Hall, so shouldn't I take some measures to ensure my unit runs smoothly?"

"That's your personal business, Commander. There's no reason for us at the Discipline Hall to accommodate..."

"Which squad's leader are you?"

"Pardon?"

"The second squad leader? Or the third squad leader?"

A look of vivid displeasure appeared on Jeon Oh's face. The First Squad Leader was the highest rank in the Discipline Hall besides the Director, Deputy Director, and a few special guards. He had held the position for some time, yet this man didn't even know his rank?

"I'm the First Squad Leader."

"And your name?"

"Are you curious about my name as well?"

"Listen here, First Squad Leader."

"Please continue."

"......" Jeon Oh startled. Do Heon had suddenly appeared right in front of his nose. Do Heon was taller and larger than Jeon Oh. He couldn't help but flinch as such a man looked down on him from point-blank range.

"So, what's your name?" Do Heon asked, his voice deeper. A chilling gleam shone in his eyes, devoid of demonic qi or killing intent.

Jeon Oh swallowed dryly. "I... I'm Jeon Oh."

"Right, Jeon Oh." Do Heon smiled and patted his shoulder. "It's a nice day. You all must be overworked and unable to rest properly. Why don't you get some fresh air while I finish my preparations?"

"......"

"I won't be late, so don't worry."

Do Heon turned his back, clasping his hands behind him.

Jeon Oh's face instantly turned red. He had shown a disgraceful side of himself in front of the warriors. He cried out reflexively. "This series of actions you've just taken will all be reported to the higher-ups!"

Without looking back, Do Heon waved his hand.

Seething with humiliation, Jeon Oh gritted his teeth.

After entering his quarters, Do Heon organized some documents and checked a training list he had prepared. The process was incredibly simple and took less time than drinking a few sips of tea.

After his rough preparations, Do Heon summoned Hwang Museok. "If I happen to return late, conduct the training as written here."

Hwang Museok said worriedly. "Commander. This is clearly a ploy by the Discipline Hall Director to find fault with you. It's dangerous for you to go now."

"Finding fault?"

"Pardon?"

Do Heon let out a short laugh. "Isn't it a fact that I took a demonic gladiator from the Demonic Coliseum? Before we talk about finding fault, a crime is a crime."

"B-But, Commander!"

"Furthermore, if I refuse this summons, there'll be greater bloodshed. Not just for me, but for all of you as well."

"......"

"If it's a lightning strike you can't avoid, you must face it head-on with confidence."

"Commander..."

Do Heon rose from his seat, draped his long robe over his shoulders, and said. "I'm leaving the unit in your hands for a while."

After leaving the office, Do Heon visited the separate room where Lee Cheonsang was staying.

"Things have gotten a bit complicated."

He briefly explained what had happened.

Lee Cheonsang nodded. "I see."

"No matter who the Discipline Hall Director is, he can't lock up a Commander of the Six Companies for such a minor charge. At worst, he'll harass me for about half a month and then I'll get a pay cut."

"......"

"Have you memorized the entire secret manual?"

"I memorized it and burned it."

"Good." Do Heon's expression turned serious. "Stay here and practice the demonic art until I return. This might actually be for the best. Now that you have time alone, you can reflect on your martial arts..."

"Are you certain?"

"Hm?"

"I asked if you're certain."

"About what?"

Lee Cheonsang's tone was as monotonous as ever. "That the Discipline Hall Director won't be able to imprison you. Are you certain of that?"

For a moment, Do Heon was at a loss for words. Of course, he thought so. The probability was extremely high. It was difficult to give a clear 'yes' when asked if he was certain, though.

After a moment of thoughtful silence, he replied, "Even if I'm imprisoned, there'll be efforts from the outside to get me out. Of that, I'm sure. At most, it won't even take a full month."

"During that one month, the anger of the Bright Demon Company will build up to its peak."

Do Heon's eyes widened. "...?!"

Lee Cheonsang tilted his head. "Was my guess wrong? Seeing the bond you all share, it seems entirely possible. Isn't that how it usually is with organizations that have strong hierarchical ties?"

"...No, you're right."

"Just as I thought." Lee Cheonsang shook his head. "I'll give up on joining the Bright Demon Company."

"......"

"It might have been different before, but if I'm involved now, there's no need for me to join the Bright Demon Company."

"You don't trust me?"

"I don't know about trust, but I do know that this will be a burden to you."

"......"

Just then, Lee Cheonsang's eyes shone with a strange gleam. It was a light Do Heon had never seen before, sharper than anyone else's precisely because it was so emotionless.

"Instead, I have an idea."
