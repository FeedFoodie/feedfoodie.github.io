FWOOSH!

The world blazed with fire and death. Black smoke filled the sky, forming billowing clouds that loomed over the Shiwan Mountain Range. Churning darkness stretched as far as the eye could see, like a path to the underworld or the surging Sanzu River, accompanied by the acrid stench of burning flesh and blood.

DRIP, DRIP...

At first, blood streamed endlessly from Yoo Yisang's mouth, but over time, the flow gradually ceased.

*Is it because I have lost too much, or because of the falling raindrops? Or is it because of someone's tears?*

"What's with that expression? Fix your face," he muttered.

"......"

"That was a magnificent slash. It's a better parting gift than I deserve for my journey to the underworld."

"......"

"With this, the battle is finally over... Hey, did you know? I always thought..."

"......"

"I thought I was the best, that I was the only one who could change the world."

"......"

"Deep down, though, I knew you were the only one who could step over me and rise higher... COUGH!"

Blood spurted from Yoo Yisang's mouth and splattered the man's face and chest, but he paid it no mind. His face remained so indifferent that it was hard to believe he had just stabbed someone.

However, Yoo Yisang knew he was crying. He knew his murderer was so overwhelmed with emotion that he didn't know what expression to make.

"Aren't you happy?"

"......"

"Right, you have to carry this burden alone from now on, so you can't possibly be happy."

"......"

"Still, don't be sad. It will be hard... but sometimes, you should smile..."

"......"

Yoo Yisang went limp.

PATTER! PATTER! SHWAAAAA!

The sky seemed lost in sorrow, or perhaps it was terrified. The rain, which initially fell in single drops, suddenly began to pour. The man pulled the sword from the dead man's body, watching the bloodstains wash away in an instant.

Only then did he realize he was still alive. He had always lived like a rigid doll, but before he knew it, his time with his companions had given him a new life.

He had never smiled brightly, offered a kind word, or cried from overwhelming sorrow. Nevertheless, as his last comrade drew his last breath, he realized he was no less human than everyone else.

DRIP, DRIP...

A clear liquid streamed down his cheek. Was it a tear or a raindrop? No one knew. Still, he remained expressionless.

"Can I meet you? If I die, can I meet all of you?" he whispered hoarsely, his tone cold and stiff. Although his words seemed to tremble faintly at the end, perhaps it was just an auditory hallucination caused by the wind and pouring rain?

The man was that kind of being. He clearly existed, yet he was an indescribable being, part human and part monster.

SHWAAAAA!

As time passed, the rain grew heavier.

The man raised his head and looked at the sky. The ceaseless downpour doused his face and obscured his vision, but his murky eyes never wavered.

"...This stings."

He had lived his life thinking he was an aberration, something not quite human. Only now did he realize that he was indeed human, but at the same time, he realized that he could no longer be considered human.

*Fate is a cruel mistress indeed. Why is it that the moment I realize I'm human, I'm forced to give up my humanity? In the end, I can never live as a human.*

The man's face slowly lost its luster, illuminated only by a streak of blue lightning that flashed over his shoulder and then faded away.

"You should have told me."

His narrowed eyes grew increasingly bloodshot. From that moment on, the liquid flowing down his cheeks looked different from the rain.

"You should have told me that I'm no different from you."

"......"

RUMBLE! CRASH!

Two streaks of lightning lit up his features for a moment, but even that quickly dimmed.

The man's features warped more intensely and deeply, stricken by endless despair and sorrow. In that moment, he looked more 'human' than anyone else.

"You should have told me that if I killed you all...!"

SNAP!

A snapping sound rippled through the air. At the same time, a red liquid trickled from the man's eyes.

"You should have told me how lonely I would be after killing everyone!"

CRACKLE!

An incredibly thick, gray lightning bolt wrapped around the man's entire body as he unleashed a ferocious wave of qi.

CRACKLE! BOOM! BOOOOOM!

The lightning surged through the rainwater, mercilessly shattering the crumbling buildings.

"AAAAAAARGH!!"

He screamed at the sky, tears of blood streaming down his face. Standing there, he was the very image of a human at the pinnacle of despair.

CRACKLE!

However, the lightning only grew clearer and darker. Bit by bit, it erased the tiny sliver of humanity that had been granted to him.

"AAAAAAAH! UWAAAAAAAAH!!"

He knelt and clutched his head, releasing thirty years of pent-up emotions.

Resentment towards those who had left first. Disgust towards himself for sending them away. Despair at the reality that he could never see them again. Grief over the fact that no one was by his side.

"......"

At some point, the man lowered his head. His eyes were now hollow, devoid of all emotion. The pouring raindrops had washed many things away. Sorrow, regret, despair, and even death itself dissolved into the flowing water and cascaded down the mountain.

"......"

The man slowly rose, his body seeming somehow larger than before.

WHOOSH!

Heat radiated from his mouth with every breath. The fierce downpour no longer touched his body.

The power of the Demon King of the Realm of Desires, who stole Indra's power, was now coursing through him. Even though it was yet incomplete, his rampant emotions instantly took the form of the Demon King.

Normally, those who had not opened the Door could not reach the Realm of Desire. However, this man entered it without opening the Door. His intense emotions transcended the limits of a demon. The seed of a god that had lost its humanity, planted within his eroded sentiments, made it all possible.

Still, neither the ferocious downpour nor the divine lightning could wash away one particular emotion.

FWOOOOSH!

A massive cloud of steam, created by intense heat, dominated the area in front of the palace.

"......"

From within the steam, thicker than fog and darker than clouds, a pair of brilliant blue eyes appeared. In those two eyes, an unbelievable madness flickered, seething with rage and blazing like an inferno.

The man took a powerful step forward. He was Lee Cheonsang, a rebel against the old era's tyranny and the Demon King who would open the door to a new era.

KWAANG!

The falling rainwater shot up into the sky to welcome the newly crowned demon god of the mortal realm.

"Open the gates of the Demon God Palace," he commanded quietly.

ZIIING! KABOOM! RUMBLE!

Overflowing demonic qi manifested as blackish-gray lightning, devastating the surroundings. As if on cue, the great gates of the massive palace creaked open.

"WAAAAAAH!!"

Hundreds of armed soldiers roared within the palace grounds. Dressed uniformly in ornate armor, they resembled a mythological army of demons that had crawled up from hell.

They were the Heavenly Demon Army, the strongest and most vicious army in the world, handpicked to protect the god revered by all demons. They were the last bastion of the Divine Cult, the final shield protecting the Cult Leader.

Lee Cheonsang's eyes grew indifferent.

*How vulgar! They believe that a demon is desire, and desire is sordid. Since desire is sordid, a demon should wallow in dark mud.*

The Heavenly Demon Army's antiquated and vulgar eyes were the spitting image of the master they served.

*They're wrong. It should not be this way.*

He tightened his grip on his sword.

VWOOOONG!

The jet-black blade, Black Night, cried out in acknowledgment.

Lee Cheonsang's charismatic voice echoed from the tip of the blade. "From this day forward, no unqualified individual shall be allowed to sit upon the Divine Throne."

His leisurely pace quickened, one step at a time, unfazed by the Heavenly Demon Army shrieking and charging toward him.

RUMMMMBLE!

The Inner Court of the Divine Cult rocked as if struck by an earthquake. The roar of the advancing army, coupled with vicious battle cries steeped in killing intent and fear, drowned out all else.

Lee Cheonsang kicked off the ground, appearing instantly at the forefront of the Heavenly Demon Army.

He launched into a frenzied sword dance.

The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, home to the demonic murim, was synonymous with fear itself. However, due to the sudden death of the former cult leader, who left no successor, the cult faced an unprecedented crisis.

Since their god had vacated his seat, the cult could only wait for another god to be born. In the meantime, a proxy god was appointed to fill the position.

However, demons were belligerent creatures, and their method of choosing a ruler was brutal. The Divine Cult quickly became a battleground where fiends vied for power. In the ensuing chaos, countless lives were lost, and many were driven from their homes.

Amidst that hellish war, one great demon finally seized the throne—the Violet Lightning Divine Demon, Jo Baekcheon. He was second in rank among the Ten Great Demon Kings of the Elder's Hall, and a formidable martial artist who had greatly revitalized the Divine Cult's economy with his outstanding managerial ability.

However, Jo Baekcheon could not be called an outstanding ruler, even as an empty compliment. As time passed, people's evaluation of him worsened, and Jo Baekcheon himself realized his own shortcomings.

When his inferiority complex reached its peak, Jo Baekcheon ushered in an era of absolute tyranny. Thus, the Divine Cult, which had stabilized for a time, was thrown into yet another turbulent era.

It was in this era that the one absolute being who would later be revered as the Light began turning the wheel of fate bestowed upon him.
