The two men were heading toward the warehouse when they suddenly spun around, sensing an unexpected presence.

The Bright Demon Commander blinked in surprise. "Black Demon Commander?"

Entering through the Bright Demon Pavilion's main gate, the Black Demon Commander, So Gong, smiled and waved. "Yo!" he called out cheerfully.

"What brings you here without prior notice?" the Bright Demon Commander asked.

"Do I really need to give notice to pay you guys a visit? I felt lonely, so I dropped by."

The Bright Demon Commander chuckled. "Are you that free?"

"You know how it is. We've been pretty free lately."

The Black Demon Company was the Divine Cult's most unconventional military unit. Instead of focusing on practical combat, they specialized in assassination.

Originally, the Divine Cult did not have an assassination team because such missions were handled by the Guardian Court. Above all, it was strange for a religious organization to even entertain the idea of having such a force.

Nevertheless, the Black Demon Company was formed, and the reason was Jo Baekcheon.

"The Seven Clans are quiet, and lately, well..." So Gong licked his lips. "Anyway, I don't have anything to kill time with."

"Why don't you train instead?"

So Gong scratched his head. "Don't you get tired of always training? I thought we could have a drink if you're not busy. Are you very busy?"

The Vice Commander cut in before the Bright Demon Commander could answer, "Yes, he is very busy."

So Gong pouted. "He doesn't look busy to me."

The Vice Commander fought to keep his composure. "The Bright Demon Company also handles organizing confidential operational matters. Just the paperwork alone..."

"Every unit does that."

"Of course, that's true, but this time, in particular..."

"If he's really that busy, why don't you help him out?"

"......" The Vice Commander was rendered speechless.

The Bright Demon Commander shook his head with a smile. "Don't pick on my innocent subordinate. He's always helping me."

"Kahaha!" So Gong burst into hearty laughter.

"Sharing a drink isn't a difficult request," the Bright Demon Commander added. "Just wait a little. I need to take care of one more issue before I can drink with you."

The Vice Commander sighed inwardly. He honestly disliked the Black Demon Commander.

The Black Demon Company was a new organization formed to replace the dissolved Blood Demon Company. Naturally, the cult members did not view them favorably, including him and his fellow vice commanders.

The Bright Demon Commander was the only person who treated So Gong well. The Vice Commander respected his superior's amiable character, but wished he would keep his distance from the Black Demon Commander.

*Now is a very bad time to be attracting unnecessary glares.*

So Gong stretched and said, "Should I wait in your office?"

"Yes..." the Bright Demon Commander began, but changed his mind when he stared intently at So Gong.

So Gong blinked in confusion. "...Yes? Is there something on my face?"

"Do you want to see him too?"

"Huh? See who?"

"A senior warrior from our main force was murdered. I'm on my way to see the culprit right now."

"Ah! Are you talking about Myeong Han?"

"So you remember."

So Gong tilted his head slightly and stroked his chin. "Is that really necessary?"

"......"

"Have you heard? The Discipline Hall Deputy Director got promoted to Director. He's the guy who's intensely jealous of you, right? Do you think he'll let you see the criminal if you ask nicely?"

The Bright Demon Commander glanced at his Vice Commander.

The Vice Commander cleared his throat, "Ahem!"

Chuckling, the Bright Demon Commander gestured toward the warehouse with his chin. "That's why we locked him up in the warehouse."

"Oho? Did our always lawful Bright Demon Commander actually break the Cult's rules?"

"Sometimes, we just can't help wanting to stray from the straight and narrow, right?"

The Vice Commander wanted to say that wasn't it, but chose silence instead. He understood his superior's feelings.

So Gong smiled. "Good, good. The world's going crazy, and it's tiring to be the only one talking about rules. When you're in a position to take care of kids, you need to know how to be flexible."

"Is that so?"

"Now you seem human."

The Vice Commander bit his lip, feeling a pointless surge of anger. 

Whether he knew his subordinate's thoughts or not, the Bright Demon Commander repeated, "Let's go see him together."

"Is it okay to show him to me?" So Gong said, but quickly fell in step beside him.

The Bright Demon Commander said seriously, "Something's bothering me about all this. I'd like a second opinion."

"That's great!" So Gong exclaimed, overjoyed by the invitation. 

However, to the Vice Commander, So Gong's expression was unsettling.

Two warriors guarded the warehouse entrance. 

"Open the gate," the Vice Commander ordered.

"Yes, sir."

KUGUGUNG!

The warehouse stored the unit's various supplies, so it was bound to be large. It opened with a magnificent sound, revealing a wider space than expected.

So Gong whistled. "It's much bigger than our unit's warehouse."

"Is that so?"

The three men entered the warehouse. Their eyes fell upon a young man tied to a central pillar.

"Is that the guy?"

"Yes, that's him."

The young man was bound tightly with thick ropes. He had a thin build and was slumped over as if he had no energy left. 

Even so, he lifted his head to glare at the three men, a cold, venomous killing intent surfacing on his exhausted face.

The Bright Demon Commander approached the young man, their gazes meeting.

WHOOSH!

The Bright Demon Commander's martial arts were outstanding, even among his peers. The dignity of an organization's elite exuded from him, and he made no effort to hide it.

Pressured by that aura, the young man's face gradually turned pale.

The Bright Demon Commander asked, "What is your name?"

The young man didn't answer. He simply glared at the Bright Demon Commander with venom-filled eyes.

The Vice Commander shouted in a low voice, "Answer!"

Only then did the young man reply, "Yoo Yisang."

His voice was hoarse. He was malnourished and hadn't had a sip of water for three days, so his thoughts were hazy and his life hanging by a thread. Still, he gritted his teeth and maintained eye contact, displaying his remarkable mental fortitude.

The Bright Demon Commander slowly crouched down.

"......" Yoo Yisang's eyes wavered for a moment.

The Bright Demon Commander introduced himself, "I am Do Heon, the Bright Demon Commander of the Divine Cult."

"......"

"I heard you killed one of our warriors."

"......"

"Your inner arts have just barely surpassed the third-rate level. Even with outstanding talent, he wasn't an opponent you could have defeated... and yet, you killed him."

Yoo Yisang remained silent, glaring fiercely at the man before him.

Do Heon continued calmly, "Whether it was a surprise attack or something else, this is no ordinary matter. However, what I want to know isn't how you killed Myeong Han."

"......"

"I'm sure you clearly felt the gap between you and your opponent, yet you tried to kill him. I would've understood if it had been a desperate situation with no way out, but was it really like that?"

"......"

"You could've run away but you drew your sword instead."

"......"

"I want to know why you drew your sword even though you could have run. Why did you choose to kill Myeong Han out of all the warriors?"

Yoo Yisang still didn't answer, only casting an even more murderous glare at the Bright Demon Commander.

The Vice Commander, Hwang Museok, shouted, "Answer him!"

Yoo Yisang glanced at Hwang Museok before turning his gaze back to Do Heon.

Do Heon nodded wordlessly.

Glaring at Do Heon, Yoo Yisang growled, "All of you."

"What do you mean?"

"Not just him. If I had the ability, I would've tried to slaughter every single member of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult."

Killing intent filled Hwang Museok's eyes. "How dare you!"

"Enough." Do Heon stopped Hwang Museok by raising a hand. "That isn't the answer I want."

"......"

"Why did you kill Myeong Han?"

The rims of Yoo Yisang's eyes turned red, but no tears fell. "That bastard killed my younger sister."

"...Your sister?"

"He killed the man who was to be my brother-in-law, violated my sister in front of him, and then killed her."

...!!

"You're all demons."

Do Heon's face contorted.

A flustered Hwang Museok shouted, "Shut up! Myeong Han was an honorable man! Are you trying to avoid execution with such ridiculous lies?!"

Feeling a sudden prickling on his neck, Hwang Museok flinched and turned his head.

So Gong was smiling, but it didn't reach his eyes. His expression had become even more distorted than Do Heon's. "Your superior is talking."

"...!"

"Don't interrupt him so rudely."

Hwang Museok gulped as goosebumps rose all over his skin at the sound of So Gong's voice. This wasn't the debauched and troublesome man he knew.

Do Heon asked gravely, "Is that true?"

Yoo Yisang closed his mouth again. The deep-seated resentment that filled his eyes served as his answer.

Do Heon slowly straightened.

Hwang Museok felt a chill run down his spine. Just by looking at his superior's back, he could tell how enraged he was.

"Hwang Museok."

Do Heon used no title. Just the three cold syllables of his name made Hwang Museok's face instinctively harden. "Yes, Commander?"

"Gather all the warriors at the training ground."

"...Yes, sir."

Hwang Museok bowed his head deeply and left the warehouse.

Do Heon said to Yoo Yisang, "Even if that's the truth, I cannot guarantee your life."

Yoo Yisang gave a twisted, malicious smile. His sneer seemed to contain all the dark emotions a human could harbor, so much so that anyone who saw it could be swept away by its desperation.

Do Heon looked at Yoo Yisang with a complicated gaze, then reached out and touched the nape of his neck lightly. 

Yoo Yisang lost consciousness, his eyes fluttering closed.

Do Heon sighed. "Truly..."

So Gong, who was quietly watching him, remarked casually, "Hwang Museok probably didn't know."

"...Right."

"Did you already suspect something?"

"......"

"I thought so. For you, who cares so much for your warriors, to ask for the reason first, I figured there must be something to it."

Do Heon lamented, "Stagnant water is bound to rot. The atmosphere among the warriors has been strange lately, and I've been hearing some bad rumors here and there. Still, I never thought it would come to this..."

So Gong shook his head. "There are guys like that everywhere. By your logic, there shouldn't be a single bad person among martial artists, but reality isn't like that."

"......"

"I'm sorry. I don't have anything else to say."

Do Heon sighed.

So Gong folded his arms. Do Heon was a man of rare character in the mess the Divine Cult had become. His thoughts must have been in turmoil. "So what are you gonna do with that guy? Are you really gonna kill him?" he asked.

"......"

"I knew you wouldn't."

"Whatever the reason, this man killed a member of our Cult."

"But you don't want to kill him."

"I am the Bright Demon Commander."

"To me, you're just Do Heon."

Do Heon closed his mouth.

So Gong watched him silently, then shrugged. "Should I take him?"

"What?"

"I think I know why you called me over. If this guy didn't have a story, you were probably going to ask me to uncover the method he used to kill your man."

"But there was a story, and now dealing with him has become difficult."

"I can't accept him as a warrior if he has no talent, and he wouldn't want that either."

"There will be talk. Looking at this guy's temperament, he could be dangerous."

"If you entrust him to me, then from that point on, it's no longer your concern."

"What a disappointing thing to say! I'm saying this to lessen your burden."
