Lee Cheonsang looked down at the other Secret Manual. Unlike the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art, which had a blank cover, this booklet had eerie red letters written on it.

"The Adamant Blood Claws?" he read out loud.

Do Heon nodded. "Do you know of the Blood Hand Divine Demon?"

"I've heard of him."

"I thought you would have. He was quite a famous figure."

"Famous in a bad way, wasn't he?"

"That's right."

Two years ago, the Black Evil Sword Sect, a prestigious clan among the unorthodox sects, was annihilated. The culprit was a single martial artist, the Blood Hand Divine Demon.

Known only by his alias, he was a madman. He could speak, but communication was impossible. His eyes were always bloodshot. His appearance was so filthy that even beggars would be appalled. He was literally a Madman who wandered the streets.

No one paid him any mind. Although he was clearly insane, he had never harmed anyone.

The world was horrified when he stormed the Black Evil Sword Sect, though. In just two hours, the sect, renowned as a giant of the unorthodox sects, was shattered. No, it would be better to say it was erased. He killed everyone, from the gate's martial artists to the servants, maids, and even the young children.

He didn't leave a single dog alive; this was no metaphor. Every living thing within the walls of the Black Evil Sword Sect, including animals and blades of grass, was burned to the ground. It was a horror beyond all bounds.

Immediately after, the Seven Great Demonic Clans dispatched demonic practitioners to capture the Blood Hand Divine Demon, but they never returned.

In the end, the Seven Great Demonic Clans contacted the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Headquarters. It took three of the Ten Great Demon Kings to finally kill the Blood Hand Divine Demon. They had tried to capture him, but they had no choice but to kill him when they realized he couldn't be restrained.

The fight was so brutal that two of the three Demon Kings had to recuperate for over half a year. The martial arts of the Blood Hand Divine Demon were just that powerful and cruel.

"The Secret Manual for the Adamant Blood Claws was found in his dead grasp. It seems he insisted on carrying the Secret Manual with him, even in that state."

"If the Blood Hand Divine Demon was that strong, one would think the higher-ups would have learned it."

Do Heon shook his head. "The Adamant Blood Claws is a demonic art that can't be learned with a sound body."

"Why is that?"

"Because it tampers with the upper qi center."

A glint appeared in Lee Cheonsang's eyes. There were three qi centers in the human body. The lower qi center was the foundation of the physical body, the middle qi center the foundation of the heart, and the upper qi center the foundation of the mind. The upper qi center was clearly involved with a person's mental and spiritual power.

"The extreme destructive power of the Adamant Blood Claws is unmatched by any other demonic art in the Divine Cult today. The only demonic art that could suppress it would be the Purple Lightning Demonic Martial Arts that the current Cult Leader is learning, and..."

"......"

"...The world's most powerful demonic art, the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, which the previous Cult Leaders learned."

"The Heavenly Demon Divine Art."

Lee Cheonsang felt his heart rate inexplicably rise the moment he heard the words 'Heavenly Demon Divine Art'.

*What's happening? Why am I reacting like this?*

He lightly pressed a hand to his chest.

Do Heon continued, "But the problem is that it tampers with the upper qi center to generate that extreme destructive power. The oral formula is also incredibly flawed. That's precisely why the Blood Hand Divine Demon went mad."

"I see." Lee Cheonsang tilted his head. "Are you telling me to go mad?"

"Haha! Of course not." Do Heon shook his head. "I said the oral formula was flawed, but if you exclude the part that tampers with the upper qi center, it contains the profound principles of unarmed combat arts."

"......"

"I'd like you to train your bare hands before you use a sword."

"Why is that?"

Do Heon smiled. "You'll understand once you fight."

"......"

"Well, that's one reason, but giving you the Secret Manual for the Adamant Blood Claws is..." Do Heon's expression grew complicated, as if finding it difficult to continue.

A brief silence fell. Lee Cheonsang waited calmly.

Finally, Do Heon's lips parted. "Just promise me this. Don't ever perform the Adamant Blood Claws until you're confident you can execute it properly."

"To gain that confidence, don't I have to train it constantly?"

"That's right. So I know very well how absurd what I'm saying right now sounds." Do Heon sighed. "I don't know. To be honest, that demonic art is worse than the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art. People don't touch the Vajra Yaksha despite its excellence, but the Adamant Blood Claws is literally one of the forbidden demonic arts."

"......"

"At least, in its current state."

"What's your point?"

"Can you fix it?"

Lee Cheonsang hesitated, taken aback by the sudden request. After a while, he shook his head. "I'm a total novice when it comes to martial arts."

"Of course I know that, but the reason I'm saying this is..."

"I can't be certain about the future."

"Hm?"

"I'll learn much more in time. However, there must be a reason why so many people haven't touched it."

"T-That's right."

"You seem worried that I might go mad while learning this and inflict a devastating blow to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. Of course, I won't do that. If I go mad, I won't be able to judge right from wrong, nor will I be able to learn about the world."

Do Heon's face brightened slightly. He believed the two martial arts would be of great use in the future, and now he had handed both to Lee Cheonsang. It was as if he had given him a part of his own future. He felt trust, a sense of repayment, and an even greater anticipation for Lee Cheonsang.

Lee Cheonsang declared, "I'll keep your words in mind. I won't touch it until I'm confident."

Do Heon sighed in relief. "Thank you for understanding my intentions."

In truth, his actions were exceedingly dangerous, like giving a child a razor-sharp sword. He had given that much of his heart to Lee Cheonsang, though, and Lee Cheonsang had given him the best possible answer in return.

His words and actions were one. Do Heon didn't doubt for a second that Lee Cheonsang would keep his promise.

"Memorize both demonic arts until you know them by heart. Read them and then burn them. I've also memorized the oral formulas for both."

It had taken him two full hours to return because he needed time to write them in the books himself.

Do Heon clapped his hands. "Now, since I've given you what I had to give, let's talk about the future."

"The future?"

"Will you join the Bright Demon Company?"

Lee Cheonsang's eyes deepened. While living in the Demonic Coliseum, he had learned a fair amount about the internal organizations and famous figures of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. He never forgot information he heard once.

Do Heon smiled. "The Bright Demon Company is..."

"I know it as one of the Divine Cult's martial units, an organization at the pinnacle of the Inner Court's combat forces."

"Hoho, you're making me blush."

"?" It was nothing new, but Lee Cheonsang couldn't understand why Do Heon would be blushing.

"Just as you said, the Bright Demon Company is one of the top-tier units the Divine Cult prides itself on. Your skills are very lacking right now, but if you learn under me, you'll grow quickly. Besides, you even have the Vajra Yaksha."

"......"

"Join me. It might be quite difficult, though."

Staring quietly at Do Heon, Lee Cheonsang spoke in a subdued voice. "If I'm under you, can I get rid of the Demonic Coliseum?"

The question might have drawn a laugh, but Do Heon didn't laugh. Instead, his expression turned serious. "Do you think the Demonic Coliseum is the only thing wrong with our Cult?"

"...?"

"The fact that a place like the Demonic Coliseum is thriving openly means our Cult is heading in the wrong direction."

"You mean there are many things to correct?"

Finally, Do Heon felt he was getting to the heart of the matter. "That's right. There are many things to correct."

"......"

"For you, for me, and for us to correct what's wrong, we must become stronger. We must grow in every aspect, and quickly."

"I see."

"I want you to join the Bright Demon Company."

If it had been anyone else, the good-natured Do Heon would have proceeded with the enlistment without asking. That was the natural thing to do. He constantly gave Lee Cheonsang a choice, though. He respected him and wished for him to see the consequences of his own decisions.

Lee Cheonsang's answer was negative. "It doesn't seem like a good idea."

"Why? Do you dislike us that much?"

"It's not a matter of liking or disliking. I understand that the Divine Cult's martial units are for hand-picked talents."

"That's why I want you to join."

"If my skills were up to par, it would be a different story, but if I join now, it'll only affect the warriors' morale."

"That's something for me to handle, and furthermore, something for you to handle."

"Isn't that dangerous?"

"Everything in the world is accompanied by risk."

"Are you okay with your warriors not following you when you must do the right thing?"

"......" For a moment, Do Heon's eyes wavered.

"I learned something from the merchant troupe and the Demonic Coliseum. A tightly-knit group is sensitive to the entry of outsiders, for better or for worse."

Do Heon's heart pounded. *...That's true.*

"What I have to handle is no big deal. The problem is you. If a rift forms between you and your subordinates because of me, it's like losing a hundred to gain one."

"......"

"That's why I believe I shouldn't join the Bright Demon Company. I'm not qualified right now anyway."

"Is that so?"

"However."

"...?"

"If you have your own thoughts on the matter, I'll accept the risks and join."

Do Heon's eyes widened. "Are you sure about that?"

"A creditor can only rest easy when the debtor is healthy. That's why I refused, but if it's a problem you can handle, there's no reason to take the long way around."

"...Hah."

"Can you handle it?"

"Hahaha!" Do Heon burst out laughing. "Of course, I can handle it. How could I call myself the Commander of the Bright Demon Company if I can't even handle one person like you?"

"Understood."

"You've chosen well, and thank you. For trusting me."

Do Heon stood up, his face slightly flushed. "It's getting late, so get some rest. You need to treat your wounds, too. We'll fill out the official enlistment papers in three days. I'll get everything ready by then."

"Understood."

"In the meantime, you do what you need to do. I'll come find you whenever my work is done, so if you have any questions about the demonic arts, feel free to ask."

"I'll do that."

"Rest well. I'll see you tomorrow."

"You rest as well."

Do Heon left the private room.

Lee Cheonsang stared blankly at the door, then turned his gaze down to the two Secret Manuals. Surprisingly, he wasn't focused on the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art, but the Adamant Blood Claws.

"...The upper qi center, huh?"
