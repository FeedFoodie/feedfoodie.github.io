<u>**Six months later**</u>

"Today's training is over," Hwang Museok declared.

Every Bright Demon warrior collapsed onto the training ground floor.

The Divine Cult's combat units were driven warriors. It meant something when the Bright Demon martial artists, who competed for the top spot among them, showed such exhaustion.

Hwang Museok didn't blame the fallen warriors. Training intensity had climbed steadily over a number of months. Today's session drained even him, the Vice Commander.

"First Squad Leader."

"Gasp, gasp, yes?"

"Once the men recover, send them all to their quarters. No training for the next three days."

The First Squad Leader's eyes widened. "Three whole days?"

"Commander's orders."

"I-I see."

The Bright Demon Company lived by a simple creed: one drop of sweat in practice replaced a bucket of blood in real battle. No matter how brutal the training, they always resumed the next day. The intensity might decrease, but they rarely got a day off.

The Bright Demon Company's training schedule had changed significantly in those months. Quality and quantity increased, but they now took mandatory rest after extreme training, even with their powerful recovery abilities. From the warriors' perspective, it was welcome but puzzling.

And now, three days?

"You all worked hard today."

The Group Leaders bowed their heads. ""Thank you!""

After receiving their greetings, Hwang Museok headed for the office.

"Commander, it's me, Vice Commander Hwang."

"Come in."

Hwang Museok opened the door and entered. Do Heon stood with his back turned, looking out the window.

Hwang Museok bowed his head. "Training has concluded."

"Good work." Do Heon turned around.

For a moment, Hwang Museok's eyes wavered.

*He's changed.*

Do Heon possessed calm, settled qi. His eyes were so deep their bottom couldn't be seen.

Originally, Do Heon had a gentle disposition, unusual for a demonic practitioner. Through brutal life in the Divine Cult, that gentleness had transformed into something ideal. A superior who was brave, held no grudges, valued rules, and cared for his subordinates.

However, over the past six months, Do Heon's qi had changed again. His gentle disposition had grown even softer, yet he radiated a more unapproachable spirit.

*He's gotten stronger.*

A person with a goal develops quickly, and it seemed Do Heon had found such a goal. Having served him for years, Hwang Museok could tell without being told.

"Are you alright?"

"Of course."

"As you should be."

His reaction was subtly different too. In the past, he would've said something like, 'Take it easy,' or 'Thanks to you, I had an easy day today.'

Do Heon turned back to the window. "It's already late afternoon."

"......"

"Wash up and come back. There's somewhere you need to go with me."

Hwang Museok tilted his head. "The Commanders' Meeting is starting soon, though?"

Tonight was the regular friendly gathering for commanders of the Divine Cult's martial units. Though called a meeting, it was practically a drinking party. Not mandated by higher-ups, but an exclusive event for the martial unit leaders, passed down through long tradition.

Do Heon shook his head. "There won't be any more Commanders' Meetings from now on."

"What?! N-No more meetings?"

"The higher-ups issued a ban. They said instead of us commanders gathering for useless plotting, we should use that time to train our men."

Vivid anger flashed across Hwang Museok's face. "Useless plotting? How could that possibly be considered plotting?! Are the higher-ups even in their right minds...?!"

"Vice Commander Hwang."

"......"

"Be careful. If you spit out whatever you want just because you're angry, you could be taken away without anyone knowing."

Hwang Museok clenched his teeth. "I misspoke. I apologize."

Do Heon let out a small laugh. "I actually prefer it. Going to that quarterly drinking party was quite tiring. The reason they gave is infuriating, but in the end, it worked out well for me."

"Commander. Forgive my impertinence, but the Commanders' Meeting is where the glorious leaders of the Divine Cult's martial units build camaraderie. If even this is eliminated, what demonic practitioner in our Cult would aspire to lead one of the Six Companies?"

"Does aspiration come from that kind of camaraderie?"

"......"

"It's enough to do one's best in one's position. So what if the demonic practitioners don't aspire to our positions? Are we really that special?"

"B-But, Commander!"

"We just need to show them through our actions. In the end, that is the truth." Do Heon shook his head. "If we dislike the behavior of the higher-ups, shouldn't we go a different way? The meeting of the Six Commanders, if that too is simple camaraderie, there's no need to set a specific schedule for it."

Hwang Museok was bewildered. *How...*

The Commander still possessed pride in leading the Bright Demon Company and self-respect as the Bright Demon Commander. However, he was no longer looking only at the Bright Demon Company. The Commander who had always sought preservation of the Bright Demon Company while being swept up in surrounding affairs now seemed to be looking at a wider world.

Whether that would be good or bad for the Bright Demon Company, no one knew.

Hwang Museok bowed his head. "My remarks were out of line."

"Not at all. Everyone's thoughts are different. If something's wrong, you fix it. If something's different, you adjust to it. That's all. Isn't that right?"

"I'm sorry."

Do Heon smiled. "That's enough. Hurry and wash up. We'll eat after we've taken care of our business."

"Understood."

So Gong raised his hand. "Has our stiff Commander Do arrived?"

Hwang Museok's face hardened unconsciously.

Do Heon nodded. "Commander So."

"I hear an order is coming down from the higher-ups soon. You must need time to prepare this and that, so how did you manage to find the time?"

Do Heon shook his head. "If you do your usual work well, you can always make time."

Hwang Museok's eyes widened. An order from the higher-ups? This was something he knew nothing about.

"Wahahaha! As expected, the solemn and serious Commander Do is on a different level from a loafer like me."

"Thanks for the compliment."

So Gong twisted his body like a tragic heroine and bowed his head. "Reacting like that hurts my feelings, you know?"

"Don't be ridiculous."

"Kekeke." When he lifted his head, So Gong's face was as playful as ever. "Hey, Vice Commander Hwang's here too? Have you been well?"

Hwang Museok bowed his head. "Greetings, Black Demon Commander."

"That displeased tone of yours is the same as ever."

Hwang Museok flinched.

Do Heon waved a hand. "I told you not to bother my precious subordinate."

"How is this bothering him? I'm just stating a fact." So Gong kept chuckling, finding something immensely amusing.

Do Heon asked, "When did they say it would start?"

"They said it's starting soon."

"I see..." Do Heon's eyes deepened.

So Gong waved his hand. "Looks like you have someone you need to watch closely. I'll watch from a distance, so observe them well."

With those words, So Gong left.

Hwang Museok cautiously approached Do Heon's side. "Commander."

"Speak."

"Why are we here...?"

"Why? Did we come somewhere we shouldn't have?"

"N-No, that's not it, but..." Hwang Museok looked down at the large training ground below. Though called a training ground, it was closer to an unmanaged dirt field. It was four or five times wider than the Bright Demon Company's training ground and surrounded by countless seats.

It looked like a dog fighting arena, and in fact, this place was a dog fighting arena. Except real dogs didn't fight each other here.

*To think the Commander would come to the Demonic Colseum.*

The Demonic Colosseum, located in the outer city, was built in the third year of current Cult Leader Jo Baekcheon's reign. It served as a sort of gambling den and amusement place. They would take low-level or criminal demonic practitioners, make them fight, and have people bet money or valuables on the predicted winner.

The Divine Cult's discipline was incredibly strict. The people of the world called the Divine Cult the Demonic Cult and shunned it as a group teeming with fiends who would go mad if they didn't see blood for a single day, but that wasn't the truth.

Because they had to manage demonic practitioners with belligerent tendencies, the laws and regulations had to be all the more strict. Naturally, creating such a gambling den, a fighting pit where demonic practitioners disregarded life and death, was absurd. There were many other ways to relieve fatigue from strict discipline and dark desires stemming from their belligerent instincts.

In fact, when the Demonic Colosseum was being established, many demonic practitioners expressed opposition and concern, but Jo Baekcheon executed them all and built the Demonic Colosseum anyway.

And now, the Demonic Colosseum had become one of the most famous landmarks in the Divine Cult.

*A place for a daily festival of blood.*

Hwang Museok looked at Do Heon. Do Heon's eyes were serious. That made it even harder for Hwang Museok to understand.

*He hates the Demonic Colosseum more than anyone.*

Given Do Heon's personality, it was impossible for him to like the Demonic Colosseum. He was so faithful to higher-up orders, yet he couldn't hold back his loathing remarks whenever the topic of the Demonic Colosseum came up.

Hwang Museok, who had been quietly watching Do Heon, asked casually, "Did the higher-ups issue an order to our company?"

"They did," Do Heon replied. He didn't even turn his head toward Hwang Museok. He just looked down at the Demonic Colosseum with cautious eyes.

Hwang Museok's eyes deepened. "...I see."

Originally, orders from higher-ups or news of current events came to him first. He was the one who delivered those orders and information. However, now, the Commander knew something he hadn't heard.

The meaning was clear. Do Heon intended to control information going to the Vice Commander and hear about surrounding situations himself.

He didn't know if that was truly Do Heon's intention, but interpreted differently, it could mean he no longer trusted his Vice Commander.

*It started back then.*

Hwang Museok recalled the image of Do Heon from six months ago, cutting down and killing everyone involved with the First Squad Myeong Han matter. Do Heon had been strict with his subordinate warriors before, but he'd never drawn his sword on them, yet that man had cut down warriors with genuine intent.

*The Commander started changing from that moment on.*

It was subtle, little by little, but he had definitely changed.

*...Is it inevitable?*

During that incident, the fault lay with the Group Leader who failed to manage his warriors, and also with him, the Vice Commander who failed to manage his Group Leader. Do Heon must have thought that the greatest responsibility lay with himself, for failing to properly manage his men.

"......" Hwang Museok bit his lip. In truth, he was the one who had led the First Squad and this team at the time. If one were to assign blame, he, the field guard captain, also deserved punishment.

However, he wasn't punished. Instead, afterward, for some reason, he felt as if Do Heon had put up a wall between them.

He felt hurt. It would've been easier if he'd been cursed at and beaten half to death.

Hwang Museok looked at Do Heon. *I would lay down my life for our company.* *Please, just remember that.*

"They're here," Do Heon suddenly whispered.

Hwang Museok turned his head toward the Demonic Colosseum.

"WAAAAAAH!!"

Along with the audience's cheers, thick demonic qi rose up. The audience, like the demonic gladiators who entered the fighting pit, was also mostly composed of demonic practitioners.

Hwang Museok's eyebrow twitched. *What is this?*

His eyes swept over the twenty or so demonic practitioners inside the Demonic Colosseum, split into two sides.

*Wasn't this supposed to be a single company fight?*

Do Heon opened his mouth. "Vice Commander Hwang."

"Yes, Commander?"

Do Heon handed a heavy wallet to Hwang Museok. "Bet everything in here on Squad B."

"......"

*Squad B...?*

Hwang Museok looked at the ten demonic practitioners who had emerged from the left corridor of the Demonic Colosseum, marked with the character 'B'. Perhaps because demonic qi was raging all around, it was difficult to sense their individual auras. However, every one of them had a look of clear tension on their faces.

In contrast, the ten demonic practitioners on the right were full of fighting spirit. They were Squad A. Similarly, he couldn't tell their auras, but he could guess the outcome just by looking at their expressions.

"Are you planning to bet on Squad B?"

"......"

"...Understood."

Hwang Museok secured the wallet, bowed his head, and disappeared.

A smile appeared on Do Heon's face. His eyes were fixed on a single young man at the back of Squad B.

"As expected, you survived."

There was only one man among the demonic practitioners here who was completely expressionless.

It was none other than Lee Cheonsang, holding a single sword.
