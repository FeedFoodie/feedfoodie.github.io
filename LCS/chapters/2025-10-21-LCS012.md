WHOOOOSH!

The wind blew, carrying heat with it.

Lee Cheonsang's eyes gleamed. *He's different from his usual self.*

Do Heon had always shown a gentle and benevolent side until now, but his entire demeanor had changed in an instant. His eyes glowed red. Strange, shimmering smoke, both red and dark, poured from his back.

It was strange. His hand holding the dao felt heavy. His legs wouldn't move easily, as if shackled.

*Ah, this is the pressure from his inner qi.*

Lee Cheonsang finally understood why his body felt so heavy. The formless energy pouring from Do Heon already bound his entire body.

It wasn't just him, either. The moment Do Heon released his energy, the entire area fell under his will. Hundreds of invisible arms seemed to feel out the area, telling him how the air flowed and where everything was.

*So this was possible too.*

It was the first time he had seen something like this. So this was the high-level ability of a true Master to handle qi. It was a terrifying state where one could render an opponent immobile without lifting a single finger.

*If he were to reach an even higher state than this...*

If Do Heon could handle a much larger amount of qi, it seemed he could kill people with just his qi and his gaze.

"What's wrong?" Do Heon's voice was much rougher than before. "You're not so weak that you can't move from just this, are you?"

Lee Cheonsang closed his eyes.

WOOOOONG!

The energy of the True Demonic Art, dormant in his qi center, slowly began to stir.

The corner of Do Heon's mouth lifted slightly. *As expected.*

Among all the martial arts in the world, no system had a clearer hierarchy than demonic art.

Hierarchy meant a strict food chain. One who possessed a lower-tier demonic art couldn't easily challenge someone with a higher-tier one. Even if one's skill was a level higher, the fight would be close at best if the opponent's demonic art was superior.

Despite learning only the basic True Demonic Art, Lee Cheonsang flared his energy in front of him. He did this even with such a clear difference in skill.

*As expected, you don't disappoint.*

WHOOSH! WHOOOOOSH!

The wind rustling through the bamboo suddenly shot skyward as if throwing a tantrum. It couldn't easily pass between the two men.

Lee Cheonsang opened his eyes and began his attack.

PAK!

He kicked lightly off the ground and thrust his blade toward Do Heon's chest.

Do Heon's expression turned peculiar. *Well, well.*

Blades were weapons more suited for slashing than stabbing, though of course, stabbing wasn't impossible depending on its shape. In a real battle, one must freely slash and stab to be considered a Master. Even so, he chose to thrust with his first strike while holding a long blade designed for slashing.

SWOOSH!

Do Heon's body moved diagonally. Lee Cheonsang's blade pierced empty air.

Suddenly, surprise flickered in Do Heon's eyes. Lee Cheonsang had vanished!

*Lower left.*

His reflexes and qi senses were far superior to Lee Cheonsang's. There was no way he wouldn't know how Lee Cheonsang had moved.

*He immediately lowered his stance to reduce his speed and aimed for the lower body...*

Such a movement would put severe strain on the joints. It wasn't as easy as it sounded. Even if one was prepared for the strain, it couldn't be used easily.

PWAK!

Lee Cheonsang's left palm struck Do Heon's knee. Do Heon's body swayed, but that was all.

SWISH!

Do Heon's fist plunged down from above with tremendous force.

Lee Cheonsang's body moved again.

TING! KUNG!

Do Heon's fist smashed into the ground.

SHWAAAK!

Lee Cheonsang had already closed in behind Do Heon. The moment the fist was thrown, he had deflected it with the back of his blade, using the force to rotate. If the area of contact had been larger, the blade would have broken. If it had been smaller, he couldn't have received that much force.

It was an exquisite movement. One couldn't even attempt it without accurately calculating the density of the force in the fist.

Do Heon's eyes shone brilliantly.

PABABABAK!

A fist, knee, and blade attacked Do Heon's back. None of Lee Cheonsang's attacks landed an effective blow, though. Even Do Heon's clothes were fine, merely torn a little by the blade.

Power gathered in Do Heon's right foot.

BWOOOONG!

He pivoted on his right foot, striking upwards towards the rear. It was a truly menacing strike. A few strands of Lee Cheonsang's hair, who had dodged diagonally, were caught in the fierce wind of the foot technique and turned to dust.

Lee Cheonsang didn't retreat, though. He accurately dodged the attack that would have taken his head off if he had shown even the slightest error. He then swung with both hands without any fear.

SHUK!

A gruesome sound echoed. Lee Cheonsang, who had swung his blade with all his might, immediately retreated.

"...Hmph."

Do Heon looked down at his chest.

"You cut me."

He had cut him. His skin was without a single scratch. Lee Cheonsang had actually swung with enough force to cut him in two, but only the hem of his clothes had been cut. This wasn't because Do Heon had trained in external arts. His mastery of inner arts was so profound that demonic qi enveloped his entire body.

The same was true for the clothes he wore. Since his control over them wasn't as strong as over his flesh, though, they had been cut.

Even so, to have cut the clothes of the Bright Demon Commander Do Heon, one of the best in the Inner Court combat units, with a single blade was an incredible feat.

Do Heon, who had been gazing blankly at his exposed chest, raised his head to look at Lee Cheonsang. Far in the distance, Lee Cheonsang was glaring at him with even deeper eyes.

"Why didn't you press the attack?"

"......"

"I was defenseless just now, wasn't I?"

Lee Cheonsang didn't answer. It seemed he couldn't hear him properly. His eyes, now as deep as an abyss, fiercely scanned Do Heon's body from head to toe.

He was searching for a weakness. A place where the blade could connect, where a fist could land, a place where he could finally deliver a proper blow.

*He's serious.*

Do Heon felt a sense of astonishment, even as he was inwardly dumbfounded.

*He seriously intends to cut me.*

With this level of difference in skill, it was normal for one's spirit to break first. It was impossible to cut him.

Lee Cheonsang was different, though. He couldn't feel emotions properly, but that also meant he didn't know how to give up. He was like a heavy carriage that was difficult to get rolling, but once it started, it would never stop. Once he decided to do something, he did it.

*Even in the face of this pressure.*

Even at this very moment, Do Heon's demonic qi was relentlessly binding Lee Cheonsang's body. It wasn't a physical binding. It was an energy that made him feel as if he were bound. That made it more terrifying because it delved into the spirit to draw out fear and despair.

*It's not working.* Do Heon's expression grew serious. *At my level, even if I were to fully unleash my demonic art, I can't break his spirit.*

If one thought about victory or defeat, it was a meaningless fight. What Do Heon wanted to see from Lee Cheonsang wasn't the level his martial arts had reached. It was how long he could endure, but even facing this murderous pressure was of no use.

*This is interesting. Let's see what kind of moves you'll try now.*

He lowered his stance. It was a stance clearly different from before.

PAAK!

In that instant, Lee Cheonsang charged at Do Heon again. His speed was even faster than before.

A blood-red light shot from Do Heon's eyes.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

*Amazing!*

Lee Cheonsang's swordsmanship was astonishing. There was no form or formality to be found. It was only natural. He had never learned a blade technique in the first place.

As he parried all of Lee Cheonsang's strikes with his two hands filled with demonic qi, Do Heon was constantly amazed.

*Every single one aims for a vital point...!*

Each and every strike aimed for a vital point with such natural ease. When blocked, he used the rebounding force to strike down with even greater power. He didn't even think of steadying his wavering body as he aimed for a vital point again. It was a chillingly natural combination, as if he had intended to move that way from the start.

SCREEECH!

The blade and fist crossed, making a sharp metallic sound. The blade was getting chipped. Although it was imbued with the demonic qi of the True Demonic Art, Do Heon's fist contained a high-level demonic art that not just anyone in the Divine Cult could learn.

THUD!

The tip of Lee Cheonsang's foot struck Do Heon's side. It was a brutal strike aimed at the inside of the lowest rib. If it had landed properly, the bone would have broken and his internal organs would have been damaged.

Of course, it was of little use against Do Heon. In fact, he had essentially allowed it to happen.

CLANG! SCREECH!

A clash of blade and fist. Unlike Do Heon's unwavering demonic qi, Lee Cheonsang's demonic qi was gradually becoming unstable. That was to be expected; just mastering the True Demonic Art to this level in half a year was worthy of high praise. On top of that, this swordsmanship and movement naturally aimed for vital points.

A smile formed on Do Heon's lips. *This is enough.*

Lee Cheonsang's energy was fading rapidly. He was almost out of qi.

Do Heon lightly pushed his blade aside. Lee Cheonsang's body was pushed back along with his blade.

*To reach this level in just half a year, he's a talent you could present anywhere without shame. As expected... huh?*

Lee Cheonsang, who had been pushed back, charged forward once again.

*Again?* Do Heon laughed inwardly. *Single-mindedness, that's good too.*

If this continued, though, he would never give up. The results of the duel were more than sufficient. It was time to end the fight.

WHOOSH!

Do Heon's hand moved at a speed incomparable to before. Before Lee Cheonsang could even swing, the blade was caught in his hand.

"Stop..."

FLINCH!

"...?!"

Do Heon's expression changed drastically. Veins bulged on the arms of Lee Cheonsang, who was gripping the hilt with both hands.

TWITCH! TWITCH!

*What is this?*

The blade touched his palm. That blade, once held fast and unmoving, was slowly, slowly, descending.

*...Could it be?*

Do Heon looked at Lee Cheonsang's face. Lee Cheonsang's eyes had become so deep, it was like looking into the sea hundreds of fathoms below. An unknown darkness that even the brilliant sun couldn't reach. It was an abyss of unfathomable depth, something that even the battle-hardened Do Heon had never seen before.

CHILLS!

Goosebumps erupted all over his body. For a moment, his demonic qi wavered. At the same time, Lee Cheonsang's upper body moved swiftly downwards.

SHRRRRK! KRAAANG!

Bright red drops of blood scattered into the air. Behind them, fragments of the broken blade caught the moonlight, turning into starlight on the ground.

PAAAAK! PWAK!

Do Heon's gaze took on a grave light.

"Gasp. Gasp."

Lee Cheonsang's breathing was extremely ragged. Despite that, his gaze was still venomous. Glaring at his target with those incomparably murderous eyes, the face of Lee Cheonsang, who had grabbed Do Heon's neck with his torn hand, was that of an expressionless demon.

DRIP.

A drop of sweat trickled down Do Heon's forehead as Lee Cheonsang's left hand shot towards his eyes.

THUD!

In that instant, Lee Cheonsang's body collapsed. Do Heon's hand had struck his paralysis acupoint like lightning.

The duel ended like that.

"......" Do Heon, who had been staring blankly down at Lee Cheonsang, suddenly turned his gaze to his right hand. There was a deep wound on his palm, cut by the blade.

Even though his demonic qi had wavered, it was only for a moment. Even aiming for that opening, someone at Lee Cheonsang's level shouldn't have been able to leave a single scratch.

He slowly clenched his fist. The tightly clenched fist was trembling faintly. The sharp pain rose from his palm. More than the pain, though, it was Lee Cheonsang's abyss-like gaze from a moment ago that stimulated his nerves.

As he looked down at Lee Cheonsang, there was no longer any pure goodwill. "What in the world are you?"
