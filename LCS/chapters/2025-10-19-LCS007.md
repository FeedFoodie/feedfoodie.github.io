"WAAAAAAAH!!"

A thunderous roar erupted from the crowd. The Demonic Coliseum's atmosphere burned so intensely that even masters with heightened senses couldn't hear the person beside them.

DUNG! DUNG! DUNG!

War drums echoed through the arena, signaling the start of the battle.

"Kill them! Kill every last one of those bastards!"

"I bet ten taels on you! You'd better not lose, or you're dead!"

"Fuck! It's been a while since I've seen some blood!"

"Ugh, I think I'm going crazy!"

Pure madness swirled through the arena like a living thing. Could blood, violence, struggle, and murder truly be humanity's primal desires?

The moment those drums thundered, demonic qi erupted from the crowd in doubled concentration. A storm of malevolent energy raged through the coliseum, transforming it into a living hell.

Even though the qi was low-grade, such massive amounts gathered together created crushing density. Even a peak master would struggle to breathe.

Within that fanatical atmosphere, the group battle between Demonic Coliseum Squad A and Squad B finally began.

Do Heon's eyes tracked Lee Cheonsang across the battlefield. The demonic gladiators of Squad B trembled with excitement, tension, fear, and strange yearning. Only Lee Cheonsang walked forward step by step, his face utterly indifferent. 

The contrast created an eerie dissonance.

*He's changed tremendously. He spent the last half-year as if it were more than five years, living each day to the fullest.*

Lee Cheonsang had transformed, yet somehow remained unchanged. That expression. That gaze.

Do Heon watched Squad A and Squad B slowly approach each other, recalling Lee Cheonsang's words.

\> "I will go and see how different it is."

*Damn it, I still can't understand why you insisted on starting from there. Still, it's a relief you've held on as I expected.*

Looking back, Do Heon had no real reason to help Lee Cheonsang.

*Is it because his lower and upper qi centers are wide open? Or is this the peculiarity of the empty middle qi center?*

The Divine Cult had countless people. Anyone could find individuals more unique than Lee Cheonsang if they looked hard enough, so why did his eyes keep being drawn to him? Was it because he'd gained enlightenment from their conversation?

*I don't know, and I don't want to know right now.*

Do Heon's eyes glinted with razor sharpness.

*The First Corps will see achievement.*

For a demonic gladiator, achievement meant survival. It wasn't that the strong survived, but that those who survived became strong.

*Prove your worth as one of the strong.*

Squad A and Squad B clashed with bone-crushing force. A Squad A demonic gladiator brought his axe down on a Squad B fighter's head. The brutal crack echoed through the arena as the gladiator's skull split in half. Blood and brain matter gushed out, soaking the dirt field and announcing the battle's true beginning.

"UWAAAAAAH!!"

As blood spurted and a man died, the spectators' cheers doubled in volume.

Jang Oh of Squad A was the one who had drawn first blood. He smiled with satisfaction and swept his gaze over the Squad B demonic gladiators.

Terror replaced the excitement on Squad B's faces. Their morale had shattered in a single devastating blow.

*How boring.*

Jang Oh licked his lips. Boring, but that was good too.

*It's been a while since I've had a proper killing spree.*

For two years, Jang Oh had fought brutal battles to finally climb to Squad A. After the individual matches, you entered the group system starting from Squad D. Survive the life-and-death duels among Squad D members, move to Squad C. Win against Squad C members, become Squad B. Win among Squad B members, and the victorious squad earned the right to fight Squad A.

Each match lasted fifteen minutes. It ended when a squad had fewer surviving warriors than its opponent, or faced complete annihilation. 

The survivors of a defeated Squad A returned to Squad D.

Squad A's skill naturally overwhelmed all other squads' martial power. It was only natural, since only the strongest survivors gathered there.

Furthermore, Jang Oh was a warrior who'd already achieved three victories in the current Squad A. Just two more wins and he could seize the opportunity every Demonic Coliseum gladiator dreamed of.

CLANG!

Jang Oh's vicious smile widened as he blocked the sword of a Squad B gladiator who rushed in screaming, a mix of terror and battle cry. His massive fist crushed the Squad B gladiator's face.

Jang Oh's body immediately spun with fluid motion. His enormous axe struck the gladiator's abdomen, sinking clear to the spine. The man wouldn't survive.

Jang Oh kicked the gladiator away and laughed with pure joy. "Hahahaha! Try to struggle a little!"

"You bastard!"

The remaining Squad B gladiators charged at Jang Oh in desperate fury. However, the other Squad A gladiators, who'd been waiting for the perfect opportunity, moved in behind him like hunting wolves.

CHA-CHA-CHA-CHANG! JJEONG!

Swords and blades clashed in a brilliant, deadly symphony.

Cruel light flickered across Squad A gladiators' faces. Two enemies already lay dead. The victory scales had tipped decisively.

PWEOK! SEOKEOK!

Punches made them vomit blood. Sharp blades tore away chunks of flesh. Short spears pierced abdomens. Wildly swung elbows shattered noses and teeth.

It was a free-for-all brawl with cruel rules. Lose and get demoted even if you survived. Sometimes you had to team up with the very bastards you'd just tried to kill.

Naturally, there was no cooperation or camaraderie. They lacked the knowledge to form proper formations, possessing only the primal will to kill their opponents.

Jang Oh's grin stretched wider. *This is good.* *So good.*

Through his two years in the Demonic Coliseum, he'd come to savor the taste of blood. This was his third time reaching Squad A. He had had chances to leave the Demonic Coliseum behind, but he had intentionally lost before by abandoning his allies. He was addicted to fighting and slaughter.

PPAGAK!

Jang Oh's kick shattered a Squad B gladiator's groin, dropping him to his knees, then he coldly brought his axe down. Just like that, another Squad B gladiator met a futile death.

This was one reason the Demonic Coliseum was perfect. The fights had no formality whatsoever. Survival was everything, so no dirty trick ever got booed. The spectators, needing to make money, cheered louder for the most despicable acts.

Jang Oh shouted with bright, savage joy, "You bastards! Can't you do any better...!"

His eyes widened in sudden shock.

*What? Why have the numbers dwindled so much?*

In the chaotic battlefield, Jang Oh looked around frantically. Including himself, only five Squad A gladiators were still standing.

While Jang Oh was bewildered, a Squad B gladiator rushed at him.

PAAK!

He was fast. Among the fastest Jang Oh had killed so far.

Jang Oh swung his axe in a diagonal arc.

JJEOOONG!

The Squad B gladiator blocked the downward strike with his sword and dropped to one knee.

Jang Oh's eyebrow twitched. He hadn't learned any high-level demonic arts, but he possessed innate divine strength. Few had ever blocked his axe when he swung with killing intent.

*Look at this?*

The demonic gladiator gritted his teeth and slammed a high kick at Jang Oh's face.

Jang Oh's face snapped to the side, but he felt no real pain. He had mastered the Iron Skin Style of external arts and possessed a body that could repel most attacks. Still, feeling like shit was a different matter entirely.

"Son of a bitch!"

KWADEUK!

PUK!

*What? What is this? Why was my stomach pierced? No, who pierced it?*

The gladiator with the severed ankle sneered. He foresaw his own death, so his face was empty of fear as he declared viciously, "You're going to die too!"

"What're you talking about, you crazy bastard!" Jang Oh glanced around wildly. Three Squad A gladiators fought three from Squad B. In that short time, another of his allies had fallen, but he didn't register that fact. "Which bastard! Who did this to me...!"

SEOKEOK!
