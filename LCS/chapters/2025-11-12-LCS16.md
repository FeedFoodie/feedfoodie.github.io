Jeon Oh's eyes twitched; he felt humiliated and afraid. He might have acted arrogant with the Discipline Hall's authority, but he couldn't ignore the Bright Demon Commander's reputation.

He had come himself precisely because Do Heon was difficult to deal with. Even the great Discipline Hall would be burdened if the Inner Court's demonic practitioners saw them send a less experienced Group Leader to bind a Commander.

The Bright Demon Commander Do Heon was famous among all the Six Companies Commanders and even the Inner Court's executives for his gentle disposition. Jeon Oh had come personally, thinking he, the First Squad Leader, was the right person to break Do Heon's spirit. Binding an angry Do Heon required immense courage, though, even for him.

After staring at Do Heon for a long time, he growled reluctantly, "I'll trust in your honor, Commander."

Do Heon replied cheerfully. "I am grateful for your trust."

Jeon Oh turned around irritably. "Let's go!"

Surrounded by the Discipline Hall's First Squad warriors, Do Heon left the Bright Demon Pavilion.

Before passing through the main gate, he briefly glanced back. His wavering gaze held an unconcealable mix of surprise and worry.

Numerous demonic practitioners watched Do Heon cross the Inner Court on his way to the Discipline Hall. Their mouths were firmly held shut. Uttering any unnecessary words could get them noticed by the Discipline Hall members.

Do Heon sighed inwardly. *Whispering amongst themselves isn't a pleasant sight, but...*

It was hard to believe this was the true face of the fiercely competitive demonic practitioners who had terrified the Central Plains. They were now as quiet as dead mice, only rolling their eyes.

Today's demonic practitioners were merely preoccupied with looking good to their superiors or avoiding their bad side.

Demonic practitioners were people too. In any world where people live, this could be seen as natural, but as Do Heon watched not a single person meet the eyes of the Discipline Hall members, he couldn't help but lament.

*Where has the recklessly bold 'us' of the past gone?*

The road to the Discipline Hall was long. The actual distance wasn't far, but Jeon Oh deliberately took a detour. His intention was to show all the demonic practitioners of the Inner Court the sight of Do Heon surrounded by Discipline Hall members.

It was an incredibly petty act, but Do Heon showed no reaction and walked silently with them.

Two hours after leaving the Bright Demon Pavilion, they arrived at the Discipline Hall.

CREEEEAK!

The interrogation room door opened. Do Heon kept his eyes closed, not even turning his head.

THUD, THUD!

A man with measured footsteps and a cold aura stood opposite him. It was Hang Hwe, the Discipline Hall's first-class investigator.

CLACK!

A concise, merciless sound echoed as Hang Hwe threw the documents onto the steel desk for show, then slowly sat in his chair.

Only then did Do Heon's eyes slowly open.

Hang Hwe introduced himself stiffly, "I'm Hang Hwe, a first-class investigator of the Discipline Hall."

His tone was intimidating, but not particularly rude. Hang Hwe always presented the same demeanor to everyone who entered the interrogation room. All the investigators were like that. They couldn't conduct a proper investigation if the subject intimidated them.

"Before the confrontation, I'll first confirm your identity."

Hang Hwe shuffled through the documents, stopping at a certain point. "Are you Commander Do Heon of the Bright Demon Company, one of the Divine Cult's martial units?"

Hang Hwe's eyes narrowed. "Answer the question directly. Name, Do Heon. Is that correct?"

"Yes."

If not for his pride as an investigator and his numerous past experiences, his entire body would have already been drenched in a cold sweat.

Hang Hwe growled. "Even a great Six Companies Commander shouldn't relax his shoulders here. In this place, even figures at the Inner Court director level don't get out easily."

A vein throbbed near Hang Hwe's eye. Do Heon's completely relaxed appearance had gotten on his nerves.

CLANG!

He wore a flashy red silk robe and a gold bracelet. His neatly combed-back hair didn't have a single strand out of place. A strong fragrance emanated from him, as if from a sachet. His appearance was incomparably flashy and didn't belong in the grim interrogation room.

His story was famous in the Inner Court, though. He entered the Discipline Hall, was promoted from member to investigator in three months, and then became a Group Leader a year later.

Then, three years later, he went from Group Leader to Deputy Director. More surprisingly, it took him only two more years to become Director.
