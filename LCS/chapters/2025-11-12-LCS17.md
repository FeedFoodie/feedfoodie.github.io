*A certain mad demon wants to join the Discipline Hall?*

The statement sparked all sorts of wild imaginations.

Gong Muwae stared blankly at Do Heon and tilted his head. "You... What?"

"Of course, I can't use the Bright Demon Company for personal matters. There are too many eyes watching."

"...?"

"However, if I become the Director's *friend*, won't I be a reliable asset for your future endeavors?"

Gong Muwae's mouth slowly fell open. The atmosphere around Do Heon felt different from what he remembered, but he never expected this. "Did you just say you want to become friends?"

"That's right."

"Seriously?"

"Is there a problem?"

Gong Muwae stared blankly at Do Heon before bursting into a roar of laughter. "Uwahahahaha! Today keeps getting more surprising. I can't recall a more amusing day in recent memory."

His booming laugh shook the entire interrogation room, echoing for a while before stopping abruptly.

"Are you trying to joke with me?" he snapped. His composure vanished. He gave Do Heon a terribly sinister look.

"This is no joke," Do Heon replied.

"Everyone who needs to know knows your personality. You must be truly desperate to say something you don't mean. I'm not so gullible as to be fooled by such wordplay and let you go."

"Until now, that was true."

"What?"

"You were quite the pushover; that's why I disliked you. I could never understand what made you so arrogant and insolent when you lacked any real skill."

The remark silenced Gong Muwae, whether it was honest or not.

Do Heon's gaze intensified. "But you're not a pushover anymore, are you?"

"...Oh?"

"From a mere member, you became the head of the Discipline Hall in just six years. That would be impossible without ability."

He was right. No matter how corrupt the Divine Cult had become, one had to prove a minimum level of competence to seize the leadership of an important organization like the Discipline Hall. It was a position stained with the filth of bribery and corruption, but if Gong Muwae had possessed no skill whatsoever, he could never have attained the seat of Discipline Hall Director, not even in his wildest dreams.

Of course, how one behaved after obtaining the Director position varied from person to person.

*Strange.* Undeniable bewilderment surfaced in Gong Muwae's eyes as he looked at Do Heon. *Is he really trying to come under me?*

Do Heon valued rules and laws. He had a gentle nature but drew a clear line between public and private matters. The man had joined the Outer Court as a low-ranking member, achieved numerous merits with overwhelming skill, and became a Commander of the Six Companies before turning forty, all with no connections in the upper echelons.

He was that remarkable. There weren't many people of his caliber in the current Divine Cult. Across all combat units of the Inner and Outer Courts, there were no more than five.

Naturally, the higher-ups couldn't help but view Do Heon favorably.

The problem was his overly stubborn attitude, which failed to adapt to the changing times. Now, people regarded Do Heon as a mere faded prodigy who had once received great expectations.

Of course, the position of Bright Demon Commander itself was still quite impressive.

Gong Muwae's brow furrowed slightly. *A man like that suddenly changed his attitude like this? Does he have some ulterior motive?*

He was suspicious, but it seemed unlikely. Three of the Six Companies' Commanders had personal ties with him, and their assessment of the Bright Demon Commander was no different than before.

Do Heon was said to be close with the Black Demon Commander So Gong, but it was just a friendship. So Gong was small-minded and had a peculiar personality, so he had no friends other than the Bright Demon Commander.

In other words, they were just two outcasts associating with each other. The power they possessed was too meager to suggest any ulterior motive.

"I figured you'd be suspicious," Do Heon remarked.

"Hm?"

"I've always held my head high before you, Director. For me to suddenly declare I'll serve under you... it's only natural you'd be suspicious."

Gong Muwae's eyes narrowed. It was natural for him to be suspicious, but he disliked having his thoughts read so openly. "So?"

"As you know, I have no one to stand with me. My only personal connection is with Commander So, and frankly, he's too narrow-minded a fellow to expect anything from."

"...Whew." Gong Muwae was dumbfounded.

*Is this bastard really the Do Heon I know?*

Do Heon was known for his integrity, just as he was for his clear distinction between public and private matters. Still, to call his friend So Gong a narrow-minded fellow, no matter how bad the situation was...

Do Heon smiled bitterly. "I'm tired now, too."

"Tired?"

"I've realized there's a limit to how high one can climb with personal skill and achievements alone."

"So you've decided to cast some bait and grab onto a line, is that it?"

"Director, do you intend to remain the Discipline Hall Director for the rest of your life?"

"Of course not."

"It's the same for me. You know how the higher-ups see me. A useful swordsman's blade must not run dry of blood. They intend to keep me as a field swordsman until the day I die."

He wasn't wrong. Since Violet Lightning Divine Demon Jo Baekcheon rose to Cult Leader, the Divine Cult's power had weakened considerably. Even weakened, it still wielded immense influence over the Central Plains, but neither the Cult Leader nor the high command wanted it to weaken further.

The problem was that they only wished for it and didn't propose any proper countermeasures.

To them, the Divine Cult was like heaven. The stifling laws and pride of the past were nothing but shackles. When they could obtain every pleasure on earth just by reaching out, why would they care about the state of affairs?

That was why they needed capable men like Do Heon even more. They wanted the pleasures of heaven in their hands, with martial artists around to protect that heaven. They wouldn't want to see a mere swordsman on the same level as them.

"I'm human, too. I may not have the same lust for power as others, but I have no desire to remain the head of a field unit until I die."

The corners of Gong Muwae's mouth lifted slightly. "So you want to climb higher?"

"Every member of our Cult would."

"Unfortunately, that's not possible. It's fine to have dreams, but if it were a world where everyone achieved them, the world would have long since descended into chaos."

*It's already a chaotic mess that's hard to watch.* Do Heon bit back the words rising in his throat and instead said, "As I said, I don't have the desire to reach the very top. I simply have no intention of remaining a Commander for my entire life."

"Everyone starts like that, but as you climb one step, then another, you start eyeing the position of Inner Palace Captain, then Division Head."

"Then the Director can control me as you see fit."

"Control?"

"It won't happen, but if I were to get too greedy, couldn't you use your connections to have me demoted or kicked out?"

"...!"

Do Heon closed his eyes. "I want to work with you because I know you possess that kind of power. After all, without it, you wouldn't even be able to pull me up from my position as Commander."

Gong Muwae's expression grew more peculiar. *This guy, is he serious?*

At first, he didn't believe it. No, he couldn't believe it. Do Heon's personality was that famous, and Gong Muwae had experienced it firsthand.

But the more they talked, the more he began to see Do Heon in a different light.

*So even a man who stood straight as a bamboo his whole life eventually bows like a ripe stalk of rice before immense power.*

Gong Muwae hated Do Heon. He hated Do Heon's rigid personality, his dismissive gaze, and every action that seemed to say, 'I am different from you.' Whenever they clashed, others would placate Gong Muwae while secretly sneering, implying he couldn't compare to Do Heon.

Gong Muwae was the one who had shut all of their mouths. That was why he believed in this method, and thanks to it, he had risen to the position of Discipline Hall Director.

And now, the Bright Demon Commander, whom he had loathed and hated, was crawling to serve under him.

*How strange.*

Now that Do Heon was showing such a weak side, Gong Muwae felt an inexplicable emptiness.

*In the end, all people are the same.*

Do Heon shouldn't have been like this. He should have been thoroughly crushed by Gong Muwae's power and met a miserable end.

But that Do Heon was now bowing his head.

Gong Muwae felt anger at the action, but also a greater joy, and an emptiness that surpassed even that.

*Did I grind my teeth so hard just to crush a man of this level?*

He let out a hollow laugh.

*I still have a long way to go.*

On his way to becoming the Discipline Hall Director, he had thrown countless cult members' lives into the gutter.

In the end, Do Heon was the same as any other human. He had simply held out longer than the others.

But Do Heon possessed a special quality that others lacked.

"Then why did you act so stiffly?"

"What do you mean?"

"If you'd quietly allowed yourself to be bound by the Blue Ghost Rope and calmly complied with the investigator's questions, things could have gone much more smoothly."

Do Heon shook his head. "I simply didn't want to drag things out."

"Was that the only reason? If you truly wanted to join me, you shouldn't have ignored our procedures."

"If I had done that, you would never have accepted me. Even if we had this exact same conversation, you would have suspected me to the very end."

"Oho!"

Do Heon opened his eyes.

Seeing Do Heon's clouded, exhausted eyes, Gong Muwae's heart shook.

"Unauthorized removal of a demonic gladiator, insulting a Group Leader, and obstructing an investigation. I want to be with the person who can make all those crimes disappear, right here and now," Do Heon said firmly.

"......"

"If you're not someone who can do that much, then just add every crime you can think of and send me to prison."

A smile spread across Gong Muwae's face. This was Do Heon's special quality.

Do Heon's true nature wasn't gentleness. It was confidence. Even while offering to submit, he tested the ability of the one above him. Once he confirmed that ability, he never looked back.

That once-grating confidence now left a profound impression on Gong Muwae.

"Do you want to see the extent of the abilities of the person you wish to join?"

"I've seen plenty from the outside, but it never felt real to me."

"It seems this is that moment."

"If the Director is also moved, then this moment is indeed that moment."

"Hahaha!" Gong Muwae burst out laughing. It was a sincere laugh, brimming with joy. "Of course I have that ability. This place is my kingdom. No one can refuse my orders."

"......"

"But to show you that ability, wouldn't some sign of sincerity be required?"

Do Heon stood up without hesitation. He pushed back his chair, took three steps to the side, and slowly knelt on one knee. Then he silently bowed his head.

"...!!" Gong Muwae's eyes shook violently. The action was worth a thousand words. With it, Do Heon proved his worth and admitted his complete defeat.

A brief silence fell.

Gong Muwae rose from his seat and stood before Do Heon. "Raise your head."

As Do Heon raised his head, he saw Gong Muwae extending a hand.

Gong Muwae's face was solemn but couldn't hide its joy. His hand was adorned with numerous flashy bracelets. "A gesture of sincerity I won't see again. I accept it well."

Do Heon took his hand and rose to his feet.

Gong Muwae smiled and gripped both of Do Heon's shoulders. "Yesterday's enemy has become today's friend. I didn't expect this outcome, but it has brought me far greater joy than simply crushing you."

"...Thank you."

*Even his manner of speaking had changed.*

Gong Muwae felt a thrilling pleasure, greater than obtaining a thousand pieces of gold or spending a night with a beautiful woman.

"Hahahaha!" He laughed with his head thrown back, lost in the ecstasy of victory.

Unknown to him, Do Heon's eyes grew chillingly calm.

"My, we can't stay in a gloomy place like an interrogation room on such a joyous day. I must find out just how much Commander Do can drink, shouldn't I?"

"It would be my honor."

"Good, very good. Let's drink until our noses are crooked today."

"Of course."

"Before that."

"...?"

"We should take care of what needs to be taken care of before we go. It's always better to leave things clean."

Gong Muwae's expression turned serious. "The incident at the Demonic Coliseum, what really happened there?"

A strange light flashed in Do Heon's eyes. The time had come for him to play his real hand.
