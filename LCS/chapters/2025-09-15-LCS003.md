The next evening, an unknown bird's cry decorated the gloomy night. Do Heon stood in the center of the training ground instead of his usual office spot and reflected on the past two days.

*Is this truly the right thing to do?*

His gaze fixed on the dried bloodstains covering the training ground floor. Yesterday, after sending Yoo Yisang away with So Gong, he had interrogated the others to confirm if Myeong Han's crimes were real.

Unfortunately, they were. Worse, several junior members had chosen not to report the criminal acts of their senior.

Since Myeong Han was a First Squad member, he had brought the entire squad to justice. The members who were aware of and had ignored Myeong Han's crimes were flogged harshly.

The punishment was justifiable by the internal regulations of the Bright Demon Company and the rules of the entire Divine Cult combat forces. After all, the Vice Commander held the power of life and death over his warriors.

However, the next step was the problem.

*Was it right to not send them to the Discipline Hall?*

The first round of punishment within the company was over. The next stop was the Discipline Hall.

Though rarely practiced, the law stated that a warrior who committed a grave sin must be transferred to the Discipline Hall. This was separate from any internal punishment.

Nevertheless, Do Heon still hadn't sent his men there.

*If I don't send them, this incident will be forgotten.*

Even rehabilitation had a proper process. He had no intention of keeping warriors in the Bright Demon Company who had ignored a senior's crime. It didn't matter whether Myeong Han had threatened or cajoled his juniors, they had still committed an unforgivable sin.

If he sent them to the Discipline Hall now, they would either end up serving a life sentence in prison or be executed by the Punishment Hall.

Do Heon sighed. *It's all my fault. I used being busy as an excuse and didn't pay proper attention to my subordinates. In the end, the responsibility is mine.*

Even So Gong had said that was just how he was. His friend had also reassured him that while he might feel responsible, this hadn't happened because he'd failed at managing his men properly.

He made his decision.

*Whatever the case, this is the result. Even if the Divine Cult is heading in the wrong direction, this cannot be allowed. The Divine Cult became what it is today because of people overlooking minor offenses one by one. I cannot be one of them.*

Suddenly, he pictured So Gong's mischievous smile and closed his eyes.

*I'll have to bring that murderer back.*

If he reported this to the Discipline Hall now, So Gong would also get in trouble. He couldn't give an ambiguous testimony, so bringing Yoo Yisang back seemed best.

*Yoo Yisang...*

He closed his eyes again.

*I'm sorry. As the Bright Demon Commander, I should apologize to you, but I am also a demon of the Divine Cult. If I reveal the whole truth, you too will have no choice but to be dragged to the Discipline Hall.*

He felt uneasy. He rarely reversed a decision, even in complex situations, but this time was different. A sense of reluctance lingered in his heart even after deciding.

Do Heon took a deep breath. *Okay, time to move.*

"What are you thinking so hard about?" someone asked.

Do Heon blinked, startled. Lifting his head, he saw So Gong standing at the edge of the training ground, hands clasped behind his back.

His eyes wavered. "Since when...?"

"I've been here for a while. I even made my presence known, but you didn't notice."

"Did you?" he sighed. He really had been lost in thought. "I'm sorry to tell you this, but..."

"You changed your mind?"

Do Heon nodded gravely. At times like this, So Gong was as sharp as a tack[[1\]](#_msocom_1) . "I think that would be for the best."

"...Hmm." So Gong looked up at Do Heon, then stepped onto the training ground.

Do Heon watched So Gong in silence.

So Gong crossed his arms. "May I ask why?"

"There's something very wrong with the current Divine Cult."

"Well, everyone knows that."

"The Divine Cult didn't end up like this because of one person's mistake. At first, sentences were reduced for acquaintances, and bribes were exchanged. Now, though, the corruption has even reached a point where those in power will cut off the heads of innocent demons if they displease them."

"......"

"Because the organization isn't functioning properly, the laws and systems have collapsed, and the gap between the powerful and the powerless has only worsened. In the past, our Cult ran as smoothly as flowing water while upholding the principle of survival of the fittest, thanks to a clear self-awareness of one's position and absolute loyalty to our god."

"But that's gone now?"

"That's right."

"What does that have to do with you changing your mind?"

"The Divine Cult of today was built by people who ignored the law for what they called minor sins, one after another."

"......" So Gong tilted his head. "So you're saying you can't be one of them?"

"That's right." Do Heon straightened his back. "I am a demon of the Divine Cult. The Divine Cult is my parent and my home. No matter how flawed a parent is, one should not turn away from them or ignore them."

"......"

"I believe I must repent for my mistakes and uphold the rules, starting now."

So Gong stared at Do Heon, then said bluntly, "You're saying some right things, and some wrong things."

"...?"

"Did you say your parent is the Divine Cult?"

"That's right."

"Right, the Divine Cult. This Divine Cult is your parent, but the head of the cult isn't your parent. He's just a temporary nanny pretending to be a parent."

"...?"

"If that's not clear enough, then he's a nanny who doesn't even do his job. Instead, he oppresses and persecutes his own children like the scum of the earth."

Do Heon's eyes wavered once more.

"Just as you said, it's not any single person's fault that the Divine Cult has come to this shitty state. However, there is a fundamental culprit who caused the Divine Cult to become this way."

"...!"

"Obviously, that is none other than the current Cult Leader."

"So Gong!" Do Heon's voice was stern, but laced with unconcealable panic. "Stop spouting nonsense! The Cult Leader is...!"

"Shush! Someone might hear you."

"....." Do Heon bit his lip.

So Gong smiled mysteriously. "Want me to tell you one thing for sure?"

"...?"

"If you truly love the Divine Cult that much, you would already be dead."

"...What do you mean?"

"If you truly cared for the Divine Cult, you should have marched proudly to the Cult Leader's Hall and shouted for him to stop this nonsense. You should have advised him to fix what's wrong with the Cult immediately."

"...!!"

"I know you're not the type to sacrifice your life over your beliefs. Still, your vision is terribly narrow. In the end, you're only pretending to be a good cultist within your limits. Isn't that so?"

Do Heon had many rebuttals, but he could not voice them. So Gong's scathing words bypassed logic and reason, embedding themselves in his chest like a dagger.

So Gong continued, "I'm not telling you to break the law just because everyone else is doing it."

"......"

"I'm saying stop looking left and right, and look up at the sky."

"...The sky?"

So Gong stared at Do Heon, then grinned. "In that respect, the kid I took is much better than you. He's like a block of wood, but at least he lived every single moment to the fullest."

"...?"

"I left the guy I planned to hand over to you at the main gate. If you talk with that kid, you might realize something." So Gong turned around and continued, "If you change your mind, come over tomorrow. I'll buy you a drink. Whether you decide to stick to your principles or not, just come. You should at least give our young murderer a drink before his final journey."

With those words, So Gong vanished like a ghost.

Confusion clouded Do Heon's face. So Gong's words circled in his chest, making him look back not just at the past two days, but at a much more distant past as well.

After some time, he left the training ground and headed for the main gate. There, he found a young man standing next to a small tree.

*That must be him.*

Do Heon walked up to the person and stopped. Whether it was the wall's shadow or the clouds covering the moon, he could not see the man's face clearly, even though his martial arts had reached peak realm.

He recalled So Gong's words.

\> "If you talk with that kid, you might realize something."

He quietly observed the young man, then asked, "Is it you?"

"......"

"I asked if you are the hot potato the Black Demon Commander brought."

The young man nodded slightly.

Do Heon's eyes grew dark. He asked bluntly, "You killed the Director of the Lee Family Trading Company?"

The young man nodded again. His face remained unclear in the darkness, and he didn't say a word.

"Why? Did you have a grudge against him?"

The young man neither shook nor nodded his head.

Frustrated, Do Heon raised his voice. "I asked why you killed him! Answer me!"

"...Because he wished to die," the young man explained, his voice heavy and cold.

Do Heon's expression hardened. "The Director wished to die?"

"That is correct."

"How did you know that?"

"Because he said so."

Do Heon's jaw dropped in disbelief. *The Director said he wanted to die?*

"Is that why you killed him? You killed him because he said he wanted to die?"

"That is correct."

"Who are you? Who are you that the Director would say such a thing to you?"

"I'm his adopted son."

"...His son?!" Do Heon's jaw dropped. "Y-You killed your own father?!"

"Yes."

Do Heon couldn't believe it. Even as an adopted son, since they had formed a parent-child bond, this was a violation of basic human morality. "You might be his son, but does it make sense for you to kill your own father just because he said he wanted to die?!"

The young man did not answer. It seemed he did not know what else to say. For a moment, doubt flickered in his faintly visible gaze.

Do Heon shouted once more. "How can a son kill his own father!"

"Because he was my benefactor."

"W-What did you say?"

"He was both my father and my benefactor, so I granted his request to die."

"...?!"

"......"

"You killed him... because he was your father and benefactor?"

"There were many others who were indebted to him, but no one stepped forward when he asked to die."

"Even so, how could you?!"

"......"

"You, do you feel no guilt?"

"I don't know."

"What do you mean, you don't know?"

"I don't know what guilt is."

"......" Do Heon was utterly dumbfounded. Just as he was about to say something, though, the young man unexpectedly talked without being asked a question.

"Also, whether I feel guilty or not isn't important. He was in pain, and with my abilities, there was no way for me to free him from that pain."

"......"

"He sincerely wished to end his life. By granting his request, I repaid a small part of my debt to him."

Do Heon stared blankly at the young man, bewildered by his bizarre logic. Suddenly he recalled So Gong's words.

> "Right, the Divine Cult. This Divine Cult is your parent, but the head of the cult isn't your parent."
>
> "Instead, he oppresses and persecutes his own children like the scum of the earth."

*Parent. Home. That's right.*

Do Heon felt like his mind had been struck by lightning. Indeed, his parent was not the Cult Leader, but the Divine Cult, and the Divine Cult was now groaning under a tyranny unprecedented in its history. It was in pain.

The Divine Cult was a parent, but not a person. It did not speak. It could not feel emotions. In that case, what must be done?

> "Look up at the sky."

The sky was also a parent, a home. Therefore, the sky was the Divine Cult, and to look at the sky meant to look at the Divine Cult itself.

That was what So Gong had meant. He wanted Do Heon to stop looking around half-heartedly and see what he must do for the Divine Cult's sake.

Do Heon looked toward the sky. Tears flowed down his cheeks, past his chin, and onto the ground.

*I have lived in vain.*

He closed his eyes.

*I lived righteously, but I did not live like a demon.*

The wind caught the falling tears of bitter regret, soaking the front of his robe.

A demon who thought he had served the Divine Cult more than anyone else was now in despair, realizing that at the root of it all, he was just an insect who cared only of his own survival.

After some time passed, a strangely dry wind blew.

WHOOSH!

The scattered clouds moved, revealing the moonlight. 

Do Heon wiped his tears with his sleeve and looked at the young man. The moonlight, freed from the clouds, slowly illuminated the young man's face from the left, as if making an introduction.

When the bright moonlight fully revealed the young man's face, he asked, "What's your name?"

The young man replied, "My surname is Lee, and my given name is Cheonsang."
