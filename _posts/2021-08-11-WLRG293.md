"Master…"

"Aaaargh!"

The word had barely left Lee Sungmin's lips when Yehwa screamed. She staggered forward and collapsed before the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's corpse. Tears streamed from her trembling eyes as she bowed her head, her sobs wracking her body. A trembling hand reached out to touch him.

Lee Sungmin quietly approached her side, his own heart pounding like a drum inside his chest. His head throbbed as if it might explode, and his throat was desert-dry. A strange, terribly unpleasant sensation crawled over his skin and tingled at his fingertips. He raised a hand to clutch at his chest, the memory of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's death rising unbidden.

His gaze fell upon the Chairman's corpse. The body was missing its left arm, yet there were almost no other wounds. His martial arts uniform was dirty and his hair slightly disheveled, but his face was peaceful, devoid of any pain. Not a single drop of blood trickled from the severed limb. A faint smile graced the Chairman's lips, and as Lee Sungmin looked at it, his master's final words echoed in his mind.

-I have denied the future.

-Don't be too sad, don't be too angry, and don't act like a helpless child.

-It couldn't be done with the Martial God. It couldn't be done with the Moon Queen either. It wouldn't have worked even if the Spirit had intervened. The future of death, this one has denied it. That's right. This one does not die.

-I am merely going to a distant place. Perhaps you too will come here someday. When that time comes, do not hesitate, and leave no regrets behind.

The Chairman had only smiled when asked if they weren't going to Wudang, replying that he no longer needed to go.

*What does that mean... I don't understand.* Lee Sungmin sank to the ground beside Yehwa, his eyes vacant as he stared at the body. He simply couldn't accept it. How could a man of such superhuman strength be dead before his very eyes? This was the man who had shattered the Dog Beating Stick Formation with ease and subdued the Black Dragon Knight like a child. Even the Martial God, the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven, had been no match for him. He had even withstood a joint attack from both the Martial God and the Moon Queen.

[I don't get it,] Void Monarch muttered. [I'm talking about when that wench, the Moon Queen, and the Martial God attacked together. The Chairman was overwhelming them both, even then... but his strength just suddenly drained away. I don't get why that happened.]

It was a question that plagued Lee Sungmin as well. The Chairman's superiority had seemed absolute. If the fight had continued, he would have defeated both the Martial God and the Moon Queen. He shouldn't have died.

Unable to solve the mystery of his master's death, Lee Sungmin looked at the corpse and asked a different question. "...Why is the body here?"

Osla's wings drooped as she floated in the air, and she slowly descended to the forest floor.

"A long time ago, the Coalition Leader mentioned something," she said. "He said if he were to die, and he insisted it would never happen, but *if*... he wanted to be buried in this forest. I wanted to honor that promise."

"...Why are we here?"

"It was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's request," Scarlet answered. With a gloomy expression, she crumpled the torn scroll remaining in her hands. "It was a request he made only to me. If... he were to die, or if things got dangerous, he told me to use a teleport scroll to get you and Yehwa away."

"That's a scroll the Coalition Leader got from a Dungeon ages ago," Osla added with a sigh. "I helped him input the coordinates for this place afterward. I never thought... it would actually be used like this."

"...Why," Yehwa's voice trembled as she spat the words. "Why did the Coalition Leader have to die? Why! If it was a teleport scroll, he could've used it with us!"

"No, that was impossible," Osla immediately refuted. "The Coalition Leader fought inside the Spirit's barrier. It's impossible to escape from within it, even with a teleport scroll, and... knowing that, it was the Coalition Leader who chose to fight. That's what he wanted."

"But why..."

"Because he wanted to," Lee Sungmin answered in a small voice. "That's all there is to it, I suppose."

Just as he always had, Lee Sungmin thought, his gaze falling back to the smiling corpse. Osla crouched down by the Chairman's head, her small hands reaching out to caress his face.

"...The Coalition Leader isn't dead."

"What...?" Yehwa asked, her face suddenly filled with a glimmer of hope as she looked at Osla.

Osla's smile was bitter as she saw the look in Yehwa's eyes. "That doesn't mean he can come back to life, though. I'm sorry I can't meet your expectations."

"What... what does that mean?"

"The Coalition Leader's soul... it's moved on to another world, not this one. He has completely escaped the shackles of mortality and departed to become a higher-level being. Actually," she added, her voice dejected, "it might be more accurate to say he's dead. He can't return to this world, so you could say he's gone to the afterlife."

Perhaps that was why her voice was so dejected. *Completely escaped the shackles of mortality. A higher-level being…* Lee Sungmin didn't understand, but Void Monarch knew exactly what those words meant. He too had seen such a 'path' before.

"...There was... a will," Scarlet muttered, approaching Lee Sungmin. "When the Chairman entrusted the scroll to me, he also gave me this. His last will. I've never read it... and honestly, I never thought I'd have to."

Scarlet took a rolled-up letter from her Subspace Pocket and handed it to him. Lee Sungmin looked down at the scroll, which was tied with a red string. After a moment's hesitation, he untied the string and unrolled the long letter, finding it densely packed with characters.

<Has this one died?>

The moment he read the first sentence, Lee Sungmin's expression hardened.

<I thought I would never die, but it seems I have. Should I consider it fortunate that I prepared this in advance? I wrote this will because I felt I should, but in truth, this one doesn't think for a moment that this one will die.>

Lee Sungmin hadn't been able to imagine it either.

<Nevertheless, the fact that I have died must mean something I couldn't imagine has happened. Did the Vampire Queen kill this one? Or was it the Martial God or the Sword Immortal? Well, since this one is already dead by the time you're reading this, there's no point in the current this one, who is writing this will, wondering about it. So, how are you all feeling right now.>

Yehwa, reading over his shoulder, covered her mouth as a fresh burst of crying turned into stifled sobs. Lee Sungmin bit down hard on his trembling lower lip.

<Yehwa must be crying. Is the Crimson Archmage feeling gloomy too? Disciple, what about you? Are you saddened by the death of this one, your Master?>

*Of course.*

<Don't.>

It was as if he could hear the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's laughter in the uninhibited handwriting.

<If this one has died, it would be a death of this one's own choosing. A death that this one decided upon and chose to meet here, and without a doubt, this one would have no regrets about that death. Who in this world could kill this Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon? If this one met such a death, this one would surely have been satisfied with it.>

*Even so, don't force your feelings on us,* Lee Sungmin thought. *Even if you met a satisfying death, those left behind cannot be satisfied. Even if you met a death without regret, one you did not shy away from, those left behind can only be sad.*

<This one has lived for hundreds of years. I lived for a thousand years in Denir's world. The power gained through that is this one's pride, and this one has always been satisfied with my own achievements. At the same time, I always wished for something. This one wanted to choose the place and situation of my own death, but this one's power was far too great to allow for such a choice. Nevertheless, the fact that this one has died means that this one has obtained what I wished for.>

He knew that self-destructive wish all too well.

<In truth, this one knows that saying all this won't soothe the sorrow and anger you're experiencing. Because this one is already dead. I am only writing these words out of excessive worry and meddling. I don't have much else to say besides that, but since it's a fact that this one has died, I will write down what I have to say to those left behind.>

"......"

In the will, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman called her name.

<It's obvious you must be crying a lot, but do not make any extreme choices. This one is dead, but do not do anything that the dead this one would dislike. Do not seek revenge. With your abilities, it would be impossible. It would only be a dog's death. This one has met a happy death, so don't have the foolish thought of following this one to serve me in the afterlife.>

Yehwa, reading along, choked back a sob as if her innermost thoughts had been exposed. It was just as the Chairman had said. She had been planning to recklessly seek out the Martial God and die fighting him.

<It was this one who took your life. This one may be dead, but since it is a life this one took, this one will decide how it is to be used. You are to live, and not just you. All the Royal Guards this one took in must live. You may leave the murim or remain, but remember this one thing. Since you have been given a life to live, live it long and well. Yehwa, you and the other Royal Guards are to no longer involve yourselves in the affairs of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.>

"...My lord..." Yehwa lowered her head, her voice choked with tears.

<I don't have much to say to the Crimson Archmage. Ah, right. I should say thank you. The fact that you are reading this will means the Crimson Archmage has successfully carried out all of this one's requests. Did you know? As I write this, not once did I think that the ones reading it would be anyone other than you all. That's how unconditionally I trust the Crimson Archmage.>

"...It wasn't that difficult a task," Scarlet muttered, clearing her throat.

<You might wonder why it had to be the Crimson Archmage. It's because this one trusted the appropriate distance kept between this one and the Crimson Archmage. If I had given this request to my foolish disciple or Yehwa, it's clear they wouldn't have listened properly.>

That was true. If Lee Sungmin had been told to flee if something happened to the Chairman, or if he had been given the will, he would never have run away. The same went for Yehwa.

<I'm sorry I have nothing to repay you with. Go ahead and squeeze it out of my disciple. This one is already dead, after all, and, Black Dragon Knight. This one is not sure if you will be reading this will.>

At the moment of his death, the Chairman had retracted the oath he had forcibly taken from the Black Dragon Knight. Lee Sungmin couldn't understand why. What was even more puzzling was why the Black Dragon Knight had helped them escape and blocked the Martial God.

<This one doesn't know you well, but I learned that you're not such a damn bastard. You don't sympathize with the Martial God's ideology, you follow him because he can give you what you want. I don't know how you will act after this one's death. I don't know if you've become an enemy or an ally. Do as you please.>

-Forcibly suppressing one's own will is an unpleasant thing.

Right before he died, the Chairman had said that to the Black Dragon Knight, and when he said it, he seemed to have realized something.

<Osla. If things went as this one wished, you would be reading this as well. I don't think I've ever seen you look sad. It's a shame I have to die without seeing it, but it can't be helped, and... this one feels sorry towards you. I said I would grant your request someday, but it seems this one will be unable to.>

"The Coalition Leader is an idiot," Osla muttered, sitting with her knees drawn up. "An idiot, a fool, a moron."

<Osla is probably cursing me at this part. I suppose I'll have to take that much, but Osla, you know better than anyone what this one's wish was, and what kind of death this one dreamed of. So don't cry. I've never seen it, but your crying face must surely be ugly.>

"I'm not crying!" Osla shrieked.

<And, to this one's incompetent disciple.>

At the end of the will, Lee Sungmin was mentioned.

<As this one has said before, this one likes a struggling mediocrity. I find it more human than a calamity. This one quite likes effort, and you fit that mold rather well. I did find out you had other blessings besides effort, but I still don't dislike you. This one may have called you incompetent and stupid, but this one does not regret taking you as my disciple.>

Lee Sungmin's fists clenched. His vision blurred, and though he fought it, he felt tears begin to well.

<This one wanted to do many things for you. Enough so that you could do things on your own, even without this one. If possible, this one wanted to create that situation for you, but this one probably failed. I don't know what your role is in bringing about The End, but your existence was probably more important to the world's destiny than this one's. In the end, this one had to make an exit. Yes, I'm sure that's how it had to be.>

"...That's not true," Lee Sungmin muttered in a choked voice. *No. Destiny or whatever, you shouldn't have died like this. Whatever my role is, you could have taken my place more easily, more quickly than I ever could.*

<I thought a lot. I opened your blood vessels to allow you to use the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder more efficiently, but that won't be enough. I don't know what The End is, but what you're trying to stop is the end of the world. With your abilities, it's an opponent far beyond your reach. No matter how much you struggle, you will fail. No matter how hard you try, you will fail.>

*That's probably true.* Lee Sungmin knew it well. This time, too, he had been able to do nothing.

<I leave the affairs of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance to you. You could lead the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance as this one's successor, but you're not well-suited to leading an organization. I don't know if you think so yourself. In truth, without the Royal Guards, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is all size and no substance. It won't be of much help against the ones you must fight.>

Lee Sungmin knew that well. He read the next sentence.

<So. This is a rather terrible thing to say, but.>

Reading the sentence that followed, Lee Sungmin gasped.

<Eat this one.>

Everyone reading the will was horrified.
