The word came suddenly.

*Duel.*

Lee Sungmin could only stare at Wiji Hoyeon, his expression one of pure surprise. Feeling his gaze on her, she let out a soft chuckle.

"It's not like I said anything strange."

"That's true, but... isn't this a little sudden?"

"I don't really think so. It's not a big deal, is it? We're not fighting to the death."

"Even so..."

"I," Wiji Hoyeon began, cutting him off with a shake of her head. "I want to see for myself. How much stronger you've become. How much the distance between us has closed."

She left the last part unsaid, the silent question of how much farther apart they had grown.

Lee Sungmin closed his mouth and studied her face. Her eyes, looking right back at him, did not waver. She was smiling, but it was a smile that held a firmness that would not permit refusal.

"...Right now?"

Wiji Hoyeon simply nodded her head. It was too sudden. They had only just met, and there was so much he wanted to say, yet, he found he didn't want to refuse her request.

In truth, he felt the same way.

Just as Wiji Hoyeon wanted to see for herself, Lee Sungmin also wanted to confirm it. He remembered what the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had said, that right now, Lee Sungmin was stronger than Wiji Hoyeon. The Chairman claimed she couldn't last a Ten Exchanges Match against him, but Lee Sungmin could last up to fifteen seconds.

It had been an ambiguous statement, and one he didn't fully believe. In a battle between masters, the superiority of one's martial arts was not the only thing that mattered. Of course, a great difference in power was another story, but between opponents of relatively equal skill, the compatibility of their styles or other variables would decide the outcome.

[Just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said,] Void Monarch chuckled and muttered. [Do you want to win against that wench? Or do you want to lose?]

*...Honestly.*

Either one was fine. With that thought, Lee Sungmin drew his spear. He had already put away the armor and weapon he used as Jung Hyunsoo, and now held his familiar Magic Armor and Celgerus's spear.

As she watched him grip his weapon, Wiji Hoyeon draped the Black Dragon Armor over her body.

"Stop."

ZAP, a current of electricity sparked as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman inserted himself between Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon. His movement was nothing like a conventional movement art. If Sungmin had to find a comparison, it was like the Spatial Movement technique Blink, used by archmages.

Standing between them, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman spoke in an annoyed voice. "A duel is fine, but you can't just fight recklessly."

"What are you talking about all of a sudden?" Wiji Hoyeon's brow furrowed, her annoyance clear at his sudden intervention.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman glanced at her and asked, "This forest is where the Fairy Folk live, and it is the Fairy Queen's domain. You can't just start a fight and damage it."

"You didn't care about your duel with me."

"The situation was different then," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said with a smirk. "That was a one-sided duel. You couldn't even land a proper attack and just got beaten down by this one's attacks from start to finish. Did I harm the forest in the process?"

Wiji Hoyeon clamped her mouth shut.

"No. Other than you rolling on the ground, there was no harm done, but if you and that kid fight, the forest will be destroyed. Your martial skills are on a similar level, after all."

"...So?"

"Ask for permission," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said. "Go to the Fairy Queen and ask if you can have a fight in this forest. If you do, the queen will take appropriate measures."

At those words, Wiji Hoyeon could no longer be stubborn. She had lost to the Chairman and acknowledged his martial prowess. Lee Sungmin offered no objection either. After a brief silence, Wiji Hoyeon spoke.

"Then let me meet the Fairy Queen."

"My, aren't we impatient," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman taunted, but Wiji Hoyeon did not react. He glanced at Lee Sungmin. "Do you also want to fight that wench right now?"

"I don't mind."

Hearing Lee Sungmin's answer, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed heartily. He spun around and began to walk deeper into the forest. Wiji Hoyeon fell in step behind him, and Lee Sungmin walked beside her.

"It is sudden," she was the first to speak. "But I didn't want to wait. Over ten years have passed, haven't they? The original promise... was one year at the Ruvus Plains. We were supposed to meet there and see how much we had grown."

"It was."

"Back then, I was weakened by a curse, but now, I think it was for the best. Because of it, the promise was postponed, and with another year passing, you've grown even stronger." Wiji Hoyeon let out a small laugh. "Honestly, I never imagined it. That in eleven years, you would become this strong. That's why I couldn't wait any longer. A year has already passed... I don't want to wait anymore. I want to see for myself."

"How much stronger I've become?" he asked.

Her silence was an answer in itself. It wasn't just that. During the year she spent in this forest, Wiji Hoyeon had constantly thought about Lee Sungmin. She thought of her helpless self, weakened by the curse, and the image of him fighting the Dark Supreme to protect her. She remembered their first meeting as children, when she was first summoned in Zenavis, and their reunion in the Dungeon, and she wondered what would happen in the future.

"This is the place."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's steps came to a halt before a small lake nestled in the middle of the forest. The water was clear and sparkling, yet not a single fish could be seen within its depths. It was set in the middle of a beautiful and vibrant flower field, and Wiji Hoyeon let out a small gasp at the sight.

Lee Sungmin glanced at her, marveling at the scenery. Come to think of it, it was the first time he had seen such a reaction from her.

"Osla," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman called out as he approached the lake. "Don't pretend you can't hear me and come out. You were listening the whole time. Thanks to you not showing yourself, this one had to come all the way here."

He grumbled as he stared at the water. Then, the lake began to ripple. Its surface bubbled and boiled, and both Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon watched the foaming, churning water. However, the lake just kept bubbling for a while, showing no other changes.

"You thought I'd come out of the lake, didn't you?"

A playful voice called out as a pure white light gathered in the sky above them. Appearing from within the light was a Fairy much larger than normal, about the size of a young girl. Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon looked up at her in bewilderment. She was smiling, her large, magnificent butterfly wings spread wide behind her back.

"This is why teasing people is so much fun. The Coalition Leader is no fun to tease anymore since I've done it so much."

"This one was never fooled, not even in the beginning," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman grumbled. "You heard everything, so there's no need for me to explain, right?"

"Still, you're so kind, Coalition Leader. You knew I was listening, but you still came all this way. Or, did you come because you wanted to see this Your Majesty's face?" Osla asked, the corners of her lips turning up.

At her words, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman snorted and pointed at Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon. "These kids want to see who's stronger. That should be fine, right?"

"Their skills are about the same, so why bother competing?"

"Because humans are idiots who want to compete over things like who can take a bigger shit."

"Don't say such crude things, Coalition Leader," Osla pouted. "Well, I don't mind. If you had fought without asking for my permission, I would have punished you, but since you came all this way to ask, I'll see your sincerity and permit the fight."

"There you have it," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded.

Osla's wings fluttered lightly, and the space around them began to shimmer. The flower field vanished, replaced by a stark white expanse. She was a being of a different class. Osla, like Mushii or Denir, was a transcendent being beyond human reach.

"Do as you please," she giggled.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman took a few steps back. Wiji Hoyeon glanced at the two of them and muttered, "It feels unpleasant to become a source of entertainment."

"Are you complaining?"

"No, it's fine," Wiji Hoyeon muttered and turned back to Lee Sungmin. The Black Dragon Armor draped over her shoulders slowly rose. "It's a duel, but I hope you'll give it your all."

Lee Sungmin silently caressed his spear. Watching him, Wiji Hoyeon gave a bright smile.

"I intend to do the same."

How many times had he imagined this? Fighting Wiji Hoyeon again. The day she left Zenavis, he had dueled her and suffered an overwhelming, irreversible defeat. So much time had passed since then. On Mount Mushii, nightmares had plagued him every single day, and she had appeared in them many times.

In his nightmares, he suffered terrible defeats at her hands, again and again. Not even once had he ever won. It was the same in the Mental World. In that white world of endless solitude, Lee Sungmin thought of her. Wiji Hoyeon was his life's goal, his ideal, his aspiration.

This was a fight he had longed for.

In Zenavis, eleven years ago, when Wiji Hoyeon had reprimanded him, Lee Sungmin had retorted in defiance. He had told her he would make her his goal. That one day, he would pierce her heart with his spear. Of course, he didn't intend to literally fulfill those words.

He had said he would defeat her.

From beneath Wiji Hoyeon's feet, crimson enhanced qi rose up, and the Black Dragon Armor draped over her shoulders began to sway.

He had fought her Doppelganger before. At that time, he had barely managed to win by using Demonic Energy. However, there was an enormous gap between the Wiji Hoyeon of that time, the Doppelganger, and the real one. Years had passed since then, so Lee Sungmin couldn't even imagine how strong she would be now.

[There's no need to be so intimidated,] Void Monarch said. [You are plenty strong.]

Lee Sungmin raised his spear. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was a martial art that originated from Wiji Hoyeon. She had modified the original and taught it to him. The Violet Dawn Divine Art was the same. The two arts that formed the foundation of his martial arts came from her, and she knew all the spear forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

It would be difficult to gain an advantage with Spear Technique. Lee Sungmin accepted that fact. Even if he added his own flair, as long as he followed the forms, Wiji Hoyeon would know and counter them.

"Eleven years ago, I gave you a twenty-second head start," Wiji Hoyeon said. "Should I give you one this time too?"

"...No." Lee Sungmin gripped the spear with both hands. "You don't have to give me anything."

At that answer, Wiji Hoyeon beamed a radiant smile.

PAT.

Her figure vanished. The movement was swift, though it couldn't compare to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's impossible speed. Lee Sungmin immediately twisted his upper body, raising his spear just in time.

The spear collided with a section of the Black Dragon Armor that shot out from Wiji Hoyeon.

Through his fight with her Doppelganger, Lee Sungmin had a general idea of her fighting style. Her only weapon was the Black Dragon Armor. The armor, which could extend and spread at will, reacted to her enhanced qi, making it more powerful, faster, and more versatile than any other weapon.

Powerful, fast, and versatile also described Lee Sungmin's spear. He wasn't talking about the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, but about wielding the spear itself. It was his Spear Technique that had troubled the Sword Saint even before he entered the Transcendent Realm.

The widely spread Black Dragon Armor swung and met his spear. Lee Sungmin used Ran's Technique to deflect it to the side, only for another section of the armor to fly at him from the other direction.

WHOOSH.

His body vanished, leaving an afterimage as he employed the Traceless Step of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

Wiji Hoyeon knew very well she couldn't handle him with the Black Dragon Armor alone. Her hands spread wide. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art, the foundation of her martial arts, was the most terrifying and powerful she knew. The Black Dragon Armor she had swung returned to her body, and she lifted her right foot.

KABOOM!

As her foot stomped the ground, red enhanced qi exploded in all directions. It was an explosion of ridiculously immense power, devoid of any technique.

However, Lee Sungmin was not flustered. He executed the Two Step Gentle Ripple of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. With just two steps, he completely changed the flow of the enhanced qi rushing toward him. It wasn't just walking. Imbued with the insights of the Transcendent Realm, the technique twisted the spatial axis itself, forcibly diverting the attack's direction.

*Nice.*

A bright smile appeared on Wiji Hoyeon's face. Watching him escape the flow of qi and point his spear at her, she grabbed the Black Dragon Armor wrapped around her shoulders.

"It seems I shouldn't be using clever tricks," she said as she took off the armor.
