Mushii.

The God of Trials and Asceticism.

Lee Sungmin had never met the god in person, but he was certainly conscious of her existence. Ever since entering the mountain, he had cursed her name under his breath, and a few times, Mushii had grumbled back inside his head.

In the world of Eria, beings called gods definitely existed. Temples and holy grounds dedicated to them were scattered across every region, and the Junior Disciples who served them were connected to their power, which they wielded as their own. The very 'prohibition' Lee Sungmin currently suffered under was an authority granted by Mushii's divine power.

The shimmering haze before his eyes began to coalesce, soon taking the form of a small girl. Yes, a girl. That was Mushii's appearance. Her black hair was so long it dragged on the ground, and she wore dirty rags that resembled a robe. Bandages were wrapped tightly around her body, even her neck, and from within them, her two eyes shone with a pale silver light.

"Human," Mushii called out to him. Her face was serene, a strange contrast for a god who bestowed trials and asceticism. She approached the sobbing Lee Sungmin.

"Why are you crying?" she asked. "Have the trials of this mountain become so painful that you wish to give up?"

That wasn't it. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip so hard that blood trickled from the split skin. He wasn't crying because it was too hard or because he wanted to give up.

He was just afraid that the end would be insignificant. That was the only thing he feared. His encounter with Flam had filled him with despair, and with it came a flood of regret, self-loathing, and inferiority.

*Why wasn't I born a calamity?*

"Human," Mushii called out to him again.

Lee Sungmin raised a hand and roughly rubbed his eyes. He couldn't cry forever. Crying wouldn't change a thing.

He had always comforted himself with inferiority, turning away from his own weakness by calling it 'knowing his place'. He had lived his previous life that way right up until he died. Even with a fresh start, the experiences and memories of those thirteen years had acted as a poison. If he had never met Wiji Hoyeon, Lee Sungmin would not have changed at all.

"…I want to add more prohibitions."

Pushing his crouched body upright, Lee Sungmin felt an immense weight settle over him.

"Oh?"

Mushii's eyes lit up as an emotion finally flickered across her serene face. She regarded him with a curious expression.

"You want to add more prohibitions?"

He wondered if it was truly all right to speak so informally to a god, but Mushii didn't seem to mind such things. In fact, she seemed to like his attitude and his answer, letting out a low laugh.

"You wish to undertake more asceticism yourself? As the god who presides over it, there's no reason I wouldn't listen." She added one more thing, however, her eyes narrowing as she studied him. "Can you handle it?"

Mushii slowly approached. Lee Sungmin's body was currently that of a fifteen-year-old, his height just over 160 centimeters. Mushii was a full head shorter than him.

"You can't even handle the two prohibitions you have now," she sneered. "And you think you can handle more? How truly arrogant."

Lee Sungmin clenched his fists. Mushii was right. He still hadn't grown used to the prohibitions of 'Taste' and 'Heaviness'. The weight seemed to increase the more he was conscious of it, and the same was true for the taste of his food and water.

"…I can do it." *No. I have to do it,* he corrected himself, glaring at Mushii's face.

In response to his answer, Mushii chuckled. "Are you saying that because you've experienced death once and returned?"

At those words, Lee Sungmin's face stiffened. "…How do you…?"

"Because I can see it," Mushii answered. "All beings that are born live and eventually die. It is a law that can never be defied. Yes. Being born is the cause, and facing death is the inevitable effect. That is the causality from which a being can never escape."

She raised a finger and pointed it at Lee Sungmin. The tip, wrapped in a pure white bandage, poked him.

"Of course, not all beings are bound by causality. To escape it means to have transcended the law. If one escapes the inevitable causality of existence and transcends the law, they become a transcendent being, an Immortal, but you are different."

Lee Sungmin couldn't understand what she was saying. Talk of causality, laws, transcendent beings, and Immortals belonged to a world far from his own.

"You are neither a transcendent being nor an Immortal, yet your causality is twisted. Not everyone can recognize it, but there's no way a god like me wouldn't."

"…This twisted causality… does it have some kind of negative effect on me?"

"All beings accumulate karma," Mushii answered. "But in your case, you died once and for some reason came to defy death. That is a truly peculiar thing. In the process of defying causality, your soul has accumulated immense Karmic Retribution. Of course, that won't affect you in your life itself, but after you die… kekeke! You will suffer for eons."

Defying thirteen years of life did not simply add thirteen years of karma. Because he had defied 'death' itself, a sin that directly opposed the laws of existence, his karma had accumulated to an extent an ordinary human could not even imagine. He had not transcended the law and escaped causality. He had 'defied' it.

"…If it's after I die, then it doesn't matter," Lee Sungmin replied, though his heart was not at peace. *After death… she said I would suffer for eons.* It was a story for after he was dead. "The fact that I died once and came back doesn't matter. I said I want to receive more prohibitions."

"And I said you won't be able to handle it, arrogant human," Mushii said, shaking her head. "You see your vessel as too large. No. Your vessel is small."

"Aren't you the God of Asceticism and Trials?" Lee Sungmin spat out. He raised a hand and beat his own chest. "I myself am asking for trials and asceticism. Why are you trying to stop it?"

"You're not listening," Mushii said with another shake of her head. "What kind of prohibitions do you want?"

"…First, there's something I want to ask."

"You intend to have a question-and-answer session with a god without offering any price?"

"I have nothing to give even if I wanted to…"

"You're lying." Mushii bared her teeth in a smile. "How about your soul?"

"…What?"

"Your soul, I mean. A soul with twisted causality… I feel it has value as a collectible."

"Are you telling me to die right now?" Lee Sungmin asked, dumbfounded.

Mushii let out a laugh at his question. "No, that's not it. Your soul isn't ripe yet. Yes… Someday, when you die. You will hand over your soul to me then."

"…I refuse," Lee Sungmin answered, swallowing hard. To take his soul as collateral. The god who suggested it felt more like a demon.

"Well, I'm not asking for an answer right now. I'm just telling you to think about it. I'll answer your question. What do you want to ask?"

"…You said the more prohibitions are added, the greater the reward. I want to know more about that reward."

"The trial on this mountain is itself the reward," Mushii answered. "You must be feeling it too, no? Or are you so dull that you can't even feel that?"

He knew what she meant. In the three months he had lived on this mountain, his martial arts had progressed. To be precise, the proficiency of his 'skills' had risen. Just as Mushii said, the asceticism of this life was itself becoming the reward.

"If you take on additional prohibitions, the reward will grow. By taking on two, you gained a certain speed of growth. If you add more, you will gain even greater speed. However, one definite and painful prohibition is more valuable than several trivial ones."

"…I see." Lee Sungmin nodded his head.

Mushii knew what he was thinking. She added with a giggle, "So you intend to substitute your lacking talent with the rewards from prohibitions and asceticism. What is your reason for going so far? What are you in such a hurry for?"

"…I made a promise."

He had made a promise with Wiji Hoyeon. Ten years. No, it was nine years now. To meet again in nine years.

"Voice." Lee Sungmin raised his hand and pointed to his own throat. "Dreams."

He had originally intended to add the prohibitions Scarlet had, but after hearing Mushii's words, it didn't seem like adding those two would be very meaningful. Besides, his situation was different from Scarlet's. He had to push his body to its limits every day. If he received prohibitions on washing and changing clothes and happened to fall ill, there would be no solution.

"Four prohibitions." Mushii smiled a mischievous smile. "I'll tell you in advance, the prohibitions become harsher as their number increases. You're the first human to step forward and ask for four… I wonder just how long you can endure?"

Muttering this, Mushii reached her hand out to him.

The first thing to disappear was his voice. Lee Sungmin opened his mouth to say something, but no sound came out. The other was dreams. It was an incredibly vague prohibition, but he already knew from the prohibition of 'taste' just how terribly a vague prohibition could work.

"You will come to fear falling asleep every day," Mushii whispered.

Lee Sungmin thought so too.

With those last words, she disappeared from in front of him. The loss of his voice was still unfamiliar and felt incredibly awkward. He moved his mouth, trying to make a sound, but nothing came out. Lee Sungmin let out a sigh and picked up a nearby tree branch. From tomorrow on, he would have to write to speak, just like Baek Sogo.

He approached the deer carcass that Baek Sogo had brought. Although eating was frightening and painful, he couldn't skip meals for that reason. He had to eat to live.

Lee Sungmin silently began to butcher the deer. The task itself wasn't difficult. The ordeal was always the eating part. Knowing the horrific meal that was to come, his fingertips couldn't help but tremble as he worked. The smell of blood was nauseating.

He didn't stop, however. After finishing the butchering, he lit a new fire in the campfire. Night had already fallen, and after he finished his meal, when the darkness deepened, he would have to sleep.

*What kind of dreams will I have.*

Lee Sungmin endured his fear.
