The first to react to the scream was Namgung Heewon. He blurred into motion before the long cry had even ended, his movement arts carrying him away from the camp. A moment later, Jaegal Taeryeong shot to his feet with a contorted expression, followed by Tang Ahee and the other startled martial artists. Aladur simply tore at his hair, a look of grim expectation on his face.

Arriving at the source of the scream in an instant, Namgung Heewon froze. Moyong Seojin, who had collapsed to the ground, saw him and hastily tried to straighten her clothes. Her face was a pale mask of fear, her hands trembling so violently she fumbled with the hem of her pants. Averting his gaze, Namgung Heewon shrugged off his long robe and tossed it to her. After a brief hesitation, Moyong Seojin accepted it and covered herself.

"What happened?!" Jaegal Taeryeong demanded as he and the other martial artists from the Noble Clan arrived.

His glare fell upon Moyong Seojin, now huddled in Namgung Heewon's robe, and a surge of irritation eclipsed any concern he might have felt. It was pure anger that she had done something so foolish, that she had embarrassed him, her husband.

"Th-there...!"

It was Tang Ahee who spoke for the terrified Moyong Seojin, her own jaw dropping as she raised a trembling finger. She pointed across from where Moyong Seojin had fallen. Something whitish swayed beyond the bushes, in a patch of darkness where the light did not quite reach. No one was mistaken. As they all watched, the shape revealed itself, solidifying into the clear, horrifying outline of a monster.

"Yokai!" Jaegal Taeryeong shouted, his surprise untainted by fear.

He launched himself from the ground, sword flashing as he rushed the swaying creature.

SWOOSH!

A sharp slash bisected the Yokai's body.

*No...?*

A wave of bewilderment washed over Jaegal Taeryeong. The blade had passed through, yet he had felt no resistance, no sensation of cutting anything at all. He quickly retreated, putting distance between himself and the creature. The two halves of the Yokai wavered like a heat haze before merging back into one. Its monstrous form then dissolved, shifting into an unidentifiable mass of smoke.

"Stand back!" Aladur's urgent shout cut through the confusion.

Here, the true expert was not a martial artist skilled in killing men, but the sorcerer with his cause of death sorcery. His blackened hands moved, delving into his robes to produce a small wooden statue with its eyes closed. It resembled no Buddha they had ever seen. Aladur held it forward carefully in both hands, his lips moving in a silent chant. Suddenly, the statue's wooden eyes flashed open.

KAAAAAA!

Its mouth creaked wide, unleashing a piercing scream that tore through the night. The swaying smoke instantly scattered with a sharp POP.

"What... was that?" Namgung Heewon asked cautiously, having watched the entire spectacle.

Aladur let out a long, choked breath before answering. "An Evil Spirit."

"An Evil Spirit...?"

"You've heard ghost stories as a child, haven't you? That's what it is. No, to be precise, I should probably call it a Bound Spirit. It's the soul of someone who died in this forest, unable to move on. It's become an Evil Spirit, trapped here by the forest's Demonic Energy." The explanation seemed to drain the last of his strength, and Aladur's legs gave out, sending him collapsing to the ground.

"Don't be too alarmed by something like this," Aladur said from the ground. "That kind of Evil Spirit doesn't have much power. You won't be possessed as long as you keep your wits about you. Well... that's not always the case, though."

His face clouded over. He explained that powerful Evil Spirits existed, ones that could easily shatter a human's mind and steal their body.

"If we run into a powerful one, even I can't do anything. My exorcism skills are rudimentary at best. So please," he pleaded, "keep your minds straight. If your spirit weakens, you could lose your body to a pathetic Evil Spirit, no matter how strong your martial arts make you."

Jaegal Taeryeong bit his lower lip hard. While the Jaegal Clan was famous for its formations and knowledge as well as its martial arts, he unfortunately excelled only in the latter. It had never inconvenienced him before. The Jaegal Clan was a murim Noble Clan, after all, and martial arts were what mattered most.

*I should have brought my uncle.*

The thought was useless. It was far too late for that. His picky uncle would never come just because he was called, and the journey back to the clan would take over a week.

"Get up," Jaegal Taeryeong spat, his glare fixed on Moyong Seojin.

She staggered to her feet under his fierce gaze, and he immediately spun around and stalked away. Moyong Seojin thought him cruel but could not bring herself to blame him. She unwrapped the robe and handed it back to Namgung Heewon.

"...Thank you."

"It's alright," Namgung Heewon replied, his gaze lowered.

As Jaegal Taeryeong led the other martial artists back toward the camp, Namgung Heewon sent his Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps ahead before extending a hand to the still-seated Aladur.

"...I want to apologize for directing my killing intent at you earlier."

"So you're even speaking formally to me now," Aladur sneered. "The renowned Sword Dragon of the Namgung Clan himself. Should I consider this an honor?"

Namgung Heewon remained unoffended by the jibe. Surprised, Aladur took the offered hand and allowed himself to be pulled to his feet.

"If a sorcerer more skilled than you were to come," Namgung Heewon asked, "could we get help breaking through this forest?"

"If one came, yes, but there's probably no one who would agree to it. Sorcerers know the dangers of this forest better than anyone."

"...Huu!" Namgung Heewon sighed, a sound of resignation. Just then, a boy in a black suit rose from the shadows at his feet.

"Did you call?"

"Gasp!" Aladur choked out, startled.

Namgung Heewon ignored him and addressed the boy directly. "I'd like to broker for a sorcerer who can enter the Bewitching Forest and support us right now."

"Could you please wait a moment?" The boy vanished as suddenly as he had appeared.

Aladur stared, his jaw hanging open. "...I see," he muttered, looking at Namgung Heewon. "The Sword Dragon. You're also a member of Erebrisa..."

"You know of them?"

"Only the name. A brokerage guild with brokers who appear like ghosts anywhere in the world. My master had contact with them once but refused to connect. It's surprising that you're a member... but you probably won't find any sorcerers they connect you with who will agree to come to the Bewitching Forest. They're in it for profit, and there's no profit in this."

"I can pay any amount of money."

"Keke! Money is a wonderful thing, but it's not omnipotent. You can't buy your life with it. If I hadn't been caught by that bastard Jaegal, I'd never have come to this forest either."

Namgung Heewon didn't blame Aladur for the insult. He disliked Jaegal Taeryeong just as much. A moment later, the broker reappeared.

"I apologize. I asked the nearest sorcerers in the vicinity, but they all recoiled and refused the moment I mentioned the Bewitching Forest."

"Hahaha!" Aladur burst out laughing at the broker's words. "See? What did I tell you?" he said, as if he had known all along.

While Moyong Seojin's scream caused a small commotion in one camp, Lee Sungmin, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, and Rubia were calmly observing an Evil Spirit of their own. It swayed just beyond their campfire, causing Rubia to hold her breath and the Crazy Heavenly Demon to frown.

[It's just a trivial lesser spirit,] the Void Monarch said. [Pay it no mind.]

Lee Sungmin simply stared at the thing as it swayed in the darkness. An unintelligible noise seemed to ring in his ears as he watched, but it caused him no discomfort. He had grown accustomed to such trivial sounds on Mount Mushii, and the 2100 years in the pure white world had subjected him to far worse during his bouts of lunacy.

Under Lee Sungmin's steady gaze, the Evil Spirit's body trembled. Its twisting form unraveled and began to change shape. Lee Sungmin's eyes narrowed as the noise in his ears grew clearer, coalescing into distinct words.

*This... way...*

His eyes widened. The sound reached not only him but also the Void Monarch, who was spiritually connected to him.

[What? The Evil Spirit is talking...?]

The Void Monarch was incredulous. Most Evil Spirits could not speak. As undead beings whose souls were bound after death, they had lost their reason, leaving only wickedness behind. A higher-ranked spirit might regain its senses, but this one was incredibly crude.

*What kind of trick is this?*

[I'm not sure,] the Void Monarch admitted. [It's impossible for a lesser spirit to speak. How about we go and check it out?]

Since even he could not say for sure, he had no choice but to suggest they investigate. Lee Sungmin rose cautiously to his feet. Though his sharp senses allowed him to see clearly in the dark, he reached for a torch anyway.

"You don't need to do that," Rubia grumbled, conjuring a small orb of light with her magic.

Relying on the two light sources, the three of them approached the Evil Spirit. Lee Sungmin did not let down his guard. The Void Monarch had already advised him that while defeating a bodiless spirit with physical attacks was difficult, it was not impossible.

*This way...*

The mumble grew clearer as they drew near. The spirit watched them approach, then turned its body and began to slide across the ground. Lee Sungmin's party followed.

The forest felt even more ominous at night. Tangled branches took on the shapes of demonic faces, and the rustling of bushes sounded like muffled sobs. The Evil Spirit moved slowly but without hesitation, leading them on a path that seemed to circle back on itself. After this repeated a few times, the air suddenly shifted.

WHOOSH!

The scenery, once faint and soaked in darkness, sharpened into focus.

[This is...] Surprise colored the Void Monarch's voice.

Lee Sungmin looked around to see whitish lights circling them. They were similar yet different from the will-o'-the-wisps he remembered from the Sleeping Forest, where he had first met his companion.

[Could it be...!]

With a mental shout, the Void Monarch expanded his presence. A sinister light pulsed from Lee Sungmin's Magic Armor as a hazy form of Demonic Energy rose from his body. The Void Monarch moved with this spectral form, gathering his arms before his Demonic Energy suddenly scattered. It became hundreds, then thousands of will-o'-the-wisps, just like in the Sleeping Forest, which swirled out in all directions to circle the other spirits.

*Ahh...*

*Oh...*

Halting gasps of awe escaped the Evil Spirits. They bowed their heads and raised their hands in a clear act of worship. Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon could only watch, their mouths half-open. Before long, the scattered lights returned, coalescing back into Lee Sungmin.

[I understand now,] the Void Monarch's voice rang with excitement. [This forest was mutated by my Demonic Energy. A lot of time has passed, and it has become its own entity, but a part of it is still connected to me. These Evil Spirits have been influenced by that connection and are subservient to me!]

Even after four hundred long years, the influence of the one who had once ruled this land had not completely vanished. Lee Sungmin looked back at the bowing spirits and asked, "Are you saying they can help us?"

[Of course! They're part of this forest, so the barrier doesn't affect them. They can find their way freely in a place where others would wander until they die.] A hint of pride crept into his voice as he puffed out his chest in satisfaction and said, [Hahaha! To think I still wield this much influence even after being dead for four hundred years. Isn't this Elder quite something?]

"Uh... yeah," Lee Sungmin nodded reluctantly.

He had never imagined he would end up being guided by an Evil Spirit. Still, it was not a bad thing. He had just gained an undying guide in a forest that even the most skilled sorcerers and guides refused to enter.
