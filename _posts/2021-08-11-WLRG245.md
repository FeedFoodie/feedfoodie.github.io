Moyong Chan and Moyong Daeun were alive, and against all odds, so was Chwi Geol.

*It seems I'm quite resilient to bad luck.*

Chwi Geol thought to himself, reflecting on how he had once again survived in the face of certain death. It had been the same in the past, in the Dungeon he entered with the Little Heavenly Demon, and it was the same now.

Of course, the subjugation was not yet over, but he knew it was impossible to continue. The Mages Guild's position was irrelevant. To press on after suffering such extensive damage would be nothing more than a pointless death. By now, the Phantom Spear must have entered the forest as well, especially if he wanted to protect the Crimson Archmage.

*A direct confrontation with the Phantom Spear is out of the question... There's no chance of winning.*

He had seen the Moyong Patriarch treated like a child. The scattered warriors of the Pure White Martial Squad and the Moyong Clan must have suffered great losses throughout the forest. If they clashed with Lee Sungmin in this state, they would not escape a pointless death. Even attacking with full fighting spirit would only lead to the same end.

To ascend to the Transcendent Realm, talent and outstanding martial arts were not enough. One had to be chosen by the heavens.

Destiny. Yes, that was what his master, the Beggars' Gang Chief, had called it. The Chief himself was the greatest master of the Beggars' Gang and had practiced martial arts for a very long time, yet he still had not broken through the wall of the Transcendent Realm.

It was a state so high that it was said to require destiny. How many martial artists had practiced until now? The number would surely exceed tens of thousands, yet among them, the only confirmed masters of the Transcendent Realm were the Wudang Sword Immortal, the Murim Alliance Chairman Black Dragon Knight, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman Demon Emperor.

*And now the Phantom Spear. Destiny... so it's destiny.*

Chwi Geol smiled bitterly. He had never wanted to believe in such a vague concept, but considering he had survived this long on sheer luck, could destiny truly exist? Perhaps even he could one day enter that realm.

He pushed the vague thought aside for a moment and glanced at Moyong Daeun. Persuading the man was impossible. He was hell-bent on killing the Phantom Spear to avenge his daughter.

*Securing the Crimson Archmage here is impossible, and continuing the subjugation is also impossible.*

If that was the case, the answer was simple. First, they had to get out of this forest. Chwi Geol approached Moyong Daeun, ready to use the Chairman's order as an excuse to escape.

"I have an order from the Murim Alliance," he began.

Returning to the edge of the pit, Kim Jonghyun threw back the hood he was wearing. He had already grasped the situation unfolding in the forest and anticipated what would happen next.

Everyone would know that the subjugation had failed. The Mages Guild would give up and leave. Their insides might be churning with frustration, but they would not throw their lives away for nothing. The Murim Alliance would be no different.

Still, he erected a defensive barrier just in case and had the Demon Races stand guard as escorts. Even if someone attacked, he could handle them without any trouble, but what if Lee Sungmin himself came? He saw no reason for it, but if he did…

How long could they hold out?

They would not last long. He had raised a thick defensive barrier and used the four surviving Demon Races as shields, but it would not be enough to stop Lee Sungmin.

*It's fine. Buying just a little time is enough.*

This ritual would not take long.

Kim Jonghyun slowly descended into the pit piled high with hearts. The gate was continuously widening, a chilling and ominous wind from the Demon Realm pouring out from the growing rift. In the center of it all, he listened to the chorus of beating hearts.

Reversal Magic. This was a magic that reversed the very nature of a species, a magic that could make a human no longer human. Its applications were endless. Depending on how it was used, it was not a difficult task to turn an Undead back into a person. In his memories, it was this Reversal Magic that Arbes had coveted most.

Arbes, unable to handle the Grimoire's power, had devised soul transition magic as a last resort, but since Kim Jonghyun could wield the Grimoire, using Reversal Magic posed no major problem.

The only issue was the price. The being he sought to reverse demanded a cost so great that it had required the annihilation of seven villages and the luring of an entire subjugation force to this place.

The hearts throbbed. The collected fear and souls swirled together with the wind from the Demon Realm. Kim Jonghyun felt the demonic air, a breath he was taking for the first time, was not unfamiliar at all. The Demon Lord's magic power within him rejoiced in the wind of its homeland.

He spread his arms wide. The resonating hearts began to melt, dissolving into a dark red liquid of blood that drenched his body. The ritual had begun.

As his human form collapsed, a new body was formed from the sacrificial hearts, souls, and fear. Guided by the Demon Lord's magic power that he possessed, the Reversal Magic was changing the 'human' that was Kim Jonghyun into a being closer to a Demon Lord than a man.

*It's a success.*

There was no pain. What filled his body and consciousness was a ferocious ecstasy.

It was all according to plan.

Up to this point.

KWARURURUNG!

A black light shot down from the high sky, shaking his mind to its core. The ritual, which had been so steadily progressing towards completion, suffered a fatal blow.

"Wha, what!?"

Kim Jonghyun cried out, his composure shattering for the first time in a long while. He quickly raised his head.

He scrambled to check the magic he had deployed. Lee Sungmin had not yet broken through the defensive barrier. The one interfering and attacking him right now was someone other than Lee Sungmin.

From within the blood that covered his body, Kim Jonghyun snapped his head up. Black clouds were swirling in the center of the sky.

*Oh my god.*

What slowly revealed itself from within the clouds was a Dragon.

Before Kim Jonghyun could complete a defensive spell, the Dragon opened its mouth to fire its breath again. This time, its target was not him. The gate, which had been on the verge of fully opening, was struck directly by the breath and began to collapse.

"No!" Kim Jonghyun screamed.

The Dragon that had fired the breath was enveloped in a black light. When the light faded, a middle-aged man wearing a wide dragon robe stood in its place.

"I flew this long distance because of you."

It was the Black Dragon Knight.

It was only yesterday that he had received an urgent request from the Spirit Medium. The Phantom Spear was not the problem, she had said. He had to stop Kim Jonghyun's ritual. He had retorted that if that was the problem, they could just send the Spear King who was nearby, but the Spirit Medium had insisted that 'you, the Black Dragon Knight, must go yourself'.

The Spirit Medium's words were the Spirit's words. He could not disobey. Thanks to that, the Black Dragon Knight had been forced to fly the long distance from the Murim Alliance all the way to this place. He had flown for an entire day and had barely made it in time, though completely stopping the ritual had been impossible.

*I don't know why on earth I had to come myself, though.*

There must have been a good reason for the Spirit Medium to insist so much. The Black Dragon Knight grumbled as he slowly descended. As a half-human, half-dragon, he could use a portion of a Dragon's power, but he could not fathom the will of the Spirit that the Spirit Medium possessed.

"How annoying. You Black Mage bastard," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, looking down at Kim Jonghyun submerged in the pool of blood in the pit.

The ritual, which had been on the verge of completion, failed due to his intervention. Reversal Magic could only be cast once. No matter how many more sacrifices he offered or how many times he tried to perform the ritual again, casting the magic was now impossible.

*That damn lizard bastard…!*

He did not know who the Black Dragon Knight was, but he had never imagined he would be interrupted by a Dragon. Why would a Dragon, which had vanished hundreds of years ago, suddenly appear and ruin everything at the last moment?

However, contrary to his boiling rage, Kim Jonghyun could not act on his emotions. Because the ritual had stopped midway without being completed, his reconstructing body was not fully formed. It was a relief that it ended here. If the Black Dragon Knight had appeared just a little sooner and shattered the gate, the ritual would have failed before his body could even reconstruct, and he would have simply vanished.

"I wasn't told to kill you, though," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, looking down at Kim Jonghyun.

What he had heard from the Spirit Medium was to prevent the ritual from being completed, not to kill Kim Jonghyun. Therefore, the Black Dragon Knight had to ponder for a moment. The directives given by the Spirit Medium through Spirit Possession were always incomprehensible, but he 'had to' follow them exactly as they were given.

*There was no order to kill.*

If so, there was no need to go out of his way to do something he did not have to. Flying all the way here was quite a hassle. The Black Dragon Knight grumbled while rubbing his shoulder muscles. A real Dragon could handle all sorts of magic, including Polymorph Magic, and even perform Spatial Movement, but the Black Dragon Knight could not use magic. He could barely change his form between a Dragon and a human, and Spatial Movement was out of the question. He had inherited the physical abilities of a Dragon, such as its immense strength, scales, and bones, from a father he did not even know, but he had not inherited the Dragonic and magic that made a Dragon a Dragon.

The Black Dragon Knight left the immobile Kim Jonghyun and turned away. He had no more business here.

*No.*

The Black Dragon Knight's thoughts turned to the report he had heard from Chwi Geol. The Crimson Archmage. There was no need to send the Assassination Squad and waste them, was there? Since he had come all this way, taking the Crimson Archmage himself would make everything simple.

*If it's that easy, that is.*

He might end up clashing with the Phantom Spear, but the Black Dragon Knight was not too worried. He had no intention of killing the Phantom Spear. He had not received such an order from the Spirit Medium. As per the Martial God's will, the handling of the Phantom Spear would be left to the Spear King. He would just secure the Crimson Archmage.

*The Magic Tower Masters are alive.*

What to do about this. Kidnapping the Crimson Archmage in front of them would not look very good. Should he make up a rough excuse involving the Phantom Spear? He had planned to use such an excuse from the beginning when he ordered Chwi Geol and the Assassination Squad to be sent. Still, for the Murim Alliance Chairman to come and kidnap the Crimson Archmage himself did not look good, did it?

*It probably won't be a big problem, though.*

The Black Dragon Knight did not think much of it. This forest. Countless members of the subjugation force had already died. Even if a few more corpses were added here, no one would suspect a thing. Even if the great Magic Tower Masters of the Mages Guild were all killed and turned into corpses, people would just think it was a tragedy caused by that oh-so-great Demon King of the North.

It would be fine as long as he killed all the witnesses, would it not?

{sep}

Tang Ahee could not keep up with Lee Sungmin, whose speed was simply too great. In truth, at this point, Lee Sungmin no longer felt the need to protect her. The darkness of the forest had vanished, and the demonic beasts, Chimeras, Undead, and Demon Races that had been roaming inside had all gathered around Kim Jonghyun.

There was no longer any need to protect Tang Ahee. If she survived and had her birthday in a few months, he could use that opportunity to meet the Dark Supreme.

"......"

While running toward Scarlet's location, Lee Sungmin's steps came to a halt.

KUUUUNG!

The ground shook as if there was an earthquake. A chill ran down his spine, and Lee Sungmin raised his head to look at the sky.

It was the moment the Black Dragon Knight attacked Kim Jonghyun, and when Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky, the black clouds that had gathered were scattering.

*What is that?*

Lee Sungmin stood rooted to the spot, staring at the sky where the dark clouds were dispersing.
