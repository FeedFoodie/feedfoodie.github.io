The Sword Saint's words shattered a silence that had lasted for years, leaving the Fist Honored One utterly speechless. He had appeared from nowhere, after all this time, only to pronounce what sounded like a death sentence.

That he might die.

Fist Honored One stared, his face pale, at the man before him.

"...Die?"

The word was a choked whisper, barely escaping his lips after a long, stunned silence.

The Sword Saint gave a silent nod. At this confirmation, the Fist Honored One's face twisted in disbelief. He struggled to raise his stiff upper body, his voice rising to a shout.

"I'm... I'm going to die? Me?!"

"Calm down," the Sword Saint said, his own face a mask of tranquility.

However, Fist Honored One could not calm down. Elves lived for hundreds of years. He himself had already seen four centuries pass, and still had so much of his natural lifespan left. Having entered the Transcendent Realm, he was destined to live even longer than that. Though he had lived for four hundred years, he had so much more time ahead of him.

The thought was unacceptable. Being an Elf did not make him indifferent to his own mortality. His shoulders trembled as he spat out the words.

"The Spirit Medium... he said it?! That I'm going to die?!"

"'That you *might* die,' he said."

The Sword Saint's tone was serene, a stark contrast to the Fist Honored One's violent agitation. He continued speaking, his gaze fixed on the other man's face.

"I came here to prevent that. The Spirit Medium said you might die, but he didn't say who would kill you, or why, or how. Since it isn't a certain death, it can be prevented."

"But...!"

"Don't be agitated. Am I not here? Or do you think I'm not enough?"

"He said someone might kill me... The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Could it be that Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon is coming for me? Yang Ilcheon took in the Little Heavenly Demon. Maybe after making her his disciple, he's coming to kill me to end her curse..."

"That possibility is slim," the Sword Saint replied. "Although the Martial God has entered seclusion, his very existence keeps the Demon Emperor in check. I don't think Yang Ilcheon would turn against us over the Little Heavenly Demon."

"The Sword Immortal. Could the Wudang Sword Immortal be coming to kill me?"

"That's even more nonsensical. The Sword Immortal doesn't leave Wudang. Do you really think that old man, playing at being a deity who has left the mortal world, would come out of his mountain just to kill you?"

"Then who in the world!"

"You are weak right now," the Sword Saint stated, his calm voice cutting through the Fist Honored One's outburst.

At his words, the Fist Honored One's face hardened.

"While you maintain the curse, you are not a master of the Transcendent Realm. As you are now, even someone at the peak realm could kill you without difficulty. There's no need for the Demon Emperor or the Sword Immortal. Even a nameless, insignificant nobody could come and kill you."

"Why I'm in this state..."

"I know. It's for Heaven Beyond Heaven's grand design, is it not? Although things went awry because of the Demon Emperor... the curse on the Little Heavenly Demon must be maintained. That is why I came. To prevent your death. So you don't have to worry about a thing."

The Sword Saint's words, spoken as if they were a matter of course, were filled with an unshakeable conviction. He was a master worthy of such confidence. Among the Six Honored Ones, excluding the Spear King and Moon Queen, the Sword Saint was considered the strongest. Blade Supreme saw him as a rival, but everyone except Blade Supreme knew the truth. The Sword Saint's sword was a cut above his.

"You will not die," the Sword Saint said, staring directly at the Fist Honored One. "Because I will not let it happen."

At those words, the Fist Honored One's stiff expression finally relaxed a fraction.

*Right, it's fine.*

As long as the Sword Saint himself was here, his death would not come to pass. Fist Honored One chewed on his lower lip, though the thought did not completely resolve his displeasure and worry. The very fact that the Spirit Medium had foreseen such a possibility clouded his mind.

*Who in the world could it be?*

He had no intention of leaving this forest. He was determined to stay as long as he was maintaining the curse, yet, if the Spirit Medium had foreseen his death, it meant someone would invade the Elf Forest to kill him. What kind of madman would come all the way into the Elf Forest? And why go to such lengths? A grudge? The only person who came to mind at the word 'grudge' was the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon. The title of 'the Phantom Spear' was so insignificant to him that he did not even recall it.

Lee Sungmin stared into a forest that seemed to exist on a different axis of time. He had seen many forests in his travels, but the Forest of Ancients was more alien than any he had ever encountered. The trees were of an incomparable mass, soaring into a sky barely visible through their canopy.

It was not just the trees. Everything here was huge. Lee Sungmin glanced at a nearby flower. It was a nameless wildflower, yet it stood as tall as a small child. The fragrance it emitted was as intense as its size, but he had no desire to stop and smell wildflowers.

*Who could it be.*

Before entering the Forest of Ancients, Lee Sungmin had done his research. Though it was called the Elf Forest, Elves were not its only inhabitants. It was a place of beasts so large they were nearly monsters, with the Elf village hidden deep within its heart. This was the largest known Elf habitat on the continent, yet despite being so well known, almost no one dared to target it.

Elves had high value as slaves, but enslaving one would earn you the grudge of their entire race. Of course, the world was full of fools who would risk such a thing. Nevertheless, they did not come to this forest.

Elves are loved by the forest. The 'beasts' that lived here, not monsters, communed with the Elves, and the forest itself moved to protect them. That racial ability was a terrifyingly good match for the Forest of Ancients, where everything was gigantic. Countless people had targeted the Elves of this forest over the years, but not one had ever managed to cross it and reach their village. Thus, this place became a forest for Elves alone.

However... just a few days ago, someone was said to have entered this forest alone.

Lee Sungmin recalled the Forest Keeper he had met at the entrance. Elves were not ferocious. They were rational beings who knew they could not get everything they needed just by living in the woods. That was why a Forest Keeper was stationed at the entrance, trading necessary goods with merchants on their behalf.

The Forest Keeper had told him that someone had entered the forest a few days ago. When asked his destination, the man had nonchalantly replied that he was going to meet the Elves. After hearing the full story, Lee Sungmin had pressed the Keeper's acupoints, putting him to sleep. He figured things would get troublesome if the man unnecessarily relayed the news.

[A man with a sword at his waist... was it? Well, the world is full of guys carryin' swords.]

Void Monarch grumbled. It might have been different if he at least knew the man's name. With 'carrying a sword' as the only characteristic, it was impossible to deduce his identity. Lee Sungmin was bothered by this mysterious man, but he did not dwell on it.

*Have you ever met an Elf?*

[I have. Slept with plenty of 'em, too.]

*How were they?*

[What? Why, you curious about what kind of nights this Elder spent with Elves?]

*No, not that. I mean the Elf race.*

Lee Sungmin scowled and snapped at Void Monarch. Celgerus was a Dark Elf, but Elves and Dark Elves were to be considered almost different species.

[Didn't you hear about Elves from that lanky bastard?]

As Void Monarch said, Lee Sungmin had already heard the points of caution from Nebul. Elves are loved by the forest and possess superior Mana and Elemental affinity compared to humans. Their Mana affinity allows them to easily handle Mana and cast magic, while their Elemental affinity lets them form contracts with Elementals with little effort. In other words, Elves were naturally gifted mages and Elementalists.

[Their senses are fundamentally different from humans. That sixth sense you guys get from trainin' your asses off? Elves are born with it. Also, thanks to the forest's protection, they don't get tired in the woods. Their eyes are sharp, and their ears are sharp,] Void Monarch grumbled on. [They gain all sorts of advantages inside a forest. The beasts, the insects. Everything is an Elf's ally. Fightin' an Elf inside a forest is a foolish thing to do. They become several times stronger than their actual skill level. Perhaps even now...]

Before Void Monarch could finish, a sudden wave of hostility washed over him, and Lee Sungmin froze.

THWAP! THWAP! THWAP!

Five arrows embedded themselves in the ground right before his feet.

[As expected,] Void Monarch said with a chuckle.

Lee Sungmin looked down at the deeply buried arrows, then raised his head. His gaze swept in the direction they had come from, finding nothing at first. He focused his vision, pushing it farther, until he saw him. An Elf, bow in hand, perched in the distant branches.

Their eyes met. The Elf looked flustered, as if he had never imagined he would be spotted from such a distance. He immediately leaped from the branch. Lee Sungmin did not lose sight of his movements.

*Movement arts?*

An Elf using martial arts? Living so much longer than humans, having all sorts of advantages, and now using martial arts on top of that... wasn't that cheating?

With that thought, Lee Sungmin unleashed the Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

WHOOSH!

His body shot across the space in an instant. Although the Elf had racial advantages, he did not seem that fast to Lee Sungmin. It was only natural. Lee Sungmin was on the verge of the Transcendent Realm, and his body, which had become close to a Yokai, was comparable to that of a Transcendent master.

"Gasp!"

The Elf let out a startled sound, seeing Lee Sungmin close the distance in a flash. Even so, the moment he landed on a nearby branch, he aimed his bow.

Lee Sungmin bent his body back in mid-air and then shot forward, accelerating even faster. An arrow flew toward his linear path. Just as the sharp arrowhead was about to reach his face, Lee Sungmin's body collapsed into an afterimage.

The Elf, however, did not panic. From Lee Sungmin's approach speed, he already knew he was facing a master with abilities surpassing a human's. Something hazy drifted around him. It was a wind Elemental. When the Elf leaped from the branch, the Elemental supported his body, allowing him to fly freely through the air. Five consecutively fired arrows, aided by the wind, changed direction to surround Lee Sungmin from all sides.

WHOOSH!

Purple enhanced qi erupted from Lee Sungmin's body. The Protective Qi Aura flared to life, incinerating all the arrows. The wind Elemental holding them shrieked and dispersed.

Lee Sungmin did not even swing his spear. He raised a hand, gathering the enhanced qi of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, and it became a palm strike from the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

BAAANG!

The force that shot from his hand engulfed the Elf. The Elf hastily wrapped himself in the wind Elemental and cast a defensive magic, but he could not completely block the attack.

"Keuheuk!"

His body fell onto a large tree branch. It was only to this extent because Lee Sungmin had controlled his power. If he had put his mind to it, the Elf's body would have burst like a water balloon.

Lee Sungmin kicked off the air, crossing the space and landing in front of the fallen Elf. Lying there with a pale face, the Elf was as beautiful as one would expect from his race. It was hard to tell if he was male or female, but a prominent Adam's apple gave him away.

Lee Sungmin approached and grabbed him by the throat.

"A sentry?" he asked.

The Elf looked up at him with eyes full of disbelief. Lee Sungmin felt the need to suppress that attitude and tightened his grip. The Elf struggled, making choking sounds from the pressure.

"I have business in your village. I'd like you to guide me."

"Keu... keuleuk...!"

The Elf made pained noises, but the defiance in his eyes did not waver.

Lee Sungmin acted without hesitation.

SLAP!

His swinging hand struck the Elf's cheek, snapping his head to the side.

"You may have lived longer than me, but I doubt you want to die, right?"

"Geureureuk..."

The Elf began to struggle again. *Should I pull out a few teeth?* Lee Sungmin thought as he looked at the Elf's white teeth. His hand moved toward the Elf's open mouth, planning to pull out the most visible front tooth first.

However, Lee Sungmin never got the chance. Dozens of arrows were suddenly aimed at his body.

"I don't want to fight all of you," Lee Sungmin grumbled as he leaped away, still holding the Elf by the neck.
