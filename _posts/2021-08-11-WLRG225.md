"Wow, you little punk," Hans chuckled, extending a hand. "You've changed a lot. You've shot up, haven't you? Broader shoulders, different face entirely."

Lee Sungmin smiled back and firmly clasped the offered hand.

"What brings you here?" Hans asked, plopping back down onto his spot. "This isn't exactly a place worth coming back to."

"I had some business near Behenger, so I was in the area. I started thinking about the old days and decided to stop by Zenavis."

"What old days? How long did you even live here?"

"I heard rumors about you from time to time," Hans said, glancing around. "Though, I couldn't be sure if it was really you."

"What kind of rumors?"

"The Phantom Spear," Hans said, his voice a little lower. "They weren't very good rumors, though."

"They're rumors with a lot of misunderstandings."

"So that means you *are* the Phantom Spear. Good heavens." Hans clicked his tongue in amazement. "To think that little kid from thirteen years ago became a celebrity with a title like that. Wow… you never know what life has in store."

He muttered to himself, his gaze drifting over the goods spread out on his mat.

"Thirteen years ago... It was so long ago I barely remember it, but I do recall why I was pretty nice to you. How should I put it? It was a strange thing, but looking at you never felt like I was looking at a stranger. I don't even have a younger brother, but I thought, if I did, would it feel like this? Yeah, that's exactly how it felt."

It was because of the Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena.

"Well, feelings aside," Hans continued, "I always thought you'd become someone great. That's why I tried to get on your good side in advance, selling you things cheaply and all. I've seen a lot of Otherworlders and Classless in this city, and among the Classless, I'd rarely seen anyone as tenacious and fast-growing as you."

That was understandable. The talent Lee Sungmin possessed at the time was not great, but the knowledge from his past life allowed him to grow quickly even without it. On top of that, his miraculous friendship with Wiji Hoyeon had set him on a path that no other Classless could compare to.

"Have you met Jack?"

"No. I'm about to go now."

"Then you should get going. It's about time for my customers to start showing up." Hans smiled. "Still, it's good to see your face again. Even better to know you're alive and not dead."

Lee Sungmin reached a hand inside his clothes, but Hans quickly raised a hand and shook his head.

"No, don't."

"What do you mean?"

"Don't go giving me money or anything, trying to repay some favor. It was thirteen years ago, so I barely remember it, and nothing I did for you was all that special."

"......"

"It's fine. It's not like I'm doing this because I'm short on money. I've already saved up enough to live on, you know? I'm just doing this because I have nothing else to do and I'm bored."

Since Hans refused so firmly, Lee Sungmin nodded as if he had no choice. Instead, he handed over the Subspace Pocket he had once received from the man.

"In that case, I'll at least return what I received from you back then."

"You punk," Hans chuckled, taking the pocket. He opened it, shoved his hand deep inside, and then laughed as if he had expected it. "I don't remember giving you any jewels."

He sneered, pulling a large jewel from within. Lee Sungmin gave a bitter smile and shook his head.

"You might think they weren't a big deal, but the things you did for me were more than enough back then. Without your help at that time… I would have had a hard time surviving."

"Even so, this is too much. I don't want to accept this much," Hans grumbled, putting his hand back into the pocket. He took out the smallest of the jewels Lee Sungmin had placed inside, though even that single gem would be enough for him to live comfortably for the rest of his life. He grumbled again as he transferred the jewel to his own Subspace Pocket. "Since you're forcing it on me, I can't exactly refuse."

"Just take it, and don't get yourself killed by someone over it."

"......"

After chatting a bit more, Lee Sungmin left the square. It was time to meet Jack, the other person to whom he had been indebted during that year in Zenavis. The city had changed quite a bit, but Jack's Inn remained much the same. The building had been renovated, yet it retained the familiar shape he remembered.

At this hour, the inn's guests would be having breakfast. Recalling the meals he had eaten here thirteen years ago, Lee Sungmin opened the door and stepped inside.

"Welcome!" a cheerful voice rang out.

It was not Jack's voice. Looking toward the sound, he saw a woman with long, tied-up hair peeking out from the kitchen with a smile.

Lula.

Jack's daughter, one year his senior. Memories from thirteen years ago surfaced, and Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh.

"Looking for a room? Or just a meal?" Lula asked, coming out and wiping her hands on her apron.

"It's been a while," Lee Sungmin answered with a chuckle.

At his words, Lula's eyes widened in confusion.

"What is it? A customer?" an aged voice called from deeper within the kitchen.

Hans had gotten older, but Jack, who had already been middle-aged, now looked like an old man. Still, his build was as sturdy as it had been thirteen years ago. Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply to him.

"It's been a long time."

"Who…?"

Just like Hans, neither Jack nor Lula recognized him. He had simply changed too much.

When Lee Sungmin introduced himself, Lula's jaw dropped and Jack's eyes went wide. Their surprise quickly melted into delight, and they led him to a table.

"What's this? Miss the taste of the food you ate when you were a kid?" Jack said with a grin.

Lee Sungmin smiled warmly. Across from him, Lula pouted her lips and looked him up and down.

"You grew up well."

"You too."

"And you still won't call me big sister," she said abruptly. "Did you know I got married?"

Lee Sungmin was not particularly surprised. Lula was almost thirty now, so it was hardly shocking.

"Who did you marry?"

"A Classless who was summoned to this city after you left," Jack answered for her. "He's a guy who knew his place early on and didn't act up, saying he was going off on an adventure. He's a little indecisive, but he's a good guy."

"He reminded me of you," Jack continued, "so I let him stay at the inn on good terms, and in the meantime, he and Lula ended up falling for each other."

"Fell for each other? He was the one who hit on me relentlessly," Lula grumbled, though she did not seem to dislike her husband at all.

*They're doing well.* Lee Sungmin felt a little relieved and nodded.

"Where's your husband?"

"He went to work," Lula answered. "My husband's a carpenter. His skills are just so-so, but he's learning diligently."

While they spoke, Jack brought over some warm soup and bread. He smacked his lips and said, "We don't have much food prepared since it's morning."

"This is more than enough."

Lee Sungmin tore off a piece of bread, dipped it in the soup, and put it in his mouth, just as he had fourteen years ago. It tasted much the same. The meal was short, but the conversation with people he had not seen in so long stretched on. Jack and Lula asked him many things, but they never brought up the rumors. Perhaps they, too, could not imagine that he was the infamous Phantom Spear. Lee Sungmin did not bother to bring it up either.

"I should get going now."

"Already?" Lula asked, her eyes widening as he stood up.

Lee Sungmin nodded. "Yeah. I have business elsewhere."

"You show up so suddenly and now you're leaving just as fast. Why don't you stay the night since you came all this way? The room you used to stay in happens to be empty."

"No, it's okay. I'm really in a hurry."

It was not a lie. He had to stop by the Blue Magic Tower before heading to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. It was quite a distance from here, so he had to hurry.

"Ah, and," Lee Sungmin said, taking a small pouch from his clothes. It was a Subspace Pocket he had acquired before coming here. He placed it on the table. "This is for the food, and a wedding gift. It's way too late, but still."

"There's no need to pay. It was just leftover food anyway," Jack said.

Lee Sungmin did not take back the pocket. He simply gave them both a warm smile. At his expression, Lula tilted her head and was about to say something more, but he did not wait to hear it.

CRACKLE!

Before their eyes, Lee Sungmin vanished in a flash of crackling electricity. He had put quite a few jewels in the Subspace Pocket and knew they might try to refuse it, just as Hans had. To avoid their protests, he had quickly escaped the inn with Gale Thunder.

[Are you done repaying your debts?] Void Monarch asked.

Outside the inn, Lee Sungmin activated his movement arts, speeding through the gates of Zenavis as he answered.

"Yeah."

[You were planning to meet that guy, the Blue Magic Tower Master, first, right? When are you going to the village of the dwarves?]

Dwarves. He was talking about the Dwarves. Lee Sungmin had always planned to visit their village someday. The Spirit Queen had enabled him to consume the Dragon Heart, but he still had not been able to process the dragon's scales, teeth, and bones. To do that, he had to visit the Dwarf Village and meet their chief.

He knew the method, but he could not just go there without a plan. Even if he found the way, there was no guarantee that a meeting with the chief would even happen. That problem, however, had been solved thanks to Osla, the Fairy Queen.

He recalled his meeting with Osla before leaving the forest. He had thought he might be able to get something out of the favor she supposedly held for him. Osla had seemed dumbfounded by his open demand for proof of her favor, but she had not refused and offered her help. The fact that a meeting with the Dwarf chief was now possible was a direct result of that help. He thought of the letter from Osla tucked away in his Subspace Pocket, a letter she had written herself to the chief of the Dwarves.

*I can't stop by the Dwarf Village on the way. I'll go later if I have time.*

[Not necessarily, right? If you rode the 'horse', you could get there in no time.]

*I can only summon it three times, and I don't want to use it for something like this. I want to save it for a moment when I really need it. Besides, I've never been to the Dwarf Village, so even if I summoned the horse, it would be impossible to get there.*

The 'horse' Void Monarch mentioned was another gift from Osla. He could only summon it three times, but it could instantly take him anywhere in the world, as long as he had been there before. He had no intention of using it now.

After passing through the gates of Zenavis, Lee Sungmin ran without rest, heading toward Odun, where Syaos, the Blue Magic Tower Master, resided.
