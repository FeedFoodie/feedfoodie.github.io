A demonic dog burst from the forest, but Lee Sungmin remained unflustered. He did not even reach for his spear. Instead, he simply raised an index finger and pointed it at the charging beast. Sparks of electricity crackled at his fingertip before a straight line of purple light shot out, piercing the demonic dog's head.

Any notions of tough hide or regenerative abilities were rendered meaningless. The enhanced qi of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder tore through the demonic dog's body, causing it to go limp in an instant. Lee Sungmin heard nothing, not the screams that erupted from all directions, not a single sound. The demonic beasts that emerged from the trees posed no threat to him. He was a predator, a nemesis operating on a plane far above the creatures Kim Jonghyun had unleashed in this forest. Even with his senses blocked, the Protective Qi Aura of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder manifested to completely shield his body.

It was not just demonic dogs. A varied and uniformly ferocious menagerie of demonic beasts, each with a vicious appearance, swarmed the woods. Nevertheless, they could not get close. The Chimera and other summoned beasts created by Arbes were hunted down the moment Lee Sungmin found them.

However, not everyone who had come to this forest as part of the subjugation force was as strong as Lee Sungmin. Their greatest mistake was not entering the forest with him. If they had not doubted him, if they had entered this forest together as a unified force...

Nothing would have changed.

{sep}

Lloyd steadied his breathing, his brow furrowed in a deep frown. The moment they entered the forest, an absurd magic had scattered them from the other subjugation teams. Magic, with enough preparation, could generate firepower beyond common sense, a principle the Mage Army, the pride of the Mages Guild, was built upon. Its ranks were composed of combat mages trained to focus on that very potential. In a situation like this, however, they could not exert much of their power. The sixty combat mages dispatched for this mission were meant to link their spells together to maximize their firepower, and they had prepared these linked incantations long before the subjugation began, but upon entering the forest and being scattered, the combat mages lost their advantage.

They had never anticipated a situation like this. They knew Kim Jonghyun had prepared some kind of trap, but they never imagined it would be this kind of magic. To twist the space of the entire forest and scatter everyone was not a feat a human should be able to perform.

Lloyd's situation was relatively better than most. The Green Tower Master was by his side, as were warriors from the Murim Alliance. In this chaotic state of random separation, being with trustworthy and proven individuals could be called a stroke of luck, yet even with such fortune, it was not easy to face the beasts that could leap out of the darkness at any moment. Even an archmage of his caliber did not possess infinite magic power, nor did he have a spell prepared for every possible contingency. A mage's role was to be protected in the rear, unleashing prepared magic from a safe position. This chaotic melee was the worst-case scenario.

*Kim Jonghyun is a mage. He knows exactly how to kill a mage.*

Of course, the subjugation force knew this as well and had prepared many ways to counter him. None of them were working. Scattered as they were, coordinating with each other was impossible. The anticipated firepower of the Mage Army could not be brought to bear. They had established a shield to mark the front lines with support from the Murim Alliance and the mercenary group, but they had been separated from them as well.

Lloyd glanced at the Green Tower Master, who had wiped the relaxed smile from his face. His expression was now filled with anxiety and tension.

Separated from them, the Holy Knight Commander was with some of the Holy Knights he commanded and a portion of the Mage Army. The Holy Knight Commander and his knights, however, felt no such fear. They countered their terror with steadfast faith and were enraged by the emerging demonic beasts and Chimera. There was no hesitation in their movements. The Holy Knight Commander took the lead, and the knights following him were not paralyzed by fear. They roared at the demonic beasts, swinging their swords and unleashing their holy power.

Meanwhile, the Archbishop and the other bishops did not move. They knelt, not because they had collapsed in fear, but in prayer. A pure white light emanated from the center of their group, a desperate attempt to dispel the Dark Magic that enveloped the entire forest.

{sep}

Chwi Geol was with the Pure White Martial Squad and the Moyong Patriarch. They had no intention of running away. Moyong Daeun was searching for Lee Sungmin, who he knew would be entering this forest, and fortunately, Moyong Chan was with his father. Chwi Geol, for his part, was looking for Scarlet rather than trying to kill Kim Jonghyun. His orders from the Murim Alliance were to secure her.

Tang Ahee, however, was only trying to escape. Shih Tzu, an executive of the Mad Dog Mercenaries who had been with her, had just died. He had been a mercenary of outstanding skill, but he could not handle the Undead that leaped out of the darkness. A headless monster, a Dullahan, had just cleaved Shih Tzu in two from the top of his head. The creature held its severed head in its left hand while its right swung a massive axe.

*This is insane.*

The Dullahan was a powerful monster among the various types of Undead. It was a much lower class than a Lich or a Death Knight and possessed almost no reason, but its physical abilities were not to be taken lightly. Not only Shih Tzu but other mercenaries were also cut down by the merciless axe. The combat mages with Tang Ahee frantically cast magic, but their spells only managed to stop the Dullahan for a moment.

Tang Ahee had not tried to flee recklessly at first. She had her pride as the daughter of the Tang Patriarch and was confident in the hidden weapons techniques and martial arts she had learned, but the hidden weapons she threw and the poisons coating them proved largely ineffective against the Undead creature.

So she had no choice but to run. She had no desire to die here. No, she never wanted to die. Tang Ahee wanted to live as long as possible. After throwing the last of the hidden weapons in her hand at the Dullahan, she whipped around and tried to flee.

However, escape was not so simple. The very ground swelled beneath her, and crimson tentacles shot up. Terrified, Tang Ahee swung her dagger to cut them, but the tentacles writhed around her blade, refusing to be severed, and tried to wrap around her body.

"Kyaaaaak!"

Tang Ahee screamed. In that brief moment of struggle, the Dullahan was upon her. The warriors and mages of the Tang Clan she had abandoned had not even managed to buy her much time. She did not even sense the blood-dripping axe as it rose high above her head.

"......"

Before the axe could fall and split her head, a streak of purple enhanced qi pierced the Dullahan's arm. It slid sideways, completely severing the creature's wrist. The headless Dullahan could not even scream.

THUD!

The massive axe, still clutched in its severed hand, fell right next to Tang Ahee's shoulder.

"Kiyaaaaak!"

Tang Ahee let out an even louder scream. Lee Sungmin, who had walked out of the darkness, frowned as he looked down at her. When her eyes met his, she shrieked again as if she were about to faint, and once more, she could not stop herself from wetting her pants.

Lee Sungmin was unaware that Tang Ahee had instigated the Holy Knight Commander, so he held no particular malice toward her. That was not to say he felt any obligation to save her. The reason he had intervened was simple. He had something he wanted to ask her. Lee Sungmin flicked a Finger Bullet to finish off the Dullahan completely before approaching.

[Where's the Crimson Archmage?]

The Thought Transmission echoed in her head, and Tang Ahee, who had been screaming for some time, belatedly came to her senses. She gasped for breath and looked up at Lee Sungmin. To her, the man who had appeared at her most desperate moment seemed like the only savior who could overcome this nightmare.

[Ye-yes?] she sent back, her thoughts stammering.

Lee Sungmin glanced at her soaked crotch and replied. [The Crimson Archmage. I mean Scarlet. Have you seen her?]

[Uh... I, I don't know.]

*I saved her for nothing,* Lee Sungmin thought as he started to walk past her.

At that, a terrified Tang Ahee reached out with both hands to grab his ankle. Lee Sungmin deftly lifted his foot, avoiding her grasp.

[P-please, help me.]

[I don't have a change of underwear for you.]

Tang Ahee's face flushed crimson at his response. She covered her wet crotch with her hands and hurriedly sent another Thought Transmission.

[Th-that's not what I meant! Can't you... can't you take me with you out of here?]

[I'm not leaving this forest, and why should I bother with you?]

[I-I'll give you anything you want!]

[And what could you possibly give me?] Lee Sungmin sent back as he strode forward.

Tang Ahee, who had been crawling after him, stumbled to her feet and quickly stuck close to his side.

[My... my body...?]

It was not something one would say while sober, nor even while dead drunk. As the words formed in her mind, Tang Ahee felt a deep sense of shame, and her shoulders trembled. Lee Sungmin, however, answered without the slightest hesitation.

[I don't need it.]

His sharp refusal left her speechless. Trembling all over, she sent another thought.

[Really...?]

[Yes. What kind of nonsense are you talking about in a situation like this?]

[Well... I mean... not here, obviously, but after we get out and I've washed up...]

[I don't need it.]

[H-how about the Tang Clan's martial arts? Antidotes... or hidden weapons...]

[I don't need it.]

Tang Ahee had nothing she could give him. Poison? He did not need it. Though he had not had a chance to test it, obtaining the Void Monarch's treasure had perfected his Myriad Poison Immunity. No poison from the Tang Clan could affect his body. The same went for hidden weapons. It was not that he had no interest in the art itself, but he was not confident he could handle them properly even if he received good ones.

[You have nothing I want. I only saved you to ask about Scarlet's whereabouts... and aren't I an enemy of the Murim Alliance?]

His subtle words were a threat, telling her to get lost. At his meaning, Tang Ahee's face turned deathly pale. She desperately racked her brain, trying to find a way to stick with him and save her own life.

[Ch-Chwi Geol is after the Crimson Archmage.]

[Of course he is. She's good bait to draw me out. Why are you stating the obvious?]

[I mean... uh... I thought you might not have known...] Tang Ahee added, stammering.

Just then, another demonic dog leaped out. Lee Sungmin flicked his fingertip, and its head shattered.

[I-if you take me hostage, you'll be able to negotiate with the Tang Clan.]

[And what would I negotiate with the Tang Clan for?]

[I-I can't give you anything, but my father would be different. So...]

[Take you hostage and demand something from the Tang Clan? I have no reason to do something so troublesome, and I don't want anything from you or your clan.]

[T-Tang Mugi.]

Tang Ahee said the name, trembling. That name. Lee Sungmin's steps came to a halt. He glanced back at her.

[Tang Mugi?]

He had heard that name before. Where... Lee Sungmin searched his memories. Right. Ruvus. The place where he had his reunion with Wiji Hoyeon. After leaving the city gates, he had been attacked by the Dark Supreme. He had nearly been killed, but his life was saved by the intervention of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. At that time, the Chairman had called out the Dark Supreme's name. 'Tang Mugi.' That was his name.

[He's my distant... ancestor.]

"......"

Four of the Six Honored Ones remained. He had a rough idea of the Blade Supreme's location. The Mercenary King was a famous figure. He also had some idea of where the Moon Queen was. He had just met the Spear King. The location of the Dark Supreme, however, was unconfirmed.

[Perhaps he... could give you what you want. He's a very mysterious person and has many possessions...]

[Are you in contact with him?]

The story about the Dark Supreme was enough to pique Lee Sungmin's interest. When he showed a flicker of interest, Tang Ahee nodded her head eagerly.

[Yes. I met him when I was young, and he found me quite cute. So every year on my birthday, he secretly visits the Tang Clan to give me a present.]

"......"

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. This was a chance to meet the Dark Supreme, whose whereabouts were unknown. For such a meeting, the man would likely move secretly and alone. If he met the Dark Supreme then, he could kill him without any martial arts theory.

[I understand.]

Lee Sungmin smiled faintly.

[Leaving this forest is a matter of martial arts theory, but I'll protect you so you don't die.]

Unaware that she was leading the Dark Supreme to his death, Tang Ahee let out a sigh of relief.
