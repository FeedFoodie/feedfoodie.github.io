Lee Sungmin tilted his head. "What do you mean?"

Kim Jonghyun offered only an unreadable smile in return. He did not answer, turning his gaze instead toward Bern.

"By the way, what brings you here?" he asked.

A dry laugh escaped Bern. "Didn't you say it yourself? That you wanted to meet the mercenary who subjugated the Ogre Chimera in person."

"Ah. Ahhh... I see." Kim Jonghyun nodded, a grin showing his teeth. He then turned his attention back to Lee Sungmin. "So you're the mercenary who subjugated the Ogre Chimera?"

Lee Sungmin remained silent.

"You're younger than I thought," Kim Jonghyun remarked. "I couldn't have imagined."

As he spoke, he moved his hand, swiping it through the empty air. The nearby floating head of the Ogre Chimera immediately flew toward him. Lee Sungmin frowned and took a few steps back. The Ogre's head, cleanly severed at the neck, drifted in the air before them. Its two eyes were gouged out and the threads that had sewn its mouth shut were all pulled free, leaving its jaw gaping wide.

"How very interesting," Kim Jonghyun hummed. He reached out with both hands and grabbed the floating head. "This Chimera has various kinds of high-level magic cast on it. It's already a corpse, so I can't analyze it completely. However, one thing is certain. The mage who created this Chimera is at a level where they could be called, at the very least, a great mage."

"That's hard to hear," Bern retorted, his brow furrowed at Kim Jonghyun's words. "Great mage isn't a term you can just throw around so easily."

"You can't say that. Even if our fields are different, shouldn't we acknowledge what deserves acknowledgment?"

"Ugh...!" A bitter groan escaped Bern. He clutched his collar tightly, heaved a deep sigh, and turned around. "There's no need for me to listen in. I'll be heading back to the Guild first."

Having spat out the words, Bern left the room. Suddenly alone with Kim Jonghyun, a flustered Lee Sungmin tried to stop him, but the door was already closed. From behind him, Kim Jonghyun chuckled.

"Don't you think jealousy is such an ugly thing?"

"...Jealousy?"

"Bern has been stagnant for a long time. It's... similar to martial arts. To advance to a higher level, you have to overcome a wall, and Bern has been unable to overcome that wall for a very, very long time." Muttering this, Kim Jonghyun suddenly pushed the Ogre head he was holding toward Lee Sungmin with both hands, as if he were throwing a basketball. Lee Sungmin dodged in alarm as the head came tumbling through the air. "If he could overcome that wall, he might reach the realm of an archmage. Since he can't, imagine how frustrated he must be with himself."

As he said that, Kim Jonghyun moved his hand again. The floating Ogre corpse and its entrails drifted toward him.

"Well, that aside. As I said before, this corpse is extremely interesting. If I had my way, I'd love to explain one by one how so many different kinds of magic were harmonized using an Ogre as a base, and what types of monsters were used... but that would be a difficult story for a non-mage like you to understand."

"Just get to the point."

Kim Jonghyun shrugged. "How was it?" he asked. "I heard the story from Rudd, who went on the Ogre subjugation with you. However, I wanted to hear it from you, the one who fought it directly. While you were fighting... how was it?"

"...I'm not sure what exactly you're asking."

"Didn't you feel anything strange while fighting it?" Kim Jonghyun questioned.

At that, Lee Sungmin recalled his fight with the Ogre. Something strange... He had not felt anything. It had been his first time fighting an Ogre, so it was impossible for him to notice any differences by comparing it to others of its kind.

"Nothing."

"What about its durability? Its regeneration?"

"An attack I threw with all my strength didn't even leave a scratch. The best I could do was to attack weak points like the joints. I pierced its eyeball, but it regenerated the wound in less than a minute."

"Were there any other differences?"

"It was... agile, I thought, and its attacks were heavy. I felt like I'd be beaten to a pulp if I took a direct hit."

Lee Sungmin explained what he had felt while fighting the Ogre Chimera. Kim Jonghyun listened quietly, nodding along.

"I understand."

"Is hearing all this helpful in any way?"

"It's better than not hearing it." Kim Jonghyun chuckled and grabbed the Ogre's intestines. "It was quite a helpful story. A corpse holds a lot of information, but in the case of a Chimera, there isn't much you can find out from the body. That's because the moment a Chimera dies, the magic cast on it starts to unravel."

The intricately tangled large intestine straightened out. Lee Sungmin's face twisted in disgust as he took a few more steps back.

"Ogres are monsters classified as apex predators," Kim Jonghyun continued. "However, that doesn't mean they're tough enough to take a direct hit from a peak master. They have regenerative abilities, but not to the extent of regenerating a shattered eyeball in under a minute. This was very helpful."

Lee Sungmin stared blankly at him as he spoke. After a moment, he opened his mouth. "Before. What were you about to say?"

"Ah. Ahh? Ah... It's nothing." Kim Jonghyun gave a strange smile. "You. You're twisted."

"...Excuse me?"

"Your causality is twisted. Are you aware?"

At Kim Jonghyun's words, Lee Sungmin's face stiffened. A being with twisted causality. The only others who had recognized it were Mushii and the Brokers of Erebrisa.

"...How did you know?"

"So you knew. Wow, that's incredible. If I were in your shoes, I would have gone mad with Despair." Kim Jonghyun let out a chuckle. He took a few steps back and waved his hand. Two chairs from the corner flew over, settling near him and Lee Sungmin. "Would you like something to drink? I have coffee, black tea, and green tea."

"No... I'm fine." He did not feel like drinking anything. *I would have gone mad with Despair.* That added comment bothered him. Meeting Lee Sungmin's sharp, glaring gaze, Kim Jonghyun let out a dry laugh.

"Who told you?" he asked, crossing his legs. Though he had offered, he did not drink anything either.

"...Mushii."

"Aha. You mean the God of Trials and Asceticism. Did you train on Mount Mushii?"

"...Yes."

"I see. For how many years?"

"Is that an important question?"

"No. It was just my personal curiosity. You don't have to answer. Right. So... you're saying Mushii told you. That your causality is twisted."

Lee Sungmin said nothing.

"Do you understand what that means?"

At Kim Jonghyun's question, Lee Sungmin took a moment to compose his breathing before answering. "He said that after I die, I will suffer for eternity."

"What an unkind explanation." Kim Jonghyun chuckled, suppressing his laughter. "Why is your causality twisted? Can you tell me?"

"And if I tell you?"

"I'll be able to answer what you're curious about."

"I can't trust you."

"I swear it. I will never divulge what I hear from you to another person. This is an oath I make to the great Mana as a mage, and it can never be broken. Because if I break it, I will die." Kim Jonghyun said it as if it were nothing. The moment he spoke, blue Mana enveloped his body. An oath to Mana. Lee Sungmin knew about it. Mages who walked the path of magic could never break such an oath. He glared at Kim Jonghyun for a moment before finally opening his mouth.

"...I died once. After dying, I came back."

"You mean you regressed?"

"That's right."

"Indeed. So that's why your causality is twisted. Yes, that would make sense. To be born, to live, and to die. That is the most fundamental causality and the first rule that constitutes this world. All beings are bound by that causality."

TAP.

Kim Jonghyun tapped the armrest of the chair with his hand.

"It's not just about life. Take the chair I'm sitting on right now. A carpenter made the chair. The chair was used as a chair, then it broke. That, too, is causality." The corners of his mouth twitched upwards. "What do you think needs to be done for a broken chair to return to its original state?"

"...It would have to be repaired."

"Yes. That's right, but is it truly the same chair? Reinforcing the broken parts, driving in new nails. In that very process, the chair becomes a different chair. If it's like that for a chair, what about a life? For a dead human to return to the past... is an act that defies the most fundamental law constituting this world. What is defied is causality, and in that process, it becomes twisted. That becomes the Karmic Retribution the soul accumulates."

Lee Sungmin listened to his words in silence. A hint of excitement now colored Kim Jonghyun's voice.

"All Karmic Retribution is settled after death. What Mushii said is true, but it was too unkind. It doesn't simply end with suffering. Ah, of course, I don't know what kind of suffering you will face either."

"That's no different from what Mushii said."

"Well, I haven't died either. A mortal can never know of the existence of an afterlife. Hmm... the study of magic is the pursuit of truth that exists at the edge of the irrational. If one were to glimpse that truth, one might come to know of forbidden knowledge not permitted to mortals, like the afterlife... but if I had glimpsed that, I would have become a sage who transcended even an archmage."

However. Kim Jonghyun let out a snort of laughter. He stared at Lee Sungmin's face for a moment before continuing.

"If all you heard from Mushii was that your causality is twisted, then I have something more to tell you."

"...What is it?"

"You're strange." Kim Jonghyun's eyes narrowed. "It's not just that your causality is twisted, but also..."

Also...

Also.

Also, also, also.

The end of his sentence trailed off, echoing. Lee Sungmin raised a hand to cover his left ear. *What is this?* The moment panic flared within him, Kim Jonghyun's mouth snapped shut. He blinked silently, then shrugged.

"It seems I can't."

"What?"

"I must not say it," Kim Jonghyun said with a bitter smile.

At his words, Lee Sungmin's lips parted in a daze. "What in the world does that mean?"

"If I say it, I will die." Kim Jonghyun stood up as he spoke. "Please understand."

"W-wait. What is that supposed to mean?"

"I'm not sure if I should even be saying this much," Kim Jonghyun muttered. "Your life may not be your own."

They were words he could not comprehend. Naturally, Lee Sungmin did not understand what he was saying. Just as he opened his mouth to ask another question, Kim Jonghyun raised a hand to stop him.

"Could you please visit me again next time?" It was a polite request. "I'm also confused, so I think I need some time to organize my thoughts."

"...I don't understand."

"Neither do I," Kim Jonghyun admitted with a bitter smile. "However, this is an undeniable fact. If I tell you this, I will die. I don't want to die. At least, not yet."

After saying that, he swallowed once.

"Please, visit me again next time."
