In a single, brutal instant, their number had been cut from twenty to thirteen.

The Hunting Team Leader could only stare, her face a stiff and hardened mask. She had been forced to admit her misjudgment. While she knew Lee Sungmin was a master who had reached a high realm, she had never imagined he could so effortlessly dismantle the combined assault of her Hunting Party. Every one of them, herself included, were masters who had attained the Supreme Peak. They never left this forest, but if they did, they wielded enough power to look down upon most major sects, and yet, against him, they were nothing.

Lee Sungmin's expression remained placid. He felt no killing intent because they were not opponents worthy of it. These people had come to kill him, and since he had no desire to die, since he could not afford to die, he would simply kill them instead. Such an action, driven by such a simple mindset, required no malice.

The distortion he had kept quietly suppressed while traveling with the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia was now revealing itself, unleashed by his solitude. Perhaps it had even grown stronger, fed by the inhumanity he had subdued within himself. Lee Sungmin was aware of this possibility, but he had no intention of hesitating. To kill the Fist Honored One, he had to become an enemy to the Elves of this forest. Having made that decision, there was no room for mercy. Even if he spared them, the Elves would never forgive him.

Thirteen remained. If he killed them all, would the Sword Saint appear? Or would more Elves come? Either way, it made no difference. He just had to kill every last one of them. Still, a small part of him hoped the continued slaughter might frighten them into offering up the Fist Honored One on their own, though he knew it was an unlikely outcome.

"Team Leader."

One of the hunters called out to her, his voice trembling.

The Hunting Team Leader's glare remained fixed on Lee Sungmin. She said nothing. Despite being masters of the Supreme Peak, her party had a critical weakness, a profound lack of experience. Every member, including herself, had been born in this forest and had never once set foot outside it. To them, the Fist Honored One was their only Master. It wasn't that they had never dueled, but their Master was far too strong to be a practical sparring partner, and the deep kinship among the Elves made them merciful in contests against each other.

Of course, this inexperience had never been a disadvantage when hunting the intruders who had attacked the forest before. The mercenaries hired by slave hunters were mostly mediocre, as were their mages. In the mercenary world, a master of the peak realm was considered S-Rank, while reaching the Supreme Peak earned the highest grade of SSS-Rank. The mercenaries they usually faced were merely First-Rate, not even qualifying for S-Rank. Against such low-quality opponents, experience was irrelevant.

However, that was not the case now. Even within the same Supreme Peak realm, there were significant differences in power, but vast experience could compensate for such a deficiency.

[What a waste of martial arts on these fools.]

Void Monarch sneered from within him.

Lee Sungmin agreed. The Hunting Party had reached a high realm, but their application of it was pathetic. They possessed fast movements, sharp senses, and powerful attacks, yet they were riddled with openings. The fight was easy. All he had to do was strike when he saw a weakness. They tried to create variations by mixing in feints, but from his perspective, their attempts were crude and predictable.

"Thirteen," Lee Sungmin muttered again, as if to ensure they heard. A shimmer of inner qi and demonic energy enveloped his body.

The wolves began to back away. The hunters stared at him, their faces pale with fear. They wanted to retreat, but they couldn't. They were the Hunting Party of this forest, and they were obligated to kill the man who had invaded their territory and slaughtered their kin.

Chains could not catch him. Crossbow bolts could not reach him. Their only option was to charge in with their bare hands. Every remaining hunter, the Team Leader included, rushed toward Lee Sungmin as one.

[Foolish bastards.]

Void Monarch laughed heartily. The timing was perfect. Fighting twelve Supreme Peak masters at once was not an experience one could easily come by, and this was the first time he had fought like this after properly harmonizing his Demonic Energy and inner qi. There were many things he wanted to test.

Lee Sungmin extended his foot.

He moved, unleashing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the sole martial art of Silent Flash Baek Sogo. It was an outstanding movement art with few equals, encompassing far more than mere footwork. Traceless Step was a teleportation technique of the highest level, creating afterimages to deceive an opponent's eyes and evade attacks.

His first step, One Step Dancing Shadow, filled the air with dozens of afterimages. His second, Two Step Calamity, simultaneously released a burst of enhanced qi that poured out in the direction of his movement. He could also use Two Step Gentle Ripple to disperse unavoidable attacks that invaded the body.

The true value of the art, however, began from the fifth form. Three Step Fatality. One, two, three. Just three steps, but when all three were taken, the technique became a fatal attack, just as its name implied. With the first step, he swelled his enhanced qi. With the second, he dominated the surrounding space. With the third, he detonated it.

It was unavoidable. As Lee Sungmin took his third step, two hunters were upon him, their fists extended. By the time their instincts screamed of the danger, it was already too late.

KWAAAAANG!

A storm of violet enhanced qi raged out from him. The bodies of the Elves caught in the blast disintegrated, scattering blood everywhere. He hadn't even swung his spear. The power of Three Step Fatality was even greater now, amplified by his Demonic Energy.

Eleven. Lee Sungmin extended his foot again, unleashing the sixth form of Dancing Shadow Soul Break, a technique he had never before used in reality.

Four Step Chaos.

The flow of the exploded enhanced qi twisted upon itself. By taking one more step from Three Step Fatality, he completed Four Step Chaos, creating a storm with his steps alone. Lee Sungmin became the master of that storm, wielding a tempest of pure enhanced qi. The earth was torn asunder and the atmosphere vibrated violently. Three more Elves caught in the vortex died instantly.

Only seven remained.

"Aaaargh!" a scream like a death cry rang out.

KRAAANG!

The wolves were terrified, but the tiger was not. The massive beast roared and leaped toward Lee Sungmin, only to be engulfed by the raging storm of Four Step Chaos. Naturally, the tiger died.

"To the village!" the Hunting Team Leader shouted, her cry directed at the survivors. He was unstoppable. Even if they sacrificed their lives, they would not take him with them. They would simply be annihilated. Understanding her meaning, the remaining hunters turned and fled. They couldn't call for the Fist Honored One, who was busy maintaining the curse, but they could ask the Sword Saint for help.

Lee Sungmin did not stop them. He figured that if they went to the village for help, either the Fist Honored One or the Sword Saint would finally come.

"You're not going?" he asked, looking at the Team Leader, who remained, glaring at him.

She didn't answer. She had never been very talkative. Instead, she looked down at the two daggers in her hands.

...Hoo.

With a deep breath, she infused her inner qi into the weapons. The blades shifted and reformed, the daggers becoming a pair of gauntlets that encased her hands. Lee Sungmin watched her calmly, noting that she didn't rush to attack. He knew she was strong, having already ridiculously surpassed the level of a sect leader from the Nine Sects One Gang.

*I used to think the Nine Sects One Gang was the best.*

Thinking about it now, those who boasted of their lineage, even those from the so-called prestigious clans, seemed utterly insignificant.

The Hunting Team Leader charged first. Instead of swinging his spear, Lee Sungmin lifted his foot. The instantly executed Two Step Calamity swept over her. She didn't evade. Instead, she thrust both hands forward in a strange and startling display. The wave of enhanced qi that was about to engulf her was 'caught' in her hands. Then, with a sharp movement, she tore it apart.

[Oh.]

Void Monarch sounded surprised. Lee Sungmin was also taken aback by the phenomenon, but he moved his spear without pause, aiming for the advancing Team Leader.

PWAK!

Deathly Flash, the fastest technique of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, shot toward her. She hurriedly moved her right hand, thrusting it into the path of the attack.

*I can pierce right through her hand,* Lee Sungmin thought, but that was not what happened.

JJEONG!

A completely different sound rang out. The impact pushed the Team Leader's body back, but the hand that had blocked the Deathly Flash was not pierced.

[Those are dragon scales,] Void Monarch said. [It reminds me of when I hunted dragons. They deflected most attacks with their scales. It was quite annoying.]

*Those are dragon scales?*

[Dragon scales are the hardest material in this world. Even you won't be able to pierce them easily.]

"Those gauntlets," Lee Sungmin said, looking at the Team Leader's face. "They look good. Where'd you get them?"

She didn't answer. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue. He also had an abundance of dragon scales, bones, and teeth, but with no way to process them, they were just sitting in his Subspace Pocket.

[Subdue her and ask. About where she got them.]

*That's the plan.*

The spear moved again. Dozens of phantoms descended upon the Team Leader. She had barely managed to block the first strike, but she would not be so lucky this time. The power infused into the spear he wielded now was on a different level. She relied on her instincts, defending as best she could, but her body quickly became covered in blood. The spear's thrusts were faster than her hands could move, preventing her from closing the distance. She needed to get closer to strike, but the relentlessly thrusting, swinging, and striking spear wouldn't let her.

Thanks to the gauntlets, she was defending against fatal wounds, but it wouldn't last. Like a drizzle soaking the body, wounds steadily accumulated on her. This was exactly as Lee Sungmin intended. His plan was to first exhaust her until she couldn't move, then calmly ask about the gauntlets.

*This won't be easy.*

He clicked his tongue softly. It wasn't that fighting her was difficult.

The spear's path twisted, its trajectory suddenly altering in a way she couldn't react to, but before his spear could pierce her leg, a formless slash collided with it.

"That child is the Fist Honored One's daughter," a low voice interjected.

Lee Sungmin glanced once at the Team Leader's face before turning toward the source of the voice. A man with short-cropped brown hair was staring at him.

"Elves have difficulty reproducing," the man continued. "The Fist Honored One probably... won't be able to have any more children. So I don't want to let her die."

"The Sword Saint?"

"......"

The Sword Saint glanced at the Team Leader. She clenched her fists in humiliation. She hadn't wanted to rely on a human to solve an Elf's problem, but her own abilities had proven insufficient.

"Return to the Fist Honored One," the Sword Saint told her.

She opened her mouth to protest, but before she could speak, a chilling killing intent washed over her. It was a clear sign that he would not listen to any objections. The Team Leader stumbled back, her face pale.

"You're in the way," the Sword Saint spat.

Lee Sungmin no longer paid her any attention either. The Sword Saint began to approach slowly.

"What's your name?"

"Lee Sungmin."

"Lee Sungmin... yes. Your title was the Phantom Spear, wasn't it? I've heard of you. You were the kid who stepped up to protect Wiji Hoyeon, right?"

"Are you stronger than the Dark Supreme?" Lee Sungmin asked abruptly.

The Sword Saint shrugged. "The Dark Supreme is an excellent martial artist. He's strong."

"Than you?"

"If we fought with the intent to kill, I'd probably win eight times out of ten."

"And against the Fist Honored One?"

"Nine times."

He had heard the story from Wiji Hoyeon. She had fought the Fist Honored One for days on end but couldn't win. Now, the man before him was confident of a nine-out-of-ten victory against that same person. In other words, the Sword Saint was stronger than Wiji Hoyeon had been back then.

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae," Lee Sungmin said, fiddling with his spear. "Do you remember him?"

"I remember. He wasn't a martial artist with skills you could just forget."

"How do you remember him?"

"His skills weren't forgettable, but they weren't impressive enough to leave a deep impression either." As he answered, the Sword Saint drew the sword from his waist. "It was probably ten years ago. I didn't kill him because I thought he had potential, but even after ten years, he failed to reach the Transcendent Realm. Come to think of it, you fought alongside him when you fought the Dark Supreme, didn't you? Where is he now?"

"He's dead," Lee Sungmin replied.

Hearing that, and hearing the Sword Saint's dismissive words, a certain emotion began to boil inside his chest.

"Is that so?" the Sword Saint muttered, glancing down at his sword. "He never reached the Transcendent Realm in the end. If I'd known that, I would have killed him ten years ago."

The boiling emotion intensified.

"But more than that... you're surprising. I heard about you from the Dark Supreme, but it seems you'll be able to reach the Transcendent Realm soon. Normally, I would fight you, but I wouldn't kill you. It's a waste to kill someone with that kind of potential, and I would have you join Heaven Beyond Heaven. However, it doesn't seem like you'll be joining us."

"You're right."

"Besides, the power you use is suspicious. You use the power of a Demonic Spirit, don't you? It seems unlikely the realm you've reached is purely through your own efforts. I don't think I have any reason to let you live."

"Just like the Dark Supreme," Lee Sungmin spat in a low voice. "You Six Honored Ones are so... arrogant."

"We simply have every right to be," the Sword Saint said, raising his sword. "I know you're exceptional, but you haven't reached my level yet. If you don't believe me, I'll prove it to you. The possibility the Spirit Medium spoke of, that the Fist Honored One might die, must be referring to you. Since you have no intention of joining Heaven Beyond Heaven and you even use the power of a Demonic Spirit, I will show you no mercy."

"Do as you please." He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

"Let me ask one last thing." The enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art coiled around his spear. "Do you remember what the Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae's martial art was?"

"No," the Sword Saint answered without a moment's thought. "Didn't I tell you? It wasn't impressive enough to leave a deep impress..."

"The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art." The enhanced qi coiling around the spear gathered at its tip. "It's the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

A boiling emotion.

A sharp killing intent arose.
