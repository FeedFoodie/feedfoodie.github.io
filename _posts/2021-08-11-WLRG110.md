Lee Sungmin opened his eyes to a languid sensation, a familiar feeling that told him exactly what was happening.

*A dream?*

This was not the first time he had experienced a lucid dream. He had lived through countless of them within the nightmares of Mount Mushii's trial, and the memory alone made him shudder. People often imagined lucid dreams as fantasies bent to one's will, but his had never been so convenient.

He held no such expectations this time either as he sat up and surveyed his surroundings.

"You're awake?"

A voice spoke. Lee Sungmin turned toward the sound, unsurprised. This felt different from a nightmare, and different from an ordinary dream too. The sensation was closer to the Mental World he had entered with Denir's power.

The owner of the voice was the Void Monarch.

His body was a wavering mass of Demonic Energy. Where a face should have been, there was only a featureless void, a trait shared by his entire form.

"What's with that form?"

"You want to see my real form?" the Void Monarch asked with a chuckle.

Lee Sungmin wondered why the being was in his dream at all, but he nodded. At his assent, the Demonic Energy that composed the Void Monarch's body swelled, then solidified into bone and flesh. He reformed into a giant with broad shoulders, a full head taller than Lee Sungmin, and hands large enough to crush a man's skull with ease.

"You look more human than I expected," Lee Sungmin commented, his voice indifferent. He had pictured a hideous monster befitting a great yokai, complete with horns, fangs, and claws. The Void Monarch's true form, however, was not so different from a human's, aside from his towering height and powerful build.

"That's because Yokai resemble humans. If you had to classify them, Yokai are mutants, not monsters."

"Like an Orc?"

"Don't compare me to such inferior beings," the Void Monarch grumbled, swinging his arms. "It's not real, but it feels good to have a body after so long."

"Why are you here?"

"I was just testing something. When I lent you my power, we became spiritually connected, so I tried to see if I could interfere with your dreams while you slept. Turns out I can. I couldn't control your body, though."

"Sounds like you tried."

"It was a good opportunity, but I failed. The protection bestowed upon you is something even my power can't break through. In the Sleeping Forest, I couldn't get past your divine power, but this time I was pushed back by a different force entirely. It seems no ordinary being can take control of your mind."

That reminded Lee Sungmin of how Preskan's mental magic had failed to penetrate his defenses.

"So, are you done? If you are, I'd like you to leave. I'm tired and I want to sleep."

"I have a few things I want to ask." The Void Monarch beckoned to him. "Come here."

Lee Sungmin frowned but approached the giant. "What is it?"

"I just told you. I have questions."

"Then ask them."

"You. What kind of person are you?" the Void Monarch asked immediately. "When I lent you my Demonic Energy, our spiritual connection was strong. I could feel a part of your emotions, the ones you felt fighting that half-baked monster and later when you faced that inhuman wench."

"You read my mind as you please."

"I just felt it. It's not something I can control."

"What exactly do you want to hear?"

"What you've done with your life, your relationship with that wench, and what you want to do in the future."

"You want to hear quite a lot. Why should I tell you any of that?"

"We'll be spending a long time together from now on, so there's no harm in getting to know each other, is there? Besides, you owe me."

"A debt?"

"If I hadn't helped you, you would have died fighting that half-wit."

He knew it was true. If the Void Monarch had not supplemented his Demonic Energy, Lee Sungmin would never have handled Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger. With his current body, the limit of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was the fourth form, Nine Dragons Slaughter, and for the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, it was the Third Form, Two Step Calamity. He knew the higher-level martial arts and had even used them in the Mental World, but he could not execute them perfectly with his physical body.

It was an absurd reality. Lee Sungmin's body was that of a complete Supreme Peak martial artist who had undergone Body Reformation. No matter how outstanding the techniques were, there was no reason a body at this level should be unable to perform them. His inner arts were not lacking either, in fact, they were quite abundant compared to other masters.

The problem was not that the martial arts themselves were too high-level, but that the level of the arts he 'remembered' was too high. The level his mind had experienced was so advanced that his physical body simply could not keep up. With the aid of Demonic Energy, he had managed to execute the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Void Path, but if he tried to perform it now, the technique would not even manifest properly.

"...Didn't you say you helped me because you took a liking to me?"

"That's also true. Do you have no intention of telling me willingly?"

"What's the point?"

"Are you embarrassed?"

"A little, yes."

"You brat. Playing hard to get. You know you have a problem, don't you?"

"What's that supposed to mean?"

"You're a selfish brat who only wants to hear what you're curious about. If you want to know things, then tell this Elder what he's curious about," the Void Monarch threatened.

Lee Sungmin looked at him with a reluctant expression. It was not a difficult thing to tell him, he just did not feel like it. Still, the Void Monarch's mention of a 'problem' weighed on his mind, and he could not ignore it. With a short sigh, Lee Sungmin opened his mouth.

Since the Void Monarch already knew he had died and returned, there was nothing left to hide. Lee Sungmin told him many stories. He spoke of meeting Wiji Hoyeon in Zenavis, of how their lives became entangled, and the promise they made. He recounted meeting Baek Sogo on Mount Mushii and descending the mountain after his training. He told him of the events in Behenger, of Preskan, the Black Heart, and Aine. He described his training in Shaolin and at Mount Hua, all the way to his encounter with Denir.

The story was long, so the Void Monarch sat on the ground to listen, his arms crossed and his expression bored. As soon as Lee Sungmin finished, he spoke.

"Why don't you use magic?"

"...Huh?" Lee Sungmin's eyes widened at the sudden question. "Uh..." he trailed off, pulling up his status window.

"Ah."

He had forgotten. On Mount Mushii, Scarlet had taught him several spells. Fatigue Recovery, Brainwashing, Strength, and Haste. In fact, those four spells were the only reason he had been able to endure his time there.

"I can feel the presence of magic from you, though I don't know exactly what kind you learned."

"...A few support spells."

"The buff type?"

"There's that, but..."

"You fucking idiot," the Void Monarch cursed scathingly. "You learned all that, so why don't you use it? Did you catch some disease that'll kill you if you use martial arts and magic together?"

Lee Sungmin had nothing to say. He simply shut his mouth. When was the last time he had used magic? He had focused so solely on martial arts during his training in the Mental World that, carrying those memories over, he had completely forgotten magic even existed.

"If you try to use offensive magic and martial arts together, you might become an idiot who can't use either properly, but support magic can definitely be used alongside martial arts. Have you even tried?"

"A long time ago..."

"You idiot," the Void Monarch sneered, then shot to his feet. "Well, whatever about that. I've taken quite a liking to you."

"...Why?"

"Because I like simple-minded fools." The Void Monarch bared his teeth in a grin. "Let's be frank. Even if another year passes, it's impossible for you to stand on the same level as that wench, Wiji Hoyeon, or the Little Heavenly Demon, whatever you call her."

"I know."

"The trials of a god are tricky. You trained for twenty-one hundred years in a mental world? Heh heh! No wonder you were using martial arts beyond your natural talent. You compensated for your lack of it with an enormous amount of time. It's laughable that after all that crap, you're only at this level, though."

"Am I weak enough to be looked down on?"

"I'll admit your strength is beyond that of a human," the Void Monarch declared, "but it's far from enough to compare with those who have transcended humanity. That wench is in the process of transcending. If you truly want to be her equal, you'll have to do the same. Even bringing over your full martial prowess from the Mental World wouldn't be enough."

"...There's nothing I can do about that."

"There are many ways to stop being human." The Void Monarch narrowed his eyes, his gaze inscrutable. Lee Sungmin tilted his head, confused.

"The easiest way is to become a vampire. Or a Lycanthrope. They are the most common types who were once human but are no longer. The method isn't difficult either."

"I don't want to do that." The word vampire brought the Sword Phantom to mind, which was not a pleasant memory.

"Is it stubbornness, wanting to remain human?"

"It's not that. I just don't feel the need to."

"Keke! I don't recommend it either. The hierarchy between vampires or Lycanthropes is absolute. If you become a vampire, you can never defy the one who turned you. Plus, you'll gain annoying restrictions."

"What are you trying to say?"

"There's also the option of becoming a Yokai." The Void Monarch's eyes gleamed. "A Yokai is a being that belongs to both monsters and mutants. Some are humans who transformed, some are just born that way, and some are born from two Yokai getting it on."

"...Are you telling me to become a Yokai?"

"It's one of the methods. You've experienced Demonic Energy, haven't you? Your heart isn't human in the first place, but a monster's. If you mutate with that as your core, using my Demonic Energy, you could become a very interesting being," the Void Monarch said with conviction.

Lee Sungmin was not keen on the idea. He had lived his entire life as a human, so he could not easily accept the suggestion to suddenly become something else. The Void Monarch saw his expression and laughed heartily.

"Well, it's just another method. I'm telling you because I like you, and you should know this isn't a bad opportunity. You'd be receiving this Elder's grace, after all."

Leaving those words behind, the Void Monarch dissipated into mist. Lee Sungmin felt the dream closing around him and let out a deep sigh.

"He just barges into someone else's dream, says what he wants, and disappears," he grumbled as his consciousness faded away.

{sep}

He opened his eyes to the chirping of birds. Lee Sungmin pushed the blanket away and heard the steady breathing nearby. Looking down, he saw Rubia curled up asleep on the floor beside the bed.

"I'd forgotten."

Lee Sungmin muttered to himself as he sat up. He lifted Rubia's small body and placed her on the bed. She must have been a deep sleeper, for she only let out soft breaths and did not open her eyes. The spear leaning nearby, which housed the Void Monarch, hummed.

[What are you going to do today?]

*My body hasn't fully recovered yet.*

Despite taking several potions, his body still had many problems. Lee Sungmin ignored the Void Monarch and sat on the floor. He then took out the half of the Great Cycle Pill he had saved and a Magic Stone from his Subspace Pocket.

The Void Monarch had said there would be no major problems if he consumed it. Lee Sungmin first placed the half-pill in his mouth. He felt the inner arts accumulate directly in his qi center, bypassing the need for circulation. His dantian was significantly larger than it had been a few days ago, a natural result of consuming Shaolin's greatest miracle herb. After checking the size of his dantian, he absorbed the Magic Stone as well.

*I see why people go crazy over Dungeons.*

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue as he confirmed the surge in his internal energy. While he himself was not particularly bound by the benefits of a Magic Stone, gaining an immediate boost in power without any refinement was an incredible thing. The amount of inner arts enhanced by the stone was almost comparable to that of the Great Cycle Pill. It was no wonder so many people wandered in search of Dungeons when they could obtain not only such efficient Magic Stones but also Orichalcum of a purity that would make a Meister blacksmith's eyes pop.

*Come to think of it, I also have the Eighteen Dragon Subduing Palms.*

He took the manual out of his Subspace Pocket. What should he do with this? He considered selling it through Erebrisa, but the precedent with the Hundred Steps Divine Fist suggested he would not get a very good price.

*Should I take it to the Beggars' Gang?*

Not right away, of course. First, he had to receive the information he requested from Erebrisa about Baek Sogo and the Void Monarch. After that, he had to pursue Preskan. *And I also have to go to the North.*

He had business in the North, which the Spirit dwelling in Grandmaster Bulyeong had mentioned. He had to be there by winter, so while there was still some time, he did not have the leisure to stop by the Beggars' Gang.

Lee Sungmin began his qi circulation, operating the Violet Dawn Divine Art. In the process, he became aware of a strange power at the bottom of his dantian. It was a power that remained separate, refusing to mix with his inner arts. It was Demonic Energy.

*What's this now?*

He stopped his circulation and spoke to the Void Monarch, who replied with a bored tone.

[It's a remnant of me. A trace of our spiritual connection.]

*It's bothering me. Can't you get rid of it?*

[Leave it be.]

*Won't this Demonic Energy have a negative effect on me?*

[You're worried about such trivial things when you have a monster's heart in your body. There's no problem, so just leave it.]

At the Void Monarch's reply, Lee Sungmin smacked his lips and nodded. Immersing himself in the Violet Dawn Divine Art, he entered a state of selflessness. With every breath, a vast tide of inner arts enveloped his body, and a violet energy drifted around him like a fine mist.

When he opened his eyes, it was already noon.
