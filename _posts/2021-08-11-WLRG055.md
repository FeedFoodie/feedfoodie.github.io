"It's been a while."

As Lee Sungmin opened the door and stepped inside, Kim Jonghyun offered him a faint smile from within the workshop, a space buried deep in the basement of the Mages Guild.

The room itself had changed little in the few months since his last visit, but the airborne corpses were different. The Ogre Chimera must have been fully researched, replaced now by a menagerie of other monsters floating in the air. The sight was unpleasant, and the stench was worse.

Kim Jonghyun pulled up a chair and sat. "You've been scarce for a few months. What brings you here?"

The question hung in the air. Lee Sungmin had not visited since their first meeting, partly because he felt no particular fondness for the man, and partly because it bothered him that Kim Jonghyun was hiding something crucial, even if it was due to a forced geas against his will.

"I have something to ask." The words came out blunt, but Lee Sungmin felt a pressing urgency that overrode politeness.

"What is it? Ah, but let me tell you in advance, I still can't answer that question from last time. Like I said, it's beyond my control, and I'd rather not die."

"That's not what I came to ask."

"Then what is it? Ah. Did you come to interrogate me? Wondering if I've told anyone your causality is twisted, that you're a being who once died and came back?"

"That's not it either. You swore an oath on Mana, didn't you?"

"That's right," Kim Jonghyun replied with a chuckle. "It's an absolute oath. A mage can never break it."

Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the man's face from across the short distance. The reason for his dislike was simple. He had met with Scarlet quite often since their encounter three months ago, and she had painted a detailed picture of the kind of person Kim Jonghyun was.

Kim Jonghyun was a Black Mage, though no one knew when he had been summoned to Eria as an Otherworlder. The existence known as Kim Jonghyun was treated as extremely heretical and special even within Behenger's Mages Guild. He had lived in Behenger for nearly a decade without aging a day, and Scarlet did not even know his true age. On top of that, his eccentricities were famous. Heavily ostracized as a Black Mage, he paid it no mind, instead dedicating himself to some unknown research while lining his room with monster corpses procured from who knows where.

He was a suspicious man from every angle, and Lee Sungmin had no desire to get close to him.

"I want to know about Black Mages," he said.

Nevertheless, he had come to Kim Jonghyun for this very reason, because only he could provide the answers he sought. Neither Bern nor Scarlet were Black Mages. Asking them would yield nothing of substance.

"Did you apply for the Black Mage's Dungeon subjugation?" Kim Jonghyun asked with a smile.

Seeing the surprise on Lee Sungmin's face, he continued. "Well, it's not much of a deduction, is it? It was only natural for the Behenger Mercenary Guild to track the Black Mage through the Ogre Chimera. If you, a mercenary, are asking about Black Mages, it must mean they found his dungeon."

"...That's right. A Black Mage's Dungeon was discovered, and I applied for the subjugation."

"I wouldn't really recommend it," Kim Jonghyun muttered. He flicked a finger, and a teacup from the corner of the room floated toward them. When he glanced at Lee Sungmin, Lee Sungmin shook his head. In the end, only one teacup approached Kim Jonghyun.

"I've already finished my investigation of the Ogre Chimera's corpse. It was an exceptionally well-made Chimera, with no rejection responses between the different organs and cells. Capturing an Ogre alive and tuning it like that requires tremendous magical skill in the first place. I said this last time, but the Black Mage who created it is incredibly skilled. He's probably reached a level sufficient to be called an archmage."

If Bern had been there, he would have shouted at him to stop talking nonsense. Lee Sungmin, however, listened to Kim Jonghyun's words without refuting them.

"...Is that why you don't recommend it?"

"No. I didn't say that because of the Black Mage's skill. I said it because the opponent is a 'Black Mage'."

"What do you mean by that?"

"A Black Mage who has approached or surpassed the level of an archmage will see right through you, just like I did," Kim Jonghyun muttered. "Dark Magic... it's on a completely different track from regular magic. You could say its very foundation is different. Normal magic manifests phenomena through formulas, using the Mana in the atmosphere as fuel. Think of it like this. A car. Mana is the fuel, and the formula is the engine. You use your 'will' to press the accelerator and make it go. Don't tell me there were no cars in the Korea you lived in."

"There were. I understand what you're saying."

"But Dark Magic is different. Of course, it uses Mana too, but normal magic requires three things. Mana, formula, and will. Dark Magic demands one more. The soul."

"The soul?"

"It's similar to Spirit Magic. A Spirit Magic user makes a contract with an Elemental to command it, promising something in the process. A Black Mage makes a contract with a Demon God, using their own soul as the price."

"...Demon God..."

"Of course, it's not a single entity. Strictly speaking, it's a contract with a Demon Lord subordinate to a Demon God. Well, it's not like you're being initiated, so there's no need to explain it from the beginning. To get to the point, it's like this. A Black Mage uses Dark Magic by contracting their soul to a Demon God or a Demon Lord. Because of the nature of this magic, all Black Mages possess 'eyes' that can see things that don't exist and things that are unseen. Just like me."

Kim Jonghyun raised a hand and pointed to his own eye.

"Souls are a very attractive material for a Black Mage. Upon making the contract, they gain eyes that can see souls, and they collect valuable ones to offer to their contracted Demon Lord, or... well. They use them in various other ways."

Lee Sungmin pressed his lips together tightly. Through Kim Jonghyun's story, he was beginning to understand the warning.

"Of course, not every Black Mage will be able to see through you, but if that Black Mage is still in the dungeon, and if he sees you... huhu! I don't know him, but a Black Mage of that caliber would recognize your value, and something very interesting will happen. Yes, it will happen for sure. That Black Mage will try to take your soul by any means necessary. I imagine you wouldn't find that very pleasant."

"...What about you?"

"Me? You mean me?" At Lee Sungmin's question, Kim Jonghyun burst out laughing as if something was incredibly funny. "I have no desire for you. Ah, that's because I'm a little different from a typical Black Mage. The Dark Magic I learned is... extremely heterogeneous compared to all the other Black Mages in Eria. It's a power I gained through that ridiculous Monster Outbreak in the country I came from."

"What's different about it?"

"I didn't contract with a Demon Lord or a Demon God. To be precise, the Demon Lord I contracted with died. It's a very rare case. The Demon Lord was named Caladra of Lagres, and he suddenly vanished. The contract was forcibly terminated, and a portion of the dark magic power that Caladra possessed was transferred to me."

He said this with a nonchalant expression, as if discussing the weather, not the death of a Demon Lord and the inheritance of its power. In other words, Kim Jonghyun had become free from the contract that bound all Black Mages, and in the process, had become the owner of the immense dark magic his former master had possessed.

"A part of my body changed in the process. I stopped aging, and my eyes became able to see not just souls, but things deeper and more secret. That's how I was able to read your existence. It's also why I don't covet your soul, but other Black Mages are different. They... they would be greedy."

Lee Sungmin fell silent. Just hearing those words made his visit worthwhile. Black Mages with eyes that see souls could perceive that he was a being with twisted causality. That was why they would covet him.

At that point, he realized a contradiction. The Brokers of Mushii and Erebrisa had also known of his nature and had shown a little greed for his soul, yet Kim Jonghyun claimed he felt none.

"Those eyes of yours. Are they better performing than the eyes of the god, Mushii?"

"Performing! What an interesting word. Well... it would be difficult to compare, wouldn't it? As a human, I have no way of knowing what Mushii sees or knows, but this is an undeniable fact. I have no greed for your soul. Though I am interested."

Kim Jonghyun brought the teacup to his lips as he spoke.

"For a regressor like you to go to a Black Mage's Dungeon, there must be a good reason, but it would be better not to go. It will be dangerous."

"...What if the Black Mage isn't in the Dungeon?"

"Isn't the probability of that fifty-fifty? Well. A Black Mage who thinks rationally would choose to flee rather than confront the Mercenary Guild that came to subjugate him."

"Can you help me?"

Kim Jonghyun let out a laugh at the question.

"That would be difficult. I'm not the type to go out of my way to make enemies," he said, before adding, "and besides... I don't want to help hunt a comrade walking the same path, though I have no intention of stopping you."

After finishing his conversation, Lee Sungmin left the Mages Guild. The thought of asking Scarlet for help crossed his mind for a moment, but he shook his head. Although he had met her several times and built a friendly relationship, he did not feel they were close enough for such a request. Besides, he did not want to ask for help on a matter like this from someone who had nothing to do with it.

*What should I do?*

It was just as Kim Jonghyun had said. The chances were fifty-fifty. It was about three months early, but was the Black Mage still in the dungeon or not? If the dungeon had been discovered three months later as scheduled, Lee Sungmin would not be having this dilemma. It would have been a certainty that the Black Mage was gone.

However, now was different. Three months. How would this variable play out? Should he take the risk?

His deliberation did not take long. The wall he was facing was too high and too solid. He needed the help of the Magic Armor. He was not certain it would truly be helpful, but he had to grasp at straws.

The one silver lining was that he would not be undertaking this dungeon subjugation alone. The Guildmaster, Bern, had said he would participate, and other S-Rank Mercenaries led by Zeon would also take part. Mercenaries from other groups, not just the Corona Mercenary Group, would be participating as well.

In the end, Lee Sungmin made his choice. He would participate in the dungeon subjugation.
