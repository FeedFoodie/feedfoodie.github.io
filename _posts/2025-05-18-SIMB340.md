*Is this... an illusion created by sorcery?*

Baek Suryong looked around.

It was the old bookstore he had visited just a few days ago.

The arrangement of the shelves, bookcases, and the books displayed, however, was entirely different. The items also didn't look as old as the ones he had seen then.

*This is the old bookstore from long ago. From the time Master Eun and Moon Yul used to meet.*

Baek Suryong assessed the situation without panicking.

He might have been flustered if it were his first time experiencing something like this, but discovering his own diary and experiencing his past life in a dream before helped now.

*It's a similar situation to back then.*

The difference was that the current Baek Suryong was not the protagonist of this dream but an intangible observer.

He couldn't speak, nor could he touch or feel anything. He could only watch the scenes unfold.

The scenes of Eun Yerin and Moon Yul.

*Creak...*

The door of the old bookstore opened, and a woman entered.

It was Eun Yerin, her face completely hidden by a black hat and veil. Only her clothes indicated she was a woman.

"Welcome."

Moon Yul, who was organizing books in the store, greeted her with a smile. Their first meeting. A cold voice flowed from Eun Yerin's lips.

"Reading in such a dark place will ruin your eyes."

It was Moon Yul, holding a small oil lamp.

At his absurd kindness, Eun Yerin looked at Moon Yul with suspicious eyes.

Moon Yul, however, was someone who hadn't learned martial arts. He lacked the skill to see through her veil.

"I'm a martial artist, so it's fine."

"Still, having a light would make reading more comfortable. I'll leave it here."

"......"

"...Thank you."

"Take your time reading."

Sometimes, emotions sprout from a single, insignificant action or word.

Though it was a trivial, short conversation, Eun Yerin's gaze lingered for a long time on the frail-looking scholar of the bookstore.

It was quite amusing how he brought her a lamp, only to bury his own nose back in a book.

*I can feel Master Eun's emotions.*

Baek Suryong watched them with the heart of an audience member. A smile had unknowingly formed on his lips.

"...I'll come again next time."

"Take care."

Leaving the bookstore, Eun Yerin walked slowly along the path.

Looking up, she saw a crescent moon hanging in the sky.

As if a single ticklish breeze mixed into the night wind that had only felt cold, she chuckled softly and muttered, "Strange person."

Moon Yul had asked him to let him meet the Eun Yerin within Baek Suryong's memories.

He didn't know the exact sorcery, but Moon Yul had borrowed Baek Suryong's memories to manifest Eun Yerin's image here.

*This is Moon Yul's dream. And it's my dream too.*

The last dream Moon Yul would ever have.

He projected his desire into the dream, hoping that Master Eun and her lover could attain the happiness they sought, at least here.
