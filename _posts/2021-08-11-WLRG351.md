The Yuzukia Mountains were a place intertwined with all manner of legends. To reach them, one had to travel north from the Yokai-haunted lands of the South, pass through the ancient forest the Elves called home, and cross several more jungles. Only then would a traveler face the Yuzukia range, a formidable barrier of peaks so high they seemed to have no end, their landscapes stern and rugged.

*The Yuzukia Mountains, along with the Hyujael range, are known as the most sacred and mystical mountains in the South. Though, I've never been here myself.*

An ascent from the bottom would have taken a very long time, but Yana spared them such hardship with her ability to leap freely through the sky. She launched herself higher than the clouds in a single bound, a golden streak running toward the tallest of the towering peaks. It was the first time any of them had ever been so high. Lee Sungmin, Baek Sogo, and Scarlet peeked their heads over the cozy fur that enveloped them and gazed down at the world below. Hazy clouds swirled far beneath them, a sight that made Scarlet click her tongue.

"We'd die if we fell from here, wouldn't we?"

"Die? No," Lee Sungmin added playfully. "But it would be incredibly painful."

Of course, there was no real chance of them falling. He and Baek Sogo had long since surpassed simple movement arts like the Airwalking Technique, and Scarlet could easily use magic to halt any descent.

Upon reaching the summit, Yana gently let the party down from her back. She returned to her human form, placing a hand on the small of her back and arching it with a groan as if it were stiff. Feeling a pang of guilt, Lee Sungmin bowed his head to her.

"Thank you."

"It's alright," Yana replied, her eyes scanning their surroundings.

A thick fog shrouded the summit, so dense it was difficult to see more than a few feet ahead, but such an inconvenience was trivial for their group. Not far away, they heard the sound of flowing water that grew into the roar of a waterfall. Yana raised a finger and pointed.

"It seems to be over there."

The cold moisture in the air soaked their clothes. With a light flick of her fingers, Scarlet manifested her magic. In an instant, their wet clothes were dry and shielded from the dampness.

Before long, the group arrived at the edge of a wide lake, its far side spilling over into the mouth of the waterfall. Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky. The moon had not yet risen. Legend claimed the Moon Palace appeared from beyond the clouds only when the full moon was at its highest point, but he had no intention of blindly trusting in folklore.

"Looks like it's my time to shine," Scarlet announced, stepping forward boastfully. She clearly did not believe in the legend either.

She checked the gloves she already wore and raised both hands. As she tapped her fingertips and swept them through the air, a multitude of golden, glowing runic characters materialized around her. Humming a soft tune, Scarlet skillfully combined the runes, scribbling new characters with her fingers.

"It's worthy of being called a sacred mountain," she remarked. "The amount of magic power here is incredible. I'd say it's comparable to the Fairy Forest."

"Is it that much?"

"Yep. You might not be able to feel it, but the presence of Elementals is extremely strong. That's probably why this mountain is overflowing with magic power."

Lee Sungmin nodded, listening to her explanation. His own research on the Yuzukia Mountains had mentioned stories about Elementals. This place was considered a holy land for Elementalists, who came here to borrow the power of the land when contracting with high-ranking spirits. It was one of several places in the world where the power of Elementals could be felt so strongly.

"The Moon Palace appearing from the moon is nonsense," Scarlet muttered, her hand movements growing faster as she tapped at the empty air. "From what I can see, it looks like someone was playing tricks with space… hmm, hmm… Just as I thought. There's an artificial space between spaces. They mixed in some shamanistic barrier formulas, but fortunately, I think I can decipher it."

The magic Scarlet used was not pure. It originated from the Yin-Yang Path and Qimen Dunjia, a system she had developed by mixing magic with a foundation in shamanism. Dozens of combined sentences now floated around her.

Before long, she grinned. Her right hand pointed toward the middle of the lake.

VWIIING!

The runic characters circling her body began to glow all at once.

RUUUMBLE...!

Space itself started to vibrate. A small ripple formed on the surface of the lake, growing larger and larger until the entire body of water was sloshing violently. As the vibrations intensified, cracks appeared in the air above the lake. Watching this, Scarlet, who had been laughing, clenched her outstretched right hand into a fist.

CRACKLE!

The cracks shifted violently and then collapsed inward. As the space imploded, a hidden dimension revealed itself. It was an oriental-style mansion built of pure white wood, which settled down upon the churning surface of the lake. They worried it might sink, but the Moon Palace floated buoyantly on the water.

"Let's go," Scarlet said coolly.

Lee Sungmin looked at her in admiration. She had been so confident before they came, and true to her word, she had located the Moon Palace almost as soon as they arrived.

"That was incredible."

"You might be much better at fighting than me, but I'm good at things other than fighting too," Scarlet puffed out her chest and boasted. If they had not brought her, they would have wandered the mountains for days, likely returning to the Fairy Forest without ever finding their prize.

"So, is that it? Don't you have any more compliments for me?"

"As expected, Scarlet is the mage of mages."

"How obvious," she grumbled, though she seemed to be in a good mood.

Wary of any unforeseen dangers, Lee Sungmin took the lead. He walked across the surface of the lake, approaching the Moon Palace. Blade Supreme had said the Moon Queen was not dead, but Lee Sungmin found that hard to believe, since he had seen her death with his own eyes. Even if Blade Supreme had sworn an oath to only speak the truth, he had not met her directly. He had only heard she was alive, so Lee Sungmin thought there could be some misunderstanding.

Even if the Moon Queen was dead, it seemed unlikely she would have lived in such a large mansion all by herself. There must have been disciples, or others.

"...What is this?"

A terrible stench assaulted the group the moment they opened the mansion's door. Lee Sungmin's nose twitched, and he wrinkled the corners of his eyes. He knew this smell well. It was the smell of corpses, bodies that had been left to rot and fester without any care.

The group hurried toward the source of the stench, which led them to a staircase descending into a basement. The foul air seeped from the gap of a half-open iron door at the bottom of the stairs. After they pushed the door open, Scarlet used magic to light up the darkness, though even before the light flared, everyone knew what had happened inside.

They examined the rotting corpses. They were less than a week old.

*[Did they kill each other?]*

*Looks like it.*

The wounds left on the bodies were varied, but all of them were traces of martial arts. Lee Sungmin looked back outside the door, at the stairs leading up. Dried bloodstains remained in the shape of footprints. Someone had made it out of this pile of corpses alive.

"It's one person," Yana muttered.

"Who could it be?"

"Before that, we should find out who these people are."

They could find out quickly enough if they could ask the corpses, but unfortunately, Scarlet had not mastered necromancy. In that case, they just needed the help of another necromancer. Lee Sungmin summoned Nebul.

"A Black Mage, you mean?"

"Yes. Preferably one who isn't under the protection of the Blood Alliance."

"How about a Lich?"

"No."

After Kim Jonghyun, Black Mages had been ostracized in Eria. Those who became targets of hunts often entrusted themselves to the Blood Alliance, whose Chairman, the Blood Demon, was one of Jeniela's second-generation vampires. Contacting a Black Mage from the Blood Alliance carried the risk of information leaking to Jeniela. The information here probably was not anything major, but there was no harm in being cautious.

"Hmm… Not affiliated with the Blood Alliance, and not a Lich," Nebul mused. "That's not too difficult a request."

"Is that so?"

"Yes. After Kim Jonghyun's great massacre, the Black Magic Tower was dismantled from the Mages Guild. While most Black Mages flowed into the Blood Alliance, not all of them fell so low. Black Mages with sufficient power, those who reached the level of archmage, refused to be placed under surveillance and instead went into the shadows, building Dungeons to live in. Some of those individuals are members of Erebrisa."

Nebul said this and asked them to wait a moment. After a short while, he reappeared. When he parted space, the figure of a Black Mage wearing a black robe could be seen on the other side.

"There's no need to reveal my name, is there?" A special magic seemed to be cast over the hood, as only pitch-black darkness could be seen within, completely obscuring the Black Mage's face. "But I know very well who you all are. That magic power and those gloves… Should I say it's an honor to meet the Crimson Sage?"

"Go screw yourself," Scarlet retorted, raising her middle finger at the cackling Black Mage.

"And you over there… hmm, I don't know, but I know exactly who you are. With that immense Demonic Energy and nine tails, it's just too obvious, isn't it? In that case, you must be the Phantom Spear. The one who killed Kim Jonghyun. The fact that you two travel together is famous, after all the ruckus you caused in the cities of the North. Then is that woman the Silent Flash?"

The events in the cities of the North and Kron had already spread as rumors throughout Eria.

*Should I have worn a disguise if I knew this would happen?* Lee Sungmin thought, but it was too late now.

"Do you know Kim Jonghyun?"

"That bastard was the glory and the end of the Black Mages," the Black Mage laughed, lacing his withered fingers together. "He accomplished what no other Black Mage could, but he went too far. Honestly, I think it's a good thing Kim Jonghyun died. Serves him right." He gestured with his hands. "What do you want to ask of me? Speak."

"I want to ask the corpses what happened here."

"Not a difficult task. There are a few souls lingering here, unable to go where they're supposed to…" the Black Mage muttered and began to form a beastfolk.

Before he could finish, Lee Sungmin glanced at Nebul. "Can you make it so that Black Mage can't hear about those matters?"

"Understood."

"I'm not even curious," the Black Mage grumbled. "It's just a job I need to get paid for."

Even so, he steadily formed the beastfolk. The moment the magic unfolded, Nebul closed the open space. A cool wind stirred the room where the corpses lay scattered. Wisps of pale smoke rose from several of the bodies, gathering and taking the form of specters.

"Ask what you want to ask. I've suppressed their wills with magic," the Black Mage's voice echoed from the now-closed space.

Lee Sungmin glanced at Nebul, who confirmed, "The conversation on this side will not be transmitted."

"What was that? Magic?" Scarlet said, clicking her tongue in amazement. "I've never even heard of spatial magic on that level."

Lee Sungmin approached the specters. "Who is the corpse that isn't here?"

At his question, the specters looked down at the faces of the dead with vacant eyes.

"Revias is gone."

"Revias killed me."

"She's the one who survived. She will inherit our Master's legacy."

"Who is Revias?" Lee Sungmin pressed. "And by Master, do you mean the Moon Queen? Why did you kill each other?"

The specters nodded their heads and answered as one story.

"Revias is my senior sister."

"She came to the Moon Palace ten years ago. Our youngest. She came here saying she would avenge her father. An Elf… her martial arts were strong. The Master called Revias the daughter of the Fist Honored One."

"The Master told us to kill each other. She said she would give everything she had to the one who survived."

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. The daughter of the Fist Honored One. The Hunting Team Leader. He had not killed the Hunting Team Leader in the Elf Forest. Had she left the forest and become a disciple of the Moon Queen? No, more importantly.

"The Moon Queen told you to kill each other?" he asked urgently. "But the Moon Queen should have died ten years ago."

"The Master is not dead."

"She hasn't shown herself for ten years, but the Master's voice has guided us."

"We're the ones who are dead."

Lee Sungmin quickly left the room and followed the bloodstains up the stairs. The faint, dried trail stopped in front of an antique door. There was no sign of anyone inside. When he opened the door and entered, he found chunks of flesh, their original form unrecognizable, stuck to the floor.

"...This is..."

Among the chunks of flesh, something was visible. Looking closer, he saw it was a fragment of a shattered head. The flesh was decomposed to a similar degree as the corpses in the basement.

*What could have happened?*

Blade Supreme had been right. The Moon Queen, who he thought was dead, was alive. The daughter of the Fist Honored One had become her disciple and, in the end, received everything from her. What did that mean? There was no way to know. Lee Sungmin gathered a few items from the room. They were the Moon Queen's belongings, and he planned to ask Frau to track her whereabouts with them.

RUMBLE.

The moment he finished gathering the belongings, the Moon Palace shook violently.

"This is…" Scarlet flinched, hunching her shoulders as she quickly formed runic characters in the air. "Spatial erosion? No way, why all of a sudden? This shouldn't be possible!" she shouted in panic.

This was a phenomenon that was impossible with magic. The surrounding scenery began to waver violently.

"We have to escape!" Scarlet yelled. "Space is being eroded! If we're unlucky, we'll be trapped in this space forever!"

At her words, Lee Sungmin quickly tried to summon the Fairy Horse. The horse, however, did not answer his call. The space had already changed into one it could not cross.

*No way. Could it be… now?*

The words left by the fading Slaughter Predator flashed through Lee Sungmin's mind.

*That one day, the Spirit Queen would open her eyes.*
