With a sickening crackle, the bones in Jeniela's neck rotated back into place. Her vision swam, her head spinning with it, and she stared up at Lee Sungmin with a dumbfounded expression. She had been slapped.

*Yes, a slap. Me?*

The elation that had coursed through her veins cooled instantly, ecstasy souring into irritation and pleasure curdling into rage. This was not part of her plan. She had intended to fight him just enough to raise his hopes, only to snatch that hope away at the last moment and inflict an even greater Despair.

Giving him this much hope was never the intention. *This shouldn't be happening.* Jeniela's face twisted at the thought.

Lee Sungmin watched her expression contort, his own face a mask of indifference. He knew precisely what she was thinking. She still failed to grasp the situation, so accustomed to her position of overwhelming strength that she could not comprehend the reversal of their roles.

And that was good. Just as she had wished for his Despair, he now wished for hers. He wanted to see that arrogant Vampire Queen fall into a despair of her own, broken by a reality she could not control. He felt he had earned at least that much.

"If only you," Lee Sungmin began, his hand rising. As Jeniela pushed herself up, he chanted the word. Dragonic. The spell and the mysterious phenomena it commanded unfolded as one.

RUUUUMBLE!

An immense pressure crushed Jeniela's shoulders, pulverizing bone and compressing her organs until she could not withstand it.

"Weren't here."

The pressure slammed her body into the ground. She gasped, her body twisting as a surge of magic tried to protect her and push back. Space distorted as the forces collided.

*Why is this happening?* Jeniela held her breath. The night was long. It was the full moon. She was the Vampire Queen, seven hundred years old. Only moments ago, she had overwhelmed him, played with him like a toy. *So why, now?*

"If only you… weren't here…"

The words were a low growl, the emotions churning beneath his calm facade finally boiling over. Cold reason gave way to a torrent of resentment that needed a target. He didn't have to force it. It was her fault. If Jeniela had never existed, if she had never wished for The End, then the Void Monarch would not have vanished today. The Spear King would not have died. The Fairy Forest would still stand, and so many others would not have had to suffer.

CRACKLE!

The distorted space shattered. The forces of Dragonic, the mysterious phenomena, and Jeniela's magic offset each other and scattered. Jeniela seized the moment, dissolving her body into a red mist to escape his restraint. Lee Sungmin let her go.

The mist coalesced, reforming her body. She glared at him, her face a hard mask. She did not want to admit it, but she had no choice. Dragonic. The Asura Heaven Destruction Art. The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. He was handling all of it perfectly.

Slaughter Predator, she muttered, the words barely a whisper. Just as he had acknowledged it himself, she now had no choice but to do the same.

The realization sparked a conflict within her. The future she desired was one where the Slaughter Predator appeared. If Lee Sungmin had become that predator, then she must not kill him. To do so would be to deny the very future she had wished for over centuries, but she knew this Lee Sungmin was different from the one she envisioned. Even as the Slaughter Predator, he would never truly slaughter and devour the world.

*In that case… I have to kill him.*

Her mind was made up. She would kill him completely. It was too late to trample him and make him Despair. If she tried to afford such a luxury, she would be the one devoured instead. Jeniela acknowledged the power he had achieved. She would have to be satisfied with carving Despair into him through death, with seeing his distorted expression one last time.

Her mindset shifted. With the resolve to kill him now firm, Jeniela held nothing back. Her infinite magic power moved ferociously, tinged with a murderous intent.

"How arrogant…" Lee Sungmin muttered in a low voice.

Jeniela vanished. She leaped across the shattered space, her hand swinging through the air. A tide of red magic followed in its wake, staining the night.

Lee Sungmin did not move. His awakened insight told him he had no need to. He simply stood his ground, spinning his spear in a light, almost casual motion. Her claws clashed against the Formless Spear. The magic that followed her transformed into blades, slashing at his body, but they could not cut him. The Protective Qi Aura around him was too solid, and her magic shattered against it in a crackle of sparks.

Blood sprayed from her mouth as the spear pierced her chest. Before she could even think to pull it out, the Formless Spear exploded, tearing her body to shreds.

Only then did Lee Sungmin take a step. He saw her head among the scattered pieces of flesh, her dislodged eyes holding a new emotion. He confirmed it just as he raised his right foot high.

KRRRRRUMBLE!

The explosion of Two Step Calamity completely blew away her fragments. Far in the distance, her body reconstituted, but before she could grasp the situation or ready her stance, Lee Sungmin took another step. The storm of Three Step Fatality erupted directly in front of her. His Dancing Shadow Soul Break was no longer bound by distance. With his insight fully expanded, and having inherited the martial theories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Blood Demon, the technique had far surpassed its original limits.

"Kugh…!"

A groan escaped her lips as she clutched at her tearing body, pouring out her magic in a desperate reverse flow. Her magic thrashed desperately within the storm, barely managing to alter its trajectory. It was not enough. Lee Sungmin was already standing nearby.

He stomped his foot once more, as if to make a point. Four Step Chaos. Demonic Energy ran wild, and Jeniela's body swayed within it like an autumn leaf in the wind. Gritting her teeth, she unleashed her magic and soared high into the sky, gasping for breath. Her body was continuously exploding, but with the power of the full moon, regenerating was not a major problem.

"Tell me," a voice said from directly behind her.

Jeniela flinched, bending backward in surprise as she swung her arm. Lee Sungmin caught her wrist and crushed it, then drove his knee into her abdomen. Her body bent into an L-shape, her back bursting open as her spine snapped. Blood spewed from her mouth. Lee Sungmin grabbed her hair with his other hand.

"How does it feel?" he asked.

He threw the very words she had sneered at him back in her face, holding her hair tightly as he stared into her eyes. Her eyes trembled. She could not name the emotion she was feeling. It was too unfamiliar.

"…Let go!" she shrieked, swinging both hands to push him away.

In response, Lee Sungmin twisted the hair in his grasp. Her neck bones snapped with a sickening crack, and her body spun halfway around, her flailing arms scratching at empty air. Her killing intent seethed, an audible hiss in the night. To escape, Jeniela cut her own throat. Headless, her body plummeted downward.

In mid-air, her body exploded into a massive pool of red blood. Crimson waves surged, covering the sky. Lee Sungmin threw his spear down into it.

A large hole tore through the crimson sky. The Demonic Energy swirling around Lee Sungmin swelled dramatically. He descended slowly, bending his upper body as he began to spin. Swift Storm Extinction.

In an instant, the sky's color changed. Lee Sungmin became a bolt of violet lightning, filling the crimson expanse with thunderbolts. The lightning exploded, scattering her magic power. Covered in blood, Jeniela fell. He was now in perfect command of the super-speed he once could not properly control.

He caught her by the head as he fell, grinding it against the ground without slowing his descent. Her headless body twitched. He kicked it. It flew a long way, landing outside the forest, where Lee Sungmin immediately plunged his spear into her chest.

CRUUUNCH!

The ground split wide open, sending shattered earth shooting upward. Her head regenerated just in time for her to vomit blood and flail, grabbing the spear with both hands.

"I asked how it feels."

She did not answer. Her distorted eyes, filled with a venomous emotion, merely stared up at him.

"Is it not enough?" he muttered, as if asking himself. "Should I make you understand more clearly?" A new spear formed in his hand, the motion completely natural. He imbued the spear's tip with the pinnacle of martial arts. Jeniela saw it, gasped, and tried to dissolve into mist, but his mysterious phenomena caught her body before she could.

The Spear of the Extreme Void pierced her left arm. A scream erupted from her lips, a sound of pain more excruciating than any she had ever known. Lee Sungmin twisted the spear, severing the arm completely.

"Try regenerating that," he said, gazing down at her struggles. Not even blood flowed from the wound.

A cold sweat broke out on her skin. She gritted her teeth. *It's the Night of the Full Moon. My magic is infinite. I can't die, and any wound should regenerate in an instant., but the severed arm did not regenerate. The pinnacle of martial arts pierced the very origin, a technique infused with the insights the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had reached at his end.

She looked at her left arm with trembling eyes. The pain, the sense of void where her arm should be, it was all utterly alien. The emotion churning inside her was the same.

"I said, try regenerating it," he urged in a low voice.

Gemini watched it all, holding her breath. The annoyance she felt at being killed by him had vanished, extinguished by the rapidly changing situation and replaced by a deep, unsettling conflict. She had a love-hate relationship with Jeniela. She hated her older sister for turning her into a vampire without consent, for stubbornly forcing the ruin she desired upon her, and yet, Gemini loved her. She loved the sister who had turned her into a monster as she pleased. For hundreds of years, Gemini had never doubted that love. That was the source of her conflict now.

It was a ridiculous conflict, she knew. Even if she intervened, she could change nothing. Her power, while strong, was not enough to alter this outcome. Inwardly, she had hoped for this. She had hoped Lee Sungmin would defeat Jeniela, certain it would grant her the freedom she had craved for centuries, but now that it was actually happening, the hundreds of years they had spent together held her back. Before her very eyes, her sister was dying. It was what she had wished for, yet now that it was here, her emotions were in turmoil.

Even if she couldn't change the situation, she could probably die with her. So that the road ahead wouldn't be lonely. Her heart could find no clear path. *Do I really have to die like this, but isn't this what I wanted all along?* She had brought Lee Sungmin here for this very purpose, believing he had the best chance to kill Jeniela because she could not do it herself.

While Gemini was lost in her thoughts, Lee Sungmin kicked Jeniela's head again. Her body fell face down on the ground. Gemini ground her teeth at the sight. Through a gasping breath, Jeniela's eyes found Gemini's.
