The Spear King forced his crumpled body upright. His legs trembled violently, and a terrible churning seized his stomach. No matter how he swallowed, using every martial arts principle he knew, the blood kept surging up his throat. Each breath was a chore, a testament to the shattered ribs that could barely support his upper body.

His left arm was nearly useless, its muscles torn to shreds by claws, but the wound itself was not the true problem. It was the poison. The Spear King clicked his tongue, looking down to see the black venom squirming its way up his arm. Teresa's holy power could purify it, but there was no time for that. In the distance, the monstrous bastard Ju Won was already getting back to his feet.

*That monstrous bastard.*

How many times had he driven his spear through that body? How many times had he shattered its heart or crushed its skull? And yet, the bastard was getting up just fine.

The Spear King chuckled grimly and glanced back at his left arm. Wielding a spear with it was now beyond the realm of martial arts. If he left it alone, however, the poison would continue its relentless spread. It had already crept past his forearm and reached his shoulder, the pain excruciating. Thanks to the agony he had endured on Mount Mushii, it was bearable, but the poison could not be allowed to spread past his shoulder and into his body.

Without a moment's hesitation, the Spear King seized his own left forearm.

CRUNCH!

He tore the limb from his body just below the shoulder. He immediately pressed his acupoints to staunch the bleeding, his head spinning for a moment from the dizzying pain, but it was bearable. Running to Teresa for treatment was not an option. Fleeing this fight was unthinkable. The Spear King intended only to advance, unconditionally.

Ju Won was worthy of being called the strongest opponent he had ever faced, stronger even than the Martial God's fighting spirit from ten years ago. The Spear King bared his teeth in a grin.

Across the field, Ju Won grinned back at the man who had just torn off his own arm. Ever since Howon's death, he had been steeped in lethargy and boredom, but this fight made him forget all of it. Just as the Spear King acknowledged Ju Won, Ju Won acknowledged the Spear King.

"You're too good to kill," Ju Won muttered with a chuckle.

CRACK... CRACKLE.

He cracked his thick fingers, his eyes narrowing.

"I was told to subdue Jeniela and bring her back. They said it didn't matter, as long as she was alive."

"And a guy like you uses poison?"

"If your weapon is the spear, mine is poison. Is there a problem with using your weapon faithfully?"

The bastard had no concept of cowardice, and the Spear King had no intention of blaming him for it. You used everything you could. This was not some neighborhood squabble or a formal duel with observers. This was a duel to the death.

"No," the Spear King replied, a grin spreading across his face. "No problem at all."

He gripped the spear with his one remaining hand. Suddenly one-armed, his balance felt off. He swung the weapon a few times, testing its new feel. Ju Won stood his ground, making no move to rush him. He had enjoyed the fight enough, and he could tell it would soon be over.

The Spear King was a magnificent opponent, better even than Howon, the former leader of the Lycanthropes, or his trusted subordinate, Brock, but the victor of this fight was Ju Won. Anyone could see that. The Spear King, having lost an arm, was exhausted, while Ju Won was not. He was a little tired, it was true, but otherwise perfectly fine. Ju Won felt a pang of pity that such a warrior was only a weak human. If he had been an inhuman, they could have fought longer, more joyfully.

The Spear King, however, was not thinking of defeat. He knew better than anyone the wretched condition of his body. His poisoned arm was gone, he was dizzy from blood loss, and a few broken bones made every movement a struggle, yet none of it meant defeat. He could still fight. The pain was intense, but fear was absent. Despair had no place here. There was only the joy that he could fight more. The Spear King lifted his foot and walked forward.

He had used every technique he possessed. Based on Spear Unification, he had unleashed every skill he had mastered over a lifetime, every art he could wield to perfection. His body was weaker than Ju Won's, but the techniques he had honed bridged that physical gap, allowing him to fight on equal terms. If Ju Won had possessed a human body like his own, this fight would have ended long ago with the Spear King's victory.

"It's not enough," the Spear King muttered, his lips twitching. The techniques he had spent a lifetime mastering, the ones he could wield perfectly, were simply not enough.

He tightened his grip on the spear in his right hand.

*To a place further ahead. To a place more distant.*

A smile formed on his deathly pale face. He knew. He knew he could not handle it yet, but he had chased after it for ten long years. The battle between the Martial God and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had been on a level so high he could not have dared to intervene. The Chairman's methods, which had overwhelmed the incredibly strong Martial God, had completely shattered the framework of martial arts he had practiced his entire life and shown him a more distant world.

He wanted to reach it. After running for a decade, he had barely managed to glimpse its edge. He had only glimpsed it, not stood on equal footing, not surpassed it. It was the same now.

The Black Dragon Knight used to sneer at him whenever he suffered a minor qi deviation, saying that if a crow-tit tries to follow a stork, its legs will tear. He was right. Their legs were different, making it impossible to keep up. Still, he had to chase. He became a crow-tit trying to match a stork's single stride. To keep up with that one stride, he took countless small steps without rest.

The spear in his hand felt heavy.

It was time to end this.

BOOM, KABOOOM!

Explosions echoed from not too far away, the sounds of Jeniela's battle. Ju Won must not be allowed to interfere in that fight. Still, he wanted to see their clash before the duel between Jeniela and the Phantom Spear was completely over.

Ju Won approached. The Spear King's vision was blurry, but he did not bother to wipe the blood from his eyes. For him, 'seeing' no longer had any meaning. His senses were wide open. The cool breeze touched his skin. The stench of blood brushed past his nose. From the newly gripped spear came the smell of steel and oil. Heavy. The weight of the spear, a feeling he had known his entire life, felt new.

*Were you always this heavy?*

The Spear King was the spear. He could not even remember why he had first picked it up. The reason was not important. He continued his stride. The crow-tit's steps were slow, its stride narrow. A stork would have already been far ahead. He should not chase the stork by walking.

*He probably thinks he's won.*

The Spear King was accustomed to battle. A human body was not sturdy like a monster's. It did not regenerate if its head was blown off or its heart burst. Ju Won fought by taking full advantage of this fact. The bastard was used to hunting humans. He rarely defended. If he could not dodge, he would simply take the hit with his bare body. For a human, it was a tactic of mutual destruction, but for a monster that revived even after death, it was an efficient hunting method.

Ju Won knew that the Spear King's spear was not fatal to him. No matter how strong and fast it was with martial arts, his undying body would just keep regenerating. The Spear King's spear could not kill Ju Won.

He had taken enough time. He had pushed his body to the brink of death and even cut off his own arm. The bastard was certain of his victory. He had to be. The Spear King had fought to instill that complete certainty in him. He wanted two things.

For the bastard to be certain of his victory.

And for the bastard to acknowledge me as his rival.

The bastard was coming to kill him. The Spear King walked forward without stopping, raising the tightly gripped spear before him. Certainty? There was none. It would be good if it worked, and if not... no regrets. He had done his best. The Spear King twisted his lips into a smile. How far had the stork gone? Where was he walking? No, was there even a need to walk?

His foot stomped the ground.

The crow-tit spread its wings and flew. The straight line toward Ju Won looked like a long, single path to the Spear King. The scenery distorted. His heart yearned for only one thing. The thing in his hand was heavy, and then the weight vanished. For a moment, he could not tell what he was holding. *Is this a spear?*

*What, of course it is.*

With his only hand, the Spear King thrust forward with the thing he held, something he could not tell if it was a spear or not, yet firmly believed to be a 'spear'.

Ju Won did not dodge. Certain of his victory, he decided to indulge the final struggle of the worthy opponent who had relieved his boredom and lethargy. Ju Won's fist, imbued with terrible power, moved to shatter the Spear King's weapon.

The wind stopped.

The Spear King looked down at his own body. His right arm was barely attached. Ju Won's strike had blown away more than half of his upper body. At this point, he could not even feel pain anymore. His legs gave out, and he collapsed on the spot.

He looked at his outstretched right arm, the spear he still held, and what lay beyond it. The path he had glimpsed for a moment had not vanished. It remained.

The Spear King twisted his blood-drenched lips into a smile. *Did you see that?* He kept the question that arose in his mind to himself. There was no need to say it out loud. At the entrance to the path where the spear pointed, a path without an end, only the legs of Ju Won, whose upper body had completely vanished, stood there blankly.

"Bastard."

Ju Won's body staggered and fell backward. He could no longer regenerate. The Spear King's spear, having transcended the realm of transcendence, had completely obliterated Ju Won's soul. The Spear King chuckled, his shoulders shaking.

"You should've dodged, you idiot."

Though he had made sure he would not. A sense of relief washed over him as he comfortably closed his eyes. They were both in the same boat, but still... Ju Won had died first. That pleased the Spear King. Yes, the victor of this fight was not the monster Ju Won, but the human, the Spear King.

*Still, it was pretty good.*

He was not talking about the life he had lived. He smiled in satisfaction, replaying the fight with a little fighting spirit. The last time he had unfolded this technique, he could not even wield it properly and had coughed up blood. This time, he wielded it to the very end without coughing up blood.

Meaning, his legs did not tear. *Black Dragon Knight, that son of a bitch should have seen this...*

Yes. That was a small regret. That I was so much stronger than you, and that I had magnificently accomplished the very thing you sneered about, the crow-tit's legs tearing.

*It was fun...*

It was a satisfying and enjoyable fight. It was a shame he could not get his revenge on the Martial God, a shame he could not finish his fight with the Phantom Spear. Still, those things did not remain as lingering regrets. It could not be helped. Because he knew it was not over.

Before annihilating Ju Won's body, the path the spear advanced upon. Ju Won, who stood before it, and beyond him, the long, single path that stretched on.

The Spear King felt his consciousness fading. In that moment, he realized how small this world was, Mana. How insignificant those who struggled within it were, Mana, and how insignificant even he was with a little fighting spirit, Mana.

*This is the ultimate hell...*

He closed his eyes and smiled. He saw the place where the 'real' monsters, who had surpassed the status of humans, roamed, walking that path.

*I am going to that place.*

The Spear King did not let go of the spear he still held, not until the very end.

At the entrance of the single path, the Spear King slowly took a step. Far in the distance, the stork that had gone ahead with its large strides spread its wings and welcomed him with a smile.

The path with no visible end was wide and far. The Spear King was small.

It did not matter.

The Spear King shouldered the spear he had not let go of and walked the path.

With small steps, without stopping, on and on.
