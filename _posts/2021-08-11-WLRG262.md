The spear shot forward.

Forward.

Forward...?

The Spear King's eyes widened. He tried to track the approaching weapon, but the spear Lee Sungmin had thrust advanced only a short distance before dissolving into a fine mist. The wavering haze that was once a spear scattered in all directions. Unsure of what was happening, the Spear King refrained from acting rashly and took a cautious step back.

Almost immediately, the scattered mist surged toward him. The spear in the Spear King's hand began to spin. To him, a man who had perfected the true meaning of Spear Unification, the spear was not merely a weapon but an extension of his own body. It was the Spear King himself. Just as he willed it, the spear moved, crossing freely between the realms of consciousness and unconsciousness.

"......"

His spinning spear tore the entire misty martial arts theory to shreds as it rushed him. The mist, however, had no form. The Spear King watched the hazy assault retreat and flinched, a flicker of surprise crossing his face. He quickly glanced back to where Lee Sungmin had been standing, but the man was no longer there.

"......"

He realized then what kind of martial art Lee Sungmin had unleashed. It was a form of illusion arts. Deceiving the eyes could be called the basis of such techniques, but what Lee Sungmin had displayed had already far surpassed that simple realm. How could something containing such a clear and potent killing intent be definitively called a mere illusion?

The Spear King's thoughts were correct. The eighth move of Lee Sungmin's Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Illusionary World, was a martial art containing the theory of Extreme Illusion. It was a technique that the fighting spirit of Lee Sungmin could not have properly executed, but that was no longer the case for the man standing before him. This martial art required an enormous amount of internal energy, and the current Lee Sungmin possessed enough power to astound even the Spear King. Not only that, but the Illusionary World, touched by the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, had become a completely different technique from the one the fighting spirit had known. This was also the first time Lee Sungmin had ever used this new version in a real battle.

The mist that so intensely distorted the space was a fog of death, created from enhanced qi heavily infused with Demonic Energy. Lee Sungmin's spear moved as if gliding through it, making it impossible to track with the eyes or sense with one's energy. Even for a master of the Spear King's level, the result was no different.

*It's filled with a troubled energy.*

The Demonic Energy Lee Sungmin possessed was the cause. The mist of enhanced qi covering the area was saturated with it, making it difficult for even the Spear King's sharp senses to track Lee Sungmin's true body.

And Illusionary World was not over yet. This was a spear technique, yet it was a martial art that transcended the very realm of spearmanship.

SHHH.

SHHHHHHH.

The Spear King listened intently as the mist flowed around him. He strained his ears, trying to pinpoint the sound, but he knew he must not chase it. He understood that fact well. A master on the level of the Phantom Spear would never make such an unnecessary sound while moving.

*He's trying to deceive me.*

He looked around. The mist had already filled the entire area. The Spear King bared his teeth in a grin.

*A martial art initiated with a spear, yet it transcends the realm of spearmanship. The internal energy required to dominate space itself is incredible, but it's even more amazing that he still has energy to spare after pouring out so much. He knew this was a disadvantageous fight, so he created a space that was an absolute nightmare for me. Yes, is this that kind of martial art?*

He had experience with illusion arts. The Martial God was also a figure with considerable skill in them, and the Spear King had once challenged the man and had a terrible time against his techniques. Thanks to that memory, facing illusions was not exactly pleasant, but he had no intention of treating this as a mere nuisance.

There was only one way to break an illusion art, a method the Spear King knew well. He had to shatter it head-on with greater power. Recalling this, he immediately took action.

KWAAANG!

The spear he swung tore through the air, shaking the mist-filled space to its core.

SHHHHK.

SHHHHHHHHK.

The sound of something scraping the ground did not stop. Just as the Spear King was about to swing his spear again, the grating noise abruptly ceased, and then.

PWAK!

A section of space burst open as a spear shot through. As if he had been waiting for it, the Spear King twisted his stance and swung his own weapon to meet it. At the moment of impact, however, the attacking spear once again dissolved into mist.

*Hmph!*

Sensing an instinctual threat, the Spear King sharply twisted his upper body to the side.

PISHUT!

The thrusting spear grazed his side. That was only the beginning. From all directions, the mist surged toward him once more, transforming into dozens of spear strikes that assailed him from every angle. If he failed to defend properly, he would be pierced dozens of times and torn to shreds. Picturing that chilling image of death, the Spear King smiled again.

"Uhahaha!"

With a loud laugh, he spun his spear wildly.

KWADEDEUK!

The encroaching mist was caught in the powerful rotation created by his spear, but Illusionary World was not over yet. From beyond the swirling haze, the 'real' spear, held in the hands of the endlessly moving Lee Sungmin, exploded forward.

"There you are!" the Spear King roared.

He thrust his own spear, now fully imbued with power. The mist could not withstand its momentum and retreated. Lee Sungmin's spear and the Spear King's spear clashed. Through the thinning haze, the Spear King saw Lee Sungmin's eyes, dyed a brilliant, burning gold.

The tip of his opponent's spear wavered.

"......"

The Spear King sucked in a sharp breath. For a brief, disorienting moment, he felt as if the very ground beneath his feet had suddenly given way. That sudden sensation was enough to momentarily fluster even a master of his caliber.

It was a very brief opening. As if waiting for that exact gap, Lee Sungmin's spear moved.

PWAK!

A spear shot out at close range, aiming for the Spear King's chest. The Spear King, however, was a veteran. He immediately split the spear he was holding into two. With his staggering body, he leaned back to create distance from Lee Sungmin's attack.

TTAK!

The short spear he swung collided with Lee Sungmin's. Even in a posture where delicate movements seemed impossible, the Spear King did not stop and repeatedly attempted to attack, but this time too, the moment he saw the spear tip, he perceived the distance between himself and Lee Sungmin to have greatly increased.

*Illusion arts? Or movement arts?* In that short moment, countless possibilities flashed through his mind.

He paid it no mind and shot his spear forward anyway. Lee Sungmin's body, which had been moving away, suddenly closed the distance. The distortion of his senses was the illusion art. Then what about this?

PUUK!

Lee Sungmin's body was pierced by the Spear King's spear.

Still, the Spear King's hands felt no sensation of 'piercing'. No blood splattered. It was an illusion. The moment he realized it, Lee Sungmin's body collapsed into mist. The collapsed mist did not remain inert. It transformed into spear strikes again, and this time, it was impossible for even the Spear King to completely defend or evade.

"Keuk!"

PABABAK!

He hurriedly swung his spear to scatter the strikes, but the ones he couldn't completely disperse grazed his body one after another. Even in that situation, ending it with only minimal injuries was the best the Spear King could do.

He felt a thrill from the throbbing pain that rose from all over his body. How long had it been since he was injured in a fight? Blood poured from the shallow wounds covering his body, and the Spear King laughed loudly.

"This is great!"

Lee Sungmin, meanwhile, was feeling a dizzying headache. Illusionary World was a combination of extremely high-level illusion arts and physical techniques. The spear's movements had to continuously follow the movements of the illusion, making it impossible to execute other forms. Unless he kept moving the spear to continue the art, Illusionary World would shatter.

It was a powerful martial art, but it had many drawbacks, chief among them a lack of sustainability. Even with Lee Sungmin's immense internal energy, sustaining Illusionary World for a long time was impossible. His inner qi were already depleted. The reality was that he was now sustaining the technique with only Demonic Energy.

"Huu, huuk..." Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, glaring at the Spear King. The blood-soaked, laughing man didn't look like he would fall anytime soon. This was the first time he had ever sustained Illusionary World for so long.

[How about you retreat?] Void Monarch advised. [You're only using Demonic Energy right now. This Elder is holding on in your Mental World, but... this is a pretty dangerous situation. To defeat that bastard, you'll have to fight for a lot longer, and your Demonic Energy might run rampant and try to swallow your consciousness during the battle.]

*I know.*

[If you know, then retreat.]

*I don't think he'll let me go.*

Lee Sungmin suppressed his throbbing headache and looked at the Spear King, who was swinging his spear like a madman, paying no mind to his wounds. A tremendous power swelled each time his weapon moved. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and raised his spear above his head.

KURURUNG!

Purple lightning seethed within the mist. To the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear's Illusionary World, he added the incantation of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. The Demonic Energy that had been boiling in his qi center spread throughout his body as a flashing light wrapped around his spear.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Eighth Move, Illusionary World Myriad Thunder.

The mist that had been covering the space turned entirely into a roiling thundercloud.

KURURUNG!

At the loud crack of thunder, the Spear King felt a pleasant tension and swung his spear.

KWAAAANG!

The thundercloud exploded all at once. A continuous stream of purple lightning descended upon the Spear King, who let out a laugh-tinged roar and swung his spear to meet it, splitting the lightning bolts apart.

"......"

The lightning vanished. The loud thunderclap and the threat of the descending storm had been a lie. An illusion. The Spear King hastily withdrew the inner qi he had unleashed.

It was too late. The light that had illuminated the area disappeared, and for a moment, the world became a pitch-black night. Then, a long, violet bolt of lightning split the darkness in two.

"Kereuk!"

The Spear King swung his spear and the hastily recovered inner qi to block the purple lightning. In the illusory world, the Myriad Thunder was also just a trick. The real attack was the single spear strike hidden within it. Wrapped in dense purple lightning, it possessed a power greater than a bolt that could split the sky. The Spear King swallowed the blood surging up his throat and thrust his spear to the very end.

JJIJIK, JJIJIJIK!

The lightning was torn apart. Lee Sungmin poured all his remaining strength into his pushing spear.

"......"

His heart pounded loudly as his blood and qi stirred. He felt a nasty sensation in his qi center. The Demonic Energy was swelling. Inside his head, Void Monarch shouted something, but Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip with a terrible premonition. Blood trickled from his lip, which he had chewed until it was raw.

The price for repeatedly overusing Demonic Energy was coming due. A normal human body would have been shattered, but his body, transformed into a Yokai, would not break. His consciousness, however, was fading. An unknown, wailing sound mixed in his ears, becoming an unpleasant noise.

PAT.

Illusionary World ended. The mist that had covered the area vanished cleanly, as if it had never been there. The Spear King had succeeded in thrusting his spear to the end.

The lightning scattered. Without hesitation, the Spear King threw himself toward Lee Sungmin. Panting heavily, Lee Sungmin sensed his approach and hurriedly raised his spear.

KWAAANG!

The spear swung by the Spear King, who had leaped into the air, collided with Lee Sungmin's. Unable to completely deflect the impact, Lee Sungmin vomited blood and staggered backward.

"Haat!"

With a sharp cry, the Spear King's spear shot forward. Lee Sungmin clung to the fading thread of his consciousness, but in his condition, facing the Spear King's attack was impossible.

KWADEUK!

The swung spear struck Lee Sungmin's side. The impact penetrated his armor, breaking his ribs and rupturing his internal organs. His body flew helplessly, landing far away and tumbling across the ground.

"...What is this," the Spear King muttered, slowly lowering his spear as his brow furrowed. He turned his head and spat out bloody saliva. Ptoo.

"Are you tired?" he asked, approaching Lee Sungmin while spinning his spear. It had been a long time since he had a proper fight like this. His entire body was hot with tension and excitement, but what was this? He wanted to enjoy it a little more, to fight a little longer.

"Get up," the Spear King urged the man who was staggering to his feet.

Lee Sungmin, however, couldn't manage to stand up completely. In the middle of rising, the strength left his legs, and he collapsed back down. At that, the Spear King's face twisted.

"I said, get up!"

*You think I want to be like this...!*

Lee Sungmin heard the roar through the noise in his head. In the end, he couldn't get up and collapsed again as his strength gave out. Seeing this, the Spear King made an angry face and stomped his foot on the ground.

"This feels like being interrupted while taking a shit!"

It was an incomparably crude metaphor, but it perfectly described how he felt.
