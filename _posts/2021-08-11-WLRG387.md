Darkness gave way to a sky stained a deeper red than twilight. Beneath a massive full moon, Jeniela spread her arms wide to face Lee Sungmin, the ancient queen of monsters promising no mercy this time.

He knew then that there would be no running today, that any thought of escape was meaningless. He had told her he killed Aine. If he failed to kill Jeniela here, if he turned and ran, she would become a calamity of The End. She would undoubtedly become the monster that brought this world to ruin, long before the Greed buried deep within the Sleeping Forest could even stir.

No one could stop her. Even now, Jeniela was likely stronger than anyone in the world. If she decided to continuously feed, build an army, and strengthen herself, any slim chance of stopping her would vanish completely.

So he had to stop her here.

He could not run.

He had promised Wiji Hoyeon he was here, told her to believe in him. He refused to make his words a lie or become a coward who fled in fear.

His gaze locked back on Jeniela, whose eyes curved into crescents. Her narrowed pupils watched him, filled with a terrible anticipation.

Beside him, Osla spread her two wings wide and brought her hands together before her chest as if in prayer. Light erupted from her wings, enveloping Lee Sungmin's body in the Fairy Queen's blessing. The suppressed fear vanished, his mind shielded from Jeniela's magic, a power that could shatter it with mere contact. His heart, still feeling alien in his chest, pounded as if it would burst.

"Aaaaaah!"

With a roar, Lee Sungmin kicked off the air, the heavy spear feeling like an extension of his will. He unleashed Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, its theory of Extreme Speed merging with the Violet Dawn Divine Art. Though his Body Reformation was incomplete, not yet the full transformation the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had described, his speed still reached a realm beyond anything he had known before.

BZZZZT!

He became a streak of lightning surging toward Jeniela, who only cackled with laughter and spread her arms wide in welcome.

KWAANG!

His spear pierced through her magic and impaled her chest, driving her body back. Blood surged from her throat, staining her lips a deeper red. She smiled, revealing blood-soaked teeth.

"Good," Jeniela said, her voice thrilled.

Her magic instantly ran wild, a red explosion erupting from the space between them. Lee Sungmin used Gale Thunder to leap through space just before the blast caught him, his head feeling ready to burst from the flood of visual information the high-speed world forced into his brain. A wide expanse of red light swept over him. He drew the spear back, then thrust it forward to unfold the Void Path. The technique, which had always opened a way for him, now collided with Jeniela's magic.

The spear, thrust with all his might, tore a narrow hole. He added rotation, widening the gap just as the magic exploded. Beyond the blast, Jeniela was pointing her index finger at him. A flash of light burst forth.

KUUUUNG!

A crushing weight descended upon him. His eyes widened as he drew upon his mysterious phenomena, the Demonic Energy within him erupting outward. The sound vanished as magic and Demonic Energy collided, producing nothing but silence. Free from the pressure, Lee Sungmin moved.

[That's your problem,] the Void Monarch spat inside his head. [You think too little of yourself. You are not weak. No talent? You damn bastard, you've gone through more than enough hellish training and miraculous encounters to make up for that.]

Jeniela was not the only one receiving the full moon's blessing. Lee Sungmin, with the body of a Yokai, felt its power as well.

[Think about the source of the Demonic Energy you possess!] the Void Monarch roared.

A boiling sound came from within Lee Sungmin's body. He remembered taking Urmuri's Demonic Energy ten years ago, a power he had mostly been unable to use since Aine's death, but that was not what the Void Monarch meant. The ancient yokai's consciousness rose to the surface, intervening directly in a way he rarely had before.

[Remember who this Elder is!]

The Void Monarch. The great yokai subjugated four hundred years ago. The strongest and most wicked of his kind, a being who would have considered even the current Jeniela an easy opponent. If the Spirits had not intervened back then, his subjugation would have been impossible. Though most of the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy had vanished, his existence had not been extinguished and remained within Lee Sungmin's body.

"Void Monarch!" Jeniela shouted with a loud laugh as his Demonic Energy surged, adding to the aura already surrounding Lee Sungmin. The splitting headache subsided, a welcome effect of Osla's blessing.

"Do you think things will change just because you've joined in? Ahahaha! I've already surpassed you!"

Jeniela's magic raged. Her hands clawed at the empty air.

KRAAACK!

Space tore apart, and a crimson radiance flooded out. Flashing light exploded in all directions as a world of seamless magic power unfolded.

[Surpassed me? Bullshit!]

The shout echoed in Lee Sungmin's mental world, his own emotions surging with it. He grit his teeth hard.

KRRRRRRR!

An immense amount of Demonic Energy flowed into his spear. The weapon, made from the remains of a Dragon, trembled violently. Lee Sungmin swung the spear with all his might.

The red world vanished in an instant. His Demonic Energy filled the gaps in the scattering magic power. He had to change the battlefield to a place where he held the advantage. The Demonic Energy coiling around his spear tip gathered to a single point before expanding outward.

The Illusionary World opened. Demonic Energy swallowed the space. Jeniela allowed it to happen. Through the senses of her Blood Demons, she had already grasped all the martial arts Lee Sungmin had used in their fight. She knew what this was too. She did not know its name, but she knew the phenomenon it caused.

*It's closer to magic than martial arts. No, in this case, should I call it yokai magic?*

The space filled with Demonic Energy. Lee Sungmin's body blurred and vanished as a thick fog blocked her vision. Even her eyes could not pierce it. One by one, her senses were cut off. Ignoring her useless ears and nose, Jeniela spread her hands wide. She knew what was coming, and that knowledge kept her from flinching. In truth, she would not have flinched even if she hadn't known.

[Jeniela probably knows what kind of martial art this is.]

From beyond the fog of the Illusionary World, Lee Sungmin observed her movements.

GRRRR...

Jeniela's magic blazed red. Smiling joyfully, she looked at the empty space where he was not. It didn't matter that she knew.

Purple lightning erupted from all directions, Myriad Thunder filling the space swallowed by the Illusionary World. Even facing such a wondrous sight, Jeniela just chuckled.

KWAAAAANG!

A massive sound echoed, as if the world were ending. Myriad Thunder continued to explode around Jeniela, but she remained at its center, arms spread wide. The red barrier she had deployed did not waver even amidst the tremendous impact.

*The firepower is insufficient.*

Lee Sungmin grit his teeth. Even with the ceaselessly exploding Myriad Thunder, he could not break through her barrier. He raised the spear high.

The insights of the Transcendent Realm were about seizing and dominating space, a pinnacle the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had embedded into the Illusionary World.

*That's not enough.* Lee Sungmin's eyes flew open, his pounding heart making his blood boil.

His heightened mind glimpsed an arrogant place beyond his reach. The Illusionary World, fixed by Wiji Hoyeon and infused with the Chairman's insights, was not enough. His brain felt like it would explode as the incantations of the perfectly mastered technique filled his mind.

*Not enough.*

It wasn't that the Chairman's insights were lacking. The martial art he had fixed was a divine art unparalleled in the world, but it was tailored for the Lee Sungmin of that time, a man he had now grown incomparably beyond.

Therefore.

In his mind, the incantations of the Illusionary World deconstructed piece by piece. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had said that if he achieved a true Body Reformation, his insight would expand, allowing him to perfectly master even the most esoteric martial incantations the moment he saw them.

"Grrrrr...!"

Lee Sungmin's lips trembled. His Body Reformation was incomplete, his insight not fully expanded as the Chairman had described. Nevertheless, he had to do it. He shoved the desired incantations into the deconstructed framework of the old.

His head, his heart, his very body felt like they would explode. Inner power and Demonic Energy erupted from his qi center, gushing out from his skin as the world itself shook.

The Demonic Energy that had swallowed the space now moved as Lee Sungmin wished. Amidst the ceaseless explosions of Myriad Thunder, Jeniela's eyebrow twitched. She had just been thinking about breaking through, but the very nature of the Demonic Energy eroding the space had changed.

[You...]

Even the Void Monarch was surprised. The spear was heavy, so heavy he could barely swing it. His tightly clenched teeth shattered, and blood streamed from his gums. He poured all the inner arts and Demonic Energy filling his body into the spear as a single, new incantation filled his mind. Through a complete Spear Unification, the spear became him, and he became the spear.

BZZZZT!

Dozens of cracks formed in space. Inside her barrier, Jeniela's eyes widened.

"Ahahaha!"

She opened her mouth and laughed loudly. In response to the laughter from afar, Lee Sungmin thrust his spear forward. Intangible spears, each containing the martial arts theory of Genesis, shot out from the cracks in the air.

Inside her barrier, Jeniela spread her hands.

KWAAAAANG!

Dozens of Genesis spears collided with Jeniela's barrier. The barrier could not withstand the force and shattered into pieces. The spears that broke through descended upon Jeniela, leaving no trace of her body behind. She was swept away, not even a single piece of flesh remaining.

[She's not dead!] the Void Monarch roared.

Lee Sungmin was already moving, kicking off the air and shooting forward, the sound of his own heart pounding in his ears.

"I know," he answered in a cracked voice.

Despite unleashing such an attack, he was not exhausted. The limits of his inner power and Demonic Energy were unfathomable, so vast he wondered where in his body such power could be hidden.

Jeniela's annihilated body reformed. Despite being struck by so many Genesis spears, she had taken no damage. By the time her head had reformed, Lee Sungmin was thrusting his spear right before her nose.

Her head exploded again into a red mist that swallowed his body as he pushed the spear forward. The mist was the essence of magic power, terrifyingly strong. Lee Sungmin reflexively raised his Protective Qi Aura.

[It's alright,] Osla's voice reached him.

Though she had not entered the Illusionary World, she was observing everything from the outside. A brilliant, five-colored light covered his body.

PAAAANG!

The bursting light drove back the mist. The scattered particles gathered, and Jeniela revealed herself from within, smiling sweetly with a flawless face.

"The Fairy Queen's blessing... Right. This is her forest."

Lee Sungmin's body vanished into the fog again. *This place is annoying.* Jeniela thought, looking up at a sky where the full moon was no longer visible. Even so, its blessing still affected her. It was just annoying that she couldn't see it.

"I'll have to break it."

*It's not enough.*

Just modifying the incantations of one technique was not enough. Blood trickled from Lee Sungmin's nose. He needed something more fundamental. Not just deconstructing a single technique, but every martial art he had ever learned. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Violet Dawn Divine Art, Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. All of them opened up and lined up in his mind.

[Be careful!] the Void Monarch shouted.

There wasn't enough time for that.

KWAJIK!

The magic power that erupted from Jeniela's body swelled immensely, beginning to erode the Illusionary World itself. She was not simply an ancient vampire but also a mage who had studied for hundreds of years. Her encroaching magic grasped the very structure of the space.

*No.*

His bloodshot eyes turned crimson.

*I'm faster.*

He had to be faster than she could grasp and deconstruct the spatial structure. The world of the Illusionary World changed.
