Ten years.

"Ten years?"

The words felt absurd on his tongue, a hollow, disbelieving sound. He had thought five years at most, but the man said ten. A decade. Enough time for mountains to crumble and rivers to change their course, all while he was trapped under a Sealing. Lee Sungmin repeated the words in a small voice, a dry laugh catching in his throat.

*So I was sealed in Germud for ten years, but by whom?*

"Who sealed me?"

"...I'm not certain. However, the one who sealed you wasn't a magic user, but a shaman, and a shaman capable of such a Sealing is not common."

*Frau.*

The answer clicked into place with a single name. He understood, at least in part, what must have happened. Frau had been there in Germud ten years ago, during the battle of fighting spirits. Abel had given his lifespan to Gries and perished, but Frau had survived. Then came the Reversal Magic, Kim Jonghyun's final gift. It had torn Lee Sungmin's body from his consciousness, turning him into a complete, rampaging Yokai. Frau must have sealed him to stop that monstrous form.

*It's a relief I'm not dead.*

"Who are you?" Lee Sungmin asked, his gaze settling on Rao Xian before sweeping across the room.

Dozens of Vampires stood watching. The Lycanthropes had stopped their drinking and now stood nearby, arms crossed. He saw Black Mages and martial artists, too. He raised a finger, pointing to the bound prisoners from the orthodox sects.

"And them? Why are they tied up?"

A man waking after a decade was bound to have questions. Though answering each one was tedious, Rao Xian showed no sign of impatience, determined to make the best possible impression.

"...My name is Rao Xian," he began. "I am one of the Vampire Queen's own."

Vampire Queen, Jeniella of Blood Charm. At the mention of her name, Lee Sungmin's eyes narrowed.

"We came all the way to this distant southern city to release your Sealing."

"Are those martial artists also Predators?"

"Of course not. How could a human join the Predators? They're just errand boys we've taken in," Rao Xian replied, glancing at the martial artists of the Blood Alliance. They showed no offense at the title.

Just as Lee Sungmin had done, Rao Xian gestured with his own finger toward the bound prisoners.

"Those are from the Orthodox Murim Alliance. They heard we were up to something here and tried to interfere, so we captured them."

"Then why are those ones dead?" Lee Sungmin asked, pointing to the bodies still bound and collapsed on the ground.

Rao Xian snickered. "That was to release your Sealing. Our Black Mages aren't skilled with shamanistic seals, so we had to use... other methods."

People had died for his freedom. The feeling was not a pleasant one, but he wasn't shameless enough to confront Rao Xian about killing innocent people. Lee Sungmin simply nodded and posed another question.

"Was it the Vampire Queen who ordered you to release my Sealing?"

"...Yes," Rao Xian answered after a brief pause.

It was a lie. Jeniela had never ordered him to release Lee Sungmin's Sealing.

"Where is Jeniela?"

"The Queen is at her mansion in the North."

"Why didn't she come herself?"

"She said she wanted to wait," Rao Xian replied.

Lee Sungmin let out another hollow laugh. She wanted to wait? Was that just typical Jeniela, or was there some other scheme at play? Perhaps a reason she couldn't move in the current situation? He certainly had no intention of obediently trotting off to the North just because she summoned him, and Jeniela had to know that. Unless she came herself, he would have to be crazy to go to her as she bid. What could she possibly want?

Lee Sungmin shrugged. "What if I don't go?"

"...Then we'll have to take you by force," Rao Xian muttered.

This was wrong.

Jeniela had not ordered this. In fact, she had given no orders concerning Lee Sungmin at all. She had not foreseen his existence or a future entangled with him, and so she had intended to leave him be, but a year ago, Jeniela's Eye of Futuresight had gone blind. She could no longer see what was to come. Since then, she had not left her mansion. Her mental link with her vampires had all but severed, leaving them neglected.

Rao Xian had acted on his own for his unresponsive queen. He had gathered Lycanthropes, Vampires, and Black Mages. He had even used the Blood Alliance Jeniela had assembled before her seclusion to travel all the way to this distant southern city.

"Do you think you can?" Lee Sungmin asked, staring intently at Rao Xian.

The situation grew grim. Tang Ahee swallowed hard, unable to tell if this turn of events was good or bad for them. Nearby, Moyong Chan stared at Lee Sungmin's back with bloodshot eyes.

"...Save me!" Moyong Chan shouted after a moment of silence. "I'm Moyong Chan! The younger brother of Moyong Seojin, the man you killed!"

Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinched at the name. He slowly turned his head, his glowing golden eyes meeting Moyong Chan's. The young man gulped. Ten years had passed, and Moyong Chan had changed so much, from his build to his face. A flicker of bitterness passed through Lee Sungmin as he looked at him. He hadn't killed Moyong Seojin. Moyong Daeun's death in the North had been the result of his own madness for revenge, for crossing a line he should not have.

"You've grown up," Lee Sungmin muttered, then turned back to Rao Xian. "It seems they have no further use."

"If you come with us, I won't harm them."

"Aren't you misunderstanding something?" The corners of Lee Sungmin's mouth lifted slightly. "You can't force me. It might be a different story if the Vampire Queen came herself."

"...Aren't you the one looking down on us?" Rao Xian's brow furrowed. He did not underestimate Lee Sungmin. This was the man who had defeated the Death Lord, Vollander. He had prepared accordingly. He was Jeniela's fifth vampire, one who had received a particularly large amount of her power. The other Vampires with him were high-ranking monsters in their own right. He even had the support of the Lycanthropes. He would have liked to bring Ju Won as well, but the man had disappeared a few months after Jeniela secluded herself.

Lee Sungmin chuckled. Rao Xian was right. The inhumans gathered here were powerful monsters. The martial artists were nothing, mere errand boys, and the Black Mages were not much of a threat. The Vampires and Lycanthropes, however, were a different story.

"I don't want to fight you. If you would just come with us..."

Their desires were at odds, and with neither willing to yield, the conversation had reached a stalemate. This was how conflict was born. All that mattered now was who would move first.

Lee Sungmin glanced at his empty hands. It was a little disappointing. His body felt light, and he yearned to swing his spear.

*It can't be helped.*

The tips of Lee Sungmin's feet lifted from the floor.

"......"

His figure blurred and then vanished, the air he once occupied tearing apart with a deafening explosion. The shockwave alone clawed at the earth, sending nearby martial artists screaming and tumbling across the ground. The screams were still echoing when Lee Sungmin reappeared in the center of the sprawling Blood Alliance, straightening his body. His movement was its own wide-range attack. He lowered his stance again.

"Gasp!"

A Black Mage met his eyes and fumbled for his staff, but before a single word of incantation could be uttered, Lee Sungmin was already past him. The bodies of the Black Mages were flung high into the air, already unconscious. There had been no need to even strike them.

*So fast...!*

Rao Xian launched himself from the ground, the twenty Vampires he had brought surging after him. The Lycanthropes let out bestial howls as their bodies twisted and transformed.

"......"

They kicked off the ground and leaped. Lee Sungmin's hand instinctively went to his side for a spear that wasn't there. *It can't be helped,* he grumbled internally, raising both hands. He preferred a spear, but he was far from unskilled in hand-to-hand combat. The martial theories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a man who used no weapons, were etched into his mind.

Huge claws descended on him as he leaped into the air. To his eyes, their movements were agonizingly slow. In contrast, his hands were a blur. One hand shot under the descending claws, seizing a wrist. He twisted, breaking the bone, then snapped his waist to send his leg scything upward in a vicious arc.

"......"

He didn't need to look to know his foot had smashed the Lycanthrope's head. He wrenched the broken wrist again, wringing the entire forearm until the muscle twisted like a pretzel. He sensed an attack from the side. By the time violet electricity sparked, Lee Sungmin was gone, reappearing in the center of the airborne Lycanthropes. They hid their panic behind furious roars.

-VMMMM!

The inner arts and Demonic Energy enveloping his body swelled. He threw his hands wide, and it erupted.

KRA-KA-KOOM!

A torrent of current shot forward, piercing through the Lycanthropes' bodies.

"Kurg!"

They swallowed blood. In terms of pure physical toughness, they were the strongest of the inhumans, and they instantly began to regenerate their bleeding wounds as they pressed toward him. Between the gaps in their assault, crimson eyes flashed. The Vampires were using the Lycanthropes as shields, aiming for his blind spots.

Rao Xian's eyes met his.

*He's different.*

Rao Xian felt it with chilling certainty. After ten years under a Sealing, he had assumed Lee Sungmin would be weakened. He was wrong. The decade of imprisonment had not made him weaker. Instead, his body, once a rampaging Yokai, had completely stabilized without the influence of the Slaughter Predator.

SPLURT!

The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder tore through the Lycanthrope shields. Lee Sungmin closed the distance. Rao Xian gritted his teeth and thrust out a hand wreathed in crimson light, meeting Lee Sungmin's palm strike.

KABOOM!

The explosion engulfed him, smashing his body through the building's debris and sending him flying.

"Rao Xian!" Rebecca screamed. Jeniela's fifth vampire had been defeated so easily. Even without a moonlit night, Rao Xian's power was not something to be suppressed so casually.

The Lycanthropes that had fallen staggered back to their feet. They were critically injured but not dead. Rebecca and the other Vampires revealed their killing intent and charged.

[How foolish.] the Void Monarch grumbled.

Lee Sungmin agreed. His Demonic Eye flashed.

It did not take long.

The area was stained with the blood of Vampires and Lycanthropes. The Black Mages and Blood Alliance martial artists who had fainted early on were the lucky ones. Lee Sungmin brushed the blood from his body and approached the prisoners. The Vampires and Lycanthropes were not dead, but they could no longer regenerate their wounds. That was enough. The fact that Rao Xian was Jeniela's fifth vampire was what stayed his hand from killing them. He had no desire to needlessly provoke her.

"Hic... Hihic..."

Tang Ahee trembled as he approached. The martial prowess he had displayed in such a short time had horrified everyone. Was it possible for a person to move like that? Was that truly martial arts?

"Are you alright?"

A faint smell of urine wafted from where Tang Ahee sat collapsed on the ground. Lee Sungmin pretended not to notice and began to untie the prisoners' bonds.
