The Mercenary King's presence in Kron was for a simple reason. Eria Murim was currently split between the orthodox sects of the Murim Alliance and the unorthodox sects of the Blood Alliance, and war was brewing on the horizon.

The old Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, a coalition that had lasted ten years, had been a force that largely kept to its own territory. The Blood Alliance, however, was a different beast entirely. It had begun its aggressive expansion by recruiting Black Mages, and its provocations and brutality had grown more audacious with each passing day. A full-scale war felt not just possible, but imminent.

For mercenaries, war was a golden opportunity. For hundreds of years, Eria had been almost unnaturally peaceful. A proper war had almost never occurred in this vast world. There was only one nation, its nobles did not fight amongst themselves, and the lords of the cities never tried to invade one another. The only place where conflict regularly erupted was the murim, where territorial disputes between small and medium-sized factions provided minor battlefields for mercenaries. Other than that, their work was limited to monster subjugations.

There had been an incident in the past when Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, then the Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance, invaded the Murim Alliance. He had shattered the great Dog Beating Stick Formation, the pride of the Beggars' Gang, before defeating and kidnapping the Chairman, Black Dragon Knight. It was a shame and humiliation without parallel in the Murim Alliance's history, yet they could not declare war. They could not retaliate, for the power of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon was simply too immense.

However, that was the past. The Demon Emperor was now dead and the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance had collapsed with him. In its place, an outstanding martial artist known as the Blood Demon had suddenly appeared and forged the Blood Alliance. Just how powerful this new figure was, however, had never been properly demonstrated. To the Murim Alliance, the Blood Alliance under the Blood Demon seemed a far easier opponent than the old alliance under Yang Ilcheon.

Furthermore, the disposition of the current Murim Alliance Chairman played a significant part. The former leader of the Beggars' Gang, Martial Beggar, was a completely different man from Black Dragon Knight. Where Black Dragon Knight had been a puppet of Heaven Beyond Heaven, content to avoid true conflict, Martial Beggar was different. He was a man who detested the unorthodox sects, and the humiliation he had personally suffered at the hands of Yang Ilcheon had twisted his aversion into a burning hatred.

"It'll probably be difficult to get a favorable reaction," Lee Sungmin said as they passed through Kron's castle gates.

"Ten years ago, I came here with my Master, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. I watched him personally smash the Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation. I also saw him storm the Murim Alliance building and beat Black Dragon Knight to a pulp."

The Murim Alliance had tried its best to suppress the story, but with so many witnesses, the rumor had eventually spread.

"Should I go then?" Baek Sogo asked, tilting her head.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before shaking his head. "No. Let's go together. If we can't get a favorable reaction, we can just use force."

"Is there really a need to make an enemy of the Murim Alliance?"

"It depends on the situation. In the last ten years, Predator has united the unorthodox sects through the Blood Alliance. We don't know what might have happened with the Murim Alliance in that time." The true variable in this case was Heaven Beyond Heaven, which was precisely why he was going to meet Blade Supreme first, rather than stopping by the Murim Alliance. If a commotion broke out there, Blade Supreme would almost certainly use the chaos to escape.

Just in case, both Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo donned human skin masks. They intended to avoid any trouble until they found their target. He had offered a mask to Yana as well, but she had no need for one.

"Is that magic?"

"It's yokai magic."

Yana, standing beside him, had transformed into a woman with black hair and black eyes. Her luscious tail had vanished, and she now wore simple martial arts attire instead of her conspicuous hanbok. There was nothing left in her appearance to arouse suspicion.

Baek Sogo glanced at Lee Sungmin's long hair, which was tied back tightly. "Junior Disciple, you don't have any plans to cut your hair?" she asked.

"This is the first time I've ever grown it this long, so I'm just leaving it. It hasn't been particularly inconvenient."

"Still, I think I liked it better when it was short," Baek Sogo said with a faint smile.

At her words, Lee Sungmin blinked and looked at her, then stroked his tied-back hair. "In that case, I'll cut it next time."

"It's not bad now, either."

With the quiet conversation hanging between them, they made their way to the inn known as 'Eunru'. Blade Supreme, the Mercenary King Dmitri, and the mercenary group he led were renting out the entire establishment.

Lee Sungmin stood before the high-class inn and expanded his senses, feeling the presences within. The Dmitry Mercenary Group numbered three hundred strong. Most of its members were S-Rank or higher, with the lowest being A-Rank. Mercenaries below B-Rank were treated as little more than errand boys. A single mercenary group possessed enough military strength to face a major sect head-on, and with Dmitri, a high beastfolk of the Transcendent Realm, leading them, their power surpassed even that of the Nine Sects One Gang.

*He's here.*

He had wondered what he would do if Dmitri was absent. Lee Sungmin let out a small, humorless laugh and approached the gates of Eunru. A boisterous noise leaked out, reaching them even from the street. It seemed a drinking party had started in broad daylight. Whether their rank was low or high, the pleasures mercenaries enjoyed were all the same, differing only in the price of their liquor and their women.

Lee Sungmin slowly opened the gates and stepped inside without hesitation. A wide garden and the large mansions beyond it came into view. The mercenaries partying in the garden glanced at him, but quickly lost interest and returned to their drinks. They must have felt no need to be wary. Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo had perfectly completed Origin Reversal, so no hint of their martial prowess leaked out. The same was true for Yana, who could conceal her power within herself as much as she wished.

Unimpeded, he passed through the raucous garden party. After moving past several mansions, Lee Sungmin stopped at an annex that was even more luxurious than the main building. He could feel Dmitri's presence from within, along with a few others who were stronger than the mercenaries outside. It seemed the group's executives were using the annex with their leader.

"The smell," Yana muttered, wrinkling her nose. "It doesn't seem like we're about to see a pleasant sight."

Lee Sungmin agreed, but they could not afford to wait. Without making a sound, he leaped to the top floor of the annex. As he opened a closed window and slipped inside, his eyes met Dmitri's on the bed.

Dmitri, who had been entangled with two naked women, widened his eyes in shock.

"What… the hell!?" he roared, shooting to his feet. His scar-covered body was a thick armor of writhing muscles.

Seeing his dangling appendage, Baek Sogo cleared her throat and averted her gaze. Yana simply stared at Dmitri with undisguised contempt. Dmitri flinched under her gaze and backed away, covering his groin with one hand while reaching for the giant dao propped in a corner.

"Who are you!" he bellowed. His shout sent a commotion through the entire annex as the sounds of mercenaries rushing over echoed from below.

Lee Sungmin stared intently at Dmitri and lifted his foot.

TAK.

As his foot tapped the floor, a silent, intangible energy erupted from him, a wave that washed out in all directions. It ignored all physical obstacles, engulfing the entire mansion in an instant.

"Mysterious phenomena…!" Yana gasped in admiration.

Mysterious phenomena were the power of a Yokai. While a master of the Transcendent Realm could interfere with space, a great yokai, who had gained immense Demonic Energy from countless offerings of fear, could attune that energy to wield yokai magic. This was now the case for Lee Sungmin. The Dragon's Roar, which he had absorbed with his Black Heart and used subconsciously, had gained even greater power now that he was a complete Yokai. It was a power truly worthy of being called a mysterious phenomenon, one related to the strange, the valorous, the perverse, and the ghostly, something difficult to explain through reason alone. The soulmates that appeared only in stories were unfolding perfectly, a power he had learned to handle completely during his time in the Fairy Forest.

If he wanted, he could kill them with his presence alone. Those who had deeply mastered orthodox martial arts, possessed profound faith, or had outstanding willpower might have resisted, but against these disorderly mercenaries, he barely needed to exert himself. He could kill them, but he had no intention of committing a massacre.

"Gasp…!"

Dmitri's expression twisted as the crushing, intangible pressure bore down on him. He hurriedly drew upon his inner arts to resist. The women on the bed, however, could not. The light faded from their eyes as their heads drooped limply. The same fate met the mercenaries who were climbing the stairs and running down the hallways.

"Just who…?"

"Get dressed," Lee Sungmin said, removing the human skin mask from his face. "I'll give you that much time."

He had revealed himself so Dmitri would recognize him, but the Mercenary King clearly did not. Dmitri glared at him and cautiously began to pick up his clothes, his mind racing. *What the hell is this situation? Who the hell is this guy?* No matter how much he thought, he could not place the man before him. *The two women behind him… not worth worrying about. The problem is this guy…*

Dmitri acknowledged that Lee Sungmin was a master superior to himself. The man had effortlessly performed soulmates that should have been a monumental task even for someone at Dmitri's level in the Transcendent Realm. *Does such a master exist in the world today? And why would he be pulling this shit on me?*

"Well… let's at least hear who you are. Just what kind of person are you?"

"I'm the one asking the questions."

*Damn bastard,* Dmitri cursed inwardly at the ice-cold reply. *Should I surrender? There's no guarantee he won't kill me. I have no choice but to look for an opening and escape.*

As he finished putting on his armor, Dmitri steeled his resolve. Fortunately, the man seemed to be off his guard. *Isn't that always the case? When you know you're overwhelmingly stronger than your opponent. You look down on them, you let your guard down.* Dmitri was the Mercenary King, Blade Supreme, and mercenaries were far from fair fighters. If his opponent let his guard down, all the better. Even if he couldn't kill him, he could certainly create a chance to escape.

Dmitri gripped his beloved dao tightly. When his preparations were complete, Lee Sungmin lowered the spear that had been resting on his shoulder. It was no surprise that Dmitri was not trying to run. Though Lee Sungmin had never met the man before, he intuited that Dmitri was not a martial artist with the will to risk his life in a direct attack. In that case, his aim was obvious.

*A spear… fuck, this is a bad omen.* The title 'the Phantom Spear' drifted through a corner of his mind. The man who killed the monstrous Sword Saint, the Dark Supreme who licked the Martial God's asshole, and even the Fist Honored One. *Didn't they say the Spear King was his equal? Still, no way… there's no way this guy is the Phantom Spear.* The rumor that the Phantom Spear was alive had already spread throughout Eria, but Dmitri dismissed the possibility. Traveling from Germud to Ruvus in less than a month was an impossible feat.

Dmitri, who had been stealthily dragging his feet, suddenly kicked off the ground.

KWAANG!

With a vibration that shook the entire mansion, Dmitri's body shot forward. He closed the distance in an instant and swung the giant dao held in both hands. Lee Sungmin watched the attack with a calm expression. Amidst the sound of the blade tearing through the air, a 'click' was heard as small hidden weapons shot out from the man's wrist armor.

Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh and twisted his body slightly, allowing the projectiles to fly harmlessly past him. He extended his spear toward the giant dao falling in their wake. Unable to complete its swing, the great blade stopped futilely. Dmitri gasped and tightened his grip, but the dao, blocked by the spear, refused to budge. While Dmitri was putting all his strength into the two-handed strike, Lee Sungmin held his spear with only his left hand.

"You… son of a…!" Dmitri's face contorted.

A grating sound came from where the dao and spear shaft met as the space around Dmitri began to shimmer. He had no intention of waiting for that. Lee Sungmin's right hand, the one not holding the spear, shot forward. Terrified, Dmitri raised a Protective Qi Aura and tried to escape with his movement arts, but it was a futile struggle.

When Lee Sungmin's right hand touched Dmitri's chest, the breastplate did not break. Instead, a violent Demonic Energy seeped through it, tearing apart Dmitri's blood and qi and roiling his inner arts. Blood gushed from his nose and mouth.

"Keuk!"

It was overwhelming. Though they were both in the Transcendent Realm, an immense gap existed between them. Just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had once treated Black Dragon Knight like a child, Lee Sungmin could now do the same to Dmitri.

His legs trembling, Dmitri finally collapsed to his knees. He never even had a chance to activate the other hidden weapons and enchanted magic concealed throughout his armor. Not that it would have changed the outcome. Lee Sungmin approached the trembling man and grabbed his wrist.

"Tell me about Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"You… who the hell are you…"

"I told you I'm the one asking questions."

SQUEEZE.

Lee Sungmin's thumb pressed into the center of Dmitri's wrist. Dmitri's body trembled as if struck by lightning. The Demonic Energy seeping into him caused no pain, but he felt a distinct sense of wrongness, a blatant threat. *If I don't cooperate, I'll die here. Or I'll become a cripple. Either way, I'll feel a terrible pain I've never experienced before.*

"I… I understand."

"Swear that you'll tell the truth."

Dmitri was quick-witted. He knew that the man before him was far more terrifying than the Martial God, whose whereabouts were unknown.

After that, everything went smoothly. Dmitri answered every question. For the past ten years, the Martial God had been in seclusion, and Heaven Beyond Heaven was effectively dormant. Dmitri had not received any orders from the Spirit Medium, so he had been living faithfully as the Mercenary King, not as Blade Supreme.

"How are the Spirit Medium's orders delivered?"

"There's a… crystal ball."

"Hand it over."

Dmitri, who had started speaking formally at some point, obediently opened his Subspace Pocket and produced the crystal ball. Lee Sungmin took it and placed it in his own, intending to use it to locate the Spirit Medium.

"Heaven Beyond Heaven… uh… hasn't done anything for the last ten years. In fact, the only ones belonging to it right now are the Martial God, the Moon Queen, and myself…"

"But the Moon Queen should be dead."

"Pardon? What do you mean? The Moon Queen entered seclusion training at her residence…"

At those words, Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched. *The Moon Queen should be dead?* The statement was questionable, but he did not press the matter. He was planning to visit her residence after he was done here anyway. There was nothing more to learn from Dmitri.

As Lee Sungmin stood up, Dmitri swallowed hard and looked up at him. Lee Sungmin stared down at the man for a moment, then flicked his finger.

PWAK!

The fired Finger Bullet pierced Dmitri between the eyes. He collapsed without a sound, his life extinguished.

"Did you really have to kill him?" Baek Sogo asked.

"There was no need to keep him alive. He might have become an enemy someday."

"But… Junior Disciple. They may be deceived by the Spirit, but isn't Heaven Beyond Heaven's goal to stop The End? In that case, that man too…"

"The only ones acting to stop The End were the Martial God, the Moon Queen, and the Spirit Medium," Lee Sungmin cut in. "That bastard didn't even know what The End is. He lived only for his own gain. He wasn't righteous, and he wasn't good. He's not a person worthy of your sympathy, Senior Sister."

Baek Sogo just smiled bitterly at his words and offered no rebuttal. Lee Sungmin picked up the spear he had set down.

"It's noisy outside," he said. "It seems people have gathered because of the commotion from before."

"Should we sneak out?"

"There's no need for that."

With that, Lee Sungmin left the room.
