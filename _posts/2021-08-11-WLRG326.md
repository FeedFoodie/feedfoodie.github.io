It had been a gamble. He had no idea how much time had passed since he, in his spirit form, had pierced his own body with White Fang. At the moment of the act, he had not understood its meaning. Only after he had done it, after hearing the Slaughter Predator's despairing cry, did the significance of his actions crash down upon him.

It was a mental suicide. By turning the blade on himself, Lee Sungmin had managed to kill the Slaughter Predator that had risen from his subconscious, taking his own spirit along with it. This was only possible because several coincidences and conditions had aligned perfectly with the situation. Normally, the moment he awakened as the Slaughter Predator, his own consciousness should have naturally vanished, with the predator filling the void.

Still, Lee Sungmin's consciousness had not disappeared. It had remained in his Mental World as a spirit, and even White Fang, which should not have been there, was clutched in his hand. Then the Void Monarch had intervened, preventing the Slaughter Predator from taking the spear from him.

Such coincidences and conditions. No, he wondered, were they truly coincidences? Lee Sungmin could no longer bring himself to believe in such a word. He had lost count of how many times these so-called coincidences had manipulated him.

*I don't know what happened.*

Questions swarmed his mind. How much time had passed since his spirit had died and was now revived? And what of his body? That, in fact, was his biggest question and his deepest worry. While his spirit was dead, what had happened to his physical form? He remembered back in Urmuri, when he had first awakened as a Yokai, how his body had rampaged uncontrollably.

Reason had been lost, and only instinct had remained. In that state, he had fought against the inhumanity inside his own head. Thinking back on it now, it seemed that inhumanity had been a part of the Slaughter Predator. When he had finally suppressed it and regained control, it had sunk back into his subconscious with the words, 'See you next time'.

The only reason he had been able to suppress it back then was because The End had not yet begun. The End had started after his death in his past life, after he had obtained the Stone of Reincarnation.

*The fact that I'm alive means my body isn't dead.*

This couldn't possibly be the afterlife. So what on earth had happened? While his consciousness was absent, what had his body been doing? When the Slaughter Predator vanished and his own consciousness disappeared with it, what became of his physical self?

"Don't you know?"

"How would I know?" the Void Monarch grumbled. "This Elder tried to take control of your body while you were gone, just in case… but it was completely impossible."

"Still, it's certain you didn't die."

"How much time has passed since then?" Lee Sungmin asked, unable to gauge the passage of time himself. He was sure, however, that it had not been just a few days. Even in Denil's Mental World, it had taken him at least a few months to wake up after losing consciousness once.

"I don't know, but… hmm. I'd say a few years have passed, roughly?"

"…A few… years…?"

"That's the minimum estimate."

Good heavens. A hollow laugh escaped Lee Sungmin's lips, followed by a chill that ran down his spine. Anxiety and worry washed over him as he imagined what his uncontrolled body might have done while he was unconscious for several years.

THUD, THUD, THUD, THUD...

*What in the world is that sound?*

Lee Sungmin glanced toward the grating, dull noise as he expanded his consciousness.

WHOOSH!

His spirit form swelled dramatically. His consciousness, which had been under no one's control, instantly came under his command.

His closed eyes opened.

What his eyes now saw was not his mental world. He was in a pitch-black darkness where he could not see an inch ahead. It was not because of the darkness itself, but because his bangs had grown so long they completely covered his eyes. He tried to move, but the sharp clanking of metal echoed in his ears. His body, which he had returned to after several years, was tightly bound in thick, pure white chains.

"What is this now?" he muttered in disbelief. It was not just the chains. Talismans, densely covered with incomprehensible red letters, were plastered all over his body.

He tried to force his body to move. Though he felt a strong resistance, moving was not a major issue. Lee Sungmin ignored his restraints for a moment and observed his physical state. It was the body he had returned to after several years, but its condition was not as bad as he had feared. In fact, it felt much better than before. For some unknown reason, his Demonic Energy had decreased significantly, though that did not mean he had gotten weaker.

*The Sealing is completely gone.*

This was not a bad thing. The Sealing carved by Osla was completely shattered, and his body had become a full Yokai. However, the Slaughter Predator, which should have awakened with that transformation, had been completely annihilated. In other words, Lee Sungmin had become a complete Yokai, yet his mind had remained firmly human.

Because of this, the fighting spirit dissonance had vanished entirely. The reason he had rampaged when overusing Demonic Energy was because his body had been a clumsy mix of Yokai and human, not a complete whole, but not anymore. Now that he was a complete Yokai, there was no burden or risk in using Demonic Energy, and he could maintain his sanity perfectly.

[This actually turned out to be a gain for you,] the Void Monarch muttered inside his head.

He was right. The Reversal Magic that Kim Jonghyun had left behind had ultimately benefited him. Of course, if he had become a Yokai only to awaken as the Slaughter Predator, it could hardly be called a gain, but with the predator gone, the Reversal Magic had completely eliminated the chronic weaknesses and dangers he had possessed.

*But I haven't undergone Body Reformation.*

That would be too greedy. Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly. In a way, the fact that he had not undergone Body Reformation reassured him. He wanted to achieve that with his own power. It also meant there was still more power to be gained through it.

[Looking at this state, I can roughly guess what happened. It seems someone sealed you when you awakened as a Yokai and went on a rampage.]

*Who could it be?*

[This isn't magic, it's sorcery. I suspect it was done by the Grand Sorcerer who was traveling with you… but it's strange. Suppressing and sealing you with his power alone should have been close to impossible?]

Could Abel have helped? No, that was impossible. Lee Sungmin's expression turned bitter. Abel was dead. Hadn't the Slaughter Predator told him? Abel had scraped together the last of his lifespan to banish Kim Jonghyun from spacetime.

Abel.

Lee Sungmin called his name in a small voice. Until the very end, he had put the world before himself and had succeeded in completely erasing Kim Jonghyun, the calamity of The End, from this world.

THUD, THUD, THUD.

The sound did not stop. Lee Sungmin slowly took a breath and drew out his power.

RUUUUMBLE!

A sound like a thunderclap echoed from within his body. His inner arts and Demonic Energy surged in an instant, flowing through his meridians and mixing together. There was no rejection, no conflict. The two forces completely followed his will.

CRACKLE!

Purple electricity sparked from his body. It instantly vaporized the chains binding him and burned away the talismans.

[That was just the primary Sealing. The entirety of this Sealing is this space itself. Even for you, it won't be easy to break out… but.]

The Void Monarch trailed off. Lee Sungmin also understood why. In the darkness where he could not see an inch ahead, a small light appeared. Looking closely, he saw a thin crack around the light. With each thudding sound, the crack spread further, and the hole grew slightly larger.

[It seems someone is trying to get you out of here.]

That sound he had been hearing was the sound of someone trying to break his seal. Lee Sungmin slowly approached the hole. Even then, the thudding did not stop.

"Too slow."

He raised his hand, then suddenly realized there was no spear in it. He was belatedly flustered by this, but as the saying goes, if you do not have teeth, you chew with your gums. Lee Sungmin raised both his hands.

RUUUMBLE!

Purple electricity wrapped around his palms. That current, created by the mix of Demonic Energy and inner arts, surged forward with his twin palm strike and collided with the hole.

KABOOOM!

With an explosive sound, the crack grew even larger. Lee Sungmin continuously pushed out with his twin palms, making the hole bigger and bigger until it had expanded enough for a person to pass through.

BZZZZT!

His body was enveloped in purple electricity. He executed the movement arts of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and leaped out of the hole.

{sep}

"S-stop it!" Tang Ahee shouted in a tearful voice.

Dozens of the captured warriors from the Murim Alliance were already dead, sacrificed for Dark Magic. Only their souls had been extracted. Tang Ahee trembled as she looked at their limp bodies. There was no particular order to the deaths, or at least none that she could see. Each time the magic circle flashed, one of the prisoners would go limp and die without any discernible pattern.

That randomness made her even more terrified. The fear might have been less if she at least knew the order, but the thought of not knowing when her turn would come was terrifying enough to give her goosebumps.

"At least, at least give us time to write a will…" Tang Ahee's words trailed off.

Beside her, Jegal Young contemplated 'Sealing' and 'sacrifice' rather than the fear of death. Hwangbo Myeongun looked for a chance to break his bonds and escape. Moyong Chan secretly struggled to release his sealed acupoints, while the other prisoners slumped over and trembled.

"Oh."

The Black Mages overseeing the magic exclaimed. The Lycanthropes, who had been sitting nearby drinking as if they had no interest, turned their heads. The Vampires, who were watching the magic's progress beside the Black Mages, also widened their eyes.

Having emerged from the hole in space, Lee Sungmin slowly descended, drawing everyone's attention. As he drifted down from not too high in the air until his feet touched the ground, he turned his head to look around.

Ten years.

He did not recognize Moyong Chan, who had become a young man, but he barely recognized Tang Ahee, who had reached middle age. It was only thanks to her maintaining an appearance that defied her age.

*Tang Ahee? Why is she here?*

Who were those people next to her? And… he looked at the Lycanthropes who were drinking. He did not realize they were Lycanthropes, but he could read the interest and fighting spirit in their eyes. He saw the Black Mages, too.

*Why are there Vampires?*

THUD.

His feet touched the ground. First, he looked down at his body. He had no spear, but he did have his Magic Armor. Dented or torn parts had returned to normal thanks to its self-repair function, but things like blood and flesh were still stuck to it, emitting a rotten stench.

Lee Sungmin ignored it for now. His bangs were too long, making it hard to see. He roughly pushed them to the side with his hand. The hair on the sides and back had also grown so long it reached his waist. He sighed and swept his hair back with both hands.

*How much time has passed?*

He was not particularly hungry. His fingernails had grown so long they were starting to curl, which was not a pleasant sight. His teeth, too. No, were his teeth like this because he had transformed into a Yokai? He clicked his sharp teeth together as he looked around.

"…Could someone please tell me what's going on?"

The question was not aimed at anyone in particular. He just hoped someone, anyone, would answer. However, no one responded. He sighed and looked at Tang Ahee.

"It's been a while."

"Wh-wh-who?"

"Don't you recognize me? We've met a few times. I'm Lee Sungmin. The, uh, the Phantom Spear."

"The Phantom Spear!" Jegal Young exclaimed at his words.

Only then did she understand everything. The Sealing, the sacrifice. Right. To think that the one sealed here was the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin, who had supposedly gone mad ten years ago.

"Y-you… How are you here…?"

"That's what I'd like to ask you. Where is this place?"

"Germud," the one who answered was Rao Xian. He took a few steps forward, approaching Lee Sungmin. "Surely you haven't forgotten?"

"…Germud… Of course I haven't forgotten, but, who are you? No, more importantly," Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed. "In, in Germud. I mean… after Kim Jonghyun committed the massacre in Germud. How much time has passed?"

"Ten years," Rao Xian answered.

Lee Sungmin's jaw dropped.
