Wiji Hoyeon's eyes fluttered open, her lids heavy as stone. A terrible, constant pain gnawed at her from within, like a rat chewing on her insides, and she barely managed to swallow the groan that rose in her throat.

"A-are... are you okay?"

Rubia's voice trembled as she studied Wiji Hoyeon's complexion. She couldn't help but sound that way. Unlike Wiji Hoyeon, who had been deeply asleep, Rubia had witnessed everything.

The surroundings were in ruins.

The Dark Supreme had blocked sky-piercing, but Lee Sungmin had immediately followed it with Three Step Fatality. It was the most violent and powerful of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break's steps, a technique that twisted and rampaged through the flow of battle, making it difficult to block even when anticipated.

And it had worked. The Dark Supreme, an absolute master of the beastfolk who had reached the Transcendent Realm, now lay sprawled on the ground, covered in blood. He had been unable to counter Lee Sungmin's martial arts and had suffered a grievous wound.

"......"

Lee Sungmin doubled over and vomited a spray of red blood. His vision swam as if he were heavily drunk. After a series of violent heaves, he barely managed to lift his head. A profound weakness washed over his body, yet he desperately forced strength into the hand gripping his spear.

It wasn't over yet. Linking sky-piercing and Three Step Fatality had been a good move, one that had successfully threatened the Dark Supreme, but it had not been enough to kill him. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and took a step forward. He was using all his strength, having already cast supplementary magic like Haste and Strength at the start of the battle. He was even drawing on the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy. He didn't know how severe the wound he had inflicted was, but this was the first real opportunity he had managed to create in the entire fight.

"...Heok!"

As Lee Sungmin dragged his heavy feet toward the fallen man, the Dark Supreme shot to his feet with a strangled cry. He staggered badly, unable to support his own weight, and looked down at his body in disbelief before snapping his gaze back to Lee Sungmin.

"You bastard...!"

The Dark Supreme's face twisted in a mask of fury. The combination of sky-piercing and Three Step Fatality had dealt a significant blow, but it had not been a fatal one. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth again as the Dark Supreme gasped for breath, checking his own condition.

The final attack Lee Sungmin had unleashed was bizarre. There had been more than enough time to dodge, yet he couldn't. The strike had pierced his Protective Qi Aura and inflicted a critical internal injury.

*Was this his limit? Even if he used the power of the Demonic Spirit...!*

The Dark Supreme ground his teeth. Had he underestimated his opponent? No, he simply hadn't expected it. Something impossible had just happened. The Lee Sungmin he saw was by no means a master of the Transcendent Realm. Even with the added power of the Demonic Spirit, that gap should have been impossible to close, and until that moment, it had been. The Dark Supreme had consistently overwhelmed him even after he started using Demonic Energy.

And yet, this was the result. The Dark Supreme had been too faithful to his promise not to kill Lee Sungmin, while Lee Sungmin was fighting to kill him even if it meant their mutual destruction.

"GRIT!"

Lee Sungmin bit his lip and lunged forward. Moving his body was an agonizing effort, but he had to do something. He forced strength into his powerless hands. The Spear Technique he unleashed was pathetically weak compared to before, but the Dark Supreme was in no better condition. He gasped and drew a blade from the wrist armor on his right arm.

"......"

With a dull sound, the Dark Supreme's body was pushed back. His internal injuries were deep, his inner qi refusing to circulate properly. His face twisted in pain. Lee Sungmin's spear was also repelled. He fought back a wave of helplessness that threatened to collapse him and twisted his spear into a sideways swing. The Dark Supreme folded his fan and swung it like a club, barely managing to block the attack. Through the exchange, he realized the true danger of his situation.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon was still there. Though he was restrained, it wouldn't be long before he tore those restraints apart. It would be impossible for the deeply injured Dark Supreme to face both Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon at the same time.

His face contorting, the Dark Supreme reached into his robes and pulled out a cylinder the size of his palm. He aimed the end at the charging Lee Sungmin.

FWIP-FWIP-FWIP-FWIP!

Hundreds of ultra-thin needles fired simultaneously. They were too fast for the nearby Lee Sungmin to dodge, especially since he couldn't properly draw out his Protective Qi Aura. Thanks to his Magic Armor, the needles didn't pierce his body, but the force of the impact threw him back. Having created an opening, the Dark Supreme ignored his internal injuries and drew upon his inner qi. His outstretched arms moved in a circle, his hands blurring into dozens, then hundreds of afterimages, each holding a different hidden weapon.

"Flower Rain," the Dark Supreme spat out in a strained voice.

SH-SH-SH-SH-SH!

Hundreds of hidden weapons shot forth. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he unleashed Two Step Gentle Ripple, swinging his spear in a wide arc to block the assault. It was futile. Flower Rain was the ultimate technique of the Tang Clan's hidden weapon arts. Hundreds of weapons pierced Lee Sungmin's body. Even his Magic Armor, coated in Orichalcum, could not completely protect him this time.

Lee Sungmin's body flew backward and slammed into the ground. The Dark Supreme vomited a mouthful of blood, his shoulders trembling from the exertion.

Wiji Hoyeon was struggling to get up. In that moment, she hated the powerlessness of her cursed body more than anything. She trembled as she looked at the fallen Lee Sungmin. She opened her mouth, wanting to say something, but her voice wouldn't come out.

"You persistent bastard...!" the Dark Supreme spat.

Lee Sungmin, pierced by countless hidden weapons, was getting back up. He looked like he could drop dead at any moment, but he wasn't dead. He gasped for breath that reeked of blood and let go of the spear with his left hand. Then he began to pull out the weapons embedded in his body, prioritizing the ones that hindered his movement.

The Dark Supreme was at a loss for words. Even if he hadn't been able to unleash it perfectly, Flower Rain was still Flower Rain. How could he still move after being hit by that? In his confusion, the Dark Supreme ground his teeth.

"Stop it!" Wiji Hoyeon finally managed to scream. She shouted in a trembling voice at Lee Sungmin's blood-soaked back. "I'll... I'll go, okay? So just stop..."

"No," Lee Sungmin answered. His voice was so low it was almost gone. He didn't look back as he stumbled forward. "You don't have to do that for me. You... you don't want to go, do you?"

"But you...!"

"I'm fine."

He wasn't fine. The wounds he had sustained were fatal. Still, he had to say he was fine.

"I don't want to let you go."

The Dark Supreme's face twisted as he remained silent. He had never thought he would face a situation like this, nor that it would become so troublesome. However, he had to do what needed to be done. Taking Wiji Hoyeon was just that important.

[Damn it!] the Void Monarch cursed internally. He couldn't just let Lee Sungmin die, but there was nothing he could do. He had already tried several times to intervene and seize control of Lee Sungmin's body, just as he had when subduing the Crazy Heavenly Demon, but it was impossible. Lee Sungmin's mental barrier had become much stronger than before. When he was hit by Flower Rain, Lee Sungmin had lost consciousness for a moment. Normally, the Void Monarch would have been able to move his body for him, but this time, even that didn't work. Lee Sungmin's divine power had grown too strong in the past few months.

Lee Sungmin dragged his tattered, wounded body to block the Dark Supreme's path. The Dark Supreme trembled as he silently watched Lee Sungmin raise his spear once more.

"Must you really die..."

"It can't be helped."

Just as the Dark Supreme resolved to kill Lee Sungmin, the air began to CRACKLE! with black electricity. Seeing it, the Dark Supreme's eyes shot wide open. A figure materialized from within the crackling energy. The man's long hair was tied up neatly, and a half-mask covered one side of his face. He wore a large robe the color of fresh blood.

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman!" the Dark Supreme cried, his face turning pale.

At the cry, Lee Sungmin's own face couldn't help but harden. Only a few hours ago, the Chairman had spoken to him through Gwan Hu's body. Now, that same man had suddenly appeared right before his eyes.

"It's been a while, Tang Mugi."

"Don't call me by that name!"

"Don't you hate it a bit too much? It's a pretty good name, you know. Though this one's name is cooler." Behind his mask, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman wore a smiling expression. The Dark Supreme's shoulders trembled at his flippant tone. He knew the man before him was not his real body. He didn't know where the real Chairman was, but this was merely a clone.

"How about we stop here and you back off?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman suggested, standing with his hands clasped behind his back.

At those words, the Dark Supreme ground his teeth and spat. "Are you saying you'll interfere in the affairs of Heaven Beyond Heaven...?!"

"I guess it comes down to that? Well... then let's just say I'm interfering. I wasn't originally going to pay it any mind, you know, but seeing that kid act so desperately has melted this one's heart, which is usually as cold as ten-thousand-year-old snow."

"Nonsense!"

"I'm serious," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied. He looked intently at the Dark Supreme without unclasping his hands. "Even if this is just a clone, I can kill an injured man like you without much trouble. Care to test it?"

"Are you... threatening me right now...?!"

"Depending on how you hear it, it might sound like a threat."

"Do you think Heaven Beyond Heaven will forgive..."

"This Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon is wary of only three opponents. One is that damned Sword Immortal old man from Wudang, the Martial God at the top of your Heaven Beyond Heaven, and Predator's Slaughter Predator. I wonder if the Martial God will really make a move over this."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled, and the Dark Supreme couldn't answer. He hesitated before taking a step back. His silence was proof that he was reluctant and afraid to make an enemy of the Chairman.

"...Regarding this matter, I will definitely..."

"Farewell." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman cut him off and waved his hand. It wasn't a simple wave. Black electricity writhed in his palm. The Dark Supreme turned and instantly vanished from Lee Sungmin's sight.

"You handle the power of the Demonic Spirit quite well," the Chairman remarked, watching the Dark Supreme's retreating back for a moment before glancing at Lee Sungmin.

"...I could have killed him."

"I know," the Chairman chuckled. "If you had pushed it further, you might have been able to kill the Dark Supreme, and in less than a week, you would have been killed by Heaven Beyond Heaven, and the Little Heavenly Demon would have been taken by them. That's what it means to kill one of the Six Honored Ones, but if you let him go here, Heaven Beyond Heaven will remain silent for a while. Why? Because I intervened. Several of the Six Honored Ones are in seclusion, and the Martial God of the Outer Heavens, who can threaten me, is also in seclusion. Among the active ones, there's no one with enough guts to pick a fight with me."

After saying that, the Chairman turned his gaze toward Wiji Hoyeon. She shivered slightly under his stare.

"If you don't want to go to Heaven Beyond Heaven, come to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance," he began. "I'll at least offer you protection. The choice is yours, of course, but if you, weakened by a curse, travel with that kid, you'll only hold him back."

"Wait, what are you saying...!"

"This one saved your lives," the Chairman snapped, glancing at Lee Sungmin. "If you have any manners, don't interrupt while this one is talking. To end the Fist Honored One's curse, you must kill the Fist Honored One, but you don't know where he is. If you go to the South, you might be able to find his location. The sorcerers over there are quite talented."

After muttering that, he turned his gaze back to Wiji Hoyeon. "I'll promise you this. If you come to this one, this one will never hand you over to Heaven Beyond Heaven. Nor will I restrain you."

Wiji Hoyeon remained silent. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman smirked as he looked at her downcast eyes.

"You don't have to answer right away. In four days, some people sent by this one will come find you. If you want to come, come then. If not, just send them back."

"Oh, and," the Chairman added, looking at Lee Sungmin. "You'd better be careful of the Murim Alliance Chairman."

"...What...?"

"And you. Your tone is insolent. The next time you speak so informally in front of this one, I'll give you a spanking."

With that final threat, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman disappeared.
