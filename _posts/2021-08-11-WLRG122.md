Lee Sungmin could not fathom the ecstasy radiating from Kim Jonghyun, so he held his tongue. The conversation died in the ensuing silence, and after a moment, Kim Jonghyun broke the quiet to show Lee Sungmin and Rubia to a room on the second floor.

"I won't interfere in your lives," he said, pulling on his robe. "And I'd appreciate it if you didn't interfere in mine."

*Where are you going?* The question rose in Lee Sungmin's throat, but he swallowed it back. He had, after all, just been told not to interfere.

"I was invited to a party today," Kim Jonghyun offered anyway, though Lee Sungmin had not asked.

"It's a party for Black Mages. I'd invite you along, but... you're not a Black Mage, are you, Lee Sungmin?" Kim Jonghyun laughed, brushing a hand over his robe. "I could teach you some basic Dark Magic if you'd like, though. Interested?"

"I'm fine."

"A shame." Kim Jonghyun shrugged at the swift refusal. "Dark Magic is a fascinating field of study... If you ever change your mind, let me know."

With those words, he left the house. Lee Sungmin went upstairs with Rubia to inspect the room Kim Jonghyun had offered them.

[He hasn't played any tricks.]

"I've scanned it with magic, but there's nothing suspicious. The whole house is like that. Usually, a mage's residence would have at least one or two barrier spells..." Rubia muttered, her voice laced with suspicion.

Lee Sungmin was lost in thought for a moment before he nodded. "It feels a bit wrong to leave without saying anything, but let's just find somewhere else to stay."

"...Alright."

Kim Jonghyun had always been a suspicious character. He had never directly harmed Lee Sungmin before, but in a city like this, their acquaintance was not enough to warrant unconditional trust. Lee Sungmin had no idea what the man was thinking. Perhaps it was simple goodwill that had led Kim Jonghyun to open his home to them.

However, the 'what if' was a constant shadow in any situation, and Lee Sungmin had no desire to be swayed by it. It was better to sever the possibility from the start. If he stayed in this house and got caught up in something, he would fall victim to an unimaginable scenario involving Kim Jonghyun.

So, he would leave.

His plan was to leave not just the house, but the Central District entirely. From what he had heard, Jeniela held not just the Central District but all of Trabia in the palm of her hand. If that was true, then even if he left this district, he could not escape her eyes as long as he remained in the city.

*What the Holy Spirit mentioned was the North. I don't have to think of it as just Trabia. The North...*

Lee Sungmin's gaze drifted northward. Trabia was a giant city at the northernmost point, but was this truly the end of the North? No, it could not be. *Maybe I should go a bit further north.* He turned and headed for the northern gate.

"Where are we going?"

"To the North."

"...You're not planning on leaving the city, are you?" Rubia asked, her voice anxious.

Lee Sungmin offered no answer. Rubia had been with him long enough to know well what his silence meant in response to such a question. It meant 'yes'.

"A bed... a bath..." she lamented, her ears drooping.

"We had a delicious meal," Lee Sungmin replied indifferently. "Be satisfied with that."

*

*Why am I here?*

The man had no idea. For some time now, his memories had been fractured, and whenever he came to his senses, he could never recall what had happened during the gaps. One thing, however, was certain. Whenever he regained his senses and pieced his memories together, he was always covered in blood.

*It happened again. Again... Where is this place...*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae, pushed himself up with a self-deprecating laugh.

This was different. He had come to his senses, yet he was not covered in blood. Ever since falling into qi deviation, he had periodically lost his memory and reason, each time returning to himself soaked in gore. His martial arts were so extraordinary that while the sight disgusted him, he never felt any pain. The blood was always someone else's.

*I can't guess how long I was out. Snow... winter... where am I?*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon groaned, clutching his dazed head. A vast snowfield stretched out before him. The wind was fierce, but no snow was falling. In the distance, he saw a huge mountain, and when he turned his head, the walls of a city stood not far away.

*The North... a city... Could this be Trabia?*

*No way. Did I really come this far?* Just in case, the Crazy Heavenly Demon sat down and performed a light qi circulation.

"This is crazy..."

He had a good idea of what had happened. *Did I really use my movement arts nonstop to get to this distant city, but for what purpose?* His eyebrow twitched. Until now, whenever he lost his sanity to qi deviation, he had carried out indiscriminate massacres. He was used to such slaughter, but this was a first.

Though he could not remember, the Crazy Heavenly Demon was a martial artist. He quickly checked his body, using qi circulation to search for traces of martial arts used upon him. There were no signs of battle. The only art he had used was for movement. *Why on earth did my other self run all the way here without rest?*

KWAANG.

His body flinched at the sound echoing from the distance. He slowly got up as the sounds continued in succession. A fight was happening nearby. He did not know what was going on, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon headed toward the noise without hesitation.

*First, I should ask where this place is.*

He suspected it was Trabia, but he needed to be sure.

Aine attacked him. Lee Sungmin met the five converging tentacles with a forward thrust of his spear. KWAANG! The explosive impact sent Aine's tentacles recoiling, but Lee Sungmin had not escaped unscathed. The enhanced qi coating his spear flickered and thinned at the tip.

The attack had come not long after he had left the city, heading north. The one who leaped from the darkness was Aine, the same creature he had encountered several years ago and had only managed to defeat after learning to use enhanced qi.

*Should I call this lucky?*

Lee Sungmin ignored the tingling in his palm and swung his spear wide. With a heavy thud, Aine's tentacles were knocked back once more. Her attacks were probing. She was not fighting aggressively, but merely swinging her tentacles to gauge his response.

[What the hell is with that kid...?! What on earth did you do to earn a grudge from a kid like that?!]

Rubia, now a sphere of light hiding in Lee Sungmin's arms, screamed inside his head. Lee Sungmin ignored her cry and took a few steps back, but Aine's tentacles writhed and lunged again. He immediately unleashed Homing Chain Lightning to intercept them.

"Where is Preskan?" he demanded, watching the tentacle fragments scatter.

Aine stared at his face with her yellow eyes before opening her mouth. "In the city."

Preskan was under the Vampire Queen Jeniela's watch. She had not forced any particular actions on him since he joined Predator, but Preskan could not help but feel self-conscious about leaving the city while under her gaze.

That was why he had sent Aine instead of coming himself. It did not mean he was not observing the battlefield. A few birds hovering high in the air were watching from above.

*This is actually for the better.*

*Lucky.* Lee Sungmin decided to see it that way. He had been hoping for a reunion with Preskan himself; this just saved him the trouble of searching. If he could subdue Aine here, he could draw Preskan out. He could already feel the gazes of the familiars circling in the sky. He did not know why the man had not come himself. Perhaps he was plotting something.

Wary of that possibility, Lee Sungmin deployed his Magic Armor. The armor, which had only covered his upper body, expanded in a flash to envelop him completely. He gripped the spear with both hands and settled into his stance.

Five years ago, when Aine had first attacked him, he would have died right then and there if not for Scarlet's help. He had been a peak master at the time, but he was lacking in many ways, riddled with insecurities. Only five years had passed since then, but for Lee Sungmin, the time he had accumulated felt like much more.

Aine stopped her probing. Now that Lee Sungmin was getting serious, she realized her playful tests were meaningless. She retracted the long tentacles back into her body.

[What a nasty bitch.]

Lee Sungmin agreed with the Void Monarch's mutterings. The tentacles that shot from Aine's body regenerated endlessly, no matter if they were burst or severed. Aine narrowed her eyes, glaring at him. Her hands became coated in a black carapace, her fingers sharpening into five deadly blades. With a sickening CRACK, black stalks erupted from her back, forming a pair of malformed wings while the rest writhed like limp tentacles.

Aine did not unconditionally obey her father, Preskan. She had her own personal feelings about Lee Sungmin.

*It was the first time.*

The first time she had felt such real pain. Aine licked her lips, remembering the moment her body was pierced by an attack coated in enhanced qi. The strange kinship she had felt five years ago was still there. Her father, Preskan, had ordered her not to kill Lee Sungmin. He had told her to bring him back alive, even if he was missing a few limbs.

Aine had no intention of following that order. From the moment they met, she had felt an intense impulse. She had felt it five years ago, too, but back then, she did not know what it was.

Now, however, she knew. It was appetite. She was not hungry, yet she wanted to eat him. An urge so powerful she could not stand it. If possible, she would vomit everything she had ever eaten just to make room for him. Her mouth watered. It was not just his heart she craved.

*Where should I start eating?*

*Should I start from his toes and work my way up?*

Aine swallowed hard as she imagined it. At the same time, her feet kicked off the ground. She shot toward Lee Sungmin at a speed that defied her size and logic.

[Those are the eyes of someone looking at prey.]

The Void Monarch muttered in a cheerful voice. Lee Sungmin could also feel the raw appetite writhing in Aine's eyes. BAAANG! Aine launched herself from the ground, the snow beneath her feet exploding upward. Piercing through the flurrying snow, she brought her large claws down toward him.

Lee Sungmin stepped forward without hesitation, seemingly walking straight into the attack, but the figure was an illusion, a flicker of teleportation. Using Traceless Step, he appeared at Aine's side. Aine, still airborne, spread her wings and twisted her body with impossible speed. One tentacle pierced the afterimage he had left behind, while another whipped around to check him as he approached from the side.

Lee Sungmin drew a circle with his spear, creating a raging torrent of force that tore the incoming tentacles to shreds. Aine's claws, however, were unharmed. She extended them, aiming to pierce his chest, but Lee Sungmin kicked off the snowfield to retreat, pulling his spear back.

*Her reaction is fast.*

As he activated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, a Protective Qi Aura surged around him. The Orichalcum-coated Magic Armor generated a protective aura that exceeded the consumption of his inner qi, and the same was true for the spear he held. An intense, flame-like enhanced qi erupted to cover the entire spear before it slowly subsided, condensing into a deadly point.

"I know what that is," Aine muttered, her eyes fixed on the condensed energy. A Protective Qi Aura flared to life around her own body.

"I can do that too," she declared with a bright, predatory smile.
