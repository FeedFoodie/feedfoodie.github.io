Theos, whose expression had been troubled, raised a single eyebrow. He glanced back with a look of surprise before hesitating for a moment. Then he turned to Lee Sungmin and Abel and bowed his head deeply.

"...The Saint... she wishes to apologize to you directly."

"Without even showing her face?" Abel asked.

"I apologize."

"Which carriage is it?" Abel's attitude softened slightly at the offer.

Though Theos wore a look of dismay, he led Lee Sungmin and Abel toward one of the carriages under the watchful escort of the Holy Knights. It was a white carriage, outwardly no different from the others, but as they drew near, the air around the Holy Knights crackled with tension. They stiffened, their hands dropping toward their weapons.

Theos shot a warning glance at them.

"...Just in case..." he began.

"Don't worry," Abel cut him off. "The kind of idiotic things you're imagining won't happen. I may be a cranky old mage, but I'm not an unreasonable or thoughtless person. Would I, the Master of the Mages Guild, really cause a scene with the Church's Saint?"

"That... your choice of words is a bit..."

"Should I just open it and go in?" Abel sneered.

Theos let out a deep sigh and opened the carriage door himself. Standing behind Abel, Lee Sungmin peered inside.

For a moment, he doubted his own eyes.

Abel felt the same. He blinked slowly, his arms uncrossing from where they had been tucked into his sleeves. He turned his head to look back at Theos, who offered a bitter smile, as if he had expected this very reaction.

"What kind of joke is this..." Abel started.

"It's not a joke," Theos said, shaking his head.

[...Hm.]

It seemed even the Void Monarch had not anticipated such a situation.

In the end, Lee Sungmin and Abel climbed into the carriage, taking seats opposite the Saint of the Church. Lee Sungmin had never heard anything about the Saint, not even when searching the memories of his past life. During the three days he had traveled with Abel, however, he had listened to the stories the old mage knew.

Her existence was certain, but little else was known. Not her age, nor her gender. Few among the Church's many followers, Junior Disciples, and even Holy Knights knew the Saint's true identity. It was said, however, that the holy power she possessed far surpassed that of the Church's highest Priests.

He had not been expecting an old high Priest who had dedicated his entire life to faith, but he certainly had not been expecting a young girl in a white nun's habit either.

"My name is Teresa," the girl said, her voice clear. She pushed back the hood of her habit, which had been pulled down low.

Even Abel was at a loss for words, having never imagined the Church's Saint would be such a young girl. Amidst the uncomfortable silence, Teresa looked between Abel and Lee Sungmin with eyes full of curiosity.

"This is the first time I've met the Mages Guild Master. You're known for being a person of few rumors. Right?"

"Uh... you... no, you... what should I call you?" Abel stammered.

"You can just call me Teresa. The Priests and Holy Knights treat me with difficulty, but you're not part of the Church, mister."

"Mister...?"

"Or would you prefer I call you oppa?" she asked, tilting her head. The question only deepened Abel's bewilderment. He was not used to conversing with this kind of person.

"Abel... I think... you can call me Abel-nim," he mumbled.

Teresa beamed at his reply, then shifted her gaze to Lee Sungmin. She giggled when she saw the Ghost Spirit face mask he wore.

"That's a scary mask. Why are you wearing something like that?"

"I have my reasons."

"I know who you are," Teresa declared, puffing out her chest. It was barely developed, but the motion made it stick out a tiny bit. "You wear a mask and use a spear. There's only one famous person with those features. You're the Phantom Spear, right?"

"......"

*Correct*, Lee Sungmin answered without hiding it. Teresa muttered the word in a small voice, seeming quite proud of herself for getting it right.

"You and we have had a strange connection since the North. No, should I say it's a connection through Kim Jonghyun? You participated in the Northern Pacification Battle too, didn't you?"

"There was some friction with the Church's subjugation force at that time."

"I'll apologize for that," she said. "I can't say I represent the entire Church, but..."

"I don't hold it against you," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking his head. "The situation couldn't be helped, and I was lacking in my explanation as well."

"Still, I'm grateful to you. Back then... thanks to you, the lives of the Holy Knights who participated in the subjugation force were saved. Though many people died..." *It couldn't be helped*, Teresa murmured. The subjugation in the North had ended in failure, and the forces of each faction involved had suffered great losses. The Church's own subjugation force had sustained heavy casualties, with one of the high Priests dying alongside other high-ranking Priests and Holy Knights.

"I heard what was said outside the carriage, Abel-nim," Teresa continued. "We share the same purpose as you. We are on our way to subjugate the Black Mage Kim Jonghyun for his terrible atrocities."

"Aren't your forces too small?" It was Lee Sungmin who asked the question. "I don't know how much you grasp of Kim Jonghyun's true power, but he is no mere Black Mage. He is a being close to a Demon Lord, and the lord of the Death Knights and hundreds of Death Knights follow him."

"Kim Jonghyun has made the city he first attacked his base and isn't moving," Abel added. "By the time we reach the city, Germud will have turned into a living hell. Beyond the city walls, tens of thousands who couldn't escape and died will be wandering as Undead."

"I suppose so," Teresa nodded. A shadow passed over her face, as if she were imagining the horrific scenes being described. Still, she answered without hesitation. "Even so, we can't just leave Kim Jonghyun alone. The more time passes, the more damage he will cause."

"Is that the will of God?" Lee Sungmin asked, gazing at her. He was reminded of the Moon Queen, who had claimed it was the will of the Spirit when she joined the Martial God to attack the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Perhaps the god the Church served was something like a Spirit. It was a question filled with such doubt, but Teresa only widened her eyes as if the question was strange.

"No. This isn't God's will. It's the will of the Church. We... don't follow God's will. Do you understand the group known as the Church?"

"To some extent."

"The Priests and Holy Knights of the Church are broadly divided into two groups," she explained. "One is Otherworlders like me. Beings who lived in another world but were summoned to Eria. When we were summoned, our spiritual connection to the gods we originally served was severed. Thanks to that, we can't hear the voices of our gods. Only the holy power we originally possessed became our own."

The other group, she continued, consisted of Priests and Holy Knights who served the gods existing in Eria. The Junior Disciples who managed the entry of ascetics at the foot of Mount Mushii belonged to that category, though not all Junior Disciples of all gods belonged to the Church.

"The gods of this world also don't force any particular actions upon us. So... to put it simply, the group called the Church can be described as polytheistic. Most of us serve different gods. Therefore, even if someone claims to have heard a god's voice, the Church doesn't act on that as the will of the group."

"In that case, are you saying this subjugation is the will of the Church alone, not the will of a god?"

"If I had to put it that way, yes. Kim Jonghyun has killed too many people. Those who died by his hand cannot leave this world and are wandering as Undead. Someone has to stop him."

Teresa's words were universally right. In the eyes of anyone in this world, Kim Jonghyun was a villain committing horrific acts.

A villain.

The thought of him made Lee Sungmin instinctively think of Baek Sogo. Perhaps she was also on her way here. Kim Jonghyun had committed acts that anyone would call evil. He had already killed thousands in the North, and in Germud, he had led Death Knights to annihilate an entire city. If he was not stopped, he would continue to move north and kill tens, even hundreds of thousands more.

"What is your position in the Church?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Teresa blinked at his question. After tilting her head in thought for a moment, she answered. "It's a little awkward to say it myself, but I'm the only one in the Church who is called a Saint."

"Do you know about The End?" Lee Sungmin pressed.

Abel glanced sharply at him. He took out Gries and held it in his hand, ready to erect a barrier if the conversation summoned the Apostle of the End. The question hung in the tense air, but Teresa just widened her eyes and tilted her head.

"What's that?"

Lee Sungmin glanced at Abel. He could not tell if her answer was genuine or a lie. Abel's eyes gleamed as he stared at her. Teresa was not so foolish as to miss the suspicion directed at her. Instead, she raised her eyes to meet theirs.

"I'm not shameless enough to lie about something I don't even know."

[Do you think the Church is unaware of The End?] Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission to Abel.

Abel stroked his chin, lost in thought. [Knowledge of The End is limited. My brother and I learned of it by sacrificing our lifespan to Gries... The Vampire Queen learned of it through her All-Seeing Eye, and the Martial God learned of it through direct contact with a Spirit.]

[Did Envirus also sacrifice his lifespan to Gries?]

[Yes. Though my brother never used Gries again after that,] Abel grumbled.

Teresa truly seemed to have no knowledge of The End. It was too early, however, to assume that the entire Church was ignorant just because she was.

[It doesn't really matter if she, the Saint, doesn't know about The End. She and the Church's forces are undoubtedly on their way to stop Kim Jonghyun.]

"The Phantom Spear," Teresa said, unaware of their private conversation. Her expression was nonchalant. "You're strange."

"In what way?"

"I can feel so many things from you. The word chaos... in all my life, I don't think I've ever met anyone who fits that word as well as you do."

"I have my reasons."

"Don't you need help?" Teresa asked with a smile.

Lee Sungmin did not answer immediately. He looked into her eyes, trying to grasp the true meaning of her words. They sparkled and shone as if they held countless stars. At least, he could find no hidden malice in them.

"I can help you."

"In what way?" Lee Sungmin asked with a small laugh.

In response, Teresa raised her hand and slowly extended it toward him. "By purifying it."

"...Purifying?"

"The things mixed up inside your body. If you'll allow it, I can do it for you."

"What do you want from me?"

"No, no, no, I'm not saying this because I want something. I'm saying it because you look troubled, the Phantom Spear, and because it's something I can do."

"What kind of help can you specifically provide? Purification? That's too vague a term."

"It's about completely purifying the malevolence dwelling in your body and the power that doesn't harmonize with what you originally have." For example. Teresa caressed the back of Lee Sungmin's hand. "Like the pitiful soul dwelling in your body, unable to ascend. Or what's stuck in your chest... what is this? A heart? This and... and..."

Poison.

After pondering the word for a moment, she added that. "The poison spreading throughout your entire body. It seems to be dormant for now, but if you keep leaving it alone, it will definitely kill you."

[She seems to have grasped it properly.]

The Void Monarch let out a laugh in his head. Hearing it, Lee Sungmin looked directly into Teresa's eyes.

"...By purification, you mean you'll completely remove them from my body?"

"Yes? Of course."

"Then, no thank you."

Lee Sungmin pushed away the fingertips that were caressing the back of his hand.
