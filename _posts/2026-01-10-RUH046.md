The situation was bizarre.

A stern-looking man glared at me with killing intent, as if I were his mortal enemy. Behind him, martial artists stood frozen, just as they were about to attack.

Was he someone with a grudge against Ma Cheonso? Then I could only nod in understanding. Ma Cheonso was certainly the type to make such enemies.

However, the man’s next words filled me with new doubts.

"How dare you... Did you think you could treat my sister like this and still call yourself a martial artist?!"

I understood his fuming anger. The key to this problem was the word ‘sister’.

What had I done to his sister? Could it be... that Ma Cheonso committed some unimaginable act?

Before entering the Heavenly Demon Cave, when he was not even sixteen? My mind went completely blank.

"How dare you neglect her like this, pretending she doesn't exist? How is this any different from declaring yourself an enemy of our Palace of Blades?"

"Hey, you bastard! So what if your sister is His Reverence's fiancée? Huh? His lust for women is just a sign of his great stamina! Get it? Do you?! How dare a man with less worth than a mole's dick try to comprehend the will of a giant?!"

Nonsense poured from Yayul Tak's mouth, but only one word stuck in my mind.

I must have misheard.

I _had_ to have misheard.

"I... had a fiancée?"

"_Are you seriously asking that right now?!_"

I staggered slighty at his roar. A fiancée? Who? Me?

Sure, I was once engaged. To a woman of the Namgung Clan.

But that was as Yang Jagak!

To think that Ma Cheonso was betrothed! Something utterly unimaginable had happened. Even if this was the Demonic Cult, a place the world reviled— even if he was its Heir—

Who in their right mind would offer their daughter to this man, the sleazy scoundrel known as the Little Lecher?

And yet, such a person existed!

"How could you do this? Even as the heir to the Cult, you cannot act this way. Forgetting my sister, your betrothed, entirely and visiting the Palace of Bliss, how is that reasonable?!"

Even though he boomed with righteous anger, I had no face to raise my head. My body, which had more than Four Gapja worth of qi, staggered again.

My ashen-white face grew even paler.

"Your Reverence, that's enough. You don't need to listen to a man who doesn't have a speck of courtesy!"

For the love of—

"Dan Jiho, you must think the Palace of Minor Light is a joke, don't you? Just you wait. Your Reverence! Call for Uncle Blood Asura. Show that mole bastard the authority of a husband!"

—please just shut the fuck up???

"Blood Asura. Get him out of my sight."

"...As you command."

"Woohoo! As expected! Your Reverence!"

I shot Yayul Tak a venomous glare, and Blood Asura flew over and clamped a hand over his mouth.

"Mmph? Mmph?"

_FWOOSH!_

Then they vanished far over the wall.

I took a heavy step forward, approached them, and spoke.

"What is her name?"

He tilted his head, his expression full of bewilderment.

So I asked again.

"My betrothed's... name."

"Dan Yehwa. Have you already forgotten my sister's name? How could you...!"

The man erupted in indignation once more, but I could not hear him.

They say marriage is the Grand Master of human relations. I'd once planned to someday visit the Namgung Clan and meet her, but the owner of this body also had a fiancée.

"Dan Yehwa..."

I repeated the name and looked up to the sky. The dazzling sunlight seemed to tell me to avert my sight, like a fleeting desire to turn away from it all.

However…

"...I will go to the Palace of Blades."

Not once, but twice.

One cannot simply abandon a betrothed woman without a single word. I must go in person, express my regrets, and set things right.

Thus was my next destination decided.

To the Palace of Blades.

{sep}

"Nanny! Nannyyyyy!"

Dan Yehwa, who had vanished for some time, reappeared at the Palace of Blades. She dashed into her room, ostentatiously calling for her Nanny in a loud voice.

Her Nanny, who was tidying clothes, sighed deeply and entered the room. The Nanny had been so worried when the grown young lady disappeared without a word! This time, she was determined to give her a firm talking-to.

"Where have you been! Do you have any idea how worried I was?"

"Sorry, sorry."

When Dan Yehwa smiled affectionately and hugged her arm, the Nanny sighed once more. Her accumulated worries melted away.

"From now on, please tell me before you go."

"I know. So, about that. I'm leaving."

"Where to now! You just got back, are you really planning to cut your ties with the Palace Lord?"

"What ties? He's ruining his daughter's life."

"Oh my, Young Lady. Please don't say such things. It startles my heart so much I can't sleep at night."

"Really? Because of me?"

Dan Yehwa looked at her with worried eyes, like a puppy with its tail tucked.

At this, the Nanny let out a hollow laugh and said, "It's because I'm worried, of course. The two of you have reached the right age, you should be getting married soon..."

"Then you don't have to worry. I found him…"

Dan Yehwa beamed again and continued.

"…my man."

"Pardon? Are you finally starting to see the Young Cult Leader as a man?"

"Are you nuts? Of course not. Why would I marry a guy who thinks of me as a maid? I can just break the engagement... Mmph."

The Nanny finally clamped a hand over Dan Yehwa's mouth and spoke sharply in a hushed voice.

"Young Lady, please don't say such dreadful things. If someone were to hear you..."

Though she could feel the Nanny's earnest sincerity in every word, Dan Yehwa shook her head free and shrieked back.

"So what if they hear! I heard he was having a grand time at the Palace of Bliss just the other day! Am I not allowed to have fun? I can find a man too! Maybe I'll spend the night with him!"

She was being utterly unreasonable. As Dan Yehwa screamed like the world was ending, the Nanny collapsed to the floor.

How on earth had it come to this? She had thought she would have no regrets if she could just see the young lady marry, have children, and live a happy life.

But now a scandalous new folktale was about to be written.

"Who on earth is this about? Young Lady, until now, no matter how much you disliked the Young Cult Leader, you never gave any other man a second glance."

The Nanny was also displeased with the Young Cult Leader. However, what could be done? The Young Lady had never cared for any other man. That was why she had thought there was no other match for her but the Young Cult Leader, who at least had his looks.

But this time, as if she had truly found the one, Dan Yehwa bounced like a kitten and said, "The Star of Black Flames."

The Star of Black Flames. An unexpected alias popped out of her mouth.

"Pardon? What?"

"Don't be shocked, just listen. He's a newcomer who wears a black cloak, and do you know who he defeated? Khan Esen of the Northern Plains, one of the Five Emperors of the Outer Regions…!"

As Dan Yehwa spoke, her eyes went hazy as she drifted into a daydream.

The Nanny remembered this look clearly. It was the same one she had when she first saw the Young Cult Leader.

"But that's not all. After he defeated Esen, he saved the Emperor Emeritus Yingzong _and_ our Shining Light Cult's escorts! He accomplished what both the Palace of Martial Blood and the Palace of Merchants had given up on!"

From the way she spoke, they might as well be married already. Her bouncing around with joy, as if those were her own accomplishments, was truly adorable and made one want to pat her head, but was this making any sense? It was utter nonsense.

"Young Lady. Listen to me carefully. Your betrothed is the Young Cult Leader and only him. If he finds out you've changed your mind because of another man, you'll truly be cast out."

"I don't care. Besides, Daddy would prefer the Star of Black Flames over the incompetent Young Cult Leader anyway. You know he's always preferred strong men."

It was maddening.

But strangely, it was persuasive.

The Nanny thought that the Lord she knew, despite the arrangement with His Eminence the Cult Leader, would prefer a man on par with the Five Emperors of the Outer Regions over a lecher who frequented the Palace of Bliss...

_Oh my, what am I thinking._

"No. Absolutely not."

"Nanny..."

Dan Yehwa pouted with teary round eyes.

The Nanny steeled her weakening heart, shook her head, and said firmly, "Pretending to cry won't work. For a man you've never even seen... Don't go anywhere, just stay right here. His Reverence will be arriving soon."

Predictably, Dan Yehwa raised her fine eyebrows and shouted without a trace of tears.

"What?! Why is he coming!"

"Why is he coming? To greet you, of course, Young Lady. So stop thinking about the Star of Black Fire or whatever he's called! Understood? Ah, it seems he's just arrived."

It grew noisy outside, and the Nanny turned her head toward the window. Dan Yehwa followed suit, her eyes filled with despair.

And then—

"_His Reverence the Young Cult Leader has arrived!_"

—a thunderbolt struck the Palace of Blades.

{sep}

The Palace of Blades.

Among the Seven Hallowed Palaces, it and the Palace of Poison were the most neutral, detached from political strife.

It was a place in which all those who joined were given the Dan surname and recognized as family; the place where Dan Jeonghak, fifth in the Cult Registry, resided, along with the small but elite Martial Sword Corps— most of whom had reached Peak realm.

Judging by their actions, they had more in common with the righteous path than with the Cult itself, and were constantly clashing with the Palace of Murder.

Countless pieces of information swirled through my mind, but none of it was of any use right now.

Dan Yehwa.

Ma Cheonso's fiancée. Did I not know a single thing about her?

_I was the one who judged everything by martial prowess._

Laments filled me the entire way here. The Shining Light Cult's ranking list only recorded up to the fiftieth rank, those close to the supreme peak realm. Unless they were a prodigy like Yayul Jin, I had not considered them worth my attention.

However, more than anything, I had made no effort to learn about myself.

"You may stay here. Don't even think about going any further inside. Wait until the Lord summons you."

At Dan Jiho's words, I glanced up and saw a long, single-story house built on land packed with flat earth, like a training ground.

He pointed to a room at the very end and even gestured with his head, as if telling me to hurry up and get inside. Normally, I would have lambasted him for his rudeness in true Ma Cheonso fashion, but I was not in the mood to care about such things right now.

I trudged into the room like an empty cart and sat on a chair by the window.

_Huu._ A sigh escaped me.

I'd meant to visit this place eventually, but I never imagined I would arrive weighed down by such guilt.

It was clear what the Lord would think of me. Given all the outrageous rumors I had spread regarding my stay in the Palace of Bliss, he would surely regard me as the world's greatest scoundrel.

"But why didn't Yayul Tak say _anything_?"

Come to think of it, that bastard might be a spy. No, he is totally a spy, for sure! I gritted my teeth, and killing intent surged through me.

"When I get my hands on him, I'm going to _squish_ and _mince_ him like a block of _stinky tofu_... Huh?"

Just then, as I glanced out the window, a familiar figure caught my eye: she wore a cloth over her face and was dressed like a man, but she could not escape my sharp gaze!

That woman was definitely... my handmaiden.

"_Eek!_"

As our eyes met, she shrieked out of nowhere and tried to run, but I leaped out the window and grabbed her shoulder.

In response, she spun her body and unleashed a kicking technique.

Oho! Her skills have improved! She has such talent, enough to achieve complete mastery anywhere. Too bad she's just a handmaiden…

I gave a slight smile, tapped her foot away, and grabbed the back of her neck.

"_Ack!_ Let go! Won't you let go?"

How can a grown young woman be so difficult?

I clicked my tongue in pity and said, "How long are you going to live like this? To think you've even come all the way to the Palace of Blades..."

"What did you say? Then why did _you_ come here, Young Cult Leader? You talked as if you'd never see me again."

Yes, she was right. I was in no position to point fingers. Not even knowing I had a fiancée, I went and spread all those rumors…

"It has nothing to do with you," I said with a deep sigh.

"Of course you'd say that," she replied curtly, turning her head away with an even angrier expression.

_But why are you the one who's angry?_

My already gloomy mood made me feel needlessly spiteful.

"Why are you always so angry whenever you see me? Do you hate me that much?"

"Yes I do. I hate your guts! So please, do let me go now."

"What...?"

She glared up at me from under the cloth.

Right, I asked the obvious.

"I hate myself too."

"What are you talking about," she grumbled, turning away with a huff.

But I smiled bitterly and said, "Even so, I don't hate you."

"W-what?"

The handmaiden blushed in surprise. She must be a tender child on the inside, who just does not know how to express herself.

I turned and walked back to the window.

"Since you've learned martial arts, I'm not too worried, but still, don't wander around needlessly. Go and stay at the Palace of Minor Light."

"W-what are you talking about! Why would I go there!"

"Because that's where you belong."

At least there, she would not be oppressed for being a handmaiden.

Leaving her behind me, I turned back to the room.
