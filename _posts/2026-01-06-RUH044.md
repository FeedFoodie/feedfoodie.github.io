Several days had passed, and with the new arrivals, the once-desolate Palace of Minor Light was now quite lively.

_Shrrrrk._

The paper door slid open, and a handsome man calmly offered a greeting. "Have you risen?"

This was Commander Cheon Birang of the Golden Cavalry Company from the Palace of Merchants. After returning from the Palace of Bliss, he had been loitering around the Palace of Minor Light. Now, he had come first thing in the morning, openly asking to stay in an annex.

"Does the Lord of Merchants know you're doing this?"

"Not yet... I suppose he doesn't know?"

"You're more willful than you look. What are you planning to do, staying at the Palace of Minor Light without your Lord's permission?"

"Well... doesn't it mean we should get to know each other a little?"

The fact that he had not reported meant he had yet to make a proper assessment. So, it must mean he intended to stay by my side and weigh his options: which was better, the Palace of Martial Blood or the Palace of Minor Light? Or perhaps: who was behind the Palace of Minor Light?

His intentions were obvious, but I did not let on and answered bluntly. "Are we dating? What's there to get to know? If our intentions align, we get on the same boat. If not, we step off."

"As you may know... the Palace of Merchants has already boarded the same boat as the Palace of Martial Blood."

"So did the Palace of Bliss and the Palace of Murder, but keep this in mind. If you linger on a sinking ship, you could lose your entire fortune. The time it takes to sink isn't very long."

"What should I do? To be honest, I need time. Time to decide if I can bet on you, Young Cult Leader, and if I can report as such."

At his candid words, I smirked and nodded. "That's true. Fine. You can stay."

"Pardon?"

"That's what you asked for."

"That's true, but... why so suddenly..."

_Why? Because just as Senior said, there is no one better suited than you to handle the Palace of Merchants. It was obvious his mind was busy with calculations. It is better to keep someone like him close rather than at a distance._

"However, you will stay with the Blood Asura."

"Yes. Yes? Ah, no, why would I... with him..."

"If you don't like it, you can wash your hands of this and go back. Oh, the Blood Asura will see you out. Bye."

"Wait! Please give me some time to think."

"You've already done all the thinking you needed before coming here. What more time do you need? Get out quickly. The Blood Asura is waiting outside."

"Doesn't this mean you're going to kill me without anyone knowing!"

"No. It means I'll kill you for all to see. I'll even hang your head on a pike along with the letter you wrote."

Cheon Birang frowned, his body shaking. "Who would believe that? Anyone can see it's a fabrication."

"I know, but wouldn't it be enough of a pretext to insist on after I've killed you? Don't forget. Nobody cares if someone like you dies here."

I stared at him coldly. Cheon Birang bit his lip hard and finally nodded. "I understand. I will follow your orders."

"Good. You may go."

He retreated with a gloomy face, his shoulders slumped.

{sep}

The next person to appear was Ji Hami, the Young Palace Lady of the Palace of Bliss.

"Oh my, Young Cult Leader. Weren't you cold last night? I was so cold. Haa..."

"It's scorching hot outside."

"Is that so? I didn't know."

If she did not even know that, there must be something wrong with her body.

"Um, Your Reverence. Please forgive my past rudeness and please... to my Palace Lady..."

"Out."

I waved my hand dismissively, gesturing for her to leave. From the moment Han Soso learned that I had mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, she was as good as abandoned.

When the interrogation continued, she quickly confessed that no impropriety had happened between us. Even Soaring Spirit Commander Siwol could not hide her anger.

_How could the Young Palace Lady... claim such things! Because of that, we... the humiliation our Palace Lady suffered...!_

Han Soso tried to hold her responsible, strip her of her position, and punish her severely, but at the Blood Asura's request, she was transferred to the Palace of Minor Light.

_May I take charge of that child?_

_We are disavowing her. Do as you will._

In the end, it seemed Ji Hami had rather taken the Blood Asura's fancy. When I asked why he brought her, he answered like this.

_Some brat asked me to spare her._

I could not understand it, but I accepted it. Strangely, I trusted the Blood Asura when he said something. He was a man of few words, but he was never one to speak nonsense.

{sep}

Meanwhile, Jaegal Tong, Han Soso, and I had our first meeting at the Shining Light Archives. The Blood Asura, Yayul Tak, and Eul Haryeong did not attend. They were busy taking care of the increased number of residents at the Palace of Minor Light.

"It's a pleasure. So this is how I meet Black Crow. I'm Han Soso."

"I am Jaegal Tong. I never imagined the Lady of Bliss would join us like this," Senior's characteristically gloomy voice flowed.

It seemed _Black Crow_ was Senior's alias within the Cult.

…Come to think of it, how did Senior come to establish himself in the Tianshan Mountains?

"Is this not simply the Cult's way?" Han Soso said. "A place where yesterday's enemy might share a drink and a laught with you tomorrow. Just like how the Reader of Heavenly Fate came to be in the Shining Light Cult."

Unlike my composed Senior, I was filled with bitterness.

"That is also true," Jaegal Tong replied.

"Was this recent operation involving our Palace of Bliss your handiwork as well?" Han Soso asked.

"I merely assisted His Reverence."

"Is that so? Shall we bet on it?"

After their friendly introductions, we began to talk about the events of the past few days. The most urgent problem was that I had confronted the Palace of Bliss. Many people had witnessed it, and afterward, the doors of the Bright Summer Parlor were shut tight; strange rumors immediately began to spread.

"Rumors can be covered up with other rumors."

To get straight to the conclusion, we decided to frame it as me having a wild time at the Palace of Bliss before returning to the Palace of Minor Light.

@[_I knew it... that Little Lecher. So that was his goal, huh? That bastard who's all talk!_]@

@[_Isn't it obvious? Still, seeing how the Lady of Bliss just let it slide, that bastard must be a big deal. Or at least_ **_have_** _a big deal. Heheh._]@

@[_That can't be right..._]@

@[_Fuck that guy! Ptui!_]@

Thanks to that, my infamy for mastering the bedroom arts would spread, as would my moniker of Little Lecher; but nothing else would leak out. With the Palace of Bliss and Senior joining hands, everything would proceed swiftly and smoothly.

"It would be better to amplify the rumors, actually. We best delay the truth as much as possible."

"Great minds do think alike! Since the mouths of the Palace of Bliss and the Palace of Merchants are sealed, they'll find it hard to sift through all the tales."

"Damn it. Do whatever you want."

They were right, but I did not feel very good about it.

As for Yuwol, who had performed the Chant of Euphoric Spirits...

_Was the one who died that woman?_

_So they say._

_Are you okay?_

_It couldn't be helped._

The one person who died trying to flee the Palace of Bliss turned out to be Yuwol.

Han Soso's tone was colder than anyone's, but according to Siwol, she was deeply grieved by Yuwol's death. There were many suspicious points, but after hearing that, I could not bring myself to ask any further questions.

And as for the Palace of Murder...

"Fortunately, they seem to be completely unaware of the news here. Judging by how they don't even know Your Reverence has come," Han Soso said.

I heard later that the Palace of Bliss and the Palace of Murder had a much deeper relationship than I thought. It was not just a matter of helping each other. The Palace of Bliss provided funds and passed on information about the Palace of Martial Blood and the Council of Elders.

The Lord of Murder's independence and eccentricity belied a contempt for politics, but Han Soso had still given him her support.

"No matter how restricted the information is, to know nothing... is it perhaps due to that matter?" Senior asked meaningfully, as if he had an inkling.

"Yes. It's probably because of their latest mission," Han Soso replied.

"A mission?" I asked skeptically.

"The missions the Palace of Murder receives are always the same. Key figures."

"Assassination..."

The Palace of Murder, like the Palace of Blades, was a martial group composed of a small number of people. However, their underlying strength was by no means at a level that could be ignored.

I'd only recently learned as much, but in the past ten years, the number of figures from the orthodox sects who'd died at their hands exceeded three digits.

Dispelling the somewhat heavy atmosphere, Han Soso said, "I heard the Murim Alliance has been up to some cute tricks lately under the name of 'Eradication of the Demonic Path'. So, the Palace of Murder has stepped forward to set an example."

Was she really calling the _Eradication of the Demonic Path_ a cute trick... anyway, so the time has already come?

The Murim Alliance, which valued justifications, would occasionally use this phrase as a slogan to rally righteous warriors. Among us, we called it the _Time of Unity_.

Most warriors truly cried out for unity and threw their lives away, but we knew better.

_When the Alliance needs internal consensus, that's when it begins._

This "Time" would be used merely as a political lever, without any real purpose. That was the Time of Unity, and the meaning of the _Eradication of the Demonic Path_.

Therefore, I could say with certainty that the orthodox sects would never ascend the Tianshan Mountains. They would simply reach Xinjiang and then, faced with the Cult's response, announce a retreat.

"Look into the Murim Alliance. The Wind Dragon Brigade will definitely be the first to move."

The Wind Dragon Brigade moves on the battlefield first and fastest. Naturally, if the Eradication of the Demonic Path had begun, they were likely already on their way here.

Of course, I was not too worried.

_Moyong Hwi, Namgung Yena, Chun Gae, Tang Pyeongsan. They are there, even if I'm not._

"Hmm... I will do so," Senior replied, letting out a suspicious breath.

"Palace of Murder... even against curs like you, they shall not easily break. This I guarantee," I muttered, clenching my teeth.

"Pardon? What do you mean by that?" Han Soso asked.

"No, I mean... shouldn't we send more support? The Murim Alliance won't be such a pushover."

"That is not a concern. The Lord of Murder has moved personally."

"What?"

The Lord of Murder is a master of the Extreme Demonic Realm, sixth in the Cult's hierarchy. If he has moved, the Wind Dragon Brigade alone...

"I told you, didn't I? He's a man who will do anything as long as it's fun."

_Crazy bastard._

A curse rose to my throat.

"We move on next to the Palace of Murder," I spoke decisively, and Senior and Han Soso both nodded.

Even if the situation were different, the Palace of Murder posed the greater risk.

The Palace of Martial Blood and the Council of Elders would try to act within the bounds of common sense; they would not rashly attack the Palace of Minor Light until they were certain.

But the Palace of Murder was different. Judging by the Lord of Murder's temperament, were he to learn that Han Soso and I had joined hands, he might invade the Palace of Minor Light at once.

"Until then, they must be kept in the dark."

"Of course," Han Soso smiled warmly.

"And for the time being," added Senior, "the Palace of Minor Light might be a little noisy."

"Why? Did someone else send assassins?"

At my question, Senior smoothly rolled his wheels toward the wall. "Not yet. Since the Palace of Merchants and the Palace of Bliss have sealed their lips, your rivals' movements will be slower than ever. Interest in you, Your Reverence, will also wane. However…"

Senior paused, then calmly continued speaking as he suddenly tore pages from a directory one by one. "Since they have no way of getting news, they will soon try to seek out this place, the Shining Light Archives."

That made sense. Lacking a pheasant, you use a chicken. Lacking a chicken, even a sparrow's egg will do. There had always been alternatives, so they'd never had need of Senior Jaegal, and considered him a tertiary option.

They would doubtlessly seek him out at once.

"I've sent people, so please excuse any noise made during the construction."

Apparently he meant to say that the Archives were moving to the Palace of Minor Light. But was that something that required construction?

"Won't rumors spread?"

"I will ensure they do not. Not even a single ant passing by the Palace of Minor Light will escape my gaze."

"If you say so."

Aren't we overconfident.

"While we're at it, let's move the Palace of Bliss to the Sun Peak as well," Han Soso suggested. "It was originally built for Your Reverence, after all. Is that alright?"

_It was for the Master, not for me._

"Not yet. There's no point in drawing that kind of attention. But I'll keep an annex empty for you, so you can come anytime."

"Very well."

"We must give them no time to prepare," I said. "Leak stories so they can't distinguish truth from falsehood. Anything is fine, so long as it draws the interest of the lay followers. In the meantime, I will..."

_Take down the Seven Hallowed Palaces._
