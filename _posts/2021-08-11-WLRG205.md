Lee Sungmin stared, his mind struggling to process the sight before him.

*What is this?*

In the center of the plaza sat the Sword Dragon, Namgung Heewon. He wondered why on earth the young master of the Namgung Clan was in such a state. His gaze remained fixed on the cage, a stark and bizarre fixture in the middle of the bustling square.

The people milling about paid it no mind. A large cage holding a man sat in the middle of their crowded plaza, yet they acted as if it were a perfectly natural sight, not sparing Namgung Heewon a second glance as he sat cross-legged inside.

"Ex-excuse me," Lee Sungmin said, stepping in front of a man passing through the plaza.

The man blinked, surprised to be stopped. "What is it?"

"It's just..." Lee Sungmin hesitated before pointing a finger at the cage. "What in the world is that?"

The man followed his gesture and glanced at Namgung Heewon. "Ah," he said with a knowing look. "Is this your first time in Bellador?"

"......"

"That man in the cage over there is the young master of the Namgung Clan, the Sword Dragon Namgung Heewon."

"Why is the young master of the Namgung Clan in there like that?"

"I heard he defied his father, the Namgung Patriarch," the man explained.

"What?" Lee Sungmin asked again, dumbfounded.

"I don't know the full details, but it seems the Namgung Patriarch was furious about it. Thanks to that, the young master has been in that state for over three months now."

He could not understand it. What could Namgung Heewon have possibly done to be locked up in a crowded plaza like this? What was even more incomprehensible was that Namgung Heewon seemed to be accepting his fate. The iron bars of the cage looked thick, but Lee Sungmin knew that if Namgung Heewon put his mind to it, he could cut through them and escape at any time, yet, he remained silent.

"Don't get too close out of curiosity," the man warned. "If you do, the Namgung Clan won't just stand by."

"I understand."

Lee Sungmin stood at a distance, watching Namgung Heewon. From this range, a Thought Transmission could reach him.

[Why are you in there like that?] he sent.

Namgung Heewon, still sitting cross-legged, flinched at the sudden voice in his head. He lifted his bowed head, his eyes shining through a curtain of messy, tangled bangs as he slowly looked around.

[Over here,] Lee Sungmin sent, letting him know his location.

Namgung Heewon's gaze finally fell upon Lee Sungmin. Though a considerable distance separated them, Lee Sungmin could clearly see the puzzled expression on his face.

[Who are you?]

Lee Sungmin ignored the question, pressing on with his own. [What on earth did you do to end up in that state?]

The distant Namgung Heewon made a dumbfounded expression. [Who are you to ask that?]

[Someone who knows you.]

[There are people who know me all over this continent. The name of the Sword Dragon Namgung Heewon is quite famous, after all.]

Lee Sungmin sighed, finally relenting. [It's Lee Sungmin.]

The name hung in the air between them, and Namgung Heewon's face stiffened. *Did I say the wrong thing?* Lee Sungmin wondered, suddenly anxious. He did not know how Namgung Heewon had taken the news about Jaegal Taeryeong and worried that introducing himself might cause him to lash out.

[I see,] Namgung Heewon replied after a brief silence, nodding slowly. He looked at Lee Sungmin with a bitter expression. [There's something I want to ask.]

[What is it?]

[Were you the one who killed Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin?]

As expected, he had asked about it. Lee Sungmin pressed his lips together as Namgung Heewon waited for his answer.

[I didn't kill them,] he answered honestly. [You saw it yourself, didn't you? I didn't kill them. I let them go and told them to return on their own.]

[I know.] Namgung Heewon's shoulders shook, as if he had known all along what Lee Sungmin's answer would be. [I thought so. The rumors must have gotten twisted. You didn't kill Jaegal Taeryeong or Moyong Seojin. Yes. The you I know isn't that kind of person, but everyone says you killed them both.]

[I didn't kill them.]

[Then this must be a conspiracy. Someone... yes, it was most likely the Murim Alliance's Black Hound Squad. The Black Hound Squad killed Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin in the Bewitching Forest, and they framed you for it.]

[That's probably it.]

[So the Murim Alliance went out of their way to frame you. Haha! I knew it. I was right.] Namgung Heewon raised a hand and tore at his disheveled hair.

Lee Sungmin watched him with a bitter expression. He never imagined he would meet Namgung Heewon here, in a place like this.

[Why are you locked up in that state?] Lee Sungmin asked again.

[They told me to go and kill you,] Namgung Heewon answered. [I was in the Bewitching Forest at the time, you see. Two direct descendants of prestigious clans died in that forest. Because of that, the prestige of the Noble Clans fell, and our Patriarch... he's someone who cares deeply about that kind of prestige. So he told me to go and kill the Phantom Spear.]

"......"

[I refused. I didn't believe you killed Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin. I said it was a conspiracy, that some other faction killed them, but really, what other faction could it be? The ones who reported their deaths were none other than the Murim Alliance's Black Hound Squad. In the end, I voiced my suspicion of the Murim Alliance right in front of the honor-bound Namgung Patriarch, my own father.]

Namgung Heewon lowered the hand that had been tearing at his hair. A murderous light gleamed in his eyes.

[My father was furious. I knew it well. My father, the Namgung Clan, they have a close relationship with the Murim Alliance. A long time ago, he talked about gathering the power of the Noble Clans to create a new organization called the Oryonghoe, but that was so long ago. It was something he talked about before I was born, so it's been over thirty years. My father was young then, but now he's too old. When I questioned the Murim Alliance and refuted his words, he became enraged, and thanks to that, I ended up in this state.]

[If you wanted to, you could escape at any time, couldn't you?] Lee Sungmin asked again.

Namgung Heewon did not answer.

[Why are you just sitting there? Your inner arts seem fine, and it's not like your body is injured.]

[Because I wasn't sure,] Namgung Heewon chuckled. [Whether I was right, or if my father was right. I wasn't sure. Being locked up here was inconvenient, but it wasn't all that bad. The most inconvenient thing was probably figuring out where to relieve myself.] He slowly rose to his feet. [Did you... come here knowing I was locked up?]

[It was a coincidence.]

Although that was his answer, Namgung Heewon did not seem disappointed.

[It doesn't matter if it was a coincidence. Thanks to you coming here, I've found my conviction.]

[Couldn't I be lying?]

[So. Are you lying?]

[No.]

[Then why would you even say that in the first place?] Namgung Heewon grumbled as he approached the iron bars. He raised both hands and gripped them. [I'm thinking of escaping from here.]

[And after that?]

[I used to respect my father, but now, I'm not so sure. My father chose to side with the Murim Alliance rather than believe my words, and he locked me up here to establish his authority. My actions might bring shame to my father and the Noble Clans, but well. I don't want to worry about that anymore.]

Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, looking at Namgung Heewon's face. Namgung Heewon, who had been stroking the iron bars, laughed out loud.

[Once I escape, the Namgung Clan and my father will come for me. I'll shake them off and leave this city. I plan to go to the Murim Alliance and ask for the truth.]

[Do you think they'll listen?]

[I'm not sure about that either. Perhaps, just as they did to you, the Murim Alliance will pin some ridiculous crime on me. If that happens, so be it. That will be how I know for sure that the Murim Alliance is not in the right.]

Lee Sungmin was silent for a moment.

KIIIGIIK…

The sound of groaning metal cut through the plaza's chatter. The iron bars in Namgung Heewon's grip began to twist and deform. People passing by stopped in their tracks, their mouths agape as they stared at the bending bars and the man who now walked freely from his prison.

[I'm going to meet the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman,] Lee Sungmin said to him.

Having completely exited the cage, Namgung Heewon stopped in his tracks and looked at Lee Sungmin with a surprised expression. [The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman? Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon?]

"......"

[Why him?]

[The Murim Alliance has branded me a demonic practitioner, and I'll have to keep fighting them from now on. It's too much for me to handle alone, so I plan to borrow the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. As it happens, he holds some goodwill toward me.]

[…That's surprising. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is a man shrouded in mystery…]

[Will you come with me?] Lee Sungmin asked.

At his words, Namgung Heewon's expression froze. He hesitated for a moment before shaking his head. […No. I believe the Murim Alliance is in the wrong, but that doesn't mean I think the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman are in the right. Of course, I've never met the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, so I don't know what kind of person he is. Still, I'm not ignorant of the evil deeds committed by the unorthodox sects and the demonic practitioners who belong to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.]

[I understand.] Lee Sungmin nodded. He had made the offer but honestly had not expected much. Even if Namgung Heewon was disappointed with the Murim Alliance, he was still born and raised among the prestigious clans of the orthodox sects. For someone like him to join the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was an impossibility.

[I understand,] Lee Sungmin repeated, raising a hand to touch his face. It was not his own, but the face of the human skin mask he had grown accustomed to over the past two months. [If it's not too much trouble for you to move, leave this city right now.]

He sent the Thought Transmission with a smile as purple inner arts began to flare from the tips of his toes.

[What… are you planning to do?]

[I'm going to buy you some time.]

[What…?]

[You say that, but isn't it awkward for you to fight the Namgung Patriarch and the martial artists of the Noble Clans?]

[Why would you…?]

[Well, I did call you Hyung-nim in the past,] Lee Sungmin said, looking around. The distant thunder of galloping hooves reached his ears. The martial artists of the Namgung Clan, having heard that Namgung Heewon had escaped, were approaching. [And because you believed me without suspicion. That's all.]

"......"

[Don't worry,] Lee Sungmin said with a smirk. [The Namgung Patriarch, and the martial artists of the Noble Clans. I won't kill them.]

WHOOSH.

He infused his spear with inner arts, and purple flames engulfed the entire weapon. Watching this, Namgung Heewon swallowed hard. Even back in the Bewitching Forest, Lee Sungmin's martial prowess had been on a much higher level than his own. Only a few months had passed, yet in that short time, Lee Sungmin had reached a level that Namgung Heewon could not even dare to imagine.

*At that level… even if my father came at him himself, it wouldn't even be a Ten Exchanges Match…*

The head of the Namgung Clan, the Heavenly Venerable Sword King, was once called a great martial artist. Even now, he was considered one of the most skilled swordsmen alive. However, even the Heavenly Venerable Sword King was only about as skilled as the current Namgung Heewon.

[Go,] Lee Sungmin said to him again.

Namgung Heewon hesitated for a moment longer before dashing away. Lee Sungmin simultaneously sensed his presence leaving the plaza and the presence of the Noble Clan martial artists closing in.

[You're just looking for trouble.]

"Tell me about it," Lee Sungmin replied with a smirk to the Void Monarch's grumbling.
