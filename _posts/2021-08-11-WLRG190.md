The carriage had not even left Urmuri when a voice broke the silence.

"...Wait... a moment."

The words came from the Crazy Heavenly Demon, whose lips had been sealed shut until that moment. Lee Sungmin, sitting in the driver's seat, flinched and glanced back over his shoulder. Beside the dying man, Frau wore an equally surprised expression as she tended to his condition.

"Don't speak," Frau warned. His life qi was completely gone, his body already a breath away from death. Her magic was the only thing holding him together, and even that was an imperfect solution.

Lee Sungmin climbed from the driver's seat into the cargo bed. He looked down at the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who lay breathing faintly on the floorboards.

"...Elder."

"Let... me go," the Crazy Heavenly Demon rasped, the words a struggle. His hair and skin were gone, burned away to nothing, yet somehow he was still alive.

A request to be let go. Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled as he understood the meaning behind those words. He reached out with a shaking hand and grasped the old man's wrist.

"Elder..."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon could not speak again. Lee Sungmin ground his teeth and lowered his gaze, his mind a storm of thoughts. If they reached the Hyujael Mountains, they might be able to save him by performing a Spirit Possession with a Demonic Spirit. Frau had told him this, admitting she could not be certain, but it was the only option they had left. They had already consulted high-ranking priests and healers from Erebrisa, but every one of them had declared it impossible. With his life force completely incinerated, revival was beyond their power.

"this one has lived long enough." The trembling voice broke off intermittently, but to Lee Sungmin, it flowed as clearly as a river. "I don't have any major regrets. I got my revenge... yes. That's enough. I think I lived a decent life."

"Elder."

"Let me go."

His face was a ruin of burned flesh, his expression unreadable, but Lee Sungmin felt certain the Crazy Heavenly Demon was smiling.

"It is this one's life. I wish for its end... so please respect that."

Lee Sungmin closed his eyes. He thought it was a cowardly plea. *It's my life, so respect my wishes.* He had no counterargument to such a statement. Still, he wanted to say something, anything. He opened his mouth, but in the end, no words came. After a long moment of silence, he barely managed to speak.

"...I'm... sorry... for being late."

"It's alright. Even if you had been here from the start, this one would have done the same. ...It's a shame I have nothing to pass on to you," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said with a dry chuckle.

His bowed head felt heavy. He struggled to keep his eyes open as his vision wavered. Lee Sungmin clenched his fists so tightly that his nails dug into his palms, drawing blood.

"the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said. "It is the martial art that is this one's everything. It might not be a good fit for you, a Spear Technique user... but even so, please don't forget it. this one failed to enter the Transcendent Realm... but you, someday, will surely enter it. Even when that time comes, please don't forget the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

"I won't... forget."

"That's all I need." His breathing grew shallow. "Do you know this one's name?"

"...Byeong Wonpae."

"I was called by the title the Crazy Heavenly Demon more often than that name. Hehe... thinking about it now, it was a title that didn't suit me at all. the Heavenly Demon. Haha...!" He let out another dry laugh. "I'm worried about Rubia. If you're willing to grant an old man's request, someday... could you check if that child is doing well?"

"Of course... I will."

"Yes... hearing that answer puts my mind at ease. This is fine. This is enough. this one... I. I've lived without regrets."

That was a lie. The Crazy Heavenly Demon himself knew that better than anyone. A life without regrets did not exist. No matter how well one lived, it was human nature to have regrets at the moment of death. Lingering attachments were inevitable. He had wanted to enter the Transcendent Realm, to reach that unattainable height as a martial artist.

The wall of reality, however, had been much higher than he had ever thought. In the end, this was as far as he could go. Even burning away his life qi had not allowed him to break through that wall or attain enlightenment. To die like this was his greatest regret, but he refused to be bound by it.

He had no confidence. Even if he were to live again, he had no confidence that he could train his martial arts and finally reach the Transcendent Realm. That was why he wanted to give up. It was enough. *This is my limit.* The Crazy Heavenly Demon quietly closed his eyes.

"Burn the body. So that all the bones are incinerated... like that. Don't collect the ashes, just leave them be. So they can fly anywhere."

"Elder..."

"You are different from me," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered, his words so quiet that Lee Sungmin could not hear them clearly.

"Elder," Lee Sungmin called out urgently.

No answer came. The shallow breaths grew more intermittent until, soon after, they stopped completely.

Lee Sungmin stared with a blank expression. Frau, sitting beside him, scratched the back of her head, her own expression complicated.

"...The end?" Lee Sungmin blurted out, the words escaping him before he realized it. *Is this the end? Is this death?* He checked for a pulse. Nothing throbbed beneath his fingers.

The death of someone close was an unfamiliar feeling, even for a man who had returned from death himself. Lee Sungmin's shoulders began to tremble violently. He gazed at the Crazy Heavenly Demon's face, a completely burned mask where his eyes were no longer visible. His stomach churned, and he bit his lower lip hard to swallow the urge to vomit.

If only someone had killed him. If Chuseong, the man who fought him, had not died. If it had been something like that, he might have felt a little more at ease. He could have made revenge his goal, but Chuseong, the target of that potential revenge, had already died at the hands of the Crazy Heavenly Demon. There was no one left to blame, no target anywhere to vent the emotions building inside him.

That was what truly shook Lee Sungmin. The unventable grief swirled in his heart. It was not the first time he had seen death. He himself had experienced it, had watched others die, and had killed many with his own hands, but this death, the death of someone close, was shaking his very soul. They had only traveled together for a year, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon had been his companion.

"...I guess there's no need to go to the Hyujael Mountains," Frau muttered, inwardly relieved. The Demonic Spirit of the Hyujael Mountains was fickle. Yana had been lucky to get what she wanted, but if Lee Sungmin had gone to meet it, there was no telling if the spirit would have been so generous. It might have been better for the old man to die cleanly like this. Of course, Frau did not share these thoughts with Lee Sungmin.

"How long are you going to hold on?"

"...Just a moment."

"Holding on like that won't bring a dead person back. You have to let him go."

At Frau's cold words, Lee Sungmin lifted his head. A fierce gaze turned on her, and she cackled upon meeting his eyes.

"Those are the eyes of someone who wants to kill me right now. You'd better control your emotions. Your body has become close to that of a Yokai, and that yokai nature has settled in your mind. That thing enjoys violence. If you act like a beast, your humanity will be devoured by it."

Frau's warning was true. Lee Sungmin knew it, so he bit his lip harder. She was right. Besides, it was thanks to her that the Crazy Heavenly Demon had been able to deliver his final words.

"...I'm sorry." Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply.

The funeral was held quietly, with neither Yana nor Rubia present. It was Frau who conducted it. As per the Crazy Heavenly Demon's last will, his body was burned. Lee Sungmin stared blankly as the corpse was consumed by flames Frau had conjured. The body did not burn for long before turning to bone, and the bones soon crumbled into white powder. A wind blew from afar, extinguishing the flames and scattering the ash and bone dust into the air.

It was over.

Lee Sungmin stood there, watching it all with a profound sense of emptiness that overshadowed any solemnity. He looked around without realizing it.

No one was there. No one. Not Rubia, not the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

The moment that realization struck him, Lee Sungmin collapsed to the ground. He used to be accustomed to being alone, but not anymore. The fact that he was alone again shook his mind to its core. The sturdy divine power that even the Void Monarch could not penetrate was, just for this moment, as fragile as tofu.

[Don't be too depressed,] the Void Monarch muttered, seeming uncomfortable himself. From the flames still burning on the ground, something whitish shot up from the scattered ash and bone dust. It circled the fire once before flowing into Lee Sungmin. The demonic energy the Crazy Heavenly Demon had possessed had returned to its source. Lee Sungmin clutched his chest tightly.

[Still, didn't he die after achieving his revenge? He must have been satisfied with that. Since that fellow died satisfied, you have no reason to be depressed.]

*...It's not as easy as it sounds.*

[True. That's why humans are weak.]

After the funeral was over, Frau approached him. "Do you plan on staying in Urmuri?"

Lee Sungmin shook his head. "......"

"There's no reason to... stay here any longer. I'm leaving." He saw no point in waiting for Yana's return. All his business in Urmuri was finished. There was nothing left for him here. *What next?* He looked down at his bloodstained hands. He still had business in the South. The Fist Honored One who had cursed Wiji Hoyeon was hiding in the Elf Forest, not far from this very place.

"Is that so? Then this might be the last time we meet. Though I doubt that's actually the case," Frau chuckled, shaking her head. "Your Destiny Force is incredibly strong. Perhaps... I've been swept up in it as well. We might meet again someday. Whether it will be a good meeting or a bad one, I don't know."

She muttered as much and turned away. Lee Sungmin watched her retreating back without offering a farewell. After a moment, he looked at the embers still glowing on the ground. *Should I say goodbye?* He thought of the villagers Yana had taken in but decided against it. He did not want to form any more connections. That was his honest feeling. He looked down at the crackling, dying flames and bowed his head deeply.

As he left Urmuri, Lee Sungmin composed his emotions. He first called for Nebul. He had consumed the Dragon Heart, but he could not feel any change. Before heading to the Elf Forest, he wanted to know his exact condition.

"Hoho... my... I can't believe it. A human consumed a Dragon Heart?" The one Nebul connected him to was the Blue Magic Tower Master, the same man who had wished to purchase the artifact. He wore a blue robe befitting his title and stared intently at Lee Sungmin's face from beyond the portal Nebul had opened. "It's an unprecedented event, so I can't really say anything. If I saw you in person, it might be a different story." The Blue Magic Tower Master looked Lee Sungmin up and down. "If you get the chance someday, stop by the Blue Magic Tower. I think I could give you more definite advice then."

He was not the only one who said such things. Since a human consuming an entire Dragon Heart was unheard of, no one could offer a clear opinion.

"This is just my personal opinion, but," Nebul began carefully after the consultation ended, "the Dragon Heart is a tremendous mass of power. Dragons are beings that live for thousands of years, wielding magic freely through Dragonic. Though they are no longer seen in this world... just a few hundred years ago, Dragons were said to be beings comparable to gods. Of course, it's not as if there's no precedent for such Dragons being hunted, but in all of history, there has never been a case where a Dragon Heart remained in a complete form like the one you took, Lee Sungmin."

Nebul swallowed hard before continuing. "All Dragons, in the face of death... that is, when they are about to be killed, first destroy their own Dragon Heart. Thanks to that, even if one succeeded in hunting a Dragon, obtaining a complete Dragon Heart was impossible. To get one, you must kill the Dragon before it can kill itself. You must possess enough power to overwhelm it completely."

[Ahem.] Hearing Nebul's words, the Void Monarch preened.

"Even a fragment of a Dragon Heart holds immense magic value and contains great power," Nebul went on. "But... to consume a complete one, not a fragment... a human body cannot handle it. No inhuman or mutant could withstand that power. Regardless of the method, if you consumed it like some miracle herb, it would be natural for your body to fail and explode."

"But I didn't explode, did I?"

"That's why I said it's unprecedented, and... this is my personal opinion. The power of the Dragon Heart is likely dormant deep within your body, Lee Sungmin, if it hasn't dissipated externally."

It had not dissipated. Lee Sungmin was certain of that. The immense power had definitely entered his body.

"Perhaps it's a defense mechanism," Nebul suggested. "If the power had been directly reflected in your body like a miracle herb, your body might have judged that it couldn't withstand it."

Since it was an unprecedented event, Nebul could not be certain either, but Lee Sungmin found his words quite plausible. Until now, the Black Heart had evolved his body in the face of crises, allowing him to survive. Consuming the entire Dragon Heart was surely another crisis where his body could have exploded. Perhaps the Black Heart was suppressing the Dragon Heart's power so that he could endure it.

"I'm sorry if I've spoken out of turn."

"No. It's fine. It was very helpful," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking his head. "And. There's some additional information I'd like to purchase."

"What is it?"

"Forest of Ancients," Lee Sungmin said in a quiet voice. "I want to know the location of the Elf Forest."
