A rumor drifting down from the North was more than enough to capture Lee Sungmin's attention.

The North was not unfamiliar territory. It was there he had first learned of the inhuman beings called 'Predator' and had come face to face with one of its leaders, the Vampire Queen Jeniela. He had also encountered the Mad Wolf Ju Won, a monster known as one of the strongest Lycanthropes.

Beyond that, he had a connection to Kim Jonghyun, the very source of the rumor. Thinking back, he realized they had met quite a few times. Their first encounter had been in Behenger, and he had later received Kim Jonghyun's help on his way to kill the Sword Phantom. They had even reunited in the northern city of Trabia. Lee Sungmin, who had been helping Arbes in Urmuri, remained unaware that the Black Mage who ultimately attacked him and stripped Arbes of everything was Kim Jonghyun himself.

Because of this, Lee Sungmin held no particular ill will toward the man. Kim Jonghyun had always been friendly toward him. Even in Urmuri, he could have easily threatened or killed Lee Sungmin, but he had chosen not to.

This history made the news of the Demon King of the North all the more bewildering. The Kim Jonghyun he knew was a kind and good person. How could that same man have massacred seven villages in the short span of a week? How could he now be called a Demon Lord, an object of fear?

*The Magic Tower Masters gathered to subjugate Kim Jonghyun.*

An incredible force was assembling. Not only were the magic corps of the Mages Guild mobilizing, but the masters of the Golden, Green, White, and Red Magic Towers had also gathered. The 'Church' announced its intention to send support, a group of Junior Disciples and Holy Knights optimized for persecuting Dark Magic. Even the Murim Alliance had dispatched warriors, including some from the Moyong Clan.

Hundreds had gathered to deal with a single Black Mage. It was a rare sight to see such a large-scale force assemble for a subjugation, especially when the target was just one fallen mage.

Lee Sungmin stroked the bracelet on his left wrist. He had never paid it much mind after receiving it from Scarlet more than five years ago. In all that time, she had never once sent a warning of danger through it. She had become the Crimson Archmage and was doing well for herself. Even now, the bracelet remained silent.

He could not just ignore this, however. Such a large force had gathered. Kim Jonghyun would surely be subjugated. No matter how outstanding a Black Mage he was, he stood alone against hundreds, and these were no mere rabble. He faced the Mage Army, specialized in combat magic, and Magic Tower Masters who stood near the pinnacle of their fields.

*There's probably no need for me to go.*

However, was that really true? The location bothered him. The North. It was the land of the Vampire Queen Jeniela and the Mad Wolf Ju Won. Could they have joined forces with Kim Jonghyun? According to the rumors, Kim Jonghyun was not even trying to flee, despite the massive subjugation force gathering to deal with him. He surely was not wishing for his own death. He had to be aiming for something, standing his ground with the confidence that he could survive.

[So. Are you going to go?] the Void Monarch asked.

Lee Sungmin did not answer right away. If it were only the Mages Guild, things might be different, but with the Murim Alliance involved, he could not move recklessly, even with his worry for Scarlet. Weren't the Moyong Clan part of their support forces? As long as he could not clear his name from the false accusation, he was nothing but the man who killed Moyong Seojin in their eyes.

"...You never know."

Lee Sungmin made up his mind. He was indebted to Scarlet. Setting aside what happened on Mount Mushii, he might have died during Aine's first attack without her help. Besides, he had made a promise to help her if she was ever in a dangerous situation.

[Looks like you don't want to have any regrets later, huh?] the Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin nodded. In his past life, he had lived to be twenty-seven. Now that the time he remembered from the Beggars' Gang in the Dungeon had passed, he knew nothing of the events that would unfold in the world. He had no choice but to be cautious. He did not know what Kim Jonghyun was plotting, and the thought that Scarlet might die filled him with anxiety.

"Where are you planning to go?"

First, he went to ask the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman for permission to leave. The Chairman was lounging comfortably in his office when Lee Sungmin announced he would be leaving for a while. His voice was indifferent as he asked the question.

"I'm thinking of making a quick trip to the North."

"Is it because of that fellow, the Demon King of the North?" the Chairman asked immediately, the name coming to him as soon as the North was mentioned. That was how much of a hot topic Kim Jonghyun had become.

"How did you know?"

"Because he's the most famous bastard these days," the Chairman said with a smirk. "He massacred seven villages and brazenly piled the corpses at the entrance, didn't he? And not just any corpses. He wove them all together after tearing out their hearts. It's no wonder they call him a Demon Lord for committing such a crazy act."

He paused. "Why? Do you have some kind of connection with that Demon King fellow?"

"That's not it."

"Then do you have an acquaintance among the villagers he killed?"

"No."

"Then it seems you have an acquaintance in the subjugation force."

"......"

Lee Sungmin answered honestly. The Chairman's voice was laced with laughter when he spoke again.

"This one has no intention of stopping you. It's your choice to throw yourself into a dangerous situation, after all. I don't know what that Demon Lord fellow is plotting, but I'm curious. A man who massacred seven villages and committed such atrocities. I wonder what on earth he's trying to do." The Chairman stroked the mask covering his face. "Do you plan to hide, or not?"

"I won't see anything good if I don't, so I plan to hide."

"You're not planning to just cover your face with a mask and call that hiding, are you?"

"I have a way to change my face."

"Well, if you want to avoid unnecessary and troublesome conflicts, then do so, but keep this in mind. If you go there and the Murim Alliance discovers your identity... huhu! Things will get very interesting." The Chairman's eyes curved into a grin behind his mask. "For the Moyong Clan Head, it would be seeing his daughter's killer. For the Murim Alliance, it would be like the successor of this Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon appearing without any backing. They'll either kill you or take you prisoner... huhu! Either way, it won't be fun for you."

"Don't you think I'll be causing trouble for you?"

"If you look at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance as a whole, that might be true, but I have no intention of restricting your freedom for such a reason," the Chairman said. "If you die, this one will just use your revenge as a pretext to crush the Murim Alliance. If you're captured, I'll crush the Murim Alliance to rescue you. If you kill the Black Dragon Knight, Heaven Beyond Heaven will also make a move."

"What if I fight the Murim Alliance when they attack me?"

"I don't think you'll die unless the Sword Immortal, the Spear King, the Moon Queen of Heaven Beyond Heaven, or the Martial God himself comes. If that happens, the Murim Alliance won't stand still, so the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance and they will end up fighting each other."

The Chairman's voice was calm as he spoke of war. He did not seem worried at all, even though a clash between the two great alliances might be imminent. Lee Sungmin could not quite understand his attitude. After a week in the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, he had come to understand just how flimsy an organization it was, contrary to its name. In terms of organizational power, it could not compare to the Murim Alliance. From his perspective, an all-out war would inevitably end in a crushing defeat for the Chairman.

"Are you confident about an all-out war?"

"I wouldn't say I'd do it if I wasn't," the Chairman answered without hesitation. "The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is not as weak as it looks. It's not like this one was completely hands-off during the hundred years I was away, and there's you, too." He raised a hand and pointed at Lee Sungmin. "They say the number of those in the Transcendent Realm is small, but that's not a big problem. Among the Six Honored Ones, I think you can handle the Blade Supreme and the Dark Supreme by yourself. The Moon Queen... she probably won't step forward in an all-out war. Though the Spear King seems to be a variable."

The Chairman crossed his arms and muttered, "To be honest, I'm not sure how the Sword Immortal will act, but it's not that I'm not confident. In fact, if the Little Heavenly Demon came to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, I wouldn't even have to worry about this power difference." He laughed heartily. "It's not something you need to worry about. So. When are you planning to leave?"

"I don't know how things will turn out, so I plan to leave right away."

"It's a very long way from here to the North. Even at your speed, it would take several months. Are you planning to ride a 'horse'?"

Lee Sungmin nodded. There was no telling how the situation might change during the long journey.

[Still, he's taking pretty good care of you since you're his successor.] the Void Monarch chuckled as Lee Sungmin left the office.

*Tell me about it.*

After all, the Chairman had just promised to personally step in to avenge or rescue him if he was killed or captured by the Murim Alliance.

Returning to his room, Lee Sungmin summoned the Fairy Horse. It was the first time he had called upon it since making the contract through Osla. A pure white light gathered before him, coalescing into the shape of a beautiful white horse. The creature gazed at him with its transparent eyes and lowered its head. Lee Sungmin stroked its mane and climbed onto its back.

[Two more times left. So, where are you planning to go?]

*It's closest to go from Trabia.*

Lee Sungmin pictured Trabia in his mind as he stroked the horse's mane. The Fairy Horse lifted its head, stomped its hoof once, and Lee Sungmin felt his body float for a moment.

When the sensation ended, he was in the sky above Trabia. The sudden northern cold did not affect him. He pulled a human skin mask he had prepared in advance over his face. The face he had worn last time had been exposed, so this one was different. Its job done, the Fairy Horse dissolved back into white light and disappeared. Lee Sungmin immediately began to fall, but he did not panic, slowing his descent to land in a deserted area.

[This isn't good,] the Void Monarch muttered. [Others might not have, but Jeniela would have detected the Spatial Movement. Jeniela might even come...]

The Void Monarch's warning was still echoing in his mind when the shadows in the alleyway stirred. They expanded greatly, and from their depths, a woman with crimson hair rose abruptly.

"...You are?" Jeniela asked, a surprised expression on her face as she fully revealed herself.

Lee Sungmin took a few wary steps back. The Vampire Queen was a monster who had lived for hundreds of years and was considered the strongest within Predator. Indeed. Even after entering the Transcendent Realm and training for a year in the Chairman's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, he felt himself being overwhelmed by her presence alone.

"...It's been a while."

When they last met in Trabia, Jeniela had been quite friendly. Would she be the same now? Lee Sungmin gauged her mood and offered a greeting first.

"You've changed a lot," she observed. "So much that I couldn't recognize you by your presence alone."

To be honest, Jeniela had thought a Dragon had appeared. The sudden fluctuation in space was clear evidence of Spatial Movement, a magic that only Dragons or transcendent beings of a similar level could perform. Even she was incapable of such a perfect feat.

"Just now... what was that? I definitely detected a Spatial Movement."

"I didn't do it. I just received some help."

"Help...?" Jeniela narrowed her eyes at his answer, staring at him intently. Her two eyes, engraved with the Demonic Eye, opened wide. The All-Seeing Demonic Eye, which could see through everything about an opponent, was now fixed on Lee Sungmin.

"You... you have the protection of the Fairy Queen. I never thought I'd meet a human who rides a Fairy Horse... No, should I even call you human?"

Jeniela had completely grasped his condition. She knew he had used a Fairy Horse to travel here. She saw that his body had mutated to become something close to a Yokai, and that his heart had been replaced with a Dragon's. Her questions were answered, but the knowledge only left her more confused.

"...Hmm." Jeniela continued to study him with narrowed eyes before her lips parted.

"......"

Her lips lifted slightly into a smile.

"You've become quite delicious."

Between her parted, plump lips, sharp fangs gleamed.
