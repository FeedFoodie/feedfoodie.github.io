The Mages Guild was often represented by the Magic Tower, but the Tower was not its entirety. In fact, most mages living in Eria were registered with the Guild, drawn by the many advantages it offered. The Magic Tower itself was merely a research organization where some of these mages gathered, and many more chose not to belong to it at all.

Among those who operated outside the Tower were mages who specialized in combat. These combat mages, while belonging to the Guild, were not affiliated with the Magic Tower but with another institution entirely. They were the Mage Army, individuals who immersed themselves in practical, battle-oriented magic. Whenever trouble arose within the Mages Guild, they were the first to act.

In Hedor, a city deep in the North, the chilling cold was a constant presence, a reminder of how close it was to Trabia, the northernmost city of all. It was here in Hedor that the Magic Tower Masters of the Mages Guild had gathered, and with them, the Mage Army itself. They had come to punish a mage who had strayed from the proper path.

Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master, broke the silence. His cold eyes swept over the four mages seated before him. "Kim Jonghyun."

"The fact that he, the Black Magic Tower Master, has broken a taboo is hardly surprising," Lloyd continued.

"It's something of a tradition for the Black Magic Tower Master," an old man with a bushy beard chuckled. It was the Green Tower Master. "You know, Preskan, the Master before the last. Didn't he also break a taboo and get exiled? It's a shame we couldn't kill him back then."

"Let's not talk about that." A flicker of irritation crossed Lloyd's face. Ever since Preskan's exile, Lloyd had been under orders to kill the man and had spent a long time tracking him. A few years ago, he had even discovered Preskan's Dungeon and launched an attack, but he had failed.

"I'm not blaming you," the Green Tower Master said. "I'm just saying it's not unusual for a Black Magic Tower Master to fall to taboos. Once you're directly connected to a Demon Lord, your soul is bound to be corrupted."

"Kim Jonghyun's corruption is different from Preskan's case," Lloyd said with a sigh. "Kim Jonghyun has the Grimoire."

"Isn't that why we've all gathered here?" The Green Tower Master grinned, revealing yellowed teeth.

The Black Magic Tower was different from the others in many ways. All who belonged to it were Black Mages, and they kept to themselves, rarely interacting with the other Magic Towers. This did not mean the Mages Guild ignored them. On the contrary, the Black Magic Tower received the most surveillance and control of all.

"Are you certain that Kim Jonghyun has the Grimoire?" the White Tower Master asked.

"I'm certain." Lloyd nodded firmly. The Grimoire, a magic tome that every Black Mage dreamed of and yearned to possess.

"Why was the Grimoire guided to Kim Jonghyun? Its master was surely Arbes."

"It's unlikely that Arbes gave the Grimoire to him," the Green Tower Master muttered, stroking his beard. "But it was clearly written in the Gries. The master of the Grimoire had changed to Kim Jonghyun."

Originally, the Grimoire had been the property of the Mages Guild. Gries was its twin magic book, and the Guild had long worked to decipher them both. The two tomes were considered the property of the entire Guild, and no single individual was permitted to possess them or learn the magic written within, but in the past, Arbes, a Guild member, had become a Lich and vanished with the Grimoire. Soon after, on the first, previously blank page of Gries, new letters had been inscribed. The name 'Arbes' was engraved there.

"Arbes joined Predator after taking the Grimoire, but he didn't commit any evil acts with it," Lloyd mused. "I suppose even the great Arbes couldn't properly wield its magic."

"Perhaps Kim Jonghyun has become the master of the Grimoire, but he might not be able to use its magic properly either," the White Tower Master suggested.

"But that's not what's important," Lloyd said, his voice grim. "The fact that Kim Jonghyun, the Black Magic Tower Master, has obtained the Grimoire is what matters."

Like all mages of the Black Magic Tower, Kim Jonghyun was under the constant surveillance of the Mages Guild. A location-tracking spell placed on him was still reliably reporting his position. Until recently, he had not caused any major problems. He diligently reported to the Guild when he was away and even submitted periodic reports on his research. This did not mean the Guild trusted him, however. Ever since Arbes had betrayed them to become a Lich and join Predator, the Mages Guild had not trusted any Black Mage. It was a justifiable distrust, especially after Preskan, the Master before the last, had also betrayed them by becoming a Lich.

"The tracking magic is still functioning," the White Tower Master noted. "It's probably a trap."

Kim Jonghyun knew he was being tracked. As a Black Mage of the Guild, he would have known that possessing the Grimoire would cause his name to be written in the Gries, yet the tracking magic remained, as if to lure them here.

"The forces of the Mage Army will arrive soon," said a mage who had been silent until now. He was a representative of the Mage Army, present until the main force arrived. "I think it's better to see this as an opportunity. When Arbes stole the Grimoire, there were no mages superior to him, but that's not the case now, is it? Once the entire Mage Army arrives, we'll be able to kill Kim Jonghyun and take back the Grimoire."

"Perhaps Arbes is with him," the White Tower Master worried.

"It's been a long time since Arbes was called a great mage," the young man scoffed. "Hundreds of years have passed. The skill level of the Guild's mages has risen considerably."

*So young.*

The thought passed through the minds of both the Green Tower Master and Lloyd as they watched the young mage speak with such conviction. It was not as easy as it sounded. Even if the Guild's power had grown, Arbes, who had practiced magic for centuries, was not an opponent to be taken lightly. Of course, none of them knew that Arbes had already been stripped of all his magic by Kim Jonghyun.

"I don't want to risk my life," the Crimson Archmage, Scarlet, announced, an annoyed expression on her face as she crossed her legs. "I didn't even want to come here in the first place. I was only dragged along because the Golden Magic Tower Master forced me." She grumbled to herself about why the Red Magic Tower had to be right next to the Golden one. "Besides, none of the other Magic Tower Masters even came, did they? Well, I guess they were worried about the potential danger. I'm the same. I have to go since I was dragged here, but if my life is in danger, I'll run away without hesitation."

She had already taken out an insurance policy for that. Scarlet thought of Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo. She had notified them through the bracelets they wore, but would they really arrive in time? Scarlet was skeptical. Eria was vast. If they were far from Devon, it would be difficult to make it.

"Run away! And you call yourself the Red Magic Tower..." the young mage began.

"Hey, little mage," Scarlet shot back, her tone scathing. "Sorry, but I'm more important to myself than the title of Magic Tower Master, and are we comrades or something? The Guild is just a place where people with similar goals gather for profit. Why are you forcing me to die for the Guild?"

Her words left the young mage speechless.

"Don't be so harsh," Lloyd interjected. "I also feel sorry for forcing you to come when you didn't want to."

"Don't give me that crap. Sorry my ass."

"...Ahem. I'm not forcing you to risk your life. If the situation becomes dangerous..."

"Enough," Scarlet cut him off. "I'll do what I have to do to survive. That bastard Kim Jonghyun, why'd he have to stir up shit and make me come all the way out here?" She grumbled and looked down at the bracelet on her wrist. It was a promise made a few years ago, after she had trained with Baek Sogo and Lee Sungmin at Mount Mushii. If she ever found herself in danger, they would come and help her. She had given them bracelets identical to her own, allowing them to pinpoint her location no matter where they were.

*But the world is wide.*

Scarlet chewed on her lower lip. She had no idea where Baek Sogo was, but she knew Lee Sungmin's location. Haras, a place very, very far from here. Even if he left right now, it would take him months to reach Hedor.

"......"

Lloyd's eyebrow twitched. He pressed his fingertips to his ear, listening to a voice from a communication spell. A moment later, he spoke with a relieved expression. "It seems the Murim Alliance will be sending personnel as well. Since the Moyong Clan is not far from here, warriors from their clan and reinforcements from the Murim Alliance will be arriving."

"Kekeke! The Mage Army, Magic Tower Masters, and even martial artists from the Murim Alliance just to catch one Black Mage?" the Green Tower Master cackled. "With this many troops, we could probably even catch a Dragon."

Though he said it as a joke, whether they could actually catch a Dragon was another matter. It was, however, an undeniable fact that this was an excessive amount of force to deal with a single Black Mage.

"The Demon King of the North. That's what they're calling him," the White Tower Master muttered.

"He deserves it," the Green Tower Master grinned. "Do you know how many people that bastard killed in a week? Seven villages were annihilated at his hands. We don't know if their deaths are related to the Grimoire's magic, but Kim Jonghyun, whatever that bastard is thinking, he's too extreme."

"Furthermore, this area is the Vampire Queen's territory," Lloyd added.

At those words, even the snickering Green Tower Master wiped the smile from his face. To them, the Vampire Queen, a monster among monsters who had lived for hundreds of years, was a far more terrifying existence than a mage who had annihilated seven villages in a week.

"The Vampire Queen is in Trabia," the White Tower Master said.

"That may be so, but shouldn't we consider the entire North to be effectively under her influence?" Lloyd countered. "The fact that she has remained silent even though Kim Jonghyun has committed massacres here must mean there's something between them."

"Are you saying Kim Jonghyun has joined Predator?"

"Perhaps he has become a Lich without our knowledge."

The possibility silenced the mages. Becoming a Lich meant becoming inhuman, and it meant the conditions for joining Predator had been met.

"What should we do if the Vampire Queen intervenes?" the young mage asked, his earlier confidence gone.

"We run," Scarlet answered as if it were obvious. "Vampire Queen Jeniela is a monster among monsters who has lived for hundreds of years. Besides, she has plenty of powerful vampires under her command. I have no desire to die. In fact, I want to wash my hands of this ridiculous manhunt right now."

"The Queen will not intervene," the White Tower Master said, his face pale. "They may be grouped together as Predator, but they are inhuman monsters. They won't have any loyalty to each other."

"But they aren't hostile to each other either," Lloyd pointed out. "The Queen condoned the massacres Kim Jonghyun committed in her territory."

"She's probably just not interested."

Listening to them, Lloyd pressed his temples. After a brief silence, he cautiously brought up another topic. "You have connections to the Church, don't you? Just in case, could you ask the Holy Knight Regiment and the Priests for help?"

"What? That's..."

"Our opponent might be an Undead," Lloyd reasoned. "If they help, things might go more smoothly, don't you think?"

"I understand," the White Tower Master said, nodding with an uncertain expression. "I'll try making a request."

The forces were swelling. Scarlet sighed and rested her chin on her hand.

*If the Murim Alliance is coming, I can't ask for help.*

Scarlet knew the rumors. She knew Baek Sogo had left the Murim Alliance, and she knew Lee Sungmin had become the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Putting Baek Sogo aside, it was now completely impossible to ask Lee Sungmin for help. In truth, Scarlet didn't fully believe the story that he had killed Moyong Seojin and Jaegal Taeryeong. If he had, there must have been a reason, but even so, with warriors from the Moyong Clan coming as reinforcements, she absolutely could not call for him.

*It can't be helped.*

Scarlet let out a deep sigh and ruffled her hair. Unwilling to endanger Lee Sungmin for her own safety, she pretended to be unaware of the bracelet on her wrist.
