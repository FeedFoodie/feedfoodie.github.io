Lee Sungmin had harbored a quiet worry that the Murim Alliance might have attacked in their absence, but a wave of relief washed over him as he saw the carriage, still parked exactly where they had left it.

He found the scene peaceful. Yehwa stood guard, a silent sentinel patrolling the surroundings just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had ordered. In the driver's seat, Scarlet sat leisurely, engrossed in a book. The tranquil sight eased the tension in his shoulders.

"Coalition Leader."

Yehwa's voice cut through the quiet, and she was the first to spot their approach. At her call, Scarlet looked up from her book, snapping it shut. Her eyes then widened as they fell upon the figure floating beside the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"What is that?"

"It's the Murim Alliance Chairman."

The words sounded absurd even to his own ears. Not even half a day had passed since he and the Chairman had stormed the Murim Alliance headquarters in Kron. In that short span, they had left a trail of chaos, knocking out most of the city's martial artists until they foamed at the mouth. They had shattered the great Dog Beating Stick Formation, a technique deployed by a hundred members of the Beggars' Gang and their Chief. Now, the warriors of the Murim Alliance lay unconscious, and their leader, the Black Dragon Knight, had been subdued and dragged here like a sack of grain.

"...Is that even allowed?" Scarlet stammered, her face a mask of disbelief. Though she belonged to the Mages Guild, she was not ignorant of the delicate balance between the Murim and Unorthodox-Demonic Alliances. To subdue the Chairman himself and drag him away was an act that defied all common sense, even to her.

Lee Sungmin offered no answer. The one responsible for this entire mess was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"There's no reason why not."

The Chairman wrenched open the carriage door. He unceremoniously threw the Black Dragon Knight's body inside and ordered their departure. He gave no destination, but none was needed. Yehwa already knew to guide the carriage toward Trabia, where they were scheduled to meet the Vampire Queen, Jeniela, and hear of the future she had foreseen.

A flicker of discomfort crossed Scarlet's face, but the alternative was sitting in the driver's seat, so she climbed into the carriage. Her nose wrinkled at the sight of the dried blood caked around the Black Dragon Knight's mouth and chin.

"He's not dead, is he?"

"It would be a waste to kill him," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, sitting down opposite his captive. Lee Sungmin took a seat beside the Black Dragon Knight just as the carriage lurched into motion.

The Chairman raised a hand and grabbed the Black Dragon Knight's wrist. A fierce surge of energy pulsed through the captive's body, and the Black Dragon Knight's eyes flew open with a jolt. A thin scream escaped him as he coughed violently, spitting a spray of blood that had clogged his throat. The crimson droplets flew toward the Chairman, only to evaporate inches from his nose.

Lee Sungmin watched, a flicker of disappointment passing through him. *It would have been quite amusing to see his face covered in blood.*

[You think a master of his level couldn't dodge that?]

*You seem to acknowledge him, then?*

[Of course I acknowledge him. Anyone who can shatter Dragon scales with his bare hands is worthy of sharing a drink with this Elder. If I had met that fellow when I was alive, I would have made him my drinking buddy.]

The Void Monarch seemed quite impressed with the Chairman's martial prowess. Lee Sungmin couldn't help but feel a little sullen at the praise. The shift in his emotions was immediately felt by the Void Monarch, who cackled mockingly.

[You brat. Are you jealous?]

*Why would I be jealous?*

[Because I've never said anything like that about you. You... hmm. You wouldn't make a drinking buddy, but you might have passed for a squire I'd take along.]

*Shut up.*

[Why are you getting pissed at me for your own weakness and incompetence?]

Lee Sungmin ignored the Void Monarch's sneering retort.

Meanwhile, gasping for breath, the now-conscious Black Dragon Knight frantically assessed his surroundings. He tried to speak, but the words wouldn't form properly around the fresh wound in his caved-in chest. It was an injury so severe that only the tough body of a Dragon had prevented his certain death from delayed treatment.

The Chairman tossed a small bottle toward him. "Drink."

The Black Dragon Knight caught the Elixir and, after a moment's hesitation, drank it obediently. His acupoints remained unsealed, a deliberate choice by the Chairman. It was a form of psychological pressure, a constant reminder that any attempt to escape or attack the Phantom Spear beside him or the mage across from him would be utterly meaningless. The message was received. The Black Dragon Knight knew he would be subdued or killed before he could even complete the thought. In the end, he gave up all resistance and drank.

After his wounds had healed, he let out a long sigh.

"...What do you want with me?"

"That depends on what you can give me." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's mask was still off, a clear sign he was prepared for any resistance. The psychological pressure was working perfectly. The Black Dragon Knight lowered his gaze and muttered.

"Then first, tell me what you demand."

"I have a few questions. It would be wise to answer them honestly."

"...In this situation, I have no intention of lying."

"One can never know. Do you want to live?" the Chairman asked, his gaze turning cold. A surge of anxiety shot through the Black Dragon Knight, and he nodded quickly.

"I swear to tell the truth."

His oath did nothing to change the Chairman's expression. Lee Sungmin watched the Black Dragon Knight with careful eyes. He had heard about Heaven Beyond Heaven from the Fist Honored One, but their positions were different. The Black Dragon Knight would surely be able to speak more accurately about the truths the Fist Honored One did not know.

"The goal of Heaven Beyond Heaven is a contradiction," the Chairman began. "They aim to eliminate all inhuman beings from this world, leaving only humans. Mutants like Elves might just fall into that category, but you, Black Dragon Knight, do not. How can a half-human, half-dragon possibly be called human?"

"...That's true."

"So why do you follow their goal?"

"What I follow is not the goal of Heaven Beyond Heaven, but the Martial God," the Black Dragon Knight answered, closing his eyes. "A hundred years ago, I met the Martial God. To be precise, he came looking for me. At the time, I... was searching for traces of any Dragon other than myself."

"......"

"The Martial God told me that Dragons no longer exist in this world. He said they had already abandoned this world hundreds of years ago and left for another dimension."

At those words, both Lee Sungmin and the Void Monarch were stunned. The whereabouts of the Dragons were a complete mystery. All that was known was that they had suddenly vanished several hundred years ago, around the same time as the fighting spirit.

"Of course, I didn't believe him. I told him not to spout such nonsense and challenged him to a fight... but I was defeated. To me, the vanquished, the Martial God revealed the truth. The Dragons had abandoned this world. Realizing they could no longer live here, they had all migrated to another dimension together." As if recalling a distant memory, the Black Dragon Knight's voice grew melancholic. "For me, it was a devastating truth. As a half-human, half-dragon, I couldn't live as a human, and I wanted to meet other Dragons no matter what. ...To be honest, a part of me also wanted to meet the father who abandoned my mother and me. Though I knew it was meaningless now."

As he spoke, the Black Dragon Knight's gaze drifted to Lee Sungmin, finally settling on the Magic Armor he wore.

"...I'm just asking, but that armor..."

"It's made of Dragon scales," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman answered for him.

Lee Sungmin shot a resentful glare at the Chairman, his eyes screaming, *Why did you have to say that?* The Dragon Heart Lee Sungmin had consumed, the very scales woven into his armor, had belonged to the Black Dragon Knight's father.

At the Chairman's answer, the Black Dragon Knight's shoulders trembled. From his perspective, Lee Sungmin was his mortal enemy, a man who had devoured his father's heart and fashioned clothes from his hide.

"...Grrr..."

As a low growl escaped the Black Dragon Knight, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman reacted instantly. He raised a hand and struck him across the head.

"......"

With a sharp crack, the knight's head was forced down. Humiliation burned within him, but he quietly suppressed his anger. In this carriage, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman held all the power.

"...Any... way. The Martial God swore that if I followed him, he would one day send me to the world where the Dragons migrated."

"Is that why you follow him?"

"Just as I am doing now, the Martial God also swore an oath on it."

"Do you think it's possible for his words to come true?"

"I do," the Black Dragon Knight said without the slightest hesitation. "Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, I admit that you possess an overwhelming martial prowess that seems beyond human... but the Martial God has other secrets besides his strength. He knows about the end of this world, and he is preparing for it."

When he spoke of 'the end', the Black Dragon Knight glanced around nervously. It seemed he also knew about the Apostle of the End. His behavior told them a great deal. The fact that the Martial God was preparing for the end meant he was getting ready to confront The End itself.

"There's no point in asking about 'the end'," Lee Sungmin spoke up. He had experienced this several times before. Talking about The End was taboo. If one spoke of it, the Apostle of the End would cut off the conversation. Abel had used Gries' magic to block the intervention back in the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, but Abel was not with them now.

"Where is the Martial God?" the Chairman asked after a moment of thought.

The Black Dragon Knight shook his head. "I don't know. ...The Martial God... left to meet the Little Heavenly Demon, but contact has been lost since then. I don't know where he is either."

"When was the last time you were in contact?"

"The Hyujael Mountains."

Thanks to his oath, the Black Dragon Knight was speaking only the truth. The name of the mountains sparked a memory in Lee Sungmin. The South, Urmuri, Yana, the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. The words connected in his mind, and his expression hardened. The Hyujael Mountains... that was where Yana had gained her power as the Nine-Tailed Fox.

*The place where the Demonic Spirit is.*

He said contact with the Martial God was lost there. The Martial God had left to meet Wiji Hoyeon... so for him to have gone to the Hyujael Mountains... didn't that mean Wiji Hoyeon was there?

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman glanced at Lee Sungmin's stiffening face. "Did he capture the Little Heavenly Demon?"

"I don't know. The Martial God didn't say anything about it. However... I believe he failed. If he had captured her, there's no way he wouldn't have made contact."

"Why are you people so obsessed with the Little Heavenly Demon?" the Chairman asked, crossing his arms.

The question made the Black Dragon Knight, who had been answering steadily, freeze. The reason Heaven Beyond Heaven was obsessed with Wiji Hoyeon carried a completely different weight than the questions he had answered so far.

"If... if I answer this question..." The Black Dragon Knight had sworn to tell the truth. This allowed him to postpone his answer slightly by saying something else. "The Martial God might kill me."

"Is that my problem?" the Chairman asked with a smile.

The Black Dragon Knight bit his lower lip hard. If he answered, the Martial God would kill him. If he kept avoiding the answer, he would die because of his oath. There was no way out. Just as the cornered knight was steeling his resolve, the Chairman spoke again in a mocking voice.

"Will you come to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?"

"...Wha... at...?"

"If you no longer need to follow the Martial God, then come to us. I can at least protect your life."

"Are you telling me to betray the Martial God right now?"

"Only if you no longer hold any lingering attachment to what he promised you."

The Black Dragon Knight's eyes trembled violently. Migrating to the other dimension where the Dragons were. That was the promise. He couldn't give that up so easily. Seeing his hesitation, the Chairman spoke again.

"What are you so afraid of? Do you think I'll go blabbing to the Martial God that you said something you shouldn't have?"

"...That's... not the problem. Even if you don't tell him, and I don't say anything... the Martial God will know of my betrayal."

"Does the Martial God have clairvoyance or something?"

"Something like that," the Black Dragon Knight muttered gloomily. "...Damn it."

He had no choice. Either way, only death awaited him. Of course, he didn't want to die. So what could he do? He looked at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman with anxious eyes.

"...If I join you. Will you really protect me?"

"As much as I can."

At the Chairman's noncommittal answer, the Black Dragon Knight cursed again under his breath. After steadying himself, he finally spoke.

"There is a Spirit Medium by the Martial God's side."

"The reason we're obsessed with the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, is because of her. I don't know if what the Spirit Medium says is true... but the Martial God believes her words implicitly."

"A Spirit Medium..." The Chairman stroked his chin thoughtfully.

Watching his reaction, the Black Dragon Knight continued. "The Little Heavenly Demon. She is... to borrow the words of the Spirit Medium, an absurd existence. Do you think it makes sense? For a human, one who hasn't even lived for thirty years, to trample upon beings who have walked the single path of martial arts for hundreds of years."

At those words, the Chairman glanced at Lee Sungmin. No, Lee Sungmin didn't count. He had received training from both Denir and Mushii, and had reached his current level thanks to the Black Heart and the Grace of Fate. The Little Heavenly Demon was different.

"Innate talent? Haha, that's ridiculous. You, the Martial God, and... even myself. We are all beings who have never considered ourselves lacking in talent, but the Little Heavenly Demon's is too much. It's too absurd. It's as if someone instigated her to become a more absurd existence than anyone else."

"...Is that the only reason you're obsessed with her?"

"Of course not. Her absurd talent wasn't enough to draw the full attention of Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Little Heavenly Demon... was simply necessary for us. Not her power, but her very existence. The Spirit Medium said that she is under the interference of a great law."

"A law? What's that?"

"I don't know. Neither the Spirit Medium nor the Martial God spoke about it. However... what I did hear was this." Having come this far, the Black Dragon Knight abandoned all lingering attachment to his promise with the Martial God. Now that he had spoken of the Spirit Medium, he could never return. "That by 'using' the Little Heavenly Demon, it's possible to interfere with the law."

"...A law..." Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice.

The Black Dragon Knight continued, his voice low. "It was such an incomprehensible statement that I also asked the Martial God about it. I asked what on earth he meant. Then he told me. When the time is right and the preparations are complete, it will be possible to interfere with the law by using the Little Heavenly Demon. To interfere with the absolute, unchanging laws that constitute this world and arbitrarily modify them."

"I see." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded slowly, his eyes glinting with understanding. "Now I understand why the Martial God adopted such an absurd ideal of eradicating all inhuman beings from this world."

Lee Sungmin understood as well. If the Martial God's words were true, if Wiji Hoyeon could truly be used to interfere with and arbitrarily modify the very laws of existence, then it meant anything was possible.

Even the law stating that inhuman beings existed in this world could be rewritten.
