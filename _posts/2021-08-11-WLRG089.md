Appealing to sentiment was a fool's errand. It was not such a shallow thought that crossed his mind, for even if they had trained together, sharing the space called Mount Mushii, no bond existed between Lee Sungmin and the Sword Phantom. The only people he had formed a connection with on that mountain were Baek Sogo and Scarlet.

In his memory, the Sword Phantom was a permanent fixture by the cliff's edge. He was a swordsman whose two eyes could not see, a man who stared down the dizzying precipice with an unseeing gaze. More than anyone else training on that mountain, he fit the image of a true ascetic, which was precisely why Lee Sungmin's impression of him was so deep.

However, he had to cast that image aside.

Perhaps a part of him had admired the Sword Phantom, the man who seemed more like a true ascetic than anyone. That admiration had to be discarded. What moved Lee Sungmin now was a contradictory sense of responsibility and the self-satisfaction that came with it.

The spear in his hand raged wildly as he unleashed the second form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Homing Chain Lightning. Its swaying tassel blurred his vision while dozens of spear images, each carrying lethal power, descended upon the Sword Phantom.

The wildly shaking tassel did not deceive the blind swordsman. In his right hand, he held a straight sword, a weapon much shorter than the spear Lee Sungmin wielded, but this proved no disadvantage.

KAKAKAKANG!

The sound of steel clashing against steel echoed endlessly. Lee Sungmin's hands moved without rest, his spear a flurry of dozens of movements. The Sword Phantom met the onslaught head-on, not retreating a single inch as sword and spear collided, the distance between them never closing.

*What kind of swordsmanship does he use?*

There was no time to study his opponent. Lee Sungmin focused solely on the movements of his spear, the basics of sweep, parry, and thrust melting into the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. A continuous barrage of lightning-fast strikes deflected the Sword Phantom's sword whenever it tried to slip past or stab at him. Seizing a momentary pause to draw breath, Lee Sungmin's spear stopped for a single instant before shooting forward. Soul Chasing Elimination was as fast as a lightning flash, yet no surprise showed on the Sword Phantom's face. The sword in his right hand spun. Gripping it in reverse, he swung it upward in a great arc that deflected Lee Sungmin's spear.

The Sword Phantom said nothing as the sun began to set. Lee Sungmin ignored the tingling in his grip and slammed the raised spear downward, turning it into a blunt weapon that fell toward the head of the advancing swordsman. The Sword Phantom's feet slid to the side. As he sidestepped, he swung his sword, aiming for Lee Sungmin's waist.

Just as the blade was about to tear into his side, Lee Sungmin's body blurred and vanished. The Traceless Step of Dancing Shadow Soul Break had triggered a teleportation.

The Sword Phantom did not panic. He simply altered the direction of his swing. Lee Sungmin, now several steps away, saw the sword aiming for his chest and his eyes widened. He pulled the spear into his chest and twisted.

JJEOOONG!

With a heavy sound, Lee Sungmin's body was thrown back. He staggered, forcing strength into his waist to keep from falling over, and tasted blood between his tightly clenched teeth.

Towards the blades that rushed him in succession, he unleashed his next form.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear: Third Form, Serpent Soul Devouring.

The spear scattered, then vanished. The form of the thrusting weapon was invisible. Was it the fluttering tassel that covered his eyes? Was he deceived? He had to discard his eyes, feel it with his senses. From below to above, the Sword Phantom threw his upper body back.

SHWAAAK!

Blood spurted from the tip of the Sword Phantom's nose. Had he been even a fraction of a second slower, the spear would have pierced him from under his chin to the crown of his head.

"Hahaha!"

The Sword Phantom, who had retreated a few steps, burst into loud laughter. He had never imagined a spear would shoot up from below. No, it was not just that. A simple upward thrust would not have wounded him. How many variations were contained within that single motion? The Sword Phantom nodded his head in pure admiration.

"Indeed. To think the ignorant brat from that mountain has grown so much."

He wiped the wound on his nose with the back of his hand. It was strange. Though it was a shallow cut that had definitely broken the skin and drawn blood, the wound was no longer there when he was done.

"I'm envious," the Sword Phantom said in a small voice. He gripped his sword with both hands, lowering his upper body halfway and holding the upright blade horizontally. His posture looked like a predator preparing to pounce.

"I'm envious of your talent."

The words were heavy, lodging in his throat.

[Ah.]

Kim Jonghyun's voice brushed past Lee Sungmin's mind.

[I see. I was wondering why you didn't run, why you insisted on fighting here...]

The Sword Phantom ran, his speed incomparably faster than before. In an instant, he vanished from Lee Sungmin's sight. Lee Sungmin moved according to instinct rather than trying to follow with his head and eyes.

PAAK!

His left shoulder was cut. Feeling fortunate that the pain was only a sting, Lee Sungmin twisted his waist and escaped by creating an afterimage with One Step Dancing Shadow.

"It is night," the Sword Phantom spat out.

In the darkness, the light in the Sword Phantom's eyes was as red as blood. His movements were as free as if he were swimming through the night, and the sword strikes that rained down from all directions were an absolute death, carrying the weight and finality of a guillotine's blade.

KWADEUK!

The spear in Lee Sungmin's hand broke. He had tried to block the Sword Phantom's sword with the shaft, but it was impossible.

[Be careful. The night is his time.]

There was no time to answer Kim Jonghyun. Lee Sungmin hurriedly pulled a new spear from the Subspace Pocket at his waist.

[He is a Vampire.]

He was not unaware. The inhuman race known as Vampires was that famous. They were monsters that were once human, monsters that grew stronger as the sun set and could not live without drinking blood.

Staggering in the darkness, the Sword Phantom's two eyes glowed red. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, tightly gripping the new spear. The Sword Phantom's shoulders shook with laughter as he opened his mouth.

"Do I look pathetic in this form?"

Between his parted lips, pointed fangs were visible. Vampires were famous, but this was the first time Lee Sungmin had ever seen one.

"...Since when?"

"After I came down from the mountain," the Sword Phantom said, looking up at the sky. "It was when I fell into despair over the limits of my vessel. I... I hated growing old. I wanted to do more, swing my sword more, go further, but my body was already aging. It was holding me back when I wanted to advance."

"I swung a sword for fifty years. I believed I had great talent, and I thought I would one day become the world's number one. Huhu... huhuhu. I was small. I was a clueless fool who didn't know how vast the world was. To think I hit a wall ten years ago and couldn't even find a way to overcome it."

So that was why he became a Vampire.

"I had to overcome old age, but with my small vessel, Rejuvenation was an impossible dream. I needed more... more time. So I searched for a way to overcome time, but... even after becoming this thing, I'm still not satisfied."

The Sword Phantom glared at Lee Sungmin with blazing red eyes.

"By ceasing to be human, I gained time, but my body is still old. I need more... more blood."

[It seems the Vampire who sired the Sword Phantom is not a very powerful being. Either that, or they have no interest in the Sword Phantom.]

[What do you mean?]

[If one is turned by drinking the blood of a superior, even old age can be overcome. The rewards for abandoning humanity are that sweet, but the Sword Phantom still has an old body, does he not? He either became the fledgling of an unimpressive Vampire, or... he did not receive enough blood and only underwent a minimal transformation. It must be one of the two.]

"What a beautiful moon," the Sword Phantom said. "The moonlight was beautiful when I killed Daoist Seongha, too... It was a truly wonderful night."

"...The Daoists of Mount Hua will come for you for killing their Elder."

"It's already too late to turn back," he replied. "The moment I crossed the mountain gate and challenged Daoist Seongha, there was no going back for me. It's night... I'm glad. I can rampage even more. I can drink more blood. How much more must I drink to regain my youth?"

The Sword Phantom laughed at the moon, a madness devoid of humanity. Or perhaps it was just a blind pursuit of his desire. *I'm envious of your talent.* The Sword Phantom's words echoed in Lee Sungmin's head.

[Even if the Sword Phantom's power as a Vampire isn't that strong, a Supreme Peak master who is also a Vampire is an extremely dangerous existence. Especially tonight, with a full moon. The plan has failed. You must let the Sword Phantom go for now and aim for the daytime.]

"Are you afraid of me?" the Sword Phantom asked, looking at Lee Sungmin.

Whooong...

A crimson current swirled around his sword. Soon, it became a red, glowing enhanced sword qi.

[...The Sword Phantom doesn't seem to have any intention of letting you go. I suppose it can't be helped. Try to hold on a little longer. The Daoists from Mount Hua will be here soon.]

That was easier said than done. A Supreme Peak master and a Vampire, blessed by the full moon. For Lee Sungmin, who was barely at the threshold of Supreme Peak, fighting him was impossible.

However, Lee Sungmin did not want to run. He steadied his breathing and drew up his inner qi. The power surged from his qi center and circulated through his body, and a purple aura coiled around the spear in his hands.

It was inefficient. Both Namgung Heewon and Jihak had pointed that out to him. It could not be helped. With his mind, spirit, and body all tangled, Lee Sungmin was not very adept at handling inner qi, yet the fact that he could wield enhanced qi despite not having reached the Supreme Peak was also proof that the insights he had gained from his fight with Aine were real.

"...Astonishing."

The Sword Phantom smiled, showing his fangs as he looked at the purple enhanced qi surrounding Lee Sungmin's spear.

"This fight with you seems like it will be fun."

The sword danced.

Wrapped in crimson enhanced sword qi, the blade was as wicked as a will-o'-the-wisp crossing the darkness. It slid and then vanished, blurring the light and dazzling the eyes, all while containing the ferocious power to destroy and slash everything it touched.

The moment they both began using enhanced qi, the weight of a wound changed drastically. Where a grazing blow in their previous exchange would only cut the skin, now even a graze became a fatal wound. That was the nature of such an attack. It turned every strike into a finishing blow.

However, a Supreme Peak master had countermeasures.

*Protective Qi Aura...!*

A thin red aura enveloped the Sword Phantom's body. Being able to freely deploy a Protective Qi Aura was proof of reaching the Supreme Peak. Lee Sungmin, who was only forcing the use of enhanced qi through his insights, could not use it.

That created a huge difference between them. If Lee Sungmin took even a single hit, he would be fatally wounded and die, but the same was not true for the Sword Phantom.

Even so, Lee Sungmin's Spear Technique demonstrated sufficient power. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, augmented with enhanced qi, displayed incomparable might that sealed the Sword Phantom's movements. The Sword Phantom responded to his spear with minimal motions. Was it because Lee Sungmin's spear was overwhelming him? No.

The Sword Phantom was enjoying it.

He was like a predator playing with its prey. He toyed with Lee Sungmin's Spear Technique, pretending not to see the openings that appeared. As the number of exchanges increased, Lee Sungmin realized it. The Sword Phantom was having fun, and he was not.

It was an incredibly

*Fucking awful.*

That was how it felt.

*Come to think of it, I have such bad luck.*

He had trained at Shaolin for four years. Dueling with Jihak every day had considerably stabilized his martial arts. He had already broken through the wall of the peak realm and was heading toward the Supreme Peak. Considering his age, a martial artist who had reached such a level was rare. Only a few, like the heirs of prestigious clans such as the Namgung Clan, or the direct disciples of great sects like the Nine Sects One Gang, would be on par with or ahead of him.

Objectively speaking, Lee Sungmin was by no means weak. He possessed skills that would place him among the top of his peers. The reason he was plagued by an inferiority complex and self-loathing was because of the memories of his past life that shackled him, and at the same time, because of the many true calamities he had encountered.

That was why he was unlucky. If he had lived in a smaller world, seeing only smaller things, he would not have had to feel this self-loathing and inferiority, but his bad luck led him to meet a whole host of more incredible people.

He realized people did not change easily.

When he met Flam on Mount Mushii, and when he overcame Mushii's trial and stopped caring about him, Lee Sungmin had thought he had overcome his own inferiority complex.

He was wrong. Mount Mushii was small, and the inferiority he had felt on that mountain was also small. When he left that small world and encountered the larger one, he realized that his belief was a delusion.

His training at Shaolin had taught him to completely abandon that inferiority.

*So, did 'I' become stronger?*

No.

*'I' am still weak.*

This was not an inferiority complex or self-deprecation. For Lee Sungmin, it was simply a fact. *I am weak. Weaker than the Sword Phantom. That is why I cannot take responsibility for him killing Daoist Seongha. It is all because I am weak.*

*I don't want to be weak.*

*Really?*

The sensation of the hand gripping the spear felt unfamiliar.

*I know. I'm weak.*

The feeling transmitted to the fingers tracing the shaft.

*That's why I trained.*

The sensation in the toes of the right foot, stepping forward, supporting him.

"......"

The falling sword.

*Because I didn't want to remain weak.*

The thrusting spear.

*Because I didn't want to be ashamed.*

The Sword Phantom's chest was pierced by the spear.
