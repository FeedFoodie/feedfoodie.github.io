The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman met the Moon Queen's words with a booming laugh, yet her expression remained utterly unshaken by his dismissal.

The sheer gravity of her claim, so sudden and absolute, was enough to fluster even the Martial God.

*The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is The End?*

Even if he wasn't The End itself, she had called him one of the axes leading to it.

Could it be true? The claim was too radical to accept without question, yet the Martial God felt his own heart leaning toward the impossible truth.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was simply too strong.

When he had emerged from a century of seclusion, the Martial God had believed without a doubt that he had reached the pinnacle of human strength. He had never tested himself against the monsters of Predator, but he was confident he could defeat even the near-immortal Vampire Queen with little difficulty.

He had been wrong. Far from facing the Vampire Queen, he hadn't even reached the pinnacle of humanity. When he finished his training, he had considered the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Sword Immortal to be his peers, but he had never once imagined being so thoroughly outmatched.

That strength... it was absurd, unnatural. It made no sense. *He can't possibly be stronger than me.* Not if he was human, but what if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman wasn't merely human? What if he was The End? If that were the case, then his ridiculous power would finally be understandable.

"Do you need a reason to convince yourself?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked the silent Martial God.

He knew escaping this space was impossible. Perhaps he could get out if he defeated them both, but in truth, he could have escaped earlier if he had truly wanted to. He could have thrown himself out instead of blocking Lee Sungmin's entry.

He didn't want to.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman clasped his hands behind his back and looked to the sky.

Yes, he didn't want to. Thinking back, it had all been an excuse. There had been no real need to cause such a commotion at the Murim Alliance with Lee Sungmin. He had simply wanted to face the Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation and fight the Black Dragon Knight who served as the Murim Alliance Chairman. He had deliberately provoked Jeniela for the simple reason that he wanted to fight her.

Staying here now when he could have left... yes, it was all because he had wanted to.

What did it matter? The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled. The reason wasn't important. He did it simply because he wanted to.

*Perhaps today is the day,* he thought, slowly turning his head.

Beyond the invisible wall, Lee Sungmin was screaming his lungs out. He poured out his Demonic Energy without restraint, creating a torrent of enhanced qi that slammed against the barrier, but the wall would not break.

This was a barrier much like the one the Demonic Spirit had used to trap the Martial God for months. Even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself would struggle to break it with his full power, making it an impossible task for Lee Sungmin.

*It seems he can't hear my Thought Transmission either.*

Though visible, the space itself was isolated, cutting off any mental connection. Escape was impossible unless the barrier itself was destroyed.

Not that he had any intention of escaping. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman lowered his gaze from the sky and bared his teeth in a grin at the Moon Queen and the Martial God.

"What are you doing?" he asked with a smile. "Didn't you say I'm an axis of The End? Are you just going to stand there and watch?"

*I should do as the Crimson Archmage asked,* the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman thought as he unclasped his hands.

RUMBLE...!

Thunder roared as black currents of electricity sparked and crackled all over his body.

The Martial God watched him with a tense expression. In truth, he was still skeptical. Even with the Moon Queen lending her power, he had no confidence he could kill this monster.

"It's alright," the Moon Queen said, as if reading his mind.

KIIING!

With an eerie sound, a white light enveloped her body. The power she drew forth was not inner qi. The Martial God startled at the foreign energy, a power eerily similar to that of the Demonic Spirit.

"What in the world is that..."

"The Spirit has lent it to me," the Moon Queen said, wrapped in the dazzling light. The bizarre flow of power even sparked a flicker of interest in the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

He smiled faintly and moved forward.

For an instant, the space was dyed white.

Then, it was plunged into pitch-black darkness as countless ink-black lines violated the light. The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, unleashed to its absolute limit, stripped the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman of his human form.

PAPAPAPAK!

A countless barrage of strikes hammered the Martial God's body, driving him back. Shock was etched deeply onto his face, impossible to conceal.

*That wasn't his top speed...?!*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was faster, even faster than before. The Moon Queen, wreathed in white light, was also pushed back by his offensive. Her surprise at his speed only served to ignite a sublime sense of mission within her.

The Spirit's words were true. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a human who had attained a martial prowess unthinkable for his kind, was a monster in his own right. He was undoubtedly an axis of The End, the true end of this world.

Therefore, he had to be stopped. The Moon Queen's hands glowed with an even brighter white light. She was a master who had perfected the art of ice, but the Chairman's speed was not something that could be caught with ice arts.

It was no problem. The current Moon Queen was seeing what the Martial God could not. She could perceive the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's super-speed, which was invisible to the Martial God's eyes. The authority of the Spirit now filled her, a power she had obtained in exchange for abandoning every martial art she had ever learned. After this, her body would become one that knew no martial arts, but the Moon Queen did not regret her choice.

JJEONG!

A palm from the Moon Queen intercepted the ink-black blur that relentlessly assaulted the world. The light palm strike elicited a flicker of surprise from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He looked down at his left hand, which he had thrust out to meet hers. It had collided with her palm of chilling frost, and a white rime now coated his own. It was proof that her strike had pierced his Protective Qi Aura and inflicted a blow of cold energy.

"......"

Until now, the Martial God's palm strikes had never reached him. The Moon Queen's had. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman found a sense of enjoyment in it. He shook his hand and smiled faintly.

KURURURUNG...!

Once again, thunder roared.

PAT.

The figures of the three vanished.

KWAAANG!

The Martial God's body was sent flying by a crash that shook the heavens and the earth. Swallowing the blood that rose in his throat, he kicked off the empty air and shot back in the opposite direction. His hands, moving so fast they left afterimages, gathered before his chest. A fist technique, thrust out as if to sever something, struck the empty air.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who had been dealing with the Moon Queen's palm techniques, immediately responded to the fist strike approaching from behind.

TADADADAK!

While parrying the Martial God's flashy fist techniques with only his right hand, he sent a knife-hand strike toward the Moon Queen with his left. She did not evade, instead reaching out with both hands to envelop his attack.

KIIIIING!

With an eerie sound, an absolute cold enveloped the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's left hand. His Protective Qi Aura was meaningless. The Moon Queen's attack, blessed by the Spirit, ignored it and froze his hand solid.

"Keuk!"

It was the Moon Queen, however, who let out a groan. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's foot had kicked her body. Normally, that single blow would have ruptured her internal organs and killed her, but she didn't die. The blessing bestowed by the Spirit for the sole purpose of killing him had barely closed the vast gap between them.

Though not on the same level, the Martial God was still strong. He grabbed the Chairman's right hand that was pressing down on him, twisted it, and pulled him in close. At the same time, he shot his right hand, wrapped in a blue light, toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's side.

At that extremely close range, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman reversed his posture and dodged the Martial God's hand.

KWAAAAANG!

The light fired by the Martial God struck the ground, creating a deep and massive crater. Heat rose from the gouged earth, rendering the cold of the North meaningless.

"Myriad Thunder," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered.

-KWA, KWA, KWA, KWAKWANG!

It was as if hundreds, thousands of thunderclaps were exploding at once. The sound alone shook the space. Ten thousand bolts of lightning erupted from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, sweeping across the area in all directions.

All the snow inside the barrier melted and evaporated, scorching the ground black. As if that wasn't enough, the residual electricity in the air gathered into a giant sphere at his gesture. The Martial God, who had been knocked to the ground by that terrifying power, staggered to his feet. The Myriad Thunder had slammed both him and the Moon Queen into the earth.

"It's not enough," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered. The power of the Moon Queen, blessed by the Spirit, was surprising, but it wasn't enough to kill him.

The Moon Queen could no longer maintain her composure. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman aimed the black sphere condensed in front of his palm downward.

"You two are not enough."

The sphere fell. As it descended, it exploded once more. The sky was no longer blue or white. It became a perfect night, and within that night, currents of electricity darker than the darkness itself flowed.

"Lightning Strike," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said with a faint smile.

Countless bolts of lightning fell.

AAAAAAH!

The Martial God roared, raising and spreading his arms. A tremendous power erupted from his body, swirling upward to meet the onslaught. Countless lightning bolts were caught in the flow of that power. All he could do was endure. It was impossible to return it or extinguish it. Tears of blood dripped from his bloodshot eyes.

The Moon Queen gasped for breath, raising her hands toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*Please, please.*

She called out to the Spirit in her heart. *Please, drag down this demon.* Her eyes turned pure white.

*It is an ancient law.*

The humans of this world cannot attain transcendence in the truest sense. This world was created based on that absolute law. This world has functioned according to the purpose of its existence. This place is a giant Slaughterhouse, a place where guinea pigs are put through the wringer to obtain perfected techniques.

No matter how strong the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power was, even if his actual martial prowess had already surpassed the level of transcendence, he was not a true transcendent. He was not a more absolute being. In the end, he was still human.

Therefore, this was.

"......"

A pure white light pierced through the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's body.

Floating in the air, his body staggered violently. He looked down at himself with a surprised expression as the lightning that had been raging around him stopped.

"...What is this."

For the first time, a hint of bewilderment colored his voice. His inner qi had stopped. His body felt heavy. His inner qi, which had been endlessly full just a moment ago, were now so depleted he could see the bottom. His body, once faster than anything, felt incredibly heavy. How long had it been since he had felt its weight?

"Ah!" the Moon Queen exclaimed, her face bright.

Neither the Martial God nor the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman knew what had happened, but she had felt it. The Spirit had answered her call. She immediately kicked off the ground. The Martial God didn't know what had just transpired, but he instinctively sensed that something was wrong with the Chairman. The immense power he had been gathering was fired directly at him.

KWAAAAANG!

The storm of power redirected by the Martial God engulfed the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. His expression hardened as he countered the force, but it wasn't enough. Neither his inner qi nor his body's movements were sufficient. For the first time since his fight began, he tasted blood in his mouth.

The Moon Queen rushed toward him as he was pushed back. She swung her pure white hands, intending to harm him. He swallowed the blood rising in his throat and thrust out his left hand.

PAAAAAT!

His palm strike and hers collided. The Chairman's hand, which was always wreathed in black electricity, was now unable to emit any light at all.

JJEOJEOK... JJEOJEOJEOK...! The air froze. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman watched as his entire left arm froze solid.

"The Spirit," the Moon Queen spat. "The Spirit desires your death. The Spirit has answered our wish to avoid The End."

"I see," he muttered, looking at his freezing arm. "I was wondering why this was suddenly happening. Hehe, the Spirit... so it's the Spirit. See? Wasn't I right in the end?"

A smile touched his face.

"You two were not enough."

JJEOOONG!

His left arm exploded. The fragments of the shattered limb were all frozen solid, his blood scattering like grains of ice. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman paid no mind to his missing arm and slowly extended his right hand.

The joy that had filled the Moon Queen's face at destroying his arm vanished, her expression stiffening. Before she knew it, his hand was resting on her chest.

"Moon Queen, even with you, it wasn't enough."

KUUUUNG!

The light palm strike made blood gush from her eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. As she fell, spewing blood, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman flicked his fingertips toward her. A thin current of electricity shot out in a straight line and pierced her body. Wrapped in the current, her body twisted and then exploded.

Letting her guard down at the last moment had cost the Moon Queen her life.

"Don't feel too bad." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman swallowed a mouthful of blood and glanced down at his missing left arm. The Martial God roared as he watched the Moon Queen explode without even leaving a corpse behind.

"Still, you took my left arm," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered, looking outside the barrier. He looked at Lee Sungmin, frozen with a horrified expression, and at Scarlet, who didn't know what to do.

*Not yet?* he thought with a chuckle.

"I didn't want to show you this side of me."
