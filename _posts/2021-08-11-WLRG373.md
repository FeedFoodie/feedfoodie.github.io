Lee Sungmin had no desire to fight Gemini. He did not know what the vampire was plotting, but he held to the old saying that the enemy of his enemy was his friend. While he had no intention of recruiting Gemini, he did not plan on killing him right away either. In the first place, that would be no easy task.

In terms of age alone, Gemini was comparable to the Blood Demon. To fight a vampire of that caliber on a moonlit night was out of the question, especially with Jeniela present somewhere on this mountain.

[If you back down here, things will end smoothly,] Gemini whispered into his mind. [I told you. The Queen has no intention of killing you. Even though she's trapped inside the space created by her Demonic Eye, she hasn't been rendered powerless. She could force her way out if she wanted to. She's just... choosing not to.]

The Sword Immortal was dead. A bitter regret washed over Lee Sungmin at the thought of his death. The man had failed to avenge his disciple and had ended up dying himself.

However, there was no time to dwell on the surging emotions. Aine's movements were a blur, while Chen and Kun relentlessly aimed for his blind spots. Lee Sungmin parried their attacks, his senses still stretched thin as he tried to pinpoint Gemini's location.

[I'll warn you again. Back off,] Gemini's voice insisted. [Don't make me step in because of my position.]

Lee Sungmin had no intention of fighting for long anyway. He had deliberately dragged out the battle for one reason, to buy time for the Wudang Sect's cultivators to descend the mountain and escape. He considered it a final show of respect for the Sword Immortal, who had died fighting Jeniela, and for Cheongmyeong, who had died in vain.

He had stalled long enough. By now, they should have made it down the mountain.

"Alright," Lee Sungmin replied with a nod.

At his quiet voice, Gemini chuckled. [Good thinking. As things stand, there's nothing you can do on this mountain. You can't stop Jeniela right now. Not that I think you'll have a way later either... but isn't this better than dying now?]

Listening to Gemini's taunts, Lee Sungmin slowly lowered his spear. The action was meant to show he had no hostile intent. Chen and Kun immediately stopped their movements, likely having received a signal from Gemini. Aine, however, was different. Still driven by a ravenous hunger, she lunged forward, intent on killing him.

In fact, Lee Sungmin was grateful for her foolishness. It would prevent any suspicion from Gemini.

He leaped into the air, dodging Aine's attack. Like a cat pouncing on a fishing line, she immediately jumped after him, soaring to the same height. Lee Sungmin glanced down. The distance was sufficient. He aimed his spear at the recklessly charging Aine.

He had already prepared a method. Violet Demonic Energy coiled around the tip of his spear and flared with intense light. When the point of his thrust reached Aine, the condensed energy swelled dramatically.

"......"

The bursting Demonic Energy engulfed her body.

The Illusionary World opened.

"Ah."

Watching from the darkness, Gemini's eyes widened. A thought flashed through his mind, and he hurriedly emerged from the shadows. He knew what that martial art was. He did not know its name, but he recognized the space that had trapped the Blood Demon, even if only for a moment. It was a technique that had given even the Blood Demon considerable trouble escaping on his own.

Gemini could not manage his usual smile. *Surely not.* Grinding his teeth, he shouted, "Stop him!"

At the loud command, Chen and Kun leaped upward toward the violet cloud hanging in the sky. Gemini took action himself. Crimson magic power gathered above his hand. When he opened his palm, the collected energy swelled to several times its size. He glared at the cloud and fired.

RUMBLE!

The magic tore through space and collided with the Illusionary World.

"Give Jeniela a message," a voice called from within the illusion. "Tell her I'm willing to negotiate."

"......"

The Illusionary World vanished. The condensed violet cloud scattered in all directions. Standing on the ground, Gemini looked up with a dreadful expression.

Lee Sungmin and Aine were gone.

{sep}

It was an undeniable fact that Aine's existence kept Jeniela from acting directly. What Jeniela wanted was not to become The End and destroy the world with her own hands. She wanted the future she had seen to be realized exactly as it was. There was no room for doubt about that. As long as she intended to make Aine the Slaughter Predator, Jeniela would not come to the forefront.

If Aine were to die, Jeniela would no longer have a reason not to act herself. That was not what Lee Sungmin wanted. What he wanted was time, and a variable.

"......"

Lee Sungmin and Aine fell from the Fairy Horse together. Not knowing what was happening, Aine screamed and swung her claws at him. He grabbed her wrists with both hands and slammed his forehead into her face. With a dull thud, her head snapped back.

"......"

Blood spurted from her crushed nose. Lee Sungmin twisted the wrists he held. The enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, gathered in his left hand, struck her solar plexus.

THUD!

Inside Aine's body, the strength control technique of the martial art erupted. Dark red blood gushed from her seven orifices. Lee Sungmin knew this blow had shattered her internal organs, but he also knew that just that much would not be enough to subdue her.

He flicked a finger toward the spear on the ground. It immediately flew into the air and shot toward Aine. Her mouth flew open as she spat blood. She struggled, hastily moving her hands to pull out the spear that had pierced through her armpit. Lee Sungmin kicked her down as she struggled and got to his feet.

[So you've arrived,] the Void Monarch said with a chuckle.

Lee Sungmin wiped the blood splattered on his face with his hand. He had pulled Aine into the Illusionary World, immediately summoned a Fairy Horse, and then grabbed her by the scruff of the neck to leap through space. He had been worried her struggling would make them fall, but he had succeeded in arriving safely at the Fairy Forest.

"What's going on?"

People gathered at the sudden commotion. Lee Sungmin approached the struggling Aine. No matter how powerful her regenerative abilities were, she could not heal properly as long as something pierced her. He unhesitatingly kicked her body as she tried to pull out the spear. With a terrible sound, Aine rolled across the ground.

"Aahk!" Teresa screamed.

It was not a pretty sight, but it could not be helped. Glancing back, he saw the Black Dragon Knight had already stepped forward to cover Teresa's eyes. The others wore expressions of confusion, but none tried to stop him. He drove the spear piercing Aine's body deep into the ground.

"Just stay still."

Aine's golden eyes flashed as she ground her teeth. Her body squirmed, and tentacles shot out. Lee Sungmin was faster. He grabbed the collar around her neck before the tentacles could reach him. Knowing full well that she would not die so easily, there was no mercy in his actions.

"......"

Aine's neck broke in his grasp. Her body went limp. It would regenerate before long.

"What's going on?" Osla appeared belatedly. The Fairy Realms who followed him stared with wide eyes at the struggling Aine. The unknowing fairies fluttered their wings and tried to approach her to welcome the new guest, but Osla raised a hand to stop them.

"Don't," he warned, before approaching Aine himself. He pouted and grumbled, "The Fairy Forest has too many guests now."

"I'm sorry," Lee Sungmin said, bowing his head with a bitter smile.

Osla snorted and waved his hand lightly. Glittering powder filled the air. It slowly drifted down, enveloping Aine's body. Her convulsing form slowly stilled. She looked back and forth between Lee Sungmin and Osla with an expression of disbelief before her trembling eyelids fluttered shut. Lee Sungmin stared at the sleeping Aine, his own expression incredulous.

"What's with that look?" Osla asked.

"How did you do that?"

"Do you think I'm the Fairy Queen just because my wings are the biggest? I'm the Fairy Queen because I have the authority that comes with it. There's almost nothing I can't do in this forest," Osla puffed out his chest boastfully.

Lee Sungmin pulled the spear from the sleeping Aine's body. She did not wake, but the wound from the spear regenerated rapidly before everyone's eyes. He was astounded by the speed of her regeneration.

"So, what is this?" Osla pressed for an answer.

Lee Sungmin looked around at the assembled people. Everyone was gathered, except for Scarlet at the Magic Tower and Yana at the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. Baek Sogo, the Black Dragon Knight, the Spear King, Teresa, Rubia, and Lloyd were all present. Among them, only Rubia and Lloyd had met Aine in person. As a result, the two of them were staring at her with shocked expressions.

Lee Sungmin explained to everyone why he had brought Aine here. If she died, there was a high probability that Jeniela would go on a rampage. However, he could not leave the girl, who had become a monster by devouring the Sword Immortal's corpse, under Jeniela's control. That was why he brought her here. The Fairy Forest was a place that even the immensely powerful Jeniela could not easily challenge. Since he had left room for negotiation through Gemini, he should be able to influence Jeniela's movements to some extent.

"Will the Vampire Queen agree to negotiate?" the Black Dragon Knight muttered with a frown. "She might come looking for her precious Pae Clan member since she was taken... but there's also a high chance she'll give up."

"If that happens, we can just kill her then," Lee Sungmin replied with an indifferent expression. The outcome would not change. If Jeniela went on a rampage without trying to find Aine, he would simply kill Aine. At the very least, that would completely eliminate the threat she posed.

"And if the Vampire Queen tries to negotiate?" the Spear King asked, looking down at Aine.

"The goal is to buy time. If Jeniela agrees to negotiate, we'll be able to buy that time."

"The time it takes to get from Wudang to the Fairy Forest? If the Queen is determined to come, it won't even take a month. What then? Are you planning to quietly hand that wench back to her when she arrives?"

"No. I have no intention of waiting for the Queen in this forest. I plan to prepare and then run from her."

At those words, the Spear King grinned. "You think that's possible?"

"We won't know until we try, but even if we have to fight, we should be able to create a favorable situation to some extent."

"The Queen can create her own situations too. What are you going to do if that wench chooses a full moon night to attack us?"

"We'll just have to hope that doesn't happen," Lee Sungmin said with a bitter smile. This was why the Void Monarch had said it might be a bad move. Now that he had taken Aine, Jeniela would try to find him by any means necessary.

"No, there's no need to run," Osla, who had been listening in silence, finally spoke up. "If a fight breaks out in this forest, I can actively intervene. I can't kill the Queen... but I can interfere. If you fight here, you'll have a decent chance of winning, even under a full moon."

Lee Sungmin did not want to get Osla involved. He had considered fighting here, but he did not want to endanger the Fairy Queen who had helped him many times. He also did not want to ruin the forest that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had loved.

"It's fine," Osla said, shaking his head before Lee Sungmin could speak. "It's to stop The End, isn't it? I told you, right? If The End begins and everything is over, I'll be in a state where I can't even die and will have to suffer through something terrible. I don't want that either. Now that things have come this far, there's no reason to save face."

"...I understand."

"I won't be fighting directly, though. I'm no good at fighting."

Lee Sungmin could not really imagine Osla fighting in person either.

[You don't plan on recruiting her?] the Void Monarch asked suggestively.

Lee Sungmin answered without a hint of hesitation.

*Never.*

It would not be possible in the first place.
