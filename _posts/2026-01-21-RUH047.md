"So that's where I belong now? He doesn't hate me?"

Left alone, Dan Yehwa stared dazedly at his room and muttered. A subtle, unknown emotion knocked on her heart and made it beat fiercely. A flush bloomed on her face; it felt like steam might rise from her head. It was the same as when she first met him, and again later when they met at the Bright Spring Parlor.

"It's evil arts. He must be using evil arts!"

She shook her head vigorously to erase the afterimage of his smile. Rumors said he had learned Bedroom Arts, and it had to be true. He had easily blocked her strike, no matter how flustered he was.

Bedroom arts. Seduction arts. _Evil_ arts.

No, it didn't matter what it was. All that mattered was that those feelings for him were false!

"Who, me? No? Absolutely not! Me, for a guy like that? That's ridiculous! Hmph!"

Dan Yehwa strode forward, vehemently denying it, but even as she did, she was swept by a longing to look back dozens, hundreds of times, as if he might be watching her from behind.

"I'm crazy, I'm crazy. Dan Yehwa. You're being crazy, right? Yes, that's right. I'm crazy. He has nothing going for him but his face."

He's a fiend of the highest order! Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso is a lecherous demon who sways women's hearts with his wicked smile! This was just a temporary feeling!

Dan Yehwa clenched her fists and tried hard to comfort herself.

"I'll go to the Valley of Sorrow. They say the Star of Black Flames was spotted there... I just have to meet him. That'll be enough!"

Declaring her ambition, she energetically unfolded her movement arts, and so, the distance between the two grew once more.

The academic world and the nation both agreed on one established theory: Exclude all emotion and passion from marriage.

Of course, whether that would work in the murim, and in the Shining Light Cult at that, remained to be seen.

{sep}

The Palace of Blades was distinctly different from the Palace of Merchants or the Palace of Bliss; it looked more like a temporary military encampment.

The palace was simple and concise. There was no garden. The only decorations were training tools. Shouts of exertion constantly erupted. The ground never cooled from the constant running of the Martial Sword Corps.

The residence of the Lord of Blades was clearly full of passion for martial arts.

A private training ground, a single-story house. A man mad for martial arts— the description perfectly suited Dan Jeonghak, the Lord of Blades.

Ah, but he did seem have one hobby: collecting famous swords.

The Palace of Blades housed an extraordinary obsession for martial arts.

_Even so... this training hall is a bit much, isn't it?_

I wasn't expecting any decent treatment. I had treated my fiancée poorly, so it was only natural for her family to be indignant. I'd silently endured the contemptuous gazes from the Martial Sword Corps on my way here, but they'd claimed the Lord was looking for me, only to bring me to an indoor training hall.

I looked around. All four walls were filled with menacingly sharp swords.

_Are they planning to kill me?_

My fiancée's older brother, Dan Jiho, slammed the door shut and left me alone. About a quarter of an hour passed.

Finally, he appeared.

@[《The Shining Light Cult Ranking List》

*   @[Official Name: Dan Jeonghak]@

*   @[Alias: the Sword Demon]@

*   @[Position: Lord of the Palace of Blades, one of the Seven Hallowed Palaces]@

*   @[Martial Arts Ranking: Fifth]@

*   @[Estimated Realm: Extreme Demon]@

*   @[Profile: "Life and Death by the Sword" is his personal philosophy. The sword comes before his family, and he finds all answers with the sword. Despite being a master who has reached the Extreme Demonic Realm, only a small household of less than three hundred belongs to the Palace of Blades. However, they are the Palace of Blades and the Martial Sword Corps, so in terms of individual military strength, they are comparable to the Shining Heavenly Demon Company. Dan Jeonghak is...]@

Information about him flashed through my mind, but facing him in person made it all feel meaningless. The countless modifiers used to describe him were pointless.

A sword.

He himself was a sword. He wasn't projecting his aura, yet he was so sharp the wind seemed to part before him. He gave an impression of indifference rather than coldness, and despite his simple attire, an extraordinary spirit radiated from the man.

"Sword Demon... Dan Jeonghak."

"It's been a while, Your Reverence."

He offered his greeting in a tone so plain that no emotion could be felt. His indifferent gaze remained fixed on me. His sharp aura was also directed at me. I felt as if I were being stripped bare before an invisible sword.

_To think I'd see the Sword Demon like this..._

The Shining Light Cult had once revealed the Sword Demon's existence to the Central Plains.

_In terms of swordsmanship alone, our Sword Demon is the best!_

The Murim Alliance used to say it was a ploy to diminish the prestige of my father, the Sword Venerable.

I thought there was some truth to that.

Ma Taepyeong and Yang Taehong— the Cult Leader and the Alliance Chairman, the Demon Supreme and the Sword Venerable. Amid their famous rivalry, a new topic of gossip had emerged: the Sword Demon versus the Sword Venerable.

Sure enough, others jumped on the bandwagon. Mount Hua's Sect Leader even changed his alias to 'Sword Saint' and boasted of being the Revered Demon Star of the Sword.

_But..._ **_his_** _isn't just a made-up alias._

Facing him in person, it was clear his alias wasn't a baseless boast like 'Sword Saint'.

"I heard you'd finished your secluded training. I brought you here so we can have a conversation, father-in-law to son-in-law."

"...Is that so?"

I may be the Heir to the Cult, but he was my future father-in-law. I answered with the utmost seriousness.

But instead of an answering, he...

_SWISH! CLACK._

…threw a sword at me.

"What's the meaning of this?"

"Let us see how much my son-in-law[^1] has improved."

Didn't he summon me here to _talk_ about his daughter? What was this all of a sudden?

Bemusement washed over me. The Sword Demon, however, simply extended his fingertips and beckoned me forward. It was a clear sign to stop talking and attack.

"Lord. Was this not going to be a conversation?"

"Is there a more sincere conversation than one held through swords?"

What kind of conversation can be had with swords?

I was also a sword wielder. Defiance rose in me; I was curious about his skill. I wanted to clash with him immediately, to know whether his swordsmanship truly compared to my father's First Sword of the Ten Armaments of the Great Phoenix… but this wasn't right. How could we possibly speak through swords?

"I apologize for neglecting my fiancée, but even so..."

"Your sword will attest to the truth of your words. Mine will show no mercy, so ready yourself."

I couldn't get through to him at all. Was he that mad?

I lowered my head and stared blankly at the sword in my hand. The grip was richly textured. How long had it been since I last held a sword? The principles of the First Sword flashed through my mind.

_He is a mountain I'll have to brave anyway. I might as well..._

Having made up my mind, I slid the sword from its scabbard with a resonating ring, led the sword's tip down by my right foot, and stepped forward with my left. My upright posture leaned forward slightly. The fingers of my left hand bent into the shape of the Fifth Claw.

_@[Ten Armaments of the Great Phoenix]@_

_@[Tenth Martial]@_

_@[Absolute Stance]@_

The Sword Demon's eyes flashed, but it wasn't because he recognized its origin. Rather…

"What is that stance? Have you already forgotten how to hold a sword?"

His face was filled with scorn. The stance must have looked full of openings. It was a reasonable judgment if one only considered the sword as the means of attack.

However…

The _Ten Armaments of the Great Phoenix_ were composed of ten martial arts, and all of them were the basis for the final one, the _Tenth Martial_; it was an ultimate martial art that mixed ten different styles into one. The Absolute Stance was a posture only possible after completely mastering the _First Sword_, _Third Palm_, and _Sixth Kick_.

Only my father and I could perform it, so naturally, it has never been shown to the world.

"Have I misjudged you?"

The Sword Demon muttered, his contempt now tinged with anger. Then he drew his sword and took a light step forward.

I closed my eyes and felt his aura.

_THUD._

He hadn't even used qi, yet the floor vibrated as if Mount Tai itself was moving.

_THUD._

Another step. The massive presence grew yet more. Before this monstrous Mount Tai, I felt infinitely small.

_THUD._

This time, his aura formed a sword as endlessly long and heavy as Sun Wukong's staff.

The monstrous mountain raised it high. If it struck down, I would be obliterated without a trace.

_FWOOSH_.

Then it all vanished like a mirage, leaving naught but a gentle breeze in its wake. I scanned my surroundings but sensed nothing.

At the same time, a shape emerged before me, sculpted from the finest of fragments.

A sword.

A single massive sword fell toward me.

_SHIIIIIIIK!_

Indifferently it fell. Could the single stroke of the character for 'one' be drawn more exquisitely? There was not the slightest deviation.

A perfect stance.

_Heavy, and fast._

Physical condition, martial arts principles, the Transcendent Realm. None of it mattered.

It was simply the crystallization of the painstaking effort the Sword Demon had poured into swinging his sword tens, hundreds, thousands, millions of times.

_A martial artist to his core, this man._

I quietly opened my eyes. Then, falling backward in a smooth slide, I swung my sword in an inward arc.

**_CLAAAAANG!_**

The deafening roar left aftershocks in my ears.

Dan Jeonghak indifferently looked down at his own grip. His sword was broken in two, precisely in the middle. The break was as straight as a ruler.

He shifted his gaze to Ma Cheonso, lying on the floor, and saw the sword in his hand was broken just as precisely.

It was a coincidence. It had to be a coincidence.

But was such a coincidence even possible?

Swords of equal strength, striking at the exact same point— so, rather than shatter, their blades had cut through each other.

The flying halves of the blades were each lodged into the ceiling and floor, still vibrating. His fingertips tingled fiercely, numb.

_Are we equals in swordsmanship?_

The factual reality clashed with its sheer impossibility.

Sword Unification.

Had the Young Cult Leader awakened to a realm that he himself had only reached in his forties?

Even he wouldn't feel confident in executing it from such a stance.

Though it was unbelievable, Dan Jeonghak suddenly remembered a day from the past.

Years ago, marriage talks had taken place at His Eminence, the Cult Leader's request.

To that degenerate Heir.

Dan Jeonghak had intended to reject the command outright— until he met the Young Cult Leader.

> _"Please stop my father."_
>
> _"Your Reverence... what do you mean by that?"_
>
> _"Because I will never get married."_
>
> _"..."_

Unlike the bizarre rumors, his eyes back then were more intelligent and sorrowful than anyone's.

"......"

Dan Jeonghak wanted to ask many things, but he remained silent. Such was his nature; he believed only in that which he saw and felt.

Just then, Ma Cheonso rose and dropped the severed sword to the floor with a thud, only to say:

"…I will not go through with the marriage."

Did he mean to break off the engagement? The Sword Demon Dan Jeonghak's brow twitched.

"Those words are not for you to say, Young Cult Leader."

"...What would you have me do? Please let me meet my fiancée. I will offer my sincere apologies."

"_NOT SO FAST!_"

Dan Jeonghak roared with the combined wrath of a dragon and a tiger, then continued, in a calmer tone:

"The matter of the marriage is for us to ponder and determine, not something for the Young Cult Leader to carelessly decide… not unless you wish to make an enemy of the Martial Sword Corps."

The parties involved hadn't wanted the marriage in the first place; the Cult Leader had suggested it, and he himself had agreed. That was how the engagement came to be. It had been greed, but now that they had met again, that greed grew even larger.

"Surely you have heard enough about me. You cannot have missed the news of my latest exploits."

Ma Cheonso boldly revealed his own disgrace, but Dan Jeonghak turned around and replied, resolutely:

"...for the time being, you shall stay here and spend time with the Martial Sword Corps. I'll give you an answer... within a few days."

Left behind, Ma Cheonso could only glare bitterly at his retreating back.

[^1]: Jeonghak uses 부마도위(駙馬都尉), which translates to "Imperial Son-In-Law"— specifically, the non-royal consort of a princess. In sum, he's subtly asserting that the Heir to the Cult is marrying _up_, and that his daughter is superior in every way.
