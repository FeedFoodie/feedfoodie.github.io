Slaughter Predator.

The name was familiar to Lee Sungmin. Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, the Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance, had once spoken of it when he named the three opponents in the world he found troublesome.

The Wudang Sword Immortal, the Martial God of the Outer Heavens, and Predator's Slaughter Predator.

The Void Monarch, however, had claimed the Slaughter Predator was a myth. He had insisted it was merely a title for an inhuman monster that stood at the pinnacle of Predator, a being that had never once appeared from the time he was alive until the present day. This was because no single one of the five monsters known as the Black Star, the apex of Predator, could be called the absolute pinnacle.

"...Slaughter Predator?"

"Didn't the Void Monarch tell you?" Vollander asked.

They stood close to one another, near enough that either could launch an attack in an instant, yet Vollander showed no such intention.

"I've heard the name."

"The Slaughter Predator," Vollander repeated, his eyes narrowing. "It has never appeared, not until now. It isn't a title for a monster that once existed, but a name for one that will one day emerge. The pinnacle of the inhuman. Once human, but now something else entirely. A monster that kills and devours all things is called the Slaughter Predator."

"And you think that's me?"

"I'd say the chances are quite high," Vollander chuckled. "Look at you. You are human, yet you are not. Your body is already that of a Yokai, not a man. Your mind seems to remain human for now, but can someone like you truly be called human?"

"I am human."

"You might tell yourself that, but from my perspective, you are far closer to being inhuman, and on top of that," Vollander chuckled again, "you even exude the aura of a Dragon. As far as I know, no existing inhuman possesses such power. Wouldn't that make you the closest thing to the Slaughter Predator that our kind has been waiting for?"

"That's just speculation."

"True enough. After all, the Slaughter Predator has never existed," Vollander conceded, taking a few steps back. "You pique my curiosity in many ways. It's been a very long time since I've felt like this."

"What are you doing here?" Lee Sungmin questioned him.

In response, Vollander simply shrugged as he returned to his seat. "I was sailing."

"For what purpose?"

"I'm looking for a Dragon," Vollander answered.

The reply was so unexpected that Lee Sungmin could only stare, his eyes wide with disbelief. He had never imagined such an out-of-the-blue reason. Seeing his surprised expression, Vollander let out a laugh.

"Surprised?"

"...A little. It's a very unexpected answer."

"Myself and every Death Knight on this ship are cursed beings. Some wished for this and chose to become what they are, while others had no choice in the matter. Regardless, the fact remains that we are all cursed."

Vollander muttered this as he lifted the helmet from his head. There was no head beneath it, only a shimmering darkness where a pair of golden lights floated.

"The Death Knights of this ship, myself included, have lived for a very long time. We do not die unless we are killed, and after death, we will fall into the clutches of the Demon Lord and suffer for eternity."

He placed the helmet back on his empty shoulders.

"To escape that fate, we search for a Dragon. They say Dragons are the progenitors of magic, that most of the magic in this world originated from them."

"Are you saying a Dragon can break the curse?"

"I don't know. I've never met one."

"Is there a Dragon in this sea?"

"Dragons haven't shown themselves in this world for hundreds of years," Vollander replied. "However, this sea is one of the last places a Dragon was seen. In any case, we have more than enough time. If we wander these waters, perhaps one day... we might meet one."

Vollander trailed off, his gaze returning to Lee Sungmin.

"My curiosity in you stemmed from the strong Dragon aura you carry. I had some expectations, but it seems they were in vain." He let out a low laugh. "There's one last thing I want to ask."

"What is it?"

"Are you interested in joining Predator?"

"No," Lee Sungmin answered without a hint of hesitation.

Vollander laughed as if he had expected as much. "Right. Since you still think of yourself as human... it was a pointless invitation. However, if you ever change your mind, I will recommend you."

"I won't need it."

"You might, one day. When you've completely become a monster. When you are no longer human. The people around you will be the first to cast you out. Humans and monsters cannot live together."

Lee Sungmin fell silent, glaring at Vollander, who continued to speak, undeterred by his fierce gaze.

"Just as humans feel secure among other humans, so do monsters. When that time comes, you will wish to join Predator."

"I don't think that will happen."

"Huhu! I suppose not, for now. As it happens, the position of Yokai is currently vacant. It's been empty ever since the Red Ghost had his heart torn out by the Nine-Tailed Fox."

Lee Sungmin listened no longer. He turned sharply away, and Vollander did not try to stop him. He left the room, walked the ominous corridor, and emerged back onto the deck. The Death Knights were nowhere to be seen.

[King of Monsters, you say,] the Void Monarch muttered. [Indeed. You are not a Death Knight, a Vampire, a Lycanthrope, or a Lich. If you were to join Predator, there would be no fitting position for you other than Yokai.]

"I have no intention of joining."

[I'm not urging you to join. I'm just saying there's no need to make an enemy of them. If you end up fighting Heaven Beyond Heaven, receiving help from Predator wouldn't be a bad thing.]

The words sounded plausible. His first thought had been to seek shelter under the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the Chairman aside, was no match for Heaven Beyond Heaven.

Predator, however, was different.

Vollander alone was stronger than the Six Honored Ones Lee Sungmin had faced. Considering the military strength of the Death Knights he commanded, they could easily crush one of the Nine Sects One Gang. Still, he had no desire to join them. Vollander's invitation did, however, feel like another option had been placed on the table. If one of the Black Star took a friendly stance, there would be less friction should he ever need to join Predator later.

With that thought, Lee Sungmin leaped from the deck of the Ghost Ship, landing lightly on Zod's vessel.

The corpses of the Hundred-Faced Thief and his handmaidens still lay on the deck where they had fallen. Zod and the others stood frozen, terrified and unsure of what to do. Lee Sungmin approached Zod, who reeked of urine, and spoke.

"Move the ship."

"Ye-yes... Yes?"

"I said, move the ship. We're going to Bellador as planned."

"A-a-are you sure it's okay to go?"

"......"

Lee Sungmin simply nodded. Zod gulped and looked at the captain beside him. The coarse-bearded man hesitated for a moment before ordering the sailors on deck to get the ship moving.

The old deck creaked loudly under the sound of their running feet as the darkness began to thin.

Just as it had arrived, Vollander's Ghost Ship vanished, taking the suffocating darkness with it. As the blackness receded completely, daylight returned to a sky so clear it was as if nothing had ever happened.

The ship set sail again, but the atmosphere was frozen solid. Even for the captain and crew, who had sailed for many long years, it was the first time they had seen the Ghost Ship, a thing of legends, with their own eyes.

Amidst the frozen air, Lee Sungmin gathered the corpses of the Hundred-Faced Thief and the handmaidens.

"Wh-what are you doing?" Zod stammered.

Lee Sungmin ignored the question and silently began searching the three bodies. It was just as he thought. The Hundred-Faced Thief was a notorious pirate in these seas, and though he and his crew had been annihilated by Vollander's ship, the Subspace Pocket tucked carefully in his bosom held a fortune in gold and treasure.

In truth, Lee Sungmin wasn't so desperate for money that he needed to do this. Still, it seemed a waste to throw ownerless money into the sea. A few mercenaries watched, hesitating, but none dared approach him to ask for a share.

After taking the Subspace Pocket, Lee Sungmin threw the three bodies into the sea.

The mediator who had challenged him earlier watched with his lips pressed into a thin line. He had acted so high and mighty, calling himself a peak master, but after witnessing Lee Sungmin's true strength, he could do nothing but remain silent.

After the encounter with the Ghost Ship, the voyage proceeded without any further problems. A few times, they passed the wreckage of ships drifting on the water, the remains of pirate vessels that had likely met Vollander's wandering ship and been sunk.

And then, the voyage finally ended. Two months later, Zod's ship sailed into the port of Bellador.

He wanted to jump off the ship immediately, but he couldn't. As Eria's largest port city, Bellador enforced strict entry procedures. The entire city was allied with the Mages Guild, which used detection magic to monitor all ships and people entering from the sea. Even as a master of the Transcendent Realm, Lee Sungmin could not enter Bellador without being caught.

*It's a hassle, but.*

This was why he had bought a C-Rank Mercenary Badge in Erebrisa. Traveling by land would have taken several more months. Since he had arrived in just two, he had to endure this much.

"Don't say anything foolish," Lee Sungmin warned, his gaze sweeping over Zod, the sailors, and the mercenaries gathered on the deck.

Everyone who met his gaze turned pale and clamped their mouths shut. It was only a verbal warning, but they all knew the truth. If they spouted any nonsense, they would die instantly.

The presence of a mediator from the Merchants' Guild allowed them to bypass most of the procedures quickly. Lee Sungmin watched from a distance as Zod spoke with a port official. One wrong word and the man's head would fly. Zod knew this better than anyone, and he flinched, trembling under the weight of Lee Sungmin's murderous gaze.

Once all the procedures were finished, the mercenaries and sailors began to unload the goods. Thanks to his Subspace Pocket, Lee Sungmin had little luggage. By the time the sailors started their work, he had already disembarked. He no longer needed to act as a mercenary hired by that ship.

*It's not that far from here to Legro Forest.*

At most, it was a journey of half a month. Until he reached the forest, Lee Sungmin planned to continue using the name of the C-Rank Mercenary, Jung Hyunsoo. Unlike in the South, the Murim Alliance's influence was strong here. Bellador itself was home to the Namgung Clan, one of the murim Noble Clans.

The Namgung Clan.

The name made Lee Sungmin think of Namgung Heewon. The last he had heard, Namgung Heewon had escaped from the Bewitching Forest. Though he was now in Bellador, he had no business with the man. It would probably be an awkward reunion if they met. A rumor spread by the Murim Alliance, that the Phantom Spear had killed Moyong Seojin and Jaegal Taeryeong, had circulated throughout Eria. Namgung Heewon had been in love with Moyong Seojin, even after she married. He likely believed the Murim Alliance's lies and now considered Lee Sungmin his enemy. If it was a misunderstanding, he wanted to clear it up, but not right now.

Lee Sungmin left the port with these thoughts. However, his plans to leave Bellador immediately were shattered.

He stopped dead in his tracks, staring ahead in bewilderment.

In the middle of a wide plaza stood an iron cage, the kind used to lock up wild beasts. Inside that cage, a man dressed in clothes no better than rags sat cross-legged.

Lee Sungmin's mouth fell half-open as he stared at the man's filthy appearance.

The person inside the cage was Namgung Heewon.
