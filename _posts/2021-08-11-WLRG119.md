"You've chosen well," Jeniela said, her eyes lingering on Lee Sungmin's expression before drifting down to scan the Magic Armor he wore, her gaze piercing the fabric of his robe. "You're carrying some interesting things with you. Is one of them a familiar? No, it feels a bit different from a typical one."

"Ugh..."

Rubia trembled as that penetrating gaze fell upon her. Unable to maintain her materialized form, she shot out from Lee Sungmin's robe and collapsed to the floor, panting and drenched in a cold sweat.

"Cat ears," Jeniela mused. "So you're not one of the mutants, but you're not human, either. What a peculiar creature."

Her eyes flashed. It was the All-Seeing Demonic Eye, one of the many Demonic Eyes she possessed as the Vampire Queen, an ability that allowed her to see through a being's very existence. The sensation of being utterly exposed sent a shudder through Rubia, goosebumps prickling her entire body.

"Your body is based on a human's, but your soul is not," Jeniela continued, her voice a clinical whisper. "You have bones, organs, and blood, but it's all just for show. For whose satisfaction, I wonder? Your creator's? Or your own? A well-made doll... no, you're an artificial Elemental. You seem to have a metabolism, but you're not bound by it. Can I even call a thing like you a living being?"

"S... stop..." Rubia pleaded.

"Does it displease you to be seen through?" Jeniela burst into a mischievous laugh. "Anyone would feel the same. This Demonic Eye sees all." She turned her gaze back to Lee Sungmin, or more precisely, to the Magic Armor he wore.

"Void Monarch. You, the so-called nightmare of the South, have fallen quite disgracefully. Or... should I congratulate you for managing to cling to life in such a state?"

[Hahahaha!]

The Void Monarch's laughter erupted at Jeniela's whisper, his Demonic Energy surging from the armor.

[The little wench has gotten bold. This is why you have to discipline brats properly, right from childhood. It's the only way to beat some manners into them.]

"And?" Jeniela's smile widened. "Are you going to discipline me now?" Through her All-Seeing Demonic Eye, she saw right through his situation. "Your body and soul are completely separated. Is your soul so intense that it's maintaining its existence despite that? I'll admit, it's an exceptional case, but compared to you in your prime, you're severely lacking."

What she said was true. The Void Monarch in his prime might have been another matter, but with only his soul and Demonic Energy left, his power was no threat to her. That was a given. Jeniela was the apex of the vampire species in Eria, a monster among monsters whose strength ranked among the highest even within Predator. She was steps above even Werewolf Ju Won, the master of the Ghost Wolf Sect once known as the Mad Wolf.

"And you..." The All-Seeing Demonic Eye turned towards Lee Sungmin. "...you are..."

Jeniela's mouth moved, but no words came out. After a brief silence, she tilted her head, then rubbed her eyes and looked at him again.

"What are you?"

For the first time, a hint of confusion crept into her voice. It was faint, but it was proof of her unease. She rubbed her eyes again, staring at Lee Sungmin, but the 'unseen' remained.

"How strange," she murmured, her fingers straightening. "Why can't I see?"

SQUELCH.

Two of her long, straight fingers pierced her own eyes. She twisted them, severing the optic nerves and pulling the eyeballs free. Then she opened her mouth and swallowed the pair of eyes she had just plucked out. The instant she swallowed them whole, two new eyes were recreated in their sockets. With her newly formed All-Seeing Demonic Eye, she looked at Lee Sungmin once more.

[This one is a strange fellow,] the Void Monarch said with a laugh. [Even with your All-Seeing Demonic Eye, you can't see through him. Huhu! Isn't it delightful?]

"...Indeed." Jeniela spat out what was left in her mouth, a single strand of optic nerve that had come attached to an eyeball. She stared down at it for a moment before raising her gaze back to Lee Sungmin. "Pardon me. I've shown you an unsightly display."

Her tone of voice changed. "I don't intend to be forceful. However, I would sincerely like to make you an offer." She began to speak faster, the words tumbling out without a single pause for breath. "Rather than walking down creaking hallways in a garbage heap like this, sleeping on a musty bed in a room infested with cockroaches and rats, making meals from leftover ingredients which then become leftovers themselves in a repeating cycle, eating disgusting food that not even a pig would touch, and spending your days listening to the chatter of your prey, who are no better than vermin and parasites..."

Having said everything she wanted without her breathing faltering in the slightest, she stopped and smiled at Lee Sungmin.

"Wouldn't it be better to come with me?"

He couldn't fathom her true intentions. From the moment they met, Lee Sungmin had felt a constant sense of dissonance. This was an encounter that had never happened in his past life, though he had experienced countless such deviations by now. The dissonance emanating from Jeniela was different. It was an unidentifiable, unsettling feeling, yet one he didn't want to push away. He even harbored the thought that she might be the nobleman he was meant to meet in the North.

"...Very well." Lee Sungmin helped Rubia to her feet and nodded.

[No.]

The Void Monarch's voice cut through his thoughts, bringing him back to his senses. The Monarch no longer flared his Demonic Energy, quietly concealing his power within the armor.

When they followed Jeniela outside, a magnificent carriage waited before the shabby inn, a sight one could never imagine finding there. The carriage door opened as she approached, and she gestured for Lee Sungmin to enter rather than boarding first herself.

"Please, get in."

Lee Sungmin boarded the carriage with Rubia without a word. Only after they were both inside did Jeniela climb in, taking a seat opposite him.

[The emotion you're feeling is the 'charm' possessed by high-ranking vampires,] the Void Monarch explained. [A vampire's prey is human. They don't always hunt by overpowering them with brute force. Their primary method is to approach intimately and quietly, without any commotion, sink their fangs into a neck. Charm is a convenient tool for their hunts.]

Lee Sungmin focused his mind, sharpening his consciousness. The fogginess in his thoughts cleared, and as it did, the strange dissonance he felt from Jeniela faded away. His eyes flashed as he glared at her. Meeting his gaze, Jeniela chuckled softly.

"You truly are unusual," she remarked, pulling the ornamental pin from her hair. A thick, red wave cascaded down her back. "Normally, even if someone realizes it, they can't break free immediately. A vampire's charm is like a subconscious suggestion. Your mental fortitude, however, is exceptional. Unthinkably so for a normal human."

The carriage began to move.

"Why did you approach me?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"I was curious about the human accompanying the Void Monarch, it's true," Jeniela said, tilting her head. "But even without that, I had personal business with you. With the Sword Phantom."

A name he never would have expected left her lips.

"He was a boy I was quite fond of. At least, before I got my hands on him. So tenacious, so passionate, and yet so full of Despair. Unwilling to give up, even though he yearned for things beyond his reach. Huhu! That's why I liked him. I'm fond of people like that." Just as she was fond of the Blood Heavenly Demon. "I wasn't able to look after him much. Ah, did you know? For a 'Lord', the vampires in their coven are like their own children. In other words, the Sword Phantom was my child. My youngest, whom I took in a few years ago."

Her voice was low, and Lee Sungmin listened quietly. The meaning was clear. Jeniela was the one who had turned the Sword Phantom into a vampire.

"I see."

What did that matter? It was the Sword Phantom who had chosen that path. He would have become a vampire in the past life, too. Wiji Hoyeon had been the one to kill him then, not Lee Sungmin, but his choice would not have changed regardless of the circumstances. The fact that Jeniela had turned him was no reason for Lee Sungmin to feel anything towards her.

Jeniela, however, was different.

"Is it because I killed the Sword Phantom?" he asked. "Do you want to hold me accountable?"

"I did, a little," she answered honestly, a brilliant smile on her face. "But not anymore. Toys, well, they can break, can't they? It couldn't be helped. Let's just leave it at that." As she spoke, she peered into his face. "...You. Have you ever considered becoming a vampire?"

This was a highly unusual offer. Jeniela had countless thralls, but she did not increase their numbers indiscriminately. For a Lord at the apex of the vampires, creating more thralls meant dispersing one's own power. While a portion of that power returned as the thrall grew stronger, the absence of the 'blood' given to them created a critical weakness as their numbers grew. Thus, pure-blooded vampires chose their prospects carefully, selecting only those who would bring more gain than loss. Jeniela was no different, but while she considered ability, the most important condition was that they satisfy her own dark amusement.

Just like the Sword Phantom and the Blood Heavenly Demon had.

"If you wish, I'll turn you into one," she whispered, her voice coaxing. "It's not difficult. See? You can become one right now. You'll become a thrall of me, the Vampire Queen, Jeniella of Blood Charm. What do you say?"

She felt a profound interest in Lee Sungmin, an unusual feeling she concluded. The Sword Phantom had fallen into Despair over his aging body, and after failing to overcome it as a human, he chose to abandon his humanity for more time. The Blood Heavenly Demon was not so different, falling apart in Despair over his severed arm and crushing defeat. Soon, he too would choose to become a vampire.

Lee Sungmin, on the other hand, was bizarre to her. His martial skill, which didn't match his age, wasn't important. This absurd world was filled with calamities possessing preposterous talent, and it was not rare for young monsters to surpass warriors with decades of training. That pattern had repeated itself throughout the six hundred years she had lived.

She wasn't drawn to his power. So what was it? She didn't know him or what kind of life he had lived. The fact that her All-Seeing Demonic Eye could not see through him was certainly interesting, but the reason she felt the desire to make him her thrall...

*Fragile.*

Jeniela looked into Lee Sungmin's eyes. They were eyes that did not match his age, eyes that held an accumulation of something so vast that even she could not fathom its length. She was drawn to those eyes, and at the same time, to the fragility within them. A fragility that seemed as if it would shatter into a thousand pieces at the slightest provocation was stirring something in her heart.

"I refuse," Lee Sungmin replied. The question wasn't even worth considering. He had no intention of becoming inhuman, and certainly no desire to become a vampire controlled by Jeniela. If he truly wanted to become inhuman, he had other options, like the Yokai the Void Monarch had suggested.

"Oh, really?" Jeniela smiled brightly at his answer. "Then it can't be helped."

She didn't try to persuade him any further. She would have been disappointed if he had accepted right away. In fact, his refusal filled her with a deep sense of satisfaction. A heat rose inside her as she wrapped her arms around her shoulders.

*Keep refusing.*

*Because I love that.*

*Tearing down a will that believes itself to be unshakable, just like a sandcastle.*
