A mask of disbelief settled over Envirus's face. "Surely, you don't have a Dragon Heart, do you?" he asked.

Though his irritation had subsided slightly, Lee Sungmin simply nodded, and Envirus's jaw dropped.

"No way… you didn't hunt a dragon, did you?"

"Don't be ridiculous."

"Right, I suppose so," Envirus conceded. "A dragon isn't some giant beast you can just hunt down… and they're hard to even find these days. So how did you get your hands on one?"

"I was lucky."

"What do you plan to do with it?"

"I'm going to eat it."

Envirus blinked at the words, then burst into laughter.

"You're going to eat a Dragon Heart? Haha! Do you think a Dragon Heart is something you can just consume like an elixir or some miracle herbs?"

"Is there a difference?"

"Of course there's a difference! Consuming a Dragon Heart means your body has to withstand all the immense mana the dragon possessed. A human can never handle that much magic. You'd absorb half, no, even a quarter of it, and your body would just explode!"

"That's my business."

"Easier said than done!"

Ignoring the mage's grumbling, Lee Sungmin asked, "Can you help me?"

His question made Envirus fall into thought. In his eyes, Lee Sungmin was a bomb that could go off at any moment. He was maintaining his humanity for now, coexisting with the Void Monarch and demonic energy, but that balance was incredibly unstable.

"…I refuse. The Dragon Heart is too dangerous for you."

"Is there no way at all?" Lee Sungmin asked again, clearing his throat.

Envirus's answer, however, was the same. He stubbornly shook his head, his voice firm.

"No. Under no circumstances will I handle the Dragon Heart for you. You're just too dangerous."

[That son of a bitch!]

The Void Monarch, who had been listening to their exchange, roared in frustration. It was an outcry Lee Sungmin deeply sympathized with.

With no desire to argue further, Lee Sungmin plopped down right where he stood.

"I'll do as I please," he spat out, beginning to regulate his breathing once more.

Envirus opened his mouth to say something to Lee Sungmin's back, but his body suddenly flinched.

"…Hmph!"

Envirus's eyes narrowed, his expression hardening as he scanned his surroundings. Ignoring him, Lee Sungmin continued his conscious breathing. The mage focused his mind, and his magic power swirled around him, manifesting into an image that appeared before his eyes.

"…Good heavens!" Envirus cried out, shocked by what he witnessed.

He stood dumbfounded for a moment, unsure what to do, before hastily grabbing Lee Sungmin's shoulder.

"What is it now?" Lee Sungmin asked, turning to face him with open annoyance.

Envirus paid no mind to his glare and insisted in an urgent voice, "There's a problem."

"What kind of problem?"

"Something is about to happen in this city."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. It did not seem like Envirus was making a fuss over nothing. He slowly got to his feet and looked at the mage with a blank expression.

"What's going on?"

"An evil… something. It's about to descend on this city."

"What exactly is this evil something?"

"…Hmm." Envirus hesitated for a moment before swallowing his reluctance. "I don't know why, but a powerful Lich is about to cast a wicked magic in this city. I don't know what kind of magic they're trying to cast, but it will definitely not lead to a good outcome."

"…So?" Lee Sungmin asked back. The only Lich he knew was Preskan, so when Envirus mentioned one, his thoughts naturally turned to him.

"We have to stop them, no matter what, but… I might not be strong enough on my own. I think I'll need your help."

"Why should I help you?"

"Are you planning to just ignore them then?"

"Didn't you say it yourself? This damn, fucked-up fate. According to you, wouldn't their success or failure just be part of that flow?"

"That… I suppose so, but we might be able to change it," Envirus said with conviction, despite his earlier hesitation. "I don't know what they're trying to do, but there's no guarantee that it will lead to the end of fate, is there?"

Lee Sungmin could not help but laugh. He remembered what Frau had said about the process of taking a shit being the variability of fate, even if the act itself was its end.

"Are you asking for my help for free?" he asked with a grin.

Envirus's shoulders trembled. The upper hand had shifted, and it now belonged entirely to Lee Sungmin. Envirus knew this well. He hesitated for a long time before finally squeezing his eyes shut.

"Fine. The Dragon Heart. I'll handle it for you."

"Your oath?"

"I swear on my mana."

It was a choice he had to make. If Lee Sungmin became a monster, he would gain the power of the Dragon Heart and become a terrible being, but that was a matter for a distant, unrealized future.

*Perhaps this too is fate.*

Envirus chewed on his lower lip. If this was fated to happen, he could not stop it. Not even dragons or gods could completely twist the flow of fate. In that case, he had to resolve the immediate crisis.

"Let's go."

Envirus waved his hand, and space split open with a CRACK, connecting the forest to the Fairy Realm. He quickly walked through the passage, and Lee Sungmin followed behind him.

"So, where is this Lich?"

"…They're heading for the city's Central Plaza. I don't know what they're planning to do there, but I have a very bad feeling about this…"

As he spoke, Envirus slowly floated into the air. Lee Sungmin looked up as he rose, unsurprised. Plenty of mages could use magic to float.

"Don't resist," Envirus said first, then reached a hand out to Lee Sungmin.

A gentle flow of magic enveloped Lee Sungmin's body, and just like Envirus, he floated upward.

"I'd be fast enough just by running."

"But you don't know the way, do you?"

With that, Envirus flew higher into the air with Lee Sungmin in tow. Once they rose to a height where the buildings were no longer an obstacle, the two of them accelerated toward the Central Plaza.

"I heard you're a great mage."

"Who told you that?"

"Lady Rubia said so, and I heard that Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master, is your disciple."

"Well… Rubia, that child tends to overestimate me. It's true that Lloyd is my disciple. Our paths crossed a long time ago, and I had the chance to teach him magic."

"Even someone like you can't use something like Spatial Movement?"

"You really do ask the stupidest things," Envirus spat, a look of disbelief on his face.

It was a question born of pure curiosity, and Lee Sungmin stared back, dumbfounded by the response. Envirus cleared his throat and elaborated.

"…Spatial Movement isn't a magic humans can perform. A few of those so-called 'archmages' can manage a Blink over a visible distance, but true Spatial Movement, something that exceeds a Blink's limits, is impossible for a human. The spatial coordinates are always changing. Even if you could somehow grasp them, your body would be crushed while escaping the current space and arriving at the target."

"But don't they find scrolls in places like dungeons that enable Spatial Movement?"

"That's why dungeons are such mysterious places. They are places where common sense becomes meaningless and the nonsensical becomes the norm. I've tried a few times to perfect Spatial Movement magic using a scroll from a dungeon, but… ahem."

"What is it?"

"Let's just say I failed. A lot. The number of animal corpses I've crushed to death with it is probably enough to feed an entire village."

While they were talking, they arrived above their destination, the Central Plaza, or rather, just short of it. Envirus had stopped their flight abruptly.

"…A barrier."

Envirus's face twisted. Just as he said, a translucent black membrane covered the Central Plaza in a perfect hemisphere. He moved closer and reached out a hand.

CRACKLE!

The magic power he sent forth collided with the barrier's surface, sparking with electricity. Envirus ground his teeth, biting his lower lip.

"It seems they're determined to block all interference… Damn half-corpse bastards!" he cursed harshly.

He seemed genuinely enraged by the situation. In the end, he and Lee Sungmin descended from the sky and landed on the ground. The atmosphere was strange, the streets utterly devoid of life. It had the eerie feel of a ghost town from some horror story.

Envirus looked around, his face cold and hard. Neither of them knew what was about to happen, but even to Lee Sungmin, the situation looked incredibly ominous.

"We have to stop it," Envirus muttered, as if trying to convince himself.

He rolled up his worn sleeves, then spread his hands wide and extended them toward the barrier. His lips moved, chanting an incantation as a mystical light enveloped both his hands. He touched the surface of the barrier.

SIZZLE!

Black electricity flared from his hands.

"Ugh…!"

Through that contact, Envirus realized the Lich who had cast this barrier was a monster who had reached an incredible level as a mage. However, the same was true for Envirus. He suppressed the electric current and continued to synchronize his magic with the barrier. Ripples spread across its surface, and an opening large enough for one person to pass through comfortably was created.

"…If something is happening in this city, my companions might be in danger."

"That…! You don't need to worry," Envirus gasped out, his voice strained. "The evil power is right here. There might be some commotion in the city, but it won't be too dangerous…!"

At his words, Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. If it were just Rubia, he might be concerned, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon was by her side. With his skills, he should be able to break through most dangers without a problem. If anything, Lee Sungmin was in more danger than they were.

"Go in first…!" Envirus shouted, holding the passage open.

Lee Sungmin wasted no time, ducking through the passage first. Envirus followed right behind him, and the opening in the barrier sealed itself shut behind them.

Inside, a black fog drifted through the street leading to the plaza. Unlike the emptiness outside, he could sense presences all around. There were sounds too, a sickening 'crunching' noise that came and went, the intermittent sound of something being chewed, drunk, and swallowed. A nauseating stench filled the air, making his nose wrinkle. It was the same terrible odor he had smelled several times on Mount Mushii, like blood and filth mixed together and left to ferment.

"…Get ready."

Envirus did not roll his sleeves back down. Lee Sungmin's gaze fell to the mage's arms, which were as thin as reeds but engraved with faint patterns of various shapes. Envirus, who had been clenching and unclenching his fists, reached inside the cloak draped over his shoulders and pulled out a wand the length of his forearm.

Lee Sungmin did not reply with words, but with a nod. He had understood for a while now what Envirus meant.

KIEEEEEK!

The moment Lee Sungmin gripped his spear, the door of a nearby building shattered. A rotting corpse burst out, letting loose a bizarre cry.

"It's a Ghoul," Envirus muttered, aiming his wand. "It seems we have a necromancer in here."
