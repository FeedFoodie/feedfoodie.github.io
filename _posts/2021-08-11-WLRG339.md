He recounted the story of all that had transpired. After hearing everything, Baek Sogo closed her eyes, needing a moment of solitude to sort through the chaos in her mind. Her silent wish was granted.

"Thank you," Lee Sungmin said to Yana again. He knew he had only been able to act so decisively because she had accompanied him, even though he had not seen things through to the end with Jeniela. While he and Scarlet shared a connection with Baek Sogo, Yana was a complete stranger to her. Nevertheless, she had joined him in confronting his nemesis for his sake. *No, for the Void Monarch's sake.*

"Scarlet, thank you too..."

"Don't say it to me," she cut in. "I wanted to save Baek Sogo too."

Lee Sungmin offered a bitter smile at her words. Things had turned out well, remarkably so. They had stormed the Lycanthrope den and skirmished at Jeniela's mansion, yet no one had died or even suffered any serious injuries.

"So, what are you planning to do?" Scarlet asked, her gaze shifting to him. "She's opened her eyes for now, but her condition isn't good. We have to do something."

Osla had been clear that he could do nothing about the poison afflicting Baek Sogo. Ju Won's poison, he had said, was incurable even if Ju Won himself were to appear. If left unchecked, the toxin would continue to eat away at her body until only death remained.

He refused to let her die. Of course he did. Baek Sogo was his lifesaver. Just as he had saved her life, she had saved his, and this time was no different. Lee Sungmin fully intended to save her again.

"...The Saint," Lee Sungmin finally said. "I'm thinking of visiting the Church's Saint, Teresa."

He remembered her from ten years ago, the woman protected by Holy Knights on the way to subjugate Kim Jonghyun of Germud. The Saint of the Church had guaranteed then that she could completely purify the Demonic Energy that Osla could only manage to seal. Teresa had been there during his rampage, and fortunately, she had survived.

"She probably won't like that, you know?" Scarlet said with a frown.

It was an unavoidable truth. When Lee Sungmin had rampaged ten years ago, the Church's Holy Knights, Teresa included, had been right beside him. They had suffered considerable damage because of it.

"I'll at least try asking."

*And if necessary, I'll threaten her.* The thought was genuine. He planned to apologize for the incident with his fighting spirit, but if an apology was not enough to grant his request...

"Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo's voice called to him.

Lee Sungmin heard it clearly, but his body refused to react. A tremor ran through him, an emotion he knew all too well.

*This is fear.*

Ten years ago, Baek Sogo had not killed him. Setting aside whether she even could have, it was she who had shouted to Frau that she did not want to kill him, that she could not, and begged for him to be sealed instead.

*Would she still feel the same way now?* he wondered. *Perhaps she hasn't changed? Or would she regret not killing me ten years ago? Could it be that she regrets being swayed by affection and failing to resolve herself?*

"Aren't you going to go?" Scarlet prompted.

He swallowed the fear that had seized him and slowly rose to his feet. The thought of speaking with her was terrifying.

*I'm the same as I was ten years ago, but other people aren't. The ten years Baek Sogo lived through might have changed her.*

Even so, he slowly approached her. Baek Sogo was sitting under a large tree, her back leaning against its trunk. As he looked into her listless eyes, a heavy weight settled in his chest.

"Thank you," she said with a faint smile as he drew near. "For... saving me. You know, I never imagined that you, Junior Disciple, would come to my rescue."

"...Is your body... alright?"

"It's not good. I've lost a lot of strength, and my inner qi are barely moving... It's something I thought about a lot over the past year. I acted so... recklessly." Baek Sogo closed her eyes. "Though it couldn't be helped. What about you... Junior Disciple? Are you okay?"

"I'm... fine."

"Do you resent me?"

The words she tossed out landed like a stone in his chest. Lee Sungmin flinched in surprise and looked at her. She was waiting for his answer.

"How could I possibly resent you, Senior Sister... Ten years ago, if you hadn't stopped me..."

"No, that's not what I'm talking about," Baek Sogo said, shaking her head. "I'm a nuisance, aren't I?"

The question came with a bitter, self-deprecating laugh. Lee Sungmin's eyes wavered at the words. He clenched his trembling hands, his body sinking to its knees before her as if his legs would no longer support him. He glared at her, and she met his gaze as she slowly continued.

"Junior Disciple. Ten years... is a long time."

"...Yes."

"For ten years, I wanted to do many things. I wanted to... annihilate the evil of this world. I tried to distinguish good from evil by my own standards. Though my perspective might have been narrow, to avoid that... I went around erasing the 'evil' that most people spoke of."

Her actions, at the very least, had not been wrong. Over the decade, many evil masters had died at her hands. She had wandered the world without rest, righting the wrongs she found wherever she stopped. The name of Silent Flash Baek Sogo had become renowned as that of a righteous warrior.

"I never thought my conviction was an easy one, but... I didn't want to break. In the end, it turned out like this, though. I... I just didn't know enough. When I first learned that monsters called inhuman were causing chaos from behind the Blood Alliance, I realized just how narrow my perspective was."

So she had gone to Cheped.

"Predator. I thought they were the root of the evil making this world rot. To be honest, I was a little tired. I had wandered for ten years erasing evil, but... the world just wouldn't become peaceful."

*I didn't want to break.*

"When I learned about them, I thought I had found the answer. I thought that if I annihilated them, the world would finally become peaceful. Ah, I know. I know how shallow a thought that is, but... at least. I thought it would bring about a clear and certain change, one that anyone could see, different from anything I had done before."

"...Senior Sister."

"I failed."

Baek Sogo opened her eyes. He looked into their murky gray depths, which no longer sparkled as they once had.

"Poisoned, I wandered the Snowfield. I had a premonition of death... and in that moment... I missed everything. You, Junior Disciple, and Scarlet too. I lost consciousness, and when I opened my eyes, I was in a subterranean mansion in Trabia. That's when I saw the Vampire Queen for the first time. The root of this world's evil, which I had been so sure of, was right before my eyes. Though I was utterly powerless."

"......"

A dry laugh escaped her.

"And in the end, I escaped that place with the help of the Junior Disciple I had sealed with my own hands. Isn't it... pathetic? I've caused nothing but trouble. Because of me, you faced great danger. If I hadn't gone there, not knowing my place, you would never have been..."

"Stop."

Lee Sungmin did not want to hear any more.

"I have never thought of you as a nuisance, Senior Sister. I've never considered you pathetic either, and I have no desire to mock your conviction. To annihilate evil... haha, evil. What's so wrong with that?"

He reached out and took her hand.

"What happened to you?" he demanded, glaring at her face. Her attitude was strange. Even after ten years, he could not understand how her spirit could have become so weak.

Baek Sogo looked down at his hand holding hers. It was warm. Her eyes trembled slightly.

"The End."

The words fell from her lips.

"The end of everything. Gemini told me about it. That someday... no, that very soon, the world would come to a complete end."

That was what had broken her. To annihilate evil. Baek Sogo had lived with that one conviction. To gain the strength for it, she had endured a thousand years of training with Denir and withstood terrible ascetic practices on Mount Mushii.

The End rendered her entire life meaningless. No, it was not just her. In the face of The End, the lifelong efforts of every being in this world were meaningless. A world where destruction was predetermined, where everything would soon end. To learn of that was to have one's entire life and all one's efforts completely negated.

"Then we just have to stop it," Lee Sungmin said, looking directly into her eyes. "Even if it's a predetermined end, there must be a way. I will stop it. Without fail, I will stop The End."

"...Aren't you... afraid, Junior Disciple?"

She had grown weak. For ten years, she had lived for her conviction. They say that what is too rigid is easily broken. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had said as much after hearing of her conviction. That sometimes, it was better to bend. Because what is rigid is easily broken, he had said that Silent Flash would die an early death. Or else fall into Despair and be broken.

It was just as he had predicted. Baek Sogo had fallen into Despair and was breaking. The sight was completely unfamiliar to Lee Sungmin.

"I am afraid," he said without hesitation. "I'm human, too. How could I not be? I'm afraid of death. I'm afraid that the life I've lived, the efforts I've made, the person that I am, will all come to a complete end."

Even having experienced death once, he was still afraid of it. He was even more afraid now that he knew the truth of this world, that his current self was ultimately just a pawn arranged for the sake of The End. All of it.

"Because I'm afraid, I struggle so I don't have to face it. Senior Sister. I've... been through a lot. The people around me fought against this world's damn The End, and they died and disappeared playing their parts to stop it."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and Abel.

"Someone, even now, is struggling to stop The End, which could come in any form."

Wiji Hoyeon.

"I... will stop The End. I must. If I don't, I won't be able to bear the guilt I feel toward those who died for me, who died to stop it. I don't want to end like this. I want to live longer, to be happy. For that reason alone, I will stop The End."

Baek Sogo did not answer. Her conviction had snapped and broken when she realized her own powerlessness. It was not because she had been defeated and poisoned by Ju Won. It was because she had realized she was just a small being, powerless before the certain conclusion known as The End.

"The things you've done were not meaningless, Senior Sister," Lee Sungmin said, his voice desperate. "Thanks to you, I survived on Mount Mushii. Thanks to you becoming my Senior Sister and teaching me martial arts, I've been able to live until now. Because you stopped me ten years ago, I am here now."

"...What... meaning does that have?"

"Because I will stop The End."

At the sound of his voice, Baek Sogo's eyes widened.

"I, the one you saved, will stop The End. I won't let your life be meaningless. I will create the good and peaceful world you spoke of. A world without evil might be impossible, but I will create a world with less evil."

So, please.

"...Don't fall into Despair. Just as I haven't."

At those words, her head slowly lowered. Her shoulders were trembling slightly.

"...Ha... hahaha."

A laugh, choked with tears, escaped her.

"I'm so pathetic," she whispered, covering her face with her hands. "You've become so admirable, Junior Disciple, but I... I've become so pathetic."

"You're not pathetic," Lee Sungmin insisted, wrapping his hands around hers. "I will cure your poison, Senior Sister."

"...Will I... be of any help?"

"......"

Lee Sungmin pulled the weeping Baek Sogo into an embrace.

"Without a doubt."

In his arms, Baek Sogo sobbed silently.
