"What are you planning to do now?"

At Yana's question, Lee Sungmin, who had been hanging his head, slowly looked up.

The events of that day in Germud, ten years past, flooded his mind. He now knew what had happened during the fighting spirit incident. Baek Sogo had been there. She had stood against him when he became an uncontrollable monster, yet in the end, she had been unable to bring herself to kill him.

He remembered her vow to annihilate evil. By that measure, the man he had been in Germud back then was surely a villain. He had lost his reason, rampaged, and attacked the Holy Knights. If there had been living humans around him instead of zombies, he would have attacked and devoured them instead. Baek Sogo must have known that. She had seen him as evil according to her own narrow standards, a mindless beast on a rampage.

Nevertheless, she couldn't kill him. She didn't kill him. She was simply unable to.

*My senior sister... is wandering.*

For the long decade that followed, Silent Flash Baek Sogo had made a name for herself as a knight-errant among knight-errants. She roamed the world, rescuing commoners in distress and striking down the leaders of unorthodox sects.

A question formed in his mind. The murim was currently split in two, the Orthodox Murim Alliance and the Demonic Blood Alliance. The Blood Alliance was a new power, formed by the unorthodox sects under the Blood Demon, and the two factions had been clashing repeatedly in conflicts both large and small.

By Baek Sogo's standards, the Blood Alliance was evil. Unlike the old Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, its sects had become objects of resentment for excessively exploiting the villages and cities around them, and Baek Sogo was strong. Over the past ten years, she had appeared from time to time, and most accounts told of her defeating a regional demonic leader or bringing down an unorthodox sect. The leaders and sects she toppled were almost always affiliated with the Blood Alliance. Considering her personality and her vow, there was no doubt she saw them as evil.

*No way.*

A chilling thought surfaced, and Lee Sungmin focused his mind, delving into the information Nebul had given him. The last time Baek Sogo had been seen was a year ago, in a city called Cheped. It was one of the gateways to the North.

*No way.*

A tremor ran through him as an ominous premonition took hold. For an entire year after that sighting, Baek Sogo had not appeared in the world. Why would she go to Cheped, a city leading to the North? At the very end of the North, in Trabia, was the Vampire Queen Jeniela, the apex of the Predator. A monster among monsters.

Lee Sungmin shot up from his seat. Nothing was confirmed, but his anxiety felt like a certainty.

The pieces began to click into place with horrifying speed. Baek Sogo, wandering the world for a decade. The Blood Alliance martial artists he had just seen in Germud. Rao Xian had called them his errand boys, linking the Predator directly to the Blood Alliance. Perhaps the true power commanding the Blood Alliance from the shadows was Jeniela and the Predator. If Baek Sogo had discovered that fact, her arrogant desire to annihilate evil would have led her to one conclusion. In the current world, the greatest evil in her mind would be the one who had lived for hundreds of years, standing at the apex of the inhuman group called the Predator, the one who oppressed citizens from behind the Blood Alliance and clashed with the Murim Alliance.

*No way.*

It wasn't confirmed. His anxiety was just growing, but what if it was true? What if Baek Sogo had headed north for a final confrontation with Jeniela? The fact that all rumors of her had vanished for a year was unsettling and frightening. Why had all news of her been cut off after she was seen heading to Cheped?

"What's wrong?" Yana asked.

Lee Sungmin bit his lip hard. There were so many questions, all of them piled up during the ten years he was asleep.

His thoughts spiraled, jumping from one terrifying possibility to another, and inevitably, they landed on Wiji Hoyeon. How would she have reacted to news of his death? Could she have simply accepted it and wandered the Dungeon for ten years?

*No.*

That couldn't be. That wasn't the Wiji Hoyeon he knew. If she had truly believed him to be dead, she would have dropped everything and come straight to Germud, but she never came. There was no information suggesting she had even been near the city.

*Wiji Hoyeon is connected to the Demonic Spirit.*

He remembered the feeling of White Fang being placed in his hand in the Mental World. The one who did that was, with high probability, the Demonic Spirit.

*Could the Demonic Spirit have told Wiji Hoyeon that I wasn't dead? Is that why she didn't come?*

He pushed the thought aside. *No, that's not what's important right now.* The immediate danger, the one that felt like a certainty in his gut, was what mattered.

"...I have to go," he said, the words escaping him.

"Where?" Yana asked, tilting her head.

Frau, meanwhile, grumbled something about not wanting to get involved and flopped back onto the bed. Yana, however, was different.

"...A place I need to get to urgently has come up," Lee Sungmin explained. "Legro Forest, near Bellador. I have to go there."

The distance from Urmuri, near the southern tip of the continent, to Cheped was far too great. His plan was to first go to Legro Forest, meet Osla, and get another Fairy Horse. Fortunately, he had passed near Cheped before on his first journey toward Trabia.

"What is it?" Yana pressed. "Even if you have to hurry, please tell me. Isn't it possible I could be of help?"

At her words, Lee Sungmin hesitated. A sliver of distrust lingered in a corner of his heart. Laplace's warning echoed in his mind, causing him no small amount of confusion. *Don't trust the Demonic Spirit.* And Yana was a being who had received her power from that very entity.

However, this was no time to be picky. It was ridiculous to be swayed by Laplace's words and unconditionally distrust the Demonic Spirit, not when it had undeniably helped him throughout his life. It might have even been the one to place White Fang in his hand. Pushing his doubts aside, he gave Yana a brief, summarized version of why he had to reach Legro Forest.

"Let's go together," Yana said with a nod after hearing his story.

"Pardon?"

"You said Legro Forest, right? I know how to get to Bellador."

"Wait..."

"Didn't you say you had to hurry?"

Yana turned and headed for the door. Lee Sungmin gave Frau a quick farewell and followed her out. After opening a spatial door and stepping outside, Yana looked up at the sky.

"Lee Sungmin."

"...Yes."

"Are you planning to fight the Vampire Queen?"

"If I have to."

"I'll be honest," she said. "I have no reason to be sad about your death. However, I will be sad about the death of the Void Monarch inside you."

Nine tails unfurled from behind her back.

"The Vampire Queen... is a monster whose power surpasses anything you can imagine. She is a monster that even I cannot guarantee victory against. If you fight her, and she decides to kill you, you will certainly die."

"So... is that why you're coming with me?"

"Yes. I do not wish for the Void Monarch's death. Since the Void Monarch chose to live with you and resides within you, I, who do not wish for his death, must protect you."

The Demonic Energy of the Void Monarch stirred within him.
[You intend to leave this city?]

The rumbling voice was met with a bright smile from Yana.

"You're finally speaking to me."

[Ahem...]

"This city is not that precious to me. For you, I can abandon it at any time."

The Void Monarch smacked his lips at her blind devotion. Yana, her nine tails now fully revealed, extended a hand toward Lee Sungmin.

"I will go with you."

"...Thank you," Lee Sungmin replied, taking her hand.

Her nine tails wrapped around his body like a silken shroud. A brilliant golden light enveloped them, swallowing them whole in a soaring sphere. Inside, Lee Sungmin felt no sense of floating, only a profound stillness. The hand he held seemed to dissolve, and when the light settled, he found himself riding on the back of a beautiful golden fox.

WHOOSH!

Yana kicked off the air and began to run. Urmuri grew distant at a tremendous speed. Just as there had been no sense of floating, there was no sense of speed either. Her nine tails trailed long behind her, scattering golden light across the sky. From atop her back, Lee Sungmin stared down at the world below with a dazed expression. She was moving faster than his own movement arts at full power.

[While you were gone, I was contacted by Jeniela,] Yana's voice echoed in his mind. [It was an invitation to join the Predator. Of course, I refused. There was no reason for me to join.]

"Did they just back off when you refused?"

[It wasn't Jeniela who came in person. Since then, she has not contacted me.]

He looked down again. The scenery was a blur flashing by. Yana was continuously accelerating. In the past, Kim Jonghyun had estimated it would take her two days to get from Urmuri to the Hyujael Mountains, a gross underestimation of her true speed.

Yana ran without rest. Even when night fell, she ran without sleeping, her pace never dropping in the slightest. Since she was running through the air, she could travel in a straight line directly to Legro Forest.

After four straight days of this, they arrived near their destination. Without Yana's help, it would have taken Lee Sungmin over a month to make the same journey, no matter how fast he was.

He descended with Yana at the entrance to the forest.

"Thank you."

Yana looked a little tired, perhaps from running at full speed for four days straight. When Lee Sungmin expressed his gratitude, she gave a faint smile and shook her head.

"It wasn't anything that great."

Lee Sungmin entered the forest with her, not bothering to hide his presence. He blatantly radiated his aura, and before long, the scenery began to waver. In the distance, he could see the twinkling lights of the Fairy Realms. As he was about to approach, he sighed and hesitated.

*Should I dodge this or not?*

"Just stay still," he spat out quickly to Yana.

The instant he finished speaking.

KABOOM!

A sphere of flame shot across the space and collided with his body. Lee Sungmin staggered slightly before righting himself.

"You son of a bitch."

A curse filled with irritation was shot his way. The sound was loud, but the actual power behind the attack wasn't that great. Lee Sungmin wondered what kind of expression he should make as he turned his head.

He saw Scarlet with her hands on her hips, her eyebrows deeply furrowed.

"...It's been a wh—"

KABOOM!

Before he could finish his words, another sphere of flame floating around Scarlet shot out and struck him in the face. This one was a little more powerful. If he hadn't hastily raised his Protective Qi Aura, all of his hair would have been burned off.

"Hey, you son of a bitch," Scarlet said, pointing a finger at him. "Back then, you just abandoned me and left!"

"That was... for your sake, Scarlet..."

"You think that makes me feel better? Huh? Someone was genuinely worried and offered to go with you, and you just push me away right before leaving!"

BANG!

Another fireball exploded against him.

"And it'd be one thing if you came back perfectly fine, but you show up after ten years! It's obvious, isn't it? It's because of the Fairy Horse! I knew it. That's why I deliberately told them to take the Fairy Horse back!"

"Is that what happened?"

"That way, you'd have to come back, at least for the Fairy Horse!" Scarlet shrieked.

"...That's a relief. Does that mean you knew I wasn't dead, Scarlet?"

"Hmph."

"And even if it wasn't for the Fairy Horse, I would have naturally returned here. I figured you would be worried."

"So? Did you come back here to say you're sorry because you thought I'd be worried?"

"Well..."

As Scarlet snapped at him, Lee Sungmin trailed off without realizing it. At that, her face twisted.

"In the end, you only came now because of the Fairy Horse!"

Lee Sungmin had nothing to say, so he shut his mouth.
