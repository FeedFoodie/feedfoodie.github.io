His escape from Nerod was a failure.

He had leaped over the city gates with a simple plan. He would immediately leave the area, move to another region, and only then relax before returning to Haras, the headquarters of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Just as he had feared, however, the situation refused to unfold as he had hoped.

It happened in an instant. Something plummeted from the heavens, piercing the ground directly in front of his feet.

It was a simple sword, devoid of any decoration.

The moment he saw it, Lee Sungmin knew his worries, and those of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, had become a terrifying reality.

*Sword Immortal…!*

His body moved the instant the thought crossed his mind. Unleashing Gale Thunder from Heaven Reversal Black Thunder again and again, Lee Sungmin tore through the space around him. He needed to get as far away as possible before summoning the Fairy Horse. He could only use it one last time, a final resort he desperately wanted to save, but this was no time for such sentiment. If he did not use the Fairy Horse now, he would never escape.

The repeatedly unleashed Gale Thunder ripped through an immense amount of space. This Extreme Speed movement art, which pierced through the very gaps in the world, was a technique no one below the Transcendent Realm could use, and one even he was not confident he could wield perfectly.

Still, he forced himself, ignoring the strain on his body. Guided by martial arts theory, his monstrous physique instantly healed and pushed his strained form forward.

However, the sword continued its chase.

It was the Flying Sword Technique, raised to its highest level, piercing space in the exact same way as his Gale Thunder. Despite his repeated efforts, the sword remained right behind him. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's warning echoed in his mind. If he were to encounter the Sword Immortal, he must never attack. He had to run unconditionally.

Even with the cold steel closing in on the back of his neck, Lee Sungmin clung to those words, trying to shake the relentless blade. That pursuit, however, did not last long.

When he could no longer muster the energy for Gale Thunder, Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and skidded to a halt. The Flying Sword Technique was still there, so close that the slightest movement would draw blood. He stood rooted to the spot, not daring to move.

When Lee Sungmin stilled, so did the sword.

[Why'd you stop?]

The sword hummed and vibrated.

[Keep running, little one. This game of tag is getting fun.]

"...Are you the Sword Immortal?"

[Kekeke! Who else but this old man could handle a sword with such skill? What do you think? Aren't my tricks impressive?]

"Why are you doing this to me?"

Lee Sungmin turned completely to face the hovering blade, which now aimed directly between his eyebrows.

[What an impudent brat. Little one, are you really asking 'cause you don't know why I'm doing this?]

"I haven't caused any harm to Wudang."

[Little one, I am, at least on the surface, affiliated with Wudang, which belongs to the orthodox sects, and you've caused quite a ruckus in the lands of the orthodox sects, which are closely related to us, haven't you?]

"Is that why you're doing this to me?"

[Kekeke! No, that's just an excuse. I just thought you were a skilled and interesting fellow, so I decided to play a little prank. You turned out to be quite a good runner. It got this old man excited.]

"You call this a prank?" Lee Sungmin spat, glaring at the sword. This was his first time speaking with the Sword Immortal, and he could not begin to fathom the man's personality.

[He seems like a senile old man?]

Lee Sungmin could not help but agree with the Void Monarch's mutterings.

[If this isn't a prank, what is it? I didn't kill you. I didn't even cut you. I just sent a sword to chase you around. Didn't you have fun?]

"I didn't have any fun at all."

[It's fine as long as I had fun. You seem a little lacking to be called Ilcheon's successor, though. Your Gale Thunder still looks awkward! You're a far cry from Ilcheon of the hundred-year fighting spirit.]

"...Are you going to kill me?"

[Don't use such scary words. I'm a Taoist master. I don't kill without reason,] the Sword Immortal said in a stern voice. [Although, I will kill someone if they deserve it. So, little one, tell me. Do you deserve to be killed?]

"...I don't think I'm that bad."

[That's for me to judge,] the Sword Immortal said, his voice full of laughter. [Little one, I have ears, you know. I've heard all about the evil deeds committed by the one they call the Phantom Spear.]

"Most of those things are probably misunderstandings."

[Are you saying you're innocent? Can you prove it?]

"If I could prove it, I would have done so long ago."

[Kekeke!]

At Lee Sungmin's words, the Sword Immortal laughed boisterously.

[I'd like to meet you in person and talk, if possible, but for me to drag my weary body all the way there just to see you is a bit much...]

"Are you telling me to come to Wudang? Do you think I'd go?"

[And what'll you do if you don't come? If you say no, I'll just kill you right here.]

"Didn't you say you don't kill without reason?"

[I don't think killing you would be without reason.]

The sword hummed and vibrated.

Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. From the Sword Immortal's words, he had a rough idea of where the man was, but he could not bring himself to believe it.

"...Sword Immortal. Are you on Mount Wudang right now?"

[That's right.]

The Sword Immortal answered without hesitation. At his words, Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh. It was not a lie. He sharpened his senses, scanning his surroundings, but he could not feel the Sword Immortal's presence anywhere. From Wudang all the way here? He was using the Flying Sword Technique over such an enormous distance?

"What kind of Flying Sword Technique is..."

[This is the true Flying Sword Technique. I've wielded a sword for hundreds of years. When it comes to the sword, I possess the greatest talent in history, and I've honed that talent for centuries. To be called the Sword Immortal, shouldn't one be able to do at least this much?]

Indeed. Lee Sungmin chewed his lower lip, his thoughts turning to the Sword Saint. He too was a monster who had wielded a sword for hundreds of years and entered the Transcendent Realm, yet compared to the Sword Immortal, he seemed like a child.

[Go.]

After a moment of deliberation, the Sword Immortal spoke that single word.

[I've thought about it, and it seems it would be better not to kill you here.]

"...Why is that?"

[I'm letting you live, and you can't even say thank you?]

The Sword Immortal only grumbled, not explaining his reason for choosing not to kill him.

[What are you doing? Not running? Hurry up and get out of here before I change my mind,] the Sword Immortal spat out. At his words, Lee Sungmin turned without hesitation. It was a relief he would not have to use his last chance with the Fairy Horse.

He ran with all his might, putting as much distance as he could between himself and that spot.

A voice cut through the silence of the room where he sat with his eyes closed.

"Looks like you're well enough to move?"

Kim Jonghyun half-opened his eyes, his gaze falling on the door. He did not question the Vampire Queen's rudeness for entering without knocking. He was, after all, the unannounced guest.

"Thanks to your care."

"Could you not say such disgusting things?" Jeniela replied, her tone laced with genuine disgust. A deep annoyance swam in her blood-red eyes.

After the ritual in the forest failed, Kim Jonghyun had chosen Jeniela's mansion as his hiding place. Predator had lost much of its power. Arbes, the Arch Lich, had been annihilated by Kim Jonghyun himself. Red Ghost, the King of Monsters, had his heart torn out by Yana of Urmuri and his whereabouts were unknown. Ju Won was steeped in the ennui of his long life, focused only on fulfilling the last wish of Howon, the man he had killed. Vollander, leader of the Death Knights, was wandering in search of a Dragon to break his curse.

And Jeniela did nothing.

"It would have been quite interesting if you had completed the ritual."

Jeniela wore a colorful kimono, an outfit she had once used to seduce the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon. Even after his death, she still wore a kimono from time to time for the simple reason that it was flashy and restrictive. Jeniela sometimes enjoyed that kind of discomfort.

"Thanks to your failure, you've become a very ambiguous existence. A half-made Demon Lord... no, perhaps even less than that. Though you do seem stronger than Arbes was."

Kim Jonghyun let out a small laugh at her words. He had failed a ritual on the very verge of completion, and because of that burden, his body had become a bomb that could explode at any moment. The half-fighting spirit ritual was meant to completely change one's species. Even coming to the brink of success, a failure was still a failure, and he now bore a commensurate risk.

"What will you do now?"

Jeniela walked over to the bed and sat down, her annoyance replaced by a flicker of interest.

"Performing such a ritual again would be impossible, but you still have the Grimoire. You should be able to handle its magic even with that incomplete body of yours."

"That's right," Kim Jonghyun answered without hiding anything. Jeniela's gaze drifted to the Grimoire sitting on the desk. It was the magic tome every Black Mage dreamed of, but she had no interest in it whatsoever.

"What did you plan to do after becoming a Demon Lord, anyway?"

"I planned to act like one," Kim Jonghyun answered without hesitation. "Like the ones from the old stories. Try to destroy the world, maybe fight a hero."

"The Demon Lords in those stories always lose to the hero."

"That wasn't my plan. I didn't intend to be too greedy. Ah. To be honest, I never thought much about what I'd do after. I just wanted to become a Demon Lord because I could."

"But you were disappointed that you failed, weren't you?"

"I was angry, too. It was something I could have done, but I failed. I anticipated a lot of things, but I never expected the Black Dragon Knight to show up."

"That intervention probably happened because you weren't meant to become a Demon Lord."

Hearing Kim Jonghyun's mumble, Jeniela burst out laughing.

"I suppose it's fate. Because it was my destiny not to become a Demon Lord. That's why I couldn't become one."

"Fate, of all things."

Jeniela cackled at Kim Jonghyun's reply. At her words, which seemed to deny the very concept of fate, Kim Jonghyun's brow furrowed slightly. Jeniella of Blood Charm. To him, she was an inhuman being shrouded in mystery.

"Vampire Queen, I have some questions for you."

"I know you chose this place as your hideout specifically to ask me questions," Jeniela said, staring at him. "I'm just not sure why you're asking now."

"There's always a 'what if', isn't there?" Kim Jonghyun said as he got up. He tested the condition of his body. The halfway transformation had made him stronger than before. He had spent months tuning this incomplete form. It was not perfect, but it was much better than it had been right after the failure.

"This question I'm about to ask... Vampire Queen, if you find it rude, you might try to kill me."

"If you're worried about that, shouldn't you just keep it to yourself?"

"It's in my nature to find out whatever I'm curious about," Kim Jonghyun said, baring his teeth in a smile. "With my body as it is now, fighting you would be impossible if you were serious... but I think I could escape."

"Aren't you overestimating yourself?" Jeniela asked. A smile played on her lips, but her eyes had turned as cold as a viper's.

"Perhaps. Vampire Queen, ever since I joined this... organization called Predator, I've always had a question. You're a monster. From what I can tell, you're the strongest among us, and you wouldn't be outmatched even by the likes of the Martial God, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, or the Sword Immortal, who are said to be the strongest among humans."

"So?"

"Someone like you, who possesses that much power... what in the world do you desire?" Kim Jonghyun chuckled as he spoke. "I'm truly curious. You've lived for hundreds of years, and decades ago you settled here in the North, living so quietly it was as if you weren't even here. You only started to make a move recently. The Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon... was he really so incredible that he could break your long silence?"

"If he were that incredible, he'd still be alive and well."

Jeniela's mood had not soured yet, but Kim Jonghyun knew he was in a position where he had to be careful with every single word. He was having this conversation because he had some confidence he would survive, but that confidence was less than fifty percent.

"Vampire Queen."

Kim Jonghyun took a deep breath.

"Between you and the Dragons, what kind of promise was there?"
