Once the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had vanished, Osla, who had remained behind, drifted toward Lee Sungmin. He flinched at her sudden proximity, instinctively taking a few steps back.

She was the Fairy Queen, appearing as a girl in her early teens, yet she stood at eye level with him. Magnificent butterfly wings spread wide behind her.

"...Hmmm."

Osla's eyes, which sparkled with an unnerving light, narrowed as she stared at him. Lee Sungmin felt as if her gaze could see through everything, and he tensed, swallowing dryly without realizing it.

After a long moment of scrutiny, a mysterious smile played on Osla's lips as she finally spoke.

"I'd like to ask a small favor. Would that be alright?"

"What kind of favor?"

"I want to touch you."

The unexpected request made Lee Sungmin's eyes widen. He didn't misunderstand her words, of course, but he hesitated before asking why.

"It's curiosity," Osla answered, her voice clear and ringing.

"You're a rather interesting human. The Coalition Leader was interesting too, but you're... quite different from him. You're a mix of various things, yet they're quite harmonious, and you're not dead. Besides, it's not common for a human to use Dragon's Pressure, is it? I've lived for a very long time, but aside from Dragons and half-dragons, I've never seen a human use it."

She moved a little closer as she spoke.

"And besides that, I feel something... unsettling from you."

"...Unsettling?"

Osla's words brought to mind 'the Apostle of the End', a term various beings had mentioned before. Regaining his composure, Lee Sungmin posed a question of his own.

"I have a question for you."

"What is it?"

"You... I mean, the Fairy Queen. Are 'you' beings like gods?"

"That's a strange way to put it. 'You'?"

"...I've met the Spirit Queen before."

Lee Sungmin's answer made Osla's expression stiffen instantly. Her narrowed eyes flew wide as she stared straight at him.

"...The Spirit Queen? Don't tell me you went to the Elemental Realm?"

"No."

"Then how could you have met the Spirit Queen? She doesn't exist in this world. She shouldn't."

Osla's declaration was firm, but it didn't change the fact that Lee Sungmin had met her. He shook his head, denying Osla's words.

"I definitely met the Spirit Queen in this world."

"...Impossible," Osla muttered, her face a mask of disbelief.

"She manifested in this world? Good heavens, that's a breach of contract...!"

"Contract?"

"That's something you don't need to know." Osla grumbled, her brow furrowing. "That woman, what on earth is she thinking...?!"

She let out a small "Ah" and looked back at Lee Sungmin.

"Gods... and us are a little different. I can't say we're completely different. Should I say our fields are different, or that we pursue different things... It's not a matter of right and wrong, but what we pursue is different from the gods. It's not something a human like you needs to understand. Anyway."

Osla slowly raised her hand, opening it wide before his face.

"I want to touch you. That's okay, right?"

"I don't mind," he replied, "as long as nothing bad happens to me."

"If I wanted to harm you, I wouldn't need to ask for permission like this," she grumbled. "I'm asking because I have no intention of harming you."

With that, her outstretched hand came to rest on his shoulder. He felt nothing at the point of contact, but Osla's eyes narrowed once more.

"...You're connected to a tremendous monster. A great yokai died in the South hundreds of years ago. I wondered if such a monster could even die, but it did. It was an unsettling death in many ways, and that death has led to you."

It was different.

The ominous feeling she sensed, Osla thought to herself, did not emanate from the Void Monarch, who was spiritually connected to Lee Sungmin.

Deeper than this... Osla closed her eyes, and the wings spread behind her began to emit a soft, bluish light.

Deeper.

She delved into the very core of Lee Sungmin, into the unconsciousness he himself was not aware of. It accepted her without resistance. Before he knew it, his grip on consciousness was slowly slipping away as fragments of light from Osla's wings floated around them like fireflies.

"...Ugh!"

After wandering through his subconscious for a time, an unknown force violently pushed Osla away, throwing her back. His hazy awareness snapped back into focus. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, barely managing to keep himself from collapsing.

"What did you do?!" he shouted at the retreating figure.

Osla managed to spread her wings just in time, avoiding the disgrace of tumbling to the ground. She looked back at him with a bewitched expression.

"...I see."

She stared at him for a moment longer before nodding.

"Too much time has passed. So... I had forgotten without realizing it. Or maybe I didn't want to think about it..."

"What's that supposed to mean?"

"You are a pitiful being," Osla muttered with a sigh.

"It's just a shame that I can't tell you much, but... still, I think it's okay to tell you this much."

"Dammit."

Lee Sungmin cursed, kicking at the ground. He was growing far too used to this kind of vague, cryptic talk.

"Be faithful."

"What do you mean?"

"To each day. Live each day to the fullest. So you won't have regrets... later."

Osla looked at him with a bitter expression. He had met others who had seen through his existence and had heard incomprehensible words from them many times before.

However, Osla was the first to wear such an expression.

Lee Sungmin shut his mouth and glared at her for a long moment. He knew instinctively that the Fairy Queen knew something the other beings he had met did not.

"...I can't understand what you're saying."

"I suppose not," Osla replied, slowly nodding her head.

"I know you're confused, but I can't answer anything for you."

"Because of the Apostle of the End?" Lee Sungmin spat out. "Because that thing, whatever it is, is threatening you? I've heard this kind of talk several times now. Not a single one of the people who told me this ever gave me a straight answer. Just when I'm about to forget, someone pops up and always says things like this, confusing me. It's like you're all in on it together!"

"...It can't be helped."

Despite his harsh reaction, Osla didn't question his rudeness. She just repeated that it couldn't be helped.

"This is an old promise. A promise that has existed since the beginning of this world. Me, the gods... we cannot escape this promise."

The air began to grow cold. Lee Sungmin felt an inexplicable chill on the back of his neck. Startled, he looked back, but no one was there.

"If you can answer, please answer... What is The End?"

"The end."

"And the Apostle of the End?"

"The one who carries out the end."

"Then, what am I?" Lee Sungmin spat out, glaring at Osla.

At his words, she fell silent. It seemed she couldn't answer this question. The Void Monarch was silent. Osla also said nothing.

In the ensuing silence, Lee Sungmin's mind raced, recalling everything he had experienced, done, met, and heard up to this point.

Mushii had said there were no coincidences in the world and that he would have to prepare for some day.

Kim Jonghyun had spoken about the Apostle of the End.

Grandmaster Bulyeong had looked at him and said he was favored by mysterious phenomena.

Frau and Envirus had spoken about the Destiny Force centered around him.

No coincidences. It must mean that everything is fate. A fate heading where? The End is the end. The Apostle of the End is the one who carries out the end.

*Then what about me?*

"...Am I the Apostle of the End?"

As the words left his mouth, he thought how ridiculous it sounded. He couldn't help it. In his past life, he was no one special, just a common, mediocre C-Rank mercenary. He had learned a Third-Rate martial art, barely reached the Second-Rate level, then gotten greedy and died trying to clear a Dungeon alone. Right before he died, he happened to obtain something called the 'Stone of Reincarnation' and returned to the age of fourteen, when he was first summoned to Zenavis.

That was all.

Osla, who he hoped would scoff and deny it, gave no answer. Her silence drew a humorless laugh from him.

"How... am I supposed to take your silence?"

"I can't say anything," Osla finally spoke after a long pause. "Your question strikes at the heart of the old promise, and since I am a being bound by that promise, I can't give you any answer. It's not just me. There is no one in this world who can answer your question."

At her words, Lee Sungmin laughed once more.

"But, not yet... not yet. Nothing is set in stone."

The chill on his neck sharpened. Osla flinched, her wings drooping slightly.

"You... are you. That's an undeniable fact," she said, then ran both hands through her hair. She seemed confused too. After messing up her neatly braided hair, Osla let out a deep sigh.

"Ah, really!"

"What is it?"

"I'm just as frustrated as you are! I want to tell you everything I know too, but I can't!"

"Why are you getting angry at me?"

"Because you're getting angry at me!"

After shouting, Osla took a deep breath and pressed down on her messy hair with her hands.

"I don't know. I have nothing more to say to you. However."

Despite saying she had nothing more to say, she left some room at the end.

"...I'm friendly towards you. Just know that," Osla spat out, glaring at him.

Lee Sungmin opened his mouth to say something more, but she squeezed her eyes shut and covered her ears.

"I can't hear you!"

With that final shout, Osla vanished in a flash of light.

Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh, pressing a hand to his forehead. It was all so confusing. Every time he met someone and heard about the Apostle of the End or the nature of such things, the confusion only piled up. Nothing was ever clearly resolved. They just spoke in circles, piling up suspicion and anxiety.

[...Hmm.]

The Void Monarch, who had been silent all this time, finally spoke.

[I'm not interested in stories about the Apostle of the End or whatever...]

*Ahem.* The Void Monarch let out a dry cough inside Lee Sungmin's head.

[It's not like that 'end' thing has arrived right this moment. I don't think you need to worry about it now.]

*Easier said than done.*

[Isn't the matter at hand more important than worrying about something you don't even understand?] the Void Monarch asked in an indifferent voice. [You've become an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven and a disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. It would be better to focus on the present rather than thinking about the distant future. If you become an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven and continue to fight them, you'll die, nine times out of ten.]

Lee Sungmin didn't retort and kept his mouth shut.

[The only reason you've survived this long is because of those 2100 years of training. The experience from that has kept you alive until now, but that won't be the case from now on.]

*That's right.*

Lee Sungmin sighed and nodded his head.

[You have no more room for growth,] the Void Monarch pronounced in a calm voice. [You have once again reached the state you achieved in the Mental World. You don't know the state beyond that. There's no room for you to get stronger from now on.]

*...That's true.*

It was a gut-wrenching truth. The current Lee Sungmin had completely reached the state he had attained in Denir's Trial. In other words, he could no longer grow by chasing the strength within his 'experience' as he had before. He lacked talent. A calamity like Wiji Hoyeon had plenty of room to keep growing, but Lee Sungmin did not. It had taken him 2100 years to reach this state. He couldn't even guess how much time and effort it would take to reach a higher one.

[This is actually for the best. You became a disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman right at the point where you've grown to your limit. Since he is someone who has reached a state far beyond yours, he should be able to guide you, albeit slowly.]

*Maybe so.*

"......"

The Void Monarch cleared his throat with an "Ahem."

[Let's be frank.]

His voice lowered.

[You and the Little Heavenly Demon. What exactly do you want to do with that wench?]
