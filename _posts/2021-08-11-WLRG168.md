[*It breathes fire, and you burn just by touching it... Hmm. Fire-breathing Yokai are a dime a dozen.*]

Their path led them to the graveyard. The destination promised no comfort, yet the Crazy Heavenly Demon insisted on seeing the grave with his own eyes. Lee Sungmin watched his back, noting the heavy weight in his steps as he walked ahead.

A somber mood settled over the group, affecting everyone. Aladur, in particular, kept his mouth shut and walked with a timid quietness. His earlier teasing, suggesting the woman might already be dead, had vanished completely.

[*Well, if I really think about it... Hmm... A huge body that breathes fire...*]

The Void Monarch was lost in deep thought.

In truth, Lee Sungmin knew there was no practical reason to hunt this Yokai. Not for him, anyway. Pervas could not handle Demonic Energy, and finding a villager who could required them to find the Nine-Tailed Fox, not the creature that had massacred the village. Pervas had been clear that the Nine-Tailed Fox had taken the surviving warriors.

"I want revenge."

They were searching for the Yokai for one reason alone, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's burning desire for vengeance. The bond he had formed in this village, even if it was just a ten-year fighting spirit connection, had been precious to him. After leaving the village and succumbing to lunacy, he had never expected to return. He certainly had never imagined he would come back to find everyone murdered.

Lee Sungmin understood that thirst for revenge and wanted to help him achieve it. He had traveled with the Crazy Heavenly Demon for nearly a year now and had grown quite fond of the man.

Weeds choked the graveyard. Pervas was the only one left to look after it, and since he was not of sound mind, the grounds were practically abandoned.

"...That way."

Aladur's voice was small when he finally spoke up. He pointed toward one of the weed-choked graves, his nose twitching as he glanced furtively at the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"That's the grave."

"Thank you," the Crazy Heavenly Demon replied, his voice stiff. He shuffled over to the spot Aladur had indicated and stood before it.

He simply stood there, staring down at the grave with a blank expression. No one knew what thoughts consumed him, and no one dared to ask.

[*I don't know. There are too many fire-breathing Yokai... How about we try meeting that Nine-Tailed Fox instead?*] the Void Monarch suggested after a long silence. [*If things go well, you might be able to meet the tribesmen she supposedly took with her. You'll also find out who the Yokai is that massacred the others.*]

Lee Sungmin could not agree so recklessly. The Nine-Tailed Fox Pervas mentioned was likely Yana, a major figure who ruled Urmuri. It was unknown if she had any connection to the Predator, but they could not casually decide to meet a being said to have torn out the Red Ghost's heart and to be hostile toward humans.

[*But it's necessary, isn't it? You have to bear the risk.*]

*I know,* Lee Sungmin replied, a bitter taste in his mouth.

The sun was beginning to set when the Crazy Heavenly Demon finally turned around. He fiddled with the bracelet in his pocket.

"This one is thinking of going to Urmuri," he said.

"That's good," Lee Sungmin said with a nod. "I was thinking of going to Urmuri as well."

"I'm not going," Aladur declared, shivering. "Seeing what you folks are up to, it looks like you'll get tangled up with the Nine-Tailed Fox or some other Yokai in Urmuri. I don't want to die, so I'll just quietly head back."

"Even if I give you more money?"

"Jeez, don't make me say it again! I love money, sure, but is it worth my one and only life?"

"Then do as you please." Lee Sungmin nodded. He had no desire to force Aladur to come along. They had been guided this far, and with a clear destination in Urmuri, there was no need to drag an unwilling companion with them.

"Don't take it too hard," Aladur said, looking genuinely apologetic. "You guys are incomparably better people than... what's-his-name, that bastard Jaegal... but still, I don't want to go to Urmuri."

Lee Sungmin retrieved a large jewel from his Subspace Pocket and handed it to the man. Aladur's eyes widened as he stared at the offered gem.

"...What's this?"

"Your reward for guiding us this far."

"...Hngh."

Aladur made a groaning sound and reached out with both hands to accept the jewel. He was surprised by its heavy weight, but the money-grubbing snobbery he had shown before was gone. Mumbling with his thick lips, he let out a deep sigh.

"...I won't go to Urmuri with you, but. Hngh..."

"What is it?"

"My reason for not going isn't just the Yokai. In Urmuri... well. The Master who taught me sorcery is there. If he hasn't kicked the bucket, he should still be living there." As he spoke, Aladur glanced at the silent Crazy Heavenly Demon. "I don't know his exact location, but... well, you're a member of Erebrisa. You should be able to find him if you just know his name. My Master's name is Frau, so try asking around. If you meet him and tell him you came because you're friends with Aladur, he might act like a bit of an asshole, but he'll probably grant most requests. As long as you pay the price, that is."

"Alright," Lee Sungmin nodded. It seemed Aladur felt guilty about backing out.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon stared at Aladur's face for a moment before bowing his head deeply.

"Thank you."

"Ah... no need for thanks. It's this damn mouth of mine, sheesh. This damn mouth." Aladur made a fuss, slapping his thick lips.

{sep}

They left the village. Aladur said he would return alone to a different city or a moderately sized village, but not Devon.

Lee Sungmin requested information from Nebul about Frau, the Nine-Tailed Fox, and the fire-breathing Yokai.

[There is only one Nine-Tailed Fox in Urmuri. There may be other fox Yokai, but there is only one true Nine-Tailed Fox with nine tails. Only Yana. Fire-breathing Yokai are too common... but we know of Master Frau.]

*Is he in Urmuri now?*

[Yes. Master Frau is an especially outstanding sorcerer among the many in the South. We desperately wanted to recruit him as a member, but we were repeatedly refused.]

Information on Frau's location flowed into Lee Sungmin's mind, followed by details about Yana. She was a classic Nine-Tailed Fox, a mutated fox that had lived for a very long time and acquired a yokai nature. It was true that she currently ruled Urmuri, but she was not doing anything overtly. This was quite different from what Aladur had told him.

[Yana is not hostile to humans. In fact, she shows them a slight degree of favor.]

*I heard that the number of humans going missing in Urmuri has been increasing recently.*

[Hmm... that is true. In fact, Yana is not entirely without blame for such incidents. It's happening because she doesn't manage the other Yokai.]

*What do you mean by that?*

[Yana is a Yokai who suddenly became famous a few years ago for tearing out the heart of the Red Ghost, who had ruled Urmuri for hundreds of years. Originally, Urmuri was home not only to the Red Ghost but also to many great yokai with powerful strength. They served under the Red Ghost, but they were biding their time and waiting for a chance to betray him, but then... Yana suddenly appeared.]

It was not just Lee Sungmin. The Void Monarch was also listening intently to Nebul's story.

[Yana's appearance was sensational. The Red Ghost couldn't even put up much of a fight before his heart was torn out and he fled. Until her arrival, he was the undisputed ruler of Urmuri. Yana, who reigned over the city after him, merely reigned. She did not control the other Yokai. It was a different kind of rule from the Red Ghost's time. He had used his power to suppress the Yokai of Urmuri and keep them from running wild, which prevented them from devouring humans and growing stronger.]

[I see what you mean,] the Void Monarch nodded.

[However, because Yana does nothing, the great yokai in Urmuri have stealthily begun hunting humans to increase their power. As time passes, the number of humans disappearing there will only grow.]

After a moment, Nebul made a small sound of realization.

[Come to think of it. You mentioned a large, fire-breathing Yokai, didn't you? One that burns you on contact. As it happens, there is a great yokai with those exact characteristics. It's a Yokai named Chuseong.]

[Chuseong? That's a Gangcheori.]

*Gangcheori?*

[There are many types of Yokai. Chuseong is a Yokai called a Gangcheori. They say it's a mutated imugi that failed to become a dragon, but hmm... when this Elder saw him, he was just an impatient and ignorant serpent. However, four hundred years have passed, so he might have become something more formidable.]

[Chuseong is also in Urmuri. Just in case, would you like to hear the information on him as well?]

*Please.*

There was no guarantee that Chuseong was the Yokai that had attacked the village, but it seemed necessary to find out.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon said little on the way to Urmuri. He meditated and reviewed his martial arts whenever he had a spare moment, and his silence cast a subdued pall over their travels. Rubia glanced around nervously while Lee Sungmin reviewed his own martial arts alongside him. Along the way, a message arrived from Celgerus, informing him that he had failed to process the Dragon's scales and bones. To use them, Lee Sungmin would ultimately have to visit the chief of the Dwarves.

Three days after parting with Aladur, Urmuri came into view. It was night, but the area around the city was as bright as day.

[The nights in Urmuri are beautiful,] the Void Monarch muttered. [It's because creatures like Yokai prefer the night over the day.]

Lee Sungmin wondered if there was any need to wait until morning. The Void Monarch answered his unspoken question.

[If you want to fight a Yokai, it's wiser to go when the sun is up rather than at night.]

Those were the words of a Yokai himself. In the end, they decided to camp near the city. They lit a campfire and prepared a meal, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not sit down. He stood apart, staring toward Urmuri with cold, chilling eyes.

"Are you alright?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"I'm fine."

It was a question he had asked several times on their journey, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon always gave the same answer. Lee Sungmin nodded, watching his back.

After a long time, the Crazy Heavenly Demon finally came and sat by the fire.

"My apologies."

"It's alright."

"...What a pathetic sight. To think I'd be this shaken," the Crazy Heavenly Demon chuckled dryly. Lee Sungmin did not know what had happened between him and the dead village woman ten years ago, and he had not bothered to ask.

"I never thought I'd return someday," the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat out, staring into the flames. "It was a casual connection, but... I did return, and I found out she was murdered. So I have to get revenge."

Early morning came. Though no one woke them, the party packed up their camp as soon as the sun rose. The morning in Urmuri, which had glittered with bright lights all night, was quiet. They approached the wide-open city gates, where no guards were in sight. There was no need for them in the first place. This was a city of Yokai, not humans.

The street visible past the gates was wide and long. Weaving between the closed stalls, Lee Sungmin organized the information in his head. First, he planned to meet Aladur's Master, Frau. He had already learned where Frau was located through Nebul.

"...Hmm."

Lee Sungmin wore a troubled expression as he recalled the man's location. The place where Frau could be found was Urmuri's red-light district.
