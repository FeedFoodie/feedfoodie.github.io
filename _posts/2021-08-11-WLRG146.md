He closed his eyes.

The letter from the Murim Alliance that had arrived this morning was the reason he had added action to his flawed logic. It was a letter demanding the Little Heavenly Demon's death, the very thing that had brought Yoo Hojeong here and ultimately led to his demise, yet, Yoo Hojeong did not consider his own death shameful.

"Ah..."

Yoo Hojeong's gaze drifted toward the sky. With somber eyes, Lee Sungmin looked down at him. Yoo Hojeong blinked and opened his mouth to speak, but the words would not form properly. Instead, a torrent of crimson blood poured from his lips.

He had failed to block Lee Sungmin's spear. Neither the iron armor that had earned him the title Iron Armored Divine Spear nor his Protective Qi Aura had been enough to save him. Yoo Hojeong had come to kill Lee Sungmin, and in response, Lee Sungmin had faced him with all his sincerity and strength. This was the result.

Yoo Hojeong raised a trembling hand to his chest. He felt the hole, wide enough to see through to the other side, but it was so full of blood that only red could be seen. He coughed again and again, blood splattering from his lips. He managed to twist his bloodless mouth into a smile before his eyes found Lee Sungmin again.

"That... choice... Do you regret it...?"

"I don't."

The Murim Alliance Chairman's letter had ordered the death of the Little Heavenly Demon, but in truth, it was no different from telling Yoo Hojeong to commit suicide. Yoo Hojeong didn't resent the Chairman for it. There must have been a good reason.

Just as Yoo Hojeong had things he believed to be right, the same was true for Lee Sungmin. He let out a sigh. Even if it was a choice he was pushed into, he refused to regret it. To some, Wiji Hoyeon might be evil. She was a demonic practitioner, after all, and might one day act the part.

However, not to him.

"Then that's enough..." Yoo Hojeong muttered.

He didn't resent Lee Sungmin. It was a simple fact that he had tried to kill the man. He was just dying in return because he lacked the strength to win. Yes. That was the nature of a fight. As a martial artist, Yoo Hojeong had long known he would one day meet such an end. That was why he could close his eyes without resentment.

With that final acceptance, the Iron Armored Divine Spear, Yoo Hojeong, died.

Even without regret, it was not a good feeling. Lee Sungmin had never wanted to kill him. Though their connection was brief, he knew the man as a martial artist of high renown, a spearman who had gained fame long before Lee Sungmin himself. He may have called himself narrow-minded, but he was a long-standing warrior of the orthodox sects.

[This will be troublesome.]

Void Monarch's voice echoed in his mind. He knew. By killing Yoo Hojeong, the reputation Lee Sungmin had painstakingly built would vanish. This act would bring him infamy, cementing the name Lee Sungmin and the title 'the Phantom Spear' in people's minds as belonging to a demonic practitioner. Lee Sungmin tore his gaze from Yoo Hojeong's body. He approached the Crazy Heavenly Demon and gently took the sleeping Wiji Hoyeon back into his arms.

"Let's go."

"Aren't you going to bury him?" Rubia asked, her voice a low hum from within his robes.

Lee Sungmin answered as he walked past the corpse. "There are plenty of other people to bury him besides me."

Gazes were already fixed on him. People were running from the South Gate, their voices rising in a chorus of curses and insults. He didn't hear them. He didn't try to listen. Not wanting to cause any more friction, Lee Sungmin simply used his movement arts to leave them behind.

They didn't follow. They had just witnessed Lee Sungmin kill the Iron Armored Divine Spear right before their eyes. Among the martial artists gathered there, not a single one was skilled enough to threaten him. With the Crazy Heavenly Demon also present, they wouldn't dare attack so long as they valued their lives.

*Did the Iron Armored Divine Spear not value his life?*

He didn't know. A sticky, unpleasant feeling pooled in a corner of his chest. That narrow-minded justice. Was that belief truly worth betting one's reputation and entire life on?

"Are you tired?"

Lee Sungmin's steps stopped.

A man was standing under the shade of a tree not far away. If he hadn't spoken, Lee Sungmin would never have noticed him. The man's presence was that faint. To say it was faint was an understatement.

He felt transparent.

"I saw your fight with the Iron Armored Divine Spear," the man said. "He's one of the top experts in the Murim Alliance... and you handled him quite easily."

The man wore a large bamboo hat and a loose, wide robe with long sleeves that hid his hands. He stepped out from the shade, his sleeves swinging gently.

"Is the Little Heavenly Demon asleep?" the Dark Supreme asked.

Lee Sungmin's face hardened as he stared at the man. Even though the Dark Supreme stood right before him, not too far away, he still felt strangely transparent. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, standing behind Lee Sungmin, also grew tense.

"It seems the Fist Honored One's curse is getting stronger, isn't it? To be this effective even on a Transcendent Realm martial artist... huhu! That Fist Honored One. I've never thought he was amazing when it comes to martial arts, but I have to admit his skill in curses."

"...Who... are you?"

"Heaven Beyond Heaven."

At Lee Sungmin's question, the man revealed his affiliation without hesitation.

"Have you heard about the Six Honored Ones from the Little Heavenly Demon? I am one of them, the Dark Supreme."

He was out of luck.

He had expected to meet an emissary from Heaven Beyond Heaven someday, but he never imagined it would be today. Only two hours had passed since he left the South Gate of Ruvus.

"You don't look too happy," the Dark Supreme said with a smile. He took off his bamboo hat and set it beside him. "There's no need to be so afraid. I have no intention of killing you. I just want to test you a little."

"A test?"

"I rate you highly. I've met many talented and promising young martial artists, but... huhu! I've never seen a seed as great as you."

"Is that... all you're here for?"

"Of course not," the Dark Supreme burst into laughter. He glanced at the sleeping Wiji Hoyeon in Lee Sungmin's arms and continued. "I'm also here to take the Little Heavenly Demon."

Lee Sungmin's face froze. From the moment he heard the names Heaven Beyond Heaven and Six Honored Ones, a red light had been flickering in the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes. He strode forward and spat his words.

"Didn't the Sword Saint come?"

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae." The Dark Supreme's eyes shifted to him. "It seems you still haven't forgotten your defeat to the Sword Saint ten years ago. Unfortunately, he didn't come. He's entered seclusion, perhaps having gained some new insight, but why are you looking for him? Do you want to avenge your fighting spirit from back then?"

"If possible."

"Impossible," the Dark Supreme said with a hearty laugh. "With your skills, you wouldn't be able to leave a single scratch on him."

He turned his gaze back to Lee Sungmin, his tone turning dismissive. "Don't interfere. Heaven Beyond Heaven is no longer interested in you. If you couldn't enter the Transcendent Realm after ten years, then that is your limit and your destiny. Heaven Beyond Heaven is never kind to the unqualified."

The Dark Supreme's attention settled once more on Lee Sungmin. "I intend to take the Little Heavenly Demon. If possible, I'd like her to agree and come with me... but it seems things won't go that smoothly. The one who should answer is asleep."

"I won't let you."

"......"

The Dark Supreme tilted his head. "Do you know what Heaven Beyond Heaven is? Do you know why, for what purpose, we are trying to take the Little Heavenly Demon? Are you trying to stop us unconditionally while knowing nothing? Think about it. We could have killed her many times, but we didn't."

"But you cursed her!"

"That was something the Fist Honored One did on his own," the Dark Supreme answered without a change in expression. "He was quite shocked that he couldn't subdue her with his own martial arts."

"That's why I came here. The Fist Honored One has been incapacitated. The Little Heavenly Demon has been weakened by his curse. Even someone at the peak realm could easily kill her in her current state, yet she made it to Ruvus without dying. Why do you think that is?"

Lee Sungmin couldn't answer. The Dark Supreme hadn't expected one anyway and continued.

"I did many things for her while she was unaware. I killed the many who came to recognize and threaten her when she wasn't looking. The Little Heavenly Demon was able to come all the way to Ruvus because I protected her from the shadows. I intentionally let the trouble with the Iron Armored Divine Spear happen. I was hoping she would start to despair at her own powerlessness. Though I never thought you, the Phantom Spear, would intervene and protect her."

"What are you trying to say?"

"Not much... just that we don't hold any great malice towards her. On the contrary, we are looking out for her. Do you know what kind of destiny the Little Heavenly Demon has?"

"What...?"

"It seems you don't know. I suppose there's no need to explain it to you. Still, this works out well. Our interests align, don't they?"

The Dark Supreme lightly shook his right hand. The wide sleeve rose, revealing a dagger the size of his palm. He held it up, shaking it as if to show it off.

"I must test you and take the Little Heavenly Demon, and you want to stop me. Am I wrong?"

"Where is the Fist Honored One?"

"There's no reason to tell you. You seem to have a lot of questions. Do you know? To easily get the answers you want, you must be stronger than your opponent. Are you stronger than me?"

[We have to attack together,] the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat. This was no time for pride. The man before them was the strongest martial artist Lee Sungmin had ever seen. He could never handle him alone.

*Will you help me?*

[I must,] the Crazy Heavenly Demon answered without hesitation.

Watching him raise his fighting energy, the Dark Supreme let out a laugh. "Planning to attack together? That's not a bad idea. Alright... Crazy Heavenly Demon. If you attack with the Phantom Spear, I won't kill you either."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's face contorted at the words, which were spoken as if granting mercy. His hair shot upwards. Lee Sungmin reached into his robes and pulled Rubia out.

"Wha-what is it?!"

"It might be dangerous. ...Please take Wiji Hoyeon."

Rubia hesitated for a moment before nodding. While keeping his eyes on the Dark Supreme, the Crazy Heavenly Demon carefully set down the pale, sleeping Wiji Hoyeon. Rubia scurried over and gently took her into her arms.

"I'm concerned that small fry might see," the Dark Supreme said as he reached into his robes. He pulled out a transparent sphere that fit in one hand. After shaking it lightly, he threw it into the sky.

PWAT!

The sphere emitted a bright light that enveloped the surroundings. Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon looked around, bewildered by the sudden sense of incongruity from the space itself.

"Don't be alarmed," the Dark Supreme said, beginning to walk toward them with long strides. "I've only sealed off the surrounding space. Isn't this more convenient for both of us? We won't have to worry about prying eyes."

"This is also a promise I'm making to myself. I won't lay a hand on the Little Heavenly Demon unless I subdue you two."

Lee Sungmin didn't listen to the end of his sentence.

He exploded forward, unleashing Dancing Shadow Soul Break at full power. Traceless Step created a blur of afterimages while One Step Dancing Shadow produced dozens of illusions. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was right behind him, his body wreathed in crimson qi, each leap shaking the very ground.

In the face of their reckless charge, the Dark Supreme stood without moving. He lightly flicked the dagger in his right hand.

"......"

The blade shot forward and slammed into the earth directly in front of Lee Sungmin's advancing foot.

KABOOOM!

The ground exploded, tearing upward and sending Lee Sungmin staggering backward. A thick cloud of dust scattered in all directions. Without panicking, he swung his spear wide to disperse it.

"Ugh...!"

Lee Sungmin's body froze. Faster than his sixth sense could scream a warning, daggers materialized from the dust cloud, grazing past his body from every direction.

PAPAPAK!

He trembled slightly as he looked at the dozens of daggers embedded in the ground around him. Thrown from beyond the dust, they had narrowly missed him, and the Protective Qi Aura surrounding his body hadn't even disturbed their trajectories.

"I missed on purpose," the Dark Supreme said, waving his now empty hand. He was holding a palm-sized disc, which he spun on his index finger as he glanced at the approaching Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"......"

A palm strike from the Crazy Heavenly Demon shot out. The Dark Supreme's body, however, flickered like smoke and vanished. He reappeared beside the Crazy Heavenly Demon, passing him with light steps. The Crazy Heavenly Demon contorted his face and swung his fist again. The result was the same. The Dark Supreme was a phantom, visible but without substance, his form dissipating like mist every time the Crazy Heavenly Demon's fist drew near.

[He's on a different level,] Void Monarch said in admiration.

Lee Sungmin leaped over the daggers in the ground and rushed at the Dark Supreme again. Glancing his way, the Dark Supreme threw the disc he had been spinning. It sliced silently through the air in a spinning arc, aimed directly for him. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear toward it, but the disc was not pierced. Just as his spear was about to connect, the disc's trajectory shifted impossibly. It swerved through the air with liquid grace and skimmed the back of Lee Sungmin's neck.

"......"

Blood flowed from the thinly sliced skin. Startled, Lee Sungmin raised a hand to the wound. If it had cut any deeper, it would have been fatal.

*I won't kill you.*

It was just as the Dark Supreme had said.
