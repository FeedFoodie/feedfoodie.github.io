*Could I win?*

For the first time in a long while, she felt no certainty. In all her life, only one opponent had ever made Wiji Hoyeon feel this way, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself. When she fought him, she had not been certain of her own victory. Not even the Fist Honored One, whom she had fought to a draw, had instilled such doubt. She knew that if he had not resorted to such a treacherous Buddhist method, if their fight had lasted just a little longer, victory would have been hers.

Wiji Hoyeon regarded him from within her floral martial arts theory, her expression calm.

*You've gotten really strong.* The acknowledgment was genuine, and a part of her was pleased to witness his achievement, but she could not bring herself to smile.

She refused to lose. Not yet. Not ever. She had to remain his goal, his aspiration, his ideal. To do that, she had to win, even if by the slimmest of margins. She wanted this victory.

Had she ever craved a win so intensely?

With that thought, Wiji Hoyeon kicked off the ground. Dozens of the flowers surrounding her burst open at once, exploding into hundreds, then thousands of petals that scattered with a mere gesture of her hand. They were petals of enhanced qi, each one engraving death with a single touch.

*I want to win.*

*Really?*

The thought echoed in Lee Sungmin's own mind, a familiar, ambiguous emotion, the same one he had felt when enraged by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's words. It felt like something would change if he won against her now, but one thing was certain. Wiji Hoyeon was giving it her all. To hold back against such an opponent would be a deception.

Besides, wasn't this what he had hoped for? He wanted to show her everything, to show her how much he had grown in eleven years.

Hundreds of petals rushed toward him. Demonic Energy and inner arts flooded the spear in his hands.

KWAREUREU!

When Lee Sungmin thrust his spear forward, nine dragons erupted from its tip, their maws opening wide to swallow the petals.

KWAAANG!

The Nine Dragons Slaughter scattered in a massive explosion, dispersing the deadly flowers. Wiji Hoyeon pierced through the ensuing blast without a hint of hesitation, her body wreathed in a chillingly beautiful Protective Qi Aura of flowers. She extended her hand forward.

Dozens more flowers exploded. The scattering petals coalesced into a palm force that struck at Lee Sungmin once more.

He met the attack head-on. The spear in his hand writhed, a single movement creating hundreds of phantom tassels. He advanced again, dragging his foot forward as he unleashed a devastating combination. The Three Step Fatality of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Blood Ring Typhoon of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, and the Four Step Chaos. The three linked martial arts completely dominated their surroundings.

Against the ferocious storm of enhanced qi rushing toward her, Wiji Hoyeon simply spread her arms. Hundreds of flower buds bloomed in succession, splitting into tens of thousands of petals.

*This isn't a human's martial art.* The thought struck him as she ceaselessly created such powerful petals of enhanced qi. The advantage of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, which endlessly accumulated inner arts, was on full display. The storm and the petals collided, and in the aftermath, nothing remained.

"......"

Wiji Hoyeon closed the distance silently, her open palm flying toward Lee Sungmin. He swung his spear to block, but she anticipated the move, her hand bending with unnatural flexibility. It dug in under the spear shaft, aiming for his chest, but Lee Sungmin dragged his foot back just before the palm could connect.

The distance opened again, just enough to swing a spear. He reversed its direction, striking back at her.

It didn't hit. She broke her posture, lowering herself so far she nearly fell over. Her movements were so swift, so fluid, it seemed as if she had no joints at all. Lee Sungmin mixed variations into his own spear techniques, matching her every move.

Chasing, creating distance. A fierce, close-range chase unfolded.

KUUUNG!

It ended with both of them getting what they wanted. Wiji Hoyeon's palm struck Lee Sungmin's chest as the spear shaft he swung slammed into her shoulder.

"Keuk!"

Lee Sungmin tasted blood, the force of her palm having penetrated his Magic Armor. Wiji Hoyeon staggered back, a sharp, throbbing pain shooting through her left arm.

Neither had suffered a severe internal injury, and no bones were broken. Neither had gained a clear advantage. In that case, they could only continue to fight. Wiji Hoyeon clenched her throbbing left arm into a fist and swung it.

A flower bud bloomed again. The petals burst forth at close range, her fist strike closing in simultaneously.

A Protective Qi Aura, tangled with Demonic Energy and inner arts, engulfed Lee Sungmin's body. He had chosen to endure rather than dodge. Countering her fist, his spear dropped down, then shot upward.

Serpent Soul Devouring. Wiji Hoyeon already knew that spear path.

*No.*

Just as she was about to react, her eyes widened. The spear path was twisting. A trajectory that should not connect was connecting, an impossible feat. The form was different, the movement was different, but how?

It was a movement that would shatter a human's joints and tear their muscles, but for Lee Sungmin, it was possible. He was used to *that* kind of pain. He forcefully moved his broken arms. The joints and muscles healed instantly, without need for an Elixir or potion.

The spear, swung in such an impossible way, aimed to pierce her stomach. Wiji Hoyeon, who knew the path of Serpent Soul Devouring, had been guarding high, leaving her lower body wide open. She hurriedly bent her waist back to dodge, but she couldn't avoid it completely.

Her Protective Qi Aura tore, and the clothes on her chest ripped along with it. Wiji Hoyeon backpedaled, fiercely swinging her hand. Her bandaged chest was visible, but she paid it no mind, and Lee Sungmin wasn't conscious of it either. He was watching her swinging hand.

Space was wavering. *This time*, enduring it with his Protective Qi Aura was impossible. As her hand stretched out, the wavering spread. Lee Sungmin hastily pulled his body back.

"...Huuuk!"

Wiji Hoyeon exhaled. Once again, hundreds of flowers bloomed, withered, and scattered. Amidst the swirling petals, Lee Sungmin saw the wavering of space. An unstoppable death dwelled within the splendor.

Lee Sungmin dragged his foot back to create distance, lowering his spear to infuse it with a swirling torrent of inner arts and Demonic Energy. Along the long spear shaft, the two forces tangled and swirled. Though he lacked the time to fully charge it, the resulting sky-piercing was still formidable.

The first blast collided with the petals, and as he took a few more steps back, he fired a second. The two strikes, fired with a slight delay, withered every last petal.

Wiji Hoyeon could feel her breathing growing rough. Her head felt a little dizzy, and the sensation in her hands and feet seemed to have dulled. She was overusing her still-incomplete martial art and continuously drawing upon the insights of the Transcendent Realm.

She couldn't gain the upper hand.

Unlike Wiji Hoyeon, who was gradually growing tired, Lee Sungmin did not tire. His inner arts and Demonic Energy were not running dry, and his Yokai body instantly healed most wounds.

JJEONG!

Lee Sungmin's spear thrust sent Wiji Hoyeon's body flying far back. The flowers she had turned to defense failed to bloom, simply bursting and vanishing. Panting, she extended her hand forward, but she was a little slower now. That was all the opening Lee Sungmin needed.

"......"

A short swing of his spear knocked her hand aside. Swinging sideways from there, the upward-striking Changjun hit her stomach.

"Keuheup!"

Wiji Hoyeon swallowed a sharp breath. She retreated a few steps, and Lee Sungmin stepped forward. The flowers no longer bloomed.

PABABAK!

Dozens of spear phantoms descended upon her. Wiji Hoyeon furrowed her brow, pushing them away with her one good hand. Staggering back, she knew that if this continued, she would be defeated.

*Ah.*

Then she realized it didn't even need to continue.

A sharply honed spearhead stopped right in front of her nose. If it had moved just a little further, that sharp tip would have pierced her head.

"It's over," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered.

Lee Sungmin said nothing, his spear held perfectly still. Seeing him like that, Wiji Hoyeon let out a small laugh.

"Why're you just standing there?"

He didn't answer. The flower buds that had failed to bloom vanished. Wiji Hoyeon pulled together the front of her clothes, which had been torn by the earlier attack.

"You won. Aren't you happy?"

*I don't know.*

Lee Sungmin slowly lowered his spear. Deep down, he had been hoping for this day. To meet Wiji Hoyeon again. To fight, just as they had promised in their childhood. He had thought that maybe he could win, but he had thought more about defeat than victory.

That was only natural. Compared to her, his natural talent was insignificant, and he was lacking in every aspect. That was why, even though he had imagined this moment, he couldn't quite accept the result.

"I lost."

Despite her defeat, Wiji Hoyeon actually felt relieved. *I don't want to lose,* she had thought over and over, but in the end, she had. What was she lacking? No, now wasn't the time to review the reasons for her defeat. She chuckled and looked at him.

"You... you've gotten incredibly strong," she said, her tone calm.

Lee Sungmin hesitated before nodding his head. Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing at his expression.

"What's with that face? Anyone would think you were the one who lost."

"...If you weren't cursed," Lee Sungmin finally managed to say. "If that were the case, you would have been even stronger than you are now."

"That's right." Wiji Hoyeon tapped him on the shoulder. "Don't state the obvious. I've only lost this one time. Eleven years ago, when we parted, I won. Today, you won. That makes it one to one, right?"

"Yeah."

"Next time, the result will be different. Honestly... while fighting you, I kept thinking. I didn't want to lose." She closed her eyes. "I felt that if you won, the meaning of my existence to you... it would fade a little."

"What do you mean?"

"You saw me as your goal, didn't you?" Wiji Hoyeon opened her eyes again. "I wanted to remain your goal forever. Seemingly within reach, but never quite attainable... but you've finally reached it."

"Do you hate that?"

"I do," she said, then burst out laughing. "That's why I have to run away. Again, to a place your hands can't reach. Because I... want to remain your goal forever."

*It's a contradiction,* she thought to herself. She was aware that the words she was speaking now were completely different from the emotions she felt deep in her heart. However, she didn't want to honestly reveal her inner thoughts.

*I'm clumsy.*

At talking with others. At speaking her emotions honestly. Wiji Hoyeon stared blankly at Lee Sungmin's face. To her, he was someone who held many meanings.

Her first friend.

After that, she had numerous encounters in Eria. There was even a time when followers who revered her martial arts theory gathered by her side. She had considered all of it bothersome and fleeting. The things they wanted from her were irritating.

"I need to rest a bit," Wiji Hoyeon said to him. "There's a lot I want to think about, and thanks to fighting you... I've realized the shortcomings of my newly created martial art. For the time being, I want to focus on supplementing it."

With that, she turned her body. Just as Lee Sungmin was about to say something, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman blocked his path.

"Let her go," the Chairman said with an indifferent face. "She's a prideful kid. Don't you think she needs some time alone?"

"I can hear you."

"I meant for you to hear it," the Chairman chuckled in response to her retort.

Wiji Hoyeon shot a glare at him. Space wavered and became a forest again. She kicked off the ground and disappeared into the woods.
