The forest teemed with monsters of all kinds. Some were beasts incapable of communication, while others possessed enough intelligence for a conversation of sorts. 

In terms of hunting difficulty, the former were much easier. Lacking intellect, they were simple creatures of instinct, like the giant rabbits and wild boars that roamed the woods. Of course, they were not 'real' rabbits and boars, only creatures that looked the part.

While beast-type monsters were easier to hunt, intelligent ones like goblins and orcs offered far greater potential gains. They were tricky to deal with, but defeating them could yield a variety of additional profits. The teeth and blood of goblins and orcs were valuable as magical ingredients, and their crude equipment could be sold at a blacksmith for a little pocket money. With a bit of luck, one might even find a monster carrying coin, though it was almost certainly stolen from the victims they had killed.

His first task was preparation. Lee Sungmin gripped the spear he had been carrying over his shoulder and sat down, pulling the dagger from the sheath behind his waist. It was not one of the throwing daggers he had purchased, but the one he had taken from the man who tried to rip him off the day before.

The spear shaft was over two meters long, so he began cutting it down to fit his height. Sawing through the hard wood with a small dagger was not easy, but he persisted until the task was done. Though the cut surface was rough, it was not a major problem. Lee Sungmin slung the severed piece of the shaft across his back, then grabbed the shortened spear and rose to his feet.

One dagger, three throwing daggers, one spear, and one club. Compared to his first foray into the hunting grounds in his past life, his equipment was overwhelmingly better. The lack of armor was a drawback, but with no money, it could not be helped.

*In my past life, I came out here with just a single club...*

He had joined three other Classless back then, and they had headed to the hunting grounds together. The memory was not a good one, and a bitter smile touched his lips as he recalled it.

He decided to start with a rabbit. Gripping the spear with both hands, Lee Sungmin began to move cautiously through the trees. He had no intention of picking a fight with goblins or orcs right from the start. Orcs were tougher than humans, and while goblins were weaker, they were cunning creatures who built martial arts theory and shot poison darts. In terms of sheer intelligence, goblins were superior to orcs.

Rabbits, however, were an easy opponent. Though large, they were only about the size of a medium-sized dog, and their attacks were limited to ramming or biting. As long as he was not careless, even a Classless could beat one to death with a simple club.

He had not walked far when the bushes rustled and a rabbit with grayish-white fur hopped out. It was a little smaller than a medium-sized dog, a young one rather than a full-grown adult. It was the perfect opponent to test his current condition.

The rabbit rolled its red eyes, looking at Lee Sungmin, but it did not attack. If left alone, it would simply nibble on the surrounding grass, hop around a few times, and then disappear.

This knowledge made Lee Sungmin bold. He quickly drew up his inner qi and executed the One Thunder Movement Arts, a technique that served as both a light-foot art and a movement art. Its basis was simple, releasing inner qi from the soles of his feet to make his body lighter.

His body shot forward at a speed unthinkable for a fourteen-year-old. As his entire body accelerated, Lee Sungmin thrust the spear.

Soul Chasing Spear Art solar eclipse, One Strike One Kill. His left hand supported the spear shaft, acting as a cannon. The forward-thrusting spear slid across his left palm like a cannonball being fired. When the spear had advanced far enough, his right hand struck the butt of the shaft, acting as the firing pin that pushed it forward.

"......"

The spear tip pierced the rabbit's head, which exploded on impact. One Strike One Kill was a spear art built entirely on acceleration. First the body accelerated, then the spear itself was launched from the cannon of his left hand by the firing pin of his right. Since it was all about shooting in a straight line, adding variations was impossible, and it would never work against a tough opponent. Against a rabbit, however, it was more than enough.

*My palm stings...!*

Lee Sungmin frowned and looked down at his left hand. His palm was red and burned from the friction of the spear shaft sliding across it. His right hand was in a similar state, throbbing from striking the rough, freshly cut end of the spear.

"I'll have to build up some calluses first."

A fourteen-year-old's skin was far too soft and weak. Lee Sungmin grumbled to himself, shaking his stinging hands.

There was not much to be gained from a rabbit, and he did not have an 'inventory' to store it in anyway. Inventories enchanted with subspace magic were sold in Zenavis, but they were traded at a price so high he could not even dream of affording one. Since he lacked even a simple bag, he decided to focus on his original plan for the day, which was to hunt a few goblins.

Of course, that was for after he had fully adapted to his body. Lee Sungmin wandered through the forest, hunting a few more lone rabbits. In the process, he confirmed several things. His current level of inner qi was not enough to properly execute the Soul Chasing Spear Art, a Second-Rate martial art, and a prolonged fight was out of the question. Moreover, his body was so weak that he became exhausted just from swinging the spear a few times.

*My physical strength is too low. My Earth power, too... To think I'd miss my past life after finally returning to the beginning.*

He grumbled as he tore a strip from his collar and wrapped it tightly around his bleeding palm, which had torn from executing the spear technique. He was hungry and thirsty, and the realization of how easily he tired from just a little movement made him let out a deep sigh.

He had no water and no food. If he had owned a bag, he would have packed some bread before leaving the inn, but he did not. Sighing repeatedly, Lee Sungmin began to walk, circulating the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. It was not as effective as sitting cross-legged to perform proper qi circulation, but since he was only walking calmly, his inner qi gradually replenished.

The deeper he went into the forest, the closer he got to the goblin and orc nests. He knew that heading to a nest in his current state was suicide, so he had no intention of going that far. He tried to recall his memories of this vast forest, but he could not remember a map. He had to rely on the fragmented images that remained.

Lee Sungmin walked on, examining the ground and the trees. Soon, he found what he was looking for, a tree with a circle drawn on it a little below his shoulder height. It was a goblin's territory marker.

Though he referred to them simply as goblins, the goblins in this forest did not all belong to a single tribe. They built martial arts theory and lived in settlements of about fifty, competing with other goblin tribes for territory. This area belonged to the settlement that used circles as its marker.

He examined the surrounding trees and confirmed that the only markers nearby were circles. Satisfied, he moved to the very edge of the territory and tore his sleeve a little more. He made a shallow cut on his upper left forearm, not too deep, and as blood began to trickle down, he soaked the torn piece of cloth with it and tossed it into a bush. After that, he put down his spear and rolled around on the ground for a long time, until his head was spinning and his body was covered in dirt.

Next, he urinated near the spot where he had thrown the blood-soaked cloth. Spreading his feet wide to avoid getting his pants wet, he staggered around, marking the area.

That should do it. Lee Sungmin tore another strip from his collar to tightly bandage the wound, then began to climb a nearby tree. With the limited physical strength of his young body, even this was not easy, and he had to use his inner qi to help himself up.

Hanging from a thick branch, Lee Sungmin slowly steadied his breathing. Now, all he had to do was wait. Goblins engaged in territorial disputes patrolled their areas relatively often, both to search for prey and to guard against goblins from other settlements.

About thirty minutes passed as he waited. Finally, the patrolling goblins appeared. There were three of them, each armed with a dagger and carrying a long stick hanging from its waist.

Poison darts.

goblins' physical abilities were nothing special. What made them tricky was their cunning, their skill with poison, and the fact that they built martial arts theory. The poison they used was not lethal but a paralytic toxin that robbed a target of their freedom of movement. For the current Lee Sungmin, facing three goblins alone was too risky, so he had planned to avoid a direct confrontation.

The goblins sniffed the air, catching the scent of urine, blood, and a human. They snorted and followed the smell to the spot where Lee Sungmin had prepared his trap. From his perch, he had a clear view of the tops of their heads.

Lee Sungmin drew a throwing dagger from his belt. After steadying his breath a few times, he drew up his inner qi.

PWAK!

The swiftly thrown dagger plunged downwards.

"KWEK!"

It embedded itself in the back of a goblin's neck as it tilted its head. The moment he confirmed the hit, Lee Sungmin threw his other daggers in quick succession. They fell from above without wavering, piercing the necks of the remaining two goblins. Lee Sungmin then leaped down from the branch.

It was quite a height, but by protecting his legs with inner qi, the jump was manageable. He drew the dagger from behind his waist and swung it at the staggering goblins, finishing them off.

The three goblins were dead. Lee Sungmin steadied his breath and pulled the daggers from the backs of their necks.

After that, he searched their corpses. Their daggers were covered in blood that had not been dry for long, which meant they had killed something in their territory before coming here. It was probably a Classless. A bitter smile formed on Lee Sungmin's face as he collected their daggers.

He found three daggers, three blowguns, and thirty poison darts. In addition, there were three vials of poison. He was lucky. They were also carrying a wallet, which must have been stolen from the Classless they killed.

The wallet contained 20,000 Eru. After taking everything the goblins had, Lee Sungmin used a dagger to slit their throats. He filled five potion bottles with goblin blood and pried open their mouths to pull out each of their teeth. With no bag, he had no choice but to stuff all the pulled teeth into his pockets.

And with that, the day's hunt was over.
