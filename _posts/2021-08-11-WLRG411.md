The Martial God's corpse burned to ash in a matter of moments. Once the flames had consumed all they could, they flickered and died. A passing wind scattered the last of the ashes, carrying them into the distance.

From the Moon Queen's remains, the light of the Spirit burst free. Her body began to boil and bubble, collapsing into a formless, twitching mass of flesh.

Lee Sungmin grimaced at the sight. He recognized the hunting leader he had met in the Elf Forest, or what was left of her. Revias's corpse. She had never regained her personality, not even at the very end. In a way, he thought, that might have been a relief. Even if she had regained her senses and cried out for vengeance for her father, she would have never succeeded. Dying so meaninglessly, without even a thought of revenge, was perhaps the kindest fate for her.

He clicked his tongue as the light of the Spirit drifted closer. This was his first direct contact with the administrator of this world. The Spirit, the being that had crafted a grand destiny with its traitorous counterpart, the Demonic Spirit, and now insisted on guiding that destiny toward The End.

Lee Sungmin had escaped fate and could not be entangled in another. The Spirit's power, which had claimed the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Void Monarch, held no sway over him. He knew this, yet a knot of tension tightened in his gut. It was his first time facing it. Within his mind, the Demonic Spirit let out a low groan.

"Interesting."

The light hummed, its voice forming from the ambient air.

"Very, very interesting. You are the very proof of humanity's potential."

The light quivered, and Lee Sungmin realized with a jolt that it was laughing.

"Even if it was the result of repeated coincidences, that too is a possibility. Because of you, this world has had meaning."

The Spirit sounded sincere. In response, Lee Sungmin channeled power into the Formless Spear clutched in his hand.

*If I attack it here, can I kill it? Can I put an end to this damn thing called The End?*

"Stop," the Spirit said. "It is true that your power is strong enough to threaten my existence, but you cannot harm me here."

[It's a mere terminal,] the Demonic Spirit remarked quietly.

The Spirit's light quivered again.

"I wondered what you were doing after your precious toy was stolen. You, of all beings, should know how futile this is."

The sneer was not for him, but for the Demonic Spirit.

*A precious toy.*

The words made Lee Sungmin's cheek twitch. He released a torrent of mysterious phenomena and Dragon's Pressure that made the very space tremble.

"It's simply marvelous. In all the repetitions until now, the Black Heart has always existed, but it has never produced such a result. Even considering the overlapping coincidences and the Grace of Fate… it's a shame. A great shame. It's such a pity that the Void Monarch and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman escaped spacetime."

The Spirit knew that even if this world met its scheduled End and a new one began, it would be impossible to replicate Lee Sungmin. The two who had contributed most to his creation, the Void Monarch and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, had followed Kim Jonghyun's path into the Battle God Chronicle, escaping spacetime forever. They would not exist in the next world.

"…I have a lot of questions," Lee Sungmin said, reining in his hostility. Smashing this terminal would not harm the Spirit's main body, and besides, he was not even sure if killing it was the right thing to do.

[Shouldn't you first consider whether it's possible or not?]

He ignored the Demonic Spirit's jab. This world was a tapestry woven from intersecting interests. He knew of the Elemental Realm, the Fairy Realm, the Great Demon World, and the Battle God Chronicle. Perhaps every dimension that had ever sent an Otherworlder to Eria was entangled here.

[That's right. This place is a Slaughterhouse used by everyone. The Battle God Chronicle only joined in most recently, though.]

The Demonic Spirit confirmed his unspoken thought just as the Spirit asked, "What are you curious about?"

The surprisingly favorable reaction made Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitch.

"…If I ask, will you answer honestly?"

"I see no reason why I can't."

"But I'm supposed to be your enemy."

"You are an excellent guinea pig," the Spirit declared. "You've shown the greatest results in this world. Because of your existence, I can joyfully plan for the 'next' one."

*An excellent guinea pig.*

Lee Sungmin shrugged and let out a dry laugh.

"…Why did you let the Martial God and the Moon Queen die?"

"You've avenged your Master. Aren't you pleased?"

"Strictly speaking, you're the real enemy. It's just… I can't accept it. It bothers me. You were the one protecting the Martial God all this time. You ruined the tracking attempt made with sorcery."

"Because the time wasn't right," the Spirit answered without a hint of hesitation. "At that point, there was no reason for you to meet him. I judged that the Martial God wasn't enough of a stimulus for you, and likewise, you weren't enough of a stimulus for him."

"…Stimulus?"

"In the end, I was disappointed with the Martial God, and when you think about it, that's because of you. I had high hopes for the potential you showed, and I held the Martial God to the same standard."

The Spirit's words painted a chilling picture.

*Stimulus, growth.*

The words were obvious, weren't they? He had been weak, talentless, and unskilled. In his fight with Jeniela, if she had truly tried to kill him from the start… he would have died quickly. Was that also a coincidence? Or was it the Spirit's design? Jeniela had toyed with him, trying to inflict Despair rather than death. That event had driven the Void Monarch into the Battle God Chronicle and awakened Lee Sungmin.

"With that, Jeniela fulfilled her entire role."

"…What if I had died to her?"

"Then I would have tried using the Martial God instead," the Spirit replied with a laugh. "Though, he probably would have died to Jeniela as well."

Lee Sungmin understood what the Spirit wanted. It was simple. 'Results.' This world, unlike all the repetitions before it, had too many variables. Consequently, it had succeeded in heading in a completely new direction.

"Thank you."

Who was that gratitude for?

"Thanks to you, I was able to achieve good results. If the Martial God had also overcome his wall through his fight with you, it would have been an excellent outcome, but what's not meant to be is not meant to be. Even without the Black Heart, he had outstanding talent, so I had hoped… but his inferiority complex and pride held him back. Still, I can look forward to the 'next' time. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who gave him that complex, won't exist then."

He remembered the Martial God taking his own life with a self-deprecating laugh. What must he have thought in his final moments, realizing he was nothing but a puppet?

"And the Moon Queen is not even worth mentioning," the Spirit added dismissively. "Her only role was to support the Martial God, a vessel prepared to stimulate him. Is that a satisfactory answer?"

"…Why… why did you take Wiji Hoyeon?"

"Because I thought I could use her," the Spirit answered instantly. "I know about your relationship with her. You can never leave Wiji Hoyeon alone. You just can't. Your feelings aside, if you were to ignore her, she would bring this world to an end on her own."

However, it was not just Wiji Hoyeon.

"You are entangled in many things. You can escape fate alone, but what about the others? Can you abandon them?"

He couldn't.

"The time has come to decorate the grand finale. An ending will come, one way or another. Come to the Sleeping Forest. I will open the way for you…"

The Spirit's light flickered. As it began to disappear, Lee Sungmin shot the Formless Spear forward.

BAAANG!

Space ruptured. The light was utterly annihilated. He knew destroying the terminal would not harm the Spirit, but the simple act of venting his rage felt necessary.

"Son of a bitch."

Lee Sungmin spat, staring at the spot where the Spirit had vanished. He let the foul feeling wash over him as he willed the Formless Spear to disappear. He descended slowly to the ground and strode toward the castle gate.

"Hiik…!"

The people near the gate recoiled, letting out terrified sounds at his approach. Lee Sungmin ignored them. His hand stretched out toward the twitching mass of the Moon Queen's flesh.

FWOOSH!

A surge of flame incinerated it completely. He met the terrified gazes one by one.

Monster.

Someone muttered. It was not wrong, so he did not refute it.

[Are you going?]

Instead of answering the Demonic Spirit, he turned and walked away. There was no reason to remain in the city. The fight with the Martial God had barely tired him. He did not hurry, however. He simply set his direction toward the Sleeping Forest and walked, lost in thought.

*The time has come to decorate the grand finale…*

The Spirit's final words echoed in his mind. He knew what the grand finale was.

[There's nothing more to be gained,] the Demonic Spirit said. [Jeniela is dead and Predator has been annihilated. Gemini is rallying the Vampires, but they can't produce any results through them.]

*The inhuman are strong enough.*

[But they're weaker than you. Since no more results can be achieved, it's time to wrap things up. If The End is carried out and the next world opens… it will actively intervene in fate using the results gained from this world.]

The Demonic Spirit muttered, [Though it would be difficult to create an excellent guinea pig like you again.]

[It has learned the potential held within the Black Heart. In the next world, the Demon King of Fear, who contracted with Preskan, will gladly offer Preskan's soul. Then, that thing will create a Black Heart again.]

Lee Sungmin listened quietly to the rambling.

[Through this world, the Spirit has clearly learned the turning point for changing fate. I don't know who the Black Heart will be guided to in the next world… but the Spirit will try various things. It's a turning point finally discovered after tedious repetition.]

*What happens to you?*

[I don't know… I won't be annihilated, though. Perhaps I'll still be managing this world in the next one, just like now.]

Even the Demonic Spirit was not certain. *Useless bastard,* Lee Sungmin grumbled internally.

"What the hell are you guys?" he asked aloud, a question he had always wanted to ask. "I know you're not all-powerful. You manage the world and govern fate, but you can't even fix major errors or interfere with those who escape. What the hell are you?"

[…We are… administrators,] the Demonic Spirit sighed. [Beings created when this world first opened. That's why we cannot do things beyond our authority. No, it's the same for everyone.]

[As you already know, this world is entangled with various interests. The common goal is to obtain the perfect technique…]

*Why do they want to obtain it?*

[That's a stupid question,] the Demonic Spirit retorted. [Let me ask you this instead. Is there any reason not to obtain it? The world is irrational and absurd. Some are born with outstanding talent, while others are foolish from birth. Birth, death, reincarnation. Not even the greatest of gods can govern these things. They are the laws and promises of the universe.]

*But I regressed.*

[This world is the sole exception to those laws. The many dimensions connected to Eria, the dangerous and powerful Demon Lords of the Great Demon World, the Elemental Realm, the Fairy Realm, and the Battle God Chronicle… all of them have tacitly accepted Eria as an exception.]

Because each of them wanted something.

[This world has existed under their silent approval and assistance. It has produced sufficient results so far. The techniques that have progressed in different directions with each iteration are recorded in the 'library', and that is made public to all those who allow this world to exist.]

Lee Sungmin's steps came to a halt.

"Ending The End… means we can no longer receive their assistance."

[You have the key to the library,] the Demonic Spirit muttered. [It was meant for Wiji Hoyeon, but it's actually better that it belongs to you. What you do… with it is up to you.]

Lee Sungmin felt the presence of the key inside his robes.

"It's just a replica, though."

[Don't be mistaken,] the Demonic Spirit snapped, provoked by his dismissive tone. [It may not be the key to the 'real' library, but it is the key to Eria's library. The real library is something a being like me cannot even dare to interfere with.]

[What are you going to do? Are you going to kill… Wiji Hoyeon, who is under the Spirit's protection?]

*Say something that makes sense.*

Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh at the question.

*There's no way I could kill her.*
