From his hiding spot near the Tang Clan building, Lee Sungmin watched Tang Ahee slip out through her window. He had been tracking her condition with the crystal ball in his hand, and the moment she jumped, he shot forward like an arrow loosed from its bow.

He vaulted the wall and bounded across the rooftops, a silent blur in the night. In the backyard of the annex he reached in an instant, the Dark Supreme stood with Tang Ahee.

*The Dark Supreme.*

The sight of him was all the confirmation Lee Sungmin needed. Without a moment's hesitation, he unleashed the Gale Thunder of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and charged.

Violet currents arced through the air, and the Dark Supreme's eyebrows twitched. He had felt the vicious energy radiating from the approaching figure, but even after discerning its power, Lee Sungmin's speed was simply too great. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was a martial art that pursued the very pinnacle of speed, and Gale Thunder was its fastest movement technique.

"Hmm?!"

The Dark Supreme's expression flinched. He immediately swung a wide sleeve, firing a dagger toward the crackling violet energy.

It was no threat.

FWIP!

Lee Sungmin's spear, already in hand, carved a half-circle through the air and batted the blade aside. In the same fluid motion, he closed the remaining distance.

Startled by this sudden assault, the Dark Supreme moved to shield his great-great-granddaughter, swinging both hands forward. Dozens of needles, each thinner than a strand of hair, shot out from within his sleeves. In the past, Lee Sungmin would never have been able to follow the dagger's movement, let alone block it, but that was no longer the case. A wave of violet qi erupted from the spear's sweep, atomizing the cloud of needles.

Though surprised by Lee Sungmin's skill, the Dark Supreme continued to swing his hands, unleashing a torrent of various hidden weapons with each flick of his wrists.

*Who is this guy?*

He could not place Lee Sungmin's identity. The man wore a human skin mask, and the energy he emitted had changed so drastically from their last encounter in Ruvus that it was almost unrecognizable.

*This is...*

However, the martial art he was using, the spear technique and the electric currents that flared with every surge of qi, was unmistakable. The Dark Supreme's face grew rigid. He knew that art all too well. There was no way he could ever forget it.

Over a hundred years ago, when he had first left the Tang Clan to make his name in the world, he had suffered the first, most terrible, and most humiliating defeat of his life. At the time, Yang Ilcheon had not yet become the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, nor had he earned the title of Demon Emperor, but even then, he was a monstrously strong individual, a fact not so different from the present. A chance encounter had led to a duel, and the Dark Supreme had learned firsthand, with his own body, just how formidable the then-incomplete Heaven Reversal Black Thunder truly was.

*Don't tell me... Heaven Reversal Black Thunder?*

Anxiety sprouted in his chest as he watched the flowing currents. As far as he knew, only two people in the world used that art. One was the Demon Emperor, Yang Ilcheon. The other was the successor he had chosen, the Phantom Spear, and wasn't this man now wielding a spear? The Dark Supreme's face turned pale.

"...The Phantom Spear?" he murmured.

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod, not bothering to remove the mask. He had to subdue the Dark Supreme as quickly as possible.

Behind her great-great-grandfather, Tang Ahee was flustered by the sudden attack. She was not a complete fool, however, and the combination of the spear technique and the violet currents pushing back the Dark Supreme was enough for her to see through the disguise.

"It's him!" she shouted. "Great-great-grandfather, please subdue him! That's my request!"

She couldn't understand why the Phantom Spear had appeared and started a fight, but she was actually pleased with this turn of events. It had saved her the trouble of having to search for him. Tang Ahee had no doubt that her great-great-grandfather would capture him.

A grim understanding settled on the Dark Supreme's face. He knew exactly why this was happening. He had come here for a brief meeting on his great-great-granddaughter's birthday, a fact known only to the two of them. Not even Heaven Beyond Heaven was aware of this annual outing, which lasted only a few hours.

*She told him!*

This was no coincidence. The Phantom Spear had known he would be here and had been waiting. The Dark Supreme bit his lower lip so hard he tasted blood. A surge of rage toward his careless great-great-granddaughter washed over him. In the twenty-odd years he had been meeting the child, he had never once scolded her. If she were not his cherished descendant, he would have torn her to shreds on the spot.

RUMBLE!

The violet Protective Qi Aura surrounding Lee Sungmin's body swelled, crackling like a thundercloud. The Dark Supreme gritted his teeth. He knew this man had killed the Sword Saint. A head-on fight was a battle he could never win. The Phantom Spear had become incomparably stronger than when they had met in Ruvus a few years ago.

*I have to run. No, if I do that, what about the child? What about the Tang Clan?*

The thoughts rooted him to the spot. Though he had left the clan, it was still his family, and though he was furious at Tang Ahee's idiocy, she was still a descendant he cherished.

With a roar, the Dark Supreme made his choice.

"Wake everyone in the family! Get them out of here immediately!"

"G-great-great-grandfather?!"

"Hurry!" he shouted, drawing upon the full depth of his internal energy.

WHOOSH!

A jet-black Protective Qi Aura enveloped his body. He could not pose a significant threat with throwing weapons alone. In that case, he would have to use something else. He ran a hand over his belt, and a thin, flexible sword was released into his grasp. Hidden weapons were not just for throwing. They were concealed weapons, and befitting his title, the Dark Supreme was a master of them all.

He swung the flexible sword, which bent and whipped through a curving trajectory like a sharp-bodied snake. Lee Sungmin met the complex attack without hesitation. The moment their weapons collided, the flexible sword shattered into pieces, and the Dark Supreme's eyes filled with shock.

He roared once more as Tang Ahee hesitated, frozen in place. The Dark Supreme's hair stood on end and his robes billowed out as he prepared to unleash his full power, something Lee Sungmin had never witnessed in Ruvus. The vicious energy he emitted made Tang Ahee tremble in fear.

"Don't make me kill you!"

At his shout, Tang Ahee let out a startled shriek. She had wet herself again. Covering her wet crotch with her hands, she turned and fled.

Seeing her leave the area, the Dark Supreme acted without delay. He had told her to evacuate the entire family, but in truth, it was enough for him if only she escaped. With her safety confirmed, he lunged at Lee Sungmin.

The Dark Supreme had not mastered poison arts, but that did not mean he was ignorant of their use. He rarely resorted to them, but if victory was the goal, he had to use even the things he disliked. A small bead slid down from inside his sleeve. It was a poison pellet created with the Tang Clan's secret formula, designed to fill the surroundings with a deadly mist when shattered. Prepared for the poison himself, he crushed the pellet without a second thought.

PSSH!

A black poison mist engulfed Lee Sungmin.

The Dark Supreme, however, did not know about his opponent's unique constitution. Even as the mist spread, Lee Sungmin's movements did not slow in the slightest. A master of the Transcendent Realm would be resistant to most poisons, but this was no trivial toxin. Seeing Lee Sungmin's actions remain completely unchanged, the Dark Supreme was inwardly shocked.

[It seems that man doesn't know you have Myriad Poison Immunity,] Void Monarch chuckled.

The Dark Supreme extended both hands and unleashed a palm strike, his deadly enhanced qi mixing with the poison mist, but having perfected Myriad Poison Immunity, Lee Sungmin felt no significant threat. Furthermore, his armor and spear, forged from Dragon materials, would not melt, and he could easily cut through that level of enhanced qi.

The Dark Supreme was horrified as Lee Sungmin charged through the mist. In many ways, this was the worst possible opponent for him. His hidden weapons were fast and stealthy but lacked decisive power, and Lee Sungmin's new armor and Protective Qi Aura were too sturdy to be pierced. His swift hand movements were several steps behind Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, and even his area-denial poison arts could not breach Myriad Poison Immunity. Among the Six Honored Ones, there was likely no master with a worse compatibility against the current Lee Sungmin.

That did not, however, mean the Dark Supreme was a pushover. His feet began to move, unfolding an elusive movement art that left no trace of his presence. He darted through the poison mist, leaving behind faint afterimages like a Ghost Steed.

In response, Lee Sungmin lifted his foot, choosing to meet movement arts with movement arts.

Shadowless Soul Severing Three Forms. Two Step Calamity, thunderclap. His first right step summoned a thundercloud. When he took the second step.

KRA-KOOM!

The thunderclouds that had spread throughout the area simultaneously shot out bolts of purple lightning. Dancing Shadow Soul Break, now infused with the insights of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, could no longer be called a mere movement art. It was a fatal energy technique created through steps.

"Keuk!"

Black blood spurted from the Dark Supreme's mouth as he collided with the erupting lightning. Despite being cloaked in his Protective Qi Aura, he felt an excruciating pain, as if his internal organs were on fire. He twisted his face in a grimace and moved his right hand, a disc the size of his palm gripped within it.

WHIRRRR!

The disc, hooked on his finger, began to spin furiously. Entwined with poison mist and enhanced qi, it left his hand. This was no simple throw. He had keenly felt that ordinary hidden weapon arts could not threaten Lee Sungmin. His eyes widened as he followed the weapon's movement.

SHWACK!

Space tore open, and the disc vanished inside. It had penetrated a rift in space only to reappear by piercing through a completely different point. This was the insight of the Transcendent Realm combined with his hidden weapon arts. It was an unavoidable attack. Even Lee Sungmin was inwardly surprised. He quickly twisted his body, trying to dodge the weapon that now came flying from right behind his back.

*Too late.*

He knew it instinctively. It would not be a fatal wound, but he could not dodge it completely. It was a poisoned weapon. If not for his Myriad Poison Immunity, even a slight graze would have been a great threat. Lee Sungmin felt a flash of gratitude toward Void Monarch.

A completely different sound from what the Dark Supreme had expected rang out.

Lee Sungmin was also momentarily stunned, his movements freezing. The hidden weapon that grazed his left shoulder, no, it couldn't even graze it. The moment it made contact, the disc's rotation stopped, its edge blunted. Both men stared down at the disc that had lost its power and fallen to the ground, at a loss for words.

[That armor is quite sturdy,] Void Monarch muttered, sounding as dumbfounded as they were.

The deadly hidden weapon, which had warped space itself to attack, had failed to tear through the Magic Armor Lee Sungmin was wearing. Part of its power had weakened while penetrating his Protective Qi Aura, but Dragon scales were one of the sturdiest materials in existence. On top of that, the armor had been crafted by the Dwarf chieftain Mackendor and the Meister Celgerus.

"What kind of armor is..." the Dark Supreme breathed, having never imagined such an outcome. He had been confident it would at least inflict a small wound, yet it had not even left a scratch.

"...Hmm," Lee Sungmin muttered, looking at the fallen weapon. "I've been dodging and blocking for no reason all this time."

With those words, he charged straight at the Dark Supreme.
