"The Phantom Spear protected the Little Heavenly Demon and took her away."

Back in his room, the man settled onto the bed and picked up the crystal ball. It reacted instantly to his words, humming with a silvery light.

[The Phantom Spear?] a voice echoed from the crystal. [You mean that little demonic practitioner who killed the Blood Heavenly Demon of the North?]

The man remained silent.

[I left him alone because he seemed so immature, but it seems our fates are intertwined after all. Should I say this turned out well?]

"I think you're wrong about him being immature," the man said, pulling the wide-brimmed hat from his head. He let out a dry laugh, using both hands to lift the long, black bangs from his face. "The Phantom Spear subdued the Iron Armored Divine Spear. Even if the Iron Armored Divine Spear wasn't using his full strength, it's an astonishing martial achievement for someone his age."

[He's not on a level to be compared with the Little Heavenly Demon,] the voice from beyond the crystal replied coldly.

The man began tying back his hair with a strap. "I think that old man, the Spear King, will be interested."

[That old man?] the other party sneered. [The one who, at his age, still finds his greatest joy in just swinging his own spear?]

Having secured his hair, the man shrugged. "He can't be compared to the Little Heavenly Demon, of course, but I think he's reached a level where we need to pay attention."

[If the Phantom Spear is protecting her, then we'll get entangled with him too. More importantly, where are they now? The Little Heavenly Demon and the Phantom Spear?]

"At a branch of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance."

[I thought they would have fled the city.]

Hearing that, the man clicked his tongue and shed the cloak he was wearing. Beneath it, his body was a lethal silhouette, clad in tight black clothes and bristling with an arsenal of hidden weapons. He began setting them down one by one as he spoke.

"I don't know why I have to clean up the mess Fist Honored One made."

[It can't be helped,] the voice said placatingly. [Among us, you are the one who can move most stealthily. The Dark Supreme.]

The Dark Supreme let out another dry laugh. Fist Honored One had left triumphantly, declaring he would bring back the Little Heavenly Demon, only to return covered in wounds. A battle with Wiji Hoyeon that had raged for three days and nights had exhausted him, but the curse he inflicted upon her was what truly immobilized him. As an Elf, Fist Honored One was not constrained by a short lifespan and had practiced his fist arts for hundreds of years, a long life that also allowed him to develop a deep knowledge of sorcery. The other Six Honored Ones of Heaven Beyond Heaven often mocked him for wasting time on such things, claiming his martial arts were not that outstanding as a result, yet they all avoided conflict with him, because his sorcery and curses were just that exceptional.

Sorcery, however, and curses in particular, demanded a heavy price for their certainty. Fist Honored One was now suffering from the exact same curse as Wiji Hoyeon. Unable to even move properly or circulate his inner qi, he was hiding deep within the Elf Forest.

"When would be a good time to bring her?" the Dark Supreme asked.

[I was going to wait for her to give up, for her spirit to break,] the voice from the crystal sounded annoyed. [I thought her spirit would shatter if her life was threatened by a weakling she would normally ignore, so I was planning to leave her be. I never expected a kid like the Phantom Spear to step up and protect her.]

[If they're holed up in a branch of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, it's tricky to touch them. Even if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman isn't interested in external affairs, he won't overlook things happening in his own territory.]

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, was a silent dragon. Provoking him needlessly would only make things troublesome.

[There's no way to break the curse without killing Fist Honored One. Well, there's no need to rush. It will happen soon enough.]

*She lost her power overnight and became weak. I don't think the Little Heavenly Demon, who remembers her position as one of the strong, can endure that humiliation.*

[If the Phantom Spear and the Little Heavenly Demon leave the city, kill the Phantom Spear right in front of her eyes.]

"It's a waste to kill him," the Dark Supreme countered. "And he's with the Crazy Heavenly Demon."

[We have no more business with the Crazy Heavenly Demon. If he hasn't entered the Transcendent Realm after ten years, he's a lost cause. Dark Supreme, you seem to rate the Phantom Spear very highly. Have you taken a liking to him?]

"Exceptional youths always catch my eye," he admitted. "In my opinion, that fellow is the most outstanding after the Little Heavenly Demon. Jihak of Shaolin, Chwi Geol of the Beggars' Gang, Cheongmyeong of Wudang. That Chwi Geol fellow is nothing special. Jihak still needs to be watched, and Cheongmyeong is untouchable because the Tai Chi Sword Emperor is keeping him under wraps."

The three he mentioned were young masters known as the future of their respective sects. Among them, the one most highly regarded by both the Dark Supreme and Heaven Beyond Heaven was Cheongmyeong of Wudang. He was the sole disciple of the Tai Chi Sword Emperor, the Wudang sect leader from two generations prior, and his skills remained a complete mystery.

"From the Four Great Clans, the Sword Dragon is quite decent, and there are many promising ones in the Six Demonic Gates and Three Demonic Clans as well."

[And if you compare them to the Phantom Spear?]

"Honestly? I covet him." The Dark Supreme grinned, baring his teeth.

Heaven Beyond Heaven was greedy. If a master showed the potential to enter the Transcendent Realm, they had to be met and tested. In response to the Dark Supreme's answer, a laugh echoed from beyond the crystal.

[Then do as you please. You can kill him, or you can test him.]

The Dark Supreme nodded and set the crystal ball down. As it left his hand, its light faded. A quiet stillness fell over the room as he began to maintain his discarded hidden weapons one by one. He had reached a level long ago where he no longer needed such things, yet he always carried them on his person.

It put his mind at ease. The distinct feel and coldness of the blades gave him a sense of comfort.

*I wonder when they'll come out.*

He thought this as he looked out the window, where the building of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance branch was visible not far away.

{sep}

There was no way for them to find Fist Honored One. Even after mobilizing all of Erebrisa's information network, his location could not be confirmed, which was proof that the organization called Heaven Beyond Heaven operated beyond the notice of all information Guilds. Lee Sungmin had even sought information from the murim's own networks, like the Beggars' Gang and the Rogues' Guild, but all he could obtain were fragments.

[Erebrisa is an intermediary Guild,] Nebul's voice echoed in his mind. [Our role is simply to act as an intermediary with connected Guilds and deliver messages through a Broker. If they don't have information on Heaven Beyond Heaven, we can't provide it. Even if they do know, we can't deliver it if they choose not to share. Out of goodwill for you, Lee Sungmin, I've acted on your requests a few times until now, but this case is different.]

For Nebul to personally deliver a letter to those who were not intermediary partners was something he should not have done in the first place. Lee Sungmin sighed and lowered his head. It was strange. An organization with six masters of the Transcendent Realm, yet information about them was almost nonexistent. He bit his lower lip.

[Going to the South might be the answer,] Void Monarch spoke to him. [Sorcerers are common there. A Buddhist might be able to lift the curse, but you'll have a better chance of finding a way in the South than just suffering here.]

He had already met quite a few sorcerers through Erebrisa's intermediation, but none could lift Wiji Hoyeon's curse. Unlike the Mages Guild, sorcerers were not organized, which limited the number he could meet.

"The hunting dogs of the Murim Alliance are heading to Ruvus," Gwan Hu said, serving him tea. "The Blood Chasing Execution Squad are particularly nasty ones. In terms of martial power, they may be weaker than you, but they are skilled in pursuit and harassment."

Lee Sungmin did not answer. Gwan Hu's words were forcing a choice. Joining the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance would grant him its protection, but it would also make him a complete enemy of the Murim Alliance.

He closed his eyes. Orthodox and unorthodox. He had lived his life without caring about such things because he never had a choice. He had been a mercenary, a Classless. The Lee Sungmin of his past life was not great enough to stand at such a crossroads, but what about now?

"What kind of place is the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?" he asked.

"It's a place teeming with demonic practitioners," Gwan Hu said with a calm smile. "The distinction between orthodox and unorthodox is meaningless in this world, but most demonic practitioners come from the unorthodox sects. The martial arts they learn are more violent, and the arts they master change their disposition. Perverts and murderers are quite common within our ranks."

"Don't you control them?"

"We do, to some extent, but desire is like an awl in a pocket. If you push it in deep, it will eventually pierce through the cloth and stick out." Gwan Hu laughed heartily. "That's how they become demonic heads. Their suppressed desires explode, and they run rampant. When that happens, the Alliance no longer protects them. The righteous Warriors of Justice or the Murim Alliance will take care of them for us."

His laughter quieted.

"Let's leave," Lee Sungmin said to Wiji Hoyeon.

She looked up at him with listless eyes. "To where?"

"Let's head to the South first. They say there are skilled sorcerers there, so we should try to meet them."

"The South... that's far from here. What are you going to do about the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?"

"There's no guarantee they'll unconditionally protect us just for joining. The Murim Alliance will pursue us, but we'll have to shake them off as best we can while we travel."

"If you do that, you'll become a public enemy of the orthodox sects. Don't you care?"

"So what?" he retorted. "Do you want me to abandon you and leave?"

At his words, Wiji Hoyeon let out a low laugh and said nothing more. After a short silence, she spoke again. "What if Heaven Beyond Heaven comes for us?"

"It doesn't matter."

"They want to take me."

"But you don't want to go."

"No."

"Then that's all that matters."

"What's all that matters?" Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing.

This feeling, this situation, was a first for her. She felt helpless, having never imagined she would be protected by Lee Sungmin, who was weaker than her. Surprisingly, it didn't feel bad. It was a strange sensation, being protected by someone, becoming this helpless. She stared at him with a dazed look in her eyes.

An envoy from Heaven Beyond Heaven could arrive at any moment, and the Murim Alliance's hunting dogs were already on their way. The moment they left the branch, they would become targets.

However, they couldn't stay here forever. This place was not unconditionally safe. He had come here seeking immediate shelter while she was unconscious, but now that she had awakened and he had met up with the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia, there was no longer a need to remain.

*Maybe the Golden Magic Tower... no, that place isn't unconditionally safe either.*

He had considered entrusting her protection to Lloyd but decided against it. He didn't want to involve his friend, and there was no guarantee that place was safe. *Then is it safe for her to be with me?* Lee Sungmin couldn't answer that question. He and the Crazy Heavenly Demon were among the strongest of the Supreme Peak masters, but the problem wasn't the Murim Alliance. It was the envoy from Heaven Beyond Heaven, who could arrive at any time.

Lee Sungmin did not know that the Dark Supreme, one of the Six Honored Ones, was watching from nearby. If he had known, he might have made a different choice. Because he didn't know, he informed Gwan Hu of his departure.

"Is that so." Gwan Hu was not surprised, as if he had already known what Lee Sungmin would do. He nodded with a calm expression. "Do you have any regrets about that choice?" he asked.

Lee Sungmin paused and looked into Gwan Hu's eyes.

His pupils were spinning.

"I won't say this place is absolutely safe," the man who looked like Gwan Hu said. "One never knows what might happen, but in the current situation, it's quite safe. If you leave, the hunting dogs drunk on the illusion of justice will try to tear you apart, and the old monsters who put the Little Heavenly Demon in her current state will be after you."

"...Who are you?"

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman," the being answered. "Don't be too nervous. I'm merely speaking through a terminal engraved in the branch manager's spirit."

"How could I not be nervous when you tell me not to be?"

"Haha. That's true. So, what's your answer? Any regrets about your choice?"

"You said it yourself. That this place isn't unconditionally safe."

"That's right. I may be speaking through a terminal, but my own location is quite far from here. If the Murim Alliance's hunting dogs use brute force, our forces in Ruvus won't be able to stop them. Not to mention Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"You know about Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

"Of course, I do."

"Nothing will change by dragging our feet here."

"It seems you have no regrets. I have no intention of stopping you. If you want to leave, then leave," the Chairman replied in a clear voice. "I didn't speak to you to stop you."

"Then what is it?"

"Whatever the result of your choice, it is something you must bear. Whether you wish to remain gray or simply don't want to choose is not my concern. However, if you choose to become black, come find me in Legro Forest. I will be there."

"...What?"

"There's no need to rush. You said you're going to the South? Come after you've finished your business there. Even if you came looking for me now, I'm not in a position to meet you."

"Wait. I never said I would go..."

"That too is your choice," the Chairman said with a laugh. "I'm not forcing you. Whether you come or not, you are the one to decide."

The Chairman's words ended there. The light vanished from Gwan Hu's eyes. He stood blankly for a moment before the light returned, and he let out a deep sigh.

"Goodness, the Chairman. It would have been nice if he'd given me a heads-up!"

Lee Sungmin was just as flustered by the sudden contact, but he knew pressing Gwan Hu would be useless. He rose from his seat.

"Are you leaving?"

"..."

Lee Sungmin replied by turning away.
