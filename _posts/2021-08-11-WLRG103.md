He took his position before the wall of flesh. Though he could not tell how thick it was, this was a far better option than blindly running to the end of a corridor. Lee Sungmin's gaze shifted to the minimap floating in the corner of his vision, which displayed four distinct paths. His own path was currently shared with Chwi Geol, Jang Deuksu, and the Blood Granny.

As he studied the map, he saw that two of the four paths showed a mix of yellow and red dots coexisting. Another path held two yellow dots, while the last one contained only a single cluster of red. Lee Sungmin's eyes narrowed, fixing on that final path.

*Who is it?*

Whose Doppelganger could that be? For only a Doppelganger to remain, it must have killed everyone else who shared its path.

*Is it Wiji Hoyeon?*

A sinister premonition washed over him, the chilling thought that perhaps Baek Sogo was already dead. He bit his lower lip, swallowing the ominous feeling as he forced his attention back to the other routes. One path held three people. Another held a person and a Doppelganger. The last had two Doppelgangers.

For now, he would have to exclude the path with only the red dots. That left him with a choice. From his current position, he could break through the wall to his right or his left. To the left lay a lone person and a Doppelganger. To the right, three people.

Lee Sungmin's eyebrows twitched as he watched the three yellow dots on the right path draw closer to one another.

What should he do?

The worst-case scenario was that the three dots were not Wiji Hoyeon but her fanatical followers. If it was Wiji Hoyeon herself, there would be no issue, for she would surely remember him, but her demonic practitioners? They would not leave him be.

*They probably wouldn't believe me even if I used Wiji Hoyeon's name.*

The distance between the yellow dots was shrinking rapidly. They would encounter each other any second now, if they had not already. He glanced at the path to his left again, noting the considerable distance between the person and the Doppelganger.

"Damn it."

He chose the right. Lee Sungmin aimed his spear, its length already sheathed in enhanced qi, at the wall beside him. In the absolute worst case, the two demonic heads, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist, awaited him on the other side. Or perhaps it was Wiji Hoyeon and Baek Sogo. It could even be Geuk Cheondo or the Peerless Eccentric.

Nothing was certain. He had to see for himself. The qi enveloping his spear began to flow, gathering at the very tip of the spearhead. The energy condensed upon the sharpened edge, shrinking smaller and smaller until it was a point of pure power.

"……"

The Void Monarch made a sound of surprise at the sight. He had known Lee Sungmin was a skilled master, but he had never realized his control over enhanced qi was so profound.

[That should be impossible with the martial realm you've reached. How can you do that?]

"I've done it before," Lee Sungmin replied, his eyes fixed on the spear's tip.

Judging solely by the martial realm displayed on his status window, such fine control should have been beyond him. In truth, his proficiency in the Violet Dawn Divine Art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, and the Dancing Shadow Soul Break were all at the eighth star, yet, separate from his current state, he held memories of reaching a much higher plane. It was a strange paradox. His mind remembered the techniques, but his body and the arts he had learned could not fully execute them.

Even now, the impossible feat demanded an immense toll on his inner arts. He felt a hollowing emptiness in his qi center, but he ignored it, continuing to pour his strength and inner arts into the hand gripping the spear. Rubia watched in astonishment while the Void Monarch fell silent. The enhanced qi condensed at the spear's tip now glowed a deep, menacing violet.

*It's certainly different from the Mental World.*

Back then, it had been faster, easier. A bitter smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips, but that was all. He thrust his spear forward into the wall of red flesh.

[An interesting fellow.]

The Void Monarch laughed heartily as the spear burst through the wall.

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath as he stepped through the newly made hole. The massive drain on his inner arts left him dizzy, his legs weak and trembling. He steadied himself and bit his lower lip, forcing his body to obey.

*I have no choice.*

He immediately retrieved a potion from his Subspace Pocket and drank it in one gulp. While it eased some of his body's fatigue, it did nothing to restore his depleted inner arts. Recovering those would require qi circulation, and he had no time for that.

Next, he took out the Great Cycle Pill. He broke the precious herb in half and placed one piece in his mouth, where it instantly dissolved and slid down his throat. Normally, consuming such a miracle herb would necessitate a lengthy session of qi circulation to safely absorb its power, but Lee Sungmin, with his Black Heart, needed no such process. The inner arts from the pill flowed directly into his qi center, accumulating immediately.

"……"

This was the very reason he had saved the Great Cycle Pill and the Magic Gemstone. While he had worried about the risk of qi deviation, their true value was in emergencies like this, allowing for a rapid restoration of his power. His empty qi center began to fill. As expected of Shaolin's greatest treasure, just half of the pill was enough to restore most of what he had consumed.

*No qi deviation. The Void Monarch might be right.*

He still did not trust him completely, though. Once he confirmed his inner arts were restored, Lee Sungmin broke into a run. The two yellow dots were now in the same location, not far from his position.

He ran for what felt like an eternity before he saw them. Two figures were locked in combat. The moment he saw their backs, his heart began to pound. One wore a pure white martial uniform, her hair a striking gray. Lee Sungmin knew her instantly.

It was Baek Sogo.

Her attacker was a giant of a man in a black uniform, his build even larger than Jang Deuksu's. He wielded no sword, relying only on his bare fists, yet his ferocious attacks were so menacing that simply watching them made one's heart race.

*Fists. Is that the Demon Wolf Iron Fist?*

Both Baek Sogo and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist fought unarmed, but their styles were polar opposites. Where Baek Sogo launched a relentless flurry of attacks built on the speed and illusions of her Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Demon Wolf Iron Fist was slow and heavy. It was too early to tell who had the upper hand, but from Lee Sungmin's perspective, Baek Sogo seemed to hold a slight advantage.

Her situation, however, was far from good. A short distance away, a scrawny man stood with his arms crossed, his eyes narrowing as he spotted Lee Sungmin approaching.

It was the Phantom Sword of Dokgo.

It could be no one else. He did not know what Geuk Cheondo or the Peerless Eccentric looked like, but they would have no reason to attack Baek Sogo, and indeed, a long sword hung from the man's waist.

Lee Sungmin pushed more strength into his legs, accelerating his movement arts. His luck had been good so far, but it had just run out. He had never expected to encounter both the Phantom Sword of Dokgo and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist here. The only small mercy was that they were not attacking together.

The Demon Wolf Iron Fist's pride was the only reason she was still alive. Seeing that she too was a master of unarmed combat, he had insisted on challenging her himself to test his skills.

*Even if I win here…*

Baek Sogo bit her lower lip. The Demon Wolf Iron Fist was a formidable master, but she was about half a step better. Defeating him would not be easy, but as long as no other variables came into play, she was confident she could claim victory.

The problem was the Phantom Sword of Dokgo. He was reputed to be the most skilled of all Wiji Hoyeon's followers. If she were in peak condition, the fight might be different, but facing him after a grueling battle with the Demon Wolf Iron Fist? Her chances of survival were infinitesimally small.

*If I subdue the Demon Wolf Iron Fist and threaten his life… no. That's not possible. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo wouldn't care about his life.*

Still, she could not give up. She could not die yet. Gritting her teeth, Baek Sogo pressed her assault, slowly pushing the Demon Wolf Iron Fist back. His face contorted with rage. He had stepped forward, leaving the Phantom Sword's subtle smile behind him, only to suffer this humiliation. He was boiling.

"You bitch…!" he spat, but he could do little more than receive her relentless attacks.

In the midst of their clash,

BOOOOM!

A loud explosion erupted nearby. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo reacted instantly, and Baek Sogo's shoulders flinched at the sound. It created a small opening, but the Demon Wolf Iron Fist was just as startled and failed to capitalize on it.

"Someone's coming," the Phantom Sword of Dokgo muttered.

The Demon Wolf Iron Fist and Baek Sogo felt it too, the presence of someone approaching at high speed. *Who is it?* Baek Sogo's pupils trembled. For her, the true worst-case scenario would be the arrival of the Blood Granny, or even Wiji Hoyeon herself. If that happened, nothing short of a miracle could save her.

"Who the hell is that?"

Baek Sogo could not afford to look back, but the other two could. The Demon Wolf Iron Fist, however, was too focused on her to see who was coming.

"Was there a spear user among the Murim Alliance guys?" the Phantom Sword of Dokgo mused as he drew his sword.

A spear. There wasn't one. Chwi Geol and the Peerless Eccentric were unarmed fighters, Jang Deuksu used an axe, and Geuk Cheondo a saber. A spear… a spear. A single person surfaced from the depths of Baek Sogo's memory. She knew one martial artist who used a spear.

"He looks young… I don't know who he is," the Phantom Sword of Dokgo muttered and moved forward, his interest piqued by the unknown arrival.

Baek Sogo felt an overwhelming urge to look back, but she could not.

"Should I kill him first?" the Phantom Sword of Dokgo asked the Demon Wolf Iron Fist.

"No need!" the man answered in a rough voice.

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo chuckled at his reply. Just then,

CLAAANG!

A spear thrust, fast as a flash of light, collided with the Phantom Sword's blade. The man's stance did not waver in the slightest. He stood firm, effortlessly receiving the speed-laden attack. The relaxed smile vanished from his face, his eyes narrowing. That single strike told him his opponent was no ordinary master.

"Who are you?" the Phantom Sword of Dokgo asked.

Baek Sogo retreated a few steps from the Demon Wolf Iron Fist's assault. With the sudden intrusion of a third party, he did not immediately press his attack, instead panting as he looked between her and the man who had just engaged his companion.

He looked at Lee Sungmin.

Baek Sogo's eyes widened as she stared at Lee Sungmin's back. It was the same back she had seen a year ago. His appearance had not changed much, but why? Why did that back, which had not grown much larger, now seem so immense?

Lee Sungmin did not look back, though he desperately wanted to. He wanted to turn and greet Baek Sogo, his senior sister. He wanted to say it had been a long time and ask if she had been well, but he could not. Before him stood the Phantom Sword of Dokgo and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist. If he showed even the slightest opening, the Phantom Sword would not hesitate to strike.

"Senior Sister."

He raised his spear, both of his hands tingling from the impact. He had crashed into his opponent with all his speed, yet the Phantom Sword of Dokgo had received the blow effortlessly. Even though Chwi Geol had defeated the man's Doppelganger, his skill was clearly no less than the old master's. The strength Lee Sungmin felt from him was the greatest he had ever encountered, making even the Sword Phantom under the full moon seem several steps below in comparison.

"It's been a long time."

He did not look at her face. His glare remained fixed on the Phantom Sword of Dokgo as he spoke. At his words, Baek Sogo's shoulders trembled slightly. It had only been a year. She had certainly grown stronger in that time, but having already reached a high realm, her skills had not improved so dramatically.

However, what about Lee Sungmin?

Baek Sogo could not believe it. The junior disciple she had known a year ago was nowhere near this level. Back then, he had been close to the Supreme Peak but had felt unstable. Now? The man before her had become so strong that even she was astonished.

"…Ju… Junior Disciple?"

*Why are you here?* she wanted to ask, but she knew this was not the time for such questions and remained silent.

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo chuckled as he looked between them. "Silent Flash had a Junior Disciple? This is the first I'm hearing of it."

A crimson sword qi surged from his blade. Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with both hands and controlled his breathing. They would never believe he was an acquaintance of Wiji Hoyeon. Should he try to escape with Baek Sogo? It did not seem like they would give him the chance.

*So, can I do it?*

His opponent was the Phantom Sword of Dokgo, a man he had never fought before. He had learned firsthand how difficult it was to face a skilled swordsman. Lee Sungmin knew instinctively that the man before him was stronger than his current self.

*Can I do it?*

In response to the resurfacing question, Lee Sungmin answered by taking a step forward.

"I have to."
