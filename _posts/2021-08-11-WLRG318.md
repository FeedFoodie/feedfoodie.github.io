The question hung in Abel's mind. Could Lee Sungmin truly kill Kim Jonghyun?

He considered it a near impossibility. Even if Kim Jonghyun's immortality was not perfect, the task of completely killing someone who could not die was a monumental one. The only exception would be if Lee Sungmin were overwhelmingly stronger than Kim Jonghyun, a possibility that led to a chilling thought.

*He's dangerous.*

Abel did not take the fact lightly, for he knew Lee Sungmin's circumstances all too well. The young man was aware of his own instability and had even consulted Abel about it, haunted by the possibility that he might, just maybe, be The End.

There was no telling when Lee Sungmin's incomplete Sealing might break. Abel could not let him go on a rampage against the undying Kim Jonghyun.

While Cain had never trusted Lee Sungmin, Abel did. He was convinced the kid was not The End. As long as Lee Sungmin himself believed that, he would never become that monstrous fate.

*I can't place that burden on him.*

The current situation only reinforced his conviction. Kim Jonghyun was not a desperate man. Though he had lost the lord of the Death Knights and his legion was dismantled, it was not a loss that would prevent him from dreaming of a comeback. Kim Jonghyun had the luxury of a 'next time'. If the battle turned unfavorable, he could leave at any moment, and a mage of his caliber would be impossible for Lee Sungmin to catch. That could not be allowed to happen. Kim Jonghyun had to be eliminated today, by any means necessary.

Behind him, Cain lay dead. Ever since they were boys, his older brother had been the one Abel felt he must surpass. Cain's talent for magic, his very instincts for it, had been superior from childhood. Even before he was old enough to truly understand, Abel had harbored the hope of one day surpassing him. After coming of age, he had seriously contemplated and trained for the day he would defeat his brother in a battle of magic.

He had never imagined it would end like this, that he would be the one to kill him. More than the act itself, it was the *way* he had killed him that filled Abel with disgust. They had not competed with magic. His brother had not remained the great mage Abel had once wanted to overcome. He had learned too much, and in the end, had wished only to preserve his own life, leaving no trace of the figure Abel had admired in his youth.

Abel swallowed his bitter feelings. The discomfort of killing his brother in such a manner was compounded by the fact that he had broken a taboo he had never once broken in his entire life. It could not be helped, though he offered himself no such consolation. In any case, he would soon pay the price for breaking the taboo and killing his own brother with his own hands.

"I'm counting on you."

His body was filled with endless magic power, a gift from the Demon God's Dragon serum. His dwindling lifespan had also been replenished to some extent, a grim reward for killing his own brother and breaking the taboo.

Abel looked at Frau one last time. Frau met his gaze with bitter eyes and shook his head.

"Should I say... farewell?"

A small laugh escaped Abel at those words. Frau sighed deeply and shot Aladur a look. Aladur moved quickly, supporting the fallen Rubia. Abel shed his cumbersome robe and turned his head toward the place where explosions roared without rest. Fortunately, it seemed Kim Jonghyun was still there.

*It's a good thing the Fairy Queen is asleep.* At least she would not go on a rampage about him killing Cain.

{sep}

This is...

The thought was instantly obliterated. His opponent allowed no such idleness. Layered defensive barriers shattered like glass. Kim Jonghyun cast Blink an instant before impact, only to find the Noble Clan of the spear descending upon the very space he had jumped to, as if waiting for him. It could no longer be called spearmanship. It was merciless, widespread violence that swept through the air itself, forcing him to do everything he could to avoid a fatal wound.

Once he started getting pushed back, there was no way out. The immortality Kim Jonghyun had obtained was not perfect. Even with his instantaneous regeneration, a fatal wound severe enough to cause unconsciousness would mean facing death dozens of times without a chance to escape. The only reason Kim Jonghyun, a mage, had been able to overwhelm Lee Sungmin in close combat before was because he possessed the body of a Demon Lord that continued to grow even during battle.

That was not the case now. He might have gained the upper hand by growing if their levels were similar or if he was only slightly weaker, but the gap between the current Lee Sungmin and him was far greater than that. Even now, as Kim Jonghyun continued to grow, he was still being pushed back. He moved constantly, using his combat instincts as a Demon Lord to find an opening and gain an advantage, but Lee Sungmin's speed, power, and range completely devoured his every attempt.

[Are you okay?]

*For now,* he replied quickly to the worried Void Monarch. It was a strange feeling. His head felt as if it were about to split open, and his heart hammered against his ribs, beating too fast and too hard.

Every time he moved his body, the headache would vanish for a moment. Every time he swung his spear, his body could not withstand the force and his muscles would tear, only to instantly recover as he pushed Kim Jonghyun back again. No matter how much power he poured out, his Demonic Energy did not run dry.

He was still far from his ideal. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was faster, sharper, and stronger than he was now. Lee Sungmin was not the Chairman, but he wanted to emulate the perfect martial arts he had witnessed.

*In my own way. Yes, in my own way.* Was that voice he heard at the end of the fight with Vollander just an auditory hallucination? Or was it the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, now in another world, showing him the path forward?

"Keuheuk!"

He had lost count of how many times it had happened. Lee Sungmin's spear pierced through Kim Jonghyun's stomach, completely tearing apart his intestines and bursting out his back. Kim Jonghyun forcibly clung to his fading consciousness as both of his hands turned a grayish-white.

KWARURURURU!

He had not dodged, even knowing the spear would pierce him, because he needed that sliver of time. He also needed to slow Lee Sungmin's movements as much as possible. Command spells to restrain him did not work, a fact he had already confirmed several times. Defensive command spells were just as useless. Both command spells and barriers were too weak against the current Lee Sungmin. Only offense remained.

"Explode!" Kim Jonghyun activated the command spell with a shout.

KWAAAAANG!

A massive explosion engulfed Lee Sungmin, but it was nothing more than a trick of the eye. It was lamentable that an explosion activated by a command spell could only achieve such a meager effect. This, however, was different. Kim Jonghyun unleashed a wide-area soul-destruction magic, a unique spell that Arbes had spent his entire life creating, which annihilated the soul itself. Against such magic, a Protective Qi Aura could not function as a proper defense.

Bigger, stronger. He had to shatter the very space where the soul-destruction magic was cast.

PAJIJIJIK!

Lee Sungmin's body became a streak of lightning, shooting backward. As he created distance, the spear in his hands began to spin in a fierce rotation.

Sky Piercing Lightning Strike.

The sky turned purple. Below, the surviving Holy Knights and Priests who were watching covered their eyes against the brilliant flash of light. When the light faded, there was nothing left in the sky.

*Damn it.* Kim Jonghyun swore, which was unlike him. The very space where the Soul Destruction Magic had been cast was annihilated. How could this possibly be considered a Spear Technique or martial arts?

*How much time is left?*

According to his calculations, he should have subdued Lee Sungmin by now, but what was this? Far from subduing him, the reality was that he was being relentlessly pushed back.

*This is really dangerous.*

Soon, the magic would end. If that happened, even escaping would become difficult, and that would be troublesome. Dying unnecessarily when he did not have to was something Kim Jonghyun had no desire for. With his mind made up, he committed to his decision. There would be a next time. There were still more things he could do. To die here, despite that, was absurd. Having no attachment to life was entirely different from dying foolishly.

KURURURUNG!

A pitch-black darkness surged around Kim Jonghyun as he drew out all the magic power he possessed. The Grimoire floating around him received his power and emitted a black light. He knew that escaping would not be easy. In that case, he had no choice but to act with all his might to flee.

Lee Sungmin noticed that Kim Jonghyun's actions were unusual. *No way, is he trying to run? Now?* Of course, he had no intention of letting him go. Lee Sungmin kicked off the air and leaped, jumping through space using Gale Thunder.

He knew. Lee Sungmin's movement was more like magic than martial arts, resembling the Space Leap magic, Blink. He did not know the detailed principles, but leaping over an existing space to appear in a completely different one was identical to Blink. It was just superior to the magic in many ways, from its instantaneous speed to its leaping distance. For high-level mages, Blink was not omnipotent, because the moment one leaped and appeared in a new space, a fluctuation of Mana in the atmosphere was bound to occur.

Lee Sungmin's Gale Thunder, however, caused no such phenomenon, making it impossible to predict his point of appearance, but... even if the principles were different, if that unknown martial art was a Space Leap...

Kim Jonghyun's eyes widened. The strain of concentration caused blood to trickle from his nose. He was a cautious person, and even if he had a technique he could use, he would save it for the most appropriate moment to gain the greatest advantage. Besides, it was not something he could use easily even if it was available.

Amidst his fading concentration, Kim Jonghyun caught sight of a streak of lightning crossing space. His senses as a Demon Lord and his combat-specialized instincts allowed him to glimpse it for a moment. *Now.* Kim Jonghyun's hand twisted.

While leaping through space consecutively with Gale Thunder, Lee Sungmin felt a strong sense of unease. Danger. A premonition of fatal death, something he had never felt even in his fight with Vollander, washed over him. He hurriedly stopped his Gale Thunder, failing to reach his target space. For Lee Sungmin, that was a relief.

*He dodged this?* Kim Jonghyun could not help but be greatly flustered. *No, that's fine.* He should not be too greedy. The move he had judged to be his most powerful had gone to waste, but it was enough to instill sufficient caution in Lee Sungmin. That would slow the speed at which he closed the growing distance. With that much time, he could continue to cast his magic.

Kim Jonghyun's hands pointed downward. The direction of gravity changed, and Lee Sungmin, who had been soaring upward, slowed noticeably. He layered command spells on top of that and even added debuff magic.

*It's done.*

Just in case, he also wrapped his entire body in defensive barriers. Only after putting enough distance between himself and Lee Sungmin did Kim Jonghyun feel relieved. At this range, he could get away before Lee Sungmin could reach him.

*He's not The End yet.* Kim Jonghyun was certain of it. If so, that meant there was plenty of time to try 'again'. He might end up starting something else. Yes, he just had to learn from this failure. He was still alive. As long as he was alive, he could try something else again.

*Still, it's a shame. I was so close to succeeding...*

Failing to anticipate Abel and Gries was a mistake. *Yes, it's fine.* Even if Abel interfered next time, he would have prepared an adequate response by then. Kim Jonghyun glanced at Lee Sungmin, who was rushing at him with a scream. *You'll probably interfere next time, too. The fact that you were this strong was also unexpected. I'll have to be a little more careful next time.*

Yes, next time.

*It's a shame I can't go without saying goodbye.*

Abel watched Kim Jonghyun soar high into the sky. He probably would not think him heartless, he mused. It was simply that he had no luxury for such sentiment. With a bitter smile, Abel opened Gries. His magic power and his lifespan were just barely enough. He slowly extended a hand, pointing it toward Kim Jonghyun's distant figure.

There was no way he would miss.

*If I knew it would come to this, should I have used this magic from the start?* Back then, he never thought it would turn out like this. He had left it as a last resort, but he thought he would never have to use it. He did not think Kim Jonghyun would have obtained incomplete immortality. Nor did he think the connection with the Elemental Realm would lead to this result.

Well, it was done. This was as far as he could go. He had done everything he could to free this world from the fate of The End. He had generously offered his lifespan. It was ugly, but he had also fulfilled his childhood wish of surpassing his brother.

Still, he was not without regrets. He had wanted to save the world. Abel smiled bitterly. When he learned that this world would one day be destroyed, when he lacked nothing in his own talent and even had the magic tome called Gries in his hands, Abel had believed without a doubt that he was the one who could save this world from ruin.

He was not. In the end, Abel could not change the fate of The End. All he could do was erase a part of The End from this world.

"Still, I'm glad I can at least do that."

It was better than his brother, who could not do anything.

Abel's lifespan was sucked into Gries.
