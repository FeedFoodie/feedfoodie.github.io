Sarahenne, the Spirit Queen, did not understand. She narrowed her eyes, her gaze fixed on Wiji Hoyeon, who stared back from a distance. This was the Elemental Realm, a place ruled by Sarahenne herself, where no being other than an Elemental could possibly exist. It was the most fundamental law of this place, one that even she, the queen, could not defy. That very law was why she had been unable to bring Cain into the Elemental Realm itself, instead creating a small, overlapping space for him to stay.

Still, here stood Wiji Hoyeon, who was clearly not an Elemental. Her very presence negated the most fundamental law that constituted this realm.

"…Who… are you?" Sarahenne questioned, glaring at the intruder. *Why is a human here?* No, more importantly… *Is that truly a human?*

Wiji Hoyeon did not answer. With an expression of utter boredom, she extended a foot forward.

The moment her foot touched the ground…

KWADEDEUK!

The entire earth erupted into a forest of awls and blades that surged to tear her body apart. Though some of the Elementals filling this world had been annihilated, far, far too many still remained. At Sarahenne's will, all of them were trying to kill Wiji Hoyeon. The Earth Elementals had become the land itself, but their attacks failed to threaten her.

The moment the earthen weapons reached her, their trajectories were forcibly twisted, shooting off in every direction. The very space around Wiji Hoyeon distorted to protect her, forming a defensive barrier of the highest level, one that made a simple Protective Qi Aura seem like a child's toy.

"There are so many," Wiji Hoyeon muttered, slowly turning her head. Her eyes saw them all, the countless Elementals hidden within the world around her.

She intended to kill every last one of them, and the Spirit Queen along with them. That purpose alone had brought her all the way to the Elemental Realm.

Of course, she knew it would not be an easy task. Her opponent this time was the Spirit Queen, Sarahenne, a powerful being incomparable to the Dungeons and boss monsters she had faced for the past ten years.

*Still, the last five Dungeons were a bit tough.*

She had almost died in those final five Dungeons, where the monsters of The End, meant to truly bring about the world's destruction, had slumbered, but she had not died. In the end, Wiji Hoyeon had survived and erased every Dungeon from the world, ensuring they would never appear in Eria again. Those ten years had been the most intense period of her entire life, a relentless cycle of battle after battle that forged her into a monster among monsters. Innate talent, given talent, and raw experience had all combined.

*This time will be quite difficult, too.*

Wiji Hoyeon sensed it. The endless number of Elementals, and standing behind them, the Spirit Queen. She had to be especially careful of the queen, a transcendent being in the truest sense, and one who was also Immortal. Moreover, this was the Elemental Realm, a space where Elementals and their queen could exert their power without any penalty.

Still. She could do it. She had to.

Wiji Hoyeon advanced, absorbing the hostility of all the Elementals with her body. When the Fire Elementals unleashed their power, she found herself walking through the middle of hellfire as surging heat and flames assaulted her from all directions. Beyond the inferno, the Wind Elementals whipped up a furious storm. Through it all, Sarahenne remained still, simply watching her.

Wiji Hoyeon gave a slight nod.

"Mm."

Teresa began to speak. "I have something I want to ask."

Lee Sungmin, who had been checking on the sleeping Baek Sogo, turned his head to look at her. He felt a deep sense of gratitude toward the woman. If even she had been unable to do anything about Baek Sogo's poison, he would have been truly at a loss. Because of this, Lee Sungmin had developed a profound goodwill toward Teresa. He intended to grant any request she made, as long as it was within his power.

"What is it?"

"What is it you're trying to do?" Teresa asked, staring at him intently. It was a completely unexpected question. When Lee Sungmin just watched her without answering, she took a light breath and continued.

"It's just… strange, isn't it?"

"What is?"

"You, Lee Sungmin," she began. "You defeated the Death Lord and the Demon Lord ten years ago. Then you went mad from the Demon Lord's curse, and now, after ten long years, you've finally regained your sanity."

"That's right."

"So what do you plan to do from now on?" Teresa asked, then glanced down at Baek Sogo. "Silent Flash Baek Sogo. She's famous as a wandering warrior, and her martial prowess is considered to be among the top five in the entire murim."

"You know quite a lot."

"When you're cooped up in the Holy Palace, the rumors from the outside world are quite entertaining," Teresa said with a bashful smile. "I know a lot of things. I've heard many stories, and the Crimson Sage," she went on, "she left the Mages Guild to establish her own Magic Tower, teaching magic to students without discrimination. It seems the Mages Guild isn't too fond of her, but I hear her skills have already surpassed most of the Magic Tower Masters."

Lee Sungmin glanced at Scarlet, who puffed out her chest proudly when their eyes met.

"And the Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri." Yana was not nearby, seemingly uncomfortable with Teresa's powerful holy energy, but she was sitting close enough that every word would still reach her ears. "More than ten years ago, the master of the Yokai city, Urmuri, changed. It was Yana who defeated the previous master and seized the position, and… even the Fairy Realms."

"It's a complete mishmash, isn't it?"

To someone like Teresa, who didn't know the circumstances, the combination must have seemed bizarre. A top-tier mage of the era, a wandering warrior, a Yokai, and a Fairy Queen, all individuals with nothing in common, gathered in one place.

"It's because of you, isn't it?" Teresa asked with a smile.

"……"

Lee Sungmin answered without hiding anything. She had saved Baek Sogo's life, and he intended to treat her with that much respect. Besides, this wasn't a conversation that needed to be kept secret. As he answered honestly, Teresa's eyes sparkled with curiosity. She tilted her head toward him and continued her questions.

"What in the world do you want to do? No, what are you trying to do, gathering this much power? Even if the numbers are small, the people gathered here, including you, are powerful enough to easily overturn a city. Surely, you're not planning on world domination, are you?"

"Haven't you been reading too many novels?"

"I thought it sounded quite plausible…"

"I have no such intention. I'm just…"

Teresa did not know about The End. The same was true for Yana and Scarlet. Yana was helping him not to stop The End, but because the Void Monarch was inside his head. Scarlet had lent her strength out of goodwill for him and concern for Baek Sogo. Considering what lay ahead, perhaps it was better to reveal everything now and ask for their cooperation.

There was no telling when the Spirit Queen would awaken, but even if she had not awakened yet, there was still the Vampire Queen. There was the Predator. Unlike the Spirit Queen, whose Advent was uncertain, they were threats that existed in this world right now.

"…Yana," Lee Sungmin called out quietly.

How should he explain this? He was lost in thought for a moment. *That the world will end someday.* Should he just say it bluntly, without any context?

As he was organizing his thoughts, Osla flew over. "It should be fine to tell them," she said. "The Vampire Queen can't see the future anymore, right? That means this world's fate is completely locked into The End. At this point, talking about it and the reasons for it won't change a thing."

It was not a hopeful statement. If what Osla said was true, it meant that no matter what desperate struggles they made now, The End was unavoidable.

As a test, Lee Sungmin tried speaking the words aloud. He spoke of The End, specific words and topics that should have triggered the threat of an Apostle, a system designed to prohibit the disclosure of the world's truth.

However, there was no chill on the back of his neck. It was just as Osla had said. This world was already locked into its fate.

When the whole story was finished, Yana said nothing, merely watching him with her deep eyes. Unlike her, Scarlet and Teresa showed more overt reactions. Teresa, in particular, closed her half-open mouth and swallowed hard.

"What does the Void Monarch desire?" Yana asked, once again focused on his intentions.

"He desires what I desire."

"Then I, too, desire what you desire," Yana said, bowing her head slightly. For her, nothing had changed, though the fact that she received power from a Demonic Spirit, and Laplace's warning about that spirit, continued to weigh on his mind.

"So…" Teresa's voice trembled. "You're… trying to stop… that, The End…"

"……" Lee Sungmin replied with a silent nod.

Teresa covered her face with both hands. She had no idea what she was supposed to say. The purpose of this world's existence and its predetermined destruction were topics she had never once considered, truths she had never imagined could be real. The bewildered Teresa was only in her mid-twenties, with far more time left to live than she had already lived.

*I don't want to die.*

Most people would probably think that, which made it even harder for Teresa to understand.

"Why does the Vampire Queen desire The End?"

"How can a human understand a being that is not human?" Lee Sungmin had no choice but to say that. For hundreds of years, the Vampire Queen, Jeniela, had been preparing for the appearance of the Slaughter Predator. She had wished for its arrival even though she knew its existence would end this world, and now, she was trying to create a new one with her own hands.

Teresa covered her face again and bowed her head deeply. It was something that required no deliberation. If the fate of The End could be changed, then changing it was unconditionally the right thing to do, wasn't it?

"…I don't think you'll need to take me back," Teresa sighed, lifting her head. "If there's anything I can do to help, I will. Although, it would only be things like healing injuries like this… no, that probably won't be much help to you either." She looked at Lee Sungmin with a dejected expression. For him, who had the body of a Yokai, or for Yana, a complete Yokai, Teresa's holy power was more of a poison than a help.

"Thank you," Lee Sungmin said with a warm smile. He and Yana might not benefit from her help, but Baek Sogo and Scarlet could, and it would be useful against the monsters of the Predator they would have to fight.

"What a fucked up world," Scarlet spat. "I haven't even done everything I want to do yet! I dedicated my beautiful youth to walking the single path of magic. I spent my most beautiful and vibrant younger years on Mount Mushii, unable to wash or change clothes, reeking of shit."

He recalled the Scarlet of those days. With her grimy appearance, she had looked dirtier than a beggar.

"This damn mage life, I haven't even dated or gotten married yet, and now The End? The world is going to end?" KADEUK. She ground her teeth. "That can't happen. I'm going to live a long life. A damn long one. As much as I've suffered and missed out on fun until now, I'm going to live it up and enjoy myself from now on."

"Couldn't you just start enjoying yourself now?"

"Are you serious? My research isn't even finished yet, what is there to enjoy!"

Without any thought for the big picture, Scarlet was exploding with rage about The End for purely personal reasons. In fact, that was probably the normal reaction. When it came down to it, Lee Sungmin's own reasons for wanting to stop The End were also extremely personal, just with a few more things added on. The death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the death of Abel. For Baek Sogo's sake.

*And you.*

Lee Sungmin thought of Wiji Hoyeon.

*For what reason did she want to stop The End?*
