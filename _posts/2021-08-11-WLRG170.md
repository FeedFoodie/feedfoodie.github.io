Lee Sungmin fell silent, his glare fixed on Frau.

The term Destiny Force was new to him, yet as Frau spoke, something clicked into place. A cascade of memories flooded his mind. The Spirit speaking through Grandmaster Bulyeong's body in Shaolin. The turmoil in the North, Ruvus, and every step that had led him to the South.

However, the chain of events stretched back even further.

It began the moment he grasped the Stone of Reincarnation and returned to the past, the moment he met Wiji Hoyeon in Zenavis. From that point on, there had always been something he *had* to do.

He recalled the aimless days when he had wandered, unsure how to live this second chance at life. In those times, he had simply trained, and that training had always led him to a new purpose, a new task waiting just over the horizon.

On Mount Mushii, he found his objective to meet Denir, the God of Time. After descending, his work as a mercenary in Behenger led him to Zeon's death, which in turn gave him a Shaolin martial arts manual and a reason to go there. Through that manual, a connection formed with Grandmaster Bulyeong, who foretold his journey to the North. His training in Shaolin sent him on an errand to Dreamoor, the home of Mount Hua and Denir himself. There, an encounter with the Sword Phantom solidified his resolve to undergo Denir's training.

And after that? Denir had told him of Envirus, and his search had led him to the Sleeping Forest, where he met the Void Monarch and Rubia. Then there was the Dungeon where Baek Sogo had died. After saving her, he had followed the Spirit's words and gone to the North.

He had experienced so much there, learning of the Predator's existence and becoming entangled with it. He formed a bond with the Crazy Heavenly Demon and gained a new purpose, to travel south and learn to handle Demonic Energy. His fight with the Blood Heavenly Demon had corrected the distortions in his mind, ki, and body.

"......"

Wiji Hoyeon had been cursed and weakened. By killing the Iron Armored Divine Spear, he became a demonic practitioner and drew the attention of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens, the very ones who had cursed her. He met the Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance, and now, finally, he had come to the South. Here he met Aladur, who told him of Frau, and he came to Urmuri because there was something he had to do.

All of it.

A throb of pain pulsed in his temples, and Lee Sungmin pressed his fingers against them.

*Is all of this the Destiny Force? There are no coincidences in the world.*

The words Mushii had once muttered to himself echoed in his mind. If Frau was right, if this Destiny Force unconsciously compelled action, then his entire life was not a series of coincidences but a single, unbroken thread of fate.

What kind of fate?

"You look confused," Frau giggled. The great shamaness could perceive him in ways others could not. She couldn't see specifics like the Apostle of the End, but as a shaman, a sage, and a witch, her perspective was different from that of a god or a mage.

"The Destiny Force is what compels coincidence," she explained. "It's something you can't fight even if you know it's there, but you see, fate... it's just as I said. An incredibly ambiguous thing. Is you and I talking right now fate or coincidence? Nobody knows. Not even the gods, I'd bet."

"...Why in the world is something like this tied to me?"

"How would I know? Every being is bound by fate. The gods in this world are no different. The being that bestows the Destiny Force governs fate itself, transcending even the gods. Humans can never escape it. Not even if you become a transcendent being. In the end, you're just dancing in the palm of someone's hand."

His headache grew worse. Lee Sungmin pressed harder on his temples as Frau continued.

"You're a strange one. So stained with yokai nature, yet you cling to your humanity. Normally, someone like you... hahaha! They would've thrown that away long ago. You should be a monster by now, driven by inhuman instincts, running rampant until nothing human is left."

His left eye felt as if it were twitching.

"If it were some trivial evil spirit, I could handle it, but the yokai clinging to you is too strong. So strong that if I tried to exorcise it, the backlash would kill me. I don't know what it is, but you'd better be careful. A human is only human as long as they act like one. The human vessel has its limits, and there are things it was never meant to contain. Demonic Energy corrodes the humanity within that vessel, creating cracks. Once it shatters completely, a person is no longer human."

"...That."

Lee Sungmin barely managed to force the word out. A horrible feeling washed over him. The life he had lived, the death he had suffered, the return to the past... in all that time, he had never once considered that his choices were not his own, that he was merely being swayed by the currents of fate.

Frau's words were driving him into a terrible state of mind.

"I came here to find a way to handle Demonic Energy."

"There's no such method," Frau stated flatly. "As long as you're human, no such method exists. The moment you use Demonic Energy, you stop being human. Slowly but surely. You might find some trick to control it, but that doesn't mean you can live as a human and handle it without consequence."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon was a perfect example. He had learned to handle Demonic Energy, but he had paid for it with ten years of lunacy. If he hadn't met Lee Sungmin, he would still be suffering, running wild even now.

"I know this might sound strange," he began, "but I think I'm a special case."

"You certainly seem to be. Anyone else would've gone mad by now, yet here you are, still human. So, is that what you want from me?" Frau's lips curled into a smile.

Lee Sungmin was silent for a moment before he spoke.

"I want to meet Yana."

"Yana? The only Yana I know is the Nine-Tailed Fox."

"That's the one."

"You don't plan on fighting her, do you? No, that can't be right. The story wouldn't just jump to that, would it?"

"I don't know about that, but I have my reasons for needing to meet her."

"......"

Frau stroked her chin, her eyes twinkling as she studied him. Then she let out a soft laugh.

"Interesting. The request itself is dull, but your existence is quite amusing. Perhaps this is fate, too." She nodded. "I'll let you meet Yana."

"...Do you know Yana?"

"Didn't you ask because you already knew?" Frau gave him a look as if he were the strange one.

He hadn't known at all. He had only asked because he figured that as a resident of Urmuri, she might know Yana's whereabouts. Even Erebrisa had been unable to locate the Nine-Tailed Fox who ruled the city.

"More importantly, how did you know I was here? That damn Aladur shouldn't have known."

"...I got the information from another source."

"Was it Erebrisa?" Frau's brow furrowed.

Lee Sungmin gave a silent nod.

"I can't stand those people," Frau grumbled in an annoyed voice. He remembered Nebul telling him that Frau had refused Erebrisa's invitation to join. "I don't know what they are, but they run around treating people like 'variables' and trying to recruit them."

"For now, stay here. I'll ask Yana."

"What's your relationship with her?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Frau shrugged. "We're old friends. We knew each other even before she came to rule Urmuri."

It seemed, however, that even Frau could not contact Yana immediately. In the end, Lee Sungmin, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, and Rubia were shown to an empty room in the brothel to wait.

As soon as they were in the room, Lee Sungmin turned to the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had already settled into a cross-legged position.

"If we meet Yana, let's ask about Chuseong."

"Mm." The Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had been about to close his eyes and meditate, gave a slight nod.

They still didn't know if Chuseong, the Gangcheori, was the one who had massacred the villagers. To learn the truth of what happened, they had to meet Yana, the one who was said to have let the Yokai escape.

"I'm saying this just in case," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, his eyes still closed. "If we meet Yana and find out which Yokai killed the villagers, and if that Yokai is in this village, this one will naturally go to avenge their deaths."

"......"

"I want you to stay out of it."

Lee Sungmin fell silent at his words.

"This is my karma. It's got nothing to do with you. That's why I'm telling you to stay out of it."

"...It might be dangerous, though."

"Haha! It might be, but for such personal karma... I don't want your help."

"But you helped me with my personal matters, Elder."

"If you'd asked me to stay out, I would have. So, you should respect my words as well. Even if I fight that Yokai, find myself outmatched, and die, you are not to interfere."

"If you die, Elder, I'll have to get revenge."

"You'll avenge me?"

"Of course."

At Lee Sungmin's answer, the Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a hearty laugh. He opened his eyes slightly and looked up at Lee Sungmin.

"You know," he began, "you've changed a lot since we first met. Back then, you seemed strangely emotionless... but not anymore. It feels like you've come back from somewhere abnormal to being at least somewhat normal."

At those words, Lee Sungmin unconsciously touched his own cheek. After 2100 years trapped in the Mental World, his mind had been worn down by dozens of cycles of collapse and regeneration. Though it hadn't broken, his emotions had paid the price. Spending so long in isolation had left them frayed and numb. That had slowly improved as he traveled, as he met, talked, and spent time with others. The unnatural had begun to feel natural again.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon chuckled as he watched Lee Sungmin.

"People are bound to change. This one prefers the you of now over the you of back then. It seems you have a bit of humanity to you now."

"...Is that so?"

"Yes. The you from before... if I had died, you wouldn't have tried to get revenge, would you?"

"That's... because I didn't have a reason back then."

"That's right." The Crazy Heavenly Demon nodded. "But now, a reason exists. It's the same reason I seek my own revenge, and it's why I'm telling you not to get involved in my affairs. You have no reason to be part of this."

After saying that, he closed his eyes again.

"So please, stay out of it."

Lee Sungmin closed his mouth and watched the Crazy Heavenly Demon begin to circulate his qi. Soon, he let out a sigh and sat down nearby.

He had grown attached. After traveling together for over a year, it was only natural. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Rubia, the Void Monarch... he had grown deeply attached to them all.

[You bastard, you're having embarrassing thoughts.]

The Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin shot to his feet and began to unstrap the Magic Armor. He decided the damn thing needed to be shoved into a cesspool for three or four days, just to shut it up.

Sensing his intentions, the Void Monarch threw a fit.

[Hey, hey! You said you were attached! Hey!]

Lee Sungmin strode toward the latrine, the armor begging and pleading the entire way. It was only when he dangled it over the foul-smelling pit that the Void Monarch pathetically promised it would never happen again.
