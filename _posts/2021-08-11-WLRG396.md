Lee Sungmin stared into the world of his own consciousness, a place he had once perceived as a jumbled mess of chaos. It was a maelstrom of tangled memories, those of Yang Ilcheon, the Demon Emperor and Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance, those of the Dragon slain by the Void Monarch, and those of Ju Heebaek, the Ghost Blood Asura who was called the Blood Demon. His own past life and his present one were woven into the tapestry as well, a nonsensical tangle that had been chaos itself.

However, that was no longer the case, for with the Void Monarch's departure, the vast world of his consciousness had become entirely his own. The world was no longer hazy, the fog had lifted, and he was its sole inhabitant. He began to walk slowly through the landscape of his mind.

The Void Monarch was gone, but for a fleeting moment, Lee Sungmin had been completely connected to him. He knew what his consciousness had witnessed and what emotions the Monarch had felt. He understood the feelings with which the Void Monarch had smiled after finally shaking off the regret and lingering attachment of his departure.

*Don't cry, smile.*

That was what the Void Monarch had said. Still, a smile did not come easily. He could not bring himself to form one whenever he recalled the memories tied to that boisterous spirit. The Void Monarch had become that important to him. The simple, crushing fact that he could no longer hear that laughing, chattering voice or meet him in this world of consciousness shook Lee Sungmin's very spirit.

And yet, he did not stop walking. He had a promise to keep, not just the one about soaking himself in a cesspool after killing Jeniela. He had promised that someday, when everything was over and no regrets remained, he would go to the world where the Void Monarch, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the Spear King had gone. For that reason alone, he could not allow himself to die. He had to stop The End.

Lee Sungmin stopped walking, realizing he now stood at the very center of his consciousness. The memories he was now fully aware of began to surface, one by one.

He saw a clan from long ago, the Black Lotus Sect, and a man named Yang Mugi who was its master. He saw the birth of a son, Yang Ilcheon, a boy born with innate genius who surpassed his father's achievements at a young age. He watched him reach the Transcendent Realm and establish the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance with his martial art, Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, earning the title of Demon Emperor for his phenomenal power.

He felt the life of an arrogant Dragon from a time long past. Like all of his kind, he was superior to other races from the moment of his birth, and his pride was as vast as his mastery over magic. He remembered polymorphing to wander the human world, enjoying all that it had to offer, just as most Dragons did.

He lived the journey of a figure named Ju Heebaek, the Ghost Blood Asura. Suddenly summoned to this unknown world, he did not establish a new martial theory as he had in his home world of fighting spirit. Instead, he wandered as a vagrant, finding pleasure only in training his martial arts and fighting strong opponents, and in the midst of it all, through a coincidental, no, an orchestrated encounter, he met two Vampire sisters.

And not so long ago, there was a fourteen-year-old boy, the kind you could find anywhere, who hated going to the academy.

Lee Sungmin's eyes opened. It felt as though it had taken a long time to perceive all those memories, but in reality, not much time had likely passed. He knew well that time in the Mental World flowed differently from the outside. He slowly lowered his gaze, looking down at his own body as the memories that had filled his world scattered.

*Who am I? Am I the Demon Emperor, possessing his memories? Am I a Dragon, possessing its memories? Am I the Ghost Blood Asura, possessing his memories?*

It was a meaningless question, for he already knew the answer. 'I' had to be me. Not the Demon Emperor, not the Dragon, and not the Ghost Blood Asura.

*Then am I the Phantom Spear?*

"......"

Lee Sungmin slowly closed his eyes again, raising both hands to cover his face. His tear-stained skin felt sticky. Being the Phantom Spear was not enough. The Lee Sungmin he had been until now, from the moment he killed the Sword Phantom to the moment Jeniela had blown his head off, had always been the Phantom Spear.

"It's not enough."

*The Phantom Spear is weak. The Phantom Spear was not enough.*

The sound was a beat so loud that everyone present could hear it. Jeniela's giggling laughter abruptly stopped. She pulled her hand from the Black Dragon Knight's stomach. Nearby, Scarlet lay collapsed and covered in blood, and not far from her, Lloyd was slumped over with his head bowed.

"Ah, ahh..."

Osla lay fallen, his wings completely torn. Baek Sogo lifted her pale face, her eyes blurred with blood as she looked toward the sound. Rubia, utterly exhausted from using her blessing, also lifted her head weakly.

The sound came again, and Jeniela's gaze snapped away from the carnage she had wrought. Shaking the blood from her hands, she looked toward the source of that thumping heart. It was Lee Sungmin's. The heart in his chest, in the body whose head had been blown off, was beating with thunderous force. The heartbeat was so strong that his entire body jolted with each pulse. His missing head had already regenerated completely.

The corners of Jeniela's mouth twitched upward. *Yes, finally.*

"I was worried you were really dead."

The regeneration had been too slow. It had taken so long that she had begun to wonder if his spirit had died completely, leaving only an empty shell behind, but that was not the case, and this excited her. What Jeniela wanted was not his annihilation, but his complete Despair.

She had prepared so many things to surprise him when he finally opened his eyes. She had severed Baek Sogo's right arm and plucked out Scarlet's left eye. She had taken the Black Dragon Knight's left arm and Lloyd's right leg. She had deliberately cut or plucked only one from each, planning to do the rest right in front of him after he woke up, pinning him down as he struggled and screamed. She wanted to watch him cry and despair, to make him beg and plead for her to stop. To ask for forgiveness, to beg for his life, to tell her not to do it.

Jeniela slowly approached him. She had asked him several times, 'Have you Despaired?' Each time, he had failed to give her the answer she truly wanted, but now, she would finally hear it. Jeniela stopped walking. The heartbeat, once strong enough to make his body jolt, was subsiding.

His closed eyes opened.

It felt as though he had been in a long dream. He could not hear the Void Monarch's voice. Of course he couldn't. He was no longer by his side. Lee Sungmin slowly pushed himself up.

His body felt alien, a sense of disharmony even more intense than what he had felt after swallowing Aine's heart. As he sat up, he became aware of what had happened and impassively realized the truth. He had achieved a complete Body Reformation. His qi center was gone. The enormous reserves of inner qi and Demonic Energy it once held now flowed freely within him, no longer confined to a single point. His entire body had become a vessel, a qi center in itself.

"How is it?" Jeniela asked, her voice trembling with excitement.

Lee Sungmin looked at her with vacant eyes. Seeing his expression, she felt a slight sense of unease. *Has his brain not regenerated properly yet?* The thought crossed her mind, but she chattered on without hesitation.

"Look. See what happened while you were unconscious."

Jeniela did not explain in detail what she had done. She thought it would be more enjoyable for him to see it with his own two eyes.

Lee Sungmin's gaze slowly swept across the scene. He saw Osla's torn wings and a powerless Rubia. He saw a blood-soaked Baek Sogo, a blood-soaked Scarlet, a blood-soaked Black Dragon Knight. Blood, blood, and more blood. He slowly closed his eyes.

*No one is dead.*

Fortunately, everyone was alive. A small measure of relief bloomed in his chest. An Elixir was not a panacea, and wounds like those could not be regenerated. If an arm was severed, they would have to live the rest of their life with one arm. If an eye was plucked out, they would have to live with one eye. Still, they were not dead. Everyone was alive. Lee Sungmin took a deep breath, grateful that the worst outcome had been avoided. It was not too late to turn things around.

"How is it?" Jeniela asked again, her excited, whispering voice urging him on as she resumed her approach. She closed the distance with light, dance-like movements.

"How do you feel? While you were unconscious, this happened. You..."

"Despair," Lee Sungmin's mouth opened, cutting her off. He did not listen to the end of her words. He already knew what she was going to ask. While he was down, Jeniela could have killed everyone. No matter how they combined their strength, they were not strong enough to do anything to her under the full moon, but she had not killed them. She had inflicted wounds that would never disappear, but she had kept them alive. He did not have to think hard to know why. What Jeniela wanted was his Despair.

"...I haven't."

*They're not dead. For now, that is enough.*

At his answer, Jeniela giggled. "Still?" she muttered with a shrug. "Ah, that's good. Very... good. You haven't despaired even after all this? That's fine, just fine. The night is still long. Even if that moon sets, it doesn't matter. I can just make a new full moon. Would that actually make it easier to make you despair? Yes, good. So you still haven't despaired. Then, what are you feeling right now?"

"Rage," Lee Sungmin answered in a low voice. He looked down at his empty hands. "Hatred."

*Something's strange,* Jeniela thought, listening to his muttering. *Did his brain really not regenerate properly? He's not an idiot, shouldn't he have realized it by now after I've done all this? Or was the shock so severe that his mind shattered?*

"...You're acting a little strange." The smile vanished from her face. If his mind had already collapsed, then everything she had done would lose its meaning. What she wanted was for him to break down in despair and fear, sobbing and screaming. If he was going to break completely, she had to enjoy everything she could before that happened, and she had not enjoyed everything yet. She wanted to push him to the limit, to endure until the very end, and feel the greatest ecstasy when she was at her hungriest and thirstiest.

The look in Jeniela's eyes changed. The All-Seeing Demonic Eye, which could see through anything, activated. As she stared at Lee Sungmin, her shoulders flinched.

*The Void Monarch is gone...?*

Just a moment ago, the Void Monarch had been inside him. He had surpassed the limits of his existence, so it would not have been strange for him to disappear at any moment, but she never thought he would vanish so suddenly. No, that was not all. Something... more...

POP!

Jeniela's eyeball burst. Black blood poured from the empty socket as she staggered back, clutching her face with both hands. She could not see through his existence. The space where his being should have been visible to her was simply... not. There had been times before when she could not see him, but this was the first time she had felt such a strong repulsion.

"...What happened?"

"A lot." There was no need to explain it to her. Lee Sungmin slowly took a step forward. He was not just looking at Jeniela. He saw Preskan, still lying prone in the distance, sobbing, and Gemini, frozen stiff beside him.

"...the Phantom Spear."

"No," Lee Sungmin said, shaking his head to deny her mutter. "I am the Slaughter Predator."

And then his body vanished.

It was not that she could not see him. Her shattered eyeball had already regenerated, and she saw where he was moving. Her movements froze, not because of his speed, but because of the words he had muttered. *The Slaughter Predator?* She already knew he had awakened as such. Him calmly admitting it now was no reason to be surprised.

*The Void Monarch is gone.*

That was reason enough. No, that was not it. The real reason she was surprised was...

A brutal impact sent Jeniela flying backward, blood spraying from her parted lips. Lee Sungmin did not stop. He shot forward from the kick, instantly reaching Gemini and Preskan. He grabbed them by the scruff of their necks and chased after the flying Jeniela. The two of them left the lakeside in an instant. When they were far enough away, Lee Sungmin stopped, slamming Gemini and Preskan's bodies into the ground. The earth heaved as they were smashed into the dirt.

A short distance ahead, Jeniela stopped her flight. She licked the blood from her lips and stared at him. "What are you trying to do?" she sneered.

He did not answer her question. Lee Sungmin quietly raised his hand. The magic power of the Fairy Forest began to surge. The Mana dissolved in the air gathered in his palm. Jeniela's eyebrow twitched as she saw what he was trying to do, her bewilderment preceding any action. She stood there blankly, watching him manipulate the magic power. No formulas or incantations were needed. The magic filling the forest moved as he wished.

Lee Sungmin's lips parted slightly. The words that left them were in a language most beings, human or otherwise, could never comprehend. Jeniela did not know what the words meant, but she knew what they were.

"Dragonic...?"

It was unbelievable, impossible. The Dragons had left this dimension hundreds of years ago. No, even if they had remained, it would be the same. How could a human, though she was not sure if he could be called human anymore, a being that was not a Dragon, use Dragonic?

The Dragonic he uttered was real. The magic power moved according to his words, expanding wide to create a wall behind him. It became a powerful barrier that completely sealed off the entire lakeside.

Though it was his first time speaking Dragonic, the words felt natural, nonchalant. It was to be expected. For the man he was now, Dragonic was something he had *always* known, something he could always use. It just felt a little awkward.

The silence in his head only amplified the feeling. There was no sneering praise, no familiar voice to comment on his power.

He already missed it.
