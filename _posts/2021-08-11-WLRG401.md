Gemini kept his head bowed, the world spinning around him.

*I'm alive. I'm... alive...?*

The words Lee Sungmin had spoken moments before echoed in his spinning thoughts. Gather the other Vampires and do nothing. There was no need to remain cooped up in the North, only to act in moderation.

It was enough. To escape Trabia, the only home he had ever known, to finally be free from Jeniela's shadow… that was a satisfaction he could live with.

When Gemini cautiously raised his eyes, he found Lee Sungmin still looking down at him. He flinched, shrinking back, but his gaze was irresistibly drawn to the soul held captive in Lee Sungmin's hand. Jeniela's soul.

Love and hate had both withered away. He had considered dying with her, but Jeniela had shown no such hesitation. For her own sake, she had tried to kill him, and whatever she might have felt in that moment was irrelevant.

What mattered was the betrayal. He remembered their eyes meeting, catching a glimpse of her emotions as she moved to take the life of the younger brother she claimed to love. There had been no conflict in her, only pity without a trace of guilt. He could not even fathom what that pity was for.

*You were the one who betrayed me, Sister.*

The thought solidified his resolve, and Gemini lowered his head once more, pushing all thoughts of Jeniela from his mind.

Leaving Gemini to his thoughts, Lee Sungmin turned away. His reasons for sparing the vampire were simple. Gemini had offered help a few times and had been quick to understand his place. By bowing his head and swearing an oath, he had made killing him unnecessary. The Blood Demon's memories, swirling in his mind, had also played a large part in the decision. It was the last mercy the man who was once the righteous Lee Sungmin could offer.

His attention then turned to Preskan, who had lain face down and unmoving since the long night had ended. Lee Sungmin stopped just before him.

"Are you dead?" he asked, the question tossed out casually.

Preskan offered no answer. The wailing and sobbing had ceased.

Lee Sungmin scratched his chin, a bitter taste filling his mouth. Unfortunately, he did not possess Aine's memories. He had devoured her heart, not Aine herself. Because he had sold her soul to Erebrisa before consuming it, her memories had not been passed on to him. He therefore had no way of knowing what kind of bond had existed between Aine and Preskan. The Blood Demon's memories had earned Gemini compassion. Had he possessed Aine's, perhaps he might have shown Preskan the same.

"I didn't kill her painfully," Lee Sungmin murmured.

"Sh… shut up!" Preskan's head shot up at the words. His spiritual body trembled violently. Though he lacked a physical form and could not shed tears, his very essence was smeared with a potent mixture of sorrow and hatred.

"If it weren't for you, only you, everything would have been fine! If you just didn't exist!"

"Isn't that always the case," Lee Sungmin replied, nodding as he listened to the curse. "It was the same for me."

"I curse you, I curse you! My daughter… I wanted to do so much more with her, better things, more beautiful things…"

Before Preskan could finish his words, the Spear of the Extreme Void erupted through his chest. His spiritual body convulsed violently.

As he watched the spiritual form twist with a sickening, gurgling sound, Lee Sungmin muttered, "Aine was a monster."

"......"

"You didn't control her. You just let her be, no matter how much she devoured."

"Because… that child… wanted to!" Preskan screamed.

Lee Sungmin wrenched the Formless Spear free. Preskan's spiritual body collapsed, twitching intermittently as he muttered a single name.

"Aine…"

His voice was thick with affection for his daughter.

"I… I curse you… I should have killed you then… back then…"

"Everyone says that," Lee Sungmin muttered with a bitter smile as Preskan's soul began to scatter. He tried to capture it as he had Jeniela's, but even the Dragon's magic was not enough to grasp it. As a Lich, Preskan's soul was already bound by contract to the Demon King of Fear. No matter how outstanding the Dragon's magic, it lacked the power to break a Demon Lord's contract and bind the soul.

*I can't even show him any pity.*

He had planned to capture the soul and bargain with Erebrisa, to sell it to the same place Aine had gone. If they were lucky, a father and daughter might have been reunited, even in such a state.

However, what cannot be done, cannot be done. Lee Sungmin watched Preskan's soul vanish completely before turning away.

The barrier he had erected around the forest still held strong. He could sense the presences gathered near it, deliberately kept from coming out. He had not wanted to put them in danger, nor had he wanted to be disturbed.

What should he say to them? That it was all over? Of course, it was not. The fight with Jeniela had ended, but The End had not.

If only he had been a little faster. Then they would not have been left with such indelible injuries. No matter how outstanding the Dragon's magic, how effective the Elixir, or how incredible Teresa's healing was, a plucked-out eye and severed limbs could not be regenerated. The thought made his heart even heavier.

Yes, if only he had been faster. If only he had become stronger than Jeniela before they were all hurt so badly. If only he had parted with the Void Monarch sooner.

Standing before the barrier, Lee Sungmin clutched his chest as a wave of emotion washed over him. He grit his teeth.

*It's okay,* he told himself. Hadn't he made a promise to the Void Monarch? That he would not Despair. That he would absolutely not Despair and would instead live a happy life. He had promised they would meet again in that distant world, after he had enjoyed everything he possibly could.

So, he must not Despair. Lee Sungmin broke the barrier and stepped through.

Everyone was gathered there.

His gaze fell first on Osla. She was crouched on the ground, her torn wings now healed without a single wound, but the restoration had done nothing to erase the haggard look on her face. Lee Sungmin clenched his fists.

Then he saw Baek Sogo, her right arm gone, her remaining hand clutching the blood-soaked sleeve of her robe. Anxious and restless, her eyes darted about as the empty sleeve fluttered in the wind. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip.

Scarlet, her left eye plucked out, kept the empty socket squeezed shut. First aid had been applied, but dried blood still clung to her eyelid. She got to her feet in surprise as she saw him enter, and Lee Sungmin felt his own steps grow heavy.

The Black Dragon Knight was stroking the shoulder of a pale-faced Teresa with his one remaining right arm. Huddled with her knees drawn to her chest, Teresa's face was stained with tears. The Black Dragon Knight's bitter gaze met Lee Sungmin's. A throbbing pain pulsed deep in his chest.

Lloyd, who had lost his right leg, staggered to his feet using his staff as a crutch. He seemed to have gained more wrinkles, though his face lit up at the sight of Lee Sungmin. Nearby, a blood-soaked Gries lay casually tossed aside. Lee Sungmin felt his own lips tremble.

And in their midst lay the Spear King's corpse. No blood flowed, but the sight was gruesome. Most of his upper body was simply gone. Only his right arm, shoulder, neck, and head barely remained. Despite this, his right hand still gripped his spear tightly. Lee Sungmin saw the satisfied smile on his face and, in the end, could not help but stop in his tracks.

The Spear King had departed on the same path as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Void Monarch. He knew that. The Spear King had died here, but his soul had gone to the world he had longed for. That did not mean there was no sorrow. It was the same with the Chairman's death and the Void Monarch's annihilation. Even if it was not death, it was still a farewell. Just a farewell.

Those left behind could not help but feel sorrow.

He wanted to hear the Void Monarch's voice. He wanted to hear praise, something like, *You did well. You've grown so much…* He missed his sneering, nonsensical chatter. Unable to walk any further, Lee Sungmin stood rooted to the spot and bowed his head.

Though he tried to hold them back, tears filled his eyes, the ground and the tops of his feet rippling through his vision.

He had told me not to Despair, but he never said not to grieve. If he had, I would have treated it as another promise, another reason I was not allowed to be sad.

In the end, the tears began to fall.

He hated himself for thinking it was not a bad outcome. The Spear King was dead, but he had been satisfied with his death. Everyone had suffered indelible wounds, but they had not died. He had parted with the Void Monarch, but he had gained that much power in return.

It was not a bad outcome, not when he thought about the future. He hated himself for thinking that. The Spear King was dead, he had parted with the Void Monarch, and everyone was injured. He hated the part of himself that felt a little relieved it had ended 'at this level'.

"Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo approached him as he stood there blankly, shedding tears. Besides her severed arm, she had suffered various other injuries, but the Elixir and Teresa's healing had mended them. She was just mentally exhausted. Though her steps lacked strength, she urged herself forward.

Scarlet followed behind her. The others moved as well. The Black Dragon Knight supported the limping Lloyd. Teresa approached, sniffling. Osla moved her wings and drew closer.

"You… did well," Baek Sogo said, her voice soft as she reached him first. She wrapped her one remaining arm around his shoulders. She wished she could hug him with both arms, and the painful reality that she could not sent a fresh pang through her. "You did well… really… you worked so hard…"

*Why is he crying?*

Baek Sogo did not know the reason. She was not Lee Sungmin. She did not know what he was feeling or what farewells he had endured. She could not fully empathize with his emotions. Instead, she hugged him sincerely and offered him those words.

"Why are you crying?" Scarlet reached out, placing a hand on the other shoulder that Baek Sogo could not embrace. "You did a good thing. You won. So… that's all that matters. There's no reason for you to cry." She furrowed her brow, her blind left eye causing her discomfort.

"He went with satisfaction," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, his gaze shifting from the Spear King's corpse back to Lee Sungmin. "…That foolish old man. He should have at least shown us why he was so satisfied before he left. You don't have to be sad. He died content, and he lived long enough."

Lee Sungmin covered his face with both hands.

"Thank you, thank you…" Teresa sobbed, bowing her head. "It's all thanks to you, Lee Sungmin. If it weren't for you… we would have all…" She could not finish her sentence. Not everyone had survived. They had almost died, and the Spear King was dead. Still, she hated having to say such things.

"Honestly, I thought I was going to die," Rubia admitted. She was slumped over, her magic utterly exhausted, her expression listless. Lee Sungmin gave her a bitter smile. Before his head was blown off, he had aimed for Jeniela through the opening she had created. He had failed, and his head had been blown off, but still.

"…I'm sorry. I… I couldn't do anything…" Lloyd muttered, his voice full of guilt. He despised himself. Why had Gries' magic disappeared? If only he had seized the opening and banished Jeniela from spacetime…

"Don't cry," Osla sniffled. "If you cry… what does that make me? You did all that and defeated Jeniela in the end. If you cry… it makes me look so pathetic. I, I really… couldn't do anything. I was too scared…"

"…No, that's not it," Lee Sungmin barely managed to speak. "I'm… crying because I'm happy. We succeeded, didn't we? Yes… because we defeated Jeniela. I'm happy about that…"

"Liar…" Baek Sogo whispered in his ear.

At her words, Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled slightly.

"…Why… are you crying, Junior Disciple…?" Baek Sogo asked, looking into his eyes.

His mind was a mess. He could not define any of the emotions he was feeling right now with a single word.

"…Because I hate myself," Lee Sungmin muttered after a brief silence. "I… I really hate myself."

*The me who was weak.*
*The me who was too late.*
*The me who let the Void Monarch go.*
*The me who thinks it's a relief that it ended like this.*
*The me who has become a monster.*
