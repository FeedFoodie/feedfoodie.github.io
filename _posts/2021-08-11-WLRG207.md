The sudden attack sent the martial artists of the Noble Clan rushing out to meet the intruder.

Lee Sungmin calmly observed the level of the men approaching him. None were masters of the Supreme Peak. Most were at the peak realm, and some were even weaker than that. He knew that even for a prestigious clan famed for its martial arts theory, not everyone could be a master. A good bloodline and outstanding techniques did not guarantee mastery. To reach that pinnacle, one needed talent first, and with the right martial arts, the proper environment and support had to follow. Only a select few blessed with talent ever received such support, which included rare miracle herbs. The martial artists of the Noble Clan who fell outside that category were, at best, only slightly above the peak realm.

Even so, in a world teeming with Third, Second, and First-Rate fighters, the peak realm was not a level to be taken lightly.

To Lee Sungmin, of course, it was laughable.

His widely expanded soulmates formed an invisible barrier that was also an attack. The martial artists of the Namgung Clan charged spiritedly toward him, only to collapse within the domain of his soulmates, coughing up blood as they suffered grievous internal injuries.

"W-Who in the world are you? Do you know where this is…!"

"The Namgung Clan." Lee Sungmin knew very well where he was. He answered the man who had asked the question while vomiting blood and simply walked past him.

His unstoppable advance finally came to a halt. A new group of martial artists blocked his path, their auras entirely different from those who had tried to stop him before. Lee Sungmin reached back and grabbed the spear slung across his back. There were even 초peak masters mixed in among them. Using his spear would be faster than subduing them with his soulmates alone.

"Esteemed one, for what reason are you causing a disturbance at the main house of the Namgung Clan?"

"I'm not old enough to be called an 'esteemed one'," Lee Sungmin muttered, infusing his spear with enhanced qi. The man who had spoken frowned as he watched the deep purple energy coil around the weapon.

"What is the reason for your rampage?"

He did not answer. He hadn't thought of an excuse.

Instead of replying, Lee Sungmin raised his spear forward. The warriors of the Namgung Clan, led by the questioning man, stopped asking questions and drew their swords.

The commotion that began at the main gate was immediately reported to the family head, Namgung Baek. Someone had smashed the main gate of the Noble Clan and invaded. Hearing this, Namgung Baek asked with a look of utter disbelief.

"An intruder? How many?"

"J-Just one."

"One person…?"

Namgung Baek was even more dumbfounded by the answer. He needed to go and catch Namgung Heewon, who had escaped their grasp, yet now some fool with more guts than brains had decided to invade the Namgung Clan all by himself.

"Capture him if you can," Namgung Baek spat out in an annoyed voice. "And kill him if it's unavoidable."

"Um… about that…" The man delivering the report looked distressed. "The Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps has already gone… but they've all been subdued."

"…What?"

At the man's words, Namgung Baek's eyes widened. The Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps was one of the most representative military forces of the Namgung Clan. To think that the entire corps, which even included a Supreme Peak master, had been subdued.

Namgung Baek looked at the elders and spoke. "…It seems I have something else to do before I go catch my worthless son."

"Of course, you must," one of the elders said, clicking his tongue. "I don't know what kind of madman this is, but to think he'd come here…"

Lee Sungmin left the fallen members of the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps vomiting blood on the ground and started walking again. There had been a Supreme Peak master mixed in, and their coordinated attacks had made them quite a nuisance. Still, it had not taken long to subdue them all.

[It wouldn't have been a nuisance if you had just killed them all without holding back,] Void Monarch muttered.

He was right. Subduing them was far harder than killing them. Since he was trying to incapacitate them with as few injuries as possible by focusing on internal damage, there was much more to be mindful of.

*After stirring up this much trouble, the family head should be making an appearance soon.*

It was just as he thought. Although the warriors he had just fought were skilled, masters far superior to them were now approaching. Lee Sungmin stopped walking completely, and it did not take long for them to come into his view. A feast of high-level movement arts unfolded as he stared at Namgung Baek and the elders landing silently not far away.

"…Who in the world are you?" Namgung Baek asked. The intruder had walked from the main gate all the way here. After causing such a commotion, the martial artists of the Noble Clan must have rushed to fight him one after another, and yet… there wasn't a single scratch on the intruder's body. Not a speck of blood stained him, and he showed no signs of fatigue.

Namgung Baek swallowed hard. He could have done the same, of course. However… the feeling was different. A strange, inexplicable sense of unease gripped his senses. He was not the only one feeling it. The five elders gathered around him also felt a strange aura emanating from Lee Sungmin.

"You're not answering. What grudge do you have to cause such a rampage here…?"

"I have no grudge," Lee Sungmin said, then fell silent for a moment as he searched for an excuse. "The Namgung Clan is one of the world's most renowned prestigious clans, a gathering of those who have mastered martial arts. I simply came because I wanted to experience for myself just how great the sword of the Namgung Clan is."

"…Are you seriously saying that right now?"

"……" Lee Sungmin nodded his head. Back when he was at Shaolin, he had heard a story about Wiji Hoyeon from Jihak. 'I want to see Shaolin.' For that reason alone, she had crossed the main gates by herself and had easily defeated Jihak, who was said to be the future of their temple. He spoke now, remembering that story. He came because he wanted to see the sword of the Namgung Clan.

The reaction, however, was not good.

[Of course it isn't, you bastard. From their perspective, some crazy bastard suddenly broke into their house and is raising hell.]

"You came because you wanted to see the sword of the Namgung Clan…?" Namgung Baek's voice was filled with rage. Although the reaction was fierce, it seemed his hastily made-up excuse had worked well enough. He drew his beloved sword from his waist, and a chilling killing intent swirled around him.

"What's your name?"

"…Jung Hyunsoo," Lee Sungmin answered with the name written on his C-Rank Mercenary Badge. He would not reveal he was the Phantom Spear by giving the name Lee Sungmin. At the name Jung Hyunsoo, Namgung Baek's eyebrow twitched.

"Do you know who I am?"

"The family head?" Lee Sungmin tilted his head and asked.

At his words, Namgung Baek nodded. A blue Protective Qi Aura erupted from beneath his feet. "You seem confident in your skills, but you will pay the price for your arrogance with your life," he shouted and walked forward.

With every step he took, an intense killing intent washed over Lee Sungmin. The surrounding martial artists of the Namgung Clan let out gasps of admiration. Although he had not entered the Transcendent Realm, the aura Namgung Baek emitted was intense enough to make one tremble just by seeing it.

"The family head's martial arts have progressed even further," one of the elders muttered.

*What is he trying to do?* Lee Sungmin was a little bewildered as he watched Namgung Baek approach. He was not overwhelmed by the man's aura in the slightest.

*There are too many openings.*

Namgung Baek's body, advancing with a sword in hand and wreathed in a Protective Qi Aura, was full of them. It felt like it would all be over if he just walked up and thrust his spear once. *Is that Namgung Baek? Is that… the head of the Namgung Clan, the Heavenly Venerable Sword King?* Lee Sungmin did not even assume the starting stance of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He felt no necessity to do so.

*Ah.*

He suddenly understood the bewilderment he was feeling. It was not that Namgung Baek was weak. His skill was among the highest of the Supreme Peak masters, great enough to enter the Transcendent Realm at any time, should he overcome the wall.

*Full of openings.* That thought was wrong. There were almost no openings in Namgung Baek's body. His Protective Qi Aura completely blocked most attacks, and the sword held before his chest could parry or deflect any assault. It was just that Lee Sungmin could tear or pierce through that Protective Qi Aura like paper at any moment, and he could kill Namgung Baek before his sword could even move. That was why he looked full of openings. In fact, even if Namgung Baek showed no openings, to Lee Sungmin, he would still appear to be full of them.

*I've gotten too strong.*

Should he feel proud of that? No, he shouldn't. His enemy was Heaven Beyond Heaven, an organization that could kill the likes of Namgung Baek as if he were a child.

"Do you think you can receive my sword in that state?" Namgung Baek asked in a fierce voice. Just as Lee Sungmin saw him as full of openings, Namgung Baek also saw Lee Sungmin as full of openings.

The difference in skill… was too great. That was why he could not see it. He did not know that this man was overwhelmingly stronger than him. He was unaware that the figure that looked so vulnerable and that worn-out spear could pierce his Protective Qi Aura and take his life at any moment.

"Insolent whelp!" Namgung Baek shouted and unleashed his movement arts. His form blurred, his sword accelerating in an instant to descend toward the top of Lee Sungmin's head with the force of Mount Tai.

Lee Sungmin looked up at it and slightly moved the spear in his hand.

"……"

Namgung Baek's sword was knocked back. His eyes, which had been certain of a clean cut, widened in shock. His wrist throbbed as if it were broken. *No, I can't panic.* He recalled his composure and bit his lower lip. He gripped the deflected sword with both hands and swung his entire upper body.

*Slow. Light. Obvious.* Compared to the sword of the Sword Saint, how insignificant was this? *Is this the sword of the Namgung Clan?* Lee Sungmin felt a sense of futility as he moved his spear again. There was no need to unleash the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Even without it, his spear was faster and heavier than Namgung Baek's.

The enhanced sword qi scattered upon colliding with the spear. Namgung Baek's face turned pale.

Lee Sungmin extended his foot, closing the distance between them. He used Ran's Technique to turn the spear that was in contact with the sword outward, leaving Namgung Baek's torso wide open. Lee Sungmin's hand tapped his chest.

"Keuk!"

Blood spurted from Namgung Baek's mouth. It was an internal injury. His qi and blood twisted, and the flow of his inner arts became tangled. His feet slowed. The same hand that had struck his chest grabbed his shoulder. Then, Lee Sungmin pushed, knocking him backward.

THUD.

Namgung Baek fell to the ground and stared at the sky with eyes full of disbelief. This was impossible. In all of Eria… was there truly someone who could so easily handle the head of the Namgung Clan, the Heavenly Venerable Sword King?

*Ah.*

He remembered someone. A meeting from a long time ago. When he had just become a Supreme Peak master, a man who called himself 'the Sword Saint' had come to see him. The fight from back then… was still vivid in his memory. It was the first time in his life he had experienced such an overwhelming defeat.

"A-Are you from Heaven Beyond Heaven?" Namgung Baek asked in a trembling voice.

At his words, Lee Sungmin showed a surprised expression. "You know of Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

"Of course I do…!" Namgung Baek contorted his face and tried to get up. He was internally injured, but not to the point where he could not move. "Why is Heaven Beyond Heaven…? I met the Sword Saint thirty years ago. Why now, again…!"

"…I'm not from Heaven Beyond Heaven."

From Namgung Baek's words, Lee Sungmin could roughly understand. Heaven Beyond Heaven met with martial artists who had the potential for the Transcendent Realm. They fought them, and if they had no potential to enter that realm, they killed them. If there was potential, they let them live and waited for them to enter the Transcendent Realm, after which they would recruit them. Judging from Namgung Baek's words, the Sword Saint had met him thirty years ago. He had not died. Perhaps the Sword Saint had seen the potential for the Transcendent Realm in him.

That was thirty years ago… and Namgung Baek still had not reached it.

"Then you… who in the world are you…?!" Namgung Baek demanded, his face contorted. "You're in the Transcendent Realm… that's for sure, but you say you're not from Heaven Beyond Heaven…?"

"I'm not."

"Is it really… all just because you wanted to see the sword of the Namgung Clan?"

"……" Lee Sungmin answered and placed his hand on Namgung Baek's chest.

Namgung Baek's eyes widened in alarm. The moment he tried to say something, inner arts pushed through the palm and struck deep inside his body. Black blood spewed from his mouth.

"Though I was only met with disappointment."

Lee Sungmin left the fallen Namgung Baek and looked at the elders. He had inflicted a rather severe internal injury, so the man would not be able to move for a while. Without the family head's command, the Namgung Clan probably would not move, especially now that the main house had been attacked and left in this state. In that time, Namgung Heewon would be able to leave Bellador and get to a location where their pursuit could not follow.

However, it was not enough. Lee Sungmin looked at the elders of the Namgung Clan, who were standing frozen in stiff postures.

"Your family head is in this state, and you're just going to stand there and watch?" he asked as he approached them.

First, he planned to incapacitate everyone who could be called a master of the Namgung Clan.
