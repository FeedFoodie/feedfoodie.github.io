CRACKLE!

A deafening roar tore through the world as a blinding light blanketed the heavens and the earth.

Though the blast was not aimed at him, a definite premonition of death seized Lee Sungmin, and he stumbled back in shock.

KABOOM!

The roar was followed by an explosion that shook the very fabric of space. It was neither a direct hit nor a glancing blow. The mere collision was enough to shatter the space where it occurred.

Fragments of earth shot upward, forming clumps that instantly disintegrated into fine grains of sand. The harsh northern winds swept up the resulting hundreds of millions of grains, whipping them into a massive sandstorm.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stood at the center of it all, clad in black lightning. He looked less like a man and more like the Thunder God incarnate.

To Lee Sungmin's eyes, the Martial God was nowhere to be seen.

However, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman saw his movements. No, he felt them. The Martial God was running, stepping upon the fragments of shattered space. These fragments, which should have fallen, scattered, and vanished, served as his footholds, imperceptible to those who stood in the mortal realm. As he stepped from one to the next, his presence was faint.

This was the origin of the Dark Supreme's movement arts, created by the Martial God himself. His movements were truly as indistinct as a ghost's.

As the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's body floated into the air, the Martial God leaped again.

He threw a surging punch that contained enough power to shatter a mountain, all of it focused on its single target without dispersing.

The Chairman's hands, which had been clasped behind his back, came free. His wide sleeves fluttered as he twisted at the waist, whipping his hand out with the flexibility of a lash.

KABANG!

Power radiated in all directions from the impact, yet neither of them was pushed back. A blue light shimmered around the Martial God's shoulders as the erupting power became hundreds of bundles of enhanced qi that descended upon the Chairman.

The Chairman raised the left hand he had not yet swung. A single palm strike tore through the atmosphere, which shimmered like a mirage before lightning erupted from it. The bundles of enhanced qi and the lightning collided, annihilating each other.

The entire exchange had lasted only a few seconds, a greeting between acquaintances meeting after several hundred years. If they had been able to gauge each other's level and determine the upper hand from this alone, they would have been disappointed in one another.

However, the Martial God was not disappointed, and neither was the Chairman.

*Not yet. Yes, not yet.* A light gleamed in the Chairman's eyes. The essence of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, a technique he rarely used, filled his entire body, turning it into a single, massive qi center. The martial arts he unleashed through it were faster than anything in this world.

If the Martial God ran by stepping on fragments of space, the Chairman became the very concept of speed, his form piercing through space itself.

The Martial God did not panic as the distance closed in an instant. He genuinely intended to kill the Chairman here. If they could not join forces, the man's existence was nothing more than a great hindrance. As the Martial God thrust his palms forward, a tremendous force became a wave that crashed down on the Chairman.

The Chairman let out a short laugh.

RUMBLE!

Thunder roared when he clenched his fist. Lightning flashed when he raised it. When he shot that fist forward, everything in its path was pulverized as a streak of black lightning carved its existence deep and wide into the world.

The Martial God's palm energy lost its form, and his brow furrowed. Without panicking, he retreated to prepare his next move.

The battle they waged took place in an instant, as if a single strike had been split into hundreds, yet neither of them felt rushed. Rather, it seemed as if the flow of time itself struggled to keep up with their movements.

The power gathering in the moving Martial God's hands was that of calamity itself. He raised both hands high and slammed them down.

KRA-KOOOM!

The entire earth collapsed, a force that compelled all creation to look up and submit.

However, the Chairman's form did not falter. By the time the Martial God had compressed the space, he had already leaped through it. The Gale Thunder of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, when he willed it, was faster than anything in the world.

Interference with space was the insight of the Transcendent Realm, and how one deepened that insight determined one's level within it. The Martial God and the Chairman had long since passed even that domain.

SHIIING!

The space around the Martial God wavered. Having used Gale Thunder to get behind him, the Chairman's palm strike shattered the Martial God's body.

FWASH. Amidst the splattering blood and fragments of organs, the Chairman saw through the deception.

"Still enjoy your little tricks?" he asked with a laugh.

The Martial God, a master of all martial arts, particularly enjoyed illusions, but one could not look down on them simply because they were illusions. Once they completely interfered with space, they were no longer illusions but distorted reality and fact.

The falling corpse of the Martial God vanished. The Chairman raised both arms as a small light coiled around his palms.

He gauged the distance between himself, Lee Sungmin, and the carriage. Though he was properly controlling his power to prevent it from scattering, he had to be careful not to let his disciple die from a stray attack.

Sensing what was about to happen as the light on the Chairman's palms gradually swelled, Lee Sungmin quickly grabbed the carriage. He gripped it with both hands and leaped back, increasing the distance between them. The Chairman chuckled at his action.

"You have some sense, at least."

He muttered this, then cupped the light with both hands.

RUMBLE... RUMBLE!

The swollen light left the Chairman's hands. As the space wavered, the Martial God's body popped out. With a face full of irritation, he spread his hands to block the sphere of light.

It was too late. The light exploded, spreading in all directions as countless bolts of lightning. Those innumerable bolts then shot toward a single point, their sole target the Martial God.

The Martial God gritted his teeth and reached out with both hands to grab space itself. He seized it whole and twisted it, creating a spatial distortion in an attempt to forcibly change the lightning's flow.

KABOOM!

The redirected lightning struck the ground. After digging deep into the earth, it shot back up.

The Martial God chose a different option than facing it head-on.

FWIP!

His body flew, and the bolt of lightning no longer pursued him.

A single black streak was etched from the ground to the edge of the sky. The Chairman glanced at the surreal phenomenon he had created before turning to the approaching Martial God.

The Martial God's wavering hands were an illusion art that was not an illusion art. The outlines of his arms blurred and then split in two, then continued to multiply.

Originally, it was an illusion that made an opponent perceive multiple pairs of arms, each performing a different fist technique, hiding a killing move among the feints. Not for the Martial God. When he used an illusion that showed hundreds of pairs of arms, it truly became hundreds of pairs of arms. The space where they fought became a festival of the thousands of fist techniques he unleashed.

There was no need to aim for a blind spot.

The overwhelming volume created a barrage that pressed down on the Chairman, who lowered his stance and raised both hands.

Sound could not keep up with their movements. Silent attack after silent attack collided.

Compared to the hundreds of fist techniques unleashed by the Martial God, the Chairman's movements were simple, but that was enough.

His speed allowed him to respond to the hundreds of simultaneously unleashed fist techniques without delay. It was an absurd feat. There was not even the slightest time lag between the attacks, they were literally simultaneous.

How could one possibly counter thousands of such attacks with only two arms?

He was a little late.

Or perhaps a little early.

He extended his hand before a fist technique even began. Fist, palm, claw, the method was meaningless. Whatever the gesture, it severed the technique before its movements could connect, before it even began.

Starting with that single motion, he split time and motion, again and again, continuously splitting them. He was completely mocking the instant it took for a fist to become a technique. Even the Martial God could not help but marvel at that phenomenal speed.

By taking a few steps back, the Chairman dismantled all of the Martial God's fist techniques. Every single punch had been a killing move, not a feint, each holding enough power to destroy a Protective Qi Aura with a single hit, yet the Chairman, who had so easily deflected thousands of such attacks, showed no signs of fatigue.

"More interesting than the Dog Beating Stick Formation."

The Chairman delivered this assessment with a grin.

Being compared to the Dog Beating Stick Formation was a clear insult to the Martial God.

"...Hoo."

The Martial God steadied his breathing and brought his hands together in front of his chest.

KWUOOOOH!

A massive surge of inner qi rose from beneath his feet and swallowed his body whole.

His form vanished once again.

Startled by a premonition, the Chairman twisted his upper body just as an invisible, powerful strike flew past him.

KABOOM!

The spot where the force had grazed past split open with a crack. The faintly visible Martial God twisted his waist and swung his right hand.

SHAAAA!

With a horrifying sound, the world was split in two. The Chairman glanced at the catastrophic wreckage left in the wake of the force.

"Come to think of it," the Chairman said. "I never got to ask what happened at the Hyujael Mountains."

The Martial God did not answer. The enhanced qi that had swallowed his body swelled even larger.

POP, POP.

Fist-sized spheres of enhanced qi shot out from his body one by one. One became two, and two became four, until countless orbs of enhanced qi, each with the same power, swirled around him. They became an innumerable army following at his back.

"Aren't you going to tell me?"

"Do you have the luxury to ask?" the Martial God retorted.

The Chairman answered with a grin that showed his teeth. At that smile, the Martial God chuckled.

"Don't get complacent."

As he spoke, he flung his hand forward.

KWARURURU!

Thousands of spheres shot toward the Chairman. The Chairman shook his head from side to side and muttered, "It's not complacency."

Their power was undeniable, of course.

TAP.

The Chairman rose onto his toes and leaped lightly. He did not evade but ran forward. When a black current wrapped around his body, he became a single bolt of lightning, bending at impossible angles as he shot forward.

The spheres of enhanced qi caught in his path floated up and then exploded. A single explosion engulfed the adjacent spheres, creating hundreds of chain reactions.

The Martial God was controlling all of those explosions. He reached out, holding the power so it would not leak, and then aimed it for the Chairman's back. Even such a technique was laughable.

CRACKLE!

The erupting lightning covered the surroundings, and the Martial God's enhanced qi was caught in it, losing its light.

"I've thought about it again, but... yes. Today's not the day."

"What do you mean..."

Before the Martial God could finish his question, he hurriedly raised both arms. A punch from the Chairman sent his body flying back. It was not a particularly heavy blow, but its speed was enough to startle him.

"It's a matter of direction," the Chairman muttered. He had no intention of saying one was better than the other.

The applications for the insights of the Transcendent Realm were far too broad. To interfere with space meant to interfere with the most fundamental laws of the world, and the moment a human became capable of that, they ceased to be human. That was why the Transcendent Realm was called the Transcendent Realm.

It was not perfect. Those insights were no different from learning to take one's first steps.

From there, one chose a direction in which to develop them. In the Martial God's case, he had focused on illusion arts.

Therefore, the Chairman could not use the same techniques. Distorting space, turning movements that should have remained feints into killing moves, was something the Martial God could do, but the Chairman could not. He had not chosen that direction.

"You cannot chase me," the Chairman stated with a grin, his words filled with absolute, unwavering sincerity.
