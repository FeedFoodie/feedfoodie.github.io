He did not know what in the world had just happened, but one thing was certain. That human was dangerous. In the hundred years he had lived as a Lich, he had never experienced anything like this.

*Magic reflection... no... it's not that kind of thing. If he had that kind of blessing in the first place, my attack shouldn't have worked at all.*

The Lich braced himself against a wall, his staggering body needing the support. For a monster like him, a physical body held little meaning. The only thing a Lich needed to survive was the 'Life Force Vessel,' an artifact obtained by offering his body and soul to a contracted Demon God. As long as that vessel remained intact, a mere body like this could be reconstructed at any time.

Physical damage, in other words, was not a significant problem. Mental damage, however, was a different story entirely. It was the most fatal method of attack against a Lich, a direct assault on the mind.

*That bastard... what in the world is he...?*

His magic had been reflected, but it was no simple reflection. The spell that had surged back into him was so lethal and powerful that it had nearly shattered his mind, the damage even reaching his hidden Life Force Vessel.

*He couldn't reflect my other magic, but... the magic to read his memories and force his submission was reflected. What is this? What kind of blessing does he have...?*

It was just one more suspicious detail in a growing list. The Demon King of Fear, with whom the Lich had a contract, had refused to accept the human's soul as a sacrifice. *I cannot accept it,* the Demon Lord had declared. As a result, the Lich had been forced to pay the price for the summoning with a treasured magic power stone.

"Damn it... what in the world is going on...!" the Lich spat in fury as he forced himself to move.

The reflected mental damage made every step an ordeal, and casting magic was impossible. He had never imagined he would end up in such a state, brought on by a human who was a mere peak master.

*First, I need to replenish my lost magic power...*

Dragging his robes, the Lich walked onward. He did not want to touch Lee Sungmin again. It was a feeling akin to fear, something the Lich had not felt in a very long time.

He headed for the place where he had tossed Zeon and the other S-Rank Mercenaries, a chamber deeper than the workshop. Here, the Lich conducted the research for his lifelong ambition, the very goal that had driven him to become a Lich in the first place.

Two hearts.

The Lich looked up at the pair of hearts floating in the air. His study of the Chimera was a quest to realize the ultimate form of a species. By mixing different species, one could create an artificial life form that approached perfection. That which was born from such a process was a Chimera.

He had created the Ogre Chimera because he believed that among bipedal land monsters, the Ogre was the species closest to the ultimate. The result, however, had been unsatisfactory. For one, it was hideous to look at, and it was not easily controlled. The life the Lich wanted to create was something more beautiful and more powerful.

Those floating hearts were the culmination of all his research. The only flaw was that the body prepared to contain them was incomplete. Originally, he had planned to offer the soul with the twisted causality to the Demon King of Fear. In return, he would have perfected the incomplete body and the hearts.

However, he had failed. He had never had a sacrifice rejected before. The Lich, who had been looking up at the hearts, ground his teeth. It did not matter. He had no desire to touch that unknown human, but he would first replenish his damaged magic power, take the hearts, and leave this place. He approached the scattered mercenaries.

"You cute little thing. Pretending to be asleep."

The Lich let out a cackling laugh. At his words, Zeon's fallen body flinched. The mental damage the Lich had sustained had broken the spell he had cast. While the other mercenaries were still unconscious, Zeon, the most skilled among them, had already regained his senses and was holding his breath.

"Were you looking for an opening to launch a surprise attack? Don't even think about it. It's useless. Even if I'm not at my best, I can still play with the likes of you like a child."

The Lich's words were true. Zeon, who had been feigning unconsciousness, bit his lower lip and pushed himself up. The Lich ignored him and formed a beastfolk. The bodies of the mercenaries scattered around Zeon floated into the air.

"Annoying... so very annoying. I can't believe it turned out like this. I was sure that only joyful days awaited me... Does this mean that mortals can never be certain of a future they haven't experienced..."

The Lich muttered incomprehensible words as he drained the energy from the mercenaries. Soon, they collapsed to the floor, looking like shriveled mummies. Zeon's body trembled as he watched the gruesome sight.

"...Am I... going to end up like that?"

"Hm? No, not you."

Having recovered some of his magic power, the Lich felt like he could live again. He let out a pleasant laugh and looked at Zeon.

"I plan to use you for something more valuable. Yes. A soul of your caliber..."

"Preskan."

The Lich's body flinched. Zeon, watching him, saw his eyes widen. Preskan. That was the Lich's name. The trembling Lich turned his head.

A man was standing there, watching him. With his back turned, the Lich had not noticed the man's presence, and neither had Zeon. The man had appeared in that spot as if he were a ghost.

"Found you at last. To think you'd be holed up in a place like this..." the man muttered, clicking his tongue.

Preskan, the Lich, trembled as he looked at the man, a mixture of panic and shock twisting his features.

"L-Lloyd..."

"So you haven't forgotten," Lloyd laughed, shrugging his shoulders. He slowly approached Preskan, raising a hand. "Has it been ten years? I've been looking for you for a decade."

"You... persistent bastard...! You were still chasing me?!"

"That's right. It was a tedious chase, but you... what happened to you? You seem even weaker than when I saw you ten years ago."

Preskan did not answer. He had never imagined that Lloyd would find him here, not today. It was a damn cruel twist of fate.

"Well... it's not my concern. Even if I kill you here, I don't know where you've hidden your Life Force Vessel. I guess that means I'll have to start another tedious chase. After I kill the you that's here, of course."

Lloyd strode forward. Watching him, Zeon trembled under an immense pressure. Though Zeon had broken through the wall of the peak realm and could faintly sense the wall of the Supreme Peak, the pressure emanating from Lloyd was not something he could dare to defy.

"You... damn it...!" Preskan shouted and formed a beastfolk.

Immediately, black magic power erupted from beneath his feet. Lloyd scoffed as he watched the spell take shape. He countered with a beastfolk of his own, and a brilliant golden light erupted to push back the encroaching darkness.

"Even at your full strength, you wouldn't be able to face me. Are you really going to fight me in your current weakened state?"

"You... monster!"

"I'm not sure who's calling who a monster," Lloyd sneered.

The golden light emanating from him brightly illuminated the dark cavern. Preskan, who had faltered and stepped back, was forced to make a choice. If this continued, he would fight Lloyd, suffer a fatal wound, and lose his body, only to return to where his Life Force Vessel was hidden. If that was all, Preskan would have chosen that path without hesitation.

However, it would not end there. The two hearts. They were the ultimate secret of his lifelong ambition, the very reason he had become a Lich. In the end, Preskan bit his lip and shouted.

"Open your eyes!"

At his cry, one of the hearts began to beat. It throbbed wildly as a massive amount of magic power swirled around it. Startled, Lloyd looked up into the air. He had ignored the hearts because he had not felt anything from them, but that was no longer the case. The magic power and ominous aura emanating from the pulsating heart were enough to threaten even him.

"You... what in the...!"

"Behold!" Preskan cried out.

Although it was a choice born of desperation, he felt a deep sense of awe as he looked upon the ultimate secret he had dedicated his entire life to creating. The swirling magic coalesced, taking on a physical form. Lloyd quickly formed a beastfolk and shot magic toward the heart, but his spell could not even get close before it vanished into thin air.

The body was complete. What slowly descended to the floor had the appearance of a young girl, but she was not human. The ominous magic power that the heart had been emitting was now contained within her body.

"You... what... what in the world did you create...?" Lloyd stammered.

In response to his question, Preskan spread his arms wide and shouted.

"The ultimate being!"

As if in answer to his cry, the girl opened her eyes. He had shouted triumphantly, but both the heart and the body were still incomplete. Moreover, because he had infused it so hastily, it was not properly attuned. Even so, he had no choice if he wanted to overcome this desperate situation.

*I just need to perfect the remaining one. First, this situation...*

However.

Something was wrong. The girl just stood there blankly, taking no other action. Preskan, looking at her back, shouted urgently.

"Kill that bastard!"

The girl did not answer. She just stared blankly up into the air where the remaining heart hung silent. Lloyd's eyebrows twitched as he watched her with wary eyes. He did not know what was going on, but for him, this was a golden opportunity.

Lloyd changed the form of his beastfolk.

WHOOSH!

A wave of golden light swept over the girl. Her eyes, which had been staring blankly at the heart, lowered.

POP!

The magic from Lloyd that was about to engulf her scattered into the air. Lloyd's jaw dropped as he watched the phenomenon revert to Mana.

*Dispel? No, that's impossible. She dispelled my magic? Without an incantation?!*

"......"

Preskan cried out in delight. At his shout, the girl turned her head. He froze when he saw her eyes looking at him. There was no killing intent in those emotionless eyes, but there were no other emotions either. Looking at her lifeless, doll-like gaze, Preskan took a step back.

"Wh-what is it. I'm your master, no, your father!"

"I know," the girl nodded in response to his urgent cry. As she spoke, a flicker of regret passed through her eyes for a brief moment. Instead, her eyes turned to Zeon.

"That one is fine, right?"

"...Huh?"

"I'm hungry," the girl muttered.

It happened before Preskan could say anything in reply.

It was over in an instant. Before anyone knew it, the girl was in front of Zeon. Zeon, a secular disciple of Shaolin and a peak realm 고beastfolk, had not even noticed her approach. Before he could react, death pushed his consciousness away.

And just like that, Zeon was dead.

The girl pulled her hand out of Zeon's chest. Preskan stood dumbfounded, staring at her back. Lloyd was just as speechless. Neither of them had seen her move.

The girl stared at Zeon's heart clutched in her hand, then opened her mouth wide and bit into it.

"Ha, hahaha..." Preskan laughed, filled with ecstasy.

"...Monster...!" Lloyd burned with hostility.
