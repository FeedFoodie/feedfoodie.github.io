"This is insane...!"

Jeniela's head tumbled onto the white snow, followed by a surge of blood that stained the pristine field crimson.

Though Lee Sungmin had not seen the Chairman's attack, the result was brutally clear. A horrified cry tore from his lips.

"What are you doing?!"

There had been no need for a fight. He had met Jeniela as planned, and all that remained was to follow her to the mansion for their discussion. Lee Sungmin could not comprehend why the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would suddenly strike in a situation like this.

"What do you mean?" the Chairman asked.

"Why did you attack her?"

"Do you trust her?"

The question left Lee Sungmin speechless.

"Your opponent is the Vampire Queen, Jeniella of Blood Charm. They call it Blood Allure because she can bewitch you, and they also call it Blood Allure because she is cruel. Neither meaning is a compliment. Can you truly trust a monster who has survived for hundreds of years by drinking the blood of the living?"

Lee Sungmin's mind raced back to his past encounters with Jeniela, meetings he had once dismissed as coincidental. He knew better now, of course. There was no such thing as coincidence in this world. Regardless, Jeniela had never once threatened him. She had always been amicable, a favor that seemed to stem from her own personal tastes and interests, perhaps with some influence from the Grace of Fate. Because of this, even knowing she was a monster of immense power, Lee Sungmin had rarely felt a tangible threat from her, the kind that made him feel he might truly die.

"This one cannot trust that monster," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stated.

It was a normal reaction, but Lee Sungmin knew better. The Chairman had not beheaded Jeniela simply out of mistrust.

"Ha..."

A sound broke the tense silence. Laughter.

"Hahahat! Ahahahat!"

It echoed across the snow. Jeniela's body remained standing, headless, as blood gushed from the wound to soak her clothes and the surrounding snowfield. The blood, which had already begun to freeze in the harsh northern cold, suddenly thawed. Droplets surged into the air, circling the unfallen body like crimson satellites.

The headless form slowly turned. Lee Sungmin found the source of the laughter. The head lying on the ground was bobbing by itself as it cackled.

"Did you see?"

The laughter stopped abruptly as the head crumbled into dust. At the same moment, the circling blood droplets flowed into the severed neck. A red mist hovered where her head should have been, coalescing and reforming until Jeniela was whole once more. She caressed her newly formed neck with a delicate hand and asked the Chairman, "Do you think I didn't see it?"

"You must have," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied, his voice indifferent to the perfectly restored Jeniela.

Jeniela suppressed a chuckle. "The future I see is incomplete and has many variables, but if the conditions are met, it doesn't go astray. That's because I see a future that is set to happen, a future that must happen."

"You knew this one would attack. So why didn't you avoid it?"

"Because there was no reason to," Jeniela answered with a wide smile. "You possess great power, but even so, killing me is impossible."

"Do you wish to test that?"

"Heavens, no. I have no desire to fight you. Perhaps if you were the you from hundreds of years ago, full of fighting spirit, but if we were to fight now, one of us would surely have to die. I don't wish for your death, you see, and I wish for my own death even less."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman snorted at her answer. He lowered his raised hand, clasped it behind his back, and said, "Fighting you right now is something this one also does not desire. However, as I said, this one cannot trust you."

"You're not afraid I'll harm you, are you?"

"This one will be fine. However, you must swear to the safety of those who came with this one."

"That's simple enough," Jeniela swore without hesitation. "I will never, ever bring harm to any of you who have come here."

*Does a Mana Oath even mean anything to an immortal monster?* Lee Sungmin wondered, but even Void Monarch could not answer him.

Only after receiving the oath did the Chairman nod. The problem remained, however, that the carriage could no longer move, as all the horses pulling it were dead.

"I apologize for that," Jeniela said, raising a hand. As her open palm faced forward, a small drop of blood formed at her fingertip.

POP!

The droplet shot out, flowing into the mouths of the dead horses. Their corpses trembled as if convulsing before they staggered back to their feet. She had not revived them. She, who stood at the pinnacle of Vampires, had used her blood to turn the horses into Undead zombies.

"There shouldn't be any more problems, so let's depart," she announced. "I did ask Ju Won for his understanding before coming here, but if we stay in his territory for too long, he'll get upset."

As he was about to board the carriage, Lee Sungmin heard Jeniela's voice in his head.

[What are you?]

He flinched and looked back at her.

[It's certain. When I met you during the Kim Jonghyun subjugation, my Demonic Eye could see right through you, but... why can't my eyes see you now?]

[...I'm not sure what you mean.]

[It's strange... very strange. I've lived for hundreds of years, but I've never seen a case like yours. It would be one thing if I couldn't see you from the start, but this is different. You weren't in the future I saw... your presence at this meeting today was not scheduled. Why are you here?]

It was a contradiction. She had just claimed that the future she saw was set to happen, that it must happen, yet here he was. Jeniela herself knew that her question and the current situation were a paradox, which was why it was so bizarre and bewildering to her.

[What are you?]

A variable. A singularity. An Observer. The End. He had heard Abel's story, but Lee Sungmin still did not know for sure what kind of existence he was.

*I am me. You are you.* That was what Void Monarch had said. He tried to think that way, but there were still too many questions. His role as an Observer, simply guiding The End to this place, was already over. *If so, what am I from now on? Why couldn't Jeniela's All-Seeing Demonic Eye see me? Why was I not here in the future she saw?*

He pondered this inside the carriage, lost in thought. The question was too big. First, when had her Demonic Eye been able to see through him? It was right before the Kim Jonghyun subjugation, when he rode the Fairy Horse to Trabia on his way to Eria. Not long after he had parted with Wiji Hoyeon and gone to Haras...

*...Ah.*

*No way.* Lee Sungmin's head snapped up, his eyes finding the Black Dragon Knight.

"I have a question for you."

"That's rather sudden," the Black Dragon Knight replied with a puzzled expression.

Lee Sungmin did not know if the hypothesis forming in his mind was true, so he had to ask. "Do you remember when the Martial God went to the Hyujael Mountains?"

"...I don't know the exact date... but one thing is certain. The Martial God entered the Hyujael Mountains when I went towards Eria for the Kim Jonghyun subjugation. I heard the news from the Spirit Medium as soon as I barely returned to the Murim Alliance after being heavily wounded by Ju Won."

*As I thought.*

Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. He could not believe it was unrelated.

Why couldn't Jeniela see him with her All-Seeing Demonic Eye at first? He could guess that, to some extent, he had been strongly under the Grace of Fate as an Observer, and that protection had shielded him from her gaze.

Then, when he came to Trabia for the Kim Jonghyun subjugation, his role as an Observer, a role he had never even been conscious of, was finished. He had passed the time he should have died in his previous life, and this world had been guided to The End. With that role complete, he had lost the Grace of Fate.

And not long after that, Wiji Hoyeon met the Martial God in the Hyujael Mountains. It was not certain, but judging by the circumstances, that must have been when she met the Demonic Spirit at the Demonic Spirit Pavilion.

*Wiji Hoyeon meeting the Demonic Spirit.*

*Did the Grace of Fate reappear because of that? Is that blessing protecting me again?* He did not know. He could not know. Abel had never said that he had regained it. Still, no matter how he thought about it, there seemed to be no other reason why Jeniela could no longer see through him. It had to be connected to Wiji Hoyeon making contact with the Demonic Spirit.

*I... no, what in the world are you?*

He thought of Wiji Hoyeon. He had told her they would meet again someday, never imagining things would turn out like this. He had thought they would reunite at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance in a few months at most. He had not held her back because he knew they both had things they needed to do.

*Should I have held her back then?*

It was too late for regrets. Lee Sungmin took a breath and raised his head, his eyes meeting those of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Sitting across from him, the Chairman was staring intently at him from behind his mask.

[Are you done thinking?] the Chairman asked.

It was not a story to hide from him, so Lee Sungmin explained his speculations. In response, the Chairman snickered.

[You've thought hard with that slow brain of yours.]

[Isn't that a bit harsh?]

[I'm complimenting you.]

*How in the world is that supposed to be a compliment?*

[I believe your thoughts are correct,] the Chairman continued. [The Little Heavenly Demon met the Demonic Spirit... and because of that, 'something' happened, which must have brought about a change in you as well. The Mages Guild Master said you had no value, of course, but as I said back then, this one does not think so. There is something about you, and it seems there is something connecting you and the Little Heavenly Demon.]

His first meeting with Wiji Hoyeon had not been a coincidence. He had thought it was simply due to his own will, but now he felt it was not that simple.

[The Grace of Fate, you say. There's no way to confirm it.]

[It's not that there's no way at all.]

A long time ago, Lee Sungmin had trained at Shaolin. It was there, from the words of Grandmaster Bulyeong, the former Abbot, that he had first become aware of the Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena on his body. A lot had happened at Shaolin. The reason he had decided he must go to the North was also because of the words of the Spirit dwelling in the Grandmaster's body.

[Are you saying you'll go to Shaolin to check?]

[I think it's worth a try.]

At Lee Sungmin's answer, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman cackled.

[After the Dog Beating Stick Formation, is it the One Hundred and Eight Arhat Formation? It feels like you're going on a dojo-breaking spree.]

While they were having this conversation, the carriage stopped. The distance from their starting point to Trabia was quite far, but the horses, turned into zombies by Jeniela's blood, had run without rest and arrived at her mansion. They did not even need a coachman. Inside, Yehwa sat with a slightly tense expression.

"There is no need to worry," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, glancing at her stiff face. "Even if the worst happens, I will not let you die."

The carriage door opened.

This was Lee Sungmin's second time visiting Jeniela's mansion. The gate to the large estate was already open, revealing a splendid rose garden and the elegant mansion beyond. The Chairman, who stepped out first, faced Jeniela as she stood before the open gate. She smiled, showing her fangs.

"Please, come in."

While crossing the garden under her guidance, Lee Sungmin stopped walking.

His eyes widened. There, in a corner of the garden, was Aine, her body pierced by dozens of spikes and suspended in mid-air.

Scarlet recognized her as well. She let out a gasp and grabbed Lee Sungmin's arm.

"That's, th-that. That's her from back then, right?"

"...Yes."

When he was first attacked by Aine, he had only managed to make her retreat by manifesting his enhanced qi with Scarlet's help. Now, Scarlet's body trembled at the sight of Aine's miserable state.

"Why is she here?"

As if she had heard the question, Aine's bowed head lifted.

Her yellow eyes turned toward Lee Sungmin.

...RUMBLE.

The Demonic Energy inside his body vibrated.
