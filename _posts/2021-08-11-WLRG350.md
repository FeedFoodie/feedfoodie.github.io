The Black Dragon Knight could only stare in bewilderment as Teresa stroked his side. The pain there, a constant companion for ten long years, was simply disappearing. He had thought he would carry it for the rest of his life, yet the white light emanating from her hand was erasing it completely.

Sensing his gaze, Teresa tilted her head up and offered a bashful smile.

"All done."

For her, this was a simple task, especially when compared to the time she had treated Baek Sogo's system-wide poisoning.

Teresa dusted off her hands and rose. The Black Dragon Knight stared up at her blankly for a moment before his senses returned in a rush. He scrambled to his feet, cupping his fist in his hand.

"...Thank you."

"Don't mention it. It wasn't that difficult," Teresa replied with a cheerful laugh. Her attention then shifted to the Spear King, who was watching the flitting Fairy Realms with a sullen expression. The mischievous creatures showed no hesitation, even under his glare, and chattered as they drew closer. The Spear King gulped, watching their approach.

After leaving the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight to their own devices in the Fairy Forest, Lee Sungmin turned his thoughts to the Yuzukia Mountains. Nebul had given him the location, and with the Blade Supreme dealt with in Kron and his two new allies recruited, he felt a pressing need to finish everything he could before the Spirit Queen's Advent. Honesty forced him to admit he still lacked the confidence for an all-out war against Jeniela and her Predators.

Jeniela and her high-ranking vampires were one thing. Then there was the Lycanthrope, Ju Won, and his trusted followers, and who knew what powerful individuals lurked among the Death Knights, Liches, and Yokai he had yet to encounter? So little was known about them, making the Predators' true strength a complete mystery.

*Don't you know anything?*

[What I know is knowledge from three hundred years ago.]

*Still, it's better than knowing nothing.*

[Hmm. I also know little about Jeniela's vampires. Still, haven't you met all of those high-ranking ones except for the one called the Blood Demon?]

He had. There was Gemini of the First Bloodline, and below him, the Blood Demon of the Blood Alliance. Then there were Chen, Kun, and Rao Xian, whom he had met in Germud. He had witnessed some of Chen's and Kun's skills and knew they were on a similar level to the Sword Saint of the Six Honored Ones. Rao Xian was much the same.

[Jeniela herself possesses great power. However, you have yet to face her under a full moon. Even this Elder can't quite predict how much power the current Jeniela can wield on the Night of the Full Moon.]

*How much stronger do Vampires get under a full moon?*

[It varies from individual to individual, but you can think of it as at least one and a half times stronger. The full moon grants a great deal of power to Vampires and Lycanthropes. Their immortality increases and their magic power grows stronger. Not to mention their physical strength.]

"That's not all," the Void Monarch's voice cut in.

[If Jeniela wishes it, that wench will reclaim all the power she has bestowed upon every vampire that originated from her.]

*What?* Lee Sungmin asked, startled.

The Void Monarch clicked his tongue. [Those high-ranking vampires you considered a threat, even excluding the exceptional case of Gemini, all originated from that wench. Jeniela, the Lord of vampires, can reclaim their power whenever she wishes. Do you understand what that means?]

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard at the implication. If he had to fight Jeniela, who already possessed immense power, on the Night of the Full Moon with the added strength of all the other vampires…

[It would be annihilation,] the Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin had to admit it was true. It was a situation he wanted to avoid at all costs. If a fight with Jeniela was inevitable, he absolutely had to avoid the full moon, but could he defeat her even then? If things went south, she would just take back the power of the other vampires anyway.

[Isn't that why you're trying to avoid a fight right now?] the Void Monarch said with another sneer. [Well, let's set the Vampires aside for now. Among the Lycanthropes, Ju Won goes without saying. That fellow Nero you met was also quite famous back when this Elder was alive. He was a guy who possessed great power, despite not being very Lycanthrope-like.]

*Is Nero the only one I need to worry about?*

[At your level, the ones to watch for are Ju Won, Nero, and one more. Brock. They were powerful Lycanthropes under Howon, the chieftain from three hundred years ago. Though Howon was killed by Ju Won.]

*How strong are they?*

[This Elder has never met them either. Hmm. Nero is a Were-Tiger. Brock is a Were-Bear.]

A tiger and a bear. The Dangun Myth came to Lee Sungmin's mind, but he didn't bother mentioning it.

[I haven't heard anything about Brock, but I have heard about Nero. Nero was something of a successor to Howon, who was also a Were-Tiger. To be called Howon's successor, he must be considerably powerful. You, having fought him to some extent, should know that well.]

Lee Sungmin nodded. When he had been so enraged that he attacked Nero, the Lycanthrope had been terrified, yet he had still managed to dodge or deflect his attacks. If Nero had been determined to fight to the death, he would have been difficult to subdue easily.

[You don't have to worry so much about the Yokai. Yana is with you right now, after all. The same goes for the Death Knights and Liches.]

Lee Sungmin committed the Void Monarch's words to memory and got to his feet. In the end, it was the Vampires and Lycanthropes that posed the greatest threat. It was time to go to the Yuzukia Mountains. He had asked Nebul about a place there called the Moon Palace, but it existed only as a legend in the surrounding villages.

When the high moon sinks into the center of the lake at the mountain's peak, a mysterious palace appears from within the clouds drifting across the sky.

It was an incredibly vague legend, making it difficult to track the palace's location. There was no choice but to go and see for himself. As Lee Sungmin stood, Scarlet approached him.

"I'm going with you."

"Will you be alright?"

"I wouldn't be offering if I wasn't. Besides, you don't have a plan, do you? You're just going to stomp around the mountain until you find it, aren't you?"

"There's no other way, is there?"

"You… what exactly do you take me for?" Scarlet tossed out the mocking words with a pathetic look on her face. "The person helping you, and this isn't just self-praise, is a mage whose skills are among the best in this world. It would be much better for me to go to the mountain's peak and cast a search magic than for you to rummage through the entire mountain."

"Then… I'll be in your care," Lee Sungmin said with a bitter smile.

Scarlet snorted with a 'hmph'.

The Yuzukia Mountains were to the southwest, meaning their fastest route was from Prabah, the city of the Holy Palace. When Teresa heard this, she opted to remain in the forest. She reasoned that she had practically run away from home, and if she returned to Prabah, she might have the bad luck of being caught by the Holy Knights. The Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King also chose to stay. The Spear King declared he would contemplate the martial arts he had imperfectly displayed against Lee Sungmin, while the Black Dragon Knight wanted to recuperate from the damage his body had suffered for ten years.

There didn't seem to be much danger in the journey ahead. The Moon Queen, who had ruled the Moon Palace, had died a decade ago. He was going there to search for traces of the Spirit Medium, not to pick a fight.

Still, Yana and Baek Sogo decided to accompany him, reasoning that one could never know what might happen. In the end, Lee Sungmin once again found himself mounting a Fairy Horse, this time with Yana, Scarlet, and Baek Sogo.

[It occurs to me again. You're surrounded by a lot of women. This time, too, you're only going with women.]

*It's not like I'm doing this because I want to.*

[Well, it's not for a bad reason, is it? They say heroes are fond of women.]

*I'm not a hero.*

[Isn't stopping The End the same as saving the world? If you save the world, you're a hero.]

The Void Monarch laughed heartily. Lee Sungmin ignored the sound and pictured the gates of Prabah.

The Fairy Horse completed its leap, and they set off at once. He had considered renting a carriage, but there was no need. The moment they arrived at the gate, Yana transformed without a word. In a shimmer of light, she became her Nine-Tailed Fox form, her great back offered to the others.

[Which way should we go?]

*Uh… please head northwest for now.*

"......"

Yana ran through the sky. Scarlet wore a bewildered expression, her hands sinking into the warm, golden fur that enveloped her.

"...Right… she was a Nine-Tailed Fox…"

The feeling of flying without any sense of floating was strange no matter how many times he experienced it. Baek Sogo watched in amazement as the scenery rushed by below. Scarlet, meanwhile, took a magic tome from her Subspace Pocket and opened it, apparently intending to organize her ongoing work until they reached the mountain.

Watching her, Lee Sungmin grew curious. "What kind of research are you working on, Scarlet?"

"Me?" Scarlet wrinkled her nose as she looked down at the formulas that densely filled the tome.

"Didn't you already achieve your lifelong wish?"

"Establishing a school of magic in my name wasn't my lifelong wish," she replied in an indifferent voice. In Lee Sungmin's past life, Scarlet had established her own school within the Mages Guild and become the Crimson Archmage. He didn't know what other wishes that version of her might have held, but it seemed this Scarlet had a different one.

"I want to create a grand magic," she answered. "The Reshir School's Spell Engraving is a system I created by mixing in Qimen Dunjia, but you can't call it a grand magic." She took out a pen and added, "Strictly speaking, only the activation method is different."

"Most of the magic these days are just slight variations of pre-existing magic, combining them, dividing them, things like that. The study of magic is ancient, and this world brings in people from all dimensions, right? Mages who have learned all sorts of magic come in, and magic gets mixed and created again. Because of that, it's hard to create original magic. I could probably make some trivial ones."

"In that case, are you trying to create an original magic?"

"That's probably impossible," Scarlet muttered in a small voice. "I may think of myself as a calamity, but I'm not arrogant. Any original I could think of probably already exists in this world anyway. That's why I created spell engraving first. Creating new magic is close to impossible, but changing the activation method was comparatively easy."

It couldn't have been an easy task. Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh as he watched her speak so casually.

"Not a magic that has never existed in this world before. A grand magic that bears my name, one that can only be cast with the Reshir School's spell engraving. That's what I want to make. I've established a school and built a Magic Tower, so it only makes sense to have a magic that can only be cast with this, right? Once I'm done with a certain amount of work, I'm thinking of writing a magic tome too."

Passion burned in Scarlet's eyes as she spoke. She lifted her head from the thick, formula-covered book and looked at Lee Sungmin.

"That's why I can't let the world end."

"Wasn't it because of romance?"

"That's one of the reasons. I've never even been in a relationship, so I can't let the world end out of sheer frustration, and… I've dedicated my entire life to creating a school, and now I'm creating magic. If the world ends before I can even complete a single magic tome, what meaning would my life have had up until now?"

"It's all the same, I guess," Scarlet muttered.

Her words reminded him of what the Spear King had said, that he couldn't die because he hadn't seen the end of martial arts. In the end, their reasons for not wanting The End were the same.

As for Lee Sungmin, he had never been happy.

Just as he had told Vollander and Kim Jonghyun, the biggest reason he was stopping The End was simply out of frustration. He had only ever been used. The very act of returning to the past was the Demonic Spirit using him, and all the endless events that followed were part of the fate it had intended. The life he had lived so far was little more than a trick. Before awakening as the Slaughter Predator, he was a puppet.

Happiness was a foreign concept to him. Most of his encounters ended in separation, and his forced survival to bring about The End compelled him into endless battles. He had only just now escaped that fate, yet the fate of The End itself did not change.

Therefore, he wanted to change it, if only out of frustration. He had rarely been happy, and he wanted to be happy from now on.

He wanted to see the end of martial arts. At one time, he had made that his goal. It was still the same now. Since he hadn't seen the end, he had to stop The End, if only to see that end.

And then there was his desperation.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was dead. Abel was dead.

He had to stop The End, if only to ensure their deaths were not in vain.
