The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's smiling words struck a nerve, stimulating the Martial God's emotions. Deep down, the Martial God was aware that their paths were different, but he refused to admit it. He simply dismissed the Chairman's statement as incomparable arrogance.

The Martial God glared at his opponent and raised his inner arts. The power he wielded could no longer be called something as simple as inner arts.

RUMBLE...!

A wave of force erupted in all directions, causing the very scenery around him to shimmer violently.

"You can't catch me?" he growled.

"Want to try?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked.

The Martial God offered no verbal reply. His actions would be his answer.

"......"

He shot forward, his feet barely touching the ground. His movements stretched into a blur, creating dozens of afterimages with every step. Soon, the afterimages leaped in different directions, an illusion art that interfered with space and effectively divided his body. Dozens of Martial Gods, each a perfect copy, then unleashed a barrage of different martial arts, pressing down on the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

In response, the Chairman swung both his hands in a single, fluid motion, yet within that one movement, dozens of techniques were mixed, made possible by a phenomenal speed.

KWAAAANG!

A blinding explosion of lightning erupted across the snowfield.

Watching from a distance with Ju Won, Jeniela was struck with genuine amazement. Beside her, Ju Won gasped for breath, his fists clenched so tightly his knuckles were white. The same thought crossed both their minds. Were the two men fighting over there truly human?

Ju Won fought to suppress the fighting spirit boiling deep in his chest. If Jeniela had not been there, if she had not warned him never to interfere, he would have already thrown himself into that banquet of monsters, unleashing every ounce of his strength.

"Don't," Jeniela whispered.

She had foreseen this. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would fight here, and here, he would die. That was the future she had witnessed, but how? She had seen him collapsing while vomiting blood, but as she watched the battle unfold, her eyes narrowed.

*It's not enough. The Martial God is not enough. His power can't kill the Chairman. In fact, if this continues, the Martial God will be the one who dies.*

While both men were certainly at the pinnacle of humanity, Jeniela could see that the Chairman's strength was a sliver ahead of the Martial God's. The battle was proving her right.

PABABAK!

The Chairman's high-speed attacks pierced through the gaps in the Martial God's thoughts, shattering his illusion arts. The tangible clones he had created could not withstand the fierce assault and disintegrated one by one. A grimace contorted the Martial God's face as he staggered back, though this was far from all he had. He still held strength in reserve.

KUOOOO!

A ferocious power surged from the Martial God, lifting his body into the air. He took a deep breath and extended a palm, the strike transforming into a gigantic shape that descended with crushing force.

KWAAAANG!

The very ground collapsed under the impact, yet the Chairman remained free. He became a single streak of lightning that pierced straight through the palm energy. Midway through, that single streak split into dozens, scattering in all directions. It was the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder Myraid Thunder. Dozens of lightning bolts multiplied, becoming ten thousand.

The Martial God roared, spreading his arms wide to meet the assault.

KWAAAANG!

The world bleached white in a massive explosion, yet the ten thousand black lines of lightning were not erased. From the heart of the maelstrom, the Chairman chuckled. If the essence of the Martial God's power was illusion, his own was pure, unadulterated speed.

That unimaginable speed tore through the illusions. Relentless strikes hammered against the Martial God's Protective Qi Aura. In that fleeting moment, he could not even begin to count how many blows had landed. He could only firmly maintain his defense and endure the onslaught.

*I can't see it. I can't react. Dodging? That's just a theory. There's no opening, no chance to even look for a counterattack.*

The Chairman was substituting everything with speed. Power, density, and strategy were all replaced by the velocity of his attacks. The heavy strikes chipped away at the Protective Qi Aura. If the Martial God's defense was a giant rocky mountain, the Chairman's assault was a thousand chisels and hammers striking all at once. It was being worn down, chipped away piece by piece. Even the Martial God's endless inner arts lost their meaning against the Chairman's ceaselessly fast offense and defense.

The northern sky was rarely clear, usually a canvas of gray that turned pure white on snowy days, but now, though it was not snowing and the sun had not set, the sky was turning black. It was slowly being dyed, stained by the countless black lines of lightning the Chairman created, his power bleeding its color into the world.

*It's not today.*

The thought came to Lee Sungmin as he watched the black lines in a daze. They were a chaotic web of straight lines and curves, their trajectories impossible to predict. He could not even see their starting point. By the time he registered a strike, it had already begun, and by the time he realized it, it was already over. It was a speed that could only be called divine.

[Indeed. It doesn't seem to be today,] Void Monarch said, his voice filled with admiration as he watched the Chairman pressure the Martial God. [I admit that human called the Martial God is strong, but your Master is stronger than him. It's just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said. The Martial God is not enough.]

Lee Sungmin could only nod, his expression mesmerized. This was the true essence of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, a level of mastery the Chairman had not revealed even against the Beggars' Gang. His movements were the ideal form of the art, a perfection Lee Sungmin could never hope to imitate.

*How many more times must I swing my spear? How much more time must I pour into my training to even get close to that?*

*At this rate...*

The Martial God gritted his teeth. He did not want to admit it, but he had no choice. He could not catch the Chairman.

*How long can I just defend and endure? I can't withstand this forever. Lose? Me?* The thought was poison. *No. I can't. I can't die here.* His eyes turned bloodshot with desperation.

-CRACK.

The sudden sound brought the Chairman's assault to an abrupt halt. He immediately ceased his fierce offensive and retreated, wary of the sudden intervention. It was not just the Chairman who was surprised. The Martial God also looked to the sky, his eyes wide with disbelief.

A figure descended, looking less like a human and more like a celestial fairy. She came down scattering pure white cold energy, her silver hair glowing with a transparent light, her blue eyes calm and unwavering.

The Chairman blinked his wide eyes and looked up at the woman.

"Moon Queen."

"Why are you here?" the Martial God asked, his voice laced with disbelief.

The Moon Queen never left her Moon Palace at the summit of Yuzkia Mountain. Though she was one of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens, she was more mysterious than any of the Six Honored Ones, and not even the Martial God could give her orders. So why was she here now?

"My younger sister asked me to," the Moon Queen finally said.

Younger sister. The words made the Martial God flinch. It was a secret known only to him that the Moon Queen and the Spirit Medium were sisters. The Moon Queen had joined Heaven Beyond Heaven only after the Spirit Medium had brought her before the Martial God, introducing her as her older sister. That had been their first and only real meeting. Since that day, the Spirit Medium was always by his side, but the Moon Queen remained distant. She was listed among the Six Honored Ones in name only and did not even take directives from her own sister. For her to make a move now was an exceptionally rare event.

Receiving the gazes of both men, the Moon Queen slowly opened her mouth.

"We must kill the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman here."

Her impassive words made the Martial God's face tremble. Did she think he did not know that? The moment this fight began, he had already resolved to kill the Chairman, believing such an immense power would surely become an obstacle, but if it were as simple as just deciding to do it, he would not be struggling like this.

"We will attack together," the Moon Queen stated, her expression unchanged.

The Martial God's eyes widened.

Hahaha!

Before he could even respond, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman threw his head back and laughed. After a moment, he shook his head, a chuckle still on his lips.

"Will two of you be enough?"

*No, it won't be,* the Martial God thought.

Even setting his pride aside, the Chairman's martial prowess was clearly on a higher level than his own. He doubted they could kill him even with the Moon Queen's help. He knew her skills well. She had once dueled the Spear King at his insistence, and while her strength was said to be among the top in the Transcendent Realm, her full power was not much different from the Spear King's. Could adding that much power to the fight truly be enough to kill the Chairman?

"We can," the Moon Queen answered, as if she had read his very thoughts.

Her hand slowly rose.

KIIING.

A strange power began to gather in her palm, and the Chairman's eyebrow twitched. At that same moment, Lee Sungmin broke into a run, racing toward his master. He knew this sudden intervention would be disastrous for the Chairman.

The light in the Moon Queen's hand grew larger, and the Chairman instinctively understood the danger. Two options flashed through his mind. He could attack the Moon Queen to stop her, or he could flee. A smirk touched his lips. He chose neither.

His hand shot out.

-JJEONG!

The palm strike was not aimed at the Moon Queen or the Martial God. It collided squarely with Lee Sungmin, who was rushing to his aid.

Lee Sungmin had never imagined his master would strike him. Even if he had, he would not have been able to react to the speed. The force of the palm strike sent him flying backward.

"Master...!" he cried out urgently, but the Chairman did not answer.

Lee Sungmin's words died in his throat when he saw the gentle smile on his master's face.

"......"

The light in the Moon Queen's hand finally exploded.

-JJIIIIII!

Space itself began to vibrate. It seemed to dry up like parched land, web-like cracks spreading through the air. Lee Sungmin quickly regained his footing and ran with all his might, driven by an indescribable sense of foreboding. The ominous, disgusting feeling twisted his face into a mask of desperation.

His body, moving at full speed, slammed to a halt. He had collided with something, but there was nothing there. It was like an invisible wall.

"What is this...?!"

Uwaaah!

He roared and swung his spear, but the weapon, swung with all his might, could not break through the barrier of space.

Inside the barrier, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman slowly looked around. The scenery was unchanged, yet he knew this was no longer the same snowfield. It was a place that looked the same but was fundamentally different.

"This is..."

The Martial God's expression hardened at the strangeness emanating from the space. Nothing looked different, but he knew this feeling well. He had suffered in a damn world just like this only a few months ago. It was the Demonic Spirit from the pavilion in the Hyujael Mountains. The space it had trapped him in felt exactly the same as this.

"Why... Moon Queen. How do you have such power?" he demanded.

"This is not my power," she replied, shaking her head. "The death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is something that must be done. Therefore, the Spirit has specially bestowed this power upon me."

The Martial God's eyes widened at her answer. Only the Spirit Medium could perform Spirit Possession. It had always been a one-sided process, with the Spirit suddenly possessing her body to issue directives, but for the Spirit to directly bestow power upon someone else to kill a specific target, this was unprecedented. In the hundreds of years Heaven Beyond Heaven had existed, such a thing had never happened.

The Chairman continued to slowly survey his prison. The Moon Queen turned her head toward him.

"You can never escape from this place," she said. "No one can intervene. This space is a barrier created by the authority of the Spirit."

"So it seems," the Chairman replied with a slow nod. The fact that no one could interfere was actually to his liking.

"Is this something that requires going to such lengths?" the Martial God asked, his voice full of doubt.

The Moon Queen nodded without hesitation. He clenched his fists tightly. It could not be helped, but the necessity of joining forces with her against a single opponent was a great blow to his pride. Seeing the conflict on his face, the Moon Queen whispered, "Do not think of it as shameful, Martial God."

When he did not answer, the Moon Queen spoke again, her voice emphatic.

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon is The End."

"...What?" the Martial God breathed.

"The Spirit has spoken," she continued. "Killing Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon will not bring an end to The End, but his existence is one of the main pillars that will bring about The End."

"Hahaha!"

Hearing the Moon Queen's words, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman could no longer hold back and burst into raucous laughter. He cackled without a shred of dignity before finally spitting out his words.

"This one is The End? What a fantastic load of bullshit."
