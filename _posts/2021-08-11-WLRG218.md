A goal, an ideal, an object of admiration.

To Lee Sungmin, Wiji Hoyeon had always been that kind of existence. At least, that was what he had always told himself.

However, he knew that was not all. He knew, yet he refused to express or even admit the truth, for he believed himself to be unworthy.

Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon were simply different. The lives they lived, the things they possessed, and their innate talents were worlds apart. Unlike him, who was born with ordinary qualities, Wiji Hoyeon was a calamity acknowledged by all. He was not worthy of her. It was beyond his station. That was the constant refrain in his mind.

A one-sided love.

Those were the words of the Void Monarch, and he could not refute them because they were true.

Knowing they could never suit each other, he had only ever allowed himself to think about it. He had hoped he would never reach her, that Wiji Hoyeon would always remain stronger than him. He had wished for her to forever be a goal, an object of admiration, an ideal. That way, he could watch her from afar, chasing her from a safe distance. He could immerse himself in that pursuit and think of nothing else. He did not want to be greedy, so he had only wished for a world where he would never have to be.

Lee Sungmin fell into a long silence while Wiji Hoyeon waited patiently for his answer.

"...I'm no different," he finally said, breaking the quiet between them.

"It's the same for me. I... I never just thought of you as a friend. I've always thought about you. My whole life has been about the day I'd meet you again. I almost died several times, and I've endured hardships that might have been worse than death."

That was how he had made it this far.

"I thought I wasn't worthy," he confessed. "You were so different from me, so much better. So... I didn't want to harbor a wish that was beyond my station. I was afraid. I was afraid of being rejected by you if I ever let myself desire you."

"You're a coward."

"That's right." Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly. "I'm a coward. That's how precious my relationship with you was to me. What would have happened to me if I hadn't met you? If you hadn't called me your friend? What if you found out that I... thought of you as more than that?"

Nightmares just like that had plagued him on Mount Mushii.

"That's why I was so afraid. If I just kept my mouth shut, we could at least continue as friends. It has nothing to do with goals or admiration or ideals. I... I liked you. Wiji Hoyeon, I liked you."

Since when? The first time they met? No, not then. It was while he was learning martial arts from her. From the moment he made her his goal, from the moment he began to learn from her, it had started.

"It's been a long time," Lee Sungmin said, his gaze drifting into the distance. "Not eleven years. For me, it was much, much longer than that. For so long, I constantly thought of you. I wanted to get closer, yet at the same time, I hoped you would get further away. Because I was afraid. Even when I did get closer, it wasn't my power I was worried about, but my heart. I was afraid that if you came within my reach, I... I wouldn't be able to hide it."

"There's no need to hide it," Wiji Hoyeon said softly. "Because I'm the same. It's strange. I'm also quite a coward when it comes to things like this. It's probably because it's an emotion I don't know."

She raised a hand and stroked her long hair, which she had left untied to fall freely down her back.

"The me you see now is different from the me you knew in your past life." Wiji Hoyeon closed her eyes. "You said the me of the past life hid that she was a woman. Why was that? Even if it was 'me', I don't know that version of myself very well, but... I can guess. It was probably because there was no need to reveal it. Because she didn't want to be treated as a woman, because she didn't want to be seen as one."

"Is it... different now?"

"It is. Because I met you." Her reply came without a hint of hesitation. "I let my hair down. I wore martial arts robes, but not a man's. I didn't hide the curve of my chest either. Everyone saw me as a woman. I thought it might be okay for only you to know, but I couldn't accept that for myself."

The distance between them closed.

"I wanted everyone to see me as a woman. That way, I wanted to let you know. That the me of now is different from the me you knew in your past life, and... today. I didn't wear martial arts robes. Do you know what that means?"

"...I'm not sure."

"You're the first one to see me like this." Wiji Hoyeon gave a bright smile. "It must have been the same in the past life. I'm sure of it. The past me, the current me, and maybe even the me who lived in other timelines. I'm the only one who has ever worn clothes like this, stood before someone, and said these words, and you're the only one who has ever heard them."

It felt as if time had stopped.

"I don't think of you as a friend. A friend isn't enough. I'm greedy, so I want something more than that, something much more." Wiji Hoyeon's eyes locked onto Lee Sungmin's. "And you?"

"Me too."

"Then don't just stand there."

Her voice lowered, carrying with it all the emotions she had suppressed and hidden for so long.

What would he do when he finally reunited with her? What would happen between them? He had always wondered. Now, an action born from those unconscious thoughts manifested.

Lee Sungmin's hands found Wiji Hoyeon's shoulders. He drew her in gently, and she came without resistance, falling into his embrace.

"......"

He had always felt she was so big, but the Wiji Hoyeon in his arms was small.

Lee Sungmin looked down at the top of her head resting against his chest. Wiji Hoyeon slowly lifted her face to meet his gaze. Her eyes were hoping for something, and he did not hesitate, leaning down to bring his head closer to hers.

The stopped time remained still. Not a sound could be heard, neither the rustle of the forest nor the whispers of the Fairy Realm.

The first kiss of this life was a long one.

"...This is a first, too," Wiji Hoyeon giggled after their lips parted.

Her words made Lee Sungmin laugh as well.

"......"

"Me too."

"You've done it in your past life, haven't you?"

"I have... done it."

"Well, it doesn't matter." Wiji Hoyeon licked her moist lips and smiled like a rascal. "Because in this life, your first was me."

SQUEEZE. She hugged his waist tightly.

The returning sensations felt unfamiliar.

Baek Sogo slowly pushed her seated body upright. The years of asceticism on Mount Mushii were over. In truth, she had wanted to continue her penance, but contrary to her will, it had come to an end.

"If you had continued, you would have died."

Mushii stood before her. The Goddess of Trials and Asceticism, wrapped tightly in bandages, regarded Baek Sogo with a fed-up expression. Since Mushii's existence began, countless ascetics had visited this sacred mountain. Most were people who dreamed of a realm beyond their own qualities, and they usually could not endure the long asceticism and prohibitions, giving up and leaving.

"This is a first."

Mushii had no choice but to sincerely acknowledge her tenacity. Baek Sogo, who had wrapped herself in layers of severe prohibitions, had reached a point where survival itself, let alone training, should have been impossible.

Nevertheless, she had survived.

Her body reeked, and she was little more than skin and bones from malnourishment. Despite that, Baek Sogo was alive. Having endured terrible days and continued her asceticism without stopping, her eyes were as serene as if she were not human. Mushii sighed while looking at her.

"...For me, the Goddess of Trials and Asceticism, to stop an ascetic's penance out of concern for her death."

"I can do more," Baek Sogo said, her mouth opening for the first time in a long while. The voice that came out felt awkward even to her, and she raised a hand to stroke her throat. It had also been a long time since she had 'seen'.

She froze for a moment as she looked at her emaciated, bony hands. Slowly, she moved them to feel her own face. It had been a long time since she had felt sensations, too. Baek Sogo stroked her sunken cheeks and her body, so thin that her bones were showing, and smiled bitterly.

"I've become hideous."

"I suppose so," Mushii muttered. "There's no need to do any more. Even if you say you want to, I won't let you."

"This isn't enough."

"There's no longer any meaning in this asceticism," Mushii said, looking at her. "You have obtained everything you can from this mountain. Any more will only completely ruin your body. It won't give you the strength you desire."

"Then what should I do?"

Baek Sogo staggered to her feet. After steadying her breath a few times, she looked straight at Mushii.

"...I believe I told you," Mushii said, raising a hand. "That when you leave this mountain, I, a god, will personally bestow a blessing upon you."

"I remember." Baek Sogo nodded. She remembered hearing those words but had not thought too deeply about them, unable to fathom what a 'blessing of a god' truly was.

"Since this mountain began to exist, you have endured the longest and most terrible asceticism. Furthermore, you even overcame the trial called Flam."

"I did," she nodded again.

Mushii narrowed his eyes as he looked at the nearly emotionless woman before him. "Because you overcame the trial of Flam, I will introduce you to Denir, the God of Time. If you meet him, you will once again face a terrible asceticism... different from what you have done here. However, you should be able to overcome it."

"God of Time," Baek Sogo muttered.

When Mushii extended his hand, information about Denir was engraved into her mind, just as it had been for Lee Sungmin.

"And, I will also bestow my blessing upon you." A gray light blossomed from Mushii's fingertips. "Your Junior Disciple performed asceticism on this mountain before you. That human was remarkable, but I didn't think he was worthy of a blessing. You, however, are different. In your case, I have no choice but to accept it."

"What do you mean?"

"You will become the incarnation of me, Mushii, the Goddess of Trials and Asceticism."

WHOOSH!

The light that blossomed from Mushii's fingertips connected with Baek Sogo.

"Prohibitions are no longer necessary. As my incarnation, you can leave this mountain and enjoy the same privileges as when you were performing asceticism here, even without them. That will act as a tremendous advantage in Denir's Trial."

Baek Sogo nodded with a dazed expression. Mushii did not tell her anything else, neither what it meant to become the incarnation of a god, nor what role that incarnation would play at the end that would one day come. She would find out when the time came anyway. He hid his bitterness. It was cruel, but it could not be helped. Of course, it was still unknown. Not everything had been decided yet.

There would be no need to introduce her to Erebrisa. If she had become a vessel of that caliber, Erebrisa would contact her without any need for an introduction. Erebrisa's members were the world's variables, and Baek Sogo had already become a being sufficient to be one.

After bidding farewell to Mushii, Baek Sogo descended the mountain. Even though the prohibitions were gone, her emaciated body did not move well.

*Not yet.*

As she walked, she thought of her Junior Disciple. She wanted to go see him... but not yet. She knew she was not ready. The asceticism on Mount Mushii had made her grow, but she had not yet reached a realm beyond the Supreme Peak. To reach that state, which seemed within her grasp yet remained out of reach, she needed an asceticism even greater than what she had just endured.

*It's not enough.*

Righteousness and chivalry were the firm beliefs that had sustained her on this mountain, but she knew well that she could not fulfill those ideals with belief alone. Therefore, she needed strength, a power far greater than what she had now. She needed the strength to firmly establish her ideal-like beliefs in the world.

She thought she was becoming a monster.

And yet, she was hoping to become one.
