The introduction was delivered with such confidence and cheer that Lee Sungmin could not muster a response. All sorts of questions swirled through his mind.

Slaughter Predator.

He had heard the name several times, a name belonging to the strongest inhuman, whose appearance Jeniela had so desperately wished for. In fact, Lee Sungmin himself had wondered more than once if he might be the Slaughter Predator. Just as Vollander had suggested, all the conditions seemed to point directly to him, and a cold dread had coiled in his gut at the thought. He had worried that he was the Slaughter Predator, a part of The End.

"Confusing, isn't it?" the Slaughter Predator asked with a smile.

The being in Lee Sungmin's mental world was none other than the one the Void Monarch had seen in Urmuri. The entity residing within his own mind was the Slaughter Predator.

"Why… are you… here?" Lee Sungmin managed to ask.

"Because I am you."

The Slaughter Predator rose to his feet, a grin spreading across his lips as he began to walk, strolling slowly past Lee Sungmin.

"The moment the Stone of Reincarnation is activated, Eria enters the fate of The End."

He had heard that from Abel before.

"The End begins when this world is judged to have no more value, when sufficient growth has been achieved. When it's determined that any further growth would threaten the very foundation of this world, The End commences, and the great nemesis, the cleansing, starts."

"…What does that have to do with…"

"The End of this world began because of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman," the Slaughter Predator declared, his steps halting.

In the direction he now faced, another image was materializing. It was a scene of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman overwhelming the Martial God.

"He was too strong, despite being human. Four hundred years ago, the Void Monarch's strength was also enough to shake Eria's foundations, but his case was given considerable account for the fact that he was a Yokai, not a human. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, however, was different. He was a pure human who reached too high a realm with human martial arts. His case was not the same as the Void Monarch's."

*Wait.*

Lee Sungmin's eyes wavered as he began to understand what the Slaughter Predator was saying, what it meant that The End was because of his master.

"Eria's purpose is to obtain more perfected techniques. Martial arts, magic, and everything else. Grinding up countless humans to obtain techniques more advanced than what existed before is Eria's purpose, but the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman went too far."

"Wasn't… wasn't I the reason The End began?"

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's strength crossed the line, and because of that, the fate of The End was decided. Then you grabbed the Stone of Reincarnation, returned to the past, and became an Observer, which is when the world's fate truly began to flow toward The End. In the end, it's this. You and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Both master and disciple are the culprits who brought about The End."

At those words, the strength drained from Lee Sungmin's legs. He collapsed to the ground.

"Perfected techniques…" Lee Sungmin muttered, and the Slaughter Predator nodded his head.

"The humans swarming this vast world, those summoned from other worlds, easily learn martial arts and magic through the status window. If there are hundreds of thousands of people, there are hundreds of thousands of thoughts and hundreds of thousands of talents. All of that is ground up to make martial arts and magic better than before. This was the reason for Eria's existence."

A laboratory, a Slaughterhouse. The words Kim Jonghyun had spoken echoed in his memory.

"The form of The End is different each time, but in all the times The End has repeated, there is one thing that never changes. Me."

Slaughter Predator.

"The Slaughter Predator is determined the moment a reincarnator regresses. The reincarnator who returns to the past is the one who qualifies, and the Destiny Force entangled with them flows to make them a being worthy of the name."

However, this time, there had been quite a few variables. The Slaughter Predator muttered this with his back turned, watching as scenes from Lee Sungmin's current life flowed by in front of him.

"Strictly speaking, there were quite a few variables from the very beginning."

The Slaughter Predator raised his hand, and the image changed to show Lee Sungmin from his past life.

"A reincarnator isn't chosen recklessly. Certain conditions must be met. They must be motivated, possess a decent level of martial strength, and have a clear purpose. Do you know why such conditions were set? Only then can one have the desire to improve, to become greater than their past self. Only then will they act with motivation and surely alter their destiny. That's how The End begins, but you… you were different. You had strength that couldn't even be called decent, and your purpose was incredibly vague. The same goes for your motivation. You were what's commonly called a failure. If someone like you were to grasp the Stone of Reincarnation… it becomes extremely troublesome. We have to twist fate for the reincarnator from start to finish."

Someone had aimed for that.

"Countless gods exist in Eria, but only two govern the fate of this world. The Spirit and the Demonic Spirit. Like two sides of the same coin, they have faithfully managed Eria until now, but one of them betrayed the other. It seems they grew tired of overturning the world and starting over again and again…"

*Betrayal?*

Sitting on the ground, Lee Sungmin's mind became a tangled mess once more. The Spirit and the Demonic Spirit. One of them had committed betrayal. The Spirit was acting through Heaven Beyond Heaven to stop The End, and yet, their actions felt as if they were for The End instead. The Demonic Spirit had given power to Yana and drawn in Wiji Hoyeon, but its true desires remained unknown.

"In your past life, the very fact that a being like you grasped the Stone of Reincarnation was a huge variable. So you were reincarnated, and this world became full of variables. You were too weak. You weren't strongly motivated, nor did you have a great purpose. It was obvious that even if you lived as a so-called reincarnator, you would soon die or become a worthless person."

That was why a stronger Destiny Force had protected him. The Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena had made others feel favorable toward him.

"Who… was the traitor?"

"The Demonic Spirit."

"Absurd, isn't it?" The Slaughter Predator shrugged. "A stronger Destiny Force at play means that the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit could intervene in fate more strongly than before. Thanks to that, everything became a mess. The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon gained a talent even greater than in her past life, one that was utterly absurd. The Demonic Spirit directly contacted Wiji Hoyeon and began to protect her. Within the Grace of Fate given to you, the Demonic Spirit steadily raised you. Pretending to be the Spirit, it contacted Heaven Beyond Heaven and continuously made you grow. It built up your strength by giving you trials you could just barely overcome."

It had always been like that. If Heaven Beyond Heaven had been determined from the start, they could have killed Lee Sungmin long ago, but they never did. They may have thought they were receiving the Spirit's command, but the Demonic Spirit had intervened several times. The being that had performed the Spirit Possession on Grandmaster Bulyeong at Shaolin was also the Demonic Spirit, not the Spirit.

"In this world, the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit have constantly clashed. The Spirit tried to use Heaven Beyond Heaven to secure the Demonic Spirit, but there were too many variables. It's impossible to control every human within the great flow of fate. Forging a connection with the Little Heavenly Demon was not part of the plan. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who felt favorable toward you, intervening in that situation was also not part of the plan. In the end, the Spirit changed its course to a direction of eliminating those who posed a threat, one by one."

First and foremost, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"He was too strong. Even after giving a blessing to the Moon Queen, killing the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was impossible. In the end, the Spirit used its own authority to strip him of his power. After all, he had to make his exit as The End began in earnest. However, thanks to having his power stripped, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman transcended even the transcendent and was exiled from this world."

"And next, you," the Slaughter Predator said, pointing at Lee Sungmin. "The reincarnator unconditionally awakens as the Slaughter Predator at the time of The End."

That fate was decided the moment he reincarnated.

"You were the biggest variable in this entire flow. The Destiny Force of this world is a great current, and as I said before, it's impossible to control every human within it, and so, the variable that is you grew too large over time. The connections you made, the bonds, all of it became variables. It's the result of the Destiny Force focusing too much on you, because a being like you reincarnated, and outside of that, the Demonic Spirit ran rampant. You had a destiny to somehow survive and become the Slaughter Predator. You, who were so weak, obtaining the Black Heart. Meeting the Void Monarch in the Sleeping Forest. Absorbing his remnants in the Bewitching Forest, obtaining immense Demonic Energy in Urmuri, and inheriting the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. All of that is the result of an excessive amount of Destiny Force being concentrated to make you into me."

*I might have died just to meet you.*

The Void Monarch's words came to mind. He had been right. The Void Monarch's existence was the crucial key to awakening Lee Sungmin as the Slaughter Predator.

"…The Void Monarch's death was…"

"His death was also a big variable. He too possessed too much power, and he met his end after being stripped of that power by the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit. Originally, the Void Monarch should have just been annihilated, but it was different in this world. The Demonic Spirit left his soul behind and sealed it at the entrance of the Sleeping Forest."

In the end, it was good for The End. Because of it, Lee Sungmin came to possess an enormous amount of Demonic Energy. If he became a complete Slaughter Predator here, he would become a monster with more power than any Slaughter Predator that had existed in previous worlds.

"What… happens to me?" Lee Sungmin asked, his voice seething. Variable or not, he had become a complete Yokai. That meant he had become the Slaughter Predator, a part of The End.

"If no variables existed, you would be trapped in this unconsciousness forever."

"But," the Slaughter Predator clicked his tongue, "…there are too many variables in this world. Thanks to the Demonic Spirit's betrayal, even some of the gods have started to think differently. Mushii and Denir. Those two are the most important gods in this world, the ones absolutely necessary for this world's purpose, the perfection of techniques."

Mushii's penance and Denir's Mental World.

"They knew about The End, yet they had to comply with it, but in this life, they chose something different. They made the choice to defy fate alongside the Demonic Spirit."

*No way.*

"The Demonic Spirit deceived many. It used you as a variable while refining Wiji Hoyeon behind the scenes. In the end, you become the Slaughter Predator. This was an unavoidable fate. The Demonic Spirit's aim was likely to hand you over as the Slaughter Predator and make Wiji Hoyeon into an Absolute Being who could overturn all fate. That's right. The Demonic Spirit abandoned you."

Thanks to the reincarnation of the utterly insignificant Lee Sungmin, a great deal of the Grace of Fate had been wrapped around him. The Demonic Spirit used Heaven Beyond Heaven to make him grow, and the connections and bonds he built along the way made him an even bigger variable. However, in the end, Lee Sungmin still becomes the Slaughter Predator. No matter how big a variable he became, that fate would not change. That was the Demonic Spirit's plan.

To the Demonic Spirit, Lee Sungmin was a disposable pawn into which it had poured all its effort. The variables created by his existence were merely a smokescreen, and in the end, the Demonic Spirit got its hands on Wiji Hoyeon.

"Heaven Beyond Heaven… said that if they secured Wiji Hoyeon, they could interfere with the laws of the world."

"A lie from the Spirit," the Slaughter Predator laughed. "Didn't I tell you? You can't control every human within the flow of fate. What you need to command humans is incitement and lies. The Martial God is a pitiful fellow."

"…I… no, the reincarnator is the Slaughter Predator? Then what was the future Jeniela saw? The Slaughter Predator she saw was…?"

"It could be you, or it could be a completely different being comparable to the Slaughter Predator. Even I can't know the future she sees."

"Why didn't you appear back in Urmuri?" Lee Sungmin pressed. He had awakened as a Yokai back then, too.

At that question, the Slaughter Predator clicked his tongue. "The time wasn't right. At that point, The End hadn't even begun. It was too early for me to appear. Didn't I tell you?"

*See you next time.*

"What… are you planning to do?"

"I am the Slaughter Predator. I've been inside you since you reincarnated. Now that you've become a complete Yokai, I, as a part of The End, must do what I have to do. We'll see what's faster, the Demonic Spirit making Wiji Hoyeon an Absolute Being, or the entire world dying because of me."

"Hahahaha," the Slaughter Predator laughed and raised his hand. This was the moment. The moment for him to enter the vessel named Lee Sungmin, who was rampaging without reason.

However, a variable remained.

The connections and bonds Lee Sungmin had built throughout his life.

KWAJIJIJIK!

The darkness was torn apart. A massive hand shot out from beyond the rift and grabbed the Slaughter Predator by the scruff of his neck.

KWAANG!

Slamming him into the ground, the Void Monarch walked out from the torn blackness. A crimson light ignited in his eyes.

"I made a promise," he declared. "That if you became a Yokai you couldn't control, this Elder… I would stop the you who had become uncontrollable."

Even if his existence would be annihilated.

The Void Monarch did not voice those last words.
