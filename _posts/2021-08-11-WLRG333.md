With the Vampire Queen secluded in her mansion, Ju Won had gained the freedom to act. He had left the city half a year ago, declaring his intent to fight the human known as the Martial God, and had not been seen since. In his absence, Nero had clung to his master's final words.

*Just stay put.*

For the past ten years, the lord's mansion in the Central District of Cheped had belonged to Ju Won. For the last six months, it had been Nero's domain. Now, standing in the heart of that massive estate, Nero covered his face with both hands.

*Just stay put,* Ju Won had said. *Just stay put., but how could he possibly stay put in a situation like this? The city was under attack by only two people.

In truth, only the man with the spear was truly running rampant. It would have been a mercy not to know their identities, but the two invaders were far too famous for Nero to remain ignorant.

First was Yana, the Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri, the same woman who had torn the heart from the Monster Chieftain Red Demon, a former Black Star of the Predators. When Yana had challenged Red Ghost, the ruler of Urmuri at the time, their battle was said to have been so one-sided it could hardly be called a fight, even if Red Ghost was considered one of the weaker Black Stars.

And then there was the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin.

*It hasn't even been a week since his Sealing was broken in Germud. How in the world did he get from there all the way here?*

Nero knew the Sealing had been broken because some of his own Lycanthrope subordinates had gone to Germud with Rao Xian to help. Now, the rumor that the Phantom Spear had defeated the Death Lord, Vollander, had spread throughout all of Eria.

A deep sigh escaped Nero's lips.

Even as one of the most powerful Lycanthropes and Ju Won's trusted subordinate, his own strength was nothing compared to the Death Lord. To make matters worse, Yana was moving with Lee Sungmin, clearly aiding him. Gathering every Lycanthrope in Cheped would still make stopping those two an impossible task.

Lycanthropes were a belligerent race. That much was true. As humans who were closer to beasts, they possessed a violent and combative nature. That did not, however, apply to every situation. Nero, in particular, was more rational and calculating than he was violent, which was precisely why he had become Ju Won's trusted subordinate.

*Do nothing…*

Nero stood up. Lycanthropes were gathered throughout his mansion, and if he gave the order, they would rush out without hesitation to fight Lee Sungmin and Yana to the death.

He had no intention of giving such an order. He recalled Ju Won's command to do nothing, to ensure the city did not change while he was away. For half a year, Nero had managed the city from the shadows, following those orders to the letter. If they confronted Lee Sungmin and Yana, the Lycanthropes of this city would be annihilated. That could not be allowed to happen.

Sighing, Nero gave an order to the Lycanthropes awaiting his command.

"Prepare to greet our guests."

At his words, the Lycanthropes let out guttural growls and began their beast transformations. Nero watched them for a moment before covering his face with his hands again.

*Ah, they won't understand if I say it like that.*

He had meant it literally, but his kin had taken it in a completely different way.

"No… just stay as you are," he clarified. "I really mean to welcome them as guests."

Though dissatisfaction was plain on their faces, the Lycanthropes did not disobey. Among their kind, the chain of command was absolute. There were exceptions, of course, such as when Ju Won had killed their previous leader, Howon, but Nero had not become Ju Won's subordinate simply because he was rational. He possessed the strength to back up his position, which was why Ju Won had entrusted the city to him.

The Lycanthropes disappeared from sight.

{sep}

When the attacks ceased, Lee Sungmin glanced back at Yana.

"It could be a trap," she said.

"I suppose so," he agreed with a nod.

However, even knowing it was a trap, he could not stop. Baek Sogo was too important.

He knew his destination was the lord's residence in the Central District. In the past, such a journey would have required a carriage, but not now. Lee Sungmin launched himself forward, his movement arts blurring his form as he dashed toward the mansion in a single, unbroken rush. Yana followed close behind, her nine tails flowing in her wake.

The gates to the lord's residence stood wide open. *Isn't this a bit too obvious for a trap?* Lee Sungmin thought, his hand tightening on the spear strapped to his back.

"I have no intention of fighting."

The voice came from the mansion's hall, just past the gate. Lycanthropes stood in a circle, lining the entire room. Lee Sungmin stood blankly, his gaze fixed on the one who had spoken, a Lycanthrope standing on the opposite side of the hall.

The Lycanthrope gave off an impression entirely different from any Lee Sungmin had encountered before. He was not burly, nor was he dressed in wild furs. Instead, he wore a crisp, black tuxedo with few wrinkles, his long brown hair neatly tied back. Slender eyes peered from behind half-rimmed glasses as he offered a polite, detached smile and bowed his head slightly.

"This is the first time we've met in person. My name is Nero. I'm in charge of this city while Ju Won is away."

"...No intention of fighting?" Lee Sungmin asked, his brow furrowing. "I don't understand. I attacked your city without warning. I killed quite a few of your kind on my way here."

"That is a regrettable matter," Nero said with what sounded like genuine sincerity. He brought his hands together before his chest as if mourning the dead. "So, I propose we end it here. I don't wish to see any more regrettable deaths."

"End it?"

"You must be after something," Nero said with conviction. "Isn't that why you're causing all this trouble? Surely, the Phantom Spear… you're not doing this because you want to challenge the power of the Predators head-on, are you?"

It was a question that seemed to be testing him.

"If that is the case, I would recommend you change your mind now. The Lycanthropes of this city cannot stop you, but that doesn't mean the Predators as a whole cannot stop you."

Was this a threat?

"I'm looking for someone," Lee Sungmin said, staring intently at Nero. "A year ago. The Silent Flash… Baek Sogo must have come to this city. Do you remember?"

"...Silent Flash Baek Sogo?"

Nero's mind raced as he sifted through his memories from a year ago. *Silent Flash… Baek Sogo. Ah.* His eyes widened in recognition.

"Did you come here to find her?"

"......"

*What should he say about this?*

Nero hesitated for a moment, but Lee Sungmin did not miss his reaction. The anxiety deep in his chest swelled into a chilling, disgusting feeling. He ground his teeth.

This time, Yana could not stop him. The crackle of electricity came too late. Before she could even react, Lee Sungmin had already lunged, his hand shooting toward Nero's throat.

The other Lycanthropes could not follow his speed, but Nero was different. Startled, he threw his upper body back and scrambled to retreat.

"......"

Lee Sungmin's hand, which had been aimed at Nero's throat, grasped only empty air.

*He dodged?*

A thunderclap rumbled from within Lee Sungmin's body as his inner arts and Demonic Energy surged.

CRACKLE!

The electricity coursing through his vital energy channels amplified his speed even further.

"W-wait…" Nero pleaded, quickly raising both hands.

BAM! BAM! BAM! BAM!

A flurry of blows exploded in an instant, sending Nero staggering backward. The expressions of the surrounding Lycanthropes immediately soured. Nero had ordered them to stay put, but they could not simply watch their temporary leader be attacked.

As they let out bestial roars and prepared to charge, Yana moved.

WHOOSH!

Her nine tails shot out, wrapping around the entire hall like a silken cage.

"I wouldn't move," Yana warned, her voice deceptively soft.

Beyond the tails, the Lycanthropes glared at her with menacing eyes. Meanwhile, Nero was groaning as he rolled on the floor.

Lee Sungmin looked down at him and spat, "What kind of trick is this?"

"W-what are you talking about?"

"You're letting me hit you on purpose. Are you trying to deceive me?"

"Well… didn't I say I didn't want to fight you…" Nero protested as if he were being wronged.

Lee Sungmin found his attitude even more incomprehensible. Nero had intentionally taken the hits, yet he had dodged or blocked every fatal blow. He had only allowed the acceptable attacks to land, feigning weakness as he writhed on the ground.

"...What happened to Silent Flash Baek Sogo?" Lee Sungmin demanded, looking down at the prone Lycanthrope.

Nero still hesitated.

"Speak…!" Lee Sungmin's face contorted.

"A-a year ago!" Nero shouted quickly. "When Ju Won was in this city. Silent Flash Baek Sogo attacked this mansion."

It was an event that had remained unknown. Unlike Lee Sungmin and Yana, who had openly carved a path from the city gates, Baek Sogo had infiltrated Cheped in complete secrecy. She had slipped past the watchful eyes of the Lycanthrope guards, crossed the city, and entered the lord's residence in one swift move.

"She was after Ju Won's life."

She had made it all the way to Ju Won's room. Uncharacteristically, she had chosen assassination. Baek Sogo understood she had no chance of winning a head-on battle against a monster like Ju Won. When he entered the room, he failed to notice her hidden presence, and her hand pierced through his chest.

If Ju Won had been human, the fight would have ended there.

However, Ju Won did not die, even with his heart torn out. When Baek Sogo saw him instantly regenerate the wound, she must have sensed her defeat, yet she did not back down, for she was determined to kill him there, no matter the cost.

"...And… then?"

"Silent Flash Baek Sogo was defeated," Nero said, cautiously watching Lee Sungmin's expression. "Normally, he would have killed her, but Ju Won… took an interest in her. That created an opening. At the decisive moment, Baek Sogo escaped from his grasp. We offered to chase her, but Ju Won said there was no need."

No need.

"...Why…?"

"Poison."

A memory flashed through Lee Sungmin's mind. The Black Dragon Knight had carried a wound on his side that not even an Elixir could heal. It had happened when Kim Jonghyun committed his first massacre in the northern forests. The Black Dragon Knight had gone there to stop him, and Ju Won had been there too. Lee Sungmin had sent Ju Won after the knight, forcing them to fight. The Black Dragon Knight had escaped, but not before being poisoned. Not even the tough body of a Dragon could completely cure it.

Poison. That same poison had been used on Baek Sogo.

"He said that she would die without any need… to chase her. That's what Ju Won said."

*Please, please.*

Lee Sungmin's body trembled.

*So. Baek Sogo was dead? Because of Ju Won's poison?* A year had passed since that fight. If a year had passed…

"No," Lee Sungmin muttered, his voice trembling. "It can't be."

It absolutely could not be.

"Where… where did she go?"

Baek Sogo could not be dead.

"W-we don't know. Because Ju Won said there was no need to chase her…"

"Tell… tell me," Lee Sungmin pleaded. "Ju Won's poison. What do I have to do to cure it?"

"I don't know about that either…" Nero said, watching him cautiously.

*What should I do?*

Lee Sungmin clutched his head with both hands. *What, what do I do?* First, he had to find Baek Sogo. What if she was dead? No, she couldn't be. She must not be. Right, finding her came first. So… how?

His eyes fell on the bracelet on his wrist.

He hurriedly summoned the Fairy Horse. As Lee Sungmin climbed onto its back, Yana followed and got on behind him.

"To the Fairy Forest."

The Fairy Horse leaped through space.
