The Lich's words echoed in his mind. *Value as a sacrifice. Causality.* It was all a confusing tangle, and the lingering memory of fear still sent tremors through Lee Sungmin's body.

He had almost died. That was an undeniable fact, and yet, he hadn't. He could not fathom why he had been spared, why that unknown terror had not accepted him as a sacrifice.

*There was someone else besides me.*

Lee Sungmin cautiously approached the closed door, recalling the Lich's claim that he had met another being with a twisted causality. He didn't know who it was, but they clearly possessed a strength that overwhelmed the Lich, a monster who had toyed with Bern, Zeon, and the other mercenaries as if they were children.

Who could it be? He burned with the desire to ask the Lich, but he couldn't simply go looking for him. In truth, there were many other things he wanted to ask.

Right now, he was wearing the Magic Armor.

Devnak. The artifact that could turn a Third-Rate mercenary into a peak master. He had obtained it just as he had hoped, but it had no effect on him. Even the Lich had been puzzled. Why did he feel no change at all after putting it on?

KUUUUUNG!

The moment Lee Sungmin opened the door and stepped outside, a massive roar from the underground shook the entire Dungeon. He let out a startled cry and collapsed, looking around in a panic as the roaring erupted from the floor again and again without pause.

Beings of immense power were clashing. Was the Lich fighting? With whom? What had happened to the other mercenaries? What about Zeon? Questions bubbled up, but he forced them down.

He had to escape.

He didn't know where the Lich had gone or why he had left the room, leaving him behind. The reason didn't matter. What mattered now was escaping this place and surviving. With his current strength, he stood no chance if he faced the Lich again. A bitter taste filled his mouth.

After finishing his training on Mount Mushii, Lee Sungmin had become arrogant. He had obtained a strength he had never reached in his past life, becoming a master of the peak realm and an S-Rank Mercenary. He had gained enough power to be treated with respect anywhere in Eria, and he had grown complacent because of it.

Now he knew helplessness. The strength he had obtained amounted to nothing. Against a true monster, he could do nothing at all. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip. He was lucky. He had survived simply because he was lucky.

*Was that really it?*

Recalling the words that there were no coincidences, he started moving again. Regret, self-torment, and self-loathing could wait. It was better to indulge in those feelings after he had escaped the Dungeon, not now.

At some point, the rumbling from deep below abruptly stopped. The clash between the powerful beings had ceased. *What on earth happened?* Lee Sungmin pushed the question aside and kept walking, until a voice suddenly entered his mind.

[Are you a mercenary?]

He had been moving to find an exit when someone spoke directly inside his head. It wasn't the Lich's voice. Lee Sungmin flinched in surprise and froze on the spot.

[Ah… don't be alarmed. I'm not your enemy. The reason I'm speaking to you now is because… I have something to ask of you.]

The voice carried a deep, profound fatigue. Lee Sungmin looked around with a bewildered expression.

[My name is Lloyd. I am the master of the Golden Magic Tower. …Do you know of me?]

He did. Lloyd, the master of the Golden Magic Tower, was one of the archmages who had made a name for himself even in Lee Sungmin's past life. Scarlet, the founder of the Reshir School, would later become the master of the Red Magic Tower, but even before her rise, Lloyd was one of the most renowned archmages in Eria.

However, Lloyd had died. To be precise, he had gone missing. Since he had been missing for nearly ten years, he was considered effectively dead. Come to think of it, the time when the renowned archmage Lloyd went missing was right around now.

[I have a favor to ask. It's… difficult for me to move right now… Could you help me?]

"…Are you really Lloyd?" Lee Sungmin stammered. He wasn't sure if his question could be heard, but the voice replied.

[I swear on my Mana. I am indeed Lloyd. Lloyd, the master of the Golden Magic Tower, and… I will never harm you.]

Fortunately, Lloyd had heard him. For a mage, an oath on their Mana was absolute. Feeling a little relieved, Lee Sungmin asked another question.

"What happened to the Black Mage… the Lich?"

[The Lich… you must mean Preskan? He's not here. He fled.]

Lloyd's answer confirmed it. The source of the underground explosions had been a fight between him and the Lich.

"How do you want me to help?"

[First, come to me. There should be a path if you look for it. It's… hard to speak any more. My magic power is on the verge of depletion…]

The strength faded from Lloyd's voice. As Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment, Lloyd added one last thing.

[If you help me, I will reward you for it.]

Lee Sungmin let out a sigh. Since the Lich had fled, there was no one left who could threaten him, especially since Lloyd had sworn an oath on his Mana. In the end, he turned back the way he came and searched for the path leading underground.

He found a firmly shut iron door with no lock. He opened it to reveal a staircase descending into the earth. As he went down, breathing in the damp, musty air, he arrived in a vast cavern.

The cavern was a wreck. The floor was cracked like a dry rice paddy in a drought, and large marks of destruction, as if bombs had gone off, were scattered everywhere. Lee Sungmin's face stiffened as he took in the scene.

He saw Zeon's body.

Next to a shriveled corpse that looked like a mummy, Zeon lay with a pale face, a gaping wound torn through his chest. The sheer amount of blood that had pooled around him confirmed his death. Lee Sungmin had never imagined he would see Zeon's corpse here.

In his past life, Zeon's death had not occurred. Zeon had been alive right up until Lee Sungmin, as a C-Rank Mercenary, discovered this Dungeon, yet in this life, Zeon was dead. The corpse of the man who had long since surpassed the wall of the peak realm stirred a complex mix of emotions within him. Lee Sungmin had been wary of Zeon, having heard words of concern from Rudd and having seen Zeon's own attitude toward him.

"Over here."

Before Lee Sungmin could approach Zeon, Lloyd's voice called out. Startled, he turned in the direction of the sound.

"I am… fortunate."

Lloyd's face was drenched in blood. He had somehow managed to survive, but he had not escaped a fatal wound. Lee Sungmin quickly used his movement arts to close the distance.

"I never thought there would be a survivor," Lloyd said.

"…What happened here?" Lee Sungmin asked, leaning down toward him.

Lloyd let out a dry cough, and blood flecked his lips. "It was nothing much. …I tried to kill Preskan… but Preskan's Chimera was much stronger than I thought. It was only because Preskan wasn't in perfect condition that I was able to fight him to a standstill and drive him away. Thanks to that, I was able to save my own life as well."

The answers to his questions were laid bare. Why the Lich had fled the Dungeon without the Magic Armor. Why Lloyd, the master of the Golden Magic Tower, had gone missing. This was likely what had happened in his past life as well. Lloyd had come here and fought the Lich. The Lich had probably won but suffered a fatal wound, forcing him to flee, and Lloyd, having received no aid, had died here.

"…What is it you want to ask of me?"

"Take me and escape from this place. I… my magic power is depleted, so it's difficult to move. I was only able to send you that telepathic message by scraping together the last of my magic power," Lloyd said with a bitter smile. "And… one more thing. That heart floating up there. Please destroy it."

At those words, Lee Sungmin raised his head. Just as Lloyd had said, a heart was floating high above near the ceiling.

"I was originally going to do it myself, but… as I said, my magic power is depleted right now, and to come back later to destroy it… there's a chance Preskan might return. I'd feel more at ease if we destroy the heart right now."

"…What in the world is that?"

"I don't know either," Lloyd answered with a sigh. He had fought Preskan and the unidentified Chimera girl, and though he had managed to drive them away, he had no idea what the heart was. "But it is dangerous. Of that, I am certain."

The Chimera girl had been strong. No, it was more than that. The power she used was incredibly strange and ominous. The two of them, having suffered fatal wounds, had fled without taking the heart, but there was no telling when they might return for it.

"…I understand."

Since it seemed difficult for Lloyd to speak any more, Lee Sungmin nodded. He didn't know what kind of variables saving Lloyd, who was supposed to die here, would create in the future. Still, there seemed to be no harm in forming a connection with an archmage. He gripped his spear tightly and looked up.

A minor problem arose.

Lee Sungmin couldn't fly, nor had he reached the stage where he could project enhanced qi. For the current him, there were few ways to attack the heart floating so high in the air. After a moment of hesitation, he hurled his spear.

SHWIIIIK!

The spear tore through the air, flying straight toward the heart, but despite its powerful momentum, it failed to pierce its target. Even though he had thrown it with his inner arts fully infused, its speed decreased drastically the moment it neared the heart, and it fell to the ground without even making contact.

"It won't be that easy," Lloyd muttered, his voice as faint as a dying flame. Even the magic he had cast had done nothing to the heart.

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue and picked up his fallen spear. Throwing it wasn't enough. He estimated the distance to the floating heart and lowered his knees.

TAAK!

His body shot upward, launching him to the height of the heart. While airborne, he held his breath and poured his inner arts into the spear held in both hands.

Then he thrust it forward. At that moment, the magic power enveloping the heart swelled, functioning as a shield against external attacks. The sound of a throbbing beat grew louder as the magic pushed back against his spear. Feeling the resistance in his hands, Lee Sungmin bit his lip and put more strength into his thrust.

"……"

The sound of the heart beating echoed in his ears, and his own pounding heart matched its rhythm. The spear continued its slow advance until, at some point, the resistance in his hands vanished.

The spearhead touched the heart. It pierced through.

Or did it?

PWAK!

The heart vanished. Lee Sungmin's spear pierced empty air. His eyes widened, unable to comprehend what had just happened.

His own heart stopped for a moment. His breath caught in his throat, and his mouth fell open with a gasp. Staggering wildly in mid-air, he unknowingly let go of his spear. It fell to the ground, and his body plummeted along with it.

Just before he hit the ground, Lee Sungmin's heart started beating again. *Thump, thump.* He could hear the sound of it, but he felt a strange dissonance. He quickly righted himself and managed to land, but the odd feeling from his own heartbeat didn't disappear. With a bewildered expression, he placed a hand on his left chest.

[You have obtained the 'Black Heart'.]

A voice echoed in his mind. It was the same voice he heard when he learned a skill.
