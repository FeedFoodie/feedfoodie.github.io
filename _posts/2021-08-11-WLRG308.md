He was not satisfied with his own martial prowess, but to everyone else, it was nothing short of divine. Lee Sungmin pressed forward without pause, certain that Kim Jonghyun was watching this entire situation. If the necromancer realized the zombies were not enough, he would send the Death Knights.

Or he would use something else he had prepared. Kim Jonghyun had already spent several days in this city, which would have been more than enough time to finish considerable preparations.

Indeed, Kim Jonghyun was watching everything. Just as Abel and Lee Sungmin had wanted to confirm Teresa's abilities, he too was using the zombies to test her power. Even Teresa, who should have had her spiritual connection to her god severed like all clerics summoned to Eria, wielded a power that could only be described as 'holy power'. The absurd scale of that power was beyond anything Kim Jonghyun had imagined.

*Should I have split them up from the start?*

He had not deployed the barrier he used in the North Forest because he lacked the magic power to spare for it. If he had, this sort of war game would not be happening. Was it too late to think about it now? Kim Jonghyun chuckled and shook his head.

*I don't really know much about tactics.*

He looked down at the crystal ball and stroked his chin, wondering if it would have helped to have enjoyed games like chess or janggi. Unfortunately, Kim Jonghyun had never been interested in those kinds of games in his entire life.

He did, however, know one thing.

*In terms of numbers, I have a much greater advantage.*

He was superior in various other conditions as well. Among his enemies, only the Church's Holy Knights and Priests could be considered troublesome. The wandering crowd, despite their numbers and high death qi, were no different from zombies. There was no need to send out the Death Knights yet. He would watch a little longer. He formed a beastfolk and focused his mind.

KWAAANG!

The city's buildings did not just begin to collapse, they imploded. The resulting debris defied gravity, floating upward to gather at a single point. Abel's expression turned to one of surprise as he saw the dark light floating in the center of the swirling wreckage.

"A Golem!" his shout was laced with admiration.

A Golem was born on the spot. While a Stone Golem made from building debris belonged to the lowest class alongside Mud Golems, that evaluation was based on a single entity. Here, dozens of buildings collapsed simultaneously, and dozens of Golems of comparable size rose to their feet, and it was not just Golems. At Kim Jonghyun's call, the other Undead that had been held in the rear advanced. They were Dullahans and Banshees, Undead of a higher class than zombies.

Abel and Frau had not yet stepped forward. Abel was forming a beastfolk, his mind concentrated as he searched for Kim Jonghyun's location and the center of the magic ritual he was trying to perform. Finding a single person in the large city of Germud was by no means an easy task, and with the city under Kim Jonghyun's control, casting a search magic within it should have been impossible.

However, it was not impossible for Abel. As the Mages Guild Master, he possessed enough skill to call himself the pinnacle of mages. His lips twitched as he continuously changed the shape of the beastfolk. He could not use Silent Casting magic like his brother, Cain, nor had he created his own Soul Extinction magic like Arbes. He was a purist, faithful to the fundamentals of magic. Rather than creating new magic for himself, he had dedicated hundreds of years to mastering existing magic beyond its limits. It was for this reason that a search spell, cast under these impossible conditions, became possible. Because it was Abel who cast it.

Lee Sungmin's role was to buy time until Abel could pinpoint Kim Jonghyun's location. He also had to exhaust the forces of the miscellaneous Undead, including the numerous zombies, to draw out the Death Knights. He did not expect to draw out Vollander himself, but luring even a few of the Death Knights would make it easier to target Kim Jonghyun.

Dozens of Golems began to advance, stomping on nearby buildings with their feet and smashing them by swinging their arms. The fragments did not fall but floated up, further swelling the Golems' bodies. A lance charge would be useless against a Golem that had made several entire buildings its body. Such colossal Golems were best dealt with by high-powered magic attacks.

Lee Sungmin stepped forward, passing by Theos, who was pulling his horse back.

[Do you know how to fight a Golem?] the Void Monarch asked.

Lee Sungmin shook his head.

[You have to destroy the core inside… but for you, it'd be easier to just pulverize the whole thing.]

KWARURURU!

A violet current sparked and writhed, wrapping his entire spear in immense Demonic Energy and inner arts. He launched himself from the ground without hesitation, becoming a long bolt of violet lightning that shot through the air. The Golem, dragging its massive body forward, spread its hand wide to intercept him.

-KWAAANG!

The Golem's outstretched hand, meant to block him, simply turned to dust and vanished. Lee Sungmin's charge never faltered as he changed the spear's direction and pierced straight through the Golem's body.

KWARURURU!

Engulfed in the violet current, the Golem's body disintegrated into dust. Pausing in mid-air, Lee Sungmin dropped into the middle of the surging, shrieking Undead. He glanced back, took a few steps back, and saw the Undead he had not managed to block advancing.

[The Death Knights haven't even arrived yet,] Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission to Teresa.

She gulped and nodded her head, understanding what he meant. He was telling her not to use too much of her power so early. Theos, who had been mesmerized by Lee Sungmin's fighting, hastily grabbed his reins.

"Advance!"

At Theos's shout, the Holy Knights charged forward again. With eyes full of awe, Theos watched Lee Sungmin tear a Dullahan's body apart in a single strike. He moved without rest, feeling no need to dodge the axes of the Dullahans swinging wildly around him. Instead, Lee Sungmin's spear, thrusting out later than their axes, tore through and destroyed everything. A Golem's fist fell from above.

CRACKLE! Lee Sungmin's figure vanished with a spark of violet current. Standing amidst the Undead, he once again drew upon his inner arts and Demonic Energy together.

Three Step Fatality, Heaven's Roar.

A purple storm of fighting spirit erupted in an instant, sweeping through the area and completely annihilating the Dullahans and Banshees. All the large Stone Golems collapsed.

He took another step from there. The lingering purple lightning that had swept the area split and multiplied, growing larger and more numerous. Four-Step Madness White Thunder erupted, engulfing the collapsed Golems and turning them to dust.

"Hahaha!"

Kim Jonghyun clapped his hands and burst into laughter. Gripping the crystal ball with both hands, he watched everything happening at the castle gate entrance. He could not help but be genuinely impressed as Lee Sungmin annihilated dozens of Golems and hundreds of Undead in just a few minutes.

"Are you going to leave him be?" Vollander asked. "He's become incomparably stronger than when I met him on my ship. Pushing low-class Undead at him won't be enough to corner him."

"So it seems," Kim Jonghyun nodded, slowly turning his head to look at Vollander. "It seems you'll have to step in."

"Will that be alright?" A glint appeared in Vollander's eyes. "The Mages Guild Master is searching for your location."

"I know."

"Can you handle him?"

Kim Jonghyun chuckled at Vollander's needless question. He was not holding back because he was afraid of Abel. Vollander said no more and turned away.

Kim Jonghyun slowly raised his hand.

KURURURU...

A dark sphere rose from within the fountain, the same kind Arbes had created when he seized control of Urmuri and gathered its Demonic Energy and magic power into a single point. No, it was not exactly the same. Kim Jonghyun had surpassed Arbes, the great Black Mage, in the truest sense. Conscious of the rising sphere, he changed his beastfolk.

-KIIING.

Space began to vibrate as complex formulas appeared around the black sphere. This was the culmination of the magic Kim Jonghyun had been preparing in this land, a magic to complete the connection to the Great Demon World.

*The Mages Guild Master.*

His position was known, but his name and skills were not. Kim Jonghyun smiled with delight as he waited for this unknown opponent.

[Found him.]

Abel's closed eyes opened. At the Thought Transmission, Lee Sungmin stopped swinging his spear. He stood in the center of the ruins. Golems, Dullahans, Banshees. None remained. In that short time, hundreds, perhaps thousands of Undead had been killed by his hand. Thousands who might have once been human had died without a trace, even though they were Undead without proper intelligence, at best on the level of peak realm masters.

Abel had located Kim Jonghyun, but they still had not drawn out the Death Knights. Should they just clean up all the Undead here and then lead the Church and the crowd to strike Kim Jonghyun? What were the chances of him escaping?

[The zombies aren't being replenished,] the Void Monarch said.

Lee Sungmin felt it too. The endlessly surging army of Undead had stopped.

[It seems he's telling us to come further in.]

It was an obvious trap, but it was not as if they could retreat. The chain of command for the army gathered for this subjugation was not properly established. Scattering in this vast city, Kim Jonghyun's territory, was a suicidal act. In the end, the army had to gather as one, and they had no choice but to advance cautiously.

The army was not without casualties. Dozens from the Holy Knight Regiment had died, and the less capable crowd naturally suffered even greater losses. Hundreds of deaths. The corpses left by the Undead were in a gruesome state. Teresa gasped, covering her mouth with her hand.

"Your Holiness. Do not be shaken," the High Priests once again warned her.

Because there was a chance the corpses would rise again as Undead, they all had to be burned before moving on. Then, they advanced again.

Abel set the direction. Even though they had located Kim Jonghyun, they would not charge in recklessly. Kim Jonghyun's power was an unknown, and Vollander and the Death Knight Legion had yet to show themselves.

*Besides, we still have forces left on our side.*

There was no need to abandon them. If they could go together, he would take them.

"In the world of murim, just how strong are you?" Theos asked, approaching Lee Sungmin, who was walking with his spear over his shoulder. He did not know much about the world of murim. Though he was a master comparable to the Supreme Peak, Theos's martial prowess was not gained through martial arts.

"I'd be counted on one hand," Lee Sungmin answered honestly.

The Sword Immortal, who could not leave Wudang. The Martial God, who had lost an arm. The Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King, whose life or death were unknown. Those were the only masters who could be compared to the current Lee Sungmin. The Blade Supreme, renowned as the Mercenary King, had never met him, but it was clear his skills were inferior. Cheongmyeong, the Sword Immortal's disciple, was a master of the Transcendent Realm, but he too was a few steps below.

*Wiji Hoyeon.*

And Baek Sogo. Honestly, the skills of those two were an unknown.

"Indeed… I was in awe watching you fight. I thought if there was a god of martial arts in this world, he'd be just like you."

The Martial God.

"I'm not too fond of that title," Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile.

Theos tilted his head at those words but soon smiled warmly and bowed his head. "I'm glad I came here with you."

It was not just Theos. Most of the Holy Knights were looking at Lee Sungmin with respect. The mercenaries and the ronin were sending him much the same gazes, and Lee Sungmin felt a little burdened by them.

Just then, a tingle crawled across his skin. The fine hairs on his arms stood on end.

HWAAAAAK!

It was a presence that could not be seen, but it was more than enough to be felt. The smile vanished from Theos's face. The horses the Holy Knights were riding stopped advancing and trembled all over. The Priests' faces turned pale, and the faint-hearted among the martial artists collapsed on the spot. From not too far away, a dark fog was surging toward them.

[They're here,] the Void Monarch warned.

Lee Sungmin gripped the spear he had slung over his shoulder.

Vollander and the Death Knight Legion had arrived.
