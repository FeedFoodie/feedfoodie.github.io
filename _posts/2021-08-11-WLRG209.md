The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's words left Lee Sungmin's face utterly blank.
*She barks loudly for someone so weak.*
The meaning was unmistakable, yet the person being dismissed with such contempt was none other than Wiji Hoyeon.

A tremor entered Lee Sungmin's voice. "...Wiji Hoyeon is weak?"

"Hm?" The Chairman frowned, looking at him for a moment before a look of realization crossed his face. "Ah," he chuckled. "It seems you're misunderstanding something. The Fist Honored One's curse has been lifted. You killed him, didn't you?"

"...Yes."

"Just as I thought. Thanks to you, the curse was broken, and that wench, the Little Heavenly Demon, returned to her normal state."

"Then what in the world are you talking about?" Lee Sungmin demanded. "What do you mean, she barks loudly for someone so weak?"

"It means exactly what it sounds like," the Chairman replied, spinning on his heel. "Follow me."

He started walking, and Lee Sungmin followed, his eyes fixed on the lion's mane swaying around the man's shoulders.

"After the curse was lifted, that wench, for some reason full of confidence, challenged this one to a duel. We fought after she rested for three days. Are you curious about the result?"

"...I am."

"Can't you tell just by looking?" the Chairman said in a relaxed voice. "I already told you. That wench barked loudly for someone so weak. Call her the Little Heavenly Demon, call her a calamity, it doesn't matter. She couldn't even last a Ten Exchanges Match against me."

Lee Sungmin doubted what he had just heard. His steps came to a dead halt.

A Ten Exchanges Match. It meant the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, had not even been able to withstand ten of the Chairman's moves.

"...A lie."

"Does this one have any reason to lie?" The Chairman chuckled darkly. "If you really can't believe it, go ask the Little Heavenly Demon yourself. Though I doubt that prideful wench will honestly tell you how she vomited blood and collapsed after enduring just ten of my moves."

It was unbelievable. Wiji Hoyeon, the Little Heavenly Demon, was a martial artist powerful enough to be an absolute nemesis. Of course, he had met others stronger than her, monsters like the Sword Saint, Vollander of Predator, Jeniela, and Ju Won. Still, he refused to accept it. No matter how strong the Chairman was, the idea that Wiji Hoyeon couldn't last ten exchanges was unthinkable.

"...I can't believe it."

"I feel no need to convince you," the Chairman said, raising a hand. He caressed the empty air, and the space he touched began to distort. "This way."

"......"

The space split open, a technique reminiscent of the one Yana had shown him in Urmuri. The Chairman stepped through the rift, and Lee Sungmin hesitantly followed him inside.

They arrived in a forest that was completely different from the one he had been in just moments before. The trees were taller, and beautiful flowers bloomed in every direction. As Lee Sungmin took an unconscious breath, he flinched in surprise. The amount of natural energy mixed in the air was immense. He looked around in bewilderment.

"This is the true Legro Forest," the Chairman said with a smile. "It's not a place just anyone can enter. You doubt that the Little Heavenly Demon was defeated by me. Why? Is it because to you, she's an undefeated nemesis of a martial artist?"

"The Little Heavenly Demon... Wiji Hoyeon is strong. She's a calamity with more talent than anyone I've ever seen."

"That Little Heavenly Demon fought the Fist Honored One and ended in a draw, unable to subdue him, and she even got cursed," the Chairman said with a hearty laugh. "The 'calamity with more talent than anyone' you speak of is only at that level. Of course, she's young, so she can become much stronger in the future, but right now, that's all she amounts to."

He continued, "And that phrase, 'a calamity with more talent than anyone', is ridiculous. I admit her talent is outstanding. However, you shouldn't say such things so easily. After all, even this one is more talented than the Little Heavenly Demon."

"That's..."

The Chairman turned around. "Do you remember what I said last time?"

"What?"

"Didn't I say that if we met again and you didn't speak formally to me, I would spank your bottom?"

"What the—?"

Lee Sungmin started to retort, utterly dumbfounded, but the Chairman's figure vanished. His movement was so stealthy, so fast, that Lee Sungmin couldn't perceive it at all. Just as he tried to look around in bewilderment—

*WHAP!*

An excruciating pain tore through his backside, as if it were being ripped apart. Lee Sungmin's body flew into the air, soaring upwards before crashing back down to the ground. He had experienced all manner of pain in his life and was confident in his tolerance, but this was different. He hadn't even had time to raise his Protective Qi Aura, and the counter-force ki inside him failed to activate.

Groaning, Lee Sungmin clutched his throbbing, burning butt with both hands. It felt as if it had been seared by fire. He had never been hit there so hard in his entire life.

"Speak formally," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, standing with his hands behind his back. He looked down at Lee Sungmin, who was collapsed on the ground, and spoke in a stern voice. "This one is stronger than you and greater than you. So isn't it only natural that you speak formally?"

"You crazy...!"

"Your language is quite foul. Do you want another one?" The Chairman raised his right hand and waved it. Seeing this, Lee Sungmin flinched and snapped his mouth shut. He would rather be hit anywhere else, but he refused to be spanked on the bottom again.

"...No... sir."

"Good. You should have done so from the start," the Chairman said in a pleased voice.

Lee Sungmin pushed himself up, rubbing his stinging backside. Inside his head, the Void Monarch was laughing his head off.

*How is he so fast...?*

He bit his lower lip hard. He too had entered the Transcendent Realm, yet he couldn't perceive the Chairman's movements. Still touching his aching butt, Lee Sungmin followed behind the man, who had started walking again.

"When you killed the Fist Honored One, did Heaven Beyond Heaven interfere?"

"...I met the Sword Saint, sir."

"Seeing as you're alive, it seems the Sword Saint died at your hands."

"Yes, sir."

"I see. How was the Sword Saint?" the Chairman asked in an amused voice.

Lee Sungmin answered honestly. "He was strong, sir."

"But weaker than you, hence why he's dead. If the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One are dead... then only two of the Six Honored Ones remain."

Tiny points of light flew towards the Chairman. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he saw tiny Fairy Realms fluttering their wings within the light. He had heard stories about them, but this was the first time he had seen them in person.

"Coalition Leader, Coalition Leader. Is it another guest?" the Fairy Realms chattered as they settled on the Chairman's shoulder.

He ignored their voices and continued speaking to Lee Sungmin. "Congratulations on entering the world of the Transcendent Realm. Though, you've only just taken your first steps."

"First steps?"

"Yes. Just as there are gaps in power at the Supreme Peak, there are gaps within the Transcendent Realm. In my eyes, your skill is barely past the stage of taking your first steps. Still," he added, "it's better than the Little Heavenly Demon."

"...What do you mean by that, sir?"

"It means you are stronger than she is right now."

Lee Sungmin stopped walking again. He glared at the back of the man walking ahead of him. After a few more paces, the Chairman noticed he was no longer being followed and turned around.

"...Are you saying," Lee Sungmin began slowly, "that I'm stronger than Wiji Hoyeon?"

"Yes. If you're taking your first steps, she's still at the level of babbling. No... she's probably better than that. Should I say she's just started crawling? Or perhaps learning how to stand..."

"I can't believe it, sir."

"You're quite the pain in the ass, aren't you," the Chairman let out a hollow laugh. "I'm acknowledging your skill, so why are you being such a pain? The Little Heavenly Demon couldn't last a Ten Exchanges Match against me, but you could probably withstand ten of my moves. Maybe even fifteen. This one, who has fought her, is acknowledging you, so who the hell are you to complain?"

"Wiji Hoyeon is not weak, sir."

"She's not weak, but she's not strong either. You... are very strange," the Chairman mused. "Is it because the martial arts you use were learned from her? Or is it because the Little Heavenly Demon is your goal, your idol, and your ideal?"

He asked the question with a snicker, and Lee Sungmin's lips trembled. He wanted to refute it, to deny it, but he could not utter a single word of rebuttal.

Because it was true. Wiji Hoyeon was his goal, his idol, and his ideal. Most of the martial arts he knew had been taught to him directly by her. He had even learned how to wield a spear from her.

"Is that it?" the Chairman pressed. "You can't accept that the Little Heavenly Demon, your goal, your idol, and your ideal, is weaker than you? What a funny guy. Why did you make her your goal? Because you wanted to be like her? To surpass her? Are you disheartened that the object of your admiration is inferior to you? Do you feel empty now that your ideal has turned out to be not so great?"

"...That's..."

"Don't be mistaken, kid. Just because you're stronger than her now doesn't mean it will always be that way. As long as you don't struggle desperately, the Little Heavenly Demon will once again leave you far behind."

At those words, Lee Sungmin felt a wave of relief wash over him. The tension in his expression eased. *Yes, that's how it should be.* That was what he thought in that instant.

WHOOSH!

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman appeared right in front of his nose. Lee Sungmin recoiled in shock, and through the eyeholes of the mask, the Chairman's eyes narrowed. He opened his mouth, his voice growing cold.

"You were just relieved by my words. Relieved that the object of your admiration has become your idol again. Relieved that the Little Heavenly Demon, your ideal, has become your ideal again. Relieved that your goal has become distant once more."

"Is that... wrong, sir?"

"You weren't the least bit happy that you had surpassed your goal, that you could now look down on your idol and ideal. What is it you truly want to do? Why did you learn martial arts, and why did you reach the Transcendent Realm that all who practice martial arts yearn for?"

A fierce aura erupted from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The fairies hovering around his shoulders flinched and trembled.

"Do you want to be satisfied with simply following from behind? Are you content with your goal remaining just that, a goal, an idol, an ideal?"

"...No, sir," Lee Sungmin squeezed out. No. It was true that he had made Wiji Hoyeon his goal, but that wasn't the only reason he learned martial arts. He liked martial arts. He liked the spear. He wanted to see for himself how far he could go, what realm he could reach. "I was just... surprised, sir. The Wiji Hoyeon I know was always stronger than me."

"If you had intended to be satisfied and enjoy staying beneath her forever, I was planning to kill you right here and now." The fierce aura vanished as if it had never been there. "A disciple of this Demon Lord Yang Ilcheon must be the world's number one. If you lack talent, you must be vicious enough and work hard enough to devour that lack of talent. Even if it's not reality yet, your ambition must be to become the best, and you must never doubt that you will one day achieve it."

As he listened to those solemnly spoken words, Lee Sungmin realized something was strange.

"Wait."

The Chairman stopped. "What is it now?" he asked, his voice laced with annoyance.

"Disciple...? What do you mean by that, sir? Why are you saying that to me?"

"Ah," the Chairman laughed heartily. "I forgot to mention it."

"This one intends to take you as a disciple."

Lee Sungmin's jaw dropped.
