Three months passed, and it seemed Rudd's warnings had been nothing more than needless worry. Zeon never directly harmed or pressured Lee Sungmin, yet Lee Sungmin refused to let his guard down. There was nothing to be lost in caution.

In that time, he had become an S-Rank Mercenary at the Behenger Mercenary Guild and completed three requests through Erebrisa. A limit on the types of requests he could undertake prevented him from accepting any more. His finances, however, were in a much better state, so he paid Celgerus an additional sum. The original commission of twenty-seven million Eru was supplemented with a bonus, bringing the total to a handsome sixty million.

With the money that remained, he purchased miracle herbs from Erebrisa.

The herb he chose was the Peerless Pill. While it was difficult to compare it to the Minor or Great Cycle Pills Zeon had shown him, it was of a similar grade to the Holy Spirit Pill he had won in the Zenavis Coliseum. Such a prize cost tens of millions of Eru, and he ended up spending every last coin he had earned from his requests.

He had no regrets. For Lee Sungmin, completely mastering the martial arts he already knew was far more important than learning new ones, and inner qi were absolutely essential to that process.

However, Lee Sungmin was hitting a wall.

His qi center was brimming with power. The Peerless Pill had certainly enhanced his inner qi, but it was impossible for him to overcome the wall simply by possessing a large amount of energy. To forcibly shatter it with inner qi alone would require several times his current reserves, and there was no guarantee that even a Great Cycle Pill would be enough.

The wall.

He could feel its presence clearly, whether he was circulating the Violet Dawn Divine Art, executing the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, or performing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. It was too high to leap over and too solid to break through. On Mount Mushii, his training had yielded guaranteed, tangible growth, but that kind of boost was gone now that he had left. The only advantages he possessed were his Classless status and his Low-Grade Martial Skeleton. No matter how much effort he poured in, the level of his martial arts barely increased.

The Dancing Shadow Soul Break remained at the fifth stage. The Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear were stalled at the sixth. Three months had passed since he descended the mountain, yet he had made no progress. He had grown more accustomed to the techniques, but he had achieved nothing beyond that.

He needed Magic Armor.

If he could obtain it, he might be able to overcome the wall. He knew of a mercenary who had been a mere Third-Rate warrior, yet with Magic Armor, he had broken through to the peak realm. That was why Lee Sungmin craved it. If he, who was already an expert at the peak, were to acquire Magic Armor, he might even shatter the barrier to the Supreme Peak.

It was, at best, wishful thinking.

"This took longer than I thought."

Celgerus, whom he had not seen in two months, looked a bit haggard. He wore the same dirty apron and large gloves as last time. Lee Sungmin gave him a slight nod.

"It's fine. I wasn't in a hurry for the weapon."

"It's because you added that extra payment," Celgerus grumbled, bending down to lift a long spear that had been resting on the floor. "Thanks to that, it took longer than planned."

He held it up. "I didn't give it a name."

Celgerus said, "I'm not much good at naming things, you see, and I think it's a bit ridiculous to name weapons anyway. In the end, a weapon just needs to do its job, doesn't it?"

Lee Sungmin could hardly focus on his words. His gaze was completely captivated by the spear. It was more than just a weapon. No, it was the very definition of one, so perfect in its form that it could be called nothing else. To be precise, it was a spear.

It was about three meters long. Its straight shear shaft, forged from an unknown black metal, gleamed under the light. A long, lush tassel of red silk hung from the neck, and the butt of the spear, the Changjun, was sharp enough to be a weapon in its own right. What truly mesmerized Lee Sungmin was the keenly honed spearhead. Its double-edged blade was long enough not just for thrusting and striking, but for slashing as well. The overall dark color scheme only deepened his admiration.

It felt like a spear crafted purely for practicality, not for show. Though Lee Sungmin had not handled many weapons and had never held a truly fine one, the spear Celgerus had made was more than enough to enthrall him.

"I used Mithril as the main metal."

CLACK. Celgerus gripped the spear with both hands.

"Mana... or rather, ki. It's a metal that conducts it well. Its durability changes depending on how much ki you infuse, and it'll be less taxing to use sword ki or enhanced sword qi. I also enchanted it with self-repair magic, so as long as you channel ki into it, most minor damage will fix itself. It's a different story if it's completely broken, though. If that happens, bring it to me. I'll repair it for you."

"I'd like to try swinging it myself..."

"Of course you should," Celgerus nodded.

Nebul materialized beside him, took the spear from the blacksmith, and moved to Lee Sungmin's side.

"Here you are."

Lee Sungmin accepted the spear with both hands. The first thing he felt was its impossible lightness. Despite being nearly three meters long, it was incredibly light, yet it did not feel flimsy in the slightest. He gave it a test swing on the spot. The shear shaft felt perfect in his grip, and it cut through the air with a satisfying sound.

"Do you like it?" Celgerus asked.

After a few more swings, Lee Sungmin nodded slowly.

"......"

He had never held a finer weapon. He could not even imagine one better than this. Celgerus chuckled at his silent awe.

"I'm glad you like it. I made the spear to match the movements of the Spear Technique you demonstrated, so it should be comfortable to perform your martial arts. Well, you'll have to confirm that for yourself."

As a test, Lee Sungmin infused the spear with his inner qi. Violet ki immediately cloaked the spearhead, the energy channeling far more fluidly than with the cheap spear he had used before.

"......"

An impromptu thrust pierced the air. It was faster, perhaps due to the spear's lightness, yet it felt no less powerful.

"Thank you."

"No need for thanks," Celgerus replied with a shrug. "I was paid, so it's only natural."

Soon after, Celgerus vanished. Lee Sungmin looked down at the spear in his hands, a faint smile on his face. He didn't think he was good at naming things either and had no intention of giving it one.

"It seems Celgerus really took a liking to you," Nebul said with a smile. "That spear, I mean. The core is filled with Mithril and the exterior is coated in Black Luster Steel. Both are metals highly sensitive to Mana and ki, and they're extremely expensive. You paid him sixty million Eru, didn't you? Most of that was probably spent on the materials alone."

"Is that so?"

"Yes. You could say he essentially made it for you for free, charging only for the materials. For it to have such a high level of craftsmanship despite that... Celgerus isn't usually very fond of our clients."

Lee Sungmin nodded, listening to Nebul. This strange goodwill from others made him wary, but it was impossible to figure out the reason for it. So, while he remained cautious, he chose to accept it for the time being.

"Anyway, would you like to accept your next request?"

"No. Not today."

He planned on visiting the Mercenary Guild. Since he had been taking requests through Erebrisa and growing wary of Zeon, he had not paid much attention to the Behenger Mercenary Guild. It was about time he did. In a few months, the Black Mage's Dungeon would be discovered, and he needed to show his face at the guild to gather any information he could.

"Look what we have here, a rare celebrity."

As he entered the Mercenary Guild, Bern called out with a smile from his post by the bar. At Lee Sungmin's arrival, the boisterous mercenaries on the first floor fell silent. In just three months, he had become a local legend by completing a variety of requests. A Twin Head Troll had fallen to him, as had a Black Minotaur and a Lizard King, all monsters that surpassed the S-Rank.

"Did you come to pester me for a promotion to SS-Rank?" Bern asked as Lee Sungmin approached.

Lee Sungmin just shook his head with a bitter smile.

"Not today. I don't see Rudd around."

"He's out on a request, a bit far away. What brings you here?"

"I came to see if there were any noteworthy requests."

"There is," Bern replied, as if he had been waiting for him to ask. "A Dungeon has been discovered."

"...What?"

Lee Sungmin's expression froze in surprise. It was completely unexpected. He had memories of his past life, of being active as a mercenary in Behenger during this very period. Though the memories were hazy, they were certainly there, and he had never heard of a Dungeon being discovered at this time.

"You know that Ogre Chimera you caught three months ago?"

"...Yes," Lee Sungmin replied, his expression stiffening as he nodded.

"It seemed suspicious, so we searched the area. It took three months, but we finally found a lead. I said Dungeon, but it's not a naturally occurring one. It's an artificial Dungeon. That's right. It's a Black Mage's Dungeon."

The flow had changed.

Lee Sungmin swallowed dryly, staring at Bern's face. The Black Mage's Dungeon. In the original timeline, it should not have been discovered for another two or three months. In his past life, the Ogre Chimera had been found but never caught. This time, its capture had altered the course of events.

"Since it's a rare case, we're thinking of forming our own subjugation party centered around mercenaries from our Guild. We haven't set an exact date yet... but what do you think? Will you participate?"

"...Which mercenaries have applied?"

"Zeon. The entire Corona Mercenary Group isn't going, but Zeon, three S-Rank Mercenaries, and five A-Rank mercenaries from his group have decided to join."

This, too, was a change. In his past life, Zeon had not participated in the Dungeon subjugation. To be precise, he could not have. At that time, Zeon and the core members of his mercenary group had been away from Behenger on a different request.

Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip and nodded. "...I'll participate."

A variable had been introduced, but he could not give up on the Magic Armor.
