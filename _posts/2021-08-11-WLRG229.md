Pae Munhu lowered the letter he was holding and met Lee Sungmin's gaze. Lee Sungmin's own fist tightened and relaxed in a steady rhythm, a silent declaration that Pae Munhu understood without needing to read the letter's contents. He could sense that Lee Sungmin intended to follow its instructions precisely.

"…What kind of prank is this?"

"I've got no way of knowing what my eccentric Master was thinking when he wrote that," Lee Sungmin grumbled, taking a large step forward.

At the movement, Pae Munhu's eyebrow twitched, and he raised a hand. The warriors of the Pae Clan who had accompanied him immediately stepped forward, forming a protective line.

"The Coalition Leader was always mischievous," Pae Munhu said, his voice strained. "I'm sure this is just a simple joke…"

"No." Lee Sungmin shook his head, his expression unyielding. "I don't think it's a joke. He said to beat you to death… so that's what I have to do. I don't know why, though."

"Are you serious?" Pae Munhu let out a hollow laugh at Lee Sungmin's quiet declaration. It wasn't as if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself had come. The Phantom Spear's infamy was considerable, but given when he had made his name, the young man could be in his mid-to-late twenties at most.

Of course, age and skill were not always proportional, but Pae Munhu found himself looking down on Lee Sungmin. It was a natural arrogance, born from his long reign as the second-in-command of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

"I know the Coalition Leader's martial arts theory is number one in the world, but it's not like the Coalition Leader himself came…"

Pae Munhu's sneer died on his lips.

CRACKLE.

A flash of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder tore through the space between them, and Lee Sungmin appeared directly before him. Pae Munhu's eyes widened, his mind struggling to comprehend the instantaneous movement. The shock of the absurd speed came a moment after his confusion, and that brief hesitation froze him in place.

A fist slammed into his jaw, snapping his head violently to the side. His face contorted in a mask of pain as broken front teeth and a spray of blood flew from his mouth. Pae Munhu's body was launched through the air, tumbling across the ground in a heap. Even for a master at the zenith of the Supreme Peak, the speed of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder Gale Thunder, infused with insights from the Transcendent Realm, was simply impossible to counter.

"P-Patriarch?!" the Pae Clan warriors screamed.

Lee Sungmin ignored their cries, his form blurring with Gale Thunder as he closed the distance to the fallen Pae Munhu. The man had been struck so suddenly that he hadn't even had time to raise his Protective Qi Aura, and now he struggled to regain his senses. In truth, it would have made no difference. Lee Sungmin kicked Pae Munhu's groaning body.

THWACK!

The sound echoed like a foot striking a heavy leather ball, and Pae Munhu's body jolted from the impact. *Beat him to death in front of everyone.* Lee Sungmin was carrying out the letter's instructions to the word. With a blank expression, he methodically stomped on Pae Munhu's body. Sprawled on the ground, Pae Munhu screamed and writhed, hastily trying to circulate his inner arts to resist. The Protective Qi Aura he managed to conjure shattered uselessly under the careless kicks.

"Stop!"

The Pae Clan warriors rushed forward, but they found themselves unable to swing their weapons. A wave of Dragon's Pressure erupted from Lee Sungmin, freezing them in their tracks. After confirming they were immobilized, he raised his foot again and brought it down hard on Pae Munhu's body.

The other warriors of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance watched the brutal display with dumbfounded expressions. A profound cognitive dissonance set in. The man being stomped like a stray dog was none other than the second-in-command of their alliance, Blood Lord Pae Munhu. He was a giant from the most Noble Clan of the Three Demonic Clans, a man who had reigned at the pinnacle of this city while the Chairman was absent.

And now, such a giant was being stomped to death. *This should be enough.* Lee Sungmin could have killed him with that first blow, but he had held back, striking him lightly instead. He had to admit, he hadn't liked Pae Munhu's arrogant attitude either.

"P-please… stop…" Pae Munhu pleaded in a desperate, broken voice, his face swollen and bleeding profusely.

Lee Sungmin nodded and lifted his foot one last time.

CRUNCH!

The descending foot shook the ground. His head crushed, Pae Munhu went limp without so much as a final scream.

"P-Patriarch…" the Pae Clan warriors muttered, their faces pale. Not a single one of them could have imagined that today, in this place, the head of their clan would die in such a humiliating manner. A cold horror washed over them. How many people in the world could beat Blood Lord Pae Munhu to death so easily?

"A mad dog needs to be beaten to death."

Before Lee Sungmin could speak, a voice laced with laughter drifted from nearby. He recognized it instantly.

"…Weren't you in the forest?" Lee Sungmin asked, turning his head to look up at the roof of a nearby building.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stood there with his hands clasped behind his back, smiling from behind his mask at Lee Sungmin's upward gaze.

CRACKLE.

Wreathed in black electricity, the Chairman appeared beside Lee Sungmin in an instant. He glanced down at Pae Munhu's corpse sprawled on the ground.

"He's an example," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stated. At his words, the shoulders of the assembled warriors flinched.

Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the man beside him. It wasn't a clone. This was the real Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He had asked before leaving, and the Chairman had insisted he had no intention of leaving the forest. Why would he suddenly come here?

"Blood Lord Pae Munhu was conspiring with the Murim Alliance," the Chairman announced, his voice carrying over the silent crowd. "He must have leeched quite a bit from the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance while I was away. It seems that wasn't enough to satisfy him."

"That's… impossible. The Patriarch with the Murim Alliance…?" one of the Pae Clan warriors stammered.

"Do I have any reason to lie?" the Chairman asked with a smirk. The Pae Clan warriors were left speechless. These were the words of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon himself. To the martial artists of the unorthodox sects, even those of the Pae Clan, he was a figure worshipped like a god.

[Osla told me.]

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's Thought Transmission suddenly entered Lee Sungmin's mind. *So that was why he told me to beat the man to death.* As Lee Sungmin inwardly accepted the reason, the Chairman continued.

[She said that I no longer need to be in the forest.]

[Is that why you left?]

[I didn't want to, but she told me to return to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, so I had no choice but to agree. I respect her words.]

[So you were kicked out, weren't you?]

[It's not that there was no reason. I was simply asked to leave for a good one.]

[You would've been kicked out even if you tried to stay, right?]

[Do you want your buttocks spanked for the first time in a while?] the Chairman shot back, glaring from behind his mask. Perhaps because others were watching, he made no move to carry out his threat.

"It's been a long time since I've returned," he muttered, looking around. Despite his words, there wasn't the slightest hint of pleasure in his tone. "Follow me."

The Chairman turned on his heel, his tone and attitude making it blatantly obvious that he had been forced to come somewhere he didn't want to be. Lee Sungmin, knowing this was just how his master was, didn't react much, but the assembled warriors couldn't help but look bewildered.

"Aren't you being too cold?" Lee Sungmin asked as he followed.

"What does it matter?" the Chairman replied indifferently. "In the first place, I have no lingering attachment to this organization. I used to be quite fond of it, but not anymore."

"Then why don't you just step down as Coalition Leader?"

"Even if I have no attachment, I must bear some responsibility as long as I hold the title. If I were to step down, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance would be trampled by the Murim Alliance."

"You care about things like that?"

"You seem to see me as some cold-blooded man. Of course, I care," the Chairman grumbled. In reality, even while living in seclusion, he had ruled the alliance with Osla's help, implanting terminals in the heads of each branch leader to periodically convey his will.

[He says he has no lingering attachment, but that doesn't seem entirely true. He's quite a dishonest fellow,] Void Monarch remarked with a chuckle.

Lee Sungmin agreed. If his master truly had no attachment, he wouldn't have gone through such trouble, nor would he have ordered Pae Munhu's death for conspiring with their rivals.

"Master, why did the Fairy Queen suddenly send you out?"

"She said the time has come," the Chairman muttered, glancing over at Lee Sungmin. "I don't know exactly what time she was talking about. Osla… she told me that the time to fulfill the promise is drawing near."

"…Is that why she sent you out of the forest?"

The Chairman didn't answer. He pushed open a closed door, revealing a spacious office that was spotless, without a speck of dust, even though it must have been unused for a long time. He plopped down on the floor and looked up at Lee Sungmin.

"Well, it's not a bad thing. I wasn't pleased about leaving the forest, but it's also true that things have become more convenient now that I'm back."

"What do you mean?"

"By beating up that Pae fellow, you've proven your martial prowess. The rumor that the Phantom Spear has become my successor will spread far and wide. Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Murim Alliance will surely start to make their moves," the Chairman muttered, stroking the mask on his face. "I don't know what kind of moves those bastards will make, but Heaven Beyond Heaven won't stay still. It's not just me. You're also a target they're not fond of."

"I suppose so."

"For the time being, stay here in the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. We need to see what moves they'll make."

"How long do I have to stay?"

"It's not an order," the Chairman said in a listless tone. "What you do is your own choice. I have no intention of restricting you. By becoming my successor, you've gained me as your backer. However, even with that martial arts theory, you'll likely be exposed to more danger if you leave the alliance."

Lee Sungmin nodded. It wasn't as if he had anything urgent to do. Wiji Hoyeon had also said she would come here when she was finished with her business, so he had no complaints about staying until then. He would have to visit the Dwarf Village someday, but it wasn't an immediate priority. There was no telling when Wiji Hoyeon would return, but Lee Sungmin decided to wait without growing impatient.

Even at the headquarters of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, his daily routine didn't change. Occasionally, the Chairman would visit to check on his martial arts, and he would listen to Void Monarch chattering in his head. No one else came to speak with him.

Lee Sungmin paid close attention to the rumors from the outside world. It was just as the Chairman had predicted. News that the Phantom Spear had become the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was spreading like wildfire. The fact that the Chairman himself, who hadn't been seen for decades, had returned was enough to fuel the gossip even further.

And at the tail end of those rumors, another was slowly raising its head.

The Demon King of the North.

*Kim Jonghyun.*

He had revealed his true colors.
