For the first time in three years, freedom felt both awkward and welcome. Lee Sungmin blinked, looking down at the terrible sight his body had become after nearly a year without washing or changing his clothes. The stench was an old companion, a foul odor his nose had long grown accustomed to, but it was not a pleasant sight.

He slowly lifted a foot. The tip of his toe felt light. In fact, his entire body felt weightless. Lee Sungmin took a few experimental steps and let out a small, disbelieving laugh.

"How strange," he muttered to himself. The static in his ears was gone, replaced by the real, vibrant cry of cicadas.

"What should I give you," Mushii murmured.

At her words, Lee Sungmin turned. She stood with her arms crossed, her posture askew. He tilted his head, studying her contemplative face.

"Give me what?"

"You have overcome this mountain's trial and endured its asceticism for three long years. Among those who have recently entered this mountain, none have lasted as long as you, nor have they endured more restrictions."

Mushii's lips curled into a smirk as hazy smoke shimmered beside her. The smoke soon gathered, coalescing into the form of a small boy. It was Flam.

"As a reward for overcoming the Despair named Flam, I have arranged a meeting for you with Denir, the God of Time. What you gain from that meeting is entirely up to you."

The God of Time Denir in Dreamoor. He remembered the name from the memories Mushii had given him. He didn't know what he would get from meeting Denir, but he had already planned to go to Dreamoor if the opportunity ever arose.

"That is the reward for overcoming Flam," Mushii continued. "The reward for enduring three years is... yes. I shall recommend you as a member of 'Erebrisa'." She nodded to herself, having reached a decision after a moment of thought.

Erebrisa? The name meant nothing to him. Lee Sungmin tilted his head again. "What's Erebrisa?"

"An 'intermediary Guild'," Mushii answered. "An intermediary Guild that can be summoned from anywhere in the world."

As she spoke, Mushii raised her hand, her lips moving slightly. In that instant, a calm voice spoke as a man appeared from nowhere.

"It has been a while, O Goddess of Trials and Asceticism."

Lee Sungmin had no idea where he had come from. To him, it looked as if the man had simply risen from Mushii's shadow. He was remarkably tall and dressed in a neat suit. Without giving Lee Sungmin a single glance, he bowed his head deeply toward Mushii.

"What kind of support do you require?"

"I didn't call you for that today," Mushii replied. "I called because I wish to recommend a new member."

Only then did the man turn to look at Lee Sungmin, his expression shifting to one of slight surprise.

"My, my... what a rare being you are. A soul with twisted causality. You don't wish to sell this soul, but to recommend it as a member of Erebrisa?"

"That's right."

"I'm afraid he lacks the qualifications..." the man muttered, tilting his head. "A soul with twisted causality. Its sale value is high, but that's insufficient for membership qualifications. Let's see... he's learned martial arts, and his skill... is quite outstanding. Has he already surpassed the peak realm? How many years did he train on this mountain?"

"Three years."

"Peak realm after training for three years on this mountain. He has tenacity and grit, but he lacks talent. O Goddess. As you know, not just anyone can become a member of Erebrisa."

"That is why I am using my recommendation," Mushii retorted. "A member of my standing should be able to seat even one without qualifications. I see no problem with it, do you?"

"...Hmm." The man let out a low groan. He raised his hand and tapped the air a few times before nodding. "If that is what you truly wish. Considering all the business the Goddess has done with us... very well. We will register him as a new member of our Erebrisa."

Having said that, the man turned to Lee Sungmin and bowed his head politely.

"We will now make a simple contract. A little blood... would you mind?"

"...Blood?"

"Yes. Ah. I swear on my existence that this contract is solely for the purpose of registering you as a new member of our Erebrisa, and that no disadvantage will befall you. The Goddess of Trials and Asceticism Mushii and the god of promises and deals, Behan, will serve as witnesses to this."

Hearing the man's words, Lee Sungmin glanced at Mushii, who gave a slight nod of confirmation. He then bit the tip of his finger, and blood trickled from the small wound.

"Confirmed, Lee Sungmin."

The dripping blood fell but never touched the soil. It vanished in mid-air, and the man nodded as he spoke.

"Lee Sungmin's soul has been registered as a member of Erebrisa. Whenever you wish, please seek Erebrisa in your mind. If you do so, your designated agent will come to you."

The man said this and then looked back at Mushii. *You may go.* As soon as the thought was formed, the man's form disappeared again. Unable to comprehend the sudden turn of events, Lee Sungmin looked at Mushii, who simply shrugged as she met his gaze.

"Summon them yourself. It would be better for you to hear it from them than for me to explain."

"...Umm..." Lee Sungmin let out a small groan and called for Erebrisa in his mind.

In that instant.

WHOOSH!

Someone shot up from the shadow at Lee Sungmin's feet.

"A pleasure to meet you, Lee Sungmin. I am Broker Nebul of Erebrisa. I will be in charge of you from now on."

It was a different man from the one before. This one, who introduced himself as Nebul, had short-cropped hair and was also dressed in a neat suit. He bowed his head deeply to Lee Sungmin, who returned the gesture in an awkward posture.

"Ah... yes."

"Is this your first time using Erebrisa?" Nebul asked. Just as Mushii's agent had shown no interest in Lee Sungmin, Nebul paid no attention to Mushii. Lee Sungmin stared at Nebul's face and blankly nodded.

"In that case, I'll give you a brief explanation. Erebrisa is an intermediary Guild that exists to broker anything a client desires. At the same time, we maintain cooperative relationships with various Guilds, including the Merchants' Guild." Nebul paused to take a breath. "If you wish to sell an item, we will take responsibility for purchasing it. If you want an auction, we will hold one for you. Of course, it's not free. We only take a conscientious commission of five percent."

Lee Sungmin couldn't help but be surprised. An intermediary Guild. He still didn't quite understand what that meant, but a commission of only five percent was incredible. Most Merchants' Guilds and Mercenary Guilds took fifteen percent, yet Erebrisa's was a fraction of that.

"Regarding transactions, we will match the market prices of Eria. If you're looking for a blacksmith, we'll connect you with a skilled one. If you want information about a Dungeon, we'll purchase the information you desire through an information Guild we cooperate with. We swear that there are no falsehoods in the information purchased through us. Ah, of course. An additional five percent commission will be added in this process as well."

At this point, Lee Sungmin began to understand what Erebrisa meant by 'support'. They would support everything he desired. To meet a skilled blacksmith, one had to go to the Blacksmith Guild. To obtain information, one had to go to an information Guild. It sounded easy, but the process was by no means simple. Blacksmiths were generally stubborn, so one had to put in considerable effort just to persuade them.

The same went for information Guilds. It was a problem just to determine whether the information bought with good money was real or fake, but information purchased through Erebrisa was guaranteed to be true. He couldn't unconditionally believe those words, of course, but just being spared the legwork was a huge merit.

"If you need quick cash, we can also lend you money with collateral. There are various other things, but it would be faster for you to try using us yourself, Lee Sungmin, than for me to explain them."

"...As for trades... do you sell things like miracle herbs or martial arts manuals? Or magic books..." Lee Sungmin asked after a moment of thought.

Nebul grinned and nodded. "Of course."

He raised his hand and tapped the air a few times. A translucent window then appeared in front of Lee Sungmin, similar to the status window that otherworlders summoned to Eria could see.

"It also has a search function, so please give it a try," Nebul suggested.

The Erebrisa trade window was intuitive. Items could be sorted by category and also by price. Lee Sungmin gasped as he looked at the listed trade items.

Great Cycle Pill.

That was the miracle herb listed at the very top, the most outstanding one ever created by Shaolin. He had heard that all Great Cycle Pills were under the strict management of the sect, granted only to a very select few of their martial artists. For such an item to be listed for sale was unbelievable.

"What is this price...!" Lee Sungmin gasped, sucking in a breath. The price of the Great Cycle Pill was beyond imagination.

Seeing his shock, Nebul spoke with an unconcerned expression. "The Great Cycle Pill is considered one of the highest-grade miracle herbs created through alchemy. It certainly has a value that matches its price. By taking one, a person can gain at least several decades of inner qi. Even with a lot of money, one can't buy time, so if you can buy several decades' worth of inner qi with money, it's only natural that it would have such a price."

He could understand the logic, but he was still stunned by how expensive it was. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard and searched for the Holy Spirit Pill. It was there. The price was quite high, but it couldn't compare to the Great Cycle Pill.

"Although the efficiency when consumed is lower, there are also raw miracle herbs," Nebul advised. "Things like Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng, Hundred-Year Snow Ginseng, the spirit core of a Human-Faced Spider... things like that. However, I can't recommend them. Unrefined miracle herbs tend to have side effects. For example, if you eat a Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng, the balance of yin and yang will collapse and the yin energy will run rampant, creating a high probability of qi deviation. It might be a different story if you've learned a yang-aspected inner arts technique."

Lee Sungmin tore his gaze away from the miracle herbs. It wasn't that he had no desire, but the prices were too high to even consider. Instead, he looked for martial arts.

There, he couldn't help but hold his breath. Various martial arts were being sold. The Tai Chi Wisdom Sword, which could be called Wudang's ultimate technique, was there, as was Mount Hua's Plum Blossom Sword Art.

"There are a great many types of martial arts. The origins of the otherworlders who come to Eria are so diverse. Even for the same Tai Chi Wisdom Sword, the forms are slightly different. However, all of them are at a level that could be called a divine technique."

He didn't understand. Could a sect's ultimate technique be leaked and traded so easily? No, of course, the price was so enormous that it was almost impossible to purchase. As if reading his bewilderment, Nebul smiled brightly.

"Didn't I tell you? Our Erebrisa cooperates with various clients, Guilds, and the like. Ah... however. Even if you purchase and learn a martial art through us, we are not responsible for handling the aftermath."

Lee Sungmin understood what he meant. If he recklessly brandished the Tai Chi Wisdom Sword, he would attract the attention of the Wudang Sect. If he couldn't provide them with a satisfactory reason, they might resort to trickery to reclaim the martial art.

It wasn't just martial arts that could be purchased. Various kinds of magic also existed, from organized school magic to minor spells. Lee Sungmin looked over the traded goods and shook his head, dumbfounded. He had never imagined that martial arts, magic, and miracle herbs would be traded in such a way.

"...Are there some kind of conditions to become a member of Erebrisa?"

"There are. In fact, the easiest way is to be recommended by another member, like you were, Lee Sungmin. Actually, a recommendation doesn't guarantee membership, but... Mushii is a regular customer of ours. We bent the rules for her to some extent." Nebul said this, bowing his head slightly toward Mushii.

At those words, Lee Sungmin tilted his head. What could a god like Mushii have possibly traded through an intermediary Guild like this?

"Souls," Mushii answered. "The souls of those who died on this mountain. I traded them." She said it with a nonchalant face.

Lee Sungmin's expression couldn't help but stiffen. "You, a god... you sold human souls?"

"You're misunderstanding something," Mushii asked back with a strange expression. "This mountain is my sacred ground. All souls who die here are mine. Is there a problem with me doing as I please with what is mine?"

In the first place, Mushii was a god, not a human. Trying to apply human standards to her would not lead to a productive conversation.

"Souls are a good trade commodity," Nebul added, his eyes narrowing. "Many desire human souls. Black Mages, heretics, sometimes even Dragons... and the Demon Race. Isn't it better for everyone to trade them in this form? It is better to sell souls to them through trade than to have the powerful commit mass slaughter to obtain them, and... as I said. If you wish, we can give you many things with your soul as collateral, Lee Sungmin. A soul with twisted causality has that much value. Ah. Of course, collateral is just collateral. You would just need to repay the money you borrowed..."

"Don't listen to him," Mushii said, cutting Nebul off. "It would be better for you to give your soul to me and make a contract. Isn't talent what you want? As I've said before, if you give me your soul, I can give you a worthy price for it."

Through their conversation, Lee Sungmin noticed something. They were coveting his soul. The soul with twisted causality, born from returning to the past against death, seemed to hold a great value that he was unaware of.

"I have no intention of giving up my soul," Lee Sungmin said with a sigh.

Mushii had told him. His causality was twisted, and in the process, his soul had accumulated a lot of karma. It wouldn't affect his life itself, but later, when he died... he was told he would suffer for eons. In other words, death would not be a rest for Lee Sungmin. A painful end was scheduled for him.

Even so, he had no intention of giving up his soul.

"I don't have anything I want to trade right now. I don't have any money either. Later... I'll call you if there's something I want to trade."

"Please call anytime. Ah, and this is..." Nebul raised his hand.

SNAP.

His thumb and index finger collided, and a pure white light enveloped Lee Sungmin's body.

"A small favor from me, for our new member, Lee Sungmin."

His filthy appearance became immaculate. His clothes were still no different from rags, but they no longer stank or had any dirt on them. It wasn't just his clothes. His matted hair and unwashed body also became clean.

"Well then, I'll see you next time."

With those final words, Nebul's body disappeared. *Was that magic just now?* No. If he thought about it, Nebul's appearance and disappearance were magic to begin with. Lee Sungmin blankly looked down at his clean body and muttered.

"There are so many things I don't know."

"The world is a wide place," Mushii said, looking at him. "Becoming a member of Erebrisa is a great thing. I gave it as a reward for the trial you endured, so I don't regret it. So. Where do you plan to go now?"

"...First, I plan to go to Behenger and become a mercenary."

"A mercenary, huh. Huhu. That should be fine too. Go on and do as you please." Mushii muttered and took a few steps back. "Don't forget what I muttered to myself before."

With those last words, she turned to smoke and disappeared. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the spot where she had vanished, then looked down at his own body. *There are no coincidences in this world. If I returned, there must have been a reason for it.*

The words 'no coincidences' bothered him.

Throughout his life so far, Lee Sungmin thought he had benefited from quite a few coincidences. Meeting Jack and Hans. Meeting Wiji Hoyeon. Coming to Mount Mushii and meeting Baek Sogo. All of that must have been coincidence.

Did 'no coincidences' mean that even those meetings weren't coincidences?

*...I don't know.*

Lee Sungmin sighed and turned around.

First, he had to descend the mountain.
