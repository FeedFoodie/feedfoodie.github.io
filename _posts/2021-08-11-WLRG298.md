The candlelight flickered, casting dancing shadows across the room.

Across from him, the Sword Immortal sat with a faint smile etched into his wrinkled face. Lee Sungmin could only stare back, his expression tight with tension. It had been the Sword Immortal who invited him to sit, the one who suggested they talk, yet now the old man simply watched him, letting the silence stretch on for what felt like an eternity.

"How did Ilcheon die?" the Sword Immortal asked, his voice finally breaking the silence. "Can you tell me?"

Lee Sungmin swallowed, the memory vivid and sharp. Answering was not the problem. The difficulty lay in the torment that still clawed at his heart whenever he revisited that time.

"Is it painful?" the old man inquired gently.

"...No, I'm fine," Lee Sungmin lied, exhaling a breath he hadn't realized he was holding.

With no reason to hide the truth, he began to speak. He told the Sword Immortal everything that had transpired in the North Snowfield, recounting not only the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman but also the future Jeniela had foreseen, the very reason he had traveled north in the first place. The long story eventually drew to a close, and throughout the entire tale, the Sword Immortal listened with an unwavering posture, his expression unreadable.

Mulling over the story that concluded with the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Sword Immortal closed his eyes.

"Did he achieve Celestial Ascension?" he muttered in a small voice.

"Celestial Ascension?" Lee Sungmin echoed, the term unfamiliar.

"Simply put, it means he became a deity." The Sword Immortal chuckled, a dry *Kkulkul* sound. "To think that Ilcheon, who held the title of Demon Emperor, would reach a realm that even this old man has yet to attain. If what you say is true, then Ilcheon truly was history's number one and the world's number one." There was no envy in his voice, only a profound respect. "No, perhaps it can't be called Celestial Ascension," he corrected himself, still smiling. "After all, he didn't technically become a deity. Even so, the realm Ilcheon reached is nothing short of admirable."

After praising the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial prowess, the Sword Immortal raised his gaze to the ceiling.

"Do you resent the Martial God?"

"What kind of answer are you expecting from me?"

"An honest one. The obvious one, without lies."

"I do resent him."

"Of course, you would," the Sword Immortal said with a grin. "But in the end, what you both want is the same. Both you and the Martial God wish to stop The End."

"Did you hear that from the Mages Guild Master?"

"It was an interesting story," the Sword Immortal confirmed with a nod. "The very existence of Heaven Beyond Heaven is a contradiction. They aim to create a world for humans and are moving to stop The End, which is the end of all things, and yet, they move not according to their own will, but to the will of the Spirit."

"...The Spirit is a suspicious entity."

"Indeed," the Sword Immortal agreed. No matter how he considered it, the Spirit's will and the directives given to Heaven Beyond Heaven were questionable, even contradictory at times. He could not fathom its true intentions, nor could he understand why it would personally intervene to kill the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"The Martial God is a puppet," the Sword Immortal muttered. "Looking at things so far, the Spirit, which commands Heaven Beyond Heaven to stop The End, is instead acting for the sake of The End. The Martial God, however, seems to believe his own actions are saving this world."

"Sword Immortal. What is it that you desire?"

"If The End is something that can be stopped, then it must be stopped. ...However, this old man's body cannot leave Wudang."

Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched at the murmur. "What in the world does that mean?"

"It's qi deviation."

The words were so unexpected coming from the Sword Immortal's mouth that Lee Sungmin was stunned into silence. The old man continued speaking to him.

"My greed was too great. I tried to go too far without proper preparation, and the qi deviation that followed destroyed my qi center. It turned my body into something that is alive, yet cannot be called living."

"What nonsense..."

"It's not a lie." The Sword Immortal shook his head. "My boy, this old man had reached a high realm, and from that height, I fell. It is only here on Mount Wudang that I can maintain the level befitting my title. If I were to step outside this mountain, I would die coughing up blood before taking even ten steps."

"...How could..."

"Why do you think this old man hasn't left Wudang for so long?" the Sword Immortal asked with a smile.

Lee Sungmin could not answer. It was frustrating. He clenched his fists, wondering if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had hoped for the Sword Immortal's cooperation. Even he could not have imagined the old man would be this powerless.

"Are you disappointed in this old man?"

"...A little."

"Kkulkul! You probably never imagined that the great Wudang Sword Immortal was just a broken old man who can't even travel far. Ilcheon must have thought the same."

"......"

"But I can kill you," the Sword Immortal said, a smile still playing on his lips.

"......"

A chill, sharp as a blade, ran down Lee Sungmin's spine, and his expression hardened. He started to rise, but the Sword Immortal's voice stopped him cold.

"Don't move."

The old man did not draw his sword. To him, a sword was meaningless. Even with a body broken by qi deviation, a body that could not leave this mountain, he was still a master of the highest level. Here, within these sacred grounds, he was every bit the Sword Immortal.

"From the moment you set foot on this mountain, you were within my range," he continued calmly. "If I had wanted to, your head would have already fallen dozens, even hundreds of times."

"Are you going to kill me?"

"The fact that you are Ilcheon's disciple, the questions surrounding his death... that is why I called you here. I needed to speak with you directly, and now, it seems I must make a decision."

"Whether to kill me or not?"

"How many people in this world truly wish for death? That's why we try to stop The End. Because we don't wish for an unconditional end."

"I want to stop The End."

"Can you be certain that you yourself are not The End?" the Sword Immortal questioned. "Didn't you say it yourself? The Spirit declared the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was the axis of The End."

"You were the one who said the Spirit was suspicious."

"Indeed. I do suspect the Martial God is being manipulated like a puppet. You also said that the child named Kim Jonghyun couldn't fully awaken as the Demon Lord because of Heaven Beyond Heaven's interference, and that he became one of the calamities for The End as a result."

"Then why..."

"Think carefully." The Sword Immortal's eyes narrowed. "The Spirit that gives directives to Heaven Beyond Heaven. Haven't you also been in contact with it?"

Lee Sungmin was at a loss for words. *Ah.* A memory from long ago surfaced.

*Shaolin. Yes, Shaolin.*

It was a very long time ago, back when he first met the previous Abbot, Grandmaster Bulyeong. It was a memory from before he even entered the Mental World, a thousand years in the past. The Spirit dwelling in Grandmaster Bulyeong's body had spoken to him. *Go to the North. You will meet a nobleman there.*

"If the Spirit is using Heaven Beyond Heaven to bring about The End, then, my boy... what are you?"

Lee Sungmin's eyes wavered. He swallowed against the anxiety creeping up his throat.

"Didn't you also make contact with the Spirit and move according to its will? I heard from Abel what kind of being you are. Your very existence was tasked with summoning The End. You could have brought it about just by staying alive. So why would the Spirit go out of its way to contact you and compel your actions?" The Sword Immortal paused. "There are no coincidences in the world. Isn't it possible that you, too, are The End?"

Lee Sungmin was silent. He couldn't dismiss the old man's words as a lie, nor could he deny them with any certainty.

"So you're going to kill me for that?"

"I said it was the fundamental reason. If The End can be stopped, it must be. Fortunately for me, you are within my range. Within a very easy distance to cut you down."

"...I can't be sure. I'm not certain that I'm not The End."

"Then what are you?"

"I am me," Lee Sungmin answered, grinding his teeth. "The Martial God was the same, playing the Spirit's puppet while claiming to stop The End. How are you any different?"

"Sword Immortal. You lack conviction yourself," Lee Sungmin spat, his voice laced with irritation. He no longer bothered with honorifics. "You threaten me, citing some fundamental reason, yet you're not even sure if you should really kill me."

"Kkulkul! Yes, you're right. Honestly, I don't know," the Sword Immortal laughed heartily, and the sharpness that had enveloped the area vanished. "This old man doesn't know what the Spirit wants. It seems to be acting for The End, but I can't know its true intentions. Perhaps the Spirit's will and the Martial God's actions are genuinely meant to stop it."

"You want to evade the responsibility of your choice," Lee Sungmin shot back as he surged to his feet. "If you think killing me is the right thing to do, then just kill me, but you can't even convince yourself. I don't know what you want or hope to gain from this meaningless conversation."

"You strike where it hurts," the Sword Immortal said, still laughing. "I wanted to know about you, my boy. You are the disciple of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon and the Phantom Spear. You want to stop The End, yet you carry the anxiety that you yourself might be The End. I've decided. This old man will not kill you here, though I don't know what consequences my actions will bring."

Lee Sungmin didn't answer, only glaring at the old man. The Sword Immortal continued, unperturbed by his hostile gaze.

"This old man also knows that your existence is special. Having contacted the Spirit, you might be The End, but your uniqueness might also act as an exception. Kkulkul... It's difficult. Very difficult. It is so hard for a human to try and comprehend the affairs of non-human beings."

"Just as you do what you intend to do," Lee Sungmin said, suppressing his anger, "I, too, am just trying to do what I intend to do. If you try to stop me, I'll have no choice but to use all my strength to escape your grasp."

"Do you think you can escape if this old man genuinely tries to kill you?"

"Don't underestimate me." Lee Sungmin's golden eyes flashed. His inner qi and Demonic Energy stirred with his agitated emotions, and a violet current crackled around his shoulders as he naturally channeled the essence of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. "I'm not confident I can kill you on this mountain, but I am confident that I won't die from a single strike of your sword. Furthermore, before you can kill me, I can kill at least half of the Wudang Sect's ascetics, even if you survive."

The Sword Immortal guffawed at the murderous threat. "Are you threatening this old man?"

"Wouldn't it be good if this ends without anyone dying?"

"Stop. This old man will not kill you. I have little attachment to my own life, but the lives of my innocent disciples are more precious than mine."

"...Why are Jihak of Shaolin and the Sword Dragon of Namgung here?"

"It's because I wanted to leave open the possibility," the Sword Immortal smiled warmly. "Setting The End aside, I wanted to show a path forward for the promising next generation."

"...Even if their sects are different?"

"Their talents were too exceptional to be held back for such a trivial reason. Bulyeong knew that well. That's why he entrusted the two of them to me."

"If Grandmaster Bulyeong sent them, isn't it possible they also came here by the Spirit's will?"

"It's for the same reason I didn't kill you." The Sword Immortal closed his eyes. "I don't want to trample on a possibility whose outcome is unknown."

At that murmur, Lee Sungmin quietly suppressed the aura he had raised. The Sword Immortal slightly opened his eyes.

"You're handling Ilcheon's martial arts splendidly."

"...Because I received a great deal," Lee Sungmin muttered as he turned away.

There was nothing more to discuss. He considered saying hello to Jihak and Namgung Heewon before leaving but decided against it.

KWADANG!

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to leave the sanctuary, the closed door burst open.

Abel came running in, his eyes widening in surprise when he saw Lee Sungmin. There was no time for greetings.

"Something's happened," Abel blurted out urgently.

Behind him, the Sword Immortal rose to his feet.
