Jongri Mun was surprisingly calm when he received the report of Pung Yang's defeat. He had imagined this moment several times, envisioning the moment he would hear this very report. 

In a way, it was a relief to once again be reminded of Crimson Tiger's ridiculous martial prowess. If he had made the wrong call under pressure when the secret agent had infiltrated, it would have been a disaster.

Giyeong stood in front of him with his head bowed low and an apologetic look on his face. Since it was was he who had suggested using Pung Yang to eliminate Crimson Tiger in the first place, he was somewhat downcast.

Jongri Mun asked calmly, "How did the Association react?"

"They have been quiet. Today, we received a follow-up report stating that Pung Yang's identity wasn't revealed because his corpse was incinerated. The men he took with him were also individuals whose identities are difficult to trace."

"His corpse was incinerated? Jongri Mun tilted his head. "Hmmm, I see. Interesting."

"Is something the matter? Do you have a theory?"

"How often do masters of Pung Yang's caliber burn to death?"

"What do you mean?"

"Crimson Tiger might have deliberately mutilated the corpse."

"Why would he do that?"

Jongri Mun narrowed his eyes. "Something is happening that we don't know about."

KNOCK, KNOCK!

The door opened, and a subordinate entered with a letter for Giyeong.

Giyeong's eyes widened as he read it. "We've received a new report."

"What is it?"

"Apparently, when Crimson Tiger escaped from us, there was a woman with him."

"What? A woman? Who?"

"Our spies were unable to determine her identity."

The subordinate withdrew, and a brief silence fell over the room.

Giyeong opened his mouth to speak, but Jongri Mun raised a hand to stop him. He needed time to think. 

His eyes darted back and forth, glinting in the dim office. "...You said there was a fight at the document archive, right? Who was involved?

"We found Smiley's corpse near the archive."

"He was probably caught while tailing Crimson Tiger."

"That seems to be the case."

"Why would Crimson Tiger infiltrate that archive? Nothing useful is stored there..." Jongri Mun's eyes flashed. "Wait. Don't the Ironblood Nines discard their names upon entry? The only record of their real names is in that archive, isn't it?"

"That's right! Don't tell me?"

"Yes, he needed information on someone from the Ironblood Nines. Damn it! Why didn't I think of that sooner?"

Jongri Mun cursed his tunnel vision. He had focused too intently on the Ironblood Commander's death and overlooked the real objective. He asked sharply, "There was a woman among the dead Ironblood Nines. Who was she?"

"Seventh Six. Her body was found badly mutilated."

"It's her."

"Pardon?"

"That isn't Seventh Six's corpse, but rather the missing Snakeflower's. Crimson Tiger must have mutilated the body on purpose to hide its identity."

"If that's true, then... Damn it! Was the Ironblood Commander's murder just a ploy to throw us off?"

"Exactly. Bring me all the files on Seventh Six right now! Immediately!"

"Understood."

"And contact Enigma. Tell him to find out who the woman he rescued is!"

Giyeong quickly exited the office.

Left alone, Jongri Mun's eyes sparkled brighter than ever before as his strategist's intuition began to stir.

KNOCK KNOCK!

Jongri Mun sprang up from his seat at the arrival of a new guest. It was none other than the Coalition Leader, Neung Pungbi.

"It's been a while. Let's go out for a drink," Neung Pungbi said, holding out a bamboo hat he had brought.

"Yes." Jongri Mun politely accepted the hat and followed him, feeling uneasy about this surprise visit.

As they headed out of Coalition Headquarters, Neung Pungbi remained silent, as if he already knew all the sins Jongri Mun had committed.

A shiver ran down Jongri Mun's spine. He knew Neung Pungbi better than anyone. Contrary to his public image as a simple man bent on domination, the Coalition Leader was both prudent and shrewd.

They entered the Pear Blossom Inn, where Crimson Tiger had once shared a drink with Soeun, and took a seat in the corner on the second floor. Their bodyguards disguised themselves as customers and sat at the surrounding tables. 

Jongri Mun estimated their number. This was a minor detail compared to usual. Normally, when the Coalition Leader visited a place, all the other customers would be ushered out while his bodyguards formed a security perimeter. Today, however, it seemed he intended to be discreet.

The two removed their bamboo hats. Fortunately, no one in the inn recognized them.

A waiter brought them liquor and snacks.

Immediately, one of the bodyguards at a nearby table approached and quickly inspected the food and drink. After confirming that there was no poison, he withdrew.

"It's been a while, hasn't it?" Neung Pungbi began.

"Yes."

There was a time in the past when the two men spent a lot of time together. Recently, though, Neung Pungbi had been restraining himself, in part because he was accused of favoring his strategist too much.

"Let's have a drink."

The two men emptied their cups.

"So, has that man Cheonsu been bothering you?

Jongri Mun nodded. Jwa Cheonsu was the man in charge of investigating the Ironblood Commander's death. "Yes, he is very annoying," he complained.

Neung Pungbi smiled at Jongri Mun's honest answer.

Jongri Mun hastily added, "Senior Jwa may be inflexible, but he's very thorough."

Neung Pungbi smirked. Jongri Mun was not afraid to criticize others, even in front of his superiors. He must have known it might come back to haunt him, yet he voiced his unfiltered opinion with no regard for the consequences.

Neung Pungbi knew this was his strategist's unique way of demonstrating loyalty, a gesture intended to prevent misunderstandings and misperceptions. This was one of the reasons he cherished Jongri Mun.

Jongri Mun sighed. The moment had come to confess, regardless of whether Neung Pungbi had knowledge of the situation or not. 

Still, he could not disclose everything. Doing so would jeopardize some of his plans.

Fortunately, he had received the news about Crimson Tiger's true objective before Neung Pungbi's visit. Using that as an excuse, he could hopefully make up for his mistakes in regard to this incident.

"...Coalition Leader, I'm afraid this incident is not as straightforward as it seems," he said hesitantly. "The Association's main objective was not the Ironblood Commander's assassination."

Neung Pungbi's eyes widened in surprise, but he filled Jongri Mun's cup and quietly waited for the strategist to continue.

Jongri Mun explained, "He abducted a woman from the Ironblood Nines. The assassination of the Ironblood Commander was just a ruse. The woman was his primary objective."

Neung Pungbi's eyes lit up in interest. As he had expected, Jongri Mun had not just been sitting back and accepting defeat. If he understood the situation correctly, then this recent damage was nothing. 

His trust in his strategist grew even stronger.

Jongri Mun took a deep breath. It was time to reveal one of the secrets he had kept hidden from Neung Pungbi. "Actually, there is a matter I have been working on for a very long time.

"What is it?"

"I have planted one of our own in the Radiant Hall. His name is Enigma."

"Oho!"

"This is the real reason why I advised against seeking revenge when the Heaven-Destroying Corps Commander was assassinated two years ago. I was afraid that if we took action against the Twelve Phantoms carelessly, the spy we had planted would become worthless."

"So that's how it was." Neung Pungbi nodded. He wasn't bothered that Jongri Mun had hidden this fact even from him. An ordinary person would have revealed it, if only to brag, and possibly sabotaged the entire operation in the process.

*Yes, you of all people should be able to do at least this much.*

He was satisfied. This prize was worth the Ironblood Commander's life.

"Enigma has only recently begun to act in earnest," said Jongri Mun.

"You've kept him hidden very well."

"Yes, after all, the Radiant Hall is the Twelve Phantoms' command center. They are by no means easy foes to deceive. Recently, thanks to information provided by Enigma, we uncovered two spies who had infiltrated our Coalition."

"Huhu, so you did."

"That was just our secondary goal, though. Enigma's main mission is another matter entirely."

"What is that?"

"To raid the Myriad Secrets Cave.

"The Myriad Secrets Cave!" Neung Pungbi's eyes gleamed. The Myriad Secrets Cave was where all of the Association's confidential information was stored.

"That's right. If we obtain information from there, the Association will undoubtedly suffer a devastating blow. What's more..." Jongri Mun grinned wickedly. "...Enigma is now only one step away from success."

{sep}

A man crept stealthily through the document archive in the Radiant Hall. His movements were swift and silent, completely unlike his usual self.

He took out several documents and began tampering with them. Spread out before him on a cloth were brushes of various sizes and chemicals of unknown use.

If those who made a living in the jianghu by forging documents were to see his handiwork, they would have gaped in disbelief at how flawlessly he was forging the documents. His skill was so impressive that no one would be able to detect the slightest trace of tampering.

Finishing his work in an instant, the man slipped out without a trace, as stealthily as he had entered. No one would ever know what he had done in that short time.

One thing was certain, however. His work would change his own fate and that of everyone else.

DRIP!

A drop of water fell on Baek Museong's shoulder as he navigated a humid, moldy corridor that reeked of filth. Pausing for a moment, he looked up at the ceiling, only to see that it was dotted with water droplets that looked ready to fall.

He resumed walking. The person he was supposed to meet was in the room at the end of the corridor.

"Have you been well?" he asked.

From behind the bars, Jin Chung looked a little gaunt. However, his eyes were clearer than when he was free.

Jin Chung smiled. "You seem to have lost some weight," he remarked, even though he himself had slimmed down the most. Regardless, he was more worried about his master than himself.

Baek Museong looked down at himself. "Really?"

"Please eat well."

"I will."

Their gazes met. 

Baek Museong sighed. He wanted to get Jin Chung out of there as soon as possible, but he knew it would have to wait until after the wedding at least. 

"What business did you want to see me about?" he asked. Jin Chung had contacted him about an urgent matter, so he had come immediately. He knew that given his subordinate's personality, he would never have called for him if it wasn't important.

Jin Chung bowed his head in apology. "I apologize for forcing you to endure the miserable conditions of this hellhole. I simply felt that this was something I should tell you in person."

"What is it?"

"We have a spy planted in the Evil Coalition."

"And?"

"He has made contact." Jin Chung paused for a moment, as if giving Baek Museong time to brace himself for what was about to come. "...He says that when Crimson Tiger escaped from the Evil Coalition, he brought a woman with him."

"What?" Baek Museong's eyes widened. No one had surprised him more in a long time.

A heavy silence ensued.

Eventually, Baek Museong stammered, "D-Don't tell me...is that woman...?"

Jin Chung nodded. "I'm quite certain that she is your daughter."

For a moment, Soeun's face flashed in Baek Museong's mind. Never before had a woman made such a strong impression on him.

"Ah! It's that child," he gasped. He finally understood why his heart ached so much when he looked at her.

His feelings were complex. One thing was certain, however. Amidst his bewilderment, he felt more happiness than anger.

This confused him even more. No matter how shocked he was, he should have been furious!

"How did this happen?" he pressed.

"I don't know how she did it, but it seems the Third Young Lady convinced Crimson Tiger to rescue her instead of kill her."

Baek Museong was stunned speechless for a while. "...What should I do now?" he muttered blankly.

Jin Chung answered sincerely, "First, go meet her. After that... just do as your heart tells you."

{sep}

About twenty teenagers, aged thirteen or fourteen, sat in a circle in the training ground of the Tempest Martial Arts Academy, listening to their new instructor's lecture.

"You must never trifle with the sword," Soeun said. "The weapon you carry can kill people. If you stab someone with it, they will die."

Few of the teenagers were actually listening, but they were all staring at their new instructor with sparkling eyes. Her appearance commanded that much attention.

The Tempest Martial Arts Academy was an educational institution fully supported by the Association. Boys and girls between the ages of thirteen and nineteen from all over the land gathered here to study both martial arts and academics.

Students with excellent grades were immediately recruited by the Association. Occasionally, those with truly outstanding skills were admitted directly to the Inner Court, though that was uncommon. Most entered as martial artists of the Outer Court.

A few days ago, Soeun was appointed as a swordsmanship instructor at the academy. She was sent here by Ju Hwayin because Baek Museong regularly came to lecture here once every five days. Of course, Ju Hwayin also came to lecture, though on different days.

A bold-looking child raised his hand. "I have a question."

"Go ahead."

"Have you ever killed someone, Teacher?"

Soeun could feel the derision in the brat's question. The kid was basically calling her out for teaching them about things she'd never even done, like murder.

Then again, due to the long truce between the Association and the Evil Coalition, more than half of the instructors at the academy had no real combat experience.

Nevertheless, the children's primary concerns were the same as at any other martial arts academy . Which teacher fought well? Which teacher was a phony? Which teacher had killed someone in the past?

Moreover, the families who sent their children to this academy were mostly martial families of the jianghu. Having heard all sorts of rumors from their parents and family members, these children were particularly sensitive to such stories.

Soeun smiled mysteriously. "Why do you ask?"

The child grinned. "Because I can."

The other children burst out laughing.

Soeun grew flustered, unsure of how to handle the situation. It would be awkward to scold the boy, but it would also be wrong to let it slide. She definitely had experience in combat, but there was no way she could tell them about her own experiences with the Ironblood Nines!

Just then, a man's voice rang out, saying, "Don't worry, kid, I assure you Miss Baek here has a great deal of practical experience."

Soeun spun around to find Baek Museong walking toward her.

Startled, the children rose from their seats.

"First Young Master!" they greeted politely.

Although they were still young, their teachers and parents had nagged them about never being rude to Baek Museong and Ju Hwayin until they were sick of hearing it.

"Miss Baek may look young, but she is actually a jianghu veteran."

The students immediately exclaimed in renewed admiration, going "ooh" and "aah."

"She has had a lot of practice dealing with the Evil Coalition. In fact, she participated in an operation that I oversaw myself."

The students cheered even louder.

"Now, I have something I'd like to consult Miss Baek about, so shall we end the class early?"

The students looked at Soeun with reverence.

"See you tomorrow, Miss Baek!"

They bowed deeply and ran into the building.

In an instant, they had changed their form of address from Teacher to Miss Baek. Normally, the students called the young or easygoing teachers "Teacher" and only addressed the older and stricter ones formally.

"It has been a long time." Soeun greeted Baek Museong. She had never dreamed that he would appear here. Furthermore, she had not expected him to lie to the students on her behalf.

Baek Museong chuckled. "Children are as shrewd as adults, and sometimes even more so. At the same time, however, they are naive. The atmosphere of the class changes completely depending on how you interact with them. I also had a hard time adjusting to teaching at first, haha!"

Soeun smiled.

Baek Museong studied her face closely. Now that he knew she was his daughter, the family resemblance was obvious.

*To think that such a pretty woman is my daughter. I can hardly believe it.*

Soeun asked, "What brings you here?"

"I also teach a class here," Baek Museong replied.

"What? Really?"

"Indeed. I used to come once every five days, but from now on, I'll be coming more often."

"Ah!" Soeun's heart fluttered. Now she understood why Ju Hwayin had sent her here. "That's nice. I'm looking forward to seeing you more often."

"Really?"

Soeun beamed. "Of course!"

Baek Museong's heart stirred at the sight of his daughter's radiant smile. He wanted to run to her and hug her right away. He wondered if she knew he was her father.

*Now I understand why Junior Sister sent this child to me. She wants to give me an obvious weakness that she can exploit.*

A sense of coldness and unease washed over him, as if a fissure had formed in his otherwise impenetrable steel heart.

*Damn it. What if I hadn't known Soeun was my daughter? What if I fell for her?* 

Baek Museong shuddered. That was something he didn't even want to imagine.

He set aside the tangle of complex emotions in his heart and grinned at his daughter. "Let's see each other again next time. Take care, Soeun."

As with all parent-child relationship issues, he knew this wasn't something he could rush.

{sep}

Crimson Tiger knew the moment he saw Baek Museong's face that the First Young Lord had discovered the truth about Soeun.

The man's aura had completely changed. He was no longer holding back his anger or forcing himself to speak civilly with his daughter's murderer.

Of course, the reason he let Baek Museong know that Soeun was alive was not to provoke him, but to get closer to him.

Right now, he needed to recalibrate his own position between the First Young Master and the Third Young Lady and ensure that he remained impartial to both sides. To that end, the time had come for him to have a small showdown with Baek Museong.

He seized the initiative, asking, "Have you met your daughter?"

Baek Museong stiffened, and his usual mask of composure slipped for a moment. "How did you know?" he demanded.

"Because I was the one who let your spy in on it."

Baek Museong's eyes widened. "What?"

Crimson Tiger explained calmly, "I'm the one who kidnapped your daughter and informed you about it."

Baek Museong blinked in confusion. *He's the one who gave Jin Chung's spy that piece of information?*

"Why did you do that?" he asked incredulously.

"Why did I do what? Are you asking why I disobeyed orders and spared your daughter? Or do you want to know why I informed you of that fact?"

"Both."

"The Third Young Lady came to see me and threatened to kill me if I didn't kidnap her."

"And why did you inform me that she was alive?" Baek Museong demanded, releasing his killing intent.

"Because you granted a lowly secret agent like me access to the Grand Archives. I thought I should let you know, even if it meant losing my life," Crimson Tiger lied without batting an eyelid. In truth, Soeun was the 90% of the reason why he decided to let the cat out of the bag, and the remaining 10% was because he was also the father of a daughter.

Baek Museong sighed and withdrew his killing intent. "I see. Thank you."

"......" Crimson Tiger relaxed a little. He could tell that Baek Museong believed him.

Baek Museong asked again, "But why did you give me this information through an Evil Coalition spy?"

"I need to deceive the Third Young Lady into thinking that the information was leaked during our escape."

"Oh, that makes sense."

"I deserve to die for deceiving you. Please punish me harshly."

Crimson Tiger knelt before Baek Museong and prostrated himself. He didn't find it humiliating at all. For Seohyeon's sake, he would gladly kneel a hundred, a thousand times if it meant he could survive this damn power struggle.

Baek Museong looked down for a moment before pulling him up. "No, thank you. Really. I mean it."

"Not at all. I only did what I had to do."

"Can I continue to trust you in the future?"

Crimson Tiger shook his head. "I'm just a sword-for-hire, and I've only ever lived for myself. It's better if you don't trust me. For my sake, too, please do not expect too much from me."

Baek Museong's face brightened.

Crimson Tiger rejoiced inwardly. It seemed that his humble words had the intended effect of making Baek Museong see him in an even more favorable light.

"I will arrange a separate occasion to reward you for today's matter," Baek Museong said.

The brief conversation over, Baek Museong turned around and left.

Crimson Tiger's gaze grew cold as he watched the First Young Lord disappear into the distance. "I will accept everything you give me," he whispered.

He felt as if something that had been plaguing him had been laid to rest. This was the most he could do for Soeun. If she were to die even after Baek Museong discovered the truth about her, then that was her fate.

He waited around a little longer, and finally, Yeon appeared.

"Good work!" he praised.

"Will he believe it?" Yeon asked anxiously.

"Is there any reason for him not to believe it?" Crimson Tiger shrugged casually. Ultimately, the only thing that mattered was that Baek Museong believed he had disobeyed the order because the Third Young Lady had threatened to kill him. As long as Seohyeon's existence remained hidden, all else was irrelevant.

"Oh, and there's good news."

"What is it?"

"I have narrowed down the suspects to three people."

"Three? Already?" Crimson Tiger observed Yeon carefully. Her face was marred by fatigue. She had probably been investigating without sleeping at all.

"I had all three of them watched by reliable spies."

"You've worked hard."

"Will the culprit really be among them?" Yeon's face clouded with worry. Despite her thorough investigation, during which she took only catnaps, she was not at all confident. What if she had been on the wrong track from the beginning?

"Let's hope so."

"Okay. Unfortunately, I also have bad news..."

"Bad news?"

Yeon sighed. "You've been assigned a mission. What should we do?"

"We just have to carry it out as usual," Crimson Tiger replied coolly. Finding the spy was important, but so was carrying out his own work.

If his foundation was shaken, everything would collapse. He couldn't risk anyone suspecting him of anything right now.

"Where is it?" he asked.

"Four days travel from here."

"That's quite far away. What's the mission about?"

Yeon handed Crimson Tiger the mission directive.

Crimson Tiger read it, then tilted his head. "Gwe Sansan? I've never heard that name before."

"That's only natural. We've only just recently identified this man."

"What does he do?"

"He's a black market merchant who deals in people."

"Deals in people...?" Crimson Tiger narrowed his eyes. "You mean... human trafficking?"

Yeon nodded. "Yes, and he's the worst of the lot."
