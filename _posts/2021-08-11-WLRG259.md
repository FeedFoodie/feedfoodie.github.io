CRACKLE.

Black lightning. It was a sound she could see, a sight she could hear. Baek Sogo instantly recoiled, throwing both arms up to defend herself.

KWAANG!

A heavy blow slammed into her, shaking her entire body. She gritted her teeth, straining to keep her footing as the force shoved her backward. The assault did not end with a single strike. A relentless series of attacks followed, one crashing into the next, until Baek Sogo was forced to abandon her stance.

Thrown back, she twisted her body in mid-air, rotating to absorb the momentum. Then, kicking off the empty air, she launched herself forward again.

"Are you from Heaven Beyond Heaven?" a voice asked, following the attack.

Baek Sogo chewed on her lower lip.

*As I thought, was I too complacent?*

This was the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the very heart of the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon's power, a man said to be the greatest master of the unorthodox sects. She had infiltrated under the cover of night, hoping to meet the Junior Disciple, but her presence was discovered before she could even clear the high wall.

"I've never heard of someone like you," the voice continued. "Then again, it's been a hundred years of fighting spirit since this one last grasped everyone in Heaven Beyond Heaven. In that time, a fellow called the Black Dragon Knight joined their ranks, so it's possible there's another master this one doesn't know about."

Beyond the darkness, the figure of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman became visible. He wore a ridiculous mask shaped like the wrinkled face of an old man and descended slowly from the sky with his hands clasped behind his back. Baek Sogo pressed a hand to her churning chest and glared up at him.

"It doesn't seem like you've come to assassinate this one," the Chairman observed, his masked face tilting as he studied her. A smile seemed to form behind the grotesque visage. "If that were the case, the Martial God must have gone senile. In that case… yes. Did you come not for me, but to kill my disciple? Since the Dark Supreme is dead. However, isn't that too arrogant? Did you really think it possible to assassinate my disciple, here, where I reside?"

"...It seems there's some misunderstanding," Baek Sogo replied, her gaze unwavering.

At her words, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a hearty laugh. "Yes. A misunderstanding, I'm sure. This one knows. It's because it might be a misunderstanding that I didn't try to kill you with that strike."

"...My name is Baek Sogo," she said, her guard remaining high.

The Chairman blinked behind his mask. After a moment lost in thought, a sound of realization escaped him. "Ah. Silent Flash Baek Sogo. I've heard that name before."

"The Junior Disciple… I came here to meet your disciple."

"So you're a guest," the Chairman chuckled. "A good thing I didn't kill you, then."

Baek Sogo's eyebrow twitched. A good thing he didn't kill her? She had barely managed to block his first attack. If another had followed with the same intent, she knew it would have been difficult to dodge or block.

Just as she was about to protest, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman shook his head. "Don't act so wronged. Even if this one had genuinely tried to kill you, you would have had a way to escape without dying." He looked at her then, his gaze passing over her white hair to settle on her eyes. He laughed again. "You've received Denir's favor. As have I, and as has my disciple. We all received Denir's Trial… but I can feel a more direct blessing from Denir in you. Are you an incarnation?"

"...Yes."

"I don't know why Denir chose you as an incarnation. Regardless, you did well not to use your power as one. If you had used it, this one would have killed you without hesitation." With that, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman turned around. "My foolish disciple hasn't returned yet. If you've come here to meet him, I'll give you a room to use."

"...Aren't you suspicious of me?"

"Is there any reason to be suspicious when you say you've come to meet my disciple?" the Chairman asked back without turning. "If you don't like it, you can refuse. I'm not forcing you."

Baek Sogo stared at his back as he walked away as if nothing had happened. After a moment's hesitation, she followed him.

Lee Sungmin covered his face with both hands, the cool surface of the mask pressing against his palms as he breathed slowly and deeply. It was the Ghost Mask, a gift from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, forged by the Fairy Queen Osla herself. The mask's purpose was to suppress the volatile Demonic Energy and inner arts churning within his body, and in their place, draw out the draconic power he had yet to properly master.

He had worn it continuously during the year he lived in Legro Forest.

*Demonic Energy is dangerous.*

Lee Sungmin had never forgotten that fact for a moment. He couldn't. Whenever his guard slipped even slightly, Void Monarch was there to remind him of the peril. His body had once twisted into that of a Yokai, a creature where reason and humanity were annihilated, where only monstrous instinct ran rampant. If he hadn't suppressed that nature and reclaimed himself in Urmuri, he would have been lost completely.

His body was that of a Yokai, his heart was that of a Dragon, and his mind was that of a human. It was a precarious balance, one that could collapse at any time if he used his Demonic Energy too excessively. The inhumanity he had painstakingly suppressed might rear its head again. The mask was a safeguard against that, drawing out the power of his Dragon Heart in place of the tainted inner arts, greatly reducing the risk of his monstrous nature lashing out.

Recently, however, it had been difficult to wear even if he wanted to. He had been using a human skin mask for disguise, and at some point, the Ghost Mask had become a symbol of his identity, much like the one worn by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The last time he wore it to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, he had caused a huge uproar. It had become too conspicuous to wear in public, relegated to the privacy of his room while he slept.

However, from here on, it was fine.

He had left the Tang Clan and, despite a brief encounter with the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique, had fortunately avoided any other trouble on his journey back to Haras.

[How long are you going to stay like this?] Void Monarch advised.

Lee Sungmin sighed and rose to his feet. The campfire was burning down to embers.

[He's being blatant about telling you to come quickly. Are you planning to ignore him and pretend you don't know?]

"He's not the type to let it go just because I ignore him."

[The Spear King will be different from the Dark Supreme,] Void Monarch said, responding to Lee Sungmin's mumble. [You were able to defeat the Dark Supreme without drawing out your Demonic Energy. However, the Spear King is a different story. He's a master several levels above the Dark Supreme. Of course, he's a piece of shit who can't compare to when this Elder was alive.]

"Yeah, you're so great."

[Anyway. If you fight the Spear King, you'll have no choice but to use Demonic Energy.]

"I know," Lee Sungmin muttered, taking off the mask. A blatant, powerful presence pulsed not far away. He had felt it once before. The Spear King. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue. "What bad luck. To think I'd run into the Spear King right as I'm about to reach my destination."

[You were the one who promised a next time.]

"I just didn't think 'next time' would be today."

[You've gotten more shameless. Is it because you lost your virginity?]

"What does that have to do with anything?"

[It's better that you're not just filled with gloom like before. Still, you've lost a lot of your cute side.]

"I don't think I was ever cute."

[You've started talking back more, too. You rotten bastard.]

Though he cursed, Void Monarch didn't seem particularly displeased. Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh, aware that his personality had indeed changed quite a bit. The year in Legro Forest, spent with Wiji Hoyeon and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, had been boisterous and noisy. The beings of the Fairy Realm had constantly approached him with pranks, leaving no room for boredom. He couldn't recall a time as lively as that year…

JOLT.

The death of the Crazy Heavenly Demon flashed in his mind, followed by the image of Rubia, who had disappeared. The smile vanished from Lee Sungmin's face. He quietly tucked the mask into his robes.

*What a difficult guy,* Void Monarch thought, clicking his tongue. He possessed a will stronger than steel, yet at times, he was as soft as tofu. The moment his thoughts touched upon those painful memories, the ones he didn't want to recall, his consciousness would be dyed black. Living in Lee Sungmin's mental world, Void Monarch had experienced this several times. He knew how dark and unpleasant this consciousness became when filled with despair, anger, and killing intent. From the depths of that darkness, he could hear the sound of a monster grinding its teeth. It was the sound of his suppressed inhumanity.

*You must not come out.* Void Monarch positioned himself in the center of Lee Sungmin's consciousness and raised his voice. [Even if you draw out too much Demonic Energy, this Elder is in the center of your consciousness. If a problem arises, I'll take care of it, so you don't need to worry too much.]

"I know," Lee Sungmin said, reaching for the spear he had set down. It floated up and settled into his hand. He kicked dirt over the campfire, extinguishing the last of the flames, and looked toward the Spear King's presence. "That's why I'm going. Without running away."

[Speak plainly, you bastard. It's that you don't have the confidence to escape, isn't it?]

"That's true too," Lee Sungmin admitted with a bitter smile.

The Spear King had chosen his ground well, a wide plain a fair distance from the gates of Haras. There were no nearby villages or wanderers to get caught in the crossfire. His own condition was perfect. He had eaten his fill, and there was no risk of needing to relieve himself mid-battle. A body perfectly controlled by inner arts would never face such an indignity, but the Spear King was a man who thoroughly eliminated every possible variable, just in case.

He hoped the commotion might even draw out the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. In fact, that was why he had deliberately waited here, so close to Haras.

The Spear King's fingers traced the shafts of the two short spears strapped to his back. He had fought many masters in his life. He had known defeat only once, at the hands of the Martial God, and a draw once, with the Moon Queen. What about today? Defeat? A draw? Victory? The Spear King held no certainty for any outcome, which made it all the more exciting.

"I was starting to think you wouldn't come."

Lee Sungmin landed a short distance from him. The Spear King greeted him with a broad smile.

"I considered waiting for you to find me, but then I realized you wouldn't know where I was, would you?"

"So you came all the way here?"

"In the North, our circumstances weren't right, so we postponed our fight. Today seems different, however. I am in perfect condition. I have no urgent business after this. Fighting you is my only purpose here."

"Even if I explained my situation, you wouldn't let me go, would you?"

"That's right," the Spear King said, nodding with a smile. "I heard you killed the Dark Supreme. How was he?"

"He was strong."

"There's no need to be polite to the dead. He's dead anyway. He won't hear whatever you say."

"I said he was strong."

"Weaker than you, I suppose," the Spear King laughed loudly. "And weaker than me, too."

The two short spears on his back shot into the sky. He raised both hands and caught them as they fell, twirling them lightly. His eyes scanned Lee Sungmin's armor and spear.

"That's a fine spear, and fine armor. I can guess how the Dark Supreme died. His hidden weapons are sharp, fast, and unconventional. However, they lack power. They probably couldn't pierce your armor."

"You're right."

"My spears are sharper, faster, and more unconventional than his hidden weapons, and their power is far greater. No matter how good your armor is, it will be difficult to block them, so keep that in mind."

"Why bother telling me that?"

"Because if you rely on it and let your guard down, you could die in a single blow," the Spear King said, striding forward. "It's been a while since I've fought a spearman. A fight with an outstanding spearman like you, even longer. That's why I don't want to end this easily or quickly. I plan to enjoy this as much as possible."

The fighting spirit of a monster mad for battle enveloped the space.

"Think of this not as a fight to the death," the Spear King shouted, his voice full of laughter, "but as a game."
