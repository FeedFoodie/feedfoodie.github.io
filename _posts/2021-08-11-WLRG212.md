It would be a mistake to think she had shed her weapon. While the Black Dragon Armor served as both offense and defense for Wiji Hoyeon, its use was not always a measure of her true power. For Wiji Hoyeon, the greatest weapon she possessed was her own body.

The spear shot forward without a sound.

It was the Deathly Flash, a technique from the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear focused so purely on extreme speed that it allowed for no variation. Or rather, it needed none, but Wiji Hoyeon knew the Deathly Flash. She knew all nine forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, and understanding an opponent's martial arts in their entirety provided a tremendous advantage in a fight like this.

*She knew. The Deathly Flash is fast. Its speed is its power, and it cannot be varied.* Yes, that would be true for a simple Deathly Flash, but he had mixed another martial art with it. Along the silent trajectory of the spear rode dozens of enhanced qi strands from the Bloody Pursuit of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, and just as they reached her, they erupted.

An eyebrow twitched on Wiji Hoyeon's face.

That was neither the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear nor the Violet Dawn Divine Art. That powerful enhanced qi belonged to a proper enhanced qi art.

*Right. Those two martial arts wouldn't be all he has.*

A swift, teleportation-like movement art. The way he scattered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art's enhanced qi in just two steps, was that part of it too? And now this enhanced qi art. It had been eleven years. He would not have focused solely on the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He must have learned other martial arts to supplement his weaknesses.

Wiji Hoyeon's hand moved.

PANG!

With a single motion, she scattered the dozens of qi strands and dropped into a lower stance.

WHOOSH!

Her body accelerated, closing the distance in an instant. She kicked off the ground and leaped right in front of Lee Sungmin, twisting in mid-air to swing her leg.

Rather than swing his spear, Lee Sungmin employed a movement art. Dozens of afterimages flickered into existence as he used One Step Dancing Shadow, and amidst the illusion, he executed Traceless Step to appear behind her.

It was an excellent technique, but it was not Spatial Movement. It merely distorted perception by moving quickly, a high-speed run to get behind her, and as long as she understood that, she would not be deceived. Wiji Hoyeon's swinging foot stepped on empty air.

She twisted her waist and flipped clean over Lee Sungmin's spear as it thrust from behind. Her hands opened wide, stretching towards him as the terrifying power of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art became a palm technique that rushed to meet him.

Though this was a duel, a single clean hit from either of them could prove fatal. Both Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon were repeating such attacks, yet there was no hesitation in their strikes. They trusted each other. They trusted the level of martial arts the other had reached.

Lee Sungmin held the spear with only his left hand. He had already thrust it, and retrieving it now would be too slow. Instead, the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art gathered in his right hand.

Blood Ring Shattering.

The enhanced qi shot straight ahead, colliding with Wiji Hoyeon's palm technique. Fragments of energy scattered in all directions as Lee Sungmin, having landed on the ground, rushed forward without hesitation. The thrust-out spear flickered and multiplied into dozens of copies.

It was Homing Chain Lightning.

Wiji Hoyeon stretched both hands forward without a hint of panic.

PABABAK!

Hand and spear collided. She deflected the incoming spears one by one, her body pushed back slightly with each impact as Lee Sungmin continued his advance. Shadowless Soul Severance and Two Step Destruction met the Blood Remnant Calamity Flame of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. A large burst of violet flames engulfed Wiji Hoyeon.

She retreated quickly, bringing her hands together in front of her chest. A jet-black sphere formed between her facing palms, and the slowly thrown orb met the Blood Remnant Calamity Flame.

KWAAAAANG!

A huge explosion swallowed the violet flames whole.

Lee Sungmin watched the approaching storm of enhanced qi and thrust his spear straight forward. The space around the weapon shimmered. Void Path. His lips moved, unleashing the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Taoist Void. The shimmering of space spread along the trajectory of the extended spear, creating a giant hole in the approaching wall of enhanced qi.

Lee Sungmin ran down that path.

Wiji Hoyeon watched him pierce through her attack and raised both her hands. Crimson enhanced qi swirled around her. The Void Path of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. She knew that form too. However, knowing a technique and executing it yourself were two different things. Even if she were to wield a spear and perform the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, she was not confident she could imitate Lee Sungmin's mastery.

"......"

PABABAK!

The enhanced qi gathered around Wiji Hoyeon turned into hundreds of awls that rushed towards Lee Sungmin. He did not fall behind, unleashing Homing Chain Lightning and Bloody Pursuit. The spear strikes shot down Wiji Hoyeon's awls one after another.

*He's gotten stronger.*

Wiji Hoyeon did not retreat. Instead, she walked forward, a smile playing on her lips. She knew Lee Sungmin had made her his goal. She had made it so. She had been an object of admiration, an ideal. That was in the past. Now, Lee Sungmin stood on equal footing with her.

No, perhaps he was even a little ahead.

*It's not a good feeling.*

However, it was enjoyable. Fighting him like this, right now. Her only friend, standing in the same place as her, or maybe even a little further ahead. Lee Sungmin had grown so much closer.

WOOONG-

The enhanced qi seething around Wiji Hoyeon subsided, condensing to coat her body in a thin, shimmering layer. Her eyes flashed.

*I do not want to lose.*

The feeling was strong. This was fun, but she did not want to be defeated.

*I want to win. I have to win.* Only then could she remain his goal. She wanted to remain a figure of admiration, an ideal. She wanted to keep walking ahead and look back at him following behind her. She did not want to be left behind.

Wiji Hoyeon's aura changed. If her previous offensive was a fighting spirit that pushed with power centered on her enhanced qi art, this was different. The first thing Lee Sungmin noticed was her speed. It was a speed that transcended the senses. Even with his sixth sense, he could not immediately follow her movements.

He had to forcefully twist the path of his spear.

KWAANG!

A heavy blow shook the weapon, pushing back both arms that held it.

[Isn't this getting into martial arts theory?] Void Monarch muttered.

Lee Sungmin agreed. He was currently fighting Wiji Hoyeon using purely inner arts, but anything more than this delved into the realm of martial arts theory. Haste. Strength. His lips moved, chanting the activation words for the magic. He was greatly benefiting from these basic buff spells he had learned on Mount Mushii. Although they continuously consumed his inner arts, his speed and strength increased without him needing to consciously focus, creating a great advantage in a fight against an equal opponent.

Wiji Hoyeon was startled. Lee Sungmin's movements, which had been unable to keep up with her speed, had suddenly changed. She had not expected him to be hiding this much power.

*Right, until now it must have been a probing battle.* She had done the same, holding back some of her power while observing him.

*However, if it's this much…*

No. It did not end with Haste and Strength.

Lee Sungmin drew upon his Demonic Energy, calling it forth through his breathing technique. His body, which had grown closer to that of a Yokai, began to squirm and react as he drew it out. The Black Heart beat, pumping out a seemingly endless amount of Demonic Energy. A cracking sound came from his muscles as his body began to seriously transform.

A murderous light ignited in his two golden-tinged pupils. The power that had overwhelmed the Sword Saint filled Lee Sungmin's entire body.

*Ah.*

Wiji Hoyeon felt something completely different from him now, something unlike the Lee Sungmin of just a moment ago. This was not Lee Sungmin, but something else entirely. A chill ran down her spine, and goosebumps rose on her skin. It was as if a ferocious monster stood before her with its jaws wide open.

"……"

She was not the only one who reacted. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who was watching the fight, felt his eyes sparkle. Osla, floating near him, also widened her eyes.

"Hey. Was he a Dragon, not a human?" she asked.

The Chairman let out a dry laugh and shook his head, though he understood why she would misunderstand.

"Or is he half-human, half-dragon?"

"I don't think so."

"That human. He used buff magic, didn't he?"

"Magic of that level isn't difficult to learn. Otherworlders get growth bonuses and can easily learn it as a skill."

"Hmm… Still, I can't quite accept it. How can a human use Dragon's Pressure?"

Dragon's Pressure was the embodiment of the arrogance and power of the Dragon race. Dragons, called the apex of species, could exert intense pressure on beings lesser than themselves just by existing. It was not perfect, but the current Lee Sungmin was emitting a form of it. The technique he had used at the Namgung Clan, the one he thought of as soulmates, had also been mixed with that same Pressure.

"Dragon's Pressure, magic, martial arts, and on top of that, Demonic Energy," the Chairman said with another dry laugh. "It's amazing that his body hasn't collapsed despite having such a jumble of things mixed together."

"That's why I asked. If he's really human."

"He's human."

"Hmm…" Osla's eyes narrowed.

The Chairman glanced at her. "Are you interested?"

"It's because I've never seen a human like that before, and… that human. I feel something unsettling about him, beyond what the Coalition Leader mentioned."

"Like what?"

"Something very ominous…" Osla muttered. Even she could not identify what she was sensing from him, so she could only speak vaguely.

{sep}

For just a brief moment, Wiji Hoyeon had been frozen by the goosebumps. She shook it off with a deep breath and immediately rushed at Lee Sungmin once more. His golden eyes tracked her movement.

PAAAAANG!

As Lee Sungmin swung his spear, the air tore with a deafening sound. Wiji Hoyeon, who was closing in from the side, was startled and raised her hand to block.

Collision.

Her body was sent flying backward, a surprised expression on her face at the power that was beyond her imagination. She balanced her flying body, however, and spun around in mid-air. She stood upside down in the empty air, looking down at Lee Sungmin.

*The power has changed. What in the world is this?*

She was full of suspicion, but it was not something she could not handle.

GOOOOO-

A black mist began to drift over the condensed protective qi aura surrounding her body. If she faced him with a half-hearted mindset, she would be defeated without even being able to counterattack. She hated that. Because she did not want to lose. Slowly, the upside-down Wiji Hoyeon descended.

It was a martial art he had never seen before, but his instincts screamed a warning. Lee Sungmin shot his spear, wreathed in Demonic Energy and enhanced qi, at her. A tremendous force swept over Wiji Hoyeon. Wrapped in the black mist, she extended her left hand wide to meet the approaching force.

The mist enveloping her body became flowers. It gathered into buds, then bloomed fully as petals of enhanced qi fluttered around her. Lee Sungmin's attack only managed to crush a few of the flowers created by her gesture before it could advance no further.

"For one year," Wiji Hoyeon said, her voice calm, "I was under a curse and couldn't move my body, but that doesn't mean I was just playing around. I meditated and thought. About how I could become stronger."

This martial art was incomplete.

"You're the first opponent I'm using this martial art against." A small laugh escaped her lips. "You get to be my first this time, too."

Black flowers bloomed.

With countless flowers at her back, Wiji Hoyeon was more beautiful than anyone Lee Sungmin had ever seen. It was not just simple beauty. Those flowers, beautiful enough to take one's breath away, were all crystals of enhanced qi. He had encountered numerous forms of enhanced qi, but he had never seen an art so well-tuned and beautiful.

Space itself was trembling. The flowers of enhanced qi created by Wiji Hoyeon were gradually expanding their domain.

When she fought the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, she had not used this martial art. Did she not have a chance? No, it must have been incomplete. So incomplete she could not use it.

*A calamity is a calamity, I see.* The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman snickered. *Did that defeat give her some inspiration? This is why I hate calamities. There is no fun in raising them and no fun in watching them.*

He revised his evaluation of Wiji Hoyeon, placing her a little higher than the Ten Exchanges Match. That martial art looked like an enhanced qi art, but it contained the essence of enlightenment that had reached the Transcendent Realm. It interfered with space, planting seeds of enhanced qi within space itself and making them bloom.

[She's a girl who uses interesting tricks,] Void Monarch laughed heartily.

Wiji Hoyeon was approaching.
