The memory of meeting Frau in Urmuri surfaced, bringing with it the first time he had ever heard of the Destiny Force. Back then, Lee Sungmin hadn't properly understood its nature, but that ignorance was long gone. He now knew with chilling certainty that the power Frau had described was destined to bring about The End in this world.

"Who told you that?" Frau demanded. "That Kim Jonghyun is one of The End."

"The Vampire Queen."

"If it came from her, I'm inclined to believe it," Frau sneered. "Did she say she saw it with her Eye of Futuresight?"

Lee Sungmin simply nodded.

"Damn it," Frau spat, the curse sharp in the confines of the carriage. He knew of the Vampire Queen's Eye of Futuresight. Among the countless vampires, only their Queen possessed the All-Seeing Eye. "And what if she was lying?"

"What could she possibly gain from lying about something like this?" Abel asked back with a dry laugh. "Besides, even if she's wrong, Kim Jonghyun's actions have already crossed the line. It took less than a day for him to topple the city of Germud. He killed tens of thousands of people in a single day, and he has one of the Black Stars, the Death Lord, at his side."

"So you dragged me all the way out here to fight that madman," Frau grumbled.

"I'm not asking you to fight to the death. Just lend a hand where you can."

"That's easy for you to say."

Though his expression was soured with displeasure, Frau didn't back out. A promise bound him, leaving him no choice but to honor Abel's request. He stared out the window, his voice a low mutter. "It's strange."

"What do you mean?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Frau let out a deep sigh. "There's an ominous feeling flowing through the city. It's… unusual. I take it you two can't see it."

Their fields of study were far too different. Lee Sungmin had learned martial arts and Abel had mastered magic, but neither of them could perceive what Frau, a shaman, was seeing so clearly.

"The souls are stagnant."

"...Stagnant?" Lee Sungmin repeated, the word foreign to him. He had never in his life heard the phrase 'souls are stagnant'.

"Kim Jonghyun turned the townspeople into Undead, that's for sure," Frau continued. "Before you guys got here, an advance party took a peek beyond the gates. They said the place was crawling with Undead right from the entrance. Low-quality zombies, mostly."

"...Hmm." Abel's brow furrowed. He seemed to grasp what Frau found so strange, but Lee Sungmin remained lost. He was truly ignorant when it came to such matters.

Seeing his confusion, Abel stepped in to explain, as Frau clearly had no intention of doing so. "To become an Undead means your soul is offered as a sacrifice to a Demon Lord by a Black Mage. When the soul is offered, a Demon Lord's power imbues the corpse, and that's how an Undead is born. The class of the Undead is determined by the soul's value, the Black Mage's effort, and the amount of power the Demon Lord grants. Most human souls aren't very valuable, and it's inefficient for a Black Mage to personally intervene just to raise their class. So, in the end, most of them become zombies."

"...Ah."

Understanding dawned on Lee Sungmin. It was certainly strange. If becoming an Undead meant one's soul was offered to a Demon Lord, then the soul should no longer remain in this world, yet Frau had described the souls as stagnant.

"It's true that zombies are wandering around," Abel grumbled, picking up the thread of the conversation. "But in that city, countless souls of the dead are just… stagnant. That bastard Kim Jonghyun… it looks like he's trying to pull something again."

"Can you guess what it is?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Abel shook his head. "I don't know. The magic of the Grimoire has never been deciphered. Even if Gries is its twin, the magic they contain is too different. I can't figure out his scheme. Why he occupied Germud only to sit still. What on earth he's aiming for, leading a legion of Death Knights."

"You helpless bastard," Frau spat at Abel's muttering. "I thought you had some kind of plan when you called for me."

"I am glad you came. Your Ghost Soul Art will be helpful."

"Son of a bitch."

Though Abel's words were meant to lighten the mood, Frau glared at him with furious eyes. He shot to his feet and threw open the carriage door, then reached over and smacked the back of Aladur's slumped head. "It's not like we're storming the city right now," he spat. "If I sit here facing you any longer, I think I'll really want to kill you, so I'm going back to my carriage."

THUD.

The door slammed shut. Abel merely shrugged his shoulders. "His mouth is foul, but his skills are certain. He's at what you could call the pinnacle of the shaman tribe. By the way, how did you get to know him?"

"I met him in Urmuri a few years ago."

As Frau had said, they weren't entering the city immediately. Giving Kim Jonghyun more time was unsettling, but they still needed to make at least minimal preparations.

{sep}

As night fell over the plains, the subjugation force of thousands made camp. They settled in beyond the city walls, where tens of thousands had died only to rise again as undead.

A crowd of Holy Knights and Junior Disciples gathered around the carriage of Teresa, the Saint of the Church. Even those unaffiliated with her order drew near, not wanting to miss the chance to see the Saint in person. There were no grand speeches or sermons. Instead, in front of everyone, Teresa simply prayed for the souls of those who had died in the city.

It was a meaningless act. Her prayers would not help the stagnant souls ascend, nor could they destroy the Undead.

Following Abel's advice that they needed to prepare, Lee Sungmin had excused himself. He put on his mask and began to wander the perimeter of the camp. From the shadows, a few pairs of eyes stared at him. They belonged to those who knew his moniker, 'the Phantom Spear', but they weren't the only ones watching.

[The Yokai are taking an interest in you.]

The Void Monarch chuckled in his mind.

[To them, a being like you must seem very strange. A half-yokai, should I call you? And for a half-breed, you possess an absurd amount of Demonic Energy.]

*Not that you'd lose to them, of course,* the Void Monarch added with another laugh.

[Watch your back. The easiest way for a Yokai to increase its Demonic Energy is to devour a Yokai stronger than itself.]

[It's been a while.]

Lee Sungmin's steps halted. A new voice cut through the Void Monarch's cackling. He flinched, his face stiffening as he recognized its owner, and his eyes darted around the darkness.

[It's useless to try and find me. I'm currently observing you one-sidedly.]

*Kim Jonghyun.*

The voice belonged to the very man who was plotting some unknown scheme inside Germud. He had contacted Lee Sungmin.

[It's difficult to have a conversation like this. What do you say? Please come into the city. I will come out to greet you.]

*What is he plotting?* Lee Sungmin clenched and unclenched his fist. A trap? It seemed too blatant, and why would Kim Jonghyun want to meet him in the first place? He couldn't understand it at all.

[Given my circumstances… I apologize for not being able to go to you myself. I also fully understand that you don't trust me. I wouldn't trust me either if I were you. Therefore, I will swear an oath. I will absolutely not harm you during this meeting.]

It was a one-sided surveillance, a one-sided conversation. Lee Sungmin wanted to reply, but he had no way to send an answer. He headed for a deserted area and, after confirming no one was around, opened his mouth.

"Why do you want to see me?"

[Because there is something I wish to discuss with you.]

"Seems like there'd be no problem talking like this, though?"

[Wouldn't that be too impersonal? I assure you, it won't be a bad proposal for you.]

With that, Kim Jonghyun fell silent. He had extended an invitation, but he didn't press the issue. He couldn't. He had no means of threatening Lee Sungmin.

[What will you do?] the Void Monarch asked.

*Should I consult with Abel?* Lee Sungmin glanced toward Abel's carriage, an action intended for Kim Jonghyun to see. As he expected, a reply came immediately.

[By yourself. Please come by your own choice.]

Could he trust Kim Jonghyun? That was the first question. To be honest, Kim Jonghyun had never once directly harmed him. If anything, he had helped him a few times, yet, looking at the state of Eria, Kim Jonghyun was a monster. He had massacred thousands in the North and tens of thousands here in Germud. He had murdered Arbes, the Sage of Two Faces, and was the master of the Grimoire, the most dangerous magic tome in existence. He was a human who had become a Quasi-Demon King.

That was Kim Jonghyun, yet he had never harmed Lee Sungmin.

Still, that didn't mean he trusted him. He was the first calamity of The End, and he was plotting something in that city right now. Would an oath from a Quasi-Demon King even be effective?

*Perhaps this is an opportunity.*

Lee Sungmin's figure vanished into the darkness. Channeling the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder into his steps, he arrived before the city wall in an instant. He kicked off the ground, leaping onto the wall in a single, fluid motion. He couldn't unconditionally believe the oath, but this was a chance to meet Kim Jonghyun alone. If luck was on his side, he might not only grasp the man's true intentions but also find an opportunity to kill him. Yes. If he could just kill Kim Jonghyun.

He dropped down over the wall. The simple act of crossing from outside to inside changed everything. The very air grew thick and foul. A nauseating stench, one he knew all too well, assaulted his senses. It was the smell of rotting corpses. Lee Sungmin frowned as he landed, the unlit city as black as pitch around him.

[This way.]

Kim Jonghyun's voice echoed just before his feet touched the ground.

FWHOOSH.

Orbs of light flared to life along the dark street. The wandering zombies did not approach, but instead twisted their bodies and lowered their stances as if in fear of the light. The glowing trail led deeper into the town.

Lee Sungmin did not hurry. He took out his spear and removed his mask, preparing for any contingency as he slowly advanced. He searched for the presence of the Death Knights but felt nothing. There were only zombies here.

The lights led him to the largest mansion in town, its door swinging open on its own.

"Have you eaten?" Kim Jonghyun's voice drifted from inside.

He was sitting in a spacious dining room, a friendly smile on his face as he looked at Lee Sungmin. Instead of his usual robe, he wore a simple white shirt. Lee Sungmin's gaze swept over the lavishly set table. He hadn't eaten, but he had no desire to dine with Kim Jonghyun.

"Honestly, I'm surprised," Kim Jonghyun said. "I didn't think you would be here."

Lee Sungmin didn't answer. Looking at that affable smile, it was hard to believe this was the man responsible for slaughtering tens of thousands. He remembered their first meeting. Kim Jonghyun had worn that same smile in a room filled with floating, severed organs. Lee Sungmin slowly looked around, his grip tightening on his spear.

"However," Kim Jonghyun continued, "the fact that you are here has given me confidence. You are..."

His words were cut short.

The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was Extreme Speed. When unleashed with intent, the movement itself was invisible. Though he hadn't achieved Body Reformation, Lee Sungmin had inherited the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the essence of the technique remained intact in his mind.

KWWAAANG!

The air itself seemed to shatter with a deafening roar. Space warped around the point of his spear, which he slowly lowered, his eyes fixed on the impossible sight before him.

"That's a rough greeting," Kim Jonghyun said with a bitter smile.

He had unleashed the attack with all his might, yet Kim Jonghyun sat there without a single scratch.

"I didn't seek a private meeting with you recklessly. I've prepared at least some means to protect myself."

"Why did you want to see me?" Lee Sungmin demanded, raising his spear again to aim it at Kim Jonghyun's heart.

The smile never left Kim Jonghyun's face. "I wanted to make you a proposal."

"What kind?"

"A proposal to become comrades." Kim Jonghyun's smile deepened.
