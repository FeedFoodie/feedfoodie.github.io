"Wait, Junior Disciple! What in the world are you saying?"

"Attacking together won't be enough to do anything to that monster," Lee Sungmin said, his voice grim. "You know that as well as I do, Senior Sister."

He did not hesitate to call the creature a monster, even though it wore the face of Wiji Hoyeon. That thing was not her. It only possessed her appearance and mimicked her strength, which made it nothing more than a monster. Then again, the true power Wiji Hoyeon wielded was far too great to be described by such a simple word.

"But if we fight together, our chances of survival are higher! Why are you telling me to run away?"

"If we're going to discuss possibilities," Lee Sungmin began, slowly raising the spear he held in both hands. He knew he could not persuade Baek Sogo. No matter what he said, she would never run away alone, not while he was here. Even if it were someone else in his place, Baek Sogo would not flee. She would always find a way to justify her actions with those words she used like an excuse, that she was 'a good person'.

The Wiji Hoyeon Doppelganger simply stared at them, its form still and silent. Lee Sungmin could not imagine it possessed reason or a desire to talk, but he was grateful the monster was not attacking them outright.

"I made a hole in the back," he said. "Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu should be on the other side. Please, bring them here."

"But Junior Disciple…!"

"If we run, it will chase us, and no matter how fast our movement arts are, that monster is probably faster."

Baek Sogo could not refute his words. Her Dancing Shadow Soul Break was a physical technique that aimed for the pinnacle of speed and illusion, yet the display from Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger had been overwhelming. She could not imagine a creature with such power would be slow. If they ran, it would chase them, and they would be caught.

"You're faster than me, Senior Sister," Lee Sungmin said as he drew upon the Violet Dawn Divine Art. "And I can last a little longer against that monster than you can."

"What if you die?"

"Who would want to die? Whether I live or die is up to you, Senior Sister."

He was forcing the responsibility onto her. Just as Baek Sogo opened her mouth to argue, Lee Sungmin stopped listening. He executed the steps of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and dashed forward. The Doppelganger, which had been standing idly, finally reacted. The Black Dragon Armor swaying around its shoulders swelled and swung at him. To say it merely 'swung' was an understatement. The power contained in that single blow was enough to tear through a Protective Qi Aura and crush a body to pulp.

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and deployed his own Protective Qi Aura with all his might. At the same time, he unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear to counter the Black Dragon Armor's attack.

KWWAAAAANG!

The mere impact felt as if it were shattering both his arms, though it was only a feeling.

*I… endured it…!*

He heard the sound of Baek Sogo kicking off the ground behind him. Her presence faded swiftly into the distance. She must have understood. Fighting together would only buy them a little more time before they were annihilated. She must have concluded that bringing reinforcements was their only real chance of survival.

*It won't take long. At most, five minutes for her to get to the hole and come back.*

That timeframe might seem slow for someone walking or running, but with a movement art, it was a reasonable estimate. He did not think Blood Granny would have killed Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu and survived. Although Chwi Geol's left arm had been severed, he was still an outstanding master, and Jang Deuksu had also reached the Supreme Peak. The only reason Lee Sungmin had not joined the attack on Blood Granny was to save time, not because he lacked the confidence to defeat her.

*Even with all sorts of variables, she should be back in ten minutes.*

He knew this prediction was full of holes. There was a chance Blood Granny was still alive, though she would not be unscathed. He could not imagine Jang Deuksu and Chwi Geol being defeated so easily. If that had happened, Baek Sogo would be able to finish off a Blood Granny exhausted from battle.

And after that? If Baek Sogo returned here alone, nothing would change.

Then there was the other possibility, that he would not be able to hold out until she returned. What would Baek Sogo do then? What if she came back only to find his corpse? Even if Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu joined them, could they survive against that monster?

He did not know. He just did not know. There were too many holes in his plan, but it was the best option he had. He had never imagined he would run into Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger here.

His body staggered backward from the force, and he barely kept his feet. Tensing his spine, he flung his upper body forward, using the momentum to launch himself away. Both his hands throbbed as if they were torn, but he could not stop. He did not want to die. Wasn't that obvious?

He wanted to live. To live, he had to fight, and he had to endure.

The Black Dragon Armor lunged at him again. Since it was ultimately cloth, the attack trajectories it created were unpredictable, moving freely between straight and curved lines. Its form was just as fluid. The large swath of armor rolled up, transforming into a sharp awl as it stabbed toward him.

*Remember.*

Twenty-one hundred years. That was the time Lee Sungmin had spent training in the Mental World. The training there had been tedious, so tedious that he had gone mad several times. Other than the falling grains of sand, there was no way to know how much time had passed. During that long expanse, he had honed his martial arts. There was a clear division between what his body could perform and what it could not, a huge difference between the martial arts his mind had experienced and what the current Lee Sungmin could execute.

What he needed to recall and use was not the martial arts themselves. It was the experience.

At first, it had just been a way to pass the time. He had started by imagining things, tired of swinging his spear and moving his body in the empty void. He honed his martial arts by picturing an unseen opponent. *If I thrust my spear like this, they'll move like that, right? No, would moving this way be more efficient? What kind of attack would come from that evasion? The form of the attack? The movement arts? How should I respond? Should I dodge? What if I can't dodge? Should I counter? What if it's too strong to counter?*

*Then should I just die?*

The spear he swung sideways connected with the Black Dragon Armor. In terms of raw power, his spear was no match for it. He knew that, so he had no intention of engaging in a battle of strength. A direct thrust is weak from the side. If a force is too strong to confront, you deflect it or use it to your advantage.

These were things Wiji Hoyeon had taught him, things he knew but could not use properly. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear forward. It seemed as if it would connect with the Black Dragon Armor, but it only grazed the surface. As he thrust, he turned his body to the side, all in an instant. The Black Dragon Armor narrowly missed him, and the tip of his spear aimed for the Doppelganger's chest.

The Doppelganger had no emotions. It was not surprised or flustered. It simply reacted with precision to what it saw. It saw his spearhead thrusting toward it and moved the Black Dragon Armor to defend that spot.

The memories were vivid. The things he had done for twenty-one hundred years in the Mental World never faded. There were just so many of them that he had to consciously recall them, like struggling to retrieve a memory one does not often use. He had become completely absorbed in the imagining he had started to pass the time. Aware that he lacked real combat experience, he had trained as if in a duel, imagining an unseen opponent to supplement it. The imagined opponents always changed. Sword, spear, axe, bow, mage, fists, feet, and so on. He had mobilized his poor imagination to its fullest, and fortunately, he had more than enough time.

He could not pierce the defending Black Dragon Armor. Against a sturdy opponent, energy release, especially penetrating force, was effective, but could a penetrating force of his level break through the Doppelganger's defense? *I have to try.* Enhanced qi gathered at the tip of his spear. The strike, imbued with rotation, collided with the Black Dragon Armor.

KWAANG!

With a heavy impact, the armor was pushed back. Still, there was no surprise on the Doppelganger's face. The writhing cloth expanded greatly and tried to engulf Lee Sungmin's body. He readjusted his grip on the spear and swung it wide. The air burst as enhanced qi exploded outward, pushing the Black Dragon Armor back a little.

[Do you know?] Void Monarch spoke.

*I know,* Lee Sungmin answered in his mind.

The Doppelganger's attacks were ridiculously simple. Its entire offense consisted of merely swinging the Black Dragon Armor, and its defense and evasion were not particularly outstanding either.

[That thing. It's not using any martial arts. It's sticking to simple, brutish methods.]

Baek Sogo's Doppelganger had used the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, but Wiji Hoyeon's was not.

[I can't be sure that the Doppelgangers in this Dungeon are the same, but most tend to imitate the original. If the Doppelgangers here are also like that, then not using martial arts must be an imitation of the original.]

Sticking to simple, brutish methods was an imitation of Wiji Hoyeon? He could not focus on that question. The Black Dragon Armor began to move again, and Lee Sungmin focused on his actions.

Homing Chain Lightning. The spear tassel shook as dozens of thrusts descended upon the Doppelganger. The Black Dragon Armor writhed and parried every strike. From there, he took a step forward with Traceless Step, digging into the Doppelganger's side and thrusting with Soul Chasing Elimination. The armor whipped back and blocked the attack. Even with penetrating force added, he could not break through.

*What about Serpent Soul Devouring?*

The spear vanished from his hands. It shot up from below, trying to penetrate a gap in the Black Dragon Armor, but this did not work either. The widely spread cloth blocked the spear tip. He could not pierce the Black Dragon Armor even with the first three forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

*There are no blind spots.*

The Black Dragon Armor was large enough to envelop its entire body, its length and width changing freely. Originally, it was not a particularly powerful artifact. To change its form, maintain it, and use it so freely for offense and defense required an enormous amount of inner arts and Taoist proficiency.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear consisted of nine forms. So far, Lee Sungmin had only used three of them. Soul Chasing Elimination, Homing Chain Lightning, and Serpent Soul Devouring. The subsequent forms consumed a great deal of inner arts, and the level of martial arts he had reached was insufficient to perform them.

However, that was no longer the case. Lee Sungmin drew upon the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The enhanced qi imbued in his spear began to swell greatly. The Doppelganger, which had been staring blankly at it, moved its hand. It still was not using any martial arts.

The spear moved back, then forward again.

The enhanced qi exploded. The unleashed strike split into nine. Composed purely of enhanced qi, they took the form of dragons with their mouths wide open. The fourth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Nine Dragons Slaughter. Nine dragons swallowed the Doppelganger. The floor, walls, and ceiling, all made of flesh, were gouged out.

KWWAKWAKWAKWA!

Nine dragons swept through the area in front of him. Lee Sungmin's hand, which had launched the spear, trembled as he looked forward.

The Doppelganger had taken only a few steps back. The pushed-back flesh clung messily to its feet. It had not dodged. It had blocked it. It took the Nine Dragons Slaughter head-on and was only pushed back a little. Lee Sungmin felt a bit empty, but he was not discouraged.

Right. A thing imitating Wiji Hoyeon should be at least this strong.

He chuckled, feeling a strange sense of satisfaction. The Doppelganger, which had been staring at him, set down the Black Dragon Armor. The armor lay limply on the floor. As the creature strode forward, a jet-black enhanced qi enveloped its entire body. It was too sinister and immense to be called a Protective Qi Aura.

[Indeed,] Void Monarch laughed heartily. [It seems it was just playing around until now.]

Lee Sungmin felt that keenly as well. Even though the Doppelganger had removed its Black Dragon Armor, losing its weapon and its shield, it felt even more menacing than before.

The Doppelganger executed a movement art. It was instantaneous. Lee Sungmin lost track of it with both his sight and his senses. Even his sixth sense could not detect its movement.

[Behind you.]

If Void Monarch had not spat out the words, it would have been over. Lee Sungmin hurriedly turned and swung his spear.

Pain exploded through him as he was sent flying, and a single thought cut through the agony.

*Senior Sister, don't come back…*

This thing is a real monster.
