The Murim Alliance drew its strength from the Nine Sects One Gang and the Four Great Clans. In contrast, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance found its core in the Six Demonic Gates and the Three Demonic Clans.

Despite an existence as long and storied as the Murim Alliance, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance held little sway in the current world. Its very composition of unorthodox sects ensured a poor public perception. While the prestigious clans and sects of the Nine Sects One Gang were scattered across Eria, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's key powers were all clustered near the alliance headquarters in Haras.

The reason for this concentration of power, and their diminished influence, could be laid at the feet of one man: the current Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, who had remained silent for a very long time. He was a master who could contend for the title of world number one, yet he seldom involved himself in external affairs. As a result, the unorthodox sects, persecuted by the Murim Alliance and others, had little choice but to gather around Haras for protection.

[What an irresponsible bastard,] Void Monarch grumbled.

Lee Sungmin found himself agreeing. The Chairman's irresponsibility extended to the present situation. He claimed to have made arrangements, but his instructions had been vague at best. He had simply told Lee Sungmin to go to the headquarters, reveal himself as the Chairman's disciple, and let them handle the rest.

*He said I had to earn their acknowledgment. This is going to be a pain.*

[Not necessarily,] Void Monarch chuckled at Lee Sungmin's silent mumble. [You're no run-of-the-mill martial artist. A master of your caliber will command their respect. They'll see you and bow their heads without a word.]

He had a point. No matter how large the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was, the Chairman was its only master in the Transcendent Realm. Though there was a significant gap in skill between them, in an alliance without its leader, Lee Sungmin's martial might was absolute.

Haras, the stronghold of the unorthodox sects, was in many ways a different city than Lee Sungmin had imagined. He had pictured a desolate place littered with corpses, much like Trabia in the North, but the city before him was not lawless. It had its own set of rules and was not so different from the cities that housed the Nine Sects One Gang or a Magic Tower. Not everyone here was a demonic practitioner.

Lee Sungmin recalled something he had heard years ago. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was indeed a gathering of unorthodox sects, but not all its members were vicious demonic leaders. They were simply sharp points that refused to be controlled.

In truth, how different was that from the orthodox sects? He was certain there were plenty of people among them who acted virtuous while committing heinous acts behind the scenes. The only real difference was whether they did it openly or not.

A palpable tension pervaded the city, growing stronger as he moved from the outskirts toward the center. Many people walked the streets, armed and exuding menacing auras. Knowing his appearance would inevitably draw attention, he had already removed his mask and wide-brimmed hat, choosing to walk with his face uncovered.

He knew exactly why the city was on edge.

It was because of him.

Before entering the city, Lee Sungmin had purchased information about its recent happenings through Erebrisa.

*So irresponsible.*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had dropped a bomb into the silent city of Haras, and that bomb was Lee Sungmin. After nearly a hundred years of seclusion, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon had sent his successor to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. It was a bomb powerful enough to shatter the city's silence and unnerve those who had enjoyed power during the Chairman's long absence.

Chief among them was the head of the Pae Clan, one of the Three Demonic Clans: Blood Lord Pae Munhu. As the alliance's second-in-command, he had made the city his own while the Chairman was away, but no matter how outstanding a master Pae Munhu was, or how high the prestige of the Pae Clan, his power existed only in the Chairman's absence. Such was the great and lofty name of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon in this city.

[Well, I doubt they'll be openly hostile… but judging by this atmosphere, you won't be getting a warm welcome,] Void Monarch cackled.

He was right. To men like Pae Munhu, who had enjoyed everything the Chairman's absence afforded them, Lee Sungmin was an unwelcome intrusion.

Lee Sungmin's fingers brushed against the letter from the Chairman tucked inside his robes, the one he was told to open when he met the Blood Lord. He understood his position. Reluctant as he was, he had no intention of ignoring his master's words. Besides, the Chairman hadn't asked much of him. Becoming the successor didn't mean he had to get entangled in the alliance's internal politics. The Chairman had already promised that if such a situation arose, he could simply ignore it.

Lee Sungmin knew it wasn't that simple.

*Because my existence will become known to the world.*

This was a way to announce that the silent Chairman was still a force to be reckoned with, and a declaration of war against the Murim Alliance. The world would soon know that the Phantom Spear, a man the Murim Alliance had branded a demonic practitioner, had become the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The long-dormant fuse between the two great powers might finally be lit.

*That isn't so bad,* Lee Sungmin thought.

Behind the Murim Alliance stood Heaven Beyond Heaven. A conflict would force its Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, to act, which was the same as Heaven Beyond Heaven making a move. By killing the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One, Lee Sungmin had already made himself their enemy. Rather than wait for them to come for him, it was better to strike first with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's backing.

With this understanding, Lee Sungmin did not hesitate.

He strode toward the large main gate without breaking his pace. The gatekeepers blocking his path started to speak, but he paid them no mind. With every step he took, a threatening Dragon's Pressure rolled off his body. The gatekeepers' faces turned pale, their legs trembling uncontrollably.

"Wh-who…" one of them stammered, but Lee Sungmin ignored the question.

He stopped before the gate and unleashed the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

CRACKLE!

Purple lightning erupted around him as his body vaulted over the closed gate, landing inside the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's compound. It was a scene reminiscent of his visit to the Namgung Clan. Martial artists gathered, but before they could even think to act, the sheer force of his aura overwhelmed them, freezing them in place.

"Is the Blood Lord here?" Lee Sungmin's voice cut through the silence as he looked around. The subdued warriors could only hesitate, unable to speak.

Just then, men dressed in pitch-black martial arts uniforms rushed in. They surrounded Lee Sungmin from a distance but showed no open hostility.

"…Are you the successor to the Coalition Leader?" the most skilled among them stepped forward to ask.

Lee Sungmin gave a slow nod. At his confirmation, the men's faces hardened. They hesitated for a moment before bowing their heads deeply.

"Please come this way."

"And who is waiting for me?"

"The Blood Lord… is there," the man replied.

Lee Sungmin gave a bitter smile. He had come as the Chairman's successor, yet the Blood Lord himself hadn't come out. Instead, he was being summoned. Lee Sungmin knew he couldn't just follow them silently. To avoid being pushed around later, he had to establish the hierarchy from the very beginning.

"So the Blood Lord summoned me, and I'm supposed to just go to him?" His words were spoken without emotion, which only made them sound more chilling.

The man hastily tried to explain, but Lee Sungmin shook his head, cutting him off. Without even drawing his spear, he raised a hand.

CRACKLE!

Purple currents sparked as his hand was engulfed in light. Though the color was different, it was unmistakably the Chairman's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. The man's jaw dropped at the sight.

"Th-that's… the Coalition Leader's…"

"Heaven Reversal Black Thunder," Lee Sungmin finished for him, his open palm facing the man.

He didn't fire it, but the man knew instinctively that no one present could withstand that technique.

Lee Sungmin narrowed his eyes. "It wouldn't be difficult to kill all of you."

"You're the Coalition Leader's successor… and you're threatening to kill us?"

"My Master said nothing about what to do with your lives. He only told me to come here and earn your acknowledgment." It was the truth. "I don't know what he meant by that, but I think a show of strength is the fastest way, and I don't like this. I am the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Should someone like me have to walk to the Blood Lord, who isn't even my Master?"

"Th-that's…"

"Tell the Blood Lord," Lee Sungmin's voice filled with power. "If he doesn't come out right now, I will annihilate the Pae Clan first."

It was an incredibly arrogant threat, but the overwhelming pressure radiating from Lee Sungmin made everyone realize his words were neither a lie nor a bluff, and though his words were extreme, he meant every one of them. He had come all this way, and he would establish the hierarchy clearly.

"I underst…"

"Indeed."

The man began to reply, but a deep voice echoed through the area. Lee Sungmin glanced toward the source of the voice. A large-framed man was striding toward them. Though he had never seen him in person, Lee Sungmin knew this was the Blood Lord, Pae Munhu, second-in-command of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

"The Coalition Leader's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder… Just seeing it makes my body tremble." Pae Munhu stopped a short distance away. Behind him stood a group of heavily armed masters, all wearing the same insignia. They were clearly the elite of the Pae Clan. "I heard about you from the Coalition Leader, but he didn't share the details. I am Pae Munhu, head of the Pae Clan. Who might you be?"

"The Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin," he answered, making no attempt to hide his identity.

His words sent a ripple through the crowd. The expressions of Pae Munhu and the other warriors changed instantly.

"The Phantom Spear…"

"Isn't he the one who killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear and Moyong Seojin?"

"The Phantom Spear is the Coalition Leader's successor…?"

Murmurs spread through the warriors. Pae Munhu was visibly taken aback, but he quickly composed himself. "Did the Coalition Leader not come with you?"

"That's right."

"First… it seems there's been a misunderstanding. I have no intention of being hostile. I was merely curious about the kind of person our leader's successor is…"

"Is that why you brought armed troops?" Lee Sungmin sneered.

Pae Munhu's lips pressed into a thin line. After a brief silence, he frowned. "Wasn't this all rather sudden?" he grumbled, openly showing his displeasure.

As he spoke, Lee Sungmin reached into his robes. He pulled out the letter the Chairman had given him, the one he was told to read when he met Pae Munhu.

"What's that?" Pae Munhu asked.

Lee Sungmin didn't answer. He simply unfolded the letter and read its contents. In truth, there was almost nothing to read. A single sentence was written on the page. Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed as a cackling laugh erupted inside his head.

[He's a madman!]

The comment was aimed at the Chairman, and Lee Sungmin couldn't help but agree.

He folded the letter again and tossed it to Pae Munhu. The thin paper shot toward him, and Pae Munhu quickly raised a hand to catch it.

"What in the world is this…?"

"Read it," Lee Sungmin said.

With an annoyed expression, Pae Munhu unfolded the letter. His eyes widened as he read the sentence written in the very center.

Beat Pae Munhu to death in front of everyone.

That was all. There was no reason, no explanation. Pae Munhu stared at Lee Sungmin, his mouth hanging half-open.

[Seeing as it says to 'beat' him to death, I guess that means you're not supposed to use your spear, right?]

*I suppose so.*

Receiving Pae Munhu's dumbfounded stare, Lee Sungmin slowly clenched and unclenched his fist.
