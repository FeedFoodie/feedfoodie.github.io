"You've certainly kept me waiting."

Kim Jonghyun grumbled from deep within the forest. The subjugation force had finally left Hedor. He had been coiled up in this place for nearly a month, and only now were they setting out.

*They're being unnecessarily cautious. Does that mean they rate me that highly?*

Or perhaps it was the Grimoire he possessed. A smirk touched Kim Jonghyun's lips as he glanced at the tome floating beside him. The pitch-black Grimoire emitted an ominous light, and he gazed at it with eyes full of affection before turning his head to look down.

Piled in a deep pit were five thousand hearts. A considerable amount of time had passed since their extraction, yet they had not decayed in the slightest. The ritual had not yet begun. It would commence the very moment the subjugation force entered this forest.

The chances of success were slim, but Kim Jonghyun found himself looking forward to that moment nonetheless, and if he failed? It could not be helped. He would simply withdraw, analyze the reasons for his failure, and once he had reinforced those factors, he could try the ritual again.

In any case, Kim Jonghyun had nothing to lose. The Black Magic Tower Master? His public reputation? To him, such things were meaningless.

*It'll take about four days.*

Though his preparations were complete, Kim Jonghyun planned to spend the next four days re-inspecting everything he had set up.

*They've made such excessive preparations for someone with my trash skills. Shouldn't I put in just as much effort on my end?*

Dragging the hem of his robe, Kim Jonghyun disappeared into the shadows of the forest. From within the darkness, the cry of an unidentifiable beast echoed.

{sep}

A force five hundred strong had gathered to subjugate a single man. Fifty martial artists from the Moyong Clan marched alongside seventy from the Murim Alliance. There were sixty from the Mage Army, accompanied by the Crimson Archmage, the Green Tower Master, the White Tower Master, and the Golden Magic Tower Master. One hundred Holy Knights and Priests from the Church joined two hundred mercenaries from the Mad Dogs. They were not just any riffraff but individuals who would be recognized anywhere in the world.

Lee Sungmin rode on horseback beside Scarlet. It would take a good four days to travel from Hedor to the forest where Kim Jonghyun was hiding.

[It took long enough.] Heo grumbled in his mind.

A full week had passed after their arrival in Hedor before they finally departed. Though the delay was longer than he had expected, Lee Sungmin was not particularly dissatisfied. Right now, all of his attention was focused on the woman riding beside him.

Scarlet wore a discontented expression. She did not like this subjugation at all and would not have even participated if not for her promise to the Green Tower Master.

[So. Did you find anything out?] Chwi Geol, the leader of the Pure White Martial Squad, sent a Thought Transmission to Tang Ahee while keeping an eye on Lee Sungmin riding behind them.

At the mental query, Tang Ahee furrowed her brow and glared back at him. [Don't ask when you already know the answer.]

[I swallowed my pride and was completely blatant about it, but he didn't even bat an eye.]

[So in the end, you found out nothing.]

[You're not going to say it's my fault, are you? I did my best. It's just so strange. Unless he has some peculiar tastes, there's no way he wouldn't fall for my seduction.] Tang Ahee, who took great pride in her beauty, could not understand why Lee Sungmin had resisted her repeated temptations.

[Maybe he's gay or a pedophile?]

[Why are you asking me that?]

[I'm not asking for an answer. My pride is hurt, so I'm just grumbling. So, Chwi Geol. What did you find out?]

[I found out nothing,] Chwi Geol replied with a shameless expression.

Tang Ahee was momentarily speechless, just staring at him. Meeting her gaze, Chwi Geol shrugged.

[The information I found is that there is nothing to find.]

[What kind of bullshit is that?]

[Lee Mincheol,] Chwi Geol uttered Lee Sungmin's alias. [He's twenty-seven years old. He has no sect, and his place of origin is unknown. His martial arts are presumed to be at the Supreme Peak level, mid-grade or higher, but even that hasn't been properly grasped.] As he spoke, Chwi Geol clenched and unclenched the hand he had used to shake Lee Sungmin's when they first met. [His martial arts don't rely on bare hands. He definitely uses a weapon.]

[He saw through my stealth technique easily, too,] Tang Ahee added.

[It's incomprehensible that a master of his skill level is completely unknown. Lee Mincheol. I used the Beggars' Gang's information network to look into him, and there's really nothing. The only thing I managed to find is that he came south from somewhere further north than Hedor.]

[Further north?]

[The exact location couldn't be identified. However, the suspected place is Trabia.] Chwi Geol frowned as he voiced his speculation. The beggars of the Beggars' Gang were spread throughout Eria, but there were lands that even they could not touch.

The North was Trabia, a land ruled by the Vampire Queen. Even in the South, a region teeming with Yokai, the beggars were active, but they could not operate in Trabia. Too many of them had died. After the fall of the Blood Heavenly Demon, public order in Trabia had completely vanished, and the Vampire Queen, who had been inactive for decades, began her activities again. The vampires who followed her command attacked the beggars night after night, biting them to death.

It was not just the vampires who killed them. All sorts of vicious demonic practitioners and criminals had crawled into that maddened city, and to them, the beggars were enjoyable prey. After realizing that the prestige of the Beggars' Gang offered no protection in Trabia, their members died off daily. In the end, the Beggars' Gang had no choice but to withdraw completely.

[Isn't it very strange?] Chwi Geol continued. [A Supreme Peak master with no rumors about him came south from Trabia, a land ruled by the Vampire Queen. He arrived in this city and joined the subjugation force, and the one this force is hunting is the Black Mage Kim Jonghyun. It's not confirmed, but seeing as he's causing trouble in the North, the Vampire Queen's territory, it's speculated that he might have some kind of relationship with her.]

[Does that mean Lee Mincheol might betray us?]

[I believe that possibility can't be ruled out. That man, Lee Mincheol, could be someone sent by the Vampire Queen to help Kim Jonghyun.]

[If that's the case, shouldn't we kill him right now?]

[We don't have any certainty. Killing him based on a slight possibility would be too rash.]

[But we can't just leave him be because we're not certain, can we? Chwi Geol, does anyone else know about your suspicions?]

[Since I'm not certain myself, I haven't told anyone yet.]

[That's too soft! We have to tell the others right away. That Lee Mincheol might be an envoy sent by the Vampire Queen!] Tang Ahee glared at him.

Chwi Geol was lost in thought for a moment. Tang Ahee was right. If there was suspicion, appropriate action had to be taken. Nevertheless, he had not done so because he did not want to take all the blame himself.

[Lee Mincheol is acquainted with the Crimson Archmage.]

[Are you saying the Crimson Archmage might be a spy too?]

[Well, I suppose the possibility isn't zero.]

[What about the Church?] Tang Ahee asked, her eyes shining. It seemed she had used her head for once. If he might be an envoy of the Vampire Queen, getting help from the Church would be the fastest way to seek counsel. Coincidentally, Holy Knights and Priests from the Church were here to support the subjugation.

[I'll go.]

Tang Ahee began to move hastily, guiding her horse toward the members of the Church. Ever since her near-death experience in the Bewitching Forest, she had become desperate when it came to her own safety. Chwi Geol watched her back intently. He was not certain of anything. He had only vaguely dropped the hint that there was a possibility. That was enough. The foolish young lady of the Tang Noble Clan was just over-interpreting and acting on her own.

Tang Ahee abruptly sought out the Holy Knights, found the Holy Knight Commander leading them, and told him everything she had heard from Chwi Geol. The only difference was that where Chwi Geol had said 'there is a possibility', Tang Ahee was filled with conviction, stating it was undoubtedly true.

The Holy Knight Commander, who had a chiseled face like a plaster statue, did not dismiss her words as nonsense. After hearing the whole story, he personally led a High Priest and rode over to Lee Sungmin.

[What is it?] the Void Monarch grumbled upon seeing them approach.

Lee Sungmin also tilted his head, watching the two men. Scarlet, who was beside him, opened her mouth.

"What's going on?"

"There is something we need to confirm," the Holy Knight Commander answered.

At the sudden development, the entire subjugation force came to a halt. Scarlet narrowed her eyes and questioned him again. "What is it that you need to confirm?"

"We have information that that man may be an envoy sent by the Vampire Queen." The Holy Knight Commander did not reveal that Tang Ahee had been his informant. It was his own way of being considerate.

Scarlet wore a look of disbelief and spat, "What kind of bullshit is that? A spy for the Vampire Queen? Are you saying that this Mincheol might be a vampire?"

"If that were the case, it's impossible that we wouldn't have noticed," the Holy Knight Commander replied. He glanced at Lee Sungmin and continued. "However, just because he is not a vampire does not guarantee he isn't her envoy. The Vampire Queen's powerful charm is enough to break even a devout believer. No matter how outstanding a master of martial arts he is, there's no guarantee he hasn't become a puppet charmed by that monster."

"Don't be ridiculous."

"If you are truly innocent, then there should be no problem with us checking. If it turns out we were mistaken, I will personally bow my head and apologize," the Holy Knight Commander stated.

Lee Sungmin remained silent, listening to the exchange. After a brief pause, he finally spoke. "What is your evidence?"

"You are a person about whom no rumors have spread. Of course, that is not the entirety of our suspicion. The reason for our suspicion is because you came south from Trabia."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed. "I have not been charmed by the Vampire Queen."

"Anyone could say that," the Holy Knight Commander immediately retorted.

[Things have gotten troublesome,] the Void Monarch muttered.

The Holy Knight Commander and the High Priest were watching Lee Sungmin, waiting for his reaction. Lee Sungmin slowly nodded his head. "How do you intend to confirm it?"

"It is a magical procedure."

"Go ahead."

He could not refuse here. Lee Sungmin nodded in agreement. At that, the High Priest stepped forward and raised his hand. A brilliant white light gathered in his palm.

[I'm going to be exposed,] the Void Monarch said. [It's a dispel. A Divine Magic that nullifies other magic. The human skin mask you're wearing is also imbued with magic, so if the dispel touches your body, the mask's magic will be undone and your bare face will be revealed.]

*I figured.* Lee Sungmin was not agitated. He had always considered the possibility that his identity might be revealed. He glanced at Scarlet, whose eyes were trembling slightly. She too knew exactly what kind of magic the High Priest was preparing, and she knew that when it was cast, Lee Sungmin's identity would be exposed.

"I have no choice," Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice.

Just as the dispel was about to be cast, his body moved. In a blur of motion, he was behind Scarlet and struck the back of her neck with a sharp, precise blow.

"Ugh?!"

No matter how skilled a mage Scarlet was, she would never have imagined that Lee Sungmin would attack her. He caught her limp, unconscious body and held a knife-hand to her nape.

At his sudden action, the faces of everyone, including the Holy Knight Commander, hardened. Lee Sungmin's actions, though he had not said it directly, were tantamount to proving their suspicions correct.

"As expected! From the Vampire Queen!"

"No," Lee Sungmin said in a flat voice as he raised a hand. The mask covering his face slowly came off.

"Gasp...!" Tang Ahee was the first to recognize him.

"The Phantom Spear...?!"

At that cry, Moyong Daeun's face twisted like a demon's.
