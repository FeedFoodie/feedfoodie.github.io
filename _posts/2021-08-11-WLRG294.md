"What is this," Scarlet stammered, her voice laced with horror.

Even Yehwa's weeping ceased, her sobs catching in her throat as she stared at the will with wide, disbelieving eyes.

*Good heavens.*

Osla muttered the thought, pressing a hand to his forehead.

"......"

Inside Lee Sungmin's head, the Void Monarch groaned.

[As expected. He's a madman.]

Forgetting the situation and his own emotions, Lee Sungmin could not help but agree.

Still, the will was not yet finished. He steadied his breathing and continued to read.

<You understand what this means, and you're probably thinking, 'What kind of bullshit is this?, but think about it. There's really no other way. The Black Heart you possess adds what you consume to your body. You've somehow managed to obtain the violent power of Demonic Energy, and you've even absorbed the power of a Dragon Heart, which humans cannot handle. Gaining power immediately is impossible, but eating this one means you will gain the potential for further growth.>

*I know that's what it means.* Probably, no, definitely. If he ate the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's heart, he would be able to obtain his power. His body would slowly evolve to wield it, just as it had with the Demonic Energy and the Dragon Heart.

<However, you won't be able to accept this readily. This one knows how you've struggled to avoid becoming a Yokai. Eating this one, a human, might be the very thing that turns you into a complete Yokai.>

[He's right,] the Void Monarch's voice rumbled in agreement. [It's too dangerous. Your body has already mutated into that of a Yokai, and your yokai nature resides in your mental world. Given the chance, it will leap out and try to seize your body at any moment, just like with Urmuri.]

*That's true.*

[In your incomplete state, eating a human would be a complete denial of your own morality. For you, who wishes to remain human, it would be the perfect trigger to push you over the edge and turn you completely into a Yokai.]

Lee Sungmin was worried about that very thing, a concern the Chairman had clearly anticipated in his will.

<The choice is yours. Whether you will eat this one or not. This one is only giving you the option, I have no intention of forcing your choice.>

*Choice.*

Lee Sungmin stared at the word for a long time. Mushii and Osla had both spoken to him about the importance of choice, saying a moment would surely come when he would have to make one. Was this that moment? Abandon his humanity and gain power? Or abandon power? He quietly closed his eyes.

*I don't know. What is the right answer here?* The situation was already dangerous enough. If he ate the Chairman's heart now, he would probably, no, definitely, mutate into a Yokai. *I don't know if the 'me' that becomes that will still be the 'me' of now.* The version of himself he had experienced as a Yokai was nothing more than a rampaging monster, his humanity completely obliterated, with nothing that could be called an intellect.

"......"

*What should I do?* If he ate the Chairman, he would certainly gain power, but he would become a Yokai. Was the person he became still him? Lee Sungmin thought of the 'me' of 'now'.

The 'me' who couldn't do anything.

At the Murim Alliance and in the North, he had been powerless. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had caused the incident, and it was the Chairman who had advanced and resolved it. He had taken the lead in every action, while Lee Sungmin had never been the main character in any of it. What the Chairman had said was right.

As long as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman didn't make his exit, Lee Sungmin couldn't do anything.

There was no need to. In all matters, the Chairman was superior to him.

*I'm sick of it. Sick of my own powerlessness.* He had gone from a C-Rank Mercenary to a peak master, then a super peak master, and had even reached the Transcendent Realm.

However, in the end, what could he do? Yes, for most problems, the power he had obtained was a great help.

However, this was the limit. Lee Sungmin knew it well. In the events to come, his power was not enough to turn the tables or lead the situation.

That was why the Chairman had said such a thing.

[Choose rationally,] the Void Monarch warned.

At those words, a surprising chuckle escaped Lee Sungmin's lips. He found it strange himself, but his reason was suddenly ice cold. The turmoil he felt was not for the Chairman's death but for his own suffocating powerlessness.

*What more do I have to do?* He had trained on Mount Mushii. He had endured Denir's training. He had learned Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the ultimate technique of the world's number one, and had become the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, another world's number one.

Even that was not enough.

*I have to believe in myself.*

Lee Sungmin quietly came to a conclusion.

*I will not change. I must not change. I don't know what my role is, but I… I don't want to become unable to do what I want to do.*

[…Is that so.]

*I don't know. I really don't know what will happen. I don't know what role this thing called fate is forcing on me. With the Grace of Fate gone, I don't know if the choice I make now is the right one, but I'm sure of this one thing. This is what I want to do.*

[Even if it's a choice that might turn you into a Yokai?]

*I have to believe in myself,* Lee Sungmin repeated internally. *I don't want to remain powerless. This is the only way. The only way for the me of now to gain the potential to overturn the future. If the choice I have to make is between remaining human or becoming a Yokai, then I will choose the latter. I want to become a Yokai and gain power.*

[What is it you want to do?] the Void Monarch asked.

Lee Sungmin's answer was immediate.

*Master told me not to avenge him, but I want to take revenge. Because that's what I want to do.*

[And The End?]

*I'll stop it. I'll kill the Martial God and kill this thing called The End that I know nothing about. If Kim Jonghyun becomes the first calamity in the South, then I'll kill Kim Jonghyun and stop the calamity.*

[Perhaps… the you that becomes a Yokai might become a calamity just like Kim Jonghyun. There's more than enough potential for that. The Demonic Energy of Urmuri is terrifyingly vast, and a considerable amount of this Elder's Demonic Energy has already passed to you. On top of that, you even have the power of a Dragon Heart. If the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power is added to that, and you become a complete Yokai able to use all that power… you might become a monster comparable to this Elder in his prime.]

*I believe,* Lee Sungmin thought as he opened his closed eyes. *I believe that I won't become that.*

[You fucking idiot. You're going to handle becoming a Yokai with just feelings like that?]

*You're pretty sane for a Yokai.*

[That's because this Elder is great.]

*You reside within me. Maybe I might become like you.*

[Hahaha! You've gone quite mad yourself. Tell me. Is it truly yourself you believe in? Or is it the will left to you by the dead Unorthodox Alliance Chairman? Or perhaps, is it this Elder inside your head?]

*All of them.*

The answer left the Void Monarch speechless.

*I want to follow my Master's will, and I have to have faith in myself. I have no choice but to believe, so I believe. I believe I have a role. I believe that it wasn't just by chance that I grabbed the Stone of Reincarnation and returned, but that I returned 'because it was me'. You said it yourself. That you might have died just to meet me. So I believe in you too.*

[What irresponsible words,] the Void Monarch chuckled. [I'll promise you this one thing. If you become a Yokai you cannot control, this Elder will stop you, even if it means accepting the annihilation of my own existence.]

A faint smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips.

<Do as you wish.>

The end of the will was approaching.

<Do what you want, and don't regret it. Just as this one did.>

That was his intention.

<Finally. I'm sorry for dying first.>

At the very end of the will, a final thought appeared.

<A small question comes to mind. This one, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. Was I the world's number one? The strongest in history? Being the greatest in the demonic path is a given, but I'm quite curious about those two. Since I can't know how this one died as of now, I can't be certain. Of course, in my heart, this one believes that I am indeed the world's number one and the strongest in history.>

[Among humans, he was the world's number one and history's number one,] the Void Monarch muttered.

<This one will have died in a way befitting this one.>

The last sentence of the will was a testament to his unshakeable pride.

<While proving that I am the world's number one, the number one in history. I look forward to seeing how such a one as this will die.>

The will ended there. The Chairman, not knowing when he would die, was looking forward to his own death, confident in the proof it would provide.

Yehwa was no longer crying. Scarlet let out a long sigh. Lee Sungmin quietly set the will down. The silence stretched for a moment before he broke it.

"I will eat it."

Yehwa's expression was one of shock, and Scarlet shot up from her seat.

"Are you serious?"

"It was his will, and I have decided to do so myself."

"But… that's a bit… gruesome," Scarlet muttered, her body trembling. "You're not… eating it raw, are you?"

"It's… alright." Amidst Scarlet's hesitant question, Yehwa answered with a trembling voice. "It is my lord's… will. Please do as you wish, So Ryeonju."

Although she said that, Yehwa closed her eyes as if she could not bear to watch. Lee Sungmin looked down at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's corpse with a hardened expression.

"A choice. Yes, it's a choice."

It was Osla who broke the uncomfortable silence.

"The Coalition Leader is a coward. He's dead, but he knew I would do this. He knew I would remember what he said and bring his body to this forest, and… he also knows what actions I will take next."

Osla chuckled.

"This much is something I can do. It's not like I'm leaving the forest directly. Yes, so I don't need to suffer any penalty. No one can restrain my actions."

"What?"

"It's a choice," Osla answered Lee Sungmin's question. "Your choice, and my choice. Since you have chosen, I too shall choose."

As he spoke, Osla's wings slowly unfurled.

"As I've said before, I am friendly towards you. The Coalition Leader is dead, and his will has been passed on to you. So I have no choice but to be even more friendly towards you. No, putting that aside. Since you have made a choice, I, as your helper, must assist you."

"How do you intend to help?"

"I'll relieve some of your worries." As Osla said this, he raised both his hands. The fully spread butterfly wings began to emit a brilliant light. "So that you don't go berserk after eating the Coalition Leader, I will Seal your yokai nature. It may not be perfect, but you won't turn into a Yokai right away. I'll say it again, it's not perfect. In fact, this immediate Sealing might bring about a greater danger later when the Seal shatters."

"It's better than going berserk right now."

"You'll need to prepare," Osla muttered. "Because if the Sealed yokai nature runs rampant, something terrible might happen. Since the Sealing I'm doing now isn't perfect, you must prepare to be able to control yourself perfectly."

He recalled Mushii's words, the warning that he would have to prepare.

"I intend to."

Lee Sungmin looked down at the Chairman's corpse again. Seeing that, Osla shook his head.

"The meal you're about to have seems quite gruesome."

"I suppose so."

Tearing open the chest, pulling out the heart, and chewing it. No matter how he framed it, it would not be a pleasant sight. Even with his mind made up, it was impossible to do while sane.

"I'll help you," Osla said with a sigh.

As he stretched out his hand, the dead Chairman's body trembled slightly. A red orb of light rose from the corpse's left chest, resembling the power the Spirit Queen had extracted from the Dragon Heart.

"The method is also important," Osla muttered. "Tearing open your Master's chest yourself, pulling out his heart and chewing it. Drinking his blood… such depravity is a direct denial of human morality. Doing it this way is much better."

*It doesn't mean the risk disappears completely, though,* Osla added silently, sending the orb of light toward Lee Sungmin.

"......"

Osla spread his arms wide along with his wings.

"I will begin the Sealing."
