The journey was not without its minor troubles. While crossing the mountains, they were set upon by bandits several times. A carriage that looked so valuable at a glance, traveling a mountain path without any visible escort, was a natural temptation. Of course, Yehwa, from her perch in the driver's seat, dispatched them all without the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman ever needing to lift a finger.

These minor problems persisted for a time, but at a certain point, the bandit attacks on the mountain paths simply stopped. No one tried to pick a fight with them on the road either. Instead, the number of beggars increased, men and women who repeatedly blocked the carriage's path demanding alms. On the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's orders, Yehwa gave them a reasonable amount rather than chasing them off, and they continued on.

"You think the Beggars' Gang has caught our scent?"

"That's unlikely," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied. "We've been constantly changing the carriage's appearance. We even sent out additional carriages with this same form to cause confusion. They're just ordinary beggars clinging to us because we look rich."

His confidence was well founded, a fact Lee Sungmin was only now learning. This carriage had not simply departed from the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance and headed straight for Kron. The Chairman had anticipated that the Beggars' Gang or other information guilds would have eyes and ears everywhere. The carriage's appearance could be altered by magic, a feature the Chairman had used freely throughout their journey. Moreover, multiple identical carriages, each led by members of his personal guard, had departed from Haras to create a web of confusion. Thanks to these precautions, the Beggars' Gang would not easily suspect this particular carriage had come from the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's headquarters. After all, a single hole could greatly distort any piece of information.

Lee Sungmin nodded in understanding. He had also been periodically summoning Nebul to check on the situation in Kron and the rumors surrounding their alliance. It was known that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Phantom Spear had disappeared, but no whispers suggested they were heading directly for the Murim Alliance.

*I suppose the Beggars' Gang wouldn't have thought that Master and I would just recklessly march into the Murim Alliance.*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's actions were absurd. Even for the supreme leader of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, visiting the Murim Alliance with so few people was nothing short of madness, and he was not just visiting. He was going to beat up its chairman, the Black Dragon Knight.

"I can see the gates of Kron," Yehwa reported from the driver's seat.

They would inevitably have to pass through an inspection at the gate. Bribing their way through was an option, but that would only draw unwanted attention. They had bypassed cities on their way here precisely to avoid such inspections, but since their destination was the Murim Alliance inside Kron, there was no way around it. Lee Sungmin looked at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"What's your plan?"

"Let's get out," the Chairman said, rising to his feet.

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. "Pardon?"

"I said, let's get out. We're leaving the carriage here."

"......"

"It's a hassle to bring the carriage along," the Chairman said, then spoke to Yehwa in the driver's seat. "We'll be back soon. Around sunset. It won't come to that, but if this one does not return after the sun has set, escort the Crimson Archmage to a safe place."

"......"

"If the Crimson Archmage is endangered in the process, you are to protect her even if it costs you your life."

"I will obey," Yehwa answered without hesitation.

Realization dawned on Lee Sungmin, and his mouth fell half-open. "No way."

"You and this one. The two of us will break through the gate."

"But we came all this way in secret...?"

"The secrecy was just to get here," the Chairman said, his voice relaxed as he flung open the carriage door. "It was to prevent the Black Dragon Knight from sensing our approach and running away. Well, it doesn't matter if the Black Dragon Knight flees because of the commotion at the gate. If that happens, he'll be criticized for being unqualified as the Murim Alliance Chairman, and the Murim Alliance will be pushed to the brink of dissolution."

"Kron is a giant city," he stated.

"I know."

"It's also where the main branch of the Beggars' Gang is located, and there are the armed groups under the Murim Alliance, the small and medium-sized orthodox sects, and even the murim Noble Clans."

"I suppose so."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded his head. He was wearing the mask of a smiling goblin on his face. "What's the problem with that?" he asked, looking back at Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin felt a knot of tension tighten in his stomach. He too was a master of the Transcendent Realm, his skills ranking him among the top ten martial arts experts, but he had never experienced a true large-scale battle, never faced hundreds or thousands of enemies charging at him with clear killing intent.

"...Isn't this a bit much?"

"It might be too much for you," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman sneered. "Even for an outstanding master, facing hundreds or thousands of opponents is bound to be exhausting. Even guys who are nothing special one-on-one become tricky when they swarm you. In fact, the same is true for this one."

The carriage stopped, and the Chairman stepped down to the ground.

"However, they are not prepared as a group. This isn't a reckless invasion. It's a blatant surprise attack. They are not prepared for a surprise attack."

"However..."

"......"

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman grasped the mask on his face. "I've never properly shown you just how strong this one is."

"Pardon?"

"You lack respect for your Master. This is a good opportunity. While I'm beating up the Black Dragon Knight, I'll also firmly beat a sense of awe for this one into you, my disciple."

"......"

The mask came away from the Chairman's face. Lee Sungmin stared, his eyes wide with surprise. Inside the carriage, Scarlet watched, and from the driver's seat, Yehwa's expression mirrored their shock.

"What are you all staring at? You're making the one taking it off feel awkward."

Beneath the mask was the face of a handsome man with a cold, sharp impression. Scarlet stared intently at his face as he tucked the mask into his robes and muttered.

"...He's perfectly handsome."

"Indeed," Lee Sungmin nodded in agreement.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's eyebrows twitched at their reactions. "What were you expecting?"

"That you'd take off the mask and actually be a peerless beauty..."

"Or an ugly man so hideous the sight of him would induce vomiting."

"Or a weak face that, contrary to your attitude and way of speaking, looked timid at first glance."

"It would have been fun if it was the wrinkled face of an old man, too."

Listening to Lee Sungmin and Scarlet's comments, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's fists clenched. "I can't just kill them all."

As the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman subtly revealed his killing intent, Lee Sungmin and Scarlet spoke as if they had not been teasing him at all.

"You're an exceptionally handsome man."

"You must have made quite a few women cry, hoho."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman swept his bangs back and clasped his hands behind his back. "...Anyway. Yehwa, you are to remember my words. Disciple, follow this one."

"I will obey."

"......"

Lee Sungmin knew very well what it meant for the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman to remove his mask. The mask he usually wore suppressed a significant portion of his power. Even while wearing such a mask, he had been strong enough to subdue Wiji Hoyeon in the forest within ten seconds. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who was that powerful, had now even taken off his mask. It meant he was going to use his full strength.

Although he had become the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's disciple, Lee Sungmin had never personally witnessed just how much power the man possessed and could wield.

With his hands clasped behind his back, the Chairman looked at the gates of Kron. He had taken off his mask, but the power emanating from him did not feel much different from when he was wearing it.

*Was it because the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman hadn't properly unleashed his power yet?*

[No. It is because the difference in level is too great for you to feel it.] Void Monarch answered. [Just as those of a lower level than you cannot properly grasp your skills, it is the same for you.]

*The difference is that great...?*

[That's right. It is enough to surprise even this Elder. Can a human truly be that strong?]

*Compared to you?*

[He seems like he would make a decent playmate.]

In the end, it was the same old boast from Void Monarch. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman began to move with a leisurely movement art. It was a speed that even Lee Sungmin, who was suppressing his inner arts and Demonic Energy with his mask, could easily follow. Arriving before the city gate, the Chairman walked past the people lined up for inspection without giving them a single glance.

"Wait your turn," a guard conducting inspections at the gate said with a frown.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman paid him no mind. He walked past the guard with his hands behind his back, and the guard's face contorted.

"Your turn...!"

The words were never finished. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman took a single step.

KUUUUNG!

The pressure that spread out from him made the guards' eyes roll back. Foaming at the mouth, the guards collapsed in a heap. The people who had been standing in line stared in shock at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"How troublesome," he muttered, passing through the gate.

A whistle blew belatedly. The guards on the wall gathered to repel the brutish intruder, but the result was no different. The number of people who collapsed foaming at the mouth simply increased. These were merely the Kron city wall guards, not members of the Orthodox Murim Alliance.

However, the story changed once he passed through the gate. Orthodox sects martial artists who had witnessed the commotion blocked the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's path. A significant number of them were beggars dressed in rags. They shouted, spittle flying from their mouths.

"Who in the world are you to be doing this?!"

"This is the home of the Beggars' Sect Main Branch and the Murim Alliance!"

"Reveal your identi..."

"Yang Ilcheon," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman answered, cutting through the beggars' clamor. His low voice was filled with internal energy, and though it was not loud, it slammed into the ears of the shouting beggars. "Don't you know who that is?"

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman approached the beggars. "Do you know the title Demon Emperor?"

"...Good heavens..." one of the beggars made such a sound. They looked at the masked Lee Sungmin standing behind the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The Chairman's real face was unknown, but the rumor that the one with the goblin mask and spear was the Phantom Spear had already spread far and wide.

"Would it have been more famous if I introduced myself as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?" the Chairman said as he raised his hand.

The horrified beggars reacted instantly. They chose to turn their backs and flee rather than confront him. In truth, the result would not have changed either way.

CRACKLE!

The hand the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman waved lightly created dozens of streaks of black lightning. The branching bolts shot out and wrapped around the fleeing Beggars' Gang members.

"Uwaaak!"

With their individual screams, the beggars tumbled to the ground. No, not all of them did. The one remaining beggar collapsed where he stood, trembling violently. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman pointed a finger at him and said, "Go to the Beggars' Gang and tell your leader to come out."

"Ye... Yes...?"

"Is the current leader's title the Beggar King?"

"Th... That was the previous leader's title."

"Is that so? Did the Beggar King die?"

"Yes..."

"Then the current leader must be someone I've never seen. In any case, tell him to come out. Tell him to bring the Dog Beating Stick and the ones who can use the Dog Beating Stick Formation." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman walked past the beggar as he spoke. "Also tell him to run if he wants to save his own life. Though the other beggars will die instead."

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard as he looked at the fallen beggars. In truth, subduing that many people in one strike was something even he could do easily. However, Lee Sungmin could not help but tense up at the mention of the 'Dog Beating Stick Formation'. The Dog Beating Stick Formation was considered one of the three great formations of the orthodox murim, alongside Wudang's Tai Chi Sword Formation and Mount Hua's Plum Blossom Sword Formation.

"Aren't you going a bit too far?"

"I have to go this far to make the Black Dragon Knight crawl out."

Kron was not home to just the Beggars' Gang and the Murim Alliance. There were many small and medium-sized sects and murim Noble Clans that were not part of the Nine Sects One Gang. However, their power was not weak. The fact that they maintained their sects and family lines in this city, where the Murim Alliance was located, was proof that their martial arts were not to be underestimated, even if their influence was not large.

"Hmph."

The streets filled with martial artists who came rushing out. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman surveyed them and frowned.

"I came out myself to instill a sense of awe in my disciple, but at this rate... you can't inspire awe just by being good at catching insects."

Insects.

That was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's assessment of the martial artists filling the street.
