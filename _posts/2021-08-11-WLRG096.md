Lee Sungmin did not enjoy killing. That was a truth that had spanned both his lives, and it was the same now as it had been then. He had never felt any pleasure in taking another's life. It was only natural for him to avoid it whenever possible.

However, this situation was unavoidable.

A sharp killing intent pricked at his senses. Everyone in the village now recognized him as an enemy and wanted him dead. His gaze fell upon the corpse of Kuromaru. He had left the boy, feeling no obligation to save him. *Should I have saved him?* The thought was sudden, and useless. It was far too late for regrets.

He wrapped his body in his Protective Qi Aura and strode forward. Reaching the realm of Supreme Peak had granted him a freedom with the technique he had never known. Even as a peak realm master, he could enhance his qi through martial theory, but that power was a pale shadow, incomparable in quality and efficiency to the energy he now wielded.

Lee Sungmin infused his voice with internal energy. "Can you hear me?"

His call echoed far into the distance. The encroaching killing intent did not waver, but no further attacks came. He judged they were not completely ignorant barbarians.

"I have no desire to fight you," he called out again. "I would prefer to resolve this through conversation if possible."

A moment later, the projected killing intent subsided. A man then leaped over the wooden palisade and landed before Lee Sungmin.

*He's a master.*

The man's refined flow of energy immediately told Lee Sungmin what he was facing. He was a peak master who had completed Origin Reversal, though he did not seem to be a transcendent martial artist who had also undergone Rejuvenation. The middle-aged man stared into Lee Sungmin's face.

"Who are you?" he asked.

"I am Lee Sungmin."

He offered no fist salute, only a slight nod. The man peered into Lee Sungmin's eyes, trying to gauge his level.

"You're a strange one," the man muttered in a small voice. "Your ki and body have entered the realm of Supreme Peak, yet your mind seems to be gazing at something even farther away."

Lee Sungmin did not answer, instead posing a question of his own. "Who are you?"

"Kazuya," the man answered.

It was a name he recognized. Not from his past life, but from Kuromaru just moments ago. Kazuya was the clan head.

"As I said, I have no desire to fight you."

"Then we're grateful," Kazuya said. "If you were to attack with all your strength, many people would have died. We aren't a large clan, and I don't wish to reduce our numbers any further."

"Why did you kill Kuromaru?"

"Because punishment is necessary," Kazuya replied, his expression unchanging. "Elder Nobuhiro is likely dead. That couldn't be helped. He should have known the difference in skill, and it's only natural he take responsibility for picking a fight he couldn't win, but the young one… the whelp who witnessed it and neither killed himself nor resisted, who brought an enemy directly to our village… he had to be punished."

Kazuya glanced down at Kuromaru's corpse. It seemed that was the law they prioritized in this closed-off village.

"I shall receive you as a guest of the village." Kazuya extended a hand. Lee Sungmin stared at the offered hand for a moment before taking it.

"Come in."

With Kazuya, the clan head, as his escort, no one dared to stop him. Lee Sungmin had many questions, but for now, he followed Kazuya to his house. As they walked, he saw that most of the village houses were shabby and impoverished.

"A master of your level, Kazuya, would surely be well-treated in the outside world."

"You're younger than me, and yet, you're stronger," Kazuya said with a laugh. "I don't wish to despair in the wide world. That's why I live in this narrow forest."

The place Kazuya guided him to was a Japanese-style house. Lee Sungmin thought for a moment about what Kazuya had said, the 'wide world', but did not voice his thoughts.

Inside the room, Lee Sungmin sat facing him. "Do you know Envirus?"

At the sudden question, Kazuya nodded. "I do."

"Where is he?"

"He already left the forest about two months ago," Kazuya answered.

Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed. He had followed the trail of Envirus according to the information from Erebrisa, but he had not expected to meet the man so easily here.

"Do you know where he went?"

"I do not."

This was the problem. He had come all this way, but if Envirus had left no trace, tracking him would be difficult. According to Erebrisa, Envirus's most recent location was right here, the Sleeping Forest.

"...What did Envirus do in this forest?"

"Why are you chasing Envirus?" Kazuya countered.

Lee Sungmin's eyes narrowed, his glare fixed on Kazuya. In response, Kazuya let out a low laugh.

"Don't look at me like that."

"...I have a reason to meet him."

"That reason is what I'm curious about."

"I can't answer that."

"So you won't answer my question," Kazuya observed, "yet you ask your own and demand an answer."

Lee Sungmin silently clenched and unclenched his fist. Kazuya, who had been watching him intently, finally spoke.

"I'm not sure if you're the guest Envirus spoke of."

"...Pardon?"

"Before Envirus left, he told me that someday, someone would come to this forest and ask about him. When that time comes..." Kazuya rose from his seated position. "I'd like to get this over with quickly. Is it alright if we move right away?"

"I don't mind." Lee Sungmin nodded and got to his feet.

Kazuya led him outside the village. After they passed the palisade, Kazuya called out to Lee Sungmin, who was following behind.

"Aren't you curious about the clan's secret desire?"

"I doubt you'd tell me even if I asked."

"To be precise, I can't tell you. We say we pursue a secret desire, but we ourselves don't even know what that is."

Lee Sungmin could not understand. He threw a question at the back of Kazuya's head. "Then… why do you live in this forest?"

"Because we have no other choice. Our souls are bound to this, the Sleeping Forest. Those born here can never leave."

"...Is it a curse?"

"It's similar to that. Our clan has lived in this forest for generations. Our bodies have weakened from repeated inbreeding. The secret techniques and martial arts of the clan have faded, so they aren't even properly transmitted anymore. Sooner or later, our clan will simply self-destruct."

Kazuya's voice was surprisingly calm as he spoke, as if he had long been waiting for such an end.

"Even I, the head of the clan, don't know what the secret desire is. Perhaps there never was such a thing to begin with. It might just be a word used to forcibly instill a sense of purpose in the young ones born in later generations. What is certain, however, is that our clan cannot leave this forest, and we are guarding something at its end."

"...Guarding?"

"No. Should this even be called guarding?" Kazuya tilted his head as if he himself did not know. "The ghostly cry drives the weak mad. If one's martial prowess hasn't surpassed the wall of the peak realm, even living in this forest is impossible. The young ones take medicine to resist the ghostly cry and have become unable to hear it. The deeper into the forest you go, the stronger it becomes. I don't know if you'll be able to withstand it."

"I can probably withstand it."

"Huhu! Even for a Supreme Peak master, resisting the ghostly cry is difficult. It's an attack that affects the mind. I'm guiding you because of a promise with Envirus… but I'm not sure if you can endure it."

Kazuya's steps came to a halt.

It was a mystical sight. A massive wall formed from intertwined trees split the great forest in two. Lee Sungmin, who had been staring at it, shifted his gaze to Kazuya. "What is this?"

"It's a wall. Can't you tell by looking?"

"I know it's a wall."

"Without the consent of the clan's head, you can't pass this wall. Would you like to test it?" Kazuya asked, turning back to Lee Sungmin. He wore a mischievous expression, like a rascal unbefitting his age. "If you succeed in forcibly breaking through this wall..."

"What will you give me?"

"Hmm. Now that I think about it, I have nothing to give. How about you try it just for the sake of it?"

At the blatant suggestion, Lee Sungmin let out a short laugh. He took the spear from his back and walked past Kazuya. As he raised it, purple enhanced qi coiled around the shaft.

KWAAANG!

He thrust the spear forward, and a huge explosion erupted against the trees, yet the wall remained intact, not even slightly damaged. It had not been his full power, but he had put a decent amount of strength into the attack. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he stared at the wall of trees.

"See? It can't be broken through with force. Even Envirus couldn't break it."

"Then how did he get in?"

"I let him in. Because I was told to let him in."

They were incomprehensible words. Kazuya walked past Lee Sungmin and approached the wall. He reached his hand toward the trees and his lips moved, the small sound a chant in an unknown language.

The wall of intertwined trees disappeared.

"I can't go any further than this." Kazuya clasped his hands behind his back and turned around.

Lee Sungmin watched Kazuya's back as he returned to the village for a moment. Then, he turned around himself. Where the wall had vanished, a winding path now lay. He looked at the path for a moment before stepping forward.

Ah.

Ahh.

Ahhhhh.

A sound echoed in his ears. The ghostly cry. That unknown sound that made those who entered the Sleeping Forest lose consciousness grew louder, and louder, and louder, hammering at his mind. With every step he took, the cry intensified. It did not simply render one unconscious. This was a sound that drove the spirit to madness, its terrible ominousness gripping Lee Sungmin's soul and shaking it frantically.

"......"

Lee Sungmin swallowed. It was not that it was hard to endure. It was just that enduring it was annoying. This was a powerful mental attack. Even a peak master would find it difficult to resist. He keenly felt the meaning behind Kazuya's words. Even a master who had reached the realm of Transcendence in mind, ki, and body would struggle against this dreadful ghostly cry.

However, Lee Sungmin was different. The two thousand one hundred years he had spent in the mental world had broken and rebuilt his mind countless times. In that process, he had gained a madness of his own, but paradoxically, that same mind had become tens, hundreds of times more solid than before.

He did not break. He simply kept walking. He felt annoyed, but he did not run wild. The winding path was the only way forward. Looking around, he strangely saw no trees, only a thick, hazy fog that spread out in all directions.

"I was mistaken."

An indifferent voice shook the space. Lee Sungmin stopped walking.

"Causality is an absolute nemesis. A being whose causality is twisted is not common. That is why I was mistaken. The one who came a while ago was also one whose causality was twisted, so I made a mistake and let him in."

The fog raged. Lee Sungmin resumed his steps forward.

"Answer me. O being of twisted causality. Are you the Returned One?" the voice asked.

Lee Sungmin continued to walk. Within the churning fog, lights began to appear, as if hundreds of fireflies were emitting their glow.

His steps stopped. On top of a giant rock, a black flame was flickering.

"What do you mean?" Lee Sungmin asked.

At his words, the flame flickering on the rock swelled. "I asked if you are the Returned One."

"What does it mean to have returned?"

"Do you know death?" the flame asked.

The moment Lee Sungmin was about to answer, an electric current crackled through the space. Watching him, the flame let out a hearty laugh.

"A trivial trick. If I hadn't fallen this far, I wouldn't have allowed such a petty trick."

"If that were the case, my master would not have been able to escape this forest."

The flowing electric current gathered, coalescing into the shape of a small girl wearing a robe. Lee Sungmin blinked, looking at the girl.

"And who are you?" he asked.

"I'm Envirus's familiar."

It was not a physical body. She had merely transferred her consciousness here through magic inscribed in this space. The flame spat out unpleasantly toward Envirus's familiar.

"How annoying. To prevent the two of us from even having a private conversation."

"Because you are an evil being."

"Uahaha! A mere toy doll dares to judge me? Even your master would not be able to judge me!" the flame burst into laughter. Its voice grew louder, and the number of lights in the fog increased even more.

"First, can you please explain this so I can understand?" Lee Sungmin spat out, unable to watch any longer.
