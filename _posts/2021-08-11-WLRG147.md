The disc that had grazed Lee Sungmin's neck spun on, circling him long after it left the Dark Supreme's hand. The Dark Supreme himself had already turned his attention to the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was closing in on him with a long awl clutched in each hand. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art was a tyrannical qi art, yet its ferocious techniques were useless against the Dark Supreme's phantom-like movements.

His skill was rooted in a well-known truth. Among the Four Great Clans of the orthodox sects, the Tang Clan was unrivaled in the arts of hidden weapons and poison. From a young age, their martial artists trained with these deadly tools. In Eria, the unique skills of Tang Clan members from countless different dimensions and murim had fused together, creating techniques far superior to their original forms, as was the case with the arts of most great families and sects.

He had once borne the Tang surname himself. Though his humble origins had barred him from leading the clan, no one of his generation could handle hidden weapons with more skill than he. He had always disliked the poison arts and never learned them, but his mastery of hidden weapons was absolute. When he finally abandoned the Tang surname and left the clan, he had already mastered all of their techniques, and not a single member of his former family could stop him.

This was a story from a long and bloody past. After joining the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens, the Dark Supreme's skills had developed even further. To him, hidden weapons were not simply about throwing things. The art encompassed every aspect of handling them, their essence being to kill an opponent stealthily and certainly. For this reason, his techniques were designed for a single, fatal strike, specialized in shattering an opponent's Protective Qi Aura.

Chasing the phantom-like Dark Supreme was a bitter reminder of the vast chasm separating him from the Transcendent Realm. The Crazy Heavenly Demon gritted his teeth and swelled the qi enveloping his body.

KWAAANG!

A red storm erupted around him. Standing at its center, the Crazy Heavenly Demon raised his foot high and stomped it down. The ground shook as the storm of enhanced qi began to rotate fiercely, a tangible vortex of death. Anyone caught within its grasp would be torn to shreds.

Still, inside the storm, the Dark Supreme remained utterly calm. Though his robes fluttered ceaselessly, his face showed no agitation. Glaring at his opponent's placid face, the Crazy Heavenly Demon spread his hands wide and unleashed the ultimate technique of his art, the Blood Ring Typhoon.

In that instant, the Dark Supreme moved.

"......"

His body vanished and reappeared directly in front of the Crazy Heavenly Demon. He spun the awl in his hand.

PUUK!

The weapon pierced through the Protective Qi Aura with ease and struck the Crazy Heavenly Demon's shoulder.

"Keuk!"

However, the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not stop. He lunged, his left hand clamping down on the Dark Supreme's shoulder. The Dark Supreme glanced at the hand gripping him, then swung the awl in his other hand. The Crazy Heavenly Demon made no attempt to dodge the point-blank attack. He let out a loud roar and tried to tear the Dark Supreme's shoulder from its socket.

PUUK!

The Dark Supreme's awl pierced the Crazy Heavenly Demon's side. He had promised not to kill him, and he intended to keep his word. The awl had skillfully avoided his vital organs, a wound far from fatal.

Then the Blood Ring Typhoon exploded. A deafening roar shook the entire space as red enhanced qi swept through the area.

The Dark Supreme, however, was completely unscathed. He dusted the dirt from his body and looked over at the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who glared back with eyes full of killing intent, blood streaming from his shoulder and side.

At that moment, Lee Sungmin shot out from behind the Dark Supreme, thrusting his fiercely spinning spear toward his back. Once again, the Dark Supreme became a phantom. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and swerved the spear to the side, anticipating the movement. The Dark Supreme, now standing where the spear was aimed, chuckled and swung his hand.

JJEONG!

His fluttering sleeve collided with Lee Sungmin's spear. Lee Sungmin clenched his jaw against the pressure pushing back on his hands as the tip of his spear trembled. He simultaneously unfolded Homing Chain Lightning and Bloody Pursuit.

"So it's the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

The Dark Supreme nodded and waved his hand.

PABABABAK!

Dozens of daggers erupted from his sleeve, forming a steel net that completely intercepted the Homing Chain Lightning. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he watched the enhanced qi scatter in vain. He stomped his foot, weaving the Dancing Shadow Soul Break of the Two Step Calamity with the Blood Ring Shattering. In just two steps, a dense web of enhanced qi covered the space before him.

The Dark Supreme rolled up his right sleeve, revealing a dark wrist armor that covered his entire forearm. A sharp 'CHAK' sounded from the armor as he swung his right arm wide.

CHWAAAK!

The web of enhanced qi that was about to overwhelm him was sliced completely apart. The Dark Supreme retracted the blade back into his wrist armor and waved his left hand.

PABABABAK!

Dozens more hidden weapons fired, this time steel beads the size of a fingernail. Lee Sungmin immediately unleashed Two Step Gentle Ripple to alter their trajectory.

From behind, the Crazy Heavenly Demon charged again. His wounds were not fatal and did little to hinder his movements. He extended his large, open hands in sequence, firing out blasts of enhanced qi. The Dark Supreme spun on the spot and moved his left hand, revealing hidden weapons curved like a bird's talons held between his fingers.

KWADEDEUK!

The weapons tore through the enhanced qi as if talons were ripping through flesh. The Dark Supreme stepped into the opening he had created, then flicked his hand back in the opposite direction. The curved weapons flew from his fingers toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"Keuheup!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon swelled his Protective Qi Aura, but the weapons that had just torn through his attack now pierced his body, aura and all. He staggered, spewing blood from the new wounds. Lee Sungmin roared, adding the third form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang, to his Nine Dragons Slaughter. Nine dragons with rotating fangs lunged, trying to kill the Dark Supreme.

"......"

The Dark Supreme nodded his head with a pleased expression. *Just as I thought, no, even better.* The martial prowess Lee Sungmin displayed pleased him. To have reached such an outstanding level at only twenty-four meant he had a wealth of potential. *Would ten years be enough? If he continues to grow like this and doesn't fall into despair when he hits a wall, he might even enter the Transcendent Realm.*

*But not now.*

The Dark Supreme's hand fumbled at his waist. Hidden weapons were not just for throwing. They were concealed weapons, their essence lying in secrecy. His hand found what it was looking for and produced a black whip he had hidden behind his belt. Among his vast arsenal, this was a weapon he cherished, one that had never once disappointed him. He put strength into his swing, and the whip struck the Flesh Devouring Blood Fang and Nine Dragons Slaughter, scattering them into nothing.

"I hope you're not fully ripe," the Dark Supreme whispered. He swung the whip again, lashing out at the Crazy Heavenly Demon who was attacking from behind. The big man's frame spun through the air before crashing hard to the ground.

"Elder!"

"I didn't kill him."

Lee Sungmin unleashed Deathly Flash. It was the fastest technique he could perform, but the Dark Supreme's flick of his thumb was faster.

PUUK!

A hidden weapon pierced Lee Sungmin's right shoulder joint. The Dark Supreme consecutively flicked his fingertips.

PUPUPUK!

More weapons struck his right leg, which had been supporting his body, piercing it repeatedly. Lee Sungmin's body collapsed forward.

"Even if you couldn't enter the Transcendent Realm in ten years, you've trained to a point close to the pinnacle of the Supreme Peak. I'll pay my respects to that," the Dark Supreme said to the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was collapsed behind him.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon clutched his broken left shoulder and staggered, trying to get up. Lee Sungmin did the same, using his spear to support his body in place of his immobile leg.

[It's martial arts theory,] Void Monarch spat. [With your skills, you can't beat him no matter how much you struggle. You're on a different level.]

*I know.* He had known it even before they started fighting. The Dark Supreme was a formidable enemy, one he could never defeat with his current skills. Even with the Crazy Heavenly Demon fighting alongside him, they hadn't managed to inflict a single scratch.

[What are you trusting in? That heart embedded in your chest? Yes, so far that heart has given you appropriate help whenever you were in a crisis. It has evolved your body so you could reproduce the martial prowess you reached in the Mental World, but are you certain? Are you certain that the heart will evolve your body to escape death, just like it has until now?]

It was true. He had charged in trusting the Black Heart. He had nearly died several times, and each time, the heart had evolved his body to ensure he survived. It had even unraveled the tangle of mind, ki, and body that had bound him for so long.

However, the Dark Supreme wasn't trying to kill him. Lee Sungmin was fighting desperately to kill the Dark Supreme, but his opponent was different. He was utterly relaxed, deflecting their combined attacks as if dealing with children.

[Even if you've been lucky enough to continuously gain insights and survive until now, there's no guarantee it will happen again this time. So...]

*Give it to me,* Lee Sungmin said, his breath coming in ragged gasps. He glared down at his throbbing right leg.

*Hand over your Demonic Energy.*

[...What?]

*I said, hand over the Demonic Energy.*

[Didn't you say you didn't want to use it?] Void Monarch warned. [Demonic Energy is a dangerous power. It might make you, a human, into something that isn't human.]

Lee Sungmin knew that well. He didn't know what he would look like or what he would think if he became something other than human. That was why he had avoided using Demonic Energy since the Dungeon.

*I don't care.*

If he couldn't stop the Dark Supreme here, Wiji Hoyeon would be taken away. He hated that. He didn't know what kind of organization Heaven Beyond Heaven was or what it pursued. He didn't know why they wanted Wiji Hoyeon, or why the Dark Supreme had been protecting her, but he did know that Wiji Hoyeon hated the idea of joining them, and he didn't want her to be taken from him.

Those two reasons were enough.

*Hand it over...!*

[Tsk...!]

Void Monarch did not laugh. He wasn't reluctant to lend his Demonic Energy to a cornered Lee Sungmin. He simply wasn't sure that even with it, Lee Sungmin could win.

Lee Sungmin was human. Although the Demonic Energy was planted deep in his qi center, that alone wouldn't turn him into a Yokai right away, but the more he used it, the more concentrated it would become, until one day it would transform his body completely. That would take a long time.

[As a human, you can't use all of this Elder's Demonic Energy. I can amplify your power like in the Dungeon, but... I don't know if that will be enough to beat that bastard.]

The Dark Supreme was so strong that even the Void Monarch spoke without confidence, but Lee Sungmin wasn't listening.

KURURURUNG!

The Magic Armor trembled as the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy surged up. The energy, which had been raging like a crimson flame, turned a deep purple. The eyes of the Dark Supreme, who was watching Lee Sungmin, widened.

"What is that?"

The Void Monarch's Demonic Energy was so ominous that it had slightly startled him. Lee Sungmin did not answer. He couldn't.

His heart was pounding like crazy. A high-pitched BIIIIK- rang in his head. The wound on his right leg had already healed. He swallowed his ragged breath and ground his teeth, feeling as if 'I' was no longer himself. His spinning mind stabilized, and his shaking vision focused. The Dark Supreme, standing with a surprised expression, was fixed in the center of his sight.

"Kkeueueu...!"

The aftermath of the energy Lee Sungmin drew out also reached the Crazy Heavenly Demon. Blood vessels began to bulge in his eyes, and his own wounds healed. His hair, standing on end like a ghost's, swayed as an ominous Demonic Energy flowed out from him as well.

"......"

Standing between the two of them, the Dark Supreme looked back and forth and let out a dry laugh.

"The power of a Demonic Spirit, I see. Well, this is..." he muttered, shaking both his hands. His eyes narrowed as he held daggers in both palms.

"Are you saying you'll even borrow the power of a mongrel Demonic Spirit?"

The voice he spat out was laced with deep contempt.
