"I'm sorry."

The words, heavy with years of regret and guilt, hung in the air between them. Lee Sungmin fell silent, listening to Baek Sogo's repeated apologies and recognizing them for what they were. She was pouring out the very emotions that had sustained her for so long, a complex ideal formed from an accumulation of righteousness, duty, and a rigid sense of justice. Tangled within that mass of conviction were her feelings for the Junior Disciple she had been forced to leave behind. Whether the choice had been her own or not, she carried the weight of turning her back on him in that situation.

Baek Sogo had always been that kind of person. He remembered the smile she wore on Mount Mushii when she told him, "Because I'm a good person." It was because she was a good person that she felt such guilt and agonized over it. Lee Sungmin slowly raised a hand and wrapped it around her shoulder.

"Didn't I tell you it was okay?" he asked, his smile bitter.

Baek Sogo was still shedding tears. He patted her shoulder gently, as if consoling a child. While he couldn't completely empathize with the guilt she carried, he knew he was the object of it. So, he spoke to her with all the sincerity he could muster.

"That day in the Dungeon, I made the choice I wanted to make. I chose to save you. I've never once regretted it, and if I could go back to that moment, I'd do the exact same thing. You don't need to feel guilty toward me. It's not like I died, is it?"

"I was powerless back then," she whispered. "I had no choice but to turn away and leave my Junior Disciple behind. That's... what I'm sorry for. I was too weak."

"It's okay." Lee Sungmin took hold of both her shoulders, widening the distance between them slightly. She looked up at him, her eyes filled with tears, and met his two eyes, now dyed a brilliant gold. "On Mount Mushii, *I* was the weak one. My body was in a state where I could have died at any moment. The ascetic training I started was beyond anything I could have imagined, and my foolish, arrogant stacking of restrictions put me in constant danger. You were the one who saved me from that, Senior Sister."

"You don't need to feel grateful for that, Junior Disciple," she replied. "I just did what was natural for me. Because I'm... a good person."

"It's the same for me," Lee Sungmin said with a bashful smile. "I also made the choice that felt natural. Though I'm not as good a person as you are."

His words left Baek Sogo speechless. She stared blankly at his face for a moment before a small laugh escaped her lips. She raised a hand to wipe the tears from her eyes.

"I'm glad I could meet you like this, Junior Disciple."

"I think so too, but... why are you here?"

"Because you're here, Junior Disciple." After wiping away the last of her tears, Baek Sogo took a deep breath, having finally poured out her guilt.

However, Lee Sungmin understood from her words that she hadn't come all this way just to offer an apology. He slowly nodded his head.

"I have some good liquor."

[You, don't tell me.]

"Drinking in my room might be a little awkward," he mused. "Let's find a suitable place and share a drink."

"I don't mind if it's your room, Junior Disciple."

[When this Elder suggested a drink, you were so reluctant!]

*What's good is good.*

Lee Sungmin ignored the Void Monarch's grumbling as Baek Sogo murmured with a faint smile, "This is the first time I'm drinking with you, Junior Disciple."

"That's because we were too young back on Mount Mushii," he replied, smiling back.

Since she had said she didn't mind, Lee Sungmin led her toward his room. In truth, it was far too large to be called a mere room. His position as the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman afforded him an entire annex, though he had hardly used it, having been constantly on the move since joining the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

"Hm?"

As they were about to enter the annex, Lee Sungmin's feet stopped. He wrinkled his nose at an artificial scent lingering in the air. It wasn't a familiar smell, but it wasn't entirely foreign either. *Ah.* Realization dawned on him, and he let out a deep sigh.

"So that's what it was," he grumbled, opening the door and stepping inside.

The interior was filled with the smell of reagents, and the Mana in the air moved with an artificial flow. As far as he knew, only one person in the entire Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance could do something like this.

"You're back?"

Scarlet peeked her head out from the end of the hallway. Her red hair was a tangled mess, and deep, dark circles shadowed her eyes.

"Why are you here, Scarlet?"

"I said I needed a place for my experiments, so he told me to stay here. Your kind Master, that is."

"Kind?"

"He's been very good to me," she insisted. "He gets me whatever magic ingredients I need right when I ask for them." Scarlet glanced over at Baek Sogo. "Ah. So you've met up with Baek Sogo. You two must have a lot to talk about, right? I'll be quiet, so don't mind me and do your thing."

"But this is my house."

"Your Master told me to use this place," she retorted. "I can't leave right now, so please understand."

With that, Scarlet disappeared back down the hall, presumably to continue her magic experiment. Lee Sungmin sighed deeply and turned back to Baek Sogo.

"Let's go to the gazebo out back."

The night sky held few stars and offered little beauty, but he liked the cool night air. Lee Sungmin called over a waiting servant to request some snacks for their drinks, then climbed into the gazebo and sat across from Baek Sogo.

"Have you already met with Scarlet?"

"I didn't think she would be here. Yes. It's been three days since I arrived, and I met and greeted her on the first day." After saying that, Baek Sogo bowed her head deeply to him. "Regarding that, I also wanted to thank you, Junior Disciple. Because I... I couldn't go to save her, even though I promised I would help Scarlet if she was in danger."

"Everyone must have had their own circumstances," Lee Sungmin said. "Nothing happened to Scarlet, so you don't need to thank me."

"Their own circumstances," Baek Sogo muttered in a small voice.

Lee Sungmin, who was taking the Void Monarch's liquor bottle out of his Subspace Pocket, froze when he heard her murmur. Just then, a servant arrived with the snacks and cups. As the servant placed a cup before her, Baek Sogo continued to speak, her gaze lowered.

"I believe in you, Junior Disciple." There wasn't a hint of falsehood in her voice. "No matter what the situation becomes, I believe in you. Even if the whole world calls you a liar, I will believe you. It's not because you threw away your own life to save mine. It's because you are my Junior Disciple. Yes, that's all there is to it."

Listening to her words, Lee Sungmin picked up the gourd bottle and slowly poured the liquor into her cup.

"Even if you lie, Junior Disciple," she continued, "even if that lie is so obvious there's no room for doubt, I will still believe you."

"I have no intention of lying." Lee Sungmin filled his own cup as well. It had been a long time since he'd had this liquor, not since the Dwarf Village, and the Void Monarch was making excited noises in his head, completely ignoring the mood.

"Do you think I'm a demonic practitioner?" he asked.

"I don't want to believe the rumors are true."

"What will you do if I am?"

"If you are someone who goes against my beliefs and must be corrected... then I probably... will act accordingly."

That conviction was the very reason she had endured the harsh asceticism on Mount Mushii and the thousand years in Denil's Mental World. It was a conviction she believed to be righteous, even if it was composed of a tangled, twisted, and distorted collection of sympathies. Even if, to others, it was nothing more than a mass of narrow-minded stubbornness, Baek Sogo had no choice but to follow it.

"There have been many misunderstandings," Lee Sungmin began with a sigh.

The first thing he had to talk about was his relationship with Wiji Hoyeon. Only by explaining that could he answer for why he had blocked the Iron Armored Divine Spear to save her, and why he had ultimately killed the man after rescuing her.

"Is the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, a demonic practitioner?" Lee Sungmin posed the question to Baek Sogo.

She didn't answer right away, as she already understood their relationship. He had blocked the Iron Armored Divine Spear to save a friend in crisis and had simply fought back when the man charged at him, ready to die. That was all.

"She hasn't committed any distinct evil acts," Baek Sogo murmured. "Even when Wiji Hoyeon was making a name for herself as the Little Heavenly Demon, she didn't go out of her way to commit evil deeds. Those interested in her martial prowess would challenge her, and the cycle of them being defeated and dying repeated itself. Her merciless hand is what created the name, the Little Heavenly Demon."

"You couldn't call that an evil act."

"That's right," Baek Sogo replied in a small voice, closing her eyes. "It was the Murim Alliance that tried to monitor and control the Little Heavenly Demon."

"The Murim Alliance?" Lee Sungmin chuckled. He had a lot to say about them as well.

He told her about the events in the Bewitching Forest, which were largely responsible for his moniker, the Phantom Spear, becoming known as that of a demonic practitioner. To make her understand that story, he also had to explain the group called Heaven Beyond Heaven.

Baek Sogo was silent for a long time. She had never heard of Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Murim Alliance, which she had considered a righteous group, was ultimately just a subordinate organization, and the fact that its Chairman belonged to this other group was enough to leave her speechless. Furthermore, the circumstances made it clear that the Murim Alliance's Black Dog Group had killed Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin, and that their actions were also connected to the death of the Iron Armored Divine Spear.

"The Murim Alliance forced the Iron Armored Divine Spear to his death to pressure you," she pieced together, "and they murdered Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin to frame you for the crime..."

"Do you believe me?"

"You're being mischievous, Junior Disciple." Baek Sogo gave a bitter smile at his question. "I told you I would believe your words unconditionally, and... what you've just said, there's no part of it that I particularly suspect to be a lie. Heaven Beyond Heaven, Heaven Beyond Heaven..."

She lifted her cup. The liquor from the gourd bottle, which the Void Monarch was so impatient for, had remained untouched since it was poured. Baek Sogo continued speaking as she lightly swirled the cup.

"To eliminate all inhuman from this world and create a world for humans."

"What do you think of that?"

"I don't sympathize with their ideals," Baek Sogo stated. "Not all inhuman are evil. It's true that most prey on humans, but there are also inhuman who don't, aren't there?"

[What a narrow-minded wench.] the Void Monarch grumbled. [She's using whether they prey on humans or not as the standard for right and wrong, isn't she? In the end, she's just arbitrarily dividing the two from a human perspective.]

*That's because she's human.*

Lee Sungmin brought the cup to his lips, partly to appease the grumbling monarch.

"I can understand your position, Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo said. "It was an act to save the Little Heavenly Demon, and in the process, you became an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven. Yes. That's a common occurrence in the murim, isn't it?"

She brought the cup to her own lips. Her eyes widened slightly at the liquor's aroma, and then again at the taste as it went down her throat. She stared at the empty cup for a moment before shaking her head.

"Junior Disciple, on my way here, I thought about many things. About the world I know, the things I've seen, and the things I've made my conviction. Yes. The standards I've adopted and the measure by which I judge right and wrong might seem narrow-minded and unjust to others."

[Good thing she at least realizes it herself.]

"However," the Void Monarch muttered, and Baek Sogo immediately added, "even if it is narrow-minded and unjust, I intend to act as I wish. Even if that is selfish self-righteousness. Live righteously. Walk the path of duty. Those were the last words of my Master, who lived that way."

He had never heard the story of Baek Sogo's Master. He had asked a few times, but she would always avoid answering with a bitter smile.

"I don't think you are evil, Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo said, slowly rising to her feet. "However, I do think the group called the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is evil. Yes. There may be those within this group who are not villains, just as not everyone in the orthodox sects of the Murim Alliance was a righteous hero."

"What is it that you want?"

"The eradication of evil," she answered without a moment's hesitation.

The answer was so absurd that Lee Sungmin couldn't help but ask again.

"Pardon?"

"To eradicate every villain from this world. That is what I want."

[Hahaha!]

The Void Monarch burst into loud laughter.

[What a truly novel lunatic!]

Amidst the Void Monarch's crazed laughter, Lee Sungmin found that he couldn't help but sympathize with him to some extent.
