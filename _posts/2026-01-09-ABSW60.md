The Five Marauders, hidden experts from the Evil Coalition whom Jongri Mun had secretly tasked with supporting the Evil Blade, descended onto the scene and scanned the area in disbelief.

"This young punk defeated the Evil Blade!?"

"I can't believe it, even after witnessing it myself."

"Who in the world is this guy?"

"Hey, he's still alive!"

"Unbelievable! Does that mean he defeated the Evil Blade?"

They formed a circle around the critically wounded Crimson Tiger, who was barely clinging to life. If not for his last-minute efforts to staunch the bleeding from his chest wound, he would already be dead.

"We have to kill him."

"That's right."

"Kill him!"

The five experts drew their swords in unison, eager to kill the Evil Blade's slayer. After all, legend had it that staining a sword with a strong warrior's blood brought good fortune.

"Ah!"

Right as they were about to attack, however, women clothed in spotless white dresses with white veils over their faces descended from the sky like falling flower petals.

Gaping in shock, the Five Marauders spun around to face the women, though they maintained their formation with the unconscious Crimson Tiger at its center.

The women numbered around a hundred. Each had an outstanding figure and beautiful eyes, as if they had been meticulously selected.

"Look over there!" one of the Marauders shouted.

SWOOSH!

A white cloth drifted down from above, taking the form of a majestic path suspended in midair. Like a celestial fairy descending from the heavens, a woman gracefully walked along the ethereal fabric.

The five experts stared blankly at the woman, bewitched. Her presence was extraordinary. Her eyes, visible above the veil, were impossibly deep and clear, radiating intelligence beyond measure. The men were certain that a stunning face lay hidden beneath the veil. 

Moments later, the woman landed lightly on the ground.

One of the Five Marauders asked, "Who are you?"

In place of an answer, the woman's eyes glinted coldly.

FWIP!

A white cloth emerged from behind the men and coiled around their necks.

"Gurk!"

The cloth tightened, strangling the men. Desperate, they drew their swords and slashed at the fabric, but it didn't tear.

FWOOSH!

The next thing the men knew, they were flung into the air by their necks.

The woman slowly extended a hand, almost as if she had been waiting for this moment.

SHAWAAAAA! CRACKLE!

A blast of enhanced icy qi shot out from her palm, freezing the five men solid in midair. 

SHATTER!

As if that weren't shocking and terrifying enough, the frozen bodies exploded into pieces the moment they hit the ground.

The women standing nearby waved their hands, covering the carnage with yet another white cloth. When they tugged it away, the corpses vanished into thin air. Their technique was truly divine. Anyone who witnessed it would have doubted their own eyes.

The woman who had eliminated the Five Marauders in a single swoop approached Crimson Tiger, gazing down at him with wavering eyes.

Slowly, she sat down, carefully embraced him, and took a pill out from her bosom. A fragrance as pure and clear as her gaze filled the air instantly, hinting at the potency of the medicine.

The woman put the pill in her mouth, letting it dissolve, before kissing Crimson Tiger and feeding him the medicine.

Within moments, color began to return to his face.

One of the woman's companions approached her and bowed respectfully. "It's time to go, Empress."

The woman referred to as the Empress stared silently at Crimson Tiger's face, then gently caressed his cheek.

His eyelids fluttered, and his lips twitched as he struggled to open his eyes. Soon, he would regain consciousness.

"They're coming. You must go," the woman's attendant reminded.

With a reluctant sigh, the woman laid Crimson Tiger back on the ground. Once again, a path of pure white cloth appeared in midair, and she stepped on it, leaving the area. 

Still, at the last moment, she glanced back at him. A single teardrop glistened in her eye.

SWOOSH!

The hundred women rose into the air in unison, vanishing as mysteriously as they had appeared.

{sep}

"Crimson Tiger! Wake up, Crimson Tiger!"

When Crimson Tiger awoke, he found Yeon holding him. 

"Finally... you're awake," she rasped in relief.

"Yeon..." Crimson Tiger whispered, before looking around. "...Where is she?"

"She? Who?"

Crimson Tiger hesitated. Although the experience felt like a fantasy or a dream, he had a strong feeling that he had just seen a certain woman.

*Was I hallucinating?*

"Are you alright?" Yeon asked.

Crimson Tiger nodded. "Well, I'm not about to die anytime soon."

"That's a relief..."

A single tear streamed down Yeon's face.

Crimson Tiger panicked. "Yeon!"

"It's nothing. My eyes are just tired," Yeon quickly insisted, wiping away her tear. She carefully laid Crimson Tiger back down on the ground. "Please wait a moment."

Running over to the Evil Blade's corpse, she stabbed it several times with Hyeoru's sword. Once finished, she placed the sword back in Hyeoru's hand and slashed Hyeoru's body with the Evil Blade's dao. 

Since both bodies were already badly damaged, determining the exact cause of death would be difficult. Given the stab wounds, most would assume that Hyeoru and the Evil Blade had killed each other.

To finish the cover-up, she retrieved the Great Asura Illusory Life Needle from the Evil Blade's clothes.

"First, we need to get out of here," she said, lifting Crimson Tiger into her arms.

FWOOSH!

The pair left the area.

All that remained were the two men who had 'fought to their deaths', as Crimson Tiger intended.

{sep}

"Hyeoru is dead," Milyeong reported somberly.

"What?" Yagong's jaw dropped in shock. Hyeoru was the strongest martial artist among his subordinates. The man was as clever as he was cruel and boasted that his movement arts ranked among the top ten in the world. 

He shot up from his seat, countless thoughts swirling in his mind. "Hyeoru is dead? Why? Who killed him? Don't tell me Crimson Tiger took him down?"

"No, it wasn't Crimson Tiger."

"Then who was it?"

"It was the Evil Blade."

"What?" Yagong blinked in bewilderment. This news shocked him even more than if Crimson Tiger had killed Hyeoru. The Evil Blade was one of the Evil Coalition's top experts, which meant an outside party had butted into the Association's internal affairs. "Wait... are you saying the Evil Blade was the one who snatched the evidence from Crimson Tiger?"

"It seems so."

"DAMN IT AAAAAAAALL!"

SLAM!

Yagong pounded his fist on the desk. If Hyeoru's opponent was the Evil Blade, then his death was understandable, but the knowledge offered him no comfort. "If Hyeoru is dead, then… what about Crimson Tiger?"

"He was severely injured. On top of that, the Two Immortals, who were also at the scene, were grievously wounded."

"The Two Immortals were involved, too?"

"Yes, from the looks of it, the Evil Blade succumbed to the collective efforts of the Two Immortals, Hyeoru, and Crimson Tiger."

Yagong nodded. The Evil Blade might be skilled, but numbers ultimately mattered more than individual prowess. Not to mention, the experts who had ganged up on him all ranked among the Association's best. Still, he asked hopefully, "So, what happened to the evidence?"

"It fell into the river during the fight and was swept away by the current."

"Fuck!" Yagong swore, but he couldn't blame Crimson Tiger. The Evil Blade and the Two Immortals had been involved, and Crimson Tiger had reportedly sustained a fatal injury. The secret agent had likely done all he could against impossible odds.

Nevertheless, Hyeoru's death was a great loss. He was a useful man, in many ways.

Yagong sighed, "What should I do now?"

"You must feign ignorance, " Milyeong advised carefully. "Erase all traces of your connection to Hyeoru and act like you don't know anything."

"Handle it that way for me."

"I understand."

Milyeong departed hurriedly.

Yagong stared blankly into space. "...Are the heavens not on my side yet?" he lamented.

{sep}

Not far away, similar dejected sighs resounded through the First Young Lord's office.

"It would've been better if that evidence had been handed over to the Evil Coalition," Jin Chung mused.

Baek Museong tilted his head quizzically. "Why do you think so?"

"They'd surely try to control the Third Young Lady with it."

"I presume so."

"And then we might be able to obtain evidence of her conspiring with the Evil Coalition."

Baek Museong nodded, though deep down, he didn't agree with Jin Chung.

*I'm glad the evidence didn't fall into the Evil Coalition's hands. This isn't something those villains should be sticking their noses into.*

While he might not be an archetypical idealistic orthodox hero, he liked to think he still possessed at least some semblance of a conscience. While Jin Chung was hell-bent on making him the successor by any means necessary, he couldn't allow himself to stoop to that level.

*Excessive obsession always narrows one's perspective. Just look at what happened to my 'darling' wife, Sin Yeongyeong.*

"How's Crimson Tiger faring?"

"He's still in a coma. From the looks of it, he won't be out of the woods for a while."

"Considering he was attacked by the Evil Blade, it's a miracle he even survived."

"He's lucky, and truly tenacious."

"Luck is a skill too, and he's tenacious because he's skilled," Baek Museong pointed out. "Anyway, what should we do now?"

KNOCK KNOCK!

A subordinate burst into the office and urgently reported, "My lord, the Third Young Lady has awoken. She claims that this incident was a conspiracy by the Evil Coalition involving the Evil Blade, and that you were simply ensnared in that conspiracy and framed for a crime you didn't commit."

"What?" Baek Museong's eyes widened, a rarity for someone as unflappable as he usually was. "Why would she do that?"

Jin Chung went blank in confusion. "What on earth is she plotting now?"

Baek Museong narrowed his eyes. Ju Hwayin wasn't a person who would ever do him a favor. There was no way she would save him when he was practically condemned to irreversible doom.

After a long moment of contemplation, he arrived at a conclusion.

"Aha! Clearly, her plans have gone awry. Something must have happened that forced her to abandon her plans. Perhaps Crimson Tiger has indeed succeeded in his mission."

Jin Chung nodded in agreement. "It seems so."

Baek Museong's eyes glinted coldly. Whatever the truth, an opportunity for a turnaround had come.

"Now's our turn to strike back. Resume the operation we temporarily halted."

{sep}

RUMBLE!

A stone wall slid open with a deafening clamor.

"It's really opening!" the older of the two men present exclaimed.

"I told you! This one isn't a fake," the young man retorted, waving the treasure map in his hands. "The daughter of the Song Crow Sect Leader was kidnapped because of this, and the Void Darkness Fist Baek Oh had Bong Yang assassinate an Association captain to get it. In the end, though, I guess Heaven decides who the treasure should belong to! Destiny placed it in my hands! Hahahaha! This is it! The place where Sword God Dok Yahaeng's legacy is hidden!"

The young man couldn't contain his awe and excitement as he peered beyond the stone door. Everything he had done to obtain the treasure map was well worth it.

"Are you going to go in there?" the older man asked worriedly.

"Of course," the young man replied.

"Even if the map is real, it could be dangerous."

"One must be willing to take risks to obtain treasure."

"Young Master! Please don't do this. You should at least inform your father about this before you...!"

The young man's face darkened. "If you're scared, then go back alone. I'll proceed alone."

The older man sighed. He had served the youth for a long time and knew better than to argue with him when he was being stubborn. "I understand. Please allow me to take the lead," he said, resigning himself to the inevitable.

He walked through the opened door first, and the young man followed behind him.

RUMBLE!

With a thunderous roar, the stone door slammed shut behind them.

To the two men, it was merely the sound of a door closing. To someone else, however, this moment marked the beginning of a new chapter of their lives.

{sep}

Jongri Mun was devastated. The Evil Coalition was in its most dire predicament in history.

"We've lost contact with everyone," Giyeong mumbled gloomily, certain that this would be his final report. He had only held on this long because of Jongri Mun's favor. Now, for Jongri Mun's sake, it was time for him to shoulder the blame.

Jongri Mun sighed weakly. "You've worked hard. I'll try to end this with just your dismissal. Still, if things don't work out, prepare to spend a few years in prison."

"I wish you success in your endeavors," Giyeong added. He was so thankful for Jongri Mun's consideration that he could have cried. Under the present circumstances, it would not have been surprising for him to be sentenced to death.

Bowing his head in a final farewell, he left the office.

Left alone, Jongri Mun sank into his chair. The empty office seemed especially spacious today.

He couldn't understand what was happening at all. Since joining the Evil Coalition, he had never experienced so much turmoil. Everything felt like it was being sucked into a giant black hole. No matter what he did to salvage the situation, it only got worse. The same happened to whoever he sent.

His eyes darted wildly around the room. "What in the world is happening? What did I overlook?"

One name naturally came to his mind.

"Crimson Tiger." He bolted up from his seat. "Could it be?"

He quickly shook his head. For a moment, he felt pathetic for trying to blame all this misfortune on a mere secret agent, brilliant as he may be.

Nevertheless, Jongri Mun couldn't shake off a terrible sense of foreboding. Somehow, from the moment they lost the Evil Blade, both he and the Evil Coalition had crossed a line—a line that guaranteed the Coalition Leader's days of relaxing in a brothel were about to end.

{sep}

"Oh, so you were here?"

A voice startled me in the middle of my training. Turning around, I saw 'her' standing beyond the low brushwood gate.

"How did you find this place?" I asked.

She opened the gate and stepped inside. "Have you forgotten already? You told me you were training in the mountains with your master."

"Did I say that?"

"Yes, you did."

I didn't remember saying that, but since my mind was a jumbled mess the day I caught the tiger, I might have said something. In any case, I wasn't entirely displeased by her visit.

She looked around the house. "Where's your master?"

"He's away."

"Aha, so you were slacking off."

"What are you talking about?"

She picked up a wooden sword that was sitting on the outdoor bench. "Wow, this is really nice! Did you carve it yourself?"

"Master carved it."

"I thought so."

I laughed dryly, but I really didn't find her actions annoying at all.

She turned her attention to the treated animal hides hanging on the wall. "Poor things."

"Who was the one who cheered so loudly when I took down that tiger...?" I retorted, but seeing tears well up in her eyes, my voice trailed off.

She spun around to face me. In contrast to the hot tears streaming down her cheeks, she wore a bright smile.

"Hahaha! Never let yourself be fooled by a woman's tears!"

{sep}

Crimson Tiger's eyes flew open. *How long have I slept?* 

His last memory was of falling asleep once more in Yeon's arms.

He looked around. Judging by the look of it, he was in a safehouse.

He slowly sat up. He hadn't thought he could, but he managed it with surprising ease.

Carefully, he circulated his inner qi. The flow was smooth, even more so than before his injury. Despite having expended so much qi, he felt more energized than ever.

*Huh? Why do I feel so good?*

In any case, his internal injuries were not as deep as he had thought.

CREAK...

The door opened, and Yeon entered carrying a large bowl of water. "Ah, you're awake!" she greeted warmly. "But you should really stay lying down."

"I feel much better now."

"That's a real relief."

Crimson Tiger smiled gratefully at Yeon. He had run a high fever all night, and she had constantly kept him cool with a damp cloth. "Where are we?" he asked.

Yeon smiled proudly. "It's a new safehouse that I prepared just in case."

Crimson Tiger nodded and examined his body. Although the wounds on his arm and chest were deep, Yeon's exceptional emergency treatment had thankfully prevented them from becoming infected.

"Did you file a report with the higher-ups?"

"I told them what I saw, exactly as it happened."

"Did they believe you?"

"Yes, because your opponent was none other than the Evil Blade."

A grin spread across Crimson Tiger's face. Of course everyone believed the story; the enemy was the Evil Blade, an unorthodox master feared throughout the land. “What about the Evil Coalition?”

"I leaked information that our Association's top experts, the Two Immortals, were involved."

"Good work."

"Also, the Third Young Lady has awoken. She announced that the Evil Coalition was behind her assassination."

"Is that so?"

"Yes. Since she was the one who came forward, everyone is inclined to believe her."

"So, the First Young Lord has overcome this crisis."

"That's right."

Crimson Tiger nodded. Ju Hwayin had fulfilled her part of the deal, so now he had to do the same. He handed the Great Asura Illusory Life Needle to Yeon. "Deliver this to her in secret." 

"Okay."

Crimson Tiger sighed. Once again, he had overcome another hurdle.

*Things are getting more difficult by the day, but I'll overcome whatever comes my way, just as I always have.*

Yeon asked anxiously, "What will happen now?"

"The First Young Lord's counterattack will begin, of course," Crimson Tiger answered matter-of-factly.

Yeon fell silent. She was tired of expressing her fears, but she couldn't find any other words to describe her feelings. "...I'm scared," she whispered.

"Don't worry, Yeon." Crimson Tiger smiled confidently and held out his hand. 

Yeon took it, and a wave of warmth washed over her.

"I'll be sure to protect you," he added.

Yeon smiled softly at his reassurance.

Crimson Tiger gazed out the window. The loud chirping of cicadas drifted in from somewhere nearby. "This summer will also pass soon..." he murmured absentmindedly.
