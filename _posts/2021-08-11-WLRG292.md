He didn't understand the final words of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. His master had said it wouldn't be today, yet in the end, he had died. Lee Sungmin couldn't comprehend why he had looked so happy in the face of death, nor could he grasp why he had to die here, now. He had known about this in advance from Jeniela. He could have avoided it if he had only wanted to.

[He probably didn't want to avoid it,] Void Monarch said, his voice indifferent. He, at least, vaguely understood the meaning behind the Chairman's last words. Having once stood before a single, unalterable path himself, the Void Monarch knew all too well what it meant to walk it to its conclusion.

"...Escape," Scarlet barely managed to speak. "We have to run."

She had received a request from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and now was the time to fulfill it, but as Scarlet looked at the back of the collapsed Lee Sungmin, she wondered how he must feel. Whatever the reason, he had just witnessed his master's death right before his eyes. What thoughts could possibly be running through his head, she couldn't begin to guess.

The Martial God stared down at the corpse of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He was dead, undeniably dead, a faint smile still lingering on his face. While blood no longer flowed from the Chairman's severed arm, it still seeped from the Martial God's own. He clutched his left forearm, the Chairman's final words echoing in his mind as he had torn off the limb.

'I was stronger than you.' The Martial God had no rebuttal. He especially could not comprehend how the technique his rival had displayed at the very end was even possible.

*Disciple.*

The Martial God's thoughts turned to Lee Sungmin. The Chairman had said he could kill him but wouldn't, that he would only take one arm. He had said he would leave what came after to his disciple. The Martial God quickly turned his head and saw the figure of Lee Sungmin, collapsed on the ground.

"We have to run...!"

Scarlet's shout finally pierced his haze, and Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet. His eyes fell upon the corpse of his master, and beyond the fading barrier, he saw the one-armed Martial God glaring directly at him.

"......"

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and gripped his spear.

[Stop,] Void Monarch warned. [That bastard doesn't look to be in good shape, but he's still stronger than you.]

*I know.* Lee Sungmin stared hard at the Martial God as the barrier separating them began to fade away.

The Martial God raised his one remaining hand.

WHOOOOOSH!

Blue enhanced qi enveloped his palm. The barrier, having served its purpose, was now blurring and disappearing. Scarlet jumped from the carriage and ran toward Lee Sungmin just as power exploded from the Martial God's hand.

-KRRRRRRR!

A spear shot forth, creating a vortex as it tore through the air, aimed squarely at the Martial God's back. Caught by surprise, the Martial God twisted his body, his perception slowed by his internal injuries and the lingering barrier. He blocked the approaching spear with his open hand.

-KABOOM!

In the wake of the massive explosion, the Martial God's face contorted as he roared, "The Spear King!"

The spear belonged to the Spear King, who glared from a distance with ice-cold eyes.

"Why are you stopping me!" the Martial God bellowed.

"I have no desire to follow a coward," the Spear King replied, his voice seething.

He had watched the entire fight, had seen Moon Queen intervene and attack alongside the Martial God. In the end, both she and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman were dead. The Spear King could not accept such an outcome. This was meant to be a one-on-one duel between two titans vying for the title of world number one, not a back-alley brawl. The fight had not been fair.

"I will no longer follow you."

"You crazy bastard...!"

[I'll buy you some time,] the Spear King's voice echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind. [Go. The Martial God may be internally injured, but he's still too much for me to handle. This is literally just buying time, so run away quickly.]

*Run? Where to?* He didn't know. Where on earth was he supposed to run? Was he supposed to leave his master's body behind? He had done nothing. The Chairman had told him not to seek revenge, but those words did nothing to calm the turmoil in his heart.

[Is there a way to escape?] A Thought Transmission sounded in Scarlet's mind. It was from the Black Dragon Knight.

Scarlet looked at him with surprised eyes. She couldn't fully trust him. Before he died, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had nullified the oath he had received from the Black Dragon Knight, which meant he was now free of any restrictions.

[You doubt me. It's understandable, but... I'll swear this much. I will help you escape from here. If there's a way.]

"......"

[Because I want to,] the Black Dragon Knight answered without hesitation. At his words, Scarlet blinked and looked at him.

GRRRRUMBLE!

The power drawn out by the Martial God vibrated through the space. There was no time. The Black Dragon Knight spoke with urgency, and with no other choice, Scarlet quickly explained the request the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had entrusted to her.

"I see." The Black Dragon Knight gave a slight nod. He looked at Lee Sungmin's back, from which inner arts were steadily pouring out, and then his body moved.

PAPAPAK!

He struck the acupoints on Lee Sungmin's back, causing his face to turn pale. The Black Dragon Knight didn't stop there, immediately striking Yehwa's acupoints as well. Grabbing their two limp bodies, he looked back at Scarlet.

"Is this enough?"

"It's more than enough."

Lee Sungmin tried to say something, but with his vocal acupoints sealed, his voice wouldn't come out. He had been caught completely off guard, but the Black Dragon Knight was also a master of a higher realm. Having intentionally moved behind him to strike, the attack had been impossible to avoid.

"Excellent!" the Martial God exclaimed, watching the Black Dragon Knight's actions.

The Black Dragon Knight, however, didn't look at him. The Martial God frowned as he watched him hand the subdued Lee Sungmin and Yehwa to Scarlet.

"...What are you doing?"

"It's already too late to go back," the Black Dragon Knight muttered. "I have already betrayed you, and... I don't think I can trust the group called Heaven Beyond Heaven anymore."

"Do you mean that?" the Martial God spat. A pressure so intense it made his skin tingle washed over him, but the Black Dragon Knight did not back down.

His distrust of Heaven Beyond Heaven stemmed from Jeniela's words. Kim Jonghyun. The Black Dragon Knight had followed the Spirit Medium's words and interfered with Kim Jonghyun, which was why Kim Jonghyun was able to remain in this world even with the power of the Demon Lord. That wasn't all. Thinking back, the Spirit Medium's words had always been difficult to accept, especially regarding the Phantom Spear. Why hadn't they killed him sooner? There had been plenty of opportunities. Because they had intentionally ignored the Phantom Spear and only applied moderate pressure, three of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens had died, and today, even Moon Queen was dead.

"Martial God, do you trust the Spirit Medium?"

"I do," the Martial God answered as if there was nothing to think about. "Today's events have only made me trust him more. The power of Moon Queen... did you not see it? The Spirit is helping us to stop The End and sent the Spirit Medium as its messenger. Today, to stop the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a pillar of The End, it had Moon Queen directly exercise its power."

"Haha..." At the Martial God's words, the Black Dragon Knight let out a dry laugh. He shook his head and muttered, "I find it impossible to trust that suspicious entity."

"So you intend to betray Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

The Black Dragon Knight didn't answer. There was no point. The very act of standing in the Martial God's way was proof of the choice he had made. He recalled the final moments of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, whose divine presence had been enough to inspire awe even in him, one who had not learned martial arts directly from the man.

A hollow laugh escaped the Martial God. His gaze swept over the Spear King, who radiated fighting spirit, and then to the Black Dragon Knight, who stood protectively before the fallen Lee Sungmin. He raised his right hand.

"It seems I've been looked down upon."

RUMBLE...!

A colossal power swirled in the Martial God's raised hand. Though he was said to be inferior to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the martial prowess he had achieved was still enough to be called the pinnacle for a human. Even though the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight were strong, they were no match for him. The Spear King swallowed hard, and the Black Dragon Knight's expression hardened.

"...What should we do?"

"Go," the Black Dragon Knight answered.

"I'm not going."

"But..."

"With the Spear King, I can at least manage to escape with my life."

Scarlet didn't try to persuade him any further. She held the subdued Lee Sungmin and Yehwa and reached into her robes, pulling out a single scroll. Inside the carriage, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had left her with a request. He had said it was unlikely, but if he were to die, or if their entire group found themselves in danger, she was to take Yehwa and Lee Sungmin and use this scroll.

It was a teleport scroll. Long-distance teleportation was a magic not permitted to humans, but very rarely, magic scrolls enabling such a feat were discovered in Dungeons. This scroll was one of them, and the destination coordinates the Chairman had entered were for Legro Forest, a vast distance away.

*Even a monster like him won't be able to chase us that far...!*

RRRRIP!

As she tore the scroll, the magic power contained within was released, and the spell manifested. The Martial God flinched in surprise and instantly unleashed the power gathered in his hand.

KRRRRRRUMBLE!

A storm of blue enhanced qi raged in all directions. The Spear King spun the spear in his hand, rotating his own enhanced qi to meet it. The Black Dragon Knight brought out all of his Dragon scales and raised his Protective Qi Aura, blocking the way in front of Scarlet, Yehwa, and Lee Sungmin to buy time until the magic was complete.

-FLASH!

A flash of foreign magic power burst forth and then vanished.

The teleport was a success. A dizzying sense of weightlessness washed over Lee Sungmin as the world around him receded into a blur. It was the same sensation he had felt riding the Fairy Horse, and he instantly knew what was happening. He watched the figures of the Martial God and his master's corpse shrink into the distance. He saw the Black Dragon Knight standing firm against the onslaught and the Spear King charging headlong into the storm.

*Ah.*

*I.*

*Couldn't do anything.*

{sep}

THUD!

They dropped from a moderate height, and Scarlet failed to break her fall. She landed hard on her bottom, letting out a pained groan as she pushed herself up. Rubbing her backside with both hands, she belatedly remembered Lee Sungmin and Yehwa. Just as she had, they were sprawled on the ground, having fallen while unable to move their bodies. It was fortunate for them that they hadn't fallen from too great a height.

"A-Are you okay?" Scarlet asked in a worried voice as she approached.

Just then, she realized a problem. How did one undo acupoint sealing? Scarlet was a mage. She had no idea how to release it.

The sound of giggling laughter came from nearby, and Scarlet frowned. *Whose kids are laughing?* She focused her eyes and looked around.

"F-Fairy Realm...?!"

Seeing the flocking Fairy Realms, Scarlet's jaw dropped. It was her first time actually seeing one. As she gaped in astonishment, a voice spoke.

"The Coalition Leader is dead."

While his acupoints were still sealed, Lee Sungmin could only move his eyes to look at Osla. She looked down at him, her brilliant butterfly wings drooping. The playful smile she always wore was gone, replaced by a look of someone about to cry.

"But it's a relief," Osla said, extending a hand. Particles of light fluttered from her outstretched fingers and covered the bodies of Lee Sungmin and Yehwa. "The Coalition Leader didn't regret his death."

The acupoint sealing was released. Lee Sungmin quickly got up and shot a resentful glare at Scarlet before looking at Osla, who stood there with her head bowed.

"...Osla."

"It's okay," she said, shaking her head. "It was what the Coalition Leader wanted."

Osla raised both her hands. Where she pointed, beautiful flowers bloomed upon the ground, and upon them, the corpse of the one-armed Unorthodox Alliance Chairman appeared.
