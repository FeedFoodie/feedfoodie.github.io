The giant's name was Jang Deuksu, though he was better known by his title, Yeokbalsan. Lee Sungmin's memories from his past life were clear on one point. Yeokbalsan Jang Deuksu was one of the masters destined to die in this very Dungeon at the hands of Wiji Hoyeon.

"Why are you here?"

Jang Deuksu's gaze remained wary, but the raw hostility from his initial, unannounced attack had vanished. He was looking at the man who had earned the title Chang Gwi by killing the Sword Phantom, the disgraced sect leader of the Mount Hua Sect. Though Lee Sungmin's whereabouts had become a mystery since that day, his victory held symbolic weight for the orthodox sects. The Sword Phantom, after all, was a universally acknowledged demonic practitioner who had fled after murdering Daoist Seongha. It was only natural for a man like Jang Deuksu to feel a certain kinship with the young hero who had put him down.

"...I was just passing through the area by chance," Lee Sungmin began, crafting his lie. "I saw a strange red crystal standing here and touched it out of curiosity."

*You're lying through your teeth,* Rubia grumbled from within the folds of the cloak draped over his shoulder. Lee Sungmin mentally snapped at her to be quiet and turned his attention back to Jang Deuksu's reaction.

"Hmm... It seems this is your first time in a Dungeon."

"This place is a Dungeon?" Lee Sungmin asked, feigning ignorance.

At his words, Jang Deuksu nodded firmly. "We came here to stop Wiji Hoyeon. You must know who she is, right?"

"Of course I do."

It was certain now. The events of his past life were unfolding. The masters Wiji Hoyeon had killed here were the very ones who had come to stop her.

"Why do you want to stop Wiji Hoyeon?" Lee Sungmin asked. He thought the question might provoke the giant, but he didn't care.

"Because she's dangerous!"

Contrary to his worries, Jang Deuksu found no offense in the question. Instead, he spat the words out passionately, his belief completely unshakable.

"In the current murim, no, in this whole world of Zenavis, weirdos from all sorts of dimensions are summoned every day, but in the last ten years, has there ever been anyone as powerful and impure as Wiji Hoyeon?"

"...Impure?"

"She calls herself the Little Heavenly Demon! If you've learned martial arts, you must know what the title of Heavenly Demon symbolizes and just how dangerous that is, right?"

"I'm not from the murim."

"What...?" Jang Deuksu looked surprised at the answer, but he soon composed himself and nodded. "Well... I suppose that's possible. In any case, Wiji Hoyeon, who calls herself the Little Heavenly Demon, is a dangerous being. She's already demonstrated her superior strength by cutting off the left arm of the Blood Heavenly Demon of the North. Unorthodox martial artists are already forming a faction around her. Before long, they'll threaten not only the Murim Alliance but this entire world. That is why we must stop her."

"...Is that so?"

"Of course it is! You don't seem to know much about Dungeons, but clearing one grants immense power. If the already dangerous Wiji Hoyeon obtains that power, she'll truly become a great demonic practitioner we have no way of dealing with. That's why we came here to stop her."

After his impassioned speech, Jang Deuksu strode forward. A hand as large as a cauldron lid clamped down on Lee Sungmin's shoulder.

"I want you to help."

"...How?"

"Wiji Hoyeon and her followers, the Heavenly Demon Army, are already inside this Dungeon. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo, Blood Granny, the Demon Wolf Iron Fist. All of them are demonic leaders who've surpassed the realm of Supreme Peak."

Lee Sungmin, who had been listening quietly, reacted to those names. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo. Blood Granny. The Demon Wolf Iron Fist. He searched the memories of his past life, but there was no record of three people with those titles dying in this Dungeon.

"What are you asking about? I'm telling you, those three followed Wiji Hoyeon right into this place."

It was different from his past life.

*Wiji Hoyeon gained fame faster than in my past life. In my past life, Wiji Hoyeon and the Blood Heavenly Demon never clashed. However, in this life, she became famous quickly by clashing with him. That's why she gathered followers more quickly.*

"Surely you didn't come to stop Wiji Hoyeon all by yourself, Jang Deuksu?"

"Of course not! The warriors of the Murim Alliance, myself included, have been monitoring that monster ever since she started to rise in power."

"Besides you, who else has entered this Dungeon?"

"Silent Flash Baek Sogo, Chwi Geol of the Beggars' Gang, the Peerless Eccentric, and Geuk Cheondo are here."

They were all names Lee Sungmin knew. They were the masters who had been killed by Wiji Hoyeon in his past life. With their names confirmed, he was able to grasp the general situation. As Wiji Hoyeon's power grew, she had drawn followers like the Phantom Sword of Dokgo, Blood Granny, and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist into this Dungeon with her. To stop them, the warriors of the Murim Alliance, including Baek Sogo, had entered as well.

*The Sword Phantom is dead, so he didn't come. I came instead. Still, the numbers don't add up.*

In his past life, Wiji Hoyeon had killed over ten people in this Dungeon and came out alone. Although three who weren't supposed to be here had been added, the numbers still didn't match.

*More people must come later. Or maybe they won't come at all.*

He already knew that things didn't always follow the path of his past life. While Lee Sungmin was lost in thought, Jang Deuksu's intense gaze bored into him.

"Lend us your strength."

"...However... Jang Deuksu, why are you here alone? Where are the others?"

"Well... umm. To be honest, I don't know either," Jang Deuksu admitted. "A Dungeon is not a place bound by common sense. We all entered together, but when I came to my senses, I was here by myself. It seems we've been scattered."

The situation was not good. If they were scattered and couldn't find each other, it was possible that Wiji Hoyeon and Baek Sogo had already run into each other.

"I understand. I'll help you."

At Lee Sungmin's answer, Jang Deuksu's mood brightened, and he let out a boisterous laugh.

As Lee Sungmin walked behind the giant, Rubia's voice hummed in his mind.

[Hold on a moment. I'm analyzing the Dungeon's structure.]

*Will you even learn anything by analyzing it?*

[...You... seem to be underestimating me. I am the greatest familiar, created by the archmage Envirus with all his effort. It's a little embarrassing to say it myself, but among all the artificial lifeforms created so far, there's probably none as outstanding as me.]

*That doesn't mean much when Envirus himself isn't even famous.*

[That's! Because my master hates getting caught up in rumors...]

*Just try it for now.*

He didn't want a long argument. When Lee Sungmin cut her off, Rubia muttered under her breath.

"Hm?"

Jang Deuksu, walking in the lead, stepped on something. Hearing that small sound, Lee Sungmin felt a chill run up his spine.

[Oh, dear.]

Rubia sighed.

The space shimmered. A Dungeon is not bound by common sense. Watching the phenomenon unfold, Lee Sungmin understood what that meant. The ceiling vanished and the walls disappeared. Immediately after, a foul stench assaulted his sense of smell.

"What is this stench...!" Jang Deuksu shouted, his face contorting.

Lee Sungmin had to swallow the boiling killing intent he felt as he watched the back of Jang Deuksu's head, who was stomping his feet as if he knew nothing. Before they knew it, the place where they stood had transformed into a winding, single path. Jang Deuksu looked around, clutching his nose and grumbling.

"Where are we now?"

"How should I know?" Lee Sungmin grumbled and looked around.

Just then, Jang Deuksu lifted the axe he carried on his back. As he tried to strike the red wall with his axe, Lee Sungmin, horrified, blocked his path.

"Wait, wait! What in the world are you doing?"

"First, we have to break through the wall."

"Shouldn't we explore first?"

"There's no time!" Jang Deuksu roared.

Lee Sungmin genuinely wanted to kill him.

[...Hmm.]

Still, Jang Deuksu wasn't a complete idiot. When Lee Sungmin blocked his way, he didn't swing his axe, though he wore an uncomfortable expression.

[This is a strange place. The wall... no, I don't know if you can even call that a wall. It's like a mass of flesh. Don't touch it carelessly. Like I said, a Dungeon is a place that deviates from common sense. It's a place where strange powers, like magic but not quite magic, are all tangled together.]

*...So?*

[You should get ready. Something's about to happen.]

Rubia warned. It was just as she said. The floor, made of red flesh, squirmed, and something shot up from it. What revealed itself was a hideous monster.

"...What is this now?" Jang Deuksu muttered as he looked at the monster that had shot up.

The writhing mass of flesh looked at Jang Deuksu, then at Lee Sungmin. Soon, the monster's form changed.

The transformed monster was wearing martial arts robes. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he watched the monster's face squirm and twist.

"...Miss Baek?" Jang Deuksu said blankly.

The unknown monster had taken the form of Baek Sogo. The creature, a perfect replica of Baek Sogo, stood there motionlessly. Then, with a creaking sound, it turned its head and looked at Jang Deuksu.

Its form vanished. Lee Sungmin flinched and took a step back. Unlike him, Jang Deuksu's reaction was a little slow.

"Uh, uh..."

PWAAAK!

The monster's kick, appearing out of thin air, slammed into Jang Deuksu's chest. His mouth flew open, and black blood spurted out. It was a heavy blow, but Jang Deuksu was also a master. The Rebounding Qi he had summoned in that split second protected his body.

He knew.

Lee Sungmin watched the monster as it hung in the air from the recoil of its kick. He knew the movement arts it had just displayed. The solar eclipse of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Traceless Step. The same movement arts Lee Sungmin had trained relentlessly on Mount Mushii, in Shaolin, and in the Mental World.

Why was the monster using that movement arts?

Why did that monster have the appearance of Baek Sogo?

"Wait... wait! Miss Baek! What in the world is...!"

Jang Deuksu couldn't grasp the situation. Despite having seen the monster transform into Baek Sogo's shape with his own two eyes, he still called it Miss Baek. Lee Sungmin found that name disgusting. He gripped his spear.

[That's... a Doppelganger. No, it's a little different from a typical Doppelganger. Hey, are you listening?]

He knew. That wasn't Baek Sogo. It was just a monster in the shape of Baek Sogo.

[How about you calm down a little?]

*I won't,* Lee Sungmin answered in his mind.

Jang Deuksu continued to shout foolishly at the monster that looked like Baek Sogo. Lee Sungmin grabbed the shoulder of the giant who was blocking his way. Jang Deuksu turned to look at him with a surprised expression, but Lee Sungmin didn't explain. He didn't think anything good would come out if he opened his mouth anyway.

"Wait, what are you doing..." Jang Deuksu started to speak.

He didn't listen. Lee Sungmin only looked at the standing monster. The monster that resembled Baek Sogo. It blinked its eyes, which were just like Baek Sogo's, looked at Lee Sungmin, and then took a step forward.

One Step Shadow Clone. Dozens of afterimages descended on Lee Sungmin. He didn't move but raised his spear. He had no intention of using the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break... he wanted to show it directly to Baek Sogo, not this monster. Back then, on that mountain. To show her how much the boy who learned this martial art from her had grown. To show her how the kid who struggled with every single step had become like this now.

*You've grown up a lot.*

On the night he killed the Sword Phantom, how happy he had been to hear those words from Baek Sogo.

He wanted to hear those words once more.

Dozens of afterimages moved fiercely. Lee Sungmin didn't know how outstanding the 'real' Baek Sogo's Dancing Shadow Soul Break was. The technique she had shown him on Mount Mushii and the one she could use now would be on completely different levels. However, one thing was certain. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break this monster was using was superior to the one Baek Sogo had shown him on Mount Mushii.

However, he was not deceived. The red spear tassel fluttered, and dozens of thrusts shot forward.

The afterimages, pierced by the spear, all scattered.
