WHOOOOSH!

A sound like the wailing of the sea itself tore through the air.

An embodiment of all things ominous, the Undead stood at the center of the deck, drawing the attention of every soul aboard. A pair of gleaming red eyes scanned the surroundings from within its face guard, their slow sweep taking in the scene.

His gaze finally stopped on the Hundred-Faced Thief, who was sprawled across the floorboards. With a metallic groan, the Death Knight strode forward.

"Hi... Hiiik..."

The Hundred-Faced Thief began to crawl backward, his fingers scraping uselessly against the wood.

"......"

A sword was drawn from the Death Knight's waist. Unlike its worn and battered armor, the blade was so sharp it seemed it could cut a man just by looking at it. The sword plunged down without a shred of mercy, piercing straight through the Hundred-Faced Thief's body.

"KAAAAK!"

Lee Sungmin did not interfere with the Death Knight's actions, for he felt no obligation to protect the man. Flailing like a harpooned fish, the Hundred-Faced Thief writhed in agony. The Death Knight looked down at him and twisted the impaled sword. A gruesome sound of tearing flesh followed, and the man's body was ripped apart. Soon, unable even to scream, the Hundred-Faced Thief went limp. The Death Knight pulled its sword from his back and began to move once more.

Everyone watched its movements, barely daring to breathe.

The Death Knight headed for the handmaidens the thief had brought with him. They died just as their master had, their painful ends carried out with cold efficiency. With a final creak of armor, the Death Knight started moving again, its gaze sweeping over the sailors and mercenaries on the deck one by one.

Zod felt as if he was about to lose his mind. His voyage had begun with such high hopes, a journey he thought would connect him with the Delard Family through Angray. Now, everything had gone wrong. He was facing some unknown Ghost Ship and an armored monster in what felt like a terrible nightmare. Zod trembled, clutching his trousers. He had just changed into a fresh pair, but they were already soaked with urine again.

"......"

The Death Knight's gaze came to rest on Lee Sungmin. Its red eyes flickered, and a low voice, like one speaking from the depths of a cave, emerged from the helmet.

"Are those humans your prey?" the Death Knight asked.

*Prey?* Lee Sungmin tilted his head, not understanding the question. Seeing his blank expression, the Death Knight spoke again.

"The humans I killed. They were trespassers in our territory. We pursued them after they fled, and we killed them. Are you dissatisfied?"

"No, I don't care."

"Those humans," the Death Knight continued, gesturing to the terrified crew. "If they are your prey, I will not touch them."

It seemed the Death Knight had no desire to fight him. *Is it because it senses I've reached the Transcendent Realm?* Lee Sungmin couldn't tell, so he simply tilted his head again.

[No. It's not because you're in the Transcendent Realm,] the Void Monarch said. [It seems to recognize you as a Yokai.]

Lee Sungmin nodded in understanding. Death Knights were also among the inhuman. They were monsters born from the deaths of knights and martial artists who had contracted with a Demon Lord, similar to a Lich but far worse.

"...They are my prey."

He had no intention of actually eating them, but Lee Sungmin gave that answer because he wanted no further involvement with the Death Knight. A single one might not have been a problem, but the Ghost Ship docked right beside Zod's trade ship was deeply unsettling.

The Death Knight turned. Just as it was about to board the Ghost Ship, its body froze. It turned its head back toward Lee Sungmin.

"Wait."

The Death Knight fixed its gaze on him.

"The captain wishes to see you."

"What?"

"He will not harm you."

*The captain?* Lee Sungmin glanced toward the Ghost Ship. As he hesitated to answer, the Death Knight spoke again.

"I would prefer you not to refuse. The captain's command is absolute. If you refuse, I must forcibly bring you before him."

"...Are you saying that because you're confident you can?"

"No, I am not confident," the Death Knight replied, shaking its head. The armor clanked with every movement. "I cannot force you on my own. However, if I fall to you, every Death Knight on that ship will come down to drag you away."

[Wouldn't it be better to follow him for now?]

*Board a ship teeming with Death Knights?*

[There's no need to be so negative. Death Knights don't hesitate to kill humans, but they aren't so violent toward other inhuman species.]

"The captain said he will not harm you on that ship," the Death Knight repeated. "This, the captain promised on his name, Volander Evrail."

[...Vollander?]

The Void Monarch reacted to the name. *Do you know him?* Lee Sungmin asked. After a brief silence, the Void Monarch nodded.

[If Vollander has promised on his name, you can trust it.]

*Who is he?*

[The Black Star of the Predators.]

Vampire Queen Jeniela, Archlich Arbes. The Red Ghost, leader of the Yokai, had met his end at Yana's hands, and since Yana was not a Yokai of human origin, she could not rise to become one of the Black Stars. Lee Sungmin had not yet met the King of Lycanthropes, nor had he even heard his name. The only Lycanthrope he knew of was Ju Won, once called the Mad Wolf.

[Vollander is the leader of the Death Knights,] the Void Monarch explained. [A few hundred years have passed, but ha! I never imagined he'd be out on the sea.]

*Can I trust him?*

[The decision is yours. However, the Vollander I knew was a man who never broke a promise made on his name. Well, who knows. Hundreds of years have passed, so it's possible he's become skilled at lying.]

"I'll follow," Lee Sungmin said to the Death Knight. Just as the Void Monarch had said, hundreds of years could have changed the man, but Lee Sungmin decided to trust his opinion anyway.

[You little brat, how cute.]

*Shut up.*

Lee Sungmin leaped from the deck with the Death Knight, landing squarely on the Ghost Ship. The darkness that had turned the surroundings to night was flowing from this very vessel. The gloom and the single Death Knight had been one thing, but the moment he landed on the deck, Lee Sungmin realized just how insignificant the ominous feeling he had felt before truly was.

An aura of death and a dense, suffocating ominousness, thick enough to evoke images of hell itself, drifted across the Ghost Ship's deck.

A clanking sound echoed from all directions. Lee Sungmin kept his senses sharp, scanning toward the source of the noise. The sound came first, and then their forms were revealed. The drifting darkness coalesced into bodies, which were then cloaked in armor. Before he knew it, hundreds of Death Knights stood on the deck, surrounding him.

"He is the captain's guest," the Death Knight who had guided him announced. At his words, the other Death Knights took a few steps back. Their very existence evoked death, yet Lee Sungmin perceived that what they directed at him was not killing intent, but curiosity.

"This way."

The Death Knight walked ahead. Lee Sungmin followed, his hand stroking the spear on his back.

An incredible force. Honestly, he couldn't help but be astonished. There were hundreds of Death Knights on this massive ship, knights who did not fear death because they were already dead. Among them, he wondered just how many had reached the realm of the Supreme Peak. Even by a conservative estimate, dozens were at that level.

[The time they've lived is different,] the Void Monarch muttered. [They were knights and martial artists when they were alive. Even after being corrupted by a Demon Lord's magic and becoming Death Knights, they remain knights and martial artists. That is why they are strong.]

*It's absurd.*

[What is absurd? The weakness of humans? Isn't the fact that humans can be stronger than beings who live for hundreds of years the truly absurd thing?] The Void Monarch laughed heartily. [Don't be mistaken. In this world, humans are not a species with any great advantage other than their sheer numbers. If Elves had a higher birth rate, this world would belong to the Elves. If Yokai were born more easily, it would belong to the Yokai. There are plenty of others who could have taken humanity's place.]

*I know.*

[It seems this ship is Vollander's fortress. The level of the gathered Death Knights is extraordinary. Are you surprised?]

*Yeah.*

[Hahaha! Among the Predators, in terms of sheer force, the Death Knights are comparable to the Lycanthropes. Though if we're talking about the strongest of the five stars, it would be one of Jeniela, Vollander, or Howon, the King of Lycanthropes.]

Lee Sungmin thought of Jeniela. The woman he had met in Trabia was a monster, arguably the strongest among the many powerful individuals he had ever encountered. He couldn't grasp the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, as it was too mysterious, but Jeniela's power, which dominated space with fear by her mere presence, was greater than that of the Sword Saint.

[Of course. If the Five Stars of the Predators were weaker than the Six Honored Ones, Heaven Beyond Heaven would have crushed the Predators long ago.]

The Death Knight led Lee Sungmin into a hallway that ran toward the cabins. The corridor was bare, with no decorations. The floorboards creaked with every step, and that sound mingled with the clanking of the Death Knight's armor to create an unpleasant noise.

Finally, the Death Knight's steps halted. He silently took a few steps back.

"Go in. The captain is waiting."

The door opened.

Lee Sungmin slowly stepped inside. *Ah.* He realized his thinking had been wrong. The darkness that turned the sea to night, the dense aura of death on the deck, he had thought it all flowed from the ship itself, but that wasn't it.

The darkness, the aura of death, the one standing at the center of it all was that Death Knight.

Vollander was sitting in a worn chair. His armor was a clear, almost transparent black, engraved with intricate red patterns. From within a sleek helmet, a golden light gleamed in his eyes. The moment he faced Vollander, Lee Sungmin felt his heart begin to pound. The Black Heart embedded in his chest was reacting to the aura emanating from the captain.

"You are not a Yokai," Vollander muttered. "Your body is that of a Yokai, but you don't have the feel of one."

"What is the feel of a Yokai?"

"The smell of human flesh," Vollander answered. "Most Yokai eat human flesh. There might be a difference in how much or how little, but they do."

"I could be a Yokai who doesn't eat human flesh."

"It makes no sense for a being with such a ferocious, ominous aura not to eat human flesh. I see. Are you the human Jeniela spoke of, the one with the Void Monarch?"

[Time certainly has passed,] the Void Monarch grumbled. [When I was alive, I met the monsters of the Predators. Jeniela, Ju Won, Howon, Vollander, the Red Ghost, and others. Vollander was strong back then too, but it seems he's grown even stronger after several hundred years.]

"Why did you call me here?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Curiosity," Vollander answered without hesitation. "I did not intend to hunt you. I am not fond of indiscriminate slaughter."

"I heard you annihilated the pirates for trespassing in your territory."

"That's a different story. Don't humans also consider those who trespass on their territory as enemies?"

"They don't kill them unconditionally."

"We are not human," Vollander stated. "We were once human, but now we are corrupted monsters who are no longer human."

"So you killed them? Because you're not human?"

"You're misunderstanding something. I annihilated them because they were 'pirates' who invaded our territory. If they had been a merchant ship or a fishing boat, I would have given them a warning and driven them out, but they were pirates. That is why I killed them."

Vollander rose to his feet.

"You have the body of a Yokai, yet you are not a Yokai... and you even exude the aura of a Dragon."

"...What?"

"The aura of a Dragon emanates from you," Vollander said, staring at Lee Sungmin. "The arrogance of a Dragon, the belief that they are superior to all other beings in the world. The pressure that comes with it is faintly wafting from you. Surely you aren't a young Dragon in polymorph form."

"I'm not."

"Then what are you, a human in a Yokai's body who also gives off the aura of a Dragon?"

Vollander slowly approached, his steps stopping a few paces away from Lee Sungmin.

"A strange thought occurs to me," he said, his golden eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin. "I'm starting to think you might be the Slaughter Predator."
