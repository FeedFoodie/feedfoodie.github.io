Lloyd looked just as he had years ago, from his golden hair to the fine, distinguished wrinkles around his eyes. He removed the glasses perched on his nose and closed the book he was reading as Rubia bowed her head deeply before his plush chair.

"It's been a while, Lloyd."

"That it has. Seven years, isn't it? You haven't aged a day."

"Neither have you."

Lloyd chuckled at her reply. While he suppressed his aging with magic and mana, Rubia, an artificial Elemental, did not age at all. His gaze shifted from her to Lee Sungmin, and after a moment of quiet observation, he let out a low sound of admiration.

"You've changed a lot. It's hard to believe you're the same person."

"You recognize me?"

"Your face is much the same, and I have a good memory. I'm not shameless enough to forget a promise I made."

As he spoke, Lloyd rose from his chair. With a light wave of his hand, the quiet study dissolved around them, reforming in an instant into a neat reception room. It was a display of high-level spatial magic performed with startling ease.

"Have you decided on your favor?"

"I haven't decided yet."

"Huhu! I can't even guess what kind of incredible thing you're planning to ask for. I'm not omnipotent, so please don't ask for something too grand."

"I understand," Lee Sungmin answered with a bitter smile.

Lloyd gestured for them to sit. As they took their seats opposite him, he snapped his fingers, and three teacups appeared on the table between them.

"If you haven't decided on your favor, what brings you to me? I doubt you came all this way just to share some tea."

"It's about my master," Rubia began to explain.

At her words, Lloyd's golden eyes lit up. He stared at her for a moment before his gaze moved back to Lee Sungmin.

"Rubia. Are you acting with this young man under my Master's will?"

"Of course."

"It's not that I don't trust you, but my Master is a secretive person. If you'd come to me alone, it would be one thing, but since you've come with this young man, I need proof."

"I'll swear on my Mana," Rubia declared without hesitation. "As Envirus's familiar, I am acting with Lee Sungmin under Envirus's command."

Lloyd felt the fluctuation in the flow of Mana and nodded, accepting her oath.

"My Master came to see me about two months ago."

"I knew it!" Rubia exclaimed, a bright smile spreading across her face.

Lee Sungmin remained silent, watching Lloyd. Envirus was a being like him, one whose causality was twisted, a mage who had overcome Denir's Trial. Lee Sungmin had wanted to meet the man, but following his trail to the Sleeping Forest had only led him to Rubia and the sealed Yokai, the Void Monarch.

"What did my master say? Why didn't he come himself?"

"He said he was sorry," Lloyd said, his smile turning bitter. "He wanted to come himself but couldn't. Instead, he asked you to come to the South."

"What? The South?"

Lee Sungmin was inwardly stunned. The pieces were fitting together almost too perfectly. He was already on his way to the South to learn how to handle Demonic Energy, and he had selfishly hoped to travel there with Wiji Hoyeon if he met her in Ruvus.

"He said you would meet him on your way, and... he also sent a message for you." Lloyd's gaze fell upon Lee Sungmin. "He said not to stray from the path of a human."

"Pardon?"

"To me, those words sound very negative. When a human strays from their path, they inevitably become tainted by demonic forces. That's also why I hate Black Mages."

[Hmph.] The Void Monarch reacted with indifference. [That Envirus fellow. He always liked being vague. Not to stray from the path of a human? Why would someone who says that leave me be? He must have predicted that if you and I met, I would inhabit you.]

Lee Sungmin had to agree. Rubia's ears drooped, her spirits dampened. If that was all Envirus had to say, he could have delivered the message himself. What could possibly be happening that forced him to send a message through his disciple?

"Are you aware that Preskan is in the North?" Lee Sungmin asked, recalling that Lloyd had been tracking the Black Mage.

Lloyd let out a deep sigh and nodded. "I know. I only wish I'd known sooner. Now that he's fled to the North and joined Predator, I can't pursue him."

"What do you mean?"

"The North is Trabia, the domain of the Vampire Queen, Jeniela. That ancient monster is a master of all sorts of evil arts and possesses immense physical power. If I set foot in the North, she would never ignore me, but the problem isn't just Jeniela. If I were to kill Preskan, Arbes would kill me."

"Arbes?"

[He is one of the five monsters leading Predator,] the Void Monarch answered. [The Vampire Queen, the Arch Lich, the Death Knight, the Lycanthrope, and the Yokai. These five stand at the apex of Predator.]

"Arbes, the Arch Lich, is a monster whose power is incomparable to Preskan's," Lloyd continued. "In the field of Dark Magic, Arbes stands at the pinnacle. Some say he has even seen through the darkness of the abyss. Huhu! I don't believe that. It would be the same as saying he has seen the truth that all mages seek. Nevertheless, it's an undeniable fact that Arbes is a terrifying monster. Among mages, only my Master would be able to face him."

From those words, Lee Sungmin could gauge the true extent of Envirus's power. He had never seen Arbes, but the Arch Lich was on equal footing with Jeniela. If Envirus could contend with Arbes, it meant he too was a monster of comparable strength.

{sep}

Having learned all they could, Lee Sungmin and Rubia departed from the Golden Magic Tower. As they walked, a question surfaced in Lee Sungmin's mind.

*You said you weren't part of Predator, but if the King of Monsters is also at the top... do you know who that being is?*

[I do. That is, if things haven't changed in the last four hundred years. The King of Monsters I know is the Red Ghost. He wasn't as strong as this Elder, but he was quite a powerful fellow,] the Void Monarch said boastfully.

Previously, Lee Sungmin might not have believed such claims, but after seeing monsters like Jeniela and Ju Won acknowledge the Yokai, he had no choice but to accept it as truth.

Their next stop was the Red Magic Tower, but their luck had run out.

"The Magic Tower Master is currently absent."

They were unable to meet Scarlet. After coming all this way to Ruvus, it was a disappointing turn of events. In the end, Lee Sungmin and Rubia left the tower and returned to the train station.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not return until sunset. He stumbled back with a half-dazed expression, staring blankly at the train that had already departed without him. For a long time, he just watched its retreating form.

"...The iron horse is cool," he finally muttered.

Rubia let out a hollow laugh when she saw the bundles of food, like boiled eggs and beef jerky, that he was carrying. "Country bumpkin."

"That's right. This one was a country bumpkin," the Crazy Heavenly Demon nodded, not refuting her. "So the world isn't just made up of martial artists."

When they boarded the next train, he once again claimed a window seat. The ride was short, and soon they arrived near the Central Plaza, where they took a room at a nearby inn. It was incredibly expensive, being in the heart of the bustling city, but money was no object.

After a light meal, Lee Sungmin went up to his room. Lloyd's unsettling advice not to stray from the path of a human should have weighed on him, but his mind was completely consumed by something else.

Tomorrow was the day they had promised to meet ten years ago. March 14th. A tremor ran through him at the very thought. Sensing his emotions, the Void Monarch chuckled.

[The Little Heavenly Demon I saw in the Dungeon a year ago was a young monster, but... you could be called a young monster now.]

To be approaching the Transcendent Realm at the age of twenty-four was monstrous indeed. In this wide world, martial artists who neared that realm at such a young age could be counted on one hand. The highly renowned Blood Heavenly Demon had not glimpsed it, nor had Jihak, the so-called future of Shaolin. Only those who underwent the most desperate efforts could enter the Supreme Peak, and even then, they had to overcome countless more walls just to catch a glimpse of Transcendence.

*Ten years ago, I never imagined it would be like this.*

When he had made that promise in Zenavis, he could not have imagined what he would become. No, that was a lie. He had imagined it, vaguely, but never a level this incredible.

Would this be enough to stand proudly before Wiji Hoyeon? Could he tell her that he, the one who made the promise, had come this far? How much stronger had she become? How far had she gone, and how far had he come?

He could not contain himself. He tried to calm his excitement with meditation and the Violet Dawn Divine Art, but it was useless. Maybe... no, she was definitely here. Wiji Hoyeon had to be in Ruvus right now. He wanted to use Erebrisa to find her, to go see her immediately without waiting for noon tomorrow.

However, he could not. The promise was for tomorrow. He had waited ten years. Surely he could wait one more day.

Lee Sungmin stopped meditating and left his room. In the inn's backyard, various unsharpened weapons were laid out for martial artists. He picked up a blunt staff. He wanted to go to bed early, but sleep was impossible. He needed to move.

He did not perform the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Instead, he focused on the fundamentals Wiji Hoyeon had taught him ten years ago, practicing the sweep, the parry, and the thrust. Even without his inner qi, the staff whistled sharply as it cut through the air. Fast, then slow. After swinging the staff for a long while, controlling his breath, Lee Sungmin stopped.

"Impressive."

Someone was watching. Lee Sungmin slowly turned his head. A neatly dressed man stood with his hands behind his back, observing him.

"I couldn't sleep, so I came out for a walk and happened to see something wonderful. You don't look very old. If you don't mind my asking, could I know your name and title?"

"My name is Lee Sungmin."

"So you're the Phantom Spear," the man said, bursting into laughter. "I'd heard rumors that a young man skilled with the spear had appeared. I never thought I'd meet you like this, though."

"And who might you be, Elder?"

"My name is Yoo Hojeong," he replied.

The name sounded familiar. After a moment, Lee Sungmin gasped. "Iron Armored Divine Spear!"

Yoo Hojeong, the Iron Armored Divine Spear, was one of the top spearmen in all of Eria, famous for wielding a massive spear while clad in heavy iron armor. Lee Sungmin was surprised to see him looking so tidy.

"The rumors about the Phantom Spear don't do you justice," Yoo Hojeong smiled broadly. "You're no mere prodigy. When it comes to the spear, you've already surpassed the level of a grandmaster. Even I couldn't guarantee victory if I were to fight you with all my strength."

"That's not true."

"Hahaha! It's not good to be too modest. I'm not just saying that. If you're willing, I'd like to cross spears with you right now. What do you say?"

"I'm sorry. I have something important to do tomorrow."

"Is that so? Then it can't be helped. Ah, just asking, but... you're staying at this inn with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, aren't you?"

Yoo Hojeong spoke casually, but his words implied he had been watching Lee Sungmin since he arrived. Lee Sungmin's expression stiffened slightly as he nodded.

"......"

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae, is a dangerous man. He has already committed several massacres. If he goes on a rampage in this city, the Murim Alliance will hunt him down and kill him for sure this time."

The Iron Armored Divine Spear was a member of the Murim Alliance. Lee Sungmin met his gaze and nodded. "That won't happen."

"I can't say anything about you traveling with him, but don't get too close. Also, since Kron, where the Murim Alliance is located, is nearby, how about stopping by after you're done with your business in Ruvus?"

"Pardon?"

"A master of your caliber would receive good treatment at the Murim Alliance. Seeing as you killed the Sword Phantom, I assume you're also a Warrior of Justice who doesn't forgive the unorthodox, though it seems you have your reasons for traveling with the Crazy Heavenly Demon for now."

It was a self-serving interpretation. Lee Sungmin gave a noncommittal nod. Yoo Hojeong smiled warmly and patted his shoulder.

"I hope we meet again soon."

With those parting words, Yoo Hojeong went inside. Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, mulling over the man's words. He had never really distinguished between orthodox and unorthodox sects. He had learned martial arts, but it was the person, not the art itself, that defined that line. For him, who was not even a true martial artist to begin with, rigidly dividing the two was a worthless consideration.

And that would not change. Yoo Hojeong had invited him to the Murim Alliance, but Lee Sungmin had no intention of going. His destination was the South.

*Orthodox sects, a Warrior of Justice.*

Lee Sungmin let out a short laugh. He was not sure about the rest, but the title 'Warrior of Justice' felt completely out of place.

He set the staff down and returned to his room. He washed up and lay down on the bed. The encounter with Yoo Hojeong had been unexpected, but moving his body had helped. He felt refreshed, and he thought he could finally get some sleep.

*Soon.*

Just sleep, and then wake up. Lee Sungmin slowly closed his eyes.
