Mushii.

The mountain was the domain of the god who presided over penance and trials. Those who wished to gain something at the end of their suffering came to Mount Mushii, where it was entirely up to them to wish for an incredibly harsh restriction. Some entered the mountain without one, while others requested a burden for themselves.

The result, however, was the same. Whether a practitioner received a restriction or not, Mushii would ultimately give them a trial, and they would undergo penance within it.

Three possible ends awaited them.

They could give up on the penance and leave the mountain.

They could overcome the penance and receive their reward.

Or they could fail to overcome the penance and die.

Every single day was hell.

Lee Sungmin's eyes opened to a familiar, crushing weight. He sat up, his body feeling heavy despite having gotten enough sleep. It was a terrible lethargy born not just of a heavy body but a heavy mind, a direct consequence of the 'heaviness' he had restricted himself with.

"Fuck."

A curse slipped from his lips. He found himself swearing more often, his mood so foul that only profanity offered a sliver of relief. He had expected Mushii to bestow a trial personally, since that was what it was called, but that was not the case. Mushii's Junior Disciple, withered like a corpse on the verge of starvation, had told him that nothing would be provided. Not food, not drink, not even a place to sleep.

That was right. Survival on this mountain was, in itself, a trial. The mountain was harsh, too cold at night and too hot during the day. The cloak with temperature-regulating magic he had received from Jack was useless against the mountain's extreme cold and heat, which crude magic could not endure.

Lee Sungmin scrunched up his face and opened his Subspace Pocket. He still had enough bread and water left, but he could not rely on it forever.

He planned to endure on this mountain for at least two years, accepting a restriction and undergoing penance in a trial. The longer it lasted, the greater the reward from Mushii would be.

In his past life, he had heard many stories of this place but had never come himself. He had seen no need to. At the time, Lee Sungmin was overwhelmed just by living as a mercenary, a life that could have been called a penance in its own right.

His reasons for coming to Mount Mushii in this life were straightforward. Behenger was not yet an option for him, and he lacked a suitable place to train. He could have stayed in Zenavis if he only needed a training ground, but he had little expectation of gaining anything from practicing martial arts at Jack's Inn for two years.

This was a gamble of sorts. He did not know what he could obtain on Mount Mushii, and even the memories of his past life held no rumors about it. He knew of people who had entered the mountain, but he could not recall any stories about those who came out.

*It's up to me.*

This was different from the opportunities he remembered and sought. Mount Mushii did not offer chances to be found, it demanded they be earned through trial and penance.

Lee Sungmin picked up the bread.

It was a meal so terrible that he felt it would be better to die.

He forced his leaden body to move, practicing his Spear Technique. He began with Rannachal, the basics Wiji Hoyeon had emphasized, then transitioned into the forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. His body and the spear were both heavy, forcing him to pour strength into his muscles and circulate his inner qi just to endure the weight.

Because of that, he tired quickly.

*This is hard.*

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath. He wondered if he had chosen the wrong restrictions, but it was far too late for such thoughts now.

{sep}

A week passed.

The meals remained unbearable. He had thought he might grow accustomed to the disgust after a week, but that had been an incredibly naive hope.

"Fucking Mushii."

He had never imagined there could be so many different kinds of 'tasteless'. Lee Sungmin still gagged every time he ate, and even the water made him retch.

*I'm lacking nutrients.*

It was only natural, since he had been stuffing himself with only bread and water. Now, both were starting to run out. He had to hunt. Dragging his heavy body through the wilderness, he set out, only to fail. The beasts living on this mountain were fast and strong, befitting a trial of survival.

On the second consecutive day of failure, CLACK.

A small pebble landed beside him. Lee Sungmin, collapsed on the ground and gasping for breath, turned his head toward the direction the stone had come from.

A woman with pure white hair was looking down at him. She raised her right hand and waved once before slowly approaching. Lee Sungmin glanced at the spear he had set down beside him, then got up with his bare hands. On Mount Mushii, practitioners could not harm one another. That was one of the absolute rules set by Mushii.

The approaching woman mouthed something, but no sound reached him. Lee Sungmin tilted his head in confusion, and the woman sighed. She pulled a long branch from her waist.

[I came to help because you looked pitiful.]

She wrote on the ground in a script he had never seen before, yet he understood its meaning. It was the common script of Eria, which all otherworlders naturally mastered upon being summoned.

"...You can't speak?"

[My voice. That is the restriction placed upon me.]

The woman quickly added more.

[I've been watching for a few days. If you keep this up, you're going to die.]

"...I'm going to die?"

[An unbalanced diet. Your body is heavy. Is that your restriction?]

"...Yes."

[Your body has weakened from the poor diet, and it's heavy. You'll keep failing to hunt, and in the end, you will die.]

"So you want to help me?"

The woman put down the branch after writing that. She scratched the back of her head and glanced around.

Then her body simply vanished. Lee Sungmin flinched, his head whipping around to find her. WHAM! The sound of a brutal impact echoed nearby.

The deer that Lee Sungmin had failed to catch for days now lay on the ground, its head smashed in. The woman dusted off her hands and lifted the deer's body.

*An expert...!*

Lee Sungmin's face turned pale. The woman approached him and set down the deer carcass she was holding.

"...What do you want from me?" Lee Sungmin asked.

The woman tilted her head at his words.

"You're not helping me for nothing, are you?" he pressed.

She blinked at his question and then smiled. Though her expression changed, no sound came out, for her 'voice' itself was restricted.

[I want nothing.]

She picked up the branch and wrote again.

[I just helped because you seemed to be in trouble. That's all. It's not a difficult task for me anyway.]

"...So you just helped me out of goodwill?"

[Goodwill? It's not goodwill, it's sympathy. I'm a kind person.]

The woman wrote that and smiled brightly.

[There's no need to think of it as a favor. Catching a beast is easy for me. Because I'm fast.]

It was more than that. Lee Sungmin was on the verge of reaching the First-Rate level, yet he could not even follow her movements. The deer had been far away, but she had moved there in an instant and beaten it to death. This woman was a great master of movement arts.

[I live around here. I see you often. If a neighbor dies, it'll give me bad dreams. So I'm helping.]

"...In the future, too?"

[If you need it. For things like this. However, from now on, try to do it yourself.]

The woman advised. Lee Sungmin nodded with a reluctant expression. Not goodwill, but sympathy. She helped him simply because she was a 'kind person'. For Lee Sungmin, it was a very unfamiliar feeling.

"...What's your name?"

The woman wrote her name on the ground.

[Baek Sogo.]

Lee Sungmin's face went deathly pale at the sight of that name.

He knew it.

Silent Flash Baek Sogo.

A martial artist who had made a name for herself during the thirteen years of his past life. A woman who was no longer alive by the time he had died.

Eight years from now, Baek Sogo would die.

The Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, would kill her.

{sep}

Three weeks after their meeting, a full month since he had first set foot on Mount Mushii, Lee Sungmin found himself growing curious about the other practitioners living on the mountain. True to her word, Baek Sogo would appear like a ghost to help him whenever he was in trouble. Her mysterious movement arts were the very embodiment of her title, Silent Flash.

[Your restrictions are bad.]

Baek Sogo picked up her branch and wrote on the ground.

[Penance is hard. To take away the joy of eating... it's the worst restriction.]

"Your restriction seems pretty convenient," he countered.

[Not really. I've gotten used to it now, but writing is still a pain.]

Baek Sogo made a disgusted face. Even so, it was probably better than the restrictions on taste and weight that Lee Sungmin was enduring.

[Is hunting still difficult?]

"My body is too heavy..."

[The level of your movement arts is low. You seem to have decent inner qi... but instead of focusing only on your Spear Technique, how about paying attention to your movement arts as well?]

Baek Sogo advised. Lee Sungmin had been thinking about that as well. He nodded his head and asked a question of his own.

"Are there no other practitioners on this mountain besides me and you?"

[Of course not.]

Baek Sogo shook her head.

[Most people come and leave after a few days, unable to endure it, but there are some who have lasted for more than half a year. I've been here for half a year now myself.]

She continued writing.

[The one who's been here the longest has endured for a year. Did you know? The longer you endure, the more restrictions are added. I had another restriction added when I hit the six-month mark.]

"...Your left arm?"

Lee Sungmin glanced at Baek Sogo's left arm. In the three weeks he had known her, he had never once seen her use it.

[That's right. I have no feeling in my left arm. I can't put any strength into it at all.]

Baek Sogo smiled bitterly.

[It's not a good restriction in the long run. If this continues, only my right arm will develop, and the balance between my arms will be thrown off.]

"...What about the others?"

[They each have multiple restrictions too. In fact, it's rare for someone like you to take on two restrictions from the very beginning. It's that difficult. However, you'll probably gain a lot more later on.]

Baek Sogo repeatedly wrote letters and erased them with her foot.

[Are you interested in the other practitioners?]

"Well... yes."

[Then I'll introduce you.]

Baek Sogo nodded and wrote. Deciding to strike while the iron was hot, she tucked the branch into her waist and began to walk with a swagger. Lee Sungmin dragged his heavy body to follow her.

"Can't you teach me one of your movement arts?" he asked.

Baek Sogo turned her head at his words. She made a dumbfounded expression, then quickly wrote on the ground.

[No matter how kind I am, I can't just give away my livelihood.]

Lee Sungmin smacked his lips in disappointment. He had only been testing the waters, but it was a shame all the same.
