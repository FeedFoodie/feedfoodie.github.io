He felt no pity. Yoo Hojeong lifted his foot and resumed his halted steps, moving forward once more. Before him stood the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon. Though she was only twenty-three, the path she had carved over the past five years was nothing short of phenomenal. She had brought down the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon and his Blood Heavenly Alliance of the North, delivering defeat and death to the countless challengers who dared to stand against her.

In truth, her actions alone were insufficient to truly brand her a demonic head. She had committed no massacres, nor had she performed any distinct acts of evil. Nevertheless, Wiji Hoyeon had captured the Murim Alliance's attention for one simple reason. She called herself the Little Heavenly Demon.

She had clashed with the Blood Heavenly Demon, who held dominion over the North, and dealt him an overwhelming defeat. She had conquered a Dungeon entirely on her own and claimed its rewards.

Wiji Hoyeon was simply too strong. To possess such strength at such a young age was terrifying. It was openly gossiped that the Little Heavenly Demon was already a martial artist who had entered the Transcendent Realm. Even among the great sect leaders and elders who had trained for decades, those who had surpassed that realm could be counted on one hand. Many martial artists never even reached the Supreme Peak, for the wall of that realm was where the average practitioner met their limit. In this vast world, even with people pouring in from other dimensions every day, the Transcendent Realm remained a legendary state of being.

Wiji Hoyeon had not acted like a demonic head, but as long as she bore the title of Little Heavenly Demon, she was a demonic practitioner. Her power was too great. If she was already in the Transcendent Realm now, who in this world could possibly stop the monster she would become in ten years?

Therefore, she had to be killed here. He did not know how the young monster had become so weak, but this was a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Justification was unnecessary. What justification was needed when the Murim Alliance itself stepped forward to eradicate a demon? Indeed, among the gathered crowd, not a single person blamed Yoo Hojeong or sided with Wiji Hoyeon. They were merely bystanders, people who considered it a stroke of luck to witness such a momentous fight.

Their silent approval and expectant gazes emboldened him. Wiji Hoyeon no longer tried to resist, her complicated expression fixed on the approaching man. The curse prevented her from properly drawing out her internal energy. Her Black Dragon Armor and the Black Clothes she had obtained from the Dungeon had sustained her until now, but even they had reached their limit.

*Is he waiting?*

A lot of time has passed, so maybe he's already left. It did not matter either way. Wiji Hoyeon foresaw her own death. For ten years she had lived without fearing it, yet its sudden arrival felt unfamiliar. She could not remain indifferent. A low laugh escaped her lips. Should she beg for her life? Should she run away?

She wanted to do neither. She had never run away in her entire life, nor did she wish to beg for her life now. Wiji Hoyeon prepared for death. She closed her eyes, listening to the metallic clang of shaking iron armor and the heavy, approaching footsteps.

Then, at some point, the sounds stopped. The wind grew rough. Wiji Hoyeon opened her eyes.

Yoo Hojeong was no longer walking forward. His eyes were wide, staring at the man who now blocked his path. Wiji Hoyeon saw that man's back as well.

A thought suddenly occurred to her. *I've never seen his back.* It was always she who led the way, with Lee Sungmin following from behind, watching her. It had been the same when she saw him in the Dungeon a year ago. She had reached the end first, and he had come looking for her.

However, it was not like that now. Lee Sungmin stood with his back to her, protecting her. Seeing it anew, she felt that his back was quite broad.

"…Lee Sungmin?" Wiji Hoyeon called out in a weak voice.

"The Phantom Spear!" Yoo Hojeong shouted, his voice thick with anger. "Do you have any idea what it means to block my path in this situation? Are you doing this knowingly?"

Lee Sungmin did not answer. He glared at Yoo Hojeong while sensing the presence of Wiji Hoyeon, who was slumped on the ground behind him. *…She's weak.* He could not understand the reason, but she was incomparably weaker than when they had met in the Dungeon a year ago.

"…What are you trying to do?"

Lee Sungmin did not look back at Wiji Hoyeon. For now, he had to deal with the man directly in front of him. In response to his question, Yoo Hojeong raised the face guard that had been lowered over his helmet and shouted.

"I am eradicating a demonic head!"

"…Are you saying Wiji Hoyeon is a demonic head?"

"Who else here could be called a demonic head besides that wench!" Yoo Hojeong exclaimed.

It was tiresome. Lee Sungmin had heard this kind of talk countless times in the Dungeon a year ago. He had no desire to get entangled in the affairs of orthodox and unorthodox sects, but in the eyes of the Murim Alliance, Wiji Hoyeon was a demon. The distinction between the two, a concept born from the murim, was disgusting, but he had no wish to blame the concept itself.

"You're going to kill Wiji Hoyeon."

"Think carefully," Yoo Hojeong warned. "I don't know why you're blocking the path to that demonic head. Because she's a woman? Because she's beautiful? If you've stepped forward for such a trivial reason and a foolish sense of heroism, then stop."

"Are you going to kill her?"

"She must be killed when she can be killed! Ten years, no, maybe it's five years! In just five years, no one in the world will be able to stop that monster. Martial arts? Magic? No matter what means are used, they are meaningless before transcendent power…!" Yoo Hojeong roared.

Only after hearing those words did Lee Sungmin turn his head to look at Wiji Hoyeon. She was staring at him, her mouth half-open. The year that had passed had not changed his face, but she could not be sure if this Lee Sungmin, meeting her gaze like this, was really the one she knew.

"…Is it you?" she asked.

"It's me." Lee Sungmin nodded. He had never imagined they would meet like this. "Are you hurt?"

"I'm fine."

"You've gotten better at lying," he muttered.

Hearing this, Wiji Hoyeon burst into laughter. Lee Sungmin turned and took a step toward her.

KUUUUNG!

The ground shook with a loud noise as Yoo Hojeong slammed the butt of his lance into the earth.

"If you lay a hand on the Little Heavenly Demon!" Yoo Hojeong unleashed his Lion's Roar. "I will not see you as a talented junior, but as a demon siding with a demon, and I will move to eradicate you."

"Do as you please," Lee Sungmin replied in a low voice.

At his response, Yoo Hojeong's shoulders trembled. "Foolish…!"

With that, Yoo Hojeong abandoned any attempt at conversation. Clad head-to-toe in steel, he charged with a speed that defied the armor encasing him. It was a powerful thrust, his giant lance leading the way. Even without a horse, the aid of his movement and inner arts gave it more power than any mounted charge.

Lee Sungmin turned and grabbed the spear slung across his back.

KWAAAANG!

A tremendous sound shook the air as the spear swung by Lee Sungmin collided head-on with Yoo Hojeong's lance. Yoo Hojeong was shocked that his weapon, which had shot forward in a straight-line charge, was knocked aside.

"……"

With a groan, Yoo Hojeong unhesitatingly drew upon his ultimate internal energy. This was not an opponent to be underestimated. He had already grasped that much after watching him practice his Spear Technique last night, but he never expected they would end up fighting like this today.

"Rest for a bit," Lee Sungmin said to Wiji Hoyeon before deploying his Magic Armor. The greatly expanded aura enveloped his entire body. In that first clash of fighting spirit, Lee Sungmin had parried the attack from a stationary position while Yoo Hojeong had charged. That did not mean, however, that Lee Sungmin's skills were superior.

"…Haste."

Yoo Hojeong opened his mouth and muttered a single word. At the sound, Lee Sungmin's eyebrows twitched. The Magic Armor Yoo Hojeong wore began to glow as his lips moved continuously. The words he uttered were activation phrases for magic, and with each one, the light of his armor changed. Yoo Hojeong's armor was not just for defense. It was enchanted with various kinds of magic that assisted his movements.

It was not just the armor. The lance he held also began to glow. Lee Sungmin stopped watching and rushed toward him. This was not an opponent he could afford to be complacent with. The Iron Armored Divine Spear Yoo Hojeong was one of the top spearmen in the orthodox murim, and now that master was clad in buff magic as well.

KWAAANG!

Spear and lance clashed, the explosive sound as loud as heavy lumps of iron smashing into each other. Yoo Hojeong did not retreat an inch. Instead, it was Lee Sungmin who felt the resistance transmitted to his hands and tightened his grip. Yoo Hojeong swung his lance, the massive weapon moving at a speed unbelievable for its size. Lee Sungmin spun his spear in a half-rotation.

KWAANG!

The spinning shaft collided with the lance. The Spear Technique Yoo Hojeong used was different from any weapon style Lee Sungmin had ever faced. He used movement arts with short strides, minimizing the motion of his shoulders and arms as he delivered short, powerful thrusts. Lee Sungmin leaned his upper body back and gripped his spear with both hands, holding the shaft with a wide grip and spinning it to block the attack. It was at that moment.

"Bomb," Yoo Hojeong muttered in a low voice.

An explosion erupted at the point of impact. The force was not very powerful, but it was enough to startle Lee Sungmin. As he staggered back, Yoo Hojeong boldly stepped forward.

"......"

Closing the distance, Yoo Hojeong powerfully thrusted his lance. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue and unfolded the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. An afterimage from his Traceless Step was pierced by the lance while the real Lee Sungmin, having moved to the side, unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

KWADEUK!

The thrust of Soul Chasing Elimination struck Yoo Hojeong's opening. It did not break through. With his Protective Qi Aura, the defense of his armor, and defensive magic layered on top, his toughness was on another level.

He could not drag this out. Right now, it was only Yoo Hojeong, but if he wasted time, other martial artists from the Murim Alliance might arrive. The situation would only get worse if that happened.

A few months ago, with his mind, ki, and body in disarray, he would not have had an easy fight, but that was then. Lee Sungmin remained unshaken and pulled back his spear. The Violet Dawn Divine Art circulated his inner arts, and his body, which had been creaking and unstable before, now responded in a completely different way.

KWAKWAKWANG!

Dozens of spear strikes shot out, repeatedly colliding with Yoo Hojeong's lance. Yoo Hojeong's speed was slower, but he used minimal movements to thrust his weapon, gradually altering the trajectory of Lee Sungmin's spear. Immediately after a thrust, Lee Sungmin readjusted his grip on the shaft and swung it. As the Changjun flew toward his temple, Yoo Hojeong let go of his lance and raised his arm to defend. As if he had been waiting for that, Lee Sungmin released his spear. Seeing his opponent rushing in with bare hands, Yoo Hojeong could not help but be flustered.

"What in the…!"

Lee Sungmin's hands grabbed Yoo Hojeong's forearm. Yoo Hojeong tried to swing his lance, but Lee Sungmin was faster, twisting his arm. Though the armor was thick and enchanted, Lee Sungmin's hands were enveloped in the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, the famous ultimate technique of the Crazy Heavenly Demon. The armor crumpled as the joint of Yoo Hojeong's forearm was twisted. He let out a short scream.

"......"

The heavy lance fell to the ground.

"You bastard!"

Yoo Hojeong was no pushover, however. He swung his other, uninjured hand to strike Lee Sungmin's face. Lee Sungmin swayed his upper body to dodge and retreated. The surprise close-quarters combat had worked well, but honestly, he was not very confident in his hand-to-hand fighting skills. When Lee Sungmin opened his hand, the spear flew from the ground into his waiting grasp, a telekinetic trick he had long possessed but rarely used.

Lee Sungmin stepped forward and swung the arm holding the shaft. The straight wood struck Yoo Hojeong's chest. He immediately spun the spear half a turn and slammed the Changjun down on Yoo Hojeong's instep. Yoo Hojeong did not scream, his Protective Qi Aura, armor, and defensive magic absorbing the blow. Confirming this, Lee Sungmin immediately twisted the spear slightly to the side, then pulled with both hands, tilting it toward himself.

"……"

Yoo Hojeong made a surprised sound as the spear shaft, which had come between his legs, struck his groin. It did not hurt, but he could not help but be surprised at being hit in such a vital spot so suddenly. As Yoo Hojeong staggered and tried to pull his leg back, Lee Sungmin lowered his stance and held the spear horizontally. The long weapon caught the back of Yoo Hojeong's right knee. When he pulled it hard, Yoo Hojeong lost his balance and fell on his butt. Lee Sungmin glanced down at him before turning away.

"…What are you doing!" Yoo Hojeong roared. "Why aren't you killing me! Do I look like a joke to you!"

"You weren't trying to kill me either," Lee Sungmin replied.

At those words, Yoo Hojeong was left speechless. Despite claiming he would eradicate him as a demon, he had not used any killing moves. All of his attacks were meant to subdue, not to kill.

"I don't particularly enjoy killing," Lee Sungmin continued. "Unless it's a situation where I have no choice."

It was true. If Yoo Hojeong had tried to kill him, Lee Sungmin would have killed Yoo Hojeong, but he had not. That was the only reason. What about the fact that he tried to kill Wiji Hoyeon? He did not want to think about that. Yoo Hojeong of the Orthodox Murim Alliance and the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. He did not want to question their relationship or the interests involved.

"…Just because you don't kill me… doesn't justify your actions," Yoo Hojeong spat at Lee Sungmin's turned back. "Protecting the Little Heavenly Demon and leaving with her means you're making an enemy of the Murim Alliance. Do you know what that means? The city of Kron, where the Murim Alliance is located, is close to here…! Even if I don't say anything, everyone here saw what you did. Now that the Little Heavenly Demon is weakened, the Murim Alliance will immediately organize an extermination squad. If you protect her… the extermination squad will also… you…"

"We'll cross that bridge when we come to it, won't we?" Lee Sungmin replied. "The extermination squad will come whether I kill you or not. If they're coming either way, then I'll just choose not to kill you."

"Such hypocrisy…!"

Lee Sungmin did not respond to Yoo Hojeong's distorted cry. He walked toward Wiji Hoyeon under the gazes of the onlookers.

"Let's go."

He extended a hand to her. Wiji Hoyeon, who was sitting on the ground, stared up at him blankly.

"…Okay."

She reached out and took his hand.
