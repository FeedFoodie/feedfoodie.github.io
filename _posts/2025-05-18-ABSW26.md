"Crimson Tiger, I had a good feeling about that fellow," Baek Museong remarked to Jin Chung, who was reflected in the bronze mirror, as he got dressed.

"……" Jin Chung remained silent, even though his master was clearly seeking his agreement.

Giving his aide a sidelong glance, Baek Museong continued nonchalantly, "He's quite charismatic, with a decent personality."

"So, are you planning to make him your subordinate or something?" Jin Chung growled irritably.

Baek Museong gestured for the maids dressing him to withdraw, then gazed at Jin Chung's reflection in the bronze mirror. "You're still upset, aren't you?"

"I am not," Jin Chung replied.

That was the truth. He was not upset at all, but nervous and restless. Sa Gon, who should have contacted him by now, still hadn't sent word.

Baek Museong smiled. He was grateful for Jin Chung's brash attitude. Someone had to resent Crimson Tiger on his behalf, because only then could he forgive his own indifference.

Of course, he had no idea that Jin Chung had gone beyond mere resentment to actually taking action.

A brief silence fell as he turned back to the bronze mirror to adjust his attire, when suddenly, Soeun's face flickered in his mind again.

He stiffened, but fortunately the arrival of visitors pulled him away from his thoughts.

They were the Fist Master, Lee Yanghyeong, and the Boomerang Sword, Taoist Scarlet. Along with a pair known as the Two Immortals, they made up the four masters who followed him. 

Also, these were the two elders whose appointments he had recently canceled.

The Fist Master Lee Yanghyeong was a master of fist techniques who had once swept through the Jiangbei[^1] murim with his two fists. He had the demeanor of a master and the magnanimous personality of a leader.

Taoist Scarlet, on the other hand, had a more aggressive temperament and was a hot-blooded individual who bluntly voiced his opinions without reservation.

Baek Museong offered a polite clasped hands greeting to the two elderly masters. "It's been a while since we last met."

The two elders reciprocated with a casual greeting. When they first came under the First Young Lord's service, Baek Museong had been little more than a greenhorn, but he had now grown into a prominent figure with the highest chance of succeeding the Association. Because of that, the two men were considering treating Baek Museong with the etiquette due their liege lord soon.

However, the timing was a very sensitive issue. Whether it should be before or after the First Young Lord officially became the successor was crucial. If they recognized him as their master too soon, and the Third Young Lady became the successor instead, they would be purged along with the First Young Lord. On the other hand, if they waited too long, they would be estranged from the First Young Lord once he gained real power.

"I managed to procure some tender, high-quality meat, so I invited you both," Baek Museong said. "I hope I'm not taking up too much of your precious time."

As if on cue, several maids entered the room carrying plates of food and laid out a feast fit for kings.

Lee Yanghyeong chuckled, "Hahaha, not at all. We're grateful that you've invited us old men who have nothing better to do."

The three men sat down, while Jin Chung stood at a distance. As he was Baek Museong's right-hand man, the two old masters paid him no mind.

Baek Museong nodded. He would have preferred to invite all four elderly masters who supported him at the same time, but Taoist Scarlet did not get along well with the Two Immortals, so he was forced to invite them separately.

It started after they had a serious dispute with them during a private meeting over a certain rumor about Taoist Scarlet's past that every master in the Association had probably heard at least once. 

Apparently, Taoist Scarlet committed the heinous act of murdering his own master and senior brother in his youth. He was also the sole survivor when his sect was allegedly attacked by unknown assailants.

Most people dismissed it as a wild, unfounded rumor, but one of the Immortals had thoughtlessly confronted him about it in a drunken stupor. The resulting conflict had very nearly escalated to the point where swords were drawn.

Since then, Taoist Scarlet and the Two Immortals had become bitter enemies and never appeared in the same place together.

Baek Museong respectfully poured liquor for the two old men.

"You must be busy with wedding preparations," Lee Yanghyeong remarked.

"My heart is busy pounding from excitement, but I'm not really doing anything else."

Lee Yanghyeong laughed, "Haha, is that so?"

"……" Taoist Scarlet silently lifted his cup.

Baek Museong observed him discreetly. "Master Scarlet, does my marriage displease you?"

To his surprise, Taoist Scarlet nodded.

"May I ask why?"

Taoist Scarlet replied without hesitation, "If you become the Lord of Sword Heaven's son-in-law, then the rest of us will all end up becoming like dogs that chased a chicken only to lose it to the roof."

"Hoho, my friend, why are you saying such things?" Lee Yanghyeong exclaimed in dismay. Although Taoist Scarlet was a straight talker, this was a meeting with the First Young Lord. A certain amount of decorum had to be maintained.

Baek Museong smiled and shook his head. "I would never abandon you guys like that."

"Even if you don't intend to, it will naturally turn out that way."

"May I ask why you think so?"

"Let's have a drink first," Taoist Scarlet raised his cup in a toast, and the three men downed their liquor. Only then did he begin, "The Lord of Sword Heaven is a very ambitious man. He will not leave us alone."

Lee Yanghyeong, who had been about to stop Taoist Scarlet, fell silent. He had a feeling that he knew what Taoist Scarlet was about to say. In fact, this was something that he himself had been a little worried about.

Baek Museong blinked in confusion. "Are you saying that he'll target you?"

"Yes, he certainly will. He wants you to become completely dependent on Sword Heaven, which makes us four old men thorns in his side."

A brief silence ensued as Baek Museong seriously considered the possibility.

*His words definitely hold some truth.*

Although their skills might be slightly inferior, the four elderly masters were not people the Lord of Sword Heaven could easily dismiss. In addition, they would undoubtedly hinder his ability to manipulate Baek Museong.

"Hmm… what should I do to prevent him from harming you?" Baek Museong asked, acknowledging that Taoist Scarlet had a point.

"Nothing. You wish to take a wife and start a family. We old men cannot get in the way of that." Taoist Scarlet picked up a meat skewer from the side dishes. "We can only pray that we don't end up impaled by swords."

"I won't allow such a thing to happen."

Lee Yanghyeong snorted. "How can you be so sure? Although Old Master Scarlet's words are harsh, I can't say that the possibility doesn't exist, either."

"Isn't there a saying that latrines and in-laws are best kept at a distance? I intend to adhere strictly to that saying."

"If you do that, you'll just end up being pressured from both the outside and the inside," Taoist Scarlet pointed out. He didn't mention it explicitly, but 'outside' referred to Ju Hwayin and 'inside' to the Lord of Sword Heaven.

Baek Museong smiled good-naturedly. Although he couldn't say it, he was confident that Taoist Scarlet's fears would not come to pass. 

Because there was one fact he knew that they didn't.

His fiancée was Sin Yeongyeong, and that bitch was anything but a conscientious housewife. If he could get her on his side, then an entirely new situation would unfold. It wouldn't be him keeping Sword Heaven in check, but Sin Yeongyeong.

[Sir, we bring urgent news.]

Jin Chung, who had been observing the drinking party off to the side, suddenly received a telepathic message from one of his subordinates.

Discreetly exiting the room, he headed to the end of the corridor where a trusted subordinate awaited him. Together, they left the building and headed toward a deserted spot of the compound.

"What happened?" Jin Chung asked.

"We've lost contact with Sa Gon."

"What? Report in detail."

"We found some of Sa Gon's men dead in the market district. The rest have all disappeared."

"All of them?"

"All of them, including Sa Gon."

"Are you saying that they were…disposed of?"

"It seems so."

"Seriously!?" Jin Chung gulped. He knew it wouldn't be easy to defeat Crimson Tiger, but he hadn't expected Sa Gon to fail.

"We're certain. The Phantoms never leave traces of their hunts. Also… it won't be long before the First Young Lord hears about this failed assault."

Jin Chung's mind reeled. He had already embezzled official funds, and he couldn't prevent the news from reaching the First Young Lord. Besides, the First Young Lord had access to other information channels besides his.

Nevertheless, there was a more pressing problem.

"Do you think Crimson Tiger knows we were behind it?" he asked worriedly.

"Sa Gon is a tight-lipped man. I believe he would die before revealing anything... but given the opponent, we cannot be certain. We must kill him as quickly as possible."

Jin Chung's expression darkened. "If even Sa Gon failed, then who on earth am I supposed to send!?"

At the same time, a hushed conversation was taking place in Crimson Tiger's quarters.

"Here's what you asked for," Yeon said, handed Crimson Tiger a map.

"You got it faster than I thought. Good work." Taking the map, Crimson Tiger spread it out on the table. "How accurate is it?"

"It's the most recent copy."

Crimson Tiger examined the map. Indeed, the number of guards of the First Gate, which he had passed through, was accurately recorded.

Yeon explained, "The First Young Lord's residence is at the eastern end of the Second Gate area, and the Third Young Lady's residence is at the western end. The defenses at the Second Gate are a problem, but so is what comes after the infiltration. Even the menial workers there are closely watched at all times, not to mention the place is swarming with martial masters."

"What about here?"

"That's the Third Gate, which leads to the Association Chairman's residence. It's absolutely impenetrable, even for you."

Crimson Tiger smiled faintly. If Yeon said that much, the security there must be indescribable. Of course, he had no reason to go that far. He only needed to pass through the Second Gate, which was watched by thirty guards.

"The walls can't be scaled, and there are no secret passages. You have to go through the gates."

"Hmm…" Crimson Tiger fell into deep thought.

*It's the same setup as the Evil Coalition. The gates can be breached forcefully, but not secretly infiltrated.*

"How strong are the martial artists guarding the Second Gate?" he asked.

"One peak master, five first-rate warriors, and the rest are second-rate."

"…Formidable," Crimson Tiger muttered, staring down at the map. With that level of security, stealthy infiltration was absolutely out of the question.

*There must be a weakness. If I can't find it, that just means I haven't searched hard enough.*

"What kind of place is this?" he asked, pointing to one of central buildings.

"It's a teahouse."

"A place for rest and relaxation?"

"Yes. It was recently built for Inner Court martial artists, but I understand it's mostly frequented by women."

Crimson Tiger grinned evilly. "A teahouse…should have good tea, right?"

Yeon sighed. She had seen that squinty, fox-eyed look before. It was the look he'd worn just before he strolled into the Ironblood Commander's residence in broad daylight, disguised as Yong Hajin.

*If I had to guess, his next words should be…*

"You know… I believe there might be a *legitimate* way for me to pass through the Second Gate."

At dawn the next morning, groceries were delivered to the rear entrance of the Hundred Flavors Restaurant.

The central food warehouse, which managed the food supplies for the Association, sorted the groceries that arrived from outside and distributed them to where they were needed. 

Crimson Tiger approached the person in charge of delivering food to the Hundred Flavors Restaurant and the other kitchens in the courtyard, a man named Heo Seung.

"You're working hard so early in the morning," he greeted as Heo Seung unloaded the goods.

"It's my job, so it's hardly an effort. But who might you be?"

"I'm the new restaurant manager, Jin Ho."

Heo Seung blinked in surprise. "Oh. Uh, is that so? I'm Heo Seung, a deliveryman."

Although Heo Seung was older, he used polite speech as he was lower in rank than a restaurant manager. Still, he wasn't too deferential.

 

 

There was a reason for this, primarily stemming from his pride in belonging to the Central food warehouse. The Central food warehouse oversaw all food for the Association. Those who worked there had immense pride.

In short, if you got on their bad side, fresh ingredients would be a thing of the past. What they hated most was being treated like mere laborers who just carted ingredients around.

This was a kind of power play.

If there was one thing he had learned from his long organizational life, it was that if you bowed down from the start when meeting people for work, you'd have to keep bowing until the end. His philosophy was that winning the initial power struggle made things easier all the way through.

Whether he knew Heo Seung's thoughts or not, Crimson Tiger treated him with a good-natured smile.

"The dawn air is chilly. Why don't you come inside for a moment and warm yourself?"

"I think I will."

Having some time to spare, Heo Seung followed Crimson Tiger inside.

Crimson Tiger personally served him tea.

Heo Seung asked, "I heard you were new. Where were you before this?"

"I was stationed out in the remote territories."

"Then this calls for congratulations. My felicitations."

Everyone tried to get into the Association's main headquarters. Among them, many dreamed of entering the Inner Court, so it was indeed something worth congratulating.

"Thank you."

Heo Seung realized Crimson Tiger wasn't a particularly difficult person.

That wasn't a bad thing. No matter how much pride he had in his Central food warehouse affiliation, if the other party was difficult and started nitpicking about ingredients, it would be tiring for both sides. Good relations harmed no one.

Offering tea, Crimson Tiger remarked casually, "I'll be counting on you in the future."

"Of course."

With the other party being so deferential, Heo Seung felt pleased.

"But why are you staring at me so intently?"

"The bridge of your nose is gently sloped like a mountain ridge, your earlobes are thick, and your eyes are clear and unblemished. You have a very fortunate physiognomy."

"Aha! You can read faces too?"

"It's just a minor skill I picked up in my youth."

Heo Seung beamed, his mood improving even further.

All the while, Crimson Tiger continued to gaze intently at his face.

After chatting for a short while, Heo Seung rose from his seat.

"Regrettably, I must be going now."

"Of course."

Once Heo Seung left, Hong Maerang, who had been pretending not to watch from a distance, approached.

"Why are you being so kind to that fellow?"

"Why? Should I not be kind?"

"That's not what I mean, but."

"The reputation of this establishment depends on the ingredients that man brings. Naturally, I should be kind."

"If you're too nice, he'll try to walk all over you."

"Let him. What more can he do than dance on my back?"

Hong Maerang shook her head with an expression that said he was hopeless. Gong Suk, who had been peeking out from the kitchen, chuckled softly and disappeared behind the curtain.

For several days, Crimson Tiger greeted Heo Seung in the early dawn.

Heo Seung's liking for Crimson Tiger, who treated him so amicably, grew. It's human nature to feel affection for someone who says even one kind word.

Compared to the previous manager, who acted high and mighty and boasted every time he opened his mouth, Crimson Tiger was very humble and good-natured.

"Manager, you're much more diligent than you look."

When he had come out and greeted him on the first day, Heo Seung thought it was just a one-time courtesy. Crimson Tiger, however, consistently met him at dawn. It was truly no easy task.

"And how do I look?"

"I mean you don't look like someone who'd be up and about in the chilly dawn."

"Is that a compliment?"

"Of course, it's a compliment. Waking up before the crack of dawn, rubbing your eyes, how could that not be a hard life?"

"Haha. It's all how you look at it. Breathing this fresh dawn air while everyone else is asleep isn't such a bad life, is it?"

"A good interpretation. Hahaha."

The two had grown considerably closer in just a few days.

Looking at Crimson Tiger who was gazing at him, Heo Seung smiled faintly. Crimson Tiger was still staring intently at him. He figured it was a habit stemming from his proficiency in physiognomy. Anyway, since it wasn't a bad reading, he didn't much care if he was being glared at or scrutinized.

Today, he even came out and helped unload the goods himself.

"How many places like this do you deliver to?"

"Five in total."

"That must be tough."

"Well, I'm used to it by now."

Beside the cart, Crimson Tiger spotted something and sniffed it.

"Isn't this by any chance Tieguanyin?"

"It is. How did you know?"

"I used to be in the tea trade in my youth."

"Is that so?"

"Is this tea for the Inner Court teahouse?"

"Correct. Though it operates on a small scale, the tea provided is of the highest quality."

Crimson Tiger lifted the tea again and inhaled its fragrance.

"Truly, the aroma alone feels like it could melt one's entire body."

"I'll treat you to some next time."

Heo Seung pulled his cart and departed.

Crimson Tiger's eyes sparkled as he watched the departing figure.

Not long after Heo Seung had finished all his ingredient deliveries and left, he reappeared at the Second Gate, looking flustered.

"What's the matter?" a martial artist asked.

Heo Seung held up some tea.

"An item was delivered incorrectly. If the Elders of the Inner Court end up drinking inferior tea, heads will roll."

Though they saw him daily, they couldn't let him pass without due process.

One martial artist ran towards the Inner Court to confirm.

A short while later, the martial artist reappeared.

"It's true. I checked with the teahouse, and they said the wrong tea was delivered."

"Give it here. We'll deliver it."

"I must go myself. You know how fussy those women working at the teahouse are. If I just send the tea, they'll torment me endlessly."

The martial artist chuckled at his words.

"Go on then."

Seeing his face daily, they didn't act unreasonably harsh any longer.

Heo Seung passed through the Second Gate and entered.

However, the place Heo Seung headed to was not the teahouse. He was not Heo Seung, but Crimson Tiger. Crimson Tiger had earlier swapped the Tieguanyin on Heo Seung's cart with tea he had prepared in advance.

Thus, Crimson Tiger infiltrated the Inner Court.



"Please, just help me this once."

Taoist Scarlet gave no reply to Jin Chung's words.

Taoist Scarlet had never dreamed Jin Chung would come to him. Especially not to ask him to kill someone.

"Do you even know what you're saying right now?"

"Of course."

"You'd better. This matter could bring about your utter downfall."

"I am prepared for that."

"Of course, Young Lord Baek doesn't know about this, does he?"

"If he did, he would have asked you himself."

"You'll face a severe reprimand later."

"A reprimand? You're mistaken. My head will roll. And you too, Senior, will face great trouble."

Taoist Scarlet scoffed. Jin Chung was acting like a man driven to a dead end.

"Interesting, very interesting. You'd make such a request so boldly?"

"I would."

"So what do I gain from this?"

"You will have my considerable gratitude."

"Is that all?"

"There might be one more thing in it for you."

"I did."

"He probably won't be able to oust all four of you."

"...!"

Taoist Scarlet nodded. It certainly sounded plausible.

"And?"

"You will be one of the two who remain by the Young Lord's side. Perhaps the Two Immortals will be the ones ousted."

"Do you have the power to make that happen?"

Though he asked mockingly, Taoist Scarlet believed it was quite possible.

Jin Chung was the subordinate who wielded the most influence over the First Young Lord. He could surely arrange for him to stay.

Besides, if he could get rid of those detestable Seon fellows, it would be an exceptionally joyful outcome.

Incurring this level of debt to Jin Chung was certainly not a bad deal. If the First Young Lord became the successor to the Association, Jin Chung would undoubtedly occupy a core position of power. It was an investment well worth making for the future.

"What is your reason for going to such lengths?"

"I cannot tell you the reason. However, it is certain that man is a danger to the Young Lord."

This was why he had chosen Taoist Scarlet. The other three masters among the four would never agree to his request without knowing the reason.

Taoist Scarlet, however, was different from them. If he thought there was a clear benefit, he would agree. He was a man capable of murder without needing a reason. That was Jin Chung's assessment of Taoist Scarlet.

"Why me, of all people?"

"This task can only be done by you, Senior."

"That's not enough. Convince me further."

"He is the most skilled among the Twelve Phantoms."

"I've heard bits about the Twelve Phantoms. But aren't they just mere swords-for-hire?"

Jin Chung said softly, "That mere sword-for-hire is the one who returned after taking the Ironblood Commander's head this time."

"What? The Ironblood Commander's head!"

Taoist Scarlet was astonished. The operation to assassinate the Ironblood Commander was sealed as confidential, so ordinary martial artists didn't know about it.

However, masters of Taoist Scarlet's standing would inevitably find out eventually.

It was similar to how the top masters within the Evil Coalition would eventually learn of the Ironblood Commander's death, no matter how much the Evil Coalition tried to hide it.

"Is that true?"

"Two years ago, he was also responsible for eliminating the Heaven-Destroying Corps Commander."

"Khahahahahahat!"

Taoist Scarlet laughed heartily. Now he clearly understood why Jin Chung was trying to involve him.

Even among Jin Chung's own close associates, there were many quite skilled individuals. Enough that he wouldn't necessarily need to involve him. That was why he had been inwardly suspicious, wondering if there was some ulterior motive.

Hearing about the opponent's capabilities, however, everything made sense. Against such an opponent, choosing him was the right judgment.

"Quite a fellow."

"You must be careful."

Taoist Scarlet's eyes narrowed. It was a look that rebuked Jin Chung for daring to speak to him in such a manner.

If Taoist Scarlet had been an ordinary master, Jin Chung would never have provoked him. Taoist Scarlet, however, was a true master. A master who had already reached a high realm would not let pointless pride or ego ruin a task. The provocation would make him more cautious.

"Alright, let's assume that's true. How do we eliminate him?"

"I will soon assign him an elimination mission. You, Senior, will disguise yourself as the assassination target, wait for him, and then finish him."

Taoist Scarlet nodded silently.

"And you must keep this matter an absolute secret."

Just then, Taoist Scarlet raised a hand.

"Shh!"

Silence fell around them.

Taoist Scarlet's hardened gaze narrowed, then, SHWIK!

His sword shot out like an arrow and embedded itself in the ceiling.

PWAK!

No blood dripped from the hilt. If it had been aimed at someone, it meant he had missed.

The next moment.

As Taoist Scarlet's finger twitched.

SHWAAAAAAK!

The sword moved on its own, cleaving the ceiling as if parting the sea.

WHUMP WHUMP WHUMP.

Dust trickled down from the long, straight gash in the ceiling.

Still, no blood was visible.

Jin Chung tensed at Taoist Scarlet's actions. If he hadn't sensed a presence, it meant the opponent was a master comparable in skill to Taoist Scarlet.

SREUREUK.

The sword flew through the air and into Taoist Scarlet's hand.

"Not a chance!"

Taoist Scarlet kicked open the door and rushed outside.

Jin Chung followed him, his face tense. Considering the importance of their conversation, they absolutely had to catch the eavesdropper. Absolutely.



Ju Hwayin and Sin Yeongyeong's meeting was a coincidence. They ran into each other on the path leading into the Inner Court. It was Sin Yeongyeong who greeted first.

"A pleasure to see you, Third Young Lady."

Her demeanor was polite.

Ju Hwayin smiled and returned the greeting.

"It's been a while."

Their relationship was fundamentally bad. From Sin Yeongyeong's perspective, Ju Hwayin was the arch-rival of the man who would be her husband.

From Ju Hwayin's perspective as well, Sin Yeongyeong was hardly a welcome sight. Facing each other, however, the two women hid their true feelings and offered smiles.

"You've become even more beautiful."

At Sin Yeongyeong's compliment, Ju Hwayin smiled and suggested, "It's been a while, shall we have some tea?"

"Certainly."

The two headed towards the teahouse.

The place, created for Inner Court martial artists to briefly drink tea and rest, was very beautifully decorated. All those working at the teahouse were women.

"Please, have a seat."

The two sat facing each other. A woman immediately brought them tea.

"You must be busy with wedding preparations. How much longer is it now?"

"About two months left."

"It will be here soon. Congratulations."

"Thank you."

Ju Hwayin gazed steadily at Sin Yeongyeong. Sin Yeongyeong couldn't meet her gaze and looked away.

Strictly speaking, the two were not in a superior-subordinate relationship. The position of the Lord of Sword Heaven's eldest daughter was no less significant than that of one of the Association Chairman's successors.

Sin Yeongyeong, however, behaved cautiously and deferentially, as if she were a subordinate.

"Do you see Senior Brother often?"

"Occasionally."

"Does Senior Brother treat you well?"

"Yes."

"Senior Brother is a good man."

Sin Yeongyeong smiled brightly at Ju Hwayin's words.

"When I heard Senior Brother was marrying Miss Sin, I was actually a bit surprised."

"Why so?"

"How should I put it? I always thought Senior Brother was far removed from the idea of a political marriage."

At the term 'political', Sin Yeongyeong's brow furrowed slightly. She hadn't expected Ju Hwayin to suddenly use the word so bluntly after maintaining a pleasant atmosphere.

*Then again, it's a fight where one of us must die to end it. There's no real need to hide hostility.*

Sin Yeongyeong concealed her inner thoughts and smiled brightly.

"I don't think political marriages are necessarily bad. I've seen many people become unhappy after marrying without regard for status or family background."

Ju Hwayin nodded silently.

Sin Yeongyeong added, "I respect the First Young Lord. I believe that's just as important as love."

Though she said confidently, Sin Yeongyeong felt uneasy.

She felt like she was constantly making excuses to Ju Hwayin. Eventually, a sharp remark slipped out.

"Are you so confident, Third Young Lady, that you won't engage in a political marriage yourself?"

Having said it, Sin Yeongyeong regretted her words. She usually tried hard not to reveal her inner thoughts, but her composure had shattered. It was because her opponent was Ju Hwayin.

She was about to try and smooth things over with kind words, but Ju Hwayin said first.

"I'm not confident. That's why I don't intend to."

Sin Yeongyeong's expression froze. It was a complete defeat.

Forcing a smile, Sin Yeongyeong rose from her seat.

"I think I should be going now. I'll see you next time."

"Alright."

Ju Hwayin saw her off with a smile.

Once Sin Yeongyeong disappeared, Lee Dansim materialized.

"About Miss Sin, they say she looks like a bear but is a fox on the inside."

Ju Hwayin slowly shook her head.

"No."

"Pardon?"

"She's not a fox."

"What do you mean?"

Ju Hwayin's eyes narrowed.

"She's a viper, that one."

Lee Dansim was startled. She knew better than anyone how profound her mistress's insight into people was.

"When we were young, I was invited to her birthday. Being the eldest daughter of Sword Heaven, it was quite a grand banquet. I don't know if she remembers, but not only I, but all of our Senior Brother's disciples attended back then."

At that time, they were all young. It was a childhood where they couldn't have imagined they would one day engage in a succession battle where they would kill each other.

"Among the children who attended that day, there was one girl who captured everyone's attention. Even I, as a girl, was impressed."

"She was prettier than you, Young Lady? Surely not?"

"Back then, I used to run around like a tomboy. Anyway, that girl was very pretty that day. All the boys who attended were focused on her. The actual birthday girl, Sin Yeongyeong, was left out in the cold."

"So what happened? Did she harm her in some way?"

"No, nothing happened. The party ended without incident."

"Huh? Then why?"

"The incident occurred a year later. I heard news that the girl had met with misfortune."

"No way? Did she die?"

"Death would have been preferable. Her face was slashed dozens of times. Her bodyguards were all killed too."

"Was it Miss Sin's doing?"

Ju Hwayin nodded.

"She even showed the meticulousness to eliminate the subordinate who carried out the deed."

Lee Dansim shook her head, her face shocked. If it hadn't been Ju Hwayin's words, it would have been hard to believe. It was so different from the known image of Sin Yeongyeong. It must be the truth, however. Given Ju Hwayin's nature, she wouldn't have investigated such a matter carelessly.

Ju Hwayin's eyes narrowed.

"The frightening part is that it happened a full year after the party. No one suspected her. No, no one probably even connected her to it. Everyone had forgotten. She harbored that venom for a whole year. That's what's terrifying."

"At that time, Miss Sin's age... wasn't she very young?"

Ju Hwayin offered a faint smile.

"Age isn't what turns a person into a demon."

"This is rather creepy, isn't it?"

"Want to hear something scarier?"

"Gasp!"

Though Lee Dansim reacted with feigned shock, her eyes were full of curiosity.

"Senior Brother probably knows this. He also attended that day."

"Are you saying he intends to marry her even knowing her true nature?"

"I believe so."

"If that's true... it's truly terrifying."

"Don't forget who we're dealing with."

Just as the two women were about to leave the teahouse.

SHWING!

Lee Dansim drew her sword.

"Step back!"

She moved to block Ju Hwayin.

SHIIIIK!

The next moment, someone flew rapidly towards them.

It was Taoist Scarlet and Jin Chung.

"Stop!"

At Lee Dansim's shout, the two men landed a few paces away.

Coming face to face with Ju Hwayin, both men were also flustered. They had tracked the hidden presence here. They hadn't expected to meet her in this place.

Jin Chung's expression darkened.

*Damn it! Could it be her spy?*

After gesturing for Lee Dansim to step down, Ju Hwayin stepped forward.

"Senior Jeok, I presume. It's been a long time."

"Haha, it has indeed."

This time, Ju Hwayin looked at Jin Chung.

"Martial Artist Jin, have you been well?"

"Greetings, Third Young Lady."

Jin Chung offered a clasped hands greeting respectfully.

"We were in a hurry and acted rudely. Please forgive us."

Ju Hwayin replied to Jin Chung's words with a smile.

"There's nothing to forgive. However, what on earth could cause two such distinguished individuals to rush so in this supposedly impregnable place? Did you perhaps ferret out a spy from the Evil Coalition?"

Jin Chung couldn't offer any response.

Ju Hwayin smiled brightly and said, "I've been saying such silly things often lately. Please attribute it to the unsettling spring air and forgive me."

Taoist Scarlet offered a clasped hands greeting and stated, "Haha, there's nothing to forgive or not forgive. Well then. We'll see you next time."

The two men departed from the spot.

Once they were some distance away, Jin Chung quickly asked, "Was it her who was eavesdropping?"

Of course, it couldn't possibly have been her. She wouldn't personally infiltrate their territory. He asked because their meeting was so coincidental, and the opponent's martial arts were skilled enough to evade Taoist Scarlet's attack.

"No. The aura was definitely male."

"It was a mistake. We should have been more careful."

Taoist Scarlet said nothing. It was a private conversation in his own quarters. A conversation where he had been on high alert. How much more careful could he have been?

"I must have been mistaken."

Taoist Scarlet was half-convinced. When he gave chase, he definitely felt a tangible presence. However, since reaching his current realm, he sometimes encountered non-existent things.

Dueling with imaginary enemies was a prime example. Perhaps this too was some kind of delusion, he wondered. It was hard to believe he had lost the intruder so quickly. It didn't seem likely that a master of that caliber would be merely scuttling across ceilings.

Jin Chung's expression, however, remained dark.

If so, that was fortunate. But what if not?

Taoist Scarlet asked softly, "What do you intend to do now?"

After pondering for a moment, Jin Chung's head snapped up.

"There's one thing I need to confirm. Let's go together for now. I'll explain afterwards."

As soon as his words ended, Jin Chung leaped off towards somewhere. Taoist Scarlet followed him.

Meanwhile, after the two men left, Lee Dansim turned to Ju Hwayin and remarked, "I don't know what kind of scheme this is."

Ju Hwayin's expression had turned serious.

"Taoist Scarlet is the most savage blade Senior Brother possesses. Him consorting with Jin Chung?"

Ju Hwayin asked herself and answered her own question.

"They're plotting something."

Ju Hwayin, who had been about to walk out, turned back again.

She entered the teahouse building.

"Excuse me."

A woman bowed respectfully in greeting.

"Yes, Young Mistress."

"Was anyone here just now?"

"Yes. A man named Heo Seung, who delivers goods daily, was here."

"For what reason?"

"He delivered the wrong tea and came to exchange it."

"Has that happened before?"

"No. But why do you ask?"

"It's nothing."

Ju Hwayin turned to leave, then said again.

"That tea, could I have some?"

"Of course."

The woman handed a tea canister to Ju Hwayin.

"Thank you."

As she came out with the tea, Lee Dansim asked, "Why did you ask such questions?"

Ju Hwayin smiled enigmatically.

"It's frustrating, Young Lady."

Ju Hwayin grinned and said, "Let's go have another cup of tea. I think I can enjoy it this time."

At the same time, Jin Chung arrived at the Hundred Flavors Restaurant.

Reaching a building opposite the Hundred Flavors Restaurant, he observed the inside.

Inside the building, Crimson Tiger, disguised as the Hundred Flavors Restaurant manager, was talking with Hong Maerang.

Jin Chung let out a soft sigh.

Taoist Scarlet, who had followed him, asked, "What on earth is going on?"

"For a moment, I thought the one hiding might have been him. But it seems not."

"Who is that man?"

"That man is Crimson Tiger."

"That's him?"

"Yes, he's undercover as the Manager there."

Startled, Taoist Scarlet's gaze fixed on Crimson Tiger. "He looks quite ordinary."

"That's what makes him frightening."

Taoist Scarlet's eyes meticulously scanned Crimson Tiger. He saw many things Jin Chung could not.

As Jin Chung said, he was a man who knew how to perfectly conceal his aura. That was as far as it went, though. Masters of Taoist Scarlet's caliber got a feel for an opponent just by looking at them, at least enough to gauge if someone was powerful enough to pose a threat.

And he sensed it now. Crimson Tiger was a huge threat.

"So? What do you plan to do now?"

"Please handle it according to the plan. We must hurry, however."

"Understood."

Taoist Scarlet replied confidently.

At this moment, however, Taoist Scarlet was unaware of one fact.

Crimson Tiger was currently conscious of his gaze and was deliberately adjusting his aura to make Taoist Scarlet feel that way.

It wasn't Taoist Scarlet's delusion. The one who eavesdropped on their conversation was indeed Crimson Tiger. He had concealed his presence as much as possible, but the moment they decided to kill him, for a very brief instant, he couldn't control himself.

While the Third Young Lady and the others had their encounter, Crimson Tiger had given the original tea he had prepared to the woman working at the teahouse and quickly returned.

He had hurried back to the Hundred Flavors Restaurant, predicting that Jin Chung might come looking for him.

And indeed, Jin Chung, suspecting him, had come all this way.

Feeling the two men's presences recede, Crimson Tiger let out an inward sigh of relief.

It had been dangerous, but it had been worth it.
