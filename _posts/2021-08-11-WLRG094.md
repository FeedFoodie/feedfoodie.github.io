As Lee Sungmin climbed the mountain gate, he greeted the monk before him.
"It's been a while."

Seunjeo's mouth, which had been hanging open, snapped shut with a hard swallow. He stared for a long moment, his eyes wide with disbelief, before managing a hesitant nod.

"Yes… it, uh, it's been a while."

A full year had passed since Lee Sungmin had left for Dreamoor on an errand for Great Monk Bulyeong. The tale of what had transpired on Mount Hua had already spread throughout Eria, and the news had long since reached Shaolin.

"I… I heard about what happened a year ago. About the fighting spirit. You did something incredible."

"It just happened to turn out that way," Lee Sungmin replied, offering no further details about the incident.

Seunjeo found this regrettable but knew better than to press the matter.

"Is Jihak inside?"

"Ah… yes. He's probably near Great Monk Bulyeong's residence."

Lee Sungmin grinned and nodded at Seunjeo's answer. Seunjeo brought his palms together in prayer. When he blinked, the space where Lee Sungmin had stood just a moment before was empty.

"……"

Though only a year had passed since his last visit to Shaolin, it felt to Lee Sungmin like he was returning after a very long time. His memories had been overwritten by Denir's backup, but the years he had spent in the Mental World were clearly etched into his spirit, if not his body.

"…Hmm."

He was nearing the cave where Great Monk Bulyeong resided when a sound reached him, though in truth, he had sensed the presence long before he heard a thing.

"What happened to you?"

Jihak stepped into view. The Jihak that Lee Sungmin remembered was not an expressive man, yet the one before him now could not hide the shock on his face.

"Many things have happened." It was good to see Jihak's face again. Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod as he answered.

Many things. Jihak muttered the words to himself and shook his head.

"I heard the story. That you defeated the Sword Phantom."

"It was a fluke."

"A fluke? You call defeating the Sword Phantom, the man who murdered the Mount Hua Sect Leader, a fluke?"

"What else could I call it? I was weaker than him at the time."

"…Was he careless?"

"Perhaps."

Jihak let out a sigh. "…When was it?"

Lee Sungmin tilted his head, not understanding the question. "What?"

"When did you reach the Supreme Peak?"

"Ah…" Lee Sungmin finally understood and gave a bitter smile, scratching his head awkwardly. "It was half a year ago."

In the Mental World, it had taken him a thousand years to overcome that wall. In reality, guided by those memories, he had broken through in less than six months. His growth had been greatly suppressed in Denir's Trial, but treading a path one had already walked was far easier than forging it for the first time.

"Half a year… half a year?"

"Is something wrong?"

"No… it's nothing. It's just… when I look in your eyes, half a year doesn't seem right."

"It's true that I broke through the wall of the Supreme Peak half a year ago."

"I know. It's just… your eyes look like they've seen much more training than that."

Lee Sungmin offered no answer, letting a simple smile suffice. Instead, his hand went to the spear slung across his back.

"If you don't mind, may I request a duel?"

"I'd be honored, but… I'm a little worried. I don't know if what I'm seeing is right, but if we fight now, I'll have to use my full strength."

"Really?"

"At least… that's how I feel," Jihak said, bringing both hands together before his chest.

Though Jihak had mastered the Seventy-Two Ultimate Arts, Lee Sungmin knew well that his greatest confidence lay in unarmed combat, a mastery of fist, palm, and foot.

"Stop."

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to draw his spear, an elderly voice sliced through the tension between them. Jihak flinched and turned toward the sound.

Grandmaster Bulyeong stood at the entrance of the cave, his expression a strange mix of a smile and a frown as he looked at Lee Sungmin. Their eyes met, and Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply.

"Why are you stopping us?" Jihak asked.

"If you two fight now, this won't end as a simple duel. Not unless one of you is willing to concede."

"…Ugh." Jihak let out a low groan. The Grandmaster's words implied that Lee Sungmin's martial prowess was now equal to his own.

"And if neither of you is willing to concede," Grandmaster Bulyeong added, "then you, Jihak, will either die or be crippled."

Jihak's mouth fell half-open at the declaration. He glanced back and forth between Lee Sungmin and the Grandmaster, his face a mask of disbelief. Lee Sungmin simply wore a bitter smile.

"Aren't you overestimating me?"

"Kekeke! Boy, you've gotten better at lying this past year. What exactly did you come here to confirm?"

"…I wanted to see how far I've come."

"How far? Compared to whom?"

"Wiji Hoyeon."

At the name, Jihak's shoulders flinched. Wiji Hoyeon. She was the source of his first and only defeat, the woman who had shown him just how vast the world truly was.

"……"

Grandmaster Bulyeong stroked his chin.

"Boy, I don't know the Wiji Hoyeon of today. I only know the one who came to this mountain wanting to see Shaolin. That calamity with a warlord's fate wouldn't have been sitting idle since then. There must be a huge gap between the Wiji Hoyeon of today and the one who defeated Jihak."

"How do I compare?"

"You're not even as good as she was when she first came to this mountain," Grandmaster Bulyeong answered without a shred of hesitation. "I don't know what kind of training you've done this past year. A year ago, when I heard the rumor that you killed that bastard, the Sword Phantom, who murdered old man Seongha… I didn't believe it. The you I knew then wouldn't have lasted ten exchanges against Daoist Seongha."

Lee Sungmin accepted the assessment calmly. It was the truth. Back then, he had barely been peeking through the gates of the Supreme Peak, while Daoist Seongha had been a true master of that realm.

"For someone like you to have killed the man who killed Daoist Seongha… how could I believe it? I thought the rumors must have been distorted, but the you of now… keke! What in the world have you been doing for the past year?"

"A lot has happened."

He had spent the last year at Denir's house, honing his martial theory based on his experiences in the Mental World. That foundation had allowed him to overcome the wall of the Supreme Peak in six months, and with the opening of his Conception and Governing Vessels, he had achieved Body Reformation.

In the half year that followed, he had become a Supreme Peak master, but he was not satisfied. He swung his spear countless times, striving to reach the ideal spear path he had witnessed and accomplished with his Astral Body.

It did not go well. His mind could see far ahead, but his body could not yet follow. In the end, he had failed to completely unravel the tangle of his Mind, Qi, and Body.

"Did you come here to confirm how much closer you've gotten to Wiji Hoyeon?"

"I needed some kind of assurance."

"You must not have gotten it. Because you're weaker than she is."

"I thought that would probably be the case," Lee Sungmin said with a small laugh. He had never expected to have surpassed her at his current level. He still had not fully reached the realm he had seen and attained before.

*It wasn't as if he could see everything.*

No matter how great Grandmaster Bulyeong's insight was, he could not know everything about Lee Sungmin unless he demonstrated his martial arts personally.

Despite Jihak and Grandmaster Bulyeong's invitations to stay the day, Lee Sungmin descended Mount Song. He could not delay any longer. He had only stopped by because it was on the way to his destination.

*In two months, the Dungeon will open.*

He refused to entertain the question, *Can I do it?* He had already asked it of himself hundreds, thousands of times, and knew all too well where that path led. It was a road to self-loathing and inferiority that ended only in despair and gritted teeth.

*So he doesn't.*

Instead of asking *Can I do it?*, he thought, *I have to do it. I can do it.*

Lee Sungmin pulled the straw hat from his back and settled it over his head. There was much to be done. First, and most urgently, he had to prevent Baek Sogo's death.

Then, as the Spirit within the Grandmaster had advised, he needed to travel north and find an eccentric. He did not know who this person was, but he knew he had to go.

After that.

Before his promised day with Wiji Hoyeon, there was one more thing he had to do.

He had to find Preskan.

The Black Heart was currently inside his chest. Five years had passed since he acquired it, yet Lee Sungmin still had no proper grasp of what function this heart served. Only Preskan could give him answers.

*…This was the problem.*

Lee Sungmin glanced down at his own chest, fighting the sudden urge to tear it open and confirm the color of the heart beating inside. He had fought the impulse several times, and each time he did, he was forced to admit that the long years in the Mental World had planted a definite seed of madness within him.

The need to know for sure had started a few months ago.

A few things he had experienced since obtaining the heart bothered him. There was the time Aine had attacked him. He had been in a near-death situation when he suddenly understood how to use enhanced qi. If he had not manifested it at that exact moment, Aine would have killed him.

He had a similar experience when he fought the Sword Phantom. The instant the phantom's sword was about to cut through his body, he had unconsciously thrust his spear and pierced the man's chest. At the time, he had believed his preceding insights had forced his body to move, but as he organized his training and experiences, he began to think that was probably not the case.

He still could not reproduce the spear path he had used to kill the Sword Phantom. Unlike the enhanced sword qi, which he could wield afterward, albeit inefficiently, that specific spear technique remained beyond his grasp, no matter how hard he tried.

Eventually, his thoughts always returned to the Black Heart. Ever since obtaining it, he had somehow managed to survive every life-threatening situation.

*Perhaps it was because of the Black Heart.*

That was not a pleasant thought. He remembered the tears he had shed upon awakening his enhanced sword qi, the thrill he had felt. He had been genuinely moved to have reached a realm in this life that he could never touch in his last.

However, if it was all thanks to the Black Heart…

"…I can't just keep this unknown thing in my chest forever," Lee Sungmin muttered to himself, pulling his straw hat down low.
