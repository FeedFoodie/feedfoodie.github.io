*This could be dangerous.*

The Void Monarch's thought was a low mutter in his mind. Before them, Jeniela bared her fangs, her entire being radiating an intense appetite for Lee Sungmin.

A thirst and hunger she had not experienced in a very long time surged through her. She had lived for hundreds of years, yet it had been an age since she had felt such a blatant, primal desire. It was not as if she ever lacked for meals. As a personal hobby, she enjoyed watching desperate people fall into corruption, savoring both the stimulation and the sustenance that came with the process. Beyond that, she always consumed enough to sustain her existence. Just today, she had sated herself on the blood of a young maiden.

And yet, she was ravenous. Her senses, sharpened and true to her instincts, connected everything about Lee Sungmin to her appetite. It began with his scent. How hot and sweet would the blood be, flowing just beneath that skin? Was it the blood of a human, a Yokai, or a Dragon that coursed through the Yokai's body?

Overwhelmed by such curiosity, Jeniela stuck out her tongue and licked her lips.

"…Hmm."

An unnatural aura radiated from her, and Lee Sungmin sensed it immediately. He had rushed to Trabia on a different matter entirely and had never expected to run into Jeniela like this.

*If you fight, you'll die.*

The Void Monarch's warning was stark. No matter how much stronger Lee Sungmin had become, he was no match for the Vampire Queen who had lived for centuries. He might possess strength that ranked him among the top humans, but he had not even reached the pinnacle of humanity. How could he dare challenge the Queen, the very pinnacle of all Vampires?

Just as Lee Sungmin tensed, his hand moving to grip his spear, Jeniela took a deep breath. She took a few steps back and bowed her head deeply.

"My apologies," she said. "I've shown you an unsightly side of myself. Haa… It's been a very long time since I've felt this way. This intense appetite and thirst, I mean."

Jeniela laughed, covering her mouth with her hand.

"It's been so long that it feels unfamiliar and frightening, yet enjoyable at the same time, but please, don't worry. I don't want to take you by force. To be honest, it wouldn't be difficult if I chose to, but I won't. That would be too barbaric, wouldn't it?"

She let out another laugh.

"Besides, this isn't a very good place for it. Look… this messy, trash-filled alley. Even in this filthy and nauseating city, this place is particularly disgusting. I don't want to partake in what will surely be the finest delicacy of my life in a place like this. Ah, but don't worry too much about that. Even if you leave this place, I won't devour you."

"…What do you mean?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"I want to enjoy every part of you," she explained, answering his unasked questions with a smile. "On the bed where I sleep, with my favorite sheets, when I'm at my most excited and hungry… Ah. For a vampire, appetite and lust are almost the same concept."

The beautiful smile she wore was fatally enchanting, a weapon that made all those she targeted as prey loyal to her. That enchantment, however, failed to sway Lee Sungmin's consciousness this time as well.

"On that bed… when I desire it most, when I can enjoy it the most. Wanting to eat you then is my personal wish. Ah, ahh… my expression might have sounded a bit too intense. I'm not thinking of completely devouring you. I do want to drink all of your blood and chew and swallow your flesh. Your thinking brain, your blood-pumping heart, your breathing lungs, the liver, the pancreas, the bones, the blood vessels, the muscles, the flesh, and the genitals."

Jeniela's words quickened. Her crimson eyes shone with a deeper light as her breathing hitched and her shoulders trembled slightly. Only after pouring out the names of every internal organ that makes up a human did she offer a refreshing smile.

"I want to chew, swallow, and drink all of it, but more than that, more than that base and primal desire, my reason is stronger. My wish to make you my subordinate is more steadfast, so please be aware of that."

"…I have no desire to become a vampire, though."

"Don't worry. I won't force you. I told you before, didn't I? I… huhu! I don't just unconditionally increase my subordinates. They must be very persistent… passionate, in Despair, and yet, they don't give up. You're still lacking. That's why I'm waiting."

Jeniela licked her lips as she spoke. It was a personal aesthetic she had maintained for a long time. If Lee Sungmin had been a being who met her criteria, she would have already done her utmost to seduce him.

*Not yet.*

There was not enough Despair. Jeniela suppressed her disappointment and clicked her tongue.

"What brings you here? If you came riding a Fairy Horse, it must be quite urgent."

"…I don't have business in Trabia."

"Ah… in that case." Jeniela's smile turned bright. "Is it Kim Jonghyun?"

At those words, a flicker of tension crossed Lee Sungmin's features, his posture stiffening. He questioned the cheerfully smiling vampire.

"Do you know him?"

"Of course I do. He's quite an interesting human, isn't he? Ah, when I say human, I'm simply referring to his species. I don't actually think of him as a human." Jeniela hummed to herself. To her, Kim Jonghyun was an extremely interesting subject. He had stolen everything from the Arch Lich, Arbes, and had even become the master of the magic tome Grimoire, which Arbes had treasured. "It doesn't seem like you came for Kim Jonghyun's sake. Are you also here to subjugate him?"

"…For now, yes."

"Huuuhm…" Jeniela tapped her lower lip at his answer. After a brief silence, she shook her head. "I'm saying this for your own good. If you're thinking of subjugating Kim Jonghyun, you'd be better off giving up."

"Why is that?"

"The ritual he's preparing is extremely dangerous. I acknowledge that you're quite strong, but if you interfere with Kim Jonghyun's ritual… huhu!" Jeniela laughed, her shoulders shaking. "You'll die too, you know?"

*Wait.*

A surge of Demonic Energy stirred as the Void Monarch rose from behind Lee Sungmin's back to look down at Jeniela.

"Void Monarch," Jeniela said, looking up to call his name. "Were you still attached to him? I thought you would have grown disillusioned with your ridiculous predicament and chosen to perish by now. I suppose even that great pride of yours has become trivial after losing your body and spending hundreds of years like this, hasn't it?"

*You speak nonsense.*

"I'm saying this out of pity for you. After all, I once respected you quite a bit. Not anymore, though," Jeniela chuckled.

*Why are you doing nothing?* the Void Monarch asked, ignoring her sarcastic remarks. *That Black Mage named Kim Jonghyun. It seems he's performing some kind of ritual in your territory, so why are you leaving him be?*

"Is there any need to interfere?" Jeniela replied with a gentle smile. "It's more fun to watch if I let him proceed without interruption, and Kim Jonghyun diligently asked for my permission beforehand. So I decided to ignore it. I have no intention of helping him, nor do I intend to interfere. I'm just going to watch."

"What on earth is he trying to do?" Lee Sungmin pressed.

"You seem very curious, don't you?" Jeniela let out a seductive laugh. She rolled her eyes, which seemed to be brimming with blood, and looked at him. Tapping her plump lips with her fingertips, she smiled gently. "But there's no reason for me to tell you, is there? In exchange for me telling you what you're curious about, what can you give me?"

"What do you want?"

"There's nothing I want from you." At least, not right now. Jeniela firmly suppressed the greed that was gently raising its head in her heart. It did not seem like he would agree to become her subordinate under these conditions, and she did not want to ask for his blood either. She did not want to drink Lee Sungmin's blood yet. "Still… I quite like you. So if I were to give you a hint instead of the answer," she said with a smiling face, "Kim Jonghyun is trying to become something other than human."

That was the entirety of the hint she could give. Jeniela shoved her calmed desire deep into the recesses of her reason and turned away. Just as she had appeared, she melted into the pitch-black shadows and vanished.

"…Trying to become something other than human?" Lee Sungmin muttered the words she had left behind. Did that mean he was trying to become inhuman? A Black Mage had a very easy way of doing that. He could become a Lich. However, judging by what Kim Jonghyun was doing, it did not seem like that was his goal. Something *other* than human. Lee Sungmin turned the suspicious words over in his mind as he turned to leave.

*Something feels very wrong about this,* the Void Monarch muttered. *Jeniela's words, too. Judging by what that wench said, that Black Mage bastard Kim Jonghyun… doesn't seem to be simply trying to become inhuman. Didn't she say something other than human?*

"She did."

*Jeniela said Kim Jonghyun was preparing some kind of ritual. Perhaps annihilating the seven villages was part of the preparation for that ritual. Do you remember what happened to the corpses in the villages?*

"They said the corpses were all tied together and piled up. After all their hearts had been removed."

*Where did the removed hearts go?*

At the Void Monarch's sneer, Lee Sungmin's face grew cold.

*I don't know what kind of ritual it is, but it's one that requires the hearts of thousands of people. No matter how strong you are, you'd better be careful.*

"Then that's good," Lee Sungmin said. "I was wondering if I came here for nothing… but now that it's confirmed Kim Jonghyun is a dangerous bastard, I can go without any lingering regrets."

He had wondered if he really needed to come, since so many reinforcements had gathered. That worry had now completely vanished. Kim Jonghyun was dangerous. Although he did not know what kind of ritual the bastard was preparing, if annihilating seven villages was part of the preparation, then not running away and waiting for the growing subjugation force must also be for the sake of the ritual.

*I can't let them die.*

Lee Sungmin touched the face that was not his own. The owner of this face was not a mercenary, just a simple adventurer with no means to prove his identity. That would be enough. He left the alley. The distance from Trabia to Hedor, where the subjugation force was gathered, was not that far. Getting there was not a big problem. The problem would start once he arrived.

*I have to avoid being discovered if at all possible.*

If it was revealed that he was the Phantom Spear, Scarlet's position would become awkward, and the nearby head of the Moyong Clan would have a fit. He would also be attacked by the Murim Alliance. Escaping would not be too difficult, but he did not want to make things so troublesome.

"Looks like I'll have to do some acting that's not in my destiny," Lee Sungmin grumbled as he passed through Trabia's city gates.

The number of hearts pulled from the seven villages reached five thousand.

Kim Jonghyun walked through the darkness, his black robe dragging behind him. He was in a forest of dry, thorn-like trees. In a large pit dug in the center, five thousand hearts were piled high, uncrushed.

"……"

He paced around the pit, glancing at the magic tome floating beside his head. Grimoire. The worst magic tome held by the Mages Guild. The reason Kim Jonghyun had taken everything from Arbes was not only for his terrible knowledge and great Dark Magic, but also because he coveted that Grimoire.

Over the past year, Kim Jonghyun had done many things. He had returned from Urmuri and gone back to the Black Magic Tower as if nothing had happened. While consistently submitting research reports to avoid the Guild's suspicion, he had mastered Arbes's Dark Magic. Then he had visited Arbes's secret Dungeon and made everything the lich had owned his own. He then spent over half a year deciphering the Grimoire he had obtained. Thanks to possessing Arbes's knowledge, as well as his own different knowledge, Kim Jonghyun was able to decipher several of the Grimoire's spells.

*The theory is sound… hmm. I don't know if it will work out well.*

Kim Jonghyun stroked his chin as he inspected the magic circle he had drawn. He had wanted to go through a few experimental processes, but unfortunately, experiments were impossible for this magic ritual.

"It's too late to stop now," Kim Jonghyun muttered in an indifferent voice. "After all, I've already killed over five thousand people for this ritual."
