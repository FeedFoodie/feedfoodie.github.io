The large man charged, making the fatal mistake of assuming he understood everything about Lee Sungmin simply because he could not sense any particular danger. It was an error he would not live to regret, though he did not realize it yet.

Lee Sungmin's hand went to the spear on his back. By the time he drew it and leveled its point, the large man was already upon him, a fist the size of a boulder descending toward his body.

Lee Sungmin sidestepped the crushing blow, his movement a blur that outpaced the falling fist. Holding the spear forward in one hand, he spun it outward, catching the man's descending arm in the weapon's rotation.

"......"

A terrible sound of tearing flesh and snapping bone echoed through the forest. The large man stared, uncomprehending, as his own ruggedly muscled right arm flew into the distance in a spray of blood.

"I heard your hearing wasn't affected," Lee Sungmin muttered. His expression was indifferent even after tearing off the man's entire arm. His upturned eyes showed not a hint of emotion.

"So, let's talk..."

"Kuaaaargh!"

The large man gasped, his face flushing with pain as he ground his teeth. He twisted his body violently and swung his remaining left fist. A shimmering energy enveloped the attack, proving he was a master who had surpassed the wall of the peak realm.

To Lee Sungmin, however, the man's level was nothing special. He clicked his tongue and raised his spear.

It happened in an instant. A thrust, too fast for the eye to follow, pierced the large man's left shoulder. He was sent flying backward into a tree before he could even scream. The tree he struck was no tree at all. The monster opened its maw and clamped down, attempting to chew on the man's body.

"Gyaaaah!"

The large man screamed as the creature's bite tore into his back. Lee Sungmin watched this unfold and started to walk forward, but a shout from behind him stopped him.

"P-Please! Please save the elder!" Kuromaru cried out.

At the desperate plea, Lee Sungmin turned. As he tilted his head, Kuromaru quickly added, "I-If you save the elder... I'll tell you anything I know."

After listening quietly, Lee Sungmin used the spear he was holding to scratch a question into the ground.

That's strange. Wasn't that man trying to kill you?

"Even so... to me, he is like a Master."

In truth, Lee Sungmin had no reason to listen. Even if the man they called 'elder' died, he was confident he could beat the answers he wanted out of Kuromaru. Still, he decided to grant the request. He pulled the elder's body from the tree monster's bite and, while he was at it, even stopped the bleeding. Kuromaru watched with frightened eyes as Lee Sungmin bandaged the stump of the elder's severed arm. The man had already fainted and lay motionless.

Let's have a talk now.

Lee Sungmin wrote on the ground again.

Since the potion Kuromaru had taken to resist the ghostly cry still affected his hearing, Lee Sungmin simply demanded he tell him everything, rather than going through the trouble of writing out each question. The conversation that followed was one-sided.

Kuromaru talked, and Lee Sungmin listened.

The dead man's name was Hayato. Kuromaru explained that Hayato had been like a brother to him since childhood, though that was none of Lee Sungmin's business. After all, he was not the one who had killed him. The large man was Nobuhiro, a master-like figure who had taught them both martial arts since they were young, though they were not his direct disciples.

*They're all Japanese.*

Eria summoned Otherworlders from all sorts of dimensions. Earth was not limited to Koreans, so people from every nation, from Japan and China to America and even Russia, were brought to this world. Most of the Otherworlders from 'Earth' arrived as Classless. Not all of them, of course. As was the case with Kim Jonghyun, some were summoned having already learned special abilities like magic.

In a world with no allies, the concept of 'nation' created a powerful sense of kinship. It was not uncommon for people from the same country to gather and form factions, even in a different dimension. In his past life, Lee Sungmin even remembered groups with names like the Republic of Korea Marine Corps Veterans Association and something called the 'Strongest Dragon Zodiac Koreans of the Twelve Zodiacs'.

Kuromaru explained that his people were a clan living in the Sleeping Forest, and that they lived here in pursuit of their long-cherished desire.

Long-cherished desire?

Lee Sungmin wrote the words on the ground. Kuromaru hesitated for a moment, then glanced at the fallen Nobuhiro. Seeing the fear in the young man's eyes, Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh and wrote again.

If you're afraid of that big guy, should I just kill him for you?

"N-No. That's not necessary. You don't have to do that," Kuromaru shook his head, horrified.

"Our clan's long-cherished desire is to see the end of this forest," he finally answered.

The end of the forest. Lee Sungmin furrowed his brow and tilted his head, scratching another question into the dirt.

What is at the end of this forest?

"That... I don't know either. The only one who knows about it is the head of our clan."

You're following along without even knowing?

"Well... we were born and raised in this forest. We've been told since we were children that we must achieve the clan's desire, and everyone in the village is the same."

It seemed Kuromaru truly knew nothing about the desire. Lee Sungmin considered torture but decided against it, thinking it would be pointless. If he were to extract information that way, it would be better to use the fallen Nobuhiro. He wrote one last question for Kuromaru.

Do you know Envirus?

"Who is that?" Kuromaru asked back, his eyes wide.

In the end, Lee Sungmin had gained little from the conversation. He left Kuromaru and approached Nobuhiro.

A few sharp slaps to the cheek brought Nobuhiro's eyes shooting open. He opened his mouth to shout and tried to lift his head, but Lee Sungmin pressed down on his shoulder with his spear, the Changjun. The man screamed again and slammed his head back to the ground.

"Do you know Envirus?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking down at him.

Nobuhiro glared back, his face contorted in pain from his torn arm. In that moment, a thousand thoughts raced through his mind. He, one of the most skilled warriors in his village and clan, had been toyed with like a child. He had never imagined someone this powerful existed, aside from the clan head.

"Envi... Envirus...?"

"Do you know him?"

"I don't know...!"

"What is this long-cherished desire your clan pursues? What is at the end of this forest?"

"Do you think I would tell you that...?!"

"......"

Lee Sungmin looked down at Nobuhiro with a sour expression. After watching him for a moment, he turned and walked back to Kuromaru.

Where is your village?

"Th... that's..." Kuromaru stammered.

"Kuromaru! What are you doing?!" Nobuhiro roared from the ground.

Lee Sungmin ignored him, keeping his gaze fixed on Kuromaru. Staring into those emotionless eyes, Kuromaru chewed on his lip.

"The village is..."

As Kuromaru opened his mouth, Nobuhiro shot to his feet with a roar. He threw himself forward, not out of fear of Lee Sungmin, but for the sake of his village. He charged with the intent to kill, drawing on his life qi, but, WHACK! The spear spun in Lee Sungmin's hand. The spearhead, lashing out from behind his shoulder, pierced Nobuhiro's head.

Kuromaru's jaw dropped. He watched as the village elder, the man who had taught him martial arts since childhood, died with his head burst open. Fear and anger warred within him.

It couldn't be helped.

Lee Sungmin wrote on the ground as if making an excuse. The words were an excuse, but his expression was not. A true and profound fear of Lee Sungmin finally took hold of Kuromaru.

"I... I'll guide you," he stammered.

{sep}

It felt like he was escorting the god of death.

Kuromaru felt his pace constantly slowing, and it was true. His steps forward were much slower than usual. It was because of the man walking behind him. Lee Sungmin was not threatening him, nor did he blame Kuromaru for walking slowly. Even so, the act of voluntarily leading such a powerful master to his village tormented him. The images of the dead Hayato and Nobuhiro kept replaying in his mind. Shouldn't he have died like that too?

*No. Even if I had died, that man would have found the village.*

He would have, one way or another. That was the excuse Kuromaru used to soothe his wavering heart.

Following Kuromaru, Lee Sungmin was lost in his own thoughts. The clan was not very large. Though it was a village, Kuromaru had said the population was less than a hundred. Until his generation, the village had sustained itself through inbreeding. When genetic diseases began to appear from the repeated consanguineous marriages, they started kidnapping partners from the outside to maintain the village.

*I don't see why they have to go that far.*

Lee Sungmin genuinely thought so. Maintaining a village through inbreeding and kidnapping, living in a place like the Sleeping Forest where people rarely tread, all to achieve some long-cherished desire. Was that desire truly great enough to devote generations to it?

*In the memories of my past life, I never heard about this clan. I never heard about the Sleeping Forest either.*

That was how it was when he had not come to this forest. So, what would happen now that he had? Would his presence cause a different event to unfold from his past life, just as it had so many times before?

*Not my business.*

When Daoist Seongha died, and when he killed the Sword Phantom, Lee Sungmin had thought a lot. People who should not have died had died because of his existence. Everything was getting tangled up because of him. Denir had said it too. That Lee Sungmin's existence was ruining the original flow.

He had thought a lot over 2100 years.

He had reached a conclusion.

Whatever happened to the world, it was none of Lee Sungmin's business.

The only thing important to Lee Sungmin was himself.

"We've... arrived," Kuromaru said in a small voice.

The village entrance was protected by a crude wooden fence. A vegetable patch for the villagers' survival could be seen beyond it. There was a fence, but no guards were visible in front of it. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the fence and pondered for a moment.

His pondering was too long. Or perhaps, as a result of it, he felt no need to act.

THWACK!

An arrow flew from a distance, piercing Kuromaru's neck. Kuromaru made a choking sound and coughed up blood. He staggered back a few steps and tried to say something, but died before he could get the words out. Lee Sungmin had sensed the killing intent and knew an attack was coming. However, he had not warned Kuromaru.

There was no reason for Lee Sungmin to protect him. He had only saved Kuromaru from Nobuhiro because he needed information.

"They show no mercy, even to their own clan," Lee Sungmin remarked, his face stoic as he drew his spear.

He was a little curious. What was the long-cherished desire of these villagers, who were such a nemesis to any outsider, and why had Envirus come to this forest?

*I hope I can get something in return for the trouble of coming all this way.*

Lee Sungmin thought as he raised his Protective Qi Aura.
