The crystal ball turned gray in Lee Sungmin's hand the moment he received the information. He crushed it, the fine powder scattering on the wind as he fell into thought.

Baek Sogo was alive. He had already heard as much from the old beggar of the Beggars' Gang, but now Erebrisa had provided confirmation.

*Senior Sister left the Murim Alliance?*

Her withdrawal had been sudden, by all accounts. After declaring her departure, Baek Sogo had reportedly left the city of Kron and was now heading south. Eria was a vast land, making it impossible to know her exact destination from a direction alone, but if she was traveling south from Kron...

*She's worried about me.*

The distance from Kron to the Dungeon was immense. He had used a scroll to teleport instantly, but traveling that same path by normal means would take several months. Even if Baek Sogo pushed herself to the absolute limit, forgoing food and sleep, guzzling inner qi recovery potions like water, and constantly using her movement arts, the journey would still take her over a month.

"Is there any way to send a letter to Silent Flash?"

"That isn't a difficult task," Nebul replied with a nod. Seeing how the man could appear anywhere, Lee Sungmin guessed the concept of distance held little meaning for the Brokers of Erebrisa.

"And I suppose taking me to her is impossible?"

"That is impossible."

He had only asked on the off chance, but as expected, Nebul shook his head.

"We are not bound by space, Lee Sungmin, but you are. If you were to attempt Spatial Movement as we do, the torrent between spaces would tear your body apart," Nebul advised, his expression serious. "I can procure a long-distance teleport scroll for you, but it's not a very convenient tool. They can typically only transport you to pre-designated coordinates. Besides, such scrolls are extremely expensive. The reason they're so pricey is because you're essentially buying the time it would take to travel."

"...Then what about just delivering a letter?"

"There's no problem with that. I can just do it myself," Nebul replied with a shrug.

Hearing this, Lee Sungmin found himself wondering about Erebrisa, the brokerage Guild, and the Brokers who belonged to it. These beings could perform Spatial Movement so casually. Even for him, a man who had reached the realm of the Supreme Peak, Nebul's strength was impossible to fathom.

"What exactly is Erebrisa?"

"It is a brokerage Guild," Nebul answered, his expression unchanging. It seemed he wouldn't get a proper answer no matter how many times he asked.

"Could I have some paper and a pen."

"Of course."

Nebul flicked his hand. A moment later, he reached into a crack in space and pulled out a thin sheet of paper and a pen.

"Am I just borrowing these?"

"...You can just have them."

Lee Sungmin chuckled and sat down on the floor. He touched the pen to the paper, organizing his thoughts. What could he even say? That he was fine, that she shouldn't worry?

I'm sorry to be telling you of my survival through a letter like this.

I'm fine. I'm not hurt anywhere, and I made it out safely.

I'm glad you also made it out alive, Senior Sister. Senior Sister. I don't resent you. I went to that Dungeon for you, and I was hoping you would live.

So please don't worry about me or feel guilty.

I'm fine. I'm really fine.

He stopped his pen. Should he tell Baek Sogo where he was going? Knowing her personality, she would come if he told her. Was it okay to let that happen? He was heading to the North, and there was no telling what kind of incidents and dangers awaited him there.

In the end, Lee Sungmin did not write down his destination. He wanted to see Baek Sogo. He wanted to travel with her. He still... hadn't heard the words he longed to hear from her. Words like, 'You've improved so much,' or 'You've gotten so strong.' Lee Sungmin put down the pen.

*Later.*

First, he had to go to the North.

"So. You were someone amazing."

Once Nebul had gone, Lee Sungmin set off again. He practiced using his movement arts and Haste in tandem, trying to grow accustomed to the speed. To master it completely, he made even the simple act of running a part of his training.

[You're only just realizing that?] the Void Monarch said boastfully.

The information Nebul had delivered also included details about the Void Monarch. He had been active over four hundred years ago, though he had existed even before then. The Yokai chieftain who had reigned like a nightmare in the southern region of Eria was none other than him.

"Even so, you lost your body and got sealed."

[The bastards who came to seal me numbered in the tens of thousands. Mages, martial artists, knights, Elementalists, you name it. Every big shot at the time tried to subjugate me.]

"Why didn't you run away?"

[Because I didn't want to,] the Void Monarch's reply was swift.

"...Is that all?"

[That's all.]

"From what I heard, you really were something. You weren't a king, but they say you ruled that region like one. Among the natives of the South, there are still tribes that worship you."

[That's just how people are. They develop faith when they see a marvel beyond their comprehension. Humans are the kind of creatures who see lightning split the sky and call it a god,] the Void Monarch's voice was indifferent as he spoke. [Let's go to the South when we get the chance. I did say I'd give you my treasure.]

"Where did all your subordinates go?"

[They died,] the Void Monarch answered. After that, he said no more, apparently finding old stories unpleasant. Lee Sungmin had no desire to press him further anyway.

*The South, huh.*

He had never been there. The same was true for the North, where he was currently headed, but where in the North should he go, exactly? The place that usually came to mind was the giant city of Trabia. It was once the domain of the Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon, but after Wiji Hoyeon cut off his arm, Trabia had descended into vicious infighting. The Blood Heavenly Demon had suppressed the demonic leaders who originally thrived there, but with him weakened and humiliated, they had spun out of control.

*I heard it's overflowing not just with martial artists, but with Black Mages too. That inhumans roam about.*

Perhaps he might run into Preskan there. Lee Sungmin was actually hoping for that. If he did, he wouldn't have to go out of his way to find the man and could ask him about the Black Heart.

*

A crack split the empty air, and a man stepped out. His appearance was so strange, so sudden, that Baek Sogo skidded to a halt, catching her breath. She stared warily at Nebul, who stood not far away. He raised both his hands as he addressed her.

"I am not your enemy."

"...A dragon?" Baek Sogo asked after a moment's hesitation.

At her question, Nebul's eyes widened before he erupted in loud laughter.

"That's the first time I've heard that misunderstanding!" he boomed, unable to completely stifle his amusement.

Despite being laughed at to her face, Baek Sogo didn't feel particularly embarrassed. In truth, his appearance was so astonishing that it was an understandable mistake. Empty space had split open, and he had simply walked out. It seemed to be on a different level from the Teleport or Blink that only an archmage could use. She couldn't even properly perceive Nebul's presence, let alone glimpse his strength.

"Ah... I apologize. I laughed too much. A dragon, a dragon... Yes. I suppose you could make that mistake."

As he spoke, Nebul approached her. She revealed a sharp hostility as he drew closer, raising her Protective Qi Aura, but Nebul did not slow his pace.

"Didn't I tell you? I'm not your enemy. I'm just... an errand boy."

Saying this, Nebul reached into his coat. From inside his suit jacket, he pulled out a neatly folded envelope.

"It's a letter from Lee Sungmin."

"...What?" Baek Sogo's expression changed completely.

"Lee Sungmin sent you a letter."

She wanted to reach out and snatch it from him right away, but she endured the urge. Taking a deep breath, she calmed the threat she had raised, and the white Protective Qi Aura dissipated.

*Even if I had tried to take it by force... could I have?* Baek Sogo secretly wondered.

When she suppressed her hostility, Nebul smiled faintly and handed her the letter. After giving it to her, he disappeared into a crack in space, just as he had appeared. For a long moment, Baek Sogo stood in silence, reading the letter.

"...He was alive."

It was a simple letter. Although she couldn't completely trust it, Baek Sogo believed. It wasn't the handwriting that convinced her, but the very existence of the mysterious Nebul. She clutched the letter preciously and muttered in a small voice.

"He's alive... My Junior Disciple is alive."

At the end of the deep relief she felt, a small question arose in her mind.

*How did he get out alive?*

It was a natural question. She read the letter again. It would have been nice if it had mentioned where he was or where he was going. Feeling a sense of regret, Baek Sogo got to her feet.

*If I had been stronger... Junior Disciple wouldn't have had to step up to buy time.*

Baek Sogo chewed on her lower lip. Her only Junior Disciple, whom she had seen for the first time in years, had achieved an unbelievable level of growth. Just a year ago, he hadn't even entered the Supreme Peak, yet the young man she saw in the Dungeon seemed to possess skills equal to or greater than her own.

She decided where to go.

Baek Sogo pushed herself up from where she had collapsed. She folded the letter neatly and tucked it into her clothes. Looking toward the place where 'that mountain' was, a place too far to be seen from here, she recalled the conversation she had with Lee Sungmin before they parted.

The words telling her to run.

"...Those were words I should have said," Baek Sogo muttered as she headed towards Mount Mushii.

Although he had set Trabia as his destination, there was no reason to hurry. Winter was still a long way off.

"This damn camping," Rubia complained.

Lee Sungmin, who was roasting hunted meat over a campfire, glanced at her. "You complain a lot."

"If you're going to sleep, isn't it better to be under a roof and in a bed?"

"Did Envirus never camp out?"

"My master camped with class. With elegance. He'd always build a wonderful house with magic to sleep in."

"What a troublesome person."

"What do you mean?"

"You can sleep just fine on the ground without a roof over your head," Lee Sungmin replied, tearing off a leg from the whole roasted rabbit and handing it to her.

Despite her many complaints, Rubia accepted it readily. She carefully took the rabbit leg and bit into it. "How long do you plan on camping out?"

"Until we reach Trabia."

"We've been camping for a month already, and it'll take at least three more months to get to Trabia from here."

"I know."

"You're going to camp for three more months?"

"It doesn't seem that difficult. We can get food by hunting, and I have plenty of preserved food in my Subspace Pocket."

"What about other daily necessities?"

"There are ways."

"I wonder how someone like you became a member of Erebrisa," Rubia muttered, clicking her tongue.

At her words, Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinched. He narrowed his eyes and looked at her. "You knew?"

"You weren't exactly trying to hide it, were you?" Rubia retorted.

He had no rebuttal to that. After all, he was the one who had summoned Nebul without any regard for Rubia and the Void Monarch.

[I knew as well,] the Void Monarch rumbled.

Lee Sungmin had secretly thought as much. The Void Monarch hadn't shown any surprise when Nebul suddenly appeared.

*It seems they have a surprisingly long history.*

If the Void Monarch, who was active four hundred years ago, knew about Erebrisa, it meant the organization existed even back then.

*Were you also a member of Erebrisa?*

[I received an offer, but I refused.]

"...Is Envirus a member of Erebrisa?"

"Of course," Rubia answered in a cheerful voice.

Lee Sungmin stared at her and asked a question directed at both of them.

"What exactly is Erebrisa?"
