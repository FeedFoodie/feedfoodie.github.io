"So hurry up and get on your feet!" the Spear King roared.

Gritting his teeth, Lee Sungmin forced himself to stand. Though his body had taken no significant damage, a rampage of Demonic Energy prevented him from moving as he wished. His legs trembled violently and his muscles screamed in protest. The Spear King's brow furrowed as he watched Lee Sungmin struggle just to stay upright.

"What in the world is wrong with you?"

This was not simple exhaustion from their battle. The Spear King sensed this vaguely as he took a step closer. Lee Sungmin glared at the approaching man and tried to lift his spear, but the weapon that usually felt weightless was now an impossible burden. The Spear King's frown deepened, his eyes fixed on the trembling tip of Lee Sungmin's spear.

"You can't even control your own body," he mused aloud. "And you're the same guy who regenerated after I put a hole clean through your stomach."

"…Haa… haa…"

Dizziness washed over him. Lee Sungmin fought against his blurring vision, forcing strength into his bending waist to straighten his back. The Spear King scratched the back of his head, his gaze lingering on the spear that was raised just enough to warn him away.

"Good grief."

The feeling was just as he had spat out in the heat of the moment. It was like taking a crap and not wiping properly, or maybe stopping midway through. The metaphor was crude, but it perfectly captured his frustration. He had been getting excited, eagerly anticipating more of their fight, and he never imagined it would end so unsatisfactorily. The Spear King stopped, approaching no further, and clicked his tongue softly at the sight of Lee Sungmin trembling.

"No wonder," he said. "That lightning that burst out at the end, inside your illusion arts. I thought it was strange how your strength suddenly drained away."

"Hoo… hook…"

"It doesn't seem like you suffered internal injuries from our fight. Well... what an unsatisfying conclusion."

What a shame. It was a great shame indeed. What if that lightning, which had erupted from within the repeated illusions, had not lost its power midway? What would have happened if he still had the stamina to continue the fight after unleashing it? The Spear King sighed and looked up at the night sky.

The victor of this battle was clear. He stood perfectly fine on his own two feet while Lee Sungmin could not even control his own body. With that, the fight had to be considered over.

"In the last hundred years, you're the first one to make me bleed," the Spear King muttered. "You're also the first martial artist who uses a spear to make me bleed, and... you're the first one to make this Spear King fight to kill, win, and yet not feel the urge to finish you off for good."

"...What... do you mean...?"

"I will not kill you." The Spear King lowered his head from the night sky to look directly at Lee Sungmin. "Regarding your fate, the Martial God originally ordered me to do as I pleased. Honestly, I don't know what will happen in the future. The Dark Supreme offered blind loyalty to the Martial God, and that same Dark Supreme met his end at your hands. I don't know if the Martial God intends to avenge him... but perhaps next time, he might order your annihilation, but not now."

"...You're... not going to kill me?"

"I'll postpone it. The fight with you was enjoyable. It would have been even more so if you hadn't run out of strength like this, and..." The Spear King's expression soured. "The one I wanted to fight and kill was the martial artist who uses a spear, the one who holds the title of 'the Phantom Spear'. Not some other monster."

He looked into Lee Sungmin's eyes. What he saw there was not Lee Sungmin, but a vicious inhumanity that threatened to go berserk at any moment. The Spear King smacked his lips.

*It might be fun to fight that thing, but.*

He had no desire to cool the heat from a proper spear fight by brawling with some miscellaneous beast. Shaking off his disappointment, the Spear King swiftly turned around.

"Your condition looks quite dangerous, but I am your enemy. It's true that I like you as a spearman, but that doesn't mean I want to grant you the favor of looking after your body. If only so I can unleash my killing moves without hesitation when we fight again."

Leaving those words behind, the Spear King shot away, his movement arts carrying him quickly beyond the darkness until he vanished.

Lee Sungmin watched until he was completely gone. Then his legs gave out and he collapsed to the ground. He clenched his jaw against the throbbing headache and clutched his head.

[Get a hold of yourself,] Void Monarch warned. The great yokai had situated himself in the center of Lee Sungmin's consciousness, working to calm the seething darkness.

He had used too much Demonic Energy. The power Lee Sungmin possessed was but a portion drawn from all of Urmuri, the energy of Void Monarch himself, a being of terrifying might who was called the Nightmare of the South hundreds of years ago. No matter how much Lee Sungmin's body had mutated to become more like a Yokai, and even with the power of the Black Heart and the Dragon, this immense Demonic Energy was the power of a greedy beast. If overused, it would steal his body.

"It's an ill-fitting suit of clothes."

A grumbling voice sounded out, one he knew well. Lee Sungmin, who had been panting and trying to focus his mind, lifted his head in surprise.

"......"

Sparks of electricity flew. Rising from within the black currents, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman looked down at Lee Sungmin and frowned from behind his mask.

"Or is the problem that it fits you too well?"

"...Master."

"Don't talk," the Chairman commanded as he strode over. His hands, which had been clasped behind his back, blurred into motion.

WHAP! WHAP! WHAP! WHAP!

An invisibly fast acupoint sealing technique struck Lee Sungmin's body. As his master pressed the points in sequence, Lee Sungmin's entire frame went rigid. After completely subduing him, the Chairman laid him down and searched his robes.

"This one should have warned you. Illusionary World is too compatible with the power you possess. I believe I told you not to overuse it or drag it out for too long."

"...I knew that, but... look who my opponent was."

"True. I'll admit it was impressive that you endured this much and continued to fight against the Spear King. Though in the end, you were the one who lost."

The Chairman pulled the Ghost Mask from Lee Sungmin's robes. He placed it on Lee Sungmin's face and raised his own internal energy, his hands becoming dyed in a jet-black light.

"......"

As his hands pressed down on Lee Sungmin's chest, Lee Sungmin's rigid body trembled violently.

"Demonic Energy is highly compatible with illusion arts," the Chairman explained. "It's a bewitching power by nature, so it's a perfect match for arts that deceive opponents, confuse them, and tangle their senses."

The Chairman knew that Lee Sungmin possessed immense Demonic Energy. That was why, when adding the incantations of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder to the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, he had put a great deal of effort into the eighth move, Illusionary World. Originally, Illusionary World was not a martial art that created a spatial illusion on the scale he had just displayed. It was a spear technique that incorporated subtle principles of illusion to deceive an opponent in a touch-and-go combat situation.

"It was only because it was 'you' that you could unleash such a martial art, but you overdid it. If things had gone wrong and the rampage had worsened, your reason would have been buried, and you would have become a monster."

"...Were you watching?"

"Haras is not far from here, and the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is in Haras. Which means this one is here. Did you think this one wouldn't notice you two fighting so loudly?"

"If you were watching, why didn't you help me sooner?"

"What an impudent brat. This wasn't this one's fight, so why should this one have stepped in? If you're a disciple of this Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, you should be able to overcome threats and battles of this level on your own."

"What would you have done if I had died?"

"Then this one would have killed the Spear King. I was planning to at least get revenge for you, so don't be too upset."

"Instead of revenge, couldn't you have just kept me from dying in the first place?"

"Now, if you'd been crying and screaming for help, I might have stepped in," the Chairman said with a hearty laugh. "That would have been quite amusing to watch, after all."

At his words, Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh. Thanks to the mask suppressing his Demonic Energy and his master holding his qi and blood while infusing him with inner arts, his headache was gradually subsiding.

"...I killed the Dark Supreme."

"I know. You caused quite a commotion at the Tang Clan, too. Couldn't you have fought a little more quietly?"

"The Dark Supreme was a master. Is it easy to fight quietly against someone like that?"

"This one could have done it."

"I'm not you, Master."

"That's right. You incompetent fool," the Chairman grumbled as he straightened up. He moved his hands again, releasing the acupoints he had sealed on Lee Sungmin. "Refrain from drawing out your inner arts for the time being, and keep the mask on at all times. Now that you've rampaged like this once, you'll have to be more careful than before."

"Understood." Lee Sungmin sat up and nodded. Then, something occurred to him. "I met the Sword Immortal."

"You were lucky to make it back alive."

"...He didn't seem to have any intention of killing me. I didn't even counterattack the way you told me to, Master." Lee Sungmin told him about the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique.

The Chairman let out a small laugh and nodded. "It seems that fellow hasn't just been polishing his Dao for the past hundred years."

"Why do you think the Sword Immortal didn't kill me?"

"How would this one know what that fellow is thinking? Still, the fact that he didn't kill you probably means he didn't have a good enough reason to. The Sword Immortal may have been living in seclusion in Wudang for a long time, but that doesn't mean he has closed his eyes and ears," the Chairman said, clasping his hands behind his back once more. "You have a guest at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance."

"...A guest?"

"Silent Flash Baek Sogo."

At the Chairman's words, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. Why would his Senior Sister be at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance? Seeing his confusion, the Chairman continued.

"That movement art you learned, Dancing Shadow Soul Break, that was Silent Flash's technique, wasn't it? She's more skilled than I expected. She has reached the Transcendent Realm, her martial arts have matured... and she possesses another power entirely, separate from them."

"What do you mean by that?"

"Denir has made her his incarnation," the Chairman said, clicking his tongue. "I don't know why Denir chose her. Gods are beings whose inner thoughts are unknowable. Their power may not be that great, but the authority they possess as gods is something humans can never imitate."

"...Where is my Senior Sister?"

"She said she wanted to see you, so I gave her a room at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance and told her to wait. The Crimson Archmage you brought with you also had a connection with her. I haven't harmed or restrained them, so don't worry."

"You're very kind to women, Master."

"If you spout any more nonsensical bullshit, I'll kick your ass."

"Can't I even say that? I'm just saying it because I was curious. Since you like the Fairy Realm so much, I thought you weren't interested in grown women..."

Lee Sungmin's words were cut short as the Chairman unhesitatingly followed through on his warning.

"......"

With a dull thud, Lee Sungmin's body flew through the air. He clutched his kicked rear end and let out a pained groan.

"Do you think this one is looking after them out of some ulterior motive? I only took care of them because they have a connection to my idiot disciple."

"Thank... you."

"You run your mouth without even recognizing this Master's broad generosity and ocean-like grace. Stop thinking useless thoughts and get up. I had to take an unexpected night stroll because of you."

"...Thank you for your help."

"Took you long enough to say it," the Chairman grumbled, already walking with long strides, his hands clasped behind his back. "Let's go back."

"......"

Lee Sungmin rubbed his stinging rear end and followed after his master.
