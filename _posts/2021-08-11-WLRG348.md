Lee Sungmin ignored the foul smell, his brow furrowing at the Spear King's words. A fight, out of the blue. He knew the Spear King was that kind of person, but the suddenness of it still caught him off guard.

"Why would I have to fight you?"

"Because it's been a while," the Spear King said, a grin revealing his yellowed teeth. "We never finished our fight. I stopped in the middle because I lost interest."

"Are you suggesting we settle the score we couldn't back then?"

"I've spent ten years rolling around on this mountain like a dog," the Spear King spat, his eyes flashing with a bitter light. "Even when I teamed up with the Black Dragon Knight, we couldn't do a thing against the one-armed and exhausted Martial God. In fact, we were the ones pushed back. If we hadn't endured the humiliation and fled, he would've killed us right then and there."

The incident in the North ten years ago remained the greatest humiliation of the Spear King's life. He had never teamed up with anyone before, and even after swallowing his pride to do so, he had still failed to defeat the Martial God.

"Before I challenged him, I saw your Master, the Demon Emperor, fight. It was an eye-opening experience. Everything I had trained for my entire life felt like child's play. I trained on this mountain for ten years to reach that level of martial prowess, but I'm still far from him. If I fight you, the one he taught directly, I think I'll be able to know for sure."

"Is that really a reason to fight?"

"I haven't seen the end of martial arts yet, so the world can't be destroyed. However, that doesn't mean I have to help you, does it?" The Spear King chuckled. "If you want my help, you have to make me want to help."

The Black Dragon Knight, watching the Spear King's back, clicked his tongue and shook his head.

[I suppose I can't avoid this.]

The Void Monarch sneered inside his head. Lee Sungmin sighed.

"Alright," he said. "Instead, let's do this. We'll keep it moderate."

"Are you scared?"

"Of course not. I'm just suggesting we make a promise beforehand since there's nothing to gain by killing each other."

"It'll feel like stopping halfway through taking a shit."

"And it seems difficult to fight right now," Lee Sungmin added. "Aren't you quite drained of energy as well?"

"A day, no, half a day is enough. I'll be fine after eating, drinking, and doing some qi circulation."

With that, the Spear King plopped down on the spot. Lee Sungmin turned his head, seeking Yana and Baek Sogo's agreement. As their eyes met, Yana spoke first.

"If it looks like you're going to die, I will intervene."

Baek Sogo said nothing, but her expression showed she thought the same. The Spear King, now sitting, ignored their gazes and opened his Subspace Pocket. He took out a large amount of food and water, gulping down the saliva that filled his mouth. He had restricted his food during his ascetic training on the mountain and hadn't eaten a proper meal in years.

However, before that, he took out a mirror to look at his own face. The Black Dragon Knight, perhaps curious about his own appearance, sat next to him and peered into it. The Spear King scowled and shoved him away.

"Don't come any closer. You smell like shit."

"That's the smell coming from the top of your head."

"It's probably the smell from under your nose."

It seemed they had grown quite close over the ten years, though their gazes toward each other were still filled with killing intent. In the end, they couldn't stand each other's stench and shot up from their seats.

"Let's wash up before we eat."

"I agree."

The two amicably headed toward the waterfall to bathe. Meanwhile, Lee Sungmin also sat down and crossed his legs. He had somehow ended up fighting the Spear King, but he inwardly thought it was for the best.

[I'll be able to measure myself against the Martial God.]

The Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight had fought the Martial God. A fight with them would show him exactly what level he had reached.

{sep}

As promised, the Spear King finished his qi circulation after half a day and rose to his feet. His emaciated, skin-and-bones appearance had returned to normal, a testament to the perfect control he had over his body. He cracked his knuckles and picked up his spear, which had been stuck in the ground nearby.

They descended the mountain, following Mushii's guidance. Even if they planned to keep it 'moderate', there was a risk the entire mountain would collapse if they fought on it.

"The ten years we each spent were different," the Spear King muttered, standing on a wide plain. "Ten years ago, your fighting spirit was incomplete and crude. Now it's not incomplete, but it's become even cruder. You don't even seem human."

"Because that's what I've become."

"You've become a complete Demonic Spirit? For that, your mind seems to be intact. Hehe, well, it doesn't matter. It seems you've definitely gotten stronger."

Just as the Spear King sensed the change in Lee Sungmin, Lee Sungmin felt it in him. The Spear King's spirit had been honed to a razor's edge, far sharper than it was ten years ago. A martial artist of his caliber had spent a decade in ascetic training on this mountain. Though it was said that the higher one's prowess, the harder it was to advance, those ten years had successfully guided the Spear King to a new level.

Lee Sungmin gripped his new spear. *It won't shatter this time, will it?* If it shattered again, he wouldn't be able to face Celgerus.

"......"

The Spear King split his long spear into two, holding one piece in each hand. He didn't even bother with a stance. He simply lunged, his steps devouring the distance between them in an instant. Two spears of different lengths tore through space, thrusting forward in a blur of motion.

And yet, to Lee Sungmin's eyes, the movements were perfectly clear. Movements he couldn't see before. Such speed. The trajectory of the incoming spears. Against the Spear King's attack, which came from two directions to converge into one, Lee Sungmin's spear moved in the most efficient and threatening direction.

"......"

Their spears collided with a dull sound.

SCREEECH!

The weapons immediately slid against each other. The Spear King flexibly twisted both his arms, his spears dominating the space in an instant. What he wielded could no longer even be called a spear. Sparks of electricity crackled as Gale Thunder leaped across the space.

KRRAKAKAK!

The spot where Lee Sungmin had been standing just a moment ago was torn apart by a formless spear strike. In that same instant, Lee Sungmin appeared behind the Spear King.

Feeling the sharp point of a spear stabbing at him, the Spear King burst into laughter. He whipped around and swung his spear in a wide arc, tearing through the purple electricity. Beyond it, a short spear shot out, aiming for Lee Sungmin's chest.

There was no need to raise his Protective Qi Aura. Lee Sungmin readjusted his grip on the long spear and swung it in a short, batting motion. After deflecting the short spear, he lowered his stance. The power to cause death dwelled within both their weapons, contained and never scattering.

His heart pounded. An inexhaustible Demonic Energy erupted, spreading through the qi and blood of his entire body. His whole being felt like it was burning up. Having transformed into a Yokai, his sharp teeth itched. His muscles, overflowing with power, craved more. This was not an opponent to hesitate against. Lee Sungmin allowed his body's rampage.

RUUUUMBLE!

A thunderclap vibrated from within him. His body, lunging forward from a hair's breadth away, became a terrifying force of violence in itself. The Spear King crossed his two spears and held them out in front of him. The impact sent his body sliding far back.

He laughed loudly, his presence wavering. The two spears he held became the Spear King himself. The Spear Unification that had threatened Lee Sungmin ten years ago unfolded, more menacing and complete than before.

*I can see it.*

*I can see things I couldn't see ten years ago.* He understood Spear Unification clearly now. In the field of the 'spear', at least, Lee Sungmin had not yet reached the Spear King's level. Even with the body of the Slaughter Predator, his martial arts theory for wielding it still fell short.

However, he could respond. Because he could see it. The two spears created a storm. Facing an offensive with no openings, Lee Sungmin dove in without hesitation.

Faster.

He used the fastest of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Deadly Flash Lightning. Faster, and with mysterious phenomena added. Space distorted. The pressure of the extended phenomena ensnared the Spear King's spears.

BAAAANG!

The torn air exploded outwards. Their spears were thrown back.

[In terms of technique, you are lacking.]

The Void Monarch advised. If he competed with technique, he would only be toyed with. Where Lee Sungmin was definitively superior was the toughness of his body. His endless Demonic Energy and inner arts. That was what created his speed and power. Even a simple move that couldn't deceive could be thrust at a speed that was 'absolutely' impossible to dodge.

The Spear King's expression changed. In reality, he couldn't dodge it. Lee Sungmin's spear was too fast. He quickly brought his spears together in front of his chest and raised his Protective Qi Aura.

BOOOOM!

The Spear King's body was pushed far back. Rather than forcefully resisting, he naturally slid, dispersing the impact. Immediately after, Lee Sungmin exploded the Demonic Energy gathered at the tip of his spear. The projected enhanced qi engulfed the Spear King's entire body. He roared and fiercely swung his two spears, scattering the attack into torn fragments.

Just because he was superior in body and inner arts didn't mean this was an opponent he could overwhelm. Lee Sungmin waited for the Spear King's reaction, keeping him in check with his spear held forward.

The Spear King looked down at his aching wrists and grinned, baring his teeth.

"This can't be moderate," he muttered.

VIIIIING.

The two spears in his hands began to vibrate. Lee Sungmin's Demonic Eye saw an unknown energy entangling the weapons that had achieved Spear Unification.

*That's dangerous.* His expression hardened at the tingling sense of crisis. It was just as the Spear King had said. Now that it had started, fighting moderately was impossible. Against this man, he had to be ready to die.

The Spear King's spears continued to vibrate. Lee Sungmin wondered what to do. If they kept fighting, one of them might die. If he were to stick to 'moderate', he should stop now. Even knowing that, he didn't want to back down. The Spear King had trained for ten years after seeing the martial prowess of the Demon Emperor, and Lee Sungmin had inherited the power of that same man. He wanted to test it. The desire welled up in his chest.

Regardless of the outcome, he wanted a proper match with the outstanding martial artist known as the Spear King. He wanted to know how much his current self could do against the spear of a man who was technically superior, a man who had dedicated hundreds of years to his craft.

"I become the spear. The spear becomes me," the Spear King muttered in a low voice. He had realized it decades ago and had refined it for decades since. For the last ten years, he had trained to advance further than anyone else. Even so, this was the first time he was using it himself. His ascetic training had become mostly meditation after a few years. This was the first time he was unfolding with his body the martial theory he had only refined in his head.

What moves is not the body, nor the spear.

It is the mind.

The Spear King pushed his spear straight forward as Lee Sungmin unleashed Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. Lee Sungmin's body, wrapped in purple electricity, vanished from space. The Spear King's eyes could not see the movement. However, he followed it by instinct.

It was slightly off. The Spear King's spear pierced empty air. Lee Sungmin was right next to it. If the spear had been just a little more accurate, Lee Sungmin would not have been able to dodge.

The surprise did not dull his fingertips. He combined the second form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Homing Chain Lightning Flash, with Bloody Pursuit and even Flesh Devouring Blood Fang. Even while unleashing three different martial arts simultaneously, there was no strain on his inner arts. Despite movements that surpassed the limits of the body, his muscles didn't tear and his bones didn't break. In this moment, Lee Sungmin's bones and muscles bent as flexibly as rubber.

A single thrust of his spear contained countless variations. Against such an attack, the Spear King pushed the tip of his spear toward Lee Sungmin, continuously, without stopping.

It was a scene from his memory. The Demon Emperor, who had lost his power due to the Spirit's intervention. The Martial God's attack that had descended upon him. The single palm strike extended by the seemingly helpless Demon Emperor had crushed all of the Martial God's attacks.

Now, too.

Lee Sungmin saw his own attack being torn apart. He gasped, pulling back just before his spear shattered. The Spear King's eyes became bloodshot as he tried to push his spear to the very end, but then…

"Keuheuk!"

Blood sprayed from his mouth. The strength left his trembling legs. The Spear King collapsed to a sitting position. He did not let go of his spear, but he could no longer wield it. He continued to cough up blood with his head bowed.

"...Damn it," he spat, wiping the blood from his mouth with the back of his hand.

Lee Sungmin, unable to understand what had just happened, stared blankly at the Spear King.
