After forcing the Black Dragon Knight back a considerable distance, the Spear King withdrew his spear. To continue the fight would mean one of them would surely die, and honestly, the Spear King could not be certain which of them it would be.

*You sly bastard.*

It was the first time he had truly witnessed the man's skill, and the Black Dragon Knight was far stronger than he had ever imagined. Seeing the leeway his opponent still possessed, it seemed there would be little difference between them if they both went all out. The Spear King felt a deep regret at that fact. If he had his way, he would have fought with everything he had right then and there.

However, he could not.

*A pleasant dilemma.*

The Spear King pulled back, his heart fluttering with excitement for another reason. He had promised a fight with the Phantom Spear, and though the man was still weaker than him, the prospect of facing another spearman was too thrilling to pass up. Fighting the Phantom Spear came first. Honestly, he also felt a keen interest in the malevolent presence now approaching from this direction. He wanted to fight that one too.

The Spear King smacked his lips and turned around.

*The North really is a good place.*

With that thought, he shot forward, determined to leave the area without any lingering attachments. He ranked his opponents in his mind. First, the Phantom Spear. After their duel, he would return to the North. Then, he could fight the owner of that unknown presence.

The Black Dragon Knight did not give chase. He took a deep breath and cast off his tattered robes, his glare fixed on the Spear King's retreating back.

"Son of a bitch!" he gritted out.

He was uninjured, the sturdy scales of his draconic heritage having fully protected him from the Spear King's fierce assault, but the lack of injury did nothing to soothe his foul mood. He had never imagined he would be obstructed by the Spear King, another member of Heaven Beyond Heaven.

*The Phantom Spear... is he gone?*

No, more importantly... The Black Dragon Knight snapped his head around as a chill washed over him from the distance. It was not the Phantom Spear. Someone else was approaching. He did not know who it was, but his face stiffened. A presence in no way inferior to the Spear King's was coming.

*Could it be... the Vampire Queen?*

He had never met Jeniella of Blood Charm in person, but the approaching presence was so intense that it brought the legendary Vampire Queen to mind. He had no intention of fighting her. The Black Dragon Knight kicked off the ground and leaped upward. If the Phantom Spear had already escaped, there was no reason for him to remain in this forest.

A black light swallowed him as he leaped into the air. Transformed into his dragon form, he spread his wings wide, intending to soar into the heavens.

"Ugh?!"

However, he could not. As if they had been waiting for him, hundreds of black magic bullets descended the moment he took to the sky. It was a parting gift from Kim Jonghyun. The black mage's physical condition had forced him to leave, but that did not mean he was willing to let the Black Dragon Knight escape unscathed after interfering with his ritual. The countless Finger Bullets were too weak to pierce the dragon's scales, but they were more than enough to disrupt his wing beats.

In the brief moment the dragon was stalled, Ju Won rocketed into the air. The Black Dragon Knight, flapping his wings to scatter the magic bullets, widened his eyes as he saw Ju Won jump to the same height at which he was flying. He hastily opened his maw and tried to bite the intruder. It was not the Vampire Queen, he could see that with his own two eyes, but the ferocious fighting spirit radiating from Ju Won was enough to make him feel threatened.

Ju Won swung his fist directly into the snout of the dragon lunging to bite him.

"......"

With a deafening crack, the Black Dragon Knight's head snapped to the side.

"Krrrk!"

He was not used to fighting in a dragon's body. The Black Dragon Knight tried to use polymorph again, but Ju Won was faster, leaping onto the dragon's back. With an indifferent expression, he reached out, grabbed the Black Dragon Knight's wing, and tore the entire appendage from its socket with a sickening sound.

"......"

"Kuaaaah!"

The Black Dragon Knight screamed. The dragon's scales and bones had offered no protection from Ju Won's raw power. The flailing body of the Black Dragon Knight plummeted downward, but even in his agony, he managed to polymorph back into his human form.

"What are you!?" he roared, the sound like a scream as he spread his arms. He was unaccustomed to fighting as a dragon, but he was a master of combat as a human.

A jet-black enhanced qi erupted, enveloping his body as a terrible storm of power swept over Ju Won.

Ju Won, also falling through the air, plunged into the storm without hesitation. He swung his arms, and the vortex of enhanced qi created by the Black Dragon Knight was torn apart by his limbs.

*Lycanthrope!*

A feral roar escaped him as his body swelled, silver-gray fur sprouting to cover his entire form. His eyes, which had held only boredom, transformed into those of a savage beast. As Ju Won charged forward bare-bodied, the Black Dragon Knight moved his hands in a blur, firing hundreds of palm strikes in a single gesture.

He had lived for hundreds of years as a half-human, half-dragon. Even with a body far superior to a human's, he had never neglected his martial arts training. As such, the palm techniques of the Black Dragon Knight, who had reached the Transcendent Realm through pure martial skill, were powerful enough to shatter the heavens.

Ju Won, however, showed no hesitation. Although he had founded the Ghost Wolf Sect, neither he nor the other Lycanthropes belonging to it practiced martial arts. The sect existed simply because they needed a plausible identity, but even without martial arts, Ju Won's power, having reached the pinnacle of his kind, was in no way inferior to the Black Dragon Knight's assault.

KRRRRRUMBLE!

A massive sound, like hundreds of bombs exploding at once, echoed through the air. The Black Dragon Knight's face twisted as he watched Ju Won break through his barrage of palm strikes. He began to draw out a massive power in earnest. Dragon's Pressure radiated in all directions as a darkness blacker than night gathered in his hands.

KABOOM!

A powerful force descended on Ju Won. In response, Ju Won opened his mouth wide toward the encroaching power and roared. The force exploded in mid-air. The Black Dragon Knight was stunned by Ju Won's unbelievably brutish counter.

Having charged in recklessly, Ju Won swung a massive fist that slammed into the Protective Qi Aura and scales.

"Keuk!"

Blood spurted from the Black Dragon Knight's mouth. The situation was not good. He had already expended energy by firing two breaths, and while he was not injured from his clash with the Spear King, he was somewhat exhausted. Fighting Ju Won, the King of Lycanthropes, in this state was overwhelmingly disadvantageous.

*I have to escape...!*

The Black Dragon Knight's face twisted wretchedly. It did not seem like he could escape easily, but he had to get out of this place one way or another.

The Fairy Horse leaped through space, arriving in an instant over Haras, the home of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Appearing in the sky, Lee Sungmin slowly descended with Scarlet.

"You've come."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was waiting not far from where they touched the ground. Standing with his hands clasped behind his back, he looked at Scarlet and narrowed his eyes behind his mask.

"I wondered who you went for. A wench, of course."

"What do you mean, 'of course'?"

"I'm not blaming you. Don't they say heroes are fond of women? Not that I'm saying you're a hero."

At the Chairman's muttering, Scarlet's eyebrow twitched. Given her personality, she would have cursed without hesitation after hearing such nonsense, but the Chairman's aura was so stern that it made even her choose her words carefully.

"What happened?" the Chairman asked, his attention returning to Lee Sungmin. Other than that single glance, he asked nothing else about Scarlet.

Instead, he asked about what had transpired in the forest. Lee Sungmin recounted everything he had witnessed there without hesitation.

"The Spear King, the Black Dragon Knight, Ju Won..." the Chairman mused, stroking his chin after listening to the story. "I can't tell what exactly that Black Mage named Kim Jonghyun was trying to do. Summoned the Demon Race... hmm. This one doesn't know much about magic, you see."

"I don't know either."

"Useless brat. Why go all that way if you weren't even going to find out what was happening?"

"What if I ended up fighting the Black Dragon Knight or the Spear King for no reason?"

"If you were stronger than them, you wouldn't have had to worry about that. Incompetent brat."

Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled. The Chairman was talking about the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight, men who stood at the pinnacle of their fields, not some neighborhood mutts, but even though he felt it was unfair, he had nothing to say in rebuttal. When he thought about it, everything the Chairman said was correct.

"...This unworthy disciple apologizes for being incompetent and weak."

"That's nothing new, but it's good that you know."

Lee Sungmin barely swallowed the curse that rose to his throat. He did not think the Chairman would kill him for cursing, but his backside would surely burn as if it were on fire. Even at his current level, he had no confidence he could avoid a spanking from the man.

"Still, you did well to get information on the Dark Supreme. Five months left."

"Is it okay to kill the Dark Supreme?"

"Is there a reason not to?" the Chairman retorted. "Didn't Heaven Beyond Heaven try to kill you first? Surely you don't think you can hold hands with them and get along?"

"If they don't go out of their way to pick a fight."

"We'll see," the Chairman chuckled heartily at Lee Sungmin's reply. "You've heard about Heaven Beyond Heaven's goal, haven't you? To eliminate all inhuman. This one has said it before, but well... setting aside whether that's even possible... this one doesn't believe the Martial God truly desires something so absurd."

"Are you saying there's some other intention?"

"Perhaps. In fact, isn't the ideal of Heaven Beyond Heaven quite plausible and sweet to the masters who belong there? To erase all the monsters, create a world for humans, and then reign over it? Hahaha!" The Chairman burst into loud laughter as if he found his own words amusing. "The problem is that when you get old, your lust for power grows needlessly. Anyway, this one agrees with killing the Dark Supreme. You have to cut ties when you can."

"I understand."

"There are still a few months left until Tang Ahee's birthday. You should stop by the Dwarf Village in the meantime."

Lee Sungmin had the scales and bones of a Dragon. He had obtained them quite some time ago but had let them sit because he had no way to process them. That problem was solved when he received a handwritten letter from the Fairy Queen. If he took it to the Dwarf Village and met the chief, he would be able to have the materials forged.

"You have already reached the limit of your current level," the Chairman said in a stern voice. "No matter how hard you train here, it will be impossible for your martial arts to improve by leaps and bounds to the point where you can take down the Black Dragon Knight with your own skills. Therefore, you must wield a weapon good enough to make up for your lacking ability."

"Ahem." Lee Sungmin turned his head to the side and let out a short, dry cough for no reason.

"...Excuse me."

Beside Lee Sungmin, who was enduring the Chairman's relentless tirade, Scarlet cautiously spoke up.

"What should I do?"

"I will treat you as a guest." The Chairman's gaze finally turned to Scarlet. "If there is anything you need, this one will provide it to the best of my ability."

"Ah... yes."

Unlike when he was persecuting Lee Sungmin, the Chairman answered Scarlet in a rather gentle voice. At that, Scarlet became a little flustered and replied in a small voice.

[Are you interested?] Lee Sungmin found the Chairman's attitude strange and sent him a Thought Transmission.

[Do you want to get beaten?] came the immediate reply.

Lee Sungmin clamped his mouth shut.
