He crawled, then he walked, and then he ran.

Moving was a slow, laborious process. His body felt weightless, yet he could not muster any proper strength. It was a continuous, ambiguous sensation that made it impossible to tell if time was stretching or if his consciousness was simply fading away. At some point in that void, he had progressed from crawling to walking, and now he was running, wishing with every fiber of his being that he could run faster.

However, he could not. The scenery, what little there was of it, passed by in a sluggish crawl. In truth, there was no scenery to be seen at all. The world Lee Sungmin ran through was stained pitch-black, a night without a single point of light, much like the oppressive darkness he had grown accustomed to on Mount Mushii.

He knew he was running, and he wished he could fly, but his feet remained bound to the unseen ground. Eventually, his run slowed to a stop, and he found his spear in his hands. His body moved on its own, tracing the familiar paths of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, a technique learned from Rannachal.

*Just one more time.*

He wished for it desperately. Lee Sungmin tried to recall the enhanced qi he had manifested, attempting time and again to bring it forth once more. He wanted to imbue his inner qi into the spearhead and see it coated in those brilliant purple flames.

It did not work.

He could not even remember the sensation itself, how it had even been possible. He tried several times, but each attempt ended in failure. The inner qi coiled in his qi center refused to move, as if they had hardened into stone.

*Why on earth? Was the enhanced qi from that time just a coincidence?*

Amid his repeated failures, despair began to creep in. At some point, he stopped even holding his spear and simply ceased to move. The time he spent collapsed on the ground grew longer, and frustration crushed what little motivation he had left. He felt as if he were rotting away.

When he opened his eyes, the loud, drumming sound of rain filled the air. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the ceiling above him. It was not the ceiling of the carriage. He parted his lips, his breath catching for a moment as he realized that everything he had just experienced had been a dream.

"You're awake?"

Scarlet's voice reached him from a chair placed by the window. She took off the glasses perched on her nose and closed the book she had been reading.

"You've been out for three days," she said. "I was starting to wonder if you were going to die, but I guess not."

"...Where... is this?" he asked, his voice raspy and weak.

His entire body felt languid and stiff, so he had no doubt she was telling the truth about the three days. Besides, there was no reason for Scarlet to lie about something like that.

"Just some common village. We didn't have enough time to make it to a city."

"Did you drive the carriage, Scarlet?"

"I know how," Scarlet grumbled as she stood up. "I don't like it, but what choice did I have? The guy who's supposed to drive collapsed, so I had to step up, didn't I?"

She glanced at the rain pouring down outside the window.

"It started this morning. Looks like it'll stop by tomorrow... but the ground gets bad after it rains, you know. We probably won't be able to leave until the day after tomorrow at the earliest, and you just woke up today."

Scarlet approached his bedside.

"I did call this village's doctor, but he wasn't very skilled. It's not like there's a healing mage or a priest here either. For now, I used a potion to treat your external injuries somehow. Go ahead and check."

Though his mind was still a little hazy, Lee Sungmin followed her words and tried to move his arms. He remembered injuries to both his palms and elbow joints, as well as his left side and ribs.

Splints were fastened to both of his arms. When he glanced at Scarlet, she replied with an unconcerned expression.

"I did it. I know first aid."

Lee Sungmin carefully tried to move his arms beneath the splints. They were a little stiff, but there was no major martial arts theory in moving them. A potion could not perfectly heal all wounds. While effective on scrapes and cuts, fractures and internal injuries were impossible to heal completely with just a potion. Still, it was a stroke of good luck that his bones had set without much pain.

*No. Perhaps this is also a power of the heart?*

Lee Sungmin did not know the full extent of the Black Heart's abilities. The only thing he had confirmed was its power to guide miracle herbs directly to his qi center without any refinement process. He knew nothing else.

He thought of Aine, who possessed the same kind of heart yet was so different from him. She had various abilities that he could not use. Seeing her wield the Hundred Steps Divine Fist, it seemed she had stolen Zeon's martial arts through his heart. He did not know how such a thing was possible, but Aine possessed other strange powers as well. Her limbs constantly changed shape, and she could even cover her entire body in a hard carapace. Her physical abilities far surpassed his own, and she could regenerate wounds in an instant. From any perspective, they were abilities that could not be called human.

He was different. Lee Sungmin could not change his limbs, nor did he possess such a phenomenal regenerative ability. However, he might have gained some degree of regeneration, even if it was not on par with Aine's.

"I think I'm okay."

"How are the internal injuries?"

Scarlet came closer and untied the splints on his arms. Lee Sungmin moved them a little more freely before sitting up cross-legged. He had thought his internal injuries from the fight with Aine were quite severe, but in just three days, they had already healed. Even with the help of a potion, it was a bizarrely fast recovery.

"Why didn't you run away?" Lee Sungmin asked after circulating his inner qi slightly.

Scarlet, who had pulled a chair over to sit beside him, let out a dry laugh.

"Because I didn't want to."

"Wasn't it a dangerous situation? If things had gone wrong..."

"Not really. It didn't actually become dangerous for me," she said. "I just didn't want to run away, that's all, and I didn't want to abandon you."

It seemed she did not want to elaborate, so Lee Sungmin did not press her further.

"More importantly, what was with that monster kid? Why did she suddenly show up and attack us?"

"...It was probably because of me," Lee Sungmin answered in a small voice.

At his words, Scarlet's eyebrows twitched. "What do you mean?"

He owed Scarlet a debt. She could have abandoned him and fled, but she had chosen to stay. Without her help, he would not have been able to hold out against Aine. With a sigh, Lee Sungmin told her everything about his relationship with Aine. Scarlet listened with her lips pressed firmly together as he recounted what happened in the Dungeon, the Black Heart he had acquired, and Aine, who likely possessed the same kind of heart. When he finished, she let out a deep sigh and scratched the back of her head.

"...No wonder. I thought it was strange how you kept running into that psycho bastard, Kim Jonghyun. It was because of that heart?"

"Ah... yes."

"You've got some seriously rotten luck. No, in this case, should I say you're lucky? In any case, your body has gotten better than before, right? If you think of it that way, I'd say it's a fair price."

"You mean nearly dying?"

"The world is full of guys who'd risk that kind of danger for a miraculous encounter. You just have to not die from now on, right?" Scarlet grumbled as she stood up. "You should know you're lucky. Thanks to traveling with me, it'll be easier for you to stay alive."

"...Are you saying you'll continue to travel with me?"

"Why? Did you think that after hearing all this, I'd value my own life too much and stop traveling with you? You've really misjudged me, haven't you? I'm not that heartless."

Scarlet snickered. She picked up the glasses and book she had left on the table by the window.

"Anyway, I'm glad your body seems to be okay. I was originally planning to leave after the rain stops and the ground dries a bit, but after hearing your situation, I don't think we can afford to be so leisurely. Let's leave as soon as the rain stops tomorrow."

"Ah... yes."

"You don't have to worry about me. No matter how dangerous it gets, I have ways to protect my own life."

"Get some rest," Scarlet said before leaving the room. It was to be expected that she was staying in a different room. She had only been here to nurse him.

Once Scarlet left, Lee Sungmin was alone. He thought about her parting words. For now, he could not understand why she would risk danger to travel with him. Since she clearly had no intention of telling him, he knew he would not find out the reason even if he pressed her. As far as he could tell, however, there was an emotional reason behind her words and attitude, and that emotion was not the kind of affection between a man and a woman.

Lee Sungmin pushed the thoughts aside and carefully pushed himself up. His body felt languid and stiff, likely from lying down for so long. It was a heaviness similar to what he had felt in his dream, which was not a pleasant sensation.

His hand reached for the spear propped against the wall, his mind a storm of thoughts, most of them stemming from the despair he had felt in his dream. When he grasped the spear, he took a deep breath, calming his trembling heart.

Lee Sungmin tried to recall the feeling from just before he passed out. In truth, he did not really know the method. It was not as if he knew how to use enhanced qi. It had come to him at the end of tedious thoughts and self-loathing, a moment when his mind seemed to clear, and suddenly, he was able to use it.

Fumbling to remember that sensation, Lee Sungmin drew up the inner qi from his qi center. He infused them into the spear, and a purple mist began to drift around the shaft and spearhead. This was not enough. He continued to pour in his inner qi, and the color of the wavering mist deepened as it began to clump together. A little more. Trying his best to remember the sensation, he infused even more.

Then, in a single moment, the mist completely coalesced. It became a flickering flame. Although its color was paler and its size smaller than the enhanced qi he had reached in his fight with Aine, it was an undeniable fact. He had gone beyond simply coating his weapon with qi and had reached the level of enhanced qi.

It was also proof that he had overcome the biggest, hardest, and highest wall of the peak realm. While being able to use enhanced qi did not automatically make one a Transcendent Peak Master, all Transcendent Peak Masters used it. In other words, enhanced qi was proof that one had approached the realm of the Supreme Peak.

*But it's immature.*

That was an unavoidable fact. It took too long and required too much mental concentration to create. The consumption of inner qi was also high, making it impossible to use recklessly. His top priority now would be getting used to forming it.

Lee Sungmin put down the spear, and he cried a little. When he first used enhanced qi in the fight against Aine, he had wanted to cry then, too. He was moved to have entered a realm he could not even have dreamed of in his past life. These were those kinds of tears. *Have I moved forward a little? How much forward have I gone? One step? Maybe it was only half a step.*

That was not important. The important thing was that he had moved forward.

Lee Sungmin knelt on the spot and continued to cry.

Outside the window, the rain was letting up.
