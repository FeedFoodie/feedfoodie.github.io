Even before the Sword Immortal fell, Lee Sungmin was already in motion, thrusting his spear at the flame-wreathed Aine. Hot tongues of fire flickered with every movement of the weapon, trying to swallow it whole, but the electric current imbued in the spear kept them at bay. The flames dared not consume the attack.

Crouched within the inferno, Aine felt her own heart pound as if it would burst. The impulse sparked a maddening hunger, a craving that surpassed the satisfaction from the dozens of hearts she had already devoured. She salivated at the mere thought of plucking out Lee Sungmin's heart, imagining a taste so incredibly delicious it would eclipse anything she had ever known.

*Of course it would be,* she realized, her mouth filling with saliva. *It's the same as the one in my body.*

The waiting was unbearable. She had to eat it, and quickly, but she knew it would not be so easy.

Lee Sungmin steeled his resolve. *I have to kill Aine here.* Now was the perfect time, while the Sword Immortal held Jeniela at bay. He knew Jeniela was trying to turn Aine into a Slaughter Predator. It was an undeniable fact. Even without being predestined for it like him, Aine held limitless potential to become a monster on par with a Slaughter Predator, especially now that she had devoured the Spirit King of Flame.

As he executed his movement arts, evading crashing waves of fire, he answered the Void Monarch's grumbling.
[I see why Jeniela came all this way. She probably intends to have that wench devour the Sword Immortal.]

*I don't understand Jeniela,* Lee Sungmin thought back. *Why is she making Aine do it? It would be much easier in many ways if Jeniela did it herself.*

[How would I know what that crazy bitch is thinking… but she's shown too much just to say that,] the Void Monarch muttered. [That wench would rather watch than do it herself. She has seen too much with her All-Seeing Eye, and she has spent hundreds of years acting to realize what she has seen. Since a Slaughter Predator failed to appear, that wench is trying to create one with her own hands, all on the premise that this world will be destroyed.]

*So she wants to be a spectator.*

[That's right. If you kill Aine here… you might just see that wench, Jeniela, go on a truly insane rampage. If even that little monster dies, the future Jeniela saw will be completely closed off.]

The result, however, would not change, the Void Monarch added. [Because if that happens, Jeniela will truly act recklessly. Until now, she has acted to realize the future she saw, rather than taking direct action herself. She attacked this mountain to strengthen that little monster's power, but what if that's gone? How will Jeniela act?]

The Void Monarch's words came from a perspective Lee Sungmin had not considered, and he could not help but hesitate. He couldn't ignore the warning. As the Monarch said, Jeniela had moved quietly until now for the sole purpose of forging Aine into a Slaughter Predator. That was the only reason for the current reprieve. If Jeniela had been on the front lines from the very beginning…

[The world would have been destroyed during the 10 years you were Sealed.]

The Void Monarch stated it without the slightest hesitation, and Lee Sungmin had to admit it was true. In the world right now, there was no one who could stop Jeniela alone.

[It seems you've decided you must kill that wench here, but this Elder doesn't think that's necessarily the case. Her existence keeps Jeniela's actions under control to some extent.]

*Even so, Aine is too dangerous to be left alone.*

[That's true. The Sword Immortal will probably die at Jeniela's hands, and if things go according to the picture Jeniela has painted, that wench will eat his corpse. Unlike you, that wench doesn't hesitate to devour, and she's more efficient at it than you are.]

KWAREUREUREU!

The flames clung to each other, stretching and writhing before Aine uncoiled from her crouch. A massive wall of fire erupted, advancing rapidly and burning everything in its path as it surged to overwhelm him. Lee Sungmin spun his spear and took several steps back.

[Flee,] he sent a Thought Transmission to the Wudang Sect's Sect Leader.

The Sect Leader opened his mouth to protest, but Lee Sungmin cut him off. [This is the will of Elder Sword Immortal. Flee. Abandon this mountain and run as far away as you can.]

He did not mention the Sword Immortal's impending death. The Sect Leader did not seem the type to retreat obediently after hearing such news. He would likely fly into a rage, vowing to avenge his grandmaster, which was not what the Sword Immortal wanted.

Lee Sungmin opened the path of the Void, tearing a large hole in the middle of the fire wall. Before he could execute the next technique, however, the surging flames filled the gap once more. He thought calmly.

He had intended to kill Aine, but what then? What would he do about a rampaging Jeniela? It was true that Aine's existence restrained Jeniela, even if only slightly. If Aine died, Jeniela would step forward herself and become the calamity of The End.

He needed time. He had to buy it somehow. Killing Aine would unleash the disaster that was Jeniela, and the current Lee Sungmin could not handle her.

He had to create a variable. If none existed, he would have to force one into being.

[What will you do?] the Void Monarch asked.

Before Lee Sungmin could answer, a sense of crisis flared from behind. He twisted instantly, swinging his spear wide to push back Chen and Kun, who had hidden behind the flames to launch their attack. So, Jeniela's vampires were finally moving. That could only mean Aine was truly that important to her plans.

A thought bloomed in Lee Sungmin's mind, and hearing it, the Void Monarch let out a sound mixed with laughter.
[It might be a bad move, you know?]

*It will become a variable.*

[You crazy bastard!] the Void Monarch cackled.

There were three enemies, Aine, Chen, and Kun, while he was alone. That was not a major problem, not as long as Jeniela… no, as long as Gemini was not added to the mix.

"…Hoo-uk."

Lee Sungmin took a breath, his focus narrowing to the spear in his hand. Ever since unfolding the pinnacle of martial arts, he had constantly felt its weight. The Spear King had told him this was the beginning of Spear Unification, to know its weight, to know the spear, and to be conscious of holding it.

*I become the spear, and the spear becomes me.*

When he had asked about the next realm, the Spear King had only cursed at him.
"How should I know, you bastard? Are you picking a fight with me now, saying I've only made it this far with the title of the Spear King? If you're curious, go ask your dead Master!"

It meant that even the Spear King had not reached beyond Spear Unification. He had somehow reached the entrance to the realm the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had shown in his fight with the Martial God, but it was a height so profound that even the Spear King could only imitate it slightly before coughing up blood. By reaching Spear Unification, Lee Sungmin had drawn a little closer to that realm.

*Thrust.*

He followed the thought, putting the weight of the spear into a single motion, faithful to the basics. That was all.

PWAK!

A large hole blew open in the surging flames, revealing Chen's face beyond it. The expressionless man's eyes widened in shock.

KWADEUDEUK!

The spear pierced through Chen's shoulder joint with incredible force.

"Keuk!"

Lee Sungmin twisted the spear to pull it free, then lunged at Kun, who was attacking from the side.

PPAKAK!

The spear shaft struck Kun, sending his body staggering. Just as Lee Sungmin was about to deliver the finishing blow, Aine attacked from behind. Her flame-wreathed claws stabbed for his back. He felt it without looking, his senses honed to a razor's edge since achieving the pinnacle of martial arts.

It was instantaneous.

Aine's hands flailed at empty air. In the next moment, both her arms were shattered, and a large hole was torn in her chest.

Chen and Kun, their wounds already healed, gritted their teeth and charged again. They were the third and fourth among Jeniela's vampires, and while the pair could easily handle most masters of the Transcendent Realm, Lee Sungmin was no ordinary master. Even on a moonlit night, they were weaker than the Blood Demon. They could not compare. It was impossible for the two of them to pressure him, even by attacking together.

Besides, vampires were not the only ones blessed by the night. Yokai also received its blessing, though not to the same extent. Their senses became sharper, their dispositions more violent. Muscles grew stronger, movements faster, and Demonic Energy denser.

KIIIIING.

His Demonic Energy boiled over. A mysterious phenomena manifested, the energy emanating from him enveloping the entire space. Chen and Kun's pale faces turned even whiter. Aine's ceaselessly burning flames froze for a moment. The phenomena, deliberately unleashed, stopped their perception of time.

Time still moved, but everyone except Lee Sungmin was unable to act. A mysterious phenomena was a yokai magic manifested by a great yokai upon awakening, a power that did not follow common sense.

Lee Sungmin prioritized eliminating Jeniela's high-ranking vampires over killing Aine. It was no easy task to kill vampires of their level on a moonlit night, and killing them recklessly was also a problem. When vampires originating from Jeniela died, their power returned to her. He already knew the solution, though he did not want to do it. Predation was not a very efficient act for him in the first place, as the power he could gain from it had already reached its limit with the Dragon Heart. The power might remain somewhere in his body, but it was not something he could use comfortably right now.

Even so, he would not hesitate. He had to do it when he could. Recklessly eating a heart was not the answer. The priority was to push them to a point where they could no longer regenerate.

Everything happened in an instant. The mysterious phenomena pressed down on the space, and in the same instant that their movements stopped, Lee Sungmin's spear swung, and they all spewed blood and collapsed.

Aine's regeneration was faster than the brothers'. She did not understand what had just happened. By the time she leaped for Lee Sungmin again, he had already planted his spear neatly in both Chen's and Kun's chests. He pulled the weapon free and swung it behind him to check her approach, dodging her piercing claws with a slight twist of his waist before pushing his foot forward.

Shadowless Soul Severance, Two Step Destruction sent Aine flying back, while Three Step Fatality and Four Step Chaos tore her apart along with her flames. He then focused the mysterious phenomena on the shouting Chen and Kun to suppress their movements. Homing Chain Lightning riddled their bodies with dozens of holes. Serpent Soul Devouring shattered Chen's head, and Deathly Flash blew up Kun's.

It was overwhelming. Preskan, who had been screaming for the Heart Thief's death, could only watch the fight in stunned silence. It was too fast for his eyes to follow. Every time the violet electric current crackled, blood splattered, and someone fell. They would get up again, only to fall once more. He watched his daughter, Aine, spew blood over and over.

"My daughter!" Preskan cried out.

[The Sword Immortal is dead.]

The voice that echoed in Lee Sungmin's head froze him in place.

[I told you to run. Why didn't you? Why are you still here?] Gemini grumbled.

Lee Sungmin opened his senses, trying to find him, but even with his sharpened perception, it was impossible to pinpoint Gemini's location.

[Isn't this joke going too far?]

[Don't try to find me. If I decide to hide, no one but the Queen can find me. More importantly, why didn't you run?] Gemini snapped. [Thanks to you, I had to come all this way. I have a position to maintain, so if you try to force anything more, I'll have no choice but to step in and stop you.]

"There must be a good reason why the Queen isn't coming," Lee Sungmin muttered, ensuring he would be heard.

Gemini chuckled at his words. [That's right. The Queen can't come herself. She used her Demonic Eye. I don't know the details, but it seems the Sword Immortal was strong enough to warrant using it.]

The Demonic Eye that creates a full moon.

[When she uses that Demonic Eye, the Queen becomes unable to move. To be precise, she can't leave the domain of the full moon she created.]

Once again, Gemini volunteered information he had not been asked for. He was one of Jeniela's vampires, but unlike the others, he neither completely obeyed nor was controlled by her. Lee Sungmin had no intention of trying to win him over, but he made sure to remember everything Gemini told him. It was clear the vampire had his own ulterior motives regarding Jeniela.

[Back off.]

This warning, however, was real.
