On the journey from Zenavis to Odun, Lee Sungmin never removed his mask except to eat. The Ghost Mask, a gift from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, now felt as familiar to him as a part of his own body. He had also grown used to the constant sensation of his qi center being suppressed, a pressure that restrained both his inner qi and his Demonic Energy.

While wearing the mask, Lee Sungmin relied not on his usual powers but on the strength of the Dragon Heart that had melted into his body. It was a power completely different from magic, an enigma that even he could not truly grasp. Perhaps Syaos, the Blue Magic Tower Master, could provide an answer. Syaos was an individual whose vast knowledge made him an expert not only in big picture magic but also in the long-vanished Dragons.

Odun was large enough to be called a giant city, yet Lee Sungmin's first impression was that of an ancient ruin. The city's outskirts were lined with various crumbling structures that resembled cultural heritage sites, and they had drawn a fair number of tourists. Since Lee Sungmin had not come for sightseeing, he passed the ruins and headed straight for the Blue Magic Tower.

He had not arranged a meeting in advance, worried that scheduling one might entangle him in troublesome affairs. Though he had met and spoken with the man named Syaos before and had heard no ill rumors about him, that did not mean he trusted the Tower Master. Their circumstances and positions were simply too different.

Most people did not view his title, the Phantom Spear, favorably. Years had passed, but Lee Sungmin was still called a demonic practitioner, framed for the deaths of the Iron Armored Divine Spear, Jaegal Taeryeong, and Moyong Seojin. Though he had not shown himself recently, the Murim Alliance's scheme had worked splendidly, branding him with that very label. The Murim Alliance was a massive organization that had formed countless relationships over its long existence, and it held close ties with the Mages Guild. If he had arranged a meeting beforehand, Lee Sungmin could not be certain that Syaos would not inform them.

Of course, the world did not know that the Phantom Spear possessed the Dragon Heart, but regardless of Syaos's relationship with the Murim Alliance, Lee Sungmin could not bring himself to trust him. That was why he was being cautious.

*It might seem a little rude, but.*

Lee Sungmin looked up at the towering Magic Tower. He scanned his surroundings and expanded his senses, detecting a web of various magics. Infiltration from the outside was impossible. Even with Gale Thunder, it seemed it would be difficult to break through the complex magical security without being caught.

He calmly entered the Magic Tower's entrance, approached the first-floor lobby counter, and spoke.

"I'm here to see Syaos."

"Do you have an appointment?" a male mage with glasses immediately asked.

Lee Sungmin nodded in response.

The male mage's eyes widened as he asked again, "I haven't been informed of any appointments today...?"

"That can't be right," Lee Sungmin lied with a shameless expression. "I'm sure I sent word that I'd be visiting today."

"Yes...?"

The mage looked utterly confused. "Are you sure you don't have the wrong date?"

"No. It's definitely today. Don't tell me Syaos is away?"

"No... that's not it. He's upstairs, but..."

"Please lend me a pen and paper."

After confirming Syaos was in the tower, Lee Sungmin made his request. The mage hesitated for a moment before taking out a pen and paper and handing them over. Lee Sungmin quickly wrote on the paper.

Dragon Heart.

He casually folded the note and returned it to the mage.

"Please give this to Syaos."

"Ah... yes. Could you please wait a moment?"

The mage left his post and returned a short while later.

"Please come this way."

Upon seeing the words 'Dragon Heart', Syaos had apparently realized who had come to see him. This was enough. A sudden visit limited whatever preparations Syaos could make, even if he harbored malicious intent. The Dragon Heart was just that valuable, and mages in particular tended to become surprisingly aggressive toward things that piqued their interest and greed.

Syaos's room was on the top floor of the Magic Tower. When Lee Sungmin opened the door, he was met by a fussy-looking old man with a long, curly beard. The old man pushed up the glasses perched on his hooked nose with his fingertips and glared.

"Coming to see me without an appointment," Syaos grumbled.

Lee Sungmin merely shrugged, not bothering to retort. He knew his visit was rude.

"I was just passing by."

"Even so, shouldn't you have given me at least a day's notice?"

"I heard you didn't have any appointments today."

"What does that have to with anything?" Syaos snapped, irritated by Lee Sungmin's shameless attitude. He took off the glasses he had just adjusted and glared. Lee Sungmin accepted the fierce gaze nonchalantly and approached him.

"This is the first time we're meeting in person."

"Last time, we only spoke briefly through Erebrisa," Syaos said. "But what's with that? The mask?" He raised a hand and pointed.

"I'm wearing it for personal reasons."

"It doesn't look like an ordinary mask... Well, it doesn't matter," Syaos muttered and got to his feet. He looked Lee Sungmin up and down. "It's been over a year since you ate the Dragon Heart. Almost two years now. Have you felt any changes?"

It would be better to show him than to tell him. Lee Sungmin reached inward and consciously drew out his Dragon's Pressure. It was a power he had been unable to perceive or control before, but perhaps thanks to wearing the mask for a year, he could now command it.

A vicious presence emanated from him, the very essence of an arrogant race that looked down upon all creation and claimed to be the greatest.

Syaos, who had been watching with a stiff face, nodded slowly.

"I see. So this is Dragon's Pressure..."

"Have you felt it before?"

"Of course not. The Dragons disappeared hundreds of years ago, but many records remain. I see... so this is what it feels like. The records of those who experienced the Pressure used different expressions, but the meaning was the same. The feeling of a mouse before a cat. I think I know what they mean."

Syaos nodded again with a satisfied expression. For mages, the long-vanished Dragons were a fascinating subject of study. Magic and Dragons were so closely intertwined that some even said the origin of magic was the Dragon itself.

"It's a shame I can't compare it to a real Dragon, though. I thought it was impossible for a human to completely handle the immense power of a Dragon Heart, but it seems that's not the case. Or are you special?"

"You could say I'm special."

"What do you mean?"

"My body is very different from that of an ordinary human."

"It would be nice if I could dissect it to see," Syaos said with a regretful expression, licking his lips with a smack.

Syaos seemed to be subtly hoping to dissect him, but Lee Sungmin, of course, would not allow it. He changed the subject instead, briefly telling Syaos about his current condition. He did not mention the Black Heart, simply explaining that the magic power of the Dragon Heart had melted into his body and become something else entirely.

"I can't tell from words alone. Can you draw it out?" Syaos asked.

The request was not difficult. Lee Sungmin raised a fingertip, and the power of the Dragon Heart, neither Demonic Energy nor inner qi, gathered there. It was a transparent white, as it had not passed through the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The power, flicked from his fingertip, floated gently toward Syaos like a bubble.

"......"

Syaos took out a wand and swung it. A complex magic circle formed in the air and enveloped the bubble Lee Sungmin had sent.

"Indeed. This isn't magic power."

"What is it?"

"How do you understand the Dragon Heart?" Syaos asked, posing a different question.

"A massive lump of magic power."

"That's not wrong," Syaos said with a grin, as if he had expected that answer. "Fragments of the Dragon Heart have been discovered several times before. Most were massive lumps of magic power, but what you took was a complete Dragon Heart, wasn't it?"

What a waste, Syaos added with a dry cough.

"The Dragon Heart isn't just a lump of magic power. It's literally a Dragon's heart. An organ. Even if it doesn't look like one."

Indeed. The Dragon Heart Lee Sungmin had possessed was closer to a large jewel than an organ.

"The physical structure of a Dragon hasn't been clearly identified... but I've studied Dragons for a long time. In my hypothesis, the Dragon Heart isn't just a lump of magic power. It's an organ that keeps the Dragon alive. Yes, a heart. Just as a human heart circulates blood, a Dragon's heart circulates both blood and magic power through the body."

Syaos pushed his wand forward and poked the bubble, causing the condensed power to scatter in all directions.

"According to records, a Dragon's magic was incredibly threatening. Even a basic Fire Ball was said to have catastrophic power when used by a Dragon. The difference in the amount of magic power and magical proficiency was vast, but I think... the magic power itself was different. They used a kind of magic power that only Dragons could wield, not ordinary magic power."

"You're saying that's what made the difference in magical power?"

"Exactly. I can't be sure without dissecting you, but from what I can see, you... you didn't just gain a Dragon's magic power by eating the Dragon Heart. Your heart itself has been transformed to be similar to a Dragon Heart. That's why you can use this heterogeneous power."

It was an interesting theory. Neither the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Osla, nor Void Monarch had told him anything like this. Lee Sungmin unconsciously touched his chest. Could his Black Heart have been transformed to resemble a Dragon Heart?

"If possible, I'd like a little of your blood. Would that be alright?"

"......"

"If I have your blood, I can learn a lot without having to dissect you."

"That's not too difficult..." Lee Sungmin muttered as he reached into his clothes. What he pulled out was not a blade, but the Golden Key he had obtained from the Dungeon. He had come to see Syaos not only for answers about his body but also to find out what this key was.

"What's that now?"

"It's something I happened to find in a Dungeon, and it seems to be bound to me."

"Bound...?"

Interest filled Syaos's eyes. He approached with a shining gaze as Lee Sungmin held out the key on his palm for him to see more easily. Having heard it was bound, Syaos did not touch the key. Instead, he raised his wand and pointed it at the object.

After a long incantation, a blue light flashed from the tip of the wand. Syaos's eyes widened.

"This is... ancient big picture script."

"Can you decipher it?"

"Wait... just a moment."

Syaos squinted, standing for a long time with his wand raised as he concentrated his mind. He was trying to decipher the ancient big picture script entwined around the key, a script invisible to Lee Sungmin.

"There's too much... it looks like a magical formula... library...?"

"library?"

"library... This word is library..."

Syaos let out a groan. Even for an expert on ancient script and Dragons, both had long since vanished from the world. Only a fraction of the ancient script remained in records, and even knowing all of it made it impossible to fully decipher a magical formula.

"This... key... seems to be a key for dispelling the magic of some library... library... a library. Does a library protected by ancient big picture magic even exist...?"

Syaos could not fully decipher the ancient language on the key. It was needed to enter some library. That was all he could figure out, and Lee Sungmin could not help but feel disappointed. Although he had just stopped by on his way to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, he had come with some expectations, but he had learned little about the important key.

In the end, Lee Sungmin concluded his meeting with Syaos by giving him a few drops of blood as requested.

"Are you leaving Odun?"

"My business in this city is finished."

Syaos looked a little disappointed. It seemed he had not quite given up on his lingering desire to dissect Lee Sungmin.

[library, a key... I don't understand,] Void Monarch grumbled as they left the Magic Tower.

Lee Sungmin frowned, touching the key inside his clothes. It was clearly an important item, but he had far too little information.

*I'll deal with it later. Haras isn't far from here.*

Lee Sungmin decided to set aside his immediate concerns for now.
