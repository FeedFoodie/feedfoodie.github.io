Yuzkia Mountain was a place perpetually shrouded in fog. Its rugged terrain and incredibly thick mist, combined with the ferocious monsters that teemed within it, ensured that almost no people ever lived there. It was a land filled not only with barbaric mutants like Orcs but also with high-level monsters such as Ogres and Minotaurs.

The Hunting Team Leader had arrived at Yuzkia Mountain a week ago, following the words of her father, the Fist Honored One. As an Elf, neither the dense fog nor the treacherous landscape posed much of a problem for her. Even the monsters that swarmed the mountain were no match for her, for she had reached the realm of the Supreme Peak in martial arts.

For a week, she had remained at the foot of the mountain. Every night, she would leave her shelter to gaze at the numerous peaks, her goal to pinpoint the one that rose closest to the moon.

*Father.*

The Fist Honored One had told her to climb the peak closest to the moon on the night it was full. Tonight was that night.

The Hunting Team Leader began her ascent before the moon even rose. Since the mountain was also a forest, she, an Elf, received its blessing, climbing the high peak tirelessly and without rest. She scaled sheer cliffs and leaped across deep valleys as a perfect full moon climbed to its highest point in the sky. At the summit, she found a large lake formed by collected rainwater.

Revenge.

The word echoed in her mind. Her father… was likely dead. Almost certainly. The fact that she could not confirm his death to the very end had become a source of intense resentment. If she had her way, she would have fled with him or died fighting the human who came to take his life, but her father had told her to learn how to retreat. Dying together would not avenge him, and even if they had fled… they would have eventually been pursued and killed.

*Father.*

She looked up at the full moon, which seemed so close. *I want to avenge him.* With that single-minded purpose, she had come here, following her father's last words, his will, without ever witnessing his end.

Her lips parted.

"…Rebetan."

The name she called was her father's. He was the elder of the Elf village in the Forest of Ancients, and the Fist Honored One, one of the Heaven Beyond Heaven Six Honored Ones.

"Revias, daughter of Rebetan, has come here to seek her father's revenge."

Her voice trembled with passion.

"Revias, daughter of Rebetan, has come here to seek her father's revenge!"

The tremble became a shout. Revias yelled at the full moon, her voice ringing across the peak. She did not stop at one cry but screamed for a long time, glaring at the moon. Her throat grew hoarse and raspy from the strain, yet she continued, shouting until she tasted blood.

After she had screamed for what felt like an eternity, the full moon began to waver. Revias's body flinched, but she did not stop shouting. The wavering moon slowly began to crack.

From within the fracture, a single carriage, pulled by nothing, slid out. It settled slowly onto the surface of the lake. Though no beasts pulled it, its wheels turned, carrying it across the water toward her.

"Rebetan."

The carriage stopped in front of Revias. She panted, staring at it as a calm voice flowed from within.

"It's been a long time since I've heard that name. Rebetan… yes, the Fist Honored One. I, too, have heard that he is dead."

The carriage door opened, revealing an empty interior.

"Revias, daughter of Rebetan. Did your father tell you of this place?"

"…Yes," Revias answered the unseen person.

At her reply, the unknown woman let out a laugh. "I find myself unable to refuse. Had you been Rebetan's son, I would have refused… but how can I turn away a daughter who has come to me to avenge her father?"

"Who… are you?"

"It seems Rebetan told you nothing," the woman said. "Get in the carriage."

Revias hesitated for a moment before climbing inside.

"I am an old acquaintance of Rebetan's," the woman muttered. "We weren't close enough to be called friends, but our relationship wasn't so cold that I would ignore his last wish."

The carriage door closed.

"I am the Moon Queen."

He was utterly depressed.

Preskan sighed heavily in his abode. Nearly half a year had passed since he and Arbes had dragged him to Urmuri. After Lee Sungmin had shattered his spiritual body there, Preskan had awoken in the secret hideout where he had hidden his Life Force Vessel. He didn't know what had happened in Urmuri, nor did he want to know.

He had spent the time since his return recuperating, restoring his spiritual body. The magic power lost when it was destroyed was gone for good, never to return. Thanks to that, Preskan was now in a very weakened state.

*Have I been abandoned?*

No summons came from the Predator. No contact from Arbes. He must have been abandoned. Preskan slumped over, a wave of depression washing over him. He had failed to carry out Arbes's orders and had even lost his magic power. Arbes had probably achieved his goal and therefore had no more use for him. In the first place, Arbes's objective was never Preskan, but Aine, the artificial lifeform he had created. Since Arbes had engraved the obedience seal on her and activated the magic circle, it was only natural that he had no more business with Preskan.

"What a miserable fate," Preskan muttered in a gloomy voice. The lifelong ambition he had dedicated his entire existence to had been stolen by Arbes. One of his Black Hearts had been taken by a damn thief, and he had suffered the humiliation of having his spiritual body destroyed by that same thief.

*What sin did I commit in my past life to repeatedly suffer such a miserable, dog-like fate?*

*No, I can't give up.* Preskan shot to his feet. If no summons came from Arbes, that was actually for the best. *I just have to achieve my lost ambition again. Haven't I already done it once?* In truth, creating a Black Heart had been something close to a coincidence, but he had no intention of giving up. *Again. I can do it again. I can make it again. As a Lich, I have infinite time anyway. Yes, let's think positively.*

Having resolved himself, Preskan began to walk, his robes dragging behind him. His first goal was to create a new Dungeon. If he made this place his Dungeon and an intruder entered like last time, it would be irreversible. This was where he had hidden his Life Force Vessel. An attack here would not just destroy his spiritual body but would mean his complete death.

"Hm?"

Preskan, heading out to begin his work, stopped dead in his tracks. The complex detection and intruder interception magic he had laid here were reacting.

His body trembled.

*Oh, gods. What kind of dog-like sin did I commit for you to give me such an ordeal?*

Preskan genuinely resented the gods as he quickly turned around. He didn't know what kind of bastard had entered, but in his current weakened state, he had no confidence in fighting an intruder. Judging by the speed at which his magic was being destroyed, this was no easy opponent. He absolutely could not fight, not when he wasn't in a state of full fighting spirit. His first thought was to escape. He carefully cradled his Life Force Vessel.

"Where are you going?"

He failed.

Preskan stood blankly, clutching the Life Force Vessel to his chest. Before him stood Aine, dressed in shabby clothes and looking at him with a face full of deep exhaustion.

"Y-You… how are you… here…?"

"You told me, Father."

The location of the Life Force Vessel. Preskan had told only Aine the location of this place.

"N-No. That's not what I'm asking… You were with Arbes…"

"I don't know," Aine muttered, pressing her throbbing temples. She had no memory of the time the obedience seal was engraved on her. "When I came to my senses, I was in a forest. There was nothing around me… I don't know what happened to Arbes."

She looked at him, her expression a mixture of relief and desperation. "I don't know what happened, and you weren't around, Father… so I came here. I was worried you wouldn't be here… I'm glad you are."

"Oh… ohhh…"

Overcome with emotion, Preskan dropped to his knees. He didn't know what had happened in Urmuri or what had become of that fearsome, dog-like Arbes, but look! Aine had come to find him herself! His body trembled with overwhelming emotion.

"…Father," Aine began, looking down at him. "Something's wrong with me. Ever since that day, the day I woke up in the forest… I've been constantly hungry. No matter how much I eat, I'm still hungry."

She placed a hand on her stomach. "I ate so many things on my way here. I ate people, monsters, beasts, insects, I just kept eating, but I'm still hungry. Eating only makes me feel a little less hungry for a moment, then the hunger comes back."

"Yes… yes," Preskan nodded, as if possessed. He shot to his feet. "Then let's go eat. Yes, there are plenty of things for you to eat."

"Come, let's go, my daughter," Preskan exclaimed in a cheerful voice. "Let's go out to eat with your father for the first time in a while."

Despite his words, as a Lich, it was impossible for him to eat food. Aine let out a small laugh as she looked at him.

"Okay, Father."

On her way here, she had thought several times about just turning back, but now that she was here, she didn't feel that way anymore. It was a good thing she came.

Aine walked alongside Preskan with that thought.

After devastating the Namgung Clan in Bellador, Lee Sungmin headed straight for Legro Forest. News that the clan had been attacked by an unknown master, and that its head, elders, and experts had all been utterly defeated, spread not only throughout Bellador but all of Eria. Rumors tended to get exaggerated as they spread, but the crushing defeat of the Namgung Clan was a huge issue in itself, without any need for embellishment.

Who was this master named 'Jung Hyunsoo'? A reclusive master? Was it an act of vengeance? What kind of history was entangled between him and the Namgung Clan? All sorts of gossip circulated, but Lee Sungmin ignored it. He had no need to use that name anymore anyway.

In front of Legro Forest, he took off his human skin mask. He had been wearing it for months, and as he caressed his real face, the sensation felt awkward. He let out a small laugh.

Legro Forest wasn't very large. It was a common forest, the kind one could find anywhere in Eria. To think the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was hiding in a place like this. Lee Sungmin looked down at the woods, confident that a forest of this size could be completely searched in half a day. He didn't know where exactly the Chairman was hiding, but that wasn't a big problem.

*Wiji Hoyeon should be here too.*

With that expectation, Lee Sungmin entered the forest. He didn't bother to hide his presence, instead letting it flare out, hoping the Chairman and Wiji Hoyeon would feel it and come find him.

The forest began to tremble.

"How noisy."

It was just as he'd thought. He hadn't walked far before he heard a grumbling voice.

-CRACKLE!

Black electricity sparked around him. When Lee Sungmin looked toward the sound, he saw the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman rising from within the black currents. He wore the mask of a lion with a shaggy mane.

"If you'd just come in quietly, I would have come out to greet you," the Chairman snapped. "Why are you advertising that you're here?" He grumbled as he approached, completely unshaken by the intense pressure radiating from Lee Sungmin. "They say that weak dogs bark the loudest. You're the perfect example of that right now."

"The Little Heavenly Demon," he continued. "You're just like that wench."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched.

"…What did you say?"

"I said that your habit of barking loudly despite being weak is just like the Little Heavenly Demon," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied, his annoyance unconcealed.
