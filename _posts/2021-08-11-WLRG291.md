As she traveled, Scarlet's thoughts returned to the request the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had made of her.

*But how?*

Her expression hardened as she looked toward Lee Sungmin. Yehwa, who had been driving the carriage, could no longer remain a bystander and now struggled beside him, both of them desperately trying to shatter the barrier. Nearby, the Black Dragon Knight stood as rigid as a statue, his gaze fixed on the distant clash between the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Martial God, and the Moon Queen.

Anyone could see the Chairman was in crisis. Perhaps he was exhausted from fighting without rest, but the result was undeniable. His left arm had been severed completely, and a thin trickle of blood flowed from between his tightly closed lips. Though he had succeeded in killing the Moon Queen, the victory had cost him an arm and inflicted severe internal injuries. The Martial God, in stark contrast, remained unharmed.

And so it continued.

"Aaaaaargh!"

Lee Sungmin relentlessly swung his spear, thrusting and striking against the invisible wall. He poured out his inner arts and Demonic Energy without distinction, a chaotic torrent of power. He knew the risk of going berserk, but he could not stop himself. A terrible premonition was solidifying into reality before his very eyes.

He had promised it wouldn't be today, yet it felt as if today was the day.

*No, it is definitely today. If I do nothing now, it will certainly become today.*

Even the Void Monarch could offer no advice in this situation, for the Spirit's barrier was far too strong. Beside him, Yehwa screamed as she added her own strength to the futile effort.

Watching the two of them, Scarlet bit her lower lip.

She wanted to change it, too. She wanted to avert the future where the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman died. While she had no particularly close relationship with him, the Chairman had always been friendly to her, and she held at least that much goodwill for the man, but it wasn't just that. If the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman were to die, if her Master were to die…

What would happen to Lee Sungmin?

"You bastard…!" the Martial God snarled, gritting his teeth as he kicked off the ground.

He leaped into the air and unleashed a torrent of palm techniques, thrusting both hands toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Though he had sustained internal injuries during their fight, the Martial God still had plenty of strength left.

The Chairman knew his opponent well. He knew his own condition was poor. He knew he would die here.

Yes, he understood. He had thought it wouldn't be today, but as he watched the charging Martial God and the countless palm forces erupting from his hands, a faint smile touched his lips.

*How embarrassing.*

Hadn't he boasted to his disciple before coming here? He had claimed he would deny the future, that today would not be the day.

*Well, I can only hope he'll understand.*

Still, one thing he had said was not wrong. The Martial God alone was not enough. Even with the Moon Queen, it had been insufficient. The only annoyance was that she had come cloaked in the Spirit's protection. If the Spirit had not intervened and stripped him of his transcendence, this fight would have been his overwhelming victory.

He had no intention of dying easily. He had not thought that today, right now, this place would become his grave, but choosing not to avoid what could have been avoided was a decision he himself had made. This was the very grave he had chosen, and it would be he, not the Martial God, who would decide how he would die.

With a silent command, all of his remaining inner arts ignited, spreading throughout his entire body. The speed and power were sorely lacking compared to before, a fact that displeased him greatly. This, however, was the best he could muster. In that case, he had no choice but to use it.

With a sound so small it was almost imperceptible, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman moved. He could not use Gale Thunder, nor did he even bother cloaking himself in a Protective Qi Aura. Instead, he leaped bare-bodied into the seamless wall of palm forces.

To the Martial God, this action was no different from suicide. Had he finally given up after being cornered? No, that couldn't be right. The Martial God was certain of it. There was no trace of despair in the eyes of the man facing him.

*It's a waste of inner arts to form a Protective Qi Aura.*

*Is my body sturdy enough? It'll be soft before the Martial God's attacks. A mere graze would be a fatal wound.*

*Good.* A smile filled the Chairman's face as he ran through the air. Had he ever faced such a desperate moment in his life?

He dodged, and dodged again, weaving and twisting. His body was not as fast as it once was, but his senses were sharp and his eyes were bright. He moved precisely through the gaps between the palm forces, his phenomenal movements astonishing the Martial God. It was surprising that he still had that much energy left. The Martial God cooled the reason that had been inflamed with rage over the Moon Queen's death.

A direct clash was out of the question. The Chairman understood this clearly. If he scraped together his meager inner arts for a collision, he would be the only one to break. The Martial God's hands blurred, creating afterimages that became hundreds of fist techniques. Before, he could have shattered them head-on, but not this time. He had to dodge, to create distance, but then, the Martial God reached out and seized the very axis of space.

The Chairman's body, which had been pulling back, was suddenly dragged toward the Martial God. The insights of the Transcendent Realm consumed a great deal of inner arts.

*However. I can do it.*

His inner arts were drying up. His internal injuries made it difficult to even move his body, yet the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman did not doubt himself for a second. He knew for certain. His body was being pulled forward, straight into the fierce winds of the hundreds of fist techniques. Even amidst the blade-like gales that could pulverize his body with a single touch and the colossal enhanced qi that seemed to bombard the entire world, he was confident.

*Let's drop the word 'perhaps' now. There's no need for it anymore. No, would the Sword Immortal be disappointed then? I haven't fought the Sword Immortal yet.*

*He'd probably scold me for that much.*

*He probably doesn't even know in the first place.*

"This one is," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman opened his mouth as the axis of space pulled him forward. Facing the hundreds of fist techniques rushing to pulverize him, he muttered in a voice full of relief, "the evil faction's strongest."

Toward the storm of fists, he extended his hand.

"And the world's number one."

He clenched his outstretched hand into a fist.

"And history's strongest."

They vanished.

The hundreds of fist techniques simply evaporated into nothing. The Martial God could not comprehend what had just happened. He froze, his own fist still outstretched, and stared at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman with wide, disbelieving eyes.

Hahaha!

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed loudly. Admitting it to himself like this felt liberating, pleasant even. In this moment, he felt he had no regrets. The Wudang Sword Immortal? The Tai Chi Sword Formation? Shaolin's One Hundred and Eight Arhat Formation? All of it was meaningless. He knew, even without having fought them. *I am stronger than them, and I am truly the world's number one and history's number one.*

His inner arts were depleted. He didn't even understand what that display of fighting spirit had been. He felt there was no need to.

The Martial God roared at the serene-faced Chairman and charged, determined to kill him. As his enemy rushed at him, fully cloaked in a Protective Qi Aura, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman smiled faintly.

KAAANG.

The Martial God's fist met the Chairman's palm.

Something impossible happened. The hand of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a man who could barely use his inner arts and was suffering from severe internal injuries, did not break. The Martial God's fist, thrown with all his might, could not break it.

His eyes widened as he looked at his fist, unable to advance. *What is this nonsense? Why… why can't I move forward? I definitely made contact. Why can't I break through that hand, from which I can't even feel any inner arts?*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was looking at something other than the Martial God. He saw a single, winding path with an unknown destination. He looked behind him but saw nothing. He looked down at his own body. His left arm had been severed, but to his eyes now, it looked perfectly fine.

Haha.

He let out a laugh as he looked at his restored body and the path stretching out before him. He could see many beings there. Whether they were people or something else, he couldn't tell. Some were gathered at a bend, sitting down to rest, while others moved forward, albeit slowly. Among them, not one was trying to turn back. They must have known it was too late to return, and having come this far, they probably didn't want to go back anymore.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman raised his head and saw a man standing at the very end of the single path. He was a man with broad shoulders and a solid build. The man, who had been standing with his back turned, seemed to sense the Chairman's gaze and turned his head to look at him. The Chairman trembled at the ferocious and absolute power in the man's eyes.

"Ah, I see," he nodded. The path to completely transcending the mortal realm began here. Once you started walking, it was impossible to turn back. He could start walking right now.

*Just a moment.*

*Just a very brief moment will do.*

The single path vanished from before his eyes. Time, which seemed to have stopped for a moment, resumed. The Martial God, who had been frozen in his charging posture, lunged forward again.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman gave a small smile and extended his hand.

GRAB.

He caught the Martial God's left hand.

"I'll go on ahead," the Chairman muttered. "I don't know if you'll be able to come. Huhu… but I know one thing for sure. If this one kills you here, you won't be able to come."

"What…?!"

"I'll take just one thing. Just one," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered and moved his hand.

CRUNCH!

The Martial God's left arm was torn off with shocking ease. His mouth fell wide open in a silent scream.

"Since this one made it disappear, a price like this is rather cheap," the Chairman said calmly. "The reason I stopped at one arm is because this one respects you quite a bit, and, if you must be killed, I've decided I'd like to leave it to this one's disciple."

"Aaaaaaaargh!"

The Martial God finally screamed from the horrific pain in his arm.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled and threw away the severed limb. Then, with a relieved expression, he turned his head. He saw Jeniela and Ju Won, standing at the far end of the snowfield. Jeniela's startled eyes met his.

"The future has been denied."

The voice, which should have been blocked by the barrier, was transmitted clearly to her. Watching Jeniela's expression change, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed loudly. She had said that in the future she saw, he would die. She had been certain of it, that he would cough up blood, collapse, and die.

She was wrong.

"Don't be too sad. Don't be angry, and don't act like a helpless child."

That voice was directed at Lee Sungmin.

"Didn't this one tell you? The Martial God isn't enough. The Moon Queen wasn't enough either. Even with the Spirit's intervention, it wasn't enough. This one has denied the future of death. That's right. This one will not die."

The single path appeared again before him.

"I'm just going to a faraway place. Perhaps you too will come here someday. When that time comes, don't hesitate, and don't leave any regrets behind."

"Master!" Lee Sungmin shouted, the Chairman's voice echoing clearly in his head. The ominous feeling was now a crushing reality. He placed his hands on the unbreakable wall and cried out, "Wudang… Didn't we promise to go to Wudang…"

"You should go someday," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed heartily. "This one no longer needs to go."

The Martial God, blood gushing from his torn left arm, charged at the Chairman with bloodshot eyes, but his attack did not reach him.

"Crimson Archmage," he said to Scarlet. "You remember the favor I asked, don't you? Now is the time."

Then he looked at Yehwa.

"I shouldn't have brought you here. I've made you see a sight you really didn't want to see. Don't think of revenge. There's no need for it. My last words… huhu! If I were to leave a last will, it would be this. Be happy with the other children. The world is quite fun until you get tired of it."

"Coalition Leader…" Yehwa collapsed to the ground.

Lastly, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman looked at the Black Dragon Knight.

"I release you from your oath. Forcing you to suppress your own will is an unpleasant thing."

The Black Dragon Knight did not answer.

"This is it."

He thought about it, but there was nothing more to say. The Chairman glanced at the Martial God one last time and, just before he left, grinned at him.

"This one was stronger than you."

"Aaaaaaaargh!" the Martial God roared.

Even that roar sounded distant.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stared at the single path before him. He had no regrets. He was history's strongest and the world's number one. He had seen the limits of being human, but it was still not enough. Now that he had seen a path that led further, he intended to walk it.

"Evil faction's strongest, world's number one, history's strongest," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman declared, as if for all to hear. To all those walking the path. To the man standing at its end.

"This one is the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon."

He began to walk the path.

The light vanished from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's eyes. His soul had departed for another place, but his physical body remained. The limp body plummeted to the ground.

"Wha-what is this," the Martial God stammered, watching the Chairman suddenly collapse and fall. "Why…?"

A series of incomprehensible events was taking place.

Watching the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's corpse fall, Lee Sungmin collapsed to his knees.
