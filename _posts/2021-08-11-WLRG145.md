Thoughts of Legro Forest and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would have to wait. For now, the South was his priority.

The journey south would be long, but it was necessary. He needed to find the indigenous tribes to learn how to handle Demonic Energy, and while there, he would claim the Void Monarch's treasure. At the same time, he had to find a shaman, someone who might hold the answer to Wiji Hoyeon's curse. It was a long road ahead, but he told himself he didn't mind.

He left the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's branch and went straight to the train station. Thanks to the Alliance's intervention in yesterday's commotion, the station guards merely glared at him instead of rushing to apprehend him.

The station agent handed over the ticket with a stern warning. "Don't cause any trouble."

Aboard the train, the Crazy Heavenly Demon wore a complicated expression, torn between the pleasure of riding a train again and the sorrow of leaving the city.

"We can just come back next time," Rubia said, settling in beside him.

Lee Sungmin helped Wiji Hoyeon into the seat next to his own. The train lurched into motion, the rattling of the seats smoothing out as it gained speed. Wiji Hoyeon stared out the window at the passing scenery, her silence absolute. She offered no complaint about their sudden departure, nor did she seem anxious.

Rubia blinked, her gaze shifting between Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon. Their meeting had been so sudden, and they had barely spoken. Rubia knew little about the woman, only that she was the famous Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon. After all, she had nearly been killed by Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger in the Dungeon and had even shared a brief, tense encounter with the real one at its end.

"Impressive," the Crazy Heavenly Demon began. He crossed his arms in a show of dignity, trying to look at Wiji Hoyeon while still glancing out the window. "Twenty-three... you said. To reach the Transcendent Realm at that age. It was unprecedented in the history of the murim this one lived in, and it must be unprecedented in this world as well."

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae," Wiji Hoyeon said, her voice quiet. Her gaze shifted from the window to meet his. "Ten years ago. I heard stories about you, ever since I left Zenavis."

"And what do you think, now that you've seen me in person?"

She spoke to him informally without a second thought, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon didn't seem to mind.

"Subduing you would be a hassle," Wiji Hoyeon continued, her eyes fixed on his face. "But I don't think it would be difficult."

"Hahaha!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon roared with laughter at her reply. "I suppose so. This one has never even glimpsed the Transcendent Realm. I've clung to it for decades but never reached it."

"That's a common story," Wiji Hoyeon muttered.

Even then, the Crazy Heavenly Demon just laughed, the sound holding no trace of self-pity. "Right now, your body's in bad shape, so it'd be impossible, but if you ever recover, I'd love to have a fight with you."

"Do as you please," she replied, leaning her head against the window.

The curse was getting stronger. Before Ruvus, the pain and inability to use her martial arts hadn't stopped her from moving. Now, that was no longer the case. Her inner qi wouldn't stir, no matter how she willed them, and even simple movements had become a struggle. It wasn't from her duel with the Iron Armored Divine Spear. Her injuries, both external and internal, were already healed. Wiji Hoyeon could feel it with chilling certainty. The curse eating away at her body was growing.

*It's getting harder to stay conscious...*

Even now, an irresistible drowsiness pulled at her. Sleep was coming for her. Wiji Hoyeon bit her lower lip, fighting it.

PAT.

Lee Sungmin's hand gently covered the back of hers. She turned to look at him, her eyelids fluttering.

"You can sleep."

"...Okay."

At his words, Wiji Hoyeon gave a slight nod. She didn't want to sleep, but it was strange. The moment she heard him, her heart felt at ease.

*Ah.*

*I'm feeling relieved.* It was a belated realization. She stopped fighting the Sleep Demon and closed her eyes. Before long, her breathing evened out as she fell fast asleep.

"How pitiful," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered. "To think a martial artist who has reached the Transcendent Realm could be so helpless. The Little Heavenly Demon right now... she couldn't even handle a peak master... no, even a First-Rate or Second-Rate martial artist."

"Perhaps." Wiji Hoyeon's sleeping head came to rest on Lee Sungmin's shoulder. Listening to her steady breathing, he clenched his fists.

"...I need your help, Elder."

"Why do you ask? This one already decided to go to the South with you. Just because our party has grown doesn't mean we're not going, does it?"

"We might have to fight the Murim Alliance."

"And what of it? You seem to have forgotten, but this one's title is the Crazy Heavenly Demon. I've been fighting those Murim Alliance bastards for decades."

"And Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"Even better," the Crazy Heavenly Demon laughed heartily. "I hope I get to meet the Sword Saint again. I want to meet him and see for myself how far this one has come in ten years. I want to see just how far ahead he still is."

Lee Sungmin, however, wanted no such thing. He had no desire to meet an emissary from Heaven Beyond Heaven. He wasn't confident. Despite reaching a considerably high level within the Supreme Peak, he knew he couldn't protect Wiji Hoyeon in a fight against one of the Six Honored Ones, all of whom were in the Transcendent Realm.

The train pulled into South Gate Station. Without waking Wiji Hoyeon, Lee Sungmin carefully lifted her onto his back. The Crazy Heavenly Demon stood behind him as Rubia transformed into a sphere of light and settled into his embrace.

As they exited the station, they found a familiar figure waiting. The Iron Armored Divine Spear, Yoo Hojeong. It was the very spot where Lee Sungmin had subdued him two days ago. Yoo Hojeong had clearly not left, remaining at the South Gate ever since. Lee Sungmin met the seated man's gaze.

"Leaving?" Yoo Hojeong asked.

"Are you going to stop me?"

"I can't stop you," Yoo Hojeong replied. "You're not alone, and you have the Crazy Heavenly Demon with you."

"Then why are you here?"

"Because there's something I want to ask you."

He wore no armor, nor did he hold his lance. Instead, the massive weapon was stuck in the ground right beside him. Yoo Hojeong rose slowly to his feet. "Do you intend to continue protecting the Little Heavenly Demon?"

"If I can."

"I believe I told you before. That is an act of turning against the Murim Alliance."

"I know."

"It's not too late. Have you no intention of changing your mind?"

"I don't."

"I see." Yoo Hojeong nodded. "I know that my actions, trying to kill the weakened Little Heavenly Demon, were not just, but I acted despite knowing that. Do you know why?"

"Because Wiji Hoyeon is a demonic practitioner."

"Even if she hasn't committed any evil deeds for now, there's no guarantee she won't in the future. What's more, that young monster is growing stronger at an unprecedented rate. In just a few years, no one will be able to stop her."

"So that's why you tried to kill her."

"I know it's not just. This is a narrow-minded justice. It might not be right, but it could also be right."

Yoo Hojeong reached out, his hand closing around the lance beside him. "I cannot stop you," he said. "However, I must stop you. Because to me, this is right. Even if it is narrow-minded, this is my justice."

The lance was engulfed in light, a brilliance that flowed from it to envelop Yoo Hojeong's body. When the light faded, he stood clad in the armor that had earned him his title. He slowly lowered the raised faceplate of his helmet.

"Two days ago, I did not try to kill you. I wasn't sure whether it was right or wrong, but now... I am certain. Killing you here is the right thing to do."

"Why are you going this far?" Lee Sungmin let out a sigh. "Regardless of whether you can kill me or not, I'm not alone, and Wiji Hoyeon hasn't committed any evil deeds yet..."

"Did I not tell you? That it is a narrow-minded justice."

THUD.

Yoo Hojeong took a step forward. "From your perspective, I, who am trying to kill the Little Heavenly Demon, may not be right, but to me, it is the right thing to do. Me, attacking you while you are with the Crazy Heavenly Demon. That too is the right thing for me to do."

It was narrow-minded, yet resolute. He spoke of the unshakable conviction he held deep in his heart. This was Yoo Hojeong the martial artist, separate from the will of the Murim Alliance. The iron-clad warrior took another heavy step. He knew it was impossible, that he couldn't kill them by charging in like this. On the contrary, he would be the one to die. Nevertheless, Yoo Hojeong did not fear death. He acted because he believed it was right, and if he couldn't handle the consequences, he would simply die. He might blame his own weakness, but he would not fear his end.

"......"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a small groan and took a step back with a sigh. "A disagreeable type. This one doesn't want to get involved..."

"I understand."

Lee Sungmin didn't want to do it either. He had never enjoyed killing. He wouldn't kill an opponent unless they were trying to kill him, not in the Sleeping Forest, nor in the Dungeon. He had even spared the annoying Jang Deuksu. The instability from his two thousand one hundred years of training had long since settled, shaping him into the man he was now.

The Iron Armored Divine Spear was someone he did not want to kill. He didn't want to sympathize with the man's beliefs, nor did he want to deny them. He simply wanted to pass by like a flowing stream, uninvolved, but that was not possible. Yoo Hojeong stood before him like an unbreachable wall. Lee Sungmin carefully handed Wiji Hoyeon to the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

[Running away instead of fighting is one option.]

"Are you going to run?" the Void Monarch muttered, and Yoo Hojeong asked the same question aloud. "If you run, I might not be able to chase you right away, but I will pursue you, again and again. How long will you make running your only answer?"

Black and white.

"If you're serious about protecting the Little Heavenly Demon, then don't run. You must understand right and wrong, conviction. Not letting her die. If protecting her is your conviction, your righteousness, then don't run! Kill me here and go!"

Yoo Hojeong roared, accelerating toward Lee Sungmin in a blur. Lee Sungmin didn't hesitate, drawing his spear to meet the charge.

"......"

The close-range collision forced him back half a step. The impact was even heavier than the lance charge from two days ago, the one enhanced with buff magic. It was proof that Yoo Hojeong hadn't been using his full power then.

Was the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, a villain? He didn't know. In his past life, aside from the massacre in the Dungeon, she hadn't committed any other distinct evil acts, and he knew that massacre was the work of her Doppelganger, not Wiji Hoyeon herself. He had died at twenty-eight in that life, four years from now. In those four years, she wouldn't commit any major evil.

However, after that? Five years, six years, ten years later? Was there any guarantee she wouldn't? For something that might never happen, Yoo Hojeong was risking his life to kill her now.

He was being forced to choose, and it couldn't be a choice made in the gray. To protect Wiji Hoyeon, he had to step from gray into black. He had to become a demonic practitioner, kill Yoo Hojeong, and make an enemy of the Murim Alliance. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth, the straight spear in his hand trembling.

"......"

His extended spear blurred, creating dozens of variations as it descended upon Yoo Hojeong. The man withstood the onslaught, relying on the defense of his sturdy iron armor, his Protective Qi Aura, and defensive magic. Against the flurry of thrusts, a single, heavy counter-thrust shot out.

"......"

Spear and lance collided and recoiled. Yoo Hojeong did not retreat. He twisted his body, gripping the shaft with both hands. The lance became a giant blunt weapon, swinging down on Lee Sungmin. To counter, Lee Sungmin spun his own spear half a turn, raising the shaft to block. A dull roar echoed as his upper body staggered. He dragged his foot to the side, changing his stance, and shot his spear forward again.

*Let's stop this.*

Lee Sungmin's eyes grew cold. The recent exchange of blows had made it clear. Yoo Hojeong was genuinely trying to kill him, acting on the conviction that this was his justice, however narrow-minded.

However, Lee Sungmin had not yet chosen.

Orthodox or unorthodox. He didn't want to make such a choice. He knew, however, that he would continue to face this. Men who held their own sense of justice as an unshakeable conviction would block his path. The Murim Alliance had already unleashed its hounds.

Lee Sungmin made his decision.

If those who believed themselves to be justice condemned him as a demonic practitioner, then he would accept it.

The spear in his hand turned a deep purple. Wielding his surging, enhanced qi, Lee Sungmin leaped forward. Watching him, a single thought passed through Yoo Hojeong's mind.

*"I can't stop him."*
