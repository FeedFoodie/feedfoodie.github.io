His conversation with Laplace, or rather, with Homerusos, had revealed a great deal. The Dragons were not lying about their purpose in creating Erebrisa. They had no reason to deceive him. Lee Sungmin understood their plight all too well, for he carried the memories of a past life and knew this world was trapped in a cycle of destruction and rebirth.

To escape the paradox of time, the Dragons had shed their physical forms, leaving only their spirits, and they were trapped in that state as long as the world repeated. It was a terrible existence. Having watched the world cycle three times already, they must have been reaching their limits. Even for beings who lived for an infinite amount of time, observing the same world repeat with only slight differences was not something one could endure while maintaining their sanity.

Homerusos's words echoed in his mind. *Jeniela's strength is abnormal.* The Jeniela of this era was far too strong compared to her previous incarnations. There was only one explanation.

Lee Sungmin retrieved the soul bead from his pocket. Inside, Jeniela's soul churned with a tempest of emotions. He scraped a fingernail across its surface, pushing a sliver of magic into the bead to stimulate the soul within.

[Aaaaaaaack!]

A shriek of unimaginable agony, a pain far worse than the tearing of flesh, erupted from the bead. Lee Sungmin had no need for questions. He could make her talk, certainly, but that was inefficient. Instead, his magic coiled around Jeniela's soul, seizing control and stilling its convulsions.

Now possessing it completely, he rummaged through the memories etched within, searching for any trace of contact with the Demonic Spirit or the Spirit.

*Nothing.*

The search was over almost as soon as it began. Neither entity had ever contacted her directly, and yet her power had grown at an explosive rate, far beyond what was normal for even a vampire. A smirk touched Lee Sungmin's lips. Indeed. 'Someone' had been intervening in her growth from the very moment she became a vampire. Just as he had grown through the Grace of Fate, so had Jeniela. The woman with the Eye of Futuresight was, in the end, nothing more than a puppet for either the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit.

He started to summon the Fairy Horse, then paused. The creature had been useful, but the man he was now had no need for it. Spatial Movement was a magic forbidden to humans. The best a mage could hope for was Blink, a short-range leap.

However, Dragons were different. Their magic, Dragonic, was superior to any other system. Lee Sungmin focused his mind, pulling the knowledge of Spatial Movement from Keiserod's memories. Unlike the Fairy Horse's simple method of thinking of a destination, Dragonic required precise spatial coordinates. It was more cumbersome but infinitely safer. With the proper coordinates, there was no risk of being lost in a dimensional gap.

He recalled the coordinates for the Hyujael Mountains, a memory from hundreds of years ago. Just to be safe, he picked up a roadside stone and cast the spell on it. The stone vanished and reappeared safely in the mountains.

*I should have become a mage.* Who knew magic could be this convenient? If I had known, should I have entered Scarlet's tutelage to learn magic instead of swinging a spear on Mount Mushii?

*What are you talking about, you stupid bastard? Magic with that head of yours? You only got this far because it was physical work.*

The Void Monarch would have sneered at him just like that. The voice was still vivid in his memory. Lee Sungmin pressed his throbbing temples, let out a deep sigh, and chanted the Dragonic spell.

A light, floating sensation washed over him, and then his feet touched solid ground. The feeling of using Spatial Movement in this body was almost nostalgic, a phantom sensation from Keiserod's memories.

He drew a sharp breath and looked up at the dizzying, rugged peaks of the Hyujael Mountains. This was a place where even the most ferocious and mindless Yokai of the South made their home, a wild land for those who could not coexist in the cultured city of Urmuri. Somewhere on this mountain was the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, the place where one could meet the Demonic Spirit itself.

Though he did not know its precise location, that was hardly a problem. He glanced down at the bracelet on his wrist, the one Scarlet had helped make when Yana first left for this place. It lacked a tracking function, but that was something he could add himself. With the memories of a Dragon, he was now a greater mage than Scarlet. He focused, and a moment later, he could sense Yana's presence high above.

Without hesitation, Lee Sungmin unleashed the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. In the past, he had been reluctant to approach the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, unsure of the causality such a meeting would create. Not anymore. His conversation with Homerusos, combined with his own open insight into all that had happened, gave him certainty. It might be different for others, but for him, a man who had escaped fate, these beings were not so formidable.

A thick fog suddenly rolled in, blocking his path. He slowed, standing in its midst as the sense of Yana's location vanished. He waved a hand, creating a fierce gust of wind, but the fog remained untouched. He then lifted his right foot.

KUUUUNG!

He stomped down, and the very space around him shook violently, the ground trembling as if a mountain peak were about to collapse. The fog churned in response. He lifted his foot again.

"Stop."

The voice came from beyond the mists just as he was about to stomp again. Lee Sungmin calmly lowered his foot.

"Enough with the pointless illusions," he called out. "Or are you trying to test me?"

The being beyond the fog did not answer. A smirk played on his lips as he gathered his power, his aura and Pressure bearing down on the entire space until the fog itself trembled. He lifted his foot again, a clear threat.

The fog vanished in an instant. He found himself standing before a lake, unsurprised, his eyes fixed on the figure in its center.

Yana.

However, it was not the Yana he knew. He began to walk slowly across the surface of the water as she stood motionless.

"Amazing... truly amazing," Yana murmured in a voice he recognized, though it was not her speaking. Someone else, someone wearing her body, spoke through her lips. "It's an amazing and interesting thing. This world has repeated itself many times, but there has never been a case like yours. The heart made by the Lich was a mass of possibilities, but..."

Lee Sungmin stopped walking. He did not know who possessed Yana's body, whether it was the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit. Thinking simply, it was likely the Demonic Spirit. This place was called the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, and Yana herself had claimed to receive power from it.

"Are you the Demonic Spirit?" Lee Sungmin asked, his eyes fixed on her face. "Or are you the Spirit?"

At his question, the corner of Yana's mouth twitched. The delayed response made Lee Sungmin frown.

"Why the hesitation now?" he asked, plopping down onto the surface of the water. A small ripple spread out, but his body did not get wet. "If you called me out so openly, shouldn't you tell me why?"

"You were too cautious," Yana murmured. "Those foolish Dragons spoke out of turn and made you suspicious. It would have been so nice if you had come to this mountain right after you opened your eyes."

"I had no choice but to be cautious. I was too weak back then, I didn't know anything, and my head wasn't working very well."

"Does that mean you don't have to be cautious now?"

"You administrators can't directly intervene with me, since I'm outside the fate of this world. I'm already quite certain of that. If you could have intervened, you would have done so long ago. You wouldn't have gone through the trouble of messing with Gries and letting Jeniela run wild."

Lee Sungmin chuckled. "And don't blame me for being so stupid. My stupidity might be innate, but weren't you the one who chose an idiot like me? Or should I say, 'you all'?"

"This outcome isn't so bad," Yana replied with a shrug, ignoring his taunt. "Thanks to your insight opening, you understand things quickly now."

"So don't be frustrating. First, how about you introduce yourself. Are you the Spirit? Or the Demonic Spirit?"

"It's a rather meaningless name, but... I am the Demonic Spirit," Yana answered with a bitter smile.

Lee Sungmin laughed softly and nodded. "I did consider that you might be the Spirit using the Demonic Spirit's name. When I was at Shaolin, Great Monk Bulyeong guided me to the North, saying it was the will of the Spirit, but it was actually you. A name is just a convenience to you, isn't it? In the first place, mere humans can't even properly sense your existence."

"My counterpart was a Buddhist monk from Shaolin. Isn't it easier to order someone around by saying you're a Spirit rather than a Demonic Spirit?"

True. The names Spirit and Demonic Spirit evoked completely different feelings.

"A pointless trick."

"At least call it wisdom, and don't be so hostile. I have still done my best to help you in my own way."

"I know that," Lee Sungmin said. He did not deny it. The Destiny Force granted by the Demonic Spirit was the reason he had survived when he was still bound by fate. Even when the Slaughter Predator nearly annihilated him in the Mental World, he had only destroyed it because he had obtained the mistletoe, albeit at Wiji Hoyeon's request.

"Giving you the mistletoe was something I greatly regretted at the time," the Demonic Spirit said, shaking its head. "The mistletoe was the only one of its kind in this world, a tool that could do many things. I secretly planted it here before my conflict with the Spirit began, intending to use it at the right moment, but..."

"It was used to destroy the Slaughter Predator, so what's the problem? Don't be so stingy and call it a waste."

Yana was at a loss for words at his retort.

"And in the end," Lee Sungmin continued, "I survived by using it and became the biggest variable by escaping fate. The current me can do much more than the mistletoe."

"But you'll be difficult to use as a tool," the Demonic Spirit muttered, then plopped down on the water's surface just as he had. "And I've done many things besides giving you the mistletoe. After you became a big variable, I did my best to keep you from breaking. My efforts are part of the reason why those around you haven't disappeared."

"I know that too. I'm not grateful, though. Aren't you doing all this just to use me?"

"To ask so directly."

"Because it's obvious," Lee Sungmin cackled as the Demonic Spirit muttered in disbelief. "However, it's true that the Spirit has done more than you, but I still don't know its exact purpose. Why did it create Heaven Beyond Heaven? Why did it create Gries to have the brothers Cain and Abel prepare for The End? Why did it give power to Jeniela?"

"Because the Spirit is the one who seized the initiative," the Demonic Spirit answered in a weak voice. "The Spirit suspected me from the start and took most of the authority in this world. Thanks to that, I've become a body that cannot leave the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, but the Spirit is different. That bastard has a proper physical body and is moving around directly."

"The Spirit Medium, or the Moon Queen?"

His mind was clear. He understood the Demonic Spirit's words immediately and knew what he had to ask. It was a world away from when his insight had been closed.

"...The Spirit Medium," the Demonic Spirit answered the swift question, its voice bewildered.
