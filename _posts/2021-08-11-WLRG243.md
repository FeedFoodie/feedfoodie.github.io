So many had died.

Kim Jonghyun's gaze fell upon the Grimoire. He had scattered them from the very beginning, and thanks to the grand magic that consumed three thousand souls, nearly half of the subjugation team was already gone.

A faint smile touched his lips. *This should be enough.*

There was a reason for casting such a spell, a purpose behind creating this very atmosphere. The fear had already spread sufficiently, enough to pry open the 'gate' to some extent.

*Shall I give it a try now?*

Kim Jonghyun extended his hand, and the Grimoire drifted forward until it was within his reach. He beckoned with a single finger, and the book flew open, its pages turning with a slow, deliberate rustle. As he began to chant, his lips moved in a silent rhythm.

To open the 'gate', a vast quantity of fear had to be gathered. He had started by slaughtering seven villages, harvesting the hearts of the people to begin his collection, but it had not been enough. The fear and souls of those with some qualification were far more valuable than the terror of the powerless and destitute. That was precisely why Kim Jonghyun had made this forest his base and refused to move. He was waiting for the powerful and the qualified to come and try to subjugate him.

The arrival of the Church's Holy Knights and Priests was a boon. They had come to execute him for slaughtering commoners and employing wicked magic, but to a Black Mage, the fear and screams of those with such steadfast faith were among the most valuable offerings imaginable.

It did not matter how strong their faith was, nor how solid their will and conviction. When their senses were blocked and they were crushed by the fangs of a monster leaping from the darkness, they had no choice but to succumb to fear. That was the nature of humanity, after all. No matter how many defenses they wrapped around their body and mind, what lay within was ultimately a fragile human spirit, and primal fear was one of the simplest ways to break it.

Yokai were not the only ones who savored fear. The beings beyond the gate that Kim Jonghyun intended to summon also reveled in it. To call forth the Demon Races that corrupt humans, steal their souls, and toy with them, high-quality fear was essential.

As Kim Jonghyun's chant continued, the Grimoire erupted in black light. The very space around him trembled with the incantation, and the hearts piled in the pit began to shudder violently. Long since extracted, they started to beat again, and a dark red blood began to fill the pit. The throbbing beats synchronized into one, and the magic power of the vanquished Demon Lord, Caladra, drained from Kim Jonghyun's body.

The magic that left him coalesced before his eyes, forming a large, plain black gate. As Kim Jonghyun reached out, the gate slowly swung open.

Five monstrous figures emerged from the yawning portal, beings that resembled humans, yet were not. What Kim Jonghyun had summoned were the subordinates of the defeated Demon Lord, Caladra. Normally, when Caladra perished, most of the Demon Race bound to him would have died as well, but these were exceptional specimens who had endured and survived their master's demise. Even so, they had lost their power and reason in the process, making them bland for their kind, but they would be sufficient.

"Kneel," Kim Jonghyun commanded.

The mindless monsters slowly lowered themselves to the ground before him. He was not here as a mere summoner. To these Demon Races who had lost their intellect, Kim Jonghyun, who now wielded Caladra's magic, was the very incarnation of Caladra himself. He had opened a dimensional gate using the Grimoire's magic and thousands of souls, and this was the best he could do. Summoning a higher-class Demon Race would be difficult to control, but since these lacked reason, obtaining their obedience was simple.

"Make it very painful," Kim Jonghyun whispered. "Make them weep with fear. Then kill them."

The mindless Demon Races nodded their heads and faithfully turned to follow his command. Without watching them depart, Kim Jonghyun looked back at the gate, which had not yet closed.

*The gate isn't fully open. It must mean it's still not enough for it to open completely. It doesn't matter. The fact that the gate was summoned and opened even a little is proof that this ritual was a success.*

*Demon King of the North, is it.*

It was a nickname given to him by others, but Kim Jonghyun liked it. He liked it because he truly intended to become a Demon Lord.

{sep}

The beings gliding through the darkness were, in truth, now ambiguous to even be called Demon Races. The intellect and power they had accumulated over long years had vanished along with their master. The very fact that they maintained their existence despite such a terrible event proved they were once high-class demons, but that was a story of the past. What remained were just monsters unbecoming of their kind, having lost their intellect, reason, and most of their glorious power.

Nevertheless, they were strong enough to make the forest tremble with their mere presence. Even having lost the majority of their former strength, a Demon Race was not an existence humans could face. In exchange for their reason and intellect, their instincts had sharpened, and they had gained a loyalty that was faithful to commands. The darkness of the forest, conjured by the Grimoire, was no hindrance to them. Rather, they felt comfortable within it, their senses growing sharper and making it easier to search for prey.

One of the scattered demons was the first to find its quarry. It discovered the high-ranking Priests and the mages of the Mages Guild, who were sitting and offering prayers. Their prayers were not meaningless. Given enough time, they might have been able to lift the darkness enveloping the forest.

Yes.

If only they had had enough time.

The unfortunate truth was that they had no such time, and there were no warriors nearby to protect them from brutal violence. In this situation, at least, the various gods they believed in did not protect their followers.

The monster that leaped from the darkness stood before a high-ranking Priest and recalled its master's command. *Painfully, until they weep with fear.* The monster was faithful. Its outstretched hand grabbed the high-ranking Priest's shoulder. The man flinched and raised his bowed head, his face turning pale as he met the hideous monster's gaze.

KWAJIJIK!

The monster's hand crushed his shoulder. The high-ranking Priest opened his mouth wide and screamed. The monster swatted its hand as if shooing a fly, throwing the man's body aside.

KWADANG!

The old body tumbled across the ground. The monster ignored him and pulled another Priest to his feet. Having heard nothing, the second Priest had not felt the high-ranking Priest's scream or his body being thrown.

"......"

He too screamed upon seeing the monster's face. The monster severed his legs. One by one, as the creature systematically disabled the Priests, the mages began to convulse and shoot out magic. Even as a Mage Army that had lost the advantage of coordination, the firepower they poured out was sufficiently threatening. The monster, however, was agile. It moved like a deep-sea fish swimming through the profound darkness, slipping through the gaps in the magic. The faces of the Priests, watching the mages meet a fate no different from their own, were soaked with despair.

"Oh God, oh God..."

They cried out to their gods with desperate voices, but the monster's movements did not stop. Receiving no answer to their calls, the Priests went mad with terrible despair and fear. The young ones with weak faith were the first to lose their minds, letting out crazed laughter. The high-ranking Priest squeezed his eyes shut and unleashed Divine Magic, but the spell he cast inflicted little damage on the darkness or the creature that was once a Demon Race.

The situation with the Holy Knight Commander was a little different. At first, it was. He and the other Holy Knights were not torn apart as helplessly as the Priests. The moment they faced their monster, they roared and charged, swinging swords enhanced with holy power at its body, but the monster did not take the attack head-on. Instead, it created distance, cunningly darting through the darkness where their vision was extremely limited. As the monster moved, the Holy Knight Commander and his men could hear nothing, not even the screams of their dying comrades.

Lee Sungmin remained oblivious to the other monsters unleashed in the forest. He had Tang Ahee walk by his side as he dealt with the demonic beasts, Undead, and Chimeras that appeared. It was not a very large forest, yet he still had not encountered any other members of the subjugation team. The only things he had met were corpses, but thankfully, Scarlet's body was not among them.

He did not think Scarlet would be defeated so easily. She would not have earned the title of Crimson Archmage for nothing. Her combat magic, in particular, was known to be of a very high class compared to other Magic Tower Masters. Although he did not fully trust such rumors, Lee Sungmin earnestly hoped she had survived.

Beside him, Tang Ahee felt like she would go crazy from wanting to change her urine-soaked and dried underwear, but she did not dare ask. Instead, her mind was on something else.

*When I meet my great-great-grandfather, I'll have to ask him to beat him half to death.*

It was not a lie that she had a connection with the Dark Supreme, Tang Mugi. Her great-great-grandfather had been very good to her since she was a child and was the great man who had contributed significantly to the formation of her awful personality. Although she had told Lee Sungmin she would let him meet the man, Tang Ahee was secretly planning to ask Tang Mugi to beat Lee Sungmin to a pulp. Or just kill him.

Unbeknownst to each other, their thoughts ran along a similar, violent track, because Lee Sungmin also intended to kill the Dark Supreme without hesitation if he met him.

Walking through the darkness was more boring than tense. From the moment he realized that whatever jumped out was not a major threat, it felt no different from a usual night stroll. That was precisely why Lee Sungmin grew more anxious. He had wandered the forest long enough to feel bored, yet he still had not found Scarlet.

The number of corpses increased. Among the fallen, he saw a few familiar faces, people he had barely exchanged a few words with. He felt no particular emotion at their gruesome deaths. It was Tang Ahee, following beside him, whose face became deathly pale as she saw the countless corpses of her fellow Pure White Martial Squad members.

The Holy Knight Commander was lucky. The members of his Holy Knight Regiment and the Mage Army that was with them had been brutally murdered, but he was still breathing. He was holding his side with divine magic, trying to keep his intestines from spilling out. It was a fatal wound that should have made him collapse immediately, but he had not given up. He cried out his god's name with all his might in his heart, but the god he believed in did not answer.

The monster dodged the Holy Knight Commander's flailing sword, waiting for him to grow exhausted and give up. It knew instinctively that this was the way to obtain high-quality fear.

The game had gone on too long.

Lee Sungmin, emerging from between the shadows, hesitated for a moment. Corpses were scattered all around. A monster that seemed fundamentally different from the forest's demonic beasts stood over the Holy Knight Commander, who looked perilously close to collapse.

*Should I help him? Do I have any obligation to?* No. When he thought about it, this whole mess had happened because the Holy Knight Commander had been needlessly suspicious. Of course, everyone had their own circumstances, but if one were to consider all of that, there would be no one innocent in the world.

In that fleeting moment, the monster changed its target. It had judged the dying Holy Knight Commander to be prey it could kill at any time.

[What a strange one, huh?]

The moment the Void Monarch muttered, the monster leaped over the Holy Knight Commander and rushed toward Lee Sungmin.
