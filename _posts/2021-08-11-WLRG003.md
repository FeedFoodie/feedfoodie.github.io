For an Otherworlder to make money in Eria, there were many options, but the most common was, of course, hunting monsters.

The world itself was roughly divided into two areas. There were the residential zones where humans and mutants lived, and then there were the hunting grounds where monsters appeared. This was a simple classification, of course, as hunting grounds came in various forms, like dungeons.

Lee Sungmin calmly assessed his current self. He had obtained the Martial Skeleton and the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art, yet neither granted him immediate strength. They were catalysts for his future growth, not instant power. 

In other words, if he left Zenavis right now for a nearby hunting ground, he would accomplish little. The Lee Sungmin from his past life could have easily wiped out the hunting grounds near the city, but that was an impossible feat for him now.

*All I have is experience. Though it's not particularly great experience...*

His past self had never possessed any remarkable skills. He had learned a second-R
rate martial art and picked up a variety of other things just to survive, but even with all that combined, he had been barely above average among the countless Otherworlders. It was the harsh limit of a Classless. While those who had a miraculous encounter shot forward quickly despite their status, the Lee Sungmin of his past life had been far from any such fortune.

Therefore, in this life, he had to seize every miraculous encounter he could.

The lodging Lee Sungmin chose was one of the many inns in Zenavis, a place where the cheap price was matched by the lackluster facilities. A potbellied, middle-aged man stood behind the counter, looking Lee Sungmin up and down as he stood there blankly.

"How long are you staying? You don't look like you've got much money," the innkeeper observed. "You're a Classless, aren't you?"

"......"

"Tsk, tsk. A pitiful one, you are," the man muttered, clicking his tongue. "You don't even look that old... how unfortunate you are to be dragged into a world like this as a Classless."

Lee Sungmin had heard those words countless times thirteen years ago. He couldn't tell if the man's words were simple pity or just a formality. After all, hadn't he just been nearly betrayed by a similar show of kindness?

"...I have enough for one night," Lee Sungmin said. "Um... if it's alright... could I start paying for the room with something other than money tomorrow?"

"......"

"With monster parts, for example... If there's anything you need, I'll do my best to get it. Ah, of course, I'll give you money too. As much as I can earn..."

"Hmm..." The innkeeper trailed off. 

If the man said no, Lee Sungmin planned to leave without hesitation and find another place. Monsters were just as valuable to the natives of Eria as they were to Otherworlders.

"Well... I guess it's fine," the innkeeper finally replied with a smirk, after a moment of contemplation. "There are always rooms available. In exchange, bring me goblin teeth or bodily fluids starting tomorrow... Oh, don't look at me like that. Goblin teeth and fluids are worth money, you know?"

"Yes... a little."

The Blacksmith and Mages Guilds were the biggest buyers of monster carcasses, though the Mercenary Guild sometimes purchased them too. Since Lee Sungmin wasn't affiliated with any of them, trying to sell the carcasses himself would be difficult. He would lose too much to commission fees. It was better to just give them to the innkeeper to pay for his lodging.

"Ah, and... could I borrow a pen and some paper?"

The room Lee Sungmin was given was tidy enough, but it carried an old, musty smell. He was told he could use the communal bath on the first floor, and that meals were served three times a day in the first-floor hall.

A one-night stay, including meals, cost 20,000 Eru. It was an incredibly cheap price, especially considering it included baths and food.

*But it's far from the hunting grounds.*

The inn was located at the very edge of Zenavis, in the opposite direction of the gate leading out to hunt. Just reaching the gate would take at least two hours on foot. He could shorten the time with a carriage or a horse, but that wasn't cheap either.

That must be why there were so few guests. For Lee Sungmin, this was actually better. It meant he could secure a cheap place to stay.

He opened the notebook he had borrowed from the innkeeper. First, he needed to organize the memories swirling in his head. For a long while, Lee Sungmin wrestled with his past, writing down every opportunity he had missed.

*One year. First, I have to stay in Zenavis for one year.*

In his past life, he had stayed in Zenavis for three years. This time, one year would be enough. In fact, he probably didn't even need a full year. With the Martial Skeleton and the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art, he could likely build enough strength to graduate from Zenavis in half that time.

However, Lee Sungmin had a reason he had to stay for a full year.

It was because of the Coliseum.

Located in the very center of Zenavis, the Coliseum was a place of entertainment for the residents and a venue where Otherworlders could test their strength. The events were held on a monthly cycle, and most of the participants were Otherworlders. Winning came with a fitting reward, and what Lee Sungmin was aiming for was the Coliseum a year from now.

*One year from now. The Star Spirit Pill will be the prize for winning the Coliseum's Classless Tournament.*

The Classless Tournament, where only the Classless could participate. Usually, the prizes were minor, but in the Coliseum a year from now, the Star Spirit Pill was on the line. It was a miracle herb that greatly helped in enhancing one's inner qi, more than worth staying in Zenavis for a year to obtain.

*The problem is, it won't be easy.*

Although only the Classless could participate, not all of them were weak. According to his memory, the one who won that tournament was a veteran who had lived in Zenavis for several years.

*There's only one chance. Since I've decided to stay in Zenavis for a whole year, I absolutely must obtain the Star Spirit Pill.*

One year. It was both a long and a short time. In his past life... how had he spent his first day? He had no money and no idea how to live in this world. He had curled up in a back alley and slept fitfully, hungry but having eaten nothing. 

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly at the memory.

*This is a good start. It's incomparable to my past life. I have a little money and a room. I have a bed to sleep in... and I can even take a bath.*

Now that he thought about it, he was hungry. He hadn't eaten or drunk anything yet. He wanted to take a bath, too. The smell of blood still lingered on his hands.

He put it off, however. Lee Sungmin quickly sat down cross-legged and opened the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. He read it from beginning to end. In this process, full understanding was unnecessary. Simply reading the words was enough.

[You have thoroughly read the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art.]

[Would you like to acquire the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art?]

*Yes,* Lee Sungmin answered in his mind.

"......"

A bright light exploded in his head. This was why understanding was unnecessary. If one thoroughly read a martial arts manual, it could be acquired as a skill. The same was true for magic.

*Magic... I'd like to learn it if I have the chance.*

The Lee Sungmin of his past life had never used magic. The two disciplines, magic and martial arts, were completely different in their direction and growth. With martial arts, the more one trained, the higher their achievement and the stronger their power became. Magic, however, was not like that.

If one learned the Fireball spell, all they could ever use was Fireball. It was less versatile than martial arts, which strengthened the body itself. 

It might have been different if he had learned various types of magic, but his past self never had the opportunity to encounter them. Joining a mages guild or academy also cost an enormous amount of money, and they were incredibly exclusive.

In fact, it was the same for the martial arts sects. What they wanted were those who had already learned basic magic abilities and martial arts, not a Classless with nothing. 

The schools, guilds, and sects were fundamentally based on mutual exchange. Even if two people were from the Wudang Sect, their different origins meant the martial arts they learned would have variations. The same was true for magic. By accepting new members, they could teach each other and share their knowledge.

However, a Classless? They had nothing to offer in exchange.

*It can't be helped. As a Classless, this is the only way for me to learn skills.*

Fortunately, memories could be utilized to gain skills. 

Lee Sungmin closed his eyes, delving into his past.

[You have recalled One Thunder Movement Arts!]

[You have recalled Iron Skin Strong Bones!]

[You have recalled Stone Breaking Fist!]

[You have recalled Soul Chasing Spear Art!]

That was it. After searching his memories for a while, a satisfied expression appeared on Lee Sungmin's face. Those four martial arts were the ones he had used in his past life.

*Extracting skills from memory... this is the first time I've tried it.*

Lee Sungmin let out a sigh of relief. He knew from his past life's experience that it was possible. If it were impossible, then martial artists and mages from other worlds wouldn't be able to use their arts and magic as skills or pass them on to others.

These four martial arts were, by the standards of his past life, at the second-rate level. One Thunder Movement Arts was a technique that lightened the body for fast running, while Iron Skin Strong Bones and Stone Breaking Fist were external arts that hardened the body. Soul Chasing Spear Art was a straightforward spear technique with few variations.

In his past life, he had used a spear and his fists. Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly at the memory. He had chosen the spear for a simple reason. It seemed safer to stab opponents from a distance. The fist techniques were for situations where he couldn't use a weapon or when his weapon broke.

*They're not amazing martial arts, but it's a much better situation compared to my past life. Back then, I tried to catch monsters by throwing rocks.*

When he had actually tried it, he couldn't hit anything. Even when he did manage to land a hit by chance, he had a terrible time. The charging monster had terrified him, and he had run away with his tail between his legs.

> Name: Lee Sungmin
>
> Occupation: Classless
>
> Skills:
> - Heavenly Truth Meditation Art (1-Star)
> - One Thunder Movement Arts (1-Star)
> - Iron Skin Strong Bones (1-Star)
> - Stone Breaking Fist (1-Star)
> - Soul Chasing Spear Art (1-Star)

After confirming the martial arts had been added to his Skill Window, Lee Sungmin got up. First, he would eat and wash up. Then, after practicing the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art for a little while, he would sleep. Tomorrow morning, he would leave the inn early, buy the necessary supplies, and head for the hunting grounds.
