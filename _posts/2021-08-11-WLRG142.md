The name Gwan Hu meant nothing to him, suggesting the man was not a particularly famous individual. Lee Sungmin watched with wary eyes as he approached Gwan Hu, who stood with his hands clasped in a fist-and-palm salute. As Lee Sungmin drew near, Gwan Hu raised his bowed head and gestured toward a seat.

"I have something I'd like to ask."

"What is it?"

"Phantom Spear. Why did you protect the Little Heavenly Demon?"

"Do I have to answer?" Lee Sungmin countered.

"I ask because I have many questions," Gwan Hu said. "You're a man of considerable renown in this world, aren't you, Phantom Spear? You earned your moniker after killing the Sword Phantom, the man who murdered the Sect Leader of the Mount Hua Sect, and that's not all. Just half a year ago, you subdued the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon in North Trabia, and later killed him after he became a vampire."

*So the rumors have spread this far,* Lee Sungmin thought, clicking his tongue lightly. It was true that he had clashed with Baek Museon outside Trabia's gates and defeated him. The fight had been loud, so there must have been witnesses. He recalled the carriage he had taken when he first went to the city, and the driver he had secretly suspected was a member of the Rogues' Guild.

"I don't know why the Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae is traveling with you," Gwan Hu continued, "but looking at your past actions, you seem more like a Warrior of Justice from the orthodox sects. Both the Sword Phantom and the Blood Heavenly Demon were demonic practitioners, after all. If you went to the Murim Alliance, they would likely recognize your achievements and treat you well."

"What are you trying to say?"

"I'm asking because I don't understand. After building such a history, why would you protect the Little Heavenly Demon, even to the point of making an enemy of the Iron Armored Divine Spear? What exactly is your relationship with her?"

"A friend," Lee Sungmin replied, gently setting Wiji Hoyeon down beside him.

At his words, Gwan Hu's eyes widened. His gaze flickered between Lee Sungmin and the unconscious Wiji Hoyeon for a moment before he burst out laughing.

"I see. A friend, you say. Many people follow the Little Heavenly Demon, but I've never heard of anyone who claims to be her friend."

"We met in Zenavis ten years ago," Lee Sungmin explained. "We got to know each other by chance and became friends."

"And that's why you blocked the Iron Armored Divine Spear?"

"I had already promised to meet Wiji Hoyeon in this city today. I never imagined something like this would happen with him."

"What will you do now?" Gwan Hu asked, a faint smile playing on his lips. "Whatever your reason, it's an undeniable fact that you subdued the Iron Armored Divine Spear and took the Little Heavenly Demon. There were many witnesses, so the rumors will spread quickly. I've heard an extermination squad from the Murim Alliance has already left Kron. They should arrive here today at the earliest, or tomorrow at the latest."

"That's why I came here."

"What do you expect from us?"

"Being pursued by a faction is a hassle, especially a well-organized group like the Murim Alliance. If it were just a few outstanding individuals, I could somehow deal with them, but the Murim Alliance has too many numbers."

"So you want the protection of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Do you know what that means?"

"Don't test me," Lee Sungmin said flatly. "Speak plainly."

"Haha… Don't be so angry. To receive our protection means entrusting yourselves to us, does it not? I was asking if the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon, who has wandered without a faction, intends to become our comrade."

"If you want to ask her that, then ask her, not me."

"Then what about you?" Gwan Hu's eyes narrowed. "You are a great master. The Iron Armored Divine Spear Yoo Hojeong is one of the most skilled martial artists who wields a spear, yet you subdued him without much difficulty."

"He wasn't using his full strength," Lee Sungmin clarified. "If we had both been trying to kill each other, there's no telling what would have happened."

"Not everyone will understand such circumstances. The Little Heavenly Demon is currently asleep and cannot answer, but you can. If you want to use our alliance as a shield against the Murim Alliance, shouldn't you give us something to make it worthwhile?"

"Then I'll forget it."

Lee Sungmin's words were clipped as he took hold of the sleeping Wiji Hoyeon's shoulder. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was not his only option. He had not remained in Ruvus just to rely on their unpredictable nature. He had a connection with Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master. While he was not certain Lloyd would protect them out of goodwill alone, he could always 'ask' for a favor if it came to that.

"It was a joke," Gwan Hu chuckled softly, seeing Lee Sungmin preparing to help Wiji Hoyeon up. "Are you planning to go to the Golden Magic Tower? Since you didn't go there right away, it seems you didn't want to impose on the Tower Master."

Lee Sungmin ignored the comment. "What was a joke?"

Gwan Hu shrugged. "As long as you are here, I will protect you in the name of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Even if you don't join us."

"Really?"

"Of course."

"As a branch manager, do you have that kind of authority?"

"This is not my will, but the will of the Coalition Leader," Gwan Hu replied. "He ordered me to have you join us if possible, and if not, to protect you regardless."

"…Why?"

"Because the Little Heavenly Demon's reputation is that high, and you, the Phantom Spear, are just as formidable. Regardless of age, how many 'humans' in this world are as exceptional as you?" Gwan Hu rose to his feet. "Servants will be here with a meal soon. For now, have something to eat, and if you need it, I'll call a physician as well."

"…Please do."

"And this is just me meddling," Gwan Hu added, looking down at the seated Lee Sungmin. "It's not easy to live in this world as gray." He paused. "You don't seem to be from the murim. Am I right?"

"I'm Classless."

"To reach the Supreme Peak at your age as a Classless! That's an incredible feat. With that level of skill, you won't be ignored anywhere. You could probably live as you please, to a certain extent. However, that doesn't mean you're free. As long as you're gray, you can belong to neither black nor white, but you'll be ostracized just as much."

"That's not your concern."

"Haha… You're stubborn. Or are you simply afraid of making a choice?"

Leaving those words hanging in the air, Gwan Hu descended from the pavilion. *Afraid of making a choice?* Lee Sungmin gazed down at Wiji Hoyeon's sleeping face. He simply had not felt the need to choose. Orthodox or unorthodox. In his eyes, they were all just martial artists. There were people like the unorthodox among the orthodox sects, and people like the orthodox among the unorthodox.

[He's not just talking about the orthodox and unorthodox sects, you know,] the Void Monarch muttered in an amused voice. [You are gray. A gray that could turn white or black.]

"I know what you mean," Lee Sungmin said quietly, his eyes still on Wiji Hoyeon. He had already heard it from the Void Monarch, back when he had nearly died fighting Baek Museon. Even without the Elixir, his body had begun to regenerate. Such an ability was not easily obtained, even for a body honed by martial arts. It might have been a power gained from embedding the Black Heart, but his regenerative power had already surpassed that of a human. Looking at his body alone, one could even call him inhuman.

Just as Gwan Hu had said, servants soon brought food. Lavish dishes were placed on the table in the center of the pavilion, but Lee Sungmin did not spare them a single glance. He said nothing, his gaze fixed on the sleeping Wiji Hoyeon.

"…Ughn…"

A small groan escaped her lips.

[It must be painful,] the Void Monarch remarked. The curse engraved on her was vicious and nasty. Her breathing grew ragged, her brow furrowed in pain as cold sweat trickled down her forehead. Lee Sungmin sighed and reached out. Just as his fingertips were about to wipe the sweat away…

"…Don't you have a handkerchief?"

Wiji Hoyeon's eyes fluttered open. She stared up at him, her own eyes faded and weary. Lee Sungmin flinched, pulling back his hand.

"…I don't."

"You idiot. You should at least carry a handkerchief…"

"Do you have one?"

"I do. Inside my clothes. Can you get it out for me?" Wiji Hoyeon asked with a mischievous smile. The joke, delivered with a pale face and a trembling voice, felt anything but playful.

Lee Sungmin silently nodded and reached out his hand. Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing.

"It's a joke. You… were you really going to put your hand inside my clothes? You pervert."

"You told me to."

"That's why I said it was a joke." Wiji Hoyeon slowly pushed herself into a sitting position. *This isn't good.* She bit her lower lip. The curse had not been this strong on her way to Ruvus. It seemed her fight with the Iron Armored Divine Spear had worsened it. Taking a deep breath, she suppressed the pain squirming inside her body, then took out a handkerchief and wiped away her sweat.

"…It's hot…" she muttered in a small voice, her brow furrowed. "And cold. It's a fucking awful feeling."

"…Does the Elixir not work?"

"It doesn't. I drank it like water on the way here, but it had no effect. I even tried painkillers… but this pain can't be suppressed. Because it's a curse."

"Who on earth cursed you?"

Lee Sungmin snatched the handkerchief from her. He began to wipe her face himself. Wiji Hoyeon, who had been panting for breath, closed her eyes at his touch.

"…What would you do if you knew?"

"I'd do something."

"You? Hehe. That's impossible. Compared to ten years ago, and even one year ago, you've become really, incredibly strong, but even you wouldn't be a match for that guy."

"Without even trying?"

"Are you stronger than me?" Wiji Hoyeon asked.

Lee Sungmin could not answer.

"The one who cursed me… is one of the Six Honored Ones of the Outer Heavens. A guy called the Fist Honored One." She let out a dry cough. Startled, Lee Sungmin reached for her, but she raised a hand to stop him. "According to him, he's the last place among the Six Honored Ones. I fought him for three days and three nights, and in the end, I couldn't kill him. He stopped the fight and left, leaving this curse on me. He said he would come back for me someday."

"…Heaven Beyond Heaven… Fist Honored One…"

He had already heard of Heaven Beyond Heaven from the Crazy Heavenly Demon in Trabia. Ten years ago, the man had spoken of his defeat at the hands of the Sword Saint. Now, Wiji Hoyeon was speaking of Heaven Beyond Heaven, the Six Honored Ones, and the Fist Honored One.

"This curse won't disappear unless he's killed. Are you saying you can kill the Fist Honored One? The same one that even I couldn't kill after fighting for three days and three nights?"

"I have to do something…!"

"Because you're my friend?" she asked with a smile.

Lee Sungmin hesitated before nodding. At his answer, Wiji Hoyeon laughed. She shook her head. "If that's the case, thank you… but it's okay. It's my problem, so I'll solve it."

"In your condition?"

"Hehe… I never thought I'd hear you worry about me like this. Let's stop talking about this. It was a promise from ten years ago, and we've finally met after a year. We didn't get to talk properly in the Dungeon back then." Wiji Hoyeon smiled as she spoke. "Today is a day I was really looking forward to. So… let's talk about the things we wanted to, and I'm hungry. Unfortunately, I can't use both my hands properly."

She let out a low laugh, deeper than the one he remembered from ten years ago.

"Will you feed me?"

"…What do you want to eat?" Lee Sungmin asked back with a deep sigh.

As he reached out and picked up the chopsticks, Wiji Hoyeon answered as if she had been waiting.

"Meat."

"Meat?"

"Yeah." She opened her mouth. "Put it in here."

"You're unbelievable," Lee Sungmin grumbled as he picked up a piece of meat from a nearby dish.
