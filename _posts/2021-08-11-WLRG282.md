The carriage could no longer advance, its progress halted not far from Trabia.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the one who had stopped their journey, emerged from the carriage to survey the plain where a snowstorm raged. Yehwa cast a nervous glance at him from the driver's seat, while inside, Scarlet let out a long sigh.

"Why do you have to make things so troublesome?" she muttered.

He had been told not to come out.

Lee Sungmin, his mask now removed, tightened his grip on his spear. He knew this plain. He had been here years ago when he first traveled to the North with Rubia, before he had met the Crazy Heavenly Demon or set foot in Trabia. A thoroughfare connecting to the city cut across this vast expanse, and it was here that he had first encountered Ju Won, the King of Lycanthropes.

This was the territory of the Mad Wolf.

The Black Dragon Knight was aware of this as well. Lee Sungmin had made sure to tell everyone before they entered the plain that it belonged to Ju Won, one of the Black Stars of the Predators. He had even suggested they go around, accepting that it might take a little more time, but his counsel had been dismissed. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had simply declared there was no need, and so the carriage had cut straight through the heart of the wolf's domain.

And now, it was stopped.

*Don't come out.*

Lee Sungmin recalled his Master's words. They were not born of absolute confidence or arrogance, but of a pure, unadulterated curiosity. It was the same curiosity that had driven him to charge into Kron to challenge the Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation and to lay waste to the Murim Alliance. Thinking back, Lee Sungmin realized most of his Master's actions were fueled by this insatiable 'curiosity'.

Of course, it was not his only motivation. The Chairman had also expressed a clear intention to instill a sense of awe in his disciple and teach him the essence of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. Still, remembering what he had said after hearing about The End from Abel, it was clear he had a powerful tendency to immerse himself in anything that piqued his interest. It was a chronic mental ailment developed over a thousand years of enduring Denir's trial.

For matters that did not interest him, he would simply demand Lee Sungmin's impulse and use him as a catalyst. For anything crucial, he thoroughly prioritized his own desires. This journey was no different. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman simply 'wanted to do it'. He wanted to meet the Black Dragon Knight to ask about Heaven Beyond Heaven. He wanted to meet Jeniela to ask about the future she had seen. His doubts about 'The End' held little weight for him.

What he had told Abel was not a lie. Death comes for everyone, an unstoppable end. If that was the case, it could not be helped. It was unpleasant, but he could accept it. Yes, those words were undoubtedly the truth.

At the same time, it was also true that he valued Osla and, to some extent, his disciple. That was why he had followed Lee Sungmin's opinion, for Lee Sungmin's sake, because Lee Sungmin had said he wanted to stop The End.

However, that was not the whole story. He wanted to experience the Dog Beating Stick Formation. He wanted to lay waste to the Murim Alliance. He wanted to meet the Vampire Queen, said to be the pinnacle of the Predators. Such personal desires moved the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and had brought him here. The reason he deliberately entered Ju Won's territory was because he wished for it. The reason he now stood outside the carriage alone, seeking no help from the Black Dragon Knight or Lee Sungmin, was also because he wanted it that way.

"In that forest," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, clutching the unhealed wound on his left side. "That's where I fought Ju Won. He attacked me without warning. I had just fought the Spear King and fired my breath repeatedly, so I was exhausted, but even so... I was overwhelmingly defeated."

A pang of guilt struck Lee Sungmin. Ju Won's lifelong ambition was to fight a Dragon. With all the world's dragons having migrated to another dimension, the half-dragon Black Dragon Knight was the closest being who could fulfill that dream. The reason they had fought in that forest was that Lee Sungmin had sold out the Black Dragon Knight to Ju Won. Because of him, the Knight had been forced to face Ju Won in an exhausted state and received a wound that would not heal.

"Ju Won is strong," the Black Dragon Knight said, lowering his hand. He stared blankly at Lee Sungmin. Throughout their months-long journey, Lee Sungmin had been wary of him, but the Knight had shown no particular behavior. It was the same now. "But your Master is stronger."

His voice left no room for doubt.

"I know."

Even against Ju Won, Lee Sungmin did not think his Master would be defeated. It was just... the Chairman's tendency to act on his own interests felt precarious. He threw himself into danger in pursuit of his desires, a self-destructive habit, even if most situations posed no real threat to him.

[Are you worried?]

*A little.*

The Void Monarch chuckled at his reply.

[Still, you're worried because he's your Master, aren't you?]

*I'm human too. Of course I'm worried.*

[There's no need. Ju Won has gotten stronger over the hundreds of years, but he's still not stronger than the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.]

*Still, if there's no need to fight, isn't it better not to?*

[Then why don't you stop him?]

*Would he listen even if I tried?*

The Void Monarch cackled at Lee Sungmin's question, and then, a chill descended.

The carriage jolted violently.

Lee Sungmin's hand flew to his spear. He was not the only one who felt it. The Black Dragon Knight and Scarlet reacted instantly. With a stiff expression, Scarlet snapped her magic tome shut. The Knight stared out the window, his face startled.

This sensation, this feeling that made the hair on his entire body stand on end, was one Lee Sungmin had felt several times before, but Scarlet had not. The Black Dragon Knight's face hardened in recognition, while Scarlet's complexion turned pale. It was a dread so profound it could overwhelm even a master of the Transcendent Realm. Though she was an archmage, it was difficult to resist the fear that gnawed at her senses.

[Grab her,] the Void Monarch said urgently. [I don't know what she's thinking, but Jeniela isn't hiding her presence. A human with weak resistance won't be able to withstand it. Their mind will collapse.]

Scarlet's body began to tremble, though she did not understand why. She raised her shaking hands to hug her shoulders as her breathing grew ragged. It felt as if insects were crawling inside her head.

Lee Sungmin's hand shot out and grabbed her wrist. He grasped her vital points and sent his internal energy surging into her. Light returned to Scarlet's dazed eyes.

"...Ugh!"

Pressing her temples against a throbbing headache, Scarlet panted for breath and looked up at him. An embarrassed expression soon crossed her face, and she gently pushed his hand away with her fingertips.

"...Thanks. It was so sudden, my response was slow."

She formed a hand seal and moved her lips, erecting a mental barrier that cleared her mind.

The Black Dragon Knight stared out the window, his expression tense. "This... is a monster," he breathed, swallowing hard as he assessed Jeniela's oppressive presence. The vicious and ominous aura she exuded was on a different level from the Chairman's. The sheer pressure on both body and mind was more intense than the intimidation he had felt when facing his Master.

Lee Sungmin had faced Jeniela before, but this was the first time he had seen her display such an intense presence. He circulated his inner qi, a rumbling sound coming from his qi center, and the weight on his body lessened slightly.

He had been told to stay inside, but worry for his Master won out. Lee Sungmin opened the carriage door.

"What is it," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said. He was standing on the driver's seat. Beside him, Yehwa trembled, clutching the reins.

Lee Sungmin now understood why the carriage had jolted. The horses could not withstand a pressure that even a Transcendent master found difficult to endure. They were fine steeds that had pulled the carriage on this long journey, but they had all died under Jeniela's presence. The only reason Yehwa held on was because the Chairman had a hand on her shoulder, infusing her with his internal energy.

His Master had taken off his mask. He lifted Yehwa with Space Manipulation and sent her into the carriage Lee Sungmin had just opened.

"It's been a while," a voice greeted them. "Did I give you the wrong impression?"

The snowstorm stopped. Amidst the fluttering snowflakes, a pair of red eyes shone with a brilliant light. Jeniela stood there, as if she had been there all along, flaunting a vicious presence that filled the entire space with terror. With every slow step she took, Lee Sungmin's heart pounded wildly. He knew she was a monster, but he had never realized the gap was this large. Just as his Master was a special outlier among martial artists, so was Jeniela among the Predators. She possessed an overwhelming strength that could not be compared to the other Black Stars.

"Wrong impression?" the Chairman asked. He had met her hundreds of years ago, before entering the Legro Forest, during a time when he wandered the world. Their meeting then had been a coincidence. Since neither held any hostility, they had passed by each other fleetingly. Their connection had ended there, but now was different.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman gave a satisfied smile at the power she displayed.

Jeniela's eyes moved, and for a moment, she froze. Seeing Lee Sungmin standing next to the Chairman, she could not hide her flustered expression.

"Why are you here?" she had no choice but to ask.

Jeniela had definitely seen a fragmented vision of the future. Her coming here to greet the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was part of what she had seen. Yes, she had certainly witnessed this meeting through her Demonic Eye, but Lee Sungmin was not in that vision. That was what flustered her. She had clearly seen the future, a future only a few hours away. Why was Lee Sungmin, who did not exist in that vision, here?

"What are you talking about?" Lee Sungmin asked back, not letting down his guard.

Instead of answering, Jeniela immediately activated her Demonic Eye. Magic power swirled within her red pupils as the All-Seeing Demonic Eye, which could see through everything, turned towards Lee Sungmin.

"......"

She had used it on him twice before. The first time was when they met in Trabia. The second was during the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun, when Lee Sungmin appeared riding a Fairy Horse. The first time, she could not see through his existence, but the second time, it had been possible.

And now?

*I can't see...?*

It was as if a thick fog enveloped him. Jeniela's bewilderment grew. He was not in her vision of the future, and she could not see him with her Demonic Eye. Why could she see him last time, but not now?

"How cluttered," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's voice reached her ears. He glanced around the plain where the snow had settled. No one but Jeniela was visible, yet he could clearly feel the presence of numerous Vampires hidden in the shadows. They were masters of the world's greatest stealth techniques, but deceiving his senses was impossible. "Do you wish to fight this one?"

"Ahaha!" Jeniela laughed, her high-pitched voice spreading out. Lee Sungmin raised both hands to cover his ears.

AAAAAH-!

The end of the laugh resonated, becoming a massive echo that shook the earth and rattled his insides. On the trembling ground, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's body did not waver in the slightest.

"No way. Ah, hahaha... No way. There's no way. I have no intention of fighting... Hah! None at all," Jeniela kept chuckling as if something was incredibly funny. "Cluttered? If you felt that way, I apologize. Since you are who you are, I thought I should show you proper courtesy. Don't worry. I didn't bring them here to threaten you."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stared blankly at her. Controlling her breathing, Jeniela wiped the laughter from her face.

"I know why you've come here."

"I hear you see the future," the Chairman murmured.

Jeniela's eyes gleamed. "Oh my. That's one of my biggest secrets... Who told you?"

"This one figured it out on his own."

"Lies," Jeniela said, baring her teeth in a smile. "Well... it's not that important, though." She turned her body. "I'll guide you to my mansion. I came all the way out here to greet you, so I believe I've shown enough courtesy..."

"I have one question," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said. "You. How did you know this one was coming here and arrive in advance?"

"I saw the future."

"Is that so?" he scoffed. "Then did you see a future like this?"

When his words ended, Jeniela's cleanly severed head was falling to the ground.
