"Alright, let's say we're done with the Spear Technique for now," Wiji Hoyeon muttered, suddenly extending her hand toward him. "I'll give you twenty percent of my inner qi."

Though she spoke as if offering something trivial, Lee Sungmin could not take her words at face value. Twenty percent of Wiji Hoyeon's inner qi was an amount he could not even begin to fathom. Besides, could inner qi truly be given and received so easily? Doubt gnawed at him.

The nature of inner qi was different for everyone. A man's arts carried yang energy while a woman's carried yin. From that basic dichotomy, it branched into countless variations depending on the technique one learned. A Taoist art cultivated Taoist energy, and a Buddhist art cultivated Buddhist energy.

"I've learned the Violet Dawn Divine Art," he said. "Is it okay for me to receive your inner qi?"

A pleased expression bloomed on Wiji Hoyeon's face at his question. "So you're not a complete fool."

"Normally, you can never give or receive inner qi from another person," she explained. "It might be possible with the purest life qi, but that's not a very efficient method. Life qi is life force itself, so tampering with it can shorten one's lifespan."

At least, he thought, she didn't intend to transfer life qi.

"There are evil arts like the Star Absorption Technique," she continued, "but that's not very efficient either. The ability to freely steal another's inner qi might seem like a great advantage, but it's not a good method. Too many impurities build up, making it the perfect way to fall into qi deviation."

Wiji Hoyeon reached a little further and grabbed Lee Sungmin's wrist. "Do you know why the Heavenly Demon Divine Art is history's number one divine art, and why it's passed down only to the successive Cult Leaders?"

"How would I know?"

"Of course you wouldn't know. To put it simply, the Heavenly Demon Divine Art doesn't go through the 'refinement' process that all other inner arts techniques have."

"...What?"

Lee Sungmin was stunned. Though he had only learned Third-Rate and Second-Rate techniques before, he knew enough to understand just how incredible and absurd her claim was. The very basis of an inner arts technique was to draw natural energy from the world into the body through breathing, then refine it into 'inner qi' according to the incantation. That refined energy would then accumulate in the qi center.

This was the foundation of all inner qi, the same for a Third-Rate martial artist as it was for someone in the peak realm. Even the Violet Dawn Divine Art he was currently learning, a technique on the level of divine arts, still included the refinement process. Its superiority lay in how it condensed the process of refining and accumulating energy, and in the sheer amount and speed at which it took in that energy, which was incomparable to other techniques.

However, an inner arts technique without a refinement process? If that were true, it meant that with every breath, the natural energy filling the atmosphere would accumulate directly in the qi center.

"The Heavenly Demon Divine Art is a technique that accumulates the natural energy brought in through breathing directly into the qi center," she said. "It's an art worthy of being called history's number one. However, not just anyone can learn it. You have to be born with a special constitution."

Wiji Hoyeon fell silent for a moment, her fingers massaging his wrist. Lee Sungmin felt the energy she infused penetrate him through the acupoint she was pressing.

"Your acupoints aren't clean," she observed. "There are quite a few impurities built up. Fourteen isn't a very early age to start learning martial arts. If anything, it's a bit late."

It couldn't be helped. The skeletal modification procedure had granted him a Low-Grade Martial Skeleton and changed the shape of his bones, but it had not cleaned out his acupoints.

"Your qi center is small too, but that can't be helped. You have little inner qi. Now then," she announced, "I'm going to give you twenty percent of my inner qi. This is easier said than done, for both the receiver and the giver."

"...It's not dangerous, is it?"

"It's not that dangerous. However, how much you can salvage from the twenty percent I'm giving you depends entirely on your own capabilities. Circulate the Violet Dawn Divine Art."

Wiji Hoyeon let go of his hand. Lee Sungmin did as she instructed, obediently sitting cross-legged and beginning to circulate his technique. The mouse-tail's worth of inner qi in his qi center squirmed and flowed through his meridians.

She moved to stand behind him. After taking a few steadying breaths, she placed her right hand on one of his acupoints and activated the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

"Don't make a sound," she warned.

Lee Sungmin closed his eyes and sealed his lips. He focused only on reciting the incantation for the Violet Dawn Divine Art in his mind as he circulated it. Then, a huge mass of inner qi flowed in through his acupoints. It was incomparably pure natural energy that had not undergone any refinement.

The energy Wiji Hoyeon was infusing had a value greater than any miracle herb. Miracle herbs were also masses of qi, but they contained impurities from their own refinement process. Refining them again through an inner arts technique resulted in a considerable loss of qi.

However, there was nothing of the sort in the energy Wiji Hoyeon was infusing. Of course, that didn't mean he could accept it as it was. He was not learning the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled violently as Wiji Hoyeon's inner qi circulated throughout his entire body. He continuously circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, desperately trying to capture the flow of that immense power.

"......"

After some time had passed, Wiji Hoyeon removed her hand from his acupoint. A rare look of fatigue lingered on her face. She grumbled as she wiped the beads of sweat from her forehead.

"This is a great miraculous encounter. Do you know that?"

Lee Sungmin couldn't answer. He was completely overwhelmed just by the task of guiding the energy flowing through his meridians to his qi center. Only after a long while was he finally able to open his eyes.

"...Ah!"

A gasp of admiration was the first sound he made. His qi center was full. It had been a long time since he had felt such a sense of fullness.

*This is only twenty percent of her inner qi?*

Disbelief followed his admiration. The twenty percent Wiji Hoyeon had infused was far more than the total inner qi he had accumulated over ten years in his past life.

"How much did you take in?" she asked.

"Uh... about... half?"

"Lies. Seeing the level of your Violet Dawn Divine Art, you probably couldn't have even salvaged half. Still, don't be too upset. It's inner qi that has seeped into your meridians, so if you consistently circulate your technique, you'll be able to guide it to your qi center."

"I'm tired," Wiji Hoyeon grumbled and plopped down on the bed. Having consumed twenty percent of her inner qi at once, even she could not help but be exhausted.

"...Why are you doing all this for me?"

"Because we're friends," Wiji Hoyeon answered. "Don't think too much of it. I mean, this is just my own simple self-satisfaction. I'm not expecting anything from you, so you don't need to worry about it."

Self-satisfaction. The words she had just uttered felt quite plausible to her. The favor she held for Lee Sungmin was perfectly described by that phrase.

"I'm tired. I'll go to bed early today," she declared, "and starting tomorrow, I'll have to overhaul the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear."

"...Is there anything I can do to help?"

"You? Help me? You should say something that makes sense. If you want to help, write down the incantation for the Soul Chasing Spear Art and leave it. I'll graft it onto the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style)."

"Do you really have to add 'Sungmin Style' to it?"

"It's my choice."

At her reply, Lee Sungmin gave up on persuading her. He brought a notebook and pen from his room, wrote down the incantation for the Soul Chasing Spear Art, and handed it to her. Wiji Hoyeon glanced over it once and grumbled.

"To think something like this is a martial art..."

To someone like her, who had encountered numerous divine arts and possessed talent that reached the heavens, the Soul Chasing Spear Art seemed like it couldn't even be called a martial art.

"...Thank you." Lee Sungmin bowed his head, his gratitude genuine.

Wiji Hoyeon glared at the back of his bowed head, then suddenly raised her hand and struck the crown of it.

THWACK!

Lee Sungmin cried out from the dull pain.

"......"

"Don't bow your head," Wiji Hoyeon spat. "You and I are friends. Don't bow your head between friends."

"Friends can bow their heads sometimes..."

"I don't like it," she replied as she got up. "How long are you going to stay here? If you're free, go to the backyard and swing your spear around."

"What about you?"

"I'm busy. Just overhauling the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear will take over a month."

"...Are you not planning to leave Zenavis?" Lee Sungmin asked. He had told her the story of his past life. Originally, at this point in time, Wiji Hoyeon would have already left the city.

"There's still something I have to do, isn't there?"

"It's because of me."

"That's right. It's because of you," Wiji Hoyeon replied with a smile. "If I hadn't heard your story about regression or whatever today, I would have said my goodbyes and left this city within a few days."

It was just as it had been in his past life. Though she had experienced different events this time, the person known as Wiji Hoyeon seemed to have no intention of living in this city for more than a month.

"You've already died once and come back here, haven't you?" she said. "If you die senselessly this time too, wouldn't your situation be miserable and truly unfair? So, I'll help you a little. So you don't die a violent death."

Lee Sungmin stared at her with a blank expression. What was this bottomless favor? Because they were friends? Was she really showing him such kindness for such a simple reason? He couldn't understand her attitude at all. Their friendship was shallow at best, hardly even a chance meeting.

It was only natural that he couldn't comprehend such favor. In the thirteen years of his past life, Lee Sungmin had never had anyone by his side who could be called a friend. He had people who could be called comrades, but they just died.

He had thought about them. Now that he had returned to the past, couldn't he do something to help them so they wouldn't die? Bullshit. Right now, he was in a position where he could barely take care of himself. At this point, more than two months later, they were probably already dead.

"What's with that expression?"

"...I'm just grateful," Lee Sungmin muttered, swallowing a sigh.

His meeting with Wiji Hoyeon was, without a doubt, the greatest miraculous encounter he had experienced in his entire life, including his past one.
