Seven hundred years ago.

The words hung in the air, a weight from a distant past Lee Sungmin couldn't simply dismiss. The Blood Demon spoke with a gentle smile, showing none of the tension or resolve one would expect before a battle to the death. Was it the confidence of the strong, Lee Sungmin wondered, or the wisdom of a master who had lived for centuries? He dared not try to judge.

Asura Heaven Destruction Art.

The Blood Demon had called it the world's number one martial art seven hundred years ago. Unfortunately, Lee Sungmin had no idea what it was. He searched through every memory of his past life, but such an ancient art was nowhere to be found.

*Do you know?*

[I do not.]

He had asked with a sliver of hope, but the Void Monarch's voice was indifferent.

[This Elder was alive seven hundred years ago, but I didn't pay much attention to human martial arts. This Elder's enemies were Yokai and the inhuman, not humans.]

The Void Monarch's disinterest was clear. Lee Sungmin's gaze fell upon the Blood Demon, who stood bare-handed. A weapon... was he not going to use one?

The man held out his loosely open right hand and gestured toward him.

"Since I'm quite old, I'll let you have the first move."

"Shouldn't I be the one yielding to you?"

"I may be old, but I don't think I'm so old that I need you to yield," the Blood Demon said with that same gentle smile.

*Should I start with a probe?* He dismissed any thought of ending this with the first move. His attack had to be a cautious probe, one that left no opening for a counter, yet it could not be half-hearted.

He unleashed a spear technique born from that mindset, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Fifth Form, Death Lightning.

Electric currents flashed, but by then, the spearhead was already aimed at the Blood Demon's chest. The distance between them was meaningless. A single step closed the gap, and the enhanced qi gathering at the spear's tip sought to pierce the Blood Demon's chest even before the metal itself.

"Fast."

The Blood Demon's assessment came slower than the spear itself. His outstretched right hand moved, deflecting the spear's tip as he muttered his evaluation.

Lee Sungmin didn't get greedy. He had expected a response. As he retrieved the deflected spear, he stepped forward again, whipping the shaft around in a two-handed swing. The Changjun on the opposite end became a blunt weapon aimed straight for the Blood Demon. It was a simple motion, but one executed with a generous use of his body, inner qi, and Demonic Energy.

The corner of the Blood Demon's mouth twitched.

KWAAANG!

A pillar of the pavilion vanished without a trace as the Blood Demon leaned back, his body bending like an acrobat's to evade the blow. From that impossible position, his fingertips, wreathed in red enhanced qi, aimed for Lee Sungmin. A chilling premonition pricked at his senses, and he unleashed Gale Thunder. He leaped through the short distance just as the spot where he had stood exploded.

*What kind of martial art is this?*

It was difficult for him to grasp its nature from a single glance. That required a calamity-level insight he simply didn't possess. He had experience, yes, but not the keen eye to match.

*He's not that fast.*

Of that, he was certain. His own speed was far greater, which only made the situation feel stranger. Lee Sungmin swung his entire spear, covering the space with Homing Chain Lightning Flash and Bloody Pursuit. Faced with the relentless offensive, the Blood Demon simply raised both hands. His hands swept across the air before him, and a red wave followed the gesture, pushing all of Lee Sungmin's attacks aside.

"This is not a good place," the Blood Demon muttered.

He was right. The attacks hadn't been nullified, only deflected. They swept across the floor, and the building supporting the pavilion began to collapse.

Lee Sungmin ignored the crumbling structure and leaped into the air. He still hadn't figured out the Blood Demon's martial art, but one thing was clear.

[It's a brutish amount of power.]

Even moving late, the man had torn apart the entire space dominated by the spear's path. His martial art, unlike his calm demeanor, was filled with overwhelming force.

"Look," the Blood Demon said, having flown into the air. He pointed a finger downward. Below, a one-sided battle was unfolding.

The warriors of the Blood Alliance couldn't stop the advance of the Black Dragon Knight and Yana. Here and there, Black Mages tried to cast magic, but their spells couldn't penetrate the barrier Yana had created with her Demonic Energy.

"The Blood Alliance is collapsing. Well... it's not like the organization called the Blood Alliance has any reason to exist anymore, anyway. If you were to join forces with them, you could probably take me down easily. What do you say?"

Instead of answering, he moved. The Blood Demon watched him approach and smiled gently. Though he had made the offer, he would have been in a difficult position if Lee Sungmin had actually joined forces with the others. He had no intention of playing tricks. He genuinely intended to die here. He wanted to fight a superior martial artist, to draw out all his strength and still face an unavoidable defeat. It was a wish any martial artist who had lived for hundreds of years would harbor.

Martial arts. No matter how much time one poured into them, reaching their end was impossible. The Blood Demon knew this better than anyone else in the world. Seven hundred years. He had dedicated seven hundred years to his art. Even after becoming a Vampire, he chose to train in martial arts alone rather than stay by Jeniela's side.

However, he could not advance. It was as if someone had set an absolute limit, a nemesis. No matter how much time he poured into his martial arts theory and training, he could not progress any further. Hundreds of years passed, and the martial arts he had once been proud of became a poison that ate away at his spirit. No matter how much he trained, he couldn't move forward, and he couldn't see the end, yet he didn't want to give up.

Eventually, he came to wish he would rather die fighting, believing it would be a relief to pour out everything he had and finally be done.

*Yes, everything.*

The smile vanished from the Blood Demon's face. At the same time, the energy flowing through his body went wild, and Lee Sungmin's expression hardened.

*All of you.*

The Blood Demon's form disappeared. He couldn't see it with his own eyes. Before he knew it, the man had slipped into a blind spot his vision couldn't cover. Sensing it with his sixth sense, he twisted his upper body and swung his spear to check the approach, but the Blood Demon's body slipped between the spear's trajectory like an intangible ghost. The loosely clenched hand he swung wasn't fast, yet it was still strange.

KWAKWAKWANG!

A series of blows hammered against his Protective Qi Aura, pushing Lee Sungmin far back. Still, he thrust his spear. Nine Dragons Killing Lightning descended with ferocious power. The Blood Demon did not retreat. His martial art was as fluid as before, but his movements were now aggressive. He raised his hands, clenched like claws, above his head.

KWAJIJIJIK!

The descending lightning was torn to shreds by his gesture, and fragments of scattered enhanced qi rained down below. The Blood Demon leaped through it without a second glance. Lee Sungmin didn't back down either, charging to meet him.

The Blood Demon's martial art wasn't flashy, but its power was immense. Seeing his Nine Dragons Slaughter torn apart so easily, Lee Sungmin prepared his next technique. Inner arts and Demonic Energy flowed into the spear. The thrust-out weapon created the Sky Piercing Lightning Strike.

The Blood Demon didn't evade the sky-piercing attack that rushed toward him. The red enhanced qi covering his body grew even more intense, until he became a massive, blinding light leaping toward the strike. He spread his hands wide, his moving gestures creating afterimages.

Tearing it apart.

There was no other way to describe it. The sky-piercing strike, launched with sufficient power, was ripped asunder by the Blood Demon's hands. Having cleared a path, he thrust his hand straight toward Lee Sungmin's chest.

Lee Sungmin didn't retreat. He unleashed the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Two Step Calamity, Three Step Fatality, Four Step Chaos. His extending steps completed the martial art, and the space dominated by his footwork enveloped the Blood Demon with clear killing intent.

"More," the Blood Demon muttered in a low voice, clenching his hands into fists. He twisted his body and swung.

KWAANG!

That single blow, filled with brutish power, shook the space. First, the Two Step Calamity shattered. The Blood Demon took a step forward and swung his other fist.

KWAAANG!

The Three Step Fatality was shattered.

[He's a more exhilarating fellow than he looks!] the Void Monarch chuckled.

Unfazed, Lee Sungmin raised the tip of his spear, but at that moment, the Blood Demon swung his fist once more and shattered the Four Step Chaos.

The Blood Demon was a formidable opponent. Lee Sungmin didn't know his martial art, nor did he know what kind of person the man had been seven hundred years ago, but the divine prowess he now displayed surpassed that of anyone Lee Sungmin had ever fought. Even the Spear King, who was at a far higher level with the spear, couldn't have torn through his martial arts this easily. The Spear King surpassed him in technique, but he was lacking in other areas.

The Blood Demon was different. His martial art seemed monotonous, yet it unerringly targeted every opening. He wasn't just mindlessly strong. His understanding of martial arts was incredibly high, and he freely controlled a body that possessed immense power. If it were night instead of day, Lee Sungmin would not have been able to gain the upper hand, and if there had been a full moon, he would have undoubtedly been defeated.

That's why he didn't understand. How could someone with such a martial arts theory, who had dedicated seven hundred years to his craft, 'only' possess this much power? To Lee Sungmin's senses, the Blood Demon seemed on par with, or slightly stronger than, the Martial God had been when he fought the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. However, he was no match for the Chairman himself.

Was it because the Blood Demon's talent was lacking? Could that render seven hundred years of effort meaningless? He thought of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had died without ever reaching the Transcendent Realm. He also thought of those born with an absurd amount of talent, all those who had reached the Transcendent Realm.

*A limit...*

As if someone had decided, 'This is as far as you go.'

Kim Jonghyun had called this world a Slaughterhouse. Lee Sungmin knew well what that meant. This world, Eria, aimed to summon otherworlders from all dimensions and to gather and develop different types of martial arts and magic into one. This great Slaughterhouse had already opened and closed several times, and whenever it yielded results, it annihilated its guinea pigs in the form of The End. If one became a complete transcendent, they would be exiled from Eria. The Slaughter Predator had said that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman left this world because he had transcended even the transcendent.

*...This is too much.*

In the end, it meant that all beings in this world had a predetermined limit to their growth from the very beginning. Very rarely, exceptions like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman or the Void Monarch occurred. However, most beings could not surpass their limits. Just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon couldn't reach the Transcendent Realm. Just as the Blood Demon, who had lived for seven hundred years, was weaker than the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*Then what about me?*

The thought occurred to him suddenly. By annihilating the Slaughter Predator, Lee Sungmin had been freed from all fate. At the same time, he had obtained the Demonic Energy of the Void Monarch, an exceptional being who had surpassed his limits, and inherited the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Through this, Lee Sungmin had forcibly acquired a 'potential for growth' that would have been impossible with his original talent.

Be yourself.

He recalled the voice of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman that had brushed past his ear when he annihilated Vollander.

The Blood Demon had called his martial art, the Asura Heaven Destruction Art, the world's number one seven hundred years ago. That was a story from another time. Having shattered even the Four Step Chaos, the Blood Demon walked through the sky and approached Lee Sungmin. In the current era, there was no place for the Asura Heaven Destruction Art in a discussion of the world's best. From deep within Lee Sungmin's body, Demonic Energy was released.

"World number one," Lee Sungmin muttered in a low voice as the Blood Demon extended a hand wreathed in crimson light toward him.

Demonic Energy completely enveloped the spear, and the space around it made a creaking sound. He had used this technique once before, on the way back to Haras, when he first fought the Spear King. He had failed to execute it completely then, as his incomplete body couldn't handle the power it demanded.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

It was the martial art given to him by Wiji Hoyeon and refined by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Even if Lee Sungmin himself wasn't the world's number one, the martial art he possessed was. It had been touched by the hands of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, history's strongest.

The space shimmered, and a violet mist swirled.

The Illusionary World opened.
