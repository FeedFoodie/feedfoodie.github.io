"Sylphy..."

The name died on Rubia's horrified lips. Before she could utter another sound, a massive gust of wind, thick with killing intent, swept over her. The attack was impossibly fast, a blur of force that threatened to tear her body to shreds. In that instant, Rubia was helpless, completely unaware of what fate awaited her.

Lee Sungmin moved, stepping in front of her. He raised his spear and thrust it past Rubia to meet the oncoming wind.

PWAK!

The gale was torn apart. Ripped into dozens of streams, it scattered in every direction before coalescing back at its source.

"Who is that?" Lee Sungmin asked Rubia. The figure's face was obscured by the raging wind, but the voice that carried on it was distinctly male.

"S-Sylphid," she stammered. "He's the most powerful of the wind Elementals, the Spirit King of Wind."

"A Spirit Queen and now a Spirit King," Lee Sungmin grumbled, taking another step forward. "What a mess."

Wind, water, fire, and earth were the four great representative spirits among the Elementals. Among them were the four most powerful Spirit Kings, and ruling over those kings and all other Elementals was the Spirit Queen.

[Are you... human? No, you can't possibly be human,] Sylphid muttered, looking down at Lee Sungmin as the wind gathered around him. All the gales blowing through the vast mountain range converged on his form. Lee Sungmin grabbed his swirling hair with one hand, slightly regretting that he had not tied it up sooner.

[Rubia. Her Majesty has not yet held you accountable for the sin of failing to protect her lover, and yet you have betrayed Her Majesty first.]

"W-Wait, Sylphid. I didn't intend to betray..."

[Do not make excuses,] Sylphid spat with a sneer. [You blabbered things you shouldn't have to that insolent, inhuman monster who intends to harm Her Majesty, and you claim it's not betrayal? The moment I saw you here and learned of what you've done, all Elementals learned of your betrayal.]

Within the massive storm, Sylphid rose fully to his feet. Lee Sungmin tilted his head back, following the Spirit King's rising form as Scarlet, Baek Sogo, and Yana gathered at his side.

Scarlet ran both hands through her hair. "The Spirit King of Wind," she muttered. "Are we really fighting Elementals now?"

[This is not a fight. It is a punishment!] Sylphid's stern shout cut through the air as if he had heard her. [Though I may have made my Advent here alone, I possess more than enough power to kill all of you!]

"He says he's alone." Lee Sungmin glanced at Rubia, noting how Sylphid had volunteered information he had not even asked for. "Please step back."

"B-But..."

"Who are you worried about? Me? Or that Spirit King of Wind? In this situation, Lady Rubia, the only one you should be worried about is yourself. Or would you rather die here at his hands?"

Rubia was at a loss for words. While she hesitated, Lee Sungmin turned to Yana, Scarlet, and Baek Sogo. "Please step back as well. I'll leave Lady Rubia in your care."

"Are you sure you don't need help?" Yana asked.

RUUUUMBLE!

The wind swirling around Sylphid had become a massive storm, and the sound roaring from within it was murderous.

"I'll give it a try first," Lee Sungmin replied as if it were nothing. Baek Sogo watched him with worried eyes, but he offered her a faint smile. "There shouldn't be any major problems."

[What an arrogant bastard,] Sylphid's displeasure was palpable, having heard their entire exchange. His body was made of wind and his face held no expression, but his voice dripped with contempt.

Lee Sungmin simply raised his spear toward Sylphid's faintly visible form. Having fully risen, the Spirit King was the size of a small mountain. Lee Sungmin looked up at the colossal figure and tightened his grip on his weapon.

[You're being quite proactive. Are you that confident?]

*That's part of it,* Lee Sungmin answered the Void Monarch in his head, lowering his stance. *She said that Wiji Hoyeon... fought the Spirit Queen.*

[She said they fought for a full fortnight, but there was no conclusion.]

*I know I'm not stronger than Wiji Hoyeon right now. It's been ten years since I obtained the body of the Slaughter Predator, but my martial arts haven't progressed much.*

He was keenly aware of this reality. Though his body was complete and the harmony between his inner qi and Demonic Energy had stabilized, his martial arts had barely improved. He had, after all, been asleep for a decade.

Wiji Hoyeon was different. Her talent was so absurd that even the Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance had to acknowledge it. With the help of the Demonic Spirit, the ten years she had spent training would have elevated her to an unfathomable level. A decade ago, Lee Sungmin had won their duel, but during the year they spent together in the forest, she had already risen to equal him. Now, she must have reached a place far beyond his own.

*If Wiji Hoyeon couldn't defeat the Spirit Queen, then it's impossible for me to do so. However... I should be able to defeat a Spirit King, who is said to be her inferior. No, I must defeat him.*

[It's turned out just as you wished, to some extent,] the Void Monarch chuckled. [Even back then, you wanted Wiji Hoyeon to be stronger than you. You wanted to chase after her back.]

*To some extent.*

[Ten years have passed, and it has come to be. Unlike you, who has stagnated, Wiji Hoyeon has advanced far ahead.]

*It's good to have a clear goal.*

Inside the storm, Sylphid raised both his hands.

*Because it makes me want to surpass it.*

Lee Sungmin kicked off the ground, soaring to a dizzying height in an instant. His unnatural speed surprised Sylphid, but the Spirit King did not panic. Instead, he unleashed all the wind he had gathered. The lake water surged out in a great wave as the Moon Palace was shattered by the gale.

Yana spread her nine tails to envelop Scarlet, Baek Sogo, and Rubia. Unflustered, Scarlet deployed a magic barrier to reinforce the defense.

Facing the violent storm, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with both hands. He twisted his waist sharply, swinging the weapon in a single fluid motion.

KWAARRRRR!

The swing itself was a thunderclap. A violet current flashed and exploded, sending forked lightning spreading in all directions to tear through the wind. Lee Sungmin stepped on the empty air and pressed forward.

KIGIGIGIK!

The spear in his hand began to spin furiously, gathering the scattered electrical currents back to its tip. The technique was completed in an instant. The seventh form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, sky-piercing, was a technique that originally required time to concentrate and gather sufficient power. Not anymore. His overflowing Demonic Energy and inner qi rose and moved the moment he willed them, allowing him to execute the technique instantly.

The spear trembled in his hands. Lee Sungmin drew both arms far back and then shot the weapon forward.

KWAAAANG!

Sky-piercing exploded. A lightning strike, condensed with tremendous power, shot into the center of the storm. Sylphid gasped at its force as the wind, gathered at a single point, swelled violently. A deafening sound erupted. The torn wind scattered in all directions, and a violet current wrapped around the entire peak. A large bolt of electricity shot out and pierced through the nearby mountains, completely obliterating their tips.

This was the result of a single swing, unleashed without regulating his inner qi and Demonic Energy as he had against the Spear King.

[This is absurd!]

Sylphid's body staggered from the blow. Being made of wind, the hole punched through him quickly filled back in, but he cried out in shock.

JJIJIJIK!

Lee Sungmin operated the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and the Violet Dawn Divine Art in tandem. He knew that chipping away at the wind from the outside would not be enough to land a direct blow on Sylphid. He did not even know if it was possible to kill an Elemental completely. Reverse-summoning, however, was possible. It meant dealing enough damage that the Elemental could no longer exist in this world.

A Protective Qi Aura enveloped his entire body. Without hesitation, he plunged into the storm.

[Foolish bastard!] Sylphid sneered. From his perspective, Lee Sungmin's actions were suicidal, even with a Protective Qi Aura.

KAGAGAGAK!

Sharp winds assailed the protective aura. They said the eye of a storm was calm, but that did not apply to the one Sylphid had created. Dense gales that could tear anything apart focused on Lee Sungmin, yet the Protective Qi Aura did not tear. His endless inner qi and Demonic Energy had formed a defensive wall sturdier than anything in the world.

Maintaining his balance was difficult. The massive winds relentlessly pushed and staggered his body, but it did not matter. Even as he struggled to stay centered, the arms holding the spear did not waver.

Inner arts and Demonic Energy concentrated in his weapon. He unleashed two techniques at once, the fourth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Nine Dragons Killing Lightning, and the fifth form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Blood Shadow Hundred Flashes.

KWAAAANG!

A great explosion erupted from the heart of the storm as nine dragons wreathed in violet electricity shot out in all directions. The cyclone burst apart, the wind scattering into chaos. Amid the raging energies, Lee Sungmin calmly prepared his next attack.

His vision was a blur of scattered wind, inner qi, and Demonic Energy. He slowly pulled back his outstretched spear and thrust it forward once more.

The sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Void Path Exploding Thunder.

A small hole formed where he aimed. The hole expanded in an instant, a violet current seething within it.

KWAJIJIJIK!

The seething current exploded, instantly annihilating the wind that enveloped Sylphid. The Spirit King's wind-form staggered greatly, too shocked to even speak. Lee Sungmin kicked off the ground and rushed toward him.

[You bastard!] Sylphid roared in a fit of rage, raising both his hands. Even without the help of other wind Elementals, he was the Spirit King of Wind. All the scattered gales gathered into his hands, forming a giant hammer. He realized, albeit too late, that he could not face this absurd monster by simply clawing at him with the wind.

It was a late realization.

When Sylphid swung the hammer, he caused a calamity. A world-sweeping storm converged on a single point and descended upon Lee Sungmin. Just before the path of the Void Path was blocked, Lee Sungmin saw the hammer coming straight for him. The Protective Qi Aura covering his body swelled greatly.

He took one, two, three steps without a foothold. The Three Steps Certain Kill Heavenly Roar pushed back the wind that preceded the hammer. One more step created the Four-Step Madness White Thunder. The thunderclap that had rung out several times before now echoed louder than ever, and the hammer of wind in Sylphid's hand vanished without a trace.

Having lost his weapon, Sylphid roared and swung his hands. The wind was endless, blowing with even his slightest gesture. As the Spirit King of Wind, each wave of his hand created a small storm. All the wind on this mountain was his weapon.

Lee Sungmin's weapons were a single spear and his body.

Nevertheless, it was Lee Sungmin who was overwhelming him. Electricity gathered at the tip of his spear, swelling until he became a Thunder God in the space he occupied. The same power that had annihilated Vollander manifested once again. Lee Sungmin looked at Sylphid with golden eyes.

There was nothing he wanted to ask him. There was no reason to hesitate.

Myriad Thunder tore the storm apart with lightning. The gathered thundercloud filled the space, and at the center of the ceaselessly exploding lightning, Sylphid could no longer summon the wind. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear toward the Spirit King and calmly declared, "Genesis."

It was the end.

KWARRRRRRR!

The wind collapsed, returning to where it was supposed to be. Lee Sungmin stood in the middle of the evaporated lake, watching the gales disperse. Annihilation or reverse-summoning, it did not matter which. What was important was that from this moment on, Sylphid would never again be able to appear in this world.

"It's a good thing Sylphid appeared," Lee Sungmin said, his voice cutting through the sudden quiet. "Lady Rubia has now become a traitor and can no longer return to the Spirit Queen. Perhaps you might think of returning with the intention of committing suicide. If that happens, I will not let you go."

Rubia stared at him with a devastated expression, unable to believe what she had just witnessed. The Spirit King of Wind, one of the most powerful Elementals in the Elemental Realm, second only to the queen, had been so futilely reverse-summoned, even if it had been a sudden Advent.

"L-Lee Sungmin... just what are you..."

"Things are about to get busy," he muttered, slinging the spear onto his back. "First, we head to the Golden Magic Tower."

"The Golden Magic Tower? Why there?"

"I need to see Lloyd."

With those words, Lee Sungmin turned and left the ruins behind.
