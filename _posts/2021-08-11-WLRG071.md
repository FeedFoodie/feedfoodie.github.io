"Welcome!"

A cheerful greeting met them as they stepped inside. The restaurant was already bustling with the lunchtime crowd, it being just past noon. Scarlet's eyes sparkled as she took in the interior, which possessed a unique ambiance that was as distinct from other cities as Bredon itself.

A waitress in a qipao with a thigh-high slit guided them to an empty table on the second floor. She smiled brightly at them.

"Is this your first time in Bredon?" she asked.

"Ah, yeah, it is," Lee Sungmin replied.

"I knew it! In that case, the food here might not suit your palate. Many of the people living in this city are Otherworlders, and even those who aren't often have ancestors who were."

The waitress explained this as she handed them a menu. Her words prompted him to look around, and he saw she was right. There were quite a few people of mixed heritage among the patrons.

"We also get a lot of martial artists from the Central Plains," she continued. "Because of that, we have many dishes that cater to their tastes. The gentleman might be fine, but the lady might find the food doesn't agree with her."

She glanced at Scarlet as she spoke. Although Lee Sungmin was not from the murim, he was an Easterner, much like the people of the Central Plains. Scarlet, however, looked every bit a Westerner, which seemed to be the source of the waitress's misunderstanding.

"I don't think there's any food I can't eat… hmm. It is my first time eating Central Plains cuisine, though," Scarlet muttered, flipping through the menu.

The waitress giggled at her words. "Central Plains food uses a lot of spices, so the flavors are very strong. Shall I recommend some of the milder options?"

"Ah, yes. Please do," Scarlet said with a nod.

Since Lee Sungmin was also unfamiliar with the cuisine, they chose from the dishes the waitress recommended.

"What are you going to do when you get to Shaolin?" Scarlet asked once they were alone.

"I'm not sure," he admitted. "I suppose I'll have to see when I get there."

"I don't know much about martial arts, but isn't Shaolin a really famous place, even among the murim sects?"

"That's true, but I don't know much about Shaolin either. All I have is what I've heard from rumors."

Over their months of travel, Lee Sungmin had explained his reason for heading to Shaolin. Hearing his uncertain reply now, Scarlet simply shrugged.

"You came all this way just to return some martial arts manuals. Don't you think they'll do something more for you?"

"I wonder. I heard the martial arts aren't that valuable. I think they'll give me something out of courtesy, but honestly, I have no idea what it will be."

Nebul had suggested they might give him at least a Minor Cycle Pill, but Lee Sungmin doubted things would work out so well.

"More importantly," Scarlet began, her expression souring. "This restaurant. The service is decent, but it's full of dung flies."

She crossed her legs and folded her arms. Lee Sungmin offered a bitter smile at her choice of words. Contrary to her elegant appearance, Scarlet had a penchant for cheap words and profanity that felt entirely out of place. This time, however, he had to agree with her assessment.

"I see," he murmured.

He felt the gazes. They were trying to be discreet, but both he and Scarlet could clearly sense the eyes directed their way. From the moment they had been led to their table, most of the martial artists on the second floor had been sending them furtive glances. It was not simply because they were outsiders. It was more than that.

*He's still immature.*

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue in thought. He was still unskilled at hiding his inner arts. A true master who reached the realm of the Supreme Peak would have completed their Origin Reversal and achieved Ascend to the Peak. At that level, the harmony between their inner arts and physical body would become so perfect that their achievements would be impossible to discern from the outside, no matter how profound their martial skill.

It was a distant realm for the current Lee Sungmin. He had not even reached the stage of Extreme Temperature Immunity, let alone Origin Reversal. While the Great and Minor Cycle Pills had deepened his inner arts, his proficiency with the Violet Dawn Divine Art that regulated them was still far too low. He could now create enhanced qi, but his overall control remained immature.

As a result, his aura overflowed no matter how much he tried to suppress it. It was why any martial artist of a certain level could not help but be aware of his presence.

"Huhu! Dung flies, she says."

Scarlet's grumbling had not been quiet, and the words had carried to those nearby. A young man sitting by the window slowly rose to his feet. Dressed in indigo martial arts robes, he looked at Lee Sungmin and Scarlet with narrowed eyes.

"The beautiful young lady has a foul mouth, contrary to her looks. No. Perhaps that makes her even more charming?"

"Listen to this bastard talk. Did you slather your throat with butter?" Scarlet muttered with a scoff.

Lee Sungmin felt a bit awkward, thinking she had gone too far. The face of the young man who had spoken to her stiffened.

"…Indeed. A beautiful flower has thorns…"

"You crazy bastard. Did you learn how to talk from a novel?"

Scarlet burst out laughing before he could finish his muttering. At her question, the young man's stiff face turned beet red. He let out a ragged breath and strode toward their table. Lee Sungmin sighed as he watched, pulling his chair back.

"Stop."

The voice came from another direction, just before Lee Sungmin could step in. The young man's advance halted.

"Young Master Bi Cheonghak. If your eyes aren't knotholes, it would be best for you to withdraw the killing intent you're harboring and quietly sit back down to continue your meal."

The speaker was another young man, who had been drinking tea by the opposite window. At his words, Bi Cheonghak stopped, his face contorting.

"If you know my name, you must also know what I'm called, don't you?"

"This is why titles are no good. They're just nicknames someone slaps on you, but then some piece of shit with no real skill starts acting like he's as great as his title."

"Piece… piece of shit? What?"

"I said you're a piece of shit. Elegant White Sword Bi Cheonghak. Or what. Are you trusting in your shitty skills and that title, or is it the power of the Bi Clan, which is quite influential in this area?"

As the young man spoke with a sneer, Bi Cheonghak's eyes grew cold. He caressed the sword at his waist and opened his lips.

"Your insults have crossed the line. Can you handle the consequences?"

"Young Master Bi Cheonghak, I'm saying this for your own good, so please listen carefully. If you want to discuss whether you can handle it, shave your head, take your family's entire fortune to Mount Song, and beg them to take you as a disciple. Then, after becoming a disciple of Shaolin, you should meditate facing a wall for fifty years. No matter what a piece of shit you are, if you take up Shaolin's martial arts and train for fifty years, you might gain the skill to have such a discussion."

The young man said this with a laugh. It was a mocking tone for anyone to hear, and Bi Cheonghak's face twisted in fury. He could no longer hold back and was about to draw his sword.

"Don't draw it."

The young man's words stopped Bi Cheonghak's action. It was not just the words that stopped him. The profound energy imbued in the young man's voice froze Bi Cheonghak's body.

"If you draw that sword, something irreversible will happen. Your less-than-impressive skills will hardly be able to protect your life, and the same goes for your title of Elegant White Sword. The power of the Bi Clan is no different."

"Who… who are you?"

Cold sweat trickled down Bi Cheonghak's forehead. The words and the energy in the young man's voice far transcended his own level. The young man let out another laugh.

"You're asking that now? Seeing what you were doing, your eyes really must be knotholes."

"I asked who you are…!"

"Namgung Heewon."

The young man answered. A deathly pallor washed over Bi Cheonghak's face. He was not the only one. Most of the customers on the second floor, who had been listening with interest, were shocked. Lee Sungmin felt the same jolt.

A martial artist with the surname Namgung belonged to one of the prestigious clans. Of course, not everyone with that name was a direct descendant or a great master. Namgung Heewon, however, was a celebrity so famous that few martial artists had not heard of him.

Naturally, Lee Sungmin knew the name. The Sword Dragon Namgung Heewon. He was the greatest prodigy of the Namgung Clan, a man who had been making a name for himself even before Lee Sungmin's death in his past life. He was also the only son of the Namgung Clan's patriarch and the number one master within the Noble Clan, the Heavenly Venerable Sword King Namgung Baek. In other words, he was a golden spoon who possessed incredible skill and was backed by the immense power of the Namgung Clan.

"Uh… ugh…"

Although the Bi Clan wielded considerable power in this area, it was nothing compared to the Namgung Clan. Bi Cheonghak backed away, his expression like he had just chewed on dung.

"You should be glad this is a city ruled by Shaolin. What are you doing? Just sit down and continue your meal."

At Namgung Heewon's command, Bi Cheonghak obediently returned to his seat. Lee Sungmin watched him for a moment before turning his gaze to Namgung Heewon. When their eyes met, Namgung Heewon smiled warmly and stood up.

"Why is he coming over here now?" Scarlet grumbled.

Namgung Heewon approached their table, cupped his fist in his palm toward them, and bowed his head.

"Please don't think I was meddling unnecessarily. Wouldn't it be a pity for the employees working at this establishment if they had to clean up a corpse?"

"A corpse? What do you mean?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Namgung Heewon burst into a hearty laugh. "If that clueless piece of shit young master had come over and tried something stupid, wouldn't he have become a corpse?"

"I wasn't thinking of that."

"Don't lie when you were about to unleash your killing intent just a moment ago."

Lee Sungmin smacked his lips at Namgung Heewon's words. He had not intended to kill the man, but it was true that he had planned to give Bi Cheonghak a considerable threat if he had started a fight.

"If you don't mind, may I join you?" Namgung Heewon asked.

"Weren't you finished with your meal?"

"I can always eat more if I want to."

"Do you want to eat? Or do you want to talk?" Scarlet cut in.

Even though he was from the Namgung Clan, Scarlet did not seem to care at all. Her words could have been seen as utterly rude, but Namgung Heewon paid them no mind.

"Of course, I want to talk."

"Then sit. You're paying for the food."

"Haha!"

Namgung Heewon laughed loudly as he pulled out a chair and sat next to Lee Sungmin. Lee Sungmin let out a dry cough, feeling the blatant stare from his side. Judging by the gaze, Namgung Heewon's interest was not in Scarlet, but in him.

"What are you staring at?"

"No, it's just fascinating. You haven't reached Origin Reversal, nor have you undergone Rejuvenation. That would mean you are as old as you look, unless you've learned some youth-retaining arts. I'm curious, how old are you?"

"I'm eighteen."

"I see. To be on the verge of the Supreme Peak at eighteen… haha! That's truly remarkable. I've seen many, many talents, and I've always thought of myself as a great talent."

Namgung Heewon muttered, his eyes narrowing as he studied Lee Sungmin.

"I'm asking just in case."

"What is it?"

"Is your title the Little Heavenly Demon?"

Lee Sungmin almost spat out the tea he was drinking. He never imagined he would hear that title here, and from Namgung Heewon's lips of all people. He could only stare back with a dumbfounded expression.

"The Little Heavenly Demon?"

"The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. A young monster said to possess tremendous martial skill despite not even reaching adulthood, but seeing your reaction, it seems you're not the Little Heavenly Demon."

It was the first time he had heard about Wiji Hoyeon from someone else. Considering the timing, however, this was about when the wandering Wiji Hoyeon would have settled down and begun to seriously build her reputation. Of course, that was the Wiji Hoyeon from his past life, the one who had never met him.

"And there's a story that the Little Heavenly Demon is a woman," Namgung Heewon added.

"…A woman?"

"There are rumors that she's a woman disguised as a man. Some people just say she's a woman."

That part alone was different from his memories. The Wiji Hoyeon of his past life had never been suspected of being a woman. It seemed the current Wiji Hoyeon did not insist on appearing as a man.

"Well, anyway. It seems you're not the Little Heavenly Demon…"

Namgung Heewon paused, peering into Lee Sungmin's face.

"Then who are you?"
