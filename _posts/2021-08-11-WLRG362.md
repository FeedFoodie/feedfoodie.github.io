As Blood Demon leaped into the light of creation, his Asura Sky-Rending Dance crashed against the pinnacle of martial arts. In that blinding moment of impact, he knew.

He understood the profound martial theory contained within that single thrust and knew his own technique would not be enough.

*I cannot withstand this.*

Having finally reached the moment he had longed for, Blood Demon calmly accepted the truth. After struggling on the path of martial arts for seven hundred years, he knew better than anyone that struggling against the inevitable was useless.

That did not mean he would give up, however. Even knowing it was futile, he had to struggle now. His goal was not to avoid death, but to feel the full, crushing weight of that attack.

Without hesitation, he thrust his hand forward. A power that could easily collapse a mountain resided in that single blow, yet it never manifested. It was not a collision so much as an unmaking. His enhanced qi scattered, his fingertips crumbled, and still, there was no pain.

Blood Demon stubbornly pushed his arm all the way into the light, watching as it completely disintegrated into dust.

*More...*

He moved his other arm, swinging his tightly clenched fist with all his might, but his fist could not pierce the light. Before the radiance of the pinnacle of martial arts, the power he wielded was clumsy and weak. After both his arms had crumbled away, Blood Demon lifted his foot without a second thought, using every means at his disposal to press the attack.

[Stop.]

It was Void Monarch who warned him.

The light vanished. Lee Sungmin felt a hollow emptiness in his qi center for the first time in a long while. His seemingly endless inner qi and Demonic Energy were almost completely depleted. He took a deep, shuddering breath, his gaze falling to the trembling spear in his hand. It still felt heavy. The final form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

Pinnacle of martial arts.

He had performed it despite the risk of qi deviation, but the dreaded backlash had not occurred. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard as he watched Blood Demon's body fall, the death trap that had surrounded him now gone. His phenomenal immortality could not regenerate his wounds. The ultimate technique, into which the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had ambitiously poured his insights, contained not only the essence of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear but also the entirety of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

The exhaustion crushing his body felt the same as when he had first used Genesis.

THUD.

Blood Demon's body crashed to the ground. He gasped for breath and looked up, feeling nothing in his arms or legs. He shifted his gaze to the side. His missing limbs were not regenerating, and yet, the internal injuries and minor fractures from the fall healed in an instant.

*This is interesting.*

Even in a state where he could no longer fight, Blood Demon let out a small laugh as he assessed his body's condition. Everything he saw and experienced was also transmitted to Jeniela, who would now learn just how powerful Lee Sungmin's final attack truly was. An attack that negated a Vampire's immortality. Should he call it a type of curse? Blood Demon glanced at the wounds from which not even a drop of blood flowed. He was curious why they were not regenerating, but it was not his role to investigate and find the answer.

"Congratulations," Blood Demon said to Lee Sungmin, who had just landed on the ground.

Lee Sungmin steadied his trembling breath. He had executed the pinnacle of martial arts. He could use it now. He had achieved true Spear Unification and finally understood the weight of the spear. The martial theory behind the technique was impossible to execute without following its precise form. It was a martial art too far beyond him to be used freely. Moreover, the aftermath was just like the first time he had used Genesis. At his current level, using it would force him into a state of utter exhaustion.

[To be like this even after getting a complete Yokai's body. You pathetic bastard.]

*It's less a problem with my body... and more a problem with myself.*

[You know that much, at least. It's because you're so thick-headed that you can't properly understand the martial art you just performed.]

*Then do you understand it?*

[How would I understand it when I'm not the one who learned it? I just accept it for what it is. It's the same as with that crazy bastard, the Spear King. A crow-tit trying to follow a stork will tear its legs apart. With the martial arts theory you possess, you can't fully execute the pinnacle of martial arts. At best, you can only imitate it by following the form.]

*...That power just now... was an imitation...?*

[It was a pretty good imitation. You're just roughly copying the method without knowing how to use it properly, aren't you?]

He could not deny those words. If he had not understood the form of the martial art, he would not have been able to perform it at all. Was his martial theory that lacking? Then what about his body? The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was said to have undergone Body Reformation once more after entering the Transcendent Realm, an experience Lee Sungmin had not yet had.

"You look like you have a lot on your mind."

Blood Demon, sprawled on the ground, somehow managed to push himself into a sitting position. He glanced at his missing arms and legs.

"You look exhausted. I take it you can't control it completely yet?"

"...Your wounds aren't regenerating."

"It's astonishing," Blood Demon replied with a hearty laugh. "This is the first time I've experienced this since becoming a Vampire."

Lee Sungmin felt a strange sensation watching him laugh so boldly in such a wretched state.

"Do you have no lingering attachment to life?"

"I've lived too long to feel it."

"You could have run away."

"Why run when I have no attachments?"

"If I interrogate you, will you answer?"

"An oath won't compel me," Blood Demon said with a grin. "If you tell me to speak the truth, I'll just lie. You know perfectly well what happens then, don't you?"

At those words, Lee Sungmin could only smile bitterly. He had no lingering attachment to life. He wished for death. If he died, the power he had achieved would not disappear but would instead return to Jeniela. In this situation, forcing an oath was impossible.

"You have no intention of answering willingly, do you?"

"None," Blood Demon answered without hesitation.

Lee Sungmin approached him. In that case, he had no more business with Blood Demon. The limbless vampire looked up as Lee Sungmin stood right in front of him.

"You're no fool," Blood Demon said with a shrug. "You must know that if you kill me, my power will return to Jeniela. So, I figured you had two options. Either sealing me... or eating me. In terms of efficiency, eating me is obviously better. Which is it?"

"Eating you."

"I see."

Blood Demon nodded and closed his eyes. He felt a little disappointed that he could not return to Jeniela. *Will you be disappointed too?* he thought, picturing Jeniela in the North. Still, he had granted her request to the very end. Even as an adoptive father, he had done everything he could for her. Blood Demon smiled in satisfaction.

[It would be best to eat his heart.]

*Do I really have to eat it like that?*

[Even if you don't like it, it's the better way. Jeniela probably... already knows what happened here. After all, Blood Demon is a Vampire who originated from her. What he's seeing right now has likely flowed to Jeniela as well. Do you understand what that means?]

*That my power has been exposed.*

He already knew that much.

[That might have been Blood Demon's aim. He provoked you into using everything you had. Jeniela used him as a disposable piece to gauge your full strength. This will be quite a disadvantage in your future fight against her.]

Even knowing that, he had no choice. It was more advantageous to kill Blood Demon here. In the end, it was just an exchange. Lee Sungmin killed one of Jeniela's greatest assets, and Jeniela sacrificed Blood Demon to grasp Lee Sungmin's full power.

[The reason I told you not to go to the Fairy Forest is because there's a chance it could be exposed.]

*Jeniela has probably already figured out that I'm using the Fairy Forest as my base.*

[There's no need to give her confirmation.]

Listening to Void Monarch, Lee Sungmin reached his hand toward Blood Demon. He agreed with the advice. There was no need to give her confirmation. Even if he disliked it, even if it was unfamiliar, he had to force himself to do it. A heart. His heart.

He was glad he had not brought Baek Sogo with him.

{sep}

Jeniela closed her eyes. The world she saw, the world Blood Demon was seeing, began to blur.

Even in the moment his chest was torn open and his heart was pulled out, Blood Demon did not scream. He was feeling only satisfaction and a sense of accomplishment. He had wished for death, and today, he had achieved it. His vision blurred completely. Watching the heart enter Lee Sungmin's mouth, Jeniela let out a small laugh.

"You've changed a lot."

*He won't hear me from here, though,* she muttered to herself. As long as he possessed the Black Heart, devouring another's heart was likely the fastest way to increase his power. Besides, now that he had become a complete Yokai, the risk of his Demonic Energy running rampant from the act was gone.

*Still, I didn't think he'd actually eat it.*

Even though he became a Yokai, his mind was still human. She had thought he would feel revulsion at the act of devouring and refuse. He was different from what she expected, however. That was just a little surprising. Jeniela stopped watching as Blood Demon's vision went completely dark.

Blood Demon was dead.

Normally, the power he possessed should have returned to her, but it did not.

"That damn Heart Thief is different from Aine," Preskan grumbled. "Aine's body is structured around the Black Heart, so she can directly add what she devours to her own power, but the Heart Thief can't. He was human to begin with. Even with a Yokai's body, he can't achieve the same efficiency as Aine."

That was the biggest difference between Aine, a Chimera, and Lee Sungmin. Even if they had the same heart, the composition of their bodies was different.

"Father is dead," Gemini, who was following behind Jeniela, spoke up. "Aren't you sad, Queen? He was with us for seven hundred years, after all. He was the vampire you made after me. When we were weak, he was the one who protected us so we wouldn't die."

"It's not like I told him to die," Jeniela said, glancing back at Gemini. Seeing the girl's bared-teeth smile, Jeniela reached out and ruffled her hair. "Father was the one who wanted to die. It would have been nice if he had died a little later, with the rest of us, but what can you do if he didn't want that?"

"That's just his personality," Gemini said. "Even after hundreds of years, he was still a martial artist, so he loved to fight."

"Thanks to him, we learned what we needed to. It's a relief we found out now."

Jeniela genuinely thought so. An attack that ignored even a Vampire's regenerative power and carved wounds that struck the soul itself. If she were to take such an attack head-on, even she would not escape unscathed. At that thought, Jeniela felt a chilling thrill.

"What are you going to do?" Gemini asked. "That human, he's gotten too strong. He was a pain even when he was alone, but now he has more allies."

"That's true. It seems Silent Flash has recovered... and there's the Nine-Tailed Fox and the Crimson Archmage. From the rumors, it looks like even the Saint from the Church has joined him."

"Are you going to leave him be?" Gemini asked with a curious expression.

Jeniela giggled at the question. "I don't think I need to step in and kill him right now."

"Why not?"

"Because I'm not his only enemy."

Lee Sungmin had many enemies. As long as his goal was to stop The End, he was bound to have many more. The Spirit Queen was not dead, and there was also the Spirit who controlled the Martial God like a puppet.

"So it's fine." Jeniela looked down at Aine, who was beside her. Having devoured the Spirit King of Flame, she listened to the conversation, thinking of the man who had a heart just like her own. Jeniela smiled warmly at Aine.

"For now, we should finish what we were doing."

Their destination was not yet in sight.

"I wonder if the Sword Immortal will be tasty?" Aine tilted her head and muttered.
