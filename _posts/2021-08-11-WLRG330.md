Lee Sungmin's original plan had been simple. He would stop in Urmuri before continuing to the Hugel Mountains to meet the Demonic Spirit, as the two locations were not far apart, yet, even without blindly trusting Laplace's words, he had already abandoned the idea of visiting the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. He had finally broken free from the chains of fate, and the last thing he wanted was to become entangled with the Demonic Spirit and be swept into a destiny not of his own choosing.

He clicked his tongue, the image of the vanished Laplace flashing in his mind. *You didn't tell me the most important thing.* He knew what Wiji Hoyeon was doing, wandering the world and conquering Dungeons for a full ten years, but Laplace had failed to mention her crucial location.

A bitter thought followed. *Wiji Hoyeon must think I'm dead, too.*

It wasn't just her. Everyone who had ever known him would believe him gone. Soon, however, rumors would begin to circulate, carried by the people he had saved in Germud. Lee Sungmin had no intention of hiding. He knew Scarlet's location for certain, but Wiji Hoyeon and Baek Sogo remained lost to him. After being considered a dead man for a decade, he needed to make it undeniably clear that he was alive.

After a night spent outdoors, Lee Sungmin set off at the first light of dawn. Two days of travel brought him before the gates of Urmuri. A pang of regret struck him. A Fairy Horse would have shortened the journey considerably. He passed through the gates and into the Monster City, a place he had not seen in ten years. The last time he had walked these streets, he was half Yokai. Now, he was whole.

Perhaps that was why the eyes of nearly every Yokai in the city turned to him. One Yokai recognizes another. They could sense the immense Demonic Energy rolling off him in waves, a power unlike anything they had ever encountered in their city. It was only natural that they stared.

Moving stealthily with so many eyes upon him would only arouse more suspicion, so Lee Sungmin crossed the streets of Urmuri at a steady, unhurried pace. His destination was the Grand Elegance Palace, the high-class Fairy Realm where Yana resided. A decade had passed, and he questioned whether she would still be here, but the palace itself had not vanished.

A Yokai guard stopped him at the entrance. "What brings you here?" the guard asked, his posture cautious.

"I'm here to see Yana," Lee Sungmin answered, making no effort to hide his purpose.

"Wha... What?" The guard stammered.

Ten years ago, Yana's presence in the Grand Elegance Palace had been a closely guarded secret, and it seemed nothing had changed. She still avoided Urmuri's external affairs, her location a mystery to most.

[Please come inside.]

Yana's voice echoed directly in his mind. Without a word, Lee Sungmin walked past the gatekeeper, who could only hesitate as he passed. Guided by her telepathy, he entered a separate annex. Through a pure white paper screen door, he could see her silhouette. He slid it open slowly.

"It's been a long time," Yana said, her beauty as unchanged as ever.

Her shining golden eyes looked up at him. As she perceived his complete transformation, a low gasp escaped her lips.

"You've become a Yokai."

Lee Sungmin remained silent.

"...How strange," she murmured. "When a human becomes a Yokai, their values completely collapse. Their very personality becomes twisted..."

"Sometimes things just happen," he replied, having no intention of explaining his circumstances to her.

Yana was friendly, but only because of the Void Monarch. She was a powerful Yokai, a Nine-Tailed Fox whose strength rivaled even Jeniela's. Worse, her power came directly from the Demonic Spirit of the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. For Lee Sungmin, her very existence was an uncomfortable reminder of what he sought to avoid.

"...Ten years," Yana said softly. "It took a long time."

Those words confirmed it. The world believed him dead, but Yana knew he had been Sealed. Only Frau, who had performed the Sealing, should have known that.

"When you were Sealed in Germud," she continued, "I lamented that I didn't go with Frau. If I had been there, my power could have resolved the situation before you became a Yokai and had to be Sealed."

"You weren't sad for me," Lee Sungmin countered, "but for the Void Monarch being Sealed, weren't you?"

Yana's silence was an honest answer. He smiled bitterly and sat down opposite her.

"...The Void Monarch is still inside me."

At his words, Yana's face brightened with sincere devotion. The Void Monarch, however, grumbled in his mind, clearly displeased.

[What's the point? I can't even eat it.]

*Eat? What?*

[What do you think? That luscious body, of course. If you want, why don't you devour Yana in this Elder's stead?]

*Shut up.*

Lee Sungmin ignored the cackling voice in his head. "I came to ask you something," he said, cutting straight to the point.

Yana's gentle smile didn't waver. "What is it?"

"It's about your friend. I need to know where Frau is."

"Ah..." Yana's voice trailed off at the question.

"Please, tell me honestly," he pressed. "You knew I was Sealed, which must mean you met with Frau after it happened."

"I'd like to ask something as well," Yana began. "What do you intend to do when you meet him? Surely you don't hold a grudge for what he did ten years ago?"

"No," Lee Sungmin said. "I don't hold any grudge against Frau. In fact, I'm grateful he stopped me. I just want to know... what happened in that city when I was rampaging. I need to know exactly what I did."

Yana gazed intently into his eyes, and after a brief silence, she rose to her feet. "Please follow me."

She slid open the paper screen door and stepped outside. A wave of relief washed over Lee Sungmin. At least he wouldn't have to wander aimlessly searching for Frau.

Yana led him to the garden behind the palace, a place he remembered. Within it lay a hidden space, home to the survivors of a village skilled in handling Demonic Energy. As Yana opened the door to this hidden realm, she paused.

"I'm saying this just in case... but if you try to harm Frau, I will have to subdue you for his sake."

"That won't happen," Lee Sungmin replied, following her through the doorway.

A magnificent mansion, completely out of place in the small village, stood in the center. Yana opened its doors without hesitation and strode inside. As they entered, pretty-looking boys and young men bowed their heads to her. Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh at the sight of their faces and clothes. A decade had passed, but Frau's tastes clearly hadn't changed.

"Good heavens," Yana muttered, stopping in her tracks.

Sprawled half-naked on a lavish bed was Frau, who stared at Lee Sungmin with his mouth agape, making no move to cover himself. He shot a glare at Yana.

"Don't tell me you sold me out?"

"I did not," Yana said, shaking her head.

*Right, that's impossible,* Frau grumbled to himself as he sat up properly. He narrowed his eyes, his gaze fixed on Lee Sungmin.

"...You look fine on the outside."

"I am fine."

Frau let out a hollow laugh. "I'm not so sure about that. Ten years ago, you were completely insane."

"I have no memory of that time," Lee Sungmin said. "...Please don't be so wary."

"How can I not be wary? It's a relief you don't remember," Frau sneered. "You were horrifying ten years ago. I've lived a long time and seen many Yokai, but I'd never seen one as violent and barbaric as you. You killed zombies on sight and just... devoured them. Relentlessly. It's disgusting just thinking about it."

"It's the same for me," Lee Sungmin shot back. "Why does everyone keep emphasizing that I ate zombies?"

"Emphasizing? I've only said it once."

"Everyone who sees me says it," he grumbled, smacking his lips for no reason.

Then a thought struck him. He had been Sealed for ten years. For a full decade, he hadn't eaten or drunk a thing. He had only resumed eating in the last few days. Before that, nothing had gone in or out. A sickening realization dawned on him. Didn't that mean the zombie flesh he had chewed and swallowed had been sitting in his stomach for ten years?

The thought made him feel like shit.

"I'm normal now," Lee Sungmin insisted.

"That's a relief, then."

"I... I don't know anything about what happened there. That's why I came to find you, Frau. What on earth happened? How did you Seal me?"

"Abel is dead," Frau spat out. "He must have been satisfied in his own way. He died after killing the brother he'd considered his rival since childhood with his own two hands."

"...What?" Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. "You mean... Abel killed Envirus?"

"He didn't have enough lifespan left to use Gries' magic. So, Abel committed a mage's taboo. He killed his own brother and stole his life force."

"Ah..."

Envirus was dead. *Of course.* That was what the Slaughter Predator had meant.

*The Spirit Queen will soon awaken.*

Until this moment, Lee Sungmin hadn't understood the mocking tone in the Predator's voice, the implication that the Spirit Queen would become one of The End. Now it all made sense. The Spirit Queen had loved Envirus so sincerely that she had manifested in this world just to prevent his death. With him gone, it was entirely plausible she would go mad with grief.

"...The Elemental who was with Envirus," he asked. "Where did she go?"

"She turned into light and disappeared around the time you started rampaging. Probably got reverse-summoned back to the Elemental Realm."

*Rubia.* Lee Sungmin squeezed his eyes shut, recalling her name and the Crazy Heavenly Demon's request to check on her.

"...How were you able to Seal me?"

"You really don't remember anything, do you?"

"I did some research," Lee Sungmin admitted. "I heard that before you Sealed me, I was in a fierce battle with someone. ...Who was it?"

"It was someone you know," Frau said with a shrug. "Silent Flash Baek Sogo."

"...Ah."

Lee Sungmin's eyes trembled. He wasn't surprised. He had even thought it likely. Given Baek Sogo's personality, she would have undoubtedly come to stop Kim Jonghyun's massacre in the South. He had secretly hoped to find her there.

"She appeared suddenly while I was running from your rampage," Frau explained. "She was crying her eyes out, heartbroken about something, but she still pushed you back."

A deep ache throbbed in his chest.

"I stopped running then," Frau continued. "I decided to help her. No matter how shameless I am, I couldn't just leave her to face that alone."

"...And so...?"

"Baek Sogo didn't want to kill you," Frau said, frowning. "I thought it would be better to just kill you, but doing it by myself was impossible. So I Sealed you in a crack in space instead. I figured it would last for at least fifty years, but I never expected the Seal to break in just ten."

"Where did Senior Sister Baek Sogo go after that?"

"I don't know," Frau grumbled, brushing his hair back. "She was pretty badly injured, but she didn't die."

Lee Sungmin already knew that from Nebul's information.

*...Senior Sister.*

She hadn't wanted to kill him.

He lowered his head, closing his eyes against the pain of the memory he didn't have.
