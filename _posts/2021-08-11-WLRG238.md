Before anyone could react, Moyong Daeun launched himself from his horse. He soared into the air, executing the Airwalking Technique with a flourish of martial prowess, and ran across the sky before plummeting toward Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin, still holding Scarlet, activated his inner arts.

KWAAANG!

A barrier of internal energy erupted to meet the downward strike of Moyong Daeun's sword. Realizing he could not cut through with a single blow, Moyong Daeun somersaulted in the air and landed gracefully on the ground. The sword posed no real threat to Lee Sungmin, but with Scarlet still in his grasp, he retreated a few steps to create more distance.

"The Phantom Spear…!" Moyong Daeun spat the title, his face a contorted mask of fury.

The goodwill he had shown only moments before had vanished, an expression worn before he knew he was facing the Phantom Spear. Now, recognizing the man he believed to be his daughter's killer, his face was completely transformed, radiating a thick and suffocating killing intent.

"Don't act rashly," Lee Sungmin warned him.

A visible panic rippled through the mages and their Tower Masters. In truth, the Mages Guild had no quarrel with the Phantom Spear. The Golden Magic Tower Master, Lloyd, was particularly bewildered. He had once been saved by Lee Sungmin and, as a reward, had sworn to grant any one of his requests unconditionally. Furthermore, Lloyd knew of some unspoken relationship between his own Master, Envirus, and Lee Sungmin, which further restricted his actions.

*Good heavens.*

Chwi Geol was just as stunned. He had been suspicious of Lee Sungmin and had even goaded Tang Ahee into involving the Church, but that did not mean he had ever suspected the man of being the Phantom Spear.

The warriors of the Moyong Clan and the Murim Alliance moved quickly. While the Church, the mages, and the Mercenary Guild had no direct reason to antagonize Lee Sungmin, the Murim Alliance and the Moyong Clan were different. The death of the Iron Armored Divine Spear at Lee Sungmin's hands was no longer their primary justification. Rumors had already spread throughout Eria that the Phantom Spear was the chosen successor to the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the strongest of the evil factions. For the Murim Alliance, the appearance of their longtime nemesis's successor, alone and vulnerable, was an opportunity that might never come again.

"…the Phantom Spear." As the leader of the Pure White Martial Squad, Chwi Geol stepped forward. "I never expected to meet you here. Why have you come?"

"If I told you the truth, would you believe me?" Lee Sungmin asked with a bitter smile.

Chwi Geol did not answer. Nearby, Tang Ahee's face had turned pale, and she took several hesitant steps back. The memory of what had happened in the Bewitching Forest flooded her mind, and her legs trembled uncontrollably. Among everyone present, she was the only one who had seen with her own eyes just how powerful Lee Sungmin truly was. Of course, the man standing before them now was far stronger than the version she had witnessed back then.

"It seems you've come alone…" Chwi Geol continued, glancing at Moyong Daeun, who was struggling to even out his ragged breaths.

[Calm down first.]

Chwi Geol sent a Thought Transmission to Moyong Daeun, a sharp reminder that the Crimson Archmage was still in Lee Sungmin's hands. Veins bulged on the hand Moyong Daeun used to grip his sword.

"What gives you the confidence to come here?" Chwi Geol pressed. "No, more importantly, how did you even get here? Even at top speed, it should take several months to travel from the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance…?"

"I don't think I need to explain that," Lee Sungmin said, his eyes scanning the crowd. "And confidence, you say… Yes. I came alone because I was confident that even if things went wrong, I could make it back alive."

"You bastard!" Moyong Daeun roared at the answer.

Chwi Geol tried to calm him. "For someone who claims to be so confident, why did you take the Crimson Archmage hostage?"

"I'll let her go."

Lee Sungmin's gaze shifted to Lloyd, whose shoulders flinched as their eyes met. Then, levitated by Space Manipulation, Scarlet's body drifted through the air toward the Golden Tower Master.

[You said you would grant me one request.]

Lloyd's expression stiffened at the incoming Thought Transmission.

[Please help so that Scarlet isn't put in a difficult position because of me.]

No answer was needed. Lloyd had sworn an oath, and now that Lee Sungmin had asked for Scarlet's safety, he had to do whatever it took to ensure she was unharmed. He raised both hands and caught her incoming form.

The moment Lee Sungmin's hands were free, Moyong Daeun, who had been holding back until now, kicked off the ground. His sword, the very blade that had earned him the title of Baek Geomhak, followed the path of the Moyong Clan's secret fighting spirit sword art as it rushed toward Lee Sungmin. Though Moyong Daeun was a more skilled swordsman than even the Namgung Patriarch, the Heavenly Venerable Sword King, his blade was not nearly sharp enough to threaten Lee Sungmin.

Without even drawing his spear, Lee Sungmin simply swung his hand.

KAAANG!

With a dull thud, Moyong Daeun's sword was knocked aside.

"My daughter!" he shouted as if coughing up blood. His wrist ached as if it were broken, but Moyong Daeun did not stop. He forcibly twisted the path of his sword, bringing it down toward the crown of Lee Sungmin's head. Lee Sungmin wore a bitter expression at the sorrowful cry. He could have easily shattered the blade with a direct counter, but instead, he used Gale Thunder to vanish from sight.

Startled by the sudden disappearance, Moyong Daeun immediately twisted his body and unleashed enhanced sword qi in all directions. It was a dangerous, indiscriminate attack that showed no regard for the safety of those nearby, but the sight of his daughter's killer had paralyzed his reason.

"Calm down," a voice said from behind him.

Lee Sungmin had reappeared. He raised his hand.

CRACKLE!

Electricity sparked as purple enhanced qi spread from his palm in the shape of a net, catching all of Moyong Daeun's wild sword qi.

"Shut up!"

[I did not kill Moyong Seojin.]

Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission, but as expected, Moyong Daeun refused to listen. Instead, he grew even more frantic, swinging his sword in a desperate attempt to kill him. The unfortunate truth was that the blade, though saturated with killing intent and a father's resentment, seemed utterly unremarkable to Lee Sungmin.

[You're not listening.]

He sent another Thought Transmission as he raised both hands, covering them in an enhanced qi that crackled with electric currents. There was no need for his spear. Lee Sungmin's hand intercepted the path of Moyong Daeun's swinging sword. His twisting hand collided with the blade, and the sword, which had been enveloped in a ferocious aura of enhanced qi, shattered into a thousand pieces.

Lee Sungmin took a step forward and swung his hands at Moyong Daeun's now-defenseless chest.

PAAAAK!

Moyong Daeun flew backward, spewing black blood. He had attacked to avenge his daughter, but the difference in their levels was simply too great.

Chwi Geol and the other Murim Alliance warriors could only stare, dumbfounded, as Moyong Daeun, the most skilled martial artist present, was so easily and utterly subdued.

"Father!"

With young Moyong Chan's scream, the warriors of the Moyong Clan charged forward. They knew they had no chance of winning, but they did not back down in fear. Lee Sungmin watched them come and lifted his right foot.

KUUUNG!

He stomped his foot on the ground, and the earth shook as if in an earthquake. Simultaneously, he released his Dragon's Pressure. Thrown off balance by the trembling earth, the Moyong Clan warriors gasped for air, unable to breathe under the crushing pressure before they collapsed where they stood.

*Impossible. What kind of human is…?*

Chwi Geol's face turned white as he took a few steps back. A considerable amount of time had passed since their meeting in the Dungeon, but the Phantom Spear's power was now incomparable. It was hard to believe that this man, who spread fear with his mere presence, was a human being just like him.

[I didn't kill her.]

Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission to the collapsed Moyong Chan. This was enough. He planned to make his exit now.

[The Patriarch won't listen to me, so I'm telling you, Young Master Moyong… I did not kill your sister. Neither did Jaegal Taeryeong.]

"What are you talking…"

[It's your choice whether to believe me or not, but know this. I can kill all of you, but I'm choosing not to. Do you understand what that means?]

Lee Sungmin said this, glancing toward the fallen Moyong Daeun.

[It means I have shown you mercy.]

Leaving those words behind, Lee Sungmin kicked off the ground and vanished. Chwi Geol shouted something, but the mages of the Mages Guild could not react. With the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun ahead, they did not want to provoke a conflict with Lee Sungmin.

[Are you planning to leave just like that?] the Void Monarch asked.

*No,* Lee Sungmin thought, shaking his head. *First, I'll leave this place and assess the situation. Then I plan to follow behind the subjugation force.*

[You seem worried about that red-haired wench.]

*I came here to make sure she wouldn't be in danger.*

The Void Monarch chuckled at that answer.

[She'll probably be persecuted quite a bit. After all, that mage is associated with you.]

*The Mages Guild didn't attack me. They probably don't want to fight me that badly. I've already asked Lloyd, so Scarlet won't be endangered because of me.*

He had also prepared for a situation like this, having spoken to Scarlet beforehand. He had told her that if his identity was revealed and she was persecuted, she should not worry about loyalty and simply reveal everything honestly.

*

*Son of a bitch. Why'd he hit me so hard?*

Scarlet had not forgotten Lee Sungmin's words. A Priest brought her back to her senses, and she immediately assessed the grim situation. The Magic Tower Masters, the head of the Moyong Clan, and Chwi Geol all surrounded her.

"Looks like he got away safely, huh?" she remarked.

"You witch!" Moyong Daeun yelled.

Scarlet gave him a bored look. "What's so new about me being a witch?" she muttered. Then, before anyone could even ask a question, she began to confess everything about her relationship with Lee Sungmin.

Even the furious Moyong Daeun was taken aback by her relentless stream of words. She recounted everything, from their first connection at Mount Mushii to the current situation where he had come to help her of his own accord, even though she never asked.

After spilling it all, Scarlet looked at Moyong Daeun with a shameless expression. "Anything else you're curious about?"

"Uh…" At a loss for words, Moyong Daeun just moved his lips.

The Green Tower Master stepped forward in his place. "Why didn't you say anything?"

"You wanted me to sell out the guy who came all this way to help me, knowing it could be dangerous? What do you take me for?"

"Don't you think your words contradict your current attitude?"

"Not at all! He escaped safely, and I'm stuck here. Now that it's come to this, don't I have to look out for my own life?"

"Why didn't you escape with the Phantom Spear?" Chwi Geol asked.

At his words, Scarlet ground her teeth and glared at the Green Tower Master. "Because I have to participate in this subjugation. You think I wanted to be part of this bullshit subjugation? I'd rather be in my workshop researching magic than doing this! I'm here because I have no choice, and that Sungmin guy knew it, so he left me behind."

"Heh heh! I see, so you've said it all. If you had escaped with that brat, you would have died for breaking your oath."

"I wasn't planning on bailing, so shut up," Scarlet snapped.

Even though she spoke in a sharp tone, the Green Tower Master just laughed heartily. The commander of the Church's Holy Knights, who had been silent for a while, stepped forward.

"Even if Lee Sungmin's identity is the Phantom Spear, it hasn't been proven that he isn't an emissary of the Vampire Queen."

"So?" Scarlet tilted her head defiantly, glaring at him.

The Holy Knight Commander looked down at her with cold eyes. "Crimson Archmage. Don't you also have some kind of relationship with the Vampire Queen?"

"How about you stop suspecting people without any grounds?"

"Isn't it a possibility?"

"So what? You're going to interrogate me?"

"If necessary."

"Are you going to torture me, too?"

"Again, if necessary," the Holy Knight Commander replied without backing down.

At his words, Lloyd finally had no choice but to step in with a dry cough. "Stop it."

"What do you mean?" the Holy Knight Commander asked with a questioning look.

"I mean… stop persecuting the Crimson Archmage."

"Why are you protecting her?"

Lloyd let out a deep sigh as he recalled Lee Sungmin, who had left him with such a difficult request.
