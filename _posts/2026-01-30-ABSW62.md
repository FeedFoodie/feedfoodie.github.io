Sagong Hu's hands moved with practiced precision as he administered the acupuncture needles.

Seohyeon lay fast asleep on an Ice Jade Bed. Once every three days, she received treatment at this secluded wing of the North Sea Ice Palace. The Ice Palace had lent them not only the Glacial Jade, but also the Ice Jade Bed, one of their most treasured artifacts. Because of it, her treatment was far more bearable.

Additionally, the North Sea Ice Palace supplied various medicinal herbs necessary for her treatment. Many of them were difficult to obtain, even with money. Without the palace's help, Seohyeon's treatment would have been much more difficult.

He and Seohyeon's grandfather, Go Wonjeong, were immensely grateful to the North Sea Ice Palace for all of this.

Go Wonjeong observed the treatment for a while before standing up to leave. The session would continue for at least another hour.

Despite his aging body, Sagong Hu fought on. Seohyeon also endured the difficult treatment well. Providing and receiving such intensive treatment every day for seven years was no easy feat.

He had once asked Sagong Hu, "How can I ever repay this kindness?"

Sagong Hu had laughed heartily at the time, saying that it was fate. Much later, however, at a drinking party, he confessed his true feelings.

It turned out that Sagong Hu felt the same way he did. He didn't want to remain a mere lifesaver, someone to be remembered with gratitude after the treatment ended. 

He wanted to develop a bond with them and see them often, like family. He was tired of living a lonely life.

Afterward, Go Wonjeong pretended to be oblivious and brought up the topic himself. He suggested that they build a large house and live together, spending their later years in each other's company. Sagong Hu hadn't shown it, but Go Wonjeong could feel his immense joy.

A smile touched his lips as he walked out of the treatment room.

*Such is life. Life is endless if you're picky, but it's nothing if you take a step back.*

Spending his remaining years with Sagong Hu wasn't so bad. Neither of them planned on meeting a new woman or starting a family at their age, so they instead decided to enjoy life as friends and brothers at least until Seohyeon got married.

Suddenly, he noticed an old woman pushing a small handcart from the far end of the hallway. She was Bing Oksim, the Grand Dame of the North Sea Ice Palace, and his senior in the murim. As the Grand Dame, she was the master of the current Empress and the palace's most powerful martial master.

Bing Oksim's eyes resembled a tiger's, giving her a naturally strong impression that suited her status. She also had taut skin that looked much younger than her age, a result of having perfected her martial arts.

"It's been a long time, Grand Dame," he greeted her politely.

"Have you been well, Master Go?" Bing Oksim returned the greeting.

Go Wonjeong squirmed uncomfortably, finding it awkward that she, his elder, called him 'Master'. He had told her to speak to him informally, as one would a junior, but Bing Oksim always insisted on the honorific.

He quickly changed the topic, asking, "Why are you pushing the cart yourself?" even as he took it from her. On closer inspection, the cart was loaded with medicinal herbs for Seohyeon's treatment.

Bing Oksim smiled. "I came out for some exercise, so don't worry about me."

Go Wonjeong returned the smile. Sometimes, he could sense Bing Oksim's loneliness. He understood its source. The deferential courtesy everyone showed her was clearly connected to the sense of isolation she radiated.

"Is the treatment going well?" she asked.

Go Wonjeong sighed and replied, "Elder Sagong is doing his best."

"Don't worry too much. With his skill in medicine, I'm sure the treatment will go well."

"I hope so too."

The two exchanged looks, still smiling.

Go Wonjeong occasionally ran into Bing Oksim when he visited the Ice Palace for Seohyeon's treatment. Despite their few meetings, they had formed a deep bond, likely because he sincerely accepted and understood her loneliness.

"Shall we have a cup of tea?"

"Let's."

Together, they pushed the cart into a nearby room stocked with different types of tea. Bing Oksim dismissed the maid and brewed the tea herself.

"This is a new tea that just arrived. I'm not sure if it'll suit your palate."

Go Wonjeong savored the tea's aroma before carefully taking a sip. "It tastes very good."

"I'm glad it suits your taste."

Bing Oksim was known to have had a formidable temperament in her youth. Considering her calm demeanor now, one would never guess at her past.

Bing Oksim asked, "Do you hear from Seohyeon's father from time to time?"

"Yes, he contacts me occasionally," Go Wonjeong replied.

That was a lie. Kwon Kangho only contacted him when absolutely necessary, fearing that Seohyeon's existence might be discovered. He even only transferred them money through the bank.

In his last letter, he had written about the power struggle between the First Young Lord and the Third Young Lady, and that the Third Young Lady had found out about Seohyeon.

Go Wonjeong instantly understood why his disciple had reported the situation so candidly. He was essentially asking him to prepare for any unforeseen circumstances. 

For this reason, he was always alert and wary these days. The formations beneath the hut alone wouldn't stop the blades that might come for them. He had also recently resumed training with the sword he had long set down. Still, he sincerely hoped he would never have to use it.

Bing Oksim silently regarded Go Wonjeong. She knew that his martial arts had surpassed the pinnacle and entered a new realm. Even compared to herself, the top master of the Ice Palace, he was by no means her inferior. It was this realization which gave rise to her utmost respect and courtesy for him.

"Is the Empress doing well?" Go Wonjeong asked.

He had only exchanged a brief greeting with the Empress once. Even then, she had been wearing a veil, so he had only been able to confirm that she was a woman.

"That girl is always busy," Bing Oksim replied, but she couldn't hide a hint of disappointment in her words.

Go Wonjeong smiled inwardly. Bing Oksim was the master of the current Ice Empress. Since conflict between a master and a disciple was natural, it seemed the Ice Empress had recently done something to displease her.

"If it weren't for your Palace, Seohyeon would have suffered greatly."

"Don't say that. A child's life was at stake. How could we not help?"

That was true in principle, of course. However, Go Wonjeong knew how reclusive the North Sea Ice Palace was.

The Ice Palace's offer of assistance was clearly more than just goodwill. At first, he thought it was because Bing Oksim had recognized his martial prowess. If not that, he figured Sagong Hu must have some old favor or grudge that even he was unaware of. Apart from those speculations, he couldn't think of any reason why the North Sea Ice Palace would support them so actively.

Suddenly, a maid shouted from outside. "Elder Sagong has finished the treatment."

Go Wonjeong grinned and rose to his feet. "I must be going now."

"I'll see you again next time."

Thank you for the tea."

Go Wonjeong left the room.

Left alone, Bing Oksim slowly emptied her teacup. Worry, which hadn't been there a moment ago, flickered across her face.

The Royal Hall of the North Sea Ice Palace was more splendid and magnificent than the residence of any other jianghu leader. Though it seemed to be made of ice at first glance, it was actually crafted from a type of stone that resembled ice.

Stone statues of successive Ice Empresses stood on the four walls of the vast hall. Most were men, but occasionally, there were some women.

Standing by the window was a beautiful woman. She was the current North Sea Empress, Seol Iyeon. Surprisingly, she was also the same woman who had saved Crimson Tiger.

Beyond the window, a breathtaking snowscape unfolded. Seol Iyeon's snow-white skin suited the vista perfectly.

In the distance, she could see Crimson Tiger's master and Sagong Hu leaving the Ice Palace, carrying Seohyeon.

Tears welled up in her eyes as she gazed at the frail little girl, her biological daughter. This was why the reclusive North Sea Ice Palace had lent the two old men the Glacial Jade and Ice Jade Bed. 

She sighed inwardly at the faint reflection of herself in the window. Gone was the youthful freshness and carefree spirit she had possessed a decade ago, replaced by a graceful dignity different from the Third Young Lady's.

"Today's treatment also ended safely." So Rang, her aide, reported.

Seol Iyeon stared out the window, saying nothing. Seohyeon had already disappeared into the distance, but tears still streamed down her face.

So Rang stood behind her, unaware she was crying. "Fortunately, the Heavenly Council doesn't seem to know that we saved him," she continued her report. "In the end, we were truly fortunate that Dok Yahaeng's treasure map appeared."

Dok Yahaeng had a past connection to the North Sea Ice Palace. Back when he dominated the jianghu, he had become a sworn brother to the then Emperor of the North Sea Ice Palace, and their deep friendship had lasted until both of their deaths.

For this reason, Seol Iyeon had personally left the palace when rumors of his treasure map appeared. Thanks to that, she was able to save Crimson Tiger.

"But you mustn't leave the palace anymore. You were just lucky this time. Please, cut off all ties to that person."

Finally, Seol Iyeon spoke up. "Are you afraid of those old men?"

So Rang hesitated for a moment before answering. Her expression showed no fear, but her voice revealed otherwise. "Yes, I'm terrified of them. I'm so scared sometimes that I jolt awake in the middle of the night."

"Why?"

"Are you asking because you don't know? They're the only ones who can bring you down, Empress. They're the ones who can chase you out of this palace!"

"Let them."

"Empress!"

Seol Iyeon fell silent. Her vacant gaze wandered over the now empty, snow-covered training grounds of the North Sea Ice Palace.

"You mustn't take the old men of the Heavenly Council lightly!" So Rang stressed.

"The Grand Dame has arrived!" a servant reported.

Seol Iyeon's eyes, which had been fixed on the window, wavered slightly.

So Rang quickly replied, "Show her in at once."

The door opened, and Bing Oksim entered. So Rang politely greeted her and then left the room.

Finally, Seol Iyeon tore her gaze from the window. "You've come?" she said, her tone polite yet cold.

Bing Oksim glanced at the tear tracks on Seol Iyeon's cheeks before sitting down. "I heard you did something outrageous."

"I don't think it was outrageous."

"It'll become a huge problem if they find out. Don't come crying to me for help then!"

"Don't worry, that won't happen."

Bing Oksim narrowed her eyes. "Are you still angry with me about that incident?"

Seol Iyeon's gaze deepened as she remembered the events of that day long ago.

Any disciple of the North Sea Ice Palace could undergo a trial to become its ruler. The trial was difficult, requiring one to surpass human limits. Those who passed became the new ruler. However, if two or more people passed the trial in one cycle, the one with the higher score won.

If the passing candidate was a woman, however, she had to face an additional trial that tested her ability to overcome emotions such as joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure. This trial involved meeting a loved one and then parting from them. This test was called the Parting Sorrow Pass.

This terrible trial was created because the Seventeenth Empress, So Hwagun, had fallen in love with Yang Jan, a renowned expert from the unorthodox sects. In truth, Yang Jan had approached her with the intention of stealing the ultimate martial arts of the Ice Palace, but believing his love was true, she smuggled out the secret techniques and gave them to him.

Fortunately, the guardians of the North Sea Ice Palace executed Yang Jan and recovered the techniques, but So Hwagun despaired over his death and ultimately took her own life. Even as she died, she cursed and condemned the Ice Palace.

This incident caused the North Sea Ice Palace's reputation to plummet and left a scar on its martial artists. It reignited the argument that a woman could never become ruler, to which the women of the Ice Palace fiercely objected.

Consequently, the Parting Sorrow Pass was created. The idea was to ensure that only a woman with a heart of steel, who held no affection for any man, could become empress.

However, there was a problem with that test. As the saying goes, one can know the depths of ten fathoms of water, yet never truly understand another person. It was impossible to distinguish between someone who was pretending to love and someone who truly loved another person.

Hence, after some trial and error, one more cruel and unnatural condition was added. A candidate had to give birth to a child and then abandon both the father and the child. Only after overcoming the pain of familial separation could one ascend to the position of empress.

The Heavenly Council oversaw that test. When a woman became empress, they monitored her thoroughly. They held no power, only the absolute authority to depose the empress if she ever met the man and child she had abandoned. 

This was what So Rang feared most.

"Didn't you say you were confident that you wouldn't truly fall in love?" she grumbled.

Seol Iyeon had no rebuttal. It was true. She had felt that way even when she singled out Kwon Kangho, a seemingly ordinary man she had met in a remote village marketplace. She had felt that way even when she was hunting a tiger with him. She had felt her heart waver a little, but even then she thought she could part with him easily. She was confident that since she had come like the wind, she could leave like the wind.

She couldn't have been more wrong. She thought love started and ended in the heart. She was confident she could control her feelings, but she realized too late that a person's emotions were not something to be dismissed so lightly.

"I can part with him, but the child... I can never give up the child."

"......"

"Please! Let me stay with my child, Master!"

"No. Absolutely not."

"Sniff... sob, Master. Please! I'll do whatever you ask from now on. So please, just the child!" Seol Iyeon's sorrowful screams echoed, and her tears flowed endlessly.

Nevertheless, Bing Oksim was resolute. "You know this is a principle that can't be changed, don't you?"

Seol Iyeon gritted her teeth. Until her belly grew, she had been confident she could abandon the child. But the moment she heard the baby's cry, the moment she first saw the child, everything changed. There were things in this world one couldn't know without experiencing them, and she was certain that seeing one's newborn child was at the very top of that list.

Bowing her head, she made a decision. "Then I'll give up the position of Empress."

She rose from her seat and strode toward the door, intending to leave the North Sea Ice Palace right then and there.

A cold remark sounded from behind her. "Foolish girl."

"I'm sorry."

"If you give up your position, you and your mother will die. So will everyone who cares for you."

Seol Iyeon flinched and stopped in her tracks.

"Do you think the new ruler will just leave you alone? You, who completed the trial in the shortest amount of time with the highest score? You, the most popular person in this entire Palace? You, whom everyone wished would become ruler? If you thought so, then you're a foolish girl. Your foolishness will kill your mother and you."

Seol Iyeon stood there blankly, shocked. Bing Oksim's attitude was cruel, but her words held an irrefutable truth. Tears streamed down her face again.

Bing Oksim added sharply, "Stop that. What's needed now are not those cheap tears, but cold rationality."

Seol Iyeon returned to the present. Bing Oksim's gaze was as unforgiving as she remembered. The only difference was her attitude. Instead of weeping and wailing, she faced her master resolutely.

*These aren't cheap tears,* she wanted to retort, but held herself back. Instead, she answered calmly, "I didn't choose this path without thinking it over."

"Then why!?" Bing Oksim shouted, on the verge of exploding with anger. Soon, however, she let out a soft sigh.

She was certain that if she hadn't persuaded Seol Iyeon back then, she and her mother would have died. The power struggles in the Ice Palace were not as beautiful as the pure white snow that blanketed the land.

"The Heavenly Council is just waiting for you to make a mistake," she reminded. "They despise the idea of a woman becoming the ruler. Haven't you been doing well until now?"

Seol Iyeon bit her lip. As Bing Oksim pointed out, she had been doing well. For the first few years, she devoted herself entirely to the countless responsibilities of her new position. She desperately tried not to think about Crimson Tiger and Seohyeon. She only shed tears where no one could see.

Only after a few years, when everyone was convinced she was no longer bound by the past, did she secretly have So Rang look into Crimson Tiger and Seohyeon. The surveillance on her prior to that had been too extensive for her to gather any information.

It was during this time that she learned that Seohyeon was terminally ill, and that Crimson Tiger had become one of the Twelve Phantoms for their daughter's sake. Since that day, she hadn't gotten a single peaceful night's sleep.

Bing Oksim shot up from her seat. "If this continues, you will walk the very path of ruin they desire."

Seol Iyeon replied calmly, "Don't worry. I know where I'm going."

"Do you really?" Bing Oksim stormed out of the room, seething with rage.

When she was gone, Seol Iyeon let out a sigh. Perhaps, as Bing Oksim said, she was tempting fate. 

However, she wasn't doing this because of Kwon Kangho. She could endure not seeing him again.

She was doing this for her sick daughter. She couldn't just stand by and watch while the child suffered, could she? 

Nevertheless, she had to act as cautiously as possible, as if she had no connection to Seohyeon's treatment beyond showing mercy and charity to the less fortunate.

Whenever Seohyeon came to the Ice Palace, she suppressed the urge to run out and hug her daughter. She also suppressed the desire to kill all the old fogeys of the Heavenly Council and meet Kwon Kangho and Seohyeon publicly.

So Rang returned to the hall. "Are you okay?" she asked, studying Seol Iyeon's face closely.

"No, I'm not okay." Seol Iyeon stared out the window again. Since that day, she hadn't been okay even for a single day. "Abandoning my daughter was my decision. I could have chosen not to."

"But if you had..."

"I would have died with dignity, at the very least. If I hadn't let go..."

"Empress!"

Seol Iyeon smiled faintly. In the end, everything she said were just excuses. The reality was that she had abandoned her daughter to become Empress of the North Sea Ice Palace.

Her gaze deepened. Even if the whole world understood, neither Kwon Kangho nor Seohyeon would ever forgive her. From the very beginning, she had approached him with impure intentions.

"Once Seohyeon's treatment is over, I'll never appear before them again."

She would provide everything for Seohyeon's treatment. Even if it meant being dethroned, she wouldn't compromise on that. However, she resolved never to meet them again after that.

"For his sake, and for the child's sake."

She would live with the pain and regret for the rest of her life. She deserved it.

"As long as the child gets better... as long as she gets better... I don't care what happens to me."

Three people climbed a mountain. Seohyeon led the way, followed by Go Wonjeong and Sagong Hu.

Seohyeon skipped and hopped ahead. She looked like she was just playing, but she was actually stepping in a specific pattern, treading precisely on the single safe path within the Primordial Four Symbols Formation.

The two old men were astounded that a child had perfectly memorized the safe path. They often came and went, but it was no easy feat for a girl her age to memorize and navigate it perfectly. Truly, Seohyeon was a bright child.

Of course, nothing would happen to her if she stepped incorrectly. The formation was primarily intended to disorient people and make them lose their way. Nothing untoward would happen to her if she made a mistake. 

Go Wonjeong had studied the Primordial Four Symbols Formation for years and installed it himself to make sure of that. Moreover, he designed the path to look as if no formation was installed at all. Anyone who wasn't an expert would end up going somewhere else entirely without realizing they were led astray. Their hut near the mountain peak was impossible to stumble upon by chance.

Sagong Hu remarked, "I'm relieved that Hyeon is enduring all of this so well."

Go Wonjeong nodded in agreement. Seohyeon certainly looked better. Compared to her formerly emaciated frame, she had gained weight and her cheeks were now chubby.

Still, this was only a temporary effect of her medication. From now until winter, they had to focus on increasing her weight and building her stamina so she could endure the final treatment procedure.

"She's a truly admirable child," Go Wonjeong marveled.

Sagong Hu smiled. Seohyeon had become indispensable to them both.

"Be careful!" Go Wonjeong suddenly shouted.

"Hehe, don't worry, Grandpa!"

Even while playing, Seohyeon led the way flawlessly. Having gotten far ahead, she stood on a small stone and waited for them.

"Grandpa, I have something to ask," she said.

"What is it?"

"What kind of place is the Association where Daddy works?"

"Are you curious about that?" Go Wonjeong asked back. He had told her before that her was working for the Association as a martial artist.

"Yes."

Go Wonjeong patted Seohyeon on the head. The girl rarely asked about her father. In truth, she was very interested in him, but she often hid her feelings so as not to worry them. She was admirably mature for her age.

"Grandpa will open the path now." Go Wonjeong scooped Seohyeon into his arms and put her on his shoulders.

"Waaaah!"

Go Wonjeong started walking with the beaming child riding on his shoulders. "In this jianghu, there's a group called the Evil Coalition."

"The Evil Coalition?"

"Yes, it's a place where all the bad people of the jianghu gather."

Although the world couldn't be neatly divided into black and white, it was the only way to explain the existence of the New Righteous Association to a child like Seohyeon.

"The Association was created to defeat those bad guys."

"So Daddy's doing a good thing."

"Of course."

Seohyeon was silent for a moment.

"What's wrong?"

"If all the world's villains are gathered there, won't there be some scary people among them?" she asked worriedly.

Sagong Hu, who had been listening from the side, laughed. "Your father is a scarier and stronger person than any of those wicked villains. All the villains of the Evil Coalition tremble in fear of him."

"Ah, of course!" Seohyeon breathed a sigh of relief. Of course, being a bright child, she didn't believe his words naively. She was just trying her best to believe them.

"Hyeon."

"Yes?"

"The day you recover isn't far away now."

"Hehehe! Put me down!"

Anticipation filled Seohyeon's face. There were so many things she wanted to do with her daddy once she was healthy. No one was waiting for that day more eagerly than her.

She stepped onto the last safe path of the formation and ran off cheerfully.

As they exited the formation, the fog lifted. A cozy hut came into view in the distance.

Go Wonjeong's gaze deepened as he watched Seohyeon dash ahead.

*Yes, as long as you keep moving forward like that, a day will come when the fog truly lifts. Your father and this grandfather will make it so.*
