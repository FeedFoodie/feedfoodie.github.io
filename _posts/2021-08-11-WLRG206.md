This incident would make him a complete enemy of the Namgung Clan, yet Lee Sungmin felt no particular burden at the thought. He had already become an enemy of the Murim Alliance, so adding the Namgung Clan to the list would change nothing. After all, the clan was just another member of the Alliance. With Namgung Heewon having already left his family, there was even less reason for him to feel troubled.

He had told Namgung Heewon everything about Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Black Dragon Knight. The warning had been given, and now Namgung Heewon would have to take responsibility for his own choices and their consequences. Lee Sungmin would have preferred to act together with him, but their circumstances were too different, leaving him no choice but to let his friend go.

[What do you plan to do?] the Void Monarch asked, his voice laced with insinuation. He seemed to be looking forward to the chaos that was about to unfold. [You said you wouldn't kill them. Doesn't that just mean you only have to leave them alive?]

*Still, I can't be too rough or cruel. It would be awkward to face Namgung Heewon later.*

[Is there any reason to care so much? They're going to try to kill you anyway, aren't they?]

*That's true, but I'd prefer to be considerate if I can.*

He had first met Namgung Heewon at Shaolin. Though he had his own reasons for being there, Namgung Heewon had shown him nothing but goodwill. It was through that meeting that Lee Sungmin had learned just how wide the world truly was. While Namgung Heewon had not taught him martial arts, he was one of the people who had broadened his perspective. They had even been sworn brothers, however briefly. If possible, he wanted to respect Namgung Heewon's position and avoid putting him in a difficult situation.

[Well. I suppose it doesn't matter,] the Void Monarch conceded, sensing Lee Sungmin's feelings. [With your skills, suppressing them should be easy as long as they're not masters of the Transcendent Realm.]

Even if he was not fully aware of it himself, Lee Sungmin held the position of an Absolute Being in Eria. Threatening him was an impossible task, even for the full force of the Namgung Clan.

The clan's warriors spotted Namgung Heewon leaving the square. They were loyal to their Patriarch's words, and though they felt pity and sympathy for the young master, their duty was to capture him for disobeying the clan head. Leading their horses, they moved to pursue him.

"Gasp!"

It was then that a colossal pressure and killing intent swept over the napes of their necks. The galloping horses were the first to react, their bodies trembling as they whinnied and stopped dead in their tracks. Amidst the animals' agitation, the warriors swallowed hard, their expressions stiffening. They scanned their surroundings, searching for the source of the aura that now dominated the entire square, and their eyes fell upon the man responsible.

"Wh... who are you?" the middle-aged swordsman leading the group asked, his voice cautious.

Holding a spear wreathed in purple enhanced qi, Lee Sungmin stared blankly back at him.

"And who are you?"

At the question, the swordsman gulped. He quickly dismounted and placed a hand on the hilt of his sword. Lee Sungmin let out a snort of laughter at the sight.

"M... my name is Namgung Jun, and my title is..."

"No. You don't have to tell me your title," Lee Sungmin cut him off, clearing his throat. He wore no Magic Armor, nor did he hold the spear Celgerus had forged for him. He had even changed his face with a human skin mask. The only connections to his title as the Phantom Spear were his Spear Technique and his signature purple qi. It felt a bit like trying to hide the obvious, but he thought it was enough. Since a clash with the Namgung Clan was inevitable, he would have to reveal some of his skills.

*Still, I don't want to call myself the Phantom Spear.*

He had no desire to introduce himself anyway. While he had the warriors occupied, Namgung Heewon had completely exited the square. Sensing this, the middle-aged swordsman's eyebrow twitched. The other martial artists, who had been standing still, stealthily began to move, intending to give chase.

Watching them, Lee Sungmin slowly extended his foot and took a single step forward.

KUUUUNG!

The pressure radiating from him crushed the warriors' bodies. A few with weaker inner strength suffered internal injuries and vomited blood. Even the middle-aged swordsman, who had the highest cultivation, suffered minor internal damage, his face turning a deathly pale.

"St... stop..." one of them pleaded desperately.

A martial artist of the Transcendent Realm could crush a space with their presence alone if they so wished, naturally using the soulmates that only appeared in stories. Lee Sungmin heard the plea but did not grant the request.

Right. There was no need to even swing his spear. He glanced at the weapon in his hand, and the enhanced qi wreathed around it vanished. Instead, he began to walk slowly toward them.

With each step that brought him closer, the warriors' faces grew paler. When Lee Sungmin had taken a total of five steps, the lead swordsman could no longer endure it and collapsed, choking on his own blood. Soon, all fifteen warriors from the Namgung Clan lay on the ground, felled by internal injuries. He had not needed to swing his spear once. They were all alive, and with proper treatment, there would be no lasting effects.

[What will you do now?]

*I'm going to the Namgung Clan,* Lee Sungmin answered as if it required no thought.

The Void Monarch sounded a little surprised. [You're acting rather aggressively. What's the reason?]

*Even if I subdue these guys, the Namgung Clan will just keep sending pursuit teams.*

[So. You're saying you're going to storm the Noble Clan's home and raise hell?]

*Isn't that the easiest and most convenient way?*

[Hahaha!] the Void Monarch burst into pleasant laughter at his answer. [Yes, that's right. That is the easiest and most convenient way. It's not a method anyone can use, but you can. After all, you're the one in the position of absolute strength.]

Hearing the laughter, Lee Sungmin looked around. His soulmates had been directed only at the Namgung Clan's martial artists, so the other people in the square were unharmed. Even so, they knew very well that he was the reason the warriors had collapsed.

"Excuse me, a question," Lee Sungmin said to a man who was standing frozen in place. It was the same man who had answered his question about Namgung Heewon when he first arrived.

"Ye... yes?"

"Where is the Namgung Clan located?" Lee Sungmin asked with a smile, as if nothing had happened.

Heavenly Venerable Sword King Namgung Baek had been famous as a Supreme Peak master for decades, yet, even after all those years, he had still not reached a higher realm. It was not something to be pitied. Eria was vast, and in this wide world, countless people practiced martial arts. Among them, the number who reached the Supreme Peak was significantly smaller, but compared to the 'real' Central Plains Murim, Eria had quite a high number of such masters.

This was only natural. All sorts of people were summoned to this place, and through that process, countless martial arts and theories became intermingled. Talents varied greatly, and those with carefully selected gifts learned more perfected martial arts and became masters. Therefore, the number of Supreme Peak masters naturally increased and would only continue to grow as time went on.

However, those who reached the Transcendent Realm could be counted on one hand. Even someone called a martial arts calamity would be frustrated before that insurmountable wall. The Heavenly Venerable Sword King was no different.

He had been summoned to Eria as a boy, and because he had the surname 'Namgung', he became a member of the Namgung Clan. He honed the clan's martial arts alongside the arts he had learned in the Central Plains, eventually reaching the Supreme Peak. He then competed with other martial artists within the Noble Clan who shared his surname and situation, and after gaining everyone's recognition, he became the head of the family. That was how the head of the Namgung Clan was chosen. The Namgung surnames and bloodlines from all sorts of worlds had already mixed, so things like legitimacy held no meaning. Simply, the strongest among them became the clan head.

The Heavenly Venerable Sword King took pride in his sword. When he had just become the clan head, he had also held lofty ambitions. Not anymore. Both his ambition and his pride had faded over the long years. It had been thirty years since he reached the Supreme Peak. His sword had grown sharper and his martial arts had advanced, but he had never been able to overcome the wall before him. It felt as if reaching the Transcendent Realm required something more than just talent and skill.

At some point, Namgung Baek gave up on entering the Transcendent Realm and began to place his hopes on his son, Namgung Heewon. His son was the 'real deal'. He had shown outstanding talent from a young age and entered the Supreme Peak in his twenties, an achievement a decade faster than his father's. Even after reaching that realm, he relentlessly swung his sword and rapidly improved his martial prowess. Namgung Baek was certain his son would one day enter the Transcendent Realm.

Despite placing his hopes on Namgung Heewon, however, he no longer harbored any ambitions of his own. Once, he had dreamed of gathering the power of the Noble Clan to create a force greater than the Murim Alliance, but too much time had passed. As the years went by, the Murim Alliance gained absolute control over Eria's orthodox murim, and the Namgung Clan, along with the other Noble Clans, could not escape its influence. Gathering their power to become independent was now impossible. The aged Namgung Baek knew this well, and though they never said it aloud, the Tang, Jaegal, and Moyong Clans knew it too.

"Heewon left the cage," Namgung Baek said, clenching and unclenching his fist as he listened to the report. Three months ago, he had locked his son in a cage for defying his words and doubting the Murim Alliance. It was merely a punishment for show, meant to establish his authority as the clan head and to humiliate his son. The influence of the Murim Alliance was absolute, and even the Namgung Clan could not go against its will.

"Foolish boy..." Namgung Baek sighed and muttered. He recalled the image of his son shouting that the Murim Alliance had plotted a conspiracy. That much was acceptable, but to defend the Phantom Spear? Lee Sungmin was a demonic practitioner who had brutally murdered the Alliance's Iron Armored Divine Spear. He had defended someone who should not be defended, so he deserved to be punished.

"We sent a pursuit team with Jun at its center, but..."

"It won't be nearly enough," Namgung Baek muttered as he rose to his feet. "I'd be grateful if they could even buy some time, but with Heewon's skills, it won't take him long to subdue Jun and the others."

"In that case..."

"Summon the elders," Namgung Baek commanded with a sigh. "If he were to come home, kneel, and beg for forgiveness, it might be different... but with Heewon's personality, he won't come back. After all, he was the one who chose to be locked in a cage for three months."

His son would head for the Murim Alliance. He would want to see and hear the truth for himself, no matter what he was told. Namgung Baek knew his son's personality well. *I can't let that happen.* The young master of the Namgung Clan going to the Murim Alliance to denounce them for a conspiracy? That must never happen. He had to stop it, no matter what.

Answering the clan head's call, the elders gathered. These were the beings who could be called the true power of the Noble Clan. They lacked youthful vigor and the potential for growth, but they had all swung their swords their entire lives and had established their own realms of martial arts.

"It seems I must go and catch my foolish son," Namgung Baek said, and the five elders nodded their heads.

The main gate of the Namgung Clan was large and its walls were long, possessing the imposing presence befitting the main house of one of Eria's most prestigious clans. Standing before it, Lee Sungmin gazed at the clan's crest carved into the center of the doors. He had never thought a day would come when he would visit the main house of the Namgung Clan, and he had certainly never imagined a visit that was not by invitation or with goodwill.

[Getting cold feet?] the Void Monarch asked with a sly grin.

Lee Sungmin answered with a snort of laughter and approached the main gate. The gatekeepers standing guard shouted something and tried to stop him, but he did not listen. The soulmates spreading out from him forced the guards to their knees. He stepped past them as they collapsed vomiting blood and stood before the massive gate.

[Are you going to open it and go in? Or break it down and go in?]

"I didn't exactly come here with good intentions," Lee Sungmin muttered as he raised his hand.

The moment his slowly extended hand touched the gate.

KUUUUUNG!

Spiderweb-like cracks exploded across the large wooden doors. Then, with a light push, the entire gate shattered into pieces. Lee Sungmin walked past the remains and entered the Namgung Clan's grounds.

*To stop the Noble Clan, subduing the clan head is the fastest way, I suppose.*

With that thought, Lee Sungmin expanded the range of his soulmates.
