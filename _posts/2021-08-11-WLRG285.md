The first.

That single word, spoken with an emphasizing force, was enough to silence everyone in the room, including Lee Sungmin. Even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who had paid little attention to the matter of The End, could not so easily dismiss what Jeniela was saying.

[The first,] the Void Monarch muttered inside Lee Sungmin's head.

Jeniela had mentioned 'The End', yet the Apostle of the End was not intervening.

"Can you really speak of this so freely?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"I haven't spoken of the fundamental story, so it doesn't matter," Jeniela replied with a slight smile. "Besides, The End has already begun."

From his question, she could tell that Lee Sungmin properly understood what 'The End' signified, and that alone was enough to pique her interest. Here was a human who could not be seen by the All-Seeing Demonic Eye or the Eye of Futuresight, and on top of that, he clearly understood The End.

"Kim Jonghyun," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman began, his voice low. "I know of that Black Mage. The Demon King of the North. I also know roughly what happened in the forest where the subjugation took place."

"Then it seems I can explain that matter to you in detail."

"Does that mean we can't stop him now?"

"It means you can't do anything with this level of information," Jeniela stated.

At that answer, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a low laugh. Information. Jeniela was the one in control, a fact he knew all too well. No matter how strong he was, he could not pressure her with force to extract what she knew. It might have been possible against the Black Dragon Knight, but it was impossible with Jeniela. She was an opponent who could neither be subdued nor killed.

"Then speak."

"The Grimoire's ritual, performed by gathering the hearts, souls, and fear of thousands. That ritual Kim Jonghyun conducted in the North was one to transform him into a Demon Lord."

"Good heavens," Scarlet muttered, her face turning pale.

While the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and Lee Sungmin were laymen when it came to magic, Scarlet was not. She understood the terrifying implications of Jeniela's words.

"To turn a human into a Demon Lord… there's no way that's possible."

"Oh, but it is possible," Jeniela countered. "The Grimoire's magic isn't human magic. I don't know if the Mages Guild is aware of it, but the Grimoire is a Demon Lord's magic tome. It's prepared with magic for a Demon Lord."

"Then isn't that even more of a contradiction?" Scarlet pressed. "You say it's a Demon Lord's magic tome, prepared with magic for a Demon Lord, so why would there be magic to turn a human into one?"

"Perhaps that's precisely the reason?" Jeniela beamed. "The Grimoire is for turning a 'certain' human of this world into a Demon Lord, and it records the magic for that human, now a Demon Lord, to use. If you look at it that way, it's better to see it as a guide for growth, a textbook, rather than a simple magic tome."

"Who in the world could have created such a thing?!"

"I don't know that either. In truth, that's not what's important, is it? Arbes couldn't use its magic even though he possessed the Grimoire, but Kim Jonghyun could. He was special, even among humans."

"But the ritual should have failed," the Black Dragon Knight muttered. He was the one who had ruined the nearly completed ritual, acting on a directive from the Spirit Medium to go to the forest and stop Kim Jonghyun.

*The Spirit Medium didn't say to kill him…*

If he had to be killed, she would have ordered him to 'kill' without leaving any room for doubt, but she had not commanded that. Jeniela's earlier word, puppet, clung to his ears like something sticky, and the thought sent a chill down his spine.

"That's right, the ritual failed," Jeniela confirmed. "Kim Jonghyun didn't become a complete Demon Lord."

"What's the problem with that?" Lee Sungmin asked. If it failed, shouldn't that be the end of it? The Black Dragon Knight had interfered and stopped him.

"A Demon Lord cannot exist in this world," Jeniela explained. "There's a reason why the Spirit Queen cannot exist here and lives in the Elemental Realm, and why the Fairy Queen cannot leave her domain. This is a very old promise. Transcendents in the truest sense, like them, must not Advent in this world. A certain degree of interference is possible, but they must not manifest. If Kim Jonghyun's ritual had succeeded and he had completely transformed into a Demon Lord, he would have been exiled from Eria the moment he became one."

She laughed, her voice high-pitched and unsettling.

"However, his ritual failed. He succeeded in becoming a half-Demon Lord, but he didn't become a complete one. What does this mean? It means he can wield the power of a Demon Lord without being exiled from this world."

"…Good heavens…"

Jeniela's gaze shifted to the Black Dragon Knight, her smile widening. "Why would Heaven Beyond Heaven give you such an order? Because Kim Jonghyun was a hindrance to them? If that were the case, there would have been no need to interfere. Without anyone needing to subjugate him, he would have been immediately exiled to the Demon Realm upon his success."

The Black Dragon Knight's shoulders trembled.

"Heaven Beyond Heaven. I know about you lot," she sneered. "Huhu… huhuhu! Those arrogant idealists. Frogs in a well. Rats on a wheel, spinning without knowing why… Huhu! Do you think Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Predators are equals? Humans who barely live for a hundred years, and among them, the ones special enough to obtain a lifespan of a few hundred years were the Martial God and the Six Honored Ones. Do you think we left you alone, fearing your less-than-ten vermin who barely managed to gain a lifespan equal to the inhuman after dedicating their entire lives?"

Her laughter echoed again.

"We left you alone because we had to. You were prolonging your existence through the mercy of the Predators. However, it won't be long now. The day our mercy ends is near. The Southern Disaster will be the signal flare, announcing why we monsters, who originated from humans, call ourselves Predators."

"What are you planning to do?" the Black Dragon Knight demanded.

"I'm not the one doing anything. It's Kim Jonghyun and Vollander."

"Vollander…!"

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. The name struck a chord. The South. The waters of the Southwest Sea. The Ghost Ship that roamed the trade routes. He had met them by chance, hundreds of Death Knights and their leader, Volander Evrail.

"Kim Jonghyun was able to give Vollander and the hundreds of Death Knights what they desperately desired," Jeniela continued. "Having become a half-Demon Lord, he could now use the authority of a Demon Lord. Do you know what their earnest wish is?"

"To become human," Lee Sungmin answered.

Jeniela nodded, a pleased smile on her face. "For the sake of that wish, Vollander and the Death Knights will carry out a slaughter more terrible than any army in this world, without mercy. The Army of Death will rise in the South. Yes, this is the first disaster. Countless people will die, and all their corpses will rise as Undead."

"If that's the first, what's the second?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked.

To that question, Jeniela only smiled without answering. "Unfortunately, I can't answer that. Ah, don't misunderstand. It's not that I know and am pretending not to. Just because it's me doesn't mean I know every future."

"Slaughter Predator," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, his voice flat. "Rumors of the Slaughter Predator have been circulating for hundreds of years, even though no one has ever seen its form. Is it you?"

"Yes, that's right," she replied, though not in the way he expected. "I saw a future where the Slaughter Predator appears. Although I couldn't tell who it was."

"I see what future you desire. Are you waiting for the Slaughter Predator?"

It was a question not worth answering. Seeing Jeniela's bashful smile, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a hollow laugh. He shook his head and asked in a voice full of disbelief, "You said The End is the end of everything. Is the appearance of the Slaughter Predator The End? Is it also a disaster, just like Kim Jonghyun?"

"I can't know that either. The futures I see are fragmented. What I can say is that the last future I saw was the appearance of the Slaughter Predator, and all my actions, and the actions of the Predators, are to reach that future."

"I don't understand," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered, narrowing his eyes. "You desire the appearance of the Slaughter Predator. I understand that. I also understand that in the big picture, all your actions are for that sake, but why do you desire that future? Do you know what lies in it? Do you think you can control the Slaughter Predator?"

"Don't misunderstand. I have no intention of controlling the Slaughter Predator," she said, her tone almost reverent. "The king of all inhuman. I just want to see that monster who kills and devours everything with my own eyes. That's the entire reason I desire that future."

"For that reason alone, you condoned a disaster even though you knew about it?"

Jeniela answered with a bright, unwavering smile.

Everyone was left speechless. A transcendent in the truest sense. While speaking of them, Jeniela had mentioned the Fairy Queen and the Spirit Queen, beings who must not Advent in this world. A Demon Lord must be a being of the same level.

However, Lee Sungmin recalled what happened in Urmuri. The Spirit Queen had certainly Advented then, albeit temporarily.

"The Spirit Queen has Advented in this world before," he stated.

"She broke the old promise herself," Jeniela said dismissively. "I don't know the specifics of that promise either, but since it was broken, a corresponding price will be paid."

Osla had said the same thing, that the Spirit Queen's actions were a breach of contract. What that contract was, what that old promise was, the current Lee Sungmin had no way of knowing.

"I see it clearly now," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman began, his voice grim. "I understand that you are not sane, and that you've told us these stories without hesitation because there's no way to stop Kim Jonghyun. I also understand that all of it is just as you wish, a stepping stone for the appearance of the Slaughter Predator."

"Of course."

"Then there's no need to converse with you any longer."

"Are you leaving?" Jeniela asked. She was about to say something more, but the words caught in her open mouth. Her eyes widened, and her lips slowly closed.

A moment of silence passed. Then, she let out a soft laugh.

Watching her, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's movements froze. Jeniela was speaking directly into his mind. After a moment, he slowly nodded his head.

"Is that so?"

"Are you going?" she asked aloud.

"You told me because you knew my actions wouldn't change just because you told me."

"That's right. No, perhaps you could change things. I'm telling you this because I'm certain that no matter what you do, it won't become a variable in the future I truly desire."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman guffawed at her answer and slowly turned his body. As Lee Sungmin moved to follow, Jeniela called out to him.

"…No, never mind." She slowly shook her head at the stopped Lee Sungmin. "There must be a reason why I couldn't see you. I don't want to needlessly poke at it and create a variable."

"…What do you mean?"

Jeniela only let out a soft laugh in response.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman quickly exited the mansion. The carriage was no longer usable, as the zombified horses could not be controlled. He ordered Yehwa to find some horses to pull it.

"……"

After contemplating something for a moment, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman glanced at the Black Dragon Knight, whose eyes met his in confusion.

DALKKAK.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman took off his mask. The Black Dragon Knight, Lee Sungmin, and Scarlet all wore puzzled expressions at his sudden action.

"……"

It was the Black Dragon Knight who let out a short scream.

His hand shot out, clamping around the Black Dragon Knight's neck and slamming his body backward.

KWADANGTANG!

Having knocked the Black Dragon Knight to the ground, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman unhesitatingly raised his fingers and struck his acupoints. With his pressure points sealed, the Black Dragon Knight could not resist and became stiff.

"Swear it," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman demanded, looking down at him. "Swear that you will never betray my disciple. Swear that you will not turn against my disciple. No, swear that you will not harm the Crimson Archmage and Yehwa."

"Wai…t… what in the…?"

"You won't?"

The Black Dragon Knight looked up with a bewildered expression. He had been subdued by him at the Murim Alliance before, but neither he nor Lee Sungmin had ever seen such killing intent in the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's eyes.

"Why are you suddenly doing this?" Lee Sungmin asked, equally unable to understand his master's actions.

To that question, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied as if it were nothing.

"The Vampire Queen told me."

"Pardon?"

"That I'm going to die."

Contrary to the content of his words, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's tone was perfectly calm.
