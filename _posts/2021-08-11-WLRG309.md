The dense Black Mist churned like a storm cloud, and at its head, Vollander sat astride a Ghost Steed.

The steed had no name. Though he had ridden it ever since becoming a Death Knight, he had never made it his beloved companion. The horse he had cherished in his human days had died of old age long ago. The spectral mount carrying him now was merely a part of his soul, one that had taken the form of a horse after his transformation.

*The Phantom Spear.*

Vollander recalled the name, Lee Sungmin. He remembered their chance encounter on the vast, open sea and the secret thought he had harbored then, that Lee Sungmin was the very Slaughter Predator the Predator desired. That conviction had not changed.

If anything, it had grown closer to a certainty. He could see it now. The man was even more unstable, more violent, than he had been on the water. A ferocious power, forcibly suppressed, seethed within him. Vollander knew that if the right trigger appeared, the violence pushed to its limit would find a terrible release, shattering the spirit that still wished to remain human.

*If you are the Slaughter Predator that Jeniela wished for.*

Vollander slowly raised his right hand. The storm cloud spreading wide behind him was the physical manifestation of the dense aura of death emanating from three hundred Death Knights. Their legion, gathered in one place, was a plague made visible. Their very existence contaminated their surroundings, and the aura of death that Kim Jonghyun so gleefully breathed stained the land black and caused the plants to wither.

Just as Vollander was watching Lee Sungmin, Lee Sungmin was watching him. Beneath a deeply worn helmet, a golden glint shone in his eyes, but his expression was unreadable. Still, his atmosphere was not. Lee Sungmin gripped his spear tightly.

Until now, it had been easy. Zombies, Stone Golems, Dullahans, and Banshees had been no true enemies to him. In this battle, Lee Sungmin had yet to experience a fight in the truest sense. He was simply too strong, and his opponents had been far too weak.

[Vollander is more than a worthy opponent,] the Void Monarch muttered in his mind. [Ever since this Elder was alive, Vollander has been noble and strong. They say that even as a human, he was a knight among knights who could find no equal. A man like that was cursed and became a Death Knight.]

Lee Sungmin slowly advanced.

[The more sublime a soul one possessed as a human, the greater their value to a Demon Lord. Vollander is the worst and strongest Death Knight in history. He became a Death Knight through a curse, not corruption, but the Demon Lord who possesses his soul granted him immense power. He's become even stronger than he was as a human.]

Vollander extended the hand he had raised.

WOOOOOH!

A sound like a wail from hell erupted from the Death Knights gathered behind him.

[Moreover, the man he is now… is completely corrupted,] the Monarch continued. [He has trampled upon his own unorthodox chivalry, the code he held onto even after becoming a Death Knight. That has made him even stronger. I can feel it even from this distance. He is stronger than when we last met on the Ghost Ship.]

*I know.*

Lee Sungmin could feel it too. The incredibly ominous fighting spirit emanating from Vollander felt as if it would swallow him whole if his will faltered for even a moment.

*I have to go.*

He took a deep breath.

WHOOSH!

The Demonic Energy and inner arts inside his body vibrated, clearing his mind and expanding his vision. Within that widened field of view, Lee Sungmin saw only Vollander, whose glowing eyes were also fixed solely on him. Only Lee Sungmin could take on Vollander. It was impossible for Frau, and for Abel as well. They had other tasks to fulfill.

RUMBLE!

The Death Knights began their advance, but Vollander's Ghost Steed did not move. Three hundred Death Knights rushed past their leader as Lee Sungmin walked on without stopping. Behind him, a grim-faced Theos raised his lance.

"WAAAAAH!"

The Holy Knights charged forward with a roar. The moment Theos was about to pass Lee Sungmin, Lee Sungmin's knees bent deeply.

-BAAANG!

The air exploded with a sonic boom as his body became a bolt of violet lightning. He shot forward, faster than the charging Holy Knights. Vollander, who had been stationary, finally urged his Ghost Steed onward, starting slightly after even the last of his own knights. The legion of Death Knights that had been charging down the street parted like a dark tide, clearing a path. They seemed to have no intention of getting in the way of the clash between their master and Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin paid them no mind either. It would be far too arrogant to face Vollander while also dealing with his army. He had no choice but to trust the Holy Knights and the assembled forces. In terms of individual skill, the Holy Knights could not stand against the army of Death Knights. The knights following Vollander had been famous even when they were human, and just as their leader had gained power through his corruption, so too had they.

*Will Teresa be the variable?* Or the support of the Priests? He pushed the thoughts away. That was their problem now, not his.

Vollander rode at a slow pace, a longsword in his right hand and the reins in his left. He did not lower his posture, keeping his back perfectly straight. Lee Sungmin did not slow down. The collision was imminent.

As Lee Sungmin threw himself forward to thrust his spear, Vollander swung his greatsword without breaking his posture. There was no clash. Just before impact, Lee Sungmin twisted his body in mid-air and unleashed Gale Thunder.

PA, JIK!

With a snapping sound, his body vanished, becoming a current of violet electricity. Vollander rotated his body along with his swinging greatsword.

"......"

The Ghost Steed beneath him dissolved into mist that coiled around his left hand, hardening into a sturdy shield.

KWAANG!

Only then did the sound of a clash ring out. Lee Sungmin was a little surprised that the tip of his spear had failed to pierce the shield. He had put sufficient power into that thrust, and to think that a spear made from the materials of a Dragon could be blocked by such a thing.

[If your spear is made from the scales, bones, and teeth of a Dragon, then Vollander's armor, sword, and shield are crystals of his soul, forged by the magic power of a Demon Lord.]

Vollander retreated a little. It was not a very large shield, but Lee Sungmin could see no opening to exploit. A black light flashed from behind it as a sharp blade shot out. It began as a thrust, but its trajectory changed, the swinging slash arcing through the air like a long, dark crescent moon. Unfazed, Lee Sungmin shot out his spear repeatedly, the consecutive thrusts shattering the dark crescent into scattered moonlight. All of it had been a single slash created by Vollander's sword.

PABABAK!

A series of lights exploded between them.

KWARURURU!

Their clash sent shockwaves tearing through the street, slicing and piercing the surrounding buildings until they collapsed.

*It's not even scratched.*

He had exploited openings several times, yet the armor his spear touched was left without a single mark. Vollander strode forward, but it was not his sword that came forth. It was his shield. The power contained in the shield as it pushed forward was extraordinary. No, was it really a shield?

SHIING!

Lee Sungmin hastily twisted his body to evade the thrusting sword. The shield had instantly become a blade. *Right, a weapon forged from his soul.* That armor, that shield, that sword. What Vollander wielded was his own soul, unlimited in its form. If he wanted a sword, it became a sword, and if he wanted a shield, it became a shield.

Now holding two swords, Vollander did not hesitate to close the distance. It took less than 0.1 seconds, and in that instant, his swords created countless slashes.

KAGAGAK!

Lee Sungmin spun his spear several times to parry the strikes. In that moment, Vollander's swords became a great axe. The axe fell. *I can't take it head-on.* Acting on that instinct, Lee Sungmin used Gale Thunder.

KWAAAANG!

The ground in the axe's path split in two, a fissure that cleaved buildings and even the distant city walls in half. A black darkness surged from within the chasm, and from it, a Death Mask howled. Vollander's axe became a long spear, and the darkness that erupted from the fissure coiled around it.

Lee Sungmin fixed his posture in mid-air and finished preparing his bombardment. What he held was not a spear, but a bolt of violet lightning.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear Seventh Form, Sky Piercing Lightning Strike.

Simultaneously with their collision, fragments of power scattered in all directions. Space vibrated, and the collapsed buildings turned to dust. In the flashing world, Lee Sungmin kicked off the air. Vollander held not a spear, but a greatsword, so large that the heavy swing of it covered his entire field of vision.

The greatsword split space. Lee Sungmin dove into the gap, lowering his body until it almost touched the ground before angling his spear and thrusting upward. The spear rose between the trajectories, and Vollander did not evade it.

KWAAANG!

The tip of the spear collided with Vollander's breastplate. The sensation was hard. He couldn't break it. Knowing this, Lee Sungmin still pushed the spear to its limit. His feet strode forward, two steps creating the Two Step Lightning Annihilation. With a rumbling sound, space vibrated as violet currents relentlessly battered Vollander's armor. The spear in Lee Sungmin's hands began to spin, unleashing Nine Dragons Slaughter: Tribulation Lightning.

The currents exploded, and nine dragons swallowed Vollander's body. The bursting lightning dazzled his eyes, but Lee Sungmin did not lose sight of his opponent, thrusting his spear one final time.

Soul Chasing Annihilation Lightning.

The spear pierced Vollander's breastplate.

-KUUUNG!

Vollander's body flew backward and crashed into the rubble of a building.

Lee Sungmin's breathing was not the least bit disturbed. He had poured out his inner arts and Demonic Energy without reservation, but the amount of power he could wield was already immeasurably vast.

"...Aren't you going to ask anything?"

From within the rubble, Vollander slowly rose. His breastplate had been battered by the repeated spear strikes to the point where its original shape was unrecognizable, and yet, despite having fought to the death just moments ago, his voice was calm.

"Is there any point in asking?"

"That depends on what you ask."

"I don't understand," Lee Sungmin muttered, shaking his head. He knew his attacks had not dealt a fatal blow. From behind him, he could hear the sounds of battle, the screams and curses, the cries for God and the howls of the damned.

"Do you know what Kim Jonghyun is trying to do?"

"He's trying to connect this world to the Great Demon World."

"I know why you're following him," Lee Sungmin said. "It must be because he promised to make you and the other Death Knights human again. What's the point? Even if you become human, it'll all be meaningless if Kim Jonghyun succeeds."

"We don't want to live as humans," Vollander muttered as he walked out from the rubble. "We want to die as humans."

"For us, the cursed, death is not rest. Our souls belong to a Demon Lord. Even if we meet our end in this world, we will only suffer endlessly in the Demon Lord's grasp. That is why we wish to become human. To find complete rest and achieve reincarnation."

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue softly. He had thought he might be able to persuade him, but from the very beginning, Vollander had been wishing for death. Lee Sungmin could not offer him anything better than what Kim Jonghyun could give.

"You are the Slaughter Predator," Vollander muttered. "I am certain of it, even if you yourself deny it. All the conditions say that you are the Slaughter Predator. Jeniela… heh heh. That wench saw the future of the Slaughter Predator. If things are proceeding as she wished, then the Slaughter Predator will inevitably appear."

RUMMMBLE...

A black darkness surged from Vollander. From the crevices of his Death Mask, his eyes flashed.

"If things turn out as Jeniela wished, then I will not be able to stop you. Because you will be completed as the Slaughter Predator."

Vollander raised his sword.

"Perhaps, I might be the one to awaken you as the Slaughter Predator."

"...Nonsense," Lee Sungmin shook his head in denial.

He ignored the vibration of the Demonic Energy seething in his chest.
