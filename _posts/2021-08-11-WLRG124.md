The Crazy Heavenly Demon's offensive was as bewildering as Void Monarch's shout. Lee Sungmin could not fathom why the man was wielding Void Monarch's Demonic Energy, but there was no time to ponder the mystery. The crushing weight of the attack forced him to hold his breath and tense every muscle in his body.

TUUUUNG!

The impact threw back both spear and hand.

An eyebrow on the Crazy Heavenly Demon's face twitched. He was impressed. For a man of his outward appearance, Lee Sungmin possessed a profound inner power, but that was not all.

*What is this...?*

The sensation transmitted through the clash of their ki was incredibly strange. He first thought it was Rebounding Qi, but it was not that either. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had faced countless inner qi, yet among all the martial artists he had ever fought, not a single one possessed an inner art as bizarre and impressive as this.

"Excellent!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's hesitation vanished, replaced by a booming laugh as a Protective Qi Aura erupted from his entire body. Stunned by its sheer intensity, Lee Sungmin had no choice but to retreat.

"Come!" the man roared, but he did not wait for an answer. He charged forward himself, a terrifying incarnation of violence cloaked in his aura.

Lee Sungmin met the charge without hesitation, unleashing Deathly Flash, the fifth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. An invisible death of extreme speed pierced toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon's center. Or so it seemed it would. The man let out another loud laugh and powerfully thrust out the right hand he had brought to his chest.

KWAANG!

A deafening explosion roared as their blows met. Through that single exchange, Lee Sungmin realized the Crazy Heavenly Demon was a martial artist who had reached a transcendent realm. While he had been confident he could defeat Aine with considerable ease, that was not the case here. The tension he felt before a powerful enemy, the same he had felt facing Ju Won in the Snowfield, Jeniela in Trabia, and Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger in the Dungeon, sharpened his senses with the faint premonition of death.

[That son of a bitch!] Void Monarch bellowed inside Lee Sungmin's head. [Some horse-boned bastard who rolled in from who knows where is using my Demonic Energy!]

The ancient being was genuinely furious. The immense Demonic Energy that Void Monarch possessed before being sealed had scattered when he was brought to his knees, lost his body, and was sealed at the end of his battle with the subjugation force. He had thought it vanished along with his physical form, but the power the Crazy Heavenly Demon now wielded was unmistakably his own. There was no way Void Monarch would fail to recognize it.

[Don't kill him! We need to capture him and interrogate him!]

*Easy for you to say...!* Lee Sungmin spat back mentally, gritting his teeth. *Don't kill him?* This was hardly the situation for such restraint. The sheer density of the fighting spirit and Protective Qi Aura radiating from the Crazy Heavenly Demon was so threatening that Lee Sungmin was more likely to be killed himself, let alone subdue the man without lethal force.

Ignoring Void Monarch's shouts, Lee Sungmin thrust his spear, unleashing dozens of illusory strikes from Homing Chain Lightning that blurred the Crazy Heavenly Demon's vision.

"Trash skills!" the man cried out, disparaging the flashy techniques of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear as crude. He thrust out with both palms, completely shattering the spear tassel of Homing Chain Lightning.

Lee Sungmin took two quick steps back before lunging forward again, blending Traceless Step with One Step Dancing Shadow. Dozens of afterimages swarmed the Crazy Heavenly Demon's vision as Lee Sungmin himself became an afterimage among them, stabbing toward the man's side.

"Worthless tricks!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon shouted, as if adding a chorus each time he witnessed Lee Sungmin's martial arts. Despite his words, his expression was one of pure joy. How long had it been since his qi deviation that he had willingly fought a strong opponent? How long had it been since he fought with his own will, without losing his reason? A crazed laugh escaped him as he raised both arms.

*Danger...!*

The warning flared in Lee Sungmin's mind just as he was about to drive Soul Chasing Elimination into the Crazy Heavenly Demon's side. His judgment proved correct. A crimson enhanced qi exploded outward from the man, a dual-purpose technique that was both a Protective Qi Aura and a storm that would sweep away anyone who drew too close.

"Good judgment!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon chuckled, swiftly extending his right hand toward Lee Sungmin.

In response, the spear in Lee Sungmin's hands began to spin fiercely.

PWAAAAANG!

The thrust tore through the air, pushing back the Crazy Heavenly Demon's right hand. Lee Sungmin twisted at the waist as he retracted the spear, aiming the blunt end of the Changjun for the man's face. The Crazy Heavenly Demon evaded by leaning his upper body far back. Planting both hands on the ground, he spun his body and swung a leg at Lee Sungmin. Startled, Lee Sungmin stepped back to dodge the kick. He had thought the man was only skilled in palm techniques, but he was also adept at improvisation and hand-to-hand combat. Then again, he would not have earned the title of Heavenly Demon if he were not this strong.

*Are all these guys called the Heavenly Demon just monsters...!*

The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. The Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae. He had yet to meet the Blood Heavenly Demon, but if these two were this powerful, he could not begin to imagine the strength of the third.

[What are you doing! Use my Demonic Energy!]

*Shut up...!* Lee Sungmin hastily replied to Void Monarch's shout. Demonic Energy was an ominous power with a significant backlash. He did not want to use it carelessly.

He deflected the Crazy Heavenly Demon's twin palms with the technique of chaos and self. Even without a direct hit, his hands tingled. Lee Sungmin poured out his inner qi without holding back. Thanks to absorbing the Great Cycle Pill and the Magic Stone in the Dungeon, the amount of inner power he could wield was not significantly less than his opponent's.

*Look at this guy...?*

Was he asking for a contest of strength? The Crazy Heavenly Demon thrust out his twin palms, imbued with Seven Star Power, and watched Lee Sungmin's response. Nine Dragons Slaughter unfolded. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes widened at the sudden resistance he felt in both arms. He poured in more power without hesitation.

KWAAAAANG!

A massive explosion erupted between them. Lee Sungmin retreated on his own to avoid the shockwave, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon also withdrew his hands.

"So you're the Little Heavenly Demon?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat. "The famous one? This is fun, but I don't get it. How can you have this much power and skill when you don't even look that old? Or are you one of those who's undergone Rejuvenation?"

"I'm not the Little Heavenly Demon, nor have I undergone Rejuvenation." Since the man was starting a conversation, Lee Sungmin saw his chance. He honestly did not want to fight the Crazy Heavenly Demon anymore. Unlike himself, who was pouring out his inner qi unstintingly, his opponent still seemed to have plenty of energy to spare. If the fight continued like this, he would have to borrow Void Monarch's Demonic Energy, a choice he desperately wanted to avoid.

"I don't want to fight anymore. I don't see any reason to, either. So, let's just stop here..."

"Nonsense!"

Lee Sungmin had not even finished his sentence. The Crazy Heavenly Demon roared as if he refused to hear another word. It was not just a shout. He raised his foot high and stomped on the ground. The snow in the area had already evaporated, and with that single step, the earth cracked open and sent fragments shooting up. The Crazy Heavenly Demon swung both hands wide, striking the empty air. The floating fragments were then consumed by crimson enhanced qi and shot toward Lee Sungmin like cannonballs.

So enhanced qi could be used like this. The man's methods were fierce, but Lee Sungmin could not help but be genuinely impressed. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's skill in handling enhanced qi was of an incredibly high level.

*How should I respond?*

It was a delightful yet terrifying dilemma. Void Monarch was shouting something in his head, but Lee Sungmin no longer paid any attention. He also gave up on trying to persuade his opponent. The training he had endured for 2100 years in the Mental World, that hellish, awful training where he did nothing but perform, correct, and agonize over martial arts like a machine, came to the forefront of his mind.

Why did he do it? He could have quit at any time. The reason he held on. Was it because he did not want to give up? Because he did not want to remain weak?

It was because he loved martial arts.

The spear tip shot forward, intercepting the fragments. Lee Sungmin executed a dizzying series of movement arts and leaped toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who grew delighted as Lee Sungmin flew into his embrace. He spread his arms wide, welcoming him as if to greet an old friend.

His breath hitched. He parried attacks where the slightest mistake would mean certain death. Deflecting, blocking, pushing, retreating, and closing in. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's hands moved busily, receiving every one of Lee Sungmin's attacks. Lee Sungmin's spear switched freely between straight lines and curves, aiming for openings, but the Protective Qi Aura surrounding the man, his quick feet, and his large hands thoroughly prevented him from gaining any advantage.

*Where is he looking?*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon looked into Lee Sungmin's eyes. The one he was facing and fighting was undoubtedly himself, yet Lee Sungmin's eyes seemed to be looking past him, at something beyond. Even that was enjoyable.

*Looking somewhere far off even during a life-or-death battle. Haha! Just what kind of incredible person is he picturing?*

Even Lee Sungmin himself did not know. Was he seeing Wiji Hoyeon? Or a monster even greater than her? Perhaps it was the Vampire Queen Jeniela, or maybe Mad Wolf Ju Won. In truth, it did not matter who it was. What Lee Sungmin projected through the Crazy Heavenly Demon was a powerful foe, an absolute nemesis, and he wanted to surpass that nemesis.

Void Monarch remained silent. He could have supplied Lee Sungmin with more Demonic Energy at any time if he wished. Just as he had when they fought Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger, he could have personally stepped forward and unleashed his power, and Lee Sungmin's body would have devoured it.

However, he did not. Void Monarch continued his silence. In the nerve-wracking exchange, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had the advantage. Lee Sungmin was fighting with all his might, but his opponent still had room to spare. For several decades, the one who had reigned as the Heavenly Demon in Eria was none other than Byeong Wonpae. Before the Little Heavenly Demon and the Blood Heavenly Demon arrived, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had lived in Eria as its one and only Heavenly Demon.

*He's strong. I don't know how much power he's holding back, but he's stronger than that girl's Doppelganger.*

Just half a year ago, Lee Sungmin had struggled against Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger. He would have died in that Dungeon without Void Monarch's help, but now? The current Lee Sungmin was facing an opponent stronger than that Doppelganger, and although the Crazy Heavenly Demon was holding back, Lee Sungmin was not being pushed back.

*His growth speed is incredible. No, this can't be called growth speed. He's just quickly retracing a path he has already walked, things he has already experienced.*

It was astonishing. Void Monarch had heard the whole story from Lee Sungmin. He knew about the 2100 years of insane training under Denir, the God of Time. Honestly, when he first heard it, Void Monarch had laughed to himself. After pouring in 2100 years, this was the level he had reached? A true prodigy would not even need 100 years, no, not even 50, to attain such martial prowess.

Even so, those 2100 years were by no means a short time, even in a mental world. His tenacity, whose source was unknown, was astounding.

Was this selflessness? The state of no-self? No, his consciousness was perfectly clear. He was fully aware of his own actions, yet the feeling of disharmony was stronger than ever. It was as if he were watching himself move from a step behind.

*That's not right.*

Lee Sungmin pointed out to himself. The exchange continued in the fragments of a split instant. He used both his conscious and subconscious mind to control all his reactions, including attack, defense, and evasion. It felt like his brain was about to burn out. Maybe it actually was.

*That's not a block. A little more forward...*

His body was moving. A part of his mind, however, had stepped back and was watching the exchange. It tried to give advice, but the advice was delivered too late. His body had already blocked.

It was frustrating. He knew he could do better, move more naturally. *No, no, no.* He kept snapping at himself, and before he realized it, his lips were moving, muttering the word aloud.

*What does he mean, 'no'?* the Crazy Heavenly Demon wondered, but he could not ask. He was starting to feel that even his Eight Star Power was not enough.

*What an amazing guy. He's growing even while fighting... No. Is this growth?*

He had seen many who gained insights in the middle of a life-or-death battle. A sudden insight, however, did not guarantee an absolute advantage. It was an ambiguous thing that flashed by quickly. Unless one used that momentary insight to kill the opponent, it would instead shatter the harmony of mind, ki, and body. What happened then was only death. An insight gained during a battle where killing moves were exchanged without rest was a double-edged sword.

However, this guy was different.

*This... isn't an insight. He's just getting stronger. How in the world?*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not know about the 2100 years Lee Sungmin spent in the Mental World. He did not know about the martial prowess Lee Sungmin reached there, or that his current body was too weak, its mind, ki, and body distorted and unable to properly receive the results of that training.

*This won't do. A little more power...*

If he had just pushed him with his full power from the beginning, he might not have ended up in this situation. The Crazy Heavenly Demon raised his power. For a master of his level, adding more inner qi during an exchange was not a problem. It would not create an opening.

However, Lee Sungmin saw it. An opening.

*Forward.*

The command, born from his detached contemplation, finally reached his body. His foot shot out, immediately connecting into Two Step Calamity, the Third Form of Dancing Shadow Soul Break. The enhanced qi that erupted from his entire body threatened the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who, while flustered by the bizarre flow of energy, made the optimal response. A gracefully flowing hand gesture received the wave of enhanced qi. Just as he was about to gently turn and scatter it, the spear in Lee Sungmin's hand spun.

The thrusting spear created a path. Void Path, the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The enhanced qi the Crazy Heavenly Demon was wielding was caught in the rotation of Void Path and scattered with a PANG. Lee Sungmin's spear shot forward along the path that had been opened in a straight line.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a surprised sound. He could no longer afford to hold back. At the moment of life and death, he brought his hands together in front of his chest. As all the inner qi from his dantian were drawn out, a massive whirlwind raged around him.

It was the ultimate technique of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Blood Ring Typhoon. The very technique that had earned the Crazy Heavenly Demon his title was now unleashed.
