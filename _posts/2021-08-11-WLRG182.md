The combined assault from Aine and her undead army proved to be more of a nuisance than a genuine threat. To deal with them efficiently, Lee Sungmin chose the proactive violence of his Demonic Energy over his inner qi.

BOOM!

A ferocious power erupted from the tip of his spear, and dozens of charging Undead disintegrated into dust.

"What in the hell is that guy?!"

The sheer power of the blast even startled Arbes.

Locked in his own struggle against both Kim Jonghyun and Arbes, Envirus felt a flicker of relief at the sight of Lee Sungmin's prowess. The situation remained urgent however. Now fully supplied with magic, the circle had already begun to activate, drawing in the Demonic Energy from across the land.

*It's too slow. What in the world is Chuseong doing?*

Kim Jonghyun was puzzled by its progress. The plan had been for Chuseong to attack Urmuri and sow chaos. The resulting fear should have accelerated the gathering of Demonic Energy. Though confused, Kim Jonghyun could not simply leave to press his subordinate, completely unaware that the Crazy Heavenly Demon had already captured Chuseong.

Envirus, having reclaimed himself as Cain, was proving every bit worthy of the title of archmage. While the unsummoning of the Fire Elemental had been a blow, he was not being significantly pushed back by the joint attack from Kim Jonghyun and Arbes. Instead of aiding Lee Sungmin, the Earth Elemental raised a wave of earth that crashed down upon the two black mages, while the Wind and Water Elementals added their own pressure.

"How annoying!"

He just needed to hold on a little longer. The magic circle was active, and though its progress was slow for now, it would soon pass a critical point. After that, Demonic Energy would flood in like a broken dam.

BANG!

The magic Arbes fired intercepted the earthen wave, while Kim Jonghyun spread his arms to block a storm of sharp wind. Knowing he had to stop the magic circle first, Envirus directed the Water Elemental to assault its protective barrier.

"No!" Arbes roared. The two black arms swaying behind his back shot toward the Water Elemental.

In response, Envirus immediately manifested his magic.

RUMBLE!

A crushing weight of gravity slammed the black arms into the ground. Arbes gritted his teeth and charged toward Envirus with a brutish fury. The archmage was slightly surprised by the direct assault but his surprise was fleeting as he immediately responded, the characters on his arms squirming to life.

He hadn't wanted to go this far, but he was too close to ruin everything by holding back. *It's fine,* Arbes decided in that fleeting moment.

"O, putrid death!" he bellowed, and the Demon Lord he contracted with answered his cry.

The whites of Arbes's eyes turned completely black. A black aura of death emanated from his body, and Envirus's magic could not breach it. Having called upon the authority of death itself, Arbes annihilated the very magic power that constituted the spell.

"Good heavens!" Envirus exclaimed in horror. "To directly manifest the authority of a Demon Lord! Do you wish to be annihilated?!"

"I will not be annihilated!" Arbes shouted, spittle flying from his lips.

Any ordinary Black Mage would have been, but Arbes was the greatest in the world, a man who had reached the highest realm. Of course, that did not mean he was free to borrow a Demon Lord's power without consequence. His soul would undoubtedly suffer a severe blow, a fatal prospect for a Lich existing as a spiritual body. When he eventually met his end, his soul would face eternal torment.

He just had to not die. If he could obtain the body of Aine, who had become a Slaughter Predator by consuming Demonic Energy, he would never have to.

Even as he felt his soul creak under the strain, Arbes barely managed to form the beastfolk.

CRACKLE!

An aura of death, incomparably denser than before, imbued his magic.

"Keuk!"

Blood spurted from Envirus's mouth. He managed to escape the magic's range by using Blink repeatedly, but the Water and Wind Elementals were not so lucky. Exposed to the spell, the two high-level Elementals were unsummoned back to the Elemental Realm. The backlash dealt a huge blow to Envirus as well, and his body plummeted to the ground.

Arbes was not unscathed either. The Demon Lord's power had shaken his very soul. The black energy vanished from his eyes, but an ominous Black Mist billowed from his mouth.

"Guuuh..."

Arbes clutched his throbbing head. He forced his trembling body upright and looked down at the collapsed and powerless Envirus, his face contorting.

"You monster...!"

It was a deeply ironic accusation coming from a Lich, but Envirus, who still endured even after three high-level Elementals had been unsummoned, was more than enough to be called a monster in Arbes's eyes.

Meanwhile, Lee Sungmin had finally defeated all the Undead. Aine had resisted fiercely, but his spear was beyond anything she could handle.

Arbes was about to shout something more, but his cry died in his throat. His face brightened.

RUUUMBLE!

The earth began to vibrate. The ground, already ravaged by their fight, trembled violently. A jet-black light erupted from the magic circle as the Demonic Energy that had been slowly drawn in began to be pulled in with terrifying speed.

Arbes roared at Aine. "Get into the magic circle!"

Aine immediately followed his command, whipping around and rushing toward it. Lee Sungmin knew very well he could not let that happen.

KABOOM!

A ferocious Demonic Energy erupted from the tip of the spear he threw. It completely blew away Aine's lower body, but even with her lower half gone, she flapped her wings and leaped toward the magic circle.

"No!" Envirus screamed.

A chilling premonition spurred Lee Sungmin into action. He did not know what was about to happen, but he could not let Aine go. He hastily used his movement arts to chase after her.

"Stop him!" Arbes yelled, spittle flying.

Kim Jonghyun slowly formed a beastfolk. He pretended to follow Arbes's order but had no intention of fully complying. A flicker of honest curiosity sparked within him. He knew that Lee Sungmin and Aine possessed the same heart. If Aine had the potential to become a Slaughter Predator by consuming Demonic Energy, then the same had to apply to Lee Sungmin.

Unleashed a moment too late, Kim Jonghyun's magic failed to catch Lee Sungmin. The barrier had already been shattered when the magic activated. Aine entered the center of the magic circle first, and a moment after her, Lee Sungmin followed.

When he stepped inside, Lee Sungmin realized he was caught in a massive flow of Demonic Energy. While he staggered in the immense current, Aine crawled to the center of the circle where the energy gathered. Faithfully following Arbes's instructions, she opened her mouth wide and began to swallow it.

[You madmen... I see what you've done,] the Void Monarch spoke, his voice filled with surprise. [You're drawing all the Demonic Energy contained within this City of Hundred Ghosts Night Parade.]

*For what?*

[Demonic Energy is flowing into that little girl. Many Yokai have lived in this city, but none have ever touched the Demonic Energy of Urmuri. Not only was there no way to do so, but there has never been a Yokai capable of swallowing this massive Demonic Energy whole and making it its own!]

However, Aine was different. It was uncertain if the Black Heart could withstand it, but if it did, she would become the master of that enormous power.

[Impossible. This is the Demonic Energy of the entire city. No matter how strong the martial arts theory heart is, there's no way it can swallow it all.]

However, there was always a 'what if'.

Lee Sungmin staggered toward the center of the magic circle. Aine had already devoured a great deal of Demonic Energy. There was no more time to waste.

*Couldn't I also receive Demonic Energy?*

[Don't even think about it,] the Void Monarch warned. [The reason you can handle Demonic Energy and remain unharmed is that it is this Elder's Demonic Energy. However, the Demonic Energy of Urmuri is ownerless. As such, it will be violent and difficult to control. You're already straddling the line between human and Yokai. If you foolishly get greedy and swallow that Demonic Energy, you might truly transform into a Yokai.]

The Void Monarch's warning was true. Even though he and Aine had the same heart, they were fundamentally different. Aine's body was formed around the heart. Unlike Lee Sungmin, she was not bound by her humanity.

He stumbled toward her through the violent storm of Demonic Energy. He raised his spear, but the inner qi of the Violet Dawn Divine Art were caught in the tempest and would not properly coalesce into enhanced qi. The Demonic Energy he drew upon instead was just as difficult to control.

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth.

A little closer.

He aimed for Aine's heart, driven solely by the thought that he had to stop her, with no certainty that he could even pierce it and kill her.

His heart began to beat wildly. His left eye felt like it was burning.

*It's dangerous,* the Void Monarch shouted.

His heart pounded crazily, as if it had been waiting for this very moment. As he neared the center, the raging storm of Demonic Energy swallowed not only Aine but him as well.

His consciousness floated away. Lee Sungmin's legs gave out, and he collapsed on the spot. He gasped for breath, and with every exhale, a foul, sticky Demonic Energy seeped into his body. His limbs convulsed. It felt as if his blood vessels were tearing while his muscles repeatedly swelled and contracted. The Void Monarch's voice grew more distant.

In the eye of the storm, Lee Sungmin's eyes met Aine's. Her face was completely blank. His consciousness... was fading.

He grasped for it. He could not lose consciousness. Lee Sungmin desperately held on. His body was out of his control, but his mind grew sharper. A human, no, even an inhuman being, should not have been able to maintain awareness in this massive storm of power, but Lee Sungmin was different. His strangely resilient divine power allowed him to maintain his consciousness even in this violent storm.

He did not want to become a Yokai. He had clearly seen the fate of those who abandoned their humanity.

*No, is that really true? Just because they ended up that way, who's to say I will too... no one knows, right?*

It was a sweet whisper. Lee Sungmin realized that the one whispering was none other than himself. It was the yokai nature dwelling within him, tainted by the ownerless Demonic Energy, that was making those enticing sounds, but he did not know that.

*No.*

Lee Sungmin strongly denied it. The life he had lived so far flashed through his mind. He thought of it as a life lived as a human. He thought of Baek Sogo.

*If I become a monster, no longer human, how should I introduce myself when I meet Baek Sogo again? What in the world should I say to Wiji Hoyeon?*

Becoming a Yokai meant he would stop being himself. That was what he feared. Thinking this, Lee Sungmin pushed himself to his feet. He tightened his grip on the spear. An unprecedented power filled his body. For this one moment, his inner qi and Demonic Energy did not clash but instead infused him with strength.

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. Through his blurred vision, he could see Aine's collapsed form.

"Uwaaaaah!"

He roared as he threw the spear. Having left his hand, the spear flew true through the storm and pierced Aine's chest. Black blood gushed from her mouth. Lee Sungmin raised both his fists.

KABOOM!

His fists slammed into the ground. A large crack formed on the surface where the magic circle was drawn. With that, the circle lost its function. The raging storm of Demonic Energy vanished. The power of Urmuri, which had been forcibly gathered, scattered as soon as the circle was destroyed.

"What?!"

To Lee Sungmin, it felt like a very long time had passed, but in reality, not much time had gone by. Arbes, who had been fighting a desperate Envirus, shouted in disbelief.

Envirus was not in good shape, but the same was true for Arbes. In fact, if Kim Jonghyun had actively helped, they might have been able to kill the archmage here. Kim Jonghyun, however, had no intention of killing Envirus. Arbes seemed unaware, but Kim Jonghyun could see the love of a Great Being that rested upon Envirus. If he were to get involved in the man's death, he would incur that being's wrath, and he did not want to make unnecessary enemies.

Confirming that the magic circle was destroyed, Kim Jonghyun glanced at Arbes. The Lich seemed flustered but knew he had no other options left. He immediately formed a beastfolk. Envirus had no strength left to stop him, and Lee Sungmin was sitting collapsed and powerless.

"Come here!" Arbes roared.

Aine's body moved. Despite having her chest pierced, she was not dead. She staggered toward Arbes, whose lips moved quickly. He intended to perform the Soul Exchange as originally planned. The expression on his fake body faded, and a black soul surged forth.

This was what he had been waiting for.

Kim Jonghyun's fingertips pointed toward Arbes.
