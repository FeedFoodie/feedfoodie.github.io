Losing one's head was certain death, yet his body, no longer entirely human, clung to his soul in defiance of that certainty and began its slow, painstaking regeneration.

During that time, Lee Sungmin's consciousness drifted through darkness, forgetting all that was happening in 'reality'. It simply wandered through the long, winding corridors of the life he had lived.

He was born the son of Lee Seongjun, the Sect Leader of the Gwangjin District Black Lotus Sect in Central Plains Seoul Murim, and he learned various martial arts from a young age. His innate talent was more than enough to be considered the greatest in history, but he grew tired of learning martial arts and the unconditional pursuit of strength. He could not understand the reality of having to go to college, so he paid no mind to his school teacher. At that young age, he had already surpassed the level of his father, Yang Mugi, and easily overcame the wall of the Supreme Peak.

He preferred kicking a ball on the playground to his parents' nagging about attending a good university and securing a good job. He enjoyed going to PC bangs, using the daily wages from cheap mercenary work to buy the company of cheap prostitutes.

He was a Dragon, a member of a great race and a master of magic. He had never personally seen the great yokai called the Void Monarch, who claimed a vast forest as his territory, but he was not afraid.

He crossed the forest the yokai ruled because he had to leave the PC bang and go home to the academy. The fact that he had not done his academy homework was a source of anxiety, but the martial arts of the Black Lotus Sect were insignificant.

Having surpassed his father's achievements and reached the Transcendent Realm, the first thing he did was create his own martial art. That was the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It contained the greatest speed in the world, magic was great, and when he came to his senses, he was in another world for the first time.

He was given the moniker Ghost Blood Asura, and in the seven-hundred-year tumultuous era of fighting spirit, he was considered a strong contender for world number one. He did not like having people around, so he lived the life of a wanderer with the Asura Heaven Destruction Art as his only companion. After completing Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, he went to find Denir to discuss a greater realm, but he did not want to go to the academy because he was worried about his undone homework.

His life as a mercenary was full of dangers, never knowing when he might die. While wandering as a ronin, he took in two sisters named Jeniela and Gemini as his adopted daughters. He grasped the Stone of Reincarnation and regressed. While flying across the Void Monarch's forest, he was caught by the yokai and suffered terrible pain.

He never made it to the academy. When he came to his senses, he was in this damn world. He missed his parents and friends, but by his side were the two Vampires he had adopted as his daughters. Life with them was enjoyable, and as a C-Rank Mercenary of the Corona Mercenary Group, he felt there was no hope for the future.

In the world he returned to, he called himself the Demon Emperor and thought he might be the strongest in history. Hundreds of years passed, the name Ghost Blood Asura Ju Heebaek was forgotten, and he became Jeniela's second vampire. He did not like the moniker Blood Demon. As a C-Rank Mercenary, he met Wiji Hoyeon. He killed the Sword Phantom and became the Phantom Spear, and everyone called him the Demon Emperor. As a Dragon, he met a futile death at the hands of the Void Monarch. I, I, I...

Who was I?

The memories were a tangled mess. Excluding Aine, who had no soul, there was the current Lee Sungmin, the Lee Sungmin of a past life, the Dragon who owned the Dragon Heart, the Blood Demon, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The memories from the three hearts Lee Sungmin had consumed mixed with his original ones, and the resulting collage was nothing short of chaos.

At the center of that chaos, Lee Sungmin became aware of his own existence.

Very vaguely.

*Who am I?*

Lee Sungmin looked down at his hands. Here and now, was he the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman? Was he the Dragon, the father of the Black Dragon Knight who was killed by the Void Monarch? Was he the Blood Demon, the adoptive father of Jeniela and Gemini? Was he Ju Heebaek, once called the Ghost Blood Asura? *Am I Lee Sungmin, the one who holds all these memories?*

"......"

Lee Sungmin looked in the direction of the sound. The Void Monarch stood with his back turned, gazing at the same swirling chaos of memories and consciousness. Beyond a faint mist, there was only turmoil. Looking at the Void Monarch's back, Lee Sungmin felt all sorts of emotions at once. The strongest belonged to the Dragon who had been killed by the Void Monarch hundreds of years ago. Despite being killed so one-sidedly, the only emotion that remained for that Dragon was an unwavering fear.

"What did you see?" the Void Monarch asked.

"...A lot of things."

"Are you afraid of me?"

"No."

The feeling of fear faintly subsided and then disappeared completely. He recognized this place. It was the world of his consciousness, a place he had visited several times before. Here, Lee Sungmin had always met the Void Monarch.

"...Why... why am I here..."

"Because Jeniela blew your head off."

Jeniela? *Why? Why would that child, my adopted daughter... No, this isn't my memory.* Lee Sungmin clutched his head with both hands.

"You seem confused."

"...It's horrible," he stammered. "This feeling is horrible... It's like... I'm not me anymore..."

This was the first time he had experienced something like this. Until now, Lee Sungmin had devoured the power of others three times with the ability of the Black Heart. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Dragon, the Blood Demon. Most of the power he had devoured that way had barely remained with him, nor had it made him grow much. All the memories he had been unaware of until now were swirling in this world. The Void Monarch watched it silently.

"It's been a long time," the Void Monarch began to speak. "It's been over ten years since I came to reside in your consciousness. During that time... I've watched you more closely than anyone else, and I've come to know the fatal flaw you possess."

"...What are you trying to say?"

"The disconnect between your soul and body."

The Void Monarch let out a short laugh.

"You've always carried that risk within you. It can't be helped. You had a complicated body, after all. Even after becoming a complete Yokai and undergoing Body Reformation, you're still incomplete. You've always had that incompleteness, and you've dealt with it in your own way, but it was never... completely resolved."

"What's the point of bringing that up now?"

"As for me," the Void Monarch slowly turned around. It was a sight Lee Sungmin had seen many times in this world. However, the expression on his face was unfamiliar. "I needed certainty. I had some suspicions, but I thought, 'Nah, there's no way.' That's what I was thinking. I didn't let on to you either. It's embarrassing to say this, but I hated it. The thought of you saying you didn't need me."

"What are you talking about..."

"I'm saying it's because of me." The Void Monarch laughed heartily. "Isn't it easy to understand? There are two consciousnesses in one body. You may be the primary one, but I've always been a parasite in a corner of your mind, occupying a piece of this vast world of consciousness. Tell me. Before you became a complete Yokai, what was it that threatened you?"

"...The Slaughter Predator..."

"To be precise, it was the possibility that you might transform into a Yokai, and what caused that? It was because of me."

"Not anymore...!"

"Do you really think so?" the Void Monarch asked, a bitter smile on his face as Lee Sungmin shouted as if in a desperate struggle. "Why are you incomplete even after gaining the body of a complete Yokai? Why are you incomplete even after the Slaughter Predator disappeared? Is it because your body is superior to your consciousness? Because you couldn't completely make the power you gained from devouring your own? No, that's not it. Your consciousness has grown enough. Look, everything you gained through devouring was inside here. You just didn't feel it."

"But now..."

"It's because my end is near," the Void Monarch said, cutting Lee Sungmin off mid-sentence as he shook his head. "Ever since leaving the Sleeping Forest, I've been continuously reaching the limit of how long I can remain in this form, and now, the real end has finally come."

"Don't be ridiculous, that can't be right! You're saying your existence was what made me incomplete? You haven't disappeared yet, but I've gained all the memories I never knew I had...!"

"It's because your body desires it," the Void Monarch said with a smile. "Losing your head is a fatal wound, one that's difficult to recover from even for you with your strong regenerative abilities, but your body is tenaciously trying to keep you alive. Your plan to devour Aine's Black Heart and push your body's performance and evolutionary potential to the limit was a success. In this extreme crisis, your superior body has found a way to somehow survive and win the fight."

As he spoke, the Void Monarch brought his hands, which had been clasped behind his back, forward. Seeing them, Lee Sungmin's face hardened. His once-large hands were gone without a trace. Starting from the wrists, the Void Monarch's body was slowly turning to dust and scattering.

"By completely eliminating the incompleteness within you. Well, it was bound to happen. I wasn't in a body that could last long anyway. The fact that I've held on this long is impressive enough."

"......"

Lee Sungmin collapsed to the ground. He did not want to understand, but he had no choice. The Void Monarch did not lie. He could not find anything wrong with the Void Monarch's words.

He hated it. To the point of disgust. He hated this entire situation, the way things were turning out. Why did everything have to disappear? Why did the beings he had given his heart to, the ones he wished would never disappear, leave him one by one?

"I was going to disappear ten years ago," the Void Monarch said. When he blocked the Slaughter Predator, the Void Monarch had prepared for his own annihilation. "Things worked out, and only the Slaughter Predator was annihilated. I could have made a cool exit, but thanks to that bastard disappearing so futilely, my reason to leave vanished. Since then... I've had a feeling. That my existence wasn't much help to you."

"That's not true...!" Lee Sungmin couldn't hold back the shout. There was never a time he was not helpful. The reason Lee Sungmin's mind had not shattered, the reason he had endured until now, was thanks to the Void Monarch. When the Crazy Heavenly Demon and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman died, it was the Void Monarch who comforted him. When Lee Sungmin had no one by his side, and even when he did, the Void Monarch was always in his mental world, listening to his worries, giving him advice, telling jokes. Always.

"I was happy when you asked me to wait." The Void Monarch waved his completely vanished arms. "But now that it's come to this, it's also a relief. Because now I'm certain. As long as I'm not helping you, I can no longer remain with you."

"No..." Lee Sungmin clutched his head with both hands, his voice choked with tears. "If you... if even you disappear... what... what the hell am I supposed to do?"

"Even at this very moment, the others are being killed by Jeniela."

Lee Sungmin's body trembled as the faint memories became clear. Before his head was blown off. The sight of Baek Sogo collapsing while spewing blood, and Scarlet, and...

"It's not like I'm dying," the Void Monarch muttered, looking at him. He saw the long road stretching out behind the collapsed Lee Sungmin. In the distance, he saw familiar faces. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the Spear King. The Void Monarch laughed heartily. "It's the same as your Master. After leaving you... I won't be annihilated. I'll leave this world and head down the path that only the worthy can take."

"But you're still leaving in the end..."

"Stop crying, you bastard," the Void Monarch spat, hearing Lee Sungmin's whimpering. He strode forward and kicked Lee Sungmin's collapsed body. "What do you want me to do? Even if you whine and tell me not to go, I have to leave you in the end. I have to disappear. I'm disappearing even at this very moment. It can't be helped. I thought it would be a bit much to disappear without even a word, so I'm staying like this to say goodbye, and you? You can't even fathom this Elder's feelings and just whine about how sad you are?"

"It's because of me...!"

"Yeah, it is because of you, you son of a bitch," the Void Monarch kicked his body once more. "Because you're weaker than Jeniela, your body is telling me to get lost because it doesn't need me, but still. Since I'm getting kicked out anyway, I'll leave on my own terms. I have no regrets. I wanted to see how far you could go, but now I can't even if I want to, but that's okay. I believe in you. I believe that you'll tear that bitch Jeniela to shreds, shatter that damn fate called The End, and live doing everything you want to do... and later, when there's nothing left you want to do, you'll follow me, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the Spear King who went ahead. I believe you'll set foot on that path."

The Void Monarch spat out the words and glared at Lee Sungmin's face. He instinctively tried to smash his fist into Lee Sungmin's face, but he could not throw the punch because his arm had completely disappeared.

"So, smile, you motherfucker. Don't show me your ugly face, sniveling and crying. I told you to smile."
