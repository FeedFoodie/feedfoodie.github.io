Insights were not unfamiliar to Lee Sungmin. He had gained them several times before and had always secretly believed it was thanks to the Black Heart. Perhaps this new understanding was no different, a result of the heart embedded in his body forcibly evolving his physique to resist death.

Even so, the feeling that now settled over him was entirely new. The tangled mess of his mind, ki, and body had finally stabilized. Lee Sungmin had experienced a Body Reformation once before, but that transformation was incomparable to his current state. If he had never known this feeling, it might have been different, but having experienced it, he never wanted to return to the body he once had.

"How did you get up?" Baek Museon spat, glaring at Lee Sungmin. "Did you drink an elixir? Potions are such a pain, aren't they?"

Despite Lee Sungmin standing before him, Baek Museon felt no wariness. He had already confirmed through their clash of fighting spirit that Lee Sungmin was the weaker of the two. What truly irritated him was the presence of the Crazy Heavenly Demon. It might be a different story on a night of the full moon, but as he was now, killing the man would be an extremely difficult task.

The spear in Lee Sungmin's hand felt too light, as did his own body.

*Who pushed my back?*

It had not been a simple illusion. Someone had clearly pressed a hand to his back. Was this also the Black Heart? *No...* he thought. There was no basis for it, but the conviction was absolute. This too was a strange and unfamiliar sensation.

*If I had my way, I'd kill them all...*

However, he could not. Jeniela's command was an absolute nemesis to Baek Museon. He had gained immense power by becoming a vampire, but the price of abandoning his humanity was clearly taking its toll.

*Ignore the Crazy Heavenly Demon. Just cut off one of that bastard's arms, and maybe a leg too if I can... then retreat.*

Having made up his mind, Baek Museon sneered at the Crazy Heavenly Demon's foolishness. He sneered at Lee Sungmin as well. Things would be so much easier if the two of them simply attacked together. Why were they so insistent on fighting alone?

"And you're not even running away..." Baek Museon chuckled and raised his hand, a crimson mist beginning to storm above his palm.

Lee Sungmin watched with narrowed eyes. He did not know its name, but the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art that Baek Museon was unleashing was creating a completely different kind of attack than before.

*How strange.*

Without lowering his spear, Lee Sungmin took a large step forward.

*I don't know what that is... but I'm not scared.*

He knew he had been half-dead only moments ago, yet his mind was surprisingly calm. It felt as if all the martial arts he had ever learned had finally settled into perfect alignment. The two thousand one hundred years of training had allowed him to reach a distant realm he could never have touched in a normal lifetime, but it had not been a pure benefit. That training had left his mind, ki, and body even more tangled, and the sheer length of time had made it impossible for him to fully utilize its results.

However, not anymore. His mind, ki, and body had stabilized, granted a miraculous encounter that gathered and reorganized all his past experiences and insights.

Therefore.

When the red mist scattered by Baek Museon rushed toward him, Lee Sungmin was able to swing his spear without a hint of panic. A strange current enveloped the spear, its flow changing through the Ran's Technique. The crimson mist that had been surging toward him was pushed outward by the spear's rotation.

Before Baek Museon could even utter a sound of surprise, Lee Sungmin's extended foot touched the ground, and he was already standing right in front of him.

"......"

Baek Museon recoiled in shock, throwing his body back. He swung both arms simultaneously, trying to shake Lee Sungmin off, but at his gesture, Lee Sungmin's body dissipated into mist.

*Teleportation!*

Where? Baek Museon instinctively spun around. His judgment was correct. Lee Sungmin was there, his eyes unfathomably deep. The moment their gazes met, Baek Museon felt his consciousness being sucked into those eyes. His body flinched and stiffened. It was for a brief moment, less than a second, but even a second was far too long.

THUD.

Lee Sungmin's open palm pushed against Baek Museon's chest. It was his hand, not his spear. There was no time to even feel doubt.

"KUEK!"

Baek Museon's mouth flew open as he spewed black, dead blood. His body shot backward and rolled across the ground. Lee Sungmin slowly lowered his outstretched hand. Clutching his chest and coughing violently, Baek Museon let out a loud roar and shot to his feet.

"Why, why didn't you use your spear?!" he demanded, unleashing a Lion's Roar from his blood-soaked mouth.

Lee Sungmin gripped his spear again with his lowered hand. "...It'd be a waste."

"What...?"

"There are more things I want to try," Lee Sungmin answered, his expression indifferent. "If I use my spear, it'll all be over."

Baek Museon's body trembled. He was being looked down upon. A crimson, bloody light erupted from his eyes. It had not even been a proper palm technique, yet that single strike had shaken his internal organs. It was a high-level display of strength control, and Lee Sungmin himself was inwardly surprised by the power his own palm contained.

"Uwaaaaah!"

Baek Museon charged, screaming a roar-like shriek. The red mist exploding from his entire body rushed toward Lee Sungmin, filled with terrifying killing intent. The mist, faster than Baek Museon himself, tried to envelop everything. Lee Sungmin pushed the spear held in both hands backward. KUUUUNG! The enhanced qi of the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art enveloped the entire weapon. He thrust it in a straight line, unleashing the Soul Chasing Elimination. A large hole was torn through the encroaching mist.

"I!" Baek Museon swung his left arm, spattering saliva and blood.

Lee Sungmin's spear was faster. He unleashed the Blood Ring Shattering, and a swirl of violet enhanced qi rushed toward Baek Museon. Instead of dodging, Baek Museon extended his left arm, which was wreathed in red mist.

KWAAAANG!

With a loud noise, Baek Museon's left arm flew through the air.

"My... arm...!" he screamed, staring at his severed limb.

Even so, he did not stop. He swung his right arm, his mind fixed on a single thought. He just needed to drink blood. Why had he become a vampire? For the immortality that allowed him to recover from any wound, for the promise that he would never again lose a death trap and fall into Despair, and also...

For what other reason had he become a vampire?

The Homing Chain Lightning and the Bloody Pursuit met. Dozens of spear tassels created by Lee Sungmin's spear were augmented by dozens of streams of enhanced qi. They descended upon Baek Museon like a meteor shower crossing the sky, trailing violet tails. His grudge against the Little Heavenly Demon, his desire for revenge, his killing intent toward Lee Sungmin, all the emotions he had pursued to the point of becoming a vampire had faded when he became Jeniela's subordinate. The current Baek Museon was a doll following Jeniela's orders. There are many ways for a human to become inhuman, but the result is always the same.

If one becomes inhuman, they gain something, but they also lose something.

Baek Museon roared and swung his right arm, a technique from the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art erupting forth, but even that could not completely block the combined assault. The red mist scattered in all directions, and blood spurted from Baek Museon's left side.

"Uwaaaak!"

His scream was long and high-pitched. Lee Sungmin continued to walk forward, driven by the desire to unleash all the martial arts that were now so neatly organized in his head. The Nine Dragons Slaughter he unleashed was smooth and powerful, annihilating the barrier of mist Baek Museon desperately tried to create. What next? Clinging to his fading consciousness, Baek Museon continuously unleashed his techniques, but it was no use. Lee Sungmin opened the Void Path, and it dismantled every one of them. Nothing remained between them.

Baek Museon leaped into the air. "Someone like you!"

Having jumped high, he slammed his only hand down toward the ground. The red mist poured down, following his gesture. Lee Sungmin lowered his spear and unleashed the Serpent Soul Devouring. It pierced the distance to Baek Museon in an instant. Beyond the mist, Baek Museon's face contorted. His right hand, which he had brought down, had vanished without a trace.

"My arm, my arm...!" Baek Museon's lips moved, muttering ceaselessly.

Normally, with such injuries, the right choice would be to flee. As a vampire, his life force was incomparably tougher than a human's, and thanks to the special power Jeniela had granted him, he could recover from fatal wounds as long as he could drink blood.

However, Baek Museon did not give up. He could not. It was because of Jeniela's command. He had not yet made Lee Sungmin Despair. Having lost both arms, he fell from the air, his legs flailing. Lee Sungmin extended his spear toward the falling man. With a slight tremor of his wrist, the vibration split the spear into dozens of images that pierced Baek Museon's body.

"Kkeueueu..."

The fallen Baek Museon was a wretched sight. During his fall, one of his legs had been torn off by the spear, and the ankle of his remaining leg was broken. His torso was riddled with wounds. He should have been long dead, but his life still clung on.

"Ah... no..." Baek Museon tried to stand, dragging his bloodied body. It was impossible. "Jeniela... Your Majesty's command..."

However, death was near. There was no blood here to heal his wounds, nor was it a full moon. Lee Sungmin looked down with stoic eyes at the squirming figure trying to push himself up with a broken-ankled leg.

"To think he'd try to follow his queen's orders even in that state."

[Because that guy is one of Jeniela's vampires,] the Void Monarch grumbled in an annoyed voice. [For vampires, the hierarchy of blood is absolute.]

"I don't think that's the only reason."

[...It's power gained by ceasing to be human. Did you think there would be no price?]

"I want to become a Yokai even less now."

[Vampires and Yokai are fundamentally different. Well... I admit you're not keen on it, but to give up your current self and become a Yokai means you have to abandon the things you possessed as yourself.]

"What do you mean by that?"

[Your thoughts as a human. Your actions as a human. It means that most of the things you do because you are human will be replaced. If you, who were a human, became a pig, wouldn't it be natural to act like a pig?] the Void Monarch said with a chuckle. [I can understand why you don't use Demonic Energy. It wouldn't be right to force you, who was born and has lived as a human, to become a Yokai. However. If what you seek is truly desperate, becoming a Yokai doesn't mean you will lose it. There will just be a difference in method. The way to reach a goal pursued as a human and the way of a Yokai will be completely different.]

Lee Sungmin swallowed a sigh. Demonic Energy was an ominous and sinister power, and becoming a Yokai meant becoming a being that wielded it. That was why he did not want to become one. The current Baek Museon had abandoned everything he had as a human and was trying only to carry out Jeniela's orders. The Void Monarch said that Vampires and Yokai were different, but to Lee Sungmin, they seemed the same. In the end, was it not just a human becoming a monster that was no longer human?

"Foolish fellow..."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon approached, looking down at Baek Museon crawling on the ground with a somber expression. He remembered the man when he was human and knew well what a witty and ambitious martial artist he had once been.

"Send him on his way." The Crazy Heavenly Demon sighed and turned his head.

Lee Sungmin nodded and walked toward Baek Museon. As he approached, Baek Museon's mouth fell open, his blood-stained fangs clattering against each other. He no longer tried to say anything, and Lee Sungmin made sure he could never speak again.

With his head shattered, Baek Museon could no longer move. Lee Sungmin swung his spear, flicking the sticky blood clean.

[Let's go to the South,] the Void Monarch said, as if tempting him. [Wouldn't it be perfect if you could handle Demonic Energy while maintaining your humanity? It seems I had to leave the forest to guide you there.]

"...I have to go." Lee Sungmin slowly nodded.

Why did Jeniela attack him through Baek Museon? If she had come herself, he would not have been able to put up a proper resistance. He looked back at Trabia with doubtful eyes. The city, visible beyond the collapsed walls, was no different from ruins. Staring at it, Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice.

"This is a place I never want to come back to."

With those last words, Lee Sungmin turned away.
