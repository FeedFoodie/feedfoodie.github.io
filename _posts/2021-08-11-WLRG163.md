The words struck Jaegal Taeryeong like a physical blow. Namgung Heewon's speech had always been rough, hardly befitting a young master of a prestigious clan, but Jaegal Taeryeong could not recall ever hearing him speak with such blatant contempt.

"You... you..."

"What? You pissed?" Namgung Heewon spat, his glare fixed on Jaegal Taeryeong. "Do you have any idea how many people died on our way here because of your pointless stubbornness? We started with nearly fifty, and now barely twenty of us are left. The Dark Illusion Blade Brigade you led was wiped out. We lost a huge number from the Namgung Clan's Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps and the Tang Clan's Dark Night Black Mist Squad too. All of it thanks to your obstinacy!"

"That's..."

"Oh, I know. We're the idiots who followed a fool like you all the way here, but I can't do this anymore. Trying to catch the Phantom Spear will just get the rest of us killed like dogs. Call it begging for my life. Think whatever you want. They say it's better to live rolling in dog shit, and I'd rather live than die a pointless death."

His tirade finished, Namgung Heewon turned to Lee Sungmin, who stood motionless, meeting his gaze. After taking a moment to steady his breathing, Namgung Heewon bowed his head deeply.

"Brother. No, Phantom Spear. I, Namgung Mo, will no longer pursue you. If you can find it in you to remember our small connection from the past, would you allow this Namgung Mo to return?"

He no longer called Lee Sungmin 'brother'. A bitter smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips as he nodded.

"If you don't bother me, I won't bother you."

"Thank you."

Namgung Heewon spun around abruptly. A part of him was concerned for Moyong Seojin, but he knew that trying to force her to leave with him now would only place her in a difficult position within the Jaegal Clan later. A bitter taste filled his mouth as he chewed on his lower lip. Perhaps, in the end, he simply lacked the courage. He was the young master of the Namgung Clan before he was Namgung Heewon. Even if he acted bravely for Moyong Seojin's sake, only turmoil would follow.

"We're returning to the Namgung Clan."

"And you're confident you can escape this forest?" Jaegal Taeryeong's voice was harsh.

Namgung Heewon glanced back at him. "We'll manage somehow. In my opinion, wandering through this forest is a better bet than dying while trying to kill the Phantom Spear."

"M-me too." Tang Ahee clutched the hem of her pants and struggled to her feet. Her eyes darted to the people around her as she spoke in a rushed voice. "I'm going back to the Tang Clan."

"Ahee, you too...!"

"Brother Jaegal... I'm sorry, but I don't think I can go on."

She had wet her pants in fear at her age. More than anything else, Tang Ahee wanted to change her underwear and pants, and for that reason alone, she refused to stay here a moment longer.

In the end, only Jaegal Taeryeong, Moyong Seojin, and the Black Hound Leader with his squad remained. Lee Sungmin's gaze settled not on Jaegal Taeryeong, but on the Black Hound Leader. The Black Hounds had suffered no harm during Red Ghost's assault. This was not because they were exceptional, but because they had moved with the sole intention of survival, deliberately avoiding the fight.

"And you?" Lee Sungmin asked. He was the one holding the sword's hilt. Things might be different in Devon, the Jaegal Clan's front yard, but within this forest, he had no reason to fear Jaegal Taeryeong, who continued to chew on his bleeding lip.

"Well... I think it would be better to return," the Black Hound Leader finally said, breaking his silence. He glanced at Jaegal Taeryeong's face as he continued. "I will organize a thorough report of this matter for the Murim Alliance. In a way that won't harm anyone, of course. However... I'm not very skilled with words. If it's alright, I'd like to ask for Daechivalry Jaegal's advice on the letter I must submit..."

Those words were enough to make Jaegal Taeryeong's ears perk up. What he cared for most were his clan, public opinion, and his own honor. If he could influence the report submitted to the Alliance, the very document that would shape those things, he could spin this incident to his advantage.

"...There's no other choice. I understand," Jaegal Taeryeong said, as if reluctantly conceding, and turned to leave.

[That bastard. What an asshole. We're showing him mercy and letting him live, but he's acting like he's the one doing us a favor.]

*Let him be.*

If Jaegal Taeryeong returned without causing more trouble, it was a good thing for Lee Sungmin. Getting caught up in unnecessary conflict would only create annoyances later.

Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin departed with the Black Hound Squad. Whether they could escape the forest safely was questionable, but that was no longer Lee Sungmin's concern.

"Well then..."

His business in the forest was finished. He had acquired the Void Monarch's treasure and gained additional Demonic Energy by extinguishing the remnant of fear. All that was left was to leave this place, head towards Urmuri, and begin the search for the tribe the Crazy Heavenly Demon knew of.

"......"

It was at that moment that a figure shot up from the pile of corpses. Startled by the sudden movement, Lee Sungmin, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, and Rubia all turned to look.

"Shit, I almost died!" the man shouted as he stood. It was Aladur. His chest had been pierced by Red Ghost, but he now appeared completely unscathed. "That son of a bitch, he probably never imagined my heart was on the right side!"

Aladur spat, rubbing the spot on his chest where he had been stabbed. The wound should have been fatal, but luckily for him, his heart was on the right side, not the left. Sensing that resistance would only lead to a pointless death, he had quietly feigned death and used an Elixir to heal himself.

"You were alive?" Lee Sungmin asked, his expression one of surprise. He had been so unaware of Aladur's presence that he had no idea the man was pretending to be dead.

"Can't you tell by looking? I'm alive!"

"Why stand up now? You could have waited until after I left."

"Hey, don't say something so ridiculous. If you left, I'd be all alone in this damn forest. It's obvious I'd just die a dog's death after that!"

"So?"

"Please take me with you."

Without a shred of hesitation, Aladur dropped to his knees and pressed his forehead to the ground. Lee Sungmin stared blankly down at him, taken aback by the rapid change in attitude.

"...You want me to take you with me?"

"Until now, I've been tracking you with those hopelessly ignorant martial artists. This forest is a strange place, and the distance between us just kept growing. I think you know how to find your way even in here... am I right?"

"You are."

"That's why I'm asking you to take me. I can give you as much money as you want."

"I have plenty of money too."

"Ah, come on. Think of our past connection..."

"The only connection we have is a monetary transaction."

"So... I'm a man of more talents than I appear. I'm especially good at finding things, so much so that I make a living off it. Isn't there something you want to find?"

That was a more tempting offer than money. A faint smile played on Lee Sungmin's lips as he nodded.

"There is something I want to find."

Finding the tribe that handled Demonic Energy by relying on the Crazy Heavenly Demon's memories would not be an easy task. With help from Aladur, who was skilled in searching and tracking, the job would become much easier.

The Evil Spirits began to gather once more. Just as before, their wavering forms started to walk.

*This... way...*

The spirits began to lead the way.

The forest remained a maze, yet Jaegal Taeryeong strode forward with confidence. Although the matter of his public reputation had been managed, his mood had not improved. He replayed the insults he had suffered from Namgung Heewon, and his shoulders trembled with rage.

"Are you thinking you should have gone with him?" His unsuppressed anger was directed at Moyong Seojin, who walked beside him.

Following him with weary steps, Moyong Seojin flinched and glanced at him.

"If you had, I would have been more at ease. I could have cleanly expelled you from the Jaegal Clan."

"...I wasn't thinking that."

"Ha! You seemed quite hesitant. Why? Are you now regretting the choice you made all those years ago?" Jaegal Taeryeong sneered.

Moyong Seojin remained silent. Before their marriage, he had not been this kind of person. Looking back now, she realized it was likely all a facade, but at the time, her heart had been drawn to the serious Jaegal Taeryeong rather than the frivolous and arrogant Namgung Heewon.

"...Other people are listening. Please stop."

"If you were so concerned about what others think, you should have been more careful with your own actions, shouldn't you?" Jaegal Taeryeong spat.

The Black Hound Leader and his squad members, following behind, showed no particular reaction. A few of them scanned the surroundings. No sound could be heard other than the conversation between Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin.

[Go.]

The Black Hound Leader sent a Thought Transmission. It was an order. The ten squad members, including their leader, launched themselves forward without hesitation. They unleashed swift movement arts and ambushed the two walking ahead.

Personality aside, Jaegal Taeryeong was a master of the Supreme Peak. He reacted instantly to the stealthy killing intent from behind, whirling around with a startled flinch.

"What?!" he shouted as he drew his sword.

No one answered his cry. A succession of blades swung by the Black Hound Squad cornered him. In that same moment, the Black Hound Leader was already upon Moyong Seojin. Her eyes widened as she saw his twin palms striking fiercely toward her. She drew her sword a moment too late, and before she could even swing it, his palms slammed into her chest.

"Keuk!"

Blood sprayed from Moyong Seojin's mouth. As she staggered, the Black Hound Leader curled his fingers into claws and swung.

RIIIIP!

Her left arm was torn from its socket and sent flying.

"Kyaaaaak!" Moyong Seojin screamed.

She had once been known by the moniker Snow Flower. Her youthful beauty had faded slightly after childbirth and the passing of years, but she was still a beautiful woman. The Black Hound Leader's hands, however, held no mercy. He grabbed the neck of the one-armed woman as she writhed on the ground. He had intended to keep her alive to break Jaegal Taeryeong's spirit, but he now clicked his tongue. Judging by their conversation, holding her hostage would do no such thing.

"Don't think too badly of me," the Black Hound Leader offered as a perfunctory excuse, tightening his grip on her neck.

Moyong Seojin struggled, choking and foaming at the mouth with blood. As her consciousness faded, she thought of Namgung Heewon. *What if I had returned with him? No, what if I had chosen him instead of Jaegal Taeryeong all those years ago?* These were her final thoughts. Until the very end, she did not wonder why she had to die like this, but only of such things.

Without a single thought of Jaegal Taeryeong.

"Why all of a sudden!" Moyong Seojin was dead. Jaegal Taeryeong roared when he saw it, not because his wife of several years had died, but because he could not understand why the Black Hound Squad was trying to kill him.

"Don't think badly of me." The Black Hound Leader tossed her corpse aside and wiped his hands roughly on his collar.

Jaegal Taeryeong was weathering a joint attack from the nine squad members. He was in a precarious situation, but he was still holding on. He was, after all, the young master of the Jaegal Clan, a master who had trained in martial arts since childhood and reached the realm of the Supreme Peak.

"Things turned out this way, but this is actually cleaner and better. It's good for scheming, too..."

"What are you talking..."

"Great Hero Jaegal. You have to die. Don't be too heartbroken, and don't try to resent me. Your death is a matter that has already been settled between the Jaegal Patriarch and the Murim Alliance Chairman."

"...What? My father...?"

"Didn't you think it was strange?" the Black Hound Leader chuckled, letting out a laugh as if he found it all absurd. "Even with support from the Namgung and Tang Clans, you were up against the Phantom Spear and the Crazy Heavenly Demon, two of the top masters of the Supreme Peak. Did you really think you could kill them?"

"Well. I suppose the Jaegal Patriarch is letting you die because he knows how foolish you are. So don't resent me. In the end, I'm nothing more than a blade swung by others. The ones holding the hilt are the Jaegal Patriarch and the Chairman."

The Black Hound Leader spoke as he strode closer. Seeing him approach, Jaegal Taeryeong's face contorted. He was barely holding on as it was. The Black Hound Leader was also a master who had reached the realm of the Supreme Peak. If he joined the combined attack, there was no way Jaegal Taeryeong could withstand it.

He rushed at Jaegal Taeryeong.

"A man has his pride. Am I not right?"
