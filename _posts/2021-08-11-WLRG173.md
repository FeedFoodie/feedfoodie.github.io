Yana had left Urmuri.

While most of the city's Yokai remained oblivious, Chuseong was well aware of her absence. It was not a secret he had uncovered through any exceptional skill of his own, but a truth that had been delivered to him.

"Are you certain?" Chuseong asked, his gaze sweeping over the shimmering night lights of Urmuri below.

Beside him, a figure cloaked in a large, pulled-over robe gave a slow nod. It was impossible to discern if they were a man or a woman.

"Yana has left Urmuri," came a voice from beneath the low-drawn hood, just as ambiguous as their form. It held a quality that could belong to a slender man or perhaps a woman. "She won't be back for at least fifteen days. Even at her speed, it will take two days to reach the Demonic Spirit Pavilion in the Hyujael Mountains. There, she must fast and pray for ten days and nights to achieve Spirit Possession with a Demonic Spirit."

"Hmph!" Chuseong grunted, crossing his arms and nodding. He knew little about the Demonic Spirit Pavilion itself, only that it was the source of Yana's unbelievable power and Demonic Energy. He had once desired to visit the place himself, but the weirdo had dismissed the notion, claiming it was not a place one could go simply by wishing it. It was a shame, but he had given up on the idea.

Fifteen days. That was how long he had until Yana returned. Within that time, Chuseong planned to seize control of Urmuri entirely. It was a feat he would never dare attempt with her present. A few years ago, he had earned her enmity and fled the city in terror, fearing she would kill him.

*That's how it was back then.*

The shameful memory of that escape was still vivid. He had abandoned everything, the honor he had built and the factions that followed him, all to pathetically save his own life. Now, things were different. He could not deny the feeling that he was merely robbing an empty house, but Chuseong chose to see it in a positive light. This small shame was a price he could endure for the sake of the bigger picture.

His mind conjured Yana's arrogant, haughty face, the venomous glare she had shot him in that village, brimming with murderous intent.

*...Mmm.*

The image sent a rush of blood to his lower body. Chuseong subtly lifted the hem of his clothes, hiding the bulge in his groin as he renewed a silent vow. One day, he would have Yana beneath him.

The weirdo standing beside him paid Chuseong no mind. He was an easy piece to handle, powerful with his immense Demonic Energy, yet simple to control. Whatever thoughts were running through his head, they were of no consequence.

*He's probably just having some stupid thoughts anyway. Something about fucking Yana.*

The guess was casual, indifferent, yet the weirdo had no idea it was precisely correct.

"Now that Yana has left Urmuri, there's no one left to stop you," the weirdo said. "If what you say is true, that is."

"If both Red Ghost and Yana are gone, then I am the strongest Yokai in Urmuri," Chuseong declared with confidence. "If what *you* say is true, that is," he retorted, throwing the weirdo's own words back.

A low laugh escaped from under the hood. "I don't lie, and I will be sure to keep my promise. Urmuri has existed for ages as the City of Hundred Ghosts Night Parade. The city itself has a yokai nature, and as promised, I will guide that nature to you."

This was the pact they had made. The weirdo had contacted Chuseong a month ago, seeking him out in his hiding place with a proposition. He would make Chuseong the master of Urmuri in exchange for a single favor. In an Urmuri without Yana, Chuseong was to kill every last one of the great yokai.

Chuseong had accepted the request without much thought. He himself was a great yokai among great yokai, second only to Yana. Wiping out the others who made unnecessary noise was a natural first step to reigning supreme anyway. He had no reason to refuse.

"Let's go," Chuseong spat, his crimson eyes gleaming like flames.

The weirdo gave a slow nod. With a heart swelling with anticipation, Chuseong turned and headed for Urmuri, the strange figure following in his wake.

It had been a long time since Lee Sungmin had to consciously exhale. Sitting cross-legged, he continued to breathe according to the tribal method he had learned as a skill. It still required his full attention, but he knew that with time and practice, it would become as natural as any other breath.

*Though it'll probably take a while to get to that point.*

He was well aware that he was never a quick study. It had been the same with every martial art he had ever learned. The thought of innate talent always left a bitter taste, but he had no intention of giving up.

Lee Sungmin observed the changes within his body. The Demonic Energy at the bottom of his qi center, once immovable unless he consciously strained to draw it out, was now stirring with every breath. It slowly spread throughout his entire qi center, eventually flowing along his meridians.

[Are you getting the hang of it?] Void Monarch's voice sounded a little distant.

Maintaining his breathing, Lee Sungmin raised a hand, and a shimmering red aura covered it. To be honest, it looked far weaker than the aura of his Violet Dawn Divine Art.

[It can't be helped. Your own Demonic Energy is faint, after all. The Demonic Energy you use at critical moments is what this Elder adds to you.]

*Give me some,* Lee Sungmin demanded internally.

Void Monarch laughed heartily at the request and sent his power surging into Lee Sungmin. A familiar pain spread through his body, but before it could become excruciating, the new breathing method circulated the energy.

FWOOSH!

The Demonic Energy enveloping his right hand erupted, growing much larger and a deeper shade of red. Lee Sungmin shuddered slightly, feeling the dreadful power radiating from it. It was a stark reminder of just how incredible Void Monarch's Demonic Energy truly was. His own Violet Dawn Divine Art and the inner qi he had cultivated were incredible, pushing the very edge of the Supreme Peak, but there was still a vast difference in class.

[That's only natural,] Void Monarch chuckled, sensing his thoughts. [This Elder and you have lived for different amounts of time and have led different lives. Even if you trained for 2100 years in the Mental World, that was in the Mental World, not reality, was it not? Still, don't get too drunk on Demonic Energy. Even though you've become able to handle it through the breathing method, it's a dangerous power. Your body may be special... but if you become too tainted, there's a good chance you'll transform into a Yokai, rendering that specialness useless.]

"I know," Lee Sungmin replied in a flat voice.

He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, maintaining the flow of Demonic Energy. He tried to mix his inner art with the newly learned breathing method, but it was not easy. When Void Monarch had forcibly infused him with power, the two had merged naturally. Now that he was handling the Demonic Energy on his own, he had to consciously focus on both techniques at once.

He centered his mind, careful not to let his concentration slip for even a moment. Slowly, the color of the Demonic Energy enveloping his hand began to change. As his inner qi mixed in, the red bled into violet. Lee Sungmin watched with wide eyes before letting out a deep breath and stopping the technique.

*It's difficult.*

Trying something so unfamiliar was not going well. He could barely manage it even with his full focus. Still, it was a start. He had a new method, and now he just needed to get used to it.

"That's amazing," Avrom said, his voice filled with admiration. He and the other villagers had been watching Lee Sungmin intently. "The Messenger's divine power is greater than all of ours combined. As expected of someone who is in direct contact with a god!"

The difference was bound to be huge. The villagers used mere scraps of leftover Demonic Energy, while Lee Sungmin received it directly from its source. He smacked his lips, looking at their envious faces.

"Has the Crazy Heavenly Demon Elder not returned yet?"

"No," Avrom replied.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia were out in Urmuri. They had claimed to feel suffocated staying cooped up in the forest. Wandering the City of Hundred Ghosts Night Parade might have been dangerous for some, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon was a master of the Supreme Peak. Barring an exceptional great yokai, no one could harm him. Besides, he needed the change of pace. He was greatly disappointed that Chuseong's whereabouts had become unknown, and the outing was born from that frustration.

The streets of Urmuri were unlike any city the Crazy Heavenly Demon had ever seen. Street stalls sold a variety of foods he had never encountered, all of them hawked by Yokai instead of humans. Embers drifted through the sky, gathering in clumps, dyeing each other in brilliant colors before scattering and coalescing once more. Rubia's gaze was captivated by the splendid fireworks.

"Don't be too upset," she said, finally tearing her eyes away.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon chuckled at the sight of her holding skewers in both hands.

"...It can't be helped, you know."

"Yeah," he sighed. "It can't be helped."

He wanted revenge, but his target was gone. He doubted the desire would fade with time, but for now, there was no way to relieve his immediate frustration and anger. The Crazy Heavenly Demon suppressed his boiling emotions with a deep breath. Watching him, Rubia let out a sigh of her own and trotted over to his side.

"Mister! We came all the way out here to have fun, so let's have some fun. Don't look so glum!"

"Yeah, yeah." He smiled bitterly and nodded. He knew she was just looking out for him, so he tried to ignore the darkening emotions and followed as she took the lead.

"It's a shame Lee Sungmin didn't come with us," Rubia mused. "It'd be nice for him to come out and have fun like this at least once."

"Doesn't it just mean he's found something so engrossing that he doesn't need a change of pace?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon countered. "That's a good thing. Perhaps this will be the opportunity for him to overcome his wall."

He recalled the image of Lee Sungmin sitting cross-legged, deep in his training. Lee Sungmin's level had already surpassed his own, and now he had a method to handle Demonic Energy. In terms of sheer martial power, if not cultivation level, it was no exaggeration to say he had already stepped into the Transcendent Realm. If he continued on this path, he might truly achieve it and become a master of a realm so rare it was almost mythical. The Crazy Heavenly Demon felt no jealousy. He too was facing a wall, and he was confident that if he could overcome it, he would reach a new level. Overcoming one's wall was a personal problem. It was better to devote oneself to their own progress than to envy the achievements of others.

"By the way," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, the thought suddenly occurring to him. "When on earth is your master going to show up?"

Rubia's excited face, which had been smiling from the thrill of the night market, froze. Her pointed ears and shoulders drooped.

"He said we could meet in the South, so maybe... we'll be able to meet him soon?"

There was no conviction in her voice. Her master, Envirus, was a mysterious figure, shrouded in secrets even from her. She figured he had his reasons for not appearing, but a small part of her was afraid that something might have happened to him.

Seeing her suddenly gloomy expression, the Crazy Heavenly Demon opened his mouth to say something comforting.

It was at that exact moment that it happened.

KABOOM!

A loud explosion echoed from the distance.
