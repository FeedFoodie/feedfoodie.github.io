Ju Won remained silent before the gloating Spear King, who thrust his axe-spear forward to gauge the distance between them.

Fine weapons held no appeal for the Spear King. He did not possess a single spear forged from Orichalcum, let alone one made from a Dragon like Lee Sungmin's. To him, weapons were merely consumables, meant to be used with ease and discarded the moment they broke or their blades dulled. He felt no lack of confidence in his ability to wield the current weapon, even with the large axe blade attached to its tip. True to his title, he could perfectly handle any weapon classified as a spear, and this was, in his hands, still a spear. He was simply aware that the method of use was different. If it had an axe blade, then he had to utilize that axe blade.

The Spear King launched himself into the air, descending upon Ju Won with the axe brought down toward his head with all his might.

Ju Won's response to the fierce attack was brutally simple. His arm, swollen thick with muscle, rose to meet the blow.

KWAAJIK!

The axe severed Ju Won's arm completely. The Spear King had no intention of stopping its momentum, but Ju Won's target was the Spear King's body. Where the Spear King saw weapons as disposable, Ju Won treated his own body as a consumable. His form, nearing immortality, regenerated in an instant even when broken or severed.

The Spear King was not flustered by Ju Won sacrificing an arm so readily. He kicked off the air and vaulted back just before Ju Won's swinging claws could tear into his abdomen. Sweeping the axe-spear downwards with both hands, he unleashed a violent gale from the axe blade, which was coated in his enhanced qi.

KUUUUNG!

Ju Won lifted his right foot and stomped on the ground. The rising gale was extinguished with that single, heavy step.

Having landed some distance away, the Spear King charged again. He turned the axe blade on its side and swung it horizontally.

KWAJIJIK.

His axe tore deeply into Ju Won's side.

*I meant to cut him in two...*

Ju Won had no Protective Qi Aura. His body was just incredibly tough, his bones hard and his muscles resilient. Not even a drop of blood flowed from the wound. From up close, the Spear King saw Ju Won's face and knew the emotion in his eyes all too well. Ju Won was not enjoying this. He was not even tense.

It was boredom. Weariness.

That was how Ju Won perceived this fight. The Spear King's eyebrow twitched.

"Is that so?"

*What, you're not having fun fighting me?* The smile vanished from the Spear King's face. He let go of the axe-spear embedded in Ju Won's side and retreated, snapping his fingers. An ember of enhanced qi sparked from the gesture.

Ju Won's body was engulfed in an explosion, yet he did not even stagger within it. He simply stood blankly amidst the flames of enhanced qi, watching the Spear King silently draw a new spear.

"Don't you worry," the Spear King said. "I'll make it fun for you now."

Within the inferno, the corner of Ju Won's mouth twitched and rose into a smirk. The Spear King took a deep breath, drawing up his internal energy. The spear in his hand felt as heavy as ever, but his feet were light. His foot shot forward.

Space folded. The distance to Ju Won closed in a single step. Inside the flames, Ju Won bared his teeth in a grin. He leaped from the fire and swung his fist just as the spear was already shooting forward.

KWAAAAA!

Ju Won's fist tore through the air with a terrible sound. The Spear King's weapon intersected diagonally with his arm and pierced his shoulder. It drove in deep but failed to penetrate completely.

That was enough.

The long spear split in two. The Spear King slammed the short spear, now in his left hand, down into Ju Won's right knee. He did not push it all the way through the joint, leaving it lodged between the regenerating bone and sinew to lock it in place. He had expected his opponent to stagger, but Ju Won was not so easily thrown. He balanced perfectly on his mangled foot and swung his left fist.

KWARURURU!

A raw wave of power, swung without technique, washed over the Spear King. He cut off his breath and drew yet another spear, meeting Ju Won's punch with a rapid series of thrusts.

Ju Won pulled out the spears piercing his knee and shoulder. They crumbled easily in his grasp. That such a fragile weapon had pierced his body was a tribute to the Spear King's skill. In recognition, Ju Won began his beastfolk transformation.

A cracking sound echoed from deep within his body as gray fur covered his swelling frame. To use his full power after transforming was the highest praise Ju Won could offer an opponent.

*Isn't this fun?*

The Spear King did not bother to voice the question. Ju Won, his transformation complete, now stood as a giant wolf on two legs.

A Werewolf.

A bestial howl escaped from its open maw. The Spear King gripped his new spear with both hands, a chilling sensation washing over him. His hair stood on end and his heart pounded. He lived for this feeling, this euphoria of battle. The entire reason he had wielded a spear his whole life was to feel this sensation more often, and for longer.

*Yes, more...*

*This too is lovely.*

The Spear King felt his body flying through the air, his insides churning. He gritted his teeth and smiled as blood rising from his throat sprayed through them. He had been hit. How was he hit? Even that inquiry was a delight. The anticipation of pleasure to come, rather than the pain, made his body tremble.

He quickly flipped his body in mid-air. From the large full moon in the night sky, a monster was falling.

KWAAAAAANG!

The entire forest shook. Ju Won looked down at his feet, but the Spear King was gone. Ju Won did not bother to look for him. His senses, expanded and sharpened beyond their limits, allowed him to feel what his eyes could not see, including the spear now coming from his blind spot. He took two steps back.

KWAGAGAK!

The space where he had been standing just moments before exploded.

"......"

The Spear King swallowed a mouthful of blood and felt his left side. At least no bones were broken. The Protective Qi Aura he had unconsciously expanded had blocked Ju Won's attack. He was lucky.

*Or maybe that bastard had no intention of killing me.*

"You want to play more?" the Spear King asked, looking at Ju Won. He had a decent grasp of his opponent's abilities now. The most troublesome thing was not the near-immortal regeneration, but the poison in his claws, which Ju Won had not yet used.

"I enjoy a life-or-death struggle."

"Don't give me that cowardly crap, you son of a bitch," the Spear King sneered, spitting out a glob of blood. "What kind of life-or-death struggle is it when you regenerate even after I hit you enough to kill you?"

Ju Won chuckled at his words. "Just as you have something I don't, I simply have something you don't."

"Oh, is that so?" The Spear King let out a dry laugh and lifted his toes slightly. He had suffered some light internal injuries, but his feet were still light. He was fine. After finishing the quick check of his condition, he grinned.

Teresa stretched out a trembling hand. Standing in the center of the magic circle, she took a deep breath and looked around. The forest was a web of Observation Eyes and familiars, placed by Scarlet and Lloyd, and from her position, Teresa could see every scene they witnessed. Inhuman monsters were advancing toward the location where Lloyd and Scarlet waited.

Focusing on one of the visible feeds, Teresa clutched her Rosario. A brilliant holy power enveloped her body.

KWAJIJIJIK!

A beam of that power shot from the magic circle, engulfing the Undead she had targeted. Her targets were the Death Knights. While her holy power was potent against all inhumans, it was especially fatal to Undead like Death Knights and Liches.

[Good job.] Scarlet sent a telepathic message.

Teresa swallowed hard and checked the location where she had sent her holy power. Through the magic circle's connection, she saw the armor of the scattered Death Knights turning to ash and scattering in the wind.

"...Oh, God..." she whispered, squeezing her eyes shut. As if calling for God here would do any good. She knew that no answer would come from a god in another world. Nevertheless, Teresa prayed for the salvation of the souls that had been extinguished from this world. This was something else she knew well. Even if she annihilated them with holy power, the souls of the Death Knights would not be saved. They would simply return to the hands of the Demon Lord.

[How are things on your end?]

"No one... has come yet."

[The Queen will arrive soon,] Scarlet warned in a stiff voice.

Teresa swallowed hard and checked the video feeds. It was just as Scarlet had said. She could see the figure of the Queen heading straight for them.

"Sh-should I attack...?"

[Hmm... She's walking very leisurely right now. Is there really a need to provoke her?]

"B-but if this continues, the Queen will get here."

[Yeah, I suppose so.]

KWAAAAAANG!

The explosion, though it started far away, sounded as loud as if it had gone off right in front of her. Teresa screamed and covered her ears.

[Sorry, sorry. That was a bit loud, wasn't it?]

"A-are you okay?"

[Of course I'm okay. You think some Lich's magic can do anything to me?]

Feeling a sense of unease just in case, Teresa turned her head. In the video feed, her eyes met with Scarlet, who was making a V-sign with both hands toward the Observation Eye.

"......"

"...Yes..."

[Don't be so scared. We've made all the preparations we can. As long as it's not that monster, the Vampire Queen, the ones attacking this forest aren't that big of a deal.]

"Yes... I understand."

[Don't cry because you're scared. I'll clean things up here as fast as I can and come help you... How are the others doing?]

Although she had set up the Observation Eyes and familiars, Scarlet did not have the leisure to check them herself. She was facing the advancing forces of Yokai and Liches, but thanks to her thorough preparations, countering their reckless advance was not too difficult.

"Lloyd is... pushing back the Death Knights. He doesn't seem to be struggling."

[You don't have to worry about him. Lloyd is skilled too. What about the big shots?]

"Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight are fighting the Vampires."

[How does it look?]

"I'm... not sure... but neither of them seems to be having a hard time."

[What about the Spear King, that crazy old man?]

At Scarlet's question, Teresa looked around, but no matter which video feed she checked, she could not see the Spear King.

"I can't see him..."

[There's no way that old man is dead already. It looks like he got carried away fighting and destroyed the Observation Eyes and familiars. Look. See the feeds that have gone dark?]

"......" Teresa replied, checking the blackened video feeds.

*Please.* She gripped her Rosario tightly, desperately praying that none of those fighting in this forest, struggling to stop The End, would die.

[What's Sungmin doing?]

What should she say? Teresa glanced behind her. Lee Sungmin had been beyond the lake since before the attack began. She did not know what had happened behind the dense trees, but she had a vague feeling that something terrible and ominous had occurred. That was why she did not want to know.

"...He's waiting."

[That guy's so slow to get moving. He must be waiting because he's confident, right?] Scarlet grumbled.

She stretched her open hand to the side, and the runic characters she had prepared beforehand began to glow. Space split open, and hundreds of magically created swords shot out from within, colliding with the Liches' magic barriers in a shower of explosions. That was not the only magic she had prepared. She had set things up so that spells would detonate instantly just by channeling magic power into them. With a single gesture, a barrage of power was unleashed.

*This is manageable.*

The resistance from the Liches and Yokai was not significant. Thanks to her thorough preparations, intercepting the invaders was not too difficult, as long as she did not leave this spot. In fact, Scarlet found it easier than she had worried. The enemy's main forces were tied up by the Spear King, Baek Sogo, and the Black Dragon Knight, while the stragglers meant for reinforcements were being intercepted by Teresa's long-range Divine Magic.

*Lee Sungmin can't handle the Queen alone.*

If the Queen had taken the lead and rampaged from the start, this method would not have worked. They would have been picked off one by one without putting up a proper fight. It was only because of the Queen's slow advance that they could resist like this. They had to deal with the forces on their end as quickly as possible.
