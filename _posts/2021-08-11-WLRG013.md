He ran.

Lee Sungmin ran, refusing to look back. His Bright Ear skill had picked up a RUSTLING in the undergrowth, but he had no idea who or what had made it. It could have been a rabbit or a wild boar, creatures he was sick of seeing in this forest.

However, what did that matter? He had seen the corpse, the body of another victim of the Classless hunt. This was no time to overestimate himself. Confidence was a luxury for those who had something to be confident about.

The sound from behind grew closer, and it was not the sound of a monster. It was not the scramble of a rabbit or the charge of a boar. The footsteps were light, far too light for a Goblin or an Orc, yet they were fast and closing the distance with unnerving speed.

A human. *And using movement arts?* Lee Sungmin's face twisted as he ran. This was no ordinary sprint. If it were, his own One Thunder Movement Arts would have left them in the dust.

*Is the one hunting the Classless a martial artist?*

This was not good. He was unlucky. A sudden SWISH cut through the air, a sound entirely different from running feet. This was an attack.

Without a moment's hesitation, Lee Sungmin threw himself to the side. His forward momentum carried him, sending his body flying through the air just as a dagger, thrown by his pursuer, grazed past him with a SWOOSH. He tried to break his fall but couldn't control his tumble, CRASHING and rolling across the hard ground.

"HUFF!"

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, scrambling back to his feet.

"So you're a martial artist," the Hunter muttered.

He stood before him, a man not very tall, with a body as thin as a reed. His cheeks were sunken, the skin under his eyes dark and dead.

"You didn't look like one." The man's brow furrowed. "I picked the wrong one."

Despite his words, he showed no sign of backing down.

*What is this bastard doing?*

Lee Sungmin forced a terrified expression onto his face, though his mind raced, staying calm. Escape was impossible.

He had already realized a few things, however. The hunter's movement arts were not particularly outstanding, otherwise he would have simply run him down instead of resorting to a hidden weapon. He did not seem confident in his throwing skills either, considering Lee Sungmin had heard the dagger and dodged it.

*He's Third-Rate.*

To think that a hunter who roamed Goblin territory for a week preying on the Classless was, at best, a Third-Rate martial artist.

Still, he could not look down on him for it. A simple murderous impulse and a single knife were all it took for a human to kill another. The body was just that fragile. A Third-Rate martial artist was someone properly trained to kill people, someone who would not hesitate to swing his weapon. To the Classless, unused to fighting and naturally repulsed by the idea of killing people, such a man was a demon.

That, however, was not the case for Lee Sungmin.

*A Third-Rate... Can I do it?*

Lee Sungmin slowly raised both hands, showing he had no hostile intent. The hunter's eyebrow twitched.

"What do you think you're doing?"

"I... I don't want to die," Lee Sungmin said, his voice hesitant. He put on a tearful face, an expression that suited his young, fourteen-year-old appearance quite well.

"No one wants to die," the hunter muttered, drawing the sword from his waist. "Who would?"

WHOOSH. He swung the sword once, the blade cutting loudly through the air in a clear attempt to intimidate. GULP. Lee Sungmin swallowed audibly, another part of the act for his opponent to hear.

"I-if you spare me."

Lee Sungmin held up the Subspace Pocket tied to his waist. The hunter's eyes narrowed.

"I'll... give you this."

"What is that?" the hunter asked. While keeping a close watch on the man, Lee Sungmin opened the Subspace Pocket and reached inside.

What he pulled out was a collapsible long spear, a new one he had bought after his old one broke fighting the Orc. CLICK! He assembled it right there in front of the hunter.

"...What in the world is that?"

The hunter's mouth fell slightly open, a look of deep surprise spreading across his harsh face. It was not the spear that surprised him, but the Subspace Pocket it had come from. The pouch was only the size of two clenched fists, so it was no wonder he was shocked to see a full spear emerge from it.

"It's called a Subspace Pocket. I'll give it to you, so... could you please spare me?"

"Can't I just kill you and take it?"

*This bastard. His way of thinking is so damn rational.* Lee Sungmin kept the thought to himself, maintaining his terrified expression.

The man did not recognize the Subspace Pocket. He lacked an understanding of this world, or more precisely, he lacked an understanding of magic, the principle behind the pouch.

"Th-this pocket has magic on it. If I die, it won't work anymore."

"And you expect me to believe that?"

"It's true...!" Lee Sungmin cried out, his face a mask of desperation. The hunter could not tell if he was telling the truth. Just as Lee Sungmin had guessed, the man was a layman when it came to magic. He had a rough understanding of the world, enough to know it was more profitable to hunt powerless humans for their belongings than to fight monsters. That was why he roamed this area, ambushing anyone who looked weak.

That was all there was to him. Magic? What was that?

"...Bring it over here," the hunter said, jutting his chin.

"And put the spear down," he added. *Just as I expected.* Lee Sungmin lowered the spear he was holding in one hand.

This was all part of his plan. He would draw the hunter's interest by showing him the Subspace Pocket's ability. Then he would prove its ability by pulling a spear out of it.

And he showed him the spear.

A weapon.

"Come closer," the hunter commanded. Lee Sungmin made his legs tremble, his face a mask of pure terror. He felt fortunate in this moment for his young, fourteen-year-old appearance.

First, he had tucked his tail between his legs. He begged for his life and offered the Subspace Pocket in return. He even put his weapon, the spear, on the ground.

He was luring him into a false sense of security.

*Can I do it?*

He was not without fear. He kept telling himself to be calm, but if this went wrong, he would be the one to die. Since escape was impossible, Lee Sungmin had only one choice. To kill his opponent.

He had to choose his method. A direct confrontation? No. Lee Sungmin did not overestimate himself. His mastery of the Violet Dawn Divine Art was still at the first stage, and the external arts he was learning were still unrefined. His inner qi were lacking. If he fought properly with a spear, he might last for a little while. However, he could not win.

In his past life, Lee Sungmin's level was Second-Rate. His opponent was Third-Rate. If he had his body from his past life, he would not need to resort to such tricks. He could have fought him head-on and won.

However, now that was impossible. Lee Sungmin's body had not fully matured yet. Even though he had spent two months building his strength, he could not compare to a grown man. Covering that difference with inner qi was also impossible. Even a Third-Rate martial artist would have more inner qi than the current Lee Sungmin. That was only natural, as it had only been two months since Lee Sungmin had started learning an inner arts technique.

Instead, Lee Sungmin had experience. The experience of living for thirteen years was extremely useful in a situation like this. A child's appearance made it easier to lie convincingly. His opponent would let his guard down simply because he was young.

The distance closed. Lee Sungmin moved a little closer.

"Wait. Throw it from there," the hunter spat. The man was not a complete idiot. If Lee Sungmin had been a simple Classless, the hunter would not have been so wary.

*Should I not have run in the first place? No. It's too late to think about that now.*

Lee Sungmin stopped walking and held up the Subspace Pocket with his left hand. His right hand hung at his side. Then, he tossed the Subspace Pocket.

A little high.

And a little to the side.

The hunter reacted. He raised his right hand high and leaned his upper body to the side to catch the diagonally thrown pouch. As the man moved, Lee Sungmin's right hand, which had been hanging at his side, shot to the belt wrapped around his waist. A throwing dagger was now in his hand.

FWOOSH! The dagger shot forward. The hunter, who was in the middle of catching the Subspace Pocket, flinched in surprise and twisted his body sharply. The dagger narrowly grazed the hunter's side, and inner qi boiled in Lee Sungmin's qi center. He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

WHOOSH! Lee Sungmin shot forward using his One Thunder Movement Arts. The hunter was too busy correcting his broken posture to swing his sword. Lee Sungmin drew the dagger from behind his back and closed in on the hunter.

"You son of a bi—!"

The hunter started to roar, but Lee Sungmin was already on him, swinging the dagger wide without hesitation.

"KUGH!"

The hunter screamed. He managed to avoid a fatal wound by hastily stepping back, but the dagger Lee Sungmin swung had sliced a thin gash across his abdomen.

"......"

The hunter shrieked. Even as blood flowed from his abdomen, he swung the sword in his right hand wildly. In response, Lee Sungmin pressed forward without a second thought.

At this distance, he could not retreat. He had to push in. A sword, no matter how you held it, could not cut something pressed right up against your chest, not unless you changed your grip to stab downwards.

This was what Third-Rate martial artists were like. They were not proactive. They lacked the ability to improvise. They could not compose themselves when flustered. They did not properly assess their opponents.

"......"

The dagger, thrust with all his might, plunged into the man's left chest. It slipped between his ribs and pierced the heart that was beating powerfully inside. The hunter's body trembled violently. His jaw fell slack, and blood that had risen up his throat trickled down his chin.

"Ugh... ughhh..."

"...Huuu."

Lee Sungmin exhaled the breath he had been holding and pulled the dagger free. He pushed the hunter's chest with his left hand, toppling him backward.

The man lying on the ground twitched for a moment before going limp. Lee Sungmin flicked the blood from his dagger and bent down. Then he searched the hunter's pockets.

"...This bastard. He really made a killing."

He had earned 210,000 Eru.
