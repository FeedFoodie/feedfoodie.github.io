The spear in Lee Sungmin's hands transformed into a colossal bolt of lightning, its rotation ceaseless. A tremendous power, a volatile mixture of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder's purple lightning and his own Demonic Energy, coiled around the entire length of the weapon. He knew, with chilling certainty, that of all the sky-piercing techniques he had ever unleashed, none had ever contained this much power.

The very landscape wavered, caught in the immense force of the technique. Space itself seemed to tangle and distort, making the sky-piercing spear appear even larger than it was.

Still,, the Spear King did not evade. He simply stood with his arms spread wide, waiting, as if urging Lee Sungmin to attack.

Lee Sungmin could not comprehend such insane confidence. No matter how strong a martial artist he was, to face an attack of this magnitude head-on was madness.

[He must be acting like that because he has his own confidence.]

*That may be so, but...*

The lightning in his hand swelled further. He poured an excessive amount of Demonic Energy into it, creating terrifying power that was just as difficult to control. Lee Sungmin pulled back the spear, now infused to its very limit, its rotation never ceasing.

Watching the light that erupted with ceaseless sparks, the Spear King let out a boisterous laugh. His own two short spears began to spin, so rapidly they looked like two humming discs in his hands.

"Come!" the Spear King roared, a challenge that echoed in the distorted air.

Any more power would be impossible to control.

"......"

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and thrust the spear forward. The sky-piercing, condensed to its absolute limit, shot out.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Seventh Form, Sky Piercing Lightning Strike.

The lightning detonated. A force sufficient to tear the entire area in two, no, to annihilate it without a trace, erupted from Lee Sungmin's hands.

*Impressive,* the Spear King thought as he pushed his two spinning spears forward.

KWAAAAANG!

Light exploded in all directions, turning the pitch-black, starless night into day.

Amidst the brilliant glare that threatened to burn his eyes, the Spear King did not so much as blink. His short spears were still spinning, tearing through the light of the sky-piercing and pushing it back. His feet slid far across the ground, and the muscles in his arms twitched with the strain.

"......"

Then, his eyes flew open. The rotation of his short spears intensified, forcing the light back just enough. In that brief opening, the two weapons combined into one.

"Ha!"

With a short kiai, the Spear King swung the unified spear upwards in both hands.

KWAAANG!

The resulting strike made the light of the sky-piercing falter. He did not rest. Starting with that one upward thrust, his spear moved dozens of times, a blur of motion as he stabbed, swung, struck, and slashed. The purple light shattered into dozens of pieces.

Beyond the fading light, the Spear King's figure became visible, looking like a giant as he held his spear high. A small light gathered at its tip. It was a faint glow, but the power contained within it was anything but.

"Sever! Slash!"

With his roar, the spear fell.

KWAAAAANG!

The descending weapon split the earth in two. Dozens of fissures spiderwebbed across the divided ground before it split again, sending hundreds, thousands of fragments shooting upwards. The power that had seeped into the ground surged back up as a wave of slashing energy.

Even then, he was not finished. The Spear King pulled his spear back, holding it in a straight line until it resembled a cannon, its muzzle aimed directly at Lee Sungmin.

SHWIIK.

KUUUNG.

The spear fired. The small light at its tip swelled immensely, completely swallowing the residual glow of the sky-piercing and reinforcing the wave of slashing energy. Lee Sungmin held his breath and stepped forward.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break Fourth Form. Two Step Gentle Ripple: Flying Lightning.

The two steps were completed in an instant, and from their center, purple lightning spread out like a shimmering web. It changed the trajectory of the rushing wave of slashing energy, but he knew it wouldn't be enough. While Two Step Gentle Ripple could protect him from most enhanced qi or magic, it was impossible to escape the Spear King's attack with it alone.

*What about Gale Thunder's Space Leap?* It would probably work, but Lee Sungmin chose a direct confrontation. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear had a technique for precisely this situation.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Sixth Form, Void Path Exploding Thunder.

The technique was designed to make things vanish. Void Path created a small hole by firing the spear against a massive, oncoming force. From that single hole, the composition and flow of the power would collapse, creating a huge opening. An empty path. Hence, Void Path, but the technique imbued with the principles of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was no longer the technique of old. Just as all the forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear had achieved an explosive increase in power, so too had Void Path.

"......"

The Spear King's eyes widened. He saw the small hole, and he saw it expand as purple lightning settled within it.

*What an incredible Spear Technique. Seeing the unending purple lightning, could this be a martial art created by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?* The Spear King bared his teeth in a grin, his desire to survive growing stronger. If he died here, he would miss his chance to fight the Chairman. *But to think... his successor would be this strong.*

SHWIRIRIRIK!

The Spear King's spear began to rotate.

BZZZZZT!

The purple lightning that had filled the Void Path exploded.

He wouldn't dodge this time either. Lee Sungmin knew the Spear King would not evade, so he had already prepared another technique. Void Path by itself lacked destructive power. It was a technique for opening a path. He was already unleashing the follow-up.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Sixth Form. Four-Step Madness White Thunder.

The light of the world shifted, turning from bright white to a world of red, no, of purple, as if the sun were setting. The Spear King realized the entire space had been swept into the sphere of influence of Lee Sungmin's enhanced qi. He burst out laughing in admiration. *To think he can handle such immense power.*

Simply firing enhanced qi was one thing, but to completely cover the space itself was proof that the sheer magnitude of his power had far surpassed the level of a human.

"How many miracle herbs did you gobble up?!" the Spear King roared. It was a tremendous amount of internal energy, unthinkable for someone so young. In sheer volume, he not only overwhelmed the Spear King but might even compare favorably to the Martial God, who stood at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven.

*But... what a messy and sinister energy!*

His inner qi weren't all there was to it. Other things were mixed in. No, it was the inner qi that were mixed with something else, a faint trace within a majority of sinister power.

*He was said to be close to a Demonic Spirit, wasn't he?* The Spear King recalled the Dark Supreme's report to Heaven Beyond Heaven and understood.

The man was not human. His instant regeneration was one thing, but the immense power he wielded was itself inhuman.

*Indeed... to have such power at that age. It would be impossible to obtain through human means.*

White Lightning exploded. The entire space, imbued with killing intent, rushed toward the Spear King, yet, he still felt no threat. It was true he was being pushed back in terms of internal energy. Nevertheless, he held the upper hand. He might be weaker than the Martial God, but when it came to the spear, he was a monster who stood at the pinnacle.

The Spear King focused on the weapon in his hands. His spirit became the spear. The spear became him. The term Sword Unification was just a glib phrase spouted by swordsmen who prided themselves on having held a sword for a while.

*Nonsense. Sword Unification?* They just hold a sword for a bit, get used to it in their hands, and call it Sword Unification when they can swing it somewhat according to their thoughts. Of course, since the Spear King did not wield a sword, he didn't truly know what swordsmen meant by Sword Unification. However, if you were to change the sword to a spear, if you were to speak of Spear Unification...

The Spear King himself believed that it had been less than fifty years since he had entered a state he could confidently call Spear Unification.

"Open your eyes and watch carefully," he said in a low voice. *You are a junior walking the same path of the spearman.* The words contained neither arrogance nor mockery, only the firm confidence of a senior who had walked the path first.

Amidst the crushing pressure of the space, the Spear King raised his spear high.

"This is Spear Unification."

HWUUUUNG!

His figure blurred. When he swung the spear once, dozens of bundles of purple lightning were slashed away and vanished.

When he swung it once more, the killing intent imbued in the space was cleanly erased. If Lee Sungmin's killing intent was a vibrant dye, the Spear King was pitch-black ink that swallowed it whole. He had no conspicuous Protective Qi Aura, yet he possessed an absolute solidity with no openings to penetrate, no matter where one looked. Though his spear was not forged from a Dragon, it possessed a sharpness keener than any other weapon in the world.

The Spear King himself was the spear, tempered for hundreds of years.

Even as his Four Step Chaos was shattered so futilely, Lee Sungmin did not retreat.

[This is not good.]

Void Monarch muttered.

[That bastard. He's stronger than you anticipated, and stronger than this Elder thought.]

*I know.*

[Will you continue?]

*I can still do more.*

He had not yet used the final two forms of the Nine Heavenly Spears, nor had he exhausted his Demonic Energy. The power grew denser as he felt the monster stir within him. Lee Sungmin drew it out even more forcefully, his own inner qi swallowed by the demonic tide.

PAT.

The Spear King's figure vanished. Lee Sungmin spun and swung his spear.

JJEEEEONG!

Spear clashed with spear. In terms of raw power, he still had the advantage, but before he could push the Spear King back, his opponent's spear reverse-rotated with a strange technique, changing its trajectory toward Lee Sungmin's chest.

CHAK.

PWAK!

The strike was soundless, impossible to perceive. It pierced his Protective Qi Aura and struck his armor. The dragon-scale armor dented inwards.

"......"

He swallowed a mouthful of blood. The armor was not completely pierced, and the inner qi that had penetrated with the blow were pushed back by repulsive force, but it was not a perfect defense. He had suffered internal injuries.

He paid them no mind. His mutated body, now close to that of a Yokai, healed such wounds in an instant.

Planting the foot that had been pushed back, he flicked his spear in the opposite direction, the flexible shaft aiming for the Spear King's body.

DALKAK.

The Spear King's weapon split into two. Without retrieving the spear half-piercing the armor, he pushed it in deeper while swinging the other to block Lee Sungmin's attack.

He had predicted such a defense. Lee Sungmin supported his spear with one hand and thrust his open left palm toward the Spear King's chest, unleashing an enhanced qi art from the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

The Spear King's cold eyes shifted, still holding the thrill of battle. His body flipped in mid-air, dodging the palm strike. The enhanced qi, having lost its target, erupted and tore through the air. The Spear King kicked the short spear embedded in the armor, driving it deeper.

"Keuheup!"

The armor was pierced. The spear bored through his stomach. Lee Sungmin staggered back, coughing up a gush of blood, but even then, he unleashed another technique.

Deadly Flash Lightning. A spear faster than light shot out, but to the Spear King, it looked slow.

The Spear King reached for the short spear in Lee Sungmin's stomach. At his touch, the embedded weapon began to spin.

"Krrrrk!"

The pain was immense. His internal organs were caught in the rotation and torn apart. The spinning spear was pulled free, dragging his tangled intestines out with it as blood sprayed everywhere.

The Deathly Flash could not reach its target. Lee Sungmin faltered and retreated, continuously vomiting blood.

"You're enduring it well."

He gasped for breath, clutching the wound with a trembling hand. It was already healing. The shattered organs, the gaping hole. As he felt the wound closing up, Lee Sungmin realized once again that his body had truly become that of a monster.

"So you can even regenerate that," the Spear King muttered, his voice delighted.

Lee Sungmin gasped for air and spat out another mouthful of blood.

*More.*

Lee Sungmin gripped his spear tightly. There were still two forms left in the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Forms he had rarely ever used.

"You still have some tricks up your sleeve, don't you," the Spear King said, striding forward. "Go on, bring them out. Let's see how outstanding they are that you've kept them hidden so carefully."

"...Huuuuu..."

Lee Sungmin let out a long breath. He was already preparing the eighth form, even before the Spear King could urge him on again.

The spear moved slowly, afterimages trailing its languid movements. His entire body seemed to stretch out as he guided the weapon through the air.

The Spear King watched, a pounding sense of anticipation growing within him.

*Fast... no. What is this?* The sight of the slowly stirring spear sent a chill down his spine. Lee Sungmin's eyes gleamed with an eerie light, and his presence, mixed with Demonic Energy, painted the face of a hideous monster upon him.

There was a reason Lee Sungmin had never used the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear beyond the sky-piercing technique. The later forms could not be used without the insights of the Transcendent Realm. He could imitate them, but he could not display their true power. Now, however, it was different. Now, he could use them.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Eighth Form, Illusionary World.

Beyond the slow, continuous movements, the spear shot forward.
