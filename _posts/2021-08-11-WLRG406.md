Yana's eyes fluttered open to a damp, watery mist. She stared ahead, dazed, before pushing herself up to survey her surroundings.

She had no memory of what happened after entering the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, a fact that left her flustered, but that confusion was instantly eclipsed by a greater shock. Lee Sungmin was beside her.

Yana scrambled back, her heart leaping in surprise.

"Hello," Lee Sungmin said, his smile bitter as he raised a hand in greeting.

Yana could only blink, staring blankly at his face. A mountain of questions rose within her.

*Why are you here?*

*What happened?*

However, before she could voice any of them, he closed his raised hand, and a small orb of light formed between his fingers.

"Look," he said simply, thinking it better to show her than to explain.

The orb drifted toward her. Bewildered, Yana reached out and accepted it.

"......"

A soft gasp escaped her lips as her expression hardened. Lee Sungmin had transferred a part of his memories, and within them lay the answers to everything she wanted to ask. Her shoulders began to tremble violently as the truth crashed down upon her. Jeniela had attacked the forest, and the Void Monarch… the Void Monarch…

"Ah… Aah…"

A choked sob broke from her, and she covered her face with both hands. Watching Yana collapse to her knees, wailing, Lee Sungmin swallowed a sigh.

*That bastard.*

He cursed the departed Void Monarch. Thinking about it now, it was just like him. The Void Monarch had never left Yana a farewell. Their entire relationship had been one of Yana's blind, one-sided devotion. He had never shown her any emotion in return, and even as he vanished, he had offered her no words.

So, Lee Sungmin could say nothing. He wanted to comfort the weeping woman, but he felt he had no right.

"I don't… resent… him…" Yana finally managed to speak through her sobs, her head bowed low. Her voice was choked. "Because it was… what he wanted. I… I'm not greedy."

However, her tears refused to stop. *Bastard,* Lee Sungmin cursed the Void Monarch again, a silent condemnation the Yokai would never hear. How nice would it have been if he had just left her some final words.

"Was he… satisfied?"

Yana lifted her head, her beautiful face a mess of tears. She swallowed another sob and roughly rubbed her eyes with the back of her hand.

"Was he… satisfied, until the very end…?"

"…Yes," Lee Sungmin replied with a nod.

Yana already knew the answer. The memories he had shown her included the sound of the Void Monarch's laughter, thrilled with anticipation for what was to come. Nevertheless, she had to ask. She needed to receive that confirmation, even if it was just like this.

She wasn't upset that he had left without a word for her. She didn't think her situation was miserable. He was always that kind of Yokai, selfish and inconsiderate of others. Still… still.

She knew. The Void Monarch hadn't even remembered her in the first place. Saving the powerless fox Yana had been an insignificant event for him, just a small act of kindness performed on a whim. Yana was the only one who cherished it as if it were some great destiny.

"If that's the case… I'm okay…"

*She wasn't okay.*

A bitter resentment existed, no matter how she tried to ignore it. *Why wasn't it me? Why didn't he cherish me the way he cherished that human?*

*Why, why…* Yana bit her lower lip, forcefully suppressing the ugly thoughts she harbored. Though the Void Monarch left no words for her, she did not resent him. She refused to show her jealousy toward the man in front of her. She knew the Void Monarch would not have wanted that.

Yana bowed her head and cried again.

[I do not understand.]

At her words pushing him away, Lee Sungmin left the Demonic Spirit Pavilion alone. The Demonic Spirit muttered inside his head.

[Wouldn't it have been better to leave without saying anything at all?]

*I feel bad for her.*

[What do you have to feel bad about? The Void Monarch was the one who left without a word. Why are you getting involved and wasting your emotions on them?]

*I did it because I wanted to,* Lee Sungmin retorted. *Don't give me any crap. Not unless you want me to leave you behind.*

[How cruel.]

*You're not much help to me right now. Why don't you give me something like the mistletoe?*

At that demand, the Demonic Spirit let out a dry laugh in his head.

[There's no way I have another one of those.]

Lee Sungmin ignored the Demonic Spirit and prepared for a Spatial Movement. He searched through his memories, but his expression stiffened slightly.

*There are no coordinates?*

The coordinates for the Sleeping Forest were gone. He searched again, but the spatial coordinates for the nearby village did not exist in Keiserod's memories. He thought about riding the Fairy Horse, but it could not be summoned. It seemed Osla had taken it back from him while he was speaking with the Demonic Spirit.

"He must be furious."

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly, thinking of those he had left behind. It was not as if he had no other way, however. There was a city nearby, though it was a little far. He set it as his destination and cast Spatial Movement.

[Impressive…]

The Demonic Spirit expressed his admiration at the perfectly executed spell. He understood most of what had happened to Lee Sungmin but could not help but be impressed seeing him handle Dragonic Magic so flawlessly.

[What happened to you was not my intention. This… I don't know whether to call it a coincidence or a miracle. I gave you the Black Heart to make you a Slaughter Predator, but…]

Lee Sungmin ignored the muttering and entered the city. *Aren't you going straight to the forest?* the Demonic Spirit asked.

To meet Wiji Hoyeon, he had to enter the Sleeping Forest. He honestly doubted that just entering would allow him to reach the space where she was, but he could not simply avoid going.

*I have something to do.*

The more he thought of Wiji Hoyeon, the more his chest ached. It was the same sense of hopelessness he had felt when preparing to fight Jeniela. In fact, the situation was even worse now. Back then, there were still things he could do. He had strengthened his body by devouring Aine's heart, received Osla's protection, and had the help of his comrades.

This time, he had nothing.

If possible, he wanted to avoid fighting her, but that was impossible. Wiji Hoyeon, connected to the Spirit, would act as a calamity of The End if left alone. In fact, at this point, she had already become one.

"You stupid bastard," Lee Sungmin muttered as he walked down the street.

The words were directed at the Demonic Spirit, who, despite being suddenly cursed, could not refute them. It was not as if he had predicted this situation. Just because he made arrangements did not mean everything would go according to plan.

The Demonic Spirit had done his best to forge Wiji Hoyeon, granting her an absurd amount of talent and the destiny of a warlord. She grew just as he had arranged. He had simply failed to check and respond to all the variables. The Slaughter Predator had appeared earlier than scheduled, and the mistletoe was consumed too meaninglessly. All those things had piled up to create this result. His failure to quickly eliminate the Spirit Queen had allowed her to struggle desperately, and the sudden dimensional connection had caused Wiji Hoyeon to fall into a dimensional rift.

In the end, it was due to the Demonic Spirit's incompetence. Lee Sungmin ignored the unresisting entity and clutched the key in his pocket. He had heard from the Demonic Spirit what this key was, but there was no way for him to use it, at least not for now. Originally, the key falling into the hands of a reincarnator was itself one of the biggest errors of this world.

[And I was the one who created that error.]

Was this a protest against being called incompetent? The Demonic Spirit did not miss the opportunity to speak.

*So what? I can't even use it right now.*

The Demonic Spirit fell silent again. Lee Sungmin stopped walking. He looked around and fell into thought for a moment. The Demonic Spirit, who had been quiet, could not figure it out and finally asked a question.

[What are you trying to do?]

*I'm choosing.*

Lee Sungmin was standing in the middle of a street lined with inns. He began to seriously contemplate which one to enter as he surveyed the rows of them.

[Are you trying to rest before going to the forest? Any place should be fine…]

*No, it's not for rest.*

Of course, he also felt the need to rest. He did not think he would gain anything from meditating now, but he felt he should review everything one last time, as if it were the end.

[Meditation is important. The one who created the Battle God Chronicle, the first human to reach the realm of the absolute, cast off the Cycle of Mortality through deep meditation.]

*I don't know what world that amazing guy is from, but I'm a martial artist.*

Just because his closed insight had opened did not mean he had gained some incredible talent. In fact, his opened insight was not much better than what Wiji Hoyeon possessed at the age of thirteen. The thirteen-year-old Wiji Hoyeon had modified the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear to suit him, and his own changing of the incantation during the fight with Jeniela was only on that same level.

Still, Lee Sungmin had knowledge.

"I've decided."

After long deliberation, he chose an inn. He took a deep breath and entered. The reason for his choice was nothing special. The patrons coming and going from this particular establishment were neater and more high-class than those at the others.

"I'd like to stay for a day," he said to the approaching innkeeper and paid for the room.

The innkeeper tried to guide him, but Lee Sungmin shook his head. There was something he had to do first.

"…Where is the bathroom?"

He had to keep his promise to the Void Monarch.

"So this is the place," the Martial God muttered, his expression solemn. He gazed at the city's tall gates, clasping his one and only hand behind his back.

Moon Queen, standing beside him, gave a slight nod. "Yes. This is the city my brother spoke of, the place where the calamity of The End is hiding."

At her words, the Martial God clenched his fist and nodded. The Spirit Medium, who had been traveling with them, could not join them here today. He had said it was the Spirit's will, and the Martial God did not doubt his words.

"In this vast city, where is the bastard?"

"Do not be hasty, Martial God," Moon Queen said with a slight bow. "There's no need for you to go searching for him."

She continued, "If you unleash your power, he will reveal himself to attack you."

"…Hmm…"

The Martial God felt his heart pound slightly. After his defeat at the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he had entered ten years of seclusion. He had returned to the world since then, but he had yet to fight a worthy opponent. The fact that he had trained to defeat The End yet had not been able to face a calamity had displeased him until now.

He had wanted to fight the Spirit Queen. He had wanted to fight the Vampire Queen, too, but he could not do as he pleased. The Spirit Medium, who heard the voice of the Spirit, always threw cold water on his fighting spirit. *It is not yet time.* The Martial God could not argue with those words.

However, not today. The Spirit Medium had permitted the fight. Finally, he could defeat a calamity of The End. The Martial God drew out his power with a sense of anticipation.

"…He is Ilcheon's disciple, you said?"

To think that master and disciple would both become calamities of The End. As the Martial God muttered, Moon Queen nodded.

"Yes. Don't let your guard down, Martial God. The man from ten years ago would never have been your opponent, but in those ten years, the Demon Emperor's disciple has become a monster who has strayed from the path of humanity."

"Then all the more reason I must kill him," the Martial God spat forcefully. "To abandon the martial path and become drunk on the power of a wretched Demonic Spirit… The deceased Ilcheon must be wailing in the afterlife."
