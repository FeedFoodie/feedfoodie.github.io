SHWAAAA!

Dozens of writhing tentacles shot from beyond the mountain gate, swelling as they passed through and swept across the area.

The prepared Daoists of the Wudang Sect met the attack by solidifying their sword formation. They drew their swords in unison and struck down at the encroaching appendages. Their blades, fully imbued with enhanced sword qi, struck down repeatedly, yet the tentacles refused to break.

As the elongated tentacles contracted, Aine vaulted over the mountain gate, her jet-black hair flying behind her. She landed silently in the middle of the Daoists, saying nothing as she licked a blood-soaked lower lip and shrugged.

A gurgling sound accompanied the writhing of Aine's tentacles as the thick appendages mutated into sharp blades, a grin spreading across her face. Sparks flew from the blazing enhanced qi that now covered the tentacles, and in the next moment, jet-black flames wrapped around the crackling energy.

"Ooooh!" Preskan exclaimed in admiration, hopping over the mountain gate behind her.

"......"

With every movement of Aine's tentacles, the black flames spread. These were the inextinguishable flames she had gained from devouring the Spirit King of Flame whole, and they now moved according to her will, descending upon the Daoists. The flames clung to their Protective Qi Auras while their swords broke futilely against Aine's tentacles.

Aine giggled, walking with light steps as her tentacles exploited the gaps in the faltering sword formation. They pierced the chests of the flailing Daoists, whose bodies were already covered in flames. The moment a tentacle touched a heart, its tip split open into a mouth that chewed and swallowed the organ whole.

Chen and Kun followed silently behind her, seeing no need to step in. Without the Sword Immortal, the Wudang Sect was merely a playground for Aine's amusement.

"S-Sect Leader!"

Watching the massacre Aine committed, the Wudang Sect's leader's face contorted. "Send someone to the Great Master," he spat, gnawing on his lower lip.

"Yes...!"

The Daoists who received the order ran off. Once the Sect Leader confirmed their departure, he drew his own sword. At his command, the Tai Chi Sword Formation unfolded. It was considered one of the three great formations of the orthodox murim, alongside Shaolin's One Hundred and Eight Arhat Formation and the Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation.

Aine grinned at the wave of swords rushing toward her. All the tentacles that shot from her back became blades. She slowly raised her hands and spread them wide as a hard, keratinous armor covered her arms and her fingers grew into long claws.

Lee Sungmin shot across the sky like a streak of lightning, leaping over the rugged peaks.

[Wouldn't it be better to run?] the Void Monarch asked. [Whatever you do here is your freedom, but you should know that what you're doing is interfering with Jeniela's plans.]

*I know that too.*

[I don't know why Jeniela wants to keep you alive. Maybe it's just for fun. However, if you keep getting in her way, Jeniela will kill you here.]

*I know that too.*

The collapsed Sword Lake came into view.

*But I have to do what I can. Killing Jeniela is impossible, but still... I can do other things, and right now, the Sword Immortal is holding Jeniela down.*

Aine was isolated. There was no telling if another chance like this would come. With Chen and Kun also separated from Jeniela, now was the perfect time to kill them.

[You've changed a lot,] the Void Monarch chuckled, having read Lee Sungmin's thoughts. [You're not swept away by emotions like before. Though you've lost your cute side.]

Lee Sungmin's body plummeted toward the chaos below.

The Tai Chi Sword Formation was on the verge of collapse. The Daoists maintaining the formation trickled black blood from between their tightly clenched lips, suffering from repeated impacts and internal injuries. The outcome of the battle was already clear to anyone watching, but the Wudang Sect's Daoists could not retreat.

Aine did not dislike such overwhelming fights. While there was enjoyment in winning through effort, she viewed fighting as a hunt. She much preferred to hunt and eat easily rather than expend energy on a difficult chase. This time was no different. There was plenty to eat, the Tai Chi Sword Formation offered no real pressure, and the hunt was easy.

BZZZZT!

Aine's body was thrown backward. She flipped in mid-air and righted herself, her eyes widening. Her senses were not dull, she simply had not felt his approach. Lee Sungmin was just that fast. He had descended before she could even sense him.

The Daoists of the Wudang Sect did not understand what had happened. Aine realized Lee Sungmin had attacked a moment too late, and Kun and Chen were even slower.

From within the crackling electric currents, Lee Sungmin rose. Aine's golden eyes widened as she saw him, a man with eyes the same color as her own. In that single moment, she felt an immense ecstasy she had never before experienced in her life. She had never thought she would meet him here. Ten years had passed, but their appearances had barely changed. No, even if they had changed tremendously, Aine would have recognized Lee Sungmin without fail.

Her heart pounded. She kicked off the ground, but perhaps because she was so excited, her steps tangled and she staggered forward, falling to the ground. She laughed maniacally, scraping the earth with outstretched hands to support her collapsing body before pushing off and leaping forward again.

Watching Aine pounce like a beast, Lee Sungmin felt more displeasure than ecstasy. Their hearts beat in unison, the resonance of Demonic Energy he had felt in Urmuri and then in Trabia shaking his chest.

"......"

Aine unleashed every tentacle she could control, sending hundreds of them raining down from the low sky.

Lee Sungmin raised the spear in his left hand. With a whoosh, it spun once before he caught it in both hands.

"......"

Light exploded as violet electric currents boiled around him.

KWAAANG!

The night sky momentarily brightened as if it were midday. Aine's tentacles were brutally shattered by the lightning Lee Sungmin unleashed.

She felt no pain. Though the tentacles were a part of her, losing them was not a significant loss. The scattered pieces of flesh simply reattached to her and became new tentacles. Even if they were completely annihilated, it would not matter. Her body, constructed around the Black Heart, would regenerate infinitely as long as her power was not depleted.

"Heart Thief!" Preskan shrieked from a distance, where he had been watching, worried about getting caught up in the fight. His eyes flashed in the pitch-black darkness, his robe trembling.

How could he ever forget? To Preskan, Lee Sungmin was the starting point of all his misfortunes. Before meeting that man, Preskan had lived happily in a deep underground Dungeon. He could conduct whatever research he wanted without worrying about others. Even when mercenaries discovered the Dungeon, it was not a major problem for him. He could have handled it easily.

Everything went wrong because of Lee Sungmin. His mental magic was reflected, dealing him a fatal blow, and as a result, he could not even kill Lloyd after joining forces with Aine. If he had never gotten involved with Lee Sungmin, he would have annihilated the mercenaries without issue and killed Lloyd when he later attacked, and after that? He might have had to be a little discreet, but he could have built a Dungeon somewhere else and settled down.

It all went wrong.

Because Lloyd survived, Preskan had to constantly flee from the Mages Guild's pursuit. That thief had stolen one of the two Black Hearts he had spent his entire life creating. While fleeing, he went all the way to the North, only to be captured by the Vampire Queen. He was forced to become a member of Predator and was managed by Arbes. He suffered all sorts of insults and contempt and almost had Aine taken away from him.

It was an age of tears, and the starting point of it all, the Heart Thief, was right in front of him.

Preskan trembled with a thrill. Now was the time to reclaim what had been stolen from him more than ten years ago.

"Aine!" My daughter! Preskan cried out. "Rip out that bastard's heart and chew on it!"

However, there was no need for his command. Aine had intended to do so from the beginning. She retracted all the writhing tentacles back inside her body, knowing that simply moving them lacked power. The muscles all over her body rippled as immense power settled within her flesh, her physique remaining unchanged.

"It's been a while," Aine whispered. She seemed quite pleased to see him, but Lee Sungmin felt nothing but a stirring killing intent in his chest.

"......"

Aine moved, slipping into his blind spot with no wasted motion. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. She was fast enough, and the fist she threw at that speed followed the trajectory of a martial art that had distilled the essence of countless fist techniques.

*As expected.*

Just by seeing that, he could tell how many beings Aine had devoured in her life. Unlike him, Aine had actively preyed on others. Of course, even if Lee Sungmin had preyed as much as she had, he would not have achieved the same level of growth. The fundamental composition of their bodies was different, which was also why the powers of the Dragon Heart, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the Blood Demon had not been fully assimilated into his own.

[Still,] the Void Monarch snickered. [You're faster.]

He was right. The exchange happened in an instant. Aine's eyes went round as she looked back at her own shattered fist. Lee Sungmin pushed the spear forward, its tip trembling slightly.

"......"

An electric current flowed. Aine's body exploded without a sound, twisting as it was pushed back. She tried to steady her tilting vision. Half of her head was gone, and her body was blown apart in several places.

However, Aine did not die. Instead, she twisted her lips into a smile as her body regenerated in an instant. Her regenerative ability was incomparable to Lee Sungmin's. Black flames wrapped around her body, and within them, her ruptured form was made whole again.

Lee Sungmin was a little surprised to see the flames enveloping her.

*Magic?*

No, the feeling was different from that. Then was it a Yang-style martial art? It did not feel like that either. Something else... yes.

[It feels similar to that fellow, the Spirit King of Wind.]

"......"

The black flames completely swallowed Aine's body. Lee Sungmin took a few steps back and stared at her. He finally understood what it meant when he heard the Spirit King of Flame had not been reverse-summoned from Femud Volcano, but had been annihilated.

Aine had devoured the Spirit King of Flame.

[What an absurd wench!] the Void Monarch said with a hearty laugh. He had never even considered that devouring a Spirit King was possible. In a way, he thought it was for the best. Lee Sungmin had reverse-summoned the Spirit King of Wind, and he had also sent the Spirit Kings of Earth and Water back to the Elemental Realm. In other words, there was no chance for Aine to devour any more of them.

And another reason to kill her had just been added.

[Queen.]

Jeniela's eyes went round at the voice that echoed in her head.

"......"

The red curtain tore apart as the Formless Sword descended upon her. Jeniela neither dodged nor retreated. The head severed by the sword shot into the air.

"......"

[The Phantom Spear has arrived,] Chen reported in a flat voice.

The head that had flown high into the air dispersed into mist. A new head formed on top of the body. Jeniela stroked her neck with her hand and chuckled.

[How mischievous,] Jeniela muttered, glancing at Gemini, who was watching the fight from a distance. She had no intention of blaming him, however. After all, they were true siblings who had spent hundreds of years together. She could laugh off her younger brother's mischievous pranks.

[Don't let Aine die,] Jeniela commanded Chen as she looked at the Sword Immortal right in front of her.

"Are you going to continue?" Jeniela asked with a smile.
