They made another grave near the lakeside, placing the Spear King's tomb right next to the one that had marked the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's resting place for a decade. It was not the spot the Spear King himself had chosen, but Lee Sungmin hoped he would be satisfied here. A bitter feeling settled in his chest as he recalled the contented smile on the dead man's face.

At dawn, the inhuman corpses that had littered the forest dissolved into dust. Lee Sungmin bowed his head to Osla, offering an apology for the devastation, but the forest spirit only shook his head vigorously. The entire forest existed through Osla's magic, after all, and would regain its former appearance with a little time.

Though the battle was over, the Fairy Realms inside the lake remained hidden. Even their mischievous nature, it seemed, was no match for the terror of the previous night. Lee Sungmin felt it had been the right choice to leave the lakeside and erect a barrier before facing Jeniela. Had he committed that terrible deed here, everyone would have been horrified.

"I suppose I'll have to wear an eyepatch now," Scarlet muttered, her hand gently caressing her left eye. She turned to Rubia, who sat quietly beside her. "What do you think? Will it suit me?"

Rubia stroked her chin thoughtfully, her gaze flicking between Scarlet's face and the closed, empty socket. "…How about a monocle?"

"What's the point of a monocle if I don't even have an eyeball?"

"I just think it would make you look smarter…"

"You think I'm covering my eye for looks?" she grumbled, pressing down on the socket. "I thought about making a prosthetic eye with magic, but it's not something I can whip up right away. If I'm going to do it, I have to do it right."

Despite her sudden injury, Scarlet spoke in a cheerful voice, trying to lighten the somber mood. She glanced over at Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight.

"How about it?" she asked. "A prosthetic arm… I think I could try making one. I've never done it before, so it'd take a lot of trial and error."

"It would be better than nothing, I suppose," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, looking down at his severed right arm. "It would be difficult to use my martial arts with it, though. In that case, there's no point in having one."

"Sogo, what about you?"

"I'm… fine too," Baek Sogo replied. "I think getting used to having one arm comes first."

Losing an arm was a fatal blow to them both. The only silver lining was that it was not a leg. While martial artists had occasionally made a comeback with one arm, losing a leg was devastating. In that respect, Lloyd was lucky. A mage of his level could easily use flotation magic to move around and Blink freely, even if he became one-legged. For a mage, the fatal loss was a hand, which made it impossible to form hand seals.

"A moment."

Lloyd approached Lee Sungmin with a grave expression. Lee Sungmin, who had been staring blankly at the Spear King's tomb, turned his head to look at him.

"What is it?"

"I wish to discuss something important."

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod and moved a short distance away from the group with Lloyd. The way he spoke suggested this was not something others should hear. Lee Sungmin cast a magic spell, instantly isolating the space they occupied.

"This is…?" Lloyd looked astonished at the spatial magic that had manifested so quickly. He was surprised that Lee Sungmin could use magic at all, but what shocked him even more was its advanced nature, so seamless that he could not see through it. "You… since when could you use magic…?"

Lloyd's voice was bewildered, but Lee Sungmin only offered a faint smile instead of an answer. Thinking he had asked a pointless question, Lloyd soon let out a dry cough. The important thing now was not why Lee Sungmin could use magic.

He frowned as he took out Gries, its cover crusted with dried blood. He scrubbed at the stain with the sleeve of his robe, then opened the book to check its contents just in case. Nothing had changed. The thick pages of Gries remained completely blank.

Lloyd's frown deepened as he flipped through the empty pages before snapping the book shut. He then explained the strange phenomenon that had occurred. Lee Sungmin stood still, listening intently. He had considered banishing Jeniela outside of spacetime as a viable option and had wondered why it was never attempted. He never imagined that something so strange would happen to Gries.

"May I see it for a moment?"

At Lee Sungmin's request, Lloyd handed over the book. Lee Sungmin ran his fingers over the cover, letting the magic power from his fingertips seep into Gries. The book gave no reaction. He opened it once more. There was no change at all.

*They've intervened.*

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue softly. The only beings who could interfere with Gries were the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit. They had never interfered before, yet they had suddenly chosen to intervene in this matter. According to Lloyd, there had been no problem using Gries's magic when dealing with the Undead in the forest. The moment he decided to banish Jeniela from this spacetime, however, he became unable to use it.

The implication was clear. The Spirit did not want Jeniela dealt with in that way. They had tacitly allowed the banishment of Kim Jonghyun, the first disaster of The End, when he awakened as a Quasi-Demon King, but when they tried to banish Jeniela, they directly interfered and rendered Gries unusable.

"If we can't use Gries's magic from now on… things will get difficult," Lloyd muttered, his voice laced with worry. "We'll no longer be able to know what's happening in places we're unaware of."

It was a natural concern. Until now, they had used Gries's power to detect phenomena and prepare for the disasters of The End. If they could no longer use its magic, they would be blind to incidents and unable to prepare.

"It's too early to be certain that Gries has completely lost its power," Lee Sungmin said to reassure him as he returned the book. Inwardly, however, he was certain they would never be able to use its magic again.

"One moment."

Lee Sungmin's figure vanished from Lloyd's sight. The spatial magic that had enveloped them dissipated at the same time. Lloyd looked around with a dumbfounded expression, but Lee Sungmin was nowhere to be seen.

After leaving the forest, Lee Sungmin created another barrier to avoid any unnecessary interference. Then, as always, he called for Nebul.

"It's been a while."

The one who emerged from Lee Sungmin's shadow, however, was not Nebul. Laplace took off his fedora and bowed his head deeply.

"I've heard the story. Seeing that you're safe… I see. Was the Vampire Queen defeated by you?"

Lee Sungmin did not answer. Laplace tilted his head, looking puzzled by the silence.

"I heard that you asked Nebul to meet with me. The Chimera soul that Lee Sungmin traded with us just yesterday was quite rare, so another one of our clients was also very satisfied…"

"Was it Rameraus?" Lee Sungmin asked, breaking his silence.

Laplace's mouth snapped shut.

"Or Homerusos?" Lee Sungmin searched his memories. *The Dragon killed by the Void Monarch. That Dragon met such a death because he was too arrogant, but that arrogance was born from a power that was particularly strong among Dragons. Power created arrogance, and acting arrogantly led to a dog's death.*

*Let's not recall the memory of when I died. I must not get too immersed in the memories. The jumble of different memories was unpleasant and disgusting. I am Lee Sungmin, but the memories are of the Blood Demon, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and a Dragon. These other three sets of memories must be used only as information. I must not let myself be eroded by the sea of memories.*

"Was it Belkraon?" he continued. "Other than those three, there's no one else that comes to mind."

"…What are you talking…"

"I'm talking about the Dragon using you as a terminal," Lee Sungmin said, staring intently into Laplace's eyes. At his words, all expression vanished from Laplace's face.

The connection with the terminal was not severed. The Dragon using Laplace was in another dimension, not here. No matter how much Lee Sungmin could now handle Dragonic and had mastered the martial arts of his predecessors, interfering with a Dragon in an unknown dimension was impossible.

"I'll make it easy for you to understand," Lee Sungmin muttered, placing a hand on his own head. He opened his lips and chanted in Dragonic.

At the sound, Laplace's emotionless eyes trembled. He was speaking the name of a Dragon that no longer existed and chanting in a language humans could not use. He was full of questions, but there was no need to ask.

A small sphere of light formed on Lee Sungmin's palm. He sent it flying slowly toward Laplace, who snatched the sphere from the air and brought it to his forehead.

"…Keiserod?" Laplace muttered with a look of disbelief. The name he spoke was that of the Dragon killed by the Void Monarch hundreds of years ago. "How can this be… even if it's just the memories… and even Dragonic…?"

"It's because this body is quite peculiar," Lee Sungmin said. "Seeing your reaction, it seems this wasn't part of the plan."

Laplace let out a dry laugh at his mutter. He shook his head from side to side. "…You, who have his memories, would know best. Keiserod's death was due to his arrogance, not a part of the Dragons' plan."

Laplace placed the fedora back on his head. His tone of voice changed completely. He no longer thought of Lee Sungmin as a member of Erebrisa.

"It's been a while… or I suppose I can't say that. You only have Keiserod's memories, you're not him. No… since you use Dragonic, should I call you a Dragon?"

"I can use Polymorph Magic, too," Lee Sungmin replied, not dwelling on Keiserod's memories. "It's inconvenient, so I don't want to. So, who are you?"

"Homerusos."

Hearing that name, the memories he tried to suppress surfaced again.

*Homerusos. The wisest, the Lord of the Gold Dragons.*

"What did you want to ask by calling me out?" Homerusos asked.

"Which number is this?"

Originally, Lee Sungmin had intended to get information about the Demonic Spirit and the Spirit from Laplace. Now that he had the Dragon's memories, he wanted to ask about something else entirely.

"…What do you mean?"

"This world. What number is it?"

At that question, Laplace's expression hardened. Seeing his hesitation, Lee Sungmin chuckled.

"Why are you hesitating, Homerusos? No matter what you say, the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit won't intervene. I'm completely free from the fate of this place, and they can't interfere with you either. I understand why you were ambiguous with me before, lacking certainty… but you can be certain now. I am not subject to their intervention."

"…It's the third," Laplace answered with a sigh.

The third. Lee Sungmin muttered the words in a low voice and plopped down on the spot.

"Keiserod failed to escape from this world," he said, pressing his temples. "Around a hundred years after Keiserod's death, the Dragons of this world escaped from Eria. The third… that must be the number you've observed since your escape."

"Yes," Homerusos confirmed. "We couldn't observe what came before. Observation began after we escaped. This world… is the third."

Lee Sungmin was lost in deep thought. Eria. There was no way to know exactly how many times this giant research area had repeated. In a repeating world, history repeated itself to some extent. The current world. The past life where he was a C-Rank Mercenary, and the world before that, which he did not know.

In that world, the Dragons of Eria realized this world was under the fate of The End and escaped the dimension. After the ruin, a new Slaughterhouse opened. Dragons existed in that world as well. The Dragons 'disappear.' The Dragons who realize The End 'escape' Eria. That history repeated itself again. In fact, even in his past life, Dragons did not exist in Eria. This world was the same.

"This is what I want to ask you, Homerusos," Lee Sungmin said. "In the world before the last, you escaped Eria. So what happens to the Homerusos who escaped Eria in the last world? And the Homerusos who escaped Eria in this world?"

He was asking about a time paradox.

"…I am the me from the world before last, the me from the last world, and the me from this world," Homerusos explained. "When we escaped Eria, we sealed our physical bodies and became beings of consciousness. As the world repeated three times, the three 'me's' merged into a single consciousness."

"Do you plan to live like that forever?"

"It can't be helped. When this world meets The End, we will be unable to interfere with the next world and will only be able to observe. Then the Dragons of the next world will create Erebrisa, escape from Eria, and 'I' will accept the consciousness of the fourth 'me'."

As long as The End did not end, it would repeat forever. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue.

"Then I can't use this method."

"What were you thinking of doing?"

"I was thinking of escaping this world, just like the Dragons did. However… that would be impossible."

He had thought there might be a possibility. If it were possible, he had intended to actively seek the help of the Dragons who created Erebrisa, but after hearing Homerusos, he knew it was impossible. Accepting those several repetitions was only possible because Dragons possessed the endless mental fortitude befitting their infinite lifespans. A human's mind would collapse just from experiencing their consciousness combining twice. There was no way to avoid the time paradox without abandoning one's physical body and becoming a being of pure consciousness. Besides, unless The End of Eria was completely finished, it would only repeat over and over.

"You've been observing this world three times."

"…That's right."

"Is the enemy the Spirit, or the Demonic Spirit?"

This time, too, Homerusos could not answer easily.
