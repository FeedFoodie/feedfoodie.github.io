"This might sound a little strange, but I wanted to become a woman."

The Lich seemed to be in a good mood, which perhaps explained the nonsense he was spouting. Just as he had warned, his words were strange. They were not just a little strange, but profoundly so.

Bound in chains, Lee Sungmin strained against his restraints, his back pressed hard against the wall. He cycled through every inner art and martial theory he knew, but it was impossible to break the chains holding this death trap.

"To be precise, it wasn't that I wanted to be a woman," the Lich clarified. "I wanted a body that could bear a 'child'. Ah, of course, men can make children too, but strictly speaking, that's not bearing a child. It's helping to create one."

Lee Sungmin had no idea what the difference was. He was in a state where he could neither answer nor ask questions. He wanted to open his lips, but they would not part. It was that kind of magic.

"I tried all sorts of magic, you know, but I couldn't change my innate gender. The only thing that can change gender is a Dragon's Polymorph Magic, since they're born genderless, and humans can't use polymorph. Do you know why? It's because of causality."

Causality, causality. He had heard the word several times but could not fathom what it meant. Amidst Lee Sungmin's forced silence, the Lich continued to move about the room.

"In the end, I fell into despair over the limits of magic. That's why I became a Lich. It's a curse that grants forced immortality in exchange for one's soul and body, but well, I was satisfied. The path of magic is endless, and though I've walked it for a very long time, I still can't see the end. Walking a path with no visible end... it's frightening, but it's also a joyful thing. It's good to be engrossed in something. It's very enjoyable."

The Lich cackled. Lee Sungmin did not know what had happened to Zeon and the other S-Rank Mercenaries. They had been captured and thrown into another room, forgotten by the Lich who no longer paid them any mind. Only Lee Sungmin had been brought to this room, alone with the fiend.

"I've walked the path of magic for a long time, and the desire I yearned for as a human can now never be fulfilled. I've lost my physical body, you see. Even if I were to complete a magic that could turn me into a woman, I wouldn't be able to use it. Keukeukeu! It sounds like a contradiction when I put it like that, but that's not necessarily the case."

After laughing for a while, the Lich looked at Lee Sungmin. No emotion could be seen in the blue glow visible beyond the mass of darkness, yet Lee Sungmin could feel the madness laced in the Lich's voice.

"Even if I can't give birth myself, there are ways to create a child."

Lee Sungmin kept trying to move his body. He strained to move his arms and his legs. He tried to part his lips, but nothing moved. The magical restraint was too powerful. This was the Lich who had effortlessly dealt with Zeon and Bern, both SS-Rank Mercenaries. He did not know the fiend's name, but the overwhelming power the Lich had displayed was far beyond the realm of common sense.

"Chimeras are a fascinating research subject."

The voice, steeped in madness, held an incomprehensible warmth and gentleness. Of course, that was only how Lee Sungmin perceived it. Even amidst that feeling, it was clear that the emotion in the Lich's voice was not directed at him.

"Creating a completely different life form using an existing one as a base... this fulfills what I've longed for since I was human. Even though it's not born from my own body. Well. Is there really a difference? If it's a life I created, then it's my child."

The Lich's movements stopped. An ominous feeling radiated from the magic circle covering the entire floor, a sensation so potent that even Lee Sungmin, who knew little about magic, could feel its malevolence.

"Causality is something that cannot be defied. All normal humans are inevitably swayed by it. To defy causality is, in itself, a sign of having touched a fragment of transcendence."

The Lich muttered this as he turned around. Chained to the wall, Lee Sungmin glared at the Lich's face. Kim Jonghyun's warning echoed in his mind. Kim Jonghyun had been right. Lee Sungmin should never have come to this Dungeon.

"Today is a very good day, and also... a very lucky day. The last one I saw, whose causality was twisted like yours, was a monster I couldn't possibly handle..."

The Lich's words sent a violent tremor through Lee Sungmin's body. He struggled to make a sound, moving his lips desperately, but they would not part. Even Thought Transmission was blocked, so the question remained trapped inside his mouth.

"If I offer your soul as a sacrifice, my dearest wish will be closer to completion."

Saying so, the Lich raised both his hands. The magic circle carved into the floor emitted an ominous light. The incantation the Lich chanted sounded as if it were not of this world, not the language of Eria. Just hearing it made his mind throb and his vision distort.

He wanted to scream his lungs out, but his sealed lips were cruelly heavy and refused to open.

The long incantation was complete. The magic circle no longer glowed. Instead, a chilling cold engulfed the room. His exhaled breath froze white. The cold seeped into his very bones, making Lee Sungmin's body tremble like an aspen leaf.

A form invisible to the naked eye took shape. It looked like a clump of darkness and also like a pure white fog. Just when he thought he had grasped its form, it became a man, then a woman again. It changed its appearance endlessly depending on how Lee Sungmin perceived it, and his mind was shaken by the ominous, ever-changing spectacle.

Fear.

Lee Sungmin felt fear. Fear was not unfamiliar to him, for he had felt it several times before. The fear he felt now, however, was fundamentally different from any he had ever experienced. It was something unknown, something he could not comprehend. The mere sight of that unknowable existence spread a fear so intense it felt as if it could drive him mad.

"I offer this."

Raising both arms, the Lich slowly knelt. The Lich, who had displayed power beyond common sense, was now kneeling, lowering his head, and offering worship.

The entity's gaze turned to Lee Sungmin. When their eyes met, Lee Sungmin thought his head had exploded. The headache was so excruciating that he wished the illusion of his head exploding was real.

"...What?"

A moment later, the Lich let out a surprised sound.

"W-wait. What do you mean? Something... no, that can't be. There's no way. Why...?"

His head ached. His breathing grew ragged. Every single one of his cells froze in fear, and his heart beat slowly. *I'm going to die.* He felt that sensation. From its gaze alone, Lee Sungmin envisioned his own death.

"That's... impossible..." the Lich muttered, his voice hollow.

The entity's gaze, which had been fixed on Lee Sungmin, was withdrawn. Lee Sungmin gasped for air. THUMP, THUMP. His heart, which had been beating so slowly it was about to stop, began to pound again. Lee Sungmin's body shivered uncontrollably.

"No... ah, no. I will offer another sacrifice. Yes... so..."

The Black Mage fumbled in a panic. He hastily rummaged inside his sleeve and pulled out a large jewel. As the Black Mage placed the jewel on the altar, the entity's gaze fell upon it. The darkness, the fog, swallowed the jewel.

And then, the entity faded and vanished. The magic circle drawn on the floor disappeared completely. The Lich stared blankly into the empty air before his legs seemed to give out, and he collapsed to the ground.

"It... refused the sacrifice?" the Lich muttered in a dejected voice.

After staring into the air for a long while, the Lich shot to his feet. He spun around to glare at Lee Sungmin, his shoulders trembling.

"You... what in the world are you?"

That was what Lee Sungmin wanted to ask. He could not understand what had just happened.

"Even if his causality is twisted, he should still have value as a sacrifice... no... rather, because his causality is twisted, he should be an even more significant sacrifice. Why would it say he cannot be accepted?" the Lich muttered in a confused voice.

He staggered toward Lee Sungmin. The Lich's outstretched hand reached for him. Lee Sungmin's body, still bound to the wall, slowly floated toward the Lich.

"Show me everything about you!" the Lich roared.

The blue glow in the darkness flared. An invisible magic assailed Lee Sungmin's mind. It was a magic that forcibly extracted memories through a powerful mental restriction, an efficient method with the side effect of turning the target into an imbecile.

Was this what it felt like to have ants crawling over your cerebral cortex? Lee Sungmin's body trembled violently. His eyes turned white. It was a sign that his mind was on the verge of collapse as his memories were forcibly read and his consciousness suppressed by the magic.

That is what should have happened.

KAAAAAK!

The Lich screamed. He shuddered as if struck by lightning, then staggered and collapsed. Lee Sungmin's eyes were still unfocused. The trembling Lich raised a hand, trying to clutch his non-existent head. It was the first headache he had felt since becoming a Lich.

"Ah... AAARGH! KEUUUGH!"

The collapsed Lich screamed. As his mind was shaken, the magic binding Lee Sungmin's body dissipated. Lee Sungmin fell to the floor, collapsing in a heap, his strength gone. After groaning for a while, the Lich scrambled away from Lee Sungmin. Half out of his mind, the Lich could not even think about what to do with Lee Sungmin. He just wanted to escape. The Lich did not try to understand the emotion and impulse. He simply acted on it and fled. The Lich's body vanished from the room.

Not long after, the light returned to Lee Sungmin's eyes. The throbbing headache was already gone. Sprawled on the floor, Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet.

"...What... what was that?"

What had happened? Lee Sungmin could not understand. The magic that had bound his body was gone. The Lich was nowhere to be seen. ...Where did he go? Lee Sungmin quickly looked around. The Lich was not in the room. He tried to recall his memories. The Lich reached out his hand... and then. He could not remember what happened next. Did he pass out for a moment, unable to bear the intense headache?

*What in the world happened while I was unconscious?* Alone in the room, Lee Sungmin wondered blankly.

No, that was not what was important right now. He could not properly muster any strength in his body, but Lee Sungmin took a deep breath and placed a hand on the wall to support himself. In the corner of the room, the spear he had received from Celgerus lay on the floor. Lee Sungmin picked up his spear and approached the closed door.

He did not know where the Black Mage had gone, but the priority was to escape this place.
