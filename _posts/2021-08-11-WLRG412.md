The journey to the Sleeping Forest did not take long. He used the time to organize his thoughts and sort through the tangle of his emotions, a process that slowed his steps, though he knew he could arrive in an instant if he put his mind to it. The current Lee Sungmin had many ways to do so, be it through magic or martial arts.

Night had fallen by the time the forest came into view. He stopped his advance and prepared to make camp, deciding against entering a nearby village. He did not want to rush.

He lacked conviction.

At this point, he knew there was nothing more he could do to prepare. Meditation no longer helped him grow, and he could advance no further with the Void Path or his magic. Lee Sungmin knew this for certain as he observed his own body. The Black Heart could no longer make him stronger. There was simply no more room for growth. He had already acquired the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial arts theory, obtained the power of a Dragon, and possessed the Blood Demon's martial arts theory. Even the potential he had gained by devouring Aine's heart had been completely exhausted in the fight against Jeniela.

There was nothing left. He was not expecting some unknown miracle either.

"……"

He stared into the crackling campfire, where the fat on a slab of meat he had casually placed over it sizzled. Lee Sungmin sprinkled it with salt and pepper.

*…I will stop Wiji Hoyeon.*

It was not a question of whether he could. He absolutely had to. No matter what it took, he had to stop her. The feeling was the same as it had been with Jeniela, yet it was different.

Lee Sungmin grabbed the cooked meat with his bare hands and tore into it savagely, chewing through the familiar emotions of anxiety, impatience, and worry. They were the same feelings he had endured while preparing for his fight with Jeniela.

This was the finale. The fight with Wiji Hoyeon would bring an end to things, one way or another. The large piece of meat vanished in an instant. He crunched on the bone and put another piece on the fire.

Perhaps this would be his last meal. The thought that roughly grilled meat was an inadequate last supper crossed his mind.

Lee Sungmin retrieved the Void Monarch's gourd from his Subspace Pocket.

*Come to think of it.*

He took out a cup, filled it to the brim with liquor, and placed it on the ground across from him.

*We rarely drank together, just the two of us.*

Of course, the Void Monarch's body could not handle alcohol, but they still rarely had memories of enjoying that kind of atmosphere together.

Lee Sungmin chuckled, then poured the liquor from the gourd directly into his mouth. He tore off some meat to go with it, his gaze continually returning to the cup across from him. He had never eaten and drunk his fill like this before. He had consumed quite a bit at the banquet in the Fairy Forest before the fight with Jeniela, but even then, it was not as much as today.

Despite eating an enormous amount, he did not feel full. He rinsed his mouth with liquor, then washed his greasy hands with magic.

"Senior Sister."

Lee Sungmin called out as he stood. He had already sensed their presence. Everyone had come, except for Osla, who could not leave the Fairy Forest.

"Eating all by yourself, are we?"

The grumbling voice belonged to Scarlet. As the curtain of magic lifted, she glared at him with a sour expression. He did not wonder how they had traveled all the way from the distant Fairy Forest. Osla must have taken back the Fairy Horse and bound it to Scarlet.

"If you'd shown yourselves, I would've asked you to eat with me."

"You've got a silver tongue."

"Don't tell me you're upset because I was eating alone?" Lee Sungmin asked with a smile.

Scarlet looked dumbfounded. She opened her mouth as if to say something, but held back a curse and let out a long sigh instead.

"…What are you doing here?" Baek Sogo, who had been keeping her lips tightly sealed, finally asked.

Hearing the slight tremble in her voice, Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly. "I'm going to stop Wiji Hoyeon."

He offered no long explanation. Those words were enough. Baek Sogo's expression hardened, and Scarlet, who had been muttering something, closed her mouth. Behind them, the Black Dragon Knight's brow furrowed.

"Wiji… Hoyeon?" Lloyd stammered.

Looking at them and their permanent injuries, Lee Sungmin felt a complicated mix of emotions. As long as they were human, there was no way to regenerate their lost limbs and eyes.

*What if they became inhuman?*

The thought occurred to him suddenly, but he erased it from his mind. It was not worth considering. If they became inhuman, everything that made them who they were would disappear.

"She has become the final calamity."

"Haha!" the Black Dragon Knight let out a hollow laugh at his answer. "What a cruel twist of fate. The Little Heavenly Demon… is the final End? Who told you that?"

"I heard it directly from a Spirit."

"From a Spirit…? How?"

"Because I just killed the Martial God."

The Black Dragon Knight's expression stiffened. Lee Sungmin shook his head at him. "It wasn't a pleasant death. …Should I have left him for you?"

"No… no, there's no need for that," the Black Dragon Knight said, though a sense of emptiness lingered in his voice. "I'm not that old geezer the Spear King. Do you think I'd fly into a rage just because you took him from me?"

He had trained on Mount Mushii with the Spear King for the sole purpose of one day meeting the Martial God and avenging the humiliation of having to attack him alongside his master.

"So," Scarlet muttered, stroking the area around her missing eye. At some point, she had covered it with a proper-looking eyepatch. "You, all by yourself, without a word, left the forest and did all sorts of things?"

"I went to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion in the Hyujael Mountains and met Yana. Fortunately, she was safe."

"…And?"

"There, I met the incompetent Demonic Spirit. From him… I heard that Wiji Hoyeon had become a servant of the Spirits. I had no choice but to head for the Sleeping Forest."

"I thought so," Scarlet grumbled. "I figured if you were going to move recklessly, you'd definitely go to the Sleeping Forest. I didn't think you'd stop by the Hyujael Mountains… but well, in the end, we succeeded in meeting you again."

"Why are all of you here?" he asked, though the question was obvious.

"Why ask something so obvious?" An equally obvious answer came back. "It's to help you, of course, since you're trying to do everything on your own."

Hearing that, Lee Sungmin fell silent. He looked at Scarlet with profound eyes.

"…What's with that intense gaze?" she asked, averting her eyes and tucking her chin as if embarrassed.

His gaze shifted. The Black Dragon Knight was staring silently at the sky, while Lloyd anxiously chewed his lip and wrung his hands. Rubia observed the situation cautiously from Scarlet's side, and Teresa tightly gripped the new rosary around her neck. Then, he looked at Baek Sogo.

Her expression… he could not quite read it. She was biting her lower lip, her gaze cast downward. Her eyes and shoulders trembled, and her lips quivered, unable to form words.

Ah. At some point, Lee Sungmin realized. Baek Sogo's emotion was pity.

"Senior Sister is a good person," he said with a slow smile. "Even now… you feel sorry for me."

"If that was unpleasant… I'm sorry, Junior Disciple."

"Not at all. I'm not offended… I'm just curious. Why do you feel sorry for me?"

"…Because I know how you feel about the Little Heavenly Demon, no, Wiji Hoyeon. Because I know… what kind of bond the two of you share."

As expected. Hearing her answer, Lee Sungmin felt a sense of relief in his chest. Baek Sogo was that kind of person. She had been a good person even before she became a monster consumed by a twisted conviction. Even after being broken during a thousand years of mental training, even after being shattered by insurmountable Despair, Baek Sogo was still the good person who had helped a young boy on the verge of death on Mount Mushii. Her conviction to eradicate evil from the world with her narrow sense of justice was simple. It was just because she was a good person.

He looked at Scarlet. Despite always grumbling, having a foul mouth, and calling herself a selfish person who only cared about herself, she never ran from the dangers they faced together. She could have tried to escape the Fairy Forest if she had wanted to, but she chose to fight Jeniela, even knowing she was just a martial arts theory.

The Black Dragon Knight, once an enemy, had become an ally after the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Ten years had passed before they met again, and there was no particularly strong bond between them. Nevertheless, he had come here, even after losing an arm in the fight with Jeniela.

He had first met Lloyd in Preskan's Dungeon. Having inherited Gries from Abel, he sought to resist The End by using its magic, unconcerned about his own lifespan. Though Gries itself was an item arranged by the Spirits, Lloyd, just like Abel before him, genuinely wished to stop The End and had helped him.

Come to think of it, after the Void Monarch, Rubia had traveled with him the most. He had many things to feel sorry for when it came to her, especially since he was involved in Envirus's death. She had been afraid of the fight with Jeniela, but she did not run away and fought alongside him.

He was also very grateful to Teresa. He had gone to her out of the blue to ask for help, yet she had listened to his request without misunderstanding or prejudice. Even after learning about The End, she remained in the forest without hesitation. The girl who maintained the barrier to stop Jeniela while crying in fear was a magnificent Saint.

He felt gratitude as well for Osla, who was not here. When the time of The End came, the one who would suffer the most terrible fate was none other than her. Despite knowing that, Osla chose to help him, the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, to confront The End.

Thinking of them all, Lee Sungmin smiled with a sense of relief.

"…Thank you," he said, expressing his gratitude honestly. "Thank you all so much for coming to help me."

"Don't say such cringey things," Scarlet shot back. "What's important is stopping The End. Right, you said Wiji Hoyeon is the final calamity? Then if we finish her off, The End will be over too."

"That might be the case."

"What's with that vague answer? Ah, forget it. Are you just going to stand there? To the forest…"

"No." Lee Sungmin shook his head at her urging. "I'm going into the forest alone."

"…Why?" Baek Sogo asked, her expression hardening.

"Because you're a good person, Senior Sister."

"What…?"

"Scarlet, the Black Dragon Knight, Lloyd, Rubia, and Teresa. You are all good people… and that's why I intend to go alone."

"…What in the world are you talking about?" Scarlet asked, her voice laced with irritation.

"I don't want to put any of you in danger," Lee Sungmin said, taking a step forward. "And I don't want you to get hurt anymore."

He knew they would not listen to reason. The mysterious phenomena and pressure he released vibrated the ground. Under the immense weight, Baek Sogo's expression stiffened, and she took a step back.

"…It might be too much for you alone, Junior Disciple."

"That may be so."

"If you know that… why won't you accept our help?"

"Didn't I already say it? I don't want you to get hurt."

"If you fail, The End will kill us all anyway," she argued. "So what's this nonsense about not wanting us to get hurt…?"

"Your presence won't turn the impossible into the possible."

Looking at it coldly, that was the truth.

"Am I…" Baek Sogo began hesitantly. "No, are we… too weak? Are you saying we'll just get in the way?"

Lee Sungmin took a deep breath.

"……"

At his silence, all the color drained from her face.
