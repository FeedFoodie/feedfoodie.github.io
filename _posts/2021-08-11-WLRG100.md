"......"

Amidst the scattering fragments of afterimages, a killing blow rushed in. The Doppelganger's attack was threatening, yet it carried not a single shred of killing intent. This very absence made it tricky to handle, as such an attack was difficult to detect.

However, not for Lee Sungmin. His heightened senses and his non-existent sixth sense clearly captured the Doppelganger's assault. The spear in his hand spun with a whir.

"......"

A series of kicks collided with the spear's shaft. Without taking a single step back, Lee Sungmin poured strength into the muscles of both arms.

WHOOSH!

A wide swing of the spear forced the Doppelganger to retreat. The creature rolled its gray eyes, identical to Baek Sogo's, and glanced at the red spear tassel. By the time it confirmed the spearhead hidden within, the weapon was already upon it.

*It's identical. To a creepy degree.*

The Doppelganger's feet began to move. Its overall motion, starting with its footwork, created a near-perfect Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Anyone unfamiliar with the technique would have been flustered by the movements of Extreme Speed and Extreme Illusion that began with those seemingly ordinary steps. Lee Sungmin, however, was different. He knew the Dancing Shadow Soul Break intimately.

From one step to two, the creature executed the Third Form, the Two Step Calamity. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break was a movement art, but it was also more. It was an extreme martial art that began with the feet. As the Doppelganger took its second step, its entire body exploded with a dense, enhanced qi. In response, the violet enhanced qi from the spear Lee Sungmin held swelled to a great size.

KWAANG!

With a single swing, he pushed back the enhanced qi of the Two Step Calamity. The spear in his hand began to spin furiously before thrusting straight forward to pierce the Doppelganger's chest.

The Doppelganger opened its mouth, but neither a scream nor a groan escaped. It merely blinked its eyes at Lee Sungmin before its form collapsed, reverting to a hideous lump of flesh. Watching the mass go limp, Lee Sungmin pulled his spear free.

"What in the world is going on?"

"It's a monster," Lee Sungmin muttered, looking down at the simple lump of flesh that had worn Baek Sogo's form. He could still feel the sensation transmitted to his hand when he had pierced it, and he recalled the final expression that had turned toward him. He shook his spear to fling off the clinging flesh, and with it, he tried to shake off the displeasure and irritation he felt.

"It had the appearance of Miss Baek, but you say it's a monster…"

"Didn't you see it for yourself? That thing is that kind of monster."

"Its response was a bit lacking, but… the Dancing Shadow Soul Break that 'monster' showed was unmistakably Miss Baek's. From what I experienced, there didn't seem to be much of a difference in skill level," Jang Deuksu muttered, his face a mask of disbelief.

Lee Sungmin had sensed something similar during the fight. The Doppelganger seemed to lack any thought process, but the martial arts it performed were powerful enough to be nearly indistinguishable from the original.

[Normally, a monster called a Doppelganger can only imitate appearances, not skills, but that's common sense 'outside',] Rubia muttered in his head. [The analysis is in progress. The information I've gathered so far is that this Dungeon is a complex maze. I should be able to act as a guide, so please trust me and move forward.]

*Can I trust you?*

[Hmph. Can't you just trust me?] Rubia grumbled.

As they spoke, the Magic Armor hummed and trembled. It seemed the Void Monarch, possessing the armor, wanted to chime in as well.

*What is it?*

[Why do you let that crude doll chatter away while telling me to shut up?]

[Crude doll? Look who's talking, a spirit dwelling in armor without even a body of his own!]

[I swear, the moment I regain my original body, I will tear your backside apart.]

The Void Monarch and Rubia started bickering. Feeling a throbbing headache coming on, Lee Sungmin tapped the Magic Armor with his hand. At the touch, the Void Monarch flinched and fell silent, and Rubia let out a triumphant, gloating laugh.

"...A terrible thought occurs to me."

Jang Deuksu walked ahead cautiously, his expression now full of a vigilance that had been absent before. The sight of the giant man, holding a huge axe in both hands while flinching at every step, looked quite comical.

"What thought is that?"

"This… Dungeon. We just fought a monster that looked like Miss Baek."

*We, no, I,* Lee Sungmin wanted to snap, but he held his tongue.

"What if the others are also fighting…"

Lee Sungmin immediately realized what Jang Deuksu was trying to say. The Doppelganger in Baek Sogo's form had performed her Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Although it had seemed unaccustomed to fighting, the martial art itself was real. Lee Sungmin had defeated it with relative ease only because he knew the technique, but what if a Doppelganger of Wiji Hoyeon appeared?

*Ah.*

A memory from his past life surfaced. In this Dungeon, everyone except Wiji Hoyeon had died. She had emerged alone and declared that she had killed all the others herself.

Could it be that the one who killed Baek Sogo and the others was not Wiji Hoyeon, but her Doppelganger?

"Let's hurry," Lee Sungmin said, passing Jang Deuksu and heading forward, conscious of the ominous feeling rising in his chest.

*

"Hah… hah…!"

The Peerless Eccentric gasped for breath as he glared ahead. A horribly mangled corpse lay on the ground where he was looking. It was Geuk Cheondo, one of the masters from the Murim Alliance who had entered the Dungeon with him. Geuk Cheondo had been a universally acknowledged master on the verge of the Supreme Peak, but despite his high-level martial arts and great fame, his death had been completely one-sided.

"You monster…!"

The Peerless Eccentric knew he would soon meet the same end. He glanced down at his own blood-caked hands. His left fist was crushed, and he could feel nothing but pain. His right fist was in better shape with its bones intact, but he could not consider himself lucky.

The monster approached, stepping over Geuk Cheondo's corpse. It wore the appearance of an ice-cold beauty. Her black hair, like the stretched darkness of night, swayed gently with every step, and her lightless black eyes reflected no emotion whatsoever.

The Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon.

He knew she was a monster. He knew she possessed outstanding martial arts for her age. The story of how she had unilaterally overwhelmed the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Wooseon, cut off his left arm, and escaped his stronghold of Trabia without a scratch had already become a legend symbolizing her strength.

Nevertheless, the Peerless Eccentric had pursued her, had followed her into this Dungeon, because of the sense of justice he held. He would not try to flee. With the resolve to land a fatal blow, he ignited his life qi. The energy he had accumulated over a lifetime suffused his entire body. Although he was only at the threshold, he too was a Supreme Peak master. If he was prepared to die with her, he might be able to take an arm. That was all he hoped for.

"Uwaaaah!"

The Peerless Eccentric unleashed a Lion's Roar and leaped forward in an attack with no defense. The resolve for mutual destruction, disregarding his own life, moved his body. Watching him, the creature in Wiji Hoyeon's form raised a hand. The Black Dragon Armor covering her shoulders swelled to a great size.

He knew this attack. Just moments ago, Geuk Cheondo had failed to dodge it and had become a gruesome corpse. That seemingly flimsy piece of cloth, when infused with her energy, became a bludgeon or a whip, harder and faster than anything in the world. He leaped over the attack that swept in, filling his vision. He could not dodge it completely. His left leg was torn off, but he paid it no mind. He forgot the pain. His eyes, pouring with killing intent, saw only the Wiji Hoyeon clone looking up at him.

Her eyes held no emotion. Her hand moved again, and the Black Dragon Armor fluttered and shot up into the air. It was a simple attack, but its speed and power could not be ignored.

The Peerless Eccentric gritted his teeth and twisted his body in mid-air.

KWADEDEUK!

His left arm was torn from its socket. The pain was dizzying, but he ignored it. He had to endure. He vomited blood as he stretched out his right hand, aiming a palm strike containing all the energy he could muster toward Wiji Hoyeon. That was as far as he saw, and it was his last thought. In his desperate final wish, his palm strike shattered her head. In reality, the one whose head was shattered was his own.

Wiji Hoyeon lowered her raised hand, letting the rain of blood shower down on her. After staring blankly at the sky, she turned and looked at Geuk Cheondo's corpse.

The monster in her form felt no emotion as it left the place.

While Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger murdered Geuk Cheondo and the Peerless Eccentric, the real Wiji Hoyeon was staring blankly at the man standing before her. The man was covered in wounds. His side was grievously torn, yet neither blood nor entrails flowed out. It was a severe injury that should have made movement impossible, but the man did not flinch in the slightest. Despite this, his eyes held no particular tenacity or resolve.

"...Hmm."

Wiji Hoyeon stroked her chin, letting the fluttering Black Dragon Armor fall. She furrowed her brow and examined the man's face once more. She knew that thing was not a real human. After all, she had seen an unidentifiable lump of flesh rise up and transform into that shape right before her eyes.

"I thought it might be."

She had not recognized him at first, and why would she? Nine years had passed. Nine years ago in Zenavis, she had the appearance of a young girl, and likewise, he had the appearance of a boy. After experiencing the passage of time and many events, Wiji Hoyeon barely resembled her younger self.

"So you were here too."

When did he get in? She had been aware of the pursuers from the Murim Alliance, but she had never sensed his presence among them. Wiji Hoyeon felt a complex sense of longing and smiled bitterly. She had never expected to meet him in a place like this, and she wondered what kind of expression she should make when she met the real him.

*If I'd known this would happen, should I have dressed up a little more? No, that wouldn't suit me either.* As she had these thoughts, Wiji Hoyeon looked down at her chest.

*This wasn't very big, though.*

That was her biggest complaint. Over nine years, her physique had changed. She had grown taller and her face had matured, but her chest had not grown much. It was not that she was flat, but it was not large enough for anyone to notice. By her standards, it was a little smaller than average. For nine years, she had lived as a woman, wearing women's clothes and introducing herself as such, not pretending to be a man. However, just as her chest had not grown in the past life she had heard about, it did not get bigger even though she lived as a woman.

*I thought I tried my best.*

She had wanted to show off a prominent curve and gloat when they met again, but what could not be helped, could not be helped. Her chest simply did not grow. She had considered getting a Breast Enlargement Pill and taking it, but when she actually thought about it, she wondered if there was any reason to go that far for him, so she did not. Besides, if her chest really grew to the size of watermelons, that seemed like it would be very inconvenient. One way or another, she was satisfied with her current chest size. It was not a burden when she performed her martial arts.

*But now I regret it quite a bit. I didn't do it, but I really wanted to see his surprised face.* Wiji Hoyeon genuinely had that thought as she raised her hand.

"This world is truly mysterious. For nine years, I have seen, heard, and experienced so much. That's how I became who I am today. The me of today is different from the 'me' you knew in your past life."

Even as she said this, Wiji Hoyeon chuckled.

"Well. You did say you'd never met the me from your past life, and this conversation is meaningless too. You're… I mean. You're not the guy I know, are you? Right?"

The Doppelganger did not answer. It ignored the wound in its side and attacked. With a forward step, it instantly closed the distance and shot the spear it held in a straight line. No, it only pretended to shoot. The spear tassel scattered, concealing the spearhead, while the straight shaft showed movements other than a straight line, following the motions of its arms and hands.

That made Wiji Hoyeon feel satisfied. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Sungmin Style. It was a martial art she had modified from the original to suit Lee Sungmin. Honestly, when she had taught it to him, she had not expected much. The talent she had seen in him was truly nothing special, so she had thought that even with ten years, he would not reach the threshold of the Supreme Peak.

However, look at him now. The thrusting spear was beautiful, covered in a violet enhanced qi. It was the energy of the Violet Dawn Divine Art. Wiji Hoyeon smiled happily and stretched out her hand. The Black Dragon Armor, which had been hanging down, spread wide in front of her according to her gesture.

The spear thrust by the Doppelganger was futilely blocked by her Black Dragon Armor.

"You've become excellent," Wiji Hoyeon muttered.

When her outstretched hand clenched into a fist, the spear, wrapped in the Black Dragon Armor, shattered with a terrible sound. The Doppelganger tried to pull back the broken weapon, but Wiji Hoyeon moved forward faster. She brought her lightly clenched fist to the Doppelganger's chest.

"......"

The Doppelganger's upper body exploded. Wiji Hoyeon stepped far back to avoid the scattered flesh and smiled bitterly.

"If you die here, I'll be very sad."

Probably.

Wiji Hoyeon added that thought as she turned away.
