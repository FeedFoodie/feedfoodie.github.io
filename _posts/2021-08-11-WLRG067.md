A sudden gust of wind tore through the clearing, snuffing out the bonfire Scarlet had created. The flames died in an instant, but the sky, still soaked in the red of sunset, provided enough light to see. In that dim light, a black-haired girl stood where the fire had been, her position perfect, a single point from which she could strike both Lee Sungmin and Scarlet.

*Since when?*

The question echoed in both their minds. Neither of them had sensed her approach, a feat of speed that defied all common sense. The girl, Aine, who had been crouching, slowly straightened. It was a stroke of immense luck for them both that she was not in a state of hunger.

Scarlet didn't know the girl, but she recognized the ominous aura coiling from her, an unfamiliar and unsettling kind of malevolence.

"...What are you?" she demanded.

Unintimidated, as was her nature, she immediately took several steps back, putting distance between herself and the newcomer. While Scarlet was already on guard and reacting, Lee Sungmin was not.

*What are you doing?*

He stood there, stunned, staring at the girl as the pounding of his heart roared in his ears. Aine remained motionless too, her yellow eyes blinking as she fixed her gaze on his face.

"...You..." Lee Sungmin's lips parted.

He had never met this girl, yet she felt familiar. No, it was more than that. It was a sense of kinship.

"Who are you?" Aine asked first, her voice cutting through the silence.

She felt the exact same thing he did, but for her, the kinship curdled into displeasure. A similar feeling was rising in Lee Sungmin, but the crucial difference lay in how each of them interpreted that displeasure.

It happened in an instant. Aine's right arm seemed to twist, transforming into a black tentacle that shot toward Lee Sungmin.

"......"

A sharp cry escaped Scarlet. She swung her hand, and a surge of magic deflected the tentacle just as it aimed for Lee Sungmin's chest. Its trajectory shifted, and Lee Sungmin gasped, scrambling backward.

"Snap out of it! What are you doing!" Scarlet shouted, her face hardening.

The sudden violence snapped Lee Sungmin's lax mind into focus. He threw himself into motion, grabbing the spear he had left on the ground, but the weapon brought him no comfort.

The writhing tentacle morphed back into an arm. Aine clenched and unclenched the small hand, her eyes never leaving Lee Sungmin's face. She paid Scarlet no mind at all.

"I'm getting a strange feeling from you," Aine muttered.

She took a long stride forward, and pitch-black magic erupted from beneath her feet. At the sight of its ominous form, Scarlet bit her lip. She reached into her robes, pulling out a pair of large gloves and sliding them on.

"Who are you?" Aine asked again.

Lee Sungmin didn't answer. A sense of unease, of foreboding, washed over him. This was an unknown hostility, but it was more than that. This girl was trying to kill him, and he couldn't just stand there and let it happen.

"...Lee Sungmin."

"Lee Sungmin? Is that your name? I'm Aine," she replied. Her brow furrowed for a moment before the corners of her mouth twitched into a smile.

"I see."

That was the end of the conversation. Aine's right arm changed shape again, swelling to a grotesque size that defied human anatomy. The monstrous fist tore through the air, and Lee Sungmin hastily executed the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. His enhanced inner arts made his body faster, allowing him to evade just as the fist shattered the ground where he had stood, sending soil flying. He swung his spear wide at the dust cloud obscuring his vision.

As it cleared, Aine's other arm, now a writhing tentacle, lashed out toward him. The words he had heard from Lloyd flashed through his mind, the story of a Chimera girl who could change the shape of her limbs. This was her.

There was no time to think. Her attacks were bizarre and erratic. The tentacle was thicker than a whip but moved with the sharpness of a blade. Lee Sungmin infused his spear with inner arts and used Ran's Technique to parry the blow.

That was his plan. A searing pain shot through his palms as if they were being torn apart, and the spear shaft spun violently in his hands. Aine's attack was incomparably heavier than the Ogre Chimera's fist.

*I can't deflect it...!*

He was only enduring because the Great and Minor Cycle Pills had increased his inner arts. Before, an attack like this would have torn his arm clean off. She was no mere girl. She was the monster who had driven the Golden Magic Tower Master to a death trap. Lee Sungmin barely forced strength back into his loosening grip, withstanding the assault.

While Lee Sungmin held on, Scarlet finished her preparations. The metal on the wrists of her gloves began to glow as her lips moved quickly, her hands weaving through the empty air. With every movement of her fingers, letters of power were written in the space before her. The still-incomplete Spell Engraving of the Reshir School unfolded, and magic manifested from the red incantation carved into the air.

Aine, who had been focused on Lee Sungmin, flinched at the threat she felt from behind. Suddenly, the existence of the mage she had ignored scrambled her senses. A blazing firebird born from the spell descended upon her back.

"Ugh...!"

Aine twisted, spreading her hands wide. The heart that powered her body made her immune to most magic, an incomplete but potent nemesis ability that had been enough to corner Lloyd himself, but Scarlet's magic was fundamentally different. Her Spell Engraving was a fusion of magic and sorcery, based on the Yin-Yang Path and Qimen Dunjia. Where ordinary magic would have failed, Scarlet's spell was another matter entirely.

KABOOM!

The firebird did not disperse into mana upon impact but instead swallowed Aine whole. Her body staggered violently within the ultra-hot flames. Aine did not know pain, but the sensation of her body burning was deeply unpleasant.

CRUNCH!

Inside the inferno, her body transformed. If simple magic immunity wouldn't work, she would simply assume a form the flames could not burn. A layer of crackling keratin turned to ash and fell away, revealing rough, carapace-like skin beneath.

"Body transformation...! Polymorph? No, don't tell me. It's not a Dragon, is it!" Scarlet spat, spreading her hands again.

Scarlet's support gave Lee Sungmin a moment to catch his breath and calm the turmoil shaking him. The confusion in his mind stemmed from the heart beating in his chest. The Black Heart. He didn't know how he had assimilated it, but if that thing was what now kept him alive...

*Right now... am I the same as that girl?*

The same as a monster who rips out and eats hearts?

He had no time to dwell on it, no time to get caught up in such a ridiculous sentiment. Aine was trying to kill him. Her first attack, aimed precisely at his heart, was no affectionate greeting. He decided to see it as an opportunity instead. This was his first chance to fight with all his strength since taking the pills. He no longer needed to hesitate. He could unleash the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear without reservation.

He used Traceless Step, closing the distance just as Aine lifted her head. Her upward gaze met his, and instinct, a sixth sense, screamed a warning. Lee Sungmin pulled back the spear he was thrusting and dragged his feet to the side.

An invisible fist grazed his ear. Even without a direct hit, his ear rang and his vision swam. A short distance away, Aine held a martial arts stance.

"...Hundred Steps Divine Fist?" he asked, dazed.

Aine didn't reply, but her expression held a hint of dissatisfaction. She clenched and unclenched her hand a few times before taking up the stance again.

Then the Hundred Steps Divine Fist began. The successive strikes were invisible, but the senses he had honed on Mount Mushii, his sixth sense, somehow allowed him to evade. He couldn't focus only on that. While her right hand executed the technique, her left hand morphed and lunged for him.

If he had been alone, he would not have held out. It was an enormous stroke of luck that Scarlet was with him. Her Reshir School's Spell Engraving, though incomplete, was the grand magic that would one day make her a Crimson Archmage. The favorable matchup was another stroke of luck. The current Scarlet was weaker than Lloyd, but the uniqueness of her magic troubled Aine. Without Preskan's assistance, Aine was naturally at a disadvantage.

Hands of earth shot from the ground, grabbing her legs. Aine stumbled but still swung her left arm, the tentacle tearing through the air. Lee Sungmin lowered his stance and closed the distance, thrusting his spear in a straight line for her chest. At the last moment, Aine twisted her upper body, taking the spear with her left shoulder instead.

CRUNCH!

The sound of tearing flesh and breaking bone filled the air. Almost simultaneously, as Lee Sungmin's spear pierced her shoulder, Aine swung her right arm and cut off her own ensnared legs. She staggered backward but did not fall. The moment she lost her footing, she did a somersault and landed, her severed feet already regenerated.

"What a monster," Scarlet spat, her voice laced with annoyance.

Yes, just annoyance. She no longer felt threatened. The short engagement had been enough to show her who had the upper hand.

"Yeah."

Aine's reply was accompanied by a nod, showing little awareness of her disadvantage. It wasn't because she was ignorant.

It was because it was the truth.

She smiled, her lips twitching, and a colossal magic power surged from her. Scarlet, who had just felt the situation was in their favor, realized how premature that thought had been. The sheer scale of the magic Aine was emitting surpassed her wildest imagination.

"What kind of monster are you...!" Scarlet muttered, her face contorting, for she did not know this was the creature that had driven the Golden Magic Tower Master to the brink of death.

Lee Sungmin faced that monstrous strength head-on. The heart beating inside his body and the heart Aine possessed were the same, but that did not make them the same kind of being. The immense power she displayed was far beyond his comprehension, an ominous torrent of magic incomparable to all the inner arts he possessed.

And that monster was targeting him. Her gaze held no hint of kinship now, only an inexplicable killing intent that she would not let end with just a look.

Lee Sungmin didn't want to die.
