It was over, and exhaustion finally claimed him.

Lee Sungmin's legs gave out, and he collapsed on the spot. Wiji Hoyeon approached, her own movements slow and unsteady, before she sat down beside him. She looked down at his wounded form, her eyes tracing the ruin of his Magic Armor. It was riddled with holes from the Flower Rain, and the hidden weapons that remained were lodged deep in his skin and muscle. Blood gushed from the wounds where others had already been pulled free.

"…You idiot…"

Her lips moved soundlessly before she finally managed to spit out the word. Hearing the insult, Lee Sungmin let out a small, weary laugh.

"That's a little harsh, don't you think?"

"Why… why did you have to get into this state?"

"I didn't want to let you go."

"Did I ask you to go this far for me?"

"You were asleep," he murmured. "…Did you sleep well?"

"You stupid bastard…!"

His reply made her lunge forward. She grabbed him by the collar and seethed, but in the end, she could not bring herself to say anything more and let him go.

"…What if you had died?"

"But I didn't die."

"You could have died!"

"I didn't, so it's fine."

Wiji Hoyeon's voice echoed in his head as his consciousness began to fray at the edges. His eyelids grew heavy. *Just a moment, just a moment.* Lee Sungmin's lips moved, his voice a faint whisper. Through the haze, he could just make out the distant figures of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia approaching.

"I'm just going to sleep for a bit."

Those were his last words.

When he opened his eyes, he was in a dark cave. The distant sound of rain drummed softly against the stone. Lee Sungmin glanced at the Black Dragon Armor that covered his body and then to his side, where Wiji Hoyeon was fast asleep, her breathing soft and even.

"Don't wake her. She just fell asleep."

A voice cut through the quiet as he gazed at her face. It was the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who sat near the cave entrance leaning against the wall. Rubia was curled up asleep beside him.

"Where is this?"

"This used to be a bear's den," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said with a chuckle. "This one here said the stench was awful and used her magic to get rid of the smell on her own."

He gestured toward Rubia before looking back at Lee Sungmin.

"You've been asleep for a full day. Aren't you hungry?"

"…I am."

"We only have some bread. We were heading south, but none of us were in any condition to move, so we came into a nearby mountain. The Murim Alliance will probably be in pursuit, but we all needed to rest."

Lee Sungmin lifted the Black Dragon Armor that was covering him. Not a single scar remained on his body.

"We pulled out the hidden weapons and poured an Elixir on you. You were lucky. To take a direct hit from Flower Rain and still have your body intact."

If he had not been wearing the Magic Armor, his body would have been pulverized the moment Flower Rain struck. The Orichalcum coating had been completely stripped away, but no matter how valuable a material it was, it was not more important than a life.

"I heard about the situation from the others," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered. "It seems I went crazy and had another fit. I didn't forget the chant and the slap, though…"

"It's not a cure-all."

"Still, it's a relief. At least I didn't hurt anyone else. I heard the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman intervened and made the Dark Supreme retreat? I never thought we'd receive help from such a mysterious figure."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. He was known as the greatest master of the unorthodox sects and stood at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Though his name and title were renowned, decades had passed since his last public appearance. Rumors had even circulated among the orthodox sects that he might already be dead.

Still, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was alive. For reasons unknown, he seemed to hold a considerable amount of goodwill toward Lee Sungmin, who found it a complete mystery why a stranger he had never met would show him such favor.

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said he would protect Wiji Hoyeon."

"I see it as a good offer," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said. "It's better for her to be under his protection than to be taken by the Murim Alliance and handed over to Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"I don't know if we should trust him."

"This one doesn't know what kind of person the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is either. I've never met him. However…" The Crazy Heavenly Demon tilted his head as he studied Lee Sungmin's face. "You there. What's wrong with your eye?"

"Pardon?"

At the question, Lee Sungmin blinked. He fumbled in his Subspace Pocket for a mirror. The face that stared back was his, yet it felt like a stranger's. It had been a long time, he realized, since he had truly looked at his own reflection. Lee Sungmin froze.

His left eye was strange. While his right eye was its usual black, his left was now tinged with a faint gold. Startled, he covered it with his palm.

[It is a Demonic Eye.]

Void Monarch's voice echoed in his head. *A Demonic Eye?* he asked back in his thoughts.

[It is proof that your body has been tainted by Demonic Energy and has begun to transform into a Yokai,] Void Monarch said with a click of his tongue. [For now, only the color has changed, so there's no problem, but if you continue to use Demonic Energy, the transformation will begin in earnest.]

Lee Sungmin tried circulating his qi. The pool of Demonic Energy at the bottom of his qi center had grown larger than before. It was proof that the side effects of drawing on Void Monarch's power to protect Wiji Hoyeon were beginning to saturate his body. He chewed on his lower lip.

[The power of a Yokai is dangerous for a human,] Void Monarch warned. [Consider yourself lucky it ended with just this. Though I may have lost my physical body, this Elder was one of the world's most renowned great yokai. You, a mere human, are trying to wield the Demonic Energy of a great yokai, not some common riffraff. If you didn't have that inexplicably strange heart in your body, you would have died the moment you used the Demonic Energy, unable to withstand it. Or you would have lost your sanity and gone on a rampage. You avoided both of those fates, but it seems you couldn't avoid the transformation into a Yokai.]

Listening to Void Monarch, Lee Sungmin bit his lip harder. He had been prepared for this. He had wanted to protect Wiji Hoyeon, even if it meant abandoning his humanity and becoming a Yokai.

[It's fine for now. The Demonic Energy pooled in your qi center will just stay there if you don't use it. However, I don't know about the future. Your case is unprecedented. While it's rare for a human to become a Yokai, it's not unheard of.]

Lee Sungmin put the mirror down. Though he had suddenly acquired a golden eye, he felt a sense of relief that it was manageable for now. He closed his left eye and chewed his lip.

"…Who is the Slaughter Predator of Predator?"

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had said it while blocking the Dark Supreme. There were only three beings in the world he found troublesome. The Wudang Sword Immortal, the Martial God of the Outer Heavens, and the Slaughter Predator of Predator. The Sword Immortal was likely the reclusive former Sect Leader of Wudang from two generations ago. While he did not know the Martial God, he could make a rough guess based on the existence of the Six Honored Ones, but he had no idea who the Slaughter Predator of Predator was.

[That is the monster of monsters that the ancient monsters of Predator long for,] Void Monarch explained with a laugh. [Vampire Queen, Arch Lich, Yokai, Lycanthrope, Death Knight. It's not a term that refers to one of those five, but a title for the king of monsters who will one day appear and stand at the pinnacle of Predator. At least, four hundred years ago when this Elder was around, the Slaughter Predator did not exist.]

He thought of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Dark Supreme. Heaven Beyond Heaven was far stronger than he had ever imagined. Even after drawing out his Demonic Energy, he could not gain the upper hand against the Dark Supreme. If the man had tried to kill him from the very beginning, he would not have even been able to resist.

He had used every means at his disposal. The Three Step Fatality of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and the sky-piercing of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, all while empowered by buff magic. It was shocking that he still could not gain an advantage. If the Dark Supreme had not been so relaxed, he would never have inflicted a fatal wound with the Three Step Fatality.

*However… I could have killed him.*

It was only because the Dark Supreme had been careless. If the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had not appeared, Lee Sungmin would have been able to kill him. In the end, the Chairman's actions were correct. If Lee Sungmin had killed the Dark Supreme there, Heaven Beyond Heaven would have immediately retaliated.

*What should I do from now on?*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said he would protect Wiji Hoyeon. Lee Sungmin did not know what to do about that. The choice belonged to her, not him.

*I'm still weak.*

If he were stronger than anyone in the world, he would not have had to face this crisis. He could have protected Wiji Hoyeon without needing anyone's help, and he would not have to worry about the Murim Alliance's pursuit.

*I need more.*

He needed power.

{sep}

For a full day, the Dark Supreme tended to his wounds. Those bizarre footsteps. The storm of enhanced qi created in three steps was a force impossible to counter, even if you knew it was coming. Although the Dark Supreme had not used his full strength, the Phantom Spear's martial arts were fierce enough to catch him off guard and kill him.

*He's not as good as the Little Heavenly Demon, but to think he was that skilled…*

The Dark Supreme bit his lower lip with a GRIND. Even with the power of a Demonic Spirit added, the man's martial arts were not to be underestimated. In fact, he had to be considered even more dangerous because of it. The man was human, still human, yet the power and energy he used were not. Perhaps the Phantom Spear was the one who would become the vessel for the Slaughter Predator.

*No. That's going too far. He was never a vessel of that caliber.*

The Dark Supreme got to his feet. A day of rest and a potion had healed all of his wounds. He wanted to continue pursuing the Phantom Spear and the Little Heavenly Demon, but the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's presence was a problem. The version he had seen yesterday was merely a clone, but its bizarre martial arts were not something to be ignored. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was nothing special compared to Heaven Beyond Heaven, but the Chairman himself stood above the Six Honored Ones.

First, he had to report this. The Dark Supreme took out a crystal ball.

[Return.]

That was the reply that came from the other side after he reported the Chairman's intervention and the Phantom Spear's unexpectedly high level of prowess. The command was so unexpected that the Dark Supreme's mouth fell half-open, and he remained silent for a moment.

"……"

Unable to understand, he asked again.

"Even if that clone of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman shows up again, it's still just a clone. Now that I know the Phantom Spear's strength, I won't be careless anymore. There should be no problem bringing back the Little Heavenly Demon."

[That may be so. However, it is no longer necessary.]

"What do you mean? Why?"

[Because the flow has changed,] the voice replied. [The Little Heavenly Demon was supposed to come to Heaven Beyond Heaven. At least, until yesterday. However, thanks to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's intervention and the existence of the Phantom Spear, it is no longer necessary for the Little Heavenly Demon to come here.]

"…Is this the will of the Spirit Medium?"

[There was a Spirit Possession after a long time. The Martial God has also given his permission, so do not be stubborn and return.]

The Dark Supreme pressed his lips together tightly. If the Spirit Medium had undergone a Spirit Possession and even the Martial God had given his permission, then there was no longer any need for him to pursue the Little Heavenly Demon.

"What about the Little Heavenly Demon's curse?"

[The Spirit Medium said nothing about that. It's unfortunate for the Fist Honored One, but he will have to continue to suffer.]

The conversation ended there. The Little Heavenly Demon's curse would remain, but Heaven Beyond Heaven would not force him to be brought there. The Dark Supreme could not accept it, but neither could he argue or refuse. He clicked his tongue and got to his feet.

His brow furrowed as he thought of Lee Sungmin.

*I've only been disgraced.*
