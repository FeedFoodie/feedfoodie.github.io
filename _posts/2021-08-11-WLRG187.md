Chuseong had to acknowledge it. He had no idea why this bastard was attacking him, but he could recognize a human of rare skill when he saw one. Indeed, one would need such strength just to have the guts to challenge a great yokai.

Still, it was all pointless. Chuseong's power was not something that could be compared to an ordinary great yokai, a fact that brought a sneer to his lips. He acknowledged the Crazy Heavenly Demon's power, but he knew it was not enough to do him any harm.

The truth was, the Crazy Heavenly Demon knew it too. His Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, the pinnacle of his training, struggled to even pierce Chuseong's flames. On the rare occasion a strike slipped through, it failed to penetrate the yokai's steel-like physique. He threw every technique he had, yet none could land a significant blow. As the battle dragged on, exhaustion began to weigh him down.

*Can I do it? No, I can't.*

The moment his thoughts leaned that way, his mind turned to Dersia. How had she died? How had the other villagers died? They must have died in agony, their bodies screaming as they burned in those very flames. His wavering heart found its resolve once more.

Chuseong guffawed as he watched the Crazy Heavenly Demon envelop his body in a Protective Qi Aura again.

"What's your reason for attacking me like this?" Chuseong asked, his curiosity genuine.

Within the blazing aura, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes were bloodshot. He spat his words, his voice thick with emotion.

"It was a few years ago. I don't know if you'd remember."

"What happened?"

"Near this city… there were people living in a forest. For some reason, you attacked them. You carried out an indiscriminate massacre."

"That kind of thing happens so often, I can't say I really remember."

"You met Yana in that forest," the Crazy Heavenly Demon continued. "And you fled because you were afraid of her."

At those words, Chuseong's face hardened. Massacres were a common pastime, but if Yana was involved… there was only one such incident.

"Yes," Chuseong ground his teeth. "I remember."

"I'd heard a story about a strange tribe, a tribe of humans who wielded Demonic Energy. It was a rumor that had started to circulate in Urmuri, but most Yokai paid it no mind. This Elder, however, was different. Humans wielding Demonic Energy? Such nonsense. So I went to confirm it for myself."

For Chuseong, it had been a trivial matter, no different from his usual affairs. It was just a casual outing.

"The rumor was true. I saw them… and I didn't like them. So I killed them! I still don't know why that damn bitch Yana suddenly appeared, though. So, are you a survivor from that village?"

"No."

There was no grand reason. The villagers had died simply because a rumor had spread, because it had reached Chuseong's ears, and because Chuseong had acted before the other Yokai. That was all.

"No? Then what is it? Why are you attacking me?"

"I was acquainted with the villagers you killed."

"Uhahaha!"

At the answer, Chuseong threw his head back and burst into loud laughter.

"Is that it? An acquaintance? You're going to die for *that*? I'll never understand you humans!"

*Laugh all you want,* the Crazy Heavenly Demon thought, glaring at Chuseong.

Whether it was possible or not didn't matter. His killing intent, his desire for revenge, was real. He charged toward Chuseong without hesitation.

Chuseong sneered again at the reckless charge. *Foolish bastard,* he muttered, swinging his hand.

KWAREREU!

A wave of crimson flames swept over the Crazy Heavenly Demon. He didn't dodge. Instead, he met the inferno with his palm.

KWAAAANG!

He had been using the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art at its peak from the very start. When the fierce palm strike hit the flames, they scattered in all directions. Simply spewing fire was not enough to stop him. Without slowing his charge in the slightest, the Crazy Heavenly Demon raised both palms and thrust them toward Chuseong. The palm technique and enhanced qi, unleashed at close range, engulfed the yokai.

Still, even as it shook the flames and struck Chuseong's body, it failed to inflict any pain.

Chuseong guffawed, swinging his wide-open hand to strike the Crazy Heavenly Demon's head. A direct hit would mean death. The Crazy Heavenly Demon gritted his teeth and twisted his waist. The trajectory of Chuseong's swing was dyed crimson as it erupted into flames, which then collided with the Crazy Heavenly Demon's Protective Qi Aura.

These were not ordinary flames but flames of Demonic Energy. The mere impact shook the Crazy Heavenly Demon's insides. Blood surged up his throat. He swallowed it back down, clenched both hands into fists, and struck Chuseong's chest with all his might.

"Hahaha!"

Despite taking a direct hit, Chuseong only laughed. The punch, thrown with all the Crazy Heavenly Demon's strength, felt as light as a cotton puff to him.

Chuseong had reigned as the second-in-command in Urmuri for a long time. Such strength put him on par with a master of the Transcendent Realm.

*No.*

The thought was immediate. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had personally experienced two masters of the Transcendent Realm in this body: the Sword Saint and the Dark Supreme, both from the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens. To him, Chuseong's strength felt greater than that of the Dark Supreme. In truth, the Dark Supreme had not been using his full power when they fought, so it was difficult to say who was truly superior.

However, one thing was certain.

Chuseong was weaker than the Sword Saint.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon had fought the Sword Saint ten years ago and suffered an overwhelming, miserable defeat. If the Sword Saint had not shown mercy, he would have lost his life to that blade. Ten years had passed since then, ten years he had spent waiting for the day he would fight the Sword Saint again, but he had yet to enter the Transcendent Realm himself. Even so, he could tell who was better. Chuseong was weaker than the Sword Saint.

However, what did that matter now?

KWAANG!

Chuseong's kick shook his Protective Qi Aura. There was little technique in the yokai's attacks. His strength was composed entirely of brutishly immense power and terrifying heat, but because the difference in their power was so great, the Crazy Heavenly Demon found it difficult to handle even these artless assaults.

*Even if he's weaker than the Sword Saint, he's stronger than me.*

His body rolled across the ground. His left arm throbbed. He had tried not to get hit, but he was hit all the same. He resented the past ten years, most of which he had spent lost in lunacy. If he had diligently focused on his martial arts for that decade, could he have entered the Transcendent Realm? No, the very thought was ridiculous. He had gained his lunacy because he met Dersia, and he would not deny that meeting, even if it had been just a brief encounter ten years ago.

His left arm hurt. *Is it broken?* This wasn't good. There was no time to drink an Elixir. Chuseong leaped high, bringing his flame-wreathed foot down upon him.

This was no time for appearances, not that the Crazy Heavenly Demon was the type to care for such things anyway. He unhesitatingly rolled to the side, dodging Chuseong's foot.

"Like an insect!" Chuseong burst out laughing.

After rolling a few times, the Crazy Heavenly Demon got up and stroked his left arm with his right hand. Fortunately, the bone was not broken. It was painful, but he could still move it without major hindrance. Relieved, he made a decision. He couldn't beat Chuseong by fighting like this. They said one could attain enlightenment in a moment of life and death, but he knew better. Enlightenment was not something you could obtain just by desperately wishing for it on the brink of oblivion. That was why he had been so surprised when he saw Lee Sungmin, who had attained it consecutively as if by miracle.

He could not rely on a miracle. He was already envisioning his own death, which made the choice before him an easy one.

Every martial artist who has learned an inner arts technique, opened their qi center, and accumulated inner arts within it possesses something they have preciously guarded at the very core of their being. It is their life qi, the essence of their accumulated inner arts and their life force itself. To lose it is to lose one's life, and to damage it is to shorten one's lifespan.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon drew it up without hesitation. The Protective Qi Aura surrounding his body surged violently as his life force became inner arts. The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, which he had trained his entire life, shone more intensely and powerfully than at any other moment he had ever unleashed it.

"What did you do?" Chuseong asked, not understanding.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon didn't answer. He had never imagined he would end up using his life qi in a place like this. He had always thought that if the day came, it would be in a fight against the Sword Saint. He didn't regret it. Rather, his heart grew calm. He would carry out his revenge with all his might, whether he succeeded or failed.

Either way, he would die, and he fully intended to succeed. He intended to die with Chuseong.

Chuseong knew something had changed, but he couldn't understand what it was. He blinked, looking at the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was now wreathed in a violent red. He tilted his head at the man's now-serene face and opened his mouth to ask again.

Before the words could leave his mouth, the Crazy Heavenly Demon was closing the distance between them.

All the martial arts he had mastered were being unleashed more powerfully than ever before, fueled by his life qi. Seeing the Crazy Heavenly Demon right in front of him, Chuseong's open mouth gaped even wider, and the palm the Crazy Heavenly Demon thrust out struck his chest.

KUUUNG!

A heavy sound, different from before, rang out.

Chuseong gasped. The heavy blow shook his heart. As he staggered back, the Crazy Heavenly Demon swung a fist at his face.

Chuseong couldn't react.

KWAJIK!

His head snapped to the side. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had hoped to shatter his skull in a single blow, but that seemed to have been too greedy. Still, it was a powerful attack, enough to momentarily sever Chuseong's consciousness.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's face was calm as he glanced at Chuseong's unfocused eyes. It was not over yet. The power gained from burning his life was still far from extinguished.

KWAKWAKWANG!

A series of palm strikes battered Chuseong's body, launching his massive frame into the air. Amidst the pain that felt like his entire body was being shattered, Chuseong forced his eyes open.

"Ughhh!"

Anger came before the pain. His face contorted as he twisted his body in mid-air. The first thing he saw was the Crazy Heavenly Demon, facing him with both hands raised.

"Blood Ring Typhoon," the Crazy Heavenly Demon's lips moved. The ultimate technique of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art was unleashed.

Red enhanced qi erupted from beneath his feet, surging violently before shooting out in all directions. Soon, a massive vortex formed around him. The raging storm of red enhanced qi battered Chuseong's body. The Crazy Heavenly Demon raised his hands a little higher.

Chuseong's face twisted, and he opened his mouth wide. A brilliant light flashed from within his gaping maw. With a harsh roar, crimson flames poured out of his mouth, streaming down in a straight line from above the Crazy Heavenly Demon's head, trying to swallow his body.

However, before they could, the Crazy Heavenly Demon clenched his open hands into fists.

The Blood Ring Typhoon exploded.

Caught in the storm of scattering enhanced qi, Chuseong's body shot high into the air like a kite with its string cut. The flames he had spewed failed to reach their target.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a ragged breath and lowered his raised hands.

KWAAAANG!

Chuseong's body crashed to the ground, shaking the earth. The Crazy Heavenly Demon turned around pointedly to see Rubia staring at him with a dazed expression. He gave her a faint smile, then turned his head back to look at Chuseong.

His feet felt slightly heavier as he approached the yokai. Embedded deep in the ground, Chuseong was a wretched sight. The iron armor that had covered his entire body was torn to shreds by the Blood Ring Typhoon, and crimson blood streamed from the gaps. The Crazy Heavenly Demon drew closer, not letting his guard down.

Chuseong's closed eyes snapped open.

"KRAAA!"

He rose with an enraged roar, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon charged toward him as if he had been waiting for it. Even half-mad with excruciating pain, Chuseong moved to kill him. His iron armor regenerated rapidly, and the hand he swung erupted with crimson flames as it swept toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

There was still no technique in his attacks. A Yokai's tough body and Demonic Energy made technique unnecessary, but in a situation like this, what Chuseong desperately needed was that very technique. If they were in a state of equal power, the one more skilled would inevitably have the upper hand. Chuseong knew this. Even knowing, there was nothing he could do.

However, the situation was by no means bad for him.

"Keoheuk!"

Blood sprayed from the Crazy Heavenly Demon's mouth.

The end was drawing near.
