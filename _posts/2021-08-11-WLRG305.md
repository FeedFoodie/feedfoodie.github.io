Something about this was fundamentally wrong.

Lee Sungmin had never considered himself a good person, for to claim so would be a disgusting lie, yet, there was one thing he would never surrender, a truth that had, at some point, become incredibly important to him.

*The fact that I am human.*

The threat of becoming a Yokai loomed over him, a future he could not predict, and so he clung to the certainty of his present humanity. He did not know what kind of creature he might become, which was precisely why he had to trust the man he was now.

Kim Jonghyun was wrong.

Lee Sungmin's thoughts did not venture further than that simple conclusion. He could not sympathize with what the man desired, nor did he want to help him achieve his goal. Kim Jonghyun was not human. Though his body was that of a half-Demon Lord, the being named Kim Jonghyun residing within it, once a man, was now a monster that could never have been called human in the first place.

Even if Kim Jonghyun's plan succeeded and this world escaped the fate of The End, just as he had said, what would come after?

Ultimately, nothing would change. It was merely a difference in method, a choice between this world being finished by The End or by the Demon Race and their Lords from a realm connected to Eria. The only real difference, if he had to name one, was whether everyone died or a few survived. Perhaps what Kim Jonghyun proposed was a radical solution, yes, but it was a solution Lee Sungmin could not accept.

"I won't say that you're wrong," Lee Sungmin stated, admitting he did not know the true solution himself. "However, I cannot join you."

"And why is that?" Kim Jonghyun asked. "I know you were under the protection of The End. Maybe you're the very one who led this world to its fate. You know that too, don't you? You came here to stop me, the first disaster, because in the end, what you want is to stop The End. Isn't that right?"

"That's right."

"......"

Kim Jonghyun stroked his chin, a brief silence stretching between them before he nodded his head.

"I have no intention of forcing you," he said. "It would've been better if you'd lent me your strength, but well, that's not something I can control."

To be honest, he had only intended to test him. At the very least, Kim Jonghyun had not lied to Lee Sungmin about the purpose of this world or the identity of The End, truths he had learned from the lords beyond the threshold of the Demon Realm. It was from them that he had also heard of the possibility of connecting the Demon Realm and Eria.

*I can do it. Should I try?* The thought had been the seed from which this entire affair had grown.

"Your existence has always been a question for me," Kim Jonghyun began, his gaze analytical. From their very first meeting, his eyes had allowed him to see through Lee Sungmin's identity to some extent, though at the time, he had not fully understood The End. All he had grasped back then was that some unknown protection shielded the boy.

"You died once and returned to the past, carrying knowledge of a me that I never knew. Your very being filled me with questions, with a relentless curiosity. What on earth was I doing in your past life? What was I like?"

"...At least, until the day I died, I had never heard the name Kim Jonghyun. I don't remember the name of the Black Magic Tower Master, but in my past life... you didn't commit the great massacre in the North."

"Then I probably wasn't the Black Magic Tower Master in your past life," Kim Jonghyun mused. "Perhaps, just as when I first met you, I lived absorbed in my own research, belonging to some Magic Tower without any particular interest or desire. Do you understand what I'm trying to say?"

"I don't."

"I became who I am because I met you." Kim Jonghyun lowered the hand he had held outstretched. "In your past life, I did nothing. Before meeting you, I had no particular desires or interests. Our meeting brought about many changes and opportunities for me. It was quite a shock, in many ways, to realize I had ended up as a figure not even worth remembering."

*Can I do it? Should I try?*

"So I became the Black Magic Tower Master and joined Predator. I won Arbes's favor, pretended to be his confidant, then annihilated him to steal his Grimoire. I tried to become a Demon Lord, failed, and now... I'm doing this." As he spoke, a delighted look spread across his face, like a child proudly presenting a test with a perfect score to his parents. "For me, it was a pleasure to be conscious of the me from a past life, something I normally couldn't be. Becoming a different me from that me... that sense of deviation has always delighted me."

"Then can't you just end it here?"

"No, I can't do that. I'm only doing this because I can. It's not like I'm doing this because I particularly want to stop The End... I'll ask just to be sure. Are you going to stop me?"

"......"

"For now, my actions can stop The End. You do understand that clearly, don't you? You're trying to stop me even though you know that, and all while you don't even have a proper way to stop The End yourself." Kim Jonghyun chuckled. "Didn't I tell you? The End is a culling. It's killing all the worthless guinea pigs in the Slaughterhouse and then opening a new one. Did I say I was the first disaster? Can you even imagine what kind of second one will come after me?"

Lee Sungmin shook his head. Kim Jonghyun laughed loudly.

"My Master," Lee Sungmin said, the final moments of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman flashing in his mind. "He died because of me. If I hadn't said I wanted to stop The End, my Master wouldn't have died like that. Because I told him, he died that way."

"You intend to carry on your Master's will?"

"I want to take responsibility for what I've done." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had decided to stop The End because Lee Sungmin had asked him to. If not for that request, he might still be alive today.

"Two days." Kim Jonghyun held up two fingers. "I will leave this city in two days. The preparations here aren't enough to completely connect the Demon Realm and this world, but what I've done will become the first point of connection. After that, once enough sacrifices are gathered, the link will be complete." He repeated the words. "Two days."

"If I kill you here, you won't be able to leave this city, and what you're trying to do will fail."

"Lee Sungmin," Kim Jonghyun said, his tone patient. "You're strong, but... did you really think I would stand here and talk with you without having imagined the extent of your power?"

*Should I test him?* Lee Sungmin's grip tightened on his spear, a purple current flowing along the shaft. His first strike had failed to break through Kim Jonghyun's defense, but what about now?

[Vollander is watching,] the Void Monarch warned, his voice a sharp intrusion in Lee Sungmin's mind.

*I know.* Unseen, numerous presences had gathered around the mansion. They were Death Knights, and among them, Vollander's presence was thick and suffocating. Could he kill Kim Jonghyun? What about Vollander and the other Death Knights?

[Retreat. It's your best option,] the Monarch insisted. [There's no guarantee you can kill Kim Jonghyun even if you bet your life on it. I feel a foreign energy from him, and Vollander is stronger than you imagine. Killing Kim Jonghyun while also dealing with Vollander is impossible for you.]

"You swore you wouldn't harm me," Lee Sungmin said aloud.

"That's right. I won't harm you."

[Even if Kim Jonghyun doesn't step in, the fact that you're at a disadvantage doesn't change. Do you think the Death Knight Legion is full of nobodies besides Vollander? His very existence makes them stronger than their individual capabilities. Your best bet is to get out of here.]

It was the first time the Void Monarch had given such advice. Lee Sungmin did not offer a rebuttal, accepting the fact that killing Kim Jonghyun here was impossible.

"I don't dislike you," Kim Jonghyun called out as Lee Sungmin turned to leave. "Your existence was a great catalyst for me. Therefore, I won't withdraw the offer I made you."

He could join hands with Kim Jonghyun whenever he wanted. The possibility remained open.

Without another word, Lee Sungmin left the mansion. The moment he stepped outside, the suffocating hostility that had pressed in from all sides vanished as if it had never been there.

"This is important."

Back in the city, Lee Sungmin flung open the door to Abel's carriage. Inside, Abel was recovering his Mana next to a mountain of scrolls. He opened his eyes and looked up.

"You went to the city?"

"Kim Jonghyun summoned me."

"What did you talk about?"

"That's what I want to discuss," Lee Sungmin said, glancing at the Gries placed beside the mage.

Abel's eyebrows furrowed. "You have a habit of making me use this recklessly, since it's not *your* lifespan on the line."

"Are you going to listen or not?"

"I have to, don't I? If the story is that important." Abel sighed and stood up. "I'll go get Frau. I can't trust the Church. The Saint, Teresa, seemed good-natured enough, but she's not someone I want to share stories with at the cost of my own life."

A short while later, he returned with Frau, who plopped down inside the carriage and tugged at her messy hair.

"Not even letting me sleep," she grumbled.

"Sleep? Don't make me laugh. You were rolling around with some man. What sin did your disciple commit to deserve listening to those lewd noises outside your door?"

"The sin of abandoning her Master," Frau shot back.

"If I had a bitch like you for a master, I'd have left too," Abel grumbled as he placed his hand on the Gries.

Frau's eyes gleamed with a strange light. "...You're using dangerous magic. The only thing waiting at the end of magic that uses lifespan as collateral is a terrible ruin."

"I know."

Once the barrier was cast, Lee Sungmin recounted his entire conversation with Kim Jonghyun. Though he had inherited the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power and could now speak freely of The End, Abel and Frau had not.

When he finished, a heavy silence filled the carriage.

"That crazy bastard," Abel finally muttered, covering his face with both hands. "Stopping The End? That's nonsense. If the Great Demon World connects to this world, it would be better to end by The End's culling. At least The End will end this world fairly."

"What exactly happens if it connects to the Demon Realm?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"The world goes mad," Frau answered. "And it's not just any Demon Realm, but the Great Demon World where the Demon God and its lords live. If we connect to that... the world will truly go mad. They are beings far beyond human reasoning, monsters who have subjugated tens of thousands of souls and lived for eons, possessing power befitting their age. Their very existence can drive those of lower status mad with terror."

Lee Sungmin had experienced something similar in Preskan's dungeon. The remnants of the Demon King of Fear had been enough to nearly shatter his mind.

"Connecting to the Great Demon World means turning this world not into a Slaughterhouse, but a butcher shop," Frau continued. "The Demon Race who cross over will destroy everything in sight for their own amusement. It won't be a total annihilation. Some will survive, and... when that number increases even a little, the slaughter will begin again. This world will become their playground."

The End would be better. Lee Sungmin found himself agreeing with Abel.

"However," Abel began, his hands slowly lowering from his face. "Kim Jonghyun committing this insane act... it's actually an opportunity."

"What do you mean?"

"It was a good thing I brought the Gries," Abel muttered, stroking the artifact. "By trying to connect the Great Demon World to this one, he's tangling this world's fate. Yes... the problem is his target. If we could connect to a different world, one that isn't the Great Demon World, we could stop The End."

"Is that possible?"

"It is," Abel said, his voice filled with conviction.

"Because I am the pinnacle of mages."
