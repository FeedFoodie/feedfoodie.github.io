The road to the Forest of Ancients was a quiet one, leaving Lee Sungmin lost in his thoughts. With the death of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia's departure, the boisterous energy of their group had vanished, and a natural silence had fallen over him.

The Void Monarch seemed to sense his mood, speaking more than usual to fill the void. Most of it was nonsense, of course, just tales of his own past exploits, but the effort was not lost on Lee Sungmin. Thanks to the old ghost, the gloom weighing on him began to lift.

One night, as he sat alone before a crackling campfire, Lee Sungmin found himself staring into the flames.

"Thank you," he muttered.

The space around the fire felt empty. He could almost picture Rubia whining about sleeping outdoors while the Crazy Heavenly Demon coaxed and soothed her. Come to think of it, he had mostly been a bystander in their conversations, yet he had never felt like an outsider. He found himself missing the simple normalcy of those days.

[What's there to be thankful for?]

"For looking out for me."

[You brat, you're spouting some disgusting nonsense. Cut the crap and eat your food.]

The Void Monarch grumbled. A small chuckle escaped Lee Sungmin as he took out some meat and placed it over the fire.

Watching him, the Void Monarch felt a bitter sensation.

*The kid is unstable.* He had become certain of it recently.

Though Lee Sungmin had been severely fragile when they first met, his condition had improved dramatically after meeting Rubia, the Void Monarch, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon. Now that he was alone again, however, his mind was beginning to waver once more.

*He's a strange one.*

Honestly, the Void Monarch could not understand him. It was a strange contradiction. How could a man possess the absurd mental fortitude to endure twenty-one hundred years, yet be so fragile as to be shaken by loneliness? It was a very human weakness, but it made no sense in the context of his impossible resilience.

He was a bundle of contradictions. That was the Void Monarch's assessment of Lee Sungmin.

*Besides, that thing… why is something like that connected to this kid?*

The Void Monarch recalled the unknown monster he had witnessed in Lee Sungmin's mental world, the thing that had bombarded him with hundreds of images of death, a being who had even hunted dragons. He had a vague idea of what the creature was, but he could not fathom why it resided inside Lee Sungmin's head.

A single path. At its end, the creature had asked him a question. *Will you come this way?*

He could have gone. Had he chosen to walk that path, the Void Monarch would have left Lee Sungmin and ceased to exist in this world. It was a path anyone would covet, for to walk it and stand at its end meant becoming an absolute being. Even he, who had lost his physical body, could have achieved it.

However, the Void Monarch did not walk that path. It was not because he lacked greed.

He had lingering attachments. He was concerned. The old ghost grumbled at Lee Sungmin, who was still staring into the campfire.

[You ungrateful brat.]

"What did I do?"

[Forget it. More importantly… are you prepared?]

"For what?"

Lee Sungmin asked, though he knew perfectly well what the Void Monarch meant. He was, after all, on his way to the Forest of Ancients, the home of the Elves.

[To kill the Fist Honored One. If he's maintaining that curse, he'll be extremely weak. You could probably kill a man in his state as easily as snapping a child's neck, and even if he were at his full fighting spirit… well, I think you could probably win.]

Strictly speaking, the current Lee Sungmin had not yet completely reached the Transcendent Realm. He had entered it within his Mental World, but he had not fully assimilated that enlightenment into his physical body. To do so, he needed the final moment of realization that led to it. That said, it was not as if he was significantly inferior to the masters who had already made the crossing.

His body, having undergone a Yokai transformation, was stronger than ever before. By wielding his Demonic Energy and inner arts in tandem, he was capable of unleashing far more powerful attacks.

Wasn't the Fist Honored One said to be the weakest of the Six Honored Ones? If he was weaker than the Dark Supreme, Lee Sungmin felt he had a good chance of gaining the upper hand, even if the man was at his peak. Of course, he could not be certain. A fight, regardless of the gap in power, could be overturned by any number of variables.

[Killing the Fist Honored One is one thing, but if you do it… you'll become an enemy of those guys, the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens or whatever they're called. The Dark Supreme retreated last time because of that Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but I doubt they'll leave you alone if the Fist Honored One dies.]

"It's not like I have a choice."

He had to break Wiji Hoyeon's curse. He remembered the sight of her, weakened and frail, shouting, 'Isn't it enough if I just go?' That image was incredibly unfamiliar. The Wiji Hoyeon he knew was always a pillar of strength and confidence, but the woman he saw then was the very picture of a weakling.

[It's not just Heaven Beyond Heaven. Since the Fist Honored One is in the Elf Forest, you'll have to invade it. You'll probably run into conflict with the Elves in the process. Elves are pretty, live a long time, and because their numbers are so small, their love for their own kind is strong. If you kill the Fist Honored One, the Elves will make you an enemy of their entire race.]

"I have a lot of enemies," Lee Sungmin muttered. "I'm not sure why, though. The Murim Alliance doesn't seem to like my existence very much. You know it too. They set a trap for me in the Bewitching Forest. I didn't kill Jaegal Taeryeong, but they spread rumors that I did. It's not a big deal right now since I'm in the South where their power is weak… but if I leave, they'll send pursuers after me again."

[So you're saying it doesn't matter if you make a few more enemies since you've already got so many?]

"It's not something I need to worry about at this point," Lee Sungmin said with a chuckle. "After I finish my business in the South, I plan to go meet the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. I've heard where he is… and Wiji Hoyeon will probably be waiting there too."

*If I go under the umbrella of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the pursuit from the Murim Alliance will be resolved to some extent. I'll be completely branded as a demonic practitioner, but that would be better. Could the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance protect me from Heaven Beyond Heaven?*

Lee Sungmin could not be sure. However, judging by the Dark Supreme's reaction, even monsters of the Transcendent Realm like the Six Sages were reluctant to clash with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*Does that mean he's a monster who stands above the Transcendent Realm? Is there another level above it?*

*There's no end to it.*

He remembered when he was a C-Rank Mercenary, marveling at peak masters and thinking they were the strongest in the world, but once he became a peak master himself, he found there were plenty of people stronger than him. It was the same even after he reached the Supreme Peak.

The saying that what you see depends on where you stand felt profoundly true. Perhaps even from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's position, if you looked up, there might be something else. The thought sent a chill down his spine and gave him goosebumps. Honestly, he could not quite imagine it.

After the night passed, Lee Sungmin started moving again. As he traveled, he continuously tried to harmonize his inner arts and Demonic Energy. When he had first entered Urmuri's Central Plaza, he had been unable to properly control his Demonic Energy. It was such a powerful force that it would often burst out on its own when it grew too large.

That condition, however, had improved a lot. It was an achievement gained from receiving Urmuri's Demonic Energy and having his body forcibly transformed into a Yokai.

*Although I'm not sure if you could call this an achievement.*

It was still difficult to control when the power swelled, but he no longer needed to be constantly on edge, focusing his mind to keep it contained. This was thanks to his body's transformation, but it had also become a double-edged sword. If he let it become too excessive, the inhumanity he had painstakingly suppressed might awaken.

Still, gaining such a powerful force, even if it was hard to control, was alluring, especially when he might have to fight an unpredictable opponent.

Lee Sungmin looked into the distance.

A forest was coming into view.

{sep}

The man sat in a comfortable position, his eyes closed as if he were sleeping. Despite this relaxed appearance, he showed no openings at all.

From where he lay, his face haggard, the Fist Honored One glared at the man.

"So," the Fist Honored One finally said, unable to bear the prolonged silence.

He willed himself to sit up, but his body refused to obey. He ground his teeth at the helplessness that crushed his entire being. He wanted to shake off this powerlessness more than anything. In fact, it was possible if he truly set his mind to it. The problem was that he could not.

It had been over a year since he placed the curse on the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, and began maintaining it. Wiji Hoyeon was likely suffering, but the Fist Honored One was also unable to move properly, trapped by the enervating effects of his own magic. To protect himself, he had returned to his homeland, the Elf Forest, one of the safest places in the world.

He had no complaints about life here, but compared to the overwhelming power he once wielded, he was desperate to shake off the curse and recover his fighting spirit as soon as possible.

"Do I… have to keep going on like this?"

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, is protecting the Little Heavenly Demon," the man said without opening his eyes. "It's an unavoidable situation. With the Martial God in seclusion, there's no one in Heaven Beyond Heaven who can face Yang Ilcheon."

"Nonsense… If all six of us attacked, even someone like Yang Ilcheon…"

"Do you think all of the Six Honored Ones combined could defeat the Martial God?" the man retorted with a smirk.

The Fist Honored One's body flinched at his words.

"Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, along with the Sword Immortal, is a worthy opponent acknowledged by the Martial God. Even if all of us attacked, we couldn't do anything to the Demon Emperor. That's why we have no choice but to remain silent."

"The Dark Supreme… that moron. He should have brought the Little Heavenly Demon back then, even if he had to use force…!"

"He retreated because the Spirit Medium told him not to."

"I can't understand the Spirit Medium. In the end, he's just a human. Even if he can perform Spirit Possession, we have no way of knowing if the words he utters are the Spirit's will or his own."

"You shouldn't doubt him, and it wouldn't make sense. If the Spirit Medium said it of his own accord, why would he entrust the Little Heavenly Demon to the Demon Emperor? The Little Heavenly Demon is essential for Heaven Beyond Heaven's long-cherished wish, and the Spirit Medium himself desires it. That is an undeniable fact."

"……"

"Do not doubt the Spirit Medium. Doubting him is the same as doubting the Martial God. Are you doubting the Martial God?"

The man opened his eyes. His piercing blue gaze turned to the Fist Honored One, whose body trembled beneath it. It had been years since they last met, yet the man had grown even stronger.

The Fist Honored One bit his lower lip hard. This only made him feel more desperate. Because he was maintaining the curse, he could not move his body properly, let alone train. While he was stuck, the other Honored Ones were continuing their own training and reaching even higher realms, leaving him running in place.

"…I do not… doubt him."

"I know your situation is pitiful. The other Honored Ones know as well. That's why I came here myself."

"Why? Are you afraid I'll stop the curse on my own?" the Fist Honored One chuckled darkly.

The man shook his head. "That's not why I came. Besides, we know very well that you wouldn't act on your own unless you were a fool."

At those words, the Fist Honored One's laughter died. He knew the power of Heaven Beyond Heaven, of the Six Honored Ones, all too well. Six monsters who had entered the Transcendent Realm. If Predator had its five Black Stars, then Heaven Beyond Heaven had its six masters of the Transcendent Realm. If they were the pinnacle of the inhuman, then Heaven Beyond Heaven was the six humans and mutants who stood at the apex of martial arts.

The Fist Honored One, an Elf, had lived a long life devoted to his fist techniques. Although he had also spent much time on sorcery, his superior lifespan and youth gave him a great advantage in learning martial arts. Even so, among all the Six Honored Ones, he was only in last place.

Fist Honored One, the Dark Supreme, Blade Supreme, the Sword Saint, the Spear King, and Moon Queen. Among them, the four who were not the exceptionally strong Spear King and Moon Queen held the title of 'Zone', and even among those four, the gap in power was quite large.

The Fist Honored One stared at the man before him. He was the Sword Saint, the master considered the most outstanding among the four who held the title of 'Zone'.

"The reason I came," the Sword Saint said, glancing at the Fist Honored One, "is because the Spirit Medium told me something. He said that you might die."

"…What?"

"I don't know why, but it seems that you might die… sometime soon," the Sword Saint chuckled as he spoke. "And that's why I'm here. To make sure you don't."
