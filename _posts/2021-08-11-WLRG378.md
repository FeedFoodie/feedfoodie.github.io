An inevitable silence fell over the forest clearing. The Queen's annihilation had been too strange for anyone to ignore.

In the first place, the pinnacle of martial arts Lee Sungmin had unleashed was not powerful enough to cause such a complete demise, and no one else had made a move. Lloyd and Scarlet had not cast any magic. Neither the Spear King, the Black Dragon Knight, nor Baek Sogo had attacked. Even the Queen herself had been bewildered by her own end, her final, stammered words echoing in their minds. *Why... me?*

*Spirit...* Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. Just before she vanished, the Queen had called out to the Spirit, as if it were the very cause of her destruction.

He immediately expanded his senses, casting out the Beggars' Gang technique like a net. His mind screamed with exhaustion from the pinnacle of martial arts he had just performed, but this was no time to rest. If the Spirit was responsible for the Queen's end, and if it controlled the Martial God like a puppet, then perhaps the Martial God himself was here, lurking somewhere in this forest.

*...He's not here...*

Though he searched the surroundings thoroughly with his widely expanded senses, he could not feel the Martial God's presence. Had the Martial God not come in person? Had the Spirit intervened forcefully, just as it had with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but why?

None of it made sense. The Spirit had only interfered with the Chairman because his power had reached a realm forbidden in this world. There was no reason for it to intervene here. The Spirit desired The End, and the advented Spirit Queen was meant to function as one of its calamities. Even if she was on the verge of defeat, what reason could the Spirit have to step in and annihilate her itself?

[Perhaps it was wary of you growing stronger?]

*I never intended to devour her in the first place.*

His body would never have been able to withstand devouring a spiritual entity on the level of the Spirit Queen. He had confirmed this several times with Scarlet and Lloyd, who had compared his physical composition to Aine's. Their bodies were fundamentally different, and the current Lee Sungmin had already reached his limit for growth through predation.

[It might have been considering the 'what ifs'. You don't belong to the fate of The End. The Spirit can't control you, and the Spirit Queen failed. It would have had no reason to keep her alive.]

He understood the Void Monarch's logic. If this world was destined to end completely, a new Spirit Queen would simply appear in the next. In this endlessly repeating cycle, she was not such an important existence after all.

*Is the Spirit aware of me?*

[That... is a bit strange. If it's aware of you, considers you an obstacle, and is concerned about you devouring the Spirit Queen to become stronger... wouldn't it be better for it to just step in and kill you directly?]

He could not believe it was because the Spirit lacked the power to do so.

[Perhaps because you don't belong to fate, the Spirit can't intervene directly.]

Nothing was certain. Chewing his lower lip, Lee Sungmin looked down at the empty space where the Spirit Queen had vanished.

"We need to know," Lloyd's voice cut through the quiet. He suddenly plopped down on the ground, placing Gries on his lap. He reached out, resting a hand on the grimoire's cover. "We have too little information," he said, his eyes narrowed as he looked down at the book. "A moment ago, the Spirit Queen shouldn't have been annihilated."

"It wasn't suicide," the Spear King muttered, a note of disappointment in his voice. He had hoped for a longer, more enjoyable fight, but the battle had ended far too easily. In fact, he did not even consider that last encounter a proper fight.

"The fact that she was annihilated is undeniable," Lloyd continued. "It's not a bad thing, rather, it's a good thing. With this, the world has once again distanced itself from The End. The closest calamity, the Spirit Queen, has vanished."

"Should we really call something of that level a calamity?" the Spear King sneered.

The Black Dragon Knight, standing beside him, shot him a glare. "We were lucky, and the situation was in our favor. If the Little Heavenly Demon hadn't run rampant in the Elemental Realm, and if we hadn't cut down the Spirit Kings who advented first. If we hadn't anticipated the Queen's advent and prepared for it, she would have undoubtedly become a calamity."

The Spear King did not refute the words, instead clicking his tongue with a sharp, 'Tsk'.

"How boring," he muttered, sitting down on the ground.

Even though they had overcome the calamity known as the Spirit Queen, no one could rejoice with a peaceful heart. They all knew this was not the end of The End, and too many questions now surrounded the Queen's sudden destruction.

Lloyd sought to find the answer. Gries could see events of the past and present, and an incident on the scale of the Spirit Queen's advent and annihilation would surely have been captured within its pages. He closed his eyes and focused his mind as the grimoire began to demand his lifespan. It was a familiar feeling. He gauged what he had left but did not think it a waste. If he were the type to regret such an offering, Abel would never have passed Gries on to him.

Ignoring the drain on his life, he commanded the book. *Show me.* Gries hummed and vibrated, and Lloyd's consciousness floated upwards.

He found himself not far from here, just outside the Sleeping Forest. Someone was standing beyond the barrier. A man in a yellow robe. Beside him stood a woman whose eyes could not be seen, and... another woman, this one in a white martial arts uniform.

*They* were the ones who had annihilated the Spirit Queen.

Lloyd's eyes snapped open. He immediately used magic to project the image he had just seen for everyone to witness. Lee Sungmin watched, his face turning deathly pale. The man in the yellow robe was the Martial God. He could not tell who the woman with her eyes closed was, but the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight could guess.

"Is that... the Spirit Medium?"

Although the two had once belonged to Heaven Beyond Heaven, this was the first time either had seen her in person.

Lee Sungmin stared intently at the face of the Elf in the martial arts uniform. He had seen her somewhere before. Racking his memory, he finally recalled the face. He had not met many Elves in his life.

*The Hunting Team Leader.*

It was Revias, the daughter of the Fist Honored One. She was with the Martial God.

"They're beyond the barrier," the Spear King declared, jumping to his feet. With excited eyes, he gripped his spear and turned to leave.

The Black Dragon Knight's hand shot out, grabbing his shoulder. "What are you trying to do?"

"I'm going to avenge what happened ten years ago."

"You're insane. Don't be so rash."

"What's rash about it? Have you already forgotten the humiliation from ten years ago? The two of us attacking together and still being unable to do anything to the one-armed Martial God! Now is the perfect chance to avenge it."

"That's why there's no need to seek revenge right now," the Black Dragon Knight snapped, his expression frustrated.

Lee Sungmin pressed his lips together tightly, his gaze fixed on the image of the Martial God. He had not changed much from ten years ago. Blood dripped from his tightly clenched fist. The Martial God, just like the Spirit, was the enemy who had directly killed the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"Are you confident you can handle it?" the Black Dragon Knight asked, his glare shifting to the Spear King. His words, however, were not directed solely at him. He was also speaking to Lee Sungmin. "We just went through a battle. You seem to be in fine shape, but some of us are exhausted."

The pinnacle of martial arts was a technique that even the current Lee Sungmin could not execute perfectly. He was so drained right now that he could not even swing his spear properly.

"It's true that you and I have reached a higher state through ten years of arduous training," the Black Dragon Knight continued, not giving the Spear King an opening to retort. "But while we were training, do you really think the Martial God was just playing around? Spear King, you admitted it yourself. That your current martial prowess is only equal to the Martial God's from ten years ago."

At those words, the Spear King's face twisted.

"I haven't progressed as much as you have," the Black Dragon Knight pressed on. "Even if you and I attack together, we probably won't be able to defeat him, and don't forget this. The Martial God is receiving help from the Spirit."

*Right.*

Lee Sungmin let out a choked breath as his boiling emotions cooled. The Martial God was his master's enemy. He had no intention of ever forgetting that, but if he let his desire for revenge sway him now, if he charged at the Martial God, what would be the outcome? He was in no condition to fight. Could the Spear King, the Black Dragon Knight, Baek Sogo, Scarlet, and Lloyd push the Martial God into a corner by themselves? He recalled the strange memories from the Moon Palace, of Revias killing her own brethren to meet the Moon Queen, and of the Spirit.

"...There's no need to clash with the Martial God right now."

"That bastard is your Master's enemy," the Spear King said, looking at Lee Sungmin with a sullen expression.

Lee Sungmin moved to help Lloyd to his feet. "...I will avenge my master. However, as the Black Dragon Knight said, fighting the Martial God now is too risky."

*Don't be swayed by emotion,* he told himself, summoning the Fairy Horse.

The Spear King looked reluctant but did not insist any further. Baek Sogo came to Lee Sungmin's side and helped support Lloyd.

"...You did well, Junior Disciple."

"It wasn't anything amazing."

"What do you mean, no?" Scarlet scowled. "Why's everyone so gloomy? We can be happy, you know. The ending was a bit suspicious and crappy, but we just saved the world. Didn't we?" She kicked at the dirt for no reason. "But everyone's so down."

"We did something amazing and none of us died," she insisted. "We successfully stopped one of the calamities of The End. Even if it was just us swooping in to finish something someone else had already half-killed!"

"Right. That's why it was boring," the Spear King grumbled.

Scarlet ignored him and glared at Lee Sungmin. "We're going to have a drinking party when we get back." She held out her pinky finger. *Promise me.* "It's kind of unfair when you think about it. We went through all this hell to save the world, but the people of the world don't even know we did something so great. So what can we do? We have to celebrate among ourselves. When we get back, let's just drink and have fun. For just one night, let's not think about anything complicated or difficult, just chat excitedly, tell each other 'good job', and cheer each other on."

"...Alright," Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile, nodding at her barrage of words.

"Do we not need to enter?" the Martial God asked, looking at the barrier that blocked the forest entrance.

Beside him, Revias stared into the barrier with a stoic expression. Her consciousness had been devoured by the Moon Queen, so while the body was Revias's, the mind was that of the Moon Queen. The well-trained body of an Elf was much more to her liking than her previous one.

"There's no need. Everything is already over."

"You mean the Spirit Queen has been annihilated?"

"Yes," the Spirit Medium said, nodding beside the Martial God.

At her words, the Martial God's eyebrow twitched slightly. He did not feel at ease. Annihilating the Spirit Queen, who had advented as a calamity of The End, was supposed to have been his duty.

"Please don't be too displeased," the Spirit Medium said to him. She could not see what was in front of her, for she was seeing something else entirely. She watched Lee Sungmin and his party escape the forest on the Fairy Horse and smiled faintly.

"Weren't they also called calamities of The End?" the Martial God asked. "Wouldn't it be better to kill them now while we have the chance?"

"No, Martial God. There is no need for that. It is best to use poison to subdue poison. The calamities of The End are fighting amongst themselves, exhausting their strength. You need only step forward at the most opportune moment."

"Is there really a need for that..."

"The Spirit desires a certain conclusion."

"Does the Spirit not trust me?"

"Of course not. Your power is already sufficient to bring about The End. However... in the picture the Spirit is painting, you are not yet meant to fight them." The Spirit, wearing the mask of the Spirit Medium, smiled faintly. *It is not yet time for the harvest. One must not reap what has not fully ripened.*

"Please step back," the Spirit Medium said, bowing her head slightly.

*You dull-witted human.*

She swallowed the mockery.
