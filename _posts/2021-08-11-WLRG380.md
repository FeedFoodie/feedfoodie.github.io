[You went through a lot of trouble pretending not to know.]

*I never pretended not to know.*

[You didn't say anything directly, did you? If you just kept your mouth shut, that's the same as pretending.]

Lee Sungmin offered no reply to the Void Monarch's taunt. The feast was over. A large bonfire that had once roared with life had dwindled to a bed of flickering embers, and the noisy fairies had finally fallen asleep, huddled together in quiet heaps.

The Spear King remained curled up in a deep slumber. Near the Black Dragon Knight, who slept with a satisfied expression, Teresa breathed softly under a blanket. Scarlet, who had spent the night asking him blatant questions between drinks, had already returned to the Magic Tower. Not far from them all, Baek Sogo was also lying peacefully asleep.

Pushing the blanket from his lap, Lee Sungmin rose to his feet.

He knew. There was no way he could not have known. The questions had been blatant and so had their actions leading up to this point, yet even knowing, he had shown no reaction. Just as the Void Monarch said, he had pretended not to know. He felt he had no other choice. He had no right, he thought, and besides, why me?

"Osla."

Lee Sungmin walked to the lakeside and called for the Fairy Queen. Osla had not chattered or laughed much during the party, a departure from her usual self. She had only sipped a little alcohol before retreating to the lake. He understood why. Osla had known the vanished Spirit Queen for a very long time. Even if they had little interaction, a being of equal standing with whom she had shared thousands of years had been annihilated.

"I'm fine," Osla grumbled as she poked her head out from the lake's surface. The rims of her eyes were a little red. "It's just... well, yes. I feel empty. Hollow. That's all. She was gone too easily."

If only it had been an all-out, mud-slinging fight. Perhaps it would have been different if they had struggled and fought until one of them died, but the Elemental Queen's demise had been anticlimactic. She had Advented with power far inferior to her original strength, so the fight had not been difficult. In the end, the Spirit intervened and erased the queen's existence completely.

"That's what I'm afraid of." Fear, greater than sadness, crept into Osla's small voice. She let her wings droop and wrapped her arms around her shoulders. "I may not look it, but I've lived for a very long time. Since the beginning of this world. It's not that I'm clinging to life now. Still... it's just too easy. I'm afraid that my existence could be so easily erased without me even knowing. I'm so scared of vanishing without being able to do anything."

That fear was the same for everyone. If the Spirit intervened, they would be annihilated without a chance to resist. The fact that he could not guess why the Spirit had not done so already was even more unsettling. Was it not yet time for the harvest? What more was it planning?

*You'll die a dog's death if you're with him.* He recalled Frau's taunting words and found he could not refute them. Frau was selfish, but she was also correct. What meaning would saving the world hold if he himself died in the process?

"And I'm anxious." Osla emerged from the water to stand upon its surface. She looked up at the sky, lowering the hands that had been wrapped around her shoulders. "The Spirit erased Sarahenne, but why doesn't it erase me? Or you? The others? Is it because there's no need to?"

"There must be some reason." It would not be a meaningless act. There had to be a reason.

Osla glanced at Lee Sungmin's impassive face. "Aren't you afraid?" she asked.

"I am."

"Then why don't you give up?"

"If I give up," he said, "everything up to this point will become meaningless."

Could he ever have another day like this, drinking his fill while laughing and chatting with friends? A day where no one died, where everyone survived in a world where The End never came. That was impossible. It was too much to ask for. The idea that not a single person would die was absurd. If that was all it took, it could not be called The End. No matter what, there would be sacrifices.

The realization made his chest ache. It was an obvious truth, but Lee Sungmin still wished for no one to die, even while knowing it was impossible.

He approached Aine, who was collapsed near the lake. She was an easy prisoner to handle. He had given her nothing to eat or drink since bringing her to the forest, yet Aine remained alive and well, completely subdued and in a deep sleep under Osla's authority.

"What are you trying to do?" Osla asked as she drew near.

"Nothing." Lee Sungmin nudged Aine's collapsed body with his foot, turning her over. Her chest rose and fell with faint breaths. Osla watched with an anxious expression as he stared down at Aine.

"...Don't get any dangerous ideas."

"I have to consider the possibility."

"You know it too," she insisted. "Your body is at its limit. You can't get any stronger through predation."

"This case might be different from my previous predations."

"The risk is too great."

"I have no talent." There was no bitterness in his voice as he spoke the words now. It was simply an obvious fact. He had reached such a level and become so strong only because of a destiny that transcended talent, along with the help and teachings of others and various other conditions. "The biggest reason a talentless person like me could come this far was because I had a destiny that required me to possess at least this much power. That destiny vanished along with me when I annihilated the Slaughter Predator. Now that I'm no longer bound by destiny, growing along its path is impossible."

Of course, that did not mean there was no room for him to grow. As the Blood Demon had said, his body was incomplete. The level of his martial arts and his physical body were not in balance. Because room for growth had been forcibly given to him, Lee Sungmin could still become stronger.

However, the situation was different. When he followed the path of destiny, he was unconditionally forced to become stronger. He always faced trials that he could just barely overcome, and he grew to survive them. Not anymore. Having broken free from destiny, he could no longer receive that convenient Grace of Fate.

"I don't plan on trying it right away." Consuming Aine was too risky. One wrong move and his body, already at its limit, might collapse. It was dangerous to take such a gamble now.

*Not yet.*

Yes, not yet.

{sep}

Where was Jeniela now? She might already be marching toward the Fairy Forest. Originally, Lee Sungmin had intended to attack her during the day, not at night, but now that he knew the final ability of her Demonic Eye was to summon a full moon, such an attack had become meaningless.

Even if he could not decide the time, he could now decide the place. The Fairy Forest was Osla's domain. Jeniela would not give up on Aine, which meant she had no choice but to come to this forest. If they fought here, he could secure at least a slightly better chance of victory.

*It's not enough.*

He had to consider every possible means. Leaving open the possibility of consuming Aine was not enough. There was one thing Lee Sungmin had that was overwhelmingly superior to Jeniela. It was speed. No matter how fast Jeniela moved, she could not be faster than him while he had the Fairy Horse.

The next day, Lee Sungmin headed for Trabia with the cooperation of Lloyd, Scarlet, and Baek Sogo, who were all suffering from hangovers. His intention was to conduct a grand cleanup of the city. When Jeniela came to Mount Wudang, she had not brought the other vampires from Trabia. The only ones moving with her were Gemini, Chen, and Kun. That meant the other vampires were still there, ripe for the picking.

"Oh my."

The voices in her head were disappearing. Jeniela stood blankly and brought a finger to her temple. She felt the vampires connected to her vanishing one by one. They were not simply dying. If that were the case, the power of the dead should have returned to her. She raised her hands and covered her eyes, her vision connecting with another vampire in Trabia. Before long, that vampire's sight went black. That was enough.

*Sealing... yes. There was that method, but for you, wouldn't predation be easier and better than sealing?*

Surely it could not be that he no longer wanted to perform the act of predation. Did that mean there was a reason he could not? The voices continued to disappear. Jeniela lowered her hands and chuckled.

"What's wrong?" Gemini asked, tilting his head as he looked at the laughing vampire queen.

"Trabia is under attack."

"Is it the Phantom Spear?"

"Yes. Really, he's irritating me in so many ways. This makes me lose any desire to negotiate." As if she had any such intention in the first place, Gemini thought with a shrug.

"Are you not bothered?" Ju Won, who had been silent until now, opened his mouth. He had joined them a few days ago, leaving the deep southern forest where he had been holed up after Jeniela showed up unannounced. She had thought he might be with Brook, the Were-Bear who was once as powerful as Howon. When Jeniela met Ju Won, however, Brook was already in his stomach.

"How could I not be bothered when my children are being massacred?" Jeniela replied. "I'm very angry."

"You seem rather calm for someone who is."

"They were all fated to die anyway." Jeniela stopped listening to the disappearing voices. She felt regret, a sense of waste, and sadness, but it could not be helped. Even if a terrible rage rose within her at their deaths, traveling from the South all the way to Trabia was impossible. Trying to head north now would take several months.

"More importantly, are you okay?" she asked Ju Won. "After Trabia, it'll be Cheped. The Lycanthropes you rule are there, aren't they?"

"I suppose so," Ju Won nodded. "I'll have to get my revenge," he muttered, gazing toward the distant North.

In the past, when Howon was the leader of the Lycanthropes, Ju Won had challenged and defeated him, devouring him to become the new leader. At that, Brock, Howon's right-hand man and old friend, left the pack and departed for a distant place. Only Nero had remained by Ju Won's side to become his confidant. Ju Won trusted Nero. That was why he had left Cheped in Nero's care before leaving to find and kill Brock. Nero would not leave the city. Ju Won smiled bitterly at the thought.

"Doesn't this motivate you instead?" Jeniela said with a twirling smile. Almost no voices remained in her head. She spun around on the deck, feeling the salty sea breeze. Turning her palms to the sky, she slowly raised her arms. "He really makes me want to kill him."

KUUUUNG...!

Red magic power enveloped the ship. The Ghost Ship that had wandered the West Sea still remained even after Vollander's demise, and the Death Knights who had survived the battle of Germud were also on board. Jeniela had taken them all in. That was not all. While coming down to the South, she had brought aboard particularly powerful Yokai belonging to Predator. Through Arbes and Kim Jonghyun, she had even gathered the now masterless Liches.

*Aine.* In the center of the robed Liches, Preskan placed a hand on his chest and hardened his resolve. *I will save you, even if it costs me my life.*

The ship, enveloped in Jeniela's magic, rose above the waves. She slowly aimed her hands forward, gauging the direction to the Fairy Forest.

"Will it take about five days?"

In the past, it had taken Lee Sungmin a month to reach the Fairy Forest by ship, a normal amount of time for an ordinary vessel. This, however, was no ordinary ship. It was a Ghost Ship, unaffected by waves or wind, and now Jeniela's endless magic power was added to it. The Ghost Ship, now floating in the sky, shot across the sea.
