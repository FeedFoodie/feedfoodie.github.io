"I'm indebted to you."

Lloyd's voice was raspy from where he lay half-propped against the pillows of a large bed. His magic had recovered to a degree and his more serious injuries had regenerated, but the mental fatigue had not faded. A profound exhaustion clung to his features.

"It's alright."

"No, I mean it. I truly owe you. If you hadn't helped me… I would have died alone in that deep underground, and I never would have destroyed that heart either."

Lloyd offered a bitter smile. The words sent a pang through Lee Sungmin's chest, but he kept his expression neutral. He remembered what Kim Jonghyun had said. Other mages would not be able to see the heart he had acquired.

"I heard you were the only survivor," Lloyd said.

"Ah… yes. I was lucky."

Just as Kim Jonghyun had predicted, Lloyd had not noticed the Black Heart. He had not seen the twisted causality of Lee Sungmin's soul either. If even the Golden Magic Tower Master could not see through it, then only a Black Mage who had reached a remarkable level would ever be able to discern his secret.

"You really were lucky," Lloyd continued. "Preskan is… a madman. It's an incredible feat to have survived under his hand."

"That Lich… Preskan. What was he even doing in that dungeon?"

"Hm?"

Lloyd's eyes widened at the question. As the questioning gaze settled on him, Lee Sungmin quickly schooled his features, looking at Lloyd with a carefully crafted expression of fear mixed with admiration.

"As the Golden Magic Tower Master, aren't you a mage with skills near the pinnacle of the Mages Guild?"

"Ahem. Well, not exactly… but I'd be counted among the top five."

"For someone like you to be so seriously injured and collapse… I don't quite understand. What was Preskan doing in there?"

"…Well… it's not exactly a secret," Lloyd muttered, letting out a sigh. "The ultimate life."

It was the same answer Kim Jonghyun had given.

"For ten years, it's been like this. Preskan was researching the ultimate life, and in the process, he committed a taboo. He should have been killed upon his exile… but he escaped, so I was assigned the mission to hunt him down. After a ten-year chase, I finally found his dungeon."

Lloyd paused, sighing again.

"…In those ten years… Preskan's research had made considerable progress. Originally, there were two hearts. One of them transformed right before my eyes… into the shape of a young girl. She was terrifyingly strong."

"A girl… you say?"

"Her appearance was that of a girl of about ten or so. It was very… strange. She could dispel my magic without a single chant, and her limbs would change shape… but what I found truly horrifying was that the girl chewed and ate the other heart."

"The heart?"

"That's right. She said she was hungry… and ate it right in front of me."

The image of Zeon's corpse, a gaping hole blown through its chest, flashed through his mind. As Lloyd spoke, Lee Sungmin unconsciously pressed a hand to his own left breast, feeling the thrum of the Black Heart within. He was reminded once again that this was the heart of a monster.

"Thanks to her, I lost Preskan," Lloyd lamented. "If it weren't for that monster girl, I could have killed him. I might have even been able to track his Life Force Vessel. …Hoo! No, in this case, I should consider myself lucky to have escaped with my life."

Lloyd's gaze shifted to Lee Sungmin, and he bowed his head deeply.

"Thank you for your help. As promised, I want to reward you. Is there anything you particularly want? I can't promise to grant absolutely everything, unconditionally. However, I will do my best to grant what you desire."

A promise from the Golden Magic Tower Master was a blank check, and Lee Sungmin could not help but consider the possibilities, yet… there was nothing he truly wanted. He thought about asking for a magic book, but he had not even properly mastered the 무Void Path and had no room to learn magic as well. If he were to receive an artifact, he would not know what to get, and he did not need any particular artifact right now.

"…Could I ask for it later?"

"Planning on holding a debt over my head?" Lloyd asked with a chuckle.

"Ah… that's not what I meant. It's just that I don't have anything to ask for right now."

"Well, I suppose that's possible. I understand. I'm usually at the Golden Magic Tower, so if you think of something you want, come find me anytime."

"Weren't you chasing Preskan?"

"A mage doesn't exactly run around on his own two feet, does he?" Lloyd replied with a dry laugh.

After finishing his conversation with Lloyd, Lee Sungmin returned to the Mercenary Guild and explained what had happened in the dungeon to Jo Young. There was little choice for Jo Young and the other mercenaries but to accept his story. It was, after all, a fact that every other mercenary who entered had been annihilated.

The massacre had dealt a great loss to the Behenger Mercenary Guild. The Guildmaster, Bern, was dead. The SS-Rank Mercenary, Zeon, was also dead. Countless S-Rank and A-Rank Mercenaries had perished alongside them. With the deaths of Zeon and the other S-Rank Mercenaries, the Corona Mercenary Group was, for all intents and purposes, disbanded.

It was not until dawn that Lee Sungmin could finally leave the guild. A wave of fatigue washed over him as he returned home, and he let out a heavy sigh.

*I should leave Behenger soon.*

He returned to his room and slumped down. His first goal was to find the God of Time, Denir, in Dreamoor.

However, before that, he took out the subspace pockets he had collected. The pockets of the other mercenaries contained only money and potions. A flicker of disappointment passed through him, but it vanished the moment he pulled the Great Cycle Pill and the Minor Cycle Pill from Zeon's pocket.

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard, his hand reaching for the Minor Cycle Pill. He decided to try it first. Although he had experience with miracle herbs, he knew its efficacy was incomparable to the Holy Spirit Pill he had taken in Zenavis.

With a somewhat reverent heart, he opened the wooden box. He lifted the Minor Cycle Pill out with both hands.

The pill, placed in his mouth, melted and slid down his throat before he could even chew it. GULP, he swallowed. Lee Sungmin immediately moved to operate the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

"…Huh?"

However, before he could even begin, he stopped. He slowly opened his eyes, which had been closed in concentration, and looked down at his body. There was nothing. He remembered the Holy Spirit Pill, how he had known it was a miracle herb the instant it touched his tongue. He had struggled to circulate the massive inner arts that flooded his system, to guide it through his blood vessels and gather it in his qi center.

However, not this time.

"…What?"

Lee Sungmin sat cross-legged again and attempted the Energy Circulation Exercise. Following the incantations of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, he lightly prodded the fighting spirit within him.

He was at a loss for words as he looked down at his own body. Inside his qi center… he could feel a massive amount of inner arts. As he focused his consciousness, he could feel the detailed changes his body had undergone. The size of his fighting spirit had grown enormously, and the amount of inner arts had multiplied several times over.

He recognized the identity of that energy. It was the inner arts of the Minor Cycle Pill. The power of the pill he had taken had flowed directly into his qi center and accumulated there without any refinement process. That was an impossible feat. All miracle herbs required refinement. It was common knowledge that one was lucky to absorb even half of the inner arts a miracle herb possessed.

*The inner arts accumulated without even performing qi circulation. Why…?*

The Black Heart. The realization struck him, and he nodded to himself. This had to be one of its abilities, to absorb the inner arts of miracle herbs directly. He did not know the details, but it was the only explanation for what he was feeling.

*Come to think of it… Wiji Hoyeon said it. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art she learned doesn't go through the refinement process essential to all inner arts techniques.*

She had said it was a special ability of her art and that one needed a special constitution to learn it. Could eating the Black Heart have changed his constitution to be like that? Just in case, he tried operating the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

He circulated his energy for a few hours, but he did not feel much of a change in refining natural energy into inner arts. It seemed that was a specialty of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, not a matter of constitution.

It was still too early to be certain. Lee Sungmin took out the Great Cycle Pill. To think he would be taking both pills together. He opened his mouth and swallowed it. Though slightly larger than the Minor Cycle Pill, it also melted the moment it entered his mouth and slid down his throat.

This time, he was consciously aware and noticed the change. The dense inner arts passed down his throat, slid through his blood vessels, and reached his qi center directly, accumulating there. His body trembled at the feeling of fullness that washed over him.

He had absorbed the full inner arts of both the Minor and Great Cycle Pills directly into his qi center. The accumulated energy was several times greater than everything he had built until now. In that moment, one of his most fatal weaknesses had been overcome.

It had only been a little over four years since he had started martial arts. Though he had received Wiji Hoyeon's inner arts and taken the Holy Spirit Pill, Lee Sungmin severely lacked the depth of inner arts for the realm he had reached. He had surpassed the wall of the peak realm in a short time while training on Mount Mushii, and compared to other martial artists who had trained for over a decade to reach that point, his own power was bound to be insufficient.

However, that weakness had now completely disappeared. In fact, he now possessed enough inner arts to overwhelm martial artists of a similar realm, or even those a few steps ahead.

By the time Lee Sungmin opened his eyes again, the long process of qi circulation complete, dawn had passed into morning. He had not slept a wink, but he felt as refreshed as if he had slept soundly.

Now that he had absorbed the pills, a thought of visiting Kim Jonghyun again to ask about the Magic Armor's curse crossed his mind, but he pushed it aside for the moment. Lee Sungmin took out the Hundred Steps Divine Fist, the Dharma Meditation Art, and the Arhat Movement Arts. Ignoring the latter two, he opened the Hundred Steps Divine Fist and began to read.

He knew projecting qi was an incredibly difficult skill, one that required a high level of enlightenment and consumed vast amounts of inner arts. The Shaolin's Hundred Steps Divine Fist, an art designed to strike from a hundred paces away, was built entirely on that principle. Since he could not yet project his qi, the martial art itself, focused entirely on that one skill, would be the perfect guide. By learning it, he could finally grasp the knack.

The Arhat Movement Arts and Dharma Meditation Art were also excellent martial arts, but Lee Sungmin had no intention of learning any other than the Hundred Steps Divine Fist. Once he had committed it to memory, Lee Sungmin called for Nebul.

"Did you call?"

Nebul popped out of the shadows, bowing his head deeply. Lee Sungmin handed the martial arts manuals he was holding to the creature and spoke.
