"Both, actually?"

Tang Ahee admitted with a dry cough. "Honestly, I'm a greedy woman. A master of the Supreme Peak with no affiliations? That makes me a little suspicious. I just don't believe someone like you could pop out of nowhere."

"So?"

"But suspicion is one thing and greed is another, isn't it?" she continued, her honesty shameless. "So I was planning to stake my claim on you before the Moyong Clan or anyone else could."

Her frankness drew a hearty laugh from Lee Sungmin.

"So don't push me away just because you don't like the idea," Tang Ahee said with a smile. "You don't know me very well, do you? Though I'm sure my title is quite famous."

*I know you're a bedwetter,* Lee Sungmin thought, shaking his head.

"Even so, I don't feel like having a drink or tea right now."

"What a shame," she purred, giving him a look full of temptation. "It would be fun to hang out together."

While Lee Sungmin brushed it off nonchalantly, Moyong Chan, who was standing beside him, turned bright red. He quickly averted his gaze, trying his best to ignore her form-fitting clothes and the subtle movements that emphasized her curves.

"Perhaps there will be a chance someday."

[She's desperately asking to be devoured. Aren't you going to eat?]

*I'm not.*

[Why? Are you worried about the Little Heavenly Demon? Hehe, didn't the Little Heavenly Demon say it herself? That she wouldn't mind if you held another woman.]

*Just because Wiji Hoyeon said that, wouldn't it be ridiculous for me to just accept everything offered to me?*

[You brat. You take everything else that's offered to you, but you're being a picky eater about the weirdest things.]

*What have I taken?*

[You took martial arts, you took Demonic Energy, you took a miraculous encounter, you little shit. You took it all so damn well.]

The heavy truth of it left Lee Sungmin with no rebuttal. He ignored the Void Monarch's words and gestured with his eyes toward the inn. "The night air is chilly. I'll be heading in now."

"You're not impotent, are you?" she blurted out.

"What are you saying all of a sudden?"

"Nothing, it's just... I was curious," Tang Ahee grumbled, her gaze flicking over to Moyong Chan.

Moyong Chan, who had been stealing glances at her, immediately straightened his face and pretended nothing was wrong, which only made Tang Ahee burst out laughing.

"What a shame," she teased. "If Young Master Moyong were just a bit older, this big sister would've played with you well..."

"Wh-what do you mean by that...!" he stammered.

"You're not thinking of anything strange, are you? Something lewd, perhaps."

"I wasn't...!"

"Hmph. Well, imagination is free."

Tang Ahee stuck her tongue out pointedly before spinning around and leaving without any lingering attachment. Lee Sungmin watched her disappear down the alley, then returned to his room.

[Let's drink.]

The Void Monarch pestered him, but Lee Sungmin ignored him. He truly wasn't in the mood. Instead, his thoughts turned to Moyong Daeun, picturing the man dressed in his black martial arts uniform.

What must a father who has lost his daughter feel? Lee Sungmin found he couldn't easily imagine it.

*He must be resentful.*

He recalled Moyong Chan's vow to kill the Phantom Spear on sight. The words themselves held no threat for him, but the resentment behind them was palpable. A bitter smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips. If only he could reveal that he wasn't the one who did it, but there was no proof. If he announced himself as the Phantom Spear right here and claimed innocence in Moyong Seojin's death, neither Moyong Daeun nor his son would ever believe him.

After all, the Murim Alliance itself had made the announcement. Even if he, the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, claimed it was unfair, the Moyong Clan's ties to the Murim Alliance meant they would never believe him.

The next day, Lee Sungmin left the inn to find Scarlet and ask about the subjugation schedule. A sufficient force had already gathered, but the mercenaries had yet to arrive.

Scarlet's answer was just as he had expected. "We'll leave as soon as the mercenaries arrive."

"The Mages Guild and the Mercenary Guild are quite close, so I tried asking the Mercenary King for help, but he refused," Scarlet explained with a regretful expression.

At the mention of the Mercenary King, Blade Supreme, one of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens, Lee Sungmin's jaw tightened slightly.

*I see why he isn't coming.*

This was the North, the territory ruled by the Vampire Queen, Jeniella of Blood Charm. Given the long-standing hostility between Heaven Beyond Heaven and Predator, not even the Mercenary King would dare set foot here. Perhaps they had also noticed the connection between Jeniella and Kim Jonghyun. Even the Sword Saint was a child compared to Jeniella, so Blade Supreme, who was weaker still, would never throw himself into a situation where he might become her nemesis.

*Blade Supreme isn't coming here. Should I be disappointed?*

Lee Sungmin had planned to watch for an opportunity to kill the Mercenary King if he had come in person. He was already on bad terms with Heaven Beyond Heaven, so it only made sense to strike first when the chance arose.

The year of training under the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had turned Lee Sungmin into a monster, one who now possessed the skill to overwhelm the version of the Sword Saint he had fought in the Elf Forest a year ago. His master had said it himself. Setting aside the Black Dragon Knight, whose skills were unknown, no one in Heaven Beyond Heaven could kill him, save for the Spear King, the Moon Queen, or the Martial God.

Knowing this, Lee Sungmin felt it was a shame Blade Supreme wasn't coming. It was, however, probably fortunate for the man himself. If he had come, he would not have returned alive.

"If the Mercenary King isn't coming, then who is?"

"Oh, there are tons of mercenary groups who want to connect with the Mages Guild. We got several love calls from large groups when we put out the request. We chose the one closest to here... They're called Mad Dog. Know them?"

"I do."

Having lived as a mercenary in his past life, Lee Sungmin knew a great deal about such groups, and he had certainly heard of Mad Dog. They operated mainly in the North, a mixed group of humans and various mutants, including beastfolk. Their standing was quite high, with many skilled members led by an SSS-Rank beastfolk master at the Supreme Peak, but...

"I understand they have a rather bad reputation," Lee Sungmin noted.

"The reputation of the guy we have to catch is even worse," Scarlet said, her expression unchanging. "I've heard they're a nasty bunch, but our opponent is a madman who massacred thousands. I don't think it's a bad thing to use a son of a bitch to catch a son of a bitch."

Lee Sungmin found himself agreeing with her to some extent. They might have a bad reputation, but it was true that the Mad Dog Mercenaries were highly skilled.

"To be honest, the mercenaries don't have a major role in this subjugation," Scarlet continued. "If I had to give them one... I guess it would be dying in our place?"

"I suppose so," Lee Sungmin agreed with a nod.

Still, no matter how skilled, a mercenary group was a class below the forces of the murim's Noble Clans or the Murim Alliance, whose members trained in martial arts from childhood. While there were certainly skilled individuals among them, even a master of the peak realm would be treated as a high-ranking S-Rank or better in the mercenary world.

The Mad Dog Mercenaries would know this. They would understand their role was to buy time for the Mage Army to cast their spells, to stand at the vanguard and die in place of their employers. They were cannon fodder, but the prospect of a connection with the Mages Guild was apparently attractive enough for them to apply anyway.

Over the next two days, at Moyong Chan's request, Lee Sungmin had dinner with Moyong Daeun each evening. Perhaps his promise to one day visit the Moyong Clan had been well received, because the older man no longer pressed him for anything excessive. They only shared light conversation over their meals, as if Moyong Daeun truly just wanted a dinner companion. Of course, that likely wasn't all there was to it. The man was probably trying to build a friendly relationship by showing a favorable attitude.

Tang Ahee was just as open in her intentions. Every day, as Lee Sungmin finished his meal and headed back to his inn, she would appear from an alley to ask him for a drink or tea. Her attire changed daily, each outfit a little more revealing and excessive than the last. While the sight was enough to make Moyong Chan's face turn red enough to explode, Lee Sungmin remained completely unmoved by her temptations.

[You impotent bastard.]

*Why don't you call it having principles?*

[There's nothing cheaper than a man's principles.]

The Void Monarch's grumbling increased with each passing day. He repeatedly asked Lee Sungmin to drink the liquor from the gourd, but Lee Sungmin always refused, claiming he wasn't in the mood.

[You son of a bitch.]

In truth, Lee Sungmin was curious about the liquor's taste, but he deliberately refused for the simple fun of teasing the Void Monarch.

The Mad Dog Mercenaries finally arrived in Hedor. Lee Sungmin went near the city gate to watch as the hundred-strong company passed through. He had no desire to greet them, but he wanted to see the infamous mercenaries for himself.

The leader, who went by the name Doberman, looked the part. He was a tall, slender black man with sharp, fierce eyes. An SSS-Rank Mercenary at the level of the Supreme Peak, but in Lee Sungmin's eyes, he was merely so-so. Behind him followed other mercenaries who had also taken dog breeds as their names, and the choices didn't seem random. The one called Bulldog truly resembled one, as did the one named Husky.

[It's a real dog show.]

Lee Sungmin silently agreed.

Doberman, Bulldog, and Husky headed straight for the inn the Mages Guild was using as a base. Upon news of their arrival, representatives from the Murim Alliance, the Moyong Clan, and the Church also gathered there. It was time to begin the serious discussions for subjugating Kim Jonghyun. Scarlet invited him to join, but Lee Sungmin shook his head, seeing no need for his presence.

The meeting lasted until evening. Once Lee Sungmin confirmed people were leaving the inn, he went to find Scarlet.

"What was decided?"

"In three days," Scarlet answered, her face a mask of annoyance. "The Mad Dog guys requested time to recover from their journey. It's not like Kim Jonghyun has run away yet... though I kind of wish he would," she grumbled. "Anyway, we're heading to the forest where he's hiding in three days."

"Kim Jonghyun isn't a fool," Lee Sungmin pointed out. "He must have made preparations to greet us."

"And we've made our own preparations to catch and kill him," she retorted. "The Mages Guild and the Mage Army haven't just been sitting idly in this city."

Despite her words, Scarlet didn't seem very motivated.

"...It won't be dangerous," she murmured, more to herself than to him. "It's fine. I'm not alone, after all. Still... it's a strange feeling. I have a bad feeling about this."

"Nothing will happen," Lee Sungmin said, his voice firm as he looked at the anxious mage. "I came here to make sure of it, Scarlet. I can't speak for the others, but you will be safe."

"Hearing you say that somehow makes me even more anxious," Scarlet replied, letting out a low laugh as she twirled a strand of her red hair. "...Still... I'll trust you, but listen. It won't happen, but if it's a really dangerous situation... and you end up in danger because of me..."

"Let's not talk about that," Lee Sungmin cut her off. "They say words can become reality, you know."

At that, Scarlet let out a small, final laugh.
