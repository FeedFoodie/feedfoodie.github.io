The confusion was brief, for Abel knew exactly why Kim Jonghyun had revived completely unscathed. He understood precisely why his own magic had failed to kill him.

Quasi-Demon King. The ritual in the North had not been a complete success. Had it been, Kim Jonghyun would have become a full Demon Lord and been banished from this world entirely. The ritual's failure, however, had allowed him to remain while still possessing a Demon Lord's power.

It was an incomplete power, to be sure. Compared to the true Demon Lords who reigned in the Demon Realm, it would be sorely lacking. Kim Jonghyun's abilities as a mage were also inferior to Abel's. Though their paths had diverged, Abel had dedicated hundreds of years to becoming an archmage with the sole purpose of targeting Cain, making him superior to Kim Jonghyun in the field of mage combat.

Still,, all his skill was meaningless if Kim Jonghyun was an immortal being who simply could not die.

*His power is less than a true Demon Lord's, but damn it... did he gain immortality?*

Abel's face twisted. His hands blurred through seals as his lips moved.

"......"

The ground shattered, unleashing plant stems that coiled toward Kim Jonghyun's body. In response, Kim Jonghyun merely smiled and waved a hand.

WHOOSH!

A gray light washed out from him, turning the grasping vines to dust. It was Arbes's Soul-Annihilation magic. With that, Kim Jonghyun began to unleash the Grimoire's magic in earnest. This was a tome for a Demon Lord, containing magic far beyond the standard. Even as a Quasi-Demon King, Kim Jonghyun couldn't unleash its full potential.

"I forbid it."

Kim Jonghyun opened his mouth. A Spoken Command, magic that only Dragons were thought to wield in Eria. It stood to reason that a Demon Lord could use something a mere Dragon could, even if imperfectly. While Abel could intervene just before a spell formula was complete and cast a dispel, the Spoken Command from the Grimoire was different. *I forbid it.* The shield Abel had deployed against the incoming Soul-Annihilation magic simply vanished, his own magical abilities momentarily nullified by the command.

"Kuh?!"

Abel's body, held aloft by levitation magic, staggered violently and then plummeted toward the ground. Arbes's Soul-Annihilation magic swept toward him. The Grimoire's Spoken Command could only forbid magic for a few seconds at most, but a few seconds were enough. An Abel stripped of his magic was no more than an ordinary human, easy to kill.

"......"

Frau's shout tore through the air. Enslaved by her Ghost Soul Art, the zombies roared, kicking off the ground and leaping to form a grotesque shield of flesh. They intercepted the Soul-Annihilation magic meant for Abel, vanishing without a trace. Watching them disappear, Kim Jonghyun clicked his tongue. Right, Abel wasn't alone. He wouldn't have brought a Grand Sorcerer all this way just to deal with some zombies.

*Sorcery and magic are different. Even the Grimoire's prohibition cannot forbid sorcery.*

Had he brought Frau for that reason? It was half right, half wrong. Abel didn't know what kind of magic the Grimoire contained. He had brought Frau because she was the closest person he could ask for help, and he believed it would be possible to pressure Kim Jonghyun with sorcery instead of magic.

"Thanks."

Just before hitting the ground, Abel cast his levitation magic again. A chill ran down his spine. He had never imagined the Grimoire would have the ability to forbid magic. What should he do?

His brain spun rapidly. It wasn't impossible to counter. Gries's barrier should be able to resist the Grimoire's prohibition, but it wasn't an efficient solution. Kim Jonghyun, with the power of a Demon Lord, suffered little penalty for using the Grimoire's magic. Abel, on the other hand, would consume his own lifespan just by using Gries's barrier. It wasn't that he was worried about his lifespan now. If he had been, he wouldn't have come this far, but Abel's lifespan was not infinite. He had already spent much of it using Gries' magic and trying to learn about The End, leaving him with little time. If he wasted it meaninglessly here, seizing Kim Jonghyun's magic would become impossible.

*Can Frau handle Kim Jonghyun?*

Frau was an excellent sorcerer, but she was bound to struggle against a Quasi-Demon King with immortality who wielded the magic of both the Grimoire and Arbes's Dark Magic. Abel's thoughts turned to Lee Sungmin. Was his fight with Vollander not over yet?

*How much longer?*

Then, a new presence approached. Abel, flying back into the sky, sensed it first. Kim Jonghyun, who was about to unleash his Soul-Annihilation magic again, also froze. He hadn't noticed it while focused on Abel.

*Did Vollander perish?*

Had one of the Black Stars of the Predators fallen? Kim Jonghyun squinted, his gaze shifting to the space behind Abel.

Lee Sungmin was walking toward them, his spear slung over his shoulder. He looked ahead with tired eyes, taking in the scene. The halted legion of zombies, Kim Jonghyun with his black robe fluttering above them, Frau and Aladur standing on the ground, and Abel, confronting the Demon Lord.

[It would have been nice if things over here had been sorted out while you were dealing with Vollander.]

He had honestly hoped for that. Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly and quickened his pace. Kim Jonghyun stared blankly at his approach, knowing this was no longer a time to be complacent. He had neither Vollander nor the Death Knight Legion now. There were zombies, but he knew well that those things couldn't threaten his enemies.

"Where did Vollander go?"

"To the place the dead return to," Lee Sungmin answered in a blunt voice.

My goodness. Even in a situation where he couldn't afford to be complacent, Kim Jonghyun let out a laugh. From the beginning, his own success or failure wasn't important to him. He simply did what he wanted to do, what he could do, because he knew he enjoyed it. Of course, his choice of 'what he could do' was limited to things the version of himself from Lee Sungmin's past life had not done. That was why he was feeling a certain satisfaction with the current situation.

"The offer I made you is still valid."

"I've got no intention of accepting it."

Kim Jonghyun smiled at that answer too. Well, if he had intended to accept, he wouldn't have killed Vollander.

"You intend to stop me, don't you?"

As Kim Jonghyun spoke, Abel's voice echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind.

[He has immortality, but it doesn't seem to be complete. He himself isn't a complete Demon Lord, after all. It's an imperfect immortality.]

Abel was certain of it. If Kim Jonghyun had truly obtained perfect immortality, he would be an absurdly unreasonable existence. Even the Vampire Queen didn't possess such a thing.

[There's a limit to his immortality. It's difficult for me to break through that limit. If we drag this into a magic battle, I'm definitely superior to him, but the restrictions on me using Gries' magic are too clear compared to him using the Grimoire's magic.]

What Kim Jonghyun could forbid was limited to magic. Lee Sungmin, who used martial arts, was free from that prohibition.

[I'll do what I can. I'd like to ask you to keep Kim Jonghyun from interfering. Can you do it?]

"......"

Lee Sungmin's silence was an answer for them both. Kim Jonghyun smiled faintly. Lee Sungmin's choice didn't make him angry. He enjoyed even this kind of situation. However, that didn't mean he thought he had enjoyed himself enough and would now give up. *I can do it, so shall I try?* He had started this with that mindset, and he still wasn't convinced that he would fail. Therefore, Kim Jonghyun would do what he could, what he wanted to do.

FWOOSH.

Even as Kim Jonghyun's fingertips pointed toward Abel, the archmage was already reacting. He dodged the blast of magic with a Blink and leaped over Kim Jonghyun. Kim Jonghyun moved his hand to follow, but in that same instant, Lee Sungmin's pace shattered. As he was walking slowly, purple electricity crackled around him, and his figure vanished.

Kim Jonghyun had never experienced Lee Sungmin's top speed. He had been dealing with Abel and hadn't seen the fight with Vollander. Thanks to that, he was once again flustered. As Abel leaped over him, Kim Jonghyun targeted the archmage again.

Almost simultaneously, Lee Sungmin's spear tore through Kim Jonghyun's stomach.

Kim Jonghyun choked, gasping for breath. He spat out the blood that filled his throat, ignoring the terrible pain of being impaled.

KRRRRR!

The spear piercing his body began to rotate. Kim Jonghyun raised both hands, bringing them to the spear spinning inside him.

KIIIIING!

The magic power he emitted stopped the spear's rotation. He then abandoned all the flesh and organs around the wound and used Blink to escape from Lee Sungmin's spear.

[Indeed,] the Void Monarch muttered. No matter how much he had reigned as the strongest monster hundreds of years ago, he had never fought a Demon Lord. The same was true for Lee Sungmin. Still, he roughly understood what 'immortality' was. [It's not that different from you.]

Kim Jonghyun's wound regenerated. Frau cut through the zombie legion and ran after Abel. Kim Jonghyun watched the retreating pair and chuckled.

"What are you thinking? Trying to interfere with my magic? Well, if you use Gries, it would be possible to ruin the magic. In the end, did you choose to accept The End?"

*He doesn't know.*

He didn't know that Abel's magic would connect the dimension to a place other than the Great Demon World.

"Go ahead and try. That would be interesting too. You won't be able to kill me, and this world will face The End. We'll all just die together in the end."

"It's not like you did this for the sake of the world."

"Of course. I did this because I enjoy it. Ah, and I haven't given up. I'll keep going as long as I can." Kim Jonghyun caressed his stomach where the spear had pierced him, the black robe covering his body. "I'll kill or subdue you here. Then I'll go to Abel and kill him. I have to admit he's a better mage than me, but that doesn't mean he can kill me. We can have our chat again later, step by step. That is, if I only subdue you instead of killing you."

Kim Jonghyun spread his arms, the black robe billowing out behind him. The Grimoire, wreathed in black light, floated before him as he extended his staff toward Lee Sungmin.

"Did you know? I was favorable toward you, and I helped you several times. Who was it that warned you about the Black Mage? Who helped you when you were chasing the Sword Phantom? It was me. Is that all? I was in Urmuri. I restrained you with a barrier and even ignored you when you were defenseless after taking the Dragon Heart. Because I didn't want to kill you. When you came to the North to subjugate me, I even intentionally protected the Crimson Archmage, with whom you have a connection."

Kim Jonghyun chattered on with a joyful expression.

"Well, I suppose I brought this on myself. I did all those things for you, but I never made it obvious. If I had, would our relationship be a little different now?"

"I never asked you to do any of those things."

"Hahaha! Now that you mention it, that's true. They were all things I did on my own whim. Well, I don't intend to blame you. Now, you came here to stop me, didn't you? After getting past the zombies and Death Knights, and even killing Vollander."

And now.

"You've come to fight a Demon Lord."

Kim Jonghyun's smile deepened. His opponent was a martial artist who specialized in combat, one who even Vollander couldn't handle. The interception magic he had prepared here had all been dispelled by Abel. In this situation, for a mage like Kim Jonghyun to fight Lee Sungmin was suicide. No matter how fast his chanting was, it wouldn't be faster than Lee Sungmin.

The light emanating from the Grimoire was unusual, vibrating around Kim Jonghyun in a way that resembled the death qi of Vollander far more than a mage's magic power. The Grimoire contained magic for a Demon Lord. To be precise, it was magic used by a non-Demon Lord to become one, closer to a textbook for ascension. It used Reversal Magic to turn a human into a Demon Lord. It summoned a gate to allow travel between the Demon Realm and this world, and by connecting the dimensions, it made it possible to turn this world into a Demon Lord's colony. The Grimoire was presenting the duties of an 'ideal' Demon Lord in the form of magic.

However, that wasn't all. Not all Demon Lords were mages.

CRUNCH.

A sickening sound of twisting bones echoed from within Kim Jonghyun's frame. A large-scale magic was reshaping him from the inside out, consuming most of his power in the process. It wasn't a flashy spell, but it was necessary for him in this situation, and it was a magic he had no intention of using against Abel. The reason was simple. Abel was a mage.

Lee Sungmin was not. It might be different if he were thoroughly prepared, but for an unprepared mage to face a martial artist skilled in combat, one who was near the pinnacle of his craft, was a death sentence.

He knew something was happening. He didn't know much about magic, so he didn't approach rashly, but his gut screamed that he couldn't let it continue. His body didn't move as he wanted due to the aftermath of his fight with Vollander, but Lee Sungmin ran.

Kim Jonghyun's eyes were red. Their gazes met, and the tip of the spear met Kim Jonghyun's hand.

"Block," Kim Jonghyun muttered.

A Spoken Command. The defensive magic was complete.

CLAAANG!

Lee Sungmin's spear shattered the defense, a testament to the Spoken Command's incompleteness. Its degree of completion was lower than a magic cast with careful chanting, and Kim Jonghyun knew that well.

PWAK!

The defense shattered, and Kim Jonghyun's hand was pierced by the spear and vanished. He smiled and retreated, the vanished arm regenerating in an instant. Regenerating a severed limb like that was impossible without immortality. His red eyes gleaming, Kim Jonghyun straightened his bent body.

By that point, his 'transformation' was complete. It hadn't taken very long in the first place.

To make a Demon Lord more like a Demon Lord.

This was the magic from the Grimoire that Kim Jonghyun had cast. His incomplete body was temporarily strengthened. It was a spell that would have been far more beneficial if he had the body of a complete Demon Lord, but for the current Kim Jonghyun, this was the limit. Even so, his physical toughness was lacking compared to real Demon Lords.

What was lacking could be supplemented with magic. This spell didn't just strengthen the body.

*I can see.*

He could see Lee Sungmin lunging forward. He could see how the spear was moving, where it was aiming. It was all clearly visible to his enhanced eyes. His body and senses had been heightened together, and that wasn't all.

"Block."

The Spoken Command activated once more. The previous one couldn't stop Lee Sungmin's spear, but this time was different.

KAGAGAGAK!

The spot Lee Sungmin had been aiming for was precisely blocked by the command. Even the Spoken Command had been strengthened. Kim Jonghyun bared his teeth and smiled. Though temporary, the power of a Demon Lord was alluring.

*Why is he smiling?* Lee Sungmin thought, watching him. He pulled back the spear he had extended.

Deadly Flash Lightning.

The moment the light burst, the spear pierced Kim Jonghyun's defense. Lee Sungmin pushed his body forward with the spear, aiming for Kim Jonghyun's body.

"Harde..."

Lee Sungmin's spear was faster than the word Kim Jonghyun could utter.

KWAANG!

The half-baked Spoken Command hadn't strengthened Kim Jonghyun's body enough. Still, it hadn't been completely pierced. Lee Sungmin roughly figured out how the magic worked. It had to be spoken. Casting magic with just words was an amazing feat, but against his speed, it wasn't a significant advantage. Lee Sungmin's spear was faster than chanting, faster than forming hand seals.

*Harden.*

It was faster than uttering that short phrase. No matter how fast one spoke, there was a limit. Lee Sungmin's motion of 'thrusting the spear' was faster than speaking a word.

[He's a bad matchup,] the Void Monarch muttered. It was just as he said. The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was a divine art worthy of being called the pinnacle among martial arts that focused on 'speed'. Even though Lee Sungmin hadn't mastered it as perfectly as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he had inherited the Chairman's power and martial theory, allowing him to handle its Extreme Speed to some extent. Even if it wasn't perfect, it was faster than casting magic or chanting a Spoken Command. It might have been different against another martial arts master, but Kim Jonghyun was fighting the sole successor of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It was a terrible matchup.

*Faster than I thought...!*

Kim Jonghyun gritted his teeth. Lee Sungmin's speed was beyond what he had imagined. Today was a day full of surprises. He was surprised that Abel was Envirus, Cain's brother, surprised that he specialized in combat magic, and surprised that he could cast dispel so quickly, but more surprising than all of that was how much faster Lee Sungmin was than he had expected.

*Of course, he wasn't using his full power back then.*

He recalled the conversation they had a few days ago when he had invited him over. Lee Sungmin's spear had been fast and strong then, but not like this. Even if he had been holding back, the difference was too great.

However, Kim Jonghyun was wrong. At that time, Lee Sungmin had genuinely thrust his spear with the intent to kill him. In other words, Kim Jonghyun had already seen Lee Sungmin's full power. However, the Lee Sungmin of then and the Lee Sungmin of now were different. Even though only two days had passed, a change could certainly occur in such a short time. No, it wasn't even two days. The reason Lee Sungmin's spear could advance further was thanks to his fight with Vollander. If he had met Kim Jonghyun right away without fighting Vollander, he wouldn't have been able to surpass his Spoken Command with speed. The potential for growth that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had given him had certainly made him grow.

Despite experiencing it with his own body, Kim Jonghyun was still smiling. He was surprised, but it wasn't fatal. *To make a Demon Lord more like a Demon Lord.* The full meaning of this magic had not yet been demonstrated.

Toward the smiling Kim Jonghyun, Lee Sungmin's spear shone once more. Another Deathly Flash. Was he planning to toy with him using speed? The Spoken Command wouldn't work. In that case, he had no choice but to use another method.

Kim Jonghyun's wide-open hand came forward.

CLAAANG!

The moment they met, the spear couldn't pierce his hand. Instead, the repulsive force twisted Lee Sungmin's forearm. Although it wasn't pierced, Kim Jonghyun's hand was twisted just the same. The impact had been split and returned to both of them.

Lee Sungmin was startled that his arm had been twisted, but he didn't waver for long. He ignored his twisted right arm and grabbed the spear with his left hand. He pulled it straight toward himself and simultaneously unleashed Gale Thunder.

Kim Jonghyun couldn't read that incredible speed. Instead, he felt the vibration of space. The body of a Demon Lord was more specialized for combat than any other body in this world, and Kim Jonghyun himself knew that best. Moreover, even at this very moment, the Demon Lord's body was rapidly adapting to this fight. A Demon Lord, as the ruler of the Demon Realm, is stronger than any Demon Race. That's possible because of the endless potential for growth a Demon Lord possesses.

*I don't understand how Caladra, who had a body like this, could have perished.*

Kim Jonghyun felt a small doubt as he recalled the Demon Lord he had once contracted with. The immortality of a transcendent being and the massive growth correction given to the body, allowing it to grow endlessly during battle. Besides that, he must have possessed powerful authority as a complete Demon Lord. What on earth could have happened for such a being to perish?

He set that question aside for a moment. That wasn't what was important right now. He knew where the spear he felt behind his back would strike. As he was 'now', it was impossible to properly respond to Lee Sungmin's spear.

Still, if it's just this much.

*It's not like I can't take the hit.*

{sep}

Could Lee Sungmin defeat Kim Jonghyun?

Abel offered no prediction. He didn't know Lee Sungmin's full power. If the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had come without dying, he wouldn't even be having this worry. The strength of the Chairman that Abel had glimpsed was something that couldn't be considered human. Even if Kim Jonghyun had attained the status of a Quasi-Demon King and acquired immortality, he would have been no match for him.

However, that Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was dead.

"Can he kill Kim Jonghyun?"

"He doesn't need to be killed right away," Abel replied, glancing at Frau following behind him. He had called Frau thinking she could counter Kim Jonghyun's Undead and his magic, but she was necessary now for a different reason. "First, we need to seize the magic he's casting."

Lee Sungmin's role was to hold Kim Jonghyun down until then.

*After the magic succeeds... after it succeeds? What do we do about Kim Jonghyun?*

Not all problems would disappear. Even if this world escaped The End by connecting to the Elemental Realm, Kim Jonghyun would remain. Imperfect immortality. Killing him wouldn't be completely impossible, but it would by no means be easy.

No, don't think about it.

Abel quickened his pace. The top priority was to free this world from the fate of The End.

The mansion where the lord of Germud once lived no longer had any survivors. He couldn't care less about the death of a noble. What Abel was looking at was a terribly withered garden that must have once been magnificent, and floating in its center was a black sphere.

"This feels awful," Frau muttered. Her eyes saw things Abel could not. She had looked at Germud and said the souls were 'stagnant'. This was the very place where they had pooled. Tens of thousands of souls were entangled around that sphere. Abel took Gries from his robes.

"I can't see what you're seeing, but I know this. I feel a damn ominous presence from over there."

"You'll die if you get close," Frau warned. "...That's if I wasn't here. You're lucky. If I hadn't come here, you wouldn't have been able to get near that sphere no matter what you did."

At those words, Abel felt his heart stop. His breath caught in his throat. He whipped his head around to look at Frau. She tilted her head, looking at his pale face and wide eyes.

"......"

The word tangled in Abel's mind.

Lucky. Yes, lucky. If Frau hadn't been in Urmuri. If Kim Jonghyun hadn't chosen Germud, which was relatively close to Urmuri, out of all the many regions. Abel would never have asked Frau for help, and Frau would never have come to this city.

"......"

"Ah." Frau also understood what Abel was thinking.

There are no coincidences in this world.

"...Damn it."

"Wait... hold on. Maybe you're overthinking it," Frau blurted out, flustered in a way that was utterly unlike her.

What if this wasn't a coincidence? What if it was orchestrated by fate to turn out 'like this'? If Kim Jonghyun's scheme, Frau's arrival here, if everything was fate.

The end of this world's fate is The End.

"Fuck that."

Abel gritted his teeth. He threw off the robe he was wearing and unfurled Gries, striding toward the black sphere without a shred of hesitation.

"Open it!" he roared. Fate this, fate that, he was sick of hearing it. Every time he thought about fate, the image of his damn brother came to mind. His brother, who had abandoned himself out of fear of a fate that might never happen, a catastrophic death. The image of that idiot who had thrown away his brilliant talent for magic and even the name Cain surfaced.

Abel hated him. If only he had the same talent as his brother.

"So impatient...!" Frau grumbled as she raised both hands.

Her hair shot upward. The souls moved by her Ghost Soul Art. The tens of thousands of souls enveloping the sphere parted in two. Abel trusted Frau and kept walking. Her Ghost Soul Art reliably opened a path for him to approach the sphere.

In front of it, Abel looked down at Gries. He reached out and touched the sphere. He had been worried there might be some resistance, but there was no rejection.

*The magic is almost complete.*

If he just confirmed the coordinates and infused enough magic power, the magic would activate immediately. He could do it. He had made this plan because he could do it. Abel focused his mind on Gries, offering most of his remaining lifespan.

The sphere began to vibrate. Gries' magic activated, capturing dimensional coordinates that could never be detected in this world. Abel caressed the sphere and focused his mind. He interfered with the complex magic formula contained within, erased the existing coordinates engraved there, and then input new ones.

From then on, Gries' magic would manifest the spell anew.

A brilliant, multicolored light erupted from the sphere, washing over the entire space.
