Lee Sungmin had no idea what had just happened. He stood blankly outside Kim Jonghyun's room, his hand pressed to his ear. The moment Kim Jonghyun had tried to speak, something had occurred. He could not name it, but the strange, creeping unease was undeniably real.

*What in the world was that?*

Confused, he pressed his ear firmly. *You will die if you speak.* What kind of nonsense was that? Only he and Kim Jonghyun had been in the room, and Lee Sungmin certainly had no intention of killing the man. So who else could have done it?

*Wait.*

He forced himself to think, to remember what had happened on Mount Mushii. He recalled the words Mushii had muttered, insisting they were only to himself. At the end of that strange monologue, Mushii had warned someone, someone other than Lee Sungmin.

*Stop. This is my domain. It would be best not to come unless you wish for mutual destruction.*

At the time, he had not paid the words much attention. Who had Mushii been warning back then? A chill traced its way down his spine, and Lee Sungmin rubbed the back of his neck. The cold sensation he had felt while hearing that story was deeply etched into his memory.

"Hm?"

As he stood before Kim Jonghyun's door, unable to leave while trying to sort through his thoughts, someone strode toward him. Lee Sungmin turned, reacting to the approaching presence.

"......"

A woman with wide eyes stopped and opened her mouth as if to speak. Lee Sungmin tilted his head, watching her stare at his face. After studying him for a long moment, she furrowed her brow.

"Aren't you that psycho from back then?"

"...Excuse me?"

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened at her sudden remark. "It is him," the woman muttered, her gaze fixed intently on him before she spoke again.

"You don't recognize me?"

"...Who are you?"

"Wow, what kind of guy are you?" the woman exclaimed, pounding her chest in frustration.

Red hair and red eyes. Something belatedly clicked in Lee Sungmin's mind, and his mouth fell half-open.

"Scarlet?"

"You finally recognize me!" Scarlet roared.

Startled by her sudden shout, Lee Sungmin took a few steps back. It was Scarlet Reshir, the woman he had met while training on Mount Mushii. She had been on the mountain for a year longer than him and had left after completing her training alongside the Sword Phantom, Dok Bijun.

"Scarlet? Why are you here?"

"I should be asking you that," she retorted.

It was simple enough why he had not recognized her. Three years had passed, but the Scarlet he remembered was a woman wrapped in rags, unwashed and grimy, with greasy hair that gave off a foul stench. The Scarlet standing before him now was completely different. Her clothes were spotless, and her face and hair were clean. Seeing her like this, Lee Sungmin realized for the first time that she was a stunning beauty.

"This is the Mages Guild," she said. "Weren't you learning martial arts? What, did you change paths to become a mage or something?"

"No, it's not like that..."

"Then what is it? Trying to learn magic and martial arts at the same time? Geez, don't do something so pointless. Just focus on what you were doing..."

"I already said that's not it," Lee Sungmin cut in with a deep sigh. "I didn't come here to learn magic."

At his tone, Scarlet pouted. "Then why are you here?"

"I have some business to attend to."

"Business?" Scarlet's eyes flicked to the red door behind him, and her face twisted into a scowl. "Don't tell me your business has something to do with that crazy bastard, Kim Jonghyun."

"Do you know him?"

"Of course I do. Who doesn't know that psycho?" she grumbled, scratching the back of her head vigorously. On the mountain, white flakes of dandruff would have fallen like snow, but now, nothing did. It seemed scratching her head was just a habit. "It's nice seeing you after so long. I'd like to chat for a bit, and I'm pretty free right now. How about it? Wanna come to my room?"

"To your room?"

"Hey, hey. You pervy little brat. Don't get any weird ideas. I'm just bored and thought we could kill some time, okay?"

"What did I say that was pervy?"

"How old are you?"

"...I'm eighteen."

"Boys your age are all perverts. Their heads are filled with nothing but dirty thoughts."

"What kind of nonsense are you spouting..." Lee Sungmin grumbled.

Scarlet, however, seemed to have no intention of backing down. She grabbed him and dragged him toward her room. Unlike Kim Jonghyun's space, where the dismembered limbs and organs of an Ogre floated about, Scarlet's room had no such gruesome displays. It was, however, still quite messy.

"Just sit wherever," she said, perching herself on a mountain-like pile of books.

Lee Sungmin looked around the room and muttered, "So you're just a messy person, Scarlet."

"Hey! You wanna say something rude? Most mages live like this, and how am I messy? Do you know how many times a day I've showered since leaving that damn mountain?" Scarlet grumbled and crossed her legs. "So. Why are you here?"

At her question, Lee Sungmin explained the circumstances that had brought him to the Mages Guild. It was not something worth hiding. When he finished his story, Scarlet furrowed her brow.

"Kim Jonghyun," she muttered. "I'd heard that crazy bastard was messing with a Chimera. I just never imagined you'd be the one to catch it."

"...What kind of person is he?" Lee Sungmin asked. It occurred to him that he knew nothing about Kim Jonghyun, other than the fact that, like him, he was a Korean from Earth.

Scarlet shrugged. "He's a madman. His mind is a little, no, a lot messed up, but his skills are the real deal. If nothing else, there's no mage in the Behenger Mages Guild more knowledgeable about Dark Magic and Chimeras than him."

Lee Sungmin could not help but agree with her assessment. A man who played with floating fragments of an Ogre's corpse was far from normal.

"Why are you here, Scarlet?"

"Because I'm a mage," she answered with an indifferent expression. "It's not strange for a mage to belong to the Mages Guild, is it? It's convenient. As long as you have decent skills and produce research results, they give you plenty of funding. That's why I'm here. Behenger was the closest place to Mount Mushii."

Scarlet Reshir. In his past life, he had only heard her name in rumors. To think she had been in Behenger all this time.

*No. At this point in time, it's before Scarlet becomes an archmage.*

Considering the flow of time, she would likely build her foundation here in Behenger before leaving to officially become an archmage.

"My body is comfortable, but my mind isn't," Scarlet grumbled. "My research hasn't been going well lately."

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh at her words. Even if she was frustrated with her stalled research now, her future as an archmage who would establish a new school of magic was already guaranteed. Unlike him, Scarlet would eventually reach her destiny.

"It'll work out," he said.

"What do you know? More importantly, Baek Sogo… you didn't leave with that girl?"

"No. My senior sister left the mountain a year before I did."

"She's one tough cookie. She was there for almost three years, right? You too. I left because I felt like I'd go crazy if I stayed there even a few more days."

"Did you part ways with Dok Bijun?"

"I split up with that old man as soon as we arrived in Behenger," Scarlet answered with a chuckle. "He asked if I wanted to go on a journey with him, and I told him to go by himself. Still, it's nice to see someone I know after so long. I haven't really talked to anyone lately, what with all the research. Besides, my personality is so shitty that not many people talk to me in the first place."

"......"

"Come visit next time you're free. I'll at least give you some tea."

"......"

Leaving the Mages Guild, Lee Sungmin felt conflicted. He could not simply dismiss Scarlet's words as mere friendliness, because what Nebul had said weighed heavily on his mind. That thought, in turn, mingled with the strange reaction Kim Jonghyun had shown.

*What is going on?*

*Your life may not be your own.* The incomprehensible words echoed in his head. What kind of bullshit was that? It was his life, no one else's. If it was not his, then whose was it?

{sep}

After stopping by the Mercenary Guild to report his conversation with Kim Jonghyun to Bern, Lee Sungmin returned to his house. He had intended to practice his Spear Technique, but his mind was too cluttered and his head too heavy to concentrate. In the end, he put down his spear, shut himself in his room, and sat in a meditative posture.

He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, hoping to shake off his complicated feelings, but it did not work well. The words from Kim Jonghyun and the words from Mushii, all incomprehensible and inexplicable, began to interlock. He had been told there was a reason he had returned, that there were no coincidences. His strangely good luck. If it was not a coincidence, then what was it? What about his connections with others? This life, the life of the Lee Sungmin who was here now, thinking… whose was it?

By the time he managed to stop the chain of spiraling thoughts, night had fallen. Pushing himself up, Lee Sungmin sighed and pressed his throbbing temples.

"...I don't need it," he spat, dragging his feet out of the mansion.

Standing in the overgrown garden, he picked up the spear he had tossed aside. He did not want to care anymore. Thinking, worrying, and doubting would lead to no answers, and no one was going to simply give him the truth. In the end, it was all meaningless suspicion. Therefore, Lee Sungmin gave up on thinking about it.

He ignored it.

Martial arts were different. The spear was different. Unlike those ambiguous questions, they showed him a clear answer. As he began to move, his mind grew calm. Lee Sungmin swung his spear for a long time, practicing the forms of Rannachal and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. No one had told him these were the right answers either, but he knew that the spear he wielded was close to one.

It was not the complete answer, however. That was what he pursued. Saving Baek Sogo, surpassing Wiji Hoyeon. Those were his goals, along with the ones he had to achieve before them.

By the time Lee Sungmin finally put down his spear, morning had arrived. Drenched in sweat, he felt refreshed rather than tired. He belatedly realized he had not even eaten and let out an awkward smile.

"This is why my body won't grow," Lee Sungmin grumbled as he went back inside the house.
