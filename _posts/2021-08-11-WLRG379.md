A great bonfire blazed in the forest, its light pushing back the shadows. Beyond the dance of scattering embers, the cackles and movements of the Fairy Realm filled the night.

Empty liquor bottles lay scattered around the fire, testaments to the night's revelry. Demon God the Spear King, having drunk himself into a stupor without the aid of his inner arts, had curled up quietly in a corner to sleep. Sprawled out beside him was Demon God Lloyd, who had been cornered by the Spear King for a martial arts discussion from the very first drink.

Nearby, the Black Dragon Knight chattered away to Teresa, whose cheeks were flushed red from a taste of the Demon God's liquor. Lee Sungmin paid them no mind, knowing the knight was likely sharing the kind of gossip a woman Teresa's age might enjoy, so there was no need to listen.

Scarlet swirled the wine in her glass, her expression serious. "What happens to a child born between a half-dragon and a human?" she murmured.

"Can a half-dragon even have children in the first place?"

"Hmm... I don't know," she continued, thinking aloud. "Aren't they usually infertile in cases like that? You know, like mules."

"A Dragon isn't a donkey or a horse."

"Still," Scarlet countered, "it's a bit much to go up and ask him if he's impotent, and before that, shouldn't we consider if the Saintess can even get married and have children?"

Her words made a strange kind of sense, and Lee Sungmin found himself nodding.

"But the age gap is a bit..."

"He's at least two hundred, right?" Scarlet mused. "Even so, it probably wouldn't be a huge problem… as long as they love each other."

"Do you think there's a possibility?"

"He doesn't have any visible flaws," she said, "and his personality… well, compared to that old man passed out over there, he's perfectly fine. Though you'd be hard-pressed to find anyone worse than that old man." She giggled at the thought, as if finding it immensely amusing.

Her amusement faded, and she turned her gaze on Lee Sungmin, her eyes blinking as she looked slightly upward. "What's your relationship with the Little Heavenly Demon?"

"Where did that suddenly come from?"

"I'm just curious. You've never said it directly, but I can tell it's a very… sticky relationship without you having to say a word."

"Aren't you a little too drunk?"

"I'm just pretending to be drunk for you. Who else would ask something like this?"

"No one besides you is curious, Scarlet."

"I wouldn't be so sure about that," Scarlet guaranteed, bringing the wine glass to her lips. Her gaze flickered over to Baek Sogo, who sat near Lee Sungmin with her arms wrapped around her knees, her own cheeks tinged with a reddish hue.

Baek Sogo glanced their way, her eyes meeting Scarlet's for a brief moment before she started and looked away, pretending not to notice as she picked up her cup.

"You know, women like this kind of talk," Scarlet said. "Isn't it a great topic for a drinking party?"

"That's right!" Rubia's voice piped up. She had been sipping a sweet fruit wine made by the fairies, disliking bitter flavors, but now her cat ears perked up. She shuffled over on her knees and sat right next to Scarlet. "Well, as for me, I already know what kind of relationship Lee Sungmin and the Little Heavenly Demon have."

"How do you know?"

"I saw the two of them having a secret rendezvous in the forest. Not only that, but things that happened in the past too..."

"Don't say anything unnecessary," Lee Sungmin warned, glaring at Rubia.

Rubia pouted in response and took another sip of her wine. A story cut short like that only became more intriguing.

"Are you still confused?" Scarlet pressed her. "I'm your master. Who's the great mage who broke your contract with the Spirit Queen and took you as her familiar?"

"That... would be you, Scarlet."

"And since the Spirit Queen is dead, I'm your one and only master. Now, answer. What is their relationship?"

"They've been friends since childhood."

Unsatisfied, Scarlet's eyes gleamed as she pressed for more. Rubia, perhaps wanting to hear the full story from Lee Sungmin himself, stayed quiet. Meanwhile, Teresa was laughing happily at the Black Dragon Knight's jokes, and Baek Sogo had subtly scooted closer, straining to listen.

So Lee Sungmin spoke. He recounted their first meeting in the Dungeon, their time in Ruvus, and their journey through the Fairy and Sleeping Forests. He told the stories intertwined with Wiji Hoyeon, abridging what needed to be abridged and leaving out what shouldn't be said.

If anything, his omissions only spurred his listeners' imaginations. At the mention of them spending a year together in this very forest, Rubia cupped her blushing cheeks with her hands. Baek Sogo's expression, however, hardened slightly. Watching her, Scarlet clicked her tongue.

"This is too disadvantageous."

"......"

"No, it's nothing," Scarlet said, resting her chin on her hand, feeling conflicted. *She, too, felt quite favorable toward Lee Sungmin.* When she thought about it, he was the only man she had known for a long time who was well-built, handsome, had a decent personality, and was highly skilled. In the first place, Scarlet didn't have many options. Like most mages, she had lived a life devoted to magic rather than to things like love, dating, and marriage. She felt it was a bit of a shame, a waste even, but she wasn't sad about it.

*Well, if it comes down to it, I don't mind being second or third.*

In that respect, Scarlet was very much like the Beggars' Gang. Of course, she had no intention of acting on it right now. First, she had to finish The End, and after that, she had to achieve her lifelong ambition as a mage. Love, dating, and marriage would probably come after all that.

However, Baek Sogo was different.

Scarlet glanced at Baek Sogo's pale face. The flush from the alcohol had vanished, likely driven out by inner arts as she listened.

*It can't be helped. I have no choice but to play Cupid.*

She sent a telepathic message to Baek Sogo, whose lips were trembling.

[Aren't you going to confess?]

Baek Sogo flinched in surprise and looked at Scarlet.

[What are you talking about all of a sudden?]

[What do you mean, 'all of a sudden'? Do you have any idea what your face looks like right now? Should I get you a mirror?]

[What's wrong with my expression?]

[Forget it, forget it. More importantly, what are you going to do? Not going to confess? I think the timing is perfect right now. If you just make up your mind, I'll set the mood for you properly.]

[I... don't have those kinds of feelings for my Junior Disciple.]

[Then why do you have that look on your face?]

[What kind of look do I have on my face?]

[It's the face of a foolish woman who can't confess to her one-sided love, who kept it in her heart for over ten years with the baseless confidence that she was the only one, only to find out that he was actually kissing and sucking with someone else ten years ago behind her back.]

Baek Sogo couldn't answer and bit her lower lip firmly. Scarlet clicked her tongue and glanced at her. Lee Sungmin, feeling like he had said something unnecessary, poured liquor from his gourd into his cup.

[Anyway, I get it. You don't want to say it yourself yet, right?]

[...I don't think it's the right time for such talk, and, I... I mean, I don't have any feelings for my Junior Disciple beyond that of a fellow disciple...]

[That's what you think. Don't worry, I'll talk to him for you.]

It seemed her role as Cupid was just a martial arts theory. Still, Scarlet didn't give up. She snatched the gourd from Lee Sungmin's hand and filled his cup herself.

"What do you think about polygyny?"

"...What's with that sudden bullshit..."

"Just answer me."

"...Polygyny, that's when one man has multiple wives, right?"

"That's right."

"Wouldn't the women fight?"

"They might understand each other and not fight."

"Still, it's a bit..."

"Then what about polyandry?"

"I don't like it."

He answered instantly, without a second thought. Scarlet's expression hardened. "You piece of trash," she cursed. "So you don't like one woman having multiple husbands, but you like one man having multiple wives?"

"I never said I liked it."

"There was a difference in your answers. Your nuance was that it could work if the women understood each other."

"That's not true."

"Just shut up. You piece of trash, all men are the same anyway. I mean, what man would refuse a woman who comes on to him? Especially if she's pretty, he'd never be able to say no."

"Why are you taking this so negatively?"

"Because you're annoying, you son of a bitch."

"What did I do..."

Scarlet whipped her head around to face Baek Sogo. "Baek Sogo. What were you doing ten years ago?"

"...Huh?" Baek Sogo, who had been slumping with her shoulders drooped, flinched in surprise.

"I asked what you were doing ten years ago."

"Um... I was wandering the world."

"And before that?"

"I was on Mount Mushii..."

"You were on that damn mountain again, weren't you?" Scarlet snapped. "Well, I was better off than you! I was cooped up in the Magic Tower, smelling musty old books, writing in a magic tome until my fingertips went numb, and cramming my head with theory until it felt like it would explode, but you?" She jabbed a finger toward Lee Sungmin. "You were off kissing and sucking with the Little Heavenly Demon!"

"Is that all I did? And 'kissing and sucking', what kind of language is that..."

"So you didn't do it?"

"Well..."

"You're really unnecessarily honest about things like this. Can't you just lie and say, 'I didn't do it'?"

"I didn't do it."

"I really want to kill you," Scarlet spat out, her shoulders trembling as Lee Sungmin replied with a reluctant expression.

He cleared his throat and drained his cup.

"I'm the fool for trying to talk to you," Scarlet muttered. "How could I with such a clueless guy..."

"Why am I clueless?" he asked, staring intently at her. His eyes were unnecessarily deep.

Scarlet flinched, drawing back her chin. Lee Sungmin sighed and shook his head. "I don't know."

"......"

"The End isn't over yet," he said, his voice low. "We may have annihilated the Spirit Queen, but Jeniela and the Martial God are still out there. We don't know what other disasters might be waiting. So..."

"I knew it," Scarlet groaned. "I suggested we drink comfortably and change the mood, so why are you the only one getting all serious again?"

"I'm just saying. Polygyny, polyandry... what does any of that matter right now?"

"The world could end tomorrow, so shouldn't you live a life without regrets?"

"Are you going to plant an apple tree or something?"

"What kind of bullshit is that? Why plant an apple tree?"

"It's a saying from my world. Even if the Earth is destroyed tomorrow, I will plant one apple tree..."

"What kind of crazy bastard was that? And what the hell is Earth? Besides, why plant an apple tree when the world is ending? I'd rather just eat one more apple," Scarlet shot back. Lee Sungmin wanted to rebut her, but he didn't even know the name of the philosopher who had said it, so he just kept his mouth shut.

"...Anyway, I don't really want to talk about that. Even now, while we're doing this, Wiji Hoyeon is..."

"Probably suffering like hell," Scarlet cut in. "Thanks to that amazing person, we were able to take down the Spirit Queen easily. In fact, couldn't the Little Heavenly Demon stop The End all by herself, even if we did nothing?"

"I don't want to place all the burden on her."

"I'm just saying. I don't want to blindly trust the Little Heavenly Demon, who I've never even met. I have to do it myself, see it for myself. Why did the mood get like this again? I mean, why the hell are we talking about The End for no reason..."

"Ah."

Before Scarlet could finish her grumbling, Lee Sungmin let out a loud noise as something flashed in his mind. Baek Sogo, who had been staring at the ground trying to sort out her complicated feelings, was startled.

"Spinoza."

"What?"

"It's Spinoza. The name of the person who said he'd plant an apple tree."

"So what?"

"I just... suddenly remembered."

"Crazy bastard..."

"Ahahat!"

Teresa's loud laugh cut through the air. Scarlet's eyebrows furrowed as she looked across the bonfire at Teresa and the Black Dragon Knight. "What the hell is so funny that she's cracking up like that?"

"He must be surprisingly humorous."

"He's still impotent, though."

"That's not certain yet, is it?"

"The probability is high," Scarlet sniffed. "By the way. What would the Little Heavenly Demon think?"

"About what?"

"About polygyny."

"Are you still on about that?" Lee Sungmin asked, dumbfounded. Once again, Baek Sogo pretended not to listen while straining her ears.

Scarlet nodded with a serious expression. "On second thought, the Little Heavenly Demon's opinion seems more important than yours."

Come to think of it, he had talked about this with Wiji Hoyeon before. He recalled a conversation they'd had before they parted ways after leaving the Fairy Forest.

"...She said she didn't care, since she was the first."

"The first?"

"That is..."

"Ah, forget it. I get it," Scarlet interrupted. "She's unconditionally the first, right? Doesn't matter if there's a second or a third. Wow, what incredible confidence. I've never even met her, but I can tell exactly what kind of personality she has."

This was a sufficient gain. From the sound of it, Wiji Hoyeon wouldn't mind much even if there was a second or a third. Scarlet grinned at Baek Sogo.

[She says it's fine even if there's a second.]

[...I told you, I don't have those kinds of feelings for my Junior Disciple...]

[Yeah, no. From where I'm standing, you definitely have those feelings.]

Scarlet cut Baek Sogo off, not listening any further. She poured the gourd's liquor until the cup was full, a pleased smile on her face as she tossed back the drink.

"Ah, this liquor tastes good."
