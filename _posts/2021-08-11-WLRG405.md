Though Lee Sungmin had never met the Spirit Medium directly, he knew the stories well enough. Heaven Beyond Heaven was born from the Martial God's encounter with him. The Martial God's crusade to stop The End had been a lie, his will ultimately a puppet's, dancing on the strings of the Spirit Medium. Every action taken in that grand name was nothing more than the Spirit Medium's own design.

"Why didn't you kill the Martial God?" Lee Sungmin asked, his eyes fixed on the Demonic Spirit.

He knew that ten years ago, Wiji Hoyeon had been called here in a dream. Here, she had met the Martial God, who in turn must have made contact with the Demonic Spirit.

"I get that you can't leave this pavilion," Lee Sungmin pressed, "but he came to you. Why didn't you kill him then?"

"Because I couldn't," the Demonic Spirit answered, its voice weak. "Every being has a role, an unequal one. The Martial God's role and destiny were to be protected by the Spirit. I could not intervene."

"How powerless," Lee Sungmin sneered. The insult hung in the air, and the Demonic Spirit had no choice but to accept it.

In the end, the being had never truly lied to the Martial God. When asked if he was the Apostle of the End, the Demonic Spirit had claimed to be a guide, not a Taoist. When the Martial God had asked about his own role, the Demonic Spirit had simply replied that he could not show him the path.

*What if the Demonic Spirit had killed the Martial God back then?*

The thought was a fleeting one, and Lee Sungmin did not let it sway him. Even if the Martial God had died then, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would have met his end one way or another. In fact, that fight had been a gain for the Chairman. It had convinced him that he was history's strongest, allowing him to finally let go of his greatest lingering regret.

"What is the Spirit's purpose?"

It was a question he had carried for a long time, one that none of his memories could answer, but now, the being who held that answer was right in front of him.

"...This world has many variables," the Demonic Spirit muttered. "There were those I intended, but the world now has far more than I ever planned."

"I made contact with the Dragons of Erebrisa," Lee Sungmin stated.

At those words, Yana's eyebrows twitched. His learning Dragonic and finding the dragons who had escaped Eria was part of no one's plan, merely the result of a series of coincidences.

"I heard many things from them," he continued. "That this world is very different from the last one, and the one before that."

"...It was bound to be different," the Demonic Spirit admitted. "Until this world, I was cooperating with the Spirit."

"Why did you betray him?"

"For the same reason Mushii and Denir began to doubt. This world has repeated itself so many times... and we have watched it all, mechanically. I have seen civilizations begin and end more times than I can count. I have seen the nearly unchanging conclusions and the people who died in vain within them."

"It seems you developed some sympathy."

"We are not perfect beings," the Demonic Spirit muttered with a bitter smile. "A small wavering was the beginning, and it grew into a great sense of disconnect. I just wanted it to end now. Of course, the desire to save this world from its endless cycle of death and life... that wasn't the only reason. How long do you think I've been repeating this work?"

The memories of gods like Mushii and Denir were reset when the world was destroyed and a new one began. Transcendental beings like Osla and Sarahenne simply left, replaced by a new Spirit Queen and Fairy Queen in the next world, but the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit were different. As the managers of this world, they retained their memories through every single repetition. The Demonic Spirit's desire to end The End was likely a significant part of a selfish wish to escape that tedious cycle.

"Was it impossible to persuade the Spirit?" Lee Sungmin pressed.

"If that were possible, I wouldn't have gone to such trouble. Instead, my actions only stimulated him. The variables I created to bring about the end only raised his motivation. Among all the countless repetitions, this is the first time there has been such a difference. Thanks to that, the Spirit is now intervening in the world directly."

The existence of Gries, Jeniela's excessive power, Heaven Beyond Heaven itself—one could see the Spirit's changed methods in all of it.

"Originally, at this point, The End should have finished everything," the Demonic Spirit continued, but it had not. "The Spirit took note of this world's many variables and the countless possibilities they created. He focused on giving a 'survivable' disaster rather than destroying the world immediately. The magic tome called Gries was arranged for that very purpose. The humans who learned of their fate focused their efforts on stopping it. As a result, the Spirit's intention succeeded."

Unlike Cain, who fled from fate, his younger brother Abel had chosen to confront it head-on. He had acted to escape the fate of The End, but in the end...

"So it was the Spirit's arrangement," Lee Sungmin muttered, his voice low. Changing fate through Gries had been impossible from the beginning. He recalled the image of Abel and realized his had been a dog's death. Blood dripped from a fist he had not realized he had clenched.

"A being who learns of a predetermined ending does one of two things," the Demonic Spirit said. "They either struggle desperately or they give up. The one the Spirit paid attention to was, of course, the struggling side. That struggle couldn't change fate, but it created a different history than before. As you said, the purpose of Eria's existence is to obtain completed techniques, and this world... is made with the help of a great many others."

"...The Elemental and Fairy Realms?"

"And others besides them. How much power do you think is needed to construct a world of this scale and repeat it so many times? And did you really think that only the Spirit and I support this world? That's impossible. We are only its managers, after all."

"...The Great Demon World," Lee Sungmin said, the Demonic Spirit's self-deprecating mumble triggering his memory of Kim Jonghyun. Kim Jonghyun had known the truth of Eria without ever having contacted the Demonic Spirit or the Spirit. He had obtained that forbidden knowledge by becoming a Quasi-Demon King and making contact with the true Demon Lords of other dimensions.

"They too contribute a significant amount of power to the composition of this world," the Demonic Spirit confirmed. "This 'research area' is for those 'investors'."

"Investors," Lee Sungmin repeated, a hollow laugh escaping him. It was a rather fitting word. They were contributing power because there was something they wanted.

"The Grimoire is a possibility the Great Demon World planted for themselves. It was the first time in all the repetitions that a guinea pig from this research area awakened as a Demon Lord using it. If Kim Jonghyun's intention had succeeded, the Great Demon World would have succeeded in turning this entire massive research area into a colony."

"...You spoke about possibilities," Lee Sungmin said, roughly wiping the blood from his hand on his trousers. "Are you saying that our desperate struggles in the face of The End are those possibilities?"

"Think of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman," the Demonic Spirit replied. "And the Spear King, too. The Void Monarch was a fellow whose end was already determined in the repetitions so far, but the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Spear King were not. Until now, they died without ever transcending being 'human', but this time... thanks to the repetition of variables, they transcended humanity. They succeeded in escaping this world, but the 'techniques' they achieved are recorded in this world."

The Spirit's intention had succeeded. Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly as he mulled over that fact. Their desperate struggles to escape The End had become variables and possibilities.

"...What is the single path?"

"Single path?" The Demonic Spirit tilted its head, seemingly confused.

Lee Sungmin told the Demonic Spirit about the single path that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Void Monarch, and the Spear King had seen before they left. After hearing the whole story, the Demonic Spirit sighed.

"The Battle God Chronicle."

"...The Battle God Chronicle...?"

"Originally, it was impossible for humans to escape the Cycle of Mortality. However, in another dimension, a certain human not only escaped the Cycle of Mortality but also obtained a flawless, absolute authority. That being became a precedent for all dimensions, and starting with him, beings who could escape the Cycle of Mortality began to appear, albeit rarely, in each dimension. They... were not bound by their physical bodies. Their consciousness connected to a vast world. That is the Battle God Chronicle. I didn't know it took the form of a single path, though."

He recalled the words the Void Monarch left behind, that they would meet again there someday.

"The Spirit has already obtained everything it can," the Demonic Spirit muttered, its voice weak and drenched in powerlessness. "And... soon, everything will end."

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. He strode forward and grabbed the Demonic Spirit's shoulders.

"I haven't heard it yet."

Lee Sungmin glared into the Demonic Spirit's face. Yana's face. Looking at it made a corner of his chest ache. Yana had been blindly devoted to him because she adored the Void Monarch within him.

"The reason you called me here, even going so far as to bind Yana to this place."

"Because you wouldn't have come if I didn't..."

"Tell me the reason."

He had a vague idea. He just needed a definite answer. To the Demonic Spirit, Lee Sungmin was not an important being. What the Demonic Spirit had arranged from the beginning was...

"What happened to Wiji Hoyeon?"

The voice that came out was cold. His emotions threatened to boil over. Lee Sungmin glared at the Demonic Spirit. The Demonic Spirit avoided his glare and answered.

"...Was this also the Spirit's intention?"

"Tell me."

"...As the Spirit Queen suddenly tried to Advent into the world, Wiji Hoyeon fell into a dimensional rift, and... she escaped my protection. I couldn't leave this place, but the Spirit was different."

There was nothing more to hear.

Lee Sungmin let go of the Demonic Spirit's shoulders. He let out a hollow laugh and looked up at the sky. He recalled the image of Wiji Hoyeon, drifting in the pitch-black space. The sight of her shoulders trembling, her heart weakened unlike her usual self. That moment when he reached out his hand but couldn't touch her. Her voice, muttering that she believed in him.

"...Can't Wiji Hoyeon defy the Spirit's will?"

"Impossible... Wiji Hoyeon's existence is fixed. Beings of this world are not permitted to obtain transcendental power. If they attain such a status, they are forcibly exiled from the world. Or they head to the Battle God Chronicle."

To prevent that, the Demonic Spirit had fixed Wiji Hoyeon's existence to this world. No matter how much power she obtained, Wiji Hoyeon could not be exiled, nor could she go to the Battle God Chronicle. As a result, it made her possess a lower status compared to the power she held.

"Defying it is impossible."

"...Haha..."

Lee Sungmin let out another hollow laugh.

The Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun was exiled from the world.

The Spirit Queen was annihilated.

The Vampire Queen was defeated.

All the Dungeons were closed.

*What's next?* He had always thought about it. Not knowing what would come next had always filled him with an unknown fear, and now, he knew what was next.

"Yes..."

He recalled the image of Wiji Hoyeon smiling.

*I must not Despair.* Lee Sungmin calmed his agitation. The final question. Muttering to himself, Lee Sungmin took the key from his bosom.

"What in the world is this?"
