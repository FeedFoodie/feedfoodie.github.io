Getting used to it was not so simple a task, not something he could accomplish merely by being conscious of the need. Of all the restrictions shackling his body, he adapted fastest to the one on his voice. It was an absolute silence, a ban on every sound his mouth could make. No scream, no groan, not even a kiai could escape his lips, reducing communication to something far more fundamental than simple conversation.

In truth, the silence was not the greatest of his burdens. The only person Lee Sungmin met with any frequency on this mountain was Baek Sogo, and she was under the very same vocal restriction. In fact, it was thanks to her that he had managed to grow accustomed to it so quickly.

Their silent conversations, conducted by scratching letters into the dirt, were hardly convenient, but adapting to them was not particularly difficult.

The other restrictions, however, were a different matter entirely.

The weight Lee Sungmin felt grew heavier by the day. It felt like an anchor chained to his ankles, especially after he became Baek Sogo's Junior Disciple and began learning the Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

The Dancing Shadow Soul Break was a movement art, and like most of its kind, it was built upon the extremes of illusion and speed, favoring constant motion over stillness. For Lee Sungmin, learning such a technique while under the restriction of weight was an incredibly harsh trial. The art itself was an outstanding martial practice that bordered on the divine, which only served to make the burden he felt even greater.

*The moment I try to move quickly, the weight grabs my ankles.*

This was the wall the Dancing Shadow Soul Break slammed into. Even when he followed the oral formula perfectly, he could not achieve a satisfactory speed. To overcome the weight, he needed enough strength and speed to master it, a simple thought that was far easier to conceive than to execute.

*Thinking is easy.*

Reality was always harder.

While the weight held back his martial arts, the restrictions on taste and dreams were destroying Lee Sungmin himself. Meals remained a terrible ordeal, and sleep offered no comfortable rest, but the torment of his taste buds was nothing compared to the terror of his dreams.

He grew to fear falling asleep, found himself desperately wishing for the night never to come, for his body to never be overcome with exhaustion, but such desperate wishes were futile against the setting sun. His body, pushed to its limits from swinging his spear and running like a madman, would inevitably grow tired. His mind would become hazy, and he would plunge into a death-like sleep only to be ripped awake by a nightmare. He would let out a long, silent scream before crouching by the campfire, his body trembling, until exhaustion claimed him once more, dragging him back into the cycle.

"You crazy bastard."

It was a phrase he had been hearing quite often since entering the mountain. Lee Sungmin stopped his training and turned to face Scarlet. He had wondered what she was doing here, and the answer seemed to be standing right beside her. He glanced at Baek Sogo, but she offered no explanation.

"What're you two mute bastards making eyes at each other for? You two dating?"

Scarlet grumbled as she stomped toward him. Lee Sungmin set down his spear and retrieved the wooden stick tucked into his belt. The sight of it made Scarlet's brow furrow sharply.

"Don't bother asking, I'll just tell you. Ah, seriously! Why'd you have to go and become a mute? Makes it so damn hard to talk."

She grumbled, scratching her head vigorously. With each motion, white flakes of dandruff fell like snow.

"Baek Sogo. This mute bitch here," Scarlet spat the words out, glaring at Baek Sogo. "She came to me three days ago and started begging. Asking me to do something about you."

A rotten smell wafted from her lips with every movement, but Lee Sungmin couldn't spare a thought for the stench. His attention was fixed on Baek Sogo, his expression one of pure surprise.

"I'm going to teach you some magic."

Scarlet strode closer, the stench growing stronger with every step until it was right under his nose. Lee Sungmin didn't pinch it shut.

"You should be grateful," she said, her voice thick with condescension. "Normally, even simple magic isn't something you can just pick up and use... but this world makes it possible."

Though she grumbled, the meaning of her words was simple. She was going to teach him magic.

In his past life, he had no connection to the discipline whatsoever. Of course, he could have learned a few of the most basic spells if he had set his mind to it, but for a mercenary who relied on his body, martial arts were far more intuitive and desperately needed. To learn directly from Scarlet Reshir—the woman who, in ten years, would establish the 'Reshir School' and be called an archmage—was an incredible, miraculous encounter.

"Look at this kid's face."

Scarlet glanced at his face and pouted.

"Hey! I told you, just *some* magic. I'm not teaching you everything. Magic is my livelihood, I can't just teach it for free. This isn't free either. That mute, Baek Sogo, already paid the price."

[The price?]

Lee Sungmin wrote on the ground.

"It's nothing much. She promised to help me once, no questions asked, if I ask for her help later."

Nothing much? It meant she could command a master of Baek Sogo's caliber once, unconditionally. Lee Sungmin's head whipped around to look at Baek Sogo, but she looked as if it were no big deal.

"I heard about you, you crazy brat," Scarlet cackled. "Took on two more restrictions? You're really... completely insane. They say ignorance makes you stupid, and you're the perfect example."

"I'll teach you four types of magic. First, Mental Cleaning. You... have nightmares, right? You can't just ignore them as simple dreams. If you keep having them, your mind might collapse. Mental Cleaning is for that. It cleanses a worn-out mind... though for someone like you who has nightmares every day, it'll only be first aid."

As she spoke, Scarlet pulled a magic scroll from her robes.

"And then Strength and Haste. They're basic buff magic. Make you stronger, make your body faster. The last one is Fatigue Recovery. It's... well, a fatigue recovery magic."

A total of four scrolls were placed in his arms. Lee Sungmin blinked, looking down at them. The magic Scarlet offered was exactly what he needed. Mental Cleaning would protect his mind from the nightly assaults of his nightmares. Strength and Haste could lessen the crushing burden of the 'weight' restriction, even if it was not a complete solution against a burden that grew heavier each day, and Fatigue Recovery would relieve the exhaustion that accumulated in a body denied deep sleep.

"Remember. Magic isn't a cure-all," Scarlet advised him. "It's convenient, but it's not a cure-all. Strength and Haste use magic to boost your physical strength, but your body still bears the burden. It's the same for Mental Cleaning and Fatigue Recovery. Overuse them, and you'll wear your body out."

She fell silent for a moment before letting out a short sigh.

"...Well. If you were wise enough to take care of your body, you wouldn't have accepted such an idiotic restriction in the first place. Anyway, it'll help. With enduring."

With that, Scarlet returned to her dwelling. Lee Sungmin immediately read the scrolls and added the four magic to his skills.

Just then, Baek Sogo approached. He tucked the scrolls into his robes and looked up at her.

[Did you do all this for me because you're a good person, Senior Sister?]

"......"

The answer he received was a confident expression from Baek Sogo. It was the answer he had expected, yet hearing it so directly was still draining. He looked at her with a weary resignation.

[This is my problem. Why did you get involved, Senior Sister?]

[You're not close with Scarlet, but I am. So I could ask her.]

Her reply was simple and true. Had Lee Sungmin gone to Scarlet himself, she would never have listened. The only reason he now held these scrolls was because Baek Sogo was close to the mage and possessed the means to meet her demands.

[Connections are important, Junior Disciple. Sometimes... it seems like you're afraid of forming connections. No. Is it a little different? It also seems like you don't trust people.]

She had hit the nail on the head. Lee Sungmin stared at her, his surprise evident. A small smirk played on Baek Sogo's lips, as if she had expected as much.

[There's nothing wrong with being cautious, but if you're too cautious... you can't connect with others. People can't live alone.]

Those words stirred a great many things within him. *People can't live alone.* In his past life, Lee Sungmin had no comrades, no friends. He trusted no one. His status as a Classless in this damn world had forced a harsh, solitary survival upon him. He lived like that for thirteen years, and he died alone.

This life was different.

He had received kindness from Hans and Jack, even if it stemmed from a misunderstanding of his talent. He had received it from Jack's daughter, Lula, and certainly from Wiji Hoyeon, and now, from Baek Sogo.

*People can't live alone. Connections are important.* A whirlwind of contradictions raged in his heart. Was it wrong to be cautious, to fear betrayal? The life he had lived and the life he was living now overlapped and clashed, breeding only confusion.

[It seems those were difficult words for you, Junior Disciple.]

Baek Sogo wrote again after a glance at his expression.

[Don't take it too much to heart. You and I are different people, Junior Disciple. What I say won't necessarily be right for you. Our values are different.]

She vigorously erased the words with her foot.

[This must be your first time using magic, right? Give it a try.]

At her encouragement, Lee Sungmin nodded and focused on the magic in his mind.

As Scarlet had said, simple magic was not something one could normally learn and cast so easily, but this world was different. Much like martial arts, magic could be cast with relative ease. Casting it was one thing, but developing and utilizing it was entirely up to the individual.

*So it uses inner qi.*

He felt the inner qi in his qi center stir. Even without circulating the Violet Dawn Divine Art, his inner qi were consumed by the magic. First, he cast Mental Cleaning. The dull, heavy feeling in his head immediately cleared, replaced by a refreshing lightness. Next was Fatigue Recovery. If the first spell had cleared his mind, this one lightened his body, melting away the exhaustion that had built up from his lack of sleep.

*Using this long-term will be poison to my body... I know, but...*

He knew it, but it was the best method available to him. With a much more refreshed expression, Lee Sungmin looked at Baek Sogo. He wanted to test Strength and Haste as well, but first, he needed to bow his head to her.

[Thank you, Senior Sister.]

He wrote the words on the ground and bowed deeply. Baek Sogo scratched her cheek at the sight.

[Junior Disciple. Still, remember this. I believe this is the proper way for a person to live. Don't forget your grudges. No. You can forget your grudges, but never forget a kindness.]

Baek Sogo wrote.

[So. It's like this. Follow righteousness and don't stray from the proper path. If you receive a kindness, even if you can't repay it immediately, keep it in your heart. As for grudges... I won't tell you to throw them away. If that were possible, why would we learn martial arts? We'd just cultivate the Tao. I may be a good person, but I don't easily forget a grudge, and I certainly never forget a kindness.]

Reading her words, Lee Sungmin nodded. He could accept such a philosophy from Baek Sogo without any mental turmoil.

At the very least, he had no intention of ever forgetting the kindness she had shown him.

*Even in eight years.*

He would not forget. In eight years, Wiji Hoyeon would kill Baek Sogo.

And Lee Sungmin was going to stop that from happening.
