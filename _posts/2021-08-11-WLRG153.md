155.

Beyond the forest lay the city of Devon. Magic Towers, martial arts sects, and Guilds were spread evenly across the vast continent of Eria, and Devon was home to one of the Four Great Orthodox Families, the Jaegal Clan. Though not a large city, Devon was held firmly in the Jaegal Clan's grasp, its influence woven deeply into the lives of the residents. As Lee Sungmin reviewed the information he had purchased through Nebul, he frowned.

The farther south they traveled, the fewer cities they found. Forests grew more numerous and dense, and the sunlight beat down with greater intensity. Devon stood as the last bastion of stability and wealth before the great southern jungle truly began.

*Silver Flying Dragon Jaegal Taeryeong.*

Lee Sungmin had seen him once before, at Shaolin, though they had never exchanged words. When he first went to Shaolin, he had formed a connection with the Sword Dragon, Namgung Heewon, a sworn brotherhood lightly offered and accepted. It was Namgung Heewon who had taken him to Shaolin and helped him meet with its Abbot, Grandmaster Bulyeong.

A few years ago, the young master of the Jaegal Clan, Jaegal Taeryeong, had married Snow Flower Moyong Seojin of the Moyong Clan. She had been living with the Jaegal Clan ever since, and in just a few days, their child, Jaegal Woo, would celebrate his first birthday.

To celebrate the occasion, the ever-friendly Namgung and Tang Clans had sent envoys. Among them were the Sword Dragon Namgung Heewon and the Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee, both of the same generation as Jaegal Taeryeong.

The other guests were none of Lee Sungmin's concern, but Namgung Heewon's presence at the Jaegal estate bothered him. He remembered Namgung Heewon's words at Shaolin. "Come visit the Namgung Clan sometime." It was an invitation he had not yet accepted.

An invitation he could likely never accept. Having killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear, he was now branded a demonic practitioner, pursued by the Murim Alliance. Even if that pursuit was little more than distant surveillance, for him to appear at the Namgung Clan's doorstep would create an awkward situation for them both.

*The ones pursuing me are the Demon Extermination Unit's Black Hound Squad.*

He knew about them, of course. Their leader was a master said to have just barely entered the Supreme Peak, followed by nine squad members who all hovered around the peak realm.

In truth, his own martial strength was more than enough to slaughter the Black Hound Squad head-on. It would not even be difficult. The reason he held back was to avoid needlessly adding to his infamy. To wipe out a force directly under the Murim Alliance was to declare open opposition. In the worst case, an extermination order could be issued against him throughout the entire orthodox murim.

"We should avoid the city," Lee Sungmin said, having confirmed the information.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon nodded in agreement. "Even for a baby's first birthday, all four Noble Clans have gathered. The family heads might not be here, but their children are. There's no need to stir up trouble by stopping in the city."

It was not just the Sword Dragon and Poiso, butterfly who had come. The Sword Dragon had brought the Namgung Clan's Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps, and Poiso, butterfly was accompanied by the Tang Clan's Dark Night Black Mist Squad.

"Devon's a small city," Rubia added, twisting a lock of her hair. "The southern jungle is crawling with Yokai and monsters as common as wild animals. If it weren't for the Jaegal Clan, Devon wouldn't have developed this much. Because of that, their control here is absolute."

He should not enter the city. If he had no reason to, there would be no need.

However, there was a reason.

[There is a shaman in Devon named Aladur. He is a shaman not connected with us, but he is widely rumored to be outstanding within Devon.]

Lee Sungmin pressed his throbbing temples. His journey south was not only to learn how to handle Demonic Energy but also to find the Fist Honored One who had cursed Wiji Hoyeon. He had already asked the few shamans and mages connected to Erebrisa to track the man using the lock of hair he received from Wiji Hoyeon, but they had been unable to confirm the Fist Honored One's location with her hair alone.

*There's no guarantee that Aladur will be able to find the Fist Honored One's location.*

Even so, he could not simply ignore the possibility and leave Devon behind.

He certainly could not walk through Devon's gates without a care. Since he now had a reason to enter, he would have to sneak in. Through Nebul, Lee Sungmin purchased clothes, a wig, and other necessities.

"A human skin mask?"

"It's not actually made of human skin, you know." Nebul chuckled, holding the mask up for Lee Sungmin to see. "The name is just what its creator called it. A traditional human skin mask is made with intricate work on human or animal skin, but this one borrows from that craft and adds a bit of magic. It's not permanent, though."

Lee Sungmin tried it on. As he put it on, the inside of the mask felt like sticky mucus slathering across his skin. He checked the mirror and saw a stranger's face looking back at him.

He changed into new clothes. Rubia, unable to hide her cat ears, simply transformed into a sphere of light and hid in Lee Sungmin's arms as she usually did.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon disguised himself as well, shedding his martial arts uniform for the garb of a Hunter, a common sight in the forested South. With his hair let down and his body wrapped in animal hides, he looked more like a bandit, but once the human skin mask concealed his fierce expression, he transformed into the image of a seasoned Hunter.

Lee Sungmin adopted the appearance of an ordinary scholar. He even removed the Magic Armor he always wore and placed it in his Subspace Pocket. Though the Void Monarch grumbled, it posed no real problem. Thanks to the Demonic Energy pooled at the bottom of his qi center, they remained spiritually connected even without the armor.

Shaking off the Black Hound Squad was a simple matter.

Once night had deepened, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon slipped from their shelter. They employed their movement arts, vanishing from the Black Hound Squad's sight in an instant. After completing their disguises, they made for Devon's city walls. Guards patrolled atop the ramparts.

Though stealth was important, their actions were bold. Masters of the Supreme Peak had long surpassed the limits of human physicality. One after the other, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon vaulted the high wall, their movements so swift and silent that the patrolling guards noticed nothing.

"Sir, they've escaped our surveillance."

"I let 'em go loose on purpose," the Black Hound Leader replied, chewing on a piece of beef jerky as he listened to his subordinate's report. "They've been leading us on a quiet chase 'til now. For them to suddenly disappear means they've got some tricky business to take care of."

"Do you think they went to Devon?"

"They were strange guys. Camped out for over a month with almost no resupplies. They hunted sometimes, but they ate well even when they didn't. Drank plenty of water, too. Seemed like they had a lot of food stored in a Subspace Pocket, but I guess they finally hit their limit," the Black Hound Leader grumbled. In reality, Lee Sungmin had simply been purchasing supplies through Erebrisa, but the man, unaware of the circumstances, could only assume it was a Subspace Pocket.

"This is perfect. We're going to Devon, too."

"What? Do we have to go in, too?"

"Of course we do, you brat," the Black Hound Leader cursed at his subordinate's reluctance.

*

Having cleared the wall, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon melted into the city's darkness. Finding Aladur immediately was his goal, but Devon was a large city. Worse, the shaman had no fixed location. He was known to wander the streets, laying down a mat wherever he pleased to conduct trivial business like reading faces or palms.

Judging by that alone, Aladur did not seem particularly impressive, but it was not palm or face reading that had earned him his fame as an outstanding shaman.

"He's that good at finding people?"

"To be precise, they say he's good at finding corpses."

The two men entered a shabby inn. They found a spot in the noisy first-floor tavern and ordered drinks to quench their thirst and some simple food.

"Many people die in the forest, but not many are found. Monsters, beasts, Yokai, there are plenty of things in the southern forest that feast on corpses. They say Aladur finds those bodies as if he were a Ghost Spirit."

"Is that shamanism?"

"That's what they say. I don't know the specifics. Maybe he can use this hair to track the Fist Honored One who cast the curse."

"But we don't know where he is."

"We'll find out soon enough."

Aladur was an elusive figure. He was certainly in Devon, but even the local information Guilds could not confirm his whereabouts. Lee Sungmin had already requested information on Aladur's location through Nebul. He did not know where in Devon the shaman might appear, but with a reputation like his, rumors were sure to spread the moment he showed himself.

"We're in disguise, but it's still risky to wander the streets. We shouldn't go outside. We'll just get a room and wait."

"Let's hope it works out," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered, lifting his cup.

The room was shabby, but after growing accustomed to camping for so long, Lee Sungmin had no complaints. He sat on the bed, fiddling with the mask on his face. It was not uncomfortable, but acting while wearing a completely different face gave him a continuous, unpleasant sense of incongruity.

As he toyed with the mask, Lee Sungmin took out his Subspace Pocket. He retrieved the lock of Wiji Hoyeon's hair he had stored deep inside. Neatly bound with a white string, it had not changed at all since the day it was cut, preserved by the near-timeless nature of the Subspace Pocket.

*Where are you now?*

Over a month had passed since he had parted with Wiji Hoyeon and left for the South. He had no way of knowing where she was now. He had tried to track her through Erebrisa but could not confirm her location.

"I hope you're not in pain," Lee Sungmin muttered, gently stroking the hair.

{sep}

Wiji Hoyeon leaned her head against the carriage window.

The carriage had been moving constantly for a month, never stopping in any city or village. There was almost no discomfort. The carriage, enchanted with spatial distortion magic, contained everything needed for living. It was, in effect, a moving house.

The women protecting her, who called themselves the Red Flower, barely spoke to her. Their attitude was businesslike, focused solely on her convenience.

*Where are you?*

Wiji Hoyeon gazed out the window. Unlike Lee Sungmin, she was able to check his location through Erebrisa. While the Red Flower's movements were secretive, his were exposed. The most recent information placed him near Devon in the South. She was relieved to know he had not been attacked by Heaven Beyond Heaven and was still alive and well.

The curse was no longer worsening. The pain and weakness remained, but it grew no more severe. This was partly because the Fist Honored One, who maintained the curse, could not withstand anything more himself.

"Are you anxious?"

A voice spoke.

Wiji Hoyeon turned her gaze from the window and looked forward. Suddenly, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was sitting in the opposite seat. She was not surprised. She had already found herself sitting face-to-face with him like this several times.

"How much longer are we going to be moving?"

"The place I'm at is quite far. Don't be so impatient."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman smiled gently from beneath his half-mask. Was this the fourth time they had spoken? Wiji Hoyeon stared into the man's eyes, visible through the holes in his mask. Through their four conversations, she had come to understand a little about him. He was talkative and frivolous, a demeanor that seemed unbecoming of the man at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

And he held neither favor nor interest in her.

*So why does he keep talking to me? Is it just to kill time?*

Or was he trying to observe something? Wiji Hoyeon looked away with an indifferent expression.

"It seems you're not enjoying our conversation, are you?"

"How could a conversation with someone who dislikes me be enjoyable?"

"Haha! What a strange thing to say. I don't dislike you. I simply don't *like* you."

"I don't see much of a difference."

"No, it's different. If I disliked you, I wouldn't have sent the Red Flower to protect you. Nor would I be bringing you to where I am."

"Then you could have just ignored me."

"I couldn't do that. I've taken a liking to that boy, the Phantom Spear," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled. "You see, I prefer a struggling mediocrity over a calamity who thrives even when left alone."

"…A mediocrity? Lee Sungmin? I don't think so. At first, I thought he was, but in just ten years, he…"

"You don't know about the ten years that boy spent." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman shrugged. He seemed to be enjoying their conversation.

"I do."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman looked out the window.

"And that's why I like him."
