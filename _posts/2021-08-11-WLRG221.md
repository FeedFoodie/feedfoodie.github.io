"Friend."

Contrary to Lee Sungmin's worries, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman did not smack his rear. His usual levity was gone, replaced by a serious expression as he answered.

"Or you could call her a collaborator."

"A collaborator?" Lee Sungmin echoed.

"We are collaborating with each other," the Chairman explained, raising a finger to point at the Ghost Mask in Lee Sungmin's hands. "This one made a promise with Osla a long time ago. In return for fulfilling that promise, Osla agreed to cooperate with this one until it is fulfilled. Making the mask is also part of that cooperation. It's a mask that not even an archmage can create, but for the Fairy Queen, it's not a difficult task. The power of humans means little to a being of a higher class."

"What on earth did you promise a being like the Fairy Queen, Master?"

"I can't tell you about that." The Chairman shrugged. "It's not that I don't trust you. It's just that this promise came with the condition that I keep silent on pain of death." He paused, his eyes narrowing slightly. "You seem to have quite a few questions. Is this curiosity your own? Or does it belong to the great yokai attached to you?"

[A sharp question.]

Void Monarch muttered inside him, and Lee Sungmin answered for them both. "It's both." He was genuinely curious about the relationship between Osla and the Chairman. "Void Monarch said that even when he was alive, he had heard of the Fairy Forest but never knew its exact location."

"That's understandable. This place is secret and mysterious." The Chairman stroked his chin thoughtfully. "The reason this one was able to come here... hmm. I can't say it was a coincidence."

"Then what was it?"

"I was told to come here by a being that transcends humans, and it wasn't Osla. It was a very long time ago."

"A long time ago...?"

"This one has lived for a long time," the Chairman stated plainly. "I learned of this forest's existence about a hundred years ago and came here. This one was alive even when that Yokai attached to you, Void Monarch, was alive."

Lee Sungmin could not hide his shock. If what the Chairman said was true, it meant that he, a human, had already lived for three hundred long years. A hearty laugh escaped the Chairman at Lee Sungmin's expression.

"Why are you so surprised? For a master of this one's level, a human lifespan is already meaningless. Though I say that, this one was a child not yet ten years old when that great yokai met his death, and it's not just this one. The Wudang Sword Immortal and the Martial God of the Outer Heavens have also lived for a similar length of time. Well, that's not what's important. The one who told this one to go to the Fairy Forest and meet Osla was a Dragon."

"What?"

The word left Lee Sungmin's lips before he could stop it, his surprise even greater than before. As far as he knew, Dragons had not shown themselves to the world for three hundred years, yet the Chairman was saying he had heard about the Fairy Forest from a Dragon a hundred years ago, a time when they were thought to be completely absent from the world.

"Why they came to find me, why it had to be me... this one doesn't know such things," the Chairman muttered, his gaze drifting as he looked around the forest. "That Dragon, who appeared suddenly, asked this one to go to Legro Forest and meet the Fairy Realm. There was no particular reason to refuse, and this one was also quite intrigued by the mysterious existence known as the Fairy Realm. That's all there is to it. This one came here because of the Dragon and met Osla. She wasn't wary of this one. It seems things had been arranged beforehand."

He fell silent for a moment, a fond look in his eyes. "This one grew fond of this forest. The Fairy Realm does not lie. There is no malice in their pranks. They are different from humans. So, for a hundred years, I lived here as if in seclusion. When I truly needed to show myself to the world, I received Osla's help."

"...Was appearing before the Dark Supreme also with the queen's help?"

"That's right. What she uses is a mystery that far transcends magic. With Osla's help, this one was able to show my form in a different space while remaining in the Fairy Forest when the situation required it. It's just a clone, though, and can't exert satisfactory power, but that's enough. The weight of the power and name this one has built up over my life can make most people back down with my appearance alone."

It was just as he said. From the moment the Chairman had appeared, the Dark Supreme had harbored a deep fear of him. If even a high-ranking beastfolk of the Transcendent Realm was frightened by his mere presence, it went without saying for those lesser than him.

"I've received Osla's help in many other things as well," the Chairman continued, chuckling as he put his hands back inside his sleeves. "It was with her help that I was able to connect my spirit with the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's branch manager in Ruvus. It's very convenient. There's no need for this one to move personally. It allows this one to keep the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance completely in the palm of my hand while staying here." He looked at Lee Sungmin. "Osla seems to hold quite a bit of favor for you. Honestly, this one doesn't know why she favors you. However, you should know that it is a great stroke of luck. I don't know just how much her favor will permit you, though."

As he finished speaking, the Chairman took out a new martial arts manual and tossed it over. Lee Sungmin caught it and saw it was the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the very technique he had learned from Baek Sogo.

"It was more fun to fix that movement arts than to overhaul the inner arts technique," the Chairman said with a grin. "As I said, this one's Heaven Reversal Black Thunder is a martial art that pursues Extreme Speed. That's why it matched this movement arts quite well."

Lee Sungmin opened the manual. Originally, the Dancing Shadow Soul Break consisted of five movements: Traceless Step, One Step Dancing Shadow, Two Step Calamity, Three Step Fatality, and Four Step Chaos. Now, the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder had been added to all five. That was not all. Two more movement arts had been added after the final step, Four Step Chaos. One of them was Gale Thunder, the very technique the Chairman often demonstrated.

As Lee Sungmin stared intently at the incantations for Gale Thunder, the Chairman grinned.

"Does that one look the best among this one's martial arts?"

Lee Sungmin answered honestly. "Yes." He had not seen all of the Chairman's techniques, but he could not help but admire Gale Thunder every time he witnessed it. It was on a different level from something like teleportation. Gale Thunder was vanishing from space itself and reappearing in another. A martial art of that level could no longer be called a martial art.

"To perform Gale Thunder satisfactorily, you must have a deep understanding of the insights of the Transcendent Realm. The difficulty is on a different level from face-changing, which was simple repetitive training."

"I know."

"What good is just knowing? You have to try it." The Chairman raised his hand and pointed to the mask Lee Sungmin was holding. "And... it's fine not to wear the mask when you're training your martial arts, but otherwise, make sure to keep wearing it."

"I understand."

His internal energy and Demonic Energy would be controlled, but since he could take the mask off during training, there would be no problem. If the magic power of the dormant Dragon Heart inside him could awaken just by wearing the mask, without him having to consciously think about it, that was exactly what he wanted.

The form of his training changed. Lee Sungmin returned to Wiji Hoyeon's hut and devoted himself to mastering the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, now infused with the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. Just as the Chairman had warned, it was incomparably more difficult than the face-changing training. Even with his improved talent, it was impossible to perform the modified movement arts satisfactorily.

He did not despair. He was well aware that he himself was lacking. He had already despaired over such things countless times long ago and had come to accept it, and even though they were training different things, the simple fact that he was training in the same space as Wiji Hoyeon made him happy.

After the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, he received the manual for the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, also infused with the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. The fighting spirit techniques within it were also significantly modified. With that, Lee Sungmin had received everything from the Chairman. The Violet Dawn Divine Art, Dancing Shadow Soul Break, and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Everything from the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, excluding the Demon King of Light's Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, was now incorporated into his three core martial arts.

"One year," the Chairman said as he handed over the final manual. "It doesn't matter if it takes longer, but setting a deadline will probably help you focus more."

"Am I to leave this forest in a year?"

"Only you. This one will not leave the forest. There's no need to."

"Alone... where am I going?"

"The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance," the Chairman answered immediately. "You have become this one's disciple, and this one has passed on the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and all of my insights to you. In one year, you are to go to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, announce that you have become this one's disciple, and be formally recognized as this one's successor."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's expression hardened. Although he was the Chairman's disciple, no one knew of this fact except for the Chairman and Wiji Hoyeon. While staying in Legro Forest, Lee Sungmin had been consistently receiving information about the outside world through Erebrisa. He learned that Baek Sogo had entered Mount Mushii and had not yet shown herself to the world. He learned that Scarlet, the Crimson Archmage, was doing well in Ruvus. He learned that Namgung Heewon, who had left the Namgung Clan, had shaken off his pursuers and was heading towards the Murim Alliance.

What concerned him most were the rumors about himself, the Phantom Spear. It seemed his guise as a C-Rank Mercenary had been effective, as rumors about him were not circulating much. However, if he were to head to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, many things would change. It would become known that the Phantom Spear, who had not been seen for a while, had become the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. If that happened, he might end up clashing head-on with the Murim Alliance.

"What do I do if the Murim Alliance pursues me?"

"That's for you to figure out," the Chairman said dismissively. "This one has given you the background of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. To make that background your own, you must act accordingly, mustn't you?"

"Are you telling me to fight the Murim Alliance alone?"

"That is for you to think about and decide for yourself. This one will speak to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, but it will probably not be an easy journey." A short laugh escaped him. "And... I don't see it being a major problem. Weren't you planning to leave this forest with the Little Heavenly Demon?"

Lee Sungmin slowly nodded. He had no intention of staying in this forest with Wiji Hoyeon forever. When the time came, the two of them would leave this forest together. Wiji Hoyeon seemed to have no intention of joining the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, but she had already agreed to leave with him.

"I've set the deadline at one year," the Chairman added, his tone turning serious, "but if your skills are not satisfactory in this one's eyes, I have no intention of sending you out."

"To what degree do I need to be for you to be satisfied, Master?"

"You need to be strong enough not to get beaten to death somewhere."

At the Chairman's words, Lee Sungmin was not sure what kind of expression he should make. He was being treated like a child, yet he was also a master who had reached the Transcendent Realm. Still, he could not just be dissatisfied. Having become an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Murim Alliance, he had to possess enough martial strength not to be killed by them.

"I understand."

In the end, Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply and replied. One year. He would aim to leave this forest in one year.

As he thought about that time, something suddenly came to his mind.

*...One year from now.*

It was gradually getting closer. In his past life, he had met his death at the age of twenty-seven. Right now, he was twenty-five. If he left this forest in a year, he would be twenty-six.

That Dungeon.

Everything had started from there. At age twenty-seven, when he was a C-Rank Mercenary. The Dungeon he had discovered by chance. He remembered its exact location. He even remembered the exact day. He could not help but remember.

Because it was the day he died.

*I'll have to go there once.*

He remembered the words he heard at the moment of his death. From now on, he could no longer use the Stone of Reincarnation. Even if he found the stone in the Dungeon this time, there would be no returning to the past if he died again.

He knew that, but he still intended to visit that Dungeon.

Because that was where it all began.
