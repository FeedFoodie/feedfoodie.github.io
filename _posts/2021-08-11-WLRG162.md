The Yokai strode forward with movements that were not particularly fast, yet Lee Sungmin could not afford a single rash move. Just facing the remnant of fear, naked and in the form of the Void Monarch, stirred a strange emotion within him.

"Why? Are you overwhelmed because this Elder's thing is so magnificent?" the Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin ignored the taunt, but he could feel the Demonic Energy pooled below his qi center begin to stir. It threatened to boil over simply from being in the presence of the remnant, even without his conscious effort.

"That thing is merely imitating this Elder's form," the Void Monarch muttered, his gaze falling upon Red Ghost's corpse. "Compared to this Elder, it's absurdly weak. However… it seems it was strong enough to kill Red Ghost. What a foolish bastard."

Had Yana of Urmuri not torn out his heart, the result might have been different, but Red Ghost had been far too weak. It was impossible for such a diminished version of himself to overcome a remnant of fear engraved by the Void Monarch, even if it was just a remnant.

*Compared to the Dark Supreme…*

Lee Sungmin tightened his grip on his spear. The strongest opponent he had ever fought was the Dark Supreme. Back then, if the man had been serious about killing him instead of being careless, Lee Sungmin would not have lasted ten seconds. What about now? What if it was the current him, his inner arts explosively increased after consuming the Ten Thousand Year Snow Ginseng and the Human-Faced Spider's inner core?

He did not know the answer. His fight with the Dark Supreme had taught him that the sheer amount of inner arts was not the most important factor in a battle against a martial artist who had entered the Transcendent Realm. Of course, more was always better than less, but in a fight between masters of that level, what truly mattered were things like the mastery of one's techniques, compatibility, and judgment.

"It's coming."

The Void Monarch's warning cut through his stray thoughts. The approaching Yokai's steps quickened, soon becoming a violent sprint.

KWAANG!

The charge began with a single stomp that shattered the ground beneath its foot. Crimson Demonic Energy wrapped around the Yokai's body, making it look like a red cannonball as it hurtled forward.

KWAAANG!

Lee Sungmin met the charge with a thrust of his spear. The weapon, enveloped in the enhanced qi of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, collided with the Demonic Energy, but there was no impact, no struggle for ground. The moment his spear made contact, the Demonic Energy scattered, and at the same time, the Yokai's form vanished. Teleportation? No, it was nothing like that. Lee Sungmin felt a surge of confusion, unable to track the creature that had disappeared completely not only from his sight but also from his senses.

"It is a mysterious phenomena," the Void Monarch spat, his warning following immediately. "Behind!"

At the shout, Lee Sungmin swept his spear in a wide arc without looking back.

KWAREUREUNG!

Violet flames erupted, forming a protective circle centered on him.

*Mysterious phenomena?*

"It's a power that only the greatest of great yokai can use," the Void Monarch explained. "A strange power that cannot be understood by common sense. If you want to fight and survive against the inhuman in the future, remember this. In a fight against one of them, do not think with human standards and criteria."

Lee Sungmin took the advice to heart. Though he had become deeply involved with the inhuman, he still knew very little about them. The shimmering Demonic Energy coalesced out of thin air, reforming the shape of the Yokai. It swung a tightly clenched fist toward Lee Sungmin, who spun his spear and blocked the blow with the shaft.

"……"

He gritted his teeth, enduring the shock that traveled up his arms. The Yokai lowered its fist and raised its foot. It was a simple kick, but its power looked extraordinary. Lee Sungmin flowed away from the attack using his movement arts, flicking the long spear shaft to aim the Changjun at the Yokai's chin.

"......"

With a dull thud, the Yokai's head snapped back. Lee Sungmin immediately lowered his stance and initiated the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The first form was Soul Chasing Elimination.

PPEOEOK!

A straight thrust struck the Yokai's chest. He had wanted to pierce straight through, but the creature's body was tougher than he had thought. He raised the output of his inner arts and unleashed Homing Chain Lightning. Dozens of thrusts hammered the Yokai's body, punching holes through its tough skin and splattering blood. The staggering Yokai clenched its fist and swung it at Lee Sungmin's head. He dodged the blow, twisting his upper body to the side as he unleashed Serpent Soul Devouring. The spearhead shot up from below, simultaneously piercing the Yokai's chin and the crown of its head.

"Not yet," the Void Monarch warned again.

Even with its head impaled, the Yokai's movements did not stop.

PAAANG!

Its body scattered once more, the power of the mysterious phenomena allowing it to escape Lee Sungmin's senses completely.

KURUREUREUNG!

An explosive amount of inner arts flowed into Lee Sungmin's spear as the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art enveloped the entire weapon. He brought out a familiar combination, merging the second form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Bloody Pursuit, with the fourth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Nine Dragons Slaughter.

Everyone watching the battle was captivated. It was a practical fight, lacking in flashiness and faithful only to the raw intent of killing. They had seen Red Ghost, a man who had appeared like a sudden disaster to carry out a massacre, be killed so futilely by this very creature. Because of that, all the martial artists of the Noble Clans were overwhelmed by and terrified of the Yokai.

However, now, the Phantom Spear they had been chasing was fighting that same Yokai without being pushed back in the slightest. In fact, to the eyes of the Noble Clan martial artists, it looked as if he was overwhelming it.

The scattered Demonic Energy took form once more. The Yokai that stood there was without a single wound, despite having had its head pierced moments before. This time, Lee Sungmin did not wait and charged first. His body felt light as his swift spear thrust pierced the Yokai's body. The creature staggered but did not fall. Instead, the moment he pulled the spear out, the wound regenerated as if it had never been there.

An immortal monster. The words of the shaman from Erebrisa echoed in his mind.

"No, there is no such thing as immortality in this world," the Void Monarch declared. "It's just difficult to kill. Unconditional immortality is something established by completely twisting causality. Not returning from death, but never dying at all, is fundamentally impossible."

Even the Void Monarch, who could hunt Dragons, had eventually met his end.

"That thing is this Elder's remnant. It's absurd to think it possesses an immortality that even this Elder could not obtain."

The forest trembled as the Demonic Energy around them began to boil. The Yokai opened its mouth, but it did not speak. It could not. Yokai were born from fear as their mother, but this one was fundamentally different. It was a simple remnant, an existence that did nothing more than carry out long-lingering fear as fear itself. It possessed no reason.

The Yokai spread its arms wide.

KURUREUREUNG!

Dark-colored Demonic Energy rose from the ground and soared above its head. Seeing this, the Void Monarch laughed heartily.

"A faithful reproduction indeed! Except for the size of its junk, that is."

*Why are you so obsessed with the size of its junk?*

"I might've let it slide if they'd made it bigger, but they made it smaller, so of course I'm offended. This Elder was not so pathetic!"

Lee Sungmin could not easily dismiss those words. The greatest difficulty he felt while fighting the naked Yokai was not its strength, but the presence that dangled and swayed with every movement, and that was smaller than the original? He couldn't believe it.

*Don't bullshit me.*

"You brat, do I look like the kind of person who would lie about something like this!"

The Yokai, shouldering the crimson Demonic Energy, raised its arms even higher. The energy that had soared upward churned with the movement of its arms and rushed down to engulf Lee Sungmin. He raised his spear toward the encroaching storm.

"Four hundred years," the Void Monarch muttered as inner arts were injected into the spear. The Orichalcum weapon trembled as it consumed the power. "If you count the era this Elder ruled, it would be a thousand years. Yes, a thousand years. That thing is the remnant of a fear that reigned for that long. Can you win against a thousand years of fear?"

Lee Sungmin did not answer. To him, a thousand years was no big deal.

WHOOSH.

He extended his spear, and a small hole appeared in the surging Demonic Energy.

KIIING!

The flow of demonic power was caught in the spear's trajectory. It was the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Void Path. The annihilated Demonic Energy did not scatter. Lee Sungmin ran along the path his spear had pierced and thrust it toward the Yokai. The creature tried to escape using its bizarre mysterious phenomena, but his thrust was faster.

PUHWAK!

A large hole was torn in the Yokai's body. However, that too began to regenerate immediately. The sight was nothing short of an immortal monster.

However, there is no immortality in this world. The spear continued to move. The instant the flesh regenerated, the spear destroyed it again. The Yokai flailed its arms, trying to push Lee Sungmin away, but he dodged its gestures and repeatedly moved his spear. Its footsteps, powerful enough to shake the forest just by walking, could not make him yield.

Because he felt no fear, Lee Sungmin was not intimidated. His spiritual connection to the Void Monarch prevented his mind from breaking, allowing him to face the Yokai not as an object of terror, but as a simple monster. If he had not possessed that advantage, the fight would have been much more difficult.

The Noble Clan martial artists watching the two fight now fully realized just how strong the Phantom Spear was. In this forest, their perception began to have a more tangible effect. They feared Lee Sungmin more than they feared the Yokai, and this was gradually weakening the remnant of fear. Four hundred years, in the end, was nothing more than the past.

He felt a slight throb in his left eye.

KWADANGTANG!

The Yokai's body tumbled to the ground. It tried to get up, but its severed leg was no longer regenerating. Lee Sungmin approached it without rushing. The Yokai, which had been trying to stagger to its feet, glanced at its non-regenerating leg and gave up the struggle.

"What a strange feeling," the Void Monarch grumbled, clearly not enjoying the sight of his own likeness meeting its end at Lee Sungmin's hands. "To think it dared to imitate my appearance with such pathetic power. That's what I find unpleasant."

Listening to the Void Monarch's words, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with both hands.

PUUK.

He pierced the Yokai's chest, and its body trembled once.

"......"

Then, it scattered like mist. It had not escaped with a mysterious phenomena. It had been annihilated.

The Demonic Energy that had composed the Yokai's body lingered in the area before being guided toward Lee Sungmin, as if it was the natural course of things. He was slightly flustered, but he stopped moving when the Void Monarch told him it was fine to stay still. The Demonic Energy flowed into his body, but it did not fill his qi center. Instead, it flowed to the Void Monarch residing inside the Magic Armor.

"It has merely come where it belongs," the Void Monarch grumbled. "That said, it's quite insignificant. If I had my way, I'd want to swallow all the Demonic Energy in this forest whole…"

He would have done so if it were possible, but the Demonic Energy remaining in this forest had already become a part of the forest itself, so he could not swallow it even if he wanted to.

"So. What do you plan to do now?" the Void Monarch asked.

Lee Sungmin looked back at the surviving martial artists of the Noble Clans. Although he had annihilated the remnant, it was not over yet. The people who had been pursuing him still remained.

"Are you going to kill us?" Namgung Heewon asked.

Jaegal Taeryeong was sitting on the ground, still chewing on his lip, while Moyong Seojin did not know what to do. Tang Ahee was also unable to say a word, her head hung low to hide the cuff of her pants where she had wet herself. The Black Hound Leader and his squad members, part of the Murim Alliance Demon Extermination Unit, seemed to be waiting for Lee Sungmin's answer as well.

"Hyung-nim," Lee Sungmin began.

At his words, Namgung Heewon let out a chuckle. "Are you going to kill me?"

"Why ask something that's impossible in the first place?" Namgung Heewon said with a bitter smile.

Though he was a master who had reached the realm of the Supreme Peak, he had not an ounce of confidence that he could fight Lee Sungmin and win. Perhaps if the other Noble Clan martial artists attacked together something might happen, but he did not think so. Even while fighting that Yokai, Lee Sungmin had seemed to have plenty of leeway. Besides, wasn't the Crazy Heavenly Demon also present?

"If my younger brother will let me go, I'd like to return quietly."

"What is that…!" Jaegal Taeryeong shot to his feet in a fit of rage. "Are you begging a demonic practitioner for your life!"

At the shout, Namgung Heewon let out a deep sigh and turned to look at him.

"……"

"…What?"

"Just shut up, you bastard," Namgung Heewon finally lost his patience and spat.
