Fast.

It was a sensation Lee Sungmin had almost forgotten. During his three years on Mount Mushii, his body had grown accustomed to a profound heaviness. Though the weight existed only in his senses, the restriction had always shackled his movements, slowing him to a crawl. To endure that phantom burden, he had been forced to use every means at his disposal, from raw physical strength to his inner arts and magic. His Dancing Shadow Soul Break had improved at a glacial pace under such conditions, and its highest form, a supreme movement art, had been so sluggish it barely deserved the name.

However, that was no longer the case.

This was not Mount Mushii. The crushing weight that had plagued him was gone. Unleashed in this newfound freedom, the solar eclipse of his art, the Traceless Step, was so swift that it even startled him.

*Don't panic.* Lee Sungmin fought to calm his racing heart and remain composed. It was an unavoidable reaction, he realized. This was his first true battle since descending the mountain.

Before him stood the Ogre, though it was no simple monster. He did not know what kind of deranged mage had tampered with it, but the creature was a Chimera, one with an Ogre as its base. It was a testament to the vile research that pursued the limits of a species, an artificial creation that had come dangerously close to surpassing the natural boundaries of Ogre-kind.

The creature's eyes tracked his every move. Its mouth was sewn shut, robbing it of a threatening roar, but its thick lips trembled, its nostrils flared, and its wide eyes cast a silent threat more potent than any sound. Among bipedal monsters, the Ogre was considered an apex predator.

And such a monster was looking at him with the intent to kill. The gaze itself was violence, a pressure his honed senses read with chilling clarity.

*I'll die.*

*It's trying to kill me.*

*I can't die.*

The thoughts cascaded, one after another, until only a single, absolute conclusion remained. *I can't die.* His body moved in accordance with that resolve. As his hands tightened on his spear, he saw the Ogre's left arm move.

It was a deformed hand, not an Ogre's. It looked as if it had been torn from some other monster and grafted on. He could not identify the beast it belonged to, but its long, sharp claws, like freshly honed blades, lunged straight for him.

"Watch out!" Rudd's warning cry came from behind.

*I know. You don't need to tell me.* Lee Sungmin moved with a calm that defied the situation, his feet sliding straight forward.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break Second Form

One Step Dancing Shadow

A dance of shadows erupted from Lee Sungmin's form. The movement art, which pursued the very extreme of speed, allowed him to vanish with a single step. The Ogre's claws sliced through empty air, failing to find their target.

Closing the distance, Lee Sungmin tightened his grip on the spear. He channeled his power, a technique of 'energy release' he had learned from Wiji Hoyeon, now as natural to him as breathing. He fueled it with the explosive force of his inner arts, and as the Violet Dawn Divine Art circulated, a violet aura enveloped his spear tip. To this, he added the vicious rotation of the Binding Spear.

Rannachal. The 'chal' in its name signified a thrust.

the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear one strike, Soul Chasing Elimination. The spear shot toward the Ogre's chest. The strike landed true, yet the Ogre's body was not pierced. The creature merely stumbled back a few steps as Lee Sungmin felt a jarring resistance travel up the shaft and into his hands.

*It's tough.*

That single strike told him just how durable the Ogre was. He had not expected to fell it in one blow, but he had needed to test its toughness.

*No way. It took that and doesn't even have a scratch…?*

It was Rudd, not Lee Sungmin, who was stunned. From his perspective, the thrust had been perfect. *Could I have blocked it? No, I'd have dodged. Taking an attack with that much rotation and ki head-on would be suicide.*

And yet, the Ogre had withstood it. No matter how powerful a monster it was, that was unbelievable.

*No. It's not a simple Ogre. A Chimera… Is there a Black Mage nearby?*

Rudd frowned, hesitating. The rational choice was to flee. The strength of an Ogre Chimera was an unknown quantity, and there was no reason to risk their lives confronting it. The reward the villagers offered was for a mere Ogre, not nearly enough to justify a fight against a Chimera.

*No. It's already too late to run… damn it. This isn't worth the risk.*

Biting his lower lip, Rudd took a step forward. Now that they had engaged the monster, it was unlikely to let them leave. Their best chance was to join forces and bring it down.

[It's okay.]

Just as Rudd was about to move, Lee Sungmin's voice echoed in his head. Rudd flinched, his eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin's back. The young man did not look back, his posture low as he gripped his spear.

[Are you crazy? That thing's a Chimera, not just some Ogre!]

[I know.]

[The hell you know! You told me you've never even fought an Ogre! I've never even heard of an Ogre Chimera, and if it can shrug off an attack like that, it's as strong as a Twin Head, maybe even stronger!]

Twin Head. An Ogre with two heads. The comparison meant little to Lee Sungmin, who had never fought one of those either.

[I can do it.]

Lee Sungmin's reply was simple and firm. Even as they communicated, the Ogre did not relent. Starvation had made the sewn-mouthed creature impossibly ferocious. It had avoided the village only out of fear of being hunted, but it was not the kind of monster to forgive a human it encountered in the wild, especially not one who had attacked it.

"Krrrrrr!"

A guttural roar tore through its tightly sealed lips. A wave of killing intent so potent it made the hair on his body stand on end washed over Lee Sungmin. His breath caught, not from surprise at the Ogre's roar, but by his own deliberate will.

In that single, held breath, he moved. One Step Dancing Shadow. The shadow danced again, distracting the Ogre's eyes as he drew upon his inner arts with even greater force. The power of the Violet Dawn Divine Art surged through his muscles, supporting his body.

the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear second form.

Homing Chain Lightning.

From the dancing shadow, the spear struck. The thrusts came from all directions, branching like lightning as the technique's name suggested. Each and every strike was imbued with a powerful rotation and his inner arts.

Though the Ogre's tough hide prevented the spear from piercing, the sheer force of the strikes drove it back, step by step. Its face contorted in a silent snarl. Its skin might have been hard, but it was not immune to pain. The Ogre roared from within its sealed mouth and swung its right arm in a wide, devastating arc.

*I can see it.*

However, it was more than that. It looked… slow. No, that was not right. The Ogre was not slow, Lee Sungmin was simply faster. He existed in a world of different speeds, and in his world, the Ogre's attack seemed to crawl.

Its strike possessed enough power to pulverize his body in a single blow, but an attack that did not hit was meaningless. He ducked under the swinging arm, spinning his spear as Wiji Hoyeon's teachings echoed in his mind. *A spear isn't just for thrusting. You have to use the whole weapon.*

The changjun at the end of the shaft, meant to balance the spear, became a weapon itself. *It's still a piece of metal,* he thought. *How could it not hurt to be hit by a piece of metal? Especially one covered in ki?*

KWAANG!

The changjun became a blunt instrument, crashing into the Ogre's ribs. The creature took a sharp, pained breath, its nostrils flaring. He could read the emotion in its eyes.

It was flustered.

On the other end was the spearhead, the fang, and below it, the tassel. *It stops the blood,* he recalled, *and it distracts the enemy's eyes.* The red tassel danced, swaying wildly. *Red draws the eye. It excites the opponent.* In this case, he wanted its focus. The dancing color was an illusion, a change that created an opening.

*Yes. This is the feint.*

*And the real move holds the killing intent.*

KWAAAK!

The spear tip soared high, piercing the Ogre's eye. The creature screamed, a muffled, horrifying sound that escaped from between its sealed lips. No matter how tough its skin, its eyes were vulnerable. Its gaze, which had been chasing the swaying tassel, had completely missed the spearhead that shot out from within the blur of motion.

It had lost its left eye. He tried to drive the spear deeper, but as the Ogre thrashed and swung its claws wildly, Lee Sungmin released the weapon without a moment's hesitation. He used the Dancing Shadow Soul Break to create distance, his hand already reaching for the Subspace Pocket at his waist.

He drew a new spear, gripping it firmly. In that brief moment, the Ogre tore the first spear from its socket and snapped the shaft in two. Blood gushed from its ruined left eye, staining its face red.

Lee Sungmin felt no impatience. He tested the feel of the new spear in his hands and slowly controlled his breathing.

*If I can't kill it in one blow, I just have to keep wounding it.*

And he knew one thing for certain.

A wounded monster was always the most dangerous.

*That guy…*

Rudd could only stare at Lee Sungmin's back, speechless. *Is he really… the Classless who came here just four years ago?* The thought was absurd. He could almost accept someone training for four years and reaching the peak realm. Such prodigies, such calamities, existed in the world.

However, this was different. This was real combat, something that could only be honed through experience, and the boy had claimed this was his first time fighting an Ogre.

*How can he be like that?*

In that moment, Rudd saw more than an eighteen-year-old boy.

He saw the image of a veteran mercenary.
