"Excellent."

Revias lowered her blood-soaked hands. A soft clap cut through the sound of her own ragged breaths.

"From the moment I chose you, the daughter of the Fist Honored One, as my disciple, I never doubted you would become the most outstanding of them all."

Listening to the Moon Queen's words, Revias looked down at the senior sister she had just beaten to death, now sprawled at her feet. She was not the only one. This secret room held fifteen people, including Revias herself, all of them women the Moon Queen had taken in and raised throughout her life. Only a few days ago, they had lived together as martial siblings, laughing as if they were bound by blood.

The past ten years had held great meaning for Revias. She had come to this mountain filled with vengeance after losing her biological father, becoming a disciple of the Moon Queen. Her life at the Moon Palace was meant to be a means to an end, a path to future revenge, but the decade had slowly faded her thirst for it.

This place was tranquil and peaceful. The Moon Palace, perched high in the sky and completely separated from the mortal world, was close to the moon and far from the earth. It was a kingdom of abstinence and restraint, detached from all the conflicts of the world below. Everyone was quiet and considerate of one another, and their master, who rarely showed herself, generously provided her disciples with food, drink, and martial arts to learn.

For ten years, Revias had lived this way. Her senior sisters, who were like family, were all kind to her. None of the Moon Queen's disciples were without their own stories, and together they licked and soothed each other's wounds. Revias had thought she was becoming softer, that she was forgetting her vengeance.

She was wrong.

It was the same for everyone. When their master, who had not shown herself for a decade, suddenly announced she would choose a direct disciple, everything changed. When she declared she would pass down her secret arts only to the strongest, to the one who survived out of the fifteen, they all attacked each other at once. The true natures and individual circumstances they had suppressed within the Moon Palace had only been hidden deep in their hearts, never losing their sharpness.

Revias was no different. In that moment, she knew she was not becoming softer, and she realized she had never forgotten her vengeance. She had merely hidden it away, buried it so deeply that no one else would notice, so that it would not emerge in an unnecessary situation.

And she had survived. She had killed those she had lived with like sisters. Ten years. It was only ten years. Was it not natural to value revenge for her father, with whom she shared a bond of hundreds of years, more than a mere decade of sisterhood?

*You all must have thought the same thing. Your own circumstances, your own stories. You must have tried to kill each other for reasons just like that.* With bitter eyes, Revias flicked the blood from her hands.

"Come here."

The door to the secret room opened, but the Moon Queen was nowhere to be seen beyond it. For ten years, she had not shown herself to her disciples, yet her voice was always heard, guiding them.

Revias followed the voice out of the room. She walked down a long corridor, heading toward the deepest part of the Moon Palace, toward the Moon Queen's bedchamber where no one was allowed. Her hand did not hesitate as she opened the closed door. The door swung open, and she saw an empty room, a small sphere of light floating in the air, and inside it, pieces of flesh writhing.

She stumbled back, trying to flee, but the door slammed shut behind her. The Moon Queen's voice echoed from within the sphere of light.

Come here. Closer.

The flesh twitched with each word. Looking closer, Revias saw that the voice was coming from a fragment of a shattered head. A piece of a jaw, with the lips still attached, was producing the Moon Queen's voice.

"I will give you the power to take your revenge."

Revias's body trembled violently.

Teresa decided to remain in the forest rather than return to the Holy Palace. Still, not wanting to cause worry, she asked Lee Sungmin to deliver a letter for her. In the end, Lee Sungmin rode the Fairy Horse to the Holy Palace in the middle of the night and left Teresa's letter by the sleeping Theos's bedside.

With this, Lee Sungmin had secured a force of his own to stand against The End, or more precisely, against the Vampire Queen and Predator. Baek Sogo, Scarlet, Yana, and Teresa. Their numbers were small, but they formed a group no one could ignore.

And there were more.

His thoughts turned to the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King. If those two were still alive, there was a high probability they would side with him. They would not want everything to end with something like The End, after all. Both were once part of Heaven Beyond Heaven, but they had grown disillusioned with the Martial God's actions and betrayed him. They had survived the fight against the Martial God back then, so they were likely still alive even now, ten years later.

The problem was finding them. Although they had distinct characteristics, even the information obtained through Erebrisa could not pinpoint their locations.

A week passed. During that time, Baek Sogo fully recovered from the poisoning that had weakened her body for a year. Lee Sungmin took his time to check his own condition. Now that his body was complete, it was an undeniable fact that he was stronger than he had been ten years ago.

However, he could go further. There was still room for improvement, and the power he had inherited from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was showing him a clear path.

Body Reformation.

He remembered what the Chairman had told him in the carriage with the Black Dragon Knight.

*Once you ascend to the Transcendent Realm and there's no more room for development in your martial arts and body, you undergo Body Reformation once more. In that process, the body and martial arts that had no room for growth advance to a higher, more transcendent place. At that point, the body essentially surpasses humanity by a vast margin. Thought and action become one, and your insight opens wide, allowing you to perfectly understand most martial arts manuals after reading them just once. Your inner arts never run dry, and the qi center becomes meaningless. The entire body becomes a qi center, so there's no need to draw out inner arts.*

Just in case, Lee Sungmin summoned Nebul and purchased a high-level martial arts manual. He sat down and began to read it, trying to understand it with his insight alone, not by easily learning it with his skill.

"...Hmm."

He could understand it to some extent. The martial prowess he had reached and the theory he had accumulated were among the best in the world. However, that did not mean his insight was exceptional. He had obtained such prowess through immense effort, the events he went through, and the luck that followed, not because of innate talent.

*Insight opening wide, perfectly understanding most martial arts manuals after reading them just once... I can't even begin to imagine what that feels like.*

Having lived his entire life with dull insight, he could not comprehend the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's words at all. How could a person completely understand a martial arts manual just by looking at it once? He had been secretly hopeful, but it seemed his body had not yet reached the level the Chairman had attained. It was disappointing, but he also thought it was a good thing. It meant there was still room for him to grow stronger.

In the meantime, Celgerus completed the spear. The new weapon fit perfectly in Lee Sungmin's hand. When Lee Sungmin mentioned that the temporary spear he was using had been shattered, Celgerus made a dumbfounded expression but did not get as angry as expected. It seemed he was just used to it by now.

"By the way, Senior Sister, what happened to the spear I originally used?"

"...Um... that... it broke," Baek Sogo said with an apologetic expression.

He did not mind. He actually felt it was a relief that it ended with just a broken spear.

When the new spear had grown somewhat familiar in his hands, Lee Sungmin prepared to go out.

He had several questions that demanded answers. To start with, where in the world was Wiji Hoyeon? What were the Demonic Spirit's true intentions? What had Heaven Beyond Heaven been doing for the past ten years? Where was the Martial God, and where was the Spirit Medium?

He planned to tackle them one by one. Fortunately, he had some clues. He knew that the man called the Mercenary King was Heaven Beyond Heaven's Blade Supreme. Since the Mercenary King was not yet dead, investigating him would be the first step to finding out about Heaven Beyond Heaven's current situation.

And then there was the Blood Alliance. The Blood Demon.

The current mystery was this. Back then, at the Vampire Queen's Mansion where he had gone to save Baek Sogo, Gemini had told him quite a lot. Gemini had claimed it was simply to stall for time, but thinking back on it now, there were too many questions. He had informed Lee Sungmin that the Blood Demon of the Blood Alliance was the Vampire Queen's second vampire.

Why would he have said such a thing? In that situation, there was no need for Gemini to say that. They were words that did not need to be said, information that did not need to be revealed. The fact that the Blood Alliance was connected to Predator was not publicly known.

With his current strength, Lee Sungmin could not challenge Jeniela in Trabia. She possessed an unknown level of power, and Trabia was the territory she had inhabited for ages.

[They say even a mutt has the upper hand in its own yard, and Jeniela is no mutt.]

The Void Monarch's analogy was crude, but he was not wrong. Yes, challenging Jeniela in Trabia was, for now, purely theoretical.

The Blood Demon, however, was different. The Blood Demon of the Blood Alliance, Jeniela's second vampire. He did not know how much power was concentrated in the Blood Alliance, but it could not compare to Trabia, where Jeniela herself resided. Lee Sungmin could not kill Jeniela, but what about the Blood Demon?

*I don't know what he was thinking.*

Could Gemini have intended this? He could not help but think so. It was the same when he rescued Baek Sogo. Despite stalling for so long, Gemini had not tried to stop him.

[It might be a trap. Though, as long as Jeniela doesn't step in herself, there are few traps that could stop you.]

Ju Won might be there, the Void Monarch grumbled. Indeed, if Ju Won was at the Blood Alliance, it would be dangerous in many ways.

However, if he were to challenge the Blood Alliance, Lee Sungmin had no intention of going alone. Although the Fairy Horse could barely carry four people, they could just travel on foot if riding it was difficult.

*Not now.*

He had already figured out the location of the Blade Supreme, the Mercenary King. Since the man was such a famous figure, it was not too difficult to pinpoint his location. Moreover, the city where the Mercenary King was located was one Lee Sungmin had visited before.

Kron.

It was the city where the Murim Alliance was located. Ten years ago, he had gone to Kron with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman to beat up the Black Dragon Knight, who was the Murim Alliance Chairman at the time.

It worked out perfectly. He had been planning to visit Kron soon anyway, so he figured he could take care of his other business there while going after the Blade Supreme. His other business was at the Murim Alliance office. He needed to get his hands on one of the Black Dragon Knight's belongings that might be there. With just that, he should be able to find out where the Black Dragon Knight was through a spell like Prauna Aladur.

*It would be great if he's with the Spear King.*

Yana and Baek Sogo approached him, saying they would go to Kron with him, just in case.

Teresa looked like she wanted to go, having never been to Kron before, but he could not take the non-combatant Teresa when he did not know what might happen.

"Don't pout like that."

"There's nothing in this forest, and it's boring," she grumbled.

"You seemed to be having fun with the Fairy Realm."

"That's me playing with *them*."

*That's not quite how it looked.* Lee Sungmin recalled Teresa laughing and chatting with the beings from the Fairy Realm but did not bother to tease her about it. He looked at Scarlet, who was standing with her arms crossed, and bowed his head slightly.

"Well then, we'll be going."

"You must be happy, going out with two women," Scarlet said.

"It's not like we're going out to have fun."

"I'm telling you to go have fun. To put it bluntly, the world could end tomorrow, so you should enjoy yourself while you can."

"You don't enjoy yourself either, Scarlet."

"I need a partner to have fun with. I'm married to magic. Once my research is done, I'll get a divorce and marry a person," Scarlet grumbled, rubbing the dark circles under her eyes with her fingertips.

Teresa, who had been pouting sullenly, spoke up. "Bring me back a souvenir. Something Kron is famous for. What's popular there?"

"Among the souvenirs they sell, the famous one is the Beggars' Gang Dog Beating Stick. After the Martial Beggar became the Murim Alliance Chairman, the beggars of the Beggars' Gang started doing business."

"I don't need a dog-beating stick..."

"Then I'll pick up something suitable."

Lee Sungmin chuckled and mounted the Fairy Horse. Yana and Baek Sogo got on behind him.

"I don't have any good memories of Kron," Baek Sogo muttered with a bitter smile.

Lee Sungmin turned his head slightly at the words coming from behind him. "Is that so?"

"Yeah. When I had to leave my Junior Disciple behind in the Dungeon... the place I teleported to with the scroll was Kron. I ran right out of there after that and have never been back."

It had already been over ten years. Baek Sogo giggled and held onto Lee Sungmin's waist.

"I wonder if I'll make some good memories this time?" she murmured in a small voice.

Before he could answer her, the Fairy Horse leaped through space.
