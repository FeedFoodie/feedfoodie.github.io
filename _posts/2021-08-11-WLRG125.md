Rings of enhanced qi stormed into existence, linking together and multiplying into dozens before merging into a single, colossal vortex. A typhoon of fiercely rotating rings swept through the area. Standing at its heart with both hands raised, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had a single, sinking thought. *Oops*.

*I didn't mean to kill him…*

He had only wanted an enjoyable fight. While their exchange had been so powerful that a mere graze was anything but, he had maintained his composure, ready to withdraw his killing moves before they became fatal.

However, that moment had passed. It was too late. He had unleashed the Blood Ring Typhoon, the ultimate technique of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, and this storm of death would surely tear Lee Sungmin to shreds. It would reduce him to hundreds of pieces of flesh, just as it had every other time it had been unleashed.

*What a waste. That martial art… it's too much of a waste to kill him like this. If only ten more years had passed, he would have become incomparably stronger than he is now…*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's lamentation was cut short. A sharp killing intent shot out from within the raging gale, a sensation too real to be an illusion.

PWAK!

A straight path opened as a spear pierced through the storm's edge. Void Path, the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, had breached the Blood Ring Typhoon's outer wall.

*Impossible!*

Startled, the Crazy Heavenly Demon gestured, and the gale intensified, rushing to fill the pierced section. The straight path created by Void Path did not close immediately, however, and through that opening, Nine Dragons Slaughter erupted. The nine dragons could not penetrate the typhoon head-on.

Before the gap sealed itself, the Crazy Heavenly Demon caught a glimpse of Lee Sungmin. He stood within the madman's wind, a tempest that seemed ready to collapse at any moment, his spear fully extended. His breath came in ragged gasps, his eyes cold and sunken. Those same eyes, which had seemed to look at nothing, now stared directly at him.

"…Haha!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon laughed, and with his laughter, the Blood Ring Typhoon exploded. Scattered red qi spread in all directions like a mist, staining the sky crimson. He gazed at it for a quiet moment before lowering his eyes.

Not far away, Lee Sungmin lay collapsed. Despite taking the full force of the Blood Ring Typhoon, his death trap armor was intact. Still, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had been certain the sheer power of the enhanced qi art would have ruptured his internal organs, even with the armor's protection.

"I've killed a promising one," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered as he approached.

Though he had started the fight out of interest and greed, a sliver of regret pricked at him for ending such a talent. There was no bringing back the dead, but he wanted to at least recover the body.

He had taken only a few steps when Lee Sungmin's body twitched violently. *Is he alive?* The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes twitched. The trembling subsided, and then, suddenly, Lee Sungmin shot to his feet.

"What in the…"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's jaw dropped. Not a single wound after taking the Blood Ring Typhoon head-on? It wasn't long before he realized that the strange sense of unease he had been feeling had suddenly intensified.

Lee Sungmin opened his mouth, making strange sounds. *Ah, ah.* Then he whipped his head around. The Crazy Heavenly Demon stared wide-eyed into Lee Sungmin's eyes. They were different, so very different. *Is this the same person I was just fighting?* Could those eyes, which looked as if entire blazing flames had been embedded within them, truly belong to a human?

"…Ugh…"

Something stirred in his chest. His mind went dizzy, and he staggered backward, his heart pounding violently as the hair on his body stood on end. He was bewildered, unable to identify the emotion gripping him.

"You insolent bastard…" Lee Sungmin spat, chewing on his lip.

The voice was Lee Sungmin's, but the words were not his own. The Void Monarch had taken over the unconscious boy's body. As if to prove it, an ominous, violet Demonic Energy writhed and leaked from the Magic Armor.

"I have many things to ask you," the Void Monarch declared.

Drenched in a cold sweat, the Crazy Heavenly Demon glared at the being occupying Lee Sungmin's body. The Void Monarch offered no explanation, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon knew instinctively that this was a completely different person. The Void Monarch strode toward him, glanced at the spear in his right hand, and then set it down without hesitation. He did not use a spear.

Demonic Energy erupted from the Void Monarch's entire body, far more ominous and incomparably more violent than the Protective Qi Aura Lee Sungmin used. The face of the backpedaling Crazy Heavenly Demon contorted. He could not understand what was happening, but he was enraged by the emotion he now felt.

"You bastard!" he shouted and ran forward.

The Void Monarch, who had been striding forward empty-handed, slowly raised his right hand.

KUOOOO!

An immense Demonic Energy coiled around it. It was a Demonic Energy that Lee Sungmin's body had been unable to handle, but that was no longer an issue. With the Void Monarch in control, the power flowed according to his will.

With a roar, the Crazy Heavenly Demon thrust out both palms, unleashing the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art to its peak and creating a crimson storm. The Void Monarch sneered at the raging tempest of enhanced qi and swung his right hand. It looked like a casual swing, devoid of technique, yet the Demonic Energy coiling around his hand stretched out viscously and crushed the storm entirely.

"Heok!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon screamed at the sight, but he did not back down. Eyes wide, he continuously unleashed his martial arts, choosing the very technique that had defeated Lee Sungmin, the Blood Ring Typhoon. Crimson enhanced qi surged and rose, forming ring after ring until it became a massive vortex.

The Void Monarch strode toward the menacing typhoon. The Demonic Energy covering his body shot up like smoke and expanded greatly. He threw his tightly clenched right fist forward once, and the Blood Ring Typhoon shattered. The Void Monarch ran toward the gawking Crazy Heavenly Demon, leaping in front of him in an instant and grabbing him by the collar.

KWAAANG!

He slammed the man's body into the ground.

"Where did you get it?" the Void Monarch growled, pinning the man.

"Keoheuk!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon struggled, trying to hold onto his fading consciousness, but the Void Monarch did not let him go.

"The power you use. My Demonic Energy. Where in the world did you obtain it?"

"What… are you saying…"

"Don't pretend you don't know!" the Void Monarch roared.

At the same time, the Demonic Energy writhing in the hand that held the Crazy Heavenly Demon was sucked into the man's body. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's mouth fell open. An ordinary human could never withstand such an intrusion. A human's body was fundamentally different from a Yokai's, and a Yokai's Demonic Energy was like its blood. Receiving the wrong type could be fatal.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon, however, was different. He did not die, despite feeling a pain like his entire body was being torn apart. He opened his mouth wide in a silent scream. The Void Monarch held the struggling man and continued to push Demonic Energy into him until something deep within the Crazy Heavenly Demon's qi center reacted.

"Kkeueueu…!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes rolled back, the whites becoming soaked in a crimson light. The essence of Demonic Energy deep within his qi center, awakened by the Void Monarch's own, was the source of his qi deviation and lunacy. The madness that had driven him nonstop all the way to the distant North Trabia was a direct result of the Void Monarch's presence heading to this very place.

"So you were this guy's nobleman," the Void Monarch uttered in disbelief.

The madness-soaked man had always gone on rampages, slaughtering everything around him, but not now. Despite having surrendered his mind, he was staring at the being who had taken over Lee Sungmin's body. The Void Monarch looked into those eyes and let out a hollow laugh.

"What in the world happened…"

The Void Monarch's confused mutterings came to an abrupt halt as a hand shot up and grabbed the neck. The hand was not his, but Lee Sungmin's. The Void Monarch frowned, feeling the brutal force of the grip tightening.

*This crazy bastard…!*

It was Lee Sungmin. Having regained consciousness, he was trying to reclaim his body. The Void Monarch could not help but be flustered. He knew the boy's divine power was much stronger than that of other humans, but he never imagined it could exert influence even when he had complete control.

"No, this Elder wasn't trying to steal your body. The situation was what it was, so I had no choice…" the Void Monarch spoke quickly, but Lee Sungmin was not listening.

The hand choking the neck grew stronger, drawing out enough force to seemingly snap its own neck.

"I get it. I get it!" the Void Monarch finally relented and withdrew.

The Demonic Energy coiling around the body was sucked back into the Magic Armor, and Lee Sungmin's blazing red eyes returned to their original color.

"Heok!"

Lee Sungmin lowered the hand from his neck and gasped for breath. The sensation in his body felt intermittent, and his entire frame ached. On top of that, his breathing was labored from having been squeezed so tightly. Gasping for air, he felt a dizzying headache and collapsed to the ground.

[You son of a bitch… you don't even recognize your savior!]

"I know," Lee Sungmin replied, rubbing his sore neck.

He knew the Void Monarch had helped him again. Piercing that tiny gap in the Blood Ring Typhoon had been his own doing, but when the storm exploded, the Void Monarch had brought out Demonic Energy to protect his body. That was the only reason he had merely lost consciousness. If he had taken that powerful storm head-on, he would have been blown to pieces.

"That's that… and this is this," Lee Sungmin answered as he sat slumped on the ground.

He had survived thanks to the Void Monarch, but he could not forgive him for arbitrarily taking over his body. It was just that… Lee Sungmin looked at his own hands with bitter eyes. He had regained consciousness when the Void Monarch shattered the Blood Ring Typhoon with a single gesture. It was his own body that had accomplished it, but the method was on a level he could never hope to imitate. That intense Demonic Energy, the technique of forcing power into the infinitesimally brief moment the rings connected to forcibly sever the link…

The Void Monarch, sympathizing with his emotions, sneered.

[If you become a Yokai, you can do it too.]

Lee Sungmin ignored him. He sighed and glanced at the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was not on a rampage but lying unconscious, his mind consumed by the triggered Demonic Energy.

"…What is he?"

[This Elder doesn't know either, but what that bastard is using is undoubtedly my Demonic Energy. Deep in his qi center… lies its essence. I thought a part of my power was extinguished when I was Sealed 400 years ago, but it seems that wasn't the case.]

The Void Monarch fell silent for a moment.

[In this Elder's opinion, that mad bastard seems to be the nobleman you were meant to meet in the North. He's a human, yet he wields Demonic Energy. It seems his mind goes haywire sometimes as a side effect… but even so, to wield Demonic Energy while remaining human is an incredible feat.]

Lee Sungmin listened silently as the Void Monarch continued.

[I don't know how that mad bastard does it, but if you learn his method, you'll be able to use it far better than he can. You'll be able to use Demonic Energy without becoming a Yokai, and unlike that madman who only has a part of my power, you are directly connected to this Elder.]

"…Ugh…"

Just as the Void Monarch finished, the Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a low groan.

[First, let's hear what he has to say.]

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes snapped open.
