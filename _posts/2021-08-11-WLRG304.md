Lee Sungmin could not believe what he was hearing. He struggled to comprehend why Kim Jonghyun would make such an offer, especially now.

*A colleague? Me, but why?*

The questions tumbled through his mind, one after another.

Kim Jonghyun's gaze drifted down to the untouched feast spread across the table. "Are you bewildered?" he continued.

"Of course I am."

At Lee Sungmin's reply, Kim Jonghyun simply shrugged. "I'm not just saying this because we're from the same place. Ah, or maybe… are you reluctant to become my colleague because I've killed tens of thousands of people?" He smiled, a chillingly casual expression. "It couldn't be helped, though."

A profound sense of alienation washed over Lee Sungmin as he watched Kim Jonghyun smile as if it were nothing. He had lived a long time and met all sorts of people, but never anyone like this. Not dozens, not hundreds. Tens of thousands had died. It was not a war, and there was no justifiable reason for their deaths, yet here was Kim Jonghyun, smiling and dismissing his own massacre as something that 'couldn't be helped'.

*[He's not human,]* the Void Monarch muttered. Kim Jonghyun's way of thinking was vastly different from that of most humans. Even Sungmin, whose own mind had grown unstable after so long and so many experiences, was nothing like him.

"Why do you want me as a colleague?"

"Because I believe our interests align, to some extent," Kim Jonghyun said, spreading his hands. "However, it's too early to speak of it now. I don't know what your purpose is."

"To stop it," Sungmin answered.

A strange light gleamed in Kim Jonghyun's eyes. "The End?"

"And you."

"That's why our interests align," Kim Jonghyun said with a laugh. He waved a hand lightly. "The food will get cold." A warm current of air enveloped the table, reheating the slightly cooled dishes. "I also aim to stop The End. In that regard, our interests are perfectly aligned. There's no harm in becoming colleagues and lending each other our strength, is there?"

"You, who claim to stop The End, have killed tens of thousands, and the Vampire Queen told me you are the first calamity of The End."

"Do you believe her words?" Kim Jonghyun countered. "She's a monster who has lived for nearly a thousand years. Do you know what kind of future she sees and desires? A future where the Slaughter Predator appears would be The End, wouldn't it? So why would she call me the first calamity? Anyone who wants to stop The End would naturally try to kill me after hearing that, and she should know it."

"Because your death wouldn't change the future she desires."

Kim Jonghyun scoffed at the answer. Well, that was true. He and Jeniela were not allies. Their desires and goals were different.

*Was I an obstacle to Jeniela?* Because he was an obstacle? Or because he was unnecessary? Either way, joining forces with her was impossible. He stroked his chin, realizing Jeniela had intended for him to act this way all along. Of course, he had already decided to deal with Vollander before she even mentioned him.

*When I failed the ritual and was unstable, Jeniela protected me. Yes… at that point, I was necessary for the future she desired. Meeting Vollander was necessary. Causing these incidents starting from the South must have been necessary for her too.*

Did that mean he was no longer needed? No, that was not her personality. If he had truly become an obstacle to her desires, she would have stepped forward and killed him herself, yet Jeniela remained in the North, inactive.

*You came here because she told you about me. The reason she bothered… huhu, I see. It must mean that no matter what I do, I cannot disrupt the future she is heading towards.*

A faint smile touched Kim Jonghyun's lips. "Do you know what I intend to do?"

"I don't."

"In the North, I tried to kill seven thousand people to turn myself into a Demon Lord."

"I know you failed, and that the failure benefited you."

"Jeniela told you more than I thought," Kim Jonghyun let out a low exclamation. Jeniela had not told him such things, but he now knew well that the ritual's failure had ultimately been to his benefit. At the threshold of the gate to the Demon Realm he had opened in the southern sea, he had heard many stories from the masters within.

"What do you know about this world?" Kim Jonghyun asked. "Eria. The reason it summons Otherworlders. The 'status window' is a privilege given only to us. It allows us to grow comfortably and easily, to conveniently learn skills like martial arts and magic."

"...What are you trying to say?"

Kim Jonghyun raised a finger and pointed at the ceiling. "This world is a giant research area, and a Slaughterhouse." He smiled. "Someone outside this world is getting what they want through us. The countless people living in this vast world, the coexistence of martial arts and magic, the moderately controlled technology that develops through them… Who? Why? For what purpose?"

His laughter grew a little louder. "This world is a mix of all sorts of techniques. Even martial arts with the same name have different contents, and the same goes for magic. The interesting thing is, they are ultimately similar in the grand scheme but very slightly different in the details. Over long ages, they have been supplemented and developed, becoming close to perfection. Yes, this world is such a place. The people who live here dedicate their entire lives to perfecting techniques, and we, through the status window, easily learn them, develop them further, and make them advance."

Lee Sungmin listened in silence as Kim Jonghyun's words hung in the air.

"That's why this world is a Slaughterhouse and a research area," Kim Jonghyun continued. "We are the guinea pigs inside it, and The End? The End is a culling. It's closing the Slaughterhouse, shutting down the research area. Once they've gotten what they want, there's no longer any meaning in maintaining this world, and after that? They just open it again."

Slaughterhouse. Research area. The End. Culling. The words created a huge storm in Lee Sungmin's heart. It was not an easy story to accept, yet the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and Abel had shared a similar one in the past.

"Stopping it is impossible," Kim Jonghyun stated. "Because the world itself is made that way. However."

"...Are you saying you have a way?"

Kim Jonghyun smiled brightly. "I'm going to connect this world to the Demon Realm."

"...What?"

"That will solve The End," he said, his voice excited. "This world cannot escape The End because it was created based on that fate, but what if we connect Eria to the Demon Realm? Then the fate Eria holds will not be unconditionally fulfilled. Just as this world has the causality of The End, so does the Demon Realm."

He explained that Eria summons people from all sorts of worlds, but there was one it never touched. "The Great Demon World. A Demon Realm in a single dimension usually has one Demon Lord, but the Great Demon World is different. It's a place where countless powerful monarchs and a true Demon God who rules over them live. No matter how strong Eria's fate of The End is, if it's connected to the Great Demon World, the causality will become entangled. The End will not arrive."

"Crazy," Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh. "What's a Demon Realm, and what's a Great Demon World?"

Kim Jonghyun tilted his head. "Headquarters and a subsidiary? It might be easier to understand it that way. The Great Demon World is ruled by a Demon God, and under him are powerful Demon Races and monarchs. Demon Lords and Demon Races dispatched from there to other dimensions create a Demon Realm in the dimensional rift and rule over it. Their ultimate goal is to invade a dimension and turn it into a complete colony."

However, Eria had no Demon Realm. Kim Jonghyun picked up his wine, swirled it, and raised the glass toward Lee Sungmin. "Would you like a drink?"

"No, thank you."

"Hmm. It's quite a good wine… well, that's not important. Anyway, Eria has no Demon Realm, but a connection to it still exists through Black Mages and the Undead. I will fully expand that connection and completely link this world with the Great Demon World."

"And after that?"

"The world will not be destroyed," Kim Jonghyun said as he poured wine into his glass.

"...If it connects with the Demon Realm, what will happen to this world?"

"Well? We'll only know when it happens. It'll probably become an interesting world. Demon Races will freely come and go, and perhaps the monarchs themselves will step in. To them, this world is a vast land with no ruler. They might even compete to see who will rule it. Many people might die, but, haha. In the end, it won't face The End, so it's not a big problem, is it?"

"Not a big problem?"

He was wrong. Lee Sungmin now had a firm grasp on the man named Kim Jonghyun. No, Kim Jonghyun could not be called human. He was not incomplete like Sungmin was. He had been complete from the very beginning, complete with that kind of detachment. He knew full well what consequences his actions would bring. Connecting the Great Demon World and Eria would not stop The End, it would just unleash a catastrophe of equal measure. A massacre would occur that would make Kim Jonghyun's own deeds seem trivial, as the ferocious monarchs and Demon Races of the Great Demon World roamed free.

"He's insane." What Kim Jonghyun intended was not to stop The End, but to bring a new cataclysm to this world.

"Do you think I'm not right?" Kim Jonghyun asked. "Why do you think I'm telling you this? Why am I asking *you*, of all people, to be my colleague? Why isn't the Apostle of the End intervening while we have this conversation?"

Lee Sungmin could not answer.

"The Apostle of the End is a system that maintains this world, a system to control the guinea pigs of the research area so they don't learn too much. The truth of this world is permitted only to a very few… transcendent beings of a higher status than humans. Any guinea pig that learns the truth is disposed of."

Still,, right now, the Apostle of the End was not intervening.

"I failed to become a complete Demon Lord, but that doesn't mean what I did was meaningless. Because I succeeded in becoming a half-Demon Lord, I was able to learn the truth of this world, and you… I don't know why, but you've also obtained a status similar to mine. In other words, you've become able to know the truth of this world."

*[It's the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman,]* the Void Monarch said. *[Inheriting your Master's power is helpful in this way, too. In his final moments, he vastly transcended humanity. He reached a power not permitted in this world. You inherited the power of that Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Even if you can't wield that power yourself, you've gained enough potential to obtain a transcendent status.]*

"This world has no future," Kim Jonghyun continued. "All beings living here, except for those like the Fairy Queen, the Spirit Queen, and gods, can never escape the Cycle of Mortality. Especially humans." He closed his eyes as he brought the wine glass to his lips. After taking a sip, he went on. "Humans in this world have had most of their potential restricted. If they overcome that restriction and vastly transcend humanity, their transcendence is stripped away by the master of the Slaughterhouse."

Ah.

Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled slightly. He remembered the fight between the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Moon Queen, and the Martial God. His master, who had been overwhelmingly dominating the fight, had lost his power from a certain point. The Void Monarch had a similar thought, recalling his own death. How could he have been hunted down by a mere Dragon?

"In the end, no matter how strong a human in this world is, they are still human, so they are bound to be powerless against beings of a higher status. However, if you try what I did, reversing your status itself to become a Demon Lord… and firmly grasp transcendence, you will be exiled by the laws of this world."

If Kim Jonghyun had become a complete Demon Lord, he would have been exiled. What about the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman? No, in his final moments, he had chosen to leave this world himself. His case was different.

"What meaning is there in such a world?" Kim Jonghyun asked. "Inside this Slaughterhouse, humans whose potential has been restricted can only act as guinea pigs and be culled when the time for culling comes."

He stood up. He approached Lee Sungmin and held out his hand.

"If we connect to the Demon Realm, The End will not come. There's no guarantee that we'll regain the human potential restricted by the laws of this world, but The End will not come."

Lee Sungmin looked down at the offered hand. After a moment of silence, he asked Kim Jonghyun, "Are you human?"

Kim Jonghyun laughed. "Haha, what a strange question. I'm not human. I'm closer to a Demon Lord."

That answer was enough.
