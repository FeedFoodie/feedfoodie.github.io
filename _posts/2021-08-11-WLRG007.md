Lee Sungmin reached the forest's entrance and shrugged the large bag from his back. It was a simple kindness from the innkeeper, Jack, an ordinary sack with no spatial distortion magic, but he had packed it with everything he would need.

*I didn't think I'd be fighting an Orc so soon.*

He had planned to wait, to let his body grow and put on some muscle before he fought anything. At only fourteen years old, Lee Sungmin's body was still frail, his inner arts insufficient and his physical strength underdeveloped.

Calluses had formed on his palms, it was true, but two weeks of strength training had not built any significant muscle. He had only gained enough to handle the Soul Chasing Spear Art to some extent.

the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art 2nd Star. Soul Chasing Spear Art 4th Star. One Thunder Movement Arts 2nd Star. iron skin and strong bones 2nd Star. Stone Breaking Fist 2nd Star.

This was the current state of his martial arts. The Soul Chasing Spear Art, a Second-Rate martial art, had progressed quickly thanks to the experience from his past life, yet without sufficient inner power and physical strength, he could not unleash its true power. His external arts, iron skin and strong bones and Stone Breaking Fist, had barely improved because his body was still weak, and his lack of inner arts prevented him from properly performing the One Thunder Movement Arts.

He was not ready. He had intended to train for at least half a year before ever attempting to fight an Orc. Having died once already, Lee Sungmin knew all too well how suddenly, absurdly, and emptily death could arrive.

He knew because he had died before, and he did not want to die again. That was why he had resolved to prepare thoroughly, without rushing. Dying to an Orc would be an utterly pointless end.

*Still. It's a chance to get a Subspace Pocket for free.*

A Subspace Pocket worth 600,000 Eru was an item he found hard to give up.

*And I wouldn't have to worry about the items inside getting broken.*

Lee Sungmin pulled a glass bottle from his bag. After shaking it a few times, he uncorked the stopper sealing its mouth.

A sour smell wafted up. It was a poison he had improved himself, using the Goblins' own concoction as a base and adding poisonous herbs he knew he could find in this forest, all based on the experience from his past life.

The poison still paralyzed its target, but its duration and immediate effect were different. If it entered the bloodstream, this paralytic poison could render an entire body immobile in less than five minutes.

*It works on Orcs, too.*

He knew this well from his past life. In times when he lacked strength, he had been forced to survive by using such tricks. Considering himself fortunate to have learned a little about poisons, Lee Sungmin began to apply the viscous liquid to his weapons.

He meticulously coated the entire blade of five throwing daggers, one dagger, and one spear.

With that, his general preparations were complete. Lee Sungmin steeled his resolve and rose to his feet.

He had already scouted the Orcs' territory. For the past two weeks, he had not just been diligently using trash skills on Goblins, rabbits, and wild boars. Information was vital.

*I've figured out their patrol route, but I still have to be careful.*

Five Orcs. For the current Lee Sungmin, they were by no means an easy opponent. He had wondered if it would be better to recruit some companions and join forces.

It was not a thought he had dismissed lightly.

*If I get companions, I'll have to share the reward.*

There was no guarantee that Hans would keep his mouth shut about the Subspace Pocket. While joining forces might make it possible to take down the five Orcs, his companions would surely raise hell about the personal reward Lee Sungmin was set to receive.

Tens, even hundreds of Otherworlders came and went through this forest each day, and they generally tried to avoid one another.

The reason was simple distrust.

Lee Sungmin was no different. A bond was a nice word, but just because you thought of it as a bond did not guarantee the other person would feel the same. There was no promise that the kindness you showed someone would be returned in kind.

He could not trust anyone, especially not now, while he was still weak.

"CHWIIK!"

An Orc lumbered by, its equipment shoddy. It carried a chipped handaxe and wore no armor, its body only roughly wrapped in ragged cloth. While Orcs from other regions sometimes wore decent gear, the Orcs of Zenavis were not so well-trained.

Still, they were creatures that competed with the likes of Goblins. Their intelligence was low, and they had nothing but physical toughness.

Nevertheless, they were dangerous. Their low intelligence made them more instinctual, their senses sharper than a Goblin's, and their bodies stronger. Just as he had done to lure the Goblins, Lee Sungmin had generously spread his scent in a suitable ambush spot.

This time, however, he had not made a wound. An Orc's sense of smell was far sharper than a Goblin's. If he needlessly drew blood, they might catch the scent and discover him.

A surprise attack. That was the method Lee Sungmin had chosen.

If he had more time, he would have prepared a trap, but he had no such luxury. In the first place, there were no impressive traps he could prepare with this body anyway.

He had considered various methods, but a surprise attack remained optimal. Lee Sungmin held his breath. An attack from above would not work.

He had a paralytic poison stronger than the Goblins' and the poison darts they used, but an Orc's hide was not thin enough to be pierced by a dart shot from the mouth.

For that same reason, throwing a dagger would not work either. Even if he combined his current physical strength and inner arts, he was not certain he could pierce their hide, even if he threw a dagger with all his might.

"A smell. There's a smell."

There were five Orcs on the outer patrol, the exact number Hans had requested. Hans probably knew that Lee Sungmin would target this group. As a man who made his living scavenging from corpses in the forest, he would surely know the patrol routes of the Orcs and Goblins.

*Five of them… I'd like to split them up if I could, but it won't be that easy.*

The Orcs bent their upper bodies, sniffing with their large noses at the spot where Lee Sungmin had pissed.

"......"

No, not yet.

Lee Sungmin lay prone on the ground like an insect. He had smeared dirt all over his body and even dug a shallow trench to press himself into. He minimized his breathing. In the middle of his dirt-caked face, only his wide-open eyes gleamed, waiting for the right moment.

"It's a human smell."

"A human. Where is it?"

"There's no body. No blood either..."

As the Orcs muttered, the time came. Lee Sungmin hurled the stone he was gripping tightly in his hand. The stone flew in an arc and landed behind the Orcs, who turned with a start at the sound.

The instant their heads turned, Lee Sungmin shot forward. He swallowed a deep breath instead of shouting a kiai and, gripping a dagger instead of his spear, surged forward with the One Thunder Movement Arts. He plunged the dagger into the back of the nearest Orc.

"CHWIIIIK!"

The Orc screamed as the blade sank deep, the force of the blow making Lee Sungmin's wrist ache. Blood splattered, but it was not a fatal wound. The dagger had barely managed to pierce the thick hide.

Lee Sungmin let go of the dagger without hesitation, not even trying to pull it out. The poison had seeped in. The toxin slathered on the blade would now spread throughout the Orc's body with its circulating blood.

*Four left.*

"Human!"

"Kill it!"

The Orcs roared and charged recklessly. They simply ran forward without any tactics, but the impact of their hideous faces and burly frames was tremendous.

"HUUUK!"

Lee Sungmin exhaled sharply and drew two throwing daggers. He unleashed the One Thunder Movement Arts. PAK! His body leaped to the side, dodging an axe that swung down at his head. He twisted his waist sharply.

KWAAAK! The dagger he swung wildly lodged in the Orc's shoulder. He was not aiming for a fatal blow. A simple wound was enough. Leaving the dagger embedded, Lee Sungmin kept moving.

"You rat!" the Orc roared, swinging its axe in a wide arc.

Even without inner power, an Orc's physical strength surpassed that of a Saint. The chipped blade would not cut him if it hit, it would shatter his bones. At his current level of external arts, he could not withstand even a single blow.

*I can't let it hit me…!*

Lee Sungmin was desperate. Three Orcs still remained, each with enough strength to tear a fourteen-year-old boy limb from limb. Feeling a chill as an axe grazed his chest, he immediately rolled on the ground.

TUMBLE. As he rolled, Lee Sungmin switched the dagger from his left hand to his right. He shot to his feet and threw it at the Orc.

"KREUK!"

He had not imbued it with inner power. The Orc looked slightly surprised but swung its axe and blocked the dagger. That was enough. Lee Sungmin quickly drew his spear and executed One Strike One Kill. PAAAAK! His spear pierced the Orc's now undefended chest.

"KURK!"

Blood spurted from the Orc's mouth. Lee Sungmin gripped the spear with both hands and pulled, but it came free with an unpleasant sensation. No, it would have been better to let it go. A nearby Orc roared and brought its axe down on his head.

"Keuk!"

Lee Sungmin hurriedly raised the spear shaft to block. PAAAAK! The shaft, struck by the axe, snapped in two.

"You son of a bitch!" Lee Sungmin roared, lamenting his broken spear.

Even as he shouted, he continued his attack.

Soul Chasing Spear Art Second Form, Countercurrent Kill. The spear tip, which had been pointing down, shot upwards. Though the shaft was broken, Lee Sungmin used it to create distance, swinging it from below like a sword. PAAAK! The chin of the Orc, too slow to retreat, was grazed by the spear.

*I already used the poison once. It's not enough…!*

This was the spear that had already been stuck in an Orc's body. There was not enough poison left on it. Lee Sungmin tightly gripped the broken shaft in his left hand. The axe had been dull, leaving the broken end rough and jagged. That was fortunate for him.

"KERK!"

With all his strength, he jammed the spear shaft into the Orc's neck. The creature's mouth fell wide open. There was still one left.

Though he was tired, dealing with a single Orc was not that difficult. Lee Sungmin panted heavily as he looked around. Four Orcs, afflicted by the paralytic poison, were twitching on the ground. The Orc with the spear shaft in its neck was dead.

"...I'm exhausted."

KAAK, PTUI! Lee Sungmin grumbled as he spat out a thick glob of saliva. This was a hell of a job, fighting an opponent that required martial arts theory when he was lacking in so many ways.

"...Still."

A swell of pride rose in his chest. He had taken down five Orcs. In his past life, the Lee Sungmin of this age could never have imagined doing such a thing. A pleased smile touched his lips as he approached the twitching Orcs.

It was time to gouge out their eyeballs.
