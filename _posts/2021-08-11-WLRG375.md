Through Gries, Lloyd perceived the incident. The artifact allowed its owner to know what had happened and was happening in the world without ever seeing it directly or being at the scene, but such knowledge came at a price. It demanded a tribute of lifespan, a currency that Lloyd, much like his predecessor Abel, held in little regard.

The foundation of Lloyd's magic had been laid by his master, Envirus, the Golden Magic Tower Master, yet the Lloyd of today was more heavily influenced by Abel, despite the incomparably shorter time they had spent together. Having become the owner of Gries after Abel's passing, Lloyd had been continuously offering his life force to wield its power ever since he obtained it.

His fingertips caressed the artifact resting on his lap. "It will be tomorrow night at the latest," he announced. Gries hummed in response, emitting a soft light. "By tomorrow night, the Spirit Queen will Advent in this forest."

"Still, that's a relief," Scarlet muttered, her gaze fixed on her gloves. "We can aim for the moment she Advents."

She was right. Rubia had explained that the Spirit Kings, and the Queen who ruled them, were at their most vulnerable immediately after their Advent. No matter how powerful they were, the strength of a monarch who commanded Elementals was determined by the number of subordinates they could gather. Right after Adventing, they had almost no Elementals to command and could not exert their full power. This was their one chance, born from Wiji Hoyeon's solitary invasion of the Elemental Realm, which had forced the Queen into a hasty and incomplete Advent. She could not bring her subordinate Elementals, and of the Spirit Kings, the King of Flame had been annihilated while the others were reverse summoned.

"It'd be more fun if she were at full power," the Spear King grumbled.

"Stop talking nonsense," the Black Dragon Knight snapped, his expression one of pure disgust.

The Spear King glared back at him and shouted, "When else in my life will I get to fight a being like the Spirit Queen?"

"How about you just die and fight King Yama instead?"

"I'll get my chance with Yama when I'm dead and gone. Since I'm still breathing, I have to fight the Spirit Queen too."

Of course, Lee Sungmin had no intention of indulging the Spear King's stubbornness, and neither did anyone else. He felt a pang of disappointment over Yana's absence. Her power would be a great help in the coming fight, but she still had not returned from the Demonic Spirit Pavilion.

[It can't be helped,] the Void Monarch muttered.

Ten days had passed since Yana left. The plan had been simple. They would wait for a signal from her bracelet once the ritual was complete, then travel to Urmuri to bring her back, but even after ten days, the bracelet remained silent. In truth, she was not late. She had said from the beginning that her business would take about half a month. It was the situation that had changed. The day of the Spirit Queen's Advent had arrived before she could return.

The whereabouts of the Predator group, led by Jeniela, also remained unknown. For Lee Sungmin, that was actually a small mercy. It meant that Jeniela, having lost Aine, was not yet rampaging publicly. He had succeeded in buying time, just as he had hoped.

[Is it alright not to worry about the forest?]

*I have no choice but to trust Teresa and Osla.*

Teresa and Aine remained in the Fairy Forest. Osla had to constantly use his abilities to suppress Aine, who would have a seizure and go berserk the moment she opened her eyes. Teresa had volunteered to stay behind, and upon learning that the forest had become a battlefield, she had spent several days wandering its outskirts, weaving a protective barrier.

"Reverse summoning won't work," Lloyd warned. "It's just a stalling tactic. As long as the Queen intends to destroy this world, she will just Advent again to bring about The End."

Being sent back to the Elemental Realm was no penalty for the Queen. On the contrary, she would return even more prepared for her next attempt. This was their first and last chance.

"The Spirit Kings don't have the ability to Advent directly into this world," Rubia added from her place nestled in Lee Sungmin's arms, making a humming sound. "If Your Majesty disappears... there will be no danger from the Elemental Realm."

The problem was how. They needed a way to annihilate her, not just send her away.

The first method that came to Lee Sungmin's mind was predation. He could devour the Spirit Queen, just as Aine had devoured the Spirit King of Flame.

"That's too dangerous," Scarlet warned immediately. "You and that crazy brat are built completely differently. In the first place, that brat's body can't even be called a body." This was the conclusion she and Lloyd had reached after studying the fallen Aine. "If you try to devour a being on the level of the Spirit Queen, your body will explode. If you want to commit such a spectacular suicide, I won't stop you."

He had only considered it one of many options. Lee Sungmin caressed the spear he had placed beside him. There was something else he wanted to try. The final form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the pinnacle of martial arts. It was the very technique that had decided his battle with the Blood Demon. The powerful Vampire had been unable to regenerate the wound it left, and had even seemed surprised by that fact.

*I am too, but the Spirit Queen is a transcendent.*

He recalled Osla's grim warning before they came here.

*Physically killing a transcendent is... impossible,* Osla had told them, his expression bitter as they prepared to leave for the Sleeping Forest. *From the moment we become transcendent, we gain a near-perfect immortality. Your attacks can destroy a transcendent's body, but it's impossible to kill their existence. That's why we resurrect endlessly. Especially Sarahenne. She is a transcendent and the Spirit Queen. The foundation of every Elemental's existence is connected to the Elemental Realm. In the worst-case scenario, Sarahenne could escape there.*

However, there was no such thing as unconditional immortality.

*Our immortality is near-perfect, but not perfect,* Osla had continued. *Repeated resurrections consume our power, and if our very existence is erased, even we find it impossible to resurrect. Other than that... there's also the method of Sealing. If a transcendent is Sealed in a sufficiently weakened state, they have no choice but to remain Sealed.*

So they had prepared several methods. Their party included Lloyd and Scarlet, who, despite their different systems, were both near the pinnacle of magic. Based on Lee Sungmin's judgment and public rumor, they were the most skilled mages currently active. With their support, he would not feel lacking in magical force. The two would focus on long-range magic and, if the situation allowed, Seal the Spirit Queen. Sealing was not a completely safe method, but binding her to this world was far better than letting her be reverse summoned to the Elemental Realm.

Then there was the frontline. Lee Sungmin himself, the Black Dragon Knight, the Spear King, and Baek Sogo. The four of them would fight the Spirit Queen head-on. Their attacks could not be so excessive that they triggered a reverse summon. The primary goal was Sealing, but if possible, they would aim for annihilation.

*It might be possible with the pinnacle of martial arts.*

The Blood Demon could not regenerate the wound from that technique. The tip of the Spear of the Extreme Void might even work on the Spirit Queen. The Spear King's own spear, which had momentarily recreated the technique of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, might also lend its strength to finishing her.

He did not know.

Right. Lee Sungmin did not know what the outcome would be. He did not have a shred of certainty. He had made the best preparations he could, but there was no guarantee things would go according to plan. Perhaps none of their strategies would work, and they would be miserably defeated.

And after that? He would probably die. Even if he managed to escape... honestly, he did not know. If they failed to attack the Queen now, with all these preparations, it would be impossible to win against a version of her that would only grow stronger with time.

Amidst his anxiety, Lee Sungmin closed his eyes. He calmed his trembling heart, repeating the thought that he could do it. No, 'I can do it' was not enough. He absolutely had to do it. If he did not stop the Spirit Queen here, the story would not move forward. If they lost, it would be The End for the world, shattered completely by either the Spirit Queen or Jeniela.

The Martial God... what was the Martial God doing? What was the Spirit using him as a pawn aiming for? Could the Demonic Spirit be trusted? Uncertain questions piled up, fueling an impatience that made him wonder if this desperate struggle of his had any meaning at all. The fear of the unknowable gnawed at him.

Still, he could not run away.

What was Wiji Hoyeon doing right now? She was fighting alone in another space that overlapped this forest, a place absolutely inaccessible from this side. She was killing the monsters Sealed there, cleaning up before the creature called 'Greed' crouching in its deepest part could awaken. Ten days had passed since she had been isolated. He had come to this forest several times since then, but he had not been able to meet her. Even with Scarlet and Lloyd's help, searching for her location or finding a way to interfere had proven impossible.

He trusted her. There was no way she was dead. She was still alive, struggling as best she could. He recalled her slumped shoulders, not those of an iron man, and the tremor in her voice as she muttered that she had to do it, but could not. When she had turned her back to hide her expression, what had she been looking at? What had been writhing in the deep darkness he could not see? The countless monsters, the gazes he had felt when he first entered the forest... what kind of expression was Wiji Hoyeon wearing at that time? What on earth must she have been feeling? The woman who had wandered alone for ten years, holding back The End by herself.

'For you,' she had said.

Lee Sungmin pressed a hand to his chest. That was it. Wiji Hoyeon was doing this for him. She had asked him to trust her. That she could do it, in his place.

"Faster than expected," Lloyd muttered.

The dawn was breaking. Lee Sungmin quietly rose to his feet. Lloyd, holding Gries in one hand, floated into the air and retreated far back. Scarlet put on her gloves and cracked her neck from side to side. Baek Sogo, who had been guarding Lee Sungmin while his eyes were closed, looked at his shoulder with a bitter expression. She wanted to reach out and pat it, to embrace him, but she could not.

*Why... is that.*

She felt like she should not.

The Spear King, though not particularly pleased with the situation, did not insist on his stubbornness any longer. No matter how reckless he was, he knew how to read a room. Of course, he would have preferred an honorable fight if possible, but he understood what was most important.

"Don't start any shit," the Black Dragon Knight warned, glancing sideways at him.

"I won't, you bastard," the Spear King scowled and snapped back.

"Well... this is... it's hard to say 'good luck'..." Rubia sighed. Although she had completely betrayed the Spirit Queen after becoming Scarlet's familiar, the Queen was still her creator. She could not bring herself to cheer for Lee Sungmin and his companions.

"Just stay quiet," Lee Sungmin muttered, his gaze turning to the sky.

[She's here.]

CRACKLE.

A pitch-black crack spread across the dawning sky. The fissure widened with a splitting sound, and a brilliant light poured out from the gap. The light swelled for a moment before bursting forth in a flash, then gathered into a single point.

The Spirit Queen stood there, looking down blankly. It was not the aftereffect of a hasty Advent, nor was she particularly tired. Though she had broken through a complex maze between spaces and barely found the exit, what she felt was not fatigue, but annoyance at having wasted her energy on something so pointless.

"...What are you?" the Queen questioned.

Lee Sungmin did not answer. He acted.
