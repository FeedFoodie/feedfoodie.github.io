Having suddenly lost both his arms, the Martial God twisted his body in mid-air. Lee Sungmin lowered his spear, his gaze fixed on the man's writhing form.

The Martial God's eyes rolled back in their sockets as he screamed. Flailing without his arms, his body resembled a grotesque roly-poly toy, a crude but fitting comparison.

"Ugh-aaaaah!"

The excruciating pain from his severed limbs was too much to bear. The sense of loss from having both arms torn away was driving him mad.

"The Martial God!" Moon Queen shouted, pushing herself up through the dust and dirt near the castle gate. She knew that as a 'human', the Martial God could not regenerate his severed arms.

No magic existed that could restore a lost body part. Even Teresa, with her incredible holy power, was incapable of mending such a wound.

[It's always easier to break things,] the Demonic Spirit muttered. [It's also a limitation of humans. Only they have a spiritual body and a physical body that are one and the same. To transcend one's class means to transcend the limits of the physical body. As long as you are human, no magic or holy power can regenerate a lost part of you.]

*I know.*

That was why it had been impossible to regenerate Scarlet's eye, the arms of Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight, or Lloyd's leg. The Martial God was no different. No matter how profound his martial arts were, he was still human. He did not even possess the qualifications to enter the Battle God Chronicle. The enlightenment the Spear King had gained in the moments before his death was higher than anything the Martial God had reached in his entire lifetime.

*Ah.*

As he approached the flailing Martial God, a realization struck Lee Sungmin. The emotions he felt when he saw the man, the resentment, the hatred, and the desire for revenge, were entirely his own. When he had bound Jeniela's soul and shown mercy to Gemini, he had been influenced by the Blood Demon's memories. When he met the Gold Dragon Lord, Homerusos, in Erebrisa, the memories of Keiserod, who had been killed by the Void Monarch, had swayed him.

However, now, it was different. The memories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman were having no emotional effect on him. The Martial God was the direct cause of his master's death, yet Lee Sungmin felt no resentment channeled through those memories. The bitterness he tasted was not his master's but his own.

Realizing this, he felt a strange sense of futility and emptiness. On the verge of death, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had held no resentment toward the Martial God. It would have been more comforting if he had. Lee Sungmin watched the man's pathetic thrashing with a bitter feeling.

He understood his master's memories. The emotion the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had felt for the Martial God just before his death was pity. He also understood why. At one time, their accomplishments had been similar, but from a certain point, his master had advanced to a place far beyond, while the Martial God had barely progressed at all. It was pity for a man left behind, a pity born from once being in a similar position.

*If this one kills you here, you will not be able to come.*

Those were the words his master had spoken. He could have killed the Martial God then, but he did not. He had secretly hoped that the Martial God would one day ascend to that solitary path. He had wished it for him, if only out of pity and sympathy. *What about me?*

Lee Sungmin watched the Martial God with narrowed eyes.

"How dare, how dare, how dare you...!" the Martial God shouted, spittle flying from his mouth. His eyes, glaring this way, were bloodshot and filled with a demonic energy. "You, someone like you. How dare...!"

His voice trembled. His patched-up self-esteem was in tatters, but he could not bring himself to completely abandon it. He was afraid that if he did, he would seem too pathetic. He should have died at the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman ten years ago. If he had died then, he would not have had to suffer today's humiliation.

"Th-th-The End. The calamity... the calamity gave you such monstrous power. If it weren't for that, the Spirit, the Spirit would've... me more... ugh... ughh..."

"Do you truly think so?" Lee Sungmin asked the stammering, muttering man.

At those words, the Martial God's shoulders flinched.

"The power I use is the power of the Demonic Spirit. That's true, so I can't deny it. However... these martial arts. Do you really think this is a power I reached through the calamity of The End?"

"It's... a power... that doesn't make sense... How can martial arts..."

"Do you really think so?" Lee Sungmin asked once more.

The Martial God clamped his mouth shut. He did not want to think about it. He had already run away from that question and its answer long ago. Ten years of seclusion training, repeated meditation, questions about himself, the fight in the Snowfield.

He knew.

That power was not from the Demonic Spirit, nor was it a power gained from the calamity of The End. It was a strength that bordered on the absurd, a speed that defied logic. The Martial God was not a fool. He had only been looking away. If he stopped looking away and saw it for what it was, if he thought about it properly, he would understand.

It was unmistakably martial arts. Even if it was absurd and defied logic, it was a martial art that had reached the pinnacle and transcended common sense.

"......"

He had sought an answer and Despaired. He had Despaired at the fact that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, whom he once considered a worthy rival, had gone so far ahead. The coward was not his master but the Martial God himself. The Spirit had stolen the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power, and the Martial God had killed him. *No, back then... was it really me who killed him?*

Amidst the swelling confusion, the Martial God forgot how to think and turned away from all his worries. He hated how pathetic he became the more he thought, so he wore a mask made of his patched-up, torn self-esteem.

History's strongest.

The Martial God looked at Lee Sungmin with listless eyes. His hair had turned completely white, and as if that was not enough, strands of it snapped and fell away. Deep wrinkles formed on his nearly unlined face. His once-straight back became stooped, and he let out a long, deep sigh.

"...I know..." the Martial God, who had aged decades in an instant, muttered in a hoarse voice. He was no longer the Martial God. He was just an old man who knew his limits, who had Despaired at his inability to progress, who had wrapped himself in self-esteem and stubbornness and turned away from understanding.

"That Ilcheon's power... is not from the Demonic Spirit... that it's not a power gained from the calamity of The End... I know... That I didn't die back then... was because of Ilcheon's pity..."

"...My Master respected you."

"That... made it even more painful for me... Heh... hehehe..." The Martial God let out a laugh filled with self-loathing. He looked down at his still-aching arm stumps, then curled up his body and sobbed. "What... what was I lacking...?"

His voice was filled with Despair.

"I never thought I lacked talent. I also... worked hard enough... I had a sense of duty... I wanted to save the world, the world..."

*I know.* The Martial God's desire to stop The End had been genuine.

"But... why couldn't I... move forward? Why, why..."

Lee Sungmin did not explain. Instead, he pulled out his own memories and formed a sphere of light.

The Martial God stared blankly as the sphere approached him. When it touched him, his body trembled violently. It was only for a brief moment, but in that instant, he understood everything. Everything he had turned away from and refused to think about.

His face contorted as he learned the truth. Tears of blood streamed from his wide-open eyes.

"Ha... haha! Hahahaha! Uhahahaha!"

The Martial God laughed like a madman. Lee Sungmin watched him with calm eyes.

"It was all, all a lie. I... uhahaha! I was deceived, me! Why, why didn't I think about it? Why, why...!"

The suspicion had always been there, but it had always remained just a suspicion. He had never tried to confirm it. Was even that a trick of the Spirit? The Martial God's shoulders shook. He looked at Lee Sungmin with a silly grin.

"Yes... that's how it was..."

His feet moved.

"The calamity of The End was me."

The Martial God's body vanished. Though Lee Sungmin saw the movement, he did not stop him. He turned slowly, just in time to see the Martial God's foot crushing the head of Moon Queen, who had been approaching them.

"I was foolish..."

The headless corpse of Moon Queen fell to the ground. Even at the moment of her death, she could never have imagined that the Martial God would kill her.

The Martial God looked at her corpse on the ground and turned his body. He twisted his blood-tear-stained face into a smile.

"I've... done many terrible things to you, and to Ilcheon, too..."

Lee Sungmin did not answer. The Martial God stopped laughing and stared blankly up at the sky. After a moment, he muttered, "I cannot go."

The Battle God Chronicle... he muttered the name in a small voice.

"I have neither the right nor the shame... The Spear King... huhu. He has ascended to that path. If, by chance, you meet him later... tell him I'm sorry, and of course... to Ilcheon, too."

"I will," Lee Sungmin replied with a slight nod.

The Martial God took a deep breath. He closed his trembling eyes and asked, "May I... make one last request?"

"Say it."

"Let me... take my own life."

He had not expected such a request. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened in surprise.

"......"

"I have much to be ashamed of... I want to end this... fleeting... and foolish life with my own hands..."

At those words, Lee Sungmin slowly nodded his head. That much respect, he could also give.

"......"

With a low groan, the Martial God's body jolted violently. He used his inner qi to rupture all of his blood vessels. Black blood spewed from his tightly shut eyes, nose, ears, and mouth. His body staggered greatly before plummeting downwards.

*I was...*

*Foolish.* The Martial God let out a self-deprecating laugh. Memories from the distant past flashed by. In the short time it took to reach the ground, he wandered through his long, long memories.

When he first learned martial arts.

When he met the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Sword Immortal.

When he devoted himself to martial arts with the vague hope of becoming history's number one.

When he met the Spirit Medium.

From then on, he abandoned that vague goal. He heard about The End and took on the mission of saving the world with her. Through her introduction, he met Moon Queen, gathered the Six Honored Ones, and created Heaven Beyond Heaven...

"......"

That was the majority of the life he had lived. He had lived with the purpose of stopping The End. Until today, he had never once thought that his actions were wrong. Such a thought would have been a denial of his entire life.

*How futile...*

At the end of his wandering memories, the Martial God thought. The entire life he had lived was a lie. The Spirit had deceived him and used him like a puppet. He had tried to stop The End, but he himself was the servant of the Spirit who desired it.

He chuckled. The ground was close. He closed his eyes. The height was great, and his body, with its ruptured blood vessels, was incredibly weak. Without even a Protective Qi Aura, falling from this height was certain death.

Lee Sungmin felt a sense of bitterness as he looked at the Martial God's corpse sprawled on the ground. Even though he had avenged his Master, he felt no pleasure. It was a completely different feeling from when he had defeated Jeniela.

Just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had, Lee Sungmin also pitied the Martial God. He made a light gesture toward the corpse. A jet-black flame rose up and consumed it. He had considered predation, but he did not want to predate the Martial God's heart. He did not want to add anything more to his already chaotic memories, and in truth, with his current martial prowess, the Martial God's arts were unnecessary.

The soldiers on the castle walls and the onlookers at the gate were looking at him with terrified faces. To those who did not know the truth, it could only seem as though the Martial God and Moon Queen, who were trying to save the world, had met a gruesome end at the hands of the cruel Lee Sungmin. He was used to being misunderstood.

And right now, there was something else to do that was more important than explaining.

A pure white light seeped out from Moon Queen's crushed corpse.

[Ugh...] the Demonic Spirit groaned.

The light of the Spirit approached Lee Sungmin.
