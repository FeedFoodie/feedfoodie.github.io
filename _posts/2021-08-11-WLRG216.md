"What?"

The question caught Lee Sungmin completely off guard. He had never considered what he wanted to do with Wiji Hoyeon, and the Void Monarch's inquiry left him flustered.

The Void Monarch clicked his tongue, the sound dripping with condescension.

[Through that duel of spirit, you came to stand on equal footing with the Little Heavenly Demon wench. Isn't that what you so desperately wanted?]

"…I… guess so."

[Have you never thought about what comes next? Don't you dare say no. This Elder is spiritually connected to you. While I may not know your deep subconscious, a portion of the emotions that surface when you think of her are transmitted to me.]

Lee Sungmin's jaw tightened, but he said nothing. The Void Monarch continued, ignoring his silence.

[You call her a friend, but the feelings this Elder senses are… different. To you, she was always an unreachable existence, someone you believed you should never approach. Am I wrong?]

He couldn't deny it. The truth in those words was the very reason he had bristled when the Chairman called Wiji Hoyeon weak, the reason victory had tasted like ash in his mouth.

[Your feelings are contradictory. You genuinely wish to defeat that wench, but at the same time, you wish you couldn't. No, you don't want her to stagnate to the point where she becomes your equal.]

"…"

[Whether you wanted it or not, you now stand as her equal. Your relationship is bound to change.]

"It won't change," Lee Sungmin insisted.

[Oh, but it will, and the Little Heavenly Demon is likely thinking the very same thing.]

"Don't say such strange things."

[Do you truly think so?]

The Void Monarch cackled, a sound Lee Sungmin had no desire to hear. Turning away, Lee Sungmin ignored him and began to search for a place to stay, since he would be living in this forest for the time being.

[When you think of the Little Heavenly Demon… those feelings… I think they're closer to affection than friendship.]

"No, they're not."

[Denying it so flatly only makes it sound like I've struck a nerve.]

That was it. Lee Sungmin resolved to find a cesspool. He had no idea if the Fairy Realm even contained such filth, but if not, he would dig one himself just to dunk the Magic Armor into it.

[I'm sorry.]

The Void Monarch offered an immediate apology.

Since his duel, Lee Sungmin had wandered the forest several times in search of Wiji Hoyeon, but no matter how thoroughly he searched, he could not find her. He was certain she hadn't left, a fact the fairies he had befriended over the past few days had whispered to him.

[It seems she doesn't want to see you,] the Void Monarch remarked with a cackle.

Lee Sungmin thought of the cesspool again, and the spirit promptly shut his mouth.

During the week he failed to find Wiji Hoyeon, Lee Sungmin replayed their duel in his mind. The reality of his victory still felt distant, the elation nonexistent. As he dissected every moment, he was forced to confront an undeniable truth.

*I could have won more easily.*

If he had used buff magic and Demonic Energy from the start, or even gone beyond that and unleashed all the martial arts at his disposal, the victory would have been his much more easily. In their duel, he had only used the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear up to the seventh form, the Seven Stars Penetrating Heaven. Had he used the eighth form and the final ultimate technique, the ninth, he could have won faster… and more decisively.

He had no regrets, though. He shouldn't have any. Using the eighth and ninth forms might have killed Wiji Hoyeon, or at least inflicted a wound equivalent to death. Since their duel was never meant to cause serious harm, he refused to regret holding back.

"Are you meditating?"

"…"

A spark of electricity crackled behind him, followed by the voice of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Having appeared with the Gale Thunder of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the Chairman stood with his hands clasped behind his back.

"You've come?" Lee Sungmin asked, rising from his meditative posture.

The Chairman wore another mask, this one vibrantly colored with a design that was impossible to decipher.

"Observing your martial arts has led me to a realization," the Chairman began. "You are stronger than I thought. Had you fought to kill from the start, you could have easily slain the Little Heavenly Demon."

"But I wasn't supposed to kill her, was I?"

"That's right. Subduing someone is harder than killing them. I'm not blaming you. On the contrary, it was amusing." A laugh rumbled from behind the mask. "You are the first disciple I have ever taken, and I find myself quite fond of you."

The Chairman paused, his tone shifting.

"Therefore, I intend to do my best to guide you. The sole disciple of this Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon must possess skills worthy of the title."

"…As you know, Master, my talent isn't exceptional," Lee Sungmin said, voicing a worry that had been on his mind for a week. "Even if you do your best, I'm worried I won't be able to keep up."

"It's fine," the Chairman replied instantly. "I am also aware that your talent is lacking."

"…"

Lee Sungmin gave him a reluctant look. "I'm… I'm still not sure. Is it really okay for me to learn a different martial art at this point? I'm not confident I can use it well."

"What a foolish thing to say." The Chairman just laughed heartily at the concern Lee Sungmin had deliberated over for so long.

Stung by the laughter, Lee Sungmin glared and retorted, "Learning a good martial art doesn't guarantee I'll get any stronger, does it? It's better to wear clothes that fit than ones that don't…"

"That's it," the Chairman cut in sharply. "The martial arts you have been using are a child's clothes."

"…Pardon?"

"The state of Transcendence exists completely beyond the martial realms, beyond theory, beyond all the common sense you once knew. The arts you have practiced, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Violet Dawn Divine Art, Dancing Shadow Soul Break, and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, were all created by minds that had never conceived of such a state."

"What in the world does that…"

"It means that those martial arts alone cannot fully contain the martial theory of the Transcendent Realm," the Chairman chuckled. "Consider yourself lucky, disciple. Martial arts that contain the theory of Transcendence are typically created by a martial artist over a long period of time after they have entered that state. However…" He paused, looking Lee Sungmin up and down. "…With your insight, it would take hundreds of years to create a supreme martial art that contains such truths."

It was an irrefutable statement, so Lee Sungmin could say nothing. The Chairman continued speaking to him as he listened quietly.

"That is why your clothes must be tailored. They are far too small for you now, so they must be remade to fit." As he spoke, the Chairman reached into his robes and produced a thick book. On its cover were the words 'the Violet Dawn Divine Art'.

"First, master this inner arts technique. I have added my own insights to the existing Violet Dawn Divine Art and included the incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It will be quite arduous, but you must master it. Learn it even if it means cutting back on sleep."

Lee Sungmin caught the book as the Chairman tossed it to him. A sudden question surfaced as he held the new manual. "Are the insights of the Transcendent Realm really that amazing?"

"You've entered the Transcendent Realm yourself, yet you don't know?"

"…I know that it's abstruse, but I've never felt it was particularly difficult to execute."

"That's because you're only scratching the surface. If you truly don't understand, recall the new martial art the Little Heavenly Demon unleashed in her duel with you. Why would that great calamity go to the trouble of creating a new martial art?"

The Chairman still had his hands clasped behind his back. He wore a ridiculous mask, yet when he spoke of martial arts, he seemed more imposing and grand than anyone Lee Sungmin had ever seen.

"Martial arts is the study of using the body efficiently, of receiving and using qi to far surpass the body's limits. To enter the Transcendent Realm, however, is to leave one's own body and intervene in the space that contains it. Upon reaching that state, martial arts is no longer just martial arts."

Lee Sungmin nodded with a blank expression, absorbing a martial theory that could be called the very foundation of learning.

"If it's hard to understand… yes. Think of a tail. Or wings. Humans don't have them, but the insights of the Transcendent Realm are like them. That's why it's difficult to use them properly. It's because you suddenly have something you never had before." The Chairman raised a hand and pointed to the book in Lee Sungmin's grasp. "That's why you must learn it anew. Don't resist, just learn it. Perhaps… you won't need my help to master the art. You too have entered the Transcendent Realm, and in doing so, you must have established your own philosophy on martial arts. Besides, you're an Otherworlder, so learning the art itself shouldn't be too difficult, right?"

"…"

"So stop with the excuses and just properly learn and train that inner arts technique. I haven't finished modifying the other martial arts yet. First, practice this fundamental technique, and once you reach a certain level, I will give you the others."

With those words, the Chairman vanished in a flash of Gale Thunder, once again disappearing after having said all he wanted to say.

Lee Sungmin felt no dissatisfaction, however. He sat down again and opened the Violet Dawn Divine Art, his expression serious.

*It's difficult.*

After spending a long time carefully reading the manual to the end, Lee Sungmin let out a sigh. The new text made him keenly aware that not all Transcendent Realms were the same. Learning it wouldn't be hard, thanks to his status as an Otherworlder, but mastering it to the point of being truly usable would require a tremendous amount of effort.

[Would you like to learn the Violet Dawn Divine Art?]

Answering the voice, Lee Sungmin immediately sat cross-legged and began to circulate the new art. His fighting spirit and Demonic Energy twitched, reacting instantly. The modified Violet Dawn Divine Art had new incantations woven into the original ones, and as he circulated his energy accordingly, there was an immediate response.

SIZZLE!

The sound erupted not from the air around him but from within his own body. He felt his inner arts and Demonic Energy surge through his meridians, a torrent moving with incomparable speed. A Great Heavenly Cycle completed in an instant, drawing an unconscious exclamation from his lips.

The sensation was too immense to contain while sitting still. Lee Sungmin shot to his feet, and in that same instant, his power responded.

SIZZLE!

A Protective Qi Aura flared into existence around him. It was the violet color of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, but now an electric current from the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder coursed around it.

Using Space Manipulation, Lee Sungmin lifted the spear he had set aside and gripped it with both hands. He willed his enhanced qi into the weapon, and in a flash, a dense aura enveloped the spear. It was much faster than before, yet its speed did not diminish its density in the slightest.

The newly learned art made Lee Sungmin's heart pound. Even though it was a modification of an art he already knew, circulating it for the first time and seeing such visible changes filled him with anticipation for what was to come.

He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art until nightfall. He knew he couldn't achieve great accomplishments in a single day, but for now, he decided not to be impatient. Osla's incomprehensible words still echoed in his memory, and the chilling thought that he might be the Apostle of the End occasionally surfaced, but he pushed it away. Just as the Void Monarch had suggested, he would focus on the present, on what he could do right now, rather than lose himself in an unknown and distant future.

"That martial art."

As he finished his qi circulation and was about to rise, Lee Sungmin heard Wiji Hoyeon's voice.

"It's the one the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman passed down to you, isn't it?"

She was standing under a tree.
