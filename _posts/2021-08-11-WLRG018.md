A mercenary was an opportunist at heart. Their code was simple. When faced with a choice, they always considered which option would yield the greater profit. Beyond that, they did not trust their comrades. The payment for any request was divided among the survivors, and more than a few mercenaries would gladly kill their partners to monopolize the reward.

Having been a mercenary himself, Lee Sungmin knew this all too well. Woon Heeryong, Wang Pae, Do Sangryang, Lenire. He did not trust the party that had proposed this subjugation request for a moment. He had agreed to join them in raiding the orc village, drawn by a reward difficult to find in Zenavis, but that did not mean he considered them his comrades.

*If an opening appears.*

Or if their attitude suddenly shifted.

Either side could betray the other, and the same held true for Lee Sungmin. They were four and he was one. The difference in numbers was an undeniable fact that put him at a disadvantage, a reality he was well aware of.

Still, the reason he had followed them was simple enough.

The orc village lay deep within the forest, their settlement located even farther in than a goblin's, requiring a half-day's walk into the woods. They could have moved faster using movement arts, but Woon Heeryong, Do Sangryang, and Wang Pae did not use them. Lee Sungmin could guess why. They were not particularly skilled experts. Woon Heeryong, who acted as the party's leader, was the best among them, yet even he seemed to be barely above the second-rate level. His reserves of inner qi were not plentiful, and he was not adept enough to run through a complex forest using such techniques. That was why the party walked at a slightly fast pace, conserving their inner qi for the battle to come.

As they traveled, Lee Sungmin pieced together what he could about his temporary companions. It was clear Woon Heeryong was acting as the party's leader. The one who had brought this request from the Mercenary Guild was the man called Little Monkey Marquis, Do Sangryang. Lee Sungmin couldn't tell his mercenary rank for certain, but he guessed it was around D or E-Rank, the highest one could receive at a second-rate level. In his past life, Lee Sungmin had also possessed second-rate skills but had been a C-Rank Mercenary. He had achieved that rank not through pure skill but through experience. A man like Do Sangryang, with his shallow experience, would be D-Rank at best.

Lenire, the mage from the Bellas School, was also a member of the Mercenary Guild. Seeing him speak so openly with Do Sangryang, it seemed they were probably the same rank. Black Dagger Wang Pae was a man of few words. He had kept his mouth shut since the initial introductions, so Lee Sungmin couldn't glean much from him. However, judging by the way Woon Heeryong treated him, it seemed the two had known each other before this job.

Then there were the two Classless who had come along as porters. They had not introduced themselves, nor did they speak to each other. They simply walked along behind the group, carrying large backpacks befitting their role. The bags looked designed for carrying loot that couldn't fit into a Subspace Pocket.

"Haa... haa..."

"Kuhuk! Hooo!"

The gasping pants of the Classless came from behind. It was to be expected. Walking through a forest was not an easy task. The ground was uneven and the sunlight filtering through the canopy was hot. Even the martial artists were sweating, so there was no way the Classless, burdened with large bags, could endure it without struggling.

No one paid them any mind. They would occasionally glance back to check if the porters were keeping up, and if they fell too far behind, they would just shout at them to hurry.

"How about we rest for a bit?" In the end, it was Lee Sungmin who made the suggestion. "Worrying about them is just going to slow us down anyway. We can't leave them behind, and it seems we've all gotten tired from walking for so long. It would be good to rest, even for a little while."

"That's true," Woon Heeryong agreed.

They gathered in a shady spot and sat down. The Classless collapsed nearby, desperately catching their breath.

"Slowpokes," Do Sangryang grumbled.

Beside him, Lenire sat with his knees together and took out a small pouch from inside his robe. The fist-sized pouch was similar in size to the Subspace Pocket Lee Sungmin possessed.

*Is that the Subspace Pocket the mercenary group provided this time?*

They had called it a large Subspace Pocket. Even if it was small, the spatial distortion magic imbued in it was likely incomparable to the one Lee Sungmin had. Lenire took out some simple snacks from his pocket and distributed them. While the mercenaries ate, the Classless chewed on jerky they had brought for themselves.

Lee Sungmin tore into the bread he received from Lenire. It was warm and fluffy, as if freshly baked, and incomparable to the dry jerky the Classless were gnawing on.

"...Are they also members of the Mercenary Guild?"

"Hm?" Lenire tilted his head.

Thinking Lenire hadn't understood, Lee Sungmin gestured toward the Classless with his eyes.

"Ah. Them? Yeah, that's right," Lenire answered. "They're part of the Mercenary Guild, but they aren't mercenaries."

They were apprentice mercenaries, men who couldn't undertake requests on their own. In fact, you couldn't even really call them mercenaries. Apprentices were not given a rank and received almost none of the benefits of being a Guild member. Their existence, to put it simply, was to be laborers that the Mercenary Guild could easily and conveniently exploit. They were used as porters on requests like this one or made to do odd jobs within the Guild.

*They must have applied to the Mercenary Guild but lacked the skill to receive a rank. That's why they became apprentice mercenaries.*

Low-ranked mercenaries were not treated well. It went without saying for apprentices, who didn't even have a rank. They were constantly cursed at, beaten, and harassed. In his past life, Lee Sungmin had started as a G-Rank mercenary, the lowest possible rank. Even so, he had been beaten and harassed as if it were a daily routine. The mercenaries' rough words and vicious fists had been so relentless that they made him contemplate suicide every single day. If a G-Rank like him had received such abuse, the pressure on apprentices was unimaginable. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue and looked at the two men.

*Still, you have to live.*

Although being an apprentice mercenary was a poor job in many ways, it at least guaranteed minimal food and lodging. They had probably become apprentices hoping for just that much.

"About how many orcs are in the village?"

"Roughly fifty of them," Do Sangryang replied.

Ordinarily, orcs and goblins were creatures with incredibly strong reproductive capabilities. A typical village would usually have several hundred of them living tangled together. The orc and goblin villages in Zenavis, however, were not that large. At most, they numbered around fifty.

"There won't be any danger," Do Sangryang said with a sneer. "orcs are stupid and slow."

Lee Sungmin did not like such overconfidence. He had rarely seen anyone so full of confidence from the start who actually had the substance to back it up.

After finishing their break, they started moving again. From this point on, they were in orc territory. Lee Sungmin had been here a few times before.

"Are you planning to go head-on?"

"That is the plan."

Hearing Woon Heeryong's reply, Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh. It wasn't just Woon Heeryong. Do Sangryang and Lenire, both members of the Mercenary Guild, also agreed with that opinion.

*Crazy bastards. What gives them the nerve?*

"...Isn't that a bit too reckless?" Lee Sungmin cautiously offered. He wanted to curse them out, but he held his tongue.

"What's reckless about it?"

"A single orc may not be stronger than any of you. Yes, that's certainly true, but aren't there fifty of them?" Lee Sungmin tried to persuade them, keeping his tone as even as possible. "If they swarm us and use their numbers, we're bound to get exhausted."

"You're a cautious one, Young Master," Woon Heeryong said with a smile. His tone made it unclear whether he was complimenting or mocking him. Lee Sungmin did not react.

"orcs may be numerous, but individually, they're not that impressive. They can't use martial arts or inner qi."

"That's right," Lenire chimed in. "If you guys just buy me some time in the front, I can take them all down with my magic."

*Idiots.* To call themselves mercenaries with heads like that. It was one thing for Do Sangryang, who wasn't familiar with orcs, to say such a thing, but it was surprising to hear it from Lenire. It seemed the mage was not accustomed to real combat and was an inexperienced practitioner who had only learned magic within his school.

"I've fought orcs quite a few times," Lee Sungmin stated. "As Do Sangryang said, orcs aren't that formidable. However, they form groups and are as brave as they are ignorant. If we get surrounded by them, we could face the worst-case scenario."

After saying that, he looked directly at Lenire.

"If our line is broken, you will also be in great trouble, Lenire."

*You'll die.* Lee Sungmin did his best to hold back the urge to convey that nuance.

"...Hmm. Then what should we do?" Woon Heeryong gently prompted him.

Do Sangryang and Lenire were inexperienced, and Woon Heeryong and Wang Pae were not very familiar with the monsters known as orcs. In the end, Lee Sungmin, who had the most experience, had no choice but to take the lead.

"...First, Lenire. My knowledge is shallow, so could you tell me about the magic of the Bellas School?"

"...The Bellas School specializes in wind magic," Lenire replied with an embarrassed expression. "That's true, but... I don't know that many types of magic, you see?"

*As expected.* Lee Sungmin understood inwardly. Lenire, who did not have a good physique, had kept up with the martial artists' pace through the forest. While the fully grown adult men serving as apprentice mercenaries were drenched in sweat and gasping for breath, Lenire was perfectly fine. If he was a mage who specialized in wind magic, it was understandable. Magic was a convenient skill, as capable of easily killing people as it was of helping them.

"...Alright."

Lee Sungmin was lost in thought for a long while before giving a slight nod. When he opened his mouth, the others looked at him.

"Let's make the orcs come out."

He had thought of various methods and run simulations. There was no guarantee it would work, but Lee Sungmin hadn't climbed from G-Rank to C-Rank Mercenary for nothing. He still had the experience from his ten years as a mercenary.

"orcs are monsters with a clear concept of their territory. First... Lenire, please use magic near their village to send a 'scent' toward their settlement."

"Scent?"

"Yes. The scent of humans. If they catch the scent of humans, their patrols will leave the village to hunt down the source."

"Won't that put me in danger?" Lenire spat out, frowning.

Hearing this, Lee Sungmin shook his head. "Some of us will lie in ambush and attack the orcs heading your way, Lenire. In the meantime, you can prepare an attack magic to help with the battle."

"Is that all?"

"No. We will lure the orcs out of the village in two waves." Lee Sungmin looked at Lenire and Do Sangryang. "You have a signal flare, don't you?"

"...You mean this?" Do Sangryang took a palm-sized stick from his coat. It was a signal flare provided to mercenaries by the Guild. If you tore off the cap and struck it against the inside, it would ignite and emit red smoke. It was an item used to request help or signal the discovery of a target.

"We'll use it at a location a short distance from their village," Lee Sungmin explained. "When they see the smoke, the orcs will become wary and come out. You all will confirm that the orc patrol has left the forest, then lure them out with Lenire."

"Are you saying you'll go alone, Young Master?"

"I don't intend to engage them. I'll rejoin you soon, so you don't need to worry."

"...Hmm..." Woon Heeryong let out a low groan. Then, he shook his head. "If we're luring the orcs out in two waves, it would be better for us to split into two groups as well. Young Master. I will go with you."

"...If that's the case, then I'm grateful."

Lee Sungmin did not refuse Woon Heeryong's consideration. The party split into two. Lee Sungmin, Woon Heeryong, and one of the apprentice mercenaries would use the signal flare to draw out the orcs. Lenire, Do Sangryang, Wang Pae, and the other apprentice would lure the orcs from near the village.

"Now, let's hurry as well," Woon Heeryong said in an energetic voice after receiving the signal flare from Do Sangryang.
