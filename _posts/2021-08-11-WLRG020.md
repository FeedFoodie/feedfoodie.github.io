"You're quite the master, Young Master!" Woon Heeryong's hearty laugh boomed through the quiet aftermath.

Lee Sungmin regulated his breathing, his glare fixed on Woon Heeryong's face amidst the carnage. The battle was over. Thirteen orc corpses, slaughtered by their hands, lay sprawled across the ground.

"Thank you," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking clinging flesh and blood from his spear.

From beyond the bushes, Brass crawled out cautiously, his body low to the ground. He took one look at the orc corpses and his face went pale.

"......"

He clamped a hand over his mouth, fighting the urge to vomit. Woon Heeryong clicked his tongue, watching Brass with disdain.

"So weak-hearted. Hey! Stop retching and get over here to pack up the corpses. Let's see... we were told to gouge out the eyeballs, weren't we?"

"Y-Yes..." Brass staggered over, having already thrown up everything he had eaten. If only they had the large Subspace Pocket from the Mercenary Guild, they could have stored the bodies whole, but that was with Do Sangryang and Lenire's group.

Brass took out a dagger. With trembling hands, he approached an orc corpse, grabbed it by the head, and began the grisly work of gouging out its eyeballs. The eyes themselves had no value. They were simply proof of the kill to be submitted to the Mercenary Guild.

Finding Brass's movements frustratingly slow, Lee Sungmin stepped forward. "I'll help you."

"Do you have a spare dagger?"

"Ah, yes. Here you go."

Lee Sungmin skillfully plucked out the orc's eyeballs while Brass hurriedly took them and stuffed them into a bag. Woon Heeryong, watching from a distance, spoke up again.

"You are a righteous one, Young Master."

"Not really," Lee Sungmin answered vaguely, roughly wiping the blood from his hands onto his clothes.

All they had to do now was wait for Do Sangryang, Lenire, and Wang Pae's group to finish their task and send the signal.

Just then, a flare shot into the sky.

"Young Master, you seem quite different from your appearance," Woon Heeryong remarked as they started toward the smoke. "You're cautious and seem to have a lot of experience."

"I learned from my Master."

"An excellent Master. If you continue to grow like this, Young Master, you'll surely become a master who can make a name for himself."

Lee Sungmin walked behind Woon Heeryong as they talked. "Thank you for saying so."

"Haha, I'm just saying what I feel. Now, let's hurry. They must be waiting."

The distance was considerable, but they moved quickly and it didn't take long. When they arrived at the source of the smoke, the first thing they saw were more bodies. A dozen or so orcs lay sprawled out like the others.

"How did it go on your end?" Do Sangryang asked from his perch on a rock where he was catching his breath. He looked completely exhausted. Lenire sat next to him, her complexion just as pale, suggesting she had expended a great deal of her martial arts theory.

"We got them all," Woon Heeryong answered. As he replied, his eyes met Wang Pae's. Lee Sungmin, standing just behind Woon Heeryong, saw Wang Pae's gaze shift. He squinted one eye in what looked like some kind of signal.

*...What's this?*

They were definitely exchanging some kind of message. They had to resort to such a crude method because neither Woon Heeryong nor Wang Pae were masters capable of using Thought Transmission. Of course, he couldn't be certain. It was possible Wang Pae had simply squinted because his eye was itchy.

Lee Sungmin, however, did not dismiss it so easily. Suspicion, after all, allowed one to prepare.

He quickly reviewed their relationships. Do Sangryang and Lenire belonged to the same Mercenary Guild. They had brought the request, and as guild members, they had to see it through. The apprentice mercenary Brass and the other one were in the same boat, both affiliated with the guild.

However, what about Woon Heeryong and Wang Pae? They weren't part of the guild. From what he had heard, they had volunteered when Do Sangryang was looking for companions to take on the request.

*Woon Heeryong and Wang Pae knew each other.*

That was an undeniable fact. It wasn't enough to turn suspicion into certainty, but Lee Sungmin firmly filed away the thought that 'something was suspicious'. The real problem was what those bastards were after.

"Alright. Let's go clean up the remnants left in the village," Woon Heeryong announced in an energetic voice. Do Sangryang and Lenire, though not fully recovered, nodded and got to their feet.

"Now that I've fought them, they weren't so tough after all."

*Bastard. Acting all tough.* Lee Sungmin swallowed a dry laugh. The man was exhausted after fighting just a dozen orcs. Considering even Lenire, a mage who had time to prepare, was tired, they would have been annihilated if they had charged into the orc village as originally planned.

That worry was gone now. By luring the warriors out in two waves, they had likely cleared out most of them. The old and female orcs weren't much of a threat, leaving only the young orcs as a potential danger.

"The Do Sangryang chivalry is right. These orc fellows, they're nothing once you fight them," Woon Heeryong seconded.

"To the point of being useless," he added.

The words had barely left his mouth when Wang Pae, who had been walking with his arms hanging loosely at his sides, suddenly snapped a hand upward.

"......"

His sleeve fluttered wildly as a jet-black dagger shot from within.

"Aack!" A scream tore from Lenire's lips. She stared down in horror at the dagger lodged deep in her chest. She opened her mouth to speak, but only a gush of blood spilled from her lips.

"Huh?" Do Sangryang, walking beside her, watched with wide, uncomprehending eyes. In that same instant, Woon Heeryong drew the sword from his waist and charged at him.

KANG!

Woon Heeryong's sword clashed against Lee Sungmin's spear. His life suddenly spared, Do Sangryang stumbled back awkwardly. Lee Sungmin furrowed his brow, feeling the resistance vibrate up the shaft of his weapon.

"You're interfering," Woon Heeryong muttered, his expression betraying his surprise at Lee Sungmin's speed.

*I knew it. Fucking bastard.* Lee Sungmin's brow tightened. He had been right to be on guard. The way Woon Heeryong and Wang Pae had been acting, their haste, it had all felt wrong. If he had been spacing out for even a moment, he never would have reacted in time.

"S-Sir Woon. What in the world...?"

*Fucking idiot.* Lee Sungmin found the hesitating Do Sangryang pathetic. Lenire was dead at his feet and he still hadn't grasped the situation.

"Just go peacefully," Woon Heeryong replied as Wang Pae moved again.

"......"

Another dagger flew through the air, aimed straight for Do Sangryang. Startled, he quickly twisted his body, the blade grazing narrowly past his shoulder.

"You bastards!" Do Sangryang finally roared, the reality of the situation crashing down on him. He charged at Wang Pae with his bare hands, forgoing a weapon. It seemed he was trained in fist and foot techniques, an external artist.

"Let me at least ask the reason," Lee Sungmin said, a faint sense of composure in his voice. Do Sangryang and Wang Pae seemed to be on a similar skill level, but unlike the exhausted Do Sangryang, Wang Pae looked perfectly fine. The martial arts they used were completely different. While Wang Pae had just thrown daggers from a distance, Do Sangryang had fought the orcs with his entire body. It was only natural that his stamina was far more depleted.

Still, Do Sangryang would win this fight. Wang Pae had conserved his stamina by throwing daggers, while Do Sangryang had lost his, but just as that created a difference, so too did their martial arts.

"It's nothing much," Woon Heeryong muttered, subtly dragging a foot back with a wary expression. He had witnessed Lee Sungmin's skills on their journey and knew that despite his youth, he was not an easy opponent. If he'd had his way, he would have started by killing Lee Sungmin, not Lenire. There just hadn't been an opportunity.

"The Subspace Pocket they received from the Mercenary Guild. Just selling that would earn us millions of Eru."

"I suppose so." *So that's what it was.* At first, he had thought they were trying to monopolize the request fee. That was impossible. If Do Sangryang or Lenire, the ones who accepted the request, died, then Wang Pae and Woon Heeryong couldn't collect the fee even if they presented proof of the subjugation.

In the end, the only profit they could gain by backstabbing the party was the large Subspace Pocket. Woon Heeryong was right. A pocket of that quality could fetch millions of Eru, even sold as stolen goods.

"You morons," Lee Sungmin sneered, a mocking laugh escaping his lips. "What a stupid thing to do."

Woon Heeryong looked genuinely surprised by the sudden change in his tone. "...What?"

"I said you're doing something stupid, you fucking morons. Selling a Subspace Pocket provided by the Mercenary Guild? Haha! They say ignorance makes you brave, and you're a perfect example." Lee Sungmin burst into genuine, derisive laughter, feeling a strange sense of gratitude for their foolishness. Thanks to them, his share of the reward would increase dramatically.

"...What are you talking about?"

"You fucking idiot. If it were that easy to sell, why would the Mercenary Guild just hand out Subspace Pockets? Huh? Why wouldn't mercenaries just take the pockets they're given and run?" In his past life, Lee Sungmin had been a mercenary. He knew exactly how monumentally stupid it was to run off with guild property.

"If you run, you die. You idiot."

The Mercenary Guild was neither a pushover nor a fool. They provided expensive items like a large Subspace Pocket for free because they were confident they could 'absolutely' retrieve it. All items provided by the guild were enchanted with top-tier tracking magic. Take one and run, and a guild tracker would be assigned to you immediately.

"Your skills are Second-Rate at best. You can't even dream of reaching First-Rate. Am I wrong? At your level, you'd be a C-Rank at most in the Mercenary Guild, but... if you run off with that thing, mercenaries of A-Rank or higher will be sent to track you."

The guild's ranking was an intuitive measure of strength. A Second-Rate martial artist, no matter how outstanding, was still Second-Rate and would remain at C-Rank. Occasionally, one with exceptional experience might rise to B-Rank, but that was an outlier. A typical B-Rank mercenary possessed the skills of a First-Rate martial artist. Among them, an exceptionally skilled one received an A-Rank rating.

"An S-Rank Mercenary is a peak master. You think you can escape the pursuit of a peak realm master with your level of skill? Puhaha! Selling it? Fuck me, you're bullshitting. You'll be dead in this forest before you even reach the next city."

"...This bastard...!" Hearing Lee Sungmin's taunts, Woon Heeryong's face twisted in anger. He dropped the formal speech that never suited him anyway.

"What, you son of a bitch. Does it hurt getting hit with the facts?"

GRIP.

Lee Sungmin's hand tightened on the shaft of his spear.

PWAK!

Woon Heeryong leaped forward, using a movement art to close the distance. Recalling Wiji Hoyeon's advice, Lee Sungmin jumped back as if recoiling.

The inner arts of the Violet Dawn Divine Art spread throughout his entire body.
