"......"

A voice cut through the crackle of the campfire as it consumed the firewood. Lee Sungmin, who had been meditating in a cross-legged position, opened his eyes just enough to see the Black Dragon Knight sitting on the other side of the flames.

"...Do you know my name?"

"I know your title," Sungmin replied. "The Black Dragon Knight."

"My name is Regon."

The declaration was sudden, Sungmin thought. Three days had passed since the Black Dragon Knight joined their party, three days of utter silence from the man. He had not spoken to anyone, nor had he eaten or slept. Scarlet had tried to engage him a few times, but he never answered, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked him nothing. While the knight remained a silent statue, their carriage had thundered nonstop toward the North. They had heard the Murim Alliance's pursuit had begun, but the pursuers were so slow they hardly seemed a threat, allowing an enormous distance to grow between them.

For the Black Dragon Knight to suddenly open his mouth, to state his name so unexpectedly, was a surprise. At this, Scarlet looked up from her magic tome, and Yehwa, who was tending the fire, shifted her gaze toward him. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, wearing his mask again, maintained an attitude of disinterest.

"My mother gave me that name," Regon continued, his voice indifferent. "A bit thoughtless, don't you think? It's just 'Dragon' with the spelling changed. Couldn't be helped, I suppose. My mother was illiterate."

"My mother was a woodcutter's daughter who lived deep in the mountain forest. Her meeting with my father… the Dragon… was just a coincidence. There wasn't any romance like you'd find in the old tales. He just happened to see her, and I guess her appearance was to his taste. That was it. My birth was a complete accident."

Lee Sungmin unfolded his legs to face the Black Dragon Knight. He could not understand why the man was suddenly telling this story, but he did not believe he was lying.

"Whenever my mother spoke of my father, she would get the expression of a dreaming girl. It wasn't hard to understand why. The illiterate daughter of a woodcutter met a Dragon and bore his child. On top of that, they say the polymorphed Dragon had looks superior to any man she had ever seen, so that one-night fling must have felt like a dream to her."

"...So?" Sungmin prompted.

"That's how I was born. When I was young, I was filled with nothing but resentment for my Dragon father. My mother began to die from the aftereffects of giving birth to a being she shouldn't have, and my woodcutter grandfather resented me. The villagers treated my existence more like a curse than a blessing. To me, my father, the Dragon, was the source of it all."

It was a depressing backstory. Sungmin had not expected to hear something like this so suddenly and could only wonder what kind of expression he should make. Scarlet quietly closed her magic tome. Yehwa swallowed her discomfort and added more firewood to the campfire.

"Dragons are called the sovereigns of magic," Regon went on. "But I couldn't use Dragonic, which only Dragons can use. Being a half-blood doesn't make you superior. My body was unable to use magic at all. I couldn't use Dragonic, and while I could handle Mana, using magic was impossible. It was because the human and Dragon blood mixed poorly."

"So you learned martial arts?"

"There was resentment in that choice, too. Since I resented Dragons, I didn't want to use magic. Fortunately… martial arts suited me very well. My body, though not as strong as a Dragon's, was tough, and I retained some of the Dragon's superiority in handling Mana."

"You don't seem to like Dragons very much," Sungmin noted. "Why did you try to move to a dimension where they exist?"

"Because I was lonely," the Black Dragon Knight answered. "As a half-human, half-dragon, I don't belong anywhere. That's why I wanted to meet them. I didn't know if they would accept me, but I wanted to meet… Dragons, and my father."

Regon chuckled. "I've thought a lot over the past three days. I don't particularly like my father. I hated him in my childhood, but as hundreds of years passed, I came to think that I wanted to meet him. When I learned he was dead… I was furious. When I found out the Magic Armor you wear was made from his corpse, I hated you."

"I suppose so."

"I'm going to let it go," the Black Dragon Knight said, shaking his head. "As I said, he was a father I didn't like, one I had never even seen. I also know you didn't kill him. So… I've decided not to hate you. Telling you my past and my name was my way of sorting out my feelings. Since you said we should get along."

Sungmin recalled saying it without much thought.

"My actions as the Murim Alliance Chairman caused you trouble," Regon continued. "For that… should I apologize? I'm not sure. Most of what I did was based on directives from Heaven Beyond Heaven."

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before shaking his head. "...No. You don't need to apologize."

He had never imagined the Black Dragon Knight would cast everything aside like this. Of course, he could not be certain that the man's current attitude was genuine, but the candid story was enough to leave him feeling confused.

*He doesn't seem to be narrow-minded. Or is it because of the situation?*

The presence of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was a constant restraint on the Black Dragon Knight. Although he traveled with them, he had no choice but to be wary of the Chairman. This new attitude and his story might just be a way to lower their guard.

The Black Dragon Knight spoke first. "I don't know how long we'll be traveling together, but I look forward to it." He extended his hand.

Lee Sungmin met his gaze, then raised his own hand to grasp it. As they shook, a faint smile touched the Black Dragon Knight's lips. Sungmin found the smile suspicious, but he was in no position to question the man, so he remained silent.

{sep}

The journey to the North was long. Lee Sungmin's party knew all too well that the Murim Alliance's pursuit was just for show. They might not have been able to confirm the Black Dragon Knight's status, but they could not simply let the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and his group go after they had laid waste to the Murim Alliance. A pursuit team had to be organized, if only to restore the fallen prestige of the Murim Alliance. In the grand scheme of things, that was the reason.

However, the Murim Alliance knew the truth. They knew that with their strength alone, they could do nothing to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Therefore, the pursuit was slow. The fact that the Dog Beating Stick Formation was broken and the Black Dragon Knight subdued and kidnapped was an act that had completely shattered not only the pride of the orthodox murim but also their common sense. That one master had shattered a formation deployed by a hundred men. That alone carried a terrifying meaning.

"The Sword Immortal might make a move."

The distance to North Trabia was still far. During the journey, the Black Dragon Knight showed a more active attitude, as if to prove he had sorted out his feelings. He now offered his opinions voluntarily, without being asked.

"Even though I was the Chairman, I never really tried to do anything for the Murim Alliance myself. Most of it was just directives from Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"So you were a puppet," Sungmin observed.

"That's right. In truth, the position of leading the alliance didn't suit me well. I had no motivation either. To them, I was a half-blood Dragon, not even human, and unlike the other Chairman candidates, my public reputation wasn't high. I was just good at martial arts."

Lee Sungmin recalled the events at Shaolin. At that time, Grandmaster Bulyeong had been recommended for the position of Murim Alliance Chairman, though he had refused. Considering Grandmaster Bulyeong's level of martial arts, he was no match for the Black Dragon Knight. However, being the highest elder of Shaolin and a renowned high priest made him a fitting candidate for the Chairman's seat, regardless of his martial skill.

"The existence of the Sword Immortal was awkward for me as Chairman, and for Heaven Beyond Heaven as well," Regon said. "Even the Martial God said not to provoke him."

"And yet you say the Sword Immortal might make a move?"

"The Sword Immortal is… apart from the Murim Alliance, an orthodox sects martial artist. Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, your actions were too excessive. Because of what you did, the pride of not just the Murim Alliance, but the entire orthodox murim, has been completely torn and trampled."

"Are you saying he might step in to restore it?" Lee Sungmin asked, recalling the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique. That sword, which shot out fiercely from a vast distance, was a divine might that the current him would find impossible to handle.

The Black Dragon Knight nodded. "There's a high probability. Even though he's in seclusion, he is certainly at Wudang."

"It doesn't matter," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, his voice relaxed. Despite the long journey, he looked out the window with the leisurely air of a man on a neighborhood stroll. "When I decided to strike the Murim Alliance and beat you up, I already anticipated that I might clash with the Sword Immortal. If I feared him, I wouldn't have done such a thing in the first place."

"Are you confident?"

"We'll have to see when it happens, but right now, this one's interest outweighs any fear."

"Are you not afraid of death?" the Black Dragon Knight asked with a look of disbelief.

To the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the question was a stupid one. He laughed heartily. "Isn't it just a matter of sooner or later? It's far more enjoyable for this one to choose, enjoy life, and then die, rather than waiting to die helplessly."

The Black Dragon Knight could not understand the Chairman's words, and the Chairman himself did not expect him to. Looking at Regon's dumbfounded expression, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman turned his gaze back out the window.

The sun was setting. It was an established rule on this long journey to stop the carriage when the sun went down, an order from the Chairman to enjoy the trip without any need to hurry.

"My disciple."

"Yes, Master."

"How is your body?"

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's voice was exceedingly gentle as he asked. Lee Sungmin could see the man smiling gently behind his mask, and a chill ran down his spine.

His physical condition was not bad. Since the fight with the Spear King, the Chairman had tended to his acupoints every day, and thanks to that, the Demonic Energy accumulated in his qi and blood had completely disappeared. However, he had not drawn upon his internal energy since that fight. Following the Chairman's words, he had kept his mask on the entire time.

"...It's not bad."

"Not bad? It must be incredibly good," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman jeered. The carriage jolted to a stop. He had sent a Thought Transmission to Yehwa in the driver's seat. He then raised a finger and pointed at Lee Sungmin and the Black Dragon Knight.

"It's about time to set up camp. I'll check on your martial arts for the first time in a while."

"...Pardon?" Lee Sungmin's eyes widened at those words.

"You haven't moved properly for a long time, but your body shouldn't have stiffened."

"Is it alright?"

The rampage of Demonic Energy was a thing of the past, but it was the Chairman who had warned him to be cautious, not to take off his mask, and not to use his martial arts. Although several months had passed, Sungmin still could not fully trust his own body.

"I can't just leave it be forever, can I?"

"But..."

"There's no need to be afraid, and it's not like this one can act in your place forever, is it?"

*Can't act in your place.*

The words left Lee Sungmin at a loss.

[It's necessary to prepare,] the voice of the Void Monarch echoed in his head. [While traveling with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, you've been able to do things you couldn't do on your own by having him act in your stead, but you can't hope for his help forever, can you?]

*I know that too.*

Lee Sungmin was well aware of that. He had no intention of relying on the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. If he did not possess the strength to solve problems himself, he would end up helpless at a critical moment, unable to do anything.

[The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman must know that as well. That he can't help you forever.]

*Could he just be acting out of spite?*

[I don't think so. I don't know what you think of that man, but as your Master, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman has been doing things a master should. It's the same now.]

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly at those words. He knew it too. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was, in ways both seen and unseen, looking out for him, his disciple.

[You'll probably be able to feel it for sure.]

Although he felt a little puzzled by the Void Monarch's confident assertion, Sungmin did not ask. He simply raised his hand and grasped the mask on his face.

...CRACKLE.

The moment the mask came away, a current of electricity surged through his qi and blood.
