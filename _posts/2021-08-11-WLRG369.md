Something brushed past Lee Sungmin, a chillingly honed sharpness that descended upon Jeniela. The Sword Immortal had not drawn his blade, nor had he unleashed his Flying Sword Technique. With a simple gesture, a mere lift of his hand, he turned space itself into a slash that tore Jeniela apart.

Jeniela did not retreat an inch. She chuckled and flicked her fingertips, scattering the blood that drenched them into droplets.

POOF.

The droplets swelled instantly, forming a crimson curtain that enveloped both her and Gemini.

KRRRRR!

The Sword Immortal's slash crashed down upon it. The thinly spread barrier of blood wavered but absorbed the attack completely.

A muscle in the Sword Immortal's cheek twitched at the sight. It was not the blocked attack that angered him, but the realization that Jeniela's technique was fueled by Cheongmyeong's blood.

"Monster."

He spat the word, his voice thick with murderous intent as he took a large step forward. The frail old man who had been lying helpless only moments ago was gone. In his place stood the greatest master of the Wudang Sect, a man at the pinnacle of swordsmanship. He stomped his foot forcefully onto the ground.

THUD.

A small tremor shook the floor as a formless slash erupted from below, tearing the blood curtain to shreds.

"Ahat."

Jeniela let out a laugh. Unlike the Sword Immortal, whose every move was filled with killing intent, she just kept smiling, her power already immense and now amplified by the night. The Sword Immortal moved past Lee Sungmin.

"Go."

"……"

"This old man's disciple is dead. The disciple I was prepared to give everything to… It's not your place to avenge him."

Still, how could he just turn a blind eye and walk away? In a way, this could be an opportunity. They might be from the Night of the Full Moon, but this was Mount Wudang, and the greatest master of the orthodox sects was genuinely trying to kill Jeniela.

[It might be a chance,] Void Monarch chimed in.

It was not a full moon. A vampire at night was stronger than in broad daylight, but the full moon, which granted them an absolute advantage and blessing, had not risen tonight. Only Gemini was with Jeniela.

*Where are Chen and Kun? What about Aine?*

Had they not joined Ju Won yet? He did not know. For now, at least, it was only Jeniela and Gemini.

*If it's now… if I join forces with the Sword Immortal, can't we kill Jeniela?*

While Lee Sungmin was lost in thought, the Sword Immortal was already closing in on Jeniela. With every step he took, formless slashes surged forth, trying to tear her apart. Jeniela merely giggled and flicked her fingers, sending droplets of blood from her soaked hand to collide with the slashes.

"Was it delicious?" Jeniela asked, her question not for the Sword Immortal, but for Lee Sungmin. "I'm talking about the Blood Demon. My father. Was he delicious?"

*Father…*

Lee Sungmin recalled the Blood Demon's muttered words about a parent having no choice but to give in to their child's pestering. He raised the tip of his spear.

"…Was the Blood Demon your father?"

"We weren't related by blood… Ah, I guess we were, in a way, since I was the one who turned him into a Vampire. Well, aside from that, the Blood Demon was mine and Gemini's adoptive father. When we were young, over seven hundred years ago, when Gemini and I had just become Vampires, we received a lot of help from him."

"Weren't you the one who drove the Blood Demon to his death?"

"I didn't force him. It was his choice. Besides, you're the one who ultimately killed him, aren't you? Ah, but don't worry. I don't think of you as my father's enemy."

Jeniela giggled. The Sword Immortal glared at her casual chatter and raised his hand.

"Stop," Jeniela said with a smirk, spreading her blood-soaked hand wide.

FWOOSH!

The crimson curtain expanded dramatically, enveloping the interior of the Taoist temple. The space was dyed in blood and began to flow. Jeniela shrugged and laughed.

"You can't kill me with such petty tricks. I thought you were angry because your disciple died, but I guess that's not really the case?"

"……"

The Sword Immortal let out a hollow laugh at her mockery, the smile vanishing from his face instantly. His hand clenched in mid-air, gripping something invisible. Watching his back, Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinched.

VRRRIP!

The Sword Immortal swung his hand. The invisible sword he gripped tore through space, splitting the blood-red world Jeniela had created in two. The ceiling of the Taoist temple blew away with it. In the resulting gust of wind, Cheongmyeong's head rolled across the floor, but the Sword Immortal did not look back as he kicked off the ground.

His aged body leaped through space, appearing right in front of Jeniela so suddenly that her eyes widened. She raised her hand to defend herself, but in that same instant, the Sword Immortal's Formless Sword slashed through her body.

"Oh?"

A diagonal wound split Jeniela's torso, and she collapsed in two pieces.

As the Sword Immortal raised his Formless Sword high to finish the job, Gemini moved. She kicked off the ground and shot forward, her target not the Sword Immortal. She sped past him and thrust both hands toward Lee Sungmin.

KABOOM!

The impact sent Lee Sungmin flying, his body smashing through the wall of the Taoist temple. He righted himself in mid-air and aimed the tip of his spear at Gemini.

Ahahaha!

A loud laugh shook the night sky. The entire Taoist temple was engulfed in a red light and then exploded. Amidst the crimson light, Jeniela rose to her feet. The Formless Sword wielded by the Sword Immortal could not push back the light radiating from her.

Engulfed in the bloody light, Jeniela descended to the ground, now wearing a red dress. The Sword Immortal did not retreat an inch, facing the raging crimson gale. His worn Taoist robes flapped in the wind as his gaunt arms gripped the Formless Sword tightly.

"Why don't you run?" Gemini asked as Lee Sungmin's body landed on the ground. She stood not far away, looking back over her shoulder.

RUUUUMBLE!

In the middle of the crimson storm, the Sword Immortal's body began to glow with a silver light. As he took a step forward, the silver radiance enveloping him intensified. It was the gleam of a well-honed blade. Hundreds of Formless Swords materialized around him, a truly befitting sight for his title as he advanced without hesitation against the storm Jeniela had conjured.

"The Sword Immortal is going to die," Gemini said without a hint of hesitation. "I'm not saying he's weak, but his opponent is just too much for him. Even if it were daytime, he wouldn't be able to kill Jeniela, and it's nighttime."

She pointed a finger toward the sky.

"At night, Jeniela is immortal. You can't kill her. Ever."

"If she's this powerful even on a night without a full moon… isn't now the perfect time to kill her?" Lee Sungmin muttered.

"Because today isn't a full moon?" Gemini smiled, revealing her sharp fangs. "That's why I'm telling you to run. You don't know Jeniela. You think today is the perfect opportunity to kill her because there's no full moon?" She chuckled as if she found her own words amusing.

[That's something you'd only say because you don't know Jeniela,] a whisper came via Thought Transmission. [Understand? It doesn't matter if it's day or night. It doesn't matter if there's no moon or a crescent moon. If Jeniela wishes it, she can turn day into night and make a full moon rise anytime she wants. That is the power of the last of the three Demonic Eyes she possesses.]

Hearing Gemini's words, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened.

[That's why I'm telling you to run. Fortunately, Jeniela has no intention of killing you right now. However, that doesn't mean she'll be merciful if you decide to attack her. Are you starting to understand what I'm saying?]

[…Why are you telling me all this?] Lee Sungmin sent back, glaring at Gemini. He had felt her behavior was suspicious ever since their first meeting. Unlike the other Vampires, she was not blindly devoted to Jeniela. Just like when she had handed over Baek Sogo at the mansion, she was now telling him things he had not even asked for.

[Because it seems more interesting that way,] Gemini replied with a coy smile. [Believe it or not, that's up to you, but if you attack Jeniela here, it's certain you'll die a dog's death. Even if you help the Sword Immortal, killing Jeniela is impossible, and don't you dare underestimate my presence here, either.]

While they communicated, Jeniela and the Sword Immortal were still clashing. The Sword Immortal relentlessly swung and fired his Formless Sword, trying to tear through the storm of magic power Jeniela had conjured. She was still smiling. His attacks could not break through her defensive barrier.

Gemini raised her hand and pointed behind her. "The Sword Immortal can't kill Jeniela alone. It'll be the same even if you join in."

[Be careful,] Void Monarch advised. [You need to distinguish between what you can do and what you can't. You can't just blindly believe that little vampire's words. The third Demonic Eye Jeniela possesses can create a full moon? Isn't that too absurd of an ability?]

If it was true, it meant Jeniela had no weaknesses. Until now, Lee Sungmin had constantly considered ways to defeat her. The easiest was to fight her during the day. In the worst-case scenario of a night battle, he would avoid a full moon. Fighting under those conditions was his top priority, but if Gemini's words were true, such conditions were impossible to secure. No matter what kind of day he chose, if Jeniela activated her Demonic Eye, a full moon would rise, and he would be forced to fight in the situation most advantageous to her.

[What will you do?]

Void Monarch's words echoed in his mind. *Distinguish between what you can do and what you can't.*

Defeating Jeniela alongside the Sword Immortal was something he could not do. It was an impossible task, even if he used all his power, even if he used Creation and the final form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, even if he used the final ultimate technique of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break which he had never used before. What was impossible was impossible.

What, then, could he do? He could run. Just as Gemini had said, Jeniela did not seem intent on killing him here and now. The same went for Gemini. If he tried to escape, he could.

However, he could not take the Sword Immortal with him. The Sword Immortal himself would not want that, and his body, sustained by the energy of Wudang, was incapable of leaving the mountain. In the end, he had to escape alone. To fight Jeniela here would be a dog's death.

*Where is Aine?* he wondered. *Chen? Kun?*

A chilling premonition snaked down his spine. He began to drag his feet backward. Something he couldn't do… and something he could.

"…I'll back down."

"Good thinking," Gemini said with a bright smile.

Lee Sungmin looked past her shoulder with a conflicted gaze. Compared to the Sword Immortal, who was fighting desperately, firing off his Formless Sword, Jeniela had plenty of leeway.

*Monster.*

He slowly backed away. The Sword Immortal… was going to die. It was inevitable.

*I have to stay calm.*

Lee Sungmin clenched his teeth. It was a terrible feeling, but there was no time to mourn. *Something I can do,* he etched the words into his mind once more. He turned his body, no longer looking at the Sword Immortal.

"See you next time."

Gemini smiled and waved. Lee Sungmin ignored her farewell and kicked off the ground. He ran to the cliff in a single breath and leaped from its edge. As he soared over the dizzying precipice, he fully opened his senses, expanding his perception wide. Sounds from far away reached his ears.

Shouts, screams.

It was just as he thought. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip.

The Wudang Sect was under attack.

{sep}

Sword Lake had fallen.

Beyond the shattered mountain gates of Wudang, Taoist priests stood in formation, every remaining master in the sect gathered. They all knew this would be the worst day in the history of their sect since its founding. The inhuman beings who had passed through Sword Lake were savages who knew nothing of courtesy.

"Shouldn't we ask the Great Grandmaster for help?" one muttered.

"What kind of disgrace would it be to bother him over something like this?" another replied.

The Sect Leader of Wudang stood with his hands clasped behind his back, gazing out at the four figures beyond the gates. Four of them. He swallowed a deep groan. He could not call this 'something like this'.

*They don't seem like it, but they're being cautious,* Preskan thought, dragging the hem of his robe as he climbed the stairs. Aine, leading the way, was already soaked in blood from tearing apart every Wudang priest who had attacked them after they passed Sword Lake.

*Or maybe she's just greedy.*

Jeniela had declared she would kill the Sword Immortal herself and had gone to his residence. Meanwhile, Aine, Chen, and Kun headed for the Wudang Sect's main building.

"I'll get to eat my fill today," Preskan said, his voice pleased.

Kill them all and eat.

That was the only order Jeniela had given Aine.
