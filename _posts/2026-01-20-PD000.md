5 Years Old: Became an orphan. Did my best to eat and shit.

7 Years Old: Met Master.

10 Years Old: Started to hate Master enough to want to kill him.

12 Years Old: It was tolerable enough.

18 Years Old: I am the only suitable disciple in heaven and earth? Master must be a frog in a well.

20 Years Old: Fuck you, Master...

21 Years Old: Started to live as a martial artist.

24 Years Old: I miss Master.

25 Years Old: Met the person I love. Master said that people eventually only live to eat and shit, and since martial artists only know how to use their bodies, they try to make a living with that talent somehow.

Unless one is born with a gold spoon in their mouth, that is how we all live.

Master was not wrong.

## Author's Foreword

This is Noh Gyeongchan.

I am presenting my ninth work, 《Peerless Dad 》, to you readers.

While writing 《Constable Lee Kangjin 》, I really wanted to write a story with the theme of fathers and children.

It is partly because I have become a father to a son who has just passed his first birthday, and also because as I get older, I have been thinking a lot about the burden of a father.

Just as I wrote my previous work, 《Constable Lee Kangjin 》, with the single goal of 'a crazy guy becoming a normal person', I intend to write this 《Peerless Dad 》 with a single goal as well.

'Let's write a story that most fathers can sympathize with.' I want to start with this one thing and end with this one thing.

However, since I am a martial arts writer, I want to write it in a way that suits that genre.

Although my writing skills have improved, I have not yet reached the level where I can write exactly what I want the way I want. Still, isn't it a satisfactory piece of writing if it can at least make the readers smile a few times? I started with this thought.

So I intend to write it that way.

Thanks to that, for the first time, I have put forward a protagonist who is already fully grown, rather than a developing protagonist.

Because Noh Gajang is a father of three children.

Peerless Dad. Knowing the will of the heavens, there is no one who can compare to a father.

I would appreciate it if you could enjoy not only the story of the father Noh Gajang but also the story of the martial artist Noh Gajang.

August 20, 2013: A novice writer who wishes to become a real writer begins writing.
