He had fought Wiji Hoyeon several times before. The first time was when he was fourteen years old and she was only thirteen. Even to his ignorant eyes back then, the Wiji Hoyeon of that time was so exceptional that it was difficult to believe they were the same species. Her status as the Young Cult Leader of the Central Plains Demonic Cult provided the best possible background for survival in Eria. She was accustomed to martial arts, to fighting, and to killing. She knew a great many techniques and possessed a limitless potential for growth.

For Lee Sungmin, meeting Wiji Hoyeon was the greatest miracle of his life. While he had intended to make contact with her to some extent, looking back on it now, it felt as if the two of them were fated to meet and become entangled from the very beginning. It was because of her that he was able to obtain the highest level of martial arts with surprising ease, not some collection of low-grade techniques.

The day before she left Zenavis, they had sparred. The result was a natural and overwhelming defeat. Even with a ten-second handicap, he could not manage to graze the hem of her clothes. The outcome was so obvious that he felt no frustration, only a sense of awe. From that moment on, Wiji Hoyeon became his most important goal. A thirteen-year-old girl had lectured him, telling him to find a purpose in life. If he did not know what to live for, she had told him, he should try to surpass her.

And so he lived. He entered Mount Mushii with the sole purpose of surpassing Wiji Hoyeon. He trained relentlessly in the martial arts. When he later defeated her doppelganger in the Dungeon, he thought he had finally gotten a little closer. Then he met her again in Ruvus, weakened by a curse. The Wiji Hoyeon who had once been so impossibly strong was now vulnerable. He came to understand that the woman he had thought of as an iron man was, in the end, only human.

They sparred again in the Fairy Forest, and this time, he won. The victory brought him not elation but a deep sense of conflict. It was then he realized the truth. He did not actually want to surpass Wiji Hoyeon. He wanted to keep her as his goal, to forever chase after her back rather than stand beside her as an equal.

He changed his mindset. They lived together for a year, sparring often. He won most of their matches, and by the end of that year, they were evenly matched on several occasions, yet even as they spent the same year training side by side, her rate of growth was simply incomparable to his own.

And now.

Wiji Hoyeon crossed the space to enter at a speed that was beyond imagination. Caked in blood, she moved so fast that the dried flakes scattered from her body like red powder. Her mind was shattered. She did not recognize Lee Sungmin. She only vaguely remembered why she was here, and as for why she was doing this…

She could not remember.

*I don't know…*

*Still, I have to do it.*

He recalled the words she had muttered. She did not know why she was here or why she was doing this. Nevertheless, she had to. Her initial reason for stopping The End had twisted into desperation, and now it was a curse, a binding spell she herself could no longer comprehend, yet she did it. She believed she must, and she did not question it.

It was a horrible feeling, seeing her pushed so far that she had finally broken. What stood before him now was a woman whose mind had collapsed, unable to bear the weight of annihilating an entire world. She had intended to do it all for him, and in the end, she could not endure it. She had fallen into Despair and gone mad.

She was the woman Lee Sungmin loved.

For that reason, he could not avoid this. He must not run away. Even if it were possible to escape into the Battle God Chronicle, he would absolutely not flee.

The Spirit was clever. A mentally broken Wiji Hoyeon was the best puppet it could possibly control. She was different from the Martial God, who had been blindly loyal but ultimately lacked power. The Spirit had wanted something different from Wiji Hoyeon in the first place. Judgment was unnecessary. She just needed to be able to fight. The mentally broken woman swung her hand.

Lee Sungmin hastily created distance between them and whipped his Formless Spear forward.

KRAAANG!

Space shattered before the spear and her hand could even collide. As their protective qi auras clashed, light scattered violently in all directions. The force was immense, so heavy that the very tip of his Formless Spear began to disintegrate. Her power was beyond anything he could have imagined.

Honestly, a small part of him had thought it. He had considered that no matter how strong Wiji Hoyeon had become over ten years, she could not possibly be as strong as he was now. It was not arrogance but a perfectly reasonable assumption. The current Lee Sungmin was qualified to enter the Battle God Chronicle. He could perfectly utilize the martial arts from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's memories. He could wield Dragonic and the Blood Demon's Asura Heaven Destruction Art. He lacked nothing, possessing inner arts, magic power, and Demonic Energy. The body of the Slaughter Predator was superior to any other in the world, capable of regenerating from wounds that would surely kill, like having his head blown off or his body sliced apart.

Wiji Hoyeon, on the other hand, was human. Ten years ago, she had only managed to be an equal to the Lee Sungmin of that time. He had heard that for a decade she had faced the disaster of The End, conquering all the Dungeons alone while absorbing their power. He also knew she had invaded the Elemental Realm by herself and had cornered the Spirit Queen, but she had not been able to destroy her. From that fact, Lee Sungmin had judged her level to some extent. No matter how strong she was, how absurdly talented, even with a Constellation and the Grace of Fate, Wiji Hoyeon was still human. Her existence was fixed. She could be nothing more. She could not destroy the transcendent Spirit Queen.

Those were the grounds on which he had predicted her power before entering the forest. He thought it would be difficult, but he believed he would be able to subdue her.

It was a gross miscalculation. Her power was beyond imagination. Where did his prediction go wrong? He widened the distance again.

Wiji Hoyeon closed the gap with blinding speed. The spear was too long and cumbersome to deal with her continuous palm strikes.

*Should I increase the distance? No, first, an assessment.*

Lee Sungmin's hands became a blur, moving based on the memories of the Blood Demon and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He unleashed the overwhelming strikes of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art with the speed of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

BA-BA-BA-BANG!

The successive impacts sounded like a string of explosions. Lee Sungmin looked down at his own throbbing hands. It was his side that had burst open.

*I see.*

His assessment was complete. The question of where his prediction went wrong was not even worth considering. In this space, Wiji Hoyeon had surpassed her own limits time and time again. This world projected every being that had lived in the last one. All of them had rushed at her with the intent to kill, and she had killed every single one. Here, she was not the Demonic Spirit's arrangement to resist The End but a disaster executing it.

In the end, she had killed every being in the world. In this space, Wiji Hoyeon was the Slaughter Predator and Greed. She fought without rest, turning every living thing into a corpse. In that process, her martial arts shed all minor superfluities and reached the ultimate form for the sole purpose of killing. Faster, stronger, more perfect.

She also had no hesitation. Lee Sungmin knew his opponent was Wiji Hoyeon. He loved her and did not want to hurt her, which forced him to hold back. He was not even considering killing her. He had to subdue her somehow.

Wiji Hoyeon, however, did not know him. Her broken mind could not even recall his existence. The very reason she had become this way was for him, because she wanted to return to the time they had spent together. The current Wiji Hoyeon did not even remember that. She was only thinking that she had to do it. Killing was a familiar act for her now. She performed it mechanically, with not a shred of personal emotion.

That hurt so much. The sense of Despair that Jeniela had wanted to give him was creeping through his chest, a cold and spreading poison. He wanted to kill the Spirit. He wanted to rip apart the being who had made Wiji Hoyeon like this, who was probably recording and gleefully observing this fight even at this very moment.

"I have to do it."

He heard her mutter again. Her attacks were neither predicted, nor seen, nor felt. He held on, putting up a solid defense, but his Protective Qi Aura shattered and his armor broke.

There was a crunching sound.

It was the sound of his left shoulder shattering. He instantly regenerated and took half a step back, thrusting a palm forward to explode the space between them. Wiji Hoyeon's body was pushed far back. In that brief moment, their eyes met. Looking into her two eyes, which held only killing intent, he felt as if his own emotions were being murdered.

A half-hearted approach would not work. Subdue her without injury? That was being far too greedy. Against a Wiji Hoyeon who was dead set on killing him, such a soft heart would only get him killed.

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth as a violet Protective Qi Aura wrapped around his entire body.

Though she was pushed back by the shock of the explosion, Wiji Hoyeon was uninjured. She moved recklessly. At this close range, her movements could not be tracked by his senses. He would have to predict them. Trusting an uncertain prediction, Lee Sungmin moved. He did not need to block or dodge everything. In this situation, that was the one overwhelming advantage he had in this fight.

KWA, KWA, KWANG!

Explosions burst intermittently. The entire space shook, and the corpses piled on the floor vanished without a trace. Wiji Hoyeon's body fell downward. Before her feet could touch the ground, Lee Sungmin pursued her with the speed of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

She looked up at his approach. Her Black Dragon Armor, which had served as her limbs, had vanished, but in its place, the enhanced qi she released took an offensive form. The swelling energy gathered behind her back and exploded. It was as black as her hair, and the dark enhanced qi completely covered Lee Sungmin's vision.

The Formless Spear in his hand shot forward. In cases like this, the technique that always opened a path was Void Path, one of the forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The fiercely rotating force enveloped Wiji Hoyeon's power entirely, opening a direct path to her.

Wiji Hoyeon was not flustered in the slightest. She did not know who Lee Sungmin was, but she recognized the martial art he was using. The scattered power gathered back to her as her hands came together in front of her chest.

KABOOOOOM!

A massive explosion erupted. If this place had been Eria instead of an isolated space, the very geography of the land would have changed. The moment Lee Sungmin sensed the explosion, he used Gale Thunder and Blink consecutively to escape the blast radius.

"Crazy…" Lee Sungmin let out the mutter without realizing it.

He could not see anything. The mountain of corpses had vanished in an instant. In the gaping hole left in the ground, chaos swirled before it was immediately filled with earth. It seemed that destroying this space with force was impossible.

*In the end, I have to do something about Wiji Hoyeon.*

He could not use the key in this situation. The key was something whose existence even the Spirit did not know, created by the Demonic Spirit with great care alongside Wiji Hoyeon herself. Originally, it should have been given to her, not him. The key being given to Lee Sungmin was itself a deception by the Demonic Spirit. At that point, the Spirit's attention had been focused on Wiji Hoyeon, not him.

Spirit, Demonic Spirit, Slaughter Predator. There was no communication between these three beings. The awakened Slaughter Predator did not even know of the key's existence, and the Spirit was the same. Only the Demonic Spirit created and hid it. If things had gone as planned, Lee Sungmin, awakened as the Slaughter Predator, should have been killed by Wiji Hoyeon, and the key would have been given to her.

He dismissed the thought of the key. There was no point in thinking about something he could not use right now. To use it, he had to defeat Wiji Hoyeon somehow and make Greed reveal itself.

Despite causing such a cataclysmic explosion, she was not the least bit tired. She chased him with emotionless, blunt eyes. When their gazes met, Lee Sungmin forced a smile. There was no change in her expression.

"I'm sorry," he muttered in a small voice.

He knew it could not be helped. He knew she was not doing this because she wanted to, and he knew that the fundamental reason she had become this way was because of his own weakness. So he had to apologize. For coming too late, for placing such a heavy burden on her, and for the pain he would now have to inflict.
