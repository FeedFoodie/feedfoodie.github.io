Was this luck or misfortune? Jaegal Taeryeong could not decide. He had only come to Devon seeking Aladur, who had finally resurfaced after a long absence. His goal was simple, to have the sorcerer track down the Phantom Spear, who was rumored to be hiding in the city, yet before he could even make the request, he had stumbled right into his quarry.

This was no planned meeting. If it had been, he certainly would not have come alone. While he had no concrete proof that the clumsy scholar and the quiet Hunter before him were the infamous Phantom Spear and Crazy Heavenly Demon, a certainty settled deep in his gut. Faces and clothes were trivial things, easily disguised.

"I'm not sure what you're talking about."

Lee Sungmin had no desire for friction with Jaegal Taeryeong. Though he was a branded demonic practitioner with a target on his back, he did not want to step forward and escalate the problem. For now, he took a step back, feigning ignorance.

"I asked who you are."

Jaegal Taeryeong's attitude softened slightly as he assessed the situation. He was alone. They were two. If they truly were the Phantom Spear and the Crazy Heavenly Demon, his own skills would not be enough to handle even one of them. What would happen if he pressed the issue and provoked them into violence?

*A pointless death.*

That thought alone was reason enough for Jaegal Taeryeong to change his tone.

[What are you going to do?] the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked, his eyes fixed on Jaegal Taeryeong.

Lee Sungmin had also sensed the shift in the man's demeanor. Their interests had aligned. Regardless of the truth, Jaegal Taeryeong did not want a confrontation, at least not right now.

"I'm just a scholar passing by," Lee Sungmin answered.

"And I'm a Hunter passing by," the Crazy Heavenly Demon chimed in.

Lee Sungmin wondered if that was really necessary, but Jaegal Taeryeong merely twitched an eyebrow, offering no other reaction.

"I am Jaegal Taeryeong," he said, introducing himself. "Let's meet again next time."

"If we can."

He could kill Jaegal Taeryeong right here. The thought was a fleeting one, dismissed almost as soon as it formed. Doing so would make him a sworn enemy of the entire Jaegal Clan, and his infamy would only grow, and then what? Would he have to kill every martial artist they sent after him for revenge? It might not be difficult, but it would be a troublesome, unending annoyance. If his reputation as a demonic practitioner grew like that, people would flock from all over just for a chance to kill him.

[All you need is to have power,] Void Monarch interjected. [You just need enough power to slaughter every last one of those bastards who come to kill you. Limitless power becomes an object of fear, and eventually, it even earns reverence. Don't you have any desire to reign?]

*Someone like me?* Lee Sungmin replied with a mental chuckle.

[At your level, no one would laugh if you spoke of reigning.]

*Others might see it that way, but not me. My perspective has become too high.*

The Six Sages of the Outer Heavens. The monsters of Predator. The Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance. Those were only the powerhouses he knew of, and then there was Wiji Hoyeon. Mages likely had their own outstanding experts, and the true strength of Envirus, Lloyd's Master, remained an unknown.

[There's no end to comparing yourself to those better than you,] Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin found himself laughing at those words.

*I know.*

Even so.

*I'm glad I can go further.*

He liked that ambition, the feeling that he could go farther and become greater. Perhaps this was his limit, but he refused to think that way. Wiji Hoyeon was still so far ahead, and he wanted to close that distance.

The Fist Honored One's location had been identified. The Elf Forest. Unsure of where that was, Lee Sungmin had asked Nebul.

[If you mean the Elf Forest... you must be referring to the Primordial Forest,] the answer had come back almost instantly. [The Primordial Forest is in the South. However, getting inside won't be easy. Elves, both now and in the past, are traded as a premium commodity, so many slave Hunters target them. To fend off such wicked people, the Primordial Forest has many protective measures in place. The home of the Elves is one of the safest places in the world.]

So he knew the location. By placing a curse on Wiji Hoyeon, the Fist Honored One had become weakened, just as she had. With his current strength, Lee Sungmin was confident he could kill the man without much difficulty, even if he was a master of the Transcendent Realm. The true challenge was entering the Elf Forest itself.

Though he wanted to head there immediately, Lee Sungmin pushed the thought aside. Not yet. He first had to finish his business in the South, which meant learning to handle Demonic Energy and claiming Void Monarch's treasure. Heading to the Elf Forest after that was the proper course of action.

"Who were those guys who just came by?"

Only after confirming that Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon had vanished did Jaegal Taeryeong turn on Aladur, pressing him for their identities.

Aladur blinked. "Weren't they a Hunter and a scholar?" he answered, dropping his usual flippant demeanor in the presence of a Jaegal.

Jaegal Taeryeong let out a frustrated breath and strode toward him. "I want to find the Phantom Spear and the Crazy Heavenly Demon."

"Well... Jaegal Taeryeong, sorcery isn't omnipotent, you know. If you want to find someone, you need one of their belongings, or something like a strand of hair."

He had already secured such a thing. Through the Black Hound Leader, he had received fine hairs from the cave where Lee Sungmin's party had stayed. When Jaegal Taeryeong handed them over, Aladur began his spell, just as he had when searching for the Fist Honored One.

"...Huh, but these are the guests who were just here..."

There was no need to hear more. Without waiting for another word, Jaegal Taeryeong activated his movement arts and vanished from before Aladur.

Namgung Heewon showed no emotion. Ever since entering the Supreme Peak, his lighthearted fighting spirit had disappeared. Gossips across the world often whispered, wondering if his sword had already surpassed that of the Namgung Patriarch. What was certain was that the Sword Dragon, Namgung Heewon, was the finest heir among the four great Noble Clans.

*He's become a completely different person.*

Tang Ahee watched him from a crooked stance. Surrounding Namgung Heewon were the fifteen members of the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps. She was similarly attended. Ten members of the Dark Night Black Mist Squad, dressed in black martial arts uniforms, stood like faint shadows escorting her.

"The Phantom Spear and the Crazy Heavenly Demon," Tang Ahee said with a snickering laugh.

Namgung Heewon glanced in her direction.

"If my memory serves me right, the Phantom Spear is the person you introduced as your little brother at Shaolin about seven years ago."

"You're probably right," Namgung Heewon replied, his tone indifferent.

Wanting to provoke a reaction from him, Tang Ahee pressed on. "The little brother you so proudly introduced has become a demonic practitioner, and now we're here to hunt him down. How does it feel?"

"What the hell is your problem?" Namgung Heewon's mouth finally opened.

Tang Ahee's eyes widened at the sudden curse.

"I'm asking what your damn problem is," he repeated, his voice low. "You've always been a pain, but you're especially aggravating today. Are you on your period or something?"

"...What did you just say?"

"Are you bleeding from your crotch? I'm asking because you shouldn't pick a fight with someone who's minding their own business. Not unless you want to die."

Tang Ahee looked as if she had been struck on the back of the head by his coarse words. The Dark Night Black Mist Squad did not stand by idly. They drew sharp hidden weapons, unleashing their killing intent toward Namgung Heewon, who stood stock-still, receiving it head-on. Behind him, the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps tensed.

"Enough." Jaegal Taeryeong raised a hand, looking at the two of them with annoyed eyes. "I'm grateful you've stepped up to help, but if you're going to fight amongst yourselves, you're worse than useless."

"I just don't want to miss out on such good prey," Tang Ahee spat. She shot a cold glare at Namgung Heewon before turning back to Jaegal Taeryeong. "The Phantom Spear is a demonic practitioner who suddenly gained infamy, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon is a long-standing one. It's a mystery why he was left alone until now. I can't let you, Brother Jaegal, monopolize the honor of capturing and killing them both."

"You should speak correctly," Namgung Heewon chuckled. "You mean you want to stick your spoon in. Huh?"

"...You're the same!"

"I just want to see my little brother after a long time."

"Surely you don't intend to side with the Phantom Spear?"

"If my little brother walks a stray path," Namgung Heewon replied, "then as his big brother, I must correct his way."

Jaegal Taeryeong gave up trying to stop them. It did not matter how it turned out. The forces of the Tang and Namgung Clans were formidable, and Namgung Heewon's sword was so outstanding that even he could not handle it.

"Isn't this too much?" Aladur grumbled from beside him. "Forcing someone who doesn't want to do it to be your guide!"

"I'll give you as much money as you want." At Aladur's shout, Jaegal Taeryeong mentally pictured the character for patience and spat out his words. Aladur had been making such noises the entire way here. "I said I'll give you as much money as you want. So stop throwing a fit and just do your job. Don't try to squeeze out any more!"

"Aigoo, I understand." Realizing he could not get any more money by throwing a tantrum, Aladur nodded.

He picked up one of the few remaining strands of Lee Sungmin's hair and set it on fire. While his other spells were mediocre, his tracking magic had already reached the level of a Grand Sorcerer. From the time he studied under his Master long ago, he had focused all his effort on learning tracking magic, believing it would be a useful way to make money.

The smoke from the burning hair swirled around him. He opened his mouth wide and swallowed it. The combined forces of the Namgung, Jaegal, and Tang Clans moved as one in the direction Aladur indicated.

"What kind of mess is this to get into after coming for a first birthday party," Namgung Heewon muttered with a reluctant expression. Even so, his eyes flickered toward Moyong Seojin, who stood beside Jaegal Taeryeong. He had secretly hoped she would remain with the Jaegal Clan, but Jaegal Taeryeong had brought her along, claiming he was short on manpower. At the rear of the group, not only the four great Noble Clans but also the Black Hound Leader and his squad members followed the march.

Having leaped over the gates of Devon, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon ran forward without looking back. Information from Nebul had already confirmed that the Jaegal Clan's subjugation force was on the move. The forces of the Namgung and Tang Clans had joined them, as had the Black Hound Squad that had been tracking them all along.

"What's your plan?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked, running beside him. "The pursuit until now has been mere surveillance, but it's different now. They're coming to kill us."

"I don't want to cause any friction yet."

"So you intend to keep going with that tail on us?"

"If possible."

Whether killing them all was easy or difficult was irrelevant. If he did, he would become a public enemy of the orthodox sects. They could not pursue him forever, so for now, he planned to put as much distance between them as possible.

[This turned out well,] Void Monarch said in a cheerful voice, having confirmed the direction Lee Sungmin was heading. [If you go in this direction for about a week, you'll reach the forest this Elder once ruled.]

*What?*

[Didn't I tell you before? That I would give you the treasures this Elder has collected. There are quite a few interesting things there, so take this opportunity to claim them.]

Void Monarch had mentioned it subtly before. Lee Sungmin was not in dire need of money, but the thought of treasures collected by the ancient being piqued his curiosity. Since it was on the way to his destination, there was no reason to refuse.

*I just hope they give up and fall off before then,* Lee Sungmin thought, clicking his tongue at the thought of the pursuers on his trail.
