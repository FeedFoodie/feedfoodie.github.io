The undead swarming his path were no match for Abel. He strode forward without chanting a single spell or forming any beastfolk, yet none of the creatures could get close to him. If any dared to cross a certain threshold, an explosion would erupt, annihilating their bodies without a trace. This magical barrier consumed almost no energy, a clever adaptation of a spell he had originally developed for catching mosquitoes in the summer. By adjusting the total amount of magic poured into it, he could catch things far larger than a mere insect.

"You brute," Frau grumbled.

"Call it being resourceful," Abel retorted with a shrug.

He had simply increased the spell's firepower and set its targets to undead instead of mosquitoes, incinerating any that came within a five-meter radius. No matter how much the undead struggled, they were instantly reduced to ash the moment they crossed that invisible line. The stench of burning corpses was foul, but it was a clean way to advance.

"It's noisy."

KWA-A-A-ANG!

As if on cue, another explosion rocked the ground, the massive vibration nearly throwing Abel off his feet. He steadied himself and glanced toward the source, the direction where Lee Sungmin and Vollander had been thrown. Pitch-black darkness and violet currents erupted ceaselessly from that area.

"I agree," Abel said, his walk coming to a halt.

From the sky, Kim Jonghyun descended, slow and deliberate. His black robe fluttered as he looked down and smiled. At his arrival, the hundreds of zombies made bizarre noises, faltering before they retreated and knelt. Receiving the worship of the corpses, Kim Jonghyun hovered above their heads while Abel watched him with a bored expression.

"Mages Guild Master."

Abel's eyes scanned Kim Jonghyun's attire. The robe looked ordinary, but he could faintly sense the power of nearly ten grand magics woven into its fabric. The staff in his hand pulsed with an unusual power, and the grimoire in his left was surely just as potent. A direct battle of firepower would put him at a disadvantage, a fact that made him conscious of the Gries tucked inside his own robes.

"I tried to find out about you through various means, but you're a more secretive person than I expected," Kim Jonghyun said. "Then again, even back when I was the Black Magic Tower Master, I barely managed to hear your voice."

"Do you remember that? It was years ago," Abel sneered. "I thought you were damn suspicious even back then. It was just a hunch without any proof, but looking at it now, my gut was right. I should've just crushed you then and there."

"Hahaha!" Kim Jonghyun laughed loudly. "Won't you at least tell me your name?"

"Abel," he answered without hesitation. Giving his name was hardly a difficult task. "This might be our first time meeting like this, but you've met my older brother, haven't you?"

"...Pardon?"

Kim Jonghyun's eyes widened. He knew many things and had orchestrated many events, but he had never known that Abel was the younger brother of Envirus, Cain.

"I know you were in Urmuri," Abel continued. "I also know you backstabbed that idiot, Arbes, to gain power you didn't deserve."

"What are you talking about?"

"Have you forgotten the name Envirus? Or do I have to say Cain for you to get it?"

"Ah." A flicker of understanding crossed Kim Jonghyun's face before he burst into laughter. He threw back the hood of his robe and shook his head. "Ah, ahh. I see. I was wondering what you meant. An older brother. To think the Mages Guild Master is Envirus's brother. Do you know just how disgracefully your brother acted in Urmuri?"

"I know," Abel nodded. He knew everything that had happened there. Envirus had failed to subdue Arbes, though the situation had been absolutely disadvantageous for him. He had stagnated for a hundred years, unable to use his original magic, while Arbes had a century of growth behind him and was wielding the magic power of all of Urmuri. Envirus's specialty, Silent Casting, was only on par with what Arbes could achieve with that power, and his Elemental Magic was a poor match against Arbes's soul-destroying spells.

"My brother was a useless fool." Abel offered no defense for Envirus. It was Envirus and no one else who had brought a century of stagnation upon himself by fleeing from his own destiny. He knew about the fate called The End, yet he had failed to prepare adequately. "If it had been me in Urmuri instead of my brother, I would've killed both you and Arbes."

"Hahaha!" Kim Jonghyun laughed again.

Nearby, Frau narrowed her eyes, watching him. Her sight perceived something Abel could not. All the souls stagnating throughout the city were connected to Kim Jonghyun.

"You must have heard from Lee Sungmin what I'm trying to do," Kim Jonghyun said.

"I did. I hear you're trying to pull off something truly insane."

"I'm doing this for the sake of this world," he declared. "The End... huhu! To avoid that fate, I'm simply using the most effective method available to me."

"No, that's a lie," Abel shot back. "You're doing this for your own amusement. As a Quasi-Demon King, when the Great Demon World connects to this one, you'll receive the favor of the Demon God. Did he promise you the rank of a Great Demon Lord or something?"

"How could a lowly being such as myself ever be granted an audience with the Demon God? I have been promised nothing."

"So you really are just doing this for your own amusement," Abel said. "Still, I'll ask just in case. If you and I joined forces, we could steer this world away from the fate of The End, and we could do it without connecting to the Great Demon World."

"I refuse," Kim Jonghyun replied, not even giving the proposal a moment's consideration. "That doesn't sound like any fun."

"Crazy bastard," Abel muttered with a hollow laugh. In the end, Kim Jonghyun couldn't care less if Eria was culled by The End. It made sense. If he chose, he could simply escape this world that was destined for ruin, just as he could have crossed through the gate to the Great Demon World back on the vast sea.

"See? You're just a crazy bastard." As he muttered, Abel stepped forward.

FWOOSH...

The 'mosquito net' covering the area vanished. Kim Jonghyun smiled faintly, sensing the change in the concentration of magic power around Abel. There was no need to give an order.

KRAAAAA!

The zombies that had been bowing their heads roared and charged forward.

"Frau," Abel said, raising both his hands.

Frau sighed as her hair slowly began to float upward. Having come this far, there was no turning back, though she honestly hated getting involved in this kind of mess. Behind her, Aladur flinched and retreated, failing to live up to his large frame.

Frau raised both her hands, and Kim Jonghyun's eyes narrowed as he watched her. Frau Hoz. A Grand Sorcerer. He knew that much, but he had no way of knowing what kind of power she actually possessed.

A low vibration started at Frau's feet, and moments later, grayish Evil Spirits erupted from the ground around her. With a slow extension of her hand, she sent them surging toward the charging zombies. The dozens of spirits were ethereal bodies, immune to physical harm. They weaved between the zombies, their unsettling laughter echoing as claws passed harmlessly through their forms. The bodies of any zombies touched by the Evil Spirits trembled violently. They made bizarre noises, turned around, and began to attack their own kind. Kim Jonghyun's eyes glittered as he watched the chaos unfold.

*An ability that could be called the antithesis of the Undead. If they had put her at the forefront, the battle with the zombies at the gate would have been completely one-sided.*

And yet, they had brought her all the way here. Kim Jonghyun didn't overlook that fact. There must be something else, something only Frau could do. This was the first time she had displayed her abilities since they invaded Germud.

"What are you looking at?" Abel's voice rang out.

He had risen to the same altitude as Kim Jonghyun and was ostentatiously forming beastfolk.

*Ah.* Kim Jonghyun chided himself in amusement. *Right, now is not the time to worry about Frau.* His opponent was a secretive mage, a man known only by his title.

Kim Jonghyun quickly raised his staff. He had not been idle during his time in Germud. Though he had hoped Vollander would be enough to handle things, he had prepared thoroughly for the chance that he wouldn't be. His staff trembled. The reason he had come all this way was...

*He must have laid down magic traps.*

He had embedded formulas and magic power into the space, with the smallest motion as a trigger, allowing the magic to unfold instantly. It wasn't for nothing that people said never to fight a mage in their own territory. A sufficiently prepared mage could manifest magic with no casting time.

Abel knew that fact better than anyone, and Kim Jonghyun didn't know Abel. He was different from Envirus, from his innate talent to his very disposition. Envirus had created Silent Casting, but Abel lacked such a gift. He had no idea what his brother might be thinking, but to Abel, Envirus was just an annoying son of a bitch. He didn't like that his brother was more talented, and he liked his innate personality even less.

"Son of a bitch," Abel cursed, thinking of the brother who wasn't even present as his eyes flew open.

Abel himself knew best what to watch out for in a fight against another mage. Most of his life had been dedicated to practicing magic, and like any other mage, he had a lifelong ambition. His was to completely devour all of his brother Cain's magic.

Abel's eyes flashed. In the instant before Kim Jonghyun's pre-set magic could manifest, he saw it all. He perceived the hundred attack spells woven into the very space around them and saw the nine that were about to activate at Kim Jonghyun's will. He could intervene. As his lips twitched, Abel's own magic surged forth, intervening in that fractional moment to distort the formulas just before they could take shape.

Kim Jonghyun's expression turned to one of surprise as the magic failed to activate. For someone else to forcibly intervene and nullify his spells was not impossible, but it was by no means easy. All nine spells had been dispelled, and that wasn't the end of it. Abel had already finished scanning every spell installed in the area, discerning which had priority and which were easiest to dismantle. All he had to do now was calmly unravel them one by one. For Abel, this was easy. He had spent most of his life learning magic, focusing specifically on becoming proficient at this sort of thing.

"...Huuuk!"

A ragged breath hissed from Lee Sungmin's lips as he slowly lowered his outstretched spear. Before him, in the center of the clearing, stood Vollander. Lee Sungmin had been relentless, his spear a constant storm of thrusts that had shattered every one of the Death Knight's attacks and smashed his armor beyond recognition.

[But he is not dead.] The Void Monarch clicked his tongue. That was the important part. Vollander had not died. Since he was already dead to begin with, it was more accurate to say he hadn't been annihilated.

"...Ah."

A small voice leaked from beneath the helmet. Vollander moved his creaking body and slowly lifted his head, the light in his dim eyes flaring once more.

"If only I were human," Vollander muttered as he stepped forward. His crushed armor restored itself to its original state, and after just a few steps, he was completely unscathed. As a Death Knight, he felt no pain and no fatigue. "If I were, I could have met a satisfying death at your hands just now."

[What a persistent bastard.] The Void Monarch clicked his tongue in amazement.

Lee Sungmin's body was fine. It could still move. He had plenty of Demonic Energy and inner qi left, and physical fatigue wasn't a problem. He slowly raised his spear again.

*This is actually for the better.*

He truly thought so. The more his body broke down and regenerated, the more he moved his spear, the more proficient he became at executing the martial arts of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman that he held in his mind.

[But it is dangerous,] the Void Monarch warned. [The body's regeneration is not all-powerful. The more it repeats, the closer you become to a Yokai, not a human.]

A human body did not possess such regenerative power.

"I know," Lee Sungmin said, slowly spinning his spear.

Amidst the whistling of the weapon, he had only one thought.

*Will Vollander break first, or will I?*
