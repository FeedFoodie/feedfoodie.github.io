"A dragon's... heart?"

The words left Lee Sungmin's lips in a stunned whisper. He had trained himself not to be easily surprised, but the Void Monarch's claim was so staggering that his jaw fell slack. His gaze dropped to the object resting inside the box, a genuine Dragon's Heart.

"You don't mean... a *Dragon Heart*?" he asked, the words catching in his throat.

[They call it that, too.]

The Void Monarch attempted a nonchalant tone, but an undeniable pride bled through his words, and it was a pride well deserved. A Dragon Heart was one of the most difficult treasures in the world to obtain. In this era, even encountering a dragon was a near impossible feat. To actually kill one and extract its heart was a task beyond imagination.

The Void Monarch's excited laughter boomed in his head.

[Back in this Elder's prime, I tell you! Dragons? Puhaha, those little bastards, if they flew over my head~ WHAM! Hey, you punk!] he chattered on. [I'd jump on their backs, snap their wings right off, and when they'd fall crying 'Oh, please spare me,' I'd just finish 'em off right there!]

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard, only half-listening to the rambling boast. To think he would ever see a Dragon Heart with his own eyes. He cautiously reached out, but the Void Monarch frantically stopped him.

[Wait!]

"What is it?"

[You can't take it out. A Dragon's Heart is a tricky thing. If it's exposed to the outside air, it'll turn to dust and disappear immediately.]

"Then why did you stick it in here?"

[I put it in as a trophy.]

"Something you can't even use?"

[What does it matter? This Elder had no need for it anyway.]

"I need it."

[To eat it? Well, that might be fine... but you can't use it right now, so take the whole box. If you ask a skilled mage later, you might find a way.]

Following the Void Monarch's advice, Lee Sungmin closed the lid of the box. He had no idea how to consume a Dragon Heart anyway and decided he would ask Erebrisa about it later. For now, he would keep it safe.

[Now, open the other boxes. There's a lot of good stuff.]

Heeding the urging, Lee Sungmin moved to another chest. This one was filled to the brim with dazzling gold coins. He picked one up. The currency used in Eria was the Ern, which were regular coins, bills, or checks, not solid gold.

[Their value is different from that flimsy paper money. With the gold coins in that box, you could probably buy an entire city.]

His own funds were starting to run low. While he rarely had expenses from sleeping outdoors, he tended to spend excessively when he did, and he had not earned any money in a long time. Lee Sungmin placed the entire box of gold into his Subspace Pocket.

The next box he opened contained a trove of jewels, their value likely several times that of the gold. He stored that one in his Subspace Pocket as well. Several more chests after that also held gold and jewels. While Lee Sungmin believed one could never have too much money, the sheer amount before him was beginning to strain his sense of reality.

*Just how much is all this?*

His Subspace Pocket was soon full. He took out another one and continued storing the boxes until all the gold and jewels were packed away. Only a few chests remained.

[The real stuff starts now,] the Void Monarch announced.

Lee Sungmin approached the nearest box and opened it. A blast of cold air instantly hit his face, so intense that he stumbled back a few steps. White frost had already begun to creep over the lid. Though he had reached the state of Extreme Temperature Immunity, the chill emanating from the box pierced straight through his resistance, freezing him from the inside out.

[That's a 10000 Year Snow Ginseng.]

Fighting off the cold, Lee Sungmin moved closer. Lying in the center of the intense chill was a ginseng the size of his forearm.

[Try eating it. Normally, the chill is so intense that you'd have to suppress it before eating, but you should be able to handle it.]

He remembered that his Black Heart allowed him to absorb miracle herbs directly. He picked up the 10000 Year Snow Ginseng. Despite the powerful cold it emitted, it felt only moderately cool in his hand. Lee Sungmin opened his mouth wide and bit down.

A refreshing coolness spread through his mouth as he chewed. The ginseng went down his throat and flowed directly into his qi center, where a cold energy began to swirl. He immediately sat down and began circulating his qi. The inner arts contained within the 10000 Year Snow Ginseng were enormous, at least twice that of the Great Cycle Pill he had taken before.

"......"

When he finished his qi circulation and stood, his body felt much lighter.

[Open the next box,] the Void Monarch urged.

Lee Sungmin was reminded once again what a miraculous, once-in-a-lifetime encounter this was. When they first met, he had considered the Void Monarch an annoying pest, but not anymore. The spirit had helped him overcome several crises.

He opened the next box. Inside lay an inner core the size of a fist.

[It's the inner core of a Human-Faced Spider. It was a pretty old and troublesome one. They say if you eat it, you'll complete... what was it... Myriad Poison Immunity. This Elder quite enjoyed eating poison, so I never ate it.]

"You enjoyed eating poison?"

[The tingling sensation is quite nice. It's the best when mixed with alcohol,] the Void Monarch said with a chuckle.

It was a story Lee Sungmin could not comprehend, but the prospect of achieving Myriad Poison Immunity was tempting. Poison could be fatal if one was caught unawares. He popped the Human-Faced Spider's inner core into his mouth.

The taste was absolutely foul, but he could clearly feel the increase in his inner arts as a strange energy spread throughout his body.

[You have acquired the skill Myriad Poison Immunity.]

It had been a while since he had heard that voice. Lee Sungmin brought up his status window and confirmed that Myriad Poison Immunity had been added to his skill list.

[These are the dragon's bones and scales. They belong to the owner of the heart you took earlier.]

Several boxes were filled with gleaming golden bones and scales.

[If you take them to a skilled blacksmith, they'll be able to process them for you.]

Only one box remained. Inside the last chest was a single gourd bottle.

"What's this?"

[That is one of the most precious treasures this Elder possesses!] the Void Monarch exclaimed excitedly.

"So what is it?"

[Prepare to be amazed. That gourd produces an endless supply of alcohol, and not just any alcohol, but a heavenly liquor so fine that once you take a sip, you can't stop drinking until you pass out dead drunk! That kind of divine drink just keeps flowing! Kaaah! How this Elder has missed that drink during the 400 years I've been without a body!]

"...It produces alcohol?"

[That's right! An endless supply of alcohol no matter how much you drink! You can't even imagine how many drunkards challenged this Elder, coveting that gourd.]

Despite the Void Monarch's rambling, Lee Sungmin felt no desire for the gourd. He did not particularly enjoy alcohol, and the inner cores felt far more valuable than a bottle of endless liquor.

[Damn brat. You wouldn't know a treasure if you held it in your hands,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

With that, he had collected all the treasures. The Dragon's Heart, the 10000 Year Snow Ginseng, the Human-Faced Spider's inner core, the dragon's remains, and the mountains of gold and jewels. He honestly wanted to leave the gourd behind, but the Void Monarch pestered him until he finally put it in his Subspace Pocket. With nothing left to take, Lee Sungmin exited the space.

"You're back?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes gleamed the moment Lee Sungmin walked out. He nodded as he saw that the Evil Spirits, scattered by the Red Ghost's appearance, had gathered around them again.

"...Hmm."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's expression shifted to one of surprise. The aura now radiating from Lee Sungmin was incomparably stronger than before. It was only natural. Consuming the 10000 Year Snow Ginseng and the Human-Faced Spider's inner core had more than doubled his internal power.

"Did you have another miraculous encounter?" The Crazy Heavenly Demon was not even surprised anymore.

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to offer a bitter smile, the forest convulsed.

RUMBLE!

A massive tremor, far greater than when the Red Ghost appeared, shook the ground. The Evil Spirits trembled violently and fell to their knees as the earth heaved. The Void Monarch's mansion, which had been barely holding its shape, finally collapsed into rubble.

[My house!] the Void Monarch cried out.

Tense, Lee Sungmin scanned his surroundings as the vibrations continued. ZING, a shiver ran through his body. It was not a warning from his senses. He found himself turning in a specific direction without even realizing it.

[Hmm...]

Even the clamorous Void Monarch fell silent, having felt the same pull.

Something was not far away. He did not know what it was, but the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy inside his body was urging him toward it.

[...Are you going?] the Void Monarch asked. If he had a body, he would have already been on his way.

Lee Sungmin remained silent. He saw no reason why he had to go.

[I won't push you. It could be dangerous.]

"There's no real need to go, is there?" Lee Sungmin said after some thought. It was true. He had taken everything he could from this forest. All that was left was to follow the Evil Spirits out.

The moment he gave that answer, the forest moved again.

RUMBLE!

The trees writhed and intertwined, forming massive walls that blocked every exit. In contrast, the trees in the direction of the ominous energy bent aside, creating a straight and undeniable path.

"What is this now?" Rubia spat, dumbfounded.

Lee Sungmin frowned, looking at the living walls and the single open road.

[It seems this forest doesn't want to let you go,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

Lee Sungmin glanced at the Evil Spirits. They were collapsed on the ground, bowing their heads toward him.

"Damn it."

It meant he had no choice. He had to go, whether he wanted to or not.

After a week of wandering the forest, the number of Lee Sungmin's pursuers had decreased significantly.

The nights were terrifying, and the days were no different. Unidentifiable Evil Spirits roamed the darkness, luring them with unpleasant screams and sobs, while the Yokai that leaped from the shadows were incomparably stronger than their daytime counterparts. Martial artists from the Noble Clans died fighting them. During the day, they tried to track Lee Sungmin using Aladur's magic, but navigating the forest's maze made following any direction impossible.

Night would fall again, the Yokai would attack, and the Evil Spirits would tempt them. It was a situation where going mad was only natural. The martial artists of the Noble Clans were strong, skilled enough to be respected anywhere in the world, but they were still human, and like any normal person, they felt fear in the face of such terror.

"We must stop the pursuit," Namgung Heewon repeated, his voice strained.

Jaegal Taeryeong tore at his disheveled hair and turned to face him. "After all this damn trouble, you want us to go back with nothing to show for it?"

"If we continue, we'll all just die a dog's death," Namgung Heewon replied, not avoiding his gaze.

The Namgung Clan's Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps had already suffered losses, as had the Tang Clan's Dark Night Black Mist Squad and the Jaegal Clan's Dark Illusion Blade Brigade. They had entered this forest as a group of forty-five. Now, not even twenty remained. Some had not died but had deserted under the cover of night. No one knew if they had escaped, died, or were still wandering lost within the trees.

"I can't allow that." Jaegal Taeryeong had grown noticeably thin and sharp-edged over the week, and what kept him going now was a useless stubbornness. "If I go back without even meeting the Phantom Spear, everyone will laugh at me. I can't let that hap..."

"You're trying to protect your honor now?" Namgung Heewon shouted. "If you continue to be stubborn, I will take the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps and leave. So..."

"You really think you can do that?" Jaegal Taeryeong sneered. "Moyong Seojin will be with me, you know?"

He smiled mockingly. "Don't say things you can't do. Of course, if you're willing to abandon Moyong Seojin and leave, I won't stop you, but can you really do that?"

Namgung Heewon's face twisted. He glanced involuntarily toward Moyong Seojin, who looked as if he never imagined he would be dragged into the argument.

"I, I..."

"You're not going to say you're leaving now, are you?" Jaegal Taeryeong taunted.

Moyong Seojin was at a loss for words. Nearby, Tang Ahee glared at them. She also wanted to stop the pursuit, but like Jaegal Taeryeong, a stubborn part of her refused to return empty-handed after coming so far.

"It would be better to just go back quietly..." Aladur began, his voice weary, unable to bear the tension any longer.

It was then that it happened.

SHLICK!

A hand burst from behind him, piercing clean through his chest.
