It was her thirty-first birthday, and though dignitaries had gathered from far and wide to celebrate, Tang Ahee's mind was elsewhere. She offered polite formalities in response to their congratulations and deflected questions from her parents and relatives about marriage, all while waiting for the banquet to finally end.

The great houses she had been close with since childhood, the Moyong, Namgung, and Jaegal Clans, had each met with misfortune. Namgung Heewon had abandoned his clan and vanished, while Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin were both dead.

The Moyong Clan's fate was particularly pitiful. After participating in the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun, the clan head Moyong Daeun and most of his elite warriors had met gruesome deaths. The barely surviving Moyong Chan was far too young and weak to rebuild his fallen house. To make matters worse, the responsibility for his father's actions, driven by a lunatic thirst for revenge before his death in the forest, fell squarely upon Moyong Chan's shoulders.

The Murim Alliance held the Moyong Clan accountable for Moyong Daeun's transgressions. He had unilaterally attacked the Mages Guild, shattered their relationship, and wielded the Pure White Martial Squad as he pleased. Consequently, the Moyong Clan was pushed to the brink of collapse. Moyong Chan possessed neither the ability to evade the responsibility demanded by the Murim Alliance nor the eloquence to argue his case. The old elders of the clan stepped forward, questioning if the treatment was too harsh, but the Alliance forcefully carried out the punishment for the crime. The Moyong Clan's wealth and land were confiscated, and the warriors of the Noble Clan abandoned the powerless family and departed.

Chwi Geol, the leader of the Pure White Martial Squad, also received disciplinary action, though to a lesser degree. He was charged with the crime of abandoning his subordinates and fleeing the forest. Chwi Geol argued that he had done it for the Phantom Spear's trash skills, but his plea failed to defend him. His excuse only led to a harsher interrogation, as he had failed to even notice the Phantom Spear leaving the forest and could not track his whereabouts.

In Tang Ahee's case, however, she received no particular pressure from the Murim Alliance. Although she had not played a significant role, she had remained in the forest and had not fled. In truth, she had run, but it was largely thanks to her joining up with other survivors of the Pure White Martial Squad before leaving the forest.

*This is my chance.*

Tang Ahee's mind raced. Among the promising prodigies of the Noble Clans, she was the only one left standing. There were currently no young martial artists receiving much attention, and her solid background as a member of the Tang Clan had not yet faded, still shining brightly behind her.

*Today. The Phantom Spear will come here.*

She was almost certain of it. It was no exaggeration to say that the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin, was the source of the biggest rumors in Eria's murim at that moment. After years of being unseen, he had suddenly garnered attention for becoming the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He had also boldly deceived everyone by infiltrating the subjugation squad composed of the Moyong Clan head and the Murim Alliance's Pure White Martial Squad.

*If I can capture the Phantom Spear.*

Tang Ahee felt a small thrill of excitement, the pounding of her heart a pleasant rhythm in her chest. If she captured the Phantom Spear alive and handed him over to the Murim Alliance, the name of Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee would echo throughout the world. She would rise to a high position within the Murim Alliance, and a bright future would be promised to her, not as a daughter of the Tang Clan, but as a martial artist in her own right.

Tang Ahee understood her position quite well. As a woman, it was impossible for her to become the head of the Tang Clan. At best, she had two choices. She could either marry a handsome man who possessed both fame and power and become the lady of the house, or she could become a great female master with immense fame and power herself.

Tang Ahee wanted both. Because she was greedy, she kept silent about matters concerning the Phantom Spear. She wouldn't borrow the hands of others. If she told the Murim Alliance or her family, they could have prepared the means to capture him in advance.

*Because there's no need.*

She didn't have to. With her skills alone, it was absolutely impossible for Tang Ahee to defeat Lee Sungmin. However, she had a method she could rely on, a method more trustworthy than any Assassination Squad in the Murim Alliance.

Tang Mugi.

Her great-great-grandfather was a figure from two hundred years ago. Although he was the most skilled master the Tang Clan had ever produced, Tang Mugi could not become the clan head because of his humble origins. He had valued his own martial arts more than his family, however, so he paid it no mind. At some point, he left the Tang Clan and vanished without a trace. After he disappeared, the direct line of the Tang Clan at the time died in a series of accidents, and Tang Mugi's bloodline became the new direct line.

The first time Tang Ahee and Tang Mugi met was when she was ten years old. Saying she resembled a daughter he had lost in an accident, Tang Mugi had stroked her hair and introduced himself as her great-great-grandfather.

*If it's my great-great-grandfather.*

Tang Ahee knew very well how skilled a master Tang Mugi was. She had never told anyone about her meetings with him, not even her father. In her childhood, she had thought she must keep her promise to her great-great-grandfather to never tell her family. Now, Tang Ahee was proud of her innocent younger self. Thanks to that, tonight, she would be able to capture the Phantom Spear with Tang Mugi's help, without anyone knowing.

After the banquet ended, Tang Ahee returned to her room early. There was still time until midnight. On any other birthday, she would have found even a brief meeting with Tang Mugi at midnight to be bothersome and annoying. She was no longer a teenage girl, after all, but things were different now. She wanted midnight to come quickly so she could meet her great-great-grandfather. It had been a long time since she had felt such anticipation for a meeting with him, an anticipation not for what kind of mysterious gift he would give her, but for him defeating Lee Sungmin for her.

Tang Ahee lay on her bed, grinning.

Lee Sungmin was watching her.

He wasn't in her room, of course.

[And here I thought she was just a moron who only knew martial arts.]

*I just didn't use it until now because there was no need.*

Lee Sungmin replied as such while looking down at the crystal orb under his palm. He had wondered what he would do if he were caught, but it seemed Tang Ahee hadn't noticed she was being watched. It was a miniature magic artifact he had purchased in Erebrisa. Its lifespan was a day at most, but once installed, it could be used to eavesdrop on the video and audio of that location.

During the banquet, Lee Sungmin had secretly infiltrated the Tang Clan, snuck into Tang Ahee's room which he had located beforehand, and installed the artifact. Since her room was filled with various valuables and ornaments, finding a place to hide it hadn't been very difficult.

*Still. I didn't think it would be this easy.*

What on earth was she thinking? Lee Sungmin frowned as he watched Tang Ahee hug a pillow and giggle. Even though the artifact was small, he wasn't accustomed to this kind of work. Deceiving the eyes of the Tang Clan martial artists, who were weaker than him, and entering her room wasn't a particularly difficult task. Still, he had been worried he might get caught because the hiding place felt precarious.

[What a fortunate wench.]

*The fact that she's not suspicious at all is what's suspicious.*

[Isn't it stranger to be suspicious? She came to her room on her birthday. What kind of lunatic would search their room thinking, 'Maybe someone stopped by while I was gone?']

*People like that could exist.*

[They're not common, and that wench doesn't seem to be that kind of person. Besides… she seems incredibly excited. Is she happy because it's her birthday?]

*…I don't know what she's thinking either.*

As Lee Sungmin pondered this, Tang Ahee was standing at the center of praise from countless people in her own delusion. She imagined cutting off the limbs of the notorious Phantom Spear, destroying his qi center, and dragging him like a dog before the Murim Alliance. Everyone would lavish praise upon her for capturing him alone, and the Murim Alliance Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, would personally commend her achievement.

*Who should I marry? Cheongmyeong of Wudang? He's a Taoist, but he's the most distinguished among the young martial artists right now. I don't like Jihak because he's bald…*

Unaware that his limbs had been severed and his qi center destroyed in Tang Ahee's delusion, Lee Sungmin watched her giggling carefully.

*Maybe that entire dazed look is an act. Maybe she already knows an artifact was installed in her room and is deceiving me without showing any sign of it. Maybe when I go to attack the Dark Supreme, the Black Dragon Knight or the Spear King will be waiting in ambush to kill me.*

[What bullshit.]

The Void Monarch let out a hollow laugh at Lee Sungmin's thought.

[I've met that wench a few times. Do you really think that little piss-ant is capable of devising such a scheme?]

*No.*

[Then why are you wasting your energy on useless worries? Stop thinking such nonsense. There's still plenty of time until midnight, so let's have a drink.]

*I let you drink yesterday, too.*

[Alcohol is meant to be drunk every day.]

*Not for me.*

The alcohol from the Void Monarch's gourd was indeed excellent, but that didn't mean he wanted to drink it every day, especially not on a day like today. The Void Monarch grumbled when Lee Sungmin didn't fall for the temptation.

[You worry too much. Didn't they say that bastard, the Wudang Sword Immortal, never leaves Mount Wudang? There's no chance the Sword Immortal will intervene in the fight with the Dark Supreme.]

*That's not necessarily true,* Lee Sungmin thought with a frown. *The easiest thing for me would be to attack the Dark Supreme as he leaves the Tang Clan and this city, but honestly, I'm not confident about that. I doubt the Dark Supreme will just return without any caution after his meeting with Tang Ahee.*

He would most likely be on guard. They said Tang Ahee was the only one in the Tang Clan who communicated with the Dark Supreme. Every year on her birthday, he supposedly met her briefly at midnight, gave her a gift and some well wishes, and then disappeared immediately.

*I'm stronger than the Dark Supreme. However, I'm not confident I can see through his stealth and track him. In that case, I have no choice but to opt for a direct confrontation. I won't let the Dark Supreme leave the Tang Clan. The moment he appears before Tang Ahee, I'll attack right then and there to take him down.*

However, even if he was stronger, killing a desperately struggling Dark Supreme would inevitably cause a commotion. A commotion would erupt within the Tang Clan, located in the heart of the city. Even at midnight, the lights of the clan compound would flare to life, and people with the surname Tang would emerge from all directions.

*That much is fine. My notoriety would grow, but I don't care about that anymore. However, if a commotion breaks out, if that commotion reaches Wudang, what if it rouses the reclusive Sword Immortal into action?*

[So. Are you not going to do it?]

*No.*

Lee Sungmin shook his head. There was no guarantee another chance to kill the Dark Supreme would come. A situation had been created where he could definitively kill a master of stealth, and he refused to kick this chance away worrying about a potential risk. If he killed the Dark Supreme, only four would remain in Heaven Beyond Heaven, including the Martial God.

*As quickly as possible.*

Lee Sungmin sat down in a cross-legged position. He recalled the movements of the Dark Supreme, whom he had fought in Ruvus several years ago. Elusive movement arts that left no trace. A variety of hidden weapons flying in an instant. The various hidden weapons he wore on his body. He must not let the Dark Supreme escape.

{sep}

It was midnight.

TAP, TAP.

Lying in bed, Tang Ahee's closed eyes snapped open at the sound of tapping on her window. She shot up and approached it. Small pebbles were rolling around outside. A bright smile spread across her face as she opened the window and leaped down.

The tree in the backyard was one she had planted herself as a child. Beneath it stood Tang Mugi, dressed in black martial arts robes. Tang Ahee gave her great-great-grandfather a radiant smile.

"You're here?"

"It's your thirty-first birthday," the Dark Supreme, Tang Mugi, replied with a pleased smile of his own.

"I thought you would be married this year."

"There just aren't any men who catch my eye."

"True. No ordinary man would dare take you for his wife."

The Dark Supreme sent a look full of affection toward his great-great-granddaughter and reached into his robes.

"I've thought long and hard about what to give you..."

"Great-great-grandfather," Tang Ahee interrupted, clinging to his arm with a voice full of charm before he could take out the gift. "Instead of a gift, could you grant me a favor?"

"A favor?"

"Of course, I must grant the request of my only great-great-granddaughter. Yes. What is it you wish to ask?"

"Well. I'd like you to subdue a certain someone for me..."

Before Tang Ahee could finish her sentence.

CRACKLE.

A violet current erupted from the darkness.
