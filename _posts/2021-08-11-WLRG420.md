The Spirit remained hidden despite the shout, for it knew its place. Here, the one who held the key was the master of the house. The Spirit ground its teeth, never having imagined the Demonic Spirit would prepare such a contingency.

It was a mistake, a colossal one. The Demonic Spirit had only ever seen Lee Sungmin as a diversion, a disposable pawn meant to hide Wiji Hoyeon, yet now, that same pawn held the key to the library.

Lee Sungmin felt no need to rush, nor did he try to locate the Spirit. He simply sat down, his mind calm. Greed was no longer moving, and The End had stopped, but that was all. The End was not truly over, for Eria still languished under its fate. What Lee Sungmin wanted was not a temporary halt but a fundamental change to that very destiny.

This was merely a way to buy time, not a solution. The Demonic Spirit's preparations had ended here. He had only explained what the key was and how to use it, not how it could alter the fate of The End.

Lee Sungmin's voice cut through the silence. "You thoughtless bastard."

"This was the best I could do," the Demonic Spirit protested, his voice dripping with frustration. It was an understandable defense, as he had truly used every method at his disposal just to barely create this situation. From this point on, everything depended on what the owner of the key did.

"Give up," the Spirit insisted from its unseen position. "Your struggles alone can't possibly change the fate of this world. It was created with that intention from the very beginning."

Lee Sungmin toyed with the key, listening to the Spirit's words.

"Do you have any idea how many incredible powers are involved in this world? Do you know how many great beings had a hand in creating and maintaining this absurd reality? This is a world that ignores even the most fundamental laws of the universe, like birth, death, and reincarnation."

*I know it well.* To be born, to die, and to reincarnate. Eria was exempt from such laws. The beings born here were nothing more than summons from the library's records, unable to even die and reincarnate. When the world finally met The End and was destroyed, it would simply repeat according to those records. This place was that kind of Slaughterhouse.

"What you're doing is just stalling for time," the Spirit pressed. "Or what, are you going to sacrifice everything you have just to live here for eons?"

"I'm considering that too," Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation. It would be one way to do it.

"You madman," the Spirit cursed.

He nodded his head. "It's not a fundamental solution, though." As long as he remained here, Greed would not devour the world, and the Spirit, also bound to this place, could pull no tricks outside. If he stayed, the world would not be destroyed, but that was all. The current Eria was merely paused on the brink of its fate, and this was not the answer Lee Sungmin wanted.

"Demonic Spirit," he called.

"......"

"What if this key had been given to Wiji Hoyeon as you planned? If she had succeeded in entering this place, what were you going to do next?"

The Demonic Spirit did not answer.

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh. "You wouldn't have made the key without a plan. You prepared this to change the fate of The End, and things went according to your plan, to some extent. The only difference is that I'm the one here, not Wiji Hoyeon."

"...I've said it before," the Demonic Spirit said with a sigh. "This world is absurd, yet convenient. In this iteration, Wiji Hoyeon obtained an absurd talent. The original plan was to bring her here and have her acquire all the knowledge in the library."

"You madman," the Spirit spat, not Lee Sungmin. "Did you really think that was possible?"

Lee Sungmin listened silently, offering no objection to the Demonic Spirit's words.

"Regardless of whether it was possible, it was the only method I could devise. For that purpose, I granted Wiji Hoyeon that much talent and fixed her status as a human, all to prevent her from heading to the Battle God Chronicle, no matter what."

"And if she acquired all the knowledge here, what then?"

"From there, I had to bet on her potential. If she could shatter the forcibly fixed status of human and transcend it through sheer will, Wiji Hoyeon would have become an Absolute Being with a corresponding status. I thought a way might open up then. I wasn't certain..."

"It was impossible from the start," Lee Sungmin chuckled after hearing the story. "It's actually a relief it turned out this way. If Wiji Hoyeon were here instead of me, she would have had a terrible time. As a human, she never would have been able to endure it." In the Spirit's world, her mind had collapsed just from killing every being in the world. It was fortunate that it had only ended there. "Forcibly fixing her as a human was the problem. When you gain power that transcends humanity, your very essence is bound to change."

The Demonic Spirit fell silent at the rebuke. After a brief pause, he offered his excuse. "I couldn't help it... I had never tested it before."

Lee Sungmin scoffed and stabbed the key into the floor. The Spirit screamed while the Demonic Spirit watched his actions. As he turned the key another half-turn, the white space trembled, but Lee Sungmin remained seated and unmoving.

"And this isn't enough to become an Absolute Being."

Records, overwritten through countless repetitions, unfolded before his eyes. He watched the fleeting images pass by as the essence of techniques accumulated by the repeating world was laid bare. Every martial art, every magic that existed in Eria, records of all those things, down to the most trivial, filled the space.

"Though it would depend on how one understands and develops them," Lee Sungmin muttered to himself, looking at the records that now filled the space.

Eria was inefficient, yet efficient. It was a contradictory statement, but that was how he saw it. The first Eria had summoned otherworlders from all sorts of worlds.

"It's a strange thing," he muttered, looking at the records. "Different dimensions have different martial arts and different kinds of magic. Some murim have the Nine Sects One Gang, and some don't. Some worlds have magic, and some don't. Even swordsmanship with the same name has minor differences in form because it developed to suit its own world. The same goes for magic. Take a basic spell like 'Fire Ball.' It's just about making a ball of fire, but the methods are diverse."

"Eria is a Slaughterhouse meant to mix all those diverse things to obtain something perfect," the Demonic Spirit said.

"By repeating a single world over and over," Lee Sungmin continued. "You should admit it. The first few times probably produced some results, but at some point, it stopped yielding them."

The Spirit and the Demonic Spirit fell silent.

"As a new Slaughterhouse opened, you probably summoned additional otherworlders to use as variables, but you couldn't have achieved any significant results. This world has already lost its reason for existing."

"No," the Spirit spat. "This world has achieved definite results. We found a turning point. We also learned that humans have far more potential than we thought, even with sufficient restrictions placed on them."

"So, do you think you can achieve greater results in the next world than in this one?"

"If we prepare thoroughly and start anew, we can achieve as many results as we want."

"No. You can't," Lee Sungmin declared. "This is the first and the last time. No matter how many times you repeat the world from now on, you will never achieve the same results as this time."

"......"

"Stop being so stubborn. You know it's true," he spat. "No matter what variables you add in the next world, you won't be able to produce results greater than this time. Maybe you could create a similar flow of events, but what's the point of that? It would just be a repeat of this world in the end."

Lee Sungmin held not a shred of doubt in his conviction. Repeating the world in the future to achieve the same results as this time was impossible.

"How... can you be so sure of that...?"

"Because it's me," Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation. "My very existence is the greatest result that can be reaped from this world."

It was an incredibly arrogant statement, yet Lee Sungmin was not the least bit ashamed. He did not even think it was arrogant, because it was an undeniable fact.

"You said it yourself that I am the proof of humanity's potential. That's right. My existence is the proof, and no greater proof will ever be born in any future world."

Lee Sungmin looked again at the records unfolding before him. Eria. This Slaughterhouse had been repeated for the seventh time. When it had repeated four times, the Dragons escaped. In the fifth and sixth worlds, there were no Dragons, and those worlds were uneventful. The Dragons' escape had been the biggest variable, and after that, two worlds had met The End and been destroyed peacefully, just as the previous worlds had been.

"You found a turning point? So what? You won't even be able to create the same flow of events in the next world."

"......"

"You can't," he said with a chuckle. "Because the most important figures have already escaped this world. The Black Heart is important, of course. It's thanks to the Black Heart that my potential was amplified, but it wasn't everything. I became this way because of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon and the Void Monarch."

*Those two will not exist in the next world.*

"Who will replace them in the next world? And who will receive the Black Heart?"

"There's the precedent of Wiji Hoyeon," the Demonic Spirit argued. "If we forcibly grant talent, we can always..."

"You don't understand. Starting with excessive talent isn't what's important. It is said that all rivers flow into the sea. Even if someone starts with an absurd talent, they won't be able to produce results greater than what we have now, and... I already told you. I am the greatest result that can be reaped from this world."

As he spoke, Lee Sungmin pulled the key from the floor, and the records that had filled the space vanished.

"Seeing the records confirmed it for me. Future repetitions are meaningless. This is the first and the last time."

"No, that's not right. In the future..."

"Your opinion doesn't matter," Lee Sungmin said with a smirk.

The Spirit shut his mouth, realizing the meaning behind his words. The Demonic Spirit also fell silent, taking a sharp breath. When he had asked about the library, the Demonic Spirit had answered him.

*As the regressions repeat, the techniques that develop in different directions are recorded in the library, and they are open to all who contributed to this world's existence.*

A place open to all who had a hand in creating this world.

Lee Sungmin took a deep breath. Everything he had said up to this point was not to persuade the Spirit. Even if the Spirit had been persuaded, as a mere administrator, he lacked the authority to change the fate of The End. The same was true for the Demonic Spirit. The ones Lee Sungmin was trying to persuade were the Absolute Beings who created Eria.

"...It can't be possible..." the Spirit muttered.

"Do you think they'll be persuaded?" even the Demonic Spirit asked in a trembling voice. "The idea, the very idea is too dangerous. They are beings beyond human comprehension."

Lee Sungmin remained silent. Perhaps even with the Dragons' magic and the martial arts of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Blood Demon, he could not perceive the Absolute Beings who looked into this world from outside Eria's dimension. To them, his power was not that great. They were Absolute Beings who could judge his existence as, at best, a superior transcendent being.

"And... even if you persuade them, in the end, nothing will change..."

The space began to tremble.

"Even if the Absolute Beings acknowledge your worth, even if they acknowledge the value of this world... true freedom will not be granted. You will only gain a distant reprieve..."

"I know," Lee Sungmin said, nodding. "I'm not asking them to acknowledge me and the value of this world to grant us a reprieve."

If that were the case, Eria would never escape its fate as a Slaughterhouse. He looked down at the key to the library. The conditions had been met. The library, a neutral ground, was a place free from all the laws that governed Eria, and he was here.

"I'm asking who will possess me and this world."

The 'true' divinities were preparing for their Advent.
