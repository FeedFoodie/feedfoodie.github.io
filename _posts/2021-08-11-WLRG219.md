Lee Sungmin opened his eyes to an empty space beside him. He sat up, running a hand through his messy hair as a flush of embarrassment crept up his neck. The memories of the previous night were still vivid. While it was not his first time with a woman, it was the first in this new life, and this was far from a simple tryst with a prostitute to satisfy some base desire.

Her dwelling was little more than a hut, which made the bedding on the floor seem all the more out of place. It was a high-quality item, soft and plush, a luxury that clashed with the rustic surroundings. His eyes fell upon a bloodstain, still clearly visible on the fine fabric, and a complex mix of emotions churned within him. He found himself wondering if someone like him truly deserved to be her first.

[What a goddamn stupid thought.]

Void Monarch's voice grumbled in his mind, yet it held no irritation. Instead, it was laced with a distinct pleasure.

[So. Was it good?]

*Shut up.*

Lee Sungmin was dumbfounded by the crude question, especially coming from that pleased tone. He told the spirit to be quiet, but Void Monarch was persistent. After remaining utterly silent the entire night, he now unleashed a flood of questions he had clearly been holding back.

[It was good, right? Huh, but you know, your technique is a bit pathetic. Moving like that isn't good for either of you. A man's hips should…]

*Ah, I said shut up!*

He wished he could cover his ears, but there was no escaping the cackling voice that continued to ring out in his head. Lee Sungmin pulled on his clothes and stood up. His threat to throw the spirit into a cesspool did nothing to deter him. Void Monarch poured out a relentless stream of questions and lectures, his resolve to say everything he wanted so firm that he seemed to welcome the prospect of a swim in filth.

"You're awake?"

The voice came from outside. A small part of him had worried she might have left, but there she was, standing just beyond the hut's entrance. She had changed out of the clothes from the night before and now wore simple martial arts attire. Lee Sungmin looked at her nonchalant expression and let out an inward sigh of relief.

"Why were you out here?"

"I woke up early," she answered with a slight smile. "And I thought it would be a little… no, very embarrassing to face you naked inside when you woke up."

Lee Sungmin flinched, his gaze darting away from hers. He never imagined Wiji Hoyeon would admit to being embarrassed. Then again, it was only natural. The events of the night had been a series of firsts for her, a venture into experiences she had never known.

"So… uh… are you okay?"

"No, I'm not," Wiji Hoyeon replied, her brow furrowing. "Maybe because it was my first time, it was very different from what I expected. It's embarrassing to say, but that was my first time doing anything like that. I've never even… done it by myself."

She shifted, spreading her legs slightly as if the movement was cumbersome.

"I haven't heard many stories about this sort of thing," she continued. "I received some basic education at the Demonic Cult when I was young, but what I knew was only theory."

"I… see."

*What kind of theoretical education could she have received?* Lee Sungmin was curious but knew better than to ask.

"I was disappointed because it was so different from my expectations," she admitted. "The pain was stronger than the pleasure. I'm used to pain, but what I felt last night was different in many ways from any pain I've known. Especially… how should I put it? I couldn't quite accept a situation where I was the only one experiencing it."

"I see…"

"It even felt a little unfair," she added. "Why did I have to be the only one in pain?"

He had no answer for that. Lee Sungmin stood there, unsure what to do, as Wiji Hoyeon narrowed her eyes and glared at him.

"You weren't in pain, were you?"

"Uh… no, I wasn't."

"That's why it's unfair," she declared. "I don't understand why I have to be the only one who hurts. If we do it again, I'll just have to hurt you every time I feel pain."

At her words, Void Monarch burst out laughing inside his head.

[She says it hurts because you're bad at it. So, follow this Elder's advice and practice day and night so it doesn't hurt.]

*How the hell am I supposed to practice?*

[There are many ways. The best is real combat, but since there's no one here to practice with… I guess you'll have to do hip exercises by yourself.]

*You're crazy…*

Despite his retort, Lee Sungmin found himself listening to Void Monarch's words quite seriously.

Wiji Hoyeon spread and closed her legs a few more times before speaking again. "I'm going to train. What about you?"

"Me? I… I have to train too," Lee Sungmin replied, feeling awkward. Despite what had happened between them, her reaction seemed no different from usual. She had admitted to being embarrassed, but her attitude remained unchanged, a strange disconnect between her words and her demeanor.

[It must be because she's embarrassed,] Void Monarch snickered. [Or maybe it's her pride. In the end, the Little Heavenly Demon is a martial artist through and through. If she was a woman last night instead of the Little Heavenly Demon, that's not the case now. Isn't that better? A single-minded martial artist by day and a shy woman by night. My, she's too good for you.]

[You need to get your head in the game too. You know full well your talent is inferior to hers. If the Little Heavenly Demon starts training in earnest, I'd say she'll surpass you in less than half a year. You don't want to be chasing her back again, do you?]

*No.*

He had made up his mind about that just yesterday. He was done chasing after Wiji Hoyeon's back. She was still his goal, his aspiration, his ideal, but he no longer wanted to watch her from an unreachable distance. He had finally reached her level, or to be precise, he was a little ahead. While he had no greedy desire to stay ahead of her forever, he certainly had no intention of falling behind.

Lee Sungmin sat down, crossing his legs to begin cultivating the Violet Dawn Divine Art he had received from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Just as he was about to start circulating his energy in earnest, his master's voice echoed in his mind.

[Come to where this one is.]

It was a one-sided command. Lee Sungmin tried to reply with his own thought transmission but could not sense the Chairman's presence anywhere. With no other choice, he had to stand up again just moments after sitting down.

"What is it?" Wiji Hoyeon asked. She was already frowning in concentration, with flowers of enhanced qi blooming around her. It seemed she could not sense the Chairman's presence either.

"My master is calling me."

"Go on," she said simply.

Her blunt dismissal made Lee Sungmin let out an unconscious chuckle. He turned and left the area around her hut. He did not know where the Chairman was, but he decided to head for his cabin first.

As expected, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was waiting in front of his cabin. He had been standing with a crooked posture among a crowd of chattering Fairy Realms, but as Lee Sungmin approached, he pushed them aside and clasped his hands behind his back. He was, of course, wearing a new mask.

"What is it?"

"Did you do it with the Little Heavenly Demon?" the Chairman asked bluntly, forgoing any greeting.

The sudden question left Lee Sungmin speechless. He hesitated, unable to form a reply, and the Chairman scoffed.

"You must have. It seems you worked hard, even putting up a sound barrier, but you can't fool this one. Besides, all the Fairy Realms in this forest know you two kissed."

"…Well, that can happen."

"What a shame," the Chairman grumbled, kicking at the ground with genuine disappointment. "Because you did it with the Little Heavenly Demon, one of your potential paths has disappeared."

"A potential path? What do you mean?"

"Virgin martial art," the Chairman answered.

Inside Lee Sungmin's head, Void Monarch erupted in laughter.

"When this one examined your body, this one was surprised to find you were a virgin at your age. As a man, it's a bit pathetic, but for a martial artist, it opens up the possibility of a wonderful skill called virgin martial art."

"What the… virgin martial art…"

"Don't look down on it," the Chairman chided. "It's incredibly difficult to remain a virgin for an entire life, but the art itself is extremely efficient if you learn it. It doesn't clash with other martial arts either. This one was planning to compensate for your lack of talent with it… hmm, but now that you've slept with the Little Heavenly Demon, that's no longer possible."

[Hey. Now's your chance. Ask him.]

*Ask him what?*

[Ask him if he learned virgin martial art or not!]

Void Monarch urged him on, practically buzzing with mischief. Lee Sungmin had to admit, after everything the Chairman had said, he was curious himself.

"Did you learn virgin martial art too, Master?"

"What are you talking about?" The Chairman let out a hollow laugh. "This one has lived a very long time, but your question is one of the most absurd things this one has ever heard. Asking if I learned virgin martial art? Are you crazy?"

"So you didn't learn it?"

"Of course I didn't. I never felt the need. This one's libido isn't particularly strong, but I quite enjoyed playing around when I was young."

[Maybe it's because he's old and can't get it up?]

"Is it because you can't get it up?"

The words slipped out before Lee Sungmin could stop them, a direct echo of Void Monarch's mutterings.

"You crazy bastard." The Chairman's voice turned to ice.

CRACKLE.

With a crackle of black electricity, the Chairman vanished. Lee Sungmin knew instinctively what was coming and tried to react, but his master was faster. A blur of Gale Thunder put the Chairman directly behind him.

A loud smack echoed through the clearing, and Lee Sungmin's body flew through the air. He landed with a groan, once again clutching his aching backside.

"It is a shame this one has no homosexual tendencies," the Chairman said in a solemn voice, "as this one cannot show you just how well and magnificently this one's member stands."

"Why my butt again…!"

"Be grateful I only smacked your bottom once for saying such insolent things to your Master."

As the Chairman spoke, Void Monarch laughed like a madman inside Lee Sungmin's head.

"I had a hunch, but I called you to confirm if you really did it with the Little Heavenly Demon," the Chairman continued as if nothing had happened. "Since you did, virgin martial art is out of the question. I'll have to think of another way."

"Another way?"

"In truth, there are plenty of methods. It's just… they're a bit much. Like virgin martial art, to gain power and efficiency that defy reason, you must give something up."

"What must I give up?"

"Do you know of a martial art called the Sunflower Manual?"

"What's that?"

"To put it simply, you give up your manhood. If you learn the Sunflower Manual, your penis will wither and eventually fall off. You'll have to live your whole life as neither a man nor a woman, but in return, you'll gain a powerful martial art and compensate for your lack of talent."

"I refuse."

"I thought so."

[I wonder if that bastard learned the Sunflower Manual too?]

Once again, Void Monarch subtly prompted him. This time, however, before Lee Sungmin could even open his mouth, the Chairman answered.

"This one did not learn the Sunflower Manual, so don't ask any pointless questions."

"I wasn't going to ask."

"As if," the Chairman grumbled.

He reached into his wide sleeve and pulled out five masks.

"Do you know face-changing?"

"I don't."

"Simply put, it's the art of rapidly changing masks. Like this."

He moved his hand in a blur.

"......"

The mask he wore changed in an instant. Lee Sungmin narrowed his eyes, trying to follow the movement, but it was impossible. The change had happened faster than his eyes could perceive.

"What this one's martial arts emphasizes is speed," the Chairman explained. "With enough speed, there is no need to add power. My Heaven Reversal Black Thunder is a martial art that pursues that ultimate speed."

Lee Sungmin had felt it himself. Since incorporating the incantations of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, his Violet Dawn Divine Art was incomparably faster. The generation of his inner arts and Demonic Energy had accelerated, which in turn increased the speed at which he could create enhanced qi and empower his own body.

"These five masks will forcibly control the inner arts and Demonic Energy you possess. You are to practice changing between them."

"For how long?"

"Until this one is satisfied. It won't be easy. This one also trained this way through face-changing after entering the Transcendent Realm and creating Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. I considered other methods, but given your talent… I think overly advanced training would be difficult. Repetitive training is the answer."

The Chairman tossed the five masks to him. Lee Sungmin caught them and looked from the masks in his hands to the one on his master's face.

"Is the mask you're wearing now also for training, Master?"

"Half right, half wrong," the Chairman said, showing off. "This mask completely blocks this one's presence while suppressing my inner arts. That's the training part. The other half is simply my preference. I told the Little Heavenly Demon this as well, but don't I look quite mysterious wearing a mask? I have many I can use, so I can choose one depending on my mood. The Fairy Realms like it too."

So even now, his master was training. Lee Sungmin was stunned. He could not follow the Chairman's movements at all, even when the man was wearing a mask that actively suppressed his power.

"Mindlessly pouring in inner arts won't create the ultimate speed," the Chairman advised, taking a step back. "Aim to create satisfactory movements with minimal energy."

He paused for a moment.

"And I won't stop you from being with the Little Heavenly Demon, but do it in moderation. What do you think the Fairy Realms will think when they see you?"

"…Yes."

Lee Sungmin nodded obediently. A flicker of satisfaction seemed to cross the Chairman, and then he vanished with a final crackle of Gale Thunder.
