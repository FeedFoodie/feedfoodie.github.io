Lee Sungmin wandered through a darkness so absolute it felt like the end of all things. No matter where he looked, there was only the pitch black of the void. He knew this space was merely a construct, a barrier designed to buy his enemies time. The suppression of his senses had been unpleasant at first, but even that was becoming a familiar sensation as the moments stretched on.

Looking around the empty expanse, he raised the spear he had already swung several times before. He knew from his previous attempts that it was largely useless. He had tried to escape this prison in various ways, but each time the space had only rippled violently in response. Completely destroying it seemed impossible.

[This is a well-made barrier space,] Heoju commented. [An ordinary barrier would have shattered, unable to withstand your power, but the level of the one who made this is different.]

Lee Sungmin knew that much. The Black Mage who had blocked his path was undoubtedly a master of his craft, though his identity remained a mystery.

*Why was I only trapped?*

[You idiot, don't ask such obvious questions,] Heoju scoffed. [If you can't affect the outside from within, how could the outside affect what's inside?]

The logic was sound. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue, knowing he couldn't afford to be complacent. Although he had been treating Envirus's problem as if it were someone else's, he couldn't just stay cooped up quietly in this space.

He steadied his breathing and tightened his grip. The spear in his hands vibrated with a low hum as his inner qi and Demonic Energy began to pour into it. The martial art he chose this time was the Seven Stars Penetrating Heaven, a technique from the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The infused energies intertwined, amplifying each other, creating a power that would continue to grow with enough time.

Of course, that power could not increase indefinitely. If it became too great, even Lee Sungmin would be unable to control it. He couldn't completely command the enhanced qi art born from his inner qi and Demonic Energy even in the heat of battle. Now, however, he had the time to distribute the power carefully.

The enhanced qi and Demonic Energy climbed the spear's shaft, twisting and rotating around each other. It had been a long time since he had executed a martial art with such meticulous care. A massive vortex of power soon enveloped the entire weapon. Lee Sungmin slowly extended it forward.

*No more than this.* This was the absolute limit of what he could handle.

How long had it been since he had truly felt the weight of his spear? He raised the speartip, aiming it into the void. With nothing directly in front of him, there was no reason to hesitate.

-KWAAAAANG!

A sound like the world collapsing rang out. The sky-piercing technique exploded from the end of his thrust, firing in a straight line that tore through the pitch-black darkness. The space rippled violently, its axis twisting until a large hole ripped open.

Lee Sungmin threw himself toward the opening that led back to the outside world.

{sep}

Having summoned the Wind Elemental, Envirus began to use his magic in earnest. As one beloved by the Elemental Queen, he could freely command the Elementals through his contract with the queen herself, and all Elementals were faithful servants to her word.

Arbes, who wielded soul-destroying magic, was not being pushed back. He actively used both the magic power of his contracted Demon Lord and the Earth's Mana to fight Envirus on even ground.

In Kim Jonghyun's eyes, their fight was nearly a stalemate. If Envirus had Elemental Magic, Arbes had soul-destroying magic. While Envirus might have been the more skilled mage, Arbes was overwhelming him in terms of raw firepower by drawing upon the Demon Lord's magic in addition to the Earth's Mana. Envirus countered that overwhelming force by summoning various Elementals, but there was a clear difference in the amount of magic power they could each command.

A Black Mage gained great power in exchange for their soul. The magic power of a Demon Lord was superior in quality, quantity, and every other aspect compared to what a human could wield. On top of that, Arbes was also drawing on the Earth's Mana. When the amount of magic power added was greater, there was bound to be a difference in power, even when using the same magic.

Elemental Magic was not without its own consumption. Even in the vast world of Eria, Spirit Magic users were extremely rare, and forming a contract with even a low-level Elemental was no easy task. If one managed it, they could receive all kinds of help from the Elemental. However, summoning an Elemental to Eria from the Elemental Realm, and then maintaining that summons, consumed a great deal of magic power.

*The efficiency is excellent. Magic cast directly by an Elemental doesn't consume the caster's magic power, but the mage has summoned the highest-ranking Elementals. Just keeping them summoned must consume a great deal of magic power, and he's casting other magic on top of that.*

No matter how skilled a mage was, their magic power had a limit. The same was true for a Black Mage. However, Arbes, who was drawing on the Earth's Mana, clearly had a much larger reserve than Envirus. It was an even match for now, but if he could just stall for time, Arbes would get what he wanted.

Kim Jonghyun's eyebrow twitched. He looked back with a disbelieving expression.

The Sealing barrier was destroyed.

*This quickly? I thought it would hold for three hours at most, or at least one hour at the very minimum.*

No, did this just mean Lee Sungmin's power was that great? Kim Jonghyun found himself laughing unintentionally.

*I have no choice.*

He hadn't intended to step in here. He had planned to watch the situation unfold, but if Lee Sungmin escaped the barrier and joined the fight, things would get complicated. Kim Jonghyun rose from his crouched position.

Even Arbes, who had reached the highest level as a Black Mage, did not know what his supposed ally was hiding.

"Kim Jonghyun?"

As Kim Jonghyun stood, Arbes noticed his presence and smiled faintly. His goal had been to stall for time until his magic was complete, since he couldn't overwhelm Envirus. With Kim Jonghyun joining in, that would no longer be necessary. The Kim Jonghyun that Arbes knew was a man inferior to Preskan, but right now, he needed all the help he could get.

Arbes wasn't the only one who noticed. Envirus's face hardened. He knew well that he would be at a disadvantage if Kim Jonghyun joined the fight.

*Just when is Lee Sungmin going to get here?*

Feeling a surge of anxiety, Envirus moved both his arms. The characters etched on his skin squirmed as they manifested his magic. A blade-like wind from the Wind Elemental, amplified by his spell, swept over both Kim Jonghyun and Arbes. Arbes immediately created a barrier to defend himself, and Kim Jonghyun, not a moment too late, formed a beastfolk to create a barrier of his own.

"You bastard! What were you doing, showing up only now?!" Arbes roared.

"Just what you said. I was holding onto the rat. Though I failed to stop that mage."

"You damn bastard...!"

"A problem has arisen," Kim Jonghyun continued calmly. "The Sealing barrier I created has been shattered, and the rat I captured is on his way here."

"What!" Arbes's face twisted in fury. He thought he could pressure Envirus with Kim Jonghyun's arrival, but if Envirus's helper interfered, the situation wouldn't change after all. No, it would actually put him at a disadvantage.

[How about we release the barrier first and use Aine?] Kim Jonghyun secretly sent a telepathic message. [The magic circle is already complete, but it will take time for it to fully activate. If we add Aine to the fight to buy time until then, I believe we'll have a good chance of winning.]

[Damn it...!] Arbes's tone was one of clear displeasure. It wasn't that he hadn't considered using Aine. He just didn't want to fight Envirus with the help of a creature made by Preskan. Doing so felt like admitting that his own abilities as a mage were inferior. At this point, however, such thoughts were a luxury. The moment he decided to accept Kim Jonghyun's help, his pride had already been trampled.

His face contorted, Arbes reached his hand toward the magic circle. A gap opened in the protective barrier, and Aine walked out with a blank expression.

"Fight!" Arbes roared.

At his command, a light ignited in Aine's eyes. Misshapen wings sprouted from her back, and she soared quickly toward Envirus, her claws sharpened like blades as they descended upon him.

The Wind Elemental moved to block her. Then, the Fire Elemental, its entire body ablaze, lunged at Arbes. That still wasn't enough. Tying the highest-ranking Elementals to the mortal plane consumed a great deal of magic power, but if Envirus worried about conserving it now, his side wouldn't be able to hold on.

DRRRRR!

The ground vibrated as a giant composed of rock and earth rose up. He had summoned the Earth Elemental as well. The Elemental moved its heavy body and charged toward Kim Jonghyun.

As if he had been waiting, Kim Jonghyun formed a beastfolk. The space behind him opened wide, and a Golem with a body of steel walked out. Even a well-made Golem could not be a match for a highest-ranking Earth Elemental. It was a simple stalling tactic. Following the massive Golem, others of various sizes rushed out. Kim Jonghyun had no intention of consuming much of his own magic power and planned to use the Golems as disposable pawns instead.

When Lee Sungmin arrived, the situation was a chaotic, jumbled mess.

The Earth Elemental was smashing Golems amidst a field of scattered, destroyed ones. Arbes was emitting a Chilling Mist to cool the Fire Elemental's flames, and Aine, regenerating her shattered body, was repeatedly charging at Envirus.

"You're here!" Envirus's face lit up when he saw Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin's eyes met with Kim Jonghyun, who was still wearing his black robe. It might not have meant much at this point, but Kim Jonghyun was still hiding his face from him.

"Stop him!" Arbes shouted.

At his command, Aine, who had been attacking Envirus, turned without a moment's hesitation and dropped toward Lee Sungmin. The magic Arbes had been preparing while pushing back the Fire Elemental with his black arms was now complete. He spread both hands toward the Elemental and roared.

"Begone!"

A brilliant blue light exploded with the great shout. The temperature of the area instantly dropped below zero. The Fire Elemental, covered from head to toe in frost, could no longer move. From within its block of ice, the Elemental turned its eyes to look at Envirus.

KWAJANGCHANG!

The ice shattered, and the Fire Elemental was reverse-summoned back to the Elemental Realm. It would be impossible to summon it again for a while, and the blow from the forced dismissal was returned directly to Envirus.

Black blood gushed from his nose.

"Keuk...!"

The reverse-summoning caused his magic power to flow backward. Envirus barely managed to support his staggering body as the black hands of the soul-destroying magic closed in on him. He used Blink to escape and attacked Arbes through the Wind Elemental.

"Join in!" Arbes shouted toward Kim Jonghyun.

Kim Jonghyun glanced at Lee Sungmin clashing with Aine. Was Arbes planning to deal with Envirus first and then capture Lee Sungmin?

*It won't be that easy.*

Although he thought that, he intended to follow Arbes's orders for now. He needed time until the magic was complete anyway, and it was necessary to tie up the troublesome Envirus first. Kim Jonghyun flew high into the air.

Aine was faithful to her orders. She swung both her arms and lunged at Lee Sungmin, who effortlessly blocked her attack. It was the first time they had met since their encounter in the North, and Aine was much stronger than she had been then.

Of course, she was not as strong as Lee Sungmin.

Even though they possessed the same heart, they had experienced different events. When her claws were blocked by the spear, Aine immediately swung her leg to attack. Her leg mutated into writhing tentacles that tried to wrap around Lee Sungmin's body.

He took half a step back and twisted his wrists, which were gripping the spear.

PEOBEOBEOK!

Pierced by the Spectral Spearhead, Aine's body was sent flying far back, tumbling across the ground. She did not die. Aine staggered back to her feet, the holes in her body regenerating the moment she stood, leaving not a single wound behind. With no expression on her face, she lunged toward Lee Sungmin once more.

Arbes glanced in their direction and formed a beastfolk. A wicked aura of death descended upon the land. The ground split open, and from the cracks, the Undead rose again.
