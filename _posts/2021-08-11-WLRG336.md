Ignoring Gemini's grumbling, he felt for Baek Sogo's pulse. It was faint, yet it was not thin. A tenacious, unbroken beat pulsed steadily beneath his fingers. Lee Sungmin relaxed a little as he felt it, relieved. The woman before him now looked far healthier than the one he had seen upstairs, the one wrapped tightly in bandages upon the bed.

"This is no fun."

Gemini grumbled once more, and Lee Sungmin turned his head to look at him. This entire situation was a stage Gemini had intentionally set. The mansion reeked of death, the bed sat in the middle of the room, and a clearly weakened Baek Sogo lay upon it. It had all been a performance, a carefully directed play designed to force Lee Sungmin into the exact conversation Gemini wanted.

"What are you after?"

He had seen through the charade and turned the tables. He could do so only because of the intense sense of wrongness that had radiated from the Baek Sogo on the bed.

No, the source of his unease wasn't Baek Sogo.

*The space itself?* No, that wasn't it either. Lee Sungmin's glare settled on Gemini.

"You said you wanted to talk to me."

The source of the unease was Gemini himself.

"What did you do to me?" Lee Sungmin demanded, his eyes fixed on the vampire. This entire space was wrong, from the stench of death to the woman lying on the bed.

Gemini simply shrugged his shoulders.

"I was deluding you. The Demonic Eye I possess is that kind of power. I cast a pretty powerful suggestion on you. I even made sure Yana and the Crimson Sage, who'd be familiar with this kind of thing, were kept outside the mansion to isolate you. So how'd you notice?"

It was the blessing bestowed by Osla, the heightened senses he had gained upon awakening as a complete Yokai. That was what had allowed him to feel the unease. At some point, Lee Sungmin had fallen under the delusion of Gemini's Demonic Eye.

[A Demonic Eye?] the Void Monarch's voice sounded inside his head. [Strange. Only pureblood Vampires possess a Demonic Eye. Didn't you say that boy is Jeniela's First Bloodline? How can a vampire of vampires, who isn't even a Lord, possess such a thing?]

Lee Sungmin voiced the question aloud. At his words, Gemini bared his teeth in a wide grin.

"That's why I said let's talk. Not with the Queen, not with the Predator, but with me."

Gemini was hiding something. Or rather, it was more accurate to say he had a specific intention. It had been the same just a moment ago. The only reason Lee Sungmin had been able to walk past him to reach Baek Sogo was because Gemini hadn't been on guard at all. His reaction was completely abnormal. He had used suggestion and even his Demonic Eye to hide the real Baek Sogo, yet he showed no great alarm even after being found out.

"I'm different from basic vampires." With nowhere to sit, Gemini grumbled and began twirling a long strand of his hair with a finger. "I… was turned into a Vampire on the same day as the Queen, hundreds of years ago. Though right after, she bit me and made me one of her own."

So Gemini, like Jeniela, was a pureblood. Jeniela had simply forced him, another Vampire created at the same time, to become one of her own. That was why he possessed the Demonic Eye, a power said to belong only to purebloods.

"A vampire of vampires can never defy their Lord, but I'm different. Because I wasn't turned into a Vampire by the Queen."

"…What is it you want?"

"I want things that I can't do myself. I have no intention of forcing you, though. What I want to do is tell you a story." Gemini raised a finger and pointed it at Baek Sogo. "First. A year ago, the Queen captured Silent Flash Baek Sogo, who was fleeing after Ju Won defeated her. She used her Demonic Eye on the weakened woman and learned that you hadn't died in Germud."

"And so?"

"The Queen captured Baek Sogo. She wanted to make you Despair, and she thought she could restrain you by taking her."

It was just as Jeniela had intended. Lee Sungmin had indeed come all the way to her mansion to save Baek Sogo. If she hadn't been here, he never would have set foot in this place. Even though he had grown stronger by taking the body of the Slaughter Predator, Jeniela was a monster far beyond his imagination.

"The Queen lost her Eye of Futuresight," Gemini continued, shaking his head as if in pity. "It brought her a great deal of Despair. She was used to giving Despair to others, but the Queen wasn't used to experiencing it herself. The fact that the things she had seen, wished for, and intended to happen for hundreds of years had completely vanished from her sight drove her to it."

That was why Jeniela had stopped leaving the mansion.

"The vampires of Vampires were flustered. To them, the Queen was an absolute nemesis. In fact, all the Predators were like that. Arbes died, Vollander died, and the Red Ghost died. The leaderless Yokai and Liches, and the Death Knights scattered from Germud, were all gathered together by the Queen. Ju Won, the King of Lycanthropes, was no different from her own limbs from the very beginning."

As he spoke, Gemini snickered. His entire attitude, especially when talking about Jeniela, was filled with open ridicule.

"The Predator, who had been silent for hundreds of years, began to move in earnest ten years ago. The Blood Demon is the Queen's second vampire, a martial artist she took in long ago. The five high-ranking vampires, with Rao Xian being the last, have lived for hundreds of years, existing only by her side."

It had been that way until now. Several years ago, the Blood Demon had emerged in the murim. The unorthodox sects, splintered after the collapse of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, had gathered under him to form the Blood Alliance.

"The Predator began to act in earnest, just as the Queen wished. Originally, the Blood Alliance led by the Blood Demon was supposed to destroy the Murim Alliance and take control of the murim. However… when the Queen lost her Eye of Futuresight and fell silent, everything for the Predator came to a halt. In fact, by this point, the Blood Alliance should have already destroyed the Murim Alliance."

Lee Sungmin already knew there was some kind of relationship between the Blood Alliance and the Predator, but this was the first he had heard that the Blood Demon was one of Jeniela's vampires, her second one at that.

"What's the point of telling me all this?"

"There are several reasons… yes. In this case," Gemini said, taking a few steps back, "it's to buy time."

RUUUUUMBLE!

A great vibration shook the space. Lee Sungmin quickly supported Baek Sogo's body, helping her up.

"It's done," Gemini exclaimed like a child, clapping his hands together. "I didn't want to fight you, you see." He sounded sincere. "There's nothing for me to gain by fighting you. It's just dangerous. Ah, you can take Silent Flash with you. We only kept her to lure you here. Now that you've come, there's no need to keep her anymore."

The darkness scattered. Through the dissipating cracks, a red light spread, and a dreadful, terrifying magic power enveloped the space.

KWAJIK!

Yana and Scarlet burst through the crumbling darkness. Yana spread her nine tails wide and lunged at Gemini.

"Aack," Gemini screamed with a laugh, but before her tails could reach him, he whined, "You're late."

Two Vampires blocked Yana's tails, their transformed hands reaching out to hold them firmly.

"Chen, Kun. The fact that you two are here must mean the Queen has left the mansion, right?"

One of the Vampires nodded. Chen and Kun were twins, brothers turned into Vampires by Jeniela hundreds of years ago. They were her third and fourth vampires.

"Lee Sungmin," Yana said, her voice filled with caution as she looked at him standing behind Gemini.

The situation was not good. Holding the unconscious Baek Sogo in his arms, Lee Sungmin used Dancing Shadow Soul Break to appear beside Yana. Neither Gemini, Chen, nor Kun tried to stop him.

Gemini had said his goal was to buy time. Lee Sungmin's arrival at the mansion, his meeting with Gemini, his search for Baek Sogo, his discovery of the illusion, and their entire conversation—all of it had been a stall, but for what?

"The Queen."

Yana pulled her tails back. Chen and Kun immediately knelt on one knee where they stood. Gemini looked up and gave a bright smile. With her tails drawn back, Yana looked up with a tense expression. Scarlet bit her lower lip, her gloved hands clenched loosely at her sides.

Feeling Baek Sogo's shallow breaths against him, Lee Sungmin looked at the sky. The fragments of darkness shattered and scattered as if the night itself were collapsing.

A being wreathed in red magic power descended slowly, shattering the last of the darkness. The queen, her long red hair flowing behind her, looked down with two eyes redder than the magic wrapped around her body.

"Gemini. Sometimes, your pranks are too mischievous."

"Everything was for you, Queen."

When he had spoken of the Queen before, Gemini's tone had been filled with open ridicule, naturally leaving the impression that he was defying her. His background as a pureblood forced into her service and his laughter as he claimed she had fallen into Despair only reinforced it. Even now, Gemini wore that same smile, but was he truly defying the Queen?

"You didn't even come out for a year. That's why I had to go this far, isn't it?"

At those words, Jeniela let out a small laugh. She stood on the ground, receiving the reverence of Chen and Kun. She looked at Yana, then at Scarlet. Finally, her gaze fell upon Lee Sungmin, who was supporting Baek Sogo.

"It's been a while," Jeniela said with a smile.

Lee Sungmin felt not the slightest bit of warmth in her greeting.

"Was the goal to lure you out?" he asked, staring at her.

Jeniela chuckled and shrugged her shoulders. "I didn't order it. Gemini… my lovely first son, is very different from my other vampires. He acts without being ordered, and he's mischievous."

"You like it when I'm like this, don't you, Queen?" Gemini snickered.

"I heard you were holed up in your mansion in Despair?" Lee Sungmin pressed.

"Despair? Ah, that's a misunderstanding. I wasn't in Despair or anything. It's true I was severely flustered, though. I mean, it couldn't be helped, could it? The future I had been watching for hundreds of years has closed. I can no longer see the future where the Slaughter Predator appears, nor any other future."

*What should I do?* Yana and Scarlet were with him, but the unconscious Baek Sogo couldn't fight.

"For the past year, I've done a lot of thinking," Jeniela continued. "There's one thing I'd like to ask. Are you the Slaughter Predator?"

"Ten years ago, I was."

"Strange… It's so strange. I've met you several times until now. Each time, you brought me something new. There were times I could see you with my All-Seeing Demonic Eye, and times I couldn't, and now… I still can't see you." Jeniela tilted her head as she spoke. "You were the Slaughter Predator, and you awakened… but you're different from the Slaughter Predator I saw. This is the confusion I experienced. When you went berserk in Germud, I thought that you must be him. Are you the Slaughter Predator?"

"Ten years ago, I was," Lee Sungmin answered again, hiding nothing.

At his words, the corners of Jeniela's mouth curled up. "For the past year, I experienced great confusion. If you are the Slaughter Predator, then what was the Slaughter Predator I saw? Before I could even sort out my confusion, I could no longer see the future. During my year in the mansion, I tried to organize everything I had done and the futures I had seen, and I came to my own conclusion." Her hand rose. "And today, you came."

"You are not the Slaughter Predator I was waiting for."

A terrifying power swirled above her hand.

Lee Sungmin had a premonition. For the first time, Jeniela was trying to kill him. She had threatened him before, but never had she truly intended to end his life. Now was different. The Jeniela of this moment wanted him dead, and in this situation, he could not avoid it.

[What will you do?] the Void Monarch asked.

*I have to try.*

Lee Sungmin gripped his spear.
