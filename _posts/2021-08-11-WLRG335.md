The path to becoming a vampire was most often a violent one, an unwilling transformation forced upon a victim as their blood was drained. If the attacking vampire willed it, the target would rise again, their own desires rendered meaningless in the face of their new curse. This was how the vast majority of vampires in the world were born.

Still,, not all of them shared this origin. Every vampire to ever exist had once been human, and among them were those who turned without ever being bitten. It was a rare occurrence, a spontaneous metamorphosis that struck like a disease or the onset of puberty. A perfectly normal person could awaken one day as a vampire, as if they had merely caught a cold, their transformation a grim lottery with no rhyme or reason. A vampire born in such a way was known as a pureblood. Even if they had been human just the day before, their mortal way of thinking vanished the moment they transformed.

While infectious vampires often retained their human mindset and struggled with the act of sucking blood, a pureblood never suffered such conflict. They became pure vampires from the very beginning, their nature as pristine as the blood they had gained. A pureblood born this way possessed the power of a mighty monster from the instant of their transformation.

A vampire created through infection found it difficult to defy their maker. While the command was not absolutely unconditional, a pureblood at the very top of the hierarchy held an absolute right of command over the lesser vampires who originated from their bloodline. Every vampire Clan began with a single pureblood.

The Lord, Vampire Queen Jeniela, commanded many vampires, but those who originated directly from her were almost entirely unknown.

*First Bloodline.*

The first vampire Jeniela had created after becoming one herself. Lee Sungmin watched the back of Gemini as she walked lightly ahead of him. Her build was incredibly small, no larger than Osla's, yet the ominous feeling that emanated from her was on par with Jeniela's own.

[Gemini. Jeniela's First Bloodline… A fellow I've never even seen myself.]

*Really?*

[Jeniela was hiding quite a few things even before this Elder died. Actually, it's more likely because there wasn't much contact between this Elder and Jeniela.]

When he was alive, the Void Monarch had claimed the South as his domain. Jeniela had not descended from North Trabia since that time, so there would have been few opportunities for them to meet face to face.

[It's true for most inhumans, but for them, the time they've lived becomes a measure of their power. Unlike humans, inhumans don't grow senile just because they've lived a long time.]

If Gemini was Jeniela's First Bloodline, it meant she had lived for an immense time, nearly as long as Jeniela herself.

[It seems it's not just about living a long time. Jeniela… she invested a great deal into that little wench. She must have given her considerable power from the very first transformation. This Elder has met quite a few Lords, but that little girl possesses power that is overwhelmingly superior to theirs.]

Predator.

His first involvement with them had been when he initially came to the North to meet a nobleman. Since then, Lee Sungmin had been continuously entangled with the organization. Back then, however, his main enemy had been Heaven Beyond Heaven. Thanks to their constant clashes, Lee Sungmin had managed to cut their power in half. He had personally killed three of the Six Honored Ones, and the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight had betrayed the Martial God after their association with him. The Moon Queen had been killed by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He did not know what had transpired in the past ten years, but before his Sealing, all that remained of Heaven Beyond Heaven were the Martial God, the Blade Supreme, and the Spirit Medium.

Kim Jonghyun and Vollander were dead. The Death Knight Legion was disbanded, but did that mean Predator's power had weakened? To that, Lee Sungmin could not possibly say yes.

Heaven Beyond Heaven had a clear set of enemies in the Six Honored Ones. Predator also had the five pinnacles of the inhumans, the Black Star: Vampire, Lycanthrope, Death Knight, Lich, and Yokai. Among them, Archlich Arbes, Monster Chieftain Red Demon, and Death Knight Lord Volander were dead.

However, there was a decisive difference between Heaven Beyond Heaven and Predator.

[Numbers.]

The Void Monarch sneered. Even with the Red Ghost dead, countless Yokai transformed from humans still served Predator. Even with Arbes gone, there were many Lich. The Death Knights who had retreated from Germud would have returned to Predator as well.

*Heaven Beyond Heaven was surviving on Predator's mercy…*

He remembered Jeniela shouting it with a laugh. She had not left Heaven Beyond Heaven alone because she found their power troublesome. It was because they were not much of a threat. It was because Heaven Beyond Heaven was not an enemy of Predator, and because their existence fit into the future Jeniela desired.

"Our relationship," Gemini said as she crossed the garden. "We are quite favorable toward you, the Phantom Spear."

"Is your will the will of all of Predator?"

"My will is the Queen's will," Gemini replied with a chuckle, "and the Queen's will is Predator's will." She paused. "You should think rationally. Weigh what you'll gain against what you'll lose. Figuring that out is the first step in any deal, isn't it?"

"And what can you give me?"

"Oh, it's not me who can give you something, it's Predator," she corrected him. "But as the Queen's first child, I can tell you what we're offering. For starters, we can guarantee your lives. All of your lives, right here and now."

"That's hard to just listen to and ignore."

The one who spoke was Yana. As she raised her voice, her Demonic Energy encroached upon the viciously ominous aura emanating from Gemini.

"The Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri," Gemini mused. "I know you're strong, but this city holds all of the Queen's vampires. If we wished, the Lycanthropes or Lich would swarm here too. Do you think you can handle all of Predator just because you've joined them?"

"If I offer my life, I can at least ensure his escape."

"I see. You value the Phantom Spear more than your own life. Why is that, I wonder? What kind of bond exists between you two that makes you willing to give up your life? Or is it because of the Void Monarch inside him?" Gemini muttered in a delighted voice. Yana did not bother to answer. "If you're going to be so radical as to throw away your life, hmm… fine. Then I'll say something else. You ask what we can give you, Phantom Spear? You should know that well. We can return someone precious to you."

Baek Sogo.

"A year ago, on the Queen's orders, we rescued Silent Flash Baek Sogo. We took every measure to keep her from dying of Ju Won's poison and have maintained her that way. All without turning her into a Vampire."

Gemini's steps halted at the back of the garden, in front of one of the many annexes.

"We can turn her into a Vampire at any time."

She turned her head to look at Lee Sungmin.

"Do you know what happens when a human becomes a Vampire? Their way of thinking changes. There's resistance at first, but once they start sucking blood… keekee, after that, it's very smooth."

He knew what she was saying. He had seen the Sword Phantom go mad and the Blood Heavenly Demon lose his mind.

"That will give you a very new sense of Despair," she continued. "Because the person you remembered will have become a completely different monster."

Those words were more than enough to provoke him, but he did not explode. *Endure it.* Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and swallowed the erupting emotions.

Gemini glanced at his contorting expression and opened the mansion door. "You come in alone."

"......"

"What? Are you scared? Don't worry, I won't harm you. If you don't want to, then don't." Gemini sneered and pulled the doorknob again. The opening door began to close. Lee Sungmin clenched his fists at her attitude.

"Fine."

"......"

Scarlet and Yana were surprised by his answer, but what choice did he have?

"That's more like it." Gemini grinned and opened the door again.

Beyond the threshold, a long corridor reeked of death. The smell of blood and rotting flesh hung in the air, a disgusting, mixed stench. Lee Sungmin glared at Gemini with a pale face. Before he could snap at her, she spoke first.

"We were doing our best," Gemini said, walking down the corridor. "We stopped the poison's progression while keeping her human. Ju Won's poison is very powerful. Very, very powerful. It's especially potent against humans. The powers of inhumans are developed to kill and eat humans, after all."

Even the half-dragon Black Dragon Knight had suffered because of Ju Won's poison.

"This is the important part."

At the end of the corridor, Gemini opened a closed door without hesitation.

"We are favorable toward you."

His eyes fell upon the bed in the middle of the room. A woman wrapped in pure white bandages was lying there, dressed in white and covered with a white blanket. Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, looking at her. If he listened closely, he could hear a shallow breath. An indescribably fishy and foul smell emanated with every breath she took.

The smell of poison. Lee Sungmin's eyes trembled. Her complexion was pale, her eyes closed. Was it because ten years had passed? No, it had to be the poison. Baek Sogo's appearance was far more haggard than he remembered.

"Baek So…"

Lee Sungmin could not bring himself to say her name. Gemini watched his reaction with an amused expression. She walked with light steps and stood beside the bed, stroking the back of Baek Sogo's white, dry hand.

"I'll say it again. We are favorable toward you."

Lee Sungmin did not answer. He stared at the prone form of Baek Sogo. Emotional turmoil was natural, but he could not let himself be unconditionally swayed by it. In this situation, Gemini held all the cards. As long as she held Baek Sogo's life, he had no choice but to be dragged along as she wished.

Yes, just like always. *Think.* Amidst the violently churning emotions, Lee Sungmin tried to sharpen the blade of his reason. What did Gemini want? Why was Baek Sogo here? Why did Jeniela capture her? And why was Jeniela not showing herself?

He had to look at it from a different perspective, to step back a few paces from this situation. *Don't be swayed,* Lee Sungmin whispered to himself. He must not act according to Gemini's intentions. She had already set the stage, and he had stepped onto it. No matter how much he struggled here, he would only end up thinking and moving as she wanted.

The moment he resolved himself, he felt a strong sense of wrongness. He had been drawn into Gemini's conversation, into the smell of death that filled the room. The stench of poison. Intoxicated by it, he had failed to notice something else.

*Ah, right.*

He understood the situation.

"This is strange."

It was Jeniela who had eagerly awaited the appearance of the Slaughter Predator, but she had not been at Germud ten years ago. It was an undeniable fact that Predator was involved in releasing his Sealing, but Jeniela had not acted directly in that matter either. There had to be a reason.

"Even at this moment, the Queen doesn't show herself."

"Do you think I'm not enough?" Gemini asked, her eyes shining.

Lee Sungmin did not answer. He felt there was no need to.

"The Vampire Queen used to tell me she wanted me to Despair. Did she put her here for my Despair? If so, why isn't the Queen showing herself?"

Gemini giggled.

"Where is the Queen?" Lee Sungmin's hand rose. "And my Senior?"

KWAJIJIJIK!

Purple electricity exploded from his hand. Gemini laughed loudly as the space tore apart. The intentional stench vanished. The bed rippled, and the figure lying on it, the one in the shape of Baek Sogo, distorted and disappeared.

"The Queen won't come out."

The floor collapsed, revealing a pitch-black darkness into which Lee Sungmin and Gemini plummeted.

"She lost her Eye of Futuresight a year ago," Gemini said, her voice laced with laughter. "She can no longer see the future of the Slaughter Predator's appearance."

In the darkness, Gemini's two eyes gleamed.

"I don't know what she's thinking, but the Queen has shut herself up in her mansion. Before losing her Eye of Futuresight, she saw a future about Silent Flash, and through Silent Flash, we found out you weren't dead ten years ago but were Sealing in Germud. The Queen… is very interested in you. So I thought I'd use you to provoke the secluded Queen… but, it doesn't matter. I was holding Silent Flash captive just in case you didn't follow Rao Xian."

"I didn't lie," Gemini said as they landed slowly on the floor. They had fallen to a dizzying depth. This place was filled with the undisguised smell of death.

"Now. Let's talk again." Gemini spread her hands. Behind her, beyond thick iron bars, Baek Sogo could be seen collapsed on the ground. "Not with the Queen, not with Predator, but with me."

In front of the gloating Gemini, Lee Sungmin took the most rational, most certain, and most proactive action he could.

He moved past her, shattered the bars, and stood beside Baek Sogo.

"…Hey."

Gemini slowly lowered her outstretched hands, puffing out her cheeks.

"This isn't fun."

Ignoring her words, Lee Sungmin checked Baek Sogo's pulse.
