"...That," Homerusos began after a long moment of thought, "I honestly don't know."

Lee Sungmin found the words hard to believe and stared intently at the dragon's face. This was Homerusos, one of the Dragons who had escaped Eria to create Erebrisa, a being who had observed this world through three of its cycles.

"You must have been watching all this time," Lee Sungmin pressed.

"That is precisely why it is so unknowable," Homerusos countered. "Unlike this time, the previous two cycles had far fewer variables. By this point, the world should have already been destroyed. It was that way the time before last, and the last time as well."

"How was it destroyed?"

The question burned in his mind. He knew nothing of how the previous world had met its end, let alone the one before that. It was not a difficult question to answer, and Homerusos opened his mouth to speak.

"The pattern was always the same. The reincarnator would pass the observation point and trigger the fate of The End. Not long after, that same reincarnator would awaken as the Slaughter Predator. Their names were different, but in the end, they always became the Slaughter Predator."

"Were there no other disasters besides that?"

"They were arranged, but they never occurred. This time is a very unusual case. Usually, they don't even notice The End is coming. Since they didn't know, they couldn't respond."

"An unusual case, you say. To what extent?" Lee Sungmin's eyes gleamed as he asked. In the current world, only the dragon standing before him could answer such a question.

A Dragon who had observed the world three times would surely know what set this world apart from the others. It might have been a sensitive question, but Lee Sungmin had no need to worry. He had escaped from fate, becoming a being that the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit could not directly interfere with. He did not have to fear his power vanishing into thin air or meeting a sudden death like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Void Monarch. The same held true for the Dragons who had escaped this world.

The Spirit and the Demonic Spirit could only take away the power of men like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman or the Void Monarch because they completely managed this world. Those born here belonged to the world's fate by that fact alone, and since the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit directly governed that fate, no one bound to it could refuse their interference. If one succeeded in escaping that fate, however, they were free from such meddling.

The last time Homerusos had contacted him through the terminal called Laplace, he had not been certain if Lee Sungmin had completely escaped the Destiny Force. Or perhaps, rather than a lack of certainty, he had simply thought there was no point in telling him. Lee Sungmin had been too weak back then.

"...Hmm..." Homerusos paused, sifting through ages of memory before he spoke again.

"First of all, the death of the Void Monarch is different. In the previous two worlds, the Void Monarch died and disappeared hundreds of years ago. In this world, only his body died while his soul remained."

Lee Sungmin had heard that story from the Slaughter Predator as well. The Void Monarch should have been annihilated, but he had remained as an exception. It was because Lee Sungmin, the designated reincarnator, had been too weak, and his shortcomings had to be compensated for. The Black Heart being given to him was another part of that compensation.

"The death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is also different. Originally, his death was a suicide."

"...Suicide?"

"The actions of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman were one of the biggest changes in this world," Homerusos explained. "Last time, and the time before last, he was a carefree and unconcerned man. Even when the reincarnator awakened as the Slaughter Predator and began to devour the world, he didn't break his seclusion. He simply killed himself."

Lee Sungmin almost scoffed at the absurdity of it, but the protest died on his lips as he recalled the kind of man the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had been. He possessed the man's memories and completely understood the self-destructive impulses he had held until the very end. Even in this world, the Chairman's final actions were, in a way, the same as suicide.

"Kim Jonghyun's significance is also different. Originally, he didn't stand out much, not in the last world nor the one before, but in this world, he entered Predator, betrayed Arbes, and became the master of the Grimoire. In the end, he even attained the rank of a Quasi-Demon King with that power."

Kim Jonghyun's words, spoken with a smile, echoed in his mind. *It's because I met you.*

"And it goes without saying for the Slaughter Predator," Homerusos continued. "In the last world and the world before, the actual executor of The End was the Slaughter Predator. In this world, he disappeared far too quickly."

"What about Jeniela?"

"It's the same for her." Homerusos furrowed his brow. "Her strength is abnormal. Jeniela was the Vampire Queen in the previous cycles as well, but her power fell far short of what she has achieved in this world."

A pattern was starting to form. Lee Sungmin was lost in deep thought.

"...You're saying you don't even know which side is the enemy, the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit."

"It's only in this world that their existence has become so prominent. In the first place, the organization called Heaven Beyond Heaven didn't even exist in the previous worlds."

"What about the Martial God?"

"The organization didn't exist, but the individuals belonging to it did."

"Was Yana there?"

"In the last world, Yana was also the Nine-Tailed Fox who ruled Urmuri. Though she was devoured by the Slaughter Predator not long after the time of The End..."

Too many things were different. Just as Homerusos and the Slaughter Predator had said, there were too many variables in this world.

"This is also the first time a magic tome called Gries has existed," the dragon added. "Originally, there was only the Grimoire."

He had thought as much. The reason Envirus and Abel could know about The End in advance and act was thanks to Gries's magic. The reason the Martial God and the Six Honored Ones tried to stop The End was also because they were bound together as the organization Heaven Beyond Heaven.

"...What about Wiji Hoyeon?" In Lee Sungmin's memories, she had existed in the previous world as well.

"She was also killed by the Slaughter Predator. In this world, Wiji Hoyeon's fate was greatly changed by the Demonic Spirit's interference. She was given an absurd level of talent and even the Grace of Fate."

Wiji Hoyeon was the joker prepared by the Demonic Spirit. Lee Sungmin was the disposable card meant to deceive the Spirit's eyes.

"The Wiji Hoyeon of the last world had no such blessing. She fought valiantly against the Slaughter Predator, but she couldn't last long and was killed. Last time, and the time before last, the world was destroyed that way. The Martial God died, the Sword Immortal died, Abel and Cain, everyone died, and then..."

"And then?"

"After devouring every living thing in the world, the Slaughter Predator disappeared. Then it starts all over again. People with a certain level of civilization appear, with memories of having always lived in this world from the beginning."

"Even the so-called otherworlders," Lee Sungmin finished, letting out a short, humorless laugh. Perhaps when this world first opened, it really did summon people randomly from other dimensions. Maybe even now, such 'real' otherworlders were still being brought here, but this was a world that had met its conclusion and started anew, its inhabitants living through a repeating history.

Lee Sungmin understood how it worked. It was simple repetition.

*Just like a game.*

He recalled the single-player games he had played a long, long time ago. First playthrough, second playthrough, third playthrough. Countless games progressed like that. After seeing the ending once, you started again from the beginning, the only difference being that your stats were not carried over. You played the game again, and sometimes the story of the second playthrough would be slightly different from the first.

He understood the purpose of this world's existence. It was just a guess, but it felt plausible. It was a forge, designed to obtain perfected techniques. Eria had repeated this process many times, and within it, hundreds of thousands, millions of people learned various skills. Martial arts and magic, and many other things besides. In the repeating history, there must have been endless small variables, tens of thousands of instances of someone thinking in a slightly 'different way'. That pushed martial arts, magic, and techniques to advance in a slightly 'different' direction. The 'different' skills possessed by the occasionally summoned 'real' otherworlders were added to the existing ones. In this way, the world moved closer to obtaining the 'perfect thing'.

"What do you think?" Lee Sungmin rose from his seated position. He had heard most of what he wanted to hear. What remained was a small, personal curiosity. "Do you think I can stop The End?"

"...I don't know," Homerusos replied, shaking his head. "There are too many variables in this world. It's flowing in a completely different direction than before. The form of The End is different, and so is the existence of Gries. However, it's true that there's more hope than in the previous worlds. The disaster of The End has already been blocked several times, and besides you, there's also the big variable named Wiji Hoyeon. It's just..."

"The Demonic Spirit can't be trusted."

Homerusos nodded his head. Lee Sungmin was well aware of that fact.

"Thank you for telling me all this. Do you need any compensation?"

"...No, it's fine. This wasn't a transaction. To think you inherited Keiserod's memories. Thanks to you, I think I can have a little more hope. Perhaps the endlessly repeating End will finally be over, and we might no longer have to be bound in our spirit forms."

"I'll try my best," Lee Sungmin replied with a faint smile.

"I'm not Keiserod, but talking with his memories... haha. I feel a sense of nostalgia, or gladness. Something like that."

Homerusos chuckled. "If it were Keiserod himself, he wouldn't have felt that way. You are not Keiserod, but I like you, who is more human, better than the arrogant and foolish Keiserod."

After saying that, Homerusos disappeared into the shadows. A feeling of regret, born from Keiserod's memories, lingered for a moment before fading. It was only a temporary emotion.

*Does that make me the Black Dragon Knight's father?*

The thought occurred to him suddenly. Fortunately, Keiserod's memories offered no sentiment on the matter. The dragon had not even been aware that he had a half-human, half-dragon son. Lee Sungmin was just a human who possessed Keiserod's memories, not Keiserod himself.

He stood there, not returning to the forest right away. He slowly turned his head and looked toward the trees, a small worry rising in his chest. He recalled what Frau had said, that by escaping the Destiny Force, his actions would make everyone around him miserable. That if you throw an egg at a rock, the egg will naturally shatter.

It was just as he said. This time, too, everyone had become miserable as a result of his actions. They might not consider it misfortune, but from his perspective, they had become miserable. Eyes gouged out, arms and legs severed.

*No more.*

The part of his heart that had not become a monster told him so. He did not want to feel sorrow anymore. He did not want to lose anyone else. He did not know what would happen from now on. Without Gries, he could not even prepare. No matter how carefully he prepared, what was meant to happen would happen, just like how the Spear King died this time, and everyone was injured.

He was sick and tired of it. Feeling sorrow, being left behind, consoling himself that it was okay, that it was nothing. So, it would be better to...

*At least a farewell...*

He couldn't do it. A bitter smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips. There was no way they would let him go. Scarlet would curse at him, and he could not even imagine what kind of expression Baek Sogo would make. The Black Dragon Knight and Lloyd would not back down now either.

Knowing that, Lee Sungmin could not say his goodbyes. He took a step back.

He looked at the forest with eyes full of lingering attachment, wondering if he could ever come back, if he could do this right, what he could do from now on.

Swallowing that anxiety, Lee Sungmin turned his body.

Where should he go?

He had already decided where he needed to go.
