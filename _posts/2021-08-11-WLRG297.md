The long sensation of floating ceased as Lee Sungmin dismounted the Fairy Horse. He surveyed his surroundings, recognizing the plains near Nerod immediately. It was here he had been relentlessly pursued by the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique on his way back to Haras, just after killing the Dark Supreme.

[So why'd you leave that woman behind?]

"It's dangerous."

[The Sword Immortal, is it?] Void Monarch asked with a knowing chuckle.

An answer was hardly necessary. Void Monarch knew Lee Sungmin's heart well enough. The Sword Immortal's ability to launch his Flying Sword Technique across vast distances was a terrifying threat. He had only intended to head for Wudang in the first place because the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would have been there to stop the Sword Immortal, even if he appeared as a nemesis.

However, the Chairman was gone now. If the Sword Immortal decided to unleash his technique, there was no one left to block it. That was precisely why he had left Scarlet behind. Should the Sword Immortal's Sword Ki target them, Lee Sungmin had no confidence he could protect her while also defending himself.

Even now, he sharpened his senses, wary of the technique's imminent approach.

*This distance is well within the Sword Immortal's range.*

He knew this from bitter experience, having been chased across these very plains for so long. He remained on high alert, but even after a considerable amount of time passed, the attack never came. Could the Sword Immortal have failed to notice him? Then again, for all his outstanding martial theory, the Sword Immortal was still human. The distance from Wudang was by no means short, and detecting someone at this range might be impossible even for him.

[You'll be resented for this later.]

*That might be true.*

He pictured Scarlet's eyes, wide with bewilderment as he left. He remembered her words, how she pitied him, how she said that becoming numb to the sorrow of losing others meant a person was broken. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard.

[You've been broken for a long time,] Void Monarch said, his voice laced with laughter. [You pretend to be fine, but your mind is truly strange and your psyche is extremely unstable. It might be because of your inhumanity, though.]

*Is that so?*

[It would be better to be conscious of it. You need to have at least that minimal awareness, the fact that you yourself are not okay.]

*Thanks to you, I'm constantly aware of it, even when I don't want to be.*

[I'm only saying this for your own good.]

*Are you worried I'll go crazy?*

[Of course I'm worried. Didn't we make a promise? That when you finally go crazy and can no longer control yourself, this Elder will personally step in and stop you. I hope such a day never comes.]

"Me too," Lee Sungmin muttered, pulling his spear from his Subspace Pocket. He truly hoped that day would never arrive. If it did, it would mean a complete farewell to Void Monarch, the being with whom he had conversed for so long. He did not want that.

[What's this? Feel like you're about to burst into tears?]

"Shut up."

After spitting out the curse, Lee Sungmin slung the spear over his back and gazed at the distant mountains.

*We were supposed to go to Wudang together.* Recalling the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's words, he unleashed his movement arts. He did not lower his guard for an instant, keeping his senses sharp so as not to miss the slightest hint of danger, yet as the distance continued to shrink, no threat from the Sword Immortal materialized. Had he truly not noticed? The uneventful and smooth journey only made him more nervous.

Thanks to running without rest, he soon found Mount Wudang looming before him. Lee Sungmin stopped.

*Is it okay to just go up like this?*

[It's not like you can choose not to go up.]

That was also true. Lee Sungmin began his ascent. The terrain was rough, but to him, it was no different from running on flat ground. It was his first time visiting Wudang in person, but the sect was so famous in Murim that much was known about it even by those who had never been. Sword Lake, its sword techniques, its formations, and so on.

The Sword Immortal, Wudang's previous sect leader, resided far past Sword Lake, on the highest peak of the mountain behind the main buildings. Even for fellow martial artists of the Wudang Sect, that peak was a forbidden place, like a sanctuary. To invade it head-on was to challenge Wudang itself, and fighting the entire sect was not something Lee Sungmin wanted. However, it was not as if he could show courtesy and make an appointment in advance. Now that he was here, he had to climb, but heading straight for the Sword Immortal's peak was a matter of concern.

*What would the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman have done?*

[Don't compare,] Void Monarch snapped at the thought. [You are you, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Your Master is dead, and you've inherited his power. That doesn't mean you can become the same person as him. It's a ridiculous hope.]

*Why is it ridiculous?*

[Because you are you,] Void Monarch emphasized again. [It's like wearing clothes that don't fit. Your disposition is too different from his to begin with. You overthink things and worry too much. You have enough power, yet you lack confidence. Well, I suppose that can't be helped. You're definitely one of the strongest martial artists alive, but most of the enemies you've made were unluckily stronger than you.]

Void Monarch grumbled at the thought.

[In any case, stop comparing yourself to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.]

*I just want to be like him.*

[Like him, my ass. Look at you now. 'What would the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman have done?' You're tormenting yourself with such useless thoughts. That kind of idiotic thinking only leads to self-torment, because you are a lesser person than he was.]

Though he felt it was true, Void Monarch's words were a heavy form of verbal abuse. Lee Sungmin cleared his throat, brushing the comments aside. In the end, he had no choice but to start climbing again, not because he was wondering what his master would have done, but because he simply had no other option.

He did not cross Sword Lake directly. His wide-open senses clearly detected the Wudang Taoists standing guard nearby. The mountain terrain was treacherous, but there were plenty of ways to go where there was no path. Finding the highest peak was easy.

Lee Sungmin's steps came to a halt. The sun had set long ago, and a high moon hung in the sky. He ignored the distant lights of Wudang's buildings and instead stared into the deep darkness ahead.

[There's something there,] Void Monarch murmured.

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. He did not draw his spear. There was no need, and he felt it was unnecessary to show hostility from the start. He simply stood there, staring into the shadows. After a moment of silence, someone walked out from behind a tree.

It was a young man dressed in a blue martial arts uniform with a long wooden sword at his waist, though his hands did not touch it. Just as Lee Sungmin showed no hostility, the man raised both hands and walked forward.

"Who are you?" Lee Sungmin asked.

The young man lowered his hands and answered, "My name is Cheongmyeong."

It was a name he recalled from a distant memory. Jihak of Shaolin, Chwi Geol of the Beggars' Gang, and Cheongmyeong of Wudang. The three were said to be the most outstanding prodigies of their generation. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened slightly.

Cheongmyeong stared at Lee Sungmin's face for a moment before turning. "Please come. My Master is waiting for you."

He was the Sword Immortal's direct disciple. His age was likely similar to Jihak's or Chwi Geol's, but the aura emanating from him was incomparable to theirs.

Transcendent Realm.

Lee Sungmin was amazed at Cheongmyeong's achievement. He had thought that besides the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens and the Black Dragon Knight, the only master in the orthodox murim to have entered the Transcendent Realm was the Sword Immortal himself. He had never imagined that Cheongmyeong, who was shrouded in mystery because he never left Wudang, would be a master of such a level.

[But it's not that strange,] Void Monarch muttered.

He was right. Wiji Hoyeon, who possessed an absurd amount of talent, had also broken through that wall on her own. Lee Sungmin himself had reached it after enduring the Black Heart, Mushii, and Denir's Trial. If the Sword Immortal had taken Cheongmyeong as a direct disciple, then he must also be a figure worthy of being called a calamity. Furthermore, the greatest master in Wudang had taught him continuously. With sufficient aptitude, an ideal environment, and a measure of effort and luck, becoming a Transcendent Realm master was not an unreasonable outcome.

*It's absurd.*

Following behind Cheongmyeong, Lee Sungmin could not help but think of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had died without ever reaching that realm. Aptitude, environment, effort, and luck. What had his old master lacked?

"You're not asking any questions," Cheongmyeong remarked from ahead.

"The Sword Immortal himself is waiting," Lee Sungmin replied as he walked. "What else is there to ask?"

"Do you trust my Master?"

"I don't think a great man like the Sword Immortal would send his disciple to lure someone like me into a trap."

At those words, Cheongmyeong let out a low laugh. "The title of the Phantom Spear. I've heard it many times, even though I've never left Wudang."

"I'm quite famous. I've heard your name as well. They say you're the best among the Wudang prodigies."

Cheongmyeong just smiled, neither confirming nor denying it. "There are many guests here."

"Who are they?"

"Jihak of Shaolin and the Sword Dragon of the Namgung Clan. The Mages Guild Master and the Golden Magic Tower Master."

He had expected Abel and Lloyd to be here, but the news that Jihak and the Sword Dragon were also present brought a surprised expression to Lee Sungmin's face.

"…Is there anyone else?"

*Is that all, just the four of them?* He thought of Baek Sogo, whom he had parted with at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. In the letter she had left, she had written that she would go to Wudang. More than enough time had passed for her to have arrived.

"There is no one else," Cheongmyeong replied.

*There should have been enough time. Did she stop by Shaolin and go somewhere else?* Lee Sungmin felt a flicker of doubt but did not ask any further.

Cheongmyeong's steps stopped before a small Taoist temple built on the edge of a cliff. "My Master is inside." He turned and bowed his head deeply to Lee Sungmin, who returned the gesture before approaching the temple.

He could feel a presence within. Lee Sungmin grew tense, clenching and unclenching his hands, which were damp with cold sweat.

*Just in case, I should secure an escape route.* As long as he could freely ride the Fairy Horse, escaping from the Sword Immortal would not be too difficult.

"Was he satisfied?"

The words came from inside the moment he opened the door.

"……"

Lee Sungmin closed the door behind him and took a deep breath before speaking. "What do you mean?"

"I'm talking about Ilcheon."

It was a voice devoid of laughter. Lee Sungmin flinched, his body stiffening at the name the Sword Immortal had spoken. No rumors had spread about the fight in the North, yet the Sword Immortal's words sounded as if he knew exactly what had happened to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"…You knew?"

"One of the few hobbies this old man has is watching the stars in the sky," the Sword Immortal chuckled. "A week ago, I saw a great star fall. It's a skill I learned by rule of thumb, but it happens to be right on occasion. …Yang Ilcheon. Did he leave satisfied?"

"…Yes."

"That's a relief. We belonged to different groups and had different ideologies, but this old man didn't dislike that friend of mine. Anyone who has reached such a level has to be acknowledged, no matter who they are."

Lee Sungmin walked toward the light of a flickering candle. Before it, the Sword Immortal sat with his back turned.

"Ilcheon. Why did he die?"

"…In a fight with the Martial God."

"Was the Martial God stronger than Ilcheon?"

"No," Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation.

"Then why did Ilcheon die?"

"…The Moon Queen intervened."

"Was the Moon Queen that strong?"

"…It was a strange fight. My Master was definitely stronger than the Moon Queen and the Martial God combined, but…"

"I see." The Sword Immortal, still facing away, laughed heartily. "It seems Ilcheon was meant to die there."

Lee Sungmin's expression hardened at those words. It was difficult to suppress the turmoil in his heart, but his opponent was the Sword Immortal. He was not someone he could lash out at just because he was displeased.

The Sword Immortal slowly rose to his feet. "Why have you come here?"

"Because my Master wished to go to Wudang."

"You came to follow his will? Isn't that a bit arrogant? Your power has grown, curiously enough, since the last time you fled my Flying Sword Technique. Even so, you're not yet strong enough to avoid my blade."

"I was confident I could escape."

"Shall we try?"

"I have no intention of doing so."

The Sword Immortal laughed loudly at his answer. He turned around. Unlike the Martial God and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who maintained youthful appearances, the Sword Immortal looked like an old man, fitting for one who referred to himself as such.

"Child." A smile formed on his wrinkled face. "Do you know what this old man is thinking?"

"…I do not."

"I'm thinking about whether to cut you down or not."

The Sword Immortal held no weapon, yet Lee Sungmin felt the phantom tip of a blade pressed against his throat. The air did not shimmer, no warning sign manifested itself, there was only a pure, one-sided sharpness targeting him alone.

"This old man is a Taoist master," the Sword Immortal began. "I do not kill needlessly, but… as I said back then. Whether killing you is meaningless or not… this old man is still not sure."

"Because of the evil deeds I've committed?"

"It's more fundamental than that." The Sword Immortal shook his head. "What do you think of this old man?" It was a sudden question. "No. Let me ask more clearly. Child. Do you think this old man will kill you?"

"…Is that an important question?"

"It is an important question. My actions will change depending on your answer."

"I don't think you will kill me."

"Because you want to live?"

"I have many things I must do."

"In the end, doesn't that just mean you want to live?"

"Is it strange to wish for that?" Lee Sungmin countered.

The Sword Immortal grinned. "Yes. If that's the case, then it can't be helped." He said so and plopped back down. As he did, all the sharpness that had been aimed at Lee Sungmin vanished.

"Come here and sit." The seated Sword Immortal gestured to him. "Let's have a little talk."

Not a hint of hostility could be felt from him as he spoke.
