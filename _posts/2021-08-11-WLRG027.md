Though he had graduated two years ahead of schedule, a problem remained. Lee Sungmin was currently fifteen years old, yet he had first become a mercenary in Behenger at the age of seventeen. For the three years before that, he had survived in the city of Zenavis. He knew that for the next two years, no noteworthy prizes would be offered at the Coliseum, a direct result of the Holy Spirit Pill incident. There was simply no reason to stay in Zenavis.

However, did that mean he should go straight to Behenger?

*No.*

The two years stretching before him were a complete unknown. Lee Sungmin had no knowledge of what would happen in Behenger during that time. The opportunities he remembered, the ones he was determined to seize, were all still more than two years away. In fact, the rumors about the chances his past self had missed were things he had only heard about much later, while working as a mercenary.

In other words, he had no memories of the life he was about to live for the next two years.

"……"

Lee Sungmin's spear shattered the head of a charging Orc. Without pausing for breath, his spear lashed out three more times, each thrust crushing the skull of another Orc.

*First-Rate…*

He had reached the level of a First-Rate master, a realm he had failed to attain even after thirteen years of effort in his past life. It was a feat made possible by the inner arts from Wiji Hoyeon, the power from the Holy Spirit Pill, and the combination of the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

However, it was not enough.

His goal was the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon. Half a year ago, during their farewell duel, his skills had hovered somewhere between Second-Rate and First-Rate. Even so, he had been unable to so much as graze the hem of her clothes, and she had not even been fully grown. At just thirteen years old, Wiji Hoyeon was already a master whose skills surpassed the peak realm. The difference between a peak realm master and a First-Rate master was like the distance between heaven and earth.

*With a Low-Grade Martial Skeleton and the Classless correction, I can make rapid progress. The problem is…*

The problem lay with him, the one using those gifts. His proficiency with the Violet Dawn Divine Art was at the second star, and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was at the third. His growth had only been this fast because of his unique advantages. Without them, he would still be floundering at the first star.

The arts themselves could advance, but he was the one who had to wield them. He was the one who fought, who made every decision in the heat of battle, who moved his body and chose every attack and defense.

*I need time.*

There was no point in going to Behenger now. Even if he became a mercenary, the most he could do was take on requests from the guild and save a little money. He needed time for training far more than he needed coin.

Lee Sungmin stopped walking. The path straight through the forest would lead him to Behenger. Instead, he turned his head.

He saw a mountain.

{sep}

"It's been a long time since we've had an ascetic so young."

Among the many gods worshipped in Eria, 'Mushii' was the one who presided over trials and penance. This place, Mount Mushii, was managed by the god's Junior Disciples and was located relatively close to both Zenavis and Behenger.

"Is my age a problem?" Lee Sungmin asked. The journey, though relatively short, had taken four days overland, and his appearance was disheveled from the lack of a proper wash.

"No, age isn't a problem," the Junior Disciple of Mushii answered. His cheeks were sunken, and dark, dead-looking circles ringed his eyes. He parted his lips in what was meant to be a smile. "What does age matter when it comes to trials and penance?"

"Do you know about this mountain?" the disciple asked.

"Roughly."

Ascetics permitted by the Junior Disciples received Mushii's protection on the mountain, which meant they could not harm one another. Any attempt to inflict harm would be stopped by the god himself. This was because the 'trials' and 'penance' Mushii presided over were meant to be strictly for oneself.

"Will you accept a restriction?" the Junior Disciple asked.

Lee Sungmin stared blankly at him. During the four-day journey, he had already decided what kind of restriction to place upon himself.

"My sense of taste," Lee Sungmin said, raising a hand to point at his mouth. "Also, I'll accept a restriction on the 'heaviness' I feel."

"Understood."

The Junior Disciple of Mushii simply nodded his head. He performed no particular magic, nor did he offer a prayer. He merely took a few steps back, clearing the path for Lee Sungmin.

"We provide nothing. Eating, drinking, sleeping… surviving on that mountain is entirely up to you, young ascetic."

Lee Sungmin passed the disciple without a word.

He had heard rumors of Mount Mushii several times in his past life but had never come here himself. There had been no need. What one gained on this mountain was entirely up to the individual. One could waste their time or even lose something, which was usually their life. Mushii did not forgive ascetics for killing each other, but he did not care if an ascetic died alone.

A reward, however, was given at the end of the ordeal. The harsher the self-imposed restriction, the more difficult the trials became, and when one overcame them, Mushii would extend a hand.

That was what Lee Sungmin was hoping for. With his Ilcheon talent, reaching a higher realm through conventional means was difficult. That was why he had come.

The moment he passed the temple, a voice brushed past his ear.

[Welcome.]

*Hah.* A sound of surprise escaped his lips before he could stop it.

His body was heavy.

It was so heavy that he had to draw out his inner arts just to take a single step. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip hard. He had accepted a restriction on 'heaviness', an intuitive choice. While under its influence, his body would feel unnaturally heavy, and not just his body. Everything would feel heavier to him.

*My sense of taste, too…*

He felt no strange sensation in his mouth. He took out his Subspace Pocket, which now felt impossibly heavy. The bread he pulled from inside felt like a solid rock in his hand. Lee Sungmin glared at it before opening his mouth wide.

CRUNCH.

It was like chewing on stone. No, it was worse than stone. Lee Sungmin's face twisted in disgust. The restriction on his sense of taste was not a loss of it, but a complete alteration of it. The texture was like chewing mud, and the breadcrumbs on his tongue felt as if they were squirming like the corpses of insects. The more he chewed, the more horrific the 'taste' became.

"Uweeegh!"

In the end, he couldn't swallow. He had prided himself on the strong stomach he had developed as a mercenary, but the sheer horror of this altered taste was not something he could endure with a clear mind. Lee Sungmin spat the chewed bread onto the ground and dry-heaved several times.

"Hah… Heok…!"

After retching for a long moment, he rummaged through his heavy Subspace Pocket and took out a water flask. He hastily poured the water into his mouth to wash away the lingering disgust.

"Keuaargh!"

It was not just the food. The taste of the water had also changed, like pouring raw sewage into his mouth. Lee Sungmin panted, clenching his teeth. Taste and weight. He had thought they would not be so harsh. If asked which would be worse, he would have chosen weight without a second thought.

He was wrong.

The worst restriction he had received was the one on his sense of taste.

He wanted to quit.

Was there really a need for this? He could just go to Behenger and become a D-Rank mercenary at the very least. After a few requests, he would become C-Rank. Even with his Ilcheon talent, his martial arts would grow thanks to the correction. Reaching the level of a First-Rate master was already guaranteed.

As a First-Rate master, he could become a B-Rank Mercenary. That alone would be a better life than his previous one. Those missed opportunities, what were they really? He had already surpassed his past self and secured a foothold for a better future.

He could just live without being too greedy. Go with the flow. A purpose? Living happily was a fine purpose. He could save money as a mercenary, avoid the most dangerous jobs, meet a suitable woman, and get married. Have children. Grow old and die.

Wouldn't that be a perfect life? It might be ordinary, but for a Classless to live an ordinary life in this world was an incredible feat. Yes, it would be a happy way to live and die.

He just had to not be greedy.

He could turn back now.

Go to Behenger. There was no need to endure such trials. Every meal from now on would be hell. His body would be heavy, and he probably wouldn't be able to execute the Void Path properly. He didn't even know what kind of reward Mushii would give him for this suffering. He might just be wasting his time. He might even realize his own lack of talent, fall into despair, and kill himself.

Let's quit.

When his thoughts reached that point, Lee Sungmin saw Wiji Hoyeon's face.

She was his first master and the only one he could call a friend. The Little Heavenly Demon, with whom he had promised to meet again in Ruvus in ten years.

Lee Sungmin took another piece of bread from his Subspace Pocket. He squeezed his eyes shut and forced the bread into his mouth. His body already remembered the horrific taste. The moment it touched his tongue, his insides rejected it. His stomach churned, and vomit rose in his throat. He chewed. Forcibly. Continuously. He crunched down on the maggots that squirmed in his mouth and danced on his tongue, their bodies bursting and spewing fluids that scraped the inside of his cheeks.

His cheeks puffed out. Trembling, he lifted his water flask and poured its contents into his mouth. The water was hot. The moment it entered his mouth, it became boiling. Lee Sungmin forced himself to gulp it down.

"Heok! Heok!"

He thought he must have been burned. The inside of his mouth throbbed and stung, but when he felt inside with his hand, there was no trace of a burn. It was only what he was feeling.

"Damn you… Mushii…"

Though he had wished for this restriction himself, he hurled curses at the god for applying it so horribly.

[Insolent human.]

Mushii whispered in his ear. It seemed the god of this mountain had far better hearing than he had imagined.
