*Black Heart? What is this?*

Utterly flustered, Lee Sungmin looked down at his own body. He could feel no change within the confines of his Magic Armor. The grating thrum of the heart that had echoed in his ears had fallen silent, and he could no longer consciously hear its rhythm as he usually did, yet, the uncomfortable sensation of something unpleasant filling a corner of his chest remained. He pressed a hand to his left breast, steadying his breath.

First, he opened his status window. He had no idea what a 'Black Heart' was, but the voice he had heard was just like the one that announced a new skill, so he figured he could check it there.

He was right.

-Black Heart.

The ultimate desire that Lich Preskan had longed for.

The problem was the description itself. Like his other skills, the information in the status window was frustratingly vague. It claimed to be the ultimate desire Preskan had longed for, but what on earth was that desire, and what made it ultimate? Lee Sungmin's only impression of the Lich was that of a madman who spouted nonsense about wanting to become a woman or give birth to a child.

"Um... Lloyd?"

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before calling out the wizard's name. Lloyd had asked him to destroy the heart, but he had unintentionally absorbed the very thing he was supposed to destroy. Troubled, he tried again.

"Lloyd?"

Still, Lloyd did not answer. A jolt of alarm went through Lee Sungmin as he hurried over to the wizard's side. Lloyd had collapsed and was not moving. Lee Sungmin quickly felt for a pulse.

Fortunately, he was not dead, merely unconscious. The wounds that had seemed so severe were, upon closer inspection, not that serious. In fact, they were becoming less serious by the second. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue in amazement as he watched Lloyd's injuries regenerating at a visible rate.

He reached down to help Lloyd up, but a sudden thought made him pause. Lee Sungmin turned and instead approached Zeon's body.

Had it been a sudden death? Zeon's eyes were wide open, staring at nothing. Lee Sungmin let out a short sigh and reached a hesitant hand toward the man's face, gently closing his eyes. Then, he began to search his clothes.

A small cloth pouch came into his hand. Lee Sungmin carefully pulled it free. It was only the size of his palm, but when he opened the entrance and put his hand inside, his entire arm disappeared within it.

Groping around, his fingers brushed against a few miscellaneous items. Lee Sungmin grabbed one of them and pulled his hand back out.

*Hundred Steps Divine Fist.*

The words were written on the cover of an old book. The moment he saw it, Lee Sungmin's heart began to pound. The Hundred Steps Divine Fist was one of the most famous techniques that came to mind when one thought of Shaolin, and it was the very divine art Zeon had learned.

While he was at it, he searched a little more and pulled out a few more martial arts manuals. Lee Sungmin looked down at the Dharma Meditation Art and the Arhat Movement Arts, letting out a gasp of admiration. They were all ultimate techniques of Shaolin. As a practitioner of the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, he did not covet the Dharma Meditation Art or the Arhat Movement Arts, but he desperately desired the Hundred Steps Divine Fist. He figured it would not hurt to learn a fist technique for situations where he could not wield his spear.

*I probably won't have time to learn it properly...*

Still, if he learned it, he could use it in an emergency. He could not learn it right away, however, so Lee Sungmin placed the three manuals into his Subspace Pocket. Then he searched Zeon's Subspace Pocket again.

He found two wooden boxes. One of them looked familiar. It was the same box Zeon had shown him when trying to recruit him into the Corona Mercenary Group. Lee Sungmin gulped and opened it.

A Minor Cycle Pill, one of Shaolin's secret miracle herbs, rested inside. He felt so happy he wanted to dance, but he suppressed the urge and opened the other wooden box.

A refreshing fragrance rushed out, clearing his sinuses. Inside this box was a red pill, slightly larger than the Minor Cycle Pill. A Great Cycle Pill. Lee Sungmin had never seen one in person, but he had no doubt that was what it was. With trembling hands, he closed the wooden boxes and put them into his Subspace Pocket.

"...Don't resent me too much. Isn't it better for someone to take these than to just leave them here?"

After muttering the words to the already dead Zeon, Lee Sungmin searched his body a little more. Unfortunately, he found nothing else of value. After taking Zeon's Subspace Pocket, he moved on, searching the bodies of the other mercenaries and taking their pockets as well.

Once he had gathered everything he could, Lee Sungmin helped Lloyd up. He then looked for the way out of the Dungeon, leaving the bodies of Zeon and the other mercenaries as they were. His original plan had been to find a large Subspace Pocket on Bern or another mercenary to collect the corpses of the Undead, but that had not worked out. Aside from Zeon and the mummified mercenaries, the bodies of the others were simply gone.

In the end, Lee Sungmin carried the bodies of Zeon and the mummified mercenaries, along with the unconscious Lloyd, and climbed the stairs.

It was a shame he could not take any other artifacts from the Dungeon, but since he had obtained the Great Cycle Pill, the Minor Cycle Pill, and Shaolin's secret manuals, he could not call it a loss by any means.

The sun was setting when he finally emerged from the Dungeon. He had entered before noon, so at least half a day had passed. Lee Sungmin sighed and glanced at Lloyd, who was slung over his back, breathing evenly in his unconscious state.

Fortunately, he had left the carriage and horses not far from the Dungeon, so returning was not a problem. The resting coachmen were horrified to see him return with an unconscious man and several corpses, but they accepted his rough explanation for the time being.

"Let's return to Behenger," Lee Sungmin said, his voice thick with fatigue.

He had only spent half a day in the Dungeon, but he had been through so much. His mind was a tangled mess of thoughts, feeling as if it were about to explode.

Inside the rattling carriage, Lee Sungmin decided to organize the facts he had learned. He took out the notebook he always carried and held a pen.

1. There is another being besides me whose causality is twisted. I don't know who he is, but the powerful Lich called him a being he dared not defy, so he must possess skills beyond that of a peak master.

2. There is something else about me besides my twisted causality. Kim Jonghyun knows something about it but can't tell me. The being summoned by the Lich refused to accept me as a sacrifice.

2-1. However, Mushii and Nebul covet my soul. I don't know what the difference is between the being the Lich summoned and them.

2-2. Since regressing, I have been receiving an unusual amount of goodwill from those around me.

3. In my past life, the Magic Armor turned a Third-Rate mercenary into a peak master, but I feel no change at all.

4. I obtained something called a Black Heart, but I don't know what it is.

5. Lloyd, who should have died, has survived. He promised to repay me, but I don't know what I will receive. All the other mercenaries are dead. I obtained the Great Cycle Pill, the Minor Cycle Pill, and Shaolin's secret manuals from Zeon.

Lee Sungmin looked over what he had written. Putting his complicated thoughts on paper helped calm his mind somewhat. The problem was that even after writing it all down, he could not find an answer to any of it.

*Was there something about the Stone of Reincarnation from the start?*

He frowned, lost in thought. When he had held the Stone of Reincarnation, it had not seemed like a big deal. He just thought it was some kind of stone, and he had died shortly after without being able to hold on for long.

Lee Sungmin considered it fortunate that he at least knew someone who could give him answers about things like the Magic Armor or the Black Heart.

It was late at night when they arrived in Behenger. Lee Sungmin went straight to the Mercenary Guild to report the tragedy, laying down the bodies of Zeon and the mummified mercenaries as proof.

"Wh-what happened to the Guildmaster?" the middle-aged mercenary who served as the Acting Guild Master asked urgently. It was the S-Rank Mercenary, Jo Young. Lee Sungmin had seen him a few times and recognized his face, but they were not particularly close.

"He passed away." Lee Sungmin felt a pang of bitterness and regret over Bern's death. The man had been quite favorable toward him.

"N-no way."

"I'll explain the details later."

"What? Where are you going! What could be more urgent than this...!"

"The master of the Golden Magic Tower, Lloyd, has been injured," Lee Sungmin cut in. "I was only able to save my own life because he appeared in the Black Mage's Dungeon and drove the Black Mage out. In the process, Lloyd sustained serious injuries."

"The master of the Golden Magic Tower...? I-is that true?"

Jo Young's eyes widened. The master of the Golden Magic Tower was of a higher rank than the branch manager of the Behenger Mercenary Guild. A Magic Tower was a place that received special treatment even within the Mages Guild, and its master was an archmage with skills and fame ranked within the top ten. It was only natural for Jo Young to be shocked.

"Yes. So first, I'd like to take Lloyd to the Mages Guild..."

"...In that case... I suppose it can't be helped. I understand."

In the end, Jo Young had no choice but to back down. He looked at Zeon's body in the carriage with a bitter expression.

"To think that Zeon, the leader of the Corona Mercenary Group... would die like this, and Bern, too... Haa!"

Lee Sungmin left the sighing Jo Young behind and hoisted Lloyd onto his back. The Mages Guild was not far, so it did not take long to get there.

When he arrived with Lloyd, the Guild was thrown into chaos. The news that the Golden Magic Tower Master had been carried in injured was enough to shake the entire Behenger branch to its core. Though it was late, the mages moved busily, trying to devise a way to heal their esteemed guest.

"They're overreacting."

Scarlet, who had approached at some point, grumbled the words. Lee Sungmin, who was leaning against a wall, gave a slight nod to her.

"From the looks of it, his wounds have already regenerated. He's just unconscious because his magic power is depleted. He'll wake up just fine once some of it returns."

"Then doesn't that mean he doesn't need any special treatment?"

"That's true. Still, you need to put on a show. They have to pretend to be busy with something so they can save face when that great Golden Magic Tower Master wakes up," Scarlet giggled. From the way she spoke, it was clear she was not very fond of 'Magic Tower Masters'.

"Don't all mages respect the Magic Tower Masters?"

"Respect? What respect? I'm not too fond of those old geezers. They're full of secrets. They don't take disciples, and they don't share the magic they've learned or the knowledge they've gained with others."

It felt strange to hear such words from Scarlet, who in just a few years would establish the Reshir School and become the master of the Red Magic Tower. People were bound to change, but he wondered what kind of change of heart she would undergo.

"Anyway, you... that's some nice armor. Where'd you get it?"

"...I had a chance encounter."

"I heard all the other mercenaries died? ...You were lucky. I'm glad. If you had died, I would've had nightmares."

"Are you worried about me?"

"Don't say such cringey things, kid. It's only natural to feel awful when someone you know dies," Scarlet grumbled and smacked him on the head.

"I was wondering what was so noisy this late at night."

Hearing a familiar voice, Lee Sungmin turned around.

Kim Jonghyun, dressed in a pure white gown, stood there yawning.
