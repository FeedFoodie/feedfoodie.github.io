After a moment of hesitation, Rubia followed Lee Sungmin. He rubbed the shoulder he had used to swing his spear, recalling the brief flicker of fighting spirit from the battle. For a being called the Spirit King of Wind, Sylphid had been surprisingly weak. Lee Sungmin had not felt any real sense of crisis during their fight. The winds and storms he had conjured failed to penetrate Lee Sungmin's Protective Qi Aura, let alone leave so much as a scratch.

"Are Spirit Kings usually that weak?"

"Of course not," Rubia answered, her reluctance to follow him clear in her voice. "The Sylphid you just fought wasn't at his full strength. To be precise, he was in a situation where he couldn't use his full power. A Spirit King's strength is amplified by the Elementals he rules. He gathers even the lowest-ranking wind Elementals to create his storms."

In the Elemental Realm, Sylphid could have exerted his full power, but not here. While the sudden spatial erosion had succeeded in causing the Spirit King's Advent, it had failed to bring the other wind Elementals with him.

"Given time, he would have grown much stronger," Rubia said, stretching her hands toward the blowing wind. "The Spirit King of Wind creates wind Elementals just by existing, and a place like Yuzkia Mountain is especially rich with them." She let her hands fall. "...Well, it's meaningless now. He was reverse-summoned before he could even start to regain his original power."

"You're being quite cooperative."

"It's because of you, Lee Sungmin!" Rubia snapped, glaring at him.

Lee Sungmin just chuckled and continued with his questions. "Well, since it's come to this, why not tell me a little more? I'm sure you don't want to die an unjust death, Lady Rubia."

"Why is this happening to me?"

"Didn't I tell you? Some bastard is playing games with this world's fate. All these damn coincidences we can't understand, things that shouldn't be happening, they're all because of that son of a bitch. The death of your master is no different, Lady Rubia."

Her face crumpled at his words.

"Didn't Sylphid say it?" Lee Sungmin pressed. "Every Elemental now knows you're a traitor, Lady Rubia. Are you still going to go back?"

"I don't want to betray Her Majesty."

"And yet you want to help me?"

"...Her Majesty is trying to destroy the world," Rubia said, her shoulders slumping. "...I don't want that."

Her feelings and her situation were a tangled mess. She was an artificial Elemental, a creation of the Spirit Queen, and as such, she could not defy her creator. From the moment of her birth, she had been by Envirus's side, acting with him. It was her own end at Abel's hands that had ultimately triggered the Spirit Queen's rampage, yet Envirus's desire to stop The End had been genuine.

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon... grandpa... he died too, didn't he? He just wanted me to be well."

Rubia's steps came to a halt. Lee Sungmin, who was walking ahead, looked back at her.

"I'm destined to die one way or another," she muttered in a gloomy voice. "I'm an artificial Elemental created by the Spirit Queen. If she calls for me, I have no choice but to be summoned to her side, and if the Spirit Queen dies, I'll die too. So you see, there's no need for you to kill me."

"I can solve that for you," Scarlet spoke up, having listened to their story. "I don't know the full story, but... you say you're an Elemental, but you sound more like a familiar. If that's the case, you just need a new master. I could make a contract with you, if you want. Take you as my familiar."

Rubia's eyes widened at the sudden proposal. "But that would be a complete betrayal of Her Majesty...!"

"What has the queen ever done for you, Lady Rubia?" Lee Sungmin's question cut through her protest. "Judging by your attitude, you don't seem very loyal to her. You're only following her because you have to, not because you sympathize with her goals. Am I wrong?"

Rubia couldn't say a word and hung her head. She had no choice. To her, the Spirit Queen was no benevolent mother. In the first place, Rubia's very existence was as a tool, created only to die in Envirus's place should he ever be in danger.

"We don't have time," Lee Sungmin urged her. "The queen's true body may not have had its Advent yet, but once she does, you'll lose your freedom of action."

"...Alright," Rubia finally answered, her voice weak after much deliberation. "But you should know this. If I become a familiar, my connection with Her Majesty will be severed. That means I won't be able to tell where she is anymore."

"Then tell us now. Where is she?"

"...She hasn't had her Advent yet. It seems she's wandering the rifts between spaces."

"Then isn't it possible she might not be able to come here at all?"

"No chance of that. The hole opened by the incomplete spatial erosion is just too small for her to enter yet. Her spiritual body is simply that massive."

At those words, Lee Sungmin pictured the Spirit Queen struggling to squeeze through a tiny hole.

[Foolish boy.]

He ignored the Void Monarch's grumbling.

Once Rubia made up her mind, Scarlet worked quickly. The fact that the Spirit Queen was wandering between spaces instead of residing in the Elemental Realm worked to their advantage, thinning the spiritual connection between her and Rubia. Thanks to this, the familiar contract was completed in a flash. As an artificial Elemental, Rubia's existence was already closer to that of a familiar, so all that was needed was to sever her spiritual connection with the Spirit Queen and forge a new one with Scarlet.

"The four spots in Eria, including this place, the Yuzukia Mountains, each symbolize one of the Four Great Spirits," Rubia said, having collapsed onto the ground. "Cheneron Great Lake has Water Elementals. Dahar Ruins has earth Elementals, and Femud Volcano has many Fire Elementals."

"Have the Spirit Kings had their Advent there as well?"

"That... I'm not sure."

"What about the Fairy Queen?"

"She will probably have her Advent in the Sleeping Forest. Though not yet," Rubia said, pouting. "...And, it's late, but... thank you... for saving me."

Lee Sungmin smiled warmly at her words. "I'm glad we didn't have to fight, Lady Rubia."

"It wouldn't have even been a fight," she grumbled.

Lee Sungmin called for Nebul, requesting information on Cheneron Great Lake, Dahar Ruins, and Femud Volcano. Then, before Nebul could bring back an answer, he summoned the Fairy Horse.

"It's been a while since I've seen Lloyd."

The party climbed onto the Fairy Horse. Rather than squeeze into a tight spot, Rubia transformed into a small sphere of light and settled into Lee Sungmin's arms, just as she had before. Scarlet watched this with a look of displeasure.

"I'm your master now, so why are you going into his arms?"

"Uh... um... I'm just used to it. I did it without thinking..." Rubia replied in a startled voice.

Scarlet puffed out her cheeks sullenly but didn't say anything more. Instead, she glanced at the indifferent-looking Baek Sogo. Knowing that Yana was devoted to the Void Monarch, she didn't even spare her a look.

"Doesn't it bother you at all?"

"What do you mean?"

"Never mind, forget it. Looking around, it feels like I'm the only normal woman here. Or is this the new trend?"

"I don't really understand what you're saying."

"It's nothing. Just the grumbling of a narrow-minded woman," Scarlet grumbled and crossed her arms.

The Golden Magic Tower was located in Ruvus, the very place where he had promised to reunite with Wiji Hoyeon. Upon arriving in the city, Rubia emitted a small tremor from within Lee Sungmin's arms. She seemed to be remembering her time here with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, how he had been fascinated by his first train ride and how she had proudly explained it to him. Lee Sungmin remembered that time clearly as well. When he first came to Ruvus, he had been filled with high hopes, thinking he would be able to reunite with Wiji Hoyeon without any incidents.

That was in the past. He had come to Ruvus again with Rubia, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon was no longer by his side.

"Lloyd is a pitiful man," Scarlet muttered on the way to the Golden Magic Tower. "Ten years ago, the Mages Guild Master died. His will was made public, and it stated that all his belongings were to be handed over to Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master. It also appointed Lloyd as the next Mages Guild Master."

"...But isn't he still the Golden Magic Tower Master?"

"That's why he's pitiful," Scarlet said with a sigh. "Those dog-like geezers in the Council of Elders were ready to die opposing the appointment. They said he was too young, too inexperienced, that his magic skills were lacking and he had few research achievements. To be honest, they weren't entirely wrong. Unlike other mages, Lloyd never had a clear, ultimate goal he was pursuing, and he'd done almost no research related to one."

He hadn't known that story.

"Lloyd didn't protest much either. 'If you don't want me, then so be it.' That's what he said, and he decided to be content with his position as the Golden Magic Tower Master."

"Then who is the current Mages Guild Master?"

"The elders tried to nominate the Green Tower Master, who had the highest seniority, but he refused. That old man is surprisingly normal, despite his appearance. In the end, the position of Mages Guild Master is vacant. It has been for ten years." Scarlet grumbled, her eyebrows furrowed. "The elders are jointly acting in his place, but what's going to improve when the ones at the top are just a bunch of guys filling their own greedy pockets? I got completely fed up around that time and left the Mages Guild. It seems Lloyd is still sticking with it, though."

"By the way, why are you planning to meet Lloyd?" Scarlet asked, tilting her head.

"There's something I need to confirm."

Abel was dead. Even though Abel always had Lloyd with him, he hadn't brought Lloyd to the fight with Kim Jonghyun. Abel had claimed he left him behind because he was useless, but Lee Sungmin hadn't quite believed that. Now, because of what Scarlet had just said, his faint suspicion turned into certainty.

*Abel gave all his belongings to Lloyd. That probably includes Gries, too.*

Standing before the Golden Magic Tower, Lee Sungmin was about to knock, but the door opened before he could. He looked inside, his eyes wide with surprise.

"Perfect timing."

Lloyd stood just beyond the door. He wore golden robes and looked at Lee Sungmin with a slightly aged face.

"Did you know?"

"I didn't use Gries. I just confirmed it with the Observation Eye I have set up around the Magic Tower."

Those words also meant that Lloyd had Gries, the grimoire Abel had left behind.

"I was wondering when you'd come."

Lee Sungmin realized something. There was no one else inside the Golden Magic Tower except for Lloyd.

"The others...?"

"I sent them away. The Golden Magic Tower will no longer be a Magic Tower. They all had many complaints, but they understood when I spoke firmly. If I manage to survive without dying, I plan to take them back."

If he managed to survive.

"Ten years was a long time," Lloyd said in a flat voice. "I knew little, and the things I didn't know were written in the will Master Abel left behind. The things I still didn't understand, I saw through Gries. Though I had to offer up quite a bit of my lifespan."

Lloyd's eyes turned to Lee Sungmin. He gave a bitter smile toward Rubia, who was in Lee Sungmin's arms.

"Gries can glimpse everything. It can hardly see the future, but it can see almost everything about the present and the past."

"...What did you see?"

"The things I didn't know. How my Master died, and how Master Abel died. What you are. What happened."

He had offered much of his lifespan as payment, but Lloyd didn't consider it a waste.

"What did you come here to ask?"

"...About what is happening to this world right now."

"It seems Gries can answer that," Lloyd smiled faintly.
