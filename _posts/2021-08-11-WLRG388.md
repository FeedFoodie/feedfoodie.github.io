A chaotic battle against many was one thing, but against a single individual of immense power, the tricks of the Illusionary World were not very meaningful. This was especially true if the opponent was an immortal like Jeniela, a lesson he had already learned during his fight with the Blood Demon. Even if he blocked her senses with martial arts theory and attacked from beyond her perception, an opponent with power equal to or greater than his own could still react to an assault from a blind spot, and even if she failed to react, the immortal Jeniela would instantly recover from any damage he managed to inflict.

So he abandoned the illusions. Faster than Jeniela could destroy the Illusionary World, Lee Sungmin modified its very formula. His head throbbed as if it might burst, but his mind, having reached a distant and focused state, accomplished the task without faltering. In that moment, Lee Sungmin was no longer the slow-witted dunce with lacking insight he had once been. The half-complete Body Reformation had awakened his potential to this extent, and while it still could not compare to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman or Wiji Hoyeon, his insight was now worthy of being called a calamity in any world.

The fog that had obstructed his vision vanished, and all his blocked senses rushed back in. The Illusionary World, however, remained. Jeniela gauged the Demonic Energy that now saturated the space, realizing this could no longer be called an Illusionary World or a Void Path. Though the mists of illusion were gone, the oppressive Demonic Energy was still present. In fact, by shedding its deceptive nature, the space had become a cauldron of seething, violent aura.

"Ahahat…"

Jeniela giggled, still feeling not the slightest hint of threat or tension.

Lee Sungmin pressed his temples hard against the throbbing headache. His overworked, burnt-out brain cells were already regenerating. The shimmering light of the Fairy Realm continued to envelop his body, Osla's blessing preventing fatigue from accumulating while protecting him from fatal attacks.

"……"

Rubia, tucked safely in his arms, made a frightened sound. She wanted to run, to flee this battlefield where her power was too meager to be of any help, but Rubia did not run. She knew there was something she could do just by being with him.

[You… truly… have grown a lot.]

The Void Monarch praised, clicking his tongue in amazement at what Lee Sungmin had accomplished.

KIRIRIK…

Demonic Energy swirled around Lee Sungmin. He no longer needed to swing his spear. The very space of the modified Illusionary World was under his control, and all the Demonic Energy within it acted as his hands, his feet, and his spear.

His consciousness expanded. The swirling Demonic Energy coalesced around him as the formula of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear imbued dozens of formless lances.

"Aaaaaah!"

Lee Sungmin roared and leaped forward, his steps becoming the Formless Phantasm. He consecutively executed the Two Step Calamity, Three Step Fatality, and Four Step Chaos, creating a raging storm of Demonic Energy.

As Sungmin charged, Jeniela ran to meet his advance as if she had been waiting, red magic power surging from both her hands. The surrounding spears of Demonic Energy executed the same technique in unison.

BZZZZZT!

Soul Chasing Annihilation Lightning shot out, the formless spears transforming into dozens of searing streaks of lightning. Jeniela let out a heated breath and swung her hands wide.

KRRRRRNG!

Her fingernails tore through the dozens of lightning bolts in a single, devastating strike. The scattered Demonic Energy instantly reformed into spears, their numbers multiplying from dozens to hundreds, then thousands. There was no room to dodge. Caught in the middle of the Homing Chain Lightning, Jeniela's magic power swelled immensely.

WHOOOOOSH!

The space vibrated as if it would explode. The high-density magic expanded, swallowing all the attacks and returning them to nothingness.

Lee Sungmin thrust the spear held in both hands toward the oncoming wall of magic power. *Can I break through?* He didn't entertain such a question. He had to break through, no matter what.

KZZZZZKT!

The tip of his spear collided with the wall of magic, and beyond it, he could hear Jeniela laughing like a madwoman. Her laughter was grating.

[Shut her up!] the Void Monarch roared.

More Demonic Energy flowed into him. The Void Monarch held nothing back, pouring all of his power into Lee Sungmin. Before, he would not have been able to withstand it, but not anymore. Their wills were not clashing. Just as Lee Sungmin wanted to kill Jeniela, so too did the Void Monarch.

"……"

A crack formed in the wall of magic power.

"......"

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. A crack appeared on his overworked spear, but it didn't matter if the weapon couldn't hold. Pushed with all his might, the spear finally broke through the wall.

The light of Genesis erupted.

KWAAAAANG!

The Genesis that exploded from within the barrier swallowed Jeniela's body whole. Unable to withstand the internal shock, the barrier shattered into pieces.

Lee Sungmin did not stop. He leaped forward as dozens of Formless Spears moved according to the formula of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Nine Dragons Slaughter. Hundreds of dragons were unleashed.

Amidst the wildly rampaging dragons, Jeniela rose to her feet, her body, which had been annihilated without a trace, regenerating once again.

She couldn't truly be immortal. Lee Sungmin moved without rest. Unless she was a complete transcendent being, there had to be a limit to her regeneration. She was just endlessly reconstructing her body with that enormous magic power. If so, he would push her until she could no longer regenerate.

Deathly Flash, the fastest of the nine techniques, was unleashed.

PABABAK!

Blood spurted from Jeniela's mouth as her body, pierced by dozens of spears, staggered back, yet the corners of her mouth twitched upward. She was still smiling. Her body collapsed into a pool of red blood, which then transformed into a massive wave.

KWARURURU!

He would not retreat against the surging magic power.

sky-piercing.

The formula for the seventh technique surfaced in Lee Sungmin's mind. He cast aside all superfluities. The inner arts and Demonic Energy needed to unleash its maximum power were overflowing. Stronger. The Demonic Energy in the space swirled, and dozens of Formless Spears merged into one. He would make it absolutely unavoidable. He would pierce through even if she defended.

Lee Sungmin raised the spear held in both hands above his head. The giant Formless Spear merged with the weapon in his hands.

sky-piercing shot forward. A huge hole was torn in the crashing wave of magic power. The light of the technique, having reached the end of its path, transformed into Genesis at its tip. The flashing explosion annihilated all the magic power.

Jeniela's laughter could no longer be heard. He could do it. Lee Sungmin swallowed a ragged breath and leaped forward.

Even if tonight was the night of the full moon. Even if his opponent was the Vampire Queen who had lived for hundreds of years. Even if she was the most powerful monster among all the inhuman beings in this world, possessing a regenerative ability worthy of being called immortal.

His attacks were working. The terrifying magic power Jeniela wielded was repeatedly failing to withstand his assault, being pierced and annihilated. Though her body kept regenerating, coming back from death again and again, his attacks simply killed her again and again.

So, he could do it. He had thought he had reached his limit, that he was cornered.

*I can do it.*

He could defeat Jeniela.

He still had plenty of power to use. In his current state, it felt like he could fight for an eternity. Thanks to Osla's blessing, no fatigue was building up, and the headache was gone. Even if his body was shattered by his own martial arts, Lee Sungmin's body was that of a Yokai. Though not like Jeniela, he could still regenerate continuously.

Lee Sungmin ran toward the remnants of the annihilated magic power, ready to kill Jeniela's body again the moment she regenerated. His heart felt much lighter. The corners of his mouth twitched upward. His gambit had worked. Thanks to his thorough preparations, he was handling Jeniela more easily than he had ever expected.

[Behind you!] the Void Monarch yelled.

The warning came too late. The attack was faster than the shout, faster than his senses could warn him. His consciousness momentarily blanked.

Lee Sungmin's body flew through the air like a kite with its string cut. The light of the Fairy Realm enveloping him thinned. It was only this much thanks to the blessing. If he hadn't had it… Blood surged up his throat with a gurgle.

"You were smiling, weren't you?"

A whispering voice sounded from right behind him. Lee Sungmin flinched in surprise and twisted his body around.

KWAK!

An impact slammed down on his back, bending his body like a bow. Blood sprayed from his wide-open mouth. As he plummeted downward, Lee Sungmin hurriedly regained his senses and balanced himself, only for his vision to be completely filled with crimson magic power. Within it, he could see Jeniela, a bright smile on her face.

"You were smiling."

Jeniela's finger beckoned.

KIRIRIRIK!

The tip of the magic power twisted like a pretzel, turning into a sharp awl before shooting forward. Lee Sungmin hastily extended his spear to meet it.

BAKGAK!

The Demonic Energy wrapped around the spear collided with Jeniela's magic and was annihilated. The advancing magic did not stop there, shattering the spearhead forged from Dragon material.

"What were you thinking?" Jeniela asked, reaching out from amidst the fragments of his broken weapon.

Demonic Energy swirled behind Lee Sungmin's back, instantly unleashing the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Jeniela simply clenched her fist toward it.

POW!

The Formless Spears that were shooting out exploded before they could ever reach their target.

"I told you, I'm an excellent mage. I've already seen through the nature of this space. Did you really think I'd just let myself get beaten so helplessly?"

Jeniela's hands drew near, grasping Lee Sungmin's shoulders.

"Tell me. You were smiling, weren't you? Why? Because I couldn't dodge or block your attacks? Because it seemed like I was resisting somehow but couldn't properly respond and just kept dying? My head exploded, my body exploded, and when I regenerated, I died again. So…"

So, so. Hm?

Jeniela's face came right up close until their foreheads touched. He saw his own face reflected in her wide, red eyes.

"What were you thinking? Why did you smile? Why? I know, I already know. I know even if you don't answer. Don't lie, it's obvious, but I want to hear it from your own mouth."

"……"

He could smell her breath. Despite being mixed with the stench of blood, it was alluring and sweet. The hands on his shoulders were gentle as she giggled and pulled him closer, pressing their bodies tightly together. Lee Sungmin tried to swing his broken spear to push her away.

PAK!

The weapon was completely shattered by her magic power.

"You're not going to tell me?"

Inside the still-intact Illusionary World, their bodies floated. Jeniela extended her long tongue, licking the dried blood on Lee Sungmin's cheek.

"You thought you could win."

Her eyes curved into crescents. Lee Sungmin's face, reflected within them, distorted in horror.

"You thought you could do it. You thought you were pushing me into a corner. You thought you could kill me on the Night of the Full Moon. Right? Don't say no. What's the point of lying now?"

SHWIIIIK…

Jeniela's magic power wrapped around Lee Sungmin's body. The Fairy Queen's blessing resisted it, and Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth, raising his Protective Qi Aura.

"Not answering?"

KWADEDEUK! Her magic power pierced through both the blessing and the Protective Qi Aura at the same time.

"Keoheok!"

Blood spewed from Lee Sungmin's mouth as the magic that pierced his stomach tore his insides apart.

"That's what you thought. That you could win, that you could do it. Hm? It's okay, you don't have to answer. I already know everything. I was hoping you'd think that, and I was just playing along. You don't need to be so embarrassed. It's only natural, isn't it? Anyone would have thought the same, not just you. Fighting me, pushing me into a corner like this… anyone would think that. Anyone would feel hope. Anyone would think they could do it."

I love that so much.

Jeniela beamed.

"I love snatching hope away right before someone's eyes. I love letting them almost grasp it, only to snatch it away. I love seeing the smile that unknowingly forms at the thought of 'I can do it' crumble into distortion. Ah… you're making a really nice face right now. Should I have held back a little longer? Should I have let it mature, waited until it was even better to look at… Oh well. It's already too late."

SHUK.

The awl of magic power was pulled from his body.

"How does it feel?" she asked, looking down at Lee Sungmin's pale, horrified face.

"Are you in Despair?"

The queen of monsters asked with a smile.
