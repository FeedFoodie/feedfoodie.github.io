One month.

The promised month came and went, yet the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear remained incomplete. Wiji Hoyeon had claimed she could overhaul the divine art in that time, but the task proved impossible even for her. In the Central Plains where she had lived, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was considered one of the top three spear techniques, a masterpiece of martial arts.

Even for a calamity in martial arts theory like Wiji Hoyeon, who had encountered countless divine arts, the work was more than a simple fix. It involved deciphering the intricate initial forms and fusing them with the Soul Chasing Spear Art, a process that required a tremendous level of genius to harmonize with the subsequent oral formulas.

Though the deadline had passed, Wiji Hoyeon was not in a hurry.

"The progress is slower than I thought. I think I'll have to stay a little longer."

"Am I holding you back?"

"I'm doing this because I gave my word. You don't need to feel responsible, so stop it." Wiji Hoyeon glared at him. "You're too pessimistic about yourself."

"A person's personality doesn't change in a day or two."

*This is what happens when you get pushed around at the Mercenary Guild for ten years.* It was a feeling Wiji Hoyeon would probably never know in her life. Lee Sungmin grumbled as he rose to his feet, his break now over.

A month of relentless training had forged significant changes in Lee Sungmin's body. Swinging his spear with almost no inner arts had built his muscles and increased his stamina, but he had not neglected the Violet Dawn Divine Art either. Instead of his daily routine of hunting, he had focused entirely on his own training. Wiji Hoyeon, in turn, dedicated her time outside of her research to teaching him.

In just one month, his spearmanship had improved dramatically. Because he had only ever trained against monsters, taught by no one, his technique was skewed heavily toward practical application at the expense of fundamentals.

"You can actually use energy release without inner arts," she explained. "At its core, energy release is just a method of using strength efficiently. Adding inner arts just amplifies the power."

"I can't learn it just from your words."

"Then do you want me to hit you with it?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, pouting.

At her question, Lee Sungmin quietly shut his mouth.

"......"

Her spear thrust forward in an instant, piercing nothing but empty air.

"Energy release comes in all sorts of forms," she began. "You have Hidden Power and Inch Power, which depend on distance. Then those are divided again by method… it's endless if I try to explain every single one. Among the martial arts of the Central Plains, the one who best understood it, who delved into it and made it known, was the founder of Wudang, Zhang Sanfeng. He's long dead, of course."

Wiji Hoyeon muttered this as she rose to her feet, picking up the spear she had placed beside her.

"Springing Power, the Binding Spear, Cross Power, and so on. There are countless types, but the purpose is always simple. To generate immense power from very little strength. It's about using your entire body, not just your muscles, to create force. The fundamental premise is to use your body's elasticity, rotation, and overall momentum, which is then explosively amplified with the help of inner arts."

Lee Sungmin nodded for the time being. Seeing this, Wiji Hoyeon narrowed her eyes.

"Don't just nod if you don't get it. It's confusing."

"Fine. I don't get it at all."

"Then you have to learn. You need to mix energy release into your sweep, your parry, and your thrust. That's how you'll get more power than you expect." After saying that, she let out a deep sigh. "It's absurd. This world makes no sense. To think you're using martial arts without knowing these basic things!"

He had heard this from her countless times lately. As he began to learn from her in earnest, Lee Sungmin realized how much he lacked. The things she taught were less like advanced martial arts and more like the common knowledge required to use them at all.

"You don't know acupoints, you don't know restraining techniques. A guy who doesn't even know acupoints is using martial arts…"

"How would I know if I was never taught?"

"I shouldn't even bother," Wiji Hoyeon grumbled.

With a sullen expression, Lee Sungmin practiced the energy release movements she had shown him. Then, a thought suddenly occurred to him.

"How do you use Thought Transmission?"

[Like this.]

Wiji Hoyeon's voice echoed directly in his mind. Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh and stopped swinging his spear.

"If I could do it just like that, I'd be a calamity."

"That's true. You're not a calamity," she said with an indifferent face. "But you're not a dunce either."

"What?"

"I said you're not a dunce. Just as you're not a calamity, you're not a dunce. Maybe a little better than average…" Wiji Hoyeon muttered, crossing her arms. "It feels like you're better suited to learning from others than figuring things out on your own. It's probably because you've never been properly taught."

At her words, Lee Sungmin's expression went blank. She was right. He had never been properly taught by anyone like this before.

"Well, anyway. Just think of it as speaking with your inner arts."

"What kind of bullshit is that? How do you speak with inner arts?"

"Sigh…"

At his grumbling, Wiji Hoyeon patiently explained the method step-by-step. Lee Sungmin tried to follow her instructions, but it did not go well. The amount of inner arts he possessed was still too small.

And so Wiji Hoyeon's lessons continued. She taught him about acupoints and the methods of sealing them. She drilled him on energy release and restraining techniques. She even sat by his side while he circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, offering advice on his Internal Energy Guidance Method.

In truth, a significant reason she had not completed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear within a month was the sheer amount of time she had devoted to teaching him.

Having finished the Great Heavenly Cycle, Lee Sungmin opened his eyes.

"Why are you doing all this for me?" he asked.

While he had been circulating his energy, Wiji Hoyeon had been sitting in a corner of the bed, deciphering the oral formulas. She looked back at him.

"Are you asking because you don't know? It's obviously because…"

"Other than the 'because we're friends' answer."

"Because I don't want to see you die a miserable death," Wiji Hoyeon changed her answer. "Didn't you say you'd make me your goal? I can't let someone like that die a miserable death at the hands of some nobody in some unknown place."

"So you're just being nosy?"

"Call it concern," she grumbled. She put down her pen and rolled her stiff neck from side to side. "…And I just don't want to leave behind any major regrets for myself. Because I'll be leaving soon."

"…Regrets? What regrets are you talking about?"

"You are my first friend." Her eyes grew calm, and when she looked at him that way, he could never tell if it was intentional. It felt as if her gaze alone could overwhelm him. "Even if our meeting itself wasn't a coincidence, my thoughts haven't changed. You are the first friend I've ever had. So I want to look out for you. For your sake, and for mine."

He had heard it several times now. The giant known as the Little Heavenly Demon in his past life, Wiji Hoyeon, was this kind of person. She was a woman, not a man, and despite being the Demonic Cult's Young Cult Leader, she was quite considerate. Though she lacked something fundamental in her communication skills, she tried to take care of those she had taken in until the very end.

"One more month. It's taken twice as long as I first said… but after that, I'm leaving."

"…Where do you plan to go?"

"I don't know. I just… plan to wander for a while without settling down. Just as my past self did." Wiji Hoyeon paused, then looked at him. "What do you plan to do?"

"Me? I… after I get the Holy Spirit Pill, I'm thinking of going to the city next to Zenavis."

Behenger. It was a city that took three days to reach even after passing through the forest. In his past life, he had gone to the Mercenary Guild in Behenger and started his life as a mercenary.

"I see. So, when should we meet again?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, tilting her head. "If I leave this city in a month, I'll wander for a few years, just as my past self did. After that, I'll settle down somewhere as I please. In your past life, you and I never once crossed paths during the thirteen years you lived."

"Because our positions were different."

"Let's say ten years from now," she suggested. "I was thinking of making it thirteen years, but since you said you don't know what happens after that, it seems better to meet sooner. Let's promise to meet in ten years. You decide the place. I don't know anything about geography."

"Ruvus," Lee Sungmin answered immediately, without a second thought. "It's one of the largest and most bustling cities in Eria. I think we can meet there. When would be a good date?"

"March 14th," she said after a moment of thought. "It's my birthday. I won't forget that. You remember it too. March 14th, ten years from now. You and I will meet again in the city of Ruvus."

That promise forged a new purpose in Lee Sungmin's heart. He would not die for the next ten years. He *must not* die before he saw her again. No, survival alone was not enough.

He had made Wiji Hoyeon his goal, and he had to strive to close the distance between them. At the level of a First-Rate martial artist, he would not even be able to reach her toes. He had to go beyond that. He had to advance to a level his past self could never have dreamed of.

In this new life, Lee Sungmin had gained several purposes.

First, he would firmly seize every opportunity his past life afforded him. The Martial Skeleton in Zenavis, the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art from the old bookstore, the Holy Spirit Pill from the tournament, and that was not the end of it. There were a few more things he could obtain.

Second, he would survive.

And third, there was Wiji Hoyeon. He did not think he could ever become stronger than her. The chasm between what they possessed, what they were born with, was simply too vast.

Still, he made it his goal to surpass her.

{sep}

Two more weeks passed, and Wiji Hoyeon finally completed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style). She had successfully lowered the entry barrier of the original art and mixed in the familiar forms of the Soul Chasing Spear Art at the beginning.

Even then, she did not leave.

"I said I'd leave in a month. For the next two weeks, I'll guide you through the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style)."

For the next two weeks, Lee Sungmin received her guidance. When those two weeks were over, they stood facing each other in the backyard of Jack's Inn. Today was the day Wiji Hoyeon would leave Zenavis. Lee Sungmin had lent her 100,000 Eru for travel expenses. Her attire was simple, just the martial arts uniform she wore when she was first summoned and a bag Jack had given her. It held beef jerky and other supplies for her journey. She carried no weapon, for she had never needed one.

"I'll give you a twenty-second head start," she said, setting down her bag. "Let's have a duel."

It was her suggestion, and Lee Sungmin did not refuse.

He already knew the outcome. He knew that no matter how desperately he fought, he could never win against her. The twenty-second head start would change nothing. He could thrust his spear a hundred times and never land a single blow.

Knowing all this, Lee Sungmin still took his stance.

He wanted to see it for himself, the true skill of the woman he had made his goal.
