"......"

A yawn died on Kim Jonghyun's lips. His mouth snapped shut, and his slitted eyes gleamed as they fixed on Lee Sungmin. No, to be precise, his gaze was locked onto the armor Lee Sungmin wore.

"That's... quite a unique artifact," Kim Jonghyun remarked, a strange smile playing on his face.

It was just as Lee Sungmin had thought. The self-described unique Black Mage had seen right through the nature of his Magic Armor.

"I have some things I'd like to ask."

"This place is too noisy," Kim Jonghyun said with a shrug. "Let's go to my room."

Lee Sungmin glanced over at Scarlet, whose eyes met his with a disgusted scowl. She shook her head vigorously.

"I don't want to go into that psycho's room."

"I don't recall inviting you, Scarlet," Kim Jonghyun sneered.

At his words, Scarlet glared daggers at him, but Kim Jonghyun simply met her fierce gaze with a placid smile.

"Well then, let's go."

Though he was worried about Lloyd, the man had not yet woken up, leaving plenty of time to talk. Lee Sungmin followed Kim Jonghyun down to the basement.

Kim Jonghyun's room was as grisly as ever, filled with severed limbs and organs floating in jars. The lethargy had vanished from his eyes, replaced by a gleaming intensity as he offered Lee Sungmin a seat.

"What happened?"

"...Do you know what this Magic Armor is?"

"Of course I do," Kim Jonghyun laughed. "I don't know its name, but I can see what kind of performance that Magic Armor has as an artifact, and I have a rough idea of what's troubling you." He rubbed his hands together, staring intently at Lee Sungmin's face. "That Magic Armor is a type of contract. I've said this before, but the source of Dark Magic can be described as a contract with a transcendental being. That armor is a nasty piece of work that skips the formal process and forces a one-sided agreement."

"...Pardon?"

"Let's see..." Kim Jonghyun muttered, standing up. "I think I'll need to touch it myself to know the details." He strode over and ran his hands over the armor covering Lee Sungmin's body.

"Hmm. It's an intuitive contract. It triggers the wearer's potential."

"What does that mean?"

"To put it simply, it's like this. Let's say there's a Third-Rate mercenary who has the potential to be on par with a peak realm master, but he hasn't been able to draw it out due to a lack of effort. If he were to wear this Magic Armor, he would gain the skills of a peak realm master without needing to put in the work."

It was an easy-to-understand example. Recalling his past life, Lee Sungmin nodded blankly. "Then isn't that a good thing?"

"Huhu! Didn't I tell you? It's a one-sided, nasty contract. Most contracts with demonic beings are for life, where you gain power in exchange for your soul. Do you know why Black Mages covet the valuable souls of other humans? It's to free their own souls, which have been mortgaged to the being they contracted with. They offer up other souls to terminate the contract and receive even greater power."

"In that case..."

"If you wear that Magic Armor, you'll be forced into a contract. Your soul will be mortgaged regardless of your will. However... from the looks of it, it seems you haven't contracted with it."

"...I didn't feel any change."

"There are two possible answers for that. One is that you... have no potential to be triggered. Or, your very existence possesses a soul with which a contract is Buddhist."

At those words, something clicked in Lee Sungmin's mind. The being the Lich had summoned refused to accept him as a sacrifice. It seemed the reason a forced contract hadn't formed was related to that.

*...Although, there's also the possibility that I simply have no potential.* Lee Sungmin did not want to entertain that thought. If that were true, wouldn't it mean that no matter what martial arts theory he tried, further growth was Buddhist?

"...Can you remove the curse on this Magic Armor?"

"It's not a difficult task," Kim Jonghyun said. "However... what can you give me?"

"...What do you want?"

"Nothing grand. I'm simply... curious about what happened in that Dungeon. The Golden Magic Tower Master, Lloyd, is a mage powerful enough to be counted among the strongest in the Guild. To think that such a man returned seriously injured and carried on someone's back... it's a hard story to believe."

If information was all it took to remove the curse, it was not a bad deal. Lee Sungmin organized his thoughts for a moment before explaining what had transpired. As he listened, Kim Jonghyun's eyes sparkled with interest.

"Preskan," Kim Jonghyun muttered, nodding when Lee Sungmin spoke the Lich's name. "I see. So that Dungeon was Preskan's... In that case, it's no wonder the mercenaries were wiped out."

"...Do you know who he is?"

"Of course I do. Preskan was originally the master of the Black Magic Tower, which is composed solely of Black Mages within the Mages Guild. That was until just ten years ago. He broke a taboo for the sake of his own tragic ambition and was expelled."

"A taboo?"

"Human experimentation," Kim Jonghyun said with a chuckle. "Ah, of course... most Black Mages conduct human experiments as a matter of course. The important thing is whether you get caught or not. You know, isn't that the rule? It's fine as long as you don't get caught. Preskan was unlucky. He was discovered right in the middle of his experiments. Now I see why Lloyd headed to the Dungeon. It was Lloyd who received the mission to hunt down and kill Preskan ten years ago."

"Was Preskan a Lich back then too?"

"It's hard to find a Black Mage who has reached the level of an archmage and isn't a Lich. Becoming a Lich allows a Black Mage to gain power very easily. Losing one's physical body is a drawback, but those who walk the path of magic are all out-of-their-minds madmen. They don't care about losing something like a body." He shrugged, muttering to himself, "As for me, well, I'm a special case."

Kim Jonghyun leaned his chair back and stroked his chin. "This story gives me a lot to think about. The fact that there's another being with twisted causality... Preskan tried to offer you as a sacrifice but was rejected, wasn't he? Hmmm... The demonic being Preskan contracted with is the 'Demon King of Fear'. The Fear that Drenches the Abyss. He's a transcendental being also known by that name. As someone who hasn't contracted with him, I don't know his true name, but... this is a very peculiar case. Due to the nature of his authority, the Demon King of Fear can drive any being of a lower class who hasn't contracted with him insane with just a glance, yet you didn't go mad. Well... that's understandable, as it connects to the story of him rejecting your soul."

"Why is that?"

"I cannot say. It's for the same reason as last time. Try to think about it yourself. It's not a very difficult problem," Kim Jonghyun said with a smile.

The room... felt a little cold. Lee Sungmin shivered lightly and rubbed the back of his neck. Was it his imagination? It felt as if a cold blade had just touched his skin.

"...Because my soul belongs to someone else?"

"I cannot answer."

"The being who possesses my soul is of a higher class than the Demon King of Fear, so he couldn't accept me as a sacrifice?"

"I cannot answer," Kim Jonghyun repeated with his eyes closed. It seemed confirming it, even with a nod or shake of his head, was also Buddhist.

"...But Mushii wanted to take my soul."

"I cannot answer."

In the end, Lee Sungmin gave up trying to get an answer from him on the matter. He could not blame Kim Jonghyun. He probably did not want to die either.

"...Black Heart. I don't know what this is."

"...It's truly strange."

CREAK, THUD.

The tilted chair returned to its original position. Kim Jonghyun crossed his legs and stared intently at Lee Sungmin's chest.

"Black Heart... You said the heart in the air suddenly disappeared and was absorbed into your body. However, my 'eyes' can't read any such foreign energy from you."

"...Pardon?"

"It's very strange. Even if Preskan's magic skills are on a higher level than mine a martial arts theory, there shouldn't be anything I can't see with my eyes... In this case, should I consider the link between the soul and the body? No, even so, there's no way I can't see it... Is it something shamanistic, not magic, but in the end, they should be on the same track...."

Kim Jonghyun's lips moved as he mumbled, his words shifting from an explanation into a private monologue.

"Ah... I'm sorry. This is one of my bad habits," he said, snapping out of it. "In any case, I don't know what that heart is, but I'm certain of this. Other mages probably won't be able to notice its existence inside your body either."

"...That's a relief, but. I don't even know what this thing is..."

"What Preskan was researching was the ultimate lifeform," Kim Jonghyun stated, the corners of his mouth twitching upwards. "That's why he devoted himself to Chimera research. The human experimentation that caused his expulsion was also part of his experiments. If he conducted the same research for ten years, and the Chimera with that heart reduced Lloyd to such a state... then the heart you absorbed must be for the ultimate lifeform that Preskan desired."

"But I don't feel any changes."

"It might be because the heart hasn't fully assimilated with your body yet. Actually, the fastest way would be to cut open your chest right here and check... but if I did that, I couldn't guarantee your life."

"I'll pass."

"A shame, but it can't be helped. Ah, and. You said you were mentally suppressed by Preskan, right?"

"I... think so."

"But your mind is fine, and you haven't gone mad. Huhu! Your protection is quite special. I don't know if being protected by such an excellent blessing is a fortune or a misfortune." As he muttered this, Kim Jonghyun's eyes lit up as if he had just thought of something. "Ah. I can't answer your questions, but I know of a being who can."

"Who is it?"

"If it's a being whose causality is twisted like yours, they might be able to answer your questions."

"I don't know who that is."

"The best thing would be to find Preskan and ask him... but that's practically Buddhist. Otherwise, how about visiting another 'god'?"

"A god?"

"Yes. Mushii didn't answer your questions. To be precise, he avoided answering, but another god might be able to."

Ah.

A short sound escaped Lee Sungmin's lips as he nodded. The God of Time in Dreamoor. The being whose existence he had learned of through Mushii might be able to answer his questions.

"...I understand."

"I don't know if my words were helpful."

"They were very helpful."

Just as Lee Sungmin finished his reply, a knock came from beyond the door. Kim Jonghyun glanced toward it, and the closed door swung open.

"Lee Sungmin?"

Standing there was one of the mages he had seen upstairs.

"Lloyd has woken up. He said he would like to meet with you. Would you please come with me?"

Hearing this, Lee Sungmin looked at Kim Jonghyun, who let out a small laugh and nodded.

"We don't have anything more to talk about, do we?"

"...Thank you for the valuable information."

"Don't mention it. Come again next time. I've taken a liking to you," Kim Jonghyun grinned, showing his teeth.
