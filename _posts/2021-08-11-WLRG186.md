The promise had been clear from the beginning.

When Envirus had first found himself lost in the chaos of the square, he had needed Lee Sungmin's help. In return for that aid, Lee Sungmin had demanded one thing, that Envirus handle the Dragon Heart in his possession. Envirus had accepted.

It was an oath bound by Mana, and breaking it meant a death of being torn apart by the very same power.

"...I refuse."

Still,, knowing this, Envirus refused.

His life was already forfeit, exchanged with Mana. His fate was sealed. What difference would dying for a broken oath make?

At those words, Lee Sungmin's face hardened.

*That damn bastard. I went through all this hell because that bastard said he'd handle the Dragon Heart, and now he's going to go back on his word?*

"Didn't you make an oath to Mana?"

"I did."

"I'm no mage, but I know what happens when you don't keep an oath to Mana."

"If you don't keep an oath, there's only death," Envirus said, a dry chuckle escaping his lips. "I know that well, but what does it matter now? I'm going to die soon anyway."

He had failed, even after trading his life with Mana. A bitter smile touched Envirus's lips. His life was already nearing its end, and though he still drew breath, he was not confident he could kill Lee Sungmin in the little time he had left.

To Envirus's eyes, the current Lee Sungmin was a strange and unsettling mixture. The Demonic Energy and inner arts that had been so erratic just a moment ago were now in a state of bizarre stability. Of course, that was only how it appeared from the outside. In reality, Lee Sungmin had only just gained the upper hand over his inhumanity, seizing control of a body that had already mutated to be near that of a Yokai.

This balance, however, could shatter at any moment. He did not know when his inhumanity would lash out again, or when his body would succumb completely. Lee Sungmin knew this as well, but for now, his anger at the unreasonable man before him overshadowed any other concern.

"Why do you say you're going to die?"

"I exchanged my life with Mana to kill you, but... haha! This failed too. Nothing but failure," Envirus said, his voice gloomy. A fate of ruinous death. To think he would meet his end less than a day after getting involved with Lee Sungmin.

*Was it always meant to turn out this way?* Envirus felt a surge of bitterness. Yes, perhaps this was something that could never have been stopped.

"You will not die."

It was Kim Jonghyun who spoke.

At the sudden sound of his voice, both Lee Sungmin and Envirus looked up. Envirus opened his mouth to ask Kim Jonghyun what he meant, but his face suddenly turned deathly pale.

"BLECH!"

A violent tremor wracked his body. This was not the collapse from his exchange with Mana, but the agonizing price of his broken oath. It was an excruciating pain, the ruinous death he was fated to reach as Cain. Envirus coughed up blood again and again as he crumpled to the ground.

"Sorry for breaking the promise..."

"No," Kim Jonghyun said with a hearty laugh. "I told you, you won't die."

Kim Jonghyun's eyes saw what the others could not. Spacetime itself was wavering. A being whose very existence shook the fabric of reality was about to Advent. Worried he might get caught up in it, Kim Jonghyun took another step back. Lee Sungmin, not yet sensing the Advent, tilted his head in confusion.

"...No... way..."

Through his fading consciousness, Envirus realized something. He hurriedly looked up at the sky. Lee Sungmin followed his gaze, and in that moment.

KWAAAAA!

A dazzling light descended from the heavens. Startled by its brilliance, Lee Sungmin staggered backward.

"You're lucky," Kim Jonghyun burst out laughing. He had never imagined he would see a being of such caliber with his own two eyes.

The pillar of light, connecting heaven and earth, shook violently, scattering particles of light in every direction. When they gathered again, they coalesced into the form of a beautiful woman.

Spirit Queen.

The queen who ruled the Elemental Realm, a being who should never have appeared in this world, had personally made her Advent.

"Ah...!" Envirus looked up at her with an expression of utter despair.

The queen should not have come to this world. Its tainted air was like a deadly poison to her incomparably pure existence.

"Why... Why are you...!"

"Because I did not permit your death," the Spirit Queen murmured with a sigh. It was their first reunion in a hundred years, and there was so much she wanted to say, but she, more than anyone, knew they could not speak at length in this world.

"Human."

The queen turned her head to look at Lee Sungmin. Her eyes, which had been filled with fond emotion for Envirus, were now as transparent and emotionless as a doll's.

"I shall fulfill the contract made by Cain, no, Envirus, in his stead."

"What are you doing! You don't need to do that, I'm already..."

"I believe I told you. I did not permit your death. Before you contracted with me, and after. Your life belongs only to me."

"I've already exchanged my life with Mana..."

"What does that matter!" the queen cried out sharply. "Even if you exchanged your life with Mana, Mana will never be able to take it. Because I will not allow it."

After spitting out those words, she looked at Lee Sungmin again. Her gaze, completely different from the deep emotion she had shown Envirus, was now befitting the Spirit Queen, full of pressure as it looked down on him.

"State the terms of the contract."

Honestly, it did not matter to Lee Sungmin. Whether it was Envirus or the queen, he just needed the Dragon Heart. He took the wooden box from his Subspace Pocket and opened it. The queen's eyebrows twitched.

"A Dragon Heart. You already possess sufficient power, yet you covet even this."

"Can you do it?"

"It is not even worth discussing."

With those words, the queen raised her hand. Where Envirus had said he needed time and preparation, the queen needed neither. He was, after all, merely a human archmage. He and the Spirit Queen were on completely different levels of existence.

"Please, stop. That man is an incomparably dangerous being. If he obtains the Dragon Heart..."

"You will no longer concern yourself with that matter," the queen declared. "I will not permit your ruinous death, and what kind of end this world will meet is not your concern. For I, too, will not concern myself with it."

"But...!"

"Enough!"

The queen did not want to hear any more. She waved her hand, and Envirus's body was consumed by a bright light, vanishing from sight. Even the Spirit Queen could not simply erase the exchange of a life with Mana as if it had never happened.

"Is it alright to bring a human into the Elemental Realm?" Kim Jonghyun sneered, knowing exactly what she had done.

The Spirit Queen's eyes widened as she glared at him. "Silence, you filthy beast who sold his soul to a Demon Lord."

"That's true for all Black Mages, I suppose."

"The Elemental Realm is my domain. When I, the queen, decide to bring a new subject into my domain, who can defy me?"

"Well, if you put it that way..." *This probably isn't a problem that can be solved by just insisting.* Kim Jonghyun snickered but said nothing more.

His attention was now focused on Lee Sungmin. A body that was close to that of a Yokai, though not yet complete. A man who had not abandoned his humanity, and whose martial skill stood at the very edge of the Supreme Peak. No, perhaps he had already entered the Transcendent Realm, and if the Dragon Heart were added to that?

A brilliant light sparkled from the queen's fingertips. It enveloped the wooden box, and the Dragon Heart inside trembled as it absorbed the light. The heart floated up, changing its form before Lee Sungmin's eyes. It transformed into a shimmering red liquid that drifted toward him.

"Will you take it?" the queen asked.

Lee Sungmin slowly nodded his head.

With an expressionless face, the queen flicked her finger. The shimmering liquid moved to hover in front of Lee Sungmin's face.

"Take it."

Lee Sungmin carefully opened his mouth. The liquid had no taste when it first touched his lips. As he opened his mouth wider, it filled completely with the Dragon Heart. He had imagined the metallic taste of blood, but it had no smell or taste at all. He moved his Adam's apple repeatedly, swallowing all of it. He could feel the viscous substance slide down his throat.

After watching Lee Sungmin swallow the entire Dragon Heart, the queen turned around. Perhaps she had stayed in this world longer than intended, for her face held a look of extreme displeasure as she dissolved into particles of light.

KWAANG!

A pillar of light shot high into the sky, piercing the clouds and vanishing as suddenly as it had appeared.

"...Ugh..."

Lee Sungmin clamped a hand over his mouth. He had swallowed the entire Dragon Heart. Only then did something begin to happen inside his body. Pain and nausea surged through him, and he curled up from the intense agony he felt in his qi center.

His heart pounded like a war drum. This was a heart that had swallowed a Great Cycle Pill without any trouble, but the magic power contained in the Dragon Heart was on an entirely different scale from refined miracle herbs.

Though he wanted to watch until the end, Kim Jonghyun knew this was his chance to slip away. He had taken all of Arbes's magic and power, but knowing it and being able to use it were two different things. He needed time to master it. He had no desire to clash with Lee Sungmin, and he knew that Lee Sungmin was not yet aware of his true identity.

With Envirus's binding curse gone, Aine lay limply on the ground. Kim Jonghyun glanced at her, a slight curiosity stirring within him. Lee Sungmin had regained consciousness, but what about Aine? She was still alive, and Kim Jonghyun had no intention of killing her, and at this moment, Lee Sungmin was collapsed, unable to control his own body.

"......"

Kim Jonghyun stroked his chin and formed a hand seal. Aine's limp body floated into the air. He wondered how she, who possessed the same heart as Lee Sungmin, would mutate. He was quite curious. Things had not gone according to Arbes's wishes, but Kim Jonghyun agreed that Aine possessed the qualities of the Slaughter Predator that Predator had long desired.

*Though the same could be said for you.*

Kim Jonghyun glanced at the collapsed Lee Sungmin, who did not even notice him leaving with Aine.

He snickered and turned away. They would surely meet again.

*By then, what will I have become, and what kind of being will Lee Sungmin have become?*

With that thought, a pleasant smile spread across Kim Jonghyun's face.

His departure from the square with the unconscious Aine brought all the events there to a close. Thanks to the Spirit Queen, Envirus had barely escaped with his life, returning to the Elemental Realm with her to recover from his fatal blow. The Void Monarch had learned something, and Lee Sungmin had gained the clumsy body of a Yokai and Demonic Energy.

In truth, that was not the end of everything in Urmuri.

This was around the time Lee Sungmin and Envirus had just broken through the barrier.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon clung to the frayed edges of his consciousness. If he lost it here, he would die. No, was he already dead? The fact that such a thought was even drifting through his mind showed just how serious his condition was.

*Not yet.*

He shook his head violently, trying to clear the blood that covered his vision. Staggering to his feet, the Crazy Heavenly Demon glared at Chuseong, who was wreathed in flames and radiating an immense heat.

Having perfected his Extreme Temperature Immunity, the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not feel the heat, but the flames emanating from Chuseong, a Gangcheori, were hot enough to burn the soul. He grabbed his almost completely burnt-out upper garment and tore it off.

"Stop it!" Rubia screamed from behind him.

He was sure he had told her to run. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's expression soured as he moved his arms lightly. He had been sent flying by a heavy blow, but fortunately, his arms were not broken. That was enough.

"Foolish bastard," Chuseong sneered.
