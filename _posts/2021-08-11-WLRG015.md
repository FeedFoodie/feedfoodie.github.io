"……"

*Did I hear that wrong?* Lee Sungmin stared, his eyes wide. The face of Wiji Hoyeon, the target of his disbelief, remained utterly impassive.

"What did you just say?" he asked, his voice barely a whisper.

"I said I'm a woman." Wiji Hoyeon raised a hand and pointed to herself with a simple, direct gesture.

Lee Sungmin's mouth slowly fell open. He had not misheard. She was telling him she was a woman.

"No way!" he shrieked.

The commotion brought Jack and Lula, who poked their heads out from the kitchen to see what was wrong.

"My apologies for the disturbance," Wiji Hoyeon said politely toward them before turning back to Lee Sungmin, her brow furrowed in annoyance. "What do you think you're doing, screaming like that?"

"What! Of course I'm going to scream when I'm surprised!"

"Hmm, I suppose that's true." She nodded at his reply, and her nonchalant reaction left Lee Sungmin speechless.

He frantically searched the memories of his past life. The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. There had never been a single rumor about her being a woman. Setting aside his past life, Lee Sungmin looked at the Wiji Hoyeon standing before him now. A high-bridged nose. Long, slender eyelashes. Large, dark eyes and pale white skin. He had always assumed her build was small simply because she was young, but hearing her claim to be a woman made him see her in an entirely new light.

"…S-Seriously?"

"Seriously. Would I lie about something like this? Or do I need to go to my room and take off my pants for you to believe me?" Wiji Hoyeon said with a chuckle. As she spoke, a few things started to click into place for him.

Wiji Hoyeon had a pretty face. He had thought that if she grew up like this, she would become a handsome man. Yes, that was what he had thought, but seeing her now as a woman… she looked different.

*Come to think of it, we never bathed together.*

This inn used a communal bath. Lee Sungmin always took one before sleeping, right after he finished his hunting and training, but Wiji Hoyeon had never once tried to join him. He hadn't thought there was any particular reason for it. He had simply concluded that the Little Heavenly Demon was messier than he expected. That was all.

"You don't have a chest…"

"I'm wearing bandages," she said, slightly lifting the front of her martial arts uniform. Sure enough, he could see white bandages wrapped tightly around her chest. "They haven't grown much yet. I don't know if they'll get bigger in the future, but I'd rather they didn't. It's quite uncomfortable having lumps of flesh on my chest."

"…No way…" Lee Sungmin muttered again, his mind reeling. To think that Wiji Hoyeon was actually a woman. He had never once considered such a thing.

"Why were you dressed as a man?"

"The great Cult Leader wished for it," she explained. "I was born with a Martial Skeleton perfectly suited for learning calamity-level martial arts, but unfortunately, I was a woman. It seems the Cult Leader found my talent and Martial Skeleton too precious to waste, so he forced me to live as a man since I was young." She added with a laugh, "That was probably the first command I ever received from the Cult Leader, my father."

"But the Demonic Cult doesn't exist in this world. Neither does the great Cult Leader. There's no reason for me to live as a man anymore. Still, I've lived as a man for thirteen years. I couldn't just throw it away overnight. I'll probably continue to dress this way, as long as this chest of mine doesn't grow too big to be hidden by bandages."

"I hope it doesn't," Wiji Hoyeon mumbled. She met Lee Sungmin's dazed expression and opened her mouth to speak again. "This is my secret. What's yours?"

"…Umm…"

He couldn't believe it was a lie. *A woman, she's a woman… the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon is a woman.* Lee Sungmin desperately tried to regain his clouded senses. After hearing a secret like that, he couldn't stubbornly refuse to share his own. He swallowed a sigh and finally spoke.

"…I'm a regressor."

"What kind of bullshit is that?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, tilting her head.

Although her words stung a little, Lee Sungmin drew a patient breath and let the comment slide. He told her the story of his regression, of how he had obtained something called the Stone of Reincarnation, died, and returned to the very beginning. Despite having called it bullshit, Wiji Hoyeon listened to his words in silence once he began his proper explanation.

When the story was over, she simply nodded her head. "I see."

"It's hard to believe, but I have no choice but to believe it," she murmured, looking up at the empty air for a moment. "This world, magic, and all that. I have to believe it. In the Central Plains, I would have dismissed it as nonsense, but it's different here." She looked back at him. "What was I like in your past life?"

"…What?"

"I must have existed in your past life, right? What was I like?"

"What you were like…" Lee Sungmin recounted the image of Wiji Hoyeon that remained in his memories. Since he had never met her directly, all he could do was talk about the rumors that had surrounded her.

"I see. It seems I was living well. At the very least, it means I didn't die for thirteen years."

"I… guess so."

"And I continued to dress as a man," Wiji Hoyeon chuckled. "Probably. It seems my chest didn't grow much even after thirteen years. That's a relief."

He couldn't tell if she was joking or being serious.

"But there's one thing I'm curious about," she said. "Why did you ask me not to kill you?"

"…Our meeting wasn't fate or coincidence. I… I wanted to see the beginning of the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. So I was at the square, matching the date and time you were summoned, and I was watching you."

"Ah, I see. Because our meeting was a lie. Did you think I would beat you to death out of anger for being used?" She hit the nail on the head. Lee Sungmin remained silent instead of answering.

"What a strange thought," Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing. "Think about it. When you and I first met, you were just watching me. It was I who approached you first, intrigued by you. It was I who first suggested we become friends. Teaching you the Violet Dawn Divine Art and giving you advice on martial arts? Haha! Wasn't it I who offered to do so first? You never asked me for anything. I was the one who offered it to you."

Put that way, he had nothing to say. When he thought about it, she was right.

"You worry too much. Is it because you've already experienced death once? Or is that just your personality?"

"Both," he admitted.

"Heh. In any case, I have no intention of killing you. In fact, I've become more interested in you. To die and return to the beginning… it's an interesting story, and it explains the contradictions I felt about you. Thirteen years. No, was it ten years of learning martial arts? Ten years should be enough to get used to it. Though it's a pity that you're still at that level after holding on for ten years."

"Son of a bitch," Lee Sungmin spat out a curse at her mutterings. The factual attacks that came out of nowhere hurt too much.

"According to you, the me from your past life left this city a month after being summoned to Zenavis and disappeared for three years."

"Yeah."

"What could I have been doing for three years?"

"How would I know?"

"That Wiji Hoyeon and the current me are probably not the same person, but our fundamental way of thinking, our desires, would be the same. During those three years, I probably didn't do anything particularly grand. I likely just traveled here and there."

"Traveled?"

"What the person named Wiji Hoyeon desired most desperately was freedom. Freedom from the oppression of the Demonic Cult, of the Cult Leader. I'm happy to have been summoned to this world because I've gained the freedom I longed for." Those were her true feelings. "It's the same now. I want to leave right this instant. I've wandered this city for a week and savored my freedom, but this city is too small. I want to see more and experience more."

"…Are you saying you're leaving?"

"I was planning to leave soon, but…" Wiji Hoyeon looked at Lee Sungmin. "What is it that you want to do? You've already lived in this world once. You lived for thirteen years, and you died. You gained a miraculous encounter and returned to the beginning. So. What is it you want to do in this life?"

Lee Sungmin couldn't answer her question right away. It was a topic he had been trying not to think about.

"You have all the memories from your past life. What do you want to do based on those memories?"

"…A better life than my last one."

"I saw many people while wandering the city for a week. The Classless. Not all of them go hunting in the forest. They do other kinds of labor to survive, in their own ways. Have you thought about living like that?"

"…It'd be a waste," Lee Sungmin grumbled. "I have the memories of my past life. I've written down all the information I need to live a better life than before."

"How greedy. Well, I don't want to blame you, but what is it you ultimately want to do? A better life than your last one? Isn't that incredibly vague? Don't you have some kind of goal?"

"Something like that… I don't know." Lee Sungmin pondered for a moment before letting out a short sigh. "In my past life, I only thought about earning enough to eat for the day. Just as you say whenever you get the chance, I lack talent. Still, I did my best to survive. I died an unlucky death and came back to life. Honestly, I don't have anything you could call a goal. I'll probably just live similarly to my past life."

"I see," Wiji Hoyeon nodded. She had no intention of offering him advice on his direction in life. After all, she was only thirteen years old, while Lee Sungmin was twenty-seven. It would be ridiculous to receive life advice from a thirteen-year-old.

"But you," Lee Sungmin started, "are you… going to continue being friends with me?"

"Is there a reason not to?"

"I'm older than you, you know? My mental age is…"

"That's not a problem." So she intended to keep speaking informally to him.

"Let's go upstairs." Wiji Hoyeon stood up.

Lee Sungmin followed her as she walked ahead of him, up to the second floor and into her room. Come to think of it, this was the first time he had ever been inside.

"I shall bestow upon you the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear," Wiji Hoyeon announced with a solemn expression. Her face alone was that of a grandmaster. She sat cross-legged on the bed and stared down at him.

"…Should I kneel or something?"

"Just grab a chair and sit. We're friends, what's the fuss?" she grumbled.

Lee Sungmin pulled over a chair and sat across from her.

"Of all the Spear Techniques I remember, this is the best. It's a martial art used by a martial artist called the Spear King a hundred years ago, who dominated the world with the weapon known as the spear. Isn't the name itself arrogant? The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, meaning the greatest spear in the nine heavens. It is a truly incredible Spear Technique."

"I can't learn a martial art that's too difficult."

"I know. Your talent is pathetic. Just like the Violet Dawn Divine Art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear is also one of the divine arts. If you try to touch it with your talent, you'll never learn it in your lifetime."

"I know I don't have talent, but do you have to put it like that?"

"What can I do when it's the truth?"

He had no retort to her question.

"So I'll modify it to suit you. It will take quite a while, but it's better for you to learn a modified version than to learn it as is."

"…You can do that?"

"I am a calamity," Wiji Hoyeon replied, her face solemn once more. "It's a fact acknowledged even by the Cult Leader of the Demonic Cult. I'm not completely overhauling it, just lowering the entry barrier a bit and mixing in some other useful Spear Techniques. I can modify it that way. Even so, it will only be a little easier to learn at the beginning. The fundamentals can't be changed. The Spear King who created the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was also an absolute nemesis of a martial artist and a calamity."

Even so, it was an incredible feat. To think she would modify an already established martial art. It just went to show that Wiji Hoyeon was a calamity, born on a different level from Lee Sungmin.

"Let's call it the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style)."

"Why on earth?"

"It's a Spear Technique tailored for you, so your name should be in it. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style). Isn't it great?"

"You think that's great?"

"If you don't like it, make it yourself. I like this name," Wiji Hoyeon insisted stubbornly.

Lee Sungmin was about to protest but closed his mouth. She was going through the trouble of making it for him, so he felt a little too sorry to complain just because he was dissatisfied with the name.
