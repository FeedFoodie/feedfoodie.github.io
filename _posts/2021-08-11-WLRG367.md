Wiji Hoyeon had given him a single, stark command. He must not die. Acting on this, Lee Sungmin proposed that Kazuya accompany him to the Fairy Forest, but Kazuya only shook his head with a bitter smile. He was bound to the village, he explained, for he had to remain there to maintain the forest's Sealing until the day he died.

It did not seem right to leave the man to subsist on fasting pills alone, so Lee Sungmin used Erebrisa to prepare a meal for Kazuya, leaving it in his room before he departed.

"……"

Wiji Hoyeon was in constant communication with the Demonic Spirit, which meant she likely knew more about the form of The End than he did. As he prepared to leave the village, Lee Sungmin cast one last look back at the Sleeping Forest.

The Spirit Queen, the Vampire Queen, and Tam.

[You said you failed to reap the harvest.]

The Void Monarch's voice echoed in his mind, lost in deep thought.

[This world was said to be a Slaughterhouse for obtaining a completed technique. Does that mean the 'technique' has not yet been acquired?]

*…He said the Master was greatly involved in the beginning of The End.*

The Slaughter Predator's words returned to him. The End had begun in earnest because the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had grown too powerful for a mere human, because the martial realm he had reached was too profound. By that point, the Chairman's technique must have been fully complete. The Slaughter Predator had also explained that the form of The End differed each time, with the only constant being the appearance of the Predator itself. A regressor who returned to the past was designated as the Slaughter Predator from the moment of their return, and when The End commenced, they would awaken as a monster to kill and devour every being in the world.

He had learned one more thing. The Sleeping Forest held a monster Sealed in its depths. Tam. Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. The Slaughter Predator devoured beings, and Tam devoured the world itself. Inside Tam's stomach, the world would return to nothingness.

If that was the case, then Tam's appearance would mark the very end of The End. Wiji Hoyeon had been certain that Tam would not appear yet, which also meant Kazuya would not die unless the final stage was upon them.

Still, he could not feel relieved. He would have to watch over Kazuya's safety from now on. Wiji Hoyeon had called Tam a monster that even she could not handle, a creature that would not awaken from its deep slumber unless the world was truly at its conclusion.

Things to do.

A low hum vibrated from the bracelet on his wrist.

[Who is it?]

Rubia asked, nestled in his arms. Lee Sungmin checked the bracelet, which was connected to Scarlet and Baek Sogo. They had gone to the lake, and this reaction could only mean one thing. It seemed they had succeeded in reverse-summoning the Spirit King. Lee Sungmin summoned a Fairy Horse and galloped toward Behenger.

{sep}

A week later, the Spear King and Lloyd joined them in the Fairy Forest.

"It was boring," the Spear King announced, his expression soured with disappointment as he recounted his experience.

He explained that they had spent nearly ten days in the Dahar Ruins and had successfully attacked the Advented Spirit King, forcing its reverse-summon.

"Its attack patterns were tricky, and it was annoyingly tough, but that was it. It was boring. I waited for nearly ten days, getting my hopes up, only to be disappointed by how weak it was."

"It's a relief that it ended easily," Lee Sungmin offered.

"Are these things called Spirit Kings always this weak?" the Spear King grumbled. In truth, the Spirit King had not been weak at all. The Spear King was simply too strong. He was a martial artist at the apex of spear mastery, a man capable of comprehending insights from the very Unorthodox Alliance Chairman who had brought The End upon the world.

"Spirit Kings who have just Advented are usually weak," Rubia chimed in.

"Look at this one," the Spear King sneered. "Are you taking its side because you're both Elementals?"

"I'm not taking sides, it's just…"

"What do you mean you're not? 'Course you are. Would you have preferred I died fighting it? Huh? Or maybe I should've given it some time to power up after it Advented before I fought it?"

Rubia's shoulders trembled at his sharp tone. Of all Lee Sungmin's companions in the Fairy Forest, the Spear King possessed the most abrasive personality.

"…You're just incredibly strong, old man. Happy now?" she muttered.

"What part of me looks like an old man?"

"Well, you're old enough to be one."

The Spear King snorted at Rubia's grumbling. A short distance away, Lloyd sat slumped over, letting out a deep sigh. In just ten days, his cheeks had become hollow. It was easy to guess how much the Spear King had tormented him in the Dahar Ruins.

"You've suffered a great deal," the Black Dragon Knight said, patting Lloyd's shoulder with an understanding expression.

Scarlet was at the Magic Tower, and Baek Sogo was meditating in the shade of a nearby tree.

[Lee Sungmin.]

Yana's voice cut through the Spear King's complaints. Lee Sungmin turned to see her standing at a distance, watching him.

[What is it?]

[For a moment…]

Yana wore a troubled expression, a look he had never seen on her face before. Ignoring the Spear King's chatter, Lee Sungmin approached her.

[…It is time for me to go to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion.]

Her words made his cheeks tense. The power Yana possessed as a Nine-Tailed Fox was a direct gift from the Demonic Spirit. Because of this, she had to periodically travel to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion in the Hyujael Mountains to perform a ritual.

Lee Sungmin had never been there himself. He had originally planned to visit the pavilion and meet the Demonic Spirit after waking from the Sealing, but he had never gone. Laplace's warning not to trust the Demonic Spirit too much had bothered him, and he had avoided a meeting ever since.

[…What happens if you don't go?]

[The power I have as a Nine-Tailed Fox will disappear,] Yana said with a bitter smile.

A Nine-Tailed Fox was a Yokai that a fox with a yokai nature transformed into after a thousand years of training. Yana, who had lived for just over four hundred years, was originally far from achieving such a state, a fact she herself acknowledged.

[What I received from the Demonic Spirit was Demonic Energy that I could not have obtained otherwise. No matter how talented I was as a Yokai, there was nothing I could do about the natural Demonic Energy accumulated through a thousand years of training.]

The Demonic Spirit had granted Yana's wish, giving her the energy necessary to become a Nine-Tailed Fox.

[If I don't go to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion and perform the ritual, the Demonic Energy I received will disappear. That was the contract from the beginning.]

*…I understand.*

Losing her power was something neither of them wanted. Lee Sungmin knew there was no other choice. Perhaps he was just overreacting. His only reason for suspicion was Laplace's warning, and it was possible the Demonic Spirit had no ulterior motives.

Lee Sungmin took Yana to Urmuri on a Fairy Horse. As it happened, he had some business there as well.

"Again," Frau said, looking at Lee Sungmin with a fed-up expression.

In response to that blatant stare, Lee Sungmin meekly produced a tribute from within his robes. A large gold ingot and several jewels landed on the table, prompting Frau to click his tongue.

"It's too much money to refuse."

"Please."

"Let me see," Frau grumbled, extending his hand.

Lee Sungmin placed the item from the Moon Queen that he had found in the Moon Palace on the table, giving Frau a subtle look. Frau licked his lower lip in response.

"What. You wanna go for a round?"

"Pardon?"

"No? Then why are you looking at me like that?"

"No… it's just, isn't it a bit much for me to keep coming and asking for favors like this? And isn't it inconvenient for you too, Frau? So…"

"Get lost," Frau spat, his eyes flashing. "This kind of relationship is just right. I can't see your fate anymore, and it's true that a forced destiny isn't entangled with you… but that makes you more dangerous. Being with you is the perfect way to die a dog's death."

"What do you mean by that?"

"Not being bound by fate means you can do whatever you want," Frau muttered, lifting the bronze mirror from the table. Its surface was old and dirty from long neglect. "And a guy like that isn't living quietly. He's openly defying the fate of this world. How do you think the beings called gods, who set fate and guide the world according to it, will take that?"

"…They probably won't be very interested."

"Maybe so. It's impossible to change the position of a boulder by throwing an egg at it, but you know, the egg that hits the boulder will always break," Frau said with a short laugh. "…I don't want to call Abel's death a dog's death. I don't want to say he was less than an egg, either, but in the end, Abel died."

"If The End comes, everyone will die."

"If everyone dies, then it's rather fair," Frau's laughter grew louder. "If you struggle to stop The End, end up dying, and as a result, The End is changed… so what? You're already dead. I'd be dead, while other people live laughing happily in a world where The End didn't come. I hate that kind of thing. What, do I seem selfish to you?"

"……"

"I like your honesty. That's right, I'm selfish. I only care about myself. I don't want to die a dog's death either. Anyway, I'll pass on getting involved with you and dying a pointless death."

"……"

"I'll give you one piece of advice. I told you about the Destiny Force a long time ago."

The force that acts on fate. Any being living in this world was inevitably, unavoidably, and naturally entangled in its great current. Fate was an ambiguous thing that could be altered, but its current was so vast that fate itself would not completely change unless something extraordinary happened.

"You, who have escaped the Destiny Force, do not belong in that current. Therefore, your existence can create a large variable against fate. However… if you repeatedly clash with fate, you will get swept up whether you like it or not, and you will be shattered. Both you, and those who act with you."

Frau muttered as he formed a hand seal. A hazy light enveloped the Moon Queen's belongings on the table.

"…What is this?" Frau muttered.

"……"

The items enveloped in the mist suddenly shattered. Frau quickly withdrew his spell and threw his hands out to create a barrier.

FWHOOSH!

A pure white light erupted from the shattered items and rushed toward Frau, but the barrier he had created slammed into it, blocking the attack.

"……"

Frau spat with an annoyed expression.

"I told you, being with you leads to a dog's death."

"What was that just now?"

"Can't you tell by looking? The owner of the item set up this device from the start. They planted a curse that activates if someone tries to track them through it. It's a good thing it was a sorcerer of my caliber. Any average joe would have been unable to withstand that curse and died."

Frau waved his hand, sweeping the shattered items aside.

"Tracking her location failed. Seeing as she even planted a curse, she must really not want to be found. Even though I failed, I'm not giving back the jewels. I almost died, after all."

"…You can keep the jewels."

"Go back."

Frau plopped down on the bed and waved his hands dismissively.

"Thanks to this incident, I want to get involved with you even less."

After giving the grumbling Frau a silent bow, Lee Sungmin left the mansion. He did not return directly to the Fairy Forest. Since he was already out, there was another place he wanted to visit.
