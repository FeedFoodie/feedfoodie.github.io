Gemini's gaze locked with Jeniela's, and he saw an emotion churning there that he could not name. It was a feeling foreign to them both, something terribly ill-suited for his sister. It lurked beneath the familiar layers of anger, irritation, hatred, and killing intent.

Gemini swallowed hard. The moment he identified that alien feeling, a cold understanding washed over him. The fight was over.

Jeniela… had been defeated.

The full moon hanging high in the sky on this Night of the Full Moon would not grant her victory. She should have killed him when she had the chance. If Jeniela had not tried to savor the situation so much, it all would have ended hours ago. Not just Lee Sungmin, but everyone in this forest would have been slaughtered by her hand.

However, not now.

Gemini recalled the emotion he had seen in her eyes. Something unfamiliar. Something terribly ill-suited for Jeniela. It was an emotion she had never once been conscious of during the long seven hundred years of her existence.

It was fear.

Jeniela was already denying the feeling, telling herself her mind had simply slackened for a moment. She had let her guard down because she was enjoying herself too much. She tried to dismiss it as a mere unpleasantness, like stepping on a wriggling worm only for it to leap up and cling to her ankle.

Even so, she knew her situation was dangerous.

Jeniela panted as she looked at Gemini. Directly before her, Lee Sungmin stared down with cold eyes, contemplating how to inflict upon her the most cruel, painful, and despair-inducing death imaginable.

Jeniela was thinking the same thing. She needed an answer. Her severed left arm, a part of her spiritual body, refused to regenerate. Her magic power was infinite, but even when she wielded it, she could not overwhelm him. This kind of predicament was utterly unfamiliar to her. Never before had she been the weaker one in a fight. The All-Seeing Eye had always allowed her to wisely avoid conflicts with powerful enemies, and even when a fight was unavoidable, it ensured she fought from a position of advantage.

The Eye of Futuresight had vanished a few years ago, but she had never imagined something like this could happen. For seven hundred years, she had cultivated her power. The Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance had vanished from the world. The Sword Immortal, lauded as the greatest master of the orthodox murim, could not have matched her. The Martial God was a mere puppet of the Spirit and never a consideration. The brothers Cain and Abel, who stood at the pinnacle of human magic, had conveniently died, and Lloyd, Gries's successor, lacked their talent.

There was no one in this world who could stop her. That was why this was all so unfamiliar, this feeling of fear she was experiencing for the first time.

*What should I do.*

The method of recalling the power of all her vampires was no longer an option. The Vampires of Trabia had been hunted down by Lee Sungmin while she was away. There was no aid. It was unexpected, but it seemed Ju Won was dead. Chen and Kun were also dead, their meager power already returned to her and found wanting. The power of the Blood Demon… was with Lee Sungmin.

*What should I do.* Her immortality no longer gave her an advantage. The pinnacle of martial arts he wielded so easily severed her regenerative abilities. The only reason he was not using it wantonly was because he wanted to make her completely despair.

*What should I do.*

She considered running away, but the thought was immediately dismissed. Regardless of whether it was possible, she did not want to. She was still arrogant and proud. At least, for now.

*Ah, right.*

Jeniela's eyes fell on Gemini. He was hesitating, trapped in his ridiculous conflict of love and hate for her. In truth, it was a pointless struggle. Even if Gemini added his power to hers, reversing the situation was impossible, a fact he rationally understood, yet despite knowing this, he was tormented by the thought that he had to do something. He could not just stand by and watch the death of the beloved older sister with whom he had spent hundreds of years. Still, he longed for freedom, and so he wished for her death. That, too, was ridiculous. Jeniela's death did not guarantee his freedom. After all, Lee Sungmin could simply kill him next.

*There's still one person left.*

Jeniela's thoughts moved in a different direction. From the beginning, her desire had been the world's destruction. She wanted the time of The End to come, for everything to disappear. She had witnessed the advent of the Slaughter Predator and had wished for hundreds of years that the world would be destroyed in such a way. From the moment she could no longer see the future of the Slaughter Predator, she had tried to make Aine into one herself and bring about The End.

Ultimately, her wish was the death of everything, with no exceptions. Even Jeniela herself planned to die with a happy heart when that time came. Her vampires could not refuse her, so they all awaited death with the same sentiment. The only exception among them was Gemini.

Was it because they were blood-related siblings? No, that was not the reason. The reason Jeniela never controlled Gemini's emotions was similar to the feeling of raising a pet. He was not her equal. He was someone who did not always listen, who would obey grudgingly only when treated sternly. When she bit Gemini's neck and turned him into a Vampire, she had acted on the simple intuition that she would need such a being by her side for the centuries she had yet to live. She had thought it better to make her younger sister, with whom she shared a childhood, into a pet rather than some stranger.

That action had been the right one. Among the vampires who offered unconditional loyalty, Gemini always acted like a mischievous and disobedient puppy.

Therefore, Jeniela felt no guilt. There was no reason to. Like all her vampires, Gemini was a consumable. Still, a flicker of regret touched her. If possible, she had wanted to see the end of the world with him and the Blood Demon.

*It can't be helped.*

With that final thought, Jeniela focused her consciousness on Gemini. She would take back the power he possessed as a Vampire. Of course, it would not change the situation much. While ignoring her fear, Jeniela had a premonition of her own defeat and death.

She just did not want to die alone.

"Uh…"

Gemini's body flinched. The magic power of Jeniela, engraved deep within his body, began to churn. A Lord's magic held absolute, nemesis-like control over their subordinates. At this close a distance, where they were visible to the naked eye, resistance was impossible.

"W-wait, Queen, why? Why me?" Gemini shouted in bewilderment. He had not expected Jeniela to do something like this in the slightest. *Because we're sisters… because we love each other.* He looked at Jeniela with horrified eyes, but she ignored his gaze.

"Good."

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. With a soft sound, the left Dragonic became magic, and the power filling the space materialized as a foreign barrier.

Gasping, Gemini collapsed to the ground as sensation returned to his body, which had been unable to move. The barrier Lee Sungmin had created blocked all internal and external interference. No matter how powerful a Vampire Jeniela was, inside this space, interfering with other vampires became impossible.

"What…?!" Jeniela cried out in alarm. She hurriedly lifted her head to look at Lee Sungmin.

However, Jeniela could not see his face properly. All she saw was a fist blurring toward her.

CRUNCH!

The bridge of her nose shattered. Blood spurted from the crushed bone. Her upper body snapped back, then jolted as it was struck from behind. At some point, hundreds of magic power bullets had materialized behind her. Jeniela groaned and propped herself up with her hands on the ground.

Lee Sungmin dispelled the Formless Spear in his hand and cracked the knuckles of both.

"I've been thinking."

"......"

"About how to make you despair."

"……"

Cracking his knuckles, Lee Sungmin advanced on Jeniela. She screamed and threw herself forward, her right hand swinging recklessly toward his head.

THWUMP!

The magic power bullets fired, the consecutive shots striking her side. The red magic could not protect her body. Jeniela's form spun through the air.

"First, I'm going to make it so you can't do anything you want to do."

He had not saved Gemini's life. He had simply stopped Jeniela from getting what she wanted.

"You've had fun until now, haven't you?"

Jeniela's body slammed into the ground. She glared up at Lee Sungmin with eyes full of venom. *Get up,* he seemed to prompt.

"Of course you had fun. You were orchestrating everything, looking down from on high as I struggled, weren't you? All while saying you'd make me despair." He laughed, a low, muttering sound. "You should have just stuck to me then."

"Just think of it as our positions being reversed. Now… no matter what you do, you can't make me despair. I've already felt it, and I've decided I won't let it happen. The Void Monarch wouldn't want that either."

"Aaaargh!"

Jeniela screamed as her magic power churned, but before the attack she was gathering could be unleashed, the magic power bullets moved once again. Fired from all directions, they tore her magic to shreds.

"I told you. I'll make it so you can't do anything you want to do."

Jeniela's shoulders trembled violently. She could not say anything, her lips just twitched. It was the first time she had been in such a position, so she did not even know what to say. The emotion she had tried so hard to ignore was rising with overwhelming strength.

"Try smiling a little," Lee Sungmin said as he approached.

Jeniela tried to back away, but he closed the distance faster. The method he chose to break her was the simplest form of violence. His fist struck her cheek. Her shattered jaw hung loosely. Her nose, which had just regenerated, was crushed again. He grabbed her shoulder and crushed it, then drove his knee into her abdomen.

"Gack…!"

Jeniela bent over and vomited blood. The blows were simple, but they were perfectly designed to inflict pain on an immortal like her. Her regenerative ability healed her body, but the attacks never stopped. He rained punches and kicks upon her, sending her rolling across the ground again and again until her body was caked in dirt.

"Are you used to the pain?" Lee Sungmin asked, grabbing the back of her head.

CRUNCH!

He smashed her face into the ground. Jeniela's immense magic power and her tough Vampire body were useless. Lee Sungmin's body was stronger than anything she possessed.

"Nope, you're wrong. You said it yourself. You don't get used to pain. Your tolerance just increases." He leaned closer. "How about this kind of pain?"

Lee Sungmin grabbed her wrist. The Demonic Energy he pushed inside burned her nerves and twisted her bones and muscles. The pain was like thousands of insects crawling and gnawing inside her blood vessels. The sound of crunching and cracking echoed from within her body. Jeniela's mouth fell open as she trembled violently.

"You might be used to dying, but not to this, right?"

He grabbed her neck with his left hand. Under the tight pressure, Jeniela struggled, gasping for air. The pressure made her eyes bulge out like a goldfish's. Just before she stopped breathing, he released his grip. Then, as she gasped for air, he grabbed her neck again.

"S… stop…" Jeniela muttered in a hoarse voice. She could no longer turn away from her fear. Her arrogance and pride had been shattered to pieces amidst the ceaseless, one-sided violence and pain.

"K-kill me. Just… me…"

"Of course I'm going to do that." Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. "What, did you think I'd let you live?"

"But not yet," he continued, his voice dropping. "You know it too. The night is still long."

At those words, Jeniela's face twisted in absolute terror.
