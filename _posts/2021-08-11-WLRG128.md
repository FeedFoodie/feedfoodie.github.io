Lee Sungmin had only just left the North Gate, yet he found himself passing through it once more. Trailing behind him, the Crazy Heavenly Demon gazed at the desolate scenery and let out a hollow laugh.

"So this is Trabia."

Though he had never set foot here, the Crazy Heavenly Demon knew of Trabia's reputation as the foremost giant dao city in the North. The knowledge only made the sight of its transformation into a ruin all the more shocking.

"I've never been to the North, but I've heard plenty of stories," he muttered. "The Blood Heavenly Demon… that foolish Martial God."

His words drew a curious glance from Lee Sungmin, for they implied a familiarity with the man.

"Have you met the Blood Heavenly Demon before?"

"A long time ago," the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat. "It was almost ten years ago. Back when this one was still suffering from lunacy and that Martial God fellow was practicing his trash skills with Trabia as his base."

A bitter expression settled on his face as he recalled the Blood Heavenly Demon from that time, a young master who had shone with brilliant talent. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had seen him then and thought he would soon become a worthy rival.

"It seems you still have business in the North, don't you?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked.

Lee Sungmin gave a slow nod.

"In that case, this one would like to take care of some business too."

"Are you planning to meet the Blood Heavenly Demon?"

"Even if it was long ago, we had a connection. I've only heard rumors, but I know he's in Despair. Still, as his senior, I'd like to meet him at least once and have a chat."

Traveling with the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not seem necessary, but Lee Sungmin knew having him by his side would be a great help if a fight with Preskan broke out.

[You have nothing to worry about,] Void Monarch said. [This city is Jeniela's domain, and you have her attention. I don't know how strong that Lich named Preskan is, but he can't be as strong as Jeniela.]

Lee Sungmin glanced up at the sky. Preskan's familiar was still circling high above his head. There was no need for him to go looking.

He arranged to meet the Crazy Heavenly Demon again in three days at an inn near the North Gate, after which the man left immediately. Lee Sungmin had no idea how he planned to find the Blood Heavenly Demon in this vast land, but that was his problem to solve.

Lee Sungmin set off at a slow walk, turning into a deep and deserted alley. High above, Preskan's familiar shadowed his every move, never losing sight of him.

"Are you just going to watch?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking up.

In response, the familiar flapped its wings. He stared intently, as if he could see through the creature to Preskan himself.

"It seems we both want to meet. How about it? If you tell me where you are, I'll come find you right away."

At his words, the familiar swooped down. The crow, now hovering at his eye level, opened its beak. It clicked a few times before a sharp, metallic voice, not a caw, emerged.

"You insolent bastard," the crow spat fiercely. "What gives you the right to be so confident? You, who stole a heart that someone else dedicated their entire life to creating!"

"It's your fault for not guarding it properly, isn't it? And it's not like I wanted to have it."

"How can you say that!"

"I have a lot of questions for you," Lee Sungmin said, looking directly into the crow's eyes.

The crow's beak snapped shut.

"This heart in my body. I have no idea what it is. That's why I want to hear about it directly from you, the one who made it."

"Do you think I'll just tell you if you ask like that?"

"Let's meet and talk," Lee Sungmin retorted.

The crow laughed, flapping its wings. "Go outside the city gates. Then I will come find…"

"No. I'm not leaving," Lee Sungmin answered quickly. "I'm thinking of staying in this city for a few months. Or maybe I'll go to the Vampire Queen's Mansion."

"What…?"

"The Queen seems to have taken quite a liking to me. She told me I could stop by her mansion and use it anytime. Since I don't have a proper place to stay, going back to the Queen's mansion sounds pretty good."

"You bastard…!"

Lee Sungmin could feel Preskan's trembling rage through the familiar. The reaction gave him certainty. Preskan could not act freely inside Trabia. Outside the city, it seemed he could attack through Aine, but within its walls, he was powerless.

"Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master."

Preskan flinched.

"When this heart was put into my body, I saved Lloyd when he was dying on the Dungeon floor."

"You did something useless," Preskan spat, grinding his teeth.

"Anyway, the heart was embedded in my body back then… but Lloyd didn't know about it. What do you think would happen if I told him?"

"You crazy bastard!" Preskan shrieked at the taunt.

For Preskan, Lloyd discovering the Black Heart was a far more terrifying prospect than becoming Jeniela's enemy. As a long-time mage, Lloyd would recognize the heart's value and try to study it by any means necessary. If that happened, Preskan's lifelong research and ambition would be stolen by another mage.

"You don't want that, do you? If you tell me the truth about the heart, I won't have to say anything about it to Lloyd."

"You madman. Are you trying to make a deal with me?"

"No," Lee Sungmin shot back. "It's a threat."

The crow convulsed as if having a seizure. It stopped flapping its wings and fell to the ground, rolling in the dirt.

"Son of a bitch," the crow spat, its beak wide open. "Can you swear it? That you won't tell Lloyd, or any other mage, about the existence of that heart."

"I swear. It's not a difficult thing to do," Lee Sungmin replied immediately.

Glaring at him through the crow's eyes, Preskan spoke in a trembling voice. "What I wanted to embody through the Black Heart was 'predation' and 'evolution'."

"Explain it simply."

"…By consuming the hearts of other beings, you can acquire the knowledge of the heart's owner. When you consume a condensed mass of power like miracle herbs, you can make it your own immediately, without refinement."

Through those words, Lee Sungmin finally understood. He understood how Aine had used the Hundred Steps Divine Fist and her enhanced qi. He also understood how the Great Cycle Pill's power had accumulated directly in his qi center without any refinement.

"Through this, the heart continuously evolves. Stronger, more superior," Preskan continued. "My lovely daughter, Aine, was able to fully realize the heart's potential for evolution because her body was constructed around it. For you, however, it is impossible. You merely have a heart embedded in a human body."

Lee Sungmin recalled the image of Aine freely changing the shape of her limbs, sprouting tentacles and wings from her body. He had even tried to bring out tentacles himself just in case, but he could not. His body was completely different from hers.

"Anything else?"

"…The heart contains extreme potential. Its purpose is to evolve through repeated predation, and what I wanted to create through it was the ultimate life form, superior to all other beings. Even I, the creator of the heart, do not know what that ultimate state is. Potential is an ambiguous thing."

"So in the end, you don't really know either."

Unconcealable disappointment filled Lee Sungmin's voice. At his response, Preskan shouted, the crow's beak clicking rapidly.

"If I cut open your chest and check the heart, I'm sure I could find out more!"

"I have no intention of letting you do that."

"Just you try leaving Trabia. I will definitely…!"

"Do whatever you want."

Lee Sungmin ignored Preskan's venomous shouts. He raised his foot and stomped down on the crow's body.

"......"

With a sharp crack, the crow dissipated into raw magic power.

*In the end, I didn't get a proper answer.*

Predation, evolution, and potential. He paid little attention to the predation part, as the idea of eating a human heart was horrifying to someone who had lived his life as a human. He did, however, have some thoughts on evolution and potential.

He remembered when Aine first attacked him, and later, when he fought the Sword Phantom. In the fight with Aine, he had awakened to enhanced qi. In the fight with the Sword Phantom, he had reached a level with the spear that should have been impossible for him at the time.

*What if it had evolved to avoid death? Was it that the heart evolved my body because it was given the potential to overcome the situation?*

*Maybe that's it.*

Lee Sungmin thought with a bitter smile. Was he just being swayed by it in the end? He looked down at his own body. There was, however, one thing he could be proud of. His training in the Mental World. For those two thousand one hundred years, he had not received any help from the heart. Only his own mind had been there.

"Where are we going now?" Rubia asked in a tired voice, peeking out from where she hid in his arms.

"Let's go back to the Central District."

He had gone to the North and met the Crazy Heavenly Demon. After such an encounter, venturing out there again felt meaningless. He planned to return to the Central District and spend the rest of the year there.

{sep}

While Lee Sungmin headed to the Central District, the Crazy Heavenly Demon was grabbing and beating anyone who looked annoying. He started with a vagrant, then stormed into a noisy restaurant and a tavern to repeat the process.

"Where is the Blood Heavenly Demon?" he asked constantly.

It was a reckless method, but it was effective. After he had thoroughly beaten one man, the moment he grabbed another by the collar and raised his fist, the fellow immediately blurted out the location of the Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon.

In truth, it was not much of a secret. The Crazy Heavenly Demon kept his grip on the man who had talked and forced him to act as a guide, not forgetting to threaten to kill him if he was lying.

The place the man led him to was a rundown house on the outskirts of the Central District, the Blood Heavenly Demon's residence.

"......"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon wrinkled his nose at the strong smell of alcohol as he abruptly opened the door and stepped inside. From deeper within the house, a woman's excited moans mingled with a creaking sound. His face twisted as he strode into the room where the sounds originated.

It was quite a sight.

Liquor bottles were scattered across the floor, and a strange smoke filled the room. The Crazy Heavenly Demon glanced at the incense burner that was the source of the smoke.

"Drugs, alcohol, and even women. You've completely fallen apart."

At his mutter, Baek Museon's movements stopped. He reached a hand toward the moaning woman beneath him. The woman, who was staring up at Baek Museon with wide eyes, was crushed by his hand and died without even a scream.

"…I was wondering who it was…" Baek Museon let out a snickering laugh as he sat up.

He was completely naked but felt no shame. The Crazy Heavenly Demon looked at him with eyes twisted in annoyance. When he saw Baek Museon's left arm, severed at the shoulder, a small groan escaped his lips.

"What in the world are you doing?"

"It's been about… ten years, hasn't it? Are you planning to barge into someone's house and lecture them?"

"I asked what you are doing."

"Can't you tell by looking?" Baek Museon said, plopping down on the bed. He wiped the blood from his hands on the bedsheets and took a deep breath.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon clicked his tongue and reached a hand toward the closed window. The window burst open, and with a gesture from him, the drug-filled smoke that had filled the room flowed outside.

"You're going too far," Baek Museon said with a snicker. "It's nice to see you after ten years, but… aren't you being a bit too rude?"

"You, showing such a pathetic sight in front of this one, are far more so."

"This is my house, and even if it weren't, whatever I do is none of your business, Elder."

"Why have you become so broken?"

"You're the same as ten years ago. Never listening to others and only asking what you're curious about," Baek Museon muttered, reaching a hand toward the floor. A liquor bottle clattered as it floated up and flew into his grasp. "I'm just in Despair over my own talent and in Despair over reality. I started drinking, wondering what the point of doing more was, and when that wasn't fun enough, I tried drugs and held women."

"And that's why you've fallen to this state…!"

"I, a practitioner of the fist, lost my left arm. Isn't that enough reason to be in Despair?"

"You can live perfectly fine with one arm."

"It seems I can't," Baek Museon replied with a laugh. "If you meet the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, you'll understand what I'm saying, Elder."

"You crazy bastard!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon roared, thrusting his palm toward Baek Museon.

Baek Museon did not avoid the strike.

THUD

His body tumbled across the bed with a loud thud.

"Over just one defeat!"

"Isn't who I was defeated by the important part?"

"It seems you've forgotten how wide the world is while cooped up in a city in the North. Did you start reigning like a king in this place as small as a well and delude yourself into thinking you were truly the world number one?"

"Let's just say I learned my place," Baek Museon replied, clutching his severed arm. "You see, Elder, I had no intention of stopping in the North. I wanted to go south someday and truly challenge for the title of world number one. I was confident. Among martial artists of a similar age, there was no one better than me, but… I was defeated by a mere twenty-two-year-old girl. If it had been a simple defeat, I could have overcome it, but… it wasn't. The Little Heavenly Demon, that monster's absurd level of talent and strength was enough to shatter me, who considered myself a calamity."

"You pathetic fool," the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat, grinding his teeth. "The Little Heavenly Demon, you said that girl is twenty-two? You goddamn idiot. What the hell does age matter! I was just nearly killed by a twenty-three-year-old brat a little while ago!"

"…What are you talking about?" the Blood Heavenly Demon asked, his brow furrowed.
