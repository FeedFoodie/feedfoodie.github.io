He wanted to ignore the encroaching night and press onward, but the forest remained a dangerous place, guide or no guide. At night, the demonic aura thickened and the Yokai grew stronger. To traverse this place, so deeply stained with Demonic Energy, after sundown was nothing short of suicide.

Heeding this counsel, Lee Sungmin left the Evil Spirits and returned to the Crazy Heavenly Demon. The spirits did not depart, instead lingering near him until morning came. He had not slept deeply, but after performing a cycle of qi circulation, he felt not the slightest trace of fatigue.

[Well then, shall we go?]

After a light meal, the Void Monarch's cheerful voice boomed from the Magic Armor. He seemed to be in the best mood Lee Sungmin had heard since their union, having returned to the forest he once ruled only to find his terror still lingered, unforgotten after four hundred years.

*Does he feel like some great historical figure?*

Like a legend whose fame resounded long after his death. Lee Sungmin could not completely empathize with the Void Monarch's delight, but he had no desire to pour cold water on his cheerful mood.

"What do you think happened to our pursuers?"

[Keke! I can't say for sure, but they must have suffered quite a bit during the night. Maybe a few of them have already gone mad, or even died. Fear is easily contagious, and this forest drives people mad with it.]

The Crazy Heavenly Demon could not hear the reply, but the Void Monarch was in such a good mood that he answered anyway.

"Perhaps they gave up the chase and went back?" Lee Sungmin asked aloud.

"It's possible," the Crazy Heavenly Demon replied. "They're people of the Noble Clans. They're all connected by blood, so they can't help but be sensitive about each other's lives. If they suffer damage they can't handle, they'll retreat on their own."

He glanced back as he spoke, but only dense trees were visible. There was no sign of anyone else, yet the Crazy Heavenly Demon still frowned, thinking of the pursuers who were likely out there somewhere.

"Assuming they don't make a foolish choice," he added.

Lee Sungmin did not think they would either.

As he began to move, the Evil Spirits stirred around him. The mornings and days in the forest were perpetually dim, and within that faint darkness, the spirits existed as hazy figures. *This way... this... way...* Dozens of them spoke inside his mind.

"Hah, really... You have quite a lot of talents. Are you commanding Ghost Spirits now too?"

"It just sort of happened..."

Since he had not introduced the Crazy Heavenly Demon to the Void Monarch, Lee Sungmin vaguely explained that he had learned the skill a long time ago. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was surprised, but he did not doubt him.

He walked the path the Evil Spirits led. Once he started following, they no longer spoke to him, and soon a different landscape unfolded. The familiar rock with its carved mark, which he had seen over and over, was finally gone.

*By the way, where are we going?*

[You're asking now of all times?] the Void Monarch scoffed. [We're going to collect this Elder's treasure, of course. Shouldn't we grab that first before we leave the forest?]

That was exactly what Lee Sungmin wanted. He hoped to finish his business quickly, leave this forest, and find the tribe the Crazy Heavenly Demon had met. Their location depended on the old man's memory, but he remembered the events of that time quite clearly. He had met their tribe near the Yokai city of Urmuri.

How long had they been traveling? The Bewitching Forest was vast, and even without getting lost, their progress was slow. The Evil Spirits did not move very quickly, and matching their pace meant their own travel speed inevitably slowed to a crawl.

Two days passed since they began following the spirits. It was frustrating, but urging them on did nothing to increase their speed. As they walked on at that same slow pace, Lee Sungmin came to a halt. He was not the only one. The first to stop were the Evil Spirits leading the way.

*Uu... uuuuu...*

*Ah... euuuu...*

The spirits trembled. They had been moving with them for two days, and this was the first time anything like this had happened. They had killed some insignificant Yokai along the way, but even then, the Evil Spirits had never once reacted.

Now was different. They trembled, their hazy bodies beginning to dissipate like smoke. A presence approached slowly, like a drizzle soaking one's clothes, and in an instant, that drizzle became a fierce downpour.

PAAAAAH!

The Evil Spirits' bodies scattered before the presence that suddenly washed over them. The bushes and trees trembled. The forest, itself a source of fear stained by Demonic Energy, fell utterly silent.

"How bizarre."

At some point, he was simply standing there, not far from Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon. Lee Sungmin stared intently at the man who had appeared. He had red hair and a haggard appearance that did not match the presence he was emitting. He met Lee Sungmin's gaze with deeply shadowed eyes.

[Don't tell me... Red Ghost?]

The Void Monarch spoke, his tone laced with disbelief. *Red Ghost.* Lee Sungmin muttered the name under his breath. A King of Monsters belonging to Predator. One of the monsters who reigned at the top when the Void Monarch was alive. That was the Red Ghost he knew of, though he had only ever heard stories and had never met him in person.

He had heard a few days ago that Red Ghost was in this forest.

The man he saw now, however, looked absurdly weak compared to the Vampire Queen Jeniela he had seen in North Trabia. He looked even weaker than Mad Wolf, who was said to be one of the most powerful Lycanthropes. The current Lee Sungmin could probably kill this Red Ghost, though it might be a little tricky.

He could not understand it. Red Ghost was said to be at the pinnacle of Predator. If that was true three hundred years ago, shouldn't he have grown even stronger over time? Seeing a Red Ghost who was incomparably weaker than Jeniela, who was at the same level, Lee Sungmin could not help but feel a surge of confusion.

[He's even weaker than he was three hundred years ago... What in the world happened?]

"The Void Monarch?" Red Ghost's lips parted. He blinked and stared at Lee Sungmin, who slowly nodded at his gaze. Mad Wolf and Jeniela had also felt the Void Monarch's presence residing within his body. The inhuman could sense, in one way or another, the fragment of the inhuman that he possessed.

"I thought you died four hundred years ago... Well, I guess it's not that surprising. Yokai don't die easily, even when you kill them."

Red Ghost chuckled and muttered. He too was a Yokai, so he knew that fact well. The fundamental difference between them, however, was that unlike the Void Monarch, who was born with fear as his mother, Red Ghost was once a human who had transformed into a Yokai.

"Why have you come here now, after four hundred years? From the looks of it, you haven't even completely taken over that human's body..."

[I came to take what is mine.]

Demonic Energy surged as the Void Monarch's voice reached Red Ghost, who chuckled as he listened.

"You're here early. It's only been four hundred years, after all."

[What are you doing here?]

"Because my body is in this state."

To the Crazy Heavenly Demon, it looked as if Red Ghost was standing opposite Lee Sungmin and chattering to himself, but he let it go. He already knew that Lee Sungmin was different from ordinary people, and now that he was even commanding Evil Spirits, there was nothing more to find strange.

"My heart was torn out."

Red Ghost lifted the robe he was wearing. A gaping hole was torn in his left chest. The place where his heart should have been was empty, revealing the fabric of his robe on the other side. He stuck his hand into the hole in his chest and laughed gutturally.

"It was taken by the fox bitch who rules Urmuri now. I'm barely maintaining my existence... Hehe! My power has weakened absurdly."

[So. Did you come here to scavenge this Elder's Demonic Energy?]

"That's right," Red Ghost replied. There was no point in denying it out of pride, not after coming this far. With his heart torn out by Urmuri's Nine-Tailed Fox, he had become absurdly weak compared to his prime. "This forest is full of Demonic Energy. I was planning to recuperate here and recover my body. I intended to benefit by borrowing the fear you left behind."

[How pathetic you've become.]

"But I'm not dead," Red Ghost's eyes gleamed as he said it. "Unlike you."

[Hahaha! I have nothing to say to that. Yes, I couldn't even preserve my body and am practically no different from being dead,] the Void Monarch laughed loudly in reply. [If you want to recover your strength by clinging to life like that, then be my guest. My business is not with you.]

Red Ghost nodded as he heard those words. He glanced at Lee Sungmin's face and muttered, "Standing on the border between human and Yokai. Not even a half-breed... What a strange one."

Leaving that mutter behind, he vanished from the spot. Red Ghost was gone, but the scattered Evil Spirits did not return.

[They're not needed now,] the Void Monarch said, settling back into the Magic Armor.

It was just as he had said. There was no longer any need for a guide.

A large mansion was visible not far away. It was on the verge of collapse after so many years, but the structure still retained its shape. Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon approached it. The main gate was smashed, and the garden was overgrown with weeds.

[It was a fine sight in the old days. I had many slaves to command, you know. I even took great care to cultivate a magnificent garden,] the Void Monarch muttered regretfully.

They passed through the gate and entered the garden. Rubia had already emerged from Lee Sungmin's arms and was looking around with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, her expression full of wonder.

[This should be the place.]

At the Void Monarch's words, Lee Sungmin's steps halted. They had not even entered the mansion building yet. Nearby was a hollow depression where a pond might have once been. The Void Monarch's Demonic Energy erupted from the Magic Armor, swelling for a moment before it burst with a POP. The will-o'-the-wisps that scattered in all directions moved in perfect unison, darting busily around the weed-choked garden and the crumbling mansion. Before long, an unknown current began to swirl around them.

"......"

The space before Lee Sungmin's eyes split open with a crack. The entire area where the mansion existed seemed to tear apart, revealing a black fissure.

[Go closer,] the Void Monarch urged.

Lee Sungmin approached the fissure. On the outside, it looked merely pitch-black, but when the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy imbued his eyes, the fissure appeared completely different. It was a dark, grimy door.

[Place your hand on it.]

Following the command, Lee Sungmin raised his hand and placed it on the door. The Void Monarch's Demonic Energy within his body hummed and seeped into the surface. KIGIGIGIK...! The door began to open with an unpleasant grinding sound. The Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia watched from a short distance away. To them, it looked as if Lee Sungmin was placing his hand on empty air.

This was the work of a spell carved into the very land the mansion stood on. Four hundred years ago, to prevent others from stealing the treasures he had collected, the Void Monarch had a spell embedded into the land itself. While other sorcerers had cast it, the Void Monarch's soul was the key required to activate the inscription.

The door opened completely. Lee Sungmin peered inside, and as he did, the Void Monarch laughed heartily inside his mind.

[Go on in and take a look.]

*Is it okay to go in?*

[There's no problem. You are connected to this Elder spiritually.]

Hearing those words, Lee Sungmin cautiously stepped through the door. Rubia let out a surprised cry. From her perspective, it looked as if his foot had vanished into thin air. Leaving the astonished Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia behind, Lee Sungmin pushed his entire body through the doorway.

The Void Monarch's treasure.

The place where it was stored was an isolated space. The pure white world resembled the Mental World Lee Sungmin had experienced through Denir, but it was not completely empty. Large, unadorned chests were placed everywhere. Upon seeing them, the Void Monarch spoke with pride.

[Go and open one.]

Lee Sungmin approached a nearby chest. When he opened it, a single, fist-sized jewel lay inside the spacious container.

"...What is this?"

[First time seeing one?] the Void Monarch replied, as if he had been waiting for the question.

[It's a Dragon's Heart.]
