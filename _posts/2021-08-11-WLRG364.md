Before venturing to the Sleeping Forest, Lee Sungmin settled himself cross-legged in the center of the Fairy Forest. The lake where Osla resided shimmered directly behind him. He closed his eyes, ignoring the curious gazes of the Fairy Queen, her subjects, and Teresa as they glanced his way.

"What happened?" Teresa asked.

"Nothing much," the Black Dragon Knight replied. Devouring a person was hardly 'nothing much', but he was perceptive enough to know Teresa did not need to hear about it. Though she understood that Lee Sungmin's body was that of a Yokai, he doubted her understanding would extend to cannibalism.

Osla, however, needed no explanation. She quietly fluttered her wings and drifted toward him, her hand coming to rest gently on his shoulder.

[I could have helped.]

*I just didn't want to create unnecessary problems,* he thought back.

[What do you mean?]

Lee Sungmin explained his worries to her, and in response, Osla puffed out her cheeks.

[…Thank you for being considerate.]

*What would happen if the Queen were to invade the Fairy Forest?*

[Are you asking if I can restrain her?]

"......"

[…Hmm…] Osla flapped her wings, pondering for a moment. […I probably can't kill the Queen. Didn't I tell you? She is a being of this world, yet she has surpassed the power of a transcendent. If we're only talking about our existential ranks, mine is higher… but it's not high enough to suppress her.]

*Then?*

[It means we can't kill each other. It's impossible for me to die in this forest, and it would be just as impossible for me to kill the Queen.]

*But, how did you know?*

[Your energy has changed a little. Your Demonic Energy has grown more vicious. It would be best not to eat too much. Even if you don't have extreme rampages like before, adding cannibalism to a Yokai's body is not a good thing.]

Lee Sungmin nodded, taking Osla's advice to heart. He focused his mind, delving into the memories he had consumed from the Blood Demon. They were the recollections of a martial artist, stripped of all personal history, leaving only the pure martial theory he possessed.

In truth, almost none of it was useful. Lee Sungmin already held the martial theory of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a warrior who had reached a far higher realm than the Blood Demon. Though he had not fully mastered it, the simple fact that he knew it was what mattered. It meant there was still room for him to grow.

*I need to achieve Body Reformation.*

He had to reach that level at the very least. At his current skill, the Blood Demon represented his absolute limit. He had managed to kill the man with his final technique, the pinnacle of martial arts, only to collapse from exhaustion the moment it was over.

Could he kill Jeniela with that same technique? He had no confidence he could. There was no fallback if he failed. The pinnacle of martial arts was a sure-kill technique, one that had to destroy its opponent without fail.

*What more do I have to do?*

The path forward was shrouded in fog. By finally using the pinnacle of martial arts, he had succeeded in achieving Spear Unification, but applying his other insights to his body remained nothing more than theory. He could only imbue his insights by following the rigid forms of his techniques. To unleash them freely, without relying on forms, was a realm he had yet to grasp. Should he start by abandoning the forms themselves? How long would that even take?

There were three martial arts that Lee Sungmin could execute properly. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Among them, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art was an enhanced qi art with almost no set forms, but the other two were defined by them.

He recalled the way the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman fought, using only the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It could not even be called a technique. Every single one of the Chairman's gestures was worthy of being called a divine art.

*There's not enough time.*

There was far too little. Could he undergo Denir's training again? No, that was impossible. Denir had made it clear that his training could only be received once. Should he return to Mount Mushii, then? He could not isolate himself there when he had no idea what would happen next. Abandoning forms, grasping insights, and advancing to a higher realm… these were things that might be impossible even if he dedicated his entire life to them.

[Don't be crushed by it.]

The voice of the Void Monarch broke through his spiraling thoughts.

[Focus on what you can do first. Don't be crushed by things you can't do yet, things you don't even know if you'll ever be able to do. Jeniela is a formidable enemy, but it's not like you have no chance of winning. Fortunately, you have people around you who can help.]

Right. A throbbing headache pulsed in his temples as Lee Sungmin lifted his head. He took out the mask tucked inside his robes and looked down at it. If only the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman were alive. The thought crossed his mind for a moment before he shook his head to dismiss the meaningless wish.

It was more comforting to review his fight with the Blood Demon. Besides, thinking about going to the Sleeping Forest on the Fairy Horse right now was just as theoretical as his martial arts. No signal had come from the teams sent to Cheneron Great Lake and the Dahar Ruins. They should have arrived by now, which meant the Spirit King had not yet made his Advent.

By the time he finished his review, the night had already passed. Sleep would not come. His mind was a mess, and though he did not want to be crushed by the pressure, simply deciding not to be was not so easy.

Lee Sungmin quietly got to his feet. He considered swinging his spear but did not want to wake the others.

[Why don't you go check out the Sleeping Forest?] the Void Monarch advised. [You should be able to ride the Fairy Horse now, and you can't sleep anyway. If the Spirit Queen had made her Advent, that Lloyd fellow would have notified you through the bracelet. There's been no signal, has there? It would be good to go take a look.]

*Right.*

It was a long way to go for a walk just because he could not sleep, but the journey would be instantaneous on the Fairy Horse. Lee Sungmin mounted the creature and focused his thoughts on the Sleeping Forest, picturing the place where he had first met the Void Monarch. The Fairy Horse snorted and shook its head.

*What is it?*

"It can't go there," Osla said in a sleepy voice from where she lay on the lake's surface. "Just because it's a Fairy Horse doesn't mean it can go everywhere. It can only go to places that exist in this world. I don't know where you were thinking of, but the place you want to go is somewhere the Fairy Horse can't reach. It's a place that doesn't exist in this world."

"That can't be."

Lee Sungmin looked down at the Fairy Horse in disbelief. He once again pictured the clearing where he met the Void Monarch, but the horse only shook its head again. He then recalled his first journey to the Sleeping Forest. Without the help of the clan that protected it, breaking through the path that blocked the forest was impossible. Could the forest's barrier be so powerful that not even the Fairy Horse could overcome it?

"Where are you going?"

Rubia approached, rubbing her sleepy eyes. Thinking this was perfect timing, Lee Sungmin took her hand. As a surprised expression crossed her face, he raised an index finger to his lips.

"Get on."

An explanation could wait. Careful not to wake the others, Lee Sungmin leaped away with Rubia on the Fairy Horse. They arrived near the Sleeping Forest, at the entrance to the clan's village.

"This place is…" Rubia's eyes widened as she muttered.

Lee Sungmin dismounted the Fairy Horse and turned to her. "The Sleeping Forest."

"Why did we come here?"

"I wanted to see the state of the forest before the Spirit Queen makes her Advent," he explained. "I also have a few questions."

Come to think of it, the Sleeping Forest had always been a place shrouded in mystery. The reason Envirus had come here, for instance. Lee Sungmin had never found out why. Envirus had been trying to confirm something within its depths. His own journey here had been a coincidence brought about by fate, and meeting the Void Monarch had been a coincidence as well. The Slaughter Predator had said that because Lee Sungmin was such a weak existence before his fighting spirit awakened, he had needed the Void Monarch's help.

"Why did Envirus come to this forest?"

"…That… I don't know either," Rubia replied, pouting her lips. "My master didn't tell me everything. All I heard from him was to wait here… and then travel with you, Lee Sungmin."

Envirus had attached Rubia to him to monitor his condition. Having glimpsed a bit of fate, Envirus had sensed to some extent that Lee Sungmin was a dangerous existence. It was not that he knew Lee Sungmin was the Slaughter Predator. What Envirus had been wary of was the possibility that Lee Sungmin's power as a Yokai would grow stronger and he would be devoured by the Void Monarch.

"You don't know anything else?"

"My master tried to go to the very end of the forest, but he failed."

"Failed?"

"......" Rubia nodded. "Actually, I'm not sure. He just kept going and then… decided it was no good, and came back. He had a very sad expression on his face."

Envirus was dead.

*What's in there?*

[I do not know,] the Void Monarch answered. [I believe I told you before. This forest is a place where the souls of ancient monsters are bound.]

He remembered the story from when they first met. The clan that managed this forest maintained its Sealing just by existing. The Void Monarch had said that because of the Sealing, the monsters whose souls were bound to the forest could not awaken.

*Is Kazuya still alive?*

Lee Sungmin thought of the village chief he had met long ago. Kazuya led the clan that protected the forest, pursuing their long-cherished wish, yet, Kazuya himself did not know what that wish was. When they first met, Lee Sungmin had asked the Void Monarch if he was the cherished wish of the forest clan. At the time, the Monarch had replied that he was merely a being in the middle of the path and had nothing to do with it.

Lee Sungmin slowly entered the village. Morning was approaching, but an unnatural silence hung in the air. Even ten years ago, it had been a small place with few people, but the village now was even more desolate than before. He glanced around, his senses on alert.

There was a smell.

Lee Sungmin approached a nearby house and gave the closed door a light push. It swung open easily, unlocked.

"......"

Behind him, Rubia let out a pained sound. The foul stench that washed over them made Lee Sungmin wrinkle his nose. The source of the smell was sprawled in the hallway just beyond the door, a corpse. When he went closer to check, he saw it was not the body of someone who had been killed.

*An epidemic…?*

The corpse had been dead for quite some time and was severely decomposed by natural causes. Lee Sungmin stepped past the body and went inside. There was a possibility of an epidemic, but for him, who had achieved Myriad Poison Immunity, it did not matter. It was not just the corpse in the hallway. There were more bodies in the same state inside the rooms.

The entire village was like this.

[This is troublesome,] the Void Monarch muttered. [It can't be helped that these bastards are dying off. They've become as weak as they could be after generations of inbreeding. It seems they tried to overcome it by learning martial arts, but learning martial arts with such frail bodies doesn't guarantee a long life, does it? Their lifespans were too short to begin with.]

That kind of illness could not be cured even with an Elixir, the panacea of all potions.

[Their deaths can't be helped, but if all the villagers die, the forest's Sealing won't be maintained.]

*We don't even know what's being Sealed.*

[We know it's ancient monsters. Doesn't it feel ominous just looking at it? It's possible they're one of The End.]

There might be survivors. Lee Sungmin headed for Kazuya's house. The lack of light in the mansion made him uneasy, but when he opened his senses, he detected a presence inside. He slowly opened the door and entered.

"Who is it?" a weak voice called out. "The Little Heavenly Demon. Is that you?"

Lee Sungmin's expression stiffened at the question.
