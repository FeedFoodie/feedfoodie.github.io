-ZZZING!

An intense headache struck Lee Sungmin, sharp and blinding. Strength drained from his legs, which staggered violently beneath him. He gasped for air, barely managing to keep himself from collapsing to the ground. The Black Dragon Knight and Scarlet, who followed just behind, watched him with puzzled expressions.

At some point, his breathing had grown ragged. Though Lee Sungmin himself was unaware, his Golden Demonic Eyes were glowing with an intensity he had not felt in a long time. The headache was soon joined by a sharp pain in his chest, an ache born from the frantic, rapid beating of his heart. It pounded against his ribs as if it would shatter them and burst free. He could feel the organ swelling and contracting within him, a frantic rhythm of its own.

A chorus of various sounds flooded his ears. Voices of different pitches mixed and echoed, a chaotic symphony of noise. The only discernible sound within the cacophony was the giggling laughter of a young child.

*What is this,* Lee Sungmin groaned, clutching his head. *The Void Monarch's voice… it sounds so distant.*

His ears were filled with the noise and the child's laughter, and the scenery around him began to waver. It was collapsing.

Giggle, gigigiggle...

The laughter grew clearer. The distorted world no longer showed a rose garden but something else entirely. Lanterns were rising into the sky. It was a scene he had witnessed before. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he stared at the sight unfolding before him.

The streets of Urmuri came into view.

[Aaaaaah!]

-KRAAACK!

A thunderbolt of sound erupted inside his head.

The moment he gasped, the streets of Urmuri vanished, replaced once more by the rose garden that should have been there all along. The headache subsided, and Lee Sungmin blinked his eyes, trying to clear his vision.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was holding his wrist. Startled by a sudden warmth on his shoulder, he looked up to see Scarlet gazing down at him, her expression a mixture of alarm and concern. The energy flowing through his wrist belonged to the Chairman, and the loud shout he had heard in his head had been the Void Monarch's voice.

[Are you back to your senses?] the Void Monarch asked urgently. It was he who had shattered the illusion with a Lion's Roar.

It was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman who had suppressed the Demonic Energy that threatened to erupt from Lee Sungmin's uncontrollably trembling body. At the same time, Scarlet was casting mental purification and stabilization magic, anchoring his consciousness.

"Wha, what… what was that?" Lee Sungmin stammered.

Jeniela, standing at a distance, was not looking at him. Her gaze was fixed on Aine. Unlike Lee Sungmin, who had suddenly convulsed, Aine showed no signs of agitation. Instead, even amidst the agony of the awls tearing through her body, she was watching Lee Sungmin with an unreadable expression.

The difference.

Jeniela focused on that. She had heard from Kim Jonghyun what had transpired in Urmuri and knew that Lee Sungmin possessed a Black Heart, just like Aine. She also knew that the vast Demonic Energy of Urmuri, gathered by Arbes, had flowed into both of them. In that sense, the one closest to becoming the king of Predators, the Slaughter Predator whose appearance she had foreseen and fervently prayed for, could be considered Lee Sungmin, not Aine.

In fact, based on conditions alone, Lee Sungmin was currently closer to being the Slaughter Predator than Aine was. He possessed the power of a Dragon, and his body had mutated to become something akin to a Yokai by absorbing the Demonic Energy of both the Void Monarch and Urmuri. His martial arts skills were outstanding, having already reached the Transcendent Realm. While he showed little aptitude for magic, his Black Heart meant he would likely show excellent progress if he ever set his mind to it.

That was, of course, if one considered only those conditions. Jeniela knew Lee Sungmin's disposition well. Despite having that kind of body, he desperately wanted to remain human. Such an attitude fueled Jeniela's sadism, but from the perspective of creating a Slaughter Predator, it presented many difficulties. In reality, despite possessing a Black Heart, Lee Sungmin had never once engaged in predation worthy of the name. He was the complete opposite of Aine, who, starved by her appetite, had committed indiscriminate slaughter and devoured everything in her path.

Besides that, there was a decisive reason Jeniela did not interfere much with Lee Sungmin. In the future she had seen, she had never once witnessed a vision of herself together with him. Therefore, even though she thought Lee Sungmin was close to being the Slaughter Predator, she did not try to interfere with him directly.

*However…*

She had come to realize there was a troublesome point. In the future she had seen this time, she had witnessed herself confronting the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman in the Snowfield, Ju Won's domain. She had also seen the Black Dragon Knight and the Crimson Archmage, Scarlet Reshir. She had not seen Lee Sungmin.

Still,, Lee Sungmin was here.

Her Eye of Futuresight only showed her fragmented visions, but if the situation and conditions were right, it was never wrong. Because of that belief, she had spent hundreds of years laying the groundwork to meet the conditions for the most distant future she had ever seen. The fact that it was never wrong was what made this so troublesome. Meeting Lee Sungmin in the Snowfield today was shaking the conviction Jeniela had held so firmly in her Eye of Futuresight.

No, the Eye of Futuresight was not wrong. Jeniela glared at Lee Sungmin. With her All-Seeing Demonic Eye, she could no longer see through his existence. She could no longer be certain about the being named Lee Sungmin she had seen through last time. She could no longer be certain about the future she had seen until now. What if the futures her Eye of Futuresight had shown her had all been presented with Lee Sungmin neatly cut out, just like this time? If that were the case, then her actions of intentionally not interfering with him had been based on deceit.

Who on earth had deceived her?

"Your Demonic Energy went rampant," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, letting go of Lee Sungmin's wrist. "You bother this one with all sorts of things. Why did it go rampant?"

"I… don't know."

[It was resonance,] the Void Monarch answered. [The Demonic Energy you possess and that girl's Demonic Energy resonated with each other.]

*What does that mean…?*

[Back then, in Urmuri, a considerable amount of the land's Demonic Energy flowed into that girl as well, and from the looks of it, she's completely inhuman, unlike you. Since her mind was inhuman to begin with, she didn't almost lose her sanity from the resonance like you did.]

At those words, Lee Sungmin glanced at Aine. This time, however, their Demonic Energy did not resonate as it had before. His heart was still beating fast, but there was no pain.

*You… didn't hear it?*

[Hear what?]

*A child's laughter.*

Lee Sungmin steadied his panting breaths. He had seen the streets of Urmuri. He had heard the laughter of a child who seemed to be filled with glee.

*What in the world happened to me? What was that laughter?*

The Void Monarch, however, replied that he had heard nothing.

Lee Sungmin explained to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman what had just happened. At his explanation, the Chairman's eyes, visible through the holes of his mask, narrowed.

[You are quite like an onion.]

"Pardon?"

[Why does something new keep coming out the more I peel you?]

"Aren't you coming?" Jeniela finally asked after they had stood in place for a long while.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman shrugged and turned to follow. Lee Sungmin gave Aine one last look. It was a mystery why she was here in such a miserable state.

"You keep a strange pet," the Chairman remarked.

"Isn't she cute?"

"Isn't the punishment a bit too excessive?"

"She's sturdy, so it's fine," Jeniela answered without looking back.

The place she led them to was a dining hall with a long table in the center, large enough to seat dozens of people. A massive spread of dozens of different dishes was laid out on the table. No one in the group, however, felt any appetite looking at the splendid meal. This was the mansion of a Vampire. That red wine could be blood, and they had no way of knowing if the meat floating in the red stew was human flesh.

"Please, sit."

"We didn't come here for a meal," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman began. While Lee Sungmin, Scarlet, and the Black Dragon Knight were quite intimidated by Jeniela, the Chairman was not. He held his head high, facing the Vampire Queen who had displayed immense power and an immortality that allowed her to survive even decapitation.

Jeniela chuckled and clapped her hands together. "There's no need to be so on edge."

The clap was a signal. Doors all around the dining hall opened at once. Scarlet flinched in surprise and looked around, but Lee Sungmin, the Black Dragon Knight, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman remained still, their glares fixed on Jeniela.

Entering through the open doors were boys and girls with pale skin.

"Are they also those pets of yours?" the Chairman asked.

"The mansion is so large that we're short on hands."

"They're human," Lee Sungmin muttered. He could not sense the feeling of a Vampire from the boys and girls dressed in maid uniforms.

Jeniela, seated at the head of the table, swirled a wine glass filled with a red liquid. "I don't just mass-produce Vampires recklessly, you know. It's inefficient. Besides, isn't it a waste to use a Vampire, created with my precious blood, as a mere dining servant?" She giggled. "Now, now. Don't be so tense."

The maids acted on their own. They pulled out chairs according to the number of people and filled the empty glasses with drinks. They moved utensils like knives and forks to easily reachable places and carved the large chunks of meat into bite-sized pieces.

"I didn't use human flesh. The same goes for blood. So please, don't worry so much…"

"I said we did not come here for a meal," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman repeated.

-CRACKLE!

Sparks of electricity flew from around his shoulders. A terrifyingly powerful pressure emanated from him, instantly enveloping the entire dining hall and pressing down on everything within it. The chandelier shattered, and the floor vibrated. The maids collapsed to the ground.

Amidst the vibrating space, Jeniela's body did not tremble in the slightest. She slowly swirled her wine glass and asked, "Do you understand the meaning of refusing my goodwill?"

"I suppose you didn't see this one's actions in your future?"

"That's right. The future I saw was fragmented. It ended with meeting you, you cutting off my head, and then coming here."

"Then what happens next must be things you do not know."

"Do you think that will fluster me?" Jeniela's voice rose with each word. "Do you think your existence can negate the future I have seen? Do you think you can ruin the path to the future I am destined to reach? You can't change anything. What you're doing right now is nothing more than a tiny happening to me and to the future I'm heading towards. Well, I suppose it wouldn't be so bad to enjoy this inconsequential happening together…"

Her gaze flickered toward Lee Sungmin.

"Let's stop," she said. "I'm fond of this mansion, and I cherish my garden. I don't want to destroy the home I've lived in for hundreds of years over a pointless commotion."

"So destroying this mansion would be enough to anger you."

"That will never happen," Jeniela bared her teeth and smiled. "Let's stop provoking each other with meaningless actions. I know very well why you've come here, and I also know what I must do. Of course, whether you choose to believe my words is up to you."

"Does that mean you'll tell us willingly?"

"That's the plan. I will speak the truth, but I won't make any vows. There's no reason for me to do so."

"……"

Jeniela set down her wine glass. "An omen will appear soon enough anyway. The first one, in the South. Black Dragon Knight, you made a mistake. No… perhaps it was inevitable from the start? You poor half-human, half-dragon. Unable to take initiative in anything, you were used like a puppet by the Martial God and Heaven Beyond Heaven."

The Black Dragon Knight was left speechless by her smirking words. He stammered out a reply. "…A mistake…?"

"You shouldn't have just stopped Kim Jonghyun. You should have killed him." After laughing for a good while, Jeniela brought the wine glass to her lips. She drank about half of the red liquid before continuing in a cheerful voice. "Thanks to your intended mistake, Kim Jonghyun will become the first calamity in the South. He is already moving as a calamity. Quietly, and soon, explosively."

"…Is Kim Jonghyun The End?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Jeniela laughed out loud at his question. "Someone like Kim Jonghyun?"

Her laughter abruptly stopped.

"Didn't I tell you?" she said, raising a single finger. "He is the first."

Her smile, revealing her fangs, was utterly chilling.

"The first of the calamities leading to The End."
