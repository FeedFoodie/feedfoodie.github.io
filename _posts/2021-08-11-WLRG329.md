"Weren't you dead?"

The question hung in the air, born from eyes wide with a surprise ten years in the making. Celgerus stared at him, her reaction precisely what he had anticipated. Lee Sungmin could only manage an awkward smile.

"I didn't die."

As Celgerus's curiosity was plain to see, Lee Sungmin gave her a brief account of his ordeal. Nebul stood silently to the side, his attention fixed on every word. When Lee Sungmin mentioned the Sealing, Celgerus clicked her tongue and shook her head.

"Goodness… I've met all sorts of people and experienced all kinds of situations in this line of work, but I've never seen anyone with a life as eventful as yours."

"I think so too," Lee Sungmin admitted.

"And you've gotten more shameless than before."

He found he had to agree with that, at least to some extent.

"How have you been, Celgerus?"

"I've been well. Thanks to you, I became the chief's apprentice," she said, her tone shifting. "So… did you contact me just to report that you're alive?"

"Well…"

Lee Sungmin trailed off, and the shift in Celgerus's expression was immediate. Her gaze dropped to his empty hands, and a tremor ran through her shoulders.

"Don't tell me."

"It was gone when I woke up."

"Oh my god…" Celgerus buried her face in her hands. That spear had been a masterpiece, forged without reservation from a Dragon's bones, teeth, and scales. It was a priceless, one-of-a-kind treasure personally crafted by the chief, the greatest of all Dwarf blacksmiths, and he had simply lost it.

"...Just what were you doing to lose it?"

"I'm not sure."

"......"

A string of profanities rose in Celgerus's throat, but she took a deep breath and swallowed them down. She wanted to curse him, but Lee Sungmin had provided the materials himself. What could she possibly say when the owner was the one who had lost his own treasure?

"By any chance, are there any materials left?"

When he had departed the Dwarf Village all those years ago, Lee Sungmin had entrusted his remaining Dragon materials to Celgerus and the chief, Mackendor. He had seen no use in keeping them, certain he would never need another spear or set of armor.

"There are some, yes," Celgerus grumbled. "Your body hasn't changed in ten years, has it? You haven't gotten taller or your arms longer?"

"No."

"Anything specific you want to request?"

"No."

"So easy to please," she muttered. "Just saying any spear will do. I'll try to make it better than the one from ten years ago."

With that, Celgerus turned away, still grumbling. The transaction was not yet over. She rummaged through something behind her before returning with a different spear.

"Use this as a temporary weapon until the new one is finished. It's not shoddily made, so it should be serviceable."

Nebul accepted the weapon from her and passed it to Lee Sungmin, who gave it a light swing and nodded.

"Thank you."

The transaction with Celgerus was complete. Normally, this was the point where Lee Sungmin would pay the brokerage fee and Nebul would simply disappear, but Nebul remained.

"Oh."

A flustered expression crossed Nebul's face. He staggered, pressing a hand to his temple, and then looked at Lee Sungmin with a troubled gaze.

"...Excuse me for a moment."

"......"

Before Lee Sungmin could even form a question, Nebul's body convulsed violently. The very space around him seemed to writhe. The darkness of the night, untouched by the campfire's glow, twisted and swallowed his form whole. A moment later, the man standing there was not Nebul. Clad in a black cloak, it was a figure Lee Sungmin had never seen before.

*Human? No.* A deep, inexplicable unease washed over Lee Sungmin. He couldn't even be sure if the being was truly alive, yet it was nothing like the feeling of facing an Undead.

The stranger removed the fedora from his head and bowed politely.

"It's a pleasure to meet you. My name is Laplace. I manage Erebrisa."

"...What business do you have with me?" Lee Sungmin asked, his guard raised. He sensed no hostility from the man, but he knew this was not someone he could afford to relax around.

"Lee Sungmin," Laplace began. "You became a member of Erebrisa through the recommendation of Mushii, the Goddess of Penance and Ordeals, did you not?"

Lee Sungmin did not answer. That felt like a lifetime ago.

"To be honest, there were several times I considered revoking your membership."

"...Why?"

"Because I thought your destiny was to be the Slaughter Predator," Laplace said with a shrug.

Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed. Thinking back, it seemed that Mushii, Denir, and even Osla had known all along that he was the Slaughter Predator. Why then had they shown him such favor? If what the Slaughter Predator had claimed was true, transcendental beings like them should have accepted the world's fated End, yet the Demonic Spirit had rejected him, and Mushii and Denir had followed suit, betraying that very fate.

"However," Laplace continued, "I believed we should wait and see, and now, it seems that was the right decision. You awakened as the Slaughter Predator, but you ultimately escaped that destiny."

In this world, the greatest variables the Demonic Spirit had created were Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon. By reincarnating the once-insignificant Lee Sungmin, the Demonic Spirit had focused a great deal of Destiny Force upon him. This not only ensured his awakening as the Slaughter Predator but also introduced countless other variables into the process. Gaining the Black Heart. Absorbing Urmuri's Demonic Energy. Devouring the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

Osla had told him, *I am favorable to you.* He still did not understand what she meant. Did her favor extend to him as the Slaughter Predator? No, that couldn't be it. Both Mushii and Osla had spoken to him of 'choice'.

*Osla is neutral.*

Depending on the choice he made, Osla's actions would have changed. Lee Sungmin had not wanted to become The End. That was why she had placed a Sealing on him, delaying his awakening as both a Yokai and the Slaughter Predator for as long as she could.

"What exactly is Erebrisa?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking directly at Laplace.

Laplace had said that watching over him had been the right choice. Had Lee Sungmin fully awakened as the Slaughter Predator, it would not have been. This meant that the mysterious brokerage Guild known as Erebrisa did not want The End to come.

"Hundreds of years ago, the Dragons completely vanished from this world."

Lee Sungmin had asked Nebul this same question before, but Nebul had evaded it, saying he could not answer.

"The Dragons were wise and great," Laplace explained. "They knew this world was trapped within the inescapable destiny of The End, and they knew that no matter what they did, they could not change that fate."

[Wise and great, my ass. What a load of crap.]

The Void Monarch grumbled. Lee Sungmin ignored him for the moment.

"In the end, the Dragons chose to leave this world together."

He had heard this story before, long ago from the Black Dragon Knight.

"The Dragons left, but they could not bring themselves to completely abandon the world they were born and raised in… Eria. They knew too much. They knew this world was a Slaughterhouse maintained for a specific purpose, and that it would collapse the moment that purpose was fulfilled. They knew that the world we perceive now has, in fact, been repeated several times over."

"...So?"

"The Dragons sought to change this world's destiny. That is why, before leaving this dimension, they left behind a vast magic system. That system is Erebrisa."

The qualification for membership was the potential to become a variable in the world.

"I see," Lee Sungmin nodded. He wouldn't have understood any of this back then, but things were different now. He had learned a great deal. Erebrisa wanted to prevent The End, and the 'variables' they accepted as members were those with the potential to alter the world's course.

"Why have you appeared before me?"

"You are…" Laplace trailed off. "You were our greatest gamble. We worried that supporting you might ultimately serve The End. However, we trusted Mushii, who recommended you."

Mushii had known his true nature.

"That trust has borne fruit. Lee Sungmin. You have escaped the destiny of the Slaughter Predator, which was bound to appear, and have become a being untangled from any fate. The reason I, the manager of Erebrisa, have come to meet you is to inform you of matters you do not know. Matters unknown to this world."

"What matters?"

"About the Little Heavenly Demon."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's expression hardened.

"...What do you know about her?"

"We know a great deal. The Little Heavenly Demon is the greatest variable capable of steering this world away from the destiny of The End."

"Why are you telling me this?"

"The Little Heavenly Demon is indeed the greatest variable, but she is under the protection of the Demonic Spirit. The End of this world is being delayed because the Demonic Spirit betrayed it, but… we still do not trust the Demonic Spirit."

"Am I not also under the protection of the Demonic Spirit?"

"Pardon?" Laplace's eyes widened at the question. He stared at Lee Sungmin for a long moment before shaking his head. "No. I do not sense the protection of the Demonic Spirit on you."

That answer confirmed it. Lee Sungmin now understood why his shattered mind had recovered. It was not the protection of the Demonic Spirit but the effect of the Black Heart. The heart embedded in his body had regenerated even his broken mind. It had been given to him as a stepping stone for his awakening, but ultimately, it was because of that heart that he had been able to annihilate the Slaughter Predator and be reborn.

"You are not entangled in any destiny," Laplace affirmed.

"What do you want from me?"

"We are not asking for anything grand. What we desire is to prevent The End."

[Sounds like they're asking for something plenty grand to me.]

The Void Monarch grumbled again.

"There are no rumors about the Little Heavenly Demon circulating in the world," Laplace continued. "However, we are aware of her."

"...Where is she? Where is Wiji Hoyeon?"

"The Little Heavenly Demon is wandering through the Dungeons."

And had been for the past ten years.

"While Dungeons do periodically reveal themselves, they have always existed in the cracks between worlds. When the time of The End arrives, those Dungeons will all be Beggars' Gang at once, unleashing the monsters within."

That, too, was The End.

"The Little Heavenly Demon is wandering through those Dungeons alone, destroying them, and she is monopolizing the powers that exist within them."

"Isn't her goal to stop The End?"

"As I said, we do not trust the Little Heavenly Demon… no, the Demonic Spirit." Laplace shook his head as he spoke, his form beginning to flicker and grow faint. "Avoid meeting the Demonic Spirit. The Demonic Spirit cannot leave the Demonic Spirit Pavilion in the Hyujael Mountains. Therefore, you must never go there. By ceasing to be the Slaughter Predator, you have become a being without any destiny, but if you meet the Demonic Spirit… you may be forcibly entangled in a destiny by it."

With that final warning, Laplace's figure vanished, taking Nebul with him. To continue the conversation would have been dangerous for them as well. Though the Dragons were gone, the system they left behind, Erebrisa, existed as a parasite within the world of Eria. As such, they had no choice but to be wary of the Demonic Spirit and the other Spirits who managed this world.

*Don't trust the Demonic Spirit?*

If that was the case, could Erebrisa be trusted? A heavy doubt settled in its place.
