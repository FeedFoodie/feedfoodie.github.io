At his words, the flame's laughter died. The familiar, who had been glaring at the flame, turned her head to look at Lee Sungmin as well. With a deep sigh, she threw back the hood of her robe.

"Beastfolk?" Lee Sungmin muttered, his eyes widening.

Cat ears peeked out from the crown of her black hair. At his observation, the familiar narrowed her amber eyes.

"My name is Rubia," she introduced herself. "I've been waiting for you here, on my master's command."

"Oh, is that so?" the flame chuckled. "So you're the one that damn bastard was waiting for. Then that means he's the Returned One."

The surrounding lights pulsed with every word the flame spoke. Lee Sungmin gave Rubia an indifferent look before raising a finger to point at the flame.

"What is that?"

"Look at this young brat pointing his finger," the flame grumbled. Its voice was thick with displeasure, but it took no action beyond speaking. The lights merely swelled, as if mirroring its emotions.

"It's the remnant of a great yokai sleeping in this forest," Rubia explained.

"A remnant?" the flame spat. "I'm not such a trivial being. Although I have lost my body and only my consciousness remains, I am no mere remnant."

The word 'Yokai' made Lee Sungmin tilt his head. It was not the first time he had heard the term, which in some regions was just another name for monsters. As far as he knew, there was no significant difference between a Yokai and a monster. In the end, they were all just non-human creatures that preyed on humans.

"He was once the Yokai chief who led the Hundred Ghosts," Rubia continued, "but now he has lost his body."

Lee Sungmin blinked as he listened. The flame flashed with displeasure but offered no rebuttal. He glanced back and forth between the two of them for a moment before finally asking his question.

"So. What is it you two want to tell me?" He looked first at Rubia. "I'm asking, but I already know you can't give me a proper answer. Envirus himself could answer, but you're not Envirus, are you?"

"...That's... right," she admitted. "I was waiting for you, but I can't tell you what you're curious about."

"Where is Envirus?"

"I don't know where my master is either, but if you travel with me, my master will come to find you someday."

Rubia's tone was full of conviction, but her answer did nothing to satisfy Lee Sungmin. It meant that, in the end, she was just as clueless as he was. Reading the expression on his face, she quickly added more.

"My master promised me that. He said he would come find me someday, no matter where I am."

"Are you sure?"

"Of course," Rubia said, nodding her head vigorously.

After hearing her out, Lee Sungmin turned his gaze to the flame.

"Who are you?"

"Void Monarch."

"I am the Returned One. What business did you have waiting for me?"

"I don't know," Void Monarch replied.

The voice was so confident that Lee Sungmin was left speechless, regardless of its actual content. As he stared blankly, Void Monarch added to its statement.

"I really don't know. All I know is that someday, you, the Returned One, will come for me, and I must leave this forest with you."

Hearing this, Lee Sungmin looked back at Rubia. She wore a slightly reluctant expression but met his gaze and nodded.

"What Void Monarch said is true."

"It's all the same," Lee Sungmin said, annoyance flickering in his eyes. "Everyone I've met so far, everyone who said there was something special about me, has been the same. They say there's something, but they can't explain exactly what it is. They'd evade the question, talking about The End and the Apostle of the End."

"That... my master could answer you for sure, but I..."

"Why did your master, Envirus, leave you here instead of meeting me directly?"

"I don't really know. My master is a busy person..."

"Ha!"

Lee Sungmin let out a laugh of disbelief. He tore his gaze from Rubia and fixed it on Void Monarch.

"Void Monarch. Are you the cherished wish of the clan living in this forest?"

"The clan of the forest, you say," Void Monarch sneered. "Yes. Are you talking about those foolish monkeys who have repeated incest for generations and are dying as a result? I am not their cherished wish. Their cherished wish never existed in the first place. Their only reason for being is to continue their bloodline."

"Bloodline?"

"The one chosen from those who carry that blood becomes the head of the family, and only the head can open the forest's wall. I merely exist along that path."

It was just as Void Monarch said. Behind the rock where the flame existed, a winding path continued onward, not yet at its end.

"What is this forest?"

"It's a place where the souls of ancient monsters are bound. The mere existence of those monkeys maintains this forest's Sealing."

Hearing that, Lee Sungmin glared at Void Monarch. After a moment of thought, he posed another question.

"You'll leave with me. In what form? Don't you lack a physical body?"

"There are ways," Void Monarch answered in a leisurely voice.

Lee Sungmin glanced at Rubia, who glanced back at Void Monarch before speaking in a small voice.

"The choice is yours. My master didn't give any hint about it either."

"I'm going back," Lee Sungmin replied as if there was nothing to consider. He had no desire to let some unknown great yokai inhabit his body.

As he turned to leave, Rubia quickly moved to his side.

"Let's go together."

"Until when?"

"Until my master comes for me," she answered.

Lee Sungmin let out a hollow, disbelieving laugh. He was curious about what lay deeper in the forest, but he doubted he would gain anything by venturing into a place where the souls of Yokai were sealed away.

"Uahahahaha!"

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to turn back the way he came with Rubia, a sudden, booming laugh erupted from Void Monarch. He whipped his head around, wondering what was happening.

The flame perched on the rock swelled to a great size before it exploded. In an instant, hundreds of embers that had been swaying in the mist shot toward Lee Sungmin. Rubia let out a surprised cry as he immediately unleashed his Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

The embers, however, were faster. They engulfed him before he could escape. He felt no heat from the cinders that touched his body.

An alien presence flooded his consciousness, threatening to overwhelm him. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip hard and focused his mind.

[W-what is this?!]

The invading presence was pushed back. A moment later, the thoroughly flustered voice of Void Monarch echoed in his mind.

[You...! What the hell are you that your mental barrier is so sturdy?!]

His body hummed with every shouted word from the yokai. Acting on a hunch, Lee Sungmin stripped off the martial arts uniform he was wearing. Rubia, who was next to him, made a surprised sound and whipped her head around.

"W-what are you doing all of a sudden?!"

Ignoring her reaction, Lee Sungmin looked down at his own body. The Magic Armor he had received from Celgerus, which he had put on just in case something happened in the forest, was humming. Looking closely, he saw a faint red color overlaid on its surface.

[This is bullshit... To think that I, Void Monarch, couldn't even conquer a single human's consciousness...?! Even if he is the Returned One...!]

"That's my line," Lee Sungmin frowned, tapping the armor. "What kind of bullshit is this?"

He had a few ideas as to why the possession had failed. In the past, Preskan's attempt to control his mind with magic had also failed. Kim Jonghyun had said that a blessing protecting him had reflected the spell.

*Or maybe my divine power had grown stronger from training in the Mental World.*

It seemed to be one of the two. In the end, Void Monarch had failed to inhabit his body. Instead, its consciousness had settled into the Magic Armor.

"Did Void Monarch go inside there?" Rubia asked, her eyes wide.

Feeling annoyed, Lee Sungmin took off the Magic Armor. It was a pretty decent piece of equipment that he had cherished, but now he couldn't wear it because of Void Monarch's dirty trick.

[W-wait, wait! Hold on!] Void Monarch hastily called out. [I apologize for trying to possess you so suddenly!]

"After you've already done it."

[I was in a hurry! I won't do anything strange anymore...!]

"And how am I supposed to believe that?"

[I'll swear on my name...!]

"Don't try to get away with just a promise."

[Then... uh... I'll give you treasure. The treasure I collected long ago!]

That was a little tempting.

"If it's treasure, what kind of treasure?"

[I can't tell you that.]

"You don't seem to grasp the situation. If you keep spouting nonsense, I'll just take this armor off and leave it here."

[Even I don't know how much treasure I've collected, so how am I supposed to explain it?!] Void Monarch shouted in frustration.

"What is Void Monarch saying?" Rubia asked, her eyes shining.

It was not really a secret, so Lee Sungmin told her what the yokai had said. Rubia looked surprised by the news.

"If it's Void Monarch's treasure, it's something incredible that can't be valued," she replied. "It's true that Void Monarch was one of the most powerful and famous Yokai three hundred years ago, and the treasure he amassed was just as famous as he was."

[See! I was right, wasn't I?!] Void Monarch quickly shouted at her words.

Lee Sungmin looked down at the removed Magic Armor, then put it back on.

"From now on, shut up unless I talk to you first. If you start spouting nonsense, I'll take this off and throw it in a toilet cesspool."

[To think that I, the great yokai Void Monarch, would end up like this...!]

"You brought this on yourself, so why are you bitching and moaning?" Lee Sungmin grumbled as he turned back.

He half expected to find a wall of trees blocking his path, but the way was clear. In fact, it was not the path he had taken with Kazuya at all. As the trail ended, the fog dissipated, and Lee Sungmin found himself standing outside the Sleeping Forest.

"How strange," he muttered.

Rubia, standing next to him, answered without being asked. "That forest is a place entangled with powerful and diverse spells. Don't try to understand it with common sense."

"I had no intention of understanding it."

The Magic Armor inhabited by Void Monarch hummed and trembled. It made no sound, heeding Lee Sungmin's warning, but it seemed moved to be out of the forest. Lee Sungmin disliked having the yokai in his armor, but he planned to travel with it until he obtained its treasure.

Setting Void Monarch aside, he looked at Rubia. "Are you really going to follow me?"

At his question, Rubia answered with a look of disbelief. "Didn't I tell you earlier? I'm going to travel with you until my master comes to find me."

"Even if I don't want you to?"

"Don't you want to meet my master?" she said in a confident tone. "If you harm me or abandon me, you will never get to meet him."

"Do you know where I'm going that you want to come with me?"

"Where are you going?"

"I'm going to meet the Little Heavenly Demon," Lee Sungmin replied.

At his words, Rubia's jaw dropped.

"The Little Heavenly Demon... you mean the Little Heavenly Demon. That... Wiji Hoyeon? Why with that monster...?"

"Because I have a reason to meet her."

Lee Sungmin started walking as he spoke. A hesitant Rubia came to her senses and hurried after him.

"Can't we just rest somewhere with nice air and good scenery and wait for my master to come?"

"Just like your master is busy running around, I'm also busy with things I have to do," he answered without hesitation.

At his response, Rubia's face twisted. "I don't want to go..."

"Then I'll take you by force," he stated flatly. "Didn't you say I have to be with you to meet Envirus?"

As he spoke, Lee Sungmin raised his hand. His outstretched finger carried the unspoken promise to strike her acupoints without hesitation if she refused. Rubia trembled as she saw it.

"...I'll just follow you," she said, her ears drooping.
