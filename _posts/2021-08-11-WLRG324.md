"Come to think of it," a voice murmured from the circle of figures huddled around the campfire.

The speaker was a boy, still with a youthful air that made the term "young man" seem a stretch. He tugged the blanket tighter over his shoulders against the night's chill and turned his head, his gaze fixed on the distant, collapsed silhouette of a castle wall swallowed by the deep darkness.

"Do they really come out?"

"What are you talking about?" a young man sitting nearby retorted.

The chill in his voice made the boy hesitate, but he pressed on, his curiosity overriding his caution. He was the type who couldn't stand not knowing something.

"From that city, I mean."

The young man let out a dry laugh. "Can you even call that a city? It's a ruin."

The boy, whose name was Jegal Young but was more often called Aryong, slumped at the cynical reply.

"Why are you being so cold?"

The sharp rebuke came from Tang Ahee, the precious daughter of the Tang Clan. Though she was now far too old to be called a "precious daughter," having passed forty, she still possessed a striking beauty and the mature charm of a middle-aged woman who had never married.

"He was talking nonsense, so I said something," the young man replied flatly.

"A frog forgets it was once a tadpole, I see," Tang Ahee sneered. "I guess you don't remember when you were young?"

At her words, Moyong Chan's gaze turned cold and emotionless. Tang Ahee flinched but quickly squared her shoulders, puffing her chest out proudly.

"Don't tell me you don't remember. Back then, in the North..."

"Why are you bringing that up?" Moyong Chan's eyes blazed with hostility.

*Oops.* Tang Ahee winced, her mouth snapping shut. She had touched a nerve.

The incident in the North, Kim Jonghyun's First Subjugation Battle, was a taboo subject for Moyong Chan. It was the very event that had shattered his clan. The Moyong Clan, once among the most prestigious, had fallen because of that battle. Moyong Chan's own father, the previous clan head, had been consumed by a mad thirst for revenge and attacked the mages, dying in the process. The clan's elite forces were all but annihilated in the aftermath.

The Murim Alliance had expressed its deep regret but still levied an enormous fine, forcing the Moyong Clan to sell off most of its land. The problems only compounded from there. With their elite masters dead, their strongest warrior and clan head gone, and their wealth depleted, only the young Moyong Chan remained. The clan was in ruins.

Had Moyong Chan not gritted his teeth, honed his skills relentlessly, and broken through to the Supreme Peak at such a young age, the Moyong Clan would have been nothing more than a footnote in history.

"Now, now, let's all calm down."

A large, bear-like young man with a bushy beard stepped in to soothe the rising tension. This was Hwangbo Myeongun, the young master of the Hwangbo Clan. The four great murim Noble Clans had once been the Namgung, the Tang, the Moyong, and the Jaegal, but after the Moyong Clan's fall, the Hwangbo Clan had taken its place. Despite his rough appearance, Hwangbo Myeongun offered a good-natured smile.

"We can understand why Young is curious, can't we? Chan-hyung, don't be so prickly."

At Hwangbo Myeongun's mediation, Moyong Chan let the hostility fade from his expression. Tang Ahee, however, shot him a final glare and grumbled, "Who do you think you are, you bastard, glaring at your Nunim like that?"

"Now, now, Nunim," Hwangbo Myeongun soothed.

Though he looked old for his age, he was actually a few years younger than Moyong Chan. Jegal Young, meanwhile, was not yet twenty. Among them, only Tang Ahee was over forty, though her immature words and actions often made that fact hard to believe.

These four, all members of the Noble Clans, had come to this place because of a rumor. An unusual force was said to be stirring in the ruined city that, ten years ago, had been called Germud. They suspected it might be a force from the Blood Alliance, though the rumor's authenticity could not be confirmed.

The Blood Alliance was a new power that had emerged five years ago, led by a figure calling himself the Blood Demon. After the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance disbanded, he had gathered the smaller unorthodox sects under his banner, but the Blood Alliance didn't just attract the unorthodox.

Following the two massacres committed by Kim Jonghyun, Black Mages had been completely ostracized. The Mages Guild forcibly expelled every last one, and countless others were stoned in the streets simply for practicing Dark Magic. A deep-seated fear had taken root in the hearts of the people, a fear that another Kim Jonghyun might appear at any moment.

The Blood Alliance had taken in these outcast Black Mages. They established their base in Haras, the former seat of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, and had so far made no radical moves, yet, the very act of sheltering Black Mages made them an eyesore. The long peace between the orthodox and unorthodox sects felt fragile, for unlike its predecessor, the Blood Alliance was an organization that reeked of foulness.

And now, a force from that very alliance was supposedly plotting something in Germud. The information came from the Beggars' Gang, and while not entirely trustworthy, it couldn't be ignored. The report was bolstered by sightings of a considerable number of people dressed like Black Mages entering and leaving the ruined city, making it certain that something was afoot.

"Still," Hwangbo Myeongun said, turning back to the boy, "what did you say comes out, Young?"

Jegal Young leaned in, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper as if he had been waiting for the question. "What else? Ghost Spirits." He paused for effect. "Tens of thousands of people died in that city ten years ago, didn't they?"

Ten years ago, the Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun and his Death Knight Legion had committed a great massacre in Germud, turning its innocent citizens into zombies.

"I know that much," Hwangbo Myeongun grumbled with a snort, clearly not one for such gossip. "Though I don't know the exact details of what happened."

Jegal Young looked at him with disbelief. "Ten years ago, the Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun unleashed a massacre in that city, but in the end, the Saint and the Holy Knights of the Church, along with Warriors of Justice who could no longer stand his evil, gathered to defeat the Death Knight Legion. They even succeeded in killing the Demon Lord himself."

That, at least, was how the rumor went.

"However," Jegal Young continued, "the Phantom Spear, who was so active in that battle..." He trailed off, his eyes flickering toward Moyong Chan. Jegal Young's own older brother, Jaegal Taeryeong, had been killed by the Phantom Spear, and he wasn't the only one. Moyong Chan had also lost family to the man, but to Jegal Young's relief, Moyong Chan's expression remained impassive, a mask of perfect neutrality.

"...he was suddenly seized by madness," Jegal Young resumed. "They say he lost his reason and just started rampaging. The Holy Knight Regiment and the other warriors had already suffered heavy losses fighting Kim Jonghyun. They couldn't stop the Phantom Spear. They had no choice but to retreat... and the last thing they saw was him slaughtering the remaining zombies and chewing on their rotten flesh."

"......"

Hwangbo Myeongun shuddered. So, he ate zombies?

"That Phantom Spear bastard must have a strong stomach. So?"

"After they regrouped, the Holy Knights went back into Germud to subjugate the madman, but... the city was empty. All they found were the corpses of zombies that had finally met a true death, rotting and stinking. Many of them were half-eaten."

"Where did the Phantom Spear go?"

"Nobody knows, but the speculation is that after the Holy Knights retreated, someone else must have fought him. They said there were signs of a new battle in the city."

"So, you're saying the Phantom Spear is dead?"

"That's the thing, no one knows. The Holy Knights tried to investigate further, but the whole city was filled with such a foul, demonic energy that they couldn't stand it and had to leave."

Jegal Young lowered his voice again.

"Ever since then, the city has been a ruin of death, choked by that dense, foul energy. No one can live there. They say not even rats or weeds can survive. It's been ten years, so the energy has supposedly weakened, but an ordinary person would still pass out or even die just from setting foot inside."

"So... that's why ghosts appear?"

"Yes. Tens of thousands of wrongful deaths in a city steeped in foul, demonic energy... Tons of people who've passed by here claim they've seen faint spirits or heard screams when no one was around."

"I don't know about Ghost Spirits."

Moyong Chan, who had been listening to the entire story in silence, finally opened his mouth.

"Yokai will definitely appear. That foul energy might be hard for a human to endure, but for a Yokai, it's more medicine than poison, and this is the South, it's crawling with them. You, of all people, should know that." He looked directly at Jegal Young, whose Jaegal Clan was based in Devon, a southern city not far from their current location.

"...I know," Jegal Young admitted. "I know how troublesome and cruel Yokai are, but if the Blood Alliance is in that city... if their Black Mages are plotting something, don't we have to stop them? What if they're trying to repeat what the Demon Lord did ten years ago?"

"It's all useless if you die," Moyong Chan stated, pulling his blanket tighter and leaning back to lie down. "No matter how great your sense of justice is, no matter what reputation you build, it's all over when you die."

That's right. It's over when you die. The fact was seared into his memory. He had trained day and night to revive the clan that had been ruined by the incident ten years ago. Even after breaking through to the Supreme Peak, he had pushed himself further, honing his skills relentlessly, believing it still wasn't enough. A few years ago, convinced he had finally reached a level he wouldn't be ashamed of, he had knocked on the doors of the Murim Alliance as the young head of the Moyong Clan, determined to make his family's name renowned once more.

However, it's all over if you die. Moyong Chan closed his eyes, the image of his father's death replaying behind them.

The sun rose. The group broke camp and headed toward Germud.

Their objective was to discover what was happening within the city and, if possible, crush whatever conspiracy was afoot. If that proved impossible, they would retreat and report their findings to the Murim Alliance.

Before they entered, Tang Ahee handed each of them a pill. A decade had passed, weakening the city's poisonous energy considerably, but it was still dangerous to breathe for long. As she took her own pill, she let out a quiet sigh.

*To think I have to be out in the field doing this at my age.*

*Maybe I should have just gotten married early and settled down as the lady of the house.*

*Once this job is over, I should go back and find someone suitable.*

With that thought, Tang Ahee pushed the pill into her mouth.

She could not have imagined that only a few hours later, she would wet her pants for the first time in a very long while.
