Pressed into a corner, Lloyd had no choice but to confess the nature of his relationship with Lee Sungmin. When his story concluded, a stunned silence fell over the group. They now understood the impossible situation they were in. If they threatened Scarlet here, Lloyd would be unable to grant Lee Sungmin's request, a failure that would cost Lloyd his life due to the Mana oath he had sworn.

"What a mess," the Green Tower Master muttered with a hollow laugh.

Were it any other mage, things might be different, but this was a matter of life and death for Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master himself. For his sake, their hands were tied. They could not threaten Scarlet.

Having grasped the entirety of the situation, Scarlet looked at the Holy Knight Commander with a triumphant smile.

"So, what're you going to do?"

"…Hmph."

The Holy Knight Commander's brow furrowed. He too understood that there was nothing he could do to her now. In the end, he shot Scarlet a fierce glare before turning away. Scarlet simply scoffed as she watched his retreating back.

[He'll probably show up again. The Phantom Spear.]

*Without a doubt.* Chwi Geol did not say the words aloud.

[He came all the way here to save the Crimson Archmage, even knowing the Murim Alliance and the Moyong Clan were present. Things went wrong and his identity was revealed, but since she's here… he'll have no choice but to show himself again for her sake.]

[I suppose so.]

Moyong Daeun nodded his head, listening to Chwi Geol's thought transmission. He rubbed the spot on his chest where Lee Sungmin had struck him, his brow creasing at the memory. Lee Sungmin could have killed him with that single blow, a fact Moyong Daeun was keenly aware of, but he had not.

[The Phantom Spear… he's surpassed the realm of the Supreme Peak.]

[Are you saying he's entered the Transcendent Realm?]

[There's no other way he could have dealt with me so easily,] Moyong Daeun admitted in a bitter tone. It was a truth he did not want to accept, but Chwi Geol also had no choice but to acknowledge it. Lee Sungmin had reached the Transcendent Realm. He had overpowered everyone present with his aura alone and escaped with leisurely ease. Such overwhelming martial prowess was absolutely impossible for someone at the level of the Supreme Peak.

*Does this mean… the Phantom Spear has reached the same level as Chairman Black Dragon Knight or the Wudang Sword Immortal?*

The thought stirred complicated emotions within Chwi Geol. He did not particularly like Lee Sungmin. In truth, it was not even a grudge. It was jealousy. Chwi Geol rubbed his stiff cheek and narrowed his eyes. Their forces were far too insufficient, a consequence of never imagining the Phantom Spear would reveal himself here. They might have enough power to kill a single Black Mage, but they were sorely lacking when it came to subduing a master of the Transcendent Realm.

*Still, I learned something good.*

The Crimson Archmage. Chwi Geol's gaze drifted to Scarlet. Discovering the relationship between her and Lee Sungmin was a great achievement. If they captured Scarlet and made her ask for help through the bracelet, the Phantom Spear would have no choice but to move.

*Silent Flash, too.*

Thinking of Baek Sogo brought a sorrowful feeling to him. Chwi Geol had been continuously tracking her whereabouts ever since letting her go, but he had lost all trace of her after she entered Mount Mushii.

*If I capture the Crimson Archmage, I can lure them both out at the same time.*

Even setting aside his personal desire to see Baek Sogo again, discovering a means to summon the Phantom Spear was a great accomplishment. Chwi Geol reached into his clothes and retrieved a small crystal ball. It was a magic artifact for long-distance communication, connected to the Beggars' Gang branch in Hedor. At this distance, communication was possible without relying on martial arts.

Chwi Geol relayed the information he had just learned through the crystal. The story would pass from one Beggars' Gang branch to another until it finally reached the Murim Alliance. By tomorrow at the latest, the Alliance would know that the Phantom Spear had appeared here, along with all the related details.

*The Phantom Spear will probably show up during the subjugation battle.*

Chwi Geol glanced at Moyong Daeun. The Phantom Spear had shown mercy this time, but there was no guarantee he would do so again.

*If the situation goes south, I'll have to pull out,* Chwi Geol decided, organizing his thoughts.

Moyong Chan mulled over the words Lee Sungmin had left behind. *I did not kill your sister.* He did not believe them, of course, but he had no choice but to accept the part about Lee Sungmin showing mercy by not killing them all.

*Why didn't he kill us?*

He must have had plenty of opportunities. Why on earth did he hold back? As Moyong Chan pondered the question, he recalled the troubled expression Lee Sungmin would wear whenever he was invited to dinner, but the reason he had not killed them did not matter. The only thing that mattered to Moyong Chan was that Lee Sungmin was his sister's enemy.

In his office at the Murim Alliance, the Black Dragon Knight stroked his chin as he processed the newly received information. He had many things to worry about recently. The Martial God, who stood at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven, had finished his seclusion training. That was one concern. Another was that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, whose whereabouts had been unknown, had reappeared in Haras and begun to reign over the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance once more.

*And now, the Phantom Spear on top of it all.*

He had been continuously receiving directives from Heaven Beyond Heaven regarding the man's handling. In truth, there had been several opportunities to kill the Phantom Spear, but strangely, Heaven Beyond Heaven never issued an order for his extermination.

*Observe him.* Under such a vague directive, the Phantom Spear had been left alone, his actions seemingly condoned. With the end of the Martial God's seclusion, however, the disposition regarding him had changed.

"There's no need for the Murim Alliance to send an extermination squad. The Martial God said he would leave the Phantom Spear's disposal to the Spear King."

[If the Spear King goes, it is a matter of will,] the Spirit Medium replied to the Black Dragon Knight's question.

At those words, the Black Dragon Knight chuckled. Although he held the position of Murim Alliance Chairman, it was a title in name only. Heaven Beyond Heaven had long controlled the orthodox murim from behind the scenes, and his own appointment was proof that they had begun to control it completely. In the end, even as Chairman, he had no direct decision-making authority. Everything had to go through Heaven Beyond Heaven.

"What did the Spear King say?"

[The Spear King's location is not far from the North. He also has great interest in the Phantom Spear, and I have relayed that the Phantom Spear is near Hedor.]

"Then we can just leave the rest to the Spear King."

[Just in case, I would like to request support from the Murim Alliance's extermination squad.]

"Would the Pure White Martial Squad be insufficient?"

[That is for you to judge directly, Chairman. Isn't the leader of the Pure White Martial Squad Chwi Geol of the Beggars' Gang?]

"He's not a very reliable fellow. It's typical of those who try to act smart. His skills aren't that great either. The full force of the Pure White Martial Squad is still far from enough to subdue the Phantom Spear of the Transcendent Realm."

[Then?]

"I'll send an extermination squad. However… if we kill the Phantom Spear, won't the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman make a move?"

[Even so, we can't just leave the Phantom Spear be, can we?]

"I understand the Martial God has moved to secure the Little Heavenly Demon. If the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman goes on a rampage, who do you plan to use to counter him?"

[The Martial God's outing will not take long, and… if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman shows any drastic movements, the Martial God left word beforehand to ask you, Chairman.]

The Black Dragon Knight laughed heartily at the Spirit Medium's words and shook his head.

"You want me to face that monster? I'm not confident."

[The Martial God ordered it so.]

"That's too much," the Black Dragon Knight grumbled in a small voice. "What should be done about the Crimson Archmage?"

[Just in case, it would be best to secure her.]

"First, I'll pass that along to Chwi Geol."

With those final words, the Black Dragon Knight put down the crystal ball. If the Spear King made a move, the Phantom Spear was bound to be caught. Even though the Phantom Spear was also a master of the Transcendent Realm, the Spear King and the Moon Queen were monsters who received special treatment even among the Six Honored Ones.

The Black Dragon Knight stood by the window and thought of the Phantom Spear, a man whose face he had never even seen.

*The Phantom Spear… the Phantom Spear.*

He had many things to feel sorry for regarding him, but that did not mean he actually felt sorry. It was something that could not be helped.

*This time, too.*

At this point, it had to be fate. Kim Jonghyun thought, a faint smile touching his lips. He could not help but laugh. Strangely enough, he had often been entangled with Lee Sungmin in various ways. All those encounters had been a series of coincidences, and now, Kim Jonghyun was convinced it was destiny.

*The past life you remember ended a few months ago. What a shame. I wanted to compare the me from the past life you know with the me of today.*

*Would I be different?* Kim Jonghyun tilted his head, contemplating the thought. Probably… he would not be. The Kim Jonghyun of that past life would have, without a doubt, betrayed Arbes and taken everything from him to obtain the Grimoire, and he would have felt drawn to this ritual, preparing for it just as he was now. The realization gave Kim Jonghyun a strange sensation, because if possible, he had wanted to become a different person from his past self.

*It can't be helped. Because I am me.*

That was likely an unavoidable fact. In a cheerful mood, Kim Jonghyun waited for the subjugation squad and Lee Sungmin to arrive. In two days, this forest would be filled with all sorts of screams and the stench of blood.

Yes, two days.

Lee Sungmin followed the subjugation squad from a suitable distance, ensuring Scarlet's safety. The squad probably knew he was trailing them.

*For now, they won't be able to do anything to Scarlet.*

The fact that Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master, had joined the subjugation squad was a stroke of luck. Although he felt a little sorry for the man, he had succeeded in securing Scarlet's safety by using Lloyd's life as collateral.

However, the request made to Lloyd would lose its effect once Kim Jonghyun's subjugation was over. Likewise, the oath between the Green Tower Master and Scarlet would also become void. The Murim Alliance or the Moyong Clan would undoubtedly try to secure Scarlet then, confident they could lure him out by taking her hostage.

*I have to make a move before that.*

Once they arrived at the forest and the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun began in earnest, it would be difficult for them to respond immediately to his intrusion. Moving after the subjugation ended, in any form, would be too late. When the subjugation begins. Yes, that was the time.

[Wouldn't it have been easier to just beat that old man, the Green Tower Master, to death?] the Void Monarch sneered. [If you killed him, that red-haired girl would also be freed from her oath. I'm saying there was no need to go through all this trouble.]

*The Mages Guild doesn't treat me as an enemy,* Lee Sungmin replied. *However, if I were to kill the Green Tower Master, the story would change. It's a hassle, but I thought this way would be better in the long run.*

[So you're not going to make unnecessary enemies?]

*In the Elf Forest, there wasn't really any other way, but now, there's another option.* Lee Sungmin tore off a piece of beef jerky as he answered.

[So. What do you plan to do with the redhead from now on? Are you going to take her back to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?]

*…That's the problem. Because of me, Scarlet can no longer stay at the Red Magic Tower.*

Would the Mages Guild protect her? Honestly, he was skeptical. The Murim Alliance and the Mages Guild had maintained a friendly relationship for a long time. No matter how outstanding a mage Scarlet was, it seemed likely that the Guild would choose to hand her over to the Alliance rather than sour their relationship.

*I have no choice but to take her to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.*

Although he intended to respect Scarlet's wishes as much as possible, Lee Sungmin made up his mind. For now, that was the only path.
