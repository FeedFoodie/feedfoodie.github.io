Lee Sungmin did not subscribe to the notion that the first to strike always wins. He simply had nothing he wanted to say to the Queen. She was defenseless in the immediate aftermath of her Advent, and he had no desire to answer the question she had thrown at him, a question laced with irritation and displeasure.

And he was fast. The distance to where the Queen stood closed in an instant. Her eyebrow twitched, but before she could make a move, the spear Lee Sungmin thrusted surged toward her.

KRRRANG!

The spearhead tore through space and collided with an invisible wall. A powerful resistance jolted up the arm that held the spear, and the unseen barrier blocking the Queen rippled from the impact. She looked down at Lee Sungmin's face, now close enough to touch. A tremor ran through her pupils.

"Ah."

She knew who he was. The Queen's face brightened with recognition, and the irritation that had dominated her emotions just moments before vanished completely.

Inside Lee Sungmin, Rubia curled her stiff body into a ball. The Queen's gaze saw through to even her presence.

"......"

The space between them erupted. Lee Sungmin swung his spear wide and retreated from the blast.

"Rubia," the Queen's voice rose, sharp and accusatory. "My creation. The artificial Elemental I crafted with my own hands. Why are you there? You couldn't even protect that pitiful master of yours, so what gall do you have to show yourself to me?"

[Hic...!]

Rubia trembled violently at the Queen's cry. The Queen then raised a long index finger and pointed it directly at Lee Sungmin.

"......"

Her voice was now filled with a chilling ecstasy.

"It's you. Yes, if only you hadn't existed. If you hadn't forced him to make that vow..."

The Queen's voice twisted bizarrely as her lips moved in a ceaseless babble of incomprehensible words. Her voice grew louder, her eyes turning sharp and murderous. Lee Sungmin could not understand the language, but he could guess its meaning from her tone.

[This is... an ancient... language of the Elementals,] Rubia muttered, her body shaking. She could not bring herself to relay the words she understood, lacking the confidence to properly translate the Queen's crazed curses.

Lee Sungmin frowned at the Queen's first intelligible words. *If only you hadn't existed.* When he thought about it, she was not entirely wrong. Envirus had fallen into such a state because he had met Lee Sungmin. It was in Urmuri that Lee Sungmin had asked Envirus to handle the Dragon Heart, a request Envirus had not only heard but had also sworn an oath on his Mana to fulfill.

He had done nothing wrong. He had done his best to help Envirus, infiltrating Urmuri's barrier, defeating Preskan, and fighting Aine. If anything, it was Envirus who was in the wrong. From Envirus's perspective, Lee Sungmin was an incredibly dangerous being, and at the time, Envirus had intended to kill him in exchange for his own life.

He had simply failed. Lee Sungmin had survived, and Envirus had faced a mortal crisis as the price for breaking his oath. If the Spirit Queen had not intervened with her Advent back then, Envirus's body would have completely collapsed from the Mana rampage.

"If only you," the Queen spat, her bloodshot eyes flashing as she switched back from the language of the Elementals. "Hadn't existed."

Honestly, it was quite unfair. Lee Sungmin had done nothing wrong. Envirus had made his own judgment and betrayed him of his own accord, and he had paid the price for it. Lee Sungmin had no direct involvement in what happened afterward either. In fact, wasn't it Envirus who had ruined everything? The dimensional connection magic that Abel had cast by scraping together the last of his lifespan was destroyed because of him. The one who killed Envirus was not Lee Sungmin.

And yet, the Queen was blaming him unconditionally.

*It's not that I can't understand,* Lee Sungmin thought as he stealthily pulled his foot back.

"......"

It was the signal.

An explosion followed, but its form was unlike any he had ever seen. It was not a blast of high temperature flames, nor was it a burst of light. The very space the Spirit Queen occupied condensed into a single point and then simply burst. The unannounced explosion blew her body to pieces. She spilled no blood or guts, but her expression was one of pure shock.

The magic did not end with a single blast. Just as flames consume oxygen, this explosion spread from one space to another. Down below, Lloyd had formed a rectangle with the index fingers and thumbs of both hands, framing the Spirit Queen within it.

"Explode, explode, explode," Lloyd uttered, his lips barely moving. Space was continuously devoured, condensed, and detonated.

The silent explosions continued to consume the Queen's body, twisting it grotesquely. Severed limbs swelled, burst, and vanished into nothing. However, the Queen did not die. As a transcendent being, she was immortal regardless of day or night. Attacks of a 'lower class' could not harm her existence. Even if her physical body was endlessly blown apart, her essence remained here, completely unharmed. The Queen's eyes turned to Lloyd.

KKIIIGIGIK!

The earth trembled. Blades shot up from the ground, aiming to tear Lloyd apart. An attack of that level... Lloyd silently used Blink to escape.

Scarlet aimed for the opening his move created. She used a completely different type of magic, her gloved hand tracing runes in the air. Even spatial explosions could not deal a satisfactory blow.

"Flame Curse," she incanted.

HWARURURUK!

The magic unfolded. All the pieces of the Queen's flesh, scattered by the explosions, were swallowed by black fire that turned them to ash. The Queen, now just a head floating amidst the flames, wore an expression of pure irritation. These flames were far from enough to extinguish her existence, but they were annoying and unpleasant.

She was the Spirit Queen. Even if this was not the Elemental Realm, and even if there were no Elementals to pledge their loyalty, the unfavorable conditions did not mean she was no longer a transcendent being.

Flames?

The Queen let out a hollow laugh, the corners of her lips twitching upward.

CHWAAAAAK!

Her body, her very existence, dissolved into a giant droplet of water. A surging mass of water materialized in the air and plummeted downward, unleashing a massive wave that crashed through the forest before Lloyd or Scarlet could react.

"Hahaha!" the Spear King laughed loudly. He leaped high, landing before the two mages, and slammed the spear he had been swinging over his head downward.

KWAAANG!

The ground shook violently, cracked earth shooting upward to form a wall. The wave that had been about to swallow everyone was blocked, and the splashing, surging water transformed back into the Queen's body. The Spear King's eyes flashed as he looked at her watery form.

"Come!" he roared, raising both hands high. He had already split his long spear, and now he held two short spears. He kicked off the ground and leaped.

The surging Queen looked at the Spear King with irritated eyes. Retreating was a humiliation, so she changed her form back to her original body to face him.

PABABABAK!

The Spear King's arms moved at high speed. The short spears he wielded stabbed, swung, and struck, pressuring the Queen with a storm of attacks from every direction.

The Queen's method of attack was neither flashy nor beautiful. It was simple and brutish. She knew she was a transcendent being and that low class attacks could not kill her. Therefore, she made only minimal effort to evade or defend. Fatal wounds were meaningless to her, though losing her head would blind her for a moment, and losing her legs would halt her.

Everything else she ignored. A spear grazed her side, but she had no internal organs to begin with, so it could not even be called a wound. The hand she extended forward was shattered by a spear. She paid it no mind and continued to advance as her arm regenerated in an instant. Her open palm reached for the Spear King, and just as his spear was about to shatter that arm again, her hand emitted a light a fraction of a second sooner.

Danger.

The Spear King hurriedly lowered his stance.

PAT.

A light flashed once.

KWARRREUNG!

The space behind him exploded. The trees, the ground, everything that had been there vanished without a trace. Just as the Queen was about to launch another attack, the Black Dragon Knight's foot kicked her body, sending it flying into the air. The spinning Queen quickly regained her balance and glared at the Black Dragon Knight, who was now leaping toward her.

KUDUDUDUK!

The ground writhed and shot upward. A massive wave of earth and sand expanded and swallowed the Black Dragon Knight's body.

It was their first time fighting together like this, and because of that, Lee Sungmin could not properly find an opening to jump in. In truth, there was no absolute need for him to intervene. They were all strong enough, and they were already covering each other's openings. If there was a gap, whoever could exploit it would proactively attack. The Black Dragon Knight tore through the pressing earth just as the Spear King leaped into the air. In that same moment, Lee Sungmin also dashed toward the Queen.

Not much time had passed since the battle began, but in that short period, the Queen was already feeling an immense irritation. Among those currently bothering her, none were particularly threatening on their own. Even if they were a mage and a martial artist who had reached the pinnacle among humans, they were still just humans in the end.

There were just so many of them. In terms of threat, they did not even compare to Wiji Hoyeon, who had invaded the Elemental Realm all by herself. At that point, Wiji Hoyeon had already transcended the realm of humans, which was why the Queen, despite fighting in her own domain, could neither kill nor overwhelm her. The ten day and night battle had driven the Elemental Realm to the brink of collapse, forcing the Queen to hastily attempt an Advent to escape.

She was not feeling that sense of crisis now. Their attacks were not on a level that could harm her existence. Still, it was annoying. Irritating.

The Queen perceived the roaring Spear King as a madman. The man, laughing loudly as if something was incredibly amusing, handled his two spears as if they were his own limbs. They were fast and heavy. She twisted her upper body to avoid a piercing spear but could not dodge completely. Her chest was torn away, but she did not care. If she could just touch him, she was confident she could kill him.

It was when she stretched her open hand forward.

"......"

The Queen's vision went black. Lee Sungmin pulled out the spear he had embedded in her head.

"This is my fight!" the Spear King roared.

"It's our fight," Lee Sungmin replied in a placid voice.

The Queen's tilting body trembled, then writhed and swelled up.

SHWIIIIK... SHWIIIIK...

A sound like air escaping a balloon came from her neck, where her head had not yet regenerated, and then, she exploded.

The wind that had been tightly compressed within her body burst out. However, neither the Spear King nor Lee Sungmin retreated. Even the Black Dragon Knight, who had just joined them, tore through the wind with both palms to open a path and entered the storm. Lloyd and Scarlet raised their hands and aimed upward, targeting the Queen with different types of magic.

"Mere humans..."

The Queen's words were cut short as the belligerent Spear King charged in recklessly. He tore through the wind with one spear and carefully aimed the other at her body. He threw it. The spear emitted a blue light and then exploded.

PAJIJIJIK!

A storm of enhanced qi burst from the spear, annihilating all the wind blocking its path. The Spear King charged through the path he had brutally carved open. The Black Dragon Knight, rather than wasting unnecessary energy, followed close behind him.

The Queen had planned to gather her power and wipe them all out at once, but she had only just completed her Advent. Without the support of other Elementals, she lacked instantaneous firepower. The difference was simple. She was one, and they were many.

[Isn't this the first time you've been in a position like this?] the Void Monarch sneered, cackling.
