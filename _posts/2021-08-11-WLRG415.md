"Answer me," Lee Sungmin muttered, his voice low as he pressed on through the endless sea of corpses. He ground his teeth, and the mysterious phenomena he unleashed swirled around his body in a violet shroud.

"What is that?"

The Spirit had to be watching. It was the only explanation, for the Spirit was the one who had opened the path for him to enter this place.

The sheer number of bodies was overwhelming, and the unpleasant sensation of stepping on and crushing them grew unbearable. With a surge of power, Lee Sungmin launched himself into the air.

The question of where on earth he was came far too late. Until this moment, it was a question he had not needed to ask. He knew this was a space sealed within the Sleeping Forest, a seal that would break when Kazuya and the other villagers died, unleashing the monsters trapped inside.

However, why?

The logic was a contradiction. Greed was meant to be released only when The End had left nothing in the world. To be precise, it would happen after the reincarnator awakened as the Slaughter Predator, the true agent of The End. The Slaughter Predator, true to its name, was destined to kill and devour every living thing. By the time the barrier of the Sleeping Forest vanished, nothing would be left.

So why were there monsters here? Why release them into a world where nothing living remained?

[They are corpses.]

Lee Sungmin halted his flight, glaring up at the red sky that seemed deceptively close. The Spirit had answered his unspoken question.

"I know that. I'm asking why. Why are they here?"

[Why do you ask? I thought you would have guessed by now. You're not as dull as you used to be, are you?] the Spirit said with a laugh.

Lee Sungmin's lips tightened into a thin line. The Spirit was right. A part of him had already guessed, a part he desperately wished to ignore.

[This world has already repeated itself several times. Each time, the Slaughter Predator devours everything, and greed devours the world itself.]

"The Slaughter Predator was annihilated," Lee Sungmin countered.

[When the next world begins, the annihilated Slaughter Predator simply reappears. It was never something you could call a living being in the first place. Just as this world was artificially created, the Slaughter Predator is a being that must exist to cleanse it.]

Lee Sungmin looked down at the carpet of bodies below. A horrible feeling began to churn in his stomach.

[Even when The End destroys the world, the records of this world don't disappear. They're continuously updated and recorded.]

*Why... are these things here?*

[I wanted to test a possibility,] the Spirit answered without hesitation, his words confirming Lee Sungmin's darkest suspicions.

The horrible feeling intensified. Lee Sungmin squeezed his eyes shut and clenched his fists.

[Getting my hands on Wiji Hoyeon was simple enough once she lost the Demonic Spirit's protection. However, controlling her completely was impossible. Her divine power was too strong for that. To think she wasn't swayed even after losing her protection... Thanks to that, I had to break her mind first.]

He did not want to hear any more.

[I pondered for quite a while on how to break her, but it didn't seem possible with things like pain and torture. It's messy, too. Besides, I liked this method more. I thought that if there was any potential left in her, this could maximize it.]

So this was it.

[First, I tried using monsters. There were plenty of records of them. How many monsters do you think have existed in this world? I even added the ones from the Dungeons. Without anyone needing to explain, Wiji Hoyeon naturally understood. She knew she had to kill every monster here.]

Even if it was not the way out, she would have had no choice. She had to stop The End at all costs.

[The battles were long, but they yielded no results. Wiji Hoyeon's power was simply too great. Even though I didn't get the results I wanted regarding her potential, it wasn't a meaningless time. Tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands, millions... If you kill on a scale that surpasses even that, your mind is bound to break, no matter how great a being you are. Besides, Wiji Hoyeon was human.]

He remembered her sitting in a field of flowers, reaching a hand out to a butterfly. He remembered her smiling under the sunset.

[Next, I chose the inhuman. This time, too, Wiji Hoyeon didn't hesitate. She didn't even get tired. Killing, killing, and killing again... Look, there are quite a lot of corpses, aren't there? And this is after I've cleared them out several times.]

The Spirit laughed heartily.

[Then came the humans. At that point... haha. Humans are truly fascinating. They have so much potential, but they're so weak. No matter how much you train the body, you can't do anything about the mind.]

Wiji Hoyeon was not made of iron. He knew that well. He remembered her in this very forest, weakened by the curse, in a place where they could see each other but never reach one another. He knew the Wiji Hoyeon who had trembled, muttering "What should I do?" over and over, terrified of failure, terrified that she would not be able to stop The End and that everything would be over.

[But in the end, she had no choice. She believed her actions were to stop The End. Doesn't that resemble someone?]

The Martial God, who had died laughing at himself as his own blood vessels burst after his arms were severed. He had believed until his last breath that everything he had done was to stop The End.

"It's different," Lee Sungmin spat at the unseen Spirit. "This is truly for the sake of stopping The End."

[It's not so different,] the Spirit denied. [The massacre Wiji Hoyeon committed here was meaningless. Truly, it had no meaning at all. It was just a slaughter, and even if this had been an act to stop The End, as a human, her mind wouldn't have been able to endure it.]

A vortex of emotions swirled in his chest, mixing and mingling until he could no longer distinguish one from the other, leaving only a profound sense of disgust. Lee Sungmin lowered his head and took a deep, shuddering breath. He could not imagine it, could not empathize with the horror. In this place, Wiji Hoyeon had killed everything without rest. This world. Every being recorded in Eria had been her enemy. Monsters, the inhuman, humans...

[At some point, her mind began to crumble.]

Lee Sungmin fully opened his senses, capturing the entire vast space. There, he could feel a single living presence.

[It was a ceaseless, repeated slaughter. No being in the world has ever killed so many. Yes, many people die in wars, but the one who starts the war doesn't personally kill everyone who dies in it, do they, but Wiji Hoyeon, she had to do it herself. All of it. Personally. There was no one by her side. She couldn't even tell anyone it was hard.]

Violet electricity crackled around Lee Sungmin's body as he unleashed Gale Thunder with all his might.

CRACKLE!

A jagged streak of violet lightning tore across the red sky, and the gruesome landscape blurred into a smear as he shot forward at incredible speed.

[All the beings who died here were nothing more than projections of the records of those who lived in the previous world. They can only exist in this space and can't be brought outside. Since they've all already died, it's impossible to project them again. However, within this space, they were undoubtedly alive.]

The distance was closing.

[Unnecessary personalities were eradicated during the projection stage, leaving them with a single purpose. To kill Wiji Hoyeon. Can you imagine it? Every being that lived in the previous world. The unconditional killing intent of countless beings. What happens to the mind of a human who receives that killing intent while slaughtering everything?]

Humans are weak. No matter how much you train the body, the mind is another matter. Lee Sungmin knew that fact well from the mental training he had received from Denir. His own mind had collapsed several times during the vast expanse of 2100 years. He had only managed to hold onto his sanity for a few hundred years at most before it began to repeatedly collapse and regenerate. That was only possible because a powerful blessing protected his mind.

However, Wiji Hoyeon had no such blessing. This was different from when she had trained her martial arts like a madwoman, all alone in a world of nothing. This was killing, killing, and more killing.

[The dying all screamed and spewed curses. It didn't matter if they were men, women, old, or young. Even a toddler who had just started walking tried to kill Wiji Hoyeon, swearing at her with clumsy words, and Wiji Hoyeon killed them like a machine. The result was very satisfying. It was also fortunate that the Demonic Spirit had fixed her existence. Thanks to that, Wiji Hoyeon couldn't even enter the Battle God Chronicle and remained human...]

He did not want to hear any more.

THUD.

Lee Sungmin landed on the corpses. Among all the bodies he had seen, the ones here were the most recent, and also the most gruesome. Unidentifiable lumps of flesh, once human or something else entirely, were scattered and piled in grotesque mounds. The stench of rot was thick, but the smell of fresh blood was thicker.

*This is hell,* he thought, then muttered aloud, "No, it's worse than hell."

His bitter eyes fell upon Wiji Hoyeon.

She was collapsed in a large pool of blood. Her hair, once as beautiful as ebony, was matted and soaked with it. Her fair skin was stained beyond its original color. The Black Dragon Armor, which she always wore on her shoulders and used as a weapon, hung from her in tattered, limply rags. Her clothes had no intact parts, their color a dark, rotten red from blood that had soaked, dried, and decayed.

"Wiji Hoyeon," Lee Sungmin called, his voice low.

[Her mind has already collapsed. Handling it is an easy task.]

"Hoyeon," he called again.

Her head lifted slowly.

[I know you can use Dragonic Magic, but even Dragonic Magic can't make everything possible.]

As her eyes turned to him, he saw they were completely devoid of emotion. Wiji Hoyeon pushed herself to her feet.

[Finally, the time of harvest has come,] the Spirit said, his voice filled with anticipation. [Let me reap the final reward of this world.]

Lee Sungmin chanted the Dragonic, but nothing happened. The Spirit's curse, woven into her broken mind, was too powerful to be undone. It was only natural. If such a thing were possible, the Dragons would have had no need to escape this world.

"Again..." Wiji Hoyeon's lips parted. She stared at Lee Sungmin with vacant eyes and muttered in a small voice, her shoulders trembling. "Again... yes, again. I have to do it." She nodded to herself as she spoke.

*Hoyeon.*

He called her name in his mind, but she gave no answer. The Wiji Hoyeon before him could not even recognize him. No matter how many times he called her name, his voice would never reach her. All the memories between them had been completely erased.

"I have to end it..." she continued to mutter. "Don't resent me. I... have to do it. Go back, I have to go back... I want to go back. Back then... it was good. Where? When... What? Why am I... Why am I doing this...?"

Listening to her broken words, Lee Sungmin swallowed the turbulent emotions that threatened to overwhelm him. He wanted to scream, to sob. Seeing her vaguely long for 'back then', having forgotten where she wanted to return to or when things had been good, made everything feel cursed.

"I don't know..."

The Black Dragon Armor, now no different from rags, trembled and rose. As it wrapped around her body, Wiji Hoyeon raised both hands and swept back the bangs covering her eyes. Drops of blood splattered down.

"Still, I have to do it."

Light returned to her eyes.

"For... for him, for his sake... I..." Her brow furrowed in confusion. "Who was that?"

The Black Dragon Armor shot forward, lunging with a speed that surpassed anything he had ever witnessed. It was faster than the Martial God, faster than Jeniela, and faster than the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

KWWAAAAANG!

Lee Sungmin met the attack with his Formless Spear, the collision sending a jarring shock through his arms. The spectral weapon dissipated on impact. Wiji Hoyeon's Black Dragon Armor also turned to dust and vanished, its attack the first and the last it would ever make.

"Hoyeon."

*I'm here.*

He remembered the words he had spoken to her in the Sleeping Forest as she trembled with her back to him.

*You don't have to despair over your failure. Even if you are there, I am here in this world.*

*If you can't do it, then I will.*

Lee Sungmin strode forward. Wiji Hoyeon blinked, watching the last of the Black Dragon Armor scatter into nothing. A moment later, killing intent stirred in her eyes.

'If it gets too late... can you come and get me?' he remembered her hesitant words.

*I will.*

At that answer, Wiji Hoyeon had smiled.

"I'm sorry I was so late."

He suppressed the urge to cry and forced a smile onto his face.

Without a moment's hesitation, Wiji Hoyeon lunged forward to kill him.
