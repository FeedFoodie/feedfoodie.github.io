The art that now flowed from him was the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, the signature martial art of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae. Lee Sungmin had learned it in the North, where the Crazy Heavenly Demon, believing his talent was being wasted, had all but forced the techniques upon him. Since that day, he had never once found the art lacking. Just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon had boasted, it was an unparalleled enhanced qi art, with almost no equal.

A flicker of recognition crossed the Sword Saint's face. He remained perfectly still as he watched Lee Sungmin radiate killing intent. "The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art?" he mused aloud. "So that's what it was called. Is there any reason I should know that now? The Crazy Heavenly Demon who practiced it is already dead."

"I've learned it," Lee Sungmin stated flatly.

"Even so, I don't see why I'd need to know," the Sword Saint retorted. "It seems like it was just some mediocre martial art."

The killing intent intensified. *The Crazy Heavenly Demon made the Sword Saint his goal. He wanted to fight him again someday and win.* A fresh wave of grief and anger surged through Lee Sungmin. *Why in Urmuri... why did he use his life qi against Chuseong? Wasn't his goal to defeat the Sword Saint? Was it all for vengeance?* The Crazy Heavenly Demon was not a man who should have died there. With that thought burning in his mind, Lee Sungmin strode forward.

The Sword Saint watched Lee Sungmin advance, his eyes missing nothing. In a clash between masters of their caliber, the slightest opening could be turned into a fatal blow, yet they were not equals. By the sheer realm of martial arts they had reached, the Sword Saint stood on a higher plane. He was, after all, the strongest of the Six Honored Ones.

So why couldn't he swing his sword? It wasn't that he failed to see an opening in the approaching man's guard. The gap might have been intentional, but that alone was not enough to stay his hand, and yet, strangely, he had no confidence. He could not trust that a swing of his sword would cut the man down.

*What is this?*

It felt like a hallucination. An indescribably ominous aura emanated from Lee Sungmin, a presence that seemed to contain all the horrors of the world. Was this the power of the Demonic Spirit? No, that wasn't all. There was something else, something the Sword Saint had felt before.

The Black Dragon Knight.

It had not been long since the Black Dragon Knight, who now served as the Murim Alliance Chairman, had joined Heaven Beyond Heaven. He had been brought in personally by the Martial God, the pinnacle of their order, and was not listed among the Six Honored Ones. The Sword Saint knew well, however, that the Black Dragon Knight's martial prowess, which he had never experienced firsthand, was in no way inferior to that of the Spear King or the Moon Queen.

*No, this guy isn't on par with the Black Dragon Knight., but the aura he exuded was similar. It was the presence of an absolute race that dominates and subjugates all creation. Why was the Phantom Spear exuding the same draconic aura as the half-human, half-dragon Black Dragon Knight?

The Sword Saint tightened his grip on his sword. The pressure was real, but he would not succumb to it. He was the Sword Saint.

Though the Sword Saint's blade remained at his side, dozens of formless slashes shot toward Lee Sungmin. A decent master of the Supreme Peak would have been slaughtered without ever perceiving the attack, but Lee Sungmin was different. His senses flared, detecting every invisible blade. The instant he did, his spear blurred into motion.

KWAKWAKWAKWANG!

With a single, powerful thrust, he unleashed the Bloody Pursuit of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Dozens of bursts of enhanced qi erupted from his spearhead, annihilating the formless slashes. His slow advance instantly became a charge.

PAK!

The Dancing Shadow Soul Break propelled him forward, accelerating his body to an incredible speed.

*He hasn't reached the Transcendent Realm yet, but...*

The Sword Saint's hand moved. Though they were not yet within range, both men swung their weapons. Spear and sword crossed the intervening space to clash, neither giving an inch. The Sword Saint took a step forward while Lee Sungmin's charge never faltered.

He swung the spear with both hands, the shaft and head transforming into a flexible snake. The sharp point became the serpent's maw, the long shaft its coiling body. Against this ever-changing assault, the Sword Saint responded with a simple horizontal swing. His blade contained enough sharpness to cut through anything, yet it failed. The enhanced qi enveloping Lee Sungmin's spear could not be bisected.

As Lee Sungmin drew closer, the threat of his spear grew. The Sword Saint, unpanicked, let his sword tremble slightly. Not one slash, but dozens, each with definite substance, flew from his blade. Spear and sword entangled in a furious clash, sending bursts of deflected enhanced qi to claw at their surroundings. The Sword Saint's sword was fast, but Lee Sungmin met every blow with certainty. The speed from Haste, the strength from his raw power, and the Yokai's body all enabled maneuvers his previous form had been incapable of.

"What in the world are you?" the Sword Saint demanded, his sword never ceasing its assault. He was not yet using his killing sword intent, but the man before him was an enigma. The martial arts he used were at a remarkable level, but they were not those of the Transcendent Realm.

There is a decisive difference that separates the Transcendent Realm from the Supreme Peak. A master of the Supreme Peak can never defeat one who has crossed that threshold. It was why all of the Six Honored Ones could not understand the Dark Supreme. How could he have been so careless as to be injured by a mere Supreme Peak master?

"You haven't entered the Transcendent Realm, but you're bizarre," the Sword Saint continued. "Your body has far surpassed human limits, the power you use is grotesque, and..." He wanted to ask about the presence, the one he had felt from the Black Dragon Knight, but he kept his mouth shut. The Black Dragon Knight's allegiance to Heaven Beyond Heaven was a secret that must not be revealed. Of course, Lee Sungmin would die here regardless, but there was no need for unnecessary words. "...No, never mind. There's no need to hear it. You will die here."

This was not like his encounter with the Dark Supreme. The Sword Saint had no intention of letting Lee Sungmin live.

A weight unlike anything before pushed Lee Sungmin's body back. The Sword Saint slowly raised his weapon, and the very scenery began to distort around the blade.

"Did the Dark Supreme show you this too?" the Sword Saint asked.

[That's going to be dangerous,] the Void Monarch warned.

The Dark Supreme had never shown him this, but Lee Sungmin knew exactly what it was. This was the decisive difference between the Transcendent Realm and the Supreme Peak, the ability to interfere with space itself. The Dark Supreme had never used it, perhaps out of stubbornness or pride. Or maybe it was a desire not to kill Lee Sungmin, even in a life-or-death struggle.

*No, he was confident. Confident he could kill me without dying himself, no matter how dangerous it got.*

Even without having seen it from the Dark Supreme, he knew. He had reached this realm himself once before.

"Everyone calls it something different," the Sword Saint continued, his voice echoing slightly in the warping air. "The martial arts we've learned are different, our thoughts are different, but since I hold a sword... it seems we all think the same way. The Wudang Sword Immortal called it this too."

The space-distorting sword began to move.

"The Spatial Sword."

He swung. Unlike the formless slashes, this attack was clearly visible, a high-level technique that could not be compared. Space itself became a blade, rushing toward Lee Sungmin. He had reached this state before, but this was the first time he had ever faced it. He raised his spear, intending to gauge its power.

KWARRRRR!

Inner arts and Demonic Energy entangled as they flowed into his spear. He unleashed the third form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang.

KWAAAANG!

The collision was successful, but it failed to offset the attack. The distortion spread out, reforming into a slash. If he were cut, it would not be a simple gash. The wounded area would vanish into a rift in space. His power was insufficient.

Lee Sungmin took several steps back, extending his spear behind him as inner qi and Demonic Energy surged into it once more. His eyes, now completely golden, felt as if they were burning.

GGWAAANG!

A second Flesh Devouring Blood Fang erupted, finally offsetting the spatial slash. *Two times. If I use my full power, could I do it in one?* Even so, he could not be relieved. He still could not manipulate space. The Sword Saint, now using the techniques of the Transcendent Realm in earnest, seemed like a completely different person.

However, that was no reason not to attack. Even if he couldn't interfere with space, Lee Sungmin had the experience of having reached that realm. Unless he defeated the Sword Saint, he could never reach the Elf village and kill the Fist Honored One, and he knew that just because one was in the Transcendent Realm did not mean they could manipulate space at will. They had transcended humanity, but they were not no longer human. Interfering with space placed a considerable burden on them. It was a power that could not be used indiscriminately.

The Sword Saint knew this as well. He had expected to cut him down with the first slash and was surprised it had been blocked, but it was fine. Even if he couldn't use it indiscriminately, the Spatial Sword was a powerful weapon. If a Supreme Peak master's arts were like throwing stones, the arts of the Transcendent Realm were a cannon.

The Sword Saint moved first, the distorted landscape trailing his blade. Staring at the charging man, Lee Sungmin lowered his spear.

It was impossible to kill the Sword Saint using only the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Regardless of the art's quality, Lee Sungmin's mastery of it could not compare to his Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, but he would not forget. He would carry out the Crazy Heavenly Demon's last wish. He would use the art in his stead to defeat the Sword Saint.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art unleashed simultaneously. Nine Dragons Slaughter met the fourth form, Blood Remnant Calamity Flame. Nine dragons wreathed in fiery enhanced qi shot out violently.

The Sword Saint immediately swung his sword. The entangled space was cut, and the nine dragons vanished without a trace. It was impossible to even offset the attack.

However, he knew. It was a feint, a grand technique to deceive his senses.

The Sword Saint startled as a presence appeared right before him. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break was not only fast and powerful but also stealthy. Executed to its extreme, the Traceless Step could deceive even a Transcendent master, if only for an instant.

He hurriedly twisted his upper body and swung his sword.

GWAANG!

The spear shaft and sword collided. The hasty swing lacked power, and the Sword Saint's body staggered.

*Did he say he was stronger than the Dark Supreme?*

Lee Sungmin's posture dropped low. Fired from below, the Serpent Soul Devouring aimed for the opening he had created. The technique itself was on the weaker side, but when combined with the Blood Ring Shattering, fueled by both Demonic Energy and inner qi, its power became more than enough.

The Sword Saint swelled his Protective Qi Aura, but he could not block it completely. His body launched into the air.

*For me, the Dark Supreme was a more difficult opponent,* Lee Sungmin thought, pressing his advantage. *That ghost-like movement, the hidden weapons from anywhere...*

The Sword Saint was thinking something similar, but from the opposite perspective. *It must be a matter of compatibility. For me, the Dark Supreme is tougher than you.* He had been confident of an eighty percent chance of victory against the Dark Supreme because he had seen his hidden weapons before. They were projectiles, small and not overly powerful. He could have easily intercepted them with his Protective Qi Aura and Spatial Sword.

However, this was different. Lee Sungmin's spear was as fast as a hidden weapon but carried immense weight. It was impossible to block completely, even with his aura. The Sword Saint's sword was unrivaled, but was Lee Sungmin's spear lacking? No. He had invested a long time in the spear, an amount that could compare to anyone in the world. Even without entering the Transcendent Realm, his spear technique had already reached it. His skill in wielding the weapon was not inferior to the Sword Saint's swordsmanship.

That was why the Sword Saint found it so strange. The level of his spearmanship was far higher than his apparent martial prowess suggested. He attacked relentlessly, struck intermittently, slowed down, then sped up, but Lee Sungmin's spear, twisting its trajectory as it struck and retreated, was tricky to deal with.

The Sword Saint furrowed his brow. He had not expected to be forced this far. The Phantom Spear was much stronger than he had thought. He couldn't manipulate space, but his actual skill was already on par with the Transcendent Realm.

Space shimmered.

This time, it was not just the sword. The entire area around the Sword Saint began to waver. Unlike the formless slashes, the Spatial Sword could be detected as the world began to distort.

*It doesn't matter if you see it,* the Sword Saint thought grimly.

*Because it's not something you can avoid.*
