A sadistic pleasure stirred within Lee Sungmin as he watched Spirit tremble. He had remained surprisingly calm when Jeniela's expression had twisted in despair, but this was different. He could not contain his amusement at the sight of the true architect of his suffering. The two could not even be compared. It was true that Jeniela had been incredibly annoying, infuriating even, and had directly caused him all sorts of harm. Spirit, however, had been the puppet master behind all of it. Most of what Lee Sungmin had experienced was a result of Spirit's arrangements.

"What do you intend to do?" Demonic Spirit asked, his voice cautious.

And why wouldn't he be? Lee Sungmin held all the power now, wielding a sword given to him by the master of the Battle God Chronicle itself. This world, finally free from the Slaughterhouse, no longer needed a manager.

"I'm thinking," Lee Sungmin answered, his gaze fixed on the terrified Spirit.

He held no particular ill will toward Demonic Spirit, however. Though the demon's scheme had been predicated on his death, the effort to stop The End had been genuine. Even when his plans went awry, Demonic Spirit had not given up. He had simply been underprepared.

Lee Sungmin knew that even if everything had gone according to plan, the outcome would have been the same. Had Wiji Hoyeon killed him and claimed the library's key, she still would not have changed her fate. The records here, while precious, were not magnificent enough to forcibly alter her fixed destiny or allow her to escape the Cycle of Mortality. Their value did not extend beyond the knowledge they held. If she had remained, clinging to The End, the transcendent beings peeking into this place would have made their Advent and killed her.

This entire situation had unfolded this way only because it was Lee Sungmin, not Wiji Hoyeon, who had arrived. The investors of Eria had been interested in him, the greatest Potential Saint this world could produce.

GOOOOOO!

The white space trembled. A startled Demonic Spirit whipped his head around as lights began to appear, first one, then two, until countless points of brilliance filled the void. Lee Sungmin watched, unfazed, as the souls recorded and left behind in the library were finally liberated from their stagnation.

The shining spirits soared upward, merging into a single, flowing stream that stretched beyond the library, toward a distant place beyond perception. They were heading for the cycle of reincarnation, their natural destination.

Lee Sungmin stood watching blankly as the souls were freed. A few of them broke from the stream and approached him. Though they all looked the same, he felt a sense of familiarity from these particular lights. He greeted them with a gentle smile.

"Thank you," a chorus of blended voices whispered.

They were not allowed much time. A single word was all the freedom the dead were granted here. Within that mixed sound, Lee Sungmin sensed the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Abel, and the Sword Immortal. He gave a slight bow of his head in response.

"…So… rry…"

Envirus's voice was faint, unable to finish before he was swept back into the current of souls and disappeared. A small pang of pity for the man touched Lee Sungmin. He watched until the last of the countless souls that had filled the space faded from view, then finally opened his mouth.

"What do you want me to do with you?" Lee Sungmin asked, his gaze intent on Demonic Spirit.

The former manager hesitated under his stare before speaking.

"…I want to reincarnate," he replied in a small voice. He raised his head to the sky, where the countless souls flowed toward their destination, a river of light like the Milky Way on a clear night.

"You're asking me to kill you, then?"

"Yes… To be honest, now that I've lost my authority and gained a physical body, there's nothing I can do living in this world."

"An ordinary life could be enjoyable, you know."

"For that, I know too much," Demonic Spirit said with a bitter smile. "I have all my memories from when I was a manager. Just because I have a real body doesn't mean they've disappeared. It's too late for me to enjoy the life of a mortal."

What he desired was rest. Eria was no longer a Slaughterhouse but a real world. Now, all souls who died here would flow into the cycle of reincarnation.

"I can erase your memories, if you want."

"No, just kill me," Demonic Spirit insisted, shaking his head. "I want to forget everything. That I was the manager of this world. The countless repetitions I've witnessed. I want to forget it all… enter the cycle of reincarnation, and be born as a single life. That is my wish."

His mind was set. This was the very reason he had tried to end The End in the first place, to escape his inescapable life as a manager. It was the same reason Mushii and Denir had helped Lee Sungmin and used Baek Sogo as an incarnation. Everyone was tired of the repetitive life in the Slaughterhouse.

"Have you thought about killing yourself?"

"That's a bit scary," Demonic Spirit admitted with a faint smile. "I've never died before, you see. The very thought of resolving to kill myself with this body is frightening. I'm terrified of dying slowly. Of course, it might be possible to kill myself so quickly that I wouldn't have time to fear it, but I think it would be better to die by your hand."

"Because you think it'll be painless?"

"That's right." *This is also a form of suicide, in a way,* he muttered, his voice barely a whisper.

Lee Sungmin understood his feelings. He slowly nodded his head.

"Any last words?"

"Tell Wiji Hoyeon I'm sorry," Demonic Spirit replied, a faint smile on his lips. "And… to you, too."

"Don't worry about it," Lee Sungmin said with a chuckle. "It's thanks to you that things turned out this way."

A satisfied laugh escaped Demonic Spirit at that answer. In that same moment, Lee Sungmin's hand moved.

The light vanished from Demonic Spirit's eyes. His body slumped over, and Lee Sungmin caught the falling form. He had died without even realizing it, hearing the answer he wanted in his final moment. Since he hadn't even felt himself dying, there would have been no pain. Lee Sungmin felt the soul leave the body and disappear without hesitation into the great stream crossing the sky.

"…He's gone."

There had been so many trapped souls. The galaxy they formed did not vanish but continued to flow endlessly. Lee Sungmin looked up at it with a sense of awe before slowly lowering his head.

"Hurts, doesn't it?"

He had been hearing groans from below for a while now. She was crying from the pain, trying to do something about it, only to hesitate because it hurt too much, then trying again, knowing it was her only way out.

"You can't die from biting your tongue," Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue, looking down at Spirit.

In the body of the Spirit Medium, she clutched her bleeding mouth with both hands, tears streaming down her face.

"It just hurts, that's all."

He reached a hand toward her. Spirit made a gurgling sound, foaming at the mouth with blood. He couldn't understand a word she was saying. Lee Sungmin grabbed her face and forced her jaw open. The tooth marks on her gushing tongue were clear, but she hadn't even bitten halfway through. He used magic to rinse her mouth before casting a healing spell. The ragged wound, not being a clean cut, healed quickly.

"Ki, kill… kill me…" Spirit pleaded.

Lee Sungmin stared at her, his expression impassive. Suicide by biting her tongue. It was a classic and extreme method, but for her, it was the only release. If she died now, she could enter the cycle of reincarnation just like Demonic Spirit. That was why she tried, but the pain was too much. No matter how she summoned the courage to bite down harder, this new body learned the sensation of pain too easily and too quickly. She just couldn't put any strength into her jaw.

"I have no intention of killing you," Lee Sungmin said, roughly wiping the blood from his hand onto her clothes. "Don't you think it's a bit shameless to ask for that?"

"I-I couldn't help it!" Spirit screamed, her voice pleading, but even she knew she would not be allowed to die so easily. "I-I was nothing more than a manager in the end. I had to do my best to produce the results the investors demanded. I-I was created to be that kind of being in the first place…!"

Lee Sungmin listened without interruption as she continued to yell.

"Demonic Spirit… Demonic Spirit was the strange one. For us managers, such actions should never have existed. N-no. Maybe Demonic Spirit acting that way was also part of the investors' design. In the end, because Demonic Spirit acted that way, we achieved great results…!"

Her words sounded plausible enough. Even if Demonic Spirit himself had been unaware, his defiance had produced a remarkable outcome, but there was no way to know the truth now. Demonic Spirit was dead, his soul already on its way to reincarnation.

"Th-that's right. In the end, we were all just being played. I was just doing my best in my role…! And, and… you… you should be grateful to me. I could have killed you so many times until now… but I didn't…!"

"Because you had to get results."

"But… still… thanks… thanks to me…"

A sense of despair spread across Spirit's face. She knew that no matter what she said, it was impossible to persuade him. Lee Sungmin watched her fall silent and slowly got to his feet.

"Jeniela should have killed you…" Spirit muttered. "You… I should have killed you sooner… By leaving you alive… that's why…! Hmph, I should have just killed you like the Void Monarch or the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Wiji Hoyeon, Baek Sogo, Scarlet, all of them, everyone else too. I should have killed them all… not just let them live on as cripples, but killed them completely…"

"Are you trying to make me angry?" Lee Sungmin asked after she had spat out her final words. "Why? Do you think if I listen to you, I'll lose my temper and kill you in a fit of rage?"

Spirit was at a loss for words.

"Of course, that won't happen," he chuckled. "Even if I did lash out in anger, my control isn't so poor that I'd kill you."

"......"

He watched the light of hope fade from her eyes. *So this is what it feels like.* He thought of Jeniela's soulstone inside his robes. To deliberately give hope, only to watch that ember be extinguished before one's eyes. If one went mad to the point their sense of joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure was broken, they would have no choice but to become addicted to such an act. Lee Sungmin let out a short laugh. The faint, abnormal regret that he hadn't pretended to fall for it disgusted him.

Spirit opened her mouth wide. Realizing there was no other way, she tried to bite her tongue with all her might.

"……"

Just before her teeth could connect, Lee Sungmin's foot shattered her upper and lower teeth and lodged itself in her mouth.

"Aaargh!"

She flailed, grabbing at the foot stuck in her mouth. He slowly applied pressure, breaking her jaw, and pulled his foot out. Spirit clutched her face, sobbing and screaming.

"This is too much," Lee Sungmin grumbled as he looked down at her.

She couldn't endure the pain. It was only natural. As a manager, she had never once possessed a real body. The vessels she borrowed were merely well-moving dolls. Spirit didn't know pain.

"I can't even torture you, it seems," he grumbled again and grabbed her by the hair. Her eyes trembled as she looked up at him. If he tortured her body, her weak mind would simply collapse. That wasn't what he wanted. He felt a small sense of disappointment as he looked into her eyes.

"You know Jeniela well, don't you?"

"......"

"Be her conversation partner."

Spirit's soul was pulled from her body. Lee Sungmin caught it firmly before it could mix with the stream of other souls. He then took out Jeniela's soulstone from his robes.

"You heard everything, didn't you?"

Jeniela's soul stirred violently inside the soulstone.

"The End you wished for never came. Everything you've desired since long ago wasn't your own wish, but Spirit's."

Jeniela's soul let out a scream filled with despair.

"Try to get along from now on."

Spirit's soul flowed into Jeniela's soulstone.

"That place is your hell."

Two souls churned inside the stone. Jeniela's soul hurled curses at Spirit's soul, and Spirit's soul screamed endlessly.

Lee Sungmin put the soulstone back in his robes and looked at the sky. An end was now visible to the galaxy of souls, which had been so vast and long that it had seemed endless. For a while, he stood there, watching the end of that great stream disappear into the cycle of reincarnation.

Even though he had experienced death once, Lee Sungmin didn't know what 'real' death was. He also didn't know what the cycle of reincarnation, where the dead souls were headed, was like. Whatever kind of place it was, it had to be better than remaining as a record in the library, unable to find rest.

Also, Lee Sungmin knew very well. He, who was destined for the Battle God Chronicle, would never head to the cycle of reincarnation.

"…Reincarnation…"

It was a word that had no connection to him. He gave a short laugh and turned around. To be born, summoned to Eria, live, die, and be born in the next world. When you thought about it, the 'beginning' was truly unfair and absurd.

Some are born with a golden spoon in their hand. They were simply lucky enough to be made from the sperm of some rich guy's balls, and lucky enough to be conceived in a rich woman's womb and born. Effort? For guys like that, 'effort' was nothing more than wagging their tails faster than the other sperm to rush forward. Someone else is born with talent. Depending on the case, that can turn a dirt spoon into a golden spoon.

The absurdity of such beginnings applied just the same in this world. Some guys came having learned martial arts, and some guys came having learned magic. The Starting Line was different.

"……"

*I thought I had jack shit.*

I hadn't learned the Void Path or magic. My talent was less than ordinary. Funnily enough, because I was in a position where I had jack shit, I was chosen for the scheme, and now I've come this far. I had jack shit, but I received the Grace of Fate. Because I had no talent, I received it from others. Many things had supported Lee Sungmin to get him here.

"Still," Lee Sungmin muttered as he watched the last of the souls disappear into the cycle of reincarnation. "If possible, be born holding a golden spoon, and with talent, too."

Thinking that was the best prayer he could offer the reincarnating souls, he turned around.

It was time to go outside.
