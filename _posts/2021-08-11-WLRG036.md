Effort.

Lee Sungmin had never particularly liked the word. No matter how hard he applied himself to martial arts theory, he could never hope to match those born with talent. It wasn't as if those gifted individuals neglected to put in any effort themselves.

In other words, their starting points were fundamentally different. Lee Sungmin, lacking talent, began far behind the calamities, while they started leagues ahead of him. Their running speeds were not the same either. When Lee Sungmin crawled, they ran. When Lee Sungmin ran, they rode horses.

That was why he hated the word 'effort'. Trying hard did not guarantee that you could achieve everything, nor that your work would bear fruit.

He hated it, and yet, he admired it.

It wasn't as if he hadn't tried in his past life. He had learned Third-Rate and Second-Rate martial arts and had worked tirelessly to master them. He had strived simply to survive.

And what about his current life?

He could no longer complain about having a different starting point. He had his memories. He had received peerless inner and martial arts after meeting Wiji Hoyeon. He had even enhanced his internal power by consuming the Holy Spirit Pill. He had entered Mount Mushii to dedicate himself to training and had also received martial arts from Baek Sogo.

It was no longer a matter of his starting point.

It was a matter of his running speed.

He needed the strength to endure the weight and the haste to overcome his slowness. He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, willing his body to become lighter and his power stronger. He began with Rannachal, the foundation of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He never just swung his spear. He always performed the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, his movement arts a dizzying dance of steps. Though each step was heavy and slow, he never performed them with anything less than his full commitment.

Effort.

What Lee Sungmin needed now was effort.

His days remained a living hell. The food and water were as disgusting as ever, and he was afraid to fall asleep, for his dreams were always terrible. The weight constantly crushed his body. Without the Fatigue Recovery and Mental Cleaning magic, he would never have been able to endure on this mountain. *No*, he considered, *if I'm being honest, I should first mention Baek Sogo's help when I first arrived.*

Without her aid, Lee Sungmin would not have survived.

Baek Sogo had told him to forget grudges but never forget kindness. That teaching was now deeply engraved in his heart.

He felt an immense gratitude to Scarlet and Baek Sogo and intended to repay the kindness he had received from them, no matter when that day might come. That vow became another goal for him, another reason he could not die, at least not until he had repaid their kindness.

A day passed, then two, then a week, then a month.

Amidst the constant weight, Lee Sungmin's body grew faster. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break, having reached its third level, allowed him to forget the crushing burden while he performed it.

The fundamentals of Rannachal became completely familiar to his body. He took it a step further, becoming able to naturally blend Rannachal into the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. When he finally achieved that, Lee Sungmin collapsed and cried. He felt a long-standing wall in his heart crumble, a barrier he had unknowingly built for himself named 'limitation'.

He came to know joy.

*So this is how you can use a spear. So this is how my body can move.*

Time continued to flow. One month became two. His voice was gone, but Lee Sungmin continued to make sounds. The sound of his spear cutting through the air. The sound of his body moving. Under the crushing weight, he ran without rest. Before fearing the nightmares that came with sleep, he circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, and when he woke from them, he circulated it again.

The inner qi within his qi center continued to grow. The power from Wiji Hoyeon and the Holy Spirit Pill, which had melted into his blood and qi, was gradually guided to his core. With each of Lee Sungmin's breaths, the energy drifting through the mountain accumulated there as well.

"You. You're still here?"

It was nearly a year after Lee Sungmin had entered Mount Mushii.

Flam had come to visit.

Lee Sungmin lowered the spear he had been swinging. He said nothing and simply looked at Flam's face. His impassive expression seemed to displease Flam, who strode toward him.

"I figured you wouldn't even last half a year, but you've held on for a whole year."

Lee Sungmin only watched the approaching man, offering no reply. Perhaps it was because of the growth he had experienced over the past year, but he could vaguely sense the level of skill Flam possessed.

*Calamity...*

A monster of that caliber. The level Flam had reached was so high that the current Lee Sungmin could not even imagine it. At that realization, he felt awe before he felt despair.

"For someone with no great talent, you seem to have a lot of grit. No, that's not it either. Being ignorant isn't the same as having grit. If Baek Sogo noona hadn't helped you, you would've died on this mountain long before now."

Flam spat, the corner of his mouth twisting. Even after hearing his words, Lee Sungmin felt no desire to argue. When he thought about it, Flam was right, so why bother refuting him? It was true that a year ago, when he first came to this mountain, he had been both ignorant and reckless.

"Are you ignoring me?" Flam shouted.

Lee Sungmin silently swung his spear, starting again from Rannachal.

*Look at this...?*

Flam's eyes widened. The Rannachal Lee Sungmin performed was incomparably cleaner than what he had seen half a year ago. It was not just the Rannachal. The harmony between the spear technique he executed and the movement arts he incorporated was quite plausible.

"So even a dullard can improve if he keeps at it," Flam sneered.

Lee Sungmin let him talk, continuing his spear technique with an indifferent expression. The one year he had spent on this mountain was worth more than twice the thirteen years he had spent in his past life.

"But with five restrictions, is that all you've got?" Flam giggled.

The restrictions increased their rewards according to their number and severity. Flam himself had told him that.

"Pathetic. Should I teach you instead? Huh?"

Flam's laughter grew louder. To Lee Sungmin's ears, his words sounded distant. Focusing on his spear technique, his mind had already melted into the trajectory of his weapon.

As Lee Sungmin ignored him and continued to perform his spear technique, a look of irritation crept onto Flam's face. He ground his teeth and took a large step forward.

"......"

Flam's body slipped into the path of Lee Sungmin's swinging spear. Before the startled Lee Sungmin could stop his weapon, Flam's hand shot out.

"......"

Flam's hand caught the spear shaft.

"Why are you ignoring me?" he spat, glaring at Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin sighed and pulled his spear back. As he reached for the twig at his waist to answer, Flam shook his head vigorously.

"Just say it. I can tell by the shape of your lips."

He had quite a talent. Lee Sungmin thought as much while mouthing his words.

Why are you the one bothering me?

"Because I want to."

Then ignoring you is my choice too.

"No. That's not allowed. Someone like you isn't allowed to ignore me."

*What a shitty personality.* Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh.

I don't really want to talk to you.

"Why? Does talking to me make you feel miserable about your pathetic talent?"

Not really. Your talent is yours. There's no end to it if I start feeling despair over something I don't have.

"What's with the enlightened act? It doesn't suit you," Flam sneered. He let go of Lee Sungmin's spear and took a few steps back. Then, as if to show off, he raised both his hands. "Look. You can't do this, can you?"

A pure white light of martial arts theory enveloped Flam's hands. It was something Lee Sungmin had seen several times before. Sword ki.

"Isn't the world so unfair? You and I are about the same age, but I can do almost everything you can't. No matter how hard you try with martial arts theory, in the end, you're just a slug crawling a little faster."

Flam laughed loudly. Lee Sungmin stared quietly at his face. *This bastard. Why does he keep acting like such an ass?* Lee Sungmin tilted his head, watching Flam with a slanted gaze before mouthing his words.

It doesn't matter.

"Liar."

At Lee Sungmin's reply, Flam laughed loudly again. After a while, he dispersed the sword ki surrounding his hands and shook his head.

"Meaningless effort. If you keep trying, you'll only feel despair at your own incompetence. Go on. Keep at it. It seems like it'll be more fun to watch you fall into despair."

Still laughing, Flam turned around. Lee Sungmin paid him no more mind. As Flam left, Lee Sungmin moved his spear again.

With the surrounding noise gone, it was quiet. The silence was not lonely, but familiar. He focused on the trajectory of his swinging spear. The weapon he wielded had no talent, but it had longing. Lee Sungmin longed for more speed, longed for more lightness. No. In the end, what he wanted to imbue in his spear was complete freedom.

He wanted to pour everything he knew of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear into his weapon, unbound by weight or speed.

He felt his goals were trivial.

How low and trivial were the goals he had set in his heart? To surpass Wiji Hoyeon. So what? If he surpassed her, what would he do after that? Such thoughts also scattered and disappeared from his mind.

In the end, nothing remained. No, one thing did. A longing for martial arts that he never had in his past life remained in his mind. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Violet Dawn Divine Art, the Dancing Shadow Soul Break... The three peerless divine arts and all the martial arts he had learned drifted through his thoughts.

And so another day passed. Two days passed, a week passed, and a month passed.

Mushii came to him. When told to choose a new restriction, Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation.

Hearing.

Static began to buzz in his ears. It was a painful restriction, and falling asleep became even more difficult. To make up for his lack of sleep, Lee Sungmin swung his spear and devoted himself to his martial arts. In fact, he preferred it that way. When he swung his spear and circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, he could let go of the torment of the static.

Scarlet and Dok Bijun left the mountain, having apparently obtained what they wanted from the year or more they had spent there. Lee Sungmin's world became even quieter. There was only one time he could escape that quietness. When he was with Baek Sogo. Or, on the rare occasion Flam came to visit.

Flam seemed to dislike Lee Sungmin. Every time he visited, he would pick a fight over some strange thing, and Lee Sungmin would ignore him. Ignoring him was easy. After all, he could not hear. Since Lee Sungmin ignored him so thoroughly, Flam eventually stopped visiting.

Silence.

Lee Sungmin woke from his sleep.

He had a nightmare, as always. He no longer screamed, but he still could not fall back asleep right away. He picked up his spear, as always. He walked through the forest for a bit, as always, and stopped at the clearing he always used as a training ground.

Baek Sogo was there. A year they had spent together. Lee Sungmin had grown in that year, but Baek Sogo had not. If anything, she had become a little gaunter than she was a year ago.

She pushed herself up. Just as Lee Sungmin had taken on two more restrictions, so had she. Baek Sogo's left eye could no longer see. Endless static now buzzed in her ears, just like in his.

[Did you have a nightmare?]

Lee Sungmin nodded at her question. He raised his spear.

And then, as he always had, he swung it.
