WAAAAAAH!

A chaotic roar erupted from the streets as a wave of martial artists surged toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The most skilled among them vaulted into the air or blurred into sprints, leading the charge. He saw no righteous spirit in their charge, only foolishness.

With an annoyed flick of his right hand, the ground rumbled and overturned.

RUMBLE!

As soil and stone heaved upward, he flexed his left hand, then slapped his palm against the rising curtain of dirt.

"Uwaaaack!"

His palm strike transformed the tiny grains of sand into a storm of hidden weapons that tore through flesh. Cries of agony filled the air as the charging warriors were impaled by countless projectiles. They collapsed in twitching heaps, blood streaming from their bodies to stain the earth.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman walked past the fallen, his hands clasped behind his back.

"Did you kill them all?"

"The unlucky ones are probably dead."

As he emerged onto the main road, he found it blocked by yet another crowd of martial artists. This group, however, did not attack so recklessly. They were not the same fools who had charged in blindly despite knowing their opponent's identity. They had witnessed the fate of the first wave and at least understood their own limitations.

In his eyes, however, there was no real difference between them and the fools already struck down.

"It's just the difference between being early and being late," he mused aloud, striding forward without hesitation.

He was inwardly dissatisfied. He had come here personally, under the pretext of instilling a sense of awe in his disciple, but his true intention was to demonstrate the essence of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. He had offered a few demonstrations, yet he felt they were insufficient. The problem was his opponents. They were simply too weak. To make his idiotic disciple truly understand, he needed a challenge, someone of a certain level to properly engage. With such weaklings, there was nothing to properly showcase.

"You lot won't do," he declared, his voice carrying over the crowd as he continued his unhurried pace. He didn't even bother to unclasp his hands from behind his back. They weren't worth the effort.

Lee Sungmin watched his master's back as he advanced. The Chairman's robes billowed and swelled as if caught in a strong wind, though the air was still. His figure shimmered like a heat haze on a sweltering summer day.

CRACKLE, CRACKLE...

Black currents of electricity sparked ceaselessly around him. Though not a large man, to Lee Sungmin, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's back seemed more immense than anyone else's in the world.

"Bring me your Chairman," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman declared.

His words sent a ripple of terror through the crowd. Those who could not overcome their fear screamed, and a few had already collapsed, foaming at the mouth. The ones who could still control their bodies gave up on fighting and began a hesitant retreat.

"It's the Righteous Heaven Martial Arts Corps!"

"Righteous Hero Sword is here!"

"Master Gale Flying Sword!"

Shouts tinged with hope suddenly erupted from the terrified people. The Righteous Heaven Martial Arts Corps, one of the Murim Alliance's most renowned armed groups in Kron, had been dispatched. The crowd cheered, calling out the names of the masters as they cut through the terrified martial artists and approached the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"...The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?" the leader of the Corps, Righteous Hero Sword, called out.

The Chairman sneered with open ridicule. "The murim has really improved, hasn't it? To think a brat like you is called a great hero."

Righteous Hero Sword's face stiffened at the insult. He was a Supreme Peak master, not a man to be called a brat anywhere he went.

"I believe I told you to bring me your Chairman," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, his brow furrowing. "Or are you telling me I have to go find him myself?"

As if to punctuate his words, lightning from the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder spread out in all directions.

*He's insane.*

Chwi Geol was in Kron. He shouldn't have been here at all. He was supposed to be under disciplinary confinement, a punishment for unilaterally abandoning his post during the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun. As leader of the Pure White Martial Squad, he had simply left his subordinates in the forest. That decision had led to the madness-tainted Moyong Patriarch leading the remnants to attack the Mages Guild. With the Patriarch dead, the blame had fallen on the young master, ruining the Moyong Clan and leaving Moyong Chan's whereabouts unknown.

Naturally, responsibility had also fallen upon Chwi Geol. He was stripped of his position in the Murim Alliance and disciplined by his sect, the Beggars' Gang. Still, he remained the Young Sect Leader. This was partly because no other young martial artist in the Beggars' Gang was as talented as he was, and partly because he had already mastered the non-fighting spirit martial art that only the Chief could learn.

His punishment was confinement to the beggars' den at the Main Branch. It was a disciplinary measure for show, and Chwi Geol hadn't complained. Even with his worsened situation, life as the Young Sect Leader was not uncomfortable.

At this very moment, however, Chwi Geol resented that punishment. He shouldn't have been in Kron. Who could have possibly imagined that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who should have been holed up in his own territory, would come to the Murim Alliance's home and cause such a ruckus?

"What are you doing?" his Master, the Martial Beggar and Chief of the Beggars' Gang, snapped.

Chwi Geol, who had been hesitating to attach the Dog Beating Stick to his waist, glanced up. He had never resented his Master before, but in this moment, he couldn't help it.

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is a monster of the Transcendent Realm," he said, his words a veiled plea. "In the entire murim, you can count the number of martial artists who can handle him on one hand."

"That's probably true," the Martial Beggar agreed. "Besides the Wudang Sword Immortal Elder, there is likely no one who can stop him. In this old man's opinion, it's doubtful even the Chairman could stop him if he stepped forward himself."

The answer only frustrated Chwi Geol more. He even questioned if the Black Dragon Knight, another Transcendent Realm master, could stop the man. Knowing that, why was his master acting so recklessly, insisting on going to stop him? Chwi Geol barely managed to swallow the words that had risen to his throat.

Yes, he knew the reason. The Beggars' Gang had valued righteousness and chivalry since ancient times. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the pinnacle of demonic power, was on a rampage in Kron. Warriors from minor sects and the Murim Alliance, burning with a sense of justice, had already suffered for trying to stop him. It was unforgivable. The Murim Alliance in Kron was a symbol of the orthodox sects. If they stood by while the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman rampaged, their prestige would be dragged through the mud. Worse, martial artists with courage and justice in their hearts were being killed.

*Isn't it wiser to survive and plan for the future?* he thought, genuinely believing it. *If you die, everything is over.*

He looked down at the jet-black Dog Beating Stick hanging from his waist with a bitter expression. This wasn't a stick for beating common dogs. It was a weapon for putting down an evil worse than any dog. Around him, a hundred other members of the Beggars' Gang, including the Chief himself, held identical black sticks. They were the elite, the masters chosen from countless beggars, capable of deploying the Great Dog Beating Stick Formation that was the pride of their sect, and he, the Young Sect Leader, was the youngest among them.

"It might be dangerous, but..." Chwi Geol tried one last time. *Let's not be reckless. Let's just run away. Or pretend we don't know anything.* That was what he wanted to say, but he couldn't speak his mind honestly. He was already on thin ice for abandoning the Pure White Martial Squad. If he revealed such thoughts now, he worried his qi center might be shattered and his position revoked.

"That may be so," the Martial Beggar replied, "but we can't just leave him be, can we?"

*Please, Master,* Chwi Geol begged silently, *distinguish between chivalry and recklessness.* He pressed his lips together, swallowing the words.

Unaware of his disciple's intentions, or perhaps pretending not to know, the Martial Beggar no longer looked at him. He wore a bitter expression as he commanded the assembled members.

"Let's go catch a dog."

His voice lacked vigor, but it was unwavering.

It hadn't even taken a minute for the so-called great heroes of the Righteous Heaven Martial Arts Corps to be sent tumbling through the air, spewing blood. Lee Sungmin recalled asking the Chairman about the state of soulmates in the past. The Chairman had called it an illusion, explaining that Sungmin was unconsciously using Dragon's Pressure, yet the display he had just witnessed was more than enough to be called soulmates. Without performing a single martial art, his master had made his opponents collapse, bleeding from internal injuries.

"Isn't that soulmates?"

"They were just frightened by my internal energy and gave themselves internal injuries," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was in the middle of answering when a new sound shook the air.

AH, AH, AH, AAAAAH!

It was a great shout, a mix of dozens of voices yet unified and unbroken. It was followed by the sharp CLACK, CLACK, CLACKACLACK of wood on wood and the WHOOSH, WHOOOOSH of sticks splitting the wind. The chaotic noise seemed to soften the annoyed expression on the Chairman's face.

"Do you know about the Dog Beating Stick Formation?" he asked.

"I only know that it's a formation of the Beggars' Gang."

"There are two types," the Chairman explained, a satisfied smile appearing on his face. "The regular one, deployed by thirty-four people, and the Great Dog Beating Stick Formation, deployed by a hundred. They wouldn't use the regular one on me, and judging by the sound, this must be the Great one."

He continued in an excited voice. "I have a small dream. No, it's not even worth calling a dream. Let's just say it's something I've wanted to try once if the opportunity ever arose..."

"What is that?"

"There are four ultimate formations in the orthodox murim. Wudang's Tai Chi Sword Formation. Shaolin's Arhat Formation. Mount Hua's Plum Blossom Sword Formation, and the Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation. I've never had the chance to face them, but I've always hoped that if I got the chance, I would shatter all those formations by myself. Today, the opportunity has come to smash the Dog Beating Stick Formation, one of the four."

As he spoke, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed as if he were genuinely enjoying himself. "The Dog Beating Stick Formation deployed by a hundred people consists of one commander, thirty-three attackers, thirty-three defenders, and thirty-three disorienters. That's what I know of it, but this is my first time experiencing it directly."

"What are the thirty-three disorienters?"

"The sound. They clash their Dog Beating Sticks together, they shout. They swing their sticks to make noise. That cacophony, filled with inner arts, acts as a powerful sound art in tune with the formation's flow. Just by hearing it, those with weak internal energy can't withstand it and suffer internal injuries. Also, if you enter the formation and face the sound arts head-on, your senses will be scrambled and the flow of your qi will be twisted."

"Cover your ears!" the fallen martial artists shouted.

The Dog Beating Stick Formation had not yet begun, but the sound they heard had already crossed the boundary of unpleasant noise and was shaking their qi. They hurriedly covered their ears and calmed their agitated internal energy. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, however, did not. If he was going to face the formation anyway, it was impossible to endure it just by covering his ears.

THUMP, THUMP, THUMP. A hundred members of the Beggars' Gang, approaching with unified steps, came to a halt. The Chairman, who had been waiting with his hands behind his back, smiled warmly at the Martial Beggar standing at the very front.

"Are you the current Beggars' Gang Chief?"

"...I am."

"Is the Beggar King dead?"

"My Master passed away several decades ago."

"Is that so? Well, he lived a long life for his kind."

Hearing the casual dismissal, the Martial Beggar gripped his Dog Beating Stick tightly. He ignored the goosebumps rising on his arms. The man before him looked like a robust man in his thirties at most, but in reality, he was a monster who had lived for nearly three hundred years. A monster in human form. Of course, mastering martial arts to a high realm could slow aging, but even a Supreme Peak master could only live for a little over a hundred years, and yet, this man hadn't aged a day.

"Monster..."

"I'm still human," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman joked as he strode forward. "I've lived for hundreds of years, but I've never experienced the Dog Beating Stick Formation. The Dog Beating Stick Formation, the Plum Blossom Sword Formation, the Arhat Formation, and the Tai Chi Sword Formation are commonly cited as the ultimate formations of the orthodox sects... So. Beggars' Gang Chief. Which of the orthodox sects' ultimate formations do you think is the most outstanding?"

"The Dog Beating Stick Formation."

"Excellent. To think that the first ultimate formation I shatter will be the best formation of the orthodox sects. What are you waiting for? Hurry up and deploy the formation," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman urged.

The Martial Beggar raised his Dog Beating Stick high. That was the signal.

FWIP-FWIP-FWIP!

The ninety-nine members waiting behind him moved in perfect unison to prepare the formation. In the process, only the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Lee Sungmin, and the members of the Beggars' Gang were left in the middle of the main road. The rest had retreated far away, afraid of being caught up in the chaos.

*The Phantom Spear.*

Chwi Geol was in charge of disorientation. This was so he could quickly take over as commander if the Chief were to die. He was relieved to be in this role. It was, after all, the safest place to be when dealing with the monster known as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

THUMP, THUMP, THUMP. The thirty-three members of the disorientation squad, including Chwi Geol, stomped their feet on the ground. They paired up to clash their Dog Beating Sticks and chanted in unison. Those without a partner swung their sticks fiercely, creating a melody with the sound of the wind.

"Formation!" the Martial Beggar roared.

The jet-black Dog Beating Stick in his hand shot high into the air. Thirty-three attackers, thirty-three defenders. The defense turned to offense. An attack by sixty-six began.

The very atmosphere shimmered as the disorientation of the Dog Beating Stick Formation encroached upon space. A master of the Transcendent Realm could distort space on their own, but this formation did so with the combined power of a hundred masters. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman felt the tremor of the shaking space and raised both hands, his eyes filled with anticipation.

"Watch closely," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said to Lee Sungmin. "So that even an incompetent fool like you can understand what awe is. I will show you the essence of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder."

The Dog Beating Stick Formation began.

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?"

The Black Dragon Knight's face turned deathly pale as he heard the report. Forgetting his dignity in front of his attendant, he covered his face with both hands.

*Why is this happening to me?*

The wound he'd received from Ju Won in the northern forest hadn't even fully healed. The more he thought about it, the more unfair and infuriating it felt. If only the Spear King hadn't pulled that stunt. Or if he had at least stayed to join forces instead of leaving. He wouldn't have had such a hard time with that damn Lycanthrope, and he wouldn't have had to make such a disgraceful escape.

"M-My Lord Chairman. What should we..."

"Shut up," the Black Dragon Knight spat, his voice seething. "I said shut up because I don't know either."

He let out a sigh of pure frustration. Strangely, he felt like he was about to cry.
