A sudden spear thrust snapped through the Martial God's offensive train of thought, startling him as he dashed through the air.

He twisted his body mid-stride and swung his lone hand to meet it.

KAAANG!

The impact of his hand colliding with the Formless Spear sent a jarring weight through his palm, and his expression hardened.

As the Formless Spear dissipated, Lee Sungmin glanced at its fading remnants and bent his knees. This was the moment he had been waiting for. Whatever the reason, the Martial God was his Master's enemy. The man might be a puppet of the Spirit, but that did not change the fact that he had killed the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Worse, he had insulted the Chairman's death, shown no respect. For that alone, Lee Sungmin intended to kill him. The Martial God, puppet of the Spirit, his Master's nemesis.

Lee Sungmin rose onto the balls of his feet, feeling the blatant stares directed at him. Thanks to the Martial God's earlier shouts, the soldiers on the fortress walls regarded him with open hostility, and a crowd of uneasy onlookers had gathered beyond the city gate.

Then, the Martial God appeared, materializing from thin air like a ghost. Lee Sungmin recognized the movement arts immediately, the same elusive style used by the Dark Supreme. As the originator of the technique, the Martial God moved with even more brilliance than he had during his fight with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman ten years prior.

A part of him, the memory of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman he now possessed, felt a flicker of satisfaction at the display, but Lee Sungmin himself felt only killing intent.

FWHOOSH!

Dozens of blows were exchanged in a single instant. Though the Martial God had only one arm, his attacks were so exquisite it was difficult to believe they came from a single limb.

Lee Sungmin glanced down at the Formless Spear in his hand with a bored expression. The spearhead and shaft were already mangled, crumbling and dissipating.

With a dramatic flourish of his sleeve, the Martial God held out an open palm toward Lee Sungmin.

"Come," he demanded, his glare fixed on Lee Sungmin. "A master is supposed to correct his disciple's sins, but yours has been dead for more than a decade. It's my duty to defeat you, the calamity of The End, but before I kill you... I'll teach you just how wrong your path truly is."

"Haha..."

Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh at the scolding. The man's utter seriousness was becoming comical, and with every word, his own irritation grew stronger. The Martial God kept bringing up the Chairman's death, but what right did he have to speak of it so boldly?

*Is he trying to unsettle me?* If so, the man was as wicked and cunning as a snake.

"Aren't you going to attack together?"

"This one alone is enough," the Martial God spat forcefully.

He had every reason to be confident. The Protective Qi Aura enveloping his body was so solid it seemed to have no gaps, and it certainly did not look easy to pierce. Even with only one arm, its absence was not a significant handicap for a master of his caliber, partly due to the nature of the martial arts he had trained in. He was a master who had reached the pinnacle of illusions.

Even if they were illusions without substance, it was a different story when the Martial God wielded them. His hand began to move slowly, its movement creating dozens of afterimages that each took a different stance as they targeted Lee Sungmin.

"As for my lecture to you."

Lee Sungmin felt like applauding the sheer foolishness of it all. If left alone, just how high would that ego soar? Watching it might have been amusing under other circumstances, but he was in too foul a mood to indulge him. He had forgotten in his anger, but it had not even been thirty minutes since he had pulled himself from that cesspool. His mood was already rotten, and the Martial God's speeches were like pouring oil on a raging fire.

"So," Lee Sungmin muttered in a low voice as the dozens of phantoms began to move, each one imbued with tangible power.

Facing dozens of different fist techniques, Lee Sungmin raised a newly formed spear. The verses of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear filled his mind while the incantations of the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder stirred his inner qi.

PANG.

A small sound echoed, the sound of a single spear thrust. The extended spearhead collided with the fist of one of the Martial God's phantoms, which vanished without resistance as if it were only natural. Feeling a slight pushback, Lee Sungmin took a step forward, and the spear extended that much farther.

POPOPOPOK.

In an instant, all the afterimages were gone.

"......"

The Martial God let out an involuntary sound.

KWWAAAAANG!

A delayed thunderclap finally erupted, and in that same instant, he hastily twisted his torso. A portion of his Protective Qi Aura dissipated. It was only a graze, not a direct hit, yet the force sent his body spinning. He gritted his teeth and kicked off the air to regain his balance.

Lee Sungmin did not retreat as the Martial God approached. Instead, he moved with fluid grace, his spear held low.

CRACKLE.

As electricity sparked along its length, the spear was already flying far ahead.

KAAANG!

A tremendous roar shook the heavens and the earth. The Formless Spear vanished on impact, and the Martial God's body was thrown backward. His Protective Qi Aura fluctuated violently. Startled by the heavy impact, he stared at Lee Sungmin with a hardened expression.

Just then, Lee Sungmin was already charging toward him, a new Formless Spear in hand. He was not moving particularly fast, but his advance felt unavoidable.

Lee Sungmin's attack began before the Martial God could even react. A single swing of his spear filled the space with hundreds of blades of enhanced qi.

"You bastard...!"

The Martial God's eyes flew open as he swept a wide palm strike through the area. The very axis of space twisted under his power.

KRRRRK!

A black fissure opened between them, and Lee Sungmin's attack vanished into the gap. The Martial God's hair stood on end.

"Such power from a Demonic Spirit!"

"Yeah," Lee Sungmin muttered in response. There was no need to deny it.

Just as the Martial God said, the power he wielded was that of a Demonic Spirit. It was violent Demonic Energy, not pure inner qi, and his achievements were not solely the result of his own efforts but of repeated miracles, coincidences, and fate. He did not find it particularly shameful. It was true that he had possessed no talent. He had struggled desperately against that lack of talent and had obtained his current power through layers of blessings, coincidences, and miraculous encounters.

"Still, don't look down on it."

What was unpleasant was the man's underestimation of him. Lee Sungmin released the spear in his hand.

"It's true that it's the power of a Demonic Spirit, but that doesn't mean my martial arts theory is lacking. Or have you forgotten after ten years?"

Perhaps he simply had not considered it. The Martial God did not know Lee Sungmin, after all. If that was the case, then he would just have to show him.

Lee Sungmin pushed lightly off his toes, his floating body becoming engulfed in violet electricity.

*Ah... this is...*

The Martial God's eyes wavered violently. He knew this technique. Before he realized it, he was standing in the eye of a storm, the entire world's scenery distorting around him. The storm was composed of countless bolts of lightning.

The Martial God whipped his head around, searching for Lee Sungmin, but he was nowhere to be seen. There was only the lightning that now filled the space.

"Myraid Thunder."

A voice echoed from a distance.

KZZZZZZZT!

A bolt of lightning shot out and struck the Martial God. He sucked in a sharp breath and raised his Protective Qi Aura to its absolute peak, a brilliant blue light shielding his body. Myraid Thunder had only just begun. One bolt after another exploded, targeting him relentlessly.

"Aaaaaah!"

The Martial God roared, raising his right hand high. The memory of the fight ten years ago surfaced. It was the same as back then. No, it was not the same. This was not the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and he was not the same Martial God from a decade ago. The seclusion he had undergone after that humiliating victory had elevated him to an even higher realm.

KRRRRRUMBLE!

The palm strike he unleashed collided with a bolt from Myraid Thunder, scattering the lightning. With fire in his eyes, the Martial God plunged into the storm.

Within the storm, Lee Sungmin smiled faintly. He held both hands out in front of him as if to welcome the charging Martial God. A small sphere formed between his palms, a violet orb that grew larger as it devoured the surrounding Demonic Energy. The power filling the sphere swirled without pause, sparks of electricity crackling from its surface. With wide eyes, Lee Sungmin launched the sphere just as the Martial God threw a punch.

FWHOOSH!

The greatly enlarged sphere filled the Martial God's vision.

"A thing like this...!"

He threw his fist.

KABOOM!

The orb of light exploded, and the immense force threw the Martial God's body back. Watching the sight, Lee Sungmin chuckled darkly.

Swift Storm Extinction.

His body became a streak of lightning. The Martial God gasped and began to reconstruct his Protective Qi Aura, but Lee Sungmin's attack was faster.

"......"

The Formless Spear struck the incomplete aura. The Martial God bit his lip hard, and as his body was pushed back, Lee Sungmin's spear shot forward in pursuit.

*It's different from back then.*

The Martial God gritted his teeth as he watched the spear fly toward him. The coppery taste of blood rose in his throat, its metallic tang sharpening his mind. His hand moved to his chest. A halo of light shone from behind him, and from it, formless hands materialized, looking just like the Thousand-Armed Bodhisattva extending all its arms.

The Martial God believed in himself. Ten years ago, this would have been impossible, but not now. Through a decade of seclusion, he had escaped the tormenting shadow of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and surpassed him. For ten years, he had endlessly, continuously, replayed the fight in the Snowfield. He always got stuck at the final moment, trying to understand the Chairman's techniques. He had imagined it over and over, wondering if he could kill the man if he were in that situation again. He had constantly sought an answer. His left arm, severed by the Chairman, had ached with excruciating pain for ten long years of his seclusion. The arm that was not even there had felt as real as the other. When he finally emerged from the cliff cave, the Martial God had found the answer he craved, and the pain in his left arm was gone.

So, it was different. It had to be. He had found his answer, overcome his past, and grown stronger.

*Really?*

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The Thousand-Armed Bodhisattva collapsed. Blood sprayed from between his tightly clenched lips as the Martial God flailed his arm desperately. Space fractured, spewing a torrent of blue light forward, but even under that murderous barrage of enhanced qi, Lee Sungmin did not retreat. The Formless Spear in his hand annihilated the Martial God's enhanced qi with a simple movement. A muscle in the Martial God's cheek twitched. It could not be.

That bastard was not even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*I... found the answer...*

His left arm hurt.

The non-existent limb ached as if it were on fire, being torn apart and shattered all at once. The pain shook the Martial God to his core. *This can't be happening.*

He did not know. Did the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman even die back then? The doubt he had felt during his seclusion had vanished at some point. When the Spirit Medium had asked if he had found his answer, the Martial God had replied, 'In my own way'. He had no choice but to give that answer. What else could he have said when he had not found a proper one? He still did not understand the Chairman's techniques, and he could not defeat him even in his imagination. That was right. He had simply run away without finding an answer.

In ten years of seclusion, the Martial God had not achieved anything significant. He had made some progress, it was true, but it amounted to nothing more than this.

The Martial God held his breath, watching the violet electricity that filled the air and the mocking face of Lee Sungmin beyond it. The man before him was not the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

That fact made the Martial God feel even more miserable.

"I, I..."

His face twisted. The mask he had stitched together with his pride was shattering.
