Baek Sogo was unaware of just how lucky she was.

Compared to Geuk Cheondo or the Peerless Eccentric, who had been slain without a proper fight by Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger, her fortune was undeniable. The creature she faced was the Doppelganger of Geuk Cheondo, one of the very Murim Alliance members who had entered this place alongside her.

Among the five from the Alliance, Geuk Cheondo was the least skilled, whereas Baek Sogo's own abilities were the most outstanding. This disparity made defeating his Doppelganger a simple affair. Her luck, however, ran deeper still, for she had not just stumbled upon the creature out of the blue. Baek Sogo had witnessed the entire process of its creation, which allowed her to grasp, at least to some extent, how this Dungeon operated.

*Doppelganger… I can't believe I'd encounter one in a place like this.*

Most martial artists, particularly Otherworlders who had been summoned from murim, were laymen in fields beyond combat. They held an overflowing pride in the world they had left behind and a conviction that the art of fighting was supreme. Baek Sogo, in that respect, was a rare and rational exception. She possessed enough knowledge to be considered well versed in skills other than martial arts.

*A normal Doppelganger only mimics appearances, but this one even uses martial arts. The combat itself was clumsy, but… its techniques weren't significantly weaker than Geuk Cheondo's own.*

She knew Geuk Cheondo's martial arts well, for they belonged to the same Murim Alliance. They had even dueled several times after she received the mission to monitor Wiji Hoyeon. While Geuk Cheondo was not a particularly outstanding master compared to the rest of their group, his passion for martial arts was genuine, and for that, Baek Sogo held a favorable impression of him.

*This Dungeon is dangerous.*

The fallen Doppelganger did not stir. Monsters in a Dungeon typically left behind loot, so she reached down and began to rummage through its flesh. Searching the fleshy lump that had, only moments ago, worn Geuk Cheondo's face was a grim task, yet her hands moved without hesitation, picking through the remains.

"…a potion?"

Buried in the sticky mass was a palm-sized glass bottle. She picked it up and shook it lightly, watching the brilliant purple liquid swirl inside. Though she had accumulated a fair amount of knowledge, she could not identify its contents. In such cases, the surest method was to drink it or have someone else do so, but that was also the most foolish.

Baek Sogo reached into the Subspace Pocket at her waist. She withdrew an artifact purchased with her meager Murim Alliance salary, a device that had cost her over half a year of scrimping on living expenses. It looked like a crude magnifying glass, but its function was invaluable.

*It's an Elixir. Few impurities, too… An Elixir of this quality is on par with a high Priest's blessing.*

Of course, an Elixir was not a cure-all. It could reattach a severed arm if the limb was present, but it could not regenerate one from nothing. Even so, Elixirs were considered top-tier among all potions. Baek Sogo carefully placed it into her Subspace Pocket.

"…Well… then…"

She stood lost in thought, her gaze fixed on the winding path of flesh. Which way was forward and which was back? She could not tell if the space itself had shifted or if she had been moved to another place entirely. She did not even know which way was front or back.

*Where are the others?*

For now, Baek Sogo decided to simply walk where her feet took her.

{sep}

Jang Deuksu, the man responsible for turning the Dungeon into this foul-smelling path of flesh, was proving to be incredibly talkative. He walked beside Lee Sungmin, loudly expressing his admiration for the technique Lee Sungmin had used to kill Baek Sogo's Doppelganger while rambling on with his own martial theories.

[He's noisy.]

Lee Sungmin silently agreed with Rubia. *Should I just kill him and leave?* The thought had crossed his mind, but he did not enjoy killing. It was a piece of humanity he had desperately clung to during the 2100 years he spent in the Mental World. His mind had collapsed and been rebuilt countless times, but this principle remained.

He did not hesitate to kill, but he did not enjoy it. He killed when he had no choice, when he must. Otherwise, he did not. Jang Deuksu was talkative, but not to the point of deserving death. Besides, he was a peak master. In a Dungeon where anything could happen, there was no harm in moving together.

The Magic Armor hummed.

*What is it now?*

[It's a simple curiosity.]

The Void Monarch, who had been quiet until now, seemed a little relieved that Lee Sungmin had not reacted too harshly to his humming. He clearly wanted to talk.

[You. Why don't you eat it?]

*So that's what this is about.* Lee Sungmin chuckled. He knew the object of the Monarch's curiosity. It was the loot from the Doppelganger's corpse, a Magic Gemstone, a treasure that could only be found in Dungeons and was impossible to refine artificially. It was the most efficient miracle herb in this world. Normally, even the finest miracle herb had a limit to the inner arts or magic power a user could absorb, no matter if they were a master with an outstanding inner arts technique or a high-level mage.

The Magic Gemstone, however, was different. It looked like a fist-sized gem, but it would melt in the mouth and be absorbed directly into the body. No inner arts technique or magic was needed. The moment it was consumed, power was added without any side effects. A martial artist's inner arts would be enhanced, and a mage's magic power would grow.

*My body is unstable. If I eat it for no reason, my body could break down.*

[Probably not.]

The Void Monarch replied nonchalantly.

*…What do you mean?*

[Although I failed to possess you, I was able to inhabit this armor and observe your body up close. You are certainly unstable, but for someone in your condition, your body is incredibly sturdy.]

*Sturdy?*

[There's something strange inside your body. Are you aware of it? Uhaha! A non-human heart in a human body. I, the great yokai Void Monarch, can guarantee that among those called great yokai, none had a heart as sturdy and outstanding as yours. Excluding myself, of course.]

[Showing off when you don't even have a body.]

Rubia grumbled, but the Void Monarch paid her no mind. Lee Sungmin was lost in thought for a moment. The disharmony of his body. It was a result of being unable to properly embody the martial insights he had gained. His mind was ahead of his vessel. The Black Heart could amplify his lacking ki as much as needed, but his body could not properly execute what his mind had seen and experienced. The 2100 years in the Mental World had made that disharmony severe.

He still had the Great Cycle Pill from Grandmaster Bulyeong stored in his Subspace Pocket. He had not taken it yet, worried that needlessly inflating his inner arts would only worsen the disharmony of his mind, ki, and body, and cause him to fall into qi deviation.

*But he says my body is sturdy. It's because of the heart. I can't just blindly trust him, though.*

Until he received a definite answer from Preskan, he had no intention of taking the Void Monarch's word for it. This was the same being who had tried to steal his body.

*By the way, Void Monarch. It's been a while since you left the Sleeping Forest and inhabited the Magic Armor. Why did you want to leave the forest?*

[What's with that question? I should have already answered that in the forest, no? I don't know. The only thing I knew was that I had to leave the forest with you.]

*The bastard who said he'd leave with me tried to steal my body?*

[It feels quite strange to be called a bastard by a young whelp.]

*I've lived for 2100 years, you bastard.* Lee Sungmin bit back the retort.

[It's a powerful suggestion. I don't know what great being placed this suggestion on me, the great yokai Void Monarch… but it wouldn't be some god.]

*Some?*

[Haha! You may be the Returned One, but your knowledge is lacking. In this world, beings called gods are not that great. I could have become a god if I had wanted to. I just chose not to. A god is nothing more than a mortal who has halfway escaped the Cycle of Mortality. They seem absolute, yet they are feeble beings.]

The Void Monarch laughed. Lee Sungmin thought of Mushii and Denir. Considering the authority they had shown, he could not unconditionally agree.

[Well, whatever the case. I had to leave the forest with you, and I did. It will soon be proven that it had to be that way. That's how fate works.]

*Fate, my ass.*

[Do you not believe in fate? There is no such thing as coincidence in the world. Things happen because there is a reason for them to happen. You returned from death, and I left the forest with you. It happened because there was a reason for it. That is causality, and that is fate.]

*Should I go find the God of Fate?* Lee Sungmin seriously considered it.

"Hm."

His thoughts, however, could not continue for long. A sharp warning from his sixth sense brought Lee Sungmin to a halt. Jang Deuksu stopped as well, sensing something was amiss.

"…Chwi Geol?" Jang Deuksu called out.

Ahead of them, Chwi Geol was slumped against a wall, sitting on the ground amidst the scattered corpses of Doppelgangers.

"Ah… Master Jang. If we can talk… huhu! You must be human, right?" Chwi Geol let out a hoarse laugh.

Lee Sungmin stood blankly, taking in the scene.

"…Uhm! What in the world happened?" Jang Deuksu asked, hurrying toward him. He reached out a large hand to help Chwi Geol up, but Chwi Geol just shook his head.

"I'm fine. Just resting. You see… I met the Phantom Sword of Dokgo. No, was it not him? I met a monster… one that looked exactly like him and used his same martial arts."

Chwi Geol had always been talkative and cheerful, but now he was so worn out by pain and fatigue that he could not even laugh properly. Not many people could, not with their left arm severed.

"A potion…?"

"My Subspace Pocket was destroyed. Everything inside vanished into the cracks of space. Still… I stopped the bleeding."

"Master Jang, don't you have any potions?"

"…I don't."

The reply was so confident that Lee Sungmin felt a sense of awe at Jang Deuksu's ignorance, entering a Dungeon without proper preparations.

He looked around. The corpse of the Doppelganger that had been the Phantom Sword of Dokgo was visible, but Chwi Geol's severed arm was not.

"Where did your arm go?" Lee Sungmin asked as he approached.

Chwi Geol looked up at him with hazy eyes. Jang Deuksu, catching the gaze, answered for him.

"The Phantom Spear who killed the Sword Phantom a year ago. You've heard of him, haven't you?"

"I've heard of him, but… you don't mean he's…?"

"We met by chance and have been traveling together."

Having a famous title was not always a bad thing. At the mention of 'the Phantom Spear', Chwi Geol nodded in understanding.

"…The Phantom Sword of Dokgo is a master of the fast sword. What looks like a single slash actually carries dozens of sword ki strikes. Among those chunks of flesh, the pieces that were my left arm are probably mixed in."

"What came out of the Doppelganger's corpse?"

"A martial arts manual," Chwi Geol chuckled. He picked up a stiff book beside him and waved it. Though stained with flesh, the characters engraved on it were clear. "The Eighteen Dragon Subduing Palms. Isn't it funny for me, a member of the Beggars' Gang, to get a martial arts manual from the Beggars' Gang? Of course, the Eighteen Dragon Subduing Palms is an outstanding martial art, but I have no need for it."

"potion."

Lee Sungmin spoke, reaching into his Subspace Pocket. Chwi Geol flinched and turned to look at him.

*I have plenty of potions anyway.*

If the arm were still there, he could have used an Elixir to reattach it. Since it was not, there was no need to use such a precious item. A decent potion would be enough to help him regain his energy. By using a few potions, he could forge a connection with the Beggars' Gang. It was not a bad trade at all. Jihak was called the future of Shaolin, and Chwi Geol was likely the future of the Beggars' Gang. Helping such a person would build a friendly relationship with the entire sect.

The Beggars' Gang was a famous information network. There was no city without beggars, and most of them belonged to the Gang, acting as its eyes and ears. As a member of Erebrisa, Lee Sungmin had never lacked for information, but the Gang's strength was not limited to that. As one of the Nine Sects One Gang, it was the largest of all sects in terms of numbers alone. There was nothing to lose by forming a connection with such a huge organization and one of its central figures.

"…Thank you."

Chwi Geol took the potion Lee Sungmin offered and bowed his head deeply. He then handed over the manual he was holding.

"It's a martial art I have no need for. How about you take it?"

"Thank you."

Lee Sungmin did not refuse. The Eighteen Dragon Subduing Palms was an outstanding martial art, a specialty of the Beggars' Gang. Anyone else would have considered it a huge jackpot. Even so, Lee Sungmin had no reason to covet it. He had already learned the Hundred Steps Divine Fist but had not even trained it properly. There was no reason to learn another palm technique on top of that.

*It's such a famous martial art that it probably won't be worth much if I try to sell it. Just like the Hundred Steps Divine Fist.*

Still, it felt good to get it for free.
