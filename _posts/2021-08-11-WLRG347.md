A single word surfaced in Lee Sungmin's mind.

*Lunatic*.

It was the perfect description for the man he recalled, the only one it could possibly be.

The Spear King.

He remembered the Spear King standing beside the Black Dragon Knight, blocking the Martial God a decade ago. Since then, their whereabouts had been a mystery, yet here they both were on Mount Mushii.

"How long have those two been on this mountain?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"It's been ten years."

His eyebrows furrowed between the bandages that covered half his face.

Ten years.

A wave of disbelief washed over Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo. They both knew what that meant, having trained here themselves. They understood just how difficult it was to endure for such a staggering length of time. One could last for a while by carefully choosing their restrictions, but this was a mountain that forbade most things considered natural for a human after only five years.

"They're lunatics," Mushii muttered. "Even with strong bodies that had undergone sufficient training, enduring ten years on this mountain is a phenomenal feat. I was fine with it, but now that it's been about ten years, I'd like them to leave soon."

"Where are they?"

A thought flickered through Lee Sungmin's mind. *Could I do it now?, but regardless of whether it was possible, he couldn't afford to lock himself away on Mount Mushii to train when he had no idea how much time he had left.

"Normally, once you enter this mountain, you must endure unconditional restrictions," Mushii began, offering Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo a thin smile. She glanced silently at Yana, who stood behind them, and shrugged her shoulders. "But you didn't come here seeking asceticism. I won't apply the restrictions."

[Can she do it, or can she not?]

The Void Monarch sounded curious, and in truth, Lee Sungmin was just as curious himself. Mushii was a god, and this mountain was her domain. He knew for certain that within this mountain, she possessed enough power to face the Apostle of the End head-on and ensure their mutual destruction.

[Anyway, this mountain is a strange place. To this Elder, yes. It feels similar to the Dungeon you jumped into back then, saying you'd save your senior sister.]

Lee Sungmin couldn't feel that himself. He watched Mushii's back as she walked ahead, draped in rags, ascending the rugged mountain terrain as if she were gliding.

Familiar places came into view. He saw the cliff's edge where the blind Sword Phantom had once stood. He saw the wide, flat rock that Scarlet had used as her dwelling. He passed the stone cave where he had first met Baek Sogo and lived with her.

Before he could become lost in his memories, Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission to Mushii. She didn't look back. No answer came, but he asked his question anyway.

[Is your wish to stop The End?]

[What is it that you know?] Mushii's voice echoed back in his mind.

[Probably everything.]

At that, Mushii let out a small, silent laugh.

[I suppose so. The blessing that lingered on you has completely vanished. The fate that bound you is gone too. Yes, you are no longer The End.]

It was just as he had thought. Mushii knew everything.

[Since when did you know?]

[From the beginning,] she replied, continuing her climb toward the summit. [With such a blatant fate wrapped around you, how could I, a god, not have noticed?]

Even Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo, who had trained on this mountain, had never been to the summit. They had never felt the need to climb it.

[You ask if I want to stop The End? Isn't it obvious?] Mushii continued. [If I didn't, I would have had no reason to give you that roundabout warning. I gambled my own fate, and it paid off. I won't say it was all thanks to me that you escaped the fate of the Executor of End, but it was of some help, wasn't it?]

Lee Sungmin had to admit that was true. Mushii's words had served as a crucial reminder to him several times on his journey. Still, he had another question.

[Why did you make Senior Sister Baek Sogo your incarnation?]

[I deemed it necessary. You were incomplete, and I didn't know what would become of you. I gambled my fate on you just in case, but I needed a definite insurance policy.]

[And that was my senior sister?]

[Conviction is a good thing. As long as it doesn't bend and break, it's always sharp. Even if it has been broken once. A dagger is still sharp even when it's broken.]

Mushii chuckled mentally. In the meantime, the group arrived at the summit. She spun around to face Lee Sungmin.

"Our desires are the same," she said aloud. "If an end is set in stone, one wants to avoid it. Especially if it repeats infinitely."

What kind of beings were gods in this world? The Spirit Queen and the Fairy Queen had told him they changed each time the world met The End and began anew. He wondered if gods were the same and asked Mushii through his thoughts. She burst out laughing.

[The Fairy Queen told you many things. It seems she took quite a gamble herself. If she can't avoid The End, she'll face ruin.]

[What about you?]

[We do not change. If this world ends, a 'me' that I do not know will take over as the Goddess of Trials and Asceticism. The current me will die along with The End. Huhu. In a way, you could say we do change. In the end, the gods of this world are no different from the beings who meet their death with The End. In fact, it's more painful for us. Because we know everything.]

A bitter, undeniable truth laced her thoughts. Indeed. It must be agonizing to live while being aware of the end.

Lee Sungmin's gaze drifted past Mushii's shoulder, and he saw it.

There, on the ground, was something that could hardly be called a person. An unidentifiable mass was clumped together, a mashed lump of flesh that had lost all human form. Even Yana, a Yokai, had never seen such a thing and her mouth hung half-open. Baek Sogo looked at Mushii, bewildered.

"The last thing they restricted was the 'body'," Mushii said with a chuckle. "They started with trivial things at first. After that, they shut off their senses one by one. It was quite a sight when they received the restriction of pain. A restriction of pain doesn't mean they're painless. You two should know that well."

Lee Sungmin nodded. When he had restricted his sense of taste, he had thought he would simply be unable to taste anything. The reality was the complete opposite. The restriction made his sense of taste run wild, and no matter what he ate, he only felt disgust.

"They must have felt like they were going mad, feeling all sorts of pain even when staying still," Mushii continued. "I thought they would give up and go down the mountain around that point, but they endured that too. The restrictions kept being added. In the end, they restricted the body itself, and they became like that."

"Aren't they dead?" Baek Sogo asked, her voice trembling slightly.

"Even in that state, their minds are alive. While feeling all sorts of restrictions, that is. Pain, itchiness, hunger, thirst, and so on. Still, they have to live, so they can eat and drink. Look. It seems to be mealtime right now."

The lump of flesh wriggled. It was eating the ants crawling on the ground.

"They're enduring by eating bugs and drinking the morning dew. Isn't it amazing? It's something I always feel, but a martial artist is no different from a madman."

As she spoke, Mushii raised her hand. "It's not what they wanted, but it's impossible to have a conversation with them like that. I have no choice."

CRACKLE.

A sharp sound split the air. At Mushii's gesture, the lumps of flesh convulsed with a loud noise, writhing and reshaping until two human figures emerged. They had taken human form, but the appearance of the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King was regrettable in many ways. Yana wrinkled her nose at the stench that spread in all directions. The two men, both with equally disheveled hair, raised their heads. From faces blackened with layers of grime, only their eyes shone with a startling brightness.

"What the?" the Spear King rasped, his voice like scraping metal. He looked around, shielding his eyes from the painful sunlight. "Why is my body... Kgh!"

HACK.

He coughed up a wad of yellow phlegm that contained a clump of half-chewed ants. The Black Dragon Knight scratched his head vigorously with long, dirty fingernails. Though they had regained their bodies, conversation was impossible right away.

After some time passed, they finally came to their senses. The Black Dragon Knight picked a horrifically large piece of sleep from the corner of his eye and glared at the Spear King.

"This must be a divine revelation telling us to end this damn foolishness."

"I haven't seen the end yet!" the Spear King shot back.

"You son of a bitch. If you want to see the end, see it by yourself. I can't do this anymore."

The Black Dragon Knight had no intention of missing this sudden opportunity. As he came on strong, the Spear King's face contorted and he shot to his feet.

"You bastard! Are you trying to turn all our suffering until now into nothing?"

"We should have quit long ago. Playing along with a madman's game, suffering like this for years!"

Watching them bicker, Lee Sungmin cleared his throat with a loud 'ahem'. The sound was meant to be heard, and both the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight whipped around to face him.

"Did you interfere with our training?!" the Spear King roared.

"Thanks for interfering," the Black Dragon Knight said, extending both hands toward Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin gently took the Black Dragon Knight's filthy hands. "So, uh, what happened?"

"I got dragged by that madman and holed up on this mountain."

"Get your facts straight!" the Spear King yelled. "Weren't you just as ashamed that we couldn't do anything to the Martial God even when we attacked him together?"

"Alright, I'll get my facts straight," the Black Dragon Knight retorted. "I didn't hole up on this mountain because I felt ashamed about the Martial God. It was because the martial prowess of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman who died back then was so impressive that I wanted to make it my own, so I played along with you!"

"And the guy who says that is quitting his training now?"

"I've just realized my place. How can a crow-tit follow a stork?"

"You have no pride."

"Pride, my ass. Anyway, I can't do it anymore. If you want to do it, do it alone."

"......"

The Spear King screamed until his throat was raw. Lee Sungmin sighed and stepped between them to mediate.

"Stop, stop. Aren't you curious about what's been happening while you were holed up on this mountain? Aren't you curious why I'm here?"

"I am curious," the Spear King said, his eyes flashing. "Weren't you dead?"

"The rumor I heard right before I came to this mountain was that you died fighting a Demon Lord in the South. I felt like shit back then. To think you'd go and die after I went to the trouble of sending you away to escape!"

"I didn't die."

"Why are you alive?"

"I'm alive because I didn't die."

"You're tough to kill," the Spear King grumbled.

Lee Sungmin ignored the comment and explained to them why he had come. First, he had to make them understand about The End. When he spoke of it, the Spear King clutched his stomach and began to laugh.

"So you're saying what the Martial God is doing is just a waste of time," he cackled.

The Black Dragon Knight's expression, however, turned bitter. "So. The world is on the brink of ruin, the Martial God is wasting his time trying to stop it, and the Vampire Queen of the North and the monsters she has with her are working towards that ruin?"

"That's the gist of it, yes."

"And you want to borrow our strength to stop the destruction?"

"......"

"Fine." The Spear King's agreement came with a forceful nod. He shot to his feet and pointed a finger at Lee Sungmin.

"First," he declared, his foul-smelling mouth stretched wide in a grin, "let's have a match. I'll think about it after that."
