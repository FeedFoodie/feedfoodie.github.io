The Flesh Devouring Blood Fang tore mercilessly through Preskan's magic. It was a violent power, a combination of Demonic Energy and inner qi that felt created solely for destruction from its very foundation. Even though Lee Sungmin controlled and fired it using the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, the technique was not completely his to command. A fierce tremor traveled up his arm, threatening to wrench the spear from his grasp, but he held firm and thrust it all the way through.

"......"

The power gathered at the spearhead exploded with an even greater force, scattering Preskan's magic in all directions. As if that were not enough, it shattered every single one of the layered defensive barriers he had created.

"Heok!"

Horror washed over Preskan. He now fully realized whose destructive attack had just annihilated a part of the street. He hastily used Blink to escape the trajectory of the Flesh Devouring Blood Fang, but as his figure vanished, Lee Sungmin's gaze and senses were already locked onto his new location.

Lee Sungmin leaped without hesitation.

PWAK!

Kicking his legs against nothing, he sprinted through the empty air. The sight of Lee Sungmin dashing fiercely toward him terrified Preskan, who reappeared and immediately began to form a beastfolk. Using Blink again so soon was a dangerous proposition. As a Lich, Preskan lacked a physical body, so the risk was not the same as it was for those with flesh and blood, but consecutive uses were still far too hazardous. Even if his body could not be crushed, his soul could be destroyed, or he could simply disappear into a crack in space.

*There's no time to slow him down with curse magic...!*

Would defensive magic be effective then? No, it would not. Though his barriers could perfectly block most attacks, they had been torn apart as easily as paper before Lee Sungmin's brutish power. In the end, the only option Preskan chose was to attack. He quickly changed the shape of the beastfolk he was forming and moved his lips. Dozens of orbs appeared one after another around him, the repeatedly created bullets of magic power soon multiplying into hundreds.

"Don't come any closer!" Preskan shouted, thrusting his hands forward.

Hundreds of Finger Bullets, fired from close range, filled Lee Sungmin's vision. Rather than evade, he chose to break through. With so many projectiles, dodging would be difficult even with Traceless Step. He put strength into his raised foot and stomped down. Though it was empty air with no foothold, the movement arts manifested. An irregular flow of enhanced qi raged wildly around him. The first step generated the torrent of energy, and the second closed the distance.

With the third step, he made it explode, sweeping away everything in front of him. It was the Three Step Fatality, the fifth technique of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. The energy bullets caught in the blast of enhanced qi all exploded at once, and the impact threw Preskan's body backward. Lee Sungmin passed through the fog of dissipated magic.

Preskan stabilized himself in mid-air and began forming another beastfolk.

*I can't give him an opening...!*

However, how? Powerful magic required a corresponding amount of effort. The beastfolk, a technique only a Black Mage contracted with a Demon Lord could perform, greatly reduced casting time, but it did not eliminate it entirely. Given the right conditions and enough time, a mage was more dangerous than anything else, but in a situation like this, he was far too helpless.

*If only Aine were here...!*

If he had come with Aine, she could have held Lee Sungmin down while he leisurely prepared his spells, but Aine was not by his side. While it was true that Preskan was a skilled Black Mage who had once served as the Black Magic Tower Master, it was equally true that Lee Sungmin possessed enough martial prowess to become the sect leader of one of the Nine Sects One Gang.

KWAAANG!

The spear Lee Sungmin swung struck the barrier Preskan had hastily created. The barrier did not shatter from the single blow, which Lee Sungmin had not expected it to. Inside the trembling shield, Preskan began to form another beastfolk. The moment he confirmed this, Lee Sungmin executed Deathly Flash, the fastest technique of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The barrier, already weakened from the first strike, could not withstand the second. Deathly Flash pierced through both the shield and Preskan's body at the same time.

"Keheuk!"

He was in the middle of forming a beastfolk and chanting an incantation. Deathly Flash cut that moment short. The magic, which had been nearing completion, failed and backfired. Preskan's body, made of Black Mist, swelled as if it would burst.

"KRRRRR!"

Preskan made a gurgling sound and twisted his body. He barely managed to lift a trembling finger and point it at Lee Sungmin.

"......"

A current of electricity flowed from his fingertip and shot forward. Even as his own magic power was running rampant, he had somehow managed to create an attack. The spell, however, failed to pierce Lee Sungmin's Protective Qi Aura. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear toward the twitching Lich.

"......"

The mist pierced by the spear scattered in all directions.

"Kkeueueueu!"

Preskan struggled, his body twisting. *Can he feel pain, even with a body made of mist?* Lee Sungmin was inwardly curious but did not bother to ask. He drove the impaled spear downward, slamming Preskan's body into the ground.

KWAANG!

The spear, having pierced through Preskan's back, embedded itself deep in the earth. Although the spear had penetrated his body, Preskan was not dead. A Lich with a Life Force Vessel does not die unless its soul is annihilated. Lee Sungmin knew that well. He could not be killed here. He could completely destroy Preskan's spiritual body, but that would not lead to true death.

"What are you doing here?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking down at him.

It was sorrowful. Preskan was genuinely feeling sorrow. He had felt it when Arbes took Aine from him, but he had not shown it much then. Arbes was not here now. He was in a situation where he needed Aine, but she was gone, and being treated like this by a man he had easily suppressed like an insect just a few years ago made his sorrow all the more intense.

"Eheheuheuk!"

In the end, Preskan burst into tears. Of course, he only made the sound, as his spiritual body of mist was incapable of shedding them. Nevertheless, he felt genuine sorrow and let out a loud wail. Lee Sungmin, who still had his spear driven through the Lich, looked down in disbelief.

"Why are you crying? Does it hurt?" he asked cautiously.

"Eheheuheuk! Keuheong!"

Preskan continued to let out sorrowful cries as if he could not hear him. This was a first for Lee Sungmin. In his past life, he had seen plenty of men who wailed because they did not want to die, but the people he had fought in this life had mostly faced death with composure, not crying like the world was ending.

*No. It's not like he's actually dying, is it?*

That made Preskan's crying feel even stranger. Lee Sungmin pushed his inner qi into the embedded spear.

PAJIJIK!

Preskan's spiritual body trembled violently, but even as his form shook, he cried out.

"You... you son of a bitch! You thief who stole the heart I spent my entire life creating!"

"I already told you, I didn't want to take it."

"Shut up! Why! Just why! I miss the days when I was researching alone in the Dungeon! Eheheuhuk!"

"...Where did you leave that wench you always have with you?" Lee Sungmin had been wondering about that as well. Aine, the artificial life form with the same heart as him. While fighting Preskan, he had remained wary of her intervention, but even after all this, she had not shown herself.

"Aine!" Preskan cried out. "My lovely daughter! My everything! Eheheuhuk!"

"I asked, where did you leave her?"

"She was taken!" Preskan yelled. "By the great King of Liches! Eheuhuk! It's so sorrowful to be powerless. If I had been more skilled, Aine wouldn't have been taken...!"

"What were you doing here?"

"Do you think I'd tell you that!" Preskan's voice grew harsh.

He had expected as much. Lee Sungmin did not bother trying to persuade him to answer. It was obvious he would not talk, and prying would just be a waste of time.

"......"

When he fully pushed his enhanced qi into the spear, Preskan's spiritual body scattered in all directions. The dissipated mist did not return to its original form. It had returned to the Life Force Vessel hidden somewhere. Lee Sungmin pulled the spear out of the ground and looked ahead. First, he had to follow Envirus.

{sep}

The distant commotion startled the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia, shattering the peace of their rare visit to the night market. Not knowing what to do, Rubia grabbed the hem of his clothes.

"It's all right," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, stroking her head. He had no desire to get involved. His first thought was to return to the safety of the Fairy Realm, and he turned to leave with Rubia.

If only he had not heard it.

"Chuseong is back?" someone muttered.

The words brushed past his ear. The moment he heard the name 'Chuseong', the Crazy Heavenly Demon's steps halted. Rubia, who had not heard, tilted her head and looked up at him. A ferocious aura he had been suppressing began to flow from his stock-still form. His eyes, turning crimson, trembled as he looked down at her.

"...Go back alone."

"......"

"I'm sorry," he muttered, patting Rubia's shoulder.

Startled, Rubia grabbed his wrist. "Mister, where are you going?"

"They say Chuseong has returned."

At those words, Rubia's face turned pale. She unconsciously gripped his wrist tightly with both hands.

"D-don't go."

"Nothing will happen."

"How do you know that? At least when you're with Mr. Lee Sungmin...!"

"This is this one's business," the Crazy Heavenly Demon replied, his voice chillingly cold. He shook off her hand and turned away. Rubia shouted something from behind him, but her voice did not reach his ears. He unhesitatingly used his movement arts, heading in the direction where the commotion had started. He leaped over the humans and Yokai blocking his path, running across the rooftops to move faster.

When the Crazy Heavenly Demon arrived, everything was burning. Buildings were burning, corpses were burning. In the middle of it all stood Chuseong, letting the scattering ash fall over his body. The fear felt by the corpses he had burned to death and the Demonic Energy they possessed flowed into him. Even without chewing and swallowing them, he felt a sense of fullness. Chuseong patted his stomach and burst out laughing.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon saw him from beyond the flames. Though not with his own two eyes, the vision was as vivid as if he were there. A burning village, corpses. *Did Dersia die like that too?* The thought ignited a rage he could no longer contain.

"......"

A crimson Protective Qi Aura erupted from beneath his feet.

"Aaaah!"

He unleashed the scream he had been suppressing and threw a palm strike. A massive wind blew away all the blazing flames. Chuseong stopped laughing at the sudden, fierce gust. It was an artificial wind. He turned his head to look in the direction it came from and saw the figure of the Crazy Heavenly Demon charging at him, radiating a crimson bloody light.

"What are you?" Chuseong asked, the words stabbing into the Crazy Heavenly Demon's ears.

He delayed his answer. Instead, he poured the fully manifested Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art into another palm strike. Seeing the crimson enhanced qi rushing toward him, Chuseong knew its power was not to be taken lightly.

DDUDEUK!

The skin on his raised arm turned into black iron armor.

KWAANG!

With a loud noise, Chuseong's body was pushed back.

"Uwaaah!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon roared. He clenched his outstretched palm into a fist, kicked off the ground, and leaped up. Crimson enhanced qi gathered and swelled around his right fist.

*That too! I can't let it hit me.* With that instinct, Chuseong covered his entire body in skin of steel, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not stop. He slammed his fist into him.

KWAAAANG!

Chuseong's knees buckled. The ground itself caved in. Ignoring his tingling fist, the Crazy Heavenly Demon twisted his waist. His left fist dug in under Chuseong's arm.

DDUDEUK!

Chuseong's body bent into an L-shape as he was launched into the air. His body was heavy. As he floated up low, the Crazy Heavenly Demon spread both hands and detonated his enhanced qi. Caught in the crimson storm, Chuseong's body was sent flying backward, demolishing a burning building.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon caught his breath once and ran toward the wreckage. He did not think for a moment that Chuseong would have died from that last attack, and he was right.

KWAAAANG!

An explosion from inside the building caused the entire structure to blow apart. Debris scattering in all directions blocked his path, and he swung both arms to sweep it all away. Chuseong rose from within the flames, his face distorted as he looked at the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"You human bastard!" he spat, baring sharp teeth. He had been caught off guard by the sudden attack and was surprised that the power was much stronger than he had expected. There were undoubtedly not many humans as strong as this one.

However, that did not just apply to the Crazy Heavenly Demon. It was the same for Chuseong. Back when the Red Ghost ruled Urmuri, Chuseong was the second strongest after him. After Yana appeared, he was the second strongest after her. Always number two. He had always been dissatisfied with that, but to be consistently second in a city where all sorts of powerful Yokai lived meant that Chuseong was an incredibly powerful being himself.

"You insolent bastard!"

Chuseong walked out from the flames, his human appearance gone. His frame, which had already been larger than the Crazy Heavenly Demon's, had now doubled in size. The clothes he wore had all burned away, but no bare skin was visible. His entire body was covered in black iron armor, and his face had changed from that of a human to that of a Yokai. Two crimson eyes were grotesquely slanted, and his iron-clad body flickered with clinging flames. He had abandoned his human form and taken on his complete Yokai form. He opened his dragon-like, elongated snout and exhaled a crimson breath.

Transformed, Chuseong emitted a denser and more violent Demonic Energy than before. *You can't handle him.* That was what Yana had warned. She was right. Although the Crazy Heavenly Demon was skilled enough to be counted on one hand among the Supreme Peak masters, he was not strong enough to kill Chuseong. He knew that fact as well.

Despite knowing, the Crazy Heavenly Demon strode forward. He had to do this. His desire for revenge was too strong to pretend he did not know Chuseong was powerful, too strong to run away. Revenge for Dersia's death, and revenge for the other villagers. To ignore Chuseong now would be to deny the very man he had become.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon cloaked his body in his Protective Qi Aura and charged.

Two reds of different natures collided.
