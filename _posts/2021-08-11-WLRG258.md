A twitch at the corner of Jeniela's mouth was the only reaction to his question. To Kim Jonghyun, she did not seem particularly displeased, yet he knew better than to trust appearances. The Vampire Queen was nothing if not capricious. While she could not be called impulsive, he was not so arrogant as to believe he could predict the full extent of her whims.

"A Dragon," Jeniela finally said.

Her eyes narrowed, her gaze sharpening into something that seemed to pierce through everything as she stared at him. For a Vampire, a 'gaze' was far more than a simple look, and this was especially true for the one who stood at their pinnacle, Jeniella of Blood Charm. She possessed not one but three powerful Demonic Eyes, and even Kim Jonghyun had not identified them all. The only one he knew for certain was the All-Seeing Demonic Eye, which could see through anything.

"You're just making a wild guess, aren't you?" she asked with a smirk.

Kim Jonghyun nodded, seeing no point in pretense. In truth, he had no real evidence to support his claim that Jeniela had some kind of relationship with the Dragons.

"Why don't you state your grounds?"

Jeniela revealed no killing intent, but Kim Jonghyun mentally prepared for that possibility all the same as he answered her question.

"In this world, I don't believe there are many beings capable of restraining you. At first I wondered if it might be the gods. However, despite their name, they are beings who cannot exert much power in this world. If we're talking about pure strength, those called gods are likely weaker than you."

"That's right," Jeniela said, smiling as she nodded her head. "Whether I can kill them is another matter entirely, but their power poses no great threat to me. Besides, they aren't in any position to try."

"Then it can only be the Dragons."

"You pretend to be so clever, even though you know nothing. Just like a mage."

Jeniela sneered and slowly rose to her feet. The instant she moved, Kim Jonghyun's hand shot toward his Grimoire, and the jet-black tome floated into the air.

"Don't be too scared," Jeniela said, smiling at him and baring her snow-white fangs. "I have no intention of killing you. Your question was rude, I'll admit, but not unbearably so. A Dragon... a Dragon, you say. Do you think those cowards who fled to another dimension out of fear of death are in any position to force a promise on me?"

"...What do you mean by that?"

Jeniela's sneer made Kim Jonghyun flinch.

"Oh my." Her playfulness was blatant as she tapped a finger to her lips. "Did I say something I shouldn't have? Vollander would be terribly disappointed if he heard."

Vollander, the king of the Death Knights, was currently wandering the seas with the legion he led. He searched the waters where the Dragons were last sighted, seeking them even though their whereabouts were unknown.

*Jeniela knows the whereabouts of the Dragons.*

Kim Jonghyun had long questioned why an inhuman of such immense power had remained silent in the North for so long. He had naturally wondered if some kind of restriction had been placed on her and had assumed the Dragons were responsible. Judging by Jeniela's words, however, it seemed they had placed no such restrictions on her after all.

Then what was holding the powerful Vampire Queen back? Why, after hundreds of years of inaction, had she suddenly started to move? It could not be because of the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon. Most people claimed that the Vampire Queen came to rule a masterless Trabia after his death, but that was an absurd notion.

"'Fled to another dimension out of fear of death'... does that mean the Dragons gave up on living in this world because they feared the coming The End?"

"They all ran away."

"Erebrisa," Kim Jonghyun muttered.

At his words, Jeniela's smile deepened.

"The purpose of Erebrisa is to control or support variables. A variable in this case must be one of fate, and fate must refer to the inevitable The End. I've always thought that the Dragons were behind that mysterious intermediary Guild. After all, the Spatial Movement and spatial interference those Brokers use are things only Dragons should be capable of. However, if what you're saying is true, then there are no Dragons left in this world."

"It's true that the Dragons created Erebrisa," Jeniela said, shaking her head. "It's a grand magic system they left behind for this world."

"What's certain is that there are no more Dragons in this world, and that you are not under any restrictions from them, but a question still remains. Why are you, who haven't moved for hundreds of years, resuming your activities now?"

"The time is approaching," Jeniela replied with an alluring smile.

Little by little, Kim Jonghyun sensed he was getting closer to one of her secrets.

"Are you saying The End is approaching?"

"It has always been approaching."

"Are you waiting for it?"

"Slaughter Predator," Jeniela muttered.

Slaughter Predator. The king of inhumans, known only through stories. A monster among monsters that kills and devours all beings. Kim Jonghyun frowned at the name. In his view, the monster closest to a Slaughter Predator was none other than Jeniela herself, who stood before him. A vampire, who accumulates power by drinking blood, was a being befitting the title. Or perhaps it was Lee Sungmin or Aine, who possessed a Black Heart.

"The Slaughter Predator is a symbol of the inhumans," Jeniela continued, "but it's a monster that is destined to appear one day."

"You speak as if you know the future."

"The future changes greatly depending on the variables. Don't so easily claim to know the future. That's far too arrogant."

"But you..."

"I don't know everything. My eyes only see fragments of the countless futures where innumerable variables are intertwined."

Through those words, Kim Jonghyun could vaguely guess the nature of one of Jeniela's Demonic Eyes. Seeing the future. Perhaps, no, without a doubt. That had to be one of the powers she possessed.

"I see," Kim Jonghyun said, nodding his head. "You saw a future where the Slaughter Predator appeared, and you were waiting for it."

"That's enough," Jeniela said, shaking her head.

She just stood still and did nothing, but the moment their eyes met, Kim Jonghyun felt his breath catch. A searing pain erupted inside him, as if his very blood were boiling. He bit his lip hard and stretched a hand toward his Grimoire.

"I won't kill you. In the future I saw, the future I want to reach, I didn't kill you."

"I understand," Kim Jonghyun nodded quickly.

He had misjudged. He had reversed to a state close to a Demon Lord and believed he could at least survive a direct confrontation. Jeniela was right. He had been far too overconfident. It would be difficult to escape unscathed from the fang-baring monster before him. He would lose at least an arm, and he did not want to take such a risk.

"I apologize for my rudeness."

"Fine."

As Kim Jonghyun grasped the situation and bowed his head, Jeniela suppressed the vicious power she had unleashed. He kept his head bowed as he slowly backed away.

"Reversal Magic," he heard Jeniela's voice say as he retreated. She was looking out the window, as if she no longer had any interest in him. "It's a magic you can no longer use, but you can use it for others, can't you?"

"If there is a sufficient offering."

"Whatever offering you demand, there will always be those desperate enough to pay it. They'll probably do anything you ask. They'll hang on your every word."

"I know."

When he had put enough distance between them, Kim Jonghyun turned around. He knew exactly what she was talking about. While recovering in her mansion, he had already given some thought to his next steps.

*There are no Dragons in this world.*

Jeniela's words had made that an undeniable fact. This meant that Vollander and the Death Knights, wandering the seas to break their curse, would never find the salvation they so desired. With the magic of his Grimoire, however, the Reversal Magic that had nearly turned him into a Demon Lord, it was possible to remove even the inescapable curse of the Undead. In a world without Dragons, only Kim Jonghyun could break their curse.

*Is this also part of the future you saw?*

That must be why she was not killing him. He wished he could have heard how that future ended. Swallowing his regret, Kim Jonghyun opened the closed door and stepped outside.

*I thought he was dead.*

The Spear King sat before a campfire, picking his ear. After fighting the Black Dragon Knight in the northern forests, he had left the area and had been wandering ever since. He would have to meet the Phantom Spear for their promised duel someday, but he was in no hurry. The Black Dragon Knight had protested through a Spirit Medium that he had almost died because of the Spear King, but that did not change the Spear King's position within Heaven Beyond Heaven.

He smacked his lips. That forest back then... the monster with a vicious presence that had suddenly appeared possessed power hardly inferior to his own. If the Black Dragon Knight had managed to escape from that monster, it meant he must have been hiding a considerable amount of power during their fight.

*Good, more opponents to fight.*

Even besides the Moon Queen. The Spear King rose to his feet. A short while ago, a Spirit Medium had informed him that the Dark Supreme was dead.

The Dark Supreme. The Spear King clicked his tongue. The man's martial arts skills were one thing, but he had not been such a bad fellow, though his blind devotion to the Martial God had been quite disagreeable.

*No separate orders have come down to me.*

Which meant he could proceed with his original orders. He stamped out the campfire with a raised foot. They said the Phantom Spear had killed the Dark Supreme. He had no intention of avenging the man. On the contrary, the Spear King felt a deep thrill at the news. Setting aside the Fist Honored One, who was immobilized by a curse, both the Dark Supreme and the Sword Saint had fought the Phantom Spear in perfect condition and died.

The Spear King grinned.

"I guess it's about time to go."

He had no intention of rushing this time. He would travel at a leisurely pace, ensuring he was always in optimal condition to fight. He planned to head to Haras, the base of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. The Phantom Spear was bound to return there.

*Perhaps I'll even get to fight the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.*

The Spear King inwardly looked forward to the possibility.

"Eh... hehe... ehehehe..."

Lloyd stared with a displeased expression at the Blue Magic Tower Master, who was slumped on the floor. The man blinked his vacant eyes, sitting limply and drooling. Nearby, a tall man with an annoyed expression rummaged through drawers, searching the room.

"Did you really have to use such a rough method?" Lloyd asked cautiously.

The Mages Guild Master whipped his head around to look at him. "He's not the type to hand it over just because we ask nicely, and if we tried to extract his memories, the risk of him losing his mind is too high."

"It looks like he's already lost his mind..."

"He hasn't. I just distorted his memories. He'll be back to normal after some time passes, albeit with those memories distorted."

"Just how much time will it take for him to recover?"

"Hmm. A week should do it. Of course, for that week, he'll be a drooling idiot who can't even control his own bowels."

"He's the type who'll be suspicious about ending up in this state once he comes to his senses."

"You don't have to worry about that," the Mages Guild Master answered without hesitation. "I already factored that in when I distorted his memories. I've set it so he'll think he lost his mind after taking one of his own potions by mistake."

Lloyd's expression was still not good. This was Abel, the younger brother of his Master, Envirus, and the Mages Guild Master. He was a man with a completely different personality than Lloyd had expected. He was not serious like his Master and was the type to stop at nothing to achieve his goals.

"Found it," Abel said in a cheerful voice.

He took out a potion bottle that was hidden deep inside a drawer.

"Is that it? The Dragon's..."

"The purity is a bit lacking, but it's the only serum of its kind in the world right now. The Blue Magic Tower Master really went through a lot of trouble."

It was a serum created by applying the Blue Magic Tower Master's lifetime of alchemical research to the blood he had received from Lee Sungmin. Abel grinned as he looked down at it, then put it inside his robes without hesitation.

"Don't you feel sorry for the Blue Magic Tower Master?"

"When he comes to, he won't even remember receiving the Dragon's blood or succeeding in creating the serum anyway."

"Still..."

"I'm doing this because it's necessary," Abel said, putting on the robe he had taken off. "My idiot of a brother who taught you seems to have placed a lot of importance on etiquette and the big picture. I'm not like that."

"Ahem."

Hearing Abel call his Master an idiot, Lloyd let out a low, dry cough.

"Let's go."

Abel approached the open window. Just as when he first broke in, it seemed he planned to leave the same way.

"Still, it's a relief that Haras isn't too far from here," Abel smiled, placing a foot on the windowsill.

This happened not long before Lee Sungmin was due to return to Haras.
