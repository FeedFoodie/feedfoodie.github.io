A soft light emanated from Osla's wings, a testament to the choice he had already made. He would change fate, even if it meant facing a devastating price for it later. This was his decision, and because of it, he moved with a purpose far more decisive than before.

"......"

With a furrowed brow, Osla sent his power seeping into Baek Sogo's body. Lee Sungmin watched, his gaze fraught with worry, until Osla finally shook his head a moment later.

"I've checked," he announced. "There's no Vampire trickery. It's just poison."

A small measure of relief washed over Lee Sungmin. After a year imprisoned in the Queen's mansion, he had feared Jeniela or the other Vampires had done something more to Baek Sogo than simply poison her. Thankfully, that did not seem to be the case.

"Her energy is severely damaged," Osla continued. "It doesn't look like she was abused… but the poison is too strong."

"Is there nothing you can do?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Not me," Osla stated firmly. "If it were something like Demonic Energy, I could forcibly Seal it, but this poison isn't that kind of thing. I might be able to slow its progress… but from what I can see, even that has reached its limit. For the past year, the Vampires used every means possible to keep this woman from dying."

*Why would they go to such lengths?* The question echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind. Why had the Vampires worked so hard just to keep Baek Sogo alive? Was it to lure him to the mansion, or perhaps to provoke the reclusive Jeniela into coming out? That could be one reason, but why did Gemini simply let Baek Sogo go? And why had Jeniela allowed him to escape?

Jeniela's Eye of Futuresight no longer saw the future, but did that mean all the futures she had once seen were completely negated? The tangled questions led him to another, one he hoped Osla could answer. He turned to the fairy, figuring Osla would know more about fate and the future than he did.

"The future can't be seen…" Osla's expression turned grim. "The Queen's Eye of Futuresight doesn't show a fixed future. It shows the possibilities of what might happen." He sighed, gently laying Baek Sogo's body on the grass as Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod.

Jeniela had said as much herself. The possibility of what might happen. To make that possibility a reality, she had been maneuvering for hundreds of years.

"The fact that the Eye of Futuresight can no longer see the future means that this world… Eria… has no possibilities left in its future."

"…The End," Lee Sungmin muttered, his voice barely a whisper.

For ten years, the world had miraculously avoided destruction, yet, it had not escaped The End. The first calamity, Kim Jonghyun, had vanished thanks to Abel's sacrifice. Wiji Hoyeon was wandering a dungeon for a long time to stop it. The Slaughter Predator, too, had been annihilated within his own Mental World.

However, it was not over. If The End was what Jeniela desired, then she had to be considered one of its calamities as well, and just as the Slaughter Predator had sneered, the Spirit Queen would one day awaken.

*It can be changed.*

Lee Sungmin did not doubt it. Even the Slaughter Predator, whose appearance was supposed to be inevitable, had been annihilated before it could fully awaken. Though he had been warned not to trust the Demonic Spirit, its actions had undeniably become a huge variable, altering a significant part of fate.

"Mm…"

A soft sound escaped the lips of the prone Baek Sogo. Startled, Lee Sungmin rushed to her side, and Scarlet was right behind him. He supported Baek Sogo's head and retrieved an Elixir from his Subspace Pocket. The Elixir was a panacea, capable of not only healing wounds but also restoring a certain amount of energy. He carefully poured the liquid into Baek Sogo's mouth.

[Isn't feeding it to her mouth-to-mouth the standard procedure in this kind of situation?]

*What kind of standard procedure?*

[It just feels like that kind of atmosphere, doesn't it?]

The Void Monarch chuckled. As he did, the corner of Baek Sogo's closed eye twitched. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard, looking down at her as her eyelids slowly fluttered open. Gray eyes, clouded with a deep murkiness he had never seen before, looked up at him.

"......"

Her lips parted slightly, a dazed sound escaping as she stared at him. Lee Sungmin had no idea what to say. In the silence, Baek Sogo's head shifted, her gaze falling upon Scarlet, who was looking down at her with a worried expression. Baek Sogo slowly tilted her head.

"…A dream?" she whispered.

She had to be dreaming. What she saw now was completely different from what she had seen before losing consciousness. For a year, she had been imprisoned in the underground mansion in Trabia. Though she had not been tortured or abused, she had been thoroughly neglected. Escape was impossible. The poison had weakened her body, and her qi center, the source of her inner arts, was frozen solid. No matter how much of a Transcendent Realm master she was, escaping a mansion full of Vampires with a body unable to use her arts was impossible.

At some point, she could no longer maintain her consciousness properly. She found herself sleeping more and more, though it was more like passing out. How much time had passed like that? The last thing she remembered was the same underground darkness, the thick iron bars, and the cold stone floor she had grown sick of seeing for a year.

"…It's not," Lee Sungmin said, his voice trembling. He fumbled for her wrists with both hands and held them. They were gaunt and thin, her skin rough to the touch.

Lee Sungmin bowed his head, his emotions plummeting into an abyss as bitter sorrow made his facial muscles tremble.

*In the end, you… just as you said ten years ago… you distinguished good and evil by your own narrow standards, and just as you said with a smile back then, you challenged the monsters called Predators alone to annihilate evil.*

"…Senior Sister."

*Could I have done that?* He did not know. Lee Sungmin lacked the conviction that Baek Sogo possessed, even if it was narrow-minded. For her, the conviction to annihilate evil was more important and absolute than anything else. It was something she had to do, even if it meant throwing away her own life. If she were in her right mind, she never would have challenged the Lycanthrope leader, Ju Won, alone. She would not have challenged the inhuman group called the Predators.

However, Baek Sogo had challenged them. She had failed, but she had tried to kill Ju Won. If she had succeeded, she would have marched north to Trabia without hesitation to challenge Jeniela next.

That was just who Baek Sogo was.

And she had not killed Lee Sungmin.

"…It's been… a long time," he managed to say, unable to bring himself to look into her eyes. He could only hold her hand with his own trembling ones.

Ten years ago, Baek Sogo had not killed him. By her standards, he should have been an evil to be killed, but she had not done it.

"Junior Disciple…?"

*It's not a dream.*

The realization slowly dawned on Baek Sogo. The hand holding hers was warm, a human warmth completely different from the underground chill she had felt for a year. A faint light returned to her deadened eyes. She saw Lee Sungmin, then Scarlet. She saw Yana standing at a distance, and Osla among the Fairies.

Baek Sogo could not say anything. *Where is this place? Why am I here?* More than any of those questions, the simple fact that Lee Sungmin was in front of her made her tremble in shock. She opened her mouth to speak, but no words would form, so she closed it again.

"…Yeah."

The silence that followed was short, yet to both Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo, it felt as if it stretched on for an eternity. Baek Sogo nodded along with her reply.

"It's been a long time…" she answered.

"So, how was it?" Gemini's voice was alight with curiosity. The question came just after Lee Sungmin's party had vanished from the space on their Fairy Horses.

The red curtain that had risen before Jeniela collapsed. She clenched and unclenched her outstretched hand.

"Magnificent," she admitted with genuine admiration. "He surprises me every time I see him. There's probably no being, not even among the inhuman, who develops this quickly."

"So?" Gemini asked, his eyes shining. "Do you think the Phantom Spear's spear can reach the Queen?"

At his words, Jeniela chuckled and placed a hand on Gemini's head. "Oh, reaching me will be possible at some point. Whether that spear can actually kill me, however, is another question entirely."

"But you don't wish for death, Queen."

"I wish for everything to die in the end," Jeniela answered without hesitation.

In response, Gemini smiled without a hint of agitation. He had known her wish for hundreds of years. The Slaughter Predator, which kills and devours all. Jeniela herself was no exception to what it would devour. At the very last moment, after the Slaughter Predator had consumed everything else in the world, she wished to be joyfully eaten and killed by it.

"So, Queen. What conclusion did you come to over the past year? Just as we thought, the Phantom Spear was the Slaughter Predator. Even if he isn't anymore."

"I have acted for hundreds of years for a future where the Slaughter Predator appears," Jeniela said, slowly rising into the air. Gemini exited the underground space with her, Chen and Kun following them in silence. "It's not going to change now. In the end… the Slaughter Predator is just a symbol, isn't it? Killing. Eating. That's enough."

Originally, Jeniela had expected that the Chimera created by Preskan, Aine, would become the Slaughter Predator. For the past ten years, she had been preparing to make it so. Yes, Jeniela had been like that from the beginning. She was not waiting for the Slaughter Predator to appear someday. She had been acting to create it with her own hands.

"Aren't you anxious now that you can't see the future?"

"I was just flustered. I came to a conclusion over the past year. Besides… what I desire is the end. If it's the end, it's only natural that I can't see it, right?" Jeniela crossed the red garden with Gemini following behind her.

"Nothing changes," she said. "So, Gemini. I won't blame you for your little prank."

"…Oh my. Queen, I don't know what you're talking about."

"I'm talking about you returning the Silent Flash to him."

"Did you want me not to do that, Queen? You should have told me beforehand, then. When you think about it, it's all your fault. You were alone for a year without saying anything to me."

That was it. Gemini made no more excuses for his actions, and Jeniela did not press him any further. That was their relationship, and it had been ever since she first bit his neck.

"And, Queen. You were the one who let that human go."

"He's not enough yet." The corners of Jeniela's lips curled up. "You know me, Gemini. The things I really want to eat, the things I really want to have… I save them for last. For when I'm hungriest. For when I want to eat them the most. Until I want them the most."

"So it's not time yet?" *You let him go for a reason like that? It was a clear chance to kill him.*

Gemini snickered as he watched Jeniela walk ahead. The words went unsaid, a silent thought in the back of his mind.

*That's why I hate you.*
