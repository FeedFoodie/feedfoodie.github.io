The title felt jarringly out of place. *The Little Heavenly Demon? Here?* Lee Sungmin felt a surge of bewilderment as he moved toward the source of the sound.

A foul stench clung to the mansion, the same as every other home in the village, yet this one lacked the specific odor of rotting corpses.

[Though it seems he'll die soon.]

The Void Monarch grumbled from within him. Lee Sungmin pushed open the half-closed door and stepped inside.

Inside, a gaunt Kazuya lay half-propped on a filthy bed, the floor and sheets around him soiled with filth. Rubia, who had followed Lee Sungmin in, wrinkled her nose in disgust.

"The Little Heavenly Demon… no, you're not her," Kazuya muttered. He was forcing his eyes open with all his might, but they were a hazy, unfocused gray.

It was more than just blindness. The Kazuya he had met before had been a formidable martial artist, a man who had reached the level of Supreme Peak. Now, he was simply broken. His eyes could not see and his body could barely move. The sharp senses of a warrior were sealed, and his qi center was utterly destroyed. Kazuya was dying.

"…Do you remember me?" Lee Sungmin asked, giving Rubia a slight glance.

Understanding his gesture, she spread her hands. A single droplet of water materialized between her palms before swirling through the room in a cleansing wave, washing away all the filth and dust in an instant.

"This is…" Kazuya looked surprised. He furrowed his brow, his gaze turning toward Lee Sungmin, but his clouded eyes could not recognize him. He sighed. "Who are you?"

"I came to this forest about fifteen years ago, chasing the traces of Envirus."

"Ah… right. You're Nero. I remember now," Kazuya said. "You barged into our village and killed the elder who was trying to harm you." He added a small laugh. "I'm not blaming you."

"Why have you come back?"

"There's something I need to check inside the forest," Lee Sungmin explained. "But what on earth happened to this village?"

It had not been an attack from an outsider. Lee Sungmin knew that for a fact. Nor had they died fighting amongst themselves.

"It was bound to happen," Kazuya let out a dry chuckle. "Although it did start suddenly, the omens were there. We… the clan of this village, we all share the same blood. From the very roots. We mixed it, and mixed it, and mixed it again. As a result, our bodies grew weak. We came to bear an illness that neither magic nor any Elixir could cure."

Generations of incest had led them to this.

"Even knowing that ruin lay at the end of such procreation, we couldn't help it," Kazuya continued. "The clan's cherished wish was too important. We didn't even know what that wish was, but everyone born here had to pursue it as if they were brainwashed."

The Void Monarch had told him. The people of this village maintained the forest's barrier simply by existing. That was why they could never leave, forced to live on by repeating the cycle of incest. Just as Kazuya said, the village's ruin had been preordained.

Even so, it felt too sudden.

"…When did it start?"

"About a month ago. They just… started dying. How many are gone?"

"Everyone except you."

"I see." Kazuya chuckled without humor. He staggered as he pushed himself up from the bed. Sniffing the clean air, he managed a satisfied smile. "Thank you for cleaning up. I'm used to the stench, but this is still better."

"…Do you know of the Little Heavenly Demon?" Lee Sungmin asked, returning to the question that bothered him.

Kazuya reached out to the side of the bed, opening a drawer and fumbling inside with his fingertips. A quick glance revealed it was full of grain pills. He took out a handful and tossed them into his mouth.

"Before the ruin began," he said after swallowing. "Yes, a month ago. The Little Heavenly Demon visited the village."

A month ago. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard. That would have been right after he was released from the Sealing. While he had been waking up in Germud and getting a grasp of the situation, Wiji Hoyeon had come to this village and met Kazuya.

"She was polite about it," Kazuya recalled. "Said she wanted to enter the Sleeping Forest. I couldn't refuse. Her attitude was polite, but the power I felt from her made it impossible to deny her to her face. Not that I had any particular reason to refuse, anyway."

So a month ago, Wiji Hoyeon had entered the Sleeping Forest. What happened after that? Rubia had said she appeared in the Elemental Realm ten days ago. Was there a reason for her to come here first?

"Did she tell you why?"

"She said it was something she had to do. It was a vague statement, but I didn't press her for more. The reason wasn't important." Kazuya muttered as he got off the bed. He stood unsteadily, catching his breath as he straightened his bent back. Lee Sungmin quietly moved to his side and supported him.

"I don't know how much longer I have," Kazuya murmured. "Our clan has existed to maintain the forest's barrier. Now… everyone is dead except for me. If I die too, the barrier will disappear completely."

"What happens then?"

"I don't know. We just… existed. If I die, the barrier will disappear, but I don't know what happens after that. It's never happened before."

Lee Sungmin asked about the ancient monsters Sealed inside the forest, but Kazuya just shook his head.

"I'm sorry I couldn't be of more help."

"It's alright."

Kazuya was just as clueless as he was. He and his people had simply lived here for hundreds of years for the sake of the forest's Sealing, and now, only Kazuya remained.

The End.

The phrase drifted through Lee Sungmin's mind. Osla had said this world's fate was sealed as The End, and even at this very moment, that destiny was steadily advancing. In the midst of it all, the villagers who maintained this crucial Sealing had all died, leaving only one man behind.

[This forest must also be related to The End.] the Void Monarch muttered.

And Wiji Hoyeon had entered this forest.

*What could have happened?* He recalled the Wiji Hoyeon he had seen at the Moon Palace, her expression annoyed after being caught in the sudden spatial erosion, unable to finish her fight with the Spirit Queen. She had only said her reason for coming here was because it was 'necessary'. There was too little information. Had she entered this forest to stop The End?

They stopped at the forest entrance, where a complex tangle of wooden vines blocked the path.

[It's gotten weaker.]

It was just as the Void Monarch said. The forest's barrier was noticeably weaker than it had been fifteen years ago, with many rotten spots visible on the vines.

"Is it badly rotted?" Kazuya asked, his voice thin.

"......"

"But it's still there. Because I'm still alive," he muttered, staggering toward the barrier. He made a cut on his palm to draw blood, then pressed his blood-soaked hand against the vines.

With a rustling sound, the barrier opened.

"Thank you."

"Don't mention it. This is my role, after all," Kazuya replied, wiping his bloody palm.

Lee Sungmin gave him a deep bow before stepping through the opening.

KIIING.

A strange sense of disharmony brushed past him. The Sleeping Forest was a place that not even a Fairy Horse could enter. He tried summoning his as a test, but the creature did not appear. It seemed Osla had been right. This forest was in this world, but not a place belonging to it.

[It's been a while, so it feels rather nostalgic,] the Void Monarch laughed heartily. [Still, it's the place I was stuck in for over three hundred years.]

*What did you do for three hundred years?*

[I mostly just slept. Or reminisced about the old days. It wasn't very fun. It was much more enjoyable being with you.]

*Could you not say such embarrassing things?*

[What's so embarrassing about it? It's the truth. That's why this Elder has done so many things for you. Just ten years ago, if I hadn't stepped in, you would've been completely devoured by the Slaughter Predator.]

*That… I know. Of course I'm grateful.*

[It's quite amusing to hear such things from a guy with balls.]

The Void Monarch snickered. Lee Sungmin ignored the teasing and continued deeper into the forest.

A potent sense of disharmony permeated the space, making his skin tingle as unseen gazes pressed in from all directions. He looked around, but saw only the scenery of an ordinary forest. The beings sending the gazes were nowhere to be seen. The result was the same even when he expanded his senses. No one was there. Only the feeling of being watched.

"…Ugh…"

Rubia felt the gazes as well. Seeing her tremble, Lee Sungmin pointed to his own chest. She nodded, transformed into a sphere of light, and hid inside him. Just in case, he took out his spear and held it ready.

Something was watching him. They were only observing. The owners of the stares took no action.

*Could it be the monsters that were Sealed long ago?*

Lee Sungmin ignored the eerie feeling and moved forward. Before long, he arrived at the rock where he had first met the Void Monarch, who had been waiting for him there in the form of a giant flame. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the stone for a moment as the Void Monarch chuckled at the memory.

He passed the rock, venturing beyond the point he had ever been before. The sense of disharmony grew stronger the further he went, and the gazes became more blatant. The surroundings were dark, as almost no sunlight penetrated the thick canopy.

Then, Lee Sungmin's steps came to a halt.

He stood blankly, looking ahead. In the pitch-black darkness of the forest, a figure was standing.

It was not a 'something'. Lee Sungmin knew all too well who that was.

He slowly advanced into the darkness. She stood unmoving, watching him. His chest throbbed, his heart hammering against his ribs so fast he thought it might burst. She watched him approach without saying a word.

There were so many things he wanted to say, so many things he wanted to ask.

*I can't be the only one,* he thought, clenching his trembling hands. They knew each other too well. Most of Wiji Hoyeon's 'firsts' had been with him.

Lee Sungmin looked at her face, standing in the darkness. Was she smiling, crying, angry, or all of the above? Her expression was a complex mixture of emotions as she watched him. The distance between them was short. He raised his hand and reached out toward her.

His hand passed right through her body.

He knew. He could see her with his eyes, but she was not truly here. Unlike what his vision told him, he could not feel her presence at all.

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly. Seeing him smile, Wiji Hoyeon smiled back.

*Can she hear me?*

There was only one way to find out. Lee Sungmin opened his mouth and spoke to her.

"I missed you."

After a brief silence, she replied.

"Me too."

Their voices connected. Hearing her words, the trembling in his chest intensified. He raised a hand and pressed it firmly against his heart.

"…What… are you doing here?"

"I've ended up in a ridiculous state," she said. "I went to kill the Spirit Queen, but it seems I was a little lacking in power."

"What about the Spirit Queen?"

"She's wandering in a different place than I am. Still, that wench is better off than me. I'm completely stuck between the cracks of space, but she found a way to escape."

Wiji Hoyeon said it as if it were nothing, but Lee Sungmin could not easily accept her words. Stuck between the cracks of space. He did not know much about such things, but it was undoubtedly a serious situation.

"How can I save you?"

"It's Buddhist," she said, shrugging her shoulders.

The gazes intensified. Lee Sungmin focused his eyes and looked behind her. A countless number of corpses were piled up there in the gloom.

"It's faster than I expected, but it was something that had to be done."

"…What are you doing?"

"I'm killing monsters," Wiji Hoyeon answered. "There are many monsters Sealed in this forest. Thanks to a coincidence created by that wench, the Spirit Queen, I was able to enter this space."

She muttered, "Though thanks to that, I can't touch you right now," and reached out her hand. Just as his had, her hand passed through his face.

"Still… I'm glad I can see you with my own two eyes and talk to you." Wiji Hoyeon gave a faint smile.

"Because I'm doing all this for you."
