"You fought well," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, his gaze fixed on Lee Sungmin. "I saw you fight the Dark Supreme and thought you handled the Demonic Spirit's power quite well then, but you've far surpassed that level now. It was like watching the Demonic Spirit itself in battle."

Lee Sungmin looked down at his arms. They had been broken during the fight by a move rooted in martial theory, yet he felt no pain. They had already healed completely in the heat of the battle.

"Your Buddhist-like body is interesting," the Chairman continued, "but the energy you wield is also intriguing. This one already knows that a great Demonic Spirit resides within you."

*It seems to be quite obvious,* Void Monarch grumbled from within.

"I never knew the power of a Demonic Spirit and inner qi could be so well-coordinated, but there's something else mixed in besides that."

"What do you mean?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Dragon," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stated simply. "I don't know if you're using it consciously, but you're using Dragon's Pressure. Are you aware of that?"

"Dragon's... what?"

"Pressure," the Chairman repeated, his face indifferent. "Kid, when you seriously draw out your inner qi and Demonic Energy, Dragon's Pressure gets mixed in. It goes without saying for those weaker than you, but even those who are somewhat superior will feel the weight of it. Even that girl, the Little Heavenly Demon, was momentarily overwhelmed."

The words sent Lee Sungmin's mind back to the Namgung Clan. He recalled how the warriors who had stepped forward to face him couldn't even attack properly once he had gotten serious. They had either collapsed on the spot or coughed up blood from internal injuries.

"...Wasn't that soulmates?"

"You're talking nonsense." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a dry laugh. "Do you think it's possible to inflict internal injuries with just your aura? Of course, for a great being like this one, it's possible. At your level, however, it's completely out of the question."

Lee Sungmin didn't think his level was so low as to be dismissed so easily, and he stared at the Chairman with a bitter expression.

"You call it soulmates," the Chairman elaborated, "but inflicting internal injuries with just your aura is Buddhist-impossible at your level. What you thought was soulmates was actually Dragon's Pressure."

Lee Sungmin nodded with a blank expression. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stood with his hands clasped behind his back, observing him.

"How do you feel?" he asked.

"...What do you mean?"

"Didn't you just defeat an opponent you considered a goal, an aspiration, an ideal? Don't you feel some sense of accomplishment?"

Accomplishment. Lee Sungmin mulled over the word. He honestly wasn't sure. The reality of his victory still hadn't properly sunk in.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a hollow laugh as he studied Lee Sungmin's face. "I thought so. You're too blindly focused on the Little Heavenly Demon. The world is vast, and there are plenty of masters who surpass her."

"What does that have to do with anything?"

"True, it's not this one's place to care. So... what do you say?"

"What do you mean?"

"About becoming this one's disciple," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked with a grin. "You've fought the Little Heavenly Demon, so you should know well enough how strong she is. This one can take her on in a Ten Exchanges Match."

"...You said you like struggling mediocrities," Lee Sungmin sighed, finally speaking his mind. "There must be plenty of struggling mediocrities in this world."

"Even so, I can't just take any dog or cow as a disciple. I have to take someone who is qualified to some extent, someone who has reached a certain level."

"And that's me?"

"And your situation too." Behind the mask, the Chairman's eyes curved into a smile. "You've made an enemy of the Murim Alliance. By killing Heaven Beyond Heaven's Fist Honored One and the Sword Saint, you've also become an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven. Even as a master of the Transcendent Realm, it will be difficult for you to survive alone against both of them."

"Are you telling me to become your disciple so you can protect me?"

"The Murim Alliance is connected to Heaven Beyond Heaven. Did you know that?"

Lee Sungmin nodded. "I know that the Murim Alliance Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, is connected to Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"Oh? You even know that much. Well then, do you know what that means?"

"...Is there some other meaning?"

"The Murim Alliance is an old, very large organization," the Chairman continued slowly. "It's not just a group of the orthodox murim. The Murim Alliance is connected to various powers. The nobles of this world. Merchants. Mages. Guilds. They have built trust through long years of exchange." He let out a laugh. "You became an enemy of the Murim Alliance. The trigger was killing the Iron Armored Divine Spear. After that, your killing of Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin inflated your notoriety."

"...I killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear because he wanted to die by my hand," Lee Sungmin clarified, "and I didn't kill Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin."

"Is that so? It doesn't change anything. You're alone, and the Murim Alliance is a group. If they say you did it, you have no way to overturn it. Who's going to listen to you if you go around shouting that you didn't do it, that it's unfair?"

Lee Sungmin couldn't refute it and fell silent. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled as he watched him.

"The Murim Alliance isn't moving aggressively yet. Of course, with your power, you'd have almost no match among their martial artists. You could probably annihilate an entire clan from the Nine Sects One Gang or the prestigious clans all by yourself." He had confirmed as much at the Namgung Clan. The Chairman, however, shook his head. "Wudang is a different matter."

"Wudang?"

"Because the old man, the Sword Immortal, is in Wudang."

"...Is the Sword Immortal that strong?"

"If you were to attack the Sword Immortal, your head would be cut off within ten seconds," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed heartily. "I've said this before, but this one doesn't consider anyone in this world troublesome except for three. The Wudang Sword Immortal, the Martial God of the Outer Heavens, and Predator's Slaughter Predator. Among them, Slaughter Predator is no longer alive, so in reality, there's no one who can match this one except for those two. As for the magic side, this one doesn't know much about it, so I don't even consider it."

They were incredibly arrogant words, but the one saying them was the man who stood at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. He had every right to say such things.

"I don't know the reason," the Chairman went on, "but the Murim Alliance isn't seriously trying to kill you yet. If they had genuinely tried, you would have been in a very difficult position."

"Why aren't they seriously trying to kill me?"

"How would this one know that? In any case, if you become this one's disciple, you won't have to worry much about your disputes with the Murim Alliance or Heaven Beyond Heaven. Since they've already branded you a demonic practitioner, wouldn't it be the best method to act like one and join the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?"

"Predator," Lee Sungmin said, staring intently at the Chairman's face. "Wouldn't joining Predator also be one of the options?"

"You call yourself human, yet you want to immerse yourself in the martial arts theory of the inhuman? Haha! Well, that would be one way. With your skills, you could stand on the same level as the old monsters of Predator. If that's what you want." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman burst out laughing as he spoke.

Lee Sungmin let out a short sigh. He didn't want to join Predator. From the beginning, he had no other choice. He had come to Legro Forest for a reunion with Wiji Hoyeon, but he had also come to get help from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Now that he was an enemy of both the Murim Alliance and Heaven Beyond Heaven, he needed an ally. He had simply planned to join the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, but becoming the Chairman's disciple was an even better deal.

"Do you have any other disciples?" he asked.

"None," the Chairman answered immediately. "To this day, this one has never taken a single disciple. I've never had children either."

"...You don't have children either?"

"I never felt the need to have them. Nor did I ever feel the desire."

*I wonder if that bastard's a virgin?* Void Monarch muttered, having listened to the entire exchange.

"...I understand." Lee Sungmin made up his mind and nodded. "I will become your disciple."

It occurred to him then that this was the first time he had formally become someone's disciple. He had learned many martial arts from Wiji Hoyeon, but they had never entered a master-disciple relationship. Even with Baek Sogo, they only had a Junior Disciple relationship when he learned the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. He hadn't formed such a relationship with the Crazy Heavenly Demon either, not even when he learned the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

"Good." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded.

Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, waiting for what came next. The Chairman frowned behind his mask as he looked at him.

"...Is that it?"

"Is there something we're supposed to do?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Well, when you become a disciple, don't you do the Nine Bows or... something like that?"

"There's no need to, really. Do you want to try it?" the Chairman asked, standing askew.

At the question, Lee Sungmin shook his head with a reluctant expression. He had never done it before, but that didn't mean he wanted to start now.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman looked at him for a moment longer. "Tell me about the martial arts you've learned."

"All of them?"

"All of them."

Lee Sungmin hesitated, but in the end, he told the Chairman everything. He listed the Violet Dawn Divine Art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. After hearing him out, the Chairman spoke again.

"Do you have the manuals?"

"...I do, but."

"Give them to me."

Lee Sungmin opened his Subspace Pocket. He had kept the manuals for the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear that he received from Wiji Hoyeon deep inside, along with the manuals for the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman took the four manuals and skimmed through them right where he stood. "First, we need to overhaul your inner arts technique."

"......"

"The Violet Dawn Divine Art. It's a good martial art, but this one's inner arts technique is superior. To learn this one's martial arts, you must learn this one's inner arts technique."

"Doesn't that mean I have to give up my progress in the Violet Dawn Divine Art?"

"Didn't I say we'd overhaul it? I'll add this one's heart technique without discarding the Violet Dawn Divine Art, so there won't be a problem." After saying that, the Chairman opened the manual for the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. "What's this Sungmin Style?"

"...That Spear Technique is one Wiji Hoyeon created for me. She modified the original martial art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, to..."

"A calamity is a calamity, I see. When did she make this?"

"Eleven years ago."

"Just as I thought." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman snickered. "It's well-made, but it's a little lacking. This one will supplement this as well."

"The Spear Technique?"

"This one doesn't use a spear, but it's possible to mix this one's martial arts into this Spear Technique."

"Is that enough?"

"You should know yourself that your talent isn't that great. If you were to abandon all the martial arts you've already learned and learn this one's, how long would it take for you to reach a high level?"

Next, the manual for the Dancing Shadow Soul Break was opened. "This is quite an interesting movement art. It's based on teleportation and has a wide variety of changes. Plus, it even incorporates an enhanced qi art." The corners of the Chairman's mouth curled up into a grin. "This movement art will suit this one's martial arts well."

The last one he opened was the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. "There's no need to touch this one. It's an excellent enhanced qi art. If you learn this one's inner arts technique, you'll be able to display its power through this."

"What is your martial art?"

"You. How long do you plan on addressing this one like that?" In response to Lee Sungmin's question, the Chairman glared from behind his mask. "Haven't we just entered a master-disciple relationship?"

"...Master, what is your martial art?"

"Heaven Reversal Black Thunder (?)," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman answered. "All of this one's martial arts are based on the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder." He slowly lifted his foot. "Watch carefully. Don't miss it."

After he spoke, his foot touched the ground.

CRACKLE!

Black electricity sparked. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's figure vanished from before Lee Sungmin's eyes. It was just as he had felt before. That wasn't some kind of high-speed movement. Not even an afterimage remained. The Chairman had completely disappeared from that space.

His voice echoed from everywhere at once. "The Gale Thunder of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It's a light movement art that you can only begin to imitate once you enter the Transcendent Realm. It's similar to the Space Leap those mage bastards use, but this one is superior. Compare them someday if you get the chance."

"Can I use that too?" Lee Sungmin asked the empty air.

"If you learn it, you'll be able to." The Chairman's voice now came from behind him. He tucked the four manuals into his robes. "For the time being, just hang out in the forest. This one will be looking over these manuals."

"Hang out?"

"Time will pass quite quickly if you play with the Fairy Realms." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's foot lifted up again. "I'm telling you this just in case, but even if the Fairy Realm plays pranks on you, do not harm them. If you do, this one will kill you first."

Black electricity sparked once more, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's figure disappeared.
