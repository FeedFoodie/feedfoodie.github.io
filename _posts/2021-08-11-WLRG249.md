Where had it all gone wrong?

*I did nothing wrong.*

With that simple conclusion, Kim Jonghyun pushed himself to his feet. The ritual had failed. It had collapsed at the very cusp of completion, a turn of events that was, for him, a stroke of incredible luck. He was fortunate it had ended at this stage. Had the failure come any earlier, his body would have been unable to reconstitute itself and would have simply vanished into nothingness.

"......"

A violent retch seized him, and he doubled over, vomiting a slurry of blackish blood and the unformed fragments of his own organs. Gasping, Kim Jonghyun took stock of his condition. The body that should have been reconstructed as a Demon Lord remained trapped in an incomplete state. Still, it was an improvement. Before, he had wielded the magic of a Demon Lord in a human vessel, but now, he had drawn somewhat closer to a true Demon Lord's form.

*It would have been better if I had succeeded, though.*

A successful ritual would have granted him the full measure of a Demon Lord's power. He would have absorbed all of Caladra's magic lingering in the Demon Realm and used it to perfect his new body completely, but the ritual had failed, and using Reversal Magic a second time was impossible.

Kim Jonghyun ground his teeth. He had begun the ritual more from curiosity than genuine expectation, yet having success snatched away at the final moment was agonizing.

*He's still in this forest.*

Why had the boy not killed him? He had been given plenty of chances. Kim Jonghyun dragged his staggering body from the pit.

On the ground nearby, the Grimoire lay dormant, its light extinguished. As Kim Jonghyun reached for it, the tome began to glow once more and shot into the air, hovering before him.

"As I thought," Kim Jonghyun muttered, his voice low as he flipped through its pages.

Even Arbes had never managed to decipher the entire Grimoire, but the current Kim Jonghyun could read its contents in their entirety. As his body was reconstructed to be closer to that of a Demon Lord, he had naturally come to understand the Demon Realm's script engraved within its pages.

*Using... all of it is impossible.*

This was a magic tome for a Demon Lord. Why would such a thing even exist in a world where no Demon Lord had manifested? To use the Grimoire, one required the magic power of a Demon Lord. Even Arbes had been incapable of it. Only Kim Jonghyun could wield its magic.

*To say it's a magic tome meant for me... that's too arrogant.*

It seemed impossible, but perhaps there were mages who could break their contract with a Demon Lord and wield such power. No, wait. An image of Lee Sungmin flashed in his mind. Kim Jonghyun was well aware that Lee Sungmin possessed the Black Heart, and he knew the nature of that heart all too well.

The emerging thought brought his mind to a brief, chilling halt. It was only a hypothesis, but if it was Lee Sungmin, the owner of the Black Heart, he might just be able to wield the magic power of a Demon Lord. Lee Sungmin himself would not even need to become a Black Mage. If he, with his Black Heart, were to devour the heart of a Black Mage...

*The contract wouldn't transfer. He would only gain the Black Mage's magic power.*

In that state, Lee Sungmin would be able to use the magic of the Grimoire. Yes, even Reversal Magic. Perhaps this magic tome was meant for him all along.

Kim Jonghyun quietly shook his head, dismissing the crazy thought. It was too much, even for someone who had died and returned to the past, even for someone at the center of a great Destiny Force.

*Perhaps the one destined to become the Demon Lord... was you, not me.*

He did not care. Destiny Force or whatever it was, it no longer mattered. Kim Jonghyun continued to flip through the Grimoire's pages. Because he had not completely transformed, using all the magic within was impossible, but there were still many useful spells at his disposal.

While scanning the magic, Kim Jonghyun assessed the situation in the forest. His eyebrow twitched as he confirmed the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King were locked in combat.

*Who the hell is this guy?*

Kim Jonghyun had no idea who the Spear King was, but watching the two fight, he could tell the man was a master of incredible skill.

*Ju Won?*

His eyes widened when he confirmed Ju Won's presence in the forest. It did not take him long to figure out why he was here. He already knew that Ju Won was the King of Lycanthropes and that he carried Howon's long-cherished wish to fight a Dragon. This area was Ju Won's territory. Had he come to this forest to fulfill that wish after sensing the Black Dragon Knight?

A bitter smile touched Kim Jonghyun's lips. He could not know how things would turn out, but Ju Won's intervention would certainly spell trouble for the Black Dragon Knight.

*I'd like to lend a hand... but I'm not in good shape right now.*

He had yet to confirm the extent of the Grimoire's magic he could wield with this body and had no desire to damage it further by doing anything unnecessary. Without hesitation, Kim Jonghyun summoned a Ghost Steed. His first priority was to get away from this place.

Ju Won stared at Lee Sungmin with a blank expression. After a moment of silence, he finally spoke.

"I know you're different from the Dragon I saw, but do I need to fight that one instead of you?"

"Because I'm not a Dragon."

At Lee Sungmin's reply, the corners of Ju Won's mouth twitched into a thin smile.

"That's right. You're not a Dragon," Ju Won muttered, as if convincing himself. The ferocious terror and pressure that had filled the air began to subside.

THUD, THUD, THUD.

From not too far away, the sounds of the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight rampaging through the forest echoed repeatedly. Ju Won gazed in that direction for a moment before nodding.

"However, it's also true that I can feel a Dragon's power from you. It seems I can fulfill Howon's long-cherished wish by fighting you as well."

[Are you trying to take the easy way out?] the Void Monarch sneered, having taken form with Demonic Energy. [This one is absurdly weak compared to a Dragon. He's only using a fraction of a Dragon's power because he ate the heart of one I killed long ago. Do you really think you can fulfill Howon's wish by fighting a brat like this?]

Calling him a brat seemed a bit harsh, but Lee Sungmin did not object, simply listening to the Void Monarch's words. Ju Won narrowed his eyes, his sharp gaze fixed on the Void Monarch.

"Void Monarch. It seems that human is quite precious to you."

[Precious? It's not so cringeworthy as that. Still, we've been together for a while, so it'd be a bit much to just let him die a dog's death at your hands.]

"A long time has passed."

[It's no different from you, once called the Mad Wolf for your insane deeds, now feigning politeness.]

At the Void Monarch's reply, Ju Won shrugged and laughed, slowly nodding his head.

"It's a bit late to say this now, but I admired your strength. I revered it. If you hadn't died back then and were still alive, I would have killed Howon and challenged you as well."

[Then you would have died.]

Ju Won chuckled at the Void Monarch's immediate response.

"This time, too, I'll pretend I didn't see you."

With those words, Ju Won walked past Lee Sungmin, heading toward the sounds of the battle between the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King. It would be great if Ju Won killed the Black Dragon Knight, Lee Sungmin thought as he watched his retreating back.

"...Phew!"

Once Ju Won was gone, the others who had been frozen by his presence let out collective sighs of relief. Lee Sungmin approached the pale-faced Scarlet.

"Are you all right?"

"I think... I'm okay? Ugh..." Scarlet frowned and clutched her hair. She glanced at Ju Won's retreating back and swallowed hard. "What in the world is that monster?"

"He's the King of Lycanthropes," Lee Sungmin said honestly. "We were lucky. If he had decided to start a massacre here, I would have had a hard time stopping him."

To be honest, he had been repeatedly lucky today. He had unexpectedly encountered the Spear King but had not been forced to fight. Then, just as he was about to face the Black Dragon Knight, the Spear King had intervened. Now, he had been on the verge of fighting Ju Won, only to be spared thanks to the Black Dragon Knight's presence. The Black Dragon Knight, Ju Won, and the Spear King were all opponents far too formidable for the current Lee Sungmin. Had he fought any of them, there was a high probability he would have been defeated and killed.

"We will hold the Murim Alliance responsible for this incident," Lloyd announced to the surviving warriors of the Murim Alliance and the Moyong Clan.

Most of the Moyong Clan's warriors were dead, but Moyong Daeun's son, Moyong Chan, had survived. Moyong Chan did not seem to hear Lloyd's words. He was staring blankly at the corpse of his father. Lee Sungmin felt a sense of bitterness looking at him but offered no words of comfort. Instead, he turned to Scarlet.

"What will you do?"

"What?"

"If your position has become difficult, you can come with me to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. I'll make sure a place is prepared for you, Scarlet."

"...Hmm."

Scarlet hesitated for a moment at his offer. Honestly, until just a moment ago, she had been planning to do just that. Her thoughts had changed slightly, however, after the other Magic Tower Masters had stood their ground and tried to protect her.

"You should go," Lloyd said. "Our relationship with the Murim Alliance will certainly worsen because of this, and they will put pressure on you." As he spoke, Lloyd glanced at Lee Sungmin. "The stances of the orthodox and unorthodox sects are none of the Mages Guild's business. However, the Murim Alliance and the Mages Guild have maintained a relationship for a long time. If they make a polite request, it will be difficult for us to refuse."

"Are you telling me to leave now?" Scarlet asked.

Lloyd nodded. "It would be better for both of us. For you, and for the Mages Guild."

"...Well. I was thinking the same thing," Scarlet agreed with a nod. There was no great need for her to remain at the Mages Guild anyway. She glanced at Lee Sungmin. The place she would be relying on was not a bad one. During her years as the Crimson Archmage, she had gathered enough magic tomes and knowledge to stand on her own, so resigning from her position as a Magic Tower Master was not a major issue.

"There'll be a room for me at the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, right?"

"I'm sure there will be," Lee Sungmin replied as he summoned the Fairy Horse.

Not knowing what might happen next, he planned to use the horse to leave this place entirely. Using it here meant he could only call upon it one more time, but he refused to take any unnecessary risks. If he were alone, he could manage, but with Scarlet, he had to eliminate as much risk as possible.

Seeing the summoned creature, Lloyd and the other mages wore expressions of utter astonishment.

"If I'm not mistaken... isn't this the Fairy Queen's horse?" Lloyd muttered, his eyes full of awe.

"That's right."

"To think you've formed a connection with the Fairy Queen as well." Lloyd let out a dry laugh and shook his head. "Come to think of it, I'm late in asking. Did you meet with the Master?"

At the question about Envirus, Lee Sungmin gave a bitter smile. He did not have fond memories of the man. Envirus may have had his own circumstances, but it was an undeniable fact that he had tried to kill Lee Sungmin and had never intended to fulfill his promise.

"I did meet him."

"He was doing well, I presume?"

"...He must be doing well."

He did not bother to mention that Envirus had nearly died and had fled to the Elemental Realm. At Lee Sungmin's answer, Lloyd sighed and nodded.

"In any case, the promise I made to you long ago, I have certainly fulfilled it."

"Thank you."

"It was a difficult request," Lloyd muttered, glancing at the Holy Knight Commander.

The Holy Knight Commander stood with his mouth shut, as if a cat had got his tongue. The fact that Lee Sungmin had summoned the Fairy Horse was reason enough for his journey south from North Trabia. He could no longer find fault with Lee Sungmin. In truth, he did not have much to say anyway, since the young man had saved his life.

"To think the day would come when I'd ride a Fairy Horse," Scarlet muttered in wonder as she mounted the creature with Lee Sungmin.

As Lee Sungmin stroked the horse's mane, it let out a soft snort.

*The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.*

As he thought of their destination, a brilliant white light engulfed him and Scarlet.
