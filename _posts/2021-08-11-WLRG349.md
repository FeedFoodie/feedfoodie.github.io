"What happened?" Lee Sungmin asked, closing the distance between them.

The Spear King stared down at the blood on the back of his hand, his expression horrified, before clicking his tongue loudly. He then staggered to his feet, his grip on the two spears tenuous.

"My legs have been torn."

"...What?"

"A crow-tit trying to follow a stork will tear its legs," the Spear King elaborated, his voice strained. "I thought I could do it, but as expected, it's not easy to make my body do what my mind wants."

"You're only realizing that now?" the Black Dragon Knight sneered from where he stood watching.

He strode over to support the gasping Spear King, but the old man glared and pushed his hand away.

"I don't need to be supported."

"Ignoring my sympathy, are we," the Black Dragon Knight grumbled as he withdrew his hand.

*His legs were torn?* The phrase echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind as he glanced down at the spear in his own hand. The last few moments flashed through his memory, resolving into two distinct images.

The first was of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who, despite having lost most of his power, had evaporated the Martial God's attack with a single gesture before tearing off his arm.

The second was of the Spear King tearing apart Lee Sungmin's own attack with a single, slowly thrust spear.

The two techniques were similar, yet not identical. They started the same, but somewhere in the middle, they diverged. The Spear King's spear path had collapsed midway through the technique, failing to completely push away the enhanced qi he was trying to erase. The backlash had caused him to vomit blood and fall.

*Internal injuries.*

The Spear King had spent ten years training, succeeding only in reaching the very edge of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial prowess. The techniques the Chairman had shown before his death, however, were far too advanced for the Spear King to perform. He had managed to execute the form based on martial arts theory, but he could not maintain it and had suffered internal injuries as a result.

"Are you alright?"

"I'm not."

HACK.

The Spear King scraped the remaining blood from his throat and spat it onto the ground.

"It's frustrating," he seethed. "I really thought I could do it, but I couldn't. To think that after ten years of effort, this is all I've achieved… it's infuriating."

"...How about we stop here? Your internal injuries don't look light."

"Ugh..."

Lee Sungmin had expected a protest, but the Spear King only made a displeased face and did not argue. It seemed his injuries were more severe than they appeared. The Black Dragon Knight shook his head as he watched him.

"I told you not to pick a fight out of the blue."

"I just wanted to see the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial arts."

"So, were you satisfied?" Lee Sungmin asked.

The Spear King snorted in response. "He's pretty good at imitating."

"I, too, have yet to reach his level."

"That's only natural, I suppose."

The Spear King plopped down on the spot and sank into deep thought, apparently reviewing the short exchange. A moment later, having organized his thoughts, he opened his mouth.

"Your body is superior to mine," the Spear King began, "and the power you wield is greater. Your spearmanship itself, however, hasn't improved much in the last ten years."

"I haven't been training like you for the past ten years."

"But there are some interesting things mixed in. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial arts… that is certainly compensating for the lack in your spearmanship. On top of that, your body is on an absurd level. That's why it's a shame. If your spearmanship had been superior to all else, I would have been no match for you."

The Spear King acknowledged the fact nonchalantly.

"Because it's not superior, you can't subdue me. Even if I hadn't gotten greedy at the end and tried a technique beyond my level, you still wouldn't have been able to beat me."

"In that case, are you saying you can subdue me?"

"Well… I'm confident I wouldn't die, though. Hehe, what an unfair thing. That I, who have wielded a single spear for hundreds of years, cannot guarantee I won't be killed by you."

The Spear King laughed out loud, but there was no displeasure or self-pity in his laughter. On the contrary, he was feeling joy.

"How do you think it would compare to the Martial God?"

"You? Or me?"

"Both."

"I believe I am better now than the Martial God was ten years ago. Killing him is another matter… but I don't know about now, after ten years. If the Martial God isn't an idiot, he would have trained for the past ten years due to the humiliation he suffered back then."

The Spear King's words were a surprising revelation. Ten years ago, he and the Black Dragon Knight together could do nothing to the Martial God, yet in just a decade, he had reached a level at least equal to the Martial God of that era. Those words also implied that the current Lee Sungmin was equal to or greater than the Martial God from ten years ago, even though Lee Sungmin had not revealed his full power.

"Then, what will you do," Lee Sungmin asked, looking at the Spear King. "I still haven't received an answer. I need your strength."

"Let me hear it too," the Black Dragon Knight interjected. "What do you want to do? Will you strike the Martial God, or will you strike Predator?"

"Both."

"Even if the Martial God's intention to stop The End is sincere? Isn't he just doing something stupid because of the Spirit?"

"That's beside the point. The Martial God is my Master's enemy." Lee Sungmin's voice sank coldly.

At those words, the Spear King laughed heartily. "So you're saying you'll get revenge first. Good." He nodded his head. "I'll help you."

The Spear King glanced at the Black Dragon Knight. "Now, let's go."

"Am I going too?" the Black Dragon Knight asked, his eyebrow twitching.

The Spear King gave him a strange look. "Then what did you plan to do?"

"...Let's go," the Black Dragon Knight replied with a sigh.

He did not wish for The End either, but it was not as if he could return to the Martial God, who was now a puppet of the Spirit. Would his old master even understand if he explained the situation?

*...I'd be lucky if he doesn't try to kill me on sight.*

He could not do anything on his own, either. For the purpose of stopping The End, acting with Lee Sungmin was, in fact, the best option, even if their opponent was the Vampire Queen, whose limits were unknown.

"Ah, come to think of it," Lee Sungmin said, a sudden memory surfacing as he turned to the Black Dragon Knight. "The wound you got from Ju Won ten years ago. Is it still bad?"

"It's been the same for ten years," the Black Dragon Knight answered with a frown, not wanting to recall the memory. If he had not swallowed his humiliation and fled with all his might, Ju Won would have killed him in that northern forest. "But now, I barely even notice the pain. I've suffered so much on this mountain."

"Still, isn't it inconvenient compared to when you were fine?"

"Well… that's true."

When Lee Sungmin offered to treat the wound, the Black Dragon Knight's expression changed. Even if he had grown used to it, the endlessly throbbing pain in his side was certainly an inconvenience.

*I'll have to make two trips.*

Lee Sungmin decided to first take Yana and Baek Sogo to the Fairy Forest, then return to Mount Mushii. After that, he would spend a day on the mountain. If he returned to Mount Mushii again, he would have ridden the Fairy Horse three times. Taking the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight with him right away would be too risky. He had no desire to repeat the experience of throwing up everything he ate after the fourth leap.

After dropping Yana and Baek Sogo off at the Fairy Forest, Lee Sungmin returned to Mount Mushii. He found the Spear King performing qi circulation while the Black Dragon Knight stood guard, recovering from the internal injuries he had just sustained. Lee Sungmin glanced at the Spear King's even breathing, then sat down not far from the Black Dragon Knight.

"There was something I wanted to ask you."

"What?"

"Why did you act that way ten years ago?"

It was a question he had held for a long time. At the time, the Black Dragon Knight had been under no oath to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. There had been no reason for him to step forward for Lee Sungmin, nor to block the Martial God's path to allow his escape. On the contrary, the most logical choice for him would have been to return to the Martial God's side and help kill Lee Sungmin.

"I thought you were going to ask something important," the Black Dragon Knight chuckled, swallowing the jerky he had been chewing. "It was because I wanted to. That situation… it was absurd. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman could have easily repelled the combined attack of the Moon Queen and the Martial God. The whole idea of a combined attack was unreasonable in the first place."

"So?"

"I didn't particularly like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He was arrogant and selfish, but… he was strong. In truth, for a martial artist, that's all that matters. Being arrogant and selfish just looks like confidence when you have that level of strength." Just as the Spear King had come to admire the Chairman's martial prowess after a single fight, so too had the Black Dragon Knight. "When I came to my senses, I realized I was fascinated by his martial arts. He… he didn't want you to die, and in that moment, I had the power to help you escape. So that's what I did. A big reason was also that I'd lost all respect for the Martial God. Not that I intend to blame him, since he was ultimately a puppet."

The Black Dragon Knight chuckled again.

"The Spear King. I was led into this mountain by that madman, but the reason I endured for ten years was because of the martial prowess of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, which left a deep impression on me. Every martial artist would be the same. In that fight, the Chairman's martial arts… captivated everyone there. Probably… the Martial God too."

Yes.

The Martial God too.

He had discarded his birth name long ago, for the title of Martial God was far more glorious. No one mocked the title he held. He possessed martial prowess sufficient to be called the Martial God, and he knew it well. He did not consider it arrogance. To him, the title was not a name but his very self.

Until ten years ago, he had held an unshakable conviction that among all humans who practiced martial arts, he was at the highest realm, the strongest, the only being who could be called the Martial God. Then he had suffered a crushing defeat at the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Demon Emperor, whom he had thought would be just another worthy beastfolk. The Moon Queen had said the Demon Emperor possessed such power because he was the calamity of The End. The Martial God thought so too. He could not bear it otherwise.

And yet.

For ten years, the Martial God had watched the image of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Endlessly, continuously, without rest, he recalled the fight in the Snowfield. He replayed how the Chairman moved, how he attacked, dodged, and blocked. He analyzed it all, creating the same situation in his mind and continuing to fight the deceased Chairman in his meditation.

However, he was always stuck at the end.

When the powerless Unorthodox Alliance Chairman slowly reached out his hand.

When all the attacks that had descended upon him evaporated with that single gesture.

And then, when his own left arm was torn off.

He could not comprehend that technique at all. For most of the ten years, the Martial God pondered how it was possible. How could such a technique exist in that situation? If that moment were to happen again, could he break through it and kill the Chairman with his own hands?

No.

In the first place, did the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman even die then?

He did not know. He could not understand the events of that day, the things that happened at the very end. One question only led to another, a spiraling obsession he could not escape. In the meditation he had continued for ten years, the Martial God craved an answer.

And today, he found one.

As the Martial God emerged from the cliff cave, the glare of the sun, unseen for ten years, did not register. Outside, the Spirit Medium was waiting, as if she knew he would finish his seclusion at this very time.

"And who is that?" the Martial God asked, looking at the gently smiling woman. Next to her stood a woman he had never seen before.

She was an Elf with long ears. As the Martial God stared, the Elf bowed her head slightly. The Spirit Medium introduced her.

"She is my older sister."

"...Moon Queen?"

"It has been a long time, Martial God," the Elf replied.

The Moon Queen had not died. He had thought the Spirit Medium's words absurd ten years ago, but the aura emanating from the Elf was unmistakably the Moon Queen's. The Martial God stared at her for a moment before nodding.

"I see."

It was an incomprehensible event, but the Martial God understood. It was bizarre, but if it was the work of the Spirit, he had no choice but to accept it.

"Did you find your answer?" the Spirit Medium asked with a gentle smile.

At her words, the Martial God looked up at the sky again. He looked at the sun that was not glaring and answered.

"In my own way."

The Martial God glanced at his limp left sleeve, the last wound left by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. For ten years, he had felt pain from his torn arm.

However, now, there was no pain.
