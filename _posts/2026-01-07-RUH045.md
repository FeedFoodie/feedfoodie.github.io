_— Shining Light Cult, Earth Peak, Palace of Blades_

Swords filled the frugal room, which otherwise held only a single bed and a table. A harsh voice erupted from within.

"Father. Do you really intend to send Yehwa to a wretch like that?"

Father.

It was an affectionate word, but Dan Jiho, his eldest son, only called him that when his stomach churned and he had made up his mind.

Lord Dan Jeonghak finished his qi circulation and straightened his clothes. "...are you truly causing a scene this early in the morning?"

"Yes, I _intend_ to cause a scene. If that's not enough, I'll even start a fight! How am I to stand by and watch this happen? I'd hoped he might change a little after learning some discipline in the Heavenly Demon Cave, but what is this!"

Seething with anger, Dan Jiho stomped his foot. _THUD_. He shouted again.

It was about the Young Cult Leader again. This wasn't the first or second time Dan Jiho, who adored his sister, had raised hell over the engagement.

The scoundrel of a heir to the Cult and the Frost Flower, Dan Yehwa. The entire world laughed at the mismatched pair, so how could her older brother stay silent? Just when it seemed he had quieted down after entering isolated training, it looked like he was starting up again.

However, Dan Jeonghak had no intention of reversing his decision, neither then nor now.

"If you're going to speak nonsense, then leave."

"_Father!_"

At his continuous shouts, Dan Jeonghak's sword-like eyebrows shot up as he raised his aura.

_SHIIIIK, CHAK!_

One of the hanging swords slid from its scabbard, revealing a famed blade. The sheath flew through the air into his hand.

"I told you to leave."

"I can't. No, I won't. Please, call off Yehwa's marriage immediately!"

"You bastard, until the very end...!"

Dan Jeonghak lightly swung the scabbard.

But then.

_SHIIIIIK, KLANG!_

"Do I still look like a novice to you? I'm the Leader of the Martial Sword Corps' Assassination Squad!"

"You blocked it?"

Twin flames lit up in Dan Jeonghak's eyes. This seemed to make Dan Jiho even angrier; he panted heavily as he spat out his words.

"Don't you feel any pity for Yehwa? Do you even know what that bastard did? They say he was fooling around at the Palace of Bliss just a few days ago! He may be heir to the Cult, but if you had any consideration for your own honor, Father, for the Palace of Blades, or for Yehwa— even a tiny bit!— you wouldn't stand for this!! This is unacceptable! Ptui!"

Dan Jiho spat on the floor and stormed out.

Anger surged within him. He couldn't bear to let his little sister be tied to such a scumbag.

His father had always been obsessed with swords, and he himself, lacking talent, had devoted himself day and night solely to training. Thanks to that, he had managed to rise to the position of Squad Leader based on his skill, but when he finally looked back, he found his sister all alone, accustomed to solitude.

Dan Yehwa had inherited their father's qualities completely. There was a time when he'd thought his sister had everything. But what she'd truly needed was her family— her older brother and her father.

_I refuse... I absolutely refuse to tolerate this! If Father won't step in, then I will!_

He steeled his resolve.

But of course—

"So you've overcome your limitations through pure effort... To think you're already on the cusp of the Supreme Peak Realm! As expected of my son."

—Dan Jeonghak, a martial arts fanatic, had different priorities.

Even so, Dan Jiho's words still bothered him.

"Commander, are you there?"

As he asked, a neat voice flowed down from the roof.

"_He spoke true._"

No preamble was needed. The fact was stated so starkly that Dan Jeonghak closed his eyes, clenched his fists tightly, and swallowed a groan.

"Mhn...!"

"_...understood._"

He was being told to bring the Young Cult Leader immediately. He had previously gone to fetch His Reverence under Dan Jeonghak's orders, but had returned empty-handed due to his absence.

But this time would be different. Escorting him respectfully was one thing, but capturing him was another.

_Fwoosh._

The presence on the roof vanished. At the same time, the unsheathed sword flew through the air and slid into the scabbard held by Dan Jeonghak.

His eyes flashed open, and he muttered, his mind made up.

"Training it is."

A good mood, a bad mood. A good day, a bad day. Whatever the reason… it was a reason to train.

That was the Sword Demon, Dan Jeonghak.

_— Shining Light Cult, Water Peak, Palace of Merchants_

Cheon Semin lay on his bed, clutching a single sheet of paper to his chest and grinning from ear to ear. He held it respectfully, as if it were a deed to a property with excellent feng shui.

"Fufufu."

A sly laugh, unfitting for his large frame, escaped him. It was all because of a letter from Cheon Birang that had arrived today.

@[**Palace Lord. It's me, Cheon Birang. I have successfully removed the Young Cult Leader from the Palace of Bliss on time, so you don't need to worry. For the time being, I plan to stay by his side and look for an opportunity. So please, set your worries aside. In fishing, what's important is patience and time. Perhaps... I might even catch a big fish. Of course, that is something only the heavens would know. In any case, I'll be staying at the Palace of Minor Light, so I pray for your well-being.**]@

@[**Furthermore, to gain his trust, I will need funds. Please send a generous amount to the Palace of Minor Light. It is said that crisis is opportunity.**]@

@[**From the Golden Cavalry Commander, Cheon Birang**]@

"That boy... he's already slithered into their den? Right, good, good! But...is he confident he can hook a big catch or not? Even a hint of a black fish would be fine. Anyway... fufu!"

Cheon Semin smoothed out the letter, read it again, and let out another mean laugh.

Appointing the young Cheon Birang as Commander despite everyone's opposition had been a masterstroke. Although he came from the ranks of mere lay believers, the boy had excellent qualities.

His greatest talent was his ability to blend into any group without overstepping his bounds. For a merchant, there was no better talent. That was why Cheon Semin had invested generously in Cheon Birang since his childhood— whether it was martial arts, miracle herbs, or books.

In short, Cheon Birang was an asset he trusted more than his own children.

"Fufu... As expected of Cheon Birang, my Oyiradai[^1]. I've raised one fine horse! Good. I'll trust you again this time and invest generously. Kuhahaha!"

Cheon Semin's satisfied, maniacal laughter echoed.

_— Shining Light Cult, Sun Peak, Palace of Minor Light_

"One."

"_Determination Lasts Three Days!_"

"Two."

"_Discipline Lasts Forever!_"

In the garden of the Palace of Minor Light, a group did handstand push-ups in time with the refreshing chant. There were fourteen of them. To be precise, one person gave orders and thirteen followed them.

Among the latter was, of course, the very same Cheon Birang that Cheon Semin trusted and cherished so much.

_Ugh... Just how long do I have to keep doing this! No, why do I have to be doing this at all!_

Cheon Birang was filled with indignation. Who in the world would treat a guest so poorly? Even if this was the Palace of Minor Light, and even if he was the Great Heavenly Demon, this wasn't right.

It had all started when they entered the Blood Asura's annex. As they offered their greetings, the Blood Asura had suddenly rushed at them and shoved pills into their mouths.

_I look forward to working with you... gurk?_

_This will suppress your qi._

_What... what is this rudeness?! Is this... could it be... Qi Dispersing Poison?!_

_His Reverence may trust you, but I do not. For the duration of your stay, you will all train with me._

_This is not right. I must speak to His Reverence at once!_

_He's not home._

_He was here just a moment ago, what nonsense is this..._

_I will not say it twice. Starting today... you train._

And so, for several days, they did nothing but train. The ground was already soaked dark with the pitter-patter of falling sweat.

"That's enough. Gather again at the Hour of the Tiger."

As the Blood Asura's low voice spread, everyone snapped to attention and answered vigorously.

"Yes, sir-!"

"Dismissed."

As the Blood Asura left, Cheon Birang collapsed onto the ground.

Who were they? They were peak martial artists, experts worthy of the name, and yet, here they were, doing the kind of brutish physical conditioning they hadn't done since they were third-rate.

"But you know, as ridiculously tough as it is, it feels strangely refreshing when it's over. I feel like I have more energy, too."

He turned his head at the lighthearted voice beside him. It was Yayul Tak, the second son of the Yayul Clan. He grinned foolishly as he wiped the sweat from his brow with his sleeve.

_Hmm... He's not wrong. The Heavy Hand Technique is basic, but... there's a limit if the body can't keep up. Maybe... this is why I can't break through my plateau. My current body is insufficient for the stage I'm trying to reach... Perhaps the Great Heavenly Demon gave us this trial to help us achieve enlightenment?_

Cheon Birang closed his eyes, savoring the fatigue in his body. Perhaps because of Yayul Tak's words, he felt a particular sense of refreshment today.

A man who easily blended in anywhere: the Oyiradai, Cheon Birang.

He was thus being blended into the colors of the Palace of Minor Light.

{sep}

"Hoo..."

_Clack_. He threw open the window, and a fresh breeze cooled the heat in the room. For several days, he had forbidden himself from going out, focusing on training his body and cultivating the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

He had intended to head straight for the Palace of Murder, but Senior and Han Soso had both adamantly stopped him, arguing that it was too dangerous to go in without a plan.

_The Palace of Murder, the Palace of Martial Blood, and the Palace of Blades. Unlike the other Hallowed Palaces, these are places founded for battle. How far can I, as I am now, stand against them?_

One, five, ten, fifty, five hundred, five thousand.

It was a formula commonly cited since ancient times. If the level of a third-rate martial artist was one, then a second-rate martial artist was five. A first-rate martial artist was ten. A peak master was fifty. A Supreme Peak master was five hundred. A Transcendent Realm or Extreme Demon master was rated at five thousand.

This meant that to face an Extreme Demon master, one would need a hundred peak masters, or ten Supreme Peak masters.

Of course, just as the saying goes that fate is seventy percent luck and thirty percent skill, it was the destiny of martial artists for life and death to be decided by heavenly fortune. Since this old formula excluded such variables, it was not an absolute rule.

However...

_If I face the Palace of Murder in an all-out war, defeat is certain._

I shook my head in frustration. Our only hope was to separate Guyang Ryung from his assassins...

"By the way, he said he was coming here, but there has been no news."

Senior had said he would move to the Palace of Minor Light, but there had been no further word. Only the occasional noise could be heard from underground.

"Don't tell me he's digging a tunnel or something."

For some reason, I had a feeling that this _don't tell me_ was about to be told.

Just as my thoughts drifted chaotically like clouds…

_KWAAAAANG!_

"_Little Leader, come out at once!!_"

…a rough voice scattered from beyond the wall.

{sep}

"We are the Martial Sword Corps from the Palace of Blades! Open the gate. We have come to fetch the Little Leader!"

Eul Haryeong frowned. All these people showing up at the Palace of Minor Light so early in the morning! And their tone was harsh— no honorifics, no courtesy!

Even a peek through the gate revealed it was packed with armed men. To top it off, Han Soso had left the annex to stop by the Bright Summer Parlor, and the Blood Asura was not coming forward.

"Aff, what a pain! What's the big deal? They already said they're the Martial Sword Corps, so we know who they are. Just let them in!"

"Ji Hami! Get a grip. Do you think the Palace of Minor Light is some back alley tavern? No one's 'just' letting them in!"

"If it goes south, just cry for Daddy. What's the issue?"

"What are you, a child?

"You're the child, being so wimpy."

"Are you even hearing yourself?!"

"Ah, whatever, don't get on my case!"

If she didn't care, she shouldn't opine! Ji Hami's pestering only gave Eul Haryeong more anxiety.

"What's it? What's up?"

Yayul Tak, who'd been deep in a nap after his training, approached while wiping drool from his chin.

"I don't know. They say they're from the Palace of Blades and have come to fetch His Reverence..."

"Mmmmyawn... Palace of Blades?"

Yayul Tak scratched his head and swung the south gate wide open.

_Creeeeeak!_

"That's a lot of you."

There looked to be at least thirty of them. This was the Martial Sword Corps, known for usually staying cooped up in their palace like heavy boulders, dedicated solely to training. Though few in number, each member was said to be a peak-level martial artist.

For them all to come to the Palace of Minor Light at once, there could only be one reason: Dan Yehwa, His Reverence's fiancée.

"We've come to fetch the Little Leader."

The man in the lead spoke again. As expected, he used no honorific.

"_Little_ Leader?"

"That's right."

Yayul Tak rolled up his sleeves like a ruffian and spat on the ground.

"Hey, you bastard! Is His Reverence your pal or something? Why aren't you being respectful? Huh?"

"...What a vulgar fellow. Are you a thug?"

"Ha, look at this fearless bastard. Do you know who I am? You think you'll be acting like this once you do?"

"Why should I care who the hell you are?"

The opponent stepped forward, a smirk on his lips.

"I won't say it again. The Little Leader has a responsibility to meet my sister. Make way."

"Sister? Don't tell me... you're that guy from the Palace of Blades? The successor who's said to have jack shit for talent...?"

The very same. Dan Jiho.

"I heard some crazy guy from the Yayul Clan was playing gatekeeper for the Palace of Minor Light. That mouth of yours won't let you live long."

Their identities confirmed, Yayul Tak just grinned foolishly at Dan Jiho's threat.

"I can tell you're here because of Young Lady Frost Flower, like some doting fool. Stop making a scene over a man and a woman's love life and just state your business quietly. Why have you come to escort His Reverence?"

"...I'll ask you this. Is it true that the Little Leader visited the Palace of Bliss?"

Panic flickered across Yayul Tak's face at the mention of the Palace of Bliss, and he instantly realized there had been a huge misunderstanding.

While he hesitated, a woman with a large chest bounced forward.

It was Ji Hami.

"He did visit the Palace of Bliss, but what of it? Are you planning to visit too, milord? The successor to the Palace of Blades might even rate my persona attention, kyahaha!"

"You are... Ji Hami?"

Dan Jiho's face twisted like a demon's.

This was bad.

"Hey, hold up. Ahem! It's a misunderstanding. She's just here in exile. It's not what you think! Uh, ah... _Uyeyeyeeeeek!_"

_SHIIIIIIK!_

A sharp blade tore through the air, grazing past Yayul Tak's chin.

**_CRASH!_**

The blade struck the gate door, sending wood and splinters flying as if struck by a blunt weapon.

Time seemed to slow to a crawl for everyone. Pupils dilated as heads turned.

_Clunk, thump, tap-tatap._ The south gate of the Palace of Minor Light shattered and scattered.

"Little Leader... _come out at once!_"

Dan Jiho's furious roar echoed all around, jolting everyone back to reality. Taking this as their signal, the Martial Sword Corps charged inside, ready to turn the Palace of Minor Light into a pandemonium.

_Whoooosh—_

Just then, a quiet wind stirred in the distance. Fallen leaves scattered as a man in dark robes leisurely revealed himself.

"What is this? Are we facing assassins from the Palace of Blades this time?"

A peerless Beautiful Young Master with a deep, heavy voice: Ma Cheonso.

"_Lit… tle... Leader!_"

Dan Jiho's enraged roar echoed once more.
