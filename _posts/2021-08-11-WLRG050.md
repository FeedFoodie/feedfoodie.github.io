He felt a gaze settle upon him.

Across the table, Zeon watched Lee Sungmin's face in silence, his lips pressed into a thin line. The smoke that curled up from the thick cigar in his mouth obscured his features, but through the haze, Lee Sungmin could feel the weight of the man's stare.

Zeon was intrigued by the boy sitting before him. *A boy, yes.* Lee Sungmin was still just a boy. Though hardship had aged him far beyond his years, he was only eighteen, an age that had not yet carried him past the cusp of manhood.

"Impressive."

Zeon set down his cigar. With a look of displeasure, Bern glanced around the smoke-filled room and moved to open a window, but Zeon paid him no mind. Even if Bern was the branch manager of the Behenger Mercenary Guild, Zeon's authority as the leader of the Corona Mercenary Group was greater, never lesser.

"I heard you're eighteen," Zeon said. "Very… well-trained. You're the one who caught that Ogre Chimera, aren't you?"

"...Yes."

Hearing the assessment, Lee Sungmin wasn't sure what kind of expression he should make. Should he feel happy or bitter? In his past life, he had never once earned a moment of Zeon's attention.

"You're skilled at concealing your presence, which is proof you handle your inner arts well. Your physical development seems a little immature, but the necessary muscles are certainly well-trained."

His immature physique was a result of not eating properly, a fact Lee Sungmin knew all too well. Zeon's gaze then dropped to Lee Sungmin's hands.

"Your hands. They're the hands of someone who has swung a spear not just hundreds or thousands of times, but far more than that. I like it."

"...Thank you."

"You said you were Classless, right?" Zeon picked up the cigar he had rested on the ashtray. After taking a few more puffs, he ground it out completely. "Was it hardship that got you here, or was it effort?"

"...I think it was both."

"I suppose it was." Zeon chuckled. "I was Classless too, you see. I know just how shitty this world is for the Classless. Still, I can't help but admire that you've reached such a level despite it."

Otherworlders who made a name for themselves in Eria while being Classless were exceedingly rare, and Zeon, the leader of the Corona Mercenary Group, was one of them. It was because he admired Zeon that Lee Sungmin had specifically chosen the Corona Mercenary Group out of so many others, somehow managing to join as a grunt.

No, it wasn't admiration, to be precise. What the Lee Sungmin of his past life had wanted was salvation and pity. He had hoped Zeon would save him, expecting that since they were both Classless, Zeon might take pity on him and toss him some scraps.

Of course, it had never happened. A fair number of Classless had joined the Corona Mercenary Group with the same hopes as Lee Sungmin, and Zeon had never given any of them a second glance.

"Join the Corona Mercenary Group," Zeon began. "I've already heard about your skills. A solo subjugation of an Ogre Chimera. With an achievement like that, you'll become an SS-Rank before long. If you join us, I'll place you directly under me."

"...Are you offering me the Vice Guildmaster's seat?"

"I don't think it's a bad offer."

"Are you making this offer because I'm Classless?"

"That's not it. I'm just greedy for your skills. At your age, you seem to have crossed the threshold of the peak realm. With enough time and support, you might even completely overcome that wall."

"And you're saying you can give me that time and support?"

At the question, the corner of Zeon's mouth lifted slightly. He reached into his coat and produced a small wooden box. Lee Sungmin thought it was a Subspace Pocket, but it was not. Inside the box lay a single small pill, and a refreshing fragrance drifted out, cutting through the stale tobacco smoke that had filled the room.

"This is..." Bern, watching from the side, wore a surprised expression. Lee Sungmin's face also stiffened.

"A Minor Cycle Pill," Zeon said, crossing his arms. "It's one of Shaolin's Secret Elixir Pills. It's not as good as a Great Cycle Pill, but its effects overwhelm most miracle herbs."

"...Are you saying you'll give that to me?"

"In exchange."

TAK.

Zeon snapped the lid of the wooden box shut. "Tell me about the martial arts you've learned."

"Just the names?"

"No. The incantations too."

Lee Sungmin looked at him, dumbfounded. Even if the Minor Cycle Pill was a valuable miracle herb, it was difficult to compare it to the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, or the Violet Dawn Divine Art that he had learned.

"I refuse..."

"I'll give you a Great Cycle Pill," Zeon said, cutting him off before he could finish. "If you tell me the incantations for the Spear Technique you've learned, I'll give you a Great Cycle Pill."

"...I don't understand. Why are you asking me to tell you the incantations of a martial art?"

The request made no sense to Lee Sungmin. Zeon was a master of the Hundred Steps Divine Fist. As a secular disciple of Shaolin, he had learned the secret martial arts of a sect considered the best among the Nine Sects One Gang. He couldn't understand why Zeon, who didn't even use a spear, would ask for the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

"Because I can't trust you," Zeon answered. "I have no intention of learning the martial art you tell me. I'll just be aware of it. That way, I can prepare for your betrayal."

"Do you think I'll betray you?"

"It's not just you." Zeon put the closed wooden box back into his coat. "I don't trust people. There's no guarantee that if I offer trust, the other person will offer it back in kind. That's the kind of animal a person is. You are a talent I covet, but at the same time, you're too much to embrace. That's why I'm telling you to reveal the incantations of your martial art."

Even so, wasn't asking for the incantations of a martial art too shameless? As Lee Sungmin's expression soured, Zeon added, "As I said, I have no intention of learning your Spear Technique. I just want to be aware of it. If you tell me the incantations and join the Corona Mercenary Group, I'll give you the Minor Cycle Pill along with the Great Cycle Pill. How about it?"

Asking for the incantations was shameless, but the offer was frankly tempting. If he took both pills, Lee Sungmin's inner arts would see a tremendous improvement.

"I refuse."

That, however, was what Lee Sungmin said. At his words, Zeon's eyebrows furrowed.

"Is what I'm offering not enough?"

"No, it's not insufficient. The Great Cycle Pill and the Minor Cycle Pill… I believe they're miracle herbs with a value greater than a single divine art."

"Then why?"

"For the same reason as you. I can't trust you either."

It wasn't that he couldn't trust people. He couldn't trust Zeon. At Lee Sungmin's answer, Zeon shrugged slightly.

"A shame," he said, rising to his feet. "If you change your mind, come find me anytime. The conditions won't change."

Lee Sungmin did not answer. After Zeon left the room, Bern, who had been silent the entire time, let out a long sigh.

"It seems Zeon is quite wary of you."

He felt a pang of regret for the pills, but it couldn't be helped. The risk of revealing his martial arts was simply too great.

"Ah, right," Bern said. "I have something to tell you that might interest you. It's about the Ogre Chimera you caught. It was difficult for me to investigate alone, so I decided to conduct a joint investigation with Behenger's Mages Guild."

"Is that so?"

"Would you like to take a look?"

"What would I know just by looking?"

"You're the one who fought it directly. It seems they want to hear your opinion as well."

It was a difficult offer to refuse, especially since he had nothing urgent to do anyway. Lee Sungmin nodded his head.

Behenger's Mages Guild was not far from the Mercenary Guild. Come to think of it, this was his first time visiting such a place. The Lee Sungmin of his past life had no connection whatsoever with magic. Thanks to Scarlet, he had learned a few spells in this life, but that hardly meant he had grown accustomed to it.

"Are you interested in magic?" Bern asked.

"I am... interested."

"Magic is a wonderful field of study. If someone were to ask me what a miracle is, I would answer without hesitation. A miracle is magic."

Bern's face was full of pride as he spoke. Lee Sungmin, who had been looking around the guild's interior with curious eyes, directed his gaze to an orb floating in the air.

"What is that?"

"It's a magic stone. Do you know what a magic stone is?"

"A stone that stores Mana... is what I know."

"Correct. A magic stone is a stone that stores Mana, and all artifacts use them. Subspace Pockets are no exception." As if he had been waiting for the question, Bern launched into an enthusiastic explanation of the mechanism. You put a magic stone into an existing bag or box, hide it with space-distorting magic, then cast space-expansion magic and infuse it with Mana… Lee Sungmin only half-listened to the lengthy explanation.

"This way."

Chattering on, Bern guided him to the basement of the Guild building. After they walked down a long corridor, Bern stopped in front of a red door.

"They're researching the Ogre Chimera in here."

He knocked, and the door opened a moment later. Stepping into the room, Lee Sungmin gasped at the foul air.

"......"

Even Bern seemed unprepared for the scene that unfolded before them. The room was gruesome. The dissected corpse of the Ogre floated in the air while scattered entrails were strewn across the floor.

"You've arrived?" A man dressed in a pure white gown approached them.

"...What in the world is this?" Bern asked.

"We're investigating," the man said with a shrug. Contrary to his youthful face, which had few wrinkles, the man's hair was full of white strands. After answering Bern with a nonchalant expression, he looked at Lee Sungmin.

"...Oho." A peculiar light entered the man's eyes. "This is quite... rare... hmm." Muttering to himself, the approaching man stopped in his tracks. "Ah, my apologies for the late introduction. My name is Kim Jonghyun." He gave a slight bow of his head. "And who might you be?"

His two eyes, filled with curiosity, turned to Lee Sungmin. The name Kim Jonghyun came as a slight surprise, not because he knew the man, but because the name was in the same style as his own.

"...My name is Lee Sungmin."

"Are you from Korea?" Kim Jonghyun asked with a small laugh. When Lee Sungmin nodded, he let out another chuckle. "Well, even if we're both from Korea, there's no rule that says we're from the same dimension. There's a high probability that you and I are from different dimensional Koreas."

Eria was that kind of place, randomly summoning Otherworlders from all sorts of dimensions. Just as different Central Plains Murims could exist in different dimensions, the same was true for the Korea where Lee Sungmin had been born and raised.

"The Korea… no, the Earth where I was born was a mess because of a Monster Outbreak," Kim Jonghyun explained. "Monsters popped out from everywhere, and with all that talk of awakenings and what not, hunters and mages were born. What was the Earth you were born on like?"

"...There were no monsters popping out."

"Then it's a different dimension. Well, it's still a pleasure to meet a fellow Korean." Kim Jonghyun extended his hand for a handshake.

"...Though that's not the only reason I'm pleased." He gave a strange smile.
