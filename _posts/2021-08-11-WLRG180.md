-RUUUMBLE!

A deep vibration echoed from somewhere not far ahead.

Lee Sungmin stopped walking and peered into the distance. He could feel the clash of foreign energies, each one so immense that he knew he could not easily face either. The sheer scale of the colliding powers gave him pause, and he found himself inwardly surprised. The one fighting up ahead was probably Envirus, yet the power radiating from the battle was so different that it felt as if it belonged to a completely different person from the man who had been by his side just a moment ago.

*Was he hiding it?*

Despite his confusion, Lee Sungmin started forward again. His priority, he decided, was to help Envirus and resolve the situation. His steps, however, soon came to another halt. A man in a black robe was blocking his path.

Kim Jonghyun felt deeply moved. This was undoubtedly fate. He truly believed it. It had been the same in the North when he had encountered Lee Sungmin, an encounter he had also felt was guided by destiny. The potent Destiny Force that Lee Sungmin possessed was a direct blessing from the Apostle of the End, so it only stood to reason that their meeting now was also fated.

They had met in Behenger, again while pursuing the Sword Phantom, and once more in the northern region of Trabia, and now, here. With this reunion, it became certain that the Kim Jonghyun of his past life was heading in a different direction from the Kim Jonghyun of the present. He did not know what kind of life Lee Sungmin had lived before, nor did he know what his own past self had been like in that timeline, but meeting Lee Sungmin now, knowing he was forging a different path from the one he had once walked, brought Kim Jonghyun a profound sense of joy.

"…Who are you?" Lee Sungmin asked, his guard remaining high.

Kim Jonghyun pulled the hood of his robe lower. He honestly wanted to greet him after such a long time, but he had no desire to ruin things by doing something so unnecessary. For this moment, at least, he had to ignore their connection and hide his identity.

*Impressive.*

Kim Jonghyun marveled at the power he felt radiating from Lee Sungmin. The man was always new, always different, whenever they met. Every few years their paths would cross, and each time, Lee Sungmin was much stronger than before. He had already far surpassed the limits of a Classless. Even among martial artists who trained from childhood with calamity-level talent, or mages who received similar gifts and support, few had reached a level comparable to his. Lee Sungmin might be stagnating now, but even so, there were not many known figures in the world who could defeat him.

A faint smile touched Kim Jonghyun's lips. "You can't pass beyond this point."

The voice that flowed from inside the hood was murky and laced with static, making it impossible to determine the speaker's gender. Seeing Lee Sungmin's face twitch, Kim Jonghyun raised a hand. A direct confrontation would not work. Although he was channeling the power of a Demon Lord, he was still human, and he honestly was not confident he could win a fight against Lee Sungmin, even at full strength.

So, he would avoid a fight. His role, after all, was simply to block anyone who might interfere with Arbes's magic. He had already prepared for this. He had allowed Envirus to pass for a specific purpose, but letting Lee Sungmin through as well would completely ruin his plans.

As the unidentifiable mage began to form a sigil, Lee Sungmin did not wait, instead rushing directly toward Kim Jonghyun. This was precisely what Kim Jonghyun had been aiming for. The sigil itself was a feint, a gesture intended to make Lee Sungmin approach.

CRACKLE!

A black light flashed, and Lee Sungmin, startled by the flare that consumed his vision, instinctively raised his spear in a movement that flowed naturally into an attack, but then he felt it, a strange sensation as if time itself were being stretched taut.

WHOOSH…

A black world unfolded around him. Lee Sungmin halted his thrusting spear and looked around at the pitch-black, seemingly endless void. He could feel nothing, as if all his senses had been severed. A discomfort entirely different from the sensory deprivation he had endured on Mount Mushii pressed down on his consciousness.

Kim Jonghyun let out a deep breath. The black space he had created was a temporary Sealing barrier. A wave of dizziness washed over him, and he pressed his temples to steady himself. Although temporary, the barrier was of an extremely high level. The problem was that just as Lee Sungmin could do nothing from inside it, Kim Jonghyun could do nothing to him from the outside.

It did not matter. His goal was only to buy time. Besides, even if he could kill Lee Sungmin, he had no desire to do so.

*Three hours at most… no, perhaps it will be even shorter than that.*

Kim Jonghyun knew about the strange and destructive power Lee Sungmin wielded, but this would be enough. Having blocked the threatening advance, he could delay no longer. Kim Jonghyun's body floated into the air, and he turned toward the Central Plaza, where Arbes and Envirus were clashing.

{sep}

Arbes used Blink to escape the space just a moment before Envirus's magic crushed the very spot where he had been. It had been a long time since he had fought a battle where he felt his very existence was threatened. After becoming a Lich, and then an Arch Lich, Arbes had always stood as an absolute powerhouse, a nemesis to all. He never had a reason to fight the other stars of Predator, nor did he have cause to quarrel with the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens. That was why this current battle was so enjoyable.

However, he was not just feeling enjoyment. There was also impatience. Arbes glanced toward the magic circle, which was now activated and greedily devouring the world's Mana.

*Preskan's presence has vanished. It seems his spiritual body has met its end. That stupid bastard…!*

Right now, he felt so desperate he would borrow the hands of a rat if he could. That was how strong Envirus was. When it came to pure magic, the realm Envirus had reached was higher than his own. Arbes felt a slight sense of humiliation at that fact. Casting Blink repeatedly, he rose high into the air and continuously formed sigils. While casting Blink in succession was an extremely dangerous act for most, that did not apply to him.

KWAAAAA!

Dozens of fire orbs materialized around Arbes. It was Hellfire, considered the strongest of all fire-type magic, yet he wielded it as easily as a simple Fireball.

*His skill has improved since before.*

Envirus raised both his arms. While he had been losing his abilities as Cain, Arbes had continuously devoted himself to magic. Envirus clicked his tongue softly as the characters engraved on his arms shimmered, forming sentences of arcane power.

KSHATTER!

Dozens of layered defensive walls materialized to block the downpour of hellfire. Explosions erupted on impact, the terrible heat melting the very ground below. Envirus glanced at the magic circle. He needed to destroy it, but Arbes gave him no opening, continuously forming one sigil after another.

The ground beneath Envirus suddenly split open with a crack, the earth itself becoming the maw of a giant monster. Sharp fangs and a venomous breath rose from its wide-open mouth. A transparent air bubble covered Envirus's head, and having secured his ability to breathe, he swung his arms. The monster's maw froze solid.

Envirus then soared high into the air to meet Arbes, spreading his hands wide.

RUUUMBLE!

The sound of thunder gathered in his palms, and a dazzling white flash surged toward the Arch Lich. Arbes hastily deployed a defensive barrier, but Envirus's magic was not something that could be so easily blocked.

"……"

The barrier was pierced. The flash broke through and turned Arbes's left arm to dust.

"KRGHK!"

There was no pain. Even as an Arch Lich, he was still a Lich. The body he currently possessed was nothing more than a doll modeled after a human form. Arbes would not die even if this body were destroyed, but the loss of a part of it enraged him.

As Arbes staggered in mid-air, Envirus rushed toward him. The characters engraved on his arms writhed, covering both of his arms in a blue light. Before Envirus's attack could completely destroy his body, Arbes hastily used Blink to escape.

A one-on-one magic battle was difficult. Humiliating as it was, Arbes admitted it. In that case, he had no choice but to continue the fight in a way that suited his specialty. He clutched the wound on his left arm, which did not bleed.

CR-CR-CRACK!

As his arm regenerated, Arbes's lips began to move. An ominous darkness flickered around him as the incantation began, and the power of the Demon Lord he had contracted with, Rotting Death, covered his entire body. The specialty he could not use while drawing on the world's Mana had begun.

Darkness swirled, and from within it, a mixture of unpleasant screams, sobs, and groans resounded—the sounds of death itself. The Undead rose to their feet with the creaking of bones. The vengeful spirits Arbes had collected over hundreds of years took form, becoming his soldiers. His specialty was necromancy, and he was the necromancer who had reached the highest level in this world.

*If Vollander were here, he'd be throwing a fit…!* Arbes thought of the leader of the Death Knights and ground his teeth. Unlike other inhumans, Death Knights were Undead, the souls of the dead who could not ascend, which was why Vollander greatly disliked him.

Envirus's face twisted as he looked at the risen soldiers. Necromancy was the most condemned of all magic, for it inevitably involved corpses and defied both reason and providence. The Undead that Arbes raised were different from common Ghouls or Skeletons. He had created their bodies as Chimeras and then stuffed the souls of the Undead into them, which was what made them so powerful. Unlike typical Chimeras that could not properly use their implanted brains, these creatures, powered by souls, were capable of thinking for themselves.

Winged Chimeras flew up toward Envirus. He felt no threat from them. They were just Chimeras, after all, not nearly strong enough to stop him. Without hesitation, Envirus waved his hand, and the approaching creatures were crushed in mid-air. As their bodies imploded, however, they spewed out a black Poison Mist. Guided by Arbes's gesture, the mist became a wave that swept over Envirus.

Watching the Poison Mist envelop his opponent, Arbes formed another sigil. He never thought he could stop him with Corpse Poison. In the end, it was just a way to buy time.

KWAAANG!

The cloud of mist surrounding Envirus erupted in a massive explosion. Before the sound even faded, Arbes was already preparing his next spell. It was a grand magic that could not be used with the world's Mana, one that only he, among all the mages in the world, could use.

From within the explosion, Envirus felt a fatal premonition.

Soul Destruction Magic. No matter how great a mage one was, they could not defy a magic that attacked the soul directly.

Two pitch-black hands shot up from behind Arbes's back. It was the Hand of Death, which annihilated the soul of whatever it grasped. Arbes focused his mind and shot the two hands toward Envirus, the elongated arms plunging deep into the heart of the explosion.

WHOOOOSH!

A great wind completely blew away the remnants of the Poison Mist. Just as Arbes had used Soul Destruction Magic, Envirus had also used his own specialty, something beyond Silent Casting. The wind that pushed away the poison cloud took shape, forming into a man with bulging muscles.

"An Elemental!" Arbes spat, grinding his teeth.

The wind Elemental that had taken form exuded an intimidating presence on a completely different level from a lowly Sylph.

[Are you Cain?] the Elemental asked Envirus.

Envirus slowly nodded his head. The wind Elemental let out a dry laugh.

[Your Majesty will be saddened…]

"Later," Envirus muttered in a low voice.

Even Soul Destruction Magic could not extinguish the soul of an Elemental. That was why the wind Elemental he had summoned now spread its arms and created a great wind.

Kim Jonghyun had already arrived at the plaza. He hid himself from the notice of both Arbes and Envirus, quietly lying in wait.

*Not yet.*

The time was not yet right.
