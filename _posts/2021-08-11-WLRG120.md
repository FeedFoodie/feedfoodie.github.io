The Central District of Trabia had once been home to the city's wealthiest citizens. Though most had either fled or been killed when Trabia became the Inhuman Demonic Realm, the grand mansions they left behind remained. As the carriage rolled onward, the scenery began to change, with sprawling estates and manors appearing sporadically through the windows.

After suggesting he become a vampire, Jeniela had fallen silent. The carriage continued on without stopping, and she simply watched Lee Sungmin with an unreadable smile. He felt a profound discomfort under her steady gaze.

[I wouldn't recommend it,] the Void Monarch muttered from within him. [Jeniela is a powerful vampire. As you are now, you can't face her, no matter what you try. Even with my help, like in the Dungeon, you wouldn't be able to do a thing to her.]

The Void Monarch's assessment was delivered without a hint of hesitation, and Lee Sungmin had to agree. Jeniela, the Vampire Queen, was a monster of immense power. Even if he could draw out the full results of his training in the mental world, he could not guarantee victory against her. He thought so because he could not even begin to fathom the true extent of her strength.

[To be clear,] the Void Monarch added, [Jeniela is stronger than the 'real' Little Heavenly Demon you saw in the Dungeon.]

[At least for now,] he continued. [The Little Heavenly Demon... in my view, she possesses a talent that defies all reason. It's absurd, almost too absurd. That wench will only continue to grow stronger. With each passing day, her already mature strength will become more complete and advance even further. Given the talent and potential this Elder has glimpsed in her, she might even surpass Jeniela in a few years.]

There was no conviction in the Void Monarch's voice as he said this. Hearing it, Lee Sungmin clenched and unclenched his fist. He had trained for two thousand one hundred years in the mental world, a period of time that had taken his talent and potential and elevated them to a distant realm.

Bringing those achievements into reality, however, was a completely different story. He knew what he was capable of, but he could not manifest it. Further growth was therefore impossible, as he had not even properly reached the place he had already arrived at. As long as he could not catch up to his own accomplishments in the mental world, he could never reach a higher plane.

Wiji Hoyeon, on the other hand, was constantly getting stronger. He did not know what kind of training she was doing, but at the end of the Dungeon, she had declared with a voice full of conviction that in one year, she would be incomparably stronger.

*The gap is widening.*

Constantly.

*I'm crawling. Slowly... so slowly, but Wiji Hoyeon is running. No, is she flying?*

And so the distance between them grew. Even when he thought he had barely caught up, he could not match the speed at which she advanced.

[Regardless, I don't recommend going to Jeniela's mansion,] the Void Monarch warned. [I don't know what that bewitching wench is plotting. You lack the means to protect yourself if something were to happen.]

*That might be true.*

However, he was not following her without a reason. He had to attend this meeting with the ambiguous existence called a 'nobleman'. As someone who did not know its true identity, Lee Sungmin could not completely ignore such a possibility. Perhaps Jeniela truly was the nobleman the Spirit had mentioned. What was certain was the winter that the Spirit had spoken of, the one five years from now. It was October, so winter was slowly approaching. If the Spirit's words spoke of a destiny that must be fulfilled, then he was fated to meet the nobleman within the next two months.

*And if I had refused... Jeniela would have killed me.*

[I suppose so,] the Void Monarch laughed heartily. [Should I call this a checkmate? Well... do your best to stay alive. If it really comes down to it, it's not like there's no way out.]

*What way?*

[Becoming a Yokai,] the Void Monarch said with another booming laugh. [If you become a Yokai, you can fight and win against Jeniela... I won't say something so irresponsible, but you'll at least be able to save your life. Escaping should be possible.]

*Don't talk nonsense,* Lee Sungmin retorted with annoyance.

Beside him, Rubia trembled like a leaf. He placed a hand on her shoulder, and she flinched in surprise, looking up at the side of his face. Lee Sungmin felt her gaze but offered no words of reassurance.

"How cute," Jeniela said, her silence finally breaking with a stifled laugh. "Do I seem that scary? Don't worry. I will never harm you. I can't swear an oath, but I do tend to keep my promises."

The carriage rolled to a stop.

"We've arrived."

Jeniela glanced at the closed carriage door, and without a summons, it swung open.

"Welcome."

Though she had been the last to board, Jeniela was somehow the first to disembark. When had she moved? Stepping out of the carriage, she was no longer wearing a kimono. She now stood before a garden of blooming red roses, clad in an elegant black dress. Behind her, a beautiful mansion soared into the sky, its grounds steeped in a nocturnal darkness despite the daylight.

"To the Vampire Queen's Mansion."

Before the garden of bewitchingly red roses, Jeniela was transformed into the queen of vampires and the mistress of the mansion. Non-human presences stirred in the shadows. Figures emerged from the gloom, their skin pale and their eyes red. They were Jeniela's familiars.

"Treat them with courtesy."

A vampire approached and bowed his head deeply to Lee Sungmin.

"Your robe."

"...Hm."

Lee Sungmin removed the robe he was wearing and handed it to the vampire. Another took the one Rubia wore. Jeniela crossed the garden a step ahead of them, and escorted by the vampires, Lee Sungmin and Rubia headed for the mansion.

"Prepare a human meal," Jeniela commanded a nearby vampire as she opened the mansion door and stepped inside. "And use your best skills."

The vampire bowed his head deeply and vanished.

"There are more than enough rooms," she said, turning to them. "Please use any room you wish. If you don't like the rooms in this mansion, I'll give you the entire annex."

"No, that's alright. We only came here for a meal today."

"All the rooms in the mansion are excellent. You won't find a room as fine as these in any inn in Trabia."

"I appreciate the offer, but I'm fine."

"Huuuhm..." Jeniela let out a long, nasal hum at his reply. She stared at him with flashing red eyes before slowly nodding her head. "Very well. If you refuse, I can't force you. However, if you change your mind, please tell me anytime. The same goes for my offer in the carriage. If you change your mind, please give me your answer."

With that, Jeniela guided Lee Sungmin and Rubia to the dining hall inside the mansion.

"W-will they serve human flesh?" Rubia whispered in a small, trembling voice.

She had tried to keep her voice down, but there was no way Jeniela would not have heard her. The Vampire Queen burst out laughing and shook her head.

"That will not happen, I assure you. What I ordered was a 'human meal'. Vampires do not enjoy human flesh. The only thing a Vampire enjoys is human blood. The ones who enjoy eating human flesh are the barbaric Lycanthropes."

Jeniela turned her head to look at Rubia, her smile gone.

"Your words are a great insult to a Vampire. If I hadn't made the promise not to harm you, I would have ripped your head from your shoulders the moment I heard that."

"S-s-s... sorry!" Rubia cried out, her face pale.

Her body had a metabolism that existed but did not function much, and for once, she was grateful for it. If her body had been different, she would have wet herself at Jeniela's warning.

"It will take quite some time for the food to be served," Jeniela said, taking a seat at the head of a large table. She looked at Lee Sungmin, gestured to the seat closest to her, and chuckled. "Shall we have a conversation until then?"

It would be a long, unwanted conversation where a single mistake could mean death.

"Let's do that," Lee Sungmin replied, feigning a neutral expression as he sat in the offered chair.

*

He did not know how fast the bastard would travel, but considering the distance, he should have arrived in Trabia by now, no matter how much he dawdled.

Preskan was a Lich, a mage, and mages did not use the foolish method of moving their own bodies. They were, for the most part, lazy creatures who hated to move, which was why countless magics existed to act on their behalf. Familiars were a prime example. A low-level mage could at best use small animals like rats or sparrows, but Preskan was of such a high level that he could not be compared to such inferiors.

That was not to say he did not use small animals. Creatures like sparrows and rats were common and had many advantages due to their size. The difference between a high-level mage and a low-level one was apparent in the control and sheer number of familiars one could command, and in how well one could synthesize the information they gathered.

Preskan was using countless small beasts to monitor the gates of Trabia. He watched not just the city gates, but the gates of the Central District and the entire district itself. It was an incredibly troublesome task, but it was worth it. To catch that damn Heart Thief, Preskan was determined to endure any amount of trouble. That was how much the heart he had created meant to him. It was the cherished desire he had become a Lich to achieve.

However, now...

"...Vampire Queen!"

Preskan clutched his non-existent head. The dark magic that constituted his body wavered greatly with his emotional turmoil.

"Damn it, that damn bitch! Why are you with that damn Heart Thief!"

Jeniela had entered the inn where the Heart Thief was staying. Before long, they had emerged together and boarded the same carriage, which then headed for a mansion of blooming red roses in the center of the Central District. The Vampire Queen's Mansion.

He did not know what conversation had passed between them, but one thing was an undeniable fact. The bastard who had the heart Preskan had spent his entire life creating embedded in his body had entered the Vampire Queen's Mansion.

*What should I do?*

Preskan could not help but hesitate. No matter how steeped in madness he was, he knew his life was precious. Even for a Lich who could be resurrected any number of times as long as his Life Force Vessel was intact, confronting a powerful inhuman like the Vampire Queen was a terrifying prospect.

Yes. Preskan was considering confronting the Vampire Queen. He had to, to reclaim his precious heart. He did not know why she had taken that mercenary bastard, but if the bastard became her familiar, Preskan would never get his heart back.

That was why he had to fight. He had to confront her and take it back. It was only natural, for the heart's creator was Preskan himself.

His thoughts went that far, but then he faltered. He was afraid. The Vampire Queen was a merciless monster. If Preskan tried to confront her, she would annihilate him without forgiveness.

"...For now, let's just watch."

In the end, Preskan chose compromise over conflict. To be blunt, he did not want to die. He had more than enough time, so he could just make another heart.

However, his lingering attachment remained. Preskan continued to stand before his crystal ball, peering into the Vampire Queen's Mansion. Hours passed. Then, the mansion's main gate opened. They had arrived by carriage, but they left on foot. Preskan's eyes widened as he saw Lee Sungmin and Rubia emerging from the mansion. He expanded the vision of his familiars, zooming in for a closer look.

"...He didn't become a Vampire."

Preskan uttered the words in a trembling voice. Oh, gods. He clasped his hands in prayer and soon after let out a meaningless shout filled with joy and passion.

If he had not become a Vampire, Preskan still had a chance.

"I won't kill him. Yes... yes, good. I'll make sure he doesn't die, and then I'll pull it out."

However, not right now. The Central District was Jeniela's territory. As long as he did not know her intentions, he could not attack the bastard here.

So he had to wait.

"Maybe I should just turn him into an Undead," Preskan chuckled as he entertained the pleasant thought.
