Two days later, twelve throwing knives were embedded in a straight line on one wall, spaced evenly without a hairsbreadth of error.

"Congratulations! You have now achieved complete mastery of the Twelve Storm Daggers!" Cheon Ahseong said, smiling radiantly.

Crimson Tiger blinked in shock. In just two days, he had achieved complete mastery of the Twelve Storm Daggers.

*Seriously, two days? No one in the jianghu would believe me even if I told them!*

Of course, his journey with the Twelve Storm Daggers had only just begun. He had merely grasped the technique, not the full understanding of its application and depth.

Nevertheless, Crimson Tiger knew that most jianghu practitioners wasted their entire lives without ever reaching this stage. Had he simply memorized the formulas of the Twelve Storm Daggers and left, he himself couldn't be sure how many years it would take him to get here.

At the same time, Cheon Ahseong too was shocked. Not even he had foreseen that Crimson Tiger would attain complete mastery in two days. He had only said it to encourage Crimson Tiger, and because it was possible in theory.

*If the jianghu finds out about this, another legend of the Martial God will be born. Everyone would praise me for my excellent teaching and say that it was just what they expected from me.*

Only he would know that this legend wasn't his alone. 

Honestly, Cheon Ahseong was genuinely impressed with Crimson Tiger. For every ten things he taught the young man, he understood twenty.

*As the saying goes, teaching is more than just one-sidedly handing out information. The more one teaches, the clearer and deeper one's own well of knowledge becomes.*

He had gained insight through teaching Crimson Tiger as well. In fact, he felt that he had benefited even more than the young man. After all, he had finally overcome an obstacle that had hindered his progress for a long time.

He stared intently at Crimson Tiger. For a while now, he had realized that the brat was using the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art.

"Hey, can I see your real face?" he asked.

Crimson Tiger smiled. "I apologize, but the Twelve Phantoms' identities are protected by Association law. A law you yourself established, Association Chairman."

aggAnnoy18

"Hahahaha!" Cheon Ahseong roared with laughter at the witty evasion.

*I've really taken a liking to this kid!*

Until now, the manner in which others dealt with him never strayed beyond a few familiar patterns. Only Crimson Tiger was different. The secret agent was polite but kept a certain distance, neither attempting to ingratiate himself nor fearing him.

"By the way, what is your relationship with my eldest disciple?"

Crimson Tiger's eye twitched. The very fact that Cheon Ahseong was only asking him this now told him all he needed to know about the old master's character.

He hesitated for a moment, then answered, "...I do not know him well."

Cheon Ahseong smiled enigmatically, as if to say that was the perfect answer that best captured Baek Museong's personality. He asked again, "Where must I go to see you?"

"Since you're the Association Chairman... you could probably ask any passerby, and they would tell you. Even a maid wiping a table would likely know where I am." Crimson Tiger grinned mischievously, then straightened himself and bowed deeply and formally. "Thank you, Chairman. I will not forget the teachings you have bestowed upon me."

When Crimson Tiger stood up straight again, Cheon Ahseong had already vanished.

{sep}

The next evening, Hong Maerang's birthday celebration was held at Crimson Tiger's house. When some suggested holding it at the Hundred Flavors Restaurant, she adamantly refused, saying that public and private matters had to be kept separate. As such, they decided on Crimson Tiger's house, which had a spacious courtyard, and he gladly agreed.

"Congratulations, Hongmae!" Crimson Tiger said, handing her a gift he had prepared in advance.

Hong Maerang tore open the gift excitedly. Inside was a beautiful pink silk dress.

Crimson Tiger began, "Well, now that it's spring and all..."

"When would I ever wear clothes like this? It looks expensive. How much did you pay for it?" Hong Maerang scolded. However, the tremor in her voice betrayed how deeply touched she was.

"Congratulations!"

People gathered around Hong Maerang.

Crimson Tiger smiled. Just by looking at the faces of those sincerely congratulating Hong Maerang, it was clear that she was indispensable to the Hundred Flavors Restaurant. 

As the matriarch of the establishment, she always treated her subordinates fairly. She praised them when they did well and scolded them when they made mistakes. She didn't distinguish between those she was closer to and those she wasn't. Because of this, everyone loved her.

aggAnnoy01

He glanced at Soeun, who was sincerely congratulating Hong Maerang. Although she had been unable to concentrate on work recently, she seemed to be feeling better now. She was working hard, and was noticeably more cheerful.

Meanwhile, the special dishes prepared by Gong Suk delighted everyone. 

The long-awaited party reached its peak.

BAM!

Suddenly, the door was thrown open roughly, and an old man strode in, followed by a dozen masked martial artists all exuding chilling auras.

SHING!

Drawing their swords, the masked men spread out and surrounded the Hundred Flavors Restaurant staff.

"Which one of you is Jin Ho, the manager of the Hundred Flavors Restaurant?" the old man, Pung Yang, demanded sharply.

Everyone looked around, but Jin Ho was nowhere to be found.

Pung Yang shouted again, his voice tinged with killing intent. "I asked, which one of you is the manager of the Hundred Flavors Restaurant? Don't make me repeat myself again."

"H-He's not here right now!" Hong Maerang stammered. Until a moment ago, she had been talking to Jin Ho, but he had disappeared at some point during the chaos of the assailants' arrival.

Pung Yang barked, "Kill them all and find him."

SHWIK!

"Keuk!"

Suddenly, a masked man let out a scream and collapsed, a throwing knife in his heart.

Soeun, the one who had thrown the knife, snatched the masked man's sword and spun around to face the other assailants. The other masked men immediately rushed to attack her, but Pung Yang raised a hand to stop them.

"Who in the world are you?" Soeun asked, pointing the sword at Pung Yang.

Pung Yang scoffed. If not for Soeun's beautiful appearance, which even swayed him, an elderly man, there would have been no opportunity for conversation. "And who are you, wench?"

"Anyone who has learned even a sliver of courtesy knows to introduce themselves first when visiting someone else's house," Soeun said boldly.

"Hahaha, aren't you a spirited wench?!" Pung Yang burst into laughter.

aggAnnoy06

Soeun tensed. Her opponent's aura was far from ordinary, and the masked men were quite skilled as well. Her surprise attack had succeeded only because it was unexpected. In a direct confrontation, she wouldn't have been able to subdue one of them so quickly.

Pung Yang smiled. "Fine, I'll tell you. This old man is known as One Slash Ten Kills."

Soeun's eyes widened in shock. "One Slash Ten Kills, Pung Yang? Eum Sagwon's master?"

"......" Pung Yang's expression hardened at the mention of his disciple's name.

Soeun's heart pounded as she realized that she had slipped up. Pung Yang's skill was well-known. He wasn't someone she could confront in battle.

Nervous, she quickly asked, "Why are you looking for our manager?"

"That's none of your damn business. How do you know my disciple?"

"T-That's..." Soeun stammered, then grit her teeth in determination. "Senior, you seem to have misunderstood something."

"Misunderstood?"

"Our manager has nothing to do with that incident."

Soeun steeled her resolve. She was well aware that Eum Sagwon had died while trying to enter the Ironblood Nines.

*Based on how he reacted to Eum Sagwon's name, Pung Yang must have come for me. Does he want information?*

aggAnnoy11

She continued, "I'll tell you everything you want to know about your disciple's death if you release all these people. I'm the only one you need."

Pung Yang hesitated, sensing that something was amiss. He had heard from the Evil Coalition that the restaurant manager was the culprit who killed his disciple, but now a great beauty was stepping forward, claiming it was her doing?

In the end, he said, "...Fine! You, step forward for now."

"Release these people first!"

"Insolent wench! Who do you think you're giving orders to?!"

"...I see. You intend to kill us all." Soeun sighed. She should have known he wouldn't let any of them go when he revealed himself as One Slash Ten Kills.

Pung Yang sneered coldly in affirmation.

Soeun raised her sword. She knew she was no match for Pung Yang, but she wouldn't go down without a fight.

Seeing her bravery, Pung Yang chuckled, "Shall we see how many you can save with those pitiful skills of yours?"

SWISH!

In the blink of an eye, a dozen swords were pressed against Hong Maerang's, Gong Suk's, and the other Hundred Flavors Restaurant staff's necks.

"Save Gong Suk!" Hong Maerang shouted.

Gong Suk shouted back, "Nonsense! Save Hongmae!"

"You have children!"

For a moment, Gong Suk was overwhelmed with emotion. Soon, however, he gritted his teeth and yelled, "I'll leave it up to fate! Soeun, save Hongmae first!"

Pung Yang laughed in disbelief at how everyone placed all their hopes on Soeun, not knowing that she wouldn't last three exchanges against him. His men laughed along with him.

aggAnnoy16

"Hey, scoundrel! Did you leave these guys behind and run away because you knew they were morons?" Pung Yang looked around. "Get out here, Hundred Flavors Restaurant Manager! If you don't show yourself in three seconds, I'll slit all of these mongrels' throats! One! Two! Three!"

...No one appeared.

Pung Yang scowled. "Seems like the fucking rat bastard has already fled. I guess it can't be helped then. Kill them..."

"Wait! Hold on!" Crimson Tiger suddenly revealed himself, shouting, "I was just on my way back from the latrine!"

When he sensed a dozen killing intents approaching, he had deliberately slipped away from the party and contacted Yeon. After that, he hid behind a tree and observed the situation to glean as much information as he could about the assailants before stepping in, but the old man's identity truly shocked him.

*How on earth did One Slash Ten Kills find me? Only a few people in the Association are aware of my cover... I see. There's a Coalition spy among them.*

For a moment, Crimson Tiger considered changing to Yang Hyeon's face, but quickly dismissed the idea. Now that his cover had been blown, he wouldn't be able to continue working as the Hundred Flavors Restaurant Manager any longer regardless.

Pung Yang eyed Crimson Tiger warily. "Are you the Hundred Flavors Restaurant Manager, Jin Ho?"

"Y-Yes, but what's all this about?" Crimson Tiger glanced around nervously.

"Have you met Eum Sagwon?"

"Who's Eum Sagwon? I don't know anyone with that name."

Pung Yang eyes flashed with rage. "Nonsense!"

Trembling, Crimson Tiger replied meekly, "I am merely the manager of a simple eatery."

Pung Yang scoffed. Crimson Tiger's acting was so convincing that he wondered if he had really gotten the wrong person. To add to his confusion, there was Soeun, who claimed to know about his disciple's death.

*Argh, whatever. It won't matter who the real culprit is as long as I get rid of them all!*

aggAnnoy01

Having made up his mind, he bellowed, "Kill them!"

SHWIIIK! SREUREUK! THUD!

A single, piercing whistle cut through the air, and in its wake, all the masked men crumpled to the ground, a throwing knife embedded in each of their necks.

Pung Yang's eyes widened in disbelief, and a chill ran down his spine as he recalled the details of the surprise attack.

Crimson Tiger's hand had flickered once, and then ten throwing knives flew out in all directions, taking down all of his subordinates in one fell swoop. Although his men had been careless, this was nonetheless a feat that even he would find difficult.

Furthermore, that wasn't even all there was to the attack. To prevent him from interfering, Crimson Tiger had even taken the liberty of throwing two more knives, forcing him to dodge the first and grab the second with his bare hands.

Pung Yang held up the throwing knife he had caught, only to discover blood trickling from the cuts on his fingers. *How is he that fast and accurate? I almost failed to block it!*

"I guess that wasn't enough to take you down, huh?" Crimson Tiger remarked, tilting his head casually.

This was his first time trying out the final technique of the Twelve Storm Daggers, Jarāmaraṇa. Despite failing to eliminate Pung Yang, he was still very satisfied with the results. After all, the Twelve Storm Daggers was a purely technical martial art, with very little reliance on qi.

*This is more than enough for a supplementary martial art. Now, the only thing left to do is to train rigorously until I perfect it.*

"......" The Hundred Flavors Restaurant staff watching the scene were dumbfounded. Although a dozen corpses had appeared at once, it felt so unreal that it wasn't frightening at all. Rather, they were more stunned by the realization that their manager was a master beyond their wildest imaginations.

Pung Yang gritted his teeth in fury. "It was you! You killed my disciple!"

"If you're talking about that crazy murderous bastard, then yes, I killed him."

"!!!" Pung Yang let out a vicious killing intent.

Crimson Tiger faced him head on unflinchingly, taunting, "Don't worry though. I'll send you to hell as well so you can re-educate your foolish disciple!"

"You crazy bastard! I'll kill you!" Pung Yang screamed, lunging forward. 

SHING! VWOOONG!

Crimson Tiger drew Soul Crusher and injected his qi into the sword, infusing it with a chilly aura.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

aggAnnoy09

The two men's swords clashed over a dozen times in the blink of an eye. As expected, One Slash Ten Kills Pung Yang used a fast sword style, but the speed of Crimson Tiger's sword was no less impressive.

Everyone watched the scene, spellbound, but no one was more surprised than Soeun.

*It was him! It was him all along! The manager is Crimson Tiger!*

She could never fail to recognize those fluid movements, that lightning quick swordsmanship that had broken through the Evil Coalition's encirclement.

Suddenly, she remembered the feeling of déjà vu she had when she first met the manager.

*How could I have been so foolish as to dismiss what my senses were telling me? Just because his personality and voice were different? I already knew he was a master of disguise!*

Soeun was flustered, but at the same time, she was thrilled to know that Crimson Tiger had been protecting her all this time.

CRASH!

The two combatants fell back to regain their bearings and prepare for the next round.

Pung Yang panted, feeling appalled. His opponent's skill was on par with his own. In fact, even while they were trading blows, the man hadn't broken eye contact with him for a single moment. That was no easy feat.

*He's on the same level as me, or maybe even stronger... When was the last time I was this apprehensive about facing an opponent?*

BOOM!

Crimson Tiger kicked off the ground and launched himself into the air. 

aggAnnoy04

Pung Yang steeled himself. Based on the vibration he felt through his feet, he could tell how powerfully Crimson Tiger had leaped.

*This is dangerous! Really dangerous!*

His instincts screamed, warning him of an impending deadly technique. A chilling sense of crisis washed over him, telling him that if he continued to fight as he had, he would be defeated.

Crimson Tiger raised his sword up high.

Pung Yang gathered all of his remaining qi. This was the final clash. He would not have a second chance. Besides, Crimson Tiger only had two options: he could either fly straight at him or come crashing down on him.

*It's all or nothing!*

He unleashed the secret technique that had made him who he was today.

ROAAAAAR!

A single, thick strand of sword qi poured out from his blade, splitting into ten strands as it flew toward Crimson Tiger with unimaginable speed and power.

"No!" Soeun cried out in alarm. Everyone else was in a daze, and only she could tell what a formidable technique this was.

Thankfully, Crimson Tiger wasn't sitting idly by as he was attacked.

WHOOSH-

The sound of a single, refreshing gust of wind.

FLASH!

A blinding flash of light streaking down from the sky.

"!!!" Soeun's eyes widened in disbelief, and a gasp unconsciously escaped from her lips. In an instant, Crimson Tiger had twisted his body and kicked off the air like it was solid ground, launching himself higher while firing a powerful wave of sword qi at Pung Yang.

She was truly flabbergasted. Crimson Tiger had already remained in the air for several breaths. Kicking off the air again in that situation and launching himself higher was a supreme skill only a true master of movement arts could display.

KWAAANG!

The ten strands of sword qi collided in the empty space where Crimson Tiger had been, sending a tremor through the area.

Normally, the sword qi Pung Yang unleashed would have changed its direction, relentlessly pursuing its target until it tore the opponent's body to shreds.

This time, however, it simply faded away.

The reason?

The light from Soul Crusher had struck the crown of Pung Yang's head.

Crimson Tiger descended from the sky, landing gently right in front of Pung Yang. 

Despite that, Pung Yang stood still, his face flushed red. 

"...What is this?" he choked, forcing the words out. His entire body felt like it was on fire, and he could only barely suppress the heat with what little internal energy he had left.

"The Tapana Blaze," Crimson Tiger whispered softly, so that only Pung Yang could hear. After all, this was the sixth technique of the Asura's Eight Narakas.

Pung Yang's face turned even redder. He was doing everything he could to forcefully hold back the heat consuming his body, but his internal organs were already cooked.

Gathering the last of his strength, he stammered, "W-What in the world are you, you..."

Crimson Tiger placed a hand on his shoulder and shook his head. "Sorry, but it's time for you to go."

The light in Pung Yang's eyes faded. The heat in his body had made its way to his brain.

THUD! FWOOOOOSH!

As Pung Yang toppled over like a straw doll, his corpse burst into flames.

"......" For a long time, the staff of the Hundred Flavors Restaurant watched with horrified expressions.

Finally, Hong Maerang broke the silence. "Manager?" she called out hesitantly.

Crimson Tiger turned to face her, smiling faintly as usual. "I told you, didn't I? I can get rid of all bad guys in a single strike."

"Good heavens! You were actually telling the truth!" Crying, Hong Maerang ran to Crimson Tiger and threw herself into his arms.

Crimson Tiger hugged her calmly.

"Manager!"

The other staff members also rushed toward Crimson Tiger, their faces a mess of tears and snot. As the metallic smell of blood from the corpses had filled their nostrils, one by one, they came to the realization that he had saved them.

Crimson Tiger asked gently, "Is anyone hurt?"

"No."

"Phew! That's good. Please wait here for a while. The Association will clear up this mess, so you don't have to worry about a thing."

Everyone nodded blankly.

Crimson Tiger glanced at Soeun, and their eyes met. From the way she was staring at him, it seemed like she had figured out who he was. He hesitated, wondering what he should say to her.

However, Soeun simply nodded at him the same way he had when he left her before.

That gesture alone was worth a thousand words.

Crimson Tiger clasped his hands together in farewell. "Well then, I guess this is goodbye," he said.

Most of the restaurant staff blinked in confusion, too distraught to realize the implications of his words.

Except for one, that is.

"Have a bite of this before you go," Gong Suk said, picking up a dish from the table and handing it to Crimson Tiger. It was one of the special dishes he had made just for today.

Crimson Tiger didn't refuse and took a bite. "This is delicious. I won't forget this taste."

Gong Suk nodded stoically. "From now on, wherever you go, whatever you do, always make sure you take care of yourself."

His words struck everyone like a thunderbolt. Only now did it hit them that Crimson Tiger was leaving for good.

"Manager!"

"Be well, all of you," Crimson Tiger said, smiling at each of them in turn and committing each face to memory. This was likely the last time he would ever see them again.

Everyone looked at him with regret, saddened by the unexpected parting. They were just beginning to grow fond of each other, yet it was already time to say goodbye.

Crimson Tiger turned to Hong Maerang. "I hope you get a good superior who's often away."

Hong Maerang bowed her head and said nothing. Only Gong Suk, standing beside her, knew she was crying.

With a final wave, Crimson Tiger left the scene.

"Are you all right?" Yeon asked Crimson Tiger. Having watched the entire fight and its aftermath, she couldn't help but be concerned for him.

"...I'm fine," Crimson Tiger replied.

The two began walking away from the restaurant manager's house.

Yeon continued, "I reported the incident to the higher-ups, so a cleanup crew should be here soon. The Third Young Lady's associates will pick up Soeun as well."

Crimson Tiger nodded. Now that his cover was blown, he would be assigned to a different role. Where would it be this time? Who would he meet? Before that, though, one problem remained.

"Yeon, don't you think there's something weird about all this?"

“What do you mean?”

“The one I just took care of was Eum Sagwon's master, Pung Yang. There's no way the First Young Lord or the Third Young Lady sent him. That can only mean there's a spy in the Radiant Hall."

"What?" Yeon's eyes widened in horror. "Are you saying that a spy leaked your information to the Evil Coalition?”

"That's right. I don't think this is the first time he's done this, either. Do you recall how the Ten Evils were already aware of my identity?"

"Ah!" Yeon nodded in understanding. "I will report this to the higher-ups immediately."

"Wait! Don't report it!" Crimson Tiger immediately stopped Yeon. "If even my information has been leaked, then that bastard probably has access to all the handlers' reports."

He had deliberately incinerated Pung Yang with the Tapana Blaze to ensure that there would be no corpse for the Association to investigate. If Yeon were to report Pung Yang's death, it would render all of his efforts pointless.

"Oh!" Yeon wiped a drop of cold sweat off her forehead. "You're right. I nearly missed that."

"If we move rashly, he'll just run away," Crimson Tiger added.

"Then what should we do?"

"We have to catch him ourselves."

"That won't be easy."

The Twelve Phantoms and their handlers were completely isolated from the Radiant Hall's chain of command. This was a whole other level from infiltrating the Inner Court.

Crimson Tiger thought for a moment, then asked, "Among the people we know, who is the most skilled at ferreting out spies?"

"The first member of the Twelve Phantoms, Olive Rat, of course."

Crimson Tiger's eyes filled with a new resolve. "I need to meet him, right now."
