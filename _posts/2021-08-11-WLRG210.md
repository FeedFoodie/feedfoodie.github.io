*Are you serious?*

Lee Sungmin stared at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman with eyes of disbelief. Through the mask's eyeholes, the Chairman's eyes curved into a smile, an expression that made it impossible to tell if the man was being serious or simply jesting.

This was the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, a man treated as a myth even in Lee Sungmin's past life. He was the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the union of Eria's unorthodox sects, yet he never showed himself or acted directly. Like a character in a story, only his name and deeds were known, and this living legend had just offered to take him as a disciple.

Lee Sungmin closed his gaping mouth and swallowed hard.

"...Are you serious?"

"Well, should I be lying?"

"Why me?" Lee Sungmin asked, his voice hesitant.

Although he had entered the Transcendent Realm, he knew all too well that his own talent was nothing special. He had only reached this point because he had endured the God of Time's trial for two thousand one hundred years, a feat made possible only by the death-like ordeal on Mount Mushii.

"My talent isn't that great," he started. "Even if I become your disciple..."

"I know," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman cut in with a smirk. "I know that your talent, at best, is merely average. The reason a person like you could enter the Transcendent Realm must be because you overcame Denir's Trial."

"...Pardon?"

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. How could the Chairman possibly know that? As a wave of confusion washed over him, the Chairman laughed heartily.

"Well, it's a guess. This one saw you fight the Dark Supreme. Your Spear Technique was far too seasoned for your age. What this one felt watching you was that your body couldn't keep up with your insights. A slight gap might be possible, but in your case, it was far too great. As far as this one knows, the only way to achieve such an abnormal state is to endure Denir's Trial. Am I wrong?"

"...You're right."

"How many years did you endure?" the Chairman asked.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before answering that he had endured for two thousand one hundred years. At his reply, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed out loud.

"You're a madman."

"...Have you also taken Denir's Trial?"

"That was a long time ago." The Chairman turned his body again, and the Fairy Realm that had retreated from his aura now fluttered back to his side. "It's a story from when this one was just a brat. This one endured for six hundred years. After that, I felt I couldn't take it anymore, so I came out, but two thousand one hundred years... Are you even human?"

Lee Sungmin recalled that even Envirus had only managed one thousand years, and Denir had said he had lasted the longest besides Lee Sungmin himself. To hear that a figure like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman could only endure for six hundred years drove home just how dreadful the trial truly was.

"Well, whatever the case," the Chairman continued, "this one is not trying to make you a disciple because you're some calamity with outstanding qualities who broke through to the Transcendent Realm at such a young age. Didn't this one say it? If you lack talent, you must be tenacious and diligent enough to devour that lack. Your ambition must be to become the world's number one, and you must never doubt that you will one day achieve it."

His steps halted. Not far away, an old hut stood in a clearing. The Chairman, who had been looking at it with his hands clasped behind his back, slowly turned around.

"If you endured for two thousand one hundred years, you've fulfilled the condition of being tenacious enough to devour your lack of talent. The problem is your ambition. What about it? Do you desire to be the world's number one?"

Lee Sungmin hesitated. World number one. Even after reaching the Transcendent Realm, he had never consciously considered the phrase. Before, his skills were too insignificant to even entertain the thought, and now, he had only come to realize just how vast the world truly was.

"You fool. You lack manliness. This one is dissatisfied with that," the Chairman smirked. "Then how about this. If the Little Heavenly Demon becomes the world's number one, will you... who sees her as an aspiration, a goal, and an ideal... seek to surpass her?"

"......"

His silence was answer enough. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman did not seem to like it, but he voiced no complaint.

"So. Will you become my disciple, or not?"

Still, Lee Sungmin could not easily answer. If he were at the peak realm, it might be a different story, but he had already reached the Transcendent Realm. What more could he possibly learn by becoming the Chairman's disciple?

"...If I become your disciple... what can I learn?"

"Hahaha!"

The Chairman burst into loud laughter at the question. After a long moment, his laughter stopped as abruptly as it had begun.

"I can teach you everything you lack."

"What is it that I lack?"

"Well now. We'll have to find that out as we go."

The reply was so laid-back it was baffling. Seeing Lee Sungmin's dumbfounded expression, the Chairman smirked and laughed again.

"Becoming a disciple doesn't necessarily mean you'll only learn martial arts."

"Are you saying there's more?"

"For you to become this one's disciple means you become the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. In other words, it means you will become this one's successor."

Lee Sungmin's body flinched. That was right. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was not simply a master with outstanding martial arts. He was the man who stood at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. Becoming his disciple meant becoming his official successor, the second-in-command of the entire union.

"Why me...?"

"This one prefers a struggling mediocrity over a calamity," the Chairman answered. "The fact that you reached the Transcendent Realm is proof of that struggle. I'm not asking you to answer right now. Take your time, think it over, and then decide. Don't come to me later with regrets."

"I understand."

In the past, the unfounded favor people showed him was due to the Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena he had gained upon regressing, a blessing that Grandmaster Bulyeong of Shaolin had since removed. Was the favor the Chairman showed him during the fight with the Dark Supreme simply because he was a struggling mediocrity? Lee Sungmin could not begin to fathom the man's true intentions.

"...Where is Wiji Hoyeon?"

"Ah, right. You should meet that brat first," the Chairman muttered. He raised a hand and pointed toward the forest. "That girl lives in the forest. She hasn't come out at all since this one beat her up. She probably knows you're here too. She's likely not coming out because I'm with you."

Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and then headed for the forest.

The dense woods were filled with the Fairy Realm. To think there were so many of them here, beings said to be harder to see than an Elf. Lee Sungmin watched them flutter around him with eyes full of wonder.

"What's your name?"

"Where are you going?"

The tiny figures swarmed to his side, chattering away. Lee Sungmin, who was seeing them for the first time, found them fascinating and cute. At first, he answered each of their questions one by one, but the task was endless. They kept flocking to him, throwing questions at him without rest, their curiosity seemingly boundless.

Then, at some point, they suddenly trembled.

"Not here."

"That woman is scary."

With a flap of their wings, the Fairy Realm moved away from Lee Sungmin.

*That woman.*

He recalled their words as they left and looked around.

"Wiji Hoyeon," he called out, his voice cutting through the quiet of the forest.

He could feel a gaze on him, but she did not show herself. In truth, Lee Sungmin already knew where she was. His expanded senses told him her exact location. She was watching him from the top of a distant tree.

Standing on a branch, Wiji Hoyeon's lips were pressed tightly together. It had been almost a year since they last met. She had imagined this reunion several times, but she could not bring herself to run to him as she had in her daydreams.

*He's gotten stronger.*

She gazed intently at his distant face, a mysterious feeling stirring within her. Wiji Hoyeon had always been stronger than Lee Sungmin. She had grown used to it, both when they first met and when they reunited after a decade. She could not identify the emotion now blooming deep in her chest.

A long time ago, when they were both young, she had told him to make her his goal, to not live aimlessly just for a better life than his last.

What about now?

Wiji Hoyeon slowly descended from the branch. She knew. She knew how much stronger her only friend had become while she was weakened by the curse. He was incomparably stronger than when she had seen him a year ago. How could Lee Sungmin, who possessed no outstanding talent, have become so powerful? What kind of tenacious, terrible effort must he have made to reach such a level?

*Me, right now.*

She approached him, and he watched her come.

*I might be weaker than you.*

Wiji Hoyeon chuckled to herself at the thought. Even without fighting, she could vaguely sense it. This reversal of roles was not unpleasant. Rather than unpleasant... it was a little frightening. *What if I've become weaker than you? What if I can no longer be someone you can set as your goal?*

"Hello," she said, breaking the silence first. She greeted him with a warm smile.

Lee Sungmin stared blankly at her as she approached. It was their first meeting in a year. She had barely changed, though perhaps her hair had grown a little longer. The gauntness from her curse was gone, replaced by the vitality she had regained in the months since it was lifted.

"How's your body? Are you okay?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Wiji Hoyeon nodded, her steps halting a short distance away. They stood facing each other in the quiet forest.

"Did you hear from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?" she asked.

"About what?"

"Before you came. I challenged him, and I lost." Wiji Hoyeon was still smiling as she spoke. "He's an incredible master. Even I... couldn't last a Ten Exchanges Match." She giggled, adding, "It's funny to say this as soon as we meet."

Her feet began to move again, closing the distance between them.

"The moment I took the first strike, I had a premonition. I'm going to lose here," she hummed. "When I took the second, I had another. If the Chairman had wanted to kill me, he could have done it with that first blow."

Even as she spoke of defeat, her voice was not gloomy. Instead, it was mixed with excitement.

"On the third strike, I suffered internal injuries."

And it was mixed with laughter.

"On the fourth strike... yes, I took it with my left arm. It broke. The Chairman's attack crushed my Protective Qi Aura like tofu."

Her laughter grew louder.

"On the fifth, I barely managed to think. Let's just endure ten strikes. I didn't want it to be a Ten Exchanges Match, and on the seventh strike, I collapsed. I just couldn't bear it. He... the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was a monster. All my life, I thought that if there was a human who could be called a 'monster,' it would be me. Do you think I was arrogant?"

"No. I don't think so."

"I was arrogant," Wiji Hoyeon said, shaking her head. "Fighting the Fist Honored One taught me that the world was quite wide, but the fight with the Chairman was an opportunity to realize just how small I was. It was also an opportunity to realize how stubborn and selfish I had been."

Her steps stopped. She was standing right in front of him now.

"Didn't the Chairman tell you he'd take you as his disciple?"

"...He did."

"Become his disciple," Wiji Hoyeon said. "His martial arts are superior to all the arts I've taught you. It would be impossible to abandon your Spear Technique and inner qi at this point, but someone at his level could supplement the shortcomings of what you've learned rather than teaching you something new."

"That might be true."

"Well, isn't it something not even worth considering in the first place? Becoming the disciple of a master like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is a miraculous encounter that won't happen again in this world."

"The greatest miraculous encounter I've had was meeting you."

Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing at his words. "You sure know how to say the right things." Her smile deepened. "I have a favor to ask. May I?"

"What favor?"

"Let's have a duel." Her smile deepened. "It's been ten years, hasn't it?"
