Aine was probably dead. There was no reason not to kill her, not after kidnapping her and luring Jeniela all this way. Their offer to join forces and destroy the world, to help turn the little Chimera into a Slaughter Predator, was impossible. They never had any intention of returning Aine.

Even suspecting as much, Jeniela had no choice but to come. She had lost the Eye of Futuresight and could no longer see what was to come. To know if Aine lived or died, she had to see with her own eyes.

*Should I have killed her back then?*

The thought pricked at her as she stood at the edge of the bow. Her gaze drifted past the rotten mast and its sail of stitched-together rags to the large full moon hanging in the sky. Her magic propelled the ship across the West Sea and through the night, and below her, the Fairy Forest spread out like a dark carpet.

The light of the barrier swallowed the night's darkness, shining far brighter than the moon. As a Vampire, Jeniela could not see the forest scenery beyond its glow.

If only she had killed her back then. She almost indulged in the regret, but stopped herself. It was not over yet.

She had planned to turn Aine into a Slaughter Predator, but if the girl was truly dead...

*Making them beg would be fun, too.*

A smile touched her lips. She wanted to hear their screams, their pleas to be turned into a Vampire.

"I've been lenient enough," she murmured.

There had been plenty of chances to kill them. Her curiosity about something she could not see with the All-Seeing Demonic Eye, her desire to see them fall into utter despair before making them her own... perhaps that was why. It seemed they had taken her too lightly. She had shown a reasonable amount of goodwill, had been quite lenient, but kidnapping Aine was too extreme. Her anger at things not going her way was precisely why she had chosen the Night of the Full Moon.

A faint smile played on Jeniela's lips as she stretched a hand toward the forest. By taking such a drastic measure, they had chosen this place as the battleground. She grew curious about what preparations they had made. First, she had to break through that cumbersome barrier.

KIIIIING!

Jeniela's magic, enveloping the Ghost Ship, grew denser.

On the forest floor, Teresa gasped. "...Ugh...!"

She squeezed her eyes shut, her grip on her rosary tightening until her knuckles were white. The magic circle Scarlet had drawn where she knelt began to glow, and what her tightly shut eyes saw was not darkness, but a brilliant, searing light. The circle transferred the holy power emanating from her directly into the barrier surrounding the forest. Feeling her insides churn, Teresa focused her mind even more intently.

"They're holding on?" The corners of Jeniela's mouth twitched upward. That powerful holy energy was annihilating her magic on contact.

She had intended to break through in one go, but it was not as easy as she thought. Not that it troubled her. Even without the Night of the Full Moon, she could have easily torn through a barrier of this level.

From their respective positions, Lloyd and Scarlet watched the sky. They could see the Ghost Ship pushing its way through the dazzling barrier like a stubborn wedge.

"That ignorant bitch," Scarlet spat, chewing on her lip. She had planned for the barrier to filter out minor Undead, not for them to push through with such a brutish method.

*No, it looks brutish, but it's a wise method.*

[We need to destroy the ship.]

At Lloyd's telepathic command, Scarlet nodded and prepared her magic. This area of the vast forest was her claimed territory, the ground a web of intricate, intertwined magic circles. She had forcibly diverted a portion of the forest's abundant magic and gathered it here.

Scarlet spread her hands wide, aiming at the ship straining against the barrier. Her fingers danced, writing runes in the air as the magic tomes floating around her flew open.

The bombardment began. Fired from within the barrier, the magical attacks passed through the holy energy without obstruction.

KWAAANG!

The Ghost Ship shuddered under the assault.

"Queen, it's magic," a Lich mumbled as it approached. It was not an Arch Lich, but it was the most skilled among them.

"I know," Jeniela replied, lowering her outstretched hand. "Counter it."

The Liches gathered, each chanting spells in their own way while the Demon Lords they worshipped added dark magic from beyond the dimensions.

GIGIGIGING.

Dozens of different magic circles formed around the Ghost Ship, intercepting the magic from Scarlet and Lloyd.

Jeniela's eyes curved into a smile.

*I can't...!*

Teresa knew she could hold on longer if she forced it, but it would only be buying time. Biting her lower lip, she opened her eyes. The barrier dissipated. She could not pour all her strength into this one instance. She gasped for breath and closed her eyes again.

Having broken through, the Ghost Ship crashed into the forest. Scarlet stopped her bombardment and confirmed its landing position. The barrier had not shattered because it could not hold. Teresa had simply given up resisting.

[I'm sorry, I should have held on longer...]

[No. Holding on longer would have only bought us time. The priority is to get the Undead off that troublesome ship.]

Lloyd's reply was soothing to the panting Teresa. Scarlet, meanwhile, unleashed the magic she had prepared in advance. Lloyd did the same, their familiars and Observation Eyes planted throughout the forest showing them what was happening where the Ghost Ship had landed.

"Should I just kill them all?" Ju Won asked.

"Capture them if you can," Jeniela replied. "If that's not possible, then kill them."

"What about the Phantom Spear?"

"He's mine."

Ju Won did not argue. He nodded and looked around, searching for the presences scattered throughout the forest.

*Here.*

The thought was not a voice, but if it were, that is what it would have said. One of the energies was extremely blatant, exuding a fighting spirit so full of belligerence that anyone could notice it, a clear invitation to come closer. It was not just belligerent. Excluding the Phantom Spear, the energy from that direction was the strongest.

As he turned to head that way, Ju Won's body flinched to a stop. He turned his head, looking in a different direction for a moment before clicking his tongue in disappointment. It was the direction of the Phantom Spear. The man's energy had exploded for an instant. It was quickly subdued, but the power he felt in that brief moment far surpassed that of the belligerent one. The desire to go to the Phantom Spear pulled at him, but Ju Won did not disobey Jeniela's words.

Jeniela watched Ju Won's back as he moved away, then turned her head to look in the same direction he had.

"I see," she muttered with a grin. "Chen, Kun. You don't need to follow me either. Scatter. Capture them if you can. If you have no choice but to kill, then do so... but if that happens, I'll be disappointed in you."

The two of them sensed what she was thinking, what she intended to do. They must not disappoint the Queen. With stiff faces, the two Vampires bowed and stepped back. Once they too had moved, Jeniela looked around at the Death Knights, Yokai, and Liches disembarking from the ship.

"My orders for you are the same. Capture them and bring them to me. I don't care if all their limbs are cut off, as long as they're alive. Understood? You are not to kill them. If you do, you'll die by my hand."

The content of the orders was different, but they all understood what Jeniela truly desired was to capture her enemies alive.

Everyone left to carry out their orders, leaving only Gemini, who grinned at Jeniela.

"What orders do you have for me?"

"I have no orders for you," Jeniela said, moving toward the lake. "Follow me if you want. If there's something else you'd rather do, do as you please."

"I'll follow. That's bound to be more fun. Hey, Queen. Can I ask you something?"

"......"

"How many times do you think you'll die today?"

Gemini bared his fangs in a smile. At his words, Jeniela just shrugged slightly and laughed. The large, bright full moon was elevating her mood. Her magic power, already overflowing to an immeasurable degree, was amplified to near infinity by its blessing. The urge to drink blood made her fangs itch and her throat parch.

"A moment ago, I thought I wouldn't die even once," she said, "but now I'm not so sure."

That only made it all the more exciting.

"I've come to the right place," Ju Won said, stopping.

His opponent was not hiding, but sitting cross-legged on a large rock. Several empty liquor bottles rolled around its base. The Spear King poured the last of the bottle he held into his mouth and staggered to his feet.

"For over ten years, ever since I first saw you in the forests of the North, I've been waiting for the day I'd fight you."

Ju Won did not answer, just watched him. The Spear King slapped his own flushed cheeks and grinned.

"It's fine, it's fine. A little buzz like this is nothing." He stretched languidly as his inner qi completely evaporated the alcohol. "I only drank to kill time. The waiting was both boring and exciting."

"I intend to capture you," Ju Won stated, raising a hand.

"......"

The veins on his bent fingers bulged, the tips of his curved nails stained black with poison. "Killing you would be easier, but Jeniela doesn't want that."

"Is that so? I'm planning on killing you." The Spear King laughed heartily and drew the spear slung across his back. He held it upright, the point aimed at Ju Won, his knees slightly bent and ready to spring forward at any moment. He met Ju Won's eyes, feeling a chilling thrill and an almost maddening sense of anticipation. This was far more tense and exhilarating than facing the Spirit Queen. "You'd be better off not having such a complacent thought as capturing me. You'll die if you do."

"I do plan to kill you if circumstances don't allow for your capture."

"Then come at me with the intent to kill from the very beginning," the Spear King said. "What's your name?"

"Ju Won." He did not use the name he had long since abandoned, Mad Wolf. The muscles on his lean body swelled as the full moon's blessing worked on him, his suppressed ferocity trying to awaken.

"I am the Spear King." The Spear King did not give his name. To him, that title was more precious and meaningful than any other.

Neither moved first. Then, at the same time, they vanished.

They kicked off the ground, each aiming for the other's opening. Ju Won rushed in at high speed, swinging his poison-stained claws at the Spear King without hesitation. Dashing forward, the Spear King thrust his spear to meet the air-rending claws.

Arm and spear tangled. Ju Won's claws grasped empty air as the Spear King braced his bent legs to withstand the weight of the attack. Suddenly, the Spear King's weapon split in two. The long spear, held only in his right hand, faltered under Ju Won's power, but in that same instant, the short spear in his left hand stabbed toward Ju Won's chest. The moment the spear tip touched him, Ju Won thrust out his open left hand, and the spinning weapon was sucked into his palm.

KWADEDEUK!

The short spear tore Ju Won's palm to shreds but could not shatter the bone. The rampaging weapon was caught firmly in his hand. The Spear King immediately let go of it and gripped the long spear with both hands.

KWAJIJIK!

Thrust from close range, the spear pierced Ju Won's chest. The Spear King did not push it all the way in, knowing such an attack held little meaning against a monster that was nearly immortal.

KWAANG!

The spear embedded in Ju Won's chest exploded. The Spear King had forcefully infused it with enhanced qi to detonate it, but Ju Won was not seriously injured. As he pulled the end of the spear from his chest and walked out of the smoke, the Spear King took out a new weapon.

"They call you the Spear King, but you know how to use an axe too?"

"This isn't just an axe, you ignorant beast," the Spear King clicked his tongue, gripping the new weapon with both hands. "It's called a spear-axe."
