Blood Demon froze, his body rigid within a space that hummed with a strange disharmony. He scanned his surroundings, a flicker of confusion crossing his mind. *Is this... a martial art?* He had never encountered a technique like this before and felt a momentary sense of unease.

Just a moment ago, he had been fighting in the skies above the raging battle at the Blood Alliance. He remembered the violet light gathering at the tip of the spear, and then the world had shaken.

*Where am I?*

He slowly tried to move his arm, finding no strange sensation restraining him. He strained his ears, but the clamor of battle, the shouts and the clash of weapons, was gone. There was only silence. He opened his mouth to speak, certain a sound had left his lips, yet it never reached his own ears.

*This is less of a martial art... and more like magic. No, should I call it yokai magic?*

His judgment was more or less correct. Great yokai often used their Demonic Energy to cause phenomena that were normally impossible, a power known as yokai magic. Strictly speaking, the Demonic Eye of a pureblood Vampire, their very immortality, or even Ju Won's poison could be considered forms of it, as they were all phenomena that defied common sense.

From within the mist, Lee Sungmin watched Blood Demon. This was the first time he had used Illusionary World since his battle with the Spear King ten years prior. Originally, the eighth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear had not been this kind of technique. If it had, it would have been far too esoteric for Wiji Hoyeon to have ever taught him. The initial version was a simpler martial art, one that deceived an opponent's eyes with light suggestions and illusions during the spear's movements.

However, that all changed after it passed through the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The Chairman had recognized that Lee Sungmin, despite having the body of an incomplete yokai, possessed Demonic Energy comparable to that of a great one. He had modified Illusionary World to better suit him.

Back then, just unfolding the technique had brought a splitting headache, and his Demonic Energy had later run rampant, too much for his body to withstand.

Not anymore.

There was no pain, only a mind clearer than ever. His body felt light, his heart pounded with a steady, moderate tension. His senses were sharp, his vision pristine. Unlike the struggle of the past, standing within his Illusionary World now felt comfortable, even good. It was proof that his body had finally become that of a complete yokai.

This was a space saturated with his Demonic Energy. Where unleashing Illusionary World had once simply released that energy, it now caused mysterious phenomena. Lee Sungmin did not know it, but his technique was a form of spatial erosion. The Demonic Energy filling his body had seeped into the very fabric of this place, seizing complete control.

[This feels good.]

Void Monarch, a yokai himself, chattered from within the world, feeling just as serene as Lee Sungmin.

Blood Demon raised both his hands.

KIIIIING!

Red enhanced qi flared to life, coating them as he activated the Asura Heaven Destruction Art, the very technique that had brutally torn through Lee Sungmin's martial arts before, but before he could unleash it, Lee Sungmin moved.

He could not hear a thing. This strange space was reminiscent of a barrier, and since no one would break it from the outside, he knew he had to shatter it himself to escape. He did not think it would be an easy task. They would not have deliberately pushed him into a space like this only to let him escape so easily.

SWOOSH!

A spear materialized from a space that had been empty air a moment before, thrusting toward him. Blood Demon twisted his body, barely avoiding the strike. Had he been even a fraction of a second slower, it would have pierced him. The sheer power in the simple thrust was extraordinary, and the edge of his Protective Qi Aura scattered into dust where the spearhead grazed it. As the spear withdrew, Blood Demon reached out to grab it, but the moment his hand closed, the weapon collapsed into mist. His expression hardened as he sensed the change, instinctively strengthening his Protective Qi Aura.

KAGAGAK!

The mist reformed, transforming into dozens of blades that assailed him, the force of their assault shaking his entire defense and pushing his body back. It did not end there. A mass of mist shimmered, and from within it, a violet electric current flashed.

"......"

Though he could not hear his own voice, Blood Demon could not help but mutter.

"What a cheap martial art."

He was right. From unseen locations, five sky-piercing attacks shot out at once. Blood Demon let out a dry laugh and spun his body violently.

PABABABAK!

The spin scattered red enhanced qi in all directions. Without stopping his rotation, he threw himself toward the sky-piercing attacks that converged from different directions. Just as they were about to hit him, they collapsed again, passing futilely through his body before reforming into hundreds of bundles of enhanced qi.

Blood Demon gritted his teeth at the new attack coming from behind him. He could not trust what he saw. Every technique unleashed within this space was both an illusion and a reality. It was astonishing that someone could wield a martial art like this. Was this the power of a yokai? Blood Demon clicked his tongue, a slight sense of satisfaction rising within him.

However, he knew Lee Sungmin must have more. This was not enough. He had to grasp everything about him.

Blood Demon extended both hands toward the incoming bundles of enhanced qi. Was this another illusion? He was not sure, but he swung his hands regardless.

KWADEDEUK!

His palm wind collided with the enhanced qi. It was no illusion. Bracing his legs against the weight pushing back his hands, Blood Demon gritted his teeth. The power he had accumulated and refined for hundreds of years surged within him.

KUUUNG.

A red light erupted. Behind him, a crimson Asura rose as he operated the Asura Heaven Destruction Art to its peak. Blood Demon roared and thrust out his clenched left fist.

KWAAAANG!

With a deafening explosion, the bundles of enhanced qi vanished. A roar loud enough to shake the entire Illusionary World struck the heavens and the earth. Beyond the violet mist, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear. It was time for him to step in directly.

Blood Demon saw him. His blood-red eyes gleaming, he ran forward.

Toward the charging Blood Demon, Lee Sungmin slowly raised his spear. Everything around them wavered like a mirage. Blood Demon was clearly running, but the distance between them was not closing. This space was the problem. Fighting here meant bearing too many penalties.

*A tricky martial art. However...*

It would not work on Jeniela. He was certain of it. A technique of this kind would never work on her. Just figuring that out was a great achievement. Therefore, he needed to find out something more. Blood Demon opened his mouth and roared, the sound transcending the space and making Lee Sungmin's skin tingle.

Blood Demon was not a pureblood Vampire. Seven hundred years ago, he had been a human, a martial artist. Though no one might remember now, back then, there was no one who did not know the name Ghost Blood Asura Ju Heebaek. In that era, there was no universally acknowledged, overwhelming world number one, and in those chaotic times, his name was always mentioned when connoisseurs discussed who held that title.

He held no ambition for power, finding his joy simply in the practice of martial arts, and cared little for the title of world number one. He wandered the world without settling in one place. At the time, he had thought it was a chance encounter, but now he knew better. He knew that Jeniela had engineered their meeting. That the child with the All-Seeing Eye had known this would happen and had deliberately stood in his way.

He remembered her standing there, alluring yet stained with blood amidst a pile of corpses, holding young Gemini's hand. Her request to be adopted was one she had made already knowing his answer.

It did not matter.

Out of interest and curiosity, he adopted Jeniela and Gemini. He had lived most of his life alone, but living with his adopted daughters was enjoyable. He found joy in wandering the world with others instead of by himself. Even things he had already seen felt new when he saw them with those children. He was well aware that his two adopted daughters were monsters, Vampires who drank human blood. Even so, he did not find them repulsive.

That must have been why he was chosen. At the time, Jeniela and Gemini were not very strong, and their pure blood made them good prey for other Vampires and inhumans. Having become their adoptive father, Ju Heebaek had to protect his daughters from the monsters that attacked every night. He felt no discontent about it. It was fun. Yes, that was enough. Their interests had simply aligned. Ju Heebaek needed a family to help him forget his loneliness, and Jeniela and Gemini needed a reliable guardian to fend off the monsters.

When he heard the request to become a Vampire, Ju Heebaek had smiled awkwardly. The moment he saw his adopted daughter's smile, which did not consider refusal an option, he felt he could not say no. In the end, he became a Vampire.

And today, he would die. His long life of seven hundred years would end today. He could not die in vain. He had not seen the end of martial arts, but his final death had to be as he wished, no, as his adopted daughter wished.

First, he had to destroy this uncomfortable space. The distance still was not closing. Meanwhile, the attacks came without pause. Blood Demon's hands moved slowly. The red enhanced qi covering his entire body swirled in time with his hand gestures. An unhurried palm strike met the space, and a colossal force, incomparable to anything before, shook the world to its foundations. He had already deciphered the nature of this technique. He had confirmed it would be no threat to Jeniela. There was no need to observe it any longer.

[He's trying to shatter it completely.]

The Demonic Energy that had eroded the space trembled. Lee Sungmin's own attacks had failed to pierce Blood Demon's Protective Qi Aura, a clear sign of the strength he still held in reserve. Lee Sungmin's spear moved, and a violet electric current wrapped around the entire space.

"Myraid Thunder," he declared, his lips parting.

BZZZZZZT!

All the Demonic Energy that constituted the world transformed into a storm of lightning. Watching it descend upon him, Blood Demon realized he could not avoid it. In that case, he could only face it, break through it, and endure. The Protective Qi Aura covering his body took the form of an Asura. Two pairs of arms shot up from behind his back.

Within the red enhanced qi, Blood Demon performed a dance, his hand gestures splitting the lightning. In the middle of the Myraid Thunder, his body did not bend in the slightest. The strikes were landing on his Protective Qi Aura, but they lacked decisive power. Killing a centuries-old Vampire like Blood Demon with an ordinary attack was impossible in the first place.

As Lee Sungmin moved his spear, the lightning was drawn to its trajectory. He fired the Soul Chasing Annihilation Lightning, aiming for an opening in Blood Demon's defense. The two pairs of arms behind Blood Demon's back moved simultaneously, the hands made of enhanced qi grabbing the empty air. The space that should not have been graspable, the very Demonic Energy that constituted the Illusionary World, was caught entirely in Blood Demon's hands and shaken.

Cracks began to form in the air. It was impossible to keep him contained.

A small desire bloomed in a corner of Lee Sungmin's chest. Blood Demon's six hands struck forward, and an intangible energy swept over him. Gathering the scattering Demonic Energy into one place, Lee Sungmin unleashed the Void Path. A small hole formed in the center of Blood Demon's palm force, an electric current seething within it. As he thrust his spear all the way through, the Explosive Thunder erupted and engulfed Blood Demon.

The corners of Blood Demon's mouth twitched and rose. It had been a long time since he had fought like this.

The Illusionary World shattered completely. As the world returned to normal, the gathered Demonic Energy scattered in all directions like a violent gust of wind. Lee Sungmin gathered that dispersing energy, loading it onto the tip of his spear before firing another sky-piercing attack. This was no simple thrust. He imbued it with the highest martial theory he possessed.

KWARURURURUNG!

A sound like the sky itself collapsing rang out. Blood Demon sensed this attack was different. Perhaps this one... this was it.

He recalled Jeniela's smiling face. *Please, Father.* He had never been able to refuse her requests when she asked with that sly smile.

*I wanted to hear her call me Father.*

A title she had stopped using hundreds of years ago.

Blood Demon broke free from his reverie, raising both hands as the shadow of the Asura behind him swelled to a massive size. This was dangerous.

*For me, and for that child.*

Therefore, he could not avoid it. The movement of the spear created a thundercloud, and within it, bolts of lightning seethed with immense power. Lee Sungmin fired the sky-piercing, charged to its absolute limit.

"Genesis."

The world flashed.
