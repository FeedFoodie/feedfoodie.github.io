Lee Sungmin had been attacked five times by the time he left the village, yet he emerged from the relentless assaults without a single scratch.

His assailants were all Lycanthropes, werewolves to be precise.

*Ghost Wolf Sect.*

The name the dying innkeeper had mentioned surfaced in his mind. It was the murim sect that dominated this area. Their actions marked them as unorthodox, but the Lee Sungmin of his past life had never even heard of them. The attackers themselves were not particularly skilled, but the raw power of their Lycanthrope bodies allowed them to exert a force that far surpassed their level of martial arts.

"How long are you going to keep this up?"

At the edge of the village, Lee Sungmin finally lowered his spear. Before him, a Lycanthrope with severed legs lay gasping in the snow. The creature looked up at him as he brushed away the swirling snow with one hand.

"If you stop coming, I have no reason to hunt you down. Why don't we just end this here?"

"Bullshit," the man spat. "You humiliate us like this and expect us to just stop? Don't be ridiculous!"

"You're the ones who attacked first."

"You caused a disturbance in a village we rule."

Lee Sungmin laughed at the sheer absurdity of the claim. A disturbance? Was that what they called it? *Would it have ended cleanly if I had just succumbed to the poison back there?*

"We never forget a grudge. Even if you leave this place, the Ghost Wolf Sect will hunt you down and kill you. Wolves are persistent..."

Lee Sungmin had already stopped listening. He flicked the spear he had set down.

THWACK!

The piled snow bloomed red. After confirming the man was dead, Lee Sungmin moved on.

"It'll be night soon," Rubia warned. "Vampires and Lycanthropes are classic examples of the inhuman. They're creatures favored by the night."

[That's true for most inhuman,] the Void Monarch added, his voice bored. [Those who become monsters, no longer human, are blessed by the night and the moon. They grow stronger and more ferocious. Is it a full moon tonight?]

"No, it's not."

[That's a relief. Things would have gotten quite troublesome.]

"I don't think it's that serious, is it?" Rubia countered. "The ones who attacked were so much weaker than Sungmin. It's not a full moon, so I doubt they'll get strong enough to threaten him just because night falls..."

[They did not fear death,] the Void Monarch chuckled. Lee Sungmin said nothing, and amid the silence, the monarch continued. [Vampires and Lycanthropes. They create lesser beings under a single Lord. The stronger the Lord, the stronger the beings born from him.]

"They're called a Clan," Rubia corrected.

[The name doesn't matter. Do you remember why I warned you against becoming a Vampire or a Lycanthrope?]

"You can't defy the Lord," Lee Sungmin recalled. "That's why you didn't recommend it. Not that I ever intended to become one."

[Correct. The hierarchy within their kind is absolute. To escape a Lord's control, you must amass enough power to completely overwhelm him. That's no easy task. As long as you belong to them, everything is controlled by and exposed to the Lord.]

As he listened, Lee Sungmin's thoughts drifted to the Sword Phantom. He recalled how the Black Mage Kim Jonghyun, who had pursued the phantom alongside him, had assessed the man as having a 'low rank as a Vampire'. Perhaps the Lord who turned the Sword Phantom had done so simply to keep the Supreme Peak beastfolk in check.

[A Lord influences his subjects in many ways. 'Loyalty', in particular, is a symbol of the Lord's strength. Not a single one of the werewolves who attacked you feared death, not even for a moment. That isn't loyalty born from fear, but loyalty engraved by 'rank'. I don't know who the Lord leading these creatures is, but he probably won't be an easy opponent.]

With the Void Monarch's warning echoing in his mind, Lee Sungmin grew curious about the master of the Ghost Wolf Sect. He summoned Nebul to request information and had completely left the village by the time the familiar returned.

[Don't go into the mountains.]

A low, snow-covered mountain loomed near the village. Even without the Void Monarch's advice, he had no intention of entering it. He did not know much about werewolves, but they were a mix of human and beast. In a place like the mountains, a beast would naturally hold the advantage over a human.

He skirted the mountain and set his course for Trabia. It was not long before Nebul delivered the information he had requested.

The Ghost Wolf Sect was an unorthodox sect that controlled the surrounding region, though its power was said to be relatively small. It exerted its influence over the various small and large villages rather than the major cities of the North like Trabia. The sect's defining characteristic was that every single one of its members was a werewolf. Its master was a man named Ju Won, who reportedly engaged in almost no public activities.

That did not mean there was no information about him.

*Predator.*

Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed as he processed the word.

Predator. Beings who were once human but had become something else. They were a group of monsters, particularly vicious and powerful even among the inhuman, who stood near the apex of their kind. Lee Sungmin pressed his temples. In his past life, he had not even known of their existence, but the information he now possessed included details about them.

[What bad luck,] the Void Monarch muttered.

Rubia felt the same. Her face was noticeably pale as she fidgeted nervously.

"Are they dangerous?" she asked.

"Is that even a question?!" Rubia shrieked. "The Predator don't operate in the open, but in the shadows, they're a gathering of monsters with absolute power! I can't believe the Ghost Wolf Sect is connected to them!"

"There was no information that Ju Won belonged to the Predator," Lee Sungmin clarified. "It just said there was a possibility of a connection."

[The Predator are dangerous,] the Void Monarch cackled. His tone made it clear he knew of them, so Lee Sungmin asked him about it.

The Void Monarch laughed before answering. [The inhuman live for a very long time. Having abandoned their humanity, they live far longer than humans. They were around four hundred years ago, too.]

*...Were you also part of the Predator?*

[Didn't I tell you? Yokai are inhuman, mutants, and monsters. The origins of Yokai are diverse. Since my own origins aren't human, I was never part of the Predator, but I know they're dangerous.]

"It's my fault. I'm sorry," Rubia muttered, her ears drooping. She seemed to think none of this would have happened if they had not entered the village.

Lee Sungmin shook his head. "This area is the Ghost Wolf Sect's territory. Even if we hadn't stopped at the village, we would've run into trouble with them just by passing through."

"That's right."

A voice drifted from the blizzard ahead. Lee Sungmin's expression hardened as he looked toward the sound. The fierce blizzard obscured his vision, the howling wind swallowing all sound. He expanded his senses, pushing them past the whistling gale and the driving snow.

Beyond it all stood a man clad in thick furs against the cold. Lee Sungmin silently gripped his spear. The man's gaze alone conveyed a beast-like ferocity.

*Ju Won.*

The master of the Ghost Wolf Sect. The man did not introduce himself, but Lee Sungmin was certain. If a monster like that was not the sect master, who else could possibly hold the title?

"Even if you hadn't stopped at that village, we would have detected someone as skilled as you," Ju Won's voice carried clearly despite the distance. "I would have debated whether to engage you or not, though. So... who are you?"

"Lee Sungmin."

"That name... it sounds familiar," Ju Won muttered, nodding to himself. "Ah, I remember. You're the one they call the Phantom Spear. I'm Ju Won."

In contrast to his savage eyes, his voice was low and his speech was calm. He began to walk, and in just a few steps, he passed through the blizzard and drew near.

"I heard the Phantom Spear was with the orthodox sects."

Strangely, Ju Won looked tired, or more accurately, jaded. All the werewolves Lee Sungmin had seen until now had been large and powerfully built, but Ju Won's physique was not particularly big. The eyes gleaming from beneath his drooping eyelids, however, held a light so fierce it was impossible to dismiss.

"Now that I see you in person, you don't feel like you're from the orthodox sects. How should I put it... you don't seem like you're from the unorthodox sects, either. You actually seem closer to an inhuman."

*Is it because of the lingering Demonic Energy?* Lee Sungmin toyed with the spear slung across his back as he watched Ju Won scratch his chin, lost in thought.

"Do you have any idea what I'm thinking right now?" Ju Won asked.

"No idea."

"I'm deciding whether or not to kill you," he replied, his expression unchanging.

Lee Sungmin knew it was not a simple threat. This inhuman, a werewolf who might have lived for hundreds of years, possessed a power that was impossible to fathom.

[So he changed his name,] the Void Monarch muttered.

Without any prompting from Lee Sungmin, the Magic Armor began to hum as the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy poured from it. Ju Won's eyebrows furrowed as he watched the ominous energy surge.

"This is familiar..."

[Mad Wolf.]

The air itself seemed to vibrate with the name. The voice of the Void Monarch, speaking through the Magic Armor, reached Ju Won. At the sound of it, Ju Won's shoulders twitched.

"...Haha!" he burst out laughing. "An unforgettable Demonic Energy. Void Monarch. I heard you died four hundred years ago..."

[I was Sealed.]

"Where'd you ditch your body to end up dwelling in a piece of armor?"

[I don't know where my body is either. It might've been destroyed.]

"You've ended up in a pitiful state," Ju Won said, his eyes flashing. "What did you want to say that made you show yourself?"

[I don't want a pointless fight. It's not like we're strangers, and the problem we had wasn't that big of a deal, was it?]

"So you're asking me to just let him go?"

"......"

Ju Won's expression grew strange. He frowned, his gaze shifting between Lee Sungmin and the swirling Demonic Energy.

"...It seems four hundred years is a long time. To think I'd hear such words from the Void Monarch..."

[If you want to fight, feel free to refuse,] the Void Monarch challenged.

Ju Won seemed to ponder for a moment before shaking his head. "No. As you said, we're not strangers, so let's consider this incident as if it never happened. A few werewolves died, but it's not a great loss."

With those words, Ju Won turned. He took a few steps and vanished back into the storm. Rubia, who had been hiding silently behind Lee Sungmin, let out a huge sigh of relief. Lee Sungmin lowered the hand gripping his spear.

"If we had fought, would I have lost?" he asked.

[I don't know what whim possessed him to run a sect of all things,] the Void Monarch said. [Ju Won... no, Mad Wolf is one of the most formidable monsters among the Lycanthropes. He's also part of the Predator. There was nothing for you to gain by fighting him.]

"...Haha!"

Lee Sungmin burst out laughing. The Ghost Wolf Sect. A minor unorthodox sect he had never heard any major rumors about, and yet its master was a member of the Predator and one of the most powerful monsters among the Lycanthropes. He would have had to risk death just to defeat him.

*Indeed. The world is a wide place.*

He turned, heading in the opposite direction from where Ju Won had disappeared. Even after reaching the realm of Supreme Peak, there were still many in this wide world who were stronger than him. He found joy in this, not despair. The knowledge that his path continued onward, rather than ending at an insurmountable wall, pleased him.

*The North.*

A land occupied by the inhuman and demonic practitioners. Who was this nobleman he was supposed to meet there? Lee Sungmin wiped his sweaty palms on his clothes, the moisture a lingering remnant of his confrontation with Ju Won.

'We'll act as if it never happened.' Ju Won kept his word. There were no more werewolf attacks as they crossed the snowfield. They spotted a few villages, but perhaps because of their recent experience, Rubia did not suggest they stop.

Thanks to that, they had to camp outdoors again. Lee Sungmin skillfully dug a burrow in a snowdrift and crawled inside. With the sound of the howling wind muffled, he curled up and closed his eyes.
