*Did Abel meet Kim Jonghyun?*

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, his feet dragging with every step. Though his body had recovered, movement was a struggle.

That final blow. The Gaebyeok that had erased Vollander from existence had consumed a staggering amount of his mental energy. It was a stark reminder that he still had a long way to go. Swallowing hard, he pushed his tilting body upright and, after a few deep breaths, quickened his pace.

He had done it. He had killed Vollander, the lord of the Death Knights who had reigned over Predator for centuries, annihilating him just moments ago.

Still,, Lee Sungmin felt no great emotion from the act. Any sense of accomplishment for what he had done, any sweetness of victory, was all too faint to feel and savor. He felt little accomplishment because he still considered himself lacking, and the victory felt hollow because annihilating Vollander was not the end of the fight.

Instead, a bitter taste lingered in his mouth. Vollander, once an outstanding knight, had been cursed to live as a Death Knight for hundreds of years. Throughout those centuries, he had protected his own knightly code, only to abandon it all upon finding a way to become human again. He had wanted to become human so badly that he was willing to go to such lengths.

He had wanted to die as a human, because a Death Knight could never reincarnate. To be honest, Lee Sungmin felt a pang of guilt. The number of lives he had taken was far from small. Whether they had wished for death or not, at least death would have been a clean end for them. That was not the case for Vollander, however, and it would be the same for the other Death Knights.

[So. You wanted to turn Vollander human?] the Void Monarch sneered. [If you had let tens of thousands die so he could die as a human, then yes. Vollander would have died happily, and you would've been satisfied with that?]

*That's not it.*

[Then stop with the useless thoughts. You're pitying the man you just finished off, and it's cheap pity. Don't pity the enemy you defeated. Vollander was desperate and earnest in his own way, just as you were in yours. At the very least, by defeating him, you saved the tens of thousands of lives he was going to sacrifice to become human.]

Of course, the others would never know.

The Void Monarch sneered again.

Cheap pity. Lee Sungmin mulled over the words and let out a smirk. Yes, he was right. What was the point of thinking about it now? He had not felt a single moment of pity for Vollander when he thrust the spear of Gaebyeok.

*What happened with the Church?*

Vollander might be gone, but the Death Knights he led had not been neutralized. Should he be glad that they were at least being held captive? Teresa was there, along with the Holy Knights and Priests, so they probably would not be annihilated. Of course, that was just speculation.

"What happened?" Lee Sungmin asked in a weary voice.

He glanced around at the Yokai surrounding him, the very ones who had participated in the subjugation force. Just as the Void Monarch had warned, their target was now him, a being who possessed a dense Demonic Energy.

"Before you guys got here, what was the situation like? Did the Death Knights have the upper hand, or the Holy Knights?"

The Yokai only stared back with tense expressions, offering no answer. They had seen him defeat Vollander. What Lee Sungmin had shown in that moment was a transcendent martial prowess that far surpassed their common sense. The only reason they had stepped forward despite seeing that was because Lee Sungmin, who was staggering and could not even walk properly, looked so weakened and appetizing.

"You don't know?" Lee Sungmin asked, narrowing his eyes.

GRRR.

The Yokai bared their teeth.

*Who'll go first?* As they exchanged glances, Lee Sungmin sighed and walked forward.

PWAK!

A silent purple current erupted outwards, blowing the Yokai apart. After instantly killing a dozen or so of them, Lee Sungmin searched for a direction. He tracked the flow of ki and magic power in the air. By now, Abel was probably clashing with Kim Jonghyun. Just as he thought, he detected a violent flow of ki in the distance.

{sep}

Before Vollander fell, while he and Lee Sungmin were locked in their fierce clash, Kim Jonghyun and Abel had been facing off.

Kim Jonghyun was quite flustered. The magic he had prepared in this space had failed to activate properly, dispelled by Abel before it could even form. He had never imagined there could be a mage capable of countering him like this. While theoretically possible, the sheer audacity of it, to forcefully intervene in the moments before a spell manifested, disrupting its formula to pull off a perfect dispel… and not just for one spell. Abel had instantly dispelled dozens of them in the order of their activation.

*I see.*

That continuous dispel was not some special magic. It was nothing more than an application of a basic technique known to all mages, just executed in a breathtaking instant.

*Faithful to the basics… no, at that level, you can't even call it a basic technique.*

After bewilderment came admiration. Kim Jonghyun was genuinely impressed by Abel's method. At the same time, his interest was piqued. The Mages Guild Master. The common image of a mage was an old man engrossed in some obscure magical experiment. That was not entirely wrong. Most mages in the Guild had their own personal aspirations and devoted their lives to achieving them, which unavoidably involved magical experiments.

Abel, however, was different. Kim Jonghyun had more or less figured out what kind of mage he was. He specialized in anti-mage combat. To think the Mages Guild Master was a combat mage specializing in fighting other mages. The fact that his expectations were so wrong made Kim Jonghyun feel even more delighted.

*Well then…*

Let's see just how far that rapid, continuous dispel could go.

Kim Jonghyun had a hundred spells set up in this space. A dozen had been dispelled, but he still had an endless supply of magic he could use. This gave him an overwhelming advantage in a duel between mages. It was the magical equivalent of facing a single-shot shotgun with a machine gun that could fire endlessly.

*Here it comes.* Abel's eyes widened. Dispelling magic was a basic skill, but seeing through the spells set up in the space was his own modified Detect Eye. *It's impossible for ninety spells to manifest simultaneously. They'll be sequential, so which one is the priority?* He read the flow of Mana.

It was an easy task for him. He immediately began to dispel. To disrupt him, Kim Jonghyun tried to deceive Abel with feints, repeatedly adjusting and withdrawing the influx of Mana, even directly canceling a spell just before manifestation to cast another in its place.

However, it was useless. None of Kim Jonghyun's disruptions could confuse Abel. The machine gun that was supposed to fire endlessly could not even get a shot off.

Kim Jonghyun was impressed yet again. At the same time, he pointed a finger at Abel as if to make a point. The spells laid out in the space were not all he had. If Abel was distracted dispelling the prepared magic, could he not just cast a spell directly and attack?

Abel was no fool. It was obvious that if he was busy dealing with the laid-out spells, Kim Jonghyun would attack him directly. While not to the same extent as his opponent, Abel had also prepared a great deal for this decisive battle.

HWAACK!

As black flames surged toward him, Abel took a small bead from inside his robe's sleeve.

KWADEDEUK!

The flames vanished, replaced by a pile of pure white ice that covered the empty space. *A magic item?* Kim Jonghyun formed a hand seal as he watched the mass of ice approach.

The ice melted in an instant. It was not Silent Casting, but the casting was done at high speed. Kim Jonghyun cast three spells simultaneously to pressure Abel, all while continuing to activate the spells laid out in the space.

*I'm at a disadvantage.*

He knew. As long as he was fighting in Kim Jonghyun's territory, he had to accept and overcome this disadvantage. The scrolls and magic items Abel had made in the carriage could be used in various situations. *However, this alone isn't enough. The efficiency isn't great, but I have no choice.*

Draw a circle with the right hand and a square with the left. The shape of the circle must not collapse. The square must have sharp corners. *This damn, fucking…* Abel recalled a memory from the distant past, something he had trained in long ago to crush his damn Hyung-nim in a magic battle.

The Dual Polarity Divine Art was not a very efficient martial art. It involved splitting one's mind in two to cast two martial arts simultaneously. In theory, it was a divine art unlike any other, but it was incredibly difficult to learn. Even if one managed to cast two martial arts at once, the power was not as great as casting one properly. This was because inner arts were profound, and trying to cast two formulas at the same time was bound to cause a conflict.

Magic, however, was different.

Magic, unlike martial arts, was not cast with the body. As long as the formula was completed properly, it was possible to cast spells simultaneously without conflict, even if the body remained still.

Abel opened his lips and recited the cast at high speed. While doing so, he continued the work of dispelling. As he chanted the spell, he flicked both hands to activate the magic items he had prepared.

Kim Jonghyun's eyes widened. Abel's magic collided with his own, the activated magic items added to the firepower, and yet, the dispelling never stopped.

*Multi-casting? No, that's not it.*

Kim Jonghyun could also cast two spells at once. However, what was called multi-casting was, despite the name, nothing more than skillfully fitting two formulas together. It was merely combining two formulas into one, casting two spells with a single formula. Because of that, the completeness of the magic inevitably dropped. Furthermore, a multi-cast that allowed for dispelling and casting at the same time could not exist. How could it be possible when the two types of magic were completely different?

"Done."

The corners of Abel's mouth lifted. His eyes were already bloodshot, and blood trickled from his nose. The Dual Polarity Divine Art was one thing, but the excessive mental concentration from continuously dispelling had put a strain on his brain. Abel actually found the throbbing headache enjoyable. He had pushed himself a bit from the start, but still.

He had erased every single spell Kim Jonghyun had laid out in this space.

"You're fucked," Abel spat. He stopped the Dual Polarity Divine Art and spread his hands wide. Dozens of magic scrolls he had prepared beforehand unfurled from his sleeves. Kim Jonghyun was once again flustered that the spells laid out in the space had completely vanished. He had not imagined a day would come when he would be this flustered in an anti-mage battle, and his agitation showed on his face.

Dozens of scrolls were activated simultaneously.

There were many differences between Abel and Kim Jonghyun. In terms of the amount of magic power they possessed, Kim Jonghyun was likely superior. However, Abel had something Kim Jonghyun did not.

That was experience. Even if Kim Jonghyun possessed the knowledge of Arbes, Abel and Arbes had different magical orientations. Unlike Arbes, who was a Black Mage, Abel had thoroughly trained himself as a combat mage specializing in anti-mage warfare. It was not a direction that really suited the title of Mages Guild Master, but Abel possessed the skill befitting one who called himself the pinnacle of mages.

Magic was not simply about preparing and casting a lot of spells. It also had affinities and combinations. Abel's scrolls were configured to be perfectly synergistic. The simultaneously cast spells did not conflict but instead bolstered one another, their powers weaving together. In that configuration, the level of the magic did not matter. Even a Magic Missile, the most basic of basics, could have its power increased exponentially depending on how the formula was constructed. If you combined Hellfire, considered the strongest of fire-type magic, with Magic Missile, you got hundreds of fiery bundles. Casting ice magic and fire magic at the same time only diminished each other's power, but wind magic and fire magic were a good match.

The feast of scrolls, arranged with such calculations, forced Kim Jonghyun to retreat hastily. He pulled the hood of his robe over his head. The defensive magic enchanted on the robe activated instantly, and his body was swallowed by a translucent barrier. It was the same barrier that Lee Sungmin's spear had failed to pierce two days ago.

KWARRRRRUMBLE!

The storm created by the myriad spells pushed Kim Jonghyun's body far back. *A defensive barrier.* Abel confirmed it and leaped into the air. He formed a hand seal and chanted a cast, his hands spreading wide.

"Not using the Grimoire's magic?"

Amidst that sneer, Abel's hands pushed forward. Inside the defensive barrier, Kim Jonghyun opened his mouth to say something. *Casting? Too late.* Abel's magic was faster.

He cast two spells with the Dual Polarity Divine Art. Each of those two spells was its own multi-cast. In other words, four spells were actually cast.

Kim Jonghyun's defensive barrier shattered. What pierced through the barrier was a very thin stream of water.

"Freeze," Abel declared.

The stream of water pierced Kim Jonghyun's body and expanded into a large ice awl.

PWAK!

Kim Jonghyun's body was split in two.

Abel looked down at Kim Jonghyun's falling body. The messily torn severed parts were frozen, not even bleeding.

THUD.

The two separated halves of his body fell among the zombies.

*The end?*

Kim Jonghyun had not used the Grimoire's magic. Was it arrogance? Did he think he did not need to? He had not cast an escape spell either. Kim Jonghyun had, without a doubt, been hit by the magic.

"...Hahaha."

And yet, Kim Jonghyun laughed.

"This is… really. I never thought it would turn out like this," he muttered in a laughing voice. *No way.* Abel's expression hardened as he looked down at Kim Jonghyun. He was thinking the exact same thing. Abel also never thought it would turn out like this.

*He's not human.*

That obvious fact was realized once again.

Kim Jonghyun pushed himself up. The two halves of his body lurched toward each other, the frozen wounds grinding together as they fused.

*Quasi-Demon King.*

Brushing off the ice fragments clinging to his body, Kim Jonghyun raised the hand holding the Grimoire.

Even a half-baked Demon Lord is still a Demon Lord.

And, a Demon Lord, a transcendent being, is immortal.
