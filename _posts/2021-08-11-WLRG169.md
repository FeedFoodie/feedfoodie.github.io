Lee Sungmin was no stranger to brothels.

Mercenaries and prostitutes often went hand in hand, and in his past life, when he had been nothing more than a low-rank mercenary, he had visited the red-light district nearly every day. He had even grown entangled with a prostitute at one point, and if he counted the money he had squandered there, it would have been enough to buy himself a decent martial arts manual.

That had been his former life. In this one, he had never set foot in a red-light district, nor had he ever gotten involved with a prostitute.

The brothel district in Urmuri carried an atmosphere unlike any he had known, but he felt no desire to go there. What baffled him more was why a Grand Sorcerer as brilliant as Frau would choose to live in such a place. Even so, despite his reluctance, his steps carried him toward the district that existed in his memory.

"By the way, wouldn't the brothels all be closed at this hour?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered quietly. Morning had barely begun, the stalls on the streets were still shuttered, and brothels were strictly businesses of the night. Of course they would be closed.

Hearing this, the Void Monarch laughed in Lee Sungmin's head.

\[You have no idea. Urmuri's pleasure houses never sleep. It's been four hundred years, but I doubt that district has changed. Go see for yourself.]

He was right.

From the very entrance, Urmuri's brothel street was overwhelming.

The sun was already up, but the sky above that street was shrouded in darkness. Lanterns of every color, ornate and dazzling, filled that artificial night with light. Below the endless rows of lanterns, pleasure houses stood wide open. Women of breathtaking beauty loosened their collars and beckoned to passersby, while drunks, Yokai, and strange folk unseen in the marketplace stumbled between the houses.

"Hiiik…"

Rubia shrank back and hid behind Lee Sungmin. A woman with the lower body of a snake flicked her tongue, gesturing for them to come.

Lee Sungmin's gaze swept over the lined-up houses. Among the courtesans were humans, mutants, and Yokai alike.

"Sh-she's staring right at us."

The snake woman's tongue darted again. Clearly, she had taken a liking to him. Her lips moved as though speaking, but Lee Sungmin ignored her deliberately.

\[They probably see you as half-Yokai,] the Void Monarch whispered.

Was that good or bad? Unsure how to feel, Lee Sungmin pressed forward toward the house in his memory.

It wasn't only the snake woman watching him. More Yokai sent lingering stares, and the impatient ones even rushed forward to try grabbing his wrist.

*Why are they acting like this?*

\[Because your face is handsome?]

*No, this is way beyond that…!*

\[That's the influence of this Elder's Demonic Energy. It isn't just how much you can handle yourself. You're steeped in the Demonic Energy of a high-class Yokai.]

The Void Monarch laughed loudly.

\[Lesser Yokai are drawn to the energy of a greater one. Those little wenches may look decent, but they're nothing more than hatchlings who haven't shed their skins. To them, you're irresistible with this Elder's Demonic Energy on you.]

*This is so damn annoying…!*

\[Hey. Just curious… are you impotent?]

Lee Sungmin stopped in his tracks, his irritation spiking. The Monarch's tone was serious, but the question was anything but.

"What kind of bullshit is that…"

The words slipped out before he could stop them. The Void Monarch howled with laughter.

\[I only ask because it's strange. Your body's vigorous, and it's been a long time since I settled in your mind, but I've never once seen you give in to desire.]

*Would I even want to, with you watching everything?*

\[Sexual desire isn't something you can suppress forever! Your self-control is impressive. Don't mind this Elder. I don't care who you end up with. If you want, I'll even pretend not to notice if you take care of it with your hand.]

*Shut the hell up…!*

\[Or better yet, since you're here, why not give it a try? Do you know why half-Yokai exist? Because humans who fall for a Yokai's body can't resist themselves!]

Lee Sungmin shut his ears to the vulgar laughter echoing in his mind.

He continued down the bustling street until his steps came to a halt. If the information he had been given was correct, Frau, Aladur's Master, was here.

The house before him looked no different from the others. An eastern-style building, plain compared to the gaudy neighbors.

\[Uh… um.]

There was unease in the Void Monarch's voice.

At the entrance stood a slender, pretty figure. Young in appearance, though if they were Yokai, they were likely older than Lee Sungmin.

"Are you a customer?"

The figure approached with a smile. Before Lee Sungmin could answer, the Void Monarch cut in.

\[The lantern's blue.]

*So what?*

\[This is a male brothel.]

Cold sweat trickled down Lee Sungmin's back. He stared again at the approaching figure, who, despite appearances, was a boy.

\[Unbelievable. What kind of bastard sorcerer lives in a place like this?]

"...I'm not a customer. I'm here to see Frau."

Lee Sungmin brushed aside the boy's hand as it tried to grope him.

The boy's eyes widened.

"You're here for my sister?"

"...Sister?"

"Yeah, my sister. When did she go and seduce another man without us noticing?"

He grumbled, eyeing Lee Sungmin, then the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia behind him. Clicking his tongue, he muttered, "A young guy, a beefcake, and even a boy. She's greedy, bringing all her favorites."

"...Boy? Did you just call me a boy?!"

Rubia's ears twitched, her face burning with fury. Before she could protest further, the boy turned away.

"Follow me."

"How do I look like a boy in any way…!"

Rubia stomped the ground, groaning in frustration, but they followed the boy inside.

The first thing they saw was boys bustling about. Most were so pretty they could be mistaken for girls, and some had shed the last traces of boyhood, though not enough to look like men. With light makeup, long hair, and skirts, they were difficult to distinguish unless one looked below the waist.

\[What filthy taste…!] the Void Monarch spat, clicking his tongue.

The pretty male prostitutes eyed the newcomers boldly until the boy announced, "They're my sister's guests!" Then they turned away as if nothing had happened.

The boy led them up the stairs. Embarrassing sounds echoed from behind closed doors. Rubia pressed her hands over her ears, cheeks burning, while the Crazy Heavenly Demon cleared his throat and acted as though he hadn't heard a thing.

"My sister's in here," the boy said when they reached the third floor.

At the hallway's end stood an ornate door. The boy pulled out makeup from his clothes, dusted his cheeks with powder, and painted his lips with rouge as if it were routine.

"Sister\~"

Tap, tap. He knocked in a singsong voice.

The door swung open moments later. The room inside was a mess, its enormous bed strewn with tangled blankets and pillows, while liquor bottles, underwear, and leftover plates littered the floor.

"Aigoo, Sister. You should've told me it was getting this bad!"

"...What is it?"

A sleepy voice answered. From the bed, a naked woman sat up, brushing back messy, pale purple hair.

Her gaze moved from the boy to Lee Sungmin.

"...Well now, you're an unusual one," Frau muttered, rising slowly.

Her light-brown skin and bare body drew involuntary glances away. Smiling at their reactions, Frau waved a hand, and a robe lifted from the floor to wrap itself around her.

"Are you human?" she asked, her sharp eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin. "You look human, but you don't feel like one. Half-stained with Demonic Energy… no, not exactly. What are you? Your causality's twisted, your vessel as a human cracked and leaking."

"...What?"

"You don't even know? To carry a Demonic Eye and not realize your own state…" Frau laughed, and Lee Sungmin's hand instinctively rose to his left eye.

"I sense all kinds of things in you. A great Yokai's spirit possessing you, tangled with some bizarre force of Destiny."

"What are you talking about?"

"The Destiny Force." Frau chuckled, crossing her legs and reaching for a smoking pipe.

"More importantly, who are you all? You don't look like customers. You came here for me, didn't you?"

"...Aladur recommended I come."

"What? That bastard?!"

Her eyes snapped open, and the room's temperature dropped at once. Pale Evil Spirits flared to life, circling around her.

"You came on that bastard's word?!" she roared.

"...Yes, but—"

Her fury was far beyond what Lee Sungmin had expected, even after Aladur's warning. The pressure she radiated was unlike any he had faced. His instincts told him not to fight her. Not that he would lose, but that he simply must not.

\[Ghost Soul Art,] the Void Monarch said with admiration. \[Incredible skill. You might win if you fought, but don't even try. Ghost Soul Art is dangerous. One mistake and you'll be cursed for life.]

Admiration and warning in one. Frau demanded, "Where is Aladur?!"

"...We parted a week ago."

"Where? That ungrateful disciple runs off without a word, then sends me guests?!"

Her screams rattled the room, but the boy beside Lee Sungmin tugged at his wrist.

"Leave her. She does this sometimes. She'll calm down in thirty minutes. Don't get close, or you'll get hurt."

"Son of a bitch!" Frau's shrieks filled the air, but slowly her rage ebbed. The Evil Spirits dispersed, and warmth returned to the room.

"...Hmph."

She clutched her throat, hoarse from shouting, and collapsed onto the bed.

"No use yelling. The one who leaves should come back on their own. Why should I chase after him?" she grumbled, reaching for her pipe.

"So, you came with Aladur's name? Fine. Do you have a request, or are you here to ask about the Destiny Force?"

"...If I ask, will you answer?"

"I can tell you about the Destiny Force. That's easy. As for favors, I'll decide after hearing them."

Drawing in smoke and exhaling it through her nose, Frau smiled faintly.

"My personality's shit. Unless it amuses me, I'm not interested."

"What is the Destiny Force?"

"It's exactly what it sounds like. A force that governs fate."

Her reply came without hesitation.

"Every being is bound within the great current of fate. Fate is vague, and it can shift, but the current is vast. Most of it can't be changed. Whether you walk, crawl, or run to the bathroom—that's where small changes exist."

Her words puzzled him.

"You go because you need to shit. That need is absolute. Going to the bathroom for it is also inevitable. That's fate. How you get there—that's what can change."

She pointed her pipe at Lee Sungmin.

"The Destiny Force compels that current to flow exactly as set. It does so unconsciously. When you need to shit, when you go, which bathroom, how you walk there. All of it."

She burst out laughing.

"Humans with Destiny Force are rare. Those who have it are tied to something inevitable, but you... Hah! You're astounding. A human with a force as strong as yours."

"...What do you mean?"

"Your life. Your ties. Every action. All of it has been fate."

Perhaps even now. Frau pressed a hand to her chest.

"Meeting Aladur by chance, hearing about me, coming here. It feels like coincidence, but maybe it isn't coincidence at all."
