He felt the nameless Black Mage leaving, and the bastard wasn't alone. He had taken Aine with him.

Crouched on the ground, Lee Sungmin let out a ragged breath as their presence faded into the distance. A wave of relief washed over him that the Black Mage had retreated peacefully, though he couldn't help but wonder why the man hadn't attacked.

He had never imagined such agony after consuming the Dragon Heart. Until now, Lee Sungmin had ingested various miracle herbs, and each time the Black Heart had allowed him to absorb their power without any major issues. This was completely different. A violent pain tore through him, a searing heat that threatened to burst him from the inside out, leaving him unable to move properly.

Envirus's warning echoed in his mind, the premonition that he would explode and die.

However, he did not explode. He did not die.

As time passed, the torment subsided, and strength slowly seeped back into his immobilized limbs. When the pain had finally faded to a dull ache, Lee Sungmin pushed himself to his feet. He had trusted the Black Heart, and just as he had expected, it had successfully absorbed the Dragon Heart.

*No.*

Standing upright, he looked down at his body with a bewildered expression. He felt no significant change, even though he was certain the absorption was complete. He remembered the immense power that had flooded him the moment it went down his throat.

However, now? There was nothing. Lee Sungmin circulated his inner qi, but he couldn't sense even a trace of the Dragon Heart's magic.

What on earth had gone wrong? As he stood there in confusion, the Void Monarch spoke to him.

[It seems the power was too great for a human to handle.] The voice lacked its usual confidence, betraying the Void Monarch's own uncertainty.

*But I did absorb it?*

[I don't know either. I've never eaten a Dragon's Heart... but isn't this different from usual? There's probably something else going on that you're not aware of.]

*Could the Spirit Queen have tampered with it?*

[Perhaps, but it is an undeniable fact that you ate the Dragon's Heart.]

What good was eating it if it had no immediate effect? Lee Sungmin circulated his inner qi once more, just in case, but still felt nothing. He recalled Aine, who had eaten a heart and assimilated its knowledge, martial arts included. He focused his mind, searching for a similar influx of a Dragon's magic, but not a single spark came to him.

"What the hell is this?"

No one was around to answer his question. Neither Envirus nor the Fairy Queen was anywhere nearby.

Deciding to worry about the Dragon Heart later, Lee Sungmin knew he had to leave the plaza. The plot in Urmuri, orchestrated by Arbes, had failed. He didn't know what they had been planning, but there was no longer any reason for him to stay.

He started walking, intending to return to the Fairy Realm. He needed to find Rubia first and hoped to hear more details about Envirus from her.

*Though I doubt she knows much more.*

With that thought, he headed toward the Fairy Realm, assuming the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia would have returned there by now. A pang of guilt struck him for leaving without a word, and his heart grew heavy at the thought that they might be worried. It was a little funny, he mused, that he was even having such concerns. Come to think of it, this was the first time he had ever traveled with others like this.

Even in his past life, when he had risen from a Classless to a C-Rank Mercenary, his comrades had been business associates, not companions, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia had been with him for over a year now. Their presence had done much to soothe his unstable mind, a lingering consequence of his training in the Mental World.

*What should I say to Lady Rubia?*

She had been the one most looking forward to meeting Envirus, yet Envirus had vanished right in front of him. How was he supposed to explain that?

Mulling over these worries, Lee Sungmin arrived at the entrance to the Fairy Realm.

"You're here?"

He ran into Frau, whose image was completely different from when he had last seen her at the brothel. Lee Sungmin discovered for the first time that she was someone who knew how to wear 'clothes'. Although a large portion of her skin was still exposed, she wore an outfit that covered what needed to be covered, though it was adorned with what seemed like an excessive amount of various accessories.

He tilted his head. "...Why are you here?"

"I was waiting for you," Frau replied with an indifferent expression. She looked him up and down, her own head tilting in curiosity. "It's only been a few days, and you've become an even stranger being. Are you standing completely on the boundary...? No, more than that... this is quite strange. Your body is almost a Yokai, but your mind is human?"

She muttered to herself and chuckled. "And there's something else strange tangled up in there. Goodness, I have no idea what kind of creature you are."

"What's that supposed to mean?"

"We'll talk about that later." Frau turned around. "Follow me. There's something you need to see."

Though he hadn't expected to meet her here, Lee Sungmin followed her into the Fairy Realm.

"...What is this?"

In the middle of a spacious room lay a charred corpse. No, it wasn't quite a corpse yet. A faint, shallow breath still lingered.

*No way.*

The blood drained from Lee Sungmin's face. *It can't be.* With that thought pounding in his head, he rushed toward the man lying on the floor.

"...Elder?"

"Don't get any closer," Frau said, her sharp words stopping him in his tracks. He looked back at her, and she shook her head with that same indifferent expression. "On top of burning away his life force, he took the full force of Chuseong's flames. He's still breathing, but he'll be dead soon."

"...Chuseong?"

"You didn't know? While you were in the Central Plaza, Chuseong attacked Urmuri, and then... this guy charged at him."

"...What happened?"

"Chuseong is dead," Frau answered flatly. "The artificial Elemental with cat ears who was with him was reverse-summoned to the Elemental Realm. Something must have happened to her master."

Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip so hard he tasted blood. His thoughts were a chaotic storm. Rubia's master was Envirus. Envirus had gone to the Elemental Realm with the Spirit Queen... was that why Rubia had been pulled back there as well? And the Crazy Heavenly Demon? Burning his life force... did that mean he had used up all his life qi? More importantly, why had Chuseong, who was supposed to have left Urmuri, suddenly come back?

"...Can't... he be saved?"

"No," Frau answered without a moment's hesitation. "I've taken some measures, but it's barely enough to keep him alive. His life force is already gone. Even if you shove an Elixir down his throat, his burnt body might recover, but his burnt-out life force won't return."

"Somehow."

"There's no way to save someone in his condition, no matter what famous doctor you bring from anywhere in the world. It's only because I'm a sorcerer of my caliber that he's barely clinging to life," she grumbled.

Lee Sungmin opened his mouth to say something, but no words came out. As a martial artist, he knew exactly what it meant to use up one's life qi. He looked down at the Crazy Heavenly Demon with empty eyes, the man's words echoing in his mind. *Don't interfere. This is my own problem.* He hadn't even needed to hear them. While the Crazy Heavenly Demon was exacting his revenge, Lee Sungmin had been in the Central Plaza.

"...Please," he blurted out, the word escaping him before he even realized it.

His connection with the Crazy Heavenly Demon wasn't that long, just over a year. Their first meeting had been far from pleasant, but after that? The man had genuinely looked out for him, helping him several times when he was in crisis. Was it all just a reward for suppressing his lunacy?

*No, it's different.* Lee Sungmin shook his head. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had stepped forward for him against the Blood Heavenly Demon and had helped him fight the Dark Supreme. That was not something done in expectation of a reward.

"I told you, there's no way," Frau said with a deep sigh.

However, Lee Sungmin looked at her with desperate eyes. She was the one keeping the Crazy Heavenly Demon tethered to this world, a man who should already be dead. She was the only hope he could cling to.

Frau stared back at him, and after a brief silence, she finally spoke. "...Well. It's not certain." It wasn't that there was no way at all, just that calling it a 'way' was a stretch. Normally, she wouldn't have even mentioned it. She clicked her tongue. Since he had taken care of that troublesome business in the Central Plaza, she supposed she owed him something.

"To be honest, you can't really call this a solution. It's not certain, and I don't even know if it's possible."

"What is it?"

"The Demonic Spirit," Frau answered, her face etched with reluctance. "I don't know much about what the Demonic Spirit is, but... of this I'm certain. The Demonic Spirit is a fickle being, and its whims can overturn the laws of nature." Her brow furrowed with a complicated expression. "The reason Yana was able to become the Nine-Tailed Fox without needing thousands of years was also thanks to the Demonic Spirit's help. Of course, her strength wasn't entirely due to it. The timing is right, too. Yana is on her way to meet the Demonic Spirit right now... If you leave immediately, you might be able to make it in time."

"Where... is this Demonic Spirit?"

"It's quite far from here." Frau glanced at the dying man as she spoke. "However, I can't make any guarantees. I'm holding onto his soul with my sorcery, but... whether he can endure is entirely up to him. Also, even if you go to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion and meet the Demonic Spirit, it's not certain that fickle being will actually save him."

"...Is that the only way?"

"Yes. At least, I can't think of any other method."

"...Then let's leave right now." Lee Sungmin refused to let the Crazy Heavenly Demon die. Even if the possibility was small, he had to cling to it.

Frau glanced at his determined face and continued. "The Demonic Spirit Pavilion, where the Demonic Spirit resides, is in the Hyujael Mountains. It's a journey of three or four days from here at a generous pace, and it's not like you can meet the Demonic Spirit the moment you get there."

"Then?"

"To meet the Demonic Spirit, Yana must fast and pray for ten days and nights. Do you understand what that means? No matter how fast you are, you'll have to wait at least ten days, and even then, there's no guarantee it will grant your request, and there's no telling if he can last that long."

"But this is the only way, isn't it?"

"...Well... that's true," Frau replied reluctantly.

That was enough. Lee Sungmin bit his lip. Rubia was gone, a companion he had traveled with for over a year, vanished. The loss shook him more than he had expected. He couldn't turn away from this, no matter how slim the chance.

He stood up. "...We'll need a carriage."

He couldn't just throw the Crazy Heavenly Demon over his shoulder and run. The man needed absolute rest. A carriage would slow them down, but there was no other choice.

Lee Sungmin procured a carriage from the manager of the Fairy Realm. Frau climbed into the back with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, while Lee Sungmin took the driver's seat himself.

"I'll give you the directions."

*I've gotten myself mixed up in something troublesome,* Frau grumbled, but Lee Sungmin didn't hear her.
