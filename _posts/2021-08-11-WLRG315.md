It was done.

Abel was certain of it, feeling the vast majority of his remaining lifespan drain away. The magic had not failed. He had never used this magic before, but he could still recognize success. The coordinates had been entered successfully, and the spell was undeniably active. Though the magic originated from the Grimoire, he had altered it, inputting different coordinates and using an activation formula from Gries.

All the preparations Kim Jonghyun had made in Germud, the slaughter of every person in the city, had now become Abel's to command.

Within the exploding light, Frau saw something Abel could not. Tens of thousands of souls floundered in the brilliance, sliding in long streaks as they swam through the space within the light. With each extinguished soul, a small hole tore open in the fabric of space, causing it to distort.

Ah.

Frau understood then why Kim Jonghyun had slaughtered tens of thousands in the city, pooling their souls here instead of sending them to the Demon Lord.

*They were necessary.*

Souls were the currency needed to connect dimensions. To link the Great Demon World and Eria, which were not originally connected, would require several times this number. The Elemental Realm, however, was different. Eria and the Elemental Realm already shared a connection, however faint. Therefore, the souls of Germud alone were enough.

{sep}

RUMBLE!

A deep vibration shook the very air, and Kim Jonghyun looked back, his face a mask of unconcealable surprise. He had faced many surprises today, but none compared to this.

"I see," Kim Jonghyun murmured, a slow understanding dawning on him.

Connecting to the Great Demon World was supposed to be the only way to avoid The End, a method only he could employ, but what if he wasn't the only one? What if there was another way? So this was their plan.

"And here I was," Kim Jonghyun chuckled, turning his gaze to Lee Sungmin, "thinking you'd started all this recklessly, without any countermeasures at all."

Some distance away, Lee Sungmin was slumped on the ground, covered in blood. He gasped for breath, glaring at Kim Jonghyun, unable to comprehend how the tables had turned so drastically. At the start of their battle, he had been overwhelming, his speed, honed by his fight with Vollander, too much for Kim Jonghyun to handle.

However, now? The mage dodged and blocked the spear Lee Sungmin threw with all his might. To be fair, Lee Sungmin's condition was far from ideal. The battle with Vollander had drained him mentally and physically. Even so, the sight of Kim Jonghyun, a 'mage', dominating him with sheer physical ability was unbelievable.

"So there was a method like this," Kim Jonghyun said, approaching Lee Sungmin. "Abel... I never expected his Gries magic could make something like this possible. As a result, I've failed."

He sounded almost cheerful. The power of a complete, if temporary, Demon Lord was intoxicating, enough to sweep even him away. His new body had continued to grow even as they fought, a power that would vanish when the magic ended, but for now, it was glorious.

"It can't be helped."

[Are you okay?] the Void Monarch asked, his voice laced with worry.

*I'm fine,* Lee Sungmin replied in a small voice as he pushed himself up.

The impact had ruptured organs and broken bones, but they regenerated in an instant. With each wave of healing, his head throbbed and his heart pounded, a grim reminder that this very act was threatening his already incomplete Sealing.

"I've failed 'here'," Kim Jonghyun clarified. "Well, that's fine. I can always do it again. I know the method now. Losing Vollander and the Death Knight Legion is a shame, of course. It would be nice if you could fill the void they left, but..."

"You've failed," Lee Sungmin repeated, staggering to his feet.

"And through another method, this world has escaped the fate of The End. Are you saying you'll still try to cause something like this again?"

"Why are you acting like this? You know I didn't do all this purely to stop The End."

And.

"......"

Kim Jonghyun stroked his chin.

"How strange. I don't know which world it connected to, but... there's almost no change. It seems the magic was definitely cast, though?"

That was a question Lee Sungmin was feeling as well. From Kim Jonghyun's reaction, it was clear that whatever Abel had attempted had succeeded.

Having gotten to his feet, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear. Seeing this, Kim Jonghyun raised both his hands.

"No, wait. I have no desire to fight you again right now."

He took a few steps back as he spoke.

"First, I want to confirm exactly what has happened," Kim Jonghyun said with a grin.

FWIP!

Leaving that grin hanging in the air, Kim Jonghyun's figure vanished. He tore through space with a series of successive Blinks.

Lee Sungmin's expression turned to ice. He didn't hesitate, launching himself in the direction Abel had gone. He knew that the biggest obstacle to Kim Jonghyun's plans was no longer him, but Abel, the wielder of Gries. Kim Jonghyun would undoubtedly try to eliminate him first.

{sep}

"...What is this?"

Abel stood there, utterly blank. The sphere of light had vanished, its purpose served. The magic had been cast, an undeniable fact, but this... this was not what he expected.

He had entered the coordinates for the Elemental Realm with absolute certainty, a price paid in his own lifespan to Gries. There could be no error. Eria was connected to the Elemental Realm, yet the scene before him was completely alien. A withered garden mingled with a splendid flowerbed, and beyond them stood a magnificent mansion.

A mansion?

Just as surprised as Abel, Frau stared at the structure with a bewildered expression. Hiding behind him, Aladur peeked out.

"Where are the Elementals?"

"How should I know, you brat," Frau snapped.

Abel ground his teeth. Ignoring the twitching muscle in his cheek, he strode to the mansion's door and raised a hand to the knob, but before he could turn it, the knob twisted from the other side.

The door swung open.

"...Huh..."

Abel's eyes widened in disbelief at the person who had opened the door. The man wore thin glasses and sat in a wheelchair, his body frail. White hair framed a face full of wrinkles.

"B-brother, why are you...?" Abel stammered.

Sitting in the wheelchair, beyond the door, was Envirus. His older brother, Cain.

Cain wore an expression of disbelief as well, but Abel's shock was far greater. The immense magic power Cain once wielded was almost gone. He was barely suppressing his own aging, reduced to the appearance of an old man. It seemed this was his limit. Without it, hundreds of years would have already crushed his body to dust, and his legs... Abel had never imagined meeting Cain here, but he was more horrified by the miserable state his brother, the man he had once aimed to surpass, had fallen into.

"Abel...?" Cain muttered, his blurry eyes widening behind his glasses.

He couldn't see well. Only after hearing the voice did he barely recognize the person before him.

"Master!" a startled voice cried from behind Cain. A beastfolk with cat ears came running down the hall, freezing when she saw Abel. *Why is a human here?* The question flashed through her mind, but she quickly moved to Cain's side, gripping his wheelchair.

"Why did you open the door...?"

"I heard a strange noise outside..." Cain stammered.

He couldn't even support his own body. His eyes couldn't see properly, his aging was barely held in check. *What... is that?* Abel's face twisted in disgust.

However, that wasn't important now. He strode forward and seized Cain's wrist.

"Where is this place?"

"Huh...?"

"I'm asking you, where in the world is this place!" Abel shouted.

Rubia moved to block his path, her expression hardening. "Who are you people? Why... no, how did you get here?"

"Shut your mouth! I'm asking the questions! Where... where the hell is this place? Isn't this the Elemental Realm? I definitely entered the coordinates for the Elemental Realm..."

"It is the Elemental Realm," Cain answered.

"...But there are no... Elementals."

"Explain," Abel demanded. "Please, explain."

Something was terribly wrong. He didn't want to admit it, but Abel felt a powerful, sickening premonition. There were no Elementals. The Elemental Realm was supposed to be a world ruled by the Spirit Queen, teeming with them. So why were none to be seen?

"The coordinates..." Cain began. "The coordinates for the Elemental Realm. Abel, I don't know what you were trying to do, but they've been changed. The Spirit Queen... for my sake, because I could not live in the Elemental Realm... she created a small space and overlaid it on the existing coordinates."

This mansion was that space. As a price for breaking his contract with Mana, Cain had been on the verge of death. The Spirit Queen, unable to bear the thought of his demise, had ignored the old promise, performed an Advent into the mortal world, and taken him with her.

"Don't give me that bullshit."

Abel's body trembled. *So what was all this for?* He had stolen Kim Jonghyun's magic, sacrificed most of his own lifespan, and extinguished tens of thousands of souls, all to connect Eria to the Elemental Realm, and he had connected it not to the realm itself, but to an independent pocket dimension the Spirit Queen had made for Cain.

Abel frantically gripped Gries. He knew he had little life left to give, but he didn't care. He cast a spell and then collapsed where he stood.

*Nothing had changed.*

Eria's fate remained unaltered. The End was still coming.

"The Queen..." Abel stammered, looking up. "The Queen... the Spirit Queen? Her, call her. Right now!"

"That's... impossible," Cain muttered, squeezing his weak eyes shut. "She broke the old promise to save me. As a price for that, she has fallen into a long slumber."

The Spirit Queen had wanted to save him, even if it meant going that far, even if it meant she could never see him again.

"Ha... haha..."

A hollow laugh escaped Abel's lips. It was absurd. Unbelievable. What kind of fucked-up situation was this? He had sacrificed his life to stop The End, to save Eria.

"You son of a bitch..." Abel spat, glaring at Cain with eyes full of pure resentment. He had to blame someone. Everything was ruined because of Cain. If Cain hadn't been here, the Spirit Queen would never have broken her promise, never created this pocket dimension. If that had been the case, Abel's plan would have succeeded. Eria would have been saved!

"Hahahaha!"

Laughter erupted from behind him. Kim Jonghyun floated in the air, clapping his hands, his expression one of pure delight, and why wouldn't he be? Abel had failed. Kim Jonghyun had failed too, but unlike Abel, he could simply start over.

"This is a true masterpiece!" Kim Jonghyun crowed. "To think the events in Urmuri would lead to such a result. Hahahaha!"

Below him, Lee Sungmin stood, having heard everything. He understood now. His gaze fell on Rubia, who stood protectively before Cain, and she looked back, her face a mask of shocked disbelief. Lee Sungmin forced a smile, though it felt unnatural and tight.

"So in the end," Kim Jonghyun sneered, pointing a finger at Cain, "you claimed you wanted to stop The End, but you were just living well in the safe Elemental Realm all along."

He then jeered at Abel.

"And you failed."

Finally, his gaze swept over them all.

"While I just have to do it again."

How could anything be this amusing? Kim Jonghyun laughed loudly once more.
