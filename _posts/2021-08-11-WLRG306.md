Abel laid out his intentions with an air of firm confidence.

"Kim Jonghyun's method isn't wrong," he began, addressing Lee Sungmin and Frau. "The problem is his target. The Great Demon World."

Connecting to that world meant that even if they escaped the fate of The End, this world would suffer a blow equivalent to destruction at the hands of the Demon Race. Unlike the clean annihilation The End promised, they would be subjugated by the Demon Realm and suffer for eternity. In that case, The End might even be preferable.

"We just need to connect to a different world."

He would not have suggested it if the task were impossible.

"Gries is the Grimoire's twin magic tome. It can't do exactly what the Grimoire can, but it can interfere with what the Grimoire is trying to do. Two days… did he say two days?"

"Yes. In two days," Lee Sungmin confirmed. "He said he would first connect Germud to the Great Demon World and then leave for another city."

At those words, Abel was lost in thought for a moment. Connecting dimensions was not as simple as opening a single door. It was a merging of two worlds, an act for which Germud alone was insufficient.

"Which world will you connect it to?" Frau asked. "I'm not sure if connecting to another dimension is the answer to avoiding The End. If what Kim Jonghyun said is true, he needs more sacrifices to completely connect this world with the Great Demon World. Even if the world we connect to changes, the need for more sacrifices won't, will it?"

"We can use a world that's already connected," Abel replied, opening Gries. He narrowed his eyes, studying its magic. "The Elemental Realm would be good."

"The Elemental Realm?"

"The Elemental Realm is already connected to this world, though the two aren't merged into one. Elementals can come and go when called, but it's impossible for beings from this world to travel there. If we expand that passage… yes. If we change the coordinates of the dimensional gate Kim Jonghyun is trying to open to the Elemental Realm…"

Abel muttered ceaselessly, analyzing the theory in his head.

*It's not impossible.* Using Gries, he could definitely interfere with the Grimoire's magic.

However, whether this plan would succeed was another matter entirely. Would connecting to the Elemental Realm truly erase the fate of The End that Eria carried? Could they possibly break through an army of tens of thousands of Undead and Death Knights to defeat a Quasi-Demon King and seize his magic?

*No.*

Abel chewed on his lower lip. Such worries were pointless right now. If he stood by and did nothing simply because he was uncertain, Kim Jonghyun's plan would succeed. Regardless of their own chances, the connection to the Great Demon World simply must not be allowed to happen.

"I need to talk to the Church," Abel declared, getting to his feet. "He said two days, right? We must march out tomorrow at the latest to suppress Germud and either subdue Kim Jonghyun… or kill him. Marching out in two days is too late."

Lee Sungmin nodded and rose as well, but Frau remained seated, his expression sullen. Even he understood the gravity of the situation.

[Aren't you going to ask Yana for help?] Heoju's voice echoed in his mind.

*I can't trust her,* Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation. *The Demonic Spirit is the one who gave Yana her power. As long as the Spirit's actions are suspicious, I can't trust the Demonic Spirit either. In the first place, I don't know what either of them specifically wants.*

[That statement. You're well aware it doesn't just apply to Yana, right?]

Lee Sungmin clenched his fist. He was not so foolish as to be unaware of the implication. The overwhelming power Yana had used to rip out the Red Ghost's heart came from the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, and she was not the only one who had been there.

Wiji Hoyeon.

She too had met the Demonic Spirit at the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. He had no idea what had transpired between them. Once this matter was successfully concluded, Lee Sungmin planned to visit the not-so-distant Hugel Mountains to meet the Demonic Spirit himself.

[If you suspect Yana because you suspect the Demonic Spirit, then that means you also suspect that wench.]

*…That's right.*

He hoped not. That's what he thought. He hoped with all his being that what the Demonic Spirit and the Spirit wanted were two different things.

When Abel dispelled the barrier, he looked visibly tired. He had already lived for hundreds of years and might live for hundreds more, but the magic of Gries, which used his own lifespan as collateral, was a heavy burden.

"There's no time," he said, refusing to rest. Kim Jonghyun had mentioned two days, but that could not be unconditionally trusted. Perhaps the magic was already complete. The two days he mentioned might just be a form of hopeful torture to deceive those who had gathered for the subjugation.

The Church's gathering was a place of both quiet and loud prayer. It was a contradictory scene, but it was true. Priests and Holy Knights, including Teresa, knelt in silent devotion to their respective gods, while the crowd around them cried out to their own gods for safety.

"I have a question," Abel said as they approached the clamorous assembly. "Why did you refuse Kim Jonghyun's offer?"

"Is there a need to answer?"

"Kim Jonghyun would have guaranteed your life, at least." The favor Kim Jonghyun showed Lee Sungmin was genuine. "Even if it's a crazy plan, it's an undeniable fact that his method allows one to avoid The End. If the Great Demon World and this world were connected, Kim Jonghyun, a Quasi-Demon King, would have obtained the full status of a Demon Lord. He doesn't have his own faction, but by offering the world of Eria as a colony, he would surely be favored by the Demon God. If that happened, other lords and great Demon Races wouldn't dare to touch him."

"What are you trying to say."

"Even if this world becomes a plaything for the Demon Race. If you had joined hands with Kim Jonghyun, you…"

"Stop." Lee Sungmin's voice was sharp. "What I wanted was not my own safety."

"You mean you genuinely want to save this world?" Abel asked with a dry laugh. "Not because the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman died for it, and you feel responsible?" He paused. "Or is it responsibility for bringing The End to this world by becoming an Observer?"

"Is it wrong for that to be the reason?"

"If that reason makes you desperate, it doesn't really matter. As long as it's a reason desperate enough to make you give up your own life, it's fine."

Desperation.

It was not a feeling he was unfamiliar with. He had felt it keenly when Baek Sogo faced Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger in the Dungeon, when he fought the Dark Supreme to protect a powerless Wiji Hoyeon, and when he participated in the first subjugation of Kim Jonghyun to avert Scarlet's crisis. He had felt it when he tried to break the Spirit's barrier to help the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Besides those times, Lee Sungmin had felt desperation many times.

"I am desperate."

At that, Abel's body floated into the air, soaring over the crowd gathered for the assembly. He cleverly avoided the topic of The End, which he could not discuss, and instead laid bare Kim Jonghyun's scheme. Paying no mind to their gazes, he worked to incite them, emphatically explaining what a connection to the Great Demon World truly meant.

They don't distinguish between men and women, old and young. They only distinguish between the Demon Race and those who are not. Countless people will die, and countless people will be toyed with.

He painted a horrific picture of what the Demon Race would do to them, their friends, their families, spewing threats and insults more terrible than any they had ever known.

When the passionate speech was over, the crowd was a seething mass of murderous intent and hostility toward Kim Jonghyun. The Church's forces, who had been silent during his speech, began to talk amongst themselves.

And so, the time to march was decided. At noon tomorrow, they would break down the gates of Germud and enter.

"Kim Jonghyun will be watching," Abel noted with a slight nod. This would be no surprise attack.

"The Demon Realm…" Teresa's shoulders trembled slightly. She tightly gripped the rosary around her neck.

The thousands gathered at the gates of Germud had been nothing more than an uncoordinated mob, but now, they had become a group with a single purpose. How many of them would be useful? Who had any value? Abel floated high in the air, looking down at the assembly.

*Too few.*

Most were wanderers or natives who lived in the area. It wasn't that there were no mercenaries or martial artists, but they were just barbarians with mediocre skills. The Yokai who came to feed on fear weren't even worth considering. The only ones who were somewhat useful were the Church's Holy Knights and Priests, and how much power could Teresa, the Saint, truly display?

*The important thing is to open a path.*

A path to reach Kim Jonghyun.

{sep}

"So it comes to this after all."

Kim Jonghyun removed his hand from the crystal ball. The crystal ball, having lost its light, no longer showed what was happening outside the gates of Germud. There was no need to watch anymore.

*However. I gave you the choice.*

If Lee Sungmin were to betray them, Kim Jonghyun would have gladly welcomed him. If Lee Sungmin didn't betray them, it didn't matter. He would just have to kill him.

Kim Jonghyun hadn't lied to Lee Sungmin, not about connecting to the Great Demon World, nor about the magic not being complete yet.

"The subjugation force will arrive tomorrow," he said to Vollander.

Vollander didn't answer. He knew what Kim Jonghyun intended to do. He also knew the meaning of connecting this world to the Demon Realm. However, in the end, Vollander's wish would be granted.

What he and the Death Knights wanted was to become human. Kim Jonghyun had sworn to turn them human, and he had no intention of breaking that promise. In the first place, the Death Knights had no choice. They had already massacred tens of thousands of innocent citizens to become human again.

As a result, they could return to being human. After that, this world would be connected to the Demon Realm. It would create a terrible world, but what the Death Knights wanted wasn't to become human and live happily ever after. It was to regain their souls, which were bound to a Demon Lord, become human, and then face a complete death. When their souls are bound to a Demon Lord, they cannot reincarnate even if they die. This is because their souls are subservient to the Demon Lord.

"I trust you will keep your promise."

"Don't worry," Kim Jonghyun said with a faint smile. "I promised to make you human… to give you the chance for reincarnation, didn't I?"

He knew what those who had lived for hundreds of years as Death Knights truly wanted. It was not to become human now and live a human life. It was to end their hundreds of years of life as Undead, die as humans, forget everything, and enter the cycle of reincarnation.

*Still… there's one thing I'm curious about. Lee Sungmin. What did you talk about with the Mages Guild Master in that carriage?*

Kim Jonghyun hadn't heard the conversation between Abel, Lee Sungmin, and Frau. The barrier Abel had cast with Gries excluded all interference from this world. It was clear they were up to something. Were they really marching out tomorrow simply to stop the connection with the Demon Realm, without any other scheme?

He stroked his chin, lost in thought.

*It doesn't matter.*

After sending Vollander out, Kim Jonghyun lay down on his bed, feeling pleased. It was earlier than usual, but he couldn't afford to oversleep.
