No one outside the Black Hound Squad knew that Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin were dead. Their bodies would be abandoned in the forest, left for the Yokai and beasts to devour or simply to rot away into the earth. The Moyong Clan would remain ignorant of their fate, and the Jaegal Clan would have no particular desire to retrieve the corpses. This matter had already been settled between the Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, and the Jaegal Patriarch.

Unaware of these arrangements, Lee Sungmin's party made their way out of the woods. The Evil Spirits, once hostile, now guided their steps. The forest, which had deliberately obstructed his path and forced him to confront the remnants of fear, no longer stood in his way.

"I don't know what to make of him," Aladur muttered, clicking his tongue. He shivered as his gaze fell upon the spectral figures leading them forward. "You know, controlling Evil Spirits is something only a select few high-level sorcerers can manage. Even in Dark Magic, it's unheard of to control existing spirits without first subjugating them. Did you know that?"

"I wouldn't know," Lee Sungmin replied. "I'm not a Black Mage."

These spirits were the souls of those who had perished in the forest, their essences tainted by its Demonic Energy. They guided him not because he had conquered them, but because the Void Monarch, who bound their very being, was spiritually connected to him. This meant Lee Sungmin could only receive their aid within the confines of this particular forest.

*It doesn't matter.*

While their help was convenient here, he couldn't imagine what use controlling Evil Spirits would be outside of it. Lee Sungmin was neither a sorcerer of the unorthodox path nor a mage. He had no need for such abilities and felt no regret that this power was limited to this place.

"Kuh… If those idiots who eat and sleep in graveyards trying to control Evil Spirits saw this, they'd die of jealousy," Aladur chattered on, his thick lips flapping. "He's skilled in the Void Path, has a good personality, and even handles Evil Spirits like they're old friends! How can such a perfect person exist in this world? Ah, it's so unfair."

Hearing this, Rubia giggled. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, for his part, inwardly nodded in agreement. He wasn't so sure about the personality part, but possessing martial arts that placed him on the cusp of the Supreme Peak at the tender age of twenty-five was an incredible talent indeed.

"I'm not going to abandon you, so you can stop the flattery," Lee Sungmin said. "Just be quiet for a bit."

"Flattery? I'm being sincere," Aladur grumbled.

Though he had included Aladur in the party out of necessity, after just one day, his ceaseless chatter was already proving incredibly noisy. Surprisingly, Rubia and the Crazy Heavenly Demon seemed to get along well with him. Since Lee Sungmin wasn't very talkative, it appeared they had missed having conversations.

"You're going to Urmuri? Are you out of your mind?" Aladur shrieked when Lee Sungmin finally answered his question about their destination. "Ah, fuck, I barely managed to switch ships, and this one is heading for death too. Why on earth would you go to Urmuri?"

"No," Lee Sungmin clarified, glancing at Aladur, who was twisting his body as if having a seizure. "I'm not necessarily going to Urmuri. It's just in that direction."

Only then did Aladur's expression show some relief. Rubia, who had been watching meat cook over the fire, looked up at him.

"What's wrong with Urmuri?" she asked. "I know it's called the Monster City, but that doesn't necessarily mean it's a dangerous place, does it?"

Rubia was right. Urmuri was a city where Yokai roamed freely, but it wasn't unconditionally dangerous. It was actually a famous tourist destination in the South, a place where wealthy nobles would spend a great deal of money hiring escorts just to visit.

Yokai, born from fear as their mother, were natural enemies to humans. However, not all of them cast aside reason to hunt humans indiscriminately. That would make them no different from Vampires, who subsisted on human blood. The higher a Yokai's class, the stronger its reason and the better it could control its desire for human flesh. It was the same with people. Just because people ate pork didn't mean they would hunt and devour any pig they saw on the spot.

"Young beastfolk lady, the Urmuri you know is the Urmuri of the past, when it was a good place to live," Aladur said, waving his hand dismissively. "Today's Urmuri is completely different."

He continued, "Ah, of course, to the unsuspecting tourists, Urmuri still seems like a pretty decent city. The night market of the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons is a decent attraction, and the plays and circuses the Yokai put on are fun to watch, but, young lady, think about it carefully. It's a city where so many Yokai live. What on earth do they eat?"

"…Not all Yokai eat only human flesh, right?"

"That's right. Not all of them do, but there are some who must. For instance… yes. Like the Nine-Tailed Fox."

Aladur trembled violently at the name. As he spoke the words, Lee Sungmin lifted his eyes from the fire and looked at him.

"The ruler of Urmuri changed a few years ago," Aladur explained. "The one who rules Urmuri now is a Nine-Tailed Fox named Yana."

"Are you saying the Nine-Tailed Fox can only eat human flesh?"

"Her dependence on it is strong. There are different types of Nine-Tailed Foxes, but Yana is one who must eat human flesh. To be precise, I hear she eats their 'livers'."

"Livers?" Lee Sungmin muttered.

Legends of the Nine-Tailed Fox were famous even in the modern world he had come from. Stories of the creature had been passed down in many regions, including Korea, Japan, and China. In Korea, where Lee Sungmin had lived, the Nine-Tailed Fox was known as a fox Yokai that preyed on human livers.

"It's not just the Nine-Tailed Fox," Aladur grumbled, shivering. "There are many Yokai in Urmuri with a high dependence on human flesh, and a steady supply is being provided for them. The Red Ghost, who ruled Urmuri before, wasn't particularly hostile to humans, but Yana seems to be different. The number of missing people has increased since she began her rule."

He sighed. "If possible, I'd like to part ways with the Phantom Spear before we reach Urmuri."

"What are you so scared of?" Lee Sungmin asked. "The number of missing people may have increased, but not everyone who goes there disappears. It's not like this Yana has openly declared hostility towards humans."

"It's because I've been screwed over many times by living with that kind of lax thinking," Aladur spat, flopping down onto his back. "I just hope we get out of this forest quickly."

As Lee Sungmin's party neared the edge of the Bewitching Forest, Wiji Hoyeon arrived at the entrance to another, escorted by the Red Flower under the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's orders.

"Where is this…?" she wondered aloud.

A member of the Red Flower helped her from the carriage. She had asked about their destination several times during the journey, but no one had given her an answer. Wiji Hoyeon blinked her listless eyes and stared at the forest before her. A moment later, she could not help but be astonished.

*My goodness…*

The forest was saturated with natural energy. While it was normal for a lush forest to possess such energy, the amount here was far beyond simply 'a lot'. With every breath, she swallowed a dense concentration of it, so immense that it defied common sense.

"A guest, a guest!"

"Who is it?"

"It's a girl!"

"She's pretty! She's human, right? Right? Not an Elf?"

"You idiot, can't you tell by her small ears?"

"The one who called me an idiot is the bigger idiot!"

A chorus of noisy voices approached. Wiji Hoyeon looked toward the sounds and saw palm-sized boys and girls giggling as they flew toward her, their small wings fluttering. Her eyes widened in surprise.

"Is this your first time seeing the Fairy Realm?"

It was a voice she had heard before.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman wore a mask, just as his clone had. A plain white mask covered the upper half of his face. Wiji Hoyeon's expression hardened as she looked at the uncovered portion, at the corner of his mouth.

"Indeed," he continued. "The Fairy Realm is a rare sight. If not here, perhaps you could see a bit of it in the Elf Forest, though this one has never been to the Elf Forest either."

"Elf Forest? I don't know it."

"The Coalition Leader always talks about things we don't know. All we know is this forest."

"Coalition Leader, Coalition Leader! Why are you wearing such an ugly mask? Change it to a cuter one!"

The small fairies giggled and swirled around the Chairman as he appeared. A few brave ones climbed onto his head and tapped his crown, while others perched on his shoulder and pulled his ear.

"Ah, stop, stop! I was trying to look dignified, but you're not helping at all…!"

"What's dignified?"

"You idiot, it means acting cool."

"Dignified is acting cool? It doesn't suit the Coalition Leader."

The fairies giggled. The Chairman gently pushed away a fairy pulling his hair but still changed his mask according to their demands. Wiji Hoyeon was at a loss for words as she watched him put on a brightly colored animal mask. The existence of fairies was bewildering enough, but seeing a master of the Transcendent Realm, the man at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox Alliance, being tormented by them gave her a strange feeling.

"I've ruined the first impression," the Chairman grumbled. "Was your journey here uncomfortable? I did tell the Red Flower to make sure you weren't inconvenienced."

"…It wasn't uncomfortable."

"That's a relief, then. Come this way. It's a bit of a walk, but I don't want to bring the carriage into this forest. It's really just a matter of feeling. This is a forest untouched by human hands. I simply don't want to bring something artificial like a carriage in here."

The Chairman said this with a chuckle. True to his word, the carriage that had brought Wiji Hoyeon had stopped at the entrance without going any further.

"Or what, is it difficult for you to walk?" he asked. "If you're okay with it, this one could support you. That is, if your pride allows it."

"I don't need it," Wiji Hoyeon spat in a cold voice.

At her words, the Chairman laughed again. "You even let that brat carry you on his back, but you don't want this one's support?"

"As you said. This is also just a matter of my feeling."

"Then I have no choice but to understand. However, matching your slow walking pace is a bothersome and irritating task. So I'll do this."

The Chairman raised his hand, and Wiji Hoyeon's body floated into the air. She did not resist. She lacked the energy to do so, and it was difficult for her to move her unresponsive body anyway.

"You've gotten weak," the Chairman hummed. "I'm in quite a pleasant mood."

"I am not."

"That's your feeling," he replied and started walking through the forest.

The fairies followed behind them. They approached Wiji Hoyeon, who was floating through the air, and tried to talk to her, but she did not answer.

"This is a good forest to live in," the Chairman began. "The fairies are a nuisance, but it's still better dealing with them than with humans. It's not that fairies don't lie, but their lies are simple 'pranks' that aren't distinguished by sympathy or malice. That's why this one prefers fairies to humans."

"…What are you talking about?"

"I am explaining why this one is in this forest."

"I… I thought you were in a state where you couldn't move your body. That you were dying from an illness, or had an incurable wound."

"Haha! It might seem that way, but that's not it. This one is very healthy. I've lived for quite a long time, but I'm so healthy I can't even guess when my dying day will be. The reason this one is in this forest is simply because I like it here."

"Indeed. The natural energy in this forest is incredible. Just by breathing without an inner arts technique, one can accumulate inner arts."

"You should know this too. Once you reach the Transcendent Realm, the amount of inner arts you have doesn't matter much. Still, the more the better. The reason there's so much natural energy here is because it preserves nature in its purest form. You see, the Fairy Queen is in this forest."

"…The Fairy Queen?"

"That's right. Perhaps you'll get to meet her too."

The Chairman's steps came to a halt before a small log cabin.

"…Didn't you say you didn't want anything artificial here?" Wiji Hoyeon asked.

"Hm? I did. Why, does it bother you that this one built a house to live in after saying that? Even so, this one, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, can't very well sleep on the ground, can I?" he replied with a hearty laugh. "I've brought you to the front of the house, but whether you live in it or not is for you to decide. Of course, if you do live in that house, you'll be living with this one."

"I refuse."

"Then sleep outdoors. This one will protect you, but I will do nothing for you. The reason this one stepped forward to protect you was because I took a liking to that brat, the Phantom Spear, not because I like you. The Phantom Spear? If he hadn't struggled so desperately to protect you, this one wouldn't have cared whether you were dragged off to Heaven Beyond Heaven or not."

With that, the Chairman set Wiji Hoyeon down. She staggered to her feet and glared at his masked face.

"…You won't do anything for me?"

"What is it? Were you expecting this one to do something for you? Do you want to become this one's disciple too?"

"No."

"Then what? Should this one treat you kindly like your father? We can't be lovers. This one doesn't like young girls."

"Don't say such horrible things."

"I'm being serious. When it looks like you're about to die, this one will give you a meal. So… try to live well from now on. I don't know when that Phantom Spear brat will return. You'll have to stay in this forest for at least half a year. It might be good to think of it as a recuperation trip. The air is good in this forest."

After saying his piece, the Chairman spun around.

"Let me say one thing. Just as this one won't pay you any mind, you shouldn't pay any mind to this one either. This one is merciful, but… if you happen to annoy this one, even this one doesn't know how I'll act when I'm angry."

Leaving those words behind, he departed with the fairies. Wiji Hoyeon watched the retreating back of the Chairman, whose hair was still being pulled by the tiny creatures, and then collapsed onto the ground.

*Half a year.*

Wiji Hoyeon bit her lower lip. She had never been treated this way before, and the feeling was unfamiliar, but that didn't matter. What she truly hated was her own weakened body. Gasping for breath, she closed her eyes and carefully began to circulate the Heavenly Demon Divine Art. The flow of qi was painful, but Wiji Hoyeon did not stop.

This was a forest full of natural energy.

Even if she couldn't move her body properly, she intended to absorb as much of it as she possibly could.
