"Lee Sungmin!"

A bright expression lit Theos's face as he approached. Behind him trailed the Holy Knights, their exhaustion a heavy shroud, alongside the survivors who had endured the ordeal with Kim Jonghyun. Teresa and the Saints had mended their physical wounds, but the mental fatigue from the relentless battles clung to them like a persistent shadow.

"You're truly, truly... incredible," Theos said, his voice thick with sincerity.

They had all heard the name Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun, but today was the first time any of them had witnessed his power firsthand. They had fought their way through an army of zombies only to be met by the Death Knight Legion. Had the Death Knights not retreated midway through the battle, the Holy Knight Regiment and the others would have suffered catastrophic casualties. They had barely broken through to reach this place, only to be confronted by Kim Jonghyun himself, a man who wielded a power that truly befitted a Demon Lord. The magic he commanded was less like a practiced art and more like a primal form of violence, raw and untamed.

Still,, in the end, they had won. Though they did not understand precisely what had transpired, Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun was gone, and they knew, with a certainty that settled deep in their bones, that the one who had fought the Demon Lord to the very end was the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin.

"You are a hero!" Theos shouted as he closed the distance, and a murmur of agreement rose from everyone behind him.

Without Lee Sungmin, they would all have perished. No one else there could have stopped Kim Jonghyun. Even Teresa's divine power had failed to subdue him. The Holy Knights and the other survivors, their wounds constantly healed and their stamina supplemented by the priests, had been utterly helpless against the Demon Lord.

Only Lee Sungmin, without any aid, had single-handedly defeated the commander of the Death Knights. That feat alone was worthy of being called heroic, and now he had seemingly vanquished the Demon Lord himself.

"Lee Sungmin?"

As Theos drew closer to the dazed man, a prickle of unease traced its way up his spine. A strange anxiety tightened his skin. Something was wrong.

Theos did not dismiss the warning from his instincts. He halted, his eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin.

*Get a grip, get a grip...*

The words echoing in Lee Sungmin's head were meaningless, a bothersome static that he could not comprehend. The yokai nature born from him was Lee Sungmin, yet it was not. The personality, the thoughts, everything that had defined the original man had vanished. The being standing there now was not him but a completely different Yokai. The very thing he and the Void Monarch had feared had come to pass. No matter how much they had believed in his will, the Reversal Magic had flipped his very species, and Lee Sungmin was no longer human.

"Lee Sungmin...?"

The original Lee Sungmin's consciousness had sunk into a place of profound depth. Had he become a complete Yokai, his human consciousness would have been annihilated. It was not. Instead, it was buried deep within his subconscious, asleep. This was only possible because he had clung to his sense of self until the very end, and because the Void Monarch had fought desperately to prevent it from being extinguished.

*This is...*

Theos's gaze wavered. Lee Sungmin's face, which had been turned toward the sky, slowly lowered. To Theos, the continuous motion looked jerky and disjointed. His instincts screamed at him, an endless warning. *Back away. Don't just stand there.*

*Run...*

Cold sweat trickled down his back, chilling the skin beneath his armor, but why? His reason warred with the primal alarm. Lee Sungmin was not a dangerous person. The world might see him as a demonic practitioner, the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but the man Theos had witnessed was not evil. Had he not just saved all of them?

So why… why did he feel a threat more profound than when he had faced the Demon Lord?

It took only a moment for Theos to understand. It happened when Lee Sungmin's head finished its descent and his gaze fell upon him. A pair of golden eyes, impossible to be human, stared back. Theos could not feel a single shred of humanity within them. Between slightly parted lips, every tooth was a sharp fang, perfectly shaped for tearing and chewing.

"Ah..."

The color drained from Theos's face. He wanted to run. He hurriedly raised the sword in his hand.

The instant he lifted it, the blade split in two. Sungmin had not swung his spear. His long, sharp fingernails had sliced through the steel with impossible ease.

PWAK!

Blood erupted from Theos's chest. A mere flick of his fingers had not only severed the sword but had also carved a deep gash through his breastplate. Seeing the blood gush from the opening, Teresa and the priests turned white.

"Th-Theos?!" his fellow Holy Knights cried out in alarm.

Pale and stumbling, Theos retreated. He had narrowly avoided a fatal wound. The few steps he had taken back a moment before, heeding his instinct's warning, had saved his life.

"E-everyone, run!" he choked out.

The reason was a mystery, but the Phantom Spear was on a rampage.

Lee Sungmin, however, moved faster. He did not bother with his spear, instead swinging his empty right hand in a wide arc. With that simple wave, a storm of purple Demonic Energy raged forth. Theos threw himself backward, spitting blood. The other Holy Knights, alerted by his warning, managed to dive out of the way.

The crowd of survivors was not so fortunate. The few who remained were instantly turned into a bloody mist by the tempest of energy Lee Sungmin had unleashed.

"Aaaaaaaah!"

A scream like scraping metal tore from Lee Sungmin's throat.

The Void Monarch was shouting, his voice a continuous roar inside him. *Snap out of it. Didn't you resolve to never become a Yokai?*

The shouts felt distant.

*No.*

*No. I can hear it.*

Deep within his own mind, Lee Sungmin opened his eyes. He slowly pushed himself to his feet.

*Where am I?* he wondered, panting as he surveyed his surroundings.

It was a world of pitch-black. Ah, right. In that moment, he understood. He had been here before, back in Urmuri, the first time he had awakened as a Yokai. It was the place his consciousness was trapped when his yokai nature took over.

*But it's different from last time,* he thought blankly.

Back then, he had met the Void Monarch in this mental world. He had even seen his own rampaging yokai nature. Now, there was nothing. Only an endless, empty darkness stretched out before him. The situation was different. He had become a complete Yokai.

*What in the world happened?*

He could not understand it at all. Why had the seal suddenly broken? Why had he become a Yokai? And where had Kim Jonghyun gone?

"That Black Mage," a voice echoed from the void.

Lee Sungmin, who had been walking aimlessly through the darkness, stopped.

"Before he was banished from spacetime, he left you a very interesting gift. Reversal Magic… huhu. The same magic that inverted him into a Demon Lord was directed at you. Though you became a Yokai instead of a Demon Lord."

This voice. It was not the Void Monarch's.

"You were lucky, at least. Thanks to desperately holding onto your consciousness, your humanity wasn't annihilated. Not that this is a good situation. You'll be wandering this mental world forever."

"Who… are you?" Lee Sungmin asked, his own voice dazed as he responded to the chuckling tone.

Hahaha.

An empty laugh echoed from the depths of the darkness. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard and walked toward the sound. With every step, fragmented scenes flickered into existence in the void. They were all scenes he knew, memorable moments from his life preserved in his mental world. His past and present lives were jumbled together.

He saw himself first summoned to Zenavis, a memory from his past life. He knew nothing then, a true fourteen-year-old boy forced to understand, forced to do anything to survive. He learned a cheap inner arts technique. He took up the spear, thinking it would be safer to stab from a distance, after being told it was a good weapon for a beginner.

He lived like that. A mercenary. A C-Rank Mercenary of the Corona Mercenary Group. He had admired Zeon, a man from the same no-class background who had broken through to the peak realm. The Supreme Peak was laughable to him now, but to the Lee Sungmin of that time, even a peak master stood at the apex of the world. He had admired him, had thought he wanted to be like him, yet he had made almost no effort to achieve it.

And so he died. Without having lived for very long.

"Pathetic, isn't it?" the voice said with a laugh.

Lee Sungmin kept walking. The source of the voice was not far. With every step, however, different scenes appeared. If the memories judged as 'pathetic' were from his past life, what he saw now was his current, unfinished one.

He met Wiji Hoyeon under the guise of coincidence, learning martial arts from her and making her his goal. He trained on Mount Mushii and lived as a mercenary in Zenavis. He met Preskan and obtained the Black Heart.

"An important part," the voice giggled.

After that, he went to Shaolin. Then to Mount Hua, where he killed the Sword Phantom and met Denir. Then to the Sleeping Forest, where he met the Void Monarch and Rubia.

"This place is important too."

He reunited with Baek Sogo and Wiji Hoyeon in the Dungeon. He traveled north, met Jeniela and the Crazy Heavenly Demon, and killed the Blood Heavenly Demon. He went south to Ruvus for another reunion with Wiji Hoyeon and met the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He fought the Dark Supreme. To break the curse, he went further south, to Devon, where he killed the remnants of the Void Monarch.

"This place too."

In Urmuri, he met Yana, Frau, and Envirus. He parted ways with the Crazy Heavenly Demon and obtained the Demonic Energy of Urmuri.

"Very important."

Further and further south. Killing the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One… on and on it went. Lee Sungmin watched the footage of his own life. He watched the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman again, a feeling that was anything but pleasant. At the scene where he decided to eat the Chairman's corpse with Osla's help, the owner of the voice burst into joyful laughter and clapped his hands. He did not say it, but Lee Sungmin knew that scene was also 'important'.

Important for what?

He killed Vollander and fought Kim Jonghyun. Now he understood why he had become a Yokai. Watching from a third-person perspective, he saw Kim Jonghyun, engulfed in light, point a finger at him just before disappearing.

"...Where did Kim Jonghyun go?"

"Abel gathered all his remaining lifespan to cast Gries' magic. It's a spell that eternally banishes a target outside of spacetime. Kim Jonghyun can never enter Eria again. Past, present, and future, he has vanished from all of Eria's timelines."

"Then… what about Abel…?"

"He died. He probably died satisfied. He likely never imagined that Kim Jonghyun would pull such a trick right before being banished," the owner of the voice said with a laugh. "No matter how much you yearn to remain human, this is unavoidable. Reversal Magic is absolute. It's a great feat that your personality even remained."

"What… will happen to me?"

"You will wander this subconscious forever. That is, unless some variable intervenes. No matter how much you struggle, breaking out of your own subconscious is impossible. You've already become a complete Yokai."

"I can't understand," Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice. "The Yokai I've seen, most of them maintained their reason. In some ways, they were even more human than humans..."

"Whoa, whoa. That's too hasty a conclusion. You only know a handful of Yokai. The only ones you've properly communicated with are just two, the Void Monarch and Yana. Those two stand at the pinnacle of all Yokai, and, hahaha! You say they were more human than humans? That's a rash and arrogant thing to say. What do you know of the Void Monarch? What do you know of Yana? Do you think the side they showed you is all there is to them?"

He laughed in a scathing tone, as if he found the notion genuinely ridiculous.

"They held an excessive amount of goodwill toward you. That's why they were good to you. If the Void Monarch was as magnanimous and foolishly naive as he appeared, do you think he would have been called the Nightmare of the South four hundred years ago? Do you think even the Dragons would have joined forces to subjugate him?"

No. The Void Monarch was not a benevolent Yokai. The anecdotes Lee Sungmin had heard from the man himself proved that. He used to boast about catching and killing a Dragon just for flying over the forest he claimed as his territory.

"The side Yana showed you was out of respect and affection for the Void Monarch inside you. Do you think Yana would have been so kind if you were a mere human?"

No. Yana had tried to subdue him and turn him into a complete Yokai for the Void Monarch's sake.

"More human than humans? What does it even mean to be human? And what does it mean to be a Yokai? Hahaha… Yokai have almost no concept of good and evil. For them, the most absolute criterion for any action is their own desire. It's the same way a human who is faithful to their desires is treated like a beast."

Lee Sungmin stopped walking. In front of him, someone was sitting with their back turned.

"I know what you're curious about. The you that has become a Yokai has no reason. It only tries to kill and devour everything it sees. The only words it can speak are guttural screams. It has no thoughts, so its movements are simple, and it doesn't know how to properly use the power it possesses."

Why was that?

"The reason is simple. Your reason as a Yokai doesn't exist in the first place. The you that has transformed into a Yokai is nothing more than an empty vessel, and I am the one filling that empty space."

"...What… in the world… are you…?" Lee Sungmin asked, his voice trembling.

Hahaha. The man laughed once more.

He slowly turned to face Lee Sungmin. He had no eyes or nose. On his empty, white face, only a mouth remained, smiling faintly.

"Me?"

The man raised a hand and pointed to himself.

"I am the Slaughter Predator."

Ha, hahat. Hahahahat.

As horror seized Lee Sungmin, the Slaughter Predator burst into laughter.
