Lee Sungmin was well aware of his own limitations. He was no prodigy blessed with outstanding talent, nor was he particularly lucky. Even the experience he carried from his past life was hardly remarkable, filled only with memories of major opportunities and none of the minor details.

Even so, his current situation was overwhelmingly better than when he had first arrived in Zenavis all those years ago.

Morning found Lee Sungmin leaving the inn. The innkeeper was not yet out, perhaps because it was still early, but a large basket filled with dry-crusted bread had been left in his stead. 

He devoured a few pieces right there. The taste was awful, but it was better than an empty stomach. Only after eating his fill did he finally step outside.

The moment he was outside, he called upon the One Thunder Movement Arts. Although he had circulated the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art through the night, he had not accumulated much inner qi.

"...Haa!"

He surged forward for about five minutes before his inner qi hit rock bottom. 

Lee Sungmin skidded to a halt, hands on his knees as he gasped for breath. At his current level, five minutes was his absolute limit. If he slowed down a bit and regulated his energy, could he maybe stretch it to ten minutes?

*It's so unfair.*

The feeling, one he had endured so many times in his past life, weighed heavily on his mind once again. Even with the synergistic effects of being Classless and having a Low-Grade Martial Skeleton granting him the growth potential of a Mid-Grade Martial Skeleton, this was his limit... for now, at least. A year of dedicated training and monster hunting would naturally bring improvement, but how much stronger could he truly get? 

Lee Sungmin knew all too well how absurd this world of Eria was, a fact made blatantly apparent in the City of Beginnings, Zenavis. He remembered his past life here, when the Super Rookie who had taken the city by storm was an otherworlder from the murim.

The Demonic Cult's Young Cult Leader.

He had no idea why such a formidable person had been summoned to this world, but from the moment he arrived, he possessed martial arts that far surpassed the peak realm. The profundity of his inner qi went without saying, and the Heavenly Demon Divine Art he wielded instantly crowned him the Super Rookie of Zenavis. 

It had taken Lee Sungmin three years to graduate from the city. The Young Cult Leader did it in a week.

Lee Sungmin had never seen him in person, but he had heard the rumors countless times during his thirteen years in Eria. In truth, there was more than one otherworlder who was formerly the 'Demonic Cult Young Cult Leader', but the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon was the one he heard of time and again. If his memory served him right, Wiji Hoyeon would be summoned to Zenavis in one month.

Setting aside his thoughts of Wiji Hoyeon, he entered a market alley. Though it was early, many shops were already open and street stalls were being set up. Among them, a stall with various weapons laid out on the ground caught his interest.

"Well now. See anything you like?" the stall owner said with a grin. "You're up bright and early. My first customer of the day! How about it? I'll give you a good starting price."

Lee Sungmin offered no particular answer, instead crouching down to carefully examine the equipment. "Is this stuff used?"

"That's right," the stall owner replied. "I have some new stuff, too, but the used items are definitely cheaper."

Lee Sungmin raised his hand slightly. "Is it okay if I touch them?"

"As long as you don't run off with them," the stall owner laughed heartily.

Lee Sungmin plopped down on the ground and reached for the haphazardly displayed equipment. He picked up a crudely made throwing dagger. The blade was well-sharpened, but the handle was stained with grime from use.

"An otherworlder used that," the owner explained. "The owner... is dead. I just collected it from a goblin nest yesterday."

Collecting and selling the belongings of dead otherworlders was a common occurrence. Lee Sungmin himself had often rummaged through the corpses of others to take anything useful back in his past life.

"How much is it?"

"I'll give it to you for 3,000 Eru. A throwing dagger is a consumable item, after all, and well, it's something I just picked up anyway."

Three thousand Eru for one dagger was not that expensive, but could he haggle? Lee Sungmin lifted his gaze from the ground and met the stall owner's eyes. "Could you do 2,000 Eru?"

"Puhaha! Are you trying to slash the price by a third?"

"I don't have much money..."

"Hey, kid, you're Classless, right? You look young, too... So what, you want me to feel sorry for you and give you a discount?"

"I'd be grateful if you did."

"You cheeky brat!" the stall owner laughed loudly once more, though he did not seem particularly offended. "You know, I like guys who are properly aware of their own situation. I prefer someone who struggles to survive over one who just whines."

Smiling, he spread his hand wide before folding three of his fingers. "I'll make it 2,000 Eru, and... I have three more throwing daggers just like that one. If you buy all three, I'll give them to you for 5,000 Eru."

"I'll take it," Lee Sungmin said, a bright smile spreading across his face. He took out his wallet.

The stall owner chuckled. "You're a strange kid. You know how to properly use your young age and innocent face. Are you doing it consciously?"

"I don't know what you mean."

"Well, it doesn't matter. You... seem like you'll become a regular. Next time you come, I'll adjust the price to match your circumstances."

*Is this a gesture of goodwill?* Lee Sungmin bowed his head and said, "Thank you," all while pondering the stall owner's true intentions. He could not be sure if the man just wanted to secure a regular customer or was genuinely being kind.

Regardless of his motives, he was pleased to have bought three daggers, originally 3,000 Eru each, for a mere 5,000, but he needed more than just throwing daggers. 

He pointed to a long spear lying by itself on one side of the mat. "How much is that spear?"

"Thirty thousand Eru," the stall owner answered.

Lee Sungmin reached out and gripped the weapon's wooden shaft, feeling the weight of the metal blade at its end. It was over two meters long, far too long for him to use right now.

*A mere two meters... is too long for me now...*

At fourteen, he was not even 160 centimeters tall. If he grew as he had in his past life, he would eventually reach 180 centimeters before he turned twenty, but that was a long way off. He also currently lacked the physical strength to handle such a weapon properly. 

Even with his experience, physical limitations were unavoidable. Ample inner qi would increase his strength enough to wield it, but the amount he currently possessed was pitiful.

Still, if it was too long, he could always shorten it.

"...Can't you make it cheaper?"

"You won't even be able to handle it properly with your build, you know?" the owner said. "Why not use a sword instead? What was it? The otherworlders from murim often say that. That the sword is the King of Weapons. I wouldn't know."

"I don't really know how to use a sword..."

"Oh? And you know how to use a spear?"

"You just have to stab with it, right?" Lee Sungmin gave a vague answer. Having used a spear in his past life, he knew very well how tricky and profound a weapon it truly was.

"Puhaha! I like your simplicity. Alright, if 30,000 Eru is too expensive, I'll give it to you for 20,000."

The stall owner's loud laugh echoed as Lee Sungmin made his final purchase. Three throwing daggers, one spear, an old leather belt that could hold potions, and five empty potion bottles. He bought all of it for 30,000 Eru. It was an incredible price. Even if they were all used items from dead otherworlders, they were in perfectly usable condition. The belt in particular would be very useful in the future. 

Lee Sungmin adjusted its length around his waist and bowed his head deeply. "Thank you."

"Don't mention it. I told you, kid. You seem like you'll become a regular. The tough ones survive for a long time."

*That would be nice*, Lee Sungmin thought with a bitter smile, genuinely meaning it. He knew very well that this world was not an easy place.

His lack of inner qi was a keen and present problem, one he could do nothing about for now. A Star Spirit Pill would improve his situation significantly, but that would be offered as a prize at the Coliseum a year from now, and he had no confidence he could win it.

The journey from the inn to the hunting grounds took at least two hours. He would use movement arts as much as possible, and when his inner qi were depleted, he would run. He had to train this weak body. The more one used martial arts, the more one's proficiency grew. It was just like a muscle.

The area around the North Gate was crowded with people, mostly otherworlders or merchants selling goods to them. Lee Sungmin did not buy anything here for a simple reason. They grossly overcharged. Because it was the closest point to the hunting grounds, otherworlders had no choice but to buy their goods, even knowing they were being ripped off.

"Anyone want to go to the Goblin nest!"

"Looking for a fellow countryman who studied at the Mount Hua Sect!"

"Does anyone know the Ruer School?! Any mages skilled in fire magic!"

People shouted loudly for their own purposes, some looking for companions to hunt with, others for martial artists from the same sect or mages from the same school. Lee Sungmin paid them no mind and headed quickly towards the North Gate. He remembered his past life, when he had first headed to the hunting grounds after arriving in Zenavis. He had gone with companions then, too.

It did not end well. The people who went with him at that time were all Classless in the same situation, with no connection whatsoever to the reality of fighting monsters. Casualties occurred one after another, and Lee Sungmin barely escaped with his life. The trauma from that experience had kept him from even going near the hunting grounds for a week.

*Companions... they're convenient, but...*

The problem was that this was Zenavis. The only companions he could find were, at best, other Classless people in the same situation. If he were to head to the hunting grounds with them, he would have to be the one protecting them, an incredibly difficult task for him right now. Even with his memories and martial arts, all the techniques he possessed were 1-star. To be frank, he could barely take care of himself.

What about joining martial artists or mages? He had considered it, but it was hopeless. His level was too low. Since he could not pull his own weight, there was no way they would accept him.

"Name?" the guard at the North Gate asked, looking Lee Sungmin up and down.

"It's Lee Sungmin."

"Do you have an identification tag?"

"Not yet..."

"Well, whatever. It doesn't really matter if you have one in this city or not... huhu! Don't die, kid."

Ignoring the guard's sneering words, Lee Sungmin walked out of the city gate.

A vast forest came into view.
