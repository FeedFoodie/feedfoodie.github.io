"......"

Nebul slowly flipped through the martial arts manuals Lee Sungmin had given him, the silence stretching between them.

"These are from Shaolin," he finally said. "Hundred Steps Divine Fist, Dharma Meditation Art, Arhat Movement Arts… I can't quite call them divine arts, but they are certainly valuable techniques."

"How much Mana would they fetch?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"I wouldn't really recommend selling them, however."

At Nebul's reply, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. "Can't they be sold?"

"No, that's not it. We will purchase any item our members wish to sell, even if it doesn't find a buyer immediately. In such cases, however, we determine the price based on the market value." A bitter smile touched Nebul's lips. "There are many Shaolin techniques on the market. Representative techniques like these are so abundant that there's a surplus. We sell the techniques we purchase through auctions, the Merchants' Guild, or the black market, but since Shaolin is such a famous sect, their arts are everywhere. If you wish, we can purchase them, but it will be difficult to give you their full value."

"Is it that bad?"

"Because it's Shaolin," Nebul confirmed. "Ah… if you don't mind, may I ask the origin of these techniques?"

"...Do I have to tell you?"

"I am your dedicated Broker, Lee Sungmin. Regardless of how you obtained these manuals, I will not reveal that information to anyone. The reason I ask for their origin is that the advice I can offer you will change depending on it."

Lee Sungmin was lost in thought for a moment, though it didn't require much deliberation. It was unavoidable that he had come to possess Zeon's techniques, and it wasn't as if he had gone out of his way to kill the man.

"...The original owner of these manuals died, and I found them while recovering his body."

"In that case," Nebul replied immediately, "I recommend you take these to Shaolin and return them."

He elaborated, "Shaolin is a prestigious orthodox sect. Among the ten powerful orthodox sects that make up the Nine Sects One Gang, their Noble Clan is particularly strong. As they are a traditional Buddhist sect, they are far removed from evil deeds. If you take these manuals to Shaolin and return them, they will provide a suitable reward, if only to save face."

"A reward?"

"If you bring them manuals of this level, they'll probably give you at least one Minor Cycle Pill. A Great Cycle Pill seems unlikely. At the very least, you'll be able to form a connection with Shaolin, so it won't be a bad outcome. To be frank, even if we were to purchase these manuals from you, the price wouldn't be enough to buy a single Minor Cycle Pill."

Lee Sungmin couldn't help but feel a little disappointed. To think that three whole martial arts manuals wouldn't even earn him enough to buy one pill.

*...Where was Shaolin again...?*

He searched his memories. While the Nine Sects One Gang had branches in various regions, each had only one main headquarters. The Shaolin headquarters was located in 'Bredon', a city quite far from Behenger.

*Bredon and Dreamoor are pretty close. I can stop by Bredon and then head to Dreamoor.*

It seemed better to go to the main headquarters to return the manuals rather than a branch. Having decided on a course of action, Lee Sungmin asked another question, just in case.

"By any chance, are there any other members of Erebrisa whose causality is twisted like mine?"

Normally, someone of his skill level could not become a member of Erebrisa. He had been told he wasn't qualified even when he surpassed the level of a peak master, which meant one needed to be at least at the Supreme Peak level. In mage terms, that was the equivalent of an archmage. Preskan had said that the other being with twisted causality was a monster he wouldn't even dare to challenge. A monster of that caliber could very well be a member of Erebrisa.

"...That is difficult for me to answer," Nebul said carefully. "Information about other members is top secret."

"You can't even tell me if such a person exists or not?"

"Yes. I apologize."

In the end, he learned nothing. Unless he met Preskan again, there was no way for him to find out who the other being was.

After sending Nebul away, Lee Sungmin let out a sigh. Many things weighed on his mind, but his path forward was not bleak. He had a clear destination. He would go to Bredon, visit the Shaolin headquarters, and return the manuals. If he was lucky, he might receive a Minor Cycle Pill.

By absorbing the Great and Minor Cycle Pills without any loss, his inner arts had seen great improvement. However, simply having a massive amount of inner arts didn't make one stronger. He had yet to even glimpse the wall that existed at the end of the peak realm.

*Still... a long way to go.*

The incident in the Dungeon had taught him a clear lesson about reality, about just how insignificant and weak the being known as Lee Sungmin was. After becoming a peak master and easily defeating the Ogre Chimera, he had thought he had become incredibly strong.

That wasn't the reality. There were plenty of people stronger than him. Even though Preskan was a Lich and the Black Magic Tower Master, being toyed with like a child by him left a complicated feeling in its wake.

What would it have been like if it were Wiji Hoyeon?

No, there was no need to even bring her up. Even Baek Sogo was far stronger than Zeon. From the moment Lee Sungmin had entered the mountain, she already possessed skills that surpassed the peak realm. Perhaps she had already become a transcendent peak master.

To prevent her death in the Dungeon seven years from now, to stop Wiji Hoyeon, his current level was far from enough. He had to become stronger, much stronger.

*I couldn't benefit from the Magic Armor. No. In this case, should I consider that a good thing...?*

It was probably fortunate that he hadn't been forced into a contract. Lee Sungmin sighed, looking down at the armor still covering his body. It was nice to wear, but…

"How am I supposed to take this off?" he grumbled with another sigh.

*

Two days later, Lloyd left Behenger. The heavily damaged Mercenary Guild was temporarily placed under Jo Young's leadership as acting branch manager. From what Lee Sungmin heard, however, the position would be handed over to Rudd once he returned from his request. Jo Young himself was refusing the position, claiming he wasn't qualified.

Lee Sungmin's name had also been mentioned for the role, but he vehemently expressed his refusal. He couldn't afford to be tied down to Behenger as the branch manager.

Once things at the Mercenary Guild were more or less settled, he went to the Mages Guild to have the curse on the Magic Armor removed, a task he had asked Kim Jonghyun to handle.

"It's all done," Kim Jonghyun said, returning the armor.

Lee Sungmin took it and infused it with his inner arts. The armor opened up and covered his body. Although the contract was gone, the other magic enchanted onto it remained. He could don the armor by infusing it with inner arts, and the method to take it off was the same.

"I'm planning to leave Behenger soon," Lee Sungmin told him.

"Is that so? Where are you planning to go?"

"First, I plan to go to the Shaolin headquarters in Bredon."

"You're embarking on a long journey. It'll take at least a few months to get to Bredon from here."

"I have some business there."

"Do you have some connection with Shaolin?"

"......"

Lee Sungmin offered no further explanation. At his silence, Kim Jonghyun chuckled and nodded his head.

"I thought we could become quite good friends. It's a shame we have to part ways like this."

"...Well. If fate allows, won't we meet again later?"

"I suppose so. It's not like I plan to stay in Behenger forever either. Ah… speaking of which." Kim Jonghyun, who had been nodding, glanced at Lee Sungmin's face. "Scarlet Reshir. I heard that woman is leaving Behenger too. Don't tell me you're going together?"

"......"

"I guess not. You two seemed quite close, so I just assumed you were in that kind of relationship."

Scarlet was leaving Behenger. When he had met her a few days ago, she hadn't mentioned anything of the sort. Kim Jonghyun shrugged at Lee Sungmin's confused expression.

"Well, it's not my place to gossip about it. Why don't you ask Scarlet yourself?"

He had formed quite a bond with her, so he had no intention of leaving without a word. Lee Sungmin left Kim Jonghyun's room and headed for Scarlet's. After he knocked a few times, her door swung open abruptly.

"What is it?" Scarlet glared up at him.

He flinched slightly at her sharp gaze, but then his eyes widened as he saw the state of the room over her shoulder. Her quarters, which had been as messy as a battlefield, were now neatly organized and looked like a completely different place.

"...I heard you were leaving. Is it true?"

"Who'd you hear that from? Ah, no need to ask. That psycho Kim Jonghyun told you, didn't he? Why does that bastard go around blabbing about other people's business?" she grumbled, scratching her head vigorously.

Watching her fluttering red hair, Lee Sungmin realized something. In his past life's memories, there were no rumors of Scarlet Reshir living in Behenger. Her stay here was only temporary. He had already guessed as much, he just hadn't expected the time to be now.

"Being cooped up in the city isn't good for my training," she explained. "I didn't realize it myself, but I guess I'm better suited for… you know, somewhere a bit dirtier. Huh? Like doing old-fashioned training in the mountains."

"Don't tell me you're thinking of going back into Mount Mushii?"

"Are you crazy? Why would I go there? I'm never going back. That goddamn place! Ugh… and, you probably don't know this, but if you train in Mount Mushii, leave, and then go back in, the fighting spirit restriction carries over. Do you know what that means? It means if I go back, I'll have to deal with those damn restrictions all over again."

He hadn't known that, though it made a certain amount of sense. If the restrictions were reset just by leaving, there would surely be ways to exploit it.

"Anyway, I'm not going to Mount Mushii," she continued. "For now… I think I'll wander around a bit. Then, when I find a place I like, I'll settle down and start training in earnest."

"Ah… I see."

"Did you come all this way just to ask me that? What, were you afraid I'd leave without saying a word to you?" Scarlet taunted with a chuckle. "I'm not that heartless. It's just… yeah. I've only just decided and haven't finished preparing yet. I was going to tell you when I was ready."

"...Is that so? That's a relief. Actually, I'm also planning to leave Behenger, so I came to say my goodbyes."

"What? You're leaving Behenger? Why? Where would you go after leaving here?"

"...There's no reason I can't leave, is there?"

"So where are you planning to go?"

"For now, I'm aiming for Bredon."

"Bredon? That's damn far. Have you ever been?"

"No, but if I follow a map…"

"Then we can go together," Scarlet cut him off.

At her sudden words, Lee Sungmin's eyes went wide. "...Huh?"

"Let's go together. I was planning on wandering around a bit anyway. It's better for both of us to have a travel companion than to go alone, right? It won't be as lonely."

"W-well, that's true, but still…"

"What, are you worried I'll hold you back?" Scarlet sneered and let out a dry laugh. She raised her hand and smacked him on the head. "Don't be a smartass, kid. I'm probably way stronger than you. In fact, it'll be easier for you if you travel with me. If someone stronger than you shows up, I can protect you."

"No, but I'm an S-Rank Mercenary and a peak realm master…"

"Oh, is that so? You charmless little brat. Just shut up, say thank you, and travel with your big sister here."

As Scarlet ruffled his hair, Lee Sungmin felt a mix of complicated emotions. His mind was almost thirty years old, after all, to be treated like this by someone younger than him.

*...No. Wait a minute.*

"By the way, how old are you, Scarlet?" he asked out of pure curiosity.

Her eyes blazed. "Wanna die?"

"You're not supposed to ask a woman's age."

It seemed she was older than she looked.
