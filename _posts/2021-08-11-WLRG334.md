Scarlet and Osla were flustered.

Not even half a day had passed since Lee Sungmin and Yana had departed for Cheped on the Fairy Horse. In that short time, he had returned alone, his face a demonic mask of fury. Scarlet's expression hardened as she watched his shoulders tremble violently.

"What happened?" she asked, sensing that something terrible had occurred.

Lee Sungmin fought to steady his voice, but no matter how he tried, the words came out ragged and strained. He finally gave up and told her everything.

"You crazy…!" Scarlet's face contorted when she heard the full story. "Why didn't you tell me it was about Baek Sogo?!" she shouted, her gaze snapping down to the bracelet on her wrist. It was one of a pair she had given to Baek Sogo and Lee Sungmin before they left Mount Mushii.

No matter where they were in the world, Scarlet could send fragmented information to the wearer. Communication was impossible, but a conversation was not what she intended. She just needed to know the location.

Scarlet focused her mind, infusing the bracelet with magic. As long as she wore its twin, she could grasp a general sense of the other's whereabouts.

A chilling sense of deja vu washed over Lee Sungmin. He had experienced this before, when the Crazy Heavenly Demon had wanted to find a village girl. He had succeeded then through Aladur's spell, but the owner of the bracelet had been a corpse in a grave.

*Surely, not this time too.*

He gnawed on his lower lip, desperately hoping it would not be the same.

"Found her," Scarlet said, her eyes opening. To get a more precise fix, she closed them again and focused her mind even more intently. "That's a relief. It seems she's not dead."

"Does that mean…?"

"She doesn't seem to be in a normal state," Scarlet clarified. "And… this place is… hmm…"

"Where is it?" Lee Sungmin asked, his voice trembling.

Scarlet hesitated for a moment before meeting his gaze. "It's a place you've been to before."

The end of the North.

"The Vampire Queen's Mansion."

For a moment, the strength drained from his legs. Lee Sungmin staggered, barely catching himself before he collapsed. Trabia, the Vampire Queen's Mansion. Why would Baek Sogo be there? She had challenged Ju Won and lost, barely escaping Cheped after being poisoned by his venom. Why in the world was she at the Vampire Queen's Mansion?

"What will you do?" Yana asked, having listened to their exchange. "Silent Flash Baek Sogo. I don't know what she means to you, but seeing you go this far, she must be someone precious."

Lee Sungmin did not need to answer. A precious person. The thought was instantaneous. On Mount Mushii, he had received Baek Sogo's help. Without her, he would have died on that mountain. The Dancing Shadow Soul Break she had taught him had saved his life several times since. He had wanted to prevent her death, which was why he had gone to the Doppelganger Dungeon to defeat Wiji Hoyeon's double. He had succeeded.

Then, in Germud, Baek Sogo had stood in his way when he was rampaging as a Yokai. *What if… what if she hadn't stopped me then?* With no memory, only an instinct for violence, he would have committed terrible acts. Because she had stopped him, Frau was able to perform the Sealing. If Baek Sogo had not been there, he might have rampaged until he was subjugated and killed.

A lifesaver.

A senior sister.

He recalled the letters she had left behind.

"I'll go."

He had been avoiding a meeting with Jeniela. Her power was so immense that even the current Lee Sungmin could not handle it, and her ability to see the future was deeply unsettling. That was why he had refused without a second thought when Rao Xian suggested they go to the North together after the Sealing was released. He had not killed Rao Xian or the other Vampires and Lycanthropes in that situation precisely because he did not want to clash with Jeniela.

The situation had changed.

"I'll go with you."

"I'm going too," Scarlet spat. "If you'd told me from the start that this was about Baek Sogo, I would've gone with you from the beginning."

"Didn't you say you couldn't leave your post?"

"Have you already forgotten what I've been doing in this forest for ten years?" Scarlet snapped, glaring at him. "I have disciples who can maintain my research while I'm gone, so don't worry. I'm just going to my tower for a moment. If you abandon me and leave again this time… I'll hate you. You're not the only one worried about Baek Sogo."

"…Understood."

Hearing his reply, Scarlet spun around and ascended toward her tower with levitation magic.

"The Vampire Queen is dangerous," Osla said, finally breaking her silence. "She was born in this world, Eria, yet she is a being close to a true transcendent. No, in some ways, she has surpassed the standards of a transcendent. The only fortunate thing is that tonight is not a full moon." Osla muttered this while looking up at the sky, where the moon had not yet risen. "Under a full moon, she is absolutely unkillable. It's just as difficult to kill her when it's not a full moon. However… the truly dangerous thing isn't her immortality, but her overwhelming magic power, which stands at the pinnacle of the Vampire race."

"Is she that strong?" Yana asked. She had never met Jeniela in person.

Osla nodded. "Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri. I know you are a being of immense power yourself, but you must not underestimate the Vampire Queen."

Before long, Scarlet returned, now wearing a brilliant crimson robe and ornate gloves. The magic of the Reshir School she had created did not use a staff. Those gloves were the implements necessary for the magic she called 'Spell Engraving'.

"I can't go myself," Osla said with a sullen face. "Instead," she added, raising both hands. Colorful particles of light gathered in her palms. "It's a Fairy's Blessing."

As Osla raised her hands high, the light filling her palms rained down upon Lee Sungmin, Yana, and Scarlet.

"It will protect your minds from the Vampire Queen's mental attacks, but don't be overconfident. Even I am not certain I can completely block her power."

"Thank you." This was all the help Osla, who could not leave the forest, could offer. Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply, expressing his sincere gratitude.

"Thank you."

"Don't die," Osla said, her face still sullen. "The Coalition Leader died for you, and he gave you his everything. You're… not the Coalition Leader, but I sometimes see his image in you. Does that displease you?"

"…It doesn't displease me."

"Don't worry. It's not like I'll like you the way I liked the Coalition Leader. Your aura is different from his, your build is different, and your face is different from the Coalition Leader's. I like faces like the Coalition Leader's."

"……"

Osla stuck out her tongue, breaking the rather serious atmosphere. Lee Sungmin was a little flustered, but he smiled awkwardly and nodded.

*Don't die.*

Those words from Osla were undoubtedly sincere. He did not want to die. Naturally, he felt the same.

"Can three of us ride?" Lee Sungmin asked, glancing at the Fairy Horse. The creature showed no reaction, only blinking its large eyes. It seemed to be okay with it.

He sat snugly at the front, with Scarlet behind him. Yana then perched on the horse's rump.

"Three people is the limit," Scarlet grumbled.

Lee Sungmin thought of Jeniela's mansion. This was the third spatial leap today alone. A long time ago, Abel had warned him that the Fairy Horse's power was not omnipotent. He was right. The moment they landed near the mansion, a powerful wave of dizziness struck Lee Sungmin. He steadied his staggering body and took a few shallow breaths, his head throbbing slightly.

It was the same for Yana. She shook her pale face from side to side and muttered, "This is very… unpleasant."

"It's the third time today alone," Scarlet, for whom it was the first, seemed fine. "We've leaped such an enormous distance three times in a row. Of course it's going to take a toll on your body. It would be best to stop at three for today. Any more than that, and your body and mind could completely separate."

"What does that mean?"

"Exactly what it sounds like," she said. "You could become an idiot. Or the opposite could happen. Your body disappears into the cracks of space, and only your mind successfully makes the leap. Yes, to put it simply, you become a ghost. Want to try it one more time and find out?"

"No," Lee Sungmin answered immediately, regulating his breathing. The throbbing headache soon subsided completely. He straightened up and glared at Jeniela's mansion.

The red mansion showed no outward change from ten years ago. He slowly approached the main gate. Before it was Cheped, now it was Trabia. The weight he felt in his heart, however, was entirely different. Ju Won had not been in Cheped, but Jeniela was in this mansion.

Lee Sungmin's hand touched the main gate.

"The Phantom Spear."

Someone called out to him from beyond the gate.

"I heard that Rao Xian succeeded, and that you have a Fairy Horse."

The main gate opened. Standing there was a girl with a small frame.

"You were going to come here eventually anyway, so why did you refuse Rao Xian's offer in Germud? You even hurt him."

"…Who are you?"

"One of the Queen's children," the girl said with a bright smile. "I know why you're here. You came for the martial artist the Queen is keeping. For Silent Flash, right?"

At those words, Lee Sungmin's expression changed. As he strode forward, the girl quickly raised both hands.

"Whoa, whoa. Don't get too excited. We're treating that woman with the utmost care. The Queen ordered it."

"…But why?"

"Because she knew that woman is precious to you. Our Queen can be quite mischievous, and she was quite lucky, too." At that time, Jeniela had not yet lost her Eye of Futuresight. "Don't resent the Queen too much. If the Queen hadn't intervened, that woman would have died from Ju Won's poison long ago."

"…Is the poison… cured?"

"No, that's impossible. Not even Ju Won himself can detoxify his poison. The same goes for our great Queen. All the Queen did was… care for her so the poisoning wouldn't progress too far. To keep her from dying. Isn't that a great mercy in itself?"

"……"

Lee Sungmin chewed his lower lip and glared at the girl. She merely shrugged under his murderous gaze.

"My apologies for the late introduction. I'm Gemini. The Queen's First Bloodline. Now, come on in. Didn't you come here to meet that human?"

"Why isn't the Queen coming out?" Lee Sungmin demanded, his glare fixed on Gemini.

At his words, Gemini chuckled and turned around. "Why? Do you want to meet the Queen?"

*If possible, he didn't want to meet her.*

"Don't be in such a hurry. There's an order to everything. First… I'll let you meet the person you want to see. Wouldn't it be better to talk about other things after that? Yes, if I had to say… things like the relationship between us and you."

The relationship with the Predator.

"You have a lot of guests," Gemini noted, her eyes sweeping over his companions. "I didn't expect you to bring the Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri, and… the Crimson Sage, too. Anyone would mistake you for coming to kill the Queen." She turned her head, a laugh in her voice. "That's not it, right?"

From the small girl, Lee Sungmin felt the same sense of danger he did from Jeniela.
