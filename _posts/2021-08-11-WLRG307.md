A crowd had gathered before the tightly shut gates of Germud. Hundreds of Holy Knights, mounted on white horses, formed the vanguard. Their volunteering was not born from a sense of grand sacrifice, but from the simple convenience of taking the lead. Behind the knights, a vengeful mob from the surrounding lands had taken their positions. They ground their teeth, their killing intent a palpable thing directed at Kim Jonghyun, the man who had massacred their city. Abel's passionate speech from the night before had been like pouring boiling oil on their rage, fanning it into a murderous inferno.

Priests clustered around the Saint, Teresa. As she stared at the firmly shut gates, she clutched her staff to her chest. Though she was revered as a Saint within the Church, this was her first time facing a true battlefield.

Meanwhile, Lee Sungmin, along with Abel and Frau, planned to move independently. Their sole objective was to strike down Kim Jonghyun, a strategy he had already discussed with Theos, the captain of the Silver Knight Order, and the high Priests.

-RUUUMBLE!

Abel raised his hand, and a heavy explosion roared as the temperature around them soared. A swirling vortex of flame erupted from his palm.

"Oh, God," Teresa muttered in awe as Abel's magic blasted the city gates to splinters.

UAAAAAGH!

The crowd, now an army, let out a unified roar. At the very front, Theos proudly raised his lance, a clear display of the Silver Knight Order he commanded. His beloved horse reared up on its hind legs.

Zombies swarmed through the breach beyond the shattered gates. It was as if they had known the precise moment of the invasion. A siege had been considered and dismissed, for their forces were ill-suited to such warfare. It seemed Kim Jonghyun had never intended to defend the gates at all. Instead, anticipating the breach, the zombies at the front held shields to block the way. They looked to have ransacked the Germud armory, bringing out anything that could possibly serve as a shield.

"Charge!" Theos roared.

His horse surged forward at a full gallop. Theos lowered his posture, aiming his lance, and the hundreds of Holy Knights behind him mirrored his stance perfectly. From the rear, Junior Disciples brought their hands together, preparing their magic. A cascade of buffs settled upon the Holy Knights, transforming them into a single, giant lance. They were no longer just knights, but a white wave poised to swallow everything in its path.

There was not even the sound of a collision. Wreathed in buffs and moving at maximum speed, the Holy Knights' charge simply pulverized the zombies blocking their path. Though they held shields, they were mindless creatures incapable of using them properly. The physical toughness they had gained as Undead proved as soft as tofu.

"WAAAAAH!"

With the front of the zombie formation crushed, the army surged forward, their morale ignited by the sight. They stomped on the heads of zombies still struggling with their tenacious unlife and swung their weapons with abandon. In an instant, a battlefield was born, and the nauseating, bloody stench of the undead blanketed everything.

The Priests moved at once. While the Holy Knights were immune to the poison from zombie blood, the gathered army was not. The clerics quickly cast detoxification buffs, advancing so as not to be left behind. In their midst, Teresa looked on with a pale face. Not even ten minutes had passed since the battle began.

*How many have died already?* she wondered, gripping her rosary tightly.

The zombie poison could not infect her, but the stench was another matter. Teresa gasped, the foul odor so thick it was difficult to breathe.

Having shattered the front lines, the Holy Knights switched weapons. Now wielding sleek spears, they stabbed down from their horses, piercing the zombies' heads.

THWACK, THUD.

The sounds rang out in perfect, brutal unison. The army rushed into the gap they created, their faces twisted into demonic masks of rage as they swung their weapons. Teresa watched it all, her shoulders trembling. *Even if they're zombies, they were once human.*

"Saint," one of the Archbishops said, catching her staggering shoulder. "Don't be shaken. Granting death to those who have fallen so low is the only mercy we can bestow."

"...Yes." Teresa nodded heavily and gripped her staff tightly. As she began to stride forward, the Priests parted to either side, clearing a path for her.

VWUUUUNG!

The rosary around her neck vibrated. When Teresa raised her staff high, the light emanating from her surged upward with it. She spread her arms, and the surging power split to both sides, forming wings of pure light behind her back.

"Ashes to ashes, dust to dust." Reciting the age-old farewell, Teresa closed her eyes.

"She's better than I thought."

Abel, Lee Sungmin, and Frau entered through the ruined gates a short time later. Abel watched Teresa, now wreathed in wings of light, and his admiration was undisguised. Particles of light rained down from her wings, purifying the foul stench and poison from the dead zombies while healing the army's wounds. Her very presence bolstered the Holy Knights' morale, and their fatigue vanished instantly under her light.

"I see why the Church was so protective of her," Abel remarked. "She looks like a hothouse flower, but that power is the real deal."

Lee Sungmin nodded in agreement. It was not a power limited to the Undead. Her mere presence bolstered the Holy Knights, healing their wounds and fatigue without need for potions or magic. Furthermore, the storm of light she emitted was pulverizing any Undead that dared approach.

*The zombies are just numerous. They must have been placed at the gates to stall for time. Or... was it to gauge our strength?*

[Or perhaps it's to make you accumulate fatigue,] the Void Monarch offered.

Of course, thanks to Teresa's presence, the fatigue of the Holy Knights and the army was continuously being restored. However, Teresa was tiring out just as much, and Kim Jonghyun's real forces, the Death Knights, had not even appeared.

[That girl's ability is precious. We can't let her exhaust her power so early.]

Lee Sungmin gave Abel a look. "I'm going in."

"Is there a need to do that already?"

"The Death Knights are nowhere to be seen. Now that we've confirmed Teresa's abilities, we have to make her conserve her strength."

"Do as you please. I'm busy in my own way."

Abel did not step forward. There was a set time for him to act, for his true nature had not yet been grasped by Kim Jonghyun. The same was true for Frau. In this situation, the two of them had to unleash their power only at the most crucial moment. Lee Sungmin was different. His existence and his power had already been exposed. There was no need to hide it now. He lowered his spear and slowly walked forward.

CRACKLE!

By the time the violet current wrapped around his body, he was already beside Teresa.

"Ah!" Teresa cried out, jumping in surprise as Lee Sungmin suddenly appeared beside her. He reached out to steady her staggering form but flinched, quickly withdrawing his hand. The Demonic Energy in his body recoiled from her light.

*Getting too close is dangerous.* Realizing this, Lee Sungmin stepped back to create some distance between them.

"It's still too early for you to step in," he said.

"......"

Teresa understood his meaning, but she glanced anxiously toward the front lines where the Holy Knights and the army rampaged. Before she knew it, her vision was filled with zombies. The tens of thousands of citizens who had once lived in Germud, now dead and twisted, let out grating screams and roars.

"I know," he said. "I know you don't want the people who gathered to kill Kim Jonghyun to get hurt. That you don't want the Holy Knights at the front to get injured and exhausted."

"Even though you know..."

"You can't," he cut in. "This isn't a neighborhood scuffle. This is a war against tens of thousands of Ghouls, hundreds of Death Knights, and the one who stands at their pinnacle. It's a war against a Black Mage comparable to a Demon Lord. Teresa, I've seen that your power is useful. That's why you have to save it."

The word 'useful' made a look of bewilderment cross her face. The high Priests around them glared at him with stern expressions.

"Before you lecture me on insolence," he said, shaking his head before they could speak, "tell your Saint to calm down and stop wasting her power."

"Wait a minute!" she protested. "I'm not—"

"Why don't you understand?" he pressed. "Do you think I'm telling you to stand by because I want them all to get exhausted and die?"

[It seems she doesn't quite understand. It must be because she doesn't know much about you,] the Void Monarch chuckled.

Well, that made sense. The Lee Sungmin they knew was the Phantom Spear. They knew nothing but the title.

"I'm telling you this," he said, his voice low, "because you don't need to go this far. Not yet."

That was enough. He would say no more. As for what his words implied, he would show them.

CRACKLE.

Wrapped in a violet current, Lee Sungmin's figure vanished. He reappeared in the middle of the horde, surrounded by rampaging zombies. In the heart of the clash, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened.

A flash of light exploded in his mind, an unceasing burst of black lightning intense enough to erase the northern snowstorms. He saw countless ink lines staining the world black.

Lee Sungmin had inherited the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but he had not yet achieved Body Reformation. He had not obtained all the power his master possessed, for it was far too immense for his current body to handle. Just as he had slowly digested the Dragon Heart, his body had not yet assimilated the Chairman's power. He knew this. He knew in his head that he could not wield it perfectly. He knew it in his mind, the martial prowess his master had reached. He understood how his master had used his arts, the combat techniques that had touched the absolute limit.

"......"

A single current flowed, and every ghoul around him exploded. Amidst a blur of afterimages, Lee Sungmin corrected his stance. As expected, knowing something in his head and executing it with his body were two different things. His stomach churned, his body unable to properly control the extreme acceleration, but it was enough. He had not missed.

"Ah."

Teresa's eyes widened. In that brief moment, everyone in that space, save for the mindless ghouls, felt a profound shock. A single strike had contained tens, no, hundreds of intertwined techniques.

[How is it?]

*I'm fine.*

Both arms that had swung the spear were shattered. Teresa hurriedly tried to direct her light toward him, but in that instant, Lee Sungmin met her gaze. His cold stare made her breath catch in her throat.

*Her power is no help to me. In fact, it's poison.*

[I'm fine,] Lee Sungmin sent a Thought Transmission to Teresa as his shattered arms regenerated in an instant. People often said the body suffered when the mind was slow, but this was the opposite. He knew it in his head, but his body could not keep up, and that made it all the harder.

He wondered how many more times his arms would have to break before he could fully use what he had received from his master.

ROOOAR!

Zombies swarmed to fill the empty spaces. Lee Sungmin charged forward without hesitation, heading for the forefront of the battle. He leaped over Theos and the Silver Knights, who were holding the line at the front.

WHIIIRRR!

The spear in Lee Sungmin's hand began to spin. The violet current stretched out long.

SWOOOSH!

As he swung the spear wide horizontally, hundreds of zombies were split in two. Theos's eyes shot open. He was a master of exceptional skill himself, but the man reflected in his eyes was a monster, one who had far transcended the limits of human power.

Landing on the ground, Lee Sungmin felt the shocked gazes on his back, but he felt no enjoyment in them. Instead, a deep sorrow washed over him, a sorrow for himself, for being only this capable.

*Ah.*

He walked forward slowly, the weight of every stare upon him.

*I was so glad you were my Master.*

He had received far too much. For that reason alone, he could never become a disciple who would bring shame to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.
