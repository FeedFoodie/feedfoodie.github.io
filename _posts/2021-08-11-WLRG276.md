WHOOSH!

A black wind raged through the churning atmosphere, causing the surrounding scenery to flicker and then collapse. Within the Dog Beating Stick Formation, sixty-six black staves moved as one, their trajectories merging into a single, colossal strike.

Toward the approaching tempest, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman slowly extended his right hand.

"......"

His palm met the black wind of the Dog Beating Stick Formation, not with a crash, but with a soft sound that utterly betrayed the word 'collision'.

Martial Beggar, who commanded the formation, was stunned. Lee Sungmin was just as surprised.

He recalled the late Crazy Heavenly Demon's warning. *One must be careful with formations.* Lee Sungmin had never faced a proper one before. While the orthodox sects' Four Great Absolute Formations were famous, one could not simply challenge them at will.

Due to a lack of opportunity, he had secretly held a certain arrogance. No matter how outstanding a formation's martial theory was, the individual martial artists within it were no match for him. He had therefore thought that even if he were to face the Four Great Absolute Formations, he would not feel much difficulty.

The Beggars' Gang's great Dog Beating Stick Formation shattered that arrogance. Deployed by a hundred beggars, the formation shook and dominated the space. The sixty-six Dog Beating Sticks delivered a strike that was chillingly sharp, swift, and heavy.

That single blow was so powerful that even Lee Sungmin would not have been able to counter it easily.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, however, was different. Having pushed back the strike with a lightly extended palm, he showed no sign of panic.

No, his expression did change. A flicker of anticipation in his eyes died, replaced by a faint, unmistakable disappointment.

"Formation!" Martial Beggar roared, and the attack began anew.

"......"

The sixty-six Dog Beating Sticks split into two groups, swinging along different trajectories. The very space under the formation's influence distorted, creating a black wind that rushed at the Chairman from both directions. He simply clenched his fists.

KRA-KRAK!

The sound was like lightning striking right in front of him, loud enough to deafen the ears. The black wind was torn apart, and the sleeves of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman fluttered.

He strode forward as Martial Beggar once again shouted for the formation.

Go, go.

The thirty-three beggars tasked with deception sang an angry chant.

The clash and swing of the Dog Beating Sticks grew fiercer, churning the very flow of ki. This space was now completely under the formation's control. Even Lee Sungmin, who was not its direct target, felt his insides churn. Their chant was disrupting the flow of ki and ruining one's senses.

Still, amidst it all, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was free.

Go, go. The Dog Beating Stick Formation moved in time with the beggars' chant, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman advanced in kind.

Black currents covered his body as his unique martial art, Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, showed its true worth. He released a power that neither Lee Sungmin nor Wiji Hoyeon had ever witnessed, holding nothing back.

KRUMBLE... KRRRRUMBLE...

Lightning cried out from within a thunder cloud of his own making, and his body was swallowed by transparent black lightning.

*What Heaven Reversal Black Thunder pursues is Extreme Speed.* Lee Sungmin heard the Chairman's voice in his head. The earlier excitement was gone, replaced by a cold tone that confirmed Lee Sungmin's suspicion. The Chairman was disappointed in the Beggars' Gang's great formation.

He was inwardly dumbfounded. The Beggars' Gang's formation deserved its reputation as the pinnacle of its kind. A master of the Transcendent Realm would struggle to withstand it directly. In Lee Sungmin's estimation, even the Dark Supreme or the Sword Saint would meet their end against it. If he had to fight it himself, he felt he could only hope to escape after pushing his Demonic Energy to its absolute limit.

"Wild! Strike!" Martial Beggar shouted, the words seeming to vomit from his throat. The black wind shimmered, multiplying into dozens of gales that occupied all directions.

An escape route? Lee Sungmin's eyes could find none.

However, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman saw something different.

[Watch.]

The thought echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind. Amidst the inescapable barrage, the Chairman took a leisurely step forward.

His steps seemed slow, at least to Lee Sungmin, yet the moment his foot touched the ground, he became a bolt of lightning. The fiercely raging wind left no path, but for the Chairman, who was now faster than the wind itself, the faint gaps between the gales were passages wide enough to slip through.

It was not an escape route. With his martial prowess, he could have shattered that black wind head-on without needing to evade it.

Martial Beggar was shocked, his momentary confusion causing him to forget his next command. It was only an instant, but for the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, it was enough. His hand struck forward.

KRA-RUMBLE!

Another thunderclap erupted, and the faces of the beggars turned pale. He could have given them no time to recover from their churning stomachs, but the Chairman deliberately waited, hoping the disappointment he felt might yet turn to excitement.

"Attack! Defend!" Martial Beggar finally shouted. They split, thirty-three for attack and thirty-three for defense, and once more the black wind raged.

*As expected, when hopes are dashed, the disappointment is even greater,* the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered, extending his right hand.

Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, Myraid Thunder.

KWWAAAAANG!

The explosive sound sent Lee Sungmin stumbling back. The unleashed Myraid Thunder tore through the thirty-three attackers and shattered the thirty-three defenders. This time, he did not wait, knowing that to expect more would only lead to greater disappointment.

The Chairman's left hand rose.

Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, thunder cloud. His gracefully moving hand stretched out a long line of black enhanced qi. With a flick of his fingertips, the line pushed slowly forward. He then turned his outstretched hand over, and the line of qi swelled, condensing within it an immense power that Lee Sungmin could not even begin to imagine.

"Genesis."

The sound vanished.

The enhanced qi stretched, swelled, and burst.

That was all Lee Sungmin could comprehend. The moment that immense power was unleashed upon the Beggars' Gang, all sound disappeared and his vision went black.

Lee Sungmin faltered, stepping back as the wind subsided. The chant was gone. The space, once dominated by the Dog Beating Stick Formation, had lost its master and returned to normal.

Slowly, his vision cleared, revealing the beggars twitching and collapsed on the ground.

"Hah... Heok..."

Martial Beggar was not dead. He staggered to his feet, his body refusing to move properly. He tried to support himself with his Dog Beating Stick, only to realize his Black Dog Beating Stick was broken in two.

It was not just his. Every single Dog Beating Stick used in the formation lay shattered. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman lowered his raised hand and muttered.

"My expectations were too high." His voice was laced with irritation.

"The best of the Four Great Absolute Formations? I never believed that, of course... but with that level of confidence, I thought there'd be something to it. It's nothing special."

"You... you... bastard...!"

"I thought about watching a little longer, but I figured I'd only be more disappointed if I did."

"Don't you dare insult the Beggars' Gang..."

"You're the one insulting me. You fought the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. A great man who is the strongest in the evil faction, perhaps the strongest in the murim, and maybe even the strongest in all of history."

Surprisingly, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman did not use terms like 'strongest in the murim' or 'history's strongest' lightly.

"Performing a stick dance fit for beating puppies against me. You know, there's a proper way to insult someone of my stature."

"It's not over yet..."

"No, it is over," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman insisted, shaking his head.

"I could have killed all of you. I didn't because subduing is usually more difficult and bothersome than killing. So I deliberately chose to subdue you. Killing you would have been far too easy."

At those words, Martial Beggar was left speechless.

Behind him, the elders of the Beggars' Gang shed tears of shame and humiliation.

Chwi Geol heard the entire exchange but lay quietly on the ground, pretending to be unconscious. He was grateful for the mercy the Chairman had shown and prayed desperately for his chattering master to shut his mouth.

"...Kill me..."

"You're not worth the effort," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said, turning to look at Lee Sungmin.

"I stepped in to instill a little awe in you, but dear me... I couldn't even show you anything proper."

"...I think I saw enough."

"Really? I feel like I haven't even warmed up yet," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied with a smirk.

The difference in their levels was immense.

The distance between himself and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman felt vast. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard. When he had become a master of the peak realm, he had felt the gap between himself and the Supreme Peak.

When he reached the Supreme Peak, he felt the chasm separating him from the Transcendent Realm.

And now?

[That is the limit,] Void Monarch said.

[The place that man has reached is the end for a human. If he were to advance beyond that, he could no longer be called human.]

*You mean he'd become inhuman?*

[No. Do not compare him to such things. That is an insult to him. An inhuman is merely a human who becomes a monster. To do that, one only needs to abandon their humanity.]

If you drink the blood of a Vampire, you become a Vampire. If a Black Mage offers his body to a Demon Lord, he becomes a Lich. If you are cursed by a Demon Lord or other high-level Dark Magic, you become a Lich. If you are infected by a Lycanthrope, you become a Lycanthrope. If a human is tainted by Demonic Energy, they become a Yokai.

[He has reached a level where he is no longer human through pure martial arts alone. You cannot call that inhuman. It would be correct to say he has far transcended humanity. It seems the level of his martial arts has already far surpassed that of a human... but his body still appears to be human.]

Void Monarch was genuinely impressed. Among the humans he had seen, it was an undeniable fact that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was the strongest.

"There's nothing more to see." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman raised his head, his gaze fixed on the tall pavilion of the Murim Alliance in the distance.

The Black Dragon Knight was contemplating. A head-on fight with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, in his opinion, would mean certain defeat.

His defeat would be certain even at full strength, but he had not yet fully healed from his fight with Ju Won.

He had tried various methods. He had used an Elixir and even secretly called a high-ranking Priest to use Divine Magic. He had also summoned a renowned physician. It was no use. The Black Dragon Knight gritted his teeth and felt his left side.

The wound had been torn by Ju Won's claws. If it had gone just a little deeper, his body would have been split in two. He had barely managed to hold the wound together and escape, but the injury, though healed, still caused him pain and hindered his movements.

Poison, they called it. Poison... it was frankly absurd. He was a Dragon, even if only half, yet here he was, suffering because of poison.

*Why isn't there a response?*

He knew a fight meant certain defeat, but the Black Dragon Knight was not in a position to simply leave the Murim Alliance. His tenure as Chairman was the will of both the Martial God and the Spirit Medium. He could not leave without their permission.

*How pathetic.*

The Black Dragon Knight glared at the crystal ball. Even the Spirit Medium could not hide his surprise at the news that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had invaded. A considerable amount of time had passed since he was told to wait, yet no directive had come.

The Black Dragon Knight chewed his lip in anxiety. At the same time, he secretly hoped that no matter how great the Chairman's martial arts were, surely he would not invade the Murim Alliance directly.

KRRRRUMBLE!

The very buildings of the Murim Alliance shook.

The Black Dragon Knight covered his face with both hands and let out a long sigh.

"That crazy bastard."

*I should have just ignored everything and run.*

The Black Dragon Knight thought as he rose to his feet.
