For once, Gemini was not smiling. The chuckle that always rumbled in his chest was silent, the perpetual smile that hung on his lips finally gone. In a situation like this, even he could not bring himself to feign amusement. He lowered his gaze, his lips forming a sullen pout as he rubbed his clasped fingers together, a gesture that openly revealed his anxiety for what was to come.

Even so, Gemini's expression was the best of the group. Chen and Kun trembled beside him, their faces pale with fear.

Jeniela, perched on a rock, said nothing. The small full moon she had created still hung in the sky above her, and beneath its light, she watched her vampires with a blank expression. The stiffened corpse of the Sword Immortal lay beside her, its neck broken. No one dared to speak. In that cold silence, only Preskan seemed detached, having collapsed in a spot where he would not get on Jeniela's nerves. He was sobbing quietly, unable to even cry his heart out for fear that the sound might anger her. The misery was all the greater for it, to lose his beloved, his only daughter, right before his eyes and not even be allowed the freedom to grieve.

The seemingly endless silence was finally broken.
"...S... sorry..."
A flustered Kun had hesitated for a long time before forcing the words out. For him, it was an act of courage born from a maddening fear, but his apology was cut short.

"......"

A whip of red magic power lashed through the air and shattered Kun's head.

"Get up," Jeniela spat, not even glancing at Kun's body as it staggered and collapsed.

After his shattered head regenerated, Kun staggered back to his feet. Jeniela was not the only one receiving the full moon's magic power. She looked at the standing vampire and let out a deep sigh.

"I debated whether to kill you or not," Jeniela muttered, resting her chin on the back of her hand. "Still, yes. It would be a waste to kill you in a fit of anger over something like this. Chen, Kun. I cherish you both very much. I still remember vividly the time I first made you my vampires. For something like this... no, I can't just call it 'something like this'. In any case, the hundreds of years we've spent together are too heavy for me to kill you just to vent my anger."

"Th... thank..."

"Still, I'm angry."

Jeniela smirked, and at her words, the bodies of Chen and Kun trembled. They were her third and fourth vampires, perfectly obedient to her will. That was precisely why they had no choice but to tremble now. Jeniela had ordered them to protect Aine, and they had failed to carry out that order.

"I am angry. It can't be helped, just as it couldn't be helped that you failed to carry out my command. It can't be helped that I'm angry. It's even more so because I trusted you both so much."

Chen and Kun did not answer. Jeniela looked at the silent pair and clicked her tongue before her eyes turned to Gemini.

"Gemini."

"Yes, Queen."

"Was it unavoidable for you too?"

"I don't lie to you, Queen."

"But you do play pranks."

"They're just pranks. It was unavoidable for me too. Don't you trust me, Queen?"

"I do trust you, Gemini. My lovely younger brother," Jeniela said with a smirk. "I know very well that you secretly want to kill me, but I love you. Because I know that even while you want to kill me, you also love me."

At her words, the corners of Gemini's mouth twitched and rose.

"It's only natural that you hate me. I sank my fangs into you without your consent at first and forcibly made you my vampire."

"I know very well that you did it because you love me, Queen."

"That's right, I did it because I love you. I wanted to possess you completely after you became a Vampire. It's the same for you, isn't it? You love me, so you don't try to kill me yourself."

Gemini did not answer Jeniela's smiling question. He simply stared back at her, his eyes curved into a smile of his own.

"So it's a love-hate relationship. Because I love you too, I can understand the pranks you pull. Like sending Silent Flash Baek Sogo to the Phantom Spear, and telling the Phantom Spear about me."

"Because the things I talk about aren't a threat to you, Queen."

"That's right," Jeniela did not deny it. "Handing over Baek Sogo is no threat to me. Nothing changes even if the Phantom Spear learns about my Demonic Eye. Those are things he can't do anything about even if he knows, but Gemini, this time is different. Aine should not have been taken."

"...I told you it was unavoidable. I had no intention of handing her over either. I never thought the Phantom Spear would go that far."

"Yes, I believe you. Because I love you."

THUD.

Jeniela came down from the rock. She glanced at the strewn corpse of the Sword Immortal.

"What a waste. I wanted to feed him something good."

"Should I keep it?"

"No."

Jeniela stretched her hand toward the Sword Immortal's corpse. The body made a CRACKLING sound as it shriveled up like a mummy. A red mist flowed out from its pores, gathering in her hand. Jeniela opened her mouth and swallowed it whole.

"He called it a negotiation, didn't he?" Demon God Jeniela asked, glancing at Gemini after drinking every last drop of the Sword Immortal's blood.

"Yes. That's what he said."

"It seems he has no intention of killing Aine."

"What are you going to do? Go find her? Or abandon her?"

"I've put too much effort into her to just abandon her. A negotiation... a negotiation, you say." Jeniela repeated the word and chuckled. "He'll be in the Fairy Forest, right?"

"He might have run away."

"If he wants to set the stage and fight me, there's no way he'd give up the Fairy Forest."

"Maybe he really does want to negotiate?"

"Perhaps, but I refuse."

She was furious. Gemini could feel it. Though she spoke with a smile, Jeniela was not smiling on the inside at all. He thought of Lee Sungmin and clicked his tongue. He had told the man a lot and had looked after him with some expectation, but he never imagined he would provoke Jeniela's fury like this.

"It seems I've been too lenient," Jeniela said and turned her body. She began to walk down the mountain without another word.

Gemini followed behind her, while Chen and Kun moved hesitantly. Preskan, who had been sobbing, also rose quietly to his feet.

"Let's go meet Ju Won," Jeniela announced. "And then we'll trample the Fairy Forest."

[If you can get the Fairy Queen's help, you should obviously use it. The Vampire Queen is a monster you can't handle as you are now.]

Lee Sungmin knew that all too well. Kidnapping Aine had bought him some time, but the true problem remained. He had to gather enough strength to face Jeniela before that time ran out.

He ran through the matchups in his head. Ju Won would take the Spear King, and Nero would face the Black Dragon Knight. That left Chen for Baek Sogo and Kun for Yana. Lloyd and Scarlet could handle the other Lich and Black Mages, while the Undead could be countered with Teresa's holy power. If it came to that, Lee Sungmin would have to face Jeniela alone.

The chances of victory were slim. Even with Osla's support, he had no confidence he could fight and win against Jeniela under a full moon. There was also the possibility that Jeniela would bring more forces than he predicted. Hadn't he heard there was a Were-Bear named Brock who was as strong as Nero?

[You're worrying a lot. What, are you regretting it now?]

*A little.*

Lee Sungmin admitted with a bitter smile. Just as Wiji Hoyeon was not made of iron, neither was he. No matter how you dressed it up with martial arts theory, he was just a mere human struggling to escape his nemesis. He did not possess the kind of power that the world itself would move to check, like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman or the Void Monarch. He had achieved almost nothing on his own. Most of it had been given to him by someone else. He did not think he lacked effort, but he was not sure if it could be called exceptional.

*Why me?*

Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky.

*He said... anyone would have done. In the end, I'm just here by chance... no, it's not entirely by chance. I met certain conditions, and I was unlucky enough to be here... and this is the result. It's too much for me to handle.*

[Are you whining?] the Void Monarch chuckled.

Lee Sungmin nodded his head. *Sometimes I feel like it. Besides you... there's no one else I can really complain to.*

It was true. Too much had been placed on his shoulders. He remembered the words he had said to Baek Sogo, who had despaired over her fate, and to Wiji Hoyeon, who was trapped in a dimension, mocking herself.

Words like, "Trust me."

Despite having said such things, Lee Sungmin could not bring himself to trust himself. He lacked the conviction that he would absolutely, without a doubt, succeed.

[You've done well,] the Void Monarch said. [For a guy like you, you've done extremely well. Even with the Grace of Fate, the loss and pain you experienced were real. The training you did had meaning. The connections you made did too. That's why you are who you are today.]

*I remember when we first met,* the Void Monarch chuckled.

[Back then, you were emotionally unstable. A little out of your mind.]

*That was right after I came out of the Mental World.*

[Your body was abnormal too. Like a bomb that could go off at any moment. Your emotional instability made it worse, but you endured, quite... rationally... without breaking down. The encounters and events you experienced stabilized your mind.]

*Not necessarily.*

[Partings were inevitable. I won't tell you not to be sad, but you can't stay drunk on sorrow forever. What this Elder wants to say is, all of that made you who you are today. You are not a meaningless existence.]

*Are you afraid of failure?* the Void Monarch asked.

*Of course I'm afraid. If I fail...*

[It'll be the end. There's the variable of Wiji Hoyeon, but, hmm... I'm not sure, and, for you, failure is death. The world might not end if you die, but at least your world will. Are you afraid of that?]

*I am.*

[Then will you run away?]

*Where would I run?*

[Who knows. At the very least, you could stop this foolish attempt to block The End and run away now. The world won't end immediately, even if we don't know when it will. How about enjoying some pleasure first? Run away, crawl into some fancy city, and indulge in all sorts of entertainment. Drink, hold women, you have plenty of money, so do all the things you haven't been able to do at once.]

*I can't do that.*

[I suppose not. Because you have to stop The End. You can't run away, if only for the sense of responsibility you feel for those who died before you. That's the kind of man you are. Sometimes you seem selfish, but at times like this, there's not a selfish bone in your body. You're a pretty good guy.]

Lee Sungmin smirked at that.

[If you fail, you fail. I don't know where you'll go when you die, but it probably won't be a bad place. I'm not even sure if there is an afterlife in the first place.]

*But I don't want to die.*

[What do you want me to do? You don't want to run away either. Then you have no choice but to struggle so you don't die. Don't worry too much. When you die, this Elder will be with you.]

*Though I won't die with you,* the Void Monarch laughed heartily.

"What are you thinking about?"

Baek Sogo's voice pulled him from his thoughts. Lee Sungmin lifted his bowed head and looked at her. She watched him with a slightly worried face.

"Are you not feeling well?" she asked.

"No... I'm fine."

"Aren't you too nervous?" Scarlet looked up from where she had been sitting nearby, checking her gloves with a serious expression. "Or are you worried? That what happens here might threaten the Little Heavenly Demon in the other dimension?"

"That's not it."

"Good. We've already confirmed it several times. No matter how much we dug into and searched the spatial coordinates here, we couldn't find any connection to another dimension. I'm not even sure if it's true that you met the Little Heavenly Demon here in the first place."

"There's no reason for me to lie."

"That's true," Scarlet conceded in a confident tone. "Anyway, what happens here won't reach the Little Heavenly Demon. Lord Lloyd and I have personally confirmed that."

More importantly, Scarlet squinted her eyes and looked up at the sky.

"When is the Spirit Queen going to show up?"

They were in the Sleeping Forest.
