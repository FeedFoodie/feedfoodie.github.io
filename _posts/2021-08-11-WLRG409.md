A tangle of feigned emotions erupted from the Martial God in a deafening roar as he swung his hands with reckless abandon. His movements seemed random, a wild flailing, yet each gesture held immense power. A single blow would have been fatal, but none of them connected. Lee Sungmin was simply faster, and stronger.

An impact from behind bent the Martial God's body in half, forcing a spray of blood from his wide-open mouth. He twisted his veiny neck, trying to look behind him, but Lee Sungmin was already gone.

*How... how can he be this fast...!*

The Martial God's face distorted as a nightmarish memory resurfaced, the ghost of their ten-year battle. In the end, nothing had changed. He had only run away from that fight, finding no answers in a seclusion that had yielded almost nothing. He had let his mind escape to forget the phantom ache in his left arm, but now, that very pain was returning with a vengeance.

KWAAANG!

His body slammed into the ground, which buckled and spat up fragments of earth.

"The Martial God!" screamed the Moon Queen from the sidelines. She genuinely believed in him, respected him, followed him. To see him thrown about so wretchedly was more than she could bear.

"You... you wicked monster!" she shrieked, kicking off the ground. Wrapped in a dazzling white light, she glared at Lee Sungmin with eyes full of malice.

As she closed the distance, Lee Sungmin glanced at her, a flicker of recognition in his eyes. It was a familiar face, the Elf Hunting Team Leader from the Forest of Ancients, and the daughter of the Fist Honored One he had killed. She seemed to hold no desire for revenge. Revias's personality had vanished completely, leaving the well-trained Elf's body as a mere vessel for the Moon Queen. It was amusing that such a person was calling him a monster. When one thought about it, wasn't she quite the monster herself?

A frigid chill summoned by the Moon Queen froze the very space around them. The temperature plummeted, and white vapor plumed from his mouth as a wave of chilling energy swept over him, capable of freezing a man to the bone with a mere touch.

Against this grand assault, Lee Sungmin simply pushed his spear forward. That light thrust was all it took. Every martial arts theory in his head was contained in that single, practical movement, stripped of all superfluity. The power infused into the spear was ferocious enough to crush any opposing force, and the wall of chill split in two, opening a path straight to the Moon Queen. Her eyes widened in shock.

"Moon Queen!" the Martial God shouted, aiming a palm strike at Lee Sungmin's back.

However, just as it was about to connect, Lee Sungmin's body vanished. The Martial God's attack sailed through empty air and struck the Moon Queen, who was just beyond.

Blood sprayed from her mouth. The Martial God's own mouth fell open in disbelief. Lee Sungmin, having used Gale Thunder to reappear at his side, thrust his spear toward his waist.

Though flustered, the Martial God was still the Martial God. He snatched the incoming spear with his right hand and swung his leg, aiming for Lee Sungmin's head. Lee Sungmin swallowed a smirk and swung his free hand.

PAAK!

With a dull thud, the Martial God's leg was knocked back. He gritted his teeth, spun once in mid-air, and swung both legs together. An Asura rose behind Lee Sungmin as the Asura Sky-Rending Dance of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art unfolded.

KWAKWAKWANG!

The successive blows sent the Martial God flying. From behind, the Moon Queen, still coughing up blood from the Martial God's errant strike, rushed him again. Her movements were obvious. Lee Sungmin quietly chanted the word for Dragonic.

KUUUUNG!

The Moon Queen's mouth fell open as an immense weight pressed down from above. She struggled against the pressure, but the Dragonic Magic that followed sealed her movements.

BZZZZZT!

A spear of light shot down, piercing her body. With a large hole punched clean through her from back to chest, she sprayed blood and plummeted to the ground.

"Aaaaaaaah!" the Martial God roared at the sight. This wasn't supposed to happen. None of this was supposed to happen. How could the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a joke just ten years ago, have become so strong? They had both lived through the same decade.

Collapsed on the ground, the Moon Queen watched through blurry eyes. The Martial God's attacks, swung with the fury of a demon, never reached the terrible monster before them. Lee Sungmin became a streak of lightning, toying with him, pushing him back helplessly with every blow. She couldn't understand it. Her younger brother, the Spirit Medium, had said that their combined strength could surely defeat the Phantom Spear, the calamity of The End. It wasn't just his guarantee, but the will of the Spirit itself.

However,, why? Why was the Martial God, who had seemed so strong, so great, now so weak? Or was it that he wasn't weak at all? This couldn't be happening. The Moon Queen tried to rise, pressing a hand to the bloody hole in her stomach.

"Please, O Spirit..." she called out in a desperate voice. If the Phantom Spear was truly the calamity of The End, the Spirit would surely answer, just as it had ten years ago when it gave her the strength to kill that vicious and powerful Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"Please, please..."

Nothing could be heard.

When she had desperately sought the Spirit back then, it had answered her call. It had bestowed upon her a brilliant light that made her heart swell and had stripped the absurd power from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the calamity of The End. If the Phantom Spear was truly the calamity, the Spirit had to answer again. She took out an Elixir, pouring it on her wound while continuing her plea.

Her muttering reached Lee Sungmin, stirring memories of the ten-year battle in the North Snowfield, his own experiences mixing with those of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He watched the floundering Martial God, who chewed his blood-soaked lips and kept moving, his eyes wide with disbelief. Lee Sungmin could see every one of his movements, the profound illusions he had mastered unable to deceive his eyes.

The Spirit could not intervene directly in this situation. Unlike the Chairman, who had been bound by fate, Lee Sungmin had escaped it. The Spirit could not take his power away as it had from the Chairman and the Void Monarch.

*However, it should be able to grant power to the Moon Queen or the Martial God.*

As if on cue, a pure white light fell from the sky. Two streams wrapped around the bodies of the Moon Queen and the Martial God.

"Oh, oooooh...!" the Moon Queen cried out in admiration as she rose to her feet, her wounds and fatigue completely gone. Vitality and power coursed through her. The Martial God looked down at his own body in bewilderment.

"Th-this is...?"

"The Spirit!" the Moon Queen exclaimed, her voice high with elation. "The Spirit has given us an answer!"

Her cry steadied the Martial God's wavering heart. His crushed self-esteem stirred once more. Yes, he thought, this was how it should be. It was absurd that he would suffer such humiliation from a mere disciple, not even from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself. That ridiculous power wasn't earned through the path of martial arts. It was the power of a vile Demonic Spirit, the power of a calamity of The End.

It wasn't entirely wrong. Lee Sungmin listened to the Martial God's muttering and nodded to himself. No, it was completely right. He readily admitted it, his pride unstirred by the accusation. The reason he was so strong was undoubtedly because of the Demonic Spirit and his status as a calamity. His very body belonged to the Slaughter Predator.

However, his martial arts.

The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

Clad in the Spirit's protection, the Moon Queen and the Martial God charged. Lee Sungmin stopped moving, simply feeling them approach as he quietly closed his eyes. His mind settled into a state of perfect calm. He refined his hatred and killing intent, sublimating them into pure martial theory as he contemplated the entire space around him. His senses, now wide open, allowed him to feel everything despite his closed eyes. The movements of the Martial God and the Moon Queen... felt so slow. It was a sense of déjà vu he had never felt before, yet it existed within his memories.

One step forward.

He did not move his body, but his mind. In that instant, the darkness shook. For the first time, he saw it directly, a long, winding, single path stretching out before him with no end in sight. He had heard of this place from the Demonic Spirit. The Battle God Chronicle, a realm reached by mortals who had transcended their limits, a vast world connected by consciousness, unbound by the physical body.

Lee Sungmin looked down and saw the tips of his toes barely touching the entrance to that path. The fully awakened Lee Sungmin had earned the right to enter. If he chose, he could walk it right now.

He raised his gaze and looked ahead. Far in the distance, he saw the figure of the Void Monarch, who stopped running and slowly turned his head. Lee Sungmin could see his grinning smile. Just as he was looking at the Void Monarch, the Void Monarch was looking back at him.

Behind him were other figures, among them a familiar back. Lee Sungmin unknowingly bit his lower lip. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was not wearing a mask. He was looking at Lee Sungmin with a faint smile on his cold face. At that gaze, Lee Sungmin unconsciously placed a hand on his chest.

The figure of the Spear King was also there, though he did not look back. He must have felt Lee Sungmin's presence, but the Spear King would rather use that time to move further forward than to glance behind him. That was just like the Spear King. Lee Sungmin smiled at the man who didn't look back.

Then he bowed his head deeply toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Void Monarch. He had received too much from them. Meeting them, sending them off... it had all made him who he was now. Without raising his head, Lee Sungmin took a step back.

Not yet. He could walk that path, but he shouldn't. He had not kept his promise to the Void Monarch, who had told him to live, to enjoy everything, and to come only when he had no regrets. He hadn't enjoyed a single thing. He was full of regrets.

So, he couldn't go yet.

When he had fully stepped back, the path vanished, but he did not forget the scenery. He didn't forget where the people he knew were walking, nor the distance between them. How far can I go? Can I, who have only received things from others, walk that path with my head held high?

*That's the regret.*

Because it's a little, no, a very embarrassing thing.

*What?*

The perception of time was different. In the moment Lee Sungmin had taken to visit the Battle God Chronicle, the Martial God and the Moon Queen were still closing the distance. The Martial God felt his emotions surge as he watched Lee Sungmin stand there blankly, his eyes closed.

*Is he ignoring me?*

How dare he. The Martial God's face twisted as a tremendous power gathered in his right arm. He poured all his strength into his hand, preparing a merciless palm strike.

Lee Sungmin opened his eyes. He was still in that different flow of time. He saw the charging Martial God and the Moon Queen behind him. He saw the power gathering in the Martial God's hand as clearly as a flowing river. Good grief, he muttered in a low voice, the Formless Spear already gripped in his hand.

A flash of light erupted.

The two differently perceived times synchronized with a violent snap. The Moon Queen screamed as she was sent flying back by the sheer wind pressure of the mysterious event.

KWAAANG!

She crashed into the distant castle wall, not far from the gathered onlookers.

The Martial God was not sent flying. The power gathered in his right hand did not erupt. It simply vanished, along with the arm that had held it.

"......"

With trembling eyes, the Martial God looked down at his right arm, from which he felt nothing. It couldn't be. He hadn't seen a thing, hadn't had a chance to react. His right arm was gone, as if it had never been there in the first place. No blood flowed from the severed surface, and he felt no pain from the wound.

"Guh, aaaaaargh!"

The Martial God screamed anyway.

Because instead of his missing right arm, the place where his left arm had once been hurt like crazy.
