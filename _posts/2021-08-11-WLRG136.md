The Crazy Heavenly Demon trailed silently behind Lee Sungmin, his mind reeling. He had just witnessed the ugly downfall and death of Baek Museon, the Blood Heavenly Demon he had secretly cherished, who had cast aside his humanity only to become a monster, yet the confusion churning within him stemmed from more than just that single, grotesque sight.

*Just what in the world is he?*

He stared at the back of the man walking ahead. Baek Museon had been no easy opponent. He himself had fought the Blood Heavenly Demon and failed to secure a victory as effortless as the one Lee Sungmin had just claimed. The thought was a troubling one. Only a few months ago, Lee Sungmin had been no match for him. In fact, he didn't even need to look that far back. Just this morning, when Lee Sungmin first clashed with Baek Museon, he had been weaker than them both.

However, what about now? He glared at Lee Sungmin's back. *If we fought with all our strength, could I win?*

"Heh heh!"

A hearty laugh escaped him. He would have to try it to find out, but a nagging doubt told him he had no confidence in victory.

*However... he hasn't reached the Transcendent Realm.*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon knew what a martial artist of that caliber looked like. He had seen the Sword Saint, one of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens, who was undoubtedly a master of the Transcendent Realm. Though he hadn't fought the current Lee Sungmin directly, if he were to compare the two, the Sword Saint would still hold a significant advantage.

*Considering his age, it's an incredible feat.*

He set aside his competitive spirit. Today was not the only day. Since they would be traveling together on the long journey south, a day would surely come when he could fight Lee Sungmin again.

"My motivation is soaring," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered in a cheerful voice.

*

Jeniela rested her chin on her hand, lost in thought. Her own eyes had witnessed Baek Museon's end and the entire battle that led to it.

*Did the Void Monarch intervene? No, he didn't use Demonic Energy...*

It made no sense. She had bestowed a great deal of power upon Baek Museon. Among the many vampires in her retinue, the number who possessed as much power as him could be counted on one hand.

And yet, he was defeated. She had ordered Baek Museon to make Lee Sungmin despair, but he had failed completely. A small laugh escaped her lips as she rose to her feet.

"How interesting."

She felt no regret over Baek Museon's death. From the moment he became a vampire and joined her retinue, her interest in him had vanished. With his death, the power she had granted him returned to her completely. There was no need to retrieve the body either. The corpse of a vampire that has lost its power cannot withstand the sunlight and simply scatters into dust.

*It's been a long time since I've wanted a human this much.*

Jeniela licked her lips and chuckled. Her interest in the human named Lee Sungmin had only deepened. As she slowly got up, she noted the banquet was coming to an end. The terrible screams that had echoed through the hall were no longer heard. Kim Jonghyun stood nearby with his hands clasped behind his back, watching everything. He remained completely unfazed, even as dozens of humans became food and drink for the vampires right before his eyes.

"Baek Museon is dead," Kim Jonghyun said, breaking the silence. Like Jeniela, he had also been watching the fight.

Jeniela nodded. "He's a very interesting human. He contains Demonic Energy, yet he's not a Yokai. Nor is he a half-yokai."

"You seem to have taken a deep interest in him. Are you planning to make him a vampire?"

"If he wants it."

"Vampire Queen, I know your wicked tastes all too well. You tempt humans who've fallen into despair, make them submit, force them to crawl on their knees and beg. Do you really think he will do that?"

"I only present the inhuman choice. The decision is always theirs to make."

"A small question comes to mind. If you present the choice, there will be plenty of humans who would take it. What is your criterion for choosing one?"

"A human who seems like they won't despair," Jeniela answered without hesitation. "And yet, a human who fears despair. A human who seems like they won't despair, but would if given the chance. A human who had dreams and ideals, but had them broken."

"There must be many such humans if you look for them."

"Not necessarily. For starters, you're not one of them," Jeniela said with a radiant smile.

A muscle in Kim Jonghyun's cheek twitched. He stared at her for a moment before tilting his head as if he did not understand. "What do you mean?"

"You are human, yet you are not human," she replied as if it were obvious. "And you've already fallen into despair. I think you're a rather interesting human yourself, but I don't turn humans like you into vampires. There's no fun in toppling you."

"Is that a compliment?"

"No, it's a curse," she said. "And it's also a prophecy. Humans like you rarely achieve their ideals, and they meet a terrible end."

"...Haha!" Kim Jonghyun burst out laughing. "I certainly hope so."

With those words, he said no more. He adjusted his robe and turned away. Jeniela did not stop him. She swirled the wine in her glass, made redder with mixed blood, and chuckled to herself.

*Spouting whatever she pleases.*

Kim Jonghyun quickened his pace with that thought. Now that he was a member of Predator, he had no more business in the North. His leave from the Guild was also ending, which meant it was time to return to the Black Magic Tower. Unlike other Magic Towers, his was monitored by the Mages Guild, a consequence of the ominous and dangerous reputation that followed Black Mages and their ideologies. He would leave the Guild if he could, but that was still impossible. The surveillance was a nuisance, but the advantages of being affiliated with the Mages Guild, especially for a Magic Tower Master, far outweighed the inconvenience.

*The End... the Apostle of the End, causality.*

His thoughts turned to Lee Sungmin, a truly mysterious individual. They met like this every few years, and each time, Lee Sungmin was incomparably stronger than before. A Classless, a path at a disadvantage for survival in this world, had reached a realm close to the absolute limit.

*A powerful Destiny Force is acting upon you. As long as the Apostle of the End is protecting you, you will flow along with fate, whether you are conscious of it or not. Even I, who keeps meeting you as if by chance, might be entangled in that fate.*

Such thoughts delighted him. *What is the meaning of my existence? What happened to me in a past life that I don't know, and that you don't know either? Am I acting differently now from the me of that other world? The next time I meet you, what will I be like?*

Kim Jonghyun let out a low laugh.

*

Wiji Hoyeon dragged her feet, the Black Dragon Armor that enveloped her body hanging limp. Her usually proud face was uncharacteristically haggard.

*How much longer?*

Walking became too difficult, so she stopped to rest in the shade of a nearby tree. The journey to Ruvus was far too long. There was not much time left, and she had never expected the path to be this arduous.

Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Six Honored Ones. Wiji Hoyeon chewed on her lower lip as she thought of them. They were every bit as strong as their name suggested. Even the Fist Honored One, said to be the weakest of the six, was the greatest foe she had ever faced in her life.

*I shall seek you out again next time.*

The blood-soaked Fist Honored One had left those words behind before departing. In a battle that had lasted for three days, she had failed to kill him. Likewise, he had failed to kill her. He had only left a curse.

"......"

Catching her breath, Wiji Hoyeon could not hold it in and vomited black blood. She stared at the dark, dead pool she had coughed up and bit her lower lip. The curse engraved by the Fist Honored One could not be healed, not even with an Elixir. It was a curse carved not on her body but on her very being, and it was slowly eating away at her. She pressed down on her nauseated chest.

*He didn't kill me. Why?*

Should she feel grateful for that? To think something like this would happen with the promised day so close. Wiji Hoyeon staggered to her feet. She had to end this curse.

"...Seren," Wiji Hoyeon managed to say.

A woman in a neat suit rose from within her shadow. It was Seren, the Broker from Erebrisa assigned to her. She looked at Wiji Hoyeon's pale face with a somber expression and bowed her head.

"...Yes."

"The method to break this curse... have you still not found it?"

"...As I've told you before. The curse eating away at you cannot be broken unless you kill the one who cast it."

She already knew that. Through Seren's brokerage, she had met with skilled shamans and mages, but they all just repeated the same thing. There was no way to lift the curse without killing its caster.

"Then what about the Fist Honored One's location?"

"We are continuing to search through the information Guilds that cooperate with us, but the organization called Heaven Beyond Heaven is so secretive that the Fist Honored One's location has not been confirmed."

Erebrisa was convenient, but it was not omnipotent. Its information was obtained through cooperating information Guilds. However, the organization called Heaven Beyond Heaven, despite its clear existence, was like a ghost. No information circulated about it at all.

"So in the end, I have no choice but to wait?" Wiji Hoyeon muttered with a bitter smile.

The Fist Honored One had said he would come again. Cursed as she was now, could she win if they met again? Or would she have to beg him to lift the curse?

*He didn't kill me. It must mean he wants to bring me into Heaven Beyond Heaven somehow... What is their goal?*

She had looked into the organization, but there was no proper information. What little she learned was that Heaven Beyond Heaven was a mix of humans and mutants, and that the Six Honored Ones were merely its most exposed members. The Fist Honored One himself had said he was the weakest of the six. In fact, only three of them called themselves 'Honored'. The other three were considered to be on an entirely different level.

"...Haha!"

Wiji Hoyeon let out a low laugh and turned away. Seren watched her back with a complicated expression before disappearing. Wiji Hoyeon did not stop laughing as she thought of Heaven Beyond Heaven, and of the Fist Honored One.

To be honest, she had always thought things were easy. The ten years she had lived in Eria had been a simple path, or so it had seemed until she learned of Heaven Beyond Heaven. Not anymore. Now she knew the truth. She was not as great as she had believed.

*I didn't want to show you such a shameful side of me.*

The thought spurred her onward. Wiji Hoyeon began to walk again, dragging her feet toward a destination that was still a long way off. Ruvus. She had to keep going without rest.

*I have to go.*

She would not break the promise she made ten years ago.
