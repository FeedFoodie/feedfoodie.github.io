It was a hidden weapons technique he had poured considerable effort into, a skill that could rightly be called his hidden card, yet, that very technique had been blocked, utterly failing to pierce the damned armor.

Flustered and horrified by the situation, the Dark Supreme did not stand idle and regret the life he had spent mastering his art. Instead, he let out a ferocious roar and charged straight at Lee Sungmin. The large sleeves covering his hands fluttered as he revealed a pair of forearm-sized swords, his arms swinging in a dazzling blur.

Against the crisscrossing storm of twin swords, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with a short hold and swung.

KWAANG!

The blades shattered the instant they collided. This was the result, even though both weapons had been coated in enhanced qi.

*Even if my enhanced qi is weaker, I didn't think it would fail to withstand even a single blow. No, this isn't a problem with the enhanced qi. That spear!*

The Dark Supreme's eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin's weapon. Forged purely from Dragon materials, the spear was simply too outstanding, regardless of the quality of the qi empowering it. He spun the hilts of the now-bladeless swords, his fingertips fumbling for a device installed within them.

PWAK!

The ends of the hilts exploded, launching dozens of ultra-thin needles toward Lee Sungmin.

He knew there was no need to dodge. The hidden weapons, fired by a mechanism, would be incinerated by his Protective Qi Aura alone, never even touching his armor. Knowing this, Lee Sungmin's actions grew bolder. He raised his Protective Qi Aura and charged straight through the volley of needles.

*He's no amateur. He's my equal... no, he's stronger.* The Dark Supreme acknowledged that Lee Sungmin was a master of a higher level than himself. He also knew that crude, mechanism-powered weapons would never work against such a man.

*I can't even buy time.*

Watching Lee Sungmin advance as if nothing had happened, the Dark Supreme gritted his teeth. He untied the belt from his waist and held it in his hand. The flexible sword hidden within it had already been used, its blade shattered and useless. Still, he lightly swung the hand holding the belt, wrapping it tightly around his left hand and forearm.

*How long will this last?* he wondered, swinging his arm.

The belt wrapped around his hand transformed into a wide-surfaced whip that tore through the air as it flew. Just as his hidden weapons had done, the whip wielded by the Dark Supreme distorted space itself. It was like the Mind Sword, which created spatial slashes, but it was not nearly as threatening as the Sword Saint's technique. In any case, against the current Lee Sungmin, even the Sword Saint himself would pose little threat with his Mind Sword.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break Second Form, One Step Dancing Shadow, thunder cloud.

"......"

With a single step, Lee Sungmin created dozens of afterimages. These were not mere illusions. Imbued with the incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the afterimages became shadows of the thunder cloud, each one capable of killing an opponent on its own.

KWAREUREUNG!

The afterimages shattered by the whip exploded, shooting lightning in all directions. From there, the martial art continued. With another step, Two Step Calamity, thunderclap was formed.

KWAAAAANG!

With a sound like dozens of bombs exploding at once, the Dark Supreme's whip burned away without a trace. He raised a powerful Protective Qi Aura to withstand the shock of the explosion, but it was not over yet.

The Dark Supreme's hands moved continuously, tirelessly. With each movement, a different type of hidden weapon appeared in his grasp, and he threw it the instant it materialized. Each differently shaped weapon had one of dozens of ways to be used.

*Too tough!*

Dozens of hidden weapons, dozens of ways to use them, and not a single one worked. He was continuously using the insights of the Transcendent Realm, distorting space to attack from completely different directions, using the distorted space as a slash to increase his power, and launching weapons that targeted all directions simultaneously.

However, he could not break through. Even when he penetrated the Protective Qi Aura, he could not pierce the armor.

Lee Sungmin was grateful to Mackendor and Celgerus. The armor, forged by generously feeding it the liquor from the gourd said to be the Void Monarch's treasure, was more than proving its worth.

[You son of a bitch. You didn't even take it out when I begged you for a drink.]

The Void Monarch grumbled, but Lee Sungmin ignored him.

Even as his repeatedly thrown weapons had no effect, the Dark Supreme did not stop. He knew very well that it was impossible to shake Lee Sungmin's pursuit and escape. No matter how unpredictable his movement arts were, he could not completely evade Lee Sungmin's notice at this distance.

*It's one of two things.*

The Dark Supreme's eyes grew calm. Either he would kill the Phantom Spear, or he would die. Understanding this with certainty, his heart grew surprisingly serene. How long had it been since he had used his martial arts with all his strength? A thirst he hadn't even been aware of, one that had built up for a long time, was finally being quenched.

Yes, that was enough. He had already lived more than long enough. He had ended up in this situation because of his great-granddaughter's foolish actions, but...

*Still... that child escaped.*

The Dark Supreme spread his arms wide. The distance to Lee Sungmin was short, a gap that could be closed in an instant. If he didn't dodge, he would die. Blocking was impossible. That spear, with its brutish power, would pierce his Protective Qi Aura as easily as tofu. He knew this, but he did not dodge.

The Dark Supreme's hands moved, creating afterimages until hundreds of them formed along their trajectory. Those hundreds of pairs of arms each held a different hidden weapon. In that state, the Dark Supreme looked like a Thousand-Armed Bodhisattva holding a thousand deadly instruments.

It was coming. Lee Sungmin knew exactly what this heralded. In Ruvus, he had nearly died to that very martial art.

Flower Rain. In a fleeting moment, a fraction of a fraction of a second, a thousand hidden weapons were thrown simultaneously. It was an attack that made defense meaningless. No matter how tough his armor was or how dense his Protective Qi Aura, the Flower Rain, filled with the insights of the Transcendent Realm, was the strongest martial art the Dark Supreme could unleash.

Lee Sungmin knew it too. He could not rely solely on the toughness of his armor as he had before. He pulled his left foot back and raised his spear. The hidden weapons would overwhelm him in an instant. He had to respond immediately.

Deadly Flash Lightning.

The fastest spear he could thrust. Light flashed twice, intercepting the nearest hidden weapons, and Lee Sungmin slid his back foot forward again. He immediately transitioned from Deathly Flash to Homing Chain Lightning.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear: Third Form, Homing Chain Lightning Flash. He mixed in the Bloody Pursuit of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art as well.

PPAJIJIK!

The purple lightning covering the spear erupted with a violent light and a series of explosive sounds. It was a single thrust, but the purple enhanced qi covering it split into dozens of bundles and shot forward. That was not the end. He retrieved the extended spear and thrust it again, repeating the motion and firing the enhanced qi once more. Dozens of streams of purple lightning became hundreds as he thrust the spear dozens of times.

Flower Rain and Homing Chain Lightning Flash collided head-on. The Dark Supreme looked at the swelling purple thunder cloud and raised both his hands again. There were no hidden weapons in them. Instead, he held a folding fan.

CHWAAK!

Flicking the fan open with a gesture, the Dark Supreme half-closed his eyes.

*Come.*

Three steps were needed. One step to raise the thunder cloud. Two steps to swell the thunder cloud. Three steps to detonate the thunder cloud. Those three steps were the Three Step Fatality of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break that Lee Sungmin had originally learned. It was a martial art that had always possessed a power befitting its deadly name, but this technique also incorporated the incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

Three Steps Certain Kill Heavenly Roar.

KWAAAAANG!

The thunder cloud exploded, and the hundreds of gathered streams of purple lightning swept through the area.

The Dark Supreme did not dodge. He roared and swung his fan.

KWAREUREUREUNG!

As the fan swung, a great wind arose. It twisted and tangled, changing the direction of the surging purple lightning. The fan caught fire. Without hesitation, the Dark Supreme ignited his origin energy. He had changed the flow of the purple lightning with a single swing, creating the only opening he would get.

The Dark Supreme pulled a pure white dagger from his robes. He had already used all the hidden weapons he possessed. This was the last one he could use.

*I have to break through. I will die here.*

He could not change that outcome. However, the Dark Supreme intended to do the best he could in this moment.

*The Martial God.*

The Dark Supreme kicked off the ground and leaped. He threw himself into the lightning, a place overflowing with death and exploding with light. The brilliant light stole his vision. The Dark Supreme knew his eyes would never see anything again. His Protective Qi Aura burned away, then his skin, then his muscles. His nerves, however, were still connected.

The hand holding the dagger moved. His eyes, burned by the light, could see nothing but pure white. In such a world, the Dark Supreme thought of the Martial God.

After his devastating defeat at the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he had wandered. During that time, he met the Martial God and entered Heaven Beyond Heaven. The world the Martial God sought to create. The ideal of Heaven Beyond Heaven. A world without monsters, where only humans lived.

In truth, most of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens knew that the Martial God's words were absurd. They sensed that his ideal was a lie and that something else lay beneath it. It didn't matter either way. The Six Honored Ones were strong, and the Martial God was the sky itself. They knew that if they followed him, they would enjoy power in a position befitting their strength.

The Dark Supreme was different. It was a monster, not a human, that had killed his daughter who resembled Tang Ahee. He hated monsters. He hated Demonic Spirits. The Martial God's ideal of creating a world without them was the same as his own.

*I wanted to see the world you would create. I will die here. However, I believe my actions can contribute to the world you seek to create. To your ideal. This one is dangerous. He's a bastard who became this strong in just a few years. Perhaps... this one. He might one day grow strong enough to threaten you. So I must stop him here.*

Lee Sungmin watched the Dark Supreme charge in, even igniting his origin energy, and raised his spear. Having lost his sight, the Dark Supreme targeted Lee Sungmin on instinct alone. Lee Sungmin saw the pure white dagger in his hand. It was no ordinary weapon. Even amidst the enhanced qi that was incinerating the Dark Supreme's body, the dagger did not lose its shape and emitted a white light.

[It would be best not to get hit by that,] the Void Monarch warned.

The light died down. The purple lightning that had raged as if to blow away the entire area subsided, leaving only residual crackles circling around Lee Sungmin. He slowly lowered the spear he had extended.

The Dark Supreme was hanging from its tip. Lee Sungmin's spear had pierced his heart precisely. Even with his heart pierced, the Dark Supreme tried to swing the dagger to stab Lee Sungmin, but his weapon ultimately failed to reach its target.

Lee Sungmin lifted the Dark Supreme's right arm, which he was holding in his left hand. The arm, twisted and torn off from the elbow, didn't even bleed. The dagger, tightly gripped in the dead man's hand, looked more like an awl than a dagger. No, to be precise, it looked like a white tree branch that had been carelessly broken off. Lee Sungmin couldn't tell exactly what it was, but he tucked it into his robes for now.

Then he approached the corpse. The body of the Dark Supreme, burned from head to toe, no longer resembled its former self. The determined expression of one who had resolved to die was also gone. Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, looking down at the dead man.

With this, Lee Sungmin had personally killed three of the Six Honored Ones. The ones remaining were the Blade Supreme, the Spear King, and the Moon Queen, and at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven stood the Martial God.

A slight regret surfaced, a fleeting thought that perhaps he shouldn't have killed him like this. He had heard about Heaven Beyond Heaven from the Fist Honored One, but he might have been able to hear a different story from the Dark Supreme.

*It's too late to think about that now.*

Feeling that regret, Lee Sungmin turned away. He hadn't managed to get any information, but he had succeeded in killing his target as planned. Now, all he had to do was leave this place and return to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

He had no desire to create more friction with the Tang Clan. In fact, there was no need to even call it that. The destruction that had spread from his fight with the Dark Supreme had completely wiped the Tang Clan's buildings from the face of the earth. Those who couldn't escape in time were likely struck by the stray power and killed.

Tang Ahee and her family had survived. For them, who didn't know the circumstances, it was like being struck by lightning on a clear day. On the night of their daughter's birthday, the clan's main house had been blown away.

"What... in the world..." The head of the Tang Clan, Poison Star Tang Jiwoo, was at a loss for words as he looked at the site of the building, where only black ash remained.

Beside him, Tang Ahee stomped her feet, not knowing what to do. She had never imagined things would turn out like this.

"Over there, there's a body," one of the surviving Tang Clan warriors reported as he approached Tang Jiwoo.

It was the corpse of Tang Jiwoo's grandfather, Tang Mugi, but the body was so damaged that not a trace of his living appearance remained.

"Whose body is this?"

"I don't know."

"Are you certain it was the Phantom Spear who attacked?" Tang Jiwoo asked, looking at Tang Ahee.

She flinched inwardly but nodded her head. "Yes? Oh, yes. The Phantom Spear. That evil bastard suddenly attacked the annex."

"Why would that vicious demonic head attack you... no, why would he cause such a disturbance in the Tang Clan?"

"It looks like someone fought against the Phantom Spear," Tang Ahee's uncle said. "Who on earth was fighting him?"

Tang Ahee shook her head. "Well... I'm not sure either."

She had no intention of talking about her great-grandfather, the Dark Supreme. If she spoke up, she would be scolded for not saying anything until now, and she thought she might be blamed for the disaster that had befallen the clan.

"Could this be the Phantom Spear's body?"

"But there's no spear..."

"The Phantom Spear is a great master. I don't think he would have died..."

The gathered people murmured among themselves. Tang Ahee frowned as she looked down at the charred corpse. She was thinking whatever was most convenient for her. That wasn't her great-grandfather's body. Her great-grandfather was a great master, after all. He must have fought here and then withdrawn. She didn't entertain for even a moment the thought that the Phantom Spear could have killed him.

*How unlucky. Looks like he couldn't escape in time and died,* Tang Ahee thought, looking at the Dark Supreme's corpse. She was convinced that the body belonged to one of her servants.
