"Aaaaaah!"

Baek Sogo screamed, her body twisting in a desperate struggle. She wanted to run, to flee this room immediately, but the acupoint sealing that restrained her had stolen all freedom of movement. Beside her, Jang Deuksu fidgeted with anxiety while Chwi Geol simply stared at her with a gloomy expression.

"Just why!" Baek Sogo shouted, her voice raw and hoarse. She glared at Chwi Geol, her eyes burning with resentment.

He let out a small sigh under her furious gaze and opened his mouth to speak, but she spat her words out before he could form a single one.

"Why did you bring me out?!"

The voice that ripped from her throat sounded nothing like her own, ravaged as it was from screaming.

"My Junior Disciple... my Junior Disciple. My only... Junior Disciple. I have to save him. He faced that monster in my place! To save me, my Junior Disciple... he confronted that monster. I should have gone with you all to save him, I should have...!"

The words tumbled out of her, a frantic, broken mess. Though her speech was disjointed, her meaning was perfectly clear, and the seething emotion behind it was impossible to miss. It was only natural that she resented them. Even the clueless Jang Deuksu understood this much and kept his silence, closing his eyes and bowing his head. He at least knew shame.

Chwi Geol, however, was different. It was not that he was shameless, but he refused to avoid Baek Sogo's eyes. He could not. He should not.

It was Chwi Geol who had chosen to run. He was the one who had sealed Baek Sogo's acupoints in a surprise attack, who had persuaded a shocked Jang Deuksu when he demanded to know what he was doing. He was the one who had used the scroll taken from the Blood Granny to escape the Dungeon. The scroll's performance had been outstanding. It had carried them across a great distance, teleporting them directly to the Beggars' Sect Main Branch in the city of Kron.

Kron was a city located a world away from the Dungeon, home not only to the main branch of the Beggars' Gang but also to the Murim Alliance itself. Such an ultra-long-distance teleportation was a feat of magic impossible for humans. Even those revered as archmages found teleportation on a city-wide scale to be beyond their power, but the scroll, an item obtained from the Dungeon, was a thing that deviated far from common sense, making the incredible journey possible.

"...First, please calm down," Chwi Geol said with a sigh. He knew such words would do nothing to soothe her, and he was right. At his plea, Baek Sogo's eyes only widened as she glared at him.

"First, Miss Baek, you need to understand the reality of the situation. This is the Beggars' Sect Main Branch in Kron. It would take a month to return to that Dungeon from here, even if you used your movement arts at full power."

"With magic..."

"We were able to teleport here in an instant thanks to the scroll from the Dungeon. No matter what mage you find, they won't be able to transport you back to that Dungeon in the time you want."

"Why did you run away...?!" Baek Sogo spat, gritting her teeth. If her body were not sealed, she would have already rushed out of this place, reality be damned. She would have gone to every Mages Guild in Kron, begging for a teleport.

"Didn't I tell you?" Chwi Geol said, his voice still gloomy. "You need to understand reality."

"The three of us, even if we joined forces with your senior, the Phantom Spear, had no guarantee we could defeat Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger."

"We didn't even try...!"

"And we are alive because we didn't try. I... Miss Baek, I couldn't die. I wasn't allowed to die. The same was true for Jang Deuksu."

"Then you should have left me behind," Baek Sogo snarled, her glare piercing him.

Chwi Geol felt the pain of her gaze. In all the time he had known and admired her, he had never once received such a look. He had to endure it. He had done something to deserve it.

"I couldn't do that."

"...Why?"

"Because I didn't want to see you die, Miss Baek."

At those words, Baek Sogo fell silent. She stared at Chwi Geol for a long moment before her lips moved. A hoarse, broken laugh escaped her.

"You're a coward." Her voice was filled with lament. "Cowardly, cowardly... so... cowardly. Is this the Murim Alliance? Is this chivalry? We followed Wiji Hoyeon into the Dungeon to monitor her, to stop her from gaining great power and becoming a truly uncontrollable monster, but we couldn't stop her. No, we didn't stop her."

"We had to do what we could. Are you saying that fighting Wiji Hoyeon there and dying is chivalry?"

"At least it would have been more chivalrous than running away."

"That's recklessness, Miss Baek. I don't believe challenging Wiji Hoyeon and dying is chivalry. If we live, as long as we live, we can perform many more acts of chivalry."

"...Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo muttered in a small voice. "Is standing by and watching my Junior Disciple die... also chivalry?"

"...I believe it can be a part of it."

Absurd words. Hypocrisy. Baek Sogo muttered the thoughts in a small voice. Jang Deuksu bit his lower lip, and Chwi Geol offered no reply.

"The Phantom Spear was a hero."

"Don't say it like that."

"...Then what should I say?"

"...Release the acupoint sealing," Baek Sogo said, her glare fixed on Chwi Geol. "I can't be with you. I don't want to. Don't you... don't you realize your words are hypocritical?"

"I'm just talking about reality."

"I joined the Murim Alliance for my ideals."

"And what are these ideals you cherish, Miss Baek? To eradicate evil in this world? A world where people are summoned every day, without knowing where they're from or what ideologies they hold? That's impossible."

Baek Sogo didn't answer. She only glared at him through tear-stained eyes. Feeling the sting of her gaze, Chwi Geol forced himself to continue. He had to.

"Everyone... probably has ideals, but living according to those ideals is impossible. So we compromise. Because we know reality."

"I'm quitting."

She knew. She knew she could never achieve her ideals. Reality was too overwhelming to live by ideals alone, so one compromised.

"I know it too. I know the ideals I wish for are absurd. Nevertheless, I joined the Murim Alliance because I thought this place was the closest match to my ideals, but... not anymore."

"...My opinion is not the opinion of the entire Murim Alliance."

"Release the acupoint sealing," Baek Sogo repeated.

Chwi Geol's mouth tightened. He approached her and released the seal. She staggered to her feet, letting out a deep breath as she looked down at her own body. Then, without a moment's hesitation, her hand whipped through the air and slapped Chwi Geol across the cheek. The sound was loud and sharp as his head snapped to the side.

Jang Deuksu jumped. "Wh-where are you going?"

"I don't want to be here," Baek Sogo spat. "It's disgusting."

Chwi Geol didn't try to stop her as she left the room. He raised a hand to his throbbing cheek. It was far too painful to be a simple slap. He let out a hollow chuckle.

"Did I just get dumped?" he asked, his voice sounding as if a screw had come loose.

As Wiji Hoyeon exited the treasure vault, the Dungeon sealed itself shut completely. Lee Sungmin naturally found himself outside along with it. A pang of regret hit him for not being able to talk more with her, but he could not let himself be swayed by it. One year was not a long time.

He found an inn in a nearby village and collapsed onto the bed.

It hurt.

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, pressing down hard on his arm. It seemed he would have to lie as still as a mouse for the next few days. He stared up at the old ceiling, mulling over the recent events. He had prevented Baek Sogo's death. Still, he needed to be sure. He looked down at his shadow and called for Nebul.

"......"

Nebul rose from the darkness. He looked a little surprised to see Lee Sungmin's fatigue-ridden face but asked no questions.

Lee Sungmin met his gaze and said, "Please find information on the Silent Flash."

"What kind of information do you want?"

"Her whereabouts."

"Understood. I'll make inquiries through the information Guild. Is there... anything else you require?"

Nebul's gaze shifted to the spear placed beside the bed. It was a new weapon he had received not long ago, but it was now in a terrible state. Nebul sighed.

"My, my... Celgerus will be angry. To think a weapon painstakingly crafted by a Meister would be shattered like that. Did you hunt a Dragon or something?"

"Of course not."

"It was a joke. If your opponent had been a Dragon, you would already be dead, Lee Sungmin. If you wish, I can connect you with Celgerus."

"...Ah, before that. Do you happen to know what this is?"

Lee Sungmin reached into his Subspace Pocket. He took out a fist-sized ore, the one he had obtained from defeating Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger. Seeing it, Nebul's eyes widened.

"May I... take a look?" Nebul asked cautiously.

It was not a difficult request, so Lee Sungmin handed the ore over. Nebul examined it with a careful eye, then let out a cry of admiration.

"Good heavens. Where on earth did you get this?"

"...What is it?"

"This is Orichalcum," Nebul said, his eyes shining.

At those words, Lee Sungmin's jaw dropped in surprise. He knew of the metal called Orichalcum. It was one of the rarest minerals among the countless ones existing in Eria.

"I'm not a blacksmith, so I can't say for sure, but in my judgment, this Orichalcum is of the highest quality with almost no impurities. If you were to sell it, you could probably buy several fortresses."

"Is that so?"

He was surprised, but that was all. Lee Sungmin calmed his heart. In his past life, it was a mineral he had only heard rumors about, something he had never encountered. Now it belonged to him. There was nothing to be so surprised about.

"This is perfect timing. I can't use my old spear anymore. I should ask Celgerus to craft a weapon with this Orichalcum."

"Shall I connect you right away?"

"......"

At Lee Sungmin's silent affirmation, Nebul immediately vanished. Not long after, the space in front of Lee Sungmin split open with a CRACK, connecting to Celgerus's workshop.

"Orichalcum?!" Celgerus screamed the moment his face appeared. Not a trace of his usual languor remained. He looked at Lee Sungmin with a very excited expression. "Is it true? You have Orichalcum, and an unrefined ore at that?"

"Ah... yes, and I'm sorry, but the spear I have..."

"I don't care about that!" Celgerus shouted. Lee Sungmin was surprised the man could even produce such a loud voice. "Give it here. I have to see it for myself."

"Ah... yes."

Lee Sungmin handed the Orichalcum ore to Nebul, who had reappeared beside him. Celgerus received the ore through Nebul and exclaimed repeatedly, scanning it with wide eyes.

"So... I'd like to request you make a spear with it. The size isn't very big, but I wonder if it's possible..."

"Don't be ridiculous," Celgerus scoffed at Lee Sungmin's words. He grabbed the ore with both hands and infused it with magic power. The fist-sized piece of Orichalcum swelled up. "With Orichalcum this free of impurities, one spear is no problem. In fact, there'll be some left over."

"In that case, you can have the remaining Orichalcum as payment for your work."

When Lee Sungmin said that without much thought, Celgerus's eyes widened as he looked at him.

"Are you crazy?"

"...Huh?"

"The rest as payment? What nonsense! You really don't seem to know, but with Orichalcum of this quality, any blacksmith would agree to craft it for free. Orichalcum is just that rare of a metal."

"But to ask for free..."

"No, it's fine. I don't need payment. This is my first time handling unrefined Orichalcum, too. How about I make you a secondary weapon instead of a spear? With this much Orichalcum, I could make a few daggers."

"In that case... I'd like to ask for that..."

[No. Instead of daggers, repair this armor.]

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to answer, the Void Monarch interjected.

[This armor is usable as a medium, but it still can't fully handle my power. Orichalcum has excellent magic power conductivity, so it should be able to handle my power better than before.]

"...Instead of daggers, could you repair this armor with the Orichalcum?"

"Doing the whole thing would be difficult...?"

[Tell him it doesn't matter.]

"It doesn't matter."

There was no point in having Orichalcum daggers anyway. It would be better to repair the Magic Armor with the Orichalcum as the Void Monarch suggested, rather than having daggers he would never even use.

*But. Is it okay to reforge the Magic Armor with you inside it?*

[I just need to move my residence for a while. That broken spear is also made of Mithril, so it should be able to contain my soul for a short time.]

Lee Sungmin asked for Celgerus's understanding and then grabbed the spear. The Magic Armor hummed as a hazy, smoke-like substance stretched out from it and seeped into the weapon.

[It is done.]

The spear hummed. Lee Sungmin took off the Magic Armor and handed it to Nebul.

"...You are commanding a strange being," Celgerus noted.

[Commanding? This bastard wants to die.]

The Void Monarch growled, but neither Celgerus nor Nebul could hear him. Something suddenly occurred to Lee Sungmin, and he made another request to Nebul.

"I'd also like to request information on the Void Monarch."

"The Void Monarch...?"

"Yes. I heard he was a Yokai who made a name for himself several hundred years ago."

"Understood. I will deliver it along with the information on the Silent Flash."

With that, his business with Erebrisa was finished. Lee Sungmin put down the spear and flopped onto the bed.

"...I'm tired," he muttered as he closed his eyes.
