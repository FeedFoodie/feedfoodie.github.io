The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman lowered his outstretched palm, standing amidst a field of fallen Murim Alliance warriors. They lay sprawled around him, all of them unconscious with foam frothing at their mouths.

None were dead. This was not an act of mercy, but a testament to the Chairman's power. Subduing them all without exception was a far more difficult feat than simply killing them. Choosing the intricate path of subjugation over the easy one of annihilation was his way of wringing some small enjoyment from this tedious situation.

"It seems he hasn't run away," the Chairman muttered, his gaze lifting to the roof of the Murim Alliance's main hall.

While Lee Sungmin could not sense it clearly, the Chairman could. A presence lingered on the roof, and a faint smile touched his lips. He was somewhat satisfied by the aura of the Black Dragon Knight, feeling this was a worthy opponent to demonstrate his dignity as a Master to his disciple.

"Do you have any requests?" the Chairman asked, glancing over at Lee Sungmin.

Requests? Lee Sungmin tilted his head, confused by the question. The Chairman chuckled heartily.

"I'm talking about when I beat down the Black Dragon Knight. If there's anything you want, I'll do my best to make it happen."

"...Can you subdue him within ten seconds?"

"Ten seconds? Hmm." The Chairman's brow furrowed. He stroked his chin in thought for a moment before shaking his head. "I've never fought the fellow, so I can't guarantee a Ten Exchanges Match. However, I'll give it a try."

It was an incredibly arrogant statement. The Black Dragon Knight was a master on par with the Spear King, a tier just below titans like the Chairman, the Martial God, or the Sword Immortal. Moreover, his half-dragon heritage granted him immense physical strength that had nothing to do with his martial arts. Having been defeated by the Spear King, Lee Sungmin could only assume his own defeat if he were to face the Black Dragon Knight.

And yet, the Chairman was casually discussing a Ten Exchanges Match against such a foe. Even with the caveat that he couldn't guarantee it, Lee Sungmin recalled the divine martial prowess the man had displayed on their way here. Effortlessly shattering the Dog Beating Stick Formation had been incredible, but what truly amazed him was the Chairman's soulmates technique. It was different from the Dragon Pressure Lee Sungmin used. With inner qi alone, he had inflicted internal injuries on dozens, even hundreds of people, all while perfectly controlling its power and distinguishing between targets to pressure and those to spare. Lee Sungmin could not handle his own inner qi with such delicate precision. He could achieve something similar with Dragon's Pressure, but it would never be as refined.

"He's coming," the Chairman muttered, looking up.

A black robe fluttered as the Black Dragon Knight descended slowly, his eyes taking in the scene below. He saw the Murim Alliance warriors collapsed on the ground, foaming at the mouth. Among them were high-ranking executives.

*How useless...*

No, the Black Dragon Knight swallowed a sigh. It wasn't really their fault. The Murim Alliance was not teeming with masters as the public perceived. Eria was vast, and while the orthodox sects held enormous territory, their masters were dispatched far from the headquarters. The Nine Sects One Gang, the smaller sects, and the murim Noble Clans that formed the alliance did not abandon their own lands. In other words, the Murim Alliance building in Kron held little military power beyond its symbolic meaning. The true masters were in their own territories, and while most unaffiliated masters were registered with the alliance, not all of them remained here either.

*Even if they had been here, the result would not have changed.*

The ground grew closer. The Black Dragon Knight clenched his fists. Even if the full strength of the Murim Alliance and the Nine Sects One Gang had been gathered here, they could not have stopped the Chairman's advance. He was that kind of monster. Though he had never fought the man, the Black Dragon Knight could clearly sense the terrifying depths of his power. He compared him to the Martial God he had met a hundred years ago. If he measured the Chairman against the Martial God from back then...

*There's no comparison.*

He had not experienced how strong the Martial God had become after finishing his seclusion, but he could not help but feel a sense of awe toward the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*Is it possible for a pure human to possess such power?*

Pushing his awe aside, the Black Dragon Knight posed the question. "For what reason are you doing this?"

THUD.

He landed a short distance away, watching the Chairman with a wary expression. The Chairman, his hands still clasped behind his back, stared intently at the Black Dragon Knight's face.

"As far as I know, our relationship with the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is not yet a bad one. The Murim Alliance has not yet picked a fight with you."

That was true. They had attempted to contact Blood Lord Pae Munhu to manipulate the organization in the Chairman's absence, but aside from that, there had been no direct conflict. Pressuring the Phantom Spear? They were not in a righteous position on that matter, but was it significant enough for a monster like the Chairman to make a move himself?

"A reason," the Chairman muttered, unclasping his hands. He let them hang at his sides and shook his head. "The relationship between our alliances isn't the reason."

"Then... why?"

"You were simply the easiest opponent to pick on."

The Black Dragon Knight was left speechless. He could not comprehend the meaning behind the Chairman's words. No, he tried not to. He didn't want to. This was the Murim Alliance, and he was its Chairman. His opponent was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"Don't you consider our respective positions..."

"I don't," the Chairman cut him off.

"It's meaningless, isn't it?" he laughed heartily and strode forward. "Start counting from now."

He said it not to the Black Dragon Knight, but to Lee Sungmin, who swallowed hard. Lee Sungmin understood what he was meant to count. His question about ten seconds. The Chairman had said he couldn't guarantee it, but his actions now suggested he fully intended to try.

And he did. The Chairman's form vanished from Lee Sungmin's sight.

The Black Dragon Knight's martial skill was greater, allowing him to see what Lee Sungmin could not, but even he couldn't completely follow the Chairman's high-speed movement. Horrified, he hastily covered his entire body in Dragon scales and raised a Protective Qi Aura. He had never imagined the Chairman would attack without even a proper conversation.

It was too late.

KWAJIJIJIK!

With a gruesome sound, the Black Dragon Knight's body was sent flying backward. He couldn't even tell what had just happened. Through his shaking vision, he saw his own black scales tear off and turn to dust. He tried to speak, but hot, coppery blood rose in his throat.

He was hit... with what? When? He tried to regain his balance in mid-air.

KWADEUK!

His body was hurled again, this time in the opposite direction of his momentum. His back screamed as if it had been shattered. Once again, he had no time to raise his scales. His Protective Qi Aura was meaningless. Whatever he was being hit with, the Chairman's attacks were directly crushing his defenses to deliver the blow.

However, the Black Dragon Knight did not just stand there and take it. Fury rose within him at the sudden assault. He drew upon his inner qi, radiating a fierce killing intent.

KUWAAANG!

A black light swelled around him. Forming the dark energy into a sphere, the Black Dragon Knight, standing within it, barely managed to steady himself. His face contorted. He knew. The attacks that had come faster than his senses could track had abruptly stopped. The Chairman couldn't possibly be tired after just two hits.

He had been given an 'opening' to compose himself. Indeed, the Chairman stood watching him, casually brushing some dust from his shoulder.

"GRRRRR!"

The Black Dragon Knight's pupils narrowed into reptilian slits. They were not the eyes of a human, but of a Dragon.

AAAAAAH!

His shout became a Lion's Roar that shook the very space around them. No, it wasn't a Lion's Roar. A chill ran down Lee Sungmin's spine, and he clenched his fists, which were slick with cold sweat.

The dark light that had enveloped the Black Dragon Knight's body gathered into a single point. The Chairman stood leisurely, waiting for it.

-KWAAAAANG!

A great tremor and a deafening roar accompanied a brilliant beam of light. The Black Dragon Knight fired a breath attack with all his might. Though he was in his human form, that did not diminish the breath's power.

The Chairman raised his right hand toward the approaching blast.

CRACKLE!

A streak of electricity sparked from his fingertips. His gentle pushing gesture made contact with the breath.

It swelled and burst. The massive flow of power was torn to shreds by the Chairman's inner qi, which had penetrated the attack and unraveled it from within. The Black Dragon Knight watched with eyes of disbelief as his breath was futilely shattered, scattered, and extinguished.

A cruel smile played on the Chairman's lips.

"KEUK!"

A gasping sound escaped the Black Dragon Knight. He clenched his teeth, but blood burst from between his lips and from his nose. In an instant, the Chairman was right in front of him, his fist buried deep in his stomach. The scales and Protective Qi Aura were still meaningless. To the Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight's defenses were thinner and more fragile than paper.

Coughing up blood, the Black Dragon Knight swung both hands, his techniques shifting dazzlingly between palm and fist. From Lee Sungmin's perspective, there seemed to be no openings to penetrate.

For the Chairman, however, it was not so. A single punch was enough to pierce through the assault of ever-changing techniques. The Black Dragon Knight's chest caved in as if it had collapsed. As he staggered back, his eyes shook with terror. He raised his trembling arms. Both were bent at angles they should not have been. The Chairman's punch hadn't exploited an opening. It had simply pushed aside everything in its way and advanced.

*We're on a different level...*

They were both in the Transcendent Realm, yet this wasn't even a fight. He knew the Chairman was strong, but he never imagined this. Even Ju Won of Predator, who had endangered him before, would be no match for this man. The Spear King was out of the question. It would never happen, but the Black Dragon Knight was not confident he could win even if he, the Spear King, and Ju Won attacked the Chairman together.

"UAAAAAH!"

The overwhelming gap filled him with despair and humiliation. He was still a Dragon, even if only half. The pride flowing through his blood overthrew his reason.

Watching the Black Dragon Knight charge at him while screaming, the Chairman nodded to himself in satisfaction.

*It ended in under ten seconds.*

The Black Dragon Knight wasn't as strong as he thought. No, perhaps it would have been different if he were in a full fighting spirit state. The Chairman glanced at his opponent's side. His movements weren't natural. It seemed he had a serious injury there. Not that he had any intention of going easy on him because of it.

"......"

A dull thud reverberated at length inside the Black Dragon Knight's head. It was the last sound he heard.

"Finished in under ten seconds," the Chairman announced in a proud voice. The Black Dragon Knight, who had gone limp and lost consciousness, did not fall. The Chairman levitated his body with Space Manipulation and approached Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin was at a loss for words. Only after closing his mouth, which had been hanging half-open in shock, did he comprehend what he had just witnessed. The Chairman's movements hadn't been flashy. What had beaten the Black Dragon Knight to a pulp wasn't some brilliant enhanced qi art, but the Chairman's bare fists.

That was enough. Imbued with the Extreme Speed of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the Chairman's body was a weapon more powerful and faster than anything else in the world.

During the subjugation of Kim Jonghyun, Lee Sungmin had fled without facing the Black Dragon Knight, unconfident he could break his scales. The Chairman had shattered those Dragon scales with his bare hands. It was a level Lee Sungmin couldn't even dream of imitating.

"...What are you going to do now?"

"First, we leave," the Chairman said, then added, "Isn't that obvious?" He scanned his surroundings. The Murim Alliance warriors were all down. The Beggars' Gang members likely hadn't healed their internal injuries yet, so there would be no obstacles on their way back.

"Let's go to the carriage."

"What about the Black Dragon Knight?"

At those words, the Chairman glanced to the side. The Black Dragon Knight was floating in the air, unconscious, with blood trickling from his mouth.

"Let's take him with us."

"Pardon?"

"We need to interrogate him. If we question the bastard here, more insects will swarm us, so it's better to just take him."

*Won't that be dangerous?*

Lee Sungmin quietly swallowed the words that had risen to his throat. He now knew all too well that it couldn't possibly be.
