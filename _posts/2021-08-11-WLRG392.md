Pain was a familiar thing.

Or so he had thought. He had experienced many kinds of pain until now, and though the memory was faint, he had even died once before.

His life after returning from death had not been smooth. There was always an enemy, and he always fought. Lee Sungmin had never won a single fight with ease. Thinking back on it, every opponent he had ever faced had driven him to the very brink of death.

Still, he had never died. Lee Sungmin had always emerged victorious, even from such states, because the protection of the Destiny Force had allowed him to survive. The more he fought, the more his body became like that of a Yokai, and the regenerative power he gained healed even fatal wounds in an instant, forcing him to keep moving.

This regenerative power had become a curse. If only he still had a weak human body.

"Ah, ahhhhh…"

Then he would not be experiencing this kind of pain.

Jeniela had been right. One never grew used to pain. One's tolerance for it simply increased. Even when it hurt immensely, you stopped thinking of it as pain, not because you were used to it, but because your tolerance had grown. No matter how much it hurt, you started to think, *This much is nothing...* You endured it because you had already experienced this level of pain before. You told yourself this much was fine.

However, that was only until it surpassed a certain threshold, until it reached a realm of agony you had never felt before.

Jeniela was skilled at inflicting pain. Having lived for hundreds of years, she knew exactly how to make a human mind surrender and shatter through pure agony, and Lee Sungmin was the perfect toy for her, for he had a human mind that refused to break easily.

She had already ripped off his fingers and arms dozens of times, and each time, Lee Sungmin regenerated the wounds. The severed arm would reconstruct itself from the bone up. A perfect Yokai body and an overflow of Demonic Energy, all of it made Lee Sungmin refuse death.

[You damn bitch!] the Void Monarch roared, gritting his teeth as he felt the full weight of his own powerlessness. All he could do was continuously call Lee Sungmin's name to keep his mind from shattering while using his Demonic Energy to try and push Jeniela away.

[Lee Sungmin!] Rubia cried out, her voice choked with tears. She was just as helpless. She resided within Lee Sungmin's embrace for one reason, to block a fatal attack that would surely lead to his death. The blessing of absolute defense, granted by the vanished Spirit Queen who had once saved the Crazy Heavenly Demon, could only be used a single time. She had been holding her breath, waiting for the decisive moment to unleash it, but Lee Sungmin had been cornered like this before she could ever act.

"Get out of the way...!" Osla screamed as he charged forward. Wreathed in light, his attack looked quite powerful, and had it connected, it truly would have been.

"I told you," Jeniela said, lowering her outstretched hand. She giggled as she looked at Osla, whose body was now pierced by dozens of spikes. "You're not enough. You're just too weak."

"Ah… ugh…"

"You're an Immortal, so are you used to pain? You don't really look like it… Why? Did you think you could actually do something to me in this forest? Ahaha, that's just too arrogant. Where did you get that confidence? True, you've lived longer than me, but you've spent all that long time in this peaceful forest, playing house with the mischievous Fairy Realms. How could you possibly stop me?"

Osla writhed as he listened to her taunts. Excruciating pain turned his mind white with every movement.

Coalition Leader, Coalition Leader…

Osla called the name in a sobbing voice.

"He's dead." Jeniela snapped her fingers, clearly not wanting to hear any whining.

PWAK!

A red spike shot up from under Osla's chin and pierced straight through his head.

"If you whine and call for the dead Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, is he going to come back to life? Ah, sorry, my apologies. He was your Master, wasn't he?" Jeniela stopped sneering and looked down at Lee Sungmin. "He was a foolish human. I was kind enough to tell him he would die soon, but even after hearing about his own death, he didn't run away. Did he really think that was brave?"

"Sh... ut... up...!" Lee Sungmin spat out, vomiting blood.

Jeniela cackled and easily swatted away the Demonic Energy with her hand. "Void Monarch, please. You should have learned by now. As just a soul, you can't do anything. You think I don't know? You've lingered for too long. You're already at your limit, aren't you?"

[Shut up.]

The Void Monarch's Demonic Energy surged. *Did I hit a nerve?* Jeniela giggled. "Of course you're at your limit. It's been hundreds of years since you died. Even for a Yokai with immense power, to remain as just a soul for all those years… you're barely clinging to this world by scraping together the remnants of the Demonic Energy you once wielded. Why have you even stayed this long? Do you have some lingering attachment to the world?"

[Shut up...!]

Jeniela was right. The Void Monarch had lingered in this world for far too long. The moment he left the Sleeping Forest, his limit had approached with terrifying speed. If he had not absorbed the remnants of his old Demonic Energy in the South, he would have hit that limit even sooner.

"You can't even forcibly move that helpless body now, can you."

*Damn bitch. I should have killed her 400 years ago.* The Void Monarch ground his teeth in regret. That was right, too. Before, he could have briefly moved Lee Sungmin's body while he was unconscious. Now that he had reached his limit for remaining in the world, even that was impossible.

"I wonder what you're hoping to see by staying in this world."

The Void Monarch was not speaking to Jeniela.

[Are you listening?]

Lee Sungmin listened blankly to the Void Monarch's voice. It hurt. It hurt so, so much. His entire body was a canvas of agony. His thoughts would not connect properly, a problem made worse by the red spike piercing through his eye.

[Listening to her, she's not wrong about a single thing. Why the hell am I still in this world, hoping to see something good?] The Void Monarch chuckled darkly. [I could have left this world over ten years ago, but I didn't. I wanted to see your end, not mine. I wanted to see... what you would do. I was curious if you could really save this dog-shit world with your struggles.]

Are you listening? the Void Monarch asked again.

[You tried hard. For someone like you, that is. You're one unlucky bastard. Does it hurt a lot?]

*Yeah.*

[What are you going to do? Your plan failed. Even fighting in this forest, you couldn't defeat that crazy bitch. It can't be helped. Jeniela was much stronger than you thought.]

What are you going to do? the Void Monarch asked once more.

[You did your best. If any son of a bitch scoffs at you, saying this is all you could do, I'll kill that bastard myself. You worked hard. You did everything you could. Still... sometimes things are just like this. No matter how hard you try, some things are just impossible. It's okay to fail.]

Trying your best did not guarantee success.

[After all this... I think it's okay to give up.]

*Haha... that's not like you.*

[I'm saying this... because I feel sorry for you.]

Because I pity you.

Lee Sungmin chuckled darkly at the Void Monarch's added words.

"Good," Jeniela said, licking her lower lip. "It seems you can endure this level of pain. I'm bored of pulling out fingers and eyes now. Shall we have a little more fun? How about we go through your intestines? Have you ever had a good look at your organs? Your stomach, your liver, your large intestine... Did you know? The large intestine is incredibly long. I wonder how long yours is."

Or.

[…Let go of your consciousness,] the Void Monarch advised.

"Maybe I'll torment someone else instead of you."

[Don't listen.]

Jeniela straightened up from her crouched position. His senses were sharp. A scent wafted over, not the coppery smell of spilled blood, but something different. Lee Sungmin knew that scent.

Don't feel it, the Void Monarch said again. His voice was trembling. He was genuinely saying this for Lee Sungmin's sake, so that he would not fall deeper into despair, so that he would not scream.

[This is too cruel.]

*I know.*

She always had this scent about her. The scent of some nameless wildflower.

Lee Sungmin's fingertips trembled violently.

Do you want to see? Jeniela asked with a smile. Her magic forcibly twisted Lee Sungmin's head to the side. He widened his one remaining eye and saw Jeniela standing there, her shoulders shaking with laughter and her feet tapping lightly in anticipation. In front of her stood Baek Sogo, her face a deathly pale.

She had come after killing Kun. With his death, his power would return to Jeniela, but Baek Sogo had thought it would be fine if she joined her Junior Disciple to fight. Kun was strong, and the full moon had made his power and regeneration even stronger, which was why it took longer than she expected. It had not been a difficult task, however. His attacks were not fatal, nor were they as fast as hers.

It had been easier than she thought. On her way here, she had believed the fight with Jeniela would also be easier than expected. She believed that if she fought alongside her Junior Disciple, it would be possible to defeat even the notoriously powerful Vampire Queen.

"Jun... ior Disciple...?"

"Don't worry," Jeniela answered, not Lee Sungmin. "Even in that state, he's alive. He's still regenerating even now. Isn't it amazing?"

"Junior Disciple…"

"He can hear you calling him. Why? Do you want to hear your Junior Disciple's voice? If so, I'll help you out." Jeniela lifted her hand and flicked her fingers. A red spike shot up from the floor, piercing Lee Sungmin's stomach. His coiled intestines rose right up to his nose.

"Aaaaaargh!"

A scream erupted from Lee Sungmin's mouth. *Senior Sister, please.* Lee Sungmin bit his trembling lips.

Run.

He mouthed the word. The Void Monarch said nothing more. *I didn't want you to see him like this.* The monarch's mutter sounded distant.

"Junior Disciple!" Baek Sogo yelled, her mind going blank. Her feet kicked off the ground as the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, unfolding as naturally as breathing, moved her body. She glared and charged toward Jeniela.

"Please, Senior Sister," Lee Sungmin called out to her, his voice choked with tears. *You can never defeat that monster. Run, you have to run.* His consciousness wavered, feeling as if it was about to shatter.

"Mm." Jeniela let out a soft laugh.

Baek Sogo's hand, reaching for Jeniela's throat, was caught. Jeniela smiled gently as she twisted and broke Baek Sogo's wrist. Without resisting, Baek Sogo rotated her airborne body to prevent the break, and in the process, her other hand sliced off Jeniela's wrist.

Blood sprayed from the severed limb. Lee Sungmin watched Baek Sogo, tears of blood streaming down his face. Their eyes met.

*Why are you crying?* Baek Sogo thought. *Does it hurt a lot?*

*Don't worry, Junior Disciple. It's okay. Just like you saved me from crises many times, this time, I can save you.*

Baek Sogo's hand shot out, her open palm flying toward the face of the grinning Jeniela.

"Please."

"Hm?" Jeniela turned her head to look at Lee Sungmin. "What did you say?"

Baek Sogo collapsed to the ground. She blinked blankly, watching the blood drip down as Jeniela pulled her hand out of her stomach.

"Why didn't you say so sooner? I could have stopped at the right time."

"Ah… ugh…" Blood dripped from Baek Sogo's mouth.

"Aaaaaah!" Lee Sungmin roared, vomiting blood as his regenerated body moved. Before it could fully obey him, the magic Jeniela flicked at him blew his head apart.

"It's okay, it's okay. I won't kill her. Yes, not yet., but humans were a pain. Jeniela took out a bottle of Elixir and poured it on Baek Sogo's gaping wound. "It's hard to control my strength," she muttered as she raised her gaze.

"Are you just going to watch?" Jeniela threw the question to those hidden in the darkness.
