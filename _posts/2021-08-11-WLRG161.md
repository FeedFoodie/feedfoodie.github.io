"Keuk."

A choked gasp escaped Aladur as blood spurted from his mouth. His disbelieving gaze dropped to the hand impaling his chest, then flickered to the figure behind him. The hand was brutally ripped free. As a torrent of blood gushed from the wound, Aladur's body staggered.

"I told you... fuck..." Aladur spat, his voice trailing off as his face crumpled in devastation.

"This forest is fucked up..."

With those final words, he pitched forward.

Aaaargh!

A collective scream tore from the throats of the watching martial artists, all composure forgotten. The killer, a pale-faced man, licked the blood from his hand and met their terrified gazes.

"So many," the Red Ghost muttered. He had been wandering the forest since his encounter with Lee Sungmin when he stumbled upon the gathered martial artists of the Noble Clan. There was no reason to hesitate. Yokai ate humans, just as monsters did, but for a different reason. It was not simply to fill their stomachs. To kill and eat a human was to consume the fear they felt as they died, reduced to prey. For a Yokai, this act provided not only satiety but also power.

"The first time I've seen so many humans since settling in this forest," the Red Ghost sneered, muttering to himself. "Such fearless fools."

For him, the situation could not have been better. Ever since Yana had torn out his heart, he had been continuously weakening, slowly dying. He had settled in this forest to halt death's progression, but his meager skills had only slowed the process, not stopped it. That was why he needed this. He needed human fear. The more terrified they were, the more power he would gain when he devoured them. Though Yana had defeated him and driven him from Urmuri, the Red Ghost had not given up. If he could recover his strength here and claim the forest's Demonic Energy, defeating Yana would be a simple task.

"Are you afraid?" the Red Ghost asked, his eyes sweeping over the faces filling with terror. Fear was contagious, and with Aladur's sudden death, it spread like a plague across the faces of the Noble Clan. The Red Ghost found the sight both satisfying and enjoyable. He may have been weakened, but the power of a former Predator pinnacle was more than enough to kill them all.

"You... who are you?" Jaegal Taeryeong demanded, scrambling to his feet and drawing his sword in a desperate attempt to hide his fear.

The Red Ghost stared blankly at him. "Red Ghost."

"Red Ghost...?"

The name meant nothing to Jaegal Taeryeong, but Namgung Heewon was different. He had heard of the Yokai in the Bewitching Forest from Erebrisa.

"Draw your swords!" Namgung Heewon roared.

At his cry, the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps drew their blades as one. Jaegal Taeryeong belatedly shouted the same order to the Dark Illusion Blade Brigade.

"Resistance is good," the Red Ghost said, baring his teeth. "The more it's broken, the more humans feel despair and fear. It's been a long time since I've feasted. Entertain me."

As he spoke, Tang Ahee fumbled to retrieve her hidden weapons while the Dark Night Black Mist Squad scattered in all directions. Dozens of projectiles shot toward the Red Ghost.

"......"

His back split open and a thick carapace emerged, deflecting the weapons with ease.

"Profound Illusion Killing Sword Formation!" Jaegal Taeryeong bellowed. The Dark Illusion Blade Brigade moved to obey, though their dwindled numbers made it a shadow of its true form. Namgung Heewon's eyes darted to Moyong Seojin, who stood frozen, sword in hand, clearly unsure of what to do.

The Red Ghost blurred into motion. He dashed forward, targeting a member of the Dark Night Black Mist Squad who was still throwing weapons. The man tried to evade with his movement arts, but the Red Ghost's hand shot out and clamped around his neck. With a demonstrative flex of his grip, he tore the man's head from his shoulders.

"......"

The head flew into the air.

"Hahaha!" the Red Ghost laughed, his mouth stretching to an impossible angle. He caught the falling head in a single bite.

CRUNCH!

The sound of bone crushing between his teeth sent a tremor through the Noble Clan's martial artists. Normally, they would not have been so terrified, but a week of suffering in this forest had left their minds brittle. Fear spread with terrifying speed.

"Uwaaah!" Jaegal Taeryeong screamed, trying to drown out the terror squirming in his heart as the Dark Illusion Blade Brigade charged. Their divine power was weak, their fear was palpable, and their numbers were broken. What should have been a precise, impenetrable sword formation was little more than crude swordplay.

The Red Ghost leaped into their midst, a whirlwind of slaughter. He did not kill them intentionally. Instead, he tore off arms and legs, listening with rapt attention to the screams of the maimed as they tumbled to the ground, crying out in pain and calling for their parents. Such sounds only amplified the fear, deepening the taste of blood and human flesh in his mouth. For a Yokai, a human body steeped in fear was the most delicious meal in the world.

Namgung Heewon was thinking of escape, not vengeance. His eyes found Moyong Seojin, and he resolved to grab him and flee at the first opportunity.

[T-take me with you.]

The quick-witted Tang Ahee's thoughts suddenly entered his mind.

[What?]

[I'll make the Dark Night Black Mist Squad charge to draw their attention. So take me with you!]

Namgung Heewon's face twisted in disgust. He could understand abandoning Jaegal Taeryeong, but to hear a member of the same Noble Clan so readily sacrifice her own people was sickening.

As the number of dead increased, the screams grew louder, and the fear amplified. The Red Ghost, lost in his bloody feast, was unaware of anything else. He knew this forest was steeped in the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy and had become a forest of Yokai, but that was the extent of his knowledge.

SHHH.

He did not notice the forest fall silent for a moment, nor did he see the figure that rose from among the corpses and the dying. The man who stood was completely naked, his presence as faint as a ghost, yet he was undeniably there. He had answered the call of the amplifying fear, manifesting to claim it for himself.

"What the hell is that...?" Jaegal Taeryeong, who had backed away from the slaughter to plot his own escape, was the first to see him. The naked man did not look at Jaegal Taeryeong. His emotionless eyes were fixed on the Red Ghost. To this newcomer, the Red Ghost was a scoundrel stealing his prey. Therefore, he would be killed. Whether his opponent was Yokai or human was of no importance.

"Hm?" The Red Ghost paused, licking blood and flesh from his hand, and turned. His eyes widened at the sight of the naked man striding toward him. He knew that appearance, which was why he could not help but be surprised.

"Void Monarch...?"

The man wore the face of the Void Monarch. The Red Ghost couldn't understand. He had seen the Void Monarch only a short while ago, a wretched thing that had lost its body, its soul clinging to a human. Such a downfall was the fate of the great Yokai who had once ruled this land.

"What is this..."

The slaughter stopped. The Red Ghost finally grasped the situation, his face a mask of disbelief. Four hundred years had passed. The Void Monarch had died, but the fear and Demonic Energy he left behind had transformed this place into a forest of Yokai, and it hadn't ended there. The land itself had resurrected the great Yokai who once reigned here.

No, not a resurrection. This thing had merely copied the appearance of the most powerful being in this land. It was not the Void Monarch himself. Greed flickered in the Red Ghost's eyes. The true Void Monarch would be one thing, but a mere copy, a manifestation of fear born in this forest? If he ate *that*, he would gain more power than from a thousand of these insignificant humans.

"How lucky!"

The Red Ghost threw aside the head he was holding and rushed at the Yokai. He felt no fear. Even weakened, he was once a pinnacle of the Predator. A manifestation of fear that was at most four hundred years old could never be his match.

It had to be so.

*Charge in, tear with his hands.* The Red Ghost's thoughts stopped there. He never even realized his own arms had been torn off, his head ripped away. His headless, armless body tumbled across the ground, carried forward by its own momentum.

The Yokai ignored the Red Ghost's corpse and turned its gaze to the remaining martial artists. The survivors could not comprehend what had just happened, but they were forced to recognize that the naked man was an even greater monster than the one he had just destroyed.

"S-save... save me," Jaegal Taeryeong stammered, collapsing to the ground as fear finally broke him. He trembled like a small child. Namgung Heewon and Tang Ahee were just as terrified, their faces deathly pale. Tang Ahee felt her crotch grow damp, but she felt no shame.

"...Seojin," Namgung Heewon whispered.

Moyong Seojin, hands clamped over his mouth, looked back at him.

"Run. Don't look back."

Even as he said it, Namgung Heewon knew escape was impossible. This was a forest one could wander endlessly. Fleeing this spot would not mean escaping the forest itself. Still, he had to try.

However, the Yokai did not approach. It stopped, turning its head to stare into the dense trees. The ground began to shake as if from an earthquake. Trees broke and fell, carving a path through the woods. The Yokai stood watching it, and the survivors dared not move. They all felt it. The moment they moved, the Yokai would kill them all. It was not a certainty, but the fear of 'what if' held them paralyzed.

[I see what's going on,] the Void Monarch muttered. [It seems this forest wants you to clean up that piece of shit.]

Lee Sungmin stopped walking.

The path carved by the trees was not long. He had never imagined the pursuers would be this close, nor had he expected to walk into such a gruesome scene. Lee Sungmin took in the sight of the dying, the wounded, the corpses, and the few martial artists of the Noble Clan still clinging to life.

"...Gwi, the Phantom Spear," Jaegal Taeryeong stammered.

"...It's been a while, little brother," Namgung Heewon said.

A dry laugh escaped Lee Sungmin. "You're still calling me little brother?"

"Why not? Once a big brother, always a big brother, right?" Namgung Heewon replied.

Lee Sungmin stared at him for a moment before his gaze shifted to the Yokai, which was now staring intently in his direction. The face was familiar. He had seen it before.

[He has the same face as this Elder,] the Void Monarch answered without surprise.

Lee Sungmin studied the naked Yokai. Aside from its solid muscles, its lower body was particularly magnificent. Behind him, Rubia covered her eyes with both hands, but her own eyes sparkled through a cleverly parted gap between her fingers.

[His thing actually looks smaller, though,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

*I have to kill that thing?*

[Isn't that the situation? You tried to leave quietly, but the forest blocked you. When you followed the path it opened, there he was. What, are you scared?]

*He looks strong.*

[He is strong. He was born from this Elder's fear, but you're plenty strong too.]

The Void Monarch seemed to be enjoying the situation.

[Give it a try.]

There was no need for him to urge him on.

The Yokai had already moved.
