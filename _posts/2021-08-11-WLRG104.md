A strange one, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo thought, looking into the man's eyes. They were so deep they seemed to belong to someone who had undergone Rejuvenation, and yet the first attack they exchanged had not been particularly heavy. Of course, that did not mean the man was weak. The weight of that single blow alone surpassed anything from the Demon Wolf Iron Fist.

*To think Silent Flash had such a Junior Disciple.*

He could not know everything yet, but he could feel that the man was either on par with Silent Flash or perhaps half a step ahead. That was not enough, however. His skills were not strong enough to completely turn this situation around, even if he had suddenly intervened. A smile stretched across the Phantom Sword of Dokgo's face, showing his teeth as his beloved sword let out a sound.

KIIIIII KIIIIII.

It was like the groaning cry of a dying child.

Lee Sungmin met the Phantom Sword of Dokgo's gaze. He knew that in terms of martial prowess alone, his skills could not yet compare, but he could not back down. Gripping his spear, he closed his eyes once and then opened them, his breath steadying. His fighting spirit inner qi were strong, leaving no room for bewitchment in his heart. There was only resolve.

*Strange.*

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo took his stance.

*This should be easy... so why doesn't it feel that way?*

Those unfathomable eyes gave him that premonition.

Neither man waited. They rushed at each other simultaneously. Lee Sungmin did not hide his Dancing Shadow Soul Break, his body vanishing from where it stood as Traceless Step sent him dashing forward. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo took half a step to meet him, slashing his sword sideways to intercept the long spear's thrust.

No sound of impact followed, for both attacks were feints. The thrusting spear shot upward from its straight path while the Phantom Sword of Dokgo twisted his upper body, bringing the sideways slash down in a sharp arc.

JJEOOONG!

This time, the clash was real. It was an attack swung with all his might, a blow that used his entire body without looking back. A tingle shot through both his hands. Lee Sungmin relaxed his knees halfway, appearing to be pushed back by the force, but in reality, he executed a controlled seven-step retreat.

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo was like a mad dog that never let go of its prey. Lee Sungmin remembered what Chwi Geol had said, remembered the wounds he had suffered, remembered his severed arm. A single slash from this man contained dozens of sword auras. It would not cut just once. A mere graze would be enough to sever flesh.

He shot out his spear, a quick series of jabs to keep his distance and check his opponent. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo's eyes moved quickly, distinguishing the feints from the real attacks among the consecutive thrusts.

*They're all killing moves. He seems young and full of vigor, and yet... huhu! What a strange fellow.*

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo caught his twitching lips. He should not laugh yet. A crackling sword aura shot out and deflected the spear.

*He seems to be all-out attacking, yet he's cautious. He knows when to retreat and when not to. Indeed... this is your advantageous distance right now.*

There was no reason for the man not to attack aggressively. He would not want to allow a swordsman to get close.

Half a step.

That was the step the Phantom Sword of Dokgo took. The Protective Qi Aura covering his body swelled greatly. His sixth sense blared a brief warning, and Lee Sungmin's body immediately responded. He began to fully unleash the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The swaying spear tassel was like a red wave, and the sharp spearhead pierced through its midst.

The attack he sensed from the warning was sharp. A clean killing sword clashed with the spearhead.

*No.*

It was not a clash. It was climbing. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo was not a wolf but a snake. His sword, and the hand gripping it, slid up the spear shaft, aiming for Lee Sungmin's neck. Lee Sungmin's footwork changed. Instead of retreating, he moved forward.

Traceless Step.

PWAK!

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo's sword cut through an afterimage, which split into dozens of pieces and scattered. Sensing the premonition of an attack from his side, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo twisted his body. When had the man moved to his side? He had to acknowledge it was as good as teleportation. Spinning his body, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo flung his sword. As dozens of sword auras rushed him, Lee Sungmin pulled his arm back.

Numbers against numbers. He shot dozens of spear thrusts to counter the dozens of sword auras. Amidst the continuous explosions, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo could not hold back and burst into laughter. As if to cut down his own laughter, he unleashed a single sword strike. Lee Sungmin spun his spear.

KAAANG!

He deflected the sword with Ran's Technique and immediately pushed his steps forward.

One step, two steps. Thus, the Two Step Calamity began. Killing intent and enhanced qi tangled together and shot towards the Phantom Sword of Dokgo, who tossed the sword from his right hand to his left. The sword, caught in mid-air, created a flurry of blades that scattered the strands of severed enhanced qi.

*He's gone?*

Beyond the flurry, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo lost sight of the spear. It shot up from below. Serpent Soul Devouring. It was a technique that had surprised the Sword Phantom, but the Phantom Sword of Dokgo did not panic. Instead of retreating, he swung his sword. His swordsmanship involved embedding dozens of sword auras into a single slash, making him the most skilled master of the fast sword Lee Sungmin had ever seen. Serpent Soul Devouring was blocked. As soon as he confirmed this, Lee Sungmin shifted his steps to the side. One Step Shadow Clone. Dozens of his clones surrounded the Phantom Sword of Dokgo and thrust their spears.

"Hahaha!"

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo laughed loudly. He spun his body in a wide arc and swung his sword. The enhanced sword qi that swept in all directions toppled the afterimages of the One Step Shadow Clone. This was different from the level he had perceived with his 'eyes'. The Phantom Sword of Dokgo drastically revised his first impression of Lee Sungmin. The man's spear was tricky and sharp, and his footwork was dizzying.

*I wonder how old he is.*

Judging by his martial arts alone, he was no different from an old master who had polished his skills for a long time. Was there really a young spearman with such outstanding skills? If there were, it was impossible that rumors would not have spread.

*I'd like to fight a little longer, but I can't afford to drag this out.*

The Demon Wolf Iron Fist was the problem. Entangled once again in a fight with Baek Sogo, he could no longer retreat and was being worn down. A conclusion would be reached soon, and the Phantom Sword of Dokgo could not just stand by and watch. If the Demon Wolf Iron Fist died and Baek Sogo and Lee Sungmin attacked together, even he would not be able to hold out.

*A delightful fight like this is rare, but I have no choice...*

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo's thoughts were cut short.

It happened in an instant. The wall of flesh right beside him tore apart, and a black torrent erupted from within, engulfing his body. He had never imagined such an attack and could not even defend himself. In truth, it was not that he had no defense. The Protective Qi Aura was intact and shielding his body. The only problem was that it was not sturdy enough to block the assault.

The attack's power had not diminished in the slightest despite tearing through the wall. Half of his upper body was blown away, and the Phantom Sword of Dokgo could not even scream. As he flew through the air spraying blood, one last thought passed through his fading consciousness.

*Black Dragon Armor...? The Little Heavenly Demon? Why...*

KWADANGTANG!

His body tumbled across the floor. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the tumbling form. The twitching Phantom Sword of Dokgo had suffered a fatal wound, one beyond any hope of recovery.

The fight between Baek Sogo and the Demon Wolf Iron Fist stopped. With a pale face, the Demon Wolf Iron Fist looked down at the Phantom Sword of Dokgo as his twitching body came to a complete stop.

The Phantom Sword of Dokgo was dead.

"...the Little Heavenly Demon..." the Demon Wolf Iron Fist stammered.

Baek Sogo's mouth hung half-open. Lee Sungmin stared down at the Phantom Sword of Dokgo's corpse with a dazed expression. The man who had just been unleashing ferocious, swift sword strikes was no more. All that remained was a gruesome corpse with half of its torso crushed. He moved his stiff neck and turned his head.

What had killed the Phantom Sword of Dokgo was a rippling black cloth. Lee Sungmin had seen it before, not with his own two eyes, but in the information he had purchased from Erebrisa.

She walked through the gap in the wall she had torn open, merely glancing at the Phantom Sword of Dokgo's corpse without a word. Through those emotionless eyes, Lee Sungmin realized she was not the real Wiji Hoyeon, but her Doppelganger.

The Doppelganger placed a head she was holding on the ground. Lee Sungmin recognized it as the head of the Blood Granny he had seen earlier. Seeing that no blood flowed from it, however, he knew it did not belong to the Blood Granny herself, but to her Doppelganger. The two of them had been on the same path. It seemed Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger and the Blood Granny's Doppelganger had met.

"The Little Heavenly Demon... why...!" the Demon Wolf Iron Fist cried out, still stammering.

He did not seem to realize that this was not Wiji Hoyeon herself. Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo, however, knew it was a Doppelganger and had no choice but to take a few steps back. It would have been better if it were Wiji Hoyeon herself. They might have been able to resolve things through conversation, but their opponent was a Doppelganger, with whom conversation was impossible. Although a Doppelganger's power was not exactly the same as the original's, it was a different story for Wiji Hoyeon. She was overwhelmingly strong to begin with, so even her Doppelganger would possess transcendent strength. The death of the Phantom Sword of Dokgo proved it. He had perhaps been the strongest person in this Dungeon aside from Wiji Hoyeon, yet he had been caught in a blind attack, unable to react, and died.

"...Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo called to Lee Sungmin in a trembling voice.

Lee Sungmin glared at Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger and bit his lower lip hard. He had thought it was strange. The people had been evenly divided among the four paths, yet everyone except Wiji Hoyeon herself had died. It seemed that in his past life, the Doppelganger had likely done the same thing, breaking through walls and killing everyone it saw.

*And then Wiji Hoyeon herself must have killed her own Doppelganger.*

She must have gone outside the Dungeon and said that she had killed everyone. It was not entirely wrong. Even if Wiji Hoyeon herself had not killed the others, her Doppelganger's actions were ultimately no different from Wiji Hoyeon having massacred everyone in the Dungeon.

"The Little Heavenly Demon!" the Demon Wolf Iron Fist shouted, his voice rough. "What in the world are you doing?! Why did you kill the Phantom Sword of Dokgo! And isn't that Blood Granny's head!"

The Demon Wolf Iron Fist did not know about Doppelgangers because he had not encountered one on his way here. That was why he roared with genuine anger at Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger.

"Even if you said you wouldn't take us as your subordinates, we still considered you our lord! Someday, when you reign over the world... we wanted to see the world ruled by you, a demonic practitioner! That was all we wanted...!" he spat out, as if chewing his words.

With his eyes full of killing intent, he stomped forward. He radiated a fighting spirit even more intense than when he fought Baek Sogo and shouted at the Doppelganger.

"But why! Even if you didn't acknowledge him, the Phantom Sword of Dokgo truly followed you!"

Wiji Hoyeon, the Doppelganger, did not answer. She merely looked at the Demon Wolf Iron Fist with emotionless eyes. Seeing those eyes, he could no longer hold back. He roared and lunged at the Doppelganger. The Demon Wolf Iron Fist knew what kind of end he would meet. There was no way he, who had followed and revered Wiji Hoyeon, would not know of her monstrous strength.

Nevertheless, he had no other choice. Even knowing he would die, he could not bear the thought of not attacking her. The Demon Wolf Iron Fist met the end he had imagined for himself. The Black Dragon Armor erupted, and his body was turned into a chunk of meat.

Watching the splattering blood and flesh fall, Lee Sungmin finally opened his mouth.

"Senior Sister."

"Uh... yes?"

"......"

He had to speak. After all, this was why he had come here.
