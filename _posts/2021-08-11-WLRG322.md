76. Unconsciousness (3)

"Ha, hahaha! Hahahaha!"

Slammed hard against the floor, the Slaughter Predator erupted in a fit of crazed laughter. The Void Monarch looked down at the creature's crumpled body and slowly withdrew his hand while Lee Sungmin stared, his mouth agape in shock at the sudden appearance.

"Now that's quite a martial arts theory!" the Slaughter Predator said as it scrambled back to its feet. Where eyes and a nose should have been, the creature had only sunken hollows, but even these swelled and sealed in an instant as its wounds mended. "Breaking through the wall of unconsciousness when you've nearly lost all control of your Demonic Energy. You must have been pretty desperate and earnest, huh?"

"I made a promise, and I simply didn't want to lie," the Void Monarch spat, his voice cold.

His words only made the Slaughter Predator laugh even louder. "You must've heard everything. The reason someone as strong as you had to die. A great yokai like you died for nothing more than to become nourishment for that brat. You know that, and you still want to step up for him?"

"What does that have to do with anything?" the Void Monarch returned with a dry laugh of his own. "It wasn't an unjust death at all. If I had to die for being too strong, then it was a joyous end! Nourishment? I don't care. Thanks to that, I've experienced quite a few interesting things even after dying."

There was no lie in his words. The Void Monarch glanced once at Lee Sungmin.

"This is actually for the best. So you're the source of all that foolish brat's anxieties. Without you, he'll no longer have to suffer that idiotic fear that he might be one of The End."

"Have you forgotten?" The Slaughter Predator bared its teeth in a grin. "You're overestimating your own strength. You saw me in Urmuri. I still remember the look on your face. You felt fear, didn't you?"

The Void Monarch did not answer. He simply strode forward with his mouth shut as the Slaughter Predator shot him a scathing laugh.

"You should have vanished long ago, but you survived thanks to the Demonic Spirit's machinations! Still, I didn't dislike you. After all, you were the strongest of the Yokai race."

Strictly speaking, the Slaughter Predator was not a Yokai. It was an executioner of The End, a reincarnator who awakened at the appointed time and was no longer human.

"I took pity on you, toyed with by the Demonic Spirit, and showed you a path forward. If you had chosen it, you could have escaped this world and moved on to a place of freedom."

The Void Monarch listened to the creature's ridicule, knowing it spoke the truth. When he first confronted the Slaughter Predator residing in Lee Sungmin's mind, he had understood the sensation of fear for the first time in his life. Simply facing it, every single movement, everything about it had exuded death, and the path it had shown him, that winding, single path, had the Slaughter Predator sitting at its end, asking if he would come, but why? Back then, he had felt fear just by facing it.

Now, the Void Monarch felt no fear. Instead, a pleasant sense of liberation filled him.

It was back then, in Urmuri, that the Void Monarch had first become aware of the 'single path'. After that, he had not been idle in Lee Sungmin's mind. He was mentally connected to the boy, seeing and feeling everything Lee Sungmin saw and felt, which became a diverse and new experience for him. The Void Monarch had originally known nothing of martial arts, but now he understood them. He had also learned just how strong a human who mastered martial arts could become.

The power Lee Sungmin inherited from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was especially potent. While it could not become the power of the Void Monarch, who was only a soul, the martial arts theory of the Chairman engraved in Lee Sungmin's mind was also transmitted to him. All of it. Recalling every martial art Lee Sungmin had learned and every theory engraved in his mind, the Void Monarch slowly advanced on the Slaughter Predator.

The Slaughter Predator let out a dry laugh and shook its head. "You can't change anything." Its voice was laced with pity. "You were such a powerful Yokai, yet you couldn't even escape this world and met your end. Even after death, your soul remained without rest, becoming a toy of fate. That's you. You have no reason to exist anymore. The reincarnator has become a perfect Yokai, and I will inhabit that body to become the Slaughter Predator."

The Void Monarch did not stop.

"It's not difficult to defeat you and move on. Don't tell me you think that human will be of any help? Hahaha, he's no help at all. I've already taken complete control of his consciousness."

It was just as the creature said. Lee Sungmin had tried to attack several times already, but his body refused to obey, unable to muster even a shred of strength. His vast inner arts and his endless Demonic Energy were of no use to him.

"......"

Lee Sungmin could only look at the Void Monarch's back. The Void Monarch did not turn around.

"I told you last time, didn't I? That perhaps, I might have died just to meet you."

At those words, Lee Sungmin clenched his fists tightly. Perhaps he did not need to feel guilty about this. It was not as if he had known the truth, nor had everything happened with his consent, yet, Lee Sungmin felt guilty. Even if he had not known, even if these things had happened without his consent, was it not all his responsibility in the end?

*In the end, it was because I was too weak. Too insignificant. That's why the Destiny Force focused on me.*

Even if someone of his caliber being chosen as the reincarnator was the Demonic Spirit's intention from the start.

"So what about it?"

THUD.

The Void Monarch planted his foot forward with force. He braced his knees, facing the Slaughter Predator laughing in the distance.

CRACK, CRACKLE!

The sound of cracking bones echoed from his entire body as his muscles repeatedly swelled and contracted.

"I don't regret meeting you. Watching what you did from inside your head was quite enjoyable. Rather than dying like that back then, remaining as a soul and traveling with you like this has been more fun for this Elder."

"You're saying things unbecoming of a Yokai," the Slaughter Predator sneered.

The Void Monarch nodded and laughed at its words. "Can it be helped? I've been living as a parasite in that idiot's Magic Armor with only my soul remaining. It's only natural that my old fighting spirit and ferocity have diminished."

CRACK... CRACKLE.

The Void Monarch's muscles tightened to their absolute limit.

"Remember this. I enjoyed meeting you. I don't know about crap like fate or whatever, but the few years I spent with you were as enjoyable as the entire lifetime this Elder lived, and... this Elder made a promise to you. That I would stop you."

*No.*

Lee Sungmin's face turned deathly pale. A terrible, dreadful premonition washed over him, chilling him to the bone. He was remembering the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the death of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, too. The Void Monarch's existence was difficult to compare to anything else. When Lee Sungmin was at his most anxious and incomplete, it was the Void Monarch who had not left his side. Whenever Lee Sungmin was in crisis, it was also the Void Monarch who advised him and even stepped forward with martial arts theory. For Lee Sungmin, the Void Monarch had been a master, a friend, and a father.

He desperately reached out to grab the Void Monarch, even though he knew the gesture was meaningless. He could not bear to watch the Void Monarch disappear completely because of him.

"Don't be afraid."

The voice lingered in Lee Sungmin's ears.

KABOOM!

The Void Monarch kicked off the ground and shot forward, the force of his launch flinging Lee Sungmin's body backward.

"If I had wanted to leave, I could have left you long ago!" the Void Monarch's shout echoed throughout the entire space.

RUMBLE!

A tremendous roar shook the world of unconsciousness. Thrown far back, Lee Sungmin could see neither the Void Monarch nor the Slaughter Predator. He could only hear the Slaughter Predator's loud laughter and the sound of something exploding and shattering.

*Please.*

Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet.

*Don't make me Despair.*

He clutched his head with both hands. The constant headache he always had was gone now, but a fear greater than that was making his body tremble. Everything he had just learned, things he had not known until now, was pressing down on him. The Demonic Spirit had betrayed the fate of The End, and because of that, an unqualified person like him had returned to the past.

*Ah, ahh. So that's why Mushii said those things.*

Mushii had already told him everything. Unkind muttering. There are no coincidences in the world. The same privilege applies to all Otherworlders. Skills. A convenient privilege that makes learning something very easy. It exists for a reason. Everything has a reason. There is a reason for returning to the past, even if it looks like a well-packaged coincidence. If you have returned, you must prepare to become a being worthy of the reason for your return.

Lee Sungmin collapsed to the ground.

*Ahh, right. Mushii. You told me everything back then.*

The reason why Otherworlders had skills. In this world without coincidences, the reason someone like him returned to the past.

*I was the Slaughter Predator.*

Even if he was an insignificant being in his past life, having returned to the past meant he carried the destiny of the Slaughter Predator. Deep inside his mind, the consciousness of the Slaughter Predator lay dormant, waiting for the right time, and now, that time had come.

*My existence...*

Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet again. In the end, he was just a puppet. His existence was that of a reincarnator and an Observer meant to bring about The End, and he was the Slaughter Predator, one of the calamities of The End. The treacherous Demonic Spirit used me to deceive everyone and get his hands on Wiji Hoyeon. Yes, that's what it was in the end. In this world, Lee Sungmin was not the protagonist. He was a disposable pawn that existed for Wiji Hoyeon's sake.

*So. So, I... Am I going to be unable to do anything, just like this?*

He could not do anything about the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's death. Was he to do nothing this time, too, and watch as the Void Monarch might disappear?

*I refuse.*

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth.

*I refuse. That, I refuse.*

Of course he would. He loathed himself for having been unable to do anything at the death of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and at the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He hated it, so he inherited the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He did it because he did not want to remain his utterly powerless self.

*Ah.*

Suddenly, Lee Sungmin realized something was in his hand. He lifted it to see a pure white... awl. It was the object the Dark Supreme had held in his hand at the final moment, trying to stab Lee Sungmin a long time ago when he was killed. The Martial God had called this strange white wooden awl the White Fang, mistletoe.

Lee Sungmin did not know what this object was used for. He also did not know why the White Fang was in his hand at this very moment.

*Is this also fate? If so, where is the destination of this damn, screwed-up fate? If awakening as the Slaughter Predator is the destiny given to me...*

"...Just..."

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth again. He did not bother with calculations. He did not know what the White Fang in his hand was used for, or what function it had. He just had to do something. He had to because he hated his powerless self.

"......"

To be honest, the Slaughter Predator had not thought the Void Monarch would be this strong. After all, when it had seen him in Urmuri, the Void Monarch was no match for it. The Void Monarch's power was surprising, but what truly shocked the Slaughter Predator now was not him.

It saw what was held in Lee Sungmin's hands far in the distance. *What is he thinking?*

The Slaughter Predator's thoughts froze. Lee Sungmin was aiming the sharp tip of the White Fang directly at his own heart.

"Stop!" the Slaughter Predator roared.

There was no composure in that shout. The massive cry made the Void Monarch flinch in surprise. When he looked back at Lee Sungmin, the Slaughter Predator was already flying across the world of consciousness toward the boy with all its might.

"You bastard!" the Void Monarch bellowed, chasing after the creature. He grabbed the flying Slaughter Predator's shoulder and slammed him into the ground.

The Slaughter Predator resisted violently, struggling to get free.

"Keep going!" the Void Monarch shouted, pressing down on the Slaughter Predator's shoulders with both hands. He did not know what on earth Lee Sungmin was trying to do. However, seeing the usually composed Slaughter Predator react so vehemently, it seemed certain that what Lee Sungmin was about to do was something troublesome for it.

Hearing all the shouts, Lee Sungmin tightened his grip on the White Fang. He plunged it into his chest until it pierced his heart and came out the other side.
