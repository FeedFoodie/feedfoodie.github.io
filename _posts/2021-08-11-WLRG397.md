Lee Sungmin walked forward slowly, the barrier he had created shimmering at his back. Preskan remained a still, dead-looking heap where he had been slammed into the ground, while Gemini cursed as he pushed himself to his feet.

"What the hell all of a sudden..."

Lee Sungmin did not listen. He simply moved his foot and kicked Gemini's body.

"......"

There was no flight, only a wet pop. Gemini's body burst like a balloon right where he stood, splattering blood and flesh in all directions.

Honestly, Lee Sungmin held no particular malice toward him. He had even benefited from Gemini's help a few times, and during the fight with Jeniela, Gemini had never intervened. He had only stood in the distance and watched, never once joining the battle.

Nevertheless, here, Gemini was his enemy. Lee Sungmin ignored Preskan's unmoving form and stepped toward Jeniela.

A silent current of electricity flowed between them.

Jeniela took a half-step back. She wanted to hear the full story, to understand what had changed. The vast gap that had existed between them had suddenly, impossibly, narrowed. She did what she had to do. Her magic power coalesced into a barrier, a solid wall between herself and Lee Sungmin.

From behind its protection, Jeniela took another half-step back. She raised her hands from her sides, preparing for both defense and attack.

The barrier shattered before Lee Sungmin's strike. In that fleeting moment, Jeniela sensed her defense was meaningless and launched an attack of her own. The magic power from the shattered wall gathered at a single point, attempting to blast Lee Sungmin back.

It was too late.

Lee Sungmin's spear thrust was faster than the explosion of gathered magic. His spear no longer followed the principles of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. These were the techniques he had practiced tirelessly in every spare moment since he knew nothing, the ones he had first learned properly from Wiji Hoyeon. It was the most fundamental of those basic techniques, the simple thrust, that he now unleashed.

The spear pierced through the air before the magic could detonate, scattering the energy under Jeniela's control. Before she could even mount a defense, her chest was impaled.

And then it exploded. Bewilderment flickered across Jeniela's face. She staggered back, looking down at the gaping hole in her chest. The wound regenerated in an instant, but unlike before, this was not an injury she had permitted. The attack had been obvious, not even particularly fast. She had thought she could block it easily, yet she had failed.

*What?*

She could not see through him with the All-Seeing Demonic Eye. The presence of the Void Monarch was gone. The only one before her was Lee Sungmin, and yet, was that really the same Lee Sungmin? He had denied his title, the Phantom Spear, because the Phantom Spear was weak and could not defeat her.

There was no headache. His mind was clear. Instead, his chest throbbed with a sense of loss far greater than Despair. There was no need for Despair. The spear in his hand scattered and vanished. He clenched his fists. Dragonic worked just as he had imagined.

*Then what about the other abilities?* The current Lee Sungmin had so many things he could use. So many, in fact, that he had to wonder what to use first.

"Do you know what this is?" Lee Sungmin asked, his voice low. His boiling emotions had calmed, but they had not disappeared. The hatred and killing intent for her churned just beneath a placid surface, and he simply let it be. He neither suppressed it nor rushed it. There was no need.

He knew very well how to use his fists. He had never used these techniques before, yet they felt as familiar as breathing. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had not used weapons. Likewise, the Blood Demon had not used weapons either. Just as he had used Dragonic so naturally, the current Lee Sungmin could use everything they had ever used with the same effortless ease.

Ghost Blood Asura Ju Heebaek, who was considered the world's number one seven hundred years ago. His signature martial art was the Asura Heaven Destruction Art, and its essence was not flashiness, but a simple, straightforward, and overwhelming power.

Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, history's strongest. His signature martial art was Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, an art that pursued the very pinnacle of Extreme Speed.

Those two martial arts unfolded through Lee Sungmin's body. It was not difficult. Even though the two arts possessed different philosophies, they were both intimately familiar to him. Power infused speed, and speed infused power. He added the might of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art to Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, and he added the velocity of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder to the Asura Heaven Destruction Art.

Gale Thunder moved his body. Jeniela, her wound fully regenerated, twisted and swung her claws.

Lee Sungmin's fist pierced through space. Jeniela's magic power and her entire arm were annihilated. Her eyes widened in shock. Starting with that single punch and a half-step forward, Lee Sungmin continued the sequence of movements that flowed through his mind like water. The purple Protective Qi Aura covering his body swelled, taking the terrifying form of an Asura.

It was the Asura Sky-Rending Dance, the final ultimate technique of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art, combined with Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

CRACKLE!

The Asura was wreathed in crackling purple lightning.

Her consciousness flickered in and out. Jeniela could not defend properly and was continuously forced back. Any attempt to defend was meaningless. Lee Sungmin's attacks and his enhanced qi completely transcended the concept of a strike as she understood it. The trajectory of his punches was obvious and lacked finesse, but their overwhelming power and speed robbed her of any chance to react.

*Asura Sky-Rending Dance...!*

There was no way she would not know it. Jeniela knew all of the Blood Demon's martial arts, and yet, was this truly the Asura Sky-Rending Dance?

She ground her teeth as her simmering irritation finally exploded. Her eyes began to glow with a crimson light.

"Aaaargh!"

The magic power, released from her control, erupted. It tore space to shreds and annihilated every tree in the forest as a massive storm of energy erupted in all directions. Lee Sungmin stopped moving and held out his open right hand.

He chanted Dragonic again. A Protective Qi Aura shielded his body as a defensive wall created by the spell rose before him.

RUUUUMBLE!

Jeniela's magic was blocked by the wall. Within the raging storm, she clutched her head with both hands. She no longer felt any pleasure. Her enjoyment came from controlling the situation, from making things flow according to her intentions. That was not happening now. Lee Sungmin had become a being she could not fathom.

The storm of magic, expanding with enough force to swallow the entire forest, failed to break through either the wall or the barrier he had created. Jeniela did not like that one bit. Her eyes grew even redder. The magic power intensified, and the wall shattered.

Beyond it, Lee Sungmin leaped forward, unarmed. His movements followed the forms of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art. If it were merely that, there would be no reason for Jeniela to be unable to counter it.

The problem was the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

CRACKLE!

An electric current infused his movements. The instantaneous blow sent Jeniela's body flying backward. Blood trickled from between her tightly clenched lips as the magic she had unleashed collided with Lee Sungmin's fist.

Just before impact, his fist opened into a palm, and the technique that exploded forth was not the Asura Heaven Destruction Art.

*What is this...?* Jeniela's expression changed as the enhanced qi tore sharply into her.

It was the Flesh Devouring Blood Fang of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. The martial arts of the Crazy Heavenly Demon were so complete that even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had acknowledged them. The Flesh Devouring Blood Fang tore through Jeniela's magic power and slammed into her chest.

"......"

With a heavy sound, Jeniela's body was launched into the air. Lee Sungmin's arms moved, leaving a blur of afterimages as all the techniques of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art were linked together in an instant. Blood Ring Shattering, Bloody Pursuit, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang, Blood Remnant Calamity Flame, Blood Shadow Hundred Flashes, and finally, Blood Ring Typhoon.

Lee Sungmin did not have the memories of the Crazy Heavenly Demon. However, the memories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Blood Demon, who possessed profound martial arts theory, allowed him to perfectly execute the entire art.

Struck by the full combination of techniques, Jeniela exploded into a bloody mist that scattered in all directions. Not a single piece of flesh remained, yet she was still not dead.

"Yes, now this is fun," Jeniela spat, her voice laced with irritation. Her bewilderment was short-lived, just a brief happening. They say even a worm will squirm when stepped on, and this was no different. Nothing had changed. Jeniela was certain of it.

It seemed she would have to make him understand properly. That no matter how much he struggled, nothing would change. To give him a more certain, easy-to-understand Despair, she decided to first tear his body to shreds.

The storm of magic power vanished. Jeniela's upper body slowly lowered from the mist. She kicked off the ground, her claws wreathed in magic, and collided with the wall made of Dragonic.

The wall could not hold and shattered. Behind it, Lee Sungmin gripped the Formless Spear. His swift thrust did not reach her body. She became a gust of wind, slipping past the spear and closing in on him.

Jeniela's open palm struck Lee Sungmin's chest.

KABOOM!

With a heavy sound, his body was sent flying backward. Blood trickled from between his tightly clenched lips. Lee Sungmin followed Jeniela's movements with wide eyes. Her speed increased even more. Her infinite magic power granted her immense velocity, and her physical body moved in perfect tandem with it.

The Formless Spear and her claws clashed. The tip of the spear was crushed and disintegrated. Lee Sungmin backpedaled, putting distance between them. He lowered the spear held in both hands, aiming for her neck. His eyes were adjusting. He had not been able to react properly before, but now he could. It was only natural. Jeniela was finally getting serious.

*I wonder what kind of face he's making? He must think he can finally win. Yes, using Dragonic was certainly enough to surprise me. He must have gained something when the Void Monarch was annihilated, right, but that's not nearly enough. I'll admit he's stronger than before and the gap has narrowed, but nothing has fundamentally changed. In fact, this makes it more fun. This time, you will truly fall into Despair.*

She wanted to hear that scream again. She wanted to see his face twist in Despair. As she anticipated it, her irritation melted away like snow.

*Come on, struggle some more.* Jeniela watched Lee Sungmin's movements with a smile. A grin spread across her previously stiff expression. The corners of her lips curled up, and her eyes curved into crescents.

The spear thrust toward her left breast, aiming for the heart. *He should know it's pointless. Such an attack is meaningless against an immortal. It seems he's a slow learner. When will he finally get it? Well, it doesn't matter if he never does. If he doesn't know, I'll just have to teach him...*

SNAP.

Her thoughts cut off. Her eyes flashed red once, and then her vision went completely black.

Her body was still moving. It took a few steps back, and then her vision returned. A moment ago, her head had exploded. Why? She could not grasp the reason.

"......"

A sense of wrongness permeated space itself. All her senses felt like they were floating. A tickling sensation, like ants crawling over her skin, started at her fingertips. Soon, the tickling turned to pain. Starting from her fingertips, she was being crushed, mashed...

Jeniela's body burst apart. Lee Sungmin lowered his outstretched hand, his brow furrowed. He felt like he had forgotten something, but what was it... In the meantime, Jeniela's exploded body regenerated. She still felt that sense of wrongness. She did not fully understand what had just happened to her.

"Ah," a soft mutter escaped his lips.

"You can't think if your head explodes."

The words snapped Jeniela out of her confusion.

"......"

The blow was far too powerful to be called a mere slap. Jeniela's head spun around several times. Her vertebrae shattered like glass rods, and her neck twisted like a corkscrew. Her vision spun wildly as she lost all strength and collapsed to the ground.

"You were smiling, weren't you?" Lee Sungmin brushed his blood-soaked hair back with one hand and looked down at her crumpled form.

"Just now, you were smiling."

He said it just as Jeniela had sneered at him.
