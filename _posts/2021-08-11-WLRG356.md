Lee Sungmin fell into thought, recognizing the confirmation of the Queen's location as a definite opportunity. It was an unavoidable fact that he would have to face her someday, and he needed to take appropriate action now to increase his chances of victory, even if only slightly.

His immediate choices were Nero and Blood Demon.

Between the two, his heart leaned toward Blood Demon. The fact that the Femud Volcano was not a great distance from Cheped played a large part in swaying his decision. In truth, defeating Nero in Cheped would not be very difficult. Nero was a powerful Lycanthrope and Ju Won's right-hand man, but he was no match for Lee Sungmin.

Besides, the current Lee Sungmin was not alone. He had the assistance of Yana the Nine-Tailed Fox, Silent Flash Baek Sogo, the Spear King, the Black Dragon Knight, and Crimson Sage Scarlet. He even had Teresa, the Saint of the Church. Although Teresa possessed almost no combat ability, her powerful hold could purify Ju Won's poison and was fatal not only to the Undead but to most inhuman beings.

"Will you help me?" Lee Sungmin asked, turning his gaze to Lloyd. From what he had seen so far, the mage had been favorable toward him, even now offering Gries's lifespan without hesitation to inform him of the events unfolding in the world.

"You ask the obvious," Lloyd said with a smirk. "I don't wish for The End either, and when you think about it, didn't the 'current' The End originate from my Master and Lord Abel, who killed him? As a disciple, and as one who formed a bond with them, I wish to take at least a minimum of responsibility."

His ranks had swelled. With the Golden Magic Tower Master and the master of Gries on his side, he had gained one of the most skilled mages in the world. After a moment of consideration, Lee Sungmin posed another question.

"By any chance, do you know the location of Ju Won, the Lycanthrope leader?"

"What Gries captures are 'events'," Lloyd explained. "The significant events that occur in this world are captured by Gries and recorded within it. The Advent and annihilation of the Spirit King was an event Gries could capture, so I could see what happened, but that isn't the case for Ju Won."

"Are you saying he's not doing anything?"

"There have been no rumors of the Lycanthrope leader causing any incidents in the world right now, have there? Ah, of course, Gries doesn't just capture rumors. It also captures events that occur in secret. However... there is no word on Ju Won. The last record of him is from a year ago, when he defeated Silent Flash Baek Sogo in Cheped."

Lee Sungmin felt a pang of disappointment at those words. If he could have found out Ju Won's location, he would not have had to worry about who to strike first. He could have simply killed Ju Won while the Lycanthrope was alone, but as it was, Ju Won's location remained unknown.

[Do you know what it means to kill Blood Demon?] the Void Monarch asked inside Lee Sungmin's contemplating mind. [Blood Demon is one of Jeniela's vampires. If you kill him, the power he has achieved as a Vampire will return to Jeniela.]

*Dammit.*

[That is how Vampires accumulate power,] the Void Monarch continued with a grin. [However, it's not as if there's no way around it. The easiest method, and the one that will help you the most... is predation.]

*Predation?*

[Literally. You just have to eat that bastard, Blood Demon.]

He said it as if it were nothing, but Lee Sungmin's expression hardened.

[If you simply kill him, his power will return to Jeniela, but the story changes if you devour him. Even if your mind remains human, your body is that of a complete Yokai. That alone fulfills the condition for predation, and you also have the Black Heart.]

It was not that he failed to understand what the Void Monarch was saying. On the contrary, Lee Sungmin knew all too well what he meant, for he had experienced it once before.

[The situation is different from back then. Your body is now a complete Yokai, so you don't have to worry about awakening as one or your personality disappearing just from devouring him. If you're squeamish about drinking blood and chewing flesh, can't you just ask Osla for help?]

*That's true, but... the idea of devouring a Vampire is a bit unsettling.*

[Don't worry, eating a Vampire won't turn you into one, and you have a great advantage in a fight against them. Do you know what that is?]

*What is it?*

[The fact that you can never become a Vampire,] the Void Monarch chuckled. [Only humans are infected and transformed by them. A Yokai won't become a Vampire just by being bitten and drinking a Vampire's blood.]

That was precisely what made fighting Vampires so difficult. No matter how much stronger a human was in martial arts, if they were infected in a moment of carelessness, the tables would be turned instantly.

*I can't take Senior Sister with me.*

The same went for Scarlet. It was best to eliminate as much uncertainty as possible, and as it happened, both Baek Sogo and Scarlet were right by his side.

"Will you be okay?" Scarlet asked with a frown after hearing Lee Sungmin's plan. "I don't want to become a Vampire either, so I don't particularly want to go help. Still... are you sure you'll be okay?"

"We don't know how many Vampires are in the Blood Alliance, Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo added. "Isn't this too reckless?"

"I don't think it's reckless," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking his head. If anything, his forces were more than enough. Jeniela and the other top-tier Vampires were on the move with her. Nero was in Cheped, and while Ju Won's unknown whereabouts were a small concern, it would actually be a good thing if he was at the Blood Alliance. If Lee Sungmin could kill both Blood Demon and Ju Won this time, he could reduce Jeniela's forces by a significant amount, if not by half.

"The Black Dragon Knight and Yana will help me, and I'm not weak either."

"I just saw you beat up the Spirit King of Wind. Of course I don't think you're weak," Scarlet grumbled.

"...But I want to help," Baek Sogo spoke up. "What if... just what if, it could be dangerous?"

"If it's dangerous, I'll run away," Lee Sungmin said without hesitation. "So, Senior Sister. Please trust me."

Since he put it that way, Baek Sogo did not press him any further. She looked at him with bitter eyes before slowly nodding her head.

{sep}

First, they had to leave this place. Lee Sungmin summoned a Fairy Horse, planning to spend the day in the Fairy Forest and invade the Blood Alliance tomorrow. This was partly due to the limited uses of the horse, but also because the sun would soon set.

"Don't we have to split up?" Scarlet asked.

Their party numbered six. Although Rubia had become a sphere of light resting in Lee Sungmin's arms, there were still two men and three women. The Fairy Horse was large, but it was too small for five people to cling to. After a moment of thought, Scarlet came up with a clever idea.

"How about you hold one person in your arms?"

"Pardon?"

"Or give them a piggyback ride."

Neither option was appealing. Just then, Yana quietly stepped forward. Nine tails wrapped around her body as a golden light flashed. When the light faded, a golden-furred fox, small enough to fit snugly in one's arms, stood on all fours.

"...Yana...?"

[This way, we can all go at once, can't we?] Yana asked, raising her head. She did not open her snout, but her voice was heard clearly by everyone.

Scarlet gulped as she stared at Yana's new form. Even the usually composed Baek Sogo trembled as she looked at the small creature.

"...So cute..."

[Do not touch me,] Yana warned in a cold voice. She leaped nimbly and landed on Lee Sungmin's shoulder. Worried she might fall, Lee Sungmin carefully lifted her with both hands and held her in his arms. Yana shifted slightly in his embrace, adjusting her position. As Lee Sungmin looked down, her golden eyes gazed back up at him. An impulse to run his fingers through her luscious fur arose, but he refrained, certain he would earn her contempt.

Holding Rubia and Yana, he rode the Fairy Horse back to the forest with Lloyd, Scarlet, and Baek Sogo.

"You. You have a lot of friends," the Spear King muttered with a dumbfounded expression, having been meditating in a cross-legged position.

Lee Sungmin could only smile bitterly at his words. In truth, he had not formed many relationships. He had lived a very busy life, and the deep connections he had made were few. Looking at it now, however, his network was incredible. Yana was the Nine-Tailed Fox who ruled Urmuri, considered the strongest among the Yokai. Scarlet and Lloyd were archmages counted among the best in magic, while Baek Sogo, the Black Dragon Knight, and the Spear King were masters of the Transcendent Realm, standing at the pinnacle of martial arts. Even Teresa, though lacking combat ability, was a being at the apex of divine magic.

It all fit together too well.

The situation being this good only made him more anxious. Come to think of it, until now, Lee Sungmin had been almost alone. The Void Monarch was there, but he mostly served as mental support from inside his head. This time was different. By his side were masters of a similar level, all for the purpose of stopping The End.

For Lee Sungmin, meetings had soon become farewells. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and Abel. Those who had acted alongside him, who had helped him, had died. Their deaths had matured him rather than driving him to despair, but that was why the anxiety would not go away. What if all of this was a trick of fate? What if The End was something that could never be overcome? What if, in the end, all of this was nothing more than a meaningless struggle, and everyone was destined to die?

*I escaped from the fate I possessed.*

However, the fate of the world remained the same. As long as he was part of that great flow of destiny, Lee Sungmin had escaped his own fate, yet he had not. He stroked his stiff cheek, trying to shake off the gnawing anxiety.

While spending the day in the Fairy Forest, Lee Sungmin organized his thoughts on what to do next. The Spirit Queen had not yet made her Advent in the Sleeping Forest. Although Rubia had become his familiar and her connection to the Spirit Queen was severed, Lloyd's presence meant they could now detect events with Gries. An event on the scale of the Spirit Queen's Advent would undoubtedly be known through Gries, and when that happened, he could head to the Sleeping Forest.

The problem was the still-unmanifested Spirit Kings of Earth and Water. The Spirit King of Wind had been reverse-summoned by him, and the Spirit King of Flame had been annihilated, but the other two had not even made their Advent yet.

"The Spirit Kings will grow stronger as time passes after their Advent," Rubia advised. "The spots where they Advent are places specialized for them, and from the moment of their Advent, all the Elementals in Eria will head there."

The longer they waited, the more disadvantageous it became. Lee Sungmin had succeeded in reverse-summoning the Spirit King of Wind with relative ease, but that did not mean the Spirit King was weak. Its winds were powerful enough to slice through hundreds of Supreme Peak masters with ease and overwhelm the Transcendent Realm. The Spirit Kings, who possessed such power right after their Advent, would only grow stronger with time.

*If only I had multiple bodies.*

As he was thinking that, the Spear King, who had been listening to the conversation, spoke up. "There are plenty of people. Can't we just split up and go?"

"...Pardon?"

"If you're going to worry and struggle all by yourself, why did you gather us all here? Didn't you gather us to help with things you can't do, or things that are difficult for you?" the Spear King grumbled as he cracked his neck from side to side. "The Black Dragon Knight and the Nine-Tailed Fox said they'd go with you to the Blood Alliance, right? In that case, I'll go to the Spirit King of Earth. I'd rather fight the earth than fight water." He laughed heartily.

"I shall accompany you as well," Lloyd declared with a solemn expression.

"This bastard, how old are you to be talking down to me?" the Spear King shot back at him.

Lloyd, who had never imagined he would be called out like this, stared at the Spear King with a bewildered look. "...I've lived long enough...?"

"Hey, I'm a little over two hundred this year. Are you older than me?"

"...I'll call you Hyung-nim."

Uncharacteristically, the Spear King had a bit of a boomer-like streak.

"Then I'll go with Baek Sogo to deal with the Spirit King of Water," Scarlet said, chewing on a piece of beef jerky. "If we think we can't handle it, we'll run, so just know that."

At that point, Lee Sungmin could not help but be flustered. It had not been long since he had forced himself to shake off the anxiety that all of this might be a trick of fate, yet everyone was stepping up to take on such dangerous paths.

"The Spear King Elder is right," Baek Sogo spoke up. "Junior Disciple doesn't have to do everything. We also decided to be with you to stop The End. We want to help in any way we can."

"But... it could be dangerous..."

"You're also going to a place that could be dangerous, Junior Disciple," Baek Sogo said with a faint smile. "A little while ago, you said it, didn't you? To trust you. It's the same for me. Trust me... no, trust us."

He had no words to reply. Her eyes were sincere and earnest. Receiving her gaze, Lee Sungmin could only nod his head.

"...I understand. I'm counting on you."

"Why are you being so serious?" the Spear King let out a big yawn. "We're just going out to play."
