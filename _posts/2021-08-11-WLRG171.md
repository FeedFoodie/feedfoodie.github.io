For four days, Lee Sungmin's party waited in the brothel. Four days since their meeting with Frau, and still no word from Yana. Frau had assured them she delivered the message but warned that a reply would take time. It seemed Yana was quite busy, though with what, he could not guess.

On the fourth night, Frau summoned him to her room.

"Setting that aside," she began, a strange smile playing on her lips, "you're quite something."

She sat perched on her bed in an alluring pose, clad only in a thin, see-through slip.

"You said you learned martial arts, right? That must mean you're strong. So, how about it?" Frau lifted a bottle of liquor, her question hanging in the air.

A cold sweat broke out across Lee Sungmin's skin, and he took an instinctive step back.

"...I'm not sure what you're talking about."

"Don't lie. You know exactly what I mean, so why pretend you don't? Why don't we just think of it as invigorating each other and have a little tumble?"

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard. Frau was undeniably beautiful, yet he felt no desire to sleep with her. It was not a matter of suppressing his urges with reason. He simply did not want to. The image of her screaming curses as she channeled an Evil Spirit into her body was still seared into his mind.

"You're no fun," Frau grumbled, taking in his troubled expression. She was not offended. In fact, she found his shy, virginal act rather cute. It only made her more enthusiastic, and she licked her plump lips.

"If you're shy, we can just turn off the lights. You can lie still in bed, and this big sister will take care of everything."

"No, I just don't want to..."

"Even if you say that, a man becomes a beast when his lower half stands at attention. You think this big sister doesn't know that?"

"No means no."

"What a strange fellow. Why? Is there some other girl you've given your heart to? Is it guilt?" She cackled. "Whoa, don't worry. This big sister is tight-lipped. You think I'd go around town spreading rumors that I devoured the Phantom Spear?"

Lee Sungmin needlessly tapped his cheek and let out a dry cough. Snickering, Frau brought the liquor bottle to her lips and took a long drink. After gulping down several mouthfuls, she set the bottle down.

"A reply came from Yana."

The sudden shift in topic made Lee Sungmin, who had been contemplating a retreat to his room, snap to attention. He lifted his bowed head and met her gaze.

"Tomorrow, by noon," Frau said. "She wants you to come to the annex of the Grand Elegance Palace. She said I don't need to be there, so only those of you with business should go."

"...Just us?"

"Why? You want this big sister to tag along?" she sneered. "Even if I did, there's absolutely no way I'd take your side if something happened."

"No, it's fine," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking his head. Frau had called Yana a friend. It was only natural she would not help them if a fight broke out. His relationship with her was not nearly deep enough to expect that kind of loyalty.

The next day, Lee Sungmin's party made their way to the Grand Elegance Palace. It was a high-class Fairy Realm, not far from the brothel district. They arrived with time to spare before noon, but when they mentioned their appointment at the annex, the gates opened for them immediately.

"She is already waiting."

A one-eyed female Yokai greeted them from the other side of the gate. She narrowed the single eye in the center of her face and bowed her head deeply. They followed her past a beautiful garden and a tranquil pond until a large annex came into view. There, the one-eyed Yokai stopped.

"She is inside."

With her words, Lee Sungmin walked past the guide and entered the annex. The interior was spacious and empty, with no sign of anyone. He hesitated for a moment, unsure of where to go.

Just then, a voice echoed in his mind.

[This way.]

The voice, belonging to a woman, resonated through the space. Lee Sungmin's expression hardened as he turned in the direction it had come from. Behind him, Rubia swallowed nervously, and even the Crazy Heavenly Demon's face tensed. They were about to meet the great yokai who ruled Urmuri, the Nine-Tailed Fox Yana. He knew nothing of her personality or power, but she was certainly not someone he could face with a relaxed mind.

The voice guided them to a set of pure white paper doors. Lee Sungmin reached out and slid them open.

In the center of a lavishly decorated room sat a beautiful woman. She was dressed in a colorful hanbok, her calm, pale blonde hair and blue eyes giving her an air of serenity. Lee Sungmin stood there blankly, staring at her.

"...Yana?" he uttered in a small voice.

Yana offered a faint smile and nodded. She possessed an impressive beauty, but it was far different from what he had expected. He had honestly imagined an aura so intimidating that a single glance would be overwhelming, an impression so vicious he would not dare meet her gaze. The woman before him seemed to be the very opposite of that.

With an elegant-looking smile, Yana raised a hand. "Please, sit."

She offered them a seat first, and Lee Sungmin pondered how to receive this unexpected kindness. He could not fathom any reason for Yana to show him such courtesy.

"Are you flustered?" she asked, studying his expression.

"...Yes," he answered honestly, nodding his head.

Yana let out a small laugh, as if she had expected as much. Her gaze remained fixed on Lee Sungmin, sparing not a single glance for the Crazy Heavenly Demon or Rubia. No, to be precise, she was looking at the Magic Armor he wore.

"Is the Void Monarch doing well?" Yana asked.

Lee Sungmin's expression stiffened. The Void Monarch was just as surprised, and the Magic Armor hummed in response.

[What? How does that Nine-Tailed Fox bitch know me?]

"...How do you know of the Void Monarch?" The Void Monarch's voice was inaudible to Yana, so Lee Sungmin asked on his behalf.

Yana's smile deepened slightly. Her gaze seemed to drift past the present, looking into the distant past. "Back when the Void Monarch was still alive. Five hundred years ago... I was once indebted to him."

[Indebted? What damn debt. I don't remember a thing.]

"What kind of debt?"

"The Void Monarch probably doesn't remember. At the time, I was nothing more than an ordinary fox."

[Then of course I don't remember... Wait. You were just a fox five hundred years ago?] The Void Monarch was horrified by the implication. [An ordinary fox became a Nine-Tailed Fox in just five hundred years? This is crazy. That's ridiculous!]

*Is that surprising?*

[Of course it's surprising. An ordinary fox has to live for over a hundred years just to gain a yokai nature and become a fox spirit. They usually grow one tail every two hundred years after that. It takes two thousand years to become a Nine-Tailed Fox. That's why the Nine-Tailed Fox is considered an especially powerful and special existence among the great yokai!]

The Void Monarch spat out his explanation. It took at least fifteen hundred years to become a Nine-Tailed Fox, and for an inhuman being, such a long time translated directly into power.

"You became a Nine-Tailed Fox in five hundred years?" Lee Sungmin asked aloud.

Yana remained silent.

[Unbelievable,] the Void Monarch muttered.

Yana continued to speak, her eyes on the constantly humming Magic Armor. "Is it not possible to speak directly with the Void Monarch?"

[Hmph.]

At her words, Demonic Energy surged from the armor. A flickering flame shot up above Lee Sungmin's head, coalescing into the Void Monarch's form as he looked down at Yana.

Yana looked up at him and gave a bright smile. "Void Monarch."

[Don't speak to me so familiarly. I don't remember you,] the Void Monarch replied harshly, but Yana's smile did not falter.

Her gaze shifted from the Void Monarch back to Lee Sungmin, focusing on his gold-tinged left eye.

"Not yet?" she asked.

"Pardon?"

"Void Monarch," Yana's voice lowered. Something slithered up from behind her back. Nine sleek tails, swaying like golden smoke, unfurled into the air. "I can help you."

[What are you talking about,] the Void Monarch's voice grew cold.

With her tails revealed, the atmosphere around Yana had changed completely. A destructive power, the same power said to have unilaterally defeated Red Ghost, the Yokai leader of Predator, pressed down on the entire room. Lee Sungmin unconsciously grabbed the spear he had set beside him.

"That human's body seems to be sufficiently prepared already," Yana continued. "If I help, Void Monarch, you will be able to take his body without any trouble."

"What?!" Lee Sungmin shot to his feet, startled.

In that same moment, Yana extended her hand. Two of her tails shot out in an instant, binding his arms and holding him fast.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon shouted and rushed at her, but another tail swung out and struck him.

KWAANG!

With a deafening crash, his body was slammed into the wall.

"I want to help you," Yana said, her attention never leaving the Void Monarch. "You lost your body in the fighting spirit subjugation four hundred years ago. You lost your power to despicable schemes, and the Dragons bound your body and destroyed it. When I learned of that, do you know how much I lamented my own powerlessness?"

The force of the tails binding his arms was too strong to shake off easily. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and summoned his strength. Yana's expression changed slightly at his resistance, and she sent two more tails to wrap around his arms.

"With that human's body, you should be able to get close to your prime, even if not quite there. What are you hesitating for? Do you doubt me?"

"Void Monarch...!" Lee Sungmin shouted, his voice rough.

The flickering flame above his head said nothing.

"That human's body is already steeped in Demonic Energy," Yana pressed on. "Although he hasn't completely turned into a Yokai, if I help, I can annihilate his soul and fully insert your soul into the empty shell. Isn't that why you possessed his armor? For this very moment, to seize his body and be resurrected. Wasn't it for this that you planted Demonic Energy in him?"

Hearing those words, Lee Sungmin's face contorted. He had suspected the Void Monarch at first, but as they weathered one crisis after another, those suspicions had faded. The Void Monarch had saved his life many times, and he had come to believe the spirit genuinely wanted to help him.

However, was all of that just for a moment like this? The Demonic Energy pooling in his qi center, his body slowly turning into a Yokai, his left eye becoming a Demonic Eye... was it all so the Void Monarch could steal this body?

[...Hmph.]

The Void Monarch snorted.

[Don't spout such nonsense. Do you think this Elder is doing all this just because I covet a mere body like that?] he spat. The flame of Demonic Energy that was his body swelled to a massive size. [Stop your foolish tricks. If you truly have my best interests at heart, then tuck in your tails, bow your head obediently, and apologize for this matter.]

His voice was thick with annoyance. At his words, Yana's eyes widened. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, cursing, pushed himself up from the wall, while Rubia stood flustered, not knowing what to do.

Seeing it all, the Void Monarch roared, his voice full of anger.

[Did you not understand my words!]

"...Really?" Yana flinched, her shoulders shrinking at his shout. She hesitated for a moment before sighing and nodding in response. "...I apologize."

She bowed her head. The crushing pressure vanished from the room, and the tails holding Lee Sungmin's arms dissipated like smoke.

Lee Sungmin lowered his freed arms and looked up at the Void Monarch.

[What, you bastard,] the spirit replied, his voice still laced with irritation. [Did you really think I'd just bite the bait and snatch your body like that Nine-Tailed Fox bitch said?]

"Yeah."

[You son of a bitch. You can't trust a person, no, a Yokai that much?!] the Void Monarch shouted in a fit of rage.
