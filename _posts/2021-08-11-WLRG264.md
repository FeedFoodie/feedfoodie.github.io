Forbidden from drawing out his power, Lee Sungmin could not use his movement arts. He did his best to run toward Haras, but his unassisted speed was far from satisfactory for the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who had already shot ahead in an instant.

"Useless brat."

The Chairman spat the words and reached a hand toward Lee Sungmin. An invisible force seized him, lifting his body into the air with Space Manipulation before mercilessly flinging him forward. An incredible speed pressed down on Lee Sungmin, but he endured the crushing force without much trouble.

He arrived at the walls of Haras in a flash, his body sailing over the ramparts before falling toward the ground inside. Lee Sungmin performed a practiced breakfall without panicking. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who was already waiting for him beyond the wall, addressed him.

"Sometimes, I have this thought."

"What thought is that?"

"That this one might be raising a monster." The Chairman clasped his hands behind his back as he began to walk slowly, the distant building of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance visible on the horizon.

Not understanding what his master meant, Lee Sungmin tilted his head and followed. "Are you raising a monster?"

The Chairman spoke without looking back. "It's a bit of an ambiguous statement, I admit. However, you have more than enough potential to become one."

"I'm sure you're not talking about my unremarkable talent."

"It's good that you know," the Chairman said. "Your talent is nothing that could be described as monstrous. Since it's worse than ordinary… I suppose you could be called a monster in a different sense."

"Do you really have to put it like that?"

"I believe it's better to make you an idiot who knows his place with a cold but accurate assessment, rather than turning you into a vermin who doesn't know his place with half-hearted praise."

"Then why did you take me as your disciple?"

"Didn't I tell you? I like struggling idiots."

At those words, Lee Sungmin firmly shut his mouth. Inside his head, the Void Monarch cackled.

[He said he liked a struggling mediocrity at first. Now you've gone from a mediocrity to an idiot.]

*Just shut up.*

Lee Sungmin cursed the mocking spirit and spat on the ground. He was used to the Chairman's barbs. He had long since felt and understood that beneath the harsh words, his master was considerate and cared for him in many ways.

"Then why on earth are you saying such things?"

"It's about potential," the Chairman replied. "Not you as a 'human', but the potential you possess. What do you think would have happened if you had completely transformed into a Yokai during your fight with the Spear King? That dreadful and ominous Demonic Energy you have… your body would have become that of a Yokai, not a human, but that doesn't mean you yourself would have truly been one, but what if you had completely become a Yokai? Your body would have mutated again from the immense Demonic Energy you possess. If that had happened, the Spear King would have died by your hand."

"…Is it to that extent?"

"It's just a guess, but the Demonic Energy you possess is not only vast, its quality is far too high. It might be the influence of the great yokai's remnants lingering inside you, but besides that, there's the Demonic Energy of Urmuri that you took… huhu. The fact that you've consumed both and can only manage that much is proof that you're vermin."

"Why do you have to put it like that again…"

"You hold many possibilities." The Chairman was still walking ahead of him.

Lee Sungmin recalled the rampaging Demonic Energy and swallowed a sigh. The power, not fully under his control, offered explosive strength, but it was a sharp, double-edged sword that could carve out his mind and seize his body at any moment.

"The fact that you might become a Yokai is your greatest potential, and this one thinks of it as a turning point for you. Will you remain human? Or become a monster? And if you become a monster, what kind will you be? What kind of Yokai? Having turned from a 'human' into a monster, you could be called an inhuman. Do you understand what I'm saying?"

"Predator."

"This one holds no distinct malice or hostility toward that group of inhuman species. I don't sympathize with the world of only humans that the Martial God babbled about. However, I know they are dangerous. Especially the queen of the vampires. She is a monster with immense power."

"Is she that strong?"

"I encountered that monster long ago. We didn't fight, as there was no hostility between us, but at the time, I was not confident of winning. That hasn't changed much even now. Because she possesses that level of power, even Heaven Beyond Heaven, with its six Transcendent Realm masters and the Martial God himself, could not wage an all-out war against Predator."

Lee Sungmin couldn't help but be surprised. He was well aware that Predator was strong and already knew what a terrifying monster Jeniela was, but to think that even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman couldn't be certain of victory was another matter entirely.

"Slaughter Predator. A monster whose existence has not even been confirmed, but for a long time, Predator has spoken of its existence, saying such a monster would one day appear. This one… thinks you are the closest being to it. The potential you have is the potential for the Slaughter Predator."

"So you're saying I'm a monster?"

"If you give up on being human, you will become a monster in the truest sense. I put a mask on you and taught you various martial arts to make you stronger as a 'human'. However, you're such an idiot that your speed of growing stronger as a human is too slow."

"…I have no desire to become a monster."

"If you do become one," the Chairman said in an indifferent voice, "this one intends to kill you."

His words made Lee Sungmin's expression harden as he stared at his master's back.

"If that happens, you will become a calamitous existence and end up doing countless things you do not wish to do. I'm not saying I'll kill you because you're dangerous. If you are forced to do things you don't want to, and the self that would regret it is annihilated… I believe that killing you with my own hands, as your Master, is both a courtesy to you and the proper martial theory for a Master."

"…Since I don't want to die, I'll have to hold on to my humanity."

"You are an existence shrouded in many questions." Before long, the building of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was near. "What's particularly questionable is the great yokai possessing you. Void Monarch. A Yokai who died four hundred years ago… At that time, the subjugation party included numerous Dragons, outstanding warriors, mages, and even shamans. It's said the most renowned figures gathered. The soul of a Yokai who should have been subjugated by them has leaped across hundreds of years to connect with you. Why you, of all people?"

[Because you were the one who returned.]

It was the Void Monarch who answered the Chairman's question. That was right. They had met in the Sleeping Forest. Although their meeting had been guided to some extent by Envirus, the one the Void Monarch had been waiting for was indeed Lee Sungmin. If something like fate existed, then him following Envirus to the Sleeping Forest must have been part of some grand destiny.

[A subjugation party… numerous Dragons. Outstanding warriors. Mages, shamans. Hmm… hmmm.]

The Void Monarch muttered, mulling over the Chairman's words. He barely remembered his own death. Hearing the account, Lee Sungmin thought that if a force of that magnitude had come, even the Void Monarch would have had no choice but to die.

[No.]

However, the Void Monarch denied it.

[According to the power I remember having, this Elder would never die to a subjugation party of that level. Numerous Dragons? No matter how many gather, lizards are just lizards. Humans aren't even worth mentioning. If the magic of Dragons couldn't harm this Elder, do you think human magic or shamanism would work? Or that their martial arts could have cornered me?]

*Then why in the world did you die?*

[I don't know. I have no idea. Why did I die? There's no way I died to a subjugation party of that level. Did I eat something bad?]

The Void Monarch grumbled. His death had long been a mystery to them both, but there was no one in this world who could explain it. Besides that, there were other questions. Why hadn't he vanished upon death, instead remaining as a soul bound to the Sleeping Forest? Why had he been waiting for Lee Sungmin? And what exactly was inside that part of the forest where he had been?

RUSTLE.

The questions could not continue. He could not find the answers just by pondering them. Lee Sungmin stopped walking, his eyes widening at a small sound in the night.

"It seems she was in a hurry," the Chairman muttered.

CRACKLE. Sparks of electricity flew. The Chairman concealed his body beyond the black currents and said, "I'll leave you to it. You must have a lot to talk about."

Thanks to his master's consideration, Lee Sungmin was able to stand face to face with Baek Sogo. The woman he hadn't seen in years had changed a lot. Her hair, which had once been close to gray, was now completely white, and her eyes were so deep one could not see their end. The changed hair color and the deepened eyes gave her a completely different atmosphere from the person he remembered.

"…Senior Sister."

Lee Sungmin called out to her in a small voice. Meeting Baek Sogo, his Senior Sister, was something he had pictured many, many times. He had waited and hoped for it, but not today, not in a place like this, and certainly not so suddenly. He had never imagined a reunion like this. Lee Sungmin hesitated, not knowing what to say.

*Should I make an excuse?*

The thought came to him unbidden. The Senior Sister he knew, the one he remembered, was a person faithful to her beliefs. Righteousness, chivalry, and justice were the pillars that had sustained Silent Flash Baek Sogo. It was why she had belonged to the Murim Alliance when they reunited after he first came down from Mount Mushii.

She had left the Alliance after the incident in the Dungeon, and he hadn't seen her since, only hearing of her through occasional rumors. He knew she had climbed Mount Mushii again and then descended. The Chairman had told him that she had met Denir, undergone his trial, and become his incarnation.

However, even after all that, he couldn't imagine her beliefs had crumbled. She was a good person. A just person. A righteous person. The Baek Sogo he had known on Mount Mushii was always that kind of person. Even if she had been disappointed in the Murim Alliance, her convictions would not have collapsed.

To such a person, how did he appear now? It was an undeniable fact that he had killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear, and while he wasn't involved in the deaths of Moyong Seojin and Jaegal Taeryeong, he had no way to prove his innocence. Furthermore, he had become the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the man at the pinnacle of the very forces that stood opposite the orthodox sects.

*How do I look in her eyes? How will she accept me? Should I try to explain?*

"…Senior Sister," Lee Sungmin called out once more.

Baek Sogo, who had been silent, simply shook her head.

*What does that mean?* As he opened his mouth to ask, her body rustled forward until she was right in front of him. He could feel her breath, and a faint scent of rapeseed flowers drifted from her.

"Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo opened her arms and pulled Lee Sungmin into an embrace. He froze on the spot, bewildered. She gently closed her eyes and held him tighter. Lee Sungmin looked down at the top of her head, at a loss for words.

"…It's been a long time," Baek Sogo whispered. "I'm glad you're not dead. I'm glad we could meet again. I'm glad that you're not hurt, Junior Disciple… that you're safe."

"…Senior Sister."

"I'm sorry," Baek Sogo said, her voice trembling.
