"Hahaha!"

Kim Jonghyun laughed again from above. Below him, Lee Sungmin had pushed himself to his feet, radiating an aura more sinister than any being Kim Jonghyun had ever witnessed. While it paled in comparison to the Demon Lords of the Other Dimension, the energy coiling around Lee Sungmin was nothing a mortal could produce.

[This is…!]

A flustered sound escaped the Void Monarch. To his disbelief, his own Demonic Energy was being drawn toward Lee Sungmin. It made no sense. Even as a Yokai, Lee Sungmin's status was nothing compared to his own, yet the monarch could not control his power. He could only watch as Lee Sungmin siphoned it away.

Golden Demonic Eyes stared out from the risen Lee Sungmin, seeing nothing. A crimson Demonic Energy shimmered around him, creating the same disgusting and terrifying haze of distorted Death Masks that Preskan had witnessed.

He made no move to find his spear. While his consciousness fought desperately against the forced cradle of his mind, his body had already become a beast, surrendering to a frenzy beyond his control.

"......"

Envirus, barely maintaining his defensive barrier, let out a cry of despair. The fate of Cain, a catastrophic death, was a destiny he had once again acquired. Just moments ago, he had believed it was not yet his time, but was that truly the case? In this worst-case scenario, unable to cast his magic properly or even maintain a simple barrier, what could he possibly do? What awaited him but that very catastrophic death he so feared?

*Not yet…!*

Envirus raised his bloodshot eyes, his gaze fixed on Lee Sungmin.

Aine's assault ceased. The blades that had erupted from her body retracted, and despite having unleashed a barrage powerful enough to reshape the terrain, she showed no sign of fatigue. She turned, her expression blank, and fixed her gaze on Lee Sungmin. Her unconscious body was a slave to instinct, and the Black Heart beating within her chest yearned for the one inside him. It demanded she rip it out and devour it.

Lee Sungmin's body was a calm sea after a storm of fighting spirit, utterly silent, but the calm of the sea is always temporary. Aine's body shot forward like a released spring, rushing toward him. The storm was about to rage once more.

The calm was over.

As Aine charged, Lee Sungmin turned his eyes toward her. Black tentacles erupted from the ground, centered on her advance. A moment later, he raised a hand, his open palm caressing the empty air as if to soothe it. The frenzy was ready to be unleashed, yet its beginning was deceptively quiet.

The tentacles lunging to tear him apart froze before his palm. They had no choice. The Demonic Energy rising from beneath his feet had seized every one of Aine's lances, holding them fast.

"Uuuh…"

A low sound, like an echo in a cave, rumbled from deep within Lee Sungmin's throat, long and trembling. Aine's ferocity only grew in the face of her thwarted attack. She became even more frenzied, leaping toward him with her right hand forward and her left hand clenched into a fist. Even her instincts knew what was coming. Tentacles shot up from her shoulders, intertwining to form a desperate defense.

KWAANG!

The brutish punch shattered her defensive tentacles. The impact was too much to withstand, and Aine's body was slammed violently into the ground.

"......"

The silence was over. Lee Sungmin roared and kicked off the ground, grabbing Aine's tumbling body and rolling with her. He opened his mouth wide, intent on tearing out her throat with his teeth, but even with her head half-crushed, Aine did not die. Unlike him, a human with a Black Heart embedded inside, her entire body was composed of one. Her life force and regenerative abilities were practically immortal. She raised both hands, grabbing his face as blades shot from her body, trying to tear him apart. The hazy aura around Lee Sungmin materialized, deflecting the blades.

Watching the dog-eat-dog fight, a savage struggle where each tried to devour the other, Envirus was at a loss for words. He felt no relief that they were fighting each other, because whoever survived would undoubtedly become a monster capable of rewriting history.

*Should I stop them? Or should I run?*

He was conflicted. In his current state, he could not handle either of them, but if he ran to save his own life, what would happen then?

Envirus bit his lower lip hard. He had never wished for Lee Sungmin to become imbued with Demonic Energy. He had gone to the Sleeping Forest for his own reasons, not to lure Lee Sungmin there. It had all just flowed that way by coincidence. Lee Sungmin passing Denir's trial, hearing of Envirus, and giving chase was, in the end, a coincidence.

In the Sleeping Forest, Envirus had met the Void Monarch. He had learned that the monarch was waiting for 'the Returned One' and that this person was Lee Sungmin. He also learned that Lee Sungmin was chasing him. Someday, perhaps soon, Lee Sungmin would follow his trail to this very forest and meet the Void Monarch. It had already become an absolute, nemesis-like flow of fate, and Envirus knew he could not defy it. That was why he had left Rubia behind, to continuously monitor Lee Sungmin's condition.

Where did it go wrong? Should he not have gone to the Sleeping Forest? When he met the Void Monarch's spirit, should he have challenged him, however impossible the task, and attempted to seal or annihilate him completely? Should he have warned the clan that maintained the forest's seal to never let Lee Sungmin enter?

No, what was the point of such thoughts now? It had already come to this. It was Envirus himself who, unable to decide, had chosen to simply go with the flow. This was the result. Perhaps it was always destined to be this way.

*My choices and actions might not matter.*

Envirus steadied his breathing and gave up on maintaining the defensive barrier. If he was already swept up in a great current, his own choice was meaningless. No matter how great an archmage he was, the best in the world, he was still just a mortal. He could not change the flow of fate. He knew that to a sickening degree.

"Fucking fate," Envirus spat with genuine feeling. The damage he had sustained from the reverse summoning was spiritual, something no elixir could heal. A mana potion would be equally useless.

*However, with this.*

A lonely smile touched his lips as he slowly raised both arms. Unlike in the Sleeping Forest, where he had given up choosing, Envirus now made a choice for himself. All the runic characters on his arms floated into the air.

"O, great Mana."

A low voice flowed from his lips. Kim Jonghyun, who had been watching from the sky like a bystander, looked surprised. He too had been a mage before becoming a Black Mage and knew what Envirus was about to do.

"I offer this life."

Life and Mana Exchange. It was a technique to gain immense mana by sacrificing one's own life. Since a person has only one life to give, the exchange would inevitably lead to death.

WHOOOSH!

A vast torrent of mana flooded Envirus's body, the enormous power instantly healing his spiritual wounds. He reached for the black sphere Arbes had left behind.

CRACKLE!

The sphere flared blue as the planet's mana connected with him.

On the ground below, Aine lay sprawled and bloodied. The blades and tentacles that had shot from her body had all been torn off by Lee Sungmin's hands, yet she still resisted. Her body, crushed several times, regenerated without rest while Lee Sungmin continued his frenzy, rampaging to devour her.

Envirus watched them both with calm, sunken eyes.

*If possible, both of them.*

*I can't let it be taken.*

Lee Sungmin was still holding onto his consciousness, but his body would not listen to his screams. It just rampaged greedily, driven to devour Aine, yet even that body was not solely focused on predation. It sensed the immense magic power rushing toward it and leaped away. Aine, too, hurriedly threw her body aside.

PWHAACK!

Crimson flames swept across the land.

Confirming that both had escaped the attack's range, Envirus immediately prepared his next spell. His magic power was overflowing, but he had no time. Even now, his life was racing toward its end.

Who was the most dangerous?

Lee Sungmin.

Envirus spread his hands and aimed. The runic characters on his arms squirmed and rose as immense mana, refined into magic, exploded forth. Lee Sungmin, who had leaped into the air, swung both arms toward the oncoming wave of power.

KWADEDEUK!

The magic was torn asunder, splitting into two streams that pierced the sky. Even seeing it with his own eyes, Envirus could not believe it.

*Right. That's why he's even more dangerous.*

"I'm sorry," Envirus apologized sincerely. In a way, Lee Sungmin was also a victim, but he had no intention of stopping.

Next. Envirus considered the countless spells he could cast, searching for one that could more efficiently capture the rampaging beast.

"......"

Space shattered, scattering like glass. Silver chains shot from the rifts, lashing toward Lee Sungmin in the air. He thrashed, trying to shatter them, but they bent flexibly, wrapping around his joints. The captured chains then funneled a massive amount of magic into his body, causing his limbs to swell and bend in unnatural directions.

*No.*

*I don't know why Envirus is attacking me.*

*But one thing is certain. This body, overflowing with power, is unbelievably brutish. It isn't using the Void Path, just rampaging with its own physical performance. That would be enough against most opponents. The Demonic Energy is overflowing. I feel like I could defeat even the Dark Supreme with this body, even without martial arts.*

*But Envirus is different. His spells are harder to deal with and more powerful than any of the Dark Supreme's hidden weapons.*

*If this continues, I'll be killed by Envirus. I don't want to die.*

*But my body won't listen.*

KWAANG!

Lee Sungmin's consciousness reeled, and his vision changed in a flash.

"What are you doing, you bastard!"

The one who had jumped in was the Void Monarch, appearing in the same form he had shown in Lee Sungmin's dream long ago. The black cradle was shattered by his kick. Lee Sungmin, now a conscious entity separate from his real body, was grabbed by the collar.

"Get a grip."

The Void Monarch bared his teeth and growled before raising a fist and slapping Lee Sungmin hard across the cheek. Lee Sungmin cried out in pain.

"Why'd you hit me?!"

"That's for all the times you threatened to dunk this Elder in a cesspool. You damn bastard. Be grateful I'm letting you off with just one punch to the face!" the Void Monarch spat, releasing his collar.

Though it was not his real body, the stinging pain was definite. Lee Sungmin looked around the space, a swirling mix of red and black.

"Where… is this?"

"It's your mental world," the Void Monarch answered. "No different from a dream."

"Why am I here… no, why are you here?"

"I came to beat you awake," he replied. "If possible, this Elder wanted to do it alone, but your mental barrier was too strong. I couldn't do anything. That's why I needed to wake your consciousness first."

"So what are you trying to do?"

"Are you going to die like this?" the Void Monarch asked, his eyes wide. "Yeah, well. People die, but do you want to die like this? Didn't you say you didn't want to become a Yokai? Look at yourself now. Your body has become a Yokai, and your consciousness, what's left of your humanity, was about to fall asleep in the cradle of the inhuman. If you had fallen completely asleep there, your humanity would have fallen into an eternal slumber, and inhumanity would have filled the void. Do you know what that means?"

"…That I'd become a Yokai."

"It means you become a being that is you, but not you, you bastard!"

"It's not like I wanted this to happen…!"

"It's not too late yet," the Void Monarch said, turning around. "This is a first. You're… too strange. To think you've maintained your humanity even after your body became a Yokai. No matter how strong your divine power is…"

"You said it's not too late," Lee Sungmin interrupted.

The Void Monarch flinched, then nodded. "…Look over there." He raised a finger. "That's the reason I said you're strange. Perhaps because your human consciousness remains… the Yokai-like inhumanity is separate. If you had fallen asleep in the cradle, that thing would have become you."

Beyond a shimmering wall, a figure was rampaging madly. Its body was formed of a sinister color, and it thrashed about as if fighting an invisible foe.

"That's you," the Void Monarch said, raising his hand to point at the sinister, rampaging being. "The inhumanity that's moving your body right now. The you that has turned into a Yokai. If only this Elder could break through that wall, I would have beaten the crap out of that bastard." He grumbled and plopped down on the floor.

"You go and beat that thing up."

"And then?"

"This is a Tug-of-War Between Natures. It's about who gains the upper hand. Right now, that thing has the upper hand and is controlling your body… but if you gain the upper hand, you might get your body back."

There was no certainty. A case like Lee Sungmin's was a first, and since the Void Monarch was a Yokai from birth, he could only speak in vague terms.

"It's not like you can do nothing, right? Time in the mental world is just a brief moment in reality… but your body is approaching death right now."

"I know."

Before the Void Monarch had awakened him, Lee Sungmin had seen his own body bound by Envirus's chains. There was not much time. He strode toward the blurry wall.

*I never thought the day would come when I'd have to fight myself.*

He figured that if you live long enough, days like this are bound to happen.
