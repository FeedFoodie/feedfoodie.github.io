I had always known the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was a man whose words rarely matched his true intentions. He had called the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance a nuisance, and knowing his personality, that was likely no lie, but that didn't mean he ever neglected it.

For decades, even while absent, he had managed it from the shadows. He implanted terminals in the heads of his branch chiefs and placed trusted subordinates within its ranks to monitor for any discord. Upon awakening from his long silence, his first act was to send his successor, me, to root out the seeds of conflict festering within the alliance.

I could never know what the Chairman, now dead, no, now transcended, truly felt about the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

However, I was certain of one thing. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance had once been his home, a group ruled and led by the great Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon himself, and though it was I who disbanded it, my choice was merely the execution of his final will.

To the public, the Blood Alliance was simply the successor to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. The only difference they saw was a new leader and the addition of Black Mages. Some even whispered that the Blood Demon was the Chairman's hidden disciple, a rumor that was understandable. After all, he was an absolute master who had appeared from nowhere, gathering the scattered factions to forge his new organization.

I didn't like that.

The Void Monarch had been right. I wasn't just 'a little' annoyed. I was 'very' annoyed. The irritating chatter of people who knew nothing grated on me.

The Blood Demon was not the Chairman's disciple. The Blood Alliance was not the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's successor.

And that was why I wore the mask.

"Uh..."

Mouths agape, the guards at the castle gate could only stare blankly at my approach. I offered them no explanation for the hideous goblin mask I wore. I had come for invasion and for war. With Jeniela confirmed to be in the North, the time was perfect to cut the Blood Demon off from his master.

[Have you made up your mind?]

*There's no other way. If killing the Blood Demon will only return his power to Jeniela... then I have to make sure it doesn't return.*

[So you mean to devour him.]

The mysterious phenomena I unleashed dominated the space. The light drained from the eyes of the guards, who had been frozen in confusion at the sight of my mask. I left them to stagger and collapse.

"I'll go on ahead."

With those words, my feet kicked off the ground, and I shot forward like a bolt of lightning.

The Black Dragon Knight watched me disappear into the distance and shook his head.

"I wonder why he brought us along if he was just going to do this."

Despite his complaint, he picked up his speed to follow, and Yana did the same.

I leaped into the unobstructed sky above the city, flying in a straight line toward the Blood Alliance's headquarters. It was the shortest route. For a moment, I considered smashing through the main gate, but I dismissed the idea. I couldn't risk the Blood Demon escaping while I wasted time on such theatrics.

However, the Blood Demon did not run.

He stood in the very center of the pavilion, hands clasped behind his back in a relaxed posture. His black hair was tied up, and he wore a red dragon robe. He could feel the presence of a Great Being closing the distance, yet he felt no fear. His narrowed eyes tracked the approach, but he made no move to flee.

Instead, the Blood Demon's red-gleaming eyes were fixed on something far beyond the pavilion's scenery.

"Perhaps, no, undoubtedly. I will die."

Even as he spoke of his own death, his voice was calm. He was seeing Jeniela, far away at the edge of the North. He did not use honorifics when speaking to the Lord who stood at the pinnacle of vampires. In fact, if one considered age alone, he was older than she was.

"Aren't you even going to worry about me?" the Blood Demon asked with a smirk.

In the North, Jeniela faced him, a gentle smile on her face.

"You're the one who wished for death, aren't you?"

"That's right."

He nodded. He had lived for hundreds of years, a span he would have approached even as a human, and he was weary of it all. Destruction was inevitable anyway. Everything would end soon enough. While it might have been interesting to witness the ruin Jeniela would create, the Blood Demon preferred to die like this.

"There's always a chance, you know," Jeniela whispered.

"Maybe you won't die."

"We'll have to see about that."

Even as he spoke, the Blood Demon felt the premonition of his own death. He knew that if it were a moonlit night, he would 'definitely' win, but it was early morning, with no moon in the sky. *He's coming soon.* The Blood Demon prepared for the end.

"Do you have any last words?"

"It might not be the last."

"No, it will be the last."

"In that case... thank you for everything until now. I hope I can see you again inside me later."

"It won't be that easy," the Blood Demon said with a chuckle.

Seeing him, Jeniela's smile wavered slightly. For the first time in a long while, she felt a strange emotion. *Is this loss? Or regret?* She had long forgotten such feelings, but now, facing a parting with the second of her vampires, a companion of hundreds of years, the forgotten emotions stirred, if only slightly.

"Thank you."

"You always made requests I didn't like, but your last request was fine. This is quite an enjoyable task."

"I'm glad you liked it."

KWAAAAANG!

A blast of enhanced qi, wreathed in violet electricity, shattered the pavilion's ceiling, sending fragments scattering through the air.

Amid the raging wind, the Blood Demon lowered the hands he had clasped behind his back. He had hoped, since it was the end, that she might call him father. He chuckled to himself. They had grown too old for such words. A distant memory surfaced. Jeniela, standing in a pile of corpses, holding the hand of a young Gemini.

*Was that really 700 years ago?*

She had brazenly asked to be his adopted daughter, and he had found it so cute that he agreed. Ten years later, he became the second of her vampires.

"How violent," the Blood Demon remarked, looking up at me as I hovered in the sky.

I looked down at him, my expression unshaken. The middle-aged man smiled gently and beckoned.

"I have no intention of running, nor do I plan on any tricks. It seems you've come to kill me... If you have the ability, then by all means, kill me."

*This is... unexpected.*

Beneath the mask, my eyebrows twitched. I could glimpse the extent of his power. Looking up from a low place, one cannot see the top, but from a high place, many things become clear. The Blood Demon's realm was not fully visible, but I could tell that his martial theory was at a higher level than my own.

I knew he was one of Jeniela's vampires, a being of immense power. That was all the more reason to cut him down here. It would be a headache if he joined Jeniela later, or if she simply killed him and absorbed his power herself.

[You must not kill him.] the Void Monarch advised. [Don't forget. If you kill him, his power will return to Jeniela. If that much power is added to her, that bitch will become an unstoppable monster.]

*I have to devour him.*

"What is the Void Monarch saying?" the Blood Demon asked with a smile.

[That arrogant bastard. How dare he speak my name so carelessly without knowing his place?] the Void Monarch seethed.

I ignored him, gripping my spear with both hands and taking my stance. The Blood Demon clicked his tongue as he watched me.

"There's no need to rush. Didn't I say? I'm not running, and I'm not pulling any tricks. You're a martial artist challenging me to a death match, and I intend to face you as one. I just want to talk a little first."

"...Why don't you run? Does that mean I'm not threatening enough for you to flee?"

"No, that's not it," the Blood Demon denied, shaking his head. His actions were unhurried and relaxed, which was why I couldn't understand his attitude. "You are plenty threatening. What's especially threatening is that despite having the body of a monster the likes of which this world has never seen, your spirit... that is, your martial arts, don't match your body."

"Why is that threatening?"

"Because it means you still have room to grow," the Blood Demon laughed heartily. "If there's no room for growth in body and spirit, one can't be called a threat. After all, growing beyond that point is difficult unless a great miracle occurs. Especially, as you know, once you reach a certain level, it becomes even harder to advance. You, however, are different. Because your body is far ahead of your martial arts, you can easily create variables that would be impossible with martial arts alone. You have room for growth."

Unlike me, the Blood Demon added.

"I have been stagnant for hundreds of years. I've continued to practice my martial arts, but they've barely improved. It might have been different if I had remained human, but... huhu, that's a meaningless thing to say now."

"...You left the North and created the Blood Alliance here. Why?"

"It was to create chaos. At the time, Jeniela intended to use me to create the Blood Alliance and start a war. That was one of the pictures she had designed. She wanted to cause chaos through war, incite conflict everywhere, and ruin this world. In truth, that wasn't the future Jeniela had seen. At that point, she could barely see the future... It seems she intended to use me as a variable of fate, but it looks like it didn't work out in the end. Do you understand what I'm saying?"

The Blood Demon shrugged his shoulders.

"I am a disposable piece."

"...She used a vampire of your caliber as a disposable piece?"

"It's because I'm a vampire of this caliber that she used me as a disposable piece. She must have thought that someone with my level of power would be enough to twist fate. Well, it was a failure in the end, though."

"So... is this how she's disposing of you?"

"Disposing, you say. I was the one who wished for it to be this way."

The Blood Demon took a few steps back, shrugging off the dragon robe he wore.

"I am a martial artist, after all. I want to die fighting a strong martial artist."

I silently watched him take off the robe. He rolled up his sleeves, interlaced his fingers, and stretched.

"It's been a long time since I've done this."

"...You... what's your name?"

"Ju Heebaek. You wouldn't know it. The time I made a name for myself as a martial artist was 700 years ago. Back then, there was no Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, no Sword Immortal, and no Martial God. Everyone from my era is dead. No matter how profound your martial arts become, living for 700 years is impossible. Unless you become a vampire like me, that is."

"Why did you become a vampire?"

"It's a common story. A parent can't help but give in to their child's pestering."

*Parent... child...?* I tilted my head, not understanding. Having finished his light warm-up, the Blood Demon dusted off his hands and spoke.

"Shall we begin?"

KWAAANG!

A crash echoed from below. Yana and the Black Dragon Knight, arriving later than me, had just smashed through the main gate of the Blood Alliance.

"Does wearing a mask mean you've come to punish me as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's disciple?" the Blood Demon mused. "It would make a good story for the gossips to chew on. That is, if you kill me and make a grand escape from this place."

I silently raised my spear.

"The martial art I use is called the Asura Heaven Destruction Art," he warned, thrusting both his hands forward. "You'd best be careful. 700 years ago, it was known as the world's number one."

A powerful energy erupted from the Blood Demon's body.
