That was it. He did not want to die. He must not die. The reasons why did not matter, not here, not now. He simply could not die. From what felt like a great distance, he heard Scarlet's warning shout. Lee Sungmin gripped his spear and lowered his stance.

Aine's martial arts were clumsy. Though she could perform all of Zeon's techniques, she lacked his mastery. She possessed the memories and the same proficiency, but Aine herself was inexperienced in their use. Her fighting style was to cover for that clumsiness with immense magic power and the sheer unpredictability of her physical transformations. If Scarlet had not been supporting him, Lee Sungmin would have been killed already.

No, perhaps he was already dead.

A tentacle grazed him, and he knew he lacked the strength to parry or redirect her attacks. The world's sounds grew distant. Forgetting to breathe, Lee Sungmin dashed forward. The tentacle that had narrowly missed his head shifted its shape, and his sixth sense screamed of an attack stabbing at his back.

He answered with the Second Form of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, One Step Dancing Shadow. His body left a dancing afterimage as he maintained his charging speed and unleashed Soul Chasing Elimination, the one strike of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

Aine did not dodge. Her bare, carapace-covered body met his spear.

"......"

With a dull thud, the spear trembled violently in his hands. The weapon, infused with so much of his inner qi, failed to pierce her body.

With no time to force the spearhead deeper, Lee Sungmin released his grip and moved his feet. He disengaged with One Step Dancing Shadow just as Aine's fist obliterated the spot where he had stood.

Not even the smallest mistake was permissible. It was just like the fight with the Ogre Chimera, no, it was even worse. He had possessed some leeway when responding to the Ogre Chimera's attacks, but now he had none. There could be no room for error.

*If it weren't for Scarlet.*

The thought exposed his own powerlessness, a truth he had first realized in Preskan's Dungeon. How insignificant a so-called peak realm master truly was. He had believed he had shed his larval skin, not yet a cicada but something more, yet the reality of his pathetic state was undeniable.

His opponent was that kind of monster.

Even with the same heart as him, she was beyond comparison. She was the monster who had killed Zeon and pushed Lloyd to the brink of death.

*Could I do it?*

Could he stand against Zeon, the leader of the Corona Mercenary Group? Or against Lloyd?

What would change, what would be gained, by thinking such thoughts?

He wanted to see the end of martial arts. He had actually thought such things. When he left Mount Mushii, Lee Sungmin had been under the delusion that he was a seeker walking a solitary path. He had luck and connections to compensate for his meager talent, and he had packaged himself with the label of 'effort'. Yes, that had been the Lee Sungmin of the past.

However, now, he saw only his own weakness. He found himself wondering what Wiji Hoyeon or Baek Sogo would do, but thinking from their perspective was just an escape, a half-surrender. He had resolved to see the end of martial arts, to work hard, to train himself, yet he had relied on the Magic Armor to overcome a wall he could not surmount, never even trying to break it or leap over it himself.

It was because he knew reality. Lee Sungmin had told himself that several times before falling asleep. Completing requests was easy, but they were S-Rank requests at best. His skills had not improved, and his martial arts accomplishments had not increased. Outside of Mount Mushii, he could not receive rewards proportional to his hard work. So he ran away. From effort. From training his martial arts.

*Is this why I am dying?*

"......"

Lee Sungmin's body tumbled across the ground. A small mercy, he thought, that the Magic Armor, though stripped of its original abilities, still functioned as excellent protection. Without it, his injuries would not have been so minor.

Still, the injuries were severe. Though nothing showed on the outside, the damage inside was heavy. As Aine lunged toward his fallen form, Scarlet hurriedly cast her magic to keep the monster in check.

The world was a cacophony, a loud explosion that churned his sore stomach. A metallic taste filled his mouth, his head throbbed, and his heart pounded against his ribs. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath as he pushed himself up. His left side pulsed with pain. He did not know if it was from the blow, the fall, or both.

He had not even realized night had fallen, the world now cloaked in darkness. Even to his layman's eyes, Scarlet's magic was incredible. Each time her hand swept through the air, unknown characters embroidered the void before manifesting as violent explosions.

Watching her, Lee Sungmin felt the bitterness in his mouth was like poison. What welled up from under his tongue was not saliva but a potent toxin. He hated it. He hated his own powerlessness. Lee Sungmin dragged his feet forward.

*I. I had to change. I wanted to change.* He knew that the world changed when 'he' changed. He had learned that on Mount Mushii. The Lee Sungmin of that time had certainly changed. He had become different from the Lee Sungmin of the past, the one before the mountain.

It just had not been enough. For someone like her, a person commonly called a 'calamity', everything would be easy. Moving forward, changing. A single step for a calamity was equal to several, or even tens of steps for a mortal.

Lee Sungmin, who was not a calamity, had to keep walking. A single epiphany was not enough. He had to attain enlightenment again and again.

About himself. He had to understand reality. He had to understand how different his ideals were from the reality crashing down on him.

*I know.* He pieced together his scattered thoughts. *I... I know.* He tightened his loosening grip on the spear. *I know I'm insignificant. I know I'm pathetic, and I know I'm not a calamity. I know the 'change' I felt and experienced wasn't all that special. I know I'm an idiot who acted like a seeker aiming for the end of martial arts, only to give up and despair at the first wall I met.*

*And yet, I am looking at such high places.*

*My ideals are too high.*

*I want too much.*

Amidst his self-loathing, Lee Sungmin silently began to walk. Scarlet was an excellent mage, but her Spell Engraving was an incomplete art. Its magic consumption was inefficient, and while its incorporation of Qimen Dunjia and the Yin-Yang Path was effective against the magic-immune Aine, it lacked decisive power. The firepower the current Scarlet could muster was far too insufficient to determine the outcome. Aine was an ultimate lifeform, her absurd regenerative abilities allowing her to heal again and again as she tried to overwhelm Scarlet.

Lee Sungmin saw it all.

He saw it from the depths of his disgusting self-loathing, crushed by his own sense of powerlessness. He hated it. That revulsion was what compelled him to move. He did not want Scarlet to die. He did not want to die either. There was a reason he could not die.

*I have not changed yet.*

As she lunged toward Scarlet, Aine sensed a sharpness from behind. It was a shallow attack, one she could have ignored, but she deliberately turned. From the start, it was not Scarlet who irritated Aine, but Lee Sungmin. She did not know who he was, only that she disliked him, that he irritated her. Since she knew nothing about solving such problems, she had reached a simple conclusion.

*Wouldn't it all be over if I just killed him?*

Watching Lee Sungmin thrust his spear, Aine bared her teeth and smiled. It was a light, slow attack. Taking it with her bare body would be no problem at all.

And that was exactly what happened. A light swing of her hand sent Lee Sungmin's spear flying pathetically upward. The skin of his palm tore, and his elbow joint groaned, unable to withstand the force. He instinctively twisted his body to avoid her other hand, her sharp claws aiming for his exposed chest.

KAGAGAK!

With a sharp, metallic screech, the Magic Armor covering his side was sliced open. Blood poured from the gash. Any deeper and his intestines might have spilled out.

He could not let his guard down for an instant. Aine was the predator, and he was the prey she was toying with. A fight cannot exist between a tiger and a rabbit. No matter how a rabbit struggles, it is bound to die from a single playful swipe of a tiger's paw.

Lee Sungmin hopped about like a rabbit. Or perhaps it was more accurate to call him a flea. He moved to evade Aine's violence, to survive. His ceaselessly moving feet performed the arts of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, and between each step, he forced his groaning arms to execute the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Scarlet could no longer support him. She gasped for breath, gathering her magic, and could only watch him with a look of complex emotions.

Even if she were to flee, Lee Sungmin would not blame her, but Scarlet had no intention of running. She was seriously contemplating whether or not to use her final gambit.

Of course, Lee Sungmin knew nothing of this. He did not have the luxury to care.

KWAAANG!

A kick from Aine sent his body flying backward. His legs gave out, and he almost collapsed, but he barely managed to stay standing. Dark blood gushed from between his tightly clenched lips. Aine cackled, her transformed hands huge and sharp.

*The spear is heavy.*

It was a weight he had not felt in a long time, not once since coming down from the mountain. His body was heavy, too. His battered eardrums rang with a dull drone, and the scent of blood wafted into his nose.

*I am going to die.*

*Is this my end? I'll die without meeting Wiji Hoyeon, without saving Baek Sogo, and after needlessly dragging Scarlet into this to die with me. A sudden death, just like always.* There was still inner qi left in his qi center. *This damn body isn't worth its keep.* Lee Sungmin suppressed a laugh as he drew upon his inner qi. He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art. He circulated his inner qi. Watching Aine charge at him, he thought that it still was not time. It was not his time to die yet.

*Because I can still move.*

Not many people could welcome death. Those with lingering regrets certainly did not wish for it. Even those without regrets would likely not remain composed in the face of their end. Most people would struggle desperately. Lee Sungmin struggled, too. Countless reasons why he could not die existed, and they became the lingering attachments that drove him.

There was no one here who could save him. Not Wiji Hoyeon, not Baek Sogo. Not even Lloyd. To survive, 'he' had to do it himself. He could not rely on anyone else. Just as the world had changed when he changed, the agent of all action was 'himself'.

*His mind felt a little clearer.*

Scarlet came to a decision. No matter how much she deliberated, there were only two possible answers. Run, or fight. Running was out of the question for her from the very beginning. The only reason she had hesitated was simply because it felt like a waste.

She could not afford to be picky in this situation, however. Scarlet clicked her tongue and reached into her robes. It was at that moment.

A swell of purple smoke erupted from Lee Sungmin's spear. Aine, who had been lunging forward, instinctively knew it was dangerous. She immediately halted her charge and widened the distance between them.

The billowing smoke coiled and condensed, transforming from a faint haze into a shimmering light, no, a blazing, burning flame. Aine, seeing such a light for the first time, stared with wide eyes, her mouth agape. Lee Sungmin, his face pale from blood loss, quietly gazed down at the purple flame enveloping his spear.

He felt like crying.

When he thrust the spear forward, it was not a technique from the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. It was 'Chal' from Rannachal, the simple thrust he had practiced more than any other. Yes. It was a simple thrust. It was not particularly fast, but Aine could no longer ignore it. She responded with a hardened expression, her carapace-covered hand moving to block the spear.

She failed to block it. The moment the spearhead made contact, the carapace disintegrated. Before the metal blade could even penetrate, Aine's palm was pierced through. Neither speed nor strength was necessary. The spearhead pushed forward without resistance and pierced Aine's right shoulder.

Aine screamed. She, who knew no pain, found this sensation alien. The wound was not regenerating. Her sharp shriek turned into a roar as she swung her left hand. Lee Sungmin took half a step back and rotated the spear shaft using Ran's Technique.

Caught in the rotation, Aine's left arm was torn off. This, too, required no great strength. Scarlet froze, her hand still inside her robes, while Aine's eyes widened in horror. Lee Sungmin was incapable of complex thought. He attacked Aine again using a Rannachal technique, and the terrified Aine twisted her torso. The spot where it grazed her split open with a tearing sound, and blood gushed out.

"Ah, ah, it hurts!" Aine shrieked.

She wanted to clutch the wound, but she no longer had both her hands. Aine leaped backward. Then, without hesitation, she started to run away. Lee Sungmin moved to chase her, but he collapsed after just a few steps. His head spun, and blood streamed from his nose. Scarlet did not pursue Aine either, instead rushing to his side.

"...Ah."

Lee Sungmin did not watch Aine retreat. He simply looked at his spear, which he still held in his hand. The flame that had enveloped it was gone.

"I did it," Lee Sungmin muttered as he let his fading consciousness go.

What Lee Sungmin had just unleashed was enhanced qi.
