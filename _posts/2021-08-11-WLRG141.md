Lee Sungmin caught Wiji Hoyeon as she staggered, her legs giving out from under her. Just as she was about to collapse, he shot out an arm and wrapped it securely around her waist, supporting her weight.

"...Ahaha!"

A sudden laugh burst from Wiji Hoyeon's lips. As he steadied the swaying girl, Lee Sungmin glanced down at her, puzzled by the outburst. She shook her head, her shoulders trembling with mirth.

"Things in this world really don't go as you'd expect," she said, trying to put strength back into her legs. They only trembled like aspen leaves, refusing to support her. "When I promised to meet you today, ten years ago... I never imagined I'd see you like this. To meet you in such a... shameful state."

"I don't think it's shameful," Lee Sungmin replied.

Wiji Hoyeon tried once more to stand on her own, but with no strength in her limbs, she eventually gave up. She let out a short sigh and looked up into his face.

"Really? That's a relief, then. In that case, maybe I'll make a slightly more... embarrassing request. It's hard for me to stand on my own. Carry me on your back."

Lee Sungmin did not answer with words. Instead, he stored his spear in his Subspace Pocket and lifted her onto his now-empty back. She felt far too light, a realization that made his heart ache.

Yoo Hojeong, still collapsed on the ground, did not get up. He only stared with bloodshot eyes as Lee Sungmin settled Wiji Hoyeon on his back, biting his lower lip so hard it nearly bled.

[...Leave Ruvus.]

Yoo Hojeong's Thought Transmission entered Lee Sungmin's mind.

[I've already sent a carrier pigeon to the Murim Alliance, telling them that the Little Heavenly Demon is weakened. It's not that far from here to Kron... The Murim Alliance probably knows this and might already be forming an extermination squad.]

[Why are you telling me this?]

[Because you didn't kill me.] Yoo Hojeong's face was a mask of misery as he replied. [This is all I can offer. When the Murim Alliance's extermination squad arrives... I'll have to act with them. Since you've defeated me, your threat level will only rise. Unless you abandon the Little Heavenly Demon, you're in for some terrible days ahead, and I will be there to contribute to your misery.]

[I'm used to it,] Lee Sungmin replied, though he was grateful for the warning. He turned to leave through the city gate.

[Wait...]

The Void Monarch started to say something, and Lee Sungmin's steps halted. Though the thought remained unfinished, Lee Sungmin continued to consider his options. A moment later, he turned completely around, his back now to the gate. Where he sat, Yoo Hojeong's eyes widened.

"...You're not leaving?"

"It seems safer this way."

Yoo Hojeong's advice to flee was sound, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia were still in the city. Without the Crazy Heavenly Demon, he would never be able to meet the southern tribes that handled Demonic Energy. That was not his only reason for staying in Ruvus, however.

"The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is also in Ruvus, isn't it?"

At his question, Yoo Hojeong's face stiffened.

Just as the orthodox sects had their Murim Alliance, Nine Sects One Gang, and prestigious clans, the unorthodox sects had their own factions. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the Six Demonic Gates, and the Three Demonic Clans all held influence. Ruvus was a city where powers like the Golden and Red Magic Towers intertwined with both the Murim Alliance and the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. This complex web of interests made the city large and developed, with a paradoxically stable public order born from the factions keeping each other in check from the shadows.

As an organization of the unorthodox, the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was naturally hostile to the Murim Alliance. Not everyone in their ranks was a demonic master, and since a certain degree of righteousness was needed to survive in the world, not all of them were evil. In fact, they sometimes rooted out demonic masters themselves. The Little Heavenly Demon was a famous figure. Even if the Alliance did not want a direct clash with the Murim Alliance, they might protect someone of her stature. It was a gamble, but it seemed a better one than taking an unwell Wiji Hoyeon out of the city only to be pursued.

[You're not an idiot,] the Void Monarch grumbled. He had been about to stop Lee Sungmin from leaving and tell him to stay.

*Did you think I was an idiot?*

Lee Sungmin sneered inwardly and walked past Yoo Hojeong. The gathered onlookers hesitated, then backed away as he approached with Wiji Hoyeon on his back. Her arms were draped limply over his shoulders, and she let out a soft chuckle.

"It's new... no. I've realized it for the first time."

"What?"

"Your back. It's broad... and nice. This is the first time I've ever been carried on someone's back. Maybe I was when I was too young to remember... but as far as I can recall, this is the first time."

Wiji Hoyeon leaned forward, resting her chin on his shoulder. She then tilted her head slightly, letting it rest against the nape of his neck.

"...It feels nice. Calming, I guess... Hehe! It's strange, isn't it? When I think about it, you've given me a lot of firsts. Being a friend. Teaching me martial arts. Making a promise. Having it kept. ...And being carried like this."

Her voice was quiet, her breaths shallow and warm. The sensation tickled his ear, feeling as if it vibrated against his eardrum. His heart pounded, and for a moment, he worried she might hear it.

"I'm... glad I met you."

Her limp arms slowly rose and wrapped around his neck.

"I'm sorry I'm late. I never thought I'd break our promise... Hehe! My steps were much slower than I thought. Still... it might sound like an excuse, but please don't blame me too much. The road here was very difficult..."

"What happened? Why are you... so weak?"

"Next time..." Wiji Hoyeon answered, her voice faint. "...Later."

After those words, she fell silent. Startled, Lee Sungmin turned his head to look at her. Her face was right beside his, and he felt a wave of relief as her faint breaths brushed against his skin. She had simply fallen asleep. Lee Sungmin put strength back into his faltering legs and let out a sigh.

"You scared me...!"

He glanced at her peacefully sleeping face for a moment before quickening his pace. The onlookers did not follow. In the distance, he saw Yoo Hojeong finally getting to his feet. Now, where was the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance? It would be faster to ask than to search.

*Nebul.*

"......"

Just by calling the name in his mind, Nebul's voice responded. At times like this, Lee Sungmin had to wonder about the intermediary Guild, Erebrisa. While convenient, it could not perfectly handle ambiguous things like information, yet the abilities of its affiliated Brokers were nothing short of extraordinary.

*The locations of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia, and the geographical information of Ruvus.*

He requested the city's layout rather than just a map, wanting to imprint it firmly in his mind. Before he had gone far, Nebul emerged from his shadow and handed him the crystal ball used for conveying information.

The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was in the Central District, quite far from the Murim Alliance's branch and near the Mercenary Guild. Rubia and the Crazy Heavenly Demon were currently riding the Outer Circular Train, which circled the outskirts of the district.

*Please tell them both to come to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.*

[Understood.]

With the request made, Lee Sungmin started moving again. He could not take the train, not after having ignored the guards and used his movement arts so freely. He could hardly start acting meekly now. Just as he had done to get here, he began to run along the tracks. He heard the shrill sound of whistles and the shouts of guards once more, but he did not stop. He believed it was crucial to reach the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance first and learn their stance.

[This girl... she's carrying a curse,] the Void Monarch muttered as he ran.

Lee Sungmin's body flinched.

*A curse?*

[Yes. A very strange type of curse. It's not magic... it resembles the curses used by the shamans of the South, and it's a very nasty one.] The Void Monarch clicked his tongue. [It's twisting her ki and blood and obstructing the gathering of her inner force. That's not all... it's shaking her body from its very foundation. No matter how skilled a master of martial arts theory is, there's no way they could move properly with a curse like this in their body. Her inner qi can't gather properly, and the twisted ki and blood cause constant pain. On top of that, since it's shaking her body, she can't use her physical strength properly either.]

He recalled the sight of Wiji Hoyeon collapsing, her limp limbs and fading voice. Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip.

*How can I break the curse?*

[Shamanism is different from magic. Magic can be suppressed with superior magic, but shamanism is a different case. Especially with a curse of this type, it's impossible to break unless you kill the caster who placed it.]

For now, there was no way of knowing who the caster was. He would have to wait for Wiji Hoyeon to regain consciousness and tell him herself.

[The only fortunate thing is that the curse itself can't kill this girl. Is its purpose simply to steal her strength and cause pain... What a nasty curse.]

Wiji Hoyeon had been cursed. The same Wiji Hoyeon who was so strong. On his journey, Lee Sungmin had met various powerful individuals he never would have encountered in his past life. Excluding centuries-old monsters like Jeniela or Ju Won, Wiji Hoyeon was the best among the 'humans' he had met, and yet, she had been weakened by a curse?

After running for some time, he stopped his movement arts, leaped over a railing, and started running again. Before long, he arrived at the location stored in his memory.

The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's building was a massive, eastern-style mansion. Two fierce-looking giants stood guard at the entrance. Lee Sungmin approached them, the soft breathing of the sleeping Wiji Hoyeon on his back a steady rhythm.

"The Phantom Spear?" one of the guards spoke up.

Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched.

"Is the one on your back the Little Heavenly Demon?" the guard muttered, studying Lee Sungmin's face before glancing at the sleeping girl. Then, without waiting for a reply, he turned. "Come on in. The branch manager is waiting for you."

The two guards opened the gate themselves. Lee Sungmin had not expected them to recognize him, let alone open the gate first.

[It might be a trap.]

"It's not a trap," the guard said, as if reading the Void Monarch's mind.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before stepping through the gate.

Inside stood a middle-aged woman dressed in elegant attire. She bowed her head deeply to him. "My name is Bo Hye, and I am the general manager of this mansion. Please come this way."

Bo Hye raised her head. It was clear she had learned martial arts, though not to a level that would impress him. Lee Sungmin followed her as she led the way across a garden connected to the mansion.

"The branch manager is in the backyard garden. On nice days like this, he often comes outside," Bo Hye said with a smile in her voice.

Behind the mansion was a rather large artificial pond. An octagonal pavilion sat over the water, connected to the shore by a bridge.

"The branch manager is over there."

Bo Hye stopped walking. Lee Sungmin slowly approached the pond, where he could see a man sitting inside the pavilion. The man smiled warmly as Lee Sungmin stopped in front of the bridge.

"Come this way."

The middle-aged man appeared to be around the same age as Bo Hye. He had not reached the realm of the Supreme Peak, but he was a fully matured master of the peak realm. Lee Sungmin slowly crossed the bridge.

"Did you know I would come here?"

"Just as the orthodox sects have the Beggars' Gang, the unorthodox sects have the Rogues' Guild. I just heard the rumor that the Phantom Spear rescued the Little Heavenly Demon from the Iron Armored Divine Spear and, instead of leaving the city, came deeper inside."

"Is that your answer to my question about knowing I would come here?"

"I merely anticipated that you might," the man said with a low chuckle. "Defeating the Iron Armored Divine Spear and rescuing the Little Heavenly Demon means you intend to stand against the Murim Alliance, and yet, you didn't leave the city... which suggests you might be seeking the help of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. It wasn't a great leap of logic."

He stood and clasped his fist in a martial artist's salute.

"My apologies for the late introduction. My name is Gwan Hu, and I am the branch manager of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance's Ruvus branch."
