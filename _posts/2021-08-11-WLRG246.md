WHOOOOSH!

A fierce wind raged. Descending before Lee Sungmin in the heart of the gale was the Black Dragon Knight, his black robes fluttering. The wind was no simple breeze but a manifestation of the immense power radiating from his body. He had appeared without reducing his energy in the slightest, a clear display of power, and his gaze fixed intently on the stationary Lee Sungmin.

*Who is it?*

It was not the Spear King, and it could not be the Moon Queen. *Could it be the Martial God?* Lee Sungmin unconsciously recalled the man who stood at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven, for the aura of the man blocking his path was just that incredible.

*No, I need to be calm.*

Lee Sungmin steadied his breathing. The man before him was undeniably strong, but compared to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he seemed a little lacking.

[What the hell is that bastard?] Void Monarch's voice echoed in his head. [I can feel the aura of a dragon, but he doesn't seem to be a dragon... He's a half-breed.]

*Black Dragon Knight.*

The words solidified in Lee Sungmin's mind. At Void Monarch's assessment, he knew who his opponent was. It could only be the Murim Alliance Chairman. The moment he realized the man's identity, a wave of confusion washed over him. Why was the Black Dragon Knight here? Shouldn't he be in Kron, with the Murim Alliance?

"The Phantom Spear?" the Black Dragon Knight spoke first.

His sudden appearance was less important than the reason behind it. Why was he here, and why was he blocking Lee Sungmin's path? His stance was certainly not friendly. That much was a given. After all, the Murim Alliance Chairman was the one who had framed him for everything. Though not one of the Six Honored Ones, he was still connected to Heaven Beyond Heaven. Because of this, Lee Sungmin could only glare back, his entire body tense.

However, while Lee Sungmin was confused by the sudden appearance, the Black Dragon Knight was experiencing a confusion of his own. He knew perfectly well that the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin, was here. He had even seen the man's face in reports a few times, so the lack of unfamiliarity was natural, yet, he felt something else entirely.

*What is this?* The Black Dragon Knight's eyes trembled.

Longing.

*Longing? Me?*

Why on earth would he feel such an emotion? He tried to suppress it, but his heart refused to obey.

Lee Sungmin briefly contemplated how to respond. The Black Dragon Knight was hesitating, shaken by something he could not see. In truth, the best choice was to escape with Scarlet without a fight. He had a means of escape. Even if the Black Dragon Knight gave chase, he still had two more opportunities to ride the Fairy Horse.

*It's a waste, but...*

It was better than saving it until it was lost for nothing.

The Black Dragon Knight, who had fallen silent after speaking Lee Sungmin's title, bit his lower lip hard. He could not understand the source of this longing. Was it for his own kind? He had never met another Dragon. His father was said to be a Dragon, but his human mother had no useful memories of him. She had died young, a victim of the aftereffects of giving birth to him.

*I've never met another Dragon... Is that why I'm feeling this?*

He did not want to be swayed by such things. Despite being half-dragon, the Black Dragon Knight did not particularly like that side of himself. He had never even seen his father's face, and the mother who raised him had her life cut short because of his birth. Still, he had often thought that he wanted to meet his father someday. *Could he also be a half-human, half-dragon like me?*

With that thought, the Black Dragon Knight raised his draconic energy even further. He had wanted to meet Lee Sungmin, and now, feeling the aura of a Dragon from him, he felt a strange sense of kinship.

KUUUUUUNG!

An intense pressure washed over Lee Sungmin. It was Dragon's Pressure. To resist the force that suddenly bore down on him, Lee Sungmin also drew out his own.

KUUUUNG!

The two pressures vanished as they clashed. The Black Dragon Knight's eyes widened.

"......"

He knew. Instinctively, without anyone needing to explain, he understood the reason for his longing. The Black Dragon Knight hesitated before opening his mouth.

"Ah... Father...?"

"...What?"

Lee Sungmin's expression was one of pure bewilderment. The Black Dragon Knight, realizing how ridiculous he must have sounded, let out a dry cough, but it was incredibly strange. From Lee Sungmin, he felt the aura of the father he had never met.

"No... that can't be right. My father couldn't possibly be as weak as you... what in the world are you...?"

[Could it be,] Void Monarch spoke up quietly at the question. [Could the Dragon I killed be that bastard's father?]

*What?*

[Otherwise, there's no way that guy would call you father, and now that I look at it, the Dragon's Pressure you're emitting and that guy's are remarkably similar.]

If that were true, things were about to get seriously complicated. Lee Sungmin furrowed his brows.

While he did, the Black Dragon Knight was forming a hypothesis of his own. The Phantom Spear could not possibly be his father. Then why did he exude the same aura?

*Brothers?*

No, that was impossible. The birth of a half-dragon was a miracle in itself. It was unimaginable that the same Dragon would have sired two of them. If that was the case... a dark killing intent filled the Black Dragon Knight's eyes.

*Dragon Heart.*

Though it was an impossible feat for a human to take a Dragon Heart, he could think of no other possibility. He ground his teeth and took a large step forward. A fierce, incomprehensible anger, a mixture of emotions, made his blood boil. For over three hundred years, he had secretly hoped he might meet his father again someday. To think he would encounter a trace of him in this way.

"You ate a Dragon Heart." The Black Dragon Knight's face hardened into a cold mask. The black energy undulating around him became enhanced qi, thick with a distinct killing intent. "My father's Dragon Heart."

[You're fucked,] Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin felt a surge of indignation. He had eaten the heart, yes, but wasn't it Void Monarch who killed the man's father?

*Out of all the Dragons, why'd you have to kill that bastard's father?*

[You disrespectful little shit! This Elder gave it to you for your own good, and instead of being grateful, you're trying to shift the blame? Did you think I knew that son of a bitch would sire a bastard like this? Huh? Don't give me shit and just get ready!]

Just as Void Monarch warned, the Black Dragon Knight exploded forward.

SHWIIK!

He kicked off the ground and lunged, his approach not as fast as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's Gale Thunder, but still so swift that his presence vanished for a moment. Already on guard, Lee Sungmin rotated his spear and thrust it to meet the charge.

KWWAAANG!

The spearhead slammed against the back of the Black Dragon Knight's hand. Lee Sungmin's eyes twitched at the unyielding firmness he felt. The back of the man's hand was covered in jet-black scales.

[Those are Dragon scales. Even for you, it won't be easy to pierce,] Void Monarch advised.

*How did you pierce it then?* Lee Sungmin retorted.

[I smashed it with my bare fists,] Void Monarch boasted.

That was impossible advice. Lee Sungmin fell back, drawing upon the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

"..."

Deadly Flash Lightning bolts erupted from his spear in succession, but the Black Dragon Knight simply swatted them aside with his scale-covered hands. His defense was protected not only by his Protective Qi Aura but also by the Dragon scales, making it impossible to penetrate.

*Could I break through if I use my full power?*

He could not even be sure of that. Although Lee Sungmin still had some leeway, the Black Dragon Knight seemed to have even more to spare. The man's martial prowess known to the world was a gross underestimation of his true skill. He might not be on the level of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, but it was clear he was a master who would be difficult for the current Lee Sungmin to face.

The Black Dragon Knight set aside his momentary excitement and anger. He did not think of Lee Sungmin as his father's enemy. The boy could not have killed that Dragon. It was just a nasty coincidence that the Dragon Heart he took happened to be his father's. There was no need to consider him an enemy. It was just... uncomfortable. Annoying. It was unpleasant to learn of the death of the father he had never seen, the one he might have met again someday, in this way.

*His attacks are sharper than I thought.*

They were not as sharp as the Spear King's, but considering his future potential, he was dangerous. It would be for the best if he could be brought into Heaven Beyond Heaven... but why on earth was I told to leave him alone? A talent this dangerous, with potential impossible to predict, and the successor to that monster, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

*I can definitely kill him now.* It would not be a difficult task. Even after flying this long distance and firing his breath twice, he had more than enough leeway to kill Lee Sungmin here. The Black Dragon Knight's eyes held a chilling light. *I'll kill him here.*

The moment he made up his mind, something tore through space, aimed directly for his chest. The sudden, fierce attack caught the Black Dragon Knight off guard. He hastily retreated, solidifying his defense.

KWWAAANG!

A heavy impact sent his body flying far back.

"Keuk!"

Even with his Protective Qi Aura and scales raised, he could not completely nullify the force. The Black Dragon Knight glared, his eyes wide, at the weapon that had stopped upon reaching his chest. It was a jet-black short spear.

"The Spear King!" he roared, his shout filled with a pressure that shook the forest.

Lee Sungmin was just as surprised by the sudden attack. He was truly shocked because he had not been able to detect the thrown spear until the very moment it collided with the Black Dragon Knight.

"Why are you interfering with me?!" the Black Dragon Knight continued to shout. The Dragon's Pressure, born from a terribly arrogant race that considered itself greater than any other existence, erupted without stopping. Lee Sungmin frowned and stepped back a little. The Black Dragon Knight was genuinely enraged. The Spear King's attack had been sharp enough to be a fatal blow had his defense been even slightly late.

The short spear touching the Black Dragon Knight's chest trembled and then shot back to where it had been thrown from. The Spear King was standing not too far away. He was glaring at the Black Dragon Knight with wide eyes, not even looking at Lee Sungmin.

"He is my prey," the Spear King spat out.

He was a completely different person from the man Lee Sungmin had encountered before entering the forest. This was not a martial artist mad for a fight, but a monster exuding a fierce killing intent that seemed capable of tearing the entire world apart.

"Are you trying to steal my prey!" the Spear King roared.

At his shout, the Black Dragon Knight's face distorted.

"Prey? What nonsense...!"

"It was none other than the Martial God who told me to face that man. The Martial God told me! Me! To fight him."

"Then fight the Phantom Spear right now and kill him!" the Black Dragon Knight shouted back.

Only then did the Spear King glance at Lee Sungmin. "No. I won't fight now."

"...What?"

"Next time," the Spear King spat out forcefully. "When I am at my best, and when he is at his best. We have already promised to fight then."

"You crazy bastard!" the Black Dragon Knight cursed. He swung a hand wrapped in black enhanced qi toward Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin, without panicking, swung his spear to parry. Just as he was about to counterattack, the Spear King let out a fierce shout.

"You bastard!"

He threw his short spear again.

KWAKWAKWA!

The weapon tore through space as it flew, seeming impossible to dodge or block even if one saw it coming. The Black Dragon Knight, however, did not retreat and instead extended both hands forward.

KWWAAANG!

The sound of an impact that shook the entire space echoed in all directions.

"Don't steal my prey!"

"Stop spouting bullshit!" the Black Dragon Knight spat out in frustration at the Spear King's cry.

[Now's our chance,] Void Monarch whispered while the two monsters bellowed at each other. [Let's bounce.]

As it happened, Lee Sungmin was thinking the exact same thing.
