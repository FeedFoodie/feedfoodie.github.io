Lee Sungmin was lost in deep thought. He knew Heaven Beyond Heaven needed Wiji Hoyeon to interfere with the laws, a fact the Fist Honored One had shared and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was also aware of, but it was an incredibly vague statement. Their goal was to interfere with the laws and annihilate the inhuman, and Wiji Hoyeon was the key to that interference.

The Fist Honored One had never mentioned The End. The Black Dragon Knight, however, had implied it without saying the words, connecting the Martial God's goal to stopping that final calamity. This new context changed everything, causing Lee Sungmin and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman to interpret the phrase 'interfering with the laws' in a completely different light.

*The annihilation of the inhuman is connected to stopping The End.*

That was the conclusion, assuming the Martial God's true purpose was indeed to stop The End.

Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. The implication was chillingly understandable. He himself was human, yet not human. To be precise, he was a being far closer to an inhuman than anything else. If stopping The End required the annihilation of the inhuman, then he was inevitably destined to die.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman seemed to understand his grim realization. He considered asking if there was another way, but knew it was pointless to press the Black Dragon Knight on the matter. Instead, he changed the subject.

"So, what is this Spirit Medium?"

"That... I don't know either," the Black Dragon Knight admitted. "She's a very mysterious being. I've never met her in person, only heard her directives. I don't even know where she is."

"Then there's nothing more to hear from you."

With the Chairman's dismissal, the Black Dragon Knight fell silent. A wave of pathetic self-pity washed over him, though he was careful not to let it show on his face. The martial prowess he had just experienced from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was nothing short of mythical. He remembered the Martial God as a powerful figure, but that strength was from a hundred years ago, before his seclusion. The Martial God of the past could not compare to the Chairman of today.

*It's a shame, but...*

As long as I'm alive, there will be plenty of ways to move to another dimension.

The Black Dragon Knight comforted himself with this thought, but as he wrestled with his lingering attachments, his gaze kept drifting toward Lee Sungmin. His shoulders trembled as he stared at the magic armor the man wore, a grotesque shell crafted from the scales of a father he had never met, a father he once thought he might one day see again. Complex emotions boiled inside him, laced with a potent hatred.

Even under that fierce glare, Lee Sungmin could not stop thinking about what the Black Dragon Knight had said. The reason for Heaven Beyond Heaven's obsession with Wiji Hoyeon. Why had she gone to the Hyujael Mountains? It was probably, no, undoubtedly, because her destination had been the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, the very place where Yana had gained her power as the Nine-Tailed Fox.

*Why did she go there?*

He remembered their last conversation before they parted ways. She had spoken of a dream, of a place full of fog. She had vaguely stated that she did not know where it was, but she would know where to go. Yes. Wiji Hoyeon had headed for the Demonic Spirit Pavilion because someone had intended for her to go there.

However, who?

"Where are the Hyujael Mountains?"

"...The South," Lee Sungmin answered. "I heard it's a week's journey even from Urmuri."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman stroked his chin, briefly calculating the distance before nodding.

"That's a long way from here. Even if we went down to the South now, it's unlikely the Little Heavenly Demon would still be there."

"It was already several months ago that the Martial God met her in the Hyujael Mountains," the Black Dragon Knight added.

"Then it's even more unlikely she's still there," the Chairman concluded. "The Demonic Spirit Pavilion... what do you two know about it?"

His question was directed at both of them. The Black Dragon Knight could not answer, for he knew nothing of the Hyujael Mountains, but Lee Sungmin was different.

"The Demonic Spirit Pavilion is in the Hyujael Mountains."

"The Demonic Spirit Pavilion?"

"It's a place where one can perform a Spirit Possession with a Demonic Spirit... or so I've heard."

Lee Sungmin recounted the story he had heard from Yana. After a moment of thought, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded.

"This one has also heard of the Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri. They say she is a monster with great power. Are you saying the Demonic Spirit of that pavilion has the authority to artificially grant that level of power?"

"I'm not sure about that myself."

"So, as you said, it seems the Little Heavenly Demon went to the Hyujael Mountains to meet this Demonic Spirit. I can't guess the reason... but perhaps the Martial God died there."

"What?" the Black Dragon Knight exclaimed in surprise. "The Martial God... might be dead?"

"If there's no contact, isn't it because there's a reason he can't make contact? I believe there's a good chance he's dead."

"That's... The Martial God's martial prowess reached the heavens. For him to have been killed by a Demonic Spirit, it makes no sense."

"This one has never met a Spirit or a Demonic Spirit," the Chairman said. "However, I know that they, unlike the gods that exist in this world, are beings that resemble gods in the truest sense. No matter how high one's martial might is, a human is ultimately just a human."

It was a truth the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman often repeated, even to himself. He had said it several times before, that no matter how strong he was, he could do nothing against beings like Osla or the gods. It was not a matter of their strength, but that their very existence was on a different level from humans.

"We should head to the North first."

"Are you going to meet the Vampire Queen?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"The plan doesn't change. The South is suspicious, but it takes too long to get there. Besides, even if we go, I doubt we'd be able to enter the Demonic Spirit Pavilion and meet the Demonic Spirit."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman glanced at the Black Dragon Knight.

"This one does not break his own word. I said I would protect you. It's questionable whether the Martial God is alive, but if he tries to kill you, I will protect you."

"...Underst..."

"However," the Chairman cut in before the Black Dragon Knight could finish his reply. "I never said that this one wouldn't kill you."

"You crazy bastard! You're going to kill me after I told you everything you wanted to hear?"

"Don't be so hasty." As the Black Dragon Knight flared up and tried to rise, the Chairman raised a hand, stopping his movement. He had not even drawn on his internal energy, but the sight of that hand moving was enough to frighten the Black Dragon Knight into sitting back down quietly.

"I'm merely saying it's a possibility."

"...Damn it. Just explain it so I can understand. What more do you want from me?"

"It's not a demand. If you don't do anything worth killing over, this one will not kill you."

"I don't know what you want me to do."

"First, do not harbor any killing intent toward this one's disciple."

At those words, the Black Dragon Knight's shoulders trembled. He deeply suppressed the emotions stirring in his chest, but he could not help feeling it was unfair. In the end, he could not hold back completely and protested.

"You may not know this, but your disciple... the Phantom Spear and I have a bad history. That bastard ate the heart of my father, a Dragon, and made armor from my father's scales and hide."

"You think he only made armor? He made a spear, too," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman replied with a sneer.

"Grrr..." The ends of the Black Dragon Knight's hair stood on end.

Lee Sungmin glared at his master for the unnecessary comment and protested in an aggrieved voice. "First, let me clear something up. I didn't kill your father."

"You don't have the ability to, anyway," the Chairman added teasingly.

Lee Sungmin ignored him. "I obtained the Dragon Heart and scales by chance, and I'm just using them after processing them to suit my needs."

"Even so, isn't it an unavoidable fact that I, his son, would feel displeased?"

"That's right. It's unavoidable, but I couldn't help it either. You people from Heaven Beyond Heaven are targeting me, and since I lacked the ability to kill all of you, I just used the Dragon materials I had."

"You were the one who made an enemy of Heaven Beyond Heaven first."

"Why are you talking like you're part of Heaven Beyond Heaven when you betrayed the Martial God?"

The Black Dragon Knight's face twisted at Lee Sungmin's retort. It was not as if he had wanted to betray him, and being treated like this made him burn with anger.

"It's quite a long way from here to the North," Scarlet, who had been listening to the back-and-forth, finally spoke up. She took out a large magic tome from her Subspace Pocket and placed it on her lap. "Are you going to keep arguing the whole way there?"

"Right," the Chairman chimed in. "How about getting along? You've become companions, after all."

"...Ugh..." The Black Dragon Knight squeezed his eyes shut and calmed his agitated emotions. Right. Come to think of it, there was no reason to be so angry now. To him, the being called 'father' had never once shown a fatherly side and was someone he had never even met. There was no reason to be angry about his death now.

"Let's get along from now on," Lee Sungmin offered casually.

The Black Dragon Knight calmed himself with that thought, but he could not help but feel a fresh surge of anger at Lee Sungmin's words. Of course, his anger remained unexpressed, as he was wary of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

Sensing the clashing atmosphere, Scarlet smacked her lips without showing much reaction. She grumbled while looking down at her magic tome.

"I shouldn't have come."

Although she said that, she still thought it was better than staying with the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance.

RUMBLE!

The mountain shuddered as if in a landslide. More precisely, an entire peak was collapsing, crumbling into a massive pile of rock. Then, with a brilliant flash of light, the rocky mound exploded, its heavy boulders instantly disintegrating into fine sand that scattered in every direction.

The Martial God who emerged from the remains of the mountain was a wretched sight. His hair was a disheveled mess, and his clothes were little more than tattered rags clinging to his frame. His cheeks were sunken, dark shadows hung under his eyes, and his wide-open eyes were bloodshot and red.

He gasped for breath as he strode out from what was now just a pile of sand.

"Kughhh..."

The Martial God pressed down on his throbbing temples. How much time had passed? He could not tell. The barrier that had trapped him was neither a formation nor magic. It was a barrier in the truest sense, created by the authority of a transcendent being. The flow of time within it was different, and the events that occurred far transcended common sense. His legs suddenly gave out, and he collapsed on the spot.

As the Demonic Spirit had acknowledged, he was an inhuman monster who had reached the highest state among humans. Even so, he could not help but expend a great deal of mental energy to escape the barrier. The prison, which jumbled all his senses and made him wander endlessly, was not a great threat to him, but forcibly breaking out of a barrier created by authority was by no means an easy task.

*To think it would turn out like this...!*

The Martial God ground his teeth and suppressed his headache. He had underestimated the Demonic Spirit's power, or more precisely, its authority. While his martial might may have been enough to annihilate the being known as the Demonic Spirit, he lacked the means to resist its power.

"I was arrogant."

The Martial God reproached himself and immediately began his qi circulation. His dried-up qi center filled to the brim just by circulating his inner arts once. He took a deep breath and got to his feet.

*However, there were results.*

He had taken a gamble by meeting the Demonic Spirit empty-handed. This was something he could not learn even through the Spirit Medium, so he had to throw himself into the dangerous situation. The gamble had paid off, however, and the Martial God had learned a great deal.

*The Demonic Spirit cannot kill me.*

He had been insulted, called an ant, and trapped in a barrier of authority. However, he had not died. That barrier was meant to trap him, not to kill him. Even as an ant, he had a role to play.

*The Demonic Spirit was waiting for the Little Heavenly Demon.*

Fate had moved. The End had already begun. The Demonic Spirit and the Little Heavenly Demon had met. Their purpose was unknown, but since The End had begun, he had to keep a close watch on the Little Heavenly Demon's movements. Stopping The End was undoubtedly his purpose.

*As long as one is human, it is difficult to resist the Demonic Spirit's authority.*

That was the greatest achievement. Martial arts aside, he could not resist the Demonic Spirit's authority. He had expended an enormous amount of mental energy breaking through that barrier. So, how much time had passed? The Martial God looked up at the stars filling the night sky.

"Several months have passed."

He thought he had been trapped for only a few days at most. To think this much time had passed. Dismayed, the Martial God called for a 'Broker'. The moment the Erebrisa agent appeared, he demanded to know all the major recent rumors. Before long, the Broker had compiled the information and fed it directly into the Martial God's mind.

He had once been wary of this mysterious intermediary Guild and its Brokers, but he had cast aside such suspicion long ago. It was a grand magic system left behind by the Dragons, who had fled this world in fear of The End but had nevertheless created Erebrisa for the convenience of the 'variables' who would stand against it.

"...Hmm."

The one fortunate thing was that while he wandered the Demonic Spirit's barrier for several months, no major incidents related to The End had occurred. Besides the Little Heavenly Demon, the one he was wary of was the Vampire Queen of North Trabia. She was still in the North.

*However... how long will she remain there?*

The Little Heavenly Demon's whereabouts were unknown. Where in the world could she be? The Martial God felt impatient, but he recognized that as long as the Demonic Spirit was involved, he could not track her through Erebrisa. Since it had come to this, he had no choice but to trust the Spirit Medium. The being she performed Spirit Possession with was a Spirit, a being with properties that were the polar opposite of a Demonic Spirit.

*...The Demon Emperor has made a move?*

Hearing the story about the Murim Alliance, the Martial God's face contorted. Kron had been devastated, and the Murim Alliance was shattered. The Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, had been subdued and abducted by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The Martial God's shoulders trembled.

Making the Black Dragon Knight the Chairman had been a superficial matter. Heaven Beyond Heaven had seized control of the Murim Alliance's core long ago, securing a reliable, massive force to counter The End when it finally arrived.

"Yang Ilcheon... that... son of a bitch..."

However, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's actions had twisted everything. Because he had gone on a rampage in Kron, the prestige of the Murim Alliance had plummeted. The Chairman was defeated and even abducted. The Martial God's shoulders trembled with rage. With the great disaster called The End approaching, that man had not helped, but had instead made a complete mess of things.

The Martial God swallowed his anger. Without a doubt, the Black Dragon Knight would have told the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman everything about Heaven Beyond Heaven.

*I don't know what that bastard will do next... but if he becomes a hindrance instead of a help, I'll have no choice but to kill him.*

However, before that, he had other priorities. The Martial God looked toward the distant North.

The End had already begun.

He had to do something about the Vampire Queen, who posed a threat.
