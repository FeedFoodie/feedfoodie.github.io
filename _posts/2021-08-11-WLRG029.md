Few Otherworlders in Eria ever achieved continent-wide renown. Regional fame was one thing, but to have one's name known everywhere required not only mastery of a great skill but also actions significant enough to spawn legends.

'Silent Flash' Baek Sogo was a master who met both criteria. Her name and title marked her as someone from murim, and though the exact location of her origins remained a mystery, she had appeared like a comet, suddenly making a name for herself just as other Otherworlders had.

Lee Sungmin had first heard her name when he was just a fledgling mercenary. This meant that, at the latest, Baek Sogo would leave Mount Mushii and venture into the world within two years.

*But she dies.*

He knew this with chilling certainty. Baek Sogo's death was foretold, a fate that stalked her like a nemesis. In his past life, Silent Flash Baek Sogo had died at the hands of none other than the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon.

Eight years from now.

Lee Sungmin watched Baek Sogo walk ahead of him, his own body burdened by the restriction of 'heaviness'. Each step was a monumental effort.

Baek Sogo's specialty, then as now, was speed. The title 'Silent Flash' was no exaggeration. She moved as fast as a flicker of light. During the nearly three weeks he had known her, Lee Sungmin had witnessed this incredible velocity firsthand. While he could not even properly join a Hunting Party, Baek Sogo effortlessly caught the nimble beasts that roamed the mountain. Her movements were a blur that his eyes could not properly follow.

[You're too slow.]

Baek Sogo stopped walking, pulling out a twig to scratch words into the dirt.

[Should I carry you?]

"...Yes?"

She wrote again, her strokes sharp and impatient.

[You saw what I wrote, so why ask? You're too slow, so I'm saying I'll just carry you.]

Lee Sungmin hesitated. The thought of being carried on the back of a woman like her felt awkward, but this was no time for petty pride. He eventually nodded, and she hoisted him onto her back. He wrapped his hands tightly around her shoulders.

*A good samaritan.*

*A good person.*

It was a strange thing to call someone, but from his perspective, the term fit her perfectly. For three weeks, she had hunted in his stead. She had insisted that eating only meat was unhealthy, bringing him edible greens and showing him where to find water.

And she expected nothing in return. In that way, Baek Sogo was similar to Wiji Hoyeon, yet also different. The kindness Wiji Hoyeon had shown him was born from the bond they shared as 'friends'.

However, what about Baek Sogo? Were they friends?

*I don't know.*

He did not think friendship was something that simply began with a declaration. In truth, Lee Sungmin had no idea where the boundary of friendship lay. He had never had a friend in his past life, so how could he possibly know?

Friend or not, he was grateful for her sympathy. The month he had spent on this mountain had been so terrible it might have been the worst of his entire thirteen-year past life. Without Baek Sogo's compassion, he might not have endured.

*But... she dies.*

And her killer was Wiji Hoyeon, the first friend he had ever made in this life.

"......"

*Just why?*

He wrestled with the question. Why did Baek Sogo have to die? Wiji Hoyeon had killed her, and it had not been a misunderstanding. The incident that had truly cemented the Little Heavenly Demon's name across Eria was the slaughter of Baek Sogo.

*No. It wasn't just Baek Sogo.*

He remembered the full scope of the 'incident' now. Though it had nothing to do with him at the time, the event itself had shaken all of Eria, so much so that even a low-level mercenary like him had trembled at the news.

Dungeons. They existed in various places across the continent, each a place where strange magics intertwined. The monsters inside would be 'recreated' a certain time after being killed, and unlike normal monsters, their corpses yielded no loot. Instead, they dropped different kinds of treasures. Miracle herbs, martial arts manuals, magic books, potions, or powerful enchanted artifacts and exceptional weapons and armor could all be obtained from them.

An even greater reward awaited at the end of each dungeon. Once claimed, the magic sustaining the dungeon would dissipate, and the place itself would vanish.

Eight years from now, a dungeon would be discovered. Not only Silent Flash Baek Sogo, but many other giants who had made names for themselves would enter it, seeking its final reward.

Only one person emerged from that dungeon alive. Wiji Hoyeon. No one ever learned what she obtained inside, for she never spoke of it, but while she remained silent about the reward, she was not quiet about what had happened.

She had killed everyone who entered the dungeon with her.

Baek Sogo's steps came to a halt. The 'heaviness' Lee Sungmin felt was his alone and did not transfer to her. Even after running a considerable distance along the mountain path, her breathing remained perfectly even. She patted his bottom, and he slid off her back. As his feet touched the ground, his knees buckled slightly under his own weight.

"Who's that kid?" a sharp voice demanded.

"......"

A woman appeared before them.

"Ugh...!"

A powerful stench assaulted Lee Sungmin's nose the moment she materialized. He scrunched up his face and pinched the bridge of his nose. Even Baek Sogo took a few steps back.

"You're both incredibly rude!" the woman spat.

Her appearance was a sight to behold. Her long hair was not only greasy and tangled but also caked with so much dirt, dust, and filth that its original color was impossible to guess. Her face was streaked with grime, and the robe she wore was so tattered it looked more like a collection of rags.

*How can she be so dirty...?*

"Baek Sogo. You damn mute bitch. I asked who that kid is."

A foul odor, like an open sewer, wafted from her mouth with every word. Lee Sungmin thought she might be quite beautiful if she were cleaned up and properly dressed, but her wretched state and the stench clinging to her erased any trace of charm.

[So...] Baek Sogo took out her twig. [First, I should make introductions. That dirty young lady's name is Scarlet. She's a mage. This boy's name is Lee Sungmin.]

"Lee Sungmin? Is he a martial artist?"

[Probably.] Baek Sogo replied with her writing.

A mage. He had assumed everyone here would be a martial artist, a practitioner of some kind. He never imagined a mage would be on this mountain.

"...I'm Lee Sungmin."

"Scarlet," the woman said. "And just so you know, the restriction I got is 'no washing'. I can't even brush my teeth, let alone bathe. That's why I look like this." She spat on the ground and rubbed her eyes vigorously, picking away the sleep crusted there. "Recently, I also got the restriction of 'no changing clothes'. So, you get it? This smell is unavoidable."

"Ah... I understand," Lee Sungmin nodded at her sharp tone. He wanted to say more, but the stench was so overwhelming he was afraid to ask anything else.

"So. You mute brat. Why'd you come here?"

[This boy was curious about the other practitioners.]

"Ha! What a weird thing to be curious about," Scarlet scoffed, whipping her head around to glare at Lee Sungmin. A flurry of white flakes drifted down from her hair as she moved. "Why? Were you curious about what kind of restrictions other people have?"

"No... that's not it. It's just, since we're in the same situation, I wanted to know..."

"Same situation? Listen to this little brat," Scarlet sneered. "Hey! I've been living here for over half a year, you know? Who do you think you are, trying to act like we're equals?" She raised a fist and bonked him on the head.

KONG!

It did not hurt much, but the feeling of her dirty knuckles on his scalp was deeply unpleasant.

[Don't be so harsh,] Baek Sogo wrote. [It's only been a month since this boy came to the mountain, but he has received two restrictions.]

Scarlet's expression shifted to one of genuine surprise. "...Two restrictions? Isn't this kid a complete psycho? Why'd you do something so crazy?"

"Uh... I thought it would be helpful."

"Helpful? You crazy bastard!" Scarlet burst out laughing. "Kid. There are four practitioners who have lasted more than half a year on this mountain, including me and that mute bitch over there. Among them, the only crazy one who took on two restrictions from the start was her."

"...Huh?" Lee Sungmin turned to look at Baek Sogo, shocked.

With an unconcerned expression, Baek Sogo wrote on the ground.

[My voice and Thought Transmission. Those are the two restrictions I received.]

No wonder. He had found it strange that a master who should have surpassed the peak realm was not using Thought Transmission.

"But now another psycho like that mute brat has shown up," Scarlet said, spinning around as if she had nothing more to say. "I don't know how long you'll last, though."

As they moved on, Lee Sungmin asked, "What kind of training is Scarlet doing?"

[Why did you come to this mountain?] Baek Sogo wrote, answering his question with one of her own.

"...To achieve mastery in martial arts."

[That's right. Those who come to this mountain are enduring trials and hardships to gain something at the end. I also wish for mastery in martial arts, and Scarlet wishes for mastery in magic.]

Baek Sogo lifted him onto her back again. As she carried him, Lee Sungmin tried to place the name Scarlet.

*Ah.*

He remembered.

Scarlet Reshir. The archmage who had been alive until his death in his past life, the founder of the renowned 'Reshir School' of magic.

*What is this?*

First Baek Sogo, and now Scarlet. Everyone who had endured on this mountain for more than half a year was a giant whose name was known throughout Eria.

"Baek Sogo. It's you."

The next person they met was an old man standing at the edge of a dizzying cliff, his back turned to them.

"It seems there's someone else with you. Who is that child? Surely he's not unable to speak like you, is he?"

The old man turned around, and Lee Sungmin's mouth fell open. The man's face was a horrifying tapestry of scars. It was not just his face. All of his visible skin was a web of mangled tissue.

[That old man's restriction is 'sight'.]

A blind old man. With his hands clasped behind his back, he walked toward them with steady, confident steps, his sightless eyes fixed in their direction.

[So you'll have to relay my words,] Baek Sogo wrote. [Ah. That old man's name is Dok Bijun.]

Dok Bijun.

*Dok Bijun? The Sword Phantom!*

Lee Sungmin swallowed a scream.

It was the appearance of another giant.
