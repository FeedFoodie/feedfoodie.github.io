The forest stirred with the distant cries of beasts.

Lee Sungmin knew this Forest of Ancients well enough to understand the sound. The massive beasts that roamed its depths were more threatening than most monsters, which was precisely why creatures like the Ogre, an apex predator on land, or orcs who hunted with martial arts, could not survive here. This domain belonged to the beasts, and the Elves, under the forest's protection, commanded them. In other words, the countless creatures living in the Forest of Ancients were the Elves' army.

Heavy footsteps drew his attention, and Lee Sungmin turned his head to see dozens of wild boars charging forward, smashing through the trees he had already felled. Their house-sized bodies and sharp, menacing tusks meant nothing to him. His reaction was one of pure indifference.

Without even lifting his spear, he raised a hand. The enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art coiled around his palm. Ever since the death of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Lee Sungmin had found himself using the art more frequently, honoring the man's last wish that he not forget it.

"......"

He unleashed Blood Ring Shattering, a one-strike technique, as a palm strike. A thick, long stream of enhanced qi erupted from his hand and crushed the bodies of the charging boars.

Once more.

He fired the technique again with his other hand. The remaining boars tumbled through the air, their bodies bursting like water balloons and scattering blood and entrails everywhere.

It was not just the boars. The wind seemed to stir, and then massive birds of prey descended from the sky.

"Looks like they're starting to come," Lee Sungmin muttered.

He thought of the Hunting Party. This sudden, coordinated attack could only mean they had set out on a hunt. He raised his right hand above his head.

"......"

Dozens of streams of Bloody Pursuit shot out, tearing the flapping wings of the birds to shreds. Even if they were beasts stronger than monsters, they were no different from ordinary animals before him. It was only natural. A skilled peak master would consider an Ogre little more than a troublesome piece of meat, so a beast that could threaten such a creature was nothing special to Lee Sungmin, who stood at the pinnacle of the Supreme Peak.

Thanks to the otherworlders who had migrated from various dimensions, humanity had learned to hunt monsters with ease. Of course, not all humans were so capable, but Lee Sungmin was a powerful individual who stood at a very high level among them.

The beasts' attacks continued, but not a single one of the charging creatures succeeded in even getting close to him. Corpses began to pile up, and the smell of blood and ruptured filth spread far and wide.

Lee Sungmin felt gazes upon him, though they were not the gazes of Elves. He opened his senses wide, identifying the sources as detection magic and Elementals. It seemed the Hunting Party was still maintaining their distance, observing his movements through their proxies.

"You'll only increase the number of corpses," he muttered, intending to be heard.

The Hunting Team Leader heard his voice. She stroked the head of the growling tiger she rode, knowing the man was right. The beasts of the forest had easily hunted slave Hunters and the mercenaries they brought with them, but they could not even get close to this human.

"He's an incredible master."

"Not as much as the elder," the Hunting Team Leader replied.

Fist Honored One served as the elder of this forest. Though he had left after joining Heaven Beyond Heaven, he had lived here until a hundred years ago, teaching and guiding the Hunting Party in martial arts. All twenty members, including the leader herself, were his disciples. She was his direct disciple. They had practiced for decades, some for over a century, yet none of them had entered the Transcendent Realm. That state was not something one could reach simply by training for a long time. Still, the one among them who was closest was the Hunting Team Leader. She was the daughter of Fist Honored One. The first thing she had ever read was a martial arts manual, and she had learned to fight as she learned to walk.

"The damage will only increase," one of the party members said with a worried expression. It was the same one who had killed Rajun.

The will of the Elves, to kill the intruder, had spread throughout the forest, making the beasts forget fear and instilling in them a deep madness. As time passed, more and more of them would attack the human, and with greater ferocity. As the number of beasts increased, so too would the number of corpses.

"......"

The Hunting Team Leader nodded. The deaths of the beasts were not particularly important to her, but trying to exhaust the human by throwing them at him seemed pointless. Gauging his level through their deaths also seemed to be a futile exercise.

TUK.

Her foot lightly kicked the tiger's belly. The great cat kicked off the ground, and the Hunting Team Leader lowered her body onto its back, becoming one with the beast as they ran. Wolves let out long howls and followed the tiger as it raced ahead.

Lee Sungmin heard the howls. He was reaping the benefit of having knocked down all the surrounding trees, for he saw the tiger and wolves charging from a distance with the Elves riding atop them.

*It must be the Hunting Party.*

Unlike the sentries he had killed earlier, these Elves were equipped with clothing focused on agility and a variety of armaments. Before he could make a move, the wolves scattered. One, two, three. He counted twenty members in total.

[The one on the tiger must be the leader. She's quite skilled,] Void Monarch observed.

Lee Sungmin felt it too. The other members were also masters who had reached a high level, all of them at the Supreme Peak. He could not help but be impressed.

[Elves live long and receive the protection of Mana. They have all the conditions to become stronger more easily than humans. Still... the Elves of this forest are quite unique.]

*How so?*

[Elves don't learn martial arts. It's because they're arrogant. The origin of magic may be the Dragons, but martial arts were created by humans. Elves are contradictory morons. They refuse to learn martial arts created by humans, but they have no problem using tools made by humans.]

Void Monarch let out a sneer.

*Were they influenced by Fist Honored One?* Lee Sungmin wondered as he pulled the spear he had stuck in the ground. His Magic Armor deployed, enveloping his body. The Hunting Party looked as strong as the dead Elf had sneered, so he planned to get serious as well.

The scattered wolves and the tiger did not charge him right away. Twenty beasts and twenty Elves surrounded him. They took the crossbows from their backs and aimed them at him.

"Human," the Hunting Team Leader called out, glaring at him.

Lee Sungmin nodded in acknowledgment.

"Why've you come to this forest?"

"Don't you already know?" he replied. He knew the Elementals had been watching when he killed the sentries.

Hearing his answer, the Hunting Team Leader's expression turned cold. "...What do you plan to do when you meet the elder?"

"I'm going to kill him."

The Hunting Team Leader decided there was no need for further questions. Since Elves had already died at this human's hands, questions were a luxury from the start.

PABABAK!

The crossbow bolts were fired. Imbued with Elementals and inner qi, they streaked through the air like thin bolts of lightning.

KWAAANG!

The volley slammed down, tearing up the earth and raising a large cloud of dust, but Lee Sungmin was no longer there.

The Hunting Team Leader immediately located his vanished presence. The innate sixth sense that Elves are born with could be developed to a point close to premonition, depending on their training. She drew the two daggers from her waist and swung them instantly.

"......"

The spear Lee Sungmin thrust out collided with her daggers.

*She blocked it.*

[Her reaction is fast, too,] Void Monarch chuckled.

Lee Sungmin gripped the locked spear tightly and kicked at the empty air, using the Airwalking Technique to keep from falling as he unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The Hunting Team Leader moved her daggers in a busy blur, blocking the dozens of Homing Chain Lightning strikes.

It was not just her he had to worry about. The tiger raised a massive forepaw and swung it at him, its attack faster and fiercer than that of a decent master. Lee Sungmin twisted his body in mid-air to dodge the paw.

Dozens of bolts were fired at his back. Guided by Elementals, they changed their trajectories freely, trying to confuse his senses. His Magic Armor hummed as Demonic Energy and inner qi erupted into a Protective Qi Aura, incinerating any bolts that came close.

The Hunting Team Leader and the tiger retreated as the other party members rushed in. The wolves bared their teeth to bite, and the Elves swapped their crossbows for different weapons. They were kusarigama. The weights, which had been spinning around, shot toward Lee Sungmin. A direct hit from a weight imbued with enhanced qi would end with more than just broken bones.

Lee Sungmin held his spear with one hand, spun his body, and swung it wide.

CHWARRRREUK!

Five chains wrapped around his spear. The Elves who had thrown the weights ran with their wolves in different directions, pulling the chains taut and making the captured spear tremble. Two more party members leaped toward him. Though he held the spear with only one hand, he was not dragged in the direction the chains were pulling. He raised his right foot and kicked out at the jaw of an attacking wolf.

KWAJIK!

The wolf's head shattered from the impact. The Elf riding it, however, swung his curved sickle in an arc aimed at Lee Sungmin's head.

His free hand shot up. The back of his right hand moved inside the sickle's trajectory, struck the Elf's wrist, and then moved up to grab his forearm. The party member, his wrist joint shattered by the single blow, dropped the sickle. Lee Sungmin pulled the captured forearm closer.

PEOK!

A sickle striking from another direction tore into the captured Elf's side.

"KAAAAK!"

*Nineteen.* Lee Sungmin counted the remaining members.

The Elf whose side was torn screamed, and the face of the one who had swung the sickle hardened. Lee Sungmin let go of the man's forearm and opened his hand wide. The palm strike he unleashed struck at the second Elf. The wolf leaped hastily to evade, and its rider gripped its waist tightly with both legs to maintain his balance.

Lee Sungmin changed his open palm into a fist and aimed at the air. Flesh Devouring Blood Fang, the third form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. The sharply honed enhanced qi pierced through the bodies of both the wolf and the Elf simultaneously.

"Why don't you get down?"

*Eighteen.* He erased another number in his head. He gripped the spear with both hands and pulled hard.

BBADEUK!

All five chains snapped.

"Riding on wolves doesn't seem to be working out for you."

The Hunting Team Leader ground her teeth. At her signal, the seventeen remaining party members dismounted. The five whose chains had been broken threw away their kusarigama. They held no weapons, raising only their clenched fists. The fist arts they had learned from Fist Honored One were the best martial arts they knew. The other members kept Lee Sungmin in check, either by spinning their chains or by raising their crossbows again.

"Are you not using magic, or can you not use it?" Lee Sungmin asked.

They could not use it. The Elves of other forests focused on learning magic and Spirit Magic, but the Elves of this forest learned Spirit Magic and martial arts. Not that their level of Spirit Magic was high. They could only handle low-grade Elementals at best, but in all their lives, the Hunting Party had never felt a great need for magic. When Fist Honored One taught them martial arts, he had also instilled in them a sense of pride.

The five weaponless Elves rushed toward Lee Sungmin. Having dismounted, they charged from all directions, using dizzying movement arts.

Lee Sungmin calmly controlled his breathing and circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The light of the art, now even more ominous, enveloped his spear. The first Elf to charge threw a clenched fist. It was fast and heavy. Lee Sungmin's feet were faster, and his spear was faster than his feet.

The spearhead collided head-on with the fist, piercing through it and driving all the way up the forearm. Even though they were both at the Supreme Peak, their levels were different. Having lived for decades, or even over a hundred years, training in martial arts did not mean they were stronger than him.

He pulled the spear out, slightly twisted his wrist, and thrust the tip high. The Elf could not even scream before his head was pierced. They were clad in a Protective Qi Aura, but Lee Sungmin's enhanced qi, which used both inner qi and Demonic Energy, pierced through it with far too much ease.

*Seventeen.* The number dropped by one more.

And it continued to drop in succession. It did not take long for all five of the Elves who had charged in bare-handed to die.

"Thirteen," Lee Sungmin muttered in a low voice.

Blood dripped from the tip of his spear.
