*A hilt to take my place.*

Gong Muwae was no fool. He knew exactly what that meant. "Are you suggesting we raise him as your successor?"

"To be precise, I'm suggesting we empower him for the benefit of both you, Director, and myself." Do Heon looked at Lee Cheonsang. With an unreadable and indifferent expression, he looked like a statue carved from hard rock. "This kid has talent. He's indifferent to worldly affairs and has no lust for power, but one thing is certain. He's the type to pay the price of 'himself' for someone who recognizes him."

A small glint appeared in Gong Muwae's eyes.

*Lust for power.*

The five human desires were food, sleep, sex, money, and fame. The desire for power meant a desire for money and fame. Furthermore, power was a tangible force that made it easy to satisfy the primal desires of sex and food. By properly grasping power alone, one could easily satisfy four of the five greatest desires.

He felt he could understand why people went mad for power. He felt he could truly 'understand' it.

"What do you think?" Do Heon asked with a smile.

"Are you interested in raising this kid properly? To make him a hilt to replace me and a treasured blade that serves only us?"

"......" Gong Muwae slowly stroked his beard. "The talent you've taken a liking to... If he was truly practicing the True Demonic Art until yesterday, then that kid's talent is extraordinary."

"That's right."

"However, does it have to be this kid? His talent is great, of course, but there are plenty of useful talents under your command, not to mention my own."

Do Heon nodded. "You're right."

"Then..."

"Director. Do you know why I want to make this kid my hilt?"

"Isn't it because his talent is outstanding?"

"That's part of the reason, of course. However, this kid is the least likely to betray me among all those I've seen."

Gong Muwae's eyes flashed. The expression of Lee Cheonsang, the person in question, showed no change. He just stood there silently, listening to their conversation.

"There are many capable people in the world. Countless talented individuals, too. However, it's by no means easy to find someone you can trust with a task, someone who won't betray you even in the worst-case scenario."

Do Heon smiled. "Isn't that also the reason you were tempted by my words, Director? I may be lacking in many ways, but I'm not so faithless as to betray someone I've decided to work with."

He was right. Even though he had disliked Do Heon so much, the reason he was pleased to have him on his side was precisely because of that personality.

Capable people were a dime a dozen, but trustworthy people were less than a handful. Those words from Do Heon pierced Gong Muwae's heart with surprising ease. 

"You're right. Truly, you are. However..." A doubtful expression crossed Gong Muwae's face as he looked at Lee Cheonsang. "Can you really trust a kid you've only known for a few days?"

"I've never met another kid who gave me the confidence to raise him after knowing him for only a few days."

"......"

Do Heon did not say anything provocative, like asking if Gong Muwae did not trust his eyes or if his judgment was that poor.

"Taking a demonic gladiator from the Demonic Coliseum isn't a major crime according to the rules, but it can't be considered a minor one either."

"......"

"When I first found that kid, I took the risk because I judged him to be worth it. It would’ve been great if you hadn’t caught me, Director, but I was prepared to get on my knees if I were caught."

"......"

"That kid will become a good sword."

Do Heon's confidence was genuine.

He could not say his eye for people was better than anyone else's, but Gong Muwae was a veteran in this field.

Suspicion was a way of life for Gong Muwae, and he always mixed falsehood with sincerity. He had his own backing, but there was a reason he had risen to this position himself.

From his perspective, Do Heon was being sincere.

*He seems certain, but...*

Gong Muwae looked at Lee Cheonsang and said, "Commander Do, I hope you understand that I'm not doing this because I don't trust you."

"Of course."

"I know your worth. That's why I trust you. However, no one knows if a sword picked up from who-knows-where will become a treasured blade, or a demonic beast that hurts its master's hand like a sword without a guard."

"That's a natural judgment to make."

"That kid, where is he from? If he's a gladiator, he can't be the son of a good family... and looking at his face, he doesn't seem like some punk who was rolling around in prison before being recruited."

*He's cautious.*

Do Heon had not thought Gong Muwae would be this cautious. He had thought of Gong Muwae as someone who knew his way around schemes, not as a meticulous person. However, Lee Cheonsang was different.

"If he asks about my origins, you have to tell him the truth. Don't hide that the Black Demon Commander brought me from the Lee Family Trading Company."

"You think he'll go that far?"

"I don't know much about the world, but I know this. The greedier a person is, the more cautious they are."

"...!"

"With the power of a Discipline Hall Director, it wouldn't be difficult for him to have someone investigate my background. You shouldn't lose his trust because of me. You must be completely honest."

"You could get hurt if you do that."

"I won't get hurt."

"Are you sure?"

"I am. The happier the Discipline Hall Director is to have you, the safer I will be."

"Hmm."

"Now it's your turn to trust me."

Do Heon told the truth about Lee Cheonsang. Of course, he did not mention the most important part, the deep trust between the two of them. He also disguised the fact that Lee Cheonsang went to the Demonic Coliseum because Do Heon himself had sent him, not by choice. He mixed nine truths with one lie to transform it into a 'fact' that seemed complete.

It was a method Gong Muwae used so well, and Do Heon now used it without even realizing it.

"......"

Gong Muwae was startled when he heard Lee Cheonsang's story. "He killed his father? Because he asked to die?"

"That's right." Do Heon said with a serious face, "That is how this kid sees the world."

"The fact that he went all the way to the Lee Family Trading Company just to repay the kindness of a rice ball is absurd enough..."

"That's precisely why this kid is someone you can trust."

It felt a bit like he was just being insistent, but after he went this far, there was no way not to believe him.

No, he had to at least pretend to believe him. Although Do Heon had bowed his head and offered to become his subordinate, he was still the Bright Demon Commander. The influence of that position was nothing to scoff at.

To gain a great subordinate, one must also know how to manage their subordinate's feelings.

Having finished his internal calculations, Gong Muwae nodded. "When you work in this field, a knife can slip through an unexpected opening. I've always had to be cautious."

"......"

"However, if he's a talent you're willing to back this much, shouldn't I support him whether it's true or not?"

A look of delight spread across Do Heon's face. Of course, it was a different kind of joy than what Gong Muwae was thinking.

Gong Muwae smiled and nodded emphatically. "Good. I'll do as you say. I'll give that kid a good push."

"Thank you."

"What's there to thank me for? You introduced me to a great talent, so I should be the one thanking you."

"That's not it. I'm thankful that a cautious person like you would trust me without questioning further."

Gong Muwae burst out laughing. "Hohoho, I'm glad you recognize that. I will use you well. When I rise, you will rise too, so I'll be in your care from now on."

"It goes without saying."

Gong Muwae patted Lee Cheonsang's shoulder. "You've met a good superior. Do great things for our Cult from now on."

Lee Cheonsang bowed his head without a word. He now knew well that such a demeanor actually inspired trust in others. 

Gong Muwae laughed heartily again and again. "Commander Do, you've worked hard today. Take this kid and go to the bar first. I'll join you after I take care of some leftover work."

"Understood."

"Be prepared to get soaked in alcohol tonight."

"Of course."

And so, a certain pact was formed.

Gong Muwae, who had entered the office, opened his mouth. "How long will it take?"

The answer to the question thrown into the empty air surprisingly came from the ceiling.

"Two hours will be enough."

"Go investigate."

"I accept your command."

Sitting at the table, Gong Muwae slowly clasped his hands together. "Commander Do. I hope you didn't lie to me. No matter how trivial the lie, if it becomes a habit, it will one day become a venomous snake and bite you in the ankle."

{sep}

On the way to the bar, Do Heon asked, "Did you master the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art?"

"I did."

"...That's truly incredible."

Even back in the investigation room, he had barely managed to stop his mouth from itching to ask.

Do Heon clicked his tongue in amazement. "How on earth did you learn it? I thought it would take a full three days just to prepare for the training."

"It just happened that way."

From Lee Cheonsang's perspective, it was difficult to explain. He had simply judged that to be the fastest way to assimilate the Vajra Yaksha into his body. 

However, now was not the time for such a conversation.

"Where do you think they'll send me?"

"......"

Do Heon's eyes gleamed. "We talked about a few things before you arrived. It seems the higher-ups are creating a new combat unit."

"A combat unit?"

"A combat unit, yes, but it seems they plan to make it a rather vicious group. They're splitting it in two. One in the Outer Hall, and one in the Inner Palace."

"If it's a new combat unit, won't there be a lot of backlash from the existing units?"

"Hoho, you know about that too?"

"Isn't that how it works?"

"It is. That's how the world is."

As they conversed, they arrived at the bar. The owner, who seemed to have been notified in advance, guided the two to a lavish room on the top floor.

Do Heon sat down on a cushion and said, "It's the Yaksha Command."

"Yaksha Command?"

"That's the name of the unit. The Yaksha Command belonging to the Inner Palace will be called the Yaksha Protection Command, and the one in the Outer Hall will be called the Yaksha Death Unit."

"......"

"The Yaksha Protection Command's work is similar to what the Wardens' Court does, so I heard most of the personnel will be selected from the Wardens' Court."

"If their work is similar, isn't there no reason to create it, especially if it invites backlash from the existing units?"

Do Heon smiled bitterly. "The Cult Leader isn't someone who cares about such things."

"......"

"On the other hand, the Outer Hall's Yaksha Death Unit is said to do work similar to External Inspection."

"Inspection?"

"Well, they call it inspection, but it's closer to a strike force that attacks the demonic path sects on the outskirts."

"......"

"The Cult Leader fears the weakening of the Divine Cult's power. The Yaksha Command is an organization being created for that purpose. If there are any sects that slander our Cult... or sects that have connections with the orthodox or unorthodox sects, like the Lee Family Trading Company, he intends to annihilate them immediately."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes deepened.

Do Heon smacked his lips with a bitter taste. "You'll probably end up in the Outer Hall, in the Yaksha Death Unit. Of course, you'll have to build up your skills first."

"I see."

As if wanting to change the mood, Do Heon asked in a much lighter voice, "By the way, do you think the Director will trust us?"

"After the investigation is over."

"Investigation?"

"He said he had some leftover work, didn't he? He'll probably come after confirming whether your words were true."

"...That's what that was?"

"I'm just guessing, but that man, he didn't seem to believe your words."

"How did you know that?"

Lee Cheonsang pointed to his own eyes with his finger. "His gaze was different."

"......"

"Since it's not a lie, he will trust you completely once he confirms it, and..." Lee Cheonsang looked at the door to the room. "He'll come in smiling. If he's pleased, that is."

Two hours later.

"Hoh, you haven't even shared a drink yet? Why didn't you go ahead and have a round first?" Gong Muwae laughed heartily as he sat in the seat of honor. "Let's drink ourselves silly today!"
