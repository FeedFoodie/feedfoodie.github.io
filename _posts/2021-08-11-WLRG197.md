The space around the Sword Saint began to distort, the very air transforming entirely into a storm of sword strikes. This was no simple spatial slash unleashed by a swing of his blade. The power to sever space was now imbued within the formless strikes themselves, a technique the Sword Saint knew was unavoidable. At such a close distance, an assault that turned the entire surrounding area into a weapon would be impossible for anyone to evade.

In an instant, Lee Sungmin vanished with the Traceless Step of Dancing Shadow Soul Break. The distance between them widened rapidly, but the Sword Saint, determined not to lose his prey, dashed forward in pursuit. As he moved, the spatial slashes swirled violently in his wake.

KRRRRRRA!

The ground was torn to shreds. The corpses of wolves and Elves that littered the forest floor were minced into fine pieces of flesh, the bloody confetti scattering into the air. Even without swinging his sword, a continuous torrent of slashes followed the Sword Saint's every move. It was as if he had become a living blade, a walking vortex of destruction.

[There's no end to this if you just keep dodging. What will you do?]

Void Monarch seemed to find the situation quite interesting. While Dancing Shadow Soul Break was fast, the Sword Saint's relentless pursuit was just as swift.

Lee Sungmin altered his footwork. The enhanced qi art, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, was compatible with any martial art, and it merged seamlessly with the enhanced qi generated by the footwork of Dancing Shadow Soul Break. The art was composed of six forms, including Blood Ring Shattering, Bloody Pursuit, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang, and Blood Remnant Calamity Flame. Now, he would unleash its ultimate technique, the very one that had nearly killed him in the North.

Three steps created the Three Step Fatality.

The enhanced qi that had spread in all directions coalesced into dozens of rings which then linked together, rotating in a fearsome display. Through the power of Three Step Fatality, the space around Lee Sungmin was completely dominated by his enhanced qi. The fierce rotation merged perfectly with Blood Ring Typhoon, creating a violet tempest.

The Sword Saint, who was rushing toward him, jerked back, startled by the massive flow of power. The indescribable ominous feeling he had felt when he first saw Lee Sungmin was now shaking the very space around them. It was a completely different method from the spatial intervention possible only in the Transcendent Realm, but he knew it could not be underestimated.

*Should I clash with it head-on? Can the storm of spatial slashes I've created gain the upper hand against that ominous force?*

How long had it been since he felt such uncertainty? And to think his opponent was a mere martial artist, not even someone from Heaven Beyond Heaven. He could not accept it. His pride in his martial arts, his pride in Heaven Beyond Heaven, pushed aside the creeping anxiety and compelled him forward.

The slashes of distorted space tore into the violet storm.

Inside the tempest, it was quieter than he had expected. *Was it all just for show?* The thought was fleeting, shattered the moment he entered the domain of enhanced qi. The swirling energy immediately focused on him.

*It's huge. Just how vast an area is he controlling?*

The spatial slashes enveloping the Sword Saint erupted outwards.

KRRAAANG!

The storm of enhanced qi rushing in was cut apart by the spatial slashes. *As expected.* A faint smile touched the Sword Saint's lips. No matter how condensed and sharply honed it was, the enhanced qi could not match a true spatial slash.

From the eye of the storm, Lee Sungmin raised his spear. *Not yet, not yet.* The spatial slash consumed a great deal of mental energy. He had deliberately used a grand technique to draw the Sword Saint in, forcing him to actively use his power.

[So, what's your plan?]

Lee Sungmin held his spear ready. The distance was not far. The Sword Saint was preoccupied, his focus entirely on cutting down the rampaging enhanced qi. Three Step Fatality and Blood Ring Typhoon had successfully tied down the Sword Saint, but they could not kill him, and the techniques were not over yet.

One more step.

Lee Sungmin extended his foot forward.

Four Step Chaos unfolded. The very nature of the enhanced qi changed as the rotation of the rings reached its violent peak.

-KWAAAAANG!

Explosions mingled in a deafening roar. The Sword Saint's face hardened. The spatial slashes that had spread in all directions returned to him, a necessary defense that drained his mental energy just to maintain. He could not retract them now. The entire area was under Lee Sungmin's control, and if he withdrew his power, his body would be torn apart by the enhanced qi.

The combined might of Blood Ring Typhoon, Three Step Fatality, and Four Step Chaos exploded outwards. The Sword Saint simultaneously shot out the spatial slashes he had gathered around his body while swinging his sword in a wide arc, unleashing not just spatial power but his own enhanced qi as well. Hundreds of slashes erupted from his blade. The clashing forces could neither advance nor retreat, and they simply scattered into nothingness.

Lee Sungmin watched the Sword Saint's every move. The Sword Saint was just as aware of him. *How long does he plan to keep aiming?* He had already guessed Lee Sungmin's strategy, to make him overuse his spatial slashes and drain his mental energy.

*He's too seasoned for his age. He must be accustomed to fighting.* Had he crossed the line between life and death in his own way, but the same was true for the Sword Saint. Though not from the Central Plains, he had been fascinated by martial arts his entire life. No, to be precise, he was fascinated by the sword. It was a blade he had dedicated his entire life to mastering. It would not easily bend or break. The Sword Saint's edge was still keenly sharp.

"……"

The encroaching enhanced qi was completely cut down by the Sword Saint's slashes. The attack, a combination of two Dancing Shadow Soul Break movement arts and the ultimate technique of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, had been utterly dismantled. Lee Sungmin could not help but feel a genuine admiration for the Sword Saint's martial prowess.

The scattered enhanced qi began to entwine around Lee Sungmin's spear, the violet energy enveloping the entire weapon in a furious rotation. He generously poured his inner arts and Demonic Energy into the spear. With various ways to replenish his power and the Demonic Energy of both Void Monarch and Urmuri at his command, his qi center still held an overflowing reserve.

KRRRRRR!

The rotating spear, the sky-piercer, let out a deathly sound just as the power of the spatial slashes faded and disappeared.

Lee Sungmin shot the sky-piercer forth. Its power momentarily escaped his control, forcing him back a step, but the spear flew in a perfectly straight line, just as he had intended.

The Sword Saint had no intention of dodging. His spatial slashes had weakened from cutting down all the enhanced qi, but he still had some leeway. It was enough to cut down any ordinary attack.

"……"

His face twisted. The rapidly approaching power was not something he could simply block. He hastily twisted his body, swinging his sword to scatter more spatial slashes. The space before him distorted completely, and the massive distortion collided with the sky-piercer.

Space shattered.

*Impossible…!* Could condensed enhanced qi really exert that much power?

The Sword Saint dragged his feet back and swung his sword again and again. The successive slashes he created collided with the sky-piercer, yet its momentum did not diminish. The Demonic Energy infused into the spear craved blood.

"Damn!"

Dismantling it was impossible. A wave of humiliation washed over the Sword Saint as he threw his body aside.

-KWAAANG!

Nothing remained where the sky-piercer had passed. Before the Sword Saint, who had barely escaped its range, could feel relieved, Lee Sungmin lunged forward. His body, mutated to be close to a Yokai, felt no fatigue despite repeating such movements and attacks. His heart pounded as if it would burst from his chest.

The Sword Saint's eyes widened at the sight of a completely unfatigued Lee Sungmin. He leaped freely through the air, swinging his sword.

KRRAAANG!

Formless slashes descended upon Lee Sungmin, but he did not retreat. Instead, he unleashed Bloody Pursuit and Homing Chain Lightning, the sheer power of his enhanced qi overwhelming. The Sword Saint gritted his teeth and dropped heavily to the ground, then boldly threw himself into Lee Sungmin's range. The enhanced qi that had been overflowing from his sword condensed onto the blade before spreading out in a wide arc like a net. He pressured Lee Sungmin, swinging the net of enhanced qi with his sword. If it touched him, he would not be caught, but sliced to pieces.

Soul Chasing Elimination and Blood Ring Shattering shot out, tearing a hole in the net.

"What a powerful spear technique…!" the Sword Saint exclaimed.

"It's not just a spear technique," Lee Sungmin replied, his foot shooting forward through the pierced net. Two Step Calamity and Blood Remnant Calamity Flame erupted, sending violet flames of enhanced qi surging in the direction of his step. "I'm adding the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art to all my martial arts."

With it, the power of his martial arts was greatly amplified. He compensated for the lack of finesse in the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art with the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear and Dancing Shadow Soul Break, while the demonic art added the raw power he needed.

"It's not a martial art to be ignored," the Sword Saint conceded.

Deathly Flash and Flesh Devouring Blood Fang. The Sword Saint reacted with a start as killing intent pierced his chest. His hastily swung sword deflected the Deathly Flash, but the scattered enhanced qi of Flesh Devouring Blood Fang collided with his Protective Qi Aura. His face twisted in pain.

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon wasn't a martial artist to be ignored either," Lee Sungmin said, never stopping as he continued to use his movement arts. Amidst the dizzying speed of the One Step Dancing Shadow, a killing move flashed.

Once again, Deathly Flash shot out. The Sword Saint steadied his churning stomach and spun his sword. The flat of the blade and the tip of the spear clashed. Shallow. Light. The Sword Saint's eyes snapped to Lee Sungmin. Only his right hand held the spear. Lee Sungmin's wide-open left hand was aimed straight at him. Blood Ring Shattering erupted as a palm strike.

"Keuk!"

Struck squarely by the blow, the Sword Saint's body flew backward. He still had mental energy to spare. He could still use his spatial slash. The space around him shimmered again. Although the consumption of mental energy was great, he could not adequately respond to Lee Sungmin's attacks without it. The enhanced qi that bastard used was incomparably more powerful than any he had ever faced.

[Here he comes again.]

His heart pounded. His heated body moved just as he willed it.

Lee Sungmin aimed his spear forward. The moment the spatial slash descended, he unleashed Void Path. The scenery distorted around the outstretched spear. *Ah.* Lee Sungmin faintly realized that he had finally reached it, to some extent. This was the ideal spear movement he had longed for, the state he had wanted to attain.

The spatial slash descending on him touched the Void Path, and the slash lost its sharpness. The distortion spread in the direction the spear advanced. The Sword Saint watched, his mind going blank as the spatial slash he had unleashed lost all its power right before his eyes. A violet light flashed from the tip of Lee Sungmin's spear. The Void Path…

Lee Sungmin walked calmly forward. The spatial slashes all scattered, and the distortion of space vanished.

The Sword Saint stood there blankly, unmoving. A large hole had been punched through his chest. He was so shocked that he could not help but ask.

"You… space…"

It was only for an instant, but there was no mistake. Lee Sungmin's move was undoubtedly the prowess of the Transcendent Realm.

Lee Sungmin gazed at the Sword Saint's face, his eyes calm and subdued. A sense of emptiness washed over the Sword Saint, and he let out a dry laugh.

"The last one… what was it?"

"The one that scattered your spatial slash was the Void Path of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear," Lee Sungmin explained in a calm voice.

"…And the one that pierced and killed me?"

"Flesh Devouring Blood Fang of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

"That's too much…" the Sword Saint muttered, coughing up a spray of blood. "Entering the Transcendent Realm… congratulations. However… you will die soon."

"Heaven Beyond Heaven will come to kill me, I suppose."

"You are… outstanding. Unbelievably so… and still so young. However… your spear. It was excellent… but not as good as the Spear King's…"

The Sword Saint's knees buckled.

"Don't be too confident… about killing me. Still, if I were to give you some advice on my way out… Keke! If you meet the Spear King, run. The Spear King is…"

That was as far as he got. The Sword Saint could not continue speaking. The wound piercing his chest was fatal. An Elixir might have allowed him to cling to life, but this was not a situation where he could use one.

*The Spirit Medium… she didn't mention my death…*

The thought surfaced from beyond his fading consciousness. All the Spirit Medium had said was that the Fist Honored One might die. Hearing those words, the Sword Saint had come to this forest to protect him. Could it be that she had not foreseen his death? His consciousness faded as death approached. He did not want to close his eyes, but contrary to his wish, his vision was gradually fading to black.

*Perhaps… she didn't tell me on purpose…*

On the verge of death, that was the Sword Saint's final thought.

Lee Sungmin gazed down at the man, who had died in a kneeling position. The Spear King. It was a title he had never heard before, but it was not something to worry about right now. The Spear King was not here. Although he had killed the Sword Saint, he did not feel particularly refreshed. If possible, he had wanted to kill him using only the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

*Because I am not you.*

Lee Sungmin thought of the Crazy Heavenly Demon as he slung the spear onto his back.

It was time to go to the village.
