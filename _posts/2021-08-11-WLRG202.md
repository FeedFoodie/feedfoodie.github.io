Though the voice was not loud, it carried to every ear on deck.

Zod's wide grin vanished, his gaping mouth snapping shut as he slowly turned his head to face Lee Sungmin.

"You… right. What was your name again?"

"Jung Hyunsoo," Lee Sungmin answered, still using the alias he had adopted.

"What in the world are you talking about?" the mediator demanded, his voice radiating killing intent as he strode purposefully toward Lee Sungmin.

Yangsu and the other mercenaries, who had never imagined Lee Sungmin would say such a thing, could only stare with bewildered expressions.

"I'm saying we stick to the plan," Lee Sungmin explained to the mediator. "The destination was Bellador from the start, so let's just go there. Is that so difficult?"

Behind the mediator, Angray's brow furrowed. To him, Zod, and the rest of the crew, it looked as if some insignificant mercenary was trying to dictate the ship's course on a whim. The mediator's hand went to his sword, drawing the blade with a sharp hiss.

"This ship is going to Rovcos. If you don't like it, get off now."

"No," Lee Sungmin replied calmly. "I have no intention of getting off, and I have no intention of going to Rovcos either."

"You just don't listen!"

"Neither do you," Lee Sungmin grumbled.

The mediator wasted no more words and swung his sword. The killing blow, an arc of steel that could easily take a man's life, flew toward Lee Sungmin's neck.

Lee Sungmin merely glanced at the blade. It stopped dead in mid-air, unable to complete its swing.

"Huh…?"

The mediator's eyes widened in disbelief. His sword refused to move, held fast as if by an invisible hand. While he struggled, Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the blade.

"……"

With a sharp crack, the sword snapped in two.

"We are going to Bellador," Lee Sungmin repeated, his voice unchanged.

*Magic?* The mediator's face turned pale. No, even if it was magic… he realized his mistake had been to look down on a C-Rank Mercenary and swing so carelessly. He stumbled back a few steps and shouted, "You bastard, you were a mage!"

"I'm not," Lee Sungmin answered honestly, but the mediator was no longer listening.

A blue sword aura erupted from the broken blade, drawing gasps of admiration from the other mercenaries. For C-Rank Mercenaries, emitting a sword aura was a feat comparable to reaching the heavens. The mediator roared and charged at Lee Sungmin again.

There was no need to draw his spear. Lee Sungmin simply released the aura he had been neatly suppressing.

RUMBLE!

An intangible energy swirled out from him, a silent tempest that completely scattered the sword aura imbued in the mediator's blade. The man coughed up a spray of blood and collapsed, unable to move. He was not the only one. Across the deck, every person sank to their knees as their legs gave out from under them.

"We're going to Bellador," Lee Sungmin said again, looking down at the collapsed figures. Even a master at the Supreme Peak could overwhelm those ignorant of martial arts just by releasing their aura. To Lee Sungmin, who had entered the Transcendent Realm, the dozens of people on deck were not people but insects, or perhaps even less than that.

"Don't say you refuse. I'm not persuading you. I'm threatening you. Killing all of you would be easy for me, and sparing you would be just as easy." As he spoke, his gaze fell upon Angray, who was drenched in a cold sweat as he stared back from the floor. "You may be from the family that leads the Merchants' Guild, but that's none of my business. If you really want to go to Rovcos, go back to that little boat still floating on the sea."

"You…! Do you know who I am…!"

"I told you. The Merchants' Guild is none of my business."

"As long as you live in this world… becoming an enemy of the Merchants' Guild means…"

"Are you threatening me?" Lee Sungmin asked in a quiet voice. Making an enemy of the Merchants' Guild would indeed make life in Eria difficult. That was an unavoidable, natural consequence, since the Guild was involved in most of the commerce that took place in this world. However, as a member of Erebrisa, Lee Sungmin was not subject to their influence.

"How about this," he proposed. "I kill you right here. Your ship was already sunk in a pirate attack. If you hadn't been picked up by this one, you would have died drifting at sea. If you die here, everyone will just think the pirates killed you."

"The crew of this ship…"

"Then I'll just have to kill the entire crew as well. I can spare a few who promise not to talk and make them sail the ship. We should be able to reach Bellador that way."

"B-but… the pirates…"

"If we meet pirates, I'll kill the pirates," Lee Sungmin said with a smirk. "Do you still not grasp the situation? You can't threaten me."

Angray's eyes trembled. After a moment of hesitation, he reached a hand into his coat. "If we go to Rovcos, I'll give you money…"

"I have more than enough money, and I'm not going to Bellador to make a profit."

*That's enough.* Lee Sungmin turned from Angray to Zod, who stood with his lips half-parted, still failing to grasp the situation.

"Order the crew to head for Bellador."

A faint killing intent brushed against Zod, who suddenly felt a warm wetness spreading through his lower half. It seemed he had drunk too much wine last night. Covering his dripping trousers with both hands, Zod spoke with a face on the verge of tears.

"L-let's go to Bellador."

His ambition was slipping away.

*

Through the commotion on deck, everyone came to a single, chilling understanding. Their lives were not in the hands of pirates or monsters. They were in the hands of the C-Rank Mercenary, Jung Hyunsoo. The mediator, after regaining consciousness, had charged at Lee Sungmin again, only to fail once more to break through his aura before collapsing.

With no reason to remain in his room, Lee Sungmin came out on deck. No one tried to stop him or even speak to him. Even Yangsu, who had been trying so hard to act friendly, kept his distance. They now realized he was not just a strong mercenary for a C-Rank, but someone who lived in an entirely different world.

"Who… who in the world… are you?"

It was Angray who finally approached Lee Sungmin as he stood looking out at the sea. Lee Sungmin turned his head to look at him. It seemed the boy had finally grasped the situation, for his tone was now filled with respect.

"C-Rank Mercenary Jung Hyunsoo," Lee Sungmin replied. He had no intention of revealing his real name or his title. For now, he had no desire to kill Angray, nor did he want to get entangled with him later by revealing his true identity.

"That's ridiculous. A C-Rank Mercenary subdued all of us with his aura alone…?"

"It's possible."

"Can't you just tell me honestly? Who you really are…"

"There are a few things I don't understand," Lee Sungmin interrupted, turning his body completely to face Angray.

The boy flinched under his direct gaze and, after a moment's hesitation, bowed his head deeply. Lee Sungmin looked down at Angray's bowed head and began to speak.

"I don't know much about the sea. However, I do know that pirates generally don't mess with the Merchants' Guild's trade ships. Especially not a ship belonging to the Delard Family. Even the most reckless pirates wouldn't dare touch it."

Angray remained silent, his mouth shut tight.

"The ship you were on," Lee Sungmin continued, "it must have been incomparably larger than this one, right? With many people on board and plenty of fighters too, and yet, everyone was wiped out, and the ship sank."

"That's… right…"

"Someone with the Delard surname would have been a valuable hostage. Why didn't they capture you?"

"Because my escorts sacrificed their lives to help me escape…"

"This is the decisive part." Lee Sungmin stared intently at Angray. "Angray Delard never went out to sea."

The boy's face stiffened.

"I heard he's in Bellador. As of yesterday, in fact. So, who are you?"

*I wondered what kind of person this Angray was, so I requested information through Erebrisa. Even though it's the sea, where information is hard to come by, as a member of Erebrisa, I can obtain information from them no matter where I am in the world. Angray is in Bellador. That is a certain fact.*

"I was curious," Lee Sungmin went on. "I don't know who you are, but I wondered why you'd go to such lengths to get on this ship. At first, I thought you were trying to seize it, but that doesn't seem right. This ship isn't worth the trouble, and the trade goods it's carrying are nothing special."

Angray's eyes trembled slightly. Lee Sungmin slowly reached out, and an invisible force wrapped around the boy's body, pulling him forward. Lee Sungmin grabbed his wrist, and Angray's face turned deathly pale.

"I did some digging. Among the pirates active in these waters, I heard there's one called the Hundred-Faced Thief. They say he's mastered a body modification technique that allows him to freely change his face and bone structure."

"Guuh…!"

The inner arts Lee Sungmin infused into him surged through the man's qi and blood. A cracking sound began to emanate from Angray's body. Lee Sungmin watched until the very end as the form of the small boy twisted and transformed. It was the first time he had seen someone who had mastered the body modification technique in person, and the sight was so disgusting he wished he hadn't.

He let go of the hand he was holding. The man who had been Angray, now revealed as the Hundred-Faced Thief, collapsed to the deck. His true form was a middle-aged man, as gaunt as a mummy.

"Lie, and you die," Lee Sungmin stated.

The Hundred-Faced Thief looked up at him, his body trembling violently.

"Why you insisted on going to Rovcos instead of Bellador. Why you, who had a decent pirate ship, boarded this vessel in such a state. Tell me everything."

"A-attacked," the man stammered. "We were attacked… my pirate ship… my ship and my crew are all dead."

"……"

"I-I barely escaped with my life, along with those two women. Since I could use the body modification technique to look like Angray… I used that to get on this ship."

"Why did you insist on going to Rovcos?"

"Because if we head toward Bellador, we'll run into those monsters again!" the Hundred-Faced Thief shouted, his face a mask of terror.

Lee Sungmin glared at the man, who was trembling like a leaf. "Speak clearly. What monsters? Who attacked your ship?"

"I-I don't know. Suddenly, it became night… and when I came to my senses, my ship was in the middle of a dense fog. Then, screams started coming from all directions. I was a pretty famous pirate in these parts, but my crew was massacred without being able to put up a fight."

"I don't understand what you're saying," Lee Sungmin grumbled and reached a hand toward the pirate.

Seeing the hand extend toward him, the Hundred-Faced Thief's face went white and he staggered backward. "I-it's not a lie! Really, without a shred of falsehood, it suddenly became night…"

He couldn't even finish his sentence.

A sudden chill ran down Lee Sungmin's spine, and he spun around. A pitch-black darkness was approaching from the distance, expanding rapidly before his eyes until it covered the sky. The sun was still high, with a long time until nightfall, yet everything suddenly grew dark, as if night had fallen in an instant.

"Wh-what is this?!" the mercenaries and crew cried out in confusion, looking around in a panic.

Lee Sungmin stood still, fully opening his senses. He had been through many things, but this was a first.

"It's… it's coming…" the Hundred-Faced Thief collapsed, clutching his head and grinding his teeth as he trembled.

SHHHHH…

A sound like something slicing through the water reached them. Lee Sungmin approached the railing and looked out at the sea. Even with his enhanced vision, he couldn't properly pierce the thick darkness. He could, however, faintly make out something approaching.

It was a ship.

It looked like a derelict vessel on the verge of sinking, but with its tattered sails fully unfurled, it was steadily cutting through the sea toward them. A grotesque statue woven from skulls hung from its bow. Before they knew it, the ship was right alongside Zod's trade vessel.

"Ughhh!" the Hundred-Faced Thief screamed. The handmaidens he had brought with him were also trembling in fear.

THUD.

Something leaped from the derelict ship and landed on the deck. A clattering sound echoed every time it moved, the sound of an empty suit of armor clashing against itself. From within the helmet's visor, a crimson light gleamed.

"…A Death Knight?" Lee Sungmin muttered, his voice laced with shock.
