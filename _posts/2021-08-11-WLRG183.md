Arbes never realized Kim Jonghyun's fingertips were aimed at him. He had already shed his false, doll-like body for a spiritual form, and Kim Jonghyun's movements were so secretive they were practically nonexistent.

From the very moment he had entered Predator and met the Lich, Kim Jonghyun had been aiming for this. Every bowed head, every obedient word, had been a carefully placed step toward this single, decisive action.

Luck, he knew, was on his side. Had Arbes not cast the Soul Exchange magic, achieving his goal would have been a far messier affair. He was truly lucky. The intervention of Envirus and Lee Sungmin had cornered Arbes, forcing him to cast the spell hastily and in an incomplete state. It was as if the entire situation was flowing in his favor, a realization that brought a slow, blooming smile to Kim Jonghyun's face.

Kim Jonghyun was no ordinary Black Mage. His contract with Caladra had been shattered when the Demon Lord was annihilated, and from that ruin, he had gained much. He now possessed an ageless body, eyes that could perceive the unseen, and even the authority of a Demon Lord.

He could not wield such power freely, for a human using a Demon Lord's authority carried an immense risk. A single misstep could mean the annihilation of his very soul, yet, for this moment, it was a risk well worth taking.

PANG.

A black light flared from Kim Jonghyun's fingertips. Only then, as the ghastly flash shot toward him in a straight line, did Arbes's spiritual form belatedly grasp the situation.

Betrayal!

The thought screamed through his soul, which trembled with rage and disbelief. He tried to react, to do something, but in his current state he was helpless, too powerless even to form a contract with a simple beastfolk. Still, he would not go down quietly. Arbes swelled the magic concentrated in his spiritual form, a desperate attempt to block the incoming light. It was useless. The beam passed through his magic as if it were not there, creating no collision, no resistance at all.

Seeing this, Arbes's soul screamed.

[Kuaaaargh!]

Pierced by the beam, his spiritual form convulsed violently. The light was an attack akin to a natural enemy for a Black Mage, for all Black Mages were beings whose souls were bound to a Demon Lord. Though it was a different one, Kim Jonghyun could also wield a fraction of that same authority. Arbes's spiritual form trembled as if electrocuted. His contract was not broken, but the attack had struck his soul itself.

Normally, a soul that lost its body would simply return to its Life Force Vessel to reconstruct a spiritual form. It would be a reverse-summoning, a loss of magic power, but for a Lich with an abundance of it, a single death was no great matter. This, however, was different. Kim Jonghyun's light had targeted Arbes's soul itself.

He was using Soul Binding, the very authority Caladra had possessed as a Demon Lord. It could only be used on Black Mages, and the risk was too great for frequent use, but Arbes's soul, Kim Jonghyun thought with a bitter smile, was worth it. He reached out, and Arbes's soul, unable to muster any real resistance, was pulled onto his palm as if drawn by a magnet.

It rested there, a dull black mass that resembled the Lich, twisting and struggling desperately as if it could not comprehend how things had turned out this way.

Now came the important part. If he took Arbes's soul entirely, he would also acquire the Lich's contract with Rotting Death. That was out of the question. He had finally been freed by Caladra's annihilation and had no desire to bind himself to another Demon Lord.

Kim Jonghyun grasped the soul with both hands. Though it struggled, it was bound to him and could not escape. He carefully scanned it, not to take the soul itself, but to extract the information and power it possessed.

"I'll return your soul," Kim Jonghyun muttered with a grin.

Of course, the soul that returned to its Life Force Vessel would belong to a being who was no longer a great mage. All of Arbes's memories, his knowledge of magic, and his immense power would belong to him. He carefully extracted the magic and information, a shimmering light that he shoved directly into his own mouth.

No, the soul would not even be able to return to its Life Force Vessel. It would not have enough magic power left to form a spiritual body. Either way, it did not matter.

A brilliant explosion erupted in his head. The vast knowledge and immense power Arbes had accumulated since his time as a human flooded Kim Jonghyun's body. The pain was excruciating, like his body was being torn apart and his brain set on fire, but it was only momentary.

"Haha...!" he cried out, clutching his head with both hands. His face was a mask of agony, yet his laughter was pure joy.

He was newly reminded of just how outstanding Arbes had been. Kim Jonghyun had been proud of his own level as a Black Mage, but compared to the Lich who had honed his craft for hundreds of years, he was a mere child.

*Soul Extinction magic. This is incredible. Fortunately, it seems I can use it too.*

The same was true for necromancy. Arbes had used Rotting Death's magic to cast them, but that could easily be replaced with Caladra's. Checking Arbes's memories, Kim Jonghyun grinned and sent the pathetically shrunken soul flying into the sky.

"Farewell," he called out in a cheerful voice.

That spiritual form, now lacking the strength even to possess Aine's body, would return to its Life Force Vessel. There, unable to form a new body, it would meet a complete death. After that, it would go to Rotting Death's side to suffer for eternity. That, however, was none of Kim Jonghyun's concern. In fact, he considered it a just end from a causal standpoint. Arbes, the Two-Faced Sage, had committed countless evil deeds for hundreds of years, including numerous human experiments. He deserved to be punished, even after death.

"What have you done...!" Envirus gasped.

He could not even support his own body, but he understood what Kim Jonghyun had accomplished. Still, seeing it with his own eyes, he could not believe it. He had never imagined a method existed to exploit the soul of a Lich on Arbes's level, not some mere dead spirit, but it was a fact, an unfortunate one for Arbes, who happened to be a Black Mage.

Kim Jonghyun stretched languidly, confirming the great magics now filling his mind. With this, he had far surpassed his previous level as a mage. He looked at Envirus with a smile.

"I have no intention of killing you. So please, rest assured."

"What...?"

"Killing you in your severely injured state would be quite easy for me now," Kim Jonghyun continued, "but I don't want to kill you needlessly and be cursed."

As if to show off, he demonstrated the Soul Extinction magic. A jet-black arm shot up from behind his back. He had grasped the formula, and the necessary magic power was simply replaced with Caladra's. Watching him cast the spell, Envirus fully realized the truth. Kim Jonghyun had taken everything from Arbes.

"I've obtained what I wanted," Kim Jonghyun said, a thick sense of satisfaction evident in his voice.

With this, he had obtained power close to the pinnacle even within Predator. He had not become a Lich, but from now on, Kim Jonghyun would be the Black Star of the Predators, standing above all others. The other inhuman leaders would have no choice but to acknowledge him. He was human, yet not human, and among them, there would be no one who cared about loyalty to Arbes.

"Aaaaargh!"

It was at that moment, as Envirus's lips trembled with indecision, that a scream tore through the air. With Arbes's annihilation, the Mark of Obedience had been erased. Aine, who had been limp on the ground, was now screaming.

WOOOOOOO!

The dark night sky resonated with her cry. Envirus flinched and looked back at her in surprise.

"Oh dear!" Kim Jonghyun burst into cackling laughter. He had deliberately left Aine's mark of obedience alone, seeing no need to take it and wanting to avoid unnecessary intervention.

Along with the scream, Aine's body twitched. Her collapsed body rose in an unnatural manner, like a marionette pulled by strings.

"I have no intention of killing you," he exclaimed cheerfully, "but I'm not sure what that pitiful Chimera will do."

Aine had no consciousness, yet she was moving. Arbes's magic circle was destroyed, but the Demonic Energy she had consumed from the land was still absurdly immense. That ownerless energy, now hers, craved only indiscriminate violence. Formed around the Black Heart, it faithfully followed that desire and took shape.

KWAKWAKWAKWA!

Aine's body exploded, or at least it looked like it did. Sharp blades shot out in all directions, sweeping through everything without distinguishing between friend or foe.

[Damn it!] the one who shouted was the Void Monarch.

Lee Sungmin had lost consciousness from consuming too much ownerless Demonic Energy. The Monarch hastily raised a shield of his own, unable to move Lee Sungmin's body as before but still able to protect it.

KWAANG!

The shield collided with Aine's attack and was utterly powerless. The Void Monarch ground his teeth, missing the days he had a physical body. Then, he could have suppressed such a crude power with a single gesture.

"Kugh!"

Envirus felt the same powerlessness. His high-ranking Elementals had been reverse-summoned one after another, leaving him barely able to cast even a simple defensive magic. The incomplete barrier he managed to create was struck by Aine's attack, sending him tumbling across the ground. Elemental Magic was efficient, but the risk of reverse-summoning was too great. The reverse-summoning of three high-ranking Elementals would turn even a skilled Elementalist into an irrecoverable wreck. The blow Envirus had taken was so severe that even his remaining Earth Elemental returned to the Elemental Realm, its existence no longer sustainable. The only reason Envirus was not a wreck was because he stood at the pinnacle of mages and was contracted with none other than the Spirit Queen.

"Hahaha!"

Having flown high into the air, Kim Jonghyun looked down at the rampaging Aine and burst into laughter. His shoulders shook as he watched not her, but Lee Sungmin. He did not think for a moment that Lee Sungmin would die here, but he had no intention of helping either. He had already taken what he wanted from this situation. From now on, he would be a thorough observer, watching to see which way Lee Sungmin's Destiny Force would flow.

*Is my observing now also fate? Or is it fate that I should intervene?*

He could not tell. Even his eyes, which could see so many things, could not see his own fate, nor could they know where the Destiny Force would lead. Kim Jonghyun decided to stick to his role as an observer.

*If this world is a story... you are the protagonist.*

He suppressed a laugh. If it were a story, Kim Jonghyun knew very well that he himself was not lacking in any way to be a protagonist. He was a special being who had experienced special situations. However, he was not the protagonist.

*Although, if you die here, the story will end.*

Kim Jonghyun was certain that would not happen.

[Get a hold of yourself!]

The Void Monarch's shout echoed from a distance, awakening his sleeping consciousness. Lee Sungmin's eyes fluttered open. He felt awful, like he had forced himself to eat the terrible food on Mount Mushii or taken a breath that smelled only of stench. His lips turned down as he fought the nausea rising from within. His vision swayed as if he were drunk, and the very ground seemed to shake.

[Are you awake?] the Void Monarch asked, his voice brightening.

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, staggering to his feet. His consciousness had returned, but he was still dazed. KWAANG! The Void Monarch took the brunt of rushing blades for him.

"...What happened?"

[I don't know!] the Monarch roared.

Lee Sungmin looked down at his empty hands. His spear was gone, thrown at Aine earlier and never retrieved. His heart pounded like crazy, and his left eye ached as if it were on fire. The foreign pain spread through his entire body. He clutched his chest with a groan, feeling a profound disconnect, as if the blood in his veins was not his own.

A throbbing pain struck his head, and he unknowingly raised a hand to his face. His eye. It felt like his right eye was being pulled out.

When his hand came down after an animalistic cry, both of his eyes had turned gold.

[No!] the Void Monarch shouted urgently.

He knew very well that Lee Sungmin did not want to abandon his humanity and had always respected that wish, but now, both of Lee Sungmin's eyes shone with a brilliant gold.

In an instant, the pain vanished as if washed away. His consciousness floated. His fighting spirit inner qi fell silent as the ownerless Demonic Energy from the magic circle spread throughout his body.

DDUDEUK, DDUDEUDUK.

A bizarre sound began to echo from within him. The Void Monarch hurriedly tried to enter Lee Sungmin's body to push the energy out, but it did not work. A Mental Barrier rejected his intrusion, as if Lee Sungmin himself had wished for this to happen.

[Hold onto your consciousness! Weren't you the one who said you didn't want to become an inhuman!]

Even that desperate shout scattered into the air. Amidst an inexplicable comfort, Lee Sungmin's consciousness prepared to fall asleep. The violent Demonic Energy had become a cradle for him.

*I don't want to sleep,* he thought, a faint and distant emotion.

However, contrary to that thought, his body was preparing for a frenzy.
