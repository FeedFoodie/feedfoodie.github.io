Was this Despair? Lee Sungmin could not answer.

He had truly believed he could do it, that he stood a chance. He had convinced himself that if he kept fighting like this, he could deplete Jeniela's magic power and perhaps even defeat her.

It was nothing but arrogance.

The gaping wound in his abdomen regenerated as torn organs, muscles, skin, and bone all healed anew. Jeniela's loving gaze fell upon the sight, and her long fingers caressed Lee Sungmin's stomach. The tips of her fingers traced the lines between his mended abdominal muscles.

"Isn't it enough?" Jeniela asked with a chuckle.

What wasn't enough? The hand she had on Lee Sungmin's shoulder tightened with immense force. Her once-gentle touch became a horrific grip that crushed his shoulder to pieces.

"Are you still holding onto hope? I certainly hope so. It'll make me feel so much better when you truly fall into Despair."

He glared at Jeniela's face. *Right, there was no way it would be this easy.* The fight with the Spirit Queen had been far too simple.

"Try harder."

Jeniela released his shoulder. As if to prove that everything until now had been a game designed to give him hope, the magic power surging from her body possessed a density incomparable to before.

As his shattered shoulder regenerated, he clenched his empty hand.

*I'll get another earful from Celgerus.* The thought came to Lee Sungmin even in this situation. *He put so much effort into making it for me.*

He moved his newly regenerated shoulder, testing it.

[You... are you okay?]

[Are you alright...?]

Void Monarch and Rubia asked worriedly. Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile.

"There's no way it would be this easy."

He had held onto hope, it was true. He had believed he could win, that he had a chance, and the shock of having that hope trampled was just as real. A sense of emptiness washed over him, a feeling born from the realization that his dominance over Jeniela had been entirely by her design.

And yet, a part of him felt it was only natural. The queen of monsters, a being who had lived for hundreds of years, could never be this weak. Even with all the preparation in the world, martial arts theory dictated that he had no way of overwhelming her. This was different from the hardships he had overcome with the help of the Destiny Force.

The Demonic Energy gathering in his hand formed a long spear. With no other weapon to wield, he would have to use this instead.

Though his hope was trampled, he did not Despair. Jeniela had only been weaker than he had thought, and now she was showing the strength he had expected. In the end, nothing had changed.

So, he had to struggle with all his might. Lee Sungmin flipped his body in mid-air and righted himself.

Jeniela watched his expression, and a tremor ran through the corners of her lips. She swallowed a heated breath, savoring the deep pleasure welling within her.

There was still fun to be had. She loved a resilience that refused to break even when pushed to the brink, for she enjoyed watching someone struggle so desperately. At the very, very end, the ecstasy she felt watching that resilience shatter completely was unparalleled. Even without the All-Seeing Eye, Jeniela could imagine the sight.

*Your Despair will be the most exquisite pleasure.*

It would be more potent than the combined Despair she had seen from all those she had turned into vampires over hundreds of years.

*I want to make you Despair, knowing you can never win.* Jeniela pressed a hand to her pounding heart.

Her throat grew parched. It had been a long time since she had felt such a thirst. She truly, truly wanted to eat, to drink. Her fangs itched.

She had held back for so long. There had been many chances to kill him, from their first meeting to every moment after, even now. Jeniela could have killed Lee Sungmin at any time. She had refrained only to let him ripen more sweetly. Now that the time had come, there was no need to hold back any longer. Jeniela's body shot forward.

Wreathed in red magic power, she plummeted toward Lee Sungmin like a meteor.

*The density is different.*

Lee Sungmin kept that in mind as he expanded his consciousness. Jeniela had claimed to have grasped the structure of the Illusionary World, but that did not mean the martial arts he could use within it were completely powerless against her.

He leaped up to meet the falling Jeniela, swinging the Formless Spear in his hand. As the space behind him distorted, it fired out a volley of spear strikes, sending hundreds of bolts of Homing Chain Lightning shooting upward.

Jeniela's magic power spread out wide, and the Homing Chain Lightning scattered before impact. He let it happen. He had used it as a feint to briefly restrict her movements, and in that opening, he thrust the Formless Spear forward.

CRACKLE!

Violet currents sparked in all directions as Jeniela's body shot out from within the scattered magic. Her hand shot out, reaching for his neck, but the Crouching Snake Soul Devouring: Inverse Lightning twisted his spear's trajectory upward. The rising spear tip met her advance and shattered her fingertips before they could find their mark.

CRACKLE!

Lee Sungmin's body was thrown back as it collided with Jeniela's magic power. The level of impact was on another scale. The light of the Fairy Realm grew fainter, and his breastplate crumpled as the shock permeated his body and shook his internal organs.

He endured it. Lee Sungmin swung the spear held in both hands horizontally, trying to fend off Jeniela's approach. She did not dodge and instead pushed her body forward.

CRUNCH!

Jeniela's body was split in two. It immediately collapsed into mist and reformed right next to him. Her regeneration speed was as formidable as ever. Lee Sungmin released the spear with his right hand and struck out at her with his palm. The Flesh Devouring Blood Fang of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art collided with her magic power, but Jeniela's hand broke through it and grabbed his.

CRUNCH!

His wrist was torn off. Though his right hand was gone, Lee Sungmin did not scream and instead shot his foot forward. A wound like this would regenerate. The One Step Dancing Shadow of Dancing Shadow Soul Break created dozens of afterimages, and the thunder cloud that formed with them enveloped Jeniela's surroundings.

"More," Jeniela whispered, her hands moving.

RUMBLE!

The raging red magic power tore the thunder cloud to shreds. Lee Sungmin gripped the Formless Spear with his regenerated hands. Deathly Flash pierced Jeniela's body, or it should have. The raging magic power blasted the Formless Spear apart.

"More," Jeniela urged in a heated voice.

He compressed his Demonic Energy and inner qi. The two forces of different natures created a new Formless Spear. He charged the swirling, gathered power to its limit and fired sky-piercing. Jeniela laughed loudly and leaped toward the attack.

CRACKLE!

Her body split sky-piercing in two as she drew closer.

"More."

*Don't get impatient. Don't be afraid.* Lee Sungmin gave himself the suggestion as he gripped a new Formless Spear. The light of Genesis filled the weapon. The world within the Illusionary World filled with Demonic Energy as the martial arts theory of Myriad Thunder and Genesis enveloped the entire space.

*My opponent is a monster. I'm a monster too.* His mind grew distant. The headache that had disappeared returned, his brain cells repeatedly dying and regenerating. His fading consciousness and awakened insight searched for a way out. *Change the incantation, make it stronger, more perfect.* Jeniela was close.

Magic power unfurled from behind Jeniela's back, forming what looked like giant wings of light, like the twilight just before sunset, or like gushing blood. The unfurled light folded in, and a wave of magic power with tremendous density covered the world filled with Myriad Thunder and Genesis.

"More."

He thrust the spear. Lee Sungmin stared ahead with wide eyes and saw the tip of the spear he had thrust disintegrate.

*Hold on!* Void Monarch shouted.

His Demonic Energy was added.

"Even though you died hundreds of years ago," Jeniela giggled, "what are you trying to do as just a soul?" she sneered.

Genesis and Myriad Thunder scattered. Lee Sungmin's body was pushed back by the flooding wave of magic power. The protection of the Fairy Realm's queen shone with a great light, as if struggling desperately. A glittering light enveloped his body. He gritted his teeth and raised his Protective Qi Aura.

Jeniela's body slowly approached. She grinned and stretched out her open hand. All the magic power that covered the Illusionary World gathered into her palm, where it condensed into a small sphere.

"Now then," Jeniela said with a playful smile.

The sphere, pushed slowly forward, floated gently toward Lee Sungmin.

THUD.

The sphere grew larger.

THUD.

It grew larger still.

THUD, THUD, THUD.

The fully grown sphere became large enough to fill the entire space. Lee Sungmin watched it with a pale face.

*More...*

The word Jeniela had whispered, he was now muttering.

It was overwhelming. Against magic power of a different league, the means available to him were limited. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. He gripped the Formless Spear tightly and leaped forward, relentlessly unleashing the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear from the first strike to the eighth. He did the same with the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art and the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, but the sphere Jeniela had fired only had its outer layer worn away, its core remaining completely undamaged.

*Should I use the pinnacle of martial arts? What about the exhaustion? The final secret art of Dancing Shadow Soul Break...*

He was too slow to act.

The Illusionary World shattered, and the world Lee Sungmin had created collapsed around him. The sky returned to a pitch-black night, a large full moon rising in its center. Jeniela stood with the full moon at her back, looking down. Lee Sungmin fell powerlessly, his wavering eyes fixed on the woman who stood in the very center of his blood-soaked vision.

*I was too late.*

Lee Sungmin calmly assessed his mistake. The throbbing headache intensified. A little too late, he understood the situation he had just been in. He had been hasty in the face of imminent danger, impatient because he could not rashly use the pinnacle of martial arts, and flustered by the power of Jeniela's attack. The martial arts he had unleashed recklessly were far from enough to push back the threat. The same went for the final secret art of Dancing Shadow Soul Break. If he was going to use it, he had to do it decisively and without delay.

*If it weren't for Osla's protection... it would have been dangerous.*

Lee Sungmin flipped his body mid-fall. He saw a lake below, ripples spreading across its surface. The battle within the Illusionary World had not destroyed the scenery of the lake. His eyes met Osla's, who was looking his way with a tense expression. He wanted to smile, if only to reassure her, but he could not bring himself to.

His awakened insight calculated the odds of victory, searching for a path to survival against that absurd monster. Escape was impossible. If he decided to flee and acted on it, Jeniela would never let him get away.

What about Osla's support? It was out of the question. He had already discussed that with her before Jeniela arrived. Osla's martial power was incomparable to Jeniela's. She could protect him from Jeniela's attacks with various blessings, but fighting the vampire queen directly was impossible. Even if she used her authority to bind Jeniela to this forest, that too had its limits. Osla estimated the maximum time she could hold Jeniela was three days.

Ultimately, that meant Jeniela would be free from this forest after three days. By then, Jeniela would no longer have the blessing of the full moon, but she possessed the Demonic Eye that could forcibly summon one. Escaping from here would not put him in an advantageous position. In fact, if he were to fight after three days, he would have to do so under the full moon without Osla's protection.

[Now that it's come to this, we have no choice but to trust your final gambit. Do you think it's possible?] Void Monarch asked.

Lee Sungmin, who had stopped his fall in mid-air, checked his internal injuries. There was no damage. He had regenerated, thanks to Osla's renewed protection.

*It's a gamble.*

It was a gamble, and one with low odds of success. He had strengthened his Black Heart by devouring Aine. His gambit was to evolve his body during this battle using the Black Heart's ability. Until now, he had saved his life and achieved victory several times thanks to such evolutions.

*I can still do this.*

Excluding that uncertainty, he calculated his chances of winning.

They were not zero yet.
