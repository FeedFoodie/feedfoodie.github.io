He had to admit, he had little experience facing a swordsman like this. It was more than that, really. He had rarely faced any master who, like Namgung Heewon, had properly learned the martial arts. The Namgung Clan was a renowned family of the sword, one of the prestigious clans that everyone acknowledged. While many sects were famous for their swordsmanship, only the Namgung Clan had established a great house built entirely upon the sword. Of course, the inexperienced Lee Sungmin had never before faced their supposedly incredible techniques.

*In the first place, I have little experience fighting swordsmen.*

Just how skilled was Namgung Heewon? They already called him 'the Sword Dragon', so he must have at least surpassed the peak realm. Yes, that was the minimum. Since Namgung Heewon had seen that Lee Sungmin was knocking on the door of the Supreme Peak, perhaps he was doing the same.

That did not mean their levels were equal. Even among peak masters, the gap could be immense. Furthermore, the experience Namgung Heewon possessed would be on a different level from his own, both in quality and quantity. He had been born into the outstanding Namgung Clan and had received guidance from masters since childhood.

Lee Sungmin had experience too, but the thirteen years he had lived as a C-Rank Mercenary and the four years he had accumulated since returning to the past could hardly be called great. The Lee Sungmin of his past life had never even stepped over the threshold of the peak realm.

For that reason, he could not help but be tense. Lee Sungmin lowered his knees and gripped his spear, feeling as if time had stopped. The standoff stretched into what felt like an eternity, though in reality, not even a minute had passed. His consciousness stretched out like taffy while his breathing grew ragged. The pounding of his heart echoed in his ears as his vision narrowed, the surrounding scenery distorting until only the figure of Namgung Heewon, standing with his sword, remained clear.

Namgung Heewon was the first to move.

Lee Sungmin did not know what kind of movement arts he was using, but each step was so strange and mysterious that it dazzled his eyes. He instinctively dragged his feet and unleashed the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. If Namgung Heewon's movement contained illusion, then his own contained speed.

Traceless Step, the footwork within the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, pursued Extreme Speed. With a WHOOSH, Lee Sungmin's body vanished and closed the distance to Namgung Heewon, who smiled with delight at his approach. A spear, no, a staff. He liked Lee Sungmin's recklessness, charging in while wielding such a long weapon.

The wooden sword in Namgung Heewon's hand moved. It seemed to drift slowly, yet the semi-circular trajectory it created held the essence of absorption, as if forcibly pulling in everything nearby. Lee Sungmin firmly gripped the staff as it squirmed in his hands and met the wooden sword with the 'Na' technique.

"......"

With a dull sound, their weapons bounced apart. Lee Sungmin readjusted his grip on the staff and pulled both arms far back. The first exchange was to gauge distance and reaction speed. He had chosen a long weapon and had no intention of playing along at close range. It might not have been a problem if Namgung Heewon were less skilled, but the man he sensed was by no means his inferior.

He stepped back while thrusting the staff forward. By extending his arms and upper body, the distance he could cover became nearly double that of Namgung Heewon. This was the advantage of a long weapon. Once you established and maintained that distance, it was difficult for an opponent to break through.

PAPAPAK!

The staff exploded from his hands. The tip rushed forward in a wild flurry, and though it was blunt, it was not so harmless that one could afford to be hit. Namgung Heewon used his movement arts and held his sword upright. Each time the staff made contact with the slightly trembling wooden sword, the trajectory of Lee Sungmin's thrusts was deflected. It was not a matter of weight. Namgung Heewon's timing in applying force to bend the trajectory was simply perfect.

He pulled back the thrusting staff and, in that instant, put a spin on it. He unleashed the first strike of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Soul Chasing Elimination. Since it was a friendly duel, it did not contain enough power to cause harm, but it was a thrust imbued with a distinct weight compared to his previous attacks.

"Oh."

A short exclamation of admiration escaped Namgung Heewon's lips. The sword in his hand immediately spun. He swung the reverse-gripped sword upward from below. TWACK! Even though it was not a killing move, Soul Chasing Elimination was deflected midway. Namgung Heewon took a few steps back and whistled.

"This is quite fun. If it were a real spear, it would've been even fiercer… Haha! This is getting exciting, but it's not enough. So… how about this?"

The corners of Namgung Heewon's mouth curled up.

"Let's make a little bet."

"What kind of bet?" Lee Sungmin asked, his eyes on the wooden sword in Namgung Heewon's hand. Namgung Heewon, who had been holding the sword in a reverse grip, corrected his hold.

"Since this is a friendly duel, it'd be best if it doesn't harm our friendship. If I win, how about you start calling me Hyung-nim?"

"…Serve you as my Hyung-nim?"

"I don't think it's a strange suggestion. After all, I'm older than you."

"What happens if I win?"

"Then I'll serve you as my Hyung-nim. Isn't that great? You'll become the Hyung-nim of me, Namgung Heewon, the young master of the Namgung Clan," he said with a grin. "Of course, I have no intention of becoming the younger brother of someone younger than me. So I'll do my best to win."

"…Then isn't that too advantageous for me? I'm younger than you. Even if I lose…"

"If you don't do your best because of that, then you're just that kind of martial artist."

Lee Sungmin was left speechless.

"Anyone who has learned the martial way should never accept defeat sweetly."

Those words completely eliminated any room for defeat Lee Sungmin had been entertaining. He resolved to use his full strength to win.

"Now then, let's continue our fun," Namgung Heewon said, brandishing his sword.

Once again, he moved first. If his previous approach was illusion, this time it was speed. Namgung Heewon closed the distance rapidly, the sword in his right hand moving busily. Sword strikes filled Lee Sungmin's vision. His expression hardened as he infused inner arts into both hands gripping the staff.

A true master of the spear should be able to respond to all attacks with just the three basics of Rannachal, without complex arts. Those were the words Wiji Hoyeon had spoken while teaching him. Receive attacks from the outside with Ran. Receive attacks digging in from the inside with Na. If you see an opening, thrust with Chal.

He had heard those words countless times. They had already become a truth and a faith in his mind. However, knowing something and executing it perfectly were two different things. Namgung Heewon's sword was fast and heavy, yet full of variations. It was so viciously out of place that one could hardly call it the sword of the prestigious and orthodox Namgung Clan.

His impression was that it was a sword that thoroughly pursued only victory. That practical swordsmanship was so sharp, despite being performed with a wooden sword, that he was afraid to even approach.

He had to create distance. Every time Lee Sungmin slid his feet back, Namgung Heewon strode closer. The staff held in both hands did not perform a 'Chal' thrust. Instead, a widely swung staff flew toward Namgung Heewon's head. Just because it was a spear did not mean thrusting was all it could do.

Namgung Heewon bent his upper body forward to evade the attack. Lee Sungmin bent his right hand downward, letting the staff slide within his palms. In an instant, its range changed. Namgung Heewon skillfully responded to the staff flying toward his temple. The wooden sword, swung with a WHIP, collided with the staff. No, rather than colliding, it 'touched'. The staff and the wooden sword stuck together as if glued, visible proof that Namgung Heewon was extremely skilled at handling his inner arts. Every time he moved the wooden sword, Lee Sungmin's staff followed. Lee Sungmin infused even more inner arts, rotating the staff outward. TWACK! With a dull sound, the weapons separated.

From there, Lee Sungmin unleashed the second form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Homing Chain Lightning. Dozens of afterimages of the staff aimed for Namgung Heewon, who extended his wooden sword straight forward. CLANG! With a loud sound, the staff Lee Sungmin thrust out was knocked upward.

No matter how fast or consecutively he thrust, in the end, he was only holding a single staff. Namgung Heewon's technique of precisely meeting one of them was simple yet accurate.

More.

Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip. He mixed his movement arts and spear technique, no longer just creating distance and attacking in a straight line, but aiming for various angles. Namgung Heewon countered even that. His response was so perfect that it showed no openings, and his wooden sword seemed harder, lighter, and heavier than Lee Sungmin's staff. He could not imagine any other way to describe such a contradictory assessment.

The sword of the Namgung Clan.

The traditional swordsmanship of a prestigious family, which Lee Sungmin was experiencing for the first time, pressed down on him. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear could not break free. At some point, he started being pushed back. Despite wielding a long spear, he could not withstand Namgung Heewon's sword and was forced to retreat. Sweat pooled in his palms, and blood started to flow from the lip he was chewing to shreds in his anxiety.

Namgung Heewon's sword pierced through the path of his spear.

"Gasp."

Lee Sungmin sucked in a breath. The wooden sword was touching his Adam's apple.

"I win," Namgung Heewon said with a mischievous smile. "From now on, you will call me Hyung-nim."

"…Umm…"

"If you really want, we can have another match with real swords instead of wooden ones, and a spear instead of a staff. I think that would be more fun."

"No, that's alright," Lee Sungmin sighed, shaking his head. "I… lost. Hyung-nim."

At those words, Namgung Heewon beamed.

"You're very peculiar, younger brother."

The moment Lee Sungmin called him Hyung-nim, Namgung Heewon immediately changed his way of speaking. He plopped down on the spot and looked up at Lee Sungmin, who hesitated for a moment before sitting down opposite him.

"What's so peculiar about me?"

"Your spear arts have already reached the level of a master. Yes. The spear arts, that is, but that's what's so ambiguous."

What did he mean, it was at a master's level but also ambiguous? Lee Sungmin frowned. At his gaze, Namgung Heewon chuckled.

"Rannachal. The three basics that every spearman knows. In that regard, you're at a master's level, but your other spear technique, the one that isn't Rannachal, is a bit lacking."

"…What do you mean by that?"

If not Rannachal, he must be talking about the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Namgung Heewon smiled at his question and replied.

"From what I can see, you lack real combat experience. Especially experience fighting with advanced spear arts. You know how to use the basics, but advanced techniques can't be skillfully executed with theory alone. You seem to have far too little experience fighting against masters."

Lee Sungmin could not refute those words. He was right.

"And another thing. You're too unskilled at handling your inner arts."

"Inner arts… you say?"

"That's right. It feels like your body can do it, but your inner arts can't keep up. Not that you seem to be lacking inner arts… In this case, it means either the level of your technique is low or your qi and blood vessels aren't properly opened. Is it because the Conception and Governing Vessels aren't open? Or Body Reformation?"

"Body Reformation?"

"When the Conception and Governing Vessels are opened and the qi center grows, the body changes accordingly, but this is a strange case. Your inner arts can't keep up with your body, but your body hasn't undergone Body Reformation either. Mind, qi, and body. They're all tangled up in a mess. What in the world is your body, younger brother?" Namgung Heewon muttered, tilting his head.

At those words, something occurred to Lee Sungmin. The Black Heart. The heart that had suddenly assimilated with his body… it seemed to have twisted him in a strange way.

"Well, that… can be supplemented if you train hard in your inner arts from now on. The same goes for experience. Do you know why I wanted to make you my younger brother?"

"…I don't know."

"You will one day enter the realm of the Supreme Peak. I don't know when that will be, but it's an undeniable fact," Namgung Heewon said with a grin. "It means you're someone who's worth having as a younger brother."

"You don't care about my personality, background, or past?"

"What are you talking about?"

"Just because I become your… no, the Hyung-nim's younger brother. There's no guarantee that I'll sincerely serve you as my Hyung-nim, is there?"

"Do as you please," Namgung Heewon said with a shrug. "It's not my business. What's important is that I call you younger brother, and you call me Hyung-nim. That's enough to show others."

"Did you make me your younger brother just to show off to others?" Lee Sungmin asked with a look of disbelief.

Namgung Heewon nodded. "Didn't I tell you? I have to go to Mount Song Shaolin and pay my respects to Great Monk Bulyeong along with the heirs of other families," he said as he got to his feet.

"Putting on a show is important."

As he said that, Namgung Heewon's smile looked full of enjoyment.
