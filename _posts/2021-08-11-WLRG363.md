A disgusting, fishy taste coated his tongue.

Lee Sungmin scooped water into his mouth and spat. He gargled, spat again, and rinsed until the foulness at the back of his throat began to recede. The taste lessened, but the memory of the texture as he chewed lingered, an unpleasant sensation he hated to even acknowledge. What felt worse was that even as the disgust and the fishiness had churned within him, a small part of his mind had whispered, *It's better than I expected...*

[Because your body is a Yokai.]

It was a simple fact that Yokai ate people. He may have once been human, but now that he was a Yokai, cannibalism was a natural act. His mind recoiled with physiological revulsion, yet his body accepted it. Thanks to that, he had not vomited, a fact that only irritated him more.

His inner qi had greatly increased, as had his Demonic Energy. It was not a palpable surge, however. His depleted qi center had been replenished, but the sheer immensity of the power he already possessed meant that eating the Blood Demon had not boosted him by much. It was the same as when he had consumed the Dragon Heart, and similar to when he had inherited the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The absolute amount had risen slightly, and the memories... they were a little confusing.

They came to him like shattered fragments. Even when he had inherited the Chairman's power, Lee Sungmin had not been able to take everything. It was nothing like how Aine could freely wield martial arts she had never experienced before through predation. *Is it a problem with my body after all?* He felt a flicker of disappointment but knew it could not be helped. Excessive greed would only ruin him.

"Was it good?" the Black Dragon Knight asked, his expression one of pure curiosity.

Lee Sungmin answered not with words but with a foul look that made the knight clear his throat. Just then, Yana approached them.

"There were no Vampires," she reported.

"They didn't run away?"

"I doubt any Vampire skilled enough to escape my notice was here. Unless it was the Queen herself."

"There were plenty of Black Mages, though," the Black Dragon Knight added, pointing a finger to one side. Hundreds of them were sprawled in a corner of the Blood Alliance's spacious training ground. "Fewer than I thought. Most of them were small fry. It seems the ones you could call the real deal had already left."

The Blood Alliance was a sacrificial pawn. Lee Sungmin recalled the Blood Demon's words. Just as he had claimed, the organization was large in size but lacked any real substance. It had many martial artists, but their potential was not enough to tempt Jeniela. The high-ranking Black Mages, on the other hand, would have been far more appealing to her. What a martial artist and a mage could do were different, and Jeniela, who did not lack for power, might have had a greater need for those who wielded Dark Magic.

*There are no such memories.*

A portion of the Blood Demon's martial arts had been added to his own, but any knowledge of his relationship with Jeniela or her objectives was completely cut off. A fresh wave of disappointment washed over Lee Sungmin as he turned away.

"There's no need to kill them," he said. "Let's leave the aftermath to others."

"To whom?"

"The Blood Demon who ruled this place is dead. The Blood Alliance has fallen. Someone will fill the void, whether the Mages Guild intervenes or they choose a new leader among themselves. Without the Blood Demon, they're no threat."

The Black Dragon Knight tilted his head. "Are you satisfied with that? Weren't you furious that the place the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman once ruled was defiled?"

"The proof is already complete." Lee Sungmin glanced at the spot where the Blood Demon had been lying.

There was no corpse. After his heart was torn out and eaten, the Blood Demon's body had simply dissipated into a red mist. Lee Sungmin recalled the expression the man had worn just before he vanished. Despite meeting such a gruesome end, he had held a satisfied smile until the very end.

What had satisfied him? Was it dying in battle? Or was it the knowledge that he could convey what he had seen and experienced directly to Jeniela? Either way, it seemed a futile conclusion to a long life of seven hundred years. That thought left a bad taste in his mouth, one that had nothing to do with what he had eaten. He felt unsettled because he could not understand the man, no, the Vampire known as the Blood Demon at all. It was a strange feeling to have about an enemy.

The Blood Demon... was strong. Setting aside the fact that he was a Vampire, he had also been an outstanding martial artist.

Lee Sungmin forced the thoughts from his mind. He did not understand, nor did he want to. He had to think about what came next. The Blood Demon's power had not returned to Jeniela. Now that he had personally stepped up and cut off one of her limbs, he had to be prepared for her retaliation.

*I don't even know what Jeniela is trying to do in the first place.*

If she left Trabia to feed the Spirit King of Flame to Aine, where would she go next? Would she return now that her business was done? Or would she move on to have Aine devour another Spirit King? He could not let Aine's power grow. If the Spear King, Lloyd, Scarlet, and Baek Sogo succeeded in reverse-summoning the Spirit Kings who had not yet Advented, they could avoid the worst-case scenario.

*At the very least, it would be good if I could figure out where she's going.*

He had already failed to locate Jeniela using Erebrisa's information network. Her whereabouts had been a mystery since her last appearance at Femud Volcano. Could she have returned to Trabia? That would be a relief, but it seemed unlikely that Jeniela, having finally left her mansion, would quietly go back. She was clearly up to something.

For now, he could only watch the situation. He had finished his business here, so he would return to the Fairy Forest and then... visit the Sleeping Forest. The Spirit Queen had not Advented yet, but he felt it was necessary to check on her.

Lee Sungmin, the Black Dragon Knight, and Yana headed for the main gate of the Blood Alliance. Taking the lead, Lee Sungmin pushed it open.

A large crowd was standing outside, which was to be expected. Most of the Blood Alliance's pavilions had collapsed amid world-ending roars that had erupted without pause. The clash of weapons, the explosion of magic, the screams. All those sounds had echoed unrestrained, so it was only natural for people to gather.

"The mask..." someone muttered.

Lee Sungmin's gaze swept over the crowd. Among the commoners who knew no martial arts, he could see people who had suppressed the traces of their practiced skills to the absolute limit. Their appearances were neat, but a few beggars from the Beggars' Gang were mixed in as well. He wondered if the ones whose eyes darted about, trying to grasp the situation, were from the Rogues' Guild, the Beggars' Gang, or another information guild. It did not matter which.

"The Blood Demon is dead," he declared, his calm voice silencing the murmurs. "It was for personal, trivial reasons."

He did not say the embarrassing words, 'For the honor of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance'. Most of the people gathered here, however, understood what those 'personal reasons' were. The Phantom Spear was wearing a mask. Why else would he wear one to invade the Blood Alliance and kill the Blood Demon? The mask was the symbol of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who had died ten years ago.

"Quite a few have died, but many are still alive," Lee Sungmin continued. "If you have family or acquaintances inside, you may go in and check. I won't entertain anyone who comes at me for revenge, though."

Revenge? No one harbored such murderous thoughts. These were monsters who had invaded the Blood Alliance with only three people and emerged victorious. The Phantom Spear in particular was the legitimate successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the one who had dismantled the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, and the being who had eliminated Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun and Death Knight Lord Volander. Today, another story was added to the tales surrounding him.

[Young Master.]

Lee Sungmin, who had been about to leave, froze. He traced the source of the Thought Transmission, his eyes meeting those of a familiar woman in the crowd. His shoulders trembled slightly as he recognized her face. Ten years had passed and she had changed a little, but not so much that he could not recognize her.

Seeing his surprised expression, Yehwa gave a faint smile and bowed her head slightly.

[...Have you been well?]

[I have thought a lot, and I wandered for a time... but I have lived according to the Coalition Leader's last will.]

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's last will was for his personal guards, including Yehwa, to leave the murim and find their own happiness. Do not seek revenge. Do not get involved in the affairs of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. For ten years, Yehwa had lived that way. Lee Sungmin did not press her about the wandering she mentioned. The image of her clutching the Chairman's corpse and sobbing flashed in his mind.

[...That's a relief.]

[Do you still hold the Coalition Leader in your heart?]

[He was someone I could never forget, even if I tried. He... would surely deny it, but to me and the other guards, he was like a father.]

That was why they could not ignore his last will.

[In the end, everyone followed the Coalition Leader's will.]

Do not make any extreme choices. Do not do anything I would hate. With your abilities, it would be suicide, so do not attempt revenge. Do not have foolish thoughts like serving me in the afterlife. Live. All of the personal guards must live. It is fine to leave the murim or to remain, but since you have been given a life to live, live long and live well. Do not involve yourselves in the affairs of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance any longer.

[We... are doing well.]

*What about me?*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had left a clear will for his personal guards, but he had not done the same for Lee Sungmin. Among his last words, the only one that tried to compel his actions was the command to eat his corpse. For everything else, the Chairman had told Lee Sungmin to choose for himself.

Do as you wish. Do as you wish, and as long as you don't regret it, that's enough.

The Chairman had said that at the end of his will. *What about me?* Lee Sungmin thought once more. *Did I... do as I wished? Regrets?* He did not know. His current reality was too damn screwed up to even consider such things. Didn't he have to do something about the damn The End before he could even think about doing as he wished?

[Young Master... have you been well?]

[I don't know,] Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile. [I wonder if I truly am doing well.] The spear on his back felt heavy, and the mask on his face felt heavier. [...I'm trying... to be well.]

[I pray... that you achieve all that you desire, Young Master.]

Yehwa sent the Thought Transmission as she bowed her head deeply. Lee Sungmin offered a faint smile and turned away. He saw the wide-open gate of the Blood Alliance and its collapsed buildings, then looked no more.

"What about my present?"

He heard the words the moment he dismounted his Fairy Horse, having returned to the Fairy Forest with a complicated mix of feelings. Lee Sungmin froze mid-step and looked down at Teresa. With the Black Dog Beating Stick hanging from her waist, she puffed out her cheeks sullenly.

"You forgot, didn't you?"

"...Of course not," Lee Sungmin replied in a calm voice, reaching into his Subspace Pocket. He took out a jewel-encrusted tiara that could be used as an accessory.

Teresa accepted the tiara studded with colorful gems, but she continued to pout. "You didn't buy this in Haras, did you?"

"That city doesn't have any specialty products. It's because the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance... no, the Blood Alliance rules it. Or what, should I have bought you a demon-slaying sword used by unorthodox martial artists? Or a robe worn by Black Mages?"

"Forget it. You want me to carry a dog-beating stick and a demon-slaying sword? And wear a Black Mage's robe? What do you take me for?"

"That's why I bought an accessory that would suit you well, Teresa. What do you think?"

"Well... considering your sense of style when you bought me a dog-beating stick, you've made great strides."

*What's wrong with the Dog Beating Stick?* Lee Sungmin wanted to argue, but he quietly shut his mouth.

From behind him, the Black Dragon Knight stealthily stepped forward. He slowly reached into his robes and pulled out a necklace that did not suit him at all.

"...Ahem."

As the Black Dragon Knight silently handed it to her, Teresa's eyes widened. Lee Sungmin was just as surprised.

[When did you buy that?]

[When you ran ahead.]

[You bought it in that short time?]

[I just... saw it on the street,] the Black Dragon Knight answered, his voice trailing off.

Teresa, who had been blinking at the necklace, broke into a bright smile. "Thank you."

At her words, a muscle in the Black Dragon Knight's cheek twitched. Lee Sungmin shot a sideways glance at him and snapped.

[Consider the age difference.]

[Hey, don't get the wrong idea.]

[Wrong idea? ...Take a look in the mirror before you say that.]

The Black Dragon Knight lowered his eyes under the sharp glare.

"Is there nothing for me?" Rubia asked, watching Teresa put on the tiara and necklace.

Lee Sungmin quietly took a jeweled bracelet from his Subspace Pocket and handed it to her. As she took it, Rubia glanced at the Black Dragon Knight.

"I have nothing," the knight replied with a confident expression.

Rubia clicked her tongue and linked arms with Teresa. The subtle, pressuring gazes made the Black Dragon Knight's cheek twitch again. He reached into his own Subspace Pocket and, after rummaging for a while, pulled out an old book.

"...This is a technique called the Immeasurable Deity Grand Art..."

"I don't need it," Rubia cut him off, looking disgusted.
