The Crazy Heavenly Demon, having ridden a train for the first time, was utterly captivated. So when morning came and Lee Sungmin announced his plans to visit Central Plaza, the old demon declared he would simply ride the train again. For today, Rubia decided to abandon her usual post at Lee Sungmin's side and accompany the demon instead.

"You have a good time," Rubia said with a teasing giggle. Without waiting for a reply, she dragged the Crazy Heavenly Demon out of the inn.

[Are you trembling right now?]

Rubia was not the only one enjoying his discomfort. The Crazy Heavenly Demon might not have known the full story, but Rubia and the Void Monarch certainly remembered the day Lee Sungmin first met Wiji Hoyeon in the Dungeon. Lee Sungmin chose to ignore the Void Monarch's taunt, though it was true that he was trembling. He had felt perfectly at ease until he fell asleep last night, but now, as the hour of his meeting with Wiji Hoyeon drew near, his heart refused to listen to reason and began to pound.

[You're like a little kid in his first love!]

"It's not like that."

Unable to bear the Void Monarch's words any longer, Lee Sungmin balled his fist and struck the Magic Armor.

"......"

The armor vibrated with the sharp sound, but the Void Monarch only laughed heartily, completely undeterred.

[That girl was an object of your admiration. It's always like that. Admiration between a man and a woman? It turns so easily into affection. Isn't that what's happening with you?]

"No."

[Then what is it? Simple admiration? Your heart is beating like it's trying to escape your chest. Is that from admiration too?]

"Just shut up."

[Your reaction is cute. Very cute.]

Finally having reached his limit, Lee Sungmin tore off the Magic Armor. He marched without hesitation toward the stables, and as he prepared to hurl the armor into a large pile of horse manure, the Void Monarch screamed in horror.

[Sorry, I'm sorry! I was wrong. I won't do it again, so don't!]

"If you spout that bullshit one more time," Lee Sungmin warned, "I'll leave you buried in horse manure for three days."

[You crazy bastard, what do you gain from doing something like that? You're the one who has to wear this armor anyway!]

"Smelling horse manure is better than listening to your bullshit."

[Fine, I get it! I'll shut up, so don't do it!]

Only after the Void Monarch begged and pleaded did Lee Sungmin put the Magic Armor back on. It seemed that even for a great yokai who reigned like a nightmare in the South, being buried in horse manure was a fate to be avoided.

He checked the time. Their promise from ten years ago was for noon at the fountain. It was not yet ten in the morning. From the inn, it would take an hour to reach the plaza's fountain even if he walked at a leisurely martial pace. It was early, but Lee Sungmin left the inn anyway.

He did not hurry, instead beginning a slow walk toward the plaza. Ruvus in March was deep in spring. The wind was not cold and the sunlight was not hot. It was a perfect day for a walk. Flowers bloomed in abundance along the path, and merchants were selling them here and there.

*Should I buy some flowers?*

The thought crossed his mind for a fleeting moment, and Lee Sungmin let out a disbelieving laugh. Flowers? What for? Even as he shook his head at the ridiculous idea, his eyes lingered on the bouquets displayed at a flower shop. He forced himself to look away, turning his head completely.

His feelings for Wiji Hoyeon were admiration and friendship. It had been that way for ten years, and it would be the same now. He wished for nothing more. There was no reason for it. The jokes Wiji Hoyeon had made before he left, the words about the Breast Enlargement Pill when they met a year ago, they all lingered in his mind, but they were just lighthearted jests between friends.

*Is that really so?*

The question that surfaced was not from the Void Monarch. *I must be crazy.* Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh and pressed his temples. The absurd dream he had last night came rushing back, a dream of meeting Wiji Hoyeon, and it was not just a dream about meeting her.

"How foolish."

Lee Sungmin slapped his own cheek. Come to think of it, he had held many women in his past life. Not met, but held. There had been plenty of prostitutes targeting the meager earnings of low-rank mercenaries. Compared to that life, the current Lee Sungmin was living a very wholesome existence. He rarely drank and had not held a woman even once. He did not particularly feel the desire to. If he wanted to find a woman, he could find one anytime.

He did not. He had neither the thought nor the desire.

He had walked without rushing, yet the plaza seemed to arrive too soon. It was bustling with people. There were stalls everywhere, children running about, and lovers walking hand in hand. Elderly people basked leisurely in the sun. They were all people enjoying a peaceful day, and not a single one of them wore martial arts attire or armor. Only Lee Sungmin was clad in armor.

*Should I have worn better clothes?*

The thought occurred to him suddenly. *No, clothes aren't what's important.* A small laugh escaped him. *I'm laughing a lot today,* he thought. The laughter just kept coming, unbidden. His steps felt lighter than when he performed movement arts, and his heart, feigning composure, was actually racing ahead in a great hurry. Perhaps Wiji Hoyeon was already here. Lee Sungmin's pace quickened.

*What should I talk about when we meet?* A greeting first. Then, should he ask how she had been? Their meeting a year ago had been so sudden that they had not been able to talk much.

*Make me your goal.*

Those had been her words. In response, Lee Sungmin had spat out in defiance that when they met in ten years, he would be strong enough to pierce her chest. Wiji Hoyeon had burst out laughing at that, saying friends should not do such things to each other.

Right. Friends. For ten years, Lee Sungmin had chased her back. Wiji Hoyeon's existence was so distant and so grand that even after running nonstop for a decade, he still lacked confidence, but that was fine. The farther away she was, the more he could run to get closer. He had not been able to properly check a year ago, but today he would. He would see where he stood, and where Wiji Hoyeon stood. Had they become equals? He did not hope for it. That was greed.

*Have I become someone you wouldn't be ashamed of?*

This, however, was something he hoped for, something he was greedy for. He wanted to be acknowledged. He wanted to be praised. He wanted to hear words of encouragement. Wiji Hoyeon had always spoken frankly, telling him he had no talent, that he was stupid. While those words had hurt him deep down, he had struggled desperately to be acknowledged. If that Wiji Hoyeon were to acknowledge him now.

*Would that be the end of it?*

A transparent stream of water shot up from the fountain, its high arch creating a rainbow in the sunlight. He did not know if it was magic or dye, but each stream of water was a different color. It was beautiful. Many people were resting around the large fountain. The time? Lee Sungmin turned his head, searching for a clock. He spotted one on a tall pillar. It was now eleven o'clock. Only eleven. There was still an hour left. Lee Sungmin sat on a bench near the fountain.

He closed his eyes, enjoying the trembling of his pounding heart. One more hour. There was no way Wiji Hoyeon would break her promise. When they met a year ago, had they not both looked forward to this day? She would come. He had no doubt.

The chatter of the crowd changed its tune several times. The fountain spouted its colorful water again and again. Each time, Lee Sungmin turned his head to check the clock. When it finally struck twelve, Lee Sungmin stood up from the bench. He circled the fountain, thinking Wiji Hoyeon might be somewhere else in the large area, but she was not there. Not there. She could be a little late. He himself had only just arrived yesterday. Lee Sungmin walked around the fountain without rest, glaring at the clock.

It became one o'clock, then two. *...Did I get the date wrong?* He stopped a passerby to ask. "What day is it today?"

"It's March 14th."

He had not mistaken it. *Is she just running late? If I keep waiting, she'll come.* Nevertheless, an unknown anxiety coiled in his heart. Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before calling for Nebul.

"......"

Nebul appeared. Lee Sungmin asked urgently, "I want to know where Wiji Hoyeon is."

"Would you please wait a moment?" Nebul bowed his head and vanished.

Lee Sungmin waited anxiously for his return. Less than ten minutes later, Nebul's voice echoed in his mind.

[If you mean the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon, she is in Ruvus right now.]

"...Yes? Wiji Hoyeon is in Ruvus? Where is she exactly?"

[Well... she is currently clashing with the Iron Armored Divine Spear at the South gate.]

"What did you say?"

Hearing those words, Lee Sungmin shot up from his seat. The Iron Armored Divine Spear, Yoo Hojeong. The very man he had met at the inn just last night. Why was he clashing with Wiji Hoyeon?

[The situation is strange. With the Little Heavenly Demon's martial prowess, she should be able to handle someone like the Iron Armored Divine Spear with ease... but the Iron Armored Divine Spear is pushing the Little Heavenly Demon back.]

Impossible. Lee Sungmin knew Yoo Hojeong was a famous spear master, but he was no match for Wiji Hoyeon. Why on earth was she having trouble with him? No, this was not the time to think about that. Without a second thought, Lee Sungmin unleashed his movement arts and dashed toward the South gate.

"No movement arts in the streets!" a patrolling guard shouted after him.

Lee Sungmin did not listen. It was a long way from Central Plaza to the South gate, and the densely packed buildings and crowded streets prevented him from running at full speed. As he continued to use his movement arts without stopping, the guards began to blow their whistles.

[Go to the railroad.]

The Void Monarch, silent since the earlier threat, finally spoke.

[It'll be faster for you to run than to take the train. If you run on the open railroad tracks, you should be able to get to the South gate quickly.]

The advice was sound. Lee Sungmin ran to a nearby train station, vaulted over the fence, and landed on the tracks. A stationary train blared its loud horn, but he ignored it. The shrill sound of whistles grew louder in the distance, a warning from the guards. He ignored that too. Even at this very moment, Wiji Hoyeon was in trouble. Unless something was terribly wrong with her, there was no reason she would struggle against someone like Yoo Hojeong.

So he had to go. Lee Sungmin drew upon his inner qi to their fullest and executed his movement arts to the extreme. The sounds of the horn and whistles faded behind him. He ran like a madman along the railroad tracks, a blur of motion heading south.

It did not take long for him to arrive at South Gate Station. It was only possible because he used his movement arts without sparing any inner qi, and because Dancing Shadow Soul Break was an exceptionally outstanding technique. Without even pausing to catch his breath, Lee Sungmin leaped over the fence again.

The gate was visible not far ahead, a large crowd gathered nearby. *That's the place.* Lee Sungmin's eyes flashed as he ran toward it.

"You're not as great as the rumors say!" Yoo Hojeong shouted with a hearty laugh.

As if to symbolize his title, Iron Armored Divine Spear, he was covered from head to toe in heavy, thick iron armor, including a helmet. The spear he held was a large, gleaming black lance.

Wiji Hoyeon stood before him, her face a pale mask of pain. She could barely remain upright, one hand braced on her knee as her legs trembled, threatening to give way at any moment. A trickle of dark blood flowed from her tightly clenched lips. The curse she had received from the Fist Honored One was preventing her from unleashing her true martial prowess. Every time she tried to draw upon her inner qi, her ki and blood would twist, and the gathered energy would scatter. In such a state, even if she was a Chojeol master, overwhelming Yoo Hojeong was impossible. It was only because she was Wiji Hoyeon that she could endure against him at all.

"...I don't understand why you're attacking me. I haven't caused any trouble."

"If a tiger is wounded, you must hunt it," Yoo Hojeong replied. He knew she was not in a normal condition. That was precisely why he had picked this fight. Even if Wiji Hoyeon had caused no trouble in the city, the name of the Little Heavenly Demon was heavily guarded against within the Murim Alliance.

She must be killed when the opportunity arises. He had not received permission from the Murim Alliance for this, but he had already sent a carrier pigeon to them. Their meeting had been a coincidence in the first place. He had come to the South gate to return to Kron and had just happened to run into her here.

"That's not very righteous," Wiji Hoyeon said, a faint smile touching her pale face.

At her words, Yoo Hojeong's eyes flashed from behind his faceguard. "Don't you, a member of the unorthodox faction, speak of righteousness."

"I'm being sarcastic."

"Do I look like a fool who can't even understand that?" Yoo Hojeong lifted his foot and stomped on the ground.

"......"

A great tremor shook the earth. Unable to withstand it, Wiji Hoyeon collapsed to her knees. She glared up at him, breathing heavily.

"I know my actions aren't justified. I know they aren't righteous," Yoo Hojeong said, striding toward her. "However... if it's the life of the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon, it's worth being narrow-minded and cowardly to take it."

"...What time is it?" Wiji Hoyeon asked.

Yoo Hojeong's eyes widened at the sudden question. "What?"

"It's past noon, right?"

"What kind of stupid question is that... Noon passed a while ago."

"Is that so?" Wiji Hoyeon wiped the blood trickling down her lips and chuckled. "Looks like I broke a promise."

Yoo Hojeong did not try to understand her lamenting words.
