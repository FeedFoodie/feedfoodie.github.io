Light engulfed everything in a silent, blinding explosion.

Swept away by an irresistible wave of brilliance, Lee Sungmin desperately searched for the others. Scarlet, Baek Sogo, Yana. *Are they safe? How are they?, but the light was so intense it bleached the world white, making it impossible to see even his own hands. Even his heightened senses, which could feel what he could not see, failed him. He could grasp nothing of their presence.

[Are you okay?]

Only the Void Monarch's voice cut through the void. His eyes could not see and his senses were closed off, but other than that, nothing was wrong. Lee Sungmin flailed his arms as if treading water, trying to move forward.

He remembered Scarlet's warning about a sudden spatial erosion. She had called it dangerous, but was this really it? The light flashed past him. Though he stood still, an inexplicable sense of acceleration pulled at him, a strange but not entirely threatening sensation.

And then, something materialized within the flashing light.

Time itself seemed to stutter, moving fast then slow, until at some point, it simply stopped. Lee Sungmin stood blankly within the frozen tableau, unable to tell if the scenery before him was past, present, or future.

His eyes glazed over as he saw her. Standing beyond the frozen storm of light was a face he could never forget.

She had barely changed, yet she was different. Her hair had grown longer, and she seemed a little thinner. He noticed her face was a bit more gaunt than it had been ten years ago, though she had always been slender. Her expression was a complex mask of fatigue, weariness, irritation, and anger, all directed at someone or something he could not see.

"Wiji…"

Hoyeon.

He suppressed his shock, his voice a desperate whisper. Did it reach her? Beyond the storm of light, Wiji Hoyeon's head turned. Her eyes merely widened, but that simple act transformed her entire impression.

Their eyes met. She opened her mouth to speak.

And the frozen scenery began to move. The landscape flashed by in an instant, a blur of flames, wind, and water.

*What was that?* He ran forward recklessly, trying to trash skills the image of Wiji Hoyeon that had just vanished, but she was too far. No, his body was barely moving, the space that had flashed by too vast to cross even at his speed.

Was it just an illusion? Why now?

Something else appeared beyond the storm of light.

A pair of perked-up cat ears.

Without a single thought, Lee Sungmin lunged and grabbed it.

KWADANGTANG!

The storm vanished, and his forward momentum sent him tumbling across the ground. Fearing he might hurt what he held, he pulled it tight into his arms and activated his Protective Qi Aura. The impact, however, was not as strong as he expected. Instead, the cold shock of a lake enveloped him.

PUHWAAK!

Sucked underwater for a moment, Lee Sungmin quickly leaped out of the lake.

"Aaaaaaaaaaaaaah-!"

A loud, unending scream pierced the air. Ignoring the water dripping from his clothes, Lee Sungmin looked toward the sound. Scarlet was crouched inside a magic barrier, clutching her head. Not far from her, Baek Sogo slowly straightened from an awkward posture, while Yana lowered the nine tails she had wrapped around her body.

Scarlet's long scream finally stopped. She looked around, cleared her throat, and got up, waving a hand to dispel the magic barrier that had protected her.

"…I thought I was gonna die."

"Me too," Baek Sogo agreed.

"We should have died, actually," Scarlet continued. "A spatial erosion of that magnitude is a disaster you can't even dream of with magic, but surprisingly, nothing happened."

"Did you not see it?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Are you messing with me?" Scarlet snapped, her earlobes turning red. "You saw me screaming with my eyes shut, and you're asking if I saw anything? You wanna die?"

That wasn't what he meant. Lee Sungmin took a subtle step back, looking to Yana and Baek Sogo. They both shook their heads when their eyes met.

"The light was too bright, I couldn't see anything," Yana said.

"It was the same for me," Baek Sogo added.

Then was it really an illusion? No, he couldn't just dismiss it. He looked down at the figure in his arms. He hadn't been wrong. The perked-up cat ears. His first thought wasn't confusion, but relief. The last words of the Crazy Heavenly Demon echoed in his mind. *Someday, if you get the chance.* He never thought 'someday' would be today.

"Lady Rubia."

The one who had fainted in his arms was indeed Rubia, the girl he had briefly seen in Germud ten years ago, the one with whom he never had a proper unorthodox. He grasped her wrist. She had only lost consciousness. When he sent his inner arts into her acupoints, her body trembled.

"…Ugh…!"

Rubia's eyes fluttered open. As her pupils filled with light, they fixed on Lee Sungmin, and her mouth fell wide open.

"Now, now. Calm down," he said, raising a hand before she could scream. Calming her down would take forever if she started with that.

He looked into her eyes and quickly explained the situation. He told her about being caught in the spatial erosion, seeing something strange in the storm of light, and then grabbing her on impulse the moment he saw her.

"Are you crazy?" Rubia blurted out, her initial shock at the sudden turn of events giving way to genuine disbelief.

"Why would you grab me in that situation? We were lucky! I could have fallen into a spatial rift and become a lost soul forever! Or my body could have just exploded and I'd be dead!"

"…I'm sorry about that. I didn't know the circumstances. Still, isn't it a relief that we survived and didn't die, one way or another?"

"You've gotten awfully shameless since I last saw you."

"People get shameless as they get older," Lee Sungmin replied.

Rubia let out a dry laugh at his response. She glared at his face for a moment before letting out a deep sigh.

"First, could you let go of my hand?"

"Ah, yes."

He released her wrist, which he was still holding. With a sullen expression, Rubia struggled out of his arms. Ten years, no, even more time had passed, yet she maintained the same youthful appearance she had when he first saw her in the Sleeping Forest. It was not that she did not age, but because she was a man-made artificial Elemental.

"I didn't even get to say it's been a while," Lee Sungmin began. "We couldn't have a proper unorthodox ten years ago, could we?"

"…Have you been well?" she asked quietly.

"I haven't been well. Back then, the mission the Spirit Queen gave me was to look after my master… Lord Envirus, but when I came to my senses, I was back in the Elemental Realm. In that moment, I realized that the master I served had passed away. Normally, I would have been annihilated by Her Majesty's wrath, but at that time, Her Majesty was in a deep sleep."

Her expression turned bitter. She had survived only because of the Queen's decade-long slumber, a fact she did not consider fortunate. Guilt gnawed at her for losing consciousness at the most crucial moment, for failing to prevent her master's murder.

"I thought I'd be punished when Her Majesty woke up, but… before I could be punished, someone invaded the Elemental Realm."

"…Yes?"

Everyone listened intently to her words. Rubia's gaze kept flicking to Baek Sogo, a flicker of recognition from their time in the dungeon long ago.

"It's someone you know well," she said, then added in a small voice, "Though I don't know if you can call her a person."

"About half a month ago, a human… entered the Elemental Realm. The Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon. I don't know how she got into the Elemental Realm. Probably, not even the other Elementals of the Elemental Realm, or even Her Majesty, would know how she got in. Because it's normally Buddhist for a human to enter the Elemental Realm."

"…After that, what happened?"

"That… Wiji Hoyeon, she attacked Her Majesty without any warning."

Rubia seemed at a loss for how to explain what she had seen. It was the first time she had witnessed a battle between beings of such scale and caliber. The Elemental Realm was the Queen's domain. At a single gesture, the highest-ranking Elementals, beings of terrifying power, would move to her command, and yet, even while fighting on her own ground, the Queen could not overwhelm Wiji Hoyeon. The highest-ranking Elementals were annihilated, caught in the mere fragments of power that flew from the clash between the two.

"The fight continued for half a month without stopping."

That was why she said Wiji Hoyeon did not seem human. It was natural for the Queen's power to be inexhaustible in her own realm, but Wiji Hoyeon also showed no signs of fatigue or power depletion during the two-week battle.

"The continuous battle went on to the point where it was about to destroy the Elemental Realm itself. At that moment, to prevent the Elemental Realm from being completely destroyed, Her Majesty hastily made the Elemental Realm erode into this world. It was something that shouldn't have been done… but Her Majesty didn't care."

"There were many Elementals on this mountain," Scarlet, who had been listening, muttered.

"It seems this mountain was a spot with a strong connection to the Elemental Realm. We couldn't have worse luck. Why did this have to happen while we were here of all times?"

"If the Elemental Realm eroded into this world, what happened to the Elementals and the Queen who were in the Elemental Realm?"

"…It was an erosion that was carried out without enough preparation and while Her Majesty was exhausted. In fact, if Wiji Hoyeon, if she hadn't intervened, Her Majesty would have succeeded in the erosion without any issue after all preparations were complete."

"Then what would have happened?"

"The end," Rubia answered, her voice listless.

"In many ways. Just as the mage over there said, there are several other spots in Eria with strong Elemental energy, just like this mountain. The erosion, starting from those places, would have first completely obliterated the surrounding areas, and then the powerful Elementals of the Elemental Realm and Her Majesty would have Advented into this world with their full power intact, and then, Her Majesty would have done what she intended to do."

The End.

After hearing Rubia's story, Lee Sungmin fell silent. An incomplete erosion. He could not know to what extent the Spirit Queen's wish had been fulfilled. However, Lee Sungmin could not help but think that even if the martial arts theory was incomplete, the Spirit Queen herself had Advented in Eria. The reason was simple. In the current world, the Spirit Queen was the prophesied End. From the moment Envirus was killed by his younger brother, Abel, the Spirit Queen was fated to Advent as The End and destroy this world.

"…Besides this mountain, do you know of any other spots with strong Elemental power?"

"…I don't know," Rubia replied, her voice gloomy. "I'm… honestly confused. Her Majesty is trying to destroy this world… but my master wanted to save it. I…"

"Lady Rubia, what is it that you want?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking straight at her. "I also know that Envirus wanted to save this world. Although he died like that, his desire to save the world must have been sincere. The Fairy Queen is trying to hold this world responsible for Envirus's death."

Rubia said nothing.

"…Where did Grandpa the Crazy Heavenly Demon go?" she asked after a brief silence.

At her words, Lee Sungmin's expression hardened. After a moment's hesitation, he sighed and answered.

"…Back then, in Urmuri… he passed away."

"......"

Rubia's mouth hung open. As she stood there blankly, her two ears drooped.

"The Crazy Heavenly Demon, before he died, left me with these last words. Someday… he asked me to check if Lady Rubia was doing well."

"I… was… doing… well," she stammered.

"In the future, too?"

Rubia lifted her eyes. Perhaps because of the fighting spirit memories, her eyes were moist with tears.

"In the future, the Spirit Queen will destroy this world. I plan to stop her with all my might. In doing so, I might have to fight you too, Lady Rubia. That is, if you side with the Spirit Queen."

"…I'm… not that strong."

"Then you will die by my hand."

Rubia finally let her tears fall.

"Why did it turn out like this?" she sobbed. "Grandpa the Crazy Heavenly Demon died, and my Master died too. Why are these things happening to me?"

"It's because some damn bastard is letting this world go to hell," Lee Sungmin spat, his voice thick with killing intent. "My goal is to beat that damn bastard to death, and now, I'll have to kill the Spirit Queen. I'll be honest. Lady Rubia, it would be great if you helped me, but I understand you have your own position. You're an Elemental, after all. If you're loyal to the Queen, you'll side with that mad bitch who wants to destroy this world. If I ever stand before the Spirit Queen."

He reached out and wiped away Rubia's tears.

"Run away. Then there will be no reason for me to kill you."

Lee Sungmin turned around as he said this. Apart from finding the spots, he had something else in mind. To do that, he first had to leave this place.

"…Cheneron Great Lake. Dahar Ruins. Femud Volcano," Rubia muttered. "And the Sleeping Forest and… this place, the Yuzukia Mountains. These five places are the spots with strong Elemental power. Her Majesty's presence… I can't feel it yet. If Her Majesty had Advented in this world, I would have definitely known."

"Are you saying the Advent failed?"

"No. It's not that…"

Rubia's words trailed off.

[You talk too much.]

A booming voice vibrated through the space. A great wind swept through the area.

[Do you intend to betray Your Majesty?]

Amidst the raging wind, a giant man rose to his feet.
