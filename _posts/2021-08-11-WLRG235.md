"I've heard the story," the man said. "You're a friend of the Crimson Archmage, aren't you?"

"That's right."

Chwi Geol was surprisingly cheerful for a man whose left arm had been severed. Several years had passed since he lost it, so he must have grown quite accustomed to being one-armed. He extended his right hand, offering Lee Sungmin a handshake.

"I look forward to working with you," he said with a grin. "We haven't departed for the subjugation yet, so I hope we can get to know each other well before then."

"I look forward to it as well."

Since Chwi Geol showed no hostility, Lee Sungmin returned his warm smile and shook his hand. After the handshake, Chwi Geol moved past him and headed downstairs. Lee Sungmin watched his back for a moment as he descended before turning to go up to his own room.

The room was not very spacious but it was clean. He had no luggage to unpack, so Lee Sungmin simply stood by the window and looked down at the grounds below. A wide backyard was clearly visible from his vantage point. In the yard, men dressed in the martial uniforms of the Moyong Clan swung their swords in perfect unison.

*So it's a joint attack formation.*

Come to think of it, he had never faced a proper sword formation. He had heard warnings about them a few times before, cautionary tales that even a master at the Transcendent Realm would struggle against a superior formation. Lee Sungmin watched them for a while longer before turning from the window and lying down on the bed.

Even if his identity were exposed, he had no intention of clashing with the Moyong Clan. The most ideal scenario was for everything to end without his identity ever being revealed, but if he were discovered, he planned to withdraw without a confrontation. Of course, he was not overconfident that things would go exactly as planned.

Outside, a fierce blizzard caused the window to rattle. Lee Sungmin sat in a meditative posture, letting time pass amid the noise.

Around sunset, a knock came at his door. He opened his eyes and rose from his seated position. Beyond the open door stood a boy with a youthful face that Lee Sungmin found somehow familiar.

"Who are you?"

"I'm Moyong Chan."

The youngest son of the Moyong Clan's Patriarch. Lee Sungmin nodded slightly. "What is it?"

"It's about time for dinner," the boy explained. "I came to ask if you've eaten yet."

"I haven't yet...?"

"The Patriarch asked if you would join him."

Lee Sungmin tilted his head at the invitation. A request to share a meal was surely a sign of goodwill. To be invited on their very first meeting... did it mean the Patriarch wanted to build a closer relationship? He was not sure how to take it. Lee Sungmin stared at Moyong Chan's face, noting that the man had sent his own treasured son in person. After a moment of thought, he nodded. It would be rude to refuse such a blatant show of favor.

"I understand."

He had nothing to prepare, so Lee Sungmin simply closed the door and followed. Although Moyong Chan had not yet reached the Supreme Peak, his innate talent was outstanding. As the young master of the Moyong Clan, he would receive their full support, and it felt certain that he would enter the Supreme Peak in his twenties.

*Moyong Seojin's younger brother.*

Lee Sungmin did not have any particular impression of Moyong Seojin, only that Namgung Heewon had been in love with him and that he had been falsely accused. That was all. Moyong Chan greatly resembled the face of Moyong Seojin from his memories. The Moyong Clan's Patriarch, Moyong Daeun, had only two children, Moyong Seojin and Moyong Chan. Now that Moyong Seojin was dead, only Moyong Chan remained. Did bringing his only son on a potentially dangerous subjugation mission mean that the Moyong Clan did not view it as very dangerous?

Moyong Chan led him to a restaurant not far from the inn. After entering, he guided Lee Sungmin up the stairs.

"Did you not find it rude?" Moyong Daeun asked as he rose from his seat across the table.

Lee Sungmin shook his head. "I didn't think that at all."

"That's a relief, then," Moyong Daeun said with a benevolent smile. "Hedor. It's been a while since I've come to this city, but I always eat at this restaurant whenever I'm here. The food is quite good. My daughter was also quite fond of the food here."

"...That was a tragedy."

"It's alright. It was several years ago," Moyong Daeun said, shaking his head. Despite his words, he was still dressed in black clothes that resembled mourning attire.

Moyong Chan sat next to his father, and Lee Sungmin took the seat opposite them as Moyong Daeun suggested.

"Thank you for the invitation."

"It's hardly an invitation," Moyong Daeun replied with a hearty laugh. "I simply called for you because I needed someone to talk to."

Lee Sungmin did not think he meant it literally. If the Patriarch needed a conversation partner, there were plenty of others besides him. He looked around and saw warriors of the Moyong Clan eating at other tables, but Chwi Geol and the other warriors from the Murim Alliance were nowhere to be seen.

*What does he want?*

The food arrived, not extravagant but neat and well-prepared. The meal began. Although he had claimed to need someone to talk to, Moyong Daeun did not speak to Lee Sungmin as they ate.

"Well now," Moyong Daeun said in the middle of the meal, letting out a dry laugh. "Come to think of it, I haven't even asked your name."

"It's Lee Mincheol," Lee Sungmin replied, giving the alias he had prepared.

At his words, Moyong Daeun fell silent for a moment, seeming to search his memory for the name Lee Mincheol. "I've never heard that name before."

"That's because I haven't done anything worth spreading rumors about."

"A master of your skill level couldn't possibly avoid having rumors spread about him, could he?"

"My innate talent isn't that great, so I've spent most of my time training. During that time, I happened to meet Scarlet and form a connection with her."

"Not that great of a talent... To be honest, I find that hard to believe. How old are you?"

"I'm twenty-seven now."

"To become a Supreme Peak master at that age means you're by no means lacking in talent," Moyong Daeun chuckled. "Aren't there countless people who train their whole lives and never reach the Supreme Peak? I'll be honest. I'm very interested in you, and I'm also greedy for you."

"Greedy... you say?"

"So much so that if I had... another daughter, I would have had her marry you."

Lee Sungmin wondered what kind of expression he should make at those words.

"But unfortunately, I have no daughter. Only one son is left."

"...Um. I'm not thinking about marriage yet." *Wiji Hoyeon would try to kill me.*

"It just goes to show what a desirable talent you are," Moyong Daeun continued. "The fact that you don't belong to any sect makes you even more coveted. While there are quite a few Supreme Peak masters in this world, it's not a level to be ignored, is it? Especially since you're still in your twenties, your potential is limitless."

"Thank you for your high praise."

"I'm just stating the facts. That's why I'm so greedy. Have you any thought of becoming a guest elder for the Moyong Clan?" Moyong Daeun asked suggestively.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before shaking his head. "I have no intention of settling down anywhere just yet."

"A pity... truly a pity. If you ever change your mind, please visit the Moyong Clan. We would give you the best possible treatment," Moyong Daeun said with sincerity. "Or, even if they're not of my bloodline, there are many girls with the Moyong name within the clan. Many of them are quite beautiful."

"That's alright."

"Haha! Isn't that a bit of a sharp refusal? Still, at least let me say it. Who knows, there might be a girl among the Moyong Clan's children who could capture your heart."

Since it seemed Moyong Daeun would not back down, Lee Sungmin took a step back. "...If I have the chance later, I will visit the Moyong Clan."

At that, Moyong Daeun smiled cheerfully.

After finishing the meal, Lee Sungmin left the restaurant with Moyong Chan. He had said there was no need for an escort since the inn was right across the street, but Moyong Daeun had insisted on sending his son to see him off. It was likely his way of overtly expressing his goodwill and desire for friendship.

"You don't have to think too deeply about the Patriarch's words," Moyong Chan said as they walked.

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly and nodded. "He enjoys mischievous jokes, I see."

"I don't think he was joking... but the Patriarch has changed a lot since my sister's death. He always wears black clothes and forces himself to smile." Moyong Chan wore a bitter expression, one that did not quite suit his age of fifteen. He was a father before a patriarch.

"Young Master Moyong, why did you come here?" Lee Sungmin asked, voicing the question he had been inwardly curious about.

"Pardon?" Moyong Chan's eyes widened as he looked at Lee Sungmin.

"This subjugation mission. I heard it might be quite dangerous..."

"I have the martial arts to protect myself," Moyong Chan replied with a serious expression, seeming to have misunderstood. Although he was mature for his age, the way he abruptly asserted his pride made him seem every bit a fifteen-year-old.

Lee Sungmin shook his head. "I'm not saying your skills are lacking, Young Master Moyong. However, it could be dangerous, couldn't it? There was also the tragedy with Moyong Seojin and Young Master Jaegal."

"...But it's not like the Phantom Spear is here, is it? If the Phantom Spear were to appear before me, I would kill him."

[As if you could,] the Void Monarch sneered.

"Besides," Moyong Chan added, "this subjugation mission won't be that dangerous. With so many people gathered, we should be able to kill a single Black Mage without much difficulty."

"...Is that so?" Lee Sungmin nodded slightly. They were underestimating Kim Jonghyun. Then again, it was understandable. The forces gathered for the subjugation numbered five, the Mage Army, the Murim Alliance, the Moyong Clan, the Church, and the mercenaries. The number of people gathered in this town to deal with Kim Jonghyun's trash skills already reached the hundreds.

The distance from the restaurant to the inn was short, an insignificant distance, but before they reached it, Lee Sungmin stopped walking. Moyong Chan looked at him with a puzzled expression. Without a word, Lee Sungmin stared into a dark alley.

"What's wrong?" Moyong Chan asked, tilting his head.

Still staring into the alley, Lee Sungmin spoke. "We're not having a particularly important conversation. Why are you eavesdropping?"

From the alley came the sound of a dry cough, a woman's voice. The person who walked out of the darkness was Tang Ahee, dressed in tight-fitting black clothes. The Poiso, butterfly, daughter of the Dang Clan Head. Moyong Chan looked at her with a surprised expression. With his level of skill, he had not been able to detect her hiding there.

"I was just out for a night walk," she said.

"You have a strange way of taking night walks. Why don't you just say you were practicing your concealment technique?"

"I was practicing my concealment technique, too," Tang Ahee replied to his sneer with a shameless smile.

Moyong Chan's expression turned serious as he stepped forward. "Why were you eavesdropping?"

"It wasn't exactly a conversation worth eavesdropping on, was it? And what do you mean eavesdropping when you were talking loud enough for anyone to hear?"

"That's..."

"Looks like your business with the Moyong Clan is finished, right?" Tang Ahee turned her gaze from Moyong Chan to Lee Sungmin, shooting a seductive smile in his direction. "It seems you've already eaten, and to be honest, I have too. The night air is nice, so..." Snowflakes were mixed in the chilly northern wind. "How about it? A drink, perhaps. I happen to have some good liquor with me."

"Ah." Good liquor. The words reminded him of the gourd that produced Infinite Beautiful Wine, which he had collected with the Void Monarch's treasures. At the thought, the Void Monarch threw a fit.

[That's right, the gourd! You son of a bitch, why haven't you used it even once?!]

*I didn't have time to drink.*

[Liquor is something you make time to drink, you bastard!]

*Do I have to listen to you curse at me just because I didn't drink?*

[Shut up and drink that liquor tonight. Let me have a taste too!]

Lee Sungmin ignored the voice rampaging in his head and shook his head at Tang Ahee. "I don't feel like drinking."

"Oh my... I guess you don't enjoy drinking much?"

"No."

*Why is he being such a stone wall for no reason?* Tang Ahee frowned at him. "Still, how about just one drink? Since it's our first time meeting, we can get to know each other."

"I'm not fond of getting to know people over drinks."

"I'm going this far, why don't you just play along?"

"I'm sorry," Lee Sungmin refused, shaking his head again.

At his words, Tang Ahee's shoulders trembled slightly. "...Then let's have some tea."

"I'm not thirsty..."

"You're not acting like this because you don't know what I'm trying to say, are you?"

"I know," he replied. "You want to get to know me, don't you?"

"If you know, then why are you acting like this?"

"I'm not sure why you want to get to know me, Miss Tang."

"Because I'm interested."

"In me?" Lee Sungmin asked with a slight smile. "Or in the fact that I'm a master with no affiliation?"

When he asked, Tang Ahee was at a loss for words.
