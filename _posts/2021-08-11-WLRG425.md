When Lee Sungmin stepped outside, the library had reverted to Greed, though its insatiable hunger for the world was gone. He watched it scatter and vanish into the air, his hand reaching out instinctively to catch a fragment. Despite his power, the dissipating entity slipped through his fingers.

*Well. It's not like I'd have any use for it even if I got my hands on it.*

He had no desire for such greed anyway. Once he was certain the last trace of it had disappeared, he turned away.

The Sleeping Forest was now just a forest. The barrier had fallen, and with it, the ghostly cry that had once lured travelers to their madness. The souls that fueled the lament had been liberated, their torment finally over. No barrier, no Greed, and the ancient clan that had guarded this place was no more.

*Will she be waiting quietly?*

His thoughts turned to Wiji Hoyeon. He had not been able to bring her into that final space with him. There had been no time to explain or persuade her, so he had simply used Spatial Movement to send her to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion atop the Hyujael Mountains. Sending her to the Fairy Forest had been an option, but he could not risk her learning of the situation and rushing back on a Fairy Horse with the others.

A profound relief washed over him now that The End was finished, yet it was quickly followed by a minor, almost petty worry. What if Wiji Hoyeon was angry with him? The absurdity of worrying about something so trivial made him laugh. With the hope that she was waiting patiently, he activated Spatial Movement.

"You're back sooner than I thought."

SPLASH!

A cold spray of water slapped him across the face, but Lee Sungmin remained perfectly still. He could have easily dodged or blocked the splash, but he knew better than to make a pointless move that would only earn him an unnecessary beating.

"Your reaction's no fun."

Wiji Hoyeon grumbled from where she was half-submerged in a clear lake, evidently having been washing away the blood, flesh, and foul stench that clung to her.

He let out a low, dry cough, his eyes drawn to her water-slicked skin and hair. People did not normally wear clothes when they bathed, and in that, Wiji Hoyeon was no different. She was completely naked.

Wiji Hoyeon chuckled at his subtle aversion of her gaze and rose from the water. She gathered her long, wet hair and shook it out behind her.

"That reaction's fun, though."

"Shouldn't you put on some clothes?" Lee Sungmin asked, glancing at her from the corner of his eye. She had not changed much in ten years… or had she? Had her chest gotten a little bigger?

In that fleeting moment, his eyes, with the observational skill expected of a master, caught the small change in her body.

"Did you notice?"

She did not miss the flicker in his eyes, and the corners of her lips curled into a smirk. As if to show off, she leaned back slightly, pulling her shoulder blades together. With her hands on her hips, she thrust her chest forward and lifted her chin in an incredibly arrogant pose.

"I didn't take any Breast Enlargement Pill," she declared, flaunting her slightly larger chest. "It just started to grow, little by little. Might even get bigger."

"I, I see…"

It was a subtle change, unnoticeable to a lesser eye, but Wiji Hoyeon seemed extremely satisfied with even that small amount of growth.

"Well? Aren't you going to take your clothes off?" she asked, tilting her head.

Lee Sungmin could only blink stupidly at her question. "…Why would I do that?"

"Because I'm naked."

"What's that got to do with…"

"Or are you just not in the mood?" she asked, tilting her head again as she dropped the arrogant pose. With an indifferent expression, she flicked the water from her skin.

Lee Sungmin watched her with a reluctant expression and shook his head. "…No, that's not it."

"Forget it."

Her body floated upwards until she stood on the surface of the lake, where she instantly evaporated the remaining moisture from her skin. She then retrieved new clothes from her Subspace Pocket and began to dress. As he watched, a belated sense of regret washed over Lee Sungmin.

"I'm sorry."

"What are you sorry for?"

"That… I didn't take my clothes off."

"It's fine. I'm not really in the mood either. You just happened to show up right as I was finishing my bath, that's all," she replied with feigned indifference, pulling her hair free from her collar and shaking it out. Her impassive eyes then settled on him.

"Is it over?"

He simply met her gaze.

"Everything?" she pressed.

"The traces of the Demonic Spirit… they're completely gone from here."

"The Demonic Spirit wanted to die. Its only wish was to enter the cycle of reincarnation, forget everything, and be born again. So I granted it."

"Reincarnation… I see. So fate has completely changed. You didn't just stop The End, you actually changed the very structure of this world."

A faint smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips at her words. Wiji Hoyeon slowly lowered her gaze from the sky to meet his.

"It's not just the Demonic Spirit's existence that's gone. The blessing I was given… I felt it vanish."

"That's because this world isn't a Slaughterhouse anymore."

The blessing had been a gift from the Demonic Spirit, possible only because Eria was a Slaughterhouse. In that world, the administrator could use its authority to forcibly grant absurd talents and fix a being's status, but now, with the world changed and the administrator gone, such things had vanished. "Once again, you're taking my first."

"You've made me free… for the very first time."

He wondered what to say, but sighed and brought his fingers to his temple. A moment later, a fragment of his memory detached itself and floated toward her.

"What's this?"

"My memories."

"No." She cut him off, receiving the memories with both hands. "I want to see." A faint smile touched her face. "I want to feel." She gently cupped the intangible light. "It's a way for me to know the you that I don't know."

She accepted his memories without hesitation, taking in everything he had experienced since they parted. She stood perfectly still as she absorbed them. It did not take long. When she had seen it all, she let out a small laugh.

"Let's go," she said, taking his hand. "I want to meet them too."
