It's over.

It's really over. The journey that began on January 19th, 2017, has finally come to an end after a little over a year.

Honestly, I never expected to write for this long. The story grew as I wrote it, with new scenes constantly appearing that I just had to include. Because of that, I even ended up cutting scenes I had originally planned. To be perfectly honest, when I first started, I never imagined so many people would read it. At the time, I was coming off one failed novel, and since this is my full-time job, I had no choice but to write my next work, no matter what. This story was something I came up with and wrote in a hurry, so I was completely flustered by how much more attention it received than I ever expected.

I probably couldn't live up to all that interest. There must have been times when the story's development veered away from what readers hoped for, and I'm sure many of you left out of frustration with the foolish protagonist. I always want to write a story that satisfies everyone, but it seems that's a truly difficult thing to achieve.

Actually, I wanted to write even more scenes of him struggling and being desperate. As the story progressed, I considered several different endings, and I found myself heavily leaning towards a bad one.

The first ending I settled on was a loop. Seongmin would ultimately fail to stop The End, and the story would conclude with him returning to the past once more, all his memories intact, to meet Wiji Hoyeon again. That was the kind of ending I intended to write, but that would feel like being cut off in the middle of taking a dump, wouldn't it? So I scrapped that idea.

My next plan was a total annihilation ending. Everyone would resist until the very, very end, but they would eventually fall one by one until Seongmin himself died too. Of course, I scrapped that one as well. I was scared of getting cursed out by all of you, and besides, poor Seongmin deserved better. So I think it's a relief that we got a happy ending. In the end, I believe everyone found their own salvation.

As a little footnote, I should mention that Baek Sogo was originally scheduled to die. I even dropped plenty of hints pointing to her death. Her becoming a divine incarnation was itself a major hint, but at the last minute, I had a change of heart. I just couldn't kill her. Thanks to that decision, Baek Sogo's character became very ambiguous.

Well, even with that, I had fun writing it. It had been a while since I'd written a protagonist quite like him, which made it even more so. He was more frustrating, more pathetic, and at the same time, desperate and hardworking. I've always had the desire to write a character like that, and I was able to relieve some of that desire with this story. Although in the end, he did become a bit of a jerk ㅎㅎ…

I always enjoyed reading the comments too. I'm especially grateful to the readers who scolded me, as I believe those kinds of comments help keep a story from running wild. Still, please don't fight with each other in the comments section. To me, all of you are precious readers.

After writing for so long, I want to rest a bit, but I have too many ideas I want to write about. I'll take it easy while I fill out the volume for my next work and then I'll return. I've already thought of several things for what's next. It will probably be a survival story. Or maybe I'll try rewriting my previously failed work, 'ready to die Kim Eundong'. Of course, since this is my full-time job, I'll have to write a new story if both of those fail. After all, there's no way to know if readers will find a story enjoyable just because I had fun writing it.

Writing a protagonist like Seongmin for a year was fun, but it was also frustrating. For my next work, I'm planning to go with a straightforward and refreshing guy. I don't know what kind of material I'll write yet, but I've definitely decided on his personality. I'm going to go with the type I love to write so much, a real bastard. Perhaps some of you might be thinking, 'Again?'. I'm sorry, but I really do love a protagonist with that kind of personality.

I'm relieved it's still January.

It's late, but Happy New Year.
