As he led the way toward the North Gate, Lee Sungmin's thoughts churned with the complexities of his new reality. He had somehow formed a connection with the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, a giant who would one day shake the foundations of Eria. They had an ambiguous relationship, one she called a friendship. He had no idea how sincere she was, but simply having this connection was something his past self could never have imagined.

*Friend. …A friend…*

The word left a bitter taste in his mouth. In his previous life, Lee Sungmin had never known anyone he could call a friend. Thirteen years as a Classless had taught him nothing but a profound distrust of other people. Even those in the same desperate situation never trusted one another, instead fighting like dogs to steal what little they had. He knew from bitter experience that humans were nasty creatures, incapable of watching someone from a similar background rise above them. The animal called man could not simply laugh and watch a dragon emerge from a ditch.

Lee Sungmin had never possessed anything of value in that life. Even the small things he managed to acquire were eventually torn away from him. Because of that, the word 'friend' held no positive meaning for him at all.

"You. You said you've learned martial arts, didn't you?"

Wiji Hoyeon's voice cut through his thoughts from a few steps behind.

"Yes… no, yeah."

She had told him to speak informally, but the words felt incredibly awkward on his tongue. The title he had heard to the point of exhaustion in his past life, 'the Little Heavenly Demon', kept echoing in his mind.

"Then why aren't you using movement arts?" she asked.

Lee Sungmin turned his head to look back at her and saw a puzzled expression on her face. She was right. If they ran using movement arts, they could reach their destination several times faster.

"That's… because my inner arts are pathetic," he replied with a bitter smile.

He had already told her he'd learned martial arts by picking up a few techniques from an old bookstore. Since Wiji Hoyeon still lacked a Taoist understanding of this world, she didn't seem to find his explanation strange.

"Ah, I see," she said, nodding with a look of understanding. "You weren't a martial artist." She paused for a moment. "You said it's only been a month since you started? And you probably haven't eaten any miracle herbs… so it makes sense that your inner arts are weak."

Was this brat mocking him? Her words felt like an awl stabbing him in the chest.

"How about you try using it once?" Wiji Hoyeon suggested.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before nodding. He immediately exploded forward, unleashing the One Thunder Movement Arts. He knew that with his current reserves, he could maintain this pace at full power for about ten minutes. Normally, he would conserve his energy for the hunting grounds, but this time he scraped together every last bit of his inner arts. The scenery blurred into streaks of color as the wind whipped his hair back.

"…Haa! Haa!"

Just as his inner arts were about to sputter out, he skidded to a halt. He bent over, hands on his knees, gasping for breath. Sweat drenched his entire body and his legs ached with the strain. He could feel the distinct hollowness of his depleted qi center.

"……"

He quickly raised his head and looked forward. Wiji Hoyeon stood a few steps ahead of him, perfectly composed and not even breathing hard.

"That's not a very good movement arts technique," she commented appraisingly.

Hearing that, a hot surge of anger boiled in his chest.

*Of course it isn't, you brat. The One Thunder Movement Arts is a Second-Rate martial art at best.*

And it wasn't as if he had the inner arts to compensate for its shortcomings. Still panting, Lee Sungmin forced his aching back to straighten. His head spun.

"If this were the Demonic Cult, I would have given you a miracle herb," Wiji Hoyeon muttered as she walked toward him.

*How nice would it have been to be reincarnated in the Demonic Cult?* The silly thought crossed Lee Sungmin's mind as he watched her approach. It was just a fleeting fantasy, of course. Had he been reborn there, becoming friends with Wiji Hoyeon would have been impossible.

"Give me your hand."

He held out his hand as she instructed. Wiji Hoyeon's fingers found his wrist, and she infused her own inner arts into his qi channels. In an instant, his empty qi center was full again.

"Your qi center is small," she observed. "The Heavenly Truth Meditation Art… was that what it was called? It seems to be a Taoist family's technique. This too… is not a very good inner arts technique."

"…Ahem."

*What a bitch!* Lee Sungmin cursed internally. She might have said it without any ill intent, but to him, each word was a blunt, painful jab.

"Taoist inner arts techniques are slow to develop but stable… that's what people often say," Wiji Hoyeon mused, letting go of his hand.

Lee Sungmin opened his mouth, his face sullen. "Thanks."

"It was nothing special," she replied with a gentle smile. "Not something worth thanking me for." She pointed toward the distant wall. "Is the gate that way?"

Thanks to his ten-minute sprint, they were now quite close to the North wall. Lee Sungmin nodded.

"I'll go on ahead. Follow me."

With those words, Wiji Hoyeon's body vanished. No, she hadn't vanished, she had just dashed forward too quickly for his eyes to follow. A hollow laugh escaped Lee Sungmin's lips as he watched her distant back.

"Fuck," he grumbled to himself. "How great would it be if I were born as the Demonic Cult Young Cult Leader too?"

He pushed off the ground and followed after her, once again using the One Thunder Movement Arts.

{sep}

*"Wild boars, rabbits…"*

Wiji Hoyeon walked through the forest with her hands clasped behind her back, overflowing with the leisure of someone out for a stroll. Beside her, Lee Sungmin trudged along with a sour look on his face.

"You said that monsters and beasts are different… right?" she asked.

"Yeah. They're monsters that look like beasts."

They had been in the forest for some time but had yet to encounter a single one.

"Are they tasty?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, still walking with her hands behind her back.

The question left Lee Sungmin speechless. Tasty? He had never eaten one. The rabbits and boars in this forest were omnivores, but their diet also included human flesh. Because of that, he had never even considered trying to eat them, at least not while he was in Zenavis.

"…They probably don't taste good. They have too much muscle."

"It seems you've never tried one?"

"That's… right."

"Then let's take this opportunity to try one," Wiji Hoyeon declared with a hearty laugh. "Gastronomy is one of my few hobbies."

So the Little Heavenly Demon's hobby was gastronomy. This was information he had never known in his past life, and it was also completely useless.

"Something's here," Wiji Hoyeon muttered as she walked.

*What's here?* Lee Sungmin tilted his head in confusion. A moment later, the bushes rustled violently and a giant rabbit leaped out.

"Is that the rabbit you were talking about?" she asked, her voice calm. "It's quite big."

"Uh…"

Wiji Hoyeon wasn't surprised at all. Instead, she stared at the rabbit with eyes full of interest. The creature twitched its ears, watching her warily. Her lips parted.

"That thing looks delicious."

"What?"

The moment Lee Sungmin asked, Wiji Hoyeon struck. As her long index finger pointed forward, a hole was bored into the rabbit's forehead.

"......"

Blood belatedly spurted from the wound. The rabbit staggered for a moment before collapsing to the ground.

*What in the world did she just do?* Lee Sungmin stared, his mouth half-open. He understood what had happened. The moment she extended her hand, she had unleashed the Finger Flicking Technique, piercing the rabbit's head with a formless attack.

The Finger Flicking Technique! In his past life, he had trained for thirteen years and couldn't even manage to coat his spear with inner arts, yet this girl was already using such an advanced skill.

*The thirteen years I lived through hell and hardship are worth less than a thirteen-year-old brat!*

At that moment, he wanted to cry. He had heard of her fame to the point of sickness in his past life, but seeing the skills of the thirteen-year-old Wiji Hoyeon up close made his heart ache with a profound sorrow.

"Shall we eat?" she asked.

"…Later, later…" Lee Sungmin muttered, swallowing his lament.

Wiji Hoyeon smacked her lips but listened to him. He staggered toward the rabbit and drew the dagger from his waist.

"One leg should be enough to fill our stomachs," she commented as if in passing.

Lee Sungmin silently carved off a large chunk of the rabbit's leg. He then took out his Subspace Pocket and placed the meat inside.

"What's that?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, her eyes wide with curiosity.

He explained what the Subspace Pocket was, and she let out a sound of admiration.

"That's quite a fascinating tool."

After putting the rabbit leg away, they started moving again. Lee Sungmin took his spear out of the Subspace Pocket as well. He hadn't planned on hunting today, but since they were already in the forest, he figured he might as well catch a few Goblins to extract their teeth and blood.

"You use a spear?"

"Yeah."

"A spear is a good weapon. It's relatively easier to get started with compared to other weapons."

"…What weapon do you use?"

"I've learned them, but I don't use them. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art, passed down only to the Cult Leader and Young Cult Leader of my sect, is history's number one martial art. Once you master it, your body no longer requires a weapon."

"Then why did you even learn to use weapons?"

"To build a foundation," Wiji Hoyeon said, her expression turning solemn. "From long weapons to short ones. You must know how to handle every weapon in the world to be able to face those who wield them."

These were the words of a thirteen-year-old kid. A damn prodigy. Someone like him had swung a spear for thirteen years and was still in this state, yet a prodigy was claiming to know how to use all weapons at the age of thirteen.

"What is the Spear Technique you've learned?"

"Soul Chasing Spear Art."

"There was a Spear Technique with that name in the Central Plains Murim where I lived," she said dismissively. "It wasn't a very impressive martial art."

The Soul Chasing Spear Art from her world wasn't necessarily the same as the one he had learned. For instance, there were many martial artists from the Wudang Sect in Eria, but the ultimate techniques they performed were all different.

"Soul Chasing Spear Art, Soul Chasing Sword Art, Soul Chasing Saber Art… I don't know why martial artists love the words 'Soul Chasing' so much. Do you know what they all have in common? Every martial art with 'Soul Chasing' in its name is completely unremarkable."

As she spoke, her face was overflowing with pride in the martial arts she had learned. Seeing that expression, Lee Sungmin felt depressed all over again.

*She doesn't have a bad personality…*

She was just the type of person you disliked for no reason. Lee Sungmin's shoulders slumped as he walked forward, heading toward the Goblin territory he remembered. He was careful not to always go to the same area, having designated eight different points in the forest. If he kept ambushing the scouting Goblins at the same spot, even creatures as stupid as them would eventually notice something was amiss.

For about a week, he had relied on ambushes and luring tactics. He had graduated from that level now. To more actively build up his body and grow accustomed to his spearmanship, he had started facing the Goblins head-on.

"KWEEK!"

It was strange. On their way to the territory, they kept encountering rabbits and wild boars. The rabbits were one thing, but even the boars, which usually charged recklessly, were now fleeing for their lives. Lee Sungmin sighed heavily as he watched the backside of a hastily retreating boar.

It was because of Wiji Hoyeon. He couldn't feel it properly, but the killing intent she was emitting was making the monsters flee.

"We should be in Goblin territory soon," he said.

"You said the green-skinned dwarves were called Goblins. Are you going to catch them?" Wiji Hoyeon asked.

Lee Sungmin nodded. He had taken out his spear, but thanks to her scaring everything off, he hadn't even had a chance to swing it yet.

"KIIK!"

Not long after they entered the territory, they encountered a Goblin patrol. Three of them cried out upon seeing Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon.

"They're truly hideous," Wiji Hoyeon muttered from behind him.

Lee Sungmin raised his spear and took his stance. It seemed she had no intention of stepping in.

*To think I'd be demonstrating my spearmanship in front of the Little Heavenly Demon.*

The fact that Wiji Hoyeon was watching made him more nervous than the Goblins themselves. He cautiously shuffled his feet, observing the creatures. They didn't pull out their poison darts, instead swinging their daggers menacingly with a swishing sound.

In that moment, Lee Sungmin shot forward. He used the One Thunder Movement Arts to put speed into his spear thrust.

"......"

The spear pierced the chest of the nearest Goblin. He retrieved it with a sharp twist and narrowed his grip on the shaft.

"......"

The swung spear struck a second Goblin's head. He immediately pivoted, slamming the spear into the body of the third Goblin. In an instant, all three lay dead on the ground.

"…Hoo!"

*It was clean.* Lee Sungmin grinned, praising himself internally. For a month, he had swung his spear every day and circulated the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. He had also gained a fair bit of muscle, and his execution of the Soul Chasing Spear Art had improved to a point where it was hard to compare with a month ago.

"Hm."

Behind him, Wiji Hoyeon stroked her chin.

"How pathetic."

The words struck Lee Sungmin's heart like a blade.
