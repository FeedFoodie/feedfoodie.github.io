Lee Cheonsang sat in the lotus position, his figure strangely solemn.

WOOOONG!

A tremor of demonic qi vibrated through the air, audible only to him.

*It's working.*

The demonic qi filling his lower qi center shimmered like smoke and began to rotate slowly. The form resembled a desert whirlwind, but its speed was several times slower.

Rotating qi was dangerous for someone with an untrained qi center. If the energy lashed out, it could tear the immature center apart.

Lee Cheonsang was relentless, though. When he decided to do something, he did it. He never looked back if something had to be done.

He focused with extreme concentration, delicately rotating the qi. He had to prevent his qi center from tearing and stop the energy from flowing backward and causing internal injuries.

WOONG! WOOOONG!

As time passed, the rotation sped up. His lower abdomen grew heavy with the increased speed.

Rotation is movement, and movement is power. The qi gradually increased its density as if the air were being compressed.

This increase in density was only momentary. The qi's density would slowly return to its original state once the rotational force was released.

Lee Cheonsang wanted the high-density qi to maintain its power while it completed a full cycle. To achieve that, he needed a stronger rotation.

WOOOOONG!

After one hour, the rotation speed had doubled from its starting point.

DRIP... DRIP...

Sweat trickled from Lee Cheonsang's forehead, ran down his cheek, and collected on his chin. The bead of sweat grew larger until it finally fell to his feet.

The rotational force generated power, and that power turned into heat. The air inside the private room grew hot.

*A little more. This isn't enough.*

It was an intuition.

*A little faster.*

WOOOOONG!

A sharp pain shot through his qi center.

Lee Cheonsang calmly analyzed his condition based on the pain.

*I can endure this.*

Pain was just pain. It couldn't shake a spirit as strong as an iron fortress. It had always been this way.

He was always calm, whether wild beasts tore at his body with their claws or a sword stabbed him in the Demonic Coliseum. There was no reason not to be.

*It's risky, but it's fine.*

Lee Cheonsang increased the rotational force.

WOOOOOOONG!!

The swirling qi instantly became noticeably faster.

SQUELCH!

The eruption of surging power made his internal organs feel like they were twisting. He felt an urge to vomit.

Lee Cheonsang suppressed even that. If he broke his posture or opened his mouth, everything he had done would be for nothing. He even risked destroying all his internal organs, though he had no intention of dying.

*More.*

WOOOOONG!! WOOOOOOONG!!

*More.*

FLASH!

Suddenly, a faint light leaked from Lee Cheonsang's lower abdomen. It was clearly light, but its brightness was extremely low. It was the dark radiance of a demon.

Lee Cheonsang opened his eyes. The demonic qi, gathered into a single point, instantly circulated throughout his entire body along the acupoints.

This wasn't the qi circulation of the True Demonic Art he had learned. It was a far more complex and bizarre movement, one where even the caster could lose their way if their concentration faltered.

TWITCH!

Lee Cheonsang's left arm twitched.

TWITCH! TWITCH!

The qi coiled around his opposite hand after passing over his shoulder and neck. It then began to dart between his upper and lower body like lightning.

Lee Cheonsang's body continued to twitch. Sometimes his chest convulsed, sometimes his back, and sometimes both his legs trembled. It looked as if his muscles were spasming.

This wasn't because of his muscles, but because the high-density demonic qi was breaking through blocked acupoints.

*It's sharp.*

Every time the qi pierced an acupoint, he felt a pain like a large needle stabbing into tender skin.

Such pain flared up three or four times all over his body in the blink of an eye. It was an agony that ordinary mental fortitude couldn't endure.

DRIP...

Red blood trickled from Lee Cheonsang's lips.

HISSSS...

Heat radiated from his entire body and escaped through the window. The contaminated qi embedded in Lee Cheonsang's body had vaporized.

FLASH! FLASH!

The speed of the cycle quickened.

Once one cycle succeeded, the qi that had been losing density received a boost of power and increased its density even more. The newly enhanced qi completed the cycle faster than before, and the qi that finished the cycle increased its density yet again.

*Stronger, deeper, faster.*

The qi, which had amplified its power through successive cycles, now far surpassed the purity of the original demonic qi.

SHUDDDER!

Lee Cheonsang's body began to tremble, but he didn't feel cold. His body, unable to handle the amplified demonic qi, was sending a warning that it couldn't take any more. His muscles and bones could be torn apart if he continued.

Lee Cheonsang thought. *I can manage three more rotations.*

He felt pain, but not a sense of crisis. It would be possible if he pushed himself to the absolute limit.

WHOOSH!

*One rotation.*

WHOOSH!

*Two rotations.*

Lee Cheonsang closed his eyes. Thick veins bulged on his sweat-drenched forehead.

*...Three rotations.*

BOOM!

A deafening roar sounded, as if a giant iron hammer was shattering a castle wall.

"!!?" Lee Cheonsang found himself sprawled on the floor. He hadn't fainted, but he had lost consciousness for a moment.

Even if he had lost consciousness, his body would have remained in the lotus position if his will had persisted. His body had automatically broken the posture and naturally released its strength to disperse the immense power.

"......"

He pushed himself up from the floor, feeling as if his body had become several times lighter.

HISSSSSS!

Translucent smoke rose from all over his body with a strong heat.

"I succeeded."

Muttering softly, Lee Cheonsang looked down at his own body. His appearance was distinctly different. He hadn't been short before, but his height seemed to have increased by about three centimeters.

His sun-darkened skin had turned slightly paler, and the sword scars and beast claw marks decorating his body had faded.

His somewhat thin body also seemed to have become a bit larger. Rather than an increase in muscle, it seemed his very bone structure had gotten thicker.

His entire physique had been slightly adjusted. His body had grown to contain the powerful demonic qi and to withstand the burden of the power it generated.

While it couldn't be called a Body Reformation, this was a truly surprising event.

*This is a good method. I can use this again in the future.*

Because his lower qi center had been open from the beginning, he was able to cultivate the True Demonic Art faster than anyone. His Foundation Establishment was also fast, and his stable qi center could easily withstand even violent inflows and outflows of qi.

The idea of increasing density by rotating qi, however, wasn't something one would expect from someone at his level. In terms of ingenuity alone, it wouldn't be strange to see it as the craftiness of a peak master.

No Master, even if they had thought of such a method, could put it into practice. It was too dangerous.

Rotating qi where the internal organs were gathered, not outside the body, carried the risk of destroying the very source of bodily activity. He was able to use such a method because he did not feel fear.

The method was dangerous, but the gains were just as great.

CRACK, CRACK!

He slowly moved his body, and a sound like twisting bones came from all over. It felt refreshing. He became aware of the changes from the fine-tuning of his skeleton as he stretched.

*The human body adapts quickly.*

If the qi's density had risen any higher, his body would have given up on adapting and collapsed. Lee Cheonsang, who had precisely calculated that limit, had obtained the best body he could at his current level.

His body had now transformed on its own through a radical change, achieving development that would have been impossible through gradual training.

*Arise.*

FWOOSH!

A demonic qi clearly different from before began to reveal its true nature.

Unlike the soft, smoke-like demonic qi of the True Demonic Art, this was a demonic qi that blazed like a flame the moment it was drawn out. It was far fiercer and harder than before. At the same time, it also possessed the flexibility to disperse even more gently than the True Demonic Art's qi when its master commanded.

Lee Cheonsang raised his hand.

WHOOSH!

Demonic qi, invisible to the eyes of others, gathered in his hand. As the formless energy gathered, the temperature of the private room rose even further, like the heat of a great fire.

Lee Cheonsang could see it, though. A golden demonic qi revealed its distinct form only to its master's eyes.

The image of a heavenly king, who once led countless Yaksha and Rakshasa, was contained within his palm. The king was now possessed by a demon and howling like an evil spirit.

It was the manifestation of the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art.

*It will take time to handle this properly.*

He had skipped several stages in a single moment. It was a feat that any martial artist would dismiss as impossible. Becoming accustomed to it would require constant effort and time.

Lee Cheonsang stared down at his hand for a while.

*Hmm?*

Someone was approaching.

The demonic qi of the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art was incomparable to that of the True Demonic Art. On top of that, his newly developed physique had sharpened his five senses. His body could now feel more, and more keenly than before.

WHOOSH!

Lee Cheonsang withdrew the demonic qi.

A moment later, a voice came from outside. "Are you in there?"

"Come in."

The door opened and Hwang Museok appeared. "The Discipline..." Hwang Museok, who had been speaking, suddenly frowned. He felt an unusual heat in the room. His gaze sharpened. "You, what were you doing in here?"

"Why are you asking?" Lee Cheonsang asked back, confused.

Hwang Museok's expression turned murderous. The Commander seemed to cherish this brat, but he didn't. To him, Lee Cheonsang was just a nuisance who had disgraced the Commander. "Come out. Someone from the Discipline Hall is here."

Lee Cheonsang's eyes flashed. *So he succeeded.*

He had succeeded with the demonic art, and Do Heon had succeeded in his endeavour.

Lee Cheonsang walked past Hwang Museok with a blunt expression.

As he passed, Hwang Museok spoke. "Let me make this clear. I won't forgive you."

Lee Cheonsang stopped walking and looked back at Hwang Museok. There was no killing intent in Hwang Museok's eyes, but they expressed a vivid anger.

He had no idea why the man was angry at him. He could usually understand most things, even if he couldn't empathize, but he found the current situation completely incomprehensible.

Lee Cheonsang stared at Hwang Museok without a word.

Hwang Museok's face gradually contorted. "What are you staring at!"

Despite the shout mixed with anger and irritation, Lee Cheonsang's expression remained unchanged.

Hwang Museok's completely contorted face soon began to stiffen.

*What is this?*

Lee Cheonsang continued to stare at him silently. He wasn't even blinking. Being stared at so intently by those emotionless, almost transparent eyes was strangely unpleasant and uncomfortable.

Hwang Museok shouted, "What are you looking at! Get out now!"

Lee Cheonsang tilted his head slightly. "I don't understand."

With those words, Lee Cheonsang turned and left.

Staring daggers at that sturdy back, Hwang Museok spat on the floor. "What a truly detestable bastard."
