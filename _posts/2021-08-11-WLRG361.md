The sound felt distant, as if a newly opening world was approaching Blood Demon in its entirety. He did not dare try to comprehend the massive, writhing power contained within it, preparing instead to silently receive its full force.

He knew he must not avoid it.

While avoiding it would have been a valid martial theory at this point, the situation only served to steel his resolve. With no path of retreat, he could either stand his ground or break through. Blood Demon smiled quietly at the fact that there was nowhere left to fall back to.

The pinnacle of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art, operated to its absolute extreme, enveloped his body. His slow steps then culminated in a great stride, launching him into the sky. The very space around him fractured under the strain, creating a web of cracks. If Lee Sungmin's martial theory in the Transcendent Realm was speed and illusion, Blood Demon's was purely overwhelming power, a violent force that crushed everything in its path as it permeated his entire body.

The thunder cloud exploded and the Genesis opened. It was the same power that had annihilated the Death Lord, Vollander, but this time it manifested even more smoothly than before. Carrying the Genesis, the sky-piercing technique became a light that opened the world.

*Watch closely.*

Blood Demon's eyes shot open. Jeniela was not here, but he knew she was seeing everything through his eyes. He wondered what kind of expression his daughter was making right now, a thought that made him a little curious.

KWAANG!

Six arms stretched out toward the Genesis as lightning crackled around them. In an instant, two of the arms, forged from enhanced qi, vanished without a trace. His Protective Qi Aura wavered violently, and a churning in his stomach told him he had suffered internal injuries. Blood Demon paid it no mind and pushed his foot forward.

He could not be pushed back. *Forward, further forward,* he muttered to himself, thrusting his arms out once more.

His entire body trembled as all the arms of enhanced qi were annihilated. Even so, Blood Demon did not retreat. Instead, he put strength into his legs and advanced even further.

BZZT, BZZZZZT.

The mass of light finally touched both of his hands. He felt no sensation on his skin, and the very fact that there was not even pain was ominous. His blood and qi burned hot, as if lava were flowing through him. Blood Demon twisted the corners of his mouth into a smile as the muscles in his arms bulged enormously.

KRRRRK!

He bent his numb fingers and gripped the light.

"AAAAAH!"

Blood Demon roared. The ceaselessly crackling lightning clashed against his Protective Qi Aura, and his wide-open mouth filled with blood.

JIIIIIIK!

The light of the Genesis began to tear. With his bare hands, Blood Demon was ripping the light apart and opening a path.

[Good heavens.]

Even the Void Monarch was in awe of such divine might. Lee Sungmin, however, was more shocked than impressed. The martial theory of the Genesis was the highest among all the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder techniques he understood. It was still difficult to compare it to the one the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself had used, but this was the power that had annihilated the all-but-immortal Vollander.

And Blood Demon was not just enduring it. He was tearing it apart with his bare hands. Lee Sungmin put more strength into his thrusting spear, and Blood Demon's body trembled even more, but he was not pushed back.

KWAANG!

In the end, the Genesis was completely torn apart by Blood Demon's hands.

"Keuk!"

Blood spit from Blood Demon's mouth. Even though it was not nighttime, a Vampire of his level possessed an immortality that made him difficult to kill. His arms, which had been torn to shreds while ripping apart the Genesis, and his internal injuries regenerated with astonishing speed. Blood Demon swallowed the blood pooling in his mouth and ran toward Lee Sungmin.

*The Genesis… was torn apart.*

Lee Sungmin's expression hardened as he swung his spear, filling the space with Homing Chain Lightning. Blood Demon no longer tried to dodge. He trusted in the firmness of his Protective Qi Aura and swung both arms, tearing the bundle of enhanced qi apart in vain. As Blood Demon's hands thrust out in a double palm strike, Lee Sungmin's feet stepped on empty air.

It was the Dancing Shadow Soul Break Second Form, One Step Dancing Shadow thunder cloud. The body Blood Demon struck turned into a thunder cloud, and the exploding lightning engulfed him, yet Blood Demon's Protective Qi Aura was too solid, and a Vampire's body could regenerate even fatal wounds in an instant.

*What should I do?*

Lee Sungmin pondered this as he appeared behind Blood Demon. He shot his spear toward his opponent, who was already whipping around to face him. The spear vanished from Blood Demon's sight, its tip soaring up from below to aim for his head. He quickly bent his head and upper body backward to dodge the attack, diverting the trajectory of the Serpent Soul Devouring White Lightning.

However, Lee Sungmin also possessed a non-human body. His joints twisted at impossible angles, creating a sudden variable in the spear's movement. Blood Demon's eyes moved quickly, identifying the spear's new path as he swung both hands to meet it.

JJEONG!

They clashed once, and the sound of impact rang out repeatedly. In the meantime, the fight below had already been settled. The Black Dragon Knight had left the unconscious martial artists in a heap and was now looking up at the sky. Next to him stood Yana, frowning. She wanted to join the attack, but Lee Sungmin's request for her not to was keeping her from moving.

[What're you gonna do?] the Void Monarch asked.

Blood Demon's hand shot in. Lee Sungmin knocked away the hand trying to grab his collar and struck with the shaft of his spear.

PWAAAK!

Struck by the Changjun, Blood Demon's head snapped back. Lee Sungmin had intended to shatter it, but his head did not break.

[It'll be hard to trash that guy with the martial arts you've shown so far,] the Elder noted. [In this Elder's opinion, you seem to have a slight advantage, but… you lack the decisive power to end the fight. Or are you gonna keep fighting like this? If it becomes night, you'll be at a disadvantage.]

Lee Sungmin knew that well. He overwhelmed Blood Demon in inner arts and Demonic Energy, but just as he had been against the Spear King, he was lacking in technical skill. Against the Spear King, it had been possible to push through with overwhelming firepower, but that was because the Spear King was human. Blood Demon was not human, but a Vampire. Any ordinary wound would regenerate in an instant.

[You can't annihilate him in one go either,] the Void Monarch continued. [You have to inflict a wound so severe he can't fight anymore and subdue him. After that, you can interrogate him or whatever, and then devour him. What'll it be?]

The desire dwelling in his heart wavered. It was the one he had never had a chance to use until now. No, to be precise, it was the one he lacked the confidence to use even if he wanted to.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was composed of nine forms. One strike Soul Chasing Elimination. Second form, Homing Chain Lightning. Third form, Serpent Soul Devouring. Fourth form, Nine Dragons Slaughter. Fifth form, Deathly Flash. Sixth form, Void Path. Seventh form, sky-piercing. Eighth form, Illusionary World, and among them, the ninth form. Lee Sungmin had never once used the ninth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear in actual combat.

He had used up to the ninth form while training in Denil's Mental World, but never in reality. It could not be helped. With his incomplete body at the time, it had been impossible. Furthermore, that final form, much like Illusionary World, had become a completely different technique after passing through the hands of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. That was why it had become even more difficult to use.

However, now. Now that he had obtained a complete body as a Yokai and his physical form no longer went berserk, he could use it.

*I want to try it.*

The desire whispered, and Lee Sungmin's fingertips trembled. *Can I use it now?*

When the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had modified and passed on the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, he had warned him. He had warned that he had been too greedy, that he had incorporated a martial theory unsuited for Lee Sungmin's level. If he could not use it properly, he might suffer from qi deviation.

Qi deviation was a burden one must always bear when performing martial arts. No matter how high one's realm, one should never disregard it. In fact, one had to be even more careful of qi deviation when at a high realm, for it was more dangerous to fall from a high place than from a low one. Hadn't the Sword Immortal, the greatest master of the Wudang Sect and the orthodox sects, become unable to leave Mount Wudang because of it?

*What's the point of hesitating now.*

Lee Sungmin made up his mind. If he hesitated out of fear of something that had not yet happened, he would accomplish nothing. He had to do this. He must. Blood Demon was not the last person he had to defeat. How strong was Jeniela? What about Ju Won? Gemini? And The End?

The pinnacle of martial arts. Everything he had felt while learning martial arts until now. The entirety of his insights. Everything the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had greedily infused into it.

The spear trembled as a power different from any before flowed into it.

"Endure," Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice. It was something he said to himself, and also to the spear he held.

His mind calmed. The desire that had been stirring in his chest just moments ago cooled completely. He must not be agitated. Composure was the foundation of martial arts.

When his attack stopped, Blood Demon showed a puzzled expression, but he did not stop. To see all of Lee Sungmin, he himself had to show everything first. If Lee Sungmin were to die in the process… so what. If that happened, it would just mean today was not his day to die. He himself would be a little disappointed, but Jeniela would be satisfied. Or… would she be a little happy that her adoptive father did not die?

The light covering Blood Demon's body swelled greatly as the pinnacle of the Asura Heaven Destruction Art unfolded. He stretched out both arms, and the space around him was dyed red. The form of an Asura soared up behind him, a flickering, three-headed, six-armed Ghost Spirit that looked down with eyes that held hell itself. Blood Demon's two hands moved, and with each motion, the six arms moved in unison while the three heads let out a laugh.

The Asura Heaven Destruction Art's ultimate technique, the Asura Sky-Rending Dance, had begun.

Lee Sungmin felt the weight of the spear in his hand. It had been a long time since he had truly felt its weight. Every single muscle fiber squirmed as if it were breathing. He felt the inner arts and Demonic Energy flowing through his blood and qi as his senses opened wide, taking in the aura of death from the Asura Sky-Rending Dance that drenched the space.

*The spear in my hand is heavy.*

In truth, it must have always been heavy. He just had not felt it because he had swung it so easily until now. This was a weapon for killing people, a spear as tall as he was. His immense strength had allowed him to swing it haphazardly, but it was originally a long weapon that could not be wielded in such a way.

It was hard to recall the first time he held a spear. He had not been talented with it in the first place, nor was he familiar with weapons. He just happened to pick one up. In his past life, he was a coward who feared death and was not accustomed to fighting, but to survive in this world, he had to wield a weapon. He could not get used to shooting things like bows and had no confidence in hitting targets from afar. The thought of missing or running out of arrows terrified him so much that he could not bring himself to do it. The spear seemed decent enough. Since it was long, he thought he could just poke at things from a distance.

It was because of Wiji Hoyeon's teachings that he began to truly understand the spear as a weapon. He learned the basics, starting with Ran, Na, and Chal, and was taught the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. After that, it was almost all real combat and self-study. Mount Mushii, Denir's Trial, countless other fights, and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

The memory of when he first held the spear. All the experience from training and fighting until now. The me of today. The spear is still heavy.

It had to be heavy. This was a martial art refined over a lifetime by the martial artist known as the Spear King. It was tailored for him by the calamity with an absurd level of talent, the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon, and this was the final ultimate technique of a Spear Technique into which history's strongest Demon Emperor, Yang Ilcheon, had greedily poured his own insights.

The spear is heavy.

The spear was Lee Sungmin himself. Spear Unification was achieved. He had never managed it before, but the moment he recognized the spear's weight, the spear became him, and he became the spear.

And further still. On the surface of his calm mind, a single spear materialized. The death of the Asura Sky-Rending Dance washed over him. He stared at Blood Demon, who danced with the three-headed, six-armed Asura. He was an outstanding martial artist. Perhaps, if he had not been a being of this world, where the limits of growth were clearly defined, wouldn't he have been as strong as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?

He had to defeat such a man.

This was the place where the organization called the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance once stood, the place ruled by Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. Lee Sungmin felt the weight of the mask on his face.

"…pinnacle of martial arts."

He thrust once.

That single thrust contained all of his insights. Swift lightning, scattering Lightning Annihilation, soaring inverse lightning, the overwhelming lightning calamity, flashing lightning, bursting Explosive Thunder, crushing lightning strikes, the flowing thunder cloud, the resounding thunderclap and Heaven's Roar, streaking flying lightning, the White Lightning that encompassed them all, the Myriad Thunder that surpassed even that, and the Genesis that opens the world.

The pinnacle of martial arts reached the end of the world opened by the Genesis.
