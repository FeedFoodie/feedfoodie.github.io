*It's coming.*

Teresa's teeth chattered. Fear, an unfamiliar and terrifying sensation, crept up her spine. A part of her wanted to run, but a stronger instinct, the thought that she shouldn't, made her grab her own trembling shoulders.

*Are you okay?* Scarlet's telepathy echoed in her mind.

*What is it?* Lloyd, who had been silent until then, asked as well.

"I'm fi..." The word caught in her throat, refusing to come out. Teresa drew a deep, shuddering breath. This was not a fear she was accustomed to.

Even as a snot-nosed child, she had never believed in the existence of a Ghost Spirit. When she was first summoned to this world and the voice of God fell silent, she had not felt the Despair or fear that gripped the other priests. Though she could no longer hear His voice, Teresa never doubted God's existence in another world. She had always received His protection and had, as a result, almost never known true fear. Even after learning of The End, she had held onto hope rather than surrendering to Despair.

It might have been arrogance. A vague belief had taken root in her that as long as she was here, this world would never face The End.

Because she was the Saintess.

However, now, she was afraid. So afraid she could not bear it. She wanted to scream, to turn and run away.

Teresa clutched her rosary tightly. *Oh, God.* She called the name she had called so many times before, but His voice still did not answer. Staggering to her feet, she wiped the tears welling in her eyes with the back of her hand and checked the projection.

[What's wrong? Is it here?] Scarlet appeared, her expression etched with worry. Even in the middle of her own fight, she was constantly checking in, trying to ease Teresa's anxiety.

[Don't be too martial arts theory.] Lloyd also appeared, his face stiff.

*I won't run away,* Teresa muttered, pressing the rosary to her chest.

Her gaze swept over the projection, seeing Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight pushing back Chen and Kun. It stopped on the Black Dragon Knight. After studying his face for a moment, Teresa pushed back the hood of the Junior Disciple uniform that covered her head. She rubbed her pale cheeks with both hands, then slapped them with a sharp sound. The stinging pain brought a flicker of focus, and she bit her lower lip.

"It's here."

She gripped the rosary and checked the projection before her. A leisurely strolling Jeniela was just entering the lakeside. From Teresa's position, she couldn't yet spot the vampire with the naked eye, but she knew it would be too late by the time she could. Perhaps Jeniela was already watching her.

And indeed, she was. Jeniela saw the lone figure standing before the lake. She opened her lips and stuck out her tongue, licking her lower lip as if suddenly thirsty. *That must be the Saintess. She has a face that wants to run, yet she doesn't.*

"How admirable," Jeniela chuckled.

KIIIIING.

Space itself vibrated. A burst of light erupted, annihilating the darkness as a dazzling barrier blocked the path to the lake. It was incomparably stronger than the one that had enveloped the entire forest. Jeniela reached out, her hand moving toward the wall of light.

PZZZZZT!

The moment her fingers touched it, Sacred Flames erupted, burning her hand. She casually shook the flames from her flesh and grinned as the charred hand regenerated in an instant. Breaking through with her bare body would be undignified. Jeniela took a few steps back.

KWARURURU!

Red magic power, as viscous and crimson as blood, surged from her body. She gathered the energy into her right hand, forming it into a round sphere, and then slowly threw it from her palm.

KWAAAAANG!

A massive explosion shook the space. The magic spewing from the exploded sphere encroached upon the barrier of holy power.

"...Ah...!" A suppressed scream burst from Teresa's lips. She forced strength into her staggering legs and gritted her teeth. *It can't collapse already.* She swallowed the blood filling her mouth and reinforced the barrier.

"Aaargh!"

KWAAANG!

Jeniela's magic slammed into the barrier once more. A scream tore from Teresa's mouth as her legs, which she had been tensing to hold on, finally gave way. She collapsed to the ground, blood trickling from between her trembling lips.

*Please.* With a trembling hand, she gripped the rosary. Teresa barely managed to lift her eyes, searching for the Black Dragon Knight.

"...Help me..."

Her tearful whisper did not reach him.

Again. The barrier shook. Black blood dripped from Teresa's eyes and nose.

*God...* She opened her trembling lips to find him, even though she knew no answer would come, no matter how martial arts theory she called.

KUUUUNG!

An impact incomparable to the previous ones shattered the barrier's outer layer. *Cough.* Teresa couldn't hold it back and vomited a mouthful of dark blood that pattered onto the ground below. She stared at it with eyes full of Despair.

*I have to hold on longer...*

A hand grabbed her shoulder.

She turned her head. Lee Sungmin looked down at her with a pitying gaze, taking in the black blood streaming from her eyes, nose, and mouth. His hand pressed against her acupoints.

"You did well."

Teresa heard his words from the edge of her fading consciousness. *Is it okay to rest like this?* The anxiety and fear lingered, even as her mind went dark.

Lee Sungmin lifted her limp body into his arms and entrusted her to Osla, who stood behind him.

"Please, take care of her."

"I will," Osla nodded, taking Teresa into her arms. She approached the lake and gently laid the Saintess down. Droplets from the water enveloped Teresa's body, and below the surface, small Fairy Realm showed worried expressions as they received her.

With Teresa unconscious, the barrier vanished. Lee Sungmin took a deep breath and walked forward. In the distance, he could see Jeniela standing with a bright smile. Just as he was watching her, she was watching him.

[Quit grinning,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

Lee Sungmin gripped his spear tightly. Osla stood behind him, her face tense.

"I thought you wanted to negotiate?" Jeniela asked. Though the distance was considerable, her voice sounded as vivid as if she were speaking right beside him.

"If you came to negotiate, you shouldn't have come like this," Lee Sungmin replied.

"If you want to argue about that, you're the one who kidnapped Aine in the first place. So, how was she?" Jeniela bared her teeth in a smile. "Was she delicious?"

"No." The beating in his chest still felt foreign.

"I thought you'd be angry."

"Now that I've come this far, there's no reason for you to keep that child alive. Do you think I'm a fool? I never expected you to quietly return Aine just because I came here in person."

"Then why did you come in person?"

"Because I can't see the future right now," Lee Sungmin said. "So I came to confirm it myself. Besides, I have no intention of letting you off the hook any longer."

"I've let you off easy enough, haven't I?" Jeniela shrugged. "This is the end of the line. Why didn't you just run away?"

"Because fighting here was the best option."

"Ahahat... I suppose you trust in the Fairy Queen's power?" Jeniela's gaze shifted to Osla, who met it with ice-cold eyes. The corners of Jeniela's mouth twitched upward. "Indeed. If today wasn't a full moon, fighting in this place would have been very disadvantageous for me, but today is a full moon."

Jeniela did not underestimate Osla's power, especially here in the Fairy Queen's domain where she could exert her full strength.

"I wouldn't have come if I wasn't confident."

Jeniela started walking. Gemini didn't move. She looked at Lee Sungmin with a sullen expression, inwardly hoping he would run away. No matter how martial arts theory positively she thought about it, it was a Buddhist-able matter for Lee Sungmin to defeat Jeniela. The full moon alone offset every other condition.

*It shouldn't be this way.*

The killing intent Gemini held for Jeniela was real. She loved the Vampire Queen, but at the same time, she wanted to kill her by any means necessary. As long as Jeniela desired the destruction of this world, Gemini had to kill her, no matter what it took. That insane queen desired suicide for herself and was forcing the world's destruction and suicide upon all her vampires. Gemini, however, had no desire to die whatsoever.

"What...?" Preskan's consciousness, which had been frozen as if time had stopped, stirred. "Aine is... wait... that can't be. Aine is..."

"Dead," Jeniela answered without looking back. Even Preskan's Despair was a source of amusement for her. "Did you really think she'd be alive? Weren't you hoping for too optimistic a development? If you think about it, there's no reason at all for Aine to be alive."

"Th...at... that can't be... Queen...?"

"It couldn't be helped. If I had been there, I would have stopped it, but she died while I was away."

"Aaaaaaaargh!" Preskan wailed, collapsing to the ground with a cry filled with Despair and hatred. To Jeniela, the sound was like beautiful music. She smiled, satisfied.

"You won't be able to run away this time," Lee Sungmin said.

"I have no intention of running."

"Then do you plan to die?"

"No."

"Do you think you can win?"

"No."

"What's that supposed to mean?" Jeniela let out a short laugh as a red sphere swallowed her. Her body floated up gently. "That wouldn't be any fun, would it?"

Lee Sungmin watched her form rise with the full moon at her back.

*Is Gemini not going to move?* It would be nice if she stayed still. He had hoped she might even actively help, but in truth, there was no reason for her to do so. Gemini had always only given as much help as she could; she wouldn't do something that would cause a complete break with Jeniela now.

"I'll support you as much as I can," Osla said in a stiff voice. "I can't support you on the offensive. My attacks are weaker than your spear. Instead, my blessing will protect you."

[Don't trust your eyes and ears,] the Void Monarch's voice echoed in his mind as violet Demonic Energy surged, enveloping Lee Sungmin's body. [Under the full moon, Jeniela is an immortal and an incarnation of magic power. Trust your senses. Trust the experience you've gained from all your fights, and trust me. I will see what you cannot.]

[Don't forget. Your opponent is immortal. Fight with the knowledge that she cannot die. Don't waste your finishing blows. I don't know how much your current body can handle, but don't use the pinnacle of martial arts rashly. If you collapse from exhaustion like before, it's over. With your death.]

Lee Sungmin nodded. He must never forget that fact. His opponent was a monster that could not die. She wouldn't die even if he pierced her heart and shattered her head.

[There's still a lot of time before the moon sets. Even if you're lucky enough to last until then, it won't mean much. Not as long as that wench's Demonic Eye is what's creating the full moon. Heh heh! Even with all these conditions, you don't have a single advantage.]

He knew. He had imagined this moment countless times. In his imagination, whenever he fought Jeniela, he always lost. He could have pictured himself winning at least once, but the only thing he could ever envision was his own defeat.

He knew it well. *I am weak. I'm no match for that queen of monsters.*

Even if an egg could break a rock, Jeniela was not a being that could be compared to a mere rock. Lee Sungmin pushed off his toes and leaped.

Even knowing that, he could not run away.

Jeniela grinned at his approach.
