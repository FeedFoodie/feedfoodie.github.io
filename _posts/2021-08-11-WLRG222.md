"I guess our situations are just different," Wiji Hoyeon announced without warning.

Lee Sungmin tilted the brim of his bamboo hat with his fingertips, his gaze finding hers across the table. She sat with her arms crossed, a sullen expression clouding her face. A waiter soon broke the short silence, bringing their food. As he placed the simple dishes on the table, his eyes kept darting toward Lee Sungmin.

It was an understandable reaction. Beneath the wide bamboo hat, Lee Sungmin wore the hideous Ghost Mask. The man's curiosity warred with his professionalism as he stole another look at the hidden face before finally setting down the last dish and making a hasty retreat.

Wiji Hoyeon grumbled, her arms still crossed as she eyed the mask. "To think you have to wear it even out here. It just draws unnecessary attention."

"It's fine. I take it off when I eat," Lee Sungmin replied, suiting action to word. As he removed the mask, the tight constriction on his qi center loosened, though no trace of his inner qi or Demonic Energy surfaced.

Wiji Hoyeon studied his calm, subdued eyes before pulling a bowl of steaming noodles toward herself. "It's been a while since I've had noodles like this."

"You're right," Lee Sungmin said, picking up his own chopsticks. "We didn't eat this kind of food in the Legro Forest." He still had not responded to her initial declaration, and she did not seem to be pressing for an answer. He decided to address it now. "I understand that our situations are different."

He sipped the clear broth and then ate a mouthful of the well-loosened noodles. It had been a long time, but he felt no particular sentimentality. They had simply walked into the first restaurant they saw after leaving the forest, giving the choice no real thought.

"I'm the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman," she continued. "I have to go to the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, announce I've become the official successor, and get their recognition, but you're refusing to join."

"Because I have no desire to," Wiji Hoyeon stated plainly. "I admit the Chairman is a great martial artist, but I don't want to be under him."

"Not even with me?"

"No."

Her answer came without a hint of hesitation. Lee Sungmin felt no sting from her words. They had discussed this many times over the past year, and he had no intention of forcing her to agree with him.

"Then I guess there's no helping it."

"Does it not bother you at all?"

"Of course it does. I just don't want to control you. You wouldn't like that either, would you?"

A small laugh escaped her. "If I'd gotten pregnant, I would've had no choice but to go with you."

"What can't be done, can't be done," he said. "Even with your outstanding martial arts theory, some things are just impossible."

This time she burst into laughter. "You answer so calmly. You used to get quite embarrassed."

"I think a year is enough time to overcome embarrassment."

"You've lost some of your cute charm."

"Really? I still think you're cute."

"Do you think saying something like that will make me blush?" she scoffed at his smiling words.

*It's not like I was expecting her to,* Lee Sungmin thought, continuing to eat.

"If we part ways, what do you plan to do?"

"I'll travel again," she answered, her voice calm. Her gaze remained unwavering as she continued. "I've learned the world is a wide place. There might be other masters out there who are better than me."

"You can just travel with me."

"I told you, I have no intention of joining the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance."

"I'm not telling you to join," he countered. "I'm just saying, let's be together. I have no intention of forcing the Alliance's business on you."

A hollow laugh escaped her. "Easier said than done. You know it wouldn't work out that way. I'm not some nobody. How do you think the world would react to me being with you, the disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?"

"They wouldn't look upon it favorably."

"You're asking even though you know?"

"That's how much I want to be with you," he said, his expression placid. The calmness of his delivery only made the words sound all the more shameless. Even Wiji Hoyeon was struck speechless, her chopsticks hovering mid-air as she stared at him.

"...You say such shameless things."

"I'll stop if you don't like it."

"I never said I didn't like it." She cleared her throat with a 'Hmph' and leaned back slightly.

After another brief silence, Wiji Hoyeon sighed and shook her head. "...I am going to leave."

"If you just want to travel, you don't have to leave."

"That's... not all of it," she admitted. "I want to test myself. The things I gained in that forest. The things I gained through repeated defeats. I want to see how much I've changed because of them."

Her statement was vague, as if she did not want to reveal everything, or perhaps had not even formed a clear objective herself. Lee Sungmin understood and slowly nodded. Disappointment was there, but he wanted to respect her choice.

"Are you planning to leave right away?" He put down his chopsticks, his appetite gone.

Wiji Hoyeon seemed to feel the same. She stared at her own lowered chopsticks before nodding.

"......"

"Is there a reason to leave so suddenly?"

"...There is."

*There was something.* Lee Sungmin picked up the Ghost Mask and fitted it back over his face. The mask had been a constant companion for a year. At first, he had taken it off to practice, but for the last six months, he had worn it even during training. Now, he only removed it to eat. The familiar sensation returned as his qi center froze over, tightly restraining his inner qi and Demonic Energy. His mind grew even calmer.

"Is it a reason you can't tell me?" he asked.

Wiji Hoyeon fell silent again. The frequent pauses stretched the conversation thin, but he did not find the quiet awkward. He simply waited.

"It's a personal matter," she finally said. "I've been having a dream recently. It's not a dream I can just dismiss as nonsense. I've had the same one every day."

"Since when?"

"For the past week."

"You mean you've been having it since you started sleeping next to me. Why didn't you tell me?"

"I needed to sort out my thoughts."

"So. What was the dream?"

"Fog," she muttered, frowning. "It's a vague dream. I was wandering in the fog... and there was no one around me. No one. I knew it was a dream, but that didn't mean I could do anything inside it."

"It's a vague dream, just like you said, but that's not a reason to part ways with me, is it?"

"Maybe not, but it's bothering me too much. The fact that I was all alone in the dream... that bothers me too." She pressed her temples firmly. "You have your own business to attend to, don't you? I think this is for the best. For now, you go your way, and I'll go mine. We're bound to meet again anyway." She put a special emphasis on those last words.

They paid for the meal and stepped outside. Lee Sungmin pulled his bamboo hat low, hiding his masked face from the world. Wiji Hoyeon stopped in front of the restaurant and looked up at him.

"I don't know how much time it will take, so it's hard to set a time to meet again. So... when my business is finished, I'll come find you. That'll probably be faster."

"Aren't you sad?"

"Don't ask when you already know the answer," she spat. She strode forward, her hands gripping his shoulders as she glared into the eyeholes of his mask. "Of course I'll miss you, and I hate being apart, even for a short while. So don't you forget. You took all of my firsts, so you must never forget."

"I won't forget."

"You'd better not. If you forget, I'll kill you."

A small smile touched his lips, hidden by the mask. "Isn't that a bit too harsh to say to a friend?" he replied, recalling her words from long ago.

However, Wiji Hoyeon was not smiling. Her glare remained fixed on his eyes. "I'm not saying it to a friend. I'm saying it to 'you'. To you, who took all my firsts. ...Well, I'm not telling you not to meet other women, but just as you became my first, you must also consider me your first. Got it?"

"I got it."

As she released her tight grip, Lee Sungmin's hands moved to her shoulders. With his free hand, he lifted the mask away and kissed her. It was a short kiss, but when he pulled back, he murmured, "I'll be waiting for the day you come find me."

Her eyes wavered slightly, and he saw her ears turn a little red. She took a few steps back, sighing. "You're making it hard to leave."

"Then don't leave."

"Don't... push it," she replied, her voice small. "It won't take long. Yes, probably. I can't be sure, but even if it's something that takes a long time, I'll make it so it doesn't."

SQUEEZE.

She clenched her fist as she muttered the promise to herself. Then, as if consciously shaking off her lingering feelings, she composed her expression. The smile she gave him was the same one from their parting in Zenavis twelve years ago.

"See you next time."

"Yeah." He wanted to hold her back, to tell her not to go, but he knew he should not. He smiled back. "See you next time."

Wiji Hoyeon turned and vanished, her movement arts carrying her away in a blur. She was gone from his immediate sight in an instant, becoming a small, wavering dot at the very edge of his vision. Lee Sungmin stood there for a long while, watching blankly until even that dot had disappeared completely.

[Why didn't you stop her?]

*Don't ask when you already know,* he answered the Void Monarch in his head. He turned around. Wiji Hoyeon must have agonized over this choice. It might have been just a dream, but to her, it was clearly more than that. It was why she had to leave.

[There aren't many beings who can interfere with another's dreams. The only one this Elder can think of... is a Spirit.]

*Are you saying a Spirit interfered with her?*

[I don't know. I've felt this many times, but you get involved with non-human beings a lot.]

*I guess it's fate,* Lee Sungmin muttered to himself and started walking.

Where should he go? First, he had to follow the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's instructions and report to the main building of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, but there was no need to rush. He had already told the Chairman there was a place he needed to stop by first.

[Can you make it in time?] The Void Monarch already knew his destination.

Lee Sungmin nodded without hesitation.

*It's enough.*

He kicked off the ground and broke into a run, heading toward the place where he had died in his past life.
