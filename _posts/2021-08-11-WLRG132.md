December arrived, and throughout the month, Lee Sungmin received guidance from the Crazy Heavenly Demon on the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. In truth, it was less instruction and more a trial by fire. The fundamental purpose was to help him master the new martial art through a series of grueling daily spars.

"You are..."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon chose his words carefully as he watched Lee Sungmin rest on the ground. After a moment of hesitation, he let out a deep sigh.

"You're not very accustomed to using new things."

"Just be blunt and say it. That I'm stupid," Lee Sungmin grumbled in response.

The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, a technique that contained the Crazy Heavenly Demon's own insights, was a divine art comparable to the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Mastering it was no easy task. Fortunately, Lee Sungmin had already reached a high level as a martial artist, so his proficiency in the new art was rising quickly. The problem, however, lay elsewhere. Even as his proficiency increased at a rapid pace, Lee Sungmin could not properly integrate the art into actual combat.

"It would've been better if you weren't a spearman!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon lamented.

He himself fought using only the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art without a weapon, but Lee Sungmin was different. For his overall inner qi coordination, he used the Violet Dawn Divine Art. For his spear technique, he used the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. For his movement, he used Dancing Shadow Soul Break. He had completely mastered these three martial arts through his training in the Mental World, giving him an innate understanding of how to use them harmoniously. He could choose his techniques through improvisation even if his body could not always keep up.

The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, however, was a different beast. As an enhanced qi art, it dealt exclusively with enhanced qi. In theory, it did not conflict with the Violet Dawn Divine Art, nor did it clash with the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear or Dancing Shadow Soul Break. If he mastered it, he could even add the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art to the forms of his spear techniques.

However, Lee Sungmin's talent did not make it easy. It was all far easier said than done. The task was no different from being told to execute two, three, or even four martial arts simultaneously. When he added supplementary magic to that, the number of things he had to execute in a split second increased exponentially. Since a fight never went as expected, acting improvisationally, whether consciously or unconsciously, required an effort so strenuous it felt like he was shitting blood.

"You're not stupid," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered with a sullen expression. He sat with his arms crossed, staring at Lee Sungmin. "You're just slower than others at getting used to things."

"Doesn't that mean I'm stupid?"

"No, that's not it. If you hadn't learned any martial arts before, you wouldn't be struggling like this, but you've already mastered your existing arts to a grandmaster's level. You're on the verge of the Transcendent Realm with them, and now you're adding a new martial art on top. It's only natural that you're not perfectly coordinated."

This was nothing new. Lee Sungmin was well aware of his own problems. The only reason he was able to adapt to the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art at all was due to the intensive guidance of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Void Monarch. While the Crazy Heavenly Demon guided him on the techniques he could see, Void Monarch gave advice from within his mind.

[It would be easier if you used Demonic Energy.]

*It's that 'easier' part that I'm reluctant about.*

Using Demonic Energy carried a high chance of turning him into a Yokai, and becoming a Yokai meant he would no longer be human. In truth, that was what Lee Sungmin feared. What would he become if he turned into a Yokai? To no longer be human was to abandon one's humanity, to become unable to act and think like a person.

Lee Sungmin remembered the Sword Phantom, who had become a vampire. He remembered the sight of him drinking blood despite having once been human. Although vampires and Yokai were different, Lee Sungmin did not know what a complete Yokai looked like. What if he blindly trusted Void Monarch's words, used Demonic Energy, and turned into one?

*What would the 'me' of that time be like? What would I think? If I did change, what would happen to the 'me' from before that? Would the 'me' who remembers Baek Sogo and Wiji Hoyeon remain intact?*

Sensing Lee Sungmin's feelings, Void Monarch did not press him. A choice like this was up to the individual. It was about what the choice was in pursuit of, what would be gained, and what would be lost through its consequences.

"How boring."

Rubia grumbled as he watched Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon. A break from the tedious life on the road and the relentless journey had been nice for a day or two, but now that they had been staying at the inn for over a month, he was getting restless.

*When is my master going to come find me?*

Rubia sipped warm milk, lost in thought. When he first started staying in the Sleeping Forest, he thought that meeting Lee Sungmin meant he would soon meet his master, Envirus, yet almost a year had passed since then, and there was still no sign of Envirus coming for him.

*Don't tell me I've really been abandoned...? No, that can't be right. It's just not time yet.*

Along with such worries, Rubia was feeling a strange emotion. His journey with Lee Sungmin had been quite enjoyable. Constantly running into incidents he had never experienced when traveling with Envirus was making him excited.

*I feel like something is about to happen again soon.*

Rubia thought this with a little excitement.

However, as December came to an end, the event he had been anticipating did not occur. That was not to say Trabia was peaceful. In this city, things were constantly happening, both beneath the surface and above it.

The Black Mages' parties continued. It was unknown what they were pursuing, but at the center of the gatherings, there was always one man. The man, who wore a mask that covered half his face, did not say much, but he was always surrounded by people.

Today's party was special. Only a select few from the many Black Mages gathered in Trabia participated, but that alone was not enough to call it special. What made today's party special was the participation of the Vampire Queen.

Jeniela was sitting on a large sofa in a private room on the second floor, which overlooked the hall. Around her sat the Vampires who originated from her. Kim Jonghyun, standing in front of the door, raised a hand and took off the mask that covered half his face.

"You greedy human."

"Greedy? I don't know what you're talking about."

"How shameless," Jeniela burst out laughing.

Kim Jonghyun looked at the man sitting right next to her. The man with vivid red eyes exuded the strongest presence in the room, aside from Jeniela herself. He was once known by the moniker the Blood Heavenly Demon, back when he was human. Now, he ceaselessly drank a strong liquor mixed with blood. His left arm, which had been severed in his fight with the Little Heavenly Demon, was now perfectly reattached.

"I've seen many Black Mages, but none were as special as you," Jeniela muttered. "You're a Black Mage, yet you're not subordinate to a Demon Lord. It's not common for a Demon Lord to be annihilated, but it does happen from time to time. In such cases, a Black Mage whose soul is forfeit meets the same fate as the annihilated Demon Lord."

"It was due to a series of coincidences," Kim Jonghyun replied with a smile. He had originally become a Black Mage by contracting with the Demon Lord Caladra and should have been annihilated along with him. However, being summoned to Eria had allowed him to escape the same fate.

"You've become a mutation," Jeniela muttered. He was becoming a being that was human yet not human. That was enough to be called a mutation. "So, what is it that you want? You arranged this meeting, so you must want something."

"Please let me join Predator," Kim Jonghyun said with a smile. His tone sounded less like a request and more like a one-sided notification. That attitude made Jeniela laugh.

"You, who are not even a Lich, wish to throw yourself into a group of the inhuman?"

"I am not a Lich because I have no need to become one. Do you feel that I am insufficient?"

"Ahaha!" Jeniela burst out laughing and shook her head. "I'm not refusing. If I intended to refuse, I wouldn't have even agreed to talk. You want to join Predator? Fine. I'll accept you as a member, but... what is it you want to do?"

Jeniela's eyes, which had been filled with cheerful laughter, narrowed. She sent a sharp gaze at Kim Jonghyun, as if seeing right through to his inner thoughts.

"What do you think Predator is?"

"I think of it as a group of monsters who were once human but are no longer."

"That's right. We are monsters who are no longer human. Monsters that reject and devour humans. If you want to join Predator, you must become a monster."

"Are you telling me to eat human flesh?"

"That's not necessarily what I mean. How will you prove your monstrous nature?"

It was a question like a test. Kim Jonghyun intuited that his fate would be decided by his answer here. Jeniela was a monster so powerful it was hard to find her equal in this wide world. The Vampire Queen, who had lived for ages, possessed immense power and a terrifying level of life force. Even if Kim Jonghyun wielded the power of a Demon Lord as a mutation, he was still lacking in many ways to be compared to her.

"...Is the drink to your satisfaction?" Kim Jonghyun began. "The blood of a pure maiden, of course, as well as newborns, pregnant women, and so on. I'm not a Vampire, but I have prepared various things to suit a Vampire's tastes."

"Ahaha! Do you think such offerings prove your monstrous nature?"

"Of course not," Kim Jonghyun said, looking down below. He could see the Black Mages and witches enjoying the party. Serving them were beautiful young men and women whose minds had been suppressed, slaves purchased through the broker Erebrisa. "All of them are offerings to you."

"How trivial," Jeniela cackled and shook her head. "I can receive such offerings whenever I wish. However... huhu! I shall be satisfied. It is lacking in many ways and insufficient to prove your monstrous nature, but it seems to be enough to prove your madness."

As Jeniela said that, she raised her hand. Space split open, and she took out a necklace with a pitch-black, inverted pentagram emblem.

"This is the Black Star, proof of membership in Predator. I shall give it to you."

Kim Jonghyun spread his hands and received the Black Star. The five inverted stars were a symbol of devil worship and also symbolized the five monsters at the apex of Predator. The Vampire Queen Jeniela, the King of Lycanthropes, the King of Death Knights, the King of Liches, and the King of Monsters. As a Black Mage, Kim Jonghyun would be under the management of the King of Liches.

"Well then, shall we enjoy the offerings?" Jeniela slowly rose to her feet. The other Vampires sitting beside her also rose. The Black Mages, witches, and servants enjoying the party had no inkling of the slaughter and feast that was about to unfold.

[My Queen.]

As Jeniela descended to the hall, one of the vampires sent a voice into her mind.

[The human you favor is about to leave Trabia.]

"Ahat."

The content of the report, which was always the same, had changed. Jeniela narrowed her eyes and chuckled. She glanced at Baek Museon, who was following her down.

*Go to Preskan at once and tell him to remain silent and not to try any foolish tricks. Also, tell him that if he disobeys my words, I will annihilate him immediately without going through Arbes.*

Arbes was the King of Liches, who held one of the seats of the Black Star alongside Jeniela. Originally, the Vampire Queen could not interfere with the annihilation of Preskan, a Lich. However, if she chose to ignore Arbes, she could interfere as much as she wanted. In fact, even if Jeniela were to annihilate Preskan, Arbes would not care.

"The child who defeated you is about to leave Trabia," Jeniela said to Baek Museon.

At her words, his shoulders trembled.

"Keep this in mind. Do not kill him. As long as you don't kill him, I won't care what you do."

"...Yes, Your Majesty," Baek Museon replied, killing intent pouring from his red eyes.

If he were still human, he would not have listened to Jeniela's words, but the current Baek Museon was not human. That was why, for nearly two months after becoming a Vampire, he had been unable to move. It was because of Jeniela's command. He did not even feel dissatisfaction with her order not to kill Lee Sungmin. All the emotions he had held for Jeniela were completely castrated when he was reborn as a Vampire. Nevertheless, his killing intent and hatred for Lee Sungmin remained. Baek Museon bowed his head deeply and turned away.

"Despair must be shared," Jeniela muttered in a cheerful voice as she thought of Lee Sungmin. "Just as you pushed that man, that man must also push you."

She burst out laughing.
