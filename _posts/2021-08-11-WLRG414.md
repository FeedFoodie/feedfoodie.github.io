Dawn broke as he arrived at the village nestled near the forest, the rising sun chasing away the last vestiges of night.

Lee Sungmin walked slowly into its streets. The last time he was here, he had been the one to clear away the bodies and the rubble of the collapsed homes.

His work had left the village empty, with no sign of life. The only living human should have been Kazuya.

*No.* He corrected himself, his gaze falling to his own wrist. *Not a single person is alive now.*

SSSSH...

One of the bracelets he wore was dissolving into black ash, scattering into the morning air. A bitter smile touched Lee Sungmin's lips as he watched it go.

He had forged that bracelet with Scarlet's magic, a link to Kazuya from his last visit. Its instant destruction could only mean one thing.

Kazuya was dead.

Lee Sungmin made his way to Kazuya's mansion in the center of the village. The unlocked door swung open under his touch.

He walked through a corridor where the faint scent of a person still lingered and headed for the bedroom. Beyond the paper door, the light of a single candle flickered.

He did not bother to call out a name. When he slid the door open, the bedroom came into view.

Kazuya lay upon the bed. He had been blind and frail when Lee Sungmin had last seen him, but the man on the bed looked even older, even more withered than before.

Lee Sungmin slowly approached. Kazuya had died with his eyes open.

"You've worked hard."

He gently closed the cloudy gray eyes with his hand and gave a slight nod of respect. This village and Kazuya's clan had been tasked with sealing Greed.

Once The End began, their fate had been to die. Lee Sungmin lifted Kazuya's body with Space Manipulation. He flicked his fingers, extinguishing the lonely candle flame, and left the mansion.

In the clearing that served as a cemetery, he fashioned a grave. Lee Sungmin bowed his head to the freshly raised mound of earth, then turned and walked away.

By then, the sky had grown considerably brighter. The Demonic Spirit in his head remained silent.

Lee Sungmin left the village without rushing and headed for the entrance to the Sleeping Forest, which had been blocked by Kazuya's presence.

Where thick, intertwined vines had once obstructed the path, now only rotted tendrils hung limply, leaving the way open.

Kazuya's death had undone the seal. Lee Sungmin took a deep breath, put strength into his steps, and entered the forest.

The woods were surprisingly quiet. Only a few weeks ago, he had fought the Spirit Queen here.

She had not been that powerful, having lost most of her strength, but the traces of their battle still scarred the forest.

*There are no gazes.*

He could not hear the ghostly cry either. The Sleeping Forest was supposed to be a place where an incessant noise deluded the minds of any who entered.

Now, he felt and heard nothing. The current Lee Sungmin knew the source of that cry and those gazes.

They were the emanations of Greed, which was sealed in this forest, and of the monsters that would be released along with it.

*Where is Wiji Hoyeon?*

[A little deeper.]

The Demonic Spirit's voice was faint. This forest was cut off from the outside world, and it was inevitable that the presence of a being who could not leave the Demonic Spirit Pavilion would fade.

How much deeper?

Lee Sungmin looked around as a tremor ran through the space. The trees of the forest shimmered like a mirage. He walked forward without stopping.

He glanced up at the sky. The light of dawn was bright. He lowered his head and looked ahead. Though the sky was bright, the path before him was dark.

The farther he walked, the deeper the darkness became. He looked at the sky again. It was still bright.

Still, his surroundings were a darkness where nothing could be seen. The void, where one could not see even an inch ahead, felt strangely comforting.

Lee Sungmin looked down at his body. It was clearly visible, as if painted over the blackness.

He focused his vision and tried using Dragonic, but the darkness did not disappear, and the space continued to writhe.

The sensation of walking on the ground grew faint. The Demonic Spirit's voice could no longer be heard.

This is as far as it goes. Lee Sungmin raised his hand, and the dark-colored Formless Spear materialized in his grasp.

"How long will this last?" Lee Sungmin asked in a small voice.

The moment he finished speaking, the darkness scattered. A great gust of wind blew through his hair.

A terrible stench was mixed in the wind.

The smell of blood, the smell of flesh, the smell of entrails, the smell of all of it rotting.

They were smells one would expect to find in the middle of a battlefield long after the fighting had ended.

His floating body fell downward. SPLAT. Something under his foot was crushed.

There was no need to look down. Even without doing so, he could see it right in front of him.

It was not a pleasant sight. No matter how strong one's stomach was, seeing something like this would make them throw up what they had eaten.

Lee Sungmin also felt a sense of displeasure and bit his lower lip hard. How many were there? A thousand, no, it was over ten thousand.

It was full of corpses.

The first thing he saw were the corpses of monsters.

Searching through all his memories, from his time as a mercenary to the memories of the Dragon, he could not recall any monsters that looked like these. The monsters' corpses were gruesome.

Part of it was due to severe decay, but even at a glance, they had not died clean deaths. They were savagely torn, smashed, and crushed. Such corpses were tangled together in a grotesque heap.

"...This is worse than a cesspool," Lee Sungmin muttered and looked up at the sky.

The light of dawn was gone. The sky was blood-red, and there was nothing else but that color.

No clouds, no sun, no stars, no moon. He was not even sure if that was the sky in the first place.

"Where are you?" Lee Sungmin muttered and walked forward.

He knew what kind of place this was.

Even though he had never been here, he could guess. When he had met Wiji Hoyeon in this forest, they could see each other but were in different spaces, unable to touch.

At that time, Lee Sungmin had seen the countless corpses behind her back.

*What are you doing?*

*I'm killing monsters,* Wiji Hoyeon had answered.

*Because there are many monsters sealing in this forest.*

Were these the bodies of those monsters? Lee Sungmin looked into the distance.

He could not see the end.

No matter how much he enhanced his vision, he could not see a place without corpses. It was impossible to count them.

It was the first time he had seen so many corpses, and he even felt a sense of grandeur. Searching through all of Lee Sungmin's memories, he had never seen so many, so gruesome and terrible.

He felt a slight dizziness and pressed his temples. The scenery felt too strange, not real at all.

However, it was real.

Everywhere he looked, there were only corpses.

Mountains of them piled high, and bodies strewn about so thickly that the ground was not visible. Monsters.

Were there really this many types of monsters? Certainly, this many would be fitting for the final disaster of The End.

If this many monsters poured out into the world, the world would not be able to withstand it.

Thinking that, this space filled with corpses felt terrifying. At the same time, he felt a sense of awe.

The one who killed all these monsters was Wiji Hoyeon. The same Wiji Hoyeon who had cornered the Queen in a fighting spirit state in the Elemental Realm had been swept up in the Queen's sudden Advent and fallen into this space.

It was a space one could not normally enter. A coincidence, yes. It was a simple coincidence. Caught up in that damn coincidence, Wiji Hoyeon fell into this space and lost the protection of the Demonic Spirit.

How must she have felt?

He suppressed the chilling awe. He did not entertain the thought of 'what if it were me'. The act of killing itself probably would not be that difficult.

Even before his awakening, Lee Sungmin was strong, and he was not weak enough to feel threatened by mere monsters.

No matter how strong the monsters here were, Lee Sungmin was probably stronger.

It went without saying now that he had awakened. The current Lee Sungmin could freely handle Dragonic, and his enlightenment in martial arts had reached its peak.

Whether in one-on-one combat or a chaotic group battle, the current Lee Sungmin was overwhelmingly superior in any kind of fight.

Mass slaughter is easy. It is not difficult. He could annihilate a huge number in an instant just by spamming a few Dragonic Magic spells.

However, could his mind endure it?

He did not know. Honestly, he was not confident. There were an uncountable number of corpses. Tens of thousands? No way. Lee Sungmin chuckled. Even that unit was a laughable number.

This was a world Wiji Hoyeon had created.

What must she have felt? Lee Sungmin recalled Wiji Hoyeon's back as she stood turned away from him.

The Wiji Hoyeon that Lee Sungmin remembered and knew was always strong and confident. She always had conviction in her own actions and never hesitated. There were times when it felt like she did not have the emotion of fear.

However, Wiji Hoyeon was human.

Lee Sungmin knew a side of her that she did not show to others.

If others heard it, they might scoff and tell him not to lie, but Wiji Hoyeon liked flowers and butterflies.

The Fairy Forest, where he spent a year with her, had many flower fields.

Wiji Hoyeon disliked the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, so she did not go near the lakeside where he had made his residence.

Their home was a cottage near a flower field, away from the lakeside.

The time Lee Sungmin returned after receiving teachings from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was always around sunset.

The Fairy Forest had many trees, but there were no tall trees around the area Wiji Hoyeon had chosen as her residence. Thanks to that, the sky was clearly visible.

When the sky was tinged red by the sunset, Wiji Hoyeon was always sitting out in the flower field, waiting for him. The image of her sitting quietly, meditating with her eyes closed, he could still recall it vividly. Amidst the faint scent of wildflowers, Wiji Hoyeon always wore a thin smile.

The Fairy Realm of the forest were inwardly wary of Wiji Hoyeon, so they did not approach, but many butterflies would flutter around her instead.

*I'm doing this for you.*

He recalled the words she had said. His chest throbbed with pain. During the ten years Lee Sungmin was sealed, Wiji Hoyeon had done many things.

Even with the protection of the Demonic Spirit, she was always alone. She did not have a pillar of support like the Void Monarch as Lee Sungmin did.

She did not have companions like Scarlet, Baek Sogo, or the Black Dragon Knight either.

Wiji Hoyeon confronted The End more than anyone else, and she was always alone.

*Do you know?*

*What I came to know. Learning truths I never wanted to know, that my existence in this world was not of my own will, but for someone in a high place.*

*I was angry and annoyed, but you know, in the end, I had no choice but to accept it. Well, there was nothing I could do. I just had to accept it.*

When she said those words, Wiji Hoyeon's voice had been trembling.

*There's no one but me.*

Crushed by the weight of a responsibility she never asked for, she trembled.

Wiji Hoyeon did not even have a choice. In this world, she was the Demonic Spirit's arrangement, a device to end this world's The End.

*What should I do?*

The words she muttered while striking down monsters.

*What should I do?*

He knew then.

That Wiji Hoyeon was not an iron man. In the midst of helpless frustration and despair, she was always lonely.

During the time Lee Sungmin was sealed, Wiji Hoyeon cleared all the dungeons in this world.

What kind of feelings did she have during those ten years? It probably was not arduous, because Wiji Hoyeon was strong. However, was it easy? Was her heart at peace? Was it fun?

*For you.*

He knew all too well what those words meant.

*I was happy when I was with you.*

Wiji Hoyeon was yearning for those days.

Those days of watching butterflies while sitting in the middle of faintly scented wildflowers, under a sky beautifully colored by the sunset.

Those days when she would rise to greet Lee Sungmin, who had returned from training, and say, 'You worked hard'.

Those days when she was not crushed by worries about The End, frustration, despair, or a forcibly given sense of responsibility.

*That's why I'm doing this.*

That was the driving force that moved Wiji Hoyeon.

With the sole desire to return to those days, she wandered the dungeons, invaded the Elemental Realm alone to kill the Spirit Queen, and fell into this space, creating this countless number of corpses.

Lee Sungmin stopped walking.

What he saw was the same. Everywhere he looked, there were only corpses.

However, Lee Sungmin could no longer move forward. He could not believe what he was seeing.

"...What is this..."

Corpses.

There were other corpses.

It was full of human corpses, not monster corpses. Was it an illusion? Lee Sungmin pressed his temples.

He recited Dragonic to clear his mind and circulated his inner qi. Still, it did not disappear. What Lee Sungmin was seeing now was the unvarnished truth.

Why are there human corpses here? Lee Sungmin slowly bent down to look at a corpse.

It was a corpse that had been dead for quite some time, though it was relatively more recent than the monsters' bodies.

And it was unmistakably a human corpse.

"...Ah."

Lee Sungmin made a small sound and looked up.

He could see the red sky, devoid of sun, clouds, stars, or moon.

No matter how much he focused his vision, he could not see beyond it. His heightened senses caught the array of foul odors. A smell, masked by the stench of rotting corpses, became distinct.

"...What is this..."

Why are there corpses here?
