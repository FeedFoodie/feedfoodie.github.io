When Namgung Heewon asked who he was, Lee Sungmin could only stare. The question was simple enough, yet the answer caught in his throat. Before him stood the Sword Dragon, the young master and future head of the Namgung Clan. That alone was startling, but it was the mention of the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, that had truly surprised him.

"…My name is Lee Sungmin."

"Lee Sungmin? Can't say I've heard the name. Ah, my apologies. My knowledge is a bit… selective. I tend to only listen to what I want and tune out the rest. Makes me a bit of an ass, I suppose," Namgung Heewon answered with a shrug. "Perhaps you have a title you go by, something other than your name?"

"I don't. I'm not a martial artist, I'm a mercenary."

"Not a martial artist, but a mercenary. Does that mean you're not from the murim to begin with?"

"I am Classless," Lee Sungmin replied.

At his words, Namgung Heewon's expression hardened slightly. A short silence passed before he let out a stifled laugh.

"So you're even more incredible than I thought. It seems you're already knocking on the door of the Supreme Peak. Am I not right?"

"I am not a martial artist."

"But you've learned martial arts, haven't you?"

"I have, but not like a 'real' martial artist like you…"

"Calling someone a 'real' martial artist is a rather funny thing to say," Namgung Heewon laughed loudly. "I've learned martial arts and I have the Namgung surname, but that doesn't mean I know the 'Central Plains Murim'. My great-great-grandfather was summoned to Eria as an Otherworlder, and he gathered the martial artists with the Namgung surname scattered across this world to establish our clan here. That's how I came to be. I've never been to the Central Plains Murim, nor was I born there. My home is Eria."

He crossed his arms as he spoke.

"Should someone like me be called a martial artist? I learned my manner of speech from a Noble Clan, and my Mu Void Path from a Noble Clan. Does that make me a martial artist? I wasn't born in the Central Plains and then brought to another world."

"But you're closer to a real one than I am," Lee Sungmin replied.

Namgung Heewon laughed once more at his answer. "Perhaps so. Can you use enhanced qi?"

"I can use it."

"But you haven't completed Origin Reversal. You haven't achieved Ascend to the Peak either. Then what about mastery?"

"I haven't achieved it."

"Does that mean your Conception and Governing Vessels aren't open?" Namgung Heewon tilted his head, his expression strange.

Lee Sungmin nodded with a bitter look on his face. "They aren't open."

"…That doesn't make sense," Namgung Heewon muttered, frowning at the answer. "You've reached the stage of enhanced sword qi without even opening your Conception and Governing Vessels? How is that possible?"

"…If the Conception and Governing Vessels aren't open, can you not use enhanced sword qi?"

"In a typical case, yes. To use enhanced sword qi, your blood and qi must withstand the rough flow of inner arts and circulate at high speed. Opening the Conception and Governing Vessels means opening the fastest path for that flow."

"Is it that important?"

"Of course it's important. Using enhanced qi without opening them is an amazing feat, but the efficiency is garbage. The consumption of inner arts also increases."

Only after hearing Namgung Heewon's explanation did Lee Sungmin realize that the 'concentration' process he went through to create enhanced qi was actually unnecessary. He had thought he simply lacked the knack for it, but it was never a matter of skill. His efficiency was just poor because his Conception and Governing Vessels were not open.

"But well… I suppose such cases exist," Namgung Heewon continued. "I've never experienced it myself, but I've heard stories. It happens when the body cannot fully accept the insights one has gained. They say the body becomes unable to follow the martial arts."

"Are you saying that's my case?"

"It's a possibility, but you should hope it's not. Such cases usually… don't end well. The disharmony of mind, body, and spirit will inevitably lead to qi deviation."

Lee Sungmin's face stiffened. He couldn't tell if that disharmony was happening in his body right now, but it certainly sounded like a long-term risk.

"How long are you going to keep talking about such difficult things?" Scarlet, who had been listening with a sullen expression, suddenly blurted out. As a mage, she could not understand most of their discussion.

At her words, Namgung Heewon let out a hearty laugh and bowed his head. "Haha, my apologies. It was a matter of concern, so I got carried away."

"About, uh, the Little Heavenly Demon." Lee Sungmin felt sorry for Scarlet, but he couldn't resist asking about 'her'. "What kind of person is she?"

"Are you interested?"

"I'm not a martial artist, but I know the weight the title 'the Heavenly Demon' carries. Even if she's called the Little Heavenly Demon, a Heavenly Demon is still a Heavenly Demon. …It's simple curiosity."

"Huhu! How much do you know about those called 'the Heavenly Demon'?"

"I know almost nothing."

Namgung Heewon chuckled at Lee Sungmin's reply. "There are three people in Eria currently called 'the Heavenly Demon'."

Just then, the food arrived. Scarlet gave up on stopping their conversation, pouting as she poked at the seasoned vegetables with her chopsticks.

"One is the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon," Namgung Heewon began. "He's a young man just past his coming-of-age, and he's been making a name for himself for about four years. He's active in the permafrost region at the northern tip of Eria. Despite his young age, he reigns as the supreme leader there. It's speculated that he will soon unify the region and advance into other territories."

Lee Sungmin knew of him. Around the time of his own death, Baek Museon had left the North and headed south, though Lee Sungmin did not know what happened after that.

"Another is the Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae. I actually think this man is the one who truly fits the title. He's quite old, and his Mu Void Path is said to have reached the heavens. The Blood Heavenly Demon is certainly a figure who has surpassed the Supreme Peak, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon… he can be called a true transcendent who has reached a level beyond that. In addition, his personality is cruel and his hands show no mercy, so he is more than qualified to be called a demonic practitioner. While the Blood Heavenly Demon reigns as the supreme leader of the North and serves as the Chairman of the Blood Heavenly Alliance that follows him, the Crazy Heavenly Demon commands no followers. He wanders alone. If you ever happen to encounter him, you should run. He is a man without forgiveness."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae. Lee Sungmin knew that name as well. Compared to the other two, however, the name was not as deeply ingrained in his memory. Byeong Wonpae was famous for being arrogant, cruel, and unforgiving, but his severe wanderlust kept him roaming from place to place, never at the center of any major rumors.

"And the last one is the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. If you only look at the title, she might seem inferior to the other two… but that's not the case. In fact, she is a figure shrouded in mystery, so her power cannot be properly gauged. What is certain, however, is that the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon is a monster in no way inferior to the Blood Heavenly Demon and the Crazy Heavenly Demon."

These were the words of the Sword Dragon, and their weight was certainly conveyed to Lee Sungmin.

"It's not clear what kind of person Wiji Hoyeon is. They say she is cruel, unforgiving, merciful, and magnanimous… Haha! It's nothing but contradictory rumors. No one even knows if Wiji Hoyeon is a 'he' or a 'she'. However, they say her strength is real. I hear she hasn't even reached her coming-of-age yet, but she has definitely reached the Supreme Peak. Those three who hold the title of 'the Heavenly Demon' are calamities acknowledged by all."

*He knew.* Wiji Hoyeon was a calamity. More than anyone, Lee Sungmin knew the calamitous nature of Wiji Hoyeon.

"You two." Scarlet, who had been picking at her food, glared up at them. "Aren't you going to eat?"

At her words, Lee Sungmin obediently picked up his chopsticks. Namgung Heewon let out a dry cough and did the same.

It seemed the food of the Central Plains did not quite suit Scarlet's palate. Thanks to that, Lee Sungmin and Namgung Heewon had to finish most of the meal. Despite claiming he had already eaten, Namgung Heewon devoured the majority of the food. As they were drinking the tea that was served to cleanse their palates, he began to speak again.

"I heard you're going to Shaolin."

"Were you eavesdropping on our private conversation?"

"Not exactly. I just happened to overhear. My hearing is exceptionally good, you see," Namgung Heewon replied slyly. "If you're going to Shaolin, how about accompanying me? As it happens, I also have business there."

"May I ask what kind of business?"

"It's to pay my respects to the head of Shaolin, Grandmaster Bulyeong. The four prestigious clans, including the Namgung Clan, visit him once a year. It's because of an old fighting spirit promise."

The four prestigious clans referred to the Namgung, Moyong, Tang, and Jaegal Clans. Lee Sungmin was curious about this 'old fighting spirit promise', but he felt it would be too tactless to ask.

"I've started to hate this city," Scarlet grumbled after they finished their meal and left the restaurant. She had been looking forward to the food, but her mood had soured completely. Her original plan to tour the city was abandoned, and they decided to head straight for Shaolin.

The city of Bredon was so large, however, that the sun had set before they reached the western gate leading to Mount Song. Thinking it would be a nuisance to knock on Shaolin's gates late at night, they found a room at an inn to wait for morning.

"Ah, if you don't mind."

Perhaps it was because they were near Mount Song, or because there were many martial artists, but a spacious training ground lay behind the inn. Seeing it, Namgung Heewon turned to Lee Sungmin and spoke.

"Would you be willing to have a light duel?"

The invitation caught Lee Sungmin by surprise. He had not expected Namgung Heewon to suggest such a thing. After a moment's thought, he nodded.

"…If it's just a friendly match."

"Of course it will be," Namgung Heewon said with a bright smile.

Scarlet, declaring she was not interested, went up to her room. Fortunately, the training ground was empty, allowing the two of them to face each other right away. After a word with the innkeeper, they were able to borrow a wooden sword and a staff, both without blades.

"I thought you were a spearman," Namgung Heewon muttered, lightly swinging the wooden sword.

Lee Sungmin had taken off his Magic Armor and stood in light clothing. A spear and a staff were different, but using a bladed weapon in a friendly duel was too dangerous.

"To be honest, I prefer a real duel, but with no observers, that kind of duel would be too dangerous. So let's just take it easy and have some fun."

"I'm not sure I can live up to your expectations."

"Haha! You're too modest. Well, we'll know once we start."

Saying so, Namgung Heewon took his stance. The air around him shifted. His affable expression vanished, replaced by a sharp focus that made his eyes gleam fiercely. He had simply adopted a sword stance, yet it was as if he had become a completely different person. A palpable aura flowed from him, and Lee Sungmin, gripping the staff with both hands, swallowed hard.

One thing was certain.

Namgung Heewon was strong.
