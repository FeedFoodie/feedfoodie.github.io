Lloyd led them into a reception room that was, despite the tower's emptiness, utterly spotless. Not a speck of dust marred its surfaces, and teacups had been set out for their exact number as if they had been expected all along.

"We still have some time before the Spirit Queen's Advent," Lloyd said as he settled into a chair. "Her spiritual body is lost in the rifts of space, searching for an exit, and unfortunately, there's nothing we can do to help her at this point."

"Even with Gries' magic?"

"Gries isn't all-powerful. Some things are simply impossible."

From the folds of his robe, Lloyd produced Gries, a magic tome with a worn and ancient cover.

"The Spirit Queen's Advent is a foregone conclusion," he continued. "While martial arts theory can predict the exact time, she will soon make her Advent in the Sleeping Forest."

"...Ten years ago, Lord Abel tried to change the fate of The End by connecting the Elemental Realm to this world. The method is different now, but hasn't this encroachment from the Elemental Realm altered that fate?"

"It hasn't changed," Lloyd said, letting out a sigh. "This isn't a complete connection. It's a forced encroachment, an invasion. A phenomenon of this scale won't be enough to change the fate of The End that this world carries."

A sliver of disappointment pricked at Lee Sungmin. He had been clinging to a one-in-a-million chance, but it seemed the world's fate was not so easily escaped.

"So what about the other Spirit Kings?"

Lloyd placed a hand on Gries, infusing it with his magic power without a moment's hesitation.

"...The Water and Earth Elemental Kings haven't made their Advent yet, but it'll be soon. Considering the distance, our best bet is to move immediately and attack them the moment they appear. We can reverse-summon them that way."

"What about the Spirit King of Flame?"

"...The Spirit King of Flame is..."

Lloyd closed his eyes, using Gries to locate the Spirit King of Flame. It should not have been a difficult task, for he was tracking a being that did not, and should not, exist in this world.

After a moment of concentration, Lloyd's eyebrow twitched.

"...Annihilation?"

Bewilderment laced Lloyd's voice. At his words, Rubia popped out from Lee Sungmin's arms in surprise.

"What? Annihilation?"

"Unless I'm mistaken... no, there's no doubt. I can sense the traces of his Advent, but he's vanished completely."

"Not reverse-summoned?"

"No. This feels different from the Spirit King of Wind's reverse-summoning. This is... complete annihilation."

"No way... That can't be possible..." Rubia muttered in disbelief.

While the Spirit Queen was one thing, the Spirit Kings who ruled the Four Great Spirits were not transcendent beings. Still, like transcendent beings, they were far removed from death. Annihilating a Spirit King or any Elemental outside their own realm was a feat considered nearly impossible.

"When an Elemental takes a fatal blow, it's supposed to return to the Elemental Realm," she whispered. "That's... an absolute rule..."

"Was it Wiji Hoyeon?" Lee Sungmin asked after a moment of silence.

Making the impossible possible. In this situation, Wiji Hoyeon was the only person he could imagine.

"No."

He had asked with a degree of certainty, but Lloyd's denial was so swift it was almost embarrassing. Lloyd frowned, stroking Gries.

"Wiji Hoyeon... Even Gries can't sense her presence. Some kind of blessing is protecting her, wrapped around her in several layers."

Demonic Spirit.

"She might already be in this world. Or perhaps, like the Spirit Queen, she's wandering the rifts of space."

"If you can't sense her... isn't it possible that Wiji Hoyeon was the one who defeated the Spirit King of Flame?"

"That's not it. I sensed who defeated him."

*I can't believe it,* Lloyd muttered under his breath.

"...When I first met you. I had been chasing the former master of the Black Magic Tower, Preskan, for a long time. I finally found the dungeon where he'd holed up. I was certain I could kill him there, but..."

*No way.* Lee Sungmin realized who 'she' was.

"The Chimera..."

"You know of her? That makes this simpler. Yes. The Chimera Preskan created is the one who defeated the Spirit King of Flame."

A chill ran down Lee Sungmin's spine.

Aine.

He recalled her name for the first time in a long while. He had clashed with Aine several times, and on each occasion, he had failed to find an opening to kill her. The last time he had seen her was when he went to meet Jeniela with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard, the image of Aine pierced by dozens of spikes flashing in his mind.

"...Where is she now?"

"Are you planning to chase her?"

"She has to be killed the moment we get a chance. The potential Aine holds is dangerous."

"I wouldn't recommend it," Lloyd said, his brow furrowed. "The Vampire Queen is by her side, and not just her. There are quite a few other vampires with them. You still want to go?"

Lee Sungmin was at a loss for words.

"It worked?" Gemini exclaimed, a look of faint surprise on his face.

Jeniela stood with her arms crossed, a gentle smile playing on her lips. Behind her, the twin brothers Chen and Kun stood with their heads bowed.

A short distance away, the robed figure of Preskan had collapsed to the ground, his entire body trembling. He trembled not from fear, but from a thrilling ecstasy. He could scarcely believe what he had just witnessed.

One of the Four Great Spirit Kings.

The Spirit King of Flame, said to be particularly violent and powerful among them, had just been defeated. Not reverse-summoned, not annihilated.

Devoured.

There was no other way to describe it. Recalling the miracle his creation had just performed, Preskan felt his unbeating heart pound as if it would burst. If a Lich were an Undead capable of expressing emotion, he was certain he would be shedding thick tears, his face contorted into the happiest smile the world had ever seen.

"How could a psycho like that create such a Chimera?" Gemini whispered to Jeniela with a cynical expression.

Jeniela chuckled in response. "A fluke."

There was no other way to describe it. As a Black Mage, Preskan's skills couldn't compare to the Arch Lich Arbes. Of course, their fields of expertise were different. Arbes himself, impressed by Aine, had once pushed Preskan to recreate her using the exact same method, but he had failed. That was why she was a fluke. A Chimera like Aine could not be recreated, even by following the exact same process.

"...A fluke..." Gemini muttered under his breath, his gaze fixed on Aine, who stood in the center of a crater.

Just a moment ago, Aine had defeated the Spirit King of Flame in that very spot. The Spirit King had wielded lava so intense that merely looking at it felt like it would burn one's eyes from their sockets. She had initiated the fight the moment he made his Advent, before he could receive help from other Fire Elementals, but the Femud Volcano itself, a place of ceaselessly boiling lava, was the perfect location for the Spirit King to exert his full power.

She had not overwhelmed him, yet in the end, it was Aine who had survived and devoured him.

She stood in the middle of the crater, clutching a stomach that gurgled and boiled. Ten years had passed, but she had barely grown, still possessing the appearance of a young girl. She could have matured if she wished, but Aine felt no dissatisfaction with her current form and refused to do so.

"Aren't you giving her too much?" Gemini whispered to Jeniela. "She devoured the Spirit King of Flame. That means the Chimera can now use his flames at will. Can you handle that?"

"Are you asking if I can handle her after only this?" Jeniela scoffed at his subtle question. "And, Gemini. I want that Chimera to become so strong that I can never handle her. I've put in so much effort over the past ten years, but I couldn't find a satisfying meal. Now, though, there's plenty to eat, isn't there?"

Jeniela pointed a finger at Aine.

"Still, she's not enough. Not yet. I can't exactly say she's a satisfying meal."

"Then, the other Spirit Kings too?"

"That seems difficult."

It was a practical reason. The time it would take to travel from the Femud Volcano to the other locations was considerable. No matter how exceptional Jeniela and her vampires were, they could do nothing about the distance.

"We won't make it in time for their Advent, and they'll probably be subjugated by the time we arrive. They have the Fairy Horse, after all."

Jeniela was conscious of Lee Sungmin. She knew she could not compete in speed with the Fairy Horse, which could leap through space. That was not the only problem.

"This is the last one, right?"

"......"

Jeniela gave a slight nod in response to Gemini's question. She had lost her Eye of Futuresight ten years ago, and the future she had seen before its loss ended here, with the retrieval of Silent Flash Baek Sogo in the Snowfield and the Spirit King of Flame's Advent in the Femud Volcano. Though she could no longer see what was to come, this much was certain.

"Everything has gone as I wished so far."

The future she had seen had come to an end. The path where the Slaughter Predator appeared had been closed off, but Jeniela felt no despair. Until this point, everything had gone as she had seen and wished, with only the ending slightly changed. In fact, Jeniela preferred this altered future. Rather than the advent of some unknown Slaughter Predator, she preferred this, creating one with her own hands. The enjoyment of it sent shivers down her spine. After being cooped up in the northernmost region of Trabia for hundreds of years, her current reality was an even greater pleasure.

"...Hoo!"

Aine finally walked out of the crater. Having devoured the Spirit King of Flame, she now held an unprecedented power within her body, incomparable to before she challenged him.

Overcome with emotion, Preskan approached Aine. Jeniela watched them for a moment before turning away.

"Let's go."

"Where to? Back to Trabia?"

"No," Jeniela said with a gentle smile.

{sep}

The Queen.

Knowing the Queen was with Jeniela, Lee Sungmin had no choice but to abandon any thought of pursuing Aine. Jeniela was not alone. To pick a fight with her and all her vampires would be suicide with his current strength. Even if he brought everyone from the Fairy Forest, victory was far from guaranteed.

*It seems Ju Won and the Lycanthropes aren't with them, though.*

Still, it was impossible. Or rather... this could be an opportunity of a different kind.

Stroking his teacup, Lee Sungmin fell deep into thought. Jeniela, the vampires, and Aine were all at the Femud Volcano, a location not far from North Trabia. This meant it was the perfect time to cut off Jeniela's isolated limbs.

*The Blood Demon.*

Lee Sungmin thought of the Blood Demon, a figure he had never met. He was the second vampire Jeniela had dispatched, the one sent to rule the Blood Alliance.

*Nero.*

Ju Won's trusted subordinate, the ruler of Cheped.

*Who should I strike first?*
