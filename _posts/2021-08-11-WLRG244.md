He disliked the situation, but that was no reason to ignore the monster charging at him. He especially couldn't ignore it when he agreed with Void Monarch's assessment that this was a 'strange one'. The creature was unlike any monster Lee Sungmin knew, and its speed surpassed them all.

*What kind of monster is this…*

The demonic beasts he had already encountered in this forest were as strong as a typical peak master, if not stronger. The high-level Undead and Chimeras scattered among them were powerful enough to be considered on par with a Supreme Peak expert, yet the monster now closing the distance was on another level entirely.

Lee Sungmin raised his right hand and flicked his fingers at the rapidly approaching creature. A long bolt of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the same enhanced qi that had slaughtered countless demonic beasts, Undead, and Chimeras, shot out. The monster's body, however, was not pierced. The creature simply swung its right hand wide, swatting the bolt of qi aside.

Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched at the sight.

SWOOSH!

Swinging claws descended upon him. Lee Sungmin did not dodge. Instead, he raised his Protective Qi Aura, and a purple barrier wreathed in electric currents enveloped his body.

The aura trembled slightly. Though the blow was blocked, he could clearly feel its immense weight and power.

A disbelieving laugh escaped him.

*What kind of power is this?*

In terms of physical ability alone, it was comparable to the Transcendent Realm. Of course, comparing it to a true martial artist of that realm would be ridiculous. These monsters, once high-ranking members of the Demon Race, had lost most of their intellect and past power, with only their physical abilities barely remaining.

Taking a small step back, Lee Sungmin drew the spear from his back.

CRACKLE!

The enhanced qi of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, generated by the Violet Dawn Divine Art, coiled around the spear shaft. He did not know where this monster came from or what it was, but two thoughts struck him at once. *It's different. It's dangerous.*

If a monster with this much power existed, Scarlet could truly be in danger.

He did not hesitate. In a more relaxed situation, he might have leisurely continued the fight out of curiosity, but now was not the time for such indulgence.

Lee Sungmin's eyes grew cold, sinking with a chilling light. Thin currents of electricity formed around the purple enhanced qi gathered at the tip of his spear. He unleashed the third move of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang. It was the only martial art he had learned that did not incorporate the insights of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

'There's no need to touch it,' the Chairman had assessed. Even to an absolute master like him, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art was a well-crafted enhanced qi art. Lee Sungmin had never forgotten the Crazy Heavenly Demon's last wish to not forget the art, and he had not neglected his training in it even while studying under the Chairman.

His handling of it might be less refined than that of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had practiced it for many long years. In terms of sheer power, however, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art wielded by Lee Sungmin, who had entered the Transcendent Realm and mastered the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, was already far stronger than when the Crazy Heavenly Demon performed it himself.

KABOOM!

A massive explosion threw the monster's body backward. The strike he had unleashed was powerful enough to inflict a fatal wound on even a Transcendent Realm expert if it landed squarely.

Nevertheless, the monster endured. It had no Protective Qi Aura, nor did it dodge or use any other means to reduce the impact. Aside from a deep crater in its chest where the blow had landed, the creature seemed unharmed.

*What in the world is that?*

[This Elder doesn't know either. It's a very… strange one. A creature with that much power should have some intelligence. Why don't you try talking to it?]

Heeding Void Monarch's suggestion, Lee Sungmin called out, "You. What are you?"

No answer came. Perhaps it could not hear him. It did not matter. He was not particularly curious or concerned about where the creature came from. He had planned to ignore it and walk away, regardless of whether it tore the Holy Knight Commander to pieces. The creature, however, seemed to have no intention of ignoring him. In that case, he had no choice but to get rid of it.

His grip on the spear tightened. He had failed to kill it in one blow, but now he understood it was tougher than he had expected.

The monster roared. Its body, consumed by instinct, moved to hunt. To Lee Sungmin, its movements were simplistic. The leaping swing of its claws was slow. By the time the electric currents from its attack scattered, Lee Sungmin was already behind it.

The spear held in both hands rotated lightly. He chose the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art's Blood Ring Shattering.

And speed.

The unique martial art of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, pursued Extreme Speed. While the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art did not, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear that Wiji Hoyeon had taught him in his youth had many shortcomings in the eyes of a great figure like the Chairman. No matter how much of a calamity with outstanding insight Wiji Hoyeon was, it was impossible for her to completely overhaul a top-tier Spear Technique at the mere age of thirteen.

The fifth move of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Deathly Flash, was the fastest of its nine techniques, but Deathly Flash, enhanced with the principles of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, transcended the realm of 'fast'.

Deathly Flash, lightning.

Watching Lee Sungmin's spear from not too far away, Tang Ahee doubted her eyes. The Holy Knight Commander was the same, unable to even perceive the spear's movement.

A single flash of purple light, and the monster's head was gone.

It was not just the head. There was also a hole in its left chest where its heart should have been. In that brief moment, two spear strikes had left their marks.

The headless monster staggered and collapsed. Its limbs twitched a few times, but it did not get up again. Even its powerful regenerative abilities could not restore a completely destroyed head and heart.

"The Phantom Spear…" the Holy Knight Commander groaned, clutching his spilling intestines as he looked at Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin could not hear the man's voice, but seeing his lips move, he sent a Thought Transmission.

[You should treat your wounds.]

[Why… why'd you save me?] the Holy Knight Commander stammered in reply.

Lee Sungmin frowned at his words. [I didn't intend to save you. I only killed it because it attacked me.]

[Are you going to kill me?] the Holy Knight Commander asked, swallowing hard.

Lee Sungmin shook his head. [You're not important enough for me to go out of my way to kill you. Besides, not killing you will put my mind at ease. Didn't you suspect me of being an agent of the Vampire Queen?]

The Holy Knight Commander was left speechless by the smirk that accompanied the question.

[…I'm sorry for doubting you.]

[I wasn't looking for an apology,] Lee Sungmin said as he walked past him.

The Holy Knight Commander hesitated before sending another Thought Transmission. [That monster… it was of the Demon Race.]

[What?]

[The Demon Race… I had a strong feeling. I never knew they could fall so far, but it must have once been a powerful member of their race.]

[Are you saying Kim Jonghyun summoned a Demon?]

[I suppose so. If that's the case… we must stop this ritual, no matter what.]

[That doesn't sound like my job.]

At Lee Sungmin's reply, the Holy Knight Commander flared up, about to say something. He bit his lip, however, holding back the tirade he had in mind.

[…The debt for saving my life, someday, if I make it out of here alive… I will be sure to repay it.]

[Do as you please.]

The mention of the Demon Race heightened Lee Sungmin's sense of urgency. He did not know how many more of those monsters had been unleashed in this forest, but from his experience fighting one, the subjugation force would have a difficult time handling them, especially in their current scattered state.

*I have to find her quickly.*

{sep}

This entire forest was under Kim Jonghyun's control. That, however, was now coming to an end. It was a grand magic accomplished by sacrificing three thousand souls, but it was impossible for it to last forever. If he were to continuously offer souls, he could extend the duration indefinitely, but the souls of the dead and the fear gathered here had to be used for the main ritual.

*It's about time.*

The grand magic would end soon. He felt neither regret nor a sense of crisis. By summoning demonic beasts, Undead, Chimeras, and even a fallen Demon, he had annihilated the majority of the subjugation force. The only survivors were mages on the level of a Magic Tower Master or Supreme Peak experts like the head of the Moyong Clan.

*You're an exception.*

He had seen Lee Sungmin easily defeat one of the Demons. Frankly, Kim Jonghyun did not want to fight him. He held a genuine fondness for Lee Sungmin. If the situation turned sour, however, it could not be helped. Kim Jonghyun smiled wryly at the thought.

Having taken everything from Arbes, he was a being who stood in an absolute position in the field of magic. It might have been a different story if Envirus were still around, but with the Archwizard having suffered a fatal wound and departed for the Elemental Realm, there was no mage left who could threaten Kim Jonghyun's position. Even if the magic spread throughout the forest was lifted, he had more than enough power to deal with the remaining subjugation force.

Knowing that, Kim Jonghyun ended the magic he had cast over the forest without any lingering attachment.

The ritual had already been performed. The gate had been summoned, and the half-open gate was slowly, continuously opening wider. Enough high-quality souls and fear had been gathered. The Grimoire was emitting a more ominous black light than ever before.

{sep}

The darkness enveloping the forest disappeared.

Scarlet was exhausted, but she had been lucky. She could not have been anything but lucky, for when Kim Jonghyun gave orders to the summoned Demons, he had made an exception for her and the party she was with. If Scarlet were killed, Lee Sungmin would go on a rampage. The other members of the subjugation force were not a big problem, but if Lee Sungmin went wild, things would become difficult.

Unaware that she was alive thanks to Kim Jonghyun, Scarlet looked up as the darkness faded and let out a breath.

"Is it over?" she muttered.

In front of her, Doberman let out a groan, his shoulders slumped. "Aigoo, I'm dead."

He had fought with all his might. Although his reputation was poor, Doberman had not abandoned Scarlet and fled just because the situation was dangerous.

"I'm gonna have to ask for more pay," he grumbled, turning his head now that he could hear her voice again. "I never thought I'd have to go through this much crap."

Scarlet pouted at him. "You should direct your request for additional pay to the Mages Guild, not me. I have no intention of paying you out of my own pocket."

"Now that's just too harsh. We crossed the line between life and death together, however briefly. Shouldn't that build some camaraderie?"

"Talking about camaraderie while asking for more pay is just ridiculous bullshit."

"That's how mercenaries are," Doberman chuckled.

{sep}

As the darkness vanished, Lee Sungmin's senses expanded. He did not know why the darkness had suddenly disappeared, but rather than dwelling on it, he immediately pushed his senses further. He could feel the presences throughout the entire forest.

Ominous presences.

They were the presences of the 'Demons' the Holy Knight Commander had mentioned. Four of them. They were all moving simultaneously toward a single location.

That place… a chill ran down his spine. It was an ominous feeling he had felt before, back when Preskan tried to offer him as a sacrifice. He had felt the same dread then.

*Kim Jonghyun.*

Kim Jonghyun would be there, and the rest… Lee Sungmin frowned as he assessed each of the presences he could detect.

*There are few survivors.*

The subjugation force, which had numbered five hundred, had been reduced to a few dozen. They had not even met Kim Jonghyun yet, and already there had been so many casualties.

*The subjugation is a failure.*

A disastrous failure. It was not that the subjugation force was complacent, but that Kim Jonghyun had prepared far too much.

*Where is Scarlet?*

Lee Sungmin continued to expand his senses. He found her. A look of relief washed over his face. Thankfully, Scarlet was alive.

"Let's go," he uttered without looking back at Tang Ahee, who was following him.

He had to leave this forest with Scarlet before things got any more troublesome.
