"…Is this really okay?"

Jang Deuksu's voice was laced with reluctance, a sentiment Chwi Geol clearly shared. There was no way they could feel good about this. What they were about to do, whatever the reason, was by no means the right thing.

"It's suicide for the three of us to try and stop Wiji Hoyeon."

"That… I know that, but…"

"We've failed. Doesn't someone have to survive and report back?"

Chwi Geol's voice dropped low, a wretched mix of shame and humiliation, yet it was also laced with the self-comforting logic of inevitability and a desperate will to live.

"I can't let Miss Baek die, and I can't die either. For the Beggars' Gang… for the Beggars' Gang."

He knew it was just an excuse, a way to justify running away because he was terrified of dying. Chwi Geol's gaze drifted to the corpse of the Blood Granny. Even that cruel demonic leader had begged for her life in the end, pleading to be saved, crying that she didn't want to die.

*I don't want to die. Everyone feels the same way. It's because you only have one life.*

"And you, Jang Deuksu?"

"…I don't want to die," Jang Deuksu muttered, his voice deflated. "I don't. I want to live, even if it means living with the shame."

What was the point of saving face now? At his words, Chwi Geol let out a bitter laugh and reached into his clothes, pulling out a rolled-up scroll.

"We were lucky."

It was the scroll the Blood Granny had possessed, still bearing flecks of flesh that suggested she had torn it from one of the dungeon's Doppelgangers. Using an artifact of Baek Sogo's, they had analyzed the magic engraved upon it.

*Dungeon Escape.*

The scroll held a spell for immediate escape from the dungeon, usable by up to three people. It was a spell the Blood Granny herself hadn't understood until the moment of her death.

"…Miss Baek will resent us for this…"

"I'll take responsibility for it," Chwi Geol replied, his eyes finding Baek Sogo slumped against a wall. He was the one who had ambushed her, sealing her acupoints to render her unconscious while she was still shouting about saving her Junior Disciple, Lee Sungmin.

*Please resent me.*

The thought was sincere as he recalled his brief meeting with Lee Sungmin. Then, with a final, heavy resolve, he tore the scroll.

[Please designate the people to use this with you.]

In response to the voice that echoed in his head, Chwi Geol spoke their names. Jang Deuksu, and Baek Sogo.

{sep}

It hurts.

By the time the pain registered, Lee Sungmin was already rolling across the floor, with no time to even analyze the attack that had sent him there. Inside his head, the Void Monarch roared.

[Snap out of it! It's still coming!]

Lee Sungmin scrambled to his feet just as the Doppelganger lifted its foot high and stomped. The fleshy floor rippled like a wave, sending a tremendous force surging toward him. Gritting his teeth, he threw himself aside.

KWAAAAANG!

The spot where he had been standing a moment ago was completely devastated.

[What do we do? Uh, what do we do?] Rubia's voice buzzed anxiously in his mind.

*I don't know.* Lee Sungmin swung his spear in a desperate arc. KWAANG! The Doppelganger's palm force slammed into the shaft, twisting his left wrist with a sickening wrench. His entire arm throbbed, the sensation in it dulling to a faint buzz.

*My hand…!*

He could barely put any strength into it, but he forced the issue. Centering his grip with his right hand, he spun the spear once, sending the shaft and then the spearhead descending upon the Doppelganger in a fluid sequence.

The Doppelganger's face remained indifferent. It was a copy, weaker than the original, but that didn't make it any less of a monster. By shedding the Black Dragon Armor, the Doppelganger—no, Wiji Hoyeon—was acknowledging him as an opponent. She had been toying with him until now. From this moment on, she would genuinely try to kill him.

For Lee Sungmin, this was a terrible misfortune. His attacks were deflected with absurd ease. Clad in a black Protective Qi Aura, the Doppelganger was an incarnation of destruction, obliterating everything in its path with the slightest movement, even if it wasn't the real Wiji Hoyeon.

The outstretched hand looked huge. A slight flick of the wrist created hundreds of afterimages. *A feint? An illusion? A fake? Where is the real one?*

[Ten to the right of the center, seven to the left…]

The Void Monarch's warning came too slow. Wiji Hoyeon's attack was simply too fast to comprehend and counter based on words alone. Instead of trying to intercept a single strike, he spun his spear to block them all, a decision that made him a fraction too late. The impact sent him flying backward, blood spraying from his mouth.

[How inconvenient,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

*What do I do, what do I do… If I die here, I won't be able to meet my master…* From beyond his consciousness, Rubia kept muttering.

"Just… shut up for a second."

Lee Sungmin gasped for breath as he pushed himself up, his legs trembling slightly.

[You ungrateful brat. If I hadn't taken part of that attack for you, your body would have exploded from that last blow.]

He was right. Considering he had taken the blow head-on, the sheer pressure behind it should have been unbearable. It had been surprisingly survivable.

*Why did you block it for me?*

[Because it would be troublesome for me if you died here. I can't very well take over a body that's been turned into a pile of flesh, and there's something I want to ask you,] the Void Monarch answered.

Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet. The Doppelganger blinked, as if surprised that he had gotten up at all.

[A considerable amount of time has passed. However, the woman you sent to escape has not returned.]

*I know.*

He wasn't an idiot. He knew Baek Sogo hadn't returned with Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu. He knew a considerable amount of time had passed, and still, no one had come.

*Did Baek Sogo betray me?* He didn't think so, though the truth was that he didn't want to think so.

[Don't you resent it? Don't you regret it? If you hadn't come here, you wouldn't have to die in that wench's place. Frankly, I don't understand. You've already died once, haven't you?]

There was no time to recover. The Doppelganger attacked again, its right hand shooting out. *A fist or a palm? No, a knife-hand?* Sharply honed enhanced qi aimed for his throat. Lee Sungmin spun the spear in his right hand.

KAGAGAGAK!

Enhanced qi ground against enhanced qi, and Lee Sungmin was the one forced back. Rather than fight for balance, he shifted his entire stance. At the same time, he spun his spear and followed up with Serpent Soul Devouring, striking from the bottom up.

It didn't work. The Doppelganger shifted its body slightly, perfectly evading the trajectory of his attack.

[Having experienced it, you should know better than anyone how sudden and empty death is. Surely you're not thinking you can return this time as well?]

*Of course not.*

He answered and immediately lunged forward. Shadowless Soul Severance. Two Step Destruction. Erupting with enhanced qi, he unleashed the fourth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Nine Dragons Slaughter. A stream of enhanced qi, filled with clear killing intent, swept toward his foe.

She had taken off the Black Dragon Armor she used for defense. The Doppelganger didn't defend but moved forward, using only her right hand. The Wiji Hoyeon it was imitating was that kind of person. She had acknowledged him and removed her armor, but she wouldn't use a butcher's knife to kill a chicken. She would only use her right hand.

[Answer me. Do you regret it? Do you resent that wench?]

*I.*

He spat out the answer in short bursts as the Doppelganger shattered his Two Step Calamity and Nine Dragons Slaughter with a single palm strike. It leaped through the scattered fragments of enhanced qi.

*I don't regret it, and I don't resent her. In the first place… if I hadn't met my Senior Sister, I would have died eight years ago.*

He had recklessly entered Mount Mushii, placing excessive restrictions on himself out of overconfidence. If Baek Sogo hadn't helped him then, he would have died on that mountain.

*My Senior Sister saved my life. While living with her, I came to think that I didn't want to let her die. That's why I came here, and why I stayed behind.*

[Did you fall for that wench or something?]

*You're talking nonsense.*

[Hahaha!]

The Void Monarch laughed heartily, as if he found something incredibly amusing. Rubia was still muttering nervously, and the Doppelganger's attacks were fierce.

[I've taken a bit of a liking to you. I can't let you die, so how about I help you out a little,] the Void Monarch said in a boastful tone.

Lee Sungmin didn't pay close attention to his words. The situation was the worst it could be. Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger, now using its martial arts in earnest, was a monster on a level he couldn't possibly handle. Nine years had passed since they parted. Was this how much she had grown? Lee Sungmin let out a wry laugh.

*Even if I ignite my life qi, I'm no match for her. Is it… too late to run? Should I try to push forward?*

[You can't run away with an enemy like that at your back.]

*Winning is difficult. I think I told you. I don't want to die.*

[You don't have to die.]

The Void Monarch laughed loudly as crimson flames erupted from the Magic Armor covering Lee Sungmin's body. Startled, Lee Sungmin unconsciously took a few steps back.

[As long as there's a medium, drawing out power is a simple matter!] the Void Monarch declared with a burst of laughter.

The flames spewing from the Magic Armor covered Lee Sungmin's body, but he felt not the slightest bit of heat from them.

"You… what are you doing?"

[Hahaha! Speaking to me so informally without permission. Well, it doesn't matter. I've taken a bit of a liking to you.]

The Doppelganger lunged. The same powerful palm technique that had shattered his Nine Dragons Slaughter and Two Step Calamity rushed toward him. As she struck, a massive wave of enhanced qi swept over Lee Sungmin, but before he could react, the flames surged forward to meet it.

[Behold! This is a true mysterious phenomena!] the Void Monarch shouted with a laugh.

KWAAAAANG!

Power clashed with power. Wiji Hoyeon's enhanced qi vanished as if it had never existed. The flames didn't retreat an inch and surged forward. The Doppelganger hurriedly raised both hands, forming a blood-red sphere between its palms. Holding it in front of its chest, it threw the sphere forward like a ball. KWAAAAANG! As the two powers of different calibers collided, space itself trembled. Lee Sungmin staggered and retreated.

"What the hell is this…!"

[You're slow to understand, you fool! I am manifesting a portion of my power. If you faced that half-wit alone, you'd just end up dead!]

"Are you… helping me?"

[That's right! I can't let you die! However, this isn't very efficient. I'll lend you my power. So try using it as you please.]

The moment the Void Monarch finished speaking, the surging flames scattered and seeped into Lee Sungmin's body. In that instant, he understood. What he had seen in the Sleeping Forest wasn't mere flames. What had just pushed back Wiji Hoyeon's attack wasn't fire either. It was the immense power that the Void Monarch commanded.

[It is Demonic Energy. A power that you humans normally cannot handle. However, you should be able to wield it. Your heart seems closer to that of a Yokai than a human.]

The Void Monarch's Demonic Energy settled into his body, and his heart devoured it. Lee Sungmin gasped. The fatigue that had weighed down his entire body vanished. The throbbing pain was gone. His broken ribs and wrist? They moved perfectly fine.

Through the power now dwelling in his heart, Lee Sungmin understood the nature of Demonic Energy. It was neither inner arts nor magic power. Demonic Energy was a simple, brutish power that knew only destruction, an inhuman force that humans could not wield. Demonic Energy and inner arts, which should have been impossible to combine, now coexisted within his body. They intertwined but did not mix. As the two powers, like oil and water, flowed through him, Lee Sungmin felt a pain as if his qi and blood were being torn apart.

[Endure it!] the Void Monarch shouted.

The Doppelganger lunged. Its opponent's status had been elevated from a plaything to an enemy, and now to a worthy rival. It began to unleash its full power. Its body split into five as it rushed forward, attacking from all directions. Each one was a real body with substance. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and moved his spear.

Homing Chain Lightning.

The Homing Chain Lightning, now mixed with Demonic Energy and inner arts, was incomparable to the one he had used before. With a series of explosions, the Doppelganger's clones were shattered. The main body escaped via teleportation.

[Behind you!] the Void Monarch announced.

He knew even without being told. The Demonic Energy had sharpened all of his senses beyond their normal limits. Lee Sungmin swallowed a groan and turned. He couldn't swing his spear. The Homing Chain Lightning he had just unleashed contained terrifying power, and his arms, unable to withstand its force, had their bones shattered.

However, Lee Sungmin knew this wasn't a major problem. The shattered bones were already regenerating at an impossible rate. It was, however, a problem for the immediate moment. The Doppelganger that had moved behind him was thrusting both fists toward his chest.

Traceless Step. Lee Sungmin's body vanished. Before he could even complete the step, teleportation activated. Though astonished by the incredible speed, he controlled his body. Two steps from Traceless Step. Two Step Calamity. The enhanced qi, a mixture of Demonic Energy and inner arts, exploded outward. The Doppelganger hurriedly raised both hands to defend against the attack coming from its side, but it couldn't block the blast completely. Its body was pushed back.

In that moment, Lee Sungmin's arms regenerated. He took a breath and unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

The fifth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Deathly Flash.

It was a thrust containing such extreme speed that it produced no sound. It was an attack that approached the ideal thrust that had killed the Sword Phantom, a state Lee Sungmin had desperately tried to reach in the Mental World. He had already reached it again there, but it was an attack he couldn't properly execute with his physical body in reality.

Not anymore. His body, aided by Demonic Energy, a body that could now perform movements that were once only martial arts theory, perfectly executed the fastest thrust, Deathly Flash.

The target was the very center of her chest.

Lee Sungmin's spear pierced through the center of the Doppelganger's chest.
