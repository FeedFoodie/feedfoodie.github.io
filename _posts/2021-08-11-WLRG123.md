The sight of Aine using a Protective Qi Aura did not surprise him. Though Lee Sungmin did not fully understand the Black Heart's function, he remembered her using the Hundred Steps Divine Fist. He also remembered the corpse of Zeon, his heart torn clean from his chest.

Aine had been a tricky opponent then, but she was a different beast entirely now. Five years ago, she had been powerful yet inexperienced, giving the distinct impression that she was unfamiliar with the very act of fighting.

That was no longer the case. Her attacks and movements now completely disregarded the limits of a human body. Her limbs twisted at impossible angles while tentacles and blades erupted from her flesh with a terrifying unpredictability.

They clashed again, enhanced qi colliding with enhanced qi in a scattering spray of power. Aine refused to back down, pressing her assault relentlessly. Dozens of tentacles shot from her back, rushing toward Lee Sungmin like a tide. He spun the spear in his hands and unleashed the Nine Dragons Slaughter. Forged from Orichalcum, the spear's projection became nine dragons, more powerful and ferocious than ever before as they swept toward Aine.

Her hand twitched. It swelled to a disproportionate size, imbued with a dense, enhanced qi.

KWAANG!

The nine dragons exploded along with Aine's hand. Her entire right arm was torn from its socket and sent flying, yet she did not even scream. In place of her right arm, she thrust with her left. The moment she did, her hand transformed into a massive awl. Lee Sungmin retrieved his spear and immediately thrust it forward again, his Soul Chasing Elimination colliding with her transformed hand.

"......"

Power crashed against power. Lee Sungmin endured the shockwave that spread through his entire body and took a step forward.

One step, then two. He unleashed the Two Step Calamity. The enhanced qi enveloping his body exploded outward, creating a violent storm. His killing intent materialized as part of that qi, seeking to crush Aine's entire body.

In response, Aine's movements became a model of simple efficiency. It was a completely mastered martial art, a set of movement arts she executed flawlessly.

PAPABAK!

Her body created dozens of afterimages, allowing her to slip free from the Two Step Calamity's range. The scattered images of Aine then merged back into one. She let out a joyful laugh and leaped at Lee Sungmin again.

Her tentacles were dozens of swords and spears at once, possessing the sharpness of every kind of blade imaginable. Facing the killing intent that now filled the entire space, Lee Sungmin poured strength into both his arms and unleashed the Nine Dragons Slaughter once more. His qi center was overflowing with inner qi, and the Orichalcum spear allowed him to wield them with greater efficiency than ever.

The greatest difference between them was the resilience of their bodies. Aine's form could be destroyed time and again only to regenerate without issue, but Lee Sungmin's could not. If her arm was torn off, it would simply grow back. If his was torn off, that would be the end of it.

This created a fundamental difference in their boldness. Lee Sungmin moved to avoid serious injury, while Aine fought with no regard for her own well-being. It was the same now. She leaped toward the Nine Dragons Slaughter without a shred of hesitation.

KWAKWAKWAKWA!

The nine dragons shot out, sweeping across the snowy field and swallowing her body whole.

*She just had to protect her heart.*

Aine had learned many things over the past five years. Though she could not use magic, she had absorbed numerous martial arts. In truth, it was less a process of learning and more a natural growth befitting a predator, but in addition to that growth, she had learned other things as well.

She learned about pain. She also learned how to use this body of hers efficiently.

Aine's entire existence was sustained by her heart. Her limbs could be blown off, her head could be smashed, but as long as her heart remained intact, she could regenerate indefinitely.

The nine dragons tore her limbs from her body. Having lost her arms and legs, Aine's torso staggered in mid-air before she spread the wings on her back wide. In an instant, her severed limbs regenerated. The same was true for her head, which had been half blown away. Aine bared her teeth in a grin and immediately leaped toward Lee Sungmin again.

It was at that moment.

"Uhahaha!"

A loud laugh echoed as a massive storm of power swept over them. The force was so great that it compelled both Lee Sungmin, who was wary of Aine's next move, and Aine, who was charging recklessly forward, to fall back. They flinched, froze, and retreated at the same time.

KWAANG!

All the snow in the vicinity evaporated. Amidst the torrent of rising white steam, the Crazy Heavenly Demon pushed himself to his feet. He had been watching from a distance but could no longer hold back and had simply jumped in. It didn't matter why. He had thrown himself into the fray, driven by an excitement and impulse that made the blood in his veins boil.

"Where is this place?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked, spreading his arms wide.

Aine didn't answer, glaring at the newcomer, while Lee Sungmin felt unabashedly flustered by the man's sudden appearance.

"You are...?"

"This one asked the question!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon roared. It was a massive Lion's Roar, backed by a profound inner strength that scattered the steam completely and shook the very ground. The shout was powerful enough to rattle the bodies of both Aine and Lee Sungmin.

"I asked where this place is. If you want an answer to your question, answer this one's question first!" he shouted again. His attitude was arrogant, but the fighting spirit surging from his body was impossible to ignore.

"...The North... It's Trabia..."

"Just as I thought!"

He had guessed as much from the falling snow and the massive city walls not far away. Now that he had confirmation, the Crazy Heavenly Demon nodded with a pleased smile.

In truth, despite his laughter, his inner thoughts were not so pleasant. How could they be, when he had lost his sanity and somehow ended up in this snowy northern land? The Crazy Heavenly Demon, however, didn't let it show.

That was that, and this was this. He had originally intended only to ask for his location, but he had changed his mind. Witnessing a fight of this intensity would stir even an unshakable heart. The Crazy Heavenly Demon strode forward, making no effort to hide his surging fighting spirit.

"Now. Let's play with this one too!"

Despite his shout, Lee Sungmin and Aine could only feel bewildered. An unknown man had suddenly appeared, radiating an immense fighting spirit as he approached, and they had no idea how to react. Lee Sungmin glanced toward Aine. Even she, who had been charging forward so recklessly, was hesitating, unable to act.

In fact, the most flustered of all was Preskan, who watched the situation from the sky through his familiar. He felt he was just a little closer to retrieving the stolen heart, but the unknown man's sudden appearance had tangled everything into a complete mess.

"...First, who are you?"

"This one is the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae!" the man boomed with another Lion's Roar.

At that name, Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae. He was a monster, one of the three figures in present-day Eria who held the title of Heavenly Demon, alongside the Little Heavenly Demon and the Blood Heavenly Demon. Lee Sungmin had heard he was a demonic practitioner who wandered endlessly, committing indiscriminate massacres. To think he would run into that very man here.

*If you meet the Crazy Heavenly Demon, run.*

He recalled the advice Namgung Heewon had given him years ago at Shaolin. Back then, Namgung Heewon had added that the Crazy Heavenly Demon was the one who truly deserved the title.

Aine was displeased. Just when it seemed she could finally have some fun, this man had appeared and ruined everything.

*...No. It's still fine.*

Her eyes filled with killing intent as she unleashed the power within her body. A massive, uncontrolled force engulfed her. The Crazy Heavenly Demon saw this and laughed heartily.

"Excellent!"

As if in response to his shout, Aine leaped forward. The Crazy Heavenly Demon, as if he had been waiting, thrust his outstretched arms toward her. An unprecedentedly massive force stirred, becoming a great pressure that swept over Aine's body. Until that point, she had not been particularly wary. She was used to destroying her own body to forcibly create an opening in an opponent's defense.

This, however, was different.

Just before the pressure hit her, Aine realized it too late. She would die. This was not the kind of attack she could break through by throwing her body at it. If she did, she would be shattered to pieces. Aine hurriedly shot her tentacles forward, but their tips disintegrated into dust the moment they touched the pressure. Not stopping there, she spread her wings and twisted her waist a full one hundred and eighty degrees. It was a joint movement no ordinary human could ever perform, but Aine didn't care. She moved without hesitation, escaping the range of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's pressure.

KWAANG!

The sound and scale of the explosion were beyond imagination. The pressure created a huge hole in the ground and shook the earth as if an earthquake had struck.

"......"

Preskan also saw that the man's attack possessed a power beyond imagination. He commanded Aine in an urgent voice. At his shout, she hesitated for a moment. The power of the attack was indeed incredible, but she judged that it wasn't enough to make her run away. Aine still had many hidden tricks and was confident she could fight on equal terms if she brought out everything she had.

[No! Run right now!]

Preskan's command, however, was unwavering. Just as Aine had hidden moves, it was highly likely the Crazy Heavenly Demon did as well. Besides, he wasn't the only one to be wary of. Aine's original purpose was not to fight him, but to take back the Black Heart that Lee Sungmin possessed. While it was true she coveted the Crazy Heavenly Demon's heart as well, her chances of survival against both of them were not very high.

[Hurry!] Preskan yelled.

Aine bit her lip and pulled back. It was the last thing she wanted to do, but she was no fool. She could judge for herself that the current situation was not in her favor. Spreading her wings wide, she glanced at Lee Sungmin before leaping into the air.

Their gazes met for a brief moment before she shot up into the sky. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was about to fire his pressure at the fleeing Aine but stopped. He had forgotten his age and jumped in, asking her to play, but seeing her turn her back and run away so easily cooled his excitement.

"Hmph."

He let out an indifferent grunt and turned sharply to Lee Sungmin. It didn't matter. Playing with the wench who ran away might have been fun, but in truth, the one who truly interested him was not Aine, but Lee Sungmin.

It was a very strange feeling. He was sure he had never seen the boy before, yet something deep in his chest was boiling. It was an unfamiliar emotion, even for someone who had lived as long as he had. He did not know the identity of this emotion, but he didn't care. He didn't know when he might lose his sanity and go on a rampage again. Before that time came, he wanted to enjoy this moment of clarity.

"Are you going to run away too?"

"I don't understand why you're picking a fight..."

"What a stupid question!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon responded to Lee Sungmin's calm words with a roar.

The thunderous shout reminded him of Yeokbalsan Jang Deuksu from the Dungeon, but the power contained in this man's voice was incomparable. Namgung Heewon had been right. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was a martial artist who had stepped into a transcendent realm beyond the Supreme Peak. The fighting spirit emanating from him was so dense it made it difficult to breathe, and his two eyes, flashing with a sharp light, made every hair on Lee Sungmin's body stand on end.

"The strong have met the strong. A martial artist has met a martial artist. Do we need a reason to fight?!"

It was a shout Lee Sungmin could not comprehend. He tried to say something more, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon was no longer listening. He took a large step forward, a movement that could not be described as merely 'fast'.

It was simply heavy.

KUUUUNG!

An earthquake erupted from just a single stomp of his foot. Lee Sungmin's body, prepared for the tremor, didn't shake, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon leaped without hesitation toward him. Instead of the immense pressure that had made Aine feel death, the man's two hands filled Lee Sungmin's vision. He hurriedly rotated his spear, trying to deflect the attack.

He wanted to, but.

KUUUNG!

The impact of the collision shook his insides. He hadn't even taken a direct hit, only deflected it, yet the force rattled his internal organs. In terms of sheer weight, the power in the Crazy Heavenly Demon's pressure was greater than that of the Wiji Hoyeon Doppelganger.

[This is...] the Void Monarch uttered in surprise.

Lee Sungmin was just as surprised. The pressure that had seeped into his body shook his organs but failed to destroy them. Deep within his qi center, the Demonic Energy of the Void Monarch, which was not his own, stirred and reacted.

[Don't run. Capture him!] the Void Monarch shouted urgently.

[That bastard! Why the hell is he using my Demonic Energy?!] he roared in a furious voice.
