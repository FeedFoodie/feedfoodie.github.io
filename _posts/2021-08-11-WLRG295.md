A faint vibration shivered through Osla's wings, summoning countless particles of light that rose around her like a swarm of fireflies. At a gesture from her, the shimmering lights drifted forward and enveloped Lee Sungmin.

The mask Osla had crafted was designed to completely suppress Demonic Energy, but its power extended far beyond that. Even a warrior as mighty as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would find his full strength shackled if he wore one of her creations. Osla herself had called this a Sealing. With her help, the risk of his yokai nature running rampant after absorbing the Chairman's power through the Black Heart would be greatly reduced.

[It is not perfect.]

Void Monarch's warning echoed in his mind. Lee Sungmin had not forgotten, and it was the same thing Osla had told him. A complete Sealing was impossible. This was merely a stopgap, a way to put out an urgent fire. Even after the Sealing was in place, he would have to continue searching for a permanent solution. The process would suppress the explosive power of his yokai nature to its absolute limit, but if that Sealing were to break later, he would be utterly unable to handle the backlash if he had not found another way.

He knew and understood all of this.

Lee Sungmin reached for the red orb of light that had emerged from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's chest. This was certainly a much better way. He was still not free from the sense of betrayal, of abandoning his own human morality, but it was infinitely better than the gruesome alternative of tearing out the heart himself, of chewing it and tasting the blood. He finally understood what Osla meant when she said the method was important. Had he chosen a more gruesome path, he would have been disgusted with himself first and foremost.

There would be no immediate change. Lee Sungmin wrapped both hands around the essence of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power. It was just as it had been when he consumed the Dragon Heart. A power so immense placed a heavy burden on his body, which was why the Black Heart applied new strength slowly, giving his system enough time to adapt. This was the greatest difference between Lee Sungmin, a human with a Black Heart, and Aine, whose very body was constructed from one.

However, by consuming this power, he would gain potential.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had advised and warned him several times that his growth had reached its absolute limit. Lee Sungmin had already fully realized the martial state he had attained in Denil's Mental World, and by learning the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, he had even taken a few steps beyond it. This, however, was as far as he could go. Both Void Monarch and the Chairman had acknowledged it.

Becoming stronger would be difficult. To advance further, to reach a higher plane, required an immense calamity that far transcended a mortal's journey. It demanded a commensurate amount of effort and destiny. One could not advance with effort alone if they lacked talent, nor could they succeed with both if they lacked destiny. Countless calamities challenged this truth, but only a precious few ever reached the Transcendent Realm. Among those few, only a chosen handful could ascend even further.

Inheriting the power of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the man who had reached the farthest and highest place, meant obtaining a definite possibility. For Lee Sungmin, who had no more room for growth, it meant gaining new potential. Even if he could not seize great power immediately, that potential alone was enough.

How much of it he could make his own was now up to him.

Lee Sungmin took a deep breath and pulled the light cradled in his hands toward himself. It felt much the same as when he had eaten the Dragon Heart. He could feel no taste.

An explosion ripped through his body.

RUUUUMBLE!

A sharp intake of breath was all he could manage against the searing pain of his insides tearing apart. He knew exactly what he had to do. Lee Sungmin immediately dropped into a cross-legged position and began to circulate the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, now mixed into his technique, started to guide the immense power that had flooded his body.

It was a tyrannical force that crushed everything in its path, making him feel as if his blood vessels were being torn and tangled. The qi center it eventually reached could not hope to contain the Chairman's power.

GRRRRUMBLE...!

The thunderclap that echoed from within his body was the sound of his qi center shattering.

SCREEE... SCREEECH...

A ringing filled his ears, an unpleasant shriek like sharp nails scratching a steel plate.

*It's coming.*

It was Void Monarch who warned him. The Demonic Energy was moving. Squirming inside his body, it rose up all at once.

WHOOOOSH!

A pillar of raw purple energy erupted from where Lee Sungmin sat cross-legged, rocketing into the sky until it slammed against the barrier Osla had woven around the forest.

RUUUUMBLE!

The barrier protecting the Fairy Forest shook violently. Blocked by the shield, the Demonic Energy scattered in all directions, dyeing the vicinity in a wicked purple light.

"Good heavens…!" Scarlet shuddered, watching the ominous energy envelop the area.

With a firm expression, Osla spread her wings wider and flew a little higher. As she moved her wide-open hands, the deepening flow of Demonic Energy was suppressed.

While his body poured out all its Demonic Energy, Lee Sungmin focused his mind. The rampaging energy had escaped his control, and from the world of his unconscious, his yokai nature began to crawl upward. The method had been different, but in the end, he was now devouring his Master. For Lee Sungmin, this act held great meaning. It was the first time he had done such a thing since gaining the Black Heart, since his body had transformed, since he had first experienced the danger of his yokai nature running rampant.

He was devouring a fellow human, a Master he had sincerely respected.

That unchanging fact, more than any psychological factor, created an opportunity for his yokai nature to lash out.

*Suppress it.* Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth.

While Osla fought to contain the physical manifestation of his rampaging Demonic Energy, Lee Sungmin waged his own war within. He reached into the depths of his unconscious and grabbed his own yokai nature by the throat. The face of the rampaging Yokai, baring its sharp teeth, was his own.

*It's not your time to come out. 'I' have to do this.* He chanted the words like a spell deep in his heart.

KYAKYAKYA... A child's laughter echoed in his head, just as it had when he resonated with Aine.

*Whose laughter is this? No, I know. The voice is different, and the age is different, but… this is my laughter.*

The Demonic Energy, now suppressed by Osla, returned to Lee Sungmin's body. The laughter in his head faded. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's power no longer felt like it was shattering him from the inside. The pain subsided, and his consciousness floated upward. The incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder filled his mind completely.

Then came the memories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The essence of his martial arts flowed into Lee Sungmin, not as a simple recollection but as a deep engraving upon both his conscious and unconscious, a legacy that could never be forgotten.

It was not over yet. Osla brought her hands together and presented them toward him. A transparent barrier of light enveloped his body. Inside it, Lee Sungmin's face held a serene expression.

The incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. The pinnacle that the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon had reached as a human. The essence of his martial arts. Everything the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had wanted to leave him, everything he had consciously passed on in his final moments as his last will, was now inherited by Lee Sungmin.

When he finally opened his eyes, the forest was dark with night.

Lee Sungmin quietly got to his feet. Thanks to the Sealing Osla had engraved upon him, his Demonic Energy did not run rampant. He observed the inside of his body. His internal energy had increased significantly, but for having inherited all of the Chairman's power, the change felt small. Just as with the Dragon Heart, his body could not immediately assimilate and fully inherit everything.

He had known it would be like this. It was enough. He could be satisfied with just gaining potential. Even in his final moments, the Chairman, as his Master, had shown him the path he should take.

"…Where did my Master go?"

"That… I don't know either," Osla replied. "The place the Coalition Leader headed to is not of this world. It's a place I can neither observe nor interfere with. It's the path taken by beings who have further transcended the true meaning of a transcendent, enabling them to reach the absolute big picture realm."

Inside Lee Sungmin's head, Void Monarch quietly listened. The absolute big picture realm. That path. He could have taken that path too. He simply had not gone because he was worried about leaving Lee Sungmin behind. Even now, he knew he could walk that path anytime he chose.

*Not yet. I can't leave him yet.*

"What are you planning to do now?" Yehwa asked in a small voice.

At her question, Lee Sungmin looked down at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's body. After a moment of silence, he answered. "My Master wished to be buried in this forest."

"The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance… What will you do about it?"

"I'm not a suitable vessel," he answered, shaking his head.

It was just as the Chairman had written in his last will. Lee Sungmin knew very well that he was not the right person to lead a massive organization like the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. He also knew that even if he tried to maintain it, he would struggle to handle and utilize its members efficiently.

"I plan to disband the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance and order all the sects belonging to it to seal their gates for the time being."

"There will be a huge backlash."

"I don't intend to force them. If they don't want to, they don't have to seal their gates. Now that my Master is dead, the Alliance can no longer serve as a shield, and I'm not good enough to take his place."

He did not know what changes the Chairman's death would bring. Perhaps the Martial God would now step forward to crush the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance completely. The Chairman must have known that could happen, yet he had still left the choice to Lee Sungmin through his last will.

"What my Master wanted to protect was not the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. It was you, Yehwa, and the rest of his Royal Guard."

"......"

"Please follow my Master's last will."

At those words, Yehwa squeezed her bloodshot eyes shut. She slowly bowed her head.

"…We might be of some help," she continued in a trembling voice. "The Royal Guard, including myself, whom our lord personally took in… we all had terminals in our heads connected to him. Most of us have been controlling the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance by serving as branch leaders."

Lee Sungmin recalled his first encounter with the Chairman in the giant dao city of Ruvus. Gwan Hu had been the branch leader there. Through the terminal implanted in his head, the Chairman had spoken with him for the first time. Judging by Yehwa's words, Gwan Hu must have been one of the Royal Guard as well.

"I know we're lacking in martial power. There isn't a single master of the Transcendent Realm among us, but the intelligence network and numbers we have…"

"It's alright," Lee Sungmin said, not letting her finish. "My Master took your lives, and he already wrote in his last will how those lives should be used. Yehwa. You and the other members of the Royal Guard who are indebted to my Master must act according to his will. Just as I am."

His voice was quiet, but it held a firmness that would not accept refusal. Yehwa could no longer argue and bowed her head deeply.

Lee Sungmin recalled the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and the Chairman's enlightenment now engraved in his mind, lost in thought for a moment.

*It didn't run rampant, but… it's an incomplete Sealing. I don't know when it might burst.* As he was now, he would not be able to handle it if it did. *I haven't even achieved Body Reformation.*

The Chairman had told him. The biggest difference between himself and other Transcendent Realm masters was another Body Reformation. He had said that because his entire body had become a qi center, he could execute any martial art faster than anyone else. That power had been proven in his fight with the Martial God. Although the Martial God was incredibly strong, he had been a step below the Chairman.

"What's your plan?" Scarlet asked.

"What will you do, Scarlet?" Lee Sungmin countered with a question of his own.

"I have to go with you, of course."

"It'll be more dangerous to travel with me."

"I'm doing it because I think you'll be lonely," Scarlet said with a chuckle.

"…One week." Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky with a dazed expression. "I think I'll need about that much time. I'm curious what kind of rumors will spread. I should also see what happens in the South during that week. What the Martial God will do, and what happened to the Black Dragon Knight and… the Spear King."

And.

"I need to sort out my thoughts," Lee Sungmin muttered, pressing his temples. "So much has come in, it feels like my head is going to explode."
