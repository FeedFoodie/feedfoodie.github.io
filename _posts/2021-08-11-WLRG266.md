"What an interesting conversation."

Laughter echoed from the darkness. Before Baek Sogo or Lee Sungmin could even register his presence, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was already standing on the pavilion. His hand reached for the gourd bottle on the table.

"What fine liquor," the Chairman exclaimed, his voice full of admiration as he smelled the aroma rising from the gourd.

He lifted an empty cup with a flick of his wrist, manipulating space to pour the liquor. As the cup filled, his eyes shifted behind his mask to settle on Baek Sogo.

"'I will eradicate evil. I will get rid of all villains.' Huhu! Aren't those incredibly arrogant words?"

The Chairman's sudden appearance did not fluster Baek Sogo. Even as he mocked her to her face, her expression remained perfectly calm.

She met his stoic gaze as he slightly lifted his mask and tilted his cup. After emptying it, the Chairman let out a sound of appreciation.

"I thought you were a completely incompetent disciple, but it seems you have good taste in liquor. It's deplorable that you'd hide such fine alcohol and not offer any to your Master."

"You never asked for it."

"Do you only think and act when you receive a direct request? This is why I call you a fool. If you have such good liquor, isn't it proper to offer a cup to your Master?"

"I'll offer you a cup now, so please stop throwing a tantrum."

"Calling your Master's words a tantrum, you rotten brat."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman grumbled and pushed the gourd bottle toward Lee Sungmin. With a deep sigh, Lee Sungmin stood. He took the gourd with both hands and refilled the Chairman's cup.

"Good and evil can be reversed depending on one's perspective, Silent Flash Baek Sogo," the Chairman continued, his tone laced with amusement. "You said you would eradicate all evil in this world. Who is it that defines the evil you seek to eradicate as evil?"

"I do," Baek Sogo answered the mocking question without a flicker of hesitation.

At her response, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed once more. He could not help himself.

"You? Are you saying you possess the standard to accurately distinguish between good and villainy?"

"No, I don't. I'm well aware that my own standards are narrow-minded."

"And yet you still say you'll eradicate evil?"

"......"

"You're insane." The Chairman let out a dry laugh and shook his head. Even knowing her own words and actions were distorted, Baek Sogo did not hesitate in the slightest. She simply stared intently at the masked man before her.

"I will follow a universal perspective," she stated, "and only seek to eradicate what the majority calls evil."

"If the powerful gather and speak with one voice, the ignorant masses will believe lies to be truth. Look. Most people call my incompetent disciple here a demonic practitioner. Is that the truth?"

"There will be those who are wronged," Baek Sogo conceded. "I don't intend to unconditionally eradicate something just because it's called evil. I will meet them and experience them for myself. If there are unfair circumstances, I will fully consider them."

"Silent Flash, you speak of a naive and ridiculous ideal. What you're saying now is no different from a child clamoring about creating a just world."

"That may be so." Still, there was no wavering in her answer. "However, I'm not a child."

"That's why it's more dangerous. There aren't many in this world who can stop you. It's dangerous because someone like you is acting no different from a child."

"I'm trying to do the right thing."

"And who will judge that what you're doing is right?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked with a scoff. "What if you yourself become evil at some point? Your actions, the justice you execute, the eradication of evil. What will you do if all of it is wrong?"

"I would die." It was a statement that required no thought. "If I become that way, I will kill myself."

"You're insane," the Chairman muttered, clicking his tongue. He understood then that no matter what he said, he could not persuade this half-mad woman. He narrowed his eyes, glaring at her. "If I am evil, will you kill me?"

"If it were possible, I would have," Baek Sogo answered, once again without hesitation. "But not now."

During the few days she had spent there, she had come to clearly understand what a monster the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was, what terrifying martial prowess he possessed.

"And... I'm not sure if you're a villain."

"Most people call me the king of demonic practitioners. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is a den of demonic practitioners, and I stand at their pinnacle, yet you say I'm not a villain?"

"I told you. I have no intention of unconditionally eradicating someone just because the majority calls them a villain." After replying, Baek Sogo turned her gaze to Lee Sungmin. She bowed her head slightly toward him. "Junior Disciple, do you also think my words are a ridiculous ideal?"

"...What answer are you expecting from me?"

"I'm not hoping for you to say that my words are right. I, too, know how absurd my desired ideal is," Baek Sogo muttered as she stood up. "The group called Heaven Beyond Heaven. The first time I heard about their true nature... was from you, Junior Disciple. I don't sympathize with their ideals, but I can't call them evil either. Even if their methods are wrong, what they speak of is for the sake of humanity."

"Are you defending Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

"Junior Disciple, why are you fighting them?" Baek Sogo asked in return. "You clashed with them when they tried to kidnap the Little Heavenly Demon. After that, they framed you. You hunted them so you wouldn't be killed by them."

"That's right."

"What if the misunderstanding is resolved? What if they no longer try to take the Little Heavenly Demon by force? What if the animosity between you and Heaven Beyond Heaven disappears?"

"Are you saying we could all gather, laugh and joke, and become friends who share drinks?"

"It might turn out that way. Relationships can always change," Baek Sogo said with a bitter smile. "I can't think of them as unconditionally wrong when they are for humanity."

"Are you trying to say that Heaven Beyond Heaven were the good guys after all?"

"I'm saying it's a possibility."

"...Senior Sister. I think we've come too far to turn back, and I... I can't possibly imagine myself walking alongside Heaven Beyond Heaven."

"......"

"Because your Junior Disciple is a monster."

It was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman who answered her. At his words, Baek Sogo's face froze.

"...What do you mean?"

"Exactly what I said," Lee Sungmin sighed, continuing his Master's words. He caressed the left side of his chest, where his heart was beating. "I... have become closer to a non-human existence. If Heaven Beyond Heaven's goal is to annihilate the inhuman, then I am included among the inhuman they wish to annihilate."

Unlike before, Baek Sogo could not answer right away. An obvious tremor ran through her eyes as she looked at Lee Sungmin. After a moment of hesitation, she managed to squeeze out her voice. "...Junior Disciple, why did you become like that?"

"Because I was weak."

He had thought about it several times. What if he had never obtained the Black Heart? What if he had never met the Void Monarch? Even if he had learned divine arts from Wiji Hoyeon, he would not have become as strong as he was now. At his current age, he might have barely reached the Supreme Peak. Denir's training would have been a variable, but he was skeptical he could have surpassed the wall of the Transcendent Realm without the help of the Black Heart or the Void Monarch.

"I know it's unorthodox. However, I had no choice. I don't... regret it either."

"Junior Disciple..."

"I don't regret it," Lee Sungmin repeated.

At his words, Baek Sogo remained silent for a long time. During that heavy silence, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman snatched the liquor bottle from Lee Sungmin and emptied cup after cup.

"This liquor tastes truly fine."

[He knows a thing or two.]

"However, this isn't the atmosphere for enjoyable drinking. I'll go drink alone, so you two can talk amongst yourselves."

Leaving those words behind, the Chairman disappeared with the liquor bottle. It happened so fast that Lee Sungmin did not even have a chance to claim the bottle was his.

[That son of a bitch!]

Void Monarch shouted.

"...Junior Disciple, are you a monster?" Baek Sogo asked, her voice cutting through the Void Monarch's curse.

"I'm probably closer to a monster than a human."

"What kind of monster?"

"A Yokai."

"Junior Disciple, do you practice cannibalism?" she asked another question. Most inhuman did. Not all of them, but a significant number had no choice but to do so to maintain their existence, like Vampires who sucked human blood or Yokai who ate human flesh.

"I don't."

"...Have you ever?"

"Never."

"Junior Disciple... you said you're not a complete monster. That must mean you're somewhere between a human and a Yokai. Answer me, Junior Disciple. Don't lie... be honest. Which of the two do you want to be?"

"I want to remain human," Lee Sungmin answered without falsehood.

For Baek Sogo, that was the right answer. The tension in her cheeks eased as she let out a sigh and nodded.

"...Junior Disciple. I'm going to leave this place." She immediately spoke of her departure. "Of course, I don't mean I'm leaving today. I... wanted to see you again, Junior Disciple. I wanted to meet you, talk to you, and ask for your forgiveness, and... if possible, I wanted to be with you."

"...Senior Sister."

"I know. I know our situations are different," Baek Sogo said, shaking her head.

"...Where do you plan to go?"

"I'm thinking of going to Shaolin."

Shaolin. The name brought to Lee Sungmin's mind not the gates of the temple, but the old Grandmaster Bulyeong and Jihak, the young man called the future of Shaolin. Indeed, those two would be close to the righteous figures 'most' people spoke of. Lee Sungmin had met them both in person and could not imagine them being involved in any conspiracy or connected to Heaven Beyond Heaven.

"Why are you going to Shaolin?"

"Mount Tai and the Big Dipper. That's what they call Shaolin and Wudang. Even if the Murim Alliance has been corrupted, I can't believe that all the orthodox sects are evil. So... I'm going to Shaolin. Because Shaolin is the Mount Tai of the orthodox murim," Baek Sogo replied, reaching into her sleeve. She pulled out a gray bracelet. "I asked Scarlet for it. It's the same kind of bracelet she gave us."

"Is it for when we're in danger?"

"That's part of it, but I also wanted to leave a token of our connection, Junior Disciple."

Lee Sungmin took the bracelet from her. Baek Sogo held up the matching gray bracelet on her own wrist and smiled brightly.

"Of course. If you're ever in danger, Junior Disciple, no matter where you are, I'll come to save you as fast as I possibly can."

"The world is a wide place," Lee Sungmin chuckled, wrapping the bracelet around his wrist. "If you're on the opposite side, you won't be able to come save me even if you know I'm in danger."

"Then don't get into dangerous situations," Baek Sogo replied with a matching smile.

When he opened the door to the annex and entered, the heavy smell of reagents still lingered inside. Lee Sungmin looked to the end of the hall, but Scarlet was nowhere to be seen. Instead, only her voice echoed from beyond the corridor.

"Sorry. I'm at a crucial point right now, so I can't leave my spot, but I'm using amplification magic, so you can hear me clearly, right?"

"......"

"So. Did Baek Sogo leave?"

"Yes. She went to sleep."

"I thought she'd leave right away, but I guess not."

"It would be heartless to leave so suddenly, wouldn't it?" Lee Sungmin replied as he walked down the hall. After navigating the long corridor and turning a corner, he entered the living room.

The space had been transformed into something completely different from when he had used the annex. All sorts of magic tools were scattered about messily. In a large pot, an unidentifiable, sticky liquid bubbled and boiled.

In the middle of this room, which was reminiscent of a witch's workshop from a children's story, the red-haired witch stood with a look of clear exhaustion, controlling the flow of Mana.

"Are you alright?"

"How could I be? I haven't slept for two days."

"What are you working on?"

"Would you even understand if I told you?"

"I wouldn't."

"Then I won't tell you. It's just frustrating and a waste of breath to explain it to someone who won't understand anyway," Scarlet muttered, her hands moving in intricate patterns. Red magic power gathered in her palms, then slid out to combine with streams of a different color. Her lips moved in a silent chant as she raised an index finger, tracing glowing runes in the air.

Lee Sungmin pulled up a chair and sat at a distance where he would not disturb her. Scarlet continued chanting, combining Mana, and writing runes for a long time. She finally stopped when dawn had passed and morning had arrived.

"Got nothing better to do?" she asked as she turned around. She picked up a towel that had been tossed aside and roughly scrubbed her sweat-drenched face and hair. Then, she smacked her lips and muttered, "I should just take a bath. I haven't washed in two days, I stink."

"Can't you just use magic?"

"Bathing isn't just about washing your body to get rid of the smell. I want to soak in a hot tub and relax," Scarlet grumbled and plopped down onto a chair. While massaging her stiff shoulders, she glanced at Lee Sungmin. "Don't you dare peek."

"I won't," Lee Sungmin grumbled in disbelief.
