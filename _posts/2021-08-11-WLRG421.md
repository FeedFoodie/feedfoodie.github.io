RUUUMBLE!

A jet-black light plunged from the sky. Its very existence was on a different plane, a stark contrast to the false divinities and imitation transcendents of Eria. This was a being of true divinity.

Lee Sungmin flinched, his eyes fixed on the entity that had materialized before him. An unpleasant feeling stirred deep in his gut, a sensation he had felt once before, a very long time ago.

"The Demon King of Fear," he breathed.

"So you remember." A sinister voice leaked from the shimmering darkness, where a pair of crimson eyes surfaced.

It was the same Demon King of Fear with whom Preskan had forged a contract. Lee Sungmin had briefly encountered the being once before, deep within Preskan's Dungeon.

"I thought the Demon God of the Great Demon World would come," Lee Sungmin remarked.

"Watch your mouth." The darkness surged as the Demon King's malice locked onto him. Lee Sungmin felt his skin tingle and his brow furrowed in response. "A being like you has no right to utter his name. Besides, do you think this weak world could possibly withstand his Advent?"

He could feel both the Demonic Spirit and the Spirit trembling within him. The Demon King of Fear was a Demon Lord of formidable power, acknowledged even in the Great Demon World. All Demon Lords were originally transcendents, and among them, the Demon King of Fear ranked high.

*Still... compared to what I thought...*

He knew the Demon King possessed terrifying power, yet he felt a surprising sense that it might be manageable. Of course, he would only know for sure after they fought.

"For someone who talks so big, you seemed to be in quite a hurry," a new voice mocked.

RUMBLE!

Another being Advented with a sensation that was quite familiar. From within a dazzling light, a creature from the Fairy Realm rose, its magnificent wings spread wide. It appeared to be one of the Fairy Kings who resided in the realm connected to Eria.

"Seeing as you were the first to arrive," the Fairy King continued.

"Shall I rip off those uselessly large wings for you?" the Demon King of Fear snapped back at the sarcastic remark.

The Fairy King only scoffed. "Why don't you watch your actions? You've come as a representative of the Great Demon World, after all. A needless provocation could lead to war."

"Do you think we fear them?"

"Don't make a scene," another voice interjected. The newly Advented Spirit King made his presence known, his gaze landing on Lee Sungmin the moment he appeared. "So you're the human who annihilated Sarahenne."

"Get your facts straight," Lee Sungmin corrected him. "I wasn't the one who annihilated her. It was the Spirit."

The Spirit within him couldn't say a word. The Spirit King stared at Lee Sungmin for a long moment before speaking again. "I have no intention of blaming you for her annihilation. From the moment she broke her promise and decided to interfere with this world, she was no longer under the protection of the Elemental Realm."

A sigh of relief escaped the Spirit. Lee Sungmin surveyed the beings who had Advented. The Demon King of Fear, the Spirit King, and the Fairy King. All of them were 'true' transcendents, and while they were certainly threatening, it was only to that extent. If Absolute Beings on par with the Demon God had Advented directly, Lee Sungmin knew he wouldn't even be able to stand straight.

"Everyone's just testing the waters," the Demon King of Fear sneered, noting that not all the beings had yet appeared.

A sense of anxiety crept over Lee Sungmin. This was not the situation he had hoped for.

"Don't you have any shame?" the Fairy King chided the Demon King. "Especially after you've already failed once."

At the word 'failure', the killing intent flowing from the Demon King swelled. In the past, the Great Demon World had used Kim Jonghyun and the Grimoire in an attempt to connect Eria to their world. Had Kim Jonghyun succeeded, Eria would have become their colony.

"Are you aware that you are in a position to make a deal with us?" the Spirit King said, his tone serious. "We have heard everything you've said. We all came here because we acknowledged your words."

"Indeed," the Fairy King added. "This will be the first and last time. No matter how many times this Slaughterhouse is opened and closed in the future, it will be impossible to achieve the same results as this time."

"There's no way a half-breed like you will be born again," the Demon King of Fear sneered. "I'll give you a chance to submit to the Great Demon World. Think carefully, half-breed. I call it a chance, but if I wanted to, I could do it by force. I'm only letting you choose for yourself because your existence has value."

"A chance to submit?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Become a Demon Lord," the Demon King answered immediately. "There's no need to worry about the method. You offer your soul, and that's the end of it. What you want is to change the fate of this world, right? That's an easy task. All you have to do is connect this world to the Great Demon World, just like that bastard Kim Jonghyun tried to do."

"If that happens, this world will become a playground for demons," the Fairy King interjected. "Human... no, should I call you human? Then again, calling you a half-breed is too undignified." The Fairy King grumbled to himself as the Demon King scoffed at his words. "In any case, I know full well what you're thinking. Indeed, you are the greatest product that could be born from this Slaughterhouse, just as you said."

The Fairy King's eyes narrowed. "However, the greedy Demon God of the Great Demon World is hell-bent on increasing his colonies. In fact, he tried to make this world a colony through the Grimoire in this very cycle."

"Watch your mouth," the Demon King of Fear spat, trembling with rage.

The Fairy King ignored him. "It won't go as you wish."

At those words, a muscle in Lee Sungmin's cheek twitched.

"Even if this place is neutral ground, only the three of us can Advent," the Fairy King explained.

The Great Demon World, the Fairy Realm, and the Elemental Realm were the three largest worlds that had contributed power to Eria's formation, and only they were directly connected to it. This was the reason why fairies, elementals, and black mages contracted with Demon Lords could exist in this world. The other worlds merely provided a moderate amount of power and nothing more. The Saintess, Teresa, having her connection to her god severed was proof of that. With no direct link, communicating with her god was impossible.

*I see.* Lee Sungmin finally understood why the situation he had expected wasn't happening.

"We've already finished our discussion with the Elemental Realm," the Fairy King continued. "For now, we've agreed not to close the Slaughterhouse. I can't say for certain until when, but this Slaughterhouse will be maintained for a sufficient amount of time."

"You should know that's not what I want," Lee Sungmin stated flatly.

"Don't be too greedy," the Spirit King said, clicking his tongue.

As Lee Sungmin listened to them, the Demon King of Fear's killing intent grew ever stronger. "Are you going against the will of the Demon God!"

"We're just telling you to be moderately greedy," the Fairy King replied, spreading his wings wide.

From their words, Lee Sungmin assessed the situation. The only ones showing blatant greed were the Demon King of Fear and the Great Demon World he represented. It seemed the Fairy King and the Spirit King had Advented directly just to keep him in check.

*No. I can't be satisfied with just getting a reprieve. It only postpones the immediate End. The fundamental problem remains unsolved.*

The Demon King of Fear moved. Instantly, the Spirit King and the Fairy King blocked his path. The Demon King genuinely intended to kill Lee Sungmin and take his soul.

*Do I have to be satisfied with getting a reprieve?*

No, he thought. I can't. This time, there won't be a next time. For a fleeting moment, he considered killing them all. It was worth a try, but he quickly gave up the idea. If he did something so reckless, he might not even get the reprieve.

"Get out of my way!" the Demon King roared.

"Do you really want to start a war?" the Fairy King challenged.

"What are you doing? Get out of here right now!" the Spirit King shouted at Lee Sungmin. If he left the library and returned to Eria, it would become impossible for the Great Demon World to interfere directly.

Lee Sungmin slowly backed away. The Demon King's darkness swelled to a massive size. To counter it, the Fairy King stepped forward as the Spirit King's power surged.

*Alright.* His thoughts were settled.

Lee Sungmin focused his consciousness, taking a step back from the torrent of power that filled the space, growing as if it would explode. With a calm mind, he tried to see the path to a distant place. 'That world', unlike the others, was not directly connected to Eria. However, even if that world couldn't reach this place, it was possible for him to interfere with it.

"Stop!" the Demon King of Fear shouted, the first to grasp the situation.

The Spirit King and the Fairy King also whipped around, realizing what was happening. The Fairy King's face contorted in disbelief. "This crazy bastard!"

The darkness raised by the Demon King leaped over the other two kings to engulf Lee Sungmin. The Fairy King frantically waved his hand to stop him, and the Spirit King did the same.

However, to Lee Sungmin, it all seemed to unfold in slow motion. Time itself stretched thin. Only his consciousness accelerated, a sensation he had experienced before in his fight with the Martial God. The solitary path that had seemed so far away suddenly drew near.

All the power that had been rushing toward him vanished.

"What in the world have you done!" the Spirit King exclaimed in horror.

"Do you know what you've done?!" the Fairy King shrieked.

"I know," Lee Sungmin replied, taking a few more steps back.

The space began to tremble. A power incomparable to the Advents of the three kings began to surge. Lee Sungmin knew what was about to happen. The target was different, but he had experienced this before at the summit of Yuzkia Mountain, where the Moon Palace was located. What was happening now, though on a vastly different scale, was a spatial erosion.

"It's coming..."

The scenery shimmered. The presences of the three kings were shaken. The Fairy King folded his wings and trembled. "Of all things, that madman's world!"

The space exploded.

A wave of light covered everything. The Spirit and the Demonic Spirit screamed. The Demon King of Fear shrieked and spewed darkness in all directions. The Fairy King flapped his wings, resisting the light, while the Spirit King struggled to twist the space around him. It was impossible. They could not stop the spatial erosion with their power.

Lee Sungmin watched as the library and the solitary path connected. The Battle God Chronicle. It was a world where beings who had escaped the Cycle of Mortality gathered, a world connected by consciousness, unbound by physical bodies. Because it was such a world, connecting directly to this place was an easy task. If one recalled the solitary path leading to it from the library, which existed as an exception to all laws, a spatial erosion would occur in that instant.

Come to think of it, the Battle God Chronicle should have been the one with the greatest greed for conquering Eria. Their world existed, but it had no clear substance. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Void Monarch, and the Spear King had all left their bodies here to enter the Battle God Chronicle with only their souls.

"It's because a physical body isn't really necessary," a familiar voice said. "I've never been to the underworld, but if it exists, it's probably similar to that place. In the Battle God Chronicle, a physical body is unnecessary. Only a spiritual body exists, walking a long, long solitary path. That's all there is to it."

He saw a broad back. Looking at the haphazardly grown, fluttering hair, Lee Sungmin found himself smiling. The Void Monarch shrugged.

"My foolish disciple." A tut-tutting sound came from beside him. "You used your head quite a bit, but you were too slow to act."

TAP. Someone placed a hand on Lee Sungmin's shoulder. His vision blurred. Without wiping his tears, Lee Sungmin looked to the side. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, his mask gone, was looking at him with a faint smile.

"Your crying face is quite ugly. Did you cry with a face like that when you read this one's will?"

"He cried a ton when I left, too," the Void Monarch laughed heartily.

The light that filled the space began to fade.

"Damn it." A grumbling voice came from nearby. Looking over, he saw the Spear King glaring at him with an annoyed expression. "To have my time wasted on something like this!"

"It won't take long," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered.

Lee Sungmin clenched his fists as he felt presences appearing one by one. The beings who had been walking the solitary path were now appearing here as the Battle God Chronicle directly connected.

"Unless the masters of the Great Demon World, the Elemental Realm, and the Fairy Realm Advent directly, the situation cannot be overturned."

The Demon King of Fear's darkness was no longer black. The shadows he shrieked and spewed were faint and thin. The Fairy King had already given up, his wings folded in defeat. The Spirit King trembled as he stepped back.

"Nope." The voice came without any warning. "Even if they come, it won't be overturned."

POW! The darkness that had been writhing in a final struggle vanished. The Demon King of Fear screamed. The being who had sent the Demon King tumbling with a single gesture let out a big yawn.

"If you've got a problem, tell the Demon God to come here himself." The master of the Battle God Chronicle sneered.
