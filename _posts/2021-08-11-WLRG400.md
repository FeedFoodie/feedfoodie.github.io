Please.

He unleashed his emotions, letting loose everything that had piled up inside him until he felt he would have no regrets. The very thought was laughable. He had not even properly started. When he considered his own martial arts theory, he knew he had endured for far longer than Jeniela ever had. For her to have prattled on about how one could never get used to pain was utterly ridiculous.

*Stop, please.*

He needed a target for his ultimate technique, and he did not have to look far. The target was right in front of him. The woman who was once a monster with no discernible weakness was no longer a monster.

*I, I was wrong, please...*

Lee Sungmin was the monster now. He enacted every form of violence and torture he could imagine upon the begging Jeniela.

He began with simple violence before moving on to magic. He found mental magic particularly amusing. Jeniela, who could instill terror with her mere presence, had never once experienced mental corruption herself.

*Please... kill me.*

A sense of accomplishment washed over him as he watched her cry and beg. It was a feeling of, *I finally did it*.

The moon, however, had not yet set. The night was still long. Though he had achieved a result, Lee Sungmin did not stop. Just as the night stretched on, there were still so many things he could and wanted to do.

At some point, Jeniela could no longer even form the words to beg. The solution for that was simple. Whenever he blew her head off, her body would instantly regenerate and her frozen mind would return. Then he would start again. Jeniela would scream and beg once more, though she never asked him to spare her life. She knew that under no circumstances would he let her live. Instead, she pleaded for him to kill her.

"Kill, kill, kill me. Kill me!"

"Not yet," Lee Sungmin would reply each time with a smile.

He recalled the saying that revenge was an empty thing and found it absurd. Empty? It was not empty at all, not in the slightest. Within him, the memories of the Blood Demon resisted the violence he was inflicting on Jeniela. He ignored the feeling as another thought consumed him.

If only Jeniela had not existed.

If she had never existed, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman would never have gone to the North. Baek Sogo would never have been poisoned by Ju Won's venom. Baek Sogo would never have lost her right arm. Scarlet's left eye would never have been plucked out. The Black Dragon Knight's left arm would never have been severed. Lloyd's right leg would never have been cut off. The Spear King would never have died. Osla's wings would never have been torn, and that forest would never have been devastated. Every consequence that had occurred in this forest happened because Jeniela existed.

No matter how he thought about it, things would have been better if only Jeniela had not existed.

"......"

If only Jeniela had not existed, the Void Monarch would never have disappeared.

Again, Jeniela went limp and unresponsive. Lee Sungmin nonchalantly blew her head off. The speed of her regeneration had become noticeably slower. He glanced up at the sky and saw that the night was ending. Dawn was approaching. He looked down at his own hands, which were soaked in blood, and casually shook them off before looking back at Jeniela. Her head reformed as she trembled, her face drenched in blood. She was no longer proud, no longer arrogant.

"...Kill me..."

She only wished for death. How many times had she died? No, when he thought about it, the number of deaths was not that high. The pain of having her mind shattered over and over was far worse than death. The night had been damnably long. The full moon's magic and her infinite regeneration had bestowed upon her a fittingly infinite and eternal pain.

Lee Sungmin looked into Jeniela's dim eyes. He remembered the Jeniela who had been filled with fighting spirit, the one who had laughed loudly and asked if he had Despaired. Not a trace of that woman remained. The woman who had asked about Despair was now more steeped in it than at any other time in her life.

He had whispered it to her several times throughout the night.

*Nothing you ever wished for will come true. The future where the Slaughter Predator appeared did not come to pass, and you failed to tame Aine into the Slaughter Predator. The destruction of this world, which you desired most, will not happen either.*

He enjoyed watching her body tremble as if she were having a seizure each time he said it.

"Kill me... now..."

*I can finally die.* The thought came to Jeniela as she watched the moon set and the dawn break. It had been a long night, so very long. The night that felt as if it would never end was finally over. She would not have to suffer any more pain.

However, the desire to escape suffering was not the only reason she wished for death. Jeniela could not bear the fact that nothing she had wished for, nothing she had done, would ever come to fruition. The Slaughter Predator had not appeared. She had failed to turn Aine into the Slaughter Predator. She would not get to see The End with her own eyes. That was the true reason she felt such Despair. That was why she wished for death. If she just died, she would not know what happened afterward. She thought she could find some pathetic solace in that. *I may have died, but after my death, this world could not have avoided The End.*

"What are you thinking about?"

Lee Sungmin lowered his head to look at her. Jeniela flinched in horror at his face, which was right next to hers, and tucked her chin in. She clenched her trembling hands tightly as he looked into her fear-filled eyes.

"Well then."

The Formless Spear materialized in his hand. He casually plunged it into Jeniela's right shoulder. She bit her lip hard against the excruciating pain.

"Guh... Ughhh...!"

A groan escaped through her tightly clenched lips. The quality of this pain was different from before. Lee Sungmin deliberately and completely severed her right arm. The wound did not regenerate. Jeniela gasped for breath and squeezed her eyes shut.

*After a few more moments of pain, it will all be over,* she thought.

"Doesn't it hurt?"

"It... it hurts..."

"How do you feel?"

Jeniela did not answer. Lee Sungmin chuckled and stabbed the spear into her thigh. She convulsed and screamed. It was not that he particularly wanted to hear her answer. He simply did what he had decided to do.

*This is the last time, the very last.* Jeniela felt the pain as all her limbs were severed, repeating a cycle of screaming and enduring.

Finally, the tip of the Formless Spear rested on her chest. Jeniela panted, looking up at Lee Sungmin.

"Understand this well," he muttered, looking down at her. "What I said about nothing you wish for ever coming true."

*What is he talking about? Just stab me already.* Jeniela swallowed the words, biting her lip. She had no desire to provoke him needlessly.

Lee Sungmin watched the churning emotions in her eyes and slowly pushed the spear into her chest. The spearhead burned through her skin. Her magic could not resist as a large hole was punched through her ribs. Ah, the spearhead touched her heart. It was piercing, piercing, piercing. A gurgling sound escaped her lips.

Death was approaching. She had experienced so many deaths tonight. In her seven hundred years of life, she had never died as many times as she had in these few hours, and for the death she was about to face, there would be no next time.

*It's okay...*

No, it was not okay. She did not want to die. She had wished for the day she would eventually die, but she had never wished for a death like this.

*I don't want to die. I wanted to see how The End would come.*

Come to think of it... why did I want to see that?

"Ah..."

It pierced through.

SQUELCH.

Her heart was impaled. The spear that had dug into her chest went straight through her back and out the other side.

CRACKLE.

An electric current sparked, and the Formless Spear vanished. The pain felt distant. Her consciousness began to float as many emotions flashed by.

*It's the end, the end,* Jeniela muttered in a small voice. She did not want to die. She did not want to die here, because she wanted to see The End, yet that did not mean she wanted to live either. She was afraid of continuing to feel this pain. She was afraid of eventually seeing that The End would not come.

So, in the end, she wanted to die.

*Ah...*

Her consciousness faded completely. There was no sensation in her body. Her head had been blown off and her heart had burst countless times, but this 'real' death felt different.

No.

Was it really different?

An electric jolt shot through her consciousness. Light shone into her darkened vision. The first thing she saw was Lee Sungmin's face, right in front of her own. Jeniela tried to scream, but no sound came out. She tried to convulse and flee, but the distance between them did not grow. A powerlessness she had never felt before in her life restrained her body.

"......"

Lee Sungmin stroked his chin as he looked at the orb resting in his hand. He had been worried if it would work since it was his first time, but it had turned out better than he thought. Reorganizing his memories to obtain all the martial arts of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Blood Demon was tremendous progress, but Dragon magic was, of course, far more versatile.

*I never knew magic could be this convenient. Right?*

He naturally posed the question to the Void Monarch, then smiled bitterly upon realizing no answer would come. Lee Sungmin gently shook the orb in his hand.

[Ah, ahhhhh!]

Inside the orb, Jeniela's soul writhed and screamed. Lee Sungmin shook it a little more, scraped its surface with his fingernail, and channeled his Demonic Energy into it. Inside, Jeniela had a seizure. He watched it and smiled faintly.

"I told you," he whispered to the soul trapped in the orb. "That nothing you wish for will ever come true."

In her final moment, Jeniela had wished for death. So he did not kill her. He killed her body and extracted only her soul, imprisoning it with Dragon magic. As long as the magic was maintained, Jeniela's soul would never be liberated. No matter what she tried, there was no way for her to escape that prison.

"Don't think too badly of it," Lee Sungmin smiled as he watched Jeniela scream in Despair from within the orb. "At least you didn't die. Who knows, you might even get to see The End in this state."

[Stop, stop...!]

He ignored her screams. Lee Sungmin blocked her voice from his mind and slowly turned around.

Gemini was sitting on the ground, staring at him with a dazed expression. She could feel Jeniela's corpse lying limp at his feet and Jeniela's soul writhing inside the orb he held. Understanding what had just happened, she swallowed hard.

"Why didn't you run?" Lee Sungmin asked as he approached her.

"Be... because even if I ran... it would be meaningless..."

"That's right." He nodded and gripped his Formless Spear.

Gemini flinched and hunched her shoulders. Then she immediately changed her posture, kneeling down.

"I, I'll swear on my Mana. I'll never, ever get in your way. I'll never be hostile to you, and, and... please... what... what should I do?"

"Do you want to live?"

"...Yes..." Gemini nodded, her voice choked with tears.

In his mind, the memories of the Blood Demon stirred.

"Gather the Vampires," Lee Sungmin said, lowering the Formless Spear. "The Vampires who didn't belong to Jeniela. Now that she has ended up like this, you will become the new Vampire Queen."

"A... and...?"

"Do nothing. I won't tell you not to drink blood, but don't increase the number of vampires. You don't need to be cooped up in the North. I'll permit you to drink blood to live... but do it in moderation."

"I... I understand, no, I understand," Gemini said, bowing her head hastily.

It was a calculated act of mercy. Jeniela had fallen, but that did not mean all Vampires in the world would disappear. In that case, he thought it better to gather the Vampire forces through Gemini and control them within reasonable limits.

*So.*

*Be satisfied with this.*

He erased the remnants of the memory strongly engraved in his mind. It was the memory of what the Blood Demon felt, during the time he traveled with his adopted daughter.
