Jeniela gazed down at the drunken, passed-out form of Baek Museon. A master of the Supreme Peak would not normally get drunk, as their body would decompose alcohol long before intoxication could set in, regardless of the quantity consumed. They could, however, choose to become inebriated, and even then, they could expel the influence of the alcohol at will.

However, the man before her now was utterly defenseless. Consumed and broken by Despair, Baek Museon had abandoned all care for his own well-being, drinking like a madman until he had lost his senses completely.

Jeniela, however, did not care. She had no intention of biting his neck, even in this vulnerable state. Though she tempted him, it was not as if they were sleeping together. She savored this subtle game of push-and-pull, finding it fun to watch the man once called the Blood Heavenly Demon, who had stood at the pinnacle of the Blood Heavenly Alliance, struggle to maintain his pride. He still wanted to remain human, acting aloof until the very end, even as Despair ate away at his soul.

Her greatest pleasure would come when he was finally and completely stained by it. The moment he gave up on being human, abandoned his pathetic aloofness, and collapsed to beg at her feet would be exquisite.

*And then I'll get bored of him.*

It was the same with all the others. A soft laugh escaped her lips. She could bite his neck at any time, but turning a human into a vampire required more than a simple bite. One had to drink the blood of the Lord who bit them and then swear an oath. It was an oath to no longer be human, an oath to become a vampire and live by drinking the blood of mortals. Only then would the transformation be complete.

[Lord.]

A voice echoed in her mind, a call from one of the many, many vampires she had turned. Watching Baek Museon's sleeping face was growing boring anyway, so Jeniela left his room.

"What is it?" she asked.

[A strange fellow has entered Trabia.]

*Who was this again?* Jeniela closed her eyes, and in the pitch-black darkness of her mind, red lights flared to life. Each light illuminating her darkened vision was a subordinate belonging to her brood. She focused her consciousness on one of the trembling, humming lights, and information about that subordinate flowed into her.

"Ah, right. It's you, Mukai," she said with a smile and a nod. She hadn't recognized the voice alone, but focusing like this told her immediately. He was the subordinate she had assigned to watch the gate of the Central District.

"A strange fellow, you say? How was he strange?"

[It's this one.]

Everything Mukai had seen was transmitted directly to her. This was the advantage held by inhuman species like vampires and Lycanthropes who turned others into their subordinates. All who belonged to a brood were spiritually connected to the Lord at its apex, who held the power of life and death over them and could control their actions at any time.

"...Aha."

A smile bloomed on Jeniela's face. The image Mukai showed her was of a man she had seen before, just a month ago. It was the face Ju Won had shown her. Her shoulders shook with laughter.

"I thought it was about time for him to show up," she muttered to herself. She then broadcast the image of Lee Sungmin to all her subordinates in Trabia.

"Find this man," Jeniela commanded, her voice laced with laughter.

"Find him and let me know. Got it?"

*Yes, Lord.* Hundreds of voices resonated in her mind.

*

Lee Sungmin walked the streets of Trabia's Central District, the city of the Inhuman Demonic Realm. A foul, unidentifiable aura clung to the air, an unpleasantness that was less a feeling and more an instinctual aversion.

"...This feels awful," Rubia muttered from her hiding place inside Lee Sungmin's clothes. Though not a beastfolk, her cat ears were enough to draw unwanted attention. She was concealed to avoid trouble, but even she could sense the oppressive nature of the street.

[The concentration of inhuman energy is high,] Void Monarch remarked. [Even for a city in the Inhuman Demonic Realm, it's unusual to have this many gathered in the Central District. It would make sense in the South, but in the North...]

*Is there a reason for this? The information from Erebrisa...*

[The information from Erebrisa is just a compilation from guilds, merchants, and information sects,] Void Monarch advised. [Don't trust it unconditionally. Information that isn't in general circulation can't be bought through them.]

[Erebrisa only acts as a broker,] he continued. [You can trust them with goods, but it's best not to unconditionally trust 'information'.]

The report on Trabia he had purchased had been simple. It stated that after the Blood Heavenly Demon was defeated and the Blood Heavenly Alliance collapsed, the unorthodox factions they had controlled reared their heads. Then, all sorts of inhuman species had flowed in, turning the area into the Inhuman Demonic Realm. That was all it said.

[The Blood Heavenly Demon and his Alliance controlled this many inhuman and unorthodox factions? Hahaha! That's ridiculous,] Void Monarch laughed loudly. [If the Blood Heavenly Demon had really been doing that, he would have been the world's number one. A guy with that kind of power was defeated by that wench, the Little Heavenly Demon? Don't make me laugh.]

[Demons attract demons. Spirits attract spirits, and monsters attract monsters. Twisted beings can't resist crawling into a dark hole when they see one, just like a cat cramming itself into a box. There's something in this city. Something is drawing them here.]

Void Monarch's voice was cheerful.

[This city. I don't know what it is, but it's here. Something that attracts inhuman monsters.]

It seemed even Void Monarch did not know what it was, and neither did Lee Sungmin.

He scanned his surroundings as he walked, but nothing particularly concerning caught his eye. He hadn't come to Trabia with any grand purpose in the city itself. He was simply here to stay in the largest and most populated city in the North while he waited for his eventual meeting with the nobleman.

This meant he would have to stay in Trabia for a long time. Although he had plenty of money, he had no intention of staying somewhere luxurious, not knowing what might happen during an extended visit.

"As long as we're not sleeping on the streets!" Rubia exclaimed cheerfully when he mentioned finding an inn. Lee Sungmin didn't particularly care, but Rubia had been very dissatisfied with their recent long-term camping.

He entered an inn that looked unremarkable from the outside, but as he opened the door, he realized just how meaningless appearances could be.

The first thing he saw were bodies.

The corpses were piled in a corner of the inn, fitting in with a strange lack of incongruity, almost like a piece of the interior design. Not a single person in the noisy first-floor tavern paid them any mind. Rubia fell silent, shocked by the sight, but Lee Sungmin wasn't too bothered by them either.

"What'll you have?" the innkeeper, a man of burly muscle, asked. Lee Sungmin could tell at a glance that he was a master who had trained in external arts. He took an empty seat at the bar.

"A light meal, and I'd like to get a room."

"How many days are you planning to stay?"

"I don't have a set plan."

"Long-term guests are welcome. Room's paid for by the day, upfront. You never know when you'll die in this city."

"Like those corpses piled in the corner?"

"Oh! You saw them?" the innkeeper chuckled. "Well, don't mind them too much. It's a common sight in this city nowadays."

He continued, "This is Trabia now, a place where a small squabble can lead to murder. Those dead guys over there... what were they again? Some folks from the Murim Alliance, they said. Hehe! In any other city, maybe, but the name of the Murim Alliance won't protect your back here."

"But messing with them would still be a headache," Lee Sungmin noted.

No matter what anyone said, the Murim Alliance was the largest orthodox force in Eria, with most orthodox sects, including the Nine Sects One Gang, affiliated with it. To touch someone from the Murim Alliance was to make an enemy of the entire organization.

"I didn't see a thing," the innkeeper grinned, revealing yellow teeth. "Everyone's the same. We're all blind, deaf, and mute cripples here. Those... uh... whatchamacallits from the Murim Alliance are dead, sure, but I've got no idea why they died."

The innkeeper chuckled and turned to enter the kitchen. So this was what the city was like. What on earth could be here to have twisted the great city of Trabia so much?

The door opened.

Time stopped. In the sudden silence, only the sound of clicking footsteps echoed. A single presence instantly overwhelmed the space. Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinched as an ominous feeling washed over him from behind, a sensation conveyed by a gaze focused solely on him. Then, just as quickly, the overwhelming presence vanished as if it had never been there.

"......"

"Hey lady, what's your price?!" the ruffians on the first floor cheered and shouted.

At that moment, Lee Sungmin debated whether to turn around. The footsteps grew closer. Even if he didn't look, she would eventually come to him. He took a deep breath and turned.

"So noisy."

As Lee Sungmin turned, the heads of every other patron in the inn snapped around, a full circle. Their bodies fell limp, and the room was silent once more.

*How did she do that?*

Lee Sungmin couldn't say a word. This was different. He had never met an opponent who gave off such an absurd feeling of power. The woman had simply walked forward, showing no other movement, and with that alone, she had snapped the necks of dozens of people.

"This place is too filthy," the woman said, her lips curling up as she approached him. She was beautiful. Her blood-red hair, the hairpin holding it up, and the ornate kimono she wore were all stunning, yet none of it could overpower the inherent beauty she possessed.

"If you're okay with it... I can guide you to a place so wonderful and beautiful it can't be compared to this."

The woman tilted her head and smiled, baring her teeth. Lee Sungmin swallowed dryly at the sight of her sharp canines.

"How about it? Shall we have a meal together?"

Vampire Queen. Jeniella of Blood Charm. She sent an alluring gaze toward Lee Sungmin as she made her offer. Her words left his mind completely blank. *A monster like this exists in the world?* He had never seen the true extent of Wiji Hoyeon's power, but based on presence alone, Jeniela was even greater.

[Get a hold of yourself,] thunder cracked inside Lee Sungmin's head. It was Void Monarch's voice. [Jeniela is the queen of vampires, who has lived for over 600 years. There is no vampire in this world with more power than her.]

Void Monarch's voice quickened, clearly not having imagined they would run into Jeniela so suddenly.

[The highest-ranking vampires can overwhelm their prey with their presence alone. Like a frog freezing when it sees a snake. Keep your wits about you. If you're not careful, your brain and subconscious might not be able to handle the fear and choose suicide!]

"...I'm fine."

At Void Monarch's shout, Lee Sungmin steadied his breathing and replied. He was just a little surprised. His mind was different from that of a normal human. A vampire queen who had lived for 600 years? He had spent 2100 years in the mental world alone.

Lee Sungmin's mind, broken and repaired countless times, successfully resisted the threat Jeniela was projecting head-on.

"Oh my," Jeniela said, watching him with a sweet smile. "You're certainly not ordinary... It would have been nice if you'd just answered obediently."

"...I already ordered a meal."

"The food they serve in a place like this is no better than pig slop."

"I'll be the judge of that after I've tried it."

"You don't seem to understand." Jeniela's smile deepened, and the more her lips curled, the more of her teeth were revealed. Her canines projected a threat just by existing. She opened her mouth and added another word.

"You're not coming?"

"...I have no choice," Lee Sungmin sighed and stood up.

"Let's go."

He had no choice but to say it, his expression gloomy.
