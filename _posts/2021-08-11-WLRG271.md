The next day, Abel and Lloyd departed from the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. The Chairman did not see them off, and they, in turn, offered no notice of their leaving. Though they had agreed to join forces against the coming of The End, Abel felt no goodwill toward the man.

"I hope he gets killed by the Sword Immortal," he had muttered.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman felt precisely the same way. He had no favorable feelings for the mage who had visited without warning only to spout a slew of irritating words without offering a single clear solution.

Lee Sungmin, who sat cross-legged before him, finally spoke. "Do you truly mean that?"

"Would I say it if I didn't? Still, hmm. Even for the Sword Immortal, killing that mage won't be an easy task."

"Was the Mages Guild Master that strong?"

"This one can't quite gauge the intricacies of magic, but in his own field, he can likely be considered on par with me, the Sword Immortal, or the Martial God."

The words surprised Lee Sungmin. Was Abel, a man who spewed such vile things without hesitation, truly such an outstanding mage? His thoughts drifted to Envirus and Arbes, the most exceptional mages he had ever known.

He remembered when Envirus tried to kill him in Urmuri. Lee Sungmin had been lucky then. Envirus had been severely weakened by the repeated reverse summoning of his Elementals, his Mana running rampant within him from the broken contract. If not for those handicaps, Envirus would not have struggled with him so.

It was the same for Arbes. Though he met a futile end at the hands of Kim Jonghyun, the Five Stars of Predator, Arbes included, were monsters that even the current Lee Sungmin could not easily challenge. It was a testament to the sheer power possessed by the group called Predator. Most of them were monsters with skills rivaling the Spear King, and as for Jeniela, their de facto leader, the extent of her power was impossible to guess.

*Vollander, Ju Won, Jeniela.*

He had met Predator's leaders only after reaching the Transcendent Realm, yet whenever he faced them, Lee Sungmin had never once felt the desire to fight.

"I plan to travel around a bit," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman said as he pressed on Lee Sungmin's acupoints. The Chairman's internal energy flowed into him, coursing through his meridians. "I'm thinking of going to the Murim Alliance and then to the North."

"...The North? Why are you suddenly going there?"

"Didn't the Mages Guild Master say? The Vampire Queen there can see the future."

*No way.* Lee Sungmin wanted to turn and stare at the Chairman with an anxious expression, but he could not move. He was in the middle of suppressing the flow of Demonic Energy within his body. Although the mask Osla had made for him kept the energy contained, the rampage had not ended without consequences. A thick residue of Demonic Energy remained in his ki and blood, so whenever he circulated his inner arts, the demonic power would unintentionally mix with it, stirring and threatening to rise again. The Chairman was now using an acupoint sealing technique, sending his own internal energy into Lee Sungmin to push the lingering corruption out.

"...Are you going to have a showdown with the Vampire Queen?"

"I wouldn't call it a showdown. I'm just thinking of meeting her for a chat."

"But your opponent is the Vampire Queen."

"What about it?" the Chairman sneered. "This one doesn't discriminate just because someone is inhuman. In fact, you, my own disciple, have this kind of fucked-up body, don't you?"

"It seems dangerous. There's her strength to consider, and Ju Won is also in the North."

"You mean that King of Lycanthropes? I'm not going there to fight them. If the situation looks bad, I'll prioritize getting away."

"......"

The Chairman's hands struck Lee Sungmin's back. A spray of black blood spewed from Lee Sungmin's mouth.

"The Mages Guild Master seemed wary of a future-seeing Vampire, but my thoughts are different," the Chairman said, standing up. "I don't know what future that monster saw, or what future she desires. Either way, I believe I need to know what she has seen. Only then can I decide whether to avoid it or not."

As he watched the Chairman stretch his arms out wide, Lee Sungmin posed a question to the Void Monarch.

*If my Master and the Vampire Queen fought to the death, who would win?*

[The matchup is bad,] the Void Monarch answered. [This Elder acknowledges that human's strength. However, Jeniela has grown incomparably stronger than when this Elder died. If it were the Jeniela from before my death, she probably wouldn't be a match for him, but...]

*You mean not anymore?*

[Frankly, I don't know. It's never been shown just how strong that human is. He wasn't using his full strength when he was teaching you martial arts.]

*Then why is the matchup bad?*

[Because the opponent is a Vampire,] the Void Monarch replied instantly, as if it were obvious. [The Vampires you fought, setting aside their power, were not of a high class. It was possible to kill them, wasn't it?]

*...Is it impossible to kill a high-class Vampire?*

[It's not completely impossible, just incredibly difficult. On a moonlit night, a Vampire of Jeniela's caliber becomes nearly immortal. Under a full moon, she becomes completely immortal. That's not to say killing her during the day is easy. That wench has lived for hundreds of years, drinking countless amounts of blood and accumulating that much power. A fatal wound that would kill a human would be nothing more than a trivial injury to her.]

That was what made it so nasty. The opponent was a nearly immortal monster with immense power, while the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, no matter how strong, was only human. How was one supposed to fight a monster that would not die?

[It's super easy,] the Void Monarch quipped. [Just beat her so fast she doesn't have time to regenerate.]

*Right, so easy.*

The Void Monarch was probably the only one who could suggest such a thing as a viable strategy against the Vampire Queen.

A thick miasma of death qi clung to the deck. Even for Kim Jonghyun, who had spent long years alongside death as a Black Mage, it was the first time he had encountered a concentration this dense. Each time the sea swayed, the old Ghost Ship's deck shrieked with a groan. Standing in the middle of it all, Kim Jonghyun waited calmly, without rushing.

The Death Knights surrounding him said nothing and did nothing. They just stood there blankly, their empty gazes fixed on him. Kim Jonghyun took a deep breath, not to shake off tension, but to inhale the death qi that saturated the air.

*Ah. This is good.*

He smiled faintly, feeling a deep sense of satisfaction. The Demon Lord's organ inside his body rejoiced at the necrotic energy that filled him with each breath.

Then, in a single moment, the death qi flowing across the deck swelled immensely. It was the aura emanating from a single Death Knight walking out of the cabin, an aura so powerful it enveloped all the death qi being emitted by the others. Kim Jonghyun knew at once that this was Vollander, the leader of the Death Knights.

"We meet for the first time," Vollander's voice muttered.

"I've heard stories about you."

Vollander and his Death Knights formed one of the pillars of Predator, but they were not on particularly friendly terms with the other inhumans. This was because the Death Knights had once been human, a fact they shared with all of Predator's members. However, Death Knights, Liches, Lycanthropes, Vampires, and Yokai all held different values. Strictly speaking, what had happened to the Death Knights was a curse. While a few may have chosen this path, most of the knights on this ship, Vollander included, were beings bound to this fate against their will.

"I hear you annihilated Arbes and stole his power," Vollander continued.

"......"

"He was a bastard who deserved to die, though it was far too late."

Vollander did not particularly like Arbes. In truth, he disliked most of them, including Jeniela and the now-dead Red Ghost. He acknowledged Ju Won's strength, at least, but in the matter of cannibalism, Ju Won was just as much a target of his displeasure.

"However, separate from you killing Arbes, I'm not too fond of the evil deeds you committed in the North."

*A Death Knight, of all things, is concerned with chivalry.* Kim Jonghyun found the attitude amusing but did not let his ridicule show. It was not the time for that. He tried to gauge Vollander's level and found his power to be in no way inferior to the Black Dragon Knight who had once driven him into a corner, and it was not just Vollander. The assembled Death Knights here represented a force that would be difficult to find even among the so-called prestigious sects of Murim.

"Do you know why I've come here?" Kim Jonghyun asked.

"I do not," Vollander said, shaking his head.

Kim Jonghyun chuckled. "I went through quite a bit of trouble to find you. The sea is vast, after all. You wouldn't believe how long I wandered, chasing down reported sightings of the Ghost Ship..."

"For what purpose did you go to such trouble?"

"I can give you something you desire more than anything," Kim Jonghyun said in a soft voice.

Vollander showed no reaction. As a Death Knight without a face, it was impossible to sense any agitation in his expression, but Kim Jonghyun felt the death qi emanating from him quietly subside.

"Explain," Vollander demanded.

SHIIING!

Dozens of Death Knights surrounding Kim Jonghyun drew their weapons in unison.

"You are human, yet not human," Vollander stated. "You're different from any other inhuman I've ever seen. However, no matter how outstanding your Dark Magic or how great your power is, it won't be easy to escape from here."

"I have no intention of escaping. I'm only here to make a deal with you."

"You think you can make a deal with us?"

"Let's not be so hostile," Kim Jonghyun said with another chuckle. "I know what you want, and I also know why you're out on this vast sea, doing something so meaningless."

"Meaningless?"

"Yes. The Dragon you're looking for isn't in this sea. It's nowhere in this world. All the Dragons of this world have already migrated to another dimension."

Vollander offered no response. He did not blindly believe Kim Jonghyun's words, but he did not dismiss them as lies either. For now, he would listen.

"As long as there are no Dragons in this world, there is no way for you to return to being human," Kim Jonghyun continued, taking the Grimoire from within his robes. The pitch-black magic tome flew from his hand and floated in the air. "However, I can turn you back into humans."

"What proof do you have that those words are true?" Vollander asked.

"Everything I've said is the truth. I can even swear an oath."

"So. What do you want from us?"

A stir went through the surrounding Death Knights. They murmured amongst themselves at the possibility of becoming human again.

"Including you, turning the many Death Knights on this ship back into humans is no easy task."

"Is it possible, or impossible? Just tell me that."

"If sufficient preparations are made, it's not impossible," Kim Jonghyun said, baring his teeth in a grin.

Seeing that grin, Vollander asked again, "Sufficient preparations, you say? What kind of preparations are you talking about?"

"Death and souls," Kim Jonghyun answered without hesitation. "Hearts, and blood."

The Death Knights murmured again. Vollander was silent for a long moment. The one thing he had protected even after becoming a Death Knight was his own code of chivalry. It was the last vestige of humanity that he and the knights on this ship possessed.

"...How much?"

"Enough to form a mountain, I'd say?"

It was meaningless. Their quest was meaningless if there were truly no Dragons left in this world, and even if they found one, there was no guarantee it would help them, or that it was even possible. They might be able to return to being human.

"First, prove it," Vollander stated in a low voice.

A wide smile spread across Kim Jonghyun's face as he swore his truth to Mana.
