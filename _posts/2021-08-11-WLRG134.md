Glee shone in Baek Museon's eyes as he watched Lee Sungmin collapse in a pool of his own blood. Even so, Jeniela's command echoed in his mind. *Don't kill him. Just don't kill him.* Baek Museon muttered the words aloud as he slowly closed the distance.

"Aaaaaah!"

Rubia's scream tore through the air, but it was more than just a cry of despair. She drew upon her magic without a thought for the consequences, and brilliant blue lightning crackled in both her hands. Before she could unleash the attack, the Crazy Heavenly Demon blocked her path.

"Don't."

Her eyes widened as she stared at the Crazy Heavenly Demon's hardened face. "You're telling me to just stand by and watch?!" she shouted, her voice fierce. "He could really die…"

"this one will step in," the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat out, his voice pained. He had said he would not interfere, and he had planned to honor that, but he couldn't. Lee Sungmin was already fatally injured. If he didn't intervene now, Baek Museon would surely kill him. *Just don't kill him…* The Crazy Heavenly Demon had also heard Baek Museon's muttering, but he couldn't blindly trust those words. Besides, the phrase itself was chilling. It sounded as if anything was permissible as long as the boy was left breathing.

In fact, Baek Museon was thinking along those very lines. First, he would rip off the bastard's left arm, and after that? Perhaps he would tear off the legs that performed those annoying movement arts. As long as he didn't kill him, that should be fine.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon stepped forward. He had not wanted to get involved, but he could not stand by and watch Lee Sungmin die. In any other situation, he would have stayed out of it. However, he had passed on the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art to Lee Sungmin. Even without a formal master-disciple relationship, having personally taught him the martial art meant he could not simply watch him be killed. That was not all. The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not want to see Lee Sungmin's potential crushed here.

KABOOM!

The Crazy Heavenly Demon kicked off the ground, instantly wedging himself between Lee Sungmin and Baek Museon. Having been steadily approaching his prey, Baek Museon flinched and glared at the old demon.

"Didn't you say you wouldn't interfere?"

"I simply couldn't stand by and watch."

"Khahaha! It seems you've taken quite a liking to that brat. He was using the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art… did you take him as a disciple?"

"No," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, shaking his head. "He is this one's successor."

"Hahaha!" Baek Museon burst into laughter at the answer. The old Baek Museon would not have dared to oppose the Crazy Heavenly Demon, but that was then. He had not yet completed Jeniela's order, and if the Crazy Heavenly Demon stood in his way, he would simply have to kill him too. "To think I'd be the one to kill the Crazy Heavenly Demon with my own hands!"

"You've grown arrogant from power you obtained so easily," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered in a low voice. Despite his words, he did not underestimate Baek Museon. The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, unleashed to its extreme, erupted from his body. His near-death at Lee Sungmin's hands was due to carelessness. His suppression by the Void Monarch was because the Monarch's Demonic Energy had seeped into his inner arts, but his pride, the pride of holding the title of Heavenly Demon longer than any other in Eria, remained.

"this one is the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae!"

He unleashed a Lion's Roar as Baek Museon's red mist and the rings of red enhanced qi collided. Amidst the clash of violent energy, Rubia scrambled over to the collapsed Lee Sungmin and stretched out her hand.

"A-are you okay?!"

His body swayed back and forth as she shook his shoulder. Rubia flinched when she saw his lifeless, unfocused eyes.

*He's unconscious…!*

On a desperate hunch, she checked his pulse. Unlike his consciousness, it was steady. The Void Monarch's voice rang in her head.

[Give him a potion.]

"W-what did you say?"

[His blood and qi are twisted, and his internal organs are damaged. He'll die if you leave him like this,] the Void Monarch spoke quickly.

Rubia acted without a second thought, grabbing Lee Sungmin's Subspace Pocket. She shoved her hand inside to find a potion, but what she pulled out was an Elixir. It could not heal severed limbs, but it had an immediate effect on internal injuries. Rubia pried open Lee Sungmin's mouth and poured the entire Elixir in.

[You're not going to feed it to him mouth-to-mouth?]

"Why are you spouting such nonsense at a time like this!" Rubia shrieked.

Though the Void Monarch's tone was teasing, he was not idle. He infiltrated Lee Sungmin's body and spread the Elixir's effects, which had just passed down his throat, throughout his entire system. He worked to untangle the twisted blood and qi and piece together the shattered internal organs.

[Hm?]

The Void Monarch made a surprised sound. This was before the Elixir had even fully taken effect. The shattered organs were already regenerating. The twisted acupoints were slowly untangling themselves, and the ruptured areas were healing on their own.

[He's not human anymore…] he muttered with a groan.

Neither Lee Sungmin nor Rubia heard him. Still, thanks to the Void Monarch's help, the Elixir spread through his body, and Lee Sungmin's recovery began.

His consciousness, however, did not return.

It drifted, severed, in a pitch-black darkness where Lee Sungmin was not even aware of his own situation. Memories of the past flashed by like a revolving lantern, and before long, he was looking down on them all. Two lives were visible. There was the me at fourteen, the me who lived in Eria for thirteen years and died in a Dungeon. Then there was the me who returned to the past and has lived until now.

Everything changed starting with his meeting with Wiji Hoyeon. His narrow world had expanded. All the lives he had lived so far flashed by, from his time at Mount Mushii, to Shaolin, and in the Mental World. He saw fleeting encounters and deeply entangled fates. At some point, his consciousness grew clearer. At its very center, Lee Sungmin looked down at his own body. His hands, which had once held nothing, were now gripping a spear.

From there, his consciousness expanded again. In the widened mental world, Lee Sungmin contemplated everything he had learned, remembered, and mastered, one by one. It was a strange and mystical experience. The sum of all the insights and enlightenments he had gained so far was visible at a single glance.

"Ah," Lee Sungmin let out a dazed sound.

He could see his own collapsed form. His blood-soaked body was not moving, but it was clearly alive. *Then why am I here?* He tilted his head. He could see Rubia massaging his limbs beside him. The Void Monarch's form… was also visible. The Monarch, who should not have been visible, looked no different from how he had in the Mental World, and he was looking down at Lee Sungmin. A loud noise made him turn his head. Not far away, Baek Museon was confronting the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"What am I doing here?" Lee Sungmin muttered with a blank expression. He should not be here like this. *Am I dead? Did I die?* That couldn't be. He had not met Wiji Hoyeon yet. He had not kept his promise to her. He had not met Baek Sogo either. No, beyond all that, he just did not want to die. He could not die. *I can still go further. I can do more.* The death of the Sword Phantom came to mind. *I can do more…* The words the Sword Phantom had muttered, the words he had uttered like a final regret, strongly shook Lee Sungmin's consciousness.

Someone pushed his back. Startled, Lee Sungmin looked behind him but saw nothing. Someone had definitely pushed him, but he could not see who it was.

Lee Sungmin's eyes, which had been closed, shot open as he exhaled sharply. Rubia, who had been looking into his face with a tearful expression, screamed and fell backward. He gasped for breath, clutching his throat.

"Cough!"

"Aaaaaah!"

The black blood he spat out covered Rubia's face. She shrieked, covering her face with her hands. After spitting up blood a few more times, Lee Sungmin clutched his chest. He actually felt better after vomiting the blood. Only Rubia, who had gotten an unexpected blood facial, was left to scrape together the remaining snow on the ground and scrub her face furiously.

[Are you okay?] the Void Monarch asked urgently.

Lee Sungmin did not answer, instead staggering to his feet. *What in the world happened? What just occurred?* He grabbed his head in confusion. His memories were clear, the past flashing by in an instant, the insights he had so clearly witnessed.

[It's a miraculous encounter,] the Void Monarch muttered, his voice full of surprise.

As Lee Sungmin stood up, staggering, he felt it. The flow of his inner arts was free, and his body felt much lighter.

[What in the world happened? You've recovered from a fatal internal injury, so why are your mind, qi, and body stabilized?]

It was something even the Void Monarch could not understand. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at his own hands. The wounds that had covered his blood-soaked body were gone. There was no pain. The agony inside his body that he felt before losing consciousness was also gone.

"…Why in the world… is the Crazy Heavenly Demon Elder fighting Baek Museon?"

"Because that crazy bastard tried to kill you!" Rubia shouted, having washed her face with magic. She wanted to complain about him spitting blood on her, but she held it in. "More importantly, your body? Are you okay?"

"My body… feels refreshed," Lee Sungmin answered, placing a hand on his chest. He could feel the steady pounding of his heart. *Is it thanks to the Black Heart? No, I'll think about that later.* He gripped his spear with both hands and stepped forward.

Horrified, Rubia blocked his path. "W-what are you doing?! You just came back from the dead!"

"I have to do it."

"But why?! If you just rest here, that old man will take care of it!"

"I can't let that happen," Lee Sungmin replied, his voice unwavering. Rubia's face contorted as she tried to say something, but he did not listen and walked past her.

"Don't blame me if you die!"

"I won't die," Lee Sungmin replied to her fierce shout in a low voice.

The Void Monarch, nestled in the Magic Armor, chuckled. [What an incomprehensible guy. To gain a miraculous encounter in a situation like this… it feels less like a coincidence and more like fate.]

The nobleman he would meet in the North. The Void Monarch had thought it was the Crazy Heavenly Demon, but was that really the case? As long as he was traveling with Lee Sungmin, he would have met the Crazy Heavenly Demon eventually, even if it was not in the North. Could a meeting like that really be called the meeting with the nobleman the Spirit had spoken of?

[So your nobleman was that Vampire,] the Void Monarch muttered, realizing the truth. The Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon, whom he met in the North. He did not teach Lee Sungmin anything. However, through the fight with Baek Museon, Lee Sungmin suffered a fatal injury, and in the process, gained a miraculous encounter where the entanglement of his mind, qi, and body was resolved. In truth, the meeting with Baek Museon alone could not be seen as the meeting with the nobleman. If the Crazy Heavenly Demon had not stopped Baek Museon, if the Crazy Heavenly Demon had not made Baek Museon and Lee Sungmin meet, this would not have happened.

[It's fate,] the Void Monarch muttered. The accumulation of such causes led to the current result. The Spirit's prophecies were always like this. They foretold destiny, made one act according to that destiny, and in the end, created that destiny.

Lee Sungmin listened to the Void Monarch's words as he slowly walked forward. His steps were light. His body was light. At the same time, he felt a fulfilling power fill his entire being. His body, with the entanglement of mind, qi, and body resolved, gave him a sense of stability incomparable to before. However, he was still far from it. The state of non-action he had reached in the Mental World was still distant. Even so, the direction of the path he had to walk was now clearly laid out for him.

"Hmph!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was blocking Baek Museon's attacks, felt Lee Sungmin approaching from behind and stopped his assault. Baek Museon also halted his relentless offensive, staring with wide eyes at the approaching Lee Sungmin.

"Weren't you the one who wasn't going to interfere?" Lee Sungmin said to the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

The old demon looked back at Lee Sungmin, blinked a few times, and then let out a laugh of disbelief. "I saved you when you were about to die, and this is…!"

"Thank you," Lee Sungmin said, his gratitude honest. "That's enough now."

He walked past the Crazy Heavenly Demon. In that passing moment, the old man shuddered. The presence emanating from Lee Sungmin was incomparably stronger than before.

"This is crazy… What kind of insights just pop out as if they were waiting for you to be on the verge of death?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat out in disbelief.

Lee Sungmin smirked at his words and muttered, "That's what I'd like to know." He raised the tip of his spear. "It seems I have a body that can't die easily."

Baek Museon's face, now at the point of that spear, contorted.
