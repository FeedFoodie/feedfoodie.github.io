"Haha…"

A long laugh echoed as Kim Jonghyun descended. To the Holy Knights who had just fought their way through the city's crushed ruins and its zombie hordes, he was the very image of a Demon Lord, his black robe fluttering and his red eyes gleaming. In that moment, he was not just like a Demon Lord. He *was* one.

*Will the Holy Knights be of any help?*

He knew the holy power radiating from them and their disciples was a force antithetical to his own, but it would be useless if the difference in their class was too great. Still, they were a nuisance, and Kim Jonghyun acted immediately to remove them.

His hands moved in a blur, wreathed in magic as they tore through space and manifested his will.

KWAANG!

Black flames erupted around him, linking together into a circular ring that exploded in a chain reaction, unleashing a storm of fire. Dozens of Holy Knights were engulfed before they could even scream. Teresa's face went deathly pale. The sight of Kim Jonghyun laughing and waving his hands within the inferno was nothing like the mindless zombies she had faced. This was a man, smiling as he killed other people, a reality that shattered one of her most fundamental beliefs.

Lee Sungmin shot forward, leaping over the heads of the Holy Knights to bring his spear down on Kim Jonghyun. A delighted smile spread across Kim Jonghyun's face as he raised both hands, spreading them to meet the attack. The moment Lee Sungmin confirmed the formation of a defensive barrier, his body erupted in violet electric currents. He descended in a flash of Gale Thunder, thrusting his spear toward Kim Jonghyun's exposed side. Kim Jonghyun twisted away just before the point could pierce him. The spear grazed his side, tearing his robe, and their eyes met.

"Kneel," Kim Jonghyun muttered.

KUUUUNG!

An immense pressure crushed Lee Sungmin's shoulders. His knees buckled, and he nearly collapsed but just barely managed to withstand the force. That single moment of hesitation was all Kim Jonghyun needed. His hands turned toward Lee Sungmin.

KIIIIING!

A piercing ring echoed in Lee Sungmin's ears as five small magic circles materialized over his body.

"Bang," Kim Jonghyun whispered.

KWAAAANG!

A sound incomparably louder than his whisper erupted. As he watched Lee Sungmin become engulfed in the explosion, Kim Jonghyun took a few steps back.

With every battle, he grew stronger. His physical abilities, his command of words, his aptitude for magic, all of it was sharpening as he became accustomed to the perspective of a Demon Lord in combat. It did not matter that it was temporary. He quickly calculated his growth rate against the remaining duration of the magic. Even if his growth was slower than expected, he was confident he could resolve the entire situation in Germud before his time ran out.

*The Mages Guild Master has lost his power. There's no need to go out of my way to kill the Grand Sorcerer. I'll just kill the Holy Knights and Priests.*

As for Lee Sungmin, he still had not decided. The lingering desire to make him a companion had not disappeared.

"You bastard!" a knight roared as they charged. Shrouded in the Priests' buffs, their bodies flowed with a dazzling white light.

An unpleasant light, Kim Jonghyun thought with a smirk, though it might have affected a low-class Undead. He swept his hands through the air, and with each pass, small magic bullets of jet-black light materialized. Magic Missile was a simple spell that required no incantation, but infused with enough power, it could easily pierce a man.

PABABAK!

Hundreds of the bullets shot forward. The Holy Knights' armor and shields offered no protection. Horses convulsed as the bullets tore through them, throwing their riders to the ground only to be pierced by more of the deadly projectiles.

"Get a hold of yourself!"

The Archbishop's roar startled Teresa back to her senses. She gripped her rosary with trembling hands.

WHOOOOSH!

A brilliant light burst from her, and the Holy Knights who had suffered fatal wounds staggered back to their feet. Kim Jonghyun watched their injuries heal in an instant and nodded his head in acknowledgment. He had to respect the holy power of the Saint. She was not a threat, however. If fatal wounds were not enough, he would simply have to kill them instantly. He brought his hands together before his chest, formed a hand seal, and smiled faintly.

Maintaining his Protective Qi Aura against the continuous explosions, Lee Sungmin finally emerged from the blast. He was met with a scene of utter carnage. Piles of corpses, both Holy Knights and their horses, were scattered across the ground. He saw Kim Jonghyun extending his hands toward the faltering Priests, whose white barrier shattered under his assault. Overwhelmed, they coughed up blood and collapsed. In the rear, Teresa stood with her hands clasped, her eyes full of tears.

*How many survived?*

He had only been trapped in that explosion for a few minutes, yet in that time, the majority of the Holy Knights had been annihilated. Fewer than ten remained, including Theos, the Silver Knight Captain. The Priests had suffered a devastating blow as well. While the Archbishop and Teresa were still standing, most of their brethren could only kneel and recite prayers in hoarse voices, and the crowd… there were far too many corpses. Thousands had died in mere minutes. Their numbers had been enough to fight zombies, but fighting a Demon Lord was impossible. It was just like the old tales. An army could not fight a Demon Lord. Only a hero could.

A hand grabbed his ankle. Lee Sungmin steadied his ragged breath and looked down to see a dying man staring up at him with bloodshot eyes.

"Please…"

The word was left unfinished, but Lee Sungmin knew exactly what the man expected. Nearby, Theos rolled on the ground, his side torn open. He had no time to groan before holy power shone upon him, regenerating the wound in an instant. He could not die even if he wanted to. Staggering to his feet, he charged Kim Jonghyun again. The Holy Knights now numbered fewer than ten, but the light from the Priests and Teresa sustained them, the blessing just enough to keep Kim Jonghyun's attacks from killing them instantly.

*Kim Jonghyun is the first disaster of The End.*

Jeniela's words had once puzzled him. After all, Kim Jonghyun claimed he wanted to help them escape The End. Lee Sungmin had assumed she meant that connecting to the Great Demon World was a disaster comparable to The End itself. He knew better now. Kim Jonghyun was more than a catalyst. He *was* The End.

His gaze met Theos's again as the knight rolled on the ground, his chest caved in. The Priests, chanting prayers as they raised their fallen barrier, looked at him. Teresa looked at him. He knew what they were all expecting, what they so desperately wished for. They might not know about The End, but they were desperate to stop Kim Jonghyun.

*Please.*

The dying man's word echoed in his mind. They called him the Phantom Spear, treated him as a demonic practitioner, yet here he had become their hero, the nemesis of the Demon Lord. The role did not sit well with him, but he was just as desperate as they were. He wanted to stop The End, and he wanted to stop Kim Jonghyun.

[It's dangerous,] Void Monarch warned.

[You're close to your limit. The fight with Vollander put a great strain on you, and Kim Jonghyun is much stronger than you thought. You've used too much Demonic Energy.]

However, Lee Sungmin remembered the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's last will. He had made his choice then, deciding to inherit that power even if it meant becoming a Yokai. The Chairman had gone to such lengths to give him the possibility of a future, to present a path forward when his growth had stagnated. He had to believe. He had to believe he would not become a Yokai, just as the Chairman had believed when he left the will telling Lee Sungmin to consume him.

Demonic Energy flooded his qi center, mixing with the inner arts of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder before spreading throughout his entire body. He cast a buff on himself, and the fatigue weighing him down vanished completely. The headache, however, remained.

It was not enough.

More.

For some time now, Lee Sungmin had kept a safety lock on himself, using only enough power to avoid going berserk. Even when his Demonic Energy ran rampant against the Spear King, he had tried his best to control it. The only time he had ever truly drawn out his power without caring for the consequences was in Ruvus, when he fought the Dark Supreme to protect Wiji Hoyeon. He had kept the lock on ever since, becoming even more careful after absorbing Urmuri's Demonic Energy, but not now. Not when he had to stop Kim Jonghyun here.

No, not just stop him. He had to kill him.

"......"

His heart pounded loudly in his chest.

KWAJIJIJIK!

The blow tore through space so violently that Kim Jonghyun's mind went blank. The enchanted robe, layered with defensive barriers, disintegrated into dust from the single strike. His body flew a considerable distance, crushing a building as it slammed into the ground. *What was that?* He hurriedly regained his senses.

A bolt of violet lightning fell from the sky. "Stop," Kim Jonghyun commanded, spreading his hands. A dark barrier materialized to block the attack, a tactic to buy just enough time to escape with Blink. It was the most logical course of action.

*No…*

He was wrong.

The command word was torn apart. The descending lightning did not stop. Its force ripped through his barrier unabated.

Lee Sungmin pulled his spear free. Kim Jonghyun barely managed to cast Blink, but not before half of his body was blown away. His immortality defied death, however, and he reappeared at a distance. Lee Sungmin silently turned his head and looked up toward the sky where he now hovered. The upward gaze sent a chill down Kim Jonghyun's spine.

*Impossible. He got stronger…? And he doesn't even have the growth power of a Demon Lord. Don't tell me he was hiding that much power until now?*

While Kim Jonghyun reeled in confusion, Lee Sungmin acted. He spun the spear once and threw it. The motion seemed light, but to Kim Jonghyun, it was devastating. His defensive barrier shattered again. Sacrificing his left arm to the blow, Kim Jonghyun flew forward, his remaining hand wreathed in black magic as he reached for Lee Sungmin. Lee Sungmin simply extended his own free hand to meet the attack.

KUWAAANG!

It was Kim Jonghyun who was thrown back. He looked down and saw that both his arms were gone. He hastily uttered a command. "Stop, stop." He repeated the word over and over, but Lee Sungmin did not stop.

*This is…*

An unpleasant premonition crept up from deep within his chest. It was the feeling that he might die here.

{sep}

Abel staggered to his feet. Ignoring his bewildered brother, he took something from within his robes. It was the Dragon serum he had taken from the Blue Magic Tower Master, the one extracted from Lee Sungmin's blood.

"Frau, this is enough," Abel said, opening the cap. "Thanks to you, we got this far, but I never thought it would turn out like this."

"…Abel."

"You should leave the city. Don't get caught up in this. It's not too late for you to get out."

"You… what are you trying to do?"

Instead of answering, Abel downed the entire serum. His depleted magic surged back to fullness. He had kept it just in case, never thinking he would actually have to use it, but now he was fortunate for the preparation. Still, it was not enough.

"Brother." Abel turned slowly to look at Cain, at the man whose eyes could barely see and whose legs could not move, who was barely suppressing the ravages of age. Abel's fingertips pointed toward Rubia. "I don't know what you'll think of this, Brother."

PAT.

A burst of light enveloped Rubia too quickly for her to react. Her eyes went blank, and she collapsed. Cain's shoulders flinched.

"I think," Abel continued, his voice low, "that now you must pay for your sins."

"I… I…"

"Don't make excuses." Abel had no ill will toward the girl. He did not even know who she was. He had only knocked her out because she would have panicked at what he was about to do. He glanced at Frau. "Don't think of it as a bother. Just take that beastfolk and leave the city."

"I know who she is," Frau said, his expression bitter. He knew what Abel intended. "…Will you be okay?"

"It's something I've wanted to do since I was a child."

"No, not that."

"Why are you being so dramatic?" Abel smirked and approached Cain, whose shoulders trembled violently in his wheelchair.

Penance.

Cain bit his lower lip. He had tried to save the world in his own way, to save it from the fate of The End, but what was the point of saving the world if he himself died?

"…Was I wrong?" he asked.

"Wanting to preserve your own life isn't wrong," Abel muttered. "But ruining everything when it was almost finished, that is wrong. Even if you didn't intend to, Brother."

"So… you're going to kill me?"

"I'm pissed off that things turned out this way," Abel admitted, "but that's not the only reason." Though it was a significant part of it. He raised his hand and grabbed Cain's throat. Cain's mouth fell open.

Immortality was a desire cherished by many mages, but the Mages Guild only permitted extending one's given lifespan with magic. Anything more was taboo. Stealing another's lifespan to make it one's own was a forbidden Dark Magic. As the Guild Master, Abel had encountered the theory a few times, but he had never used it. He had never imagined the target would be his own brother. He had never imagined he would be the one to commit such a sin.

"Kuh… ugh…" Cain let out a pained groan as Abel's hand tightened around his throat.

Penance. He tried to accept it, but his hands still flailed. He did not want to die. He had not clung to life back with Urmuri, but this was different. Here, in the Elemental Realm, he had escaped The End. Or so he had thought.

With a failing voice, he squeezed out the words. "With my… death… can The End… be stopped…"

"I don't know," Abel replied, his expression blank. "Because thanks to you, Brother, what could have been stopped can no longer be stopped."

A terrible death. In his final moments, Cain understood the fate that awaited him. He had lost everything. His entire life had been negated. No one could save him now, and no one would pity him. He was being strangled by his own younger brother, a man filled with contempt. The fact that he was once the most outstanding archmage in the world held no value now.

And so, Cain died.

Abel released his grip. Cain's body, his neck broken, slumped out of the wheelchair. Abel looked down at his brother with melancholic eyes. He had stolen Cain's lifespan.

*It's not even that much.*

Swallowing the self-loathing that came with committing a taboo, Abel turned away. It was not a long life he had taken, but it would be enough.
