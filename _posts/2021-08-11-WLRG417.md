A single drop of blood arced through the air.

Wiji Hoyeon's eyebrow twitched. She had barely steadied herself from the stagger when Lee Sungmin unleashed a torrent of attacks, giving her no moment to recover. The enhanced qi gathering behind his back coalesced into the form of an Asura, its six arms blurring into motion as the Formless Spear swirled around him. Hundreds of spears, each a manifestation of spearmanship at its absolute pinnacle, shot toward Wiji Hoyeon.

Though she had retreated a little, she was almost unharmed. She was still human and did not possess the same regenerative abilities as Lee Sungmin, but her body could no longer be called merely human. Wiji Hoyeon met the relentless assault with a direct counter, her eyes seeing much and her movements impossibly light. If Lee Sungmin's power was a destructive force that could shatter the world, she was a feather, a speck of dust carried away on the slightest breeze.

Watching her move, Lee Sungmin felt a profound sense of awe. He knew her talent was a gift from the Demonic Spirit, yet a question still echoed in his mind.

*Could I have done that?*

Wiji Hoyeon remained untouched by the Formless Spear's assault, which had dominated the entire space. With minimal movement, she achieved the optimal result. The tiny opening she created with a sweep of her hand became a path, a channel that deflected his attacks in completely different directions the moment they met her touch.

In that same instant, Lee Sungmin seized control of the space anew with Swift Storm Extinction. Lightning flooded the area in every direction, and at its center, Wiji Hoyeon quietly raised both her hands.

There were no corpses left in this world, only a pit of stench where bodies had been annihilated. Amidst it all, Wiji Hoyeon recalled the scent of a nameless wildflower. It was a sight Lee Sungmin had seen before, back in the Fairy Forest when they had lived there together.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had taught him that the insights of the Transcendent Realm involved interfering with space, and progress was made by establishing a 'directionality' based on that interference. If Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was speed, then the Asura Heaven Destruction Art was power. The martial arts of the Martial God pursued the pinnacle of illusion. One's level in the Transcendent Realm was determined by the directionality they chose and how completely they mastered it.

During their time in the forest, Wiji Hoyeon had been undergoing that very process. Lee Sungmin, as a disciple of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, had only needed to learn Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, not create it himself. Wiji Hoyeon, however, was different. Having rejected the Chairman's teachings, she had forged a new martial art for herself from scratch. Her art had been incomplete right up until they left the forest and parted ways, yet even with that incomplete martial art, she had earned the Chairman's praise and fought on equal footing with Lee Sungmin. She had never named it, reasoning that an incomplete art deserved no name.

This was a story of a ten-year fighting spirit. Within a world of crackling lightning, a field of flowers was born. Red blossoms made of enhanced qi filled the space around Wiji Hoyeon as she tracked Lee Sungmin's darting form with cold eyes. Her fingertips moved.

PANG.

The flowers all bloomed at once, their bursting petals scattering in every direction. Each petal was a fragment of enhanced qi compressed to its absolute limit, forming a martial art more beautiful than anything in the world.

"Full Bloom," Wiji Hoyeon muttered.

The flowers of enhanced qi multiplied, petals becoming new blossoms. Having conquered every Dungeon and absorbed their power, Wiji Hoyeon's inner qi knew no limit. Myriad Thunder erupted from Lee Sungmin, and the explosion he caused collided with the one she had created.

It felt as if not just the sound, but the entire world was disappearing. The colliding powers canceled each other out and vanished, shaking the very fabric of space, yet despite the immense forces at play, the space did not shatter. A pleased laugh echoed from a distance, the laugh of that damned Spirit.

*Domination, control... In terms of sheer completeness, it's superior to Heaven Reversal Black Thunder,* the memories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman judged.

KUUUUUUUNG!

The sound echoed belatedly as a great gust of wind pulled Wiji Hoyeon and Lee Sungmin toward each other. The collision had broken the laws of physics, the twisted gravity creating a black hole that swallowed everything. Wiji Hoyeon was not flustered in the slightest. Flowers bloomed once more as she brought her hands together before her chest.

PASUSUSUSU!

Countless flowers crumbled, becoming hundreds of millions of butterflies that took flight.

Lee Sungmin took a sharp breath and manifested the Formless Spear. The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear? No, that was just martial arts theory, and it was too weak for this. He gritted his teeth. He possessed the memories of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Blood Demon, men who had reached the pinnacle of martial arts, but there was no time to use their knowledge to create an art superior to hers.

Genesis, Genesis, Genesis.

The Formless Spear unleashed consecutive bursts of light that swept the butterflies away. Martial arts alone were not enough.

Lee Sungmin quickly chanted in Dragonic, wrapping his body in all sorts of magic. Faster, stronger. His eyes locked onto Wiji Hoyeon's position, read her spatial coordinates in an instant, and unleashed his spell. A fiery explosion engulfed her body, but at that moment, blooming flowers wrapped around her like a shield.

*Her offense and defense are perfect. She can react to this speed? Is it prediction, or simple reflexes? I don't know.*

He had a bad feeling. He continued to cast Dragonic Magic, the spells instantaneous and incredibly fast without the need for chanting, but they did not work on her.

*I'm sure of it.*

His unease became reality. It was not an immediate crisis, but thinking about the future, it was a damn, shitty situation.

*She's growing, even in this moment.*

He was certain of it. Her reactions now were different from when the fight began. Slowly but surely, Wiji Hoyeon was adapting and growing stronger.

Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh at the shocking realization. For now, one thing was certain. If Wiji Hoyeon had been tasked with stopping The End as originally planned, she would have done so without any trouble, even against a being that could destroy the world itself.

*I was arrogant,* he reproached himself. Saying sorry because he might hurt her? Who was worried about whom?

The butterflies moved, and the flowers had already enveloped the entire space. This place was no longer a desolate hell but a field of blooming red flowers. The butterflies frolicked among them, their patterns impossible to see through, impossible to predict. Dozens of his layered barriers shattered in an instant. He created hundreds more to endure, knowing it would not take long for them to break as well.

Lee Sungmin drew out all his power and created a rotation. It was a form of sky-piercing that was not sky-piercing, a ridiculously brutish force that pierced through the flower field.

"This is fun," a small mutter was heard.

Lee Sungmin flinched and looked at Wiji Hoyeon. Far in the distance, she was nodding her head. Their gazes met, and he saw the smile on her lips, saw a new emotion in her eyes.

Wiji Hoyeon was feeling joy from this fight.

"I..."

"Fighting you is fun, but," Wiji Hoyeon muttered, "still, I can't keep fighting. I, I mean, I have to. I have to kill... and kill again."

He had held onto a sliver of hope that in fighting him, her mind might return. Her muttered words completely denied that hope.

*It's okay.*

*It's not okay.*

He felt disgusted by the whole situation, by how things had turned out.

"When is the end?" Wiji Hoyeon muttered. She was already moving to kill him. It could not be helped. The Wiji Hoyeon of now was broken, having done too much and killed too many things alone. He knew she was not made of iron. The confidence she possessed was not something she had from the beginning. It came from her life before Eria, as the Demonic Cult's Young Cult Leader. That background and position had simply ingrained it in her from a young age.

*You might not know me now, but I know the you of now. I know the you of the past.*

When they first met in Zenavis, she was thirteen and he was fourteen. She had been flustered by the sudden summoning, yet she had not wanted to return to this world of fighting spirits. She adapted to the new world at a speed unthinkable for someone so young, and she made him, a complete stranger, her friend.

*I know.*

She had liked living freely here. More than returning to her own world to live as the Demonic Cult's Young Cult Leader, she had wanted to live in this world where no one knew her, as 'just' Wiji Hoyeon. She had told him so casually that she had no friends, that he was her first, all with a shameless smile. She had been lonely from such a young age. Even when she found out he had approached her with ulterior motives, she felt no betrayal. She still considered him her first friend.

*You were always like that. Confident and strong on the outside... but actually fragile. So, I can understand.*

He could understand that she was crushed by loneliness, that she had finally gone mad in this space. However.

*It's okay if you Despair.*

"It's okay if you're broken," he muttered, advancing. "Because I won't Despair."

Do not Despair. Do not break. Do not die.

The words of the Void Monarch supported his spirit. Even in this situation that could drive him mad with despair, Lee Sungmin absolutely could not give in to it. He had promised. He promised Baek Sogo he would return. He had also promised Wiji Hoyeon that if she could not do it, he would.

"......" Wiji Hoyeon muttered as she watched his forced smile. The flowers surged like waves, and the butterflies flew.

The Formless Spears all vanished. Lee Sungmin gripped a single spear and walked forward. With every step, he could feel her killing intent. Her power had completely dominated the space, and within it, his presence was minuscule.

"...What is this?" Wiji Hoyeon asked.

KWAAAAANG!

The spear Lee Sungmin swung caused an explosion. A flash of light erupted, becoming lightning that annihilated the butterflies. The flowers burst, their petals swirling violently.

"You... Who are you?"

At her dazed question, Lee Sungmin stretched the muscles in his cheeks into a wide smile and stomped his foot on the air. Wiji Hoyeon quickly thrust out both her palms. He did not dodge. He pulled the spear, held in both hands, back behind his waist.

Memories, experience. He made a judgment based on all of it.

*Wiji Hoyeon fights to kill, and I cannot kill her. The difference is clear. A war of attrition is not an option. Even now, she is growing, and at some point, her power will surpass mine. If that happens, it won't even be a fight.*

In that case.

*Decisively.*

Everything I have. What I learned from you. What I mastered. What I received.

"I am," Lee Sungmin's mouth opened. "Lee Sungmin."

He took a large step forward.

"Your first friend."

He took another step, shooting his spear forward to cancel out her palm strike. Facing the Wiji Hoyeon beyond it, he took a breath and told her.

"Your... first man."

He felt a little embarrassed and laughed. Wiji Hoyeon wore a look of utter confusion. She blinked at him, then frowned and muttered.

"Are you insane?"

That expression and her scornful words truly hurt, made even more painful by the memory of the Wiji Hoyeon who used to giggle, saying she wanted to touch and hug him. Still, it was okay.

Lee Sungmin did not Despair.
