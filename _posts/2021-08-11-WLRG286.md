The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's tone was calm, but his words landed with the force of a physical blow. Lee Sungmin's mouth, in particular, could not help but fall open.

He had heard it from Jeniela. The woman with the All-Seeing Eye had directly told the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman about his own death. Lee Sungmin's mind raced back to the conversation he had overheard between them before he left the mansion.

"Why... is Master going to die?"

"She wouldn't tell me about that," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman grumbled, tightening his grip on the Black Dragon Knight's neck. He looked down at the man, his eyes glinting with killing intent. "A spiteful woman, that one."

"So swear an oath," he commanded. "If this one dies, there will be no way to control you."

"W-wait! I understand the principle, but this is too drastic..."

"Do you want a demonstration of what's truly drastic?" the Chairman retorted. "In truth, I don't need an oath from you. Killing you here would be a much easier and more convenient method. Is that what you wish for me to do?"

"Th-that's..."

"Still, I've grown somewhat fond of you during our months-long journey, which is why I'm advising you to swear. If you insist on refusing, then I'll have no choice but to deal with you before you become a nuisance. Right. Since we've bonded over a few months, I won't kill you. Instead, I'll destroy your qi center and cut off your limbs. How does that sound?"

There was no hint of a bluff in the Chairman's eyes as he spoke.

The Black Dragon Knight swallowed hard. The Chairman continued to press the hesitating man.

"Think carefully. You can't return to the Martial God anyway. Even if it was under duress, you still betrayed him. If you go back and beg for forgiveness, do you really think the Martial God will mercifully forgive you?"

At those words, the Black Dragon Knight gave it serious thought. He pictured the Martial God he knew. He was not a particularly cruel person, but no matter how he looked at it, the man was not the type to be generous to a traitor. Even if he avoided death, he would certainly pay a steep price.

"What... what do you want me to do?" the knight asked, his voice strained. "You said you might die! If you die, who's going to protect me?"

"This one's disciple will protect you."

"Damn it, don't be ridiculous! How can the Phantom Spear, who's weaker than me, possibly protect me?"

"No, it's not ridiculous," the Chairman stated flatly. "This one's disciple can protect you. He will stop the Martial God, if a situation arises where he must."

Honestly, even Lee Sungmin could not understand his master's words. Although he had made great progress with the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, he could not guarantee victory against the Black Dragon Knight or the Spear King. He could not say that to the Chairman right now, however.

"Choose," the Chairman said, his voice leaving no room for argument. "Swear the oath, or refuse and become a cripple here."

"Damn it!"

As if to make good on his threat, the Chairman's hand clamped down on the Black Dragon Knight's shoulder. An agonizing pressure crushed bone and muscle. The Black Dragon Knight cursed and squeezed his eyes shut.

"I'll swear, I'll swear! I, Regon, will never betray Lee Sungmin, Scarlet Reshir, and Yehwa. I will never bring them harm!"

"That's enough."

At the knight's desperate shout, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded. He released the man's acupoints and stood up. Even a master of the Transcendent Realm could not defy an oath sworn upon their own name, mana, and inner qi. That promise was one of the fundamental laws of this world, and breaking it would only lead to one's own death.

The Black Dragon Knight covered his face with both hands, lamenting his wretched, fallen state.

"...To die..."

Only after the oath was finished did Lee Sungmin mutter the words in a dazed voice. It was unbelievable. The martial artist he knew as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was like a great mountain, a figure of immense strength who could never collapse. Even Jeniella of Blood Charm, the de facto leader of Predator, did not wish for a fight with him. Hadn't the Void Monarch himself acknowledged the Chairman's power?

And yet, he was going to die. Lee Sungmin could not possibly imagine it. The Chairman glanced at him.

"What's with that expression?" he asked. "Are you depressed because this one is going to die?"

"How can you be so calm?"

"Everyone dies someday. It's a natural thing, and it's not happening right now. Is there any need to feel depressed?"

"But...!"

"This one has lived without regrets." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman raised a hand, cutting Lee Sungmin off. "It has been so until now, and it will continue to be so. Death is inevitable, but this one will choose the time and place of this one's death. So, don't say anything else."

"But... Master..."

"Didn't the Vampire Queen say so? She foretold this one's death, and she knew her words would not restrict my actions. She was right. This one is not shaken. I already know that I will die someday."

Scarlet watched the Chairman without a word. She recalled the secret request he had made to her in the carriage during their journey, a request she had already granted. It had not been a particularly difficult task. Thinking about it now, it seemed as if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman already knew he would die soon, even without Jeniela mentioning the future.

*It might be a leap in logic,* Scarlet thought, swallowing a sigh.

"We're going to Wudang," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman announced. "I need to speak with the Wudang Sword Immortal. The Mages Guild Master might be there as well. I don't know if he's still around... but if he is, I can talk to him at the same time."

"Are we riding the Fairy Horse?"

"No, we're not," the Chairman replied, shaking his head. "There's only one chance left, and I don't think we should waste it on something like this." He climbed into the carriage. "Don't tell Yehwa about this."

Yehwa, who thought of the Chairman as a parent, would have a fit if she knew he might die.

*What about me?* Lee Sungmin wondered as he quietly followed his master into the carriage. He did not consider the man a parent, but he did consider him a Master. He respected him. He was grateful for all the help he had received, and yet, was he supposed to just watch him die?

"Don't have any useless thoughts," the Chairman said before Lee Sungmin could speak. "Didn't this one say that this one would choose? And I don't believe in the future Jeniela sees. I just can't accept that the future is set in stone. She said it would never go astray if the situation and conditions are met, but I believe there are enough variables."

"Then..."

"This one intends to defy the future she saw." The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman laughed as he put his mask back on. "Doesn't that sound quite fun?"

His voice was filled with excitement and amusement.

It was the first time he had been on a ship for this long.

He had wondered, with a detached sort of curiosity, if he might suffer from seasickness. He had never properly been on a ship before, so perhaps he would fall ill once he boarded one and started living on it. If so, it would have been an interesting experience. Seasickness did not seem to suit him, and suffering from something so mundane would be uncommon.

However, there was no seasickness, a fact Kim Jonghyun found mildly disappointing. It was something you were born with, not something you could have just because you wanted it. In truth, it was not something to be enjoyed, but he could not help but feel a little let down.

"Now, then."

While he was lost in such meaningless contemplation, the preparations were complete. Kim Jonghyun slowly rose to his feet. He had already spent over half a year on Vollander's Ghost Ship. Fortunately, the Death Knights accompanying him were neither impatient nor senseless. When Kim Jonghyun told them that 'sufficient preparation is needed', they did not rush their return to being human and joined him in waiting for several months. To them, such a delay could not even be called waiting. Thanks to that, Kim Jonghyun had enjoyed a comfortable life, escorted by hundreds of Death Knights.

There was a good reason he had specifically stayed on the sea, on this Ghost Ship. Jeniela had encouraged the deal with Vollander, but Kim Jonghyun had been considering Vollander from the beginning.

Arbes had been annihilated by his own scheme. The Red Ghost, leader of the Yokai, had his heart torn out by Yana of Urmuri, leaving the position vacant. The King of Lycanthropes, Ju Won, was an opponent Kim Jonghyun could not control. Jaded by long years, the Mad Wolf no longer possessed his former ferocity and simplicity. Besides Ju Won's disposition, Kim Jonghyun had nothing to offer him and knew of some relationship between the lycanthrope and Jeniela.

*I must not touch Jeniela.*

It was not just about her ability to see the future. Kim Jonghyun was not confident he could handle her power.

In the end, that left only Vollander, the leader of the Death Knight Order. In fact, Vollander was the most suitable opponent for Kim Jonghyun to handle. They longed to become human and had no choice but to obey his words.

Beyond being able to handle them efficiently, there was another reason Kim Jonghyun had sought out Vollander. It was the same reason he had been drifting on this vast, open sea for months without going to land. Death Knights are different from other Undead. They lost their physical bodies, forming an Astral Body from their soul and wearing armor over it. A Lich was similar, but the fundamental difference was that a Lich chose their path, while a Death Knight was a being that had been cursed or completely corrupted.

The legion led by Vollander was not composed of corrupted knights, but of cursed ones. It was not common, but it happened on rare occasions. A knight might be targeted by Dark Magic and cursed to become a Death Knight. They might be cursed in a Dungeon where the nonsensical was commonplace, or forced into a contract by a cursed weapon. Having hundreds of such knights gathered together was an incredibly attractive prospect for Kim Jonghyun. He was interested in the enormous death qi emitted by hundreds of Death Knights and the powerful Vollander.

*It is done.*

Kim Jonghyun looked down at the magic circle drawn on the floor, a pleased expression on his face. It was a design that gathered the death qi filling the air around the Ghost Ship, an aura so thick that those with low resistance could die just from breathing it. He took a deep breath of the death qi and opened the Grimoire.

The energy emitted by the Death Knights was the power of a low-quality Demon Lord. The power of the Demon Lord who cursed them had formed their Astral Body, then degraded in quality to become death qi. Nevertheless, its origin was a Demon Lord. Kim Jonghyun licked his lower lip once before focusing his consciousness on the Grimoire.

He had tried to become a Demon Lord but had failed. He could no longer use the half-fighting spirit authority and held no lingering attachment to it. What he was attempting now was not an act of trying to change his incomplete body back into a Demon Lord. It was simple curiosity.

SHHH... SHHH...

Death flowed. The death qi, gathered over several months, condensed and swirled.

Kim Jonghyun smiled faintly. The magic would not fail. There was no one to interfere this time, and even if someone did, Vollander and the Death Knights would act as a shield. He had made such preparations, but there was no interference.

The magic was completed as planned, and a pitch-black door materialized before Kim Jonghyun.

He slowly approached it. He had opened a door to the Demon Realm through the aura of death connected to the Demon Lords. He did not even need to reach out. The door slowly opened before him.

The moment he was about to step inside, Kim Jonghyun's steps halted.

*Do not enter.*

The voice echoed in his head.

*If you enter, you can never return.*

At the low warning, Kim Jonghyun's eyes gleamed.
