Wiji Hoyeon did not know where to go. The path and the place she had seen in her dream were so vague that she questioned if they could even be called such. The vision was a useless, hazy memory that offered no clue to her destination.

Despite this, she knew. It was a belief without certainty, a feeling that if she separated from Lee Sungmin and traveled alone, the way would reveal itself.

And that was exactly what happened.

The moment she parted ways with him to begin her journey into the unknown, Wiji Hoyeon realized where she had to go. The Hyujael Mountains. She had never been to the place, yet she knew the direction as if guided by an unseen hand.

*This is the first time I've come this deep.*

The journey south had been long, but she had faced no real threats along the way. Now, deep within the rugged southern mountain range, she looked around at the foggy surroundings. This was a place that rejected human intrusion, a wild land where all sorts of monsters lived and breathed. The Hyujael Mountains were a nest for barbaric Yokai, creatures so savage that even other monsters gave them a wide berth. Not far from here was the city of Urmuri, where relatively gentlemanly Yokai, those who possessed reason and controlled their desires, made their homes.

This forest, however, was different. The Yokai living here were more faithful to their instincts than to reason, ferocious beasts unable to live within the city. Even so, they were no match for Wiji Hoyeon. Clueless Yokai had attacked her from the moment she entered the mountains, but after meeting repeated, swift deaths, the frequency of their assaults dwindled. Faithful to their instincts, they had belatedly realized the human who had entered their domain was a being they could not hunt.

*What is here?*

She had not yet reached her destination. She would have to climb a little further to arrive. Wiji Hoyeon did not know it, but she was heading toward a hidden lake on the mountainside, a place known as the Demonic Spirit Pavilion. It was where Yana had once obtained the power of the Nine-Tailed Fox, a lake where one could perform a Spirit Possession with a Demonic Spirit. Unaware of this, Wiji Hoyeon continued her ascent, chasing the vague yet certain landscape from her dream. It was a place she had never been, a place she did not know, yet she felt her destination was not far.

Her steps halted before she reached the pavilion.

Through the thick fog, she saw someone perched on a large boulder. The fog was dense, but Wiji Hoyeon's vision pierced through it, allowing her to see the person clearly.

"This is a rough mountain for a woman to be climbing alone."

A rustling sound followed as he unwrapped a small, neat bundle. The Martial God took out a large rice ball and a boiled egg, a faint smile on his face.

"Aren't you hungry?"

"Not particularly," Wiji Hoyeon replied, staring blankly at him.

At her answer, the Martial God let out a hearty laugh. He took a noisy bite of the large rice ball and chewed. "I made this myself. It might not seem like it, but one of my hobbies is cooking. If you're going to eat, it's better to eat something delicious, and if you make it yourself, you can suit it perfectly to your own taste, can't you?"

After saying that, he finished the entire rice ball in another bite. He even took out some alcohol.

"You'll regret it if you don't eat."

"I'm not hungry."

"You don't appreciate the atmosphere," he said, gesturing around. "The scenery is so nice here."

Wiji Hoyeon did not know the man was the Martial God, but she could sense that the figure sitting on the rock was an absolute master, comparable to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Still, she was not shaken. She stared intently at his face.

"Did you come here to kill me?" she asked.

"Of course not."

"Then to recruit me?"

"Do you know who I am?"

"Either the Martial God or the Sword Immortal," she stated. "It must be one of the two. Since you don't have the feel of a Taoist, I'm guessing you're the Martial God."

"Haha!" The Martial God put down his alcohol bottle and laughed loudly at her answer. "Did Ilcheon tell you about me?"

"I heard you were in seclusion training."

"Anything else?"

"He said you were weaker than him."

The Martial God roared with laughter. He, who called the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman by his name like a close friend, laughed for a long while before shaking his head. "Ilcheon is still the same. Over a hundred years have passed, and he's still the same."

"Do you think he's arrogant?"

"No. Ilcheon has the right to say such things. This is a vast world, one where humans from other dimensions are summoned every day. In a world like this, his skill in martial arts is among the very best."

"Are you stronger than the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?"

"Having been in seclusion for a hundred years, I can't say I'm weaker, if only for the sake of pride. To be honest, I don't know for sure. We'd have to fight to find out, wouldn't we?" the Martial God said as he climbed down from the boulder. "However, one thing is certain, Little Heavenly Demon. I am stronger than you."

"I know."

"Then why aren't you running away?"

"You said you wouldn't kill me."

"I could change my mind."

"You won't," Wiji Hoyeon replied, her face blank.

Her answer left the Martial God speechless. After a short silence, he let out a small laugh and nodded. "Right. I won't. Why are you climbing this mountain?"

"How did you know I was here?" she countered. She thought she had been careful, hiding her identity as much as possible and avoiding any unnecessary disturbances. Eyes and ears were everywhere, trying to capture this vast world, but she had moved to avoid their attention, and yet, the Martial God was waiting for her as if he knew she would come.

"Heaven Beyond Heaven has ways of seeing and hearing things beyond the information that circulates in the world," he explained. "It's not omnipotent, though."

He was talking about a Spirit Medium, but his words meant nothing to Wiji Hoyeon.

"I have to go up there," she said, resuming her climb.

"I have no intention of stopping you. I wasn't told I had to."

"You're supposed to be the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven, yet you speak as if you're following someone's orders."

"It's true that I'm the pinnacle, but that doesn't mean I can do as I please."

Wiji Hoyeon continued walking as she listened. When she reached the Martial God, she glanced at him once before walking past. He chuckled and fell into step behind her.

"I have a question," he said. "Will you answer if I ask?"

"If it's something I can answer."

"Why are you trying to bring me into Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

"Because you're worth it," the Martial God answered simply. "Do you know about our ideals? The Phantom Spear probably does."

"To erase the inhuman from this world, create a world for humans alone, and have Heaven Beyond Heaven rule it."

"What do you think?"

"I think it's bullshit."

"Correct," the Martial God nodded. "But such a simple and obvious ideal makes the heart race. The desire for power and honor is something everyone wants. Those who are confident in their own strength develop a sense of superiority, believing they are different from mortals and must stand above them."

"Are you saying it's a lie?"

"It's not entirely a lie."

"You don't seem to have any intention of being honest, do you?"

To that, the Martial God simply smiled faintly and did not answer. He instead asked, "Do you have any intention of joining us?"

"None." It was not even worth thinking about.

"Is it because of the Phantom Spear?"

"Why do you think that?"

"Because Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Phantom Spear are hostile to each other. If you join us, we no longer have any intention of being hostile to him."

"There's no need for that," she said. "And if I did, he wouldn't like it very much."

"I know the Phantom Spear is strong, but the reason he's survived until now is that Heaven Beyond Heaven has never seriously tried to kill him. Do you understand that?"

"Maybe so. If you wanted to kill him, you should have used your full strength long ago."

"It's not too late even now. Do you think I can't kill him?"

"Why don't you stop talking and just do it?" Wiji Hoyeon challenged. "Go subdue him, bring him before me, and threaten to kill him if I don't join. Maybe then I'll change my mind."

"There's no need for that method. I can just subdue you here and drag you away by force."

"That sounds strange too," she scoffed. "From the way you talk, it seems you don't need my power, but simply my existence."

"A half-right, half-wrong guess. Your power will certainly be a help to Heaven Beyond Heaven. In my eyes, your power is already on par with the Spear King, the Moon Queen, and the Black Dragon Knight. That's an incredible fact. Just a few years ago, Little Heavenly Demon, you were only about equal to the Fist Honored One, the last place among the Six Honored Ones. In just a few years, you've become much stronger."

"So there's a reason you need me besides my combat strength."

To that question as well, the Martial God just smiled faintly and remained silent.

Their conversation carried them to their destination. They had arrived at the Demonic Spirit Pavilion, a tranquil pond nestled on the mountainside.

Wiji Hoyeon stared blankly at the water. This was it. The place in her dream had been thick with fog, not a pond, but she was certain this was where she was meant to come.

The Martial God glanced around the strange and mystical place. The Demonic Spirit Pavilion was the only location in the world where one could perform a Spirit Possession with a Demonic Spirit, and it was not a place one could enter just by wanting to. In fact, the Martial God had wandered this mountain several times before, trying to find this very spot, but had always failed.

*Does this mean the Demonic Spirit permitted the Little Heavenly Demon's entry? The reason she came here... It's certain. Fate has begun to move.*

Until a few months ago, fate had been at a standstill, but then, the great, stagnant current began to flow. It was a shift that neither the Martial God nor the Spirit Medium knew the cause of. It had begun after Lee Sungmin went to the dungeon where he had died in his past life and took hold of the Stone of Reincarnation.

The Martial God slowly prepared himself. He had not come here just for Wiji Hoyeon. He also had business with the Demonic Spirit.

The surface of the pond rippled. A jet-black light shot up from the water and intertwined, not forming a human shape, but a round sphere. Within it, a large eye opened. A single, crimson eye floated in the air, looking down at Wiji Hoyeon and the Martial God. The sight of the lone eye was repulsive and bizarre, but neither of them was overwhelmed by it.

[Child.]

Space trembled as a voice echoed, audible only to Wiji Hoyeon.

[Child who is human, yet fated to become something other than human. You were born a warlord, but you are destined to become something more and reign.]

She had heard these words before, from the monster at the end of the dungeon where she had obtained the Black Dragon Armor, the one with the Doppelganger. A talent that twists even fate. That is what the monster had called her as it was dying.

[The light of the Constellation already shines upon you, and you shall be the first to become a warlord. From the moment you realized you 'had to come' here, and arrived, you became the greatest variable and singularity in this world's fate.]

"What am I?" she asked.

[It is still too early for you to realize that. Fate has only just begun to flow.]

"O, Demonic Spirit."

Unable to hear their exchange, the Martial God stepped forward, his preparations complete. A colossal power rose from him, a force befitting one who called himself the god of martial arts. It was an aura so destructive that no human could ever hope to approach it.

"I have something I wish to ask you."

[Human.]

The Demonic Spirit's eye finally turned to him.

[You were a being who could not come to this place.]

"I know. I have sought this place several times, but I never managed to reach it, save for today."

[The reason I allowed you in is that you came with that child. It was also a small courtesy to a being such as yourself, who has neared the end of what it means to be human.]

The Martial God's eyebrow twitched. "Are you saying that even with this fighting spirit, I was lacking?"

[You lacked the qualification, and the time was not right. Had you not come with this child, you would never have been able to reach this place in your entire life.]

"...Please answer my question."

"......"

"Is The End predetermined?" the Martial God asked.
