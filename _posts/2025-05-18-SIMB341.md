Moon Yul's eyes were closed, his expression peaceful.

"Elder?"

Baek Suryong stared blankly at Moon Yul.

He had just passed through a long dream depicting the lives of two people. It took time to readjust to the sudden return to reality.

"Elder? Are you alright?"

Suspecting this too might be a dream, Baek Suryong carefully reached out a hand towards Moon Yul.

Tuk.

Baek Suryong's hand touched his shoulder, but Moon Yul remained perfectly still.

A gentle smile rested on the old man's set lips.

"Elder...!"

Baek Suryong's expression crumpled. He gripped Moon Yul's shoulders with both arms and shook him, his tone agitated.

"There's something I want to ask. Was the person I met really Master Eun? Please just answer me that!"

Moon Yul was dead.

No matter how much he shook his shoulders, he would never wake again.

Baek Suryong knew this fact, yet still couldn't easily let go of his lingering attachment. It was uncharacteristic behavior for him.

"What am I supposed to do if you show me a dream like that and just leave like this..."

Something he had wished for so much in his past life.

The happy smiles of the Masters he saw in the dream.

It was the happiest dream he had ever had, yet Baek Suryong could no longer smile.

Because ultimately, it was a dream that could never come true.

"If I had known they were the real Masters, there were things I wanted to ask..."

Muttering weakly, Baek Suryong noticed the White Ice Bangle placed on Moon Yul's right side.

And beneath the White Ice Bangle lay a single sheet of a letter left by Moon Yul.

@[**This object lost its partner long ago. Please keep it safe so they are never separated again.**]@

It was a last will, leaving the White Ice Bangle he received as a token from his lover, Master Eun, to her disciple, Baek Suryong.

That wasn't all.

Amazingly, the writing on the letter faded away, and new words appeared upon it.

@[**O one born with a heaven-defying fate. I have seen your previous life.**]@

Baek Suryong wasn't surprised. When Moon Yul asked to read his memories, he had suspected something like this might happen.

@[**I saw why Yerin could not return to me, how much she suffered, and how deeply you care for your masters. I could dare to guess how much you must have deliberated before delivering Yerin's last words to me.**]@

The handwriting on the letter was neat and gentle. It seemed to reflect Moon Yul's personality.

@[**I wish to speak with you at length, but I do not have much time left. Thus, while you conversed with Yerin, I decided to leave these few words behind.**]@

"...Don't tell me, did you yield the time given to you, to me?"

The final moments he could have spent with his lover.

Moon Yul yielded a portion of that time to Baek Suryong.

He had arranged it so Baek Suryong could converse with Master Eun, however briefly.

aggAnnoy09

What an incredibly foolish man.

@[**What you are most curious about must be this. The dream we had it is a heaven-defying sorcery that temporarily summons the spirit of the deceased using memory as a medium.**]@

Baek Suryong's eyes shot open. Part of the letter held in his hand crumpled unconsciously.

"If that's what it was...!"

Why hadn't he said so beforehand? Baek Suryong even felt a slight resentment towards Moon Yul.

@[**Truthfully, the probability of the sorcery succeeding was not high. This is because the deeper the connection between the one performing the sorcery and the deceased, and the longer the time spent together, the higher the chance of success. This sorcery was a gamble for me as well.**]@

"Such a..."

Baek Suryong had inherited the martial arts of his four Masters and spent over ten years with them.

He was even with them in their final moments, so that connection could be said to be deeper than almost any other in the world.

@[**It was a sorcery that could only be performed once in a lifetime. So I waited. Haha. It was worth the wait. Having married Yerin and lived a happy life, I no longer have any regrets.**]@

"It was still just a dream, wasn't it? You really are an incredibly frustrating person."

Baek Suryong looked up at Moon Yul. The smile lingering on the old man's lips held sincere happiness.

@[**Do not lament the brief meeting. Though this is the last for me, it will not be the end for you. Someday, you will meet your masters again.**]@

"Meet them again?"

The words left by Moon Yul held a conviction that it would surely happen in the future. It felt less like a wish or hope and more like a prophecy.

@[**O one born with a heaven-defying fate. Even at this moment when I commune with the heavens, I cannot see your future clearly. However, what I can vaguely discern is that a moment will soon come for you to make an important decision.**]@

A heaven-defying (逆天) fate.

The Dark Heaven Goddess he met at the Namgung Clan, and the dying mother Silver Tiger, had said similar things.

aggAnnoy16

What on earth is a heaven-defying fate...

Surely they wouldn't call it a heaven-defying fate simply because he learned the Heaven Defying Divine Art.

If that were the case, then all past Blood Demons would have had heaven-defying fates.

Is it because I was reincarnated with memories of my past life? Or because of my constitution? And what's this about a choice?

He could only speculate there was nothing he could be certain about.

@[**When that moment comes, remember. The people beside you, and also the people who were once beside you...**]@

Fssssss-

The letter held in Baek Suryong's hand began to disintegrate into dust. The residual power of the sorcery was fading.

@[**Please, I pray that you become the master of your own destiny.**]@

The letter completely vanished into dust, leaving only the White Ice Bangle behind.

"......"

Baek Suryong gazed at the White Ice Bangle left by Moon Yul with complex eyes.

Though he had aimed to find Master Eun's lover, the journey had originally started to find the White Ice Bangle.

Now that he had actually achieved his goal, however, Baek Suryong collected the White Ice Bangle with complicated feelings.

"I will keep today's advice firmly in mind."

aggAnnoy18

Offering his condolences to Moon Yul, Baek Suryong fastened the White Ice Bangle he left behind onto his right wrist.

VWOOOOM-!

As the White Ice Bangles, separated for over sixty years, formed a pair, a pure white current of energy enveloped Baek Suryong's body as if resonating with each other. For an instant, Baek Suryong's martial arts uniform swelled significantly.

FWHOOOSH!

A cold wind like a northern blizzard swirled around Baek Suryong's body before slowly subsiding. The pure white current seeped into his body.

"Ssssp... Hoo..."

Baek Suryong sat cross-legged and took slow, deep breaths.

Outwardly, there wasn't much change. His cultivation level had risen to a point where divine objects didn't affect him significantly.

He did feel, however, that his body's condition, previously unstable due to the Heaven Defying Divine Art, had become somewhat more stable. It was a comfortable feeling, difficult to describe in words.

When Baek Suryong fully absorbed the energy of the White Ice Bangles and opened his eyes again, Artist Pung Wol and Geomno were beside him.

aggAnnoy16

The two looked at Moon Yul with bitter expressions.

"...The fellow. He went like this."

"It's fortunate he looked happy in the end."

The two accepted Moon Yul's death calmly. Geomno lifted Moon Yul's body and carried it somewhere.

Artist Pung Wol, gazing blankly at his retreating back, turned his head and asked Baek Suryong, "Did the two you met in the dream look happy?"

"Yes."

"...That's fortunate."

Moon Yul's funeral was held simply.

Baek Suryong attended the ceremony and stayed as a guest at Artist Pung Wol's manor for two more days.

He needed time to organize the enlightenment regarding the White Ice Divine Art gained during the duel with Geomno, and there was also something he needed to receive from Artist Pung Wol.

"Here, it's finished."

A family portrait depicting Baek Muheun and Mae Yakbing sitting side-by-side in chairs, with Baek Suryong standing behind them.

Seeing the completed painting, Baek Suryong smiled happily.

"My maternal grandfather will be extremely pleased."

He rolled up the scroll, tied it securely with a string, then placed it carefully inside a long wooden box before storing it deep within his travel pack. This should prevent it from getting damaged on the way back.

There was, however, one more unexpected gift from Baek Suryong.

"Here, take this too."

aggAnnoy20

"This is..."

It was the painting depicting Baek Suryong and the Ice Moon Goddess together, performing the New Moon White Ice Dance under the crescent moon.

At the unexpected gift, Baek Suryong asked with wide eyes, "May I really take this? I thought you were quite fond of it..."

Over the past few days, Baek Suryong had seen Artist Pung Wol staring blankly at this painting for hours.

"I thought about it, and it's right for you to take it. Are you not the one and only disciple of the Ice Moon Goddess Eun Yerin?"

Artist Pung Wol let go of his attachment and rolled up the scroll, handing it to Baek Suryong.

He had considered painting another copy, but gave up, lacking the confidence to reproduce the inspiration received at that moment.

"Take it before I change my mind."

Baek Suryong accepted the scroll without further hesitation.

"I will treasure it as a family heirloom for life."

"...If you dare sell it somewhere, I'll immediately hunt you down to the Azure Dragon Academy, so be aware."

"If you come to the Azure Dragon Academy, could you perhaps paint all four of us then, including my maternal grandfather?"

"What? This punk...!"

The two who had been exchanging jokes smirked.

Though their connection was brief, its depth was by no means shallow.

People who remembered the Ice Moon Goddess Eun Yerin.

To Baek Suryong, that fact alone made the two special, and they had shared a deep exchange over the past few days.

Just then, Artist Pung Wol, who had been smiling gently, suddenly hardened his expression seriously and said, "Be careful. The Ice Palace is a dangerous place."

Artist Pung Wol had heard Baek Suryong planned to go to the Ice Palace. He expressed deep concern, however.

In the past, the source that informed the Ice Moon Goddess about the location of the Divine Physician was none other than the Ice Palace.

"The Ice Palace might have colluded with the Blood Cult. I couldn't find concrete evidence, but I still suspect them."

That was the reason Artist Pung Wol had suspected Baek Suryong when he first arrived.

"Yes. I will be careful."

Baek Suryong was now fully aware of such possibilities.

*Then I'll need to investigate further.*

If the Ice Palace joined hands with the Blood Cult, or in the worst case, was absorbed by the Blood Cult... he needed to find a way to prepare for that.

He didn't plan to charge recklessly into the Ice Palace, though. First, he needed to grasp the situation after they became closed-off.

Before that, however.

"First, I plan to visit the Black Tortoise Academy."

"You said you were going to meet your teacher?"

"Yes."

The master of Shaanxi's Black Tortoise Academy, renowned as one of the most outstanding sorcerers under heaven – the Dark Heaven Goddess.

Heaven-defying fate. I need to find out exactly what that is.

Baek Suryong planned to meet her and ask.

Artist Pung Wol, unaware of these detailed circumstances, nodded.

"If it's your teacher, she should be able to provide some answers to what you want to ask."

Though unknown to the world, Artist Pung Wol was one of the disciples who learned sorcery from the Dark Heaven Goddess.

This was from before the Black Tortoise Academy was even established in the murim.

"Well then, I shall be on my way. Elder Geomno. I'll be leaving."

Baek Suryong also bid farewell to Geomno, who had come to see him off.

Geomno nodded with a stoic face.

"I wish you good fortune."

Remembering the energetic young Geomno from the dream, a playful smile touched Baek Suryong's lips.

"Next time, let's have a match with swords."

"...Fine."

Geomno's lips twitched.

Leaving Artist Pung Wol's manor, Baek Suryong departed with steps that seemed considerably lighter compared to when he first arrived.

Artist Pung Wol, gazing blankly at his retreating back, muttered, "Seeing that figure makes me want to paint again."

"That's what we call an occupational disease, sir."

Geomno shook his head slowly.
