He ate as he fed her. Though Wiji Hoyeon's face was still gaunt, she managed to raise a hand, pointing one by one to the dishes she wanted. Each time, Lee Sungmin extended his chopsticks, picked up the food, and brought it carefully to her mouth. It was an unnecessary gesture, of course. As a master capable of freely using Space Manipulation, he could have done this with a mere thought.

However, he didn't use his power, nor did the thought even cross his mind. Time itself seemed to have broken down, stretching into an eternity one moment and vanishing the next. He couldn't tell if it was passing slowly or quickly. A constant pound echoed in his chest, and a tremor ran through his fingertips as he controlled the chopsticks, his eyes occasionally meeting hers.

"What're you staring at?"

Wiji Hoyeon giggled, breaking the silence. Lee Sungmin coughed, feeling an unnecessary wave of embarrassment. *Friend. Admiration. Goal.* The words spun in his mind, all tied to the name Wiji Hoyeon.

"It's just... funny, seeing you being fed like this."

"Is it? I find it comfortable and nice," she replied, sticking out her tongue to lick her lips. "That's enough now. I've eaten too much. I don't want to get fat from stuffing myself like a pig when I can't even move properly."

He recalled how light she had felt in his arms and doubted a little extra weight would make much difference. Feeling moderately full himself, Lee Sungmin put down his chopsticks.

"Alcohol. Want some?"

"What about you?"

"I'd like some."

Wiji Hoyeon's eyes crinkled into a smile, but Lee Sungmin immediately shook his head.

"No. You're already in bad shape."

"What I have is a curse, not an illness. It won't get worse just because I drink."

"Still..."

"Just let me have a drink." As she spoke, she reached out. Her slender, trembling hand grasped the alcohol bottle on the table. "I've ruined the day I've been looking forward to for ten years. I've shown you this... this shameful side of me. ...I'm... depressed too, you know."

Her words struck him like a hammer. He belatedly realized he hadn't considered her feelings at all. The Wiji Hoyeon he remembered was fiercely strong-willed. For that woman to show such weakness, to have her life saved by his hand... the fact must have dealt a severe blow to her pride, even if she didn't show it. He hesitated, opening his mouth to say, *I'm sorry*, but closed it without a word, thinking the apology might only hurt her more.

"You fool."

Wiji Hoyeon giggled as he pulled the bottle over with Space Manipulation. She was already holding a cup, and he slowly poured the alcohol into it.

"This is the first time I'm drinking with you," Wiji Hoyeon said, her voice dazed.

"No... it's the first time I'm drinking alone with someone else. Yes. I've had many of my firsts with you..."

He listened to her mutterings, her weak voice and the faint light in her eyes flaunting just how fragile she had become. Honestly, he couldn't understand it. No matter how powerful the curse, could it truly weaken the Wiji Hoyeon who should have surpassed the Supreme Peak and reached the Transcendent Realm?

[You're ignorant about curses,] the Void Monarch muttered.

[As long as the conditions are met, curses and incantations can exert power greater than magic. A curse this powerful probably had some demanding conditions. The caster who placed it is likely going through hell himself.]

The words offered no comfort. Was there no way to break it? His silent question earned an irritated response.

[You damn bastard. I told you already. To break a curse, you have to kill the caster. Or the caster has to undo it. I'm not a specialist in the unorthodox, so why the hell are you asking me?]

*Because you acted like you knew.*

[I wasn't acting like I knew, I told you because I do know, you son of a bitch.]

Lee Sungmin ignored the curses and shared the drink with Wiji Hoyeon. She looked as if she might fall asleep at any moment, but she steadfastly held onto her consciousness and continued her story. It was the story of the last ten years, of where she went, what she did, and what she saw. She had traveled to many places without a set direction, encountering a Fairy Realm in a deep forest, ancient ruins that were not Dungeons, reclusive masters, and wandering mages. Lee Sungmin listened silently as she spoke of the decade she had experienced.

"They all just passed by," Wiji Hoyeon said, her eyes turning to him.

"It's strange, isn't it? There were quite a few interesting people, but I couldn't form a deep connection. Why? For some reason... I just didn't feel like I 'wanted to'."

It was a peculiar thing. A childhood meeting, a single memory. Ten years was a long time, yet in all her travels, she had connected with no one. For her, the only true connection was with him, the friend she had made a decade ago.

"Was it because I thought you alone were enough?" She laughed, but the sound was cracked and frail. Her voice grew smaller as she spoke, having barely managed to empty the single cup she had wanted. Unable to watch any longer, Lee Sungmin reached out and placed a hand on her shoulder.

"Get some more sleep."

"I'm fine."

"I'm not fine watching you."

"Are you worried about me?" she asked. The question wasn't born of wounded pride, but genuine curiosity. Lee Sungmin slowly nodded. At that, she burst out laughing.

"I can't have you worrying." Wiji Hoyeon muttered, shuffling over to him on her knees. Without asking permission, she laid her head on his thigh. "...Because you'll still be by my side when I wake up."

With those words, she closed her eyes. Soon, her breathing evened out, and she was asleep. Lee Sungmin looked down at her face, a mix of complex emotions swirling within him. He had heard about Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Six Honored Ones from the Crazy Heavenly Demon, but he had never imagined becoming entangled with them like this. After watching her for a moment, he supported her and helped her up.

"I will guide you to a room."

Bo Hye, who had been waiting as they descended from the octagonal pavilion, bowed his head. Lee Sungmin followed him to a separate building behind the main mansion.

"If you need anything, please call a servant."

Lee Sungmin laid Wiji Hoyeon down on a large bed and pulled the blanket over her, his mind lost in thought. It was impossible for her to break the curse when she could barely move, which meant he had no choice but to kill the caster himself. The problem was that the caster was the Fist Honored One, a man Wiji Hoyeon had fought for three days and nights without being able to subdue. The Six Sages of the Outer Heavens were superhumans who had entered the Transcendent Realm, a stage Lee Sungmin had not yet reached.

[That Fist Honored One fellow probably isn't in good shape either,] the Void Monarch said. [Like I said before, maintaining a curse of this level requires the caster to sacrifice a lot too. He might even be just as unable to move as that girl.]

*Even so. I don't know where he is. Besides... messing with him means fighting Heaven Beyond Heaven.*

They were a group of six superhumans from the Transcendent Realm, the highest state one could reach through martial arts. In the entire world, only two martial artists were publicly known to have reached that level. One was the Wudang Sect's leader from two generations ago, and the other was the Chairman of the Unorthodox Alliance. They were human, yet not human. The former sect leader was apparently still alive but had completely withdrawn from worldly affairs, an ancient being who would see the current old masters as mere toddlers. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, despite his position, showed no distinct actions, which was why Lee Sungmin had been so surprised to hear that the Chairman himself had ordered Wiji Hoyeon's protection.

In this vast world, those two were the only ones. The Blood Heavenly Demon ruled the North partly because the major alliances ignored the barren land, but also because a master of his caliber was rare. The two absolute masters at the pinnacle of the orthodox and unorthodox factions did not move.

*It's so far...*

Lee Sungmin stood on the higher end of the Supreme Peak stage. The Iron Armored Divine Spear he had subdued was a renowned master, perhaps half a step weaker than the vampiric Blood Heavenly Demon. Peak, Supreme Peak, Transcendent Realm. People spoke of these stages, but martial arts were not so easily distinguished. Great disparities existed within the same stage, and compatibility was always a factor. One thing, however, was certain. A peak master could not defeat a super peak master. The difference in 'class' was not something one could overcome with sheer effort. Unless he entered the Transcendent Realm, he could not kill the Fist Honored One.

The Six Sages of the Outer Heavens was a group of six masters at a level where only two were known to the world. Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. In his past life, he hadn't even known such a world existed. He thought he had come so far, but looking ahead, he felt as if he had only just realized how high and rugged the mountain before him truly was.

[The view changes when your line of sight gets higher,] the Void Monarch sneered. Lee Sungmin agreed with the words and closed his eyes.

Less than an hour later, the door opened. He looked toward the sound, his thoughts interrupted. The surprised faces of the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia stood in the doorway.

"You're here?" Lee Sungmin asked, slowly getting to his feet.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a low groan upon seeing Wiji Hoyeon lying on the bed. "What in the world happened?"

"We ran into some trouble with the Murim Alliance." Starting with that, Lee Sungmin explained everything he had experienced.

When he finished, the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat out with a dumbfounded expression, "Do the guards in this city scold people for using movement arts but stand by and watch while someone tries to kill another?"

"It's the difference between using movement arts in the center of the city and a fight breaking out on the outskirts near the gate," Rubia replied, pouting. "Ruvus may be neutral, but since it's close to Kron, the Murim Alliance's influence is stronger. If their Iron Armored Divine Spear kills someone on the outskirts, claiming to be eliminating the unorthodox... well, the punishment would probably be nothing more than a slap on the wrist."

"Then isn't this place dangerous too?"

"This is the center of Ruvus, and it's also a branch of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. If they protect us, I don't think there will be much danger..." Rubia's voice trailed off, lacking conviction.

They decided to stay for now and monitor Wiji Hoyeon's condition. Lee Sungmin left the sleeping woman and went out of the room.

"Please deliver this to the Magic Tower Master of the Red Magic Tower," he requested of a servant, handing over a letter he had just written.

The servant nodded and disappeared. Scarlet, the Crimson Archmage, was also knowledgeable about curses. A short while later, however, the servant returned with the news that the Magic Tower Master was absent and the letter could not be delivered. He also asked Nebul through the sorcerer's Guild but received the same answer. The only way to break a curse this strong was to kill the caster.

Wiji Hoyeon slept on, her breathing even. The sun was setting, painting the sky in a beautiful red glow. Lee Sungmin sat by her side, looking out the window. The promise after ten years, the meeting he had anticipated so much, one of the goals that had sustained him. He closed his eyes, then opened them again.

"How do I meet Heaven Beyond Heaven?" he muttered in a flat voice.

At his words, the Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a sigh. "...I believe I told you. The Six Sages of the Outer Heavens are monsters who have reached the Transcendent Realm."

"Elder, you said you were defeated by the Sword Saint of the Six Honored Ones. What would you do if you met that Sword Saint again?"

"I'm not confident I would win, but I wouldn't be defeated as helplessly as I was ten years ago."

"The Six Honored Ones are monsters of the Transcendent Realm. Are you saying you would still fight them?"

"Because I'm not afraid."

"It's the same for me. I'm not afraid." Lee Sungmin smirked and grabbed Wiji Hoyeon's wrist. "I want to meet them because I have to."
