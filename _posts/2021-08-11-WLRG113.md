"They're a brokerage Guild," Rubia said.

*What a strange question,* she muttered to herself.

Lee Sungmin retorted around a mouthful of cooked meat, "Do you think I'm asking because I don't know?"

Rubia pouted and nibbled on her own piece.

"I know Erebrisa is a brokerage Guild," he continued.

"...Well, they are a mysterious and strange group in many ways," she admitted. "It doesn't really make sense, does it? The Mercenary Guild is divided into mercenary corps, and the Mages Guild is split into schools and Magic Towers. Most Guilds adopt that kind of structure, but not the information Guilds."

Lee Sungmin was already aware of this. Dozens, even hundreds of information Guilds, both large and small, operated across the continent. Any giant city hosted at least five or six of them. Besides, it was not just information Guilds that dealt in 'information'. The city's thief Guilds handled it, and among the murim sects, various factions like the Beggars' Gang or the Rogues' Guild also traded in secrets.

"Erebrisa extracts information from 'all' information Guilds, regardless of their different goals and ideologies," Rubia explained. "And that's not all. They also consolidate all the goods held by various merchant associations and individual merchants belonging to the Merchants' Guild to arrange trades. This is something impossible even for high-ranking nobles or kings."

He listened silently. Erebrisa was clearly not a simple brokerage Guild. The ability to consolidate and control groups with such diverse characteristics was beyond the power of any king on the vast continent.

[It's violence,] the Void Monarch stated indifferently from within his mind. [The Brokers of Erebrisa have the ability to ignore distance and space. They take the form of humans, but they are absolutely not human. In the face of such wondrous and overwhelming violence, power and status are meaningless. No matter how outstanding the security is, those bastards can appear by tearing through space and take your head.]

"...Why would beings with such abilities be running a brokerage Guild?" Lee Sungmin asked.

[Four hundred years ago,] the Void Monarch began, [this Elder was quite notorious. That is the way of a Yokai. Our nature brings infamy. Not all humans feel guilt about eating pigs, but most feel none at all. It is the same for us. By nature, Yokai are ferocious and selfish bastards who cause a great deal of trouble. This Elder was no different.]

That was why he was subjugated. Lee Sungmin recalled what Nebul had told him. Four hundred years ago, the Void Monarch who reigned over the South was a revered great yokai, but he was not put down simply for being strong. He was subjugated for committing evil deeds that warranted it.

[I can guarantee you, back then in Eria, you could count the number of beings stronger than me on one hand. Even a Dragon couldn't do anything to this Elder!]

*That seems like a bit of an exaggeration,* Lee Sungmin thought, careful not to let it show on his face.

[However, the Erebrisa Broker who came to find this Elder... he was a powerhouse so strong that even I couldn't guarantee an easy victory. It was absurd. A guy like that suddenly showed up, claimed to be from a brokerage Guild, and wanted to make me a member. So I asked him. Why, with all that power, were they running a mere brokerage Guild, and why did they want me?]

"...And?"

[He said something interesting. He called it a 'variable'.]

"What's that?"

[A being whose very existence can become a variable for the entire world. He said that to become a member of Erebrisa, one must basically possess that quality. He offered me membership because this Elder's strength met that requirement. In other words, those Erebrisa bastards are taking beings who can become variables in the world as members and supporting them. I don't know what they're plotting, though.]

Lee Sungmin remembered his first encounter with Erebrisa on Mount Mushii. The Broker had told him that he lacked the 'qualifications'. At the time, he had assumed it was simply because his skills were lacking, but it seemed there was more to it than that.

[They're bizarre. That's why I refused to become their member. I didn't want to get involved with bastards I couldn't figure out.]

"They are convenient, though," Lee Sungmin pointed out.

[That girl, the Little Heavenly Demon, is probably a member of Erebrisa too,] the Void Monarch added. [Considering her strength and potential for growth, she's more than qualified to be a variable, but... this is a really funny thing to say. Why a variable? It's not like the situation is fixed, so why use that word? A variable to what?]

The Void Monarch chuckled. Lee Sungmin shared the same doubts, but there was no way to confirm the truth. Even if he asked Nebul, it was unlikely the Broker would answer.

"You're having an interesting conversation."

The voice cut through the air, sudden and unexpected. Startled, Lee Sungmin's head snapped toward the sound. Nebul was standing under the shade of a tree, a gentle smile on his face.

"...I didn't call for you."

"Celgerus's work is finished, so I came to inform you."

"That's good timing." Lee Sungmin sucked the grease from his fingers and met Nebul's gaze. "What is Erebrisa?"

"It is a brokerage Guild," Nebul replied. "...It is difficult to tell you more than that. Please understand."

He said it as if he could not help it, but the nuance was strong. He would 'absolutely' not tell him. This was not an opponent from whom he could force an answer.

"You're difficult."

"However, I can swear that we will not bring harm to our members. Erebrisa is for its members."

As Nebul spoke, he sliced through the space with his hand. Celgerus stood on the other side, looking even more haggard than before. Dark circles from accumulated fatigue ringed his eyes, and his cheeks were slightly sunken.

"What's with the atmosphere?" Celgerus asked, tilting his head.

Lee Sungmin did not answer, and Nebul merely shrugged. After a moment of silence, Celgerus parted his lips and grumbled, "Well, it's not my business. The things you ordered are finished. The shape follows the spear I made last time."

Nebul moved to Celgerus's side and received the spear and Magic Armor. Until just a moment ago, Lee Sungmin's mind had been troubled with questions about Erebrisa, but the moment he saw the spear Nebul brought back, all that turmoil vanished from his heart. The weapon Celgerus had made was magnificent, a masterpiece that captured his eyes and his very soul.

"Orichalcum," Celgerus began. "It never breaks. That's what the idiots chatter about, but it's wrong. There's nothing in this world that can never be broken. That's not to say Orichalcum is soft. In terms of durability, few metals can match it. It has a hardness greater than a Dragon's scales or bones."

Lee Sungmin took the spear from Nebul. It was longer than the one he had used before, a necessary change since his body had grown since he first received that spear.

*It's light.*

Still, it did not feel soft. He gripped the shear shaft and applied light pressure. It did not bend in the slightest. He liked its sturdiness.

"If it's completely destroyed, there's nothing to be done, but any other damage can be repaired by infusing magic power, or in your case, inner arts," Celgerus continued. "There's no need to sharpen the blade or maintain it. Orichalcum is something that won't change even if you bury it in the ground for a thousand years."

"There aren't many artisans who can handle Orichalcum," Nebul added. "Even among Dwarf blacksmiths, you can count them on one hand. Among non-Dwarfs, only Celgerus, who holds the title of Meister, can handle it."

Despite the blatant praise, Celgerus showed no pride.

"What's the point? They don't even treat me properly because I'm a Dark Elf." Instead, he grumbled before adding, "Orichalcum also accepts magic power or inner arts incomparably better than Mithril. You'll get a feel for it once you use it yourself. The armor too."

Celgerus reached out and pointed to the Magic Armor.

"There was quite a bit of Orichalcum left over after making the spear, so I inserted it centered around the core where the armor's variable magic is engraved. It's just an overlay, but the anti-magic should function against most spells. Don't trust it too much, though. It's not an anti-magic enchantment."

"Can I ask for an enchantment?"

"There's a reason I didn't do it," Celgerus said. "As outstanding as Orichalcum is, the difficulty of enchanting it is also high. You couldn't even dream of it unless you're at the archmage level. Besides, enchanting degrades the quality of the metal. With a material like Orichalcum, adding an enchantment is actually a loss."

Being a layman on the subject, Lee Sungmin listened silently. Celgerus let out a deep breath and pressed his temples.

"...I guarantee you, that's the greatest masterpiece of my blacksmithing life. The material was also the best I've ever handled. As long as you don't try to fight a Dragon, it won't break."

"...Thank you."

"Thanks for what. I didn't give it a name, so if you want to name it, you can do it yourself. I'm tired, so I'm going to get some sleep."

With those words, Celgerus turned away. Nebul closed the open space and turned back to Lee Sungmin with a smile.

"Are you pleased with it?"

"......"

"Please do not doubt us. Erebrisa seeks only the convenience of its members, and..." Nebul paused for a moment. "...I am Member Lee Sungmin's designated Broker. I am exclusively assigned to you. Although I belong to Erebrisa, depending on the situation, I may prioritize you over Erebrisa's policies."

He then added, "Though not for questions like the one from earlier," before bowing his head deeply. "I will be waiting for you to call on me next time."

Leaving those words behind, Nebul disappeared. Not long after he vanished, the Void Monarch grumbled.

[Is that guy gay?]

"What the fuck are you talking about..." The words were so absurd that Lee Sungmin could not help but curse.

Hearing this, the Void Monarch roared with laughter. [Tastes can be diverse. Your face isn't ugly, so it would be good to keep that possibility in mind.]

"Stop talking nonsense."

[In any case, it's a good thing to have a helpful ally by your side, even if he's an ambiguous bastard.]

Listening to the Void Monarch's muttering, Lee Sungmin tried on the Magic Armor. The armor, which covered his upper body, felt much better to wear than before. When he infused it with inner arts, it expanded greatly and enveloped his entire body. He moved his armor-clad body and marveled inwardly.

*It's comfortable.*

His previous Magic Armor had not been uncomfortable, but compared to his bare body, there had been an inevitable resistance. That was gone now. There was almost no resistance when he moved. It was not weightless, but considering his physical strength, it might as well have been.

He then tried raising his Protective Qi Aura.

"......"

The power surged instantly, covering his entire body. It looked as if a violet flame had swallowed him whole. Startled, Lee Sungmin reduced the output of his inner arts.

"Your inner arts were amplified by passing through the Orichalcum," Rubia quickly explained. "That's also why Orichalcum is considered the best material. You can draw out greater power with less inner arts or magic power."

It was certainly true. When he wore his previous armor and raised his Protective Qi Aura, the output had not been this strong. Taking the opportunity, Lee Sungmin gripped the new spear. As he held the shear shaft tightly and infused it with inner arts, a violet enhanced qi immediately covered the entire weapon. Unlike the armor, which was only coated with Orichalcum, the spear was made of solid Orichalcum. Perhaps because of that, the efficiency of drawing out enhanced qi was far superior to that of his Protective Qi Aura.

[If you can't do it with your body, you should get help from your equipment.]

*Are you being sarcastic?*

[You're a twisted bastard. Are you ashamed of using equipment?]

*That's not it, but...*

[Don't lie,] the Void Monarch shot back with a chuckle. [Using what you have properly is also important. Your heart, your armor, your weapon. All of it is ultimately yours. Don't let it gather dust while you cling to some pathetic pride. Use it well. Very well.]

Lee Sungmin listened to the yokai's advice and nodded silently. He still did not like the Black Heart embedded in his body, but he could not completely hate it either. After all, it was a fact that this heart had saved his life and helped him.

*That's why I have to meet Preskan. I don't know anything about how to use it.*
