Aine had learned a great deal in the five years since her birth. By any normal measure, five years was not a long time to exist, yet hers had been incomparably denser than those of an ordinary being. This was due to her unique ability to prey and learn. By chewing and consuming the hearts of others, she could effortlessly absorb the skills of their former owners. It was for this very reason that Preskan had brought her to the North, believing its lawless expanse to be the optimal training ground for her to learn with ease.

*I never thought it would turn out like this.*

He had never imagined the Vampire Queen, Jeniela, would be residing in Trabia, this giant city in the vast North of Eria. Had he known, would the South have been a better choice? He immediately dismissed the idea. No, the situation there would have been no better. While the North of Eria was the land of the Inhuman Demonic Realm, the South was the domain of the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons, a place teeming with savage natives and a host of Yokai and Ghost Spirits. The North had its own inhuman species, of course, but creatures like Vampires, Lycanthropes, and Liches were a far cry from the Yokai and Ghost Spirits of the South. Those southern beings knew no self-restraint, their actions unhesitating and their nature steeped in violence.

"Aine."

Preskan stood before Aine's door and called her name, his voice filled with a father's love. He truly considered her his own daughter, and in a way, it was not wrong. Though he had not given birth to her, Preskan had crafted her heart and body with his own hands.

"......"

The door swung open without a sound. A single bed was the only furniture in the otherwise empty room, and Aine sat on its edge, looking up at him. A long tentacle, which she had extended to open the door, retracted smoothly back into her body.

Five years had passed, yet she had not grown at all, retaining the appearance of a small girl. People were rarely wary of a young child. It might depend on the situation, but ordinary people simply did not guard themselves against little boys and girls. Much like the stripes on a tiger or a leopard, Aine's youthful form was a predator's camouflage, designed for more efficient hunting.

"It seems that Heart Thief has come to this city."

At his words, Aine's eyes flashed open.

*

The conversation with Jeniela had been surprisingly bland. She had asked nothing of significance, and the dialogue over their meal carried so little weight that he felt no need to re-examine it.

"...It was delicious, wasn't it?" Rubia asked from beside him, her face sour.

Lee Sungmin felt the same and nodded. Jeniela had been right to state her confidence, as the meal at her mansion was excellent. Even the accompanying wine, whose color was so red he had initially suspected it was mixed with blood, had turned out to be just wine.

*Let's meet again next time.*

Jeniela's parting words weighed on his mind. He wanted to dismiss them as a mere formality, but coming from the Vampire Queen, he could not simply let them go. The power she possessed was enough to compel any of his actions. He had refused her offer to stay at the mansion, but if she had truly wanted it, she could have easily forced him.

*But she let me go. Why...?*

[She's taken a liking to you,] Void Monarch said with a chuckle. [That wench is crazy. She could have you, but she won't take you. What she wants isn't to seize her desired toy with her own power, but for the toy to come crawling into her hands, begging.]

*I don't know what you mean.*

[She offered to make you a vampire, didn't she? And you refused. That's where it started. That unpleasant hobby of hers.]

A cold realization dawned on him. *...She's waiting for me to... come to her on my own and ask to be made into a vampire.*

He had absolutely no intention of doing so. He would rather become a Yokai.

Having come this far, Lee Sungmin decided to find lodging in the Central District. The thought of trekking all the way back to the outskirts was a hassle he felt no need to endure.

*Stares.*

He felt many watchful eyes on him. The gazes were scattered, likely an attempt at camouflage, but to Lee Sungmin, they felt anything but subtle.

*Beggars... I don't see any.*

In a city this large, especially in its most developed central area, the complete absence of beggars was strange. He could guess the reason. A crazy city that would beat members of the Murim Alliance to death was no place for the Beggars' Gang to roam freely.

"Oh."

A nearby voice stopped Lee Sungmin in his tracks.

"We keep meeting in the most unexpected places, don't we?"

Lee Sungmin could not help but agree. Kim Jonghyun pushed back the hood of his robe and offered a smile. This was their first reunion since Lee Sungmin had killed the Sword Phantom in Dreamoor.

"Back then, you just abandoned me and disappeared like you were possessed. It would've been nice if you'd at least said a quick word before leaving."

"...Something came up suddenly. I couldn't help it."

"Then I suppose I'll have to understand," Kim Jonghyun replied with a calm face.

To think he would meet Kim Jonghyun again here in Trabia. Lee Sungmin scanned the man's face. A little over two years had passed, but Kim Jonghyun hadn't changed. Come to think of it, he looked exactly the same as when Lee Sungmin had first seen him five years ago.

"Why are you here?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Since the incident two years ago, Trabia has become a gathering place for monsters with various circumstances and ill-natured people with their own stories. A place like this is a good area for a Black Mage to operate."

"...Is that why you're here? Didn't you say you became the Black Magic Tower Master?"

"Just because I'm a Magic Tower Master doesn't mean I have to be cooped up in the Magic Tower," Kim Jonghyun said with a shrug. "All I have to do is consistently produce research results. I felt drawn to the city of Trabia, so I took a long vacation and came here. I never thought I'd meet you, though..."

"...Who is that person?" Rubia asked in a small voice, sticking close to Lee Sungmin.

Kim Jonghyun's eyes widened as he looked at her. "A beastfolk. Is she your slave?"

"She is not."

"Ah... my apologies. I misjudged. Not a beastfolk... hmm..." His eyes shifted, taking on the intensity of a researcher. He scanned Rubia so blatantly that she hid behind Lee Sungmin's back in embarrassment. "...An artificial life. This is... fascinating. Many mages make it their life's ambition to create an artificial life, but only a very small number, those close to the pinnacle, have actually succeeded. Where on earth did you get something like that?"

"...You say some pretty rude things for a first meeting," Rubia spat out, her shoulders trembling slightly as she glared at Kim Jonghyun. "I'd appreciate it if you didn't treat me like an object."

"Ah... I'm sorry," Kim Jonghyun said, bowing his head deeply. "My manner of speaking isn't the best. This seems like fate as well. What do you say? How about a light meal?"

"Thank you for the offer, but... I've already eaten."

"Do you have a place to stay?"

"No. I just arrived in Trabia today, so I haven't found lodging yet."

"In that case, would you like to come to my house?" Kim Jonghyun offered with a smile.

Lee Sungmin hesitated for a moment before nodding. He had met Kim Jonghyun several times, though he certainly did not think he had figured the man out from those few encounters. Still, a thought occurred to him. Perhaps Kim Jonghyun was the nobleman he was meant to meet in the North.

He did not have to travel far. The place Kim Jonghyun led him to was a two-story house, which, while it could not compare to Jeniela's mansion, was far better than the shabby homes on the outskirts.

"It's a pretty nice house, isn't it?" Kim Jonghyun said as he took off his robe. The garment moved on its own, hanging itself on a hook on the wall. "It was available for a decent price, so I bought it. I don't know how long I'll be staying in this city, but I thought it would be more convenient in many ways to get a house rather than use an inn."

"Is that so?"

"What have you been up to all this time?" Kim Jonghyun asked, gesturing for him to sit. As he did, his eyes flickered to the Magic Armor Lee Sungmin wore before rising to meet his gaze. "...A lot has changed about you since I saw you two years ago. Your strength, for one, and especially that Magic Armor... I don't know what it is, but I can feel a powerful presence from it that's hard to comprehend."

[He's a sharp one,] Void Monarch muttered.

Lee Sungmin ignored the voice and answered, "I had a fortunate opportunity."

"There's no such thing as coincidence in this world," Kim Jonghyun said with a smile. "There is, however, fate. Perhaps our reunion here is also fate."

Fate. Lee Sungmin still did not truly believe in something so incredibly ambiguous, or what it even was.

"You said you came to Trabia for sightseeing, correct?" Kim Jonghyun continued. "...... I'm not sure what there is to see in this city, though."

"Isn't it interesting?" Kim Jonghyun said, beckoning with his finger. A clattering sound came from the kitchen as a teapot and cups flew over to them. "Inhumans are gathering in Trabia."

He began to speak. "Most of the inhumans gathering here are Predator... Ah. Do you know what Predator is?"

"I do."

"Hmm. I see. In any case... most of the inhumans gathered in Trabia belong to Predator. Not all of them are monsters with fearsome power, but among them are a few who are powerful enough to be counted among the strongest in this entire world."

He was referring to the Vampire Queen, who had lived for over six hundred years, and the Werewolf, once called the Mad Wolf and considered the strongest among all Lycanthropes. Those two led the many inhumans belonging to Predator.

"The Vampire Queen has taken control of Trabia, and the Werewolf has taken control of its outskirts. In fact, it's been that way for a long time. They were here even when the Blood Heavenly Demon and his Alliance were still around. They just didn't stand out."

"It's the same now," Kim Jonghyun added. "The Vampire Queen rarely engages in public activities, though her vampire subjects monitor the city. The Werewolf is the same. His faction, the Ghost Wolf Sect, is known as a small sect with little influence, and they actually behave that way. Isn't it strange? The Mad Wolf, the master of the Ghost Wolf Sect, has the power to single-handedly annihilate one of the Nine Sects One Gang if he so chooses. The same goes for the Vampire Queen. So why did they let something like the Blood Heavenly Alliance reign over Trabia?"

"...Because they didn't want to attract attention?"

"You could think of it that way. The truth is... it was meaningless. Whether the Blood Heavenly Alliance reigned or not. They had the power to wipe out the Blood Heavenly Demon and his alliance whenever they wanted. They simply didn't do it... because there was no need. With the Alliance falling to Wiji Hoyeon, Trabia has now taken on the ideal form they desired."

It had become a city of the Inhuman Demonic Realm. With the fall of the reigning Blood Heavenly Demon, numerous demonic practitioners had risen up, all seeking to become the new masters of Trabia. As law and order collapsed and control vanished, countless inhumans had flocked to the city.

"I don't know what Predator intends to do here, but they are clearly trying to do something. That's why I'm interested. The monsters at the pinnacle of the inhuman species, who haven't made a move for hundreds of years, are about to do something."

As he spoke, Kim Jonghyun's face was colored with ecstasy.

"Perhaps a piece of history will be made here. I want to see it."
