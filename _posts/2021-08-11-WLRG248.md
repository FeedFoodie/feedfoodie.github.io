Warriors of the Moyong Clan and the Murim Alliance's Pure White Martial Squad drew their weapons, their killing intent radiating in palpable waves, yet the surviving Magic Tower Masters and mages remained unintimidated. Great losses had been suffered, but these were battle mages seasoned in the field, and the Magic Tower Masters themselves stood near the pinnacle of their respective arts. Besides, the recent subjugation had greatly diminished the strength of both sides.

Just as the head of the Moyong Clan began to swing his sword, Lloyd's hands blurred into a beastfolk. The magic it manifested was weaker than a full incantation but far faster.

CRACKLE!

A golden electric current erupted, striking Moyong Daeun. He swung his sword to meet it, but the arc of lightning Lloyd had fired simply swallowed the blade and surged onward, seeking to pierce his body.

RUMBLE!

A Protective Qi Aura billowed up like a cloud to collide with the current. While not a fatal blow, the attack was potent enough to shock his muscles into seizing.

The Green Tower Master's arm stretched out. In response, Moyong Daeun mercilessly unleashed his sword qi at the slow, decrepit limb.

"......"

The outstretched arm was sliced into dozens of pieces. The Green Tower Master's lips twitched as he watched the spray of blood, flesh, and bone. The scattered pieces of flesh writhed in midair, and from each one, dozens of green vines erupted. Moyong Daeun let out a surprised cry as the supple vines rushed toward him.

A bone grew long from the stump of his severed arm, and in an instant, the limb had completely regenerated. The Green Tower Master glared at Moyong Daeun, who was busy swinging his sword to cut down the vines, and formed a beastfolk with both hands.

GRRROWL!

The vines attacking Moyong Daeun swelled, and carnivorous plants with sharp teeth bloomed at their ends.

In the meantime, the White Tower Master had begun to prepare his own magic. After a long incantation and a complex beastfolk, a pure white light emanated from his body. It was a healing spell that mended the wounds of the mages and Holy Knights gathered around them, restoring vitality to their tired bodies and minds. In addition, he complexly cast a variety of other buff magics.

Scarlet was still flustered. She had never imagined the entire Mages Guild would step up to protect her.

[You sure this is okay?]

[What do you mean?]

Enveloped in a golden radiance, Lloyd slowly rose. He raised his index finger as he replied to Scarlet's telepathy. A brilliant light coalesced at his fingertip before shooting out as a straight beam of sympathy in the direction he pointed.

BOOM!

One of the martial artists struck by the light exploded and died without a scream.

[Are you really planning to completely break away from the Murim Alliance?]

[Then should I have just handed you over to them?] Lloyd asked back.

Scarlet made a disgusted expression at his words, though her focus was not solely on their conversation. She pulled on her Spell Engraving gloves and prepared for battle.

[The Moyong Patriarch has lost his mind. Even if he's an outstanding master, our forces are overwhelmingly superior. I can't understand why he started this fight.]

It was only natural for Lloyd to have his doubts. Even if Moyong Daeun was a master whose skills ranked among the best of the Supreme Peak, the Mages Guild's forces were far superior. The man could barely handle one Magic Tower Master alone, so facing three was an impossible task.

*On what grounds did he even start this fight?*

A thirst for revenge for his daughter? That would be enough to paralyze one's reason, but had he truly started a fight with so many mages for that reason alone? Besides, the Moyong Patriarch himself had said this matter had nothing to do with the Murim Alliance.

There were no grounds.

The truth was that Moyong Daeun was desperate. His deceased daughter, Moyong Seojin, had been the apple of his eye. Such a precious daughter had died, and because she had fallen in the damned Demon Forest, he could not even retrieve her body. The Murim Alliance Black Dog Group Leader, who had witnessed everything, had reported with a grim face just how cruelly the Phantom Spear Lee Sungmin had murdered his daughter. From that day forward, a maddened demon had taken root in Moyong Daeun's heart, a demon that thought only of revenge and had paralyzed all reason.

Even as the warriors of his clan and the Pure White Martial Squad died around him, he swung his sword without stopping. He was indeed worthy of a master of the Supreme Peak. The Green Tower Master's carnivorous plants were hewn apart by his blade, and he dodged the golden flashes fired by Lloyd with dazzling movement arts, blocking what he could not avoid with his Protective Qi Aura.

Moyong Daeun's bloodshot eyes fixed on Scarlet.

"Kuaaaah!"

He let out a rough roar and charged. Scarlet frowned and stretched a hand toward him.

"......"

Her fingertips danced, creating a character in the air.

FWOOSH!

Crimson flames erupted from the character and engulfed Moyong Daeun.

"That's enough!" Scarlet shouted with an annoyed expression.

The flames she created could not turn his body to ash, but they were enough to block his approach. Blinded by revenge, Moyong Daeun's floundering movements within the inferno were both irritating and pathetic. Scarlet adjusted the firepower appropriately and increased the distance between them. As the flames weakened, Moyong Daeun shouted and bisected the blaze with a single strike. Just as Scarlet prepared another spell to tie down the relentlessly attacking man, a sudden chill seized the battlefield.

Shiver.

A cold dread traced its way down Lloyd's spine, and he whipped his head around, his eyes widening in shock.

"What...!?"

Golden eyes flashed in the darkness. By the time anyone recognized 'his' presence, it was too late. All who met his gaze froze on the spot. Moyong Daeun, who had been charging Scarlet, was no exception. Even he, one of the strongest masters at the Supreme Peak level, could not help but freeze like a frog before a snake.

No, to call him a snake would be to overestimate everyone present, including Moyong Daeun. The being who froze them all with his mere presence was no mere predator, and they were all far more insignificant than frogs before him.

"I came because I saw a Dragon."

The man's build was not particularly large, though he stood nearly two meters tall, reminiscent of a withered old tree. He wore clothes of shaggy fur and flicked his blood-soaked hands. Overwhelmed by the immense pressure, everyone trembled like aspen leaves as they looked at him.

It was the Ghost Wolf Sect Master, Ju Won, a Lycanthrope once called the Mad Wolf.

This was a fact that all the subjugation teams had overlooked. This place, the North, this land of severe cold, was not the Vampire Queen's territory alone. Outside Trabia, the northernmost city where she had settled, was the territory where Ju Won roamed.

He walked slowly. He was not the type to go out of his way to cause trouble. There was a time when he had rampaged madly, befitting his name, but that past was now just a distant memory. Ju Won slowly scanned the frozen humans. None of them piqued his interest. A fairly skilled mage, a fairly skilled martial artist. That was all.

Ju Won began to walk. The Mad Wolf had enjoyed needless slaughter, but Ju Won did not. He would not go out of his way to crush insects not even worth killing.

"Ugh... Uuuugh...!"

Moyong Daeun's face turned deathly pale as he watched Ju Won approach. He did not know who the man was, but he was terrified by a colossal fear he had never felt before in his life.

Those who face such fear exhibit various behaviors. For Moyong Daeun and a few other martial artists, it manifested as a desperate, screaming charge.

"Uwaaah!"

Moyong Daeun let out a scream-like roar and shot his sword forward. The Flying Sword Technique, wreathed in blue enhanced sword qi, flew toward Ju Won.

Ju Won watched the attack approach and simply flicked a fingertip. The sword instantly shattered into a thousand pieces in midair. The fragments flew in all directions, tearing the bodies of the other warriors who had charged in fear into dozens of pieces.

"Hmph."

Ju Won's eyebrows, full of boredom, furrowed. *He doesn't go out of his way to kill insects, but if an insect flies right at him, isn't that a different story?*

He extended his hand toward Moyong Daeun.

THWUMP!

As Ju Won flicked his fingertip again, a tremendous force struck Moyong Daeun.

CRUNCH.

A large hole was punched clean through his chest.

"Gak...!"

Blood poured from his wide-open mouth. Moyong Daeun looked down at his own body with an expression of disbelief. His body trembled as he stared at the gaping hole in his chest, and soon, he collapsed limply to the ground.

Such was his death. Baek Geomhak Moyong Daeun, head of the Moyong Clan, had died so easily, unable to avenge his daughter and never knowing the truth of her end. It was a death full of regrets.

Ju Won, who had delivered such a death, still wore a face full of boredom. From his perspective, it was nothing more than swatting a bothersome gnat.

"Ah... Aah..."

Moyong Chan watched his father's death with a look of disbelief, feeling as if he might go mad with fear. He wanted to charge at his father's killer, who stood right in front of him, but his body was frozen and would not move.

"Pa-Patriarch!"

Though Moyong Chan was terrified, the warriors of the Moyong Clan were not. They screamed shrilly and rushed toward Ju Won. The number of gnats had increased. Ju Won waved his hand in annoyance and slaughtered the charging warriors.

"Hm."

Ju Won's eyes glinted. He turned his head toward a powerful presence that was rapidly approaching.

CRACKLE!

With the sound of crackling electricity, Lee Sungmin arrived on the scene. He had left the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight to their battle, running with all his might to reach Scarlet. He wasn't too late. Scarlet, the one he had wanted to save, was unharmed. Lee Sungmin saw Moyong Daeun, dead with a hole in his chest, and the horribly slain warriors of the Moyong Clan.

And he saw Ju Won.

"...Why are you here?"

"I saw a Dragon," Ju Won answered. "Jeniela told me not to interfere with Kim Jonghyun, but it wasn't Kim Jonghyun who interested me. It was the Dragon. That's why I came into this forest."

The Black Dragon Knight. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. Because that man had suddenly barged into this forest, Ju Won had been drawn here, and because of that, Moyong Daeun had died. He did not feel much emotion about it, only a slight sense of pity that they had been unable to clear up the misunderstanding in the end.

"How strange," Ju Won muttered, looking Lee Sungmin up and down with a frown. "You also have the feel of a Dragon."

This reunion was not a welcome one. A few years ago, when Lee Sungmin had first come to the North and encountered Ju Won, he had been overwhelmed by his monstrous presence. It was not much different now. In fact, having reached his current level, he could feel even more accurately just what a powerful monster Ju Won was.

[Why are you interested in Dragons?]

The Void Monarch stirred his Demonic Energy. Just as he had made Ju Won retreat in the past, he created his form and spoke. At those words, Ju Won raised his head and looked at the spectral figure.

"To fight a Dragon. That was Howon's lifelong wish."

Lee Sungmin had heard the name before. The pinnacle of the Lycanthropes in Predator was named Howon.

[What does Howon's wish have to do with you?]

"Howon is dead," Ju Won said. "I killed him. It was not long after you met your end, Void Monarch. Howon wanted to fight a Dragon when his power reached its peak, but his wish was not fulfilled. The Dragons vanished from the world, and I killed him."

[...Hmph.]

The Void Monarch let out a small groan at Ju Won's indifferent words.

"My lifelong wish was to defeat Howon," Ju Won muttered, clenching and unclenching his hand. "I achieved that, but Howon's wish was never fulfilled. His unfulfilled wish... I, who killed him, must fulfill it."

[This isn't good,] the Void Monarch whispered inside Lee Sungmin's head.

"...Wait."

Lee Sungmin also felt things taking a serious turn. If he fought Ju Won now, he would die. This was an undeniable fact. He had to avoid a fight.

"...I'll let you meet a Dragon."

Lee Sungmin raised his hand, pointing back the way he had come.

"There is a Dragon over there."

He felt a pang of pity for the Black Dragon Knight, but he had no other choice. Lee Sungmin was selling the man out to Ju Won.
