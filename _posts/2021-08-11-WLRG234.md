In his past life, Scarlet had never died. She had risen to fame as the Crimson Archmage and lived right up until the moment of his own death. Now, however, there was no telling what might happen. Several months had already passed since the point in time he had died before, and the future was a complete unknown.

*And it's not like things will unconditionally follow my past life.*

He had confirmed that truth time and again. The Sect Leader of Mount Hua, who should have lived, was dead. The Sword Phantom had fallen by his own hand. Baek Sogo, fated to die by Wiji Hoyeon's blade, still drew breath. Even the Blood Heavenly Demon had perished at his hands. This life had already diverged so much from his last, all because of him.

Perhaps what Kim Jonghyun was doing now was also ultimately related to him. It might have been a hasty thought, but Lee Sungmin couldn't completely dismiss the possibility. He had already met Kim Jonghyun several times, and in the end, their paths had crossed once more.

"What are you thinking about?" Scarlet asked, tilting her head as she entered the dining hall.

Lee Sungmin shook his head, pushing his complicated thoughts aside for a moment.

[Kim Jonghyun,] he replied via Thought Transmission, knowing the topic was sensitive.

Scarlet's brow furrowed instantly.

CRUNCH.

The knife in her hand crumpled under her grip.

[That complete bastard. I have no idea what he was thinking, pulling a stunt like this.]

[Have you figured out what he's after?]

[Not yet... Nothing's certain. It's not like I've met him in person to ask, nor has he been blabbing about it. Still, I have some speculations. The villagers he killed had their hearts torn out and their bodies strung together, but other than that, there's no commonality.]

She set the mangled utensil down and clicked her tongue.

[He wouldn't have done something like that without a reason. Since ancient times, the heart has been the most blatant and convenient sacrifice in magic and sorcery.]

[A sacrifice?]

[Yeah. For a magical or ritualistic ceremony. The other Black Mages at the tower mentioned the possibility of a demon summoning.]

[A demon? You mean the Demon Race?]

[The Demon Race is... different. I'm no expert, but they're a species from the Demon Realm. Mutants, if you had to classify them. Demons aren't mutants in the same way. Well, this is all just speculation for now, but the number of humans Kim Jonghyun has killed and taken the hearts from exceeds five thousand. If he performs a ritual with that many sacrifices, something equally horrific is bound to happen.]

With a complicated expression, Scarlet rubbed her head vigorously. For now, all she could do was speculate. She had consulted the other Black Mages, but even they were unsure of Kim Jonghyun's exact goal. It was understandable. Magic that used humans as sacrifices invariably produced horrific results, and if this was a spell performed not through established Dark Magic but through the Grimoire, its very form would be impossible to grasp.

[Before Arbes fell, the Mages Guild had two twin magic tomes, the Grimoire and the Gries, but even while they possessed them, they never deciphered them.]

[Why not?]

[Because they were too dangerous. The magic written in those tomes, even without being fully deciphered, was considered far too advanced for humanity. The idea was that they were so dangerous, they shouldn't be touched at all. Of course, after Arbes disappeared with the Grimoire, they belatedly began the work of deciphering the Gries.]

Lee Sungmin didn't know much about those great magic books, but he could sense from Scarlet's attitude just how dangerous the Grimoire in Kim Jonghyun's possession truly was.

[Do you think the subjugation will succeed, Scarlet?]

[It has to... succeed. Honestly, I don't know. I think the forces are sufficient. The Murim Alliance, the Church, the Mage Army, and skilled mercenaries have all agreed to join. I'm half-joking, but with this many troops, we could probably even catch a Dragon.]

The Void Monarch cackled inside Lee Sungmin's mind at her words.

[Catch a Dragon? What nonsense. A gathering of this size couldn't even tear a single wing from one.]

[Are Dragons that strong?]

[It's not for nothing that they boast of being a great race. Though to this Elder, they looked like nothing more than ridiculous lizards.]

[In the end, you were subjugated by humans too.]

[That's true, but... hmm. Now that I think about it, it's quite strange. Why *was* I subjugated?]

Lee Sungmin's jab had been meant to tease, but the Void Monarch took it seriously. He had never truly, deeply considered the moment of his own death, as if some part of him had been unconsciously avoiding the memory.

[Strange. I can't remember it well... Is it because it was the moment I died?]

The thought was genuinely puzzling to the Void Monarch himself. In that world, no being should have been capable of killing him. Even Dragons had seemed pathetically weak, and the strongest of humans or inhumans were not powerful enough to end his life. *So why did I die?* He recalled Osla's words, that it was a death unsettling in many ways.

[If this many troops have gathered, it's only polite to let them subjugate him. Don't you think?]

[What about the thought that it might be dangerous?]

[I am thinking about it. Kim Jonghyun... that bastard is a psycho who could do anything. To be honest, I don't want to get involved either.]

[Then why not just run away?] Lee Sungmin looked at her, genuinely curious. If she knew it was dangerous, she could simply leave. Could it be her honor as a Magic Tower Master?

The suggestion made Scarlet recoil, and she shook her head violently.

[Honor? Don't be ridiculous! I hate cringey stuff like that. I have no intention of dying for some stupid reason!]

[Then why don't you leave with me?]

[I'd love to, but I can't. I once swore an oath to grant a request for that old man, the Green Tower Master who's here... and that old geezer told me to participate in the subjugation.]

[Why did you make an oath like that?]

[At the time, I needed magical assistance, and only the Green Tower Master could help me. In exchange, I promised to grant a request of his someday. Dammit... why did I have to make an oath like that?] Scarlet grumbled, scratching her head vigorously. Of course, back then, she had desperately needed his help, but thinking about it now filled her with nothing but regret.

After a light meal, they left the dining hall. She and the other Magic Tower Masters were staying in a large inn they had rented out entirely.

"You said you were just getting some fresh air, but you're quite late."

An old man in a spacious robe sat on the first floor of the inn, smoking a pipe. His face, a canvas of wrinkles, creased into a grin as he watched Scarlet enter.

"I was just late meeting an old friend."

[That's the Green Tower Master,] Scarlet sent a quick Thought Transmission to Lee Sungmin while replying.

Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply toward the old man. "It's an honor to meet you."

"An honor? What's so honorable about seeing a dying old man?"

"Why don't you stop saying things you don't mean? Especially when you're researching immortality magic because you don't want to die," Scarlet sneered.

The Green Tower Master only let out a hearty laugh in response. "Isn't eternal youth and immortality the dream of all humans? I am merely dedicating my entire life for the sake of foolish mortals, researching the cherished desire for immortality that all humans yearn for. It is by no means for my own personal greed."

"What a load of crap," Scarlet shot back, grabbing Lee Sungmin's arm. "There's a spare room, right? My old friend here has graciously decided to lend his strength to the subjugation of that bastard, Kim Jonghyun."

"Your room is quite spacious. Why don't you just share?"

"We don't have that kind of relationship."

"It's not like you have to have that kind of relationship to share a room. Heh heh! You're still young, so it's better to enjoy yourself a little more."

"Please, stop with the nonsense. It makes me want to kill you. Is there a spare room or not?"

"How should I know? Why don't you ask Lloyd?"

"I should have just done that in the first place," Scarlet grumbled, leading Lee Sungmin upstairs.

The top floor of the five-story inn held the best rooms, where the four Magic Tower Masters and the commander of the Mage Army were staying. Scarlet stopped in front of Lloyd's door and knocked.

"Who is that man?" Lloyd asked, looking at Lee Sungmin.

While she had been sharp with the Green Tower Master, Scarlet's demeanor toward Lloyd was calm and polite. After she explained the situation, Lloyd nodded.

"There are no spare rooms... Do you not want to share?"

"How can you know what might happen when a grown man and woman share a room?"

"If you don't want to, I can't force you. I'll ask the inn next door. Is that alright?"

"I don't mind." Lee Sungmin was acquainted with Lloyd but chose not to reveal his identity. He didn't want to make things awkward by saying anything unnecessary.

After they left Lloyd's room, Scarlet glanced at him.

[Are you sure you'll be okay?]

[About what?]

[The inn next door is being used by the Murim Alliance and the Moyong Clan. You're not exactly in a respectable position, are you? Are you really okay with that?]

[It doesn't matter. I have no intention of causing friction with them, and I won't let my identity be discovered.]

He had considered sleeping outdoors, but that seemed more likely to attract unwanted attention. It would be better to stay quietly at the inn. They didn't seem like the type to be hostile to a stranger right off the bat, and they wouldn't suspect him without reason.

There were plenty of rooms available at the Murim Alliance's inn. As he walked, Lee Sungmin cataloged the information he had on the members present.

First, the Moyong Clan. Its head was Baek Geomhak Moyong Daeun, a master whose skills were said to rival those of the Heavenly Venerable Sword King of the Namgung Clan. Not only was Moyong Daeun here, but so was his youngest son, Moyong Chan, a fifteen-year-old prodigy. Fifty other masters from their clan were also in the city.

*Chwi Geol... and Poiso, butterfly.*

It wasn't just the Moyong Clan. The one leading the Murim Alliance forces was none other than the Young Sect Leader of the Beggars' Gang, Chwi Geol. Lee Sungmin recalled their meeting long ago. His impression of Chwi Geol wasn't bad. Although Baek Sogo had never returned after going to ask for his help, Lee Sungmin knew it was because Chwi Geol had forced her to escape the Dungeon with him. He didn't resent the man for it. In fact, he believed it had been the right thing to do, an act that had allowed Baek Sogo to completely escape her fate of death.

This would be his third meeting with Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee. The first had been in Shaolin, the second in the Bewitching Forest. One might call it fate, but despite their few encounters, he had never spoken directly with her and didn't have a strong impression.

*I do remember her pissing herself at the end, though.*

In the Bewitching Forest, Tang Ahee had wet her pants. A small chuckle escaped Lee Sungmin as he recalled the memory.

He entered the inn where the martial artists were staying. From the backyard, he could hear the disciplined cries of many voices shouting as one. It sounded like group training. He was curious but decided against going to see for himself.

"I've heard the story from the Golden Magic Tower Master."

A middle-aged man spoke as if he had been waiting the moment Lee Sungmin entered the first-floor hall. He wore a pitch-black martial arts uniform reminiscent of mourning clothes, with the symbol of the Moyong Clan embroidered on his left chest.

Lee Sungmin met the man's deep-set eyes and instantly gauged his strength.

*He's stronger than the Namgung Patriarch.*

The public evaluation was wrong. Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply.

"It is an honor to meet the great Baek Geomhak."

"You've completed the Origin Reversal," the head of the Moyong Clan, Baek Geomhak Moyong Daeun, muttered, his gaze analytical. "Even I cannot see through you. You are... a remarkable master. Do you belong to a sect?"

"I am merely a wanderer."

"Is that so?"

Moyong Daeun's deep-set eyes bored into him, as if trying to peel back every layer of his being. Normally, it would have been impossible for someone of Moyong Daeun's skill to see through him, but Lee Sungmin allowed him to see a fraction of his power, just enough to register as a moderate Supreme Peak master, weaker than Moyong Daeun himself.

The tension seemed to ease from the man's shoulders. He nodded. "If the opportunity arises someday, let's have a duel."

"It would be an honor."

Having seemingly satisfied his suspicion, Moyong Daeun handed Lee Sungmin a room key. Lee Sungmin bowed his head once more.

His room was on an upper floor. As he climbed the stairs, he came across a man in shabby clothes.

A man with only one arm.

"Hello there," the man greeted him warmly, a wide smile revealing his teeth.

It was Chwi Geol.
