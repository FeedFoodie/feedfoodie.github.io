Cheon Ahseong nodded in satisfaction. "See? You've already reached the fifth stage."

Crimson Tiger blinked in shock. This was only his first time trying out a new martial art. How could he already be at the fifth stage of mastery?

"Really?" he asked in disbelief.

"Indeed. At this point, you'll be fine if you just work on the finer details later."

Crimson Tiger instantly understood the significance of those words. For now, he was learning only the essentials, laying the groundwork for a strong structure like a tree spreading its roots. After that, it was only a matter of time before his work bore fruit.

Cheon Ahseong found a comfortable chair and sat down. "What are you doing? Come over here," he commanded.

"Ah, yes."

Although Crimson Tiger was typically confident and poised, he was visibly flustered.

It was only natural. Cheon Ahseong, the Martial God himself, was teaching him!

His diligence and dedication to training contributed greatly to his success, but he could never have reached the fifth stage of mastery so quickly without guidance.

Cheon Ahseong began lecturing again, and Crimson Tiger focused his entire mind. 

Cheon Ahseong was teaching him The Twelve Storm Daggers, but what he learned was more than just a knife-throwing art. It was true enlightenment on the nature of martial arts, delivered by the jianghu's greatest master. A heartfelt lecture unlike anything Baek Museong or Ju Hwayin had ever heard before.

The time it took to master each stage gradually increased. Nevertheless, the two lost track of time, so engrossed were they in their lively discussion.
