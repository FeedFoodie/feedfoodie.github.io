When the Crazy Heavenly Demon opened his eyes, he said nothing. For a long moment, he simply stared at the blank canvas of the sky as the dregs of madness slowly receded. He knew he had surrendered his body and lost his reason, but for how long, he could not say. It was only when his blinking gaze shifted and found Lee Sungmin that the world snapped back into focus.

"Gasp!"

A startled cry tore from his throat as he scrambled to his feet. He staggered backward, his eyes wide with caution as he watched Lee Sungmin. The memories from just before the madness took him were painfully vivid, as little time had passed since he had given himself over to it. He saw it all again, Lee Sungmin shattering his Blood Ring Typhoon with a single gesture, those eyes flashing as a hand closed around his throat.

…*No, he's different…*

The man standing before him now was not the same one from his memory. The Crazy Heavenly Demon could see it in the calm depths of Lee Sungmin's eyes and feel it in the aura that now flowed from him. That did not mean he could let his guard down. He had no idea when that monstrous, terrifying energy might overwhelm him again.

"Who… who in the world are you?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon stammered. "What kind of man are you?"

Ignoring the question, Lee Sungmin turned away. He retrieved the spear the Void Monarch had set down and walked toward the older man.

"Have you calmed down a bit?"

"......"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon grunted, his expression confused, but he did not lunge forward as he had before. A strange feeling stirred deep within his heart, an unconscious impulse that urged him away from hostility. It was a definite influence, one he could not quite understand.

[My Demonic Energy is in that bastard's qi center. That maniac is being influenced by it, so he won't be able to oppose you, who is directly connected to me.]

"What did you do to me?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's question came at the same time as the Void Monarch's voice reached Lee Sungmin, who wondered for a moment how to answer.

[Is there any need to explain? Just threaten him to answer your questions. He'll probably listen well. If he doesn't, just beat him up a little.]

*I'd have to be strong enough to beat him.*

[You don't understand. To put it simply, the relationship between you and that maniac is similar to that of vampires. Just as the lowest of them cannot defy their progenitor, the Lord, that man cannot defy you. It's not a one-sided hierarchical relationship that demands obedience… but even if you beat him to a pulp, he won't be able to kill you. If he tried, the Demonic Energy embedded in his qi center would go berserk.]

Lee Sungmin wondered if it would truly be so easy. He looked into the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes and spoke.

"What I did to you isn't important."

"Not important, you say," the Crazy Heavenly Demon grumbled.

"So… Elder Crazy Heavenly Demon. I have a few questions I'd like to ask. Will you answer them?"

"…Kngh…"

Normally, he would not have even listened, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon groaned and tilted his head. An unknown emotion kept urging him on. With a final sigh, he sat down and nodded.

"What are you curious about?"

"They say there's an essence of Demonic Energy in your qi center. Do you know what that is?"

"…There's something in this one's qi center?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's brow furrowed in thought. He sat cross-legged and began an Energy Circulation Exercise, acting without a shred of hesitation despite Lee Sungmin's presence. A short while later, his closed eyes snapped open.

"I don't know what you're talking about," he replied, his voice steady. "There is nothing but my own inner arts in this one's qi center."

[Interesting,] the Void Monarch chuckled upon hearing the answer.

[That maniac doesn't know there's Demonic Energy in his qi center, nor does he know he's using it himself, and yet he remains human. This is the first time I've seen a case like this… Hmm.]

*What is it?*

[I have an idea.]

After that, the Void Monarch fell silent, apparently organizing his thoughts. As Lee Sungmin waited, the Crazy Heavenly Demon also remained quiet, watching him with a cautious air he could not understand.

*Why am I being so cautious around a brat like him?*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon was an incredibly arrogant man. Even in his younger, weaker days, long before he earned his moniker and reigned as the stronger party in most conflicts, he had never bowed his head or acted cautiously when facing those more powerful than himself, yet now was different. He found himself watching the young man warily, and stranger still, the feeling was not unpleasant. He could make no sense of the complicated situation, and it was then that the silent Void Monarch finally spoke again.

[Ask him about the South.]

*What?*

[I said, ask him if he's ever been to the South, and ask what happened to him there.]

Lee Sungmin posed the question just as the Void Monarch had instructed. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes widened in surprise before he nodded.

"The South… It was an impressive place."

"What happened to you there?"

"…Mmm."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon groaned. He honestly did not want to answer, but a strong urge compelled him to speak. He hesitated for a moment more before finally opening his mouth.

His story began ten years ago, when he had hit a formidable wall in his martial arts training. Hitting a wall was a common occurrence, and he had always broken through them with his own efforts, but this time was different. After repeated frustration, he set out on a journey to regain his peace of mind.

"My feet led me to the lands of the South. The hot sun, the dense jungle, the desert… I saw many things. During my travels, I formed a connection with a certain tribe in the jungle. They were a strange folk. Among those they called warriors, there wasn't a single one who looked like an easy opponent. I'd say none of the boastful disciples from the Nine Sects One Gang were stronger than that tribe's young warriors, and the slightly older ones were on the level of the Sect Leaders themselves."

As he spoke, the corners of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes crinkled.

"What interested me was that despite their outstanding skills, the tribe's warriors hadn't learned any proper martial arts. Finding it a waste, I formed a bond by teaching them a few of my own techniques. In return, they taught me about their tribe's miracle herbs and a special breathing method."

[That's it!] the Void Monarch shouted in Lee Sungmin's mind. [The South is where I made a name for myself in the past. I heard some of the tribes there worship me, so it seems my Demonic Energy and the method to handle it were left behind.] His voice was filled with excitement.

"What is this breathing method?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"At first, I thought it was a type of inner arts technique, but it wasn't. I couldn't build up inner arts directly with it. However, it did help calm my mind, so it was a great help to me. Through that breathing method, I absorbed their miracle herbs and made great progress with my internal energy, which allowed me to finally break through the wall that blocked me."

A long sigh escaped him as he finished.

"I thought overcoming that wall would lead me to the Transcendent Realm… but it was much farther away than I imagined. I came close, but I have yet to reach it. After leaving the South, I wandered, continuing my practice… and my excessive greed led me to fall into qi deviation."

[Foolish bastard,] the Void Monarch sneered. [Breathing method or whatever, Demonic Energy is not a power a human can easily control. His qi deviation is the backlash. Still, it's surprising it only ended with lunacy. Normally, he would have exploded and died or turned completely into a Yokai.] He muttered this before addressing Lee Sungmin directly. [Learn that breathing method. If you master it, you can use Demonic Energy while maintaining your humanity.]

*…Is that really necessary?*

[You're the one who craved power. The choice is always yours, but let me tell you this. With your current body, it'll take you at least dozens of years, if not hundreds, to reach the level of the Little Heavenly Demon you're aiming for.]

*I'm growing stronger.*

He could say that with certainty. He had not used magic in his fight with the Crazy Heavenly Demon. Haste and Strength might have made the battle easier, but he had intentionally refrained from using them to force himself to gain insights through a life-or-death struggle. In truth, it was less about insights and more about his body belatedly catching up to his advanced mind, but either way, the outcome was what he had wished for.

[That method is too dangerous. There's no guarantee you'll conveniently gain insights from repeated life-or-death battles.]

*Demonic Energy is just as dangerous.*

[You son of a bitch. I'm trying to spoon-feed you something good, and you're struggling like you don't want to eat it. Hey, you dog bastard. This Elder is doing all this for your own good, so why don't you understand?]

The frustrated Void Monarch spewed curses, but Lee Sungmin indifferently ignored them and fell into thought. He did covet the power of Demonic Energy. That destructive force would make his body much stronger, allowing him to more easily manifest the martial prowess he had attained in his mental world.

*It was too shallow this time, too.*

Just before the Blood Ring Typhoon was unleashed, his spear had penetrated more shallowly than he intended. If he had used Haste and Strength, he might have pierced the Crazy Heavenly Demon's body before the technique could be completed. That judgment, however, had been too slow, and he was not pleased with it.

It was frustrating that his body could not perform as well as he expected, as well as he knew it could. There was no guarantee he could master the martial arts of his Mental World just by progressing step by step. It had taken him two thousand one hundred years to reach that peak, and even with the experience, there was no telling how quickly he could regain that prowess.

"…That breathing method. Can you teach it to me as well?"

"Impossible," the Crazy Heavenly Demon answered, his brow furrowed. "When I received the breathing method and miracle herbs, I swore an oath that I would never teach the breathing method to others. It wasn't just a verbal promise but one made through a binding vow, so I can never break it."

[How troublesome,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

"A binding vow?"

"It's one of the spells used by the shamans of the South. Once you make a promise with a binding vow, it's impossible to break. No matter how powerful a martial artist I am, I offered my heart for the vow, so I cannot break it."

[It's similar to the Mana Oaths that mages make. If you make a promise with a binding vow and offer your heart, your heart will explode if you break that promise.]

"Where is that tribe in the South?"

"No matter how good my memory is, I can't remember the location of a tribe I reached by wandering through a jungle."

The answer was exhausting. He had just resolved himself to try using Demonic Energy, only to find the path blocked.

[Let's go to the South,] the Void Monarch said, his voice suddenly excited. [Didn't I promise you? That if you brought me out, I would give you the treasure I've collected. The treasure is also in the South, so this works out perfectly.]

*But we don't know the location.*

[You're a complete idiot. Why don't we just take that maniac as our guide?]

*What?*

[Tell him you'll give him a cure for his lunacy,] the Void Monarch said with a chuckle. [His lunacy is a backlash from using Demonic Energy, and he came all this way because he was chasing me. When I was sealed in the Sleeping Forest, his madness probably acted up often, but when I left with you and came to the North, it wouldn't have been as severe.]

That was true. Originally, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's episodes involved slaughter and destruction, but that violence had vanished when the Void Monarch left the forest. Instead, the Demonic Energy in his qi center began to move his body in search of its original owner. This was why the Crazy Heavenly Demon had lost consciousness for months, his body following them from afar as they traveled from the Dungeon to the North.

"I'll tell you the cure for your lunacy, so let's go to the South together."

"What did you say?"

At Lee Sungmin's words, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes flew wide open. To be told there was a cure for the madness he had harbored for so long was shocking enough, but setting that aside, he felt no desire to refuse the offer.

"How do you intend to cure my lunacy?"

"Um…" Lee Sungmin trailed off, having no real answer. The Void Monarch supplied one in his head.

[Just make something up. As long as he's with me, his lunacy won't act up anyway.]

"…First… you must chant the spell 'domaksaramu' a hundred times every day… and receive one slap from me in the morning, afternoon, and evening."

The spell was nonsense he had just invented, and the slap was born from a simple desire to hit the man for picking a fight.

"Domaksaramu. I don't know what it is, but it has a very shamanistic ring to it… but a slap? Why do I have to be slapped?"

"If you don't believe me, then don't do it."

"Urgh!"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon groaned at Lee Sungmin's take-it-or-leave-it attitude. The conditions were completely unbelievable, yet for some reason, he found himself believing the words in his heart.

"I understand."

"Then, first one."

The instant the Crazy Heavenly Demon answered, Lee Sungmin slapped his cheek. With a sharp SMACK, the older man's head whipped to the side.

"……"

No matter how great a master he was, the unexpected slap was enough to startle him.

"Chant the spell," Lee Sungmin snapped quickly, before the Crazy Heavenly Demon could protest.

Clutching his reddened cheek, the Crazy Heavenly Demon ground his teeth and began to chant.

"domaksaramu… domaksaramu…"

Listening to him, Lee Sungmin swallowed a laugh.
