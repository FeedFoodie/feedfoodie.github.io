With a tense expression, Lee Sungmin approached the Magic Armor. He recognized it from his past life, a full suit of glistening, jet-black armor always worn by a certain mercenary. That mercenary's rank, however, had been too low to even participate in this subjugation.

*If I can just get my hands on that armor.*

It had been one of his goals ever since he returned to Zenavis, just as it had been in his past life. He remembered how a low-class mercenary with mediocre skills had become a peak realm master simply by obtaining that single suit of armor. It was a life-changing opportunity, the kind of chance Lee Sungmin had always yearned for.

However, he had seized his chances, hadn't he? Several of them. Obtaining the Stone of Reincarnation was a chance. Meeting Wiji Hoyeon was a chance. Mount Mushii and his encounter with Baek Sogo had been chances as well.

And now, the Magic Armor was right in front of him. Lee Sungmin reached out his hand.

The instant his fingers brushed against the cold metal, the chest plate of the armor hanging on the wall split open and swallowed him whole. He stumbled back in shock, only to find the jet-black Magic Armor already fitted perfectly to his form.

"......"

Lee Sungmin looked down at the armor enveloping his body. It had equipped itself automatically, yet he felt no different. He hurriedly checked his fighting spirit inner qi, but there was no change. The same was true for any physical enhancements. After a thorough inspection of his body, Lee Sungmin could only feel a sense of bewilderment.

Nothing had changed at all. *Why? Is the armor different?* No, there was no way to confirm that right now. He would have to return to a city and ask a professional appraiser to identify the armor's name and functions.

"Do you like it?"

A low voice tickled his ear. Startled, Lee Sungmin spun around, his spear snapping up into a defensive stance.

A man wearing a jet-black robe stood there.

"Magic Armor Devonac," the man muttered. "It's a pretty decent item, though its large rebound is a significant flaw."

"...What?"

The Black Mage wasn't the least bit intimidated, even with Lee Sungmin poised to strike just a few steps away. If anything, it was Lee Sungmin who was intimidated. The sixth sense he had honed on Mount Mushii was screaming a warning at him.

*Do not fight that thing.*

"You felt no changes at all?"

Only jet-black darkness could be seen inside the deep hood. His face was not visible, but the voice that emerged was that of a wizened old man.

"That's quite strange..." the Black Mage muttered. "Well. That's not what's important." As he spoke, his robe began to slowly swell. "I don't like intruders... huhu! To think I'd have an encounter like this in my lifetime. I never imagined I'd meet a soul with a twisted causality..."

Lee Sungmin's body stiffened at the Black Mage's words. He didn't wait any longer and charged, unleashing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break at full power. His form blurred into a series of afterimages, closing the distance in an instant as his spear, wreathed in violet energy, plunged toward the Black Mage's chest.

KRAAANG!

The impact sent a jarring resistance up the spear shaft, feeling as if his very grip was being torn apart, and his body was flung backward. A violent reversal of his inner qi ravaged his qi and blood, and he coughed up a spray of black blood.

"......"

After breaking through a bookshelf and tumbling several times, Lee Sungmin collapsed to the floor, letting out a pained groan.

"Don't do anything rash," the Black Mage chuckled. "I'm in a very good mood right now."

It felt as if something heavy was pressing down on his solar plexus. Lee Sungmin grit his teeth, biting his lower lip as he pushed himself up. Just then, the sound of running echoed from the hallway, drawn by the commotion.

"What's going on?!"

The mercenaries who ran to the door froze. They quickly assessed the situation, their eyes darting between Lee Sungmin sprawled in the middle of the room and the Black Mage standing idly by. Bern, who arrived a moment later, shouted.

"Black Mage!"

"Intruders," the Black Mage muttered. He slowly raised a hand and pointed at Bern.

When Bern saw the hand emerging from the wide sleeve of the robe, his face turned deathly pale.

"A-a Lich...!"

"......"

A voice full of mockery seeped out from inside the hood. The long, extended finger was not that of a living human but a stark white bone. A jet-black light flashed from its tip, shooting a straight line of death toward Bern.

KABOOM!

Zeon, who was near Bern, quickly threw a punch. The Hundred Steps Divine Fist, fired from a distance, collided with the Black Mage's attack. The Lich clicked its tongue.

"Shaolin's Hundred Steps Divine Fist, I see," it muttered. "So, you must be that Zeon fellow, the one who's so famous in Behenger."

"...You know me?" Zeon asked, his face stiff.

The Black Mage did not answer. Instead, it slowly removed the hood it was wearing.

What was revealed was not a face. It looked like a clump of darkness, a round mass where a pair of ghastly blue lights flickered like eyes.

He had never seen a Lich before, but he knew the rumors. It was a monster born when a mage forsook their body and soul to gain eternal life. That was right. A Lich was classified as a monster, and a powerful one at that, considered one of the worst kinds. It wielded magic comparable to an archmage and possessed a vast amount of Mana, the price for abandoning its humanity. On top of that, it retained its memories and human-like thought processes, making it more than worthy of its terrible reputation.

"Normally, you all wouldn't have even made it this far," the Lich muttered.

Bern, who had just been saved by Zeon, stumbled backward with a pale face. As a mage himself, he knew exactly how dreadful and terrifying a being a Lich was.

"But not today. You've saved me the trouble of having to kidnap a sacrifice. I was originally planning to use the Ogre Chimera, but..."

The Lich muttered something incomprehensible and raised its hand. Darkness surged from beneath its feet and coiled around its arm. Bern hurriedly began to cast, but whatever magic he was trying to unleash, one thing was certain.

He was slower than the Lich.

KWWAAAAANG!

The darkness shot forward. One of the mercenaries standing at the front, unaware of the danger, was swept away by it. An S-Rank Mercenary, a warrior of incredible caliber, died in vain. When the darkness faded, all that remained was a corpse with its flesh burned away, leaving only bone.

"R-run!" Bern roared.

His belatedly completed magic exploded toward the Lich, a wave of crimson flames washing over the creature. The Lich let out a laugh and formed a seal with its bony hands.

"You don't know your place."

It didn't even chant an incantation. A powerful shield, created instantly, blocked Bern's magic. Tang Misook screamed, turned, and ran, but she couldn't escape. A blade shot up from the ground and split her body in two.

"Did I not tell you?" the Lich muttered. "Normally, you would not have even come here. I am the one who made you come here."

Zeon leaped forward, choosing to fight rather than run. He had judged that escaping this place would be impossible unless the Lich was killed. He was not the only one who came to that conclusion. All the surviving S-Rank Mercenaries charged toward the Lich. Lee Sungmin did the same.

Zeon, the first to reach the creature, unleashed his Hundred Steps Divine Fist. The Lich was still holding its hand seal, and the shield remained intact.

THUD!

The shield shook with a heavy sound. Zeon took his stance and threw a series of punches, and with each one, the formless fist strikes hammered against the barrier.

The Lich showed no signs of urgency. It seemed to be enjoying the current situation, like a predator watching the futile struggles of its captured prey. Gritting his teeth, Lee Sungmin attacked the Lich from behind, the spear in his hands beginning to spin furiously. With Zeon here, he no longer had any thought of hiding his martial arts.

"You stay still."

The moment Lee Sungmin's spear touched the Lich's shield, the creature muttered those words. Space itself seemed to warp. Lee Sungmin's spear slid harmlessly across the surface of the shield, its trajectory forcibly altered. In that brief moment, the Lich had cast spatial distortion magic to deflect his attack.

"I can't have you getting hurt," the Lich said, changing its hand seal. "I can't afford to lose such a valuable soul."

Meanwhile, Zeon and the other mercenaries were still struggling desperately to break through the shield. The Lich ignored them. Their attacks were not even worth its attention. It was paying more attention to Lee Sungmin than to all the other mercenaries combined.

"......"

Jet-black chains shot up from the floor. They coiled around Lee Sungmin's limbs, yanking him off his feet and slamming him to the ground.

"Just stay there quietly," the Lich said with a laugh.

Lee Sungmin tried to get up, but the tightening chains wouldn't release him. His face contorted in a pained grimace.

And just like that, Bern died. When the explosion that went *pop* faded, Bern's legs, his upper body gone, fell backward. The Lich's shield remained unbroken, and with every flick of its finger, the number of corpses grew.

Pinned to the floor, Lee Sungmin watched it all.

He watched others die amidst a terrible sense of powerlessness and fear. Time seemed to stop, or perhaps, to slow to a crawl. *Why am I lying here on the floor? Why am I so powerless?* His body trembled violently. He had thought he was different now, that he had changed after descending Mount Mushii. He believed he was closer to becoming something more, even if he wasn't a cicada yet.

However, what was the reality?

This was the reality.

Lee Sungmin was lying on the cold floor. He wanted to resist, but resistance was impossible. The Lich was a monster. He now understood with absolute clarity just how much violence a being like an archmage could wield. Zeon, an SS-Rank Mercenary, was being toyed with, and Bern, another SS-Rank Mercenary who was said to be on the verge of becoming an archmage, was already dead.

And then, the battle was over. The survivors were Zeon and three S-Rank Mercenaries. They were slumped on the floor, bound by black chains just like Lee Sungmin.

"This should be enough," the Lich said, its voice cheerful. "An SS-Rank Mercenary... much better than an Ogre Chimera."

The Lich paid no mind to the corpses littering the floor and moved its hand. Lee Sungmin, Zeon, and the other two mercenaries floated gently into the air.

"Now, let's go."

The Lich waved both its arms.

"From now on, every day will be fun."

Its laughing voice was bone-chilling.

"......"

THUMP THUMP.

From deep underground, the sound of a heart beating could be heard.
