December was drawing to a close. In the two months since the fight with the Blood Heavenly Demon, the North had been quiet, offering no further encounters of note. Lee Sungmin had spent that time in the Trabia Northern District, receiving martial arts instruction from the Crazy Heavenly Demon and achieving some success.

Once the year ended, he would have no reason to remain. He needed to reach the giant dao city of Ruvus before March to meet Wiji Hoyeon, and the journey south would be a tight one. For that reason, he had to begin his preparations to leave even before the new year arrived.

"Let's get going!"

Rubia's undisguised delight puzzled Lee Sungmin, but he did not bother to ask her why. When he first came north, his companions had been Rubia and the Void Monarch. Now, heading south, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had joined them, making for a group of four. Of course, the Void Monarch remained hidden from sight, dwelling within Lee Sungmin's Magic Armor.

"domaksaramu, domaksaramu…"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's chanting mingled with the fierce northern snowstorm. Though their schedule was tight, they had started with some time to spare. With the year not yet over, there was no need to rush from the very beginning of their journey.

The scent of blood tainted the wind.

Lee Sungmin stopped walking. He was not the only one who had detected it. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's ceaseless chanting fell silent. Rubia also halted, her gaze fixed ahead.

Beyond the swirling snow stood Baek Museon. He tossed a liquor bottle he had been drinking from onto the snowy ground and met Lee Sungmin's eyes. Seen through the fierce gale, Baek Museon's eyes burned red.

"…The Martial God?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon stepped forward, his voice laced with surprise. Lee Sungmin could only stare blankly at the figure standing not far away. The presence Baek Museon exuded was so ominous it was nauseating, and the killing intent pouring from his eyes sent a chill through his entire body. Inside his head, the Void Monarch clicked his tongue.

[He's become a monster.]

The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not know that Baek Museon had become a Vampire. He simply stood beside Lee Sungmin, staring in disbelief. Baek Museon offered no explanation. His transformation was a different outcome than what the Crazy Heavenly Demon had intended. Had Jeniela not tempted him so relentlessly, he might never have become a Vampire. He might have overcome his despair as a martial artist and continued to strive, just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon had hoped.

However, the path Baek Museon chose was not that of a martial artist. He chose the path of the inhuman, and an inhuman he became. The Crazy Heavenly Demon's mouth fell agape as he stared at Baek Museon's newly grown left arm.

"A Vampire," Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice.

The words struck the Crazy Heavenly Demon with horror. He was not ignorant about Vampires. As he watched Baek Museon drip with a bloody aura, his lips moved without a sound.

"You foolish bastard…!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon finally spat, his face twisting in disgust.

Baek Museon, who had been wiping blood from the corner of his lips with the back of his hand, finally spoke.

"Elder," he called out to the Crazy Heavenly Demon, his eyes turning to him. "If you respect me at all, Elder, do not interfere in the fight between me and that bastard. I'm truly asking you. Do not interfere."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's shoulders trembled, but he remained silent. Lee Sungmin glanced at him. Though he said nothing, his attitude made it clear he would not intervene. Lee Sungmin sighed and muttered, "So you've come back with a new arm."

Baek Museon did not answer.

"Why did you become a Vampire? Because you wanted to kill me?"

"That was part of it." Baek Museon's mouth opened as if to say more, but he just smirked and closed it again. "At this point, I don't think the reason matters. I want to kill you, and I want to kill that bitch, the Little Heavenly Demon, too."

"Is that why you came here?"

"No." Baek Museon began to walk forward with long strides. A crimson mist rose with every step he took, its color incomparably darker than it had been two months ago. It exuded an indescribably intense and ominous feeling. "My queen wishes it."

The queen.

[It must be Jeniela,] the Void Monarch muttered.

"The queen wishes for you to fall into Despair," Baek Museon said, his tone blind and broken. He was one of Jeniela's now, and her command was absolute. His approach sent a cascade of thoughts through Lee Sungmin's mind. He wondered what kind of despair Jeniela desired, and he resolved with chilling certainty that he must never, ever become a Vampire.

"…I…" the Crazy Heavenly Demon began to say.

Lee Sungmin cut him off without hearing the rest. "Please protect Rubia."

The Magic Armor deployed, enveloping his body in an instant. He drew the spear slung across his back, never having imagined he would fight Baek Museon again like this. Two months ago, he had won a rather easy victory. Baek Museon had been an opponent he could defeat without even resorting to magic.

That was no longer the case. The wickedness emanating from Baek Museon's presence was just that intense.

[It seems Jeniela has given him quite a bit of power,] the Void Monarch muttered. The Sword Phantom, another who had become a Vampire, had been strong, but he was no match for Baek Museon. Besides, that had been on the Night of the Full Moon. It was not a full moon now, yet the wickedness pouring from Baek Museon was incomparable. It was only natural. Even before his transformation, Baek Museon was a martial artist of a higher realm than the Sword Phantom, and Jeniela had clearly granted him far more power. The man before him was a monster, stronger now than he had been even in his prime several years ago.

[Be careful,] the Void Monarch warned. [Don't get arrogant just because you won two months ago. You can't look down on him. Not if you don't want to die.]

Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh at the words. Arrogant? What a ridiculous thing to say. He was not the type to be arrogant in the first place. Feeling his senses go numb, he lowered his stance. His lips moved as he chanted the words for Strength and Haste.

His fighting spirit inner arts converted into magic power, casting the spell. He felt his entire body grow lighter and stronger as he tensed his knees.

PWAK!

The snowy ground exploded beneath his feet. Baek Museon charged, swinging both arms wide to unleash a twin palm strike. The red mist of the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art scattered in all directions, dyeing the snow a bloody red. Lee Sungmin quickly swung his spear to meet the attack. KWAANG! The crimson palm force and the violet enhanced qi shattered against each other. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened at the crushing pressure that slammed into him. It had been the right choice to cast Strength and Haste beforehand. Without their aid, he would have been at a disadvantage from the very first blow.

Baek Museon was faster and fiercer than before. The crimson mist of his art stained the space red, announcing his overwhelming presence. Lee Sungmin immediately retaliated, unleashing the fourth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Nine Dragons Slaughter. That was not all. He operated the Violet Dawn Divine Art in concert with the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Violet and crimson enhanced qi intertwined, forming a rotating ring that covered the entire length of his spear.

The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art's first strike, Blood Ring Shattering.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear's fourth form, Nine Dragons Slaughter.

Two martial arts were unleashed as one. Nine dragons, augmented with a crimson rotation, shot forward with a level of power incomparable to before, yet Baek Museon did not retreat an inch. He was numb to death, obeying Jeniela's command. He did not even seem to feel the threat of death from Lee Sungmin's attack. He lifted his forward foot high and stomped it on the ground. KWAAAAA! The thick layer of snow evaporated completely as a red haze surged up from the earth. The Nine Dragons Slaughter, wreathed in the Blood Ring Shattering, was blocked, unable to pierce the haze. Beyond it, Baek Museon held his breath and brought a tightly clenched fist to his waist.

KUGUGUGUNG! Ominous magic power coiled around his fist. As Baek Museon released his breath and thrust his fist forward, the very nature of the mist changed, dyeing the formless fist energy a deep red. KWAANG! With a sound as loud as a thunderclap, his punch tore through space.

[Dodge it!] the Void Monarch screamed in terror.

Lee Sungmin hurriedly used the teleportation of the Traceless Step. KWAAAAA! Baek Museon's punch carved a long, deep scar into the snowy ground. If he had been even a fraction of a second late, he would have taken the blow head-on, but he could not relax. A chilling gaze pierced him from somewhere within the red mist that now stained his surroundings. Lee Sungmin moved faithfully, following his sixth sense's warning. He twisted his waist, raising his spear to collide with Baek Museon's palm.

"Kuk…!"

A groan escaped his lips. Baek Museon started to laugh. Hahahaha! The loud, manic sound echoed from beyond the mist. Lee Sungmin chased after the sound, executing his movement arts as Baek Museon vanished back into the haze. His One Step Dancing Shadow created countless afterimages. Not stopping there, he unleashed the Third Form of Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Two Step Calamity. Two consecutive steps explosively released his enhanced qi.

The Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art's second form, Bloody Pursuit. The enhanced qi from both techniques entangled and swept through the area. The red mist thinned slightly but did not disappear.

[Jeniela, that crazy bitch!] the Void Monarch shouted in frustration.

The power of the vampiric Baek Museon was beyond imagination, enough to horrify even him. To an already outstanding martial artist, Jeniela's sinister power had been added, giving birth to a tremendous monster. The Void Monarch was not the only one horrified. Even the Crazy Heavenly Demon was shocked by Baek Museon's power. The strength of an inhuman, gained by abandoning one's humanity, was just that terrifying.

[In front!] the Void Monarch yelled.

Lee Sungmin twisted his spear upwards, drawing a circle with the entire weapon. KWADEDEUK! His feet sank deep into the ground. He gritted his teeth against the shock that rattled his internal organs. He had tried to deflect the attack with Ran's Technique but could not redirect it completely. Gasping at the metallic taste in his mouth, Lee Sungmin continued to move his spear.

PABABAK! Baek Museon's arms closed in, clashing with his Spear Technique as their enhanced qi scattered in all directions. Lee Sungmin held his breath, his eyes wide. The Void Monarch was chattering endlessly in his head, but he had no time to listen. The only thing he could hear was Baek Museon's loud, echoing laughter.

"Look!" Baek Museon cried out in joy, unleashing a storm of palm and fist techniques with both arms.

There were no openings. Lee Sungmin's spear had the advantage of range, but it was not enough to overwhelm his opponent. Baek Museon was superior in every way. Proficiency in martial arts mattered little in this situation. As a Vampire, he was faster and stronger than Lee Sungmin, and he possessed senses and reaction speeds that were inhumanly sharp. Unlike Lee Sungmin, who had to predict and react without seeing, Baek Museon was seeing and reacting.

"Hahaha!"

An opening in his spear path was breached. Baek Museon's fist shot through and struck Lee Sungmin's body. Despite mobilizing the defense of his Magic Armor, his Protective Qi Aura, and even Rebounding Qi, blood sprayed from Lee Sungmin's mouth.

"I won't kill you. I won't…!" Madness flowed from Baek Museon's eyes. The hatred for Lee Sungmin, which had been suppressed by Jeniela's will, reared its head. *If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have become a Vampire. I should never have lost to someone like you.* His lips moved, spitting out the words in a rapid torrent.

"What are you babbling about…!" Lee Sungmin spat, his feet dragging as he was pushed back. He reapplied Strength and Haste and charged toward Baek Museon again. His heart pounded, his senses screaming.

[Use your Demonic Energy,] the Void Monarch shouted.

*I don't want to.* Baek Museon's very existence was a cautionary tale, showing him just how terrible the path of an inhuman was. If he had found a way to control his Demonic Energy in the South, it might be a different story, but trying to handle it now, with a human body… there was no telling what might happen.

Lee Sungmin pushed strength into his staggering legs and kicked off the ground. Baek Museon spread his hands wide. The red mist flowing around them began to emit a fierce, surging power. Lee Sungmin knew this technique.

Blood Mist Explosion.

Baek Museon spat, his lips twisting into a cruel smile. Before the mist could surge, Lee Sungmin quickly extended his foot. He unleashed Two Step Gentle Ripple, the same technique that had broken through the Blood Mist Explosion two months ago.

[It's martial arts theory,] the Void Monarch muttered.

When the mist exploded, Two Step Gentle Ripple failed to control its flow as it had before.

"Hahaha!" Baek Museon burst into loud laughter.

Standing in the center of the mist, Lee Sungmin collapsed, covered in blood.
