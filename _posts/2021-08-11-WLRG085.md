A small-framed boy stood in the open doorway. No, that wasn't quite right. Lee Sungmin found he couldn't tell if the figure was a boy or a girl at all. From one angle, they appeared male, yet from another, distinctly female. He stared at the person, him or her, for a long moment before stammering out a name.

"...Denir?"

"That's right," the figure confirmed with a nod. Just as their appearance was impossible to place, Denir's voice was perfectly androgynous. Like Mushii, this god also wore the form of a young child. Sungmin couldn't fathom why these so-called gods chose such immature vessels, though that question was hardly important now.

"Damn that Mushii. Why'd he dump such a troublesome guy on me?" Denir muttered before spinning around.

Sungmin stared at Denir's back until the god let out a loud sigh and turned to face him again.

"Are you planning to be satisfied just by looking at my face?"

"...Yes?"

"If you're satisfied with just that, it's a good thing for me."

That couldn't be right. Sungmin quickly followed Denir inside. The moment he crossed the threshold, the door swung shut behind him as if it had been waiting. Denir strode down the hallway of the small, rural house. It wasn't long, and they soon reached a modest living room. Sungmin's eyes were fixed on the back of Denir's head, where a short bob of hair swayed over their shoulders, looking just as easily like it belonged to a boy as a girl. A strange unease settled over him. As if sensing his gaze, Denir spun around, crossed to a nearby sofa, and plopped down.

"What do you want from me?" Denir asked. The question was so sudden that Sungmin, who hadn't prepared an answer, could only hesitate. Denir addressed him again.

"I assume you didn't come all this way just to pay your respects. So what do you want from me?"

"I... don't know what to say when you ask me so suddenly..."

"Hmm. I suppose you're right. In that case, I'll change the question. What is it that you desire?"

The point of reference shifted from what Sungmin wanted from Denir to what Sungmin himself desired, yet, he couldn't properly answer that question either. In response to his silence, Denir chuckled.

"You're quite indecisive. Or do you just think too much? Well, alright. A god should be merciful. How about we try a more casual conversation instead?" Denir crossed his legs as he spoke. Even with the suggestion, it was their first time meeting. What could one possibly discuss with a god? After a moment of silence, Sungmin opened his mouth.

"Are you a man, or a woman?"

It wasn't a serious question, just a joke meant to break the awkward atmosphere. Denir let out a hollow laugh in response.

"You humans worry about the strangest things... Gods don't have a gender. We only have certain preferences. Mushii takes a female form because of his own wicked taste. A god of trials and penance appearing as a little girl. Don't you think that's wicked?"

He'd never thought of it that way, but hearing Denir put it like that, it did seem so. As Sungmin nodded blankly, Denir continued.

"It's the same for me. This form I've taken has no gender. If you wish to see me as a man, then see me as a man. If you wish to see me as a woman, then see me as a woman."

"...Is taking a young form also just a preference?"

"No. This isn't a preference, it's unavoidable," Denir muttered, pulling out a cigar. The sight was jarringly out of place, an androgynous child putting a cigar far thicker than their own fingers to their lips.

"You've defied time," Denir stated.

The words sent a twitch through Sungmin's cheek. He wasn't surprised, though. It was only natural for a god like Denir to realize his causality was twisted. Mushii had noticed, so there was no way Denir wouldn't have.

"Your causality is twisted. Well, it's not as if beings with twisted causality are unheard of. Your case, however, is unique. You've defied time. You came back from the final result of death... Hehe! How very amusing." Denir laughed, swinging his legs.

Sungmin watched him for a moment before speaking. "I'm under the protection of some unknown entity. I managed to erase a part of it, but it's not all gone."

"I know," Denir replied with a laugh. "The blessing of an entity that favors you is not something a mere mortal can touch. You erased part of it? Hehe. You didn't erase it. You were simply allowed to erase it. It likely wasn't anything important."

Sungmin pressed on. "Can you tell me more about this in detail?"

"Your existence is an anomaly," Denir stated flatly. "Having returned from death, you possess memories from before you died. Without you, this world would have unfolded exactly according to those memories. That's how it should be. However, your very existence has become a variable."

"...What... do you mean?"

"Did you live exactly the same as in your previous life?" Denir asked. "Can you say you've lived without any difference whatsoever? Can you say you've lived each day without the slightest deviation from the days you spent in your past life? Did you sleep at the exact same time every day as in your past life? Eat, drink, relieve yourself, talk, and meet people at the same times? Did you do all that?"

"Th... that's impossible. How could I possibly do that?"

"Exactly, and that's why your existence became a variable. Because you lived each day differently from your past life, your existence is a variable affecting the flow of this world."

"I'm not that important of a person."

"The flutter of a small butterfly's wings can become a great whirlwind far away. In fact, the world has already changed from what you remember, hasn't it?"

At that, Sungmin's expression stiffened. He could think of a few things.

Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master, had been missing in the past life he remembered, but because 'Lee Sungmin' had been in that Dungeon, Lloyd had returned alive instead of disappearing.

The rumors about the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon were different. She was gaining fame faster than in his past life and was known as a woman, not a man.

Even besides those two, which were arguably the biggest differences, dozens, even hundreds of other things came to his mind. Had he lived every day exactly the same? Of course not. He couldn't even remember every single day from back then.

Sleeping at the same time? Eating, drinking, relieving himself, talking, meeting people.

*Impossible.* Even if he clearly remembered every day of his past life, he couldn't have lived that way. The current Lee Sungmin was different from the Lee Sungmin of his past life. He had learned different martial arts, lived a different life, and had different encounters.

"You, as a variable, are changing this world from how it was in your past life. What do you think that signifies?"

"...I don't know..."

"Your very existence is tangling up the entire flow of this world," Denir said, grinning to show his teeth. "I am the God of Time. Time is a flow. You are preventing this world from flowing as it should, creating a new one entirely. Are you curious about the being protecting you? How could I possibly answer that? No one in this world can tell you about him. Especially us, who are revered as 'gods', can never speak of him."

"Why...?"

"Because that is 'The End', which must never be spoken of. The moment we mention it, we must be prepared for annihilation. A god like Mushii, who has his own holy land and followers and is worshipped, might be able to take 'the Apostle of the End' down with him, but it's impossible for me. I have no holy land, nor do I have any followers."

The End. The Apostle of the End. The moment he heard those names, Sungmin felt a deathly chill, a sensation he had felt before. He had felt this chill when he heard Mushii's muttering and when he heard the story from Kim Jonghyun.

"Haha... See? Even a slight mention draws the attention of the unorthodox. I have no intention of speaking further, so I'd appreciate it if you'd back down," Denir said with a joyful expression.

At his words, the chill receded. Sungmin let out the breath he had been holding and rubbed his neck.

"Wh-what was that just now?"

"The Apostle was merely about to approach. I can't say any more. Still... it seems you're someone I can have a conversation with." Denir rose to his feet as he said this. "I cannot answer your question. None of the gods can answer your question. We too are beings who have not escaped the Cycle of Mortality, utterly powerless before The End."

"Then who can answer it?"

"Find a being who has escaped the Cycle of Mortality."

"How am I supposed to know who that is if you don't tell me...!"

"Envirus," Denir replied to Sungmin's quiet shout with a bored expression. "He's such a wanderer, I don't know where he is now. That guy should be able to answer your question. He's a being whose causality is twisted in a different way from yours."

Envirus, Envirus... Sungmin committed the name to memory. Even searching through the memories of his past life, he didn't know of any being named Envirus.

"So. Shall we get to it now?" Denir asked. "What favor do you want to ask of me? Why did Mushii send you here?"

"...I... don't really know. Mushii said that if I defied death to return to the past, there must be a reason for it, and he told me to prepare to become a being worthy of that reason."

"Right. I see. So that's why that damn Mushii sent you to me," Denir said, looking Sungmin up and down. After a moment, Denir shook his head.

"But no."

"...Yes?"

"I said no. I cannot give you my 'trial'."

"Wait... a trial. What on earth do you mean? Are you saying you can give me a trial?"

"I can give you one, but you won't be able to handle it. You'll go mad... and die. This is advice I'm giving for your own good, so listen carefully."

"I endured the trials on Mount Mushii. I'm used to pain..."

"This is different from the pain you're familiar with," Denir said with a chuckle. "Mushii's penance, trials, and restrictions are actually quite kind, even though he's the god who oversees them. After all, the restrictions are something you choose for yourselves, and you can give up partway through, can't you?"

"But my trial is different. Unlike Mushii's, my trial has no guarantee that you will 'definitely' gain something. Because it's all up to you, and yet, it's terribly painful and tedious. So far, ten beings have met me and received my trial, and only three of them gained something through it."

"...And the rest?"

"They all died. Ah. The trial didn't kill them. They just gave up and killed themselves. Among the ten who challenged my trial, not a single one was less capable than you. They all had brilliant talent, passion, effort, grit, and tenacity, but even among them, only three overcame the trial."

Denir rose to his feet as he spoke.

"First, go cool your head a little. If you come back asking for the trial anytime... well, if you want to kill yourself, that is. Then I suppose I could give it to you."

"Cool my head..."

"I'm telling you to go get some sleep," Denir grumbled with a frown. "Ah, but you can't sleep here. This is my house."

In other words, he was telling him to get out.
