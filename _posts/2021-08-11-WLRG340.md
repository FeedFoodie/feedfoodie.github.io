Lee Sungmin rose, leaving behind the sleeping form of Baek Sogo, who had finally cried herself into exhaustion. His own emotions warred within him, a grating conflict of empathy and fear. He had listened to her tears, her words, her wails, and her sobs, and in them, he had felt the crushing weight of her despair.

*How small is a single human in this vast world.*

*How powerless is a single human against the fate that guides it.*

Even the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, a man of immense power, had not been able to escape his own death. Though he achieved enlightenment at the very end and transcended this world, he had only overcome his personal fate, not the fate of the world itself. The same was true for the Void Monarch, at least when he had lived as a Yokai. He too could not escape his own death, because it was so ordained. Because it had to be. That was the nature of fate.

A light, uncontrollable tremor ran through Lee Sungmin's body as he confronted this realization once more. He was faced with an outcome that could absolutely not be changed, an unalterable result.

*Everything I have done until now... no, not just me. Everything the people of this world have done has been for some purpose this world holds. When that purpose is fulfilled, the world will end, regardless of the will of those who lived here.*

It was only natural for Baek Sogo to fall into despair. The very conviction she held was the antithesis of such destruction. Not just her, but countless others who placed their hope in the future would surely despair just as she had.

However.

Even if a single human was a small existence, and even if they were powerless against the fate that guided the entire world, fate could still change. It *could* be changed. Lee Sungmin's very presence here was proof of that. Even if it was the Demonic Spirit's intention, one could still rebel against the great and absolute flow, creating countless small variables along the way.

If variable was added to variable, and if one's will to defy fate never broke, then it could be changed. Lee Sungmin wanted to believe that. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had changed his fate of death into one of escape. Lee Sungmin's own consciousness, which should have vanished when he awoke as the Slaughter Predator, had survived because variables had been added to variables. With the Demonic Spirit's help, he had turned the tables and annihilated the Slaughter Predator instead.

Fate could be changed.

*It... it must be changeable.* Otherwise, everything he had done until now, Abel's death, the Chairman's death, all of it would be meaningless.

"It can be changed," Lee Sungmin muttered to the soundly sleeping Baek Sogo. It had to be. So that the deaths of Abel and the Chairman would not be in vain. So that the actions of Wiji Hoyeon, who still wandered somewhere, would not be meaningless.

*First, I must cure Baek Sogo's poison.*

He began to consider his opponents. There were the Vampires, led by Jeniela. Her high-ranking followers were comparable to, if not stronger than, the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens. Then there was the Lycanthrope, Ju Won, and his trusted subordinate Nero, whom Lee Sungmin had met in Cheped. The man had not tried to fight him then, but if he had attacked with his life on the line, it would have been quite troublesome. Nero was that strong. How many more Lycanthropes of his caliber served under Ju Won?

Fortunately, after the death of Red Ghost, most of the Yokai from Predator had fallen under Yana's command. Strictly speaking, Yana had not taken them in. She had no intention of managing Urmuri beyond killing its former leader, but Urmuri had been the territory of Red Ghost, a Black Star of Predator, and by killing him, Yana had become its new master whether she wanted to or not. When she refused to join Predator, the Urmuri Yokai lost their place within the organization.

Even if the Yokai no longer belonged to Predator, the Death Knight and the Black Mages remained. With Vollander and Arbes annihilated and Kim Jonghyun banished from spacetime, their forces could no longer be considered on par with the Vampires or Lycanthropes, but being relatively weaker did not make them weak compared to humans. Even with Vollander dead, none of the Nine Sects One Gang could handle hundreds of Death Knights, with the sole exception of the Wudang Sect, which had the Sword Immortal and Cheongmyeong. The same was true of the Liches. The dark magic unleashed by tens, even hundreds of them, would be a formidable challenge, especially since the nature of their spells was unknown.

In comparison, Lee Sungmin had few allies. He knew very well that he could not do this alone. No matter how strong he had become, he was not confident he could handle Jeniela by himself. If he expanded the scope to include the countless in-humans she led, it would be absolutely impossible.

That was why he needed allies. Yana, who did not want the Void Monarch to die, did not wish for Lee Sungmin's death either. She would help him overcome dangers to ensure he survived. If he asked, Scarlet would also help him. What he wanted to do was stop The End. Among those living in this world, how many would refuse to cooperate in stopping such a conclusion?

*However... not the Martial God.*

The Martial God was a puppet of the Spirit, and the Spirit desired The End, but what about the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King? The problem was that he did not know where they were. If he could find them, he was certain he could receive their help.

However, treating Baek Sogo came first. He had to meet Teresa.

The next day, Lee Sungmin opened his eyes at dawn. He had already told Yana and Scarlet the previous night of his plan to meet the Saint. Yana had offered to go with him, but entering the Holy Palace with a Yokai had a high chance of causing problems, both big and small. She understood this and agreed not to accompany him this time. Scarlet decided to stay by Baek Sogo's side. For this kind of task, it was more convenient to move alone. Lee Sungmin began to leave.

"Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo's voice stopped him. It was not the voice of someone who had just woken up.

"When did you wake up? You should sleep more," he said.

Baek Sogo slowly sat up and gave a bitter smile. "...I was ashamed of my disgraceful behavior yesterday."

"It wasn't disgraceful."

"It felt that way to me... Junior Disciple. I'm sorry... and thank you."

"...It's nothing."

"...Your words that it can be changed, Junior Disciple. I... I'll believe them."

Lee Sungmin remained silent.

"...Thank you," she said again. Her eyes were bloodshot, likely from crying so much the night before.

Lee Sungmin offered her a faint smile in return. "Please wait for me."

The Holy Palace was located in Prabah, a city situated in the prosperous southern sea, past Devon where the Jaegal Clan resided, at the very edge of the South. Prabah was a city of faith. Religions from other dimensions that had settled in Eria had transformed to survive, and in their new forms, they no longer ostracized one another. Faiths from all sorts of dimensions blossomed in Prabah, united in harmony under the name of the Church. The Holy Palace was its symbol, home to the periodically elected Pope, numerous Holy Knights, Priests, and the Saint, Teresa.

Lee Sungmin had never been to Prabah, but he had plenty of experience visiting nearby cities like Devon and Germud. At his speed, he could reach his destination in less than a day.

Early that morning, Lee Sungmin leaped through space on his Fairy Horse. Perhaps because a little time had passed, the nausea and dizziness he had felt arriving in Germud were absent, though a sense of discomfort lingered. The interval between jumps had been too short. Before the feeling could fade, he unleashed his movement arts, his body blurring as he ran toward the distant location. The straight-line distance to Prabah, which he had learned from Nebul, guided him.

{sep}

The Holy Palace only became the Beggars' Gang on Sundays. Even then, it was not a certainty, and entry was forbidden outside of designated times. Only those who were qualified and truly desperate could receive blessings and purification from a high-ranking Priest.

The road leading to it was bustling with people. The Holy Palace was a holy site, a symbol of the Church. Almost no one in Prabah was without a religion. The fanatical made pilgrimages to the city and stood in line for a chance to enter the palace that became the Beggars' Gang once a week.

Thanks to running without rest, Lee Sungmin arrived a little after noon and stood at the end of the long line. The Holy Palace was a white castle, befitting its name. He glanced at the slightly open castle gate and estimated the distance. The number of people allowed inside was limited. Most would be unable to enter and would have to wait for the next week.

Of course, Lee Sungmin had no intention of doing that. He felt a pang of guilt for those waiting honestly, but his body vanished. He silently crossed over their heads without stirring the wind and slipped through a crack in the firmly shut palace gate.

TINGLE.

Something stimulated his senses.

*[I'm caught.]*

No matter how fast or stealthy his movements were, it was impossible to pass through a magic barrier undetected. It could not be helped. He knew there would be a barrier and that he could not avoid it. To some extent, this was part of his plan.

After passing the gate, he moved through several more detection spells, each one sending a tingling resistance against his senses. Lee Sungmin stopped in a secluded spot and waited quietly. Before long, a group of Holy Knights came rushing over. They wore simple uniforms instead of armor but held their weapons at the ready. Before they could demand who he was, Lee Sungmin spoke first.

"I am the Phantom Spear."

He did not hide his identity. Lee Sungmin had killed the Death Lord, Vollander. The Demon Lord, Kim Jonghyun, had also fallen to him. Strictly speaking, he had not been the one to kill Kim Jonghyun, but the rumor that started with the Church had been passed down that way for ten years. The Church could have easily hidden the truth, but they did not. They were honest. They had revealed everything that happened in Germud without concealment, and because of that, Lee Sungmin trusted them to some extent.

"I came to apologize for what happened ten years ago." And also, he thought, for a small favor. He did not bother to mention that part.

Agitation spread among the Holy Knights. Lee Sungmin waited without rushing, knowing it was best to resolve things smoothly if possible.

After a short while, one of the knights spoke to him in a cautious voice. "Theos will come himself."

Lee Sungmin recalled the name without difficulty. He was the captain of the Silver Knight Order, the man who had led the Holy Knights in Germud a decade ago. Lee Sungmin read the emotions in their gazes. Awe, mystery, and fear. To them, he was a figure known to have died ten years ago, a warrior who had defeated a Death Lord and a Demon Lord, and a tragic hero who had gone mad in the end.

"Lee Sungmin."

The group of Holy Knights parted to either side. Ten years had etched a few traces of time onto Theos, but it was only a few fine wrinkles at most. He approached with the same good-natured smile as before, and there was no fear in the hand he extended.

"It's been a long time."

"...Yes." Lee Sungmin shook his hand.

"I heard that you had woken up."

"Has the rumor already spread this far?"

"The warriors from the Murim Alliance who were harmed in Germud are staying in this city," Theos said, letting go of Lee Sungmin's hand. He slowly turned. "Please, come with me."

Theos led the way, and Lee Sungmin followed slowly behind him.

"An apology for what happened ten years ago, you say."

"Back then, I went mad. I heard that I caused a lot of trouble because of it."

"...Are you all right now?" Theos asked.

Lee Sungmin did not answer immediately.

"If you're all right... could I hear what happened back then?"

"Kim Jonghyun cast that kind of magic on me before he died. I couldn't avoid it. In the end, I was sealed by my senior sister, and I've only just woken up now, after ten years."

"Ah... I see." Theos did not seem to doubt his words.

Lee Sungmin spoke again, his voice even. "I would like to meet Teresa."

"For an apology?"

"And a small favor."

At those words, Theos tilted his head.
