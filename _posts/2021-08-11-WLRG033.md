You will come to fear falling asleep every single day.

It was just as Mushii had said. Lee Sungmin shot up with a scream, his sense of time a complete mess. How long had he slept? He felt as if he had slept for only a moment. The night was still dark, the moon still hanging in the sky, and though he couldn't recall its exact position from before, he could vaguely sense that little time had passed.

No.

Was that really true?

A tremor started in his shoulders, a violent, uncontrollable shaking born from a fear of the unknown. It was a terror Lee Sungmin had never felt before, a fear of something he could not identify or even begin to fathom. Gasping for breath, his hands flew to his cloak, clutching the fabric and wrapping it tightly around his body. The pressure was a brief comfort, a fleeting moment of relief.

He didn't want to think about what kind of dream he'd had. The very attempt dredged up a memory from his childhood, something he hadn't recalled in a very long time. To reach that memory, he had to travel back through the year he had lived in this life, then the thirteen years of his last, all the way to a time before Eria when his fighting spirit was nothing more than common and ordinary.

He had parents then. Of course he did, for no one is born without them. Lee Sungmin was human, after all. He didn't particularly want to remember them, good or bad. He had already resolved to part with those longed-for beings in his past life, or more precisely, he wanted to forget them entirely. It was a defense against the suffering that came with longing.

In any case, this memory was from long ago, during his childhood. Like all young children, Lee Sungmin had been afraid of Ghost Spirits. He feared the pitch-black darkness that came when the lights went out, and the smallest sound from within it would terrify him. He had tried not to be conscious of them, of the Ghost Spirits, because acknowledging them felt like an invitation for them to appear in his dreams.

It was the same now. He didn't want to recall the nightmare he'd had.

However, his refusal did not prevent its return. What Mushii had said was true. Sleep was no longer a comfortable rest for Lee Sungmin, and the restriction on his dreams was even harsher than the one on his sense of taste.

However, humans must sleep. They have no choice.

He lost count of how many times he woke up from his dreams. Each time he woke screaming, his body was drenched in a cold sweat and his mind a wreck. The harsh mountain cold evaporated the sweat, delivering a brutal chill that shook him to the bone. Lee Sungmin would scramble to add more branches to the campfire, coaxing the flames higher before pulling his cloak tight and crouching before the fire.

And fell asleep again

Woke up

Fell asleep

Woke up

The sun rose.

The nightmares were terrible. The disjointed visions lacked any narrative, constantly shifting shape like light in a kaleidoscope. He had endured this for just one day. No, half a day. Was it even shorter than that? Lee Sungmin looked at the sky with half-lidded, sleep-deprived eyes.

It was still dawn. He couldn't sleep anymore. He didn't want to sleep. Staggering to his feet, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear.

[You crazy bastard.]

Baek Sogo arrived not long after the sun rose. One look at his pale, drained face and tightly sealed lips was all she needed to guess what kind of madness he had undertaken.

[How many more restrictions did you receive?] she wrote in the dirt with a branch.

Lee Sungmin lowered his spear and stared blankly at her face. She sighed deeply and handed the branch to him.

"……"

He remained silent. Baek Sogo pointed to the words she had written first.

[You crazy bastard.]

She then wrote again.

[What are the restrictions? One seems to be your voice.]

[Dreams.]

Lee Sungmin finally wrote his reply. It was his first time communicating this way, and the unfamiliarity of it forced his words to be short.

Dreams… dreams. Baek Sogo mouthed the word, repeating it. She had received three restrictions herself. Her left arm, her voice, and Thought Transmission. The targets of those three were very clear. The restriction on her arm meant she couldn't use it. The restriction on her voice meant she couldn't make a sound. The restriction on Thought Transmission meant she couldn't use it.

It was the same for the others on the mountain. The two restrictions Scarlet had, and the two Dok Bijun had, were just as direct, but Lee Sungmin's restrictions? Heaviness, dreams, taste. These felt more abstract than related to specific actions. Such restrictions were known to be extremely harsh.

[Did you do it on purpose?] Baek Sogo wrote. [The stronger the restriction, the sweeter the reward at the end of the ordeal. …Why are you in such a hurry?]

[Because I have no talent.] Lee Sungmin wrote in response.

No talent.

The words on the ground silenced Baek Sogo. She was in a position where she could only be silent, unable to use her voice or Thought Transmission, but Lee Sungmin's answer had overwhelmed her so completely that she couldn't have written another word even if she had wanted to.

No talent. It was a concept that had nothing to do with her. Even before being summoned to this world, Eria, Baek Sogo had known she possessed abundant talent. Her only experience with Despair had come after arriving here, entering Mount Mushii, and meeting the true calamity named Flam, but that was only because Flam was a once-in-a-generation monster, and by comparison, the light of her own talent had merely dimmed.

[Talent.]

Baek Sogo barely managed to write the word. Had she ever keenly felt a lack of it? Yes, she had. When she first met Flam, she had felt a Despair that made her own talent seem like nothing. It wasn't just her. Dok Bijun and Scarlet had felt it too. Everyone had been overwhelmed by Flam's talent.

If people like them, all individuals of outstanding talent, were overwhelmed by Flam's true nature as a calamity, just how much Despair must Lee Sungmin, who claimed to have none, have felt? She couldn't begin to imagine it.

Baek Sogo had been watching Lee Sungmin ever since he came to the mountain, an observation that began with simple curiosity. Since a boy the same age as Flam had come for training, she had mistaken him for another calamity with great talent.

However, that wasn't the case. Lee Sungmin's skills were outstanding for his age, but he was far from a calamity. Stranger still, even though he clearly possessed skills superior to his peers, the pace of his advancement was incredibly slow. She had sensed a disharmony between his apparent talent and his visible skills, but now she understood. His words, claiming he had no talent, were sincere. The look on his face as he wrote them was steeped in self-loathing.

However, the light in his eyes hadn't died.

[It seems your goal is set very high.] Baek Sogo wrote.

Lee Sungmin looked surprised. He had never told her about the calamity named Wiji Hoyeon, the goal he had set for himself.

[Don't be surprised. I can tell by looking at your eyes. Everyone has a goal set in their heart. It's just that those who truly strive to reach that goal are rare.]

Baek Sogo paused the branch for a moment, a contemplative expression on her face, before she moved it again.

[My goal was to be the best in the murim. After coming to this world, I could no longer aim for that, but my goal changed instead. To become the world number one.]

After writing, she handed the branch to Lee Sungmin. He took it from her and tilted his head, unable to figure out what kind of answer she was expecting.

While he hesitated, Baek Sogo's expression kept changing. After a long deliberation, she nodded, her decision made. Her hand reached into her robes.

What she finally took out was an old book, which she held out to him.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

Lee Sungmin stared at her, his expression one of pure surprise. Having finished her deliberation, Baek Sogo's face was impassive. He tried to write something, but she didn't let him. The book was thrust forward until it touched his chest.

[You mentioned it last time.] Baek Sogo snatched the branch from him and wrote on the ground. [That you wanted me to teach you movement arts.]

[Dancing Shadow Soul Break. That is the entirety of the footwork I have learned. My movement arts. All of them. They are all the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. From now on, you learn it.]

[Why give it to me?] Lee Sungmin took the branch back and wrote.

He had indeed asked her to teach him, but that was just him testing the waters. He never thought she would actually do it.

[Pity.] Baek Sogo wrote.

Lee Sungmin's expression stiffened.

[I'm not talentless like you. So I don't know how great the Despair you feel is, nor do I know how harsh the journey to your goal will be. My giving you the Dancing Shadow Soul Break is a form of pity, a desire to help even though I don't understand you.]

Baek Sogo was honest. Pity. So it was pity. Lee Sungmin mouthed a laugh.

[And because I'm a good person.] she added, perhaps embarrassed to leave it at that.

Lee Sungmin didn't feel particularly offended by her pity. On the contrary, if it was this kind of pity, he welcomed it.

[Should I call you Master?] he asked.

Baek Sogo shook her head.

[I'm not the creator of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, nor am I its grandmaster. What Master? Just call me Senior Sister.]

To think he would become the Junior Disciple of the Silent Flash, Baek Sogo. This life, having returned to the past, was definitely flowing in a different direction from his previous one.

Lee Sungmin read the secret manual for the Dancing Shadow Soul Break right then and there. His insight was shallow and his talent was nonexistent, making it difficult to ascertain the martial art's true value, but even so, he could tell that it was an incredible technique.

*It's so good it wouldn't be out of place compared to the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear or the Violet Dawn Divine Art.*

Lee Sungmin swallowed dryly. In his past life, he'd had no connection to things like a miraculous encounter. The only thing that could be called that was obtaining the Stone of Reincarnation just before he died.

However, what about this life? He had been taught the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear by Wiji Hoyeon, and now he had been taught the Dancing Shadow Soul Break by Baek Sogo.

*The problem is me.*

Will you learn the Dancing Shadow Soul Break?

Yes.

Lee Sungmin closed the book.

*Nine years left. No…*

Eight years.
