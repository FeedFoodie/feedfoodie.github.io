A vibration shook the entire space, freezing Preskan in his tracks. He had only a moment to tilt his head in wonder before—

KWAARRRRR!

A massive violet storm tore past his right side.

Preskan threw up a barrier to shield himself, but the storm shattered it instantly, sending him tumbling from the force of the impact.

"Wha, what...?!"

Scrambling to his feet, Preskan stared at the path the storm had carved. Nothing remained. He froze, his non-existent jaw feeling as if it had dropped to the ground.

He forced his gaze toward the direction of the initial vibration, toward the origin of that terrifying power.

His body trembled as he muttered, "Some rat...!"

However, this was no mere rat. No ordinary being could unleash such power.

For a moment, Preskan debated whether he should go at all, but in truth, he had no choice. Even an opponent wielding such a devastating attack could not completely annihilate him so long as his Life Force Vessel existed.

Arbes, however, was different.

His master's powerful Dark Magic could annihilate the 'soul' itself, regardless of any vessel. It was a fatal power to all beings, but to a Lich who relied on a half-baked form of immortality, it was an attack akin to Despair. That was precisely why Preskan could not refuse Arbes's command. He had become a Lich to fulfill a long-cherished wish, and he could not allow himself to be annihilated before achieving it.

It was better to lose his Lich body here than to be utterly destroyed. With the grim feeling of a man forced to swallow a bitter pill, Preskan advanced toward the source of the attack.

{sep}

Wiping out the ghouls in one fell swoop had not been the end of it. Their numbers seemed endless, for every Yokai and human who had lived in this area had been turned into one. It was a cruel fate for those who had only come out to enjoy the night market, never imagining they would die and be reborn as horrifying undead monsters.

"It's a pity," Envirus muttered, raising his wand, "but ghouls are already dead. Mourning won't save them. The only way forward is to kill them all."

A brilliant light erupted from the wand's tip, illuminating their surroundings and forcing the ghouls back. The creatures could not approach him, for every time the light touched a ghoul's body, it turned the monster to ash.

While Envirus dealt with the ghouls, Lee Sungmin's thoughts returned to the Nine Dragons Slaughter. The power of the technique was by far the greatest he had ever wielded, but it was utterly uncontrollable. Even for him, a man who had nearly perfected the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, it was impossible to command completely. He had even tried to incorporate the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art and failed.

*What would have happened if I'd succeeded?* The thought made him swallow hard.

Envirus was well aware of Lee Sungmin's state. The power the young man had just unleashed defied reason. It was a power that should not exist. A flicker of regret passed through the mage for granting Lee Sungmin's request. He was already wielding such immense force, and now the power of the Dragon Heart was to be added to it? Envirus swallowed a rising sigh.

"...Hm?"

Envirus's eyebrow twitched as he sensed a new presence approaching, one that was not a ghoul. At the same time, the magic enveloping the barrier fluctuated. He turned to Lee Sungmin, his expression stiff.

"Someone's coming. Judging by the magic, it's a Lich."

"The mastermind behind this?"

"I doubt it. The immense magic I feel at the center hasn't moved."

With a wide swing of his wand, Envirus unleashed a wave of light that turned the remaining ghouls to ash.

"...It seems we can't afford to waste any more time. I need you to deal with the approaching Lich. Can you handle it?"

"And you?"

"I have to get to the square. The magic is nearing completion. It still needs time to manifest, but something unforeseen might happen."

"I understand."

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. With that confirmation, Envirus's body floated into the air and shot away from Lee Sungmin at an incredible speed.

The sudden approach of a new presence startled the reluctantly advancing Preskan, who immediately began preparing a spell, but before he could even cast it, Envirus flashed past him.

"No!" Preskan shouted.

If he let the mage reach Arbes, his punishment would be severe, but despite his desperate cry, Envirus did not stop, and Preskan knew with sickening certainty that he could never match that speed.

He trembled with a mixture of anger and despair, then noticed another human still standing in the distance.

*I have no choice. I have to do something about at least one of them.*

A sliver of relief even crept in. Though he had only seen him for an instant, that mage was clearly far superior to him. Perhaps that terrifying attack from before had been the mage's work. If so, the remaining one would surely be insignificant. While he had his Life Force Vessel, the destruction of his Lich body would still cause various losses, an outcome he wished to avoid.

As he drew closer, a sense of familiarity pricked at him.

*...Something feels familiar...*

Where had he felt this before? Something he should never forget... The thought died in his mind.

The Black Mist that constituted Preskan's body churned violently. Having abandoned his flesh for a body of soul, Preskan realized with a wave of despair just how complacent he had been.

*What in the world is that.*

He stood frozen, staring at it.

'It' was a giant silhouette, a swaying shadow behind some unknown curtain.

It was a churning death mask and the form of an indescribable monster all at once.

'It' had no physical body.

Preskan, himself a spiritual being, was witnessing the raw form of a soul emanating from a single human.

Overwhelmed by a primal fear, he unknowingly took a step back.

The Demon Lord from another dimension, the very being with whom he had contracted for his power, The Fear that Drenches the Abyss... even the terror that incarnation imparted felt lacking compared to what he felt now.

"...Gasp!"

Preskan sucked in a breath he did not need. He had only witnessed it for a fleeting moment, but to him, it felt like an eternity.

If he still had a human body, he would be drenched in a cold sweat, or perhaps he would have lost control of his bowels without a shred of shame.

He panted, shaking his head.

*It's a simple illusion. How could a being that inspires more fear than the Demon King of Fear possibly exist in this world? It is illogical. Unreal.*

He forced himself to accept this, believing his spiritual body was merely in turmoil from extreme stress. Suppressing the overwhelming feeling that he should not go there, he forced his feet to move.

{sep}

The ghouls were gone, annihilated by the light Envirus had unleashed. Lee Sungmin looked down at the spear in his hands. The perfect combination of the Violet Dawn Divine Art and Demonic Energy, amplified by the resonance of the Orichalcum, had produced an unexpected power that was extremely satisfying. He could not yet handle it perfectly, but he knew that with practice, he would wield it without faltering. It had always been this way, with the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the Violet Dawn Divine Art, and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. They were difficult at first, but once he grew accustomed to them, he had used them well.

*He's coming.*

He felt a presence approaching. Lee Sungmin moved toward it, then stopped. He realized it was a familiar presence, one he knew. His eyes widened as he looked in that direction.

"Preskan?"

He recognized the owner of the presence. He never thought he would meet Preskan here.

The approaching presence was undoubtedly Preskan, who recognized Lee Sungmin a moment later. He had no choice but to recognize him. Preskan was already forming hand signs for an attack, intending to press him from the start, and Lee Sungmin knew he would.

So he ran, closing the distance in an instant.

Preskan's eyes widened in shock at the sight of Lee Sungmin rushing toward him.

"Heart Thief!" he shouted, completing the hand signs.

PZZZZZT!

Black currents of electricity sparked to life around Preskan and surged toward Lee Sungmin. Without hesitation, Lee Sungmin met the attack with a forward thrust of his spear.

Magic was unfamiliar and threatening, but his armor and spear were both forged from Orichalcum, a metal with high magic resistance. Combined with his Protective Qi Aura, he could ignore most spells. Of course, Preskan's magic was no ordinary spell. The inner arts flowing into his outstretched spear fluctuated. Not yet used to wielding inner arts and Demonic Energy simultaneously in such a pressing situation, Lee Sungmin drew only upon the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

The arts coiled around his spear, gathering at its tip as he unleashed the sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Void Path.

KWAARRRRR!

The electric currents fired by Preskan were caught in the technique's rotation and scattered in all directions, carving a straight path between the two combatants.

*My god!*

Preskan was horrified. It had only been a few months, but in that short time, the damned Heart Thief had become incomparably stronger.

He hurriedly formed hand signs for a defensive barrier. The exact moment Preskan's barrier erected was the same moment Lee Sungmin pierced through the path created by his own technique and thrust his spear. Deathly Flash, the fastest thrust of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, shot out.

KWAAAANG!

Hidden inside the translucent sphere, Preskan mistakenly thought the world was shaking. In reality, he had just been launched into the air, still encased in the sphere that had collided with the spear.

Lee Sungmin pursued him, unfolding Homing Chain Lightning and Bloody Pursuit simultaneously.

KWAKWAKWANG!

The successive spear strikes shattered Preskan's barrier.

"Keuheuk!"

To suffer such humiliation! The blue lights that served as Preskan's eyes flared with rage. He chanted an incantation while forming hand signs, and his figure vanished completely from Lee Sungmin's line of attack.

It was Blink. Though he was no Arbes or Envirus, Preskan was still a high-level Black Mage capable of such a feat.

"I'll take back what you stole from me!" Preskan shouted, now far behind Lee Sungmin.

His lips moved as he wove a series of hand signs, casting not an attack, but a curse-type Dark Magic.

Lee Sungmin's legs grew heavy, as if held by unseen hands, and his vision clouded.

It was a temporary curse, but it was enough to buy a moment. Preskan used it to create layers of barriers between them, his robes swelling dramatically as he prepared his next assault.

"Burst and die!" he roared, a writhing black darkness exploding from his chest.

The curse-laden shout was like a command. Lee Sungmin watched the darkness encroach, but the moment Preskan had bought for himself was a moment for Lee Sungmin as well.

While the Lich wove his spells, Lee Sungmin focused his mind and consciousness, synchronizing his breathing with the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The same terrifying power from before once again coiled around his spear.

This time, he did not use the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Instead, he unleashed a martial art faithful to handling such immense power, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art he had learned from the Crazy Heavenly Demon. He chose its third form, Flesh Devouring Blood Fang.

The spear became a conduit, an extension of his arms to unleash the enhanced qi.

The awl-sharp energy collided with the darkness Preskan had unleashed.

No, it could not even be called a collision. It was more accurate to call it a one-sided trampling.
