The Spear King's thrust found its mark, his weapon embedding itself deep in the Queen's abdomen. She made no attempt to evade, taking the spear with her bare body. Her eyes flew open as she grabbed the shaft with one hand, her other hand reaching for the Spear King, yet, just as before, her attack failed to activate.

There was a limit to what she could do alone. Without the strength to wipe out multiple enemies at once, she had no choice but to respond to each assault as it came. A scowl twisted her features as she perceived the Black Dragon Knight's fist driving in from her side. She let go of the spear and contorted her body, but the knight's thrusting fist opened into a vicious claw.

PPUDDEUK!

The attack tore deeply into the Queen, threatening to split her in two. She staggered back spewing blood, just as a violet current engulfed her.

KKWAJIJIK!

With a sound like a thunderclap, her body shattered into pieces, but the Queen did not die.

KIIING!

A milky-white barrier deployed around her. Inside it, her wounds regenerated at a rapid pace, her brows twitching with irritation. This was immediately after her Advent, and she was far too lacking in power. If this were the Elemental Realm, she was confident she could kill them all without much difficulty, but not now. Though each of them was weaker than Wiji Hoyeon, their numbers more than made up for the deficit. For the Queen, who was nigh-immortal according to martial arts theory, enduring the ceaseless barrage with her bare body was both irritating and troublesome.

The problem was not the pain, but what would come after. *What if I can't hold on and I'm reverse-summoned?* A forced reverse-summoning would inflict enough damage that even she would need over a hundred years to stabilize her existence. That could not be allowed to happen. Even if she failed to destroy the world, she desperately wanted to kill the human standing before her.

If only that human did not exist. If he and Envirus had never met, none of this would have happened.

*There is no need to rush.*

Her body was far from death, and time was on her side. Her irritation would surely build, but the longer the fight dragged on, the more power she would gain. Even at this very moment, Spirit Queen Sarahenne was drawing power, having made her Advent directly into this world. The forest's barrier currently blocked the Elementals from entering, but that would cease to be a problem if she simply stepped outside of it.

[If we fight too violently here, the one maintaining the forest's barrier might be harmed,] the Void Monarch's warning echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind. It was a risk he was already considering.

[If she goes outside the barrier, the power of the Elementals will be added to hers,] Rubia advised next. The barrier of the Sleeping Forest was suppressing the Queen's power by blocking the Elementals' entry. It was impossible to gauge how much stronger she would become if she left its confines and began to receive their aid.

*As quickly as possible.* The thought solidified into a grim certainty. Lee Sungmin knew well that time was not on his side. He had to end this in a swift, decisive battle before the forest was destroyed.

The Queen decided to aim for the most vulnerable targets first. The two mages below were not leading the battle, but their constant interference grated on her nerves. Aside from their initial attack, they had only been disrupting her movements rather than launching direct assaults. Still, creatures known as mages were best cut down the moment an opportunity arose. They deployed magic that was difficult to imagine and even harder to counter. In the Queen's judgment, now was the time to eliminate them.

A spear shot forward, striking her barrier and shattering it instantly. The Queen became a great wind, paying no mind to the gash in her torso from the Spear King's earlier attack. The wind plummeted downward, a sharp tempest seeking to engulf Scarlet and Lloyd.

Unlike Lee Sungmin, the Spear King, and the Black Dragon Knight, who were all actively engaged in the attack, Baek Sogo had not moved. In this fight, everyone had a role. Scarlet and Lloyd were in charge of long-range support and Sealing, while Lee Sungmin, the Black Dragon Knight, and the Spear King worked to corner the Queen. The role assigned to Baek Sogo was to protect the two mages.

From her position near Scarlet and Lloyd, the silent Baek Sogo finally lifted her foot. Lee Sungmin watched her movement closely, curious to witness the power of the woman known as the Silent Flash and see what kind of martial arts she would display. The very movement art that had helped him survive until now, the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, was a technique she had taught him directly.

In truth, the version of the art Lee Sungmin now used was very different from the original. Much like his Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, it was filled with the profound insights of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. The mastery Baek Sogo had reached, while impressive, was bound to be shallow in comparison. If one judged solely by the level of the martial art, Lee Sungmin's version was likely superior.

However, Baek Sogo had only the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Unlike Lee Sungmin, who had learned a multitude of other arts, she had trained exclusively in that single technique, both on Mount Mushii and in Denil's Mental World. The only thing she had ever delved into was the Dancing Shadow Soul Break.

Her extended foot touched neither the ground nor the sky. With that single motion forward, Baek Sogo was already far ahead. Her feet leaped through space, her body piercing the wind. The Queen, reaching out from within the tempest, was stunned to find Baek Sogo suddenly right beside her.

"Two Step Calamity," Baek Sogo muttered, her lips parting slightly as her right foot stepped on the air.

PWAK!

The tempest scattered. The Queen stared with wide eyes, surprised by a divine presence she had not even been aware of.

"I guess I looked like an easy target," Scarlet muttered, her expression one of pure irritation. She glanced at Lloyd, who gave a slight nod in return. As the one leading the Sealing, he could not actively step forward, but Scarlet was different. Her gloved hand swept through the air, and without needing to be written, runic characters materialized in the empty space, arranging themselves into a sentence.

The runes began to glow. Having repelled the wind with her technique, Baek Sogo kicked at the Queen's body. Just before the blow landed, the Queen raised a wall of earth to block it, but that alone was not enough. She condensed her magic power, creating a second barrier. A tiny dot of light from Scarlet's spell touched that barrier and destroyed it. The erupting magic power became hundreds of awls.

As Scarlet clenched her outstretched hand, the awls descended upon the Queen all at once. There was no time to rest as attack after attack came without pause.

PEOBEOBEOK!

Hundreds of awls tore the Queen's body to shreds. Before she could finish regenerating, the embedded projectiles exploded.

"Ah... ugh...!"

For the first time, a groan escaped the Queen's lips. It was not a simple explosion. The reversed magic power coursing through her slowed her body's regeneration. Though it seemed to have been cast lightly, the spell had reached the realm of grand magic. Amidst the crackling current, the Queen could not reform her physical body.

Lee Sungmin seized the opening. He gripped his heavy spear with both hands and pierced the Queen as she flailed within the current. Her physical body had not yet formed, but she was definitely there. To a transcendent being, the physical body was a mere vessel. Even if the vessel was destroyed, the spiritual body contained within did not scatter.

Creation burst forth. A tiny light gathered at a single point and then exploded.

AAAAARGH!

The Queen screamed. She did not care if her physical body died over and over, but an injury to her spiritual body was unacceptable.

"How? You, a mere human...!"

Her eyes rolled back in fury as the pain made her rampage. He was a low-class human. It should have been impossible for his attack to damage her spiritual body. A strike like that should have passed right through without ever touching her.

*He reached the realm of transcendence?*

It was unbelievable. Such a level of mastery was not permitted for humans of this world. As a transcendent being herself, the Queen knew that better than anyone. She groaned, grabbing the spear that pierced her spiritual body with both hands as the punctured essence began to stick back together. A transcendent being did not die from something like this. To kill one, their spiritual body had to be completely annihilated.

*What is the Spirit doing?* she thought frantically, seeking the world's guardian. It was forbidden for a mortal to reach the realm of transcendence. If one attained such mastery, that excessive power should be stripped away, or they should be exiled from the world.

*He reached it.*

However, it was shallow. The spear, containing the martial arts theory of Creation, had pierced her spiritual body but had failed to annihilate it. After pulling the spear out completely, the Queen staggered back as a physical form once again covered her spiritual one. She glared at Lee Sungmin with horrified eyes.

*Maybe the pinnacle of martial arts will work.* A sliver of confidence grew in Lee Sungmin. The Queen was not as strong as he had feared. Of course, he would have struggled immensely if he were alone, but he was not alone now.

"Aaaaaah!" the Queen roared.

KUUUUUNG!

An immense power flowed into her body, making space itself tremble and shake. She advanced without stopping her screams, planting her foot to kick off the ground, but Baek Sogo was faster. The ultimate Dancing Shadow Soul Break was both silent and swift. Invading from beyond the Queen's senses, Baek Sogo kicked her body, sending it flying through the air.

Waiting there was the Black Dragon Knight, his arms covered in jet-black scales. While airborne, the Queen swung both hands toward him, creating flames in the space between them. The knight, however, easily tore through the wall of fire with his scaled arms and closed the distance.

The Queen's torn body fell, a new physical form already forming over her spiritual one. In that moment, Scarlet's magic engulfed her.

CHWARARARAK!

Chains shot out from empty space, wrapping around the Queen's neck and tightening.

"Keuheuk...!"

She struggled, grabbing the chains with her regenerated hands. It was a terrible experience. Her enemies were weaker than Wiji Hoyeon, yet this fight was more one-sided and brutal. It could not be helped. She was far too weak here, her current power even less than Jeniela's.

Lee Sungmin considered it a relief. If they had not detected her Advent in advance with Gries, this would have been much more difficult. The Queen glared at him with bloodshot eyes.

PPADEUK!

The chains tightening around her neck shattered in her grasp.

"If only... you didn't exist..."

"Would you please stop?" Lee Sungmin could not stand it anymore and retorted at her venom-filled words. "What did I even do wrong? I never did anything to Envirus. In fact, he was the one who misunderstood and betrayed me. I never even forced him to break his Mana oath, so why in the world are you blaming me?"

"It would have been fine if you just didn't exist...!" the Queen shrieked, spraying blood.

Lee Sungmin gave up on talking to her. He had no confidence in persuading her, nor was there any need to. As he sighed, the Queen screamed viciously again.

"If only you didn't exist!"

He listened to her curse indifferently and raised his spear against her charge.

The forest shook.

The Queen looked down at the spear embedded in her chest. Lee Sungmin felt a slight dizziness and forced his eyes to focus. Her lips moved as if to say something, but she could not produce a sound. The pinnacle of martial arts that pierced her chest had created a huge hole in her spiritual body. The Queen's face contorted.

"Not... yet..."

With this much damage, she could not finish her words. Unlike Creation, the pinnacle of martial arts had completely annihilated a part of her spiritual body.

*It's shallow...*

Lee Sungmin's eyes saw more than just her physical form. The wound from his attack had pierced the center of her massive spiritual body and was by no means small. However, it was not enough to completely annihilate the transcendent Queen. She grabbed the spear with trembling hands and glared at him with eyes full of venom. Lee Sungmin did not try to understand what she was thinking. He only felt disappointed that even this was not enough.

He pulled out the spear. He could either get Lloyd's help to Seal her now or try to annihilate her by attacking her spiritual body further.

There was no need to choose.

The Queen's body began to tremble violently, her mouth falling wide open.

"Uh..."

A strange sound escaped her lips. She looked down at her own body with an expression of disbelief. It was not because of the hole in her chest. The annihilation had begun from the very edges of her spiritual body. The Queen flailed as she watched her own essence dissipate.

"Uh... why? Why me? W-wait. What's happening... Stop...!" she screamed.

Lee Sungmin looked at Lloyd with surprised eyes. Lloyd was also clearly flustered. He glanced back and forth between the Gries in his hand and the disintegrating Queen, shaking his head.

"I-it wasn't me. I didn't do anything."

"Then who...?"

The Queen's annihilation was not caused by the pinnacle of martial arts. That attack had only destroyed a part of her spiritual body, not the whole.

"AAAAARGH!"

The Queen screamed, rolling on the ground.

"N... no...!"

Her scream was mixed with pure terror.

"Why me... something like this...! Spirit... I...!"

Her cries were cut off intermittently. Soon, she could no longer even writhe. Her physical body, sprawled on the ground, crumbled into dust. With her spiritual body annihilated, the vessel could no longer exist.

"Envi... Cain..."

With her half-remaining head, the Queen muttered those words.

"......"

The Queen could speak no more.
