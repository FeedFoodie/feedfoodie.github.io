"Ah."

A dazed sound, completely unlike him, escaped Kim Jonghyun's lips. He had not anticipated this at all.

KIIIIING.

A thin, white line suddenly coiled around the space he occupied, halting his flight through the sky. He had not wanted to stop. He had been forced to. The white line was weaving itself into a cubic barrier that would not allow him to move.

*What is this?*

He hastily tried to cast magic, a desperate attempt to escape the unknown barrier, but he failed. The simple act of casting Blink was impossible. His body began a slow, inexorable plummet as the white lines of the barrier grew more distinct. He quickly uttered a command word, but the silence that followed confirmed its futility. It had no effect.

*This is...*

A tremor ran through Kim Jonghyun's eyes. *Surely not.* He swept his hands through the air, a rapidly recited chant allowing him to discern the nature of the light enveloping him. A look of pure disbelief spread across his face when he confirmed its identity.

*No, really?* Just in case, he cast the discernment magic all over again.

The result was the same. In the end, all he could do was burst into loud, unrestrained laughter.

"Ha, hahaha! Ahahaha!"

Who could have done this? The question was rhetorical. Only one person here was capable of such absurd magic. Abel, the Mages Guild Master. A wide-range search spell located him far below, kneeling on the ground, his life force a faint, flickering ember. He would stop breathing soon. It seemed he had overexerted himself considerably.

"Impressive. Truly... impressive. I never imagined there was a method like this."

KIIIIIIING...!

The cubic light intensified around him.

Even as he plummeted from the vast sky, Kim Jonghyun was genuinely impressed. Abel had cast this by scraping together the very last of his lifespan, a magic that made escape impossible, no matter what Kim Jonghyun tried. All he could do now was wait blankly for the spell to fully manifest. Once cast, a target caught within the magic could not escape on their own. The spell would activate soon. He watched its progression, a fleeting hope of discerning the coordinates crossing his mind. A shame, but there was nothing to be done. *Maybe it will be more enjoyable not to know.*

*Is this the end?*

Abel's magic would banish him outside of space-time itself. The very space Kim Jonghyun occupied had already been captured. Once the coordinates were set, he, along with the cube encasing him, would be torn from this time and space. He would be banished from the dimension of Eria to a completely different world, with no way of knowing if it would be the past, present, or future.

*Should I consider myself lucky that my life will be spared?* Yes, as long as he was alive, he could do anything. The thought of being banished to an unknown place even sparked a flicker of excitement within him.

Still, it was not enough. He had not accomplished enough in this world to be banished from it just yet. Others might disagree, but Kim Jonghyun was not yet satisfied. There was no time. *What can I do right now?*

*Ah.*

Then, as he fell, his eyes caught sight of Lee Sungmin. He watched the man slowly tear his way out of the oppressive magic he had so carefully constructed, and a genuine sense of admiration bloomed within him.

*Having studied magic my entire life, I know little about martial arts. If something like that is possible, perhaps I should take the time to learn it later.* Of course, that assumed martial arts even existed in the world he was being banished to.

"......"

His fingertip pointed toward Lee Sungmin. *It was because I met you that I became what I am. You taught me of past lives, of a reality beyond what my 'present' self perceived. You showed me that my past self was an insignificant being. Yes, you were the beginning of who I am now.*

*It can only be you.*

*If the beginning of the man named Kim Jonghyun in this world was our meeting, then you must be the one to mark his end. Dying by your hand wouldn't have been so bad. Still... since it has come to this, I have no choice but to engrave my end upon you in a different way.*

He could not be certain it would work. His preparations were woefully inadequate, as he had never planned for 'this' in the first place. Fortunately, he had more than enough to use as a sacrifice. The souls from Germud were gone, but he still had those he had acquired from the Holy Knights and the crowds.

*It's not like I'm reversing him into a Demon Lord.*

That would require far greater sacrifices and ritual preparations. No, what he intended now was something else. Something other than human. *Yes, it doesn't matter what he becomes.* He wanted to bestow upon Lee Sungmin an imprint of reversal that could 'never' be erased. He did not know what kind of reversal it would be, but a being that had been reversed once could never be reversed again.

A faint smile touched Kim Jonghyun's lips, pleased with the idea. By engraving an indelible reversal imprint upon Lee Sungmin, he would be remembered by him forever.

*It's a bit of a shame, though.*

His fingertip remained aimed at Lee Sungmin.

KIIING.

A jet-black light gathered there. Escaping this space was impossible, but fortunately for him, casting magic outside of it was not.

And for Lee Sungmin, it was a misfortune.

PAAAAT!

Kim Jonghyun's body vanished. He and the cubic space that enveloped him were banished from the time and space of Eria, but the Reversal Magic he cast just before disappearing struck Lee Sungmin squarely.

A thin beam of jet-black light pierced through Lee Sungmin's body.

"Uh."

The sound escaped Lee Sungmin's lips. His heart, which had been pounding madly, simply stopped. The throbbing headache vanished in an instant, and the Demonic Energy that had raged within his body fell silent. His flailing body froze completely in mid-air before he began to fall.

[What? Hey, you! You bastard!]

The Void Monarch roared in panic inside his head, but even he did not know what had just happened. Neither did Lee Sungmin. His consciousness faded in an instant. *What?* In his receding mental world, he felt a chilling premonition as something stirred in its far reaches.

CRACK, CRACKLE.

A cracking sound echoed from deep within his consciousness. He did not need anyone to explain it. Lee Sungmin knew instinctively what it was.

The Sealing. The Sealing the Fairy Queen, Osla, had engraved for him was breaking. *Why? No.* He desperately tried to cling to his consciousness.

This was absurd. He did not know why this was happening. Had he overused his Demonic Energy? Had the repeated regeneration of his body caused it to break? No, that could not be it. There had been no major danger right before he lost consciousness. He knew it had become more unstable, but for the Sealing to shatter so suddenly, in a single instant?

Why?

[What... is this? Why all of a sudden? Dammit!]

The Void Monarch roared inside Lee Sungmin's head. The Sealing shattered, and the yokai nature crawled out to take root in his empty consciousness.

However, that was not all.

Lee Sungmin's body began to writhe. Muscles bulged and contracted while his skin tore and regenerated. Veins popped across his entire body. His fingers twisted, nails growing long and sharp as a deep-seated ferocity bloomed in the depths of his dull golden eyes.

Kim Jonghyun had not intended for this to happen. All he had wanted was to engrave an imprint of reversal upon Lee Sungmin that could never be erased or undone before he vanished completely from this world. It did not matter what he became. A vampire, an Elf, an orc, a beastfolk... the reversal technique was meant to change a being into a species completely different from their original one.

However, Lee Sungmin was becoming a Yokai. He had no choice but to become a Yokai. Lee Sungmin was human, yet he was not. His body itself was close to that of a Yokai, and the humanity he had barely maintained was being reversed by the absolute magic of the Grimoire, a power that could not be resisted. It did not matter how much Lee Sungmin believed in himself, or how much he wished to never abandon his humanity and not become a Yokai.

[Your mind...]

The Void Monarch's voice grew distant.

Lee Sungmin's consciousness was plunged into the depths. This was different from what happened in Urmuri. Back then, even when the yokai nature had seized control of his body, he had been able to suppress it from deep within his consciousness and reclaim control.

That was not the case now. The Reversal Magic had definitively transformed Lee Sungmin into a complete Yokai.

{sep}

Abel did not know what had transpired. He knelt on the ground, slowly letting go of his fading thread of consciousness.

"I succeeded," he muttered in a small voice. The magic he had cast by scraping together all his remaining magic power and lifespan had completely banished the Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun from the time and space of Eria.

Yes. That was enough. He had not been able to save the world from the fate of The End, but he had removed its first calamity, the one who sought to connect their world to the Great Demon World and turn it into a terrible hell.

*That's enough. This is the end for me.* A chuckle escaped him.

Gries floated up from his hand. *It would have been nice if I could have recovered the Grimoire as well... no, is that too greedy?* He looked up at the Grimoire, now just a blur of pure white light. Since its current master was about to die, Gries would seek its next. Abel had already chosen who that would be.

Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master. Like his older brother, he had an indecisive side, but he knew right from wrong and possessed the fortitude to sacrifice himself for a greater cause. He had disregarded the elders from the very beginning.

*But I suppose we'll only know when the time comes.*

Abel chuckled again. It was not as if he knew everything about the man. Still, in his judgment, Lloyd was the most suitable candidate for Gries among the current Magic Tower Masters.

*This is as far as I go.*

His vision was failing. Gries left his hand, disappearing in a brilliant flash of light as it headed for Lloyd at Mount Wudang. Abel closed his eyes, his sight already gone.

He had wanted to stop The End, but this was his limit. Still, he had sacrificed his life to banish its first calamity. Yes, he was satisfied with that.

*I believe in you.*

There was no time for last words. In his fading mental world, Abel thought of Lee Sungmin.

*Even if you feel the anxiety and danger that you yourself might be The End, as long as you believe you are not... you will never become The End. I was desperate to stop it. Even if I couldn't stop it completely, I succeeded in stopping a part of it. I wonder if you are just as desperate.*

Abel swallowed the question that would never be answered. Kneeling, his head slumped forward lifelessly.

THUD.

Lee Sungmin's body hit the ground, sending up a billowing cloud of dust.

He lay in the middle of it, his dull golden eyes staring blankly at the sky through the haze.

*Hey, you!*

The shouts in his head were now just meaningless noise.

And.

Lee Sungmin slowly pushed himself up.

From nearby, Holy Knights and Priests came running. They had seen Kim Jonghyun vanish in a flash of light and, not knowing what had truly happened, assumed Lee Sungmin was the one who had made the villain disappear.

"Lee Sungmin!" they called out, their voices filled with worry.

Hearing them, Lee Sungmin slowly rose to his feet.
