For a week, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon had pushed onward, sacrificing rest to widen the distance with their pursuers. Their flight had led them deep into a dense jungle where the air hung thick and humid, alive with bizarre creatures that blurred the line between monster and animal. Towering, overgrown trees wove together into a natural maze, a labyrinth so disorienting that even the sharp senses of a Supreme Peak master were rendered useless for maintaining direction.

[The flow of Mana is strange.]

Lee Sungmin was not the only one affected. Rubia, who had tried to map their surroundings with Mana just as she had in the Dungeon, soon gave up. A forest was naturally full of Mana, but this place was overflowing with it. The energy was a raging, uncontrolled torrent that even she could not comprehend.

[The forests of the South are a place where Mana and Demonic Energy are entangled. Since ancient times, humans have feared the darkness of the forest and the cries of beasts. Yokai are born from such fear, and the deeper the forest, the more Yokai there are, and the more Demonic Energy seethes. No matter how skilled a mage is, finding one's way in this forest is impossible.]

Void Monarch's voice echoed in his mind.

[Unless you have a skilled guide, that is.]

They had no such guide. They had tried to hire one through Erebrisa, but every guide they were connected with had recoiled in disgust, shaking their heads.

"The Bewitching Forest? Hah! You folks must be crazy, lookin' to die. I guarantee it, you won't find a single person willing to guide you into that forest. No matter how good the money is, a man's life is more precious."

The man had offered this unhelpful advice with a hollow laugh, a clear warning not to enter the Bewitching Forest if they valued their lives.

"Why didn't you say anything?"

[This forest was just a forest when this Elder was alive,] Void Monarch protested, his tone wronged.

Four hundred years had passed since Void Monarch walked the earth in a physical body. It was said that mountains and rivers could change in a decade. Four hundred years was enough time for them to change forty times over. It seemed that when Void Monarch was alive, this forest was not called the Bewitching Forest, but that was no longer the case.

This place was now a Forest of Death, a place nearly impossible to leave once entered. At some point, the forest's swirling Demonic Energy and magic would corrupt one's sense of direction, condemning them to wander until their chances of escape dwindled to nothing.

"Can't we just smash our way forward?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon spat, frustrated. Crimson enhanced qi swirled around his hands. He unleashed the tyrannical Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, blasting it toward the dense trees.

Lee Sungmin did not stop him. They had avoided causing a commotion because of their pursuers, but their patience had reached its limit. They had already been wandering this same area for half a day. Lee Sungmin glanced to the side, his eyes falling on a large rock bearing a scar he had carved into it himself. He had seen that rock half a day ago, and several times since. Now he was looking at it again.

KWAANG!

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's twin palms shot forward, and the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art struck the tree. The tree, however, did not break. Bewilderment crossed the Crazy Heavenly Demon's face. His Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art could crush steel like tofu, yet it had failed to break a single tree.

[It's a barrier,] Void Monarch muttered.

Lee Sungmin raised his spear in response. He chose his most powerful and tyrannical technique, the Seven Stars Penetrating Heaven of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He channeled the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art into the flow of the sky-piercing technique. In moments, his spear began to spin fiercely, unleashing streams of purple energy.

"......"

Swallowing a short cry, Lee Sungmin unleashed the sky-piercing. The attack, having taken time to prepare, was far more powerful than the Crazy Heavenly Demon's strike, yet the tree remained unchanged. Even Lee Sungmin could only gape in astonishment.

*A barrier?*

[This is strange... I've never even heard of such a powerful barrier. There are many barrier masters skilled in barrier arts in the South, but for your full-power attack to not even leave a scratch... No, that's not the problem.]

[Look up,] Void Monarch said.

Lee Sungmin raised his head. Intense sunlight poured through the gaps between the lush branches and leaves of the trees, which soared so high they seemed endless.

[Try jumping up.]

*Indeed.*

If they could not break through head-on, climbing the trees was another option. Doing so might make them too conspicuous, but having come this far, that was a minor issue.

Lee Sungmin infused his legs with inner qi and leaped, his body soaring into the sky far beyond the limits of a normal human. He landed lightly on a nearby branch and pushed off again, launching himself even higher.

"Ugh?!"

KWAANG!

He slammed his head into the empty air. He staggered, flipping over in mid-air, and looked up with eyes full of disbelief. Quickly executing the Airwalking Technique to steady himself, he reached out a trembling hand to feel the empty space above.

There was an invisible barrier. He pushed with all his strength, but it did not budge at all.

[Now this is surprising,] Void Monarch chuckled. [This entire forest is caught in a barrier. This isn't the work of a human. No matter how transcendent a sorcerer is, placing a barrier over this entire vast forest is impossible.]

"Then what is it?"

[Perhaps some annoying fellow has made a nest in the forest this Elder once ruled.]

Void Monarch's laughter grew deeper.

"Dammit. To think I'd come to the Bewitching Forest in my lifetime..." Aladur let out a sigh.

The whining grated on Jaegal Taeryeong, stirring an urge to kill the man. He restrained himself. In this dreadful forest, Aladur was the only guide they had.

"This is awful," Tang Ahee muttered, wrinkling her nose as she sat down to rest. Even for a master of Extreme Temperature Immunity, the humid heat of the South was irritating. The unchanging scenery, no matter how far they walked, felt like it would drive her mad.

"Keep your minds in check," Aladur shouted at the group.

"Our... what?" Tang Ahee asked.

"Your minds!"

Aladur shot to his feet. "Dammit! You people really don't know anything about this place, do you? I can understand the others, but Lord Jaegal, I never thought you'd be so ignorant! We're only a week from Devon, haven't you heard the rumors?!"

He glared at Jaegal Taeryeong with eyes full of resentment. If Jaegal Taeryeong had not been so coercive, Aladur would never have come to this forest.

"...I know about the Bewitching Forest," Jaegal Taeryeong replied. "A forest where people cannot live. A forest teeming with Yokai. A forest you cannot leave once you enter."

"So what are you afraid of? Do the people here look like a joke to you? We have fifteen from the Namgung Clan's Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps, ten from the Tang Clan's Dark Night Black Mist Squad, and twenty from the Jaegal Clan's Dark Illusion Blade Brigade. Forty-five masters, along with the heirs of each Noble Clan. No matter how powerful a Yokai appears..."

"This is why I hate martial artists!" Aladur's face twisted. He pounded his chest in frustration and shouted, "You people put too much damn confidence in your martial arts! You train your bodies, but a human is still a human! We're pathetically weak creatures who die with a croak if our heads are split open, our hearts burst, or our sides are torn! Why the hell don't you understand that?!"

"What...?"

"You know nothing, absolutely nothing, about the Bewitching Forest. Well, I guess you couldn't know! Only a very small number of sorcerers know the truth of this forest. Those damn old geezers, keeping their mouths shut and calling it a taboo, that's why this kind of bullshit happens...!"

"Calm down," Namgung Heewon interjected as Aladur screamed, spittle flying from his mouth.

A blade-sharp presence, an invisible and formless edge, was directed at Aladur. He swallowed hard, feeling as if the cold steel was grazing his Adam's apple.

"Explain it so I can understand."

"...There was a great Yokai four hundred years ago," Aladur muttered, unconsciously stroking his Adam's apple. "They say it was a great monster. So great that a huge number of exorcists and powerful individuals were mobilized just to catch that one Yokai."

"Four hundred years... that's a long time ago. It was before the Jaegal Clan even settled in Devon," Jaegal Taeryeong muttered.

The corner of Aladur's mouth twitched at the comment. "Even prestigious clans are ultimately made up of humans. Four hundred years is a length of time that ordinary humans cannot live through. Anyway... four hundred years ago, this forest was the territory ruled by that Yokai. However, that Yokai died."

"So?"

"Yokai are different from any monster. They are born from abstract concepts. So..." Aladur glanced at the overgrown bushes. "...Let's say the bushes rustle. Naturally, you'd think, 'Is there a small animal?' Something like that, but what if you search the bushes and find no animal?"

"I'd think the wind blew. Or that the animal that shook the bushes ran away quickly."

"That's a common thought, but some people feel fear in that moment. They wonder, 'What on earth could have shaken the bushes?'"

"......"

"Fear is a sudden emotion. Have you ever felt someone's gaze in the dark? No matter how great a martial arts master you are, there must be times you feel fear. Especially... when you were a child. Were you ever afraid of sleeping alone? Did you ever think there might be a monster under your bed? After hearing a scary story, were you ever afraid to go to the bathroom alone at night? Have you ever been afraid of the loud thunder on a rainy day?"

"...I suppose so. If you're human."

"Yokai are born from that kind of fear," Aladur sighed and continued. "Because they are born from such things, Yokai are difficult to deal with, the natural enemies of humans. Of course, if every Yokai born of fear possessed terrifying power, the human race would have been wiped out long ago. Only a very small number, called great yokai, possess power that transcends human common sense. The one that died four hundred years ago, Void Monarch, was said to be the most powerful of them all."

"Why are you telling us a story from four hundred years ago?" Jaegal Taeryeong said dismissively. "You already said this Void Monarch is dead."

"Didn't I say that Yokai are born from fear?" Aladur's eyes clouded with gloom. "Death exists for them, yes, but a great yokai, one that has been offered an enormous amount of fear, stands on an ambiguous boundary. It is both a Yokai and not a Yokai. This forest, in particular, was its territory. To feel fear in this forest is to risk awakening the remnants of that Yokai."

"You mean it can be resurrected?"

"It's different from that. An entirely new Yokai could be born, using the fear etched into this land as nourishment... and that's not all. The reason this forest is dangerous has nothing to do with Void Monarch ruling here in the past. There's another reason."

Aladur slumped back down, remembering a story he had heard before leaving his Master. The reclusive sorcerers rarely interacted with the outside world. Even when an unknown Guild called Erebrisa approached them, most refused. Aladur's Master was one of them. He did not know if his Master was still alive, but he would often gather his disciples and say this.

Never go to the Bewitching Forest.

There is an immortal monster there.

"...An immortal monster?" Hearing Aladur's words, Jaegal Taeryeong burst out laughing. "My goodness... after all that, this is what you were talking about? An immortal monster? Hahaha!"

After laughing for a while, Jaegal Taeryeong shook his head. "There's no such thing as immortality in this world. Even mutants who live for long ages eventually die. Were you really scared of something like that?"

"The monster is real."

"Have you seen it?"

Aladur could not answer Jaegal Taeryeong's question. It was his first time in the Bewitching Forest as well.

"It's not just the immortal monster. Didn't I tell you...! Because of Void Monarch's death, the immense fear he was offered lies dormant in this forest. That means there's an overflowing amount of Demonic Energy left behind! The countless Yokai who want to take that Demonic Energy..."

"If they appear, I will cut them down."

"You son of a bitch...!" Aladur pounded his frustrated chest at Jaegal Taeryeong's response.

The break was over. Jaegal Taeryeong drew the sword from his waist and pointed it at Aladur.

"Track the Phantom Spear and the Crazy Heavenly Demon," Jaegal Taeryeong commanded, his eyes holding a murderous glint. "We have to kill those two to get out of this forest."
