the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon.

The name echoed in Lee Sungmin's memory, a name he had heard countless times in his past life. She was an otherworlder summoned around the same time as him, had been in Zenavis with him, and was a martial artist who had left the city long before he did.

What she did to survive in Eria after leaving Zenavis was a mystery to him. He had only ever caught whispers and rumors, fragmented memories that refused to form a clear picture. One thing, however, was certain. Wiji Hoyeon had not only survived in Eria until just before his death, but she had also forged a legendary name for herself. Eria was home to many martial artists from the Demonic Cult, and while former Young Cult Leaders were rare, she was not the only one.

Still, it was Wiji Hoyeon who had carved the title of 'the Little Heavenly Demon' into the very soul of everyone living in Eria. In the thirteen years it took Lee Sungmin to crawl from a G-Rank mercenary to C-Rank, she had ascended from a mere rookie to a being who brushed against the pinnacle of power.

Despite the endless rumors, Lee Sungmin had never once met the giant known as Wiji Hoyeon. They had shared time in Zenavis, but the city was vast and their stations were worlds apart. He had arrived a month before her, yet the moment Wiji Hoyeon appeared, she already stood on an overwhelmingly higher plane than he did.

Even after thirteen years, nothing had changed. His own progress had been slower than a turtle's crawl, perhaps even more sluggish. The growth he had scraped together in thirteen years was likely less than what she possessed the moment she first arrived in Zenavis.

She wasn't someone he could even feel inferior to. The things they possessed were simply too different. She was not from another world or another star, but a person from another dimension entirely.

However, he was curious.

It was a curiosity born of a whim, a simple desire to witness the giant's beginning. His daily trek to the forest had become a habit as ingrained as breathing, but today, he boldly set it aside.

"Not going to the forest today?"

It was eleven in the morning when Lee Sungmin came downstairs alone. Jack tilted his head and asked, surprised. In the month Lee Sungmin had been staying at the inn, he had gone to the forest every single day.

"Yes. I thought it'd be okay to rest for a day."

"That's strange," Jack said with a hearty laugh. "Lula kept telling you to rest and you never listened to her."

"If you're not going to the forest, are you planning to rest in your room all day?"

"No. I'm planning to go out for a bit."

Once outside the inn, Lee Sungmin called upon the One Thunder Movement Arts and raced toward the Central Plaza of Zenavis. A month of practicing the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art and using his movement arts at every opportunity had considerably strengthened his inner power.

There was no set day of the week for an otherworlder's summoning. Sometimes they appeared a day apart, sometimes once every few days, and in extreme cases, only once every few months.

One thing, however, never changed. An otherworlder was always summoned to the Central Plaza of Zenavis, and the time was always noon. A month ago, Lee Sungmin himself had appeared in that very spot at that very hour.

*What does she look like?*

Lee Sungmin swallowed, suppressing his rising curiosity. It was not yet noon. The bell in the Central Plaza would ring when the time came. He found a seat on a nearby fountain and calmly regulated his breathing.

Admiration.

The name Wiji Hoyeon and the title of the Little Heavenly Demon were things that had inspired admiration in him since his past life. Most otherworlders who lived in Eria likely felt the same, especially the Classless, who struggled not only with their circumstances but with survival itself. In that respect, he was no different from any other.

That was why he wanted to see it. He wanted to witness the moment Wiji Hoyeon was first summoned to this world.

DONG, DONG, DONG.

The bell rang. It was time. Lee Sungmin gulped, his eyes fixed on the center of the plaza.

A girl was standing there. One moment there was nothing, the next, a girl stood blankly in the middle of the square, as if she had been there all along.

She wore black martial arts robes, an attire that immediately revealed her origins. Lee Sungmin swallowed dryly as he studied her.

The girl stood dazedly for a moment before her eyes began to scan her surroundings. She seemed unable to grasp the situation, which was only natural. The summoning to Eria was incredibly sudden, and this world offered no explanation to those it brought here.

Blinking, the girl opened her mouth. It looked like she was saying something, but the distance was too great for him to hear.

the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. She looked younger than he had expected, even younger than himself based on her appearance alone.

*How old was Wiji Hoyeon...?*

He had no idea. He had heard countless rumors of her phenomenal power, but never once had he heard about her age or what she looked like.

Time passed. Lee Sungmin remained on the fountain, watching Wiji Hoyeon, who did little more than look around. People passing by gave her a single glance before moving on, none of them approaching or speaking to her. For the residents of Zenavis, the summoning of an otherworlder was just another part of daily life, nothing to pay special attention to.

*...I was expecting something... extraordinary.*

Watching her stand there so passively, Lee Sungmin felt a pang of disappointment. He had heard the name of this giant so many times in his past life and had imagined her beginning would be just as remarkable. Surprisingly, Wiji Hoyeon showed no such signs.

*Well, I guess that makes sense. We were both suddenly summoned, after all...*

With that thought, he stood up. He decided he had seen enough. He was satisfied just to have witnessed the giant's beginning. Wiji Hoyeon was someone he would have no connection with for the rest of his life. In the future, she would live in Eria and make a name for herself, just as the rumors foretold.

And him? He would strive to live a life better than his previous one. Feeling a little bitter, Lee Sungmin turned away.

"......"

He had turned, but before he could take a single step, a voice from behind him made his body flinch and freeze.

"Why were you looking at me?"

It was a child's voice, one that had not yet broken. Lee Sungmin turned back around, his expression stiff.

Wiji Hoyeon was suddenly standing right behind him. Her height was similar to his own. He stared at her face, his mouth hanging half-open. She had her hands clasped behind her back, wearing a solemn expression that was entirely unbefitting a child.

"Answer me. Why were you looking at me?" Wiji Hoyeon pressed.

Caught completely off guard, Lee Sungmin could not form any words and just moved his lips. At this, her eyebrows furrowed.

"What are you so surprised about? You look like you've seen a Ghost Spirit."

"Ah... well, that's..."

"Just answer the question. Why were you looking at me?"

She pressed for an answer again. He could not remain silent any longer. Lee Sungmin stammered out his words.

"Th-that's... I was surprised to see you suddenly summoned..."

"Summoned?"

Wiji Hoyeon reacted to the word.

"It seems you know something about this situation. What is this 'summoning'? Where is this place? I was in my room doing my morning training... Why on earth am I here? And where is this place?"

She fired off a series of questions. Her eyes, fixed on Lee Sungmin, showed not a shred of doubt that he would provide the answers.

*I didn't think it would turn out like this...*

Lee Sungmin was flustered, but he tried to calmly accept the situation. He had no intention of lying to her. The answers to her questions were not exactly secrets.

"...Well... you see..."

His story began. He told Wiji Hoyeon everything he knew without hiding a single detail, explaining what this world was and why she had been summoned here.

Wiji Hoyeon listened quietly as he spoke. Though she must have had many questions, she did not ask any, nor did she press him further once he started talking.

"Surprising."

When Lee Sungmin finished, Wiji Hoyeon uttered the single word with a placid expression. Despite what she said, her face showed no sign of it.

*What the hell is with this kid?*

Now it was Lee Sungmin's turn to be flustered. What kind of person was she? She could not be old or experienced, but things like that were useless against the damn 'summoning' of Eria anyway. You suddenly open your eyes and find yourself in a completely different world. What kind of lunatic would just accept that with an 'Oh, is that so?'

"...You believe me?"

"If I had heard such a story in the Central Plains, I wouldn't have believed it. However, right now, there is clear evidence before me, is there not?" she replied with a nonchalant expression. She raised a hand and pointed to a resident of Zenavis passing nearby.

"A Colored-Eyed Person. Not a race one can see commonly even in the vast Central Plains, and yet... there are so many here. Most of the people are Colored-Eyed People, to the point that you and I, with our black eyes and black hair, seem like people from another world."

Muttering this, Wiji Hoyeon pointed from the person to a building.

"The buildings too. I've never seen buildings of that shape before. Yes... I see. So this is the world called 'Eria'."

She nodded her head in understanding.

"And this thing called a status window. It's just as you said. I only thought about it without speaking. Hmm... Name is Wiji Hoyeon... Occupation is martial artist? Haha! This is quite amusing."

Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing. Following Lee Sungmin's instructions, she recalled the oral formulas of the martial arts she was learning.

"Skill Window... it's filling up. Yes. Having seen these things for myself, how could I doubt your words?"

"Ah... yes..."

Her way of thinking was different. Or perhaps it was a difference in adaptability. Whatever it was, her reaction was completely unlike his own when he first arrived. Back then, upon learning the situation, Lee Sungmin had bawled his eyes out, begging to go back, to be sent home.

However, Wiji Hoyeon was laughing.

"You. What's your name?" she asked him.

"...Lee Sungmin."

"How old are you?"

"Fourteen."

"I am Wiji Hoyeon, and I am thirteen."

She was a year younger. Lee Sungmin stared at her, his mouth still half-open.

"I've heard a good story. This world... Eria. To live here, you have to hunt monsters? Good, that's great. Huhu! My training was getting tedious, so this is perfect."

Wiji Hoyeon laughed as if something was incredibly amusing. As Lee Sungmin watched her with a weary expression, she reached out and patted his shoulder.

"Now, let's go."

"...Huh? Go where?"

"Hmm. I suppose I should be open about this first."

Wiji Hoyeon grumbled in a small voice. She lowered the hand from his shoulder and clasped both behind her back again.

"As the Young Cult Leader of the Demonic Cult, I was revered by all from the moment I was born. However, the Demonic Cult does not exist here in Eria. This means that I, too, have come down to the level of a single human being, not the Young Cult Leader of the Demonic Cult."

It was hard to believe these were the words of a thirteen-year-old.

"In Eria, I am but a single human. Therefore, from this day forward, you are my friend."

Wiji Hoyeon declared this with a solemn face. Lee Sungmin could not comprehend her words. Since there was no Demonic Cult, she went from being the Young Cult Leader to just an ordinary person. He could understand that much, but why were they suddenly friends? What kind of twisted logic was that?

"...Uh... why are you and I becoming friends...?"

"...Hm? I find those words hard to understand. Aren't all people of a similar age supposed to be friends? Moreover, you and I have been connected by this meeting, this fate. Isn't it natural that we become friends?" Wiji Hoyeon asked, tilting her head.

Lee Sungmin could not form a reply and just moved his lips.

He could, however, understand one thing. He understood just what kind of kid the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon was at thirteen years old.

She had a fundamental flaw in her communication skills.

"Uh... yes. That's right. Friends... friends."

"Yes. From this moment on, we are friends. So why are you using formal language? Don't friends speak informally to each other?"

"Ah... uh... right..." Lee Sungmin replied with a weary face as she tilted her head again.

And just like that, at twenty-seven years old, he had become friends with a thirteen-year-old girl.
