Camping out was a familiar routine. He had done it countless times on his way to the North, and though his destination had now shifted to the South, the necessity of making camp remained. In truth, this journey south demanded even more caution than his trek north had.

[A pursuit order for you, Lee Sungmin, is circulating among the Guilds and clans that support the Murim Alliance, including the Beggars' Gang.]

Lee Sungmin listened to the voice of Nebul, whom he had asked to track the Murim Alliance's movements. The news was exactly what he had anticipated. For killing the Iron Armored Divine Spear, he, the Phantom Spear, had been branded a demonic practitioner. It seemed they had also confirmed that Wiji Hoyeon was no longer with him, but the Murim Alliance, regardless of the Little Heavenly Demon's presence, was determined to question him about the Iron Armored Divine Spear's death.

[The hunting dogs that were chasing the Little Heavenly Demon never returned to the Murim Alliance. They've changed their target and are now tracking you, Lee Sungmin. You are constantly being watched.]

A direct clash had not yet occurred, which was a relief. He had also sought the Crazy Heavenly Demon's advice on the matter.

Lee Sungmin knew the Murim Alliance was not the righteous organization the public perceived it to be. In the end, any group of people was bound to pursue its own interests. The Alliance existed to more efficiently manage the orthodox sects and their martial artists, a structure maintained to firmly establish the 'orthodox murim' in a world where countless different beings lived entangled with one another.

The orthodox sects, centered around Taoist and Buddhist traditions, defined themselves as the opposite of the unorthodox. They spoke of righteousness and chivalry, but not all of them truly followed those ideals. That purity had been corrupted long ago. Martial artists were summoned to Eria from various dimensions, each with its own definition of righteousness and chivalry, and the people who practiced them were bound to be different as well.

Righteousness and chivalry had become little more than an old-fashioned, big-picture ideology. That was the state of the modern orthodox sects. While the unorthodox, often called evil, were rather easy to distinguish, the orthodox were not so simple.

*But you know, there's a very easy and convenient way to show off one's goodness. Do you know what that is?*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's laughing words echoed in his head.

*You catch the evil that sticks out.*

It was similar to what Gwan Hu had said. The Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance exercised only minimal control over its demonic practitioners. Occasionally, one of them, whose suppressed desires finally exploded, would pierce through like an awl through a pocket. The Murim Alliance would then punish them to display their own righteousness and chivalry.

*Am I the awl now?*

No, not yet. They were only monitoring his actions, waiting for him to get sharper, to stick out more. When that time came, they would become a hammer and either pound him back in or pull him out entirely.

"It's best to avoid friction," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, his gaze fixed on the campfire. "The ones chasing you are hunting dogs trained by the Murim Alliance. They're used to chasing prey and they know how to use dirty methods. Individually, they might not be much, but they know how to handle a strong opponent as a group. Have you ever experienced a formation?"

"No, I haven't."

Lee Sungmin watched the flickering flames. Beside him, Rubia pouted, clutching her hungry stomach. Before long, the meat was cooked, and a bright smile bloomed across her face.

"Formations are tricky," the Crazy Heavenly Demon continued. "If you don't know how to break them, you're bound to get a beating. The Transcendent Realm is a great state, but it's not invincible. In fact, didn't you almost kill the Dark Supreme without even being in the Transcendent Realm?"

"That was because he was careless."

"Keke! Even so, it's an incredible feat. This one got shamefully caught up in lunacy. In any case, it's foolish for even a master of the Transcendent Realm to contend with a well-laid formation."

Unlike Lee Sungmin, the Crazy Heavenly Demon had lived his life as a demonic practitioner. He had committed several murderous rampages while surrendering his body to madness, and the only reason he was still alive was likely because the Murim Alliance had decided to leave him alone for now. To deal with a master whose martial prowess was among the best even at the Supreme Peak, the Alliance would have to accept sacrifices of its own.

If they were only observing for now, then all the better. Lee Sungmin had no desire for a direct confrontation either. He had resigned himself to being branded a demonic practitioner, but he had no intention of acting like one. He hoped to pass through smoothly, without friction.

Just as the cold deepened the farther north they went, the heat intensified with every step south. The grass covering the plains grew taller, and the forests became impenetrably dense. The days of camping repeated, but Rubia never complained. The possibility of meeting Envirus in the South was enough to keep her happy.

[Various tribes live in the South,] Nebul informed him daily, feeding him information. [They build their homes in the forest and hold diverse beliefs. Also, many Yokai live in the South.]

[Yokai are born from fear,] the Void Monarch added. [A Yokai can be born from the pitch-black darkness of night and the trivial noises heard beyond it. The Yokai born that way are insignificant creatures. However, even an insignificant Yokai can become a great one if it feeds on fear and builds its power. The South is a suitable environment for them to be born. Cities are rare here, and there are few means to light up the darkness of the forest. The tribespeople grow up hearing legends and stories about Yokai as if it were natural from childhood. All of that becomes their nourishment.]

The closer they got to the South, the more cheerful the Void Monarch seemed to become. He had been born and raised here, made a name for himself here, and died here.

[Make sure to stop by Urmuri. It's quite a cool city.]

Urmuri was a small city, and one of the most peculiar in all of Eria. It was a city where the night never ended, known as the City of the Hundred Ghosts Night Parade, a place where Yokai resembling humans roamed the streets instead of people.

"If I get the chance," Lee Sungmin murmured.

He and the Crazy Heavenly Demon took turns standing guard. Lee Sungmin hugged his spear and watched the campfire, his eyes tracing the dancing flames. Nearby, Rubia was curled up asleep. She had set up a perimeter of her warning magic, but he couldn't bring himself to trust it unconditionally.

The forest was deep.

Lee Sungmin's gaze drifted beyond the firelight, into the oppressive darkness the flames failed to illuminate. Something was swaying out there. Or so it seemed. He swallowed without thinking, his hand finding the familiar shaft of his spear. The shape of the thing in the darkness was unclear, but as he narrowed his eyes and rose to his feet, he knew a non-human being was watching him.

"Who's there?"

He felt no panic, no fear. His martial prowess was among the best at the Supreme Peak, and with a bit of luck, he could even stand against a Transcendent.

With a rustling sound, the shimmering form emerged from the shadows. It was a giant snake with black scales, its head raised high. Cold, reptilian eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin as a long tongue darted out from between its lips, making a slurping sound.

"...A snake?"

[It's a Yokai,] the Void Monarch muttered.

The snake, which had been blinking at Lee Sungmin, lifted the tip of its tail and shook it lightly.

[Who are you?] The voice echoed not in the air, but inside Lee Sungmin's head. [Your human skin is quite convincing. Did you invade this territory knowing it belongs to this Silver White Snake?]

*Silver White Snake?*

[I don't know who that is,] the Void Monarch said indifferently. [It seems to have gathered some power, but seeing as it hasn't even shed its skin, it's just a minor yokai.]

On the way here, Lee Sungmin had heard many stories from the Void Monarch. Yokai came in various forms, but as their rank increased, they could shed their skin and take on a human form. This was because most Yokai preyed on humans, and they mimicked their prey's appearance for more efficient hunting.

[Are you not going to answer?]

[It's a minor yokai that doesn't even know the difference in rank,] the Void Monarch scoffed. [Do as you please. Kill it or not.]

The Silver White Snake reared its body up threateningly and opened its mouth wide. Not particularly wanting a fight, Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh.

"Don't be a nuisance and back off."

He had genuinely said it for the snake's own good, but the creature did not listen.

[Insolent whelp!]

It lunged, its mouth agape to reveal sharp fangs. It was fast, but not nearly fast enough to startle Lee Sungmin. With a single, fluid motion, he spun his spear and drove its point straight into the Silver White Snake's gaping maw. The long body convulsed, impaled on the shaft, and thrashed violently.

[KRRRAAAK!]

Soon, the thrashing stilled. Lee Sungmin pulled his spear free from the corpse. It might have been a Yokai, but it still looked like a snake. *Could I eat it?* Snake meat was quite edible, but then he remembered it had spoken to him, and his appetite vanished completely.

The night passed. Lee Sungmin, who had fallen asleep after taking over the watch from the Crazy Heavenly Demon, opened his eyes to the morning light.

"Oh, you're awake?" the Crazy Heavenly Demon called out, a beaming expression on his face.

Lee Sungmin sniffed the air, catching the scent of cooking meat, and looked toward the fire where the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia sat.

"Well, what do you know! I found a large dead snake nearby. Thanks to that, we're having a feast this morning!"

Next to him lay the butchered remains of the Silver White Snake he had killed during the night. Rubia, knowing nothing of the situation, sat happily beside the Crazy Heavenly Demon, eating the grilled snake meat. The creature had been so large that the chunks of meat looked quite decent even without any special preparation.

"......"

For a moment, Lee Sungmin considered telling them it was a Yokai. In the end, he just let out a deep sigh and walked over to join them.

Although it was the meat of a Yokai, the Silver White Snake was quite delicious. A thought crossed his mind. *What if I had eaten its heart instead of the meat?* As soon as the idea formed, his appetite vanished as if by magic. Thanks to his Black Heart, he could rapidly increase his power by consuming the hearts of other beings, yet, even knowing such a method existed, he was reluctant to use it.

[That's because you're human,] the Void Monarch said. [If you became a Yokai, you wouldn't have any hesitation about such methods.]

That might be true. To become a Yokai meant to stop thinking like a human. Just as a vampire naturally drinks blood, becoming a Yokai meant losing one's aversion to things humans would not do. He hated that idea. He had no particular reason to remain human, but he didn't want to give up his humanity either. Besides, the words Envirus had relayed through Lloyd still weighed on his mind.

*He told me not to give up on being human.*

He still didn't know what that meant, but there must have been a reason for the warning.

"Those bastards. They're eating well."

A member of the Demon Extermination Unit muttered as he lowered the binoculars from his eyes.

"And here we are, chewing on jerky. Huh? Am I wrong?" He spat on the ground and glared down at the piece of jerky in his hand. "Doesn't this look weird? The demonic practitioners who should be on the run are comfortably sleeping by a campfire and grilling meat in the morning. Why do we, the righteous pursuers of the Murim Alliance, have to chew on this tasteless jerky?"

"It's the spirit of the hungry," a man wrapped in rags replied. He was the leader of the Black Hound Squad, the ten-man pursuit team operating under the Demon Extermination Unit. "A full pig can't chase its prey."

"For fuck's sake! Cut the crap with your bullshit philosophy, Leader. If you wanna be so hungry, you can be hungry by yourself. Why are you telling us not to eat too?"

"You son of a bitch! If you guys are eating when I'm not, it pisses me the hell off!"

"You motherfucker!" the squad member roared, beating his chest.

Their idiotic argument was a familiar sight to the other members of the Black Hound Squad.

"Just hang in there a little longer," the leader said, calming down. "Once we leave this forest, we'll be in the Jaegal Clan's territory. The Jaegal Clan has already said they'll help, so this tedious chase will be over soon."

"Our opponents are the Crazy Heavenly Demon and the Phantom Spear," another member pointed out. "Is the head of the Jaegal Clan gonna step in personally or something? If he doesn't, we won't even be able to touch them."

"I heard masters from the Namgung Clan and the Tang Clan are also with the Jaegal Clan."

HAWK, SPIT. The Black Hound Leader spat a wad of phlegm onto the ground.

"For a baby's first birthday party."
