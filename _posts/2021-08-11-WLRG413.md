A chilling quiet fell over them. Amidst the heavy silence, Lee Sungmin shrugged, his shoulders weighed down by a conflict he could not resolve. He knew, on some level, that his position was a contradiction. If he failed to stop Wiji Hoyeon, it would mean The End, and the world would perish.

However, what if he succeeded? What if he managed to stop Wiji Hoyeon and change the fate of The End?

An arm severed, a leg lost, an eye gouged out. He looked at his friends, at the price they had already paid. They were good people, so good that they had stood their ground and fought without running. They had battled a force called The End, something the rest of the world barely understood, and this was their reward.

He hated it. He could not bear the sight of it.

The human body was too fragile, too easily broken. Since the spiritual and physical were one, there was no fixing what was lost. *If, just if. If I take them into the Sleeping Forest with me...*

*What if someone dies?*

*When The End is over, what if one of them is gone forever?*

He knew he would not be able to endure it. He refused to even imagine it. The guilt would crush him, and even though Wiji Hoyeon was being manipulated by the Spirit, her actions would still be the cause. He might even come to resent her for someone's death.

He hated that thought. The thought of someone dying, and the thought of holding Wiji Hoyeon, who acted without her own will, responsible for it. If he failed to stop her...

He did not want to think about that.

"It's because I'm weak... that's why you won't take me," Baek Sogo muttered in a small voice.

Scarlet's eyes, cold and sharp, fixed on Lee Sungmin.

"You'd better think carefully before you speak," she spat. "Because I might just start to hate you."

At her warning, Lee Sungmin quietly clenched and unclenched his fist. Even now, the thought that he did not want to be hated surfaced, and he gave himself a self-deprecating smile.

"Wiji Hoyeon is strong," he said.

"I know."

"If we fight... someone will get hurt. Someone could even die."

"So?"

"That's what I hate. The fight with Jeniela... I saw how badly everyone was hurt. As long as you're all human, there's a limit to how you can heal."

"......"

Baek Sogo's voice trembled as she cut in, striding toward him. Her face was still pale and drained of blood. Taking a deep breath, she glared at Lee Sungmin with determined eyes.

"That was my choice. I don't care if I get hurt, or even if I die. I won't let you carry the great burden of The End all by yourself, Junior Disciple."

"I'm stopping you because *I* care," he retorted.

Baek Sogo bit her lower lip. A gust of wind rustled her empty sleeve, and watching it, Lee Sungmin let out a small sigh. Scarlet merely glared at him, saying nothing. Nearby, Rubia and Teresa held each other's hands, nervously gauging the atmosphere. The Black Dragon Knight, who had been gazing at the sky, lowered his head.

He stared intently at Lee Sungmin. "You're right," he said, nodding. "Compared to you, who has grown so strong, we are weak. We weren't much help against the Vampire Queen either. The only reason we bought any time was because she was toying with us."

The Black Dragon Knight chuckled grimly. "Besides, I'm one-armed now. Missing an arm is a fatal flaw for a knight. Given time, I might get used to this, but none of us have that luxury. Yes. For us to go with you would be nothing but arrogant stubbornness. If we go, it's obvious we'll just hold you back."

With those words, the Black Dragon Knight took a few steps back.

"...It's not that I don't understand how you feel," Scarlet began. "It's just like that old man said. We're weak. We'd only get in the way. Still... I wanted to go with you. I didn't want to let you do everything alone. You know? To me, you're still that emotionally unstable little kid."

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh at her words. Scarlet sighed heavily and scratched her head.

"Still. What you said... I don't like it. That we're too weak to help... In the end, you just don't want us to get hurt, right? Don't be so meddlesome. When you come this far, you start to feel a sense of duty you never had before. It's even truer now that I've had an eye plucked out."

Scarlet did not back down, and neither did Baek Sogo. Lloyd looked lost, but the Black Dragon Knight subtly reached out, placing a hand on his shoulder.

"Don't make things needlessly painful for him."

The Black Dragon Knight was being considerate. He knew that even if they insisted, Lee Sungmin would not take them. If they kept pushing, he would only find a way to leave them behind, and the act would torment him.

"...Yes..." Lloyd bit his lip and nodded, stepping back with the knight.

Teresa and Rubia also distanced themselves. Seeing this, Scarlet pouted and glanced back at those who had retreated.

"...This makes it look like I'm the only one being needlessly stubborn," she grumbled. In the end, she too stepped back with a reluctant expression.

Only Baek Sogo did not move. With a conflicted look, she glanced down at her one remaining hand and slowly clenched it into a fist.

"I know," she said. She knew all too well. "I know that I'm weak compared to you, Junior Disciple. Even when I had both arms... I'd only hold you back."

Lee Sungmin did not answer. He did not want to force the issue, hoping they could understand and accept it on their own.

"It's not that I don't know. I do, but... Junior Disciple, even so, I can't just leave you be. When you're heading into danger, I don't want to be left behind... waiting for you alone."

He knew. He knew that was her personality. No matter the situation, she would not back down until she was convinced. Even knowing her own weakness, Baek Sogo would not retreat. She would not run away. She had not been afraid when facing Jeniela, not even after losing an arm. Even now, knowing she was weak, she would not run.

"You've... gotten much stronger, Junior Disciple."

THUD.

Baek Sogo took a step forward. She swallowed a deep breath, drawing up her internal energy, and ripped off her flapping sleeve completely. Then, she looked at Lee Sungmin with a faint smile.

"Come to think of it, we haven't had a duel since Mount Mushii, Junior Disciple."

"That's because we never had the chance."

"It seems... now is the perfect chance. Yes. You don't need to yield the first move, do you, Junior Disciple?"

"......"

She was seeking her own kind of understanding. Lee Sungmin did not back down. He simply nodded.

Baek Sogo lowered her stance, her two upright eyes fixed on him. Lee Sungmin did not grip his Formless Spear. He mirrored her, lowering his own stance and preparing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Her smile deepened.

He remembered the first time he learned that art on Mount Mushii. Under a vow of silence, Baek Sogo had communicated by writing on the ground with a branch. Her teachings had been gentle. Everything about her was gentle. If he had not met her, he never would have endured the training on that mountain.

When he had collapsed after killing the Sword Phantom, he had been reunited with her. *You've grown so much.* Her words back then had soothed his anxious heart. In his previous life, she had been murdered in a Dungeon. He had gone there wanting to prevent her death, and there they had met again.

He wanted to be praised by his Senior Sister again. He wanted to show her how strong he had become, to display his level and receive her acknowledgment.

*The me who learned this martial art from you on that mountain back then. I've become this strong now. The kid who struggled just to take a single step has become this strong now.*

*You've grown so much.*

He remembered the pure happiness he had felt hearing those words from her on the night he killed the Sword Phantom. He wanted to hear them one more time.

Baek Sogo was the first to move. Unleashing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, she closed the distance in an instant. Her continuous steps linked the forms of the art, and a great surge of internal energy erupted around her like a fluttering wind, pressuring the very space they occupied. She was not holding back. She was executing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break with all the power she could muster, as if shouting, *I'm not weak. I won't hold you back.*

Traceless Step concealed her form. One Step Dancing Shadow created white afterimages, and those afterimages executed Two Step Calamity. Her internal energy shimmered, creating waves as the forms linked together as naturally as flowing water. Two Step Calamity became Three Step Fatality, and then Four Step Chaos.

Watching her come at him with all her might, Lee Sungmin slowly lifted his foot.

PAT.

All the martial arts she had unleashed vanished. The internal energy disappeared as if it had never been there, and Baek Sogo suffered no internal injuries. She stood there blankly, staring at the empty space before her. Lee Sungmin was no longer there.

Slowly, she turned around. He was standing behind her, looking at her with a bitter smile.

"...Amazing," Baek Sogo muttered, nodding her head. "Even though it's the same Dancing Shadow Soul Break... the difference is this great. At this rate, I can't even call myself the orthodox successor."

"That's because the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder is added to it."

"That version seems better. If it's okay... yes. Next time, could I receive martial arts instruction from you, Junior Disciple?"

"Of course, that's fine."

At his answer, Baek Sogo smiled and bowed her head deeply.

"Thank you, Junior Disciple." The end of her voice trembled slightly. "You've grown... so much. Truly. The time we met on Mount Mushii... feels like such a distant memory now. You're magnificent."

Saying that, she raised her head and wiped the tears welling in her eyes with her sleeve.

"I'm sorry for being stubborn."

*I want to go with you.*

"I understand now, for certain, that I'd be no help to you, Junior Disciple. If I went, I'd only hold you back... I'm sorry. I needed to be convinced, even if it was like this."

"...I know. That's just the kind of person you are, Senior Sister. I knew you wouldn't just let me go without a fuss."

"Yes... because I'm stubborn."

*I want to go with you and fight against the calamity of The End. Whatever the outcome, I want to be with you. Even if I die there. I want to die for you. If you fail, I want to die with you before the world ends. I hate having to wait, unable to do anything. I hate leaving all the burdens to you alone.* Those words swirled in her heart.

"It's alright, Senior Sister," Lee Sungmin said, placing a hand on her shoulder. "Nothing will happen. I'll... come back safely. It probably won't even take long. So... please don't worry."

*It's different from worry.*

Baek Sogo placed a hand on her aching chest. Her emotions were a complicated mess she could not put into words.

*Maybe... this is jealousy.* At that thought, she squeezed her eyes shut. *Inside there will be Wiji Hoyeon. Wiji Hoyeon, who knows a side of Junior Disciple that I... don't.*

"...Yes..." she finally managed, but she could not bring herself to voice those feelings. She thought of herself as a good person. "Go and come back, Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo smiled. She hid her own feelings, speaking a lie as if it were the truth.

Lee Sungmin quietly reached out and wiped away the tears that had started to fall from her eyes.

"...You're a terrible liar, Senior Sister."

HUP.

Baek Sogo sucked in a breath. "...If I... didn't lie, would anything... change?"

"...At least, nothing would change right now," he replied, his own voice bitter.

At that, Baek Sogo chuckled and placed a hand on his chest. "...That's why I'm lying, Junior Disciple. It's... easier for both of us that way."

Saying that, she gently pushed his chest.

"I'll be waiting, Junior Disciple," she said, wiping her eyes with her one hand. "When we meet again... I won't lie."

To Baek Sogo, who was smiling through tear-filled eyes, Lee Sungmin could say nothing. He took a few steps back as she pushed him, then bowed his head deeply to her.

In the end, she never spoke her true feelings. Even after being caught, she could not confess the emotions she held in her heart. She knew it might hold him back.

"Idiot," Scarlet muttered, glaring at Lee Sungmin's retreating back. Her words were meant for him, but also for Baek Sogo. "He's not going off to die, is he?" she added, kicking the ground with an inexplicable unease.

"He's not," Baek Sogo countered in a small voice. "He promised he'd come back."

"Jeez..." Scarlet let out a hollow laugh at Baek Sogo's blind faith. She was about to scold her but stopped herself. Instead, she linked arms with Baek Sogo and pulled her along. "Let's get something to eat."

"......"

"Watching that bastard eat made me hungry too," Scarlet said, pulling her toward the still-burning campfire. "Hey, he said it before, didn't he? Something about an apple tree before the apocalypse... I don't know about any apple trees, but I'm gonna eat my fill of meat."

"......"

A small sound like that was heard. Teresa flinched in surprise and covered her stomach with both hands, while the Black Dragon Knight let out a sneeze loud enough for everyone to hear. Scarlet clicked her tongue as she watched him.

"Putting on a show, what a showoff."

"...Ahem..." The Black Dragon Knight averted his gaze and cleared his throat.
