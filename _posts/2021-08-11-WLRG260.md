He called it a game, yet the Spear King's charge held none of the playfulness the word implied. Only his laughing expression seemed to insist that this was nothing more than sport.

He came on like an angry bull, horns lowered. In each hand, he held a short spear, both extended straight forward. Pure white enhanced qi quivered at their tips, forming like shimmering soap bubbles.

Lee Sungmin slid his left foot back, steadying his stance before swinging the spear in his hands in a wide, diagonal arc to block the Spear King's approach.

KWAAANG!

The short spear in the Spear King's left hand struck first. It shot out in a straight line and collided with Lee Sungmin's sweeping weapon. The impact jolted up his arms, pushing him back. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. A single spear thrown with one hand carried the force of one swung with two.

"Hahaha!"

With a cheerful laugh, the Spear King shot the spear in his other hand forward, aiming for Lee Sungmin's chest. Lee Sungmin spun his faltering weapon a half turn, deflecting the short spear that came for his heart. He added his body's rotation to the spear's movement, compensating for his lack of power.

"……"

Pivoting with his spear, Lee Sungmin swung the butt of the Changjun toward the Spear King's body. The weapons the Spear King wielded were short, only slightly longer than swords. In a blur of motion, the Spear King's hands crossed. With a distinct locking sound, the two short spears combined into a single, longer weapon.

The Spear King shifted his feet to the side, rotating the combined spear to block Lee Sungmin's attack. He then kicked off his knees and leaped lightly backward. Before he even landed, he took a shallow breath. In that instant, Lee Sungmin saw the single thrust of the spear become hundreds of stars.

The stars exploded at a short distance. The descending spear became a meteor shower of hundreds of strikes, just as the Spear King had said it would. His spear was faster, more cunning, sharper, and heavier than the hidden weapons of the Dark Supreme.

Lee Sungmin dragged his feet back to create distance, unleashing the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Homing Chain Lightning Flash. Blood Ring Shattering.

KWAKWAKWANG!

The two spears clashed in mid-air. An exchange of Extreme Speed collided relentlessly, leaving no room to breathe.

The Spear King was the first to withdraw his weapon. He pulled his spear back and kicked off the air with both feet as immense power gathered in the weapon he had drawn back. He unleashed a single thrust, yet the power contained within it was comparable to the entire meteor shower his spear had just unleashed.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear one strike, Soul Chasing Annihilation Lightning.

The purple lightning crackled, coalescing into a single, massive bolt.

KWAAAANG!

Enhanced qi and purple lightning scattered in all directions, scarring the earth deeply. The Spear King laughed again and raised his spear to head height.

KWAAAANG!

When he slammed the spear down, the very ground split in two.

*His movements are good.* So fast that if I hadn't been on guard, I would have lost track of his presence. *Is this the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?*

The Spear King moved without hesitation. Lee Sungmin, having used Gale Thunder to escape the strike, had already anticipated the sudden attack. He gathered power, his spear held close to his waist.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear: Third Form, Crouching Snake Soul Devouring: Inverse Lightning.

"……"

The moment the violet light flashed, the Spear King felt a sharp killing intent aimed at the nape of his neck. He hurriedly bent his waist, leaning his entire upper body back.

BBAJIJIK!

The chest of his martial arts uniform was torn open in a long gash. The spear had twisted upward at an impossible angle, an attack that far exceeded the normal range of motion for a joint, and it was true. Serpent Soul Devouring was an upward strike from below, and the variations possible within such a limited movement were finite.

That would be true for an ordinary body, but Lee Sungmin ignored his shattered joints and muscles, executing a movement that defied martial arts theory. His arm had already finished regenerating before he could even feel the pain.

The Spear King frowned as he watched Lee Sungmin's arm moving perfectly fine.

*I thought something was strange.* Is his body not that of a human? The thought only made the Spear King more delighted. It meant he was sturdy. It meant he would not die easily. Baring his teeth in a grin, the Spear King split his weapon back into two short spears and charged.

Deadly Flash Lightning.

A definite killing intent shot out from within the flash of light that defied martial arts theory. The Spear King did not dodge. He met the Deathly Flash head-on, the movement of his parrying spear containing the very essence of Ran's martial arts theory.

Caught in the rotation, Lee Sungmin held fast to his spear and stepped forward. That very motion became the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. One step, two. Shadowless Soul Severing Three Forms, Two Step Lightning Annihilation.

KWAREUREUREUNG!

The thunderclap generated by his two steps swept through the area. The Spear King burst into joyful laughter.

"It's not just your spearmanship that's good!"

He did not retreat. He swung the spears in both hands wildly against the encroaching thunderclap, his arms a blur as the rotating weapons moved in all directions.

KWAREUREUREU!

The twin rotations caught the thunderclap, drawing it into the Spear King's spears. When the seemingly endless spinning stopped, the two weapons, now wrapped in immense power, were simultaneously launched at Lee Sungmin.

He did not back down. He unleashed another technique in succession.

The Supreme Nine Heavens Spear Fourth Form, Nine Dragons Slaughter: Tribulation Lightning.

KWAAAANG!

The purple lightning covering Lee Sungmin's spear swelled dramatically, becoming nine dragons and a colossal bolt of lightning that could incinerate the world. A collision of this magnitude shook space itself. A vibration spread in all directions, feeling as if the world was truly collapsing.

*I don't have enough power.*

Lee Sungmin felt it clearly. He was the one being pushed back. The wall of enhanced qi filled with purple lightning was pierced. The Spear King broke through it completely unscathed, the smile on his face showing not a hint of hesitation.

"……"

A spear, shot out as fast as light, aimed for Lee Sungmin's chest. It was too late to dodge with Traceless Step, and the same was true for Gale Thunder. Perhaps the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman himself could have done it, but Lee Sungmin's Gale Thunder was still too imperfect for such a critical situation.

He had to block it. Lee Sungmin raised his spear and activated his Protective Qi Aura.

*Just as I thought. Let's see how tough it is!*

The Spear King's eyes seemed to say just that. He threw the spear in his left hand first, a perfect javelin.

KWAAANG!

The thrown spear, wreathed in enhanced qi, shook the spear Lee Sungmin had raised for defense. He saw the Spear King grip the remaining short spear with both hands as shimmering space coalesced around it. It was the insight of the Transcendent Realm.

*Does he intend to end this with one blow?* The space around Lee Sungmin shimmered, the distortion warping everything. A spear thrust in a straight line would have its path twisted before it could reach his defense. That might have happened if the Spear King's level was lower than his, but the Spear King was his equal, if not his superior.

Space tore. The Spear King's short spear slipped under the defending weapon and ripped through Lee Sungmin's Protective Qi Aura with far too much ease.

JJEEEEONG!

The spear met Lee Sungmin's armor. It was the same armor that had blocked the hidden weapons of the Dark Supreme without any impact, but now Lee Sungmin felt his body being flung backward. His insides burned as if they had touched a ball of fire, and he tasted the metallic tang of blood.

*This isn't an opponent I can afford to hold back against…!*

He was on a different level from the Dark Supreme or the Sword Saint. Lee Sungmin swallowed the blood rising in his throat and drew upon his inner arts. The inner arts and Demonic Energy he had not used until now enveloped his body. His eyes, which had been nearly black, turned a brilliant gold.

TUDUK, TUDUDUK!

He could feel his Black Heart pounding as if it would burst. It had been a long time since he had drawn out his Demonic Energy. He had barely used it while staying in Legro Forest, and it had been the same since. It was a power he had not even needed in his fight against the Dark Supreme.

Still, the Demonic Energy, drawn out after so long, flowed through his meridians as if it already knew what to do, sending power to every part of his body. He felt a sense of alienation. His muscles were different. So were his bones, and the very blood flowing through his body.

The Spear King felt it too. He had been charging to pursue the recoiling Lee Sungmin, but he froze for a moment and stared.

…Hwoook.

Lee Sungmin exhaled a long breath, then inhaled again. The violet enhanced qi covering his body now radiated a more vicious killing intent than before, but that alone was not what made the Spear King freeze. For just a brief moment, he had seen and felt it. A wave of immense killing intent that seemed capable of slaughtering the entire world, and the deaths of beings numbering far beyond tens of thousands.

He had only seen it for a fleeting instant, but the Spear King could not help but be momentarily overwhelmed by the countless deaths. Soon after, however, he bared his teeth and grinned. He did not know what it was, but that profound killing intent and ferocity felt exhilarating. The Spear King let out a laugh like a roar and charged toward Lee Sungmin again.

[Keep a firm grip on your mind.]

Void Monarch warned. It was the first time in a year that Lee Sungmin had drawn out his Demonic Energy so intensely. In that year, Void Monarch knew something had changed. The Demonic Energy that Lee Sungmin originally possessed was what Void Monarch's soul had held. Until a year ago, Void Monarch had been in firm control of it.

However, what about now? The Demonic Energy that Lee Sungmin had fully drawn out was not Void Monarch's. It was the Demonic Energy of Urmuri. Its total volume was comparable to Void Monarch's own, and having not been drawn out for a year, it had swelled to become even more massive. Void Monarch clicked his tongue as he felt his own Demonic Energy being swallowed by Lee Sungmin's. In the middle of the Mental World, he tried to find that unknown, enormous, and fearsome being he had seen in Urmuri, but there was no sign of it.

*Just what in the world is he?*

Although he had been with Lee Sungmin for quite some time, Void Monarch could not fathom his true identity.

The gold-tinged eyes of the Yokai flashed. The Demonic Energy dwelling in Lee Sungmin's arms, combined with his inner arts, had transformed his physical strength into that of a monster. When he swung his spear once, the surface of the earth was overturned, and the rippling waves of space became a violent force that crashed down on the Spear King.

The Spear King did not dodge. The two spears he swung and threw tore through space.

Just because he had drawn out his Demonic Energy did not mean Lee Sungmin had gained an advantage or become stronger than the Spear King. He may have closed the gap, but the Spear King's martial prowess had been far beyond his from the start.

He ran. The Spear King ran across the sky. Every time he moved his arm, a spear carrying immense weight fell toward Lee Sungmin. Lee Sungmin's eyes followed the movements of the incoming spears, deflecting them by swinging or thrusting the weapon held in both hands.

TAAK!

The Spear King dropped to the ground and charged in. To parry the crisscrossing thrusts, Lee Sungmin spun his spear once. With a dull thud, the Spear King's spear was pushed back. At some point, his two short spears had become a single long spear again. The lowered weapon shot out with a whoosh, aiming for Lee Sungmin's stomach. Lee Sungmin pushed his feet back and thrust his own spear to meet it.

JJEEONG!

Spearhead met spearhead. Lee Sungmin's muscles tensed as he pushed the Spear King's spear back with sheer force. He was being overpowered. The Spear King quickly acknowledged it. The long spear split back into two short spears. As he gripped one in each hand, Lee Sungmin seized the opening and fiercely unleashed his spear.

KWAKWAKWANG!

In an instant, the Homing Chain Lightning Flash and the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art's Bloody Pursuit descended upon the Spear King. The entire space visible to him was filled with spear tips brimming with killing intent.

However, the Spear King felt not a shred of fear. On the contrary, he enjoyed this menacing attack that sent shivers down his spine, an attack that seemed to offer no escape. He moved the spears in his hands without rest, displaying every possible variation of the weapon. Among the dizzying number of attacks, not a single one managed to graze his body.

The Spear King's strength was less than Lee Sungmin's. However, his skill in wielding the spear, that alone created an insurmountable gap between them.

After unleashing a barrage of enhanced qi, Lee Sungmin leaped backward. The spear began to rotate in his hands. Demonic Energy, inner arts, and even the power of the Dragon. All the power Lee Sungmin could muster covered his spear.

The Seventh Form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, sky-piercing. The rotation of sky-piercing, infused with the incantations of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, relentlessly discharged sparks of lightning.

"Hahaha!"

The Spear King tore through all the enhanced qi and spread his arms as he watched the rotation of sky-piercing. Once again, he had no intention of dodging. Dodging was not fun.

*Head-on.*

That was his only thought, to break through from the front.
