About four hours passed after I returned to the annex. For all that it was time spent idle, it flew by faster than I expected; the single clash with the Sword Demon was still etched in my mind, replaying over and over.

_He blocked my Breaking Flight Slash._

It was the ultimate technique of the Ten Armaments of the Great Phoenix's First Sword. _Breaking Flight Slash_ was a technique that brought certain death by discerning the angle and force of a descending sword and then redirecting it.

Of course, I had not fired it directly at the Sword Demon, nor had I executed it perfectly due to the difference in our builds.

_Even had I executed it perfectly, he would have surely dodged it._

Sword Demon Dan Jeonghak.

Had I faced him with the pure qi of the Transcendent Phoenix Style, had we held a duel of nothing but swordsmanship against my true self, what would the result have been?

Time passed in a flash as I grappled with the possibilities.

"Come outside."

As I sat lost in thought, a familiar voice rang from outside— low and full of dissatisfaction. It was Dan Jiho, the older brother of my fiancée, Dan Yehwa.

I immediately did as he bade; Dan Jiho walked on without a single glance back, and I followed him. As the Sword Demon had suggested, I intended to stay with the Martial Sword Corps for the time being. It was not what I'd originally planned, but waiting was the least courtesy I could offer.

"Mountain Toppling Strike!"

"_Mountain Toppling Strike!_"

I silently followed Dan Jiho into a spacious indoor training hall. Inside, about thirty men shouted the names of their techniques as they trained. They were the same men who had visited the Palace of Minor Light with Dan Jiho.

They saw us and quickly ran over to greet him.

"Group Leader, you're here? But..."

"Group Leader, why is he here...?"

They glanced at me, their eyes full of questions.

"Everybody shut up. The Young Cult Leader will be temporarily lodging with our Assassination Squad."

"What?"

"Does that make any sense?!"

Complaints erupted without hesitation. Their discomfort was palpable on their faces, and Dan Jiho looked the same.

"Quiet! It's an order from the Palace Lord. Just accept it and look out for yourselves. There are no women around, so who knows what he might try to pull."

What 'he' might try to pull? Am I the only one wondering what that's supposed to mean?

_Hah_— A hollow laugh escaped me.

Dan Jiho tossed out those words and was heading for a room connected to the training hall when I spoke up to him.

"We'll be seeing a lot of each other from now on. Shouldn't we at least exchange introductions?"

He turned around, smirking, and addressed the rest of the squad.

"Introductions? Hey. Who are you guys?"

Two members of the Assassination Squad turned and went back to their training spots without answering his question. The rest watched for a moment before quickly returning to their places as well.

"Can't be helped," Dan Jiho sneered, his voice laced with killing intent. "We in the Assassination Squad live on the edge, so we're hoodlums unused to rubbing shoulders with _Young Lords_. Don't trouble yourself— just keep your head down until you can leave."

He slammed the door, _CLANG!_, and disappeared into his room.

Young Lord...

_Pfft._ A chuckle escaped me. It reminded me of when I first joined the Wind Dragon Brigade.

> _'The Chairman's son? We don't know anything about that crap. If you want to play the Young Master, fuck on off to the Blue Dragon Brigade. Don't loiter in here.'_

The Wind Dragon Brigade was the most grueling infiltration unit, tasked with rescuing key figures trapped behind enemy lines, scouting battlefields, rushing into battle ahead of the main forces, and holding out until support arrived. It was a unit cursed with a one hundred percent casualty rate, where at least one person was guaranteed to be killed or injured on any given mission.

The Wind Dragon Brigade.

Notoriously rough, composed entirely of outsiders such as secular disciples and illegitimate sons.

When I first volunteered for the Wind Dragon Brigade, the world looked on with a mixture of concern and admiration, but its members glared pure death at me. In their eyes, I— the heir to the Phoenix Sect— seemed to be motivated solely by politics. How they despised me, back then!

The sight of these men brought back old memories.

_Tap, tap._

I walked toward them. Their sharp, cold gazes landed squarely on me.

As my fiancée's father, as her family members— if this is the punishment they choose to give me, I do not mind accepting it.

It iss the least I can do.

{sep}

_—Shining Light Cult, Sun Peak, Palace of Minor Light_

A meeting was being held at a small annex, its members seated in no particular order. At a glance they might seem like strangers keeping an awkward distance, but in reality, they were all individuals overflowing with confidence.

Lady of Bliss, Han Soso. Asura Blood Demon, Blood Asura. Rogues' Guildmaster Black Crow, Jaegal Tong. These were three people with enough power to shake the Tianshan Mountains.

The Blood Asura leaned against the wall and began. "His Reverence has gone to the Palace of Blades alone."

"Fufufu. They moved faster than I thought… though it was expected."

"With such rumors being spread, even the Sword Demon couldn't just stand by and watch…"

Jaegal Tong and Han Soso exchanged words as if they had clearly anticipated this.

"...You both knew?"

"Of course we knew. Ah, this engagement happened after you retired, Blood Asura, so you might not have been aware. But you still let him go alone? That's surprising."

"My Liege... is stronger than anyone. And I believe, if the Lord of Blades is the man I know him to be, he would not mistreat my Liege and would ultimately follow his will."

At the Blood Asura's answer, the corners of Jaegal Tong's and Han Soso's mouths curved. They thought the same.

"The Sword Demon, Dan Jeonghak. He has an upright character and is not one for political strife. Besides, they are to become son- and father-in-law. There's not much to worry about. The problem is..."

As Jaegal Tong trailed off, tapping the wheels of his wheelchair, Han Soso spoke up.

"The Palace of Murder, I'd imagine. We'll have to act before they figure us out. If they make the first move... we can kiss our sleep goodbye, can't we?"

She said it with a sweet smile, but the mere thought was chilling. If three hundred elite assassins tightened the noose around your neck day and night, nothing would be more nerve-wracking, and this was about to become a reality.

While not on the level of the Palace of Bliss or the Palace of Merchants, the Palace of Murder also had its own intelligence network. A perfect assassination required more than a single sword— a hawk to guide you to the target, a cleaner to tidy up the scene, and even a mole to hide you in a safehouse. There were those who lived on the outside, their identities concealed.

Therefore, it was only a matter of time before they discovered the Palace of Bliss's betrayal.

"An all-out war is unwinnable."

"I know, but what if Guyang Ryung is drawn out?"

"He's not a man who moves easily. Are you thinking of commissioning him?"

"The Parlor would starve for half a year if we did. He's the most expensive man in the Murim, after all."

"Then do you have another clever plan?"

To Jaegal Tong's question, Han Soso gave a sweet smile and took a letter from her robes.

"The Palace of Martial Blood has convened a Meeting of Palaces."

A Meeting of Palaces was the term for an unofficial conference held by the Lords of the Seven Hallowed Palaces. Any Lord could call for a meeting, and unless there were special circumstances, attendance was mandatory.

The Blood Asura was well aware of it. The Palace of Shining Light had always disapproved of these meetings, considering them a thorn in their side.

While he swallowed a groan, Han Soso continued to speak.

"It seems the relationship between Esen and the Palace of Martial Blood has completely soured. So they're probably trying to pressure the Palace of Merchants in some other way."

"What's the plan," the Blood Asura asked bluntly. He was not the type to use his head, but strength was a different story.

To this, Han Soso replied with a bright smile.

"We'll be catching two rabbits with one stone. What do you two say? Want to work with me on something?"

The Blood Asura and Jaegal Tong met each other's eyes. They soon nodded.

In the absence of Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso, the Palace of Minor Light also began to move.

{sep}

The daily routine of the Assassination Squad was simple: it began with early morning training and ended with late evening training. They did everything together; their slogan, fittingly, was 'The Assassination Squad is one.'

Meals were no exception.

"Get your bowls ready! Quickly, quickly!"

The large dining hall lined with tables was swarming with numerous martial artists from the Martial Sword Corps, not just the Assassination Squad. Each of them held a bowl, lined up for food, and then sat at any available spot to eat and chat.

I was no different.

Actually, they showed some consideration at first, but I refused it all.

> _'We will bring your meals separately. Please let us know if there is anything you desire.'_
>
> _'I decline. I will move exactly as you all do.'_

In the spirit of atonement, I decided to endure everything. I knew all too well that if I only half-committed, I would just become a target for their scorn.

"We're out of food. Come back later, or just eat the beef jerky."

"But it looks like there's plenty left in that container over there?"

"Didn't I tell you we're out?"

"Isn't it a bit unfair to be like this over food?"

"_So you wanna talk about being unfair now...!_"

"Very well. This jerky looks fine."

Of course, this was but the start, so the hazing was relentless. I just shrugged, pinched two pieces of beef jerky, and turned away.

"_Pfeh._ Does he have no pride?"

"If he doesn't like the food, maybe he should go to the Palace of Bliss and let the whores feed him. Why come here and be so entitled?"

Criticisms poured in from all over, but I ignored it all and found a seat. Two days had already passed, yet their scorn, rather than fade, had only grown.

_Scrape—_

As soon as I sat down, the martial artists around me left in a group, leaving me completely alone.

_Even so, I'm still the Heir. Isn't this going too far?_

No. I had to consider their feelings as well as Ma Cheonso's actions. If I used my paltry status to demand their courtesy, I could not call myself righteous.

Endure. I must endure. That was the right thing to do.

"Ah, my eyes are ruined. Shit. I've lost my appetite... ugh!"

_Clatter-clang!_

"Ah, sorry, I was just turning around—"

I tripped him on pure muscle memory. _Haha._ I offered a polite apology and, with a guilty conscience, began to tear at the beef jerky again.

Living with the Assassination Squad was frequently exhausting like this. To them, I was like a traitor from a fallen kingdom. Mealtime was tame by comparison.

{sep}

"How long do we have to entertain that asshole?"

In the training hall, a man with a sturdy build and a large, slanted cross-shaped scar on his forehead was the main one to voice his complaints, perhaps conscious of my gaze.

Hearing his words, a man with a slight build, arched brows, and thin eyes retorted crudely. "Does that idiot even understand what he's watching? He's probably having trouble just following along. Leave him be. Let his eyes feast at least."

Their names were Dan Taeyong and Dan Sigang. Aside from Dan Jiho, they could be considered the real powers within the Assassination Squad. In any organization, a hierarchy inevitably forms. They did not wear badges, but the atmosphere made it clear. The others gathered around these two, watching their reactions.

_It's best not to engage with guys like Cheongheo._

So I didn't react to their sarcasm at all. It was obvious that if I kept responding to them without using force, they would pick a fight over every little thing.

"Hey, rookie!"

Sure enough, their interest turned elsewhere.

"Yes!"

A boy who looked to be about fifteen came running from a distance, struggling to carry dozens of wooden swords in his arms. He was the rookie of the Assassination Squad, in charge of all the errands.

"Dan Yarang. Do you only come running when you're called? Look at this bastard, how long ago did I give you your chores?"

"Yes? No... that is..."

"'No'? 'That is'? What, do you feel wronged?"

"No, Senior Sigang!"

"Shit, who's your Senior?"

"I-I'm sorry."

"Are you? Then don't do it in the first place, you bastard!"

"......"

"Not answering? This bastard still hasn't come to his senses. Is the Assassination Squad a joke? Huh? Aren't you going to answer?"

_THWACK!_

Dan Sigang struck the boy's chest, and the bundle of wooden swords clattered to the ground; the boy staggered from the blow but quickly returned to a proper stance.

Looking at the situation, this seemed to be a frequent occurrence.

"Dan Yarang."

Dan Sigang called the boy's name softly again.

"Yes..." the boy named Dan Yarang answered with his head bowed.

Then—

_PWAK!_

—he struck the boy's chest again and shouted.

"_Dan Yarang!_"

This time, Dan Yarang was pushed back a good distance, but quickly ran back and shouted loudly.

"_Yessir!_"

_THUD! THUMP! PUNCH!_

Even then, Dan Sigang kicked him hard and muttered angrily.

"Let's get this straight. Should the prestige of the Martial Sword Corps and the Assassination Squad be tarnished because of you?"

"_Nossir!_" Dan Yarang roared.

Then Dan Sigang glared at him.

"Keep this in mind. You may be a newcomer. You may be a little kid not yet of age. But we don't go easy on _anyone_."

"_Yessir!_"

"Put the wooden swords away and bring the rest quickly."

"_Yessir!_"

Dan Yarang swept the wooden swords up, clutched them to his chest, and hastily disappeared. Dan Sigang and Dan Taeyong also glared at me before resuming their training.

_They vent their anger on someone else, even as the source of it is still going strong. Whether in a cult or a martial sect, it's all the same._

I laughed darkly in my mind. They were stopping just short of laying a hand on me. Their viciousness was different from the clumsy youngsters I had seen at the Bright Spring Parlor.

Above all, they did not fear death.

To put it nicely, they were rebels. To put it bluntly, they were bullies.

I shook my head and stepped outside.

Pay it no mind. Getting involved is out of question.
