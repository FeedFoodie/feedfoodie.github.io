The first thing Wiji Hoyeon saw when she opened her eyes was Lee Sungmin. He was crouched by her side, watching her intently from within the familiar confines of the cave. They had not moved. He had decided that hearing her decision was more important than making a hasty escape. She frowned at his unwavering stare.

"You're going to pick up a bad habit."

"What're you talking about? You just woke up," Lee Sungmin retorted.

"I think it's quite embarrassing to show my sleeping face. How does it feel to be the one peeping?"

"Peeping? You really have to put it like that?"

"It's not entirely wrong, is it? I can't have you getting into the habit of peeping at a face like this so soon."

Wiji Hoyeon grumbled and wiped the corner of her mouth with the back of her hand.

"By any chance, did I drool while I was sleeping? Or snore? Or grind my teeth? Did I talk in my sleep?"

"You were drooling so much I had to wipe it off," he said. "And your snoring was so loud I couldn't get any sleep."

"That can't be true."

"Right. Because it was a lie."

His expression remained perfectly straight as he spoke, prompting a soft laugh from Wiji Hoyeon. Seeing her laugh, Lee Sungmin chuckled as well, and for a short while, the two of them shared a silly moment.

"I've thought about it."

Wiji Hoyeon's laughter died away. She looked down at her fingertips, which she could barely move, and continued speaking.

"Until now, I've lived my whole life without ever knowing what it feels like to be helpless. I've never been in a position where I needed someone to protect me. In most cases, I was the strong one, but... not anymore."

"Do you hate it?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Of course I do. I have a lot of pride. I... I want to be stronger than you. Or at least your equal, but right now, I'm weaker. I can't even stay alive without your help."

"I don't mind."

"Well, I do," Wiji Hoyeon said, shaking her head. "I don't want to put you in danger just by existing. I don't want to trouble you either. So... I'm thinking of accepting the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's offer."

Lee Sungmin could not find the words to answer. A part of him wanted to grab hold of her, to tell her it didn't matter and that he wanted her by his side, but was that truly what was best for her? He imagined how she would feel, trapped in a state of helplessness while he protected her, and the words of protest died on his lips.

"How long did I sleep?"

"A full day."

"Then the people sent by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman will be here today. Goodness... I slept for too long. Why didn't you wake me?"

"You were sleeping so soundly."

"Hehe! So you were peeping after all, weren't you? While looking at my... sleeping face. What were you thinking?"

Wiji Hoyeon let out a low laugh. She blinked, her eyes fixed on Lee Sungmin, and slowly traced a hand along her own cheek.

"It's gotten a lot better, hasn't it?" she tossed out casually. "You said your past self didn't know my face. That you'd never even met, but... my past self was rumored to be a man, right? You know, I was quite conscious of that. I don't know the past you, but you know the past me... and I disliked that a little. So I think I tried pretty hard to act differently from my past self."

She had stopped tying up her hair. She never wore makeup, but she had secretly learned how, just in case she might need it someday. She deliberately chose clothes that revealed the curves of her body, sticking to outfits that would leave no doubt she was a woman. She had not artificially enhanced her chest, but she had seriously considered it.

"I wanted to show you."

*Why?* The question surfaced suddenly, one he had ignored until now. Time had a way of embellishing childhood memories, making even trivial things seem grand. Perhaps the memories Wiji Hoyeon held were packaged in the same way. Even so, thinking of them brought him a sense of peace, a warmth that spread through his chest. He had imagined it so many times. *What would I look like when we met again? How would I feel? What would we talk about? What would we do?*

"Answer me," Wiji Hoyeon said, looking straight into his eyes. "Am I beautiful now?"

She had learned to apply makeup but had rarely worn it. Now, she felt the urge to try. She had always worn pants, but she wanted to wear a skirt, just once. To comb her carelessly loose hair and... pin it up with a hairpin. *Yes. In front of you.* Wiji Hoyeon barely managed to swallow the words that rose in her throat. Lee Sungmin remained silent, just looking at her.

"I thought you were beautiful when I saw you a year ago, too."

"Hehe!"

Wiji Hoyeon laughed at his answer. She reached out the hand that had been tracing her face and gently touched the back of his.

"You too," she said in a small voice. "Ten years ago, you were just an unremarkable kid. Even to my young eyes back then, you were nothing special. There were probably guys like you all over the world... though you were a little peculiar. Still, I was happy to be your friend. In many ways... there were a lot of firsts. I was comfortable with you. It was easy to talk to you. Because you never tried to suppress me."

She quietly closed her eyes.

"Will we still be friends?" she asked.

What did she mean by that? A vague guess formed in his mind, leading to an answer he refused to believe. Seeing him silent, Wiji Hoyeon chuckled.

"......"

"No, it's okay."

Just as Lee Sungmin was about to say something, Wiji Hoyeon shook her head. She removed her hand from the back of his and let out another soft laugh.

"Don't answer."

Though the words were short, they carried a heavy weight. Lee Sungmin gazed at her face. She met his gaze without flinching, and for a long moment, they just looked at each other. He nodded with a bitter smile. Wiji Hoyeon smirked.

"The color of your left eye has changed. Were you trying to look cool?"

"It's not like that. It just sort of happened."

"That's a relief, then. That eye isn't very cool, you know," she said, her voice laced with laughter.

Just then, the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia returned from scouting the cave entrance.

"We have visitors," the Crazy Heavenly Demon announced.

"It's not the Murim Alliance's pursuit squad," Rubia added. "I think it's the people sent by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. We should go out and see."

"......"

Lee Sungmin helped Wiji Hoyeon to her feet, and together they exited the cave. Beyond a faint mist, lights flickered in the distance. Unsure if they were truly the Chairman's messengers, Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon remained on guard, waiting for the group to approach.

There were about ten of them. A group of people moved alongside a carriage pulled by sturdy warhorses, their formation suggesting they were protectors.

"The Coalition Leader sent us," a woman standing beside the carriage announced.

Looking closely, Lee Sungmin saw that they were all women. *Is it because they came for Wiji Hoyeon?* They were all masters, their outstanding skills carefully concealed.

"Have you made your decision?" the woman asked, addressing Wiji Hoyeon directly.

Wiji Hoyeon slowly nodded her head. Once her answer was confirmed, the carriage door opened.

"We will protect you and take you to where the Coalition Leader is," the woman said with a deep bow. "It may be a long journey, but we can promise you this. Nothing dangerous will happen." She then turned to Lee Sungmin. "And, the Phantom Spear. A message from the Coalition Leader. 'Don't come now. Come after you've finished your other business in the South.' And, 'Get a bit of the Little Heavenly Demon's hair. You'll need it in the South.' Those are the two messages."

"Hair?"

He had heard from Gwan Hu that he should finish his business first, but he had no idea what the part about the hair meant.

[You might need it when you meet a shaman in the South,] Heoju explained. [Your tasks are to learn how to use Demonic Energy from the Southern tribes and to kill Fist Honored One, who put the Little Heavenly Demon in this state, aren't they? If you use the hair of the cursed Little Heavenly Demon as a medium for a spell, you might be able to find Fist Honored One's location.]

The woman handed Wiji Hoyeon a dagger. Wiji Hoyeon took it, gathered a handful of her hair, and sliced it off.

"Don't go sniffing it or anything."

"I won't," Lee Sungmin replied with a straight face to her smiling remark.

He took the lock of hair, tied it neatly with a string, and placed it into his Subspace Pocket.

"We'll be able to see each other again," Wiji Hoyeon said before boarding the carriage. "Even if you don't come looking for me. Because someday, I'll come looking for you again. There are still so many things we haven't done."

*And there are many things I want to do with you.* Wiji Hoyeon did not say the words out loud. She forced a smile, as if to shake off her regret, and Lee Sungmin had no choice but to smile back.

"See you next time. Don't die," she said with sincerity.

Then she turned and entered the carriage. The inside was spacious and comfortable. Wiji Hoyeon sat on a seat far too large for one person and looked out the window, where she could see Lee Sungmin's gaze fixed on her. *When we meet again.* She raised a hand and touched her chest, feeling her heart pound unusually fast. She closed her eyes.

*What is this feeling?*

*Why am I feeling this way? When we meet again, will I be able to understand?* Wiji Hoyeon looked down at her powerless body.

*Not now.*

She did not want to go, but she had to. She could not endanger Lee Sungmin because of her own helplessness. Wiji Hoyeon rested her weak head against the window. With a jolt, the carriage began to move.

Lee Sungmin watched it depart. They had made a promise ten years ago, met only a few days ago, and now they were parting again. He could not deny the regret he felt, but he tried to accept it as her choice, something that could not be helped. He did not understand the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's intentions or why the man was showing him such favor. The Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena had been erased by Grandmaster Bulyeong, so the Chairman's interest likely had nothing to do with that.

*I have to find Fist Honored One.*

Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip hard.

"So the Phantom Spear killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear."

Chwi Geol's expression hardened as he reviewed the incoming report. He had met the Iron Armored Divine Spear a few times at Murim Alliance events. The man had always showered him with praise, calling him the young hero of the Beggars' Gang and its future leader. The Iron Armored Divine Spear was not only an outstanding spearman but also a high-ranking figure in the Murim Alliance, a master directly under the Chairman and unaffiliated with any sect.

*It was as if the Iron Armored Divine Spear went out there to die. Was it the Chairman's order?* Chwi Geol wondered, his fingers tracing the scarred stump of his severed arm. It was a new habit he had picked up since losing the limb.

Five years ago, the Murim Alliance Chairman had changed. The previous Chairman, Sage Myeongwol of Wudang, had stepped down. The position was supposed to pass temporarily to Great Monk Bulyeong of Shaolin, but he had refused it. The one who rose to be the new Chairman during that vacuum was the Vice-Chairman, Black Dragon Knight Ye Hu. He was a master who had learned various martial arts and possessed the superior physique of a half-human, half-Dragon. Though he was an interim Chairman, Ye Hu had earned the satisfaction of many martial artists affiliated with the Murim Alliance in just a few years.

*If the Black Dragon Knight ordered someone like Iron Armored Divine Spear to die... why?*

Was it because the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon was that important? That could be it, of course, but the death of the Iron Armored Divine Spear still did not sit right. With the Crazy Heavenly Demon and the Phantom Spear present, was there really a need to send a master of his caliber into a death trap?

Thanks to this, the Phantom Spear had gained an infamy that swallowed up his previous fame. He had been branded a demonic practitioner as defined by the Murim Alliance.

*Could it be for his sake... no, that's too much, but this turned out well.*

Chwi Geol clutched the scar on his severed arm. Silent Flash Baek Sogo. The face of the woman who had vanished a year ago came to mind. He remembered her crying out, asking why he had made her abandon her Junior Disciple. When Chwi Geol heard that the Phantom Spear had survived, he had secretly thought it would have been better if he had died. He hated the expression in Baek Sogo's eyes when she looked at the Phantom Spear, and the way she had rampaged, driven mad by grief.

"A demonic practitioner... a demonic practitioner," Chwi Geol muttered, pushing himself to his feet.

What was a demonic practitioner? He smirked at the thought.

"Use all available means to track the Phantom Spear. Don't try to harm him directly. The Murim Alliance's hounds have probably already been dispatched."

The Phantom Spear had killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear. If he was that strong, it was impossible to capture him by sending mere beggars. There was no need to catch him. Chwi Geol grabbed his flapping sleeve with his remaining hand.

*I don't know what the Chairman is thinking.*

Chwi Geol did not care about him. If the Junior Disciple he had worked so hard to save ended up dying as a demonic practitioner, what would Silent Flash think? He did not hope for Baek Sogo to return. That was too much to ask for.

However, he wanted to see it. He wanted to see how that Silent Flash would react to the death of her beloved Junior Disciple.
