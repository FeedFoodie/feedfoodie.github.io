Understanding dawned on him slowly.

The Sword Phantom stumbled back a few steps, the spear that had pierced his chest now protruding from his back. He blinked, his gaze dropping to the wound. Blood gushed from the opening, soaking the shear shaft of the spear in a torrent of red.

His gaze shifted lower, to the sword drooping limply in his hand. The red enhanced sword qi scattered into the darkness. He opened his mouth to say something, but only thick, red blood came out. After coughing up several mouthfuls, the Sword Phantom finally looked at Lee Sungmin, his eyes trembling.

"How?" he asked.

The question was short, and it was one Lee Sungmin was asking himself.

"......"

*How did I do it? I don't know.* It was a frantic question and answer session with himself.

*I know I'm weak. That's why I trained, and why did I train? Because I didn't want to stay weak. I didn't want to be ashamed. I didn't want to give up or hesitate. I wanted to be different from my past life, and that's how I got here. This is Lee Sungmin.* The experiences and thoughts that differed from his past life had forged the man he was now.

*Was this an insight? Did my heart's desire move my body? I can't be sure.* He had not experienced this kind of thing many times. When he manifested enhanced qi against Aine, it had felt more certain. There was a feeling of conviction. This was not like that. It was more like a cow catching a mouse by stepping backward, an unconscious act. It was an unintentional strike that had pierced the Sword Phantom's attack and impaled his body.

The memory of the fight was only seconds old. The Sword Phantom's sword had been faster than his spear. It had been shorter too, yet he had failed to use his reach and allowed the man to close the distance. He had been unable to block the attack. If things had continued on that path, his body would have been sliced in two.

Still, this was the result. His spear had pierced the Sword Phantom. Lee Sungmin could not comprehend the series of events that had led to this moment.

*What in the world did I do?*

"Kuhk!"

The Sword Phantom coughed up more blood, gritting his teeth as he swung his sword. The feeble strike was too dull and slow to threaten Lee Sungmin, but it was sharp enough to cut through the spear shaft piercing his chest. He staggered back a few more steps and gripped the broken shaft still embedded in his body, blood trickling from between his tightly clenched lips.

"You... what in the world...?" he stammered.

Neither of them understood what had just happened. The Sword Phantom had been clad in his Protective Qi Aura. Normally, even if Lee Sungmin's attack had been faster, it should have been blocked. That was how it should have been. It was only natural.

And yet, it was pierced.

"......"

"Keeeuk...!"

One thing was certain. The Sword Phantom had suffered a fatal wound. Martial arts theory stated that a vampire's immortality was heightened on the night of a full moon, but he was not a high-ranking vampire. His power came from being a master of the Chojeol level *before* his transformation.

Furthermore, he had never properly fed. He had desired an ageless body in its prime, and becoming a vampire had granted him that, but to covet the blood of other humans had been a difficult depravity for a man who had lived his whole life as one.

On his way here, however, the Sword Phantom had cast that humanity aside himself. He wanted to live. He wanted to go further, much further. When he passed through the gates of Mount Hua and killed Daoist Seongha, he had cut away the last vestiges of humanity in his heart. He had come this far by sucking the blood from the corpses he killed.

He had drunk a fair amount, but it was nowhere near enough to raise his rank. A wound like this, piercing his chest, would have killed a human instantly. He was only holding on because of the full moon, but it would not last long.

It could not.

This was no simple piercing wound. Lee Sungmin's spear had been imbued with enhanced qi, and that energy now churned within the Sword Phantom's body, destroying his blood and qi. His vampire vitality was the only thing keeping death at bay, and it was fading.

The wound was not regenerating. He had used his martial arts to pull out the spear, but the gaping hole continued to gush blood. He tried to press on his acupoints with a trembling hand, but then he just burst out laughing.

"Hoo... huhaha... hahaha...!"

The Sword Phantom, choking and spitting, raised a hand to cover his mouth, swallowing the blood that rose in his throat. His hand moved again, the trembling fingers finally pressing the acupoints to forcibly stop the bleeding.

"It feels like... tripping over a stone," he muttered, his face pale. The red in his eyes had faded.

"Yes... that's how it feels. I was running forward... I could have gone further... much further, but I fell. I tripped on a stone I hadn't even paid attention to..."

His blood-soaked lips twisted as he chuckled. Staggering, he picked up the sword he had dropped.

"However... hoo... huhuhu... it was a martial art... beyond comprehension. A martial art you could never perform at your level. Your spear... ahh. The spear I just saw, the one that pierced my body. It was faster than Daoist Seongha's sword, stronger than Daoist Seongha's sword, and quieter than Daoist Seongha's sword... ahh..."

The red vanished completely from his eyes. The pupils that returned to black were not a vampire's, but a human's. At the same time, a pure white aura erupted from his body.

"Show me... more," the Sword Phantom spat, his voice seething.

"That greatness I thought I could never reach. Show it to me again."

The Sword Phantom ignited his life qi. He had given up on escaping alive. It was impossible to recover from this wound anyway. Saving his life would require the blood of dozens of people, and killing that many in his current state was impossible.

That was why he gripped his sword and stepped forward. His whole body felt heavy, weighed down by the specter of old age he had not felt since ceasing to be human.

No, this was not the weight of old age. It was the weight of death, and with it, the weight of an ideal he had wanted to see but now never could.

"Show me. Show me you."

Despite everything, the Sword Phantom's expression was not one of resignation. He looked at Lee Sungmin with eyes filled with a brighter longing than Lee Sungmin had ever seen.

*I don't know.*

[There's no need to fight. The Sword Phantom has ignited his potential and is burning away his life force. If you leave him alone, he'll self-destruct on his own.]

*I don't know.*

[Haha, by the way... that was amazing. To think that you, Lee Sungmin, would actually win against the Sword Phantom! Did you perhaps achieve a great advancement in your martial arts through this fight?]

*I don't know.*

The Sword Phantom approached. Lee Sungmin looked down at his cleanly severed spear.

"......"

He dropped it, then took out a new one and gripped it tightly.

*I don't know. I really don't know anything. I have no idea what 'I' did just now. How in the world did I pierce him?* He could not comprehend the martial art the Sword Phantom spoke of, the one that was beyond understanding.

Nevertheless, he could not back down. He didn't want to run away. He didn't want to be ashamed. The Sword Phantom's eyes seemed to want to ask him so many things.

*I have no confidence I can answer those questions. I don't know what I should say., but Lee Sungmin did not avoid his gaze.

*Ah.*

*You're not some dirty and dangerous man who uses evil arts. Nor are you an Undead who despaired at his own limits and turned away. You were just someone who wanted to overcome them, no matter the means.*

*You're different from me.*

*You said you envied me. My youth, my talent. It's a lie. My youth and talent are both lies. I'm a twisted Undead, a hypocrite who only acted like he'd overcome his inferiority complex, a weak and small human filled with a selfishness that serves only myself and a self-satisfaction full of contradictions.*

Spear and sword clashed. Ignited by his life qi, the Sword Phantom's enhanced sword qi shone more brilliantly than ever, but it was slow. His dying body could not trace the sword path he desired.

Towards such a man, Lee Sungmin unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear with what could only be called cruel ferocity. He did his best. He fought to kill the Sword Phantom, and to give the Sword Phantom satisfaction.

It was an unsatisfying victory. *Like tripping over a stone.* That was what the Sword Phantom had said, and Lee Sungmin sympathized. In the face of the martial arts the Sword Phantom had reached, his own existence was probably worth no more than a pebble. The fact that his spear had pierced the man was nothing more than a fluke.

*I know. I know, and... I hate it, but I must not run away. Even if I can't be proud of this victory, I must not deny it.*

The Sword Phantom dies here today.

The man who was supposed to die at the hands of Wiji Hoyeon dies here.

Daoist Seongha, who was not supposed to die, was killed by the Sword Phantom.

*If I hadn't been here, none of this would have happened. With a self-satisfaction full of contradictions, I feel that responsibility.*

*I must also take responsibility for your death.*

He did not know where the thing the Sword Phantom wanted to see was. He had no confidence his steps could reach the end of the ideal the man sought. Nevertheless, he had to do it. He looked at the Sword Phantom's eyes, full of only longing as they drew closer to death. Those eyes, where the obsession he pursued to the point of abandoning his humanity was fading.

*I am the one who kills the man with those eyes.*

"Ha."

The Sword Phantom's sword fell. Lee Sungmin's spear was embedded in his abdomen. The man looked down at the spear in his stomach with a hollow expression. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath, staring into his eyes. The exchange of several dozen seconds had ended in his victory. The Sword Phantom, who had even ignited his life qi, was dying quickly, but it had not been an easy win.

"You..."

The Sword Phantom staggered and fell to his knees.

"In the end... you deceive me..."

Lee Sungmin's hands stiffened.

"Your spear... ah... the spear that killed me... it should have been more... magnificent than this. Faster... more so than Daoist Seongha..."

The muttered words were a curse. A lament.

"Why am I here..."

Lee Sungmin let go of the spear. He took a few steps back, watching the Sword Phantom with a complicated expression.

*I knew it would be like this.* The attack that had pierced the Sword Phantom was impossible for the current Lee Sungmin to replicate. The insight from that moment had moved his body from the realm of the unconscious, performing an attack that surpassed his abilities. To reach that level again, he would have to reflect on and refine that insight time and time again.

"...I'm sorry," Lee Sungmin apologized in a small voice. He could not give the Sword Phantom satisfaction in his final moments. *If only I hadn't fought him. If I'd just stalled for time and let him self-destruct... couldn't I at least have prevented that martial artist from spewing curses and laments at the end?*

"I... was not as great a person as the Elder thought."

*I was given expectations I didn't ask for. I tried to live up to them. I think I did... my best. I was just lacking.*

*Because I myself am too weak.*

"I... more..." the Sword Phantom's lips moved, making a sound.

Those were his last words. What he left behind were laments and curses. He was not satisfied with Lee Sungmin, not even in the moment of his death. To the Sword Phantom, Lee Sungmin had been nothing more than an unexpected stone to trip over.

The strength drained from Lee Sungmin's legs, and he collapsed on the spot.

"......"

His body trembled. *My weakness. My inadequacy. What if it had been someone else here instead of me? What if it had been Wiji Hoyeon, or Jihak, or Namgung Heewon? Could they have satisfied him? Could they have prevented that martial artist from shedding curses and laments in his final moments?*

He wanted to scream. That was how he felt, but he held it in. Even as he raged at his own weakness, he refused to cry. He felt that if he cried now, something important inside him would shatter.

"Junior Disciple."

The voice was close.

"You've grown up a lot."

A floral scent came from her.
