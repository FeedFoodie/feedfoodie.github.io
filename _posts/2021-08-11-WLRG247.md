The Spear King had already sent the thought transmission before Lee Sungmin could even try to move.

Having launched himself forward after throwing his short spears, the Spear King spread his now-empty hands wide. The two projectiles instantly reversed their course, snapping back into his waiting grasp. He spun his body mid-air and swung the twin spears in a fierce, blurring arc.

KWAGWAGWANG!

The Black Dragon Knight's face contorted as he threw his arms up to counter the attack. A protective aura of Qi flared to life, enveloping the black dragon scales that covered his limbs and transforming them into a pair of deadly blades.

Lee Sungmin watched, dumbfounded, as the two men tangled together like mortal enemies. In truth, it was a one-sided assault. The Spear King was relentlessly unleashing his killing techniques without a shred of hesitation against a fellow member of Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Black Dragon Knight could do nothing but continuously pour out his enhanced qi to resist the two spears that sought to kill him.

[Why are you letting me go?]

[I promised we'd fight next time. Fighting you here and now just didn't seem like it'd be any fun, so I was putting it off, but this damn lizard bastard is getting in the way!]

Though he said this, the Spear King actually considered this turn of events for the best. *I haven't fought the Black Dragon Knight yet. If I take this chance to fight him now and then fight Lee Sungmin later, it's like killing two birds with one stone.*

Lee Sungmin couldn't fathom the man who lived by his fighting spirit alone, but this was undeniably an opportunity. The Spear King clearly intended to let him escape one way or another. The Black Dragon Knight wouldn't just stand by and watch, but even as a half-human, half-dragon, he couldn't easily break through the Spear King's assault. Within Heaven Beyond Heaven, the man's martial prowess was said to be second only to the Martial God himself.

"You crazy bastard!" the Black Dragon Knight roared in frustration.

As the two titans clashed, Lee Sungmin seized the moment, his movement arts carrying him swiftly away from the battle. He still had no idea why the Murim Alliance Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight, had come here himself, but he no longer had the luxury of wondering about it.

{sep}

The surviving Magic Tower Masters gathered in the grim aftermath, their faces masks of misery as they counted their numbers. Most were dead. The only silver lining was that every Tower Master had survived, but the Mage Army sent for the subjugation had been annihilated. While it might not be a significant loss to the army as a whole, the senseless massacre of dozens of mages weighed heavily on their consciences.

"I told you, didn't I?" Scarlet spat. "This was a pointless fucking mess. A complete waste. We came all this way and didn't even get a glimpse of Kim Jonghyun's face."

KUUUNG, KUUUUNG!

A deep vibration shook the entire forest. Scarlet glanced toward the sound. "Seems like something's happening over there," she said, "but… everyone. You don't want to go over there and die for nothing, do you?"

The subjugation was a failure. Everyone knew it. The Mage Army was not the only group to suffer. The Mad Dog Mercenaries, hired for support, had also been decimated, yet Doberman's expression wasn't entirely bleak. His adjutants, Shih Tzu and Husky, were dead, along with most of his men, but he, the captain, had survived. More importantly, he had forged a connection with the Mages Guild. It wasn't a total loss. A mercenary group could always be rebuilt.

"What are you all doing?" Scarlet demanded, her gaze sweeping over the other Tower Masters before settling on the Green Tower Master. "Not going back? You want to stay here and get caught in the crossfire and all die?"

It was her promise to him that had tied her to this forest and this subjugation. At her blatant stare, the Green Tower Master slowly nodded.

"The magic of the forest… it was the Grimoire's," he said, glancing at Lloyd. "It's been proven. Kim Jonghyun has deciphered it and is wielding its power. This demonic magic is incomparable to our own."

"Are you saying we should just leave him be?" the White Tower Master asked anxiously.

The exhausted Holy Knight Commander spoke up. "He even summoned the Demon Race."

"So what can we do?" Scarlet shot back. "Should we all go sacrifice ourselves to stop him? And all die together? I'll pass on a dog's death like that. Besides, there's no guarantee we could even kill Kim Jonghyun and take the Grimoire if we did, is there?"

The White Tower Master and the Holy Knight Commander fell silent. Emboldened, Scarlet pressed on. "Retreating from here and waiting for another chance is the best choice we can make. To be blunt, none of us are so insignificant that we should throw our lives away here together, are we?"

Her eyes hardened as she met their gazes one by one. "And we have Gries, the Grimoire's twin magic tome, don't we? Just as Kim Jonghyun deciphered his book to unleash that terrible magic, shouldn't we first decipher Gries to obtain its power?"

Those words swayed Lloyd and the other Tower Masters. Though they were masters of their towers, they were not the ultimate representatives of the Mages Guild. That power belonged to the old elders, long retired from active duty but still the most outstanding and powerful mages the Guild possessed.

"Persuading the Council of Elders comes first, then," Lloyd said with a sigh.

Outsiders who didn't know the situation often mistook the Tower Masters for the Guild's real power, a belief that was only half-true. They were undeniably exceptional mages, but even the Green Tower Master, the eldest among them, was treated like a child by the old mages of the Council.

"For now… let's retreat," Lloyd concluded. "The Crimson Archmage is right. If we stay here any longer, we'll all just be throwing our lives away."

As he spoke, Scarlet cheered inwardly. The Green Tower Master nodded his head, agreeing with Lloyd's words.

While retreating to leave the forest, the mages encountered the warriors of the Moyong Clan and the Murim Alliance. Tang Ahee had fled long ago, and Chwi Geol, leader of the Pure White Martial Squad, had also departed, using the Chairman's orders as an excuse. This left his squad wandering without a commander, and they had fallen in line behind Moyong Daeun.

"Are you leaving the forest?" Moyong Daeun asked.

Lloyd nodded in response. "The subjugation has failed. We've already suffered many sacrifices and intend to prevent any more by leaving this forest."

"Did you not meet the Phantom Spear?"

Kim Jonghyun's fate was of no concern to Moyong Daeun. He questioned Lloyd while looking straight at him. Lloyd's brow furrowed at the question.

"We did not meet him."

"Are you not lying?" Moyong Daeun pressed. He knew of some promise between Lloyd and Lee Sungmin and was openly suspicious.

At his question, Lloyd's eyebrow twitched. His opponent might be the patriarch of the prestigious Moyong Clan, but Lloyd was the master of the Golden Magic Tower, the most powerful of them all. He was not in a position to bow down to Moyong Daeun.

"Are you doubting me?"

"Golden Magic Tower Master, did you not admit yourself that you have a connection with the Phantom Spear?"

"Don't bring the karma of the murim to me, a mage, and question me about it."

"My daughter is dead." A chilling killing intent radiated from Moyong Daeun's eyes. "You'd best watch your words."

"You are the one who should be watching your words, not me," Lloyd retorted, refusing to back down. The ends of his golden hair began to tremble and float upwards slightly.

Deciding further questions were pointless, Moyong Daeun's gaze shifted to Scarlet. "Hand over the Crimson Archmage."

At his words, Scarlet let out a hollow laugh. "What is that old man blabbering about?"

"What kind of nonsense are you spouting right now?" Lloyd asked back, feeling much the same.

However, Moyong Daeun did not back down. "If we hold the Crimson Archmage captive, the Phantom Spear will surely appear. We may have our own positions, but to me, avenging my daughter is the most important thing in the world."

"So… you're telling me to hand the Crimson Archmage over to you?"

"I will not harm her. Once I deal with the Phantom Spear, I will definitely…"

"Bullshit," the Green Tower Master sneered. His wrinkled face contorted into a scowl as he strode forward, a short wand already in his hand, blocking Moyong Daeun's path.

"You've severely underestimated the Mages Guild," he said, his voice thick with irritation. "Did you think we were cowards who just lock ourselves in our rooms, flipping through books and scribbling letters? Moyong Patriarch. Are you not aware of how rude your actions are? If you wish to demand something from us, you should go through the proper channels and negotiate with the Mages Guild. What is this insolence?"

"Do not… expect reason from me," Moyong Daeun spat out, grinding his teeth. His hand moved to the hilt of the sword at his waist.

The surviving Moyong warriors followed their patriarch's lead, gripping their own swords. The warriors of the Pure White Martial Squad hesitated for a moment, but with Chwi Geol gone, the one leading them now was Baek Geomhak Moyong Daeun. In the end, they too gripped their weapons.

"If you do not cooperate… I may have to become even more rude." Moyong Daeun's words were a blatant threat that anyone could understand.

Just as an irritated Scarlet was about to say something, the Green Tower Master raised a hand to stop her. "If you thought we would back down a few steps because of your threat, you are mistaken."

Scarlet couldn't help but be a little flustered, as she had never expected the Green Tower Master to step forward to protect her, but he wasn't alone. Lloyd, now completely enraged, flared his golden magic power, and the White Tower Master glared at the Moyong Patriarch with a hardened face. In this situation, only the Holy Knight Commander and the mercenaries remained neutral.

"You force me to be rude," Moyong Daeun muttered.

"……"

The words had barely left his lips when his sword was free. In a single, fluid motion, the blade flashed through the air, aimed directly at the Green Tower Master in front of him. The old mage hastily tried to cast a defensive spell, but Moyong Daeun's sword was faster. It sliced cleanly across the Green Tower Master's throat.

"You crazy bastard!" Scarlet screamed.

His throat half-severed, the Green Tower Master staggered backward. Blood gushed from the deep gash, and bloody foam frothed at his lips.

"Truly insane."

The Green Tower Master's mouth opened. The wound healed in an instant. Even without an Elixir, the sliced flesh sealed itself shut cleanly, without a trace. He spat a mouthful of blood onto the ground and glared at Moyong Daeun. The lifelong wish the Green Tower Master had dedicated himself to achieving was the magic of immortality. He had not perfected it, but among all mages, he was the closest a human could get to achieving it.

"It wasn't too late before, but now it is," the Green Tower Master declared. "Thanks to your idiotic actions and barbaric rudeness, the Mages Guild has now become enemies with the Murim Alliance."

Although he saw the man's neck heal perfectly right before his eyes, Moyong Daeun was not surprised in the slightest.

"What does that have to do with me."

Moyong Daeun's expression turned to ice. "The Murim Alliance or whatever, it's not important to me."
