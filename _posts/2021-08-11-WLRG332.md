"When you think about it, it's both," Lee Sungmin said, his gaze shifting to Yana, who stood just behind him. "To be unorthodox towards Scarlet, and to get the Fairy Horse back."

Yana, who did not understand the situation, simply tilted her head in confusion. It was the first time she and Scarlet had ever met. Scarlet's eyes finally found Yana behind Lee Sungmin, and a dry laugh escaped her lips.

"So you've already snagged another woman in the meantime."

"What do you mean, snagged?"

"Am I wrong?"

"She's someone who has offered to help me with this matter," he clarified.

"I am Yana of Urmuri," Yana introduced herself with a polite nod.

Scarlet repeated the name to herself, *Yana... Yana*, and then her eyes widened in recognition.

"The Nine-Tailed Fox of Urmuri?"

"Yes," Yana confirmed. "And you are... the Crimson Sage?"

At the question, Scarlet's eyebrow twitched. She let out a deep sigh and covered her face with both hands.

"It's not a nickname I'm particularly fond of. What the hell is a Crimson Sage? How old do you think I am..."

"What did you do to get a nickname like that?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"What did I do?" she retorted. "I left the Mages Guild and built a small Magic Tower near this forest. I took in a few guys who wanted to learn and taught them magic."

It was a story similar to the one from his past life. Back then, when Lee Sungmin was twenty-seven and shortly before his death, Scarlet had established her own unique magic system, the Reshir School, and earned the title of archmage. The main differences this time were that it had taken her ten years longer than it should have, and that she had left the Mages Guild to do it.

"I hate that nickname," Scarlet insisted, "so please don't call me that."

A small question surfaced in Lee Sungmin's mind. *How old is Scarlet?* After all, his own physical age was now thirty-seven, and he knew she was older than him. He was curious enough to ask, but he suspected she would not answer meekly if he did.

{sep}

"Took you long enough."

A sphere of light gathered not far away. As the brilliant glow subsided, Osla appeared from within it, her butterfly wings spread wide as she looked at Lee Sungmin.

"Did you know?" he asked.

"I knew," she replied.

His question carried multiple meanings, but Osla nodded without hesitation.

"What kind of being you are... I've seen through it for a long time."

"You said that in the end, the choice was mine," Lee Sungmin pressed. "I made this choice. Are you still favorable towards me?"

"I've always been on your side," Osla said with a sigh. "If you become a monster, I'll be on the side of the monster you've become. If you don't become a monster... I'm still on your side."

It was just as he had thought. Her position had not changed. If Osla had sided with the Slaughter Predator, she would never have performed the Sealing for him.

Osla slowly moved her wings and drifted closer. When she suddenly held out her hand, Lee Sungmin took it without being flustered.

"I see," she murmured, nodding. "It's completely vanished. I spoke of choice, but honestly, I wasn't sure this result would come to pass."

"Then why?"

"It was an old promise."

A small light formed on Osla's hand as she held his, and her memories began to flow into his mind. Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched. He now clearly understood the promise Osla had mentioned several times before.

The Spirit Queen and the Fairy Queen existed in this world as transcendents in the truest sense, but strictly speaking, they were not its inhabitants. Countless Elementals and Fairy Realms existed across all dimensions, and likewise, there were countless queens and kings who ruled over them.

Whenever the great research area called Eria closed and a new one opened, a different Spirit Queen and Fairy Queen would connect with it. This cycle had been repeated several times due to The End.

[We coexist with this world,] Osla's voice echoed in his mind. It was a relationship not unlike that of the Great Demon World, the Demon God, and the Demon Lords. A Demon God existed in the Great Demon World, and the Demon Lords born from it divided and ruled that realm. Those who advanced into other dimensions then ruled the Demon Realms there. The same principle applied to the Fairy Realm and the Elementals. The ones existing in Eria were merely dispatched to this world for a short time by a being greater than their queen.

[We must not try to change the fate of this world, but the Spirit Queen broke that promise.]

Envirus should have died at that moment, but the Spirit Queen had intervened.

*Didn't you break that promise too?* Lee Sungmin thought.

[That's right,] Osla admitted with a bitter smile. [When I said I was favorable towards you... I said that because you carried the fate of the Slaughter Predator, one of The End. You were destined to become the Slaughter Predator no matter what.]

At that point, her actions had not been an attempt to change fate. That changed, however, when the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman died and Lee Sungmin decided to inherit his power.

[In that moment, I broke the promise,] she confessed. [I tried to change fate. If this world comes to an end, I will pay the price for it.]

*...A choice.*

[That's right. I made that choice. Just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chose, and as you chose. I'm not so different from the Spirit Queen. Just as she tried to change fate because she loved a human, I did the same.]

*...You... Master...*

Osla simply smiled, offering no answer.

*The Spirit Queen has fallen into a long slumber,* Lee Sungmin recalled. *They say she will awaken soon, but... is that long sleep the price for breaking the promise?*

[It's a penalty for leaving her own domain. Just as I do not leave the Fairy Forest, she should not leave the Elemental Realm.]

The Spirit Queen had not yet paid the price for breaking the promise. Just like Osla, the price she would have to pay would come after this world ended.

*Do you want to stop The End?*

[The Coalition Leader wished for that, and he died for it, didn't he?]

Osla let go of his hand. With that, Lee Sungmin now clearly understood her intentions and her position. She gave an awkward smile and shook her head lightly, and he pretended not to see the single teardrop that formed at the corner of her eye before scattering into nothing.

"I'll give you back the Fairy Horse," Osla said, changing the subject. "Scarlet kept telling me to take it, so I just held onto it for a while."

"Hmph," Scarlet pouted. For her, it had been an act of revenge for him leaving her behind ten years ago. Since he was the one who had ignored her wishes and left, Lee Sungmin said nothing in response to her complaint.

"I'm going to the North," he announced.

"Are you going to abandon me again?" Scarlet snapped.

"Do you want to come with me?"

"I can't," she grumbled, scratching her head roughly. "My research is in its final stages. If I leave my post for even a moment, everything I've accomplished will be ruined."

"I understand."

He had no intention of forcing her. He could feel, even at a glance, that Scarlet had reached a level incomparable to ten years ago. The incident in Germud had taught him many things, especially how dangerous a skilled mage could be.

This time, however, he could not receive her help. Instead, he had Yana. This matter might even lead to an all-out war with the Predators, and Yana's power, which had so effortlessly torn the heart from the Red Ghost, would be a great help.

*Please.*

Lee Sungmin summoned the Fairy Horse. He mounted it immediately and extended a hand to Yana. Once she was settled behind him, he focused his thoughts on Cheped, a city in the North. Baek Sogo had vanished after last being seen there. If he went to Cheped, he might find out what happened to her.

*Please, let me not face the worst possible outcome.*

The Fairy Horse leaped through space.

The northern wind, felt for the first time in a decade, was still chilling. Lee Sungmin jumped off the horse as soon as they arrived, his feet landing on a vast snowfield. In the distance, he saw the gates of Cheped. In his memory, the city was a stop on the road to Trabia, free from the rule of Vampires or Lycanthropes.

That was no longer the case.

He had learned about Cheped from Nebul. Ten years ago, the Vampire Queen, who had long been silent at the edge of the North, began to move. The Vampires, once confined to the darkness of Trabia, seized entire cities. At the same time, the Lycanthropes under the Mad Wolf Ju Won took control of the northern cities near Trabia.

Cheped was no exception. A Lycanthrope named Nero, one of Ju Won's trusted subordinates, now ruled it. Originally, the city had been under Ju Won's direct control, but he had disappeared about half a year ago, leaving Nero to take his place.

That detail only made Lee Sungmin more anxious. He did not know how strong this Nero was, but with Baek Sogo's skills as a Transcendent Realm master, she should have had no trouble even if she fought him, but what if her opponent had been Ju Won? Why did Ju Won disappear? And why Baek Sogo? The anxiety was endless. Lee Sungmin lowered his tightly clenched fist and headed for the city gates.

"Are you going straight through the front?" Yana asked from behind him.

Lee Sungmin nodded without looking back.

"Won't it be dangerous?"

"Unless Jeniela or Ju Won are in that city, stopping me is impossible."

It was an arrogant statement, but for him, it was not arrogance.

The North was a barren land. After the Predators began their vigorous activities, the orthodox murim had completely retreated. The sects and families that had once based themselves there did so because they wanted no part in a fight against the Predators. Thanks to this, no orthodox murim remained in Cheped, which could be called the very front line of the North. The name of the Predators had made even the ubiquitous beggars retreat.

That did not mean the city had become a lawless zone. The Lycanthropes who occupied Cheped did nothing. They did not interfere in the city's affairs, nor did they oppress the residents. They simply existed. That was not to say they didn't claim Cheped as their territory. Information sects and guilds, including the Beggars' Gang, had tried to re-enter the city several times, but any martial artists who entered with such intentions all returned as corpses.

The Lycanthropes standing before the gate and on the walls looked down at the approaching pair. They hesitated, unsure how to react, because neither Lee Sungmin nor Yana felt human at all.

"Who are you?" one of the Lycanthrope guards finally called out.

Lee Sungmin briefly considered the possibility that they might answer honestly if he simply asked them what he wanted to know.

[Like hell they will,] the Void Monarch sneered in his mind.

Lee Sungmin agreed.

Before Yana could even react, his body became a streak of lightning.

CRACKLE!

A belated crackle of sound followed as the Lycanthropes guarding the gate spewed blood and tumbled to the ground. Lee Sungmin did not stop, leaping into the air toward the ramparts. There, other Lycanthropes stared blankly, still unable to grasp the situation. He slowly aimed the spear held in both hands downward.

"Clean," Yana muttered.

Standing on the wall, Lee Sungmin shook the blood from his spear tip and dropped down from the rampart. He passed through the gate without hesitation. The guards near the gate, who appeared to be human, wore bewildered expressions as the commotion began. A Dragon's Roar spread out from Lee Sungmin, knocking them all unconscious.

"First," he said, "let's go meet this guy named Nero."

Yana nodded.
