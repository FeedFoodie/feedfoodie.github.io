Jeon Oh’s behavior was completely different from Do Heon’s. His stiff face and awkward gait clearly showed how tense he was.

Only half a day had passed since Jeon Oh paraded Do Heon around the Inner Court to humiliate him. Now, escorting Lee Cheonsang, he had no choice but to take the shortest route. This was a direct order from the Director, who was like the heavens to him.

*Damn it, what the hell is going on?*

The order was to bring the demonic gladiator from the Bright Demon Pavilion's private room. When Jeon Oh had received that order, he had rejoiced. He had not known the details, simply assuming the Director had found a weakness in Do Heon.

However, Jeon Oh then saw Do Heon emerge from behind Gong Muwae and Gong Muwae place a hand on Do Heon's shoulder and laugh heartily. Then he saw Do Heon bow his head with a smile. 

Jeon Oh had felt that something was terribly wrong. When Do Heon had looked at him with cold eyes, the shock was so great that he felt his breath stop.

*Don't tell me they've joined hands?*

That could not be. The Discipline Hall’s authority was among the greatest in the Inner Palace. Depending on the situation, it was a place that could make even the heads of the Three Divisions organizations beg and plead.

The representative organizations of the Divine Cult were the Two Palaces, One Department, the Three Divisions, the Four Armies, Six Units, and the Ten Companies. The Discipline Hall was not among them.

It was not because the Discipline Hall lacked qualifications. It was an organization so important that it had to remain separate. Although it varied, the fact that a mere Director could punish a Head of Institute from the Three Divisions was extraordinary.

*The head of such a Discipline Hall and the head of a mere combat unit joining hands? That's impossible. It could be a vertical relationship, but a horizontal one…*

At that moment, Jeon Oh's eyes shook violently. He was so shocked that he stopped walking.

*…Did Do Heon go under the Director?*

Just then, Lee Cheonsang suddenly spoke. "Is there a problem?"

Jeon Oh startled. The question sounded as if Lee Cheonsang was asking if it was strange for the Bright Demon Commander to go under the Discipline Hall Director.

"Not at all."

With a deathly pale face, Jeon Oh started walking again.

*No way… It can't be.*

Gong Muwae cherished his people more than anyone. However, he had one principle—those who are useful. Only those whose usefulness was acknowledged could become his people. Their value was judged by their usefulness, and the greater their value, the greater the favor they received.

Nothing was certain, but if Do Heon had really gone under the Director, and if his value was higher than Jeon Oh's, and if Do Heon wanted him cast out for how Jeon Oh had insulted him… If that happened, what choice would the Director make?

*It's a ridiculous thought. Do Heon doesn't have the personality to go under the Director. Even if he did, he's not stupid enough to demand a member of the Discipline Hall be eliminated. He would only be hurting himself.*

It was an accurate assessment. No matter how Jeon Oh thought about it, there was no room for disagreement.

*But why do I feel so anxious?*

If Do Heon had truly had a change of heart, it would not be unreasonable for Gong Muwae to be so pleased. He would be getting a good 'subordinate' instead of an outstanding 'tool'. And if a superior could give an order to a subordinate, couldn't a subordinate also make a request of a superior?

Just then, an ominous voice sounded from behind him. "Does emotion stand superior to reason…"

It was a tiny voice, so quiet that no one would have noticed it.

However, Jeon Oh whipped around in surprise to look at Lee Cheonsang. Lee Cheonsang had stopped walking and was staring intently at him.

The young man did not ask why Jeon Oh had turned. He simply gazed at Jeon Oh, unblinking, with crystal clear eyes.

"......" Jeon Oh shuddered as a chill ran down his spine. He turned back around and walked on.

*It's a useless thought. I'm just being paranoid. Even if Do Heon joins the Director, he won't be able to threaten my position.*

Jeon Oh tried to soothe his anxiety. As Lee Cheonsang watched Jeon Oh's back, a flicker of puzzlement appeared on his face, but it was only for a moment.

Lee Cheonsang began to interpret the scriptures of the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art. He had no intention of wasting time.

*The seven emotions originate from a human's five senses. Without seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting, and feeling, Joy, Anger, Sorrow, and Pleasure, as well as Love, Hate, and Desire, remain as intangible illusions.*

The Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art was a demonic art, but its origins lay in a divine art of Buddhism. Perhaps that was why. Even in the short process of interpreting the scriptures, he learned countless things.

*Among them, Desire connects with the other six emotions and stands as the representative of the five desires, but countless other desires are scattered about, and the paths to them number Eighty-Four Thousand. If used nobly, it rules me. If used basely, it ruins the First Company…*

Formless fangs appeared in Lee Cheonsang's clear gaze.

*If you discard them all, you become an Evil Buddha who shatters the world with light and receives the curse of all ages.*

Desire is not something to be discarded but to be controlled. Upon reaching complete control, one embraces an even greater desire, throwing oneself into the principle to augment one's power.

It was a principle befitting a demonic art. It was a vicious reinterpretation of Buddhist teachings, solely for the purpose of gaining power.

Lee Cheonsang engraved the scriptures of the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art into his mind. They were wrong from the perspective of the Buddhist sect but brilliantly reinterpreted from the perspective of a demonic art.

*Desire.*

A demon is desire itself. Without desire, one can neither obtain power nor achieve anything. 

If a human discards desire and becomes a Buddha, they ruin the world with light. If a human lives on as a human, they cannot escape the cycle of reincarnation. If a human reaches the state of a demon through immense desire…

If one truly reaches that realm, one can escape the Mandate of Heaven. Nothing in the three worlds would be unattainable.

*Do I have Desire?*

It was hard to say he did, and hard to say he did not.

What was certain was that despite having distinct five senses like others, his seven emotions were not clear. They were not absent, but because they were not clear; they felt alien.

Lee Cheonsang's eyelids, which had been shut tight, slowly rose until they were half-open. His appearance resembled the meditation of an enlightened one who had reached selflessness.

*The pinnacle of desire is to reach a result without a reason. Because the result is important, the reason is neglected. To move without reason, one must achieve the pinnacle of the seven emotions that stir my mind and body…*

As the Discipline Hall building grew closer, Lee Cheonsang fell deeper into the righteous demon that had corrupted even the Vajra Yaksha who guards the north of Mount Sumeru.

"Hmm, so that's what happened?"

"That's right."

"You're quite something. To be honest, when I caught your tail, I wondered if this was really happening. I had always thought that you live so stiffly."

"Isn't it because I lived that way that I've reached this point? But from now on, things must be different."

"Hohoho! Excellent insight. Now that I see it, you're quite a fearsome person, aren't you? You know how to handle yourself, so to speak?"

"Until now, I was sincere, so it couldn't be called handling myself. However, I suppose I'll have to do so from now on."

"Of course. I'd be sad if you lost what makes you ‘you.’"

"I'm merely humbled."

"Hohoho!"

Before long, the curiosity on Gong Muwae's face was replaced by pure joy. Who would have thought the day would come when he would laugh and converse with the stiff-necked Do Heon in an interrogation room? He was bewildered, yet truly and utterly delighted.

However, it is human nature to grow accustomed to one's environment. Gong Muwae gradually got used to this new reality, to having a capable subordinate named Do Heon under his command.

After some time had passed, Jeon Oh's voice came from outside the interrogation room. "Director, I've brought the demonic gladiator you asked for."

"Let them in."

CREAK!

The heavy door to the interrogation room swung open with a sharp sound. Through the doorway, Lee Cheonsang and Jeon Oh entered.

A peculiar light glinted in Gong Muwae's eyes. The moment he saw Lee Cheonsang, he was inwardly startled.

*Look at this one?*

Normally, he would have shown the magnanimity to wait for a greeting, but he could not. The moment he saw Lee Cheonsang, he felt the demonic qi enveloping his body.

*For someone from a demonic gladiator background, he's too…?*

The Demonic Coliseum was a place where those with dead-end lives, including criminals, were active. As such, it was said that escaping once you entered was nearly impossible.

They were used as fighting dogs because they were not strong. Even if one was a criminal, they would not be sent as a demonic gladiator if their martial arts were too powerful. It would break the balance of the fights.

In other words, if someone belonged to the Demonic Coliseum, it was safe to assume they had just barely surpassed the third-rate level. It would be a stretch to call even the most outstanding among them first-rate.

*But this one? The quality of his demonic qi is considerable.*

Unrefined demonic qi faintly leaked out.

He did not know what kind of demonic art Lee Cheonsang had learned, but its quality was excellent. It was not a large amount, but it would not be an exaggeration to say the quality of the qi alone had reached the first-rate level.

Gong Muwae was genuinely impressed. He looked at Do Heon with a broad smile. "The Demonic Coliseum Master is no fool. He's not stupid enough to put a diamond in the rough on a gambling table. How is he so sharp? He must have grown after meeting you, Commander Do."

Do Heon, too, looked at Lee Cheonsang, unable to hide his surprise.

WUUUNG! WUUUUNG!

A faint pulse of demonic qi leaked from Lee Cheonsang's body. There was no way Do Heon would not know the identity of that demonic qi.

If it were a scent, it would be heavy. If it were a taste, it would be bitter and astringent. It lacked the flashy, ferocious aura of most demonic arts, but its powerful quality boasted a profound presence within the tranquility.

There was no doubt. That was clearly the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art.

*Don't tell me you succeeded in cultivating it in less than half a day?*

It was a truly shocking development. A martial arts secret manual is not something you can immediately assimilate just by reading it. If it were that easy, the sects of the murim would not have been so strict.

And yet, while Do Heon had been summoned here, Lee Cheonsang had completely made the Vajra Yaksha Demonic Art his own. 

*Moreover, with this level of density? How on earth?!*

Just then, Do Heon became aware of Gong Muwae's gaze on him. He gave a short, dry cough and slowly opened his mouth. "It's surprising."

He did not bother to hide his surprised expression. It was something worth being surprised about.

"Hm?"

"I did teach this one a demonic art, but he's different again from before he came here. His growth is frighteningly fast."

"Hoho, is that so?"

"Yes. Just yesterday, he was still learning the True Demonic Art."

Gong Muwae's eyes widened like saucers. "Really?"

"That's right. I was planning to spirit him away because he seemed like a rare talent, but it appears he mastered what I taught him on his own while I was brought here."

"...Whew." A look of disbelief appeared on Gong Muwae's face. Although he was not a supreme master, he himself had cultivated a considerable demonic art. He knew full well that martial arts were not so easy to learn.

Do Heon quickly composed himself. "Offer your greetings. This is Director Gong Muwae, the head of the Discipline Hall."

Lee Cheonsang bowed his head slightly. "I am Lee Cheonsang."

His tone was incredibly stiff. His movements were also awkward and rigid. However, Gong Muwae could not fault Lee Cheonsang for his rudeness. His surprise had not yet faded.

Do Heon asked, "What do you think?"

"…?"

"Isn't he a decent talent to serve as the hilt of the sword that will replace me?"
