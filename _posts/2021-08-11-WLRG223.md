The dungeon that had claimed Lee Sungmin's life was not far from Behenger, the city that had once been his entire world. In his past life as a C-Rank Mercenary, he had never once ventured beyond its walls.

His journey from Legro Forest to Behenger had been a vast one, forcing him to travel without rest just to make it in time for the dungeon's appearance. Fortunately, his efforts had paid off. He arrived with time to spare.

Returning to the place where he had died felt strange for many reasons, though the current Lee Sungmin knew very well he would not die simply by entering this dungeon again. The power he now possessed was incomparable to that of the man who had challenged this place and failed.

The dungeon had not yet appeared, so Lee Sungmin sat down on the very hill where he had first discovered it. According to his memory, it had materialized just before sunset, when a red glow spread across the sky.

In his past life, he had been finishing a monster subjugation request nearby. It was a simple task to hunt goblins that were running rampant in a neighboring forest, a request that even a C-Rank Mercenary like him could handle without much difficulty. The sun had begun to set on his way back, so he had made a campsite here to spend the night. It was then, by pure chance, that he witnessed the dungeon being generated.

Dungeons did not exist from the beginning. They simply appeared in the world, and any lucky adventurer who witnessed their creation could either conquer them or sell the information for a high price. The inside of a dungeon was a different world where common sense did not apply. Though the risk was great, conquering one could yield spoils that, much like the dungeon itself, defied all logic. That was why information about them always circulated at high prices, and there was never a shortage of people willing to risk their lives for the prize within.

The Lee Sungmin of the past had been no different. He was twenty-seven years old, an age that felt ancient to him back then. To be a C-Rank Mercenary at that age was to live a failed life, surviving day to day on whatever money he could earn. He had no savings and no hope for the future, only the fleeting comfort of alcohol and prostitutes. If he only chose requests that wouldn't get him killed, his reputation within the Mercenary Guild would drop, and the jobs would naturally dry up. The Guild, after all, wanted mercenaries who would throw themselves into dangerous, potentially fatal requests and die. It was only natural. C-Rank Mercenaries were a dime a dozen.

That had been his situation, and that was why he had been so impatient. The moment he discovered the dungeon, he had challenged it with an all-or-nothing mindset, failed, and died, and that was why the current Lee Sungmin was here now.

The sun was setting.

A faint vibration trembled through the ground. Lee Sungmin pushed himself up from his seated position and slowly rose, looking down from the hill. A small ruin, which had not been there moments ago, had shot up from between the mounds of dirt.

"……"

He stroked the Ghost Spirit mask on his face and expanded his senses, a small laugh escaping his lips. He had not known in his past life, but he was not the only one here. Faint presences lingered not far away. Their skills were not particularly great, but they were individuals the old Lee Sungmin would not have been able to handle.

*Back then, I couldn't survey my surroundings.*

He had lacked the skill for it. They must have noticed him as well, since he had not bothered to hide himself after arriving.

So, what should he do? Wait for them to enter first?

No, there was no need for that. Lee Sungmin got to his feet and considered getting rid of them before they became a nuisance, but he quickly decided against it. They were not even worth the trouble.

He descended the hill without hiding his presence, as if to make a point. As he walked, he intentionally released his power. A terrible ominousness, suppressed but still potent, flowed out from him. It was neither inner arts nor Demonic Energy. He had spent a year wearing this mask, and with his primary powers suppressed, his body had developed a different kind of strength in the process of adapting.

The Dragon's magic power.

He had eaten an entire Dragon Heart, so he had held a little hope that he might be able to use magic. Unfortunately, he could not. Even the Dragon's magic power he could now wield could not be drawn out in its entirety. It could not even be called magic power in the first place. The energy from the swallowed Dragon Heart had been altered within his body, becoming a completely different power similar to inner arts and Demonic Energy.

[You brat. Don't be greedy. If you could use a Dragon's magic, it'd be a greater power than the martial arts you've learned. It's shameless to wish for that too.]

*Still, it's a shame.*

[A shame, my ass. You should be grateful to this Elder. It's thanks to this Elder that you were able to eat the Dragon's Heart,] the Void Monarch said, puffing out his chest.

Lee Sungmin already felt a great deal of gratitude toward the Void Monarch, though he had no intention of showing it. If he expressed it through words or actions, there was no telling what nonsense the old spirit would spout, drunk on his own self-importance.

[I can feel what you're thinking, you son of a bitch,] the Void Monarch snapped.

Lee Sungmin stopped at the entrance of the ruins. He could feel agitation from the hidden onlookers, who were flustered by the ominous presence he emitted. It would be nice if they understood the situation and backed off on their own.

*But they probably won't.*

A dungeon was a cradle of opportunity, an allure so powerful it made people risk their lives. Knowing that fact well, Lee Sungmin did not resort to any drastic measures to subdue them.

The stairs at the entrance of the ruins led deep underground. He remembered this. While descending these stairs, he had felt a floating sensation. When he came to his senses, he was standing in a single path with a ceiling blocked by a stone wall. It was a maze-type dungeon, filled with various traps. The past Lee Sungmin had progressed by squeezing out every trick he had, avoiding what he could and breaking through the rest with martial arts theory.

*I didn't encounter any monsters.*

Dungeons came in various forms. Some, like the Doppelganger Dungeon he had entered years ago to save Baek Sogo, were filled with bizarre monsters that defied common sense. Others had no monsters at all, but were riddled with physical and magical traps. Some were a mix of both. One could never know what type a dungeon was without entering it. If his memories were correct, this one was a maze filled with traps and nothing more.

The floating sensation came again. Lee Sungmin did not resist, waiting for it to end.

THUD.

When his feet touched the ground, he looked up. A stone ceiling hung above him, and a single path stretched out before him. He began to walk without hesitation, his expanded senses alerting him to traps long before he reached them.

There was no need to jump over them. Standing before one such trap, the inner arts, Demonic Energy, and the Dragon's power stirred in his qi center.

-CRACKLE!

Black electricity sparked, and Lee Sungmin's figure vanished. He leaped over the complex trap in an instant and landed cleanly on the other side. It was the seventh form of Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Gale Thunder, mixed with the insights of Heaven Reversal Black Thunder.

Lee Sungmin rubbed his feet where they had landed and glanced behind him. For a year in the Fairy Forest, he had trained in the martial arts given to him by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He had sometimes dueled with Wiji Hoyeon and had even tried dueling with the Chairman himself, though it was more of a one-sided beating than a proper duel. Those sessions, and the advice the Chairman gave at the end, were a tremendous help. Thanks to that, he could now use Gale Thunder, though not as well as its master.

[Shorter than I thought,] the Void Monarch commented indifferently.

*Controlling the distance is pretty difficult,* Lee Sungmin thought with a shrug.

Once he started actively using Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the traps became meaningless. The ones in the early sections were not that impressive to begin with. Even the C-Rank Mercenary he used to be had broken through them by fighting for his life, so there was no danger at all to him now, even if he stepped on one.

At a fork in the path, he chose the left, just as he remembered. A little further from here, he would find the room where he had obtained the Stone of Reincarnation. Obtaining it again would not allow him to return to the past upon death, but he planned to secure it first anyway.

His steps came to a halt before an old door. Inside that room, a small box would be waiting, and inside that box was the Stone of Reincarnation. Lee Sungmin took a light, deep breath and grabbed the doorknob.

He turned it and entered the room, his feet moving slowly across the floor. It was exactly as he remembered. A box was placed in the middle of the small chamber.

Without hesitation, he opened it with his hands. Inside, a single, fist-sized stone lay waiting.

[Is this it?]

"Yeah," Lee Sungmin replied aloud, nodding.

[It just looks like a normal stone to this Elder. You're telling me that just by getting your hands on that… you can return to the past when you die?]

"It worked for me."

[That's hard to believe. This Elder doesn't know much about magic, but magic that turns back time at the moment of death should be impossible even for a Dragon.]

However, it was an undeniable fact that Lee Sungmin had experienced it. He reached out and grasped the Stone of Reincarnation.

WHIR, WHIR, WHIR.

The stone began to squirm in his hand. From this point on, things were different from his past life. When he had grasped it back then, the stone had shown no change at all. He had not known what it was and had simply thought it was a rock, so he had put it in his pocket.

[The reincarnator has grasped the stone again.]

A voice echoed in his head, the same kind he heard when he was about to learn a skill.

[It has been confirmed that the reincarnator has returned to this place.]

Those were the last words. The Stone of Reincarnation, squirming in his hand, crumbled into dust and scattered. Then, the scattered dust gathered back into his palm. It was no longer a stone. Resting on his hand was a heavy, golden, shining key.

"What is this…?" Lee Sungmin stared down at the key in bewilderment.

Even the Void Monarch, watching from inside his head, was startled by the sudden turn of events. Before Lee Sungmin could even think about why the Stone of Reincarnation had turned into some unknown key, the entire space began to shake violently.

[Something is happening,] the Void Monarch warned.

Lee Sungmin moved immediately. He opened the door he had come through and stepped out, but he could not go back the way he came. Once inside a dungeon, the only way out was to defeat the boss monster at the end.

RUMBLE, RUMBLE, RUMBLE.

The entire dungeon was shaking. The path wavered, and the ceiling trembled violently. He had never experienced anything like this in either of his lives, and he could not help but feel tense. This was no time to be controlling his power, so he completely removed the mask from his face.

KRRRRRUMBLE!

The floor beneath him collapsed, and his body plummeted downwards. He did not panic and tried to suspend himself in mid-air, but there was no need. In the middle of the short fall, the dungeon completely vanished.

The floor that had not been there was suddenly beneath his feet. Bewildered, Lee Sungmin looked around. He was suddenly outside. The ruins that had served as the dungeon's entrance were collapsing right before his eyes.

[Well, it seems we don't have to go through the trouble of clearing the dungeon anymore,] the Void Monarch muttered.
