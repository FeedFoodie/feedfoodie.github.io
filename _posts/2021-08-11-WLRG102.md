"Phew!"

After draining five bottles of potion, Chwi Geol pushed himself to his feet. The color had returned to his face, and his expression was much improved. He rolled his shoulder a few times, a futile attempt to ease the awkward feeling of his left arm, which was now severed from the forearm down. He knew, of course, that no amount of movement would bring it back.

"Thank you," Chwi Geol said, bowing his head deeply to Lee Sungmin. With the pain fading as the wound sealed, a natural smile finally touched his lips.

Watching him, Lee Sungmin simply shook his head. "It was nothing."

"It was everything to me. I'd have been in real trouble without you."

He was right, of course. Jang Deuksu carried no potions, and Chwi Geol's own Subspace Pocket was lost. Though he had managed to stop the bleeding by pressing his acupoints, it was far from a perfect solution. His body would have eventually buckled under the strain, and in a dungeon where death could lurk around any corner, he never would have survived in such a weakened state.

In his past life, Chwi Geol had died. So had Jang Deuksu.

*And Baek Sogo, too,* Lee Sungmin thought, chewing on his lower lip. While he was lost in these grim memories, Jang Deuksu explained the dungeon's peculiar nature to Chwi Geol, whose face stiffened as he listened to the full story.

"Then we have to hurry," Chwi Geol urged. "Even if it's not Wiji Hoyeon herself, her Doppelganger will cause immense damage if it starts moving. It might even be more dangerous than the real Wiji Hoyeon. You can't reason with something like that."

The thought that there might already be victims made Lee Sungmin unbearably anxious. He had entered this dungeon for the sole purpose of preventing Baek Sogo's death. As long as he could stop that from happening, he didn't care if anyone else died. He had helped Chwi Geol only to forge a connection with the Beggars' Gang, but if it meant saving Baek Sogo, Lee Sungmin was capable of killing the man right then and there.

"Let's go."

Lee Sungmin moved first, with Jang Deuksu and Chwi Geol following close behind. He couldn't afford to rush forward recklessly, not knowing what might leap out from the shadows, so he fully opened his senses, probing the darkness for any sign of movement.

"Are you worried about Miss Baek?" Chwi Geol asked, drawing alongside him.

Startled, Lee Sungmin turned to look at him.

"Ah. I heard it directly from Miss Baek herself," Chwi Geol explained. "That you're in a Junior Disciple relationship with her. It was a year ago… when you killed the Sword Phantom."

"...What's your relationship with my senior sister?"

Chwi Geol tilted his head, confused by the sharp edge in Lee Sungmin's question. He couldn't understand the sudden emotion Lee Sungmin had revealed. In truth, Lee Sungmin didn't understand it either. Why had he felt a flash of hostility toward Chwi Geol? Even as the one who felt it, he couldn't quite grasp the reason.

"Ah… I'm sorry," Lee Sungmin said, recovering. "That was just an unexpected question. It surprised me."

"It's fine," Chwi Geol replied with an unconcerned expression. "These kinds of things can be sensitive depending on the circumstances."

A Junior Disciple relationship within a sect was hardly a secret, but one like his and Baek Sogo's, formed by sharing martial arts outside a formal structure, was sometimes kept private.

"You don't have to worry about Miss Baek," Chwi Geol added. "She's the strongest one among us."

"But she's weaker than Wiji Hoyeon, isn't she?"

Chwi Geol fell silent at that. Lee Sungmin said nothing more and turned his gaze forward. Just then, Rubia's voice echoed in his mind.

[The analysis is complete.]

*It took quite a while, though,* she added, her voice filled with pride despite her words. Lee Sungmin stopped walking.

"What is it?" Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu asked in unison when he halted.

Lee Sungmin ignored them, instead posing a question to Rubia.

*How is it?*

[It would be better for you to see it yourself than for me to explain.]

The moment she finished speaking, a translucent window materialized before his eyes. It was similar to the status window he occasionally checked, but its contents were completely different.

"It's been a while since I've seen something like this."

How long had it been? If Denir hadn't backed up his memories, he might not have even felt the pang of nostalgia, but thanks to that backup, Lee Sungmin could recall seeing something just like this a very long time ago, back before he was summoned to Eria. Back when he lived in Seoul, South Korea. Back when he was still in elementary school, playing computer games like any other kid.

It was a minimap.

A blue dot blinked on the flatly drawn map. As Lee Sungmin's gaze fixed on it, Rubia explained.

[That blue dot is you. This dungeon has four single paths, and they all lead to one place. The dungeon's boss monster is probably there.]

Every dungeon had a boss monster. Conquering one meant not only surviving its depths but also defeating that final guardian.

[Look.]

Two yellow dots appeared next to the stationary blue one.

[These are the people currently moving inside the dungeon.]

More yellow dots appeared, bringing the total to eight, including his own.

[And these are the non-human entities. Probably the Doppelgangers.]

There were not many of them. Three red dots blinked into existence. Lee Sungmin stared at the yellow and red dots, feeling a slight sense of relief that there were so few Doppelgangers and so many yellow dots.

*Did anyone else enter the dungeon?*

[Probably not.]

When Lee Sungmin had entered, there had been ten people inside. Wiji Hoyeon and her three followers, the five from the Murim Alliance including Baek Sogo, and himself. Now, only eight survivors remained. Two were dead. He had no way of knowing who they were. *Please don't let it be Baek Sogo.* He could only pray desperately.

Three Doppelgangers were on the move. Baek Sogo's was dead, as was the Phantom Sword of Dokgo's. That meant five other Doppelgangers had already been killed. Whose were they? He couldn't imagine Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger had been defeated. Other than Wiji Hoyeon herself, there was likely no one who could kill it, though a group attack might tell a different story.

No. He didn't need to think about the moving Doppelgangers right now. If he survived, he would meet the others in the final plaza, but if he didn't, waiting there was meaningless. In fact, that wasn't what he should be thinking about at all.

"There's someone up ahead."

The yellow dot was still some distance away, but it was moving continuously.

*Are there any magic traps in this dungeon?*

[Well… I haven't been able to check for that.]

"It seems there are no more magic traps," Lee Sungmin announced, unconcerned by Rubia's uncertainty. "Let's pick up the pace."

Inside his head, Rubia clicked her tongue at his bluff. Regardless, Lee Sungmin didn't wait for the others, dashing forward first. As he started running, Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu hurried to keep up.

The distance to the yellow dot closed rapidly. There was no need to get any closer to confirm its identity. While Lee Sungmin tracked its movement on the minimap, the figure ahead must have heard their approaching footsteps, because it stopped moving forward.

No. They were still a little far, but the silhouette was now visible. The person standing there was not Baek Sogo, nor was it Wiji Hoyeon. It was a short old woman in a tattered, dirty robe. Lee Sungmin had never seen her before, but he knew at once that this was the Blood Granny, one of Wiji Hoyeon's followers.

He had to make a choice. Fight her with Jang Deuksu and Chwi Geol, or leave her to them and go save Baek Sogo? In truth, it wasn't a choice at all. Lee Sungmin already had his answer.

[I'm sorry,] he sent to Chwi Geol through a Thought Transmission.

Chwi Geol shot him a surprised look at the sudden message.

[The Blood Granny is up ahead. Fighting her will take too much time… I have to save my senior sister.]

"There's nothing to be sorry about," Chwi Geol replied aloud, quickly grasping the situation despite his surprise. He offered a bitter smile. "I want to save Miss Baek, too. You going will be more helpful than me with my severed arm."

"What are you two talking about?" Jang Deuksu demanded, just as he too spotted the Blood Granny's presence. His eyes widened as he raised his internal energy. "Blood Granny!" he roared.

Lee Sungmin wondered if it was really necessary to shout like that. He looked forward again. The distance to the old woman was no longer great. Her shoulders shook with a dry, crackling laugh.

"Kekeke! The little brat from the Beggars' Gang has lost an arm… They say ignorance is bliss. The pig sure squeals loudly." Muttering this, the Blood Granny fixed her gaze on Lee Sungmin, tilting her head. "And who are you?"

Her voice was quiet, yet it was laced with internal energy that crossed the distance and struck his eardrums. Lee Sungmin ignored her question. Instead, he raised his true qi and accelerated his movement arts.

"It's dangerous alone!" Jang Deuksu yelled from behind.

Lee Sungmin ignored him too. He felt a little sorry for Chwi Geol, but he felt nothing for Jang Deuksu. The Blood Granny seemed slightly flustered by his sudden charge, but she quickly dismissed it and raised a wrinkled hand, which began to radiate a sinister aura as she coated it in internal energy.

"What a brave little brat!" she shouted, thrusting a palm strike toward him.

A direct hit would damage him even through his Protective Qi Aura, and he had no intention of breaking through it. Lee Sungmin leaped upward, evading the palm force.

"You little rat!"

The Blood Granny had clearly anticipated such a move. She thrust her other hand, already prepared, toward Lee Sungmin as he hung suspended in the air. In that instant, he twisted his waist, his body shifting in mid-air without a foothold. Just as his foot stepped on empty air, her palm force shattered his form.

*Teleportation!*

The afterimage collapsed. The Blood Granny flinched and took a step back as Lee Sungmin's real body closed in on her. She realized then that, contrary to his appearance, he was a formidable master. A thick Protective Qi Aura flared to life around her. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear toward her.

She reached out to counter the stabbing spearhead, but it was a feint. As her hand moved to bat the spear away, its trajectory twisted. He was tempted to press the advantage and stab at an opening, but her left hand had already finished its counter and was now lashing out, trying to grab his body. Lee Sungmin let go of the spear with his right hand and reached out toward the Blood Granny.

She was a master of hand-to-hand combat. He knew he shouldn't face her with his bare hands.

Knowing this, he extended his hand anyway. His right hand was dyed a deep violet.

KKOOONG!

A loud explosion erupted as their hands collided. The Blood Granny's eyes widened at the sheer resistance she felt. *This brat's internal energy is…!*

Though surprised by the unexpected weight behind his power, she was not intimidated. All Lee Sungmin had intended was to startle her. As she prepared to launch a full-scale assault, he didn't hesitate to throw himself to the ground, evading her attack.

"Lazy Donkey Roll! You bastard! A martial artist, and you do that!" the Blood Granny roared, watching him tumble across the floor.

He paid her no mind. After rolling, Lee Sungmin immediately regained his posture and dashed forward. Only then did she realize his goal was not to fight her, but simply to get past her.

"You brat!"

The Blood Granny tried to launch a palm strike at his back, but before she could, Chwi Geol unleashed the Eighteen Dragon Subduing Palms at her. She hurriedly changed the direction of her hand to counter his attack.

"Wha-what? Where are you going?" Jang Deuksu shouted in confusion.

Lee Sungmin left the shout behind him and continued to run. He glanced at the minimap. There was no one ahead. If he kept going, he would reach the final plaza. He couldn't do that, however. If he reached the end, the boss monster would appear.

*This wall. Can I break through it?*

After putting some distance between himself and the fight, Lee Sungmin stopped. He looked at the wall and posed the question to Rubia.

[Well… we won't know until you try, will we?]

Rubia wasn't omnipotent either. At her answer, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with both hands. He circulated the Violet Dawn Divine Art, and his weapon became wreathed in violet energy.

*I have to take a different path.*

He had to meet Baek Sogo before he ran into Wiji Hoyeon or her Doppelganger.
