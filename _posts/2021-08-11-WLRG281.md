An electric current ignited within his blood vessels and surged into his qi center, filling it to the brim. Lee Sungmin flinched as a low rumble echoed from the depths of his body. That renewed internal energy then erupted from his qi center, flooding the entire network of his blood vessels. A brief sting pricked his heart, and the sensation of his peripheral nerves standing on end forced him to sit up unconsciously.

*What is this?*

A purple light flickered deep within his golden eyes. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman addressed the startled Lee Sungmin.

"While tending to your acupoints with this one's internal energy and clearing out the minute blockages, this one has paved a path for your internal energy to flow at its fastest."

"You could have at least told me beforehand...!"

"If I'd told you beforehand, I wouldn't get to see you this surprised, would I?"

Even with the Chairman's explanation, Lee Sungmin struggled to comprehend the change in his body. The man had merely cleared his blockages and paved a faster path for his internal energy. Was that truly enough to advance his Heaven Reversal Black Thunder by such a degree?

"The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder you learned is a version combined with the incantations of the Violet Dawn Divine Art," the Chairman continued, clearly enjoying Lee Sungmin's surprise. "I made them compatible so you could learn it, but it's still quite different from this one's complete Heaven Reversal Black Thunder." Seeing his incompetent disciple look so shocked was a small pleasure for him.

"Do you know why this one is so strong?"

"It must be because you're great, Master."

"You know that well."

A pleased smile spread across the Chairman's face at the answer. He raised a hand, pointing first to Lee Sungmin and then to the Black Dragon Knight. "You are both in the Transcendent Realm, and this one is as well. So why is the gap between us so large?"

"...What are you trying to say?" the Black Dragon Knight asked back. Despite his tone, he unconsciously perked his ears, curious to hear the Chairman's answer.

"Body Reformation."

"Insane."

The Black Dragon Knight let out a hollow laugh at the Chairman's answer, shaking his head. "Isn't Body Reformation something every dog and cow goes through once they reach the Supreme Peak level? Surely you don't think we haven't experienced it?"

The Chairman sneered at the retort. "You don't actually think the Body Reformation that every dog and cow goes through is the *true* Body Reformation, do you?"

Lee Sungmin said nothing, but he inwardly agreed with the Black Dragon Knight. He too had undergone Body Reformation on his path to the Supreme Peak realm. The process had brought a much needed balance to his severely tangled mind, qi, and body.

"Once you ascend to the Transcendent Realm, when your martial arts and body can develop no further, you undergo Body Reformation again. In that process," the Chairman explained, "the body and martial arts advance to a higher, more transcendent place. At that point, the body far surpasses that of a human. Thought and action become one. Insight opens wide, and you can perfectly comprehend most martial arts manuals with just a single reading. Your inner arts never run dry, and the qi center becomes meaningless."

"What do you mean the qi center becomes meaningless?" the Black Dragon Knight asked, his eyes shining with interest.

The Martial God had never told him such things. As a martial artist himself, and after his recent one-sided defeat at the Chairman's hands, he found himself yearning for a higher realm.

"It means your entire body becomes a qi center. Do you understand what that implies? You no longer need to draw inner arts from a single point."

"Then..." Lee Sungmin's mouth fell half open. If what the Chairman said was true, then it was no wonder he treated the Black Dragon Knight like a child. It also explained why Lee Sungmin could never perceive the man's movements whenever he was spanked on the buttocks.

Drawing internal energy from the qi center, no matter how fast, was still a process, but if one's entire body became a qi center, that process could be skipped entirely. The difference in speed might be slight, but at the level of the Transcendent Realm, where a life could be taken in an instant, that slight difference created an insurmountable gap.

"It would be difficult to complete Body Reformation at your level," the Chairman said, "but while tending to your blood vessels, this one used his own internal energy to set the most efficient path for you. It won't even be a taste, but it should be fun to try once."

Fun. It was just as he had said.

As he unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear before the Chairman, lightning erupted from his qi center. In a flash, the inner arts of the Violet Dawn Divine Art flooded his body, merging with the incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder to forge the pinnacle of spear arts.

The sheer power of the unleashed spear surprised Lee Sungmin himself, and it also stunned the Black Dragon Knight, who had been watching with keen interest. The Chairman, observing with his hands clasped behind his back, simply nodded.

"How is it?"

"Can this much change really happen just from tending to my blood vessels for a few months?"

"Don't be foolish. The one who tended to your blood vessels is the pinnacle of the unorthodox sects, perhaps the world's number one, and possibly history's strongest, the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon. A change of this magnitude only occurred because someone like this one was responsible."

The words were boastful, and normally Lee Sungmin would have retorted sullenly, but he could not bring himself to do so now. He set down his spear and bowed his head deeply.

"Thank you."

"In that case, bring out the alcohol."

Without hesitation, Lee Sungmin produced the Void Monarch's gourd and handed it over. The Chairman accepted it with a satisfied expression, uncorking the stopper to savor the fragrant aroma. "There's nothing more to see here." With that, he turned and went back into the carriage.

Scarlet, who had been watching from the carriage window, remained inside. She took off the glasses perched on her nose as the Chairman entered.

"You take good care of him."

"He's my only disciple," the Chairman said, plopping down in his seat. He drank straight from the gourd, not bothering with a cup.

The sweet aroma tempted Scarlet, but she considered writing her life's masterpiece, the magic tome, far more important than drinking right now.

[I have a favor to ask.]

Scarlet raised her head as the Chairman's words echoed in her mind.

The public order in Trabia had supposedly collapsed after the death of the Blood Heavenly Demon. Some people might have clamored about it, but the city's inhabitants knew this was absurd nonsense. With or without the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon, this city had long belonged to monsters, not humans.

Her Grand Mansion in the Central District boasted a garden of beautiful red roses that never withered or broke, even in the harshest winter winds. The mansion itself was filled with so many large windows that one would never believe it belonged to a vampire, for whom sunlight was a natural enemy.

Jeniela savored a red beverage, the mingled scent of roses hanging heavy in the air. Across from her, a blood-soaked Aine gasped for breath, her pained groans sounding like the most pleasant music to Jeniela's ears.

Aine staggered to her feet, her body already finished regenerating in that brief moment.

*Her regenerative ability is good enough to compare with mine.*

Jeniela brought the glass to her lips with that thought. The instant the red beverage touched them, Aine leaped.

The speed at which Aine closed the distance was phenomenal, but Jeniela did not bother to follow her movements. There was no need.

"Too-oo slow."

Jeniela set down her glass and licked the red stain from her lips. She looked ahead with indifferent eyes as the red spikes that had erupted from the ground dissipated into a mist. Aine's body, mercilessly pierced by them, was now a lump of flesh that no longer held its original shape.

"......"

Jeniela tapped her fingertips on the table to the sound of falling organs and blood. On the floor, Aine's sprawled body twitched.

"The body is impeccable, and the regenerative ability is excellent. You seem to have enough power, too... but I'm not satisfied. Can't you be faster?"

"Haa... Heuk...!"

Aine gritted her teeth and pushed herself up. Jeniela just shook her head as she watched.

"Or am I expecting too much from you? Could it be that you're not the being I was thinking of? How strange. I was so sure it was you."

"Hoo...!"

Aine gasped for breath and forced herself to stand. Jeniela laughed when she saw Aine standing there without a single wound.

*Seeing that, it seems I was right,* she muttered, her lips twitching. Jeniela had seen a future where the Slaughter Predator appeared. She could not tell who it was, but of all the beings she knew, Aine was the closest.

"What could be lacking?"

Jeniela stared at Aine blankly. Was it predation? No, that was not it. The Slaughter Predator was a monster among monsters that killed and devoured everything, which was precisely why Jeniela had given that name to the monster she saw in the future. However, that did not mean unconditional predation was the condition for its completion. Even before killing and devouring everyone, the Slaughter Predator had to be a monster with enough power to do so in the first place.

"What's missing. Hmm?"

Jeniela's eyes shone, and Aine trembled as she watched them turn red. The All-Seeing Demonic Eye pierced through Aine's very existence. Jeniela saw the overflowing, ferocious Demonic Energy. She saw the souls and power of the countless beings Aine had devoured. *Nothing is lacking,* Jeniela muttered as she rose to her feet.

"Does she need a catalyst?" Jeniela asked with a smile.

It was not a pleasant feeling to have one's hidden depths laid bare. Aine ground her teeth and glared at Jeniela, who only giggled at the murderous gaze.

"...Ah."

Jeniela's laughter stopped.

*It's here.*

She raised both hands to cover her eyes, her thumbs stretching out to block her ears. The eyes see. They do not hear. Erasing all noise completely, she closed her eyes once more within the pitch-black canvas of her own hands.

This Demonic Eye that saw the future was not something Jeniela could completely control. If she managed to intentionally view an initial future, then 'scenes' leading to that future would unfold before her eyes from time to time.

Her blackened vision wavered as a scene leading to the desired future unfolded.

Jeniela smiled brightly.

"...Yes."

The vision ended. She let out a satisfied laugh and rose to her feet.

"I'm going out. Do you want to come?" Jeniela asked, looking back at the still-glaring Aine.

Aine's expression stiffened at the question. Her gaze flickered unconsciously toward the mansion where Preskan was kept in the basement. Though her movements inside were unrestricted, she could not leave the grounds, making it no different from a prison.

"Don't worry," Jeniela said with a chuckle. "It's not like that Lich will be eaten by the Vampires I'm raising while you're gone. Liches don't have any blood to suck in the first place. It's fine, it's fine. I'm just planning a short outing. If you really don't want to, you don't have to come."

"...I don't want to go."

"Really? Then it can't be helped."

Jeniela nodded at the refusal, showing no lingering attachment. She slowly turned around.

SHLICK!

The moment Jeniela took a step, red spikes shot up from the ground and pierced Aine's body.

"Ah... Ugh...!"

"Stay still."

In the future she had seen, taking Aine along was not important. If it had been, she would have dragged her by force, regardless of her refusal. Still, she had gone out of her way to invite her out of pure goodwill. Being rejected to her face had soured her mood, so she was simply being spiteful.

Jeniela left Aine twisting in the forest of spikes and exited the garden. It was impossible for someone at Aine's level to escape, no matter how hard she tried. Jeniela smiled with satisfaction at the sound of her screams and groans.

"Queen. Are you going out?"

"......"

A cautious question came from nearby as Jeniela headed for the mansion's main gate. She simply nodded at her subordinate.

"I'm going to leave Trabia for a bit."

"Are you going alone?"

"I'm thinking about that... What should I do? I'm on my way to greet some important guests."

Jeniela stood before the mansion's gate and let out a smirk.

"I suppose it's better to be extravagant, right?"

"Enough to be worthy of the guests."
