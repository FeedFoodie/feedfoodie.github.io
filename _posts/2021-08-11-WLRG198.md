An uproar tore through the Elf village. The battle between the Sword Saint and Lee Sungmin had shaken the entire forest, and news of it reached them through the eyes of the Elementals, every detail relayed in an instant. Their powerful ally, the Sword Saint, was dead.

Disbelief twisted Fist Honored One's features as he heard the report.

"The Sword Saint… is dead?"

The words came from his own daughter, the Hunting Team Leader, who stood before him with the same stunned expression, yet she knew, as he did, that they could not afford to remain bewildered. The man who killed the Sword Saint would not stop. He intended to kill Fist Honored One next, and he would come to this forest.

And then what? Less than half of the Hunting Party remained, and it had already been proven that even their full strength was not enough to resist the intruder. It was not that the other Elves were powerless. In the forest, even an Elf child was stronger than an average human, but even if all the hundreds of Elves in the village attacked at once, they would be helpless against the man now approaching them.

"Impossible… the Sword Saint, the Sword Saint…" Fist Honored One muttered again and again. He knew the Sword Saint's strength intimately, which was precisely why he could not believe it. The Hunting Team Leader watched him with a somber expression. He was her father, and because Elves had difficulty reproducing, their familial bonds were stronger than those of any other race.

"…Father," she began, her voice heavy. "I think we have to abandon the forest."

Silence was his only answer. As the one who carried the curse, he was utterly powerless. *Would it have been different if I wasn't cursed?* A dry laugh escaped him. Even with the forest's protection, he had no confidence he could have defeated the Sword Saint, and this intruder… this man had killed the Sword Saint and was now on his way here.

"If the Elves abandon the forest, where would we go?" A final, desperate struggle was pointless. The curse was not something he could simply cast aside. Even if he stopped maintaining it, his body would need a significant amount of time to recover its strength, time the man would certainly not grant him.

"I can't even blame the Spirit Medium," he said, his shoulders shaking with laughter. "Even Heaven Beyond Heaven couldn't have predicted this. That the Sword Saint would be defeated and killed. Hahaha…" The Hunting Team Leader watched her weakened father with sad eyes. His laughter stopped as abruptly as it had begun.

"Running away is also meaningless," he muttered. An Elf's martial arts were swift in the forest, but he could not imagine the man who killed the Sword Saint being any slower. Besides, this was not one or two Elves fleeing, but the entire village. If he escaped alone, the man might slaughter everyone left behind simply to vent his anger.

*Would the pursuit be Buddhist? Not a chance.* He hung his head low, his love for his kin tangled with his own desire to live. What on earth was he supposed to do? No, he already knew the answer. He just had not yet mustered the courage to say it out loud.

"…Father, let's escape together…"

"No." He raised his eyes to meet his daughter's, his only blood relative, then quietly closed them. No one wanted to die, not even Elves who lived for hundreds of years. There was so much more they wanted to do, so much they had gained over their long lives, but now was not the time for that. Now was the time to give it all up.

"Banish me from the village," he said, opening his eyes. The Sword Saint had given him a lingering hope, a belief that he could prevent the death the Spirit Medium had foreseen, but the Sword Saint was dead. If he stubbornly clung to life now, there was no way to survive. It was only a question of how many would die with him. If he insisted on living, he would die with his people. If he did not, he would die alone.

"Father…!"

"That's the best way. If I die alone, that man won't try to kill the other Elves."

"But… Father. Since that human entered this forest and killed Elves, we…!"

"That's an outdated rule," he cut in. "No matter how much your love for your kin overflows, you must know when to bend depending on the situation. If you attack in the name of avenging your kin, you'll all just die. So let's stop here."

"Father…"

"Say no more." Fist Honored One looked at his daughter. He knew her too well. If he was banished and killed, the other Elves might give up on revenge, but she would not. She would throw herself into death's embrace to avenge him.

"If you want to avenge me," he began, reaching out a weak hand to take hers, "you must learn how to retreat. With your current skills, revenge is impossible. When I die… leave this forest and go to Yuzkia Mountain."

"What…?"

"It will be a long journey. Yuzkia Mountain is far. When you arrive at that mountain, wait for the night of the full moon, and climb the peak closest to it. At the summit, shout my name, and cry out that a daughter has come to avenge her father. Even so, regarding the death of me, one of the Six Honored Ones… as long as you say my daughter has come for revenge, that woman will not refuse you."

"What are you…?"

"Heed my words," he insisted. "Heed them, and never forget. Your dying with me cannot be my revenge, can it? If you want to avenge me, you must kill the human who killed me with your own hands."

As he continued to speak, the Hunting Team Leader's shoulders trembled. It was not that she did not understand what her father was saying. She just did not want to accept it. In truth, she knew it too. This was the right path. Her vision blurred, and tears streamed down her face. She covered her face with both hands and wept silently, not for her kin, but for the thought of letting her father die.

"There's no time," Fist Honored One muttered, his mind now composed.

{sep}

When Lee Sungmin arrived at the Elf village, its inhabitants were all waiting outside. They stared at him with faces full of hostility, but their gazes could not conceal their fear. Despite having just killed the Sword Saint, Lee Sungmin felt no particular fatigue. He could do it if he wanted to. There were less than three hundred Elves in this village. If he actively tried to kill them, he could probably slaughter every Elf in this forest before the day was over.

"So you're the human who killed the Sword Saint."

The voice was low and uneven, like that of a patient dying from illness, a slight cough mixed in at the end of his words. Lee Sungmin looked in the direction of the sound. As his gaze turned that way, the standing Elves subtly stepped aside, allowing him a clear view of Fist Honored One, who was sitting collapsed on the ground.

"You've come to kill me, haven't you?"

"So you're Fist Honored One," Lee Sungmin muttered in a low voice. Killing intent radiated from him. The Elves, with their keen senses, felt the wave of malice and flinched, taking a step back.

Lee Sungmin slowly approached. Fist Honored One hastily spat out his words.

"Just kill me."

"……"

"I… have been banished from this forest. Even if you kill me, the Elves of this forest will not be hostile to you."

"I don't see what that has to do with anything. I've already killed other Elves in this forest, and I killed the Hunting Party too. Aren't I already an enemy of the Elves?"

"Nothing happened," Fist Honored One continued, coughing. "You entered this forest and killed no Elves. We have decided it so. Even if you kill me here, neither the Elves of this forest nor any Elf will treat you as an enemy."

"I don't care if I become an enemy. The pursuit of the Elves would be annoying, but it's not something I can't handle."

"…Is there any need to continue with unnecessary killing?"

"That's for me to decide," Lee Sungmin replied.

At those words, Fist Honored One's face turned pale. He had not expected Lee Sungmin to be so reckless. No, it was not recklessness. It was because Lee Sungmin possessed enough power to act that way.

[Stop.]

It was Void Monarch who intervened.

[I also agree that you don't need to commit unnecessary killings. Getting too used to killing won't be good for you.]

*Because of inhumanity?*

[Yes. You may not be aware of it, but inhumanity is already affecting you to some extent. If you overdo it, you might get devoured by it.]

Lee Sungmin fell silent at Void Monarch's warning. He slowly reined in his sharp emotions. Void Monarch was right. If it was something he did not have to do, then he should not. Lee Sungmin also thought that if they were not hostile, there was no need to kill them all. He slowly nodded his head.

"…Fine. I'll kill only you. In return, you'll have to answer my questions."

"…What?"

"Come here."

Lee Sungmin raised his hand and pointed at Fist Honored One. His body floated up and slowly approached Lee Sungmin, steeling himself for death. Just because he steeled himself did not mean the fear disappeared, and Fist Honored One's body trembled.

"Nothing happened," Lee Sungmin muttered as he looked at the Elves. "I'll believe those words, but I want you to remember… that it doesn't matter. If you try to kill me, I'll just kill you in return. That's not a very difficult thing for me to do."

The Elves did not answer. Lee Sungmin ignored their silence and looked for the Hunting Team Leader.

"I heard the Hunting Team Leader is your daughter."

"…The Sword Saint said something unnecessary."

"If I kill you, won't the Hunting Team Leader come to kill me to avenge her father?"

"Perhaps she might… but does it matter? With your skills, you should be able to defeat my daughter easily."

"That's true, but there's no need to put up with the hassle."

"Leave my daughter alone. In return… I'll swear to answer all your questions. Without any lies."

"Even if you don't swear, I'm confident I can get the answers I want from you. I just have to kill the Elves in front of you, right?"

"If… you do that," Fist Honored One said, his voice tight, "I will take my own life."

"You think I'd let you?"

"It wouldn't hurt to test it," he bluffed in his reply.

Lee Sungmin smirked at him. He said no more and turned around, leaving the village behind with Fist Honored One. The Elves just stood there blankly as he disappeared from sight, and when Lee Sungmin and Fist Honored One were no longer visible, they shed tears.

Lee Sungmin returned with Fist Honored One to the place where the Sword Saint's corpse lay. Fist Honored One flinched, his body trembling as he saw the corpse, dead with a large hole in its chest. The proof of the Sword Saint's death, which he had not truly believed in his heart, was right before his eyes.

"I want to know about Heaven Beyond Heaven," Lee Sungmin said, setting Fist Honored One down.

Fist Honored One bit his lower lip hard.

"About the Six Honored Ones, including you and the Sword Saint, and about the Martial God, who stands at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven. Why you tried to kidnap Wiji Hoyeon, what Heaven Beyond Heaven's purpose is, and how vast a power Heaven Beyond Heaven is."

"That's…"

"Before you speak, swear to tell the truth," Lee Sungmin cut him off. "If you lie and die from breaking your oath, I will kill every single Elf in that village. Including your daughter."

"…You're being cruel."

"Swear."

Lee Sungmin sat down on the ground and glared at Fist Honored One. To think he would end up in such a state. Fist Honored One chuckled and made his oath on Mana.

"The Six Honored Ones… as the name suggests, are composed of six venerated ones. We are the true power of Heaven Beyond Heaven, and we move independently for the sake of Heaven Beyond Heaven's big picture."

"Who are the Six Honored Ones?"

"…First, there's me. I, who received the title of Fist Honored One, am in the last place of the Six Honored Ones. As you know, a fight between masters is influenced by many things. Things like variables and compatibility. Among the Six Honored Ones, four hold the title of 'Supreme' or 'Saint'. Fist Honored One, the Dark Supreme, Blade Supreme, and the Sword Saint. Among them, the skills of the three, excluding the Sword Saint, should be considered practically equal."

Lee Sungmin had met the Dark Supreme before. The Sword Saint had been killed by his own hand. Judging by his title, Blade Supreme was likely a guy who used a blade.

"And the other two?"

"They are incomparably stronger than us. Even though we're in the same Transcendent Realm, they're on a different level… Compared to those two, even the Sword Saint is at the level of a child."

"Their titles?"

"The Spear King and Moon Queen."

"You were in the Elf Forest, and the Sword Saint died by my hand. Where are Blade Supreme and the Dark Supreme, and what are they doing? And where are Moon Queen and the Spear King?"

"…Hmm…" Fist Honored One looked troubled by Lee Sungmin's probing questions. He had made an oath to save his daughter and the village Elves, but everything Lee Sungmin was asking about were Heaven Beyond Heaven's most hidden secrets.

"Are you not going to talk?"

"No… No, I'll talk." Fist Honored One's face contorted. "I… don't know where the Dark Supreme is. As I said, the Six Honored Ones act independently."

It was the truth. The Mana did not run wild.

"I don't know Blade Supreme's location either, but if you want to find him, it should be easier than you think."

"What do you mean?"

"Blade Supreme is a celebrity. He's called the 'Mercenary King', and his fame resounds throughout Eria. The other Six Honored Ones, aside from him, aren't well known, but he's different."

Mercenary King.

That nickname made Lee Sungmin recall his memories from his mercenary days.
