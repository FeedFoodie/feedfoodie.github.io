The wall the Void Monarch could not pass yielded easily to Lee Sungmin, for it was the barrier of his own mind, and it accepted him without resistance.

He looked down at his empty hands, and at his will, a spear appeared. This place felt similar to Denil's Mental World, yet it was fundamentally different. In Denil's world, his authority had granted Lee Sungmin a body as real as his own, allowing him to use his inner arts freely.

Here, however, he could sense nothing. He could feel neither his inner arts nor his Demonic Energy. He had managed to create a spear, but that was the extent of his power. No matter how he focused, his inner arts would not rise.

*This is a disadvantage.*

A bitter laugh escaped him. With no inner arts or Demonic Energy, all he had was a single spear and his own body. Still, it had to be enough. He refused to die, and he had no intention of surrendering his body to that damned thing.

The rampaging inhumanity froze. It stood blankly for a moment before its head whipped around to face him. It had no eyes, no nose, no mouth, just a human form composed of black and red, yet Lee Sungmin felt as if he could hear its inaudible roar.

The creature crouched down, planting all four of its limbs on the ground like a beast.

Before he could even register its movement, the creature was upon him. A mottled hand swung down like a hammer, aiming to smash his head. Lee Sungmin immediately dropped his stance. Defending against such an attack was impossible without his inner arts, and he couldn't even draw out his Protective Qi Aura.

An overwhelming sound grazed past his head. As he held his low stance, Lee Sungmin executed his movement arts without hesitation. The lack of inner arts denied him any explosive acceleration, but the fundamentals of his movement arts remained.

Diving into the inhumanity's reach, he held the spear tight against his side and delivered a short thrust.

THUD!

With a dull thud, the inhumanity's body was pushed back several steps. Even though he had stabbed with all his might, the impact felt light. The spearhead, which should have been sharp, had failed to pierce the creature's hide. He hadn't expected to end it in a single blow, so he felt no disappointment.

WHOOSH!

Lee Sungmin charged after the retreating inhumanity and unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Without his inner arts, Soul Chasing Elimination was slow and shallow, but it still struck the inhumanity's chest with perfect accuracy.

The creature did not simply take the hit. Its outstretched hand shot toward Lee Sungmin's shoulder, but he had already seen it coming. The moment its shoulder twitched, he predicted the attack. Physically, the inhumanity was undeniably superior, but its movements were driven by pure instinct, making them easy for him to read.

Slipping through an opening, Lee Sungmin once again unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He couldn't use Nine Dragons Slaughter, Void Path, or sky-piercing, as they all relied heavily on inner arts. Even the spear illusions of Homing Chain Lightning were impossible to create at speed without that power. In the end, only two techniques from the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear remained available to him, Soul Chasing Elimination and Serpent Soul Devouring.

This time, he chose Serpent Soul Devouring.

CRUNCH!

The spear thrust up from below and struck the inhumanity's chin. He had wanted to pierce through to the crown of its head, but again, he lacked the power. All he managed to do was snap its head back violently.

He fell back on more than just the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He used the three basics of spear technique, a sweep, a parry, a thrust. He deflected the creature's flailing arms with a parry, matching its speed, and swung his Changjun to strike its body once more. He knew he had to accumulate damage, but a doubt lingered. Could he truly defeat it just by piling on hits?

Despite his uncertainty, Lee Sungmin kept moving, kept swinging his spear.

{sep}

From beyond the wall he could not pass, the Void Monarch watched the battle unfold. It was a tug-of-war between natures, a struggle of consciousness. He knew the inhumanity would not fall easily, no matter how much damage it sustained. Lee Sungmin would only reclaim his body when his own consciousness completely overwhelmed the creature's.

*It's impossible for me to help.*

The Void Monarch felt a surge of bitterness. Interference was impossible in the first place, as he could not pass through the wall. Even if he could, and even if he struck down the inhumanity himself, control would return to neither of them. This was Lee Sungmin's battle and his alone.

Hm?

The Void Monarch's eyes widened. He blinked, certain he had seen wrong, but it was still there. *What is that?* He shot to his feet and approached the barrier for a better look, pressing himself right against the impassable wall to peer inside.

There, beyond the furious clash between Lee Sungmin and the inhumanity, someone was sitting in a relaxed posture. No matter how hard he strained his eyes, the Void Monarch could not make out the being's face, yet he could feel it, an undeniable certainty that 'he' was smiling.

"Who are you?" the Void Monarch asked, his voice a low mutter.

Though he had spoken as if to himself, the being lifted its head and looked directly at him.

The Void Monarch's mind reeled. How could neither Lee Sungmin nor the inhumanity sense this presence? It was sitting just a few steps away from their fight. He could perhaps understand the mindless inhumanity not noticing, but why couldn't Lee Sungmin perceive a being that so blatantly revealed itself?

He opened his mouth to shout a warning to Lee Sungmin, but in that instant, the world narrowed.

He now stood at one end of a thin, straight path. At the other end sat the faceless being, its posture unchanged, still smiling. *What is this?* The Void Monarch was shaken by a feeling he had not experienced in a very long time, perhaps not since the beginning of his life. Cold sweat beaded on his palms. A chill traced its way down his spine as a shiver of goosebumps crawled up from his toes.

The being slowly lifted the hand that had been resting on its knee. The Void Monarch perceived every single movement in agonizing slow motion.

This was the Void Monarch, a being whose physical body had only barely been destroyed by the combined might of dozens of Dragons, tens of archmages and Grand Sorcerers, and hundreds of martial arts masters, yet now, from this simple gesture, he felt a terror that dwarfed anything he had known in that final battle. *What in the world is that thing? Why does such a terrifying entity exist in Lee Sungmin's mental world?*

The hand continued its deliberate, slow ascent, a movement that seemed to stretch into an eternity. In that time, the Void Monarch, who had never once considered his own mortality, imagined his death tens, hundreds, thousands of times. Finally, at the end of its movement, the being that had conveyed the very concept of death with a simple gesture raised its index finger and placed it where its mouth should be.

Shhh.

"Gasp!"

The Void Monarch came to his senses collapsed on the floor, resting on both knees.

His face twisted. *A hallucination? Was it?* Whatever it had been, he gritted his teeth and forced strength into his trembling legs. The Void Monarch knew that in his prime, when he was alive, no single being in that world could have stood against him. Excluding aberrations like the Fairy Queen or the Spirit Queen, he had been the strongest among mortals.

*Its class is different. Not power, or anything like that. Its class as a being is different. The gods of Eria? No, it can't be compared to those insignificant things.*

"You… what the hell are you!" he roared, staggering to his feet.

The thin, straight path remained, with the being at its end. Having barely stood, the Void Monarch took a step forward to approach it. The being, its finger still where its mouth should be, did not move.

When the Void Monarch took a single step forward, the path stretched out before him.

An unknown voice echoed in his mind. *You are now, at most, one step.*

Startled, the Void Monarch looked back. It had been one step.

*I am the end.*

"Will you come this way?" the being asked, its voice clear.

The Void Monarch stood blankly, watching as the straight path began to wind and twist.

Ah.

He realized something then. He understood what this path was, what his single step had meant. He understood the question, 'Will you come this way,' and what would happen if he chose to go. In every alley of the winding path, something shimmered. He saw figures who had collapsed and others who were taking slow, sure steps forward.

"Not yet," he muttered, his voice dazed.

With a hollow laugh, he pulled his extended foot back. He knew that if he chose to go forward now, he could advance all the way to the end without stopping.

"I knew you would," the being said, its voice laced with a smile.

It repeated the gesture from before. Shhh. The Void Monarch nodded in understanding. He did not understand everything, but he knew one thing with absolute certainty. He must never tell Lee Sungmin about this meeting or what he had realized. It was not for anyone else's sake, but for Lee Sungmin's own.

The path vanished from before him. The Void Monarch collapsed on the spot, back in front of the barrier. When he looked again, the smiling, seated figure was gone.

{sep}

Unaware of the ordeal the Void Monarch had just experienced, Lee Sungmin was pushing back the inhumanity with all his might.

At some point, he realized his body had become faster and stronger. Though he still couldn't sense his inner arts, his physical form was continuously growing more powerful. It was proof that in the struggle of consciousness, he was gaining the upper hand. The inhumanity's attacks no longer felt threatening.

KWAANG!

His spear finally pierced the inhumanity's hide. He put more force behind it, pushing the staggering creature back. Time was running out. With a final, desperate thrust, he drove the spear completely through its body.

The inhumanity collapsed to its knees but did not vanish. Instead, the mental world began to waver. The red and black landscape started to bleach, turning white. It was proof that his consciousness had suppressed the inhumanity and gained complete dominance, yet the creature was not disappearing. It struggled against the spear that impaled it.

As the red and black of its form blurred, the inhumanity's face was revealed. It was his own face, but not exactly. It was a distorted mask with sharp teeth, as if insisting it was not human.

"See you next time," it said, before dissolving into mist.

It hadn't disappeared. Lee Sungmin glared at his spear, now piercing an empty floor. The inhumanity had awakened, and it would not be extinguished. It would lie dormant in his subconscious, waiting for another opportunity to one day rise and devour his humanity.

As the mental world faded, Lee Sungmin looked back at the Void Monarch, who was sitting beyond the barrier. He remembered hearing the man shout something during the fight. *What did he say?* Just as he opened his mouth to ask, the Void Monarch rose to his feet.

He slowly shook his head, then raised an index finger to his lips.

"Shhh."

"…What are you doing?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Just go, you bastard," the Void Monarch replied with a hearty laugh.

Envirus knew his end was near. The exchange of his life for Mana had successfully activated a body too weak for magic, but it was only a temporary reprieve.

His gaze fell upon Aine, who was collapsed on the ground. After struggling so desperately, her strength had finally run out.

"You're a monster…" Envirus muttered, his voice weak.

In the air above, Lee Sungmin hung limply, bound by chains. This binding magic was a spell of his own creation and one of the most powerful he could cast. Normally, being caught in those chains was enough to destroy a person's body and snuff out their life, yet Lee Sungmin's form was somehow holding on, undestroyed.

However, his resistance had lessened. *His strength must be gone.* Even after being caught, even as the chains pulled and destructive magic flowed into him, he had thrashed his limbs at unnatural angles and torn the spells apart. That resistance was now at an end.

"…I'm sorry. Truly," Envirus apologized once more as he raised his hand.

CRACKLE.

Light enveloped his wide-open palm. It was at that exact moment.

The chained body of Lee Sungmin stirred. He lifted his bowed head, and two Demonic Eyes stared down at Envirus.

"Sorry for what…"

His grotesquely bent limbs snapped back to their proper angles as Demonic Energy and inner arts blended together inside his qi center. His gaze flicked to the spear lying on the distant ground.

*Come.*

The unspoken command crossed the space between them, and the spear responded.

CLANG, the spear trembled and shot upward, flying with a WHOOSH to pierce the chains binding him before Envirus even had a chance to react.

"Even if you're going to do what you must," Lee Sungmin said, landing lightly on the ground amidst the scattering fragments of the chains, spear in hand. "How about keeping your promise first?"

"Dragon Heart."

At those words, Envirus's face turned deathly pale.
