The key rested on Lee Sungmin's palm.

It was what the Stone of Reincarnation had transformed into, and while he burned with the need to know its purpose, he had no way to identify it on his own. Appraising such an unknown item would normally require a trip to a city shop, but as a member of Erebrisa, he had other options.

*First, Nebul...*

Just as he was about to summon Nebul to request an appraisal, a few figures descended from the hill and began to approach him. Lee Sungmin immediately put his mask back on and postponed the call. Leaving this place was the priority.

The approaching men looked like fairly high-ranking mercenaries, but they were only slightly above the peak realm at best. He glanced at them once before his movement arts flared to life, carrying him from the area in a blur. From their perspective, a dungeon they had luckily discovered had just been stolen from them, but there was nothing to be done. It was simply their bad luck.

After putting some distance between himself and the hilly area, Lee Sungmin confirmed he was alone and finally summoned Nebul. The agent shot up from his shadow and, as always, bowed his head deeply.

"How may I serve you?"

"I need an item appraised."

Lee Sungmin held up the key. Nebul's head tilted as he studied the gleaming golden object on his palm.

"A key... I see," he mused. "Where did you find it?"

"In a dungeon."

"Is that so? May I take it for a moment?" Nebul asked politely. Since Lee Sungmin could not use Spatial Movement with him, he had to physically hand over items for transfer.

Nebul slowly reached out to take the key.

The moment his fingertips made contact.

CRACKLE!

A spark of electricity erupted, and Nebul's hand was thrown back.

"Ugh?!"

Nebul let out a startled cry. He stared, baffled, from his repelled hand to the key and back again. Lee Sungmin had not expected that either and watched with wide eyes.

"...Just a moment."

Nebul reached for the key again, but the result was the same. His hand was repelled before it could even touch the metal. He frowned, clenching and unclenching his tingling fingers.

"It seems... this key is bound to you, Sir Lee Sungmin."

"Bound?"

"Yes. Such items exist, though they are exceptionally rare. No, since it is enchanted, it should be called an artifact. Typically, items of this nature are cursed... but as long as it is bound to you, it is impossible for me to take it."

Lee Sungmin's brow furrowed at Nebul's words. The idea of being bound to an item he knew nothing about was unsettling.

"Is there no other way?"

"You could visit an appraiser in a nearby city. Or, we could introduce you to a skilled appraiser from our side."

"I'd like an introduction, please."

"Understood."

Nebul replied and vanished. He reappeared less than an hour later.

"In Behenger, the closest city, there is an appraiser named Prod. He is not one of our members, but our information suggests his skills are the best in this vicinity."

Behenger was not far. At his current speed, Lee Sungmin could arrive before the night was over. It was not too late, so if he hurried, he could likely request the appraisal right away. After getting Prod's location from Nebul, he unleashed his movement arts and set a course for the city.

Thanks to his haste, Lee Sungmin arrived in Behenger long before the night grew deep.

He had not set foot in the city for nearly ten years, and it had changed in many ways, yet for Lee Sungmin, who had lived here until this very age in his past life, the sight was a welcome one. It was not that late. He followed Nebul's directions and headed for Prod's appraisal shop.

The shop was located near Behenger's main square, and fortunately, its door was still open.

"Welcome..."

He pushed the door open and stepped inside. The man behind the counter, Prod, was middle-aged, with thick-lensed glasses and faint wrinkles. He was so startled by the sight of Lee Sungmin's wide-brimmed hat and Ghost Spirit mask that his greeting died in his throat.

Prod quickly composed himself. "Can I... help you?"

Lee Sungmin gave a short nod, and Prod's expression relaxed. Without another word, he walked up to the counter and placed the heavy key on the table.

"A key?"

"I got it from a dungeon, but I don't know what it's for."

"A dungeon... hmm, I see."

Prod was not flustered. Most of his customers were adventurers or mercenaries with items from dungeons, or those who had found artifacts in ancient ruins.

"I was told it's bound to me."

"Bound?"

Hearing this, Prod cautiously reached for the key.

CRACKLE!

The instant his fingers touched it, a spark flew, and his hand was violently pushed back.

"It's blocking contact itself."

"Will an appraisal be possible?"

"I'll give it a try, at least."

With that, Prod began his preparations. Lee Sungmin had expected him to simply use magic, but the man took out various tools, lined them up, and began examining the key with each one. All the while, his lips moved, chanting an incantation in a low voice.

"Gasp."

In the middle of his chant, Prod let out a choked gasp. He abruptly stopped and stumbled several steps back. Lee Sungmin, who had been watching quietly, tilted his head.

"What's wrong?"

"This... an appraisal is impossible."

Prod's face was pale, and he wiped a bead of cold sweat from his forehead with the back of his hand. He swallowed hard and continued in a trembling voice.

"Even if you go to another appraiser, they'll tell you the same thing. This key... it's impossible to appraise."

"What in the world does that mean?"

"The key itself is enchanted with magic of extreme complexity. If I were to force an appraisal, the magic on the key would kill me. I could not avoid it."

Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched. He looked down at the key on the table. "Then can't you just dispel the magic?"

"That won't be easy either. I'm a mage myself, but the magic on that key... regardless of its level, it's an incredibly esoteric form of ancient magic. The very formula that constitutes it is completely different from modern magic. Unless you're an expert in ancient magic, you won't even be able to properly grasp the formula."

"Ancient magic?"

"It's an old form of magic that has now disappeared. I'm sorry, customer. I failed the appraisal, so I won't take any money. Please ask another appraiser or mage to look at this key."

In the end, Lee Sungmin had no choice but to take the key and leave the shop.

He stood outside, staring down at the object on his palm with suspicious eyes. The Stone of Reincarnation had become a key, a key that was bound to him. On top of that, it was protected by obsolete ancient magic that made it impossible to even appraise. There was more than one suspicious thing about it.

"Ancient magic..."

He summoned Nebul again. When Lee Sungmin posed his question, the agent fell into thought for a moment. He did not vanish to gather information this time. Instead, after a brief pause, he answered.

"The person with the most knowledge about ancient magic is the Blue Magic Tower Master, Syaos."

The Blue Magic Tower Master. Lee Sungmin had never met the man, but he had heard his name from Nebul before. When he had inquired with Erebrisa about a mage who could handle the Dragon Heart, Syaos had expressed a strong desire to purchase it.

"Where is he?"

"Syaos is in Odun, where the Blue Magic Tower is located. Magic Tower Masters rarely leave their towers unless it's a special case."

Odun was a long way off. Fortunately, the place Lee Sungmin ultimately needed to go was in the same direction. Haras, the city housing the headquarters of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, was just past Odun.

[You've gotten your hands on a troublesome item,] Void Monarch grumbled.

Lee Sungmin tried to place the key in his Subspace Pocket, but it refused to enter, almost as if repelled by the pocket's dimension. It seemed its bond to him was absolute. In the end, he had no choice but to grumble and tuck the key inside his robes.

[At least you won't lose it.]

*I don't even know what it's used for.*

[If it's a key, it must be for opening something.]

*I know that much. The problem is that I don't know what it opens.*

[A door, probably,] Void Monarch replied indifferently. [Though I don't know what kind of door.]

Thinking about it would not yield an answer now. He decided on his next course of action. He would stop by Odun on his way to Haras and meet the Blue Magic Tower Master.

*Since I've come all this way...*

Behenger and Zenavis were not that far apart. He might come back someday, but there was no telling when that would be.

Lee Sungmin turned his head, his gaze directed toward Zenavis.

Zenavis. The city where otherworlders were first summoned, the city where they all began their lives in Eria. He had met Wiji Hoyeon there and had so many other encounters as well.

*I always thought I'd repay their kindness someday.*

He thought of Jack, the innkeeper, and Hans, the street vendor, who had treated him so well. He thought of Jack's daughter, Lula. Looking back, he knew their kindness had been influenced by the Blessing of Unnatural Phenomena he possessed at the time.

Even so, their kindness had been real, and he had benefited from it. Had Jack not offered him a room at a low price, or had Hans not sold him the Subspace Pocket and other gear so cheaply, his journey would have been far more inconvenient. He would not have died without their help, of course, but many things would have been much harder.

*I should at least go and say hello.*

He did not plan on going right away. After spending the night at an inn in Behenger, Lee Sungmin left in the early morning. More than ten years ago, the journey from Zenavis to Behenger had taken several days, but for the man he was now, it was no great distance at all.

Upon arriving in Zenavis, Lee Sungmin first sought out the square where the street vendors gathered. He was looking for Hans. Relying on his memory, he went to the spot where the man always set up his stall.

He found him.

More than ten years had passed, and Hans was now a middle-aged man, his hair streaked with white and the wrinkles on his face etched deeper. He had spread out a mat, arranged various miscellaneous items on it, and was sitting there quietly.

Lee Sungmin smiled warmly at the sight of the familiar back. He removed his wide-brimmed hat and mask, stepping up behind the vendor.

"Is business going well?"

Hans lifted his head at the voice. He looked up at Lee Sungmin, blinking and tilting his head in confusion.

"...Who are you?"

He did not recognize him. It was understandable. More than a decade had passed, and the boy Hans had known was now a young man. After everything he had been through, almost no trace of his childhood self remained.

Lee Sungmin chuckled and reached into his Subspace Pocket. He pulled out the very first one he had ever received, the one from Hans.

"Do you remember this?"

Hans's eyes narrowed, but it was clear he did not recognize it. That, too, was understandable. As a street vendor, he must have sold items to countless people. Lee Sungmin handed the Subspace Pocket to him.

"I was summoned here thirteen years ago," he said. "You helped me a lot back then."

At the mention of thirteen years ago, Hans's eyes shot wide. His mouth fell open as he scrambled to his feet.

"You... you're Sungmin?" Hans asked, his face lighting up with dawning recognition.
