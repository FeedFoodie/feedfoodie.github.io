He knew.

He knew the situation unfolding around him would be nothing like what had happened in the North. The northern subjugation force had not necessarily lacked power. Their critical mistake had been giving Kim Jonghyun too much time, time he had used to make preparations they were woefully unequipped to counter.

*But this time is different.*

Kim Jonghyun was keenly aware of this fact.

The Mage Army and the Magic Tower Masters, for all their exceptional skill, had failed to properly counter his Dark Magic. The Church had sent Holy Knights and Priests, but their numbers were too small to matter. As for the martial artists, their physical prowess alone had never posed a danger to him in the first place.

The circumstances and the situation had changed. Thanks to the tragedy in the North, they would no longer be so complacent. Having been thoroughly beaten by Dark Magic, they would surely prepare for it with meticulous care.

His only silver lining was the time he still had before the Mages Guild's main forces, the Magic Tower and the battle mages of the Mage Army, could arrive. The South was a land where the influence of both the Murim Alliance and the Mages Guild was thin. Most of the region was a dense jungle, home to many tribes with their own unique cultures. Urmuri, a city just a short distance from here, was a city of Yokai. While there were some martial artists and mages scattered about, he did not have to worry about them organizing a subjugation force for the time being.

The problem was the other factions.

The Church's Priests and Holy Knights. In truth, their circumstances were not so different from his own. Just as he had become an alien entity in Eria after his contracted Demon Lord died, most of the Church's Priests and Holy Knights had been severed from the gods they served when they were summoned here. The power they now wielded was of an unknown nature that could not even be called divine.

Even so, what they called 'hold power' was a natural enemy to a Black Mage. The Saint. Kim Jonghyun's thoughts lingered on him.

*Would it be better to head north and increase my forces, or should I settle down for a while and prepare countermeasures?*

It was a dilemma he found rather enjoyable.

He could have chosen another place. In fact, anywhere would have been better than the South. He was a Quasi-Demon King, not a complete Demon Lord, and the hold power still had an irritating and unpleasant effect on him. He knew it would be better to face martial artists or mages, foes he could at least grasp and counter, than to face the Saint, whose identity and power were unknown, and the main forces of the Church.

Still, he had not done so, for a simple reason. He had no particular desire to become a Demon Lord, nor did he wish to do anything with this world.

He just didn't care. He was only doing it because he could.

*No, would it be better to just break everything?*

Not that he could do that in the first place. Kim Jonghyun chuckled to himself.

For him, the motivation for his actions was largely determined by a simple question of capability. *Can I do it? Really? Should I try?* If the answer was yes, concepts like morals or human ethics were irrelevant. Of course, he did enjoy creating panic, and most of the things he pondered 'can I do it?' about were of that nature.

"There are no survivors."

There was no knock. The door opened and Vollander entered, bringing the thick scent of blood with him. Kim Jonghyun leaned his chair back with a creak, a faint smile playing on his lips as he looked at Vollander.

This castle, once the residence of Germud's lord, had been a social venue for nobles just a day ago. Now, the only living human within its walls was the half-Demon Lord, Kim Jonghyun.

"How many died? No, how many did you kill?" Kim Jonghyun asked with that slight smile. An undeniable stench of blood clung to Vollander, yet not a single drop stained his armor or sword.

Vollander did not respond to the sneering smile, suppressing the displeasure and hostility that welled up from deep within his chest.

"Do the numbers matter?"

"They do. I need to know how many sacrifices we've got for the ritual."

"...I don't know," Vollander replied. "Tens of thousands, I suppose."

"It's not enough," Kim Jonghyun said, caressing the Grimoire that rested on the wide desk. "Fewer than ten thousand lives were enough to invert me, but to invert you and the hundreds of Death Knights, we'll need several times that number. Strictly speaking, this is about destroying the link to the Demon Lord that binds your souls and reconstructing the bodies he stole from you upon your deaths."

"...We'll have to go to the next city."

"No, there's no need for that. The sacrifices will come to us if we wait."

"You're saying you'll make this place your domain?"

"If we raise the corpses here as Undead, our forces will be sufficient."

"But we don't have enough sacrifices to invert us, and the Church's Saint is in the South."

"The Saint is just one human. Do you really think he can stop you or me?"

"No, I don't. I just think it's more efficient to use the residents of nearby towns as sacrifices before they can evacuate, rather than just waiting for a subjugation force."

"I'm the one who decides that."

Vollander did not press the point further against Kim Jonghyun's affable smile. Ever since this relationship had formed, Kim Jonghyun had always held the upper hand. Vollander wanted to kill him, but doing so would completely destroy his only path to becoming human again. He had committed an indiscriminate slaughter for the first time in hundreds of years. He could not afford to ruin everything now.

"Understood."

Vollander turned and left the room with the clanking of his armor. Kim Jonghyun watched the unclosed door and chuckled.

*He seems pretty angry.*

The door closed. Kim Jonghyun rose from his chair and went to the window. The sight of the horribly ruined city filled his eyes. He could see Undead wandering the streets. They were on a completely different level from the Death Knights, but they were sufficient to form an army.

*First, I'll gather the Earth's Mana... There's a lot to prepare.*

He had an abundance of magic power and knowledge to match. Opening the gate to the Demon Realm from the sea had allowed him to gain many things.

Especially knowledge.

"A Slaughterhouse," Kim Jonghyun muttered, looking out the window.

If this place is a giant Slaughterhouse, and we are the guinea pigs being raised inside it.

What kind of need do the beings outside the Slaughterhouse have for a guinea pig like me?

Honestly, it was not a very pleasant feeling.

The carriage stopped.

Lee Sungmin opened one eye from his meditation and looked at Abel, who finally glanced up from the contents of Gries.

"We've run into them," Abel said.

"Isn't this good luck?"

"I wouldn't call it ideal," Abel mused. "I was hoping they'd let Kim Jonghyun kill a few of them first." That way, he added as he closed the book, they could have exploited an opening.

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh at his words.

It had been three days since he began traveling alone with Abel in the carriage. They had not spoken much, but Lee Sungmin had gotten a sense of the man. He was nothing like his brother, Envirus. Though his true intentions remained a mystery, Abel was at least far more proactive about stopping The End. He also held the firm belief, as he had just demonstrated, that a certain amount of sacrifice was necessary to achieve that ultimate goal. Lee Sungmin felt the same way. He understood that stopping The End without any death, damage, or problems was an impossible task.

"Will you accompany them?"

"Let's make contact first," Abel said, closing Gries. He slipped on the gray robe he had taken off and opened the carriage door.

A procession of simple, unadorned white carriages had stopped not far away. Abel put both hands into the sleeves of his robe and stared at them. Lee Sungmin pressed his mask onto his face. It was not a very good-looking mask, but it was necessary. Even with Osla's temporary Sealing, he had to keep his Demonic Energy suppressed outside of combat.

As Abel and Lee Sungmin stepped out of their carriage, the halted procession began to move. Lee Sungmin's gaze fell upon the Holy Knights escorting them, a large force numbering in the hundreds. The men in pure white armor exuded the same strong aura he had felt during the Northern Pacification Battle. One of them broke from the formation and rode his horse toward them.

"Where are you headed?" the man asked. His voice was deep, befitting his large build.

Four days had passed since the massacre in Germud. The residents of the surrounding cities and towns had already abandoned their homes, fearing a similar fate. Under such circumstances, it was understandable that they would be suspicious of two people heading toward the city alone.

"Germud," Abel answered first. "I am Abel, Master of the Mages Guild, and just who are you to look down on me with such an attitude?"

As Abel sneered, the man's eyes trembled visibly behind his helmet's visor. He was greatly flustered, having never imagined he was facing the Mages Guild Master.

"We're going to Germud," Abel continued. "We're on our way to kill Kim Jonghyun since he's raising hell over there."

"Ahem..." the Holy Knight coughed, flustered by Abel's rough language.

Abel looked past the messenger to the approaching carriages. "The Saint?"

"...Watch your tongue."

"I am the Mages Guild Master. I stand at the pinnacle of the countless mages in this world, so many they're a dime a dozen. I can command tens of thousands with a single word, and you're telling me to watch my tongue?"

"That's the Mages Guild's business, and we..."

"Enough." Abel raised a hand, cutting the Holy Knight off. "Even though we belong to different organizations, you show not even a minimum of respect. I thought we might be able to cooperate, but it seems I was wrong. We'll go our own way, so you all can do as you please."

Abel spat the words out and turned on his heel, giving a look to Lee Sungmin, who was standing there blankly. Lee Sungmin let Abel take the lead and did not interfere. As they were about to get back on the carriage, the Holy Knight spoke urgently.

"W-wait."

"What is it?"

"Cooperation... what do you mean by that?"

"What a stupid question. Weren't you all going to trash that bastard Kim Jonghyun? You have no other choice. An army of Death Knights joined forces with a Black Mage and annihilated a city. Surely, you're not going all the way to Germud just to console the souls of the dead, are you?"

"That's... not it, but..."

"If that's the case, then it's completely different from what we intend to do. No. In the first place, you're not in a position to speak with me, the Mages Guild Master, as an equal. I'm offended. We'll be on our way."

"Please stop."

The messenger was at a loss for words at Abel's relentless barrage. Another Holy Knight stepped forward to take his place, and the messenger quickly stepped back, bowing his head deeply.

"I am Theos, commander of the Silver Knight Order. I apologize for the disrespect."

"I'm glad you recognize it as disrespect."

"As you said, we are heading to Germud to stop and execute the Black Mage Kim Jonghyun and the army of Death Knights who committed the massacre there."

"What are you trying to say?"

"If you and your companion, Mages Guild Master, share the same objective... how about you travel with us?"

"Are you the one in charge here?" Theos was left speechless by Abel's abrupt question. "It seems you still don't grasp who I am. Have you ever heard that the older a mage gets, the shittier their personality becomes? Do you want to experience it firsthand?"

"Please... calm your anger..."

"Anger? No, it's not anger. I'm telling you, it's a shitty personality. You want to experience it?" Abel asked with a wicked grin.

[He has a calamity-level talent for pissing people off.]

The Void Monarch remarked in admiration inside Lee Sungmin's head.
