Baek Museon trudged home, his feet dragging with the weight of his defeat. The dilapidated house was a shabby insult to the name of the Blood Heavenly Demon, who had once stood at the pinnacle of the Blood Heavenly Alliance. He gritted his teeth and shoved the door open with his one remaining hand.

Rage, murderous intent, and shame swirled in his chest. Every step he had taken to get here was a leaden weight, for he felt the phantom sting of mocking gazes with each one.

"...Monster..." Baek Museon's lips parted, the word a faint mutter.

A familiar scent emanated from within the house. It was not the familiar stench of alcohol or drugs, but another scent he knew all too well. Gritting his teeth again, he stalked toward its source.

Jeniela sat on the bloodstained bed, a vision in a form-fitting black dress that was a stark contrast to the kimono she had worn last time. Without even glancing at him as he entered, she spoke, her legs crossed elegantly.

"How pathetic."

A soft chuckle escaped her lips.

"Just four years ago, there wasn't a single person in Trabia who would have ignored you."

Her whisper was all it took for Baek Museon to understand. She had been watching everything. It was hardly a surprise. Jeniela's eyes and ears were scattered across Trabia, and after the commotion he had caused in the Northern District, there was no way she wouldn't know, even if it wasn't her own Central District.

"How does it feel?" Jeniela asked, switching the cross of her legs.

Baek Museon bit down on his lower lip, a lip he had already chewed raw several times. It burst and bled, but he paid it no mind, trying to ignore her words as he dragged himself forward.

"What would've happened if you hadn't been defeated four years ago? If your arm hadn't been cut off? If you'd trained for four years without falling into Despair, you might not have been defeated today."

He didn't want to hear it. His trembling hand reached out and lifted a liquor bottle. Though it was less than half full, Baek Museon downed it in a single go.

"You're just going to keep sitting there, aren't you? And while you do, the Little Heavenly Demon who cut off your arm will continue to grow stronger. The man who defeated you today will also continue to grow stronger. How does that feel? The feeling of sitting alone, doing nothing, watching the backs of those who move ahead?"

"...Shut up..." Baek Museon set the bottle down. His trembling body and voice betrayed the misery he felt.

Jeniela cackled. "No. I wonder if you'll even be able to see their backs. They'll keep moving forward, and you'll just be sitting there. Before long, they'll advance to a place you can't even see, and you will be a loser, always."

He didn't want to hear it. The feelings of self-loathing, defeat, and Despair he had suppressed surged forth, and Baek Museon collapsed weakly to the floor. His eyes fell upon the empty liquor bottle, then to his limp left sleeve.

The fight with Lee Sungmin had been a brutal reminder of just how significant the absence of his left arm was. If it had been intact, he would not have been pushed back so helplessly. Even if he started training again, there was no way to regrow his severed arm. No magic, no potion, could ever change that reality.

"You know the way, don't you?" Jeniela's voice was a demonic whisper from the bed.

Her words made his shoulders tremble. He had heard them countless times over the past few years and had always dismissed them as nonsense. Pride had allowed him to do that. He was fine without an arm. He was down now, but someday he would stand up again, and then there would be no problem. Such a vague belief had sustained him, but not anymore. Now that he had been forced to keenly realize the truth of his missing arm, the baseless faith he had clung to had vanished completely.

"...If I become a Vampire..." Baek Museon's mouth opened.

The moment she heard those words, Jeniela knew it was over. The years of pleasure she had taken in tempting him were drawing to a close. He could no longer hesitate. He was completely broken, completely in Despair.

*Thankfully.*

And regrettably. A small smile formed on Jeniela's lips. She felt a certain gratitude toward Lee Sungmin. If he hadn't defeated Baek Museon, it would have taken much longer for the man to choose the path of the inhuman. At the same time, it was a pity. She had wanted to enjoy it a little longer.

*Fun things always come to an end.*

Jeniela looked down at Baek Museon, who was now looking up at her from the floor. His eyes were desperate, a wretched mix of longing and hesitation. She stuck out her tongue and licked her lips.

*Still, it's fine. The next one is already prepared.*

In truth, she was far more interested in Lee Sungmin than Baek Museon. He was refusing to become a Vampire for now, but how long could he hold out? It seemed the smallest trigger would be enough to make him abandon his humanity.

"Make... me a vampire," Baek Museon pleaded.

At those words, a thrill shot through Jeniela, making her shoulders tremble slightly. To finally hear him say it, to watch him cast aside all his human pride after years of conflict and temptation, was her greatest pleasure. She rose slowly to her feet.

"What do you plan to do once you become a vampire?"

"...I'll kill that bastard."

"Ahahaha!"

Baek Museon would never achieve that wish, because Jeniela would not let Lee Sungmin die. Still, she thought, it might be fine to let Baek Museon give him a Despair comparable to death. Perhaps that could become the trigger.

"Come here," Jeniela said, beckoning with a single finger.

Baek Museon tried to get up. No, not like that. Jeniela shook her head and muttered softly.

"Crawl."

The sound of her giggling laughter made Baek Museon's face contort.

*

The Crazy Heavenly Demon sat sullenly, his puffed-out cheeks cupped in both hands. Across from him, Lee Sungmin sat crookedly, glaring. The Crazy Heavenly Demon glanced at Lee Sungmin's piercing gaze before letting out a deep sigh.

"Hey, don't look at me like that. This one did it all out of concern for Martial God..."

"And what if I'd been weaker than the Blood Heavenly Demon?"

"...If that were the case, I wouldn't have brought you here in the first place."

"Let's say I was on par with him. What would you have done if he'd killed me during our duel?"

"That... I was sure that wouldn't happen. The strength this one saw in you was by no means inferior to Martial God's."

"What if I was about to die anyway?"

"Ah, for goodness' sake! If something like that had happened, this one planned to step in and stop it!" the Crazy Heavenly Demon shouted, unable to hold back any longer.

Lee Sungmin nonchalantly ignored the outburst and picked up a piece of bread. Beside him, Rubia was struggling to cut her meat with a fork and knife.

"It seems you've finished your business," the Crazy Heavenly Demon grumbled. "I'll be staying around here until next year."

"Next year? Does that mean I'm stuck in this city for several months?"

Despite his grumbling, he showed no real signs of dislike. Instead, his eyes shone as he stared at Lee Sungmin.

"You. Have you any thought of becoming this one's disciple?"

At the sudden question, Lee Sungmin, who had been chewing a piece of bread, froze. Seeing his blank expression, the Crazy Heavenly Demon pressed on.

"This one's martial art is called the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, and it's a divine art that won't be outshone anywhere. There are many divine arts in Eria, but this one can assure you, a martial art like it is not common."

"...I don't particularly feel like learning more martial arts."

"What are you talking about? The more you learn, the better. Even if you can't use it properly, a hidden martial art can function perfectly as a trump card!"

Lee Sungmin had already learned three martial arts: the Violet Dawn Divine Art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, and Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Although the latter served as both a movement and an enhanced qi art, it inevitably fell short in specialization when compared to an outstanding enhanced qi art like the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

The idea of learning such an art was tempting, but he couldn't accept it rashly.

*My talent is abysmal.*

Lee Sungmin smacked his lips. He had only managed to overcome his shortcomings with his current arts through the terrible amount of time he had poured into them during Denir's Trial. If he learned the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art now, how long would it take to reach a high level?

[You don't necessarily have to think that way,] Void Monarch interjected. [I'll take your word for it that your talent isn't all that great, but whether it's martial arts or magic, in the end, all rivers flow into the sea. At your level, you should be able to master a newly learned Void Path quickly.]

*Easier said than done.*

[It's not like it'll do you any harm, is it? An enhanced qi art is versatile. It can be applied to all martial arts. At the level you've reached, you'll be able to master it quickly enough to apply it in real combat. Having more techniques at your disposal is a good thing, and you happen to have someone right in front of you who can guide you!]

At Void Monarch's gentle coaxing, Lee Sungmin let out a deep sigh. He had a reason for his pessimism. He had learned the Hundred Steps Divine Fist in the past but still couldn't use it properly. In fact, it was more accurate to say he *didn't* use it, because the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear was simply an overwhelmingly superior art.

"I don't want to become your disciple."

"...Then?"

"Can't you just teach me the martial art?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's shoulders trembled as he muttered, "What an audacious brat..."

Despite his words, he seemed to be thinking it over quite seriously. Not long after, he nodded his head.

"...Well. I suppose it doesn't matter. Just by helping me overcome my lunacy, you can be called this one's benefactor."

"Well, that was in return for accompanying you to the South."

"I'm thinking of it as a wandering trip, so it's not something that needs repayment," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered, reaching into his robes. He pulled out a small Subspace Pocket, and Lee Sungmin couldn't help but look surprised. He hadn't expected the man to carry such an item.

"What's with that expression?"

"I just didn't think an Elder would use something like that."

"What do you think of this one?"

From his manner of speech to his seemingly ignorant demeanor, Lee Sungmin had just thought of him as a martial artist to the core. The Crazy Heavenly Demon reached into the Subspace Pocket and took out a clean book.

"This is the secret manual for the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

"Why are you carrying the secret manual around?"

"After my lunacy started, I wrote down the manual whenever my mind was clear, adding this one's insights, of course. I didn't know when I might die, and I wanted to leave proof that a martial artist named the Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae existed in this world."

He handed the secret manual to Lee Sungmin.

"This one was born in the Central Plains Murim and came to this world several decades ago. I was flustered at first, but I was impressed that the world was wider than the Central Plains Murim, and I liked that."

Lee Sungmin listened as he placed his hand on the manual.

"There were countless powerful individuals in this wide world. This one has been lucky to survive so far, but I knew my luck couldn't last forever. I might die someday. As I suffered from my lunacy, that worry and fear grew. This one wanted to leave something behind, proof that this one, this martial artist, existed in this world. That's why I created the secret manual for the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered for a moment, then burst into a hearty laugh.

"I thought it would be wonderful if someone with a fateful connection learned the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art and dominated the world with it. Though I never thought I'd be handing it over to someone else like this."

"...Don't expect too much."

"You are an outstanding master. They say the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon is amazing, but this one has never seen her in person. So among the young masters this one has seen, you are the best. You should be able to learn the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art quickly."

Listening to the man's words, Lee Sungmin swallowed a sigh. *Is that really true?* Amidst that small worry, he opened the secret manual.

[Will you learn the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art?]

At the voice he hadn't heard in a long time, Lee Sungmin nodded his head.
