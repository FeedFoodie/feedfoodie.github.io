The discovery of a barrier blanketing the entire forest forced Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon to proceed with caution. Smashing a path through the trees was impossible and so was leaping to their tops. Lee Sungmin tried running along branches at a certain height, searching for a way forward, but the barrier's disorienting power permeated every inch of the woods, tangling the senses.

[No caster could maintain a barrier of this magnitude with their own power,] Void Monarch stated with certainty. [Such a being does not exist. There are plenty who could destroy this entire forest, but tangling space to this extent is absolutely impossible.]

"What if the opponent isn't human?"

[It would be the same even for an inhuman shaman who has lived for ages. No, perhaps it is possible, but this is just too sturdy. I can only conclude that the caster formed this barrier by drawing on the power of the land itself.]

This theory prompted a change in approach. Lee Sungmin decided that understanding the Bewitching Forest was more critical than immediately finding a way out. The person he connected with through Nebul was a shaman he had seen before, the very one he had asked to remove the curse on Wiji Hoyeon, only to be told it was impossible.

"We meet again." The shaman, whose beard was a messy tangle, grinned at Lee Sungmin and revealed a set of yellowed teeth.

Lee Sungmin asked with a suspicious frown, "I want to ask about the Bewitching Forest. Why did they connect me to you, a shaman?"

"You really don't seem to know anything," the shaman chuckled, pulling at his twisted beard with his fingers. "The ones who know about the Bewitching Forest are shamans, not information brokers."

He continued, "This is the forest that the great yokai Void Monarch used as his base four hundred years ago. When Void Monarch died, the fears he left behind settled into the land."

[…Hmph.]

A small groan escaped Void Monarch at the shaman's words.

"For four hundred years, the forest warped to match the fear it had consumed, becoming a place that imparts terror to all who enter. It preys on the most common fears that beings harbor for the woods. The dread that if you enter, you can't get out, and you don't know what might pop out… things like that."

[Now I see,] Void Monarch muttered as he listened. [To think the fear this Elder left behind transformed the forest. I thought the Demonic Energy was boiling over for some reason. I never imagined it would turn out like this.]

*You didn't know either?*

[This happened after this Elder lost his body. Even if I was the original owner of that fear, there's no way I would have known.]

"Other Yokai crawled into the forest once it developed its own demonic aura," the shaman explained. "This place is attractive to them, you see. They can receive offerings of fear just by existing here. That's also why information brokers don't know much about the Bewitching Forest. Not many humans who enter here make it out alive."

The shaman laughed heartily before his eyes settled on Lee Sungmin again.

"If I were to give you a piece of advice, don't stay here too long. It's fortunate that it's not night yet. When night falls… it will get tough. You know the forest is pitch black at night, right? You won't know when or what might jump out at you. If that fear continues… people go mad. They go mad with fear, I tell you, and after that? Their minds are stolen by the demonic aura, and they become something that is neither Yokai nor human, wandering the forest until they die. Or they become a meal for a Yokai."

"…Do you know about the Yokai in this forest?"

"Do you know a Yokai called the Red Ghost?"

It was a name he had heard before. Lee Sungmin's thoughts immediately went to Void Monarch, who was psychically connected to him.

[Red Ghost?] Void Monarch's voice was surprised. [Isn't the Red Ghost the King of Monsters in Predator?]

"Are you saying the Red Ghost is here?" Lee Sungmin asked aloud.

"The Red Ghost was originally the King of Monsters who reigned over Urmuri. However, he was defeated in a conflict there a few years ago and went into hiding. Now, they say he's here, in the Bewitching Forest."

[The Red Ghost was defeated?] Void Monarch's voice grew louder in his mind. [That can't be. Even four hundred years ago, he was quite the powerhouse, though not on this Elder's level. Who in the world could have defeated the Red Ghost?]

"…Who defeated the Red Ghost?"

"The one ruling Urmuri now is a fox with nine tails. I don't know if it's her real name or not, but the Yokai of Urmuri call that fox Yana."

[A Nine-Tailed Fox… a tricky Yokai.]

A fox that lived for a long time and took on a yokai nature grew its tails one by one. When it reached nine, it transformed into a Nine-Tailed Fox with immense Demonic Energy.

"The Red Ghost isn't the only one here," the shaman added. "Other Yokai who made a name for themselves in the South are here too. However, the most dangerous one isn't the Red Ghost. There's an immortal monster in this forest."

"What's that now?"

"Exactly what it sounds like. An immortal monster born in the forest. I don't know its name or what it looks like, but it's certain that it lives in that forest. That's all I know. The other shamans probably don't know anything more than this."

The shaman chuckled as he finished. "I'll say it again, be careful of the night. Or just leave the forest. It's probably not too late."

The connection broke, leaving Lee Sungmin in silence. He couldn't blame Void Monarch, who likely had no idea his former home had become such a place.

[What will you do?] Void Monarch asked.

Continuing through the forest was risky in many ways. Crossing the Bewitching Forest, now imbued with Demonic Energy, was one thing, but the presence of the Red Ghost and an immortal monster was another concern entirely. His honest feeling was that he wanted no part of it if he could avoid it.

*Your treasures. Are they valuable enough to embrace this kind of risk?*

[Of course,] Void Monarch replied, his voice filled with conviction. [The treasures this Elder has collected are not mere money. If you obtain them, they will be of great help to you in your future, one way or another.]

The deliberation fell to Lee Sungmin. He currently had three tasks to accomplish in the South. He needed to visit the South tribe to learn how to handle Demonic Energy, and he had to go to the Primordial Forest where the Fist Honored One was in seclusion. Of these, what Lee Sungmin wanted most was to kill the Fist Honored One and end Wiji Hoyeon's curse, but since he had come this far, it did not seem like a bad idea to get Void Monarch's treasure first.

*There has always been danger.*

If the reward was certain, the risk was worth taking. Lee Sungmin stood up, his mind made. He had at least learned several things. The Red Ghost, formerly the King of Monsters in Predator, was in this forest. So was an immortal monster, and this entire place was a mutation caused by the fear left behind by none other than Void Monarch himself.

*I can't turn back after coming this far. So, where are your treasures?*

[If the forest's shape hasn't completely changed, there should be a mansion where this Elder lived in the center of it.]

*Four hundred years have passed. Do you think the mansion is still there? Someone else might have stolen your treasures. You heard him, there are all sorts of Yokai here, right?*

[No. That won't happen,] Void Monarch insisted. [The mansion might be gone, but if you go to that location, you can get the treasure. It's also impossible for anyone else to steal it.]

Since Void Monarch was so adamant, Lee Sungmin had no choice but to believe him for now. He would break through the forest. The shaman had warned him, but he had yet to encounter a situation here that he could not handle.

[He said people go mad with fear. You and that idiot are lucky.]

The idiot Void Monarch referred to was the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

[You two are stained with this Elder's Demonic Energy. Whether you like it or not, you're already standing on the border between human and Yokai. You may feel fear in this forest, but you will not go mad.]

Void Monarch was especially confident about Lee Sungmin. His divine power was so strong it was hard to think of him as human. While the weak-minded would be unable to overcome their fear and go mad, and even if the forest's Demonic Energy could break the spirit of a martial arts master, there was no chance Lee Sungmin would succumb.

[The pursuers who followed you are in for an unfortunate experience,] Void Monarch chuckled.

That was something Lee Sungmin could not afford to worry about. For a moment, he thought of Namgung Heewon. Even though their bond had not been long, he was someone he had called Hyung-nim.

*It can't be helped.*

Namgung Heewon had come here to kill him. Lee Sungmin pushed aside his thoughts of the man. He was not in a position to worry about the well-being of others.

And then night fell.

Aladur had shouted so much that Jaegal Taeryeong, despite the risk of being noticed by the Phantom Spear's group, had no choice but to build a large fire. The blaze threw flickering light against the oppressive darkness, and people gathered around the several bonfires that had been lit. With nearly fifty people, the fires had to be large.

It was not yet time to sleep. Everyone took out food and drinks from their Subspace Pockets. Jaegal Taeryeong glared at Aladur with a displeased look. Knowing he was not well regarded, Aladur sat far from Jaegal Taeryeong and shoved food into his mouth.

"For all the scaremongering, it's not that big of a deal, is it?" Tang Ahee said with a laugh, sitting near Jaegal Taeryeong.

She was right. It was hard to get their bearings and they kept getting lost, but the forest did not feel as dangerous as Aladur had claimed. They had encountered beasts but no Yokai yet. In fact, it would not matter if they did. Even if a powerful great yokai appeared, fifty martial arts masters could hunt it down without difficulty.

"Those were just words he shouted out of his own fear," Jaegal Taeryeong said. "They're not worth paying attention to."

"But it is annoying, isn't it? It's hard to get our bearings. We've already gotten lost several times."

"That can't be helped. This forest is under the influence of an ominous barrier," Jaegal Taeryeong grumbled, his brow furrowed. "I should have come with my uncle if I knew it would be like this."

The Jaegal Clan was skilled not only in martial arts but also in formations. In particular, Jaegal Taeryeong's uncle, the Divine Trickster Jaegal Wonhu, was a man more skilled in magic than martial arts.

"It's too late for that now, isn't it? By the way, Brother. Being like this reminds me of the old days. Remember? When I was a child…"

Tang Ahee chattered with a bright smile, a rare departure from her usual self. Jaegal Taeryeong responded to her words with a faint smile of his own. Moyong Seojin, sitting near her husband, stared silently into the bonfire, the space between them a cold chasm that no flame could warm. Though they were a married couple, no affection remained, a fact that was even more apparent in the current situation. He could not let it show, however. Moyong Seojin swallowed a sigh and stood up.

"Where are you going?" Namgung Heewon asked. He was sitting across from her, hugging his sword.

Jaegal Taeryeong and Tang Ahee also stopped talking and looked at Moyong Seojin. As all eyes focused on her, she let out a short, dry cough and turned her face to the side.

"…To the bathroom."

"Pfft!" Tang Ahee burst out laughing.

Jaegal Taeryeong's face soured with annoyance as he poked the bonfire with a branch. "Be quick about it. The forest is dark."

He must be embarrassed. Moyong Seojin did not resent him. The time for such feelings had passed long ago.

Namgung Heewon slowly stood up. "I'll go with you."

"…What?"

"The forest is dangerous at night. Especially since they say this is a forest of Yokai, we need to be on guard."

"Are you saying you're going to follow a woman to the bathroom right now?" Tang Ahee sneered.

Namgung Heewon's expression did not change. "Since her husband, Brother Jaegal, seems to have a heavy bottom, shouldn't I, his younger brother, go in his stead?"

Jaegal Taeryeong's face turned cold as ice. Moyong Seojin, at a loss for what to do, spun around abruptly.

"…I've learned martial arts too. Compared to you, I may be at a child's level, but I still have the strength to protect myself."

"However…"

"Don't follow me," Moyong Seojin spat out. "Are you trying to insult me?"

Then she walked away from the fire with quick steps. Tang Ahee, glancing at Moyong Seojin's retreating back, let out a sly laugh.

"See? Why did you have to go and do something unnecessary?"

"Just continue your chatter," Namgung Heewon replied, plopping back down in his seat.

Jaegal Taeryeong stared silently at Namgung Heewon's face. If this was not the Bewitching Forest, and if the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps was not here… The thought brought a smirk to Jaegal Taeryeong's lips.

"Heewon. You're getting on in years. Don't you have any thoughts of getting married?"

"Why don't you say that to the young lady from the Tang Clan over there instead of me?"

"True. You're both single, after all. Since we're on the topic, it might be good for you two to become a pair."

"Brother, what are you talking about?" Tang Ahee made a disgusted face.

Namgung Heewon felt it was not even worth refuting and closed his mouth.

A short while later.

KYAAAAAAK!

A scream echoed from not too far away.
