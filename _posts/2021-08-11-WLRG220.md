[He says to knock it off.]

*Shut up,* Lee Sungmin thought, irritated by the Void Monarch's teasing. He walked back to Wiji Hoyeon's hut, clutching the five masks he had received from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

It seemed little time had passed since he had left. He found Wiji Hoyeon just as she was before, standing among the flower buds of enhanced qi with her eyes closed in deep concentration. She surely sensed his return, yet she offered no questions, too immersed in her focus to spare the energy for words.

Lee Sungmin settled down a short distance away and looked down at the masks in his hand. Each one had a different shape, but they were, to be honest, far from cool. A frown creased his brow as he lifted the one shaped like a rabbit.

*What is this?*

[Looks like something the beings of the Fairy Realm would love.]

The Void Monarch was right. Though Wiji Hoyeon's presence kept the fairies from daring to approach, they were making a fuss from a distance, their attention fixed on the masks Lee Sungmin held.

[Look closely at the brow,] the Void Monarch urged, having noticed something.

Lee Sungmin stared at the brow of the rabbit mask and saw the number 1 written in small print.

*I get it.*

He examined the other masks. Rabbit, fox, deer, wolf, and bear. Each comical imitation of an animal face had a number etched upon its brow. The rabbit was 1 and the bear was 5. It appeared to be the order in which he was meant to switch them.

[Try one on.]

He had intended to do so even without the Void Monarch's prompting. Lee Sungmin fitted the rabbit mask, the one marked with a 1, over his face. The reaction was immediate. A stiffness seized his qi center, and when he tried to draw out his inner qi, they responded more sluggishly than before. It was not greatly uncomfortable, however. The speed had only been slightly reduced.

He tried on the masks in sequence. With each change, the suppression of his inner qi grew stronger. By the time he put on the third mask, the deer, his inner qi moved much slower than he expected. Naturally, the movement of his hands as he switched the masks also slowed.

This was not going to be easy. Though it was repetitive, the difficulty of performing face-changing while his inner qi were suppressed was extremely high. Lee Sungmin was certain this training would greatly increase his mastery of the Violet Dawn Divine Art combined with the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. He had to continuously circulate the Violet Dawn Divine Art while focusing on the incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder that produced explosive speed. The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was the unfamiliar part, and with his inner qi held back, he could not generate a satisfactory speed.

[The masks are the surprising part,] the Void Monarch muttered. [Those masks are forcibly suppressing your body just by being worn, and you're not some common practitioner. A Transcendent Realm expert is one of the strongest beings in this world. To be able to suppress inner qi simply by wearing a mask is an astonishing feat.]

*Definitely.*

Lee Sungmin tried on the final one, the bear mask. As soon as he wore it, his inner qi refused to move no matter how hard he tried. His Demonic Energy was the same. Just wearing the mask on his face was enough to completely suppress the power of a master who had reached the Transcendent Realm. If he could figure out the method behind it, the masks could be used for many other things besides training.

[It doesn't seem to be in the realm of magic. This is... I see. Did that Fairy Queen make these? If so, that makes it even stranger,] the Void Monarch chuckled. [It's not just the Spirit Queen, but the Fairy Queen too. Such beings are worlds apart from humans, on a completely different level. They are beings who can cause changes in the world simply by revealing their existence. That's why they don't try to interfere much with the world.]

*The Spirit Queen made her Advent to save Envirus.*

[I don't know the full story, but didn't you hear what the Fairy Queen said? The Spirit Queen should not have made her Advent in this world back then. She manifested to save that mage, Envirus.]

Listening to the Void Monarch, Lee Sungmin silently stroked the mask.

[The Fairy Forest. This Elder had only heard stories of it but never knew where the Fairy Forest was actually located. In reality, this forest exists in a gap, using the Fairy Queen's power to confuse perception. How did the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman know about this forest? And why is the Fairy Queen giving such help to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?]

Their relationship was certainly suspicious. The Fairy Queen's personality was unpredictable, but it was clear she was friendly toward the Chairman. It was not the Chairman he particularly suspected. Rather, the suspicious one was the Fairy Queen herself.

Lee Sungmin had not forgotten what the Fairy Queen, Osla, had said to him last time.

*Perhaps I might be the Apostle of the End.*

He had no desire to be swayed by such thoughts right now, yet he recalled Osla's other words. 'I am friendly toward you.' At the time, given the situation and with his mind cluttered, he had not paid them much mind. Thinking about it now, were Osla's words not the same as a blank check? The one who said it was the Fairy Queen, a truly transcendent being on a different level from humans. He did not know the limits of what she meant by 'friendly', but he thought it would be a good idea to test the waters the next time he met her.

[You're not a complete fool,] the Void Monarch noted with a satisfied smile.

However, that would have to wait. For now, he planned to continue the training the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had assigned him.

It was around sunset when Wiji Hoyeon finally moved. She approached Lee Sungmin, who was sitting on the ground practicing his face-changing, and plopped down in front of him.

"How was the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?"

"I got spanked."

"...What's that supposed to mean?"

"Because I said something I shouldn't have."

Her eyes fell to the masks. "Those masks. Did the Chairman give them to you?"

"Yeah," Lee Sungmin replied, handing the bear mask to her.

After a moment's hesitation, Wiji Hoyeon put it on. She flinched, her shoulders trembling in surprise.

"...My inner qi won't move."

"The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman... I heard the mask Master-nim wears is also like this."

"Is that so?" Wiji Hoyeon chuckled at his answer and took off the mask. She muttered with a bitter expression, "So he defeated me in a duel in seven seconds while wearing a mask like this."

Although her expression was sour, her voice was not dejected. Rather, she seemed stimulated by the fact that the Chairman had been suppressing his power. Wiji Hoyeon nodded and got to her feet.

"I'm not doing it today."

"What?"

"Sex, I mean," she stated in a calm voice.

Lee Sungmin felt like he was going to throw up even though he had not eaten anything. "I wasn't planning on it anyway."

"If you really mean that, I'm a little hurt," Wiji Hoyeon giggled, her eyes curving into crescents.

Lee Sungmin could not tell if she was being serious, so he did not know how to react. Wiji Hoyeon looked at him, let out a soft laugh, and stood up.

"Still, I'm serious about not doing it today. Though I did want to see you beg for it."

"...Didn't you say it hurt?"

"Separate from the pain, there was a sense of fulfillment, but I'm not in the mood for it today. Thanks to the curse, I feel like I've wasted time meaninglessly. I don't want to fall further behind you."

"You'll pull ahead again soon enough."

"Then you'll just have to catch up again," Wiji Hoyeon said with a smile.

It was true. Wiji Hoyeon would continue to grow stronger, and Lee Sungmin did not want to fall behind. Even if his pace was much slower than hers, perhaps so slow it could not even be called progress, he planned to do everything he could.

In the days that followed, a quiet focus settled over their small clearing. Though they shared the same forest and the same spot, Wiji Hoyeon and Lee Sungmin spoke little to each other. She devoted herself to the new martial art she had created, while he devoted himself to the face-changing training the Chairman had assigned him.

*Strange.*

As he repeated the training, Lee Sungmin felt a strong sense of unease. He was getting used to the face-changing faster than he had expected. He knew his own talent, and his limits, well. This was his first time attempting this training, and although it was a variation of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, it might as well have been a completely different martial art.

*My progress is fast.*

Only a week had passed, yet the speed of his face-changing had become incomparably faster than when he first started. The same was true for his mastery of the Violet Dawn Divine Art. The connection with the added incantations of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, in particular, was now so natural that he barely had to think about it. Lee Sungmin was bewildered by his progress, which was faster than he had ever imagined.

[I think it's because of the heart,] the Void Monarch, who had been observing Lee Sungmin's body for a week, offered his own answer. [The monster's heart you possess has evolved your body every time you've faced a crisis. Perhaps even your lack of talent has improved through those repeated evolutions.]

Of course, he could not be certain. The Void Monarch's tone was ambiguous, as if he knew it was too hasty to attribute everything to the heart.

[Or, maybe it's not because of the heart, but simply that you as a person have developed greatly since the days when you had no talent. In fact, I think this is more likely than the heart. You've reached the Transcendent Realm, after all. You must be far less of an idiot than when you first learned martial arts, and you have all the experience you've accumulated to reach this stage.]

Lee Sungmin could not tell which was correct, but the fact that he was learning the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder faster than expected was a good thing. Of course, even if his talent had improved, it could not compare to Wiji Hoyeon's. They both spent the same week dedicating themselves to new martial arts, but her growth rate was astonishing even to his eyes. A calamity of absurd proportions. It was what many had said upon seeing Wiji Hoyeon, and Lee Sungmin was feeling it to his very bones.

When the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman summoned him, Lee Sungmin took out the five masks in front of his hut. The Chairman stood with his hands behind his back.

"Perform the face-changing," he demanded.

Without hesitation, Lee Sungmin's hands moved swiftly. The rabbit mask settled onto his face.

"In order?"

"Just do it."

Lee Sungmin's hands continued to move, the mask on his face changing without a sound. From the rabbit to the bear. After watching him go through all of them, the Chairman spoke again.

"Don't do it in order. Mix them up. I won't give you a sequence."

Even with those words, Lee Sungmin continued the face-changing without difficulty. The rabbit became the bear, the bear became the fox, the fox to the wolf, and the wolf to the rabbit. Introducing variations made controlling his inner qi erratic. Doing it out of order was much trickier than following the sequence.

"This one has misjudged you," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman muttered as he raised his hand, and Lee Sungmin's face-changing stopped. "This one expected it would take at least a month for you to reach a satisfactory level. To think you'd make it this far in just a week."

"...Are you displeased?" Lee Sungmin asked just in case. The Chairman's words, 'I like a struggling mediocrity,' weighed on his mind.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman let out a snort of laughter at the question. "No, I'm not displeased. It's better than I thought, but not to the point of being a calamity. Rather, I think your level is human. You trained for 2100 years in the Mental World. Even if your innate talent is lacking, with all you've done, you should at least be able to do this much."

The Chairman replied with a nod. "Having you wear masks that suppress your inner qi and practice face-changing had two purposes. One was to draw out maximum efficiency from your suppressed inner qi, and through that, to make you accustomed to the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. You understand this part, right?"

Lee Sungmin remained silent.

"The other," the Chairman continued, "is because of the Dragon Heart you consumed."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's expression stiffened.

"You consumed a Dragon Heart and are unconsciously using Dragon's Pressure, but there's nothing special beyond that. I thought that by controlling the Demonic Energy and inner qi in your qi center with the masks, and continuing to do so, you would be able to draw out the magic power of the Dragon Heart that has melted into your body. It's too much of a waste to let that unprecedented power lie dormant in your body without being able to use it, isn't it?"

As he spoke, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman reached into his sleeve. What he pulled out was a mask in the shape of a distorted Ghost Spirit's face.

"It seems drawing out the Dragon Heart's magic power in just a week was impossible. From now on, wear this mask."

"Did the Fairy Queen make that one too?"

"You know well," the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman chuckled at Lee Sungmin's question and nodded.

Lee Sungmin took the mask the Chairman offered and asked cautiously, "Master-nim, what is your relationship with the Fairy Queen?"

*He won't spank me for asking something like this, will he?* Lee Sungmin worried inwardly.
