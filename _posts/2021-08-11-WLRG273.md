The road from Haras to Kron stretched out before them, an enormous distance separating the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance from the Murim Alliance. Though Lee Sungmin had wandered all over Eria for one reason or another, he had never undertaken a journey of this magnitude. Their travels would not even end at Kron, as the plan was to continue onward to North Trabia after their stop.

He was accompanied by the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and Scarlet, but their party gained one more member before they departed. The road was far too long to travel without a carriage, and none among them were suited to drive it.

"My name is Yehwa."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman maintained a secret personal guard within the alliance, a group that followed only his orders and kept a low profile. Red Flower, the unit that had escorted Wiji Hoyeon to Legro Forest, was part of this guard. Yehwa was their leader.

Lee Sungmin glanced at the woman, whose face was hidden by a fox mask and whose hair was cut short around her shoulders, before sending a Thought Transmission to the Chairman.

[Why the mask?]

[She is imitating me.] the Chairman's reply was indifferent.

Hearing that, Lee Sungmin asked again, just to be sure. [Could she be... your hidden daughter or something, Master?]

[Don't be ridiculous. How many times do I have to tell you I have no children?]

[An adopted daughter, then?]

[I don't think of her that way, but the child might see it differently. Most of the ones I've taken as personal guards are orphans with no one to turn to. Not even the bastards from the Beggars' Gang can operate in or around Haras, so the beggars in this city live day by day, pushed around by unorthodox thugs without any backing.]

[So you mercifully took them in?]

[Not exactly. I gathered those whose potential was too great to be wasted on begging, gave them food and shelter, and had them learn martial arts.] the Chairman replied as he walked past Yehwa.

As he passed her, Yehwa bowed her head deeply, not rising until he was completely gone.

[Even though that's all I do, the ones I took in would gladly accept death if I commanded it. It's because I gave them everything they have now. It was easier for me to take in young beggars who couldn't tell right from wrong and instill loyalty by providing for them, rather than taking in a perfectly fine person and trying to earn their loyalty.]

Lee Sungmin glanced back. Yehwa was still bowing. He suspected the Chairman had done more for her and the other guards than he let on. The man he had come to know was a person whose words and actions were often slightly different.

[Is the entire Red Flower coming with us?]

[That would be too conspicuous. Only Yehwa is coming. There's no one else to drive the carriage. The other personal guards, including Red Flower, will be holding their breath inside the alliance while I'm away.]

[Are you worried about the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance?]

Lee Sungmin recalled what the Chairman had said, that during his thousand years of training he had cast aside everything but his desire for martial arts. That statement, too, was something Lee Sungmin did not fully believe. For someone who made such a claim, the Chairman seemed to care a great deal about things other than himself. Even while secluded in Legro Forest, he had periodically intervened in the alliance with Osla's help and had even created a personal guard to monitor its internal situation.

[It is the minimum awareness.] The Chairman's steps halted in front of the carriage. [An awareness of the position I stand in. I am not reigning supreme alone. One way or another, I am the Coalition Leader of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. I never wished for it, but many people's lives hang by a thread at my feet.]

[You don't want to let them die?]

[If they die, they die, but did I not say it? It is the minimum awareness.] the Chairman said, climbing into the carriage where Scarlet was already waiting.

The carriage itself, enchanted with various magics for convenience, cost more than a decent mansion and was so comfortable it could hardly be called a carriage at all.

"We will depart now," Yehwa announced as she opened the door. She had removed the fox mask, likely because a masked coachman would arouse suspicion from even a passing child.

When the Chairman nodded, Yehwa immediately climbed onto the driver's seat and set the carriage in motion.

"So, what do you think?" the Chairman asked.

Lee Sungmin, who had been about to sit cross-legged and enter meditation, realized the question was for him and opened the eyes he was about to close. "About what?"

"That girl. Pretty, isn't she?"

"...What are you talking about all of a sudden?"

"You act so innocent, but you're always luring women, so I'm just asking. If you said the word, Yehwa would strip naked in front of you without a second thought."

"Hey! I'm right here, you know?" Scarlet, who had been taking out a magic tome, spat out in a fierce voice, glaring at the Chairman.

"It's not like we're discussing something you shouldn't hear, is it?"

"It's not a story I particularly want to hear, either," Scarlet grumbled.

The Chairman chuckled at her words and nodded. "My consideration was lacking."

"How very kind of you. Unlike how you treat me."

"I'm not a man without manners."

"Then why don't you show those manners to me?"

"To me, manners are just a suitable sense of distance," the Chairman said with a smirk.

Hearing this, Scarlet snorted. She had felt it to some extent. What the Chairman showed her was not simple goodwill but the indifference of a bystander keeping a careful distance.

"Should I keep my distance from you, my disciple, as well?"

"...Just act as you usually do."

"Anyway, I don't care if you mess with Yehwa, but if you're going to do it, do it somewhere the Red Tower Master and I won't know."

"I won't."

"Acting so virtuous."

Ignoring the Chairman's taunt, Lee Sungmin closed his eyes and entered meditation. Inside his head, the Void Monarch cackled.

[Acting so prim and proper. Wouldn't it be better to enjoy yourself when you can?]

*Why are you spouting bullshit too?*

After spitting that out, he cleared the distracting thoughts from his mind and replayed his most recent fight. It was the battle with the Spear King. He had to admit it. The Spear King's martial arts were on a level several steps higher than his own. He had tried to compensate with internal energy greater than the Spear King's, but the difference in class created by his opponent's technique was not something that could be overcome with overflowing energy alone.

Even after unleashing the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear's Eighth Form, Illusion World, he had failed to finish the fight. It was true that he had gained a certain advantage, but the technique was a double-edged sword. If sustained for too long, his Demonic Energy would run rampant.

*I have to find a way.*

Somehow. Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip.

{sep}

Night fell, and the carriage came to a halt. Yehwa, the driver, insisted she could continue, but the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had a different opinion. At his words asking if there was any need to hurry, she did not argue and quietly gathered firewood to start a fire.

Lee Sungmin had too much to think about and could not sleep. The Chairman had calmly gone inside the carriage, but Lee Sungmin sat near the campfire, watching the flames. The story he had heard from Abel was still confusing him, as was the ambiguous state of his own body.

Not far away, Scarlet sat huddled inside a thick robe. Lee Sungmin glanced at her as she wore her glasses and moved a pen across a page.

"What are you writing?"

"A magic tome," Scarlet answered. "It's a hassle, but I thought it would be better to write it down."

"Are you planning to donate it somewhere?"

"At first, I was thinking of donating it to the Mages Guild to foster future generations, but on second thought, I don't think those blockheads in the Council of Elders would stand by and watch the young ones succeed. Even if I donated it so everyone could read it, those bastards would just keep it for themselves and never make it public."

Lee Sungmin did not know much about the Mages Guild, but through Abel's story, he had learned that the Council of Elders was rotten to the core.

"But I have to finish it first. It's too precious to keep to myself. Someday... if the opportunity arises, there will be a time to release it," Scarlet muttered, taking off her glasses. She stretched out her arms and cracked her neck from side to side before getting up and approaching the carriage. "I'm tired, so I'm going to go in and sleep first."

"The Master is inside."

"I don't think he's shameless enough to grope my sleeping body, do you?"

"I suppose not."

Scarlet giggled at his reply. As she was opening the door, she paused and looked back at him. "You seem to have a lot on your mind. If you think and think and still can't find an answer... sometimes not thinking at all can be the answer."

Feeling as if his inner thoughts had been exposed, Lee Sungmin gave an awkward laugh.

Scarlet shrugged. "Well, I say that, but I don't know what you're worried about, and it's not like I want to hear about it either. I don't want to listen to someone else's problems and then have to rack my brain to give helpful advice."

"...That's fine."

"Then that's a relief, but you know, if you're really frustrated and struggling, I can comfort you in a wholesome way."

"What's a wholesome way?"

"The exact opposite of an unwholesome way," Scarlet said with a mischievous smile. "Or, now that you're all grown up and not a little kid anymore, does the unwholesome way sound more appealing?"

"No."

"Answering with such a straight face, you're going to hurt my feelings."

"Is that a joke too?"

"You seemed so tense, I was just trying to lighten the mood. Of course, I have no intention of comforting you in an unwholesome way just because you want it. I just mean I'll have a drink with you and listen to you vent. While nodding along soullessly, of course. I hear you have some really good alcohol, right?"

"I do have some."

"Save it. I'll get my fill later. I'm tired today, so I'm really going to sleep." Scarlet made a show of opening her mouth wide and yawning. "Good night."

With that, she went inside the carriage, leaving only Lee Sungmin and Yehwa by the fire. He glanced at Yehwa, who was sitting across from him. They had not had a private conversation since their initial introduction.

"Do you require comfort?" Yehwa asked abruptly.

Her words made him recall the Chairman's joke, and his expression hardened. "I'm fine."

"If So Ryeonju requests it, I will meet your expectations at any time."

The title 'So Ryeonju' felt unfamiliar. Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly and shook his head. "I will never make such a request, so please don't keep it in mind."

"Am I not enough to satisfy you?"

"No, it's not that." Lee Sungmin looked at her face and let out a deep sigh. Despite saying something so embarrassing, there was not a hint of shame on her face. It was as if she was simply acting as she was supposed to.

[You bastard. A guy who can't even take what's offered.]

*If you devour everything that's offered, are you even human? You're an animal.*

[Why won't you eat when you're offered something so delicious?]

*Because I'm not hungry.*

[It's been a while since you've eaten, and you're not hungry? What a strange fellow...]

*Can you please stop with the cheap metaphors?*

The Void Monarch chuckled at his retort. [I'm just frustrated because you're wallowing in such depressing thoughts.]

*Isn't it understandable to be frustrated?*

[No matter how much you rack your brain, you can't find the answer right now. The imbalance of your Demonic Energy is beyond your control, and the story about The End is also something you can't solve at this moment. You have a tendency to rush things.]

*I don't understand.* Lee Sungmin pressed his hands to his throbbing head. *I... I've always wondered about that. Why me? Grasping the Stone of Reincarnation was a simple coincidence, but everyone I met after returning to the past spoke as if I were special. That was the question. I had just returned by chance, so why did they call me special?*

He knew the answer now. It just did not matter who it was. From the moment someone died holding the Stone of Reincarnation and returned to the past, the 'me' from the previous life became unimportant. The act of reincarnating itself was enough to become an Observer, fated to lead the world to The End.

*It could have been anyone. Yes. I was just an unlucky 'someone' who got caught up in it, and so... I was thrown into this situation. Into The End that began because of me.*

[It could have been anyone. It didn't have to be you specifically.]

*I don't feel responsible. I just don't want to die.*

[You seem to have quite a lingering attachment to life.]

*I wanted to live a better life than my previous one.* Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky. *Early in this life, you know. I had that thought. Because the me from my past life was nothing special. So... I've lived until now. Not just through my own effort and strength, but because of the Grace of Fate. It's not in vain. No matter what, I survived, and I've gained power incomparable to my past life.*

[You don't seem satisfied, though.]

*My past life.* Lee Sungmin closed his eyes. *It was an insignificant life. A life as a mercenary. Living day to day, drinking. Never knowing when I might die... but still. It wasn't as frustrating as it is now. Reality was shit and my situation was pathetic, but it wasn't as frustrating as this.*

The Void Monarch listened in silence.

*What can I do?*

And.

*What on earth am I?*

[You are you.] The silence broke. The Void Monarch answered. [You are you. You are Lee Sungmin, you foolish bastard, and... in this Elder's opinion, it couldn't have been just anyone. I think it had to be you. That you had to be the one to grasp the Stone of Reincarnation and return to the past.]

His voice grew subdued.

[Why did I die? That was a question, but I didn't pay it much mind. Questioning it won't tell me why I died, and even if I found out, it's not like I could go back to before it happened.] To the Void Monarch, his own death was only worth that much. [I am here. That is enough. The me that is here is with you. Even though I died, the me that is here is the Void Monarch. It's the same for you. Even though you died and returned here, you are Lee Sungmin. The idiotic Lee Sungmin. Do not doubt him.]

*I'm not you.*

[There you go with your idiotic words again. Of course you're not me. There's a difference in the size of our dicks alone.] The Void Monarch guffawed. [Perhaps this Elder... thinks he might have died just to meet you.]

*What?*

[They say there are no coincidences, don't they?] His laughter grew louder. [That's not to say I resent you. Rather, it's enjoyable and intriguing. Was your existence so valuable that my death had to be forced?]

The Void Monarch recalled the unidentified being that had been inside Lee Sungmin's mind.

Shhh.

Recalling the image of the monster putting a finger to its lips, the Void Monarch said no more.
