"Guuuaaaah…"

A pained groan tore from the Demon King of Fear as the darkness composing his body shrank to a mere remnant. The owner of the Battle God Chronicle sneered at the diminished figure.

"Then why were you acting so tough?"

In response to the mocking question, the Demon King could only manage another groan. Just moments ago, he had been defiant, openly showing his emotions to the Spirit King and the Fairy King. Now, that defiance was gone, and he could not possibly do the same with the monster standing before him.

It was not just him. The Spirit King and the Fairy King felt it too, and he could sense the onlookers peering into the library shrinking back in fear.

Lee Sungmin held his breath, his eyes fixed on the owner of the Battle God Chronicle, who had the distinct appearance of a human dressed in a loosely unbuttoned shirt. He remembered what the Demonic Spirit had told him about this man. Originally, humans could not escape the Cycle of Mortality, but the owner of the Battle God Chronicle had been the first exception, becoming an Absolute Being in a human body. His precedent made it possible for humans in every dimension to follow. The Battle God Chronicle itself was created by those who shed their physical forms to become beings of pure consciousness.

"Take a good look," the Void Monarch said, stepping back from his position in front of Lee Sungmin. He glared at the owner of the Battle God Chronicle with a displeased expression, and he was not alone. Every being from the Chronicle who had Advented in the library stared at their owner's back with open hostility.

"That's the impossibly arrogant owner of the Battle God Chronicle," the Void Monarch continued. "He's the one who stands at the end of the solitary path, daring everyone who walks it to try and reach him."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman added his own warning. "If you ever intend to join the Battle God Chronicle, you must remember that overwhelming power. The very reason for our existence is the ambition to press forward without falling into Despair, to one day stand at the end of that path yourself."

In that moment, Lee Sungmin understood the hostility they all showed their owner. Their ultimate goal was to reach the man standing at the end of that solitary path and surpass him. The Spear King clenched and unclenched his empty fists, his teeth grinding with a sharp sound. For a man who possessed a deeper obsession with martial arts and combat than anyone else in life, the Battle God Chronicle was the ultimate hell.

Lee Sungmin was no different. A torrent of thoughts flooded his mind as he watched the owner. He tried to gauge the being's power, drawing on all his knowledge, but it was impossible. This was a true Absolute Being, one who made the status of a Transcendent seem laughable. He was the first precedent, a once-weak human who had cast off the inescapable Cycle of Mortality. He was the monster that everyone in the Battle God Chronicle aimed to overcome.

*Someday… I too…*

Lee Sungmin knew he would walk the same path. There might be a difference in his pace, but he would still advance along that solitary road. The thought sent a shiver down his spine, but what he felt was not fear. It was a mix of hostility and exhilaration, the same emotion every being from the Battle God Chronicle felt right now.

"W-what in the world do you think you're doing…" the trembling Fairy King finally spoke. His attitude was a complete reversal from the sneering confidence he had shown the Demon King of Fear. It was only natural. The Battle God Chronicle, a world of battle junkies, had directly Advented here in the neutral territory of the library. It could no longer even be called an Advent. The library was already connected to the Battle God Chronicle, its neutral ground now the Chronicle's domain.

Even though the Fairy King was here to convey the will of the Fairy Realm, his realm could do nothing to ensure his safety in this situation. He wondered if the owner would truly annihilate him, an envoy of the Elemental Realm, but he could not even trust that thought. Gaia, the dimension where the owner himself resided, was famous as the graveyard of Transcendents.

"He was asking to be devoured, so I obliged," the owner of the Battle God Chronicle answered simply.

"Wasn't the Great Demon World the same?"

"The intentions of the Elemental Realm and the Fairy Realm are different…"

"B-but this is a Slaughterhouse for everyone! We agreed to a grace period, to wait and see…"

"You didn't make that agreement with me," the owner said with a smile. It was an absurd claim, but it was enough to shut the mouths of the Fairy King and the Spirit King.

The Demon King of Fear, barely maintaining his existence, squirmed. "E-even if you are the owner of the Battle God Chronicle. The will of the Great Demon World…"

"I already told you."

With a flick of his fingertip, the owner of the Battle God Chronicle unleashed a terrible sound.

PWAAANG!

The last remnant of darkness that was the Demon King of Fear was utterly annihilated.

"If you don't like what I'm doing, tell the Demon God of the Great Demon World to come here himself."

Horrified, the Fairy King and Spirit King scrambled far back. The Demon King of Fear had truly been annihilated. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard. He could annihilate a spiritual body, but to extinguish a high-status Demon Lord with a mere flick of a finger was impossible for him.

"And give us some ground," the owner continued. "I went and created the Battle God Chronicle, but we don't have a single world to call our own."

"B-but you have Gaia…"

"That's my world. Are you deaf? What I'm demanding is for you to concede a decent world to the Battle God Chronicle. Or should I just invade and take one for them?"

The Fairy King and Spirit King did not answer. They intuited that a single wrong word could lead to war. At the owner's words, the beings of the Battle God Chronicle gathered there shot him looks of keen anticipation. War was not something they wished to avoid. It was something they welcomed.

"...The Demon King of Fear has been annihilated." Lee Sungmin, who had been silent, finally spoke. As he did, the owner of the Battle God Chronicle turned his head and glanced at him. The gaze made Lee Sungmin feel as if his breath had stopped, but he could not let that stop him from speaking.

"...If that happens, what becomes of the souls contracted to the Demon King of Fear?"

"They will reincarnate," the owner answered, a look of deep satisfaction spreading across his face as if he had been waiting for that very question.

Seeing that grin, Lee Sungmin knew. The annihilation of the Demon King of Fear had been a thoroughly calculated act, and in a way, it had been for his sake. If Lee Sungmin himself had annihilated the Demon King, the Great Demon World would have intervened directly to annihilate him in turn.

"Then in the 'next' world, the Lich who contracted with the Demon King of Fear won't exist."

At those words, the Spirit let out a groan while the Demonic Spirit cried out in admiration. With the Demon King's annihilation, Preskan's soul would flow into the cycle of reincarnation. That meant Preskan's existence would disappear from the records of Eria.

"The Black Heart won't exist either."

The Black Heart was an accidental product created by Preskan. Even Arbes, a Black Mage superior to Preskan, could not replicate it after hearing the method.

"So I'm the last one."

As long as the Black Heart no longer existed, a being like Lee Sungmin would never be created in the next world. Even the slightest possibility was now completely gone.

The owner of the Battle God Chronicle turned his head with a satisfied smile. "The reason to maintain the Slaughterhouse has completely disappeared."

"W-we can't say anything. We are, after all…"

"It's fine, it's fine. This isn't bad news for you guys either. I quite like the Fairy Realm and the Elementals. I know a woman who's an Elemental," the owner said in an amiable tone, a friendly attitude completely different from how he had treated the Demon King of Fear. "The Great Demon World is just too greedy. There's no one else as hell-bent on invading and conquering other dimensions to expand their territory, right?"

"Yes… that's true…"

"They are violent and greedy…"

The Fairy King and Spirit King listened with an intimidated air.

"That guy is just as violent and greedy," the Void Monarch grumbled under his breath. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman nodded in agreement, and Lee Sungmin inwardly thought the same.

"So, that's what I'm saying. Let's just exclude the Great Demon World."

"...Pardon…?"

"Wh-what do you mean by that…?"

"You guys also understand that this is a world worth watching, so you decided on a grace period, right? Now that the Battle God Chronicle has Advented here, things have no choice but to go as I wish. This world is already the domain of the Battle God Chronicle and my Sanctuary." The owner grinned, baring his teeth. "That said, I don't plan on kicking you all out. You've contributed a lot of power until now, and I'm not so shameless as to just take the good stuff for myself. Do you know what I mean?"

Lee Sungmin let out a dry laugh as he listened to the owner persuade the two kings. What he was saying was simple, so simple it was a problem.

"We don't need The End. Creating a Slaughterhouse greater than this one has become completely impossible. So this world will be left as it is, forever. In fact, that will yield better results than restarting it. We can also exclude the Great Demon World, which we don't like, so it's a happy outcome for everyone."

"The Elemental Realm supports your decision."

"The Fairy Realm also supports your decision."

The ones who answered were not the kings, but the masters of their dimensions, who intervened directly through them to convey their will. With that final answer, they escaped the space, clearly wanting no further involvement. The owner of the Battle God Chronicle smiled broadly at the response they left behind.

"Good."

The three greatest powers that constituted Eria were the Elemental Realm, the Fairy Realm, and the Great Demon World. Even with the Great Demon World removed, Eria would not collapse as long as the Battle God Chronicle had directly Advented.

Still, a thought occurred to Lee Sungmin, and he had to ask. "How do you plan to exclude the Great Demon World?"

"It's a world that was created in the first place. You just have to pull out what was plugged in," the owner answered with an indifferent tone. It was easier said than done. Without the consent of a being of that status and the other worlds, such a thing was impossible to even attempt.

"There, everything has turned out as you wished," the owner said, looking at Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin felt a little dazed as he stared back. "...Aren't you being a little too friendly?"

"What's so strange about that?" the owner asked. "In this limited world, three people have entered the Battle God Chronicle in this iteration alone. A world like that is more than enough for me to show interest and treat favorably. Besides, that's just three for now. You're already scheduled to join, and it looks like a few more will enter in the future." He chuckled. "Of course I'd like it."

"Isn't this the situation you wished for? You did well, in your own way. You're quite strong, but there's a limit to what you can force with just that much power. It might be different for a single world, but this one has many things involved. If you had tried to do something on your own, you would have died here."

"...What will happen to the Great Demon World?"

"Even if a pissed-off Great Demon World tries to invade, they won't have a proper way to do it. The Black Mages in this world will disappear, so opening a passage from the inside will be impossible. In that case, they'd have to try and punch a hole from the outside, and that would be great for 'us'. We'd get to have some fun."

At his words, everyone from the Battle God Chronicle let out expectant smiles. It was just as the Fairy King had said, a group of battle junkies.

"Don't think I'm just giving all this away. I started all this because there's something I want too."

"What do you want?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking straight at him.

The owner's lips curled into a smile. *Look at this one.* "Aren't you scared of me?"

"I'd be scared if you acted scary."

"Hahaha! It's fine, I'm not a scary person. What I want from you… before that, let me tell you what I've done for you." The owner raised his hand, and the space trembled. The key in Lee Sungmin's hand shivered and then disintegrated into particles of light. Then, all the records of the library opened, filling the space around them.

"The talks with the Fairy Realm and the Elemental Realm are already over," the owner said, looking into the records. "Soon, a new Spirit King will be dispatched to take charge of this world, and the Fairy Queen here will continue to exist. The fake divinities… well, we can leave them be. They have their own followers and faith. They're just created beings, so they'll only reach the level of Transcendents at best, but we need guys like that around too."

"...What happens to the people who died in this world?"

"They can't be brought back to life," the owner said first. "Even if it's a world that was created and has gone through repetitions, what can't be done, can't be done. Those who lived and died here are already recorded as souls in this library. This desolate place is Eria's underworld. Now that the laws that sealed this world have disappeared, the souls trapped here will be guided to the real underworld and will reincarnate."

At that answer, Lee Sungmin let out a sigh of relief. Wishing for the dead to be revived was too much to ask. Reincarnation was an absolute law that applied to all dimensions. Eria had been an exception because it was not a 'real' world, but now that the Battle God Chronicle had made this place its domain, Eria had become real. As such, it could no longer escape the law of reincarnation.

"Now that it's become a real world, there's no need for administrators."

"W-wait…!" the Spirit shouted urgently.

The Demonic Spirit, surprisingly, accepted it calmly. His purpose had been to help this world escape the fate of The End, and he had achieved what he wanted.

"It's fine. I'm not killing you."

THUD!

A woman fell from the empty air, letting out a groan as she writhed on the ground. Lee Sungmin stared down at her blankly.

"Spirit Medium?"

"Ugh… Uuuugh…!" the Spirit screamed, now possessing the Spirit Medium's body as a real physical form.

Unlike the woman who had fallen in an unsightly manner, the Demonic Spirit landed properly beside her. He was a young man with a more ordinary impression than expected.

"The things that were only allowed in the Slaughterhouse have also disappeared," the owner continued. "Wiji Hoyeon… yes. The absurd talent given to this kid will disappear. The restrictions placed on the growth of all beings have also disappeared. To put it simply, this world has become a real world."

He looked at Lee Sungmin. "Now then. This is everything I've done for you. I've granted everything you wished for. In comparison, what I demand from you is nothing much. In fact, it was more that I needed an excuse to clash with the Great Demon World than a demand." He grinned. "That's not to say I don't want anything at all."

Lee Sungmin tensed, watching him.

"I need faith."

"...Pardon?"

"Faith, I said, faith. I want to make the Battle God Chronicle into a real divine realm, but no matter how great I am, nothing works without faith, and if I try to gather faith in my own world… religion is already firmly established there. Ah, aren't you from Korea too? Christianity, or Buddhism… do you think I can compete with them and gather faith?"

It did not seem possible. Lee Sungmin stared blankly, having never expected such a demand. The owner of the Battle God Chronicle put on a solemn expression and patted Lee Sungmin's shoulder.

His voice was gentle, but his eyes were murderous.

"Do a good job."

"Preach diligently. Got it?"

"Yes," Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation.
