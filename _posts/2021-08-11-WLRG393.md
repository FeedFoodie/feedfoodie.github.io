*What should I do.*

Scarlet held her breath, a silent observer to the unfolding catastrophe. She desperately tried to decide on a course of action, but her mind was a blank slate, devoid of any right judgment or strategy.

*No, is there even a right answer to begin with?*

She clenched and unclenched her fists, her gloved hands slick with sweat. An uncontrollable tremor seized her shoulders and legs, a physical manifestation of the terror she knew all too well.

"Are you just going to watch?"

Jeniela's voice sliced through the air, and Scarlet swallowed hard. *She noticed me? Of course, she did. What do I do?* She gnawed on her lower lip, her mind racing.

On the ground, Baek Sogo twitched, a gaping hole torn through her stomach. Jeniela simply giggled and poured Elixir into the wound. Bubbles frothed up from the injury that had been gushing blood only moments before.

Scarlet had never imagined a scene like this. She had never wanted to.

She had kept thinking everything was easier than expected. The Lich, the Death Knights, the Yokai, none of them had been particularly difficult to face. This was partly because her group was well-prepared and partly because the enemy's advance had lacked any real power. Annihilating them all had taken time, but there had been no major issues. When communication with Teresa was cut off, she had anticipated a difficult situation to some extent.

She just had not wanted to imagine it would be this horrific.

Scarlet's gaze shifted to Lee Sungmin. A spike had pierced his death trap, and his burst eyeball dangled from the red point protruding from his left eye socket. She grabbed her own trembling shoulders, trying to hold herself together.

*What would change if I joined in?*

The thought was a cold splash of reality. She was confident in her skills, but she was not arrogant enough to challenge the impossible. Thinking about it calmly, she knew her presence in the fight would not be enough to turn the tables. The most she could do was die with them so they would not be lonely on their way.

"I don't mind if you just watch," Jeniela whispered in a small voice, a hint of amusement in her tone. That might be fun too.

As she spoke, she moved her hand. Baek Sogo's body, wrapped in red magic, floated into the air. Baek Sogo was human. It was impossible for her to regenerate injuries in an instant like Lee Sungmin or Jeniela. Her face was pale with pain as the Elixir worked to regenerate the wound, but the process was agonizingly slow.

"Let... go...!" Baek Sogo still moved, swinging her arm wide in an attempt to break free from Jeniela's restraints.

"No you don't," Jeniela sneered.

THWACK!

The projected magic power shattered Baek Sogo's swinging arm. Baek Sogo bit down hard, swallowing the scream that threatened to erupt from her throat.

"Stop...!" Lee Sungmin roared, twisting his body with all his might.

CRACK, CRUNCH.

He forcibly pulled himself off the magic spike piercing his death trap. Bones broke and muscles tore as his body was ripped apart. Overwhelming pain shook his mind, but he did not even scream. The mental anguish was far worse than the physical torment. He watched as Jeniela's hand closed around Baek Sogo's throat.

Free from the spike, Lee Sungmin crawled on the ground like an insect. He pushed off with his rapidly regenerating arms, then kicked off the ground with newly formed feet. He howled like a beast as he charged forward.

"That's a pleasant sound." Jeniela nodded and giggled. Without looking back, she unleashed her magic.

Lee Sungmin tore through the oncoming power with his bare hands, his eyes fixed on Jeniela's back. His gaze met Baek Sogo's, who was still caught in the monster's grip.

"You should have just stayed down," Jeniela sneered, manipulating her magic.

The distance was too short. It was too late to dodge. He had almost no Fairy's Blessing left.

Just then, Rubia shot out from Lee Sungmin's embrace. She did not know if this was the right timing, but if she did not do something now, that monstrous queen would kill everyone.

*Please, let it be enough.*

Rubia squeezed her eyes shut and prayed.

KRAAAANG!

The blessing bestowed upon her by the Spirit Queen erupted in a brilliant light. Jeniela's magic, unleashed at point-blank range, failed to penetrate the barrier Rubia had created.

"Oh my." Jeniela's eyes widened in surprise. She had never imagined a mere artificial Elemental could block her power.

"Go...!" Rubia screamed.

Lee Sungmin, though startled by Rubia's defense, did not hesitate and moved past her. Once more, the pinnacle of martial arts gathered at the tip of his spear.

"No," Scarlet muttered, her voice a choked whisper.

Lee Sungmin's headless body teetered and then collapsed.

"Was that too much?" Jeniela muttered with a frown. "He's not supposed to die yet."

She slammed Baek Sogo's body into the ground. The earth heaved as Baek Sogo was driven deep into the dirt.

"Aaaargh!" Osla screamed. He broke free from Jeniela's restraints, blood pouring from all over his body. His desperate struggle, however, could not change the situation. Jeniela's magic ripped his wings to shreds, and his body plummeted downwards.

"No... no..." Rubia muttered from where she lay limply on the ground, her expression terrified.

"Is he alive?"

Lee Sungmin's headless body twitched. Jeniela smiled sweetly as she watched the severed neck bubble and boil.

"He's alive," she muttered, giving the body a light kick.

"I can't," Scarlet whispered, looking down at her clenched fists. Logically, running away was the right choice. She did not have the power to reverse this situation. She knew that well, and she did not think such an action was wrong. In the end, was her own life not the most important thing? Even if the world was ending, surviving the present moment was what mattered. She was not so young that she could not distinguish between courage and recklessness. If the world were to end tomorrow, she believed it was better to just eat one more apple than to plant an apple tree.

"I can't do it."

*It's not rational. You know it's a pointless death. This isn't courage, it's recklessness.*

*Still, what I can't do, I can't do. I just can't stand by and watch. I know this is a foolish thing to do.*

Scarlet took a deep breath. *Since you've decided to do this, don't have any regrets. Promise. No regrets, and no resentment.*

"I'll make you pay for this later." *If I can, that is.*

She stepped out from behind the tree where she had been hiding. She steadied her trembling legs and cast a spell, reinforcing her wavering mind with magic. Still, the fear did not disappear.

"So you came out?" Jeniela grinned, showing her teeth. "I thought you'd run away. You're a nicer girl than I thought. Or maybe just a bigger fool."

Scarlet did not answer. The location was not good. She had set up magic in this area beforehand, but it had all been destroyed during the fight between Lee Sungmin and Jeniela.

*So unnecessarily strong. How can I even have hope like this?*

*No, don't regret it. You knew this and still came forward. What did you think you could do by coming out?*

Jeniela slowly approached her. Lee Sungmin's headless body was still just twitching. No matter how powerful his regenerative abilities were, it would take a long time to grow back an entire head.

From a deep crater, Baek Sogo was pushing herself up. Every bone in her body was broken and her internal organs were damaged, but she somehow managed to get up, glaring at Jeniela. A whirlwind of emotions churned inside her. Fear, despair, regret, hatred. In that abyss of emotions, only Jeniela felt ecstasy. To her, the forest under the full moon was a banquet hall where the mood was perfectly ripe.

"Come on, you bitch!" Scarlet yelled. She cursed to shake off her fear and swung her wide-open hands. Red runic characters completely surrounded her.

Jeniela laughed loudly. "It's been a while since I've been cursed at like that!"

She kicked off the ground. Spatial magic calculated her movement speed. Scarlet had no talent for improvising and throwing magic around. *I have to land a counter based on precise calculations. There's no time. I can't use any big spells.* Even if it was inefficient, she had no choice but to hit her directly with magic power or focus on simple spells.

Scarlet used Blink to increase the distance, her mind racing to calculate Jeniela's speed. Matching it, she instantly deployed her magic. She focused on the fastest spells she could cast, turning simple masses of magic into projectiles. She layered shields to reinforce their durability and created thousands of magic bullets. If she had a little more time, she would have calculated and inputted movement patterns, but that was asking for too much.

Thousands of magic bullets split the night sky. They gathered into a single point and pierced through Jeniela's own magic. Jeniela grinned as she watched the bullets converge and bore in.

KABOOM!

A single swing of Jeniela's hand annihilated them all.

*You monster bitch...! Her power is so much stronger than I thought. Are simple magic bullets not enough? Should I change the array to increase the power, or should I take the time to prepare a grand spell?*

No, there was not enough time for either. Jeniela was right in front of her. Scarlet's eyes flew open as she poured all her magic into her shield.

Jeniela's fingernails shattered the shield with ease. The impact sent a spray of blood from Scarlet's mouth as her body flew backward and tumbled across the ground.

"Aaaaaah!" Baek Sogo screamed and charged at Jeniela's back.

"Use your words, use them. I'm tired of just hearing screams."

"It hurts...!" Scarlet muttered, coughing up blood. She had resolved not to regret this, but regret was already flooding in.

Jeniela spun around to face the charging Baek Sogo. Her fist flew toward Jeniela's chest. Just as Jeniela snatched her hand, about to break it, something else happened.

"......"

Jeniela's body went tumbling across the ground. Before she even stopped rolling, the Black Dragon Knight charged at her with full speed.

*You're here too?*

Jeniela's body rose unnaturally, supported by red magic. "There should be one more, shouldn't there?"

The Black Dragon Knight did not answer. Teresa was nowhere to be seen. Lee Sungmin had fallen headless. Osla, his wings torn, was impaled on a spike of magic, gasping in pain. Baek Sogo could not move properly, and Scarlet was also injured.

Jeniela laughed loudly and shot out blades of magic. The Black Dragon Knight's skin was covered in jet-black scales.

SKRRRT!

Jeniela's magic failed to pierce the Dragon's scales and deflected to the side.

"It's high-performance, but not very good-looking. You look like a lizardman," Jeniela sneered.

The Black Dragon Knight reached out and grabbed her neck. He immediately tightened his grip, crushing it. Her head exploded under the pressure, sending out a fountain of blood.

"I asked where the other one is." *Why one person.* He did not ask aloud. He already knew. He had seen it before coming here. The Black Dragon Knight smiled with satisfaction as he recalled the corpse of the dead Spear King. To die like that in the end, what was so amusing that he was smiling? With half his torso blown away, only his head had barely remained, smiling.

*Didn't he say he wanted to fight the Martial God? Didn't he leave behind such lingering regrets as he died? I envy that.*

THWACK!

Jeniela's magic pierced the Black Dragon Knight's shoulder. The condensed power was strong enough to penetrate the Dragon's scales. His body momentarily staggered, but he did not stop and continued to advance. *Teresa's body is nowhere to be seen... Did she not even leave a corpse behind? Or did she survive?* The martial arts he unleashed next shattered Jeniela's magic and pressed down on her.

Jeniela took his blows with her bare body. Even as she was bursting apart, her magic seeped through the gaps in his martial arts.

Meanwhile, Lloyd was looking down at Gries.

"...Why..."

He had tried several times. He did not care about his lifespan. The only thing that could break through this situation was Gries' magic, the very magic that had completely banished the Immortal Quasi-Demon King, Kim Jonghyun, from this time and space. The magic that Abel had cast by taking his own remaining lifespan and even that of his dying brother, Cain. Only that magic could overcome the current situation.

However, he could not use it.

"Just why..." Lloyd looked down at Gries with trembling eyes.

It was not just the banishment magic. None of Gries' magic could be used. He had been able to use it just a moment ago while fighting the advancing army of Death Knights, Liches, and Yokai, but now, when he actually tried to prepare magic targeting Jeniela, Gries did not respond.

*Is this fate...?*

Lloyd gripped Gries tightly and continuously poured magic power into it. The staff still emitted no light. He opened the grimoire.

The characters that filled the pages were disappearing. Someone was making it impossible to use Gries' magic, erasing the spells that filled its pages.

*Who on earth? A Spirit? Besides a god, who could possibly do such a thing?*

"Is this... the destined end?" Lloyd muttered in a voice full of despair.
