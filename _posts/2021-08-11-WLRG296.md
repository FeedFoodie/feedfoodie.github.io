They laid the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's body in a flower bed beside a small lake nestled in the heart of the forest.

One by one, the small Fairy Realms gathered with armfuls of flowers. They sniffled and cried as they placed the blossoms over the Chairman's body until he was nearly covered.

Yehwa knelt beside them, her head bowed so low it seemed she had cried herself empty, yet still the weeping continued. Lee Sungmin stood next to her, his vacant eyes looking down at the body of the man he had followed.

[Should I be glad he died a good death?]

*No,* Lee Sungmin replied mentally to the Void Monarch's grumbling.

The sheer number of Fairy Realms meant there were just as many flowers, and soon they formed a vibrant blanket over the Chairman. Queen Osla was the last to approach, and she gently placed a crown woven from blossoms upon his head.

"It feels wrong, letting him rot," Osla murmured. "I'll make it so he doesn't. He can stay here, always. The Coalition Leader loved this forest and this flower bed."

"...Thank you."

"There's no need for thanks," Osla said, straightening from her bent posture. "It's what he wished for, and it's what I want to do."

Wearing the flower crown, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman looked as if he were merely sleeping under a blanket of petals. The lake sparkled behind him, reflecting the light from a world he had just departed.

Hk.

Yehwa choked back a sob and staggered to her feet. She then bowed repeatedly toward the Chairman's body.

"...You left me with... such difficult words."

*For a long, long time, and very well.* Those were the last words he had left for her and the other members of the Royal Guard. To Yehwa, they felt harder and more difficult than an order to die, but she had to obey. They were his final command.

A week passed before they decided to leave the forest. Lee Sungmin planned to visit Wudang first, just as he and the Chairman had intended. So far, the South remained quiet. Kim Jonghyun and Vollander had not yet committed any act that could be called a disaster.

*He didn't have to die,* Lee Sungmin thought, hugging his knees. *Even if it was the death you wanted.*

The burden of loss always fell upon those left behind. Much like the Crazy Heavenly Demon's passing, the Chairman's death left Lee Sungmin with a profound emptiness, a sense of loss far greater than any anger. He regretted being unable to do anything, even if it was all in the past. The helplessness discouraged him.

*It's not enough.*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was dead. No amount of struggling with his martial arts theory could change that fact. Lee Sungmin slowly rose to his feet, focusing on the Chairman's inner qi, which now pulsed within his own qi center. It was no longer the Chairman's power but his. At the moment of the man's death, he had been able to do nothing.

From now on, in the future, things would be different. Lee Sungmin placed a single flower he held in his hand on the Chairman's chest. He then took a few steps back and bowed deeply.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was dead. He must not fall into Despair over that fact. Lee Sungmin carved the thought deep into his heart.

Yehwa eventually left the forest. She had to find the other Royal Guard members to inform them of the Chairman's death and deliver his last words.

Scarlet, however, did not leave. Her reasoning was simple. Danger seemed to lurk everywhere, and if she had to face it, she would rather be by Lee Sungmin's side where she might at least have his protection.

"Traveling alone is lonely, isn't it?" Scarlet murmured. "Especially after going through something like... this."

"I'm fine," Lee Sungmin replied with a faint, forced smile. "It's not the first time I've lost someone."

"That doesn't mean you should get used to it." Scarlet gazed at his gold-tinted eyes. She now knew everything she had not known before. "Even if you've experienced death once, even if you've lost people you were close to, people you gave your heart to... you still shouldn't get used to it. That's how you break."

"I'm fine."

"You shouldn't be fine," Scarlet insisted, shaking her head. "You have to grieve a lot, cry a lot. It has to heal slowly, without rushing. You said you don't want to become a monster, right?"

"...Yes."

"The way I see it, you look incredibly unstable."

Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinched at her words.

"Too many things are forcing your hand, and it seems like you're obsessively suppressing yourself. I get it. You haven't had ordinary experiences. Still... yeah. Don't try to control yourself so much." She added with a bitter smile, "Of course, not that I can pretend to understand you or what you're feeling, but still, it's lonely being alone. There are attachments and connections. That's why I don't want to leave you by yourself. Honestly, that's all there is to it. So... this is pity. Is that okay?"

"What do you mean, is it okay?"

"Pitying you."

"There's no reason it shouldn't be," Lee Sungmin chuckled.

[She's right,] the Void Monarch spoke to him. [Becoming a monster by breaking your humanity isn't just about violating morality. Don't fall into self-loathing, and especially, you must not Despair.] The Void Monarch emphasized his point. [The Sealing is not perfect. Since it's been forcibly suppressed, it will be irreversible if it bursts later.]

In other words, Lee Sungmin was carrying a massive, ticking time bomb inside his body.

*I know,* he thought, closing his eyes.

Another week passed. Lee Sungmin checked for rumors from the North through Nebul, but there was almost no news about him. The territory where the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman and the Martial God had clashed belonged to Jeniela and Ju Won. Unless they spread the rumors, the mythical battle that took place on that snowfield would remain unknown.

What had happened to the Martial God? The Black Dragon Knight? The Spear King? The questions weighed heavily on him, but he had no way to find out. He did not think the three of them had died. The death of the Martial God, in particular, was unimaginable. Though he had lost an arm, he had seemed relatively unharmed, and the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight were likely not strong enough to kill him.

*Did they escape?*

He hoped they had. Lee Sungmin sincerely wished for it. At the last moment, both of them had thrown themselves in the way so that his group could escape safely. The Spear King's actions were somewhat understandable. The man Lee Sungmin had come to know was a martial artist to the core, so it was not hard to imagine him feeling contempt for the Martial God's ambush and trying to buy time for the Chairman's disciple.

However, the Black Dragon Knight was another matter. In his final moments, the Chairman had nullified the oath, freeing the knight at the very instant of his death. Even after betraying the Martial God once, the Black Dragon Knight could have offered Lee Sungmin up and likely earned forgiveness. Instead, he had confronted the Martial God head-on.

"They didn't die, did they?" Scarlet muttered.

"They probably escaped," Lee Sungmin replied, shaking his head. They would be able to meet again, if they survived and wished for it. He personally hoped to see the Black Dragon Knight again. Though he had suspected the man of many things and kept his distance, he could not deny that he had escaped thanks to his help.

Lee Sungmin summoned a Fairy Horse. Originally, he should have only been able to ride it one more time, but Osla had removed that restriction. Perhaps the Chairman's death had caused some change in her heart, or perhaps the queen who claimed to be friendly had simply made up her mind. He did not know. Regardless, being able to ride the Fairy Horse freely granted him a great deal of freedom in the vast lands of Eria.

"Thank you," Lee Sungmin said, bowing his head to Osla, who had come to see him off.

Osla gave a faint smile. "Promise me one thing."

"What kind of promise?"

"That you won't Despair," she said, echoing Scarlet and the Void Monarch. "The Coalition Leader would have wanted that too."

"I won't Despair," Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile. "Because I don't want to become a monster."

After saying his goodbyes, he summoned the horse. The freedom it offered would save him a great deal of time.

"Honestly, I don't really feel good about riding this," Scarlet grumbled.

"Me neither," Lee Sungmin replied as he mounted. He recalled the plains where the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique had hunted him down. The Fairy Horse, having heard its destination, began to snort lightly.

As the horse floated up, Lee Sungmin grabbed Scarlet's hands, which were wrapped around his waist. Before she could even react, he lifted her body and pushed her off the horse. Her eyes widened only after she was floating in mid-air.

"You..." Scarlet tried to shout something, but Lee Sungmin just shook his head.

WHOOSH!

The Fairy Horse leaped through space.

RUMBLE...

Space crumbled around Wiji Hoyeon, who stood at its center, her eyes indifferent as she looked ahead. The corpse crushed into a gruesome shape belonged to a monster, not a human. The boss guarding the end of this 'Dungeon' had been strong, but it was no match for her.

She heard the same words again. *The fate of a warlord.*

Wiji Hoyeon let out a low laugh. She had heard it so many times. Truly, so many times. After meeting the Demonic Spirit in the Hyujael Mountains, she had been wandering the world continuously. The Demonic Spirit had told her the locations of every Dungeon, and she had been traveling from one to the next based on that information.

This was the fifth one.

Wiji Hoyeon walked past the boss monster's corpse as the space around them collapsed. The body disintegrated into countless points of light that flowed into her, absorbed completely. It was the fifth time, no, the sixth if she included the Dungeon where she had met Lee Sungmin. She had already absorbed the power of six boss monsters.

In truth, their power was not of much help to her. Wiji Hoyeon knew well that simply increasing her internal energy would not allow her to reach a higher realm. The items that defied common sense found in Dungeons were not particularly helpful either. Nor did she covet the valuable treasures enough to go out of her way for them.

Nevertheless, this work was necessary. The Demonic Spirit had told her many things about the fate of a warlord, what it meant to become one, the purpose of her absurd talent, and what Dungeons truly were. He had explained what it meant to defeat their bosses and how that would come into play later.

*It's still not enough.*

Wiji Hoyeon opened the last door and entered. The piles of gold coins and jewels did not catch her eye, but she did not ignore them. While walking toward the exit, she used her Black Dragon Armor to pull all the treasures filling the room into her Subspace Pocket.

Once outside the Dungeon, she found that night had already fallen. She felt no fatigue but stretched widely anyway. She had annihilated another Dungeon, easily and without difficulty.

How many were left? Wiji Hoyeon counted the remaining Dungeons in her head and thought of the next closest one. It was a Dungeon the Beggars' Gang was not supposed to have discovered, but even so, she could still enter it.

*What are you doing right now?*

Wiji Hoyeon turned her body in that direction and thought of Lee Sungmin. When they parted ways after leaving the Fairy Forest, she had thought their separation would not be long. She had believed she could go see him as soon as her business was finished.

However, it was taking too long. It had to be long, and yet, she could not neglect these troublesome tasks. Wiji Hoyeon's actions were not for herself.

They were for Lee Sungmin.

"A warlord, huh," Wiji Hoyeon muttered in a small voice.
