The preliminary preparations for his ritual were complete.

Kim Jonghyun stretched languidly, tapping a hand through the empty air. Nothing stirred within the detection magic he had spread across the entire forest.

A disappointed click of his tongue echoed in the silence. He was not impatient, but his heart fluttered with the desire to test the arrangements he had made.

He had named this place the Forest of Death, a fitting title for a wood of nameless, gaunt trees. Here, all sorts of horrific magic lay in wait to welcome the subjugation force, and familiars he had obtained from the Dungeon in Arbes waited hungrily for their meal.

*The Mage Army and the Magic Tower Masters. The Murim Alliance and the Church…*

A smirk touched his lips as he considered the grand picture of the forces gathered for his subjugation. They were all coming to kill him, yet he felt no particular fear.

The ritual might fail. He had never performed it before, so failure was certainly a possibility. Or perhaps the subjugation force would kill him before he could even begin in earnest.

*If so, that would be fine too.*

Kim Jonghyun had never held much attachment to his own life. Interest and curiosity were the only things that moved him, and they were the very reasons he was proceeding with this ritual. If he died because of it? That was not a bad outcome. What awaited after death was a mystery humans had explored since ancient times, was it not?

The current subjugation force was valuable enough as a sacrifice, but he still found himself wanting a little more.

*Still, I wish something more would be added to the mix.*

{sep}

Meanwhile, in the city of Hedor, the air was stiff with tension.

The forces gathered to subjugate Kim Jonghyun were threefold. The Magic Towers and their Mage Army, the Murim Alliance, and the Church.

The Church was a unique entity, born from the fate of those summoned to Eria. Holy Knights and Priests from various dimensions found their spiritual connection to the gods they served severed upon arrival. The divine power they once channeled became their own.

For those with particularly fervent faith, this loss led to despair, driving them to suicide or leaving them as empty shells, but others accepted it as the will of their god. They continued to call themselves Holy Knights and Priests, wielding their now personal divine power which proved just as deadly against evil forces like the Undead. These survivors, Priests who were no longer truly Priests and Holy Knights who were no longer truly Holy Knights, gathered together to form the Church.

Over long years, their numbers grew as more were summoned to Eria, and they were joined by those who served the gods native to this world. They were the natural enemies of Black Mages and the Undead, yet the Church was an inherently reclusive group that avoided proper exchange with the Murim Alliance or the Mages Guild.

*The scale of this has gotten too big.*

Scarlet walked the streets, her expression soured with annoyance. So many people had gathered just to subjugate one man. Kim Jonghyun was no longer a simple Black Mage. She had secretly thought the nickname, Demon King of the North, was an exaggeration, but if he had truly mastered the Grimoire's magic, then a force of this size was understandable.

*I don't want to get caught up in something like this.*

Normally, her personality would have compelled her to refuse the job or flee once she realized how wrong things were, but Scarlet was trapped.

*That damn old man.*

She ground her teeth at the thought of the Blue Magic Tower Master. *Why the hell did I make a vow to that old man?* she thought, gripping her hair in belated regret. She had vowed to grant him one favor someday, and he had demanded she participate in this subjugation. It was a life-threatening Mana vow. Running was impossible.

*Still, it's not unconditional. If the situation gets too difficult, that old man will tell me to run away too… Damn you, Kim Jonghyun. You son of a bitch. Why did you pull a crazy stunt like this?*

She had known him since their time in Behenger, where they researched different types of magic in the same Guild. Their fields were different, so they rarely crossed paths. Scarlet had never particularly liked Kim Jonghyun, though not because he had done anything to earn her hatred. It was instinctive. Perhaps she simply disliked the sight of his room, with its floating monster limbs and organs, or Kim Jonghyun himself, always wandering about in a blood-soaked white robe with an inscrutable smile on his face.

"Found you."

The voice was quiet. She had been walking to clear her stuffy head and was just about to turn back. She turned toward the sound. An Asian man with a face she had never seen before was beaming at her.

Scarlet narrowed her eyes, glancing behind her. She wondered if he was talking to someone else, but no one was there.

"Scarlet."

The man approached. His face was unremarkable, his eyes, nose, and mouth leaving no strong impression. The word 'ordinary' was a perfect fit. Scarlet raised a hand to stop him.

"Who are you?" she asked, her eyes narrowed with suspicion as she looked him up and down. His build was quite good. *A mercenary?* Even his armor was plain, with no distinguishing features, which she found all the more suspicious.

Before answering, Lee Sungmin casually raised his left hand. On his wrist was the bracelet she had given him.

Scarlet's eyes widened. She had only given that bracelet to two people in this world. Baek Sogo and Lee Sungmin.

"...You...?"

"It's been a while," Lee Sungmin said with a gentle smile.

Scarlet stared, her mouth half-open, at the human skin mask he wore. After a moment of silence, she muttered as if sighing, "You were pretty handsome when you were younger. What on earth happened to your face to ruin it like that?"

This face was not that ugly, though. Lee Sungmin cleared his throat and replied, "It's not my face."

"Really? That's a relief," Scarlet said, striding over to him.

With a friendly smile, she spread her arms and suddenly pulled Lee Sungmin into a hug. Never having imagined such a reaction, a flustered Lee Sungmin tried to push her away.

[Stay still.]

Scarlet's voice echoed in his mind. It was telepathy, not a Thought Transmission.

"Thank you," she said aloud, her voice warm. "You came for me, right?"

Unlike her telepathic command, her spoken words were soft.

[I get it. You heard the rumors and came 'cause you were worried about me. Right?]

[...That's right.]

[And since you've even changed your face, you must understand your own situation and what's going on here.]

[I do.]

Scarlet was no fool. The warriors who had been watching them with wary eyes relaxed and started on their way again. They had naturally grown suspicious when some unknown man approached her.

[Is it the Moyong Clan?]

[I don't know if they're Moyong or not. What's certain is they're from the Murim Alliance. You idiot,] Scarlet spat. [You know your situation perfectly well, so why'd you come all the way here? I never asked you for help.]

[I came because I was worried about you, Scarlet. I have no intention of running my mouth about being the Phantom Spear and causing friction with the Murim Alliance.]

[You know my position will become awkward if you get caught, right?]

[If that happens, I'll take responsibility.]

Scarlet let out a dry laugh at his words. She broke the hug and naturally linked her arm with his, then began walking in the opposite direction from where the Murim Alliance warriors had gone.

[Responsibility? What kind of responsibility?]

[In any way I can.]

[What an irresponsible and shameless thing to say. It's been a while, but you've lost your old cute charm.]

[How was I cute in the old days?]

[You had that kind of cute charm, being foolish, dazed, indecisive, and pathetic. Now you're just shameless, and I don't like it.]

[I don't understand how that was cute.]

[It's cute when a younger guy acts like that,] Scarlet said, her hand groping his forearm.

"It's been a while, hasn't it?" she cooed. "Oh my, look how thick your forearm has gotten. You've been working hard, haven't you?"

"Why are you being so creepy?"

[Play along, will you?]

[Play along with what?]

[Didn't you come here to protect me?] Scarlet glared at him, though her face was still smiling. [Even if I tell you to get lost, you're not going to leave. Right?]

[That's true.]

[Then play along with me. I'm going to introduce you as my escort when I get back to the Guild later. I'll say you're a skilled adventurer I met while traveling a long time ago. Or do you have another identity prepared?]

[I don't.]

[What about a forged mercenary tag?]

[I don't have one. It would be a pain to get involved with other mercenaries if I had something like that.]

[Good. Looking at the situation now, it seems like they think dragging in the Church isn't enough, so they're trying to pull in mercenaries too.]

[What?]

Lee Sungmin could not help but be surprised. They had already dragged the Murim Alliance and the Church into this, and now they were bringing in mercenaries too?

[I think it might be overkill, but this place, the North, is the territory of the Vampire Queen, Blood Allure Jeniela. They're worried that Kim Jonghyun might have mastered the Grimoire's magic, and there's also the possibility that the Vampire Queen might intervene. So, the decision was made this morning to recruit mercenaries.]

Lee Sungmin gave a bitter smile. The Vampire Queen would not intervene. He could not tell her that, however. It was not likely they would believe him, and he would be hard-pressed to answer if they demanded to know how he knew.

[What about food? Have you eaten?]

[I haven't, but I'm not hungry.]

[Then eat anyway. I want to talk a bit before we head back.]

Lee Sungmin and Scarlet walked the streets, arm in arm, until her steps came to a halt in front of a quiet restaurant.

Only after they were guided to a table and sat facing each other did Scarlet finally let go of his arm.

"Thanks for coming," she said, breaking the silence. "I also… debated a lot about whether to ask for your help or not. In the end, I decided against it. The distance was pretty far, too… right. How on earth did you get here?"

"I happened to be nearby."

Scarlet immediately sent a telepathic message in response.

[Can you stop with the bullshit? You were in Haras, where the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance is. How did you get here from Haras?]

[There are ways.]

[You're being needlessly coy. Well, whatever. I meant it when I said thank you. I never imagined you'd come on your own.]

[I just didn't want to stand by and let you die, Scarlet.]

[Why are you saying such unlucky things?] Scarlet pouted and grumbled. [Who said anything about dying?]
