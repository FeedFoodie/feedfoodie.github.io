The ability to use magic significantly lessened the burden of the weight, though that relief only lasted for the first few days. Just as he began to feel accustomed to the load with the help of Strength and Haste magic, a dreadful realization dawned on him. *It got heavier...!*

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. Mushii's restrictions and trials were merciless, growing more terrible the more he adapted to them.

He clenched his jaw and forced a single step forward. Strength magic was already active, yet he still had to draw upon his inner arts just to command his own body.

[It's been added to.]

Baek Sogo appeared just as Lee Sungmin was struggling to lift his spear. She materialized before him, wrote the words on the ground, and then extended a hand. The spear he had fought to raise slid effortlessly into her grasp.

The crushing weight Lee Sungmin felt did not apply to her. She swung the spear a few times with a casual air before shrugging her shoulders.

[You don't necessarily have to swing a spear to practice your Spear Technique. Can't you just do it with your bare hands?]

[Still, it's better if I'm holding one.]

[Junior Disciple, you're stubborn about the strangest things,] Baek Sogo wrote, a faint smile touching her lips. [Since your body has gotten heavier, it must be difficult to practice the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. If I'd known this would happen, maybe I shouldn't have taught it to you.]

[That's too harsh.]

[I'm joking. Since things have turned out this way, I'll give you some advice.]

She returned the spear to Lee Sungmin, who accepted it with a tense expression. The moment he took it, the sheer weight almost tore it from his grip.

[The asceticism of this mountain… to put it simply, it's about heading toward a goal you've set for yourself,] Baek Sogo began to write. [It is a trial and an ordeal to reach the goal you've set in your heart. Do you understand?]

[…I'm not sure what you mean.]

[It's simple. It means you can receive rewards as you achieve the goals you've set.]

[Rewards. That's vague.]

[Not necessarily. You see, I think Mushii is a rather fair god. A reward is definitely given if you endure the asceticism and overcome the trials. In my case, it was the accomplishment in my martial arts.]

Lee Sungmin tilted his head, but Baek Sogo did not seem frustrated and continued to write.

[It's like this. For example, let's say you set a goal to master the Dancing Shadow Soul Break up to the second star. If you endure the asceticism and reach that goal, the technique will reach the second star. Depending on the harshness you endured, it might even become the third star instead of the second.]

The explanation was intuitive and easy to understand. Through her words, Lee Sungmin began to grasp the mechanism of the restrictions, trials, and rewards on the mountain. Mushii had said that growth here was a reward in itself, and that was not wrong. The goal Lee Sungmin had set upon entering this mountain was simply 'to become stronger', and as he trained, he had indeed achieved rapid growth. The rewards, in other words, had been continuously given.

[I don't know what goal you've set. You haven't told me. I don't want to blame you for aiming for something too distant, either. However, there's a saying, isn't there? A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.]

Baek Sogo smiled faintly.

[How about setting a closer goal for now?]

Her words sparked a realization in him. He had set Wiji Hoyeon as his ultimate goal and had been too blindly focused on her.

*A crow-tit that tries to chase a stork will only tear its legs.*

Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly. It was not a pleasant feeling to think of himself as a crow-tit. No, perhaps he was not even that.

Even if that might be the case.

Name: Lee Sungmin.

Profession: Classless.

Skills:

Bright Ear.

[Your hearing in the forest becomes sensitive. You can hear small sounds.]

However,cher.

[You can skillfully dismantle beasts or monsters.]

Murder.

[You have experience killing people.]

Quiet Step.

[You can lower the sound of your footsteps.]

Below the unremarkable passive skills were the martial arts and magic he had learned.

the Violet Dawn Divine Art (4th Star)

the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style) (3rd Star)

iron skin and strong bones (8th Star)

Stone Breaking Fist (8th Star)

One Thunder Movement Arts (6th Star)

Dancing Shadow Soul Break (1st Star)

"……"

Strength.

Fatigue Recovery.

Mental Cleaning.

The external arts, iron skin and strong bones and Stone Breaking Fist, had improved naturally as his inner arts deepened. The Violet Dawn Divine Art had also grown considerably from his daily circulation of its technique, and so had the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. His progress with the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, however, was slow. He had neglected practicing the movement arts because of the restriction of weight.

*First goal, get the Dancing Shadow Soul Break to the second star.*

Lee Sungmin set a clear goal in his mind.

{sep}

"I don't get it."

Baek Sogo's steps halted. She let out a deep sigh and looked toward the source of the voice. Flam was sitting on a nearby tree branch, and when he saw her look up, he grinned and jumped down.

"Why are you bothering with a guy like that?" Flam asked, the corner of his mouth twisting up. "It's not like he has any great talent. He's just a mediocre, ordinary guy, so I don't get why you care so much. From the looks of it, you've even taken him on as a disciple, haven't you?"

At his words, Baek Sogo moved her lips instead of taking out a branch. Flam understood what she was saying by their shape.

"Telling me to mind my own business? Why should I? I can't stand being curious. You know that."

The corners of Baek Sogo's eyes crinkled. She moved her lips again, but Flam just shook his head.

"Or what. Is it something like that? Are you two just a couple of nobodies licking each other's wounds?"

At those words, Baek Sogo's expression turned icy. A killing intent, honed as sharp as a blade, washed over Flam. He was slightly surprised by the initial wave but was not overwhelmed. He scoffed and waved his hand dismissively.

"Did I strike a nerve?"

Baek Sogo said no more. She pressed her lips together tightly and retracted the killing intent she had released. A look of bitterness flashed across her face, a sight that made Flam sneer.

"It's quite amusing to watch. It's a feeling I, a calamity, will never know."

Leaving those words behind, Flam shot away. He intentionally demonstrated the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, the very movement arts that Baek Sogo specialized in. The technique displayed by the young calamity was nearly flawless compared to her own. Baek Sogo bit her lower lip.

Even after Flam had left, she did not move from that spot for a while. She pressed her temples, berating herself for her killing intent that had so easily crumbled. *Are you two just a couple of nobodies licking each other's wounds?* She thought that was not entirely wrong. From Flam's perspective, she would not be a calamity.

*You don't understand.*

The struggle to reach a goal was something Flam knew nothing about. In the first place, he was someone who had no need to stay on this mountain. He remained only because he enjoyed the trials themselves. In other words, Flam was not undergoing any kind of asceticism. He enjoyed the restrictions that bound his body and used his arrogant nature as a calamity to mock the asceticism of other practitioners. That was the only reason he remained.

Baek Sogo was not a calamity.

When she first met Flam, she had felt despair. *So a calamity like that exists in the world,* she had thought, disheartened by how insignificant her own talent was compared to his brilliant nature.

However, not anymore. Having realized that nothing could be gained at the end of despair and discouragement, Baek Sogo remained on this mountain, and it was not just her. Scarlet, Dok Bijun… they were all the same.

And Lee Sungmin, too.

That was why she had taught him the Dancing Shadow Soul Break. Because Lee Sungmin did not feel despair. Because he was not discouraged. Because he did not fall into frustration but stood back up, engraving even harsher restrictions onto his own body.

She wanted to help him.

That was all.

That was the kind of person Baek Sogo was.

{sep}

When a month had passed, Lee Sungmin's Dancing Shadow Soul Break reached the second star. It was an incredibly difficult movement art, made even more so by the restriction of weight.

*No. Third star…*

Lee Sungmin could not help but be surprised after checking his status window. The goal he had set in his mind was for the technique to reach the second star. The level shown on the status window, however, was the third star. The understanding of it naturally surfaced in his mind, just as Baek Sogo had said. A reward was given once he reached the goal he had set in his heart.

"Because it is an asceticism and a trial for that very purpose."

A voice reached Lee Sungmin as he stared at his status window. Startled, he turned toward the sound. It was Mushii, appearing from within a heat haze, the rags wrapped around his body shaking as he laughed.

"However, human, do not rejoice too much. If it could be achieved so easily, how could it be called asceticism or a trial? Like the restrictions, the rewards gained from achieving goals will grow harsher as you get used to them."

Mushii's words came with a laugh. It had been a low-set goal, one he had achieved and received a reward for. He had planned to continue setting low goals like this, but Mushii was telling him in advance that it would be impossible.

[Why are you here?] Lee Sungmin picked up a branch and wrote.

Although Mushii was a god, he could be met anytime one wished. If you sought him in your heart, he would appear. In this, the god of the mountain was a truly kind being.

"Because it is about time," Mushii said with a smile.

At those words, Lee Sungmin had a feeling. Half a year. If one stayed on this mountain for more than half a year, another restriction was added. This did not just apply to him. Baek Sogo, Scarlet, Dok Bijun, and probably even Flam would have received an additional restriction.

[I haven't even gotten used to my current restrictions yet.]

"Isn't that something you wished for additionally? It's not my place to look after that," Mushii replied, shaking his head. "If you spend half a year on this mountain, you must receive at least one more restriction. That is the rule."

Currently, Lee Sungmin had four restrictions. Weight. Taste. Voice. Dreams. Except for his voice, the others were incredibly bothersome and harsh.

*The harsher the restriction, the greater the reward.*

There was no doubting that. With his meager talent, he had achieved this level of martial accomplishment and had already reached the level of a First-Rate warrior. He had not even fully matured physically, and it had not even been two years since he was summoned to Zenavis. He had already surpassed the strength he had built during the thirteen years of his past life.

Of course, this result was not achieved purely through his own efforts. There was Wiji Hoyeon, the Holy Spirit Pill, and Baek Sogo. Furthermore, without the training on Mount Mushii, his martial arts would not have improved so quickly.

[Thought Transmission.]

"So you're avoiding it."

Mushii chuckled at Lee Sungmin's answer. It could not be helped. He had a few restrictions in mind, but not yet. He could not accept another difficult one when he had not even grown accustomed to his current ones. So, he would avoid this restriction.

"Well, fine. You must feel like you're going crazy with just the restrictions you're currently enduring. Hehe. How much longer can you last?" Mushii chuckled lowly and extended a hand toward Lee Sungmin. "If you wish, you may hand your soul over to me at any time. If you do, I can guarantee it. A reward far greater than what you'd get for that paltry effort of yours will be given to you."

Lee Sungmin did not answer and turned away. A soul with twisted causality. That was the reason Mushii coveted his soul.

However, Lee Sungmin had no intention of handing it over.

At least, not yet.
