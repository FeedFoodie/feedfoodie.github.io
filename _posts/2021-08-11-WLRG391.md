He gauged his chances of winning. They were not zero, but they were perilously low. It was not an impossible fight, yet he refused to let himself be carried away by that sliver of hope. His odds were so close to nothing that he had to struggle with everything he had, a reality he was fortunately growing accustomed to.

This desperation now allowed him to use attack methods he could not have even attempted before. His high-speed assaults were impossible to follow with the naked eye, yet his awakened senses forced his body to move in response. Faced with her overwhelming magic power, a barrage of attacks so dense that no opening could be seen, he chose to advance rather than evade.

If there was no opening, he would simply leap over it entirely. He completely combined the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, creating an ultra-high-speed movement art that transcended space itself. It was no longer simple, fast movement but a realm closer to a Space Leap.

Jeniela did not rush to meet the terrifying advance of Lee Sungmin. The night was long.

*Chen and Kun are dead.*

It was the natural course of events. She had sent them to their deaths, after all, never believing for a moment that they had even the slimmest chance of victory. The twins had been loyal servants by her side for hundreds of years, but she offered no condolences for their passing.

The Spear Technique pouring down from above could no longer be called a martial art. The entire space around her pulsed with killing intent, seeking to end her life.

Jeniela let out a smirk as the magic power surging from her body gathered at a single point.

KWAAAAANG!

The clash of their attacks shook the very lake. Osla watched the collision between Lee Sungmin and Jeniela with a hardened expression.

Lee Sungmin was doing well. He had played every card he had, absorbing Aine's heart to grant his body the maximum potential possible. Indeed, it was only because of that forced growth that he had made this fight against Jeniela possible at all. If he had not used such a method, his defeat would have already been certain.

*But... it's not enough.*

Even watching from a distance, Osla could feel it clearly. Unlike Lee Sungmin, who fought with all his might, Jeniela still had room to spare. He was doing well, certainly. In this world, there were not many beings who could put up such a fight against that monstrous queen on the Night of the Full Moon.

[Just enduring isn't enough.]

The Void Monarch was not just giving advice. His Demonic Energy surged endlessly to defend against Jeniela's fatal attacks. It was not just the Void Monarch's power either. The blessing of the Fairy Queen, continuously granted by Osla, also worked to defend against her onslaught.

[Is it... too early to make a decisive move?]

*I don't know.*

The problem was his lack of conviction. He had no confidence that he could kill Jeniela even if he used the pinnacle of martial arts. He had used it twice so far, but never had he completely annihilated an opponent with a single use. Jeniela was stronger than the Blood Spirit Queen, which was precisely why he had no confidence.

KWADANGTANG!

A blow sent Lee Sungmin's body flying backward. He tried to right his tumbling form and immediately link it to an attack, but in that fleeting instant, Jeniela's hand was already touching his chest.

The Fairy Queen's blessing and Jeniela's magic power collided. The magic was offset, but as if she had expected it, Jeniela's next attack had already begun.

JJEOONG!

Her magic power tore through his Protective Qi Aura, infiltrating his body and pulverizing his internal organs. Blood gushed from his nose and mouth.

[Hold on!] the Void Monarch roared.

Lee Sungmin clung desperately to his fading consciousness.

"Aaaaaah!"

He screamed as he drew up his power, forcing strength into his body as it fell backward.

KWAAANG!

His foot slammed into the ground. His consciousness, on the verge of snapping, willed his body to move. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and forced his fist forward.

KWAJIJIJIK!

As he swung, his muscles tore and his bones shattered. Only the immense Demonic Energy contained within the blow erupted without faltering, annihilating Jeniela's magic power.

"How reckless," Jeniela sneered. The full moon granted her endless magic power.

In this battle, she was the overwhelmingly dominant force. To her, Lee Sungmin was nothing more than a sturdy punching bag that refused to break easily.

"Is it not enough?" she asked sincerely. "Do you still think you can do it?"

*The chances of winning are...*

No, don't think about it. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath. Find a way out. A way to win.

His tattered arm regenerated. He gripped the spear with both hands. *Don't cling to the form of a spear,* the Void Monarch roared in his mind.

*I know.* Lee Sungmin's foot stomped the ground. He would apply pressure with the Dancing Shadow Soul Break and keep her in check with the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Thanks to Osla's blessing, he would not die easily.

His Demonic Energy began to rotate.

"What more are you trying to show me?" Jeniela whispered. "How long do you plan to save it?" she asked with a sneer.

She already knew about the technique he was hiding. The attack that had killed the Blood Demon had not yet appeared.

"Then I'll push you even harder."

Magic power surged, dyeing the space red. Unable to watch any longer, Osla spread his wings. Even with almost no combat power, he could not just stand by granting blessings. Particles of light sprayed in all directions from his wide-spread wings. Osla's light resisted the overwhelming magic, pushing back against Jeniela's power.

Jeniela laughed loudly. "It won't work even with you added to the mix!"

The spreading magic power exploded. A chain reaction of explosions tore the space apart, and Osla's light was pushed back by the red magic and extinguished. Creating an opening was the best he could do. A throbbing headache washed over him again.

His eyes saw the flow of magic power. Where was the opening? Lee Sungmin's spear moved, refusing to be bound by the forms of Spear Technique. He forcibly twisted his arm, changing the shape of the spear as he unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear repeatedly. He ran, tearing through the storm of magic power.

The movement arts of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break unfolded.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Seventh Form.

The explosion of Demonic Energy was amplified by Two Step Chaos, Three Step Fatality, and Four Step Chaos. The energy that had been spreading in all directions came to a sudden halt.

Lee Sungmin's foot lifted high. As he slammed it down, the Demonic Energy that had spread out returned to him.

Swift Storm Extinction.

His feet flew. His consciousness flew. His mind could not keep up with the speed at which his body moved. Or was it the other way around? Lee Sungmin felt his body become a single bolt of lightning. The Seventh Form of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Swift Storm Extinction, took the martial arts theory of Extreme Speed from Heaven Reversal Black Thunder and the Dancing Shadow Soul Break to an even higher level.

"This is the first time I've seen..."

Jeniela's words were not even finished.

KWAKWAKWANG!

Lee Sungmin, now a bolt of lightning, shot past her. In that instant, a storm of Demonic Energy raged, and Jeniela's body spurted blood as it was launched into the sky. The lightning crackled and changed direction as magic power enveloped her body.

KWAAAAANG!

The impact shattered Jeniela's barrier. *Why use this only now?* she thought as she was sent flying. *So he couldn't afford to save it anymore.* It seemed he had wanted to use it at the decisive moment to end things, but that was no longer possible. His soul creaked under the strain.

Just maintaining the speed of Swift Storm Extinction caused his body to repeatedly explode and regenerate. Lee Sungmin gripped his spear with a trembling hand. He shattered the head of Jeniela, whose eyes were wide open, with his spear. Her headless body plummeted toward the ground.

*Now...!*

His mind exploded with clarity. The martial arts theory of the pinnacle of martial arts was imbued into his spear. More efficiently, more completely... there was no time. Jeniela's body was already regenerating.

*I'm faster.*

The pinnacle of martial arts was fired.

A flash of light completely drove away the darkness. Demonic Energy, inner qi, nothing erupted anymore. Lee Sungmin looked down with trembling eyes.

*Certainly, I was faster.*

"That was dangerous," Jeniela whispered.

The arm he had raised high fell to his side. The Formless Spear he was holding scattered and vanished. Lee Sungmin staggered back a few steps as blood gushed from his severed arms.

*What happened?*

He could not understand the situation. He had accelerated to his top speed using Swift Storm Extinction. He had cut off her consciousness by blowing away her head. He had unleashed the pinnacle of martial arts faster than her body could regenerate, aiming for her chest.

He could not pierce it.

The moment he thrust the pinnacle of martial arts to its limit, Jeniela's body had disappeared. It was too late to change direction.

Lee Sungmin coughed up blood. No, what he had not anticipated was her movement. Her body had vanished and...

"Don't you understand?" Jeniela asked with a smile, glancing at his regenerating arms. "I already know your spear is a threat. Do you think I'm arrogant enough not to be wary of it? The Blood Demon's death was worthwhile. It showed me just how threatening your final move is. So I was on guard. Even while fighting, I never forgot that dangerous technique could be aimed at me at any moment." She paused. "It was nothing special."

Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth. His body could still move. Unlike before, he was not exhausted just from using the pinnacle of martial arts. It was not over yet. He extended a foot forward, just as he was about to walk.

PWAK!

A blade of magic power shot up from the ground and sliced through his knees. Lee Sungmin's body lost its balance and collapsed.

"I could have done it, but I didn't," Jeniela said. "It's no different from before."

She had intentionally let him push her back, all to make him feel like he had a chance to win. She had not dodged when she could have. She had not blocked when she could have. She had consistently built up that pretense, only showing her true colors once in the middle to ask him if he felt Despair.

"How about now?"

Jeniela trembled with the pleasure rising from her toes. Collapsed on the ground, Lee Sungmin stared blankly up at her.

He understood. No, he had understood since earlier, from the moment his decisive attack failed to pierce her. Even though he had thought, *Why?*... he actually knew. He knew that Jeniela's speed had changed in that instant, allowing her to dodge the pinnacle of martial arts, close the distance, and sever both his arms.

"Do you feel Despair?"

Even the Void Monarch could not say anything. He did not know how they could possibly break through this horrific situation. The difference in what they were capable of was just too great. That was all there was to it.

"Going easy on you any longer would be meaningless," Jeniela muttered as she reached out her hand.

"Aaaah!" Lee Sungmin screamed and swung his regenerated hand.

"I told you it was meaningless."

Blood spurted from his wrist. He stumbled backward on trembling legs as Jeniela pursued him. Grabbing his shoulder, she tore his entire shoulder off with a gentle touch. There was no hesitation in her hand. Her foot pushed against his leg, and as if it were the most natural thing in the world, his leg broke.

Looking down at his fallen body, Jeniela's lips twitched. She could no longer hold it back and broke into a radiant smile. She could not help but smile like that. She trembled with ecstasy as she saw the emotion on Lee Sungmin's face as he looked up at her.

"Ah... Aah."

It was lovely. That terrified face. The feeling of Despair that comes from facing a hopeless situation where you can do nothing, no matter how much you struggle.

Jeniela chewed on her lower lip. The blood that trickled down dripped from her chin.

"I love it..." she let out a sob-like sound and bent down.

Seeing her approaching red eyes, Lee Sungmin gasped. Even in that state, he swung his hands at her using a technique from the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Both his hands were caught with absurd ease.

Looking at his arms, which had already regenerated, Jeniela's eyes crinkled into a smile.

"I told you, didn't I? I'm not going easy on you anymore." She lowered her head, bringing her face right up to his. "So tell me. I'm scary, aren't I? You feel powerless, unable to do anything, right? What do you think will happen now? What will I do? What kind of pain will I inflict on you? Think about it, one by one by one by one... It's okay. I won't give you any difficult or complex pain. I'll give you intuitive, simple pain."

She sang, "Are you used to pain? If you really think so, that's arrogant of you. You never get used to pain. You just become more tolerant of it. Don't worry. I'll make you understand, step by step."

Fortunately, "the night is still long."

Jeniela's fingernails dug into Lee Sungmin's left eye.
