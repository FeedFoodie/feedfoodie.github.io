The Dark Supreme did not care for such sinister and ominous power. While there were those in Heaven Beyond Heaven who practiced demonic arts, the evil they cultivated was fundamentally different from the pure evil of a Demonic Spirit. This power was the domain of the Predators, old monsters and longtime enemies of Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Dark Supreme himself had spent a lifetime killing countless such inhuman creatures with his own hands and hidden weapons.

"I thought he was a promising one. To think he'd rely on the power of a Demonic Spirit!" the Dark Supreme roared.

To Lee Sungmin, the man's voice felt strangely distant. This was the first time he had directly drawn upon Demonic Energy since the Dungeon, but the sensation was quite different from what he remembered.

Losing his reason, the Crazy Heavenly Demon succumbed to a lunacy that had not claimed him in months, transforming him into a violent beast. He unleashed a Lion's Roar and charged toward the Dark Supreme, who responded without hesitation by shaking both his arms. PABABABAK! From his wide, fluttering sleeves, dozens of throwing daggers swarmed the charging madman.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's Protective Qi Aura, however, was now incomparably stronger than before. With the Void Monarch, the very source of his power, right beside him, the Crazy Heavenly Demon was steeped in a denser Demonic Energy than he had been during his previous berserk episodes. KWAAANG! The Dark Supreme's throwing daggers slammed into the Crazy Heavenly Demon's charge. Though his ferocious momentum lessened slightly, he immediately began another reckless advance.

"Fiend!" the Dark Supreme snarled, recognizing the state the Crazy Heavenly Demon was in. It was a common side effect for a human who drew upon the power of a Demonic Spirit.

CHULKUK! A sharp blade shot out from the wrist armor covering the Dark Supreme's right arm. He closed the distance with ghost-like, unpredictable movement arts. SWIIIAK! His slash severed the Crazy Heavenly Demon's Protective Qi Aura, and blood spurted from his chest.

Even in this situation, the Dark Supreme stayed true to his word not to kill him, leaving only a shallow wound. The staggering Crazy Heavenly Demon snapped his waist straight and spread his arms wide in an attempt to grab his opponent. The Dark Supreme's eyes widened as he shifted his upper body away.

PABABAK!

The tips of his outstretched fingers tapped the Crazy Heavenly Demon's body in an acupoint sealing technique so swift it was invisible.

"KRRRR!"

However, it did not work. Despite the precise pressure on his acupoints, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's body refused to stop. The Dark Supreme felt a flicker of surprise at the resistance, but he would not let the fiend get the better of him. He brought his outstretched hands together, and an intangible energy shimmered between his palms before he pushed it straight toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon's chest.

HWOOOONG! The collar of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's clothes billowed out as his body flew backward. While he was still soaring through the air, the Dark Supreme swung his hand, throwing a set of metal rings that separated in mid-air to bind the madman's limbs.

Lee Sungmin strode toward the Dark Supreme's back.

His heart hammered in his chest, pumping blood faster through his body. The nausea he had felt inside had twisted into a disgusting pleasure. The Dark Supreme turned to face him. Lee Sungmin's gaze swept over the man watching him, then to the Crazy Heavenly Demon struggling against his restraints in the distance, and finally to Wiji Hoyeon, who was being cared for by Rubia.

Wiji Hoyeon had not yet woken. A world-shaking battle raged around them, yet her slumber remained deep and profound. After a glance at her chest, which rose and fell gently with her soft breathing, Lee Sungmin looked directly at the Dark Supreme.

[Keep this in mind. There's no guarantee you can beat him just by using Demonic Energy.] the Void Monarch warned.

Lee Sungmin already knew that. By using Demonic Energy, he had gained violent power, a resilient body, and a regenerative ability that surpassed human limits. Even so, the Dark Supreme was a master of the Transcendent Realm. He had only managed to close an absolute disadvantage to a near-even level.

"The power of a Demonic Spirit destroys humans," the Dark Supreme spat, glaring at Lee Sungmin. "There's no such thing as easy power in this world. It always comes with a price and a responsibility. Don't you know that!"

He knew. He had seen others dabble in the power of the inhuman only to meet their downfall. He just did not want to dwell on it now. Lee Sungmin made no attempt to reply. KWAANG! He erupted forward, his movement arts instantly closing the distance to the Dark Supreme.

Before the gap reached zero, Lee Sungmin swung his spear from his optimal range.

Empowered by Demonic Energy, his Soul Chasing Elimination was ferocious and his Blood Ring Shattering was tyrannical. The Two Step Calamity supported his every movement, blocking the Dark Supreme's path. The Dark Supreme did not evade the spear thrusting at him. KAGAGAGAK! The blade drawn from his wrist armor clashed with the spearhead. Lee Sungmin released his left hand from the spear's shaft and infused it with the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art.

He unleashed the fourth form, Shatter Explosion, a technique that destroyed everything from within. The Dark Supreme's eyes widened as he extended a hand to meet it. KWAAAAANG! The enhanced qi of the Shatter Explosion scattered in all directions. The Dark Supreme was surprised to feel a slight throb in his own left wrist.

*Even if he's using the power of a Demonic Spirit, for it to be this powerful...!*

In terms of sheer martial power, it was enough to be considered at the Transcendent Realm. That did not mean the Dark Supreme was being pushed back, however. He spun his connected hand, and like a snake, it slithered up Lee Sungmin's wrist. His fingertips tapped Lee Sungmin's forearm, and the inner qi that seeped in destroyed his qi and blood flow. For a moment, all strength drained from his left arm. Lee Sungmin swung his spear to push the Dark Supreme away.

UDUK! UDUDEUK! The broken bones in his forearm forcibly snapped back into place. The ruptured muscles and torn qi meridians regenerated in an instant.

"You're not human!" the Dark Supreme shouted. KUGUGUGUNG! Black enhanced qi surged from beneath his feet. He raised his foot high and stomped it down. KUUUUNG! The very ground beneath them sank. As Lee Sungmin's body swayed unsteadily, the Dark Supreme kicked out toward the empty air.

It was a marvelous sight as the Dark Supreme ran upon nothing. Using the Airwalking Technique to move freely through the sky, he swung his foot toward Lee Sungmin's head. SHWIIK! A sharp blade shot out from the tip of his boot, trying to claw at Lee Sungmin's face. Lee Sungmin relaxed his swaying body and leaned back. The kick grazed narrowly past the tip of his nose. He stabbed his spear into the ground to support himself. SHWIIK! Using it like a staff, he launched his body forward, his two feet attacking the Dark Supreme, who was still running on air.

The Dark Supreme's figure vanished like smoke. It was those ghost-like movement arts again. Having kicked only empty air, Lee Sungmin landed on the ground and shot forward with his spear leading the way, unfolding the Deathly Flash of Extreme Speed. Now back on the ground, the Dark Supreme clicked his tongue as he watched the spear tip aiming to pierce his body. He took out the whip that had been wrapped around his left arm.

KAAAANG! A sound impossible to imagine from the clash of a flexible whip and a solid spear rang out. Lee Sungmin brought his raised spear back down. The Deathly Flash lacked power. What about the Void Path? An enhanced qi art would be effective against space, but the Dark Supreme's whip was fast and sharp, not imbued with such energy.

He unleashed Homing Chain Lightning and Bloody Pursuit, his scattered spear strikes and enhanced qi converging to create hundreds of attacks. The Dark Supreme's eyes grew cold. His hands moved slowly, yet the slow movements created numerous afterimages, making it seem as if dozens of hands were moving at once. Each afterimage held a different hidden weapon. PABABABAK! The thrown weapons completely dismantled the Homing Chain Lightning and Bloody Pursuit, and more. The Dark Supreme walked forward. He spun sharp-edged rings on both his index fingers, then sent them flying with a light flick of his wrists.

KIIIIING! The two flying rings emitted a grating sound as they spun. It was not a simple noise but a sound arts technique, the interlocking, different sounds shaking the mind. Lee Sungmin's mind, however, did not waver. He moved his eyes rapidly to track the rings and shot his spear out with precision.

*This is different from the Crazy Heavenly Demon.*

The Dark Supreme put a hand inside his robes. When his left hand emerged, it was wearing a black glove, and from the tips of its five fingers dangled silver threads so fine they were nearly invisible. Just as the Dark Supreme was about to swing his hand, the Void Monarch shouted.

[Block it!]

Even before the warning, Lee Sungmin was already preparing his defense. He firmly raised his Protective Qi Aura, amplified by the sturdy Magic Armor made of Orichalcum. He pulled his spear in front of his chest, defending his body. The Dark Supreme's left hand swung.

KAGAGAGAK! The ground was sliced as if it were tofu. The five strands of silver thread, which had been dangling limply, received the Dark Supreme's inner qi and created a slash sharp enough to cut through anything in the world. The Orichalcum coating of the Magic Armor was torn where the attack grazed it. A deep gash was also carved into the solid Orichalcum spear.

*He hasn't lost his reason. On the contrary, he's surprisingly rational... I can't believe it. How can he maintain his sanity while using the power of a Demonic Spirit?*

His head was spinning. The more they exchanged blows, the more Lee Sungmin realized just how monstrously powerful the beings known as the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens were. So far, he had not managed to land a single scratch on the Dark Supreme, who was holding back a great deal and had not used a single killing technique.

*I'm too weak.*

It had been a long time since he had felt his own weakness so keenly, even while using the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy. His heart continued to pound. *I've seen stronger. I used to be stronger.* As his consciousness faded, he thought of Wiji Hoyeon. He did not want to let her go. He did not want to send the weakened Wiji Hoyeon to them. He unleashed the Nine Dragons Slaughter, but it was torn apart by the Dark Supreme's silver threads. The Serpent Soul Devouring was blocked by the whip. The Homing Chain Lightning was dismantled by thrown hidden weapons, and the Soul Chasing Elimination was stopped by the blade of the wrist armor.

He had to use a stronger martial art. Could he do it now? The imbalance of his mind, qi, and body had been corrected. He had plenty of inner qi, and his Demonic Energy was full. His body had grown tougher. Lee Sungmin gritted his teeth and poured his inner qi and Demonic Energy into the hand holding the spear.

As the tremendous power flowed into it, the Orichalcum spear began to vibrate violently.

He loosened his grip and flicked the spear with his fingertips, causing it to spin in his hand. The infused inner qi and Demonic Energy overflowed, swept into the torrent of rotating qi. The rotation, which started slowly, accelerated until it was so fast it almost seemed to be standing still. Space itself resonated with the spin, causing the surroundings to shimmer.

"......"

The Dark Supreme exclaimed in admiration as he watched. He did not like that Lee Sungmin was using the power of a Demonic Spirit, but he had to acknowledge his martial arts. He had considered breaking his promise and killing him if he was too deeply stained, but the Dark Supreme had now made up his mind.

He would absolutely not kill Lee Sungmin here. He was not yet at a level to be taken to Heaven Beyond Heaven, but his potential was real.

The rotation did not stop. Demonic Energy and inner qi continued to flow in. Faster. Lee Sungmin gasped for breath. This technique was the seventh form of the nine-part Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Its power was certain, but this was his first time using it in a real fight. Its power would rise endlessly if given enough time, but conversely, if he did not spend an adequate amount of time, he would not be able to generate the desired level of power.

*Just a little more.*

The Dark Supreme took off his glove. In its place, he took out what looked like an unremarkable folding fan. When he waved his hand, the fan snapped open. He held the pure white fan at chest level and approached Lee Sungmin.

Now.

The seventh form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Sky-piercing. As Lee Sungmin thrust his spear, the furiously rotating Demonic Energy and inner qi exploded. For a moment, space was dyed pure white. This tremendous, excessive power crushed the barrier the Dark Supreme had created and made space itself vibrate.

The Dark Supreme let out a cry of admiration at the overwhelming martial art rushing toward him. He was even more certain now that he could not kill him. He swung the folding fan wide. A dazzling light burst from the center of the fan and collided with the Sky-piercing. KWAAANG! A terrible sound, as if the world were collapsing, erupted. Amidst the storm of rampaging Demonic Energy and inner qi, the folding fan shook but did not tear. The Dark Supreme danced, brandishing the fan as the Sky-piercing scattered. The combination of inner qi and Demonic Energy separated, creating a dense fog in the area.

"Excellent..."

Before the Dark Supreme could finish his words, Lee Sungmin was already lunging forward, the moment he confirmed his attack had been blocked.

The fifth form of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Three Step Fatality.

The fog seethed. The scattered inner qi and Demonic Energy fell under the control of his new technique. The Dark Supreme's face hardened at the storm of energy rushing toward him.

"......"

Was it because the surroundings were too chaotic?

Wiji Hoyeon's eyes opened.
