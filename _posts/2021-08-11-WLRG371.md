Sword Immortal's gaze fell upon the severed head and body of Cheongmyeong, lying not far away. Even in this desperate situation, he was careful to protect the remains. The Formless Sword that enveloped the entire space had a single, deliberate opening, a sanctuary for his fallen disciple. Whenever Jeniela's attacks strayed in that direction, he would intervene, scattering their trajectory before they could land.

*It's meaningless...*

The thought did nothing to stop him. Sword Immortal forced his weakening knees to lock, staggering to his feet as he shook his sweat-drenched hair. Through the shimmering beads clinging to the matted ends, he saw her. That monster of monsters was smiling, a bright and cheerful expression that showed no sign of fatigue.

"Hehe..."

A self-deprecating laugh escaped Sword Immortal's lips as he looked at Jeniela. His chest felt as if he had swallowed a ball of fire whole, yet his frozen reason allowed him to view the situation with a chilling calm.

His body was not what it once was. No, even if it were the same as before, or even better, the result would not have changed. Qi deviation had stolen his body's freedom, but ever since falling into its grip, he had constantly meditated, training his mind rather than his flesh. The martial arts he had achieved through that process were not lacking, even if he had never suffered from the deviation in the first place.

It was simply a difference in class. That was all. His Formless Sword posed no real threat to Jeniela. He could pierce her defensive barrier and even butcher her body, but no matter how many times he repeated the act, he could not kill her. That queen of monsters would just regenerate with a laugh, her body moving again no matter how many times he cut off her head or pierced her heart.

"Good."

Watching Sword Immortal rise, Jeniela clapped her hands. She nodded with a wide smile.

"You're going to keep going, right? I don't really like men who give up too quickly. So, I guess what I'm saying is, I don't dislike you. If it weren't for this situation, I might have seriously considered making you one of my vampires."

She was being sincere. Jeniela truly did not dislike this type of human. She loved those who struggled, who fought against Despair even in the most hopeless situations. In fact, her most engrossing hobby over the past few hundred years had been to corrupt such humans, using subtle persuasion and temptation until they begged to be turned into a Vampire themselves. In the long seven hundred years she had lived, the only two she had turned without regard for that hobby were Gemini and Blood Demon.

"But I don't have time for that right now."

Jeniela truly found that fact regrettable. If only she had enough time, she would have driven Sword Immortal further into Despair, making him break his own convictions and thirst for revenge until he begged her to make him a Vampire. She clicked her tongue and extended a hand toward him.

"......"

A drop of blood splattered in the air, its path deflected by the flow of the Formless Sword surrounding Sword Immortal. The corner of Jeniela's mouth twitched and curled upward.

Sword Immortal gasped for breath, focusing his mind. His body, ravaged by qi deviation, was too weak for a direct fight. It was an old man's body, no matter how much he suppressed his aging or circulated his inner qi. Fighting with a sword in hand with such a body was far too inefficient. He could probably subdue an average opponent with his bare hands, but his opponent was a vampire queen hundreds of years old.

This weakness had forced his martial arts to evolve, to develop in a way that did not rely on his physical form. The Formless Sword and the Flying Sword Technique. With these two arts, he had already achieved a skill level that surpassed his prime. However, his opponent was a bad match-up. Against the undying queen of vampires, those two techniques alone were not enough.

If they would not work, what was he supposed to do? He focused his mind even more intently.

He knew very well that he would die here. His heart, burning with vengeance for his disciple's death, screamed at him to kill the damn monster, but his reason knew it was impossible. Still, he would not back down. He could not. He recalled the Thought Transmission Lee Sungmin had sent just before he left, the message that said the Wudang Sect's main headquarters was under attack.

*How many survived.*

It was greedy to think everyone had evacuated safely, but he hoped that at least a few had. He hoped that the children who could inherit the name of Wudang and carry it forward had survived and escaped.

...No. Is there any meaning in that? The End is already set, a future where everyone is destined to die and disappear anyway.

He reined in his loosening mind. Even so, he was not dying right this instant. If you do not die, if you are alive, no one knows what might happen. Perhaps something like a change in a set destiny might actually occur.

*I've lived long enough.*

He had lived for too long. This was a body that should have died long ago, a crumbling form ravaged by qi deviation that he had clung to with a desperate will to live. Despite extending his life, he had not reached a higher realm. He had often thought there had been no meaning in prolonging his existence.

No.

There was definitely a reason he had forced his dying body to stand. Because of that, he had been able to survive until today.

He thought of Cheongmyeong.

His disciple.

Looking back... he had been far too greedy. So impressed by the boy's talent, he had taken him as a disciple and never once allowed him to leave Mount Wudang. He made him practice martial arts every single day. Cheongmyeong had brilliantly become a master of the Transcendent Realm, but Sword Immortal could not remember ever properly praising him for it. He had been too greedy. Because Cheongmyeong was a disciple he had taught himself, he had considered that level of achievement to be natural. He thought the boy had to become an even more outstanding master, so he taught him harshly.

Despite being unable to leave the mountain for decades, only swinging a sword, Cheongmyeong never complained. The boy had always endured the nagging of his difficult old Master with a faint smile.

And in the end, he had died. In vain, without being able to do anything. He died without ever showing the world the martial arts he had spent decades honing, cooped up on this mountain.

The thought felt like his heart was being torn apart. Why had he been so greedy? He should have told him to go down the mountain and see the world for a year, no, at least for a month. Why was that so difficult...

Jeniela did not stop her approach. She had no interest whatsoever in what Sword Immortal was thinking. She did not care what kind of existence the Cheongmyeong she had killed was to him, nor did she care how much he was suffering from the boy's futile death.

Sword Immortal scraped together his true qi. What had supported his crumbling body was a secret Taoist art passed down in the Wudang Sect. Because of it, his body was connected to the earth's veins of this mountain, his physical form surviving by parasitizing its essence. The true qi he now gathered was the very essence of this mountain. It was like a martial artist igniting their life qi.

Sword Immortal burned his own soul, turning the mountain's essence into power.

"Struggling, are we," Jeniela chuckled as she watched him. She felt no sense of crisis. There was no being in this world that could make her feel a sense of crisis. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman or the Void Monarch might have been a different story, but they had died long ago.

Sword Immortal heard her mocking laugh and closed his eyes. The Formless Sword that had surrounded him emitted a faint light and then disappeared. He was about to do something. Jeniela knew it, but she felt no sense of caution. Even if tonight was not a full moon, she was a predator with overwhelming power. Whatever he was planning, she felt no threat from it.

Jeniela was arrogant. Naturally, she was also careless.

In truth, even if she had not been arrogant and careless, the outcome of this moment would not have changed.

THUD.

Jeniela's head fell to the ground. In that single, silent instant, she had not felt the slash. She had not even felt her own head tumble to the floor.

Her body, unaware of its missing head, kept walking.

FSH-SH-SH-SHINK!

Her form was instantly cut into dozens of pieces of flesh, and the ensuing sword wind divided it again into hundreds.

"...Hah..."

Sword Immortal sucked in a breath.

The space was filled with sword slashes. This was not the Formless Sword. His sword had advanced one step further from that realm. The power gained by staking his soul had forcibly pulled his feet to a place he could not have reached otherwise.

He could not move. He was unable to move. His consciousness was ahead of his physical body, and he knew that if his body tried to follow, this forced state of selflessness would shatter.

The hundreds, thousands of scattered pieces of flesh began to cling to each other. Jeniela's body, minced so finely it was unrecognizable, was regenerating.

Sword Immortal's eyes widened. He saw something he had not seen before. The finely divided pieces of flesh were connected by red threads. He ground his teeth, his unmoving consciousness becoming even sharper.

I must cut. I must cut. I must cut...

He repeated it between his tightly clenched lips.

He was not holding a sword. However, his mind had become a sword. A single sword, neither visible nor existing, appeared. It was a sword no one could see, but he saw it.

That was his mind. I must cut. Sword Immortal thought once more. Cheongmyeong's severed head rose in the center of his consciousness.

"Master."

The voice that used to call him.

Did you cough? How is your body? I understand. Is that so? Thank you. Yes, yes, yes.

The voice of that boy who had never once said no distorted the wrinkles formed by old age. He felt as if Cheongmyeong's headless corpse was pushing him from behind.

I must cut. No, I will cut.

The sword that had formed in his mind emerged from his body. He had seen countless swords in his life, but at the color of the sword created by his own mind, Sword Immortal let out a low sound of admiration.

The sword moved. The sword of the mind played above all the laws of this world. It could go anywhere, it was everywhere, and it was nowhere. It had the form of a sword yet was not a sword, but it could still do anything a sword could do.

Something came into view.

It was faint and hard to see clearly. It looked like... a path. The scenery wavered. He could not see Jeniela. He could not see the sword either. Within the wavering scenery that seemed about to collapse and disappear, a single path appeared. Sword Immortal stared at it blankly.

He did not dare to look at its end, and yet, he wanted to see the end. He did not even know what this path was, but he was engulfed by a desire to walk this unknown road and see where it led.

He extended his foot to walk toward it.

"It is too soon for you."

A voice drifted from far down the path. This voice... where had he heard it? The sound alone felt as if it were pushing his body back. From behind, the headless Cheongmyeong pushed his body forward, but from the front, the voice pushed him back, telling him not to come. In the end, Sword Immortal could do nothing and stood rooted to the spot.

"Unfortunately."

Ah, right.

This voice is...

"......"

Sword Immortal's body went limp.

Jeniela stared down at the neck she held caught and broken in her hand. Staring into his wrinkled face, she frowned. What was he thinking in his final moments? What on earth had he seen to make such an expression?

"If I knew this would happen, maybe I shouldn't have killed you," Jeniela muttered in a small voice. She hated the frustration of not getting answers to her questions.

However, it was already too late. She released his neck, and Sword Immortal's body slumped down powerlessly.

"Was it enough to make you use the Demonic Eye?" Gemini asked, tilting his head as he approached.

Above Jeniela's head, not too high up, floated a small sphere. It was a full moon, forcibly created using the last Demonic Eye she possessed.

"I don't know what it was, but it was dangerous," Jeniela said honestly. She did not know what Sword Immortal had been trying to do at the end. Her body had been shattered into countless pieces of flesh, and after that, something was about to happen, something faster than her body could regenerate.

What would have happened if she had not used the Demonic Eye? If she had not achieved super-speed regeneration by receiving the full moon's magic power? If whatever Sword Immortal had been trying to do had been faster than her grabbing and twisting his neck?

"I need to rest a bit," Jeniela said, sitting down on the spot. The last Demonic Eye could create a full moon at any moment, but it was not omnipotent. As it was her last resort, it came with many penalties.

"Can I trust you?" she asked, glancing at Gemini.

"Don't worry, Queen," Gemini replied with a grin.
