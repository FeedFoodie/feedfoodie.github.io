"You're an idiot."

The statement was blunt, a simple declaration of fact.

"No, not just an idiot. You're a complete fucking idiot. I can't possibly fathom how a mind like yours ever managed to learn magic, let alone become a Lich."

The words stabbed deep. Dozens of retorts, each more venomous than the last, sprang to Preskan's mind, but he crushed them all.

"Yes," he forced out, bowing his head. "You are correct."

The reply was not what he wanted to say, and the humiliation burned within him, yet, the being next to him was not someone he could treat with impulsive emotion. Preskan might be a Lich, his Life Force Vessel hidden securely far from death's reach, but that offered no protection here. The entity beside him was a powerful being, capable of annihilating him completely without any regard for the vessel's location.

He was in the presence of Arbes, a Black Star at the very pinnacle of the Predator. He was the Arch Lich, the apex of all his kind.

Hundreds of years ago, Arbes had made a name for himself as an archmage. After becoming a Lich, he had earned a terrible infamy, a stark contrast to the prestige he once held. The Sage of Two Faces, that was the title they had given him after his transformation. The world had been horrified when Arbes, who had stood at the pinnacle of the Mages Guild for centuries, suddenly reappeared as a Lich. The horror only grew when it was revealed that he had been practicing all sorts of horrific Dark Magic, including human experimentation, even during his time in the guild.

Preskan, a Lich who had also lived for a long time, was no match for him. The realms of magic they had reached were simply different. As a mage, Preskan held a pure, unwavering reverence for Arbes, a feeling that persisted even knowing the Arch Lich's personality was utter trash.

"However," Arbes said, his tone shifting to one of gentle praise as if he hadn't just insulted him, "your long-cherished wish, and this artificial life you created to realize it, are quite excellent."

At those words, Preskan's feelings twisted into a complicated knot. He felt the pleasure of being acknowledged by the great and ancient archmage, but it was soured by the anger and loss of having the creation he had dedicated his life to stolen from him.

"Though it's laughable how you made something so well and then didn't even try to control it properly," Arbes laughed heartily. He looked nothing like a Lich, his form that of a robust young man, a clear contrast to Preskan's body of black smoke.

"Isn't that right?" Arbes continued, turning around. "Playing house is fine and all, but if you're going to make something, you should use it properly. There's nothing to be gained by letting it run wild. Take that as my advice to you."

Aine stood there blankly. Her face was a mask devoid of emotion, her eyes calm and subdued. Arbes regarded her with a pleased expression.

"A tool should be a tool," he said admonishingly.

Preskan only half-bowed his head and did not reply. After creating Aine, he hadn't tried to control her much. He had plenty of ways to do so if he put his mind to it, but he had deliberately chosen not to. Regardless of how she was made, Preskan genuinely thought of Aine as his child.

To be honest, Aine was not a good daughter, and likewise, Preskan was not a good father. Nevertheless, he had been satisfied with their relationship. Although she showed discontent, Aine always followed his words, and Preskan believed that as this continued, a true father-daughter bond would form.

However, that hope was shattered. After being captured by Jeniela in the North, Preskan was forcibly made to join the Predator, a connection that naturally led him to the Arch Lich, Arbes. Jeniela had shown little interest in Aine, but Arbes was different. He saw what kind of being Aine was at a glance and stole her without Preskan's consent. Engraved with the Mark of Obedience, Aine became Arbes's faithful servant. Preskan couldn't even properly express his dissatisfaction, because that damn Arbes was truly a being who could annihilate him whenever he pleased.

"...Hm?"

Arbes, who had been inspecting the magic circle in the Central Plaza, suddenly stiffened. He straightened from his bent posture, a frown creasing his brow.

"Two rats have broken through the barrier and intruded. That bastard Kim Jonghyun. Can't he even do his job properly?" Arbes muttered, clicking his tongue.

The original plan had been for Arbes and Preskan to activate the magic circle while Kim Jonghyun incited Chuseong to cause chaos in Urmuri. Now, two unexpected rats had interfered.

"Could it be... Yana?"

"That Nine-Tailed Fox has left this city. It'll take her half a month to return," Arbes spat with conviction. "Even if she finds out something has happened in Urmuri, the Nine-Tailed Fox can't come back. If she doesn't undergo Spirit Possession with the Demonic Spirit, she'll lose all her power as the Nine-Tailed Fox."

Yana, who had torn out the Red Ghost's heart, was a great yokai that even Arbes wasn't confident he could handle. It was also impossible to go to the Demonic Spirit Pavilion and capture the powerless Yana. The pavilion was a forbidden, sacred ground that even an Arch Lich like him could not trespass.

"This has become a nuisance," Arbes muttered, looking down at the magic circle. It was not yet complete. If the intruders were truly just rats, it wouldn't be a problem, but if they were skilled enough to break a barrier he himself had set, they were far beyond that level.

"...What will you do?" Preskan asked cautiously.

Arbes answered without a moment's hesitation. "You go and buy some time."

He didn't tell him to kill them. That would have been an impossible task anyway. At the command, the Black Mist that formed Preskan's body flinched and trembled.

"The magic circle is still incomplete. It's a high-level creation that someone like you can't handle, so I can't go."

"But by myself..."

"Did I tell you to go die? I told you to just buy some time. You've hidden your Life Force Vessel, so you won't die anyway, right?"

"But..."

"It'll be more annoying if the rats get closer while you're babbling."

After that, Arbes spoke no more to him. It was unspoken pressure, telling him to get lost and do as he was told. Hesitating, Preskan glanced at Aine's face, but she didn't spare him a single glance. The Mark of Obedience that Arbes had engraved was that powerful. In the end, Preskan couldn't resist and left the plaza.

"What an idiot," Arbes muttered before resuming his work on the magic circle.

The intricate design engraved in the Central Plaza gathered all the Demonic Energy of the city into one place. The reason for inciting Chuseong through Kim Jonghyun and letting him rampage was to spread various forms of fear through the commotion, a part of the plan that was proceeding smoothly.

"You'll get to eat something delicious soon," Arbes muttered with a chuckle. His words were directed at Aine, who stood silently behind him. With the Mark of Obedience engraved on her, she just stood there blankly, offering no reply.

The Demonic Energy of an entire city was a colossal power, one that even Arbes wouldn't dare to handle. In the first place, Demonic Energy was a power that could not be wielded by anyone other than a Yokai.

However, Aine was a different story. Her body, formed around the Black Heart, could immediately absorb anything, including Demonic Energy, and apply it to her form. The magic circle Arbes was drawing was not for himself, but for her.

For now.

"Perhaps this will allow you to become a Slaughter Predator," Arbes's voice was filled with delight. "I hope so. I really hope so. Because if you become a Slaughter Predator, it means I become a Slaughter Predator."

The preparations were already complete. Once he guided the city's gathered Demonic Energy to Aine and it successfully settled within her, Arbes planned to immediately use Soul Exchange magic to swap bodies with her. If that happened, everything would go smoothly. He would retain the vast magic power and memories tied to his soul, while gaining a body that held Demonic Energy and could dominate everything it consumed.

Through this, Arbes believed he could be reborn as the king of the Predator, a Slaughter Predator, a being that had never appeared before.

{sep}

An innumerable horde of Ghouls burst from the houses, kicking down doors as they came. In life, they might have been human or Yokai, but now, such distinctions were gone. They were just Ghouls, moving corpses that flailed their limbs and made unpleasant noises as they rushed toward Lee Sungmin and Envirus.

Without panicking, Lee Sungmin began his breathing technique. He intentionally drew upon his Demonic Energy instead of the Violet Dawn Divine Art, and the Void Monarch assisted without being asked. Crimson Demonic Energy coiled around his spear. Maintaining his breath, he thrust the weapon forward with tremendous force.

KWAANG!

The spear, saturated with demonic power, swept away dozens of the charging Ghouls in a single, devastating wave. Nothing remained where the energy had passed. The power was greater than he had expected, surprising even him. In truth, this level of force was possible with the Violet Dawn Divine Art, but in terms of efficiency, the Demonic Energy was overwhelmingly superior.

"Don't overuse the Demonic Energy!" Envirus shouted in a loud voice.

Heeding the warning, Lee Sungmin tried to use the Violet Dawn Divine Art and Demonic Energy simultaneously. He was still unused to the combination, and the inner arts and demonic power he pushed into the spear became disordered, but as he focused his mind, the two forces began to mix just as he wanted.

KWARURURU!

The twin powers, forced into the spear, resonated with the Orichalcum of its construction. Envirus, who had been turning Ghouls to ash with bursts of light from his wand, flinched in surprise and glanced back at Lee Sungmin. The power now emanating from the young man was so great it astonished even the ancient mage.

For a moment, Lee Sungmin himself was overwhelmed by the terrifying violence he felt thrumming through the spear in his hands. Even though it was his own power, he hesitated slightly to wield it and release its force.

The hesitation was brief. He swung the spear, following the principles of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He unleashed the fourth form, the Nine Dragons Slaughter, and tried to add the enhanced qi of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art to it, but it was too late.

Faster than he could control it, a violent power was released. Lee Sungmin failed to master the surge and lost his grip on its immense force.

The nine dragons created by the technique manifested with the greatest power they had ever possessed since he first began learning the spear art. Even the most outstanding Nine Dragons Slaughter he had ever achieved, the one he had perfected at the end of 2100 years in the Mental World, could not compare to this.

The street vanished.

Lee Sungmin swallowed a gasp against the pain that felt like his arms were being twisted, and tightened his grip on the spear. The entire street, once lined with tightly packed houses, had been swept away by the Nine Dragons Slaughter, disappearing without a trace. He stared, dumbfounded, at the empty space where buildings had once stood.

"...My goodness..." Envirus muttered. He stared at Lee Sungmin's back, his mouth half-open, as Lee Sungmin let his spear hang low.

Lee Sungmin looked down at his Orichalcum spear, which was emitting a white smoke. He knew well that Orichalcum resonated easily with magic power or inner arts, but he had never imagined this level of power.

"You... what in the world was that just now?" Envirus asked. "Demonic Energy... was that the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy?"

Lee Sungmin didn't answer. His chest was pounding violently. *Is it the Black Heart that's beating, or is it 'my' heart?* He swallowed hard.

A fleeting thought crossed his mind.

*If I can perfectly wield this power... I can truly become Wiji Hoyeon's equal.*
