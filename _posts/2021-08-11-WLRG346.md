A short while later, Martial Beggar returned, stopping silently before Lee Sungmin. He handed over the small Subspace Pocket he was holding.

"These are the items left in the former Chairman's office," he said, his voice flat. "They aren't very important. Just brushes and things like that."

"This is enough."

While there was no guarantee he could find the Black Dragon Knight with these items alone, it was certainly worth a try. Lee Sungmin bowed his head slightly, acknowledging the bitter expression on Martial Beggar's face.

"Thank you for your cooperation."

Martial Beggar offered no reply and simply turned away. Seeing he had no desire for further conversation, Lee Sungmin turned as well, offering a bright smile to Yana and Baek Sogo.

"Well then, shall we go buy a gift for Teresa?"

Baek Sogo returned a faint smile and nodded. Yana, however, watched Martial Beggar's retreating back and muttered under her breath.

"I don't understand. Why is that human so prideful when facing you? You're overwhelmingly stronger than him."

"It's a matter of his position," Lee Sungmin explained.

"It just looks like he doesn't know his place," Yana grumbled. From her perspective, Martial Beggar's hostility was incomprehensible, a difference that stemmed from the fundamental divide in thinking between humans and Yokai.

Though his business in Kron was finished, Lee Sungmin did not return to Legro Forest right away. Baek Sogo's words about her lack of good memories in the city lingered in his heart. He wanted her to relax and make some pleasant memories, even if it was just for a day. Besides, Teresa had asked for a gift from Kron, which gave him the perfect reason to stay.

From a distance, Chwi Geol watched the three of them. Lee Sungmin, Baek Sogo, and Yana felt his gaze but paid it no mind, considering it a matter of course. Chwi Geol gripped his long, empty left sleeve as he observed them chatting while they browsed the various goods spread out on a mat. He saw the faint smile gracing Baek Sogo's face.

*It's been a long time.*

He swallowed the words. Had it truly been over fifteen years? The sting of the slap she had given him back then was long gone, but the emptiness in his hollow chest remained.

*Why must I stand here and watch you? What I did back then… I certainly did it for your sake.*

At the time, Baek Sogo had resented him, demanding to know why he had forcibly brought her out. Chwi Geol could not understand her resentment. When all was said and done, he had saved her life, yet instead of thanks, he had received only her scorn. Despised, Baek Sogo had eventually stormed out of Kron and severed all ties with the Murim Alliance.

For fifteen years, Chwi Geol had followed the rumors about her. He never imagined she would return to Kron with the Phantom Spear, a man he had thought was dead. When he first heard the Phantom Spear had become a demonic practitioner, Chwi Geol had felt a secret joy. He knew Baek Sogo's disposition well. He had been certain that the woman known as Silent Flash Baek Sogo would kill her cherished Junior Disciple with her own hands, no matter how much she cared for him.

However, she had not.

*Why in the world are you there? Why are you smiling?*

Chwi Geol could not understand it, not that he had any intention of approaching her to ask. His emotions grated on him, stirring a deep irritation. For the first time in a decade, he lamented his own lack of power. If only he had been strong enough.

What would he have done?

He turned away, feeling as if he had seen something he should not have. It seemed his mood would remain sour for some time.

For Teresa's gift, they chose a Dog Beating Stick, a popular local souvenir, even though she had specifically said she did not want one. The decision was largely influenced by Baek Sogo, who was tempted by the merchant's claim that it could be used by women for self-defense, for massages, and even as a laundry stick.

"Do you think she'll like it?" Baek Sogo asked, her voice excited.

"I don't really know Teresa's tastes," Lee Sungmin replied cautiously.

"She'll hate it," Yana stated in a cold tone.

*No, she'll love it,* Baek Sogo insisted internally. She tapped her own shoulder with the stick, a joyful smile on her face. "You can use it for massages, too."

Just in case, Lee Sungmin also bought a few accessories. He reasoned that since Teresa had likely never had a chance to adorn herself with such things while living in the Holy Palace, she might actually prefer them. Yana, however, showed little interest in choosing a gift. She had rarely spoken with Teresa even in the Fairy Forest, wary of the powerful hold she possessed.

After picking out the gifts, they walked the streets until they realized they had not eaten. Lee Sungmin asked a passerby for a recommendation and they soon entered a restaurant known for its good food.

While they waited for their order, Yana suddenly asked, "Lee Sungmin, do you have any experience with cannibalism?"

"……"

Lee Sungmin, who had been drinking water, nearly spat it out. "Wh… What?"

"Cannibalism," she repeated.

Baek Sogo shot Yana a displeased look, but Yana paid her no mind and continued.

"You're a very heterogeneous being, Lee Sungmin, but it's an undeniable fact that your body is that of a Yokai. Have you never eaten human flesh? Or felt an appetite for it?"

"…Why do you ask?"

"There are many types of Yokai. Among them, the great ones with powerful strength are the embodiment of human fear. The Void Monarch is the same. For such Yokai, cannibalism is a matter of course. As embodiments of fear, they were born as predators meant to devour humans from the very beginning."

"…Yana, do you have any experience?"

"For me, cannibalism isn't a particularly meaningful act. It's not like I have a personal taste for human flesh."

"It's the same for me," Lee Sungmin said.

"Weren't you transformed into a Yokai through the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy?" Yana tilted her head, her question born of genuine curiosity. "And yet you don't feel an appetite for humans?"

Lee Sungmin answered with a troubled expression. "My body may be that of a Yokai, but my mind is human. It hasn't changed at all. Perhaps… if I were to eat human flesh, I might unknowingly accept it as 'delicious', but I don't want to."

[If you wish to strengthen your power as a Yokai, cannibalism isn't a bad choice,] the Void Monarch remarked inside his head. [Eating human flesh now won't revive the vanished Slaughter Predator. Nor will a new inhumanity take root. If you choose to do it yourself, your body as a Yokai will grow that much stronger. With the Black Heart you possess, you could perhaps even grow stronger faster.]

Lee Sungmin had no intention of strengthening his Yokai powers any further. His body was already strong enough. He planned to focus on adding martial arts and insights to his foundation. The current Lee Sungmin was incomparably stronger than he was ten years ago, but he was still no match for the 'real' Slaughter Predator or Jeniela. The problem was not with his body, but with what was inside it.

[Was her name Aine? That Chimera. You'd do well to be careful of that wench,] the Void Monarch warned. [Her body is completely that of a Yokai. On top of that, she split that overwhelming Demonic Energy from Urmuri with you. Unlike you, she has no qualms about cannibalism. Jeniela is trying to make that wench into the new Slaughter Predator. If Jeniela puts in that much effort, she will become a monster of that caliber and stand in your way.]

*That's likely.*

The food arrived. Despite the recent conversation, Yana picked up her chopsticks without hesitation. Lee Sungmin poured more water into his dry mouth.

After finishing their meal, they rode the Fairy Horse. There was one more place they needed to stop before returning to Legro Forest. He planned to meet Frau in Urmuri, certain that the sorcerer could perform any spell Aladur could.

"It's been a while," Frau said. He was still at Yana's residence. "And you're still as sudden as ever." He grumbled as he covered the naked boy in his arms with a blanket.

Baek Sogo turned her head aside with an embarrassed expression. Lee Sungmin cleared his throat and stepped forward. Frau glared at him and spat.

"From now on, could you please… not visit like this?"

"I'm sorry."

"Yana, when are you planning on coming back? Don't tell me you've shacked up with that pretty boy… just kidding, just kidding. There's no way you'd do that." Frau quickly changed his tune when he saw Yana's eyebrows furrow. He sat cross-legged and fixed his glare on Lee Sungmin. "So. What is it this time? Isn't that Silent Flash Baek Sogo over there? Looks like you two have met."

"Yes… um, I came to ask a favor. There's someone I want to find."

"Do I look like someone who does whatever favor is asked of me?"

Lee Sungmin quietly reached into his Subspace Pocket and pulled out a jewel larger than his fist. He silently offered it to Frau, who pouted but still reached out and took it. Lee Sungmin then opened the pocket he had received from Martial Beggar, took out an elegant-looking brush, and handed it over.

"I want to know where the owner of this brush is."

"Is this the only item?"

"Do you need more?"

"It's more accurate to use several items rather than just one."

Lee Sungmin emptied the rest of the Subspace Pocket. A few more brushes appeared, along with an inkstone, some articles of clothing, and various other miscellaneous items. Frau gathered them in one place and stood a few steps back. He brought his hands together and began to chant an incantation. The sorcery began in earnest. Befitting his title as a Grand Sorcerer, Frau was well-versed in most forms of magic aside from Ghost Soul Art, and a spell like this was no exception.

"…Mountain…" Frau muttered, his eyes closed in concentration.

Mountain… Lee Sungmin repeated the word in his mind.

Frau's eyebrows furrowed. He raised both hands and clutched his head as the space around him shimmered, forming the image of a landscape.

"…Huh…"

The scenery looked somewhat familiar. *Where have I seen it before?* Lee Sungmin tilted his head, studying the desolate mountain terrain. He was not the only one surprised. Beside him, Baek Sogo let out a startled sound.

"It's Mount Mushii."

*Ah.*

Lee Sungmin's mouth fell half-open. Of course, Mount Mushii. The image was too blurry to recognize easily, but hearing Baek Sogo's words brought the memory back clearly. It was the place where survival itself had been his first trial. It definitely looked like Mount Mushii.

*Why is the Black Dragon Knight on Mount Mushii?*

After spending a night in Urmuri, he summoned the Fairy Horse the very next day. Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue as he recalled the mountain where he had undergone his trials as a child. He also thought it was for the best. He wanted to talk with Mushii. As a child, he could not understand the things Mushii had said, but now he believed he could.

"I never thought I'd come back here," Baek Sogo muttered with a bitter smile. While Lee Sungmin had been wandering the world, she too had returned to Mount Mushii to undergo trials again for several years.

"Yeah."

The one who answered was neither Lee Sungmin nor Yana. It was a voice he had not heard in a long time. Lee Sungmin looked toward its source.

"I never thought I'd see the two of you together again."

A small boy covered from head to toe in bandages and dressed in filthy rags stood before them. Mushii was grinning at Lee Sungmin and Baek Sogo.

"Did you come here to undergo trials again?" Mushii asked, walking toward them with a rustling sound. Through the bandages wrapped around his face, his eyes sparkled. "Or did you come to meet someone who is undergoing trials on this mountain?"

"You knew?"

"Don't take a god so lightly," Mushii cackled, shaking the rags draped over his shoulders. "Yesterday, someone was peeking at this mountain with sorcery. Did you think I wouldn't see through it? I just let it be." He paused, his grin widening. "I was thinking of sending them out soon, so this is perfect. Take them with you."

"You wanted to send them out?"

"They're a very nasty bunch."

'Bunch'. Lee Sungmin tilted his head at Mushii's choice of word.

"…It wasn't just one person?"

"There are two," Mushii said, turning around. "And there's one who's like a madman."
