Aine was not dead. She had only fled because the fight had turned against her. He had severed both her arms, yet he had also witnessed her feet regenerate in an instant after he sliced through her ankles.

He still did not know why she had attacked him, but one thing was certain. Aine knew he possessed the Black Heart, and Preskan, that monstrously strong Lich, was not dead either. The two of them were undoubtedly working together.

*Perhaps... they're trying to steal my heart.*

He could think of it no other way. He had been lucky. Without Scarlet, he would have died quickly. Even with her there, he would have perished if he had not stumbled upon the way to use enhanced qi in that desperate moment.

Yes. It was simply a matter of luck.

*In the end, it's all about luck.*

Unsure when Aine might strike again, they left the village the day after the rain stopped. Though Lee Sungmin's wounds had mostly healed, Scarlet insisted on driving the carriage in his place. She proved surprisingly skilled at it, and after hearing the general situation, she agreed they needed to leave the area as quickly as possible.

With Scarlet at the reins, Lee Sungmin had more time to reflect. He could now use enhanced qi, a significant breakthrough, yet his martial arts proficiency had not increased. The status window did not lie.

*I still haven't overcome the wall of the Supreme Peak.*

He had scaled a single wall only to find countless more looming before him, their very existence still beyond his perception.

*Have I gotten a little stronger?*

He could not answer his own question. He had... probably grown stronger, but it felt insufficient. Even with the ability to wield enhanced qi, he did not believe he could win against Preskan. The same held true for Aine. There was no guarantee he would be so lucky a second time.

So, he began to swing his spear again. It had been quite a while since he had focused on training with such intensity. He had been frustrated after hitting a wall he thought he could not overcome, and that frustration had led him to stop. He had not wanted to feel an even greater sense of hopelessness, afraid of the Despair that would come if his martial arts theory proved useless.

Things were different now.

Though the threat of Aine and Preskan loomed, Aine had not appeared since the ambush. The silence was unsettling, but their journey was otherwise smooth. They passed through several cities, repairing the carriage and changing horses as needed. Three months passed since they had left Behenger. Whenever he had a spare moment, Lee Sungmin practiced his spear techniques, movement arts, and inner cultivation, and whenever he did, Scarlet would open a thick magic tome and immerse herself in the study of a discipline he could not begin to comprehend.

"You're really working hard."

Scarlet's voice cut through the cold dawn air. She stepped out of the carriage, wrapped tightly in a blanket. Lee Sungmin, letting the chill dry the sweat on his skin, glanced back at her.

"You're up early," he noted.

"Didn't have a good dream."

A faint irritation clouded her face as she spoke. She glanced at the campfire and extended a hand. Without any incantation, the flames surged higher.

"That reminds me, you were under that dream restriction on Mount Mushii, weren't you? Thinking about it, you're really tough. You had nightmares every single day. I would've gone mad."

"It was surprisingly bearable. Plus, I had your magic, Scarlet."

"Brainwashing's just a temporary fix. I taught it to you, but honestly... I didn't think you'd last. I thought you'd either finish your training and bolt, or just go insane. One of the two."

Scarlet chuckled, crouching near the fire and pulling her blanket tighter.

"What are you doing? Don't mind me, just keep going."

"Aren't you going to sleep more?"

"I told you. I had a nightmare."

"What kind of nightmare?"

"You're so nosy. Why do you care what I dreamed about? You gonna solve it for me if I tell you?" Scarlet asked, the corners of her lips turning up. Lee Sungmin was completely used to her way of speaking and simply shrugged.

"I can listen."

"I don't particularly want you to."

"Then don't."

Lee Sungmin replied and raised his spear again. He focused his mind, circulating his inner qi until the purple smoke coating the spear coalesced into flames. His speed at forming enhanced qi had improved quite a bit, but it was still slow. He could not help but feel he lacked a natural knack for it.

"I dreamed of the past," Scarlet muttered.

Lee Sungmin did not stop swinging his spear, but he kept his ears open. If he were to stop and give her his full attention, she would likely clam up.

"It's from when I was young. When I was a very... dainty and cute little girl. Before I was summoned to Eria, I mean. Before all this fighting spirit. It's not a big deal, but... a war broke out."

"Well, when war breaks out, you get orphans. I was one of them. That's the dream I had. My family died, my village burned. I was lucky to survive, and I've been lucky ever since. I managed to survive somehow, and I even got to learn magic."

"When it comes to surviving, that is," Scarlet added. "Actually, I think everything is like that. You survive and live on because you're lucky. If you're unlucky, it's only natural to die, right? Around me... there were a lot of unlucky people. When you finally get close to someone and they die just because they're unlucky... well, the lucky one who survived feels a pretty big sense of loss. That's just how it is."

Dying because of bad luck. Lee Sungmin agreed with her. He had died in his past life for that very reason and had survived in this one because he was lucky, though he could not be sure if that 'luck' was truly just luck.

"It's hard to get used to that kind of loss. For me... it is. This is a kind of defense mechanism. I've been lucky so far, and I think I'll continue to be, but I can't bring myself to think that other people will be lucky too. That's why I don't want to get close to anyone."

Scarlet let out a hollow laugh. "What am I even saying."

"So, it's like this. I just have to not get close to them. People are all like that, aren't they? When someone close to them dies, they feel all sorts of things, but when a complete stranger dies, it's just... whatever. Yep. You can't even call it selfish, that's just how people are. Me included."

He moved through the forms of the Rannachal, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. The technique was familiar, practiced for so long that his body swung the spear without obstruction. The inner qi flowing from his qi center sustained the enhanced qi, but its purple light began to fade. Just maintaining the state demanded immense concentration, and listening to Scarlet's story made it difficult to focus.

"We should arrive in Bredon in about a week," she said. "We've been lucky so far and nothing's happened. I hope it stays that way."

"Are you worried about me?"

Lee Sungmin stopped his spear. At his question, Scarlet looked up from the campfire, the flickering light illuminating her face.

"Yeah." She nodded. "Because we've gotten close. I don't want to see you die."

He offered a bitter smile. "It's the same for me."

"It's not just you, Scarlet. I also... don't want to see people I know die. If I can prevent it, I want to."

That was why he wanted to save Baek Sogo. It was not because he was some special kind of person. It was simply because she was someone he knew, someone he did not want to let die. That was all.

"You should know you're lucky."

Scarlet pulled a thick magic tome from inside her blanket. She pushed a pair of glasses onto the bridge of her nose and grinned.

"Because lucky me is traveling with you. You know, right? If it weren't for me, you would've died when 'that' monster attacked."

"About that... I am grateful."

"Just thinking it isn't enough," Scarlet said as she opened the book. "If you get the chance, pay me back. I saved your life once. So you have to save my life later."

"I understand," Lee Sungmin replied with a smile, though he did not pay it much mind. He knew no danger would befall Scarlet for at least the next ten years. Soon, she would complete the magic of the Reshir School and become the Crimson Archmage. The 'luck' that had kept her alive until now would continue to protect her.

Lee Sungmin, however, was different.

*I've been lucky so far, too.*

However, what about the future?

{sep}

Bredon was a city of Buddhist statues.

Shaolin, one of the Nine Sects One Gang, was a place where the Noble Clan was particularly powerful. They were often called the Mount Tai and Big Dipper of the orthodox murim, with Mount Tai referring to Shaolin and the Big Dipper to Wudang. This was a common truth across the murim of most dimensions. Though Bredon was a city in Eria, its atmosphere was starkly different from Zenavis, Behenger, and the other cities they had passed through. Buddhist statues guarded its entrance, and the scent of incense mingled with the air.

"This is my first time coming to Bredon."

Entry into a great city like Bredon required proof of identity. While cities like Zenavis and Behenger teemed with riff-raff otherworlders, a city of Bredon's caliber did not. That sort of person would die long before ever reaching its gates.

"An S-Rank Mercenary... from Behenger. You've come a long way."

Even the guards at the city gate wore martial arts uniforms instead of armor. Scarlet's eyes sparkled as she gazed past them at the long stretch of Eastern-style buildings within.

"They say the main branch of Shaolin first came to Bredon hundreds of years ago. Isn't that fascinating? It means otherworlders have been summoned to this world for centuries."

They passed through the gate without difficulty. Lee Sungmin's identity was proven by his mercenary badge, and Scarlet's by her affiliation with the Mages Guild.

"The Divine Buddha Grandmaster Mugak, who was summoned hundreds of years ago, arrived here and named a nameless mountain nearby 'Mount Song'. It seems that in most Central Plains Murim, Shaolin was located on a mountain called Mount Song. Of course, the appearance of that mountain varied from person to person."

Walking down the street, Scarlet raised her hand and pointed. Beyond the western gate of Bredon stood a mountain that was not very large. That was Eria's 'Mount Song', home to the main branch of Shaolin.

"otherworlders have been summoned for hundreds of years, but no one's discovered why. Some mages, lunatics really, even think the 'truth' is tied to the reason for the summoning. Quite a few schools of thought have researched it, but no one has figured it out. In the first place, who could possibly answer such a question?"

"Uh... wouldn't a god know?"

"A god? Ahaha! I asked Mushii. He said he doesn't know either. I don't think there's any special reason for it. This world is just 'that kind' of world. One that summons unlucky people from all dimensions for no particular reason."

Scarlet laughed loudly.

"Ah. Maybe it's too hasty to say they're all unlucky. For some, being summoned here might have been a stroke of good fortune. In my case, it's half and half."

She looked back at Lee Sungmin, her eyes narrowing into a mischievous smile.

"We're not heading to Shaolin right away, are we? So... since we're here, let's do some sightseeing."

It was not a subtle suggestion. At the blatant invitation, Lee Sungmin laughed softly and nodded.

"Alright."

He was, after all, quite interested in the city of Bredon himself.
