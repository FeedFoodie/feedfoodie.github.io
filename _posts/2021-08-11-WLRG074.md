"Did you two spend all night stuck together or something?"

Scarlet directed her question at Lee Sungmin and Namgung Heewon, who had grown noticeably closer. At her words, Namgung Heewon laughed heartily and clapped Lee Sungmin on the shoulder.

"For men to become close, a drink, a night, and a single duel are all that's needed."

"I can accept the other things, but 'a night' sounds a little strange. You didn't do anything weird, did you?"

Horrified by the scrutinizing question delivered with narrowed eyes, Lee Sungmin shook his head vigorously. Unlike the quick-witted Sungmin, Namgung Heewon simply tilted his head.

"A night is a night. What do you mean by 'weird'?"

"Hyung-nim, please don't ask unnecessary questions."

"No, I'm asking because I really don't know. Little brother, do you know what she means?"

Lee Sungmin offered no answer. Namgung Heewon still looked puzzled, but Scarlet didn't bother to explain her meaning to him either.

"Anyway," Namgung Heewon declared, "as of yesterday, the two of us have become sworn brothers."

"Good for you," Scarlet said. "You've become sworn brothers with the young master of the Namgung Clan. You won't have to worry about starving to death anywhere you go."

As one of the prestigious clans, the Namgung held influence throughout Eria. If you went anywhere and could prove your affiliation, you would receive treatment that was by no means poor. This wasn't the case everywhere, of course. In regions where unorthodox sects held power, a connection to the Namgung Clan could actually be a liability.

The three of them departed through the west gate and headed straight for Mount Song. The mountain, where the main branch of Shaolin was located, was neither very large nor rugged. There was no way to know what Divine Buddha Mugakdaesa, Shaolin's founder in Eria, had been thinking when he named such a small mountain Mount Song and established his temple there.

"He must have gone senile in his old age," Scarlet grumbled while taking a drink of water.

Lee Sungmin inwardly agreed with her, and Namgung Heewon burst out laughing. Though it had only been a day since they met, Lee Sungmin had taken quite a liking to the man. It was because of his unpretentious nature, a trait that didn't fit his background as the young master of a prestigious orthodox clan.

"You said you made a promise long ago with Grandmaster Bulyeong, the head of Shaolin," Lee Sungmin said. "What was it about?"

"It seems my little brother was curious. Why didn't you ask yesterday?"

"I felt it would be a bit awkward to ask when we weren't close."

"That's right," Namgung Heewon chuckled. "If you'd asked then, I wouldn't have answered, but I'll tell you now."

He continued, "The Namgung Clan, the Jaegal Clan, the Moyong Clan, and the Tang Clan. These four families naturally have four heirs. Our ages are all similar, and our skills are about the same."

Lee Sungmin listened intently.

"It was back when we were still young. The heads of each Noble Clan wanted to create an organization to unify us, you know, like the Murim Alliance. They even came up with a name, the Heavenly Dragon Association, but when they actually tried to create it, a problem arose. The skills of each clan head were also similar. If they tried to appoint one as the Chairman, the other families would likely object, and there wasn't a single family head with enough skill to overwhelm the others."

The head of a prestigious clan was the greatest master within the family, responsible for leading and commanding it. Just as the Namgung Clan had the Heavenly Venerable Sword King Namgung Baek, the other families also had masters comparable to him.

"So they decided to leave it to the next generation. Since they had children of similar ages, they decided to raise us well and make the most outstanding one the Chairman of the Heavenly Dragon Association. The one who acted as the notary for this agreement was the current Abbot of Shaolin, Gwebeol Buryeong Daesa."

Lee Sungmin had never known about such circumstances in his past life, partly because he had no connection whatsoever to the prestigious clans. It was also because the Heavenly Dragon Association had not existed up until the time he had died.

*So that means they'll be competing with each other for at least the next eight years.*

Who would ultimately win and become the master of the Heavenly Dragon Association? As one tends to favor their own, Lee Sungmin found himself hoping Namgung Heewon would be the one to claim the title.

Their conversation carried them all the way to the mountain gate of Shaolin. Though Mount Song itself was small, its gate was anything but. Lee Sungmin was so overwhelmed by the towering structure that he hesitated, taking an involuntary step back. Scarlet also let out a sound of admiration and nodded her head.

"If the doors are that big, wouldn't they be inconvenient to open and close?"

"It's not like I'm the one closing them, so I don't care," Namgung Heewon said and strode up the stairs.

Lee Sungmin followed him with Scarlet, fumbling for the manuals for the Hundred Steps Divine Fist, Arhat Movement Arts, and Dharma Meditation Art, which he had placed in his robes earlier.

"It has been a while, Young Master Namgung."

The monk guarding the gate bowed his head deeply. His clean-shaven head, without a single strand of hair, glistened in the sunlight. Scarlet, who caught the full force of the reflection, frowned.

"Indeed," Namgung Heewon replied. "Have the other heirs arrived?"

"They arrived yesterday," the monk said, rolling the Flame Curse in his hand. "You are the last to arrive, Young Master Namgung."

His tone wasn't accusatory, but the fact remained that he was late. Feeling he was the reason for the tardiness, Lee Sungmin glanced at Namgung Heewon. His new sworn brother, however, seemed unfazed and even let out a laugh.

"I had a reason to be late. I had a pleasant encounter."

"Is it because of the two who came with you, Young Master Namgung?" the monk asked, his gaze shifting to Lee Sungmin and Scarlet. His eyes showed little interest in Scarlet, a woman, but it was different when they turned to Lee Sungmin. Feeling the intense curiosity in that gaze, Lee Sungmin bowed his head.

"That's right."

"He seems so young, yet his level is incredible..." the monk muttered in admiration as the three of them passed him and entered Shaolin.

"Little brother, you said you would return the martial arts manuals to Shaolin," Namgung Heewon began. "Since it's come to this, why don't you follow me and meet Grandmaster Bulyeong? I think he'll give you a better price."

"I didn't come here seeking a reward, however."

"Hey, little brother, I'm not naive. Where do you get off telling such a ridiculous lie? We're talking about three of Shaolin's secret martial arts manuals. You must want at least a Minor Cycle Pill for them, right?"

It was a direct hit. Lee Sungmin didn't argue and just let out a dry cough.

"Grandmaster Bulyeong is an eccentric old man," Namgung Heewon continued. "Let me give you some advice, little brother. Don't lie to him. That old man, I don't know if he learned some divine power from chanting scriptures, but he can spot a lie like a Ghost Spirit."

"A lie... no. What happens if I lie?"

"You get beaten," Namgung Heewon said with a straight face. "That old man isn't called the Eccentric Buddha for nothing. He's an old man whose fists move faster than his words. Sometimes it seems like he has dementia. I don't even know how an old man like that became the Abbot of Shaolin. Is it because he's old?"

Was it really okay to let him badmouth the Abbot in the main temple of Shaolin? Lee Sungmin was genuinely worried but didn't go out of his way to stop him.

"You're as rude as ever."

An annoyed voice cut in, and Namgung Heewon's steps halted.

"Tang Ahee," he muttered.

A woman dressed in a tight-fitting, jet-black martial arts uniform strode toward them. She glanced at Lee Sungmin and Scarlet standing behind Namgung Heewon before glaring at his face.

"The promised day was yesterday, wasn't it?"

"Well, I had my reasons for being late."

"Why should we have to lose a day because of your personal reasons?"

"Who asked you to wait?"

Tang Ahee was left speechless by his shameless retort. With a loud snort, Namgung Heewon strode right past her, leaving her with a face flushed crimson in his wake.

"You're really rude," Scarlet commented.

"She was rude first, so why should I have to be polite?" Namgung Heewon replied without changing his expression.

As Lee Sungmin followed, he glanced back toward Tang Ahee. She had spun around, and their eyes met.

"...Who is that Young Master?" she asked in a sharp voice, her question directed at him.

Before Lee Sungmin could answer, Namgung Heewon laughed loudly.

"Hahaha! Calling a master who is knocking on the door of the Supreme Peak a Young Master."

"The Supreme Peak... you say?"

"It seems our young lady of the Tang family lacks a keen eye. Have you been neglecting your martial arts training?"

At Namgung Heewon's taunt, Tang Ahee's face grew even redder. She let out a ragged breath and spat out her words.

"So who is that person!"

"It changed from 'Young Master' to 'person'. I see our Young Lady Tang is quite particular about such things."

"I asked who he is!"

"He is my little brother," Namgung Heewon answered with a grin.

A sudden understanding dawned on Lee Sungmin. This was why Namgung Heewon had made him a little brother. It was a fact that Lee Sungmin, at eighteen, had surpassed a peak master and was knocking on the door of the Supreme Peak. He possessed potential and skill that no one could ignore. The fact that Namgung Heewon had such a person as his little brother elevated his own status just by association.

*Should I call him clever or just rude?*

It was probably both. In any case, it wasn't a bad thing for Lee Sungmin. Just as Namgung Heewon was using his very existence, Lee Sungmin could also use the fact that he had become sworn brothers with the young master of the Namgung Clan, a background more than worthy of the connection.

"I am Lee Sungmin."

"Lee Sungmin...? I don't know that name..."

"Of course you don't. So, where are the other heirs?" Namgung Heewon asked, clasping his hands behind his back.

At his question, Tang Ahee bit her lip and spun around. She didn't say to follow, but Namgung Heewon trailed behind her as if it were the most natural thing in the world.

Two people, a man and a woman, were standing in a well-kept garden. Namgung Heewon, who had followed along, introduced them to Lee Sungmin via Thought Transmission.

[That guy over there, standing so seriously, is Jaegal Taeryeong. He's the young master of the Jaegal Clan and has the title Silver Flying Dragon. The bitchy girl from just now is Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee, and the beautiful young lady over there, like a single flower, is... the one I admire, Snow Flower Moyong Seojin.]

They were all titles Lee Sungmin knew. Silver Flying Dragon Jaegal Taeryeong, Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee, and Snow Flower Moyong Seojin. Standing before these influential figures, with whom he'd had no connection in his past life, made him feel strange.

"You're late, Heewon," Jaegal Taeryeong said.

"I had my reasons."

"It is not a big problem that we waited for you. However, you will have to apologize for making Grandmaster Bulyeong wait."

"Isn't that something I can do directly to Grandmaster Bulyeong?"

At Namgung Heewon's reply, Jaegal Taeryeong stared intently at him, but Namgung Heewon did not avoid his gaze. Moyong Seojin, who had been silent, finally opened her mouth.

"Grandmaster Bulyeong is waiting."

"Mm."

Namgung Heewon gave a slight nod. He had been arrogant with Jaegal Taeryeong and Tang Ahee, but he kept his mouth shut in front of Moyong Seojin, pretending to be taciturn. It seemed his transmitted words about admiring her were true.

"Well then, little brother," Namgung Heewon said, turning to Lee Sungmin. "I'm going to see Grandmaster Bulyeong. You and Scarlet can look around Shaolin for a bit. I'll put in a word for you with him beforehand."

"I understand, Hyung-nim."

When Lee Sungmin said that, Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin looked a little surprised. They too had sensed the level of his martial prowess and were taken aback that such a master was calling Namgung Heewon 'Hyung-nim' with such respect.

Once the two of them were left alone, Scarlet grumbled.

"I hope the temple food is edible. Come to think of it, I haven't even eaten breakfast yet."
