Ju Hwayin stood on a windswept cliff. The cool breeze not only drove away the midsummer heat and made her hair and clothes flutter, but also threatened to sweep her off her feet. Still, she wasn't afraid of the dizzying drop before her.

The surrounding scenery unfurled like a dream. Clouds drifted so close that she felt she could touch them, and a nameless bird sang as it flew into the distance. For some reason, a flower blooming tenaciously in a crack on the opposite cliff face reminded her of herself.

A few steps behind her, Lee Dansim stood with her hands gracefully placed on her hips, as if she were posing for a painting.

Ju Hwayin examined herself. Her hands were as white as ever, and the tips of her feet peeking from beneath her ornate robes seemed unreal.

"You know, I've never once truly loved you," a man's deep voice rang out from behind her.

She turned around. Crimson Tiger stood there, his face devoid of emotion. Seeing him made her realize she was dreaming. 

Ju Hwayin bit her lip, a wave of sorrow washing over her. "Yes, you only care about your daughter."

"Despite knowing that... you threatened my daughter and held her hostage?"

"I didn't have a choice."

"Really?" Crimson Tiger sneered, casting her a gaze colder than the frigid wind. "I disagree. You just chose the easiest way out."

Ju Hwayin fell silent, wondering if he might be right.

Crimson Tiger continued menacingly, "If you touch my daughter, I'll kill you in the most gruesome way possible and then devour your soul."

Ju Hwayin's eyes filled with a lonely light. "Don't do that. My soul is too bitter for you to eat."

WHOOOOOSH!

As she spoke, a sudden gust of wind pushed her toward the cliff's edge. She flailed her arms, trying not to fall.

Crimson Tiger did not help her. Instead, he warned her again, "If anything happens to my daughter… all of you will die!"

Meeting his penetrating eyes, Ju Hwayin gave up trying to hold on and fell straight down the cliff.

SWOOOOOOOSH!

Her clothes billowed around her as wind rushed over her body. Ju Hwayin fell with her arms spread wide, staring up at the sky.

Far above, she could see the worried face of Lee Dansim, the only person in the world who truly cared for her. She shouted her name, then leaped off the cliff without hesitation. 

"Young Lady!" Lee Dansim screamed, even as she fell faster and faster, her face growing larger and larger.

{sep}

Ju Hwayin's eyes flew open. Bathed in the faint light of dawn filtering through the window, Lee Dansim stood looking down at her.

"Did you have a nightmare?" Lee Dansim asked.

Ju Hwayin blinked in confusion. "Huh?"

"You were screaming."

"I was screaming?"

"Yes."

Ju Hwayin smiled wryly. The fall in her dream had been refreshing and pleasant, yet in reality, she had been screaming. That was the difference between dreams and reality.

She got out of bed and walked to a wall, looking at her reflection in the bronze mirror. Her face, gaunt and dripping with sweat, was unrecognizable.

She thought she knew why she had had such a dream. It was an ominous, prophetic dream, a manifestation of her growing fear that she would ultimately have no choice but to exploit that man's daughter to control him.

"...I have a bad feeling about this," she sighed bitterly.

At the same time, Baek Museong stood on the roof of Tempest Academy, gazing out at the breaking dawn.

"Everything is proceeding according to plan," Jin Chung reported, appearing behind his lord.

Baek Museong asked softly, "When can we begin?"

"In three days."

Baek Museong nodded. The long night had passed, and the sun was rising. "Chung."

"Yes?"

"If I happen to die fighting this war..."

"Don't say that!" Jin Chung cut his lord off and bowed his head. "Forgive my impertinence, but please don't talk about such ominous possibilities."

Baek Museong smiled faintly. "I can't do that. You're the only one I can say this to."

"Young Lord."

"If I die, don't make a grave for me. Cremate me and scatter my ashes right here."

"That won't be possible. Even if you were to die, Young Lord, rest assured that I would surely die before you."

Baek Museong's smile deepened. "Is that so?"

Jin Chung nodded firmly. "I'm certain of it."

"Then I suppose I'll have to find someone else to bury me."

"You'll have to."

"Shall I ask Crimson Tiger?"

"You don't like him," Baek Museong remarked calmly.

"He's..." Jin Chung hesitated. Describing the feelings Crimson Tiger evoked in him was not easy. After thinking for a while, he finally gathered his scattered sentiments into one word. "He's dangerous."

Baek Museong nodded. "Indeed, he is."

He's just a swordsman, yet he's involved with both you and the Third Young Lady. That's why I'm scared. I'm terrified that he might be destined to stand against you, Young Lord."

Baek Museong could relate to Jin Chung's concerns. He had thought the same thing at times. Crimson Tiger was undoubtedly a dangerous individual. Nevertheless, he still had his uses.

Jin Chung added, "Even now, I can't tell whether he will lift you up or drag you under."

"At the very least, I must repay him for saving me this time."

"I've prepared ten thousand taels."

Baek Museong shook his head. Although Jin Chung deemed that amount sufficient, he thought otherwise. Tapping his chest twice, he said, "Give him that."

Jin Chung's eyes widened in shock. "That's too extravagant a gift."

Baek Museong's good-natured smile melted away. "It's fine. I'll just take something far more precious from him later."

Elsewhere, dawn was also breaking over the temporary training ground behind the 16th Transport Branch.

Crimson Tiger stood in the middle of the field, holding his sword out straight ahead. Soul Crusher glinted with a sharpness that could cut air, but he stared at its tip for more than fifteen minutes.

The sword was telling him that the time for change was now. Well, more precisely, it was not the sword speaking, but his body expressing itself through the sword. Fortunately, his external wounds had scabbed over well, and his qi was so pure and powerful that his internal injuries had healed considerably. He was now truly ready to step into a new realm.

He focused on his qi, which flowed freely through his body. The amount had increased by more than twenty years' worth. The fact that this happened even though he had not begun meditating immediately after ingesting the pill given to him by the unknown person meant only one thing—that medicine had to be a divine elixir on par with the Great Cycle Pill.

*Who could it have been?*

He was now certain that the woman he had briefly glimpsed moments before losing consciousness was not a hallucination.

*Was it the Third Young Lady? But the face was different, and I doubt she would have disguised herself to save me. What about the First Young Lord? ...No, it's not him. If he were to send someone, it would definitely be Jin Chung. Then... Cheon Ahseong? Maybe, but we aren't close enough that I can ask him for confirmation.*

In any case, someone had shown him kindness and saved his life. Regardless of who they were, he had a strong suspicion that they would appear before him again someday. He would bury his curiosity until then.

"Nyaa!" A black cat emerged from the corner of the building and sauntered into view. 

Crimson Tiger sheathed Soul Crusher, and the cat slinked over and leaped onto his shoulder. Somehow, it knew he had survived a grievous injury and was concerned for his well-being.

He scratched the scruff of its neck. The cat narrowed its eyes and let out a long purr. It then hopped down, swished its tail, and climbed up a tree. 

Crimson Tiger chuckled at the sight. Although they seldom spent time together, he was gradually growing fond of the creature.

He walked back to the middle of the training ground and sat down cross-legged. Having cleared his complicated thoughts, he was now about to do what he had set his mind to. For some reason, however, he found himself on edge.

"Nyan!" Perhaps sensing his tension, the black cat in the tree meowed loudly, assuring him that it would alert him of any intruders. 

Finally, Crimson Tiger relaxed. The cat's presence reassured him more than any human bodyguard.

He began to circulate his qi, guiding it through a different path than usual. Throughout his recovery, the blood vessels all over his body had itched so badly that he wanted to scratch them until they were raw. If not for his master's previous advice, he would have mistakenly believed he had fallen into qi deviation.

\> "A day will come when a change will visit your body."

He had already opened the Conception and Governing Vessels before leaving his master, so this referred to a realm beyond that.

\> "As you know, the Asura's Eight Narakas is a martial art based on extreme yang qi. In order to execute it perfectly, one must either be born with the Extreme Yang Physique or achieve Acupoint Metamorphosis."

Acupoint Metamorphosis was a foreign concept to most martial artists, who were primarily focused on achieving Body Reformation. According to his master, enlightenment came naturally to those who knew when it was the right time to learn, so even he had only learned about it right before leaving home.

> " You will only be able to perform Acupoint Metamorphosis once you have completely mastered the Asura's Eight Narakas, because that is when your internal and external arts are properly harmonized. The entire process could take a year or thirty, depending on the individual. In my case, I achieved Metamorphosis fifteen years after mastering the art. This technique is completely different from what's generally referred to as Body Reformation."
>
> "How would I know when the time is ripe?"
>
> "You'll know naturally, because all the blood vessels in your body will itch like crazy."

SSSSSS!

Crimson Tiger felt a shimmering heat rise from the top of his head. Following the oral formula of the Acupoint Metamorphosis method his master taught him, he moved his qi with great care. His biggest worry was not making a mistake, but rather the unknown pain and agony that would accompany the technique. For some reason, his master had not told him about the side effects, so he braced himself for intense suffering.

*I need to stay calm. Not just Master, but his master, and the masters before him, all of them accomplished Acupoint Metamorphosis. There's no reason I can't do it as well.*

He had arrived at this point due to a combination of factors. The Great Cycle Pill was the first, learning from Cheon Ahseong was the second, and the inner qi he had just gained from the unknown elixir was the third. His intense battle with the Evil Blade served as the final piece of the puzzle.

He circulated his qi repeatedly. One cycle, two cycles, three cycles. With each repetition, he felt the passages of his blood vessels widening and his qi flowing with greater ease.

SSSSSSS!

The heat above his head coalesced into a ring. That single ring became two, and then three. Each time he completed another cycle, a new ring materialized. The process wasn't as difficult as he had thought. On the contrary, he felt incredibly refreshed, so light that he could fly.

Steadily, the number of rings increased. When they reached five, the first crisis arrived. There was no pain. Rather, he felt so good that for a moment, he forgot to circulate his qi.

\> "You fool! How could you daydream during such an important procedure?!"

He instinctively recalled his master's scolding, and the flow of qi resumed. Regardless, he kept losing focus because he felt too good. There was the joy of reaching a new realm, of flying, of embracing a woman, of eating delicious food, and of sleeping when tired. Pleasure after pleasure washed over him, threatening to drag him deep into its depths.

*I'd choose extreme pain over this any day...*

Crimson Tiger focused his mind by conjuring an image of Seohyeon lying in her bed. When he thought of his ailing daughter, no amount of joy could shake his will.

SSSSSSS...

Soon, there were seven rings, and he circulated his qi for the eighth time.

"Ugh!" he suddenly gasped. 

Immense pain ripped through him without warning, and qi churned through his body at a furious pace. Thousands of needles seemed to flow through his blood vessels, piercing through his acupoints.

Crimson Tiger gritted his teeth. Screaming while circulating qi was dangerous. After the intense pleasure, the pain felt even more acute, but he had to endure it. His instincts told him that this was the most critical juncture.

He conjured the opposite image in his mind, one of his daughter smiling brightly. A grin naturally crossed his lips. The pain was excruciating, but he could withstand it. No suffering was too great as long as his daughter could be happy.

KABOOM!

Something exploded inside his body, and the number of rings above his head increased to eight. A pleasant sensation surged through him, like he was breaking free from a muddy bog. 

Still, Crimson Tiger did not drop his guard.

SSSSSSS...

He circulated his qi once more. The finishing touch was the most important. 

Finally, he opened his eyes. His body felt as light as a feather. Sunlight glared down upon him. Even though he felt like only fifteen minutes had passed, in reality, hours had gone by. 

He could sense the changes in his body. The world appeared as vivid as a landscape after an early morning rain. His vision, already superior to that of ordinary martial artists, was now indescribably sharp and far-reaching.

Crimson Tiger rose to his feet, but he swayed and nearly fell over. His body was too light!

"Hahahahaha!" he laughed heartily.

*I did it!* he thought, even happier than the day he had mastered the Asura's Eight Narakas. Now, he had laid the foundation for reaching its true heights.

The black cat, which had been standing guard, leaped onto his shoulder and licked his neck as if to say, 'well done.'

"Thank you. With this, I've taken another step forward!" he exclaimed.

With each hurdle he overcame, he felt closer to Seohyeon. Come the day when the white snow fell and the grand ritual succeeded, he would disappear forever from this turbulent jianghu. He would live a peaceful life without power struggles or violence, a life where he did not have to kill or be killed.

He gathered his qi again. The time it took him to finish one cycle of qi circulation was reduced threefold, meaning he could recover three times more quickly. His qi center was also much larger

Joyous as these developments were, they came with a greater burden. He had to conceal his aura more thoroughly, and avoid Cheon Ahseong as much as he could. The Association Chairman would see right through his transformation.

WHAP! WHAP! WHAP!

Clenching his fists, he punched the air. He was close to fully mastering the Thirteen Shadowless Maneuvers, too. All he needed was the right trigger.

Of course, just as with the Asura's Eight Narakas, the real journey began after that. He couldn't afford to become complacent. For now, he would focus on the Thirteen Shadowless Maneuvers, and then revisit the Asura's Eight Narakas.

"Nyaaaaang!"the black cat yowled.

DING!

Crimson Tiger halted his training. Both the cat's cry and the faint ringing of his alarm threads indicated that someone was approaching.

A moment later, Jin Chung appeared. "How are you? Feeling better?"

"Yes, thank you for your concern," Crimson Tiger replied. He had anticipated a visit from the First Young Lord's side since he had successfully completed his mission to free them from their predicament.

"The First Young Lord is busy and couldn't come."

"I understand."

"May I ask a personal question?"

"Go ahead."

"How did you find the evidence?"

Crimson Tiger smiled mysteriously. "That's a trade secret."

"Haha, I thought you'd say that." Jin Chung chuckled. He didn't completely dislike Crimson Tiger. At the very least, he respected the secret agent's skill and tenacity, and now was the time to express those positive sentiments. Handing over a small pouch, he said, "This is a special gift from the First Young Lord."

Crimson Tiger took the pouch and opened it. Inside was a thin undershirt. 

His eyes widened in shock. This was no ordinary item.

"That's the Invisible Armor, one of the jianghu's Three Legendary Armors," Jin Chung explained. "It won't be cut by sword qi, or even enhanced sword qi. Of course, that depends on who's using it."

Crimson Tiger recognized the name. Although the Invisible Armor was considered inferior to the other Three Legendary Armors in pure defensive capabilities, it was thin and easily hidden under one's clothing. This advantage more than made up for its inferior durability. Most importantly, for someone of his skill, the armor was a priceless gift, akin to gaining an extra life.

I've been collecting a lot of legendary artifacts lately...

Soul Crusher was one of the Three Legendary Swords, the Poison Resistance Ring was one of the Three Legendary Artifacts, and the Invisible Armor was one of the Three Legendary Armors.

Jin Chung added, "This gift represents the First Young Lord's appreciation for your work, so please accept it without reservation."

"I understand. Please extend my thanks to the First Young Lord," Crimson Tiger replied. He had expected a considerable reward, but not the Invisible Armor.

"We'll be counting on you in the future as well."

"Understood."

Nodding, Jin Chung disappeared.

Crimson Tiger waited until the man had stepped beyond the range of his senses before excitedly taking off his shirt and putting on the Invisible Armor. The fabric clung snugly to his body. Indeed, if he wore regular clothes over it, it would impossible to tell he was wearing armor.

*Who would have thought this thin garment could block even enhanced qi?*

He was ecstatic. If it could increase his chances of survival, he would even wear clothes made of thorns, never mind a comfortable undershirt.

"Hahahaha!" he laughed in satisfaction.

He had already saved up enough money. Now, what he needed most were things that could boost his chances of survival. That made the Invisible Armor more valuable than any other artifact.

Grinning, he put his shirt back on, mere moments before a woman entered the training ground.

Yeon's eyes widened at the sight of him covered in sweat. "Are you okay?" she asked worriedly. She hadn't expected he would be well enough to resume training so soon.

"Yeah, I feel much better."

Yeon sighed in relief, but asked again, "Are you sure?"

Crimson Tiger waved his arms around to assure her that she needn't worry. He knew how much she cared for him. There was a deep bond between them that could not be expressed in words, a camaraderie deeper than mere friendship or affection.

"Phew!" Yeon's eyes reddened. Although she had feelings for him that went beyond camaraderie, she had never expressed them before and never would. "...Hmm? Wait, you seem different somehow?"

Crimson Tiger feigned ignorance. "What? Did I lose weight?" 

Yeon tilted her head. Something had definitely changed about him, but she couldn't express it in words. Finally, she exclaimed, "Ah! Your gaze has deepened! What on earth happened?"

This was the most obvious difference to her. Crimson Tiger's gaze was so deep and profound that she could not look away.

Crimson Tiger beamed. "I was lucky and experienced a brief moment of enlightenment," he confessed, feeling no need to hide his accomplishment from Yeon.

"Ah!" Ever quick-witted, Yeon immediately caught on and smiled brightly. "Congratulations, Crimson Tiger!"

"Thanks." Crimson Tiger closed his eyes, suppressed his aura, and reopened them. If before he had only shown half of his skills to peak experts, now he would have to reveal only two-tenths of it. "What about now? Do I still look different?" 

Yeon stared at his face. "Yes, you still look different from before."

Crimson Tiger sighed. It seemed he had gone beyond the stage where he could intentionally hide his strength, yet he had not reached Origin Reversal, where his skill would be naturally hidden unless he willed it otherwise.

After all, Acupoint Metamorphosis was different from Origin Reversal, which was achieved by uncovering the ultimate secrets of martial arts. To achieve true Origin Reversal, one would have to reach the realm of the Martial God, like Cheon Ahseong.

Previously, each time he reached a new stage, he had to strive anew to conceal his aura, but it was at least doable. This time, though, the transformation was so substantial that it was proving difficult. He concluded that he needed more than just a basic technique.

"Yeon, there's a place I need to go. Will you come with me?" he asked.

Yeon smiled. "Of course."

CLIP-CLOP! CLIP-CLOP!

A carriage sped down the main road, with Crimson Tiger and Yeon aboard.

"So, where are we going?" Yeon asked. She had already asked twice, but each time, Crimson Tiger merely smiled and said nothing. Since he wouldn't tell her, though, she grew more curious. "Well... I guess I'll find out when we get there."

She felt a growing sense of anticipation. She had wandered all over the Central Plains on missions before, but never with Crimson Tiger for any reason other than work. This journey felt like a lovers' honeymoon, and her heart fluttered with giddy excitement.

"How many days do you have left on your sick leave?"

"I don't know, but I doubt I'll receive new orders anytime soon."

Previously, Crimson Tiger had exaggerated the severity of his injuries, buying them some time.

Yeon gazed out the window. Taking in the rapidly shifting landscape, she was reminded of the day she first became a spy handler. She was so nervous that her heart had felt like it was going to explode on her first mission, but after that, she had remained mostly impassive. Never in her wildest dreams did she imagine she would become so deeply involved in her partner's personal affairs.

Suddenly, she recalled something her instructor used to say.

"We handlers have one privilege. A way to die easily, without having to hang ourselves or jump off a cliff. Do you know what that is? It's getting emotionally attached to the Phantoms."

Her instructors had repeatedly stressed the importance of not getting emotionally attached to the Phantoms. Some handlers followed that rule strictly, but a significant number struggled to do so. Like herself.

A faint smile played across her lips.

*Even if it comes to that... I won't regret it.*

Half a day later, the carriage pulled up to the entrance of a bustling marketplace.

Crimson Tiger handed the coachman a silver coin. "Go have a meal or something," he said.

"Thank you." The coachman grinned and left to find a restaurant.

Crimson Tiger walked in the opposite direction, moving confidently and purposefully. He turned into a small alley that was home to a small weapons shop.

"This is it," he said.

Yeon stared at the shop in surprise. *There are many larger weapons shops near the Association, so why did Crimson Tiger come here of all places?*

"Is this really the place?" she asked. "Did you come here to meet someone?"

"No. I came here to buy something." Crimson Tiger grinned and stepped inside. 

Yeon followed him. The store's interior fit the mold of an ordinary weapons shop, with dozens of swords, blades, spears, and staffs on display.

A middle-aged man greeted them, "What are you here to buy?" 

"A broken arrow."

The middle-aged man's eyes glinted sharply. "Wait a moment," he said, before going around back.

"Now I know what this place is. This is the Hidden Arrow Merchant, isn't it?" Yeon whispered. The Hidden Arrow Merchant dealt in various artifacts, from famous swords to unusual hidden weapons. She had learned of its existence during past missions, but had never visited the store herself.

Crimson Tiger nodded.

Yeon asked, "Why did you come here?

"There's something I need to buy."

"What is it?"

Before Crimson Tiger could answer, the middle-aged man reappeared, saying, "Follow me."

The pair followed him inside. There was another building behind the main one, and along the way, Yeon could detect the presence of hidden martial artists.

Next to her, Crimson Tiger discovered that he could sense them much more accurately than before, right down to their breathing.

The three of them entered the new building.

"Ah!" Yeon exclaimed admiringly. Just as she had heard, the exotic weaponry adorning the walls was a rare sight. Each one was an incredibly precious artifact.

"What did you come to buy?"

"Is the rumor that there's nothing you don't have here true?"

"That's right," the man answered confidently. As a result of running a business with hundreds of branches, their organization had accumulated immense wealth and power. If they did not have a divine weapon or artifact, then it did not exist in the jianghu.

"Do you have an artifact that suppresses qi?"

The man seemed startled. "Everyone looks for ways to increase their qi. It's rare to find someone looking to constrain it."

"So you're saying you don't have any?"

"Please wait a moment." The man took out a ledger and began searching through it. After a while, he found something and took a pair of rings covered in dust out of a display case. "Ah, here it is. This is the Force-Halving Ring. It has no effect if you wear one on each hand, but if you wear both on one hand, you'll only be able to use half of your inner qi."

Crimson Tiger nodded. "This is exactly what I want."

"No one has wanted something like this for the last ten years."

"Aren't you glad that you're selling it after ten long years?"

"That's true, but why do you need this?" the man asked. His curiosity was piqued. No matter how sharp his powers of observation were, he couldn't measure Crimson Tiger's skill. 

Crimson Tiger smiled mysteriously. Even now, his aura stirred like lava, but that was something only sensitive martial artists like Yeon or peak experts could feel. "I'm on an errand for someone who likes to collect unusual artifacts, " he replied.

"......" The man sighed. Clearly, this customer did not want to give him a clear answer.

Crimson Tiger asked bluntly, "How much?"

The middle-aged man paused, calculating the ring's value. "Although the ring went unsold for ten years, its ability to halve qi is truly unique."

"I agree," Crimson Tiger answered calmly. He had no intention of haggling. All treasures had their proper value in the jianghu.

"Ten thousand taels."

Crimson Tiger reached into his pocket, but before he could take out his wallet, Yeon contacted him via telepathy.

[Wait! Please leave this transaction to me,] she insisted, then said to the man without waiting for a reply, "That's too expensive."

The man had, of course, quoted a high price. No one would pay ten thousand taels for something that suppressed their inner qi. However, such was the nature of business. Crimson Tiger didn't seem to care, but Yeon didn't want him to get ripped off, not even over something so trivial.

"You should know that precious artifacts are priceless in this jianghu."

Yeon snorted. "That would be the case if it were really a precious artifact."

"Are you saying the Force-Halving Ring isn't precious?"

"The dust piled on it speaks for itself. Oh, I admit that it's a rare item. It's just..."

"What are you trying to get at?"

"I want to buy it for the price it's actually worth and not a tael more."

The two bargainers faced off tensely.

"Fine! I'll take off a thousand and sell it for nine thousand taels," the man conceded.

"Even three thousand taels is too much for something this worthless," Yeon scoffed and turned away.

Crimson Tiger quietly followed her lead. 

"Three thousand taels?" the man shouted from behind them. "Just go home if you can't afford anything!"

Yeon grabbed Crimson Tiger's arm. "We will. Goodbye!" she snapped, grabbing the door handle.

"...Wait!" the man yelled. 

Yeon glanced back at him.

"You're quite impatient," he softly said. "As the saying goes, one should stop a fight but encourage a negotiation. Let's try to agree on a price. How much will you buy it for?"

"I said even three thousand is too much."

"That's out of the question!" the man exclaimed. He paused for a moment to think, then said, "Eight thousand!"

"Five thousand!"

"Seven thousand!"

"Six thousand!"

"...Fine," the man relented, sighing. Who knew how many more years would go by before someone else interested in the Force-Halving Ring appeared?

Yeon turned to Crimson Tiger and smiled brightly. 

Chuckling at her expression, Crimson Tiger paid the six thousand taels. The sum was actually quite substantial, far beyond what an ordinary person could save over a lifetime.

After completing the transaction, they were about to leave the store when the middle-aged man suddenly called out, "Hey!"

"What is it?"

"Don't let her go, yeah?"

"...We're not like that," Yeon replied, her face flushing bright red. After saying those words, though, she felt even more embarrassed.

The man grinned and winked at Crimson Tiger. "Then make it so you are."

Yeon rolled her eyes as if the very idea was absurd, but inwardly, she was so pleased that she wondered if she should have gone easier on the merchant.

Finally, the pair stepped outside.

"Hehe, I did well, didn't I?" Yeon asked, beaming with pride like a child who won a prize.

"Here, take this." Crimson Tiger handed her four thousand taels. Although he had earned a lot of money, he just realized that he had never paid her for her work. Normally, he worried that it might hurt her pride, but today's circumstance was different.

"Why are you giving this to me?"

"This is the amount I saved thanks to you."

"I didn't do it expecting a reward!"

"I know."

Yeon hesitated for a moment but eventually smiled and accepted the money. "Thank you," she said.

She could guess his intentions. If her pride was hurt by this, it would be tantamount to rejecting his good intentions. They were much closer than that. At least, that was what she believed.

"Don't mention it." Crimson Tiger flashed a wide grin.

If it was for Yeon, he wouldn't hesitate to spend even a hundred thousand taels. That was why he was so careful about giving her even a single coin right now. He had much bigger plans for their future, when this ordeal ended.

At that time, he decided he would take responsibility for the rest of her life. He had something in mind, but it wasn't yet time to tell her.

Crimson Tiger put the Force-Halving Ring on his wrist. Wearing one had no effect, but when he added the other, he felt his qi being blocked.

VMMMMMM!

He took off one of the rings and put it on his other hand.

VWOOOOOM!

His qi came back instantly.

The Force-Halving Ring was a marvelous artifact indeed. In his current state, it was worth not just six thousand taels, but sixty thousand.

Crimson Tiger put both rings on one hand again.

"Your gaze has darkened even more than before," Yeon noted. Even though she knew otherwise, her senses incorrectly told her that Crimson Tiger was weaker than before they began their last mission.

Crimson Tiger smiled. This was exactly what he wanted. He needed the Force-Halving Ring to hide the intensity of his gaze.

Yeon returned his smile. She was overjoyed at his growth, which now required him to conceal his skills with an artifact.

*You shine even brighter now, Crimson Tiger.*

Of course, she only said that in her heart.
