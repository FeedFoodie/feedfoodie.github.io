How much of an advantage could he truly gain by taking its left eye?

*No. Don't think about advantages.* A wounded monster only went berserk. If he let his guard down just because he had the upper hand, he would be the one to suffer. A single blow from the Ogre Chimera was more than powerful enough to turn his body into a bloody pulp.

Gripping a new spear, Lee Sungmin held his breath and watched for the Ogre's next move. The creature covered its ruined eye, its breath coming in heavy pants as it glared at him with its one remaining orb of sight.

However, when the Ogre's hand fell away, its left eye was perfectly fine.

*Regeneration.* The word flashed through Lee Sungmin's mind. Of course. Ogres possessed powerful regenerative abilities, one of the very reasons they were considered apex predators. They could instantly recover from most wounds.

"It's a Chimera!" Rudd shouted from behind him.

An ability that could restore a pierced eyeball was beyond mere regeneration. It bordered on immortality. No matter how strong an Ogre's natural healing was, this was something else entirely. The only explanation was that this was no simple Ogre. It was a Chimera.

*I've learned something. A single wound isn't enough.*

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod, treating it as a learning experience. It was his first time fighting an Ogre, let alone a Chimera. He was glad he hadn't allowed himself a moment of superiority just from inflicting one wound.

*What should I do?*

He met the gaze of the Ogre, which seemed to sneer at him now that its injury was gone, as if asking if that was all he had. That same mocking voice echoed in Lee Sungmin's own mind, a familiar phantom from his past. It brought back the nightmares he endured every day on Mount Mushii, the static that haunted his ears, the stench that lingered in his nose, and then…

The world turned dark, a familiar restriction falling over his eyes. He swallowed, and it all vanished. He saw the forest again, not the mountain. The static in his ears was gone. The stench in his nose had faded. The world was no longer dark.

*I am not heavy.*

SHWIIK.

The spearhead shot toward the sneering Ogre's face. The monster's head snapped to the side, dodging the point, but Lee Sungmin was already in motion. Having raised the spear diagonally, he swung the thrusting weapon sideways.

"......"

The shaft struck the Ogre's temple. It was an attack meant to provoke rather than to kill, and the Ogre obliged. The blow was painless, but being hit in the face was enough to annoy it. The creature swung its fist in a wide arc, bringing it down on Lee Sungmin. He shifted his grip, took a single step back, and spun the spear.

Sweep, parry, thrust. These were the fundamentals of his Spear Technique. A thrust was a Chal, and an outward spin was a Ran. Lee Sungmin spun the spear outward, deflecting the Ogre's descending fist with the rotation of his weapon.

BAAANG!

An explosive sound erupted as the Ogre's arm was flung wide. Rannachal. He had practiced the move tens, hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of times. A Chal shot toward the chest left exposed by the deflected arm. The Ogre twisted its body, its left arm's sharp claws swiping at the spearhead.

Na. Family limited. The moment the inwardly spun spear clashed with the claws, the Ogre's entire left arm was drawn inward against its will. Lee Sungmin retrieved the spear and shifted his grip on the shaft once more.

He stabbed downward. The spear descended diagonally and lodged itself in the Ogre's left knee joint. No matter how tough its skin was, there were always soft spots. The single-point strike pierced the hide and shattered the joint, causing the Ogre's massive body to stagger.

He pulled the embedded spear free as a purple aura surrounded the spearhead like a mist. How much time would it take for the Ogre's regeneration to heal the shattered joint? Lee Sungmin paid it no mind. He moved with the sole purpose of hunting the Ogre.

Another Chal shot out. The off-balance monster flailed its arms, but with its left leg unable to support its body, its claws only cut through empty air.

KWADEUDEUK!

The spear pierced the Ogre's right knee. Lee Sungmin drove it in deep, thrusting until the spearhead tore through the back of the joint. Then, he separated the shaft into two pieces.

Even with its powerful regeneration, the Ogre's first priority would be to pull the spear from its knee. Lee Sungmin spun the separated spear, intending to use the Changjun as a spearhead. It lacked sharpness, but he could compensate for that with his ki and energy release.

The sneer was gone from the Ogre's face. As it tried to pull out the spear embedded in its knee, Lee Sungmin's body vanished from its sight. Traceless Step.

The Ogre couldn't see him, but Rudd did. He watched Lee Sungmin move out of the monster's line of sight, circling around to its left side. Just as the Ogre reached down with its left hand, Lee Sungmin raised the halved spear.

PUUK.

The Changjun dug into the Ogre's left armpit. He thrust it deep, very deep, tearing through the soft flesh until the weapon burst out of the Ogre's shoulder.

"KEUUUUUUH!"

The Ogre screamed, a sound entirely different from before. It must have hurt. Even with its regenerative abilities, the pain was real. Lee Sungmin immediately took a new spear from his Subspace Pocket.

The wounds on its legs had not yet healed. It couldn't stand. The spear in Lee Sungmin's hand exploded with power, its tassel dancing wildly. Lightning Annihilation. The second form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear shot toward the Ogre's front.

TUDUDUDUDUNG!

The barrage of strikes couldn't pierce the Ogre's body, but the force was enough to knock it backward. The monster's body fell, its right arm flailing in resistance, but to Lee Sungmin, the one-armed struggle was slow. Too slow.

The chirping of cicadas reached his ears, the same static he had heard on Mount Mushii.

It wasn't that he wanted to become a cicada. On the mountain, he might have been like a larva, huddled deep underground and waiting to emerge, but Lee Sungmin did not want to be a mere cicada, to huddle for long years only to come out, cry, and die. He did not desire the life of a cicada.

Then what did he desire? What did he want to become, if not that?

He didn't know. Lee Sungmin couldn't answer that question himself. Something other than a cicada. Something else. He didn't know what that something was. He wanted to surpass Wiji Hoyeon. He wanted to protect Baek Sogo. He wanted to see the end of martial arts.

He didn't know what lay at that end, or if he could ever reach it even if he dedicated his entire life to the pursuit. One thing, however, was certain. He could not die. If he died, he could do nothing. He would never surpass Wiji Hoyeon, never protect Baek Sogo, and never see the end of martial arts.

Death was transient and empty. Lee Sungmin knew this well, for he had already died once. At the end of that death, returning to the past again would be impossible. Every human life was lived only once. He was lucky enough to get a second chance, but there would be no next time.

So he didn't want to leave any regrets. He couldn't die here. He couldn't be stopped by a mere monster like this. Wiji Hoyeon would have killed a creature like this with ease. Baek Sogo would have too. It was absurd to think of protecting her while being weaker than she was. If he wanted to surpass Wiji Hoyeon, he had to aim higher and go further.

Would the pinnacle of martial arts be at the end of that path? It was a path a cicada could not travel. Lee Sungmin was not a cicada. On the mountain, he may have been a larva, but the Lee Sungmin who had left the mountain was something else entirely.

*I am.*

Lee Sungmin approached the Ogre. The fallen monster couldn't get up and was writhing on the ground like an insect. He gripped the spear with both hands, his face expressionless.

SHWIIK.

The spear shot out, a sharp 'Chal' that pierced through the meaningless resistance. The left eye. Then the right eye. There was no excitement, only a strange calm that even he found unsettling. He had expected a sense of pride, but there was none. Instead, he only felt a sense of 'this is how it should be'.

Rudd watched Lee Sungmin's actions in shocked silence. After taking both of the Ogre's eyes, Lee Sungmin gripped the shaft tightly with both hands and stabbed the spear downward again and again. He targeted the Ogre's throat. Skin tore. The Adam's apple was pierced. A gurgle of bloody foam rose from the Ogre's mouth. After destroying its throat, Lee Sungmin moved to the Ogre's head and repeated the downward stabs.

Before long, the Ogre's body stiffened and stopped moving. It was dead. The research of Chimeras was the pursuit of a species's limits. This Chimera, based on an Ogre and pursuing the limits of the Ogre species, had died just like that.

"You… what in the world are you?" Rudd asked hesitantly.

Lee Sungmin, who had been looking down at the corpse, answered as he pulled the spear from between its eyes. "A person."

"Are you trying to be funny right now?"

"It's the truth, isn't it?" Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile. After pulling out the spear, he looked at Rudd. "Could you give me a hand? I need to cut off its head to take it with me, but it's hard to cut a neck with a spear."

"No. We're taking the whole thing," Rudd spat out. He strode over to Lee Sungmin and opened the bag on his back, taking out a wooden box about the size of two clenched fists. "An Ogre Chimera's corpse is worth taking whole. We also need to find out what crazy mage released this thing into the forest."

Rudd placed the wide-open box in front of the Ogre's corpse, and the massive body was sucked inside.

"So I've passed the skill assessment, right?" Lee Sungmin asked.

Rudd shot him a dumbfounded look. "…Didn't you hear what I said? An Ogre Chimera is on the level of a Twin-Head. We'll have to investigate the details back at the Guild, but in my opinion, that was a Twin-Head level monster. I've never heard of anyone hunting a Twin-Head for a skill assessment. You… you'll get S-Rank for sure, and you might even get SS-Rank."

Lee Sungmin smiled brightly at his words. It felt good to be acknowledged for his skills, proof that his ordeal on Mount Mushii had not been in vain.

"What kind of crazy bastard released an Ogre Chimera here?" Rudd grumbled.

His words sparked a thought in Lee Sungmin's mind. The Black Mage's Dungeon. He didn't know its exact location. If he had, he would have gone to check it out beforehand, but he had left it alone for that very reason. What was certain was that the dungeon would be discovered within the next six months. An Ogre Chimera appearing outside the walls of Behenger, and the messy way it was handled, seemed to fit the style of a Black Mage.

*Come to think of it, in my past life, there was no story about the Mercenary Guild catching an Ogre Chimera.*

Perhaps in his past life, the Guild had ignored the Ogre subjugation request, but if that had happened, the Chimera would have eventually attacked a town. Lee Sungmin frowned as he compared the memories of his past life with his current reality.

"What are you doing? Let's go back," Rudd said, tapping Lee Sungmin's shoulder.

"……"

Lee Sungmin nodded.
