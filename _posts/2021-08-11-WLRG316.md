"What in the world… happened?" Cain stammered, his mind still reeling, unable to grasp the situation.

Abel's eyes burned with hatred as he stared at Cain. The man was completely useless. Perhaps things might have been different if he still possessed his old fighting spirit, but Cain had lost most of his abilities as a mage. He had only managed to survive by fleeing to the Elemental Realm, clinging to life with the Queen's power. For a mage, breaking a contract with Mana was a fatal mistake.

"Suppressing your aging with what little magic you have left… was it that important?" Abel spat, his voice choked with emotion. "Did you want to live that badly?! Not caring what happened to the world you were born in. Just… living like this… in a mansion the Queen gave you. Tending to some gaudy flower garden. Is that how you've been living?!"

"I… I…" Cain stammered in the face of the outburst. "I tried to do whatever I could. I've also been working tirelessly to stop The End, but as I am now…"

"Shut up."

Abel refused to listen. He had no need to hear any more. He knew about Cain's efforts and had no intention of ignoring them, but in the end, weren't those efforts prioritized around avoiding his own gruesome death? Didn't that ultimately lead to this result?

"What will you do?" Kim Jonghyun asked, his voice laced with amusement. "I'm talking to you, Lee Sungmin. Your plan has failed. Now that it's come to this, the only way to avoid The End is to connect this world to the Great Demon World."

"Don't spout such bullshit!" Abel roared, grinding his teeth. "Even if the Great Demon World connects to this one, it'll still lead to ruin! Are you going to look after humanity? Are you saying you'll protect this world from the clutches of the Demon Race?!"

"No, I have no intention of doing that," Kim Jonghyun replied. "Still, isn't it better than complete annihilation? Even if most humans die, this world won't be completely destroyed."

A choice.

The word echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind. He had heard it several times now, from both Mushii and the Fairy Queen. They had told him the time to choose would come. He looked down at the spear in his hand, mulling over Mushii's warning that he must become a being worthy of the choice he makes.

He could join hands with Kim Jonghyun and connect this world to the Great Demon World. To do that, he would first have to kill hundreds of thousands of humans. No, hundreds of thousands wouldn't be enough. He might have to kill millions. He would have to offer their souls as a sacrifice. Even after that, the deaths would continue endlessly. An Eria connected to the Great Demon World would no longer be the Eria he knew.

*When did you start caring about human lives?* a voice echoed from a corner of his mind. *Isn't the most important thing yourself? No matter what happens to this world, you will survive. With the power you have, you can surely preserve your own life.*

*Don't be ridiculous. There's no guarantee that's even true, is there?*

So, should he just recklessly try to stop Kim Jonghyun? His methods were atrocious, but wasn't he ultimately trying to stop The End?

*Stopping The End? No, Kim Jonghyun is just doing what he wants. Think about what you wanted. You felt it was unfair, a waste. Would living in a miserable world connected to the Demon Realm resolve your feelings of injustice?*

*Shut up.*

The voice swirling in his head was his own. What answer was he supposed to give? There was no right answer either way. Connecting to the Great Demon World was just another form of The End. It might be a different story if he were stronger than the lords of the Demon Realm or the Demon God himself. As he was now, he had struggled just to defeat the incomplete Demon Lord, Kim Jonghyun. If the worlds connected with him having only this much power, Lee Sungmin would have a hard time protecting even his own life.

If that was the case for him, what about everyone else? He recalled distant memories of the people who had helped him in Zenavis. Hans, Jack, and Lula. They were ordinary people with no power. Lula had gotten married, he remembered. What about the child she'd had? What about Celgerus? Scarlet? Baek Sogo? Wiji… Hoyeon? Grandmaster Bulyeong, Namgung Heewon, Jihak, and so on, and so on, and so on.

*Who are you to care about them?*

*Shut up.*

No. Kim Jonghyun was wrong. His method could not save this world.

*Oh my, since when did you care so much for this world? Since when did you want to save it? The only reason you want to stop The End is because you've never felt happiness in this world. You lived a miserable, hectic life, and now that the world is ending, you can't stand it because it feels like a waste, an injustice. Isn't that it? Or was it because you feel responsible for the death of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?*

Hearing the sarcastic voice, Lee Sungmin laughed. "Haha."

"Stop it," he muttered to the chattering voice. Deep in his heart, that was what 'I' had been thinking. He remembered what Abel had asked. *Are you desperate?* To be honest, he wasn't sure. His desperation felt small. Strangely so. He didn't want the world to end, so he would stop it. It felt as if he had naturally fallen into this course of action, that he had just gotten swept up in it.

He hated it. That was an undeniable fact.

"Aren't you willing to compromise?" Lee Sungmin began. "We could connect to a different world, not the Great Demon World."

"How? No, where?" Kim Jonghyun scoffed. "Are you suggesting we try to connect to the Elemental Realm again? Haha… Why should I do that? Connecting to the Great Demon World is what I desire."

Persuasion was impossible.

"Is there no other way?" he asked Abel, but Abel couldn't answer. As long as Kim Jonghyun was determined, there was no other way.

[What is it you want to do?] the Void Monarch asked. [What Kim Jonghyun is trying to do will ruin this world in many ways, but it will avert a complete The End.]

*I know.*

[If you stop Kim Jonghyun because you don't like his plan, then there's no way to stop The End right now. Either way, it's a shitty choice.]

*Will there be enough time?* Lee Sungmin muttered to himself. *Even if I succeed in stopping Kim Jonghyun here, The End won't come immediately. The End that will come someday… just how much time will we have?*

[You're forgetting something,] the Void Monarch said. [You went to the Dungeon where you died in your past life, and you obtained something there. You still don't know how to use it.]

*Are you saying that key could be the answer?*

[I don't know, but it must have been given to you for a reason, don't you think?]

*I myself might be a part of The End. Would a key given to someone like me really help in avoiding The End?*

[You aren't even sure of that yourself. The choice is yours to make. What are you? What do you think you are?]

Lee Sungmin.

[Do you want to become The End?]

Further questions and answers were meaningless.

Lee Sungmin made his choice.

He exploded forward in a sudden leap, and Kim Jonghyun reacted instantly, spreading his hands to block the airborne warrior.

"Stop."

The command was clearer and faster than before, a product of the Demon Lord's body growing stronger with each battle. A powerful restraining force seized Lee Sungmin, but he ignored the pressure gripping his entire being and forced his spear to move.

CRACKLE!

His strained joints shattered. He ignored the pain. Instead of moving the spear, he kicked the air as if it were solid ground. The two steps he barely managed to take unleashed Two Step Lightning Annihilation.

BOOM!

With a tremendous sound, Kim Jonghyun's command lost its power. Kim Jonghyun clicked his tongue and moved his hands. Just as his commands had strengthened, so too had his magic. It felt as if he could become addicted to this kind of authority. *If I get the chance later, I should look for a way to become a complete Demon Lord,* Kim Jonghyun thought to himself.

Hundreds of fireballs descended upon Lee Sungmin. It was an attack focused on pure firepower, lacking any subtlety. He unleashed Two Step Gentle Ripple: Flying Lightning. The fireballs that had been rushing toward him all exploded at once. From within the blasts, Lee Sungmin became a streak of lightning, piercing the distance to Kim Jonghyun.

"Block."

A command and a defensive magic spell unfolded simultaneously. He pushed his charging speed even faster. Amidst a speed that could make one's mind go blank, he unleashed Soul Chasing Annihilation Lightning.

CRACKLE!

The defensive barrier shattered. Both of Lee Sungmin's arms, gripping the spear, were twisted.

*I will kill Kim Jonghyun here,* Lee Sungmin resolved. Kim Jonghyun would never be persuaded. If he didn't kill him now, the man would only repeat the massacre from Germud and try to connect the worlds again. So, he would stop him. He knew it was an irresponsible act, done with no clear way to stop The End, but it was the choice he had made. In a way, Kim Jonghyun's plan might be the only way to save this world. Still, he intended to stop him. Yes, because there might be another way. If both The End and the Great Demon World would create the same kind of tragedy, he would rather bet on the side with even a sliver of hope. At the very least, Lee Sungmin had no desire to live in a world connected to the Great Demon World.

The darkness swirling around Kim Jonghyun sharpened into awls.

"Pierce."

The command came with an immense surge of magic. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he moved the hand gripping his spear. Homing Chain Lightning Flash and Bloody Pursuit collided with the dark projectiles. He released his right hand from the spear and thrust it forward, unleashing the fourth form of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. Blood Remnant Calamity Flame shot out as a palm strike, and flames of enhanced qi swept over Kim Jonghyun in a wide arc.

[He's getting stronger and stronger,] the Void Monarch warned. The Kim Jonghyun of now was incomparable to the one he first fought. Growing this much in such a short time was impossible, no matter how he considered it from a martial arts theory perspective.

*I know. It must be related to that magic he used earlier.*

[You're not an idiot, then. A magic like that must have a duration. Are you going to endure it?]

*Should I try to kill him instead of enduring?*

[Is that even possible?] the Void Monarch sneered.

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh from within the whirlwind of blades. The crackling sound of lightning sparked endlessly as he moved his body little by little, evading Kim Jonghyun's attacks.

*No. It's impossible.*

[Then you have to endure. Until his magic runs out.]

Lee Sungmin had reached the same conclusion, but the situation wasn't good for him either. Repeated regeneration and the overuse of Demonic Energy were shaking the very foundation of the seal Osla had engraved on him. He was fighting Kim Jonghyun immediately after Vollander. It was too much.

"Keuk!"

While continuing his rapid evasive maneuvers, he finally allowed an attack to land. He had hastily raised his Protective Qi Aura, but the blow struck his chest with enough force to send his body flying backward.

Kim Jonghyun lowered his outstretched hand and glanced down at the gasping Abel. He smirked.

"You've overused it."

He didn't know much about Gries, but the current Abel was not an opponent he needed to worry about. Frau was a bit of a concern, but he could deal with him later. There was still plenty of time before his magic ended. Kim Jonghyun accelerated, chasing after the airborne Lee Sungmin.

Lee Sungmin pushed himself up from the rubble of a collapsed building. He must have flown quite a distance. The breastplate forged from a Dragon's scales was horribly dented, though he knew it would repair itself over time. He spat out a mouthful of blood and walked out from the debris.

"L-Lee Sungmin…?"

He saw the Holy Knights and soldiers gathered around Teresa. For a moment, Lee Sungmin couldn't understand why they were here.

"What… what happened to the Death Knights?"

"They gave up the fight during the battle," Theos answered. "They fled on their Ghost Steeds." It seemed that once Vollander was annihilated, the legion that followed him had scattered.

"Wait, your wound…!" Teresa cried, rushing toward him.

Seeing the holy power extending from her hands, Lee Sungmin quickly backed away. "I'm fine." The light emanating from Teresa was poison to him. The very hand she extended to heal was toxic.

"Well, well." A voice drifted down from the sky. "To think they'd run away just because Vollander died."

His black robes fluttering, Kim Jonghyun descended among the Holy Knights.
