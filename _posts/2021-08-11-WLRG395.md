"Smile..."

Lee Sungmin knew what kind of expression he was making without needing a mirror. Tears streamed down his face without stopping, and his facial muscles twitched in a violent spasm.

"How... could I possibly do that..."

It was the same feeling he had when the Crazy Heavenly Demon died, and when the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman died. He had thought then that he never wanted to feel this way again. Even after so much time had passed, after all he had experienced, the memory of their deaths still felt terrible.

*Why couldn't I do anything?*

*Why was I so powerless?*

*Why, why am I the only one still alive like this?*

Amidst the despair, self-loathing, and regret, he had built his hatred.

"You... can't..." His voice trembled. He had no choice but to admit it, and it was only natural.

Lee Sungmin cherished his bond with the Void Monarch more than his bond with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, more than the one with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Ever since their meeting in the Sleeping Forest, the Void Monarch had been a constant presence connected to his consciousness. Everything Lee Sungmin saw and felt, the Void Monarch felt too. Even the worries Lee Sungmin could not confess to others, the Void Monarch would sense and offer advice.

He was a friend who understood everything about Lee Sungmin and never left his side. He was a Master who pointed out his shortcomings and nurtured his precarious mind. He was also a father who knew him better than anyone and empathized with him completely.

"No."

The Void Monarch shook his head.

"I have to go. I must. It's for your sake, and for mine. Begging me not to leave right now is meaningless. It's only a matter of sooner or later, and at most, it would only be delayed by a little."

"There must be... some other way."

"No, there isn't," the Void Monarch stated firmly, gesturing with his eyes toward the landscape of his consciousness. That world, once obscured by thick fog, was now quite visible.

Lee Sungmin's shoulders trembled violently. He understood. This place had originally been a pure white world of dense fog, with nothing in it, and as the Void Monarch disappeared, that world was fading with him.

"Don't cry," the Void Monarch said once more. "You know it can't be helped, don't you? It's... been too long. I should have disappeared ten years ago."

Lee Sungmin covered his face with both hands. In that brief moment, a torrent of thoughts rushed through him, but he understood what could not be changed. What if he moved the Void Monarch's soul to another body? It wouldn't work. He had asked if they could try it with Aine's empty body, but the Void Monarch had told him then that such a thing was impossible.

"There's no way for me to remain any longer, in any form," the Void Monarch said with a smirk, sensing Lee Sungmin's thoughts. "I didn't want to take over a little girl's body anyway... There's just no other way."

"...Right."

*Don't cry, smile.*

Lee Sungmin muttered the words to himself. He understood. He accepted that it couldn't be helped. If that was the case, then he had to smile. If this was their last moment together, he had to smile instead of crying, just as the Void Monarch wished. He pressed his hands to his face, rubbing away the tears that refused to stop falling. He tensed the spasming muscles in his cheeks and forced the trembling, downturned corners of his mouth upward.

"You look fucking ugly."

The Void Monarch chuckled, looking down at Lee Sungmin, but Lee Sungmin could feel the tremor in that laughter. Even as he pretended to be carefree, sneering and mocking as if nothing was wrong, the Void Monarch was shaken too.

Lee Sungmin staggered to his feet and tried to calm his emotions with a deep breath. It didn't work. The tears he had just wiped away began to flow again.

"Stop crying already."

"...How could I possibly do that..." Lee Sungmin replied in a weak voice.

The Void Monarch was disappearing. The uninvited guest who had occupied a corner of his consciousness for over a decade was vanishing. From now on, whenever he thought or acted, he wouldn't have to be conscious of the Void Monarch's presence and feel needlessly embarrassed. Whenever feelings of despair washed over him, he wouldn't be able to seek out the Void Monarch's presence for advice and comfort. He wouldn't have to bother trying to ignore his nonsensical remarks.

It was horrible.

Whenever he lost someone and became alone... the truth was, he had never been alone. At those times, the Void Monarch was always by his side.

However, not anymore. Now, Lee Sungmin would truly be alone.

He was afraid of that. It felt as if his mind was about to break. His resilience hadn't come from some innate strength of mind. It had come from the conviction that even in the most desperate situations, he would never be alone. It was because the Void Monarch was always there, speaking to him from the depths of his consciousness, telling him not to despair.

"From now on, you are alone," the Void Monarch said.

Lee Sungmin forced a smile.

"There might be someone by your side, but in the end, you yourself will be alone. Like this Elder... inside your head, there will no longer be a being who curses at you, jokes with you, and listens to you."

"I know." Lee Sungmin swallowed hard and wiped his tears once more. As he fully accepted everything, the tears strangely stopped flowing.

Seeing Lee Sungmin's face, the Void Monarch made an expression of relief. He looked down at his own half-vanished body.

"I have lived a long time."

He had been born an insignificant Yokai of the South. Most of his life had been a fight, and in those fights, the Void Monarch had always survived, won, and devoured his opponents. His power grew and grew until the Yokai named Void Monarch came to be called the nightmare of the South. Even the mighty Vampire Queen did not dare to face him, and Predator, a gathering of countless inhuman beings, did not invade his territory. Even the Dragons remaining in the world at that time did not fly over his forest.

"Living a long life, I experienced many things. I experienced various pleasures too. However... the decade or so I spent with you as just a soul was more enjoyable than when I lived in my own body." The Void Monarch spoke with sincerity. "If I had met you when I was alive, well... I probably wouldn't have gotten as close to you or cherished you as I do now. I was quite the ruffian back then. My personality only mellowed out like this because my body was gone and I was left as just a soul, understanding you so much while living as a parasite in your consciousness."

After saying that much, the Void Monarch chuckled. "If this Elder had met you back in the day, I would have punched a wimp like you to death in one blow."

"I know." The words were half-joking, half-serious. Lee Sungmin smiled back at him, his heart feeling hollow. Even in this moment, the Void Monarch's body was continuously disappearing, and the fog that had filled the world of consciousness was thinning.

"It was good meeting you," the Void Monarch said. "I'm glad the one I ended up with was a moronic bastard like you. I'm glad you weren't perfect. I'm glad you were a guy full of inferiority complexes."

It reminded him of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's words about liking a hard-working, ordinary talent.

"You were... my friend, my Master, and my father."

"And a guy like that threatens to dunk me in a cesspool?"

"That's a separate issue."

"Kahahahaha! You son of a bitch. Let's just promise one thing. Later, I mean... after the fight with Jeniela is over. You go take a dip in a cesspool too. So you can feel what this Elder felt."

"Alright. I promise."

The Void Monarch looked surprised, as if he hadn't expected Lee Sungmin to answer without hesitation. He blinked his eyes and then burst out laughing.

"Alright, you promised. Later, when you... come to where this Elder is, tell me all about how it felt. It'll be a good story to have with a drink."

Saying so, the Void Monarch raised his head. Over Lee Sungmin's shoulder, he saw a long, single path stretching out. He looked straight at it and spoke.

"Don't despair." The Void Monarch took a step.

"Don't break either." His steps did not stop.

"And don't die. You promised this Elder. That you would one day surely come to the world where I am."

The Void Monarch walked past Lee Sungmin. Overwhelmed with emotion, Lee Sungmin pressed a hand to his chest. The fog that had filled the world of consciousness gathered in one place, enveloping the Void Monarch's body as he walked toward an unknown place. Standing behind him, Lee Sungmin saw the faint, single path he was heading toward.

"It was good meeting you," Lee Sungmin muttered to the Void Monarch's back in a trembling voice. At those words, the Void Monarch's shoulders shook with laughter.

"Meeting you, who was my... friend, my Master, and my father."

Lee Sungmin unconsciously started to walk toward the Void Monarch's back. At that moment, the Void Monarch shook his head.

"Don't come."

Lee Sungmin's feet stopped.

"You can't come here yet. You have... a lot to do, don't you? You said you've never been happy. Right now you're suffering in this dog-eat-dog world, but someday... when all of that is over. It won't be too late to come after you've enjoyed yourself enough."

"Can I... really go?" *Will such a day ever come?*

At that question, the Void Monarch let out a loud laugh. "Of course you can. This Elder believes in you."

The path looked long. The Void Monarch's back, approaching that path, looked distant. Lee Sungmin raised his hand, reaching out toward him. He could see many things. Though he couldn't see the Void Monarch's current expression, he knew what it was. It was a smile full of anticipation. The Void Monarch was heading toward the path, and on that path walked the Spear King and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

Lee Sungmin felt his chest tremble and squeezed his eyes shut. He thought he had wiped his last tears, but they were flowing again.

"Farewell."

The Void Monarch spread his arms, which had suddenly appeared anew. Lee Sungmin felt the Void Monarch's presence disappearing from his mental world. At this very moment, he was heading not for this small world of consciousness, but for a wider, newer world.

"See you later."

Tears blurred his vision. Through his welling tears, Lee Sungmin saw the wavering figure of the Void Monarch. The gathered fog scattered, no longer obscuring the landscape of the consciousness as it vanished completely.

The now-uncovered Void Monarch stepped toward the path. His body shook violently, flickering as if he were there and not there, not there but there.

Like a phantom.

Just like his name, Void Monarch.

"Uhahahahaha!"

A great laugh shook the world of consciousness. A fierce wind rose, scattering the fog in all directions. The shape of the single path became clear. The Void Monarch, having set foot on the entrance to that path, no longer paid any mind to what was behind him. He truly believed that someday, Lee Sungmin would definitely come to this world. The kid would never die, nor would he despair or break. The Void Monarch believed that.

That was why he didn't look back. He didn't dwell on it. He held no lingering attachments. Why be sentimental about a guy he was bound to meet again someday?

"Hahahaha!"

After hundreds of years, his soul had found freedom. Even though his body had long since perished, such things were meaningless in this world. The pantheon these monsters reached was a place where life and death were meaningless. The Void Monarch felt a thrilling sense of liberation and put strength into the leg that had stepped onto the path's entrance. He let out a wild laugh toward the heavens and began to run down the winding, single path.

Faster than those who walked slowly, faster than those who crawled, faster than those who rested, faster than anyone.

The Void Monarch's emotions grew distant.

The Void Monarch's laughter grew distant.

It was a laugh without regrets. He felt no resentment at all. He knew that clinging on was ugly and selfish, that this couldn't be helped. Lee Sungmin also understood that while the Void Monarch felt it was a pity, he had also hoped for this day. He looked around the empty world.

The world, now devoid of fog, was filled with a chaos of jumbled memories. A profound sense of emptiness washed over him. He chased after the image of the Void Monarch, who had been visible in the distance but was now gone.

"...Right."

The Void Monarch had not perished. Lee Sungmin had clearly seen the world where the Void Monarch, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, and the Spear King had gone. Perhaps someday, he could also go to that world. Therefore, he shouldn't feel sad about the Void Monarch's departure. He had gone to that world of his own will, neither dead nor annihilated. His final laugh was filled with liberation and joy, as if to say, *I'm okay.*

*So, I'm okay too.*

*I have to be okay.*

Lee Sungmin slowly turned around to keep his promise to the Void Monarch.
