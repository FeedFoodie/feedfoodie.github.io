In his past life, Lee Sungmin had been a member of the Corona Mercenary Group under the command of Zeon. Back then, the SS-Rank Mercenary had seemed the strongest and greatest man in the world, a figure as mighty as a king. Of course, he knew now that Zeon was no such thing, but the memory of that awe remained.

To all mercenaries, the Mercenary King was a god, and that god was Dmitri. Even in his past life, the unranked mercenary's fame had been legendary. Never had Lee Sungmin imagined that the Mercenary King himself would be one of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens.

"The Spear King is a wanderer," the Fist Honored One began. "He never settles in one place and is constantly on the move. He also receives special treatment among the Six Honored Ones, so I don't know his whereabouts. As for the Moon Queen…" He hesitated for a moment before continuing. "She lives on Yuzkia Mountain, where she built a mansion called the Moon Palace."

"I heard the Martial God stands at the pinnacle of Heaven Beyond Heaven," Lee Sungmin said. "How would you, or the Sword Saint, compare to him?"

"It's not even worth discussing," the Fist Honored One said, his expression bitter. "There's no comparison. None of the Six Honored Ones can measure up to the Martial God."

"…What about the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman?"

"…The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is one of the few rivals the Martial God acknowledges. I'm not sure about now, though. The Martial God entered seclusion ten years ago, and I don't even know where he's training."

"Why did Heaven Beyond Heaven kidnap Wiji Hoyeon?"

"…For the big picture."

"Then tell me what this 'big picture' is."

The Fist Honored One fell silent for a moment. He let out a long sigh before he spoke again.

"This world… is vast. Do you know about the Predator?"

"I do."

"The Predator are monsters that were once human. They have long been enemies of Heaven Beyond Heaven, but even the Martial God couldn't do anything about them. One of our goals is to completely exterminate the Predator and all other 'inhuman' who prey on humans in this world."

[What nonsense.]

The Void Monarch grumbled from within him.

[You can't just eliminate the inhuman by killing them. If you kill all the beasts, they can't breed more, but the inhuman are different. When a human ceases to be human, they become a monster, and that is an inhuman. As long as humans exist, exterminating the inhuman is impossible.]

Lee Sungmin repeated the Void Monarch's words exactly, and the Fist Honored One nodded in agreement.

"That's right, but what if… we could extinguish the very 'law' that turns humans into monsters when they cease to be human? What if a person could transcend humanity and simply remain… human?"

[Nonsense.]

"Lich and Death Knights are prime examples of the inhuman," the man continued. "They become what they are because of Dark Magic and the power of the Demon Lord from the Other Dimension. If we can prevent the Demon Lord from interfering with this world, then creatures like them won't be created. If we can erase that law itself, it means no more inhuman will be made."

"And how do you plan to extinguish that law?"

"By interfering with it. That's what the Martial God said. The reason most in Heaven Beyond Heaven practice martial arts is because it is the inherent power of humanity, different from magic or Dark Magic. We don't know how to interfere with the law. Only the Martial God knows… and he said that to do it, the Little Heavenly Demon is needed."

"And you don't know why she's needed?"

"I don't know that much."

"Is that the only goal of Heaven Beyond Heaven?" Lee Sungmin asked again.

After another brief silence, the Fist Honored One spoke. "If the Demon Lord can no longer interfere with this world, the inhuman will disappear. This world will be left with only humans and mutants. After that… Heaven Beyond Heaven will rule this world. We will create a new murim."

"Haha!"

Lee Sungmin burst into disbelieving laughter at the Fist Honored One's words. "Create a murim? What nonsense. That doesn't even sound possible."

"…However, everyone in Heaven Beyond Heaven is working towards that goal. We meet with martial artists who have the potential for the Transcendent Realm, and if they can't be recruited, we kill them to prevent them from becoming enemies."

"How large is Heaven Beyond Heaven's influence? Surely it's not just the Six Honored Ones and the Martial God."

"Heaven Beyond Heaven… has existed for a very long time. During that time, it has extended its reach far into this world. Even the Blade Supreme, the man called the Mercenary King, holds immense influence over mercenaries. The current Chairman of the Murim Alliance, the Black Dragon Knight, also belongs to Heaven Beyond Heaven."

Lee Sungmin's expression turned to ice at those words. *Beware the Black Dragon Knight.* The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's warning echoed in his mind. The Murim Alliance had framed him for the murders of Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin, and the Black Dragon Knight's influence must have been at play. The death of the Iron Armored Divine Spear at his hands must have also been an order from the Alliance.

*The Black Dragon Knight is with Heaven Beyond Heaven. In that case, I have to assume the entire orthodox murim is moving according to their will.*

The influence of Heaven Beyond Heaven was far stronger than he had thought.

"I don't know the full extent of our power," the Fist Honored One admitted. "Only the Martial God knows everything."

"You're one of the Six Honored Ones, yet you know nothing."

It was not a lie, however. The Fist Honored One, having sworn on his Mana, had not died. Through their conversation, Lee Sungmin had learned a great deal. He now understood the ridiculous goal of Heaven Beyond Heaven and that, for some unknown reason, Wiji Hoyeon was necessary to achieve it.

As long as they insisted on this goal, they would continue to target her. Furthermore, he now knew that the Murim Alliance, which was antagonizing him and painting him as a villain, was also under their influence.

Now that he had killed the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One, Heaven Beyond Heaven would surely make him an enemy. They had pressured him with rumors before, but those rumors had already ripened. Their intervention would likely become much stronger and more direct.

"I have one last thing to ask," Lee Sungmin said as he rose to his feet. "The gauntlet your daughter had. I heard it was made from the scales of a Dragon. Where on earth did you get it?"

"…I didn't expect you to be curious about that. That gauntlet was a proof of friendship I received long ago from the Dwarves. Their chieftain at the time made it himself and gave it to me as a gift."

"Is that chieftain still alive?"

"No. That was over a thousand years ago. Dwarves live long, but the chieftain from that time has long since passed away."

Lee Sungmin clicked his tongue softly. It was not a complete dead end, though. Celgerus had also said that a Dwarf chieftain might be able to handle Dragon scales. To process the scales and bones he had in abundance, he would have to visit a Dwarf city someday.

"I've heard everything I needed to hear."

Gripping his spear, Lee Sungmin approached the Fist Honored One. The man looked up at him with the faint hope that he might be spared for his cooperation, but Lee Sungmin's face was a cold mask.

"Curse me if you want, but I won't let you live," he stated. "As long as you belong to Heaven Beyond Heaven, you're my enemy, and besides… I personally want to kill you for what you did to Wiji Hoyeon."

"…I see," the Fist Honored One muttered in a small voice.

"Do you have any last words?"

"If I do… will you remember them?"

"No."

"Then there's no point."

The Fist Honored One quietly closed his eyes. Lee Sungmin thrust his spear forward in a light, simple motion. Weakened by the curse, the man's body offered no resistance.

"......"

A hole opened in his chest as the spearhead cleanly pierced his heart. Lee Sungmin watched impassively as the Fist Honored One's body collapsed, spilling blood onto the ground.

*Still…*

In his fading mental world, the Fist Honored One thought of his daughter, who would be escaping the forest by now.

He had prevented the death of his bloodline.

He had also prevented the death of his people, and while answering Lee Sungmin's questions, he had succeeded in hiding the existence of the 'Spirit Medium'.

*That's enough…*

Lee Sungmin confirmed the man's death before turning to the task he had postponed. He searched the Sword Saint's corpse, retrieving his Subspace Pocket and his sword, a masterpiece that was clearly extraordinary at a single glance. He then moved to the Fist Honored One, but unfortunately, the man had no Subspace Pocket.

*Can't be helped.*

Lee Sungmin checked the contents of the Sword Saint's pocket, finding various famous swords, money, and Elixirs. He looked for any miracle herbs, but there were none.

After taking what he needed, he stood up.

[Where do you plan to go now?]

*I'm going to where the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman is.*

His business in the South was finished. The three of them had come here together. Lee Sungmin turned away with that bitter thought, but a flicker of anticipation remained. If he went to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he could see Wiji Hoyeon again.

Wiji Hoyeon flinched, a slight tremor running through her body. Seated cross-legged as usual while absorbing the forest's natural energy, she suddenly realized a change had occurred within her. The feeling of helplessness that had been pressing down on her was vanishing. Her eyes snapped open.

"……"

*The curse is disappearing.*

She looked down at her own body with surprised eyes. It had been so long that the feeling was unfamiliar. Wiji Hoyeon staggered to her feet.

Though the curse was gone, moving was not yet easy, but she could feel vitality welling up from deep within her, a promise that she would soon be able to move freely.

"Looks like the Fist Honored One is dead," an indifferent voice rang out.

Wiji Hoyeon whipped her head around to face the sound. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was standing there in a crooked posture, looking at her. During her time in this forest, she had not seen him often. Though they shared the same woods, neither had sought the other out. For several months, she had focused only on recuperating, absorbing the forest's natural energy.

"The Phantom Spear. It seems that kid succeeded in killing him."

She owed him a debt. Wiji Hoyeon thought of Lee Sungmin as she slowly regulated her breathing. It was a debt she intended to repay someday, without fail. She tried to circulate her inner arts, just slightly. Her qi center, which had been as hard as stone under the curse, responded.

Her inner arts began to pour out. Wiji Hoyeon's body trembled as the great flow of power surged through blood vessels that had not been able to handle it for over a year, causing them to ache. The amount of her inner arts had exploded. She had been absorbing natural energy for over a year, and thanks to that, the power she possessed had multiplied several times over. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art she practiced accepted all kinds of energy without refinement, so her inner arts had already reached an unrivaled level before. Now, they were even greater.

"…If I rest for three or four days, I should be fine," Wiji Hoyeon muttered.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who was pushing a cackling Fairy Realm away from his shoulder with a finger, glanced at her. Today, he wore a bear mask.

"So?"

"Then, I want to duel you," Wiji Hoyeon declared.

The Chairman did not answer. The Fairy Realm he had pushed away clung to his finger and bit the tip, but he did not let out his usual exaggerated scream.

"Oh?" he finally replied after a short silence. "I might kill you. Are you okay with that?"

"I don't care," Wiji Hoyeon said, glaring at him. "Because I might kill you, too."

She meant it. Wiji Hoyeon thought of Lee Sungmin. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had said he would take Lee Sungmin as his disciple if the boy came to this forest.

When she had first heard those words… *I don't like that.*

Yes. She did not like it at all.

And for that, she wanted to kill the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"Fine," he said, a smirk spreading beneath his mask. "Beating up an arrogant brat should be fun, too."
