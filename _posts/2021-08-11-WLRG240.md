The forest had once been nameless, but that was before Kim Jonghyun spent a month within its borders. Now it bore an undeservedly grand title, the Demon King's Forest, a name born from the moniker given to the man who haunted it, the Demon King of the North.

The terrible northern weather had stripped the forest of any resemblance to its former self. Dry, leafless trees clawed at the sky like long awls, their jagged branches sprouting like thorns. As they swayed in the fierce wind, they created a ghastly wail. Blowing snow had stuck to their limbs and frozen solid, turning the entire forest white, a landscape of ice trees.

And somewhere within that frozen expanse, Kim Jonghyun waited.

Thanks to Lloyd's protection, Scarlet was not under any restrictions, but she was no fool. She knew this freedom was only temporary, a fragile peace that would last only as long as the current crisis.

*It's only until the subjugation is over.*

After that, some form of interference would come. She did not trust the Church or the Murim Alliance, and she did not trust the Mages Guild much either. If the Church and the Murim Alliance decided to intervene in earnest, the Mages Guild would be powerless to act as her shield.

*I suppose I can't remain the Magic Tower Master. It's a shame... but it can't be helped.*

Scarlet swallowed a sigh. She didn't blame Lee Sungmin for how things had turned out, nor did she want to. Her only real resentment was that he had hit her a little too hard when he knocked her out. Other than that, her only problem was figuring out where she would go after leaving the Magic Tower.

*Well, he'll probably take care of it.*

He was the successor to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Scarlet knew little about the murim, but she understood that the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was a massive organization that, along with the Orthodox Murim Alliance, effectively divided the whole of Eria's martial world. Since Lee Sungmin was its successor, he would surely arrange a place for her to stay, even if she had to leave.

She didn't have any strong lingering attachment to the position of Crimson Archmage anyway. When she first left Mount Mushii, her goal had been to become the Magic Tower Master, but achieving it had only filled her with disappointment. She had thought that if she became the Master, she would be able to see all the high-level magic the Mages Guild possessed. That had not been the case. Even as the Magic Tower Master, she could not access every magic tome in their collection. The most treasured volumes were marked with viewing restrictions, preventing even her from browsing them freely. The reality was so different from her expectations that Scarlet felt deeply disappointed with the Mages Guild itself.

*I can conduct my personal research anywhere, not just in the Magic Tower.*

So, she had no regrets about leaving. No, she shouldn't be thinking about that now. Surviving was the most important thing.

"Please take the lead," Lloyd requested of Doberman. His voice was flat, making the request sound more like a command. Doberman knew it, but he didn't refuse. He simply turned, a grin revealing his white teeth as Shih Tzu and Husky fell in behind him.

The protagonist of this subjugation was not the Mad Dog Mercenaries. Their job was to buy time for the mages in the rear, or to die first, tripping any magic traps or flushing out familiars hidden in the forest. Doberman knew that well.

It wasn't a losing deal. The headcount of the Mad Dog Mercenaries would decrease significantly, but the truly skilled ones wouldn't die. The mediocre ones, B-Rank or lower, could be gathered and replaced from anywhere. They would die in droves, and it didn't matter. There were plenty of mercenaries who wanted to join his company. If he could solidify his relationship with the Mages Guild by expending such trash, it was a profitable trade.

Doberman's orders were relayed through Shih Tzu and Husky, and the Mad Dog Mercenaries began to move. The mercenaries at the very front were all men who lacked confidence in their skills, terrified by the gruesome rumors surrounding Kim Jonghyun, but they couldn't run. They knew that if they fled, their captain's sword would take their heads long before Kim Jonghyun could.

*I don't know how this subjugation will end,* Chwi Geol thought. *Either we kill Kim Jonghyun... or he escapes. Perhaps we'll be the ones defeated and forced to flee.*

No matter what, the Crimson Archmage had to be secured. That was a direct order from the Chairman. He had even said it was fine not to get cooperation from the Mages Guild. Chwi Geol let out a smirk. Would it really go as planned? The Phantom Spear was protecting the Crimson Archmage. Trying to secure her would mean clashing with him.

*I heard the Murim Alliance's Assassination Squad has departed, but can they alone handle the Phantom Spear?*

Honestly, Chwi Geol was skeptical, but it wasn't his place to worry. The Chairman had insisted he didn't need to. Chwi Geol couldn't understand for the life of him why he was told not to worry.

"Let's go in," Lloyd said, his face grim.

The preparations were complete. It was just past noon. Chwi Geol cast his stray thoughts aside. The Phantom Spear had yet to show himself. Perhaps they would encounter him inside the forest.

*Does he know about Miss Baek's whereabouts?*

Harboring that question, Chwi Geol dismounted his horse.

{sep}

As the subjugation force began to enter the forest, Lee Sungmin prepared to act. He had to follow them inside. He didn't care about the others, but he wanted to protect Lloyd and Scarlet. He had considered arriving early to meet Kim Jonghyun, but he felt there was no need.

"......"

The moment Lee Sungmin was about to move, a short groan echoed in his mind from the Void Monarch. He offered no other warning, but none was needed. Lee Sungmin felt it too, a sensation he had not experienced in a very long time. Recently, no one besides the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had exuded such an intimidating pressure.

Lee Sungmin immediately turned.

A sharp, piercing pressure targeted his entire body. The source was not yet visible, but he grabbed the spear on his back and took several steps back.

"Well, well."

A cackling voice drifted on the wind. A small dot glittered in the distance, then in a flash, it closed the vast expanse between them. The man who appeared was tall, his jet-black hair tied high and swept back. Two plain, ink-colored spears were strapped to his back. The moment he faced the man, three words etched themselves into Lee Sungmin's mind. Heaven Beyond Heaven.

"...the Spear King?"

"Kekeke!"

The Spear King cackled at Lee Sungmin's question. He had the appearance of a man in his early thirties, but his large eyes sparkled with a pure, childlike light. He didn't attack right away, instead patting the dust from his body after his long run.

"You've got a sharp eye, it seems."

"If you're carrying spears, it could only be you," Lee Sungmin replied.

The Spear King beamed at his response. Although he had come on orders from Heaven Beyond Heaven, he personally held a great deal of interest in Lee Sungmin. He was a member of the organization but cared little for its goals. He was only a part of it because the phenomenally strong Martial God was there, and because it made it easier to fight other masters of the Transcendent Realm.

"We may have met like this, but I'm glad to see you, and I mean that, so don't doubt it," the Spear King said, his voice full of laughter.

Lee Sungmin felt a slight sense of unease at his attitude. *If you meet the Spear King, run.* He recalled the Sword Saint's words before his death. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had also warned him several times that besides the Martial God, he had to be careful of the Spear King and the Moon Queen. He had said that no matter how much stronger Lee Sungmin grew, if he fought the Spear King, he would surely die.

"...Didn't you come here to kill me?"

"What?" The Spear King tilted his head.

"I killed the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One," Lee Sungmin clarified. "I'm asking if you came to kill me because of that."

"What does it have to do with me that those two moronic bastards died together?" the Spear King asked back, looking genuinely incredulous. "If you risk your life for martial arts and devote your entire life to them, isn't it natural to one day die fighting another martial artist? They died because the arts they honed were inferior to yours. That was a perfectly natural outcome. So why would I try to kill you for it?"

Lee Sungmin was momentarily at a loss for words. The Spear King cackled at his silence.

"Ah, of course, I did hear about you from the Martial God. He told me to meet you once, but he didn't tell me to kill you. He said to do as I please." As he spoke, the Spear King glanced at the spear Lee Sungmin was holding. "However. He did say to do as I please... and if you fight me, you will die. If your spear is weaker than mine, that's only natural."

The situation was not good. Lee Sungmin was acutely conscious of what was happening behind him. He never thought he would run into the Spear King here. To be precise, it wasn't a chance encounter. The Spear King had sought him out. In any case, while he was facing off against this man, the subjugation force was entering Kim Jonghyun's forest. Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched.

To be honest, he had no confidence he could escape unscathed from a fight with the Spear King. Even now, just facing him, his presence was like a sharp spear aimed at Lee Sungmin's entire body. He was strong, incomparable to the Fist Honored One or the Sword Saint. While not on the level of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the Spear King was a master of a higher realm than Lee Sungmin. If they fought, he would have to risk his life, and even then, there was no guarantee of victory. He would only know for sure by fighting, but he could feel the truth of it just by standing here.

"What are you doing?" the Spear King urged, drawing one of the two short spears from his back and holding it with both hands. "Are you confident you can handle my spear by just standing there like that?"

"I don't want to fight right now," Lee Sungmin answered quickly. Judging by his attitude, it didn't seem the Spear King held any real killing intent toward him.

"Why not?"

"Because I have to go into that forest."

"Then shouldn't you just defeat me and go?" the Spear King said with a frown. "I ran all the way here without rest just to have a match with you. I didn't even sleep."

"Then your stamina must be quite low."

"That's right. If you want to defeat me, now would be your only chance."

"No." Lee Sungmin slowly shook his head. Through their brief conversation, he was able to get a rough grasp of what kind of martial artist the Spear King was. "Although you're an enemy, as a fellow spearman walking the path of the spear, I believe your spear is worthy of respect."

"What..."

"Therefore, I don't wish to become a coward who fights you when you're not at your best and breaks your spear. I may be defeated, but I want to face you and your spear fair and square when you are in peak condition."

The Spear King's mouth fell half-open at Lee Sungmin's words. His shoulders trembled with some kind of emotion.

"Let's postpone the duel," Lee Sungmin continued. "I'm not in peak condition right now either. With so much on my mind, my spear path is bound to be disrupted."

"......"

The Spear King nodded as if he had realized something profound. "I see... You're absolutely right. Yes. To be honest, my condition isn't that great, and with things on your mind, you wouldn't be able to focus on the fight either..."

"Right."

"I... understand. Let's postpone the fight until next time."

[What a simple-minded fellow.]

The Void Monarch scoffed.
