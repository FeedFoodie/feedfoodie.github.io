Lee Sungmin swung his leg over the side of the Fairy Horse, dismounting as he reached his destination. He tilted his head back, his gaze tracing the rugged lines of the mountain before him.

Ten years, they said, was enough to change the course of rivers and the shape of mountains, yet Mount Wudang stood as it always had, unchanged. He expanded his senses, wary of the Sword Immortal's Flying Sword Technique, but the air remained still. He felt nothing of the sort.

[Are you planning to recruit the Sword Immortal?]

*The Sword Immortal can't leave Mount Wudang.*

Lee Sungmin replied with a pang of regret. The Sword Immortal would be a powerful ally, if only he would lend his strength.

The man was said to be bound to this mountain, unable to leave ever since suffering a qi deviation long ago. As the memory surfaced, the Void Monarch snorted.

[That's the part I don't quite understand.]

*What do you mean?*

[I can understand nearly dying from qi deviation, but what does that have to do with being unable to leave Mount Wudang?]

*Maybe it's just a fancy way of saying he can't go far.*

With that thought, Lee Sungmin started up the path leading to the mountain. The sun was already setting, bathing the sky in twilight hues that painted the mountain scenery a deep red. The sight stirred a memory of a promise made long ago to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

They had promised to come to Wudang together. He also recalled a letter from Baek Sogo, her words describing how the mountain would be dyed gold with blooming forsythias when winter passed and spring arrived.

However, that season had long since passed. Now, no golden forsythias greeted him on Mount Wudang.

*Someday.*

However, not today. Lee Sungmin headed toward the Sword Immortal's residence. He had not walked far before his senses detected a presence.

"Cheongmyeong."

Lee Sungmin called his name in a low voice. From behind a nearby tree, Cheongmyeong emerged, clad in a Daoist robe. A bitter smile touched his lips as he clasped his fist in salute.

"It's been a long time."

Lee Sungmin returned the salute. A flicker of embarrassment crossed Cheongmyeong's face, a clear sign he hadn't expected to be detected so easily.

Lee Sungmin gauged the man's level. Ten years ago, Cheongmyeong had been in the Transcendent Realm. Now...

*Less than I expected...*

To be honest, the level of his energy was disappointing, at least based on his senses alone.

He was about on par with the Black Dragon Knight or the Spear King from a decade ago. It couldn't be helped, of course. Those two had endured ten years of harsh, restrictive training on Mount Mushii. Even as the Sword Immortal's disciple, Cheongmyeong's decade on this mountain could not compare. Besides, the Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight had already reached a higher realm than Cheongmyeong back then. It was impossible for him to have caught up in just ten years.

"Have you come to see the Master?"

"......"

"Please come this way."

Cheongmyeong gestured for him to follow. As they walked in silence toward the Sword Immortal's hermitage, Cheongmyeong broke the quiet.

"The Thunder God, Little Demon Emperor, Divine Spear, Dark Heaven Demon Lord. Which title do you like the most?"

"Honestly? I don't like any of them. They all feel like I'm just polishing my own name."

His honest feelings were true. After he had crushed the Blood Alliance and killed the Blood Demon before so many eyes, the titles people called him by had multiplied.

Some called him the Thunder God, for the purple lightning and thunderclaps that accompanied his martial arts. Others called him the Little Demon Emperor, for being the Demon Emperor's disciple. There were those who called him the Divine Spear, for spearmanship they claimed had reached a divine level, and still others who called him the Dark Heaven Demon Lord, claiming he was the strongest among the demonic practitioners.

He disliked them all. It was embarrassing to call himself by any of those names. It felt ridiculous to claim the title of Thunder God when his Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was incomplete, and the same was true for Little Demon Emperor. The Spear King would surely laugh if he called himself the Divine Spear with skills inferior to his own, and Dark Heaven Demon Lord was no different.

"The Master is inside."

They arrived at the Sword Immortal's hermitage. Lee Sungmin stared blankly at the building for a moment before asking, "Are the Sword Dragon and Jihak still on this mountain?"

"The two of them descended the mountain a while ago. Jihak went to Shaolin, and the Sword Dragon returned to the Namgung Clan."

A wave of disappointment washed over Lee Sungmin. He had come to Mount Wudang hoping to recruit Jihak, Namgung Heewon, and if possible, Cheongmyeong. With their help, he would have gathered every Transcendent Realm master he knew. His plan was to then find Jeniela or the Martial God for a decisive talk before it was too late, but now, with the Sword Dragon and Jihak gone, was Cheongmyeong his only hope?

It was with this very question on his mind that Lee Sungmin entered the hermitage.

The voice greeted him the moment he stepped inside alone. "What do you think of Cheongmyeong?"

Lee Sungmin glanced back at the closed door. Cheongmyeong was standing just outside.

The Sword Immortal chuckled. "Worried the boy will hear?"

"A little."

"Don't worry, I've already blocked out the sound. More importantly... heh heh. The way you're looking at that door tells me you don't have anything good to say."

"For his age, it's an excellent achievement. Plenty of men can't reach the Supreme Peak at his age, and countless more die old without ever touching the Transcendent Realm."

"But for a disciple of the Sword Immortal, isn't he lacking?"

"Come here," the Sword Immortal beckoned.

Lee Sungmin slowly approached. A different smell hung in the air this time, one he hadn't noticed on his last visit. It was a familiar scent.

[We shouldn't have come.]

The Void Monarch muttered.

The Sword Immortal was lying on a bed, his shadow flickering in the wavering candlelight. As Lee Sungmin approached, the old man pushed himself up.

"You've aged a great deal since I last saw you."

"I should've grown old and died long ago. I've lived long enough as it is."

"What happened to you?"

"Nothing happened. Nothing at all... and that's why it's come to this. This old body of mine was already dying from that qi deviation. I've only been sustaining my life by leeching off the mountain's energy."

That was why he could not leave Mount Wudang. The Sword Immortal's wrinkled face crumpled into a smile.

"Did you come just to say hello after all this time?"

Lee Sungmin slowly shook his head. At this, the Sword Immortal let out another laugh.

"I pride myself on raising a disciple I wouldn't be ashamed of anywhere, but... he's lacking. Still so lacking. I've poured ten years of effort into him, yet he's only reached that level."

"Aren't you being too greedy?"

"I have no choice but to be. I wanted to raise him to be strong enough to at least delay the apocalypse, even if he couldn't stop it, but... hehe. It seems this old man wasn't a very good Master after all."

"How much time do you have left?"

"Perhaps I'll die today," the Sword Immortal said, nodding his head.

Lee Sungmin looked at him with a bitter expression.

"Are you pitying this old man?"

"I don't think we're close enough for me to pity you."

"Heh heh!"

At Lee Sungmin's words, the Sword Immortal laughed once more.

"It seems I've really gotten on your bad side."

"Because you tried to kill me."

"This old man couldn't be sure whether you were The End or not. It's still the same now."

"Ten years ago, I was The End, but not anymore."

"That is not a matter for this old man to fathom. However... I do know this much. The me of today cannot kill you."

"Because you've gotten weaker?"

"I haven't weakened that much. I'm still alive, somehow. Even if I can't grasp this entire mountain with my senses like before, I am still the Sword Immortal," the old man muttered, his gaze fixed on Lee Sungmin's golden eyes.

"Ilcheon raised his disciple well. Cheongmyeong... if that boy had become as strong as you, I could have given up my life without any regrets..."

The Sword Immortal's words were cut short.

KWAJIJIK!

The hermitage door exploded inward with a dull thud. Something shot through the splintered wood and rolled across the floor.

Startled, Lee Sungmin spun around. His eyes widened at the object tumbling to a stop on the floorboards. He couldn't believe it was real.

It was Cheongmyeong's head. It rolled to a stop, the dazed expression on his brutally torn-off face suggesting he never even knew what had happened.

He couldn't have even screamed. Was it possible? For Cheongmyeong, a master of the Transcendent Realm, to die like this? To be killed without a chance to resist, without even knowing what was happening to his own body?

"...Huh."

The sound escaped his gaping mouth. The Sword Immortal said nothing. He couldn't understand, but beyond Cheongmyeong's impossible death, another question screamed in his mind. Why hadn't he or the Sword Immortal sensed the approach of the monster that killed him?

Then, a voice. "Oh?"

The slender tone made Lee Sungmin's shoulders flinch.

*No way.*

He gripped the spear he had set down. Behind him, the Sword Immortal pushed himself to his feet.

Jeniela stepped over the broken threshold, her eyes tracking their movements. She casually dropped the rest of Cheongmyeong's body from one hand, her own eyes widening in mock surprise. Behind her stood Gemini, who tilted her head and looked at Lee Sungmin with the same feigned surprise as her mistress.

"Why are you here?" Jeniela asked.

That was what Lee Sungmin wanted to ask. A chill shot down his spine. His skin crawled. Jeniela's terrifying presence, completely absent a moment ago, now pressed down on him with suffocating weight.

That made it even harder to understand. He knew very well how sinister her presence was. So why hadn't he noticed it?

"...Cheongmyeong..." the Sword Immortal muttered.

"Ahahat," Jeniela laughed. "He wasn't impressive enough to play with, so I killed him. If I'd known you were here, maybe I would've listened to his last words?"

"How... I didn't feel your presence...?"

"Oh my," Jeniela gave a slight smile at Lee Sungmin's mumble. She parted her smiling lips and stuck out her red tongue. Licking the blood that stained her fingers, she said, "Oh, you don't know me very well, do you? I happen to be an excellent mage. Erasing my presence isn't a difficult task, if I feel like it."

This was the worst luck. He had never expected Jeniela to invade this place while he was talking with the Sword Immortal.

[That's not all.]

The Void Monarch warned. He hadn't expected Jeniela to attack like this either, and his voice was stiff.

[It's night.]

Lee Sungmin's gaze flickered past Jeniela. The sky beyond the shattered door was pitch black. This was different. He had never confronted her at night.

[Fortunately, it's not a full moon.]

A thin crescent moon hung in the sky. Under a full moon, a vampire was at its most powerful. It was night, but not a full moon. Lee Sungmin swallowed hard, and Jeniela caught the tension on his face, letting out a small laugh.

"Are you going to run away this time, too?"

"Why... did you come here?"

"I don't think I have any reason to explain that to you, but don't worry. My business today isn't with you."

Jeniela's eyes turned to the Sword Immortal.

The Sword Immortal remained silent, his gaze fixed on Cheongmyeong's head lying on the floor. His lips were pressed into a firm, thin line. A moment ago, the thought of his disciple dying like this was unimaginable, yet here he was, dead. A fact that would never change.

"...Haa..."

The Sword Immortal's tightly clenched mouth parted. A long sigh escaped him as he lifted his head.

"...It is fortunate," he rasped, "that this old body can still move."

Slowly, the Sword Immortal raised his hand.
