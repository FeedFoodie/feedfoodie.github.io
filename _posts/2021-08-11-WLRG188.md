He had vomited blood before, but this was different. The torrent that rose from his throat was incomparably foul, a nauseating tide that tasted of decay. It was then that the Crazy Heavenly Demon knew death was not far off. He had gained enough power to overwhelm Chuseong by burning his own life qi, yet it was a borrowed strength, not truly his own.

"...What is this?"

Chuseong, who had been helplessly battered by the fierce assault, slowly lowered the arms he had raised to shield his face. Before him, the Crazy Heavenly Demon stood in an awkward, unsteady posture, gasping for breath as he coughed up blood. For a moment, Chuseong could not comprehend the situation. If the attack had continued, he would have met his end.

"What is this...?" Chuseong asked again.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon could only glare back, his eyes bloodshot and his lips sealed against the scream tearing at his throat. A pain like his insides were being ripped apart made his lips tremble. To any observer, it was clear he was not a healthy man.

*Could it be...?*

Chuseong had felt something was strange all along. A sudden, explosive increase in power. A nobody had gained the strength to overwhelm him. It was hard to believe he had simply been hiding his true power. He must have used some forbidden martial art to draw out his strength, and now the price had come due.

A hideous twist contorted Chuseong's face.

"Hahaha!"

He burst into loud laughter and rushed toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

Seeing Chuseong charge, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was barely standing on trembling legs, hurriedly drew upon his inner arts. His body was difficult to move through the agony, but his inner arts still flowed as he willed. A swelling Protective Qi Aura collided with Chuseong's fist. Hot flames engulfed the aura, shoving the Crazy Heavenly Demon's body back.

"Not bad for a mere human!" Chuseong shouted, his expression joyful despite having been on the verge of death himself just moments before.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon barely managed to put strength into his legs, but his body still refused to move properly. It felt as if his nerves had been completely burned away. *Just a little more.* If he had just a little more time, he could have killed Chuseong. He could have avenged Dersia and the villagers.

"Hahaha!"

With another great laugh, Chuseong extended a hand. Crimson flames blazed hotter than ever before, and a palm strike wreathed in scorching fire shot toward the Crazy Heavenly Demon's face. He could only stare with wide eyes as the attack approached.

Then, as if someone had stretched time itself, he perceived Rubia leaping in front of him in slow motion. He had never seen such an expression on her face. The Rubia he knew was mostly playful or annoyed, pouting, bored, or frightened. Those expressions were the majority.

However, this was different. This was the face of someone who had made a decision.

Their eyes met.

Rubia opened her mouth to say something, but before her voice could form words, the flames Chuseong had unleashed engulfed her.

Or so it seemed.

The moment the flames touched her, a brilliant light suffused Rubia's body. A dazzling radiance poured out from within her, pushing back the very flames that surged forward to burn her.

Rubia was an artificial Elemental created by Envirus. He had made her with the help of the Spirit Queen before entrusting his identity as Cain to Erebrisa. Though created with the Queen's help, Rubia was an incomparably weak being compared to the other high-ranking Elementals Envirus summoned. She could use various auxiliary magic, but none of it was at an impressive level. From the beginning, Rubia was not an Elemental made for combat. The Queen had created her for one purpose.

To die in Envirus's place.

*I'm sorry, Master.*

Rubia closed her eyes tightly, conveying her thoughts to Envirus, wherever he might be. The power the Spirit Queen had planted within her body flowed out, becoming a white wall that blocked Chuseong's flames. She stopped in mid-air, her arms spread wide as she stood before the Crazy Heavenly Demon. He could only stare blankly as the fire scattered behind her back.

"What in the world..."

"You know," Rubia began, "I enjoyed traveling with you, mister, and with Lee Sungmin-nim. At first, I was just following my master's orders... I thought I'd gotten caught up in something troublesome, but after more than a year, it became fun. I got to have adventures with you and Lee Sungmin-nim, things I could never do with my master."

"What are you doing...?"

"Can't you tell?" Rubia said with a smile. "I'm blocking it."

PANG!

The wall of light crumbled.

Rubia's body fell to the ground. The Crazy Heavenly Demon stared blankly at her collapsed form as she gasped for breath.

Chuseong was slightly flustered that a beastfolk he had paid no attention to had blocked his attack, but seeing her fall so weakly, he let out a laugh as if it were nothing.

"All you did was buy him a moment of life!"

"...Mister... run..." Rubia said, her voice faint.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon stood there, looking down at her. Run. It was an impossible request. Having drawn on his life qi, he could not save himself even if he fled.

"You did something foolish..." he muttered in a hollow voice. The one who should have run was not him, but her.

Chuseong was approaching, raising a hand wreathed in flames. His plan was to burn the beastfolk to death first, then finish off the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

Death was near, but he was not dead yet.

As Chuseong's hand reached for Rubia, the flames in his palm licking out toward her, the Crazy Heavenly Demon pleaded desperately with his tingling limbs. *Move.*

*Please.*

He kept pleading with the body that refused to obey. *It isn't too late.*

His fingertips twitched, then trembled. That tremor spread throughout his entire body. His creaking frame moved on willpower alone, and the pain that had been proof of his approaching death vanished.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon roared and charged at Chuseong. Startled by the renewed assault from a man he thought was spent, Chuseong shot out a blast of fire. The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not stop. He leaped right into the inferno.

The hot fire burned his body, but his desire to kill Chuseong was greater than the excruciating pain. This was no longer just about revenge. If he died here, Chuseong would kill Rubia too, and he would not allow that.

"You persistent bastard!" Chuseong roared at the flame-wreathed figure.

Ignoring the shout, the Crazy Heavenly Demon swung his fist.

"......"

Chuseong's head snapped to the side from the blow. Even so, he swung his own hand and grabbed the Crazy Heavenly Demon's shoulder. It felt as if his left arm was being torn off, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not stop. Instead, his outstretched right hand seized Chuseong's throat.

"Keuk!"

Chuseong's breath caught. He struggled against the pressure on his neck, trying to shake the demon off. Even as flames consumed his entire body, the Crazy Heavenly Demon did not loosen his grip. To Chuseong, the man with crimson eyes flashing within the fire looked more like a malevolent demon than any Yokai.

"This... bastard...!"

CRUNCH!

Chuseong's hand crushed the Crazy Heavenly Demon's shoulder. Ignoring his ruined left arm, the Crazy Heavenly Demon raised his knee and kicked Chuseong in the stomach.

THUD!

With a loud impact, Chuseong's body bent over as blood sprayed from his mouth.

"St... op...!"

"No," the Crazy Heavenly Demon muttered from within the flames. "When the villagers told you to stop, did you?"

Those were the last words Chuseong ever heard.

"......"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's hand completely broke Chuseong's neck bone. The light vanished from Chuseong's eyes, but the phenomenal life force of a great yokai meant that even a broken neck did not bring death. The Crazy Heavenly Demon let go of the broken neck, stiffened his fingertips, and plunged them into Chuseong's chest.

SQUELCH!

Chuseong's steel-like skin was pierced. The Crazy Heavenly Demon grabbed the still-beating heart and squeezed, crushing it completely.

WHOOSH!

The flames clinging to his body scattered. Chuseong opened his mouth wide and let out a long, drawn-out scream as his trembling body began to turn to ash and scatter into the wind. The Crazy Heavenly Demon staggered and collapsed on the spot.

The flames were gone, but his body was so horribly burned that death would have been a mercy. This was the end. He had avenged Dersia and the villagers. He had protected Rubia.

With weary eyes, he looked back at her. Lying on the ground, Rubia weakly lifted her head.

"Mister..."

"It's done."

He tried to smile, but his face, completely burned by the flames, could not form the expression he intended. He was not without regrets. To be honest, he had hoped for something more. He had hoped that by fighting Chuseong while using his life qi, he might gain an epiphany in that final moment, a path to the Transcendent Realm.

However, a hope was just a hope. In the end, the epiphany did not come.

*Was I unworthy?* he thought, and chuckled to himself.

Lee Sungmin's face came to mind. It would have been nice to say goodbye. Then again, there was no need. If he had gained some final enlightenment, he would have passed it on, but he had nothing of the sort.

"...Mister?" Rubia called out in a weak voice. He was slowly closing his eyes. His life qi was fading. She did not know what was happening to his body, but she knew he was dying.

"Ah, mister... Mister?!" Rubia screamed his name as she tried to push herself up.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon didn't answer. She tried to hurry toward him, but her own end had also come.

"...Ah...?!"

With a bewildered sound, Rubia's body began to dissolve into particles of light. As Envirus moved to the Elemental Realm, she, who was connected to him, was also being summoned there. She screamed as she looked down at her dissipating hands and feet.

"Please, please, Master! Just a moment...!"

Rubia screamed, but the summoning did not stop. A moment later, her body had completely vanished.

With his vision nearly gone, the Crazy Heavenly Demon watched her disappear. *Where did she go?* Even that question did not last long.

"Good grief."

He heard a voice in his fading mental world, one he had heard before. He barely managed to open his eyes and look toward the sound.

Frau stood there, an annoyed expression on his face as he approached. The Crazy Heavenly Demon opened his mouth to speak, but Frau shook his head.

"Don't say anything useless. As if burning away your life force wasn't enough, you took Chuseong's flames with your whole body... It's a miracle you're not dead. No, you'll be dead soon anyway."

Frau grumbled and lightly shook a hand from beneath his wide sleeve. Dozens of talismans appeared in his palm. He threw them as if scattering seeds, and they moved in a uniform pattern, circling the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"Yana told me. If anything happened in this city while I was away, I should help as much as I could."

He had deliberately avoided the incident at the Central Plaza. His loyalty to Yana was important, but he had not thought it was worth such a risk. This, however, was something he could handle, and it fit Yana's instruction to help 'as much as I could'. Still, he couldn't step in during the fight. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was battling Chuseong with his life on the line, and it just wasn't an atmosphere for intervention.

Now, however, it was fine. The fight was over. Chuseong was dead, and soon, the Crazy Heavenly Demon would be too.

"Since you took care of the troublesome work for me," Frau muttered and formed a hand seal. "I'll at least give you time to say your last goodbyes."

Even for Frau, the great spellcaster, reviving a human who was dying after burning away all their life force was impossible. No healer could do it, but he could delay it. A transparent barrier enveloped the Crazy Heavenly Demon's body.

"You should at least get to say farewell before you go, shouldn't you?" Frau muttered with a bitter smile.
