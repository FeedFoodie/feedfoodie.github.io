Three days passed before Lee Sungmin returned to the inn near the North Gate, the designated meeting place for his reunion with the Crazy Heavenly Demon. He had no intention of staying long, so he entered the first-floor restaurant and found a seat. As he settled in, Rubia slipped quietly from his arms, the sphere of light nestled there transforming into her familiar beastfolk form.

"Isn't he coming?" she asked.

"He'll come."

Lee Sungmin was certain of it. The Crazy Heavenly Demon could not completely defy his words, not with the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy engraved so deeply within his body. Since he had promised to come, he had no choice but to show up.

[He has no choice. If that bastard tries to run, my Demonic Energy inside his body will go berserk,] the Void Monarch confirmed with a confident voice. Lee Sungmin felt a vague sense of trust in that assurance.

For the past three days, he had stayed at an inn in the Central District. While he could have gone to Jeniela's mansion or even Kim Jonghyun's house, he had avoided both. To Lee Sungmin, they were people whose inner thoughts he could neither fathom nor control. He was particularly bothered by Kim Jonghyun's intentions and had no desire to become needlessly entangled, so he had not approached him at all.

He had spent those three days honing the insights gained from his fight with the Crazy Heavenly Demon. In truth, they were not so much new insights as they were a deeper understanding of things he already knew. He had finally managed to internalize concepts he had understood but could not execute. Though he was still a long way from the state of non-action he had reached in the Mental World, his slowly developing body had grown explosively during the life-or-death battle.

*Is this evolution?*

Lee Sungmin pressed a hand to his left chest, lost in thought. The heart's ability, as Preskan had explained it, was evolution through predation. He had no desire whatsoever to engage in such an act, yet the evolution was making his body grow on its own, independent of his will. The difference now was that a clear goal existed at the end of this evolutionary path.

The current goal Lee Sungmin was aiming for was none other than himself.

Rubia's ears twitched.

With a clatter, the restaurant door swung open. Lee Sungmin turned his head toward the entrance just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon strode inside.

"...Who is that?" Lee Sungmin asked, his eyes drawn to the man following behind him.

The man wore oversized clothes that made it impossible to discern his build, but his left sleeve flapped wildly in the breeze, revealing that he was one-armed.

"...Ahem."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon let out a dry cough as he approached, hesitating as if unsure how to explain this sudden meeting. Lee Sungmin's gaze bypassed him entirely, fixing instead on Baek Museon, who stood blankly in his shadow. The missing arm, combined with the unusual aura he exuded, told Lee Sungmin exactly who this man was. He was the famous Baek Museon.

"...How absurd."

It was Baek Museon who spoke first. His face twisted as he looked at Lee Sungmin, then he turned back to the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"Did you lie to me?"

"What lie?"

"I was interested in the young master who nearly killed you, Elder," Baek Museon spat, his voice thick with irritation. "That brat. I can tell he's quite skilled, but he's not good enough to kill you. Were you deceiving me?"

"This one only spoke the truth," the Crazy Heavenly Demon said, his voice unwavering. "I was undoubtedly on the verge of death from that boy's spear." He vaguely knew that the Lee Sungmin who had gripped his neck was a different being from the one he usually faced. Even setting that aside, it was an undeniable fact that Lee Sungmin's spear had threatened his life. Startled by the threat, he had been forced to unleash the ultimate technique of the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art. If he had not, the spear would have killed him.

"A calamity comparable to the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon," Baek Museon said emphatically, quoting the elder. "That's what you told me."

"You've really been singing my praises behind my back," Lee Sungmin muttered in disbelief.

At this, the Crazy Heavenly Demon cleared his throat, and Baek Museon's gaze snapped back to Lee Sungmin. Lee Sungmin tilted his head defiantly, meeting his stare.

"Are you the famous Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon?"

"I suppose I was famous once. Or maybe I'm still famous now, but for the wrong reasons."

Though Baek Museon seemed displeased, believing the Crazy Heavenly Demon had deceived him, he did not leave. He sat down across from Lee Sungmin without asking for permission and called over a restaurant employee.

"Alcohol."

While Baek Museon gave the curt order, the Crazy Heavenly Demon quickly sat beside him.

[What in the world are you doing?] Lee Sungmin demanded through a Thought Transmission.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon shot a furtive glance at Baek Museon before quickly explaining the situation. He recounted Baek Museon's deep despair following his defeat by Wiji Hoyeon, painting a picture of a martial artist utterly broken and living out his miserable days.

[What does that have to do with me?]

[The man has fallen into Despair because the world is so vast. So, if you could just...]

[What can I possibly do for him?]

[Truthfully, there's nothing you can do.]

Just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon replied, the alcohol Baek Museon had ordered arrived. The old man watched Baek Museon chug the drink straight from the bottle, not even bothering with a cup, and continued his transmission.

[How about becoming friends?]

"You've got to be kidding me."

Lee Sungmin could not help but spit the words out loud. Friends? What friends? Forcing them to sit together like this and telling them to become friends was the most ridiculous nonsense he had ever heard.

[Ah, by the way... I haven't been slapped by you for three days. Is that alright?] the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked, voicing a secret worry.

Lee Sungmin answered promptly. [You've been reciting the incantation every day, I assume? Then it's fine. Still, just in case, you can take all the missed slaps at once later.]

[Understood.]

While they exchanged these thoughts, Baek Museon finished the entire bottle. He exhaled a breath thick with liquor and looked at Lee Sungmin with cold, dead eyes.

"Come to think of it, I haven't heard your name yet."

"It's Lee Sungmin."

"Never heard of it."

Even though the moniker 'the Phantom Spear' should have been quite famous, Baek Museon claimed ignorance. It was clear that after his defeat by Wiji Hoyeon, he had completely lost interest in the world's rumors.

"They say you nearly killed the Crazy Heavenly Demon Elder. Is that true?"

"If the Elder said so, then I suppose it is."

"That's hilarious," Baek Museon burst out laughing.

Lee Sungmin felt his own irritation rising at the man's cynical attitude. He clenched and unclenched his fist. "What's so funny?"

"I'm not saying you're weak. Your skills are outstanding, I'll admit that, but I just can't imagine you being strong enough to kill the Crazy Heavenly Demon, Byeong Wonpae."

"So?"

"You were lucky. Or the Elder was careless," Baek Museon sneered.

Lee Sungmin stared at his face for a moment before opening his mouth. "Then were you careless when the Little Heavenly Demon cut off your arm?"

That was Baek Museon's sore spot. The moment the name 'the Little Heavenly Demon' was mentioned, his face turned to ice. Even the Crazy Heavenly Demon's cheek twitched. Lee Sungmin's expression, however, remained calm. So what if he had touched a nerve? He found Baek Museon, who had been picking a fight from the start, just as infuriating.

"...Do you want to die?"

"No. I was just genuinely curious. It's up to you whether you believe me or not, but I met the Little Heavenly Demon about half a year ago."

"What?" Baek Museon's eyes widened. The Crazy Heavenly Demon was just as surprised.

Lee Sungmin glanced at the old man staring at him before continuing. "A while ago, I heard something. That there are three Heavenly Demons in Eria right now. The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. The Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon. The Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae. As it turns out, I've now met all three of them. The Little Heavenly Demon was without a doubt a monster, and the Crazy Heavenly Demon Elder was the same, but... you."

"Shut up," Baek Museon muttered in a low voice. He already knew what Lee Sungmin was going to say.

Enduring the Blood Heavenly Demon's murderous gaze, Lee Sungmin finished his thought. "Compared to the other two, you're the most lacking."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon did not stop them. In fact, he secretly thought this was for the best. Baek Museon's sickness of the heart was an inferiority complex born from facing an overwhelming being. Though he had never met Wiji Hoyeon himself, seeing Baek Museon's despair gave him a good idea of what kind of monster she was.

The ideal solution would be for Baek Museon to fight Wiji Hoyeon one more time, but since her whereabouts were unknown, that was impossible. The next best thing, then, was to make him clearly understand just how vast the world truly was. If he fought an evenly matched battle with a young master not much different in age from Wiji Hoyeon, perhaps he would come to a realization.

It was a crude and desperate measure, typical of the Crazy Heavenly Demon, but Baek Museon moved just as he had hoped. He shot up from his seat, glaring at Lee Sungmin as a crimson mist began to bubble up from beneath his feet. The Blood Mist Eerie Night Art, the very technique that had earned him his title, began to unfold.

"I asked if you want to die."

"Will you be alright with just one arm?" Lee Sungmin asked, looking up at him.

The question was a provocation, but it was also born of curiosity and ambition. A few years ago, Wiji Hoyeon had unilaterally cut off his left arm. The Wiji Hoyeon from several years ago, a version of her who was incomparably weaker than she was now, had done that to the Blood Heavenly Demon. The one-armed man before him might be weaker than he was back then, but even so, Lee Sungmin wanted to find out.

*How far have I come?*

If he could fight and win against the man who had fought Wiji Hoyeon, wouldn't he be able to confirm his own progress? Lee Sungmin felt his heart pound. These were things he could not have even dreamed of in his past life. Since returning to the past, he had encountered people and events that should have been beyond his reach. The man named Lee Sungmin, who had lived for thirteen years only to end up a C-Rank Mercenary, was now about to fight the Blood Heavenly Demon Baek Museon, and that was not all. He had already fought the Crazy Heavenly Demon and survived. Feeling his emotions surge, Lee Sungmin slowly rose to his feet.

"I can't take this, really...!" Rubia muttered with a deep sigh, her ears drooping.

The Blood Heavenly Demon, who had been glaring at Lee Sungmin with eyes dripping killing intent, snatched the alcohol bottle from the table and whipped around.

"Come outside."

He was clearly furious, yet the fact that he did not attack immediately suggested a certain rationality. As Baek Museon strode out of the restaurant, Lee Sungmin glanced at the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

"Get ready for your slaps."

"Ahem!" the old man feigned ignorance with a dry cough.

Lee Sungmin drew his spear and walked out of the restaurant. Standing in the middle of the street, the Blood Heavenly Demon was already enveloped in a crimson mist. The North district was mostly home to vagrants and thugs, but it was not completely empty.

"Isn't that... the Blood Heavenly Demon?" people began to murmur.

Baek Museon ignored them, his glare fixed on Lee Sungmin as he emerged.

"You will pay the price for your nonsense," he spat.

Lee Sungmin raised his spear and shook his head. "If you can make me."

"You arrogant brat...!" Baek Museon roared, his voice a harsh rasp.

He raised his right arm and the crimson mist of the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art swelled dramatically around him. At his command, the fog became a death march, surging toward Lee Sungmin. Unpanicked, Lee Sungmin's feet moved with calm precision. He activated the Traceless Step of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, vaulting over the rushing mist to close the distance between them.

Baek Museon scoffed and stomped his foot hard. The mist that had shot forward scattered and vanished, only to expand its range again, centered on him. Lee Sungmin aborted his spear thrust and twisted his upper body.

"......"

His swinging spear collided with the crimson mist. He had thought it would be intangible, but he was wrong. A solid resistance met his grip. Lee Sungmin roughly understood the nature of Baek Museon's martial art.

*The mist itself is enhanced qi.*

In that it was an enhanced qi art, it was similar to the Demon King of Light's Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, but its purpose seemed completely different. Standing in the center of the mist, Baek Museon sneered at him.

"Didn't I tell you? You're not that great."

"...I haven't even shown you anything yet," Lee Sungmin muttered, infusing his spear with inner qi.

He was feeling quite strange. Wiji Hoyeon's Doppelganger. Mad Wolf Ju Won. Vampire Queen Jeniela. The Crazy Heavenly Demon Byeong Wonpae. When he had faced those monsters one after another, he had felt the fear and tension of possibly dying, but now...

*This is comfortable.*

He did not feel like he was going to lose.
