To stand against her now would undoubtedly make Jeniela his enemy, though in truth, that was nothing new. Jeniela's sole desire was the advent of the Slaughter Predator, and even with the 'real' one annihilated by his own hand, her wish had not changed. In fact, after a year of silence, she had simply concluded that Lee Sungmin himself was the true Slaughter Predator, arriving at her own answer. From the moment she first yearned for that creature's appearance, she was fated to be his foe. The Slaughter Predator's arrival signified The End, and Lee Sungmin was trying to stop it.

He glanced down at Baek Sogo, whose head rested on his shoulder. Her eyes remained closed, but the simple rhythm of her even breathing was enough to ground him. A chilling thought surfaced. What if he had encountered Jeniela and her vampires before finding Baek Sogo? He would have been forced to fight in an impossibly frustrating situation. Not that his current circumstances were liberating, but at least now, Baek Sogo was by his side. What happened next was crucial. How could he possibly overcome this crisis?

*If you were alone, I wouldn't know, but you have Yana and Scarlet with you now,* the Void Monarch advised from within his mind. *Yana's power against Jeniela's… it's impossible to say for certain without seeing them clash, but there's a high probability that Jeniela will overwhelm her.*

Lee Sungmin thought so as well. Yana was a Nine-Tailed Fox of immense power, but Jeniela was the Vampire of Vampires, a being whose strength was unfathomable.

*And Jeniela's high-ranking vampires,* the Monarch continued. *This is the first time this Elder has seen them in person, but… Gemini. That hateful little girl's power is quite remarkable. You'd do well to be wary of her.*

Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. It was Gemini who had orchestrated this entire situation. He had come to the mansion, and she had come out to greet him. While she was stalling for time over Baek Sogo, Jeniela had emerged from her seclusion within. Buying time, a simple delay… was that really all it was? It felt as though Gemini had another motive. When he had pushed past her to rescue Baek Sogo, Gemini had not tried to stop him. It was as if she had never been concerned about such an outcome from the very beginning.

Why? Securing Baek Sogo would have been a clear advantage in a situation like this. His eyes met Gemini's across the tense space. Whether she knew what was on his mind or not, she simply offered him a hateful smile.

"Slaughter Predator," Jeniela's voice cut through the air. "To kill everything, to devour everything. That is what you could call a Slaughter Predator, isn't it? You know, I've wanted to make you Despair for a long time. You, who holds the contradiction of being human while your body is closer to a Yokai. You, who wants to remain human no matter what. I wanted to break you with Despair."

It was her sadistic preference. All the Vampires she had turned had been people on the verge of collapse. The only exception was Gemini, her First Bloodline.

"It's still true now," Jeniela mused, her hand gesturing forward. "I'm curious what you'll look like when you're broken by Despair. Will you become a ferocious Yokai, befitting that body of yours? If you can't protect anything and are forced to watch helplessly… will you Despair? That sounds quite interesting, too."

"Do you want to make me the Slaughter Predator?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"That wouldn't be so bad, either," she replied lightly. "But that's a story for after you've Despaired."

Jeniela flicked her fingertips.

FWOOSH!

A surge of crimson magic erupted like a flame. In response, Yana's nine tails whipped forward to intercept it.

BOOM!

The very air shuddered from the impact. Yana let out a low groan, the tips of her beautiful golden tails now soaked in red. A wound of that degree would regenerate in an instant, but she could not hide her surprise at the sheer power of Jeniela's attack. She had never imagined the Vampire Queen of the North would be this strong.

"Scarlet."

First, he had to break through this. They had the Fairy Horse, but performing a Space Leap with four people would take time. Lee Sungmin passed Baek Sogo into Scarlet's care. She nodded and moved her hands quickly, inscribing the air with runic characters that glowed with brilliant colors. A powerful defensive barrier of her own creation enveloped both her and Baek Sogo.

With that minimal level of safety confirmed, Lee Sungmin gripped his spear. This was the first time he would have to use his full power after awakening as a complete Yokai. There had been a few skirmishes before, but they were too easy to even be called fights. This was different. If he didn't give it his all… yes, if he didn't give it his all, it would be just as Jeniela said. He would be powerless, lose what he must protect, and Despair.

*Isn't it a little late for Despair now?* the Void Monarch jeered.

Lee Sungmin agreed.

"Queen!" Gemini shouted, her voice laced with surprise.

Lee Sungmin's movement was so explosive that it startled even her, wiping the smile from her face and forcing a warning from her lips. The body of a Yokai. The body of the Slaughter Predator. The inevitable, preordained End, the strongest and worst of its kind, destined to shut down the massive slaughterhouse called Eria. It wasn't complete, for the one controlling it was Lee Sungmin, not the Predator itself. Even so, it was far better than the incomplete and unstable body that had been burdened with ten years of fighting spirit and the Sealing.

He could see.

His eyes, now completely golden, could see so much more. The moment the 'battle' began and he resolved himself to fight, his vision opened to things he had not perceived before, not even ten years ago. He saw the movement of magic power. He saw the bottomless evil Jeniela possessed, an ominous energy that at a glance was greater than Kim Jonghyun's ten years of fighting spirit and far surpassed Vollander's. He saw Kun and Chen, their power formidable, on par with a martial artist of the Transcendent Realm, comparable even to the Sword Saint. He saw Gemini, whose power was astonishing, far exceeding that of Kun and Chen. It was not as much as Jeniela's, but it was fitting for a First Bloodline and a pureblood Vampire.

He had no choice but to admit it. Just as Jeniela had laughed, Heaven Beyond Heaven had been surviving on the Predator's mercy. If Jeniela and her vampires had stepped forward, without the need for the entire Predator force, the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens would have been annihilated long ago.

Even so.

The resolve that he must do this burned alongside the conviction that he could. If he fell here, everything would be over. He had not lived his life until now for that. He had not annihilated the Slaughter Predator for such an ending. He had not come all this way to save Baek Sogo just to Despair.

*If I were the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.*

*No, I am.*

*Be yourself.*

*Yes, I'll be myself.*

He was already holding the spear.

Jeniela reached out her hand toward the spear thrusting straight at her. At her gesture, her bottomless, ominous magic rose and consumed the space. Even on a moonless night, she was a monster of phenomenal power, but just as Gemini had warned, Jeniela saw the spear tip tearing through her magic. In that fleeting moment, she smiled.

*Slaughter Predator.*

A monster recognizes a monster.

BOOM!

With a roar, Jeniela's body was pushed back. The collision failed to injure her, her magic proving too dense and tough, but making her retreat was enough. The spear in Lee Sungmin's hand emitted a brilliant flash of light. Chen and Kun, who had rushed out to assist Jeniela, found themselves exposed to its path. They twisted their bodies with grim expressions, their mutated arms blocking the spear's trajectory. It was not an attack that could be stopped by that alone.

CRUNCH!

With a gruesome sound, both of their arms were torn apart as the space where the spear passed rippled violently. Despite pouring out an enormous amount of Demonic Energy and inner arts, he felt not the slightest headache. The current Lee Sungmin was in perfect control of his power.

He could see more. Becoming a complete Yokai had given him many things, or more precisely, it allowed him to fully utilize what he already possessed. The Dragon's magic power and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's inner arts were all perfectly harmonized within his body. Ten years had brought that body to maturity. It was perfect. The only thing that fell short was his mind's martial arts theory, but even if it didn't reach the level of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, it was not severely lacking.

"Myriad Thunder," Lee Sungmin's lips moved.

He swung the spear once, and a ceaseless torrent of violet lightning erupted from its path, engulfing the entire space. Jeniela smiled and raised a hand, spreading it out before her. Gemini quickly hid herself behind Jeniela, and Chen and Kun did the same. A crimson curtain formed in front of the Vampire Queen.

CRACK, CRACK, CRACK, BOOM!

The explosions were continuous, the sound never ceasing as the relentlessly fired lightning struck Jeniela's curtain again and again. As Lee Sungmin prepared to swing his spear once more, he froze. The spearhead was shattered. It wasn't just the tip. The shaft was riddled with cracks.

*Why is it so weak?* No, was his power too excessive? Still, this was a spear made from a Dragon's own materials. It looked certain to shatter if he swung it a few more times. He would definitely get an earful from Celgerus, but he had no choice. Lee Sungmin poured more power into the spear. The shaft trembled violently. He infused inner arts and Demonic Energy into the weapon until it was on the verge of shattering, then threw it with all his might toward Jeniela's red curtain.

WHOOSH!

The space where the spear flew was torn to shreds. Despite being battered by the unceasing Myriad Thunder, the red curtain was still intact. He could see Jeniela smiling beyond it. She only maintained the curtain and launched no other attacks. That was unsettling, but it had created an opportunity. Lee Sungmin stopped Yana, who was about to press the attack, and ran toward Scarlet and Baek Sogo. He immediately summoned the Fairy Horse and mounted it.

"I'll let you go," Jeniela's voice carried from behind him. "For today."

*Not next time.* She didn't need to say it. He really didn't want to see her again, Lee Sungmin thought as he gripped the Fairy Horse's reins. Despite carrying four people, the horse showed no signs of struggle, though they had to squeeze together tightly just to fit. He thought of a place to return to, the safest place he knew in this world.

"……"

Upon arriving in the Fairy Forest, Lee Sungmin immediately threw up. Even with the body of a Slaughter Predator, the aftereffects of performing four consecutive Space Leaps were unavoidable. It was the same for Yana. Though not as loudly as him, she exercised as much restraint as possible, turning her head and lowering her posture to vomit.

"I thought I was going to die," Scarlet said, her expression not much better. She had also performed two Space Leaps. She let out a deep sigh and glanced at the face of Baek Sogo, whom she was still supporting.

"Couldn't you have taken your time on the way here?" Osla's voice called out.

Turning his head, he saw her standing in the middle of the Fairy Realm. She clicked her tongue as she looked at the mess Lee Sungmin and Yana had made. Beside her, the other fairies were pinching their noses.

"I told you. No matter how great you are, consecutive Space Leaps put too much strain on the body. If you were unlucky, your body and soul could have been separated."

"It was an unavoidable situation," Lee Sungmin replied. "We would've been in danger if we hadn't used Space Leap."

Jeniela had intentionally let them escape. What would have happened if they had continued to fight? He would only know if they tried, but the fact that Jeniela and Gemini had not actively engaged was unsettling. In that situation, escaping for Baek Sogo's sake had been the right answer.

"So… how is she?" Osla asked, glancing at Baek Sogo, who still had her eyes closed.

"What do you think, Osla?"

"Don't answer a question with a question," Osla said with a pout. "She hasn't become a Vampire. It doesn't seem like there's any trickery involved either… Poison… well. I'll have to take a look first."

"Please."

"Aren't you working me too hard?" Osla grumbled, but she still took Baek Sogo's wrist.
