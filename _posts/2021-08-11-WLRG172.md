"I have been rude," Yana sighed.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon, having staggered back from the force of her power, glared at her. Lee Sungmin watched her with equally fierce eyes. Though she had stopped midway, the strength she had just displayed was beyond anything he could have imagined. A chilling certainty settled in his gut. If Yana truly wanted him dead, he would not survive the fight, even if he unleashed everything he had.

The Nine-Tailed Fox who ruled Urmuri was a being whose power was comparable to that of Jeniela, the Vampire Queen he had met in the North.

"I must have misunderstood," she said. "I naturally thought the Void Monarch would be after your body."

[I was, once,] the Void Monarch grumbled. [But not anymore. This kid's body and soul are strangely intertwined. I don't want to mess with it and risk having my own soul annihilated.]

"If that's your concern..." Yana began cautiously, trying to offer again.

[I said I don't need it,] the Void Monarch cut her off, his voice cold.

Yana's shoulders flinched at his tone. She knew there was another reason for his refusal, something more than what he had just said. *Could it be... affection?* Her eyebrows twitched at the thought. She had considered the possibility, but it seemed so unlikely. The Void Monarch she remembered was a true Yokai, one who never formed attachments with humans.

"I... I was only trying to help in my own way, because you wanted to see me," she said.

[You did something I never asked for. Thanks to you, I'm in a difficult position.]

"...I'm sorry."

[Enough. When it comes down to it, it was for my sake,] the Void Monarch said, then spat his next words at the silent Lee Sungmin. [Why are you staying silent? Didn't you come here because you had something to ask that Nine-Tailed Fox?]

Lee Sungmin forced himself to calm down. The sudden confrontation had left him flustered and angry, but he knew acting on those emotions would only make things worse. He took a deep, steadying breath and nodded.

"A few years ago," he began, his voice even, "you saved a tribe that wields Demonic Energy from a crisis. Do you remember?"

"Of course, I remember," Yana answered.

"I came south to find that tribe," he explained. "I have two questions for you. First, can I meet the people you rescued? And second, who was the fire-breathing Yokai that attacked them?"

Yana fell silent for a moment before a gentle smile touched her lips. "Do you want to wield Demonic Energy?"

"If possible."

"You are a strange being," she mused, her gaze analytical. "Frau told me about the strong Destiny Force entwined with you. You stand on the middle ground between Yokai and human. Your body has been tainted by the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy, mutating until it's close to a half-yokai, yet your humanity hasn't crumbled." As she spoke, she glanced at the flickering flame of the Void Monarch.

"That tribe... they were people who worshiped the dead Void Monarch as a god. Over hundreds of years, they transformed the remnants of fear he left behind into a kind of divinity, allowing them to wield the Demonic Energy lingering in the world as if through Spirit Possession, but the energy they wielded was faint, not nearly enough to turn a human into a half-yokai."

"...Were there no side effects?"

"As I said, they only managed it through centuries of worship, a kind of Spirit Possession. They used the Demonic Energy as a breathing method and condensed it into pills. Those with the tribe's bloodline could handle it without great risk, but outsiders who tried suffered from lunacy."

A low groan escaped the Crazy Heavenly Demon. The conversation had just revealed the cause of his own madness.

Yana looked back at Lee Sungmin. "Frau said it was impossible for a human to wield Demonic Energy. That would be true for a pure human, but you aren't one, are you? Even among half-yokai born between humans and Yokai, quite a few maintain their humanity while wielding Demonic Energy. Your blood isn't mixed with a Yokai's, but since you're no longer a pure human, your humanity won't crumble. As long as you follow the method, that is."

Lee Sungmin felt a flicker of relief at her words, but he kept his expression neutral, not yet ready to trust her completely.

"And," Yana continued, "the other Yokai that attacked the village is Chuseong."

At the name, the Crazy Heavenly Demon's eyes flashed. Yana glanced at his sudden hostility, tilting her head with a curious air.

"Where is Chuseong now?" Lee Sungmin asked.

"Why are you looking for him?"

"Revenge," the Crazy Heavenly Demon answered, his voice a low growl.

Yana stared at him blankly for a moment. "You're an excellent martial artist, but defeating Chuseong will be difficult. The strength of a human and an inhuman being are hard to compare, and Chuseong is a great yokai who has received many offerings of fear over a long life."

"That's none of your concern."

"Chuseong has left Urmuri," she replied with a smirk. "When I met him in that village, I intended to kill him. I wanted to protect that place where I could feel the remnants of the Void Monarch. Chuseong, fearing me, fled Urmuri."

"He left Urmuri?"

"Yes. I don't know where he is now either. Though if he ever returns, I'll kill him with my own hands."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon's face contorted, his fists clenched so tightly his knuckles turned white. He had come to Urmuri certain he would find Chuseong, only to learn his quarry had already fled. While Lee Sungmin was on the verge of achieving his goal, the Crazy Heavenly Demon was left with nothing.

"Then," Yana said, rising to her feet, "as you wish, I will let you meet the survivors of that tribe. The timing is perfect."

"What do you mean?"

"In a few days, I must leave Urmuri," she explained with a calm expression. "Because I became a Nine-Tailed Fox so quickly, there are many restrictions. I must periodically leave Urmuri and return to the mountain where I trained."

"A mountain? Don't tell me, is it Mount Mushii?"

"Ahaha. I know of Mount Mushii, but that's not where I trained. It's a mountain not so far from here." With that, Yana turned. "Follow me. They live in this Fairy Realm."

The Grand Elegance Palace, it seemed, was her sanctuary. She led them out of the annex and into a garden. Standing in the center, she raised her right hand, her index finger tracing glowing characters in the empty air. The space before them trembled and then split open with a sharp CRACK. Stepping through the rift, they found themselves in a spacious forest where a large, eastern-style mansion stood.

"It seems I have many enemies," Yana explained. "I usually live here."

Lee Sungmin could feel the presence of people all around them, their sharp gazes like needles on his skin. His hand instinctively went to the shaft of his spear.

"These people are not enemies," Yana raised her hand. "Please show yourselves."

A nearby bush rustled, and a young man with a still-youthful air stepped out. He was the first of many, as people of all ages began to emerge from their hiding places. The moment Lee Sungmin saw them, he felt an inexplicable sense of familiarity.

[It's because of the Demonic Energy,] the Void Monarch muttered. [Indeed. They have a denser Demonic Energy than that hulk, the Crazy Heavenly Demon.]

"Who are you?" the young man who had appeared first demanded. The other survivors also felt that strange familiarity toward Lee Sungmin, and it made them wary.

Receiving their suspicious gazes, Lee Sungmin wondered how he should even begin to explain.

[There's no need to speak,] the Void Monarch spat. The Magic Armor hummed, and a brilliant flame erupted above Lee Sungmin's head.

The survivors' eyes widened in shock. No explanation was necessary. They knew that flame. It was the Yokai their tribe had worshiped for centuries. In an instant, they all dropped to their knees, bowing their heads to the ground.

"Ah, my god!" someone cried out.

They naturally regarded Lee Sungmin as the messenger of their god. The Void Monarch let out a boastful laugh, as if their reaction was only natural. Lee Sungmin, feeling quite embarrassed, could only scowl.

In the days that followed, Lee Sungmin, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, and Rubia stayed in the forest sanctuary. The villagers never left its borders, and Lee Sungmin had much to learn from them. When he asked to be taught how to wield Demonic Energy, however, they reacted with surprise.

"Messenger, you can't use the divine power?" Avrom asked, his expression bewildered. He was the young man who had first emerged from the bushes.

Lee Sungmin had already told them several times not to call him 'Messenger', but Avrom and the others simply agreed before continuing to call him whatever they pleased.

*What divine power...*

[It's just what they like to call it. Hehe! To those humans, this Elder is a god, after all,] the Void Monarch gloated.

Lee Sungmin let the words go in one ear and out the other as he nodded at Avrom. "The method..."

Avrom stroked his chin thoughtfully. It was a kind of inner arts technique, though they called it a breathing method. The Crazy Heavenly Demon had learned it, but he had sworn an oath under a binding spell not to reveal it to anyone, so he could not teach Lee Sungmin directly.

"I'll have to write a book," Avrom finally decided with a nod.

Two days passed with no sign of the book. Instead, Yana came to visit.

"As I mentioned, I have to leave Urmuri for a while," she said, her expression calm. "I came to say my goodbyes."

"How long will you be gone?"

"About half a month, probably."

It was said that a fox spirit usually takes two thousand years to become a Nine-Tailed Fox. Yana had shortened that time to five hundred years, not only because she was exceptional, but also because some other help had been at play. Not even the Void Monarch had guessed what that was.

"I've left the spell open, so you can leave this forest whenever you wish, but if possible... I would like to see you again when I return."

Lee Sungmin could tell her words were not for him, but for the flame hovering nearby.

[If that's possible,] the Void Monarch answered indifferently.

"...Void Monarch," Yana's voice softened. "Even if you don't remember, I was once indebted to you. I became a Yokai to repay that kindness, but... in the end, I couldn't stop your subjugation. Protecting this tribe was my way of trying to repay you."

[Even if you say that, I don't remember. I never asked you to do this, so I feel no gratitude. However,] he paused, [I will commend the sentiment that you acted for this Elder's sake.]

Listening quietly, Lee Sungmin found the Void Monarch's attitude insufferably arrogant. Yana, however, looked deeply moved. She bowed her head, her voice trembling as she replied.

"...Thank you."

Yana left the forest after conveying her heartfelt gratitude. It was another two days before Avrom finally came to see him.

"Here it is," he said, handing over a thin book.

Lee Sungmin took it, his brow furrowing as he looked at the slim volume. "It's not very thick. Why did this take four days?"

"Well..." Avrom looked embarrassed. "It's because few among us know how to write."

"Then you could have just asked me."

"How could we possibly ask such a thing of the Messenger?"

"Then you could have asked someone else," Lee Sungmin pressed. "The Crazy Heavenly Demon Elder is here, too."

Avrom looked at him as if the answer was the most obvious thing in the world. "This is our offering to the Messenger. How could we receive help from an outsider to the tribe?"

Lee Sungmin swallowed a sigh, feeling as though he had just wasted four days for nothing.
