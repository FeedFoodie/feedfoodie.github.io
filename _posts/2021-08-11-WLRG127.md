Although his course was set for the South, he could not travel there immediately. His business in the North remained unfinished, and he still lacked certainty that the nobleman he sought was truly the Crazy Heavenly Demon.

Winter had not yet released its grip on the land. For now, he would have to remain in the North until the year's end. Once it passed, the period the Holy Spirit had spoken of would be over, leaving him free to depart without issue.

Even then, he would not head south right away. The new year would mark the fulfillment of the ten years he had promised Wiji Hoyeon. On March 14th, in the Central Plaza of the great city of Ruvus, Lee Sungmin had sworn he would meet her.

*What will happen when I meet Wiji Hoyeon?*

He did not know. They had crossed paths in the Dungeon only a few months ago, but their conversation had been brief. He did, however, remember her words from that day, her suggestion that they travel together. When they reunited in a few months, would he be able to join her? And if he could, would they perhaps journey to the South together?

He pushed the thought aside for the time being.

Lee Sungmin turned back toward Trabia, the Crazy Heavenly Demon in tow. He planned to use the city as his base for the remainder of the year, for he still had personal business to attend to there. Aine's attack had revealed that Preskan had established a foothold within its walls. With so little time to spare, Lee Sungmin could not afford to miss this opportunity. He had to meet Wiji Hoyeon in the grand plaza of Ruvus, and from there, he needed to travel south to learn of the Void Monarch's treasure and the use of Demonic Energy. That journey alone would consume the better part of a year. If he let this chance slip by, he had no idea when he might encounter Preskan again.

The plan was not without its risks, the most troublesome of which was the Vampire Queen, Jeniela. Lee Sungmin knew nothing of the relationship between her and Preskan, but he could not help but be cautious about provoking a fight with the necromancer in a city under her rule.

"domaksaramu… domaksaramu…"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon chanted the spell Lee Sungmin had taught him in a small voice as he followed behind. Seeing him continuously repeat the meaningless phrase, even after being slapped for it, revealed a surprisingly naive side to the man, one that belied his fearsome title and reputation. A thought suddenly struck Lee Sungmin, and he posed a question to his companion.

"Elder, in your opinion, where do you think your strength ranks in this world?"

The Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had been muttering his spell, turned his head to look at Lee Sungmin. The power he sensed from the man was real. Judging by his mastery of enhanced qi and his ability to improvise, his martial prowess was on a level the Wiji Hoyeon doppelganger could never hope to match. If the Lee Sungmin from the Dungeon had faced this man, he would have been killed in seconds.

"That's a stupid question."

Lee Sungmin had braced himself for an arrogant reply, but the Crazy Heavenly Demon simply let out a hollow laugh.

"The world is a vast place," he muttered, his voice laced with a bitter self-mockery that caught Lee Sungmin off guard. Seeing the younger man's bewildered expression, the Crazy Heavenly Demon chuckled.

"It's natural for a martial artist of your caliber to be curious. You wonder who's number one in the world. You wonder about your own standing. You there, how old are you?"

"…I'm twenty-three."

"Haha! To have surpassed the Supreme Peak at twenty-three and be gazing at the Transcendent Realm, even from a distance… I wonder how many people would believe you if you told them your age. Yes, at your level, you're more than qualified to discuss who is number one." The corners of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's mouth lifted into a smile. "And you're also qualified enough to realize just how absurdly out of reach that position is."

"…What do you mean?"

"Everyone has their own mountain to climb. Before you stand at its summit, you can't know. You just think, vaguely or perhaps with conviction, 'I've climbed quite high.' It's only when you stand at the very top that you finally realize it. The mountain you climbed wasn't all that high, just one small peak in a vast and endless range."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon spoke in a bitter tone, his words directed as much at himself as they were at Lee Sungmin.

"The world is vast. Perhaps you already know this. In a world where non-humans roam freely, humans are not such remarkable beings. There are those who live for several, even dozens of times longer than we do. I've devoted sixty years to martial arts to reach my current state. For a human, sixty years of training is nothing to scoff at, but what if a being who lives for hundreds of years devotes most of their life to that same training? What if that being, who has trained for centuries, also possesses a calamitous level of talent?"

At his words, Lee Sungmin recalled the pinnacles of the inhuman races he had met in the North. The Vampire Queen, Jeniela, had lived for six hundred years. Ju Won, the Mad Wolf, must have lived for several hundred as well.

"They are the Heaven Beyond Heaven. There are humans who have lived for hundreds of years, and there are beings who were never human to begin with."

After speaking, the Crazy Heavenly Demon suddenly tore off his upper garment. A long, deep sword scar was etched into his exposed torso.

"Ten years ago. This is a wound I received from the Sword Saint, one of the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens. Even as I am now, I'm not confident I could last a hundred seconds against him. Perhaps… you too will soon meet the Heaven Beyond Heaven. They like to test newly emerged powerhouses."

Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Six Honored Ones. Lee Sungmin could not help but agree with the man's words. In his past life as a third-rate mercenary, he had known nothing. The pinnacle of his world back then had been Zeon, the leader of the Corona Mercenary Group, yet that same Zeon had been killed so futilely by Preskan.

Thinking back, Lee Sungmin's world had expanded greatly since Behenger. He had met one incredible individual after another, people he had never even heard of in his past life. Through these encounters, he had come to realize just how small his world was, and how insignificant the being called 'I' truly was. It was just as the Crazy Heavenly Demon had said. You had to climb to the summit of your own peak just to see how much higher the other mountains soared.

*How high up are you?*

Wiji Hoyeon.

{sep}

She sat with her legs crossed, studying the person seated across from her. He wore a worn-out cloak and a large, conical hat that shadowed his face. Clad in the pitch-black clothes and Black Dragon Armor she had acquired from the Dungeon, Wiji Hoyeon dominated the space with her presence alone, a feat for one so young. Not content with that, she exuded a subtle aura, a silent threat directed at the man in the cloak.

He, however, remained perfectly composed under the full force of her pressure. The two of them sat opposite each other in the center of the old, empty tavern, sharing a long silence.

The man was the first to move. He slowly raised a hand and picked up the teacup on the table.

"The tea has grown cold," he muttered. As his hand caressed the cup, steam began to rise from the chilled liquid. The man slurped the now-hot tea, his eyes peering out from under the brim of his hat to fix on Wiji Hoyeon's face.

"Is it something worth such deep thought?" he asked.

Wiji Hoyeon, her arms crossed, did not answer right away. She let out a small laugh and withdrew the aura she had been emitting.

"It's just… sudden."

"Most encounters are." The man gave a low laugh as he set down his teacup. He removed the conical hat and placed it on the table, allowing a cascade of silver hair that had been tucked inside to fall free. Wiji Hoyeon's eyes flickered to his long ears. He possessed a face of impossible beauty for a man, framed by long, pointed ears. He was an Elf.

"Heaven Beyond Heaven. I've never heard of such a group."

"I'd expect not, but we do exist."

"Why do you want me to join?"

"Because you have potential." The man smiled, showing his teeth.

It had been a sudden encounter indeed. She had entered this tavern by chance, feeling thirsty and hungry as she walked down the street. She had stepped inside without a second thought, only to find the man already there, waiting. He had then approached her and started this conversation, extending an invitation to join Heaven Beyond Heaven.

"I wasn't deep in thought," Wiji Hoyeon finally said. "I was just unimpressed." Her voice was flat. "And I'm also displeased. You know me. You were waiting for me here."

"That's right."

"But I don't know you. I don't know what Heaven Beyond Heaven is, either. That's why I'm unimpressed and displeased."

"If you're displeased because my introduction was late, then I apologize." The man shrugged. "I am called the Fist Honored One, and I hold the last place among the Six Sages of the Outer Heavens."

Heaven Beyond Heaven. The Six Honored Ones. The Fist Honored One. Wiji Hoyeon parted her lips and muttered the names. She appeared calm, but a flicker of confusion stirred within her. She had never heard of the group, nor of the Sages or the title.

*Incredible.*

It had been nearly ten years since she had come to Eria. In all that time, she had never once experienced hardship, let alone a crisis. Everyone she had ever met was weaker than her. Even the formidable boss monster in the Dungeon had been her inferior.

However, the man before her? It was her first time seeing an Elf, but she was not ignorant of their kind. They were a race blessed by the forest, living for up to a thousand years. Wiji Hoyeon tilted her head and asked, "I wasn't displeased because I don't know who you are. The Six Honored Ones, the Fist Honored One… what I'm curious about isn't that. It's what the hell kind of bastards Heaven Beyond Heaven is."

"We don't pursue anything in particular. It's just… isn't it lonely? When you stand at the pinnacle of a field, you're bound to feel solitude, and that solitude often breeds madness," the Fist Honored One said with a laugh. "I myself have lived for four hundred years already. I've devoted most of those four hundred years to fist techniques… Huhu! It's enough to drive one mad. The Elf race lives for too long. For someone like me, who has lived four hundred years, I still have more time ahead of me than I have lived so far."

"Get lost," Wiji Hoyeon answered, giving a slight nod at his words.

The smile on the Fist Honored One's face vanished. "…Did I hear you correctly?"

"No. You heard me. Get lost." Wiji Hoyeon picked up the teacup in front of her, downed the cold tea in one gulp, and rose to her feet. "I have no desire to join a bunch of bastards who've gathered for such a ridiculous reason, and another thing. Guys who try to evade questions with excuses like that always have rotten intentions."

"I can't just tell you everything from the start, can I?" the Fist Honored One said, his expression shifting.

Wiji Hoyeon burst out laughing at his words. "See? There's a reason you can't tell me outright. You… you reek. You absolutely reek."

"It seems my approach was wrong," the Fist Honored One muttered as he, too, rose to his feet. The cloak he wore began to flutter violently. The once-calm aura around him went wild, and an energy like a collapsing mountain erupted from his form. "I thought you were a child with potential, so I tried to coax you… but you're too rude. It might be a good idea to teach you some manners before bringing you in as the last place."

"You're really strange," Wiji Hoyeon said, bursting into another fit of laughter. "I said no, and I've insulted you like this, yet you're not thinking about killing me. You're thinking about getting me into your group no matter what. I wonder why… Why are you so desperate to get me to join Heaven Beyond Heaven?"

The Fist Honored One did not answer. Wiji Hoyeon chuckled at the man, who was now openly displaying his power, and raised a hand to pinch her nose.

"I told you. You stink."

There was also the smell of blood, a faint scent wafting from the tavern's kitchen. From the moment she had entered, there had been no one else inside except the Fist Honored One. Only two teacups had been sitting there. She was not angry that he had killed the innocent tavern owner and his family. She was just annoyed that he was trying to bind her in this way.

"And last place?" Wiji Hoyeon raised her hand. The Black Dragon Armor coiled around her arm like a living shadow. "You're the one who's rude."

With a high-pitched laugh, the tavern building collapsed.
