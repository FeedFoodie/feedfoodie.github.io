It was like starting a car engine.

GRUUMBLE!

The Violet Dawn Divine Art surged from Lee Sungmin's qi center, coursing through his blood and qi like a current. His eyes flashed as he tracked Woon Heeryong's every move. When Sungmin immediately backed away, Woon Heeryong held his ground, refusing to charge in recklessly.

Woon Heeryong knew his actions had become meaningless, but it was far too late to back down. He and Wang Pae had already killed Lenire. Apologies like 'We're sorry, it was a mistake, we were wrong' would never convince the Mercenary Guild. Their only option was to kill to silence. They had chosen that path. The Guild's tracking magic meant they couldn't take the Subspace Pocket, but there was still miscellaneous loot to be had. Woon Heeryong's thoughts drifted to the Subspace Pocket Lee Sungmin had used. He couldn't gauge the level of its spatial expansion magic, but he knew it would fetch a good price.

"Uwaaaah!"

Behind them, a bloody battle raged between Wang Pae and Do Sangryang. The outcome would put either Lee Sungmin or Woon Heeryong at a great disadvantage, a fact that made Woon Heeryong anxious. While Sungmin had his back turned, Woon Heeryong had a clear view of the struggle. Wang Pae's knife throwing arts, so effective for an ambush, offered no advantage in a direct confrontation like this. The blades were too short to be wielded properly, and once thrown, they were gone. A true master might have retrieval methods or techniques for close-quarters combat, but Wang Pae was no master. His skill was Second-Rate at best.

The match had been decided the moment Wang Pae failed to take down Do Sangryang in the initial ambush. Now, all he could do was hold on, desperately hoping Woon Heeryong would finish Lee Sungmin and come to his aid.

*Do Sangryang has the advantage.*

Even without looking, Sungmin understood the situation perfectly. For a knife thrower who missed his first shot, the fight was already over. A master at the peak realm might have stood a chance, but someone with Wang Pae's limited capabilities could only hope to hold on.

*Anxiety.*

Sungmin saw it written all over Woon Heeryong's face. Unlike Sungmin, who was only guessing at the details, Woon Heeryong was watching the disaster unfold directly. The man couldn't control his wavering heart.

That anxiety made him act first.

"Haat!"

Woon Heeryong charged, closing the seven steps between them. His sword would only reach its target after he covered four of those steps. Had Sungmin possessed the physique of his past life, he could have attacked from outside Woon Heeryong's range, but his current body had not yet fully matured. Even so, the long reach of his spear was an advantage he could still exploit.

Sungmin watched his opponent's stance, predicting the kind of attack that would come. A swing. Just as Woon Heeryong's arm lifted to the side to begin the motion, Sungmin lunged, shooting his spear forward.

"Heot!"

Woon Heeryong scrambled back in an awkward retreat. Seizing the momentum, Sungmin shifted his grip and advanced.

SHWISH! SHWISH! SHWIK!

The spear jabbed out again and again. Caught in a posture where he couldn't swing his sword, Woon Heeryong could only backpedal, desperately evading the relentless assault. Wiji Hoyeon may have called his spearmanship pathetic, but it was more than effective against an opponent of a similar level.

It was a matter of technique, but even without it, Sungmin had other ways to overwhelm his opponent. The Violet Dawn Divine Art created an efficient explosion of inner qi, which erupted from his qi center and infused his body with power. This strength, born from the purity and volume of his arts, had improved greatly thanks to Wiji Hoyeon.

CLAAANG!

Woon Heeryong's hastily swung sword crashed against the spear's shaft, a textbook move to break the momentum of a retreat. Sungmin gripped the spear tightly as it threatened to fly from his hands. Face pale, Woon Heeryong rushed forward, extending his sword arm for a thrust.

In that same instant, Sungmin shot his spear forward and released his grip. The empowered weapon slid from his palms. Woon Heeryong's eyes widened in disbelief. He never imagined Sungmin would let go of his spear in such a critical moment.

Woon Heeryong hastily twisted his body. Having thrown the spear, Sungmin lunged in and drew the dagger from his lower back. He had borrowed it from Brass for a reason. He had his own dagger, but there was a reason he couldn't use it.

"......"

Woon Heeryong leaned his upper body sharply to the side as the dagger Sungmin swung barely grazed his forearm. He immediately tried to swing his sword in a counterattack.

"...Heok!"

His face turned deathly pale. The hand holding the sword began to tremble violently before his grip loosened, causing the weapon to clatter to the ground.

"You... you bastard... what in the..."

"Poison. You idiot," Sungmin answered, exhaling. He had coated the dagger from his back with a powerful paralytic poison. A master of inner qi who had reached a certain realm might be able to suppress it, but for someone at Woon Heeryong's level, it was an impossible dream.

Sungmin picked up his spear and strode toward the fallen man.

"W... wait... Young Master... please..."

"Shut up."

Sungmin spun his spear.

"......"

The downward strike shattered Woon Heeryong's shoulder joint. A scream tore from Woon Heeryong's open mouth. Sungmin only stopped after he had similarly destroyed both of the man's shoulders and knee joints. He considered killing him but decided it was better to keep him alive for the Mercenary Guild. They would take care of things from there.

"Is it over?" a voice asked from behind.

Do Sangryang stood there, his face etched with exhaustion. Near his feet, Wang Pae lay sprawled in a bloody pulp. The sounds of their fight had stopped at some point, and Sungmin now saw that Do Sangryang had beaten the man to death.

"......"

"...Incredible," Do Sangryang said, his voice full of awe. "I underestimated you 'cause you're so young... I think you're stronger than me."

"You're too humble," Sungmin replied with a slight bow.

Do Sangryang sighed, his gaze falling on Lenire's corpse with melancholic eyes. "...What should we do about the Orc settlement?"

"Let's rest a bit and then go clean it up," Sungmin replied. "The two of us should be able to handle it. The Orc warriors who could be called their main force are already mostly dead."

"...Alright. Let's do that."

Though he agreed, Do Sangryang was taken aback. The boy said he was fourteen, so how could he be so cold? They had known each other for less than half a day, yet one of their comrades had died and two had betrayed them.

"We're taking this bastard to the Mercenary Guild," Sungmin declared, gesturing to Woon Heeryong.

"...Right."

"Let's put the bodies of Lenire, Wang Pae, and that bastard into the Subspace Pocket Lenire had. Oh, and let's put the nearby Orc corpses in there too. Brass. You watch this bastard Woon Heeryong and make sure he doesn't try any funny business."

"Ye-yes? Ah... yes. I understand," Brass stammered.

"Don't be too scared. I've shattered this bastard's limb joints, so even when the paralytic poison wears off, all he'll be able to do is squirm like a caterpillar."

"Ugh... uuuugh..."

As if to prove his words, Woon Heeryong squirmed where he lay face down on the ground. Do Sangryang was so dumbfounded he couldn't help but ask, "You. Are you really fourteen?"

"......"

Lee Sungmin simply met his gaze with a nonchalant expression.

By the time Lee Sungmin and Do Sangryang had finished off the remaining Orcs in the settlement, the sun was beginning to set. Normally, they would have set fire to the village, but since the Mercenary Guild hadn't requested it, they left it as it was.

*I wonder how long it will last.*

Sungmin thought as he rubbed his nose, which had grown accustomed to the smell of blood. Although they had killed every Orc in this village, it wouldn't be long before Orcs from a nearby territory realized the village was empty and moved in. The reproductive rate of Orcs was incredibly fast. Soon, this place would become an Orc village again. Sungmin didn't concern himself with that. It was a matter for the Mercenary Guild, or perhaps the Hunters who lived in this forest, to handle.

"Let's head back. The sun is setting... we should hurry," Do Sangryang said, having finished stuffing the Orc corpses into the Subspace Pocket.

Sungmin returned with him to where Brass was keeping watch. The apprentice was still on edge, warily scanning his surroundings. Next to him, Woon Heeryong was squirming with a cloth gag in his mouth.

"The bastard. He shit himself," Do Sangryang grumbled, glancing at Woon Heeryong's stinking rear before spitting on the ground.

Brass and the other apprentice mercenary supported Woon Heeryong as the four of them left the forest. Partway through, Sungmin and Do Sangryang took over to get out of the forest before night fell completely.

They finally exited the forest. "Are you going to make the report right away?" Sungmin asked.

Do Sangryang nodded. He was quite exhausted from moving and using martial arts theory without proper rest, but there was no reason to delay the report. The Mercenary Guild was located near the North Gate. Its first floor served as a pub and restaurant, while the Guild's offices were on the second floor and above.

"You should get something to eat," Do Sangryang suggested.

"I'll eat when I get back to my lodgings," Sungmin answered promptly. He had a feeling Wiji Hoyeon would be waiting for him.

"...Uhm..."

After Do Sangryang took Woon Heeryong upstairs, Brass approached Sungmin. He glanced at the other apprentice mercenary before bowing his head deeply.

"...Thank you for everything."

"Why are you thanking me?" Sungmin asked.

"Well... if it wasn't for you, Lee Sungmin, I would've been killed by the Orcs, and even if I'd survived that... Woon Heeryong or Wang Pae would've killed me."

"There's no need to thank me," Sungmin replied. "Just think of it as you being lucky."

As he spoke, a thought crossed his mind. What if he gave the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art to Brass? Since learning the Violet Dawn Divine Art, Sungmin no longer clung to the old technique, but he still had the book it was written in.

*No. Let's not.*

Brass was almost thirty years old. Even if he started the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art now, he likely wouldn't see any effect. This was the fate of most Classless who came to this world. It was difficult to change one's destiny without a great miraculous encounter, especially for someone as old as Brass.

"Sungmin."

Do Sangryang called as he came down from the second floor. At some point, his demeanor had become friendly, though Sungmin wasn't sure when they had gotten so close.

"Go on upstairs."

"What is it?"

"Well..." Do Sangryang made a strange expression. "The branch manager wants to see you."
