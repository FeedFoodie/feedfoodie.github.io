"Where's the bastard?" Hyeoru demanded, looking around suspiciously.

"He's not far from here," Crimson Tiger replied. This was the truth. He had intentionally arranged their meeting not far from the inn.

Hyeoru sneered. "Were you hiding like a little girl until I got here?"

Crimson Tiger ignored the mockery. "He's a formidable expert," he explained calmly.

"Hah! The stronger the expert, the easier they are to kill," Hyeoru shot back. An assassin by nature, he was a bloodthirsty, crazed killer who favored methods normal martial artists considered dishonorable. "From what I hear, you're not so different from that guy yourself."

Crimson Tiger tilted his head in confusion. "What do you mean?"

"You took the Great Cycle Pill and sided with the Pavilion Master, didn't you?"

Crimson Tiger sighed inwardly. This was why people said there were no secrets in the jianghu. He was confident that Yagong hadn't told Hyeoru anything, yet somehow, even the most closely guarded secrets always found a way to leak out.

"Seriously, you guys act all high and mighty, calling yourselves the Twelve Phantoms or whatever," Hyeoru scoffed. 

The corners of Crimson Tiger's mouth curled up slightly. Clearly, Hyeoru was jealous of the fact that he had gotten the Great Cycle Pill and earned a place among the Phantoms.

Hyeoru immediately narrowed his eyes threateningly. "What are you smiling at?" 

"What about you?" Crimson Tiger asked back. "Are you *really* sticking around the Pavilion Master out of pure loyalty?"

"Hahaha! Now I'm starting to like you. That's right, drop the hypocrisy!"

"So? Why are you working under Yagong?"

"He gives me legitimate opportunities to kill people. It's not as easy as you'd think to kill as many people as you want without being branded a public enemy of the jianghu," Hyeoru explained, then added with a grin, "Well, of course, I also get plenty of money and women, whatever I want."

Crimson Tiger shook his head. *As expected, this lunatic has only one purpose in life. Still, isn't he being too friendly? He's so friendly it's suspicious... ah. He's been ordered to kill me at the end of this mission, hasn't he?*

His intuition told him his guess was correct.

Smiling, he said, "Alright, let's go."

"Yeah. Let's get this over with quickly!"

The two men walked side by side.

"I've heard that your mastery of movement arts is exceptional," Crimson Tiger noted casually.

"If I put my mind to it, no one can catch me," Hyeoru boasted. 

Although his combat skills were nothing to scoff at, his movement arts were his greatest strength and the reason he had survived for so long. He was confident that no one could ever catch him.

Crimson Tiger nodded in agreement. "Indeed, that's why I missed you back then."

"Back then?"

"Don't you remember? The Savage Beast Gang."

"The Savage Beast Gang? Ah, those morons!" Hyeoru finally recalled his first meeting with Crimson Tiger. "You ruined my fun by showing up early. I was planning to decapitate them all and line their heads up in a row."

"You killed the innocent women, too."

“Those women were the same kind of morons. But why bring up that old story? It's not worth remembering."

Crimson Tiger abruptly stopped walking, and Hyeoru soon followed suit.

"What happened that day... it still bothers me," Crimson Tiger mumbled gravely.

"You're a half-wit turtle bastard, that's why!" Hyeoru mocked.

Crimson Tiger snickered.

Hyeoru instantly took offense to the unexpected reaction. "Does it feel good to be insulted? Are you actually a masochist?"

"That's not why I'm happy."

"What?"

I'm happy because you're finally so close to me."

Hyeoru tilted his head in confusion, not understanding Crimson Tiger's words.

FWIP!

Until Crimson Tiger lunged at his chest, that is.

SHWIIK!

He reflexively drew his sword, but Crimson Tiger had already anticipated this move. His initial lunge was a feint designed to force that very counterattack, after all. Anyone facing that maneuver for the first time would have no choice but to react by swinging their sword at his neck.

SHWAAA!

Crimson Tiger twisted his body in a bizarre manner, and Hyeoru's sword sliced through empty air.

"Fuck!" Hyeoru swore, but he had no time to rant or figure out what was happening.

Crimson Tiger pressed tightly against him, thrusting an arm under his armpit so naturally that it seemed like they were working together.

FWOOSH!

The next moment, Hyeoru found himself in midair. He saw himself moving slowly, the surrounding scenery perfectly clear, as if watching a flashback. Against his will, he completed a full rotation while airborne, all the while struggling to comprehend the situation.

*How did I dodge that attack? When was I so helplessly tossed into the air? No, when did he even throw me? What in the world is happening?*

Unfortunately, he did not have long to think. Tendrils of black sword qi shot up from the ground like springing snakes, brushing past Hyeoru's airborne body as if to impale him.

PAPAPAPAPAPAPA!

"Kuaaaack!" Hyeoru screamed as blood gushed from wounds all over his body. He couldn't even think of dodging, much less discern the technique. All he could do was stare blankly at the black sword qi he never wanted to see again, not even in his dreams.

CRAAAAASH! CRACK!

When he crashed to the ground, his spine shattered into five pieces. He tried to sit up, but his body refused to obey him. He was a tattered mess.

*What in the world just happened to me..? This bastard... he was hiding his martial arts!*

Another thought followed.

*Why me?*

Hyeoru did not see his life flash before his eyes in the moments before death. His mind was filled with too many other thoughts. He wanted to scream, but the pain rippling through his lungs prevented him from making a sound.

Crimson Tiger slowly approached the dying Hyeoru and stood over him. The technique he had just used was a combination of the Second Naraka, the Kālasūtra Weave, and the Thirteen Shadowless Maneuvers.

Seconds ticked by, each one feeling like an eternity.

Finally, after several attempts, Hyeoru forced out a gasp, "You... you! Why?"

"I told you earlier, didn't I?" Crimson Tiger sighed exasperatedly. "The way you massacred the innocents at the Savage Beast Gang was still bothering me. I need you to die so that I can forget it."

Hyeoru's dazed expression warped viciously. "For such a... such a... trivial reason..."

Crimson Tiger's eyes turned cold. "Don't use the word trivial... for something like that."

PUAAAAK!

Soul Crusher plunged into Hyeoru's heart, killing him instantly.

"This is revenge for all the innocent people who died at the hands of a bastard like you!"

Crimson Tiger glanced at the corpse for a moment before peeling his eyes away and staring over the hill.

Now, only one last task remained.

{sep}

The Evil Blade was livid after losing track of the fleeing Two Immortals. He wanted to hunt them to the ends of the earth, but his mission was more important.

He left the half-destroyed inn without breakfast, instead lighting a campfire and going hunting to sate his hunger. Unfortunately, while he was capable of catching a tiger or even an imoogi ascending to heaven, there was no nearby prey. 

After a long search, he finally found a small rabbit , but having to skewer and roast it for nearly an hour only made his anger burn hotter.

"Fuck!"

He almost threw the skewer away, but stopped himself. Doing so would waste nearly two hours of effort. 

Once again, he ground his teeth over his failure to catch the Two Immortals, when a sudden thought struck him.

*It was him. That Crimson Tiger bastard leaked my information!*

There was no other reason for two such formidable experts to appear out of the blue.

"If I catch you, I'll tear you to pieces!" he growled.

To his surprise, someone replied.

"Are you talking about me?" Crimson Tiger asked, strolling nonchalantly into the Evil Blade's camp with Hyeoru's corpse tucked under his arm.

The Evil Blade shot up from his seat. "You son of a bitch! You came at a good time!"

THUD!

Crimson Tiger tossed the corpse to the side.

"What's that?" the Evil Blade asked.

"The one who fought you to a standstill," Crimson Tiger said.

"What?" The Evil Blade narrowed his eyes at the seemingly irrelevant answer, but he started with the most pressing question. "Those two from before, were you the one who sent them?"

"Of course."

"Why?"

"You don't need to know that."

The Evil Blade's eyes flashed coldly. "So you haven't betrayed the Association after all."

"......" Crimson Tiger smiled, neither confirming nor denying the accusation.

"I see. So this is your scheme to retrieve the evidence."

The Evil Blade felt he had grasped the situation perfectly. However, there was still something he simply could not comprehend.

Despite scanning his surroundings, he sensed no other presence. Crimson Tiger was clearly alone. He wondered if the corpse was some kind of trick, but he couldn't feel the slightest trace of life from it.

"Who or what are you relying on?" he demanded.

SHING!

Crimson Tiger drew his sword, making sure to exude the same aura as the last time he fought the Evil Blade.

Nodding, the Evil Blade drew his own weapon. "Your courage is commendable."

SWISH!

Crimson Tiger rushed toward the Evil Blade, the tip of his sword darting through space as it if were alive. Nevertheless, the Evil Blade clearly saw its trajectory and raised his blade to parry the attack.

SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!

To his alarm, however, Crimson Tiger's sword changed direction abruptly and accelerated, thrusting at him three times.

"Ugh!" he groaned involuntarily and retreated three steps, dodging the attack.

He sighed in relief. Crimson Tiger's assault was far faster and more powerful than he had anticipated, so much so that he felt as if the space where he had just stood shattered like a mirror.

*A supreme expert! Was he hiding his skills the last time?*

WHOOSH!

Undaunted by the failure of his surprise attack, Crimson Tiger lunged forward.

CLANG!

As sword and blade exchanged blows, both men found themselves under immense pressure from their opponent's offensive.

CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG!

Crimson Tiger's wrist ached. His opponent's attacks were so fast that he had to block them instinctively. Had he not undergone grueling physical training, he never would have been able to withstand this speed and power.

BOOM!

The two men leaped into the air at the same time. Dozens of lines created by the sword and blade appeared and vanished, each containing the essence of a martial master. Life and death hung in the balance with each swing.

CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG!

The magnificent spectacle continues. Neither man dared unleash his enhanced qi. Both were waiting for a momentary gap in the other's defense.

"Haaaaaaap!"

With a loud roar, the Evil Blade increased the pace and intensity of his onslaught.

CLAAAAANG!

With a loud crash, the two men leaped back abruptly, landing so quickly and nimbly that it seemed as if they had been standing there the whole time and the heated battle had been a dream. 

Both men needed time to catch their breath, as well as a chance for a proper conversation.

The Evil Blade spoke first, asking, "What's your name?"

He no longer cared who Crimson Tiger was siding with or why he hid his skills. This man was the strongest opponent he had ever faced, someone who could kill him with the instant he got careless. As a martial artist, he wanted to know the true name and identity of the person who had pushed him to his limits.

Nevertheless, Crimson Tiger simply replied, "Crimson Tiger."

"That's not your real name, is it?"

"What does that matter?"

Sighing, the Evil Blade changed the topic. "Did the Association raise you?"

If the Association had indeed created a monster like Crimson Tiger, then this fight was a battle of pride between the New Righteous Association and the Evil Coalition. He had always dreamed of killing an Association-raised master.

"...Let's just say they did," Crimson Tiger muttered hesitantly.

The Evil Blade grinned. It was by no means a definitive reply, but he assumed it was true. Who else but the Association had the skill and resources to produce such a young martial master?

He asked again, "Do you have any questions for me?"

"None," Crimson Tiger answered as curtly as ever.

The Evil Blade narrowed his eyes in annoyance at being snubbed, but his opponent had earned the right to be cocky. "Fine! Let's go again!" he snapped, releasing a sharp, blue aura from his blade.

SWOOOOOSH!

Crimson Tiger's enhanced sword qi enveloped Soul Crusher in response.

KABOOM!

Once again, the fighters clashed and were each pushed back three steps. If their previous fight had been a contest of technique and speed, this one was purely a showdown of qi.

As luck would have it, though, they had similar amounts of inner qi. The Evil Blade had time and the support of the Evil Coalition on his side, and Crimson Tiger had the Great Cycle Pill.

SWOOOOOSH!

Enhanced sword and blade qi dominated the surrounding area, shredding all the plants and shaking the earth violently.

They were done testing one another. Now was the time to pour out everything they had.

The Evil Blade unleashed his unique martial art, the Blood Corrupting Soul Annihilating Blade, one of the jianghu's ten great martial arts.

SWOOOOOOOSH!

The first form of the Blood Corrupting Soul Annihilating Blade, the Soul-Refining Solar Eclipse, surged toward Crimson Tiger like a living blade of qi.

Crimson Tiger thrust the Soul Crusher toward his opponent in an odd trajectory. Two energies, one hot and one cold, flooded the air as he let loose the First Naraka, the Sañjīva Blast.

KRRRRRRRACKLE!

A grotesque sound echoed as the two men's enhanced qi intertwined like ghosts clawing at each other.

SWOOOOOOOSH!

The Evil Blade smoothly followed up with the second form of the Blood Corrupting Soul Annihilating Blade, the Soul-Severing Form, while Crimson Tiger countered with the Second Naraka, the Kālasūtra Weave.

BOOM! KABOOM! KABOOOM!

A series of explosions rocked the space. Such tremendous power pulsed through the atmosphere that one could almost feel the air itself dying and never being replenished. Filled with qi, the sword and blade thrashed about wildly.

Up next was the third form against the Third Naraka, followed by the fourth form versus the Fourth Naraka. 

A battle of sheer spirit unfolded. Were the seasoned observers of the jianghu to witness this spectacle, they would have called it a duel between the Sword God and the Blade God.

Yet, all beginnings eventually come to an end. The fight was decided by the fourth collision of ultimate techniques.

SWISH!

The Evil Blade shot out a dozen deadly streams of blade qi, weaving them into a net. Instead of defending himself, however, Crimson Tiger boldly charged into the lethal trap, closing the distance in the blink of an eye.

SNAP!

Soul Crusher shredded the net of blade qi, carving out a small opening.

SPLURT!

Blood spurted from Crimson Tiger's left shoulder as he dived through the gap. His wound was so deep that it exposed bone, but he succeeded in breaking through the barrier and immediately rushed at the Evil Blade.

The Evil Blade was caught off guard, but he stood his ground.

"This is the end!" he howled, bringing his blade crashing down upon the advancing Crimson Tiger. 

Crimson Tiger smirked. This was exactly the panicked mistake he had hoped his opponent would make.

SWOOOSH! SLICE!

The Evil Blade's weapon dug into the flesh of Crimson Tiger's right chest.

SWISH! CRACK!

And a faint whistling of wind accompanied the cracking of bones.

THUD! THUMP!

The two men tumbled to the ground together.

"Kueeegh!" Crimson Tiger coughed, spitting out blood. Seeing the gaping wound in his chest and shoulder, he hurriedly sealed his acupoints to stem the bleeding.

Gasping through the pain, he forcefully cranked his head to the side. His injuries were far worse than he anticipated, but what about his opponent...?

He was fortunate. The Evil Blade lay dead next to him, his head twisted at an impossibly unnatural angle.

At the moment of their final clash, he had successfully snaped the bastard's neck with the Thirteen Shadowless Maneuvers.

"Hoo... fwoo..." Crimson Tiger took a deep breath to steady himself, but his vision remained blurred. To make matters worse, he sensed people approaching.

He tried to get up, only to discover that his body wouldn't obey him. Giving up, he gazed into the distance, silently praying that Yeon was heeding his instructions to steer clear of the battlefield.

*Phew... I don't sense her nearby,* he thought in relief. 

If Yeon had been close by, she would have revealed herself to save him out of worry, sabotaging his plan.

STEP, STEP...

The last thing Crimson Tiger sensed before losing consciousness was the sharp auras of several peak experts.
