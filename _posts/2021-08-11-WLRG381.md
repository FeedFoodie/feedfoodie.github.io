Contact with Yana was gone.

The fifteen days she had spoken of had long since passed, and a cold dread began to seep into him. Had something happened to her? What could possibly have gone wrong during her ritual with the Demonic Spirit? Was it simply taking longer than expected, or had someone attacked her, but who could possibly attack Yana, the Nine-Tailed Fox who stood at the very pinnacle of Urmuri's Yokai?

He had even given her the bracelet, a lifeline to send and receive signals. If she were in danger, surely she would have used it.

*Instant death, with no time to react... No, that's impossible.*

A monster capable of overpowering a Yokai of Yana's caliber simply did not exist. He considered Jeniela, but even she, for all her strength, could not have killed Yana as swiftly as she had Cheongmyeong.

His thoughts circled back to the Demonic Spirit, and with them came the chilling echo of Laplace's warning.

Avoid meeting the Demonic Spirit.

Absolutely do not go there.

If you meet the Demonic Spirit, you may be swept up into a compulsory fate by it.

He distrusted the Demonic Spirit, which couldn't leave its pavilion. Wiji Hoyeon was under its protection, but the spirit itself remained confined to the Hyujael Mountains. He could not go there to find Yana, not when it might be precisely what the Demonic Spirit intended. Nor could he ask anyone else to walk into such an unknown danger. He would not push them into that trap.

Besides, there was no time.

Jeniela was coming.

Rumors had already reached him of a Ghost Ship crossing the West Sea, a vessel shrouded in red mist that leaped over the waves at a terrifying speed. There was no need to question the truth of such a bizarre tale. A Ghost Ship meant the Death Knight's vessel, and the red mist... Lee Sungmin covered his face with both hands. After crossing the West Sea, its destination could only be here.

The Fairy Forest was not far from the port city, and after confirming the rumors with Gries through Lloyd, he knew the truth. Jeniela was on that ship. By his calculations, she would arrive in two days. He had been prepared for this, remaining in the forest to secure a favorable battlefield and lure her in. The night of her arrival would be a full moon.

He had made every preparation he could think of. He had struck at Trabia and Cheped, gathering the vampires of Trabia in one place to bind them with a Sealing. It was not a particularly powerful spell, but it would be enough. Even if it barely lasted a year, it meant Jeniela could not reclaim the power of her subordinates. At the very least, she could not grow stronger. He had even considered bringing in Namgung Heewon and Jihak but ultimately decided against it. Cheongmyeong had died so futilely at her hands, a master of the Transcendent Realm rendered powerless before the Vampire Queen.

Just yesterday, upon confirming Jeniela's approach, he had sought out Denir, the God of Time. He had gone to ask for training in the Mental World one last time, a perfect way to use the short time he had left, but Denir had refused him. The god's authority could only grant him access once. No matter how much he begged, a second entry was impossible.

Impatience, anxiety, and fear churned within him. Lee Sungmin clenched and unclenched his sweaty palms. This was nothing like the fight with the Spirit Queen. Then, he had prepared a battlefield that was definitively to his advantage. Now, he faced a defenseless, Advented Spirit Queen versus a Vampire Queen who was choosing the time of the full moon for her attack. He knew the terrifying extent of Jeniela's power. Even the mighty Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had admitted the outcome of a fight with her would be uncertain, and the Sword Immortal himself had been murdered by her hand.

"Junior Disciple."

He chewed on his lower lip, running the same simulation he had repeated countless times in his head.

When the fight with Jeniela begins.

If it were only Jeniela, they could all combine their strength and attack her together, but such a convenience was a fool's dream. She was not coming alone. Gemini, Kun, Chen, Ju Won... and behind them, an army of Death Knights and Liches. He wasn't overly concerned with the leaderless Death Knights and Liches. They had Teresa, a natural enemy to the Undead, who had already erected a barrier around the forest's outskirts. Her powerful holy magic would reduce any invading Undead to ash.

Fortunately, he had already dealt with Trabia and Cheped, so there would be no reinforcements of Lycanthropes or Vampires. He had Sealed the fifth vampire, Rao Xian, in Trabia and killed Nero in Cheped. Victory or defeat would be decided here.

"Junior Disciple."

The basic strategy was a siege, making full use of Teresa's barrier. This forest was within Osla's reach. The minor Undead and Yokai would be annihilated passing through the barrier, but Jeniela would remain unscathed. The high-ranking vampires, Chen and Kun, would be met by Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight. He had already made the request. The Spear King would face Ju Won. Lloyd and Scarlet would use the magic traps scattered throughout the forest to keep the surviving Liches, Death Knights, and Yokai in check.

*But who stops Gemini? Me? That damn bitch.*

Lee Sungmin muttered a curse, grabbing his hair. Her attitude was too ambiguous. If she had truly turned on Jeniela, he could have counted on her help, but Gemini always walked a fine line, handing over Baek Sogo and offering information while never fully committing to betrayal.

*I have to stop Gemini, but Jeniela... what about Jeniela? Do I finish my fight quickly and go to her? Alone? No, if Osla can just hold her for a moment... then I can join, and with help from the others...*

However, what guarantee was there that everyone would win their fights? Chen and Kun were not that strong in his eyes, but vampires grew more powerful under a full moon. What were the chances of Baek Sogo and the Black Dragon Knight losing? Of the Spear King falling to Ju Won? What if Scarlet and Lloyd failed to hold back the Undead and Yokai?

*What if I lose to Gemini?*

What if he died? He felt every hair on his body stand on end. 'If I'm with you, it's a dog's death.' Frau's words echoed in his mind, a venomous whisper promising that an egg thrown at a rock would surely shatter, that a variable like him could never change the great flow of fate.

[Calm down.]

The Void Monarch's voice thundered in his head, but he could not heed it.

*I can't fail. As long as Wiji Hoyeon is trapped, I'm the only one who can do this. I told her to trust me.*

[I said calm down, you fucking idiot!] the Void Monarch roared. [How many times are you going to have the same thought? Why do you keep putting such a useless, unbearable weight on yourself?]

*Because there's no one else.*

[So what if you fail? It can't be helped. If you think it's hopeless, just run away. As long as you're alive, you can plan for the next time!]

*There is no next time. The End is constantly approaching.*

[Oh, for fuck's sake. How is that gloomy and Despair-filled mindset supposed to help?]

"It does help," Lee Sungmin said aloud, lowering the hands that covered his face. "...Because it makes me desperate."

It forced him to use every card he had. He slowly turned his head and saw Baek Sogo sitting beside him, her face etched with worry.

"I'm sorry, Senior Sister," he said. "I was lost in thought and didn't hear you."

"Are... are you alright?" she asked, her voice soft. "Junior Disciple, your face..."

"I'm fine." He raised a hand to his own cheek. The skin was as cold as a corpse's. "Senior Sister," he called her name while caressing his own chilly skin.

Baek Sogo didn't seem to know what to say. It wasn't just him. Scarlet was pacing nervously, rechecking the magic traps she had laid. He had already sent the mages from the nearby Magic Tower away. Teresa was praying continuously to a god from her old world, a god that did not even exist here.

Please, let no one die.

Please, let us overcome this crisis together.

The Black Dragon Knight stood guard by her side, while a haggard Lloyd repeatedly opened and closed Gries. Only the Spear King seemed to relish the coming battle, calmly polishing his spear.

"Don't you want to run away?" Lee Sungmin asked suddenly.

Baek Sogo's head whipped around at the question. She met his gaze with unwavering eyes. "We can't run away."

"You're right." In truth, she was not so different from the Spear King. Both of them were twisted and broken. If the Spear King was mad for battle, Baek Sogo was mad for her beliefs. She felt fear, of course. He still remembered the sound of her crying in his arms, but no matter how scared she was, she would not run.

"Still," he said. "If, by some chance, you have to escape... then please, run."

"That won't happen."

"I don't want to see you die, Senior Sister," he muttered, forcing a smile. The stiff muscles of his cheeks barely cooperated, managing only to lift the corners of his mouth.

"...Junior Disciple, couldn't we ask for help even now? From the Murim Alliance, or the Mages Guild..."

"They'd be no help." It wasn't even worth considering. "The Murim Alliance, the Mages Guild... if I put my mind to it, I could annihilate them all myself. I just choose not to. If I rode a Fairy Horse to Kron, I could conquer the city in a day. The same goes for the Mages Guild."

"......"

"Besides, there's no guarantee they'd cooperate. Even if the world were ending tomorrow, there are always people who value their own lives above all else, aren't there?"

"...Even so, there must be people who would act for the big picture."

"And those people would be weak."

Baek Sogo could not refute his logic. Lee Sungmin looked up at the sky. "Senior Sister, I'm certain that we, gathered here in this forest, are the most elite group assembled to stop The End. It doesn't get any better than this. Our enemy is a legion of the inhuman, and any weak helpers who came would only become more vampires and undead for us to kill."

A dark chuckle escaped him. "It's fucked up, isn't it? I'm not running away either. I can't. I told Wiji Hoyeon to trust me, that I could do it. So I have to. Right now, there's no one else."

"...Junior Disciple."

"I'm fine," Lee Sungmin said, turning away. "So... you don't have to worry about me anymore."

He walked away without waiting for her reply, a heavy weight in every step. The words he couldn't bring himself to say swirled in his chest.

*The most frightening thing is.*

He raised his hands to cover his face again.

*More than dying... what if, after I defeat Jeniela, after I finally get rid of her... what if there's another 'next'? That's what I'm most afraid of.*

What on earth could come after this? What else had been arranged to destroy this world? Kim Jonghyun, the Slaughter Predator, the Dungeon, the Spirit Queen, the Vampire Queen... what the hell was next?

Will a real Demon Lord Advent? Or will the world just be destroyed by a great earthquake, a flood... something like that?

If such a catastrophe were to try and destroy the world, how in the world would I stop it?

[Don't worry about things that haven't happened yet,] the Void Monarch spat out in an annoyed tone.

Still, even the Void Monarch, who said such a thing, was worried that it might really happen.
