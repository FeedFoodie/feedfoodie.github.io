A crowd of beggars from the Beggars' Gang had gathered at the entrance of Eunru, just as Dmitri had intended. The man Lee Sungmin had just killed had planned to use the high-class brothel in the heart of Kron as the stage for a major disturbance. He had hoped the beggars would be the first to swarm the place, creating a chaotic mess that would cover his escape.

The fatal flaw in Dmitri's plan, however, was the overwhelming difference in skill between himself and Lee Sungmin. He had been subdued so swiftly that he had no time to create the opening he needed to flee.

As Lee Sungmin walked out of the annex, he glanced back at the fallen mercenaries. It was, to borrow Yana's words, a mysterious phenomena. Though he had gotten a feel for his so-called trash skills in Legro Forest, this was the first time he had used them so directly, and he was inwardly satisfied with their undeniable power.

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had called it 'directionality', a state achieved upon entering the Transcendent Realm where one's martial arts reached a level far beyond human understanding. It was a matter of how one chose to cultivate that power. For the Chairman, the path was speed, and its conclusion was the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. By inheriting the Chairman's power, Lee Sungmin had also accepted the martial theory behind it. While he was not yet on his master's level, the Extreme Speed he had pursued was now ingrained within him.

However, it did not stop there. Lee Sungmin was faithfully moving forward with his own directionality, and the use of his mysterious phenomena was a part of that journey.

"Your captain's dead in the room on the top floor," Lee Sungmin announced to the gathered mercenaries. Their drunkenness had faded, replaced by pale faces and the sound of them swallowing hard. He spoke again as he walked past them.

"Bury him or burn him. Do as you please."

With those words, he opened the main gate of Eunru. The overwhelming stench of the beggars waiting beyond immediately assaulted them, causing Yana to wrinkle her nose. Baek Sogo sighed, averting her gaze.

The Beggars' Gang was a faction that pursued justice. Although she had parted ways with Chwi Geol, her favorable impression of the group remained, for she pursued the same ideals.

"You are…?" one of the beggars standing near Lee Sungmin asked, his gaze scrutinizing.

Lee Sungmin answered honestly. "I am the Phantom Spear."

His calm declaration stunned every beggar present. They had all heard the rumors, but that was not the only reason for their surprise. Most of them had been in Kron ten years ago when the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had mercilessly crushed their gang.

"The… Phantom… Spear…? Why are you here…?"

"I had a personal grudge against the Mercenary King, and I've just finished settling it."

A personal grudge. The beggar who had spoken was at a loss for words, swallowing hard at the claim that the matter was settled. The Mercenary King was a figure so powerful that not even the Murim Alliance could touch him easily, not only because of his martial prowess but because most mercenaries admired him and wished to be like him. Among their kind, the name Mercenary King was like the heavens.

"The Mercenary King is…?"

"Dead," Lee Sungmin stated, his expression unchanging. To him, Dmitri was not even worth being called a giant. "I killed him because I had a reason to. The disturbance is over, and I have no intention of causing any more trouble. So, would you please step aside?"

He asked politely, but the gathered beggars only hesitated, their ranks holding firm.

Lee Sungmin spoke once more. "I can do the same thing my Master did to you in the past. I'm prepared for it, but it seems you are not. You haven't even brought enough people to perform the Dog Beating Stick Formation."

At his words, the beggars' shoulders trembled. Humiliation, anger, and fear warred on their faces.

"I'll say it again. I have no desire to fight. However, I have no intention of going around you either. So… open a path. If you don't, I'll have no choice but to force my way through."

That was all it took. A path slowly opened. Baek Sogo watched the retreating beggars with a bitter expression before her eyes turned to Lee Sungmin. Just as she had changed, so had he.

She knew it for certain. Her Junior Disciple was no longer the boy from Mount Mushii fifteen years ago, who could not even protect his own life and had collapsed in shame before the Sword Phantom he himself had killed. He was no longer the young man who had pushed her back in the Doppelganger Dungeon, declaring he had come to save her, nor the one who became the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's disciple, sharing drinks and listening to stories, and he certainly was not the monster who had lost his sanity and rampaged through Germud.

*And I.*

*Have I changed?*

Baek Sogo found it difficult to answer herself. Her conviction had always been to eradicate evil from the world, but that conviction had shattered when she learned that the world she wished to save was destined for ruin, and so, she had fallen into despair, feeling that everything she had done was meaningless, yet now, she was here, walking behind her one and only Junior Disciple.

*Ah.*

Following Lee Sungmin, Baek Sogo lifted her eyes to the sky. Saving the world and changing fate. That was the big picture her Junior Disciple desired, and she was here because he had asked her to be. No, that was not it.

It was just… it felt good. To be going somewhere with him like this. To be walking behind him. To rise from despair at his words and to do what he wished, together.

[You must become a dagger.]

The words she had heard several times sharpened their edge deep in her heart, hiding themselves even deeper.

Lee Sungmin passed through the path the beggars had made and headed straight for the Murim Alliance in the center of Kron. The distance was thankfully short.

"Are you planning to leave the surveillance as it is?" Yana asked.

"There's no need to get rid of them," Lee Sungmin replied with an indifferent expression. He was not going there to fight, so there was no need to be unnecessarily hostile.

As they approached, the Murim Alliance headquarters came into view. Ten years ago, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had nearly destroyed its largest pavilion, but the building now stood intact, likely repaired over the past decade. The main gate stood wide open, revealing the courtyard beyond, which was filled with martial artists standing in formation.

"The Phantom Spear."

A solemn voice called out to him. Lee Sungmin looked toward the middle-aged man standing at the front of the formation. The man only looked middle-aged but was likely much older, and he was draped in dirty rags. It was a face Lee Sungmin remembered. He stopped walking and stared.

Martial Beggar. The current Murim Alliance Chairman.

Ten years ago, he had been the leader of the Beggars' Gang. After the Black Dragon Knight's disappearance, he had passed the position to his disciple, Chwi Geol, and ascended to become the Chairman of the Murim Alliance.

"It's been a long time. Do you remember me?"

"…Of course, I remember," Martial Beggar muttered. He had never forgotten the events of ten years ago for even a moment. The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, the man who had brought shame to him, the Beggars' Gang, and the entire orthodox murim, was long dead. To think that after a decade, he would encounter his disciple in such a way.

"…I heard you killed the Mercenary King."

"I had my reasons. It's a personal matter, so I'd rather not discuss it in a place like this."

"Why… have you come here?"

The faces of the Murim Alliance warriors were filled with tension and fear. The young man they faced had single-handedly defeated the Death Lord and the Demon Lord ten years ago. He was also the sole disciple of the Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, the evil faction's strongest at the time, the man who had shattered the Dog Beating Stick Formation and laid waste to this very alliance.

"I've come to ask for a favor," Lee Sungmin stated emphatically. "I have no intention of fighting."

He continued, "The former Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight. Ten years ago, my Master kidnapped him. It was so sudden that his belongings must have been left untouched in the office he was using. I doubt you would have incinerated everything. I would like to receive one of those items."

"……"

"It's a personal matter." Lee Sungmin deliberately did not mention that it was to find the Black Dragon Knight, thinking Martial Beggar would not cooperate if he knew the reason.

"…It's all personal matters, I see. Let me ask you, Phantom Spear. Why should I… no, why should the Murim Alliance cooperate with these personal matters of yours?"

"Because if you don't cooperate, I'll have no choice but to use force."

At those words, Martial Beggar's eyebrow twitched, and the rags draped over his body swelled up.

"Do you think I will tolerate such an insult?"

"Think rationally," Lee Sungmin said, shaking his head.

RUMBLE…

The sound of a thunderclap echoed around him as a writhing, violet energy spread out and coiled through the air. The Demonic Energy was drawn out, and the mysterious phenomena stirred. The eyes of Martial Beggar and the other Murim Alliance warriors widened. It felt as if something invisible was tightly squeezing their hearts.

"I can do the same thing my Master did. I assure you, among the Nine Sects One Gang, no one besides the Sword Immortal can stop me. Do you understand what that means? I can kill every single one of you, right here, right now."

The warning, delivered in a low voice, was incredibly blatant.

"Do you know why I'm not doing it? It's simple. It's because I don't want to fight you, and I don't want to kill you. That's why I'm asking for your cooperation. Before I decide I have to do something I don't want to do."

"Kuh…!"

Blood trickled from the corner of Martial Beggar's mouth as he tried to resist the oppressive force. It was true that he was a figure of high standing in the orthodox murim, and it was also true that he stood at the edge of the Supreme Peak, but that was all. To Lee Sungmin, who could handle even a Transcendent Realm master like a child, the thousands of warriors blocking his path were nothing more than a swarm of ants. Their levels were just too different.

"I."

Baek Sogo stepped forward and removed the human skin mask she was wearing. At the sight of her face, Martial Beggar's eyes widened. He was not the only one. Many others recognized her.

"I am Baek Sogo, also known by the title Silent Flash."

"The… Silent Flash. Why are you…?"

"I am the Senior Sister of the Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin, who is here with me," Baek Sogo revealed with a calm expression. Astonishment filled the faces of everyone who knew her. Not many people knew they were martial siblings. At most, only those who had acted alongside her in the Doppelganger Dungeon ten years ago knew. Chwi Geol had never revealed the fact, not even to his Master.

"I swear on everything I have built up until now under the title of Silent Flash. My Junior Disciple is not asking this for any evil purpose. So I beg of you."

Baek Sogo bowed her head deeply. Watching her, Martial Beggar let out a hollow laugh. The disciple of Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon and the righteous Silent Flash Baek Sogo were martial siblings. He looked at Lee Sungmin with gloomy eyes as the young man approached the bowing Baek Sogo and shook his head.

"Senior Sister. You don't have to do that."

"But, Junior Disciple…"

"I was just being a little forceful with my words. I have no intention of killing them. If I did something like that, you would come to despise me, Senior Sister."

Baek Sogo only smiled bitterly and did not answer. At those words, Martial Beggar felt even more hollow. For such a simple reason… He closed his eyes.

"…The Demon Emperor… created a monster," he sighed. The Phantom Spear, Lee Sungmin, should be in his mid-thirties by now. To have such martial prowess at that age was unthinkable. When he had heard that the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had died, and that the Phantom Spear had died as well, he thought there would be no one of note left in the unorthodox sects. He had even thought the Blood Demon, who had appeared out of nowhere, would not be that remarkable.

He was wrong.

The dead Demon Emperor's disciple, who was thought to be dead, was alive and had become a tremendous monster. A sense of bitterness washed over Martial Beggar as he turned around.

"I will trust the words of the Silent Flash."

He felt grateful to her for stepping forward. Thanks to her vow and plea, he could at least save some face.

"Wait here for a moment."

Martial Beggar walked away with heavy steps.
