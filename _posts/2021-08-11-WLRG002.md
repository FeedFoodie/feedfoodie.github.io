Lee Sungmin remembered the things he could have had but never did. Such memories were vivid, often replaying in his mind several times before he fell asleep. *What if I had gotten that chance?* There was no end to these thoughts, and the Lee Sungmin of his past life, a pathetic Classless from the start, had always been thirsty for an opportunity.

In his memory, three distinct chances to improve his damn situation had appeared in Zenavis during his thirteen years of struggle. This knowledge allowed him to quickly set his immediate goals.

The first thing he had to secure was the Martial Skeleton.

A Martial Skeleton was, in simple terms, a skeletal structure that made learning martial arts remarkably easy. Since the past Lee Sungmin had learned martial arts, he thought it best to continue down that path to make use of his experience. A Classless already had the characteristic of learning most skills quickly, but obtaining a Martial Skeleton would accelerate that speed even further. To get ahead in the competition for the opportunities to come, securing it had to be his top priority.

The condition for acquiring a Martial Skeleton was simple. He must not learn any skills. It was a condition that was both incredibly easy and profoundly difficult, because while some skills seemed hard to obtain, others could be acquired with startling ease. If Lee Sungmin were to kill three people, for instance, he would gain the 'Murder' skill.

Currently, he had no skills, so the condition was met. Lee Sungmin crossed the street, retracing the steps of his past life as best he could. He felt a renewed sense of gratitude for his own envious nature.

*Found it.*

Recovery Bone Setting Clinic. Lee Sungmin looked up at the worn-out sign. Thirteen years ago, the fighting spirit Lee Sungmin had rushed to this very clinic as soon as he heard the rumors. He had begged for the 'procedure', offering all the money he had, only to be refused because he failed to meet the conditions.

He remembered how unfair it had felt back then.

"I'm here for the bone structure modification procedure."

The Recovery Bone Setting Clinic was empty. In his memory, the clinic had just opened and would soon be filled with customers after some lucky Classless received the procedure and the word got out. That rush would only last for half a year. After six months, the clinic would close its doors for good when a martial artist, resentful for being denied the procedure, murdered the doctor.

"...Bone structure modification procedure?"

An old man with a wrinkled face stared at Lee Sungmin with surprise. It was an understandable reaction. A boy who wasn't even fully grown had just walked in and asked for a bone structure modification procedure, something that was not yet known to the public.

"......"

"...Where on earth did you hear about this? I haven't told anyone..." the old man muttered.

*I know.* The first man to receive the procedure had gotten it by pure chance. He had a stiff neck and wandered into the clinic, where the doctor offered to perform it for free just to see if it would work. Of course, once the effects became known, he started charging the Classless who came seeking it out.

"You can do it, right?" Lee Sungmin asked, staring intently at the old man's face.

The old man still looked confused but gave a hesitant nod.

"...Haha... I never thought it would happen like this. You can keep your money. This is the first time I'm performing this on a person. In return, as for the side effects..."

"I don't care."

There were no side effects. When Lee Sungmin answered without a moment's hesitation, the old man slowly nodded his head.

"But, kid. The bone structure modification procedure isn't something you can get just because you want it. Are you... well... are you Classless? You don't have any other skills, do you?"

"I'm Classless, and I don't have any skills."

Thirteen years ago, he had questioned it. *Why are there conditions when he could just do it?*

He had learned the reason back then. Skills changed the body, and a body that had undergone such a change could not receive the bone structure modification procedure. That did not apply to the current Lee Sungmin. He was a blank slate, having learned no skills at all.

"Follow me."

The old man locked the clinic's front door and guided Lee Sungmin to a room in the back.

"Take off your clothes and lie down on here."

Lee Sungmin undressed without hesitation. With his clothes off, he could clearly see how weak his current body was. It had little muscle, and the absence of the scars that had once covered his body felt incredibly strange.

*This is the beginning.* Lee Sungmin controlled his breathing and lay down on the bed. First, the Martial Skeleton. After that, he would focus on seizing the other missed opportunities he remembered.

"This will hurt a little."

The old man rolled up his sleeves and picked up a needle. *Pain is something I'm used to.* Lee Sungmin smirked and closed his eyes.

The procedure was bound to be painful. It forcibly created a Martial Skeleton by completely twisting his existing bone structure. Having his bones reshaped into a different form while he was still alive was, as the old man had warned, extremely agonizing.

Still, Lee Sungmin endured it all without a single scream. He actually felt a sense of joy in the pain. With this, he was now at a different starting line than he was thirteen years ago. It was still a start that lagged behind those who had learned martial arts or magic from the beginning, but it was a starting line that was clearly ahead of other Classless.

[You have acquired a Low-Grade Martial Skeleton!]

When the pain finally subsided, a message echoed in his head. Lee Sungmin immediately opened his status window to check the 'Low-Grade Martial Skeleton' he had just obtained.

>Name: Lee Sungmin
>
>Class: Classless
>
>Skills:
>
> - Low-Grade Martial Skeleton.

A skeleton for learning martial arts. It is not a particularly great skeleton, but you can learn martial arts faster than a mortal.

"It's done," the old man said.

Lee Sungmin regulated his breathing and sat up. Low-Grade Martial Skeleton. As the description suggested, it was not a particularly great one. For a Classless, however, it was desperately needed. A Martial Skeleton was a very difficult skill to obtain in the first place. When the rapid growth correction of a Classless was combined with the martial arts correction from the skeleton, even a low-grade one could produce an effect of intermediate grade or higher.

"It was a success. It's the first time I've tried it on a living person... Haha! I won't take your money. Instead, make sure to spread the word properly."

The old man grinned widely. Now that he had confirmed its success, he was probably planning to make a fortune. Lee Sungmin knew, however, that the old man would be dead in six months, killed by the sword of an angry martial artist who could not get the procedure.

"I understand."

He did not bother telling the old man this fact. Even if he did, the old man would not listen to a fourteen-year-old boy. He felt no great loyalty that compelled him to speak up, and he knew the old man would not be persuaded anyway. Even if Lee Sungmin kept his mouth shut, someone else would eventually get the procedure here, and the rumors would spread regardless.

*What if I killed the old man instead?* Then the Martial Skeleton procedure would cease to exist in Zenavis. He would be the only one to have benefited.

*No, that's not right. There's no need to kill him.* Lee Sungmin smiled with a trace of bitterness. He was not so rotten as to murder someone who had done him no harm. In fact, to him, the old man was a benefactor.

He would just ignore it.

"Thank you for your hard work."

He left the bone-setting clinic. His body felt a little stiff when he first got up from the bed, but the stiffness soon lessened, and he could feel his body becoming lighter with each passing moment.

That was one. In Zenavis, Lee Sungmin had obtained one of the opportunities he could have had. Now, it was time to get the second.

*I have to go to the old bookstore.*

He had created the basic vessel with the Martial Skeleton, but the vessel was still empty. In truth, Lee Sungmin had the means to fill it right away. He clearly remembered the second-rate martial arts he had learned thirteen years ago. If he recited the incantations now, he would obtain a second-rate inner arts technique called the Spiritual Meditation Art. It was the technique he had been forced to learn back then, and the very one that had always held him back.

An inner arts technique was the study of accumulating inner energy, the most fundamental aspect of martial arts. The problem was that the Spiritual Meditation Art contained a harsh limitation. To discard it and learn a new one, he would have to either abandon all the inner arts he had accumulated or master the technique to its absolute peak. While not all techniques had such extreme restrictions, his old one certainly did.

His achievement in the Spiritual Meditation Art in his past life was eight-star. Since it was a path he had already walked, he felt he could quickly reach that same level again, but Lee Sungmin already knew where to find a superior replacement.

The Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. This technique was hidden in a small old bookstore right here in Zenavis. It had been discovered a week after he first arrived in his past life, and when he learned it had been here all along, he had trembled all night with envy.

The Heavenly Truth Meditation Art was a first-rate technique. The speed at which it accumulated inner arts was far faster than his old method, but its true wonder was something else entirely. This technique did not have the crippling restrictions like most others. This meant he could switch to another inner arts technique at any time. 

In his past life, he had spent ten years on the Spiritual Meditation Art to barely reach the eighth star. If he learned the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art, he could switch to an even better technique whenever he found one, without having to master it first.

"Found it...!"

After searching the shelves of the old bookstore for an hour, Lee Sungmin finally found what he was looking for. His body trembled with emotion as he turned the pages to check the contents. There was no mistake. It was the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. In his past life, he never even knew such a treasure existed in this dusty old shop, but not anymore.

*I can do this.* Lee Sungmin hugged the book to his chest and paid for it. The owner of the bookstore seemed completely unaware of its value, so Lee Sungmin was able to buy the first-rate technique for almost nothing.

He still had money left. The wallet he had taken from the man he killed earlier still had a considerable amount of cash in it.

*In terms of time... is it a year from now?*

Of the three opportunities he had missed in Zenavis, he had now obtained two. He had the Martial Skeleton and the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. The next opportunity was a year away.

Lee Sungmin opened his wallet and counted the remaining money. Sixty thousand Eru. Even the cheapest inn in Zenavis charged twenty thousand Eru for one night, and a person could not live on sleep alone. Including food and drink, it would cost at least thirty thousand Eru a day. The money he had was only enough to last for two days at most.

*First, I need to make some money.*
