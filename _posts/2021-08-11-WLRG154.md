Wiji Hoyeon stared intently at the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's face, unable to fully grasp his words. A faint displeasure stirred within her, born from the realization that he knew something about Lee Sungmin that she did not. Though she fought to conceal it, she felt her eyes grow colder than usual as they rested on him.

"What's with that look?" the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman asked with a smirk. "Are you jealous?"

At his question, Wiji Hoyeon unconsciously raised a hand to her cheek. Jealousy? She muttered the word in a small voice, a concept she had never once been conscious of in her entire life.

"...Of course not."

"That's what it looks like to this one. Hoho! It must be because you're in your prime. Youth is a wonderful thing, isn't it?"

The Chairman chuckled to himself as if he found something immensely amusing, and Wiji Hoyeon found she disliked his laughter. Even as she denied the feeling of jealousy, she knew she did not like the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

"Why are you wearing a mask?" she asked.

"Isn't it cool? It's just a mask, but it can give off a mysterious impression without any special effort. I'm quite fond of props like this."

"You wear a mask for such a simple reason?"

"Yes. I wear a mask for just such a reason. To appear mysterious to others. Unlike a face, a mask is easy to change."

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman raised a hand to his mask. When he pulled it away, a different one covered his face, this one modeled after a hideous beast.

"This is called face-changing. Isn't it interesting?"

The mask shifted with every pass of the Chairman's hand, the motion so swift that even Wiji Hoyeon's eyes could not follow. She knew that even at her peak, with her martial arts fully unleashed, she would have been unable to see through his technique.

"You don't seem to find it interesting."

Seeing Wiji Hoyeon's lukewarm reaction, the Chairman returned to his original half-mask. He crossed his arms and let his shoulders slump in a dejected manner, though she continued to watch him with wary eyes.

"You seem to be quite fond of that kid," he remarked.

"...He's my friend."

"Is it loyalty? To this one, it seems like there's more to it than that. Well, that's none of this one's business."

The Chairman muttered in a laughing voice. Wiji Hoyeon shot him a glare, as if trying to pierce through the porcelain hiding his face.

"...What... are you planning to do with him?"

"I'm thinking of taking him as my disciple," the Chairman answered without hesitation.

The words were shocking. Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon, the strongest of the evil faction and the man at the pinnacle of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, was offering to take Lee Sungmin as his disciple. Wiji Hoyeon knew that if such an opportunity were offered anywhere else in Eria, people would flock to him like clouds.

"Why would you...?"

"Didn't I tell you? This one prefers a hardworking mediocrity over a calamity who does well on his own. As it happens, this one has no children and no disciples. If the boy wishes it, I plan to take him as my disciple and have him inherit everything."

Wiji Hoyeon fell silent. Becoming the Chairman's disciple was an incredible, miraculous encounter, yet for some reason, a bitter taste formed in a corner of her heart. The foundation of Lee Sungmin's martial arts was the Violet Dawn Divine Art and the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, techniques she herself had taught him. They were excellent martial arts, even in her eyes, but the Chairman's exclusive technique was bound to be superior. If Lee Sungmin learned such a world-shaking divine art, wouldn't he discard the skills she had given him?

*That's impossible.*

The thought was excessive. He would never abandon the techniques that had become second nature to him, the very foundation of his power, not even for some amazing new divine art, but even as she reasoned with herself, the anxiety and displeasure did not fade. Wiji Hoyeon glared at the Chairman, who received her gaze with a smile.

"Do you know?" the Chairman murmured. "You, with your absurd level of talent, would never know. The ten years that kid lived just to be acknowledged by you, to stand equal with you... you will never understand that."

"What are you talking about...!"

"I'd like to tell you, but that's not my role. If you want to hear it, ask the kid yourself."

The Chairman rose to his feet. Wiji Hoyeon reached out to grab him, but before her hand could make contact, his body dissipated into a fine mist.

"Next time, we shall meet in my true body, not a clone."

The sound of his chuckles lingered inside the carriage long after he was gone.

The Black Hound Leader's attire was neat. He was a man who preached about the hungry spirit, but even that nonsense had to be tempered with consideration for the situation. Within his own squad, no one dared to point out his conduct, so he could act as he pleased. Here, however, was different. He had been forced to take a bath, which he hated, and had even combed his hair well. He also wore the cleanest and finest clothes he owned.

"The Phantom Spear is in Devon," he said.

"I am aware," replied Silver Flying Dragon Jaegal Taeryeong.

Jaegal Taeryeong had not yet become the head of the Jaegal Clan, but his father, the Patriarch, had already entrusted all of the clan's external affairs to him. It was for this reason that the Black Hound Leader now sat face-to-face with the clan's heir.

"The Little Heavenly Demon's whereabouts are unknown, but we can't just leave the Phantom Spear alone, not after he killed the Iron Armored Divine Spear. We already contacted you about this through the Murim Alliance, but..."

Jaegal Taeryeong slowly nodded his head. Though he had not yet ascended to the position of patriarch, he already exuded the air of a distinguished master. It was said his personal martial prowess had reached the Supreme Peak. The Black Hound Leader continued speaking while observing Jaegal Taeryeong's cold face.

"We don't wish to tarnish the Noble Clan's joyous occasion, but since the patriarch has already made a promise, by all means..."

"If he killed the great Iron Armored Divine Spear," Jaegal Taeryeong began, "then we must consider the Phantom Spear to be one of the most skilled masters among all martial artists in Eria. Is the Murim Alliance saying it will provide no assistance in killing such a man?"

"Our Black Hound Squad will..."

"What can you people possibly do?" Jaegal Taeryeong asked with a smile.

The Black Hound Leader gave an awkward smile and scratched the back of his head. In truth, he had no room to complain. Although they belonged to the Demon Extermination Unit, his Black Hound Squad was not particularly outstanding in martial strength, nor were they skilled in tracking. They had, in fact, completely failed to locate Lee Sungmin's party after they entered Devon.

"Please don't be too hard on us. It was none other than the Chairman who negotiated with the Jaegal Patriarch."

"Hoho! I know. It's just frustrating. They ask us to kill a difficult opponent like the Phantom Spear, yet the Alliance offers no support whatsoever..."

"Perhaps a secret deal was made with the patriarch?" the Black Hound Leader blurted out.

Though it was said in jest, Jaegal Taeryeong could not dismiss the words. He closed his mouth for a moment, lost in thought. His father, Jaegal Yuhwan, was a man he had lived with his entire life, yet he still did not truly know him. If his father had accepted this disadvantageous request from the Murim Alliance, there must have been a good reason for it.

"First, I will issue a search order throughout Devon."

"There's a possibility he's in disguise."

"Of course he would be. That won't be a problem. Devon has a sorcerer skilled in Taoist arts for finding people."

"...A sorcerer?" The Black Hound Leader's eyes widened. "Do you believe in things like sorcery?"

"You don't seem to know the South very well," Jaegal Taeryeong said with a smirk. "In this region, sorcerers are superior to mages in certain fields."

The Black Hound Leader looked utterly lost, but Jaegal Taeryeong didn't bother to explain. The task of hunting the Phantom Spear was a matter for the Jaegal Clan anyway. He ended the conversation and rose to his feet.

*The Tang Clan and the Namgung Clan will react as well.*

Killing the Phantom Spear would bring considerable honor. They would not attempt it alone, but this was Devon, a place practically ruled by the Jaegal Clan. Since they could coordinate with the other Noble Clans, there was no reason for the Namgung and Tang not to get involved.

Leaving the room, Jaegal Taeryeong came face to face with Moyong Seojin. Her face stiffened upon seeing him. Conscious that the door behind him had not yet closed, and that the Black Hound Leader was still watching, he stepped to the side. He put on an intimate expression and wrapped an arm around Moyong Seojin's waist.

"Let's go, my wife."

"...Yes," Moyong Seojin replied with a nod.

When she glanced up, Jaegal Taeryeong's face was ice-cold, and the arm around her waist felt stiff and unyielding. Only after they had crossed the corridor, out of the Black Hound Leader's sight, did he remove his hand. Then, without another word, he walked right past her.

Moyong Seojin silently watched his retreating back before letting out a soft sigh. She was used to such coldness. The affection between them as a married couple had long since faded. If not for the union of their clans and the child they shared, their relationship would have met a catastrophic end one way or another.

Moyong Seojin pressed a hand to her empty chest. Of all times. She looked toward the garden beyond the corridor, and her eyes met with Namgung Heewon, who was standing there blankly. She did not look away.

In the end, it was Namgung Heewon who turned his gaze.

Two days after arriving in Devon, Aladur's location was identified. Lee Sungmin didn't hesitate, bolting out of the inn with the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who had grown quite accustomed to the ways of a Hunter, following close behind.

While cooped up, Lee Sungmin had researched the sorcerer through Erebrisa. Unlike mages, sorcerers based in the South tended to avoid connecting with the network, which was precisely why he couldn't ignore Aladur. He had already tried to track the Fist Honored One through other sorcerers connected to Erebrisa, but every attempt had failed. Finding Aladur was like grasping at straws, but it was his only lead.

The man he had learned about over the past two days was even more skilled in tracking than he had thought. Though Aladur passed the time on the streets with trivial physiognomy or palm readings, it was said that when he took a proper request, he could find anything from a corpse in a vast forest to a missing person.

[There are many types of sorcery. Just like magic.]

Void Monarch muttered. He had become more talkative since they came to the South, clearly hoping to leave Devon quickly and return to his homeland.

In the back alley of a quiet street, a stray cat was meowing. Lee Sungmin and the Crazy Heavenly Demon stopped, their eyes falling on a man sitting on a straw mat. The man's build was not large, but his baggy clothes made him appear bigger than he was. Lee Sungmin scanned the surroundings, ever cautious of being pursued.

It was Aladur who spoke first. He had been sitting with his arms crossed, but now he looked up at Lee Sungmin. After a moment of staring, he grinned, revealing yellowed teeth.

"Do you want to ask this Elder for a favor?"

"...I want to find someone," Lee Sungmin said, lowering his voice.

Besides them and the cat, the alley was empty. He slowly approached Aladur, a black man with dark skin and coiled hair. The sorcerer ran a hand through his hair and smacked his lips.

"That is this Elder's specialty. As long as you pay enough, that is."

Aladur uncrossed his arms and rubbed his hands together. His materialistic attitude was actually reassuring. Lee Sungmin reached into his robes and took out a money pouch.

"What do you wish to find?"

Along with the money, he produced a bundle of hair and sat down opposite Aladur. "The owner of this hair was cursed by someone," he explained, handing it over. "I want to know the location of the one who cast the curse through this."

"That's a rather difficult request. Finding the owner of the hair isn't hard... but tracking the one who cast the curse? This isn't enough money."

"How much?"

"Ten times this."

It was not an amount he couldn't pay. When Lee Sungmin took out a much larger money pouch and set it down, Aladur broke into a wide grin.

"Then let's see what I can do."

Aladur plucked a few strands of hair, placed them between his palms, and began to rub them together. It was the first time Lee Sungmin had seen sorcery, and he watched the man's actions with curiosity. After a moment, Aladur opened his hands and raised them upwards.

Wiji Hoyeon's coiled hair floated into the air. Aladur's lips moved, chanting an incomprehensible spell. Then, with a sharp CRACKLE, the floating hair ignited and emitted a plume of black smoke.

"Vicious!" Aladur clicked his tongue.

The smoke did not disperse. It clumped together, hovering in the air. Aladur carefully reached out with both hands and caressed the black cloud, which then scattered and seeped into his nostrils.

"...A forest..." Aladur muttered, his eyes closed. "A vast forest... where... I see a tree. A very large tree... long ears... they're Elves. Their clothes are ornate. Is this the Elf Forest...?"

He murmured on, and Lee Sungmin held his breath, listening intently, but Aladur said no more. He trembled for a moment, then let out a deep breath and opened his eyes.

"This is my limit," Aladur grumbled. "The curse was cast by a considerably high-level practitioner. The Elf Forest... I know that much, but I don't know anything more."

"That's enough."

The Fist Honored One was in the Elf Forest. Knowing that was sufficient. Lee Sungmin slowly rose to his feet.

"Who are you?" a voice called from behind him.

A well-dressed man was standing at the entrance of the alley. Lee Sungmin turned his head to look at him. Jaegal Taeryeong. It had been several years, and he had only caught a glimpse of him once before, so the face was hard to recall, but the robe the man wore was embroidered with the unmistakable crest of the Jaegal Clan.

"You don't look like a scholar."

Even a complete Origin Reversal could not deceive everyone. Jaegal Taeryeong was a master who had reached the Supreme Peak, and he could faintly see through Lee Sungmin's disguise.

"You either," Lee Sungmin replied, his eyes shifting to the Crazy Heavenly Demon. "You don't look like a Hunter."

The Crazy Heavenly Demon stroked his trimmed beard. "A Hunter doesn't always have to look like a Hunter."

"Stop joking around," Jaegal Taeryeong's face contorted.
