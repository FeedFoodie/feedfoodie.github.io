"What did you say?"

Lee Sungmin's voice cut through the air as he spun around to face Wiji Hoyeon. She looked back at him with complete indifference.

"What exactly was pathetic about it?"

Finding courage from somewhere deep within, Lee Sungmin scowled and fired the question at her. After his past life, he'd obtained the Martial Skeleton and learned the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art, training in martial arts every single day. He knew he hadn't reached any remarkable level, but he'd poured every ounce of effort he could muster into his practice.

And the evaluation for all that effort was 'pathetic'. Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip and glared at Wiji Hoyeon, who met his gaze with perfect calm before speaking.

"There's too much wasted movement."

She pointed this out first, then raised her hand and extended a long finger toward the spear Lee Sungmin held.

"A spear is long. The essence of spearmanship comes from how you utilize that length, but you insisted on closing the distance with the goblin when there was no need for that. You didn't even need to use movement arts. Five steps. If you'd just moved five steps, your spear could've reached those things called goblins."

"What am I supposed to do if it lacks power..."

"Is that really so?"

Wiji Hoyeon let out a laugh.

"Those things haven't learned any external arts, and your spear isn't dull. Even if your physical strength is pathetic, you can pierce through if you strike a single point. That's what makes a spear the weapon it is."

Lee Sungmin listened to her words without retorting. Wiji Hoyeon was the first person to ever say such things to him. He'd used a spear in his past life, but no one had ever taught him proper Spear Technique.

"Of course, using a spear isn't limited to just stabbing. You can swing it to strike and bring it down to smash, but using the spear that way in a situation that doesn't call for it is what I mean by wasted movement."

Lee Sungmin let out a short groan. He'd been boiling with anger when she called him pathetic, but as Wiji Hoyeon pointed things out one by one, his defiance completely vanished.

"However, though it's full of wasted movements, it is practical. Your stance is decent enough, and the fact that you use not only the blade but also the spear shaft is commendable. Having a lot of wasted movement is unavoidable when you're self-taught."

Wiji Hoyeon nodded as she delivered her assessment.

"Your talent doesn't seem bad. Skills and whatnot... I don't really understand them, but in any case, you've been learning martial arts for a month without a Master, haven't you? Considering that, it's excellent."

*No. I've been doing it for 13 years.* Lee Sungmin's shoulders slumped.

"Let's go back..."

Lee Sungmin released a deep sigh and approached the goblins. As he pulled out each of their teeth and collected their blood in a glass bottle, Wiji Hoyeon walked up beside him.

"Go back? To where?"

"I have to go to the inn where I'm staying."

"Then where should I go?"

"Why are you asking me that?"

"Should I just follow you?"

Wiji Hoyeon asked with a smile that made Lee Sungmin's jaw drop.

"You're coming with me?"

"How about this? If you cover my room and board, I'll teach you martial arts."

At those words, Lee Sungmin's mind snapped to attention. He could learn martial arts from the Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon! This was a miraculous encounter so great it couldn't even be compared to the Star Spirit Pill he'd been aiming for at the arena.

"Y-You'll teach me martial arts?"

"Ah. Not the Heavenly Demon Divine Art. That's a martial art passed down only to the Demonic Cult's Cult Leader and Young Cult Leader. The Demonic Cult doesn't exist here, but even so, I can't teach you the Heavenly Demon Divine Art."

He wasn't hoping for such an unrivaled divine art anyway. If she offered to teach him, he would gratefully accept.

"Hehe! Isn't it a great miraculous encounter? Don't tell me you're going to refuse?"

"N-No."

Lee Sungmin swallowed hard. Wiji Hoyeon was no longer just a bratty 13-year-old who was annoying for no reason. In Lee Sungmin's eyes, she now looked like a god pushing rice right into his mouth with a spoon.

***

"Who's that?"

Lula, who had been wiping a table, asked with wide eyes as Lee Sungmin returned to the inn with Wiji Hoyeon trailing behind him. He replied with an awkward smile.

"She's an otherworlder who was summoned today."

"I am Wiji Hoyeon," she said, speaking informally to Lula right away.

Lula, who had pestered Lee Sungmin to call her 'noona', just stared blankly at Wiji Hoyeon's face with her mouth agape. It was understandable. Lee Sungmin had an ordinary face that wasn't particularly ugly or handsome, but Wiji Hoyeon was different.

*Good at martial arts, a good background, good talent, and even a good face.*

The world was so unfair. Lee Sungmin swallowed his grumbling. The 13-year-old Wiji Hoyeon was a handsome girl with a promising future. She was still young, so her frame was small and she lacked a masculine air, but in just five years, she would become a beauty acknowledged by all.

"Judging by your clothes, you're a martial artist."

Jack muttered as he walked out from the kitchen. He glanced at Lee Sungmin before continuing.

"Are you planning on having her stay at this inn?"

"Ah, yes. I'll be paying for the room."

"Doesn't matter to me. We have plenty of rooms."

The amount Lee Sungmin would have to pay would increase, but he didn't care about such things. Currently, he was paying 10,000 Eru for a long-term stay at this inn. The original price for room and board was 20,000 Eru, but Jack had taken a liking to Lee Sungmin and was only charging him half.

"The room and board is 20,000 Eru. Including yours, that's 30,000 Eru. Still, well. I'll do it for 25,000 Eru for your sake."

"Thank you!"

As Jack showed his generosity, Lee Sungmin beamed and nodded his head enthusiastically. He then took Wiji Hoyeon upstairs to the rooms. As it happened, the room next to Lee Sungmin's was empty, so Wiji Hoyeon decided to use that one.

"First, I need to know what you want to learn."

Wiji Hoyeon didn't go into her own room but came into Lee Sungmin's with him. She plopped down on the bed and stared at his face.

"First, tell me the martial arts you've learned."

"For my inner arts technique, the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art. For movement arts, One Thunder Movement Arts. For external arts, Stone Breaking Fist and Iron Skin Strong Bones. For Spear Technique, Soul Chasing Spear Art."

"The external arts are what they are. What about the incantations for the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art?"

At Wiji Hoyeon's request, Lee Sungmin lifted the mattress of his bed and took out the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art manual he'd hidden underneath. Wiji Hoyeon took the manual from him and flipped through the pages. After briefly checking the incantations, she clicked her tongue.

"As expected. It's not a very impressive inner arts technique. The only fortunate thing is that it can be learned in parallel with other inner arts techniques... hmm."

Wiji Hoyeon's brow furrowed as she asked, "What kind of martial art do you want to learn?"

"I know many martial arts. I haven't mastered them all, but I have memorized their incantations. They're all martial arts of the peak realm or higher, so whatever I teach you will be helpful to you now."

"Umm..."

How should he make use of this miraculous encounter? Lee Sungmin was lost in thought for a moment. First, he wanted to reinforce his inner arts technique. The Heavenly Truth Meditation Art he was currently learning was better than the Spiritual Meditation Art he'd learned in his past life, but if Wiji Hoyeon could teach him something better, he was more than willing to switch.

*Spear Technique... is... no. What I lack right now is inner qi.*

Fortunately, he had something in mind. Lee Sungmin lifted his bowed head and looked at Wiji Hoyeon.

"I want to learn an inner arts technique."

"I thought so."

Wiji Hoyeon chuckled.

"The Heavenly Truth Meditation Art you've learned isn't a very impressive inner arts technique. At best, it's a First-Rate inner arts technique, and I have dozens of better ones in my head. Do you know the difference between a First-Rate martial art and a peak realm martial art?"

"Insights?"

"Not wrong, but not exactly right either. The thing about insights is quite ambiguous. The difference between a First-Rate martial art and a peak realm martial art is 'kindness'. A peak realm martial art is one that's been supplemented and perfected over tens, even hundreds of years by brilliant talents. It's filled with kindness so that those who learn it later can learn and grow more easily and quickly."

Lee Sungmin could agree with that assessment.

"The Violet Dawn Divine Art should do. It can't compare to the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, but the Violet Dawn Divine Art is also a martial art worthy of being called a divine art. The speed at which inner qi accumulates is fast, and if you practice it steadily, you can even enter the Sword Energy Realm without much trouble."

After saying this, Wiji Hoyeon began to recite the incantations for the Violet Dawn Divine Art. Lee Sungmin, who had been listening silently, suddenly raised his hand.

"What is it?"

"I can't memorize it. Could you write it down for me?"

Lee Sungmin said with a bitter smile. Wiji Hoyeon clicked her tongue.

"Your memory is poor."

Despite her words, Wiji Hoyeon took the pen and notebook from Lee Sungmin and began writing down the incantations for the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

"Why didn't you ask me to teach you Spear Technique?" she asked as she wrote.

"Because what I lack is inner qi."

"You say that as if you don't lack in Spear Technique."

"I do lack, but I can't even properly digest the Spear Technique I'm learning now. Even if I learn something better, I'm not confident I could digest it."

"That is wise. I only saw you wield your spear for a moment, but at your current level, learning a better Spear Technique would just be pearls before swine."

That dose of reality was painful. After an hour of writing down the incantations for the Violet Dawn Divine Art, Wiji Hoyeon finally put down the pen. Lee Sungmin respectfully received the notebook from her with both hands.

*Should I ask her to teach me another martial art?*

The moment he was about to read the incantations for the Violet Dawn Divine Art, such a greedy thought arose in Lee Sungmin's mind. He didn't know the exact reason, but Wiji Hoyeon held a favorable view of him. It was the same now. The Violet Dawn Divine Art was a divine art that couldn't be compared to something like the Heavenly Truth Meditation Art.

If a manual for the Violet Dawn Divine Art were to circulate in Eria, a bloodbath would ensue among those who coveted it. Wiji Hoyeon had handed over such an unrivaled divine art in exchange for something as trivial as room and board.

Wiji Hoyeon's and Lee Sungmin's eyes met. His intuition, honed by 13 years of walking on eggshells, warned him not to ask for more.

"Thank you."

"As for me, I've simply paid a fair price for what I received. Well, since you're the first friend I've ever made, I threw in a generous bonus."

Wiji Hoyeon said with a bright smile.

"If you'd asked me for anything else, I would've been disappointed in you."

She added, "Because I don't think that's what a friend does."
