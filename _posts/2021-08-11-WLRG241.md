A deep regret seemed to shadow the Spear King's words. Lee Sungmin had no desire to understand the man's position, yet it was clear the Spear King had come this far driven by a pure curiosity for him and his aspirations as a martial artist. He had journeyed here not because of orders from Heaven Beyond Heaven or the Martial God, but from a sole, burning desire to cross spears with the one who had defeated the Sword Saint through skill alone.

This was the source of the Spear King's conflict. It was not the will of his masters that troubled him, but the personal regret of traveling all this way only to postpone their fight without a single clash.

Just as Lee Sungmin decided to ignore him and head for the forest, the Spear King bit his lower lip and stepped into his path.

"Wait."

"I thought you said we'd fight another time."

Urgency coiled in Lee Sungmin's gut as the Spear King blocked his way. He had no time to be held up here. The subjugation force had already entered the forest, and he had no idea what might be happening to them inside. If he was delayed and something terrible occurred, he knew he would regret it for the rest of his life.

"I know, but…"

The Spear King understood this was not a situation where either of them could fight with their full strength. He did not know how many more chances he would get to face such an outstanding spearman, and he was determined to give his all in what might be his only match against Lee Sungmin. He also grasped Lee Sungmin's predicament to some extent, knowing that a warrior could not pour their soul into a fight with a mind full of distractions. His gaze drifted toward the forest.

After a moment of deliberation, the Spear King sighed and moved aside, clearing the path.

"…Go. We'll fight next time, when we can both be at our best. I'll come find you."

Lee Sungmin answered with a nod. Though this was their first meeting, he felt the Spear King was different from the other Six Sages he had encountered. He even felt a flicker of liking for the man, a shared curiosity as a fellow spearman.

However, this was not the time for such thoughts. Lee Sungmin exploded from his spot, putting distance between himself and the Spear King in an instant.

He headed for the forest in a single, breathless rush. The considerable distance vanished in a blur, and he arrived at the edge of the woods in a heartbeat. He stopped his charge just before entering, his eyes fixed on the trees ahead. An indescribable ominousness stirred in the forest's depths. Dry branches swayed in the wind, their movements creating ghostly cries.

Lee Sungmin steadied his breathing, then slowly walked into the forest.

His senses tangled.

That was the first thing he felt as an ominous current enveloped the entire forest. The moment he breathed in the air, the senses of his entire body became jumbled. His surroundings had plunged into a darkness like the dead of night. It was clearly daytime, with the sun high in the sky, and this barren forest offered no shade to block its light, and yet… it was dark. It was as dark as walking through a pitch-black void. Lee Sungmin's vision could normally pierce any gloom, but even his eyes could not completely penetrate this one.

He could not hear a single sound. No matter how he strained his ears, there was nothing. The ominous wind he had felt just moments before entering was completely gone. His senses, which should have been razor-sharp, could not pick up anything at all.

*Magic.*

Kim Jonghyun was still in this forest. He had not run away. He was waiting for the subjugation force. *What is he after? What is this ritual he's preparing, and what did Jeniela mean when she said he would no longer be human?*

His deliberation did not last long. Finding Scarlet came first, but something was strange. Not much time had passed since the subjugation force had entered the forest. It was a force of several hundred people. Even accounting for the slight time difference, it was incomprehensible that he could not find a single trace of them.

[This Elder knows most things, but I do not know much about magic.]

*This is when I miss Lady Rubia,* Lee Sungmin thought as he scanned his surroundings. He could not feel any presence, as if such senses were completely blocked. Could the senses of a master who had entered the Transcendent Realm truly be nullified so easily? Even if Kim Jonghyun was an outstanding Black Mage… no. Perhaps this was not due to Kim Jonghyun's skill alone, but because the magic of the Grimoire, the Mages Guild's greatest treasure, was something special.

Lee Sungmin lowered his stance and examined the ground. If his senses were blocked, he would have to grasp the situation through other means. A light dusting of snow covered the frozen earth, and what he focused on were the footprints left in it.

*Footprints made a short while ago.*

There were only a few of them. The subjugation force numbered around five hundred, and these were not the tracks of so many people. At most, ten. Lee Sungmin slowly straightened up.

He looked around again. There were no other footprints. The subjugation force had definitely entered the forest from this direction. So where in the world had they all gone?

[Formations or spells… nothing of that nature exists that could cause a phenomenon of this level. This has already surpassed the realm of humans,] the Void Monarch warned.

It was just as he said.

{sep}

The great Black Mage. Even Arbes, the man called the Sage of Two Faces, could not handle this magic. The Grimoire he had stolen from the Mages Guild was filled with great magics beyond the scope of human understanding, but Arbes himself could not use them.

The Grimoire was a deadly poison to a Black Mage.

While every Black Mage dreamed of the incredible spells written in its pages, that did not mean any of them could handle its power. The magic contained within the Grimoire was too dense, too dangerous. If an ordinary Black Mage were to use it, their own black magic would likely lose control, run rampant, and cause them to self-destruct. That was why even a mage of Arbes's caliber could not easily attempt to cast its spells.

Kim Jonghyun, however, was different. He was convinced the Grimoire was destined to be his. Because his contracted Demon Lord had been annihilated, Kim Jonghyun was free from the demonic contract that bound other Black Mages. Furthermore, he had absorbed a portion of the Demon Lord's power, becoming a special existence. That was why he could use the Grimoire's magic more freely than any other.

*This magic tome wasn't meant for humans to use in the first place,* Kim Jonghyun thought, glancing at the Grimoire floating beside him. *It's a magic tome for a Demon Lord.*

That was the secret. A Black Mage on the level of Arbes could not use it, but Kim Jonghyun could. It was not because he had simply made all of Arbes's power his own, but because he, while still human, could wield the power of a Demon Lord.

*I don't know why a magic tome for a Demon Lord is in this world, but…*

A faint smile touched Kim Jonghyun's lips. He had sacrificed three thousand souls to cast the Grimoire's magic and deal with the subjugation force. The massacre of the seven villages had served a dual purpose. It had gathered the hearts needed for his ritual, and it had also procured the souls required to cast this spell. Those three thousand souls would never reincarnate. They would suffer for eons.

That was none of his concern. He had used them to make this forest similar to a Dungeon.

A Dungeon was a place that defied common sense, and this forest was now the same. While making it exactly like a true Dungeon was impossible, he had succeeded in twisting it far enough from reality. The five hundred members of the subjugation force who entered the forest were all scattered. The moment they stepped inside, they were caught in the magic. Though they entered through the same entrance, the space itself was warped, dropping them in different places throughout the woods.

Kim Jonghyun looked around at the dozens of rifts that had opened around him. Within them floated images of the subjugation force members, wandering lost in the forest. He focused his attention on one of those images.

"As expected. You came too."

Kim Jonghyun smiled as he watched Lee Sungmin. *Is meeting again like this also fate? Was I swept up in the Destiny Force you possess? Or did I create a new one?* It was an unanswerable question. A mortal could never look down upon the entirety of fate from above.

*You came here to save Scarlet.*

Kim Jonghyun shifted his gaze to another image, where he could see Scarlet moving with a group of mercenaries.

{sep}

She knew instinctively. This forest was a death trap. She did not know what kind of trick Kim Jonghyun had pulled, but this place was dangerous.

*I don't want to be here. I want to run away.* Scarlet genuinely thought so. She was bold and strong-willed, but the sheer ominousness of this forest screamed at her to turn and flee at once.

She would have, if she could. She would have even killed that damn Green Tower Master to do it. Yes, if he were by her side right now, she would have unhesitatingly murdered him and nullified the oath she had made.

However, the Green Tower Master was not here. For all she knew, he was already dead. Scarlet had no way of confirming it. If he was dead, she could flee without consequence, but if he was alive, running would mean her own death as the price for breaking her oath.

*Fuck.*

Scarlet ground her teeth. It was a dilemma. She would die if she ran, and if she stayed… she felt like she would die anyway. It would have been better if she were at least surrounded by helpful allies.

"What bad luck," Scarlet spat, her irritated voice undisguised. It was not as if anyone would hear her anyway.

She glared at the backs of Doberman and the Mad Dog mercenaries walking ahead. When she had first come to her senses in the pitch-black darkness, she had found herself with them. In this bizarre gloom, she could barely see anything in the distance, and the surrounding scenery was a blur. The only things she could see clearly were the mercenaries who had entered with her.

That fact alone evoked an even deeper fear. In a darkness where almost nothing was visible, only the figures of other people stood out with unnatural clarity.

"Do you even know where you're going?" Scarlet shouted, but Doberman and the others, who were not even that far away, did not look back.

Her voice could not reach them. In the end, Scarlet sighed and checked the magic she had prepared. She did not trust the mercenaries. The Mad Dog Mercenaries might be more skilled than other groups, and their leader Doberman might be an SSS-Rank, but that did not earn her trust. In a situation like this, when things became truly critical, she had to rely on her own strength to survive, not on others.

Scarlet's magic was different from that of other mages. It was a unique mix based on the Spell Engraving Yin-Yang Path and Qimen Dunjia, a system she had drafted on Mount Mushii. Even after descending the mountain, she had continued to reinforce her Spell Engraving, and the completed art had earned her the title of Crimson Archmage.

*Lee Sungmin… that guy must have entered the forest too. That is, if he doesn't plan on abandoning me.*

The thought of breaking the bracelet crossed her mind.

The attack came without a sound. The surroundings were dark… in such an environment, an ambush was bound to have brutal consequences.

Despite being tense and prepared, she was not the target. A beast erupted from the gloom, its teeth swift and sharp. The Mad Dog mercenary guarding the left flank failed to react. In truth, his skill was far too low to have ever reacted in time.

"Uwaaaack!"

The B-Rank mercenary screamed as teeth sank into his side, but in this soundless forest, only he could hear his own cry.

At the spray of blood and the man's flailing hands, Doberman, who was walking ahead, whipped around. Scarlet, walking behind him, clearly saw the kind of beast that was tearing into the mercenary's body.

CRUNCH!

It clenched its jaw, bit down, and shook its head violently, slamming the mercenary's body onto the ground. The man could no longer scream. He was dead. As his severed body lay sprawled on the ground, Doberman roared and swung his greatsword.

Scarlet immediately prepared her magic.

However, it was not alone.

Screams, though unheard, began to echo from the darkness nearby.

And from within that same darkness, the cries of more beasts were closing in.
