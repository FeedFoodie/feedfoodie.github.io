Only Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon remained in the middle of the empty world. Cradled in his arms, she had fallen silent. He gently caressed her shoulder, struck by how small she felt, a frame that fit so perfectly in his embrace despite being merely human.

A contradiction settled in his thoughts. She was human, yet the power she possessed far surpassed the limits of her mortal existence. The battle that had just unfolded here had transcended anything that could be called a fight between humans.

"I…" Wiji Hoyeon's voice broke the long silence. She was no longer broken. Her memories had returned—of him, of everything she had done—and the Spirit's curse had lifted. Though she had started to speak, she fell quiet again, composing the words she needed to say, organizing the turmoil in her heart. Lee Sungmin waited patiently, not rushing her.

"…I wanted to be broken," she finally said in a small voice, her head bowed low.

Those words held many meanings, but Lee Sungmin was not surprised. He had suspected as much. He thought back to the corpses she had painstakingly killed, one by one. It had been an incredibly inefficient slaughter. There had been no need to leave bodies behind, no reason to kill them so kindly. Considering the sheer power she had unleashed during their battle, the devastating explosions, she could have annihilated them all in an instant, leaving not a single corpse behind, yet Wiji Hoyeon had not committed that kind of massacre.

"With a single gesture, if I decided to, so many things would die and disappear," she muttered, nestled in his arms. "My senses felt distorted, and I… I felt like I was becoming something other than human."

"Killing them one by one was tedious, but not difficult. The numbers… were also great. To be honest, I was afraid of that. Of doing everything I could do here, and having nothing left to do. Of… being left all alone in this place."

"…Yeah."

"So I killed them one by one," she confessed. "Dragging it out as long as I could."

It was a profound contradiction. Conscious that countless lives could vanish with a single gesture, Wiji Hoyeon found her own power terrifying. She hated the feeling of her sense of life becoming distorted, the sense that she was no longer human. So, she had killed her enemies one by one, like a 'human' would.

"It's foolish, isn't it?" Wiji Hoyeon chuckled without humor. "There was no right answer for me. Doing nothing, running away from the fight, was a wrong answer not even worth considering from the start. I couldn't afford to fail, so I had to kill. At some point, I couldn't feel the presence of Predation anymore, but… I chose to keep killing rather than face the confusion."

And so, she had broken down.

"I thought it would be better to just break," she whispered. "That way, I thought I wouldn't have to deal with any worries… or conflicts. Regrets. Things like that."

The Spirit had exploited that opening. Its curse had succeeded, and Wiji Hoyeon's reason had vanished.

She lifted her head, her face looking as if it were about to cry, yet she forced a smile. Lee Sungmin held her shoulders tightly.

"It's okay," he repeated the words he had said several times before. "It's because you're human."

Lee Sungmin understood that well. He had seen many times what the difference between being human and becoming something else entailed. When one ceases to be human, their very way of thinking changes, and their human common sense disappears completely. A normal person cannot accept killing and eating people. The moment one fully accepts it, they are no longer human. The transformation from a human into a Yokai was a prime example.

The reason Wiji Hoyeon had broken was that the Demonic Spirit had fixed her existence as human. Lee Sungmin actually saw that as a relief. If her existence had not been fixed, she might have become a terrible monster in this place.

"…Thank you… for remaining human," Lee Sungmin whispered into her ear.

Strictly speaking, Lee Sungmin himself was not human. He was the sole exception to the laws governing this world, a being forged by a miraculous encounter, by coincidence, and by fate.

"It's not over yet," Lee Sungmin said, gently releasing her from his embrace.

Wiji Hoyeon looked up at him, her eyes filled with a bitter understanding. Here, she had been a calamity of The End, a force blocking his path. Even if she had not wanted it, their confrontation had been inevitable. He had defeated her, but their ordeal was far from finished. Fate had not changed one bit.

Lee Sungmin's gaze lifted to the empty red sky, his expression hardening. "Well," he began, his voice laced with irritation, "is there something else you want to do?"

Hahahaha!

A laugh that seemed to shake the heavens answered him. A small sphere of light appeared, and Lee Sungmin glared at the Spirit as it revealed itself. What appeared this time was also just a terminal.

"Are you afraid?" the Spirit asked.

"I am." The Spirit did not deny it. He was well aware of the power Lee Sungmin had achieved. Both the Spirit and the Demonic Spirit were merely administrators of this world. They possessed various powers befitting their roles, but those powers had almost no effect on Lee Sungmin, who did not belong to fate. Lee Sungmin, on the other hand, possessed enough power to pierce the Spirit's very existence.

"I'm more disappointed than I expected," the Spirit said in a calm voice. "I achieved no results here. There was more to observe in Wiji Hoyeon's growth, but her opponent was not a good match. If it had been a bit more of a slugfest, there would have been more to watch…"

A considerable amount of regret colored the Spirit's words. It was understandable. The Spirit had gone to great lengths to break Wiji Hoyeon and prepare such a stage, hoping that their fight would produce a 'result' different from anything before. In conclusion, it was a failure. Wiji Hoyeon had plenty of room for growth, but Lee Sungmin's power had given her no opportunity to achieve it.

Wiji Hoyeon glared at the Spirit's terminal with hateful eyes. Receiving her gaze, the Spirit chuckled heartily.

"Still. This has been a sufficient result. I look forward to the next."

The Spirit's light swelled. Seeing this, Wiji Hoyeon's expression hardened. A small hole opened in the space, and a jet-black light flowed out.

Lee Sungmin took a sharp breath at the sight of that light. It was his first time facing it directly, but he knew instinctively what it was. The thing he faced did not feel as terrifying as he had imagined. There was no sense of pressure, nor did it feel dangerous. He felt nothing at all. That was its nature. It was not an enemy that could be fought, not something that could be crushed with force or destroyed.

Predation simply devoured everything. No matter how strong one's power was, it was meaningless before that thing. In 'this world', Predation was a being with such an absolute law.

He felt Wiji Hoyeon's body stiffen with tension. Hahaha. The Spirit disappeared, leaving only the sound of its laughter behind. It had decided there was nothing more to see. The darkness began to multiply, clumping together and overflowing, an unfathomable chaos swirling within it.

*No, can chaos even be fathomed in the first place?*

The moment Predation revealed itself, this space could no longer be maintained. Suddenly, Lee Sungmin and Wiji Hoyeon were standing in the middle of the Sleeping Forest. Predation was there with them. Lee Sungmin quickly looked around.

[You've succeeded.]

A terminal of the Demonic Spirit attached itself to him. Lee Sungmin ignored the words and continued to survey his surroundings. Kazuya, the last of the clan that protected the forest, was already dead. There was no barrier of any kind left. Amidst it all, Predation continued to swell in size.

"Hoyeon," Lee Sungmin called her name in a low voice.

Wiji Hoyeon turned her stiff neck to look at him. He smiled warmly at her.

"It's okay."

"What do you mean, it's okay…" she asked back at his sudden words.

At that moment, Lee Sungmin placed a hand on her shoulder. Wiji Hoyeon's expression changed. Before she could shout anything, he locked onto her with Dragonic Magic.

"Wait…!"

"It's okay."

Before her cry could finish, he sent her away with Spatial Movement. Lee Sungmin then took a deep breath, wondering if his last words had reached her. He turned his head to look at Predation.

The darkness was devouring the forest. When he had heard it was a monster that devoured the world, Lee Sungmin had just imagined a beast with an enormous mouth. Realizing how poor his imagination was, he smiled bitterly.

[Are you not afraid?] the Demonic Spirit asked.

It would be a lie to say he was not. Lee Sungmin slowly approached Predation. As he did, a question suddenly occurred to him. What if he had killed Kazuya right after getting the key and forcibly released Predation then?

[That's impossible. Predation always appears at the very end of The End. The Spirit isn't stupid enough to let that happen.]

What is meant to happen, happens. Kim Jonghyun, the Spirit Queen, the Dungeon, Jeniela, the Martial God. The order might differ, but the fact that Predation was the finale would never change.

The darkness flickered like a tongue. Multiplying as it swallowed the space whole, Predation was now right in front of him. Time seemed to slow, and within it, only Lee Sungmin's thoughts stretched on. Beyond fear, the first thought that came to him was, as always, *Is this really possible?*

His current actions were based on unconditional trust in the Demonic Spirit. *What if the Demonic Spirit was actually deceiving me?*

[What's with the doubt now…] the Demonic Spirit grumbled.

If the Demonic Spirit had also been an enemy, it had possessed plenty of opportunities to act. He had only considered it as one of many possibilities. Lee Sungmin knew well that there was no deceit in its actions. It was not so much trust as it was that he just knew.

The key stirred inside his clothes, asserting its presence for the first time. Lee Sungmin slowly reached into his clothes and grasped it.

"Wait," a panicked voice called from afar.

Hearing it, Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh.

"What are you trying to do?" the Spirit shouted, its voice urgent.

Lee Sungmin pulled the key from his clothes and held it out in front of him. The Demonic Spirit laughed loudly while the Spirit screamed. Listening to their cries, Lee Sungmin continued to walk forward until the darkness of Predation swallowed his body.

In the midst of the chaos, the key shone with a bright light. Lee Sungmin slowly pushed it into the darkness. He felt a slight resistance, similar to inserting a key into a keyhole. He pushed it all the way in and turned it half a rotation.

"……"

A small, almost dainty sound, slightly different from what he had expected, rang out.

SHWAAAAAA!

The darkness began to scatter. A brilliant light burst forth from where he had inserted and turned the key. Lee Sungmin stared straight ahead at what lay beyond and pulled the key out. In that instant, the darkness completely vanished.

Spatial Movement, no, erosion? The Dragon's knowledge tried to comprehend the situation as magic but soon gave up, realizing it was a meaningless effort.

Lee Sungmin found himself in the middle of a pure white space. "So this is the library," he muttered in a low voice.

The place where he now stood was not what one would call a library, no matter how one thought about it.

*Then again, a library full of actual bookshelves would be ridiculous.*

It could not possibly be an ordinary library. Once again, Lee Sungmin scoffed at his own poor imagination. The key was no longer in his hand.

"Y-y-you crazy…!" the Spirit's voice thundered through the space. "How did you get in here…! This, this place is not for the living…!"

Lee Sungmin ignored the Spirit's voice and tried to explore the space with magic. It was impossible to grasp. The magic intertwined here was no longer what could be called magic, and with the knowledge of a mere Dragon, it was impossible to even get a glimpse.

"Of course. The things that constitute this… library are all great magics that could be called truth. They were touched by the hands of real gods… Absolute Beings, not the 'fake divinities' of Eria," the Demonic Spirit answered.

At that, the Spirit shrieked hysterically. "You're insane, truly, completely insane. Do you have any idea what you've just done…?!"

Lee Sungmin looked down at the key he was holding in his hand. It still had the form of a key.

"What were you thinking, doing something like this…! Do you really think The End will be over because of what you've done?!"

"No. I don't think so," Lee Sungmin replied in a small voice. "But I can stop it."

Predation appears at the end of The End and devours everything. Now that it had appeared, no other calamities existed in the world. Predation, which devours everything, was the library itself, where all the repeated history of Eria was recorded. The key's purpose was to forcibly open the chaos of Predation and restore its function as the library.

This meant that outside, in a way Lee Sungmin could not perceive, Predation's advance across Eria had been halted. As long as he remained here, it could not devour anything more.

"And you can't get out either," the still-unseen Spirit ground its teeth with a grinding sound.

The Spirit had been hiding inside Predation, inside this library, all along, and now, Lee Sungmin had entered.

"You're not the master of this place anymore," Lee Sungmin smirked, jiggling the key in his hand. "Because the one with the key is the owner of the house."

"A mere human…!" the Spirit roared.
