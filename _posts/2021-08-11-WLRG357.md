Of all the places Lee Sungmin had ever visited, the city of Behenger was closest to Cheneron Great Lake. He had worked there briefly as a mercenary right after descending from Mount Mushii. The Dahar Ruins, however, were nearest to Breden, the home of Shaolin. Unlike the desolate lake, the ruins were a popular tourist destination, and if the Earth Elemental King were to Advent there with malicious intent, a massacre would surely follow.

For that reason, Lee Sungmin decided to take the Spear King and Lloyd to Breden at once. He would transport them on the Fairy Horse, spend a day in the city, and then return to the Fairy Forest.

[This is the fourth time, isn't it?]

*I don't want to do martial arts theory.*

He had thought he could manage up to five trips. That illusion shattered the moment he arrived in Breden. After he finished vomiting up what little he could not stomach, the thought of riding the Fairy Horse back to the forest even one more time vanished completely.

"Well then, I'll see you next time," Lee Sungmin said, recovering.

The Spear King shifted uncomfortably, fiddling with the bracelet on his wrist. Even if he and Lloyd succeeded in defeating the Earth Elemental King, Lee Sungmin's party would be too far away to learn of their victory. Scarlet and Lloyd had therefore used their magic to create the bracelets. No matter the distance between them, the enchanted bands would allow them to send and receive a small signal. When the signal came, Lee Sungmin would return to the gates of Breden to retrieve them.

"Be careful."

"I'm not so weak that I need you to worry about me," the Spear King sneered.

Lloyd stood beside him with a sour expression, clearly uncomfortable with the prospect of working alongside a man who was not only his elder but also possessed a foul temper. Lee Sungmin was well aware of the Spear King's terrible personality and felt a pang of sympathy for the wizard.

After seeing the Spear King off as he dragged Lloyd away, Lee Sungmin turned back toward the city of Breden. It had been a long time. More than fifteen years had passed since he was last here, and the place stirred old ghosts. So much had happened in this city. He had first met Namgung Heewon here and become his sworn brother.

Looking back on it now, Namgung Heewon had been quite cunning, using the pretense of brotherhood to forcibly forge a connection with a Lee Sungmin who had just reached the Supreme Peak. Even so, Lee Sungmin held no ill will toward him. For the eldest son of the prestigious Namgung Clan, he was surprisingly honest and down-to-earth.

That connection had granted him easy entry into Shaolin. It was there he first met Tang Ahee, with whom he often found himself entangled in trivial matters, and it was there he had met Grandmaster Bulyeong.

His meeting with the Grandmaster had been deeply meaningful. After descending from Mount Mushii, Lee Sungmin's martial arts had hit a plateau, and it was thanks to Grandmaster Bulyeong that he had rededicated himself to his training. This was also partly due to Jihak, the man known as the future of Shaolin. Their duel had decisively spurred Lee Sungmin's growth and broken him out of his stagnation.

He had rarely wielded his spear with such calm desperation as he had back then. On Mount Mushii, his training had been an ascetic practice of forced restrictions, but Shaolin had no such constraints. He had to confine himself with his mind alone, focusing only on the spear.

He wondered if the cicadas still chirped there.

As memories of Jihak and Namgung Heewon surfaced, his thoughts naturally drifted to Wudang and the Sword Immortal who was unable to leave it. If only the man could leave, his power would be a great help in stopping The End.

How much stronger had the Sword Immortal's disciple, Cheongmyeong, become? And what of Namgung Heewon?

Ten years was a long time. Cheongmyeong had already been a master of the Transcendent Realm a decade ago, so he must have grown even stronger since. Namgung Heewon and Jihak might have also broken through that wall by now.

*After I've dealt with the Blood Alliance, I should pay a visit to Wudang.*

The more forces he had, the better. The Spirit Queen was a problem, but the most formidable enemy was undoubtedly the Vampire Queen, Jeniela. Could he lure her to Mount Wudang? Or perhaps to the Fairy Forest? Both the Sword Immortal and Osla were bound to their respective domains. If that was the case, couldn't he just draw Jeniela into their territory?

[Jeniela's no fool. You think she'll come just because you call?]

*And I don't have any cards to play to lure her over.*

[You know, you have a really rotten personality. Are you even aware of it?]

*Me? I don't think I'm that bad.*

[This isn't about good and evil. It's something more ambiguous. Look at the thoughts you're having right now. If you lure Jeniela to Wudang, all the unrelated Taoists of the Wudang Sect will die. If you lure her to the Fairy Forest, the fairies will be slaughtered. You aren't even considering their deaths.]

Lee Sungmin was left speechless by the Void Monarch's words.

[It's not even as if you're thinking of sacrificing the few for the big picture of stopping The End. I'll say it again, that thought doesn't even cross your mind. Do normal people think like that?]

*...I don't know.*

[Well, this Elder has told you many times that you're half-mad. It's hardly surprising. The situations you've faced would be enough to drive anyone insane. However, if you aim to live as a human being in the future, that is, if this world escapes the fate of The End and avoids destruction, if you want to live like a human in that world, then you'd better be clearly aware of your own detachment.]

At those words, Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. When it came to matters like this, he had to take the Void Monarch's advice to heart. Since he was mentally connected to Lee Sungmin, he often offered a perspective that Lee Sungmin himself could not see.

He wanted to go up to Shaolin to at least pay his respects to Grandmaster Bulyeong, but he could not. The Grandmaster had once guided him to the North while under a Spirit's Possession. Now, he could not be sure if it was the Spirit or the Demonic Spirit that had borrowed Grandmaster Bulyeong's body to guide him. Regardless of which it was, Lee Sungmin had no desire to meet either of them. The Spirit was an enemy, and the Demonic Spirit was suspicious.

[Do you trust that fellow, Laplace?]

*I don't trust him, but I'm just as suspicious of the Demonic Spirit. He used me from the very beginning, after all.*

The Demonic Spirit had wanted Wiji Hoyeon. Lee Sungmin had just been a diversion, a disposable pawn. As a result, Lee Sungmin held no fond feelings for him.

[You realize, don't you? If the Demonic Spirit turns out to be an enemy, you'll have to fight Wiji Hoyeon in the worst-case scenario. Are you confident you can win?]

*I'm not,* Lee Sungmin answered honestly. If he fought Wiji Hoyeon with his current strength, he would surely be defeated.

*Though I hope it never comes to that.*

After spending a day in Breden, Lee Sungmin summoned the Fairy Horse. He had been calling upon it several times a day recently, but the creature showed no particular signs of complaint. Was this even a living horse to begin with? Lee Sungmin gazed up at the Fairy Horse as it looked down at him with its translucent eyes. He then crept behind it and gave its tail a tug.

WHAM.

[What are you doing?]

"I was just curious," Lee Sungmin muttered, dusting off his chest where its hind legs had kicked him. He then stroked the neck of the snorting horse. "I won't do it again, so please don't throw me off."

After returning to the Fairy Forest, he immediately got back on the horse and took Scarlet and Baek Sogo to Germud. Hoping the two of them would not run into any danger, he returned to the forest once more.

"Can't I get another Fairy Horse?" Lee Sungmin asked Osla as he sank to the ground, feeling nauseous.

"You're so greedy," Osla replied, clicking her tongue and shaking her head. "Even I only have one Fairy Horse that can leap through space. I am the great Fairy Queen, but that doesn't make me omnipotent. A Fairy Horse isn't something a human should possess in the first place."

"Then why did you give it to me?"

"I, too, will face the consequences for doing what should not be done when the time of The End arrives," Osla replied, puffing out her cheeks in a pout. She always spoke vaguely about those 'consequences'.

"You won't have to face any consequences, Osla."

"...Easy for you to say," Osla muttered in a small voice. She tossed him the object she was holding. "It wasn't difficult, but why did you ask me to do this?"

"...It's not for any particular reason." Lee Sungmin tucked the item he received from Osla into his clothes and gave a bitter smile. "It's just to set the mood."

He swallowed his nausea and climbed onto the Fairy Horse's back. This was his fourth trip today. The Black Dragon Knight and Yana mounted the horse behind him. Teresa had wanted to go with them, but once again, she was left behind. She looked up at Lee Sungmin, her lower lip jutting out in a pout.

"Come back safely."

"Should I bring you a gift this time, too?"

"Your gifts aren't worth getting excited about."

Despite her words, Teresa often played with the Dog Beating Stick he had bought for her in Kron. Since the Black Dragon Knight's arrival, she had even started learning basic staff techniques from him, though it was nothing more than physical movements without the use of any inner qi.

"I'll try to bring back something decent this time."

"I'm not holding my breath."

Leaving Teresa's grumbling behind, the Fairy Horse leaped through space.

Ten years ago, this was the home of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. The home of its Chairman. The gates of Haras, which he now saw for the first time in a decade, had not changed much. Lee Sungmin looked up at them, recalling memories of the past.

The first time he came to Haras, he had been wearing the mask he received from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. It had been to suppress the Demonic Energy he could not control at the time. Now, his body had become completely that of a Yokai, and he could wield his Demonic Energy as freely as his own limbs. The mask was no longer necessary for that purpose.

He had not thrown it away, however. Come to think of it, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had given him almost nothing tangible. He had taken Lee Sungmin as his disciple, passed down his martial art, the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, and given him the status of his successor. These were gifts, but they were not things that could be touched.

The mask was different. Osla had remade it for him, and it was a symbol of his discipleship just as much as the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder. It was also the only physical object Lee Sungmin had ever received directly from his master. He took the mask from his robes. Seeing it after so long was a welcome sight, yet it made his heart ache. The image of the Chairman, who always wore a different mask, came to mind. *Today, I enter Haras. I will head straight for the Blood Alliance.*

The Blood Alliance was an organization formed after the dissolution of the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance. It was created when the suddenly-appeared Blood Demon united the unorthodox sects that had once belonged to it. Only the name and its master had changed. Otherwise, it was not much different from the old days.

He did not like it.

There was a reason he had decided to strike the Blood Demon. It was partly because attacking Nero might provoke Jeniela's intervention, but that was not the whole story. There was a very personal reason as well.

Lee Sungmin slowly brought the mask to his face. He had asked Osla beforehand to remove its function of suppressing Demonic Energy. It felt awkward to wear it after so long, but it fit his face perfectly.

"It's a bit ridiculous for me to say this after bringing you all the way here, but," Lee Sungmin said, gripping the spear slung across his back with both hands. "Please try not to get involved."

"You seem to be in a foul mood," the Black Dragon Knight muttered, his hands clasped behind his back. "Is it because your Master's home has been trampled by filthy boots?"

"...My Master probably didn't consider the Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance his home."

"Then why the rage?"

"Even if my Master didn't think of it that way, the rest of the world did," Lee Sungmin said as he approached the city gates. "After Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon died, the dismantled Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance was picked up by some unknown Blood Demon and became the Blood Alliance."

A mysterious energy pulsed from him.

"That's a little irritating."

[You're lying.]

The Void Monarch chuckled.

[Not a little. You're very irritated, aren't you?]

He did not bother to reply.
