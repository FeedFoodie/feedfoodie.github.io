For his sixth restriction, Lee Sungmin chose his sense of smell. Much like his sense of taste, this did not mean he was completely unable to smell anything at all. He learned all too well that a person could vomit from a foul odor alone, a lesson driven home on the very first day when a nauseating stench burrowed deep into his nostrils and made him retch repeatedly.

Vomiting from a disgusting smell was a far greater agony than vomiting from a disgusting taste. As the restrictions on his senses intertwined, his meals became more and more horrendous.

However, he ate. He swallowed his own vomit, squeezed his eyes shut, and did his best to ignore the taste in his mouth and the smell flooding his nose.

[Did you really have to choose your sense of smell?] Baek Sogo asked, her face looking haggard. She was currently under six restrictions herself. When Lee Sungmin first met her, she could not speak, send Thought Transmissions, or use her left arm. Since then, she had added three more penances, losing the use of her left eye and enduring a constant noise that lingered in her ears.

And she had restricted her sense of taste.

Baek Sogo knew the horror of mealtimes, so it was only natural that she looked so worn. Lee Sungmin answered her question with a bitter smile.

[Because the harsher, the better.]

At his words, Baek Sogo asked nothing more. She too was well aware that the harsher the restriction, the more meaningful the penance.

However, *that's how people are.*

Sometimes, even when you know the right answer, you refuse to choose it because you know the pain that comes with it. You know all too well how much it will hurt. Yes, that is how people are. Even if a certain martial arts theory is correct, they will choose a different, wrong answer just to avoid the terrible pain that accompanies the right one.

Baek Sogo was the same. The first two restrictions she had chosen, her voice and Thought Transmission, were not terribly agonizing. She had never been a talkative person to begin with, and she could simply write things down if she needed to converse. Losing the use of her left arm was inconvenient but not agonizing, as she could just use her right instead. The same went for her left eye.

Her senses of taste and hearing, however, were a different matter. Baek Sogo was now keenly aware of just how terrible it was to have your senses run wild. Honestly, she regretted it a lot. She should have chosen different restrictions.

In truth, there was no reason for Baek Sogo to choose such harsh penance. She had talent. While Flam was an avatar of Mushii and the trial itself, making him beyond comparison, Baek Sogo was still a human who fell well within the category of a calamity.

Lee Sungmin, however, was different. He was not a calamity, so he had to undergo the harshest penance. That was what made the difference. Baek Sogo had failed to overcome the trial named Flam. While Lee Sungmin had accepted Despair and forged his own path, she had chosen to look away. It was a cruel irony. The talentless Lee Sungmin had overcome his Despair of his own nature, while the calamity Baek Sogo had averted her eyes from a calamity even greater than herself.

[I have something to say to you, Junior Disciple.]

Lee Sungmin could not tell her about Flam's true identity because of what Mushii had said. *Had Baek Sogo overcome the Despair that was Flam?* He was curious, but he could not ask her directly.

[What is it?]

[I'm thinking of leaving this mountain soon,] Baek Sogo replied.

The words surprised him a little, but he was not flustered. It had already been over two years since she came to this mountain. In terms of years, it might not seem like a long time, but considering her talent and the penance she had undergone, she had likely reached the state she desired during her training.

[I see.]

Lee Sungmin evened his breathing and wrote the words. The foul stench filling his lungs was nauseating. If Baek Sogo left the mountain, he would be the only old practitioner left.

[It weighs on my mind, leaving you and Flam here alone. Or would you like to leave the mountain with me?]

[No. I still want to remain on this mountain,] Lee Sungmin answered. He knew from her response that she still did not know Flam's true identity.

[Is that so.]

Baek Sogo nodded her head and did not press him further. Likewise, Lee Sungmin did not try to stop her. Given her personality, he knew she would undoubtedly remain on the mountain if he asked her to stay a little longer. He knew that would happen, but he did not say it. Baek Sogo must have things she needed to do as well.

[Right now?]

"……"

Baek Sogo nodded. Lee Sungmin remained silent for a moment before asking another question.

[Will we be able to meet again?]

[I'm sure we will,] Baek Sogo answered with a bitter smile. [I'll probably wander around after I descend the mountain, but we'll be able to meet. I'm sure of it.]

No, they would definitely meet. In seven years, Lee Sungmin would go to find her.

[How much longer do you plan to stay on this mountain, Junior Disciple?]

[I don't know,] Lee Sungmin replied.

Originally, he had planned to stay for two years before going to Behenger to become a mercenary. Now, however, he did not know. Even if he went to Behenger, there was nothing for him to do right away. The best he could do was use the memories of his past life to establish himself, but he could not be satisfied with that. He had not yet reached the state he sought. He had no confidence that he could see the end of martial arts even if he lived his entire life on this mountain, but he wanted to at least reach the goal he was currently aiming for.

[Don't be too martial arts theory.]

Baek Sogo hesitated for a long time before finally writing those words. Her heart was not at ease. They were both the same, whether classless, martial artist, or mage. They were all in the same situation, suddenly summoned to this unkind world, having lost their families and friends.

*Junior Disciple.*

She looked at the young man before her. A full year had passed since they first met, and in that time, his body had grown quite a bit. Even while under the horrendously painful restriction of taste, Lee Sungmin never skipped a meal, all to make his still-growing body mature. That effort had borne considerable fruit, and he had now grown to about the same height as her. Perhaps because of all the heartache, his face looked older than his age, but a trace of youthfulness remained. Baek Sogo sighed, feeling a sense of bitterness.

She wanted to help him a little more, to stay by his side and give him advice. It was not that she had not helped or advised him until now. It was just that she wanted to do more.

[I'm fine,] Lee Sungmin wrote. [If it weren't for your help, Senior Sister, I wouldn't have lasted even a few months on this mountain. It is because of your sympathy that I am who I am today.]

The Lee Sungmin of his past life had never felt this way before. Just as he had felt gratitude toward Wiji Hoyeon, he now felt gratitude toward Baek Sogo.

[Thank you, Senior Sister,] he wrote.

Baek Sogo remained silent for a moment before slowly approaching him. She reached out with her right arm and pulled him into an embrace. Flustered, Lee Sungmin tried to pull away, but she did not let him go. For a long while, Baek Sogo held him like that, her right hand slowly stroking his back. Lee Sungmin felt her touch, the strands of her hair tickling the tip of his nose, and the warmth from her body where they touched. His face grew hot.

[Junior Disciple,] Baek Sogo released him and picked up a branch to write on the ground. [You'll do well. Because you are the most solid person I've ever met.]

[What do you mean by solid?]

[That you don't break,] she answered. [You didn't give up, Junior Disciple. You felt Despair after meeting Flam, but you still didn't give up. I think that's amazing. You didn't give up, and you kept trying. You're amazing.]

[That's not really true,] Lee Sungmin replied with a bitter smile.

Solid. Unbreakable. He thought those words were wrong. He had already been broken several times. He had just somehow endured while breaking.

[I'm not solid. I've already been broken many times. Perhaps I am even now, and I will be again in the future.]

[It's fine if you're not solid,] Baek Sogo wrote, shaking her head. [It doesn't matter if you've been broken several times. What's important is that you don't despair there. You're amazing, Junior Disciple.]

She paused before continuing.

[Junior Disciple. I don't believe in gods.]

Those words felt a little contradictory. This was Mount Mushii, a mountain where a god who governed trials and penance existed. To not believe in gods while training here seemed strange.

[But I want to pray to a god. That you may achieve what you desire, Junior Disciple. That you may not be completely broken.]

With those last words, Baek Sogo put down the branch. Lee Sungmin wanted to say more, but seeing her set the branch aside, he stopped the conversation. This was it. Her face was serene. If they talked any longer, they would both be left with lingering attachments.

"Well then, Junior Disciple."

Baek Sogo's lips parted as her restrictions ended. Her closed left eye opened, and her limp left arm moved. She took a slow breath and, in a slightly low voice, bid him farewell. "May we meet again somewhere, someday."

Then, Baek Sogo left.

At some point, Lee Sungmin had started eating his meals with her. Whenever he practiced his martial arts, she was always by his side, watching and giving him advice. Just as Wiji Hoyeon had, Baek Sogo taught him many things. She was a good person. If Wiji Hoyeon's teachings were harsh, Baek Sogo's were gentle. He had no intention of comparing them, but their different approaches had both helped him a great deal.

And now, she was gone. Just like Wiji Hoyeon.

Lee Sungmin walked quietly through the mountain, a profound sense of loneliness settling over him. It was a feeling he had not experienced in a very long time. He had felt lonely when he parted with Wiji Hoyeon, too, but back then, there had been other people by his side. There was Hans, there was Jack, and there was Lula.

Not now.

There was no one on this mountain. There might be other practitioners, but he had no intention of meeting them. Dok Bijun and Scarlet had already left several months ago. Today, Baek Sogo also left. Lee Sungmin felt a longing for human company.

A person cannot live alone.

Do not stray from righteousness and duty. Do not forget your debts of gratitude.

He had heard the words about not forgetting debts before, from Jack, the street vendor in Zenavis. A person cannot live alone. When he first heard these words from Baek Sogo, he could not accept them, but not anymore. He would not have been able to live this long if he had been alone.

*Ah.*

Lee Sungmin stopped walking. He recalled the thoughts drifting through his mind one by one, contemplating them and taking them to heart. In the midst of it, night had already fallen. His body was heavy, noise lingered in his ears, and a stench seeped into his nose. Yes, this was the same 'me' as always.

And yet, it was different.

Something inside his heart had changed. A wall he had kept from crumbling down finally collapsed. His mind cleared with a flash, and his consciousness soared. It was a sense of liberation that was both brief and long-lingering. Lee Sungmin took a deep breath. In that moment, there was no stench, no heaviness, and no noise.

As 'I' change, the world changes.

In his loneliness and solitude, Lee Sungmin smiled.
