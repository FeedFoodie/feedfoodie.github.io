A pained groan escaped the Elf as Lee Sungmin dragged him through the air, his hand clamped around the Elf's neck. The captive struggled, his own hands gripping Lee Sungmin's wrist in a desperate attempt to keep his neck from snapping.

Even as arrows whistled toward him, Lee Sungmin spared a glance for his captive. An idea struck him, and on a whim, he swung the arm holding the Elf, positioning the struggling body as a makeshift shield. Just as the projectiles were about to find their mark, they abruptly changed direction, veering sharply up into the air.

*They're controlling the arrows' trajectory with Elementals.*

The maneuver was little more than a nuisance, of course. He already knew the arrows could change direction, a fact demonstrated by the very Elf he now held captive. This test had a different purpose.

*So they don't want to kill their own kind, huh?*

The conclusion was simple and immediate. This could be useful.

He landed lightly on a thick tree branch, and the arrows that had been circling above immediately rained down on him. Without a moment's hesitation, Lee Sungmin thrust the Elf's body upward. The volley of arrows stopped dead in mid-air.

"First," Lee Sungmin said, his voice not loud but carrying a weight that reached every one of his hidden attackers.

The arrowheads, suspended in the air, trembled. He slowly lowered the Elf's body, and as the arrows flinched forward, he planted his foot on the captive's head. The Elf thrashed beneath him. Lee Sungmin gave his spear a casual spin.

KWADUK!

The blunt end of the Changjun slammed down on the Elf's thigh.

A scream tore from the Elf's throat, and the arrowheads above ceased their advance. Lee Sungmin's intentions were brutally clear. He was taking a hostage, and he felt little guilt for it. He had no friendly relations with the Elves. To him, they were simply enemies who had attacked without provocation, without a single question about his circumstances. While he had technically trespassed on their territory first, he wondered if that truly warranted an immediate attempt on his life.

"Let's talk face to face," he called out, already knowing their locations through their presence. He deliberately twisted the Changjun pinning the Elf's thigh, setting aside the thought of pulling out teeth for now. He had used the blunt end precisely because he did not want to provoke them too much. Despite how things had turned, he still had no great desire to make enemies of the Elves, though he would if it came to that.

"You bastard! Do you have any idea what it means to treat an Elf like this in this forest..." the pinned Elf spat.

"Let's have you shut up," Lee Sungmin replied, his expression unchanged.

His words were not the only threat. A faint killing intent licked past the Elf's neck, a sensation far more terrifying than the spear in his thigh, and the captive's face went pale.

Lee Sungmin waited for a reaction. The arrows hung motionless overhead, and their archers remained hidden.

*Are they calling for reinforcements?* The thought had barely crossed his mind when he lifted the spear from the Elf's thigh.

A wave of relief washed over the captive as the pain vanished. He thought Lee Sungmin was softening his stance, a belief completely at odds with what happened next.

The spear spun once more in Lee Sungmin's hand, but this time the sharp head plunged through the Elf's thigh.

"Aaaargh!" the Elf shrieked, and the arrows above trembled violently.

With a blank face, Lee Sungmin twisted the embedded spear. Muscles tore and bone shattered with a sickening crunch. The Elf clawed at the ground, weeping.

"Are you not coming out?" Lee Sungmin asked again, his gaze shifting from his victim to the arrows above. He could see the small wind Elementals holding them, fidgeting restlessly. Then his eyes moved to the side, and he exerted his will. Space Manipulation forcibly shoved the arrows aside. The wind Elementals, flustered by the pressure of his inner qi, finally let go and fled.

Now in control of the arrows himself, Lee Sungmin turned them back toward the Elf impaled by his spear.

"One by one," he muttered.

PAK!

An arrow fell, embedding itself in the Elf's calf. The captive's scream grew even louder.

"Stop!" a shout erupted from the distance.

The Elves finally revealed themselves, stepping out from behind the trees just as he had sensed. Four of them emerged, their faces contorted with rage, arrows nocked and aimed at him.

"Let Fred go!" one of them demanded.

"So your name is Fred?" Lee Sungmin asked, glancing down at the whimpering Elf.

"Who the hell are you!" another shouted. "Why did you come into our forest and cause such a ruckus!"

"What ruckus? I hadn't done a thing before you all started attacking me."

"You trespassed on our territory first..."

"This is endless," Lee Sungmin muttered, having no desire to argue about who was right or wrong. He put his strength into the spear, driving it deeper into Fred's thigh until it pierced clean through the tree branch. Fred screamed again, the spear now impossible to remove without severing his leg.

Having done that, Lee Sungmin unleashed the arrows he held with Space Manipulation, sending them flying at the Elves all at once. They leaped back in fright.

WHOOSH.

Lee Sungmin's body blurred into motion. The distance between them was considerable, but his speed was too great for even Elves with the forest's protection to track.

PEPEOPEOK!

The four Elves tumbled to the ground almost simultaneously. Lee Sungmin landed in their midst, glancing at them as they groaned and clutched their wounds. He then returned to Fred's side, the four captured Elves floating in the air behind him.

He sealed their acupoints. "I have no desire to become enemies with your race," he stated, his voice level as the immobilized Elves stared at him with trembling eyes. He pulled the spear from Fred's thigh and poured a little Elixir on the wound, which bubbled and seethed as it regenerated. "But if you attack me, I have no choice but to attack back. Let's make that clear first."

He sealed Fred's acupoints as well. "I don't intend to kill you, and I don't want to hurt you any more. Understood?"

"What bullshit are you..."

"You just need to nod," Lee Sungmin cut him off. "I'll be the one asking questions, and you will only answer. First, where is your village?"

"As if we'd tell you that!"

"A man entered this forest a few days ago, a man with a sword. Who is he? Is he in your village right now?"

"I don't know!"

"In your village, is there an Elf who has learned martial arts? I don't know if you use that name here, but is there an Elf called the Fist Honored One?"

"I won't say anything!" the same Elf roared.

"You're not making any sense," Lee Sungmin grumbled, his eyes falling on Fred. Fred's face turned white.

"This will be the best way," Lee Sungmin explained calmly. "If I torture you by sealing your acupoints, you'll sustain internal injuries, and Elixir doesn't heal those well. External wounds, however, are another matter."

With that, he grabbed Fred's hand. Realizing his intent, Fred's mouth fell open, but Lee Sungmin sealed the acupoint on his throat to muffle the scream. He twisted the thumb joint. One by one, all ten of Fred's fingers were broken. Unable to bear the pain, Fred passed out.

Next. Lee Sungmin moved to another Elf and grabbed his hands. Just like Fred, all ten of his fingers were broken. The remaining Elves could only watch in terror, their defiance gone.

"I, I'll talk," the male Elf whose hands Lee Sungmin now held blurted out quickly.

The female Elf who had been so defiant glared at him, her eyes wide with betrayal. Lee Sungmin simply extended a finger and pointed at her.

An invisible force struck her head, and her body fell backward.

"I just knocked her out," he said, leaving no room for misunderstanding. The male Elf hesitated for a moment before speaking.

The Fist Honored One was in the village. The man who had arrived a few days ago was a friend of his.

*A friend.* The word immediately brought Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Six Honored Ones to mind. *If he uses a sword... is it the Sword Saint?* The Crazy Heavenly Demon also came to mind. The Sword Saint had defeated the Crazy Heavenly Demon, who was now dead, said to have spent his last days training to surpass his rival.

Lee Sungmin stood up. He opened the Elixir and treated the Elves' wounds, releasing their sealed acupoints.

"It's already too late," the female Elf, now conscious, said with a chuckle. "Before you caught us, we sent a signal to the Elementals. We're merely sentries. Soon the Hunting Party, the ones who punish those who enter the forest with impure intentions, will come to kill you."

"...Is that so?" Lee Sungmin muttered, looking at her face. *So it had come to this after all.*

With that thought, his hand moved.

PEPEOPEOK!

The heads of all the Elves, except for the one who had answered his questions, exploded.

"Wh... what have you done..." the survivor stammered.

"We were going to end up fighting anyway, weren't we?" Lee Sungmin said, gazing at the blood-gushing corpses. "I spared you because you answered my questions. It would be best if you left this forest."

"What...?"

"Wouldn't it be a waste to lose the life you barely saved?"

With that, Lee Sungmin descended from the tree. The Void Monarch, who had been watching silently, finally spoke.

[Aren't you being a bit too aggressive?]

"If I storm the village and kill the Fist Honored One, I'll become enemies with the Elves anyway."

[I think it would have been better to act more covertly. Like entering the village and assassinating him.]

"I was concerned about the swordsman," Lee Sungmin replied. "He's probably the Sword Saint. I don't know why he's here, but he's still in the village. I'm not confident I can assassinate the Fist Honored One without the Sword Saint noticing, let alone the Elves'."

[In the worst-case scenario, the Fist Honored One might give up on maintaining the curse and join forces with that Sword Saint fellow to fight you. If that happens, you'll most likely die. No matter how strong you've become, it's impossible to fight and survive against the Fist Honored One, who has the forest's protection and has shed the curse, and the Sword Saint, who is said to be even more skilled.]

"I know," Lee Sungmin answered without hesitation. "I have no intention of fighting in that case. If the Fist Honored One gives up the curse, Wiji Hoyeon's curse will be lifted too. My wish will be granted. If he doesn't give up the curse, then I just have to defeat the Sword Saint and kill him."

[Are you confident you can defeat the Sword Saint?]

"I've never met him. I'll know when we fight."

Having said his piece, Lee Sungmin began to move. Since things had turned out this way, he intended to act boldly. He knew the village's location but had no intention of seeking it out. He wanted 'him' to come find him directly. The Fist Honored One would be unable to move because of the curse. He might come with the Sword Saint, but that did not matter much. It was better to fight here than in the Elves' home base.

The only problem was the 'Hunting Party' the dead Elf had mentioned. He did not know their strength, but the Sword Saint would only make a move if the Hunting Party proved insufficient.

*The forest is a pain.*

Lee Sungmin glanced at the tall, large trees surrounding him. He gripped his spear with both hands. Instead of condensing, the Demonic Energy and inner qi enveloping the spear stretched out long and thin. He swung the spear in a wide, devastating arc.

KWAKWAKWAKWA!

Dozens of trees fell around him. He did not stop there, moving from place to place and felling every tree in the vicinity.

Now, all he had to do was wait.

{sep}

Elves were a race blessed with beautiful appearances, long lifespans, Mana, Elementals, and the protection of the forest, but these seemingly perfect beings had a crucial shortcoming. Reproduction was difficult. This fostered a strong sense of kinship and an intense territoriality, for most who entered their forest without permission were hunters seeking to enslave them.

The benevolent, harmony-loving Elves of old tales were long gone. Over the years, they had learned that being calculating was better than being benevolent, and that survival was more important than harmony. The Hunting Party was the symbol of this change. They knew neither forgiveness nor mercy, educated from a young age to dirty their hands for the sake of their kin.

"Fred, Jin, Joseph, and Dekan have been killed."

The voice that delivered the news was somber. The wind Elemental that carried the message seemed dejected, resonating with the gloom that fell over the Hunting Party.

"What about Rajun?"

"Rajun... is alive. He betrayed his kin and sold information to the human."

The Hunting Team Leader's face, previously calm as he stroked the fur of a large tiger, turned to ice. The death of his kin was a tragedy, but the betrayal of Rajun ignited a cold rage. Because their kinship was so strong, Elves offered no forgiveness to traitors.

"Where is he?"

"He's trying to leave the forest."

"Kill him," the leader commanded without hesitation.

The Elf who had reported simply mounted the wolf at his side. As he watched the rider disappear into the trees, the Hunting Team Leader spoke again.

"We're going to hunt that human."

"...Will it be alright not to report to the elder?" another member asked cautiously.

The Hunting Team Leader shook his head. "The elder is unwell from maintaining the curse. I don't want to cause him any worry."

"Then, should we ask for help from the human who came to see him..."

"This is an Elf's problem," the leader replied, his voice cold. "I don't want to borrow a human's hand."

The party member who had spoken bowed his head deeply, regretting his words. A loud noise echoed from the distance, and the Hunting Team Leader frowned, his ears perked.

"...He's acting barbarically."

"The reasons to kill our prey have increased."
