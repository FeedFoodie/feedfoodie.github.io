The man was the Sword Phantom Dok Bijun, a martial artist as renowned throughout Eria as Silent Flash Baek Sogo. First, Lee Sungmin had met Scarlet, the archmage who founded the Reshir School, and now the Sword Phantom himself stood before him.

*Why are there so many big shots here?*

Then again, perhaps they weren't yet famous enough to be called big shots at this point in time. He recalled that the period when they truly made their names was after they left this very mountain. In other words, the Sword Phantom Dok Bijun, Silent Flash Baek Sogo, and Archmage Scarlet Reshir all became legends after completing their training on Mount Mushii.

"Who are you?" Dok Bijun asked, his face turned toward Lee Sungmin.

Under a restriction of sight, Dok Bijun could not see. Lee Sungmin glanced uncertainly at Baek Sogo, who simply picked up a branch and scratched a message into the dirt.

[Introduce yourself. You don't need to be so scared. The old man might look intimidating, but he's not a bad person.]

"...I'm Lee Sungmin," he said, bowing his head in an awkward posture.

The blind man stared blankly in his direction before reaching out a hand. Before a startled Lee Sungmin could step back, Dok Bijun's hand clamped onto his shoulder. His fingers explored the contours of Sungmin's shoulders and arms.

"It seems you don't use a sword."

Lee Sungmin remained silent.

"Judging by the shape of your muscles, is it a spear? A spear... a fine weapon. How old are you? Your voice sounds quite young."

"Uh... I'm fifteen," Lee Sungmin answered.

Dok Bijun nodded, his hand falling away to tap the sword at his waist. "Would you like to have a duel?"

Lee Sungmin hesitated, his eyes flicking to Baek Sogo. She showed no particular reaction, neither encouraging the match nor moving to stop it.

"...My skills are still lacking."

"Is that so? A pity," Dok Bijun said, looking genuinely disappointed.

A martial artist who had made a name for himself as the Sword Phantom was disappointed. No, Sungmin corrected himself, at this point in time, Dok Bijun wasn't yet called the Sword Phantom, was he?

*And Dok Bijun too...*

He dies. Just like Baek Sogo. He would enter the same Dungeon as Wiji Hoyeon and never come out alive. It felt incredibly strange to meet a man who had only eight years left to live.

[Let's go back,] Baek Sogo wrote.

As he read her words, Lee Sungmin tilted his head. "Didn't you say there were four people?"

She had told him four people were currently staying on Mount Mushii, and had been for the past half-year. He had met Baek Sogo, Scarlet, and Dok Bijun, which only made three.

"If you're thinking of looking for Flam, don't," Dok Bijun said, having been listening to their exchange. "He won't meet you anyway. Still, don't go looking for him. It's not good for either of you."

[Tell him you understand,] Baek Sogo wrote.

"Uh... I understand."

Once again, Baek Sogo carried Lee Sungmin on her back. The two left the cliffside under Dok Bijun's unseen gaze and returned to Lee Sungmin's dwelling, though calling it that was a stretch. It was just a shabby hut he had managed to build little by little over the past month.

After setting him down, Baek Sogo began to write.

[Flam is not a sociable person.]

"What did he mean it's not good for either of us?"

Baek Sogo's lips curved into a bitter smile as she wrote.

[Flam is... an incredible guy. He's been living on this mountain for over a year, and he's strong. We haven't been able to fight properly since we can't harm each other, but if we were to fight, I would lose. Easily.]

The statement was shocking. Baek Sogo, whose skills already surpassed the peak realm, was admitting she would be defeated with ease.

[It's not just me. That old man, Dok Bijun, would also lose if he fought Flam.]

"What in the world is he?" Lee Sungmin couldn't help but feel astonished.

He didn't know the exact extent of the Sword Phantom's skills, but the aura Dok Bijun gave off was that of a great master. The very fact that he had made the name 'the Sword Phantom' known throughout Eria was proof of his mastery.

[What should I call him? A calamity born once every hundred years?] Baek Sogo mouthed a silent laugh. [Or maybe a calamity born once in a million. I'll tell you an interesting story. Once, I performed my movement arts in front of Flam. He copied it immediately.]

Lee Sungmin felt as if he had been struck in the head with a hammer. He had seen Baek Sogo's movement arts a few times and found it difficult to even follow them with his eyes, yet this man named Flam had copied it instantly after seeing it just once.

[It's not just me. He also immediately copies old man Dok Bijun's swordsmanship, and Scarlet's magic too. If this isn't a calamity, what is?]

Martial arts, he could perhaps understand. Although it required a complex flow of internal energy, it was, simply put, a way of using the body, but to copy magic? That was a story he simply couldn't believe.

[This is also a piece of advice for you. Don't meet Flam.]

"...Are you afraid he'll steal my martial arts?"

[There is that, yes. Your Spear Technique. It's an exceptionally brilliant martial art. It would be a shame if it were stolen, but that's not the reason I'm telling you not to meet him.]

"Then why shouldn't I meet him?"

[Because you'll fall into Despair.]

After writing that, Baek Sogo paused. Lee Sungmin did not miss the emotion that briefly flickered across her face.

[When you meet a true calamity, most people fall into Despair.]

He couldn't help but agree. Lee Sungmin knew a person who fell into that very category. The Little Heavenly Demon Wiji Hoyeon. She was the greatest calamity he had ever seen among all the humans in both his past and present lives.

[So don't meet him. Not if you don't want to fall into Despair.]

"...I understand."

[I like you,] Baek Sogo wrote next. [I think people who blindly pursue something are admirable. Especially if the path of that pursuit is filled with hardship and trials. You can't help but cheer for them to achieve what they desire. I'm the same. I hope you obtain what you wish for.]

"Isn't it the same for you?" Lee Sungmin asked.

He was no exception. Just as he had started with two restrictions, Baek Sogo had also started with two, and she had recently received another one on top of that.

[I am tempered,] she replied. [But you are not. Nevertheless, you are enduring. I heard that so far, only Flam has endured on this mountain for more than a year. Do you know why?]

"...I don't know."

[The penance and trials become harsher the more you get used to them. The restrictions keep getting added. That's why it's so hard to endure. If you try to hold on in vain and can't overcome it, you die.]

Baek Sogo mouthed another laugh.

[I don't know how long you'll last, but I hope you endure without falling into Despair. I'm cheering for you.]

"...Thank you for saying that."

Over the past month, he had received a great deal of help from Baek Sogo. In that time, Lee Sungmin had come to feel a genuine goodwill towards her, to the point where he truly hoped she wouldn't die.

*The trials become harsher the more you get used to them.* That felt like a very distant prospect. He still hadn't adapted to the disgusting taste that constantly filled his mouth, nor had he grown used to the crushing weight of his own body.

However, he was not just running in place.

*My progress is fast.*

Four months had passed since Lee Sungmin began his training on Mount Mushii. The Violet Dawn Divine Art had already reached the third stage. Even with the bonuses from being Classless and having a Low-Grade Martial Skeleton, the growth of divine arts like this was typically very slow, yet he had advanced from the second to the third stage in just three months.

*This mountain... it's definitely like that. It's hard, but there are rewards for the trials and penance.*

Perhaps this was the reward Mushii offered. It was not just the Violet Dawn Divine Art that had improved. The spear he swung grew fierce and sharp, forged by the struggle against his body's weight. He was this fast even while conscious of the burden, so how much faster would he be without it? Lee Sungmin's heart pounded every time he imagined it.

*I am getting stronger.*

*I am getting closer to Wiji Hoyeon, my goal.*

Perhaps it was simple self-consolation. While he was getting stronger like this, Wiji Hoyeon would not be standing still. It was entirely possible she had already advanced to a place far beyond his reach.

He refused to think about that.

Wiji Hoyeon was his goal. The existence of that goal allowed Lee Sungmin to immerse himself completely. Every morning, he opened his eyes, dragged his leaden body outside, and swung his spear. He would start with the basic Rannachal, then practice the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear (Sungmin Style). As the sun began to set, he would return to his room to cultivate the Violet Dawn Divine Art.

The days were repetitive but fulfilling. This was the first time he had ever pushed himself to train in such a harsh environment. As the Violet Dawn Divine Art grew, so did his inner qi. The power from Wiji Hoyeon and the Holy Spirit Pill melted into his blood and qi, flowing into his qi center. The abundant natural energy of the mountain accumulated there as well, drawn in through his breathing and the divine art.

The food was still tasteless. He never wanted to eat. He gagged with every meal, swallowing down the real urge to vomit. His body was still heavy. He had to use inner qi just to take a single step. It felt like his muscles were tearing and his bones were crushing every time he swung the spear.

And yet, he did it.

He had to do it.

He wanted to do it.

To reach Wiji Hoyeon. To get closer to his goal.

Perhaps, one day, to surpass her.

"Hello."

Lee Sungmin froze, his spear held mid-swing.

He turned toward the sound and saw a boy who looked to be around his age, smiling brightly. When their eyes met, Lee Sungmin was overwhelmed by a strange sense of disharmony. It was as if dozens, hundreds of stars were floating in the boy's pupils. The moment their gazes locked, the stars within them seemed to explode. He had never seen such brilliant and radiant eyes.

"I'm Flam. Who are you?"

Flam.

In truth, the Lee Sungmin of his past life had never heard that name. He had heard of the Sword Phantom Dok Bijun, Silent Flash Baek Sogo, and Archmage Scarlet Reshir, the three who had lived on this mountain for over half a year, but he had never heard the name of Flam, who had supposedly lived here for over a year.

A man who could easily defeat Dok Bijun and Baek Sogo.

A man who could instantly copy martial arts and magic after seeing them just once.

A true calamity who could make one feel Despair just by meeting him. That was what Baek Sogo had said, but since Lee Sungmin had never heard the name, he had dismissed it as unimportant.

"...Lee Sungmin," he answered, setting down his spear.

He had never imagined that Flam would be the one to come looking for him.
