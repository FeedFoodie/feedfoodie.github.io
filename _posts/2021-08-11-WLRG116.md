Upon his return to the Ghost Wolf Sect, Ju Won went straight to a particular room. He stood in the center of the pitch-black space, a chamber devoid of even a single light, and placed his hand on a crystal ball. The crystal drank the power he poured into it, humming softly for a few moments before its vibrations ceased.

"I met the Void Monarch."

The moment Ju Won spoke, faint lights flickered to life within the room. A brief silence followed before someone else's voice broke it.

"The Void Monarch? I thought he died four hundred years ago."

"He claimed he was sealed."

"Haha! Sealed… right. They called him the nightmare of the South, but even the Void Monarch would have struggled against the subjugation force from back then. Still, it's surprising he managed to survive."

"It's surprising enough that he's alive, but why would he show himself now, after all this time?"

"The Void Monarch didn't have a physical body," Ju Won answered.

His reply was met with a collective silence, as if the others found it unexpected. Words were a bother, so Ju Won simply placed a hand on his own head. A moment later, he drew the memories from his mind and shared them with everyone present.

"......"

Someone let out a small sound of admiration. The beings gathered here at Ju Won's call were the monsters of Predator. These ancient beings, Ju Won included, all remembered the Void Monarch who had once reigned as the nightmare of the South.

"So his body is gone, leaving only a soul, but his Demonic Energy seems intact… Strange, and he's possessing a human. No, not even a human. A suit of armor."

"I've heard that name before, 'the Phantom Spear'. The one who killed that toy Jeniela turned into a vampire a few years back was that same Phantom Spear."

"That's right," a woman's voice replied. "It wasn't a toy I particularly cherished, but it was annoying that it broke."

"The Void Monarch's existence is surprising enough… but this Phantom Spear fellow. There's a strange feel to him. He's clearly human, but that human feeling is faint."

Ju Won had felt it too. The man was unmistakably human, yet there was a subtle but distinct sense that he was detached from humanity.

"Let's watch him for now."

Ju Won had made that judgment when he let Lee Sungmin go. He could have tried to kill him right there, but the ominous aura Lee Sungmin possessed, combined with the existence of the Void Monarch, felt like variables he could not ignore. He had called this sudden meeting simply to inform the others that the Void Monarch was alive. He had no intention of tangling with him, nor with the Phantom Spear who accompanied him.

"It seems they're heading to Trabia."

"Trabia? What business could they have here?"

The one who reacted was the woman called Jeniela. She was an especially powerful being among the vampires and the very person who had turned the Sword Phantom years ago.

"Don't make needless contact with them. I said he doesn't have a body, but the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy was real," Ju Won warned.

Jeniela only burst out laughing at his words.

After the meeting ended, Preskan remained in his chair, lost in thought. He replayed the image of the man called the Phantom Spear, seen through the memories Ju Won had shared. The face was somehow familiar. A memory was only a memory, so the strange aura Ju Won had sensed was not something the others could experience. Preskan had no way of knowing what Ju Won had felt.

"Right. I remember now."

Preskan shot up from his seat. He trembled with a mixture of rage and elation.

"Damn that Heart Thief!"

How many years had it been? He had been attacked by mercenaries in a Dungeon. That part was trivial. Mercenaries were numerous but rarely formidable, though some were treated as true monsters. From Preskan's perspective, however, the ones who came to his Dungeon back then were nothing more than vermin, only slightly better than cockroaches.

There would have been no problem at all. He could have continued his pleasant research in that comfortable Dungeon without any danger or complications, if not for that soul with the twisted causality and Lloyd, the Master of the Golden Magic Tower.

*That bastard is coming here?*

Preskan had created two hearts. Aine possessed one, but the other was lost. At first, he assumed Lloyd had destroyed it and gave up, but Aine's story later revealed that some damn bastard had embedded the heart in his own body.

He remembered the fury he had felt upon learning that fact. The cherished desire he had pursued, the very reason he had become a Lich, the heart he had barely managed to create, was now lodged in the chest of some unknown wretch, and now, finally, he knew. The thief was the mercenary with the twisted causality who had entered his Dungeon.

"I have to catch him and kill him."

Preskan shivered. The existence of the Void Monarch? He didn't give a damn. The man may have been a great yokai who made a name for himself four hundred years ago, but Preskan hadn't even been born then. He knew the other monsters of Predator were wary of him.

*So what.*

Preskan had never wanted to belong to a group like Predator in the first place. Unavoidable circumstances had led him here. He had fled to the North to escape Lloyd, to the Inhuman Demonic Realm of Trabia, a place said to be overflowing with the unorthodox. He never imagined that the Vampire Queen, an ancient monster of Predator, would be residing here. Though she had captured him and forced him to join, Preskan was ready to leave at any given opportunity.

"Just you wait," Preskan spat, consciously grinding teeth he no longer possessed. "I'll rip that heart right out."

*

A month after his encounter with Ju Won, Lee Sungmin was on the verge of reaching Trabia. From a distance, the city's walls and gate appeared large and magnificent, but as he drew closer, he realized it was an illusion. The massive gate was shattered. Its surroundings were a grim tableau of the gate's debris, corpses mangled beyond recognition, and pools of blood, all buried or frozen in the snow.

"......"

A pained sound came from Rubia, who was hiding in Lee Sungmin's arms as a sphere of light. Lee Sungmin ignored it and passed through the broken gate.

The city resembled a ruin. It seemed to be functioning, at least to some extent, but the people he glimpsed looked far from normal. They were filthy vagrants and ill-natured thugs who glanced at Lee Sungmin as he entered but did not approach him rashly.

"But Trabia is the greatest giant city in the North..."

"It used to be."

Only two years had passed since Wiji Hoyeon had cut off the Blood Heavenly Demon's arm. In that short time, the great city of Trabia had become an Inhuman Demonic Realm. Lee Sungmin had learned much about the city on his way here.

He had clashed with the Ghost Wolf Sect before arriving, and its master, Ju Won, was an ancient monster belonging to the inhuman group Predator. Here in Trabia, there existed another old monster who was in no way inferior to him.

[Don't clash with the Vampire Queen,] the Void Monarch repeated, a warning he had given several times already. [Ju Won was at least someone you could reason with. He shows uncontrollable ferocity when he snaps, but he's very rational otherwise.]

"He seemed that way," Lee Sungmin nodded, recalling the calm face of the man who had spoken to him.

[But the Vampire Queen, Jeniela, isn't like that. That bitch is crazy. In a very bad way.]

"Don't you think she's already detected me?"

[Probably, but even if Jeniela is crazy, she won't go on a rampage and try to do something to you here.]

"Because of you?"

[This Elder showed Ju Won that my Demonic Energy is still intact, even without my body. Jeniela might be crazy, but she's a bitch who's thorough about self-preservation. She might try to watch you, but she won't actually try to do anything.]

Lee Sungmin understood. Even if Jeniela tried to interfere, he was not to become hostile. That crazy Vampire Queen might be cautious, but she would not forgive him if he caused friction.

[Perhaps Jeniela is the nobleman the Spirit told you about,] the Void Monarch chuckled.

Lee Sungmin had no intention of clinging to such an uncertain possibility. The 'destiny' the Spirit spoke of would find him as long as he was in the North, whether he took action or not. The problem was that he couldn't properly judge if the encounters he faced were part of that destiny.

"...Where are you planning to go first?"

"To the center of Trabia. According to the information I bought through Erebrisa, that's the only place with any development left."

The huge city was not completely paralyzed. Its center still held strong traces of the giant city it once was. The problem was that, because of this, monsters befitting an Inhuman Demonic Realm roamed its streets.

No one tried to pick a fight with him as he walked. Lee Sungmin had completed the Origin Reversal, which made him appear as if he had never learned martial arts, but he had been intentionally revealing his aura since entering the city to prevent unnecessary trouble. Besides, the people living here near the gate were just thugs and vagrants without any significant skills.

"A carriage."

Someone called out to him.

"You seem to be heading to the Central District. It'll be a hassle to walk. So, take a carriage."

The speaker was a man with a filthy appearance. Lee Sungmin stared at his face before opening his mouth.

"If you try any tricks, you'll die by my hand."

"Hehe! Don't worry, nothing like that will happen. I just happened to be heading to the Central District myself, so I thought I'd ask," the man replied with a laugh.

Lee Sungmin gazed at him. The man was not ordinary. He could feel a certain strength behind his gaze. Slowly, Lee Sungmin nodded.

The price the man asked for was not a large sum. Lee Sungmin paid it and followed him to an old cargo carriage. The man climbed onto the driver's seat and gestured to the cargo bed with his chin.

"Get in."

"You don't have any cargo. Why are you going to the Central District?"

"I have something to do," the man replied and started driving.

The horse ran along the dirty road. The carriage rattled, its ride far from comfortable. It moved so slowly that Lee Sungmin would have been better off just running with his movement arts.

"Why did you come to Trabia?" the man asked.

"Do I have to answer?"

"You're a picky one. Well, if you don't want to answer, don't."

With that, the conversation ended. Lee Sungmin pulled the front of his robe together and curled up. The snowy wind blew in as the carriage moved, but he did not feel the cold. He had his reasons for taking the carriage. It seemed less conspicuous to enter the Central District by hitching a ride rather than walking on his own. Besides, winter was slowly approaching. Perhaps this ride could lead to meeting the nobleman.

"Have you decided on a place to stay?" the man asked after a while.

"I'll decide when I get there."

"If you'd like, how about my place? I can't say it's a magnificent mansion, but it's a decent place to stay."

"No, thank you."

"The inns will be dangerous. There are a lot of vermin and thugs there. Lots of monsters, too," the man chuckled.

Lee Sungmin said no more, and the man fell silent again.

"He sure talks a lot," Rubia grumbled, humming from inside Lee Sungmin's robe. Lee Sungmin agreed with her.

*

Although a fierce wind was not blowing at the moment, the Central District was still in the North. Despite the biting cold, the woman wore only a kimono with no outer coat. Her red hair was tied up and fixed with a long hairpin, leaving the snow-white nape of her neck exposed.

"The smell of alcohol," the woman muttered with a laugh in her voice as she burst through the door. She pushed the door, which was swaying in the draft, closed with her fingertips. As she turned, a liquor bottle flew past her face.

"......"

The bottle shattered against the wall, sending fragments flying, but the woman was not the least bit surprised.

"Get out."

The man sitting on the messy bed spat the words, glaring at her. The woman looked at his distorted eyes and smiled, the corners of her mouth lifting.

"What do you think?" she asked, lifting the hem of her kimono. "They say it's clothing from another world. Isn't it pretty? I wanted to show you..."

"I said, get out," the man spat again, gasping for breath.

His breath was thick with the smell of alcohol. It was so strong that even the woman, standing far away, could smell it. Rather than wrinkling her nose, she gave a pleasant smile.

Once, this man was called the Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon. Just two years ago, he had seized control of Trabia and reigned over it, but after his defeat by the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon, both he and the Blood Heavenly Alliance fell to ruin. The defeat was not merely a loss. As proof of it, Baek Museon became a cripple, his left arm severed.

And the woman.

"You know I won't back down from such harsh words, don't you?"

She was the monster known as the Vampire Queen.
