He could have died satisfied. Vollander truly believed it, but he could not die. This body felt no pain, refusing to perish even when martial arts theory pushed it to the very brink of death.

"Are you human?" Vollander asked. "I don't understand why you're trying to stop me. If Kim Jonghyun's plan succeeds, this world will escape the fate of The End. Connecting to the Great Demon World will bring a great disaster, it's true, but a being like you should be able to survive that invasion without any issue."

"Others will die," Lee Sungmin answered, his tone blunt.

At his words, Vollander let out a low laugh. "That answer makes me even more curious. Surely you don't think of yourself as human, do you? Not with a body like that?"

"Are you saying that to yourself?"

"Indeed." Vollander slowly nodded his head. "However, you and I are different. I am desperate. I'm desperate to become human, to die as a human. What about you? Do you want to save this world? You are destined for ruin. Even if you stop me, and even if you stop Kim Jonghyun's plan, this world cannot escape the fate of The End."

He knew. If Lee Sungmin were alone, there would have been no way, but in this matter, Lee Sungmin was not alone. Abel could do what he could not.

"I don't care what happens, as long as I can become human," Vollander declared. "That's the only reason I've lived until now."

His wish was simple. To become human and die. Whether The End came or the world connected to the Great Demon World was none of his concern.

Lee Sungmin, however, was different. If he failed to stop The End, nothing would change. Even if he killed Vollander here, and even if Kim Jonghyun failed while Abel succeeded, allowing the world to escape its fated destruction, what would he gain?

Ruin was foretold. Vollander was right. No matter which path he chose, ruin was foretold for Lee Sungmin. Either everyone would die with The End, or everyone would live while he became a complete Yokai. If that happened, would Teresa even be able to purify him?

"Come this way," Vollander offered, holding out his hand. "Kim Jonghyun holds you in high regard. If you wish it, just as he can change me into a human, he can make you human as well."

"...It's meaningless." Lee Sungmin shook his head.

Desperation.

Abel's words echoed in his mind. Vollander possessed it too, a desperation to become human. What was he desperate for? Lee Sungmin looked down at his unceasingly spinning spear.

A life better than his previous one.

Becoming stronger.

Seeing the end of martial arts.

Throughout his life, Lee Sungmin had held many goals. He believed he had been desperate for them. He had even achieved a few. A life better than his previous one, he had certainly obtained that. Becoming stronger? He had obtained that as well. The power he now wielded was nothing short of phenomenal.

However, he had not seen the end of martial arts.

"It's just a waste," Lee Sungmin said in a small voice. "In all my life, I haven't felt happiness for very long. I was always busy, you see. There were so many things I had to do."

His life had been a relentless current of events. He had never settled in one place to live a leisurely life. He endured asceticism on Mount Mushii and trained in the halls of Shaolin. He left Shaolin for Mount Hua to kill the Sword Phantom. Realizing his own shortcomings, he trained under Denir. After that, he followed the trail of Envirus to the Sleeping Forest, where he met the Void Monarch and Rubia. He had prevented Baek Sogo's death in the Dungeon, just as he had wished. Then, following the Spirit's words that he would meet a nobleman, he went to the North.

He went through so much there as well. He met the Crazy Heavenly Demon and killed the Blood Heavenly Demon. To keep his promise to Wiji Hoyeon, he traveled to Ruvus, met the Dark Supreme of Heaven Beyond Heaven, and formed his first connection with the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. When he finally met Wiji Hoyeon again after so long, she was weakened by a curse. To break it, he journeyed down to the South.

In Devon, he defeated the remnants of the Void Monarch and was caught up in the Murim Alliance's conspiracy. He met Yana in Urmuri, and also Envirus. He thwarted Arbes's plot. In the process, a vast amount of Demonic Energy flowed into his body, transforming him into something close to a Yokai. He lost the Crazy Heavenly Demon and Rubia. In the end, he went all the way to the Elf Forest, killed the Sword Saint and the Fist Honored One, and broke Wiji Hoyeon's curse.

On and on it went.

That was how Lee Sungmin had lived, caught up in things he had to do rather than things he wanted to do. No, that wasn't entirely true. The things he had to do were, for the most part, things he had genuinely and desperately wanted to do.

Good memories.

There weren't many. He had lost so much on his way here. Rubia, the Crazy Heavenly Demon, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He had not forgotten any of it. He had broken down, little by little, and perhaps he was already completely broken, but he did not stop. He could not stop.

"It's because I'm frustrated." A laugh escaped him. He couldn't help it. A life better than his previous one. He had grown stronger, yet he had so few happy memories. Perhaps his life in his previous incarnation had been happier than this one.

"At least back then, I could feel a cheap sort of happiness blowing the money I earned on drinks after a day's work. Even if I was a low-rank mercenary who could die at any moment, at least I didn't have the anxiety of not knowing when I might turn into a Yokai." He continued, "I died once and returned to the past. I've barely made it this far. In all my life, I don't have many happy memories. Even with all this power, I've never had a chance to properly enjoy it."

KIIIIING... Demonic Energy flowed into the spinning spear as purple currents sparked to life.

"If The End comes, this world is finished. Even if it connects to the Great Demon World, it won't be the world I knew anymore. That is too frustrating and wasteful to accept. That's why I have to stop it."

At those words, Vollander laughed loudly. Frustrating and wasteful. It was the same for him. He had lived for hundreds of years as a Death Knight and had finally found a way to return to being human and die. Therefore, he couldn't stop either, if only out of frustration.

"Come," Vollander said, raising his sword before his chest. "We both find it wasteful and frustrating. We both hold a desperation for our own wishes, and you and I, we are both the same in that we are destined for ruin."

"We're different."

"......"

Extending his spear, Lee Sungmin shook his head.

KWAAANG!

With a sound like tearing reality, space itself ripped open. The sky-piercing technique, amplified by countless rotations, transformed into a colossal bolt of lightning that sought to pierce the very world.

Still, Vollander did not retreat. He advanced into the face of it.

SAAAAK...

An aura of death billowed from him.

THUD.

He kicked off the ground, soaring lightly into the air.

[A battle of attrition is meaningless. Just as you possess an unfathomable amount of inner arts and Demonic Energy, Vollander has achieved complete corruption and obtained immense magic power.]

Vollander's body collapsed into pure darkness, swallowing Lee Sungmin's sky-piercing whole. Within that darkness, a near-infinite number of slashes were generated, chipping away at the colossal technique.

[If anything, you're at a disadvantage. If this fight continues, your Sealing will be shattered. That means you'll become a Yokai. You have to kill him for good, completely.]

So that not even a trace remains.

PWAK!

The sky-piercing was extinguished. The scattered darkness reformed its shape, then began to encroach upon space as it rushed toward Lee Sungmin.

His eyes narrowed. He had grasped it, however slightly. The fight with Vollander had given him a great deal of insight, teaching him how to wield the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's martial arts in a way that suited him.

Heaven Reversal Black Thunder was a martial art of Extreme Speed. Faster than anything else. Sharp, precise. He focused his power, and a purple light gathered at the tip of his spear. The spear that killed the Sword Phantom, the ideal trajectory he had reached in the Mental World, he would now transcend it. The spear that was ideal for the Lee Sungmin of that time was no longer ideal for him now.

Completed martial arts.

He had already seen it. Even though he had never performed it himself, he had witnessed it with his own two eyes and had not forgotten. The path to reach it was clear in his mind.

He saw the back of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. Was it a hallucination? Was it a projection of the Chairman's image, born from intense concentration and repeated self-hypnosis?

It was far.

He took a large step forward, but the distance did not shorten.

*Ah.*

Lee Sungmin naturally understood. This distance was the gap between the Chairman and himself. No matter how wide he made his stride with martial arts theory, the distance would not close. Could he reach if he extended his spear? He gripped the spear tightly with both hands.

In that moment, he didn't think about the forms of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. He went beyond the most ideal Spear Technique he could reliably perform. The Heaven Reversal Black Thunder that the Chairman had shown him. Even if he couldn't perform it perfectly, he would pour his desperation into it.

"Try it."

He heard such a voice. It was the voice of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. It was his back. The Chairman did not look back at him. He had left this world and headed down the path of an Absolute Being. He had chosen to walk that path himself. Yes, that was why he would never look back.

Therefore, Lee Sungmin had to stand by his side.

JIIIIIIJIK!

The current at the spear's tip swelled. The back of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman vanished. Instead, what Lee Sungmin saw were dozens of beggars rushing at him. The Beggars' Gang's Dog Beating Stick Formation. The martial art of the Chairman, who had easily suppressed one of the ultimate formations in murim. The one performing it now was not the Chairman, but Lee Sungmin. He had to perform it not with his bare hands, but with a spear.

"In your own way."

*Yes, in my own way.*

Lee Sungmin's eyes flew open. First, the Myriad Thunder erupted, tearing the world apart with lightning as a thundercloud covered the sky.

At that point, the spear was already fully extended.

*Hahaha!*

The sound of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman's laughter brushed past the tip of the spear.

"Genesis."

When the spear started and reached its end, a new world opened. It could no longer be called spearmanship. The power to alter space itself transcended even the realm of martial arts.

Vollander, charging straight ahead, realized the moment the purple lightning filled his vision just how phenomenal the technique Lee Sungmin had just unleashed was. He saw how shabby his own power, his advancing aura of death, was in comparison. When he suddenly looked back, he saw that there was no one behind him. The legion of Death Knights he led was not by his side.

Even if they were there, nothing would have changed. Vollander laughed and continued to advance. His Ghost Steed surged forward. Riding atop it, he charged with all his might, pointing the sword held in both hands as he plunged into the world of raging lightning.

It was denied. The cherished wish he had held for hundreds of years, the desire to become human and die. Vollander heard the mocking laughter of the thousands of vengeful spirits of Germud. As if to erase the sound of their laughter, he laughed even louder.

"Yes, we are different," Vollander shouted, his voice thick with laughter. "Did my ruin simply come faster than yours?"

First, the sword was erased.

"You were more desperate than I."

The Ghost Steed was erased.

"I pity you."

His body was being erased.

"Because in the end, you won't be able to escape your ruin."

-SHWIIK.

The world that had swelled so large closed once more.

-KWAAAAA!

A belated gust of wind swept through the area, whipping the thick piles of dust into a storm.

Lee Sungmin staggered and collapsed on the spot. He felt empty inside. A throbbing headache felt like it was tearing his brain apart.

"......"

The spear he was holding fell powerlessly to the ground. He looked down at his own hands with trembling eyes. Sensation was dull in his horribly twisted fingers. Slowly, feeling returned as his arms regenerated.

"Keoheok!"

Lee Sungmin spat out the blood that rose in his throat.

He did it. He had followed the path the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had shown him, in his own way.

*Vollander... where is he?* Lee Sungmin gasped for breath and forced himself to stand.

[He has been annihilated,] the Void Monarch answered. [Having been completely annihilated from this world, he has likely returned to the Demon Lord who owns his soul. It is not a complete death, but at least you will never see Vollander in this world again.]

"...Heok... heok..."

[A Black Star of the Predators. One of them has fallen to a human.]

Lee Sungmin picked up the spear that had fallen to the ground.

"...It's not over yet."

Not yet.

He dragged his feet forward, determined to erase the pitying words Vollander had shouted in his final moments.
