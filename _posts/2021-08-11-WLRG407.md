[You... madman...]

The thought came from the Demonic Spirit, who stammered, at a loss for words. He had lived for eons, watching over the repeating world, but he was left speechless by what Lee Sungmin had just done.

A wave of nausea churned in Lee Sungmin's stomach as he climbed out of the cesspool, his heightened senses a curse in that moment. The mind-numbing stench clung to his skin, dripping down his body in foul rivulets. He counted it a blessing that he had not eaten in two days, for if he had, he would have certainly thrown it all up.

"......"

[Why would you do something so crazy?] the Demonic Spirit asked, his tone laced with disbelief.

*It's because of that damn promise,* Lee Sungmin thought, squeezing his eyes shut. He lacked the courage to look directly at his own filth-covered body. He was reminded of the Void Monarch, who would always become docile at the mere threat of being dunked in the cesspool. It was an understandable reaction. Even for a great yokai who once looked down upon the world with his martial theory, being submerged in a cesspool was a horrifying experience.

He used magic to wash the filth and stench from his body, but even after several castings, he still felt grimy. Only then did he retrieve Jeniela's soul bead from his robes. Inside, Jeniela's soul was curled into a tight ball, her head buried in her knees as she muttered curses in a small voice.

[Kyaaaaaaak!]

He scratched the bead's surface with a fingernail, eliciting a sharp scream from within. A quick shake of the bead confirmed she was properly awake. Jeniela's soul jolted, and Lee Sungmin met her trembling eyes before tossing the bead into the cesspool.

[To think the Vampire Queen would fall so low,] the Demonic Spirit mused.

*It's because of the sins she committed.*

[How long are you going to keep her around?]

*Until I get tired of it,* Lee Sungmin replied internally before finally changing into a new set of clothes, yet despite the magic that had erased the stench, cleaned his body, and provided fresh clothes, he did not feel good.

If the Void Monarch were here, he would have at least whined about such a pointless promise, but the Void Monarch was gone. Lee Sungmin felt no desire to grumble to the Demonic Spirit either, leaving him with nothing to do but torment Jeniela.

The thought of the forest tomorrow made his mood sink even lower. It was an unavoidable task. *Will I be able to meet Wiji Hoyeon there? What would I even say? Persuade her? If I had to fight her now...*

His expression hardened, but not because he was imagining a fight with her. He turned his head slowly, a creak seeming to run through his very bones and muscles. The calm that had settled over him churned into turmoil as his heart began to pound. His senses sharpened, and he blocked out Jeniela's screams in his head. Her soul stone had returned from the cesspool, and through it, she sobbed and begged for death. Ignoring her, Lee Sungmin shoved the stone into his Subspace Pocket and clenched his fists.

"It's the Martial God," Lee Sungmin said.

The Demonic Spirit offered no reply. Not far away, the Martial God's energy flared, too blatant to be anything but a lure. It was an open invitation.

[I don't understand his intentions...] the Demonic Spirit muttered. [The Spirit isn't with him. However... I can feel a fragment of the Spirit.]

*A fragment?*

[I'm talking about the Moon Queen.]

The Moon Queen. A corner of Lee Sungmin's mouth twitched into a smirk. He remembered the Elf Revias, whose body the Moon Queen had stolen. There was a personal grudge between them, though he doubted Revias was even conscious of it anymore.

*Perfect.*

Lee Sungmin genuinely thought so. He had been chasing the Martial God, the Moon Queen, and the Spirit Medium for some time now, even attempting to use Frau's sorcery to find them, but he had failed. Now, the Martial God had come to him, blatantly emitting his energy nearby as if to call him over.

[I don't understand the Spirit's intentions,] the Demonic Spirit said. [Until now, the Spirit has avoided a direct conflict with you as much as possible. So why make the Martial God fight you now?]

He was right. When Lee Sungmin had defeated the Spirit Queen in the Sleeping Forest, the Martial God had been nearby. If the Spirit had incited him back then, a fight would have been unavoidable, and his party would have been annihilated nine times out of ten, but the Spirit had not done so. The Martial God had not attacked his exhausted group. The Spirit had not tried to use the Martial God to kill him then. He could not understand why the Spirit would send the Martial God to lure him now.

It felt like an obvious trap, but Lee Sungmin had no desire to avoid it. There was no reason to. The Spirit could not interfere with him, for he was outside of fate. If anything, this was a good opportunity.

CRACKLE!

An electric current sparked through the air, and in that same instant, Lee Sungmin appeared before the Martial God.

The Martial God was slightly surprised by his phenomenal speed but showed no sign of being flustered. He maintained his posture, his hands clasped behind his back and his expression solemn. As the crackling electricity subsided, Lee Sungmin straightened up and slowly raised his head to meet the Martial God's gaze.

"...Hmm."

The Martial God frowned at the sight of Lee Sungmin's gold-tinted eyes. Beside him, the Moon Queen glared with a cold fury.

"Such a wicked aura..." the Martial God muttered, his voice low. Lee Sungmin's mere presence seemed to make the very energy of the space waver. The Martial God's expression hardened as he felt a wickedness that could not be considered human. In that moment, he was certain. The 'thing' before him was surely one of the greatest disasters of The End, and a sense of mission solidified deep in his heart.

"You learned martial arts from Ilcheon, yet you ended up indulging in the wicked power of the Demonic Spirit."

Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh at the solemn, scolding words, doubting he had heard correctly.

"Or does the disciple inevitably follow the Master?" the Martial God continued, undeterred. "Even without being intoxicated by the power of the Demonic Spirit, Ilcheon's martial arts were magnificent when he was still 'human'. To think that both master and disciple would indulge in such power."

Lee Sungmin clenched his empty fists and stared into the Martial God's eyes. They were truly clear, hardly cloudier than those of the Sword Immortal, who had spent most of his life on Mount Wudang. The Martial God felt not a hint of falsehood or wavering in his own words. He 'truly' believed his actions were to stop The End. He believed the strength of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman who had overwhelmed him came not from martial arts but from the Demonic Spirit's power. It was the same now. Lee Sungmin found himself quite curious whether such blind faith was caused by the Spirit or if the Martial God was just naturally like that.

"Aren't you ashamed?" the Martial God roared. "Even if you're intoxicated with the Demonic Spirit's power, you were once a martial artist on a righteous path! To use such a vulgar..."

"The Martial God."

Lee Sungmin cut in, refusing to listen to the rest of the scolding. He glanced behind him at the large castle gate, where soldiers on the walls stared down with wide eyes.

"Have you... never doubted what you believe?"

"Do not try to delude this one," the Martial God replied, his expression firm.

KUOOOOO!

A blue light erupted from beneath his feet, engulfing his body in a massive flame. Within the brilliant blue enhanced qi, the Martial God's hair shot upwards, and the sleeve of the left arm he had lost to the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman flapped wildly.

"This one has never once doubted his own beliefs. Rather, this one wishes to ask you. You saw your Master's miserable death, so why didn't you stop? You were close to a monster back then, but not as much as you are now. Have you never held any affection for this world? Why have you become a monster of The End, ruining and trampling the world yourself to bring about its finish?"

His shout became a Lion's Roar, a resounding cry that shook the castle walls. The soldiers on the walls screamed, clapping their hands over their ears, but the Martial God did not stop.

"Everyone has the right to choose death. Everyone desires life, not death. The End is a terrible conclusion that tramples the wishes of everyone in this world and deceives all who live. This one will never wish for such an end. This one will stop that conclusion, even if it means sacrificing everything!"

"......"

The Moon Queen trembled as she listened to his cry. The soldiers, their spirits shaken by the Lion's Roar, looked from the Martial God to Lee Sungmin. They did not understand everything he was shouting, but they were overwhelmed by the man yelling about The End before the castle gate.

"Listen!" More power infused the Martial God's roar. "Soon, the world will be destroyed. That is because it is the predetermined end of fate! That is The End! Behold! The monster before this one is the monster of monsters that executes The End. Ten years ago, the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman Yang Ilcheon was the monster who executed The End, and now, his disciple has become the new monster!"

*He's just spouting whatever he wants,* Lee Sungmin thought, listening with a sense of dumbfounded disbelief.

"Killing you won't stop The End."

THUD! The Martial God took a forceful step forward. The blue flames enveloping his body grew fiercer, and his eyes shone with determination and a sense of mission.

"However, killing you will postpone today's destruction by a day. Just as we gained a ten-year reprieve by killing the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman!"

"Enough."

He did not want to hear any more. He had endured much and listened to more than enough. A flicker of pity sparked within him as he heard the Martial God's heartfelt cries, for the man was nothing more than the Spirit's complete puppet. He did not doubt his beliefs in the slightest. He was just being swayed by the Spirit, and Lee Sungmin did not think the Martial God himself was much of a villain. His desire to free this world from the fate of The End was sincere.

"Don't talk so much."

Even so, it was unpleasant. He was pissed off, displeased, annoyed, and filled with hatred. He had endured a lot. He had listened a lot. Lee Sungmin looked at the Martial God's eyes, which were filled with determination and a sense of mission. He wanted to gouge out those eyes that held not a shred of doubt and rip out the tongue that spoke his master's name with such false familiarity.

"Aren't you ashamed?" Lee Sungmin asked as he slowly walked forward.

CRACKLE!

A violet current sparked, and a long spear appeared in his hand.

"It's not like you killed him with your own strength."

At those words, the Martial God's eyebrow twitched. Lee Sungmin did not miss the momentary agitation and let out a hollow, sneering laugh.

"Ah, so you *do* feel shame. Ten years ago, you only survived because of my master's mercy."

"Shut up."

"Don't act so righteous. You couldn't do it alone, so you ganged up on him with the Moon Queen."

"This one's power was simply insufficient to face a monster of The End. Sending off Ilcheon, who had become a monster and gained an absurd power, with this one's own hands was an act of friendship for one I once shared..."

"Hahaha!"

Lee Sungmin could not hold back his laughter at the Martial God's words.

"What amazing self-justification," he sneered, his face twisting into a grin. "My master could have killed you."

The Martial God's shoulders trembled. He recalled the memory from that time, a memory he thought he had overcome. The technique of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, who had erased all of his martial arts with a single, effortless gesture.

"But he didn't kill you. He stopped after taking just one arm... How is it now? Is life as a one-armed man bearable?"

"Do not... delude this one..."

"I'm being sarcastic." Lee Sungmin gripped the Formless Spear with both hands as light gathered at its tip. "The reason he stopped at tearing off one arm was because my master respected you, and because he believed in me, his disciple. However... you... you don't respect my master. Still, that's a relief. It seems you at least know shame."

"You bastard...!"

Fire lit up in the Martial God's eyes.

THUD!

With a heavy sound, his body vanished.

"Ten years," Lee Sungmin muttered in a small voice.

CRACKLE!

The violet electric current surrounding his body sparked in all directions.

"It took a damn long time to get my revenge."

Lee Sungmin's spear shot toward the space where the Martial God had been.
