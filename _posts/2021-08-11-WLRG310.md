Though Lee Sungmin tried to ignore Vollander's words, the Void Monarch contemplated them deeply.

Slaughter Predator.

It was the king of Predators, the very being Jeniela had glimpsed through the Eye of Futuresight. Even the Void Monarch had to admit that in the current world, the monster closest to becoming a Slaughter Predator was none other than Lee Sungmin. If the Demonic Energy, currently suppressed with Osla's help, were to finally explode and eradicate his humanity, Lee Sungmin might become the Slaughter Predator itself.

*Jeniela instigated a clash between this guy, Kim Jonghyun, and Vollander.*

She had deliberately informed Lee Sungmin and the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman about Kim Jonghyun. In truth, Jeniela had never seen a future where Lee Sungmin appeared, but since neither he nor the Void Monarch knew this, they could only assume she had sent him here intentionally.

*That wench is acting for a future where the Slaughter Predator appears. She's designing the future. Perhaps, just as Vollander said...*

Vollander stepped out from the wreckage, slowly extending his sword. Lee Sungmin knew persuasion was useless. Vollander would not stop, not for the sake of his own personal wish. He understood that, but he could not simply stand aside for Vollander's sake.

To die as a human. Lee Sungmin knew what reincarnation meant, and for a Death Knight, death was not truly death. If it were possible, he would have helped Vollander and the other Death Knights become human again, but that was impossible. Not enough sacrifices had been gathered. Turning them back into humans would require the deaths of tens of thousands more, a process during which Kim Jonghyun would steadily continue his work of connecting the Great Demon World and Eria.

Therefore.

"......"

A black light flashed as a dark slash cut through space. Lee Sungmin leaped forward, unleashing Homing Chain Lightning Flash and Bloody Pursuit from the tip of his spear. Hundreds of spear tips erupted into a meteor shower that crossed the space before him, crushing a path forward. The condensed black light at the tip of Vollander's sword shone relentlessly in response.

He swung his sword and created night, dyeing the entire space black. Within that darkness, a single purple bolt of lightning flashed.

KWADEDEUK!

The thrusting spear drove Vollander's sword back. The blade, sent flying, changed its shape and became an axe.

Lee Sungmin used his movement arts to dodge the axe as it struck down at his head. Even as his posture crumbled, he thrust his spear out, its movement based on martial arts theory, and grazed Vollander's helmet. The light in Vollander's eyes shifted. He was now holding two swords, which he used to launch a cross-shaped slash at Lee Sungmin.

A Protective Qi Aura rose to block Vollander's attack. The light behind his helmet widened. *Was he surprised that it was blocked without injury at this distance?* Lee Sungmin wondered. Vollander seemed to understand, nodding his head. He took a few steps back and raised his sword.

"Fighting like a human won't work," he muttered.

As if to demonstrate his meaning, Vollander's sword fell. From the seams of his armor, which was his body itself, black darkness surged forth. Vollander spread his arms at the center of the wide-opening Death Mask.

The darkness became his cloak. He was proficient with all sorts of weapons, but that did not make him a martial artist of the Transcendent Realm. In the field of the sword, Vollander was inferior not only to the Sword Immortal but even to the Sword Saint. With the spear, he was not as skilled as Lee Sungmin. He had fought in such a way until now because, even as a Death Knight who had lived for hundreds of years, his consciousness as a human remained strong.

He just could not abandon it, not even after his body had become a cold shell with no warmth or blood. He had always longed for the day he would return to being human, but his opponent was arguably the most formidable foe he had ever faced. He could not win by fighting like a human.

Yes, Vollander wanted to win. He wanted it even if his actions went against the unorthodox principles he had upheld his entire life. In the end, his obsession with his personal wish was stronger than his principles.

He surrendered his body to corruption.

KUWAAAAAH!

Darkness swept in from all directions. Lee Sungmin's eyes widened. He quickly kicked off the ground and leaped into the air, knowing he could not fight here. Behind him was an army of thousands, as well as Abel and Frau.

[I'm moving the location.]

Abel nodded at the urgent Thought Transmission. They had successfully drawn out Vollander and the Death Knights, who had been the primary obstacles. With Lee Sungmin handling Vollander, Kim Jonghyun was now isolated. Abel looked toward Teresa, who stood at the center of a dazzling light, aiding the Holy Knights and the others.

*Leave this place to them.*

Abel gave Frau a look. She stood with her arms crossed, letting out a deep sigh before nodding. She smacked the back of Aladur's head, who was darting his eyes around as if ready to flee at any moment, and spat.

"Let's go."

"......"

Aladur replied instantly, his hesitation vanishing. Abel, Frau, and Aladur moved toward the location where they had already determined Kim Jonghyun was hiding.

Having leaped into the air, Lee Sungmin could no longer pay them any mind. The darkness chased him, a dense mass that was Vollander himself. Lee Sungmin used Gale Thunder to cross the empty air, heading to a place where others would not be caught in the fight, to a position where he would not interfere with Abel.

"It seems you don't want to see others die, do you?" Vollander's voice echoed from within the darkness.

Lee Sungmin flipped his body in mid-air. Below his feet, he could see buildings and wandering Undead, but no signs of people.

SHWIRIRIRIK!

The spear began to rotate, instantly creating a Sky Piercing Lightning Strike that tore through the darkness.

"......"

The darkness simply expanded. The hole pierced by the sky-piercing strike filled in immediately. The ominous gloom was like the power of the Demon Lord he had encountered through Preskan in the past.

[It's the real thing. The completely corrupted Vollander is using the Demon Lord's power directly.]

The surrounding sky was dyed black. The encroaching darkness held a chilling sharpness, a savage and chaotic edge different from the Sword Immortal's. It was a muddled killing intent that craved only death.

It rushed toward him. Lee Sungmin fixed his body in mid-air and gripped his spear tightly. The ultimate technique of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, which he had received from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, filled his mind.

A line.

He had to become a line. His thrusting spear had to be a single line, his moving body another, and he had to connect them endlessly, just as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had once covered the Snowfield with ink-black lines.

He squeezed out the inner qi from his qi center. The Demonic Energy boiled. He could not break the Sealing. Lee Sungmin swallowed a deep breath, the coppery taste of blood filling his mouth. His body, now close to that of a Yokai, craved blood even from a Death Knight through whom none flowed.

It exploded.

Inner arts and Demonic Energy flooded his body. Lee Sungmin's eyes shone with a golden light. He kicked off the air, holding the spear in one hand.

The spear, swung with one hand toward the rushing darkness, created a cascade of purple lightning that embroidered the black sky. His appearance was like that of the Thunder God, yet the darkness, though suffused with light, did not tear. The lines Lee Sungmin created were not enough to overcome Vollander's power.

He knew. He was not the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. He could want to resemble him, he could want to become like him, but he was not him. All he could do was try to perfectly replicate what the Chairman had done. The distance between them was still great. Lee Sungmin had no choice but to follow the path he had walked, doing his best in his own way.

A path opened in his mind, one he was being guided along, inherited from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman.

He gripped the spear with both hands.

Demon Emperor Yang Ilcheon had not used a spear, but Lee Sungmin did. Yes, the method was different from the very start. The path shown to him by the Chairman was one Lee Sungmin had to walk in his own way.

Bones broke. Muscles tore. Lee Sungmin's level was too low to walk the path he saw in his head.

The blood that burst forth inside his armor was hot. There was no pain. He consciously ignored it, knowing the wounds would regenerate in an instant anyway. Just as Vollander had given up on fighting as a human, Lee Sungmin was now doing the same.

A clash.

The darkness exploded. For a moment, the sky was visible through a gaping hole, but it was filled in an instant. Sharpness rushed in from all directions, aiming for his body.

Dodging was impossible. He swelled his Protective Qi Aura to its fullest and moved his spear, rotating his body to thrust in every possible direction and filling the gaps with swings. Little by little, a line was created, a purple line forged by Lee Sungmin within the black darkness.

A crushing force pressed down on his body. Lee Sungmin and Vollander's darkness, which had enveloped him, plummeted to the ground.

The surrounding area was completely annihilated. It was a good thing he had moved locations. There were only Undead around, so there was no longer a need to control his power. The smell of blood was potent. His wounds regenerated, but the blood from his body left him damp before his heated body evaporated it all.

Knowing he no longer had to hold back, Lee Sungmin drew out even more power. The energy he could use still showed no signs of bottoming out. He drew out all of his fighting spirit inner qi, and the inner qi of the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman filled the void. Lightning flowed through the qi and blood of his entire body.

Faster, even faster.

He recalled the mantra of the Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, which pursues Extreme Speed, and unleashed the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. From the first strike to the seventh form, the Spear Technique unfolded in an instant. Space repeatedly shattered and exploded. Not even a trace of the building wreckage remained. The outskirts of Germud, where he and Vollander were clashing, had become a plain without a single building.

Within the exploding darkness, Vollander's body stirred and lunged toward Lee Sungmin.

What weapon was he holding? A sword, a spear, an axe? Or something else? *Don't think.* It did not matter what weapon Vollander used. As long as he was faster.

Yes. As long as he was faster.

The silently extended spear shattered a shoulder. The arm was completely destroyed. All the bones and muscles activated in the thrust were shattered. Half of his body met death. A human would have died from this alone, but not Lee Sungmin. His mind endured the pain that felt like it would drive him mad.

"......"

A small sound was made. The weapon Vollander held vanished without a shape. In the time it took to take a single step, Lee Sungmin's body regenerated.

The fully charged Demonic Energy made his body move. He heard laughter by his ear, 'my' laughter. He ignored it, walked forward, and extended his spear once more. This time, too, there was no sound. It was too fast.

"......"

Vollander's leg was shattered. His armor and weapon were his very soul, forged with the power of a Demon Lord, but the soul of Vollander, who sought to achieve his wish even by corrupting himself, was soft before Lee Sungmin's speed.

It was a speed he himself could not control, so excessive that it shattered his own body, yet Lee Sungmin felt a sense of calm. At this tremendous speed, the single purple line created by his spear quieted his mind.

*More.*

He took a step. He extended the spear.

*More, more.*

The Unorthodox Alliance Chairman was faster than this. His movements were natural and effortless, not accompanied by physical collapse or pain. He could not become a disciple who would bring his master shame.

*I, who have received everything from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, must go as far as he did, no, even farther.*

{sep}

"How noisy."

Kim Jonghyun muttered while looking at the crystal ball. The clash occurring on the outskirts of Germud sent vibrations all the way to where he was, but now was not the time to worry about that. Kim Jonghyun let out a smirk.

The clash between the Holy Knights and the Death Knights was evenly matched. In terms of pure strength, the Death Knights should have been overwhelming, but the power emanating from the Saint halved their strength while bolstering that of the Holy Knights. The crowd of thousands had also become a nuisance. Still, the Death Knights were not being easily pushed back. The situation would remain a stalemate for the time being.

*Even if I wanted to step in... I can't.*

Kim Jonghyun slowly put on his robe. He gripped his staff and held the Grimoire in one hand. Pulling the hood over his head, he was satisfied with his appearance. He looked like an evil Black Mage straight out of a story. The current Kim Jonghyun fit that popular image perfectly.

*The archmage and the Grand Sorcerer... I can't grasp the full extent of their power.*

At least he knew about Frau. She was said to be at the pinnacle of Ghost Soul Art, a branch of sorcery with a different direction from necromancy. In a sense, it could be called the natural enemy of necromancy, different from holy magic.

*The Mages Guild Master has Gries.*

He had not been able to figure out what kind of magic Gries possessed, which was a bit of a shame. Still, he would be able to find out this time. The hem of his robe dragging behind him, Kim Jonghyun walked forward to greet Abel and Frau. The black sphere floating behind his back emitted a quiet vibration.

*I don't know what they're aiming for.*

However, he had no intention of being easily defeated.

Kim Jonghyun felt like he would go crazy with how much he was enjoying his current situation.
