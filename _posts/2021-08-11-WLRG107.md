The attack tore through the resistance of his Protective Qi Aura. It was a Deathly Flash, a lethal mixture of enhanced qi and Demonic Energy. In the Mental World, he could unleash it endlessly, but in reality, the technique was brutally taxing on his physical form. Even after opening his Conception and Governing Vessels to become a Supreme Peak master and achieve Body Reformation, his body was still incomparably weaker than his spirit. The ultimate techniques of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear were divine arts that placed a tremendous burden on even his reforged frame.

Now, however, things were different. Aided by the Demonic Energy supplied by the Void Monarch, Lee Sungmin's body could just barely execute the level of martial arts he had perfected in his mind.

The Doppelganger's body was thrown back. The creature did not vomit blood or groan in pain. A monster completely devoid of such sensations, it moved to counterattack even with a wound that would have been instantly fatal to any real person. It was a vicious, self-destructive assault that completely disregarded its own safety.

*My arm...!*

He felt the muscles he used to execute the Deathly Flash rupture. His weakening hand tried to let go of the spear, but Lee Sungmin bit down hard on his lip and held on. He withdrew the extended weapon and retreated, executing the Dancing Shadow Soul Break as the Doppelganger, a gaping hole in its chest, spread its arms wide.

KWAKWAKWA!

A vortex of black enhanced qi swirled around her, looking less like a human's martial art and more like a natural disaster. The raging torrent of power swept through the area. Lee Sungmin resisted by simultaneously drawing upon his Demonic Energy and his own enhanced qi.

[Impressive,] the Void Monarch said with a chuckle. [That wench... she's not even the real one, yet she wields such power. Is that truly human?]

Lee Sungmin felt the Void Monarch's Demonic Energy surge into him. He gasped for breath as his heart pounded like a war drum. Strength returned to his buckling legs, and his shattered arms began to regenerate.

*Can I win...?*

[That depends on you. I am merely lending you my energy. Or... will you surrender your body to me?] the Void Monarch asked. [If you give me your body, I can turn that half-baked thing into a pile of meat in an instant. I could even win if the real one showed up. Will you do it?]

He kept asking, his words both a temptation and a test. Lee Sungmin could not understand what the ancient being intended with his questions.

The answer, however, was already decided.

*I have to do it.*

[Hahaha! You, who are borrowing my power, say you'll do it yourself? Is this your last shred of pride, or something of that sort?]

*That's right. It's my pride. My body is too weak. There are things I can do, things I want to try, but my body can't keep up yet.*

Lee Sungmin answered in his mind, gritting his teeth. The Doppelganger's strength and Wiji Hoyeon's true strength had to be different, but he was certain of one thing. His two thousand one hundred years of training were not meaningless. He could see it faintly, the transcendent strength that Wiji Hoyeon possessed, the peak she had reached. He could see a way to stand against it.

He knew because he had already experienced it. If he could use his body and martial arts exactly as he had at the end of his training in the Mental World, he would not be struggling like this. Lee Sungmin was sure of it. At the same time, an overwhelming sense of accomplishment and fulfillment washed over him.

Two thousand one hundred years were not meaningless.

[A strange... fellow.]

The Void Monarch vaguely empathized with the emotions coursing through Lee Sungmin. Having no physical body of his own, the Monarch's soul was bound to a massive lump of Demonic Energy, which now resided within Lee Sungmin's body and was channeled through the Magic Armor. Half-assimilated with Lee Sungmin, the Void Monarch could not fully control his body, but even this partial possession allowed him to feel the emotions his host was experiencing. He did not understand the sense of fulfillment Lee Sungmin felt, but he knew that the answer, 'I have to do it,' was not just baseless stubbornness.

[Fine. I'll lend you more.]

[Can you withstand it?]

The words were ambiguous, a reflection of the Void Monarch's own uncertainty. In that same moment, an immense power, incomparable to anything before, flooded Lee Sungmin's body. It felt as if his qi center was shattering. The meridians that circulated his inner qi were forcibly expanded, tearing in the process. An incredible pain erupted in the left side of his chest as the Black Heart pounded wildly, taking in both the inner qi and the Demonic Energy.

He wanted to scream. He had never felt such horrific pain in his life. He could not, however, let the pain dull his senses. The storm of enhanced qi that had raged around the Doppelganger now swept toward him. Amidst the dizzying agony, Lee Sungmin reached for his spear. It was already in his hand.

Nine Dragons Slaughter.

The nine dragons clashed with the storm of enhanced qi. His inner qi were already depleted, but there was no need to chew the remaining half of the Great Cycle Pill and the Magic Stone. Demonic Energy filled the void. The nine dragons, stained with an ominous color, writhed in the air before opening their maws wide and devouring the storm.

A void was left where the clashing powers had vanished. The Doppelganger staggered slightly, then unleashed an even greater power than before. The martial might emanating from the monster had long since transcended that of a human. As the Void Monarch let out a cry of admiration, Lee Sungmin walked forward. The spear in his hand hummed and vibrated. He glanced down to see the shear shaft was dented and the spearhead was cracked.

*I just got it repaired not long ago.*

*Celgerus is going to give me an earful.* Lee Sungmin smiled bitterly as he fully opened his qi center and meridians. The pain was still present, but he kept moving. He had to.

WHOOOSH!

A purplish Demonic Energy enveloped the entire spear. The black enhanced qi covering the Doppelganger's body gathered in her hands. *This is the last one.* Lee Sungmin felt it instinctively. Not the Doppelganger's attack, but his own. The Black Heart was pumping the Demonic Energy, but anything more was impossible. *If I can't end it with this one strike...*

*No. I can end it.* He was certain of it. With that certainty, he prepared his technique. Soul Chasing Elimination, Homing Chain Lightning, or Serpent Soul Devouring would be impossible. The power the Doppelganger was gathering was not something Nine Dragons Slaughter could block, and there was no guarantee that Deathly Flash could pierce through that swelling enhanced qi. That left the next one. He had to choose a technique he had not used yet, one he could not use before.

The sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear.

Any technique beyond that was impossible. Those were forms he had barely managed to reach even with harsh training over a long time in the Mental World. With his current body, it was impossible. Then when would it be possible?

*I can do it.*

The sixth form of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear. Void Path.

The purple enhanced qi covering the spear swelled. Gripping it with both hands, Lee Sungmin thrust the spear forward with all his might. At that moment, the Doppelganger's enhanced qi exploded and rushed toward him. The instant it made contact, a large hole was torn through the wave of energy. The Void Path enveloped the Doppelganger's attack completely, then scattered it in all directions with a spin. Lee Sungmin endured the pressure on his hands and threw his body forward.

KWAAAAANG!

The walls made of flesh all exploded, bursting apart from just being struck by the scattered fragments. Lee Sungmin found his way through the mind-numbing sensation of floating. The path he had to take was where he had thrust his spear. His body shot forward, piercing through the empty channel. At the end of it stood the Doppelganger with a pale face.

That face.

POWOOOONG!

That was the last sound. The fragments of enhanced qi that had scattered in all directions dispersed into a mist of purple and black. Watching it dissipate, Lee Sungmin looked down at the Doppelganger wearing Wiji Hoyeon's face.

With half of its body gone, the creature could no longer move. Lee Sungmin looked into its blinking eyes and opened his mouth to say something, but he ended up closing it again without a word. It would be meaningless. That thing had Wiji Hoyeon's face, but it was not her.

He pressed his lower lip firmly and looked at his hands. The spear no longer had the shape of a weapon, unable to handle the power he had poured into it. Lee Sungmin sighed and slung the remains of the spear onto his back.

In that time, the Doppelganger's eyes closed.

It was completely dead.

[You won,] the Void Monarch muttered. [How does it feel? That was an opponent you couldn't have beaten on your own. You defeated such an opponent by borrowing my power, not your own. Are you proud?]

"I don't feel anything," Lee Sungmin muttered as he bent down. He reached out and rummaged through the Doppelganger's corpse. He had worried it might have been destroyed by the Void Path, but he felt something within the remains. It was an ore about the size of a fist. It was not a Magic Stone. Lee Sungmin tilted his head in confusion before putting it into his Subspace Pocket for the time being.

[Prepare yourself,] the Void Monarch said.

"Prepare for what?"

[I am going to withdraw the Demonic Energy I poured into your body. The backlash will likely be severe. If you don't keep a firm grip on your consciousness, you will die.]

*It hurt like death when I was receiving it, and there's more?* Lee Sungmin was momentarily dumbfounded, but he nodded and sat down on the spot. It would be strange if there was not a price to pay for using that much power. He gritted his teeth and answered in his mind.

*I'm ready.*

[Hahaha!]

With the Void Monarch's laughter, the Demonic Energy that had entered his body was sucked out. The energy erupted from his body, swirled in the air, and then settled back into the Magic Armor he wore.

He almost lost consciousness.

Lee Sungmin was no stranger to pain, but the agony he felt now was so intense it made all his past suffering feel like child's play. His qi center and meridians felt as if they were being torn apart, and his muscles felt like they were exploding. His bones grew chillingly cold as if filled with ice, then searingly hot as if heated by fire, while his blood felt like it was flowing in reverse. Lee Sungmin's mouth fell open as he made gasping, choking sounds.

[Is it bearable?]

The Void Monarch's voice sounded distant. Lee Sungmin writhed in agony for a long time. *If you don't keep a firm grip on your consciousness, you will die.* What the Void Monarch had said was true. The pain was so horrific that the moment he lost consciousness, his brain, unable to endure it, would have chosen death for itself.

How much time had passed? The pain, which had felt like an eternity, finally ended. Lee Sungmin opened his closed eyes. His entire body was drenched in a cold sweat, and his lips, which he had bitten several times, were bloody. He tried to get up, but he could not properly muster any strength in his body.

"A-are you okay?"

Rubia was nearby. She had been pacing anxiously and rushed over as soon as Lee Sungmin regained his senses. He had barely managed to sit halfway up, and he gasped for breath as he looked at her.

"You could have at least done something."

"I did! I was busy wiping your sweat and blood!"

"Don't you know how to use healing magic?"

"I don't know how," Rubia replied, pouting.

Still, it seemed she had not done absolutely nothing. Her robe was soaked with blood and sweat. It looked like she had used her own clothes to wipe him down since there was nothing else.

"How much... time has passed?"

"About an hour..."

So he had been in pain for an entire hour. Lee Sungmin let out a hollow laugh and used the spear shaft as a cane to pull himself up. His legs felt weak, but he had to move.

"Wh-what are you doing?"

"I have to go," Lee Sungmin answered, panting. The throbbing was severe, but his bones were not actually damaged. His muscles ached, but he could endure this much.

He looked at the minimap in the corner of his vision, which he had been unable to pay attention to while fighting the Doppelganger. Besides himself, there was only one other presence left in the Dungeon. He had to check.

Lee Sungmin dragged his feet, heading back the way he came. The surroundings were a complete mess, but thanks to the minimap, finding his way was not too difficult. It was confirmed that all the other Doppelgangers were dead, so even in his bad shape, he moved without hesitation.

He saw the Blood Granny's corpse. The horribly mangled body still bore the marks of an axe and palm strikes. Lee Sungmin panted as he looked around.

"There are... traces of magic here," Rubia said, cautiously watching his expression.

"...Magic? What kind of magic?"

"I'm not sure... just a moment."

Complex magic circles unfolded around Rubia. She closed her eyes for a moment, then hesitated and glanced at Lee Sungmin.

"It's fine," he said with a nod. At his words, Rubia sighed and explained.

"...A transportation magic was cast. It was probably magic to move outside the Dungeon."

There was no way Baek Sogo, Jang Deuksu, or Chwi Geol could cast such high-level magic. It was probably a scroll.

"...Can you tell me in a little more detail?"

"Ah... yes. Three people were transported by the escape magic. I don't know exactly who left, but..."

"I understand."

That was enough.

Baek Sogo did not die. She escaped the Dungeon with Chwi Geol and Jang Deuksu. That was all that mattered. He could be satisfied. He had entered this Dungeon to save her, and it was no exaggeration to say he had come this far because he wanted to prevent her death.

He had achieved it.

"...In that case..."

Lee Sungmin started moving again, panting. Seeing him, a flustered Rubia hurried to his side.

"Wh-where are you going?"

"Forward," Lee Sungmin answered, swallowing the pain in his body. "I have to go forward."

He looked at the minimap.

There was a yellow dot at the end of the Dungeon.
