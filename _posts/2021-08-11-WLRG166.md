"They say Jaegal Taeryeong is dead."

The news reached the Righteous Heavenly Sword Lord, Jaegal Yuhwan. Although he had stepped back from the front lines to live a life that was no different from retirement, public perception was a lie. In secret, Jaegal Yuhwan still led the Jaegal Clan. It might have been a different story if his son, Jaegal Taeryeong, had been a man skilled in both martial arts and literature, but that was not the case. While his martial arts were decent, his mind was not particularly brilliant, leaving Jaegal Yuhwan in a position where he could not step back with any peace of mind.

"So it has come to this in the end," Jaegal Yuhwan muttered, his hands clasped behind his back.

Jaegal Taeryeong, his eldest son, had been a dullard who fell short of his expectations in so many ways. His martial arts were excellent but not astounding, and his intellect was only enough to draw a sigh. It was for this reason that Jaegal Yuhwan had pushed him toward martial arts rather than academics from a young age. Neither path satisfied him, but he thought it better to nurture the side his son was at least somewhat good at.

In the end, even that failed to meet his expectations. Despite learning the secret martial arts of the Jaegal Clan and being bathed in miracle herbs since childhood, Taeryeong had barely managed to reach the Supreme Peak. In truth, the skill of a Supreme Peak was not lacking, but Jaegal Yuhwan had wanted a son who could reach a realm beyond it.

"What will you do about the next successor?" a voice asked from the shadows.

"She is still young, but Yeong-ah's talent is better than the deceased Taeryeong's," Jaegal Yuhwan answered without looking back. "We will place our hopes on her."

Jegal Young. Although she was only thirteen, her talent in Jaegal Yuhwan's eyes was far superior to her older brother's. With enough time and support, she would smoothly enter the Supreme Peak, and depending on her efforts, she could even aim for a realm beyond that. Of course, she was still an unscratched lottery ticket, and he did not know if she would be a winner, but after comparing the hopeless Jaegal Taeryeong with the promising Jegal Young, Jaegal Yuhwan ultimately leaned towards his youngest child.

"There's still time before the Heavenly Dragon Association is established, but we can't afford to be complacent. It's going to get busy."

"What a hassle in my old age," Jaegal Yuhwan grumbled, looking up at the sky. "All because I had one useless son..."

A long time ago, the heads of the Four Great Clans had made a promise. It was something said while they were all drunk at a party, but as the years passed, ambition was added to that promise. The Heavenly Dragon Association, an organization that would gather the power of the Four Great Clans and other Noble Clans into one. Jaegal Yuhwan, however, knew the truth. Even if the Heavenly Dragon Association were truly created, it would be unable to do anything about the Murim Alliance it sought to crush.

"I'll keep my promise, so don't worry," a smooth voice said.

"I'm not doubting you, Chairman," Jaegal Yuhwan replied, turning around.

A man was sitting in the center of the spacious room, drinking alone at a table laden with delicacies. He wore spotless white clothes, and his black hair was tied up in a topknot. His smiling yellow eyes were inhumanly sharp and deep, and despite being a man, he possessed a beauty that could rival any woman's. This was the Murim Alliance Chairman, the Black Dragon Knight.

Jaegal Yuhwan felt a strange sensation whenever he saw him. The Black Dragon Knight was not human. Though said to be half-human, he was a hybrid of a Dragon, a creature not seen for several hundred years, and a human. It had not been long since the Black Dragon Knight appeared in the murim and made a name for himself, but no one knew what the half-dragon had done before his appearance, or just how long he had lived.

"It has no name," the Black Dragon Knight said, reaching into his robes. He produced a book as clean as if it were new. "However, it is undoubtedly this one's martial art. Give it a suitable name, and either improve it or use it as is. With Jegal Young's talent, you should see great benefits in just a few years."

"Thank you," Jaegal Yuhwan replied with a slight bow of his head.

"I'm sorry about Jaegal Taeryeong."

"He was a worthless son."

"Even so, blood is blood. Once Jegal Young learns that martial art and reaches a certain level, bring her to the Murim Alliance. I'll take her as my disciple."

"Hmm...!" Jaegal Yuhwan's eyes widened.

The world severely underestimated the Black Dragon Knight's martial prowess. This was partly because he had not been particularly active since appearing in the murim, but also because he himself had hidden his true strength. Jaegal Yuhwan, however, knew well that the Black Dragon Knight was a master whose skills were not inferior even when compared to the Sword Immortal secluded in Wudang. If Jegal Young became his disciple, she would be able to reach the Supreme Peak more easily and even aim for the Transcendent Realm.

"Thank you," he said again, his voice filled with sincerity.

"It is simply what this one wishes to do," the Black Dragon Knight replied, lifting the liquor bottle. "Besides, how long are you going to stand there? Come and have a drink."

Jaegal Yuhwan sat down opposite him. "...But why are you going to such lengths against the Phantom Spear?"

"Because he's an eyesore," the Black Dragon Knight answered simply. "The Phantom Spear's martial prowess is underestimated. In this one's view, he will soon reach the Transcendent Realm."

"Is he that skilled?"

"The Phantom Spear already stands at the pinnacle of the Supreme Peak. With the right opportunity and luck, he could enter the Transcendent Realm at any time. If he were a righteous hero, he would be a blessing to the orthodox murim, but unfortunately, he is a demonic practitioner." The Black Dragon Knight poured liquor into Jaegal Yuhwan's cup. "The Iron Armored Divine Spear was a righteous hero. The Phantom Spear killed him, and he also killed the Sword Phantom and the Blood Heavenly Demon. The unfortunate death of the Iron Armored Divine Spear is not enough..."

That was why Jaegal Taeryeong had to die. With the deaths of Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin, the Jaegal and Moyong Clans would be enraged.

"I've already spread the word to the Beggars' Gang and other information guilds that Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin were killed by the Phantom Spear," the Black Dragon Knight said in a cheerful voice. "With this, the Phantom Spear will become a monster in human skin and receive the malice of all."

However, the South was a place where the murim's influence was not strong. Savages and Yokai lived in the jungle, and the distinction between orthodox and unorthodox sects held little meaning there. The Black Dragon Knight was well aware of this. In truth, he did not particularly like this kind of scheming. If he had his way, he would have acted himself and beaten the Phantom Spear to death.

However, he could not do that. He had not yet been told to do so.

*How strange,* the Black Dragon Knight thought as he lifted his cup. *Is there any reason to leave him be? He is a being who will undoubtedly become an eyesore someday, even if he isn't one right now.*

Feeling a slight sense of wonder, he brought the cup to his lips. He only felt that way, but he had no intention of acting on his own. Even for him, the words of the Spirit Medium were to be respected.

After leaving the forest, they parted ways with the Evil Spirits completely. The spirits would continue to exist there, luring in humans who entered without knowing any better. Rubia glanced back at them, unable to leave the forest's domain.

"What's going to happen to them now?" she asked.

"They'll continue to exist in that forest," Aladur answered. "They're Evil Spirits and Bound Spirits. As long as the forest itself doesn't disappear, they have no choice but to live in it forever. It's an unlucky death. It might be different if a monk with high spiritual power came and purified them, but... Keke! Even if the famous Grandmaster Bulyeong of Shaolin came himself, he probably couldn't purify the Demonic Energy of this forest. Don't you worry your head about it, young beastfolk lady. This is their destiny."

Despite Aladur's words, Rubia could not easily let it go and kept glancing back.

Watching her, the Crazy Heavenly Demon asked, "Did you grow fond of them in that short time?"

"The impossible love between a beastfolk and an Evil Spirit, how tragic," Aladur chimed in, making a fuss.

Rubia scowled and glared at him. "Stop talking nonsense. With your face all black like that..."

"Don't make racist remarks," Aladur replied with a straight face.

"Racist... what?"

"If you don't know, then forget it!"

Ignoring their bickering, Lee Sungmin listened to Nebul's voice in his head. A few days ago, he had asked the genie to look for information about a mage who could handle a Dragon Heart. Only today had he finally heard back.

[Many mages are interested in the Dragon Heart, but unfortunately... yes. Just like the scales and bones, almost no mage has seen a Dragon Heart in person. So while many step forward saying they'll try, they're a bit lacking in terms of reliability. Even though they've all reached the rank of archmage.]

*What is your opinion, Nebul?* Lee Sungmin asked internally. He did not know much about magic. He had learned a few buff spells, but that did not make him a mage. If Scarlet were by his side, he would have asked for her advice, but it had already been several years since he had last seen her.

[...The Dragon Heart is extremely sensitive. If you handle it ambiguously without certainty, there's a high risk of it being destroyed. Therefore, I think it's better for you to just hold onto it for now.]

"......"

He could not help but feel a little dejected. The Dragon Heart, the scales, the bones. He had them all, but he could not process or use them right away.

[Ah, and one more thing. The Blue Magic Tower Master asked me to tell you that he desperately wants to purchase the Dragon Heart. The price is...]

*I have no intention of selling it.*

[Of course, I assumed as much. Besides the Blue Magic Tower Master, others have also shown interest in buying it, but I will handle them for you. Ah... and.] Nebul's voice sank slightly. [A story about you, Lee Sungmin, is circulating among the Beggars' Gang and other information guilds.]

*What kind of story?*

[That Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin, who were chasing the Phantom Spear, were brutally murdered by the Phantom Spear.]

Hearing that, Lee Sungmin's expression could not help but harden. Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin were dead? He recalled a few days ago. Not even a week had passed since he parted ways with the martial artists from the Noble Clans who had been pursuing him.

*I didn't kill them.*

[But that's the information that's circulating,] Nebul confirmed.

Although Jaegal Taeryeong had not entered the village, Lee Sungmin had not killed him. The last time he had seen the man, he was moving away with Moyong Seojin and the Black Hound Squad, saying they would leave the forest.

[The source of the information is the Murim Alliance. They say they received such a report from the Black Hound Squad. Also, the Sword Dragon Namgung Heewon and the Azure Sky Radiant Sword Corps have successfully escaped the forest. It seems Poiso, butterfly Tang Ahee, the Dark Night Black Mist Squad, and the Black Hound Squad are still wandering the forest.]

*I... understand for now.*

Lee Sungmin felt a slight sense of relief that Namgung Heewon had managed to get out safely. Namgung Heewon no longer called him 'little brother', and he no longer called Namgung Heewon 'Hyung-nim', but he still held a certain degree of fondness for the man. He could not help but be relieved.

*Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin are dead.*

He could not just indulge in that feeling. Lee Sungmin's face grew cold. There was nothing to think about. If the two of them were dead and the Black Hound Squad was still alive, then the Black Hound Squad had killed them, and he had been framed for their deaths.

*Why go this far? Jaegal Taeryeong and Moyong Seojin are members of the Jaegal Clan and the Moyong Clan...* Jaegal Taeryeong, in particular, was the heir of the Jaegal Clan. Lee Sungmin could not understand the reason for framing him, going so far as to kill those two.

*Be wary of the Murim Alliance Chairman.*

The warning from the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman echoed in his mind.

*Black Dragon Knight...*

Lee Sungmin bit his lower lip, thinking of the man he had never even met. Even if he protested his innocence, his words would not be accepted as the truth.

"Why the long face?" Rubia asked, tilting her head.

Lee Sungmin rubbed his stiff cheeks and told everyone what he had heard from Nebul. When the story was over, Aladur cackled and nodded.

"That bastard Jaegal, so that son of a bitch finally kicked the bucket. Ahh, how refreshing!"

"The Murim Alliance uses some dirty tricks," the Crazy Heavenly Demon grumbled. "I can't even understand why they'd go that far."

Rubia seemed to have little interest in such stories. She had been lost in thought for a moment with her ears perked, but then she opened her mouth.

"About the Dragon Heart," she began, tilting her head as if she was unsure. "Even if other archmages can't handle it, don't you think my master could?"

"......"

Lee Sungmin's eyes widened at her words.

"My master is a mage greater than an archmage," Rubia continued. "I don't know for sure if he can do it, but if it's something other mages can't do, my master might be able to."

Her words sounded quite plausible. Envirus was the master of Lloyd, the Golden Magic Tower Master. Even if it was something an archmage on that level could not do, Envirus might be able to.

"You said we could meet him in the South, so we'll probably be able to meet him soon," Rubia said, nodding her head. "I'm sure of it."

Her words sounded like a desperate prayer.
