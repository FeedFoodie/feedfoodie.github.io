Dirty. Dangerous. The words echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind, stirring an indescribable emotion as he listened to Daoist Seongha. Those words were aimed at the Sword Phantom, Dok Bijun, but the man Sungmin knew simply did not fit such a description.

"I'm not sure what you mean by that."

"It seems you have a connection with the Sword Phantom," Daoist Seongha observed with a chuckle, "but it doesn't appear to be a deep one."

He was right. Lee Sungmin's connection with Dok Bijun was limited to a few encounters on Mount Mushii. While Baek Sogo and the other practitioners had built a rapport with the man, Lee Sungmin had not.

"You don't seem to know much about the Sword Phantom."

Lee Sungmin could not offer any response. He did not know. That was the truth.

"For the past year, the Sword Phantom has been traveling around, dueling anyone he comes across. He has challenged the masters of minor sects outside the Nine Sects One Gang, lone wandering barbarians, and even mercenaries of exceptional skill, and he has won every single time."

"...I know that much."

He had purchased information about Dok Bijun through Erebrisa, but that knowledge was strictly limited to the Sword Phantom's location.

"It's not uncommon for powerful masters to suddenly appear in this world. After all, Eria summons people from various dimensions for reasons we don't understand. That doesn't mean the people who already live here are indifferent to new powerhouses. On the contrary, they are more sensitive and pay very close attention."

"...What are you trying to say?"

"When I learned that Dok Bijun was heading to Mount Hua, I had the Beggars' Gang look into him. Huhu! That I won't duel an unknown barbarian for the prestige of the sect? That's just an excuse for the public. The Sword Phantom is a master who has reached the realm of Chojeol. As someone who also wields a sword, there's no way I wouldn't enjoy a duel with a strong opponent. Nevertheless, I have a very good reason for refusing him."

Dirty. Dangerous. The words would not leave Lee Sungmin's mind. As he listened in silence, Daoist Seongha finally revealed his reason.

"He uses evil arts."

The statement hung in the air.

"Ah. Of course, I have no intention of blaming him for it. I don't deny those who use magic and evil arts. I was summoned to this world forty years ago... Huhu! I understood decades ago that this place isn't the Central Plains, but even so, this is something I just can't accept. I don't want to duel someone who uses such methods. I can't accept them as fair and honorable."

The conversation ended there. His original errand, delivering the letter from Great Monk Bulyeong, was complete. After leaving Daoist Seongha's room, Lee Sungmin headed for the main gate. It was not that he had no desire to see more of Mount Hua, but as an outsider, he risked causing problems if he wandered the sect's grounds freely.

As he walked, Daoist Seongha's words replayed in his thoughts. *The Sword Phantom uses evil arts. His methods are dirty and dangerous, so I don't want to duel him. I can't accept them as fair and honorable.*

It was not difficult to understand the man's perspective. Even after forty years in this world, Daoist Seongha must have spent a significant part of his youth in the Central Plains. There, as a disciple of the Mount Hua Sect, he would have learned orthodox martial arts. It made sense that even after so long in Eria, he could not fully accept evil arts and magic. Lee Sungmin knew well that a person could not easily change the world, nature, and values they had lived by.

"Are you finished with your business?"

The Sword Phantom's voice cut through his thoughts as he stepped out of the main gate. Dok Bijun was still standing with his arms crossed, glaring at the entrance to Mount Hua. Lee Sungmin felt a little awkward under the man's wide-eyed stare, but he managed a nod.

He remained silent.

"I see."

"...I met Daoist Seongha," Lee Sungmin finally said with a sigh.

At his words, the Sword Phantom's eyes widened. He seemed surprised that Lee Sungmin had met the Sect Leader of Mount Hua.

"...Daoist Seongha... Keukeu! To think he wouldn't even come out when I asked to meet him."

"I heard... the reason."

It was difficult to say. The Sword Phantom practiced evil arts? Even though he had heard it directly from Daoist Seongha, Lee Sungmin found it hard to believe. The man he saw was a swordsman who knew nothing but the sword, and the memories of his past life painted the same picture.

*Is it really true?*

He realized he did not truly know what kind of person the Sword Phantom was. His memories were nothing more than overheard rumors. He had never looked deeply into the martial artist known as the Sword Phantom. In fact, the first time he had even learned of the man was when he heard that masters like him and Silent Flash had been killed by the Little Heavenly Demon, Wiji Hoyeon.

"...He said he didn't want to fight because you use evil arts."

Dok Bijun's expression stiffened. He studied Lee Sungmin's face for a moment before looking up at the sky. With his hands clasped behind his back, he gazed at the vast expanse above and finally spoke.

"Is that so," he murmured, not denying it.

Did that mean it was true? Unable to quite grasp it, Lee Sungmin mustered the courage to ask.

"What kind of evil arts did you learn?"

Dok Bijun did not answer. He just stared at the sky, his eyes swirling with an unreadable emotion.

"Didn't you practice the sword in the mountains? So what kind of evil arts..."

"Have you ever faced a limit?" Dok Bijun interrupted. "Have you ever felt the existence of a wall that you can't overcome, no matter how hard you try? Have you ever realized that you can never break, climb over, or go around that wall?"

"I ha..."

He was about to answer, but Dok Bijun cut him off firmly.

"You're too young to speak of walls."

Lee Sungmin's mouth snapped shut.

"Have you ever felt yourself growing old? Felt your body grow heavier with each passing day? My mind remembers its prime, but do you know what it feels like when your aging body can no longer keep up?"

He could not answer. Dok Bijun's questions were of a nature that Lee Sungmin could never comprehend.

"I'm already over sixty years old. My body has lost the prowess of its prime, and the more I swing my sword, the weaker it becomes. Even the ascetic training on Mount Mushii couldn't help me overcome this wall. That feeling of despair. Do you know it?"

"...I don't."

"Evil arts, you say. Yes. It is evil arts. Do you know what I was feeling when I came to rely on such a thing? You can't climb over, break through, or go around the wall you've hit. My body grows older with each passing day. There was a limit to what I could sustain with inner qi. However. However..."

The Sword Phantom closed his eyes.

"The desire to do more. Do you know that kind of desperation?"

With that, Dok Bijun said no more. Lee Sungmin watched the silent man for a long time before finally turning to descend Mount Hua. He could not say he did not understand. On the contrary, he understood all too well.

Lee Sungmin had been the same. He had given up on his training because he could not get past the wall he had hit. That was why he had tried to rely on the Magic Armor and clung to familiar, easy techniques rather than advanced martial arts. That was why he could understand.

However, Lee Sungmin was young. He was still twenty-two, his body in its prime. Unlike him, Dok Bijun was feeling the relentless effects of old age. At some point, even profound inner qi would no longer sustain his aging body. Unless he achieved Rejuvenation, his martial arts would cease to improve. No matter how important the training of the mind was, the body was just as crucial.

There were many outstanding masters in the world, but how many had achieved Rejuvenation? Even Great Monk Bulyeong and Daoist Seongha, masters of the Chojeol realm, had not. Their bodies were past their prime and slowly deteriorating.

The Sword Phantom was no different. He simply refused to give up. He was trying to overcome his wall by forcing himself to wield his sword with tenacious obsession, mobilizing means other than pure martial arts.

He could not be blamed.

Because Lee Sungmin had been the same.

The only difference was the time they had. With a heavy heart, Lee Sungmin descended Mount Hua. He could see the righteousness in both Daoist Seongha's and the Sword Phantom's feelings, and he could not judge which of them was right. In the end, it was simply a difference in values.

{sep}

Once he was back in the city, Lee Sungmin focused and recalled the memory. The image of the God of Time, Denir, that Mushii had engraved in him, came to mind with perfect clarity.

Various gods existed in Eria. Most resided in temples or holy lands where they had made their Advent, their existence no great secret. Mushii herself, for instance, existed in her holy land of Mount Mushii, a place famous enough for even Lee Sungmin to know about.

Denir, however, was different. Even searching through the memories of his past life, he had never heard the name 'God of Time'. An unknown, secretive god. The prospect of meeting such a being filled Lee Sungmin with a mix of excitement and tension.

By the time he arrived at the location from his memory, the sun was already setting despite his use of movement arts. He stopped and stared at what stood before him. It was a small country house in the suburbs, away from the city center. This was the place.

*Is Denir really here?*

Lee Sungmin tilted his head in confusion but approached the door. The memory was certain, so he did not doubt it. He hesitated for a moment before stepping forward.

He reached for the doorknob.

"......"

The moment his fingers were about to close around the metal, it simply was not there. His eyes widened. He found himself standing several steps back from the door, his hand grasping at empty air.

Slightly bewildered, Lee Sungmin approached the doorknob again.

It happened again. As he tried to grab it, the knob vanished from his reach, and he was once more standing several steps away. He moved forward a third time, and the same thing repeated.

"What... what is this?" he muttered, looking around in confusion. He could not get close to the closed door.

[Go back.]

A voice echoed in his ears, its gender impossible to determine.

"Who... who are you?"

[Go back.]

The owner of the voice did not answer his question. Lee Sungmin was certain it was Denir. Only a god could perform such an inexplicable feat.

He recalled what Mushii had said. *If you ever get the chance, go to Dreamoor. Tell the God of Time Denir there that Mushii sent you.*

Lee Sungmin immediately opened his mouth.

"Mushii sent me."

[...]

At those words, the voice fell silent. A moment later, a new thought echoed in Lee Sungmin's mind, laced with an almost human frustration.

[Damn it.]

The door swung open.
