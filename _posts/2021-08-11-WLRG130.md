From the heart of the swirling mist, Baek Museon glared at Lee Sungmin. The young man stood with his spear held forward, a statue of perfect stillness that offered no opening to exploit.

*My body has stiffened.*

Baek Museon felt the truth of it keenly. Three years had passed since Wiji Hoyeon had severed his arm. In the beginning, he had vowed revenge and thrown himself into his martial arts, but it was not long before he gave up. He had been forced to face the reality that it was all useless.

After that, he had drowned himself in alcohol, tried drugs, and held women until he was sick of them. A part of him had always known this day would come, a day when he would finally gather his resolve to practice again only to resent his own body, which had grown dull and stiff from long laziness and Despair.

*No. Not today.*

Baek Museon muttered the words to himself, clenching his right fist. Lee Sungmin, was it? The young man's achievements were dazzling for his age, he had to admit. Ten years ago, when Baek Museon had been Lee Sungmin's age, he had not accomplished nearly as much.

It was not, however, astonishing. It was not absurd. Though he had grown dull and slow, Baek Museon was the Blood Heavenly Demon. Even as a one-armed man, he was still the Blood Heavenly Demon, Baek Museon.

The swirling mist expanded even further. He had underestimated Lee Sungmin. A complete Origin Reversal could hide one's achievements, but that was not the only reason Baek Museon had looked down on him.

It was his pride.

Before meeting Wiji Hoyeon, Baek Museon's fighting spirit had been fueled by the belief that he was the best. He thought no expert of his generation was superior and had never doubted that he would enter the Transcendent Realm before long. His defeat at her hands, however, had driven him to Despair, and that towering pride had pushed him into a deep mire. Wiji Hoyeon was an exceptional being, a calamity of absurd proportions.

*You are not Wiji Hoyeon.*

Such a hasty judgment.

The attack came without a sound, a rebuke for his rashness delivered in a sharp, impossibly swift strike. Even in that moment, Baek Museon still trusted his Blood Mist Eerie Night Art, still convinced of Lee Sungmin's shallowness.

KUUUUUUUNG!

The Deathly Flash of Extreme Speed struck faster than sound, a blur the eye could barely follow as it shook the defenses of the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art with a single, powerful blow. *The thrust is shallow. I thought it would go a little deeper.*

*It's hard to break through in one strike.*

Deathly Flash pursued only Extreme Speed, which meant it was bound to be lacking when it came to piercing thick defenses. Without hesitation, Lee Sungmin withdrew his spear and shifted his feet. The Third Form of the Dancing Shadow Soul Break, Two Step Calamity, sought to kill an opponent in just two steps. He had not learned an art that dealt solely with enhanced qi, but the Dancing Shadow Soul Break was a movement art that naturally induced it with its steps alone.

A fierce eruption of enhanced qi burst forth from the Two Step Calamity. Baek Museon flinched at its sweeping ferocity as it collided with his Blood Mist Eerie Night Art. He gritted his teeth, watching the mist scatter. He hurriedly swung his right hand to block Lee Sungmin's path, but before his hand could move, the spear shot toward his chest.

"You bastard!" Baek Museon roared.

He pulled his swinging right hand back in front of his chest and rotated his open palm in a half-circle. The scattering mist began to spin with the motion, catching Lee Sungmin's spear in its current. The rotation tugged at the hand holding the spear, but Lee Sungmin did not resist. He simply changed the spear's path, adding his own rotation to the vortex as his feet took another step forward. In contrast, Baek Museon retreated a couple of steps.

KWAANG!

The tangled enhanced qi exploded in all directions.

That brief exchange forced Baek Museon to drastically revise his assessment of Lee Sungmin. He had not gained even the slightest advantage in their clash of internal energy, a fact that he could not help but find astonishing.

*This is ridiculous...!*

Gritting his teeth, he drew out even more of his internal energy, unleashing the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art to its fullest. The red mist spewing in all directions deepened in color until it no longer looked like mist but like red paint poured into the air. Baek Museon stepped forward boldly, the sleeve of his left arm flapping as he leaped at Lee Sungmin. Each time his stomping feet hit the ground, the surroundings shook as if in an earthquake and the mist roiled. His open palm strike shot toward Lee Sungmin's body.

It lacked the rough heaviness of the Crazy Heavenly Demon's techniques, but the strangeness of the accompanying mist was unsettling. Lee Sungmin twirled the spear in his hand and extended it in the single strike of the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Soul Chasing Elimination. It was not as fast as Deathly Flash, but it was a versatile technique that could be executed instantly without any preparatory movements.

KWAANG!

The spear and palm collided. Though both were coated in enhanced qi, the resulting sound was unimaginably loud for a clash between flesh and steel. Baek Museon reflexively flinched his left shoulder. His mind intended to follow with a second palm strike, but his body had no left hand to obey. In a martial contest like this, the absence of his arm still felt awkward.

*I've grown dull...!*

Such belated regret. Newly aware of his missing limb, Baek Museon lifted his foot. He was skilled in unarmed combat that used his entire body, not just his hands. Lee Sungmin lowered his stance to avoid the incoming heel kick and at the same time dropped the spear low, aiming for Baek Museon's lower body. A spear was not just for thrusting. If there was not enough distance to thrust and retrieve, one could simply swing it to strike.

Though he berated himself for growing dull, Baek Museon was no easy opponent. The man once known as the Blood Heavenly Demon was still an outstanding master. From the same stance he had used to swing his leg, he kicked off his standing left foot and leaped into the air. With nothing to stand on, he twisted his waist mid-air, completely changing his posture before bringing his raised right foot down toward the crown of Lee Sungmin's head.

Lee Sungmin used Traceless Step to evade. Baek Museon's foot crushed the afterimage from above, shaking the entire ground and causing it to crack. Baek Museon spun around and chased him, striking with his palm. A long stream of red mist shot out, trying to envelop Lee Sungmin, who immediately unleashed Homing Chain Lightning to push the palm technique back.

Baek Museon did not linger on his extended strike and tried to close the distance, intending to prevent Lee Sungmin from properly utilizing the spear's advantage. Lee Sungmin held his breath, watching the charge. Internal energy flowed into both arms holding the spear, and that was only the beginning. Countless variations imbued his thrusting spear until it filled Baek Museon's entire vision. In response, Baek Museon pushed his Blood Mist Eerie Night Art to its absolute limit.

KWAANG!

The brutish physical charge and Lee Sungmin's spear strike collided. The mist, which served as both a Protective Qi Aura and an offensive technique, scattered. A normal aura would simply vanish if dispersed like that, but Baek Museon's Blood Mist Eerie Night Art was different.

The scattered mist reconnected to him from a distance.

A portion of it circled around to Lee Sungmin's back. Before he knew it, he was standing in the center of the mist, just as Baek Museon had aimed. Baek Museon opened his right hand wide, then clenched it into a fist. The mist coiling around Lee Sungmin surged toward him without a single gap.

"Blood Mist Explosion!" Baek Museon shouted.

It was a silent premonition of death. There were no openings. *Should I try to break through with Void Path? No, rather than that.* Lee Sungmin lifted his foot. It was the first time he was performing this with his current body, but he felt a baseless confidence that he could use it now. No, there was a basis for it. It just had not been proven.

The belief that he could do it.

Dancing Shadow Soul Break Fourth Form, Two Step Gentle Ripple.

Two consecutive steps distorted the scenery. The encroaching mist was caught in the strange swirl of power created by the steps of the Two Step Gentle Ripple. A fierce current formed around Lee Sungmin, drawing a circle that entangled the red mist, completely deviating it from the form of attack Baek Museon had intended.

PAAANG!

The mist scattered. Unlike before, it did not reconnect to Baek Museon but dispersed and vanished completely. It had been nullified. Baek Museon had not expected Lee Sungmin to neutralize the Blood Mist Explosion, and he could only stand there with his mouth agape in bewilderment.

He came to his senses a moment too late, just as Lee Sungmin's spear shot fiercely toward him. He quickly thrust his right hand forward, his clenched fist forming a single punch. Rather than clashing head-on, Lee Sungmin twisted his wrist slightly. The spear's trajectory shifted upward, sliding past Baek Museon's fist as Lee Sungmin extended his foot forward. The spearhead pointed toward the sky, but the shaft became a blunt weapon as Lee Sungmin pushed it forward with his hand.

KWAANG!

The mist of the Blood Mist Eerie Night Art surrounding Baek Museon's body dispersed. He felt a shock as if his internal organs were being shaken and took several steps back. *It's fine. I can withstand this level of impact., but before he could draw the scattered mist back, Lee Sungmin's spear moved again. He focused on swinging rather than thrusting, using the entire shaft as a weapon.

"Keuheup!"

Baek Museon could not respond to the attack striking his left shoulder. If he could use his left arm, he might have been able to do something, even with some trash skills, but he had no left arm. In its place, he twisted his waist to respond with his right hand, but Lee Sungmin pulled his spear back as if he had been waiting for it.

*He's inexperienced.*

Baek Museon's Blood Mist Eerie Night Art was an excellent martial art, but he was inexperienced in fighting as a one-armed man. He did not know how to cover the opening on his left side. It was as if he had not fought properly since his arm was cut off, as the gap was exposed every time he fought.

Once Lee Sungmin began to focus his attacks on that opening, Baek Museon could not keep up. Before he could properly unleash his Blood Mist Eerie Night Art, Lee Sungmin pressed his attack, not using the spear as a spear, causing Baek Museon to flail his right arm and stagger.

"This bastard...!"

There were no fatal attacks, but the shallow, cutting strikes infuriated Baek Museon. Unlike his passionately agitated opponent, Lee Sungmin was calm. He pushed his spear between Baek Museon's widely spread legs. Baek Museon flinched in surprise and tried to pull his leg back, but Lee Sungmin twisted the spear he had pushed through and pulled it toward the back of Baek Museon's knee. As a result, Baek Museon lost his balance and collapsed to the ground.

"I could have pierced your leg," Lee Sungmin said, looking down at him.

Baek Museon's lips trembled as he looked up.

"I could have pierced your left shoulder, your chest, your side... even your head. I could have dealt you a fatal wound multiple times, and I could have killed you."

"You... you..."

"Compared to the Little Heavenly Demon, how am I?"

At Lee Sungmin's question, Baek Museon fell silent. He bit his lower lip until it bled, glaring up at the young man.

"...You think a worm... can be compared to a dragon?"

"Then you're less than a worm," Lee Sungmin replied with a nod.

Baek Museon's body trembled, his gaze shooting toward Lee Sungmin, filled with killing intent. Lee Sungmin ignored the stare and turned away.

The Crazy Heavenly Demon watched them with a complicated expression. He had expected Lee Sungmin to win, but he had not thought victory would come so easily, so one-sidedly.

*Because the Martial God's left arm was cut off... that's part of it, but. His technique has improved even more than when we fought three days ago. I especially have no idea what that technique was that nullified the Blood Mist Explosion. It seemed like a movement art... but I'm not sure. To think I can't be certain even though I saw it with my own eyes.*

"I'll be inside," Lee Sungmin said, having no desire to talk further with Baek Museon.

A dragon and a worm. Baek Museon's spiteful words lingered in his ears, but he did not pay them much mind.

*I'm glad I can at least be a worm.*

That was all he thought. As Rubia hesitated before returning to the restaurant with him, the Crazy Heavenly Demon sighed and approached Baek Museon.

"Don't be too disappointed. It's just that the world is a wide place."

"...Haha..." Baek Museon let out a hollow laugh as he staggered to his feet. "What does it matter if the world is wide? I've only just realized how insignificant and small my existence is."

He spat out the words and turned away. The Crazy Heavenly Demon wanted to stop him but knew there was nothing he could do for him here, so he lowered the hand he had raised.

Overcoming defeat was something one had to do for oneself. He had been broken for three years. If he now understood why he lost, he would try to overcome it. He had to.

*He can do it.*

The Baek Museon he remembered was that kind of martial artist. Baek Museon staggered away, glaring at his surroundings. The people who had watched the fight flinched at his fierce expression and averted their gazes.

Killing intent boiled within him. He felt as if everyone was laughing at him. Gritting his teeth, he quickened his pace. It was the last shred of self-control he held onto, along with his sense of defeat.

*Quickly.*

He wanted to leave this place.

Otherwise, he felt like he might kill everyone here.
