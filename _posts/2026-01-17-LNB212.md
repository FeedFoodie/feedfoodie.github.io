A silvery-white orb enveloped Eun Han-Seol as she attacked and Dam Soo-Cheon countered with a pure white light.

BOOM!

With every collision, thunder cracked and the earth shook violently. Unable to withstand the shockwaves, the surrounding warriors scattered for safety. From a distance, they stared at the fight, mouths agape in disbelief.

"Is this really a fight between humans?" one asked.

"The Witch of the White Night is terrifying," another said, his body trembling with fear. "The legends of the Four Great Demon Generals were true. If a young girl is already this powerful, I can't imagine how powerful the others are."

None of them had ever faced the Silent Night firsthand, and some had secretly looked down on the organization. Today, however, they learned the bitter truth of their mistake. One young girl had killed an untold number of them, yet no one had managed to leave so much as a scratch on her. Her power was staggering.

WHOOSH!

The Silver Soul Qi flowing from Eun Han-Seol's body destroyed everything in its path, lashing out like a living creature at anything that showed her hostility. While the energy claimed countless lives, though, it seemed to have little effect on Dam Soo-Cheon. 

A faint aura of light, the Holy Light Qi, shielded him. This energy, the antithesis of all demonic arts, protected him completely from her Silver Soul Qi.

Nevertheless, the two forces were equal. Sparks flew each time they met, but neither could overwhelm the other. Both combatants quickly realized they could not win with energy alone. 

When power was equal, technique decided the victor.

Eun Han-Seol's hands blurred as she unleashed the Scattered Shadow Annihilation, creating dozens of afterimages in the air.

Dam Soo-Cheon's response was even more dazzling. He spun like a top, unleashing a flurry of kicks in an instant with the Flower Slashing Foot Blade technique, which was named for how it cut down flowers using feet like blades. His kicks also created dozens of afterimages, each striking down one of Eun Han-Seol's hand shadows.

CRACK CRACK CRACK!

Their hands and feet tangled dozens of times, and the air echoed with sharp cracks reminiscent of splitting bamboo.

The watching warriors flinched with each impact. Although the exchange looked deceptively mild, the danger within was beyond imagination. The very air around them shimmered with the force of their blows.

Both young martial artists' techniques were a perfect union of offense and defense. They attacked while defending and defended while attacking. Their bodies were perfectly shielded, even during the fiercest assaults. This fight was destined to be a long one. 

As time passed, Eun Han-Seol grew anxious, while Dam Soo-Cheon became more composed.

She was in enemy territory. Countless hungry eyes watched her, and their numbers would only grow with time. Dam Soo-Cheon, on the other hand, was fighting comfortably in his own backyard. Time was his ally, not hers.

Still, Dam Soo-Cheon marveled, *Lady Eun's martial arts are truly incredible.*

His entire body tingled. Every blow they exchanged sent a shock through him. One moment of carelessness could mean instant death. Her strength was exceptional, worthy of a Four Great Demon General. 

He hadn't felt this kind of tension in a long time. Blood rushed through his veins several times faster than normal. His heart pounded violently, yet his mind was calmer than ever, taking in the situation with frigid clarity.

Suddenly, he noticed her breathing had grown ragged. The continuous fighting was draining her stamina. To an ordinary person, it was a minor difference, but to a master of his level, it was a glaring opportunity.

*I will shatter the legend of the Four Great Demon Generals today and use you as a stepping stone to rise even higher.*

He condensed a tremendous amount of power into his fist with the Triple Light Wheel Strike, one of the most powerful techniques of the Divine Flash, and sent it toward Eun Han-Seol.

WHOOSH!

Three consecutive punches flew at Eun Han-Seol. Recognizing the immense power behind the attack, she countered with the Silver Soul Fairy Claws. A silvery-white enhanced qi formed around her hand.

BOOM!

The world seemed to shake as the Triple Light Wheel Strike and the Silver Soul Fairy Claws collided.

One after another, they unleashed their ultimate techniques. Rarely had they met such worthy opponents, who matched them in martial prowess and shared their obsession for victory. The balanced fight pushed their potential to its absolute limit. They grew stronger as they fought, both admiring the other's skill. 

However, victory was another matter entirely. They could never coexist.

"Ugh!" A trickle of blood seeped from the corner of Eun Han-Seol's mouth. She was injured internally by the fierce exchange, which rocked her core and disrupted her inner qi.

*Should I use the Moonlight Chakras?*

The demonic weapon hidden in her robes cried out to her, but she suppressed the urge to use it. She did not want to win against Dam Soo-Cheon by using a superior weapon.

During her moment of hesitation, Dam Soo-Cheon launched a ferocious attack. He unleashed a torrent of the Divine Flash's ultimate techniques, such as the Heavenly Light Ray and the Heaven Stunning Martial Soul, driving her into a corner.

ROAR!

A storm of enhanced qi raged around them. Eun Han-Seol's clothes tore apart, and cuts began to appear on her fine skin. Still, she remained calm, defending herself solidly while searching for an opening. 

She had not given up. She could not give up.

WHOOSH!

A sharp sound cut through the air. Eun Han-Seol was focused entirely on Dam Soo-Cheon and never heard it. By the time the sound registered, she felt a stinging sensation near her chest and instinctively twisted her body to evade it.

THUNK!

Her waist bent backward like a cooked shrimp. She had avoided a blow to the chest, a red dagger was now embedded in her side.

"Heok!" she grunted, her face contorted in pain.

Dam Soo-Cheon stopped attacking and looked at where the dagger had flown in from. A group of men in Taoist robes stood there, led by a master who appeared to be in his late sixties. He recognized the man instantly.

“Taoist Woon-Sang?”

Taoist Woon-Sang, the man who had thrown the red dagger, was an elder of the Wudang Sect. He was a supreme martial artist, junior to the Scarlet Leaf Sage and senior to the current Wudang Sect Leader, Taoist Woon-Hwa.

"What is the meaning of this, Taoist Woon-Sang?" Dam Soo-Cheon demanded. "How dare you interfere in a fair duel!"

"We're dealing with a public enemy of the jianghu," Taoist Woon-Sang replied calmly. "There's no need to consider fairness."

As he spoke, the Wudang Sect's Taoists surrounded Eun Han-Seol.

"Taoist Woon-Sang," Dam Soo-Cheon growled, but Taoist Woon-Sang interrupted him before he could finish speaking.

"You've done enough. You showed you could gain the upper hand against the witch. Isn't that sufficient? Many in the jianghu will praise your accomplishment."

"This is not what I want, Taoist Woon-Sang!"

"Many warriors have died by her hand. Moreover, she's one of the Silent Night's Four Great Demon Generals. You know how many have died because of them over the generations. She must be dealt with swiftly and decisively. So please, step aside."

A glint of greed flickered in Taoist Woon-Sang's eyes. Defeating the Witch of the White Night, one of the Four Great Demon Generals, would bring him immense fame.

Despite being a Taoist who was supposed to be detached from worldly affairs, he hadn't abandoned his ambition. For this reason, he had rushed to the scene with his disciples as soon as Heaven's Summit sent out a request for aid.

"The witch's evil ends here, now that she's been struck by the Blue Soul Dagger, one of the Wudang Sect's greatest treasures."

"The Blue Soul Dagger?"

Taoist Woon-Sang smiled and stepped forward, his gait relaxed. "It's a sword imbued with holy energy, crafted by our founding ancestor. For those who practice demonic arts, it's their ultimate nemesis."

Dam Soo-Cheon glared at him with quiet fury, enraged that he had interfered in their duel.

Taoist Woon-Sang knew this, but he didn't care. *The world isn't so easy, you inexperienced, ignorant fledgling.*

He coolly regarded the witch's current state.

Eun Han-Seol was gasping in pain and clutching her side. As he had predicted, the pure qi from the Blue Soul Dagger was attacking her from within. Her blood and qi boiled, and her Silver Soul Qi raged uncontrollably as she desperately tried to calm the turmoil inside her.

RING!

At that moment, the Moonlight Chakras in her robes let out a cry. The weapon's demonic qi, which she had been suppressing, began to run rampant. Within moments, it invaded her body and consumed her.

The world turned red, and something inside her mind snapped.

Dam Soo-Cheon was the first to notice the change. Sensing her ominous aura, he frowned.

Taoist Woon-Sang stepped forward. "Seize the witch!" he shouted to his disciples. "We'll drag her to Heaven's Summit and punish her before the whole world!"

"Waaah!"

The Wudang Sect's Taoists charged at Eun Han-Seol, their swords glinting dangerously. To them, the injured and exhausted girl looked like easy prey. If they captured her and took her to Heaven's Summit, Wudang's fame would soar.

WHOOSH!

Sword qi rippled through the air as they swung their blades, aiming dozens of streams at her vital points. 

Meanwhile, Eun Han-Seol remained bent over, seemingly unable to move.

Exultant smiles bloomed on the faces of the Taoists, when all of a sudden, Eun Han-Seol stood up straight and lifted her head. Her silvery-white hair was stained as red as blood. A distinct crimson dot appeared in the center of her silvery-white eyes, containing nothing but rage against the world and a madness that sought to destroy everything.

"Everyone, get back!" Dam Soo-Cheon yelled.

The Wudang Taoists did not hear him. A low buzzing sound filled their ears, like a bee hovering beside them.

"What the...?"

A first-generation disciple at the front tilted his head in confusion. The world, which had been upright a moment ago, was now tilting. A searing pain shot up from below his waist.

THUD!

His torso hit the ground with a dull thud. His eyes fell upon his lower body, which was still standing upright.

THUD THUD THUD!

In the blink of an eye, a dozen Taoists lay dead, their bodies severed at the waist. 

The shocking sight brought the charging Wudang disciples to a halt. A pair of chakrams flew through the air in a wide arc, catching their attention.

BUZZ!

The legendary demonic weapon, the Moonlight Chakras, finally revealed itself to the world.

Eun Han-Seol's red eyes, now consumed by demonic qi, fell upon the Wudang Taoists. As if they had wings, the Moonlight Chakras followed her line of sight.

"S-STOP!" Taoist Woon-Sang shrieked in terror.

Alas, his cry was in vain. Before his voice even reached Eun Han-Seol's ears, the Moonlight Chakras swept through the ranks of the Wudang Taoists, leaving only death in their wake.

Dam Soo-Cheon bit his lip hard. Taoist Woon-Sang and his disciples had pushed Eun Han-Seol too far. She was no longer herself, but rather a witch who had lost all reason.

"Ahahahaha!" Eun Han-Seol cackled, even as the terrifying demonic qi flowing from her body began to consume the area.
