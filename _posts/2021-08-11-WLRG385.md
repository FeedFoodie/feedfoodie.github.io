Jeniela strolled unhurriedly through the forest, fully aware of the gazes that followed her. From the very beginning, she had felt the Observation Eye and various familiars watching from every shadow. They tried their best to hide, but their efforts were laughably blatant to her. Though she had pinpointed every one of their locations, she simply ignored them, content to let them watch all they wanted.

The forest itself was shaking, a tremor she felt in her bones without needing to see it. Her body, now exquisitely sensitive to magic, read the seething energies like a story. The chaotic currents told her everything that was happening within these woods. Just as she expected, the Lich, Yokai, and Death Knights were dying without putting up a proper fight. It was hardly a surprise. With Vollander, the Red Ghost, Arbes, and Kim Jonghyun all absent, the force she had brought was large in number but lacked any real substance.

Gemini followed a few paces behind her. He had no desire to understand her actions, yet after hundreds of years together, he understood all too well why she was doing something so meaningless and inefficient.

*It's to give them despair.*

Jeniela had always been this way. She dangled hope before her enemies' noses only to snatch it away, watching with amusement as they struggled to cling to it before she ripped it from their grasp and trampled on it. She was doing the same now, driving her insubstantial forces to their deaths just to give her opponents a glimmer of hope.

*Maybe we can win.* She was torturing them with that very thought. If she had wanted a complete victory from the start, there would have been no need for such a roundabout approach. She could have simply rammed the Ghost Ship through the barrier, unleashed her forces, and led the slaughter herself.

Excellent mages? Even with perfect preparations grounded in martial arts theory, Jeniela was invincible under the full moon. Their magic might destroy her body, but it could not grant her death. Martial artists who had surpassed the limits of humanity? It was the same for them. In the end, they were only human, and she was a vampire who had perfected the art of killing humans.

"I wonder what they're thinking," Jeniela chuckled.

"Chen and Kun are going to die," Gemini stated. "Their opponents are just too strong for them to handle."

"I suppose so."

"Ju Won will win, though. In the end, he's the only one I brought who will be victorious. The rest of them will die."

"But the Queen is still fine," Gemini pointed out.

"That's right."

His lips twitched at her smiling reply. Jeniela knew Chen and Kun were marching to their deaths, yet despite the long time they had spent together, she felt no sadness or regret for them. Any such emotions had long since worn away inside her, leaving only a singular wish to bring this world to ruin. She had once seen a future of destruction, and the vision had captivated her heart. For hundreds of years, she had worked to create a future where the Slaughter Predator would appear. Even after that future vanished and she could no longer see what was to come, her wish did not change. She still desired the world's destruction.

"I hope they become certain of their victory," Jeniela said, her eyes growing hazy. "I hope they finish their individual fights, join the Phantom Spear, and attack me with him."

A deep pleasure welled up from the depths of her heart as she imagined a future that had not yet come to pass. She pictured Lee Sungmin's face, confident in his victory. If she could just see that face in person… ah, ah. Jeniela shivered and clutched her chest.

"I want to crush it."

She had no intention of killing Lee Sungmin. His companions, however, were another story entirely. The ones in this forest.

*Who should I kill first? Silent Flash, perhaps? No, let's postpone her death for as long as possible. The Crimson Sage? It would be a waste to kill her first, too. If possible, I'll start with the one he has the weakest bond with. A clean kill, and then I'll give Silent Flash and the Crimson Sage painful deaths. Let's tear the death trap to pieces right in front of Lee Sungmin. Or maybe I'll just gut her. Turning them into a Vampire seems like a good idea, too.*

"Are you going to make him a Vampire?" Gemini asked.

"If he begs for it."

Faced with the torn, dying corpse of the death trap, what kind of expression would Lee Sungmin make? When his certainty of victory vanished, what kind of despair would fill him?

*I want to make him beg for his life. When that time comes, I will smile and make my offer. Would you like to become a Vampire?*

*Aine…*

Preskan followed behind them, holding his breath. He paid no mind to the conversation between the two vampires walking ahead, having no doubt that he was the only sane one among them. Understanding their nonsensical chatter was a waste of time. Rescuing Aine came first.

*Why are they walking so slowly?*

Jeniela and Gemini moved at a leisurely pace, and Preskan fought to suppress the frustration boiling inside him. He could not shout at them to hurry, nor could he speed ahead just because he was displeased. Preskan knew his place. If he went on alone, he would be annihilated by the hold power of the Saintess waiting up front, or by that damn Heart Thief and kidnapper, the Phantom Spear.

*Aine… just you wait.*

A father's love burned in Preskan's chest.

Aine's body lay limp, already a corpse. Her heart was gone. Beside Lee Sungmin, who knelt on the ground, Osla stood with a stiff expression.

The new beat in his own chest felt awkward, a sense of alienation washing over him as if his body were no longer his own. The transformation had been a success. Lee Sungmin's body had been that of a Yokai from the start, and with Osla's help, he had devoured Aine's heart. He had taken almost none of the power she possessed. All he had taken was her heart.

"Are you satisfied with the deal?" Nebul asked.

Lee Sungmin placed a hand on his chest and slowly nodded. His desperation had not awakened him or guided him to a new realm of martial arts. Instead, it had forced him to think until his head felt ready to burst, searching for anything he could possibly use.

Erebrisa dealt in everything. Until now, he had only ever thought of it as a convenient shop. His mind raced back through old memories, to his time on Mount Mushii. He remembered that Erebrisa used to purchase even 'souls' there.

"......"

The deal was satisfactory. He had sold Aine's soul to Erebrisa, leaving only an empty body behind. Only after her soul was extracted did he take her heart. Lee Sungmin had not wanted the power Aine possessed, for he was not confident he could handle such strength in his current body. What he needed was her Black Heart. He intended to use it to complete what his own body was lacking.

Lee Sungmin raised a hand and touched his face, pushing away the half-peeled dead skin with his fingertips. He looked down at his damp body.

*Body Reformation…*

It was not a complete Body Reformation, but the gamble had been somewhat successful. The Black Heart was known to evolve its owner to fit any situation. His own was already complete, but the addition of Aine's had forced a new evolution. Even a half-baked Body Reformation was better than nothing.

"This is the first time we've purchased a soul of this magnitude," Nebul said. "What would you like as payment?"

"I intend to fight the Vampire Queen," Lee Sungmin replied, pushing himself to his feet. "What do I need for the fight against her?"

"The courage to run away," Nebul answered with a bitter smile. "If you're truly set on fighting the Queen, you should reconsider. Today is a full moon."

"I can't run away."

"...Unfortunately, we don't have any items that would be helpful in a fight against the Queen."

"Then I'll receive my payment later."

At Lee Sungmin's answer, Nebul's expression grew worried. He thought for a moment, but no matter how much he racked his brain, it was impossible to give any useful advice for a fight against the Vampire Queen. He sighed and nodded.

"I hope we can meet again."

"I have something to receive, so we will."

"What do you plan to receive?"

"Let me meet Laplace."

Nebul's face hardened slightly at his words. "That isn't under my jurisdiction..."

"We can discuss that next time as well. Ah, I'm just asking, but… if I were to sell the Queen's soul, would you be able to purchase it?"

"That… if the situation allows, we could purchase it. However, to extract the soul, the Queen would have to be in a state of complete subjugation."

"If such a situation becomes possible, I'll call you."

Hearing that, Nebul bowed deeply and disappeared into the shadows. Lee Sungmin rubbed at the dead skin still clinging to his face. Osla, who had been watching from the side, sighed and snapped his fingers. A cool breeze swept over Lee Sungmin, clearing the grime from his face.

His face now clean, Lee Sungmin gave Osla a slight bow of his head. "Thank you."

"It was nothing. So? Do you think you can do it?"

"I'll have to try to find out."

"I'll help as much as I can, but in terms of simple power, I'm no match for the Queen. My status might be higher, but don't rely on that too much. I told you, didn't I? Jeniela isn't a transcendent, but she's stronger than one."

"No matter where I fight in this wide world, I'll never have a greater advantage than fighting here in the Fairy Forest."

Jeniela was coming. Even without anyone telling him, Lee Sungmin could feel it with absolute certainty. His heightened senses screamed of an approaching calamity. The queen of monsters, a being who had accumulated power for hundreds of years, was on her way.

[You've grown so much,] the Void Monarch muttered. [The Jeniela from four hundred years ago was strong, but not this strong.]

*It's been a long time.*

[Still, not as much as this Elder. From what I can vaguely sense… she's probably on par with your dead Master, at best. You'd only know for sure by fighting, though.]

Hearing those words, Lee Sungmin let out a dry chuckle. Even with all these advantages, he still could not stand on the same level as the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman. If his Master had been here instead of him, if the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman had not died, this situation would never have happened. Thinking about it that way, the man was truly a great figure. He, along with the Void Monarch, had caused The End to begin, and he was a being who could also have ended it.

"My Master isn't here."

He did not say his Master was dead. He had escaped this world and left on a long journey. *If my Master were alive…* Such thoughts were meaningless. No matter how much he wished or missed him, he would not return. The one here now was not the Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, nor was it Wiji Hoyeon. It was Lee Sungmin. He clutched the mask he had placed inside his robes.

"I have to do it."

[Aren't you afraid?] the Void Monarch asked.

*Why are you even asking?* Lee Sungmin retorted silently.

"You know best."

He and the Void Monarch were connected spiritually. Even if Lee Sungmin did not show his fear, the Void Monarch could feel it. The ancient being let out a deep sigh.

[When you annihilated the Slaughter Predator, I thought everything would work out.]

"Me too."

[Even when you woke up after ten years. I thought there wouldn't be any particular difficulties.]

"My opponents are always bad news. I always fight guys stronger than me."

[This time, it's a bit excessive.]

"Because I'm no longer receiving the Grace of Fate."

Lee Sungmin suddenly remembered something and put his hand into his Subspace Pocket.

[Hey, you,] the Void Monarch voiced, dumbfounded, knowing exactly what Lee Sungmin was thinking.

Lee Sungmin held the egg he had taken out in one hand. He then hurled it at the largest nearby rock.

KWAJIK!

Thrown with inner arts, the egg shattered the rock. The egg itself rolled away unscathed through the debris of the crumbling stone.

"If you throw it right, even an egg can break a rock."

[You crazy bastard,] the Void Monarch muttered with a hollow laugh.

*Why couldn't I have retorted like this when Frau gave me an earful?*

A belated sense of regret washed over Lee Sungmin.
