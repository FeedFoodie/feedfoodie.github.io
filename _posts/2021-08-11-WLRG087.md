Lee Sungmin's eyes widened as he stared at Kim Jonghyun, who seemed equally stunned, his mouth hanging half-open. It had been four years since they had last seen each other before leaving Behenger. Despite the passage of time, Kim Jonghyun looked as if time had stood still for him, completely unchanged.

"This… is a strange connection. I wonder if this is also fate."

Kim Jonghyun muttered as he strode forward. While his face was unchanged from four years ago, his attire was a world apart. The Kim Jonghyun that Lee Sungmin remembered wore a carelessly thrown-on t-shirt with a white gown over it. The man before him now, however, was dressed in a luxurious, dark-colored robe. The black pattern embroidered upon it was not merely stitched with gold thread. It seemed to live, constantly writhing and moving.

"…Do you know each other?"

The middle-aged Taoist, who still held his sword warily against Lee Sungmin, directed the question at Kim Jonghyun. At his words, Lee Sungmin's eyebrow twitched.

A master of the Supreme Peak was a rare sight, though Lee Sungmin's recent encounters had skewed his perspective. Even the largest sects only had a handful of such masters, and reaching the peak realm was considered a mark of skill above the mid-level in any sect.

The middle-aged Taoist from Mount Hua was about half a step stronger than Lee Sungmin. Considering Lee Sungmin's own skills were already knocking on the door of the Supreme Peak, the man blocking his path had to be a master holding a significant position within the Mount Hua Sect, one of the Nine Sects One Gang, yet this very master was speaking to Kim Jonghyun with extreme deference.

"Ah. Ahh. Ah, yes. We know each other, but… what happened here? It looks like there's been some kind of misunderstanding."

Kim Jonghyun muttered, his gaze sweeping across the scene. The young Taoists of Mount Hua who had been knocked down were already scrambling back to their feet. They gasped for breath and shouted their defense.

"W-we didn't do anything wrong! We were just controlling access here as ordered, but he suddenly insisted he had to go inside…!"

"…Hmm…"

The middle-aged Taoist groaned at the young Taoists' cries. He studied Lee Sungmin's face for a moment before a belated realization dawned on him.

"You're… the young man from yesterday," he muttered. "The one who had a private meeting with the Sect Leader, carrying a letter from Shaolin, aren't you?"

"…That's right."

"Yes… that's right. Woopoong mentioned it. He said you knew the Sword Phantom. Don't tell me… you came here to save him?"

"That's… not it."

"Then why did you come here? Why did you try to subdue my disciples? If you didn't come to save the Sword Phantom, then why are you trying to interfere with Mount Hua's pursuit!"

Lee Sungmin struggled for an answer to that question. *I have to meet the Sword Phantom. There's something I want to ask him. I have to take responsibility for this.*

How?

He felt the weight of that responsibility, yet he had no idea how to actually bear it. It was just a vague, powerful feeling. A feeling that he had to take responsibility. That was all. It was an impulse he felt in his heart, one he could not refine into words. Since he could not put it into language, he could not speak it, nor could he sort out his own feelings.

"…Hmm."

Kim Jonghyun, who had been silent until now, shrugged his shoulders. His eyes turned to Lee Sungmin.

[What's going on?]

Kim Jonghyun's voice echoed in his head. It was not Thought Transmission, but it was a similar art. Lee Sungmin quickly explained his situation. Since Kim Jonghyun already knew that he was someone who had died and regressed, the explanation was not very difficult.

"Let's go together," Kim Jonghyun said with a nod after hearing the whole story.

At his words, the middle-aged Taoist turned to look at Kim Jonghyun with surprised eyes.

"What do you mean…?"

"Sage Yusang. I was dispatched by the Guild to assist you at the request of your martial uncle, Sage Jinrang. My assistance is essential to pursue and kill the Sword Phantom, and to hear the full story of the incident from him."

Kim Jonghyun told Sage Yusang with a calm expression. Sage Yusang's face was reluctant, but he nodded his head.

"That… I am also aware of."

"If I can't go with Lee Sungmin, then I won't go either."

When Kim Jonghyun suddenly blurted that out, Sage Yusang's face twisted. Lee Sungmin's expression did the same.

[Isn't that a bit too unreasonable?]

[Is there any other way to persuade that stubborn Taoist? In cases like this, whining like a child is actually more effective.]

[What if Sage Yusang subdues you and takes you by force?]

[Sage Yusang doesn't have the authority to do that. No one from Mount Hua can treat me that way. Mount Hua formally requested support from the Mages Guild for this matter, and I was dispatched for that purpose. If Mount Hua mistreats me, it would be the same as smearing shit on the Mages Guild's prestige.]

[Is it that serious?]

[You've changed quite a bit in four years, Lee Sungmin. Your martial arts seem to have improved greatly. It's not like I've been lazing around either. When I heard Scarlet became the Crimson Archmage, I got annoyed and left the Behenger Mages Guild.]

[…Huh? What do you mean by that?]

[I have now become the Black Magic Tower Master.]

Kim Jonghyun stated this with a nonchalant expression, but Lee Sungmin could not take the words so lightly. Black Magic Tower Master. The title meant Kim Jonghyun had ascended to the highest position among the Black Mages registered with the Mages Guild.

[You don't have to look at me like that. Unlike the other Magic Tower Masters, the Black Magic Tower isn't very large in scale, nor does it possess great power. Most of the skilled Black Mages, like Preskan, were either expelled or killed for breaking taboos, or they never joined the Mages Guild in the first place. Well. Still, I have enough power to be unreasonable at times like this.]

Kim Jonghyun smiled faintly. Sage Yusang, who had been deliberating with a sour expression, gritted his teeth and finally nodded.

"…What if… what if that young man tries to help the Sword Phantom?"

"If that happens, I will kill Lee Sungmin myself. Without fail."

He spat out the words without a change in his expression, and Lee Sungmin could feel that he meant it. Sage Yusang felt it too.

In the end, Sage Yusang permitted Lee Sungmin to enter. Once he was accompanying Lee Sungmin, Kim Jonghyun told Sage Yusang that he did not need an escort.

"Are you saying that young man is more skilled than I am?"

"No. I'm just saying it's not necessary. I am the Black Magic Tower Master. I have more than enough skill to protect myself."

"But I was told to escort you respectfully…"

"I don't feel any respect in you accompanying me. Just go and take care of those young, hurting Taoists over there."

After he went that far, Sage Yusang could no longer follow. Lee Sungmin was amazed as he moved alongside Kim Jonghyun.

"You're… much better at being unreasonable than I thought, Kim Jonghyun."

"Doesn't the position make the man?" Kim Jonghyun replied.

Lee Sungmin walked quickly, his heart impatient, but Kim Jonghyun did not fall behind his pace in the slightest.

"You can run as fast as you like."

Only after Kim Jonghyun said that did Lee Sungmin use his movement arts and leap forward.

"By the way, do you even know where we're supposed to go?"

Glancing to his side, he saw Kim Jonghyun floating about half a hand's span off the ground. A movement art that allowed one to fly would be legendary, but what Kim Jonghyun was using was not martial arts, but magic.

"…I don't know."

"So you have no plan at all. I'll set the direction, so you just go."

Lee Sungmin realized anew just how emotionally he had been acting. If he had not met Kim Jonghyun in that situation, he would have been in serious trouble in a fight with Sage Yusang. Even if he had been lucky enough to subdue the Taoist and move on, he would have made an enemy of Mount Hua, and tracking the Sword Phantom in this vast area would have been impossible.

"But… you called it a dragnet. Why can't I see anyone?"

"If a dragnet meant filling a wide area with people and closing in, how inefficient and troublesome would that be? Well. It seems they used that kind of dragnet in murim, which isn't Eria… but this isn't murim."

Kim Jonghyun's lips twitched into a smile.

"In Eria, there are many ways to replace manpower. Magic is one of those means. To make the dragnet for the Sword Phantom's pursuit more efficient, Mount Hua requested cooperation from the Dreamoor Mages Guild. Of course, it wasn't free, and the Mages Guild accepted. Numerous mages are tracking the Sword Phantom using pursuit magic and familiars, and since I happened to be in Dreamoor, I was the one who came here. The Sword Phantom's location has already been identified. Information about him is being relayed in real-time to the martial artists of Mount Hua."

"…What… are you planning to do when you meet the Sword Phantom?"

"Me? Hmm. I probably won't have a chance to intervene in the actual pursuit. I'm just… here as a countermeasure, since it's said the Sword Phantom uses strange sorcery. I'm also here to interrogate him when he's dead."

"…Interrogate, but you said 'when the Sword Phantom is dead'."

"Sometimes, it's more convenient to interrogate a dead person than a living one. Especially for a Black Mage like me. More importantly… Lee Sungmin. A part of that ominous blessing I felt from you has disappeared."

"…You can feel it?"

"Of course. I was unable to remove it. …Hehe! Why did you get rid of that blessing?" Kim Jonghyun asked with a laugh.

Running in the direction Kim Jonghyun had set, Lee Sungmin did not answer right away, biting his lower lip slightly. A moment later, he replied.

"…I didn't want to rely on it."

"That strange blessing wasn't having a negative effect on you. In fact, it was helping you. Eliciting unconditional favor from complete strangers is a remarkable ability. Even if that favor is impulsive, being able to forcibly imprint a 'first impression'… is an ability of enormous value depending on how it's used, and yet, you threw away such a blessing simply because you didn't want to rely on it?"

"…If I hadn't been aware of the blessing's existence, it might have been different, but as long as I'm aware of it, I'll start to expect things from others. I'll expect them to show me favor, and I'll take it for granted and become arrogant. I… don't want to connect with others through that kind of impulsive, forced goodwill."

"Hahaha!"

Kim Jonghyun burst out laughing at Lee Sungmin's answer.

"Isn't that just self-satisfaction in the end, Lee Sungmin? You've already obtained most of what you needed, and the process of obtaining it was likely thanks to that unknown blessing. You took everything you could get, and now you're saying this?"

"You're right. In the end, it is self-satisfaction," Lee Sungmin did not deny it. "That's the kind of person I am. I'm selfish, and I only think of myself. My actions right now are the same. I don't even know what I want to do when I meet the Sword Phantom. I'm just doing this because I want to."

"That's nothing new," Kim Jonghyun laughed again. "Isn't that what people are like in the first place? Do you need some grand reason, basis, and justification for doing something you want to do? All animals live by impulse, to begin with. Reason is what suppresses it. Just because someone's reason is weak and they don't restrain what they want to do doesn't make them any less of a person, does it?"

He could smell something.

"Just because you act on impulse doesn't mean you need to come up with an answer for why you act this way, Lee Sungmin. You don't need a grand, noble, and universally acceptable reason for erasing the blessing. Isn't it simply this? Lee Sungmin. You did it because you wanted to."

That's why it's called self-satisfaction.

The smell grew stronger. Lee Sungmin knew this scent.

It was the smell of blood.

Ahead, bodies were strewn across the ground.
