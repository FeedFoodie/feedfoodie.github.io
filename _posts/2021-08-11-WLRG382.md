Two days. That was all the time he had. He spent them cross-legged in ceaseless meditation, forgoing food and sleep to wring every possible moment from the short span of time. For the man he was now, physical training and sparring offered no further advantage. A life-or-death battle was out of the question. He could not afford such a risk right before facing Jeniela. Instead, he reviewed every martial art he had ever learned, knowing he would need every trick at his disposal.

He mentally cataloged his arsenal. The Violet Dawn Divine Art, Heaven Reversal Black Thunder, the Blood Ring Divine Demonic Art, the Supreme Nine Heavens Spear, Dancing Shadow Soul Break. He had plenty of inner qi and Demonic Energy, and he could unleash both the Dragon Roar and mysterious phenomena.

However, would any of it work on Jeniela? At full power, his Dragon Roar could burst the heart of a peak master, killing them instantly. Against Jeniela, however, it would do nothing. At best, it might restrain her for a fleeting moment, but that was all. On the night of the full moon, she was said to attain complete immortality.

Would the pinnacle of martial arts work? It was a technique capable of annihilating even the spiritual body of a transcendent. If he stabbed her properly...

*Even when I stabbed her properly, I couldn't annihilate the queen.*

He pushed his consciousness to its limits, recalling how his Black Heart had evolved his body to save him in every past crisis.

*Perhaps that was also because of the Grace of Fate.*

Looking back, his body had been one that absolutely could not be allowed to die, but things were different now. The Grace of Fate was gone. For ten years, he had benefited from its protection, facing only crises that were just manageable enough to overcome. He grew with each one, somehow avoiding the battles he couldn't handle.

Not anymore. That protection was gone. This was different. He could die.

He continued to push his consciousness deeper.

If he died, it was over. He would fail his promise to Wiji Hoyeon, and the comrades who had lent him their strength to stop The End would die with him. Powerlessness, fear, and despair gnawed at him. At the very edge of it all, he relived his memories, replaying every fight he had ever had, clinging to any sliver of hope. This desperate situation, this continuous meditation, this frantic retracing of his past battles... perhaps through it all, he could grasp some forgotten enlightenment and achieve Body Reformation.

If he could just achieve Body Reformation, he would stand a chance against Jeniela. If he could fix the imbalance between his body and his martial arts...

"That's impossible."

The voice shattered his concentration. She was here.

Lee Sungmin opened his eyes and slowly rose to his feet, a bitter expression on his face. Body Reformation from two days of meditation? What a joke. Such a ridiculous miracle would never happen unless he was truly a calamity among calamities, the likes of which the world had never seen. A miracle like that was not for someone like him, who was mediocre at best. No matter how much he pushed his consciousness, no matter how desperately he tried, awakening to some new enlightenment and achieving Body Reformation was simply not going to happen.

"The moon is bright."

The full moon was reflected in the lake's center. Osla looked up at it, her own expression bitter. Lee Sungmin bowed his head deeply to her.

"...I'm sorry."

"I already told you, fighting here is the best option."

"Where are the other fairies?"

"Inside the lake," she replied, adding as she approached him, "It won't be a pretty sight."

She looked at his pale, drawn face with pity, then raised a hand and placed it on his head.

"I'll grant you my blessing. Just like last time, it will protect you from the Vampire Queen's mental attacks."

"Thank you."

"Don't die."

"Come here," Osla said, opening her arms and pulling his head into an embrace.

"...Really, you can't die. Understood? You're the Coalition Leader's disciple. I don't think he died for you, but... if you, who inherited everything from him, were to die as well, I think I would be very sad."

"...Yes."

"If the worst happens, you can run. The Vampire Queen can't kill me anyway. It doesn't matter if this place is reduced to ruins. As long as I'm alive, this forest will be endlessly reborn."

"I understand."

He felt a genuine gratitude for Osla. She had helped him, even breaking her old promise to do so. If they failed to change The End, everyone, including him, would simply die. Osla, however, would not. As a transcendent being, she could not even die and would have to suffer for eons as punishment for her broken promise.

Lee Sungmin glanced at Aine, who was behind Osla. Entangled in wooden vines with her eyes closed, she would likely never open them again.

"...I know." Osla gave a slight nod, sensing his gaze upon Aine.

Outside the lake, the rest of the party had gathered. Scarlet, with dark circles under her eyes, was wearing a flamboyant robe. She held a staff he had never seen before, her hands covered in decorated gloves while five magic tomes swirled around her. Earrings, a necklace, bracelets, rings, a belt, even her shoes... she was adorned with so many flashy items that any one of them would have been extravagant on its own. It reminded him of Frau, who used to wear all sorts of accessories on his bare body. This wasn't just for show, he knew. Every piece Scarlet wore was a high-value artifact.

He stared, thinking it was a bit much, and she glared back. "I just wore a pretty outfit because I might die," she spat.

"You call that a pretty outfit?"

"It's not like I'm going to get a graceful death and be laid in a coffin. I've never dressed like this, so I just piled everything on. How is it?"

"It's excessive."

"Since it might be the last time, you could've said, 'It suits you,' or 'You look pretty.' Would it kill you to say something like that?"

"I'm not saying it because I don't think this is the last time."

Scarlet's expression turned complicated at his words. She pulled the hood of her robe down and let out a short laugh.

"Right. It's not the last time."

Baek Sogo was dressed in her usual martial arts uniform. Her cheeks were also stiff with tension, but her eyes shone with conviction, not fear. He recalled their conversation from two days ago, her words about not running away. He envied her strength.

"Did you gain anything?" the Spear King asked. Among the group, he was the only one filled with joy and anticipation.

Lee Sungmin simply shook his head in response to the excited question.

"It seems there's still no word from Yana."

"......"

"She wouldn't have run away."

"There's no way."

The Black Dragon Knight stood beside Teresa, who clutched a rosary with a pale face. The two had grown much closer since their drinking session a few days ago.

"...They're coming," Teresa muttered in a small voice. "Right on our doorstep," she added, swallowing hard.

The barrier of holy power surrounding the forest was detecting the ominous presence that had drawn so near.

"Teresa, please don't leave this spot. Do your best to maintain the barrier, no matter what."

"Yes... yes."

"Where is Lloyd?"

"He's checking the magic set up near the barrier. I have to go soon, too." Scarlet gripped the staff in her hand tightly. She already knew what she had to do.

KUUUUNG!

The entire forest shook. Teresa gasped and staggered, and the Black Dragon Knight quickly reached out to support her.

"They're here...!"

Teresa refused his support and knelt on the spot. This place was the center of the barrier she had erected. Clasping the rosary with both hands, she closed her eyes and recited a prayer. A dazzling light enveloped her body, illuminating the darkness of the night. Lee Sungmin took a few steps back to avoid its brilliance.

"I'll hold on, I have to... I have to hold on..." Teresa muttered desperately as the light grew even stronger. A brilliant flash erupted from outside the forest as the holy power maintaining the barrier was amplified. Even on a moonlit night, the area beyond the trees became as bright as day.

*Lady Rubia, why don't you stay here as well?*

[I will stay with you,] Rubia answered without hesitation.

*It won't be safe just because you're with me.*

[I know. You think I won't be of any help, don't you? Well... I probably won't be much help in a fight, but there are things I can do, too,] she replied, her voice trembling.

"I have to go." Scarlet spun around abruptly. "When this fight is over..."

"Don't say pointless things," he cut her off. "Hearing that just makes me let my guard down. In novels, whenever someone says that, someone always dies."

Scarlet said nothing more, simply levitating her body with magic. "I'm going."

She didn't say, 'I'll be back.' She just accelerated her levitating body and flew toward the bright light. The Spear King gripped his spear tightly and rose to his feet.

"Today is a wonderful day," he grinned, then kicked off the ground and vanished.

The Black Dragon Knight watched Teresa, who was kneeling in prayer, his eyes full of awe. He gave her a silent nod and turned away.

"Honestly, I don't want to fight to the death."

"Are you going to run?"

"It's too late for that. If it were just you, I might have run... but now I have a reason I can't run away."

"You should consider your age."

Previously, he would have denied it was anything like that, but this time the Black Dragon Knight didn't. He smiled warmly in Teresa's direction and nodded. "I'm too smitten to worry about such things."

"Speaking of which... there's something I'm curious about."

"What is it?"

"Are half-dragons impotent?"

The Black Dragon Knight turned to stare at him, his expression dumbfounded. The corners of his mouth twitched. He seemed to struggle for words before finally composing himself and shaking his head. "They are not."

"I see."

"Why would you ask something like that?"

"Mules are sterile..."

"Do I look like a mule to you?" the knight demanded.

"It wasn't me. Scarlet was the one who was curious."

"Mages, I swear...!" he huffed, turning away.

Once the Black Dragon Knight had also left, only Baek Sogo remained. She reached out with both hands and grasped Lee Sungmin's shoulders.

"No one is going to die."

Lee Sungmin didn't answer.

"Junior Disciple, don't try to shoulder everything by yourself. You have us... you have me. I'll finish up as quickly as possible and come help you. So... okay? Don't be too reckless."

"I understand."

However, he had to be reckless. Without it, there was no way to overcome this crisis. He had to use every means at his disposal, no matter how dangerous. Fortunately, Osla was here. It was a stroke of luck that she, who could even temporarily suppress the mutation of a Yokai, was with him.

Two days of meditation hadn't produced a miracle. In that case, he would have to create one himself. By luring Jeniela here, the negotiation had already become a trap. Jeniela knew this, which was why she had brought her entire legion to invade the forest.

After even Baek Sogo had gone, Lee Sungmin moved past the praying Teresa and returned to the lake's edge where Osla was waiting. He exchanged a look with the fairy queen before approaching Aine.

He looked at the girl bound in vines, feeling nothing for her. Any sense of kinship from sharing the same heart was long gone. Aine was a monster and had been since the moment of her birth. He didn't know how many beings she had devoured, and he had no intention of ever finding out.

Lee Sungmin reached out his hand toward the monster.

Now, he had to become one himself.
