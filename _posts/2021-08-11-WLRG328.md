From a certain point of view, Lee Sungmin was a hero.

Had he not been consumed by madness during the ten-year war, had the story ended with his victory over the Death Lord and the Demon Lord Kim Jonghyun, he would have undoubtedly become one. The evil deeds he committed as the Phantom Spear would have paled in comparison to the atrocities of Vollander and Kim Jonghyun, the men he had slain.

However, Lee Sungmin failed to become a hero. He defeated the lord of the Death Knights and the Demon Lord, only to be swallowed by madness and become a monster in the end. If Lee Sungmin was the protagonist of that decade-long tale, it concluded as a tragedy.

"Let's go."

That very man, the Phantom Spear who had defeated two great evils, now stood before them. The warriors of the Murim Alliance, freed from their bonds by his hand, wore expressions of deep uncertainty. In the past, Lee Sungmin had been their enemy. Of course, none of them would dare challenge him over events from ten years ago. They were capable of making that distinction. A fight would be pointless, and more importantly, Lee Sungmin was now their savior. The reverse was also true. To Lee Sungmin, they were the ones who had saved him, their lives offered as a sacrifice to break his Sealing.

"Uh... well..." Tang Ahee stammered, wanting to say something but finding the words would not come. She could only glance at the back of the man walking ahead of them, leading them out of Germud.

"Thank you for saving us," Moyong Chan finally said, his voice tight. He glared at Lee Sungmin's back, but he did not charge forward screaming for revenge as his father might have. He was not even certain he wanted vengeance.

"...Great Warrior, do you... do you really not remember anything from the past ten years?" Jegal Young asked cautiously when Lee Sungmin offered no reply to the gratitude. She called him 'Great Warrior' even though he might be her brother's enemy, a title that acknowledged her respect for his feat of defeating Vollander and Kim Jonghyun.

In response to her question, Lee Sungmin gave a slight nod. "I have no memories from while I was under the Sealing."

*What on earth happened over the past ten years?* The question echoed in his mind. He planned to lead these warriors out of Germud and then summon Nebul to find out.

[Is there really a need to escort them? You could just leave them behind.]

*My Sealing was broken because they became sacrifices. This is my way of repaying them.*

[Repaying them? What nonsense,] the Void Monarch grumbled.

"If you have any questions, please ask us," Jegal Young offered. "We'll answer if we can."

"What is your name?" Lee Sungmin asked first.

Jegal Young was momentarily at a loss for words, not expecting such a question. After a brief pause, she hesitantly answered, "...I am... Jegal... Young."

The name made Lee Sungmin turn around in surprise. Jegal Young. If she was a Jegal...

"The late Jaegal Taeryeong... he was my older brother."

"Ah." What could he possibly say to that? As Lee Sungmin hesitated, Jegal Young quickly raised a hand.

"It's alright."

He cleared his throat. "...Ten years ago. In this city... I mean, after I went mad. What happened?"

Fortunately, Jegal Young could answer. "After you were swallowed by madness, you attacked the survivors of the Kim Jonghyun subjugation force. In the process, some damage... occurred."

*As I thought.* Lee Sungmin let out a quiet sigh. He had no memory of it at all.

"The subjugation force was too exhausted," she continued. "They couldn't stop your rampage and had to retreat. What they saw as they fled... was you killing and devouring the zombies."

"......"

The Void Monarch made a retching sound in his mind. The corners of Lee Sungmin's mouth twitched. The thought of eating the flesh of rotten zombies left a disgusting taste in his mouth.

"After regrouping, the subjugation force returned to stop you. By that point, however, you were no longer in the city. You had been put under the Sealing. According to the Vampire, it seems the Grand Sorcerer who participated in the subjugation force was the one who sealed you... but there's another mystery. The traces they found at the time suggested you had engaged in a fierce battle with someone."

"...A fight?"

"Yes. We don't know who it was, but the evidence left behind spoke of a battle so fierce it had collapsed the terrain and reshaped the city itself."

*Who was it?*

*It wasn't Frau. She isn't skilled in hand-to-hand combat. It's a mystery. Even if Frau is a Grand Sorcerer at the pinnacle of Ghost Soul Art, could she have subdued and sealed a rampaging Lee Sungmin who had fully awakened as a Yokai?*

*Someone stopped me.* The conclusion was natural. While the Holy Knights retreated, someone had held him back, and Frau must have joined forces with that person to perform the Sealing.

*But who? Who had that kind of power? It wouldn't have been Jeniela of the Predator or Ju Won. The Spear King and the Black Dragon Knight, who vanished from the North? The timing makes that impossible... no, if the Black Dragon Knight flew in his Dragon form, he might have been able to get from the North to the South in a little over a week.*

*It wouldn't be the Martial God.*

*Could it have been Wiji Hoyeon? Or... Senior Sister Baek Sogo?* He had suspicions but no certainty.

"Fortunately, the Saint of the Church was safe," Jegal Young finished. "Unable to find you, they announced what had happened in Germud. For ten years... you were believed to be dead."

"...I see., but he wasn't dead.

*There's a lot I need to find out. I need to find Wiji Hoyeon. I heard so much from the Slaughter Predator about the reason for her absurd talent. The Demonic Spirit is forging her into an Absolute Being to stop The End. Ten years have passed... has she gained that much power?*

*The world hasn't ended in the last ten years. The End is not yet over.*

*He said the Spirit Queen would awaken.* The Slaughter Predator had said so as he vanished. *Does that mean she will become the calamity of The End, but why?* Lee Sungmin didn't know about Abel killing Cain, so he couldn't understand the Spirit Queen's motive. *He also said the Vampire Queen would make her move. That must mean she is also part of The End.*

*Can I stop them with my current power?* He had no confidence. The Jeniela he had seen was a monster of seemingly limitless strength. Even with the full power of a Yokai, he wasn't sure he could win against her.

"Well then." Lee Sungmin stopped walking before the gates of Behenger. He figured escorting them this far was enough to repay the debt for breaking his Sealing. Jegal Young looked like she wanted to say more, but he didn't listen. Having regained his senses after a decade, he had too much to do. First, he would return to the Fairy Forest to meet Osla and check on Scarlet. After that, he would go to Urmuri to find Yana and ask about Frau's whereabouts. It was an enormous distance, but distance was meaningless to him.

"...Huh?"

He summoned the Fairy Horse. Or rather, he tried to. The Fairy Horse did not appear.

Lee Sungmin blinked, looking around. He called for it once more, but still nothing happened. A flicker of confusion crossed his face. Why wouldn't the horse, which had served him so well before the Sealing, appear now?

*What?*

[It seems your connection to the Fairy Horse was severed while you were under the Sealing in the dimensional rift.]

He didn't know if that was the true reason, but he couldn't use the horse. A wave of frustration washed over him. Without it, he would have to travel that enormous distance on his own two feet.

*I'll have to go to Urmuri first.*

After several more failed attempts, Lee Sungmin gave up. He gave a slight wave to the Murim Alliance warriors, who were staring at him blankly. "...Well then, I'll be going."

With those words, he used his movement arts, leaping over the city wall in a single bound. Even without the Fairy Horse, his speed was greater than anyone else's. His reserves of inner qi and demonic energy were immeasurably vast, so if he put his mind to it, he could run for days without rest. The journey from Germud to Urmuri, which normally took a week, would not even take him three days.

Before that, however, he needed information. After putting some distance between himself and the warriors, Lee Sungmin called for Nebul.

"Good heavens." Nebul emerged from the shadows at his call. His eyes widened as he took in Lee Sungmin, then he quickly composed himself and bowed his head deeply. "This is... truly, it's been a long time. You're alive?"

"Did you hear the rumors about me too?"

"They were rumors impossible not to hear," Nebul said with an awkward smile. "Though it's already a story from ten years ago."

Lee Sungmin let out a small laugh. "I want to know what happened during the ten years I was gone, and..." He requested information on Wiji Hoyeon, Baek Sogo, Scarlet, the Black Dragon Knight, and the Spear King. He also asked about Heaven Beyond Heaven and the Martial God.

"Ah... and." Lee Sungmin scratched his head, feeling the emptiness on his back. "Could you also connect me with Lord Celgerus? After I hear the information first."

"Yes, I understand."

Because he had requested so much, it was nearly sunset when Nebul contacted him again. Lee Sungmin had stopped running to camp for the night. He lit a campfire and opened his Subspace Pocket. Fortunately, the food he had stored ten years ago was still there.

"Here." Nebul held out a transparent crystal ball. Lee Sungmin took it and focused his mind.

"......"

The sphere glowed, and a vast amount of information flowed into his head.

Wiji Hoyeon and Baek Sogo had made sporadic appearances over the past decade, seemingly wandering the world. The surprising one was Scarlet.

*Crimson Sage?*

The Great Sage of Legro Forest. That was what they called Scarlet now. Legro Forest was the same Fairy Forest where Osla resided. It seemed Scarlet had been living there ever since he left her behind ten years ago.

As for the Black Dragon Knight and the Spear King, there were no confirmed rumors. *Could they have died in the fight against the Martial God?* Lee Sungmin chewed on his lower lip. Heaven Beyond Heaven still had not revealed its identity, and there was no news of the Martial God either.

However, starting ten years ago, the Vampires and Lycanthropes, long silent in the North, had begun to move actively. The Predator had begun to move. The current Murim Alliance Chairman was the Martial Beggar, leader of the Beggars' Gang. After suffering the humiliation of having his formation shattered by the former Unorthodox Alliance Chairman, he had taken over following the Black Dragon Knight's disappearance.

*The Predator has made its move... just as the Slaughter Predator said.*

*What does Jeniela want now? What kind of future has she seen?*

Lee Sungmin sat before the campfire, lost in thought. The world he had woken up to was in a state of constant conflict, with battles large and small breaking out between the Murim Alliance and the new Blood Alliance. Unlike the old Unorthodox-Demonic Alliance, the Blood Alliance was violent and tyrannical, and the Predator was on the move. It was an age of chaos.

*The Martial God's goal is to stop The End, but the Spirit that moves Heaven Beyond Heaven is a pawn of The End. What has the Martial God been doing for the past ten years? What about Wiji Hoyeon? And Baek Sogo?*

"May I connect you to Lord Celgerus?" Nebul asked cautiously.

"Ah, yes." Lee Sungmin pulled himself from his thoughts and nodded.

First, he needed a new spear.
