It was a night brilliantly illuminated by the full moon.

In the center of the Hyonwon Clan's training ground, Hyonwon Kang and Peng Sahyuk faced each other, daos drawn.

"Let's fight without using inner arts," Hyonwon Kang proposed.

Peng Sahyuk let out a short, mocking laugh. "Why? Think you can win if we don't use inner arts?"

"Bastard. What if one of us gets seriously hurt with no witnesses around?"

"Don't worry. I'll go easy enough when I beat you down."

"This son of a bitch..."

Although he had largely turned over a new leaf now, Hyonwon Kang from his delinquent days was famous for his nasty temper.

Even now, he wasn't the type to keep his mouth shut when insulted by an opponent.

"Forgot already? About the time I knocked you out cold?"

"......"

It happened when they were kids.

During a sparring match, Hyonwon Kang's wooden sword struck Peng Sahyuk's head. Peng Sahyuk collapsed, bleeding, and had to be carried away.

...And that day, the Hyonwon Clan's Patriarch knelt before the enraged Patriarch of the Hebei Peng Clan.

Peng Sahyuk was inwardly quite flustered, never expecting Hyonwon Kang to bring that up first.

Of course, he didn't let any of it show on his face.

"...Did you learn how to subtly piss people off from Baek Suryong too?"

"Well, I can't deny it."

Peng Sahyuk chuckled, "Keuk keuk," watching Hyonwon Kang shrug. Savage fighting spirit, however, flickered in his eyes.

"Fine. I don't need inner arts to thrash you anyway."

As Peng Sahyuk raised his dao into a starting stance, his entire presence changed, even without circulating inner arts.

*Grrrrr...*

It felt as if a tiger's growl echoed from somewhere.

The Hebei Peng Clan's Five Tiger Demolition Blade.

It was a peerless blade art, always mentioned first when discussing the world's strongest dao techniques.

"Come at me first. I'll give you the first move."

Hyonwon Kang raised his dao to a middle guard. He moved slowly, carefully observing Peng Sahyuk's movements.

*How much has the gap closed?*

Last time they fought, Peng Sahyuk had overwhelmingly defeated him.

That was only a few months ago.

In those few months, however, Hyonwon Kang prided himself on having trained his martial arts more intensely than anyone else.

*I'll win this time.*

Hyonwon Kang kicked off the ground, shooting forward.

WHOOSH!

The distance closed in an instant. The condition of not using inner arts applied equally to both. The deciding factor, then, was who could use their body better, who could read and deceive the opponent's movements first.

CLANG!

He gauged his opponent's strength from the first clash. As expected, he was behind in pure power. Hyonwon Kang had a sturdy build, but Peng Sahyuk was taller and heavier.

*Doesn't matter.*

He had trained external arts daily alongside inhuman monsters like Geo Sangwoong and Ya Suhyeok. Thanks to that, Hyonwon Kang knew how to fight opponents stronger and tougher than himself.

WHOOSH!

Hyonwon Kang launched himself into the air. He was confident his flexibility and elasticity were superior to Peng Sahyuk's. Peng Sahyuk's dao sliced through the space where he had been just a moment before.

WHIRL!

Hyonwon Kang spun his body mid-air. He swung his dao downwards, adding rotational force.

CLAAANG!!

"Kuh!"

A groan escaped Peng Sahyuk's lips. His knees bent slightly. Hyonwon Kang grinned, baring his teeth fiercely.

"You'll be utterly humiliated if you think I'm the same as last time."

"...Don't get cocky."

Eyes flashing fiercely, Peng Sahyuk twisted his wrist, deflecting Hyonwon Kang's attack sideways. Sparks flew as the blades scraped against each other.

KKKKRRRK!

Peng Sahyuk had also trained his body, learning the external arts from the Hebei Peng Clan's secret manuals. His well-defined muscles were like armor, and he certainly didn't lack flexibility.

SHRRIIIK!

The blade tore through the air. Its power and speed were unbelievable, making it hard to accept no inner arts were involved. Hyonwon Kang narrowly retreated backward.

*He really is strong.*

The fine hairs all over his body stood on end. If he lost sight of his opponent's movements for even a second, it was over. Hyonwon Kang's thoughts accelerated. Strangely, a joyful smile formed on his lips.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

Under the moonlight, the two daos danced together. Dozens of trajectories intertwined, stirring up fierce winds.

*I can see it.*

For the past few months, Hyonwon Kang had trained every day, imagining the day he would fight Peng Sahyuk again. It wouldn't be wrong to call it an obsession.

*I will win this time.*

The trajectory of Peng Sahyuk's dao swings, his habitual movements, Hyonwon Kang had engraved them into his mind, replaying his past defeat hundreds, thousands of times to avenge it.

"I told you I'll win this time!"

Hyonwon Kang swung his dao, his senses heightened to their peak.

The growth of a genius completely focused on defeating a single rival was astounding.

"...You've improved quite a bit."

Peng Sahyuk hid the shock in his low voice.

The movements were unbelievable, making him doubt if this was the same person from months ago.

Finding himself suddenly pushed onto the defensive, Peng Sahyuk clenched his jaw tightly.

*These damn geniuses.*

When he was young, Peng Sahyuk had admired Hyonwon Kang.

A boy the same age as him, yet who wielded the dao with skill he couldn't possibly imitate, who fought in unpredictable ways driven by natural instinct.

He seemed to sparkle. Peng Sahyuk had often thought, *I want to be like that too.* That was why whenever Hyonwon Kang visited his clan, Peng Sahyuk would follow him around, pestering him for a sparring match.

> "Kang! Let's spar!"
>
> "Okay!"

...That was all just talk from their foolish childhood days, long gone now.

The current Peng Sahyuk neither admired nor envied Hyonwon Kang.

*Turns out there are plenty of geniuses in the world besides you.*

Deciding there was nothing more to learn at the Azure Dragon Academy, Peng Sahyuk had transferred to the Heavenly Martial Academy this year.

...He thought it wouldn't be much different. Peng Sahyuk, who had reigned like a tyrant at the Azure Dragon Academy, planned to dominate the Heavenly Martial Academy too.

There, however, he saw it.

He saw that absurd geniuses truly existed in the world.

Peng Sahyuk challenged them, lost, got back up, and challenged them again. Setbacks were familiar. The Young Master of the Hebei Peng Clan didn't know the meaning of giving up, even from a young age.

Then, luck followed. At the Heavenly Martial Academy, there was a new instructor who took an interest in the third-year transfer student from the Azure Dragon Academy.

> "You. Your talent is a bit ambiguous, but you're interesting."

He helped Peng Sahyuk find his own path. He taught him how to overcome his inferiority complex, how to defeat geniuses.

> "I can help you get stronger if you want. Are you good at enduring pain?"

"...No matter how much of a genius you are."

Peng Sahyuk glared at Hyonwon Kang, his eyes burning like flames.

The third-year Peng Sahyuk of the Heavenly Martial Academy hadn't remained at the level Hyonwon Kang had imagined over the past few months.

"I am stronger."

RIIIIP!

Peng Sahyuk's muscles swelled, tearing his martial arts uniform top. His body now thicker, Peng Sahyuk swung his dao with tremendous force.

"Crazy!"

It was too late to dodge. Hyonwon Kang pulled his dao as close to his body as possible to minimize the impact.

CLAAAAAAANG!

Sent flying far back, Hyonwon Kang finally absorbed the shock and landed on the ground.

Rubbing his stinging wrist, Hyonwon Kang asked with a bewildered expression, "What was that?"

"I merely returned to my original body. I usually keep my muscles contracted using a special technique."

"Crazy... Why would you do something like that?"

"It's a form of training."

That physique was massive, perhaps not losing out even to Geo Sangwoong or Ya Suhyeok.

No, in terms of sheer intimidating presence, it might be even greater.

Peng Sahyuk strode towards Hyonwon Kang. It looked like a boulder was moving.

"Hyonwon Kang. Ready to get beaten up all night?"

"Peng Sahyuk. Think you've won just because you got bigger?"

Hyonwon Kang charged at Peng Sahyuk again. Peng Sahyuk did the same.

They only held back from using inner arts; otherwise, the two clashed with their entire beings.

CLANG! CLANG! KAKLANG!

Shockwaves spread each time the daos collided.

Blade Wind generated purely by the pressure of their strikes savagely raked the space, carving distinct dao marks onto the ground.

"Good heavens..."

"What is this..."

As the fight dragged on without a clear winner and the commotion grew, the Hyonwon Clan members, the Peng Clan martial artists, and the guests who had planned to stay overnight after the banquet gathered to watch their battle.

"For the two Young Masters' martial arts to be so outstanding..."

"Heo! So there was a reason the Azure Dragon Hero took Master Hyonwon Kang as his disciple."

"Are you saying they're this strong even without using inner arts?"

"Truly a battle between dragon and tiger. A duel between well-matched rivals, the likes of which I haven't seen recently."

Worthy rivals.

The term resonated fittingly with the two figures pouring their polished skills entirely against each other.

"Just don't get hurt..."

"Oppa..."

The Hyonwon Patriarch and Hyonwon Ran watched Hyonwon Kang's fight with anxious hearts.

Although they weren't using inner arts, the fight was so fierce it wouldn't be strange if one of them got seriously injured.

Opposite them, the Peng Clan martial artists stood tense. They looked ready to jump in immediately if Peng Sahyuk got hurt.

Just then, Hyonwon Kang and Peng Sahyuk, who had been clashing without yielding an inch, stepped back as if by agreement.

"Hoo..."

"Haa..."

They panted, glaring at each other for a long moment, then slowly lowered their daos simultaneously.

If they continued, their competitive spirits would drive them to draw upon their inner arts. Both knew very well that would escalate things too much.

"...Let's settle this next time," Hyonwon Kang said eventually.

"...In an official setting," Peng Sahyuk replied.

Regret lingered on the faces of the two who couldn't conclude their match, but they also looked somewhat relieved.

As they sheathed their daos at the same time, praise and applause rained down on the two for the excellent duel they had displayed.

"That was truly a magnificent duel!"

"I guarantee it! Those two will soon contend for the title of world's strongest bladesman!"

"May the connection between the two Young Masters continue as worthy rivals!"

Ultimately, the fight that day ended in a draw.

{sep}

The next day, the martial artists of the Hebei Peng Clan packed their belongings early in the morning.

Hyonwon Kang accompanied his father to see the Peng Clan off.

Peng Sahyuk, who hadn't said a word to him until then, said to Hyonwon Kang for the first time.

"Hey, Kang."

"...Me?"

"Is there another Hyonwon Kang here besides you?" Peng Sahyuk looked at Hyonwon Kang with an exasperated expression.

"……" Hyonwon Kang couldn't bring himself to say, *I'm more used to being called Wonkang these days.* 

Instead, he scrunched his brow fiercely and snapped, "What? Why? What do you want, you bastard?"

"...Did you hit your head hard yesterday?"

"Got a problem? Wanna go another round right here?"

Peng Sahyuk chuckled, "Keuk keuk," at Hyonwon Kang's silly provocation.

"What I showed you yesterday wasn't even thirty percent of my strength. If you think today will be the same, expect to be shattered by my hand next time."

"I only showed twenty percent of mine."

"You hate losing just as much as ever."

Smiling faintly, Peng Sahyuk casually dropped the words he really wanted to say.

"I'll win next time. At the Heavenly Martial Festival."

"Look forward to it. That's when the real fight happens."

Peng Sahyuk also bid farewell to the Hyonwon Patriarch, then departed from the Hyonwon Clan estate with the Peng Clan martial artists.

"...Bastard. Still annoyingly strong."

Hyonwon Kang muttered, glaring at Peng Sahyuk's retreating back.

He would have lost if they had fought to the end.

Hyonwon Kang knew this better than anyone, and Peng Sahyuk probably sensed it too.

Nevertheless, the guy had stopped his dao. He had accepted a draw in front of so many people.

To Hyonwon Kang, that felt like the pity of the stronger fighter. He was so frustrated he couldn't sleep a wink last night.

"Just you wait. Next time, I'll beat you so bad you won't make a peep."

That day, Hyonwon Kang wrote a reply to the letter he received from Baek Suryong.

He tried to keep it brief, but just writing about the fight with Peng Sahyuk filled several pages.

"And..."

Hyonwon Kang pondered what to write at the end of the letter, then squeezed his eyes shut and wrote:

「Teacher. When vacation ends, give me special training for the Heavenly Martial Festival!」
