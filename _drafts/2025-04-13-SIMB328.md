Thud, thud.

Just him walking was enough to put everyone on edge. He wasn't flaring his energy or projecting any killing intent. His natural air of command alone seemed to crush the crowd.

"Peng Sahyuk..." Hyonwon Kang murmured, watching him approach.

His rock-hard muscles strained against his martial arts uniform, too bulky to hide completely. Broad shoulders, thick chest muscles. Despite his size, he didn't look slow at all. He looked like he could pounce like a leopard at any moment.

He wore a navy robe over his martial arts uniform, and his eyes swept over the crowd like a huge tiger scanning for prey from a misty mountain peak.

*He's stronger.*

Hyonwon Kang knew it instantly.

Just like he wasn't the old delinquent anymore, the Peng Sahyuk standing there wasn't the same guy either.

Peng Sahyuk had left the Azure Dragon Academy, transferred to the Heavenly Martial Academy, and come back much, much stronger. Hyonwon Kang's palms felt sweaty. A strange smile played on his lips.

"The Peng Clan's Young Master?"

"What's the Young Master doing here?"

"I didn't hear anything about the Hyonwon Patriarch inviting the Peng Clan..."

The martial artists gathered here were all respected figures in Shanxi Province.

The ones who had just shown up, however, were on another level, powerful players in the murim who ruled their own territory like kings.

The Hebei Peng Clan.

A major family with hundreds of years of history and power.

As one of the Five Great Clans, they were a famous house known for producing countless masters of the blade through their aggressive and powerful dao techniques.

Even when the Hyonwon Clan was at its peak, they were compared to the Hebei Peng Clan only for their refined blade arts; they could never challenge the Peng Clan's influence and now, the heir to that clan was at the Hyonwon Clan's banquet?

*Is he here to congratulate them or warn them?*

*This isn't good. Especially with the messy history between the Hyonwon and Peng Clans.*

*Are the Peng Clan trying to stomp on the Hyonwon Clan now that they're trying to rise again?*

What did this mean?

Confusion spread among the martial artists from the various sects and families.

"Hmm..."

Just then, Peng Sahyuk spotted Hyonwon Kang too. A flicker of interest lit up his bored-looking face.

*What are you staring at?*

Hyonwon Kang glared right back at Peng Sahyuk.

Neither backed down, and it turned into a staring contest.

It ended abruptly, though, when the Hyonwon Patriarch stepped forward.

"Hoho. The Young Master of the Hebei Peng Clan has graced us with his presence! Every time I see you, you seem to reach new heights. Your progress is truly amazing, Young Master."

The Hyonwon Patriarch said respectfully to the Peng Clan's Young Master. That showed the Hyonwon Clan's current position.

Hyonwon Kang gritted his teeth seeing that, while Peng Sahyuk just took it in stride.

"Sorry for showing up unannounced. Please forgive our sudden arrival."

"Hoho. Forgive? Please, make yourselves comfortable. You've come a long way, so please stay the night here."

"Thank you for your hospitality. We'll take you up on that offer for the night."

After the short greeting, Peng Sahyuk stepped back.

He was too young to keep talking formally with the Patriarch. Peng Sagun naturally stepped into his place.

"......"

"......"

Hyonwon Kang and Peng Sahyuk's eyes met again for a second, but neither said anything.

{sep}

After the Hebei Peng Clan's group arrived, the banquet felt kind of tense.

Everyone was watching the Hebei Peng Clan now, not the Hyonwon Clan.

It felt like the hosts of the party had suddenly changed.

No, they actually had.

"Alright everyone, let's have a drink!"

Peng Sagun raised his cup. Nobody dared to refuse.

"The Hebei Peng Clan has long shared a bond of friendship with the Hyonwon Clan. We congratulate the Hyonwon Clan for finally overcoming their struggles and reclaiming their past glory."

Peng Sagun had said *Hyonwon Clan* with a slightly dismissive tone.

It might have seemed like a mistake, except an official envoy wouldn't slip up like that here.

*He's here to put them down.*

*The Peng Clan planned this.*

His words subtly looked down on the Hyonwon Clan. Everyone present could feel it.

The Hyonwon Patriarch forced a smile.

"...Hoho. Thank you."

A friendly family.

He used to believe that once.

Decades ago, when the Hyonwon Clan fell after the Crazy Demon's Massacre, the Hebei Peng Clan was the first to offer help.

*I didn't realize back then that they wanted to be masters, not friends.*

Now that they had recovered the Earth Shattering Blade, the Hyonwon Clan didn't need the Peng Clan's help anymore.

He knew the Peng Clan wouldn't like that.

Still, he hadn't expected them to show up at the banquet and pressure them so openly.

"And that's not the only thing to celebrate!"

Peng Sagun stood right next to the Hyonwon Patriarch, talking loudly.

Even if the Hebei Peng Clan was way bigger than the Hyonwon Clan, that was just rude guest behavior.

"The Hyonwon Clan has gained a strong friend in the Azure Dragon Hero, haven't they? That's also worth congratulating!"

"...Thank you."

"Of course, many still argue whether the Azure Dragon Hero really deserves to be one of the Ten Supremes. Haha!"

"......"

Every word pricked like a thorn.

Hyonwon Kang's face twisted more and more. Peng Sahyuk just drank coolly, like it was none of his business.

"Patriarch-nim. Don't push us away just because you found a new friend."

"Push you away? What do you mean..."

"You know exactly what I mean."

Peng Sagun smirked right in front of everyone.

"One person's martial arts might bring glory for a century, while a family lasts for hundreds of years. Our history and power are on a different level."

"......"

It was a warning: don't get cocky just because you have the Azure Dragon Hero.

It was also a blatant threat: try to leave our shadow, and we won't just watch anymore.

"...Master Peng Sagun. Are you quite finished?"

Hyonwon Kang snapped. He couldn't take it anymore.

Crashing the party wasn't enough? Now insults and threats too?

He strode forward until he was right in front of Peng Sagun.

Peng Sagun looked puzzled. "Did I say something wrong?"

"If that wasn't a threat, what was it?"

A smug smile spread across Peng Sagun's face.

He looked down at Hyonwon Kang like he was dirt.

"Well, look who's grown up. I remember when you were just a kid who couldn't even meet my eyes."

A twisted smile touched Hyonwon Kang's lips too.

He was done being a delinquent, though he wasn't Baek Suryong's disciple if he let that slide.

"Yeah. I've grown up a lot. Enough to punch that face of yours."

"...What?"

Peng Sagun's face went cold at the unexpected retort. Behind him, Peng Sahyuk chuckled, "Keuk keuk."

"...So you inherited the Sky Sundering Blade alias. Guess you want to show me those famous skills."

"Anytime."

Hyonwon Kang didn't flinch facing the Hebei Peng Clan's Commander.

"That's enough!"

The shocked Hyonwon Patriarch tried to stop his son, only for Hyonwon Kang to firmly shake his head.

*If we back down now, the Hyonwon Clan will never get out from under the Peng Clan's thumb.*

This guy wasn't the Peng Clan's Patriarch or an Elder.

Just a Commander.

Sure, that rank carried weight in the murim, however, he wasn't one of the top dogs in the Hebei Peng Clan.

*I don't think I'd lose.*

He couldn't have imagined thinking that just six months ago.

Now, Hyonwon Kang wasn't just being cocky.

Placing a hand on his dao's hilt, Hyonwon Kang stated, "I'll show you the Way of the Hyonwon Clan's blade."

"Arrogant brat...!"

Peng Sagun glared, his face contorted.

How dare this kid talk about the Hyonwon Clan's blade arts in front of the Hebei Peng Clan? Did learning a thing or two from the Azure Dragon Hero make his ego swell to the sky?

"Fine. I'll teach you what real blade techniques look like. Draw your weapon."

A powerful, imposing aura radiated from Peng Sagun.

Sparks were about to fly.

"Hyung-nim. Stop spoiling the mood for drinks."

"...Young Master?"

Peng Sagun stared, baffled, at Peng Sahyuk who had suddenly jumped in. They were cousins, but Peng Sagun was much older.

"I tried to stay quiet, and you're going too far. You started this fight, why are you picking on a kid?"

Peng Sahyuk chuckled, "Keuk keuk." His shoulders shook, and with the wine bottle in his hand, he looked a bit drunk.

"...Young Master. How can you speak so haughtily?"

Peng Sagun looked utterly confused.

The whole point of the Hebei Peng Clan coming here was to put the Hyonwon Clan in their place. The Patriarch had okayed it.

So why was their own Young Master messing things up?

"Haughty?"

Peng Sahyuk snorted.

"Crashing someone else's party is way more haughty. Give it a rest already. Picking on a little brat half your age? Pathetic. Tsk tsk."

"Peng Sahyuk!"

Face turning beet red, Peng Sagun glared daggers at him.

Peng Sahyuk simply met his stare calmly.

"We came to drink, so let's drink. Stop this nonsense. You guys, relax."

Peng Sahyuk nodded towards the Peng Clan martial artists. The ones with hands on their swords hesitated, then lowered them.

Peng Sagun was a Commander; Peng Sahyuk was the Young Master.

It was obvious who they had to listen to.

"You! How are you going to face the Patriarch's anger when we get back..."

"I'll deal with my own problems. I hated this plan from the start. Why does our family need to pull stunts like this?"

Muttering to himself, Peng Sahyuk turned back to Peng Sagun.

"Hyung-nim, you head back first. I'm staying to drink some more."

"...!!"

Peng Sahyuk kicked him out, something even the Hyonwon Patriarch couldn't do. Peng Sagun's face crumpled.

"Didn't you hear me? That's an order."

"...You'll regret this."

With that parting shot, Peng Sagun left, taking half the Peng Clan men with him.

Peng Sahyuk watched him go for a second, then turned to the remaining guests with a wide grin.

"Alright, everyone! Let's relax and enjoy the party now!"

His eyes scanned the crowd and landed on Hyonwon Kang again.

A strange smile touched Peng Sahyuk's lips.

"Guess I should congratulate you. You must've crawled through hell to get this far."

"That jerk. What's he mumbling about?"

The awkward feeling didn't disappear, though, lingering until the banquet finally ended and everyone left.

{sep}

Late that night.

Even after the party was over, Peng Sahyuk wandered the Hyonwon Clan courtyard, taking swigs from a liquor bottle. He looked like a predator pacing, searching for prey.

"Stop hiding like a rat and come out."

Peng Sahyuk turned and glared into the shadows. Hyonwon Kang stepped out.

Hyonwon Kang got straight to the point.

"Why'd you do that earlier?"

"Do what."

"Kick your cousin out. Aren't you gonna get chewed out when you go back?"

Peng Sahyuk laughed, "Keuk keuk." His laugh always sounded oddly light for his big frame.

"I'm the confirmed heir, the Young Master. I'll get yelled at a bit, but even my father can't really touch me."

"Right. Bet you think you're hot stuff."

"Everything else about me is thicker than yours too."

Peng Sahyuk casually showed off a muscular forearm before downing the last of his drink. Hyonwon Kang kept glaring at him.

"What now?"

"Why did you help me? I could've beaten Peng Sagun even without you stepping in."

"Idiot. You think I helped *you*?"

"You son of a bitch?"

Peng Sahyuk looked at Hyonwon Kang like he was pathetic.

"I stopped him because the main family would lose face if Peng Sagun lost. That moron couldn't even judge his opponent's strength. All flash, no substance."

"...Hmph. Well, I guess I have gotten pretty strong."

Hearing that faint praise, Hyonwon Kang felt the corners of his mouth twitch upwards. Peng Sahyuk saw it and snorted.

"Yeah. There's some real defiance in your eyes now."

Peng Sahyuk was actually surprised.

Back at the Azure Dragon Academy, when they'd agreed to fight again someday, he never dreamed Hyonwon Kang would get this strong. Things were different now...

"......"

"......"

Hyonwon Kang's eyes burned. Peng Sahyuk met his gaze unflinchingly. There was no one left to stop them this time.

"...Yeah, I can't wait either."

Hyonwon Kang broke the silence.

CRACK, CRACKLE.

He rolled his neck side to side, then drew the dao hanging at his waist.

"My body's itching for a fight. I can't wait until the Heavenly Martial Festival. Let's go."

Shoulders shaking with silent laughter, Peng Sahyuk drew his own dao like he'd been waiting for this exact moment.

"Maybe beating you up will actually be fun this time."
