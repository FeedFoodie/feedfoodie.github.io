The people of Wuhan were prideful, and it wasn't because Murim Alliance HQ and the Heavenly Martial Academy were located there, but because their city was the beating heart of the murim.

Wuhan was the capital city of Hubei, and for generations, the Nine Sects One Gang and their subsidiary sects had traveled through the region, fostering politics and trade. In addition, Hubei was bordered by Henan, home to the famous Shaolin Temple; Shaanxi, where the Mount Hua and Zhongnan Sects were located; Anhui, where the Namgung Clan was based; and Sichuan, the domain of the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, Emei, and Diancang Sects.

It was for this reason that the most important institutions in the murim had been built in Wuhan.

"T-There are so many people..." Namgung Mi muttered, looking around with wide, astonished eyes. 

Right from the city's entrance, the streets were a bustling scene, packed with people, vendors displaying unique merchandise, and the mouthwatering aroma of delicious food.

For a ten-year-old girl, it was overwhelming. Her eyes spun in all directions.

"This is your first time in Wuhan, right, Mimi?" Jaegal Soyeong asked, smiling as she gently stroked the girl's hair.

"Yes..."

"There is a lot to see and eat in this city, but if you space out too much, you might get lost," Jaegal Soyeong warned.

For her, Wuhan was more than just a city, it was home. After all, this was where the Jaegal Clan of the Five Great Clans, also known as the Clan of the Godly Diviners, was located.

Even though the Jaegal Clan was the weakest of the Five Great Clans in terms of martial strength, just the fact that the clan had consistently produced numerous exceptional figures in the more academic fields of mechanisms, formations, and sorcery was enough to solidify their dominance among the murim clans.

Jaegal Soyeong rarely boasted of her lineage, but she was in fact a treasured daughter of the Jaegal Clan.

"Mimi, can you stay close to your brother until we reach the Jaegal Estate? I'll take you on a proper tour of the city later," she added.

"Okay! Thank you, Unnie!" Smiling brightly, Namgung Mi jumped onto Namgung Su's back.

Startled by his sister's sudden action, Namgung Su furrowed his brows and sighed, "Miss Jaegal asked you to stay close to me, not hop on my back for a piggyback ride."

"But this way, I'll never lose you, Oppa!" Namgung Mi hugged Namgung Su's neck tightly, acting cute.

"......" Namgung Su shook his head in exasperation, but didn't put her down. Considering all she had been through, this much he could allow.

"By the way..."

Baek Suryong scanned the surroundings with narrowed eyes.

The women who met his gaze flinched and quickly looked away.

Frowning, Baek Suryong tilted his head and muttered, "People are staring a lot."

To the people of Wuhan, martial artists were nothing unusual.

It wasn't uncommon to see warriors missing limbs or wielding bizarre weapons in this city.

As the place with the highest concentration of martial artists in the world, it was home to all kinds of eccentrics.

That was why Baek Suryong found this situation even stranger.

Why were they attracting so much attention as soon as they entered the city?

"Something's off. I heard people in Wuhan don't care whether martial artists come or go..."

"Do you really think they're staring because we're martial artists?"

Jaegal Soyeong looked at him as if he were ridiculous.

The stir in Wuhan was growing.

Baek Suryong tilted his head again and asked, "If not that, then what? It doesn't seem like they recognize us..."

"Listen carefully."

The reason wasn't that they were unfamiliar martial artists.

"Look, look over there..."

"Oh my goodness..."

"Am I seeing things...?"

Most of the gasps came from women.

Their eyes widened as if they had discovered a legendary phoenix, their expressions filled with awe.

"I thought the Jade Qilin was the most handsome in the world, but compared to those two..."

"Who are they? Does anyone know their names?"

"Men that handsome must have aliases..."

"The one in white... his eyes, they're the legendary Golden Fiery Eyes[^1]... Haah..."

Baek Suryong and Namgung Su.

Walking side by side in their blue and white martial arts uniforms, they were like a painting come to life.

Whoosh, The wind blew, ruffling their martial arts uniforms and hair, while petals swirled around them in the breeze.

"Oh my..."

"Aah..."

Women covered their mouths in awe.

Seeing their reactions, Jaegal Soyeong turned to the two men and said, "They're not staring because you're martial artists. They're staring because you're handsome."

"...Shit," Baek Suryong swore. 

He had forgotten. His father was the legendary Handsome Prince.

*I should've worn a bamboo hat before entering the city...!*

As a city that saw a high number of martial artists pass through, Wuhan had strict security at its entrance, so things like bamboo hats, masks, and any kind of face coverings were forbidden, and the guards even touched each person's face to make sure they weren't wearing human skin masks.

If anyone was later found to have falsified their identity, they would face severe punishment from the Murim Alliance.

*If I wanted to, fooling them would be simple, but...*

Baek Suryong had committed no crime, and he didn't want to go that far. Thus, he had entered the city after revealing his identity, and fortunately, no one seemed to have recognized him.

Even in a city full of martial artists, law enforcement was ultimately handled by government soldiers, and there was no way they had a portrait of the Azure Dragon Hero.

Moreover, Baek Suryong's fame still lagged behind that of the other Ten Supremes. Aside from his striking appearance, there wasn't much to recognize. It was no surprise that no one had immediately identified him.

*However, that won't last long.*

Wuhan was crawling with informants from the Beggars' Gang and the Rogues' Guild, as well as spies from various sects. Within an hour, news of the Azure Dragon Hero Baek Suryong's arrival would spread.

*I need to finish my business and leave before it gets noisy.*

Unfortunately, Baek Suryong was so concerned about being recognized that he completely forgot about a certain other problem.

Some of the women's eyes were beginning to gleam with determination.

A few of them gathered the courage to approach.

"Um..."

"Excuse me..."

At that moment, Baek Suryong recalled the words Mae Geuklyom had said to him right before he left the Azure Dragon Academy.

> "Your face, taking after your father's, is a major calamity for murim. Be especially cautious around women."

If all those women surrounded him, he wouldn't be able to escape easily even with his martial arts skills, especially since he couldn't hurt the ordinary civilians among them.

Baek Suryong quickly leaned toward Namgung Su and whispered, "Namgung Su. Put some power into your eyes."

"...What?"

"Your eyes are terrifying. If you glare at them hard enough, they'll all run away."

"......" Namgung Su sighed heavily. *If it weren't for this damn debt of gratitude…*

CRACKLE!

A spark of lightning flashed in his golden eyes.

A side effect of his Body Reformation, Namgung Su's golden eyes exuded an aura that was both oppressive and mystical.

"Hiik!"

The women who had gathered their courage to approach stopped in shock. They felt like small prey standing before a predator. None of them dared to step closer and instead backed away.

"Did golden eyes have this kind of effect?"

Even Namgung Su himself was surprised.

Regardless, the once-crowded street instantly cleared. Grinning, Baek Suryong led the group forward.

"Let's go."

However, the crowd's attention did not disperse. If anything, even more eyes were drawn to them.

"Those people... they seem highly skilled in martial arts, too."

"The woman with them looks familiar. I feel like I've seen her before..."

"The child on his back also carries an air of nobility. She doesn't seem like an ordinary person."

"...Ah!" A man who had been staring at Jaegal Soyeong, tilting his head in thought, suddenly shouted, "I remember now! Isn't she the second daughter of the Jaegal Clan?"

"What? You mean the one who became a new instructor at the Azure Dragon Academy this year...?"

"Are you sure?"

"Wait. If that's the case..."

Once they recognized Jaegal Soyeong, it was only natural that they started guessing the identities of the two men accompanying her.

"The men beside her... could they be..."

"Wait, the *Azure Dragon* Academy…?"

"T-The Azure Dragon Hero?!"

Recently, the shocking rumors surrounding the Azure Dragon Hero and the Azure Dragon Academy had spread as far as Wuhan.

The crowd looked at Baek Suryong and Namgung Su with growing suspicion. As portraits of the Azure Dragon Hero hadn't reached Wuhan yet, they had no way of knowing which of the two men was him.

"Could it really be...?"

"If it is...?"

"Which one is it?"

As the overwhelming interest poured in, Namgung Su glanced at Baek Suryong and whispered, "Hey, it won't be long before our identities are revealed."

With all eyes on them, he avoided calling Baek Suryong by name to prevent unnecessary commotion.

"It can't be helped. We came here knowing this might happen."

"Are you skipping the Jaegal Estate and heading straight to Shaanxi?"

Baek Suryong nodded. "Yeah, I just have one quick stop to make before I leave."

Jaegal Soyeong and Namgung Mi immediately pouted in disappointment.

"Suryong-oppa, it would be nice if you stopped by my home, too."

"Can't you stay with us a little longer...?"

"This is the best place for us to part ways. I don't want to trouble the Jaegal Clan, and I'm on a tight schedule." Baek Suryong shook his head with a smile. The break wasn't long, and if he wanted to accomplish everything he had planned, he had to use his time efficiently.

After a brief moment of thought, Jaegal Soyeong nodded in agreement. "That's true. If my father found out you were here, he'd definitely hold you hostage for days and ask you all sorts of uncomfortable questions..."

"He would?"

"Well, my father is... quite the erm… *doting parent*..." Jaegal Soyeong mumbled.

However, before she could explain what she meant, a thunderous voice rang out, "SOYEONGGGGGGG!"

Jaegal Soyeong instantly covered her face with both hands, utterly embarrassed. "D-Dad..."

"My daughter! My daughter has come home! Wahahaha!"

A middle-aged man was racing toward them using movement arts, laughing heartily all the way. He was Jaegal Sun, the Patriarch of the Jaegal Clan, and a renowned master of lightning arts who was known as the Thunder Dragon Swordsman.

He was also the person Namgung Su had been advised to seek guidance from.

*Not that he needs his advice anymore.*

*…Not that I need his advice anymore.*

Both Baek Suryong and Namgung Su had the same thought, but still, it wouldn't be proper to leave without at least greeting the Jaegal Patriarch.

Baek Suryong glanced at his companions. "Looks like we'll part ways here. I was going to say goodbye at the Jaegal Estate's front doors, but..."

A sly grin spread across Baek Suryong's face. A brilliant idea had just struck him. He had been contemplating the best way to announce this, and now he had the perfect opportunity.

He suddenly turned around and clasped his hands toward Namgung Su in a formal martial greeting.

Namgung Su glared at him warily. "What are you planning this time...?"

Infusing his voice with qi, Baek Suryong announced loudly, "Then, I shall see you when school reopens, MASTER LIGHTNING DRAGON HERO!"

"You…! I told you that I hate…!"

Unfortunately, it was too late. Hundreds of people had already heard Baek Suryong refer to Namgung Su as the Lightning Dragon Hero (雷龍神劍).

Coincidentally, the alias of the approaching Jaegal Patriarch was the Thunder Dragon Swordsman (雷龍劍), but Baek Suryong had just declared that title not for Jaegal Sun, but for Namgung Su.

"The Lightning Dragon Hero?"

"What does that mean?"

Confusion spread across the crowd.

Baek Suryong grinned wickedly, then drove in the final nail in the coffin, "I will never forget the sight of your sword cutting down the Thunder Demon in a single strike, nor how even that villain paid his respects to your blade. Though he was an evil man, his final words bestowed upon you the name Lightning Dragon Hero. No martial artist is more deserving of that title than you!"

Namgung Su grit his teeth and muttered under his breath, "I'm going to *kill* you..."

"Hahaha! Well then, I shall take my leave."

WHOOSH!

Having said everything he wanted, Baek Suryong activated his movement arts and ran without looking back. His form stretched like an illusion as he vanished in an instant.

Moments later, Jaegal Sun arrived at the scene, letting out an admiring whistle. "Hoo! That was some impressive movement arts."

"...Greetings, Jaegal Patriarch."

"It's been a long time since I saw you as a child. By the way, who was that young man who just left?"

*My irritating, conniving benefactor.*

...Namgung Su couldn't say that, so he simply answered, "That was the Azure Dragon Hero, Baek Suryong."

"Oh? I suspected as much!"

The Jaegal Patriarch and the surrounding crowd erupted in astonished exclamations.

*So that's the Azure Dragon Hero!*

The rumored rising star and newest member of the Ten Supremes had just been standing before them.

"...He had urgent business, so he left first," Namgung Su added through gritted teeth. Though his blood boiled with frustration, he had no choice but to cover for Baek Suryong's sudden departure.

"Hoo. A shame, but—what was that about the Lightning Dragon Hero? And that he killed the Thunder Demon? Could it be... you?"

"That is..."

Murmurs spread through the crowd.

"The Lightning Dragon Hero?"

"He said Lightning Dragon Hero."

"And that he killed the Thunder Demon?"

*…DAMN YOU, BAEK SURYONGGGGGGG!* 

Namgung Su cursed his and his family's benefactor repeatedly as he spent the rest of the day sweating through endless explanations about the Lightning Dragon Hero and the Thunder Demon.

Also, from that day forward, his new title was the Lightning Dragon Hero.

[^1]: Golden Fiery Eyes (火眼金睛): This is the term used to describe the Monkey King Sun Wukong's golden eyes in Journey to the West, a classic Chinese fantasy novel.
