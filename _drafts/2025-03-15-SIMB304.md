Wuhan—its people carried a certain pride.

*This is the center of murim!*

It wasn't just because the Murim Alliance headquarters and the Heavenly Martial Academy were located here.

Henan, home to the Shaolin Temple; Shaanxi, where the Mount Hua Sect and the Zhongnan Sect resided; Anhui, where the Namgung Clan was based; and Sichuan, home to the Sichuan Tang Clan, Qingcheng, Emei, and the Diancang Sect—all of these regions bordered Hubei.

For generations, the Nine Sects One Gang and their subsidiary sects had traveled through Hubei, fostering interaction and exchange.

Wuhan was the central city of Hubei. That was why the Murim Alliance headquarters and the Heavenly Martial Academy had been built here.

"T-There are so many people..."

Namgung Mi looked around with wide, astonished eyes.

From the city's entrance, the streets were packed with people, filled with peculiar goods sold by street vendors, merchants loudly calling out to customers, and the enticing aromas of delicious food.

For a ten-year-old girl, it was overwhelming. Her eyes spun in all directions.

"This is your first time in Wuhan, right, Mi?"

"Yes..."

Jaegal Soyeong smiled and gently stroked the girl's hair.

"Wuhan has a lot to see and eat, but if you space out too much, you might lose track of us, so be careful."

For Jaegal Soyeong, this was home.

Hubei Wuhan—the home of the Jaegal Clan.

One of the Five Great Clans, renowned as the Clan of the Godly Diviners (神機諸葛), it had produced exceptional figures across various fields.

Though it was often considered the weakest of the Five Great Clans in terms of martial strength, in the fields of mechanisms, formations, and sorcery, it could proudly claim to be the world's strongest.

Jaegal Soyeong rarely displayed any signs of her lineage, but she was the treasured daughter of the Jaegal Clan.

"So until we reach the Jaegal Clan, stay close to Oppa. I'll take you on a proper tour of the city later."

"Okay! Thank you, Unnie!"

Smiling brightly, Namgung Mi suddenly jumped onto Namgung Su's back.

Startled by his sister's sudden action, Namgung Su furrowed his brows and sighed.

"...I don't think you need to climb on my back."

"This way, I'll never lose you, Oppa!"

"......"

Namgung Mi hugged his neck tightly, acting adorable.

Namgung Su simply shook his head in exasperation but didn't tell her to get down. Considering all she had been through, this much he could allow.

"By the way..."

Baek Suryong scanned the surroundings with narrowed eyes.

The women who met his gaze flinched and quickly looked away.

Frowning, Baek Suryong tilted his head and muttered, "People are staring a lot."

To the people of Wuhan, martial artists were nothing unusual.

It wasn't uncommon to see warriors missing limbs or wielding bizarre weapons in this city.

As the place with the highest concentration of martial artists in the world, it was home to all kinds of eccentrics.

That was why Baek Suryong found this situation even stranger.

Why were they attracting so much attention as soon as they entered the city?

"Something's off. I heard people in Wuhan don't care whether martial artists come or go..."

"Do you really think they're staring because we're martial artists?"

Jaegal Soyeong looked at him as if he were ridiculous.

The stir in Wuhan was growing.

Baek Suryong tilted his head again and asked, "If not that, then what? It doesn't seem like they recognize us..."

"Listen carefully."

The reason wasn't that they were unfamiliar martial artists.

"Look, look over there..."

"Oh my goodness..."

"Am I seeing things...?"

Most of the gasps came from women.

Their eyes widened as if they had discovered a legendary phoenix, their expressions filled with awe.

"I thought the Jade Qilin was the most handsome in the world, but compared to those two..."

"Who are they? Does anyone know their names?"

"Men that handsome must have aliases..."

"The one in white... his eyes, they're the legendary Golden Fiery Eyes (火眼金睛)... Haah..."

Baek Suryong and Namgung Su.

Walking side by side in their blue and white martial arts uniforms, they were like a painting come to life.

Whoosh, The wind blew, ruffling their martial arts uniforms and hair, while petals swirled around them in the breeze.

"Oh my..."

"Aah..."

Women covered their mouths in awe.

Seeing their reactions, Jaegal Soyeong turned to the two men and said, "They're not staring because you're martial artists. They're staring because you're handsome."

"...Come on."

... He had forgotten.

His father was the legendary Handsome Prince.

*I should've worn a bamboo hat before coming in...*

As a city that saw a high number of martial artists pass through, Wuhan had strict security at its entrance.

Bamboo hats, masks, and any kind of face coverings were forbidden.

Even facial disguises were carefully checked by guards, who touched each person's face to verify their identity.

If anyone was later found to have falsified their identity, they would face severe punishment from the Murim Alliance.

*If I wanted to, fooling them would be simple...*, but Baek Suryong had committed no crime, and he didn't want to go that far.

So, he entered the city after revealing his identity.

And in doing so, he realized something.

*No one seems to know I'm here yet.*

Even in a city full of martial artists, law enforcement was ultimately handled by government soldiers.

There was no way they had a wanted poster of the Azure Dragon Hero.

Moreover, Baek Suryong's fame still lagged behind that of the other Ten Supremes.

Aside from his striking appearance, there wasn't much to recognize. It was no surprise that no one had immediately identified him.

*But that won't last long.*

Wuhan was home to Beggars' Union informants, the Rogues' Guild, and spies from various sects.

Within an hour, news of the Azure Dragon Hero Baek Suryong's arrival would spread.

*I need to finish my business and leave before it gets noisy.*, but some of the women's eyes were beginning to gleam with determination.

A few of them gathered the courage to approach.

"Um..."

"Excuse me..."

At that moment, Baek Suryong recalled something Mae Geuklyom had told him before he left the Azure Dragon Academy.

> "Your face, taking after your father's, is a major calamity for murim. Be especially cautious around women."

If they surrounded him, even his peak martial arts skills wouldn't let him escape easily.

Because among them, there were surely women who weren't martial artists at all.

Baek Suryong quickly leaned toward Namgung Su and whispered, "Namgung Su. Put some power into your eyes."

"...What?"

"Your eyes are terrifying. If you glare at them hard enough, they'll run away."

"......"

Sighing, Namgung Su narrowed his eyes.

The damn debt of gratitude—he just couldn't refuse a request.

CRACKLE!

A spark of lightning flashed in his golden eyes.

A side effect of his Body Reformation, Namgung Su's golden eyes exuded an aura that was both oppressive and mystical.

"Could it really be...?"

"If it is...?"

"It won't be long before our identities are revealed."

"Are we skipping the Jaegal Clan and heading straight to Shaanxi?"

Baek Suryong nodded.

"Yeah. I just have one quick stop before we leave."

"Oppa, it would be nice if you stopped by home, too."

"This is the best place to separate. I don't want to trouble the Jaegal Clan, and we're on a tight schedule if we want to stay on track."

Baek Suryong shook his head with a smile. The break wasn't long, and if he wanted to accomplish everything he had planned, he had to use his time efficiently.

After a brief moment of thought, Jaegal Soyeong nodded in agreement.

"That's true. If my father found out you were here, he'd definitely hold you hostage for days. And he'd ask you all sorts of uncomfortable questions..."

"He would?"

"Well, my father is... quite the doting parent..."

Jaegal Soyeong hadn't even finished her sentence when, "Soyeong—!"

A thunderous voice rang out. Jaegal Soyeong instantly covered her face with both hands, utterly embarrassed.

"Dad..."

"My daughter! My daughter has come home! Hahahaha!"

A middle-aged man raced toward them, using movement arts with a hearty laugh.

This was Jaegal Sun, the head of the Jaegal Clan.

Alias—the Thunder Dragon Swordsman.

He was also the renowned master of thunder-based martial arts whom Namgung Su had been advised to seek guidance from.

*Not that I need his advice anymore.*

Both Baek Suryong and Namgung Su had the same thought.

Still, it wouldn't be proper to leave without at least greeting him.

Baek Suryong glanced at his companions and said, "Looks like we'll part ways here. I was going to say goodbye at the Jaegal Clan's gate, but..."

At that moment, a brilliant idea struck him.

He had been contemplating the best way to announce this, and now he had the perfect opportunity.

A sly grin spread across Baek Suryong's face as he suddenly turned and clasped his hands toward Namgung Su in a formal martial greeting.

Namgung Su looked at him warily.

"You... What are you planning this time...?"

Then, with inner arts imbued in his voice, Baek Suryong's words rang loudly through the air.

"I shall see you after the break, Lightning Dragon Hero Master!"

"You—! I told you I hate that na—!"

It was too late.

Hundreds of people had already heard him.

The Lightning Dragon Hero (雷龍神劍).

Coincidentally, the alias of the approaching Jaegal Patriarch was the Thunder Dragon Swordsman (雷龍劍), but Baek Suryong had just declared that title not for Jaegal Sun, but for Namgung Su.

"The Lightning Dragon Hero?"

"What does that mean?"

Confusion spread across the crowd.

And then, to make absolutely sure Namgung Su couldn't escape from this, Baek Suryong drove in the final nail.

"I will never forget the sight of your sword cutting down the Thunder Demon in a single strike, nor how even that villain paid his respects to your blade. Though he was an evil man, his final words bestowed upon you the name Lightning Dragon Hero. No martial artist is more deserving of that title than you!"

Namgung Su clenched his teeth and muttered under his breath.

"I will kill him..."

"Hahaha! Well then, I shall take my leave."

Having said everything he wanted, Baek Suryong activated his movement arts without looking back.

Whoosh!

His form stretched like an illusion as he vanished in an instant.

Moments later, Jaegal Sun arrived at the scene, letting out an admiring whistle.

"Hoo! That was some impressive movement arts."

"...Greetings, Jaegal Patriarch."

"It's been a long time since I saw you as a child. By the way, who was that young man who just left?"

*My damned, conniving benefactor.*

... Namgung Su couldn't say that, so he simply answered truthfully.

"...That was the Azure Dragon Hero, Baek Suryong."

"Hoo! I suspected as much!"

Both the Jaegal Patriarch and the surrounding crowd erupted in astonished exclamations.

*The Azure Dragon Hero!*

The rumored rising star of the Ten Supremes had just been standing before them.

Though his blood boiled with frustration, Namgung Su still had to cover for Baek Suryong's sudden departure.

"...He had urgent business, so he left first."

"Hoo. A shame, but—what was that about the Lightning Dragon Hero? And that he killed the Thunder Demon? Could it be... you?"

"That is..."

Murmurs spread through the crowd.

"The Lightning Dragon Hero?"

"He said Lightning Dragon Hero."

"And that he killed the Thunder Demon?"

*Baek Suryong, you bastard!* Namgung Su cursed his family's benefactor repeatedly as he spent the rest of the day sweating through endless explanations about the Lightning Dragon Hero and the Thunder Demon.

And from that day forward, his new title became the Lightning Dragon Hero.
