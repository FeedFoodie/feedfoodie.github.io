Crimson Tiger leaned against a secluded alleyway. His eyes, hidden in the shadow of the wall, were serious and somber.

The situation had changed. Instead of killing Six, he now had to kidnap her and escape. However, when he returned to the Pear Blossom Inn, he discovered that she had already left for the Ironblood Garrison.

He lifted his head and looked wistfully at the night sky, recalling his daughter's bright smile.

He missed her. He missed her so much that his eyes ached, his arms ached, his legs ached, and his heart ached. There were times when he longed to throw everything away and run to her side, but he always suppressed his desires and held himself back. He couldn't risk even the slightest chance of endangering her.

Crimson Tiger gritted his teeth. Before thinking about the future, he had to complete his mission, which had grown even more challenging.

*Can I even persuade Six? Will she accept the fact that her father wants to kill her, and that her father's greatest rival wants to kidnap her? No, she would never accept it.*

There were two ways he could achieve his goal. First, he could deceive her, and second, he could knock her out and carry her off.

Before attempting that seemingly impossible task, however, he had one more decision to make.

Crimson Tiger sighed as he looked at the bookstore across the street.

*There's no way I can do this without Yeon's knowledge, but do I trust her enough to tell her the truth? How much should I tell her?*

He was not supposed to trust Yeon, and he had indeed spent the past two years keeping her at arm's length.

However, she had never betrayed him nor revealed any of his secrets even to her own superiors. It was enough to make him agonize over his decision.

After much deliberation, Crimson Tiger made up his mind. He crossed the street and entered the bookstore.

When Yeon saw Crimson Tiger, she immediately jumped to her feet, startled by his sudden visit.

"What brings you here?" she asked.

Crimson Tiger told her about Smiley.

"You killed Smiley of the Ten Evils? And he knew your identity?" Yeon's face paled. If what Crimson Tiger said was true, then it meant that the Association's information had leaked to the Coalition. "We must cancel the mission and withdraw immediately! But even that won't be easy... The enemy must have already set a trap preventing our escape..."

"……" Crimson Tiger remained silent.

"Crimson Tiger?" Yeon tensed. This was unusual behavior for him. "Do you… have something to say to me?"

Crimson Tiger hesitated for a moment, then whispered, "You go back first."

"What?"

"There's something I still need to do."

"What's going on?" Yeon asked sternly, sensing that something bigger than an information leak and the Ten Evils' interference was at play.

"......"

"I won't let you do this if you don't give me a valid reason. You know that, don't you?"

"I do, but I can't tell you."

"Why not?"

"…Because you'll get caught up in it."

"I'm already in enough danger! From the moment you walked through that door and started spouting nonsense, I was already entangled in whatever it is you refuse to talk about!" Yeon shouted, glaring angrily at Crimson Tiger. "Well? Are you still going to hide it from me? Fine then. I'll do as you say, but I'll also report the situation exactly as it is."

"Do as you will."

Yeon slammed the door as she stormed out.

Crimson Tiger let out a small sigh. The truth was, he desperately needed Yeon. He wasn't confident he could complete this mission without her help, but at the same time, he did not want to drag her into this quagmire. 

It would be difficult, but he would take Baek Soeun and escape this place on his own.

Unfortunately, he had no way of knowing what consequences her report might bring either. It wouldn't be anything good, but that was a burden he had to bear.

Just as he was about to leave, Crimson Tiger abruptly stopped, sensing that Yeon was standing right outside the door.

The truth was, Yeon liked Crimson Tiger, and not only because he had saved her life. She respected him as a martial artist, enjoyed the camaraderie they had built through countless dangerous missions, and… she was attracted to him as a man.

Rationality told her that she should walk away, but her feet wouldn't budge. Her feelings for Crimson Tiger were too complicated.

*At this rate, I'm going to get myself killed.*

Even that thought wasn't enough to make her leave him be.

Having made up her mind, she turned around.

"Can you really handle this?" Crimson Tiger asked, sensing her determination.

"Maybe."

"…Come in."

The two sat facing each other as Crimson Tiger explained to Yeon the additional mission related to Baek Soeun.

"Killing Baek Soeun is the real mission?" Yeon asked, her eyes widening in realization. *So this is why he asked me to locate the document archive!*

"Yes, and I've already found out who she really is."

"Ah!"

"The target is Seventh Six, a woman."

"Hmm, if the Ten Evils are onto you, then we have to kill her by tonight."

Crimson Tiger shook his head. "No, we're not going to kill her. We're going to kidnap her."

"What? Why?" Yeon exclaimed, dumbstruck.

After a moment, however, she nodded. Now she understood why Crimson Tiger had told her to go back first. He was planning to disobey orders, and not just any order, but a special directive even she was not privy to.

Crimson Tiger hesitated. "Once you hear this, there's no turning back."

Yeon shrugged. "If you haven't noticed, I've already burned that bridge."

Noticing the sincerity in her eyes, Crimson Tiger nodded and explained, "The First Young Lord was the one who gave the order to kill her, and the Third Young Lady was the one who gave the new order to capture her alive."

"Ah!" Yeon understood the situation in an instant, but then she furrowed her brows. "Wait. Why are you following the Third Young Lady's orders instead of the First Young Lord's?"

"……" Crimson Tiger remained silent.

Yeon scowled. "I'm sorry, but I have to know. I need a reason to risk my life."

"It's because... I have a daughter too," Crimson Tiger whispered in a barely audible voice.

"...!"

That one sentence was enough for Yeon to understand everything.

It explained why he had to disobey. Why he had fought so desperately to survive until now. Where his occasional moments of longing had been directed. How difficult it must have been for him to say this. How much he had to trust her to even bring it up.

For a long time, Yeon said nothing as she stared blankly at the table.

Crimson Tiger waited without a word.

Finally, Yeon made up her mind, and instead of answering him, she pulled down her mask.

Crimson Tiger's jaw dropped in shock. He had never seen Yeon's face before.

Her clear, fierce eyes, radiating determination, perfectly complemented her beautiful features.

Smiling, Yeon said sincerely, "Consider that child... your daughter... as my niece now."

Two hours later, Crimson Tiger entered the Ironblood Garrison. He had been on high alert ever since stepping into the compound, but he noticed nothing unusual despite the Coalition already knowing about him.

Fortunately, at least for now, it seemed that their primary objective was not to kill him but to determine his motive for infiltrating.

By midnight, however, word of Soh Bung's disappearance would have spread. He had to grab Six and leave this place before then.

"Didn't you say you were going to the outhouse? Why did you take so long?" Six asked jokingly.

"I stepped out for a bit."

"Where?"

"Uh, for a walk..."

"Ah, I know. You went to the brothel, didn't you?"

"I did not!" Crimson Tiger denied, looking flustered.

The other martial artists around them snickered, while Crimson Tiger pretended to glare at Six in frustration.

Conscious of the surrounding eyes, she was acting completely differently than when they were alone, and he understood her. In this place crawling with men, she had no choice but to wear a mask.

Crimson Tiger sat down in his usual spot. It was already late, so he couldn't leave the barracks without permission.

Outside, Yeon was preparing to carry out the first step of their mission, though whether it would succeed remained uncertain. In the worst case scenario, he would have to eliminate all Seventh Squad members, knock Six unconscious, and escape from the Evil Coalition with her on his back.

Time passed, and Six laughed as she chatted with her comrades.

Crimson Tiger leaned against the wall, watching her anxiously.

The sweet sound of Six's giggles mingled with his memories of *that day*.

"AHEM! ATTENTION!" Seventh One shouted.

Crimson Tiger snapped back to reality as Seventh One entered the barracks and summoned the entire squad. Seeing Seventh One's stern face, the previously relaxed men immediately straightened their postures.

"We have a mission," Seventh One announced. "And we're departing immediately!"

Everyone quickly changed their clothes and grabbed their weapons.

"What's the mission?"

"We found the brats who escaped last time," One said excitedly.

Six instantly stiffened, but she quickly lowered her head to hide her dismay. If the children were caught now, they would be tortured and murdered in the most gruesome way to avenge the fallen Three and Four.

Crimson Tiger, on the other hand, smiled faintly. This was the false information that Yeon had leaked to lure the Seventh Squad out. He had predicted that if a mission related to the Hong Family Estate came up now, it would almost certainly be assigned to the Seventh Squad.

Anyway, the plan was to eliminate the Seventh Squad outside, then grab Six and escape. If she did not comply, he would kidnap her by force.

Fifteen minutes later, the Seventh Squad departed from Evil Coalition Headquarters. Just as the seven horses began picking up speed, however, First Three, whom Crimson Tiger had met in the inner compound the other day, ran toward them.

"Stop!" he yelled.

"What is it?" Seventh One tensed, worried that the mission had been canceled. This mission was of utmost importance to him; it was not only about restoring his honor and pride but also about securing his future prospects.

Fortunately, the mission had not been revoked. First Three was here for another reason entirely.

Turning toward Six, First Three called out, "Seventh Six."

"Yes, sir?"

"The Commander has summoned you. Follow me."

"Understood."

With a slightly tense expression, she turned her horse around.

As the two rode back, Seventh One shouted, "We depart!"

As the six horses belonging to the remaining Seventh Squad members galloped into the darkness, Crimson Tiger, riding at the rear, glanced back.

Far in the distance, he could see Six heading back to the compound with First Three.

He gritted his teeth, cursing inwardly.

*Damn it! Damn it all!*

He had to make a quick decision. The farther they got from the compound, the longer it would take to return. If he launched a surprise attack, he was confident he could take them all down, but the problem was getting back.

Especially at night, the Evil Coalition's security was incomparably stricter than during the day. Breaking through was possible, but sneaking back in was nearly impossible.

Also, if they were ambushed and the new recruit returned alive alone, he would naturally be the first to be suspected. He had to return with someone, no matter what.

Crimson Tiger did not hesitate for long. Pulling out three throwing knives, he steadied himself, timed his breath with his horse's movements, then threw them.

SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH!

"Ugh!"

Two missed, but one embedded itself in Eight's back, while the other knives stuck in the ground.

That was fine. Crimson Tiger had deliberately ensured that those two missed. Leaping off his horse, he hurled himself into the forest beside the road and shouted, "Ambush!"

The other five squad members simultaneously dismounted and followed him into the forest.

Silence fell for a moment as Eight, collapsed on the ground, groaned and twitched.

"Where did the hidden weapons come from?" One asked.

Crimson Tiger pointed into the darkness and whispered, "That way!"

The area where they stood was brightly lit by moonlight, yet the direction Crimson Tiger pointed to lay shrouded in deep tree shadows and darkness.

One stared into the darkness for a moment before turning to Crimson Tiger. "You have throwing knives, don't you?"

"Yes," Crimson Tiger replied, rolling up his sleeve. The throwing knives provided by the Ironblood Nines were lined up neatly, with not a single one missing.

He pulled out a few and handed them to One.

For a brief moment, One doubted him. The throwing knife had flown in from behind, yet Crimson Tiger, stationed at the very back, was completely unharmed. That was suspicious.

Just in case, he checked Crimson Tiger's throwing knives, but since the ones he had thrown came not from his forearm sheath but from those hidden in his chest guard, none were missing.

Even so, One's suspicion didn't vanish entirely. For a moment, he considered the possibility that Crimson Tiger had used a different type of throwing knife, when suddenly, three throwing knives sliced through the darkness.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

One and Two swung their swords, deflecting them.

"Over there!" Two yelled, moving to charge toward the exact same location Crimson Tiger had indicated earlier.

However, One stopped him, saying, "Hold it! They're trying to lure us in."

Since he couldn't detect any presences from the direction of the throwing knives, that could only mean that a highly skilled expert was lying in wait.

And he was right. 

This time, however, the culprit who had thrown the throwing knife was Yeon. 

Crimson Tiger had launched his ambush knowing he would be suspected, but he trusted that Yeon would support him perfectly.

Just then, Eight, who had collapsed on the ground, groaned again. "Ugh…"

"They're using Eight as bait to lure us in and kill us," Two said urgently. "But if we leave him like that, he'll die of blood loss."

One shook his head. "We have to take out the enemy first!"

"No, we have to save Eight!" Crimson Tiger shouted, springing into action before anyone could intervene.

"Stop! Don't!" One bellowed, but he was too late.

Crimson Tiger was already diving toward the fallen Eight.

SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH!

As five throwing knives flew toward him, Crimson Tiger twisted his body and swung his sword.

CLANG, CLANG, CLANG!

He deflected three, but one grazed his shoulder while another missed entirely. It was a wound he had taken on purpose, because someone at Seven's level would never have been able to deflect all of the throwing knives.

SPLURT!

Blood sprayed from his shoulder, but Crimson Tiger still threw himself over Eight as if to shield him.

SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH, SWOOSH!

Throwing knives flew from One and the other members of the Seventh Squad, and the enemy's attacks ceased.

Seizing that opportunity, Crimson Tiger carried Eight back to their group.

"You idiot! Are you trying to get yourself killed?" One scowled. He was furious at Crimson Tiger for disobeying his orders, but he no longer harbored any suspicion towards him.

Crimson Tiger quickly stripped Eight's clothing and assessed the wound. "Luckily, his life isn't in danger, but the wound is too deep. He needs immediate treatment back at the base."

Crimson Tiger sealed Eight's acupoints to stem the bleeding and applied wound healing medicine, but the unconscious Eight's breathing was sporadic, as if he were on the verge of death.

One let out a heavy sigh. Two people had already died in their previous mission. If they lost another member this time, it would cast a dark shadow over his future.

Logically, they should return immediately, since the details of the mission had been leaked and a trap might be waiting for them. However, if the Seventh Squad failed another mission even after leaving behind an injured comrade, they would be completely disgraced.

One hesitated, torn between reason, which urged him to turn back, and his emotions, which demanded he salvage the situation.

Suddenly, Crimson Tiger whispered, "They're desperately trying to stop us from going there. That means they know we've obtained information about the children. In other words, it proves that the intel is accurate."

One nodded. Crimson Tiger's logic had a few holes, but burdened by his previous failure, he couldn't think clearly. Besides, he was confident that, trap or no trap, they could eliminate anyone who stood in their way.

"Please draw their attention while I take Eight back. After that, go and complete the mission without us," Crimson Tiger quickly added.

"Can you manage alone?"

"Don't worry, Headquarters isn't far from here. I'll be as careful as possible."

Two nodded. "That's a good idea. We can't stay stuck here any longer."

Sighing, One finally gave in and also nodded. "Alright, I'm leaving Eight in your hands. Make sure he survives."

"Yes, sir!" Crimson Tiger said firmly, hoisting Eight onto his back and tearing some fabric from the man's clothes to secure him tightly.

At his signal, the other four people simultaneously threw throwing knives into the darkness and as if in retaliation, throwing knives also flew out from the darkness.

 SWOOSH! SWOOSH! SWOOSH! SWOOSH!

Using that distraction, Crimson Tiger bolted in the opposite direction with Eight on his back.

Several minutes later, however, he came to a halt and looked at a seemingly empty spot beside the road.

Yeon emerged from hiding, having escaped after attacking the Seventh Squad with throwing knives. Since she was a master of movement arts, it was easy for her to catch up to him.

"What happened to the target?" she asked.

Crimson Tiger sighed. "…She was summoned by the Commander again."

"At this hour? Why?"

Crimson Tiger shook his head. "I don't know."

"What do we do now? There's not much time left until midnight."

"We're going after her," Crimson Tiger whispered softly.

Yeon's eyes gleamed at his use of the word "we."

Eight slung over his back, Crimson Tiger safely passed through the entrance of the Evil Coalition.

Eight's life was in critical condition, and the martial artists at the gate recognized Crimson Tiger's face.

After passing through the entrance, Crimson Tiger sprinted toward the Ironblood Garrison.

The buildings of the Ironblood Garrison came into view in the distance.

As he rounded the flower garden, Bang Soso rushed over with a shocked expression. "What on earth happened?" she asked.

"We were ambushed."

"By whom?"

At that moment—

SLAAASH, THUD!

Yeon heard the sound of air and flesh being torn apart.

A throwing knife held in Bang Soso's hand had embedded itself in Crimson Tiger's chest. The ambush was so fast that Crimson Tiger couldn't avoid it.

Had he not twisted his body, the blade would have pierced his heart.

BOOM!

Crimson Tiger's fist and Bang Soso's palm strike collided.

Both staggered backward.

Eight, who had been slung over his back, rolled to the ground.

The throwing knife remained lodged in Crimson Tiger's chest.

CLINK!

A soft sword unraveled from her waist. Her aura had completely changed.

She was composed. Crimson Tiger's wound was fatal. All she had to do was stall for time, and he would die.

Bang Soso spoke coldly.

"Crimson Tiger!"

Crimson Tiger's expression twisted.

"You...?"

"Floral Viper (花蛇客)!"

She was Floral Viper, one of the Ten Evils. An assassin specialized in stealth kills. Smiley wasn't the only one deployed for this mission. It had been both Floral Viper and Smiley.

Crimson Tiger stepped back to avoid her approach.

He tripped over Eight's collapsed body and fell backward. Crimson Tiger's condition looked dire.

With great effort, Crimson Tiger stood up.

She walked forward slowly, unhurried.

"What happened to Smiley? I haven't seen him for a few hours."

Crimson Tiger remained silent.

"So, he's dead."

She clicked her tongue and shook her head.

"I warned him not to act alone."

She showed no sorrow over her comrade's death. If anything, she wore a faint smile.

"Thanks for dying by my hand. Everyone's been dying to kill you."

CLING!

Her soft sword danced menacingly in the air.

"I was ordered to find out why you infiltrated, but with Smiley dead, that order is meaningless."

Still, she added a final remark, as if she had some lingering curiosity.

"Since you're going to die anyway, care to tell me?"

Instead of answering, Crimson Tiger drew his sword.

"Oh? With that body? Well, I suppose you need to uphold your name, even in death."

Her soft sword curved as it lashed toward Crimson Tiger's blade.

CLANG!

With a single strike, Crimson Tiger's sword was sent flying.

He barely had the strength to hold his weapon.

"Don't worry! I'll make sure they say you fought spectacularly to the very end."

She approached confidently and tightened her grip, preparing for the final strike, when a sudden gust of wind came from an unexpected direction.

The next thing she knew, a chilling sensation spread from her rear ankle.

"Aaagh!" she screamed, realizing that someone had slashed her ankle.

It was Yeon, who had been lying on the ground disguised as Eight. With her chest bound, and wearing Eight's blood-stained garments and mask, her presence had gone completely unnoticed.

"Die!" Floral Viper screeched, swinging her flexible sword at Yeon.

CLING!

Yeon rolled across the ground to evade it, while Crimson Tiger tackled Floral Viper. As she fell, she flung her soft sword.

CLING!

The sword curved dramatically, flying toward Crimson Tiger's neck. Just before it struck, however, Crimson Tiger yanked out the throwing knife lodged in his chest and slashed her throat.

Floral Viper died instantly, collapsing to the ground. At the same time, her flying soft sword barely missed Crimson Tiger's neck, slicing through the air.

SPLAT, SPLAT, SPLAT!

Blood gushed from Crimson Tiger's chest where he had pulled the throwing knife out.

Yeon rushed over, tore open his blood-soaked tunic, and removed his now bloodied leather chest guard. Sealing his acupoints to stop the bleeding, she applied medicine to the wound. 

Although the wound was serious, fortunately, the thick leather guard had prevented a fatal injury.

"Are you okay?" she asked worriedly.

Crimson Tiger, his face pale, nodded. Losing his sword had been a deliberate move to make her drop her guard.

There was no way he could have beaten her in a prolonged fight with a throwing knife still stuck in his chest. The longer the fight dragged on, the worse his situation would become. That was why he had maneuvered himself closer to where Yeon lay. It was all to land a single fatal strike.

Floral Viper's corpse lay still, her face unchanged. Judging by her qi, she had to be at least thirty years old. She must have looked far younger than her actual age.

Yeon wrapped Crimson Tiger's wound with cloth.

"The wound is too deep. This mission is impossible."

Crimson Tiger silently shook his head.

"This is nothing."

With a groan, Crimson Tiger forced himself to his feet. Pain tore through his chest with every movement, but it wasn't enough to stop him. Pain could be endured. What he couldn't endure was what would happen if he failed his mission.

"You can't save her in this condition. Besides, she's with the Ironblood Commander, isn't she?"

Crimson Tiger's gaze locked onto the distant Ironblood Garrison.

At the top of the darkened building, only the Ironblood Commander's room remained brightly lit.

"Because she's with him... there's a way to make this work."

Cold, strong, undefeated.

Cheol Mugang drank his liquor and suddenly found himself wanting to see her.

The thought that he shouldn't do this lost to the thought that, just this once, it would be fine.

Her beauty dazzled him.

She felt even more flustered because she didn't understand Cheol Mugang's feelings toward her.

Cheol Mugang was intoxicated. He was drunk on alcohol, and he was drunk on his emotions.

Cheol Mugang's gaze was unfocused as he stared at her. He wanted to have her, even if it meant throwing everything away. He felt he could endure the scorn of others saying the Ironblood Commander had laid hands on his subordinate.

Cheol Mugang had made up his mind to take her tonight.

A thought crossed his mind, that maybe she wanted this, too. If it was him, he could guarantee her success. In two or three years, he could even bring her into the First Squad.

To justify himself, the thing he needed most was alcohol.

Cheol Mugang emptied another cup.

Watching him, Six felt uneasy. Cheol Mugang's heat, the aura he exuded, was palpable. One moment, she told herself there's no way, but that thought was quickly replaced by another what if? and then another no, it can't be.

"Drink it all in one go."

Just as she was pulling an awkward expression, a voice came from outside.

"Sixth Five requests an audience."

Cheol Mugang's face twisted in irritation.

"He says it's an urgent matter."

Cheol Mugang remained silent.

Then, First One's voice followed.

"Understood. I will send him back."

"Wait!"

"Yes?"

"Is it that boy?"

"Yes. The child of the Bloody Asura Commander."

If it weren't for that, he would have rejected the request outright.

"Let him in."

"Understood."

"And you all are dismissed for the night as well."

A brief silence followed.

"Understood."

One realized this was because of Six. He had always been Cheol Mugang's closest bodyguard.

He had suspected that there was more to the situation than what appeared on the surface. And if this was about Six, then there was nothing he could do. It was unfortunate for Six, but once she had caught the Ironblood Commander's eye, there was no escaping it.

Soon, Yong Hajin entered the room.

He bowed his head politely.

"What is it?"

Cheol Mugang asked coldly.

"I have a request."

"At this hour?"

"Yes. It's an urgent matter for me."

Cheol Mugang frowned. If only his father weren't who he was. Then again, if that were the case, this situation wouldn't even exist.

Was it because his mind was on Six? Cheol Mugang failed to notice that Yong Hajin's voice sounded subtly different from earlier in the day.

"What's your request?"

"Transfer me to a different squad."

Cheol Mugang's face showed disbelief.

"That's all?"

It wasn't completely out of the blue. He recalled seeing Yong Hajin arguing with Yong Cheonse earlier. He had likely gone to him with this request first and had been rejected.

"This is important to me."

Cheol Mugang abruptly stood up.

He approached Yong Hajin menacingly. No matter whose son he was, he couldn't let this slide.

Startled by his presence, Yong Hajin flinched and stepped back.

SLAP!

He struck his cheek without mercy.

"Come back tomorrow."

Yong Hajin's eyes filled with resentment.

Cheol Mugang's killing intent surged. It enraged him that he couldn't just kill this foolish brat on the spot, but he couldn't.

As Cheol Mugang turned away, Yong Hajin spoke in a low voice.

"I know you like that woman."

"What?"

Cheol Mugang was caught off guard.

"All of the Ironblood Nines know."

Hearing that everyone knew, Cheol Mugang was flustered. At the same time, his anger soared.

"You little bastard, what nonsense are you spouting?"

Yong Hajin glanced at Six behind him.

"She hates you, Commander. She finds you revolting. She shudders at the mere thought of you! I heard it clearly from her own lips!"

"What?"

Cheol Mugang instinctively turned toward her—

SWOOSH!

A faint sound of wind.

Yong Hajin leaped through the air and landed in front of Six.

TAP, TAP, TAP!

In an instant, he sealed Six's blood points, immobilizing her.

Six couldn't comprehend the situation.

*What's going on?*

She was completely bewildered. She couldn't grasp what was happening at all.

Why had Yong Hajin told such a lie? Just as she was about to stand and shout that it was false, Yong Hajin brushed past Cheol Mugang and descended upon her. His movements were too light, too fast.

*How did he reach me in a single leap?*

That wasn't the only mystery.

Cheol Mugang was still standing there.

*Why isn't he reacting, even though he's being attacked?*

That was when she saw it.

A throwing knife was in Yong Hajin's hand.

Blood dripped from the blade.

Cheol Mugang slowly raised his hand.

The moment his fingers touched his neck—

SPLAT!

A red line appeared across Cheol Mugang's throat. Blood spurted from the wound.

"Graaaah!"

Spewing blood, Cheol Mugang collapsed. His neck had been nearly severed.

"Ahhh!"

Shocked, she tried to scream, but with her pressure points sealed, no sound came out.

She squeezed her eyes shut. It felt like a dream. She half-expected to wake up in her quarters when she opened her eyes again.

CRRRKK!

A grotesque sound reached her ears.

Six opened her eyes. It wasn't a dream. Cheol Mugang's face was submerged in a pool of his own blood.

It wasn't a dream, but reality had shifted.

Yong Hajin turned around. His face had changed. It had transformed into Seven's.

Crimson Tiger aimed for a single moment.

The moment when Cheol Mugang's emotions were at their peak. Moreover, he was intoxicated.

Cheol Mugang could not withstand Crimson Tiger's full-force ambush, which had targeted that single instant. It was an unworthy death for someone bearing the title of the Ironblood Commander. And at the core of that death lay his misguided affection for Six.

As Yong Hajin's face transformed into Seven's, she was even more shocked.

Crimson Tiger spoke in a low voice.

"It's me."

"Seven?"

"Yes, I changed my face for a while. There is a martial art for that."

*Why?* With her eyes, she questioned him about the terrifying situation unfolding before her.

Crimson Tiger placed his ear against the door for a moment. He sensed no presence. The martial artists, including One, had vacated the area as ordered.

Crimson Tiger stepped closer to Six once more and locked eyes with her. "Listen carefully to what I'm about to say. I am... a martial artist of the Association."

"...!"

"There's no time, so I'll be direct. I was sent by your father. He ordered me to bring you back."

Her eyes widened.

Crimson Tiger was lying. If he didn't lie, she would never agree to come with him.

"Your name is Baek Soeun, and your father's name is Baek Museong."

Six's gaze trembled. Especially at the mention of her father's name, her mother had told her that before she passed away. She had never shared it with anyone. Only she knew that name.

From her reaction, Crimson Tiger confirmed that she was indeed Baek Soeun.

"Your father is a martial artist of the Association."

Six was half in a daze.

"Will you come with me?"

Six was unable to answer.

Crimson Tiger looked down at Cheol Mugang's corpse.

"Whether you want to stay or not, you can't. Even if they prove you had nothing to do with this, the fact that you were involved means you will be killed."

Six's pupils wavered. Crimson Tiger's words were true. One way or another, she would be held accountable.

"I'll release your pressure points. You won't scream, will you?"

Six blinked in agreement.

Crimson Tiger unsealed her acupoints.

"Ahh."

A faint sigh escaped her lips.

Crimson Tiger spoke apologetically.

"I never intended to trap you, but if I was to take you out of here, I had no choice. We don't have time. We need to leave immediately."

Even with her acupoints unsealed, she couldn't bring herself to say anything.

"Your father wants to see you."

She had been an orphan ever since her mother passed away. She had no lingering attachments to this place.

Hearing that he wanted to see her, a sudden desire to see her father arose within her. She wanted to ask him why he had abandoned her and her mother.

She had thought it was foolish when others longed for the parents who had abandoned them. She had believed such weak-heartedness was pathetic, but now that she was in that position, she understood. Just as seeing a life from afar and experiencing it up close were different, one could never truly understand another's life without living it themselves.

"Do you remember the children from the Hong Family Estate?"

Six's eyes lit up.

"We saved them."

"Ah!"

"If you feel relieved to hear that, then this place is not where you belong. Did you feel relieved?"

She slowly nodded.

"Then come with me."

At last, Six made up her mind.

"Let's go. To my father."

Crimson Tiger pointed at what was hanging around her neck.

"Do you always wear that?"

"Yes."

"I need it. Will you be okay with that?"

Six hesitated before unclasping her necklace.

"This... my mother left it to me. I've never taken it off, not even once."

Crimson Tiger took the necklace from her hand.

"That's exactly why I need it, because it is you."

Crimson Tiger intended to leave it with Floral Viper's corpse. Yeon had already dissolved Floral Viper's body. They would leave the necklace at the remains. Whether the Evil Coalition believed it or not was irrelevant. It was for the Radiant Hall's report, to claim that she was dead and that the mission had been completed.

"From now on, you are not Six. You are Soeun."
