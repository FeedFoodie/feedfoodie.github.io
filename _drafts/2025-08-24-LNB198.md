No one in the jianghu could avoid making enemies. Everyone became entangled in grudges and favors that grew alongside their reputation. This was why the people of the jianghu never claimed to be unbeatable... except for one—The Undefeated Clan.

For centuries, the Undefeated Clan had built a reputation for being unbeatable, causing their enemies to abandon any thought of fighting them.

The Undefeated Ascetic, Moyong Yul-Cheon, ruled as the clan's current patriarch. He was one of the Nine Skies who controlled the jianghu and a recluse who rarely appeared in public. The last time he appeared was ten years ago when he brought an end to the Northern Army's long history.

The world knew Moyong Yul-Cheon to be well over seventy years old, yet the man sitting before Gwan Dae-Seung looked no older than thirty. Rather, he was a peerlessly handsome man in his late twenties, with a face that looked as if it were carved from jade.

Moyong Yul-Cheon wore a dragon robe, a garment that only the Emperor was permitted to wear. A black dragon coiled around the entire robe. It was rumored that the emperor had given him special permission to wear it. No one knew the truth, but people believed it. 

Such was Moyong Yul-Cheon's stature, that of a great martial artist revered by all.

Moyong Yul-Cheon spread out a large sheet of rice paper before him, then forcefully plunged his brush onto the paper.

He made a single dot. After that, he put down the brush and gazed at the dot, showing no intention of painting further.

Gwan Dae-Seung watched with bated breath. He knew that his title as Administrator of Heaven's Summit meant nothing to this man.

Suddenly, Moyong Yul-Cheon frowned at the dot.

*What meaning could a simple dot hold for him to stare at it like that?* Gwan Dae-Seung thought. It was just a single round dot. Still, he couldn't dare guess its meaning.

After a long while, Moyong Yul-Cheon shook his head and muttered, "Phew... It's difficult, isn't it, Dae-Seung?"

Gwan Dae-Seung immediately apologized, "I'm sorry, Patriarch, but it appears that my insight is lacking. I don't know what you mean."

Moyong Yul-Cheon smiled gently. "Why are you aplogizing? It's my fault for asking something so unnecessary."

"What were you looking at so intently?"

"All of Creation."

"Pardon?"

Moyong Yul-Cheon chuckled playfully, "Huhu! It's a joke. I was just bored and tried something crazy for a moment."

Nevertheless, Gwan Dae-Seung couldn't take it as a joke.

*Has my Liege reached a state where he can contain the principles of all of Creation in a single dot?*

The world thought the Nine Skies ruled the jianghu, but Gwan Dae-Seung knew they were wrong. Moyong Yul-Cheon was the sky above the sky.

Ten years ago, he had reached the state of Reversing Aging and Returning to Youth. He cast off his old, cumbersome body and returned to the vibrant appearance of his twenties. It wasn't simply suppressing age but a literal Body Reformation. An ordinary martial artist might have been satisfied, but Moyong Yul-Cheon was different. He focused only on forging ahead and honing his martial arts.

In doing so, he pioneered an unprecedented state. He had come out of seclusion only a short while ago. Since then, all he did was paint and play musical instruments. Yet no one in the Undefeated Clan found his eccentric behavior strange. The faith they had in their Patriarch was truly blind, and Gwan Dae-Seung was one of them.

Moyong Yul-Cheon had recognized Gwan Dae-Seung's talent as a child and sent him to Heaven's Summit. Moyong Yul-Cheon also helped him rise through the ranks. Gwan Dae-Seung's own efforts were the biggest factor, of course, but he wouldn't have reached his current position so quickly without his master's help.

A faint smile touched Moyong Yul-Cheon's face as he looked at Gwan Dae-Seung.

"You've been visiting the main house often lately. What is it?"

"Well…"

Gwan Dae-Seung recounted the events at the manor and the annihilation of the Heavenly Hunters, adding and omitting nothing.

"So, you're saying someone is watching us. Do you have any suspects?"

"There are a few places I suspect, but I can't be certain yet."

"Hmm!"

"For now, I plan to strengthen surveillance on them. For that, there are a few things I need your approval for."

"Speak."

"We need replacements for the Heavenly Hunters."

"Will the Black Soul Corps suffice? Those kids are stronger and more reliable than the Heavenly Hunters."

"Thank you."

"And?"

"May we reawaken the Chaos Demon?"

At Gwan Dae-Seung's request, Moyong Yul-Cheon's smile deepened.

"So you've judged the time is right?"

"The atmosphere seems to have ripened perfectly."

"Huhu! Do as you wish."

"Thank you, Liege."

Gwan Dae-Seung struck his head on the floor with a thud.

Moyong Yul-Cheon rose and approached the window. A well-decorated artificial mountain and pond came into view. It was his favorite scenery.

"Dae-Seung."

"Yes, Liege."

"How was that boy?"

"That boy?"

"The successor of the Northern Wall."

"Ah! You mean Jin Mu-Won."

"That's right. The Northern Wall was the only martial artist I ever felt wary of. Naturally, I can't help but be concerned about his successor."

Jin Kwan-Ho's martial prowess had been below his own, but the man possessed something more than just power. His indomitable will and fighting spirit still left a deep impression.

"He is strong."

"Oh! Is he that strong?"

"It's clear he possesses martial power that at least surpasses the Ten Great Elders."

Moyong Yul-Cheon fell silent. The reply was that shocking.

"How remarkable. To have that much power at such a young age. Even I wasn't that strong in my early twenties."

"As expected, the potential of the Northern Army is incredible. To raise a martial artist like that in such a short time, even after having everything taken away… they are like weeds that grow back even after being stepped on and trampled again and again."

The smile vanished from Moyong Yul-Cheon's face.

Gwan Dae-Seung held his breath and watched him.

"If you leave a weed unattended, it will eventually ruin the entire mountain and garden. I don't want to see the mountain and garden I spent so much time and effort creating get ruined."

"I understand what you mean."

Gwan Dae-Seung bowed his head deeply, a slight tremor running through his shoulders. To him, Moyong Yul-Cheon was the heavens, and his words were law. A mere mortal couldn't defy the words of the heavens.

"I hope I never hear the words 'the Northern Army' again. If necessary, ask Seomoon Hwa or Shim Mu-Wae for help. They won't be able to refuse if you say it's my word."

"I understand."

Moyong Yul-Cheon reached his hand toward the sky beyond the window. He made a grasping motion, closing his wide-open palm.

A human hand couldn't hold the sky. Moyong Yul-Cheon knew this well. But it was human desire to challenge the impossible, and he was more faithful to that desire than anyone.

The revelation that a Silent Night warrior reached the finals of the duel shocked the jianghu beyond words. Humiliation burned through the Central Plains, enraging its heroes. The Demon Hunters' anger, in particular, pierced the heavens. Voices rose, demanding the Silent Night be eradicated immediately, and many agreed.

Heaven's Summit was immediately engulfed in the clouds of war. Ordinary people, sensing the coming conflict, were gripped by fear. Amid this strange mix of fear and madness, one person shone particularly bright.

The Lone Star of the Azure Sky, Dam Soo-Cheon.

He was the hero who had instantly slain Jo Wol, the man who had driven Shim Won-Yi into a crisis, and restored the pride of the Central Plains. Seven years ago, he had made his name famous with the Hundred Duels Trial. Now, he had stepped spectacularly onto the world stage. People chanted Dam Soo-Cheon's name, certain he would become the savior of the Central Plains.

Until now, the top talents of the jianghu were known as the Seven Young Skies. Seven little heavens. But the situation had changed.

The Northern Blade and the Lone Star.

This was a new term that grouped Jin Mu-Won and Dam Soo-Cheon together. People chattered that they existed above the Seven Young Skies. Opinions were fiercely divided over who would win if the two were to fight. Some said the Northern Blade would win. Others insisted it would be the Lone Star. They predicted the two would soon clash to determine who was superior.

In the tense atmosphere, comparing the Northern Blade and the Lone Star was the people's only comfort.

Though there was a time lag, the events at Heaven's Summit were transmitted to all corners of the world. The Heaven's Summit Northern Army Branch was no exception.

The branch was built on the site of the Northern Army, which had been reduced to ashes seven years ago. It was created to monitor the Silent Night in the northern Central Plains and suppress their initial stirrings. Jang Cheon-Woo was the branch manager. He had been given the immense responsibility of shouldering the burden the Northern Army once carried.

Jang Cheon-Woo was more surprised than anyone by the news from Heaven's Summit.

"So, a successor of the Northern Army is still alive."

His branch stood on the Northern Army's former grounds. He had to walk the same path of thorns they had walked, so the news struck him with extraordinary force.

"The will of the heavens is truly unknowable. To send a successor of the Northern Army out into the world again at this time."

Just then.

"Uwaaack!"

A loud scream erupted from outside.

Jang Cheon-Woo knew instantly that something was wrong. He grabbed his weapon and ran outside.

Intruders swarmed over the high wall. They set fires and slaughtered everyone in their path. Caught in the surprise attack, the martial artists of the Northern Army Branch fell without a proper fight.

"Who are you bastards?" Jang Cheon-Woo roared, swinging a large Green Dragon Crescent Blade. A fierce gust of wind and sharp blade energy swept toward the intruders.

"Keuk!"

Three intruders screamed as they were sliced in two and collapsed. But Jang Cheon-Woo's expression was grim.

There was only one group that would attack this place.

"As I thought. The Silent Night."

From all directions, Silent Night warriors appeared, multiplying like evil spirits in the darkness. Jang Cheon-Woo bit his lip firmly. They slaughtered the branch's martial artists like demons. He tried to stop them with all his might, but it wasn't enough. He alone couldn't handle them all.

Suddenly, the Silent Night warriors retreated like an ebbing tide. The branch members who had barely survived looked at each other, bewildered.

Jang Cheon-Woo was just as confused.

*They were gaining momentum, and now they suddenly retreat. What in the...*

DDUDADADANG!

The sound of a guqin began to resonate through the area. At first, the martial artists were confused, but then their faces twisted in pain.

"Ugh, it's sound arts."

"Kuaaack!"

Eardrums burst, and blood streamed from their ears. Those with strong inner qi hurriedly protected their heart meridians and eardrums, but it was useless. The guqin's melody bypassed their eardrums and attacked their minds directly.

Overwhelmed by the formidable sound arts, the martial artists of the Northern Army Branch writhed in agony as they died. Jang Cheon-Woo was no exception.

His heart meridian severed and his eardrums burst, pouring blood. His heart, which had beaten strongly just moments before, ruptured. Blood no longer circulated.

*Keuk! If only the Northern Army were still intact...*

That was his last thought.

Fifteen minutes after the melody began, no one was left alive.

Only then did an old musician appear, surrounded by his bodyguards. He was the Celestial Sound Demon, Yoon Cheon-Hak, and he had come to avenge his disciple, Geum Dan-Yeop.

The Silent Night's all-out offensive against the Central Plains had begun, with Yoon Cheon-Hak as its vanguard.
