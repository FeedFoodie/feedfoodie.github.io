"Have you heard the rumors about the Lightning Dragon Hero?"

"They say a new divine star has risen in murim!"

"The Azure Dragon Hero acknowledged him as a worthy rival, and the Jaegal Patriarch personally came out to greet him!"

"I saw his face in person... His appearance was not that of a mere human..."

Everywhere—on the streets, in inns, wherever people gathered.

Rumors spread like wildfire.

It was no exaggeration to say that the entire city was buzzing with stories about the Lightning Dragon Hero.

And that wasn't all.

"Is the Lightning Dragon Hero truly that strong?"

"I won't believe it until I see it with my own eyes!"

"Hoho. This old man will personally test how sharp the Lightning Dragon Hero's sword is and treasure it as a lesson."

Wuhan was a city where countless martial artists passed through daily.

Among them were those desperate for fame, those seeking enlightenment through battles with powerful opponents, and those simply obsessed with martial arts.

The appearance of a new divine star, the Lightning Dragon Hero, was more than enough to ignite the competitive spirits of such martial artists.

Numerous experts rose from their seats, determined to challenge the Lightning Dragon Hero.

""I challenge the Lightning Dragon Hero!""

Meanwhile, Baek Suryong, the man responsible for all of Namgung Su's current troubles, strolled down the street, barely suppressing his laughter.

*Now you'll get a taste of your own medicine.*

From now on, Namgung Su would have to constantly prove himself as the Lightning Dragon Hero by facing endless challengers.

Just like Baek Suryong had to at the White Dragon Manor.

*This is also a good chance for him to properly gauge his own skills through duels.*

Baek Suryong had no intention of harassing or tormenting Namgung Su.

This was all for the sake of the Azure Dragon Academy.

If Namgung Su's fame grew, so would the prestige of the Azure Dragon Academy.

This was truly a plan where he could catch two birds with one stone—or in this case, drive the fish into the net while catching the shrimp.

The reason he couldn't stop humming to himself was purely because the weather was nice.

"Hmm hmm hmm~"

Although Baek Suryong walked casually through the streets, no one recognized him.

That was because he had altered his face using disguise techniques.

When he first entered the city with his companions, he had no choice but to show his face, but now that he was alone, there was no need for that.

*I'll just take care of my business and leave immediately.*

Although Wuhan housed both the Murim Alliance and the Heavenly Martial Academy, Baek Suryong had no intention of visiting either.

The Heavenly Martial Academy was on break, just like the Azure Dragon Academy, so there was nothing worth seeing there. Meanwhile, the Murim Alliance's chairman was currently absent.

*One of the five Captains is probably acting as the chairman's proxy...*

Baek Suryong glanced toward the distant headquarters of the Murim Alliance.

His official title was the Murim Alliance Chief Instructor.

A position on par with the five Captains, but if he showed up at the Murim Alliance now, he was unlikely to receive a warm welcome.

*To the Captains, I must seem like an outsider who suddenly barged in.*

They would undoubtedly see him as a thorn in their side, and Baek Suryong had no desire to engage in an awkward exchange with them.

*If I stir up trouble now, it could also put the chairman in an uncomfortable position later.*

Of course, considering how the chairman had tricked him and left, causing him some trouble didn't seem like a bad idea... but in the end, he simply wasn't interested.

Baek Suryong moved toward the outskirts of the city.

No matter how large and prosperous a city was, there were always slums, and Wuhan was no exception.

Beggars swarmed around the ruins of collapsed buildings, their bodies covered in filth. The stench that wafted through the air was strong enough to carry for miles.

Baek Suryong pinched his nose and muttered.

"...Looks like I've come to the right place."

**<Beggar's Gang (Wuhan Branch)>**

The signboard was so worn that it looked like it would fall off at any moment, but the letters were written with bold and powerful strokes.

Baek Suryong completely shut off his sense of smell as he approached the beggars.

"Who's this?"

A beggar lazily sauntered up to him. Baek Suryong could immediately tell that the man had trained in martial arts.

The reason the Beggar's Gang was considered part of the Nine Sects One Gang wasn't just because of their numbers.

These were men so obsessed with martial arts that they didn't care if they had to live as beggars.

Their martial arts were no weaker than those of the orthodox sects, which was why the Beggar's Gang was always mentioned alongside them.

Baek Suryong politely offered a clasped hands greeting and said, "I'm here to see the Branch Manager."

"...And who might you be?"

The beggar scrutinized Baek Suryong's face. It was unfamiliar.

A stranger so casually asking for the Branch Manager?

Looking around, he saw other beggars shaking their heads. They didn't recognize him either.

That meant he was either a foreigner or his face was a disguise.

The beggar narrowed his eyes and asked, "Are you wearing a disguise?"

Already figured it out?

As expected of the Beggar's Gang.

Baek Suryong simply nodded.

"I used a disguise technique. If my real face became known, it would cause unnecessary trouble, but I was referred here by someone you know, so you don't need to worry about my identity."

"......", yet, the beggar's wary gaze only grew sharper.

To an almost suspicious degree.

"Did you schedule an appointment in advance?"

"I didn't, but if I show this, I was told he would gladly meet me."

Baek Suryong pulled out an old piece of paper from his robe and handed it over.

The paper itself wasn't anything special, but as soon as the beggar saw the signature on it, his eyes widened.

"Wang Son, the Jiangxi Branch Manager?"

A letter of introduction from Wang Son, the Beggar's Gang Jiangxi Branch Manager.

As the beggar carefully read through the contents, his expression shifted from wariness to astonishment.

Looking up at Baek Suryong, the beggar's mouth gaped open.

"C-Chief..."

"Do you understand now why I used a disguise?"

The beggar swallowed hard and nodded.

If this man really was the Azure Dragon Hero, then it made perfect sense why he had come in disguise.

Exaggeration or not, right now, the entire city was practically hunting for the Azure Dragon Hero.

"...Please wait here for a moment. I'll go report this inside."

His tone had already changed.

This was why fame was important for a martial artist.

As he waited, Baek Suryong took a moment to observe his surroundings.

Something had been bothering him for a while.

*Has the Beggar's Gang always been this tense?*

The beggars around him.

They seemed to be idly sitting or lying down, but Baek Suryong noticed that their positions formed a kind of formation.

*They must have figured out who I am by now.*, yet, the wary stares directed at him had not lessened.

If anything, they seemed ready to attack at the slightest provocation.

WOONG!

The beggars' qi resonated together.

A formation of synergy.

If activated properly, escaping it would be no simple task.

The atmosphere was as tense as a battlefield.

Even Baek Suryong found it surprising.

*Did something happen recently?*

At that moment, a man walked out from the ruined house.

As soon as Baek Suryong saw him, he was reminded of Wang Son, the Branch Manager.

Their faces were so similar that they could have been twins—small eyes, large nostrils, and a somewhat vague expression.

However, there was one crucial difference.

This one has incredibly large feet.

The approaching Branch Manager was barefoot, and his feet were twice as large and thick as a normal person's. Even the tops of his feet were covered in thick hair.

That guy... Could his name be...

"I am Wang Bal, the Wuhan Branch Manager."

Wang Bal clasped his hands in greeting. His name was so predictable that Baek Suryong involuntarily cleared his throat.

"Hmm-hmm. I am Baek Suryong."

Baek Suryong dispelled his disguise, returning to his original face. Since his identity was already revealed, continuing to hide his face would be disrespectful.

Wang Bal gave a faint smile.

"It's an honor to meet the Azure Dragon Hero, whom I've only heard about in rumors."

"Thank you."

Baek Suryong wasn't one to say things like, It's not true or You flatter me.

Wang Bal carefully observed Baek Suryong, and Baek Suryong did the same in return. A strange tension lingered for a moment.

"So, what brings you to our branch...?"

"I came to obtain some information."

Baek Suryong intended to gather intelligence on the recent movements of the Ice Palace.

Of course, he could also use the Beggar's Gang once he reached Shaanxi, but the Wuhan branch was where the most information in the world converged. He judged that the quality of information here might be superior.

However, Wang Bal's expression was strange.

"...I apologize, but our branch is currently in no position to receive guests. Would it be alright if we spoke here instead?"

It was an unexpected statement, but Baek Suryong nodded without hesitation.

He wasn't particularly eager to step into a den of beggars anyway.

"I don't mind."

"Very well. What specific information are you looking for?"

"First, the recent movements of the Ice Palace and..."

The conversation was brief and to the point. Wang Bal didn't seem inclined to prolong it.

Baek Suryong noticed that Wang Bal's eyes were slightly bloodshot.

He hasn't been sleeping properly.

Moreover, his gaze was anxious and restless. He was trying to hide it, but there was no deceiving Baek Suryong's eyes.

"I understand. If you provide a lodging address, I will have the information compiled and sent to you before the day is over."

"Thank you."

Something was clearly wrong within the Beggar's Gang, but as long as he received the information he needed, Baek Suryong had no intention of interfering. He pretended not to notice.

"Then, I'll take my leave."

Baek Suryong clasped his hands in farewell and turned to leave.

Wang Bal called out to him just as he took his fifth step.

"By any chance... do you know much about poison arts?"

"Poison arts?"

The moment Baek Suryong turned back, one of the old beggars, who had been forming the core of the formation, shouted.

"The Little Beggar!"

He had said The Little Beggar (後丐).

Among the Sect Leader's disciples, the one designated to be the next Sect Leader was given that title.

Baek Suryong's eyes gleamed with interest.

No wonder he looked strong.

Wang Bal was the appointed successor to the Beggar's Gang Sect Leader.

Baek Suryong still didn't know why Wang Bal had called him back, but if he could earn his favor, it would be an advantageous investment.

That said, he had no intention of lying.

"I wouldn't call myself an expert, but I do have enough knowledge to handle most poisons and poison arts."

Baek Suryong answered honestly.

Rather than being disappointed, the Little Beggar actually smiled.

"You're honest."

"I wouldn't want to set false expectations."

After a brief moment of hesitation, Wang Bal finally spoke with difficulty.

"...We have a patient. Could you take a look at them?"

"It's not a difficult request, but..."

"The Little Beggar! What do you think you're doing?!"

The old beggar who had called him the Little Beggar earlier interrupted again.

"How can you act so recklessly without proof that he's really the Azure Dragon Hero?!"

"If you don't believe me, Elder, then verify it yourself."

"What?!"

Wang Bal looked at the interfering Elder with an expression of sheer exasperation.

"If you fight him, you'll know. Even if the Azure Dragon Hero is merely the lowest-ranked of the Ten Supremes, I doubt it would take him more than ten seconds to put an Elder of the Beggar's Gang in his place."

"You, you insolent...!"

"Do you really think I wouldn't recognize my own brother's handwriting and signature?"

Wang Bal held up the letter Baek Suryong had brought from Wang Son as he said, So they were brothers. That explained the resemblance.

Baek Suryong nodded and watched as Wang Bal reprimanded the Elder.

"There's always the possibility of forgery! In a situation like this, letting an outsider in so carelessly is..."

"Enough."

Fwoosh!

An overwhelming aura surged from the Little Beggar's body.

His narrow eyes radiated terrifying killing intent, and the Elder clenched his teeth.

"With Master absent, all decisions fall to me. If you question me again, I'll treat it as insubordination and break your legs."

"......"

The Elder ground his teeth but could not argue further. The other beggars also looked displeased but remained silent.

He's got quite the temperament.

Baek Suryong found Wang Bal intriguing.

Not only was his bold personality refreshing, but his decisive judgment and firm leadership were admirable.

Someone like him would repay a favor several times over.

Unlike a certain someone from the Namgung Clan, who only plotted revenge while pretending to repay his debts.

"I apologize for showing you such disgraceful behavior from my subordinates."

Wang Bal looked at Baek Suryong with an embarrassed expression.

The Elder, still filled with resentment, glared at Wang Bal one last time before disappearing into the crowd of beggars.

Baek Suryong watched him leave, then turned back to Wang Bal and asked, "Where is the patient?"

"...Follow me."

The beggars forming the defensive formation stepped aside, and Baek Suryong followed Wang Bal into the ruined house.

From the atmosphere and the conversation earlier, Baek Suryong had already pieced together what was happening within the Beggar's Gang.

"Someone important in the Beggar's Gang has been struck by poison arts, haven't they?"

"...That's correct."

Wang Bal nodded, recalling the unbelievable stories about the Azure Dragon Hero.

It was the Azure Dragon Hero who first noticed the anomaly in the Namgung Clan. If not for him, the Namgung Clan would have been wiped from murim.

He had infiltrated the Valley of Evil with only a group of young students, slain the Bloody Ripper, and brought down the Valley of Evil—earning the alias Azure Dragon Hero.

He even convinced the Murim Alliance Chairman, who had come to subjugate the Azure Dragon Academy, to form an alliance instead. And the conditions were overwhelmingly favorable to the Azure Dragon Academy.

If martial arts were all he had, these feats would have been impossible. The Azure Dragon Hero has something more. Maybe, just maybe...

Wang Bal was desperate.

He was willing to grasp at any straw.

That was why he had pushed through opposition and brought an outsider here.

"This is the place."

A strong medicinal scent filled the room.

Inside, a deathly pale old man lay still.

"This is my master."

Baek Suryong's eyes widened.

If he was the Little Beggar's master, that meant he was the Sect Leader of the Beggar's Gang.

This frail old man is the Sect Leader?

The Sect Leader's body was covered in golden acupuncture needles, but his chest was completely blackened, his skin necrotic.

This was the work of poison arts, just as Wang Bal had described.

"A few days ago, he was attacked by an unknown assailant. The culprit is..."

Wang Bal trailed off, but Baek Suryong already knew the answer.

Examining the Sect Leader's wounds, Baek Suryong's expression hardened.

"The Blood Cult."
