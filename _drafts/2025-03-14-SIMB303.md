"......" Namgung Su blankly stared at his reflection in the mirror. His jade-white skin was flawless, without a single blemish. His limbs had also grown slightly longer. A vitality beyond words surged through his entire body.

*It's not just my body that has changed.*

The world appeared sharper and clearer. His senses were more sensitive than ever before, to the point where even the slightest touch felt like a sting. At first, he had almost flinched at the sensation of tiny dust particles landing on his skin.

"Right now, your senses are extremely heightened, so it'll feel uncomfortable, but once you adjust, it'll be fine," Baek Suryong mumbled in an exhausted voice. 

Namgung Su turned his head to look at him.

As their eyes met directly, Baek Suryong grinned. "If that's the only side effect, it's not bad. Your hair could've all fallen out and never grown back, or you could've ended up mute."

"A side effect, huh..." Namgung Su murmured as he traced his fingers along his golden eyes, which gleamed like jewels.

A side effect of Body Reformation. It meant he would have to live with golden-colored eyes for the rest of his life.

Baek Suryong asked, "Why? Worried about people whispering behind your back?"

"...I've never put much stock in others' opinions." Replying indifferently, Namgung Su summoned heavenly thunder energy to his fingertips.

CRACKLE!

A surge of lightning far stronger than before coiled around his palm, yet, what truly surprised Namgung Su was not the power of his lightning qi.

"...It doesn't hurt."

In other words, he had achieved complete mastery of the Heavenly Lightning Sword Technique.

Still, reaching complete mastery in the martial art did not mean that Namgung Su had reached the limits of his strength. It simply meant he had reached the final stage set by the martial artist who created the Heavenly Lightning Sword Technique.

From now on, it was up to Namgung Su to develop and refine it further.

Baek Suryong, knowing better than anyone the current state of Namgung Su's body, smirked.

"Congratulations on achieving great success. You should be able to form enhanced sword qi soon."

"...I feel like I can do it right now."

"Right now?"

Namgung Su nodded and began compressing the heavenly thunder energy in his palm.

Crackle...! Sizzle!

The lightning in his hand shrank, gradually condensing into a solid form.

A moment later, Namgung Su was gripping a sword-shaped bolt of lightning.

"That's insane..."

Baek Suryong was more surprised than Namgung Su, the one who had actually created it.

*He succeeded on his first try?*

When he had mentioned that Namgung Su would eventually be able to form enhanced qi with training, he hadn't meant that it would happen immediately, yet, Namgung Su had created enhanced qi in a single attempt.

Moreover, the nature of Namgung Su's enhanced qi was different from ordinary enhanced qi.

Observing the qi closely, Baek Suryong commented, "Nah, that isn't enhanced sword qi, it should be called enhanced lightning qi, or thunderbolt qi. Without defensive enhanced qi covering the body, blocking it would be difficult. Even someone stronger than you would find it tricky to deal with."

"......"

Baek Suryong was certain of that. Among the martial artists known as the Hundred Grandmasters of the current murim, there wouldn't be many who could withstand thunderbolt qi.

Crackle...

Perhaps maintaining thunderbolt qi was still difficult, as it soon dissipated into the air, but that was something time would naturally solve.

"Not only have you gone through Body Reformation, but you've also learned to wield enhanced qi! Even in murim history, such miraculous encounters are rare! Congratulations!"

"Congratulations, Oppa!"

Jaegal Soyeong and Namgung Mi cheered for Namgung Su as if it were their own achievement.

Thinking her Oppa had almost died, Namgung Mi clung to Namgung Su's waist, sniffling and sobbing.

"...I'm sorry for making you worry."

Still somewhat dazed, Namgung Su embraced his little sister.

Then, his eyes met Baek Suryong's again.

"You..."

"What?"

He had vowed that once the Qi Transfer was over, he wouldn't let Baek Suryong off easily, but the moment he saw that face, he found himself unable to do so.

A face utterly drained, exhausted from performing Qi Transfer, yet, Baek Suryong was grinning contentedly.

As if he had been the one to receive a miraculous encounter.

How could he get angry after seeing that expression?

Namgung Su bit his lip hard, his golden eyes burning intensely as he glared at Baek Suryong.

Seeing the look in his eyes, Baek Suryong felt a twinge of unease.

*Is he mad because the Qi Transfer hurt too much?*

Well, given that it must have felt like his body was being torn apart, it was understandable if he was furious.

Only someone with Namgung Su's extreme endurance could survive a Body Reformation; anyone else wouldn't even be able to attempt it.

*Oh well. As long as the result turned out well, that's what matters.*

Baek Suryong casually avoided Namgung Su's piercing gaze and spoke in a voice heavy with fatigue.

"Let's rest here for today. We're all exhausted."

"...Agreed."

Namgung Su bit his lip firmly as he replied.

He had wanted to say thank you, but for some reason, the words wouldn't come out. The emotions were too complex to be expressed in mere words.

*Baek Suryong. You...*

His clan already owed Baek Suryong a debt so great that it would take a lifetime to repay.

And now, he had added an even greater debt on top of that. How was he supposed to repay such an overwhelming kindness?

Clench...

Namgung Su's fists tightened, his expression twisting.

Unaware of his own actions, he continued to glare at Baek Suryong with terrifyingly intense eyes.

*What... what is this?*

Cold sweat formed on Baek Suryong's forehead.

Ever since his eyes turned golden, just being glared at by Namgung Su carried an overwhelming pressure.

*... If we fought right now, I'd still win, right?*

Namgung Su had just undergone Body Reformation and was in peak condition, whereas Baek Suryong was utterly drained from performing Qi Transfer.

The image of the thunderbolt qi he had just witnessed flashed through Baek Suryong's mind.

*I've raised a tiger cub...*

Gulp.

Just as an unnecessary tension was about to rise from their misunderstanding, "You two just stay put. We'll handle the cleanup. Don't do anything, just rest!"

"That's right! Just sit tight and relax!"

Jaegal Soyeong and Namgung Mi made a fuss as they moved into action.

Jaegal Soyeong restored the formations she had undone, concealing the cottage from outside view, while Namgung Mi tidied up the mess inside.

There was also plenty of food stored in the cottage.

""We'll handle the cooking too!""

The two of them eagerly tried to show off their skills, but soon, Namgung Su—after catching a whiff of an unidentifiable smell—took the cooking utensils from them.

"I'll do it."

Having undergone Body Reformation, Namgung Su was now better at cooking as well. His enhanced senses played a significant role.

"It's... delicious...!"

"This is the best meal Oppa has ever made!"

The two girls continuously let out exclamations of delight as they ate Namgung Su's food.

Baek Suryong also took a bite and then widened his eyes at Namgung Su.

"This is really good."

"...The ingredients were just high quality."

The atmosphere softened.

After finishing dinner together, the group finally got some proper rest.

Namgung Su swung his sword in front of the cottage, adjusting to his newly reformed body, while Namgung Mi crouched in the yard, watching her Oppa.

Jaegal Soyeong wandered around the cottage, her eyes sparkling as she found rare medicinal herbs.

Baek Suryong rested alone inside the cottage, reading through the martial arts manual 『the Primordial Thunder Divine Art』 left behind by the Thunder Demon.

"As expected..."

It was a martial art that even the Blue Sky Sword King had coveted.

After spending decades in seclusion refining it, the Thunder Demon had elevated it to a level worthy of being called a divine art.

Baek Suryong had no intention of learning it himself, but just analyzing the manual was already a great study for him.

"If I remove the Star Absorption Technique portion and reinforce this part, it could be improved further. There are also overlapping principles with the Heavenly Lightning Sword Technique..."

For a long while, Baek Suryong immersed himself in the manual with great interest. His eyes gleamed like stars as he analyzed the new martial art. There was a reason why being a martial arts Master was his calling.

Night quickly fell.

The four of them laid out bedding inside the cottage. Fortunately, the space was wide enough for all of them to lie side by side.

"Today was such a long, long day..."

After chattering for a while, Namgung Mi soon drifted off to sleep. Lying next to her, Jaegal Soyeong also blinked drowsily.

"Good night..."

"Yawn. Sleep well."

Even Baek Suryong, exhausted, yawned as he turned onto his side and soon fell silent.

SNORE.... SNORE...

"......"

Everyone must have been tired, as the conversation quickly faded into quiet, but Namgung Su couldn't fall asleep.

His body, having undergone Body Reformation, was simply too full of energy.

Srrrk.

Eventually, Namgung Su got up from his bedding and stepped outside. Before leaving, he glanced at Baek Suryong.

*Baek Suryong. Right now, I'm only receiving help from you...*

Standing under the night sky, Namgung Su made a vow.

He would no longer just receive help.

One day, he would repay this debt in full.

Clenching his fist tightly, Namgung Su murmured in his usual cold voice, "...I will repay this debt. No matter how long it takes..."

At that moment, Baek Suryong, who was supposed to be asleep inside the cottage, flinched.

*damn it. I really have raised a tiger cub!*

It was another night of deepening misunderstandings.

{sep}

The next day, after a full day of rest at the cottage, the group gathered in the yard in front of two fresh burial mounds. These were the graves of the hunter couple who had been killed by the Thunder Demon.

"Uncle, Auntie. I hope you find peace in the afterlife."

It was Namgung Mi's idea to give the hunter couple a proper burial here.

And she didn't stop at just suggesting it.

She personally dug the ground and built the graves, albeit clumsily.

Though the couple's bodies had been completely incinerated in the battle between the Thunder Demon and Namgung Su, they gathered the ashes, covered them with soil, and formed small burial mounds.

After paying her respects, Namgung Mi turned to the adults and asked, "They were good people, right?"

"...They were simple, ordinary people."

Namgung Su held his young sister's hand as he said, He, too, remembered the hunter running toward them, shouting for them to flee.

If he had realized the situation sooner and acted faster, perhaps he could have saved them.

"Why are there so many bad people in the world? The Blood Cult, the Thunder Demon..."

Tears welled up in Namgung Mi's eyes.

She couldn't stop thinking about the gentle smile the hunter's wife had given her inside the cave.

Wiping her reddened eyes with her sleeve, Namgung Mi muttered, "Sorry for crying again..."

"It's okay."

"I won't cry anymore. I'll become strong. As strong as my Oppas."

Sniffling, Namgung Mi wiped away her tears with her sleeve. A firm determination shone in her large eyes.

She was only ten years old. Even as the daughter of a murim noble family, her resilience was remarkable.

"I'll train hard and enter the Azure Dragon Academy as the top student one day!"

"Of course, you will."

"I'll be looking forward to it five years from now."

Namgung Su and Jaegal Soyeong smiled faintly.

Baek Suryong quietly patted Namgung Mi's head.

*There's no need to tell them that the hunters were probably the Thunder Demon's informants.*

Sometimes, leaving misunderstandings uncorrected was for the best.

Namgung Mi was still too young to understand the complicated circumstances of the hunters.

Baek Suryong turned to the group and said, "Let's go."

A few days later, they arrived at Wuhan, the capital city of Hubei, and the city where both the Murim Alliance HQ and the Heavenly Martial Academy were located.
