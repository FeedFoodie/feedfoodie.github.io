CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

From early morning, the clear ring of metal echoed ceaselessly around the mountain villa.

"Haaaap!"

"Chaaaat!"

Boys and girls crossed swords in the courtyard under the brilliant sunlight. Each time a louder clash rang out, startled mountain birds flew up in unison from the trees surrounding the villa.

A large signpost was planted at the villa's entrance: Azure Dragon Academy Swordsmanship Research Society Summer Vacation Training Camp

It was a consideration for any herb gatherers, woodcutters, or passersby who might be startled by the activity.

"Everyone, let's eat breakfast!"

As the students on meal duty poked their heads out and called, the trainees stopped their sparring and gathered in the dining hall. The appetizing smell made everyone's mouth water.

"Food's ready!"

"Good, I was starving."

"You guys are on duty today? Looks like we can expect something good."

The SRS had rented the entire mountain villa for their training camp. The goal was to sharpen each other's sword skills while eating and living together for about a month.

This camp period also served as an opportunity for seniors and juniors who weren't usually close to become friendlier.

"Is the spot you injured earlier okay?"

"It's fine. It just grazed my arm slightly."

"Sorry. My sword path went awry mid-way…"

"Then maybe you could put some wound salve on it for me, Senior?"

"Uh, is that okay?"

With young men and women living and eating together in one place for about a month, it was natural that several students developed into couples.

Sweet gazes were exchanged here and there, and affectionate whispers could be heard. After more than ten days into the camp, quite a few students were seen walking around in pairs.

Of course, everywhere you go, some guys have all the luck, while others don't.

"Damn couples…"

"What are they doing during our sacred club training camp?"

"It's so annoying I can't even watch."

"I'm planning to train wearing an eye patch starting tomorrow."

The students who hadn't found a partner devoted themselves to training, hoping to at least gain something in swordsmanship. Needless to say, their sword techniques grew sharper by the day.

Which was why Yoo Yiran was feeling displeased.

"…"

"President. You seem to be in a bad mood lately?"

Yoo Yiran turned her head at the voice beside her. It was Vice President Jeong Yeonhee. She wore a mischievous expression.

"Me? What reason would I have to be in a bad mood…"

"Lots of couples have traditionally emerged during the SRS summer camp."

"Couples…"

Unconsciously, Yoo Yiran's gaze shifted.

Across the room sat a boy eating amongst the male students.

Though obscured by the needlessly large male students surrounding him, Yiran could vividly imagine Wiji Cheon stuffing his cheeks full of food and chewing diligently.

"Hmm…"

"Look at that gaze. You've got it bad."

Jeong Yeonhee nudged Yiran's side and whispered.

"Half the camp is already over. Are you just going to let it pass without doing anything?"

"What am I supposed to…"

What was she supposed to do?

Yoo Yiran had never been interested in boys her entire life, let alone made an effort to gain their attention.

Usually, it was the opposite situation, making her rather cold towards men.

"Honestly, I'm not even sure exactly what these feelings are…"

"Wow, everyone around you knows, how come only you don't, Senior?"

Seeing Yoo Yiran hesitate, Jeong Yeonhee sighed deeply.

"Senior. Aren't you being too complacent? Technically, you're not even his girlfriend. What will you do if someone else snatches him away?"

"...Someone else? Who?"

"Anyone."

The Vice President crossed her arms. It seemed some shock therapy was needed. She lowered her voice further and whispered.

"He's skilled with the sword. Has a good personality. Plus, he's Mr. Baek Suryong's top disciple. There must be plenty of girls aiming for him besides you."

"Don't tell me you too…"

Before the President's eyes could narrow sharply, the Vice President firmly shook her head.

"I prefer bulky guys. If I had to pick, someone like Senior Hyonwon Kang?"

"...I, I see."

Yoo Yiran sighed in relief.

She had absolutely no idea how to express her feelings.

The Vice President decided to offer some subtle help.

"This afternoon's training session. Isn't it your day to teach the first-years?"

"Hm? Yes, it is."

"How about using sword instruction as an excuse to talk about various things? Maybe casually brush hands…"

"Y-You want me to do such a shameless thing?!"

Her voice must have risen too high for a moment, as all eyes in the dining hall turned towards the two of them.

From the far end, Wiji Cheon could be seen tilting his head curiously.

*Shh!*

The Vice President pressed a finger to her lips, then sent a message telepathically to Yiran.

[Who said anything about seducing him outright? I meant show him your charms as a woman, not just a swordswoman.]

[I understand what you mean, but…]

[Ugh, this is frustrating. Okay, so…]

The Vice President gave Yiran various pieces of advice on what to do.

Although Yiran had no intention of following her words exactly, she decided to muster her courage just once.

{sep}

During the summer training camp, the third and fourth-years took turns instructing the first-years each day.

As it happened, today was Yoo Yiran's turn to teach the first-years.

Wearing her neatest martial arts uniform, Yoo Yiran appeared before the first-years and announced, "First, I want each of you to demonstrate the swordsmanship you're most confident in. After that, we'll have guided sparring. Then, I'll observe your free sparring amongst yourselves. That's how we'll proceed."

""Yes, ma'am!! We look forward to your guidance!!""

The tense first-years demonstrated their swordsmanship one by one. Most were nervous performing in front of the President and made repeated mistakes.

Each time, Yoo Yiran offered advice to her juniors.

From general pointers on their swordsmanship to suggestions for changing minor movements. As expected of the SRS President, Yoo Yiran had a very keen eye.

"Thank you!"

"Thank you!"

The juniors all looked deeply moved by Yoo Yiran's precious, gem-like advice.

Then, it was Wiji Cheon's turn.

"I am Wiji Cheon."

After a clasped hands greeting, Wiji Cheon brought his Soul Sword up to a middle guard.

Slowly, Wiji Cheon's sword began to move.

Yoo Yiran repeatedly marveled at the smooth, flowing movements.

*There's nothing to correct.*

There were moves she might have executed differently, but that was simply due to differences in physique or muscle development between individuals, not because his form was wrong.

Wiji Cheon instinctively traced the sword paths that suited him best. His innate talent was dazzling.

Yoo Yiran, however, wasn't just standing there in admiration.

"...Excellent. But when you executed the third technique earlier, what if the opponent responded like this?"

Drawing her sword, Yoo Yiran traced the sword path Wiji Cheon had just shown.

Wiji Cheon was stronger than Yoo Yiran.

That didn't mean, however, that Yoo Yiran couldn't say anything about his swordplay.

There were definitely aspects one couldn't see when they weren't the one performing the technique.

"Indeed… You're amazing, Senior!"

"Wh-What, it's nothing."

Yoo Yiran subtly avoided Wiji Cheon's brightly shining eyes. She then addressed the other first-years.

"Alright, let's move on to guided sparring, one by one. Come up in the same order as before."

Here too, Wiji Cheon was a crane among chickens.

While the other first-years struggled to block even ten of Yoo Yiran's strikes, Wiji Cheon pushed her back, matching her or even exceeding her.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

Sword strikes flashed back and forth, too fast for most students' eyes to follow. Despite this, both fighters maintained their composure.

Since it wasn't a duel where victory or defeat mattered, the two would occasionally pause their exchange of blows to engage in sword discussion.

"Senior. Just then, I think it would have been better to shift your center of gravity further back and relax your wrist more."

"Why?"

"It's more advantageous for deflecting the attack first and then counterattacking. Since your wrist is more flexible than mine…"

"Understood. Let's try again."

The deeper she got into the swordplay, the more Yoo Yiran forgot about showing Wiji Cheon her charms as a woman.

It didn't suit her anyway. Yoo Yiran was most beautiful when she was wielding her sword with all her might.

The SRS Lieutenants watched from afar. The Vice President muttered with a look of resignation.

"...How can he not fall for her after seeing that side of her?"

"Maybe the feelings we feel watching the Senior are what the Senior feels watching him?"

At one of the Lieutenant's words, everyone else unconsciously nodded in agreement.

{sep}

Days spent diligently training at the mountain villa quickly turned into nights.

Stars densely populated the night sky. Looking up from within the mountains, the sky was literally a different world of stars.

It was mesmerizing enough to lose track of time just staring blankly upwards.

And the meat grilled together in front of the villa was the best taste in the world.

"Who caught that huge wild boar?"

"Wiji Cheon did!"

"Are you good at hunting too?"

"I lived in the mountains with my grandfather since I was young. Hehe."

Students who loved the sword crossed blades daily for a month.

It was enough time to open up to each other and share honest conversations. Before they knew it, everyone had become quite close.

There was no particular theme to the conversations they had while sitting around after eating.

They chatted aimlessly about this and that until someone abruptly brought up a topic.

"Seniors, what are you going to do after graduation?"

The third and fourth-years, nearing graduation, had many concerns about their future after leaving the Azure Dragon Academy. The juniors were also curious about their seniors' thoughts.

Everyone gathered around the campfire, and starting with the seniors, they shared their dreams one by one.

"I'm going to take over the family business and run the escort agency. I plan to expand it further under my leadership."

"I want to get a job at one of the Ten Great Companies and see the jianghu. Earn money and travel, kill two birds with one stone."

"It's the Murim Alliance for me, obviously. I'm going to take the entrance exam for the Divine Sword Corps."

"...Won't you fail the document screening?"

"They added special recruitment slots when they allied with the Murim Alliance this time, right? I'll aim for that."

While there were those with such realistic goals, others held romantic notions about the jianghu.

"I'll roam the jianghu, helping the weak and punishing villains."

"I want to keep polishing my swordsmanship and become one of the top 100 swordsmen."

"If you're talking about the romance of the jianghu, isn't it being a freelancer?"

"Freelancer is a bit…"

"It's dangerous and the pay sucks. You'll die destitute chasing romance."

The conversation started by the seniors naturally flowed down to the juniors. The juniors also talked about their own goals and dreams. It was the kind of atmosphere where everyone felt they should share.

"Wiji Cheon. What are you going to do after graduation?"

At the question, everyone's gaze turned towards Wiji Cheon.

"Ah, me…"

The boy's face turned red as a ripe persimmon as attention focused on him.

What would Wiji Cheon answer?

To the students, Wiji Cheon was a sword genius.

Moreover, his master was the Azure Dragon Hero, the lowest ranked among the Ten Supremes.

If Wiji Cheon declared, "I'm going to become the world's strongest swordsman," no one would laugh.

"I…"

Wiji Cheon spoke in a voice barely louder than an ant's.

"I want to become a teacher."

"A teacher?"

The completely unexpected answer made everyone's eyes widen.

Wiji Cheon scratched his cheek, looking embarrassed.

"The person I respect the most is Mr. Baek Suryong. Ah, excluding my grandfather, of course."

Wiji Cheon slowly began to tell the story of how he first met Baek Suryong.

He omitted the parts related to the Blood Cult, naturally.

"...Teacher saved me when I was suffering from qi deviation. And he helped me enter the Azure Dragon Academy. Thanks to him, I was able to meet all of you seniors and my friends… Also, whenever I heard the voice of the evil sword, he helped me stay on the right path."

Wiji Cheon wasn't a particularly good speaker.

Furthermore, he was very shy, so when many people looked at him, his face would turn red and his voice would get progressively smaller.

The boy's sincerity, however, reached everyone.

"So I also want to become a teacher and teach students."

Wiji Cheon lowered his head deeply after finishing his words.

A brief silence fell over the group following Wiji Cheon's unexpected confession of his dark past.

Yoo Yiran broke the silence.

"Then how about a trial run?"

"Pardon?"

"Tomorrow, for one day. You instruct everyone in the SRS on swordsmanship. How about it?"

"S-Suddenly?"

"What does everyone else think?"

Yoo Yiran pushed the idea forward instantly. The other SRS Lieutenants readily nodded in agreement.

"We'd welcome the Sword Wonder."

"I wanted to learn from you anyway."

"Sounds fun. You could probably put this on your resume later?"

As the unimaginable became reality, Wiji Cheon's eyes widened.

"Really? But I'm only a first-year…"

"What does your year matter? There's no one here better with a sword than you."

Yoo Yiran grinned and patted Wiji Cheon's shoulder. It was a gesture telling him to go for it.

After hesitating for a moment, Wiji Cheon replied with a determined expression, "Understood. I'll do my best!"

Unfortunately, no one knew the consequences of their decision at the time.

The next day, Demon Instructor Wiji Cheon graced the SRS training camp.
