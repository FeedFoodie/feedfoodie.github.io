The next morning, the instant Giyeong kicked open the door to his office, Jongri Mun understood that things had gone horribly wrong.

"......" Despite his hurried entrance, Giyeong's voice failed him, his face a canvas of shock, anger, and a deep, gnawing regret. 

Jongri Mun hazarded a guess, "Don't tell me... was the Ironblood Commander killed?"

Giyeong nodded. "...Yes."

Jongri Mun closed his eyes tightly. The bad news hit him like a thunderbolt, but at least it wasn't a bolt from the blue. They were already aware of the storm that was Crimson Tiger looming over them.

Nevertheless, they hadn't done anything to protect the Ironblood Commander. Instead, they had basically left him standing atop a mountain in the heart of the storm, holding a lightning rod at that.

Jongri Mun sank into his usual seat. "You told me that you were confident you could protect him."

Giyeong bowed his head. Of course, he had been confident. He had fully believed that Smiley and Snakeflower would be more than enough to ambush and kill a fully exposed and unsuspecting tiger.

"What about the Evils you sent? Dead?"

"Probably. We've lost contact with both of them."

"Fuck...!"

Sighing, Jongri Mun leaned back in his chair. *This sucks... but what's done is done. Getting angry won't change anything.* 

"Sit down and give me a full report," he said after some thought.

"Early this morning, we discovered Cheol Mugang's corpse in his office."

"What happened to the First Squad members guarding him? Did Crimson Tiger kill them all too?"

"No. For some reason, they vacated their posts last night."

"What? Why?"

"We're still investigating that."

Jongri Mun sighed. He already knew how this would play out—the elite martial artists directly under the Evil Coalition Leader would conduct their own investigation, and given the gravity of the situation, another bloodbath was likely to ensue.

His eyes flashed discreetly. "Erase all evidence that the Ten Evils infiltrated the Ironblood Nines," he commanded.

"There was never any evidence to begin with," Giyeong replied.

Jongri Mun nodded. The whole reason they were able to carry out this operation without the Ironblood Commander's knowledge was because the Evils had infiltrated in secret. Still, there was no harm in being cautious.

"The two Evils *must* die as martial artists of the Ironblood Nines," he added firmly.

"Don't worry," Giyeong reassured.

If this matter came to light, neither of them would be able to escape blame. Originally, the Coalition Leader might have been sympathetic to the fact that they had refrained from eliminating Crimson Tiger immediately to reveal the purpose behind his infiltration, but that didn't mean anything if Ironblood Commander was already dead.

"Head Strategist, the Coalition Leader is requesting your presence urgently," Jongri Mun's subordinate reported from outside the office.

"Understood," Jongri Mun acknowledged.

Moments later, Giyeong's own subordinate arrived as well. "Sir, the Coalition Leader has summoned you," he called out.

"Got it, and you're dismissed," Giyeong said.

The two men looked at each other. The entire Evil Coalition was now on high alert, leaving them without much time to act.

"The higher-ups will call for a blockade to capture the assassin. The Seven Ghost Corps will be the first to move, followed by the Soul Chasing Squad and finally the Iron Wall Brigade," Jongri Mun said quietly.

"Of course."

"However, the Ten Evils must absolutely not be mobilized. If they are, Crimson Tiger won't be able to escape, and we cannot let him be caught."

Since only they could command the Ten Evils, if Crimson Tiger was captured and interrogated, their involvement would definitely be exposed.

Giyeong nodded. "Understood. I'll find an excuse to delay their deployment as long as possible."

Jongri Mun let out a heavy sigh. *Fate works in strange ways, huh?*

In the end, just like two years ago, he was saving Crimson Tiger's life once again.

Giyeong sighed as well. "This entire disaster is my responsibility," he admitted dejectedly.

He had severely underestimated Crimson Tiger. Instead of two, he should have sent four, five... no, all of the Ten Evils. 

*Damn it! If only I hadn't let my pride get in the way!*

If the Association had the Twelve Phantoms, then the Coalition had the Ten Evils. He had wanted to prove that two ordinary Evils were enough to take down the strongest Phantom... but that was a mistake. He should have at least forced Slayer and Wraith to abandon their current missions and join in.

Jongri Mun watched as Giyeong ground his teeth in chagrin. "Giyeong, do you know what's even more foolish than making a mistake?" he suddenly remarked.

Giyeong blinked in confusion. "What is it?"

"Regretting a mistake you've already made," Jongri Mun replied, his eyes gleaming. "So let's clean up this mess... and then strike back at our enemies!"

Crimson Tiger's ragged breathing echoed through the forest as Soeun and Yeon watched from above, perched on a tree and hiding among the branches. 

Their position was perfect, completely concealed from below. It was a location secured by Yeon, a master of stealth, but even if it wasn't, no one below would have been looking up. 

No, the surviving pursuers had their eyes fixed on one person, and one person alone. Anyone who had dared even glance upward was already dead.

Crimson Tiger, bent halfway over, panted heavily. "Huff... huff... huff."

The two women on the tree tensed with worry. The number of corpses scattered around was uncountable, and blood flowed like a river across the ground.

Even though Crimson Tiger stood defenseless, gasping for air, the Seven Ghost Corps' warriors surrounding him did not dare to charge recklessly. The six who thought they had seen an opening and pounced just moments ago had already joined the ranks of the dead.

Still, though fear gripped them, none retreated. They had never been taught to retreat.

"Attack!" the commander shouted.

Swords from all directions swooped down on Crimson Tiger.

SWISH! SWOOSH! WHOOSH!

Before Soeun's eyes, dozens of gleaming blades flashed. Crimson Tiger's swordsmanship took so many risks that she could hardly bear to watch, yet so mesmerizing that she couldn't look away.

SPLURT!

Immediately after slicing the throat of an opponent charging from the front, Crimson Tiger stepped on the dying man's shoulder and soared into the air.

SPLATTER!

Blood erupted from the chests and necks of two men rushing in from behind.

SWISH! SWISH!

Still airborne, Crimson Tiger arched backward, narrowly dodging two swords as they grazed past his face.

SPLAT! CRACK!

Two severed arms, still clutching their swords, were sent spinning through the air.



Crimson Tiger had cut down the arms of two martial artists attacking from behind before landing on the ground. At that moment, throwing knife flew from his hand.

SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!

Of the three men charging forward, two collapsed mid-run, their bodies twisting as they fell.

SSSSHT! THUNK!

The last man was pinned to a tree by Crimson Tiger's sword.

"Huff... huff!"

Crimson Tiger's labored breathing rang out.

His legs gave way, and he collapsed to the ground. His qi was completely drained, he could barely lift a finger. Throughout the entire escape, he had never once used the Asura's Eight Narakas. That technique was immensely powerful but drained qi at an extreme rate. The swordsmanship he had relied on thus far was the same practical swordsmanship he had used on previous missions. He had conserved his qi, but there had simply been too many foes to cut down.

"Huff... huff!"

His breathing showed no signs of calming.

Drowsiness loomed over him. If he closed his eyes, he would fall asleep immediately.

Crimson Tiger struggled to stay conscious.

The sound of his own breathing grew louder in his ears.

With each ragged breath, Crimson Tiger was pulled back to that day long ago.

***

"Huff... huff!"

Cold wind seeped into his lungs, making even breathing difficult.

Every step forward felt unbearable.

The higher he climbed the mountain, the fiercer the blizzard became.

He bent his upper body slightly and looked down at Seohyeon, nestled in his arms. She was three years old now, swaddled in blankets and held tightly against his chest.

She wouldn't be cold. He was continuously channeling inner arts into her to keep her warm, but his own body was paying the price. He felt like he could collapse at any moment.

Had he been alone, he might not have endured, but he couldn't stop.

WHOOOOSH...

The snowstorm was so intense that he couldn't open his eyes.

He turned back for a moment. The mountains below stretched endlessly into the abyss.

"Hyeon, just hold on a little longer."

The child was asleep.

When the wind subsided, he turned forward again.

One step at a time, he continued climbing. There was a thought that always sustained him in moments like this, pain could be endured. What couldn't be endured were the consequences of failing to endure. He comforted himself with that thought as he pressed on.

At last, he reached his destination.

Near the mountain's peak stood a small hut. The merciless snowfall had finally ceased.

He knocked on the door weakly. "Is anyone there?"

The door creaked open, and an old man stepped out. He was gaunt, with a head full of white hair, and his gaze was ice-cold as he looked at him. "Who are you?" he asked.

"Are you Senior Sagong?"

"I am Sagong Hu (司空厚)."

"May I come in for a moment?"

Sagong Hu glanced at Seohyeon in his arms. His expression made it clear, if he had come alone, the answer would have been no.

"Come in."

"Thank you."

He followed the old man inside. Perhaps because of the intense cold outside, the interior felt incredibly warm. His body relaxed all at once.

Sagong Hu threw a log onto the brazier and said, "Lay the child down over there."

"Thank you."

Carefully, he placed Seohyeon on the bedding. As always, she wore a beautiful smile as she slept.

Sagong Hu glanced at her and asked, "The child looks exhausted. Has she eaten anything?"

"I fed her before we left the village below."

"And that's all she's had until now?"

"Yes."

"You fool! Do you think children are like adults? Dragging her up a mountain in this weather!"

Sagong Hu busied himself gathering food. He simply watched him move about.

Sagong Hu prepared porridge, then woke Seohyeon and fed her the warm meal. Still half-asleep, she rubbed her eyes but ate obediently. She had been starving.

As she chewed, Seohyeon looked at him and smiled. He smiled back.

Feeding Seohyeon porridge made by a grumpy old hermit at the peak of a mountain? And just standing by, watching it happen?

Not a chance. That wasn't who he was. Nevertheless, he had remained still for one reason.

Sagong Hu was the Medicine Immortal (醫仙), the greatest physician of their time.

Once she had finished eating, Seohyeon drifted back to sleep.

"Could it be...?" Sagong Hu placed a hand on her forehead. Then he held her wrist and checked her pulse. Soon, his expression hardened. "When did you notice?"

"Just after her first birthday."

Sagong Hu shook his head. "As you've probably been told, this disease is incurable."

"That's why I came to you, Senior. I have carried my child across thousands of miles to find you."

For the past two years, he had traveled across the Central Plains, seeking out every renowned physician, but they had all shaken their heads, unable to cure Seohyeon.

Still, he hadn't given up. No, he couldn't give up. The moment he gave up on her, he would be giving up on his own life as well.

Throughout his search, there was one name he had heard more than any other. Everyone had said the same thing, if anyone could do it, it would be the Medicine Immortal, Sagong Hu.

However, they had also said something else, that unfortunately, Sagong Hu no longer practiced medicine.

Sure enough, Sagong Hu kept shaking his head.

"Please, save her."

"It's difficult."

For the first time, he didn't say it was impossible, just difficult.

To everyone else, it was an impossibility, but to him, it was merely difficult.

"I will do anything you ask. If you want my life, I will give it to you."

"It's still difficult."

"If there is someone you want dead, I will kill them for you. Just say the word!"

"If you're going to talk nonsense, then leave at once!"

He was on the verge of driving me out, so I knelt down.

CLANG!

I drew my sword and placed it against my neck. "If you can save my child, I will die right here. If you refuse, I will kill her and take my own life..."

SMACK!

Sagong Hu rushed forward and slapped me.

I didn't dodge.

Sagong Hu shouted furiously, "Are you going to burden your child with the sin of being killed by her own parent? Never let such words leave your mouth again!"

"I'm sorry." I bowed my head.

"Leave at once. I am no longer the Medicine Immortal. I do not heal people anymore."

I rose to my feet and placed the bundle I had been carrying on my back onto the table. "Open it."

"What is this?" Sagong Hu frowned and unwrapped the bundle. "...Gasp!"

Sagong Hu let out a sharp breath and stepped back. Inside the bundle was a human head.

Sagong Hu's expression turned furious as he roared, "What is the meaning of this?!"

I spoke calmly, "Look closely."

"What?" Sagong Hu inspected the head carefully. Then, his body jolted in shock. "Could it be?"

Sagong Hu's expression changed rapidly. Joy, elation, sorrow, and anguish, every emotion surged at once. Finally, tears welled in Sagong Hu's eyes and rolled down his face.

"H-how... did you know?"

The head belonged to the man who had murdered his family. He had been a high-ranking master of the Evil Coalition.

It had happened in Sagong Hu's youth. He had struggled desperately for revenge but had failed to kill the man. Sagong Hu had no talent in martial arts.

So instead, he dedicated himself to what he excelled at, medicine. For the sake of his revenge.

By the time he became the Medicine Immortal, by the time he had saved the lives of powerful warriors who could avenge him, his enemy had vanished without a trace. No matter how much he searched, he could not find him.

From that day on, Sagong Hu abandoned medicine. Using medicine for the sake of revenge had already been a trial too heavy to bear, but failing even in that revenge had stripped him of the will to live, yet today, that vengeance had finally been fulfilled.

Sagong Hu remained silent for a long moment. "Did you kill him yourself?"

I nodded. Although it was my Master who had scoured the Central Plains to track him down, I was the one who finished him off.

Sagong Hu studied me carefully. "You're injured."

My body was a complete mess.

"It doesn't matter."

Sagong Hu let out a deep sigh and retrieved a small pill from a cabinet, holding it out to me. "Take this first. We need to have this conversation with a clear mind."

"Then I won't refuse." I swallowed the pill and steadied myself. As my energy circulated through my body, warmth returned to my face, and my mind grew sharper.

Only then did Sagong Hu continued, "There is only one way."

"What is it?"

"If the child can survive until she turns ten, there may be a chance. At that time, I can spend a hundred days using Great Meridian Displacement Art (移穴大法) and the Celestial Reversal Needle Art (四象逆鍼術) to unblock her sealed meridians. However, she must be at least ten to endure the treatment. Right now, she is not only far from ten, she may not even survive a few more months."

The mention of a few months made my heart sink. Tears welled in my eyes.

Sagong Hu said, "I will try. I will do everything I can to keep her alive until she reaches ten."

"Thank you. Truly, thank you." I rejoiced.

Sagong Hu shook his head. "It's too soon to be happy. The real problem starts now."

"What problem?"

"First, this will cost an enormous amount of money. I intend to use miracle herbs to forcibly suppress the disease's progression."

"I will do whatever it takes..."

"Shut your mouth!" Sagong Hu cut me off again. "If you obtain money through unrighteous means, I will never treat this child. Even if you avenged me. Even if you kill me for it, I will not change my mind."

Sagong Hu was serious, he was speaking from the depths of his heart.

Sagong Hu continued, "If this child is to be saved, we will need the help of the heavens. If you resort to even a single act of evil to earn money, the heavens will turn their back on you."

"I understand. I will earn money only by punishing evil."

Only then did Sagong Hu finally extend his hand and help Crimson Tiger to his feet. "Trust that I will do my best on my part as well, but whether the child can endure or not... I do not know."

I looked at Seohyeon. "My Hyeon is strong. She will endure."

She spent most of her days sleeping. I was grateful for every moment she woke up and recognized me.

I bowed my head deeply to Sagong Hu. "Then, I leave her in your care."

"Where are you going?"

Without turning back, I answered, "I'm starting now."

As I opened the door, cold wind rushed inside.

For a brief moment, Seohyeon's eyes fluttered open.

"Daddy! Daddy!" she called out from behind me.

I did not turn back. I didn't have the strength to leave if I saw her face.

Outside, the blizzard raged once more, and I stepped forward into it.

This was the first step of my new life.

*Just wait a little longer. I will save you, no matter what!*

"Haa... haa!"

Crimson Tiger returned to reality, crossing seven years of time.

The child had endured well until now.

Master had been watching over Seohyeon. That was the only reason he could focus on this mission. Otherwise, he would never have been able to bear the anxiety and longing.

In the past seven years, he had only seen Seohyeon twice. He had deliberately stayed away, afraid that someone might discover her existence. The last time he saw her was two years ago, after a mission had ended. He had barely survived and had longed to see Hyeon, but... damn it! That one time had been enough to leave a trail. He had been so careful. He had been so careful...

Regardless, seven years had passed, and this winter, the grand treatment would finally begin.

Just a little longer. She just needed to endure a little longer.

So far, she had withstood the grueling treatments.

That fragile little girl, in that delicate body, had endured immense pain... Compared to that, this hardship, this suffering, was nothing.

She would survive. No matter what, she would survive.

And he would save his daughter.

Crimson Tiger forced his heavy eyelids open, clenching his teeth.

Watching him, Soeun flinched without realizing it. She wanted to go down and help him up. She wanted to wrap her arms around his exhausted shoulders and ask if he was okay.

Yeon tightened her arms around Soeun. It was a signal, to stay put for now.

Soeun had not known how harsh and treacherous their path would be when they first left Ryeon.

They were only a day's distance from the Association's territory, yet the enemy's pursuit had reached its peak. The foes kept coming.

Escaping the Evil Coalition had been easy. Up until that point, they had still been martial artists of the Ironblood Nines.

The real trial had begun after they left the Evil Coalition.

Surround the perimeter.

Until now, Soeun had thought surround the perimeter simply meant layering many people in a siege, but she had been wrong. The Evil Coalition's surround the perimeter was terrifyingly meticulous and calculated.

Once spotted by an enemy, they would encounter five different groups within an hour. Those groups had a flawless communication network, knew no retreat, and were relentless in their pursuit.

Even with the skills required to join the Ironblood Nines, she knew she wouldn't have lasted half a day on her own.

Crimson Tiger had been fighting an unending wave of enemies without a single moment's rest.

Even when it seemed he had no strength left to lift his sword, he somehow drew upon a force beyond reason.

At first, Soeun thought it was just Crimson Tiger's physical abilities. Maybe that had been true at the start, but not anymore. Now, he was holding on through sheer willpower. And to Soeun, this was no longer just about martial arts, it was proof of how strong the human spirit could be, but it wasn't only Crimson Tiger who was extraordinary. Even now, Yeon was protecting her.

Without Yeon's stealth techniques, no matter how skilled Crimson Tiger was, Soeun would have died first. Crimson Tiger fought, and Yeon hid her. Their teamwork was flawless.

Yeon, scanning their surroundings, spoke quickly.

"Let's go down."

The two women leaped from the tree.

Soeun asked worriedly, "Are you okay?"

Crimson Tiger was not okay. His qi was depleted, and the wound on his chest had reopened, causing blood to flow again.

Over a dozen new wounds had appeared. Yeon stripped off Crimson Tiger's outer robe and swiftly applied medicine to the injuries before wrapping them again. She had done this countless times before.

Crimson Tiger forced a reassuring smile and nodded once.

He tried not to speak to Soeun more than necessary.

He was deceiving her into coming with them. What would the Third Young Lady do with her? She wouldn't kill her immediately. She would use her to strike at the First Young Lord, but at the end of that scheme... what would happen to her?

Soeun asked, "Who are you?"

"Just one of many martial artists of the Association."

That couldn't be true. If all the Association's martial artists were like this, the Evil Coalition would have been destroyed long ago.

Just as Soeun was about to say something, Yeon hushed her, signaling caution.

Crimson Tiger and Yeon exchanged a nod.

Yeon grabbed Soeun's hand and leapt. The two of them landed back on a tree branch.

Crimson Tiger slowly stood and walked to the side, pulling his sword free from the tree where it had skewered a martial artist. Without Soul Crusher, this journey would have been even more grueling.

As Crimson Tiger turned back, a man emerged from the bushes.

He appeared to be in his mid-twenties, with a playful smirk on his face.

The man grinned.

"So this is where you were. I've been looking for you."

He let out a long whistle, a signal to call for reinforcements.

Crimson Tiger's expression hardened. This opponent was on a completely different level from the martial artists he had fought until now.

He was a master. Under normal circumstances, Crimson Tiger could have handled him, but in his current state, this was an opponent he needed to be extremely cautious of. If this man was the final obstacle, it would be one thing, but he had no way of knowing who else might follow.

The man's aura was unusual.

Warm yet cold, cold yet soft.

He uncorked the gourd at his waist and took a swig.

"Ahh! That's the stuff."

As he tilted his head back to drink, the man suddenly went "Oh?" in surprise.

His gaze lifted to the tree, directly at Yeon and Soeun's hiding place.

He acted as if he had just noticed them, but Crimson Tiger knew, this man had been aware of them from the very beginning.

"What are you two doing up there?"

Crimson Tiger nodded toward the tree.

Yeon and Soeun jumped down.

Crimson Tiger said grimly, "Go ahead without me."

Yeon's eyes wavered. They had come this far together. Only one more day, and they would be in the Association's territory. 

However, the strength of this opponent proved how dangerous the situation had become.

The man grinned. "Sorry to interrupt, but... that's not happening."

Ignoring him, Crimson Tiger shouted firmly, "Go. Now! Don't worry, I'll catch up soon. Just go."

Yeon nodded.

"Uh... Sorry to spoil the mood again, but what I meant is that you should go too," the man added.

Crimson Tiger turned to him in shock.

"The mission still needs to be completed, Crimson Tiger."

Crimson Tiger and Yeon blinked in surprise.

The man smirked as he introduced himself, "Drunken Monkey, the ninth in the zodiac. I came to support you."

Yeon let out a sigh of relief. *Ah! He should have just said so earlier!*

Her heart had been pounding so hard. Even though he had teased them, his arrival was truly a relief.

Crimson Tiger, too, felt a surge of emotion, though he didn't show it outwardly.

Drunken Monkey grinned and asked, "This is our first time meeting, isn't it?"

"It is."

Their gazes met. It didn't feel bad.

CRACK!

Suddenly, a tree snapped as the corpse of an an Evil Coalition martial artist flew through the air and crashed into it.

A woman stepped forward from behind Drunken Monkey. It was Green Snake. 

She waved at Crimson Tiger with a grin, saying, "You're still alive, I see?"

Crimson Tiger chuckled, but Yeon, on the other hand, glared at her coldly.

Green Snake glanced at Yeon. "Ah! Aren't you that unfriendly handler from before?" She glanced down at her own ample figure. "Hmm, isn't your chest a bit lacking?"

Yeon bit her lip in chagrin and looked away.

Green Snake smiled at Crimson Tiger with a playful glint in her eyes. "Get going. We'll hold them off for exactly one hour. Maybe less if things go badly."

Crimson Tiger nodded. "That's enough."

"You owe me a drink later."

"Sure thing."

Drunken Monkey and Green Snake walked side by side toward the pursuing Evil Coalition martial artists.

"The moment they saw Crimson Tiger, I became completely irrelevant," Drunken Monkey complained.

Green Snake snorted. "Obviously. How could a mere drunken monkey compare to a tiger?"

"Oww, that hurts my pride."

Crimson Tiger watched their backs for a moment. It seemed the two were already well-acquainted. He had always been the one providing support, being the one receiving it felt unfamiliar, but it wasn't a bad feeling. The thought that he, too, had comrades... it was almost a first.

Drunken Monkey, walking ahead, suddenly stopped and glanced back at Crimson Tiger. "You survived for seven years, didn't you? Then how about making it ten more?"

Crimson Tiger gave him a questioning look.

Drunken Monkey grinned. "Someone has to do it, don't they? If we hold out long enough, maybe we can make it twenty. That way, at least our juniors can have a little hope. Not that anyone needs hope more than I do."

Green Snake chuckled beside him, "You won't last long with all that drinking."

Crimson Tiger smirked, and the three of them exchanged silent glances. There was nothing more to say.

"Let's meet again."

There was no need for sorrow in parting. If they were friends, they would meet again. Gratitude could wait until then.

As the sound of wailing blades filled the air behind them, Crimson Tiger and the two women sprinted forward.

***

Five days later, Crimson Tiger and Soeun arrived at the Association's main headquarters. Once they reached the Association's territory, Yeon had moved separately.

Neither of them talked. Even after changing into new martial arts uniforms, Crimson Tiger still carried the scent of blood.

Soeun felt like she was in a dream. She couldn't believe they had actually made it out.

The place they had left behind and the place they had arrived at, neither looked any different in her eyes. Merchants called out to customers, children ran and played. A woman with a sword, a young man carrying a dao, and a middle-aged martial artist with fists as large as a child's head, all of them simply walking their own paths.

She stopped in her tracks and lifted her head. "Is that where my father is?"

Not knowing what to say, Crimson Tiger simply nodded. He felt only guilt toward her.

Neither she nor he knew what fate awaited them now.

Crimson Tiger followed her gaze. The grand structures of the Association stretched into the distance, looming more ominously than even the Evil Coalition's.

The survival instinct that had protected him for so long whispered to him.

The real battle was only just beginning.
