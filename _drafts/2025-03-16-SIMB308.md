THUNK!

A thick book fell onto the table with a dull thud.

Wang Bal brushed the dust off its cover with his hand and said, "This is the Murim Alliance personnel roster."

"...There's more than I expected," Baek Suryong remarked as he flipped open the cover of the brick-thick volume. The personnel roster didn't simply list names. On the first page, the name of the Murim Alliance Chairman, Yayul Hwang, was written.

"Yes, it contains the names, residences, martial arts, and personal details of every martial artist affiliated with the Murim Alliance, including their close associates," Wang Bal explained.

"I see..."

Baek Suryong examined the roster's contents with interest. As a member of the Ten Supremes and the Chairman of the Murim Alliance, Yayul Hwang's section was particularly extensive and detailed.

Wang Bal whispered, "You must forget everything you see and hear here once this is over."

"Of course."

The personnel roster was a top-secret document of the Beggars' Gang, never to be shown to outsiders. It was a treasure of the intelligence division, containing every piece of information they had gathered so far. However, since the culprit behind the attack on the Boss was found to be connected to the Murim Alliance, Wang Bal had decided to fully cooperate with the Azure Dragon Hero.

*He still seems uneasy.*

Baek Suryong decided to ease Wang Bal's concerns a little.

"The Alliance probably has some idea. They must already suspect that the Beggars' Gang possesses the personnel roster. The Murim Alliance likely has something similar as well."

"...Have you seen it yourself?"

"Do I need to? Every one of the Nine Sects One Gang must have something like this. Of course, the Beggars' Gang's version is undoubtedly the most detailed. The Little Beggar must already be aware of this too, isn't he?"

"......"

Even the Blood Cult kept a log of key figures in the murim. Knowing one's enemies was crucial for waging war effectively.

While the Nine Sects One Gang were not outright enemies, they were undoubtedly competitors vying for dominance over the murim. Among them, the Beggars' Gang was the largest intelligence organization of the orthodox sects.

That was why Baek Suryong had first demanded the Murim Alliance personnel roster from Wang Bal.

*This is even more detailed than I imagined.*

Of course, that was not the original purpose of the Beggars' Gang when they compiled it, but depending on how the personnel roster he was reading now was used, it could expose an opponent's weaknesses, or utterly destroy them.

*In the end, it depends on how you use it.*

Baek Suryong grinned and quickly flipped through the book. It seemed to be organized by rank. After the Chairman, the next section contained the personal details of the five Commanders. While their sections were not as extensive as the Chairman's, they still held information that would not be easy to obtain elsewhere.

Baek Suryong closed the book and handed it back to Wang Bal. "I've finished reading."

"...Already?"

"I don't need to read all of it. I only checked the details of the five Commanders, the captains under them, and the lieutenants of the Alliance."

The Poison Demon's Mortal Venom had been used. If the Blood Cult had planted a rat or informant within the Murim Alliance, they would likely hold at least the rank of Captain or higher, so there was no need to check those below that level for now.

*The most suspicious person is... the one who wields the most power in the Chairman's absence, or someone close to that person.*

"Among the five Commanders, who is currently staying in the Murim Alliance?"

"...The Elimination Corps Commander and the Heavenly Justice Commander," Wang Bal's expression hardened as he answered.

{sep}

In the Murim Alliance Chairman's office, one of the five Commanders was taking care of the Chairman's duties in his absence.

"Hwaaaahm. Why the hell do I have to act as the Chairman's proxy?" The Elimination Corps Commander Ryu Seol stretched as she let out a long yawn.

Even though martial artists of the unorthodox sects feared her, calling her the One-Eyed Demon Blade, right now, her feet were sprawled out on the table, and her half-lidded eyes were dazed with sleep. She kept twisting her body restlessly as if she were itchy, her movements as flexible as a cat's. Her jet-black uniform was tailored snugly enough to be comfortable, and two sleek black Dao hung from her waist.

The Vice Commander of the Elimination Corps, Moyong Jun, sitting across from Ryu Seol, buried in a pile of documents, lifted his head with a smirk. "...Why? Because you're the only one among the Commanders who doesn't give a rat's ass about the Chairman's seat. Also. since I'm the one actually doing all the work, what the hell are you so tired from?"

Both Ryu Seol and Moyong Jun looked to be in their mid-to-late twenties, or at most, early thirties, but in reality, they were more than a decade older.

At her Vice Commander's nagging, Ryu Seol picked at her ear and grumbled, "No work, huh? Do you have any idea how heavy this seat is? No, of course you wouldn't. A lowly Vice Commander wouldn't have a clue."

"...With a Commander like you, no wonder the Elimination Corps has the worst reputation among the five armies for being ignorant and lacking in upbringing."

The Heavenly Justice Corps, the Ironblood Corps, the Righteous Corps, the Divine Sword Corps, and the Elimination Corps were the five major armies of the Murim Alliance. At major murim events, the five Commanders received the same treatment as the Sect Leaders of the Nine Sects One Gang, but of course, that didn't mean they were actually on equal footing.

Each Commander was a master from the Nine Sects One Gang or the Five Great Clans, wielding considerable authority in their own right, but the Elimination Corps was different. Unlike the other corps, the Elimination Corps had few members from the Nine Sects One Gang or the Five Great Clans. And the ones who did come from them were usually outcasts. Some were even freelancers, or former unorthodox sect members who had defected to the Murim Alliance.

Because of this, the Elimination Corps wasn't well-regarded, even among the five armies. They were, in short, the black sheep.

Ryu Seol was the Commander of this troublesome, much-maligned corps, was a woman who had repeatedly proven the Elimination Corps' worth through sheer skill, but that was only on the battlefield.

Now, being forced to act as the Chairman's proxy, Ryu Seol lay sprawled on the table like a lazy cat. "This is boring and annoying. How does that old man put up with this crap every day? He's got an even worse temper than me..."

"Enough whining, just stamp these damn documents already!" Moyong Jun kicked his Commander's rear without hesitation, then shoved a stack of documents he had already reviewed toward her.

Ryu Seol lazily pulled out the Murim Alliance Chairman's official seal from her inner pocket. Before the Chairman had left on his tour of the Five Great Academies, he had warned her in no uncertain terms that if she lost it or used it carelessly, he would crack her skull open.

"Tch, that old geezer always treats me like I'm the easiest target," she grumbled, skimmed through the documents Moyong Jun handed over, then stamped them one after another with loud thuds.

Moyong Jun's face turned pale. "You lunatic! I told you to actually read them before stamping!"

"I'm sure my capable Vice Commander must have already checked everything thoroughly."

"You really are..." At her utterly irresponsible response, Moyong Jun shook his head and sighed deeply. "Do whatever you want. In the end, it's you who'll get your skull cracked by the Chairman, not me."

"Fufu. And you think your head will be safe if mine gets cracked?"

"Damn it! If you weren't the Commander, I swear...!"

They bickered like this every day, but they had shared meals for over ten years and crossed countless battlefields together, risking their lives side by side.

"Let's just finish up and get wasted toni..." A fierce energy flashed in the Elimination Corps Commander's single eye as Ryu Seol swiftly adjusted her posture, glaring at the man who had barged in without permission. "Just what bastard walks into the Chairman's office without so much as a knock?"

"Are you saying I need your permission now?" The newcomer scowled.

He was a middle-aged man with a sturdy build. His every step was precise, and though he wasn't overtly exuding his presence, he carried an aura of weight and solidity. Even in the face of the killing intent Ryu Seol emitted, he remained completely unfazed.

Ryu Seol snorted. "The Commander of the Elimination Corps lecturing others about etiquette? Now that's funny."

The Heavenly Justice Commander Jin Gwang was a secular disciple of Shaolin, and personally taught the monastery's secret techniques by the Abbot, who regretting that such outstanding talent would go to waste. Additionally, he was a son of the World Trading Company Chairman, one of the Ten Great Companies. Though he had not inherited the company since he was not the eldest son, he played a crucial role in maintaining the close relationship between the Murim Alliance and the World Trading Company.

"Acting Chairman. If you're struggling with the workload, I can take over for you."

"I'm fine. Our Vice Commander is doing an excellent job."

The Heavenly Justice Commander was also openly aiming for the next Murim Alliance Chairman position. Moreover, he was considered the most likely candidate for it.

Ryu Seol, her feet propped up on the table, chuckled. "You might as well give up on the Chairman's seat. That old man is more energetic than ever now that the Blood Cult has reappeared. He's probably planning to hold onto it for another hundred years."

"......" The Heavenly Justice Commander looked at her with an expression of exasperation. "You're almost forty, yet you're still so immature."

"And you're almost fifty, yet you still can't let go of your thirst for power. Is that really okay for a Shaolin student? All is vanity. Didn't the monks ever teach you that?"

"...Forget it. I didn't come here to argue with you."

"Then why are you here?" Ryu Seol asked, knowing that the Heavenly Justice Commander wouldn't have come here for no reason.

"Have you heard about the Azure Dragon Hero?"

"Of course I have."

"I'm curious to see how strong he is."

Just yesterday, news had spread that the Azure Dragon Hero had appeared in Wuhan, but he had vanished shortly after arriving, leaving his companions behind.

While gossipers were busy talking about the Lightning Dragon Hero Namgung Su, the Murim Alliance was far more concerned about the Azure Dragon Hero. After all, he was the Chief Martial Arts Instructor of the Murim Alliance.

Ryu Seol's eyes gleamed. She had no particular grudge against the Azure Dragon Hero. In fact, she had no feelings about him at all. If he had sufficient skill, she would acknowledge him as the new Chief Instructor. If not, she would ignore him.

However, there was no way she would entrust the training of the Elimination Corps to someone incompetent.

*Since the Chairman acknowledged him, he shouldn't be weak...*

The seat left vacant by the death of the Blue Sky Sword King Namgung Jaehak. The Chairman had declared that the Azure Dragon Hero was fit to take that place.

Ryu Seol smirked and muttered, "The lowest seat of the Ten Supremes, huh..."

"It's just wordplay. The Ten Supremes isn't something the Chairman can decide on his own," the Heavenly Justice Commander denied firmly.

Ryu Seol agreed with him. There were plenty of experts who didn't recognize the Chairman's authority, and that included the five Commanders, especially since most of them were strong enough to contend for the empty seat of the Ten Supremes themselves.

She shrugged. "I heard he beat Cho Cheonsang in a duel. That's not too bad, right?"

"You think the Heavenly Martial Commander is comparable to us?"

"Not even close." Ryu Seol grinned. The Proud Sword of Mount Hua, Cho Cheonsang, was unmatched in his generation, but the five Commanders were older and stronger than him.

The Heavenly Justice Commander nodded. "Regardless of the Azure Dragon Hero's martial arts skill, I cannot accept him as the Murim Alliance Chief Instructor."

"Why?"

"Because he knows nothing about the Heavenly Justice Corps." A sharp light flickered in the Heavenly Justice Commander's eyes. "The Heavenly Justice Corps has its own unique ways. We won't suddenly entrust our training to an outsider. The Elimination Corps must feel the same, don't they?"

"That makes sense, but..."

If it was just about training, the fiercely unruly Elimination Corps would be an even bigger problem. Aside from the Murim Alliance Chairman and the Elimination Corps Commander, they hardly listened to any other superior.

Ryu Seol furrowed her brows. "So what's your point? Are you suggesting we drive him out when he arrives?"

"We should treat him as an honored guest, but we must also make it clear that he is only a guest. I've already gained agreement from the Ironblood, Righteous, and Divine Sword Corps."

"......" Ryu Seol had no strong opinion on the matter. "There's a chance he won't come at all. If he was going to, he would've arrived yesterday."

"...He'll come." The Heavenly Justice Commander spoke with certainty.

Ryu Seol didn't quite understand why he was so sure, but moments later, his words became reality.

BOOOM!

A deafening explosion shook the air, and both of them turned their heads in the same direction.

""Intruder!""

Along with the shouts of martial artists, a commotion erupted.

The expressions of both Commanders hardened.

"An intruder?"

"In the Murim Alliance?"

Like bolts of lightning, the two shot toward the source of the explosion.

"Who's there!"

"Stop right there!"

A little past the Murim Alliance's main gate, over a hundred martial artists had surrounded a single man. The ones in uniform white martial arts attire were from the Heavenly Justice Corps, while those in black martial arts uniforms, with varied styles, belonged to the Elimination Corps.

With the shattered main gate behind him, the young man in the center of the encirclement grinned. "Nice to meet you."

A face so strikingly handsome it was almost unreal. A relaxed posture with hands clasped behind his back, perfectly matching his pristine blue martial arts uniform. A splendid treasured sword hanging from his waist.

Even without an introduction, everyone could guess his identity.

"I'm the newly appointed Chief Instructor, Baek Suryong. Apologies if I startled you."

"......" At his shameless self-introduction, no one could find the words to respond.

Baek Suryong looked around and nodded. "It seems you've done a decent job preparing for enemy intrusions. There are a few issues, but we can work on them one by one."

He casually assessed the martial artists surrounding him as he walked forward. Faced with a superior rather than an enemy, the Murim Alliance martial artists hesitated and instinctively stepped back.

Ryu Seol asked in disbelief, "That's him, right? The Azure Dragon Hero."

"...Looks like it," the Heavenly Justice Commander stiffly nodded.

"Are you checking our training conditions? Is that why you broke the main gate?" Ryu Seol asked.

"That's right!"

For a while, the two commanders were stunned speechless. It seemed the newly appointed Murim Alliance Chief Instructor was far crazier than they had imagined.

Suddenly, the Acting Murim Alliance Chairman proxy burst into laughter, "Aha... Ahahahaha!"
