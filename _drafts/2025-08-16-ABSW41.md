Yagong stepped into the heavily guarded manor where he had previously met the Third Young Lady. His stride was brisk, his urgency reflected in every step.

When he saw Ju Hwayin, he didn't bother with greetings. "You must have heard the news, right?"

Ju Hwayin nodded. "I heard you caught one of the enemy's spies?"

"That's right."

For seven years, the spy had managed to infiltrate the Association's most sensitive department without being caught. If that wasn't bad enough, the Myriad Secrets Cave had come dangerously close to being breached because of him.

This was a grave enough matter to justify the resignations of both the Mystery Pavilion Master and the Radiant Hall Director, so Yagong rushed straight to Ju Hwayin as if his feet were on fire.

"You very nearly caused a great disaster," she said softly.

Yagong bowed his head. "It was a gross oversight on our part. I won't make any excuses."

When he first heard the report, he was so shocked that his legs gave out. How long had he devoted himself to achieving success? This incident was the worst kind of setback, the kind that could render all his efforts meaningless in an instant.

Ju Hwayin softened her tone. "In war, mistakes are inevitable. Raise your head."

To be fair, what happened wasn't entirely Yagong and Gu Yangseo's fault. The Evil Coalition had spent decades orchestrating this scheme. Trying to thwart it was akin to stopping a natural disaster.

"I'm not afraid of stepping down," Yagong said. "But all the groundwork I've laid out for you, Young Lady, will be wasted."

He looked troubled, but the truth was that he feared losing his position more than anything else. He had already given the Great Cycle Pill to Crimson Tiger and bet everything on this course of action. Turning back was no longer an option.

"It's not entirely without hope," Ju Hwayin remarked. "I heard that, while dealing with the spy, you eliminated four of the Ten Evils. That's no small feat. Was it Crimson Tiger's work?

A spark lit in Yagong's eyes. This was exactly the point he had been hoping she would raise. Nodding eagerly, he replied, "That's right, and he's already been rewarded."

The reward was a generous fifty thousand taels, the largest sum in the history of the Association, but Crimson Tiger was worth the investment.

"If we highlight that achievement, you may be able to avoid dismissal. Besides, I'm sure Senior Brother doesn't want the Radiant Hall Director replaced either. With our combined influence, we could limit the punishment to a pay cut."

"I'll put my trust in you, Young Lady," Yagong said, bowing his head. A temporary pay cut was nothing to him. If the Third Young Lady became the successor, his future would be secured. After that, wealth would surely follow.

Ju Hwayin reassured, "Don't worry, I'll do everything I can to help you."

Yagong's bow deepened. "I pledge my loyalty to you!"

After disclosing a few more details about the spy incident, he left the manor.

Ju Hwayin watched Yagong leave with a satisfied smile on her face. Despite his prior pledges of allegiance, he had always exhibited a sense of self-importance while in her presence. Only now had she truly brought him to his knees.

Lee Dansim, who had been guarding the entrance, stepped inside. "If it weren't for Crimson Tiger, we'd be in serious trouble right now."

Ju Hwayin nodded. If the Myriad Secrets Cave had been breached, the Evil Coalition would have gained access to even her and her senior brother's personal information.

Furthermore, that would have only been the beginning. If every secret spanning the Central Plains had been exposed, the loss of manpower and resources would have been immeasurable. The Association's covert organizations would have had to be disbanded, and warehouses across the land looted. Every carefully planted spy would be eliminated.

Rebuilding the entire system would cost not fifty thousand taels, but millions.

"The Evil Coalition must be in chaos right now," she said.

"Yes, the atmosphere there must be absolutely dreadful, " Lee Dansim chimed in.

"It's not something to celebrate, though. They might retaliate."

"Shouldn't we be doing the same?" Lee Dansim's face flushed with rage. "They snuck four of the Ten Evils into the deepest part of the Inner Court. If that's not a blatant declaration of war, then I don't know what is."

Fortunately, since all of the spies were killed, this matter would be quietly buried. Both sides knew it was best to let matters of espionage fade into obscurity.

Ju Hwayin reached for her cold tea and took a sip.

"We also have new information. Crimson Tiger's new undercover identity is a student at the Tempest Academy," Lee Dansim reported.

SPURT!

"The Tempest Academy?" Ju Hwayin spluttered, spraying tea everywhere in surprise. She set the cup down, frowning slightly. "That's strange."

"Yes, it's quite the coincidence."

"Any sign Senior Brother is involved?"

"None so far."

KNOCK KNOCK!

A subordinate came in with a message that Lee Dansim quickly passed on.

"Crimson Tiger wants to meet you in person."

"Me?"

"Yes, but he didn't say why."

"It's probably about the academy," Ju Hwayin replied. "...Okay, I'll meet with him."

CRASH!

A jade ornament that had sat on Jongri Mun's desk for years flew into the wall and shattered into pieces.

Normally, Jongri Mun despised people who smashed things when they were angry. He had never expressed his frustration that way himself. However, he could not control himself this time. Never before had he been this incensed.

Giyeong silently observed his superior, trying to read his expression. An immense amount of preparation, effort, and funds had gone into this matter, yet three of the Ten Evils had died alongside Enigma.

After looking down at the broken fragments in silence for a while, Jongri Mun asked, "Who did it?"

"We don't know. We've lost contact with everyone who was deployed. We're currently sending in new personnel to assess the situation..."

"Enough!"

Giyeong shut his mouth.

"How could this happen?!" Jongri Mun squeezed his eyes shut. The details of the job, why it failed, and who killed them were irrelevant now. The only thing that mattered was that they had failed spectacularly.

A flash of killing intent crossed his eyes as a sudden thought crossed his mind.

"No way... could it have been Crimson Tiger?" he muttered.

Giyeong replied, "I doubt it. According to Enigma's final report, Crimson Tiger was busy pursuing Gwe Sansan and eliminated him far from the Association. There's no way he could have rushed back in time."

SLAM!

Jongri Mun slammed his fist on the desk again. "Then who the fuck cut down four of the Ten Evils, including Slayer, the strongest Evil, at the same damned time?"

Giyeong suggested cautiously, "The Radiant Hall Protectors, perhaps?"

"......" Jongri Mun considered the possibility. According to the information provided by Enigma, all of the Radiant Hall Protectors were at least peak-level masters, but so what? He didn't think Enigma was so foolish as to execute the operation without a plan to take them down.

"Or... could the Association Chairman himself have intervened?"

"Don't be ridiculous!" Jongri Mun retorted.

Giyeong fell silent. He knew Jongri Mun had done extensive research on Cheon Ahseong and concluded that the old man had no interest in the Association's affairs. Rather, he was the type of martial arts fanatic who wouldn't bother dirtying his hands with the insignificant Ten Evils even if the Association were to fall.

Jongri Mun sighed. Regardless of who the culprit was, the deed was done. Their greatest setback was the loss of Enigma. The other Ten Evils could be replaced on short notice, but Enigma, who had infiltrated the Radiant Hall, was an asset so valuable that not even a transcendent master could replace him.

He groaned, "What am I going to do when the Coalition Leader finds out about this major fuck-up?"

Neung Pungbi was bound to be disappointed, but in any case, this wasn't something that could be kept secret for long. Instead, he needed to focus on accomplishing something extraordinary to offset this failure before the news got out.

He sat and contemplated for a while.

Finally, he barked, "Use all our resources to find out who the woman Crimson Tiger kidnapped is! Do it as quickly as possible! By any means necessary!"

It would have been better if Enigma had at least reported on this before he died, but they hadn't even received that message.

"Understood." Giyeong clasped his hands together briefly in farewell, then quickly exited the room.

Left alone, Jongri Mun sank into his chair. A sense of crisis heavier than ever before pressed down on him.

*Damn it! I won't go down without a fight!*

He repeated the vow over and over again, but it offered him no comfort. He knew better than anyone else how easily power abandoned a person.

Go Wonjeong, Crimson Tiger Kwon Kangho's master, gazed up at the night sky with his hands clasped behind his back. Forgotten memories carried on the moonlight seeped into his deep wrinkles, while the voice of his younger self echoed thunderously in his mind.

\> "Hahahaha! I am Go Wonjeong, the third successor to the Asura King Yama!"

The opponent who had fallen before him was the Dawn Moon Swordsman, Lim Chan.

He was twenty-five at the time. Having already achieved complete mastery of the Asura's Eight Narakas, he had not known defeat since his debut.

Unfortunately, compared to his martial arts prowess, his knowledge of the jianghu was that of a complete novice. No, even that expression was insufficient.

He was a total dumbass who didn't know how despicable and petty his enemies could get. 

And so he had forgiven a total trash of a human being.

"If you ever commit the same act again, I will cut off both your hands and make an example of you."

Mistaking generosity and compassion as the hallmarks of a great martial master and a righteous warrior, he had forgiven someone who should never have been forgiven.

Go Wonjeong sighed. Killing intent brimmed in his eyes as a haunting memory of a past trauma he could never put behind him flashed before his eyes.

\> "No! No! Please! This can't be happening!"

Two corpses lay before his distraught form. His wife and three-year-old son, whom he cherished as much as his own life, had been brutally hacked to pieces alive.

Sitting in a sea of blood, Go Wonjeong shed tears of blood. He thought about slashing his own neck and joining his family in death, but he couldn't bring himself to do it. Even if he were to die, he had to get his revenge first.

He immediately set out to find the murderer with a ferocity and intensity unlike ever before.

Two months later, he found the culprit, the Dawn Moon Swordsman, and beat him to a pulp.

The man clung abjectly to his trouser leg, begging for mercy, and he murdered him in the most brutal way possible. He also killed everyone connected to the crime.

His eyes wild with rage, he intended to kill the Dawn Moon Swordsman's entire family as well. However, he couldn't bring himself to cut down the child who looked up at him pitifully. He also couldn't bring himself to kill the woman pleading for her life.

After that day, Go Wonjeong left the jianghu. Eventually, he roamed the world, often contemplating suicide, yet unable to follow through.

One day, he met a villager who told him that people who committed suicide ended up in hell and couldn't meet those who had passed away before them. It might have just been a story meant to deter him, but he chose to believe it. If the villager was right, and he couldn't see his family even after death, then there was no meaning to killing himself.

He continued his travels. Ironically, the day he first met Kwon Kangho was the anniversary of his wife and child's death, which had occurred several years earlier.

After crying for half a day straight, he had descended from the graveyard and seen a boy rummaging through a trash can. 

Suddenly, the thought struck him that if his son were alive, he would be about that age.

Once that thought entered his mind, he couldn't bring himself to abandon the child, and took him in as his disciple.

*Back then, I only took him in out of guilt.*

Go Wonjeong stopped reminiscing and let out a soft sigh. If he hadn't met Kwon Kangho, he would have spent his entire life resenting the world and himself, living in self-reproach. He would have lived waiting for the day he died, missing his deceased family all the while.

*Heaven must have taken pity on me and sent me a disciple... and Seohyeon, too.*

Tears welled up in his eyes, when suddenly, a clear, bright voice rang out from behind him.

"Grandpa!"

Go Wonjeong turned around and saw Kwon Seohyeon standing behind him. The grief etched on his face vanished instantly, replaced by a bright smile and open arms.

Seohyeon leapt into his embrace. "Grandpa!"

"Hyeon! My precious little one!"

"Hehe, I couldn't sleep."

"The moon is lovely, isn't it? Want to play with Grandpa for a bit before you go back inside?"

"Aren't you tired, Grandpa? You worked all day today."

Go Wonjeong's daily routine was extremely hectic. He helped Sagong Hu with Seohyeon's treatment, inspected the formations, and cultivated medicinal herbs for Seohyeon. On top of that, he did many other chores.

Seohyeon began massaging Go Wonjeong's shoulders with tiny hands that lacked the strength a child her age should have.

"Ah! Right there! Perfect!"

"Hehe, it feels good, right?"

"My Hyeon is the best!"

"I'll do this for you every day!"

There were times when a child matured beyond their years. Usually, this happened when their family was going through a difficult time, when they faced hardship, or when they were very sick, like Seohyeon.

"Grandpa."

"What is it?"

"Daddy's okay, right?"

"Of course." Go Wonjeong stroked Seohyeon's head affectionately. "You miss him, don't you?"

Seohyeon smiled shyly and nodded.

Go Wonjeong's heart ached. He missed his disciple deeply, too, but the thought of how much this little one must miss her father pained him even more.

In an attempt to change the mood, he asked cheerfully, "Seohyeon, what do you want to do with Daddy when you're all better?"

As if the mere thought made her happy, Seohyeon beamed. "More than anything, I want to... I just hope Daddy doesn't have to suffer anymore."

No one had ever told Seohyeon that her father's work was difficult, but she figured it out on her own. After all, children were the most perceptive people in the world.

Go Wonjeong gazed at the moon. "That day will come soon. Winter will be here before we know it. By next spring, my Hyeon will be healthy."

Seohyeon smiled shyly. "That moon... do you think Daddy is looking at it too?"

"Of course."

"Yeah, he's definitely looking at it!"

Seohyeon closed her eyes, clasped her hands together, and made a wish to the moon for her father's well-being.

Go Wonjeong added his own prayer to the one from those small, fragile hands.

*Kangho! Please just hang in there a little longer!*

"What are you looking at so intently?"

Crimson Tiger turned to look at Yeon. "The moon tonight is really beautiful."

*His eyes are moist...* Yeon realized that Crimson Tiger was thinking about his daughter again. "Hey, do you believe that wishes made to the moon come true?" she asked.

"I don't know." Crimson Tiger shrugged. He didn't really believe in superstitions.

"I do. Can you imagine how bleak the night sky would be without the moon? Something so mysterious and beautiful wouldn't be floating up there for no reason." 

Smiling, Yeon clasped her hands together, closed her eyes, and prayed.

Crimson Tiger looked up at the moon. He didn't know whether the moon granted wishes or not, but it was true that looking at it brought him peace. 

*Do people get more sentimental at night? Looking at such a bright moon reminds me of Seohyeon and Master.*

He prayed in his heart for the two of them and Sagong Hu to be healthy and well.

Yeon opened her eyes.

Crimson Tiger asked curiously, "What did you wish for?"

Yeon grinned mysteriously. "It's a secret." 

She was someone who occasionally looked at the moon and made wishes. Until now, she had always wished for herself, but this time, she had wished for Crimson Tiger to be safely reunited with his daughter.

She took a slip of paper out from her pocket. "Your new undercover identity has arrived."

Crimson Tiger read through the details of his new cover identity. As he had requested, he would be a student at the Tempest Academy. 

When he finished, he looked up at Yeon. "Before you go, mind helping me with something?"

Yeon nodded, and Crimson Tiger turned around.

CRUNCH... CRACK...

The sound of his face changing echoed ominously in the still night.

Crimson Tiger had already informed Yeon that he had mastered the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art, but she had politely declined his offer to teach it to her, and he hadn't forced her to learn. While it was extremely useful, learning an advanced martial art always came with risks. 

*Well, I'll get another chance to teach her when the time is right.*

A moment later, he turned back around with a new face.

Yeon quietly studied it. "Isn't the overall impression a bit too strong for a student?"

Crimson Tiger looked at his reflection in the courtyard pond and nodded. "You're right."

The lines of his eyes and jaw gave him an impression that was far too sharp to be a novice martial artist.

"That face would be a perfect fit for an assassin," Yeon joked.

Crimson Tiger chuckled. Creating a face suitable for a Tempest Academy student was more difficult than he had thought. A young, naive face simply did not match his naturally piercing eyes. While trying to create a face that matched his features, he had made it too imposing.

"All right, I'll try again!" He spun around and started creating a new face. Not wanting to waste Yeon's time, though, he asked, "Could you tell me about the Tempest Academy while I'm working on this?"

Yeon always gave him the most accurate and helpful information before a mission.

"Of course," Yeon replied. "The Tempest Academy is fully supported by the Association. While it's an independent institution, in reality, it's no different from a subsidiary of the Association."

"Does every graduate from there join the Association?"

"Not necessarily. They have to pass a test. Every year, the Association holds an aptitude test to select new recruits. Anyway, the academy program lasts for two years. First-year students are called Green Plums, while second-year students are called White Orchids.

"Soeun must be a teacher for the Green Plums."

"That's right. There are four Green Plum classes: Heaven, Earth, Sable, and Yellow. Soeun teaches the Sable clas , and that's the class you'll be joining. Since all of this year's Green Plums have already been admitted, you're enrolling as a transfer student."

Crimson Tiger nodded silently, his back still turned. Occasional cracking and crunching sounds could still be heard as he adjusted his face.

"Most of the students who take the test to join the Association are White Orchids who have completed the two-year course, but some exceptionally talented Green Plums have passed the test as well. There are no restrictions on who can sit for the exam. If a White Orchid fails, they can repeat the year up to two more times. If they still don't pass within that period, however, they aren't given any more chances."

"How many students are in Soeun's class?" Crimson Tiger asked, putting a stop to Yeon's rambling. For this job, he only needed information about the Green Plums.

"Fifteen in total," Yeon answered. "There are several standouts among this year's Green Plums, and one of them is in Soeun's class."

"Who is it?"

"Jaegal Soyeon, a direct descendant of the Jaegal Clan. She's the brightest of the great families' prodigies. Rumor has it that if she wasn't a girl, she would have become the next head of the Jaegal Clan."

"I see," Crimson Tiger acknowledged blankly.

Yeon could tell he wasn't interested in the matter. To him, all of these prodigies were nothing more than a bunch of privileged children.

GRIND...

The sound of Crimson Tiger's bones shifting rang out again. Yeon imagined that it must be painful, but he continued the conversation nonchalantly.

"Are there any teachers to watch out for?"

"The Principal, Bang Hagi, is an unknown figure in the jianghu. My guess is that he's probably connected to the Association. The Vice Principal is the Headwind Blade Ban Yang, a renowned martial artist in the jianghu. He's famous for cutting down all of the Seven Ghost Blade Knaves, who were notorious in the jianghu a decade ago, and the story is still told once in a while."

"I'm all done. How is it this time?"

Crimson Tiger spun around. He was now a handsome young man who looked to be around seventeen.

"Ah!" Yeon gasped in admiration. This eye-catching yet assertive appearance suited Crimson Tiger impeccably. "It's perfect! It suits you so well!"

Crimson Tiger smiled. "That's a relief," he said in a youthful voice.

As he improved his mastery of the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art, his transformations became more perfect. Now, he could change not only his appearance, but also his voice.

Staring intently at the Crimson Tiger, Yeon shook her head. "All the girls are going to fall for you."

"This is what I looked like when I was young."

Yeon's eyes went wide. "Really?"

"No, I'm kidding."

Yeon's spirits sank instantly. *I wanted to see his real face, damn it!* 

Unfortunately, it seemed that Crimson Tiger never used a face that was even remotely similar to his own. That face was saved for one moment only.

Yeon took the details of the cover identity from Crimson Tiger and read it aloud. "Okay, so you're Gyu Ho, the youngest son of the Fujian Gyu Swordsmanship Family, a fake sect we fabricated. The Gyu Swordsmanship Family's fortunes have declined, and they've been recorded as having withdrawn from Fujian. Unless someone deliberately digs into your background, your cover won't be blown. Even if they do, the distance between here and Fujian is so great that, by the time they discover the truth, the situation here will already be resolved."

Crimson Tiger nodded, not at all worried. The Radiant Hall's work was flawless when it came to matters like this. The only thing that worried him was the identity of the assassin Sin Yeongyeong hired.

"I wonder how that bastard will approach her?"

As the First Young Lord had emphasized, the opponent would undoubtedly be a master among masters. If he made a single mistake, Soeun would lose her life. His gut told him that this might be the most difficult mission he had ever undertaken.

Yeon voiced her uncertainty. "There are several possible scenarios. First, he could infiltrate her bedchambers late at night. That's the most common method used by assassins, and I believe it's the most likely method this time as well."

The Crimson Tiger nodded. If this was the assassination method, he could definitely stop it because it was easier to detect an assassin's presence at night. However, this wasn't the only means of killing someone.

Yeon added, "The assassination could also be carried out boldly in plain sight. Truly skilled assassins don't distinguish between day and night."

Crimson Tiger sighed deeply. That would not be easy to anticipate. Protecting someone in a crowded place, especially a bustling marketplace, was no easy task. 

Ultimately, he would have to stand guard over her round the clock. Could he even get any sleep at this rate?

That wasn't even all. What if the enemy approached Soeun disguised as a passerby? What if they killed someone close to Soeun and approached her wearing a human skin mask? While he had a knack for identifying such masks, if the enemy used a supreme disguise art like the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art, he would be completely helpless.

Yeon let out a heavy sigh as well. She was in a similar predicament. Since she had only ever supported Crimson Tiger's missions, her knowledge of bodyguard duties was limited. Nevertheless, she was more confident than anyone when it came to stealth.

She suggested, "For now, I'll guard her surroundings..."

"No!" Crimson Tiger interjected firmly.

"What?"

"This time, you absolutely cannot interfere."

The opponent this time was a top-tier assassin. Although Crimson Tiger trusted Yeon's stealth skills, what if she was discovered by the enemy? They would definitely try to eliminate her first, and she wouldn't be able to defend herself. He couldn't risk exposing her to such danger.

"Then what should we do? You can't protect her all day by yourself, Crimson Tiger."

Crimson Tiger contemplated for a moment, when suddenly, he remembered someone and grinned. "I know an expert bodyguard we could consult."

Dressed in a white martial arts uniform with her hair tied back tightly, White Rabbit stopped her current client, Jin Gigwang, with a firm gaze instead of her usual gentle smile.

Jin Gigwang's scowl was his only answer. Shoving her aside, he strode toward the scene of the fight and shouted, "You cur, you're worse than a dog! Stop bothering that poor woman and face me!" Four men followed behind him. They were bodyguard warriors who had protected Jin Gigwang since he was young.

The Jin Sky Society had recently formed a strategic alliance with the Association, but although the words sounded grand, it actually meant their complete subservience to the Association.

In any case, this was something Jin Sky Society had long desired. The Association offered a few rewards in return, one of which was a special bodyguard. Just this mere fact alone was enough to make the surrounding sects envious. Regardless, White Rabbit's job was supposed to be a formal, one-month bodyguard assignment, but today, Jin Gigwang had decided to meddle in affairs he really shouldn't have.

All eyes in the establishment focused on Jin Gigwang.

A middle-aged man in his forties stepped forward, bellowing even louder, "Which mongrel just ran his mouth?"

Well, he was kind of too old to be called the Prince of Pleasure, but the activeness of the part below his belt remained unchanged, and unable to abandon the lust of his youth, he had made passes at a woman in the inn today. Spotting the sexual harassment, the hot-blooded Jin Gigwang simply couldn't resist saving the damsel in distress.

From White Rabbit's perspective, however, knowing full well how frequently Jin Gigwang himself flirted with women, one was just as bad as the other.

Truly, for a rising power like Jin Sky Society, this level of bravado was something he could afford. The problem was that his opponent was Chu Yang. Among the many figures in the jianghu who used the alias the Prince of Pleasure, he was the most skilled and had the most vicious nature. He was adept at using various poisons, especially sordid aphrodisiacs.

Unaware of who his opponent was, Jin Gigwang was simply excited by the prospect of a fight. He trusted the four bodyguards behind him, all reliable, first-rate masters who had protected him for a long time. However, in White Rabbit's eyes, their combined strengths were no match for the Prince of Pleasure.

The woman hid behind Jin Gigwang. "Sigh... I'm in a bind, so I'll accept your help, benefactor."

Triumphant, Jin Gigwang declared loudly, "I am Jin Gigwang of the Jin Sky Society!"

Chu Yang snorted derisively. The reason the notoriously vicious he hadn't done anything wasn't because he was afraid of Jin Gigwang and his bodyguards, but because of White Rabbit. Just as he was about to break both of Jin Gigwang's arms in one move, that woman had released her aura. He instantly realized that she was a greater master than he was. Still, he did not retreat. He had an ace up his sleeve, some Qi Dispersing Poison hidden in a fan. This simple poison had helped him overcome countless crises throughout his life.

White Rabbit sighed again. She had hoped Chu Yang would back down on his own, but it turned out to be just wishful thinking.

"Oho! So you're the First Young Master of the Jin Sky Society, the group that's shaking up the jianghu these days," Chu Yang taunted, even as his gaze remained fixed on White Rabbit.

A self-satisfied smile spread across Jin Gigwang's face. "Hmph! So your eyes haven't rotted yet, you old monster! If you know who I am, apologize to the Young Lady and get lost!"

Killing intent filled Chu Yang's eyes. Originally, he had just planned to flirt with the woman a bit and leave, but now, he had changed his mind. His lust had been stirred, so he now intended to dispose of all the men in the room and abduct the two ladies. He stepped forward, gently fanning himself, but the four bodyguards quickly blocked his path.

Chu Yang flinched, but it was not them he was wary of. Just as he was about to release the Qi Dispersing Poison from his fan, White Rabbit took something out from her pocket and held it out on her palm for all to see.

Scowling, he growled, "Fufu, as expected of First Young Master of the Jin Sky Society, you travel with some impressive masters."

The thing White Rabbit was holding was a bead. While others here might not know what it was, Chu Yang knew it was an Anti-Poison Bead, and judging by its luster like a black pearl, it was a high-grade one of excellent quality.

"Dammit!" he ground his teeth. Against an Anti-Poison Bead of that quality, his Qi Dispersing Poison was useless.

White Rabbit popped the bead into her mouth and drew her sword.

SHING!

However, Chu Yang abandoned all thoughts of fighting and leaped away, fleeing the inn. "Hahaha! Next time won't be like today!"

Standing behind him, White Rabbit shook her head and spat out the Anti-Poison Bead.

"You're still the same," a low voice suddenly whispered from behind her.

Startled, White Rabbit spun around. "You!" she exclaimed, recognizing Crimson Tiger, who was wearing the same disguise as the last time they had met.

Crimson Tiger grinned. "Long time no see. Got a moment to chat?"

White Rabbit was at a loss for words from the joy and surprise. She glanced behind her where Jin Gigwang was boasting to the woman he mistakenly believed he had saved, then turned back with a broad smile. "Looks like I have plenty of time," she said.

She chose a seat in the corner, ensuring that Jin Gigwang remained in her line of sight, and the two sat facing each other.

 "What do you mean, 'I'm still the same'?" she began, pouting. "Is it the way I worry about other people?"

"No, I'm talking about how you try so hard to protect your client."

White Rabbit giggled. "It's my job."

Crimson Tiger concluded that White Rabbit's work was truly arduous. The incident that had just occurred was a perfect example. If he had to protect such an unruly guy, he would go insane with frustration and beat the crap out of the idiot until he came to his senses.

"So, what brings you here? You didn't come just because you missed me, did you?" White Rabbit asked.

"I did miss you."

"You don't have to lie like that. Just tell me the truth."

Crimson Tiger chuckled. "There's a *personal matter* I need your help with."

White Rabbit immediately understood what he meant. She stared at him for a moment, then addressed her handler, who was hiding in the ceiling. "Sorry, Yu, but could you give us a moment?"

"Understood."

Crimson Tiger waited for the presence in the ceiling to vanish completely, then said, "What I'm about to say is highly confidential. Can you keep a secret?"

"Of course," White Rabbit replied seriously. She had a favorable impression of Crimson Tiger and trusted that he wouldn't bring her harm.

Crimson Tiger nodded and got straight to the point. "I want to ask you for tips on being a bodyguard."

White Rabbit tilted her head, taken aback by the unexpected topic. "You want tips on being a bodyguard?"

"Yes, I've been assigned an unofficial bodyguard mission."

White Rabbit caught on right away. She herself had undertaken a few such missions from the Radiant Hall that required absolute secrecy. She asked, "Is your client a woman?"

Crimson Tiger nodded.

"That makes it harder." White Rabbit sighed. Protecting someone of the opposite gender was always more difficult.

"To put it plainly, there are no official guidelines on how to protect someone. You just can't let your guard down for a single moment because that moment of carelessness is exactly what assassins aim to exploit."

Crimson Tiger teased, "Want to come and help me protect her?"

White Rabbit could tell that Crimson Tiger was joking, but the sincerity in his words was evident. If she could, she wanted to help him. Carrying out a mission with him sounded enjoyable. Still, she hid her inner thoughts and offered her advice, "In short, you have to become one with the person you're protecting.

"I see." Crimson Tiger nodded. He could tell that even as White Rabbit spoke, she was still keeping an eye on Jin Gigwang across the room.

"Regardless, protecting someone isn't easy, even for a bodyguard with years of experience. It's not just a matter of martial arts skill. You have to change your entire mindset."

"......" Crimson Tiger gazed at her silently.

White Rabbit smirked. "You didn't come all this way just to hear this, did you?"

Crimson Tiger's eyes sparkled. This was the moment he had been waiting for.

"There's no tried-and-true method, but there is a secret technique."

"What is it?"

White Rabbit lowered her voice. "Do you know what bodyguards want most of all?"

"The Ten Great Protective Armors of the jianghu?"

"Of course, that would be great to have, but that's not the answer."

Crimson Tiger thought of a few more things, but in the end, he shook his head cluelessly. If it wasn't protective armor, it was even less likely to be a weapon.

White Rabbit whispered, "It's the Monochrome Cats."

"The Monochrome Cats?"

"They're a pair of spirit beasts cats, one black and one white, and they're said to be able to tear a tiger to shreds. I'm not surprised if you haven't heard of them before, they aren't that well known in the jianghu."

"Incredible," Crimson Tiger remarked, impressed. It was hard to imagine such small creatures killing a tiger.

"Their claws are fast, sharp, and powerful beyond belief, and they're so fast that only a peak master can catch them."

"But why would bodyguards want them?"

"Because of the Monochrome Cats' special trait. If one cat senses killing intent, the other immediately senses it too. They're known to be able to communicate with each other from at least seven hundred meters away."

Crimson Tiger's face lit up as he finally understood. With the Monochrome Cats, he would know in advance when an assassin was coming. That would make protecting Soeun much easier.

"I even got my name, White Rabbit, from the Monochrome Cats.[^1] That's how much of a symbolic spirit beast they are for us bodyguards."

"If they're so great, why haven't you gotten a pair?"

White Rabbit grinned. "It's not that I can't get them, but that I don't need them."

Since she was born with the ability to sense killing intent, she didn't need the help of spirit beasts.

Crimson Tiger smiled along with her. "So, who has them?"

"There are said to be twelve pairs total in the jianghu, including a pair near the Association Headquarters."

"Who has them?"

"A man named Director Hwa. He's a famous tycoon in the area but is known for being meticulous and ambitious. He probably won't lend them out easily."

"I'll take care of that. Thanks."

Crimson Tiger rose from his seat. Seeking White Rabbit out had been worthwhile. The Monochrome Cats would be a great help on his mission. 

White Rabbit was sad to see him go, but with every meeting comes a parting. She saw him off, looking forward to the day they would meet again.

SHIIING!

As Crimson Tiger turned to leave, his sword suddenly sprang from its sheath with lightning speed.

THUD! THUUD!

The blade plunged twice into the wall next to where they had been sitting, the steel cutting through the wood as if it were tofu. Crimson Tiger pulled his sword free, and it came out dripping with blood.

CHWAAAAAK!

Crimson Tiger flicked Chu Yang's blood onto the floor with a snap of his wrist. Unbeknownst to White Rabbit, the man hadn't left. Instead, he had been lying in wait on the other side of the wall, waiting to pounce.

Ever since his days as the Prince of Pleasure, Chu Yang had possessed a knack for stealthy infiltration and concealment. Distracted by Crimson Tiger and Jin Gigwang, White Rabbit had failed to notice his presence.

Had Crimson Tiger simply left, she would have been in grave danger.

THUMP!

The sound of a lifeless body crashing to the ground resounded from behind the wall. Crimson Tiger's sword never showed mercy to sexual predators.

Crimson Tiger glanced at Jin Gigwang, who was making serious passes at the woman he'd 'rescued'. "Worry about yourself before that moron."

He turned and walked away. 

"It'd be nice to see you again before winter comes."

His words harbored a hidden meaning, serving as a final farewell, but White Rabbit failed to comprehend it. She could only stare at the departing Crimson Tiger, her gaze full of longing and gratitude.
