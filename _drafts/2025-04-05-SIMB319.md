"Where the hell do you think you're going?" Ryu Seol shouted sharply at Moyong Jun.

She wanted to chase after him and smack him on the back of the head, but Moyong Jun's movement arts were much faster than she had thought. Right now, she was barely able to keep up with him.

*He was actually this fast?*

Ryu Seol bit her lip hard. The friend she had known for twenty years felt like a complete stranger.

"...Just follow me a little longer. We're almost there," Moyong Jun replied.

After a while, he stopped halfway up a small mountain and turned around. Facing a tense Ryu Seol, he smiled faintly and said, "Do you remember this place?"

"......"

"There's no ambush, so stop looking around like that. You really don't remember, do you?" Moyong Jun mumbled, a bit disappointed, then scratched his cheek.

Ryu Seol couldn't stand seeing him so calm. She glared at him and snapped, "You bastard. Why are you acting all high and mighty? You're just a filthy traitor who clung to the Blood Cult..."

Moyong Jun interrupted her with a mischievous laugh. "Back in the day, do you remember how you'd run here and cry whenever you got beaten by the Chairman?"

"W-What the hell are you talking about!" Ryu Seol shouted in confusion, her face turning red.

It was a story from well over ten years ago. She had only cried during her first year in the Elimination Corps, never again afterward.

Moyong Jun still clearly remembered how she'd been back then. They had joined at the same time.

"See that rock over there?" he asked.

"......"

It was a dark night, but the two of them could see clearly enough to notice even the movement of tiny insects. Where Moyong Jun pointed, there was a massive rock the size of a small house. The surface was covered in countless cracks, and Ryu Seol immediately recognized them as her handiwork from the past.

"You still don't remember?"

"...Shit. This place did feel familiar."

Embarrassing memories flooded back. Memories of being beaten for defying the Chairman and running here to cry her eyes out. Memories of feeling so unfairly wronged that she repeatedly slashed that rock, then came back drunk out of her mind, bawling and sniffling as she caused a scene.

*Damn it.*

In most of those memories, Moyong Jun had been right beside her.

"Whenever you got drunk, you'd slash the hell out of that rock. You said it looked like the Chairman's big flat face, and once you got stronger you'd split it clean in half..."

"Shut up, seriously?"

Moyong Jun chuckled. Mischief glinted in his eyes. "I never imagined that crybaby girl would someday become the Elimination Corps Commander."

"What the hell, moron..."

Nobody would believe it now, but when Ryu Seol first arrived at the Murim Alliance, she was incredibly weak. The Elimination Corps wasn't the kind of soft organization that would go easy on a scrawny girl wearing an eyepatch. Most people either looked at her unkindly or outright tormented her at the martial arts academy.

> "Moyong Jun. Help Ryu Seol get settled."
>
> "What? Why me?"
>
> "Shut up and do it, you little punk."

At the time, the Elimination Corps Commander practically dumped Ryu Seol onto Moyong Jun. They were around the same age, and the Commander thought their circumstances were somewhat similar—Moyong Jun had run away from his family, and Ryu Seol had become an orphan overnight.

Moyong Jun didn't like being tied together with Ryu Seol, but an order was an order, so he had no choice... though back then, he had a pretty rough personality himself.

> "Hey, Cyclops. Let's go eat. You need something in your stomach if you want to get through afternoon training."
>
> "Call me that one more time and I'll kill you."
>
> "Look at you, acting rude when I'm trying to be nice. Want me to beat you and drag you along?"
>
> "Should I beat you until you shut up?"

That was how Moyong Jun and Ryu Seol's relationship began. They ate together for twenty years, protected each other's backs on the battlefield, and crossed the line between life and death many times.

And then, the trust they'd built collapsed in a single day.

"...Why did you betray us?" Ryu Seol asked, devastated.

"I told you. I wanted to become stronger than you." Moyong Jun laughed softly and slowly drew the sword from his waist.

SHING!

Moonlight reflected off the blade, giving it a shimmering glow. As he took his starting stance, he looked solemn, as though performing a ritual.

Moyong Jun pointed his sword at Ryu Seol and smiled faintly, his eyes filled with chilling killing intent. "I chose this place because it's full of memories. I figured it would serve well as your grave."

"You're crazy..." Ryu Seol couldn't understand Moyong Jun.

*This bastard has completely lost his mind.*

She tightly gripped her twin blades and gathered her strength. Her martial arts uniform fluttered wildly from the surge of energy.

"Stop yapping and come at me! I won't feel better until I crush you right now!"

Whatever. She'd beat him half to death and then think about other things.

Moyong Jun stared quietly at Ryu Seol. "Ryu Seol, can you really kill me?"

"Ha! Looks like you're finally scared!"

CLANG!

Ryu Seol stomped the ground and shot forward like a cannonball. Her twin black daos crossed, slicing through the darkness, dark qi flickering over her body like flames.

Moyong Jun didn't dodge and instead thrust his sword to meet hers. His form was exceptionally refined. A thin layer of intensely purified transparent qi surrounded his blade.

Their clash looked like fire against ice. Ryu Seol cloaked her Black Dao in dark, enhanced dao qi and swung fiercely, while Moyong Jun's sword moved calmly, rendering her attacks useless.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

Every time their three blades collided, the resulting shockwaves distorted the scenery. Soon, the small mountain that held their memories began crumbling, as though symbolizing the catastrophic end of their relationship.

"Acting all high and mighty, but all you do is block. Is that the only sword style you learned after betraying the Murim Alliance?" Ryu Seol mocked.

Moyong Jun smiled coldly. "Ryu Seol. When are you going to get serious?"

"What the hell are you talking about..."

At that moment, Moyong Jun blurred and reappeared right in front of Ryu Seol. He closed the distance instantly, and Ryu Seol's eyes widened. She'd lost sight of him for a split second.

SPLURT!

Ryu Seol narrowly jerked her head back. A thin line appeared on her neck, and tiny beads of blood formed along the shallow wound.

It was a strike that made the hairs on her neck stand up. Had she been a fraction slower, she'd have been decapitated.

Moyong Jun giggled eerily upon seeing the blood. "I betrayed you intending to kill you, so don't insult me by going easy. If you keep this up..."

Moyong Jun's aura began shifting as the elegant qi of the Unlimited Sword boiled over.

*Is that... Berserk Qi?*

That was the only way Ryu Seol could describe what she saw in Moyong Jun's eyes.

Moyong Jun whispered, "You're going to die in the most painful way."

"You're crazy...!"

Moyong Jun, now resembling a sword demon, unleashed his attacks. His blade overflowed with such intense killing intent it was hard to believe he was the same person from moments before.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

Ryu Seol clenched her teeth, blocking his ferocious strikes.

*What kind of martial art is this?*

She felt pressure from Moyong Jun greater than she'd ever sensed from the Murim Alliance's greatest swordsman, the Divine Sword Commander. But it wasn't demonic arts—she would've noticed.

At the same time, it didn't look like a complete martial art either.

*It’s only been a year or so since he betrayed us.*

In that short time, Moyong Jun had grown unnaturally strong.

"Did they give you an elixir? Or a divine art?" Ryu Seol asked, knowing well enough it wasn't something that simple.

This abnormal situation could only mean one thing.

"...That's qi deviation. You've been consumed by that martial art."

Moyong Jun burst into wild laughter. "Hahaha! I'm sure you want to believe that. Seeing me stronger than you must make you want to dismiss me somehow!"

BOOM!

Moyong Jun flung Ryu Seol away and raised the tip of his sword toward the sky, admiring it with a spellbound expression. "...I was chosen by the sword."

Moyong Jun felt an indescribable thrill and exhilaration. He was overwhelming the martial artist he'd revered for half his life. He believed he could do anything right now.

ROAR...!

A fierce gale surged from Moyong Jun's sword.

Ryu Seol sensed something ominous and immediately rushed in, but it was too late.

The Unlimited Tempest, one of the ultimate techniques of the Unlimited Sword created by the Sword Saint, was revived in the hands of his descendant.

CRASH!

Nothing remained where the raging wind swept through. It looked as if a section of the small mountain had been completely torn away.

"Ugh..." Ryu Seol crossed her twin blades to barely withstand the Unlimited Tempest, but slash wounds covered her entire body.

"Haha! Still not dead? That's the One-Eyed Demon Blade Ryu Seol for you." Moyong Jun strode toward the trembling Ryu Seol, confident the outcome was already decided. "...I know everything about you. Your habits when you hold your blade, the movement of your shoulders, how you shift your gaze when you feint, which technique you use in which situation."

"You perverted bastard!"

"Isn't it natural? We spent half our lives together."

"Creepy bastard..."

Moyong Jun snickered. "I just wanted you to know this was a fight I couldn't lose... Goodbye, Ryu Seol."

Moyong Jun raised his sword, aiming for Ryu Seol's heart, when suddenly his head throbbed painfully. He felt nauseous, like he was about to throw up. His heart pounded wildly, and his sword hand trembled uncontrollably.

He felt as if his body was rejecting the very act of killing Ryu Seol.

*Why am I...*

Suddenly, Moyong Jun found it strange that he was even holding a sword. Another emotion briefly surfaced in his eyes, clouded by berserk qi.

He quickly shook his head side to side. He'd feel better once he drew blood. If he killed Ryu Seol with the world's strongest sword, this trembling would immediately stop.

CLATTER!

"You're so full of yourself. You think you know everything about me?" Ryu Seol screamed, dropping her weapons to the ground.

"...What?"

"You think you're the only one hiding some big secret?"

Moyong Jun chuckled, "If you think I'll spare you just because you're surrendering now..."

"You idiot. No matter what you do, you can't beat me." Ryu Seol hadn't given up the fight. She placed her right hand on the eyepatch covering her left eye. "I really didn't want to do this... Moyong Jun. You can consider yourself dead."

It was the eyepatch she'd never removed—not during training, on the battlefield, or even in her sleep. Made from famously tough silkworm thread, it had never torn or needed replacing.

That was why even Moyong Jun, who had been her friend for twenty years, had never seen what lay beneath.

For the first time in twenty years, Ryu Seol removed the eyepatch in front of someone else.

"Your eye...!"

In her left eye, which should've been empty, a violet Demonic Eye gleamed like a jewel, emitting a sinister aura.

"This is my real demonic art."

Moyong Jun's body froze the moment he met Ryu Seol's Demonic Eye. It lasted only an instant, but that was all she needed.

POW!

Ryu Seol's fist slammed into Moyong Jun's jaw. As he staggered, she charged mercilessly.

POW! POW! POW!

Her punches landed in rapid succession. Face, abdomen, torso—she didn't discriminate. Her fists, hard as iron, pounded every inch of Moyong Jun's body.

"Need a beating to snap out of it? Huh? You think you can pull this insubordination crap on your Commander?"

"Guh! Guh-huk...!" Moyong Jun groaned, trying to swing his sword to fend her off, but each time, Ryu Seol's Demonic Eye flashed.

Whenever that violet pupil emitted its wicked glow, his body stiffened, and his mind turned hazy.

*Illusions? Evil arts?*

*No, it's not that simple!*

Before he knew it, Ryu Seol's entire body was cloaked in a deep violet current, faster and stronger than anything he'd ever seen before.

"W-Wait...!"

A palm strike from Ryu Seol struck Moyong Jun's left cheek.

In other words, she slapped him across the face.

SMACK!

Moyong Jun spat blood as he flew backward, ending up half-buried in the same rock Ryu Seol once slashed in anger at the Chairman.

"I'll split that rock today, along with your skull," Ryu Seol spat, wiping the blood from her fists onto her clothes.
