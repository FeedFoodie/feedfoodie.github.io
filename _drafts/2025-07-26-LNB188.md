Myeong Ryu-San wept bitterly as soon as he regained consciousness, but it wasn't because of his wounds. No, he was overcome with sorrow at how helpless he had been in his last duel.

Tang Gi-Mun tried to console him, but the young man's heartbroken sobs wouldn't stop.

In contrast, Jin Mu-Won offered no words of comfort. He believed that Myeong Ryu-San needed time alone more than empty consolation.

Meanwhile, the tournament proceeded as scheduled. The number of participants, which had exceeded six hundred, had dwindled to about a hundred.

Fifty-six Demon Hunters would be selected in this tournament. One more win, and the participants could secure a spot. For this reason, the young martial artists' eyes shone with more determination than ever before.

Young martial artists from the Nine Great Sects, the Five Great Clans, the Azure Dragon Society, as well as the Earthly Quartet, were included among their number. The chaff had been sifted out, leaving only the best of the best, whose goal was not merely to become Demon Hunters, but to at least become a captain, or even the commander.

The faces of the martial artists who had made it to the final main tournament reflected the tension they felt.

The final matches were so intriguing that all of Heaven's Summit's leaders, including the Ten Great Elders, came out to watch.

Of those who advanced to the final round, Shim Won-Yi stood out the most. As befitting a member of the Seven Young Skies, he overpowered the other competitors with his exceptional skill and presence, to the point where the crowd predicted he would become the Commander of the Demon Hunters.

Shim Won-Yi looked down haughtily at the people below the duel stage, feeling no need to be wary of any of them. He believed this stage was created for him, and, in reality, the situation was leaning that way.

Suddenly, his expression soured.

*Jin Mu-Won.*

The person he found most troublesome was sitting in the middle of the audience stands, trying to appear ordinary, but it was clear the nearby martial artists had already recognized him.

*He made such a powerful impression that even now, people still haven't forgotten him.*



Just as Shim Won-Yi had noticed him, the leaders of Heaven's Summit also noticed his appearance and began to whisper among themselves. Discomfort was evident on their faces.

Jin Mu-Won was an uninvited guest at their feast. It was only natural for them to be uncomfortable. What made them feel even worse was that there was no suitable way to sanction him. For now, they had to carry out the selection of the Demon Hunters. Revenge against Jin Mu-Won could wait. Comforting themselves with this thought, they glared at him.

Jin Mu-Won's expression did not change despite the hostile gazes directed at him. He had already perfected a state of mind unshaken by external agitations. This level of hostility could not stir his inner self.

While watching the tournament, Jin Mu-Won gained much enlightenment. Most of those who reached the final round were disciples of prestigious jianghu factions. They generously unleashed the secret techniques of their respective arts, techniques they would not normally display so easily. Jin Mu-Won took in each and every one of them.

Though they had branched out into countless streams, when traced back to their source, all martial arts converged into one.

To kill others while protecting oneself.

For this one purpose, martial arts had developed over hundreds, even thousands of years, to reach their current state. Therefore, though their outward appearances might differ, when one delved into their fundamentals, the principles were largely the same.

For Jin Mu-Won, the duel tournament was not a simple spectacle, but a place of learning where he could elevate his own martial arts. Above all, the greatest gain was being able to gauge the level of each sect through the duels of their young martial artists.

WHOOSH!

Suddenly, Snow Flower let out a low hum.

"Well, is it entertaining?" a familiar voice asked softly.

Jin Mu-Won turned to face the speaker. Jo Un-Kyung had made his way through the crowd and was approaching.

Jo Un-Kyung grinned. "There is no need to be so wary. Today, I too have just come out to watch."

"Is it alright for you to be wandering around like this? Should you not be trying to get the Tyrant Fist Sect and the Greatsword Fortress in order at this very moment?"

"It will be sorted out without me needing to worry about it," Jo Un-Kyung replied nonchalantly.

Jin Mu-Won narrowed his eyes. *Everything will be sorted out without him needing to worry? Is someone else handling it for him? As expected, he's not alone.*

Jin Mu-Won suddenly felt a murderous intent stir within him. A powerful impulse to draw Snow Flower right now and cut Jo Un-Kyung's throat was dominating his entire body. It was less his will than that of Snow Flower.

At the same time, Jo Un-Kyung took a step into the crowd of people nearby, a clear expression of his will to use the surrounding people as a shield if necessary.

It was a gamble. If Jin Mu-Won attacked him now, the people nearby would die. Would he take that risk and attack, or would he remain silent?

Jin Mu-Won's decision was the latter. He forcefully suppressed Snow Flower's killing intent.

Jo Un-Kyung smiled brightly. "You have not yet answered my question. Is it entertaining?"

"I am not watching the tournament for entertainment."

"You are still so rigid. Not a bit of flexibility. Then again, I suppose that is part of your charm. That stubbornness that never changes once you have made up your mind."

"What are you trying to say?"

"Hehe! Will you not take my hand, Mu-Won?" Jo Un-Kyung held out his hand, the same hand that had learned the Demonic Cross of Blood and had brutally killed countless people in Yunnan.

"Have you forgotten already, Hyung-nim? I am the man who killed your father."

"I know."

"And you still want me to take your hand?"

"By that logic, my father also killed your father, so does that not cancel things out?"

At Jo Un-Kyung's sophistry, Jin Mu-Won's gaze grew heavy. "Is this your will, Hyung-nim, or did the ones behind you order it?"

"Who is behind me?"

"The Two-Faced Asura."

"......" Jo Un-Kyung's face hardened rapidly, confirming Jin Mu-Won's suspicions. "You surpass my expectations time and again."

"So it's true."

"Did you know?"

"Know what?"

"That with this, you have firmly set foot in hell. You should never have let those words leave your mouth."

Jin Mu-Won chuckled. "Well, did you know?"

"......"

"Ever since that day ten years ago, I have never once escaped from hell."

Jo Un-Kyung's eyes turned cold, and the two men regarded each other silently for a moment.

"Ugh!"

"Why is it so cold?"

The people around the two rubbed their shoulders and stepped back. Their instincts were warning them not to be here.

"Who are the group that uses the Two-Faced Asura as a symbol?"

"If you learn that, you will die. Right here, right now."

"Go ahead and try."

"No, it does matter. Not only you, but everyone connected to you will be erased from this world. Ha Jin-Wol, Tang Gi-Mun, Nam Soo-Ryun, even the White Dragon Merchant Association. All of them will be annihilated from this world because of you. And you still do not mind?"

"No, not at all. So? Who on earth are they..."

"The world is vast, and the knowledge you possess is but a tiny fraction of it. Do not try to gauge the world with your meager knowledge. It will be a shortcut to your own destruction."

"......"

"The reaper's blade will now be looking for you. Be careful. This is advice I give because I once thought of you as a brother."

Jo Un-Kyung slowly backed away.

Jin Mu-Won stared blankly at Jo Un-Kyung's retreating figure. Not for a moment did he stop holding the nearby people hostage.

Soon, Jo Un-Kyung melted into the crowd, and the last thing he saw was the smile hanging on his lips.

"...Did you hear that?" Jin Mu-Won asked.

"Every word," Cheong-In, who was currently disguised as a fat man in his early twenties, sweating profusely, replied.

Jin Mu-Won nodded. He had purposely placed the spy nearby in preparation for a moment like this. "Please track him."

"I was about to do just that."

"If you think it is dangerous, retreat immediately."

"Heheh! Do not worry. I know how to take care of myself." Cheong-In grinned, revealing his gums.

Rising to his feet, he quickly blended into the crowd. The pursuit had begun.

Jin Mu-Won's gaze turned back toward the duel stage. A fierce battle between the Black Fog Warrior, Jo Wol, and Hyun Gong-Hwi was unfolding there.

Hyun Gong-Hwi was one of the Seven Young Skies. Though he had the humiliating experience of being defeated by Jin Mu-Won, there were not many among the prodigies of the Central Plains who could match him.

In fact, the only one Hyun Gong-Hwi was concerned about and wary of was Shim Won-Yi. He had no intention of bowing his head to the conniving young man just because they both belonged to the Azure Dragon Society.

*I will definitely become the Commander of the Demon Hunters and then get my revenge on that man, Jin Mu-Won.*

He ground his teeth. If he were to simply become a member of the Demon Hunters, he just needed to win this one match. To become the Commander, however, he had to win at least three more times. It was important to win while conserving his stamina.

Hyun Gong-Hwi raised his internal energy to its peak. "Consider yourself unlucky, you bastard. Meeting me at this moment was your greatest mistake."

"You said what I wanted to say. Heheh!" Jo Wol let out a sinister laugh.

Hyun Gong-Hwi's eyebrow twitched. "How dare you!"

Hyun Gong-Hwi drew the large staff from his back and rushed at Jo Wol. The staff, infused with his ultimate level of internal energy, was letting out a low hum.

The staff of Hyun Gong-Hwi, the man who knew how to wield the most diverse range of weapons in the world, flew toward Jo Wol like a meteor.

His staff held the power to shatter a rock the size of a house in a single blow. Jo Wol knew this, yet he did not move.

He moved only when Hyun Gong-Hwi's staff was right before his nose. His hand seemed to lightly stir the air, and the staff was cleanly cut apart.

Hyun Gong-Hwi, however, did not panic and drew a quarterstaff, swinging it. His quarterstaff also turned to dust just before it reached Jo Wol's body.

"How can this be!" Hyun Gong-Hwi's cry of alarm echoed in the air.

Goosebumps rose all over his body, and he quickly drew the wolf-fang sword and the crescent moon blade from his waist at the same time. Right now, Jo Wol reminded him of a starving wolf he had encountered in the wilderness as a clueless child, its red eyes filled only with obsession and madness for its prey.

*So what? I am the one who will survive. Just like back then.*

SHWIIIAK!

The wolf-fang sword and the crescent moon blade flew toward Jo Wol's neck with a sharp sound of tearing air.

In that instant, Jo Wol rushed toward Hyun Gong-Hwi, his black cloak fluttering. Because of this, Hyun Gong-Hwi's swords only cut through empty air.

"Damn it!"

By the time Hyun Gong-Hwi came to his senses, Jo Wol had already closed in on his chest.

The palm that had been hidden by the cloak was revealed. A palm as black as the clothes he wore exploded on Hyun Gong-Hwi's chest.

BOOM!

With a sound like a thunder-clap bomb exploding, Hyun Gong-Hwi's body was thrown backward. Like a kite with its string cut, his body flew in an arc and crashed onto the ground below the duel stage.

After twitching for a moment, Hyun Gong-Hwi suddenly coughed up blood and went limp.

He was dead.

"......"

A suffocating silence descended.

This was the first death to occur during the duel tournament. More shockingly, the deceased was Hyun Gong-Hwi, and the one who had brought him to his death was Jo Wol.

The Black Fog Warrior, Jo Wol, stood smiling on the duel stage, when suddenly, his and Jin Mu-Won's gazes met.

Jo Wol's smile deepened.
