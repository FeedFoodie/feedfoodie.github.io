Seo Mu-Sang carved a path of blood. Men screamed as their bellies split open. Martial artists gurgled with slit throats. He had wounded them all.

Ha Jin-Wol had ordered them thirty paces forward, but the distance felt longer than ten li. Seo Mu-Sang had already killed dozens, yet several times that number remained. The way ahead stretched endlessly.

"Hoo! Hoo!"

A pained groan escaped his lips.

If he were alone, he would've covered the distance and more, but he had people to protect, and they held him back. He never considered them a burden, nor did he think of giving up. He unleashed the Blue Cloud Sword Technique in a swirling defense for himself, Ha Jin-Wol, and the others. They barely held on, aided by what little strength Tang Mi-Ryeo could offer.

Her expression darkened as she threw her hidden weapons, trying to keep their enemies in check. Her supply was dwindling. If only she'd known, she would've prepared more. She'd let her guard down at the Tang estate. Normally, weapons would be hidden all over her body, but now she had only ten left, a pitifully small number to hold back the tide.

The enemy relentlessly targeted Tang Gi-Mun and Ha Jin-Wol, correctly identifying them as the group's weak points. They focused their attacks on the two men, knowing any weakness would eventually break under constant pressure. The onslaught made their heads spin and their vision blur, shattering their focus.

In the chaos, Tang Gi-Mun couldn't find an opening to spread his poison. Seo Mu-Sang and Tang Mi-Ryeo were tangled with the enemy, and a misplaced dose could harm them as well. Ha Jin-Wol was equally helpless in such chaotic battle.

He just had to hold on. Only twenty more paces to go. A strategist predicts and prepares for countless futures. He'd foreseen a situation like this and had a countermeasure ready. The problem was reaching the place where his preparations lay.

Even so, Ha Jin-Wol was in better shape than Tang Gi-Mun. Lacking stamina and agility, Tang Gi-Mun was already wounded in several places from the focused assault.

"Keuk!"

He let out another frustrated groan as an enemy blade wounded his leg. It was only a flesh wound, not life-threatening, but it would slow him down. Of all places, his leg. He tried to follow Seo Mu-Sang, dragging the limb, but his pace grew slower and slower. The distance between them widened, forcing Ha Jin-Wol and Tang Mi-Ryeo to fall behind as well.

A resolute look settled on Tang Gi-Mun's face.

"This won't work. You go on ahead, brother."

"What are you saying, Hyung-nim?"

"You can see I'll only be a burden. You, at least, have to get out of here safely."

"Don't be ridiculous! How could I abandon you and Mi-Ryeo?"

"However, if you stay with me, you'll get dragged down too."

"Hyung-nim!"

Tang Gi-Mun ignored Ha Jin-Wol's shout. He'd made his decision.

SHWIIIAK!

A spear flew toward him. The attack was impossibly fast, unavoidable even if his leg were uninjured. Seo Mu-Sang and Tang Mi-Ryeo were too busy to help. Tang Gi-Mun squeezed his eyes shut.

CRACK!

A shattering sound echoed before him. He felt no pain. He slowly opened his eyes and saw a familiar back.

"Ryu-San."

Myeong Ryu-San had struck down the spear wielder. Without looking back, he spoke.

"Hurry and follow him. I'll cover the rear."

"Ryu-San."

"Damn it! I'm sorry. I lost my mind for a moment to that bitch's temptation. Scold me later, just get out of here."

"It's alright, Ryu-San. It happens. You see your mistake. That's enough."

Myeong Ryu-San finally smiled brightly.

"Master! I can call you Master, right?"

"Yes. I am your Master."

His smile grew even wider at Tang Gi-Mun's reply. He'd longed to hear those words, but his damned pride had kept him from asking. That was enough. He'd heard what he so desperately wanted to hear.

"Master, hurry and get out of here."

"Ryu-San, let's go together."

"Someone has to stay and stop them. I'll hold them off and catch up, so please go."

"You have to come back, Ryu-San. I still have a lot of poison to feed you."

"Damn it! Just go!"

Myeong Ryu-San stayed behind, blocking the enemies that rushed toward them. Beast-like screams and shouts shook the earth.

"Let's go, Hyung-nim. It's the only way to help him," Ha Jin-Wol said, pushing Tang Gi-Mun's back. Even as he moved forward, Tang Gi-Mun couldn't take his eyes off Myeong Ryu-San.

Finally, Ha Jin-Wol called out.

"Thank you, Ryu-San. I believed in you."

"Believed in me, my ass. Still, thanks. For holding on to a sliver of hope for trash like me. I feel relieved now, thanks to you."

"I mean it. I truly hope you follow us safely."

"If I can..." Myeong Ryu-San replied bluntly.

Even as he spoke, his hands and feet blurred, deflecting and redirecting attacks. Tang Gi-Mun and Ha Jin-Wol were rapidly growing distant.

"Ah, fuck!"

Tears threatened to well up as he watched them retreat, but he forced them back.

*Still, I was acknowledged by my Master, right? That means I'm a disciple of the Tang Clan too. A country bumpkin from Sichuan has made it big, Myeong Ryu-San.*

It had been an absurd journey. He was once a nobody boasting on the western plateaus of Sichuan. Then he met Jin Mu-Won's group, became a master who could emit qi, and even earned the alias of the Unyielding Bloodhound. He still hated taking poison, but it had made him who he was today.

"Come on, you sons of bitches! I'll tear you all apart!"

Myeong Ryu-San rampaged like a madman.

CRACK!

His punch caved in an enemy's face, splattering blood across his own face and chest. He didn't care. He fought with none of the clumsy forms he'd learned in some third-rate academy. These were the movements Jin Mu-Won had beaten into him, pure instinct honed by pain. He used no special techniques, no rigid forms. He simply moved, and the enemies couldn't evade his attacks.

KWAJIK!

"Kuaargh!"

The sound of breaking bones echoed with an enemy's scream.

Myeong Ryu-San fought with everything he had. The longer he held them back, the better Tang Gi-Mun's chances of escape.

"Hah, hah!"

His breathing grew ragged. Moving like a madman had pushed his stamina to its limit. His weakness was his rapid growth. His qi and skills had increased so quickly that he lacked a stable foundation, especially in stamina and qi management, but he compensated for his physical weakness with sheer grit, substituting his lack of control with insane physical ability.

Myeong Ryu-San became a blood-soaked wolf. For every wound he gave, he received one in return. Excruciating pain wracked his body, but he gritted his teeth and endured.

While he struggled, Tang Gi-Mun and the others vanished completely from sight.

Only then did Myeong Ryu-San let out the laugh he'd been holding back.

"Heheh!"

"This son of a bitch..."

The martial artists who'd lost their targets because of him filled with killing intent.

"I'll grind your bones to dust."

"Fuck off, you bastards! What right do you petty fucks who break promises and launch sneak attacks have to talk so much?"

Myeong Ryu-San had a knack for getting under people's skin. His provocation made their faces flush with rage. They charged him desperately. A large hole was torn in his side. He sent the head of the man who did it flying, but his expression didn't relax.

"Keuk!"

He clutched his torn side and staggered back, his intestines spilling between his fingers. The pain made his mind grow distant. In that opening, a martial artist plunged a sword into his back. Myeong Ryu-San desperately twisted and kicked the man in the chest, feeling the sternum shatter under his foot. Before he could savor it, another wave of agony washed over him.

He forgot why he was fighting. He just charged like a mad dog, tearing at the enemy. His body was drenched in blood as he gasped for breath. In the chaos, he suffered more fatal wounds to his stomach and shoulder. Still, he moved. When the strength left his limbs, he used his teeth to tear at an enemy's neck.

He didn't let go until the man's breathing stopped, his mouth filling with flesh and blood. As the man's struggles subsided, Myeong Ryu-San released his jaw and collapsed onto a pile of corpses.

"Huuuh!"

He no longer had the strength to move. His punctured lungs couldn't draw in fresh air. He'd done all he could. He had no regrets. His vision blurred, the world turning red.

*Is this the end?*

He let out a faint laugh. Laughter just came. He'd always wondered how he would die and had never wanted an insignificant end. He'd burned away everything. No regrets remained.

As his consciousness faded, someone appeared before him.

*Still... one left? Damn it, I don't know anymore.*

He figured Seo Mu-Sang would handle the last one.

Just then, the newcomer lifted his body. Myeong Ryu-San managed to open his eyes. Someone looked down at him. The face was blurry, but the gaze seemed sad.

"Who... who are you?"

"Ryu-San."

The voice was familiar.

"Mu-Won?"

The man, covered in blood just like him, was Jin Mu-Won. He held Myeong Ryu-San in his arms, looking down at him.

"Sh... shit!"

"You did well."

"Bullshit... keuh!"

"Master Tang and the others escaped safely because of you."

"Heheh!"

Myeong Ryu-San tried to smile but couldn't muster the strength. He'd burned every last shred of his life force. Not even the Great Luo Immortals could save him now. Jin Mu-Won saw it at a glance. He'd barely shaken off the Black Soul Corps to get here, but he was too late.

Myeong Ryu-San's lips moved.

"Ma... ster said... I'm also... a disciple of the Tang Clan..."

"You are a disciple of the Tang Clan. If I could, I'd want you in the Northern Army too."

Jin Mu-Won spoke not as himself, but as the Lord of the Northern Army. A faint smile touched Myeong Ryu-San's lips.

"Ri... ght?"

With that, he was content. It was the last smile he would ever make.

Jin Mu-Won looked down at him, speechless. His smiling face looked as if he might wake up at any moment and start grumbling.

"Ryu-San."

Tang Gi-Mun suddenly looked back, thinking he heard Myeong Ryu-San's voice, but no one was there.

"You must come back safely. I still have a lot of poison left to feed you."

Seomoon Hye-Ryung stared blankly at the horrific scene. A mountain of corpses and a river of blood unfolded before her. The bodies of the Black Soul Corps formed the mountain, their shed blood the river.

Jin Mu-Won's martial prowess was beyond her imagination. The Black Soul Corps was strong, but he was stronger. The final technique he used completely overturned her common sense. His sword flashed, and in an instant, more than twenty corps members were sliced in two. She didn't know it was the third technique of the Shadow Blade of Destruction, Dividing the Heavenly Seas, but its power was seared into her mind as fear.

In the end, he shook them off and escaped. The surviving members pursued him, but she knew it was useless. His power was that shocking.

"Jin Mu-Won..."

"Even so, he won't survive."

Gwan Dae-Seung was much calmer, despite witnessing the same scene. Seomoon Hye-Ryung looked at him, and he offered a smile.

"The perimeter around Heaven's Summit is tighter than you think. And the most bizarre man in the world is chasing him. He'll never escape Hubei."

"A bizarre man?"

"Such a man exists. Our job is to follow his trail and watch his final moments."

Gwan Dae-Seung's words were filled with conviction. At his unwavering assertion, Seomoon Hye-Ryung found herself nodding.

However, she had to ask herself a question.

*Is that really true? Really...*

Jin Mu-Won was a man who always achieved what others called impossible. If she weren't opposing him, if the great Dam Soo-Cheon didn't exist, perhaps she might've chosen Jin Mu-Won.

*Perhaps the person who underestimated him the most was me.*

Her gaze turned to Gwan Dae-Seung. She'd known him only as the administrator of Heaven's Summit. The whole world knew him as such, but the glimpse she'd just caught of the real man wasn't so simple. Her grandfather, Seomoon Hwa, had told her to be wary of him. She'd never seen her grandfather so openly cautious of anyone. That alone made it clear Gwan Dae-Seung was a threatening figure.

Gwan Dae-Seung started walking slowly in the direction Jin Mu-Won had disappeared, as leisurely as someone out for a stroll. Seomoon Hye-Ryung and Chae Hwa-Yeong followed quietly behind.

Regardless of her feelings, she had to witness Jin Mu-Won's end with her own eyes. Only then could she sleep soundly.

The long night was still not over.
