*Wow! This strategist's abilities are truly incredible.*

Seo Mu-Sang marveled inwardly. Escaping the manor had been difficult, but afterward, they faced no obstacles by simply following Ha Jin-Wol's guidance. Armed martial artists swarmed past them, close enough to touch, yet they never recognized them.

*So this is the power of formations.*

Ha Jin-Wol had set up a series of mazes and illusions throughout Heaven's Village and its suburbs. Since he'd set them up in advance, he only needed to activate them.

It was a simple yet brutally efficient method. The martial artists from Heaven's Summit searched diligently, but couldn't find the group hidden within the illusions. Each time a search party moved on, Ha Jin-Wol deactivated the formation, led them to the next location, and activated another.

After repeating this process a dozen times, the group made it to the outskirts, far from Heaven's Village.

"Phew! I think we've shaken them off for now," Seo Mu-Sang remarked.

"It's too early to be relieved," Ha Jin-Wol cautioned. "They won't give up that easily. By now, they have probably realized that I set up formations and are tracking us with their own experts."

Tang Gi-Mun's expression darkened. "Such arrogance! They don't even consider the Tang Clan a threat."

"Do you think it's just the Tang Clan? In the current murim, only the Silent Night can keep them in check. They don't care about any other sects."

"Power without constraints is a frightening thing," Tang Gi-Mun sighed.

Having lived his entire life within the Tang Clan's power structure, he had never been oppressed by others, so he had never given the matter much thought. Perhaps that was why he'd never seen the world as an unjust place.

Ha Jin-Wol gently patted his shoulder. "You can think about it later. Right now, we need to get out of here."

"Yes, I understand." Tang Gi-Mun smiled. "Having you with us is reassuring," he added sincerely.

The others nodded, feeling the same.

"If you've recovered your stamina, let's move again," Ha Jin-Wol said.

"What about Mu-Won?"

"He'll escape on his own."

"But..."

"He's not a man who dies easily."

"Hmph!"

"I believe in him," Ha Jin-Wol stated, his voice unwavering. He truly believed it.

Seo Mu-Sang added his support. "That's right. The Liege will definitely find us alive. Escaping this place safely is how we can help him."

"I understand," Tang Gi-Mun conceded. "If you all think so..." The men who followed Jin Mu-Won most closely were certain. In that case, he had to believe in him too. "It's a shame we have to flee like this, after working so hard to clear up the misunderstanding about the Northern Army colluding with the Silent Night." Regret filled his voice. Having watched Jin Mu-Won's journey from the beginning, his disappointment was immense. If they fled now, Heaven's Summit would surely frame him again.

"That won't happen," Ha Jin-Wol said.

"What do you mean?"

"Do you think I wouldn't have thought of that?"

"Then?"

Instead of answering, Ha Jin-Wol smiled meaningfully. As Tang Gi-Mun watched him with suspicion, Seo Mu-Sang announced, "I don't sense any more presences. Let's move."

"Let's." Ha Jin-Wol casually kicked a stone on the ground. The surrounding scenery shimmered as the formation dissolved. With the illusion gone, they started moving again. Their enemies had already swept through the area, so not a single presence could be felt nearby, allowing them to move with ease.

"So, where are we going now?"

"We're going south."

"South? Not north? The south is Heaven's Summit's territory. All the nearby sects are under their influence."

"They'll expect that. That's why we're going south. We'll change direction midway. That way..."

"What's wrong?" Tang Mi-Ryeo, who'd been silent, asked with a puzzled expression when Ha Jin-Wol stopped mid-sentence and froze.

Ha Jin-Wol stared straight ahead, motionless as a stone statue. Seo Mu-Sang and Tang Gi-Mun were the same. They stood with wide eyes, like men who'd seen something they should never have seen.

"What..." Tang Mi-Ryeo's gaze followed theirs.

She didn't know when he'd appeared, but an old man stood there, watching them with his arms crossed. He looked to be in his early seventies with an ordinary face, but his eyes shone with a brilliance that seemed to contain all the world's wisdom. He wore a pure white robe with his hair tied back by a matching headband, looking like an otherworldly sage.

Still, deep fear etched itself across the faces of Seo Mu-Sang and the others. They didn't know the old man's name or identity, but from the moment they saw him, goosebumps rose on their skin and refused to subside. Their entire bodies trembled like aspen leaves. Their bodies felt the fear before their minds could even process it.

*Who is that old man?*

An intense crisis washed over them, a feeling that their lives would end if the old man so much as moved. They didn't dare to breathe loudly. The aura he exuded was that immense. The pressure Ha Jin-Wol felt, however, was beyond imagination. Unlike the others, he'd recognized the old man's identity.

"The Ghost of Zhuge Liang, Seomoon Hwa!" His voice was almost a scream.

At that, the old man, Seomoon Hwa, smiled faintly. "As expected, you recognize this old man. I knew you would."

"Why are you here?"

"I came because I wanted to see the face of the one who's repeatedly thwarted my granddaughter."

"Kuh!" Ha Jin-Wol's face twisted. *Was I trying to avoid a fox, only to attract a tiger?*

Seomoon Hwa enjoyed his reaction with an amused expression. The Seomoon Clan was known as the family of the world's smartest people, and among their prodigies, Seomoon Hye-Ryung was considered the wisest. Seomoon Hwa had high expectations for her, but recently, she'd suffered repeated setbacks. The one who caused them was Ha Jin-Wol, and Seomoon Hwa felt a strong curiosity about him. The Seomoon Hye-Ryung he knew was a rare genius. The fact that another genius had appeared to rival her had piqued his interest, though he wasn't pleased that such a talent had emerged from outside his family.

Ha Jin-Wol's face contorted. *This is the worst.*

Seomoon Hwa was one of the Nine Skies, a martial artist beyond ordinary measure. Before such power, any tricks or schemes were meaningless. What was most despairing was that Seomoon Hwa was also brilliant. To call him a genius was an insult. He was the most perfect being Ha Jin-Wol knew, skilled in both literary and martial arts.

Ha Jin-Wol found it difficult to bear Seomoon Hwa's curious gaze. To the old man, it might be simple amusement, but to Ha Jin-Wol, who knew no martial arts, it was an unexpected disaster.

Seomoon Hwa's smile deepened. "Quite impressive. You mixed bewilderment and illusion formations appropriately and even prepared to set them up at key points. You're certainly a step above Hye-Ryung when it comes to improvisation."

"I don't know what to do with such praise. Thank you."

"You have no need to be thankful. Do you know why?"

"With my shallow knowledge, I wouldn't dare to guess."

"Then let me ask another thing. Do you know why the Seomoon Clan is known as the smartest in the world?"

"That's something I've never thought about."

"I thought so. No one ever doubts it. Why are the smartest people only produced by the Seomoon Clan? Do they have some special method? Why does no one ever suspect such a thing?"

"Then let me ask. Is there a special method?"

At Ha Jin-Wol's question, Seomoon Hwa smiled brightly. When the old man smiled with such innocence, goosebumps erupted all over Ha Jin-Wol's body, but he forced himself to endure it. He had to buy time, no matter what.

"It's true that members of the Seomoon Clan are born with brains that can contend for the world's best. The experiences and methods accumulated over hundreds of years made that possible. However, the world sometimes gives birth to a breed called geniuses. Beings who can see through the principles of heaven without help, who understand ten things when taught one. A true genius."

Seomoon Hwa's gaze remained fixed on Ha Jin-Wol as he continued. "Such people are called prodigies. And we of the Seomoon Clan have always been more wary of such individuals than anyone else."

"Then?" Ha Jin-Wol's pupils trembled.

"If you were me, could you just stand by and watch as people emerge who could shake the fortress you've built? For generations, it's been the Patriarch's duty to eliminate any genius who could threaten the Seomoon Clan's fortress."

"Ugh!"

"Now you understand why I'm here, don't you?" Seomoon Hwa looked at Ha Jin-Wol with that innocent expression, like a child waiting for praise, but Ha Jin-Wol couldn't smile back.

Seomoon Hwa's intention was clear. He would eliminate Ha Jin-Wol before he could grow into a greater threat.

"...However, your granddaughter wouldn't want this," Ha Jin-Wol said, his voice strained. "She's looking forward to competing with me. Are you planning to betray her expectations?"

"As you live in this world, you can't always get what you want. To gain one thing, you must give up another. It's time for Hye-Ryung to learn that." Seomoon Hwa didn't hide his true intentions. He'd judged Ha Jin-Wol a threat and decided to eliminate him. His granddaughter's competitive spirit had no influence on his decision.

Waves of energy began to flow from his body, dominating the area. The spiderweb-like energy constricted Ha Jin-Wol and the others, preventing them from moving.

Unable to watch any longer, Tang Gi-Mun shouted, "For the great Seomoon Clan to use such a petty trick, have you no shame?"

"Shamelessness is the greatest virtue for one who manages the world," Seomoon Hwa replied calmly. "How can one manage the world by weighing morality and precedence?"

"However, Senior..."

"Do you think only the Seomoon Clan is like that? The Tang Clan grew the same way. You know very well how many people died and how many sects fell while the Tang Clan established itself in Sichuan, don't you? For one great family to rule a region, the lives of at least a thousand people must be sacrificed. That's reality."

Seomoon Hwa's words were a throwing knife that dug into Tang Gi-Mun's chest. He couldn't reply.

As if he knew that would happen, Seomoon Hwa smiled faintly and unfolded his arms. "This conversation's gone on too long. I should hurry and finish you all off so I can watch that boy Jin Mu-Won's final moments."

The moment he took a step, the world changed completely. They'd been in a forest, but their surroundings transformed into a space of absolute darkness.

"T-The Heaven Ascending Tiger Steps?" Ha Jin-Wol let out a sound close to a scream.

The secret technique of the Seomoon Clan, which could deploy formations with mere steps, was unfolding in a nameless forest on the outskirts of Heaven's Village.
