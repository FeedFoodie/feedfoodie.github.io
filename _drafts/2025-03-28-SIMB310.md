Contrary to Baek Suryong's assumption that he would be interrogated as soon as he entered the Murim Alliance Chairman's office, the Elimination Corps Commander seemed completely at ease.

"Would you like some tea? Or perhaps some alcohol?" she asked casually.

"Tea, please," Baek Suryong replied.

Ryu Seol nodded, then glanced at Moyong Jun, who had followed them in. "You heard him. Go fetch us some booze from the reception hall."

"But I just said that I'd prefer tea..."

"Get us something expensive while you're at it."

"......" Baek Suryong was at a loss for words.

*She just ignored me completely!*

The Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol, was infamous within the Murim Alliance for her terrible drinking habits. Even now, she shamelessly used her duties as an excuse to indulge herself during the day, licking her lips in anticipation. 

"You know the stuff the Chairman keeps exclusively for important guests? The one brewed from three-hundred-year-old tuber fleeceflower? Bring us that," she demanded eagerly.

"...You do realize that the Chairman always checks the liquor supply when he returns, right?" Moyong Jun sighed, his expression indicating that this situation wasn't new to him. Although he usually cursed her like a vulgar sailor, he had to limit himself to polite speech since there was a guest present.

"Did I say I was drinking it alone? We have a guest. I'm just fulfilling my duties as Acting Chairman."

"That's a ridiculous excuse. The Chief Instructor is still part of the Murim Alliance..."

"Then let's call it a celebratory toast for our new Chief Instructor."

"A celebratory toast for someone who broke down the Murim Alliance's main gate and got himself arrested seems a bit..."

BANG!

Ryu Seol slammed the table, glaring fiercely at her Vice Commander with her single remaining eye as if she were ready to kill him. "Keep running your mouth, and you can forget about this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to taste that sweet, sweet nectar."

"...Understood."



In the end, Moyong Jun reluctantly left to fetch the alcohol.

As Moyong Jun walked out, Baek Suryong clearly heard him mutter, "Damn drunk witch," under his breath, but Ryu Seol continued smiling sweetly, acting as if she hadn't heard a thing.

Instead, her eye sparkled mischievously as she turned back to Baek Suryong. "There's some precious liquor the Chairman's been hiding away in the reception hall. When else am I going to get a chance to try it?"

"Then why even bother asking me? You could've just ordered your subordinate to bring it from the start."

"I thought you'd be the drinking type, given your whole naughty pretty-boy vibe."

"Sheesh..."

Ryu Seol leaned forward and whispered conspiratorially, "Hey, when the Chairman asks later, just say it was you who wanted a drink, okay? In return, I'll go easy on you for the trouble you caused today. You get what I'm saying?"

She winked her one eye, hinting this would be their little secret.

*What a strange woman.* Baek Suryong's eye twitched in disbelief. It was difficult to find a top-tier martial artist who wasn't eccentric, but the Elimination Corps Commander seemed especially unsuited for the disciplined atmosphere of the Murim Alliance.

Now that they were face-to-face, Baek Suryong could sense it even more clearly.

*She's definitely learned some form of demonic art.*

It was faint, but Baek Suryong felt a sinister and sticky energy emanating from Ryu Seol.

*I'm not sure exactly which demonic art it is, but...*

The demonic aura from the Elimination Corps Commander was exceptionally subtle. Had Baek Suryong not been sitting this close, even he, who had studied countless demonic arts in his previous life, would likely have missed it entirely. Without having learned the Heaven Defying Divine Art himself, detecting it would have been impossible.

In other words, if Ryu Seol wished to conceal it, she could easily deceive even the other Commanders. This fact alone made the Elimination Corps Commander an obvious suspect.

*However... could she deceive the Chairman?*

Baek Suryong thought back to the Chairman, who had instinctively suspected his connection to the Blood Cult.

*Maybe for a day or two, but certainly not for over ten years.*

Ryu Seol smiled, revealing a row of pristine white teeth. Just as Baek Suryong observed her, Ryu Seol was also carefully scrutinizing him with her single eye. "What are you staring at? Haven't had a sip yet, and you're already drunk on my beauty?"

It was clearly meant as a joke, but Ryu Seol truly was attractive. Even the eyepatch covering her left eye added a distinctive charm to her appearance. Still, Baek Suryong felt not the slightest hint of romantic attraction toward someone who seemed more like a dangerous beast than a woman.

*Is this playful demeanor her real self, or...*

Baek Suryong mentally reviewed her tone, behavior, and their entire exchange up until now. He couldn't confirm anything yet, but one thing was obvious—the Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol, was attempting to gauge him just as he was evaluating her.

"Not a chance," Baek Suryong replied with an insincere smile, careful not to reveal anything.

Ryu Seol narrowed her eye irritably. "What kind of answer is that? Are you saying I'm not pretty?"

"You're not bad-looking, but you're just not my type."

"Look at this cocky little brat. You think you'll survive in the world talking like that?"

"I doubt you played the social game well yourself."

"You're amusing."

"I hear that a lot."

The two exchanged matching smirks.

Baek Suryong felt even more tension sitting calmly across from the Elimination Corps Commander now than he had facing off against the Heavenly Justice Commander.

"...Elimination Corps Commander," he began seriously.

"Just call me Senior."

"Alright. Then, can I ask you something, Senior?"

Ryu Seol leaned back comfortably into her chair and nodded. "Go ahead. Let's chat until the drinks arrive. I was planning to let the Vice Commander handle the interrogation anyway."

With her permission, Baek Suryong boldly asked, "How did you end up learning a demonic art?"

"...!!" Ryu Seol flinched and instantly shot up from her seat, but she neither attacked nor emitted any killing intent.

Baek Suryong smiled knowingly. Although Ryu Seol remained silent, her reaction was enough to confirm his suspicion.

*As I suspected, the Chairman already knew about it.*

Others might not notice, but there was no way the Chairman, who despised the Blood Cult so deeply, would overlook unorthodox martial arts.

*In other words, the Chairman is aware that the Elimination Corps Commander knows a demonic art... yet that's just as suspicious.*

Looking slightly dazed, Ryu Seol asked cautiously, "...Did the old Chairman mention something about me?"

"No. I'm just more sensitive to auras than most."

"That's impossible. Even the Heavenly Justice Commander, who trained in Shaolin martial arts, has never noticed anything..."

Baek Suryong recalled what he'd read in the Beggars' Gang's personnel records regarding the Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol.

Around twenty years ago, a young girl had been rescued after the destruction of the unorthodox Mara Sect near the borderlands. The other children rescued alongside her were reunited with their families, but Ryu Seol and a few others, whose parents had been killed by the Mara Sect, had nowhere else to go. Taking pity, the Chairman personally brought them into the Murim Alliance and made them members.

Among those children, Ryu Seol's talent stood out above the rest. Within three years of training, she joined the Elimination Corps, and ten years later, she rose to become its Commander.

*However, nowhere in the records did it state that the Elimination Corps Commander had learned demonic arts.*

Even though such a massive secret had been revealed, Ryu Seol looked deflated only for a moment before laughing at herself. "Yeah. I practiced demonic arts. A really nasty, terrifying one, too."

At that moment, Moyong Jun returned and heard her words. "Ryu Seol!" he shouted, slamming the door open with a horrified expression.

Ryu Seol shrugged nonchalantly. "Calm down. He figured it out by himself."

"...Is that true?"

Baek Suryong nodded silently, prompting Moyong Jun to let out a long sigh and plead, "This secret is known only to the Chairman and me. If it leaks out..."

"Not only me, but even the old man will be in deep trouble, right?" Ryu Seol interrupted.

"You idiot! Don't talk like it's somebody else's problem!"

"I'm the one who got caught, so why are you the one freaking out?"

Ryu Seol took the liquor bottle Moyong Jun brought and drank straight from it, gulping it down as if she'd just attained enlightenment.

Moyong Jun anxiously glanced toward Baek Suryong. "...Chief Instructor, do you understand the position of the Elimination Corps within the Murim Alliance?"

"Roughly."

The Elimination Corps had always been considered the black sheep of the Murim Alliance. From its inception, it had consisted mainly of martial artists from smaller sects, freelancers, and even defectors from unorthodox sects.

Still, for the Corps Commander herself to have practiced demonic arts was an entirely different matter.

*If word got out that the Chairman knowingly overlooked this, even his position might be jeopardized.*

Baek Suryong had unintentionally discovered one of the Chairman's weaknesses, though not all his questions had been answered yet. He decided to continue pressing. "I'll decide my next move after hearing more details. Elimination Corps Commander, why did you learn demonic arts?"

"What the..." Ryu Seol scratched her head irritably. Somehow, in an instant, the interrogator and suspect had switched roles. After a long sigh, she explained plainly, "To put it simply, the Mara Sect Leader was my biological father."

"......"

"Twenty years ago, the Chairman killed my father. In that battle, I lost my left eye." Ryu Seol touched the eyepatch covering her eye briefly, then moistened her lips with another swig of alcohol before continuing. "At the time, the old Chairman gave me a choice: I could either die there alongside my father or follow him to the Murim Alliance. What else could I do? I wanted to live, so I did what he said."

Baek Suryong had a straightforward impression of her story.

*They're both completely insane.*

The Chairman, who brought the daughter of a man he killed into the Murim Alliance, and Ryu Seol, who followed the person who murdered her father—neither of them could be called normal.

"Couldn't you have purged the demonic qi and abandoned the art entirely?"

"It was impossible. By then, it had already taken root in my body. If I tried to remove it, I'd have to destroy my qi center, and I'd rather die than become a cripple. Therefore, my only option was to carefully cultivate it alongside orthodox martial arts. Ah, don't misunderstand—it's not the kind of demonic art you're imagining. I don't drink blood or tear off human flesh."

Moyong Jun, who had been listening nervously, smacked his Commander on the head. "You think that's funny at a time like this?"

"So, should I grovel and beg for forgiveness instead?" Ryu Seol scoffed, then winked playfully at Baek Suryong. "I've already told you this much. You'll keep it a secret, right?"

"...For now, I understand," Baek Suryong reluctantly nodded. His suspicions about the Elimination Corps Commander hadn't fully disappeared, however.

*If she still harbors resentment toward the Chairman for killing her family, that's motive enough to betray the Murim Alliance.*

Although the mystery behind her demonic art had been explained, with no direct link to the Blood Cult revealed, Ryu Seol remained a prime suspect in Baek Suryong's mind.

"I've been rambling on about myself. Now, let's hear about you, Junior." Ryu Seol put down the bottle, straightened her posture, and asked, "Why cause such a scene on your first day? Why destroy the main gate?"

*To draw out the Blood Cult spy hiding within the Murim Alliance... but that's something I can't say aloud.*

Baek Suryong gave the explanation he had prepared beforehand. "To wake up the Murim Alliance, which has grown complacent during this long period of peace. It was also a good chance to evaluate the martial artists' current state and pinpoint any weaknesses."

Ryu Seol frowned slightly. "Weaknesses?"

"There were quite a few."

Moyong Jun's eye twitched skeptically. "What weaknesses? Our men reacted quickly and decisively."

Ryu Seol was renowned for training the Elimination Corps with strict discipline, and the same applied to the Heavenly Justice Corps and the other units. Due to their rigorous and competitive training, the Murim Alliance had arguably reached its strongest state ever. Now, Baek Suryong was saying there were issues?

"The response was quick, yes, but the method of rushing in altogether wasn't ideal. Had *poison* gas been used at that moment, dozens would've fallen immediately," Baek Suryong said, emphasizing the word 'poison' deliberately.

However, the reaction he anticipated did not occur.

Instead, Ryu Seol nodded thoughtfully. "Poison… I suppose that's plausible."

"Moreover, the Heavenly Justice Corps and Elimination Corps moved entirely separately."

"Well, we don't normally conduct joint training."

"Exactly. It seems there's plenty of work ahead of me as Chief Instructor," Baek Suryong sighed dramatically.

Ryu Seol chuckled softly. "In any case, even if your intentions were good, your actions were clearly unacceptable. Sorry, but I can't simply let this pass," she said, turning to Moyong Jun. "What should we do with him?"

Moyong Jun let out a heavy sigh and glanced at Baek Suryong uncertainly. Now that Baek Suryong knew Ryu Seol's secret, it was difficult to treat him as casually as before. Even so, he couldn't stay quiet forever.

"I checked the rules... and when someone at the Commander level or higher causes trouble, a formal disciplinary committee is convened to determine punishment," Moyong Jun reported.

"That's what he said."

Convening a disciplinary committee meant summoning all available Murim Alliance Lieutenants, a process that would take at least three or four days.

"Three or four days? Then what happens in the meantime?"

"By regulation, he's supposed to remain confined to his quarters until the decision is made..." Moyong Jun trailed off uncertainly.

*Is confining Baek Suryong even possible?*

Ryu Seol nodded, clearly understanding her Vice Commander's concern.

Suddenly, Baek Suryong spoke up as though he'd been waiting for this moment. "There's a clause that says if there's a guarantor, the accused can move freely under the condition that part of their body remains restrained."

"A guarantor? How do you know about that?"

"I studied the rules before coming," Baek Suryong replied nonchalantly. Naturally, he'd already familiarized himself with everything before he ever broke the Murim Alliance gate.

"A guarantor… Do you even have someone willing to vouch for you?"

Not just anyone would suffice. This person needed sufficient influence to satisfy even the furious Heavenly Justice Commander.

Ryu Seol couldn't think of a single person currently in the Alliance who would dare take that risk.

*I'm certainly not volunteering, either…*

However, Ryu Seol didn't need to agonize long, because Baek Suryong already had someone in mind.

Baek Suryong smiled mischievously. "Would a direct descendant of the Namgung Clan be enough?"

"...The Namgung Clan?"

"The Lightning Dragon Hero, Namgung Su. He's the Patriarch's third son, recently risen to fame, and he's conveniently nearby," Baek Suryong grinned slyly. Summoning Namgung Su to assist him had been his secondary goal all along.

Miles away at the Jaegal Manor, Namgung Su shivered involuntarily, feeling an inexplicable chill run down his spine.
