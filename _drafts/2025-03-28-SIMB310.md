The Murim Alliance Chairman's office.

Contrary to Baek Suryong's expectation that he would be interrogated the moment he sat down, the Elimination Corps Commander across from him wore a relaxed expression.

"Would you like some tea? Or maybe alcohol?"

"I'll have tea."

Ryu Seol nodded and looked at Moyong Jun, who had followed them in.

"You heard that, right? Go get us some booze from the reception hall."

"I just said tea..."

"Make it something expensive while you're at it."

Baek Suryong's words were completely ignored.

The Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol, was known throughout the Murim Alliance as a heavy drinker. Using her duties as an excuse, she smacked her lips at the thought of having a drink in the middle of the day.

"You know that stuff the Chairman only brings out for important guests? The one brewed with three-hundred-year-old hasuo. Make sure it's that one."

"...The Chairman will check the stock when he returns, you know?"

Moyong Jun sighed with a look that said he wasn't even surprised anymore.

Though they usually swore at each other like dogs when alone, he used polite speech now that there was a guest.

"When did I say I was drinking it alone? We've got a guest. This is part of my duties as Acting Chairman."

"That's a ridiculous excuse. The Chief Instructor is still part of the Murim Alliance..."

"Then let's call it a celebratory drink for the new Chief Instructor."

"A celebratory drink for someone who broke the Murim Alliance's main gate and got arrested is a bit..."

Bang!

Ryu Seol slammed the table and glared at her Vice Commander with her single eye like she was about to kill him.

"Keep yapping and I'll make you miss out on this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity."

"...Understood."

In the end, Moyong Jun left to fetch the alcohol. Baek Suryong heard him mutter, "Damn drunk witch," under his breath as he walked out, but Ryu Seol smiled sweetly as if she hadn't heard a thing.

Her eyes sparkled mischievously as she looked at Baek Suryong.

"There's some precious liquor the Chairman's been hiding away in the reception hall. When else am I gonna get a chance to try it?"

"Then why even ask me in the first place? You should've just ordered it."

"I thought you'd be the type to like drinking, since you've got that whole artistic Mister vibe going on."

"Sheesh..."

Just then, Ryu Seol leaned forward and lowered her voice.

"Hey, let's say it was you who wanted to drink when the Chairman asks later, okay? In return, I'll go a little easy on you for the mess you caused today. You get what I'm saying?"

Then she winked her one eye, as if to say this was their little secret.

*What a weirdo.*

It's hard to find a top-tier martial artist who isn't eccentric, but the Elimination Corps Commander seemed especially ill-suited for the discipline-focused Murim Alliance and now that they were face to face, Baek Suryong could feel it even more clearly.

*She's definitely trained in demonic art.*

It was faint, but Baek Suryong could sense a sinister, sticky energy from her.

*I don't know which demonic art it is, but...*

The demonic art aura from the Elimination Corps Commander was extremely subtle.

Even Baek Suryong, who had studied countless demonic arts in his previous life, would have missed it if he weren't sitting this close, and if he hadn't learned the Heaven Defying Divine Art.

In other words, if she wanted to hide it, she could deceive even the other Commanders.

That fact alone made the Elimination Corps Commander a prime suspect.

*But... could she deceive the Chairman?*

Baek Suryong thought back to how the Chairman had suspected his ties to the Blood Cult based on instinct alone.

*Maybe for a day or two, but not for over ten years.*

Just as Baek Suryong was observing her up close, Ryu Seol was also examining Baek Suryong with her one eye.

She smiled, revealing a row of white teeth.

"What are you staring at? Haven't had a drink yet and you're already drunk on my beauty?"

It was just a joke, but Ryu Seol truly was beautiful.

Even the eyepatch covering her left eye added a unique charm, but Baek Suryong didn't feel the slightest romantic attraction toward her.

She felt more like a dangerous beast than a woman.

*Is this playful act really who she is, or...*

Baek Suryong mentally replayed her tone, actions, and their entire conversation so far.

He couldn't be sure of anything yet, but one thing was clear, The Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol, was trying to figure out what kind of man Baek Suryong really was.

"Not a chance."

So Baek Suryong smiled insincerely, careful not to reveal anything.

Ryu Seol's expression twisted in annoyance.

"What kind of answer is that? Are you saying I'm not pretty?"

"You're not bad-looking. Just not my type."

"Look at this cocky little brat. You think you'll survive in the world talking like that?"

"I doubt you ever played the social game well yourself, did you?"

Smirk.

"You're funny."

Smirk.

"I hear that a lot."

The two smiled at each other with matching grins.

Baek Suryong felt even more tense now, sitting calmly across from the Elimination Corps Commander, than he had when squaring off against the Heavenly Justice Commander.

"Elimination Corps Commander-nim."

"Just call me sunbae."

"Sunbae. Can I ask you something?"

Ryu Seol leaned back comfortably in her chair and nodded.

"Sure. Let's just chat until the drinks get here. I was going to have the Vice Commander do the interrogation anyway."

With her permission given, Baek Suryong struck boldly.

"How did you end up learning demonic art?"

"...!!"

Ryu Seol flinched and shot up from her seat, but she didn't attack or emit killing intent.

That reaction confirmed it for Baek Suryong.

*I knew it. The Chairman already knew.*

Maybe others wouldn't notice, but there's no way the Chairman, who hated the Blood Cult so deeply, would fail to recognize unorthodox sect martial arts.

In other words, the Elimination Corps Commander had learned demonic art with the Chairman's approval...

Which was just as strange.

Ryu Seol looked dazed as she asked, "...Did the old Chairman say something about me?"

"No. My energy sensitivity is just more acute than most."

"That's impossible. Even the Heavenly Justice Commander, who trained in Shaolin martial arts, never noticed until now..."

Baek Suryong recalled the information about Ryu Seol written in the Beggars' Gang's personnel roster.

The Elimination Corps Commander, Ryu Seol.

Roughly twenty years ago, a girl rescued after the destruction of the unorthodox sect Mara Sect in the borderlands.

The other children rescued alongside her had been reunited with their families, but Ryu Seol and a few others had lost their parents to the Mara Sect and had nowhere to go.

The Chairman had brought all of them into the Murim Alliance and accepted them as members.

Ryu Seol stood out with overwhelming talent.

Within just three years of training, she joined the Elimination Corps, and ten years later, she became its Commander.

*But nowhere did it mention that the Elimination Corps Commander had trained in demonic art.*

Even after such a massive secret had been exposed, Ryu Seol only looked deflated for a moment, Then she began chuckling to herself.

"Yeah. I trained in demonic art. The really nasty, terrifying kind, too."

At that moment, Moyong Jun returned and heard what she said.

"Ryu Seol!"

He slammed the door open, his expression twisted in horror. Ryu Seol shrugged at the sight of him.

"Don't get mad. He figured it out on his own."

"...Is this true?"

Baek Suryong nodded, and Moyong Jun let out a long sigh. He spoke to Baek Suryong in a pleading tone.

"This is a secret known only to the Chairman and me. If it gets out..."

"Not only me, but even the old Chairman will be in deep trouble, right?"

"You idiot! Don't talk like it's someone else's problem!"

"I'm the one who got caught, so why are you the one freaking out?"

Ryu Seol drank straight from the bottle Moyong Jun brought, gulping it down like she had achieved enlightenment.

In her place, Moyong Jun glanced at Baek Suryong nervously and asked cautiously, "...Chief Instructor-nim. Do you know what kind of position the Elimination Corps holds in the Murim Alliance?"

"Roughly."

The Elimination Corps was a black sheep within the Murim Alliance.

That had always been the case, even from their origins.

The Elimination Corps was made up of martial artists from minor sects, freelancers, and even former unorthodox sect members who had defected, but a Commander having learned demonic art was on another level entirely.

*If it gets out that the Chairman overlooked this, even his position could be at risk.*

Baek Suryong had unintentionally learned one of the Chairman's weaknesses, but not all of his questions had been answered yet.

"I'll decide what to do after I hear more. Elimination Corps Commander. Why did you learn demonic art?"

In an instant, the roles of interrogator and suspect had switched.

"What the..."

Ryu Seol scratched her head in exasperation, then let out a deep sigh.

"To put it simply, the Sect Leader of the Mara Sect was my biological father."

"......"

"Twenty years ago, the Chairman killed my father, and in the process, I lost my left eye."

Ryu Seol touched the eyepatch covering her left eye with her fingers. Then, she wet her lips with the bottle of liquor and continued.

"The old Chairman told me to choose. Die here with my father, or follow him to the Murim Alliance. What could I do? If I wanted to live, I had to do as he said."

Baek Suryong's impression of the story was simple.

*They're both insane.*

The Chairman, who brought the daughter of a man he killed into the Murim Alliance, and Ryu Seol, who followed the man who killed her father, neither of them were normal.

"Couldn't you have purged the demonic art?"

"It had already taken root in my body. To get rid of it, I'd have to destroy my qi center. I'd rather die. So I had no choice but to train it carefully alongside other martial arts. Ah, it's not the kind of demonic art you're thinking. I don't drink human blood or tear off flesh or anything."

Moyong Jun, who had been listening with a tense expression, smacked the Commander on the head.

"You think that's funny in this situation?"

"So should I just grovel and beg for forgiveness instead?"

Ryu Seol snorted and winked at Baek Suryong.

"I've told you this much. You'll keep the secret, right?"

"...For now, I understand."

Baek Suryong nodded reluctantly, but that didn't mean all of his suspicions about the Elimination Corps Commander were gone.

IF SHE STILL HARBORED DEEP-SEATED RESENTMENT TOWARD THE CHAIRMAN!

*She'd have more than enough motive to betray the Murim Alliance.*

The mystery behind the demonic art she had learned was resolved, but it wasn't evidence linking her to the Blood Cult.

She remained a prime suspect.

"I've been rambling about myself. Now, let's hear about you, junior."

Ryu Seol put the bottle down, straightened her posture, and asked, "Why did you stir up such a mess on your first day on the job? Why break the main gate?"

To find the Blood Cult rat hiding in the Murim Alliance.

> "But that wasn't something he could honestly say."

Baek Suryong gave the reason he had prepared in advance.

"To wake up the Murim Alliance, which has grown complacent from long peace. I also wanted to assess the state of the martial artists' training and identify any issues."

"Issues?"

"There were quite a few."

Ryu Seol frowned. Moyong Jun twitched his brow in disbelief.

"What issues? I thought our people responded quite well."

Ryu Seol was known for training the Elimination Corps with strict discipline.

The same went for the Heavenly Justice Corps and the rest of the five armies.

Thanks to their competitive training, it wasn't an exaggeration to say the current Murim Alliance was the strongest it had ever been, but now he was saying there were problems?

"The response was fast, but the way they rushed in all at once wasn't ideal. If poison had been spread there, dozens would've been poisoned immediately."

Baek Suryong deliberately used the word poison, but the reaction he had hoped for didn't come.

Instead, Ryu Seol nodded, lost in thought.

"Poison, huh... I guess that could've happened."

"Not only that, but the Heavenly Justice Corps and the Elimination Corps moved completely separately."

"Well, we don't do joint training."

"Sounds like there's a lot of work ahead for the Chief Instructor."

At Baek Suryong's low sigh, Ryu Seol chuckled.

"Anyway, even if your intentions were good, what you did was clearly wrong. Sorry, but I can't let this slide. So... what do we do with him?"

Ryu Seol looked back and asked. Moyong Jun let out a deep sigh.

"I looked into it a bit..."

Moyong Jun glanced at Baek Suryong.

Now that Baek Suryong had uncovered Ryu Seol's secret, it was hard not to treat him more cautiously than before, but he couldn't stay silent forever.

"...When someone at the Commander level or higher causes an incident, a formal disciplinary committee is convened to determine punishment."

"That's what he said."

Once the disciplinary committee was called, all available Murim Alliance Lieutenants would be summoned, and that alone would take at least three or four days.

"Three or four days? What happens in the meantime?"

"By the rules, he's supposed to be confined to his quarters and not allowed out..."

But was that even possible?

Moyong Jun looked doubtful, and Ryu Seol agreed with him, but then, Baek Suryong spoke as if he had been waiting for this moment.

"There's a clause that says, if there's a guarantor, the accused can move about freely under the condition that part of their body remains restrained."

"...A guarantor? How do you know that?"

How, indeed?

He had looked everything up in advance before breaking the Murim Alliance gate.

Baek Suryong shrugged and said, "I studied a bit before coming."

"A guarantor... Do you even have someone who'd vouch for you?"

It couldn't be just anyone.

Whoever it was had to be able to take responsibility if the Azure Dragon Hero fled, someone even the furious Heavenly Justice Commander would accept.

No one came to mind for Ryu Seol.

*And I'm not about to do it...*

But Ryu Seol didn't need to think too hard.

Baek Suryong already had someone in mind.

"How about a direct member of the Namgung Clan?"

"...The Namgung Clan?"

"Namgung Su, the Lightning Dragon Hero. He's the Patriarch's third son and has been rising in fame lately. And he happens to be nearby."

Baek Suryong's second reason for breaking through the Murim Alliance gate was to summon his capable assistant into the Murim Alliance.

At that moment, far away in the Jaegal Manor, an inexplicable chill ran down Namgung Su's spine.
