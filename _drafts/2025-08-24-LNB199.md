Two days later, Heaven's Summit learned of the Northern Army Branch's annihilation, and a meeting of its leaders was immediately convened. The regularly scheduled messenger pigeon never arrived, which the Xinjiang Branch found strange. They dispatched an investigation team, who discovered the massacre.

Heaven's Summit was prepared. They already had countermeasures in place and urgently sent response orders to every branch. Their main force was also set to depart and confront the Silent Night, although the sheer scale of their troops and required supplies delayed their departure.

As a result, the Demon Hunters set out first. The plan was simple. The elite minority would hold the enemy at bay until the main force arrived to join them.

Just as the situation became urgent, however, another letter arrived.

"What's this now?" the Ruling Hall Director, Yuk Ji-Mun, asked his subordinate.

"It's a letter from the Zhongnan Sect. They're requesting help."

"The Zhongnan Sect?"

"Yes, they say a witch has appeared."

"A witch? What do you mean?"

"I don't know the details, but apparently, she annihilated dozens of martial artists from the Kunlun and Zhongnan Sects. That's why they've asked for our help."

"A witch?" Yuk Ji-Mun's expression hardened. *Could it be an advance party from the Silent Night?*

It was entirely possible, as the Silent Night had always been unpredictable. Perhaps this was a ploy, a distraction sent ahead of their main forces. If he was right, it was a matter they couldn't overlook.

"So, what is the Zhongnan Sect doing now?"

"They said they have formed and sent a second pursuit team. However, they requested our help because they're worried it won't be enough."

"Hmm!" Yuk Ji-Mun's expression grew grave. If the Zhongnan Sect was telling the truth, then the Silent Night was attacking the Central Plains from both the inside and outside simultaneously. This was a complete reversal of their previous pattern of attacking from the outskirts, making it all the more bewildering.

"What should we do?" his subordinate asked.

"Are there any troops we can spare?"

"Aside from the forces heading north and our reserves, almost no one."

"Hmm..." Yuk Ji-Mun stroked his chin, a habit that surfaced when he was deep in thought. After a long moment, he rose from his seat. "I'm going out for a bit. While I'm gone, check on the Ruling Hall's forces."

"Understood."

Yuk Ji-Mun left the Ruling Hall and headed for the guest quarters, where visitors typically stayed. The building was enormous, large enough to house hundreds of people at once, but it was rarely full. After all, to stay there, one needed either martial prowess or a reputable standing with Heaven's Summit.

The complex was currently nearly empty since the event to select the Demon Hunters had just ended. However, one person remained , and he was the reason Yuk Ji-Mun had come.

Yuk Ji-Mun knocked on the door of a small room in the innermost part of the complex.

A young voice called from inside, "Who is it?"

"It is I, the Ruling Hall Director Yuk Ji-Mun."

A moment later, the door opened and a young man appeared.

"Director Yuk," Dam Soo-Cheon said politely, clasping his hands together in greeting. He was the most brilliant young martial artist in the jianghu, a man who could have stayed in the most lavish room but instead chose the humblest of lodgings.

"I'm sorry to disturb you. May I come in for a moment?"

Despite being one of the most powerful figures in Heaven's Summit, Yuk Ji-Mun was careful not to treat Dam Soo-Cheon carelessly. The young man's demeanor was imposing, even by his own standards.

"Please come in, Director Yuk." Dam Soo-Cheon invited him in with a smile.

Yuk Ji-Mun entered, but a peculiar light flickered in his eyes. An unexpected person was already inside.

"Greetings, Director Yuk," Seomoon Hye-Ryung said. While a young woman in a room with an unrelated man could easily be misunderstood, she showed no sign of embarrassment. Rather, she simply offered Yuk Ji-Mun a faint smile.

"Ah, Young Lady Seomoon, you were here too."

"I came to discuss something with Young Lord Dam."

"I hope I haven't interrupted."

"Not at all. We just finished our conversation."

"That's a relief."

"But Director Yuk, what brings you here?"

"I came because I have a favor to ask of Young Lord Dam." Yuk Ji-Mun's gaze turned to Dam Soo-Cheon.

"Please speak. I'm listening."

"I need your help."

A peculiar glint appeared in Dam Soo-Cheon's eyes. The Ruling Hall was one of Heaven's Summit's most elite organizations, comprised only of martial geniuses. Yuk Ji-Mun himself was formidable, and the warriors under his command could each take on a hundred foes. They could wipe a major sect off the map overnight. It was unexpected that the man who commanded such a force had come seeking help.

"I'd like to know the circumstances."

"They say a witch has appeared in Hubei."

"A witch?"

"Dozens of martial artists from the Kunlun and Zhongnan Sects were wiped out by her. It's caused an uproar in both sects."

Seomoon Hye-Ryung, who had been listening quietly, had a sparkle in her eyes. "Who led the martial artists from the Zhongnan Sect?"

"The Blue Mountain Sage led them. He also died at the witch's hands."

"The Blue Mountain Sage? Isn't he the martial brother of the Sect Leader, the Blue Crane Sage?"

"That's right."

The Blue Mountain Sage was a master skilled enough to rank among the top five within the Zhongnan Sect. For a force that included him to be annihilated was no small matter.

"The witch's power is extraordinary and her methods are unusually vicious," Yuk Ji-Mun continued. "That's why I want to ask a favor of you. I want you to take care of that witch."

"Me?" Dam Soo-Cheon looked surprised.

Yuk Ji-Mun smiled. "I know many of your people were placed in the Demon Hunters. They will be a great asset to you. But you know well that it won't be enough, don't you?"

"......"

"If you grant my request, I will become a source of strength for you."

"Your request is to subdue or eliminate this witch, I presume."

"Correct. We need to focus on the Silent Night. The situation is too urgent to divert forces for a witch."

"Hmm!"

Dam Soo-Cheon looked at Seomoon Hye-Ryung. She nodded. His personal martial strength was second to none, but he lacked a significant support base. If the Ruling Hall Director supported him, it would be a great help to his future.

"Very well. I will take on this mission."

"Thank you. The Zhongnan Sect is also sending a second pursuit team, so they should be a great help. I'll also request cooperation from the nearby Wudang Sect."

"There's no need to mobilize the Wudang Sect," Dam Soo-Cheon said, his voice overflowing with confidence.

Yuk Ji-Mun nodded at his resolute, almost arrogant, attitude. "I understand. However, feel free to request their cooperation at any time if you need it. I'll put in a word for you."

"Understood."

"Then I'll leave it to you."

With those final words, Yuk Ji-Mun left.

Once they were alone, Dam Soo-Cheon asked Seomoon Hye-Ryung, "But I wonder if I really need to do such a trivial task."

"It's absolutely necessary."

"Necessary?"

"Two months ago, Young Lord, you wouldn't have needed to do this. But the situation has changed."

"Is it because of Master Jin?"

"That's right. His fame is threatening yours. As you know, in the jianghu, fame is power."

"So you're saying I need to build more fame."

"To soar even higher, you need powerful wings. This task will give you stronger wings."

"Hmm!"

Seomoon Hye-Ryung's eyes sparkled with wisdom. This was a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity and, most importantly, a logical way to send Dam Soo-Cheon outside of Heaven's Summit.

"I suppose the duel with Master Jin will have to wait," Dam Soo-Cheon said.

"There will definitely be another chance."

He nodded at her confident words. Seeing him, Seomoon Hye-Ryung wore a slightly guilt-ridden expression.

*I'm sorry, Soo-Cheon. You'll never get the chance you want. But this is all for you. You must never resent me.*

She comforted herself by thinking that everything was for his sake.

Amidst everyone's cheers, the Demon Hunters departed.

Led by Commander Shim Won-Yi, five Captains and fifty members set off on the long journey, mounted on their horses. Their faces were filled with determination.

Shim Won-Yi's complexion wasn't good. The internal injuries he sustained fighting the Black Fog Warrior, Jo Wol, hadn't yet fully healed. Normally, he would have carefully circulated his energy to treat them, but he had no time for that now. He had to make up for the humiliation he suffered. That was why he was pushing himself.

Shim Won-Yi suddenly raised his head and looked at the massive walls of Heaven's Summit, but his father was nowhere to be seen. From the moment of his humiliating defeat, Shim Mu-Wae had withdrawn his brief, concerned gaze and treated him with coldness. He believed his son had tarnished the honor of Justice Heaven. His father's coldness wounded Shim Won-Yi's heart deeply.

*When I return, it will not be in such a shabby state. I will definitely return as a hero revered by the world. Father, please take care of yourself until then.*

He gritted his teeth, his fighting spirit blazing.

At the same time, someone was quietly leaving through the rear gate of Heaven's Summit.

The man stepping out, his lion's-mane hair fluttering in the wind, was Dam Soo-Cheon. Seomoon Hye-Ryung followed by his side.

He looked at her. "Well then, I'll be off."

"Please return safely."

"Don't worry. I don't know who this witch is, but she won't be able to do anything to me."

"I'll believe you."

Dam Soo-Cheon smiled and started walking.

Seomoon Hye-Ryung stood blankly, watching him walk away into the distance. When his figure finally disappeared, her expression turned as cold as ice.

As she turned, someone approached her. The scholar with refined features was Gwan Dae-Seung.

He said, "It's starting now. Are you prepared?"

"Is there a need for preparation? This had to be done eventually anyway."

"Is that so? That's reassuring." Gwan Dae-Seung smiled.

One by one, martial artists began to gather around them, their numbers swelling to over a hundred. Dressed in black martial arts uniforms and red robes, they exuded an aura as sharp as a blade.
