The next morning.

The SRS students gathered in the training ground stared wide-eyed at the unfamiliar appearance of Wiji Cheon.

"Good morning. I am Instructor Wiji Cheon, and I will be guiding your swordsmanship for today."

Wiji Cheon wore a red headband, procured from who-knows-where, tied tightly around his forehead. Embroidered in the center were the characters for "Certain Death" (必死).

Even then, the SRS students mostly laughed it off.

"Haha! Cheon looks like he's really serious about today."

"Yeah, let's work hard. We'll do our best too."

"Are you perhaps imitating Mr. Baek Suryong? So cute, really…"

The students recalled the Wiji Cheon from the previous night, sitting by the campfire and shyly saying, "I want to become a teacher."

Everyone had decided to support the boy's dream, which was why they agreed to receive his swordsmanship instruction for the day. The entire SRS had consented.

The problem was, the students had grossly underestimated Wiji Cheon's resolve.

"Students. This instructor did not come here to play house with you."

*Shing.*

The drawn blade glinted blue as it reflected the morning sun.

The Soul Sword responded to its master's will, emitting a chilling aura.

"I will warn you in advance. For today, anyone who speaks informally to this instructor or disobeys instructions…"

The smallest and gentlest boy in the SRS transformed into someone else entirely in that moment.

SLASH!

A casual swing of his sword split a large tree in two. It was a spectacle created by sharp, clear sword qi.

"Wh-Why the tree…"

"H-Hey there?"

As the students finally sensed something was wrong and grew flustered, Wiji Cheon glared at them with unsettlingly bright eyes.

"It will be considered defiance against this instructor, and we will proceed with one-on-one guided sparring."

"...!!"

That wasn't a joke.

The moment they saw Wiji Cheon's eyes, everyone had the same thought.

*He's lost it! Look at his eyes!*

They had forgotten.

The Sword Wonder, Wiji Cheon.

Usually a boy gentler and kinder than anyone, but a person who changed completely the moment he held a sword.

"Uh, umm. Well..."

"This isn't quite what we wanted…"

"Aren't you getting a little too worked up…?"

Wiji Cheon casually ignored his seniors' words.

"First."

The boy, determined to emulate his most respected teacher for the day, raised his sword tip towards the mountain peak visible behind the students.

"Before breakfast, we will begin with some light physical conditioning. Based on my observations so far, I have concluded that your basic stamina is abysmal."

Coming from Wiji Cheon, who looked the least physically fit, the words hit harder. A few students pouted, looking displeased.

"All personnel will reach that mountain peak and return. Begin."

Still, everyone hesitated, glancing at each other instead of moving immediately.

*Guess acting like Teacher isn't easy.*

Wiji Cheon felt the need to establish dominance. He decided to push a little harder.

"Are everyone's ears clogged? Or does this instructor look like a joke to you?"

The seniors' jaws dropped at the harsh language from their usually shy and cute junior.

"Even if you're the instructor for a day, isn't that a bit harsh…"

"So, you don't want to do it?"

*Vwooom!*

The moment they saw sword qi rise from Wiji Cheon's raised sword, the mouths that had opened in complaint snapped shut.

"N-Not that we don't want to."

"You don't?"

"Don't want to, sir…"

"I will let it slide this first time."

If it had been Baek Suryong, he would have instantly smacked the head of the student talking back, but Wiji Cheon wasn't quite that far gone yet.

"I will give you exactly fifteen minutes. Any student who returns later than the allotted time can consider themselves without breakfast today."

"Reach that peak and come back in fifteen minutes, sir?"

"That's impossible, sir!"

While everyone was still grumbling, unable to grasp the situation, one person moved.

"Better to move than waste time like this."

WHOOSH!

It was Yoo Yiran.

Behind her, as she moved with the grace of a deer using her movement arts, the SRS Lieutenants and then the other students followed.

"Senior! Not fair leaving alone!"

"Wait for us!"

"...Ah, screw it!"

Only then did the other students start running like foals with their tails on fire. Reaching the mountain peak indicated by Wiji Cheon and returning within fifteen minutes was an extremely tight schedule.

Eventually, a spectacle unfolded where the entire SRS sprinted at full speed to secure their breakfast.

Most of them, however, still hadn't properly grasped reality. Some even chuckled, finding the situation amusing.

"...Laugh while you can. Later, you'll find it hard even to move the corners of your lips."

Wiji Cheon watched them and smiled. It was a smile remarkably similar to someone else's.

{sep}

Awaiting the students who barely made it back from the peak was an unidentifiable special meal developed by the Medicinal Cooking Research Society.

"What… is this?"

"You expect us to eat this?"

"Is this perhaps training against poison?"

As the students entered the dining hall with disbelieving eyes, Wiji Cheon spoke with a proud expression.

"This is a special meal made with ingredients this instructor gathered from the mountain at dawn. It contains various ingredients with diverse nutrients, made into a porridge for better absorption."

The special meal Wiji Cheon had made was a hodgepodge porridge with an ominous green tint. The fact that it was a variation of the food distributed to martial artists in the Blood Cult was a secret known only to Baek Suryong.

"Is it really okay to eat? It looks like it tastes terrible…"

At someone's timid protest, Wiji Cheon frowned.

"Student, did you come here only to eat delicious things?"

"...No, sir."

There were no further complaints.

The special meal prepared by Wiji Cheon was utterly devoid of anything resembling flavor, but hunger is the best sauce, so everyone ate diligently.

Wiji Cheon watched them, nodding in satisfaction.

"Eat heartily for breakfast. You'll be eating dinner in hell."

"Cough!"

Students spurted porridge from their mouths one after another, but the food was so easily digestible that no one suffered indigestion.

"If you've finished eating, we will begin the morning training."

What followed was group swordsmanship training.

Under Wiji Cheon's instruction, the students swung their swords under the scorching sun, dripping with sweat.

Wiji Cheon walked among them, correcting their postures.

"Overall, your swordsmanship is full of unnecessary flair. Don't be conscious of others. Focus more on the sword."

Wiji Cheon's eyes were sharp as he observed the students.

He chewed out every student equally.

That was the first principle of education he had learned from Baek Suryong.

TWACK!

"Is this the best swordsmanship you can muster? Or are you thinking of just coasting through time?"

"Sorry, sir!"

TWACK!

"Where are you looking? I told you to focus on the sword. Are you defying the instructor now?"

"No, sir!"

TWACK!

"Stop slacking! Keep your focus! Didn't you eat breakfast?"

"I did, sir!"

"Then why is your voice so small?"

"I ATE, SIR!!"

Instructor-for-a-day Wiji Cheon's class was harsh and strict. He pushed the students to their limits.

Even the students who initially approached it lightly grew increasingly pale.

Eventually, rebellion broke out.

"Damn it! I can't take this anymore!"

It was one of the SRS Lieutenants. Fourth-year Jo Yeongeol. A lay disciple of the Diancang Sect, he was a swordsman who had mastered the Sail Sword Style to a considerable level.

"I know you're good, Wiji Cheon. But everyone trains differently. I don't think my sword will get any faster training in such an ignorant way."

"…"

At Jo Yeongeol's statement, everyone nervously watched Wiji Cheon's reaction.

Some silently cheered Jo Yeongeol on. They too had felt skeptical about this brutish training.

"Very well."

Wiji Cheon nodded readily, unexpectedly.

"Then let's duel using only pure fast sword techniques, without inner arts. If Student Jo Yeongeol defeats me, we will end the training."

"...Are you serious?"

"Of course."

"Ha!"

Jo Yeongeol scoffed.

No matter how much of a sword genius Wiji Cheon was, he didn't think he would lose if they competed using only fast sword techniques without inner arts.

*If it's a single strike match, I win.*

Jo Yeongeol stated with a confident expression, "Alright. If I lose, I'll follow your instruction method without complaint."

No more words were needed. The two faced each other. There was no separate signal. The moment the swordsmen's hands simultaneously touched their hilts marked the beginning of the match.

SWISH!

Jo Yeongeol's famed fast sword strike flashed out like a beam of light. He had no doubt of his victory. While his overall ability might fall short, he was recognized as the best in fast sword techniques within the SRS.

*I was faster!*

The moment he was certain of that.

CLANG!

Wiji Cheon's sword, drawn a fraction later, flicked Jo Yeongeol's sword upwards, and Wiji Cheon darted into the wide-open defense.

*But I was definitely faster?*

Wiji Cheon clenched his small, flint-hard fist tightly. The match was decided, but he had no intention of forgiving the troublemaker disrupting the class atmosphere. This too was something he had learned from watching Baek Suryong.

"Fast sword? Don't make me laugh, you're too slow."

Wiji Cheon's fist struck Jo Yeongeol hard on the bridge of the nose.

PWAAK!

"Keoheok!"

Blood spurted from both nostrils as Jo Yeongeol staggered back and collapsed onto the ground. Wiji Cheon stood before him, looking down with arrogant eyes.

"Student Jo Yeongeol. Return to training immediately once your nose stops bleeding."

"Kuk…"

"Did you not hear me?"

"Y-Yes, sir. Understood."

Thus, the spark of rebellion was ruthlessly stamped out before it could grow.

Wiji Cheon scanned the other students and declared, "Any student with complaints may step forward at any time. However, be prepared for the consequences."

"…"

Today's Wiji Cheon was a miniature Baek Suryong.

{sep}

The arduous training continued until nightfall.

After completing all the scheduled activities, Wiji Cheon untied the red headband from his forehead.

"We will stop here."

As if some sorcery resided in that headband, the moment he removed it, Wiji Cheon reverted from the demon instructor back to the boy with innocent eyes.

"Is everyone okay?"

Towards the boy asking timidly, the SRS seniors collapsed on the ground pointed fingers at him in unison.

"You…!"

"Wiji Cheon, you brat!"

"I almost cried earlier because of you!"

Wiji Cheon looked at his seniors sprawled haphazardly on the ground with an expression full of apology.

"I'm sorry. But it wouldn't be effective if I didn't do it seriously, so I had no choice…"

Instructor-for-a-day Wiji Cheon's guidance method had been brutal.

Hardly anyone was currently standing properly on two feet.

Still, everyone had followed through to the end.

It was incredibly tough, but the students felt a strange sense of accomplishment.

*It was fun.*

Wiji Cheon felt the same. He was more exhausted than any other student, yet his face was incredibly bright. He felt like he would want to teach again if given the chance.

"Rest for a bit. I'll prepare dinner!"

As Wiji Cheon left, sighs poured out from everywhere.

"The White Dragon Manor kids train like this ignorantly every day?"

"This isn't something humans should do…"

"I'm going to be sick in bed tomorrow."

"We should have known when he said the person he respected most was Mr. Baek Suryong…"

"I heard earlier that even this is mild compared to Mr. Baek Suryong?"

"Is he crazy?!"

Everyone was shuddering as they recalled the day's training.

Clap!

Yoo Yiran clapped her hands, drawing everyone's attention.

Though she fared better than the other students, Yiran also looked considerably exhausted.

As the SRS President, she objectively evaluated instructor-for-a-day Wiji Cheon's skills.

"It was tough, true… but wasn't he surprisingly good at teaching?"

"...That's true. It seemed rough, but it was precise, and he didn't push us beyond our limits."

Vice President Jeong Yeonhee nodded tiredly in agreement. The other Lieutenants shared similar opinions.

Hidden beneath the radical and violent aspects, Wiji Cheon's swordsmanship instruction was extremely meticulous and detailed.

Other students began to nod one by one as if agreeing.

"Definitely…"

"I realized it after receiving his guidance."

"When pushed to the limit, techniques that usually didn't work suddenly came out."

"His killing intent was so intense… I had no choice but to try my best."

After receiving Wiji Cheon's instruction for just one day, everyone realized how complacent their usual training had been. Because of that, the overall mood was one of self-reflection and repentance.

Yoo Yiran looked around at the students and opened her mouth.

"...So, about that."

This was separate from her feelings for Wiji Cheon.

Yiran genuinely felt that Wiji Cheon's swordsmanship instruction would be of great help to the SRS.

"How about learning from Wiji Cheon for a few more days? I think it's an opportunity that won't come again."

"Umm…"

"A f-few more days?"

"I like it, but I hate it…"

The students of the SRS were all boys and girls who loved the sword.

If they could become stronger, if they could wield the sword better, they were prepared to endure any hardship.

Although they hesitated, eventually everyone agreed.

Yoo Yiran personally went to Wiji Cheon to convey the news.

"If it's okay with you, could we ask you to instruct us for a few more days?"

"...Really?"

Wiji Cheon's face, which had been somewhat downcast, brightened instantly.

It meant his teaching ability had been acknowledged by others.

Wiji Cheon beamed and nodded.

"I'll do my best!"

"P-Please don't try too hard…"

"Actually, I held back today because I was conscious of others. I'll increase the intensity tomorrow!"

"...Let's keep that a secret from the others."

Thus, even after that day, Wiji Cheon occasionally donned the red headband and instructed the SRS students in swordsmanship.

Time at the training camp passed remarkably quickly.

Lush green trees and leaves rustling in the wind.

Sunlight sparkling on morning dew.

Cicadas crying desperately, and youths sweating as they swung their swords with equally powerful shouts.

"Students! Stay sharp!"

Indeed, it was summer.
