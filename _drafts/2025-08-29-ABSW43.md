Crimson Tiger shouldered his large backpack and gazed up at the stone statue in the corner of the training grounds. The figure depicted a man pointing a sword forward.

He immediately recognized it as a statue of Cheon Ahseong. Those bold, masculine features bore a striking resemblance to the real man.

Anyway, today was his first day at the Tempest Academy. The massive complex spread out before him with the training grounds at its center, a central building rising ahead, flanked by dormitory buildings where students lived and ate.

"This isn't an academy, it's a training center for the Association's martial artists," a female voice said.

Crimson Tiger turned to see a girl around fifteen or sixteen with a pretty face and intelligent air about her.

The girl smiled. "First day here?"

"How'd you know?"

"None of the kids who come here every day here stare at that statue with such a dazed expression." The girl pointed at his back. "And that big bag gives it away too."

"Ah!" Crimson Tiger chuckled. She'd figured it out just from his backpack.

"What class are you in?"

"The Sable Class."

The girl's eyes sparkled briefly. They were in the same class. Still, she feigned ignorance as she looked up at the statue. "Is the Martial God really like the rumors say? Does he truly possess such incredible martial arts?"

"Why? You don't believe it?"

The girl nodded. "Heroes are always glorified."

Crimson Tiger chuckled inwardly. *Unfortunately, that's not the case for Cheon Ahseong.*

"Hey, Soyeon! What are you doing over there?" a tall, sturdily built boy shouted, running toward them.

*So she's that Jaegal Soyeon that Yeon told me about!* Crimson Tiger realized.

Jaegal Soyeon snorted. "That's Go Jin, also known as our class's moron number one. He doesn't know why I hate him, and he probably never will."

"How many morons are there?"

"Ten."

"And how many boys are in the class?"

"Ten."

"Hahaha!" Crimson Tiger burst out laughing. It was a fitting remark for this seemingly prim girl from the Jaegal Clan. "Looks like I'll be moron number eleven soon."

Jaegal Soyeon shot him a peculiar look. "Hmm... you don't seem the type."

"Then you've misjudged me."

Go Jin arrived and immediately glared at Crimson Tiger warily. 'I have a crush on Jaegal Soyeon' might as well have been written on his forehead.

"Hey, you bastard, who are you!" he yelled rudely.

"He's the new transfer student," Jaegal Soyeon explained on Crimson Tiger's behalf. With that, she walked toward the classroom gracefully.

Go Jin scowled at Crimson Tiger, warning him that he would not tolerate him speaking to Jaegal Soyeon again. Crimson Tiger avoided Go Jin's gaze submissively.

Go Jin smirked triumphantly, thinking he had won the battle of wills. "Hey, Soyeon! Wait up!" he bellowed, dashing after Jaegal Soyeon.

Crimson Tiger smiled as he watched the boy go. He wasn't angry at all. In fact, he found this situation amusing.

"How refreshing."

As far as he was concerned, the teenage years were unquestionably the most enjoyable time of one's life.

{sep}

Crimson Tiger made his way to the central building and asked a passerby for directions to the Headmaster's Office on the third floor. 

"I'm Gyu Ho, the new student who arrived today," he introduced himself.

Bang Hagi replied, "Welcome. I've been waiting for you."

Crimson Tiger observed the Headmaster, Bang Hagi. He was short and stout with a benevolent face to match, but Crimson's Tiger first impression of the man wasn't his appearance.

It was the fact that his instincts instantly screamed, *He's a master!*

The man before him was a fearsome opponent, an absolute master who had reached the pinnacle. Since he wasn't carrying a sword, it was likely that Bang Hagi specialized in fist fighting or other close-combat martial arts. Additionally, he was far colder and more resolute than he appeared.

Although they had just met, Crimson Tiger did his best to observe the man he would be interacting with from this point forward. Since this place was affiliated with the Association, he had expected the Headmaster to be extraordinary, but Bang Hagi exceeded even that expectation. 

It made sense, though. This was where the First Young Lord and Third Young Lady regularly taught classes. It was only natural that someone exceptional would be posted here.

Crimson Tiger made sure to conceal his aura, reminding himself that as of this moment, he was just a seventeen-year-old brat. After all, this mission was so secret that not even Headmaster Bang Hagi had been informed of the full circumstances. He didn't know that the new transfer student was using a fake identity or that he was here on a bodyguard mission. All he knew was that the new kid had transferred in with the support of a mid-level Association executive, as arranged by the First Young Lord.

"Everyone dreams of entering this academy. Do you know why?" Bang Hagi asked.

Crimson Tiger replied without hesitation, "Because we can get a job at the Association upon graduation."

"Yes, that's precisely why. I assume your goal is also to join the Association?"

"Of course."

"Do your best, then. You may go now. One of our support staff will lead you to your new room. "

Seeing that Bang Hagi didn't seem to have seen through his pretense, Crimson Tiger inwardly heaved a sigh of relief. Bowing, he left the office.

A martial artist guided him to his assigned dorm room. Normally, two people shared a room, but since he had enrolled late, he had one to himself.

"If another student joins, you'll have to share. Try to bear with the loneliness until then."

Crimson Tiger didn't reply. That was unlikely to happen, and he preferred having a room to himself anyway.

After the man left, he inspected the room thoroughly. Only after confirming that everything was in order did he unpack his belongings.

He put on a wrist guard and a chest guard fitted with throwing knives. Anyone who saw them would think it strange for a student to possess such items, but he had them prepared just in case.

He couldn't help but feel anxious and on edge. He understood from firsthand experience how swiftly skilled assassins eliminated their targets, because that was how he did it as well. A single moment of carelessness could mean losing Soeun in an instant.

He then checked his medical supplies. He had prepared various antidotes for poison, among other things. Besides the antidote bead he normally used, he had brought an extra, high-grade one capable of neutralizing most poisons.

Still, he wasn't confident. Assassins who used poison were the most difficult type to contend with. He could only hope that one like that wouldn't appear.

DING DONG!

The bell rang, signaling the end of class. Immediately, the training grounds bustled with noise as the children scattered in all directions.

Students normally had a one-hour break after each two-hour class, though sometimes they had consecutive two-hour classes. On average, each student took three classes a day.

Leaving his room, Crimson Tiger headed for Soeun's office. It was less than a fifth the size of the headmaster's office, and books lined the walls of the small room.

Soeun sat at a desk by a sunlit window, reading. She was beautiful, resembling the blue sky stretching out behind her. Looking at her, he felt that this was where she truly belonged.

His heart fluttered slightly, though he'd never thought of her as a woman before. Still, this feeling was different from his attraction to the Third Young Lady. Soeun's youthful appearance reminded him of his first love.

For this reason, Crimson Tiger hoped she could step away from the bloody jianghu, filled with intrigue and schemes, and live a normal life in a peaceful place.

Soeun looked up at his arrival. "Ah, you must be Gyu Ho, the new student," she greeted warmly.

"That's right. It's a pleasure to meet you, Teacher."

Soeun unfolded a document lying on the side of her desk. "You said this is your first time at a martial arts academy, right?"

"Yes."

"There's no need to feel too pressured. You'll get used to it."

Soeun offered Crimson Tiger the same words of encouragement that she had received when she first started the job. Thanks to the lie the First Young Lord had told on her behalf, no one looked down on her anymore, and teaching had become much less stressful.

She studied Crimson Tiger carefully. The teenage boy had handsome features and a steadfast gaze that would probably make him popular with the girls in class.

*Although, his calm gaze seems familiar somehow.*

A certain person came to mind, but she quickly chuckled to herself and shook off the thought. She knew Crimson Tiger was capable of perfect transformation and top-tier acting, since she never realized that he was the Hundred Flavors Restaurant Manager.

*Could he transform himself into a teenager? No, there's no way.*

While it might be possible to disguise oneself as an older person, transforming oneself to look so much younger was impossible.

Unfortunately, she had no way of knowing the wonders of the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art.

"It's almost time for class. Let's go together," Soeun said, leading Crimson Tiger out of the office.

They arrived at the training grounds where the Sable Class was held. All eyes instantly focused on Crimson Tiger, except Jaegal Soyeon, who was staring elsewhere blankly as if she hadn't spoken to him that morning.

Soeun introduced him, "This is a new friend who's just joined us. Please introduce yourself."

"I'm Gyu Ho. I look forward to working with you all."

Crimson Tiger's greeting was simple, yet the girls' eyes sparkled. His striking good looks stood out among the other boys in the class.

"Hmph!" Go Jin scoffed. He was still displeased about seeing Crimson Tiger with Jaegal Soyeon that morning.

"Pair up. We'll be continuing with free sparring practice today, just like yesterday," Soeun said.

The students each picked up a wooden sword from a rack on the side.

Go Jin pushed aside the nearest student and took their place as Crimson Tiger's opponent. He was smiling, but his intentions couldn't have been more obvious.

The other students exchanged glances, but no one tried to stop Go Jin, even though they knew he was picking on the new kid. Jaegal Soyeon didn't stop him either. In fact, she was the one watching with the most interest.

Crimson Tiger realized that Go Jin must be the strongest in the class. Regardless, he silently picked up a wooden sword without a hint of fear.

A murmur of admiration and anticipation spread through the students.

Soeun shouted, "Begin!" 

Everyone began to spar.

CLACK CLACK CLACK CLACK CLACK!

Go Jin thrust his wooden sword forward, and Crimson Tiger swiftly parried. Before long, Crimson Tiger came to the painful conclusion that the difference in their skills was so vast that it was actually difficult to hold back and match the kid's level.

CRACK! SWOOSH!

In just a few exchanges, Crimson Tiger's wooden sword flew out of his hand. Seizing the opportunity, Go Jin swung at his shoulder.

Before his blow could land, however, Soeun snatched his wooden sword away. Having sensed Go Jin's animosity earlier, she had paid close attention to them in case she needed to intervene. 

"What do you think you're doing? This is a practice duel!" she scolded, then checked on Crimson Tiger. "Are you alright?"

"Yes."

Everyone stared at Crimson Tiger in disappointment.

*He looked like he could fight, judging by his face.*

They thought the new student might have a trick up his sleeve, but he lost too easily. The sense of mystery and expectation that often surrounded a new student their age vanished in an instant.

In contrast, Go Jin was triumphant. At this rate, Jaegal Soyeon will surely be disappointed in him.

Crimson Tiger felt a little sorry for losing so easily, but he nonetheless chuckled to himself. Everything was going according to plan. Being the center of attention was the last thing he wanted.

Another student became Crimson Tiger's opponent. The class continued, and he finished his first day at Tempest Academy without incident.

{sep}

"I'm disappointed."

Crimson Tiger looked up from washing his face, only to see Jaegal Soyeon standing beside him.

"To think you couldn't even handle that moron," she added, scowling. "*And* you lost in just four exchanges."

"It was five."

"Oh, please!" 

"That's why I said I'd be moron number eleven," Crimson Tiger replied calmly as he resumed washing his face.

Jaegal Soyeon tilted her head. "That's strange. My intuition is never wrong."

"What intuition?"

"The moment I saw you standing in front of the statue, I felt this intense, thrilling sensation run through my whole body. I've felt something like this before."

"When?"

"The most prominent example would be when I first saw my grandfather, the Association Chairman."

"The Association Chairman?"

Jaegal Soyeon nodded.

Crimson Tiger stared at her. Neither he nor Cheon Ahseong would intentionally project their aura toward a young girl. If she was telling the truth, then she clearly had a special gift, similar to how White Rabbit could sense even the slightest hint of killing intent.

*Well, it wouldn't be strange for someone from the Jaegal Clan, famous for their intelligence, to have inherited such an ability.*

Crimson Tiger grinned mischievously. "That feeling you had, maybe you just fell for me?"

"What?" Jaegal Soyeon blushed furiously for a moment, but she quickly countered boldly, "I'm not interested in kids!"

"Aren't you also a kid?"

"Also, being old doesn't automatically make someone an adult, and being young doesn't automatically make someone a kid."

Crimson Tiger was familiar with this sentiment. At the core of a teenager's rebellious heart was a desire to grow up quickly. Even he had only realized much, much later just how precious that time he so desperately wanted to escape truly was.

He suggested, "Well then, maybe you were reminded of that feeling because of the Association Chairman's statue?"

Jaegal Soyeon tilted her head, wondering if that could be it, but soon dismissed the idea as ridiculous. It was a statue she saw several times a day coming and going. "No way," she denied, then stared at Crimson Tiger suspiciously. "You're hiding your skills, aren't you?"

"Of course. I'm hiding incredible skills," Crimson Tiger answered without hesitation, turning away as he dried his hair.

Jaegal Soyeon trailed after him. "Don't try to brush it off!"

Crimson Tiger smirked. He felt completely at ease, as he couldn't think of Jaegal Soyeon as anything other than a kid. Moreover, as he had become more proficient in the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art, he had almost perfectly assimilated into his disguise, leaving no trace of awkwardness in his appearance or mannerisms.

Unfortunately, Jaegal Soyeon was persistent. She followed him all the way into his room. 

"Hey, don't ignore me! I don't talk to boys often!"

Crimson Tiger didn't turn around, but he had no intention of ignoring her, even without her saying that. If he was going to deal with the assassin, he needed to adapt perfectly to this place. To that end, she seemed quite helpful.

"You're using the room alone? Lucky!" Jaegal Soyeon scanned the room, inspecting every corner.

"Why did you come here?" Crimson Tiger asked. "You're from the Jaegal Clan, right? You don't need to join the Association to make a living."

"Of course I don't. Even if I graduated as valedictorian, I'd only become a squad leader in the outer court or a regular martial artist in the inner court."

"So why are you attending this academy?"

Jaegal Soyeon's face darkened slightly. "My father sent me here. He thinks that enduring some hardship will temper my reckless and immature personality, but he miscalculated."

"Miscalculated how?"

"His idea that suffering makes one a better person is wrong."

"Isn't it true?"

"Maybe for ordinary kids, but I've lived for seventeen years as a direct descendant of the main house of the Jaegal Clan. Bossing people around rudely is ingrained in my very bones. Do you think suffering for a year or two is going to change me now?"

"Hahaha!" Crimson Tiger couldn't help but laugh. This girl's candidness was endearing.

"Besides, a woman from the main family is destined to be forced into a political marriage, and a smart girl like me especially will be sold off for an important cause..." Jaegal Soyeon's voice trailed off, her brow furrowed slightly.

A realization dawned on Crimson Tiger. Whenever Jaegal Soyeon got angry or upset, she would furrow her brow in an adorable way.

"So why did you come?" she asked, changing the topic back to Crimson Tiger.

"Ever heard of the Gyu Swordsmanship Family?"

"No."

"That's why I'm here. I have to at least get into the Association to bring honor to my family."

"With your skills, you'll have a hard time just feeding yourself."

"I'll manage somehow if I work hard. By the way, what's our teacher like?"

"Her swordsmanship is nothing special compared to her pretty face." 

Crimson Tiger chuckled. He could sense the girl was jealous of Soeun. "If she could wield a sword as well as she wields her looks, she'd be the world's strongest, wouldn't she?"

Jaegal Soyeon's eye twitched at the mention of Soeun's beauty, but she merely pouted and retorted, "You have quite the unique taste, don't you? You... Ah! SO CUTE!"

Suddenly, she spotted the black cat sitting quietly to one side, and fearlessly approached the kitty.

Crimson Tiger watched nervously. This was the first time the black cat had met someone other than himself since fusing with the white cat.

His worry turned out to be unfounded. The black cat responded favorably to Jaegal Soyeon's touch, just like a normal cat would. It was in complete sync with Crimson Tiger's emotions.

He stood by the window and left Jaegal Soyeon, who was smitten with the black cat, to play with it. As he stared out at the training grounds, his gaze deepened.

*How will they approach Soeun?*

The Tempest Academy was bustling with teachers, students, and many other people. No one would notice even if an assassin boldly strode onto those training grounds.

Crimson Tiger considered what he would do in their shoes. First, he would observe Soeun's surroundings. He'd watch her every move to find out who was protecting her, and then formulate a plan to kill her. 

*Would I kill her bodyguard first and then her, or launch a surprise attack and kill only her before escaping?*

He would have to make a decision soon. Ideally, the enemy would attempt the assassination quickly to catch him off guard. If the enemy was confident in his martial arts skills, that was a real possibility.

He had already received word that the masters sent by the First Young Lord and the Third Young Lady had withdrawn the moment he arrived. Even if they hadn't left entirely, they were at least not in Soeun's immediate vicinity, but rather watching from a distance.

However, if they were to intervene in a situation involving an assassin, it could lead to an even more dangerous situation by allowing the enemy to take advantage of the confusion between the Association's different factions. He had to move cautiously and keep all possibilities in mind.

Jaegal Soyeon walked over to Crimson Tiger, holding the black cat with one arm. "What are you thinking about?"

Crimson Tiger lifted his gaze from the training ground to the blue sky. "I was wishing for it to snow," he replied.

Jaegal Soyeon's eyes widened at the unexpected answer. "What? Why are you talking about snow in the middle of spring?"

Crimson Tiger turned to her and smiled. "Dear Young Lady, with your sharp and brilliant intuition, you should know that it's better if you get going before moron number one comes looking to punish this poor moron number eleven."

The black cat leaped out of her hands.

{sep}

That afternoon, Soeun pulled Crimson Tiger aside. "How are things?"

"What do you mean?" he asked, pretending to be puzzled. He knew what she was worried about but feigned ignorance.

Soeun smiled. "Go Jin might be rough, but he's not a bad kid. Please forgive him. Still, if he or any of the other kids try anything again, come find me right away, okay?"

"Yes, I understand."

Soeun gave him a friendly, trustworthy smile. It was common for new students to be hazed at the academy, but the boy in front of her seemed like he'd endure it well. For some reason, she felt certain of it.

"You must be lonely with your family so far away. If you want, I'm available for a chat anytime."

Crimson Tiger hesitated. Whether in reality or on paper, his family was indeed very far away. "Is that really okay?"

Soeun smiled warmly. "Of course."

"Meow!" A black cat poked its head out from inside Crimson Tiger's shirt.

"Ah!" Soeun was startled at first, but a bright grin soon spread across her face. "It's so cute!"

The black cat crawled out of Crimson Tiger's shirt and climbed into her arms. She petted it while the cat nuzzled against her affectionately.

"Would you mind looking after it for a while?" Crimson Tiger asked abruptly.

Soeun blinked in surprise. "Why?"

"I kind of busy adjusting to my new life... so please?"

Soeun looked back and forth between the black cat and Crimson Tiger. "Alright, then. I'll take care of it for a while."

"Thank you."

The black cat eyed Crimson Tiger, then snuggled into Soeun's arms as if to say it understood his feelings.

"How adorable!" 

Looking at the black cat, Crimson Tiger pleaded inwardly, *I'm counting on you.*

{sep}

CLANK!

The Radiant Hall's messaging mechanism activated and rotated until it stopped at a picture of a dragon.

Jo Birang grabbed the incoming message and reported, "Flying Dragon has eliminated the entire Nine Revolutions Gang."

Hong Sabaek nodded approvingly. He had thought it would take more time to eliminate the group, but this was Flying Dragon, after all.

"However, he was injured in the process," Jo Birang quickly added.

Hong Sabaek blinked in shock. "Injured? How badly?"

"It's not a serious injury. He's requested a leave of absence until he's fully recovered."

"You're sure it's not serious?"

"Yes."

"Then that's fine. Process it as usual."

Jo Birang quickly drafted the order and brought it over. Hong Sabaek stamped it with the official seal of the Radiant Hall, and he placed it back into the opening in the wall mechanism. Just like that, Flying Dragon was granted a brief medical leave.

"Can we finally get some rest now?" Im Yeongdal asked as he stretched, looking exhausted.

Although the newcomer had quickly learned the ropes at the Radiant Hall, they had still been busy all morning handling the Twelve Phantoms' missions. Since only three people were handling the workload of four, they were naturally overwhelmed.

"When are they going to send us a new rookie?" Jo Birang complained.

Hong Sabaek replied grumpily, "Forget it. Never mind finding a new guy, we might have to start looking for work elsewhere."

"What do you mean?"

"What do you think I mean? For seven years, none of us realized that we had a spy in our midst. Do you think they'll just leave us alone?"

Jo Birang's eyes widened. "Don't tell me you mean we're all going to get fired."

Hong Sabaek rolled his eyes as if it were obvious. "The possibility is high."

"Very high," Im Yeongdal chimed in.

"But I just started work here!" Jo Birang protested loudly.

Im Yeongdal clicked his tongue and teased, "You'll be the record-holder for the fastest dismissal in the history of the Radiant Hall."

Jo Birang had thought he was the most successful of his cohort when he started working at the Radiant Hall, but now he was suddenly facing the threat of being fired. He scoffed. "Hmph! Then I'll just quit and become a schoolteacher."

Hong Sabaek stared at the defiant young man incredulously. "Do you really think teaching is an easy job?"

"What's so hard about it? I know the material, and they're just kids. What's the problem?"

Im Yeongdal shook his head. "This is why people say there are no true educators these days. It's because of thoughtless guys like him who think they can teach."

Jo Birang pouted. "Are teachers the only ones lacking? There aren't any good students, either. Speaking of which, I heard that Crimson Tiger was sent to an academy this time."

"Yes, we're sending him there for a vacation."

"Wow! I'm so jealous. He gets to hang out with all the fresh-faced kids."

"Is Crimson Tiger you? Do you think he'd enjoy something like that?"

"What? You think Crimson Tiger doesn't have childhood memories? The way I see it, Senior, you view my Crimson Tiger as a cold-blooded man with no blood or tears."

""*Your* Crimson Tiger?"" Hong Sabaek and Im Yeongdal exchanged incredulous looks.

"Of course," Jo Birang said triumphantly. "After all, Crimson Tiger and I are comrades who have overcome a crisis together by entrusting our lives to each other."

"You mean you entrusted your life to Crimson Tiger, and he saved your ass from a crisis."

"Hey! Don't forget! I saved the Radiant Hall! Me!"

"I see you saved it again while I was away."

Jo Birang pouted, and Hong Sabaek laughed heartily. They liked teasing their junior a lot, but ultimately, if it hadn't been for the Jo Birang and his obsessive compulsive disorder, things would have gotten really bad.

Jo Birang asked seriously, "So, what do you plan to do if you get fired, Senior?"

Hong Sabaek's expression darkened. "Will we even have the chance to go job hunting?"

"Huh? What do you mean by that?" Jo Birang tilted his head in confusion.

Im Yeongdal frowned. "We know too much."

Jo Birang winced in fright. "D-Don't tell me... they're going to e-eliminate us!" He clamped a hand over his own mouth, then whispered, "S-Shouldn't we escape this place right now...?"

THWACK!

Reeling from a smack to the head, Jo Birang turned turned around tearfully to see Eom Baekyang standing there.

"That would be impossible even if your soulmate, Crimson Tiger, came to rescue you, Scholar Jo!"

"Sob! Are you really planning to dispose of us?"

"Of course, and you especially! You know the most secrets among us, don't you? I'd bet you're number one on the elimination list."

"Gasp!" Horrified, Jo Birang shouted, "This can't be happening! Aren't we employees of the New Righteous Association, which prioritizes diplomacy over violence? To think our trust would be betrayed like this! How could those who call themselves righteous commit murder just to silence someone!"

SMACK!

Jo Birang's head started ringing from another blow.

"Oh please, get a grip! Who on earth taught this kid to speak so bluntly? Don't you know that some things shouldn't be said out loud? Ugh, just thinking about it is terrifying." Eom Baekyang pretended to shiver as he hurried into the Director's office.

Only then did Hong Sabaek and Im Yeongdal start snickering, and Jo Birang realized it had all been a joke.

"You guys are so mean!" he cried.

The two teased Jo Birang again.

"What's there to worry about? Crimson Tiger will surely save you."

"Of course. You're comrades who overcame the brink of death together."

Jo Birang pouted. "Stop teasing me and tell me properly. What's going to happen to us?"

"What do you mean, what's going to happen? If we get fired from the Radiant Hall, we'll be assigned somewhere else. The only time we'd actually be killed is if we broke the confidentiality rules. After all, we're not the Evil Coalition, which commits murder just to silence someone, right?"

"Phew!" Jo Birang heaved a sigh of relief.

Watching him, Hong Sabaek and Im Yeongdal roared with laughter.

{sep}

Eom Baekyang stared blankly out the window of the director's office. "I just talked to the three stooges. Thankfully, none of them seem too traumatized."

The fact that a colleague of seven years was a spy caught everyone by surprise. He'd been worried it might cause strife within their workplace, but it seemed everyone was coping well.

"That's a relief," Gu Yangseo replied absentmindedly as he pored through a thick stack of documents.

Eom Baekyang turned to face him. "I heard you sent Crimson Tiger to an academy as a student."

Finally, Gu Yangseo raised his head. "That's right."

"As expected, was it his request?" Eom Baekyang asked knowingly.

Gu Yangseo nodded, silently confirming that the First Young Lord had given the order. 

Eom Baekyang sighed. Whenever the Radiant Hall assigned cover identities to the Twelve Phantoms, they were never assigned to be academy students who had to attend school every day. As such, he suspected there was a hidden reason, and the answer confirmed his theory.

"Why?"

"Let's go hear it directly from him."

"Pardon?"

Gu Yangseo stood up and walked out of the office. Eom Baekyang quickly followed, realizing that Gu Yangseo was on his way to meet the First Young Lord.

After leaving the Radiant Hall, the two men disguised themselves and headed to the meeting place. The location was the same construction site where Gu Yangseo had previously met the First Young Lord.

Gu Yangseo introduced Eom Baekyang, "This is Deputy Director Eom. I rely on him a great deal."

As a matter of fact, Eom Baekyang had met the First Young Lord several times at Association events. He had even crossed paths with Baek Museong in the operations room during the recent spy incident. This was, however, the first time Gu Yangseo had formally introduced him to the First Young Lord.

He was deeply moved. This proved that Gu Yangseo considered him his true successor.

"It's a pleasure to meet you."

"I am still greatly lacking."

After exchanging greetings, the three men sat side by side beneath a large tree. Baek Museong was drenched in sweat, perhaps because he was working without using qi. Furthermore, he lacked the typical arrogant air of those in power. 

Eom Baekyang felt he had a strange sort of charisma. He didn't know what terrifying things were hidden in the First Young Lord's heart, but he thought that the man's simple, down-to-earth appearance was one of the reasons he had become who he was today.

Gu Yangseo asked cautiously, "How are things progressing regarding the recent incident?"

"A high-level meeting will be held soon. A decision will be made there, but don't worry too much."

"Do you have a plan in mind?"

"It's not much of a plan. There is just one thing I'm counting on."

"What is that?"

"My Junior Sister."

"Pardon?"

"My dear Junior Sister also needs the Mystery Pavilion Master, doesn't she?" 

Gu Yangseo nodded, finally understanding what Baek Museong meant. The aforementioned high-level meeting was one that both the First Young Lord and the Third Young Lady would attend. If they had different opinions, that would be one thing, but if they both tried to protect Yagong and him, they might be able to avoid the worst-case scenario of losing their jobs. All that was left was to trust the two young leaders.

He subtly changed the subject. "Crimson Tiger has enrolled in the Tempest Academy. We plan to give him a break for the time being and won't assign him any missions."

Although it was the First Young Lord's order, he called it a break since Eom Baekyang was present, phrasing it as if it were the Radiant Hall's official stance.

"Is that so?" Baek Museong replied with finality. As this matter concerned his daughter, he didn't want to discuss it in detail. "He is an excellent martial artist. Treat him well."

Gu Yangseo picked up on the hint immediately. "Understood. We will take our leave now."

Gu Yangseo and Eom Baekyang rose from their seats.

After walking for a while, Eom Baekyang finally asked, "What on earth is going to happen at the academy?"

Gu Yangseo continued on in silence for a moment before answering, "The same things that always happen."

"Director!"

"You've already forgotten what I told you before."

Eom Baekyang's face darkened. "Are you talking about becoming more ruthless? That's a different issue altogether. The Tempest Academy is a school for children!"

Gu Yangseo halted in his tracks. "Since when have those in power distinguished between children and adults?"

Eom Baekyang was left speechless. Those were truly heartless words, yet at the same time, they were the reality of the jianghu. While the words were infuriating, he couldn't refute them.

He sighed. "At least we have someone reliable on the job, right?"

Gu Yangseo patted Eom Baekyang on the shoulder. "Yes, we should believe in Crimson Tiger. He'll do well, as he always has."
