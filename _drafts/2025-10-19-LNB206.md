The Heaven Ascending Tiger Steps was a monstrous martial art that cast formations just by walking. It was a secret among secrets, mastered only by a few direct descendants of the Seomoon Clan.

Moreover, in the clan's hundreds of years, no one had perfected it like Seomoon Hwa. Along with the Brain Enhancing Art, which stimulated the brain to maximize human intellect, it was one of the two pillars that supported the Seomoon Clan. Even Seomoon Hye-Ryung had to focus her mind to use it, but Seomoon Hwa deployed it as naturally as breathing.

"Everyone, close your eyes!" Ha Jin-Wol shouted. "If you're deceived by the illusion, your vital essence will be depleted and you'll die!"

At his cry, everyone quickly shut their eyes. Seomoon Hwa's smile only deepened.

"It's useless," he said. "These aren't simple illusions. You can't handle this just by closing your eyes, child."

"UGH!"

Seomoon Hwa's inner qi was as vast and deep as the sea. He'd embedded that qi with every step, and it now formed the sturdy pillars supporting his formations. Their power was on a completely different level.

A vast ocean appeared before Ha Jin-Wol's eyes. He was a tiny leaf floating on a boundless sea. A storm raged. Waves the size of houses struck him relentlessly while winds laced with thunder and lightning clawed at his body. Each strike tore open new wounds, and blood flowed freely. The illusion held the power of reality.

*Ugh! I have to break the formations.*

The task was nearly impossible. Seomoon Hwa was regarded as the smartest man in the world. Though Ha Jin-Wol was a genius, he still lacked much in comparison, especially in experience and application. Even in a normal fight, his chances of winning were almost nonexistent, but he couldn't give up. The lives of Tang Gi-Mun, Tang Mi-Ryeo, and the others depended on him.

Seo Mu-Sang, Tang Gi-Mun, and Tang Mi-Ryeo also suffered within their own illusions. Each vision was different, for Seomoon Hwa's formations stimulated the parts of a person they found most unsettling. Ha Jin-Wol felt terror at the sea he'd never visited. Tang Gi-Mun saw himself surrounded by poisonous creatures he'd never seen before. Seo Mu-Sang swung his sword against hundreds, thousands of martial artists. And Tang Mi-Ryeo, unexpectedly, saw the figure of a man.

*Mu-Won?*

The man standing there, holding a sword tinged with the color of ink, was undoubtedly Jin Mu-Won. He pointed his sword right at her.

"Why?" she asked.

Jin Mu-Won didn't answer. His eyes flashed with killing intent, and his endlessly cold face frightened her. Her own reflection in his eyes looked utterly pathetic. A crack appeared in her expression.

"You… why?" Her voice echoed in the illusory space as fat teardrops streamed down her cheeks. "Why won't you look at me? I'm right here by your side… Why won't you even give me a single glance?"

An emotional voice burst out of her. The feelings she'd kept tightly buried inside erupted like a flood. She knew it was an illusion, but she couldn't control her emotions. The Jin Mu-Won reflected in her eyes was just so heartless.

His sword swung, wounding her chest. A fountain of blood spurted from the parted flesh. It hurt so much she couldn't breathe. She collapsed, gasping, as Jin Mu-Won continued to swing his sword at her. His emotionless gaze hurt more than the wounds. She crouched on the floor and did nothing but shed tears. Her chest, her heart, was being mercilessly trampled. She was so miserable, so sad, that she wept endlessly.

Tang Mi-Ryeo's world was collapsing.

*A being like me… is not needed in this world.*

"No, Mi-Ryeo."

Tang Gi-Mun's scream went unheard. Tang Mi-Ryeo closed her eyes, her ears, and her heart. She would be trapped in her own world to die.

"Mi-Ryeo! Please!"

Tang Gi-Mun, barely holding on in his own illusion, saw Tang Mi-Ryeo curled up like a fetus, sinking endlessly into her inner self. He realized inner demons had found their way into her heart. The fiends that grow in the heart devour everything. A Tang Mi-Ryeo completely consumed by them would no longer be his niece, his only disciple. She was the most precious person who would inherit everything from him.

His eyes became bloodshot. He could see Seo Mu-Sang and Ha Jin-Wol also buried in their illusions, killing themselves. And Seomoon Hwa was watching them, smiling. The moment he saw the one who'd lit the fires of hell laughing at their screams, something inside his head snapped. The reason that made him who he was vanished. Only rage remained. He knew this was also an effect of the formations, but he didn't care.

*I will never forgive you, Seomoon Hwa.*

This was a world Seomoon Hwa had created. Just as Yeon Cheon-Hwa had created a world of the sword with his blade, Seomoon Hwa had created a world he ruled with formations. Here, everything moved according to his will. In a world so completely under his control, inflicting any damage on him was nearly impossible. Not by ordinary means.

*However, I'm not an ordinary person. I haven't learned martial arts and my stamina is poor, but I have a weapon that others don't possess.*

The surrounding scenery changed from moment to moment. It seemed random, but there was a rule to it that he hadn't yet recognized.

TSUTSUTSU!

Fine powder trickled from both of Tang Gi-Mun's sleeves. The powder, flowing like black sand, scattered into the air and vanished. Seomoon Hwa, his gaze fixed on Ha Jin-Wol, didn't notice at all.

Ha Jin-Wol had his eyes closed, resisting the illusion. Even in that state, he was wracking his brain to solve the formation. Seomoon Hwa couldn't help but admire him.

*This fellow isn't just some bookworm shut-in with a good head on his shoulders. He has grit. Among those with sharp minds, such a person is truly rare.*

The problem with so-called geniuses was that they calculated everything with their heads. They could deduce outcomes from just a few circumstances, so they never moved their bodies for tasks with obvious results, but Ha Jin-Wol was different. He possessed a mind that surpassed the geniuses of the Seomoon Clan and the decisiveness to act on his thoughts. He had a rare advantage.

*My judgment was correct. If I let this man live, he'll become the greatest threat to the main family.*

Killing intent flickered in Seomoon Hwa's eyes. Until now, he'd only cast the formations and watched, but it seemed he could only be at ease if he snuffed out their lives with his own hands. He was the type of person who never left loose ends.

He took out the folding fan tucked into his belt.

CHWAAREUREUK!

The Rising Dragon Fan, painted with a dragon ascending through clouds, opened wide. It was both a sacred object of the Patriarch and a weapon. Sharp energy that could sting the eyes radiated from it. Seomoon Hwa aimed the fan at Ha Jin-Wol. There was no need for any special technique. He just had to release his energy. A large hole would be pierced through Ha Jin-Wol's neck.

With his eyes closed, Ha Jin-Wol didn't notice Seomoon Hwa approaching. Even if he had known, he had no way to resist.

Just as Seomoon Hwa was about to extend the fan, he faltered. His complexion changed strangely.

"This is?"

He felt a foreign and uncomfortable energy inside his body. It was as if thousands, tens of thousands of ants were crawling through his blood vessels.

"Hmph! Poison?" Seomoon Hwa's gaze turned to Tang Gi-Mun.

The only one here who could use poison was Tang Gi-Mun, but he wasn't worried. He'd already reached the absolute realm. His body was naturally immune to a thousand poisons, and he could burn away most toxins just by circulating his inner qi once.

Seomoon Hwa circulated his inner qi with a light heart. The moment he did, his complexion changed completely. The qi center that stored his qi hardened like stone. The more he tried to move his qi, the faster his qi center solidified.

He glared at Tang Gi-Mun. "What kind of poison is this?"

"The Undetectable Blood Worm."

"The Undetectable Blood Worm?"

"It's something I created to deal with people like you. Its lethality isn't very high, but it gnaws away at inner qi, eventually depleting it. No matter who you are, it won't be easy to expel."

"Are you sure about that?"

"I'd bet my life on it."

For the first time, Seomoon Hwa's eyebrow twitched.

"You?"

"Let me add one more thing. The more you try to circulate your qi, the harder your qi center will become. And it'll attack your body."

A monster that devoured qi, the Undetectable Blood Worm attacked more powerfully the stronger one's internal energy was. The tickling sensation and pain, like tens of thousands of ants crawling and biting, was difficult to endure even for an absolute master. Indeed, Seomoon Hwa's expression twisted horribly. The pain, as if ants were chewing on his entire body, was something he was experiencing for the first time. It wasn't easy to bear.

He watched with wrathful eyes. "I seem to have misjudged you."

"And how did you judge me?"

"As a fool who possessed the wonderful power of poison but couldn't use it. That's why I was careless. I thought you'd never be able to use poison."

"Your thoughts were probably right, senior. Until now, I was a fool, but from now on, I'll change. You made me this way. From now on, I'll use every poison I possess."

"Welcome. To the world of monsters. Now that you've crossed that line once, a second time won't be difficult. That's how everyone goes from being human to being a monster."

Tang Gi-Mun's expression twisted at Seomoon Hwa's mocking words. He didn't refute them. He too felt that everything had changed.

"However," Seomoon Hwa said, "if you thought you could stop me with just this, you're mistaken. I'm Seomoon Hwa."

He circulated his inner qi. Because of the Undetectable Blood Worm, his qi center was hardening more than stone and the pain throughout his body doubled, but he didn't even blink.

"CHAHAT!"

It was then. Suddenly, someone rushed toward Seomoon Hwa.

SHWIIK!

A silver trajectory cut through the air.

The man swinging his sword at Seomoon Hwa was none other than Seo Mu-Sang. He'd overcome the illusion and was now attacking.

"You insect-like thing."

Seomoon Hwa's complexion changed completely. Thanks to Seo Mu-Sang, he couldn't fully concentrate on expelling the Undetectable Blood Worm. He recognized the swordsmanship as a third-rate style used by low-ranking warriors, the Blue Cloud Sword Technique.

"With something like that…"

Normally, Seomoon Hwa could've killed Seo Mu-Sang with the flick of a finger, but because the Undetectable Blood Worm restricted him, the time it took to deal with him dragged on.

At that moment, Ha Jin-Wol, who'd kept his eyes closed until now, opened them. "Now I understand."
