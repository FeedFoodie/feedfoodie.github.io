Dark clouds gathered, dimming the surroundings. Thunder rumbled in the distance a few times before rain began pouring down.

SHWAAAA!

Crimson Tiger stood beneath the eaves of an estate located on the outskirts, far from the Association's main headquarters, watching the falling rain. Unknown to him, Ju Hwayin used this location as a safe house for important matters, such as her secret conversation with Mystery Pavilion Master Yagong. Today was the day he was supposed to hand Soeun over, so the security around the estate was unprecedented. Even through the rain, he could sense the auras of martial artists hidden in ambush.

Soeun was sound asleep in one of the rooms, her fatigue having taken over the moment she relaxed, relieved at finally escaping.

Honestly, Crimson Tiger was only more exhausted than she was. He felt that he moment he lay down amid the soothing sound of the falling rain, he would pass out immediately but he couldn't. Soon, one of the Third Young Lady's aides would arrive.

*...She'll probably send Gahwa.*

Crimson Tiger narrowed his eyes to hide his burning rage. He had no lingering attachments to Gahwa anymore, not since the moment she mentioned Seohyeon.

SHWAAAAA! SPLASH!

As the rain intensified, muddy water splashed onto Crimson Tiger's pants, and he stepped back.

Suddenly, he found himself comparing this situation to his current predicament. He couldn't stop the rain from falling, but he could avoid the splattering mud. Similarly, while he couldn't avoid getting caught up in the successors' power struggle, he could at least protect himself and his loved ones from becoming casualties.

SWHAAAAA! KWAAAAAH!

The sound of the rain grew louder, slowly transitioning into the roar of a waterfall as his memories of two years ago surfaced.

{sep}

Despite being soaked by the pouring waterfall, the scent of blood refused to fade. Although the stains had been washed off his clothes, the stench lingered in his mind. He desperately wanted to run straight to Seohyeon, but he feared she might catch the scent of blood.

Most missions were dangerous, but none had been as perilous as this one. If not for a woman named Gahwa, he would have died for certain. If not for her, he would never have seen Seohyeon again.

Although he had been through countless life-and-death experiences, the thought of leaving his daughter behind, of dying before she did, made his chest tighten. He accepted fate in most matters, surrendering to the will of the heavens, but this was the one thing he could never compromise on.

"Kangho," someone called out to him.

He turned around, only to see his master standing atop a boulder, watching him. "Master," he greeted.

"Get out of there already. Are you trying to strip off your own skin?"

"Yes," Kwon Kangho admitted, but obediently stepped out and dried himself off regardless.

His master silently observed the numerous scars and wounds dotting his disciple's body. "Are you eating properly?"

"Yes, what about..." Kwon Kangho's voice trailed off. He had intended to ask how his master had been, but his throat clammed up, and words failed him. It had only been a few years, yet his master seemed like he had aged over a decade. 

The guilt was crushingly heavy. All of it was his fault, because he was an unworthy disciple.

Still, his master smiled as he replied to the unasked question, "I am perfectly well, peaceful in spirit, healthy in body, with no troubles at all. Hohoho!"


That same, unchanging smile.

"...Master."

"I'm fine, truly fine. I have Hyeon with me, how could I not be fine? I have no worries at all.., but what about you?"

If he were to be honest, it was exhausting. Endless missions, killing people, even if they were evil, but Master was here, standing firm, protecting his daughter. That made everything bearable.

"Let's go. Hyeon's been waiting since morning."

He walked through the forest alongside Master.

He had sacrificed everything for his daughter, but Master had done the same, for the child of his disciple.

Sacrifice? No, that was the wrong word. Calling it a sacrifice would be an insult to the depth of Master's love. He loved Hyeon more than if she were his own granddaughter.

His wandering thoughts were interrupted by the sight of a thick mist ahead.

"The fog is strange."

Master smiled.

"You noticed? You still have a long way to go."

At first, he thought he was being scolded, but then he realized Master was speaking to himself.

"I've been studying formations as a pastime lately. This is the Primordial Four Symbols Formation (四象混元陣), designed to briefly disorient travelers. It's still incomplete, just a rough imitation. If I want to move it at will, I'll need a few more years of study."

"Is it because of Hyeon?"

"Who can predict the future? When time allows, one should always make preparations in advance."

Master was already planning for possible dangers.

Considering the work he did, it was a natural concern. He had nearly died on this mission. There was no telling when another crisis might arise, one that could endanger Seohyeon.

A short while later, they arrived at a small cottage near a waterfall.

The Medicine Immortal Sagong Hu sat on a wooden bench in the yard, handling medicinal herbs.

Crimson Tiger bowed respectfully. Without him... today wouldn't exist.

"Have you been well?"

"You're here?"

Sagong Hu gave a warm smile.

"Hyeon is sleeping."

Last time, Master had told him—Sagong Hu had admitted he hadn't expected him to make it this far.

The feeling was mutual. He had never imagined Sagong Hu would devote himself so fully to Seohyeon.

Maybe it was because he was still young, but Crimson Tiger had always been wary of jianghu. He had distrusted the world, where people were two-faced, where greed and betrayal ran rampant, where warriors would do anything to grow stronger, but Sagong Hu showed him that jianghu wasn't only that.

"The treatment is progressing well."

"Thank you."

His gratitude was beyond words. Sagong Hu now treated Seohyeon like his own granddaughter.

Sagong Hu, too, had lost his family in his youth and lived alone.

Now that he thought about it, it was the same for himself, for Master, whose past was unknown, each of them had led lonely lives.

"We chose this place in the North Sea for a reason. Until now, we've been protecting the child with yang energy, but from now on, we must do the opposite. For the next two years of treatment, yang energy will be harmful to her. We must only use yin energy. Exactly twelve times a day, her meridians must be struck with the extreme yin force. This treatment can only be performed by a master who has trained in extreme yin techniques."

Crimson Tiger was shocked. The Asura's Eight Narakas was a martial art based on extreme yang. Neither he nor Master could help at all.

Sagong Hu smiled as he continued.

"Fortunately, we were able to borrow the Glacial Jade (寒氷玉) from the North Sea Ice Palace."

Glacial Jade was not only a treasure that emitted pure yin energy, but also one of the ten great artifacts of the North Sea Ice Palace, capable of strengthening qi for any jianghu warrior.

Master spoke casually, as if making an offhand remark.

"The matter must have taken a great toll on you, Sagong Seonsaeng."

Sagong Hu shook his head as if to say that such words of praise should be directed at Master instead. There was no way the North Sea Ice Palace, known for its extreme isolation, had handed over the Glacial Jade easily.

Even without hearing the details, it was obvious how much the two had struggled.

Without a word, I bowed deeply to Sagong Hu on the spot. Master nodded approvingly, as if to say that Sagong Hu's efforts had indeed been immense.

Sagong Hu quickly rushed over to lift me up.

"Come now, there's no need for this."

He took my hand and said, "There's not much time left now. Just endure a little longer."

Two more years, just two more years to endure.

At that moment, the door creaked open slightly.

The small head peeking out was Seohyeon.

Now eight years old, she had grown so much I almost didn't recognize her.

The moment I saw her face, my chest tightened.

Tears blurred my vision.

Seohyeon ran across the yard and threw herself into my arms.

"Daddy!"

I had thought she might feel awkward after so long apart, but Seohyeon acted as if we had just seen each other yesterday. Perhaps Master had been telling her about me all this time.

"Have you been well?"

"Daddy, I missed you!"

It was me who felt awkward and nervous.

"Daddy, are you crying?"

"No. My Seohyeon has grown so beautiful."

"Hehe. Grandpa said that when the world's best beauty contest happens, he'll take me there."

"The world's best beauty is a given, you should enter the greatest beauty contest of all time."

"Should I?"

I hugged Seohyeon tightly once more.

Smiling as they watched, Master and Sagong Hu quietly stepped away.

Seohyeon and I sat side by side on the wooden bench. She stared intently at my face.

"What is it?"

"You look like you've lost a lot of weight since last time."

"You remember?"

"Of course. I fall asleep every night thinking about that day."

I stroked Seohyeon's hair.

"You're listening well to Grandpa, right?"

"Of course! Grandpa teaches me to read and even how to play the flute."

"That must be nice."

"Hehe, but I still like being with Daddy more."

Unconsciously, I clenched my teeth. Guilt washed over me—I had missed the most important years of my child's life.

"Daddy, when can we live together?"

"Soon."

"Two years?"

"You heard?"

"Yeah. Grandpa said in two years, we'll be able to live together. That by then, I'll be all better."

"That's right. In two years, we'll be together."

"I hope time flies by quickly."

Seohyeon beamed at me.

Yes, I truly wished time would pass quickly. If there were a martial art that could speed up time, I would train in it relentlessly.

Once you're fully healed, Daddy has so many things he wants to do with you. We'll travel across jianghu, showing you only the good things, feeding you every delicacy under the sky.

"Until then, Daddy has to stay healthy too."

"Mm."

"Don't overwork yourself."

"As you command! The greatest beauty of all time has given an order, and I shall obey without question."

"Hehehe."

Seohyeon rested her head against my arm.

"Daddy, I'm sleepy."

We had barely spoken, yet she was already exhausted.

"I went to bed early yesterday so I could play with you when you arrived..."

Seohyeon dozed off mid-sentence.

Without realizing it, I let out a deep sigh. Overwhelmed, I lifted my head to the sky.

Even this little child was fighting against her illness, struggling to overcome her fate.

Yes, I had to overcome mine too.

For the first time, I felt that coming here was the right decision. It felt as though I had gained all the strength I needed to endure the next two years.

Carefully, I carried Seohyeon inside and laid her on the bed.

For a long time, I stood there, watching my daughter sleep peacefully. Just being alive, just being able to see her like this, it made me so happy.

I had nothing more to ask for.

She could be the ugliest in the world, the dullest in the world—I didn't care. As long as she could live her life in good health.

"Just hold on a little longer."

I stepped outside.

Creeeak.

As the door opened, Ju Hwayin entered the estate.

"You're out here?"

At her arrival, the memories of that day two years ago were washed away by the falling rain.

SWOOOOSH!

Holding an umbrella, she was disguised as Gahwa.

"It was stifling inside."

Crimson Tiger neither showed hostility nor acted friendly.

"I knew you'd pull it off."

"There's a problem."

"What problem?"

"Another of the Twelve Phantoms witnessed me rescuing her."

The issue was that Drunken Monkey and Green Snake had seen Soeun during the extraction, but the real problem wasn't Drunken Monkey or Green Snake. They wouldn't suspect anything about her. No, they wouldn't even care. They would simply assume she was part of the mission. He would have thought the same if he were in their place.

The problem was their handler. The handlers would report on the extraction, and that report would be recorded. If they were lucky, it would be overlooked, but that was only if they were very lucky.

Ju Hwayin nodded.

"I'll handle that."

It was an important issue, but Crimson Tiger didn't press it further.

She called herself the Third Young Lady's subordinate, but at times like these, the trust she exuded carried a weight beyond that of a mere underling. If her presence was this imposing, then what kind of presence did the Third Young Lady have?

"Go inside. They're waiting for you."

As if the conversation was over, Crimson Tiger turned away coldly.

"Wait a moment."

"Is there more to say?"

"You should at least know when we'll be making a move, shouldn't you?"

"What do you mean?"

"Not yet. So take your time deciding. Whether you'll trust us or not. Whether you'll leave this place or stay here."

It might have been consideration, but Crimson Tiger didn't appreciate it at all.

"As I said before, we will protect you."

"Then who protects you?"

Crimson Tiger's sarcasm was met with a playful response from Ju Hwayin.

"If you're worried, why don't you protect us?"

"Not interested."

"I figured. I won't expect that much, but for now, I want you to stay away from the Association and follow the Radiant Hall's orders."

"Why?"

"If you act unpredictably, even your last mission will come under scrutiny."

Crimson Tiger stared at Ju Hwayin for a moment. Seeing her like this left an unpleasant feeling, but there was no room for sentiment now. She was more dangerous than an enemy with a clear label.

"Fine."

"Thanks. And if you ever need to see me, rent the twelfth boat by the lakeside and borrow three fishing rods. Then, you'll find me."

Crimson Tiger nodded silently and walked away. The twelfth boat referred to the Twelve Phantoms, and the third rod referred to her place as the third among the Twelve Zodiacs. Was she using this same method to meet other Ghost Generals as well?

Ju Hwayin watched Crimson Tiger's departing figure for a long time.

Beside her, Lee Dansim approached. Glancing toward the room where Soeun was, she asked, "Will you meet her now?"

Ju Hwayin shook her head.

"She's too on edge right now. Let her rest for a few days."

"Understood, but I have something to ask."

"What is it?"

"Forgive my rudeness in advance."

While Lee Dansim spoke solemnly, Ju Hwayin remained composed.

"Just ask."

"Are you truly giving him time? Are you really planning to protect him?"

"What makes you ask that?"

"Because I believe he won't simply stay put."

Ju Hwayin chuckled.

"This isn't something to laugh about."

"Fine. Then what should we do? Should we eliminate him?"

Lee Dansim hesitated for a moment before speaking with resolve.

"Yes. If possible."

Ju Hwayin quietly observed Lee Dansim.

Unable to withstand her indifferent gaze, Lee Dansim clenched her eyes shut.

She had braced herself for a slap, but instead, an unexpected answer came.

"Yes, if he can be eliminated, that would be best."

"What?"

Lee Dansim's eyes snapped open.

"But there's no need."

"What do you mean?"

"Because someone else will try to kill him."

Lee Dansim still looked puzzled.

Ju Hwayin spoke in a soft tone.

"Imagine the reverse situation. Suppose he killed my child under my command. I like to think I'm a fairly magnanimous person, so perhaps I'd decide not to kill him. I'm certain of it, but..."

At last, Lee Dansim understood.

Lee Dansim's expression turned somber.

"...I wouldn't be able to let it go."

Ju Hwayin nodded with a faint smile.

"Senior Brother also has loyal subordinates like you."

Lee Dansim swallowed hard. It was Jin Chung.

"You never intended to protect him in the first place."

"He knows, too, that I'm lying."

"He knows?"

"Of course. He's an incredibly perceptive person, especially when it comes to survival."

"But the Young Lady...?"

"You're wondering if I love him? I have a feeling you'll ask me that question again and again before this is over. So I'll answer it now. Even if you ask a hundred times, my answer will be the same. I still love him. I will always love him. Even after he dies, I won't be able to forget him. Even if I kill him with my own hands, even if I die by his hand, I will love him. I have no intention of ever loving anyone again, so he is my last love."

"Young Lady..."

"But just because I love him doesn't mean I'll go against the tide. That is who I am, and that is my love for him."

Ju Hwayin let out a sigh and looked up at the sky.

"Fine, I admit it. It's such a selfish love, but what can I do? That's all I've ever learned about love..."

{sep}

When Crimson Tiger returned to the safehouse, Eom Baekyang was waiting there.

"I knew you'd pull it off."

Officially, the mission was a success.

They had left Six's necklace on Snakeflower's corpse and reported that Six was Baek Soeun.

The Ironblood Commander had been assassinated, and with Yeon now on their side, the Radiant Hall fully believed the report.

"You've been through a lot."

"It was nothing."

Eom Baekyang looked at Crimson Tiger with admiration. The man standing before him seemed to have no concept of failure. He had assassinated the Ironblood Commander inside his own office, flawlessly carried out the mission to eliminate the First Young Lord's bloodline, and escaped. The money in his hands felt well spent.

"Here, take this."

Eom Baekyang handed him an envelope.

Inside was thirty-five thousand nyang, five thousand more than the promised amount.

"The extra five thousand is a reward for the previous mission."

That referred to the operation to rescue Radiant Hall Director Gu Yangseo. This extra five thousand likely came from the Director's own pocket. It was more than expected.

Even though the mission hadn't been completed, Crimson Tiger took the money without hesitation.

Refusing it would immediately raise suspicion. And in the end, whether he accepted the money or not no longer mattered. Once everything was exposed, it wouldn't be a matter of morality, it would be a matter of life and death.

"I heard you were injured. How are you?"

"I can manage."

Eom Baekyang pulled something else from his robe.

"Take this."

Opening the wooden case, Crimson Tiger found a root of tuber fleeceflower inside. Judging by its size, it was worth at least several thousand nyang. With this, he could instantly gain two or three years' worth of cultivation.

"I won't refuse."

Crimson Tiger accepted it without hesitation. This tuber fleeceflower would more than replenish the internal energy and qi he had spent during the mission.

"Rest here for a few days. After that, your handler will provide you with a new cover identity. You'll hear the details from her."

"Understood."

"Continue to serve the Association with loyalty."

With that, Eom Baekyang left.

Crimson Tiger sat down. As the tension faded, exhaustion finally hit him. The nerve-racking moments in the Evil Coalition flashed before his eyes. This mission had been incredibly difficult. Without Yeon's help, he never would have succeeded.

Crimson Tiger slowly chewed and swallowed the tuber fleeceflower. It had the texture of a sweet potato but carried an incredibly fragrant taste.

There were many ways to consume tuber fleeceflower. It could be refined into a pill or boiled into a medicinal decoction. Crimson Tiger chose to eat it raw. When training with Master in the past, he had consumed many such roots.

The key wasn't how one consumed it, but how one absorbed its effects afterward.

Crimson Tiger sat cross-legged and focused his inner energy, guiding the medicinal properties of the tuber fleeceflower throughout his body.

Sssss...

A faint heat began to rise from his body, like steam escaping into the air.

How much time had passed?

Crimson Tiger's eyes snapped open.

All his fatigue had vanished. His gaze was sharp and filled with vitality. The tuber fleeceflower had been even more potent than expected, granting him the equivalent of at least four years of inner arts training.

Even with the same miracle herb, its effects varied depending on the individual's constitution and talent, but Crimson Tiger had reached a level where he could extract over ninety percent of its benefits.

Crimson Tiger rose from his seat and walked to the window.

Ju Hwayin's words surfaced in his mind.

*"You and Seohyeon will be protected?"*

A bitter smirk crossed Crimson Tiger's lips. What nonsense.

He didn't expect protection. He would be relieved enough if they simply didn't cause more harm. He was not naive enough to believe the words of those intoxicated by power.

Through Yeon, he sent a letter to Master. Using Yeon's network was the most secure and efficient option. Of course, the letter was written in a cipher only he and Master understood.

He trusted Yeon completely, but he didn't extend that trust to the transmission process. North Sea was quite a distance away, and anything could happen along the way.

The letter detailed his situation in full. Hiding the truth out of concern would be pointless.

The worst habit in difficult situations was escapism. In a crisis, the only option was to push forward. The only way to respond was with honesty.

The reason he wasn't rushing to flee to North Sea immediately was that he didn't know Seohyeon's current condition. She had endured grueling treatments. The strain of a sudden escape might be too much for her. He had to wait for Master's reply.

From what he could tell, there was one crucial thing the Third Young Lady's faction didn't know.

Master's existence.

She believed that with a single command, she could easily have Seohyeon killed. That was her mistake.

If they wanted to deal with Master, they would need to sacrifice several of their strongest warriors.

Of course, Crimson Tiger had no intention of engaging in an all-out war with the Association. He wouldn't allow Seohyeon or Master to be dragged into this. He would ensure that not even a speck of dust reached them. No matter what it took.

Crimson Tiger sat cross-legged again and sank into deep meditation.

The rich fragrance of tuber fleeceflower filled the room.

"I'm actually sick."

The three people inside the Radiant Hall operations room all turned their gazes toward one spot.

The one who spoke without being asked was none other than the newcomer, Jo Birang. He let out a deep sigh, his face filled with gloom.

Hong Sabaek narrowed his eyes at him.

"If you were trying to get our attention, then congrats, you succeeded."

Im Yeongdal stopped peeling his potato and asked, "What kind of sickness?"

Jo Birang sighed again.

"A type of obsessive-compulsive disorder... a fixation on perfection, you could say."

WHACK! THUD!

The shoe Im Yeongdal threw smacked Jo Birang right on the head.

Jo Birang clutched his head and whined in exaggerated pain.

Just then, Wang Sochan, who hadn't washed his hair in five days, scratched at his scalp and muttered, "If it's about cleanliness... I have a bit of that too. It's rough, isn't it?"

Im Yeongdal looked at Hong Sabaek with an incredulous expression.

"Oh! Who should I kill first?"

Hong Sabaek jerked his chin toward Jo Birang.

"In situations like this, the world would call *him* the culprit."

As Im Yeongdal prepared to throw his remaining shoe, Jo Birang quickly ducked behind the mountain of documents stacked on the desk.

Hong Sabaek asked, "Wait, is that guy still going through all that paperwork?"

"Seems like it."

"Crazy bastard!"

Jo Birang poked his head out.

"Not crazy, just obsessive-compulsive."

"Whatever! Just tell us why you're this ridiculously obsessed."

"When I feel like there's something I don't know, I can't focus. I can't even sleep."

Im Yeongdal cut in, "You idiot, that's not OCD, that's just an obsession."

"Oh, is that so?"

Hong Sabaek suddenly shouted, "No! You're just plain crazy!"

Jo Birang sighed again.

"Well, thanks to that, I've perfectly analyzed everything you seniors have done up until six years ago. Starting today, I'll be working on missions from five years ago."

Hong Sabaek looked dumbfounded.

"Are you seriously planning to go through *all* of it?"

"Of course. Soon, I'll be completely in sync with you all."

Hong Sabaek shook his head.

"Has anyone actually given him work to do? Is it okay for a newbie to be this free?"

Im Yeongdal resumed peeling his potato and answered, "He already finished all his assignments. This is what he's doing *after* that."

"Really?"

For all his madness, Jo Birang was surprisingly efficient.

As Jo Birang grinned smugly, Hong Sabaek just shook his head.

"Fine, go ahead and be the only decent human here. Be the only one who's perfect!"

Jo Birang, unable to hold back, chimed in cheekily, "You know, there's a saying. *Don't work for perfection, strive for excellence.* But I intend to achieve both perfection and excellence at the same time..."

Im Yeongdal's last remaining shoe flew straight at Jo Birang.
