As soon as Baek Suryong returned to his room, he said while taking his seat, "First off, the Heavenly Justice Commander isn't our guy."

They had wasted far more time at the Heavenly Justice Corps than expected. By the time they reached the Elimination Corps' training grounds, it was already lunchtime, and the place was empty, so the two of them had ended up returning to their lodgings.

"What's your basis for being so certain?" Namgung Su asked, sitting opposite Baek Suryong. At the same time, he set up a soundproof barrier within the room to prevent their voices from leaking outside.

"If the Heavenly Justice Commander was the traitor we're after, he wouldn't have reacted like that when Jang Gon went berserk." Baek Suryong recalled the earlier scene vividly, especially how the Heavenly Justice Commander had rushed forward to stop Jang Gon when he used sword qi.

Namgung Su narrowed his eyes slightly and asked, "You're saying the Heavenly Justice Commander wouldn't have tried to save you if he was the culprit?"

"Of course not."

"What if even that was an act? Maybe he expected that you'd reject any help due to your pride, and he merely pretended to intervene?" Namgung Su crossed his arms, raising a contrary opinion.

Baek Suryong, however, shook his head with a faint laugh. "If that was the case, he's even less likely to be the traitor."

"Why?"

"Because that exact moment was the perfect opportunity to kill me."

"You can't mean..." Namgung Su's expression hardened as he imagined a certain scenario.

Baek Suryong nodded slowly, smirking. "The Heavenly Justice Commander could've easily intervened earlier since Jang Gon is his subordinate. That alone would've provided ample justification for him to step in. So, here's a question." He leaned back, placing his long legs on the table. "If the Heavenly Justice Commander genuinely wanted me dead, how do you think he would have done it?"

Namgung Su replied stiffly, a grim look on his face, "...He could have pretended to break up the fight and made you collide with Jang Gon, secretly launching a fatal strike. Or perhaps, at close range, he could've used telekinesis to redirect Jang Gon's blade. A martial artist of his level could certainly pull off something like that."

Just nudging the blade slightly mid-duel could turn what should've been a minor cut into a lethal injury. Moreover, if that had happened, the blame would have fallen solely on Jang Gon.

Baek Suryong grinned and nodded approvingly. "As expected of a model student. Exactly right."

"Hah, still, to go that far..."

Until Baek Suryong pointed it out, Namgung Su hadn't even considered such a possibility.

It wasn't about intelligence or cleverness.

This was simply the limit of imagination for someone born and raised in the peaceful orthodox sects.

On the other hand, for Baek Suryong, who had lived through the brutal laws of the Blood Cult in his past life, this level of suspicion was only natural.

"Our opponent is a traitor aligned with the Blood Cult. If anything, their ruthlessness would be greater, not less."

Namgung Su nodded, seemingly convinced, yet the gaze he directed toward Baek Suryong turned oddly cold.

"...Then, you're saying you stood there calmly testing if the Heavenly Justice Commander might try to kill you indirectly, using someone else's sword?"

"I hid it pretty well, right?"

Baek Suryong smiled proudly.

Contrary to his expectation, however, the reaction was freezing cold.

"Baek Suryong. Why do you always act like this?"

"...Now what's the problem?"

Namgung Su suddenly shot up from his seat, glaring down at Baek Suryong with fierce, golden eyes that looked ready to unleash thunder.

"If you had such a plan in mind, you should've at least hinted at it beforehand. What if the Heavenly Justice Commander truly wanted you dead? Were you just planning to die then and there?"

"Who's going to die...?"

"You must have two lives, then."

Crackle!

Seeing Namgung Su glaring so intensely, lightning sparks even flickering in his eyes, Baek Suryong flinched slightly, but Baek Suryong still had his reasons.

"No, look, it's not like I planned that from the beginning, alright? It just occurred to me halfway through."

"You should've sent a signal right then. If you'd even glanced my way once, I'd have raised my guard further."

If Baek Suryong had actually died or gotten seriously hurt, Namgung Su would never have forgiven himself.

"...Hmm, my bad."

When Baek Suryong readily apologized, Namgung Su sighed softly and nodded.

"Next time, think about the people beside you too."

With that, their temporarily paused deduction resumed.

"Anyway, the Heavenly Justice Commander is off our suspect list. That leaves either the Elimination Corps Commander or the Vice Commander."

"What about the possibility they're both involved? I've heard the Elimination Corps Commander and Vice Commander have known each other closely for a very long time."

Though the possibility certainly wasn't zero, Baek Suryong shook his head.

"It's likely just one."

The fewer people involved, the greater the individual reward.

Not to mention fewer risks of the secret leaking out.

Baek Suryong strongly believed there was only one traitor.

"The odds are evenly split between the Commander and Vice Commander, both belonging to the same Elimination Corps..."

As Baek Suryong contemplated deeply, Namgung Su suddenly said, "You said the two have known each other for nearly twenty years?"

"That's right."

Ryu Seol and Moyong Jun supposedly met back when they first joined the Murim Alliance.

"If that's the case, then it means there shouldn't be much they don't already know about each other."

"Moyong Jun even knows about Ryu Seol practicing demonic arts. Why?"

Namgung Su crossed his arms and gazed thoughtfully at the ceiling. His golden eyes shone sharply.

"If something had changed about such a close friend, wouldn't they notice immediately?"

"...You're saying they might have sensed the betrayal already?"

"Maybe not outright betrayal."

Namgung Su lowered his gaze from the ceiling and stared straight at Baek Suryong.

"I think they'd at least feel something is off. Like, for example, noticing their friend behaving out of character."

When Baek Suryong smashed the Murim Alliance's front gates and caused a commotion, Namgung Su had immediately thought it unlike Baek Suryong.

It was the same when Baek Suryong provoked the Heavenly Justice Commander and deliberately swallowed Qi Dispersing Poison.

Wouldn't twenty-year friends know each other even better?

"Interesting... you have a point."

Baek Suryong nodded slowly.

With two decades of friendship, they likely had picked up at least some signs of change.

Even if they hadn't considered betrayal outright, they'd certainly hold some suspicion.

"If we provide confirmation for that suspicion?"

"We'll definitely see a reaction. Whether it's to protect their friend, confirm the truth, or perhaps..."

Both Baek Suryong and Namgung Su smiled darkly at the same time.

"Or perhaps, they'll try to frame each other."

"Let's find out then, which of the two friendships is a sham."

"What's this letter?"

The Elimination Corps Commander Ryu Seol tilted her head, looking at the letter brought by a servant.

She had just stepped out from bathing after returning home from work.

It was already quite late at night. A cool breeze blew through the half-open window.

"It's not an official document. A personal letter delivered to me at this hour... Could it be a love letter?"

There was a time when she used to receive love letters quite often.

However, since becoming the Elimination Corps Commander, she hadn't received any.

No, maybe it stopped after the number of fools who got their heads cracked open exceeded a hundred?

"Whoever it is, they're pretty cute."

Ryu Seol tore open the envelope and began reading the letter inside.

Her eyes widened as soon as she read the first sentence.

"Baek Suryong? Why is that guy... Could this really be a love letter?"

Ryu Seol read the letter with amusement.

However, her expression gradually hardened into something terrifying.

By the time she finished, Ryu Seol's face had twisted into the frightening expression of an evil demon.

"That crazy bastard!"

Rip, rip!

Ryu Seol shredded the letter into pieces, murderous intent surging from her eyes.

Startled by the noise, servants rushed into her room.

"Commander? What's wrong?"

"...It's nothing."

Ryu Seol forced herself to calm down.

Though she had momentarily shouted, the letter contained something she couldn't discuss carelessly.

She grabbed her outer garment and stood up.

"I'm going out for a bit."

"Huh? Where would you go at this hour...?"

"It might be late. Don't wait up."

Whoosh!

Using her movement arts, Ryu Seol instantly passed the servants and leaped over the wall.

She muttered to herself as she raced through the nighttime streets of Wuhan.

"What? A traitor?"

「Moyong Jun seems to have betrayed the Murim Alliance and joined hands with the Blood Cult.」

Though there were various details written besides this, those few lines were deeply etched into Ryu Seol's mind.

「I'd like to discuss this quietly. Please come alone to the address below tonight.」

Ryu Seol didn't even consider the possibility that the letter might be a trap to lure her out.

*He mentioned my demonic art. It's definitely from Baek Suryong.*

Even if it was a trap, she didn't care. She would just crush them all.

Ryu Seol clenched her teeth hard.

How dare he write such nonsense?

If Baek Suryong had been standing before her now, she'd have torn him apart, but at the same time, suspicions about her close friend of twenty years slowly rose in her mind.

Martial arts that had suddenly improved significantly after years of stagnation.

Moments when he disappeared without explanation or notice.

Subtle expressions that occasionally felt unfamiliar.

*Could it really be...?*

"Hah! Ridiculous."

Ryu Seol shook her head vigorously.

She felt ashamed for doubting her friend just because of a mere letter.

Moyong Jun betraying the Murim Alliance? For what reason?

The position of Vice Commander of the Five Divisions was already enough for wealth and honor.

If Moyong Jun had wished, she would have even given up her Commander position.

Although Chairman, that stubborn old man, would never have allowed it...

「To find solid evidence, Commander, I need your help.」

Everything Baek Suryong wrote in the letter was complete nonsense.

He must have gotten arrogant after the Namgung Clan defeated the Blood Cult.

"...I'll make him pay dearly for spouting such garbage."

A fierce glint flashed in Ryu Seol's single eye. Her figure turned into a gust of wind.

Moments later, she arrived at a rundown, abandoned house on the outskirts of the city.

Bang!

As she smashed through the wall, Baek Suryong stood up from his seat.

"You're here."

"Take a hit first."

One of the Black Daos at Ryu Seol's waist shot out, still sheathed, leaving a dark trail aimed straight at Baek Suryong's shoulder.

Clang!

Namgung Su, who had already drawn his sword, swiftly blocked the attack in front of Baek Suryong. He spoke calmly.

"To harm this man, you'll have to go through me first."

"So, you're in this together too?"

"Senior. Please calm down first..."

"Shut it! Who are you calling senior!"

Ryu Seol unleashed a lion's roar. Instantly, the walls cracked deeper and the roof shook from the force of her surging inner arts.

Rumble!

Ryu Seol's hair floated upward. Murderous intent exploded from her single eye. A terrifying storm of immense energy swept through the abandoned house.

*This much power without even using her demonic art?*

Baek Suryong widened his eyes in surprise.

He'd expected her to be strong, but Ryu Seol's presence exceeded even that.

Holding her two Black Dao blades loosely at her sides, she slowly approached the two men.

"If you two weren't members of the Murim Alliance, I'd kill you both right here."

At that moment, both Namgung Su and Baek Suryong were certain.

Ryu Seol was no traitor.

"Though I'm still considering beating you half to death."

Now, the real problem was how to convince her.
