He looked up. Crimson Tiger was approaching him to deal the final blow, his sword still dripping with blood. The bodyguard's gaze was resolute, one that would not be swayed by any plea.

Flying Dragon squeezed his eyes shut, relieved that at least he wasn't being stabbed in the back and killed by some imbecile in a back alley.

Crimson Tiger raised his sword to pierce the assassin's heart, but before he could strike, a dozen hidden weapons flew rapidly toward him.

TATATATATANG! POP!

He quickly spun around and deflected the hidden weapons, but one of them exploded, releasing a cloud of red smoke.

Crimson Tiger immediately held his breath, but despite not inhaling the smoke, his entire body tingled as if lightning were coursing through his veins, partially paralyzing him and hitting him with a wave of nausea.

*Poison! And a nasty one that can be absorbed through the skin, at that!*

Crimson Tiger quickly swallowed a general antidote, but some of the poison had already seeped into his body, and he was still surrounded by red smoke. He staggered back, preparing for a surprise attack. 

Sure enough, something soon flew in and a cloud of white smoke exploded. Thankfully, it wasn't another poison. Rather, the white smoke began to neutralize both the poison in his body and the red smoke.

Soon, the smoke turned blue, then scattered and disappeared.

Crimson Tiger glanced around, but Flying Dragon and his accomplice were long gone. Standing in their place was Yeon, who had set off the white smoke.

Sighing with relief, he slumped to the ground.

Yeon carried him in her arms and took off. "Let's get out of here for now."

{sep}

Flying Dragon and his handler, Gon, hid in an abandoned house far from the scene of the fight. 

Seated beneath a shabby roof with moonlight filtering in through the cracks, Gon carefully applied medicine to Flying Dragon's shoulder and bandaged it up. Neither of them said a word throughout the treatment. 

Only after finishing did Gon remark dryly, "Fortunately, it doesn't look like it will fester."

Flying Dragon smiled. "Thanks."

"Wow, you actually know how to say thank you?" Gon grumbled sarcastically.

Lately, he found himself getting annoyed whenever he dealt with Flying Dragon. It was all because of the bastard's toxic relationship with that bitch Sin Yeongyeong. Although it was something he should have reported to his superiors, he didn't do it... no, he couldn't. He had already grown too fond of this fool.

Right now, though, he was angrier than he had ever been.

"I am seriously shocked. Or did I hear you wrongly?"

"No matter how corrupt I've become, I'm not so far gone that I can't feel gratitude for the person who saved my life."

"So you know you've become corrupt?"

"Of course."

"You fucking idiot!"

"Ow! Stop hitting me!"

"Do I look like I'm joking?" Gon roared. "I put my life on the line for you!"

Flying Dragon, who had been attempting to brush things off with a playful remark, shut his mouth. Even he couldn't respond flippantly after seeing the genuine concern in Gon's eyes. "I'm sorry," he apologized sincerely.

Gon slowly rose to his feet and stared blankly out at the yard beyond the broken door, where weeds flourished in the cracks around the fractured stone lanterns. The barren landscape seemed to mirror Flying Dragon's troubled soul, and his heart ached for his friend.

"Did you realize it? Your opponent was one of the Phantoms," he whispered gravely.

"What?" Flying Dragon's eyes widened in shock. "That stupidly strong guy was one of the Phantoms?"

"Yes, I'm certain. The poison bomb I threw is exclusively used by high-ranking handlers like me. It's a newly developed poison that seeps through the skin and paralyzes the target without killing them."

"And?"

"At the last moment, as I was rescuing you, someone threw an antidote bomb. That bomb is also ours. The person who saved him earlier was definitely a fellow handler."

"No way! Wait, could it be... Crimson Tiger?" Flying Dragon's expression grew serious. Crimson Tiger was the only person among the Twelve Phantoms who might be able to display that level of martial prowess.

*He's already known as the strongest Phantom, and yet he was hiding his strength like me? What the heck!?*

"I think so too," Gon replied, nodding.

"Seriously? Crimson Tiger was that strong? And he's protecting that bitch? Fuck! Those nobles are treating us like their private soldiers!"

Since the First Young Lord was involved, it wasn't impossible. Flying Dragon had already despised the First Young Lord, but now he despised him even more. He knew he had no right to curse anyone, but he could help swearing, "Authority sure is awesome! And I hate the bastards that have it, damn it!"

"...Are you going to keep trying?" Gon asked, hoping his gut feeling was wrong.

Flying Dragon replied without hesitation, "Of course I am. Don't you already know I'm a shitty loser?"

Gon frowned. "This is the last time I'll save you, Flying Dragon."

Flying Dragon chuckled, "Don't be like that. Why don't you save me a few more times? Just three more times..."

"Shut up! I swear to the heavens that was the last time!" Gon yelled.

Flying Dragon smiled faintly. He knew better than anyone that Gon's anger stemmed from his deep, sincere friendship. Still, he stood up and left the abandoned house, saying, "Okay, then don't interfere again. That's an order." 

"For crying out loud, please break up with that heartless bitch! She's not worth sacrificing your life for!" Gon screamed from inside the house.

Flying Dragon halted in his tracks. He felt sorry for Gon. Although the handler knew everything about him and tried to empathize with him, he simply couldn't wrap his head around his relationship with Sin Yeongyeong.

*Then again, I myself don't understand my relationship with her.*

He gazed at the sky for a moment before glancing back. "You're the one who should break up with me, Gon. I'm even more of a heartless jerk than her."

{sep}

At the same time, Crimson Tiger was bathing in a forest stream.

Yeon spoke with her back turned, "You were hit by the Association's newest poison bomb. It wasn't designed to be lethal, and since I also used an antidote bomb, there shouldn't be any lasting effects."

Crimson Tiger plunged his face into the frigid water, which jolted him to his senses. He was confident that his opponents couldn't have killed him even if he was in a weakened state, but if it hadn't been for Yeon, he could have been seriously injured by their counterattack.

PWAAAT!

He straightened up. "You said the assassin was also a Phantom, right?"

"Yes, I'm certain. We are the only ones in this jianghu who have that particular poison bomb."

"Was it Flying Dragon?"

Yeon nodded. "Considering how he held his own against you, Crimson Tiger, I believe so."

Crimson Tiger immediately suspected that the Flying Dragon's ambush might have been ordered by the organization, but quickly dismissed the idea. Besides Cheon Ahseong, the only people who could mobilize the Twelve Phantoms for personal reasons were the First Young Lord and the Third Young Lady, and neither of them wanted Soeun dead.

"Who would benefit most from Soeun's death? Miss Sin Yeongyeong? But how did she persuade Flying Dragon, of all people, to help her?"

No matter how he thought about it, he couldn't make heads or tails of all this.

"I'm not sure either. Should I look into it?" Yeon offered.

"There's no need," Crimson Tiger replied, shaking his head. While it would be good to know the inside story, it didn't matter much if he didn't. The two must be entangled in some way, but whether it was a grudge, a monetary contract, or a love affair, none were worth putting Yeon in danger over.

Besides, his main focus was to protect Soeun, and to do that, he needed her support now more than ever.

He looked at Yeon. "About the person who rescued him, is he a handler like you?"

"Probably. If the man you fought was Flying Dragon, then the handler must be Gon."

"A handler got involved, huh?"

Handlers weren't allowed to interfere with the Phantoms' missions, and even less likely to get involved if it wasn't an official mission. In other words, Flying Dragon's handler had acted on his own discretion. 

*Either way, this is getting really serious.*

Crimson Tiger sighed. "Anyway, he'll definitely be back." 

He said that not because the opponent was Flying Dragon, but because even in the final moment, the man hadn't feared death. From experience, he knew that people like that never gave up easily.

"You must not let your guard down," Yeon reminded worriedly.

Crimson Tiger nodded reassuringly. "Don't worry."

"Then I'll be on my way." Yeon turned to leave.

Crimson Tiger called out, "Thank you for saving me, Yeon."

"Please don't say things like that anymore. At least, my feelings for you, Crimson Tiger... will not melt as easily as last year's snow."

Before Crimson Tiger could respond, Yeon melted into the darkness.
