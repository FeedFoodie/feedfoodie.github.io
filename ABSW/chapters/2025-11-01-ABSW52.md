Ju Hwayin's face flashed in his mind, though he couldn't tell if the reason was because he was worried about her, or because the succession was moving into a new, unpredictable phase. The Association was filled with incredible experts and physicians, so if she was still unconscious, she must have suffered a very serious injury.

He took several deep breaths to calm himself, then said quietly, "That's unexpected. It's not like her to be taken down so easily. What about the assassin?"

"He was captured alive at the scene."

"And the mastermind?"

"The assassin hasn't talked yet."

Crimson Tiger nodded. He had figured as much. Someone capable of successfully ambushing the Third Young Lady wouldn't reveal their backer just because of something like torture.

*Could it be the First Young Lord?* he thought, but quickly shook his head. The First Young Lord was not that dumb. There was surely some complex, hidden story behind this incident.

"There's more. Sa Horim has secretly arrived at the Association."

"Sa Horim? Isn't he an unorthodox expert?"

"That's right."

Crimson Tiger's eyes glinted ominously. "They invited him because of his Heavenly Spirit Possession Art."

The Heavenly Spirit Possession Art was Sa Horim's unique martial art, but more importantly, it was a formidable evil art that dominated and controlled a person's mind.

Yeon nodded. "Yes. The Heavenly Spirit Possession Art is the ultimate truth serum. They're going to use it on the assassin."

Crimson Tiger narrowed his eyes. Finally, the slowly swelling bubble of tension had burst. Now, who knew what would emerge or what impact it would have?

He sighed, "They're really moving fast."

"It concerns the Third Young Lady, after all. They need to interrogate the assassin before he succeeds at committing suicide."

"I suppose Sa Horim's involvement is a secret known only among the highest echelons of the Association?"

Yeon nodded. The orthodox sects would be outraged if they discovered that the Association had brought in an unorthodox expert just to uncover the mastermind.

"In any case, the identity of the perpetrator will be revealed," Crimson Tiger noted, striding forward. "Let's go back."

The real battle was only just beginning.
