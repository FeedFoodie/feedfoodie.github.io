"That's right. I checked all the letters going in this direction and found this place," Yeon confirmed. 

Crimson Tiger raised an eyebrow, genuinely impressed. This investigation couldn't have progressed without assuming insider involvement from the very beginning. It seemed that much like him, the Radiant Hall had come to the conclusion that someone within the Association was behind the kidnapping.

He didn't ask who had sent the message. It wasn't information he needed to complete his mission, and the organization wouldn't easily tell him anyway.

He turned his attention to the manor and asked, "How many kidnappers are there?"

"Between five and ten. I couldn't infiltrate too deeply for fear of being discovered," Yeon replied.

"Got it. Leave the rest to me."

Crimson Tiger put on a mask and dashed down the hill with light movements and silent footsteps, approaching the manor as carefully as possible. If there were only five to ten kidnappers, then it suggested that the kidnappers were a small but elite group. Still, to him, their strength was not so much of a concern as the possibility of them killing the hostage.

Soon, he reached the wall of the manor. Putting his ear against the wall, he extended his senses.

Around ten meters away, he could hear the sound of someone's breathing. If they were that close, then the moment he jumped over the wall, he had to subdue the enemy at once.

Taking a deep breath, Crimson Tiger leaped into the air. As expected, there was a man standing ten meters from the wall. 

Landing inside the manor, he sprinted toward the man like a lightning bolt.

Startled, the man turned around and drew his sword, but...

SWISH! THWACK!

Crimson Tiger caught him as he fell backward, killed by a throwing knife to the neck. Looking around the courtyard, he found a large tree and hid the corpse behind it.

The man's reaction was indeed fast. If his strength was anything to go by, then the enemy group was composed of first-rate masters.

Crimson Tiger continued his search. There were two more people outside the manor. One on the roof, and another behind the building.

He decided to go for the one on the roof first. Jumping up, he grabbed the edge of the roof and peeked over it. A masked man stood there with his back turned.

WHOOSH!

The man suddenly spun around, staring straight in Crimson Tiger's direction. Fortunately, Crimson Tiger barely managed to hide himself on a ledge under the roof.

The man scanned the part of the roof where Crimson Tiger was hidden, making sure no one was there, then turned back.

Crimson Tiger sighed inwardly with relief. *These men are too skilled to be wasted standing guard in a place like this! They're probably professional mercenaries, and really good ones at that. Judging by their killing intent, their main job should be fighting and assassination.*

Once he was certain that the masked man was looking away again, he climbed onto the roof silently, prepared to attack and kill the man in one strike... when he heard the sound of the building door opening and a short whistle.

The man on the roof casually raised his hand in greeting.

Crimson Tiger's heart raced. A third person was now patrolling the courtyard. With this many people, it was only a matter of time before the corpse behind the tree was discovered.

SHIING!

Sensing a presence behind him, the masked man on the roof spun around, drew his sword, and slashed in one smooth motion, as if to punish the foolish assailant who had dared attack him from behind.

He had once cut down someone who had attacked him from behind like this, and the surprised look in the man's eyes as he counterattacked in a flash was still vivid in his memory.

Unfortunately, this time was different.

SPLURT!

Before his sword was fully out of its sheath, a cold chill pierced his chest.

The man could only watch blankly as the enemy who had stabbed him kicked off his chest and leapt into the air.

From start to end, the enemy never bothered making eye contact with him.

*He could have at least have looked at me triumphantly... No way!*

Even though he had been defeated, the man didn't want to die so easily. Like a panorama flashing before his eyes just before death, the surrounding scene unfolded in slow motion.

Seeing his attacker was making a beeline for his colleague, he whistled loudly to alert him.

His colleague, a much stronger man than himself, turned around, drawing his sword.

Unfortunately, the man flying towards him was too fast.

*Why is only that guy so fast?*

Everything was unfolding in slow motion, except for the man who had stabbed him.

At this rate, his colleague's neck would be cut off.

*No! Why is that idiot trying to protect his chest when the man's aiming for his neck? It's so obvious!*

He wanted to shout, but no sound came out, and his vision faded to black permanently. The last sight he saw was his colleague's head falling to the ground.

The two men had died almost simultaneously.

SWOOSH!

Crimson Tiger immediately ran to the left side of the building, where the last kidnapper was. Although the third man should have heard the disturbance, for some reason, he didn't show himself.

*He's a cautious man, but I can't give him any more time to think. I need to finish him off before he shouts and alerts his colleagues inside the building!*

The moment Crimson Tiger turned the corner...

SWISH!

He turned his head just as a sword grazed his face, then grabbed the kidnapper's wrist before he could aim for his neck. Pivoting on one foot and rotating his body strangely, he dislocated the man's wrist.

CRACK!

Before the man could scream, Crimson Tiger reached out and covered his mouth from behind him.

CRACK!

The next moment, he broke the man's neck.

Crimson Tiger let out the breath he had been holding. From the time he ambushed the man on the roof till now, he had breathed exactly twice.

Nevertheless, he had no time to rest. Originally, he had intended to infiltrate through the roof, but there was no time for that now. If anyone inside had sensed the scuffle, the hostage would be in danger.

Crimson Tiger opened the door and silently walked down the long, quiet corridor. Soon, he saw stairs leading up to the second floor and down to the basement.

He chose to go down into the basement. Although not always the case, most prisons were located in the basement because it was more difficult to escape. Even if there wasn't a prison, given the kidnapper's experience, it was likely that they would have locked the Director in the basement.
