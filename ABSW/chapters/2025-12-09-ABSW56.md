Unlike the Phantoms, the handlers had a much closer relationship. They were replaced less frequently and exchanged a lot of information through their network. 

Nevertheless, today was the first time Yeon was meeting Iron Bull's handler, Dan.

Crimson Tiger asked the handlers, "Can you cross?"

The three handlers nodded in unison. They weren't strong fighters, but they were generally better than the Phantoms at movement arts.

"Good! Then I will take the lead." Crimson Tiger led the way across the bridge.

Green Snake followed, all the while whining about how scared she was. Iron Bull came after her. The handlers followed in reverse order. Dan went fourth, followed by Pa, then Yeon.

"You're genuinely worried about her, aren't you?" Green Snake observed.

Crimson Tiger snapped to his senses. His gaze, fixed on Yeon as she crossed, must truly have been filled with worry, but he couldn't afford to let anyone realize his feelings for her. "Of course. My life depends on my handler," he replied tonelessly.

"Is that all?" Green Snake pressed.

"Do you think I have another reason?"

Green Snake regarded him strangely, then smiled brightly. "My, you're good with words. That's why I like you." She turned to Yeon, who had just crossed the bridge, and pouted. "You're one lucky bitch."

Yeon snorted, openly scoffing at the woman.

Green Snake rolled her eyes. "Hmph, your handler is as prickly as ever."

FLUTTER!

Once all six had crossed the bridge, three messenger pigeons flew in, each bound for one of the handlers. Each handler checked their orders, then passed the messages to their Phantoms.

Yeon told Crimson Tiger, "You must return immediately."

"Why?"

SWISH! WHOOSH! CRACK!

The sound of something being cut and lashing through the air rang out, followed by a loud crack. Startled, they spun around and saw the bridge collapsing into the abyss right before their eyes.

Iron Bull had destroyed the bridge with a single palm strike.

Everyone's expression turned grave.

Crimson Tiger moved to protect Yeon, stepping in front of her. Green Snake grew tense and placed her hand on her sword's hilt.

Only Iron Bull remained calm, seemingly unfettered his apparent betrayal.

"What the fuck are you doing?" Crimson Tiger demanded fiercely

Iron Bull held out the order he had just received. "I just carried out the order I was given, to destroy the bridge on this side."

"WHAT WERE YOU THINKING?" Green Snake screamed.

Yeon stepped forward and checked Iron Bull's mission order, then glanced back at him and nodded. "The order slip is real."

Green Snake spun to face Crimson Tiger, shouting, "What in the world is going on? You know something, don't you?"

Crimson Tiger bit his lip grimly. This order was clearly something the Third Young Lady had planted in advance.

*Somehow, she's keeping track of our movements. That bitch must have given the order to Iron Bull knowing his rigid, thoughtless personality. How frighteningly meticulous.*

He sighed. He couldn't really blame the brute for following orders blindly. They were the Twelve Phantoms, specially trained to follow orders obediently and without question.

Nevertheless, Green Snake unleashed her rage on Iron Bull. 

"Fuck you, you stupid mule! In a messed-up situation like this, with orders coming in at the same time, shouldn't we at least discuss things before taking action? Are you the only loyal person here? Do you just act without thinking? What's the point of having a head if you don't use it? Does that thing on your shoulders exist only so you can keep your balance? Would you fall over without it?"

A flicker of killing intent crossed Iron Bull's eyes, and qi gathered in his fists. The word he hated most was 'stupid'. He was aware that he wasn't well-educated, but he never considered himself to be dumb. 

SHING!

Green Snake drew her sword. As a specialist in espionage, she was skilled at using her head, unlike Iron Bull. For this reason, she despised mindless fools the most.

Iron Bull slowly advanced toward Green Snake.

"Take one more step and you die by my hand," a new voice shouted.

Iron Bull flinched and halted. Someone was walking toward them.

Green Snake blinked in surprise. "Huh? You?"

She recognized the newcomer. He was Drunken Monkey, whom she had met when she went to help Crimson Tiger flee from the Evil Coalition.

Drunken Monkey stepped between Green Snake and Iron Bull.

Iron Bull's expression hardened. "Who are you?"

"Drunken Monkey."

"Drunken Monkey? So you're one of the Phantoms too."

Green Snake shrieked, "Why are you here? Hey, move it! First, I will deal with that ignorant bastard..."

SLAP!

Drunken Monkey turned and slapped Green Snake across the face. Everyone, including Iron Bull, was shocked by his sudden action. Drunken Monkey seemed genuinely angry.

"Are you just going to do whatever you want?" Drunken Monkey scolded.

"What the fuck is wrong with you? Why did you hit me?"

"Answer me! Are you going to live and die by your temper, just doing whatever the hell you please?"

Green Snake frowned. "Shit! Yeah! That's how I'll live and die! How dare you slap me, you crazy monkey!"

She raised her hand to slap Drunken Monkey back.

To her surprise, he did not move to evade.

Her hand stopped in front of his cheek. In the end, she couldn't bring herself to slap him. 

Their gazes locked.

Green Snake bit her lip. As a matter of fact, she had known for a long time that Drunken Monkey liked her. She had just pretended not to notice.

*Is love even possible for people like us?*

That was her philosophy. The reason she could flirt so freely with Crimson Tiger was that deep in her heart, she had already given up on love and only sought to satisfy her lust.

She slowly lowered her hand. "Damn monkey! You should be out drinking somewhere. Why did you come here?"

"......" Iron Bull withdrew his qi, calming down. The moment to fight had passed.

"How did you get here?" Crimson Tiger asked Drunken Monkey.

Drunken Monkey pulled out a mission slip from his shirt pocket. "I'm here on a mission, of course. I received an order to eliminate everyone who comes here."

Crimson Tiger nodded. Fortunately, like him, Drunken Monkey had also recognized Green Snake and stayed his hand.

*Just how many different layers does the Third Young Lady's trap consist of? Thankfully, while she has a good grasp of Iron Bull's personality, she's oblivious to the relationship between Green Snake and Drunken Monkey.*

Drunken Monkey narrowed his eyes as he regarded Crimson Tiger. "What in the world is going on?"

Crimson Tiger shrugged. "Why are you asking me?"

"Because I thought you would know."

After a moment's hesitation, Crimson Tiger replied softly, "The Mid-Autumn Festival is approaching."

Everyone fell silent. That one sentence was enough to explain everything. Although they had no interest in the tangled power struggles between the Association's higher-ups, that didn't mean they weren't aware of what was going on.

Crimson Tiger turned to Yeon. "Why am I being given the order to return?"

"There was an assassination attempt on the Association Chairman."

"What? Someone really tried to assassinate the Chairman?"

Everyone's jaws dropped in disbelief. All eyes turned to Crimson Tiger, wondering what to do now.

Crimson Tiger sighed. "Rather than us, it seems our handlers are the ones who need to get their facts straight."

Everyone nodded. If Crimson Tiger was right about the evolving situation, it was imperative that they confirm the status of their missions before proceeding.

"We will part ways here," Crimson Tiger added.

The Phantoms and handlers nodded in agreement. There was no benefit to sticking together in such a complicated situation.

Green Snake sighed regretfully, "Thanks for everything. Next time, let's definitely sleep together!"

Crimson Tiger whispered in her ear, "Don't you ever say that again."

"Why not?"

"Don't play coy with me. You know exactly what I mean. Besides, you two look good together."

Green Snake realized he was talking about her and Drunken Monkey. "What? You're ridiculous."

Crimson Tiger could tell. She didn't dislike Drunken Monkey, either. Otherwise, given her personality, she would not have simply taken a slap to the face without reciprocating.

Iron Bull left first, followed by Green Snake.

Drunken Monkey, the last to remain, whispered to Crimson Tiger, "She likes you."

Crimson Tiger nodded. "I think so too."

Drunken Monkey narrowed his eyes.

"But she will like you ten times more," Crimson Tiger added calmly.

"That's ridiculous!"

"If you cannot believe that, then you'll never win her heart."

"...!"

"Believe in yourself."

"......" Drunken Monkey stared intently at him for a moment before leaving without a word. 

Crimson Tiger hoped things would work out for the two of them. He had a good impression of them both.

When everyone else had left, Yeon sighed heavily. "With the bridge gone, it'll take at least ten days to get back, even if we hurry."

Crimson Tiger stared quietly down the cliff. "What if we go down there? If we can get down... we could make a raft and follow the waterway back to town. We could arrive within three days."

Yeon's eyes widened at the sight of him calmly staring down into the abyss. Just the thought of that sheer height sent a chill down her spine. "No way... You're not thinking... are you?"

Crimson Tiger smiled enigmatically.

Yeon glanced down the cliff and shuddered. "No matter how much I trust you, Crimson Tiger... I can't do this!"

"Ten days is too late. We have to return to the Association before then," Crimson Tiger insisted.

"No. The First Young Lord will understand why we were late. I'm sure of it."

Crimson Tiger shook his head. "It's not about that. We cannot let the First Young Lord lose so pathetically. If one side collapses like this... we will only end up in even more danger."

He was certain he would be the first target of the cleanup, regardless of whether the First Young Lord or the Third Young Lady won the succession war. Therefore, his goal was to maintain a balance between powers until the Chairman could make his decision.

"I have to get back as fast as possible and speak with the Third Young Lady myself."

Yeon gaped in disbelief. "That's more dangerous than jumping off this cliff..."

The security around Ju Hwayin's residence was always incredibly tight, especially now that she was supposedly ill. Infiltrating the place was absolutely impossible.

"I have to find out for sure if she orchestrated this herself," Crimson Tiger said firmly. "And if she did, then I need to find proof of her guilt."

"You heard the news that the Association Chairman examined her personally, right?"

"Of course."

"And?" Yeon couldn't understand. If the Association Chairman couldn't cure the Third Young Lady, then her condition must be real.

Crimson Tiger smirked. "The Association Chairman may be a great man, but he's not an omnipotent god."

"You have something in mind."

Crimson Tiger nodded eagerly. Although he had initially thought nothing of Ju Hwayin's unusual condition, while fighting the masked men, he suddenly remembered an incident from long ago.

Yeon sighed. "So what if you find proof? Are you going to hand it to the First Young Lord?"

"I'll think about it when the time comes."

Yeon stared down the cliff once more. Even with her excellent eyesight, she could not see the bottom. "Will the result really be worth all this suffering?"

Crimson Tiger held out his hand. "Trust me."

"This is crazy," Yeon grumbled, but grabbed his hand anyway.

Pulling her into his embrace, Crimson Tiger leaped off the cliff.

"Kyaaaaah!" Yeon let out a scream and squeezed her eyes shut.

SWOOSH!

The strong wind made it difficult to breathe. She felt like her heart had stopped. If not for Crimson Tiger's firm grip on her hand, she would surely have lost consciousness.

Still, humans were adaptable creatures that thrived even in the face of extreme fear. Soon, Yeon found herself relishing the thrill of soaring through the sky.

WHIRL!

*How long have we been falling?*

Feeling Crimson Tiger's strong forearm wrapped around her waist, Yeon's heart raced.

PANG! PANG! PANG!

Suddenly, Crimson Tiger kicked the air as if treading water, shooting out a palm technique from the tips of his feet. Slowly, their rapid descent decelerated as a result of the backward momentum.

PANG PANG PANG PANG PANG!

Once their speed was sufficiently reduced, he drew Soul Crusher and stabbed it into the cliff face.

KRAAANG! SCRRRAPE!

Lodged in the wall, Soul Crusher carved through the cliff as they slid down. The wall cracked and crumbled, showering them with dust and debris.

When they had traveled far enough to discern their surroundings, Crimson Tiger yanked Soul Crusher out of the cliff and flung himself in a certain direction, still holding Yeon.

SPLASH!

The pair fell into the water.

"Puaaah!" Yeon gasped as she surfaced. She was perfectly fine, so much so that she wondered if she had just hallucinated the entire ordeal. 

Soon, the two of them were swept away by a powerful current.

Crimson Tiger swam alongside her, asking, "Are you okay?"

Yeon lifted her gaze. She couldn't see the edge of the cliff they had jumped from at all. Although the drop had been dizzying, being hugged and carried by Crimson Tiger somehow made it all worthwhile.

She smiled brightly. "I'm okay enough to do that all over again."

SHWAAA!

The two of them drifted away, washed downstream by the rapids.

A steady stream of reports from the Phantoms reached the Radiant Hall.

CLANK, SHING!

"A report from Drunken Monkey," Jaegal Suyeon announced. "He says he did not find the enemy at the ambush point."

Eom Baekyang grit his teeth. "Those blasted fools sent Drunken Monkey to the wrong location!"

Right now, they weren't the ones giving orders. After the Association Protection Order was issued, the Radiant Hall Protectors and the Mystery Pavilion held exclusive command over the Twelve Phantoms. It was an established rule of the Association, so they couldn't do anything about it.

At the very least, they could still receive reports about the operation, since the handler network itself belonged to the Radiant Hall.

CLANK, SHING!

The mechanism in the wall stopped at a painting of a tiger.

"Crimson Tiger has annihilated all those who were at the manor," Jaegal Suyeon reported.

"That's it! Just as I expected!" Eom Baekyang smiled, satisfied. Crimson Tiger never betrayed his trust.

Furthermore, the man did not report that the operation itself was a trap. The deceased were assassins and martial artists from unorthodox sects anyway, individuals who could easily be suspected of being involved in the assassination of the Association Chairman.

"However, it seems Crimson Tiger's return will be delayed," Jaegal Suyeon added.

"Why?"

"Apparently, he needed to cross a bridge on his way back, but it was destroyed. Now, he has to take the long way around."

"Damn it! Tell him to get back as fast as possible!" Eom Baekyang trailed off, suddenly realizing his mistake. He no longer had operational command, so there was no point in him giving orders. Instead, he grumbled irritably, "No, to hell with all that! Tell Crimson Tiger to take his sweet time sightseeing in the Central Plains before coming back!"

CLANK, SHING!

The mechanism moved again, stopping at a painting of a snake.

"A report from Green Snake," Jaegal Suyeon said. "She says by the time she arrived at the mission site, everything was already over."

"What? Did those idiots send the Phantoms there without any coordination whatsoever?"

"It seems so."

"Unbelievable. Seriously, unbelievable."

Unknown to the Radiant Hall scholars, the handlers did not report the incident as it had happened. They instinctively sensed that covering up the incident was safer for everyone than revealing the complicated truths behind an internal power struggle.

Just then, Gu Yangseo stuck his head out of his office, scowling. "Deputy Director Eom, what's the situation?" 

"Crimson Tiger has completed his mission and somehow made it past the ambush point before Drunken Monkey got there..."

Gu Yangseo roared, "What the hell? How did they mess up so badly... no, they didn't! We're all being played by those motherfuckers!"

"That's right."

"Fuck!"

Currently, Gu Yangseo was even more anxious than Baek Museong. Every time people met, they inevitably discussed the assassination attempt on Ju Hwayin. Two out of every three times, they condemned the First Young Lord.

The reason the people of the jianghu were angry was simple. The succession war had become far too dull. They had all been hoping for a spectacular succession struggle that would give rise to a new hero.

"Any more news from Crimson Tiger?"

"He'll need at least ten more days to return because a bridge in his path was destroyed."

Gu Yangseo ground his teeth. "Damn it! In ten days, the First Young Lord will be reduced to dust and buried deep in the earth, with nothing left to slander."
