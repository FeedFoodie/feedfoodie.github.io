When Sin Chohui woke up, she realized someone was watching her in the darkness. Before she could scream, however, the sound died in her throat. For some reason, she felt like she knew who the intruder was.

Sitting up slowly, she whispered, "Unnie?"

Indeed, the figure in the darkness was her older sister, Sin Yeongyeong.

Sin Chohui's feelings for her sister were complicated, consisting of a mixture of fear, anger, and hatred. It was tragic, but she didn't harbor a single positive emotion for this blood relative.

"Have you ever thought of me as your Unnie?" Sin Yeongyeong sneered sarcastically.

*So it is her.* Sin Chohui's pounding heart calmed a little. Nevertheless, a different kind of adrenaline awakened, one that warned her of impending danger. "What do you mean?" she asked as innocently as she could.

"Sigh, I'm disappointed that you misunderstand me."

"Don't make me laugh!"

"I don't hate you, you know?"

Sin Chohui was about to accuse her sister of lying, but before she could open her mouth, Sin Yeongyeong added, "I think you're insignificant."

"What?" Sin Chohui glared at her sister in shock and disbelief.

Sin Yeongyeong's eyes were cold and sunken, yet they were not the detestable, falsely affectionate eyes she had grown used to. Instead, they held open, unhidden contempt.

"You whore! I can't believe you tried to break off my marriage just because you were bewitched by a man!"

"What?"

"I said, you're a hopeless, worthless bitch!"

Sin Chohui was taken aback. Her elder sister had always been polite and reserved, never uttering a single profanity. Of course, she was the only one in her family who knew her sister was not the virtuous woman the world believed her to be, but still, Sin Yeongyeong had never lost control like this before.

"So you're finally showing your true colors," she snarled through gritted teeth, but even so, she couldn't hide the slight tremor in her voice. 

She was frightened. She couldn't understand why her older sister had suddenly changed. Right now, Sin Yeongyeong seemed like she might draw a dagger from her sleeve and attack at any moment. 

*Is she revealing her true intentions because she's going to kill me?*

"I'm showing my true colors? Then what about you? What are your true colors? A bitch who spreads your legs for Young Lord Baek, trying to steal your own sister's fiancé?"

"What?"

"Or are you trash who runs around drinking with good-for-nothing scoundrels? That's your true colors."

Sin Chohui clenched her fists in indignation. "Fine, you're right! But even so, I'm a hundred times better than you, Unnie! Did you think I didn't know about all the terrible things you've done? Did you think I wouldn't know that you murdered everyone who crossed you?"

Sin Yeongyeong smiled brightly, a crazed smile brimming with killing intent.

Horrified, Sin Chohui trembled uncontrollably. Trying desperately to fight off her fear, she screamed, "Go ahead! Kill me too! Just try to kill me! I know everything about you anyway!"

Sin Yeongyeong silently rose from her seat and left without a word.

This only deepened Sin Chohui's terror. Tears streamed down her face, and even after her elder sister had left, she continued to quiver violently.

On the other hand, Sin Yeongyeong walked down the dark corridor, smiling mysteriously. "You know everything about me? I don't think so."

Four martial artists were riding at a gallop when they suddenly spotted a young man standing in the middle of the road.

The lead rider shouted, "There's someone on the road ahead! Hey, you! Move!"

Despite the man's warning, the young man didn't budge.

"Just trample him!" one of the other riders yelled.

CLIP CLOP CLIP CLOP!

Instead of slowing down, the four riders spurred their horses on even faster.

SQUELCH! THUD! THUD! THUD! THUD!

A series of ominous sounds echoed across the area.

The young man on the road stepped aside to let the four horses pass. As for the men riding them? They were lying sprawled on the ground, each one bisected at the torso.

So, what had cut them? Upon closer inspection, a blood-soaked thread could be seen stretched across the middle of the road. This was no ordinary thread, but rather a terrifying weapon made by processing Heavenly Silkworm Thread until it was tough and sharp enough to cut through steel.

The man sat down on a nearby rock. He had handsome features and an intense gaze.

He was Flying Dragon, the second most skilled agent of the Twelve Phantoms after Crimson Tiger.

Suddenly, another man materialized out of thin air and handed an envelope to Flying Dragon. "Here you go, your payment."

He was Flying Dragon's handler, Gon.

Flying Dragon took the envelope and checked its contents. "Four hundred taels? That's it?"

Gon walked toward the corpses and sighed. "These idiots used to be known as the Four Great Mountain Ghosts, but now everyone knows they're nothing more than dogs. A while ago, they even robbed and murdered passing travelers."

Gon took out two small bottles from his pocket, mixed the two powders inside and sprinkled it over the corpses.

SIZZLE!

The corpses began to dissolve, emitting a foul stench in the process.

Flying Dragon snorted coldly. "Clean that up for me, too," he commanded, pointing to the thread stretched across the road.

Gon nodded obediently. After disposing of the bodies, he carefully retrieved the thread and returned it to Flying Dragon.

"See? Wasn't it a good thing I didn't use my sword?" Flying Dragon chuckled derisively.

Gon tilted his head. "What do you mean?"

"Don't you think it's a waste to pull out my blade for a job worth only four hundred taels?"

"......" Gon's expression remained grim, even though he knew Flying Dragon was joking.

"You know, you really need to fix your shitty personality."

"What's wrong with my personality?"

"You always act like you're pissed off."

"I've never done that."

"Also, you're too quiet. Angry people aren't usually quiet, are they? Your appearance and behavior are too contradictory!"

"Doesn't that also apply to you, Flying Dragon?"

"What? What's that supposed to mean?"

"...Nothing. Just rest today. You'll be assigned a new mission tomorrow."

"Wait a minute! Tell me what you meant before you go!"

Gon ignored Flying Dragon and vanished into thin air.

"Hey! Come back! You need to fix your tendency to leave your conversation partner hanging while you disappear!"

Once he was alone, Flying Dragon let out a small laugh. His expression clouded over, and the bright mood from earlier vanished, replaced by a flicker of melancholy.

RATTLE, RATTLE. 

From the road where the Four Great Mountain Ghosts had come, a large carriage drawn by four horses approached. The driver, an old man with a harsh expression, looked ready to lash his whip at anyone who blocked the way. 

The carriage slowed and stopped beside Flying Dragon.

A woman's voice rang out from inside, "Get in!"

The carriage door opened, but the interior was concealed by a curtain, making it impossible to see who was aboard.

"Hmph! You've got the wrong person. I'm not some pushover who boards a random carriage just because a woman tells me to."

"Then how do I get you to come in?"

"Try saying this: Handsome Young Lord, for the sake of this humble girl trembling in loneliness, please grace this carriage with your presence."

"Pfft ahahahaha!"

"If you don't say it, I'm leaving."

"Handsome Young Lord, for the sake of this humble girl trembling in loneliness, please grace this carriage with your presence... if you don't want to die, that is!"

Flying Dragon feigned surprise. "Wow! To think that a beautiful young lady could spout such harsh words! This Young Lord will have to teach you a lesson."

He lifted the curtain and hopped in as the carriage began to roll forward.

Inside, the space had been converted into a large bed. A woman lay with her back to him, her thin, sheer garment revealing every curve of her voluptuous figure.

"How are you going to teach this humble girl a lesson?" Sin Yeongyeong asked, turning over with a coquettish hum. "It's been a while, Flying Dragon!"

Flying Dragon's face instantly soured. "I told you not to call me by that name, didn't I?"

"Sorry, Ah Gyeong, I forgot that you hate being referred to by your work alias."

Flying Dragon's expression remained dour. He knew that this woman's apology was devoid of substance. As always, she was merely teasing him and toying with his emotions.

Sin Yeongyeong slowly extended her hand. "Hold me. I missed you."

Unable to refuse, Flying Dragon climbed onto the bed.

CLIP CLOP CLIP CLOP!

As the carriage picked up speed, soft moans drifted from within, synchronizing with the rhythmic jostling until the tremors became a manifestation of ecstasy.

Some time later, the driver parked the carriage in a secluded forest. Inside, two naked bodies lay side by side.

"...Are you still angry at me?" Sin Yeongyeong asked.

Flying Dragon stared blankly at the carriage ceiling and grumbled, "I became one of the Twelve Phantoms for you, but what do I receive in return? Nothing."

"You have me."

"You, who's marrying another man in a month?"

"Yes, that me."

Flying Dragon bit his lip. He had met Sin Yeongyeong when they were very young and had fallen for her at first sight. He loved her looks, her smile, her voice—everything. 

He had committed his first murder for her, then his second and third, until he lost count of how many he had slain. When a girl at her birthday party received more attention than she did, this woman, then little more than a child herself, hired someone a year later to disfigure the girl and then had him kill the one who carried out the deed.

Later, she even convinced him to join the Twelve Phantoms so that he could grow stronger. She promised him that the money he earned would be for their future together.

Everything was a lie, and he knew it. What this woman really wanted was a loyal Phantom who would freely share the Association's information with her.

Nevertheless, even knowing she would become another man's woman next month, he couldn't leave her.

*Not once has she claimed that she's being forced into an arranged marriage.*

He was well aware that his was a mad love, a trap from which he could not escape, a one-way bridge where each step forward caused the path behind him to crumble.

"Do you remember when we first met?" Sin Yeongyeong asked.

Flying Dragon nodded. "Yes. Back then, you and I weren't like this."

"What were we like?"

"At least you were kind. Really kind and pure..."

"You're saying I was an idiot!" Sin Yeongyeong cut him off coldly, sitting up straight. Her anger flared, raw and primal as her voluptuous naked form.

Flying Dragon sat up after her and shouted, "Why in the world did you change so much? Why?"

"I didn't change. I've always been like this."

"No, you weren't!"

"Don't fool yourself. You only see what you want to see, cherish the memories you want to remember. You take my rare moments of purity and convince yourself that's my real self. However, you're wrong. The real me is a dirty, vicious bitch who would even kill her own sister to achieve her goals!"

SLAP!

Flying Dragon slapped Sin Yeongyeong across the cheek.

She quickly bowed her head in shame, but still, she snarled, "He's found a woman he likes."

"...!"

"Kill her for me, will you?"

Flying Dragon hesitated, but he knew. Ultimately, he would grant this ridiculous request, too.

"Her name is... Soeun."

Crimson Tiger left the Mainland Bank with a broad smile on his face, having just transferred the fifty thousand taels he had received from the Radiant Hall to the North Sea Branch. It was a substantial sum, but well deserved for assisting in the defense of the Myriad Secrets cave.

He had worried before today whether he had enough money for the final rite, but that concern was now gone. He felt as if a great weight had been lifted from his shoulders.

Walking into a deserted alley, he reverted to his usual face. He no longer wore a human skin mask, relying instead on the Thousand Transformations Hundred Faces Art to alter his appearance.

Thanks to his nightly hour of practice, the transformation was taking less and less time. Each time he improved, he marveled more and more at the martial art's miraculous results.

On the way back to the safehouse, he wandered through the marketplace. 

Suddenly, he noticed a group of children playing and stopped to watch them. Their antics, laughter, and quarrels were so endearing that he could have stayed for hours, but he left before he risked appearing like someone suspiciously watching them. 

He loved the slow, peaceful rhythm of this street.

He thought of Hyeon, who had only known life in the mountains. He wanted to teach her about the jianghu and the wider world, to let her taste the cheap snacks sold in back alleys and the rare delicacies that not even the emperor could have.

Although his own life was steeped in bloodshed, he wanted his daughter to live a life free from needless danger.

Time seemed to drag on forever, but the days passed slowly but surely. The day he had longed for, when the grand rite would succeed, would soon arrive.

When he returned to the safe house, he found Yeon there waiting for him.

"You're back?"

"Yes."

"You look well."

"I've rested well these past few days."

Crimson Tiger's condition was good, as he had not received any missions lately. The Radiant Hall had effectively granted him a short vacation, allowing both him and Yeon to fully recover from their accumulated fatigue.

"Rest well when you can."

"Thank you for your concern."

"No need to thank me. It's a Phantom's job to worry about their handler, after all."

Yeon's eyes widened for a moment before she giggled. "Pfft!"

"Did my joke land?"

"Perfectly."

They exchanged a brief laugh.

"You should rest more. Why did you come?"

"You've been assigned a new identity."

"Already? Where's the next mission?"

The news startled Crimson Tiger. The order had come faster than expected. He quickly scanned the mission details Yeon handed him.

"The Tempest Academy, Knife Throwing Instructor Gyu Ho? Why am I a teacher again?"

The Tempest Academy was not part of the Association, and his assignment there as a knife throwing arts instructor was unusual. Few knew he had learned such skills, and though the Radiant Hall scholars had seen him use them, it was still an unprecedented appointment.

Just then, Baek Museong entered. He was one of the few who could freely come and go from the safehouse. Yeon bowed politely before leaving.

"Good afternoon, First Young Lord," Crimson Tiger greeted.

"I was the one who issued that order," Baek Museong said without preamble.

"Why are you sending me there?"

"That child is there. She was recently appointed as a swordsmanship instructor."

Crimson Tiger immediately understood that he was referring to his daughter, Soeun. It seemed he had accepted Soeun as his daughter and was no longer considering killing her.

"The Tempest Academy is a place where Junior Sister and I lecture from time to time," Baek Museong remarked. Pausing for a moment, he added grimly, "However, a problem has arisen. Miss Sin has hired someone to assassinate my daughter."

"By Miss Sin, do you mean..."

"That's right, it's Sin Yeongyeong. The very woman I am to marry."

Crimson Tiger was about to ask why when a thought struck him. "Could Miss Sin have misunderstood your relationship with that young lady?"

Baek Museong nodded stiffly. "It's my mistake. I didn't realize she was monitoring me that closely."

After a brief pause, Crimson Tiger asked carefully, "Have you thought about being honest with her?" 

He believed the situation would improve if Baek Museong revealed the truth about his father-daughter relationship with Soeun. His impression of Sin Yeongyeong had been poor, finding her cold and sharp, but he felt she was someone capable of separating public from private matters.

"She might understand since it's a mistake from your youth, right?"

"Perhaps," Baek Museong admitted, "but I don't want to do that."

He had no intention of yielding even the smallest measure of control over his daughter to Sin Yeongyeong.

"I received information that she has hired a formidable expert."

"A professional assassin?"

"I am not sure of the exact details, but I am certain that anyone she chooses would be truly formidable."

Crimson Tiger sighed, "Protecting people is not my specialty."

"I know, but I want to entrust this matter to you."

Crimson Tiger hesitated. It was clear that at the root of this order was trust in him.

"If I try to smuggle my daughter elsewhere, it will only become more dangerous," Baek Museong said worriedly. "There is the assassin, but Junior Sister will not stand by either. For that child's sake, it is best that I pretend not to know about her existence until the wedding ceremony."

Crimson Tiger nodded. He was right, the Third Young Lady would not allow Soeun to be murdered easily. As long as she had use for Soeun, she would protect her in her own way, making it unlikely an assassin could kill her quickly. However, if he did not coordinate with the Third Young Lady's subordinates, their efforts might end up interfering with each other.

"I thought it would be better to eliminate the assassin while he is in the open," Baek Museong said.

Crimson Tiger fell silent, thinking. There was one month left until Baek Museong and Sin Yeongyeong's wedding. For someone who had to endure this year at all costs, this was the greatest crisis yet, and the mission he had been given was one he absolutely had to succeed in.

"Before that, I have one question."

"What is it?"

"The Third Young Lady will surely reveal your daughter's existence to the world before the wedding ceremony. That is why she had me kidnap her, after all."

"I suppose so."

"What do you intend to do then, Young Lord?"

Baek Museong narrowed his eyes. "Why do you ask?"

*Does Crimson Tiger have a crush on Soeun?* 

It was only natural to wonder. His daughter's beauty would draw the attention of any man.

"I am sorry," Crimson Tiger said quickly. "I was only curious. Please forgive me for overstepping."

He truly was curious, and he believed it was a question he could ask. At the same time, he thought that showing no curiosity at all might make Baek Museong suspicious.

"No, it is natural to be curious. To be honest, I haven't planned my next step yet," Baek Museong admitted honestly.

One thing was certain, though. He had to use his daughter to gain an advantage against Ju Hwayin, who was trying to use her against him. He didn't like treating his own child this way, but given the flow of events, it was unavoidable. 

Regardless, he had no intention of allowing Soeun to come to harm. That was the source of his headache and the reason he still couldn't decide how to proceed.

Crimson Tiger did not press further and bowed his head. "Please entrust this mission to me."

*Though, it is not as if I can refuse...*

Suddenly realizing something, he added, "However, there is a part you need to correct."

"What is that?"

"I must go to the Tempest Academy as a student, not an instructor."

"Why?"

"It is the student who spends more time with the teacher, not a fellow teacher."

"Ah, I see. I was too short-sighted. I will issue a new mission slip. Do you have any other requests?"

"While I'm carrying out this mission, please ensure that no other missions are assigned to me."

This was both a protection and an assassination mission, which meant he either could not leave his post, or had to take Soeun with him.

"Sure."

"Lastly, for this mission, I will surely need to make contact with the Third Young Lady. I ask for your understanding."

"Got it."

Crimson Tiger clasped his hands respectfully. "Then, I may be lacking, but I will do my best."

"Thank you. I am counting on you."

After patting Crimson Tiger's shoulder, the First Young Lord left.

Once alone, Crimson Tiger tossed the mission order into the air, his eyes glinting colder than a sword's edge.

SWISH SWISH SWISH!

His sword drew a dozen arcs of light in the air, shredding the slip of paper into pieces so small that they drifted to the ground like tiny snowflakes.

"If it was a request, you should have asked me nicely instead of forcefully assigning me a mission. The only reason I accepted was not for your sake, but for your daughter... and for my daughter as well."

Yes, everything he endured, he endured for his daughter.
