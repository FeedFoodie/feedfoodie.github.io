The cat's claws tore a long gash across Crimson Tiger's forearm. Still, he deliberately avoided creating a qi barrier for fear of harming the creature's claws.

SLASH!

After landing on the floor, the cat immediately launched yet another assault. Crimson Tiger took the attack without resisting.

RIP! RIP! RIP!

A long gash blossomed across his thigh. His stone-hard muscles, honed through years of training, were shredded like paper by the beast's razor-sharp claws. Fortunately, the claws were too short to actually sever his limbs.

"Hiss..."

Crimson Tiger focused on enveloping both creatures in his qi. The energy flowed warm yet powerful, collected yet not devoid of emotion.

At last, the female joined the fray after watching from the sidelines.

SLASH! SLASH! SLASH!

The relentless attacks continued.

Soon, Crimson Tiger was drenched in blood. A metallic scent filled the air as time crawled by, but he maintained his steady output of aura.

Gradually, the creatures' behavior changed as they realized that he was holding back. They were intelligent beings.

They leaped onto Crimson Tiger's body, and he allowed them complete freedom. Then, they began licking his blood, which tasted unlike anything they had ever experienced. 

The scene would have been scary enough with regular cats, but these supernatural creatures made it much scarier.

After licking their fill, the cats returned to the cage on the table.

Crimson Tiger covered the cage with a black cloth he had prepared beforehand and quietly left the safehouse. Once outside, he let out a long sigh. Waves of excruciating pain washed over his entire body. Had he lacked the necessary endurance, he would not have made it through that brutal ordeal.

"NYAAAAAAH!"

Piercing shrieks echoed from within the safehouse, but Crimson Tiger feigned ignorance.

"Meow..."

At long last, he heard gentle purring from inside.

He reentered the safe house and removed the cloth covering the cage.

An extraordinary sight greeted him. Only the black cat was still alive. The white cat was dead, its carcass resembling a withered husk as though all its life force had been drained away.

Crimson Tiger recalled Cheon Ahseong's explanation.

> After feeding the cats your blood, put them together in an enclosed space. They'll fight each other, and only the cat whose nature is closer to that of the blood's owner will survive. Rather than killing each other, it's more like they absorb each other. There's no need to mourn the death of the other cat. Such is the original fate of the spirit beasts known as Monochrome Cats. A pair of cats that hasn't undergone that absorption process isn't living up to its true destiny. The surviving cat will be several times stronger and smarter than before, with strong resistance to blades, poison, fire, water, and wind."

The black cat's transformation was remarkable. Its eyes became clearer and more luminous, its fur softer with a lustrous sheen, and its retractable claws hardened to a steel-like strength.

Crimson Tiger understood why the black cat had survived. The Asura's Eight Narakas was an extreme yang martial art. His qi and blood naturally aligned with the male cat's yang energy. Had he practiced a martial art rooted in extreme yin, the white female cat would have emerged victorious.

Of course, Crimson Tiger's real reason for having one cat absorb the other wasn't to increase its strength, but rather to awaken its other remarkable ability.

Now, instead of communicating with the other cat, the surviving cat could communicate directly with him and alert him to any killing intent it sensed while he was protecting his charge. Furthermore, only he, who was bonded to the cat through blood, could hear its warnings.

This arrangement would allow him to protect Soeun far more effectively than if he had used two cats.

Crimson Tiger gently stroked the black cat's head, and it purred contentedly and licked his finger. At first glance, it appeared to be an ordinary house cat.

After his finger, it began licking his wounds. To his surprise, the wounds bubbled as if they were being disinfected and began to heal rapidly.

*Seriously? Even though I'm seeing it with my own eyes, I can hardly believe it.*

"Let's get along well from now on," he said to the cat.

The black cat leaped gracefully onto his shoulder, as if it could comprehend his words perfectly.
