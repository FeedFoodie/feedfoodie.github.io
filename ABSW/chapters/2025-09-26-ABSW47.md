The masters of the Three Heavens and the core elders of the New Righteous Association gathered at the Pure Flower Pavilion. Consisting of the Association's highest-ranking members, this council was privy to confidential information, such as the existence of the Twelve Phantoms.

Baek Museong and Ju Hwayin were among them.

However, the meeting was delayed due to one person's absence.

Elder Hwa Jong asked a martial artist guarding the entrance, "Has the Lord of Sword Heaven arrived yet?"

"We received word that he will arrive shortly."

Hwa Jong nodded and told everyone, "Since he'll be here soon, let's wait a little longer before we begin."

This made sense. Not only was Sword Heaven the most powerful faction within the Three Heavens, it was also the strongest organization in the entire Association.

The Lord of Blade Heaven scowled. "If he doesn't show up in fifteen minutes, we should just ignore him and proceed!"

He was very upset. *Would they have waited like this if I had been late? That detestable old man would have surely started the meeting without me.*

Unfortunately, no one cared about his opinion.

The elders were more concerned about the two successors, Baek Museong and Ju Hwayin, than they were about the Lords of the Three Heavens. Though they were officially neutral, they all fought in an intense, silent battle to decide whom to support. One wrong move could spell disaster. If the successor they opposed won, they would quickly become forgotten old men, elders in name only.

Baek Museong and Ju Hwayin made eye contact. Ju Hwayin smiled, and Baek Museong returned the gesture.

Despite their outward appearances, though, both felt more nervous than they had in a long time.

Today was the day Ju Hwayin would make her decisive move, but Baek Museong had anticipated it.

Still, Ju Hwayin felt confident.

*If I reveal that Baek Museong has a child in front of all these masters, the marriage will be annulled. The Sin Paegeuk I know would be far less concerned about the child's existence compared to Baek Museong's deception. Claiming he didn't know about her will just sound like an excuse.*

*Then, the moment Baek Museong loses the support of Sword Heaven, I'll launch an all-out offensive to oust him.*

Several minutes later, Sin Paegeuk arrived.

"My apologies for being late," he said.

The meeting began as soon as he appeared, with Elder Hwa Jong leading the proceedings.

"As you have all been briefed, the Evil Coalition has targeted the Mystery Pavilion, thereby provoking us."

Everyone nodded gravely. The Inner Court had been breached, and the Myriad Secrets Cave had nearly been plundered.

"We can't simply overlook this," Elder Hwang Eom chimed in. "To think the Radiant Hall was breached! This is unacceptable."

Everyone nodded, sharing his sentiment.

"The Mystery Pavilion Master and the Radiant Hall Director must both be fired."

All the elders nodded. A few who agreed with Hwang Eom stepped forward to voice their support for the proposal. This matter was extremely serious, and having the two step down seemed to be the most effective solution.

Suddenly, however, Baek Museong stepped forward. "I agree that someone must take responsibility for this incident."

All eyes focused on him. Now that the succession war had entered its final stage, every word the First Young Lord and the Third Young Lady said mattered.

"If we only consider the end result, this punishment makes sense," Baek Museong began calmly. "However, sometimes we must pay attention to things other than the apparent outcome. This espionage operation was planned over a long period of time, and the spy was trained from a young age exclusively for this mission. It was an operation on which the Evil Coalition staked its very survival. We estimate that the preparation period spanned over twenty years."

No one disagreed with him on that point.

"Of course, there was some minor property damage, but the important thing is that we ultimately stopped them. Furthermore, we eliminated four of the Ten Evils in the process. Dismissing the two men might be unwise."

Everyone except Hwang Eom's faction nodded.

Sin Paegeuk remained silent. He didn't want to be accused of taking his son-in-law's side before the wedding.

Naturally, all eyes turned to Ju Hwayin.

Ju Hwayin revealed her stance. "I agree with Senior Brother."

Surprise spread across everyone's faces. They had expected her to oppose the First Young Lord.

"The Mystery Pavilion and the Radiant Hall have produced numerous excellent results over the years," she added. "Dismissing them now would be exactly what the Evil Coalition wants. We'd be handing those bastards a partial victory."

With both successors defending the two directors, public opinion naturally swayed in their favor. A few elders offered their support, and everyone else followed suit.

Hwang Eom, who had insisted on their dismissal, stepped back and suggested a six-month salary reduction instead. Since the two successors agreed, there was no need to object any further. The Mystery Pavilion and the Radiant Hall answered directly to the Association Chairman anyway, and they had no say in their appointments.

As a result, with the help of Baek Museong and Ju Hwayin, Yagong and Gu Yangseo kept their positions.

When the discussion concluded, Ju Hwayin remarked casually, "I'd like to take this opportunity to report one thing."

All eyes focused on her.

Baek Museong's expression hardened. *The moment of truth is here. I just have to trust Jin Chung to stop this disaster!*

"What is it?" Elder Hwa Jong asked.

Ju Hwayin smiled triumphantly. Just as she was about to speak, however, the door flew open and a martial artist rushed in.

Hwa Jong frowned. Unless the matter was extremely urgent, no ordinary martial artist would dare barge into the meeting room. He asked quickly, "What is it?"

"We've received intel about a planned ambush."

"Where?"

"Right here."

"What?"

Everyone in the hall jumped at the news.

"You must evacuate immediately," the martial artist advised.

"Nonsense!" Ju Hwayin retorted "Have you forgotten where we are?"

Baek Museong rose from his seat. "Considering the recent security breach at the Radiant Hall, your attitude strikes me as rather presumptuous."

Ju Hwayin's mouth twitched. *I knew it. He figured out I was going to expose him!*

She felt certain this was Baek Museong's doing, but she couldn't let this golden opportunity slip away. Glancing around at the masters in the room, she began, "What I was about to say relates to this matter..."

KABOOOM!

A tremendous explosion shook the building violently, sending tremors through the floor and rattling the windows.

Another martial artist rushed in.

"What happened?" Hwa Jong demanded.

The martial artist reported, "A Roaring Thunder Bomb exploded at the building entrance."

The first martial artist replied, "It seems the intel was correct. We must evacuate at once."

The masters hastily left their seats. Ju Hwayin tried to stop them, but the hall was already plunging into chaos.

"I don't know what you're talking about, but we can hear it later," Hwa Jong said as he left.

Ju Hwayin bit her lip firmly. *Cowardly old fools!*

After everyone had left, only Ju Hwayin and Baek Museong remained.

Ju Hwayin laughed, "How lazy. If the spies bothered to come all the way to the entrance of the Pure Flower Pavilion, why didn't they walk a few more steps and plant the Roaring Thunder Bomb right here in this room?"

Baek Museong smiled faintly. "They must have had bad luck."

"Thanks to that, someone got very lucky."

"It was a good attempt."

"This won't be the end."

"Of course not. I'm looking forward to the next match."

Baek Museong started to walk away.

"Aren't you worried about her?" Ju Hwayin asked from behind him.

"......" Baek Museong halted mid-step.

Ju Hwayin taunted, "There's no guarantee a Roaring Thunder Bomb won't go off at her feet."

Despite the obvious threat to Soeun's life, Baek Museong remained calm. "A heartless junior sister who killed her senior brother's daughter for power... do you think the jianghu will accept someone like that?"

"Isn't that still better than a heartless father who murdered his own daughter for power?"

"True, that does seem a little better. Hahaha!" Baek Museong burst out laughing as he resumed walking. He was confident that Ju Hwayin wouldn't kill Soeun.

Ju Hwayin knew it, too. If they exposed each other's crimes, they would both be destroyed.

When she was finally alone, she sighed. "Damn it! He really got me this time."

Since they've already failed their mission, they have nowhere to go back to anyway."

Baek Museong realized that this question wasn't about the game of Go, which was still in full swing. It was about him and Ju Hwayin.

"Because the life and death of a large group remains uncertain, it seems premature to end things here."

Baek Museong didn't answer, of course.

"Junior Sister."

Baek Museong said calmly, "It was Junior Sister who brought the child here, to the Association."
