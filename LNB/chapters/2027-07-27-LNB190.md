The Silver Horse Merchant Association traveled south through Xiangyang, a small county in the northern part of Hubei through which the main road to Wuhan passed.

The shoulders of the young master, Yu Jang-Hwan, and his escorts were covered in dust, their faces etched with fatigue. Still, they were smiling. In just a few more days, they would arrive in Wuhan and finally see their families again.

It had been nearly a year since they had last seen them, so their excitement was only natural. A deep sense of anticipation was already visible on the escorts' faces.

Yu Jang-Hwan smiled as well. *Thank goodness. I'm able to return them to their families without any trouble.*

He was the young master, responsible for the safety of the entire group. He had no choice but to worry more than the others, and the mental anguish he had suffered over the past year was beyond words. Now, however, the end of that painful road was in sight.

Of course, even after arriving at the headquarters, he would not be able to rest for long before setting out on another trade journey. The simple fact that he could rest with a peaceful mind, even for a short while, was enough to make him happy.

Suddenly, his gaze turned toward Eun Han-Seol, who was sitting on the roof of a nearby carriage. Everyone else was grimy from the road, but she was the sole exception. Though there had been no place to wash, she was always spotless. It seemed as though not a single speck of dust had settled on her clothes.

By now, Yu Jang-Hwan had realized that she was no ordinary person. It was not only difficult for a normal girl to maintain such cleanliness, but it was also impossible for her to endure a journey that even grown men found difficult so effortlessly.

*Which sect is she a disciple of?*

He thought she must be a disciple of a prestigious sect, but no matter how much he wracked his brain, he could not even begin to guess which one.

His heart pounded fiercely as he looked at her. He had wanted to deny it, but he had to admit it now. He had fallen for Eun Han-Seol.
