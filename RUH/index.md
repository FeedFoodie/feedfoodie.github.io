## Synopsis

> The first son of the Murim Alliance's Chairman, the Ten Strike Finisher Yang Jagak. He was called the greatest genius of all time but was poisoned to death before he even reached the age of thirty.

I opened my eyes, only to discover that I had pale skin and a slender figure reminiscent of a courtesan.

"This can't be happening...!"

*No way. Impossible.* 

I quickly loosened my pants and checked my crotch.

"Phew... at least I'm still a guy. Also... that was pretty impressive."

Anyway, it seems that I reincarnated into the body of a thug with no redeeming features other than his devilishly good looks. Worse, he just so happens to be the Young Cult Leader of the Demonic Cult.

*Shit... can I ever return to the orthodox sects...?*
