The Shining Light Cult's Bright Spring Parlor was a pavilion with wide, open windows that offered a clear view down from the second floor. Vermilion lanterns hung in a line between the floors, brightly illuminating the name "the Bright Spring Parlor".

If the Bright Summer Parlor, personally run by the Lord of the Palace of Bliss and acclaimed as the finest tavern, was for the elite, then the Bright Spring Parlor was for prodigies under the age of thirty. Thus, entry was forbidden to those over thirty, and the floors were segregated by status and skill.

One would assume such discrimination could be detrimental, but in reality, it was the opposite. To some, it offered an incentive to ascend to a higher floor. To others, it provided a special place to show off. Thus, it was always bustling with people, and today it was especially so.

"Hey, why can't we go in?"

"We are fully booked, so please leave."

"What the hell. What kind of nonsense is this? There are so many empty seats!"

"Those seats are reserved, Cultist," the usher stated.

At the usher's monotone voice, the ordinary cultists bit their lips and backed away. Even if they wanted to grab him by the collar and cause a scene, this was a bar managed by the Palace of Bliss; the very waiters were highly trained martial artists, and complaining could get you killed.

Still, a sense of lingering disappointment kept them from wandering off, and instead they loitered quietly around the Bright Spring Parlor, puzzled over what manner of patron could cause such a fuss.

Then, after a few moments, a loud cry rang through the air.

"Here comes the second son of the Captain of the Palace of Poison's Venom Scattering Brigade, Young Lord Dongbang Hojae!"

_FLAP!_ From the distance, a man in a silk hood approached in billowing robes. His proudly squared shoulders and dignified gait drew the gazes of the ordinary cultists. Unaffected by the attention, he scanned his surroundings before striding into the Bright Spring Parlor with aplomb.

"The Regular Meeting of the Cult's Scions is today!"

The ordinary cultists finally understood why they couldn't enter the Bright Spring Parlor, and their frustration was replaced by a surge of anticipation. Their hearts pounded at the mere thought of seeing the future leaders of the Cult up close! Some even cheered and waved, hoping to make a good impression.

The crowd began to swell even more. Soon enough the area around the Bright Spring Parlor had turned into a parade for youths.

"Have some duck to go!"

Not wanting to miss the opportunity, even street vendors joined the frenzy, offering alcohol and snacks. Given a spectacle to watch and things to talk about, spirits were high.

In this manner, numerous prodigies made their appearances, and the excitement continued to build. Then, as if to mark the final climax, another cry rang out.

"Here comes the second child of the Lord of the Palace of Blades, the Frost Flower, Young Lady Dan Yehwa!"

The usher's voice was more excited than ever before. Hearing this, the ordinary cultists could only gape; they even dug into their ears, wondering if they had heard correctly. The Frost Flower, Dan Yehwa? She was a woman whose beauty was said to reach the heavens, and the pride of the Shining Light Cult!

She was their only member to be named among the Three Flowers. She was also famous for not attending regular meetings, or any other event for that matter. Yet here she was, right now, at this very Bright Spring Parlor!

_TAP, TAP._

In the silence, the sound of footsteps as light as a feather reached everyone's ears.

"Oh..."

Who could refrain from sighing in admiration? The flutter of her skirt stirred one's heart. Her elegant gait made one's jaw drop. The faint fragrance that spread in her wake roused one's thirst.

"Welcome."

The usher, his face also flushed with excitement, guided her.

"Aw..."

Watching her vanish indoors like a fading mist, everyone sagged in disappointment, and one man even pushed his cup aside to drink straight from the bottle. Every man present would die for the chance to exchange a single word with her; they'd probably be lost in the memory of her passage for a long time yet.

Dan Yehwa, who had stepped inside, bit her plush lower lip.

_The Young Cult Leader is hardly the only man in the Shining Light Cult! If I start a scandal with someone, he'll have no choice but to annul our engagement._

She made a firm resolution to snag a proper prodigy here today. So, from the moment she entered the first floor, she meticulously evaluated her options while pretending not to...

_Why is his upper lip so far down from his nose? And that other one, he's got no charisma! Oh, that guy's begging me to approach with his eyes. Gross. Should I just blind him?_

...but no matter how much she looked, they were all lacking. The sight of them pleading for her attention was truly pathetic. However desperate her situation was, this was not worth it.

_Let's go upstairs. It ought to be an improvement._

The first floor was just an appetizer, anyway. The truly extraordinary scions, in terms of influence and skill, would be gathered on the second floor. With an euphoric sense of anticipation, she lightly ascended the stairs.

Unlike the bustling small tables below, a single large, round table was placed alone on this floor. The atmosphere was also quite different; the seats were not even wholly filled, but their occupants certainly held a sharper glint in their eyes.

"To think the Young Lady Frost Flower would grace our regular meeting! Haha, this is indeed a monumental day. Those who failed to attend will be cursing themselves, all while I praise my own great fortune."

Was that a speech, a poem, or a diary entry? Dan Yehwa sat down gracefully in an empty seat with a blank expression. Thanks to her, the man who had stood up to greet her was left looking foolish. But he quickly laughed it off with a booming voice, continuing as if he understood.

"Haha, the name 'Frost Flower' truly suits you. Allow me to introduce myself. I'm Cheon Ahmu."

When he revealed his name, Dan Yehwa glanced at him. Unlike others, he made no mention of his affiliation or his parents. Yet she knew who he was: Cheon Ahmu was the second son of the Merchant Palace Lord of the Seven Hallowed Palaces, which managed the Outer Branches and finances of the Shining Light Cult.

His name alone was enough to confirm his status.

"I'm Dan Yehwa," she replied, finally opening her tightly sealed lips to return the greeting. She felt an obligation to show a modicum of courtesy, as he, like her, was a direct descendant of the Seven Hallowed Palaces.

She then carefully studied Cheon Ahmu's face. His air of excitement was no different from that of the lower-ranked scions downstairs. And his looks...

_Is he a toad?_

He was just... so-so. Even then, as far as she could tell, he held the highest standing among the present scions. For all it was called the Regular Meeting of the Scions of the Cult, not a single other scion from either the Palaces or the Elders had bothered to attend.

_I thought at least Yayul Jin would be here..._

Among the prodigies of the Shining Light Cult, Yayul Jin was undoubtedly the most highly regarded. He was the de facto Chairman of the regular meeting, a direct scion of the Palace of Martial Blood, and a prodigy who had reached the pinnacle of martial arts in his youth.

He was also her first pick for her engagement annullment plan, and his absence overwhelmed her with a sense of futility.

"So, whatever did bring the Young Lady all this way? Did you perhaps hasten your steps upon learning that I, Cheon Ahmu, would attend? Heheh."

He had no tact and no wit. Before she even noticed, Cheon Ahmu had already sat down by her, resting his chin on his hand and grinning with half-closed eyes.

_What is he on about? And what's up with all those teeth? Is he a shark?_

His large, yellowish teeth were packed tightly together, resulting in a smile with protuberant gums. Dan Yehwa frowned and averted her gaze, no longer at ease at the table. Of course, Cheon Ahmu disregarded her discomfort and continued to shamelessly lean into her personal space.

Just as her butt was about to leave the chair in indignation...

"He-here comes the sole heir to His Eminence the Leader of the Shining Light Cult, His Reverence Ma Cheonso!"

"_What?!_"

Dan Yehwa shot up from her seat, shrieking like a nightingale. She wasn't alone in her shock; the faces of everyone in the room— and around the Parlor— were filled with consternation.

That wretch of a heir. The Little Lecher Ma Cheonso had come!

"Not me! It's not me this time!"

I met the usher's eyes. One by one, onlookers turned in my direction.

This was familiar, but not entirely.

In the Murim Alliance, everyone would have smiled warmly with friendly gazes, but here and now...

"It's the— the Young Cult Leader!"

"Why is he here? Who is his prey this time..."

"I, I'm heading out first. Take care!"

"L-let's go together!"

Perhaps because I had reached the Transcendent state, every conversation, regardless of distance, struck my ears with perfect clarity. As if fleeing a plague victim, the massive crowd parted like the Red Sea.

So even Demonic Cultists were just people. I'd expected them to be mired in fanaticism, but the sight of me was actually...

Was it out of fear, or disgust? I couldn't tell which it was, but one thing was certain. No one wanted Ma Cheonso to be here.

_Step, step._

But in times like these, one must be even bolder. I spoke softly to the usher before me.

"Announce me."

"Pardon?"

"Isn't that your job?"

"Ye-yes! He-here comes the sole heir to His Eminence the Leader of the Shining Light Cult, His Reverence Ma Cheonso!"

His thunderous voice reverberated far and wide, and my entire body coursed with a strange thrill. I resolutely stepped into the Bright Spring Parlor, followed by Yayul Tak as he buried his forehead in his palm.

I watched the dazed faces of the prodigies as they staggered to their feet, bowed in unison and shouted.

"We greet the Young Cult Leader!"

The sound was loud enough to shake the floor. I nodded subtly and strode further in; I could sense their simultaneous fear and wonder, discomfort and anticipation. Unlike in the orthodox sects, their ambition was palpable. I committed each of them to memory.

Having memorized those faces, I turned my head again only to spot a familiar figure heading for the back door. Long, glossy hair, a slender waist... I was sure of it— I never forget those I've met. She was definitely...

Judging by her scurrilous sneaking, she'd apparently caused some trouble here.

"Wait a moment."

"Huh? Where are you going? Young Lord!"

I followed her through the back door and into a deserted courtyard, confirming that she was indeed that maid. She inspected her surroundings skittishly and then sighed in relief.

"Who is?"
