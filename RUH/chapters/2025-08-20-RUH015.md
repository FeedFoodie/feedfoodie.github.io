_—The Hour of the Tiger (3:00 AM)_

It was late enough that any normal person should be deep asleep; but for those in this location, even that was not permitted.

"Ugh... uhh... Ha...ryeong..."

"Ha, this bastard still hasn't come to his senses. Stop worrying about your damn daughter and worry about yourself. She's probably seeing god in His Reverence's arms by now. Hehehe."

"N...no..."

"You filthy bastard!"

_WHAM!_ A goon from the Flying Tiger Gang ruthlessly kicked Eul Taesik in the chest as he clung to his trouser leg.

"Kugh... bring... Haryeong back..."

But Eul Taesik wrapped his arms around the warrior's leg, determined never to let go; ichor dribbled from his mouth and soaked the trouser cuff stickily.

"You stubborn bastard! You've taken this much Joy, yet you still have this kind of strength? You're just a decrepit old man who serves drinks in a gambling den."

_THUD! THUD!_ Chilling sounds accompanied the merciless blows. Though he was only a third-rate warrior, it was not a level of punishment that the ruined Eul Taesik could easily endure.

His face was covered in bruises, and blood streamed from his nose.

The warrior, having beaten Eul Taesik to a pulp, approached his colleague and snapped in irritation.

"Ha, damn it. Why does the village chief want this useless bastard alive? He's worthless. _Ptui!_ Filthy fuck."

"Why else? It's because of his daughter. But now that she's been sold off to the Young Cult Leader, won't we get to bury him soon?"

"I never liked this bastard from the start. He's nobody, and still he keeps talking back."

"Hehe. Don't they say he was a big shot back in the day?"

"Hmph! How much of a big shot could a guy like this have been? At best he was just a coin counter at the Palace of Merchants, or a bootlicker chasing the Saintess at the Palace of Sacred Flames."

Many among the Flying Tiger gang viewed Eul Taesik unfavorably.

First, when he should have been offering his liver and kidneys, everything he had, to the Flying Tiger gang, he instead prevented them from even touching his pretty daughter!

The second reason was the way he, an addicted wreck, would sometimes glare at them like a tiger.

Just like now.

"Should we just... kill this fucker?"

"Stop. You want to get your head bashed in by the village chief?"

"Fuck. We can just say he had an overdose. Look, look at that bastard's eyes. He's looking at us like that again."

As the enraged Flying Tiger thug pointed accusingly, his colleague's gaze shifted.

As he watched, Eul Taesik was barely managing to push himself up... but his glare was so fierce it made one gulp involuntarily.

"Give... back... my daughter..."

"This son of a bitch!"

The warrior shot forward, then grabbed Eul Taesik by the hair and slammed his head against the floor over and over.

"_Gack..._"

"You bastard! Try that look on me again! Huh? Go on, I dare you! I'll gouge out them eyes!"

_CRACK!_ Eul Taesik's face was drenched in blood, his eyes rolled back.

The warrior didn't stop there, now slapping his face repeatedly.

_SLAP, SLAP!_

"_Hrrack, ptui!_"

When Eul Taesik collapsed like a bag of rice, the warrior finally seemed to calm down. He spat on Eul Taesik's face before returning to his spot, seething.

"Hey, that... that old man. Are we just gonna leave him to die? That's a lot of blood..."

"Why even bother now? Are we even short on dying fuckers here? Just say this geezer fell on his face real hard. Got it?"

After he finished speaking, the warrior scanned his surroundings, and dozens of cells came into view.

It was a confinement facility, set up in a basement even deeper than the gambling den. Inside, elderly people, high on drugs, were packed tightly together.

Not a single person with a sound mind could be found, no matter how hard one looked.

"So much waste of space."

"Hehe. But their kids sure are useful, right? Working day and night to make the stuff, trying to save their parents, not even knowing they're getting dosed just doing the job."

"Heheh. They're so fucking dumb, they can't even guess that the shit they make is going straight down their folks' gullets."

"They probably think their parents are in a nice little clinic with actual doctors and shit."

Their conversation was truly horrifying.

They were exploiting the naiveté of the lay cultists, forcing the young to produce drugs while addicting their parents and holding them hostage under the guise of protection. So the villagers had no choice but to give the village chief their absolute obedience, and anyone who asked questions or raised a protest would soon vanish without leaving behind so much as a corpse.

As the years passed, even those who harbored distrust had no choice but to follow along.

The thug leaned back as if to shake off his boredom and muttered.

"Anyway, if the Young Cult Leader and the village chief strike a good deal, we'll finally be done with this shithole of a—"

_THUD._

The previously attentive warrior slumped over, trailing off mid-sentence.

"Hey? What..."

His colleague, who had turned to look at him in surprise, was no different. It was only a matter of sequence.

_THUD._

"So this is the place."

And behind the two of them, a man in a black mask stood. The blood-red robe that covered his entire body and its sharply raised collar were both ominous and sharp.

Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso...

...was debuting his new identity.

"As if building an underground gambling den wasn't enough, they even captured the elderly and got them addicted on purpose..."

I found this place based on Eul Haryeong's tale. The security was lax; I discovered an entrance while scouting the area, a hidden path leading to a deeper basement.

But to think they were doing something so revolting here.

_There has to be more to this place. The facility where they manufacture the drug... is it that door I went past?_

I bit my lip hard and turned away.

Not yet.

These people couldn't even support their own bodies. Freeing them now would only cause greater chaos, rather than allowing them to escape.

"I swear I'll be back."

It was then, as I was about to head back up—

"Ha...ryeong..."

Though it was faint, I clearly heard the girl's name.

Startled, I flinched and turned to glimpse a blood-soaked hand reaching from the third cell.

As I approached and looked inside, a face completely covered in blood, lying on the floor, caught my eye.

"You are...?"

"Ha...ryeong..."

There was no mistake; although his hair was a mess and he was covered in blood, it was the same person from the portrait at the Shining Light Library.

The former head of the Shining Heavenly Demon Company, Eul Taesik.

I immediately searched the guards' clothes, and soon a bundle of keys jingled in my hand. _CREEEAK_. _CLINK._ I opened the door, carried Eul Taesik out, laid him gently by the wide entrance, and checked his pulse.

"Are you alright?"

"Ha...ryeong..."

_His breath is shallow, his body is cold as ice. His condition..._

His condition was critical.

Perhaps it was because he'd taken Spirit Joy for too long, but his conception and governing vessels were severely damaged.

That alone would have put a normal life beyond his reach. But his brain was a bigger issue; to compound the external trauma, stagnant blood had pooled in his qianding and shenting acupoints.

At this rate, he would surely die!

"I have... a request..."

_Terminal lucidity?_

Eul Taesik's pupils regained their focus, and he somehow managed to speak clearly.

Death was undoubtedly imminent.

"Eul Taesik! Stay with me!"

He slowly turned his head to meet my eyes.

"I beg it... of you..."

What kind of request could he have when he was on the verge of death?

"Tell no one... of my death... _cough!_"

What a foolish man— he wasn't asking to be saved, nor was he asking for revenge. He simply wished for his death to be hidden so the news wouldn't reach Haryeong.

His concern for his daughter, even at his last, was unmistakable.

_His qi and blood are in turmoil. Must I watch him die like this?_

No.

I _wanted_ to save him.

I had never met him before, but for some reason, my heart was moved.

_The chances are remarkably low... but there is a way._

Physical Purification.

It was a method for circulating one's qi through another's body for one full cycle in order to clear blockages in their qi and blood.

Pinpointing the acupoints wasn't difficult, but it could be risky; if he were to muster even a handful of his own qi, we would both be in mortal danger.

But my hesitation was brief.

I hurriedly removed his upper garment, sat him up in front of me, placed my palms on his back, and unleashed the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

_WOOOONG!_

The Heavenly Demon Shade manifested at my back, and my qi poured into him as if a dam had broken.

_All demonic arts of the Cult are based on the Heavenly Demon Divine Art. As long as he didn't learn the martial arts of the orthodox sects..._

_KWOOOOOSH-_

In an instant, qi equivalent to one cycle[^1] poured into his body.

"_Cough..._"

Eul Taesik's face twisted in pain as fiery heat burned through him, but I was just as bewildered.

_He's... absorbing my qi?_

Within Eul Taesik's body lay a massive dantian, like an empty ancient city!

He relentlessly absorbed the Heavenly Demon Divine Art's qi, like a hungry ghost. Even the one entire cycle of qi I'd poured in was nowhere near enough to fill his body.

_His qi center wasn't broken... it was just depleted of energy! How can something like this be possible?_

It was an unexpected development. Eul Taesik's dantian absorbed my qi mercilessly, like parched earth drinking the rain.

This had become a battle between the size of his dantian and the amount of qi I possessed. If I couldn't fill his body even with all the qi I had... I, too, would not escape death.

_The chance to stop has already passed. I'll pour everything in until the end!_

I once again pushed in my qi.

Two cycles. Three cycles.

The Heavenly Demon Divine Art steadily filled his collapsed dantian with a solid wall of fire...

_KWAAAAANG!_

...and then, like a collapsing dam, it blasted through his blocked conception and governing vessels.

"_Heugh...!_"

Just when I thought it was over, my inner arts began to drain away again with a _WHOOSH_.

_This... is my last!_

_KWAAAAA!_ The last, fourth cycle of qi I had left in my dantian began to flood his body.

As it did, Eul Taesik's scarred skin began to shed as if he were molting, and new flesh grew anew; his bones, bent from long-standing bad habits, began to return to their proper place.

_CRACK, CRACK._

And finally.

_WOOOONG!_

An intensely hot and pure demonic qi began to emanate from Eul Taesik.

"Ha... haa..."

I slowly removed my hands.

My qi, once like an ocean, was now reduced to a mere puddle. It had been a hair's breadth. If his dantian had been even slightly larger than mine, I would not have escaped death.

I wanted to start meditating immediately.

For the first time in a long while, I was out of breath, and the strength had drained from my entire body.

"Body Reformation... to think he's reached the Transcendent Realm."

He, who had become a wreck of a man, had surpassed his previous level and entered the divine state known as the Transcendent Realm.

His wrinkles were gone, his face completely renewed. If he were to just change his style, he would become nearly unrecognizable.

Seeing him sitting there peacefully, a hollow laugh escaped me.

"...Who are you, young master?"

His voice was heavy with power, and his eyes were as fierce as a tiger's.

He had returned.

The former head of the Shining Heavenly Demon Company, Eul Taesik.

"First... give me a second to breathe."

I released my tension and slumped to the floor.
