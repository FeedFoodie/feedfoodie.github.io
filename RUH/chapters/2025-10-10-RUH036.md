"However, that doesn't mean they'll completely come over to our side."

Even if the Palace of Merchants resented the Palace of Martial Blood, they would not move easily as long as Esen was around. Neither would they willingly discard the Palace of Minor Light as a useful card.

Most likely, they would simply fund both sides and step back to watch the fire.

I was aware.

Cheon Birang. I already had shackles on him, so perhaps things will resolve more easily.

Two ornate, dark-red duandao were revealed: the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames, received from Cheon Semin.

"They're called the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames, I'm told."

That was the biggest question. The Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames were a sacred treasure of the Shining Light Cult— he couldn't be so thoughtless as to give them away this easily, and yet he'd presented them at our very first meeting.

"Fufu. Heheheheh... Hahaha!"

Why is he laughing so suddenly? It's unsettling. I stared at him, silently conveying my displeasure.

Just like that?

"Is this enough?"

What answer? What connection could there be between him telling me to draw the blade out of the blue and the Lord of Merchants giving it to me... don't tell me—

"A fake?"

...and I can't even sew that mouth shut.

The Heavenly Demon Divine Art?

"It seems you've found your answer now."

_But duandao... the Ten Armaments of the Great Phoenix taught nothing like this. And they even require a reverse grip in order to cut..._

A renowned sword unfamiliar to the hand is worth less than a kitchen knife on a chopping board. Without the martial arts to support it, it's nothing more than a pretty trinket.

The Council of Elders...

I hadn't considered dealing with them until after the Seven Hallowed Palaces were sorted out.

The Palace of Bliss sent assassins.
