They gave up on the captured emperor and installed his younger brother as the new one.

So... he was abandoned by everyone.

"But I wanted to go back."

"A traitor you may be, but to me, you are simply the benefactor who saved my life. I understand that now. The path was near, yet I wandered aimlessly, searching for it in distant places. The people I must protect are right here before my eyes. It was never where I sat that was important, but who I was. You have taught me that."

"Could you tell me your name?"

An unidentifiable sense of unease reverberated in my head.

"...that I'm sorry I couldn't return."

"They have no freedom, nor the right to choose anything."

_Rustle._ The bushes we hid within swayed for a moment before settling back into place.

_THUMP._

As he took another step, his tattered boots were exposed.

_THUMP._

I couldn't stop him, nor could I hold him back.

I could only watch.

"Your... Your Majesty...?"

I don't know who said it, but starting with that cry, everyone struggled to open their eyes and watch his movements.

His figure, walking confidently among the cages, was a wretched sight, yet shone more brilliantly than anyone else's.

"I am here!"

_CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK!_

As Zhu Qizhen's shout ended, hundreds of the Oirats warriors revealed themselves. Exuding a suffocating aura, they all aimed flaming arrows in this direction.

The moment those bowstrings were released, the many people trapped here would become corpses.

«So... you've come? To walk right into your own grave, what a foolish emperor.»

It was him— the one who chased me on the black horse.

He stepped forward, his massive frame radiating a wild aura.

«Or maybe you've just gone insane?»

The boisterous laughter of the Oirats spread through the mountains. I couldn't understand what they were saying, but I knew for certain it was mockery.

However, Zhu Qizhen paid them no mind and retorted.

"Thus it is said— in sight of profit, one is to think of justice; in sight of danger, one is to offer their life. We should have considered whether taking the profit before Us was the righteous path, and in the face of danger, We should have given Our life. That is what We should have done, but instead, We have endured under their thumbs for over half a year... fear no longer. We will be with you."

Zhu Qizhen's words were filled with all the anguish and torment he had suffered.

"Your... Majesty...!"

The wails of the Ming soldiers echoed from all around.

His small and frail-looking back had, at some point, become that of a giant.

«There's no reason to refuse when he's offering himself as a sacrifice. Seize the emperor!»

«At once!»

Ten of the Oirats warriors stepped forward, weapons in hand; Zhu Qizhen also walked toward them.

And then—

**_BLAAAAAAM!_**

The heavens and the earth shook.

"If I let you go alone like that, what would that make me?"

"You...!"

I approached Zhu Qizhen while circulating the Heavenly Demon Divine Art; as it reached the sixth level, heat surged through my entire body.

«You...! You're the warrior from back then!»

Greetings could wait. First, I would save Zhu Qizhen.

The collars of the ten warriors approaching Zhu Qizhen fluttered, and I watched them indifferently before extending my hand.

_WHOOOOOSH-!_

The Heavenly Demon Divine Art gathered in my palm like a flash. Then, I flicked it out lightly.

_FWAAAAAH!_

A dark red orb spiraled fiercely toward them. The orb, once small enough to hold in my palm, had suddenly grown as large as a house as it crashed down upon them.

**_BLAAAAAM!_**

«Uaaaargh!»

«Ugh!»

Terrified screams reached me, while all sorts of gasps of awe flowed from those trapped in the prison. Amidst it all, only his voice came through clearly.

"Are you trying to save me... again? But why...?"

What do you mean, why?

I made a promise, didn't I? That I'd save you.

«Quite impressive. However, let's see how long you can last. Archers!»

_TWANG!_

At his shout, the enemy forces drew their bowstrings in unison.

Without hesitation, I unleashed the Heavenly Demon Sacred Flame Stride and leaped into the air; flames scattered beneath my feet as I rose high up.

«Fire!»

_FWOOSH-FWOOSH-FWOOSH-FWOOSH!_

Arrows filled the sky, their sharp points raining down the field, but however fast they were, they couldn't hit me even though I was in plain sight.

I took a step in mid-air, and the flames flickering from my body turned into afterimages of myself, scattering in all directions.

This was another technique depicted in the Heavenly Demon Cave: a movement art that materialized another me at every step in order to overwhelm the enemy.

I named it the _the Heavenly Demon Formation Steps_.

_SHWAAAAAH!_

In no time, dozens of Stars of Black Flames loomed in the air.

_FWOOM! FWOOWAWOOM!_

The arrows that touched them turned to flame, and I closed in on them, constricting the space with the relentlessly appearing duplicates. It was as if a great hidden army was revealing itself one by one, marching in formation toward the enemy camp.

«Is this even possible?»

The crowd was steeped in silence. As I landed lightly in front of Zhu Qizhen, the dozens of clones shimmered like a heat haze and vanished.

He asked, unable to hide his astonishment, "What... in the world... are you?"

You said it yourself.

"A traitor."

"The Embroidered Uniform Guard's Fourth Blade was not like you."

"Because this is the Murim."

"In the Murim... are there lots of people like you?"

Judging by his expression, he was ready to declare the Murim a traitor and invade it too... but he could spare himself the needless worry.

There wouldn't be that many on my level.

«As expected, you're no ordinary man. Who are you?»

I turned my head to the voice up ahead. It was him.

I couldn't understand Mongolian, but I spoke my piece regardless.

"I will not ask why you have done this. Stand down. Should you back away now, I shall spare your life."

«Your masked face makes me curious. To meet a warrior like you in a place like this, I'd like to at least exchange greetings. I am Esen.»

Seeing him step forward with the poise of a hero, it seemed he was unwilling to back down after all.

"Fine. I had no intention of just leaving anyway."

The black flames that had been quietly fading from my body flared up once more, in a blaze.

A quick scan of the surroundings revealed hundreds of warriors aiming their bows. In my mind, I projected dozens of strategies against them.

And yet...

_Someone will die._

If their arrows were aimed at the cages, I'd have no means to block them all, and a great many prisoners would be inevitably shot to death.

_Is that... the only way?_

The desperation of the trapped people reached me, chilling me to the core.

But even as I struggled to make a decision, he spoke again:

«It's astonishing every time I see it. You people call this qi, don't you? It's certainly different from us, who train our physical bodies to the extreme. To show such spirit with that slender body, it's not an exaggeration to call you a grandmaster!»

He raised a fist. Then, surprisingly, the warriors simultaneously lowered the bowstrings they had drawn taut.

He then circled his fist in the air, and the warriors began to retreat.

_Concealment...?_

The true value of archers lay in stealth. It wasn't just about their usefulness in long range; their effectiveness soared when the enemy couldn't tell the source of their strikes.

As I urgently calculated my next move, he spoke again.

«The Yingzong Emperor is no longer Emperor of Ming. To me, he is but a hollow sacrifice, no more, no less. How about this? If you fight me and win...»

"You talk too much."

I immediately unleashed the the Heavenly Demon Formation Steps again.

There was no point in talking. I took a step forward, and my black flames once again created dozens of duplicates. I was about to pass the enemy commander and dive onto the retreating archers—

_SHWAAAAAH!_

In that instant, a sound tore through the air, and I felt an overwhelming force.

The enemy commander swung his great axe and an immense wind pressure erupted, splitting and extinguishing more than half of my clones.

_The Transcendent Realm...?_

He was far stronger than I had expected.

His cutting power was no different from a strike using enhanced sword qi.

_He didn't release any qi. It was just a slash with pure strength. How is this possible with only external arts?_

The Cultivation of Qi is the practice of creating a vessel within the human body, composed of Nine Star muscles and bones, to contain the heaven-staggering power called _qi_. Those who master it are called _martial artists_. By cloaking the body in this qi, one can exert power beyond physical limits and even imbue objects to reach a state that transcends the extremes of destructive power and range.

In the orthodox sects, those who reached an absolute level by flawlessly and completely shrouding objects in their qi were called masters of the Transcendent Realm— and from that man, I felt a force like one unleashed by a master of the Transcendent Realm.

Yet it contained not a single shred of qi, so how could I possibly understand it?

_To display this much martial prowess with physical strength alone... does that mean his very bone structure is different from the start?_

Last time when he shot that arrow, I'd just assumed he was a martial artist who had reached the Supreme Peak Realm, capable of imbuing his attacks with qi. But seeing him now, he was clearly different from a martial artist.

This must have been the source of the Yuan's power that allowed them to cross the Central Plains and dominate the continent.

_The Heavenly Demon Divine Art converts qi into the Flames of the Dark Heavens, unleashing demonic power. That's why it's called a demonic art. So, a person whose life qi and physical body have reached the absolute peak might also..._

...exist.

The Five Emperors of the Outer Regions, said to be on par with the Ten Grandmasters of the Central Plains.

Among them, the Yuan general who leads the Ultimate Flaming Horse Cavalry, known as the _Final Flame_.

"The Ultimate Flame Emperor Esen."

«Oho... You know of me?»

Once I accepted everything, my mind felt as clear as if it had been doused with cold water.

"They weren't actually worth worrying about in the first place."

I turned to face him.

Behind me, Zhu Qizhen and the others trapped in the cages watched with fervent hope.

«You're an impatient one. Is there any reason to do something so unnecessary? All you have to do is defeat me.»

_TAP!_

He kicked off the ground and leaped. It wasn't a movement art, just a simple jump, but the ten feet between us vanished in an instant.

_WHOOOOOSH!_

_FWAROOM!_

Without delay, I unleashed the Heavenly Demon Sacred Flame Stride and leaped more than five steps back.

And then—

**_CRASH!_**
