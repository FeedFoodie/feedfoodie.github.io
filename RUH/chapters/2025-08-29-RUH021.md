"Ah, I don't care! I'm just going to shave my head and go live in the mountains!"

"Hush! Don't say such things. It's natural for an engaged couple to quarrel. You don't know how wonderful it is to be able to face each other and hold a wedding ceremony, do you?"

She couldn't have known, but her nanny's words were true.

In the Ming Dynasty, where scholars were respected, it was considered impious for an unmarried man and woman to even face each other before the great matter of marriage. Thanks to this, marriages arranged by matchmakers were commonplace, and it was very frequent for couples to see each other for the first time in the bridal chamber.

_To think I married that ugly sack of rags!_

Thus, there were many men and women who wept and regretted it. Not only that, there were also cases where personalities didn't match or one partner was abusive.

The nanny had been one such person, having fled a violent husband and, after wandering aimlessly, settled here in the Heavenly Mountains.

Of course, to Dan Yehwa, who was born and raised in the murim, it was all nonsense.

"What kind of nonsense is that? Who's getting married to whom? I'd rather marry someone I've never seen."

"His family is good, his looks are outstanding. The Young Cult Leader lacks nothing, does he? Besides, your martial arts are superior, Young Lady, so you don't have to worry about being controlled by him."

Seeing her shriek like that, the nanny shook her head desperately.

She didn't want to push Dan Yehwa to do something she hated, but.

"Young Lady. Still, please lower your voice. If the Lord hears you, you'll be severely punished."

It would be a relief if she wasn't kicked out.

"Let him hear me! His Eminence isn't even here, so why should I be scared into marrying a scoundrel like that?"

"Young Lady!"

The nanny cried out desperately.

However, sometimes, when anxiety grows, it manifests in reality.

Just like now.

_CRASH!_ The door flew open, and a thunderous shout, as if a great mountain were collapsing, filled the room.

"By whose authority do you dare let such words pass your lips!"

"Your Lordship!"
