"How long have you been held captive?"

"Truly... are you saying you know nothing?"

_Is ignorance a crime now?_ After all that killing to get in, all I found was this weird little guy just sitting here.

"Am I supposed to know?"

"You came to Us without knowing anything? And you expect Us to believe that?"

My brow started to furrow.

_Why am I even trying to convince this guy?_

"What's so hard to believe? Have you lived your whole life being deceived? I just happened to come across you here."

I ignored him and scanned the surroundings for any potential clues.

But then...

"_Insolent!_" he suddenly roared.

I scowled and shot back. "What is it now?"

"Everything. From your gaze to your tone, everything about you is insolent!"

_This is driving me crazy. Is he actually insane?_

"You're insane."

"That too is insolent. Watch your tongue at once!"

"Yes, I'll do that. I understand. So you should keep your mouth shut too."

"We shall _not_."

He was so absurd, I could only stare. He looked just like a pouting child, huffing and puffing so indignantly that I felt the urge to smack him on the head.

_Huu_, I swallowed a sigh and asked again. "Then what do you want me to do?"

"You are to answer Our questions truthfully and without falsehood."

"Ask away."

"And we will _not_ tolerate such informal speech."

"Ah, sure."

"...why have you come to me?"

_That's it. This man isn't worth talking to anymore. He's definitely crazy._

I ignored him again and searched the area.

"You _cur!_"

"Look. I don't have time. I'm not going to indulge your whining or your questioning. If you want to live, run away from this place right now."

As soon as I finished, I could feel anger radiating off him. The sound of his breathing, huffed through his nose, was rough.

When I glanced at him, his eyes were brimming with tears. _What in the world does he want from me?_

"_Huu_... Fine. Let me ask just one thing."

"We have not granted you permission to ask questions!"

Ignoring him completely, I approached.

"Don't come! Don't approach!"

"...were the people in this yurt the only prisoners? I heard that the Oirats have locked up many people. Do you happen to know where they are? This lowly one would appreciate any information you saw fit to grant."

I asked him politely, clasping my hands in a bow.

_I can't just wander around looking for the prison forever._

Was my sincerity conveyed? His tightly shut mouth slowly opened.

"...North. Go north from here, follow the Chu River, and cross the mountain ridge. There is a prison there."

"Thanks!"

"We _told_ you We do not permit informal speech!"

The corners of my mouth twitched.

_Now all I have to do is hurry to the prison and set them free._

I hastily made to leave the yurt again. Just then—

"Wait! Stop right there!"

"Is there still something you need?"

"We have not granted you permission to leave."

_Here we go again._

"You tell me not to approach, then not to leave— what, do you want me to live here with you? I'm busy. I have to go."

"We cannot allow it. If you truly came here knowing nothing, then take Us... take me to Beijing."

"Nah, I'm heading north, actually. Safe journey!"

"You wretch! We will have you executed!"

"By what authority? Can you really say that, looking at yourself right now?"

_Now he was even talking about execution._

"Authority? If not Us, then who else would have the authority?"

"Who are you?"

"We are... Zhu Qizhen. [^1]"

"Oh, sure. And I'm the Star of Black Flames."

Just as I turned to leave, a sense of dissonance flashed through my mind.

_Zhu Qizhen?_ In the Ming Dynasty, the surname Zhu is not to be used, nor even spoken.

Of course— it's a surname that can only be used by the imperial family!

And the emperor's name...

_Yingzong Emperor Zhu Qizhen?_

"Given the look on your ugly face, you finally recognize Us. Indeed, We are the Emperor of this nation."

_No way. This is a load of malarkey. The Emperor of Ming, trapped in the middle of a forest in the Oirats' territory? And I'm supposed to believe that?_

"If you have understood the depths of your offense, then bow three times immediately, touch your forehead to the ground, and kowtow to Us."

I came to my senses and studied him again.

_Zhu Qizhen. A man who calls himself the emperor. Is this man a lunatic, or is he really... the emperor?_

"Pull the other one."

I scoffed and turned to leave.

However.

"Did you truly not know?"

His trembling voice continued. It was different from before. I could feel his sincerity.

"What."

"The fact that We were imprisoned here."

"I've told you several times, but since you keep asking, I'll answer one last time. I really didn't know. Got it?"

I was sick of his childish behavior, yes, but what fault was it of his that brought him here?

But then...

"...Save me."

This time, he changed his attitude and asked for help.

"You don't have to believe me. No, don't believe me. ...I will allow it. But I... I must live. There is someone I must live to meet."

Looking at him again, his desperation struck my heart.

_Everyone has their own circumstances. He surely had his own as well._

I felt sorry for him, having suffered such misfortune at such a tender age.

"...My business comes first. If you can keep up until then, I'll help you as much as I can."

"...I understand."

"Follow me."

"Are we not surrounded by Oirats?"

"We were."

_Not anymore._

The man who called himself Zhu Qizhen lifted the flap and cautiously stepped outside. Then, after scanning the area, he stopped in front of a corpse whose lower body had been severed.

"Wang Jin. You have met an end befitting of you."

"You knew him?"

"...I did. But he is not worth remembering."

After muttering those meaningful words, he began to walk. However, I paid him no mind and stood still, surveying the area.

"What are you doing? Was your business here?"

"And how far can you get on those feet? Are you going to wander the mountain path in just your socks? It's pouring. Here, wear these at least."

I gauged the size by eye and threw him boots from a corpse's feet.

_THUD._ Seeing the shoes fall to the ground, Zhu Qizhen hesitated for a long time.

"Do they gross you out?"

"No."

He bit his lip and shoved his feet into them.

_He seems stubborn and selfish, but I guess he's not a complete fool._

After putting on the shoes, Zhu Qizhen said sarcastically, "Is that all? Then let's go quickly."

"Not yet. They couldn't have walked all the way here. Their horses must be kept nearby..."

As I finished speaking, I placed my palm on the ground and focused my five senses. When I closed my eyes, I could feel the waves of vibration clearly.

_CLOP, CLOP, CLOP._

_Found them._

I lifted my hand and said to Zhu Qizhen, "I have good news and bad news."

"What nonsense is this?"

"There are horses just ahead. If we ride them, we can reach the prison quickly."

"Then that's settled. So what's the bad news? Did the horses die or something?"

"No. The opposite."

I finished speaking to him and turned my body toward the path I came from. Zhu Qizhen turned his gaze as well. The entrance to the mountain pass was hidden by bushes, so nothing much was visible.

However, I heard it.

"Warhorses are galloping this way right now."

"What?"

"So, if you want to live, run!"

I grabbed Zhu Qizhen's wrist and sprinted north. Since he had no martial arts whatsoever, I didn't even use movement arts; I just ran. Even so, for someone in his position, one of my steps was like three or four of his, so it was natural for his mouth to hang open.

"_Aaaaaaah!_"

"Shut up!"

"Mmph...!"

He even started screaming, so I clamped a hand over his mouth and ran while carrying him.

After pushing through the bushes and emerging into a meadow, I saw Ferghana horses tied up in a row at a shabby stable, grazing on grass.

_But... what are you doing? Out for a stroll?_

Zhu Qizhen was leisurely scanning the meadow with his hands clasped behind his back.

"What are you doing? Hurry up and get on."

"...We do not know how to ride."

"You're kidding, right?"

"Why should I know how? Isn't that a lowly martial artist thing?"

There's a lowly martial artist right here.

_Is he asking to be buried in the middle of this forsaken field?_

"Even if you're not a martial artist, it's an essential skill in the Central Plains, isn't it?"

"That may be so for a commoner like you!"

_Oh, really?_

"Then use your feet."

I grabbed the horse's reins and pulled.

Just then—

"Bring me a palanquin!"

"Are you for real?"

_Wow, what a shameless bastard!_

I stared at him blankly, dumbfounded.

Right at that moment.

**_FWOOOOOM—!_**

From the distance, there came the fierce blare of a horn.

«The emperor has disappeared! Find him!»

Their urgent shouts followed; I couldn't understand what they were saying, but I knew this place would be discovered in an instant.

I leapt onto the horse in a single motion and said, "Grab on."

"What are you talking about?"

"I said _grab on!_"

"I'm _saying_, why should We... _oof!_"

Before he could finish his sentence, I snatched his hand and pulled him onto the horse behind me.

_NEEEEEIGH—!_

Perhaps because a stranger had mounted it, the Ferghana horse reared up on its hind legs and thrashed about.

In response, I did my best to stroke the horse's head and said, "Please. It won't take long."

Whether my sincerity got through or not, the horse shook its head and its anger gradually subsided.

"How dare you commit such an audacious act, seating Us _behind_ you!"

_Look who's talking! I never imagined I'd end up like this, with some guy riding behind me._

«Over there!»

At that moment, a cold sensation ran down my spine. And sure enough...

_PWING! PWIIING!_

As sharp twangs rang out, I looked back to see a volley of arrows piercing through the forest, flying in a straight line toward us.

"Whoa, whoa!"

I yanked the reins, grabbed Zhu Qizhen by the scruff of his neck, pushed him down, and leaned my body to the side.

"Ugh!"

_SWISHWISHWISHH_!

The arrows, having lost their target, shot past us, ahead of the horse. They flew so far that the arrowheads eventually disappeared from view.

_They say the archery of the Yuan and Joseon dynasties is truly unparalleled..._

For martial artists, the most fearsome weapons are firearms and bows. Even if one trains their qi to possess earth-shattering power, unless they've mastered an indestructible body, there's no way to withstand a random volley of arrows from a distance. It was especially difficult in a meadow like this, with nowhere to dodge and nowhere to hide.

Of course, it wouldn't be impossible to deal with them, but...

_There's no guarantee I would survive either._

_CLOP, KA-CLOP!_

I galloped across the meadow without hesitation, but after running for a while, the figures of our enemies finally appeared at our backs.

But...

«Chase them! Don't let the emperor escape!»

Dozens. No, hundreds. Fully armed soldiers, even their mounts clad in horse armor, were packed tightly together. It was like a black tidal wave sweeping over the green meadow.

Surely they couldn't all be here to catch me. Then... what in the world is this guy?

"You... who the heck are you?"

"Didn't I tell you! We are... the Emperor of the Great Ming— Ugh!"

_This guy, who can't even keep his balance and is just clinging to my waist, is actually the emperor? This is a complete mess. To think I came to save the followers, only to end up being chased along with some mystery tag-along._

«Get them!»

"Hold on tight. _Hah!_"

_NEEEEEIGH!_

_For starters, let's just get away._

And so, with hundreds of soldiers in tow, we raced across the meadow like a beam of light.
