> The first son of the Murim Alliance's Chairman, the Ten Strike Finisher Yang Jagak. Once called the greatest genius of all time, he was poisoned to death before even reaching the age of thirty.

\[From the Heavenly Fate Murim Annals\]

* * *

"Eurgh..."

My eyes opened. I remembered being poisoned, my flow of qi and blood completely cut off; I'd closed my eyes, writhing in excruciating pain, and assumed I was dead. Yet, strangely, my senses were gradually returning to me.

"…am I dreaming?"

My back was cold, and a gray rock ceiling filled my blurry sight. My body was heavy, and my once-full qi center felt completely empty. With a groan, I forced myself to sit up, my mind too muddled to think. I just sat there, dazed.

As time passed, the feeling that I was alive became undeniable.

"Was I kidnapped? Still, I survived."

I let out a deep sigh of relief. At least I was alive. It's better to be alive in a mound of dog shit than dead in a rose garden.

"But where is this?"

It looked like I had been kidnapped, or more precisely, imprisoned. This wasn't a normal room but a natural rock cave. It wasn't dark, however, thanks to expensive luminous pearls embedded in the walls. My ambusher must belong to a large faction to afford all of these.

"The Jaegal Clan? The Mount Hua Sect? Or maybe the Evil Alliance."

There were way too many suspects to narrow a culprit down. The current state of murim was as the calm before a storm; internally, schemes over the next Chairman position were endless, while externally, the unorthodox sects had united under the banner of the Evil Alliance and were raring for a fight.

It wasn't strange that someone would try to harm me.

"It's sealed tight."

I pushed aside the unanswerable thoughts and examined my surroundings. I was in a sealed cave with no way out. Fortunately, a gap in the back wall provided ventilation; my captors had also considerately left a sack full of nutrient balls so I wouldn't starve.

"I should start by circulating my qi."

If they weren't trying to kill me, then clearly they intended to let me out eventually. Until then, healing my body was the priority.

"Leaving me, Yang Jagak, alive... I'll make you regret it. I swear it."

Those had been my thoughts as I gritted my teeth a mere two hours ago. But now I lay slumped on the floor, unable to even sit cross-legged.

"…if I am me, but I am not me, then who am I?"

I hadn't studied in the Daoist or Buddhist schools, nor had I ever held a deep interest in philosophy. lt was simply that I couldn't understand my current situation— my mind was clearly Yang Jagak's, but this body… belonged to someone else.

I'd simply been thirsty, so I had approached this pool of water collected from a crack on the wall. But upon seeing my reflection, I'd been so shocked that I'd nearly drowned right there. One might think of it as an exaggeration, but it was not remotely untrue.

I had often been told that I had an upright and kind appearance. I'd never paid much attention to my looks, but I used to be quite satisfied with the face I saw in bronze mirrors.

But _this_ face...

"Is this... a man, or a woman?"

This body's androgynous aura was overwhelming. Its skin was as pale as ash, its frame gaunt and lanky. On top of that, its face was as pretty as a courtesan's. Though its eyes were long and slanted, they held no sharpness, showing only a murky haze.

"Surely...!"

It couldn't be. There was no way. I hurriedly checked my groin.

"Whew... at least I'm still a guy. Also… that was pretty impressive!"

There was at least one good thing about— no, this was no time for such thoughts!

"This is definitely not my body. And the inner qi in this qi center is so impure. The qi of the Transcendent Phoenix Style is nowhere to be found… then who in the world is this person?"

As expected, there was no answer.

Had I ever felt so clueless? No.

I was once known as the greatest genius of all time, capable of unraveling any martial art, no matter how difficult. My alias was the Ten Strike Finisher. It was given to me because I could find a way to break down any martial art within ten moves, even if there was a bit of exaggeration added to that.

But now...

"This is the first time I've ever felt so foolish. Hah..."

I had already sighed three times. But there had to be a way, and I _would_ find the answer; I always did. I collected myself and began to search every nook and cranny of the cave. This body had inner qi, after all, even if it was impurely seething.

What if I _hadn't_ been kidnapped? What if instead my spirit had entered the body of someone who was in secluded training? A trace of that person might remain somewhere in here.

"There it is."

I found it without much difficulty after going deeper inside. A wide mural covered the wall; it seemed hundreds of years old, its traces so faint it could be mistaken for graffiti. But I recognized it at a glance— this was the martial art the owner of this body had been learning.

"Truly a forceful and unusual martial art."

That was my first thought. But the more I looked, the more impressed I felt.

"Amazing... Who on earth created this? Was it Bodhidharma? No. It's too domineering for him. And yet, I've never seen such a magnificent martial art."

I was slowly captivated, as if sinking into a swamp. The martial art on the wall was on another level, offering new enlightenment. Its origin was unknown, but I marveled, imagining the state its creator must have reached, and so I threw away my worries and immersed myself in it.

"This person died from qi deviation. He shouldn't have circulated it past the perineum acupoint to the crown acupoint. He should have broken common sense and gone straight to the crown acupoint, letting the qi circulate as if it were melting into his very roots."

I could now see why the body's owner had fallen into a coma.

At the end of the mural was a tiny scrawl, crammed like a weeping willow in a typhoon. "Within the shadow of death lies the path to salvation." This phrase, that one must endure crisis to become stronger, was the last desperate hope left by the dying owner.

He must have interpreted the mural by the standards of common sense, which led to his qi deviation. But that was wrong. This martial art sought to defy every convention. _Heaven-Defying._ It was grandiose, but one could call it a martial art that challenged the very heavens.

After another long period of time, I finally masted all that the mural had to convey. I didn't know how long I'd been engrossed for, but the depleted nutrient balls suggested several years had gone by.

After finishing my qi circulation, I looked at my reflection. Those once-murky eyes had become sharp, as if possessed by a demonic power.

The final parts of the mural were cut off, so I couldn't achieve complete mastery. Still, with its structure alone, I had surpassed my previous level and reached the Transcendent State. I had also gained four Gapja[^1] worth of inner qi; this seclusion was a blessing in disguise.

"Though I have inherited your martial arts without being able to offer the rites of a Master, I now offer the Nine Bows in gratitude. I will not forget your teachings and will devote myself to the righteous path."

I performed the Nine Bows, something I had never done for anyone. My father may have been the Chairman who led the entire Murim, but I was confident I would one day surpass him. The same went for the Divine Monk of Shaolin.

But I sensed I could never surpass this martial art's unknown creator, even if I trained for my entire life. So, I decided to serve the master of this art, which had reached me after hundreds of years, as my true Master.

_As one who walked the righteous path, my conviction will not change even were the heavens to fall. I, Yang Jagak, swear it._

"Hmph... It seems they have no intention of letting me out yet. Then I have no choice but to make my own path."

There was no reason to stay secluded any longer. In my current state, I wouldn't be easily defeated even were the Ten Grandmasters to come for me.

"HUUUUUP."

Standing where the wind seeped in, I circulated my inner qi for one full cycle; a powerful force spread through my body like an electric current before I gathered it in my hand.

**_WUUUUNG!_**

A domineering aura burst through my skin. I tapped the wall lightly with a palm strike.

**_KWAGWAGWANG!_**

The rock wall crumbled to dust, and harsh sunlight poured down on me.

I took a few steps, shielding my eyes with my sleeve until they adjusted. As I scanned my surroundings, a narrow path revealed itself, with bamboo growing neatly on both sides. Right in front of me, a man dozed.

I approached and gently tapped his shoulder. "Excuse me, may I ask you something?"

"Mmmph... Mmm... Huh? Wha... _Whaaaaaaah!_"

The man screamed and fell backward, his mouth gaping. _Don't tell me he can't speak. To think the first person I meet after so long is mute._

"Can't you... speak?" I frowned and muttered.

But then he prostrated himself flat on the ground. "I-I've committed a sin worthy of death! Please... _Please spare my life!_"

What was this reaction? He was begging me to spare him out of the blue. Did he mistake me for a mountain bandit? I suppose it was possible, since I hadn't washed or groomed myself in a long time.

"Calm down. Why would I harm you? I'm not that kind of person..."

"H-Hic... D-Don't tell me...! From the Cave of Myriad Demons...! _N-No, please just make it painless!_"

**_THUD! THUD!_** He started fervently banging his forehead on the ground. I was bewildered by his fickle preferences on the matter of his own survival, so I grabbed his shoulder to help him up.

"_GURGLE_."

He foamed at the mouth and fainted on the spot.

"What is this..."

He truly was meek. I shook my head, propped him up against a tree, and noted he had no intention of waking up.

"Hah... I apologize, but I've delayed for too long. I have urgent business, so I'll be on my way."

I left him behind and continued down the lone path. It was overgrown here and there, suggesting it was rarely used, but the bamboo forest formed a natural path that wasn't too difficult to follow.

The moment I left the forest and came out into the open, I couldn't believe my eyes.

"This place is...?!"

A magnificent mountain range rose up as if to surround the area. Ornate pavilions were decorated in hanging gardens. And on a huge, castle-like structure hung a sign.

"Shining... Light... Cult...?"

The name of that very place, hidden in the Tian Shan mountains of Xinjiang, was written there. A place said to be impossible to find, and from which one could never leave. The world called it...

…the Demonic Cult.

"Your Grace! We sincerely congratulate you on completing your isolated training!"

"We congratulate you!"

The warriors guarding the entrance exuded a powerful aura as they bowed and shouted to me— "Young Master of the Cult", "Young Lord", "Your Grace"… Given such words, the owner of this body must actually be...

"**_AAAAAAAH-!_**"

A scream erupted from my throat. I, who had never wavered, screamed with all my heart as tears poured down my face. How could such heavenly punishment befall me?

The Second Leader of the Demonic Cult—

But I'm the son of the Murim Alliance's Chairman—

Young Lord!?

"Wait..."

My tears stopped, and my body went rigid with goosebumps. Don't tell me—

The martial arts in that cave was...

It couldn't be. There was no way. But why else would such outstanding warriors be guarding a place like this?

"Your Grace, having emerged from your seclusion... has the Young Lord acquired complete mastery of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art?"

The air caught in my throat. My breath grew ragged.

Don't tell me... the one I swore to serve as my Master, the one to whom I offered my Nine Bows, was actually...

"The... Heavenly... Demon...!"

I am Yang Jagak, a renowned genius and the son of the Murim Alliance's Chairman, a pillar of the righteous murim! That is an undeniable fact! But somehow I have taken the _Heavenly Demon_, the founding ancestor of the Demonic Cult, as my Master, learned the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, and even taken over the body of the Cult's very _heir_.

What am I to do now?

There was no answer either. This time, my psyche couldn't take it. In times like these, there is but one recourse.

"_GURGLE._"

"Young Leader-!"

Those four Gapja of inner qi churned within me, and my mind went blank. To my embarrassment, I, who had reached the Transcendent stage, swooned while foaming at the mouth.

I have... reincarnated into the body of the heir to the Demonic Cult.
