I rode without restraint.

When was the last time I had ridden a horse so freely, without any restrictions? With the cool wind buffeting my entire body, and in my ears...

"_Ugyaaaaah!_"

...the shriek of a madman, presumably the Emperor.

From the way the hands gripping my waist flailed up and down, it was clear he wasn't used to riding; this is what happens when a novice rides a horse at high speed.

I immediately took my feet out of the stirrups and instructed him.

"Step on the stirrups and balance yourself with the movement. Think of squeezing with your lower body and relax your upper body."

Whether it was because he had a talent for it or because he sensed death, Zhu Qizhen quickly put his feet in the stirrups and began to find his balance.

_CLOP! CLOP! CLOP!_

As he stabilized, the Ferghana horse's gait gained momentum and became even faster.

However.

"Uh... uuuurgh!"

"What is it now?"

"...Behind! Behind!"

At his scream, I whipped my head around to see Zhu Qizhen, his mouth agape and pupils fully dilated.

_Get out of the way._

I shoved his face to the side and looked behind us.

«Did you think you could escape-! _Hyah!_»

A man had approached to within a hundred paces all by himself. Before the black horse he was riding, even the Red Hare of the God of War would not dare present its nameplate.

Solid muscles and a powerful spirit that could be felt even from a distance. An overwhelming size, large enough to carry four grown men.

Was that all? Atop it rode a giant of a man, spinning a greataxe in his grip. He closed the distance so quickly that it would not be an exaggeration to call him one with his horse.

_To think there was someone like that among the Oirats...!_

I looked forward again, gripping the reins and shaking them hard.

At this rate, it was clear we would be caught before long.

Looking into the distance, I could see lush trees and a mountain ridge to the northwest.

That must be the place Zhu Qizhen mentioned.

I made a decision.

"Grab them."

"What?"

"I said, grab them!"

I snatched Zhu Qizhen's hands and forced them onto the reins, then immediately leaped off the horse.

_CLOP, KA-CLOP!_

I executed my movement arts to match the speed of the galloping horse. If I faltered for even a moment, we would all be in danger.

After taking a couple of breaths.

HWIIK, CHEOK! I leaped back onto the saddle, sitting back-to-back with Zhu Qizhen.

"W-what was that! I asked what you were doing! Where did you go!"

_I'm right behind you._

_So stop chattering._

I observed the opponent, who had now closed the distance to within fifty paces. He had let go of the reins and was holding a massive horn bow horizontally like a crossbow, drawing the string.

"Nothing's wrong, right? Is that right? Tell me. Answer me quickly!"

_...Something's about to be._

PIIIING!

The string was released from his hand, and an arrow flew, tearing through the wind. The way it undulated as it fiercely charged forward made it feel like a giant venomous snake.

I pulled my body to the very edge of the horse, perching lightly, and circulated my qi.

_This is no ordinary attack. If I face it carelessly, I'll be pierced._

The qi that had surged up to my Baihui acupoint cascaded down like a waterfall, and the Heavenly Demon Divine Art began to open.

First stage, second stage... sixth stage!

A vicious heat spread through my body as I snatched the arrow that had, in an instant, arrived right before my nose. KWAK!

CHIIIIK-!

However, far from being incinerated, it actually started to slowly pierce through my grasp.

_Transcendent level? No, is he even stronger? To be this powerful even after I've opened the Heavenly Demon Divine Art to the sixth stage...!_

However, I had no intention of being defeated like this.

_Go on ahead._

HWIRIRIRIK!

Using the centripetal force, I spun three or four times and rose into the air. Black flames scattered beneath my feet, and I threw the arrow back at his approaching black horse, imbuing it with the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

SHWIIIIIIK!

The arrow shot back even faster than it had come. Just before its life was extinguished by the unquenchable flames, it plunged downward.

KWAAAAANG!

With a tremendous roar, a cloud of dust rose like an active volcano.

Then, something unbelievable happened.

_In that split second, he veered to the right. Is that even possible?_

Although he seemed to have stopped, hidden by the dust, it was clear he had dodged it.

They said that when the Yuan invaded the Central Plains, countless famous warriors and hidden masters, as numerous as the sands of the sea, lost their lives on the battlefield. I could not understand it when I heard it through oral tradition. However, now I understood.

_I want to see this through to the end, but..._

A competitive spirit arose in me, but now was not the time.

Looking back, I saw the Ferghana horse galloping into the distant forest as if its reins had been let loose.

I immediately unleashed the Heavenly Demon Sacred Flame Stride and gave chase.

Meanwhile, the Esen who was left behind simply stared intently at the spot where Ma Cheonso had disappeared until the dust settled and the Ultimate Flaming Horse Cavalry caught up.

«So there was still someone trying to save the abandoned emperor? Hmmm...»

A deep contemplation, unbefitting his massive frame, could be felt.

However, an abandoned emperor...

Esen turned his horse's head. In the distance, he could see hundreds of cavalrymen rushing toward them. Leading them was, of course, the general manager, Wa Nansu.

«Grand Preceptor—! Are you alright?»

«Things have gotten troublesome.»

Esen did not even bother to answer such an obvious question. Who was he? He was Esen, the Ultimate Fire Emperor, leader of the Oirats, Unifier of the Western Plains, and one of the Five Emperors of the Outer Regions.

«This servant will give chase.»

«No. If he went that way... he must have gone to _them_. We will go ahead and wait. Follow me. _Hyah!_»

«_Hyah!_»

_CLOP, KA-CLOP!_

Esen led his black horse and charged forward. The Ultimate Flaming Horse Cavalry sallied forth once more.

_The one who blocked the Ivory Horn Bow..._

Esen's mind began to fill with thoughts of a new figure, not the emperor.

"_Uwaaaaah!_ W-what am I supposed to do! Hey!"

The Ferghana horse galloped like a gale, quickly crossing the plains and reaching a verdant forest. Perhaps because it was a famous horse, it kept on galloping since no pull from the reins.

The dry, sandy ground gradually turned to yellow earth, and dozens of giant trees to the left and right flashed by in the blink of an eye.

Zhu Qizhen pressed on the horse's neck with trembling hands and shouted— but never forgetting to squeeze with his lower body.

"H-halt! We command you. I said stop! Now! _Now—!_"

However, as if there was no turning back in a stallion's life, the Ferghana horse kept on charging unhesitatingly through the forest. And it was so clever that it dodged anything it might have crashed into.

The problem was...

"A— a cliff...!"

The path that should have continued was cut off abruptly, leaving a gap of more than ten paces. It was a canyon, with the rough currents of the Chu River flowing at its bottom!

Even so, the horse ran on, unstoppable.

It was a risky situation.

"Uwaaaaaah!"

Zhu Qizhen's scream scattered in the wind.

And then—

**_SHWAAAAA!_**

The man in the black half-mask, who had been sprinting at an insane speed while emitting flames, grabbed the reins and climbed on from behind.

"Get a grip!"

Only a few steps away from the cliff, I barely managed to climb the saddle. A sideways glance revealed Zhu Qizhen in a panic, his lower body tensed up.

_He's signaling with his legs to run, so of course it won't stop!_

"Ah, is that you? Are you there? What in the world were you doing? Is it really you!?"

It's me, I'm here, I was fighting, and it's really me. He has way too many questions for a situation like this.

I gripped the reins and looked ahead over Zhu Qizhen's shoulder: there was a ravine as steep as a precipice. But at such a speed it was already too late to stop.

_Might as well..._

I pulled Zhu Qizhen's chest back, peeling him off the horse's neck. The horse, which had been hunched over, straightened its head and became even swifter.

In an instant, the cliff was right before our noses.

_Not yet— not yet— a little more— Now!_

At the very moment the horse's hind legs touched the last bit of ground, I let out a short shout and squeezed tightly with my lower body.

"_Eeeeeeeek!_"

_CLOP!_ _NEEEEEEEIGH!_

The Ferghana horse, which had seemed poised to fall, leaped from the cliff and soared across the air. My internal organs, which felt like they were about to drop, seemed to float up, and for a moment, the world felt like it had stopped.

"It's... flying?!"

Just as he'd said, it really flew. This creature could be called a Heavenly Horse[^1] without any reservation.

But it wasn't over. The horse had leaped bravely, but it was clearly not enough to reach the other side.

"I-isn't it falling short!?"

"If you don't want to die, hold on tight."

_TAP!_

I stepped on Zhu Qizhen's shoulder and leaped.

_CHAK!_ And as I landed first, I simultaneously turned and ran back to the incoming Ferghana horse.

_NEEEEIGH!_

The horse's forelegs precariously grazed the edge of the ground, and the moment it lost its balance and began to fall amid the crumbling rock—

—I slid down, clinging to the cliff, and caught the horse's hind legs with my bare hands as they scrabbled for a foothold.

A large shadow was cast over my head.

_CLAT!_

The horse leaped up again.

_WHOOOSH!_

As the shadow lifted, the sky began to recede. Unable to bear my own weight, I began to fall.

"Ah— _nooooo!_"

His shrill cry echoed through the canyon.

"I didn't allow you to die! You can't! No! Don't die!"

Is he that attached to me or something? Why is he screaming so mournfully?

And I'm sorry, but—

_BANG!_

My hand plunged deep into the cliff face. The receding sky now stared blankly back, as if it had always been in that spot.

_I'm not dying, actually._

_But if I die again, will I be reborn as the son of the head of the Evil Alliance this time?_

An absurd thought came to mind, and a chuckle escaped me.

Anyway... time to climb.

"Why? Why did you have to go like that? Did you throw your life away so lightly just to save me? For this worthless me, captured by mere barbarians? Why... why...?"

Zhu Qizhen, collapsed on the ground amid sorrowful mutters, looked painfully small and lonely.

"What in the world have I been doing with my life? It wasn't enough to allow the eunuchs' tyranny, but now I survive at the expense of a subject I was meant to protect... how am I any better than a beast!?"

Beyond sadness, he now began to debase himself. He was so filled with rage that his shouting voice was laced with lament.

_But..._

_Could you spare me a look?_

_I'm perfectly fine over here._

This is truly awkward.

Of course, the clueless Zhu Qizhen just kept rambling on regardless.

"I will never... repeat the same mistakes. I will live! O, nameless commoner! Resent Us! We will not let your noble sacrifice be in vain— _eeeeek!_"

"Why are you so surprised?"

Like he was looking at a ghost.

I left Zhu Qizhen, who'd fallen back on his ass, and approached the Ferghana horse, which was panting and collapsed on the ground.

It wasn't that I was ignoring him, I just did not have anything in particular to say.

"It must have been a difficult journey." I petted the horse and patted its rump. "Thank you. Now, go on your way."

The Ferghana horse slowly raised its heavy body and gradually disappeared into the forest.

"W-what in the world happened! You definitely fell to your death! A-are you a demon?"

_Being reborn as the Heir to the Demonic Cult is crazy enough. Now you're turning me into a demon?_

"Stop making a fuss and get up."

"A-are you really alive? How? Didn't you fall down there?"

_He calls himself an emperor, but what kind of emperor is so fidgety and has a heart smaller than a swallow's?_

"Be quiet and hurry up. We just have to get over there, right?"

I answered indifferently and walked on ahead, but at some point, his footsteps disappeared, and a faint mumble caught my ear.

"Thank goodness... What a relief. It's truly a relief."

When I turned back, the arrogant and presumptuous figure was nowhere to be seen, leaving only a kind young man with a face covered in tears.

He seems like an arrogant and self-righteous man who thinks he's the only one under the heavens, yet he is also terribly simple, straightforward, and naive.

_Did such a being ever exist?_

_Because he's the Emperor._

_And because he's the former emperor._

—that this is something only possible because he is who he is.
