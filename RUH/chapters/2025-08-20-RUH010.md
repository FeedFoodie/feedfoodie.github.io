_CLANG_. A bell tolled in my head.

_Undercover investigation._

My heart surged and my face twisted as my resolve stirred.

The senior's lips curled into an expression that said 'just as I expected'.

"I understand if it's—"

"I'll do it. The undercover investigation. Let's do it."

Though I may be in the body of a demonic cultivator, my essence hasn't changed. And an undercover investigation was the most righteous path I could take right now.

"...Do as you please, then."

The senior didn't ask again, simply raising an eyebrow in response.

"Will that be enough?"

At my question, he rolled his wheels toward one of the walls and said, "You said you wanted to build your influence, didn't you? With those who possess extraordinary talent but linger on the outskirts, unable to easily blend in."

"Yes, there are ten empty annexes left to fill in my Palace. That should be enough for new recruits, right?"

"Isn't it eleven annexes?"

To think he even knows the number of empty annexes in the Palace of Minor Light.

"One spot just found its owner."

"An annex in the Palace of Minor Light after being in this damp, shabby library... I get to enjoy all sorts of luxuries in my old age."

"Enough with the sarcasm. Ah, and keep this in mind. If there's someone who can never be changed... I will cut them down without hesitation."

"Are you planning on turning this place into one of the orthodox sects? If this is resentment toward your father, it would be a revenge of epic proportions."

"I have no such resentment. I just do what I want the way I want."

Senior Jaegal Tong seemed to give up on understanding, and simply let out a series of unpleasant, dry laughs before nodding.

"Heheheh. As you wish."

Then, he drew a circle on a portrait at the end of the wall. It depicted a man with a rather neat and resolute appearance.

"It will be difficult to find a suitable person within the Cult of Shining Light. However, he is more than qualified. This is Eul Taesik."

Eul Taesik. Even though it was a name I'd never heard before, it was deeply engraved in my mind.

Just as I was repeating his name over and over in my head—

"Eul Taesik? What? Who'd want anything with that wreck of a man? And what happened to the door?"

"Yayul Tak."

I turned my head to see Yayul Tak appear, carrying a large, sloshing water bucket.

"You know him?"

"Oof, this is heavy. Of course I know him. He use to be a big shot before his wife died, then he got hooked on drugs and completely ruined his life, I think."

At this, senior Jaegal Tong's eyes gleamed unusually as he agreed.

"That's right. A member of His Eminence's Honor Guard, belonging to the Palace of Shining Light, the former captain of the Shining Heavenly Demon Company, Eul Taesik. He's now a shadow of his former self, living in the Valley of Sorrow."

The Shining Heavenly Demon Company was the strongest martial force in the Demonic Cult.

Eul Taesik was the former Captain of that squad.

Since the Reader of Heavenly Fate said so, it must be certain. I found myself nodding.

"Yayul Tak."

"Yes, Young Cult Leader."

"Let's go back."

"Right now? Huh? What about this water!"

The next target was set.

{sep}

_Thump._ I closed the book and shut my eyes.

Four days had passed since I returned from the Shining Light Archives. The senior had decided to remain there until the Palace of Minor Light was more prepared.

I forbade myself from going out and diligently read the scriptures and ranking records I'd received from him, and finally, I had closed the last chapter.

"To save all living beings with fire and serve a benevolent god... Is this the foundation of the Cult of Shining Light? Its current state is so very different from that path."

Over time, the doctrines of the Demonic Cult had grown bloated by the comforts of leadership, and rotted from the sweetness of power. The fire meant to illuminate all living beings came to be used to burn indiscriminately, so the incarnation of fire became a demon of flames. Since it could no longer illuminate, the Cult of Shining Light became the Demonic Cult. The Three Great Edicts and Five Minor Edicts written in the scriptures were truly admirable ideologies, worthy of being called a code of law, but it was lamentable that they had become nothing more than archaic, meaningless texts.

"What made it this way?"

I questioned the past, but there was no one to answer. So, all I could do was change the future.

I opened the box placed before the bronze mirror. Despite being an artifact passed down from a distant past, it was as pristine as if it were new.

I picked up the mask and looked into the bronze mirror.

@[_Change the Demonic Cult, you? What a joke! You think that's possible? You're just the brutish Little Lecher!_]@

The pretty face of Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso seemed to approach me with a sneer.

In response, I defiantly covered my eyes with the palm holding the mask.

The black mask wrapped around my face as if coiling, then stretched out like a living spirit to drape a jet-black cloak over my shoulders.

The collar stood up sharply, and a pattern of flames was engraved on the back.

"It's me... but it's not me."

Just as Jaegal Tong had said, even though it was the same face I had seen just moments ago, it felt unfamiliar. I couldn't even begin to imagine what kind of face lay beneath the mask. This, too, must be an effect of the divine artifact.

I gathered my long hair, pulling it up to the crown of my head and tying it with a strap.

It looked quite plausible.

Now, no one will know I'm the Young Cult Leader unless I reveal it myself.

"The Hallowed Palaces and the Elders will find a way to approach me eventually. An opportunity must come up by then. And in the meantime..."

_Tap._

Just as I was steeling my resolve, my keen senses sounded an alarm. I heard someone approaching.

I hurriedly took off the mask.

_Srrrk._

"...It's you."

Seeing the reflection in the bronze mirror, it was her. The maid of the Palace of Minor Light.

Judging by her tightly pressed lips, it seemed she was embarrassed to have returned after her little escapade.

"You've come."

"...Why did you do that?"

What did I do?

Conversations with that maid always felt so strange. Looking at her made me feel both calm and tense, but once we started talking, it seemed like we were speaking past each other.

"Speak clearly."

"I'm talking about what you did at The Regular Meeting of the Scions. The rumors are all over the place."

"What of it? Instead of worrying about such rumors, you should focus on your duties."

"Duties...?"

"Yes. Your duties as a maid."

"_AAAGH!_"

My ears rang. How was she screaming so loud without even using sound arts?

I wondered how such an impudent maid had survived this long. She really should be praying day-in and day-out to thank all the gods of heaven and earth.

I was about to reason with her nicely when the senior's words suddenly came to mind.

_For now, keep as low a profile as possible._

There was nothing wrong with the senior's words. Even the most solidly built stone pagoda can crumble from a single stone wedged in a crack.

I steadied my breathing and stared intently into the bronze mirror.

A being that a mere maid would never dare to look upon. That's me.

"A mere maid like you... how daring of you to raise your voice before me. Do you wish to die?"

I hadn't even drawn upon the Heavenly Demon Shade, yet a dreadful demonic qi seemed to spread from my body. I glared at the maid, my eyes filled with killing intent.

And then.

"I came because I was worried, and this is how you act, you... you... crazy _jerk!_"

_SRRRK, SLAM!_

The maid vanished, leaving only an empty room.

Was it just my imagination, or was she getting worse by the day? I was truly worried for her future.

{sep}

_—The Cult of Shining Light, the Water Peak, the Seven Hallowed Palaces, the Palace of Merchants_

"Huhu... As expected of one of the Five Divine Treasures. To not have a single spot of rust after all these years! Just looking at the scabbard, they're truly worthy of being called divine blades."

The Lord of the Palace of Merchants, Cheon Semin, was lost in admiration as he held one of the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames, its black scabbard intricately engraved with a brilliant pattern.

Even after hundreds of years, the scabbard gleamed like new, making it more than worthy of being called a divine artifact.

However—

"If only it could be drawn..."

Ambition flared in his eyes, only to wither away just as quickly. It was just a pie in the sky, anyway.

What was a blade? It was a tool of death, made to cut others. But the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames couldn't be drawn from their scabbards even when the Cult Leader's grandfather himself made the attempt.

No matter how much inner qi was poured into them, they remained defiantly unresponsive. Thus, it had become a useless keepsake with an empty reputation, shoved away in a warehouse.

"Hoo, Birang."

"Yes, Palace Lord."

"With this, that brat, the Young Cult Leader, he'll come running with his mouth wide open. Right?"

"Yes. He is full of vanity, so he will surely fall for it."

"Hehehe. Yes, that's right. There's no way a mere childish brat could escape the judgment of me, the Lord of the Palace of Merchants."

Cheon Semin was full of confidence; the Little Lecher Ma Cheonso he knew would surely be foaming at the mouth with excitement!

"Good. Are the preparations to welcome the brat complete?"

"Yes, everything is finished."

"Well done. Prepare everything so there isn't a single error."

"Yes!"

As Birang answered resolutely, Cheon Semin once again stroked one of the Demonic Blade of Sacred Flames and muttered.

"Young Cult Leader. I am waiting, so hurry and come running. Then I shall personally wrap you around my little finger! Kuhahahaha!"

The Lord of the Palace of Merchants burst into a thunderous laugh.

But of course, no one knew when Ma Cheonso would actually arrive.

{sep}

_—The Water Peak, Outskirts of the Marketplace, the Valley of Sorrow._

A good distance away from the marketplace on Water Peak, a valley lay beneath a massive waterfall. People called this place the Valley of Sorrow.

It was a place where the lowest class resided, and it was named so because the stream flowing through the valley was said to be like their tears.

The types of people who came here were predictable. They had either dabbled in gambling and lost their fortunes, were too addicted to function, or had run out of brothels to frequent.

Or else, they had committed a grave crime and gone into hiding.

Other than these people, no one ever frequented this place. Even the Two Palaces of Light and Seven Hallowed Palaces paid it no mind, as they believed there was nothing more to gain from these people, and no way to make them repent.

A village of the forsaken. That was the Valley of Sorrow. Thanks to this, their daily lives consisted of holing up in shabby thatched huts, barely surviving from one day to the next.

Still, it was somehow bearable.

At least, until a few days ago.

"Mom, can't we go home? I'm dizzy."

"Shh! Don't say such things. A person of high status is coming. We have to endure it if we want to live. Grit your teeth and bear it. You can do it, right?"

"Yes..."

Ever since rumors began to circulate that a high-ranking noble would be visiting, the lives of the lay followers in the Valley of Sorrow began to change. They hastily removed weeds even in areas out of sight and put up a plaque with a welcoming message at the entrance. Once the village was reasonably clean, the village chief ordered them to prepare to greet the visitor in front of the waterfall at all times, since they didn't know when he would arrive.

For the ordinary lay followers who had no inner qi, enduring this sudden labor day and night was hell. Moreover, the midsummer sun was so intense that most of them were collapsing one after another.

"Who the hell is coming that they're making us do this?! Do they want us all to die so they can walk over our corpses?"

Complaints poured out from time to time.

"You there! Watch your mouth! Haven't you heard the rumor that the Young Cult Leader is coming?"

"What?! The Little Lecher... Mmph!"

The complaining lay follower flinched in shock and clamped a hand over his own mouth.

The Young Cult Leader was already notorious among the lay followers. The number of people who had died at his hands was not just one or two! It was said that whenever he passed by, it was funeral season. Meaning, many would die.

"He's— he's coming! He's here!"

"Everyone, get down! If you don't want to die, don't make eye contact and get down on the ground, now!"

As someone shouted loudly, everyone's gaze turned to the stream flowing beside the waterfall. A large ferry boat with a colorful parasol stuck in it came into view.

And as it drew closer, they saw a peerlessly beautiful man, draped in a black robe, sitting leisurely and lost in thought.

Large, sharp, murky eyes, a high-bridged nose, and long, jet-black hair.

He had appeared in the Valley of Sorrow.

The Little Lecher, Ma Cheonso.
