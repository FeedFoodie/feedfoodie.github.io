"What are you just standing there for? If you wish to kill me, this is your chance. Your very last, in fact. Ah, and send my regards to the rebels at your Palace."

"Ugh... you little...!"

The toad grasped tightly at the hilt of his sword, but then painfully let go. With a pinched expression, he said, "I... I beg your forgiveness."

"Forgiveness? What for?"

"For my... impertinence."

"Impertinence? You harm someone, and you have the gall to call it impertinence? An apology is not enough."

"But you're the one who used him as a shield—!"

_CLACK!_ I kicked my seat back and stood up, rebuking him in a heavy tone.

"A shield? Do you think all your evil deeds can be swept away by your father's authoritarian hand? You are sorely mistaken. For now, countless may kneel before you, but their grievances shall weave into a rope around your neck. And should you hold any doubt... _know that I, Ma Cheonso, will be the one to tie that rope._"

I slowly walked forward, emanating my accumulated inner qi with each solemn step, and Cheon Ahmu was so overwhelmed by my focused intent that his knees were hitting the floor before I even reached him.

"Y-your Grace, I... I have committed an unforgivable transgression! I beg you... my father must not learn of this..."

"Go. Inform the Lord of the Palace of Merchants that I, Ma Cheonso, will meet him soon."

"Hyeeek..." Cheon Ahmu's porcine eyes rolled in fear.

_The Seven Hallowed Palaces, and the Council of Elders. One by one, I'll be coming for you._

{sep}

The eavesdropping lay cultists were astonished. They had pressed their ears to the wall of Bright Spring Parlor's second floor, only to hear that the Young Cult Leader had confronted an executive of the Scions of the Shining Light Cult! On top of that, he had all but declared war on the Palace of Merchants, charging them with treason.

This was an unprecedented event. From now on, everyone had better pay close attention to the Heir's every move...

_That hypocrite... He's the one who's been abusing his father's authority all along. That despicable Little Lecher!_

But, of course, it was far too early to judge him.

{sep}

A few days later, on the Water Peak of the Cult of Shining Light...

A thunderbolt struck the Palace of Merchants.

Even after tossing a priceless celadon vase around, the wrath of Palace Lord Cheon Semin would not subside. The curled ends of his mustache seemed to intensify with his soaring temper.

"F-Father, I meant no harm to the Young Cult Leader! I just wanted to spook him a little..."

"You _idiot!_"

Prostrated flat on the floor, his second son Cheon Ahmu was trailing sweat all the way from his scalp.

For his Father, who always treated him like a treasure, to raise his voice like this... clearly this matter could not be brushed off.

"Do you truly not understand what you have done?!"

"B-But... Father, isn't the Young Cult Leader just a rotten brat nobody likes? I just don't get why you're so angry..."

"Shut up! However lowly and foolish he may be, he is still the Heir to the Cult. Do you still not understand what that means?"

"It's not my fault! I was told that his dantian was lost in a qi deviation, but... his presence felt like that of a stalking tiger..."

"How pathetic can you be...!"

Palace Lord Cheon Semin felt fit to burst with frustration. How he missed his eldest son, still off on a long escort mission!

Two years had passed since Cult Leader Ma Taepyeong vanished from the cult. Among the Seven Hallowed Palaces, all but the Palace of Sacred Flames and the Palace of Blades had agreed to elect a new Cult Leader, and a majority of the Council of Elders, too, had voted to replace him. A new sun was scheduled to rise when the Grand Festival of Heavenly Shining Light was next held.

Only for the Young Cult Leader to finish his secluded training.

_If we make an issue of the Cult Leader's absence, the Young Cult Leader might seize power. Why couldn't he just die in the Heavenly Demon Cave?_

Cheon Semin frowned thunderously. The current Heir was like a powder keg, and with the Cult Leader's seat vacant, he was next in line. Of course the brat wasn't being overlooked; he'd been bidding his time, hoping to figure out how best to handle the boy.

But then his second son went and kicked the hornet's nest.

"I can't even _stand_ the sight of you! Get your ass into the Cave of the Golden Sky, and don't you dare step out until I let you!"

"F-Father! That's too harsh a punishment! The Cave of the Golden Sky? You're telling me to go into secluded training right now...?"

"Silence! Did you really expect a slap on the wrist after such reckless actions?!"

Cheon Ahmu protested with tears in his eyes, but Cheon Semin was resolute; and so, after dismissing his son, Cheon Semin immediately summoned his most trusted subordinate, Captain Cheon Birang of the Golden Cavalry Squad.

"What do you think? Do you believe the Young Cult Leader seeks to test us by being outrageous?"

"He is well known for being erratic, Lord. It's a stretch to presume any ulterior motives. Besides, how would he know of our ongoing conflict with the Palace of Martial Blood?"

"That _is_ true... even so, that he subdued Ahmu so easily is suspicious in itself. To compare that dog to a tiger..."

"Respectfully, Young Lord Cheon Ahmu is easily intimidated; at his best, the Young Cult Leader could only barely reach the level of a first-rate martial artist. Even if he didn't actually suffer a qi-deviation, he is not an opponent you need to be wary of."

"Hmm..." Cheon Semin nodded at Birang's words, seeming to regain his composure.

The very notion of the Young Cult Leader mastering the Heavenly Demon Divine Art was blasphemous. After all, wasn't it a legendary martial art that only the Heavenly Demon himself was said to have mastered?

"Palace Lord. I believe this might actually be an opportunity," Birang said, confidently.

"An opportunity? Elaborate."

"In the current situation, where the Palaces are in a state of balance, the Young Cult Leader could tip the scales. Whether we influence him or eliminate him, a changed playing field will offer new possibilities."

"And that is my concern. In times like these, even a trumped-up charge of treason could spark war. Another palace might assassinate him and frame us for it!"

To begin with, Cheon Semin had no interest in getting back at Ma Cheonso. His sole concern was his own safety— should the Young Cult Leader die, they would inevitably be cornered and made a scapegoat.

"Therein lies the opportunity. The Young Cult Leader is easily swayed by emotions, and his words and actions are rash. I humbly suggest that we welcome him grandly, and prepare lavish gifts in order to earn his trust."

"Hmm..." Cheon Semin pretended to be lost in thought, but he was already nodding deeply. He had understood the true meaning behind the words.

"So we take His Grace under our wing and change the game?"

"Yes, Palace Lord. By shielding him from harm, we make of him an asset. If all goes well... our Palace of Merchants might even come to control the Cult."

At Birang's perilous suggestion, Cheon Semin slapped his knee and exclaimed, "I see! You mean for us to assume his regency, correct?"

"Precisely, my Lord."

"Young Leader... _Little_ Leader..." The corners of his mouth curled up beatifically as he made his decision. "Go to the secret vault and fetch me the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames."

"Th-The Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames, you say? But that's one of the Five Divine Treasures of the Cult..."

"They're worthless to us, are they not? To catch a big fish, you have to offer that much bait. Hurry and bring it— actually, I'll go myself."

Cheon Semin shot up from his seat. He was a born merchant. Once he made a decision, he was determined to make a solid investment. The Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames would be more than enough.

Thus the Palace of Merchants engaged into emergency preparations in order to protect the Young Cult Leader.

_—At the Palace of Minor Light on the Sun Peak of the Cult of Shining Light_

Even after finishing my breathing exercises, I kept my eyes closed and remained lost in meditation for a long time. My chambers were pleasantly silent. I didn't know how much of a commotion had erupted outside, but I figured it wouldn't be all that big.

_They'll probably just think of it as the antics of the reckless Young Cult Leader. Right now, they'll be busy weighing their options._

If I must live in the body of the Young Cult Leader anyway, the best I can do as a man of the righteous path is to personally take charge the Demonic Cult and ensure peace across the Central Plains. To that end, the Palace of Merchants was the perfect first target. To achieve anything great, one must be backed by wealth.

I will extract a hefty sum from them. The problem is...

_I lack numbers and knowledge. One cannot topple a great mountain alone. I need to find pure-hearted people unaffiliated with any other faction._

The only warrior belonging to the Palace of Minor Light was Yayul Tak.

_THUMP!_

Speaking of the devil, he burst through the door and stomped into the room.

"Young Lord, this is no time for a nap! You've made a huge mess!"

Yayul Tak's face was the picture of outrage. Studying him now, I concluded he was not only a coward but also a pessimist. The dark circles under his eyes suggested he had anxiously revisited yesterday's events through the night.

"It's not a nap, it's meditation."

As I unfolded my legs from the lotus position and stood up, Yayul Tak gestured wildly. "Since when do you that kinda stuff! Whatever it is, this is not the time— you need to go to the Palace of Merchants right now and tell them you were kidding. Hurry! Before we get in trouble!"

"Kidding? I was serious. Did it look like I was kidding?"

"It looked like being serious will get you locked up in the Heavenly Demon Cave again! His Lordship isn't around, why would you go around provoking the Seven Hallowed Palaces..."

"Because they hurt you? I cherish my people."

"But you're the one who made me—!"

"No need for thanks. More importantly..." I straightened my clothes while looking into the bronze mirror and addressing my reflection, "Can you get me a list of the Cult's elite ordered by influence?"

"A... a list? Do you mean the Cult's Order of Succession? I know where to get it, but... right now?"

"Yes, right now. I need to know who's available in order to fill up the annexes in this Palace."

"Huh?"

Finishing my preparations, I turned around and said to his blank face, "Quit stalling and lead the way."

"W-Wait a minute. Fill the what now?"

"The annexes. There are eleven empty ones alongside yours."

"No, I mean, why? Why all of a sudden! Did you eat something bad?"

"Why not? This is the Young Cult Leader's Palace, but it's all dull and desolate. You said you were bored, too. Won't it be nice to have company?"

"You're driving me nuts!! Wasn't it enough that you threw a gauntlet yesterday, now you're going to fill the Palace of Minor Light!? Are you trying to make a statement!? Do you wanna be seen as a threat!?"

I'm not trying to make a statement, I'm trying to make things right.

"I'd be flattered if I were."

"Ah... damn it. This is insane. Young Cult Leader, please. Don't take this lightly. They'll be laughing at that Cheon Ahmu for now, but as soon as you bring new retainers into this Palace, neither the Seven Hallowed Palaces not the Council of Elders will sit still. Do you understand how precarious our position is?"

Yayul Tak's agitation was completely understandable; he must be scared, concerned even. I did not think it wrong of him.

But he was out of line.

"Yayul Tak."

"Yes?"

"Who do you serve?"

"Is that important right now? We're about to get into huge trouble..."

"I'll ask again."

_GULP._ Yayul Tak's adam's apple bobbed. An unknown pressure seemed to halt his thoughts.

"Who do you serve."

"Well... I serve you, Young Cult Leader."

"Do you need any other reason to obey?"

"...No, sir." Yayul Tak blinked his wide eyes, lowered his head, and replied.

He was quick to adapt. He might be a follower of the demonic path, but there may be hope for his rehabilitation. A smirk played on my lips as I walked.

"Then lead the way."

"Damn it...!" Yayul Tak slapped his forehead and grumbled. "Then at least change your clothes. We can't go around advertising this to all and sundry, can we? Wear a black hat, too."

He opened a wardrobe and threw a black martial arts uniform, a cloak, and a black hat onto my bed.

"Are these dark clothes the only ones I have?"

"Yeah, you always liked black."

Seeing him grumble while still taking care of everything, I realized that, at the end of the day, he still thought of me as a friend.

I obediently changed as he suggested, and my reflection in the bronze mirror looked quite dashing. Of course, Yayul Tak just grimaced, coolly opened the door, and led the way.
