_— Shining Light Cult, Palace of Minor Light_

It was the night after the Bright Summer Parlor had barred its doors. The horizon was paling in the hour before dawn, a time when most should by all rights be asleep— yet the Palace of Minor Light was filled with vigorous shouts.

"Uaaaargh! Oooow! Son of a bitch!"

Yayul Tak clutched his shin and hopped around; before him stood the Blood Asura, holding a single tree branch.

"How do you expect to save your own life when you're this slow?" asked the Blood Asura. "Protecting the Young Cult Leader and Haryeong is out of question. _Wrist._"

The moment he finished speaking, the branch flew fiercely toward Yayul Tak's wrist.

Yayul Tak couldn't properly defend himself and just flinched, blocking with his shoulder. But the branch bent like a ghost and struck his wrist.

"Ack! No, I mean, why the hell do I have to protect them!"

"Head."

"Gah! You said head!"

"Does an assassin announce where he's going to strike?"

Days of this hellish training were driving Yayul Tak mad. To him, that man was not the Blood Asura but Yama himself, King of Hell!

It all started when the Blood Asura had called Yayul Tak over on a whim to teach him martial arts. Even the method was insane: nothing but a relentless beating.

"Father, have a late-night snack before you continue."

Eul Haryeong, ever so kind-hearted, came out from the kitchen with boiled potatoes. Blood Asura nodded, tossed the branch aside, and turned to her.

"Only his daughter is precious to him. Screw you! You're not special, everyone has a father—"

He did have one. Yayul Cheon, the pitiless Lord of Martial Blood.

Yayul Tak spat out a stream of curses and plopped onto the ground.

Eul Haryeong smiled pityingly and approached Yayul Tak with a potato skewer.

"Here, have this before resuming."

"Resuming? Resuming what! My freaking funeral? If so, at least bring a proper memorial meal. What's with this damn potato?"

Eul Haryeong chuckled and shoved the potato into his mouth, and he grumbled even as he munched it down.

"Just eat it and behave. It's for your health."

"My health's in shambles, you know? My whole body aches like hell. If you're so worried, give me a massage here."

As Yayul Tak turned and tapped his own shoulder, Eul Haryeong smacked the back of his head.

"Aaaack! What the fuck?"

"Grow up. What kind of man is so immature?"

"Screw you! I'm as serious as they come!"

"More like a serious nuisance. Anyway, just eat this. Where are you going, Father?"

She straightened up as she saw the Blood Asura heading for the south gate with a small bag.

"I'm dropping by the blacksmith. Yayul Tak, you focus on your training."

"You crazy bastard! I can barely even hold this skewer, what are you going to buy? Don't buy anything! I said _don't buy anything!_"

_CLANG!_

The door slammed shut heartlessly, and Blood Asura stepped outside.

Yayul Tak collapsed onto the floor.

"Your Reverence," he blubbered. "This worthless servant will follow you to the Valley of Sorrow or wherever the heck, so please just come back already. Come back... I'm telling you to come back... come back...! You... Heavenly Demon's Thingamajig! _Sob_."

Eul Haryeong clicked her tongue and bit a potato as she watched him squirm on the floor.

Fifteen minutes idly went by, and then dark figures flew over the wall with a flurry of thuds.

...leaving me all alone. Were they that confident?

I downed the last cup of wine and approached the large ice pillar.

_Blood Asura's Blood Heaven Asura Art reacted to my Heavenly Demon Divine Art. If that's the case, then with these ice arts..._

The Heavenly Demon Divine Art was the foundation of all demonic arts.

I slowly placed my palm on the ice wall. A frigid qi touched my fingertips.

_WHOOM—!_

As I cycled the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, heat pushed back the cold, slowly melting the ice.

_I pray your heavenly fortune has not run out._

_DRIP, DRIP._

Water droplets fell, soaking the floor around us.

_THUD._ The frozen man collapsed, freed at last.

I forced the man into a sitting position and infused qi into his back. Hot yang energy surged through him, pumping through his heart; a moment later, I felt a faint pulse. It seemed his heavenly fortune had not yet run out.

"Kugh..."

I covered his mouth and sent a telepathic message as I looked into his wide eyes.

[If you don't want to die, hide yourself as best you can and then leave this place. If you understand me, just nod quietly.]

_Nod, nod._ He struggled to nod repeatedly.

It was done.

I looked at the stairs and quietly got to my feet.

Another name for the Bright Summer Parlor was the Palace of Bliss. To be precise, the first five floors were the Parlor, and from the sixth floor up was the Palace.

And I had finally reached the sixth floor.

Unlike the wide-open space below, it was a narrow corridor, its walls painted pure white and undecorated; straight ahead there was a large iron door carved with a giant snake.

_CREEEAAAK_.

I pushed the door open without hesitation.

And then—

"Welcome, Your Reverence..."

In the spacious area, white folding screens were spread out like a maze, and several beauties stepped out to welcome me.

_What kind of trick is this?_

As I scanned my surroundings, more women stepped lightly from behind the screens; a cloyingly sweet fragrance and a hazy mist began to fill the room.

"Your Reverence... come play with us..."

"Come on... over here..."

The women surrounded me, murmuring over each other.

_Swish, swish._

They undid their clothes one by one, revealing their soft skin.

_Twang, twalaring~_

A dreamlike melody began to flow.

A subtle yin energy emanated from the women around me, and I was undoubtedly its target. My focus blurred, my mind grew hazy; before I knew it, their delicate hands were guiding my steps.

_The Chant of Euphoric Spirits?_

It was said that the Shining Light Cult possessed a dreadful demonic art that subdued the will and suppressed the soul. My consciousness remained, but my thoughts were dulled— this was undoubtedly the Chant of Euphoric Spirits.

WHOOOM!

I released the Heavenly Demon Divine Art to the first stage, and my mind cleared instantly, as if a bubble had popped.

"Why did you take so long to come back?"

"Please come along..."

Once again, the women's voices sounded clear and distinct.

I glanced around and spotted a massive bed, wide enough for dozens of people.

"Didn't you miss us?"

"It's been so long, Your Reverence."

The women led me to the bed without any hesitation.

But...

The Bright Summer Parlor was only open to guests up to the fifth floor, yet these women spoke as if I had been here often.

Questions remained, but I set aside my distracting thoughts. The women were still giggling and crowding me relentlessly.

_There are twelve women around me, and... one playing from behind that screen. She is the core of the Chant of Euphoric Spirits._

I shook my head, pushed the women away, and said firmly, "All of you, move aside."

Damn. They weren't listening.

The women blocked all sides and rushed at me even more boldly. Their lips smiled, but their eyes were incredibly cold; the yin energy pouring from their bodies intensified.

_If you're cold, put your clothes back on!_

Should I break through the roof? Or just kill them? This is insane. Maybe I should just... no, I can't do that.

Maybe... the Chant of Euphoric Spirits is so powerful that the third stage isn't enough to work?

And then—

"_Yaaaaaaan~!_"

"_Aaaaaah!_"

"_Hnghhhhh!_"

_— Bright Summer Parlor, top floor_

_Slither, slide._

Han Soso caressed the snake climbing up her body. It was a pure white snake, a little over six feet long and thinner than her wrist.

One might think it a little cute, but it was a spirit beast— harboring a venon stronger than Bone Dissolving Acid in its fangs.

"Siwol," she said, as if waking from a reverie.

"Yes, Palace Lady," Siwol answered from beyond the door.

Han Soso tilted her head. "What did you make of him?"

The subject was omitted, but Siwol knew at once that she spoke of the Young Cult Leader.

"Hasn't he always been unpredictable?" she replied.

In Siwol's memories, the Young Cult Leader had always been difficult to understand, both then and now. He was called a scoundrel and dubbed the Little Lecher, but that was just the tip of the iceberg.

He was truly a man of many facets. The Ma Cheonso that Siwol remembered was like that, and it was the same for Han Soso.

"I suppose so... it's been a long time, after all. But it's strange."

"How so, Palace Lady?"

"Why did he act as if it were his first visit?"

Siwol silently replayed her memories. He hadn't said anything of the sort, but if Han Soso felt that way, then it must be true. Siwol silently waited for her next words.

"Perhaps His Eminence did something when he took him to the Heavenly Demon Cave..."

His Eminence the Cult Leader, Ma Taepyeong!

The words 'What do you mean?' nearly leaped from Siwol's mouth. Mentioning the Cult Leader was an absolute taboo, an unwritten rule in the Shining Light Cult. So why bring him up now?

But if Siwol did not know, then she _should_ not know. Siwol calmed her heart and remained silent as always.

"Then again, what does it matter?" the Palace Lady continued. "The Young Cult Leader has returned. We must do our utmost to ensure he can live as he did before."

"Yuwol has gone in, so it shouldn't take long," said Siwol.

"Yuwol did? That's good. They must be so happy to meet again at last."

"Yes."

"And the Palace of Minor Light?"

"There should be news soon."

"Is that so? Oh dear... what a shame. I understand. You may leave now."

"Yes, Palace Lady."

Siwol retreated with light, backward steps.

After she disappeared, Han Soso smiled elegantly and muttered to herself.

"What to do? You've only just escaped, but it can't be helped. It was your fate since birth... I hope you at least enjoy this little gift of mine."

She lightly covered her lips with her delicate fingers, suppressing a laugh.
