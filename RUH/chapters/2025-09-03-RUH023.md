You were the most depressing one.

True. There's also me, who reincarnated from the orthodox sects into the Demonic Cult: the Alliance Chairman's son who became the Cult Leader's son.

And yet your martial arts are... lacking.

I didn't want to hear any more.

Please stop talking.

Yeah, no.

If any more nonsense came out of that mouth, it would be war.

It's a bit awkward to say this because it feels kinda like I'm bragging about my family, but while the Palace of Martial Blood might not have much reach in the Central Plains, it has lots of connections in the Outer Regions."

If it's awkward to say, then shut up.

"Now."

_—One hour after the three were sent out_

I sat at the desk and gently closed my eyes. With only the chirping of birds from outside the window filling the quiet room, I felt a sense of peace for the first time in a while.

_Cheon Semin..._

I leaned back and once again recalled what I knew about Cheon Semin, the Lord of Merchants.

The first thing that came to mind was the Shining Light Cult ranking list compiled by the Rogues' Guild.

@[《The Shining Light Cult Ranking List》]@

I had thought that the Palace of Merchants and the Palace of Bliss were the first places I needed to visit among the Seven Hallowed Palaces.

It wasn't just for the sake of information.

To move an organization, funds and transportation are indispensable elements. That's why the Murim Alliance had not only sects but also numerous banks, escort agencies, and trading companies.

Furthermore, they also had close relationships with numerous guesthouses and inns to stay at during business trips.

However, in the Demonic Cult, the Palace of Merchants and the Palace of Bliss monopolized all these roles.

_The problem is that the only card I hold right now is the title of Young Cult Leader._

The basis of any deal is who wants what the other person has more.

For him to want me over the Lord of Martial Blood, who is the top contender for the seat of Cult Leader, means that Cheon Semin has other ambitions.

However...

_Since I have no intention of giving anything up, it's a deal that can't be made in the first place. I might be able to gain a lot easily for now, but it won't last long._

_First... I need to completely isolate the Palace of Merchants._

The events to come flashed through my mind like a revolving lantern. I was lost in thought, tapping on the desk.

_CREAK._

"Your Reverence. It is Captain Cheon Birang. The Lord has finished his business and requests an audience. Is that acceptable?"

He's here.

My eyes snapped open at Cheon Birang's neat voice, and I observed him.

"Did you get younger in the meantime? Your face looks very different from the one I saw yesterday."

The extremely ordinary middle-aged face had transformed into that of a handsome young lord.

"I have unintentionally committed a discourtesy. Please forgive me."

So it was a human skin mask after all.

If he was the Captain of the Golden Cavalry Company, his portrait would surely have been hanging in the Shining Light Library. Then there's no way I wouldn't have recognized him.

I smiled nonchalantly and said, "I recall telling you that I wouldn't forgive you for deceiving me again, didn't I?"

"...I apologize. The situation was not favorable."

Seeing him speak so evasively, I wiped the smile from my face and said in a low tone, "So you were looking down on me."

"What do you mean...?"

"My words must have sounded very light to you. That's how little you respect me. Am I wrong?"

"That's not it!"

"The son committed a crime, but the father said he'd make amends, and even sent me a Ferghana horse— that's why I saw fit to personally confirm his sincerity. Tsk tsk, what a waste of a trip."

"Young Cult Leader!" a rough shout escaped him.

"What? Are you going to kill me or something?"

"This lowly one will bow my head and apologize. Please let go of your anger."

Cheon Birang bowed his head with a desperate look on his face. What was the reason for trying to keep me here like this?

"Is that your real face?"

"This is my true appearance."

"I don't believe you. How can I trust what lies underneath? Even if I meet the Lord now, what more can I believe? I'm going back to my Palace."

It was as if a thunderbolt had struck from a clear sky.

_THUD._ Cheon Birang even dropped to his knees and cried out mournfully.

"Please forgive my rudeness and reconsider!"

My eyebrow twitched up and down. For him to go this far, there must be a reason.

I pushed my chair back and stood up. As I walked past him indifferently, he cried out again.

"...I will ensure this never happens again!"

"You've deceived me twice, why wouldn't you do it a third time? Tell the Lord that I won't be seeing him again."

"Please... please reconsider. I swear this will never happen again."

"How can I trust you?"

"What must I do for you to trust me?"

Desperation flickered in his eyes.

The Golden Cavalry Commander, Cheon Birang...

Normally, he wouldn't be the type to reveal his inner thoughts so easily. But the reason he's giving in so easily now is probably because I'm the unpredictable brigand of a Young Cult Leader.

Well then, let's go even crazier.

I gave a triumphant smile and said in passing, "Then... how about you bet your Shining Light Identity Token?"

Shining Light Identity Token. It's an identification tag of the Demonic Cult, issued not by the imperial family, but by the Palace of Shining Light.

When I first met Yayul Tak, he had said, _Young Lord! What were you thinking, carelessly tossing your token and going into seclusion? Do you know how much trouble I went through to keep this safe... Young Lord?_

A round token made of gleaming white platinum. Seeing 'Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso' written on it, I had fainted once more.

Anyway, the Demonic Cult's token had a special meaning. It was treasured as much as life itself because it was the only way for the followers scattered everywhere to identify one another.

"...I will do so."

I like that you're decisive.

_STEP, STEP._

I tossed a blank sheet of paper from the desk to him and said, "Then write."

"What do you mean?"

"Who knows if you'll change your story later. So take this down."

"...Understood."

"_I, Cheon Birang, the Golden Cavalry Commander of the Palace of Merchants..._"

_SWISH, SWISH._ He rolled up his sleeves, picked up a brush, and began to write quickly.

"_...hereby swear to..._"

His expression gradually crumbled.

"...are you trying to make me swear to protect you?"

No. What's the point of that? It's useless after I'm dead. Even if I live, this is the mortal world. So, it's the opposite.

"_...bring down the Palace of Minor Light and His Reverence the Young Cult Leader._"

_CLATTER!_ Was it because his heart exploded in shock as if struck by lightning? The character he was writing shot up, leaving a thin line.

"That's treasonous talk!"

"Why is that?"

"How many people do you think are trying to harm the Young Cult Leader? We are only trying to protect you from them..."

"So the young lord tried to kill me, and the captain hid his face to spy on me? In my eyes, you are the greatest threat."

"Why..."

"So if that's not the case, you should just write these words. If they turn out to be true, then I can at least avenge my grudge in death."

The Golden Cavalry Commander's eyes scrutinized me relentlessly.

He must be conflicted. Was this the childish request of an ignorant Young Cult Leader, or was he pulling some other trick?

"Forget it. If this place is so dangerous, I shouldn't have come to begin with. I'd rather visit the Lady of Bliss."

As I clasped my hands behind my back and began to walk away leisurely, he scowled and hurriedly moved his brush.

"I've written it all. Is this sufficient?"

_Pfft._ I peeked over and scanned the paper.

"Well written. _As proof, this will be buried in the Palace of Minor Light along with my Shining Light Identity Token, and if I break this promise..._"

Just as his trembling brush tip was about to fill the paper, I uttered the final words.

"_...I am an apostate._"

Cheon Birang's face finally twisted into a demonic grimace. His panting breaths were incredibly rough.

However, without a shred of mercy, I had him stamp it with his palm print, then carefully folded it with his Shining Light Identity Token and tucked it into my robes.

I didn't forget to give him a cynical smile either.

"Looking at you now, you make a better impression than yesterday. You look quite reliable."

"...I will escort you."

For him to agree to such a ridiculous thing, it had to be one of two reasons. Either he was confident, or he was looking down on the Palace of Minor Light.

_Cheon Birang. You will have to protect the Palace of Minor Light no matter what._

A deep smile was etched into my soul.

_CREAK._

The door opened, and as I stepped inside, the Hidebound Miser Demon, Cheon Semin, greeted me from his seat at the desk. Whether from many worries or a lack of sleep, dark lines were drawn under his eyes.

Behind him, the shelves were filled with untitled books. They seemed to be the ledgers managed by the Palace of Merchants.

I observed him without any particular interest.

He had a stout build and wrinkles full around his eyes, suggesting he was a man who laughed often. Though he was a Lord of the Demonic Cult, at a glance, he had a benevolent impression, like a Taoist from Wudang.

"Did you rest well? Hoho. This Lord postponed our meeting to ensure you had time to rest. I trust you are well?"

He stroked his mustache, which curled up as if in a sneer, and smiled slyly.

What kind of talk was that, 'postponing to ensure'? Did he not know the meaning of his words, or was he underestimating me?

It was definitely the latter. On second glance, that mustache looked insolent.

"Somewhat. It's understandable, since you've been preparing for my Palace's visit for so long."

"Haha, of course. I intended to send a gift as soon as I heard the Young Cult Leader had emerged, but I was simply too busy with my duties to visit sooner."

"I wasn't in the Heavenly Demon Cave for just one day or two. I don't think I can settle for some trifle."

"Haha, what's so difficult about settling for trifles? Just make up for it elsewhere."

"Well. I've lived my life without knowing the meaning of _settling for trifles_."

"Everyone is unfamiliar with things they haven't tried. Would it be as difficult a second time? Just take it one step at a time."

It's like thirty giant snakes are coiled up here.

After the light exchange, I nodded and swept my gaze around the room.

Large chests filled with silver and gold ingots, decorated treasured swords and porcelain— the gold and treasures beckoned as if to tempt me. They say the Palace of Merchants holds a third of the Demonic Cult's wealth, and it seems that wasn't an exaggeration.

"It seems Your Reverence has many unmet needs. Anything you wish for, I can provide. Haha!"

"It seems I've been misjudging the Lord of Merchants all along. If I had known you were such a magnanimous soul, I would have visited more often."

"If you wish, you may stay here permanently," the Lord of Merchants replied gravely, stroking his mustache.

Now he's being completely blatant.

However... wasn't there something we needed to sort out between us first?

"It seems the Lord of Merchants has lived a very happy life."

_Pfft._ I raised the corner of my mouth slightly and nonchalantly approached, perching myself diagonally on his desk.

"What is the meaning of that?"

I glanced at him as his eyelids shot up at my irrelevant answer, then gave another cynical smile.

"_A son is always beautiful in the father's eyes_. They say a parent's love is so great they are blind to their child's sins. Since the Lord has a son he cherishes so much, how could he possibly be unhappy?"

I casually continued speaking as I flipped through a book on his desk.

"But, Lord of Merchants..."

"..."

Cheon Semin didn't answer. Instead, the corners of his raised mouth seemed to have lost their strength, flattening out and revealing his discomfort.

_Now_ you look like a demonic lord. Smile. It's not over yet.

"Isn't it ignorance to be blind to _your_ own sins? It would have barely sufficed if you'd rushed out barefoot and begged for my forgiveness, yet you had your subordinate confine me to some guest room and now you greet me from above while on the seat of honor... is this not a display of treasonous intent?"

"Your Reverence!"

"Or might it be that you've lived such a happy life that you hold no fear of death?"

My cynical smile deepened.

A quick glance at him revealed his lower jaw trembling. His clenched fist looked ready to strike at any moment.

However, I was composed.

This was a battle of wills. A struggle between the mad Heir, Ma Cheonso, and the Lord, Cheon Semin, for superiority in their joint venture.

Just as we had exchanged six or seven breaths while staring into each other's eyes, his imposing aura vanished as if washed away. The corners of his mouth lifted once more.

"Haha! As expected of Your Reverence! Seeing you so grown up, the future of the Shining Light Cult looks bright. For you to cover up for the foolish mistake of my immature son, the Cult Leader would have been truly pleased if he were here... Now! Please, sit here."

He rose from his seat, gave a silent bow, and stepped aside, politely gesturing to his own chair.

It was his way of saying he would show me respect, so I should drop the matter with Cheon Ahmu.

I had no intention of dragging it out in the first place. Even if we took it to the end, who would side with the Palace of Minor Light?

However, a wrong was a wrong.

Since he wanted this so badly, I wouldn't refuse.

I immediately stepped onto the desk and walked right across its top.
