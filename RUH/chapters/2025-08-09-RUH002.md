In the Palace of Blades, one of the Seven Hallowed Palaces of the Shining Light Cult, was a lavishly decorated chamber. Curtains of vibrant silk fluttered by the window, and the bed was carved with intricate floral patterns. This was the bedchamber of the Frost Flower, Dan Yehwa.

She was the second child of Dan Jeonghak, Lord of the Palace of Blades; she was also one of the Three Flowers, reputed to be among the three most beautiful women in both the Orthodox and Demonic factions. And yet her face, as fair as white jade, was marred by a frown.

The blame lay on the news that a certain long forgotten someone had suddenly returned.

"Why in the world is he back? He should have just died in there."

"Oh, Young Lady. Don't even say such dreadful things. What will you do if someone hears you?"

"Who else is here but you and me, Nanny? Who's going to hear? And so what if they do? Am I wrong? I really wish he'd died."

The nanny, flustered by her words, anxiously scanned their surroundings, checking the door repeatedly, opening and closing it several times to make sure it was properly shut. Only then did she return to speak.

"Still, he is your fiancé! You shouldn't say such things. It's a relief that the Young Cult Leader has returned. I was worried the Young Lady might live alone for the rest of her life..."

"_Nanny!_"

"Oh my, my words were too harsh. Hoho."

Were it anyone else, Yehwa would have had their limbs torn off on the spot; but instead she just pouted. That was just how close she and her nanny were.

It was understandable. Having grown up motherless under a father who knew nothing but martial arts training, the nanny was the only real family she had.

"The Heavenly Demon Divine Art, my foot... I had no expectations from the start. Not when he went in spouting boasts about something even His Eminence couldn't master. I heard this time he wailed his heart out, frothed at the mouth, and fainted after failing to achieve mastery."

"How heartbroken he must have been when things didn't go as planned. Others may not, but the Young Lady should understand."

"Why should I! That bastard can't do anything. He only relies on his pathetic title as His Eminence's Heir while lusting after women..."

Dan Yehwa trembled, unable to speak properly as memories of the past flooded her mind. Ma Cheonso, the Young Cult Leader of the Shining Light Cult, had quite a reputation. Naturally, it was not a good one.

Most people called him the Little Lecher.

He simply coveted women _that_ much. Whenever thwarted, he would use his status to commit acts worse than a demon's. Of course, those with a strong inclination toward tyranny supported him, but the Seven Hallowed Palaces openly ignored him. Dan Yehwa was no different.

"But he's gorgeous, isn't he? Hoho."

Dan Yehwa was rendered momentarily speechless by her nanny's enraptured tone.

Although their engagement had been arranged by His Eminence the Cult Leader and her father, there was a time in her childhood when she, too, had been captivated by his looks. But that illusion had been shattered upon their very first conversation.

_You are the Young Cult Leader, aren't you? This humble girl is Dan Yehwa._

_You're my fiancée? You're passable, I guess. Come here and massage my feet nice and good. I'll do you too. Let's see if we're compatible._

That was the conversation they'd had when he was just fifteen and she, fourteen. At the time, Dan Yehwa was so shocked that she'd run back to the Palace of Blades, crying her eyes out. Fortunately, a year after that, just as his infamy was at its peak, the Young Cult Leader had entered the Heavenly Demon Cave.

Everyone expected him to come out as a corpse. Was the Heavenly Demon Divine Art a joke to him? Dozens from the Ma clan had died trying to learn it.

Still, not a single person tried to stop him. After all, if the Heir was gone, the next Cult Leader would have to come from one of the Seven Hallowed Palaces. Who would stop him when he was willingly getting himself out of the way?

Yet here he was, back alive after three years. Thanks to that, Dan Yehwa was the only one left in a state of anxiety.

"...I can't with that bastard. I'd rather die than marry him. No, I won't do it!"

"Sigh... Young Lady, still, why don't you visit the Palace of Minor Light just once? Who knows, after three whole years, he might have changed."

"_Nanny!_"

"Oh my, oh my. I understand."

As Dan Yehwa shrieked, the nanny left with some laundry. Now on her own, Dan Yehwa turned her gaze out the window, her fair brow deeply furrowed.

I was in the Palace of Minor Light of the Cult of Shining Light, and it had been three days since I'd regained consciousness. I'd been wasting time, staring blankly into the spacious and lavishly decorated chambers. Few people had bothered to visit in that time, but I had still managed to gather some information.

First, the owner of this body, the Young Cult Leader Ma Cheonso, had a dubious reputation. No, by my standards, he was utterly reprehensible at every level. A scoundrel. Yes, that was the most fitting word.

Second, Ma Cheonso's father was the Demonic Sage, Ma Taepyeong-- the Leader of the Demonic Cult and one of the Three Supremes-- martial artists who are ranked above even the Ten Grandmasters.

"Should I be happy to have the two greatest masters who divided the world as my fathers? Hahaha..."

It wasn't a genuine laugh.

Third, despite being the Cult Leader's only son and holding the positions of Young Cult Leader and Heir, those titles were completely meaningless. Whether Ma Cheonso had no interest or was simply ignorant, he had lived his life without a shred of effort.

He had been taught advanced martial arts, but mastered none. His social skills were abysmal, and the only asset he held was this Palace of Minor Light. As I overheard the conversation of some passing maids, a sigh escaped my lips.

_Isn't the Young Cult Leader a kite with its string cut now? Since he failed to learn the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, His Eminence won't let him be, even once he returns._

_Honestly, it's only right that Lord Yayul Jin leads our Cult in the future. It's poetic justice._

_Lower your voice. Someone might hear._

The person in question did hear! But I had no intention of scolding them for it. This Ma Cheonso fellow deserved the insult.

"A kite with its string cut..."

It seemed Ma Cheonso's life would have been short, even if he hadn't suffered that qi deviation.

Anyway, the fourth point, and a comforting one, was that this body was now nineteen. Less than three years had passed since the day I died. If a hundred years had passed, or if I had gone back a hundred years, I might have spent my entire life in tears, overwhelmed by longing.

"Then again, in this body, who would welcome me back at the Murim Alliance? I'd be lucky to even keep my life."

Was it because I hadn't eaten anything, or because I had been repeating the cycle of laughing and crying like a madman? Either way, I felt as if my emotions had dried up. I couldn't even muster up any anger.

"Hoo... It's not like me to agonize over a foregone conclusion. For now... I must accept this reality."

Once I made that decision in my heart, I felt much lighter. I immediately walked over to the bronze mirror and stared at my face. Skin as pale as ash. Large, sharp eyes. A high-bridged nose. A sharp jawline. Lips as red as flames.

As expected, I just couldn't get used to it, no matter how much I stared. Just this long hair was more beautiful than that of most women...

"Ma Cheonso. However you may have lived, from now on, I will live by my standards as Yang Jagak!"

In response to my muttering, the Ma Cheonso in the mirror seemed to mock me. Not wanting to lose, I sharpened my aura even more.

Just then.

**_SRRRK_**.

The paper door slid open, and the graceful figure of a woman revealed itself. She was so beautiful, she seemed even more lovely than the daughters of the Namgung Clan! If Namgung Yena had possessed a neat and pure beauty, then the woman who had just entered was like a splendid, cold flower.

"Who might you be?"

I stared at her for a while before suddenly coming to my senses and asking that question, only for her to shoot it back.

"Who might I... _be?_"

Habits are frightening for a reason! I should avoid formal speech, given my new identity. I immediately switched to an informal register.

"Who're you?"

She replied with a sullen expression.

"Have you... already forgotten my face?"

Ah. If she could enter the Young Lord's room, we surely weren't strangers. That was shortsighted of me. I quickly scanned her from top to bottom, my mind racing.

Simple clothes and flat, practical shoes, easy to move in. A young woman who could come and go from the Palace of Minor Light at this hour. Of course!

This woman is... a _maid_.

"No, my mind was... elsewhere. I'll let you do your work."

"...What is _that_ supposed to mean?"

Her question was laced with sharp thorns. It was understandable. Who would respect a Young Cult Leader who was a kite with a cut string, an object of public outrage? But I couldn't be flustered by this. _I am Ma Cheonso. I have to strike back. Stronger, more tyrannically._

"A mere maid talking back at me? Your lifeline sure must be tough. Or are you just dumb? Whatever it is, you're out of line. If you're a maid, act like it. Clean up immediately and get lost. That's your job."

That's it... That was enough.

"Did you just call me... a mere _maid?_"

I couldn't help but feel sorry. Discrimination against service workers was unrighteous! However...

"Am I wrong? As your employer, it is only right for me to call you by your designated role. So you really have no grounds to protest--"

I couldn't finish my sentence. Tears were welling up in her large eyes. Her face had even turned bright red from how indignant she was.

Was I... too harsh? A wave of bewilderment washed over me. And at that moment...

**_SRRRK, SLAM!_**

She slammed the door hard enough to crack it and stormed out. For a while, I could only stare blankly at the door panel. And then I realized.

"Even the maids of the Demonic Cult... are tyrannical."

This place truly is the final destination of the demonic path.

"And I'm supposed to marry a thug like that? No way! I'd rather die. Should I just shave my head and become a nun? Ahh..."

Having come outside, Dan Yehwa collapsed to the ground, her face a mask of tears.

"A maid? Me? Ha. To think I came here just to see this..."

Tears poured down as she wondered what sins she had committed in a past life.

Why was the murim called the murim? It was a place where even those born as women were respected so long as they had skill and talent. But to be called a maid... how was that any different from those girls sold off to farming families for a dowry?

Soaked in lament, she looked around herself and saw only the overgrown weeds of a place where foot traffic had long ceased.

Character, power, skill-- He was a man who had none of those things. She'd come bearing a sliver of hope, but it was dashed just as she'd expected. When she looked back, the sign for the Palace of Minor Light entered her vision like a curse.

"I'd rather..."

A venomous look filled Dan Yehwa's eyes. She wiped away her tears and hurried away.

{sep}

The maid was gone, and I was once again left to my own devices. Hunger washed over me. The human body is truly cunning-- my feelings of indignation and anger had just vanished like a spring breeze. Only appetite remained.

"Maybe I shouldn't have pushed her away so quickly."

I kind of regretted sending her away on such terms. I should have at least asked her to bring me lunch. A needless regret filled me.

"Get a grip. Right now, finding out more about this place is your priority."

I'd already wasted three days; I needed to get my bearings for the sake of survival.

**_SRRRK._**

I opened the door at once, crossed the empty hallway, and entered the garden. The area around me was enclosed by wide walls; within them were a southern gate, twelve annexes, and the main building where I was staying. Without hesitation, I headed for the nearest annex.

"You there, are you in?"

After I made my presence known at the door, a man with a disheveled appearance emerged a moment later. He looked as if he had just woken up despite the sun being high in the sky.

"Young Cult Leader! What brings you here at this hour? Haha. Have you finally come to your senses?"

"Yayul Tak..."
