Overwhelmed by the pouring aura, Senior Jaegal pitched forward and fell to his knees with a thud; his frail legs, no longer supported by the wheelchair, sprawled out helplessly.

He stared at me, his pupils trembling uncontrollably.

Perhaps because the energy of the Heavenly Demon had permeated my mind, my voice when I spoke to him was heavy, toneless.

"How easily you declare that it cannot be done. I must change the Demonic Cult with my own hands, for I have no other choice. If you in your vain ambitions seek to block my path, then I shall strike you down where you lie."

"The… the Heavenly Demon… Divine Art!"

Jaegal Tong was the Reader of Heavenly Fate, a man said to see across the breadth of the world. He recognized my level of mastery in an instant— because the specter of the Heavenly Demon Shade, something only the Heavenly Demon could manifest, had been recreated before his very eyes.

Jaegal Tong finally prostrated himself, his entire body flat against the floor. Mine was a level of power that he, lacking even a wisp of inner qi, could not physically withstand.

He must have realized it.

He faced not the Young Cult Leader, petty hooligan— but the Heavenly Demon, reborn into the world once more.

His voice trembled violently when he finally spoke.

"How… how can this be…."

"Reader of Heavenly Fate Jaegal Tong, you would presume to discuss the fate of the world, all while trapped alone in your dark little hole. It is you, not I, who are truly mad and delusional. Do you understand?"

I raised a hand toward the massive iron door, and searing heat gathered in my palm.

_FWOOOM—_

Dark crimson qi roared, begging to be unleashed.

To be allowed to rampage.

Following the will of the Heavenly Demon, I set it loose without a second thought.

**_WHOOSH! KABOOM!_**

A distinct sphere of energy formed and slammed into the door; it flew down the corridor and embedded itself into the distant wall like an ornament.

A sense of urgent crisis must have sent uncountable thoughts racing in fragments through Senior Jaegal's mind. He would have been searching for an escape route, calculations flooding his brain in a torrent.

But only for a moment. The sight of the Heavenly Demon Shade standing before him seemed to clear his mind from all distractions, leaving only serenity.

For he had realized that without my permission, he had no way out.

"The Young Cult Leader has obtained the power of the heavens. But… why… why even seek change?" he asked, his voice trembling. "Your Reverence need only ascend to the top."

I swallowed a sigh and replied calmly.

"The Cult as it is now… it is a bottomless pit. We push others into a mire of our own creation, only to find we cannot escape it either. I wish only to break this vicious cycle."

"That is hardly what one expects from the mouth of the Demonic Cult's Heir. Nor is it consistent with your actions in the past."

"It is because of that very past. Because I am the Young Cult Leader, I must bring about that change."

Senior Jaegal would never understand, not even if he thought about it a hundred times. Even if he held the knowledge of the entire world.

Not unless he was born again.

"Haha… _Hahahaha!_"

The senior, still prostrated on the floor, grinned widely and burst into laughter. The sight was truly bizarre and ferocious.

"Why… are you laughing?"

"How amusing. Truly amusing! The unruly Young Cult Leader, who has inherited the legacy of the Heavenly Demon, seeks to upend the Cult of Shining light!… That… might not be so bad."

Has he truly gone mad? Or is he just steeped in demonic energy?

The turbid energy I felt from him was that of a demonic practitioner who had reached his very limits. It wasn't a matter of inner qi. The man's very essence felt that way.

"Please give me a final chance," he asked, calming down. "What threat could my single life be to the Young Lord who carries the legacy of the Heavenly Demon? So please, spare my life this once."

"Why should I?"

"There is one thing I must do."

"How is that my problem?"

"I will clear your way. Though my body may be failing, it should more than capable of handling such troublesome tasks."

Everything about him was suspicious. Because he was my senior. Because I knew him better than anyone else.

"And what do you want in exchange?"

"Only a single promise. That one day, the Murim Alliance's Chief Strategist, Jaegal Mundeung, will be mine to do as I see fit."

The senior's concise tone echoed deeply. I gazed down at him, sensing his sincerity.

_Chief Strategist Jaegal Mundeung. Is he the reason you're like this, senior?_

I felt no falsehood from him at all. Only a bitter grudge remained.

I became curious.

Why had my senior became a cripple, then found his way to the Demonic Cult? Why had he changed so much?

But he was too dangerous a man to leave alive. If my senior harbored a grudge in his heart, he would become a more terrifying existence to the Murim Alliance than anyone else.

"Do you know of the Rogues' Guild?"

Then, to my surprise, the words "Rogues' Guild" came from his mouth.

Of course, I knew of them.

The Rogues' Guild was a clandestine organization formed by those who worked in the lower rungs of society. This made them somewhat disorganized, but their information network was known to be superior even to the Beggars' Sect.

"The Rogues' Guild is everywhere, yet nowhere. Since its master cannot be found, it never disappears."

"Don't tell me…?"

"Yes. I'm the Nineteenth Master of the Rogues' Guild."

The senior took out a plaque from his robes, one that signified the Rogues' Guild. It was plated in gold.

Even the Murim Alliance received help from the Rogues' Guild. Naturally, I could recognize this much. There was no mistake. Jaegal Tong. My senior… was the Guild Master of the Rogues' Guild.

What kind of life had my senior lived?

"You had us all fooled. To think the Guild Master of the Rogues' Guild has been hiding within this heavenly mountain…"

"Much in the way that the Young Cult Leader has secretly inherited the legacy of the Heavenly Demon, isn't it?"

"And you say that… because you want my trust, now that we've both shown our hands?"

"It doesn't matter what you think. It's a pity that my plans must change, but if I can gain the Young Cult Leader's support, I am more than willing to adapt."

"...You've changed a lot."

"Not as much as you, Your Reverence."

I felt a sense of anticipation and unease at the same time.

I was still uncertain, but an alliance with my senior was worth more than a massive army. However, if there was an enemy commander I feared facing at the end of the march, it would also be him.

The Reader of Heavenly Fate, Jaegal Tong.

My senior.

The Heavenly Demon Shade faded away like smoke. With my thoughts steadied, I spoke softly.

"You'll have your work cut out in trying to keep up with me."

"…before that, can you please settle on how you will address me? If I bet everything only to find out you were just a madman, I wouldn't be able to close my eyes even in death. Huhu..." [^1]

"I _am_ a madman, so just deal with it."

Whatever story my senior has, it can't be worse than mine.

"Jaegal Mundeung. Is he all you want?"

I tossed the question out lightly, as if going for a stroll.

"It'd be even better if you brought him to me with all four limbs crushed."

"For someone from the orthodox sects, your words seem a bit harsh."

"And the Young Cult Leader seems better suited for the Murim Alliance than the Demonic Cult."

"Tell me. What do I need to do now?"

"_First…!_"

Jaegal Tong's eyes flashed as he began to speak.

"Help me up. This really hurts."

"You were speaking just fine."

"I was fighting for my life and fueled by adrenaline."

"That mouth of yours wouldn't shut even in death."

He laughed.

I laughed as well.

To think that the Reader of Heavenly Fate, once considered the greatest talent of the righteous path, and I, Yang Jagak, would be plundering the Demonic Cult together.

Fate truly works in mysterious ways.

After I helped him up and set him back in his wheelchair, he covered his atrophied legs with his blanket and got to the point.

"The current Demonic Cult is no different from an active volcano. From the Seven Hallowed Palaces to the Council of Elders, their power is more vigorous than ever before."

"You're saying there's more than one tiger aiming for the summit."

"That's correct. And so, the Young Cult Leader's reappearance has thrown a wrench in their plans. If they disavow the absent Cult Leader now, Your Reverence will naturally inherit the position. But immediately disavowing the new leader would be too great a risk."

"Hmm… so even the Demonic Cult deals with cutthroat politics..."

If they were to claim that the Cult Leader would never return, they would be simply handing me throne. So they wouldn't be able to act rashly.

It would be easiest for them to either kill me or support me.

As silence fell, Jaegal Tong, who had been watching me intently, changed the subject.

"Do you hold a grudge?"

"What do you mean, all of a sudden?"

"I ask because not only do you just stand and listen when I call the Cult of Shining Light a Demonic Cult, but you yourself also call it a Demonic Cult."

I stared at him blankly, and his eyes, soaked in a demonic nature, seemed to see right through me.

Not yet. It's not time to reveal it yet. When my senior regains his former self. We will talk then.

"Just continue with what you were saying."

The senior shrugged and chuckled. Then he continued speaking nonchalantly.

"Very well. Even if the Young Cult Leader has mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, that alone may be enough to destroy the Demonic Cult, but not to rule it."

I nodded once more.

The Demonic Cult was not a problem that could be solved by simply winning over the Seven Hallowed Palaces and the Elders.

The hundreds of thousands of lay followers. Without their support, it would be impossible to change its very foundation.

Jaegal Tong moved from his spot and took out two books and one box from a side table.

"These were originally prepared to win the Cult Leader's favor. I'll give them all to you. First is an original manuscript containing the founding doctrine of the Cult of Shining Light. It's one of the few extant copies. The other is the registry of ranks in the Cult of Shining Light. Please familiarize yourself with them."

I took the books from him and quietly tucked them into my robes.

Then, he carefully handed me a box.

When I opened it, a very thinly crafted black mask was revealed.

It was a half-mask that covered the eyes and nose, but exposed the mouth. Its sleek design was unfamiliar, something I had never seen in the Central Plains.

"This is… a mask, isn't it?"

"The roots of the Cult of Shining Light lie in the Western Regions. Its scriptures say that an evil god created an evil dragon, but a good god sent a hero to stop it. This mask… is said to have been worn by that hero."

On the surface, it looked like just a thin mask. Aside from its apparent flexibility, I couldn't feel anything special about it.

Jaegal Tong continued.

"It may look like an ordinary mask, but when worn, it's a divine object that cloaks you in a protective aura of Myriad Poison Immunity and prevents anyone from recognizing your true self."

"Are you kidding me right now?"

"If you don't believe me, you can wear it and look in a bronze mirror. The Five Divine Treasures were passed down the Cult through generations, and are said to have been used by the Heavenly Demon. They are now scattered and their whereabouts are unknown, but one thing is certain. This is one of them, the Mask of Sacred Flames."

The Five Divine Treasures….

I skeptically examined the Mask of Sacred Flames. In response, my senior stated firmly, as if it were a foregone conclusion.

"For now, you must keep as low a profile as possible. The moment they find out that the Young Cult Leader has mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, you'll have to face thirty percent… no, fifty percent of the Cult of Shining Light directly."

Fifty percent…. I didn't know the full extent of the Demonic Cult's power, but just imagining fifty percent of the Murim Alliance attacking me made my head swim. If I were to encounter just the Divine Monk and the 108 Arhats in an enclosed space, it would result in mutual destruction at best.

"That's just how long the Cult has been without legitimate leadership. However, the danger is not immediate. Just win over as many Lords and Elders as you can before the Grand Festival of Heavenly Shining Light, and you'll naturally be able to rise to the Cult Leader's seat. You have four months until then."

"Easier said than done."

"That's not something the person who wants to change this heavenly mountain should say."

The senior chuckled and continued speaking.

"Be careful. You must approach them one by one. Some may even approach first, such as the Palace of Bliss, which has an excellent information network but lacks power. Not to mention how the current Young Cult Leader doesn't have a good reputation… surely you are aware of that?"

He was probably talking about Ma Cheonso's history of terrible behavior.

"As for the things I've done… I feel deeply remorseful."

"No one cares what you feel. What is seen is what matters. Therefore… it would be better for you to present a new image."

He rolled his wheels, scrutinizing my body from head to toe. It was an uncomfortable feeling, but I waited in silence.

"Although the Demonic Cult's power has grown, it doesn't mean the lay followers have grown in tandem. On the contrary, their suffering has only increased due to the unreasonable tyranny of the Seven Hallowed Palaces and the Council of Elders."

Whenever war approaches, the hardships of the common people seep into their very bones.

But what could I do to help them now?

As I frowned, my senior looked at me with a very serious expression and said.

"There are many ways to alleviate the situation. Go and see for yourself. There will be countless people whose lives could change with just a small helping hand."

What on earth are you trying to say?

As if answering my unspoken question, my senior spoke in a confident tone.

"Go in secret. Undercover. Can you do it, Your Reverence?"
