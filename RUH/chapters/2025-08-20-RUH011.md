I arrived at the Valley of Sorrow.

The journey was so treacherous, requiring a ferry ride down a valley with rough currents, that it was certainly a place few would visit. However, perhaps because of that, the natural scenery was nothing short of breathtaking, with its river flowing from a waterfall and a field of red flowers in full bloom.

"Young Cult Leader, look over there. Wow! To think such a welcoming crowd has gathered. This makes a provincial tour worthwhile, doesn't it?" Yayul Tak snickered.

"Hmm..."

It was just as Yayul Tak had said. Thousands of lay followers were prostrated on the ground, their hands forming the shape of a flame.

To those who rejected the laws of the Ming, the Great Ming Code, and followed the doctrine of the Demonic Cult, the Young Cult Leader was like a small sun.

I gave a light wave and stepped off the ferry.

Just then, a man who looked to be in his forties with messy hair rushed over. He dropped to his knees and declared, "We greet the Young Cult Leader! For Your Reverence to grace this humble Valley of Sorrow with your presence, I, Jang Chun, the village chief of the Valley of Sorrow, am moved to the depths of my heart. Long live the Shining Light Cult, for a thousand, a thousand, ten thousand years!"

"A thousand, a thousand, ten thousand years!"

I was mortified.

This was exactly why the emperors of the Ming Dynasty called them the Demonic Cult and cried for their purge and overthrow. How could they not be offended when these cultist were shouting a cheer meant only for an emperor?

Besides, if the Young Cult Leader was worthy of a thousand years, then the Cult Leader was...

_There are so many things wrong here._

I did not let it show, but I swallowed a deep sigh.

Looking around again, I could see the beaten earth beneath the bowed heads of the followers growing damp. They seemed incredibly hot, with sweat dripping from the scorching sun. Moreover, their clothes were thick and layered.

So I asked, "Aren't you hot?"

The lay followers cautiously raised their heads. Then they quickly lowered them again.

"Pardon?" the village chief, Jang Chun, asked from the side as if he had misheard.

"...I asked if you are hot."

"Ah... No! Hot? We're not hot at all. Is anyone here hot?"

When Jang Chun asked with a look of disgust as if such a thing were impossible, the followers instantly lapped up the sweat that covered their faces.

I was momentarily speechless at the utterly primitive display.

I looked at Yayul Tak in bewilderment, and he nonchalantly gave me a thumbs-up.

"As expected... Young Cult Leader! Let's go!" he urged with a chuckle. "Hey, what are you all doing? Hurry up and escort him."

My heart ached as if it would break, but I finally started walking. Because right now, I was the debauched Little Lecher.

With each step, my feet felt as heavy as lead. The lay followers continuously slurped and swallowed their sweat, their heads bowed low.

_This is Karma. It's Karma._

The place Jang Chun led me to was a spacious pavilion, unlike the shabby houses outside.

The Ming imperial court divided its people into the four social hierarchies and the outcasts, but the Demonic Cult distinguished between lay followers, lower and upper-level members who passed a test, and those with positions in the Council of Elders or the palace.

And since the Demonic Cult occupied a vast territory, a small village like this had a lower-level member without a specific affiliation appointed as its village chief. Thanks to the salary he received, he could afford to stay in such a pavilion despite this being a humble village.

_GLUG GLUG BURBLE_.

I filled a cup from the prepared table and downed it in one gulp.

Jang Chun, who was kneeling before the table, spoke cautiously. "Your Reverence must have had a long journey. But what brings you to such a humble place...?"

I came looking for Eul Taesik, but that was not something to be broadcast everywhere.

Sensing this, Yayul Tak, who stood beside me, answered. "Hey, village chief. Does the Young Cult Leader, the small sun of the Shining Light Cult, need a reason to look after the welfare of his people?"

"Heh, heheh. That's right! Of course, he should. That's true, but... I don't feel at ease since I have nothing worthy to offer."

He was right. Compared to the Bright Spring Parlor, the spread on this small table was truly meager.

I turned my gaze out the window. The distant scenery was shabby. I had heard this was a place where the homeless among the followers gathered, and indeed, there wasn't a single decent hovel.

As if reading my mind again, Yayul Tak spoke first. "Wow. It's not that far from the market, but it's worse than the outskirts."

"Yes? Yes... well, that's right. As everyone knows, this is a very poor place..." Jang Chun replied awkwardly, lowering his head.

_GLUG GLUG BURBLE_.

I filled my cup again and swallowed. The red petals of the flowers blooming everywhere caught my eye.

"Have those flowers always been here?"

"Those... I apologize, Young Cult Leader. I'm ignorant when it comes to flowers..."

"I see. You may leave."

"Then please call me anytime if you need anything. I shall take my leave now."

Jang Chun carefully backed out of the room.

After confirming he was completely gone, Yayul Tak spoke suggestively. "Young Cult Leader. Seeing as it's such a small village, I think we'll find him pretty soon, right?"

"...Don't you find anything strange?"

"Pardon? What do you mean?"

It was strange. There were too many unexplainable things. I turned my gaze back out the window, and Yayul Tak's head followed.

"What do you notice?"

"Well... the followers seem idle, and the flowers and river are beautiful?"

"Is that all?"

"Then what else is there?"

"Nothing."

"Young Cult Leader, are you drunk?"

Should I just... kill this guy?

"This is a place where people live, yet there are no signs of farming."

All I could see were fields of red flowers in full bloom and peasants with nothing to do.

"Uh... you're right. Maybe they just buy things from the market? It's not like farming in the Heavenly Mountains is an easy task."

"There's a perfectly good river, the altitude is low, and the temperature is warm. It's a good place for farming. Besides, you think people this poor would travel up the valley to go to the market?"

"Hey... it's not called the Valley of Sorrow for nothing," Yayul Tak mumbled while stuffing his face. "Everyone's poor for a reason. Isn't it because all they do is play and eat?" He chewed and swallowed loudly.

I stared at Yayul Tak, who was obliviously putting vegetables into his mouth.

He was so carefree.

"We have to investigate. Lead the way."

"What? Without finishing all this?"

"I'm done."

"Ah, no, wait a minute! Young Lord! I'm not done eating!"

Jang Chun walked toward the entrance of the Valley of Sorrow with a rather heavy expression. There was no one from the Flying Tiger Gang in the area, but he still kept stopping and looking back as if to check if anyone was there.

After a while, once he was certain there were no witnesses, he opened his mouth.

"Ha, fuck. Why the hell did he come all the way here? _Ptooie!_"

His polite demeanor was gone, and his thuggish nature was on full display. His slumped shoulders straightened, and his expression filled with malice.

"Ha, that little bastard wouldn't have come for no reason. Don't tell me he already knows? Maybe I should've just asked him directly instead of being so proper."

Jang Chun scratched his head, complaining in frustration. He was dying to know why the Young Cult Leader had come to the Valley of Sorrow. He had a guess, but it was a strictly forbidden matter within the Demonic Cult, so he could not bring it up carelessly.

"If he came because of that... heheh. Should I take this chance to switch sides to the Young Cult Leader and move to the Sun Peak? No. If I bring it up carelessly, I could lose my head."

Jang Chun licked his lips, his eyes sinking into a greedy gaze.

However, a moment later, after passing through some bushes, a small pavilion on a cliff came into view. His shoulders immediately slumped, and his eyes changed to resemble those of a pitiful old man.

He was truly skilled at changing his demeanor. And there was only one reason he showed this side.

When he met someone stronger.

"You've arrived, my lord."

"......"

In the pavilion stood a man wrapped in a robe and even wearing a mask, his hands clasped behind his back. Despite his slender frame, the aura he exuded was truly extraordinary.

Jang Chun took a pouch from his robes and respectfully presented it.

"The amount seems less than last month," the man asked in a cold tone after a quick glance inside the pouch.

Jang Chun cursed him endlessly in his mind, but on the outside, he bowed his waist and replied in a most pathetic tone. "That is... there was a complication because the Young Cult Leader came. I don't know why he suddenly came from the Palace of Minor Light to the Valley of Sorrow..."

"The Young Cult Leader?"

_FLINCH_. Even though the man had only repeated the question, Jang Chun was visibly more nervous than when he had greeted the Young Cult Leader.

"Yes, yes. So we stopped work a few days ago to prepare thoroughly... I am truly sorry."

"I heard he'd fled his secluded training not long ago... but then he came to the Valley of Sorrow...?"

It was a relief. The man's attention had shifted to the Young Cult Leader, which meant his chances of surviving this month had increased.

Jang Chun swallowed hard and replied. "That is correct."

The masked man seemed to ponder for a moment, then spoke nonchalantly. "Next month. Bring double the amount by then. You haven't forgotten why a thug like you was made the village chief, have you?"

It was an absurd demand. If doubling it were that easy, he would not have to be so cautious!

"Oh my, how could I ever forget? I will be sure to do as you say."

However, Jang Chun was a man who valued _now_ more than _today_, and _today_ more than _tomorrow_.

"I'll be counting on you."

"Thank you! My lord."

He bowed his head three or four times. To Jang Chun, this man was a grim reaper, more fearsome than the Cult Leader himself.

His knees felt weak, and just as he was about to scurry away, the man spoke again; Jang Chun, dripping with cold sweat, scurried back and leaned in to listen.

"What happened to that man?"

"That man, you say..."

"Eul Taesik."

Surprisingly, the name Eul Taesik, the very person the Young Cult Leader was looking for, came from the man called 'my lord'.

"Ah! Don't worry. His spirit is so broken he can't even hold his head up." The man's image came to Jang Chun's mind, and he babbled excitedly as a sinister smile spread across his face. "He's just a nobody serving at the gambling den now."

"...Has he adapted? Well, it is about time to give him a gift. His daughter is coming of age, isn't she?"

"Yes, that's right. Her skin is so fine, in a little while she'll be worthy of joining the Three Flowers."

"You've managed her well so that no one could touch her, right?"

"Oh my, of course. Just as you told me to, any bastard who drooled over her, I just bashed their heads right in—"

Jang Chun got carried away, and his original way of speaking slipped out. Seeming to notice, he cleared his throat a couple of times and continued in a more refined tone. "However, I don't quite understand why you, my lord, have been interested in such an insignificant man for so long..."

"Your understanding is not needed."

"Pardon? Yes. Of course, you are absolutely right."

His mouth was hidden by the mask, but the man seemed to be smiling.

He nodded his head and said, "This is good. Since the Young Cult Leader came in person, we should at least offer him a woman. For old times' sake. That daughter of his... send her to his chambers tonight."

"Yes— what?"

Jang Chun felt genuinely disappointed. He had been saving her as per the man's orders, but she was a woman who stirred his lust every time he saw her.

However, the lord was firm, showing no interest in such matters. "He wouldn't have come all this way for nothing. He used to deal in the goods, so he must have come looking for it. So, you should subtly show it to him."

"What? No, that too? So suddenly? What if something goes wrong..."

"Do you think someone like you has the right to question me? You just have to do as you're told. That's the reason you're alive."

"_Eek!_ I understand."

Jang Chun instinctively knew what would happen if he questioned him one more time. The man he called 'my lord' was usually inscrutable, with no highs or lows, but strangely, he flew into a rage whenever the name Eul Taesik was mentioned.

In Jang Chun's experience, there was only one explanation for why such a high-ranking person would be so interested in crushing a good-for-nothing wreck.

_That bastard Eul Taesik must have stolen his woman. Heheh. What a petty bastard. By the way, is this why he had me raise the daughter so carefully? This guy is a truly vicious bastard too._

Jang Chun clicked his tongue internally. He could not even begin to guess how far that man planned to make Eul Taesik fall.

_Well, it's not my problem. It's a waste to just hand over that wench Haryeong, but there's no harm in getting on the Young Cult Leader's good side._

A moment later, Jang Chun bowed respectfully and took his leave.

The man who remained behind ground his teeth and muttered. "Captain, fall to the very bottom. Only then will you and I be the same. Huhu... huhuhuhu... kuhahahahaha!"

A wild laugh erupted from his small frame.

_WHOOSH._ And like the wind in the valley, he too vanished.
