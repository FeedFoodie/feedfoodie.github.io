_—Shining Light Cult, Water Peak, Seven Hallowed Palaces, Palace of Merchants_

After passing the marketplace and crossing one more hill, a grand palace came into view. Below the mountain, a vast sea was visible.

_SHWAAAAA._

The open air seeped in. Leaving the mountainous terrain of the pass behind felt just like stepping out of that cave after years of seclusion.

_BOOM! BOOM! BA-DOOM! BOOM!_

The sound of massive drums echoed everywhere. The outer walls, painted white and blue, evoked an exotic atmosphere. A moat had been built around the palace, making it look like an island floating on the mountain peak.

_So the Palace of Minor Light really is but a minor palace._

This place could rival the Forbidden City where the emperor resides.

"What do you think, Your Reverence? Isn't this your first time visiting the Palace of Merchants?"

Birang asked with a confident tone, like a merchant who had come to sell his wares.

I didn't want to give him the satisfaction, but the scenery was like a painting; I couldn't help but marvel at it.

"I didn't know there was a sea below these mountains."

"That's not a sea. It's a river formed from melting perennial snow."

"That's a river?"

"Yes. It is called the Hot Sea. It was mistaken for a sea during the Tang Dynasty, which is how it got its name. Below it are the Oirats, a nomadic tribe."

_CLOP, CLOP._

As we drew closer to the palace, I could certainly see a variety of non-Han people. There were some with pale white skin and long faces, and others as black as charcoal. On the flat land near the plains, countless palanquins and carriers bustled about with their escorts.

"Wow... It's so beautiful!"

"I see The Palace of Merchants hasn't changed at all."

"Father, have you been here before?"

Eul Taesik kept his mouth firmly shut, and Eul Haryeong didn't ask any more questions. Instead, the one who grew curious was the Golden Cavalry Commander, Birang.

"Now that I look at you, you seem very familiar. May I ask for your alias?"

What was the point of asking if he'd already guessed the truth? His words were cunning.

However, Eul Taesik answered without hesitation, as if he had already thought of something.

"Blood Asura."

It was quite fitting. It would have been a shame if no one had asked. Since he had erased his past, it was only natural that he would need a new name.

I'll have to call him Blood Asura from now on.

I smirked and headed toward the entrance. Birang simply tilted his head, hearing the alias for the first time, and followed behind me.

As we stepped onto the bridge built over the moat, a shout rang out.

"Announcing His Reverence The Young Cult Leader—!"

With the shout, the massive crowd parted like a blooming flower. Above the castle gate, a sign boldly proclaimed it as the Palace of Merchants.

_DING! DING-DING! DI-DI-DING!_

As we entered, musicians playing string instruments and dancers put on a feast, as if to welcome us. However, the place was so lively that you couldn't hear them properly without getting closer.

"Grain this way!"

"Don't switch lines, wait there!"

"Get your wages!"

Huge warehouses lined the entrance of the Palace of Merchants. As the place responsible for the finances of the Demonic Cult, its scale was no less than the three greatest Trading Companies in the world.

Only after we had gone much deeper did the noise finally subside. A massive pavilion revealed itself. It was the Prosperity Pavilion, where the Lord of Merchants resided.

"Damn, this is far. Are we there yet?"

As Yayul Tak grumbled, Birang calmly nodded his head.

"We are. However..."

Looking at his expression, his focus was forward, and his unease was palpable.

And then—

"Hyu... Hyung-nim.[^1]"

Yayul Tak uttered the word in a tone close to a whisper.

Hyung-nim?

_—Shining Light Cult, Palace of Merchants, Prosperity Pavilion_

Cheon Semin had been in an exceptionally good mood today. The reason was that he had received news that the Young Cult Leader he had expected for so long would soon arrive. It was his personal philosophy that one had to know how to find happiness in waiting in order to be able to sell goods at the highest price.

So, he had called for a band, had the drums beaten, and waited with a joyful heart.

However...

"Young Master, what brings you here?"

His expression was not pleased. No, it was clearly one of discomfort. It was because instead of the one he'd been waiting for, an unwelcome guest had showed up instead.

"My father is greatly concerned that the Lord may have forgotten his past promise. Please do visit our palace sometime."

A monotone voice and a cold face.

He was a man with a sharp jawline and dark eyelashes, like a painted masterpiece. If Ma Cheonso had a decadent beauty, this man had an unapproachable presence, sharp enough to cut. His long, white-bleached hair— perhaps a side effect of his training— also drew the eye, matching his pure white robes.

"The Oirats are holding our caravans hostage, which is hitting our revenue. You want to talk about money at a time like this? Don't you think that's a bit much? I will consider visiting at a later time."

Cheon Semin, the Lord of Merchants, seemed to be filled with pent-up complaints. But this man— who was not yet thirty— could not be treated lightly.

"...Very well. I heard Cheon Ahmu had an unpleasant experience at the Bright Spring Parlor. As Chairman of the Scions, I feel it's a pity, so I thought I'd see him before I leave."

"Ahem! You mean that child? Why would a busy person like you bother? It's fine. When I see the boy, I'll let him know to go see you."

As Cheon Semin's clear refusal was given, the man stared at him with eyes as deep as the sea and spoke.

"I see. So he isn't in the palace? You didn't send him to the Palace of Minor Light, I hope. Becoming too close to the Young Cult Leader could lead to misunderstandings."

"Well... the boy can be impulsive."

"I'll look for him."

The man turned his body at once, as if there was no need to listen further. This made Cheon Semin anxious.

"Erm, why even... trouble yourself?"

The man turned back to Cheon Semin unceremoniously, so fast he left an afterimage; but the movement was so fluid that Cheon Semin froze for a moment.

And then.

_FWIING!_

A flash of light.

"Hup!"

The Lord of Merchants actually had to use a clumsy movement technique to rescue his vase in time.

_SHRING!_

He even revealed enough spirit to engage in a life-or-death battle right away.

However, his opponent was as serene as a butterfly about to land, as if nothing had happened.

"An orchid with signs of spotted disease can be rescued by removing the affected leaves. But if you allow the disease to fester, all you can do is uproot the plant. To make amends for my discourtesy... I will solve the problem with the Oirats for you."

The man performed a clasped hands greeting, turned, and walked away. At his words, Cheon Semin turned his eyes to the orchid that was placed beside him.

_CRUMBLE._

The dead leaf turned to dust and fell away.

"He's already on the verge of reaching the pinnacle of martial arts. Yayul Jin... you _bastard!_"

Cheon Semin's cry echoed.

Yayul Jin, the Young Lord of the Palace of Martial Blood and the Chairman of the Scions of the Shining Light Cult's regular meeting. A prodigy who earned the alias _Flashfire Blade_ in his youth and was called a genius of the ages.

He was here.

_SCREEEEEEECH—!_

As Yayul Jin stepped outside, a large white eagle circled in the blue sky, letting out a cry. In addition, men wearing white martial arts uniforms and feathered, Western-style capes formed a straight path before him.

As Yayul Jin walked through the path, a young woman with one eye and a black eyepatch, who was at the very front, immediately followed behind him. She draped a pure white cape over his shoulders.

"Are you going to leave the Palace of Merchants as it is? The Oirats are an excuse. The funds have been drying since before that."

Perhaps she had heard Cheon Semin's cry, as a hint of killing intent flashed from her. Even at a glance, she was a woman of formidable martial prowess.

"Taehwa. Refrain from unnecessary questions."

Her name was Taehwa. She was the Captain of the Seven White Branches Martial Squad, Yayul Jin's right hand, and a master who, at the age of thirty, had risen to the forty-sixth rank in the Shining Light Cult and was on the verge of the supreme level.

"As you wish."

Perhaps only Yayul Jin could instantly quell the active volcano that was her.

_SCREEEEEEECH—!_

As Yayul Jin and the Seven White Branches Martial Squad were about to leave the entrance of the Palace of Merchants, the white eagle roared again. It began to swoop down like an arrow.

Yayul Jin, with his usual expressionless face, stuck out his right hand. The white eagle's talons caught onto the white metal bracer on his wrist and settled there.

"Did you see a familiar face?"

There was no way the white eagle could answer, but Yayul Jin seemed certain. He stopped and scanned the area in front of him.

And then.

"Hyu... Hyung-nim."

The one who'd left their palace long ago to live a pitiful life in another palace.

His only younger brother, though he didn't want to admit it.

Yayul Tak.

And the Young Cult Leader, Ma Cheonso.

They were there.

{sep}

_Is he someone who has undergone Rejuvenation?_

My first impression of him was that strong. He appeared calm and serene, but the atmosphere around him was suffocating, as if the very air had evaporated. The warriors in white martial arts uniforms surrounding him were also extraordinary, judging by their restrained movements.

_If he's Yayul Tak's Hyung-nim... then he must be from the Palace of Martial Blood._

I had never asked what kind of life he had lived, but I knew that the Yayul family belonged to the Palace of Martial Blood.

"Hyung-nim..."

Yayul Tak called out to him, his voice uncharacteristically pitiful.

However.

_STEP, STEP._

His brother didn't react at all and simply started walking toward me. He and his group approached in a crane wing formation, as if it were choreographed.

"It has been a while."

He was greeting me, his bearing as precise as if he'd been meticulously drawn.

This man was definitely...

"What business does the Palace of Martial Blood have here?"

I spat out the words, the corners of my mouth lifting.

"Is there any reason I shouldn't come? And what about you, Young Cult Leader?"

"I don't think I have a reason not to come either."

"It seems neither of us intends to explain, so I will take my leave."

It was a strange feeling. His gaze was clearly filled with contempt. Did he hold a grudge against me? But rather than feeling displeased, I feel invigorated.

He brushed past me. Our robes swept against each other, their black and white intersecting.

"Hyung-nim..."

As Yayul Tak called out once more to his fading back, the man stopped dead in his tracks and spoke.

"You're still pathetic."

"..."

"Cheon Ahmu. I'll be seeing you soon."

Cheon Ahmu, who had been hiding behind Birang like a child, jumped in surprise. He stepped out and bowed his head deeply, replying in a meek voice.

"Y-yes. Chairman..."

_STEP, STEP._

He started walking again. There was no change in him, before or after. If there was ever a person who had reached the pinnacle of composure, it would probably be him.

After he and his group disappeared, everyone let out a shallow breath, as if the air had finally settled back into place.

"Hoo... Why would Young Lord Yayul Jin lead the Seven White Branches Martial Squad all the way here... Surely he didn't come to see me, right? Heheh. Surely not. But what if he did? What if my connection with him has been severed?!"

Cheon Ahmu muttered his usual nonsense to himself. They were truly useless words, but among them was a name that caught my ear.

Yayul Jin.

It was him after all. The man who had caught my eye in the registry of the Shining Light Library. Moreover, it was a name I had definitely heard before. The name the servants had mentioned when I first arrived at the Palace of Minor Light.

> _Isn't the Young Cult Leader a kite with its string cut now? Since he failed to learn the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, His Lordship won't let him be, even once he returns._
> 
> _Honestly, it's only right that Lord Yayul Jin leads our Cult in the future. It's poetic justice._
> 
> _Lower your voice. Someone might hear._

The eldest son of the Lord of Martial Blood, was he? Then Yayul Tak is... 

I looked at Yayul Tak with complicated feelings. He was staring at the floor with gloomy eyes.
