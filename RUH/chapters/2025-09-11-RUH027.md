The Lord of Martial Blood, Yayul Cheon. This was clearly a joint effort between his father and Esen. The Oirats and the Palace of Martial Blood had interacted for a long time, so it was initially incomprehensible that they would provoke the Shining Light Cult.

That was also why he didn't particularly try to stop Taehwa when she was about to behead Esen's personal guard.

Esen approached Yayul Jin and held out his composite bow; it was three feet long, and made him look like a reincarnation of the Martial God, Guan Yu.

«Can you draw it?»

Yayul Jin cooly examined the composite bow. It was a bow made of ivory, said to be stronger than rock.

Even if he were to use his inner arts, he wouldn't be able to draw it as efforlessly.

«They say Temujin used a bow made of ivory, just like this one! But who else do you think could draw it now? The current Khan may have inherited his blood, but only I have this strength.»

Esen drew the bowstring again. Then he aimed at Yayul Jin's head and released it.

_PWIIING!_

Even though there was clearly no arrow, a fierce wind blew past, rustling Yayul Jin's hair; but unlike Taehwa, who had already drawn her sword, Yayul Jin stared at him indifferently.

«What's the difference between you, descendants of the Northern Liao Imperial Dinasty[^1] forced to cower within the Heavenly Mountains, and me, forced to settle for the lesser title of Grand Preceptor for lacking the blood of the Great Yuan Dynasty? Go back to your father.»

At Esen's words, Yayul Jin narrowed one eye and drew the white sword hanging from his waist—

No, he had merely brought his hand to it.

_PING!_ A flash of light sparked, and the string of the bow Esen was holding snapped.

_TWANG!_ The ivory, which had been bent with great effort, stiffly straightened.

«One must learn to let go of that which one cannot have. They say the Minister of War Yu Qian has introduced a Regiment-Battalion System. Grand Preceptor, capturing and imprisoning the Emperor only provoked them into using you as a pretext.»

It was a shocking statement.

Capturing and imprisoning the Emperor? Esen, a mere reject of the Yuan Dynasty, had committed a truly absurd act! Because of that, the Ming imperial court had even implemented the Regiment-Battalion System, which unified the military.

From the Shining Light Cult's perspective, this was certainly not good news.

«You say you will become the Khan of the Northern Plains? Grand Preceptor, please attend to the Grand Preceptor's affairs. We, the Shining Light Cult, will handle the Central Plains. I presume you will release the imprisoned believers, and will report as much to my father. As for today's discourtesy...»

Yayul Jin finished speaking and slowly turned his body toward Taehwa.

At that, she calmly extended both her arms forward.

_SLASH! SLASH!_

A gruesome sound spread through the yurt. Yayul Jin had slashed Taehwa twice.

Both of her sleeves were stained bright red. Although the wounds looked deep, Taehwa's expression didn't change one bit.

"We're going back."

"I hear and obey."

_SWOOSH_. Yayul Jin turned without hesitation and headed outside.

Esen bit his lip hard, shook his head, and muttered.

«A disobedient tiger cub... Yayul Cheon, you have your work cut out for you. Wa Nansu!»

When he called his subordinate, a man who looked like a bird of prey entered with disciplined steps.

«You called?»

«I will proclaim to the entire world that I have become Khan. I will begin by sacrificing the Emperor... We're going to the prison.»

«Yes!»

The leader of the Oirats, Grand Preceptor Esen.

He began to move toward the prison, leading his personal guard, the Ultimate Flaming Horse Cavalry.

{sep}

"We will be heading back to the palace."

Having said those words, Yayul Jin took a seat near the Hot Sea. It was a strange action, his words not matching his deeds.

At this, Taehwa politely asked, an awkward question forming in her mind.

"Shall I escort you?"

However—

"Sit down."

"Sir?"

"Your bleeding hasn't stopped yet."

"I-I'm fine."

"Do you intend to leave a trail leading to the Cult? Sit down and staunch the bleeding."

With those words, Yayul Jin turned his head toward the edge of the Hot Sea. The setting sun seemed to envelop his awkward consideration.

Even though he was the one who had cut her, Taehwa's eyes trembled uncontrollably. She bit the soft flesh inside her mouth in deep emotion. It felt like tears were about to burst forth.

"Th... thank you."

"...It was something I did because I, too, cannot escape the long shadow of my imperial ancestors. There's no need to be thankful."

He said it calmly, but the Seven White Branches Martial Squad grew solemn.

They had grown up in the Palace of Martial Blood, but they were men who had sworn their loyalty solely to the being known as Yayul Jin.

Therefore, they knew very well the feelings with which he had spoken. The curse and specter left by the seed of the Northern Liao Dynasty's imperial blood, the Delegated Accountability.

Making one's closest subordinates take responsibility in their stead was both a punishment and a right that the Lord, Yayul Cheon, had bestowed upon his children since their youth.

Yayul Tak left his home because of it, and Yayul Jin lost his smile because of it.

_How far do you intend to go, Father..._

Yayul Jin watched the reflecting golden waves and fell into self-deprecating thoughts.

His father wouldn't stop at the seat of Cult Leader. After that, he would strike at the Central Plains, and then the Ming Dynasty. Beyond the Hot Sea, the Yangtze River would be stained with ten years of blood; the lives of the lay followers would grow even more miserable, and the screams of the innocent would echo throughout the world.

And at the very forefront would have to be himself and his people.

His fate had been determined from birth.

It wasn't that he wished to turn back now. This was something he had to do for his father, and for the many members of the Palace of Martial Blood who looked up to him.

However...

He was merely lost in thought, wondering if he would lose what little of himself remained if he were unable to so much as look back for a moment like this.

"Who goes there?!"

At his subordinate's heavy shout, he turned his head to see someone wearing a black blood-wind robe and a half-mask.

It was an unusual attire, but he didn't question it too much, as the Tianshan Mountain Road was crowded with people from the Western Regions and all sorts of tribes.

Rather than that...

_Is he lost?_

The problem was that he hadn't entered a designated path, but a secret road meant for members of the Shining Light Cult climbing the Tianshan Mountains.

"May I rest here for a moment? The road is so long."

_Han?_

Someone dressed in a mix of Western Regions and Central Plains attire, who entered the secret road and spoke Han.

A sense of doubt arose.

"Identify yourself."

The subordinate blocked the path again and said resolutely.

But—

"I'm but a wanderer who has come a long way."

He replied with the utmost calm, and the corners of his mouth were even raised in a smile.

The setting sun cast long shadows.

Yayul Jin watched for a long moment, before turning back away and saying:

"Make a place for him."

{sep}

I never expected to meet him again in a place like this.

The eldest son of the Palace of Martial Blood, Yayul Jin.

After running for a long time, I finally reached the western edge of the Hot Sea where a plain opened up, and there he was, with the Seven White Branches Martial Squad.

_Did he come from the Oirats?_

But something was strange.

The woman who appeared to be his subordinate was pressing her acupoints to stop the bleeding, and Yayul Jin was seated as if waiting for her.

Recalling my days in the Demonic Cult, I knew they weren't as demoralized and corrupted by evil as one might think...

...although I didn't want to admit it.

_Even so... isn't Yayul Jin known for his cruelty? As the Ghost Killer?_

It was definitely recorded that way in Jaegal Tong's registry.

@[《The Shining Light Cult Ranking List》]@

* @[Official Name: Yayul Jin]@

* @[Alias: the Flashfire Blade]@

* @[Position: The Seven Hallowed Palaces, the Palace of Martial Blood, Young Palace Lord]@

* @[Martial Arts Rank: Fifteenth]@

* @[Estimated Level: Transcendent]@

* @[Profile: Some call him the Ghost Killer due to his cruelty. However, his natural talent is comparable to the Ten Strike Finisher of the orthodox sects. The Seven White Branches Martial Squad are those who only revere him...]@

The Ghost Killer. It meant he would mercilessly cut down even those who had died and become specters.

A man like that cares for his subordinate this much?

"Make a place for him."

As his order fell, the Seven White Branches Martial Squad formed a circle, creating distance and clearing a path.

_THUMP, THUMP._

I walked inside and plopped down in front of Yayul Jin and the venomously glaring Taehwa.

"Thank you. But where are you coming from?"

"..."

Yayul Jin gave no answer, no reaction.

What a silent man.

I asked again.

"Are you admiring the river?"

Still no answer this time.

However, there was a reaction.

It was the one-eyed woman, Taehwa, who was sitting with her arm down, seemingly short of breath from the pain.

"That tongue... watch it. Before I cut it off."

"In your condition?"

"You don't want to live?"

At Taehwa's words, I let out a small laugh and slightly lifted myself from my seat.

And I took a large step toward her.

At that, she tried to force her hand toward the swords on her back. CHAK. Until I snatched her wrist.

"Ugh...!"

"Those wounds are serious. If you leave them like this, you won't be able to hold a spoon, let alone a sword. Even with a skilled doctor, it would take a long time to heal."

A glance revealed the wound was so deep the bone was visible.

It was beyond the level where pressing acupoints could stop the bleeding. The problem was the stagnant blood.

Finally, Yayul Jin turned his head and asked.

"Do you know medicine?"

"Not much, but a little?"

I didn't learn for long, but I had taken a brief interest when I was staying with the Tang Clan.

"State your wishes."

Asking what I want right off the bat.

He's a truly direct person. Aren't there too many words omitted in silence?

"Young Palace Lord!"

Taehwa shouted, but Yayul Jin's attitude remained unchanged.

He just stared at me as if telling me to state my case already.

"The Young Palace Lord cannot make a request to a lowly person of unknown origins. I'd rather cut off this arm with my own hands!"

Lowly? Hey, I'm not that kind of person. Haven't you heard my name? With the alias of 'Ten Strike Finisher', I had the spirit of the orthodox... actually, I don't even know anymore.

"If you have any jerky, share some. You arm, give it here."

"You crazy fucker!"

Taehwa cursed harshly. I instantly knew what kind of woman she was... Someone with the same personality as the maid from the Palace of Minor Light! Just thinking of that girl made me chuckle.

It seems I've adapted a little to life in the Demonic Cult.

"You dare laugh?"

"You're more talkative than I thought. This is going to hurt."

"What? _Kyaaah!_"

For the first time, a delicate, womanly sound burst from Taehwa. The problem was that it was a cry.

I unhesitatingly rolled up her sleeve and pressed the acupoints around the wound.

"You probably don't have a needle. I need to seal the wound, do you have anything similar?"

At my question, Yayul Jin broke his own sword without hesitation. Then he handed me a thin shard and said.

"Will this be enough?"

"I think it will. If you would just get these swords away...?"

The swords of the Seven White Branches Martial Squad were all aimed at my neck.

Taehwa endured the pain and said sorrowfully.

"His Lordship... is not a person you can address so informally."

"He seems more interested in your wounds than in that. Don't you think so?"

"Everyone, back away."

At Yayul Jin's command, they all took three steps back.

"A pretty decent Liege."

I took the shard from him, tore Taehwa's sleeve, and made a thread to tie it.

"Endure it even if it hurts. You won't die."

"You crazy bastard!"

I brushed off the woman's shout and examined her arm.

It's a slash. Was it a blade wound? The edges were sharply cut, and there were no signs of crushing around it. It seems she didn't even resist.

There was stagnant blood and muscle stiffening.

I looked at the woman seriously and asked.

"Does it hurt?"

"...want me to do the same to you?"

"I understand perfectly."

_Did they go into the desert? Yellow sand is stuck into the wound. That's how lockjaw sets in. How long did she endure in this state without even applying any wound medicine?_

Lockjaw was a disease where muscles and skin became necrotic. I'd have to debride her wound to remove infection... but there's no way they have medicinal herbs.

"Clean water."

As soon as I said it, a waterskin was placed in my hand. When I poured it on her arm, there were no more screams, but her face contorted like a demon's.

After washing out the sand thoroughly, I unhesitatingly cut away the flesh that was becoming necrotic.

_Splurt!_ Dark red blood flowed out thickly, as if coagulated.

"Can you get me some cicada shells?"

"...Find some."

As soon as Yayul Jin's words ended, the Seven White Branches Martial Squad disappeared toward the Tianshan Mountains like streaks of light.

Now the preparations were complete.

"I have to suture it now. It's going to hurt a lot. Should I press your xunxue point to numb it?"

"Fuck you!"

"I knew you wouldn't need it."

I began to sew her wound closed with the thin blade and thread.

"Guh! You— son of a—"

Of course, Taehwa's cursing didn't stop either.
