_CRUNCH, CRUNCH._ Paper crumpled and tore under my leather shoes. I kicked an annoying book out of the way, clearing the path.

_Grrr!_ I could faintly hear the Lord grinding his teeth.

His teeth seem healthy. He'll live a long life.

_PLOP._ I finally sat in his seat and tilted my head all the way back. A forced smile spread across my face. I looked up at him, then gestured with my chin toward the seat in front of me, where I had been.

That's the kind of relationship we have. One where we should have drawn swords on each other long ago.

So, get out before we grow attached.

"Fufu..."

I couldn't tell if it was a sigh or a laugh, but his expression was unexpectedly calm. Just as the records described, he was a born merchant. The pride of a martial artist didn't seem to be an important issue for him.

And I had dealt with men like him before— two of them, in fact.

One was the head of the Manwol Trading Company, said to be the wealthiest in the Central Plains. The other was the Beggar's Gang Chief.

They were polar opposites, so I found it fascinating. I once asked the Chief how the two of them could be so similar.

The Beggar's Gang Chief's answer was clear.

_One who has everything and one who has discarded everything are a single hair apart, you punk._

_Pfft._ Funnily enough, this man resembled both of them.

And I knew exactly how to deal with men like these.

You just have to scratch them where they itch.

"Look here, the Lord of Merchants. I absolutely hate complicated things," I began. "I finish my secluded training and come out to find my father missing and my retainers gone. I try to live like I used to, but things just aren't the same, you know? Just thinking about it gives me a throbbing headache."

"Oho... is that so?"

"Don't ask questions. I'll say what I need to say. Anyway, as you know, I don't care what you all do. The Cult Leader's seat has been empty for a long time, so as long as a suitable person takes it, I don't mind who it is. Do you understand what I'm saying? You're smart enough for that, right?"

The Lord of Merchants' eyes glinted like a viper's. Ambition filled them, like a wanderer who had just discovered a mountain of gold.

I decided to lay it on thicker.

"That's why I thought we might be on the same page and planned to stop by the Palace of Merchants to pay my respects... but not only did you try to take my life, you now treat me so frivolously. Truly, I'm disappointed."

I shook my head back and forth, putting on a full display of disappointment.

In contrast, his cheekbones soared. A quick glance at his face seemed to say it all.

_Yes, of course, that's just like you. Why else would you have come to see me? I'll give you everything you want. Heheh. Just give me your name._

I deliberately looked out the distant window and rubbed my jaw, as if to say, 'If you have something to say, say it.'

And then.

"Gwahaha! It seems this humble one has been overcome with worries lately and committed a discourtesy."

A bit old and large to be calling himself 'humble one', isn't he? Isn't he just a petty man?

"Please, grant me your magnanimous mercy! I have been agonizing with the loyalty of a dog and horse over how to apologize to Your Reverence for my worthless son's rudeness."

The loyalty of a dog and horse, he says. Even a dog would laugh at that.

"Is that so? Then I'll hear it. What came at the end of all that agonizing?"

When I retorted bluntly, Cheon Semin replied with a triumphant smile. "I have prepared a gift."

"A gift?"

"Yes, something very special and rare. It will surely be to your liking. Birang!"

At Cheon Semin's call, Birang immediately and politely presented an object finely wrapped in silk, as if he had it ready all along.

The Lord of Merchants took it, placed it carefully on the table, and then gestured with his eyes for me to unwrap it.

What in the world could this be to warrant such a fuss?

Filled with questions, I peeled back the cloth, one layer at a time.

And then.

"These are... duandaos?" [^1]

They were exactly that. Two duandaos, seemingly a pair. They were ornate, with blades about a foot long and handles embossed with a black flame pattern that drew a graceful arc.

Unusually, judging by the curve of the hilt and blade, they were apparently meant to be held in a reverse grip. It was undoubtedly an item from the Western Regions, not the Central Plains.

As if to say that was enough for now, Cheon Semin covered them back up with the cloth. He handed them to Birang and said with a sly grin, "Perhaps. Have you heard of the Five Divine Treasures?"

_The Five Divine Treasures!_

One of them, the Mask of Sacred Flames, was already in my possession. Then, could it be?!

"Yes, that's right. Specially prepared for you, Your Reverence, this is one of the Five Divine Treasures, the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames. What do you think? Are you pleased?"

Was it because my expression was too shocked?

He approached my side, looking triumphant. Then he said, as if whispering, "This is a divine object that even the previous Cult Leader could not obtain. I will give it to you, Your Reverence. And that is not all. I will also send a generous amount of gold bars to the Palace of Minor Light every month. We live in a world where money can even buy officials. I will enable you to live as you please. I will build you a palace even grander than this Palace of Merchants. So."

"So...?"

Cheon Semin wore a smile full of triumph. "How about you join me? By right, the position of His Eminence the Cult Leader is yours. The Palace of Merchants will help you. I, Cheon Semin, will support your enthronement, Your Reverence!"

"You want me to become the Cult Leader?"

Cheon Semin spread his arms wide as if to embrace the world. He said, as if it were the most natural thing, "Isn't it only natural! Since ancient times, legitimacy has been paramount in the imperial family, so how can another dare to sit in that seat? This is treason. A conspiracy! The heavens will be enraged!"

My eardrums rang. How utterly absurd.

Since when was the Demonic Cult an imperial family? No wonder they were deemed traitors, harboring such dangerous ideas.

Of course...

"Truly... you are completely correct!"

"Gwahaha! I firmly believed that you, Your Reverence, would understand this Lord's intentions."

"Truly amazing. But..." I wiped the smile from my face and asked sternly, "Then what do you get out of it? I know you're quite close with the Palace of Martial Blood. Weren't you supporting Lord Yayul Cheon? Tell me. What's in it for you?"

"Haha, that is... of course..."

"Of course?"

"Of course... hmmm..."

Cheon Semin's boisterous laughter faded, and he looked at me with suspicion.

I met his gaze, widening my eyes as if urging him to hurry up and speak.

And then.

"This is truly quite unusual."

"What is?"

"That you, Your Reverence, are curious about such things."

Just how did Ma Cheonso live his life? Did he even have a brain? What did he do that everyone finds it strange for me to simply question things?

"Am I... a dead man? Am I not allowed to think?"

"It's not that you can't, it's that I don't understand."

"Why not?"

"Come to think of it... I heard you have a new, unknown bodyguard."

"Hmmm..." This time, it was I who let out a low hum at his question. It had no particular meaning.

"After returning to the Palace of Minor Light, you were absent for a while, weren't you? Did you perhaps visit another Palace before coming here?"

I visited the Valley of Sorrow.

"So, what if you've already joined hands with _someone_? What if this _someone_ gave you that bodyguard and told you to come to me, observe my movements, and get what you can... ? Then I'm truly just a frog croaking at the roof!"

Am I the only one who finds this uncomfortable? Cheon Ahmu is definitely Cheon Semin's son. Their behaviors are identical. Who am I even talking to right now?

But I only maintained a serious expression, showing no other reaction.

"Indeed... it's unthinkable for the Young Cult Leader to visit this Lord in person and remain so composed. There must already be some _other_ hand! Who is it? Quite impressive— a place capable of devising such a scheme... is it the Palace of Murder? Or the Palace of Bliss? Don't tell me... the Palace of Martial Blood?!"

All wrong.

It's Yang Jagak, captain of the Murim Alliance's Wind Dragon Brigade.

"I have no idea what you're talking about."

"It's no use pretending otherwise now. Tell me the truth. What is your real reason for visiting this Lord?"

"I believe I've already told you."

"Hmph! You expect me to believe that now?"

**_BLAM!_** Cheon Semin stomped his foot, slamming it into the floor. The stone tiles cracked, and I could feel his immense qi down to my bones.

_Did he grow his qi with miracle herbs? How many did he devour? It's vast, yet turbid._

Occasionally, when one indiscriminately consumed miracle herbs that were incompatible, you could feel such unrefined, murky qi.

_Since he raised his qi with money, it's only natural that money comes before martial arts._

As I frowned and stared blankly, Cheon Semin approached me with a sneer filling his face. Whatever he was thinking, he said, "To dare visit me after already boarding another ship means there's still room. Whether I'll board that ship or sink it is something I'll have to watch a little longer. But before that!"

Don't talk to me, just talk to the wall...

"Shouldn't we clearly determine who the captain of the ship is?"

I placed both hands on the desk and gave a serious reply to the waiting man. "The Palace of Merchants isn't the only place with capital, is it?"

"Heheh. You would compare us to those painted wenches?"

"It _is_ said that when it comes to information networks, the women of the Palace of Bliss are superior."

"That's... ugh..." For the first time, the Lord of Merchants revealed his anguish.

The Seven Hallowed Palaces each possessed their own martial organizations and formed a great power, but in terms of their roles, the context was subtly different. The Palace of Sacred Flames was the Ministry of Personnel, in charge of administration. The Palace of Martial Blood was the Ministry of War, which handled the military. The Palace of Murder and the Palace of Blades were the Inquisition, carrying out punishments. The Palace of Poison served the role of a hospital. And the Palace of Merchants and the Palace of Bliss jointly served the roles of the Ministry of Revenue and the Ministry of Rites, handling finance and foreign affairs, which was the problem.

With war on the horizon, it was only natural that the value of the places with outstanding financial power and information networks would skyrocket. However, since they were interchangeable with each other, it was also natural for their competition to reach an extreme.

> _Information is like a side dish served with drinks. That's why I have no choice but to meet them! Because the real intelligence comes out of brothels._

Cheongheo... your words are useful sometimes.

The Palace of Merchants was inferior to the Palace of Bliss in terms of information networks. That was its flaw.

I raised the corners of my lips and added, "Even if the ship sinks, it's not like the Lord will have nowhere to go. But whether you'll be at the helm or the oars might depend on the ship you board, don't you think?"

"Ahem...!" As if struck where he was most vulnerable, his eyes widened in shock and his head shot up.

"...is what he told me to tell you."

After I finished speaking, he looked around the room as if pondering for a long while. He didn't forget to mumble like a babbling infant, either.

After waiting and waiting, he turned back to me and spoke hesitantly. "Fine... I'll trust you this once. But not yet. I must meet him in person, so please arrange a date."

"Well, I'll consider it some other time..." I clapped my palms together and rose from my seat.

Then, I scanned my surroundings, looked at the piles of gold, silver, and treasures, and said, "As for the gifts you prepared, I'll accept them, considering your efforts."

A muscle on one side of his face twitched as if paralyzed.

I spoke nonchalantly as I walked past him. "Thirty percent of the assets held by the Palace of Merchants. That's the boarding fee. What do you think? Cheap, right? I'll give you one month to have it sent to the Palace of Minor Light. You can deliberate on which option is more cost-effective. And now I have business with the Lady of Bliss, so I'll be off."

The Lord of Merchants' beak opened and closed several times. A shout must have hammered at his throat hundreds of times...

"Today...! Please rest here before you go. I have prepared these accommodations with great care."

...but in the end, the Lord of Merchants took a full step back.

"Is that so? Then, out of consideration for Lord Cheon, I'll stay and rest for a few days. As you were."

I turned my back and waved my hand over my head, but didn't forget to take the Demonic Blades of Sacred Flames from Birang.

_CREAK._ The door opened, and I walked down the corridor again, heading for my guest room.

_The Lord of Merchants, Cheon Semin. If you want to climb the shining golden mountain before your eyes, you'll have to put down your weighty baggage, one by one._

My first meeting with him was over. A smile spread across my lips.

He will keep on climbing his mountain, filled with hope, never dreaming that it would turn out to be naught but a mound of dirt.

[^1]: Hanja: 短刀. _Dando_ in Korean, _tantō_ in Japanese, _duǎndāo_ in Chinese. A short sword, or in this case, a pair of them.
