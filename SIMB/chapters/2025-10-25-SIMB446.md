For this reason, the Giant Spirit Boss committed the crime of patricide. He snuck into his sleeping father's chambers, strangled and threatened him, then found the hidden secret manuals.

When morning came, the Giant Spirit Keep had a new boss.

Watching the beasts gorge themselves, he grinned to himself.

*Yes, these friends will never betray me.*

Still, there were prerequisites. The animals had to be fed on time, and they had to be constantly reminded that their master was stronger than them. As long as he adhered to these two rules, not even the most savage beast would betray him.
