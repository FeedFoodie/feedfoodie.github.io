So Jigwang had loved blades since he was a boy.

"Then I'll be the world's greatest bladesman!"

Adding 'blade' to the term 'world's greatest' made it sound even cooler. Whether it was the sword, the fist, or the spear, nothing could compare to the blade.

Even when he was a snot-nosed brat who thought martial artists could fly and break rocks, he always chose the wooden dao when playing with friends.

Later, he followed an old martial artist to the Black Snake Alliance simply because of the dao at the man's waist. The old man had praised him, saying he had good bones. This led him to give So Jigwang's parents one tael of silver to buy a disciple. If life had gone a little differently, he might have died on the road while caring for the old man. 

Fortunately, So Jigwang was lucky. His master was first-rate at best, but he had a solid grasp of the fundamentals of bladesmanship. With his natural talent, So Jigwang achieved complete mastery in less than three years, catching the attention of the Black Snake Alliance's top experts.

He met the Bow Phantom a little later.

So Jigwang traveled the world, meeting and dueling countless bladesmen. If fighting wasn't an option, they would discuss the blade all night. He won most of the time, lost some, and sometimes drew.

Even so, he never doubted that he was the man most suited for the title of world's greatest bladesman.

"Kh... haha..." The Black Snake Alliance Chairman laughed as he coughed up blood. The rock he leaned against had long been stained dark red by the blood flowing from his countless wounds, but a faint smile appeared on his lips as he recalled the past. "This must be what people talk about when they refer to seeing one's life flash before one's eyes right before death."

"......" The Third Apostle of the Blood Cult stood in front of him like a ghost. Like the Chairman, he wasn't in good shape either. His martial arts uniform was shredded as if he had been in a hurricane, and it was drenched in crimson. Especially terrifying was the deep gash on his chest.

"Is that sorcery?" the Black Snake Alliance Chairman asked.

"......" The Third Apostle did not answer, because he didn't know either.

His opponent's blade had definitely grazed his heart, yet he was alive. Not only that, he was even able to block the final move of the Ultimate Blade Technique and launch a counterattack, dealing a fatal wound to the Black Snake Alliance Chairman. Had his heart not mysteriously held out, he would have been the one defeated.

"Fuck, this sucks. I didn't expect sorcery to intervene in a fight to decide the world's greatest bladesman..." the Black Snake Alliance Chairman grumbled, but he didn't look too aggrieved.

He was the master of the Black Snake Alliance, one of the greatest unorthodox organizations. He had survived countless ambushes, schemes, and encounters with hidden weapons and poison. Even if the Third Apostle used sorcery, he wouldn't criticize it as cowardly.

He just thought it was a bit of a pity.

"I have no regrets losing to a bladesman stronger than me. What's frustrating is losing to a bladesman who hates the dao."

The Black Snake Alliance Chairman lowered his head and stroked his beloved weapon, Crimson Moon.

The Third Apostle strode toward the Black Snake Alliance Chairman.

The Chairman looked up and straightened his back, intending to meet his end calmly, but he couldn't suppress a groan escaping his mouth, "Ugh..."

A stinging pain rose from his wrecked qi center. The inner qi he had built up all his life was scattering, but still, it was more bearable than he thought.

The Third Apostle immediately recognized this phenomenon as terminal lucidity, in which a person briefly becomes energized before death.
