The newcomer gripped a dao in her right hand while holding a candle in her left, its flickering light casting dancing shadows across her features.

Wang Xiaoshi stared in stunned recognition. *Isn't she the careless person I nearly collided with in the crowded streets earlier today?*

Pitch darkness engulfed the room except for the wavering candlelight she carried. The soft glow painted her cheeks in shades of pink like ripe apricots, adding warmth to her face. Her eyes sparkled with anticipation, showing not the slightest trace of fear despite being completely surrounded by enemies.

"Those are some impressive blade skills..." Huo Dong's words died as his eyes widened in surprise. "Wait, aren't you just a little girl?"

The woman's face lit up with a pleased smile at the praise, but the moment she heard 'little girl', her expression turned sour. "How do you know I'm a woman?"

The men who had fallen silent in awe of her blade technique burst into raucous laughter.

Huo Dong pointed at himself with a teasing grin. "Do I look like a man or a woman to you?"

The young bladeswoman snapped back irritably, "Of course you're a man, what else could you be?"

Huo Dong mimicked her tone, his voice turning delicate and affected as he gestured humorously at his chest. "Then surely you must be a woman, what else could you be?"

The woman stamped her foot in fury and stepped forward with her blade raised high. "For the crimes of kidnapping and mutilating children, I'll turn all of you Six Half Hall people over to the authorities!"

Huo Dong stepped back with exaggerated fear. "You? Arrest us? All by your lonesome self?"

Laughter erupted around him in waves.

Even as Huo Dong mocked the girl, his gaze remained fixated on the gleaming edge of her blade. While she obviously lacked real jianghu experience, her skill demanded respect. Provoking her into blind rage before moving in for the kill was probably his best course of action.

Gu Hanlin copied Huo Dong's fake frightened tone. "And what will you do with us once you've captured us?"

Ding Shouhe pointed at his own trousers while grinning crookedly. "Go ahead, capture me! Should I die beneath a peony flower, I will still be a charming ghost. It's an honor to be courted by such a lovely young maiden. Please, take me!"

The men exploded in a chorus of lewd laughter.

Li Dan alone remained stone-faced, understanding the gravity of their situation. Although he worked for Six Half Hall, he and his brothers would face consequences if any information leaked.

His mind crystallized with deadly resolve. *I cannot let this woman leave this place alive!*

The woman's face instantly turned to ice while the candle flame flickered ominously.

"Careful!" Huo Dong yelled.

Ding Shouhe jerked backward abruptly.

SLAM! WHAM!

He crashed into the two men behind him, sending them flying. Seconds later, when the dust settled, he saw two clean gashes around his waist.

The blood drained from his face as he stared at his torn clothes. He glanced back at the woman, reluctant to approach her again.

Everyone stood frozen in shock. Although they had mocked the woman, they had secretly kept their guard up.

Nevertheless, the speed of her blade took everyone by surprise. Although they knew she would strike the moment her expression changed, all they saw was a flicker of candlelight before Ding Shouhe was nearly split in two. If not for his expertise in movement arts, he would be dead.

Only Huo Dong snorted and prepared to lunge forward.

Before he could move, though, Zhao Tieleng's voice cut through the tension. "How are you related to Su Mengzhen?"

The woman's eyes widened in shock. "How did you know about Senior Brother..."

Realizing her slip of the tongue, her voice trailed off into silence.

Zhao Tieleng nodded knowingly. "No wonder you can use the Frosty Peak's Starlight Blade Technique."

"So you're the young prodigy of the wulin, the Frostpeak Swallow Wen Rou!" Huo Dong cried out in surprise.

Zhao Tieleng's gaze hardened like cold iron, and his voice rang out with metallic resonance, powerful and commanding. "Since you're one of Sunset Drizzle Haven's people, you can forget about leaving here alive tonight. Don't blame us for this!"

Wen Rou held her head high with dignified defiance. "I am not from Sunset Drizzle Haven. I came to the capital on my master's behalf, to ask Senior Brother why he stirred up such chaos in the city. You might outnumber me, but I'm not afraid of you. In fact, I was hunting the culprit behind all the nasty crimes in this area. You're the ones who should forget about escaping!"

Huo Dong arched his silvery eyebrows in amusement. "It would seem that none of us intend to escape."

The others laughed along with him, but they all remained vigilant, ready to fend off an ambush from Wen Rou. After all, even a single moment of carelessness could cost them their lives.

Gu Hanlin smiled mockingly. "For the famous Wen Rou to grace us with her presence is a great honor. We can't wait to welcome you!"

Huo Dong declared triumphantly, "Capturing Young Master Su's little sister will be Six Half Hall's first great achievement in half a year."

The moment he finished speaking, the tension in the room escalated dramatically. Li Dan and Li Jiaohong, the siblings, were particularly eager to take action.

Meanwhile, Ding Shouhe, still seething from Wen Rou's previous insult, gave in to his lustful desires. As he studied Wen Rou's masculine disguise in the candlelight, he realized her charm remained undiminished despite her disguise. Her brows resembled painted ink, her eyes held the stillness of deep water, and her skin outshone frost and snow. His desire to possess her and redeem his honor surged powerfully.

However, before Li Dan, Li Jiaohong, or Ding Shouhe could act, the ever-smiling Gu Hanlin seized the initiative. His reason was simple. He desired more achievements!

As soon as he heard Huo Dong's words, he came to the realization that Wen Rou was a prize worth fighting for. And so, before anyone else could move, he dashed to her side, ready to unleash seven consecutive strikes and subdue her.

The Li siblings and Ding Shouhe, unwilling to be outdone, immediately followed suit.

Four accomplished martial masters lunged at Wen Rou simultaneously. Though they appeared to attack as one, there was still a sequence.

Gu Hanlin moved first. For that reason, he was the first to see the flash of her blade.

The moment he advanced, the light of her blade appeared.

He hastily retreated, and the faint gleam vanished.

Li Dan attacked second. His martial arts were a level above Li Jiaohong's, so even though they moved together, he was slightly faster.

Naturally, Wen Rou's blade found him second, arriving with breathtaking swiftness, delicate as whispered moonlight and weightless as a gentle breeze.

Li Dan could block four carriages with one arm and defeat opponents with brute strength, but he was defenseless against such a delicate and graceful blade technique.

He, too, fell back in retreat.

As he withdrew, the blade's gleam settled on Li Jiaohong.

She tried to parry, but missed. She tried to dodge, but she could not move in time. She wanted to leap up, but she knew she would get cut in the process. With no other option, she hurriedly retreated seven steps.

Finally, the blade's light shifted to Ding Shouhe.

Having already tasted Wen Rou's blade, fear made his movements sluggish. As he watched the other three retreat, he immediately backed away without a second thought.

Wen Rou's blade flashed four times, but even after she pulled back, the candle on her palm continued to burn steadily. Despite the efforts of the four masters who tried to surround her, she fended off all four attacks, forcing them to retreat with nothing to show for their efforts.

"Fufu..." Wen Rou grinned confidently at Huo Dong, appearing completely in control of the situation.

Peeking through the crack in the cabinet, Wang Xiaoshi found Wen Rou's beautiful and spirited demeanor increasingly endearing. Just as he tried to get a better look, however, someone suddenly blocked his view and whispered urgently, "The moment I yell 'Now,' you'll help me subdue the Li siblings. Leave the rest to me."

Wang Xiaoshi froze. The tall, slender figure in front of him could only be the young scholar who had been looking up at the sky earlier.

Meanwhile, Wen Rou felt quite pleased with herself for forcing back four assailants in a single move. A look of triumph flickered in her eyes.

Zhao Tieleng, whose expression seemed carved from right angles, turned to Huo Dong. "Ninth Brother, it looks like you can't hide your Golden Hand Seal anymore."

The two men slowly repositioned themselves, flanking Wen Rou.

Wen Rou tensed, holding her blade against her back and letting the chill of the steel seep through her clothes. Adopting a guarded stance, she asserted, "I'm not scared of you."

Zhao Tieleng and Huo Dong burst out laughing.

"Ninth Brother," Zhao Tieleng joked, "if we catch this little filly, I'll leave her with you. Only you can tame her."

Huo Dong chuckled back. "You'll have to watch out. She's quite prickly."

Zhao Tieleng smirked. "Is it time?"

Huo Dong called out into the darkness. "Brother Bai, what do you think?"

The scholar in fine clothing stood with his hands behind his back, looking at the ceiling, and replied, "Hall Master Huo has already won. Why ask me?"

Wen Rou was livid. From their conversation, it was clear that they saw her as no threat at all. She immediately prepared to lash out, but before she could move, she saw Huo Dong's gaze sharpen, and his silver brows quiver.

"Now!" he roared, releasing a dazzling golden light from his hands.

Wen Rou's heart skipped a beat. She quickly moved to shield herself with her blade, but a sharp pain shot through her left palm, breaking her concentration. The candle in her hand had melted, causing the hot wax to drip onto her palm!

This was the exact moment Huo Dong had been waiting for. Taking advantage of her brief distraction, he reached for her blade with lightning speed.

Wen Rou immediately attempted to wrench the blade free. Her Starlight Blade was no common weapon, and could slice through iron like mud.

Huo Dong nearly lost his grip and was forced to catch the blade between his palms.

"Now!" the young scholar cried out sharply. 

Zhao Tieleng struck Wen Rou from behind, his hands lashing out like the claws of a tiger.

Wen Rou's face blanched with terror. All things considered, her combat experience was lacking. Luckily, Zhao Tieleng's fists flew past her ears, one hitting Huo Dong's face and the other his chest.

Blood poured from Huo Dong's mangled face while he vomited.

"Ugh!" Wen Rou cried out in alarm. The man's face literally cracked open right before her eyes, and the sight horrified her so much that her legs gave out. Even the candle was blown out by the force of the punch.

THUD!

The room went dark, and the sound of a body hitting the ground rang out right next to her.

FWOOSH!

A candle was relit, illuminating the room once again. Looking down, she saw that Gu Hanlin had fallen and was dead.

She quickly assessed her surroundings. Everything in the room had been knocked over, and a candle now rested in the young scholar's hand. His expression remained cold and detached, as if the events that had just transpired had nothing to do with him.

Contrasting with the calm scholar, many people lay scattered on the ground. Gu Hanlin, Ding Shouhe, Li Dan, Li Jiaohong, Huo Dong, and all the men they had brought with them were strewn across the floor. The only difference was that the Li siblings merely had their acupoints sealed while the others were dead.

*Huh? Huo Dong's dead. Did Zhao Tieleng kill him?*

While Huo Dong was focused on dealing with Wen Rou, his comrade Zhao Tieleng had taken the opportunity to kill him. However, in the instant that Huo Dong fell and the candle went out, the scholar's figure darted to the east and west.

In the blink of an eye, Gu Hanlin, Ding Shouhe, and the other twelve men in the room had been fatally wounded. Gu Hanlin had pushed open the door, but a jab to the back of his neck sent him crumbling to the ground. Ding Shouhe had already half-extended his body out the window when a strike to his back left him half-paralyzed on the windowsill, unable to move another inch.

Wang Xiaoshi stood by and took it all in. Those still standing were the victors and those on the ground were the losers. The winners had plotted before acting, biding their time. Some, like himself and the woman, had won in a daze, without understanding exactly how or why. Meanwhile, the defeated could no longer stand, and many had lost their lives.

*Is life and death in the jianghu always decided so quickly and abruptly?*

Shrouded in darkness, all Wang Xiaoshi could hear was a faint, urgent gust of wind, followed by the sound of bodies hitting the floor. When the candle was lit again, the scholar was standing on the sidelines with his hands clasped behind his back, looking so relaxed that it seemed as if he had never moved.

However, Wang Xiaoshi knew that not only had this man moved, he was also a ruthless master.

For some reason, after hearing the scholar's whisper, Wang Xiaoshi couldn't help but obey when he heard the word 'now. He simply darted out, found his targets, and subdued the Li siblings without killing them.

Nevertheless, he still couldn't make sense of the situation. Why had Zhao Tieleng killed Huo Dong? Who was the scholar in brocade robes? What was their connection to Wen Rou, who had popped up out of nowhere?

Zhao Tieleng rubbed his hands together as if to cleanse them of blood. His eyes swept the room, his gaze moving in right angles, making his expression seem slow yet sharp.

Finally, he looked at the scholar in satisfaction. "It's all settled."

The young scholar smiled slightly. "Yes, it is."

Zhao Tieleng pointed at Wang Xiaoshi. "Who is this person?"

Wang Xiaoshi noticed that, as the man raised his elbow and bent his finger, every motion formed a right angle, making him look like a wooden puppet.

The young scholar smiled as he glanced at Wang Xiaoshi. "I don't know yet. We'll find out in a moment."

A hint of appreciation flashed in Zhao Tieleng's dull eyes. "He is very useful."

"Useful people are not often willing to be used," the scholar added.

Zhao Tieleng slowly turned his head. "A useful person who isn't used by others is no better than a useless person."

The young scholar countered, "Getting use out of the useless is the greatest form of use."

"What a shame, Brother Bai," Zhao Tieleng remarked. "I have underutilized your great talents. You are a genius waiting for an opportunity."

The young scholar flashed a dashing smile. "I am currently being paid one hundred silver taels."

Zhao Tieleng quickly fumbled inside his robes. "Of course, of course. I'll add an extra fifty percent to your share, Brother Bai."

The young scholar took three banknotes, held them up to the candlelight for a moment, and tucked them into his sleeve. "Thank you," he said, grinning.

Wen Rou looked from the young scholar to Zhao Tieleng, then at Wang Xiaoshi, feeling as if she had been forgotten. She had tracked this group of performers to their meeting place, only to be discovered. Before she could test her blade and confront the demons, however, a moment of distraction had nearly cost her her life. Then, within the span of an extinguished and relit candle, the floor was littered with corpses.

Who was the enemy? Who was a friend? She couldn't tell anymore. She only knew that she was no longer the central figure on this stage.

As this thought crossed her mind, she demanded, "Who are you? What are you doing? What in the world just happened?!"

Zhao Tieleng and the young scholar exchanged glances and laughed.

The questions Wen Rou asked, though, were the same ones on Wang Xiaoshi's mind.

*Who are these people? What in the world just happened?*

He forgot that Wen Rou's question had also included him. He only knew that his own questions included Wen Rou.

*Just who is she? And what on earth is she doing here?*
