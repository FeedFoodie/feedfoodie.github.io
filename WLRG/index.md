## Synopsis

When you think about it, it's absurd and unfair that one's life is largely determined at birth.

Some people are born holding a golden spoon. They were lucky enough to be conceived from a rich man's sperm and a wealthy woman's egg. Effort? I suppose you could count them waving their tails and swimming faster than the other sperm as effort.

Others are born with a talent for something. Depending on the circumstances, that talent can transform even a wooden spoon into a golden one.

The absurdity of one's starting point applies in this world, too. Some people come here having already learned martial arts or magic. 

Simply put, their Starting Line is different.

And me?

I had nothing at all.
