## Synopsis

The very idea of a "starting line" is a cruel and absurd joke.

Some people begin life already holding a golden spoon. They were born from a rich man's sperm and into a wealthy woman's womb, and if you want to call wiggling a sperm's tail faster than the others "effort," then that was the only one they ever made.

Then you had the ones born with talent, a special gift that could transform a dirt spoon into a golden one all on its own.

This world is just as absurd. People show up here having already mastered martial arts or learned the secrets of magic. Their starting lines are simply too different.

What about me, you ask?

I started with jack shit.
